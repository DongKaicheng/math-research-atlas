# Strong Green and the full height theorem — 23 November 2026

## Editorial change

Atlas presentation revision 2 makes the principal-push implication visible on
the Beilinson–Bloch project homepage, research map, direct Green route, Progress,
Plan and conjecture overview. The published mathematical source is Theorem 7.5
(PH16), `r16ph:general-height`, in the global height paper of edition v0.5.1.

The presentation now states that, within the paper's standing analytic
foundations, Strong G at every finite completion for every specified
geometrically homologically trivial rational cycle in both complementary
codimensions yields the canonical real height on both full Chow groups. It
links directly to the theorem and its proof. The principal norm formula,
Green-choice independence, reciprocity, finite summation and descent through
rational equivalence are identified as parts of the supplied proof, rather
than listed collectively as unfinished assembly work.

General Strong G remains open. The analytic foundations retain their separate
mathematical review status. Correspondence adjunction, classical comparison,
positivity, nondegeneracy and arithmetic finiteness keep their own hypotheses
and research questions. The direct theorem is distinguished from the separate
obstruction-kernel route; it does not require a converse criterion for a
preselected obstruction map.
