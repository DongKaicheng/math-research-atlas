## Mathematical changes

This edition reviews all 22 statements labelled Hypothesis in v0.5.2.
Twenty now have explicit internal proofs: three are Theorems and seventeen
are Propositions. Two remain Hypotheses. All original statement numbers and
anchors are preserved. The Sources page contains the complete 22-row audit,
including exact source hashes, former and current labels, and scope notes.

The former height-assembly hypothesis is separated into two mathematical
assertions. H16 constructs the normalized pairing on the intersection of
the local obstruction kernels at **all finite completions**. This has an
internal proof. Identifying this domain with a **fixed finite-test kernel**
requires the comparison retained as programme Hypothesis 5.2. The permanent
record `BB-H16-KERNEL-DOMAIN`, whose statement includes that finite-test
presentation, therefore remains conditional.

Zhang-route Hypothesis 5.1 retains the arithmetic pullback, pushforward and
projection identities for every model morphism selected in that route.
Comparison on supplied admissible regular models or alterations does not
construct these operations on arbitrary models or prove the existence of
such admissible data.

The current calculus is stated on the algebraic dual of compact actual
tests, with the support conditions required for the Green functionals.
It does not assert multiplication of arbitrary classical distributions by
delta-forms. Finite trace and projective integration retain normal or smooth
carriers as appropriate, all compact actual tests, the original absolute
value and full native degrees. Supported residues retain refined
intersection multiplicities.

The original analytic-operation tasks `groute:operations` and
`obroute:height-problem` are also closed at their original numbers and
anchors, as Propositions 6.3 and 5.5. Their scope is the specified operations,
common local kernels and supplied admissible comparison data.

The six permanent records for the analytic interface, rational-fibre
rigidity, finite numerical descent, algebraic equivalence, the abelian
stage and abelian Chow transfer now refer to their reviewed proofs.
The general Strong Green conjecture, general homological source-image
vanishing, full-domain existence without its stated premise, positivity,
nondegeneracy and arithmetic finiteness are not established by this edition.

## Sources and review

This was an internal AI-assisted source review and cross-review. It is not
external peer review or formal verification. Proof inspection was kept
separate from rendering, repository tests and deployment checks.

Twenty mathematical source fragments from the recovered Round 15 and Round
16 archives complete the dependency chain for the finite ambient atlas,
graphic-tree primitive, projective integration and finite descent. The public
source index now contains 199 files. Each added fragment records its exact
hash and archive member. Private audit notes and complete research archives
are excluded from the repository publication.

Seven papers receive mathematical or explanatory revisions. The construction
companion additionally receives two editorial remark labels. These eight
papers are rendered from their expanded LaTeX; the other three PDFs and
expanded sources retain the preceding edition's exact bytes. Explicit labels
on five previously unanchored definitions, remarks and an example, together
with support for italic remark headings, repair inherited HTML numbering
while preserving the PDF numbers and every existing anchor. Original v0.5.1
and v0.5.2 artifacts remain immutable.
