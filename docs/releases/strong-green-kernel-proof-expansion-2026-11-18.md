the low-dimensional endpoint argument, the empty factor product (potentially
good reduction), and the DP20 range with at least one curve or surface factor

## Two positive-host routes

The arithmetic-family host diagram retains two alternative complete local
arguments, not a single strengthened hypothesis:

- **PSH22 / AS22-local / PC16 / RI18:** the uniform signature on the whole
  numerical group over every finite residue extension gives component
  primitive positivity. PC16's canonical residual and weighted isotropy give
  native obstruction zero. RI18's inertia norm calculation and the exact FD17
  numerical kernel then treat every new cycle after every finite valued
  extension. This route does not require the boundary curve-comparison gate.
- **SU22 / RB18:** the uniform signature together with curve comparison on
  every actual boundary stratum supplies another sufficient route. The
  ample-boundary comparison argument is drawn separately, including numerical
  pushforward, weak Lefschetz and its dual Gysin map.

The component positivity has two distinct consequences: the PU18 calculation
of the full numerical quotient, and PC16's actual-obstruction argument. The
former is not substituted for the latter. The projective-space and supplied
projective-bundle applications retain the primary signature-only route.

The source check includes the public PC16 proof and its explicit historical
residual/isotropy inputs. The two earlier R15 files are not separately present
in this frozen public corpus, so those inputs remain marked hypotheses; this
revision does not claim to have independently re-proved them. The same
qualification applies to the exact FD17/VT17 analytic detector. The initial
construction field is unramified over the distinguished prime, but subsequent
finite fields in the family theorem are not restricted to be unramified.
