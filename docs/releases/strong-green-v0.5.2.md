## What this edition changes

The programme's former Hypothesis 2.2 is now Theorem 2.2, with its proof
assembly and a link to the complete companion article, **Chow Obstructions
and Prescribed Actual Green Pairs**. The companion supplies the recovered
internal proof of the Chow-invariant actual Bott-Chern obstruction and the
curved actual Green pair for each prescribed rational cycle. Its statement
retains the original valued field, exact support complement and full space
of compact actual tests.

The companion has 31 PDF pages. The programme and related papers, project
overview, Plan, Progress, routes and proof maps have been reconciled with
this construction. The permanent programme anchor `hyp:green-pairs` remains
available. The current mathematical edition is v0.5.2 and the Atlas project
revision is 3.

This edition does not establish general source-image vanishing or the
unconditional general Beilinson-Bloch pairing. `STRONG-G` and `BB-GLOBAL`
remain open. The separate height-construction paper retains its broader
analytic premises. Independence concerns the obstruction class, not unique
Green-form or curvature representatives.
