# Bedford–Taylor public edition v1.0.1

Publication date: 18 November 2026 (Asia/Shanghai).

This is the first complete Bedford–Taylor reading publication in the GitHub
atlas. It preserves the initial prepared v1.0.0 snapshot and selects v1.0.1
after the completed source-scope and shared-feedback review. The recovered
research baseline remains Round 13 / checkpoint v32, with regularization
manuscript R v2.6 and bounded-calculus manuscript B v0.1.

## Contents and mathematical status

The edition contains 74 thematic records, 26 definition groups, 66 formal
statements in the mature-results article, 14 formal open programme items,
and 105 source files. The two principal articles are available in HTML,
PDF and unsigned LaTeX. There are 12 public PDF assets in total and two
download filenames for the documented unsigned reading-source package.

All 105 original source identities were checked against the actual recovered
files. Reading-copy hashes are recorded separately where publication edits
change bytes. Every thematic record retains its source review qualification;
all 14 formal open items remain open. Publication does not provide a new
independent certification of the proofs.

The source-scope review changes O01 to “actual analytic curvature relation”
and explicitly states the supplied normal-three-model premise in the G12
and G13 summaries. These changes clarify the existing scope without extending
the mathematical claims.

In the supplied normal-three quadratic model, the action vanishes off the
centre and the remaining action is a central coefficient times a Dirac mass.
The coefficient is expressed by the realized tangent pairing. Its vanishing
for arbitrary allowed degree-one backgrounds remains open. The general
analytic-relation descent and associated research programme remain open.

The historical R21 full original ZIP/PDF has not been recovered. Preserved
or reconstructed text and recovered appendices do not replace those files.

## Feedback and future updates

All reader discussion uses the atlas repository's GitHub Issues. General
comments require no project, theorem ID or edition. Contextual feedback keeps
the optional project, permanent ID, source page and exact edition. Formal
paper anchors map to existing registered IDs; changing projects clears stale
context. Long context is omitted with an explanation rather than silently
truncated into a different reference. Opening a draft does not submit an Issue.

The original research site points to the same board. Reader contributions do
not directly edit the mathematical corpus. Accepted changes follow the linked
Issue, reviewed source change and new frozen-edition procedure described in
[maintenance](../MAINTENANCE.md). Fixed editions and source receipts are
append-only. Shared-file updates preserve all projects and use a guarded,
non-force branch update.

This publication does not configure recurring backups, unattended Issue
triage or automatic mathematical review.
