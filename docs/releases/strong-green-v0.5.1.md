# Beilinson–Bloch public edition v0.5.1

Publication date: 18 November 2026 (Asia/Shanghai).

This is the first complete Beilinson–Bloch research edition in the GitHub
atlas. It follows the original atlas outline and preserves the project’s
permanent mathematical identities.

## Contents and mathematical status

The edition contains 54 permanent records, three research routes, ten articles
in HTML, PDF and self-contained LaTeX, and 177 downloadable source projections.
The PDFs contain 190 pages in total. One additional provenance record describes
a source without publishing its download.

The routes concern vanishing of curvature obstructions, direct construction of
strong Green forms, and admissible arithmetic liftings following Zhang. Each
route has its own account, mathematical article and PDF.

The source register retains 17 records with an internal proof available,
15 open records, 11 draft records and 11 conditional records. Publication
does not change these mathematical statuses. In particular, the algebraic and
motivic transfer statements remain conditional, the model detector and
supported Green-pair interfaces remain under review, and the general strong
Green conjecture and canonical full-domain height pairing remain open.

## Publication review

Separate agent reviews inspected mathematical status, source provenance,
feedback behavior, history safeguards and public attribution. The mathematical
review checked the stated transfer premises, the kernel-domain pairing and the
published sources underlying the Zhang route. This was not a new independent
certification of every proof in the corpus.

All ten PDF author fields are empty. Extracted PDF text and public textual
sources were checked for personal bylines and unintended private details.
The twenty PDF/TeX asset hashes and 177 downloadable-source hashes match their
records. A total of 340 frozen public files have matching source/build checksums.
Historical private signed archives are excluded from the public edition.

The publication passed the thirty repository tests and the release-integrity
check. The checks cover permanent links and anchors, complete paper/record
search inclusion, feedback categories and context, safe reader-text rendering,
pagination, failure recovery, and immutable release bindings.

## Feedback and maintenance

All public comments use this repository’s GitHub Issues. General feedback
requires no mathematical target or version. Mathematical forms carry optional
project, result, page and exact edition context. Opening a form creates no
submission. Accepted changes are developed separately and linked to their
Issue, review and new publication edition.

The original Sites deployment is transitioned separately to a read-only
navigation bridge. Its private feedback database and source history are
retained; private submissions are not copied to public Issues. Historical
signed downloads receive a withdrawal notice rather than being silently
relabelled as this unsigned edition.

See [maintenance](../MAINTENANCE.md) for updates, review, concurrent work,
corrections, rollback and backups, and [paper rebuilding](../PAPERS.md) for
matching HTML, TeX and PDF production. No scheduled mathematical review or
recurring backup is established by this publication.
