A nonzero ideal lowers the strict fibre dimension

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 482

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A nonzero ideal lowers the strict fibre dimension}{非零理想降低 strict 纤维维数}]
\label{fnd:generalclosedfiberdimensiondrop}
\bi{Let $X$ be good over any complete field and let $V$ be an ordinary affinoid neighborhood of $t$ in $\mathbb A_X^{d,\mathrm{an}}$. Put $x=f(t)$ and assume that $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is a field and $F=\mathcal H(x)$ is nontrivially valued. For every nonzero proper ideal $I\subset\mathcal O_{V,t}$, its closed-subspace germ has an inner ordinary affinoid representative over $X$ with strict fibre algebra of Krull dimension at most $d-1$. Its ordinary and fibre stalks are respectively}
{设 $X$ 为任意完备域上的 good 空间，$V$ 是 $t$ 在 $\mathbb A_X^{d,\mathrm{an}}$ 中的普通 affinoid 邻域。置 $x=f(t)$，并假设 $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 为域，$F=\mathcal H(x)$ 的赋值非平凡。对每个非零真理想 $I\subset\mathcal O_{V,t}$，其闭子空间芽均有 $X$ 上的内普通 affinoid 代表，使 strict 纤维代数的 Krull 维数至多为 $d-1$；其普通茎与纤维茎分别为}
\[
             \mathcal O_{V,t}/I,
             \qquad \mathcal O_{V_x,t}/I\mathcal O_{V_x,t}.
\]
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Represent a finite generating family of $I$ and a nonzero $h\in I$ on an ordinary affinoid neighborhood. Its fibre germ is nonzero: otherwise its zero set contains an ordinary fibre neighborhood, and Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalgenericfibreneighborhood}, applied on the ordinary smooth interior of $V$, would then give an ordinary neighborhood on which $h$ vanishes at every point. By \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:smoothordinarynormalascent]{smooth normality ascent}} and Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalnormalcomparisonlocus}, take a reduced ordinary affinoid neighborhood of $t$. The spectral-radius and nilradical formulas in Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedspectralradiusformula} and Proposition~\ref{fnd:spectralnilradicalinternal} make the germ of $h$ zero. Proposition~\ref{fnd:innerstrictfibrerefinement} gives an ordinary representative with strict fibre $C$. This fibre is an affinoid domain of $\mathbb A_F^d$, so Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomainregular} and Lemma~\ref{fnd:regularaffinoidcomponents} make $C$ a finite product of regular domains. Lemma~\ref{fnd:originalfiberidempotentcut} retains the factor $C_0$ through $t$ over the original ground field. The image of $h$ in $C_0$ is nonzero, and Lemma~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomaindimensionbound} gives $\dim C_0\leq d$.}
{在普通 affinoid 邻域上表示 $I$ 的有限生成组及非零的 $h\in I$。它的纤维芽非零；否则其零点集包含一个普通纤维邻域，在 $V$ 的普通光滑内部应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalgenericfibreneighborhood}，将给出 $h$ 逐点消失的普通邻域。由\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:smoothordinarynormalascent]{光滑态射的正规性上升}}及 Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalnormalcomparisonlocus} 取 $t$ 的约化普通 affinoid 邻域。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedspectralradiusformula} 和 Proposition~\ref{fnd:spectralnilradicalinternal} 的谱半径及幂零根公式，$h$ 的芽为零。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:innerstrictfibrerefinement}，可选普通代表使其纤维代数 $C$ strict。该纤维为 $\mathbb A_F^d$ 的 affinoid 域，所以 Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomainregular} 与 Lemma~\ref{fnd:regularaffinoidcomponents} 将 $C$ 写成有限个正则整环的乘积。Lemma~\ref{fnd:originalfiberidempotentcut} 在原基域上保留经过 $t$ 的因子 $C_0$。$h$ 在 $C_0$ 中的像非零，且 Lemma~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomaindimensionbound} 给出 $\dim C_0\leq d$。}

\bi{The chosen generators define a closed subspace with strict fibre algebra $C_0/I_C$. Every prime chain in this quotient lifts to a chain containing the nonzero ideal $I_C$ in the domain $C_0$; adjoining its zero prime increases the length by one. Thus the quotient has dimension at most $d-1$. Finite closed-subspace base change gives the two stalk identifications. The closed immersion is inner, and the chosen ambient ordinary neighborhood is inner over $X$, so their composite is inner as well.}
{所选生成元定义一个闭子空间，其 strict 纤维代数为 $C_0/I_C$。该商中的每条素理想链，提升为整环 $C_0$ 中包含非零理想 $I_C$ 的链；在下端添入零素理想便使长度增加一，故商的维数至多为 $d-1$。有限闭子空间的基变换给出两个茎识别。闭浸入为内态射，而所选环境普通邻域在 $X$ 上亦内，故其复合内。}
\end{proof}
