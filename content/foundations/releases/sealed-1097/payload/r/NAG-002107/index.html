5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{The tangent torus of an initial stratum}{初始分层的切向环面}]
\label{cand:initialtangenttorus}
\bi{Use the finite complex constructed in Theorem~\ref{cand:finitegroebnercomplex}. Let $w$ lie in the relative interior of a cell $\tau\subset\operatorname{Trop}(X)$, put $L_\tau=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbf R}(\tau-\tau)$ and $M_0=M\cap L_\tau^\perp$. The ideal of $Y=\operatorname{in}_w(X)$ is generated by elements of $k[M_0]$, after multiplication of its generators by unit characters. Consequently it is extended from its intersection with $k[M_0]$, and}
{采用 Theorem~\ref{cand:finitegroebnercomplex} 构造的有限复形。设 $w$ 位于胞腔 $\tau\subset\operatorname{Trop}(X)$ 的相对内部，置 $L_\tau=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbf R}(\tau-\tau)$、$M_0=M\cap L_\tau^\perp$。将生成元乘以可逆特征后，$Y=\operatorname{in}_w(X)$ 的理想由 $k[M_0]$ 中的元素生成。因此它是与 $k[M_0]$ 的交理想之扩张，且}
\[
 Y\simeq T_{N_\tau}\times Z_\tau,
 \qquad
 Z_\tau=\operatorname{Spec}\bigl(k[M_0]/(I_Y\cap k[M_0])\bigr),
 \quad N_\tau=N\cap L_\tau.
\]
\bi{The product uses a lattice splitting, while the quotient scheme $Z_\tau$ is intrinsic to $Y$ and $N_\tau$.}
{直积分解使用一个格分裂，而商概形 $Z_\tau$ 由 $Y$ 及 $N_\tau$ 内在确定。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use the finitely many homogeneous pivot rows in the proof of Theorem~\ref{cand:finitegroebnercomplex}, and divide each row by its pivot character. A surviving exponent difference $a$ satisfies one of the affine Pl\"ucker equalities defining the cell. That equality holds throughout the relative interior of $\tau$, hence its linear part vanishes on $L_\tau$. Thus $a\in M_0$ after dehomogenization. These rows generate the initial Laurent ideal, so it is extended from an ideal $J_0\subset k[M_0]$.}
{采用 Theorem~\ref{cand:finitegroebnercomplex} 证明中的有限组齐次主元行，并将每行除以其主元特征。每个保留的指数差 $a$ 满足定义该胞腔的一条仿射 Pl\"ucker 等式。该等式在 $\tau$ 的整个相对内部成立，故其线性部分在 $L_\tau$ 上消失。因此去齐次化后 $a\in M_0$。这些行生成初始 Laurent 理想，所以该理想来自 $k[M_0]$ 中某个理想 $J_0$。}

\bi{The lattice $N_\tau$ is saturated. Lemma~\ref{cand:elementarylatticesplitting} splits $N=N_\tau\oplus Q$, and dually $k[M]$ is a Laurent polynomial ring over $k[M_0]$. Comparing coefficients in these additional Laurent variables shows $J_0 k[M]\cap k[M_0]=J_0$. The quotient ring is therefore $(k[M_0]/J_0)[N_\tau^\vee]$, proving the assertion including its scheme structure.}
{格 $N_\tau$ 饱和。由 Lemma~\ref{cand:elementarylatticesplitting}，可分裂 $N=N_\tau\oplus Q$；对偶地，$k[M]$ 是 $k[M_0]$ 上的 Laurent 多项式环。比较新增 Laurent 变量的系数，得到 $J_0 k[M]\cap k[M_0]=J_0$。故商环为 $(k[M_0]/J_0)[N_\tau^\vee]$，这同时证明了所述概形结构。}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[\bi{Finite filtrations and zero-dimensional algebras}{有限滤过与零维代数}]
\label{cand:finiteprimefiltration}
\bi{Every finite module over a Noetherian ring has a finite filtration whose nonzero successive quotients are $A/\mathfrak p$ for prime ideals $\mathfrak p$. A zero-dimensional algebra of finite type over a field $k$ is finite-dimensional over $k$.}
{Noetherian 环上的每个有限模具有有限滤过，其非零相继商均形如 $A/\mathfrak p$，其中 $\mathfrak p$ 为素理想。域 $k$ 上的有限型零维代数在 $k$ 上有限维。}
\end{lemma}
