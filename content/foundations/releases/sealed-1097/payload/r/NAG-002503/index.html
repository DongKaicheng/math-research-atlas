Native scalar-extension invariance

C Native scalar extension and finite restriction of constants

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1758

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Native scalar-extension invariance]
\label{f9cand:native-global}
Let $X$ be a good Hausdorff $K$-analytic space pure of analytic dimension
$d$, and let $\F$ be coherent on $X_G$. Then $X_L$ is good, Hausdorff and
pure of analytic dimension $d$. Coherent pullback $\F_L=p^*\F$ and
ordinary moment pullback are defined on actual completed scalar
extensions, and for every $\alpha\in\A^{d,d}_{X/K,c}(X)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:native}
 \int_{X_L/L,\F_L}p^*\alpha=\int_{X/K,\F}\alpha .
\end{equation}
The pullback has compact support. These assertions respect complete
extension towers, coherent short exact sequences, and ordinary open
restriction containing the support.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{f9cand:native-global}]
\textbf{Spaces and coefficients.}
Actual scalar extension and inverse images of domains are supplied by
[\ref{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension}, \ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange}]. If $V$ is an ordinary affinoid
neighbourhood of $x$, $p^{-1}(V)=V_L$ is affinoid and contains the open
set $p^{-1}(\Int_{\rm top}(V,X))$. Hence these inverse images give
affinoid neighbourhoods at every point upstairs, proving goodness.
Points with different images can be separated downstairs. Points
with the same image lie in the open interior of one such affinoid
neighbourhood and can be separated there. Thus $X_L$ is Hausdorff.
Lemma~\ref{f9cand:purity} proves its local purity.

On $V=\M(A)$, set $\F_L|_{V_L}$ to be the coherent module represented
by $A_L\otimes_A\Gamma(V,\F)$. The exact finite-module equivalence
[\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb}] and actual iterated tensors [\ref{fnd:iteratedaffinoidbasechange}] make these
constructions agree on restrictions; hence they glue. Faithful
flatness [\ref{fnd:generalscalarpresentation}, \ref{fnd:generalscalarfaithful}] preserves coherent exact sequences.

\textbf{Ordinary form pullback.}
Pull a representative $(V,f,\eta)$ back to
$(V_L,p^*f,\eta)$ near any point over $\Int_{\rm top}(V,X)$.
If two representatives agree on a smaller ordinary affinoid
neighbourhood $W$, their joint moment images after extension equal
the original joint images, by [\ref{fnd:scalarextensionspectralsurjective}].
Tangential equality therefore remains true on $W_L$.
These local representatives define $p^*\alpha$ independently of
choices and glue on ordinary opens. The same description proves
compatibility with $d',d'',J$, wedge and composition of extensions.
This is also the mixed-ground-field version of the representative
argument in [\ref{v037:forms:germsheaf}, \ref{v037:forms:compactintegral}].

\textbf{Support and integration.}
Put $S=\supp(\alpha)$.
Vanishing near $x$ pulls back to vanishing near every point above $x$,
so $\supp(p^*\alpha)\subset p^{-1}(S)$.
Choose finitely many ordinary affinoid neighbourhoods $V_i$ whose
interiors cover $S$, and put $Q=\bigcup_iV_i$.
The full inverse image $Q_L=\bigcup_i(V_i)_L$ is a finite union of
compact affinoids. Since $S$ is closed in the Hausdorff space $X$,
$p^{-1}(S)$ is a closed subset of $Q_L$, hence compact.
It contains the closed support of $p^*\alpha$.
The interiors of the $(V_i)_L$ cover that support.

Use the finite chart data induced by $\alpha$ on $Q$ in
[\ref{v037:forms:germsheaf}, \ref{v037:forms:compactintegral}, \ref{v037:integration:separatedintersections}, \ref{v037:integration:puritylocality}, \ref{v037:integration:coherentcoverdata}, \ref{v037:integration:compactintegral}]. Refine the cover so each member has one
presenting unit tuple and ambient form. Its complete inverse-image
cover gives the pulled-back data on $Q_L$.
Every intersection satisfies $(V_I)_L=\bigcap_{i\in I}(V_i)_L$, with
the actual algebra and coefficient module supplied by [\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange}].
Proposition~\ref{f9cand:chain} applies on every such affinoid
intersection. It equates the corresponding terms in the two finite
inclusion--exclusion formulas [\ref{v037:integration:separatedintersections}, \ref{v037:integration:puritylocality}, \ref{v037:integration:coherentcoverdata}, \ref{v037:integration:compactintegral}]. Thus their compact
integrals agree. The compact-support theorem [\ref{v037:forms:germsheaf}, \ref{v037:forms:compactintegral}] identifies
these with \eqref{eq:native}. The finite unions need not themselves
be good; they lie in the good Hausdorff pure ambient spaces just
established.

For a tower $K\subset L\subset E$, all local pullbacks compose by
[\ref{fnd:iteratedaffinoidbasechange}]. Applying \eqref{eq:native} twice gives its assertion
for $E/K$, with factor one at each step. Exact-sequence compatibility
uses the flat coherent pullback and additivity of the given integral.
Ordinary restriction containing the support uses the same compact
neighbourhoods and the support independence in [\ref{v037:forms:germsheaf}, \ref{v037:forms:compactintegral}].
\end{proof}
