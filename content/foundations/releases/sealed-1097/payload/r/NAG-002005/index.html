Product patches over arbitrary completed residue fields

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 456

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Product patches over arbitrary completed residue fields}{任意完备剩余域上的乘积开片}]
\label{fnd:generalresidueproductpatch}
\bi{Let $Y\to X$ be smooth of pure relative dimension $n$ between good analytic spaces over any complete field. Given $x\in X$ and a nonempty ordinary open subset $W\subset Y_x$, there are an ordinary neighborhood $U$ of $x$, a finite flat map $X'\to U$ with unique point $x'$ above $x$ and reduced ordinary closed fibre there, and a nonempty ordinary open subset $V\subset Y\times_X X'$ such that $V_{x'}$ maps into $W$ and}
{设 $Y\to X$ 为任意完备域上 good 解析空间之间纯相对维数 $n$ 的光滑态射。给定 $x\in X$ 及非空普通开集 $W\subset Y_x$，存在 $x$ 的普通邻域 $U$、有限平坦映射 $X'\to U$（在 $x$ 上方有唯一点 $x'$，且该点处的普通闭纤维约化），以及非空普通开集 $V\subset Y\times_X X'$，使 $V_{x'}$ 映入 $W$ 且}
\[
                         V\simeq U'\times_K D\times_K\Delta
\]
\bi{over $X'$. Here $U'$ is an ordinary neighborhood of $x'$, $D$ is either absent or a one-dimensional open annulus, and $\Delta$ is an open polydisc making the total relative dimension $n$. If $\mathcal H(x)$ is nontrivially valued, $D$ may be omitted and $X'\to U$ may be chosen \'etale. If the ground field has characteristic zero, the map may also be chosen \'etale when $\mathcal H(x)$ is trivially valued.}
{此同构在 $X'$ 上成立。这里 $U'$ 是 $x'$ 的普通邻域，$D$ 或为空因子，或为一维开圆环，$\Delta$ 为开多圆盘，使总相对维数为 $n$。若 $\mathcal H(x)$ 的赋值非平凡，可省略 $D$ 且使 $X'\to U$ 为 \'etale。若基域特征零，则即使 $\mathcal H(x)$ 平凡赋值，也可使此映射为 \'etale。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The nontrivially valued case is Theorem~\ref{fnd:nontrivialresidueproductpatch}. Suppose that $F=\mathcal H(x)$ is trivially valued. Proposition~\ref{fnd:trivialfieldsmoothgausspoint}, applied to the smooth open fibre subset $W$, gives a finite extension $E/F$ and a point $z\in W_E$ whose completed residue field is $E$ or $E_r$ for some $0<r<1$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:trivialresiduefiniteflatlift} realizes $E$ by a finite flat $X'\to U$ with the prescribed unique point and reduced closed fibre. Its fibre product with $Y$ has analytic fibre $Y_x\widehat\otimes_F E$, by associativity of complete base change, so it contains the actual point $z$.}
{非平凡赋值情形已由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:nontrivialresidueproductpatch} 证明。现设 $F=\mathcal H(x)$ 平凡赋值。将 Proposition~\ref{fnd:trivialfieldsmoothgausspoint} 用于光滑纤维开集 $W$，得到有限扩张 $E/F$ 及点 $z\in W_E$，其完备剩余域为 $E$ 或某个 $E_r$，其中 $0<r<1$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:trivialresiduefiniteflatlift}，可用有限平坦映射 $X'\to U$ 实现 $E$，且具有指定的唯一点与约化闭纤维。由完备基变换的结合性，它与 $Y$ 的纤维积具有解析纤维 $Y_x\widehat\otimes_F E$，因此包含上述实际点 $z$。}

\bi{Choose an ordinary neighborhood of $z$ in the fibre product whose intersection with the fibre is contained in $W_E$, using the subspace topology of analytic fibres. In the rational case apply Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalfibresmoothproductpatch}. In the other case $r$ is free over the trivially valued $E$; apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:freegausssmoothproductpatch} with $m=1$. Independence of the free differential also implies $n\geq1$ in this case. These applications give exactly the stated ordinary product patches. In characteristic zero the finite extension $E/F$ is separable, so the last clause of the lifting lemma makes the base change \'etale.}
{利用解析纤维的子空间拓扑，在纤维积中选择 $z$ 的普通邻域，使其与纤维的交包含于 $W_E$。在有理情形下应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalfibresmoothproductpatch}；在另一情形下，$r$ 在平凡赋值域 $E$ 上自由，故取 $m=1$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:freegausssmoothproductpatch}，而自由微分的独立性也说明此时 $n\geq1$。所得恰为所需普通乘积开片。特征零时，有限扩张 $E/F$ 可分，故提升引理的最后一项使该基变换为 \'etale。}
\end{proof}
