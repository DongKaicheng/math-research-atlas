Proper contractions as ringed-space quotients

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 767

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[Proper contractions as ringed-space quotients]
\label{v036:f5:properquotient}
Let $q:T\to Y$ be a proper birational morphism of integral
schemes, with $Y$ normal. Then
$q_*\mathcal O_T=\mathcal O_Y$. Every morphism $h:T\to Z$
which is constant on the set-theoretic fibres of $q$ factors
uniquely through a morphism $Y\to Z$.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Let $U=\operatorname{Spec}A\subset Y$ be affine. A section
$f\in\Gamma(q^{-1}U,\mathcal O_T)$ is a rational function in
the common function field $F$. For every valuation ring
$A\subset R\subset F$ with fraction field $F$, properness
extends the generic map to $T$ to a map
$\operatorname{Spec}R\to q^{-1}U$, by
\cite[Tag 0A40]{StacksAlg}. Therefore $f\in R$. The normal
domain $A$ is the intersection of these valuation rings
by \cite[Tag 090P]{StacksAlg}, so $f\in A$. The reverse
inclusion is pullback. These equalities commute with
restriction and prove the sheaf assertion.

The proper map $q$ is surjective, since its closed image
contains the generic point of $Y$. It is therefore a closed
quotient map on underlying spaces. The map $h$ induces a
unique continuous map $\bar h:Y\to Z$. For an open
$U\subset Z$, pullback by $h$, followed by the sheaf equality,
gives
\[
 \mathcal O_Z(U)\longrightarrow
 \mathcal O_T(q^{-1}\bar h^{-1}U)
       =\mathcal O_Y(\bar h^{-1}U).
\]
These maps respect restrictions. They are local on stalks:
choose $t$ above $y$; if the image in $\mathcal O_{Y,y}$ of
an element of $\mathfrak m_{Z,\bar h(y)}$ were a unit,
its image in $\mathcal O_{T,t}$ would be a unit, contrary
to locality of $h$. Hence $\bar h$ is a morphism of schemes.
Surjectivity and the injective sheaf pullback give uniqueness.
\end{proof}
