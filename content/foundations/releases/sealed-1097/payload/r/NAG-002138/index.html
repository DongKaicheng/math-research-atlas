Original-field faithful tropicalization of a split model skeleton

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 920

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Original-field faithful tropicalization of a split model skeleton}
{分裂模型骨架在原域上的忠实热带化}]
\label{cand:originalfieldfaithfulskeleton}
\bi{There is a nonempty Zariski open $U\subset X$ with
$\Sigma\subset U^{\mathrm{an}}$ and a closed immersion over
the original field $K$,}
{存在非空 Zariski 开集 $U\subset X$，使
$\Sigma\subset U^{\mathrm{an}}$，并有定义在原域 $K$ 上
的闭浸入}
\[
 \varphi:U\hookrightarrow\mathbf G_{m,K}^n
\]
\bi{whose tropicalization restricts to a homeomorphism on
$\Sigma$. On a finite subdivision of its edges, it is integral
affine with primitive derivative vector. Consequently it
preserves every edge length with the original absolute value,
when the image is measured by primitive lattice length.}
{其热带化在 $\Sigma$ 上限制为到其像的同胚。将边作
有限细分后，该映射整仿射且导数向量本原。因此，若热带
像按本原格长度计量，它保持原绝对值下的每条边长。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Take finitely many graph neighborhoods from
Proposition~\ref{cand:originalfieldskeletalcoordinates} covering
the compact graph. Gather their rational coordinate functions
into $F$. The union $V$ of the squares of these neighborhoods
is open in $\Sigma\times\Sigma$ and contains the diagonal.
On every pair in $V$ the coordinates of $F$ separate distinct
points. The complement $E$ is compact and disjoint from the
diagonal.}
{从 Proposition~\ref{cand:originalfieldskeletalcoordinates}
取有限个图邻域覆盖紧图，将其有理坐标函数汇集为 $F$。
这些邻域在 $\Sigma\times\Sigma$ 中的平方之并 $V$
为包含对角线的开集。对于 $V$ 中的每个互异点对，
$F$ 的坐标能将它们分离。补集 $E$ 紧且与对角线不交。}

\bi{Any two distinct $x,y\in\Sigma$ are distinguished by a
nonzero rational function over $K$. Indeed, any algebraic affine
open of $X$ contains their generic algebraic supports, and the
two distinct seminorms on its coordinate ring differ on some
element. Both values are nonzero by generic support. The strict
inequality of absolute values persists on an ordinary neighborhood
of $(x,y)$. Finitely many such pair neighborhoods cover $E$.
Append their rational functions to $F$, obtaining a globally
injective continuous map $G$. A continuous injection of this
compact graph into a Hausdorff real vector space is a
homeomorphism onto its image.}
{任意两个互异的 $x,y\in\Sigma$ 均可由定义在 $K$ 上的
非零有理函数区分。事实上，$X$ 的任一代数仿射开集都
包含它们的泛代数支撑，而坐标环上的两个不同半范数在
某个元素处取值不同。泛支撑保证两个取值均非零。绝对值
不等的条件在 $(x,y)$ 的通常邻域中保持。有限个这样的
点对邻域覆盖 $E$；将其有理函数添入 $F$，得到全局
单射连续映射 $G$。从紧图到 Hausdorff 实向量空间的
连续单射是到其像的同胚。}

\bi{Choose a nonempty affine open $\operatorname{Spec}A\subset X$.
Invert the finitely many nonzero numerators and denominators of
the coordinates of $G$, obtaining $B$, and then invert finitely
many nonzero algebra generators of $B$, obtaining $B'$. Those
generators and their inverses generate $B'$. Together with the
coordinates of $G$ they therefore give a surjection from a
Laurent polynomial $K$-algebra onto $B'$, hence the required
closed immersion for $U=\operatorname{Spec}B'$. Every function
inverted is nonzero at every generic-support point, so
$\Sigma\subset U^{\mathrm{an}}$. All choices and functions
are defined over $K$.}
{选择非空仿射开集 $\operatorname{Spec}A\subset X$。
将 $G$ 的坐标的有限个非零分子、分母取逆，得到 $B$，
再将 $B$ 的有限个非零代数生成元取逆，得到 $B'$。
这些生成元及其逆生成 $B'$；连同 $G$ 的坐标，它们给出
从 Laurent 多项式 $K$-代数到 $B'$ 的满射，故对于
$U=\operatorname{Spec}B'$ 得到所需闭浸入。每个被取逆
的函数在每个泛支撑点上都非零，因此
$\Sigma\subset U^{\mathrm{an}}$。全部选择与函数均定义
在 $K$ 上。}

\bi{Apply Proposition~\ref{cand:originalfieldskeletalcoordinates}
to all final coordinates and take a finite common subdivision.
On each resulting edge, one of the local coordinates has
derivative $1$ or $-1$ on a nonempty subinterval, hence on the
entire edge because it is affine there. The full integer
derivative vector is therefore primitive. Appending coordinates
does not remove that coordinate or destroy injectivity. The
primitive lattice parameter on the image is exactly the original
annular length parameter, which proves the length assertion.}
{对全部最终坐标应用
Proposition~\ref{cand:originalfieldskeletalcoordinates}，
并取有限共同细分。在所得每条边上，某个局部坐标在
一个非空子区间上的导数为 $1$ 或 $-1$；由于它在整条
边上仿射，此导数在整条边上相同。故完整整导数向量
本原。添加坐标不会移除该坐标，也不破坏单射性。热带
像的本原格参数恰为原圆环长度参数，由此得到边长断言。}
\end{proof}
