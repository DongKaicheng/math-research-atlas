Graded domination and the integral criterion

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 562

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Graded domination and the integral criterion}
{分次支配与整性判据}]
\label{fnd:gradedvaluationintegrality}
\bi{Every local graded subring of $F$ is dominated by a graded
valuation subring of $F$. For graded $k$-subalgebras $R\subset S\subset F$,}
{每个 $F$ 的分次局部子环都被 $F$ 的某个分次赋值子环支配。
对分次 $k$-子代数 $R\subset S\subset F$，有}
\[
 \mathscr P_k(F)\{R\}=\mathscr P_k(F)\{S\}
       \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad S\text{ is integral over }R.
\]
\bi{If $S$ is finitely generated over $k\subset R$, these
conditions are also equivalent to $S$ being finite over $R$.}
{若 $S$ 在 $k\subset R$ 上有限生成，则这些条件还等价于
$S$ 在 $R$ 上有限。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A homogeneous prime of a ring graded by an ordered group
is an ordinary prime: the extreme nonzero homogeneous terms
in a product cannot cancel. Let $C$ be a local graded subring
with homogeneous maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$.
Apply \cite[Tag 00IA]{StacksAlg} to the ordinary local domain
$C_{\mathfrak m}\subset\operatorname{Frac}(F)$.
Take the sum of the homogeneous parts of the resulting ordinary
valuation ring. This is a graded valuation subring of $F$;
its maximal homogeneous ideal contracts to $\mathfrak m$.
This proves domination.}
{有序群分次环中的齐次素理想也是通常素理想：乘积中最外端的
非零齐次项不可能抵消。设 $C$ 为具有齐次极大理想
$\mathfrak m$ 的分次局部子环。将
\cite[Tag 00IA]{StacksAlg} 用于通常局部整环
$C_{\mathfrak m}\subset\operatorname{Frac}(F)$。
取所得通常赋值环的全部齐次部分之和，就得到 $F$ 的分次赋值
子环，其极大齐次理想收缩为 $\mathfrak m$。这证明支配性。}

\bi{A graded valuation ring is integrally closed on homogeneous
elements. If $b$ were integral but outside it, $b^{-1}$ would
lie in its maximal homogeneous ideal. Dividing a homogeneous
monic equation by its leading power would then put $1$ in
that ideal. Conversely, if a homogeneous $b$ is not integral
over $R$, then $b^{-1}$ is not a unit of $R[b^{-1}]$:
an expression for its inverse would give a monic equation for
$b$. By \cite[Tag 00E0(2)]{StacksAlg}, choose an ordinary
maximal ideal containing $b^{-1}$. Its homogeneous part is a
homogeneous prime: a product of two homogeneous elements in
that part has one factor in it. Localize at the homogeneous
elements outside this prime and apply domination to the
resulting graded local ring. The resulting valuation
contains $R$ and excludes $b$. Thus equality of the valuation
sets detects integrality of all homogeneous elements of $S$.
These elements generate $S$, so this is ordinary integrality
as well. Finally, a finite set of algebra generators, each
integral over $R$, gives a finite module by reducing their
powers with their monic equations.}
{分次赋值环对齐次元素整闭。若齐次 $b$ 整却不属于该环，则
$b^{-1}$ 属于其极大齐次理想；将齐次首一关系除以最高次幂，
就会使 $1$ 属于该理想。反之，若齐次 $b$ 在 $R$ 上不整，
则 $b^{-1}$ 不是 $R[b^{-1}]$ 的单位，因为其逆的表达式会给出
$b$ 的首一关系。由 \cite[Tag 00E0(2)]{StacksAlg}，选取包含
$b^{-1}$ 的通常极大理想。其齐次部分是齐次素理想，因为两个
齐次元素的乘积属于该部分时，至少一个因子也属于它。
在该素理想外的齐次元素处局部化，再对所得分次局部环应用
支配性，得到包含 $R$ 而不包含 $b$ 的赋值环。因此，两个赋值
集合相等检测 $S$ 的全部齐次元素的整性。这些元素生成 $S$，
故这也等价于通常整性。最后，有限个整的代数生成元可利用其
首一关系降低幂次，从而给出有限模。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Gradings in this section are by the ordered group
$\mathbf R_{>0}$. A graded valuation subring $V$ of a graded
field $F$ is a graded subring such that, for every nonzero
homogeneous $a\in F$, either $a$ or $a^{-1}$ belongs to $V$.
Its homogeneous nonunits form its unique maximal homogeneous
ideal. For a graded subfield $k\subset F$, write
$\mathscr P_k(F)$ for these valuation subrings containing $k$.
For homogeneous elements $a_i$, the sets}
{本节的分次群取有序群 $\mathbf R_{>0}$。
分次域 $F$ 的分次赋值子环 $V$ 指一个分次子环，使每个非零
齐次 $a\in F$ 至少有 $a,a^{-1}$ 之一属于 $V$。
其齐次非单位组成唯一的极大齐次理想。
对分次子域 $k\subset F$，以 $\mathscr P_k(F)$ 记包含 $k$
的这些赋值子环。对齐次元素 $a_i$，集合}
\[
 \mathscr P_k(F)\{a_1,\ldots,a_n\}
      =\{V:a_1,\ldots,a_n\in V\}
\]
\bi{form a basis of its topology. For a graded subalgebra
$R\subset F$, use $\mathscr P_k(F)\{R\}$ for the valuations
containing $R$. A birational space with field $F$ is a connected
quasi-compact quasi-separated space with a local homeomorphism
to $\mathscr P_k(F)$. It is affine if this map identifies it
with $\mathscr P_k(F)\{R\}$ for a finitely generated graded
$k$-subalgebra $R$.}
{构成其拓扑的一个基。对分次子代数 $R\subset F$，以
$\mathscr P_k(F)\{R\}$ 记包含 $R$ 的赋值环。
具有域 $F$ 的 birational 空间是一个连通、拟紧、拟分离空间，
并带有到 $\mathscr P_k(F)$ 的局部同胚。
若该映射将它识别为某个有限生成分次 $k$-子代数 $R$ 所给的
$\mathscr P_k(F)\{R\}$，则称其为仿射。}
