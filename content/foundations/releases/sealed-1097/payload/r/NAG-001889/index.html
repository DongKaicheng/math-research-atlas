Analytic flatness of polydisc and affine-space projections

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 429

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Analytic flatness of polydisc and affine-space projections}
{多圆盘及仿射空间投影的解析平坦性}]
\label{fnd:relativepolydiscanalyticflat}
\bi{Over a good analytic space $X$, a relative closed polydisc of any
positive radii and relative analytic affine space are good, and their
projections to $X$ are analytically flat. The same holds for the
restriction of either projection to a good analytic domain of its
source. These assertions include every good base change over every
complete valued extension of the ground field.}
{在 good 解析空间 $X$ 上，任意正半径的相对闭多圆盘及相对解析仿射
空间均 good，且它们到 $X$ 的投影解析平坦。将任一投影限制到其源的
good 解析域后，同样成立。上述断言包含在基域的任意完备赋值扩域上
作任意 good 基变换的情形。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{At a polydisc point over $x$, choose an affinoid neighborhood of
$x$. Its inverse image is an affinoid polydisc containing a
neighborhood of the chosen point. Thus the total space is good,
and the preceding theorem gives flatness of its ordinary local
map. After a good base change over a complete extension, the
bounded tensor universal property identifies the pullback with the
polydisc of the same radii. Its ordinary stalk maps are flat by
the same proof over that extension. This is analytic flatness.}
{在位于 $x$ 上方的多圆盘点处，选择 $x$ 的 affinoid 邻域。
其逆像为包含所选点的邻域的 affinoid 多圆盘，故总空间 good，
前一定理给出其通常局部映射的平坦性。在完备扩域上作 good
基变换后，有界张量的泛性质将拉回识别为相同半径的多圆盘。
在该扩域上应用同一证明，其通常茎映射均平坦。这就是解析平坦性。}

\bi{Every point of relative analytic affine space has a closed
polydisc neighborhood: take each radius strictly larger than the
value of its coordinate, and an affinoid neighborhood in the base.
The interior inequalities give an ordinary neighborhood, so stalks
agree with those of that polydisc. The construction works after
every complete-extension good base change, proving the affine-space
assertion. Finally, Theorem~\ref{fnd:alldomainanalyticflatness} supplies
flat ordinary domain-stalk maps after all such base changes, with
good pulled-back domains. Composing them with the projection-stalk
maps proves the last assertion.}
{相对解析仿射空间的每个点都有闭多圆盘邻域：取每个半径严格大于
相应坐标的值，并在基空间中选取 affinoid 邻域。严格内部不等式
给出通常邻域，故其茎与该多圆盘的茎相同。这一构造适用于每个
完备扩域上的 good 基变换，得到仿射空间断言。最后，
Theorem~\ref{fnd:alldomainanalyticflatness} 给出这些基变换之后的
平坦通常域茎映射及所得域的 good 性。将它们与投影的茎映射复合，
即得最后的断言。}
\end{proof}
