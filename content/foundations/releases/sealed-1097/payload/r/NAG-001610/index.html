The structure sheaf of an atlas

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 385

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The structure sheaf of an atlas}{Atlas 的结构层}]
\label{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf}
\bi{There is a canonical sheaf of rings $\mathcal O_{X_G}$ whose restriction to every chart is its affinoid structure sheaf. Explicitly, for an analytic domain $Y$,}
{存在典范环层 $\mathcal O_{X_G}$，其在每个 chart 上的限制都是该 chart 的 affinoid 结构层。具体地，对解析域 $Y$，有}
\[
 \mathcal O_{X_G}(Y)=
 \left\{(s_V)_{V\in\bar\tau,\,V\subseteq Y}:
 s_V\in A_V,\quad s_V|_W=s_W\text{ whenever }W\subseteq V\right\}.
\]
\bi{Given any chart quasi-net $\sigma\subseteq\bar\tau$, the family $\bar\sigma$ of all affinoid domains in members of $\sigma$ is a net. Replacing $\tau$ by this net gives the same analytic domains, coverings and ring sheaf, with canonical comparison isomorphisms.}
{对任意 chart quasi-net $\sigma\subseteq\bar\tau$，其各成员内的全部 affinoid 域组成一个 net $\bar\sigma$。若将 $\tau$ 换成这个 net，则得到相同的解析域、覆盖及环层，并具有典范比较同构。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The displayed expression defines a ring presheaf. Given compatible sections on an admissible covering $(Y_i)$ of $Y$, fix a chart $V\subseteq Y$. The intersections $V\cap Y_i$ are analytic domains of $V$ and form an admissible covering. Refining by affinoid domains and using compactness yields a finite affinoid cover of $V$ subordinate to the $Y_i$. The given sections agree on its overlaps, so Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidcovercech} gives a unique element of $A_V$. It is independent of the chosen finite refinement, since two refinements have a common affinoid refinement. The elements obtained for different $V$ restrict compatibly by the same uniqueness. This proves existence and uniqueness of the glued section. On a chart $V$, the family is determined by its member $s_V$, so the sheaf recovers $A_V$ and all its restrictions.}
{所列式子定义一个环预层。设在 $Y$ 的 admissible 覆盖 $(Y_i)$ 上给定相容截面，并固定 chart $V\subseteq Y$。交集 $V\cap Y_i$ 是 $V$ 的解析域，且构成 admissible 覆盖。进一步作 affinoid 加细并使用紧性，得到 $V$ 的一个从属于各 $Y_i$ 的有限 affinoid 覆盖。给定截面在其交叠上一致，因此 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidcovercech} 给出唯一的 $A_V$ 中元素。两个有限加细都有共同的 affinoid 加细，所以结果与选择无关。同一唯一性说明不同 $V$ 所得的元素在限制下相容。这证明层粘合的存在与唯一性。在 chart $V$ 上，整个相容族由 $s_V$ 决定，故恢复 $A_V$ 及其全部限制映射。}

\bi{For a chart quasi-net $\sigma\subseteq\bar\tau$, chart transitivity gives $\bar\sigma\subseteq\bar\tau$. For two members of $\bar\sigma$, refine their intersection by the net $\bar\tau$. Each refining chart is contained in a member of $\sigma$ and is an affinoid domain there by chart transport, hence belongs to $\bar\sigma$. Thus $\bar\sigma$ is a net. Each chart $V\in\bar\tau$ has an admissible cover by members of $\bar\sigma$: restrict $\sigma$ to $V$ by intersections, then refine each intersection by chart domains. Quasi-net transitivity proves equality of the analytic domains and coverings. The just-proved gluing construction supplies the inverse to restriction of compatible section families. Its local uniqueness makes all comparison isomorphisms transitive.}
{对 chart quasi-net $\sigma\subseteq\bar\tau$，chart 的传递性给出 $\bar\sigma\subseteq\bar\tau$。对 $\bar\sigma$ 的两个成员，用 net $\bar\tau$ 加细其交叠。每个加细 chart 都包含于 $\sigma$ 的某个成员，并由 chart 传递知它是该成员的 affinoid 域，所以属于 $\bar\sigma$。因此 $\bar\sigma$ 为 net。每个 $V\in\bar\tau$ 都有由 $\bar\sigma$ 成员组成的 admissible 覆盖：先通过相交将 $\sigma$ 限制到 $V$，再用 chart 域细分每个交叠。Quasi-net 的传递性证明解析域及覆盖相同。刚才的粘合构造给出相容截面族限制的逆，其局部唯一性保证比较同构的传递性。}
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[\bi{An affinoid atlas}{Affinoid atlas}]
\label{fnd:analyticnetatlas}
\bi{Let $X$ be a locally Hausdorff topological space. A net $\tau$ of compact Hausdorff subsets is a quasi-net such that $\tau|_{U\cap V}=\{W\in\tau:W\subseteq U\cap V\}$ is a quasi-net of $U\cap V$ for every $U,V\in\tau$. An affinoid atlas consists of such a net, affinoid algebras $A_V$, homeomorphisms $V\simeq\mathcal M(A_V)$, and bounded homomorphisms $A_V\to A_U$ for inclusions $U\subseteq V$ in $\tau$. The latter identify $U$ with an affinoid domain of $V$, and are compatible with composition.}
{设 $X$ 为局部 Hausdorff 拓扑空间。由紧 Hausdorff 子集组成的 net $\tau$ 是一个 quasi-net，且对任意 $U,V\in\tau$，族 $\tau|_{U\cap V}=\{W\in\tau:W\subseteq U\cap V\}$ 都是 $U\cap V$ 的 quasi-net。一个 affinoid atlas 由这样的 net、affinoid 代数 $A_V$、同胚 $V\simeq\mathcal M(A_V)$，以及对 $\tau$ 中包含关系 $U\subseteq V$ 给出的有界同态 $A_V\to A_U$ 组成。后者将 $U$ 识别为 $V$ 的 affinoid 域，并与复合相容。}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[\bi{Analytic domains and the analytic site}{解析域与解析 site}]
\label{fnd:generalnetanalyticdomain}
\bi{An analytic domain $Y\subseteq X$ is a subset for which $\bar\tau|_Y$ is a quasi-net, equipped with the induced topology and the restricted atlas. A covering by analytic domains is admissible if it is a quasi-net. The associated site is denoted $X_G$.}
{若子集 $Y\subseteq X$ 上的族 $\bar\tau|_Y$ 是 quasi-net，则称 $Y$ 为解析域，并配备诱导拓扑及限制 atlas。若解析域覆盖为 quasi-net，则称它为 admissible 覆盖。对应 site 记为 $X_G$。}
\end{definition}
