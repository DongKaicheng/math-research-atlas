\begin{proposition}[Norm projection]\label{f9ds:normprojection}
For line bundles $\cL$ on $Y$ and $\cM$ on $X$, there is a canonical
isomorphism
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ex68:f9ds:norm-projection}
 \Nm_q(\cL\otimes q^*\cM)
       \simeq \Nm_q(\cL)\otimes\cM^{\otimes n}.
\end{equation}
In particular $\Nm_q(q^*\cM)\simeq\cM^{\otimes n}$.
The formulas commute with analytic base change.
Consequently the kernel of $\Pic(X)\to\Pic(Y)$ is killed by $n$.
\end{proposition}
\dep{Proposition~\ref{clause:nag002525:coherent_tensor_projection},
Lemma~\ref{f9ds:normrank}, \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:finitecoherentdirectimagebasechange]{\texttt{NAG-001752}}}}

\begin{proof}
The tensor projection isomorphism gives
$q_*(\cL\otimes q^*\cM)\simeq q_*\cL\otimes\cM$.
For any rank-$n$ locally free module $E$, local exterior multiplication
gives
\[
 \det(E\otimes\cM)\simeq \det(E)\otimes\cM^{\otimes n}.
\]
Changing a frame of $\cM$ by $a$ multiplies the top wedge by $a^n$,
and changing a frame of $E$ by a matrix multiplies both sides by the
same determinant. These local maps therefore glue canonically.
Cancel $\det(q_*\cO_Y)$ to obtain \eqref{eq:ex68:f9ds:norm-projection}.
The norm line of $\cO_Y$ has its canonical trivialization, which gives
the specialization.

Finite coherent direct image commutes with analytic base change,
including nonflat base change. Exterior powers of locally free
modules and the displayed frame maps do too. This proves the stated
compatibility. If $q^*\cM$ is trivial, apply its determinant norm and
the specialization to get $\cM^{\otimes n}\simeq\cO_X$.
No injectivity of the unadorned Picard pullback is asserted.
\end{proof}
