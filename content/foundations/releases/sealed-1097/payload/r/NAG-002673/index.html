\begin{proposition}[The comparison preserves both pairings; F8U-P]
\label{prop:pairings}
In the setting of Proposition~\ref{prop:gluing}, pullback identifies
the ordinary smooth moment sheaves on \(X\) and \(X^{\mathrm u}\),
including products, \(d'\), \(d''\), supports, and compact zero
extension. Their top-dimensional structure-module integrals agree.
For a regular analytic \(f\), the top-dimensional Cartier-cycle
pairings agree. For
\(\eta\in\A_c^{n-1,n-1}(X)\) with support in \(\Int(X/K)\),
the tested logarithmic pairings agree whenever defined by the smooth
extension of \(\log|f|\,\dd\eta\); that extension is supplied by
F8F7-S in the companion note, using the exact parent-reported reviewed
F7 support lemma.
\end{proposition}
