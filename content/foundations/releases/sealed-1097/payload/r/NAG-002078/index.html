Factorization and orders at the marked ends

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 697

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Factorization and orders at the marked ends}
{分解与标记端点的阶数}]
\label{cand:markedmodelslopeformula}
\bi{Assume in addition that $\mathcal X$ is projective over
$O$, that $k$ is perfect, and that its generic fibre is a
smooth integral curve $X_K$. Identify its analytic generic
fibre with $X_K^{\mathrm{an}}$ by
Proposition~\ref{cand:projectiveformalcompletion}. Let
$f\in K(X_K)^\times$, and suppose the support of
$\operatorname{div}(f)$ is contained in the marked set $P$.
Writing $r_P$ for the retraction onto
$\Sigma(\mathcal X,P)$, one has}
{再假设 $\mathcal X$ 在 $O$ 上 projective，$k$ 完美，且
泛纤维为光滑整曲线 $X_K$。通过
Proposition~\ref{cand:projectiveformalcompletion}，将解析
泛纤维识别为 $X_K^{\mathrm{an}}$。设
$f\in K(X_K)^\times$，且 $\operatorname{div}(f)$ 的支撑
包含在标记集 $P$ 中。以 $r_P$ 表示到
$\Sigma(\mathcal X,P)$ 的回缩，则}
\[
 F=-\log|f|=F\circ r_P
             \qquad\hbox{on }X_K^{\mathrm{an}}\setminus P.
\]
\bi{The restriction of $F$ is affine with integral slope on
each finite edge and on each marked ray. On the ray toward
$a_i$, its outgoing slope is $\operatorname{ord}_{a_i}(f)$.
At every component vertex it satisfies the residual-degree
weighted balancing identity of
Corollary~\ref{cand:weightedcomponentbalance}.}
{$F$ 在每条有限边及每条标记射线上的限制，均为具有整数
斜率的仿射函数。在朝向 $a_i$ 的射线上，其出射斜率为
$\operatorname{ord}_{a_i}(f)$。在每个分量顶点处，它满足
Corollary~\ref{cand:weightedcomponentbalance} 的带剩余次数
权重的平衡恒等式。}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\bi{The rational function is an algebraic unit off $P$,
because the local rings of the smooth curve are discrete
valuation rings, \cite[Tag 0B8Y]{StacksAlg}. Analytification
therefore makes it an analytic unit on $X_K^{\mathrm{an}}\setminus P$.
On every nodal tube, Corollary~\ref{cand:standardopenunitnorms}
gives $|f|=|c||z|^n$ with $n\in\mathbb Z$. Thus $F$ factors
through the annular retraction and is affine of integral slope
on its edge.}
{由 \cite[Tag 0B8Y]{StacksAlg}，光滑曲线的局部环为离散
赋值环，所以该有理函数在 $P$ 之外为代数单位。因此，
解析化后它在 $X_K^{\mathrm{an}}\setminus P$ 上为解析单位。
在每个节点管域上，Corollary~\ref{cand:standardopenunitnorms}
给出 $|f|=|c||z|^n$，其中 $n\in\mathbb Z$。故 $F$ 经
圆环回缩分解，并在相应边上具有整数仿射斜率。}

\bi{On an unmarked smooth tube with rational residue point,
the same corollary makes $|f|$ constant. For a separable
nonrational residue point, split its residue field as in
Proposition~\ref{cand:separablesmoothtubehomotopy}. The
pullback of $f$ has constant norm on each resulting disc,
equal by endpoint continuity to its value at the corresponding
component point. Those component points all map to the original
vertex, so all these constants equal $|f(v)|$. Surjectivity of
projection shows that $|f|$ has this constant value on the
original tube.}
{在剩余点有理的未标记光滑管域上，同一推论说明 $|f|$
为常数。对可分但非有理的剩余点，按
Proposition~\ref{cand:separablesmoothtubehomotopy} 分裂
其剩余域。$f$ 的拉回在所得每个圆盘上具有恒定范数；
由端点连续性，此常数等于相应分量点处的值。所有这些
分量点都映到原顶点，所以常数均为 $|f(v)|$。投影的满性
说明 $|f|$ 在原管域上也具有此恒定值。}

\bi{Fix a marked tube. Its coordinate $z_i$ can be chosen
from a smooth algebraic chart and centered at $a_i$, as in
Proposition~\ref{cand:puncturedsmoothtube}. The generic-fibre
chart is \textup{\'etale} at $a_i$ and has residue field $K$.
By \cite[Tag 02GU(6)]{StacksAlg}, $z_i$ generates the maximal
ideal of the curve's local ring at $a_i$. It is therefore a
uniformizer. Put $m_i=\operatorname{ord}_{a_i}(f)$. The
quotient $g_i=f/z_i^{m_i}$ is an algebraic unit near $a_i$;
on the remainder of its tube it is an analytic unit, because
$z_i$ has no other zero there and $f$ has no other zero or
pole there. These expressions agree on their overlap, so
$g_i$ is an analytic unit on the whole tube. Hence}
{固定一个标记管域。按
Proposition~\ref{cand:puncturedsmoothtube}，其坐标 $z_i$
可来自光滑代数坐标片，并以 $a_i$ 为中心。泛纤维坐标映射
在 $a_i$ 处 \textup{\'etale}，且剩余域为 $K$。由
\cite[Tag 02GU(6)]{StacksAlg}，$z_i$ 生成曲线在 $a_i$ 处
局部环的极大理想，因而为一致化参数。令
$m_i=\operatorname{ord}_{a_i}(f)$。商 $g_i=f/z_i^{m_i}$
在 $a_i$ 附近为代数单位；在管域的其余部分为解析单位，
因为 $z_i$ 在那里无零点，而 $f$ 在那里无其他零点或极点。
这两个表达式在重叠处相同，所以 $g_i$ 在整个管域上为
解析单位。因此}
\[
 |f(y)|=|g_i(a_i)|\,|z_i(y)|^{m_i},
 \qquad
 F|_{R_{a_i}}(\tau)
      =m_i\tau-\log|g_i(a_i)|.
\]
\bi{This proves both the factorization on the marked tube
and the exact order at its end. The displayed local formulas
cover $X\setminus P$ and agree at the component vertices by
continuity, proving the global factorization. Each residual
component is proper, since it is a closed component of the
projective special fibre. Thus
Corollary~\ref{cand:weightedcomponentbalance} applies at
every vertex and proves the last assertion.}
{这同时证明标记管域上的分解及其端点的精确阶数。
所列局部公式覆盖 $X\setminus P$，并由连续性在分量顶点处
相容，故得到整体分解。各剩余分量都是 projective 特殊
纤维的闭分量，所以在 $k$ 上 proper。因此每个顶点均适用
Corollary~\ref{cand:weightedcomponentbalance}，得到最后的
断言。}
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
\bi{Let $D_i=]q_i[$ and $C_i=D_i\cup\{v_i\}$. Each $C_i$
is closed in $X$ by
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure}. The space
$X\setminus P$ has the finite closed cover consisting of}
{令 $D_i=]q_i[$、$C_i=D_i\cup\{v_i\}$。由
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure}，每个 $C_i$ 在
$X$ 中闭。空间 $X\setminus P$ 具有下列有限闭覆盖：}
\[
 X\setminus\bigcup_iD_i,\qquad C_i\setminus\{a_i\}
 \quad(1\leq i\leq m).
\]
\bi{On the first set restrict the model homotopy. That set
is preserved because each unmarked smooth tube, each nodal
tube, and every vertex are preserved. On $C_i\setminus\{a_i\}$
use Proposition~\ref{cand:puncturedsmoothtube}. The
intersections consist only of fixed vertices, so finite closed
pasting, also after product with $[0,1]$, gives a jointly
continuous homotopy. Its initial map is the identity, its final
image is the stated graph with rays, and it fixes that image
pointwise. These are the strong retraction identities.}
{在第一个集合上限制模型同伦。该集合被同伦保持，因为
每个未标记光滑管域、每个节点管域与全部顶点都被保持。
在 $C_i\setminus\{a_i\}$ 上使用
Proposition~\ref{cand:puncturedsmoothtube}。各集合只在
固定顶点处相交，所以有限闭粘合（包括与 $[0,1]$ 作乘积
之后）给出联合连续同伦。其初始映射为恒等，最终像为所述
带射线图，并逐点固定此像。这正是强回缩的恒等式。}

\bi{The coordinate $\tau=-\log|z_i|$ gives the ray its
ordinary interval topology and length. As $\tau\downarrow0$
the ray tends to $v_i$; as $\tau\to\infty$ it tends to $a_i$
in $X$, by the disc seminorm formula. Adjoin each $a_i$ as the
other endpoint of its compactified ray. The resulting finite
compact gluing maps continuously and injectively into the
Hausdorff space $X$, hence is homeomorphic to its image.
Removing the $a_i$ gives precisely the asserted finite graph
with infinite rays. The local coordinate
independence in Proposition~\ref{cand:puncturedsmoothtube}
and the model homotopy's invariance prove the final assertion.}
{坐标 $\tau=-\log|z_i|$ 赋予射线通常的区间拓扑与长度。
当 $\tau\downarrow0$ 时射线趋于 $v_i$；由圆盘半范数公式，
当 $\tau\to\infty$ 时它在 $X$ 中趋于 $a_i$。将每个 $a_i$
作为相应紧化射线的另一端点添入。所得有限紧粘合连续且
单射地映入 Hausdorff 空间 $X$，故与其像同胚。再移去
各 $a_i$，恰得所述带无穷射线的有限图。结合
Proposition~\ref{cand:puncturedsmoothtube} 的
局部坐标无关性与模型同伦的不变性，得到最后的断言。}
\end{proof}
