A compact component star and its contraction

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 680

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{A compact component star and its contraction}
{紧分量星形空间及其收缩}]
\label{cand:compactcomponentstars}
\bi{Let $\mathcal X$ be a curve model, and let $S_\eta$ be the set
of closed points on the component of $\eta$ where $\mathcal X$ is
smooth of relative dimension one. Assume that $\kappa(q)/k$ is
separable for every $q\in S_\eta$. Then}
{设 $\mathcal X$ 为曲线模型，以 $S_\eta$ 记 $\eta$ 的分量上
使 $\mathcal X$ 相对维数为一且光滑的闭点集合。假设对每个
$q\in S_\eta$，$\kappa(q)/k$ 可分。则}
\[
             A_\eta=\{v_\eta\}\cup\bigcup_{q\in S_\eta}D_q
\]
\bi{is a compact closed subspace of $X$. The canonical contractions
of the completed tubes $C_q=D_q\cup\{v_\eta\}$ paste to a jointly
continuous strong deformation retraction
$H_\eta:A_\eta\times[0,1]\to A_\eta$ onto $\{v_\eta\}$.}
{是 $X$ 的紧闭子空间。完备管域
$C_q=D_q\cup\{v_\eta\}$ 的典范收缩粘合为到
$\{v_\eta\}$ 的联合连续强形变收缩
$H_\eta:A_\eta\times[0,1]\to A_\eta$。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Proposition~\ref{cand:separablesmoothtubehomotopy} identifies
the compact closure of every $D_q$ with $C_q$ and supplies its
canonical contraction. Distinct $C_q$ meet only at $v=v_\eta$,
which their homotopies fix. Thus there is a well-defined pasted
map $H_\eta$.}
{Proposition~\ref{cand:separablesmoothtubehomotopy} 将每个
$D_q$ 的紧闭包识别为 $C_q$，并给出其典范收缩。不同的
$C_q$ 只在 $v=v_\eta$ 相交，而所有同伦均固定此点，因此
粘合映射 $H_\eta$ 定义良好。}

\bi{To prove compactness, take an open cover of $A_\eta$ and a
member containing $v$. It is the intersection with an open
neighborhood of $v$ in $X$. By
Lemma~\ref{cand:componentcofinitetubes}, it contains all but
finitely many whole $D_q$. Each of the finitely many remaining
$C_q$ is compact. Finite subcovers of those $C_q$, together with
the chosen member, cover $A_\eta$. This proves compactness;
closedness follows from the Hausdorff property of $X$.}
{为证明紧性，取 $A_\eta$ 的任意开覆盖，并选取其中包含
$v$ 的成员。它是 $X$ 中 $v$ 的一个开邻域与 $A_\eta$ 的交。
由 Lemma~\ref{cand:componentcofinitetubes}，它包含除有限个
以外的全部完整 $D_q$。每个剩余的 $C_q$ 紧，故可选有限
子覆盖。把这些有限子覆盖与原选定成员合并即可覆盖
$A_\eta$。这证明紧性；闭性来自 $X$ 的 Hausdorff 性。}

\bi{Continuity away from $v$ follows because every $D_q$ is open
in $X$. For continuity at $v$, let $N$ be a relatively open
neighborhood of $v$ in $A_\eta$. There is a finite subset
$E\subset S_\eta$ such that $C_q\subset N$ for $q\notin E$.
For each $q\in E$, continuity on $C_q\times[0,1]$, together with
compactness of $[0,1]$ and the equality $H_q(v,u)=v$, gives a
relatively open neighborhood $M_q$ of $v$ in $C_q$ such that}
{远离 $v$ 的连续性来自各 $D_q$ 在 $X$ 中开。为证明 $v$
处的连续性，取 $A_\eta$ 中 $v$ 的相对开邻域 $N$。存在
有限子集 $E\subset S_\eta$，使得 $q\notin E$ 时
$C_q\subset N$。对每个 $q\in E$，由 $C_q\times[0,1]$
上的连续性、$[0,1]$ 的紧性及 $H_q(v,u)=v$，可得 $C_q$
中 $v$ 的相对开邻域 $M_q$，满足}
\[
                   H_q(M_q\times[0,1])\subset N.
\]
\bi{Indeed, finitely many product neighborhoods of the compact
set $\{v\}\times[0,1]$ suffice, and the intersection of their
first factors is such an $M_q$. The finite union
$F=\bigcup_{q\in E}(C_q\setminus M_q)$ is compact, closed in
$A_\eta$, and does not contain $v$. Hence $M=A_\eta\setminus F$
is a neighborhood of $v$ and}
{具体地，用有限个乘积邻域覆盖紧集
$\{v\}\times[0,1]$，再取其第一因子的交即可。有限并
$F=\bigcup_{q\in E}(C_q\setminus M_q)$ 紧，在 $A_\eta$ 中
闭，且不包含 $v$。因此 $M=A_\eta\setminus F$ 是 $v$ 的
邻域，并且}
\[
                    H_\eta(M\times[0,1])\subset N.
\]
\bi{This proves joint continuity uniformly in the homotopy
parameter, despite the possibly infinite set $S_\eta$. The
strong deformation retraction identities hold on every $C_q$
and at $v$, so they hold globally.}
{这证明了对同伦参数一致的联合连续性，即使 $S_\eta$ 可能
无限。强形变收缩的恒等式在每个 $C_q$ 及 $v$ 处成立，
因而全局成立。}
\end{proof}
