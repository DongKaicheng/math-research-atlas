Quantitative comparison of a finite double complex

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 186

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Quantitative comparison of a finite double complex}{有限双复形的定量比较}]\label{fnd:strictfinitecechcomparison}
\bi{Let $D^{p,q}$ be a double complex of Banach $K$-spaces in a finite rectangle $0\leq p\leq a$, $0\leq q\leq b$, with bounded commuting differentials $d_h,d_v$. Use $d=d_h+(-1)^p d_v$ on its total complex and maximum norms on finite sums. Let $L^\bullet$ be a complex of Banach $K$-spaces with bounded differentials, concentrated in degrees $0,\ldots,b$, and let $\alpha^q:L^q\to D^{0,q}$ be a bounded compatible horizontal augmentation. Assume that every augmented horizontal row}
{设 $D^{p,q}$ 是有限矩形 $0\leq p\leq a$、$0\leq q\leq b$ 中的 Banach $K$-空间双复形，有界微分 $d_h,d_v$ 交换。在总复形上使用 $d=d_h+(-1)^p d_v$，有限直和使用最大范数。设 $L^\bullet$ 为 Banach $K$-空间组成、微分有界且集中在 $0,\ldots,b$ 次的复形，并设 $\alpha^q:L^q\to D^{0,q}$ 为有界且相容的水平方向增广。假设每个增广水平行}
\[
 0\longrightarrow L^q\xrightarrow{\alpha^q}D^{0,q}
   \xrightarrow{d_h}D^{1,q}\longrightarrow\cdots
   \longrightarrow D^{a,q}\longrightarrow0
\]
\bi{is strictly exact. Then $\alpha:L^\bullet\to\operatorname{Tot}(D)$ is a quasi-isomorphism of underlying algebraic complexes. Let $P$ be a Banach $K$-space and let $\epsilon:P\to L^0$ be a bounded augmentation with $d_L\epsilon=0$, placing $P$ in degree $-1$. Its augmented complex is strictly acyclic if and only if $P\to\operatorname{Tot}(D)$ is strictly acyclic. All lifting and inverse bounds for either complex can be obtained from finitely many bounds for the rows, the differentials, $\alpha$, and the other complex. If all these maps are contractive and every horizontal row has a contracting homotopy of norm at most one, a strict acyclicity bound $Q\geq1$ for $P\to L^\bullet$ also works for $P\to\operatorname{Tot}(D)$. The corresponding assertions with the two axes interchanged hold as well.}
{均严格正合，则 $\alpha:L^\bullet\to\operatorname{Tot}(D)$ 是底层代数复形的准同构。设 $P$ 为 Banach $K$-空间，且 $\epsilon:P\to L^0$ 为满足 $d_L\epsilon=0$ 的有界增广，将 $P$ 放在 $-1$ 次。它的增广复形严格无环，当且仅当 $P\to\operatorname{Tot}(D)$ 严格无环。任一复形的提升界与逆映射界，都可由各行、微分、$\alpha$ 及另一复形的有限多个界得到。若这些映射全为 contractive，且每个水平行都有范数至多为一的收缩同伦，则 $P\to L^\bullet$ 的严格无环界 $Q\geq1$ 也适用于 $P\to\operatorname{Tot}(D)$。交换两个坐标轴后的相应断言也成立。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The algebraic assertion is the version with interchanged variables of \cite[Tag 0133, Lemma 12.25.4]{StacksAlg}, in the abelian category of $K$-vector spaces, or of the retained algebraic modules. Its finite-diagonal hypothesis holds because the rectangle is finite. We prove the additional norm statement directly. Choose $B,C\geq1$ so that $B$ bounds the differentials and $\alpha$, and every horizontal cycle of positive horizontal degree has a preimage bounded by $C$ times its norm, while $\|u\|\leq C\|\alpha u\|$. Such a common $C$ exists because there are finitely many strictly exact rows and terms. Lifts need not be linear or chosen compatibly.}
{代数结论是 \cite[Tag 0133, Lemma 12.25.4]{StacksAlg} 交换两个变量后的版本，应用于 $K$-向量空间或保留的代数模所成的阿贝尔范畴。由于矩形有限，其每条对角线有限的条件成立。下面直接证明额外的范数结论。选择 $B,C\geq1$，使 $B$ 控制微分与 $\alpha$，并使每个水平次数为正的水平上闭链都有范数至多为其范数 $C$ 倍的原像，同时 $\|u\|\leq C\|\alpha u\|$。严格正合的行与项均只有有限个，故可取这样的公共 $C$。这些提升不要求线性，也不要求彼此相容。}

\bi{Let $z\in\operatorname{Tot}^k(D)$ be a cocycle. Starting with the largest $p>0$ for which its $(p,k-p)$ component is nonzero, that component is horizontally closed: the $(p+1,k-p)$ component of $dz=0$ says so, because the component one column farther to the right is zero. Lift it horizontally to $y\in D^{p-1,k-p}$ and replace $z$ by $z-dy$. This removes that component and introduces a possible new component only one column to the left. After at most $a$ repetitions, only $z_{0,k}$ remains. It equals $\alpha u$ for a unique $u\in L^k$, and $d_Lu=0$ follows from $d_v\alpha u=0$ and injectivity of $\alpha$. Thus}
{设 $z\in\operatorname{Tot}^k(D)$ 为上闭链。从满足 $(p,k-p)$ 分量非零的最大 $p>0$ 开始，该分量在水平方向闭：$dz=0$ 的 $(p+1,k-p)$ 分量给出这一结论，因为再右一列的分量为零。将其沿水平方向提升到 $y\in D^{p-1,k-p}$，再以 $z-dy$ 替换 $z$。这消去该分量，只可能在左侧相邻列产生一个新分量。至多重复 $a$ 次后，只剩 $z_{0,k}$。它唯一地写为 $\alpha u$，其中 $u\in L^k$；由 $d_v\alpha u=0$ 及 $\alpha$ 的单射性，得 $d_Lu=0$。因此}
\[
 z=\alpha u+dy,\qquad d_Lu=0.
\]
\bi{At each elimination the current norm increases by at most $BC$. Consequently $\|u\|$ and the norm of the sum of the chosen lifts are bounded by $C(BC)^a\|z\|$. This bound is independent of $k$. If $u=d_Lv$, then $y+\alpha v$ is a primitive of $z$. In degree zero the same construction, followed by the inverse bound for the augmentation $P\to L^0$, controls the unique preimage in $P$. Injectivity of that augmentation is also preserved. This proves one implication.}
{每次消元使当前范数至多乘以 $BC$。因此 $\|u\|$ 以及所选提升之和的范数，都至多为 $C(BC)^a\|z\|$，此界与 $k$ 无关。若 $u=d_Lv$，则 $y+\alpha v$ 为 $z$ 的原像。在零次中，同一构造再结合增广 $P\to L^0$ 的逆映射界，控制其在 $P$ 中的唯一原像。增广的单射性也保持成立。这证明一个方向。}

\bi{For the converse, let $u\in L^k$ be a positive-degree cocycle and suppose $\alpha u=dw$. Apply the same elimination to $w$. Although $w$ need not be a cocycle, every component of $dw$ of positive horizontal degree is zero, which is precisely the condition used in each elimination. The result is $w-dy=\alpha v$ with $\|v\|\leq C(BC)^a\|w\|$. Therefore $\alpha u=d\alpha v=\alpha d_Lv$, and injectivity gives $u=d_Lv$. A bounded primitive for $\alpha u$ thus yields one for $u$. In degree zero, a preimage of $\alpha u$ in $P$ maps to $u$ by injectivity of $\alpha$, with its norm bounded by $B$ times the corresponding total-complex bound. These estimates also prove the converse injectivity statement. For example, multiplying the bound for the other complex by $(BC)^{a+2}$ is sufficient in either direction.}
{反向，设 $u\in L^k$ 为正次数上闭链，且 $\alpha u=dw$。对 $w$ 作相同消元。虽然 $w$ 未必闭，但 $dw$ 的每个正水平次数分量为零，这恰好是各步消元所用的条件。最终得到 $w-dy=\alpha v$，且 $\|v\|\leq C(BC)^a\|w\|$。于是 $\alpha u=d\alpha v=\alpha d_Lv$，由单射性得 $u=d_Lv$。因此 $\alpha u$ 的有界原像给出 $u$ 的有界原像。在零次中，$\alpha u$ 在 $P$ 中的原像由 $\alpha$ 的单射性知必映到 $u$，其范数可由总复形的相应界再乘以 $B$ 控制。同样的估计也给出反向的单射性。譬如，在任一方向，将另一复形的界乘以 $(BC)^{a+2}$ 就足够。}

\bi{If the row contractions and all maps have norm at most one, use the row contraction to choose each horizontal lift and its degree-zero preimage. No elimination increases the norm. Thus $\|y\|,\|u\|\leq\|z\|$, and the asserted unchanged bound follows. Interchanging the two axes changes the total-complex convention by the isometric sign map $x^{p,q}\mapsto(-1)^{pq}x^{p,q}$; hence the same argument proves the symmetric version. Finally, exactness identifies each differential image with the kernel of the next bounded map. The images are therefore closed, and the primitive bounds are exactly the needed bounds for the inverse maps from image norms to quotient norms. This is norm-strictness, without an appeal to an open mapping theorem.}
{若各行收缩及所有映射的范数均至多为一，则用行收缩选择每个水平提升及其零次原像。任何一次消元都不增加范数，故 $\|y\|,\|u\|\leq\|z\|$，从而得到所述不增大常数的结论。交换两个坐标轴时，总复形约定通过等距符号映射 $x^{p,q}\mapsto(-1)^{pq}x^{p,q}$ 相互转换，故同一论证证明对称版本。最后，正合性将每个微分的像识别为下一有界映射的核，故这些像闭；原像界正是从像范数到商范数的逆映射所需的界。这就得到 norm-strictness，无须使用开映射定理。}
\end{proof}
