Residual-degree weighted balancing

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 690

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Residual-degree weighted balancing}
{带剩余次数权重的平衡}]
\label{cand:weightedcomponentbalance}
\bi{Assume that $k$ is perfect and that the residual component
$C$ is proper over $k$. For a nonzero analytic germ $f$ at its
component point $x$, with $f(x)\ne0$, index the model branches
at $x$ by the closed points $p$ of $C^\nu$ and write
$d_pF(x)$ for the slopes of $F=-\log|f|$ constructed above.
Then only finitely many of these slopes are nonzero and}
{假设 $k$ 完美，且剩余分量 $C$ 在 $k$ 上 proper。对分量点
$x$ 处满足 $f(x)\ne0$ 的非零解析函数芽 $f$，以 $C^\nu$
的闭点 $p$ 为模型分支的指标，并将上面构造的
$F=-\log|f|$ 的斜率记为 $d_pF(x)$。这些斜率中只有
有限个非零，且}
\[
 \sum_{p\in C^\nu\ {\rm closed}}
           [\kappa(p):k]\,d_pF(x)=0.
\]
\bi{If $k$ is algebraically closed, every weight is one. For
a general perfect residue field, the weights are the degrees
over the original $k$, not over a splitting field.}
{若 $k$ 代数闭，则全部权重均为一。对一般完美剩余域，
权重是相对于原 $k$ 的次数，而不是相对于某个分裂域的次数。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The normalization is finite by
\cite[Tag 0BXR]{StacksAlg}. Hence $C^\nu$ is a proper
normal integral curve, with discrete valuation local rings at
closed points by \cite[Tag 0B8Y]{StacksAlg}. Perfection makes
all its finite residue extensions separable, so
Proposition~\ref{cand:separableresidualbranchslope} applies to
every closed point and gives}
{由 \cite[Tag 0BXR]{StacksAlg}，正规化有限。因此
$C^\nu$ 为 proper 的正规整曲线；由
\cite[Tag 0B8Y]{StacksAlg}，其闭点局部环均为离散赋值环。
完美性使所有有限剩余域扩张可分，所以每个闭点都适用
Proposition~\ref{cand:separableresidualbranchslope}，并给出}
\[
 d_pF(x)=\operatorname{ord}_{p}(\widetilde f_x).
\]
\bi{A rational function on a Noetherian integral curve is a
unit on a dense open subset, whose complement is a finite set
of closed points. This proves the finiteness assertion. The
degree-zero formula for its principal divisor is exactly
\cite[Tag 02RU]{StacksAlg}, applied over the original field
$k$ to the proper curve $C^\nu$. It gives the stated weighted
sum.}
{Noetherian 整曲线上的有理函数在某个稠密开集上为单位，
其补集由有限个闭点组成，故得到有限性。其主因子次数为零
恰是 \cite[Tag 02RU]{StacksAlg} 对原基域 $k$ 上 proper
曲线 $C^\nu$ 的结论，从而得到所述带权和。}
\end{proof}
