Almost contractive finite-dimensional projections

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 268

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Almost contractive finite-dimensional projections}{有限维近压缩投影}]
\label{fnd:finitealmostprojection}
\bi{Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional normed $K$-vector space and $W\subset V$ a subspace. For every real $c>1$ there is a $K$-linear map $P:V\to W$ with $P|_W=1_W$ and $\|P(v)\|\leq c\|v\|$ for every $v\in V$.}
{设 $V$ 为有限维赋范 $K$-向量空间，$W\subset V$ 为子空间。对每个实数 $c>1$，存在 $K$-线性映射 $P:V\to W$，满足 $P|_W=1_W$，且对每个 $v\in V$ 有 $\|P(v)\|\leq c\|v\|$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First suppose $\dim(V/W)=1$. By the finite-dimensional assertion of Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}, $W$ is complete and therefore closed in $V$. Choose $v_0\notin W$ and put $\delta=\inf_{w\in W}\|v_0-w\|>0$. There is $w_0\in W$ such that $v=v_0-w_0$ satisfies $\|v\|<c\delta$. Define $P(w+av)=w$. If $a\ne0$, homogeneity and the definition of $\delta$ give $|a|\delta\leq\|w+av\|$. The same estimate is immediate for $a=0$. Thus}
{先设 $\dim(V/W)=1$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 的有限维范数断言，$W$ 完备，所以在 $V$ 中闭。取 $v_0\notin W$，令 $\delta=\inf_{w\in W}\|v_0-w\|>0$。存在 $w_0\in W$，使 $v=v_0-w_0$ 满足 $\|v\|<c\delta$。定义 $P(w+av)=w$。当 $a\ne0$ 时，齐次性及 $\delta$ 的定义给出 $|a|\delta\leq\|w+av\|$；$a=0$ 时此估计也成立。因此}
\[
 \|P(w+av)\|
 \leq\max\{\|w+av\|,|a|\|v\|\}
 \leq c\|w+av\|.
\]
\bi{For codimension $d>1$, choose a flag $W=V_0\subset V_1\subset\cdots\subset V_d=V$ with successive codimension one. Compose the corresponding projections with bounds $c^{1/d}$. For $W=V$ use the identity; the zero-dimensional cases are immediate.}
{余维 $d>1$ 时，选择旗标 $W=V_0\subset V_1\subset\cdots\subset V_d=V$，使相邻空间的维数相差一。将界为 $c^{1/d}$ 的相应投影复合即可。$W=V$ 时取恒等映射；零维情形直接成立。}
\end{proof}
