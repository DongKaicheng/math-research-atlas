2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Analytification preserves \'etale morphisms}
{解析化保持 \'etale 态射}]
\label{fnd:analytificationetalepreservation}
\bi{An \'etale morphism of finite-type $K$-schemes
analytifies to an \'etale analytic morphism. More precisely,
near every analytic source point it restricts on ordinary
open neighbourhoods to a finite flat morphism whose
relative analytic differentials vanish.}
{有限型 $K$-概形之间的 \'etale 态射解析化后为 \'etale。
更精确地，在每个解析源点附近，它可在通常开邻域之间限制为
有限平坦态射，且相对解析微分为零。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Fix a source point $y$ over $x$. By
\cite[Tag 02GT]{StacksAlg}, choose affine Zariski
neighbourhoods containing their scheme images on which
the morphism is standard \'etale. In the notation of
\cite[Tag 00UB]{StacksAlg}, its rings have the form}
{固定 $x$ 上方的源点 $y$。由
\cite[Tag 02GT]{StacksAlg}，可在它们的概形像附近选择
仿射 Zariski 邻域，使态射为标准 \'etale。
使用 \cite[Tag 00UB]{StacksAlg} 的记号，其环具有形式}
\[
 R\longrightarrow S_g,\qquad
 S=R[T]/(P),\quad P\text{ monic},\quad P'\in S_g^\times.
\]
\bi{The scheme $\operatorname{Spec}(S_g)$ is the open
subscheme $D(g)$ of the finite free scheme
$\operatorname{Spec}(S)$ over $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$.
Analytification preserves this finite morphism and this
open immersion, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:analytificationfiniteclosed}
and Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationzariskiopens}.
Take an affinoid neighbourhood $V=\mathcal M(A)$ of $x$
in $\operatorname{Spec}(R)^{\an}$.
The inverse image in $\operatorname{Spec}(S)^{\an}$
is $\mathcal M(B)$ with
$B=A\otimes_R S=A[T]/(P)$ and its canonical finite
module norm. It is a finite free $A$-module.}
{概形 $\operatorname{Spec}(S_g)$ 是
$\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ 上的有限自由概形
$\operatorname{Spec}(S)$ 的开子概形 $D(g)$。
由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:analytificationfiniteclosed} 与
Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationzariskiopens}，
解析化保持此有限态射及开浸入。
在 $\operatorname{Spec}(R)^{\an}$ 中取 $x$ 的 affinoid
邻域 $V=\mathcal M(A)$。它在
$\operatorname{Spec}(S)^{\an}$ 中的逆像为
$\mathcal M(B)$，其中
$B=A\otimes_R S=A[T]/(P)$ 带有典范有限模范数。
它在 $A$ 上有限自由。}

\bi{Apply Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation},
requiring the neighbourhood of $y$ to be contained in
$D(g)^{\an}$. It gives ordinary open neighbourhoods
$W$ of $y$ and $U$ of $x$ for which $W\to U$ is finite.
For each affinoid $V'\subset U$, its inverse-image
algebra is a summand $eB'$, where
$B'=A'\otimes_R S$ and $A'=\mathcal O(V')$.
Since $B'$ is finite free over $A'$, this summand is
projective, and in particular flat, over $A'$.
The element $g$ is a unit in $eB'$: it vanishes at no
point of its spectrum, so the affinoid unit criterion
applies. Therefore $P'$ is also a unit in $eB'$.}
{应用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation}，
并要求 $y$ 的邻域包含于 $D(g)^{\an}$，得到通常开邻域
$y\in W$、$x\in U$，使 $W\to U$ 有限。
对每个 affinoid $V'\subset U$，其逆像代数是 $eB'$，
其中 $B'=A'\otimes_R S$、$A'=\mathcal O(V')$。
$B'$ 在 $A'$ 上有限自由，所以直和因子 $eB'$ 为射影模，
特别地在 $A'$ 上平坦。元素 $g$ 在 $eB'$ 中可逆：
它在其谱上处处非零，故可用 affinoid 单位元判据。
因此 $P'$ 在 $eB'$ 中也可逆。}

\bi{For the finite algebra $B'=A'[T]/(P)$, a bounded
$A'$-derivation into any finite Banach $B'$-module is
determined by $dT$ with the sole relation $P'dT=0$.
Conversely this relation defines a derivation on all
polynomials modulo $P$; it is bounded because the
monic remainder basis is a finite module basis.
Thus its analytic differential module is
$B'\,dT/(P'dT)$. Restriction to the open-and-closed
summand $eB'$ tensors this module with $eB'$, by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialdomains}.
It is zero because $P'$ is a unit there.
The local maps $W\to U$ are therefore finite flat
with vanishing analytic differentials, which is the
asserted \'etale local form.}
{对有限代数 $B'=A'[T]/(P)$，到任意有限 Banach $B'$-模的
有界 $A'$-导子由 $dT$ 决定，唯一关系为 $P'dT=0$。
反之，此关系在模去 $P$ 的全部多项式上定义导子；
由于首一余式基是有限模基，所得导子有界。
因此其解析微分模为 $B'\,dT/(P'dT)$。
由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialdomains}，
限制到开闭因子 $eB'$，就是将该模与 $eB'$ 张量。
$P'$ 在此因子中可逆，故微分模为零。
于是局部态射 $W\to U$ 有限平坦且解析微分为零，
正是所需的 \'etale 局部形式。}
\end{proof}
