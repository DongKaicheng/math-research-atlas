Weighted Gauss points in the bidisc boundary

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 619

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Weighted Gauss points in the bidisc boundary}
{双圆盘边界中的加权 Gauss 点}]
\label{fnd:bidiscboundaryinternal}
\bi{For $X=\mathcal M(K\langle T,S\rangle)$, the Shilov
boundary is $\{\zeta_{1,1}\}$. For every real $0<\rho<1$,
the weighted Gauss point $\zeta_{1,\rho}$ lies in the
absolute analytic boundary of $X$.}
{对 $X=\mathcal M(K\langle T,S\rangle)$，Shilov 边界为
$\{\zeta_{1,1}\}$。对每个实数 $0<\rho<1$，加权 Gauss 点
$\zeta_{1,\rho}$ 都属于 $X$ 的绝对解析边界。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The unit Gauss norm is multiplicative and is the spectral
norm, so its point alone is a boundary. The minimality
assertion of Lemma~\ref{fnd:minimalfiniteboundary} gives the
Shilov statement. Reduction of the coefficient series gives
$\widetilde A=\widetilde K[t,s]$: only finitely many
coefficients of a restricted series can have norm one, and
the others disappear modulo $A^{\circ\circ}$.
At $\zeta_{1,\rho}$ the reduction kernel is $(s)$.
Indeed, a power-bounded series has value one there exactly
when its part of $S$-degree zero has a coefficient of norm
one. All positive $S$-degrees have norm at most $\rho<1$.
The prime $(s)$ is not maximal, since its quotient is
$\widetilde K[t]$. Apply
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictabsoluteinteriorreduction}.}
{单位 Gauss 范数可乘且等于谱范数，所以对应的一个点就构成
边界。Lemma~\ref{fnd:minimalfiniteboundary} 的极小性断言给出
Shilov 边界的结论。对系数级数取约化得到
$\widetilde A=\widetilde K[t,s]$：受限级数仅有有限个系数
的范数可以为一，其余系数在模 $A^{\circ\circ}$ 后消失。
在 $\zeta_{1,\rho}$ 处约化核为 $(s)$。事实上，幂有界级数
在该点取值一，当且仅当其 $S$-次数为零的部分有一个范数为一
的系数；所有正 $S$-次数项的范数都不超过 $\rho<1$。
素理想 $(s)$ 不是极大理想，因为其商为
$\widetilde K[t]$。应用
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictabsoluteinteriorreduction} 即可。}
\end{proof}
