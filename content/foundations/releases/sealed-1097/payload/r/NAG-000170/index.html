Result 2.6.1

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 130

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}\label{fnd:fibreproduct}
\bi{Fibre products exist in the analytic category. For affinoid morphisms $A\to B,A\to C$, and for a complete extension $L/K$,}
{Analytic category 中存在 fibre products。对 affinoid morphisms $A\to B,A\to C$ 及 complete extension $L/K$，}
\[
\mathcal M(B)\times_{\mathcal M(A)}\mathcal M(C)
 =\mathcal M(B\widehat\otimes_A C),\qquad
\mathcal M(A)_L=\mathcal M(A\widehat\otimes_K L).
\]
\bi{If $f:Y\to X$ and $x\in X$, its analytic fibre is the $\mathcal H(x)$-analytic space $Y_x=Y\times_X\mathcal M(\mathcal H(x))$. For affinoid $X,Y$, its algebra is $B\widehat\otimes_A\mathcal H(x)$. Its underlying topological space identifies with $f^{-1}(x)$.}
{若 $f:Y\to X$、$x\in X$，则其 analytic fibre 为 $\mathcal H(x)$-analytic space $Y_x=Y\times_X\mathcal M(\mathcal H(x))$。当 $X,Y$ affinoid 时，其 algebra 为 $B\widehat\otimes_A\mathcal H(x)$。一般地，其 underlying topological space 与 $f^{-1}(x)$ 识别。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout} constructs the affinoid completed tensor product and proves its bounded universal property. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticproducts} glues these products on arbitrary analytic atlases. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension} supplies complete scalar extension. The actual fibre identification, including its topology and its field of definition, is Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres} in affinoid charts and Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticfibres} after gluing.}
{Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout} 构造 affinoid 完备张量积并证明其有界泛性质。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticproducts} 在任意解析 atlas 上粘合这些乘积，Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension} 给出完备扩标量。实际纤维的识别，包括其拓扑与定义域，在 affinoid chart 上由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres} 给出，再由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticfibres} 粘合。}
\end{proof}
