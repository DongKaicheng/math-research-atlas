Tate acyclicity for finite strict affinoid coverings

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 254

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Tate acyclicity for finite strict affinoid coverings}{有限 strict affinoid 覆盖的 Tate 无环性}]\label{fnd:strictaffinoidcovercech}
\bi{Let $K$ be complete, with trivial valuation allowed, let $A$ be a strict $K$-affinoid algebra, and let $M$ be a finite $A$-module. Let $(V_i)_{i\in I}$ be a finite covering of $X=\mathcal M(A)$ by strict-test affinoid domains. Give each intersection algebra $A_{V_{i_0}\cap\cdots\cap V_{i_q}}$ a norm-admissible Banach norm, and its finite modules their canonical norms. Then the augmented complex}
{设 $K$ 完备，允许平凡赋值，$A$ 为 strict $K$-affinoid 代数，$M$ 为有限 $A$-模。设 $(V_i)_{i\in I}$ 是 $X=\mathcal M(A)$ 的一个有限 strict-test affinoid 域覆盖。给每个交叠代数 $A_{V_{i_0}\cap\cdots\cap V_{i_q}}$ 配备 norm-admissible Banach 范数，并给其有限模配备 canonical 范数。则增广复形}
\[
 0\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow
 \prod_i A_{V_i}\otimes_A M\longrightarrow
 \prod_{i_0<i_1} A_{V_{i_0}\cap V_{i_1}}\otimes_A M
 \longrightarrow\cdots
\]
\bi{is norm-strict exact in every degree. Equivalently the all-tuples version is norm-strict exact. Every ordinary finite-module tensor in these complexes agrees, by a bounded isomorphism, with its completed tensor. No reducedness, separability or residue-characteristic hypothesis is needed.}
{在每个次数均为 norm-strict 正合。等价地，全部指标版本也为 norm-strict 正合。复形中每个有限模的普通张量积，通过有界同构与其完备张量积相同。不要求约化性、可分性或关于剩余特征的假设。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose for each $V_i$ the finite covering by $A$-rational domains supplied by Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis}, and let $\mathcal W$ be their finite union as a covering family of $X$. It refines $(V_i)$ with a specified containing index for each member. Its augmented complex is norm-strict exact by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfiniterationalcech}.}
{对每个 $V_i$，由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis} 选取有限个 $A$-rational 域覆盖；将这些覆盖族合在一起，得到 $X$ 的有限覆盖族 $\mathcal W$。它细化 $(V_i)$，且每个成员都有一个指定的包含指标。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfiniterationalcech}，它的增广复形 norm-strict 正合。}

\bi{Fix a nonempty tuple of coarse indices, and put $Y=V_{i_0}\cap\cdots\cap V_{i_p}$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttestdomainintersections} gives its strict algebra $A_Y$ and all compatible intersection maps. Each member of $\mathcal W$ restricts to a rational quotient of $A_Y$: take its original unit-ideal tuple in $A$, map it to $A_Y$, and use the rational point theorem. These quotients cover $\mathcal M(A_Y)$. The module $A_Y\otimes_A M$ is finite over $A_Y$. Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfiniterationalcech} over this algebra. Associativity of ordinary tensors, \cite[Tag 00D2, Lemma 10.12.7]{StacksAlg}, and Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitetensor} identify its terms, restrictions and augmentation with the mixed intersection diagram. Thus the required local complexes are norm-strict exact on every coarse intersection. Empty intersections have actual zero algebras by the proposition, so their terms are zero for a proved algebraic reason.}
{固定一个含至少一个指标的 coarse 多重指标，并令 $Y=V_{i_0}\cap\cdots\cap V_{i_p}$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttestdomainintersections}，得到其 strict 代数 $A_Y$ 及所有相容的交叠映射。$\mathcal W$ 的每个成员限制为 $A_Y$ 的 rational 商：将它原来在 $A$ 上的单位理想 tuple 映入 $A_Y$，再应用 rational 点定理即可。这些商覆盖 $\mathcal M(A_Y)$。模 $A_Y\otimes_A M$ 在 $A_Y$ 上有限。对该代数应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfiniterationalcech}。由普通张量积结合律，即 \cite[Tag 00D2, Lemma 10.12.7]{StacksAlg}，以及 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitetensor}，其各项、限制和增广都与混合交叠图表一致。因此，在每个 coarse 交叠上，所需局部复形均为 norm-strict 正合。由上述 proposition，空交叠具有实际的零代数，故其项为零有已证明的代数依据。}

\bi{All hypotheses of Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictacyclicrefinement}(1) now hold. It transfers norm-strict exactness from $\mathcal W$ to $(V_i)$, in every degree and with the given augmentation. The same proposition supplies the all-tuples comparison. The finite-module completion statement is Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitetensor}. The lifting bounds may depend on the finite cover, the module and the chosen equivalent norms; no bound uniform over all covers is asserted. If $X$ is empty, $A=0$ and $M=0$, and the conclusion also covers its empty covering.}
{现在 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictacyclicrefinement}(1) 的全部假设均已满足。它将 $\mathcal W$ 的 norm-strict 正合性传到 $(V_i)$，适用于每个次数并保持所给增广。同一 proposition 给出全部指标复形的比较。有限模完备化的断言来自 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitetensor}。提升界可以依赖于有限覆盖、模及所选等价范数，并未断言对所有覆盖有一致的界。若 $X$ 为空，则 $A=0$ 且 $M=0$，结论也包括它的空覆盖。}
\end{proof}
