Residual dimension of a Gauss field

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 602

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Residual dimension of a Gauss field}
{Gauss 域的剩余维数}]
\label{fnd:gaussvaluedfielddimension}
\bi{Let $F$ be any complete valued field and let $E$ be the
completion of $F(T_1,\ldots,T_m)$ with its weighted Gauss absolute
value, for arbitrary positive radii. Then $d(E/F)=m$.}
{设 $F$ 为任意完备赋值域，$E$ 为有理函数域
$F(T_1,\ldots,T_m)$ 关于任意正半径的加权 Gauss 绝对值的完备化，
则 $d(E/F)=m$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Consider one variable $T$, with $w=v(T)$. Its Gauss fraction
field has value group $v(F^\times)+\mathbf Z w$, since the value
of a polynomial is the minimum of its term values and quotients
subtract these values. If no positive multiple of $w$ lies in
$v(F^\times)$, every nonzero polynomial has a unique term of
largest size. In a rational function of size one the quotient of
the two leading terms has exponent zero; it differs from a scalar
of norm one by an element of norm less than one. The residue field
is therefore $\widetilde F$, and the value-group quotient has
rational rank one.}
{先考虑一个变量 $T$，记 $w=v(T)$。Gauss 分式域的值群为
$v(F^\times)+\mathbf Z w$，因为多项式的值为各项值的最小值，
而取商使这些值相减。若 $w$ 的任何正整数倍均不属于
$v(F^\times)$，则每个非零多项式都有唯一最大绝对值项。
对绝对值一的有理函数，其分子、分母的首项之商的指数为零；
它与一个范数一的标量之差具有小于一的范数。
因此剩余域为 $\widetilde F$，值群之商的有理秩为一。}

\bi{Otherwise choose the least $q>0$ with $qw=v(c)$ for some
$c\in F^\times$, and put $U=T^q/c$. Its residue is transcendental
over $\widetilde F$: a nonzero polynomial in $U$ with coefficients
of size at most one and at least one unit coefficient has Gauss
norm one. Every residue of a rational function of norm one belongs
to $\widetilde F(\widetilde U)$. To see this, divide its numerator
and denominator by a maximal term of the denominator. The terms
that survive reduction have exponent differences divisible by
$q$, by its minimality, and become scalar multiples of Laurent
monomials in $\widetilde U$. The reduced denominator is nonzero
by the just-proved transcendence. Thus the residue field is
$\widetilde F(\widetilde U)$, and the value-group quotient is
torsion. In both cases $d=1$. Completion changes neither
invariant.}
{否则，取最小的 $q>0$，使某个 $c\in F^\times$ 满足
$qw=v(c)$，并令 $U=T^q/c$。其剩余类在 $\widetilde F$ 上
超越：系数绝对值至多为一且至少有一个单位系数的非零
$U$-多项式，其 Gauss 范数为一。范数一的任意有理函数的剩余类
都属于 $\widetilde F(\widetilde U)$。为证明这一点，将分子、
分母同除以分母中的一个最大项。由 $q$ 的最小性，约化后留下的
各项，其指数差均被 $q$ 整除，因而变成 $\widetilde U$ 的
Laurent 单项式乘以剩余域标量。由刚证的超越性，分母的约化
非零。因此剩余域就是 $\widetilde F(\widetilde U)$，而值群
之商为挠群。两种情形均有 $d=1$；完备化不改变这些不变量。}

\bi{Adjoin the variables one at a time. The resulting completed
field agrees with the completion for the simultaneous weighted
Gauss norm. Indeed the norms agree on rational functions over
$F$, and a rational function with finitely many coefficients in
an intermediate completed field is approximated by replacing
those coefficients by elements of its dense rational subfield.
A sufficiently close denominator has the same positive norm,
so its inverse also converges. This proves density at each step.
The tower formula now gives $d(E/F)=m$, including $m=0$.}
{逐一添入变量。所得完备域与同时加权 Gauss 范数的完备化相同：
在 $F$ 上的有理函数中范数相同；中间完备域上一个只含有限个
系数的有理函数，可以用稠密有理函数子域中的系数逼近。
足够接近的分母具有相同的正范数，所以其逆也收敛。
这在每一步给出稠密性。塔式等式于是给出 $d(E/F)=m$，
也包括 $m=0$。}
\end{proof}
