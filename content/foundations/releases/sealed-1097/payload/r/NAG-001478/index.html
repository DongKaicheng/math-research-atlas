Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Rational structure sheaves and finite modules}{Rational 结构层与有限模}]\label{fnd:fixedrationalmodulesheaf}
\bi{The covering families just defined form a pretopology. The assignments $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}}$ and $\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$ are respectively a sheaf of rings and a sheaf of modules on the resulting site, independent up to canonical isomorphism of the presentation choices. One has}
{上述覆盖族构成预拓扑。$\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}}$ 与 $\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$ 分别是所得 site 上的环层与模层，并且在典范同构意义下与表示的选择无关。有}
\[
 \Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}})=A,
 \qquad \Gamma(X,\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}})=M.
\]
\bi{The functor $M\mapsto\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$ from finite $A$-modules to $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}}$-modules is exact and fully faithful. Each finite covering in this site has a norm-strict exact augmented \v Cech complex for $\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$, with canonical finite-module norms.}
{从有限 $A$-模到 $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}}$-模的函子 $M\mapsto\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$ 正合且全忠实。对该 site 中每个有限覆盖，$\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$ 的增广 \v Cech 复形均为 norm-strict 正合，使用 canonical 有限模范数。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation} represents $V\cap W$ by a rational algebra over $A$. It is the categorical fiber product in this partially ordered category. Identity coverings, pullbacks of finite set-theoretic coverings, and finite composites of such coverings satisfy the pretopology axioms, since intersection commutes with finite union. This uses only objects rational over the fixed $A$. It does not require a theorem saying that every rational domain over every intermediate algebra has a rational presentation over $A$.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation}，$V\cap W$ 可用 $A$ 上的一个 rational 代数表示；它正是此偏序范畴中的纤维积。恒等覆盖、有限集合覆盖的拉回以及这类覆盖的有限复合满足预拓扑公理，因为交与有限并相容。这里只使用在固定 $A$ 上为 rational 的对象，不需要证明任意中间代数上的每个 rational 域都能在 $A$ 上作 rational 表示。}

\bi{Uniqueness in Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaldomainup} proves that restriction maps compose, that identity restrictions are identities, and that two choices of a presentation give compatible bounded inverse isomorphisms. If $V_i\subseteq V$, the joint algebra $A_V\widehat\otimes_A A_{V_i}$ is canonically $A_{V_i}$ by the same theorem. Viewed over $A_V$, its defining tuple is the image of a unit-ideal tuple from $A$, hence still generates one. Thus any finite covering $(V_i)$ of $V$ becomes a finite rational covering of $\mathcal M(A_V)$ in the scope of Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfiniterationalcech}. Apply that theorem to the finite $A_V$-module $A_V\otimes_A M$. The ordinary tensor associativity of \cite[Tag 00D2, Lemma 10.12.7]{StacksAlg}, together with Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule}, identifies all its terms and arrows with those in the asserted \v Cech complex.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaldomainup} 中的唯一性，限制映射满足复合律，恒等限制为恒等映射，并且不同表示之间给出相容的有界互逆同构。若 $V_i\subseteq V$，同一定理给出典范识别 $A_V\widehat\otimes_A A_{V_i}\simeq A_{V_i}$。在 $A_V$ 上看，其定义 tuple 是 $A$ 中某个单位理想 tuple 的像，因而仍生成单位理想。因此，$V$ 的每个有限覆盖 $(V_i)$ 都成为 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfiniterationalcech} 范围内的 $\mathcal M(A_V)$ 的有限 rational 覆盖。将该定理应用于有限 $A_V$-模 $A_V\otimes_A M$。由 \cite[Tag 00D2, Lemma 10.12.7]{StacksAlg} 的普通张量积结合律及 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule}，其全部项和箭头与所述 \v Cech 复形相同。}

\bi{Degree-zero exactness is precisely the equalizer condition for a sheaf. The value on the empty object is zero, as required by its empty covering. Taking $M=A$ gives the ring sheaf, and multiplication on the local tensor modules gives the module sheaves. Their global sections have the displayed values by the chosen presentation of $X$.}
{零次正合性恰为层的 equalizer 条件。空对象上的值为零，符合其空覆盖的要求。取 $M=A$ 得到环层，而局部张量模上的乘法给出相应模层。由 $X$ 所选的表示，整体截面正是所列的值。}

\bi{For a short exact sequence of finite $A$-modules, rational flatness from Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalfiniteelementary} proves exactness on every object when the valuation is nontrivial. If the valuation is trivial, equip $A$ with a defining quotient norm; all nonzero elements have norm one. In the proof of that lemma the unit certificate then has coefficient norms at most one, so one may take $a=1$. Its same elementary chain proves rational flatness in this case as well, using Corollary~\ref{fnd:finiteelementaryflatness}, which allows trivial valuation. Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitetensor} gives the corresponding normed finite-module identifications. Objectwise exactness implies exactness of the sequence of sheaves.}
{对有限 $A$-模的短正合列，当赋值非平凡时，Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalfiniteelementary} 的 rational 平坦性给出它在每个对象上的正合性。若赋值平凡，则给 $A$ 配备一个定义商范数，此时每个非零元素的范数都是一。在该引理的证明中，单位理想证书的系数范数均至多为一，故可取 $a=1$。由允许平凡赋值的 Corollary~\ref{fnd:finiteelementaryflatness}，同一个 elementary 链论证也在此情形给出 rational 平坦性。Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitetensor} 给出相应的赋范有限模识别。逐对象的正合性推出层列的正合性。}

\bi{An $A$-linear map $u:M\to N$ produces the sheaf map $1\otimes u$. Conversely, let $\psi:\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}\to\widetilde N^{\mathrm{rat}}$ be a morphism of $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}}$-modules. Its map on $X$ is an $A$-linear $u:M\to N$. For every $V$, compatibility with restriction gives $\psi_V(1\otimes m)=1\otimes u(m)$. Since every element of $A_V\otimes_A M$ is a finite sum of elements $b\otimes m$, $A_V$-linearity forces $\psi_V=1\otimes u$. Thus the map on Hom groups is a bijection. The norm-strict \v Cech assertion was obtained from the same application of the theorem.}
{$A$-线性映射 $u:M\to N$ 给出层映射 $1\otimes u$。反之，设 $\psi:\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}\to\widetilde N^{\mathrm{rat}}$ 为 $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}}$-模同态。它在 $X$ 上给出一个 $A$-线性映射 $u:M\to N$。对每个 $V$，由与限制映射的相容性，$\psi_V(1\otimes m)=1\otimes u(m)$。由于 $A_V\otimes_A M$ 中的每个元素都是有限个 $b\otimes m$ 之和，$A_V$-线性迫使 $\psi_V=1\otimes u$。因此所得 Hom 群映射为双射。Norm-strict \v Cech 断言已经由上面对同一定理的应用得到。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{The acyclicity theorem already gives a useful sheaf construction. We specify the site so that no theorem about arbitrary affinoid domains is concealed in its definition. Let $A$ be a strict $K$-affinoid algebra over a complete field with its fixed non-Archimedean absolute value, allowing trivial valuation, and put $X=\mathcal M(A)$.}
{刚证明的无环性定理已经可以给出结构层。下面明确指定所用的 site，从而不在定义中隐藏关于任意 affinoid 域的定理。设 $A$ 是完备域 $K$ 上的 strict affinoid 代数，固定其非阿基米德绝对值，允许赋值平凡，并令 $X=\mathcal M(A)$。}

\begin{definition}[\bi{The fixed rational pretopology}{固定 rational 预拓扑}]\label{fnd:fixedrationalpretopology}
\bi{Let $\mathcal R_X$ be the partially ordered category of subsets of $X$ admitting a rational presentation over the original algebra $A$ with radii in $|K^\times|$. It contains $X$ and the empty set. Arrows are inclusions. A covering family of $V\in\mathcal R_X$ is a finite family $(V_i\subseteq V)$ of objects of $\mathcal R_X$ whose union is $V$. The empty family covers the empty set. Choose one rational presentation algebra $A_V$ for each $V$, with $A_X=A$ and $A_\varnothing=0$, and use the canonical bounded restriction maps of Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaldomainup}. For a finite $A$-module $M$, write}
{令 $\mathcal R_X$ 为如下子集构成的偏序范畴：它们在原始代数 $A$ 上具有半径属于 $|K^\times|$ 的 rational 表示。该范畴包含 $X$ 与空集，态射为包含映射。对象 $V\in\mathcal R_X$ 的一个覆盖是由 $\mathcal R_X$ 中对象组成的有限族 $(V_i\subseteq V)$，且其并等于 $V$。空族覆盖空集。对每个 $V$ 选择一个 rational 表示代数 $A_V$，并取 $A_X=A$、$A_\varnothing=0$；限制映射采用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaldomainup} 给出的典范有界映射。对有限 $A$-模 $M$，记}
\[
 \mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}}(V)=A_V,
 \qquad \widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}(V)=A_V\otimes_A M.
\]
\end{definition}
