Generic regularity of arbitrary analytic germs

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 349

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Generic regularity of arbitrary analytic germs}{任意解析芽的泛正则性}]
\label{fnd:generalizedgenericgermregularity}
\bi{Let $A$ be an integral generalized affinoid algebra over any complete field $K$. There is $0\ne a\in A$ such that, for every $x\in X=\mathcal M(A)$, the rings $\mathcal O_{X,x}[1/a]$ and $\widehat{\mathcal O_{X,x}}[1/a]$ are regular.}
{设 $A$ 为任意完备域 $K$ 上的整 generalized affinoid 代数。则存在 $0\ne a\in A$，使对每个 $x\in X=\mathcal M(A)$，环 $\mathcal O_{X,x}[1/a]$ 与 $\widehat{\mathcal O_{X,x}}[1/a]$ 均正则。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose $a$ by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticseparableregularopen}, and choose a nontrivial free strictification $K_r$ of $A$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:gaussanalyticseparable} shows that this scalar extension is analytically separable, so $B=A_r$ satisfies $B[1/a]$ regular. For $z\in\mathcal M(B)$ above $x$, put $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ and $S=\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(B),z}$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictordinaryregularcomparison} makes $B_{\ker(z)}\to S$ and $S\to\widehat S$ regular. At a prime of $S$ avoiding $a$, its contracted localization in $B$ is regular and its closed fibre is regular; Tag 031E therefore proves regularity of that localization of $S$. The same argument for $S\to\widehat S$ proves that both $S[1/a]$ and $\widehat S[1/a]$ are regular.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticseparableregularopen} 选择 $a$，并为 $A$ 选择赋值非平凡的自由 strictification $K_r$。Lemma~\ref{fnd:gaussanalyticseparable} 说明此标量扩张解析可分，因此 $B=A_r$ 满足 $B[1/a]$ 正则。对 $x$ 上方的 $z\in\mathcal M(B)$，令 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$S=\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(B),z}$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictordinaryregularcomparison} 说明 $B_{\ker(z)}\to S$ 及 $S\to\widehat S$ 正则。在 $S$ 中避开 $a$ 的素理想处，其在 $B$ 中收缩的局部化正则，闭纤维也正则，故 Tag 031E 给出 $S$ 在该处正则。对 $S\to\widehat S$ 作同样论证，得到 $S[1/a]$ 与 $\widehat S[1/a]$ 都正则。}

\bi{The map $R\to S$ is faithfully flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}, and its completed map is faithfully flat by Tag 0C4G. These properties persist after inverting $a$. Every prime of either source has a prime above it in the target by Tag 00HQ; the corresponding localized flat local map has regular target. Tag 00OF descends regularity in each case. Thus both $R[1/a]$ and $\widehat R[1/a]$ are regular, including at algebraic primes of these rings that arise when $a(x)=0$.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}，$R\to S$ 忠实平坦；由 Tag 0C4G，其完成映射也忠实平坦。在可逆化 $a$ 后，这些性质仍保持。由 Tag 00HQ，各源环的每个素理想在目标中都有上方素理想；相应局部化给出目标正则的平坦局部映射。Tag 00OF 在两种情形中均下降正则性。因此 $R[1/a]$ 与 $\widehat R[1/a]$ 都正则，包括当 $a(x)=0$ 时仍出现在这些局部化环中的代数素理想。}
\end{proof}
