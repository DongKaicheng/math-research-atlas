Finite projection for smooth moment forms

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1052

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Finite projection for smooth moment forms]
\label{v037:forms:finiteprojection}
Let $p:Y\to X$ be a norm-admissible finite morphism of
good Hausdorff analytic spaces pure of the same analytic
dimension $d$, over the specified complete field.
Let $\mathcal F$ be coherent on $Y_G$.
For every $\alpha\in\mathcal A_{X,c}^{d,d}(X)$,
the form $p^*\alpha$ has compact support and
\[
       \int_{Y,\mathcal F}p^*\alpha
                  =\int_{X,p_*\mathcal F}\alpha.
\]
These identities are compatible with composition of
finite morphisms. If $p_*\mathcal F$ is locally free of
constant rank $\rho$ on the analytic G-site over an
ordinary open neighbourhood of $\operatorname{supp}(\alpha)$,
then
\[
       \int_{Y,\mathcal F}p^*\alpha
                  =\rho\int_{X,\mathcal O_X}\alpha.
\]
The absolute value is unchanged, and the assertions
allow trivial valuation and nonreduced spaces.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Put $S=\operatorname{supp}(\alpha)$.
The ordinary smooth-germ pullback is defined by
Theorem~\ref{v037:forms:germsheaf}.
If $y\notin p^{-1}(S)$, a representative of $\alpha$
vanishes on an ordinary neighbourhood of $p(y)$.
Its pullback vanishes near $y$, by continuity of $p$
and the definition of pullback on representatives.
Thus
\[
                \operatorname{supp}(p^*\alpha)
                                      \subseteq p^{-1}(S).
\]

Choose finitely many affinoid domains $V_i\subseteq X$
whose ordinary interiors cover $S$, using the affinoid
neighbourhood basis in Theorem~\ref{v037:forms:germsheaf}.
Put $Q=\bigcup_iV_i$. Finiteness of $p$ makes
$W_i=p^{-1}(V_i)$ actual affinoid domains, and
$R=\bigcup_iW_i=p^{-1}(Q)$ is compact by
Theorem~\ref{v037:integration:compactfinitepushforward}.
The set $p^{-1}(S)$ is closed in $R$: $S$ is compact
and therefore closed in the Hausdorff space $X$.
It is consequently compact. The support of the
pullback is closed and contained in this compact set,
which proves the first assertion.

For every $i$, the open set
$p^{-1}(\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(V_i,X))$
is contained in $W_i$, and therefore in
$\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(W_i,Y)$.
These interiors cover $p^{-1}(S)$ and hence the
support of the pullback. Thus $Q$ and $R$ are valid
compact neighbourhoods for the two integrals in
Theorem~\ref{v037:forms:compactintegral}; neither
finite union needs to be good.

The coherent direct image $p_*\mathcal F$ exists by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:finitecoherentdirectimagebasechange}.
The finite chart data induced on $R$ by the ordinary
section $p^*\alpha$ are the pullback of the data
induced by $\alpha$ on $Q$.
To check this, take the finite presenting affinoid
cover supplied by Theorem~\ref{v037:forms:germsheaf}.
On its full inverse images both constructions use
the same pulled-back analytic units and the same
ambient superform. Their relations agree on every
overlap by that theorem's restriction and pullback
compatibility. If further affinoid restriction is
needed, both sets of data are compared on that
same finite common refinement.

Theorem~\ref{v037:integration:compactfinitepushforward}
now gives
\[
 \mathcal I_{R,\mathcal F|_R}(p^*\alpha)
                  =\mathcal I_{Q,(p_*\mathcal F)|_Q}(\alpha).
\]
By Theorem~\ref{v037:forms:compactintegral}, these
are precisely the two ordinary compact-support
integrals in the statement.

For composable finite maps, functoriality of ordinary
pullback is Theorem~\ref{v037:forms:germsheaf}.
Direct images compose on sections over full inverse
images, as in
Theorem~\ref{v037:integration:compactfinitepushforward}.
Applying the proved identity twice therefore gives
the identity for the composite.

Finally, under the local rank hypothesis choose
all $V_i$ inside the indicated ordinary open
neighbourhood of $S$. The scalar-rank assertion of
Theorem~\ref{v037:integration:compactfinitepushforward}
applies on $Q$. It multiplies the structure-module
integral by $\rho$, with the generic Artinian lengths
retained. Independence of compact neighbourhoods in
Theorem~\ref{v037:forms:compactintegral} identifies
that structure-module integral with the one on $X$.
\end{proof}
