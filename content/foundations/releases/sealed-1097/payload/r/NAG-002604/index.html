An invariant midpoint over a dense value group

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1904

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[An invariant midpoint over a dense value group]
\label{cand:f5sub:invariantmidpoint}
There is a complete nontrivially real-valued field \(K\), with
algebraically closed residue field of characteristic zero and
dense value group \(\Gamma=\mathbb Z[1/3]\), and a split projective
semistable model \(\mathcal X\) of \(\mathbb P^1_K\), for which the
following holds. Its geometric skeleton is an interval of length
\(1\). Adding its midpoint gives a semistable geometric vertex set
fixed by every isometric \(K\)-automorphism of
\(\Omega=\widehat{\overline K}\), but no semistable original-\(O\)
model has that geometric vertex set. The midpoint is realized
after the ramified quadratic extension \(K(\sqrt t)/K\).
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Fix an algebraically closed field \(k\) of characteristic zero.
Take compatible Laurent-series fields
\[
 F_n=k((t^{1/3^n})),\qquad
 F_n\hookrightarrow F_{n+1},\qquad v(t^{1/3^n})=3^{-n},
\]
and let \(K\) be the completion of \(F=\bigcup_{n\geq0}F_n\).
For nonzero \(a\in K\), choose \(f\in F\) with
\(|a-f|<|a|\); then \(|a|=|f|\).
Thus completion introduces no new nonzero values and
\(v(K^\times)=\mathbb Z[1/3]\), which is dense in \(\mathbb R\).
For \(a\in K^\circ\), approximation with error less than \(1\)
identifies its residue with a residue from some \(F_n^\circ\).
Hence the residue field is \(k\).

On \(\mathbb P^1_O\), with affine coordinate \(T\), blow up
the ideal \((T,t)\), extended as the unit ideal outside its support.
The two affine charts over the \(T\)-line are
\[
       \operatorname{Spec}O[T,u]/(Tu-t),
       \qquad \operatorname{Spec}O[z],\quad T=tz.
\]
Their standard monomial bases make these rings free over \(O\),
hence flat; the displayed presentations are finite.
The first is a nodal chart and the second is smooth.
Together with the unchanged infinity chart they give a
projective semistable model \(\mathcal X\), by \stacks{02NS} and
\stacks{0C4P}, with special fibre two
copies of \(\mathbb P^1_k\) meeting at one split node.
Its geometric vertices have \(v(T)=0,1\), and the node tube is
\(|t|<|T|<1\), of length \(1\).

Choose \(b\in\Omega\) with \(b^2=t\), and put \(L=K(b)\).
Since \(1/2\notin\Gamma\), \(b\notin K\) and \([L:K]=2\).
The two terms in \(a+cb\), for \(a,c\in K\), have distinct
valuations when nonzero, since \(\Gamma\cap(\Gamma+1/2)=\varnothing\).
Thus \(|a+cb|=\max(|a|,|c||b|)\); completeness follows coefficientwise
from completeness of \(K\). Since \(v(b)=1/2\), the extension is ramified.
Lemma~\ref{cand:f5sub:originalsymmetric}, using the accepted charts
\dep{cand:symmetricnodeblowup}, at \(\delta=1/2\) on
\(\mathcal X_{L^\circ}\) inserts exactly the midpoint.
Consequently its base change to \(\Omega^\circ\) realizes the
three-element semistable geometric vertex set
\[
        \{\zeta(1),\ \zeta(|t|^{1/2}),\ \zeta(|t|)\}.
\]
Each of these Gauss seminorms is fixed by every isometric
\(K\)-automorphism of \(\Omega\): its radius is fixed and its
formula on \(\Omega[T]\) uses only the absolute values of the
coefficients.

If an original semistable model realized this set, then
\dep{v036:f5:vertexarithmetic} would make the image \(w\) of the
midpoint a component vertex satisfying
\(v(\mathcal H(w)^\times)=\Gamma\).
But \(T\) is a nonzero element of \(\mathcal H(w)\) with
\(v(T(w))=1/2\), a contradiction.
Thus invariance, even with already split branches and residue
field, is insufficient. The use of \(L^\circ\) above is a
ramified construction, not an etale descent step.
\end{proof}
