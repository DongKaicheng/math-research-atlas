Rational restriction preserves completed residue fields

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 275

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Rational restriction preserves completed residue fields}{Rational 限制保持完备剩余域}]
\label{fnd:rationalresiduefields}
\bi{Let $U\subset\mathcal M(A)$ be a generalized rational domain, represented by $A\to A_U$. At every $x\in U$, the induced isometric embedding}
{设 $U\subset\mathcal M(A)$ 为由 $A\to A_U$ 表示的广义 rational 域。对每个 $x\in U$，诱导的等距嵌入}
\[
 \mathcal H_A(x)\longrightarrow\mathcal H_{A_U}(x)
\]
\bi{is an isomorphism.}
{为同构。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Write $A_U=A\{\rho^{-1}T\}/(gT_i-f_i)$ with $(g,f_1,\ldots,f_s)=A$. At $x\in U$ the denominator is nonzero, and $|f_i(x)/g(x)|\leq\rho_i$. Evaluation at these ratios gives a bounded map $A_U\to\mathcal H_A(x)$ by Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundedsubstitution} and quotient infima. Its seminorm is the unique rational point over $x$ by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints}. Proposition~\ref{new:residueuniversal} gives an isometric map $\mathcal H_{A_U}(x)\to\mathcal H_A(x)$. Its composite with the map in the statement fixes the dense field $\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$ and is therefore the identity. The reverse composite fixes the image of $A_U$: this follows first on the coefficients and the forced ratios $f_i/g$, then on their convergent power series. It consequently fixes the dense fraction field of the evaluated $A_U$, hence is the identity.}
{写成 $A_U=A\{\rho^{-1}T\}/(gT_i-f_i)$，其中 $(g,f_1,\ldots,f_s)=A$。在 $x\in U$ 处，分母非零，且 $|f_i(x)/g(x)|\leq\rho_i$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundedsubstitution} 及商下确界，在这些比值处取值得到有界映射 $A_U\to\mathcal H_A(x)$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints}，其半范数为 $x$ 上方唯一的 rational 谱点。Proposition~\ref{new:residueuniversal} 给出等距映射 $\mathcal H_{A_U}(x)\to\mathcal H_A(x)$。它与陈述中映射的复合固定稠密子域 $\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$，所以为恒等映射。反向复合固定 $A_U$ 的像：先对系数与必定等于 $f_i/g$ 的变量成立，再对收敛幂级数成立。因此它固定所取值的 $A_U$ 的稠密分式域，故也为恒等映射。}
\end{proof}
