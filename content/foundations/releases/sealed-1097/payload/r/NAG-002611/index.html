Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[The affine special-fibre criterion over a DVR]
\label{cand:f5tame:dvrspecialfibre}
For a flat \(R'\)-algebra \(B'\) with the specified semilinear
cyclic action, effective descent over \(R\) holds if and only if
\(G\) acts identically on \(B'/sB'\). Under these equivalent
conditions, finite presentation and nodality descend as in
Theorem~\ref{cand:f5tame:charactercriterion}.
No smoothness, branch-splitting, or diagonalization of \(B'\)
is required for this criterion.
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Necessity was proved in the theorem. Suppose the special-fibre
action is the identity, and retain the Fourier eigenspaces \(M_i\).
For \(1\leq i<e\) and \(a\in M_i\),
\((\zeta^i-1)a\in sB'\). The coefficient \(\zeta^i-1\) is a unit,
since its reduction is nonzero. Hence \(a=sb\) for a unique
\(b\in B'\). Applying \(\sigma\) and cancelling the
nonzerodivisor \(s\) gives \(\sigma(b)=\zeta^{i-1}b\).
Induction, with the indices \(i,i-1,\ldots,1\), gives
\(M_i=s^iM_0\). The opposite containment is immediate.
Thus \(\Lambda_i=B=I_iB\), and the theorem applies.
\end{proof}
