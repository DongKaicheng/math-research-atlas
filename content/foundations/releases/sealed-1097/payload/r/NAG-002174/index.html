Intrinsic bounds on displacement coefficients

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 900

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Intrinsic bounds on displacement coefficients}
{位移系数的内禀界}]
\label{cand:intrinsicdisplacementbounds}
\bi{For a bounded presentation (J), every analytic unit
$v\in B^\times$ satisfies}
{对于有界表示 (J)，每个解析单位元 $v\in B^\times$
满足}
\[
 |v(H_B(x,t))|=|v(x)|,\qquad
 |D_\nu(v)(x)|\leq|v(x)|
                   \quad(x\in\mathcal M(B),\ 0\leq t\leq1).
\]
\bi{If $b\in B$ satisfies $|b(y)|\leq1$ at every
$y\in\mathcal M(B)$, then $|D_\nu(b)(x)|\leq1$ for all
$\nu,x$. Consequently, for $0<\delta<1$ and
$E=\mathcal H(x)$, the evaluated displacement has}
{若 $b\in B$ 在每个 $y\in\mathcal M(B)$ 处满足
$|b(y)|\leq1$，则对全部 $\nu,x$ 有
$|D_\nu(b)(x)|\leq1$。因此，当 $0<\delta<1$ 且
$E=\mathcal H(x)$ 时，取值后的位移满足}
\[
 \left\|\frac{\sigma_{\delta,x}(v)}{v(x)}-1\right\|_\delta
       \leq\delta,
 \qquad
 \|\sigma_{\delta,x}(b)-b(x)\|_\delta\leq\delta.
\]
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The constant coefficient in the displacement expansion
gives $|c(H_B(x,t))|\geq|c(x)|$ for every $c\in B$.
Apply this to $v$ and $v^{-1}$. Multiplicativity makes the
product of the two left sides and the product of the two
right sides both equal to one, so both inequalities are
equalities. For each coefficient,}
{位移展开的常数系数说明，对每个 $c\in B$ 有
$|c(H_B(x,t))|\geq|c(x)|$。把它用于 $v$ 及
$v^{-1}$。由乘性，两左侧之积及两右侧之积都为一，
故两个不等式都是等式。对每个系数，}
\[
 |D_\nu(v)(x)|t^{|\nu|}\leq |v(x)|\qquad(0<t<1).
\]
\bi{Letting $t$ increase to one proves the coefficient
bound for a unit. For the second assertion,
$H_B(x,t)$ is an actual point of $\mathcal M(B)$, so
$|b(H_B(x,t))|\leq1$; the same limiting argument gives
the coefficient bound. Since $D_0$ is the identity, the
remaining terms have positive total degree, and their
Gauss norms at radius $\delta$ are at most $\delta$.
These arguments use only the values of the functions on
the analytic space, not a comparison of quotient norms.}
{令 $t$ 递增到一，得到单位元的系数界。对于第二项，
$H_B(x,t)$ 是 $\mathcal M(B)$ 中的实际点，所以
$|b(H_B(x,t))|\leq1$；同样的极限论证给出系数界。
由于 $D_0$ 为恒等，其余项的总次数为正，在半径
$\delta$ 处的 Gauss 范数至多为 $\delta$。这些论证
只使用函数在解析空间上的取值，不比较商范数。}
\end{proof}
