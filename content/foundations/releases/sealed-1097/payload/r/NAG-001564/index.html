NAG-001564 · Accepted whole

Actual coefficient tensors for finite modules

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 112

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Actual coefficient tensors for finite modules}{有限模的实际系数张量积}]\label{fnd:generalizedcoefficienttensor}
\bi{Let $A$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra. Let $\Gamma=\mathbb N^p\times\mathbb Z^q$ for finite $p,q\geq0$, and let $w:\Gamma\to\mathbb R_{>0}$ satisfy $w_0=1$ and $w_{\gamma+\delta}\leq w_\gamma w_\delta$. Put $B=\mathcal C_w(\Gamma,A)$ with convolution multiplication. For a finite $A$-module $M$ with a quotient norm from a finite free surjection, the natural map}
{设 $A$ 为 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数，$\Gamma=\mathbb N^p\times\mathbb Z^q$，其中 $p,q\geq0$ 有限。设 $w:\Gamma\to\mathbb R_{>0}$ 满足 $w_0=1$ 及 $w_{\gamma+\delta}\leq w_\gamma w_\delta$。令 $B=\mathcal C_w(\Gamma,A)$，采用卷积乘法。对配备有限自由满射商范数的有限 $A$-模 $M$，自然映射}
\[
 \Phi_M:B\otimes_A M\longrightarrow\mathcal C_w(\Gamma,M),
 \qquad b\otimes m\longmapsto(b_\gamma m)_\gamma
\]
\bi{is a $B$-linear isometric isomorphism for the projective tensor seminorm. In particular that seminorm is definite and complete, and}
{对于 projective 张量半范数是 $B$-线性等距同构。特别地，该半范数正定且完备，并且}
\[
 B\otimes_A M\simeq B\widehat\otimes_A M
       \simeq\mathcal C_w(\Gamma,M).
\]
\bi{With any other canonical norm these are natural bounded isomorphisms with bounded inverses. For a short exact sequence of finite canonical $A$-modules, both its ordinary and its separated completed tensor sequence with $B$ are exact and norm-strict. In coefficient norms the original inverse bound for the injection and lifting bound for the surjection remain valid. No affinoid or Noetherian hypothesis on $B$ is required.}
{对于其他典范范数，这些映射为自然的有界同构，且逆有界。对有限典范 $A$-模的短正合列，其与 $B$ 的普通张量序列和分离完备张量序列都正合且 norm-strict。对于系数范数，原单射的逆映射界和原满射的提升界保持有效。不需要假设 $B$ 为 affinoid 或 Noetherian。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose a finite presentation $A^s\xrightarrow{D}A^m\xrightarrow{q}M\to0$, using the surjection that defines the given quotient norm. Its relation module is finite by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}. Ordinary tensor right exactness, \cite[Tag 00DF, Lemma 10.12.10]{StacksAlg}, gives}
{选择有限表示 $A^s\xrightarrow{D}A^m\xrightarrow{q}M\to0$，其中满射 $q$ 给出所选商范数。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}，关系模有限。由普通张量积的右正合性 \cite[Tag 00DF, Lemma 10.12.10]{StacksAlg}，有}
\[
 B\otimes_A M\simeq B^m/D_BB^s.
\]
\bi{The constant matrix $D_B$ is exactly $D_w$ of Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedcoefficientpresentation}. By that theorem, its image is closed and the quotient is isometric to $\mathcal C_w(\Gamma,M)$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientconvolution} supplies the $B$-action. The coefficient generator map is $B$-linear, first on finite supports and then by boundedness and density; the resulting algebraic isomorphism is precisely $\Phi_M$. This identifies actual relation modules before any completion is taken.}
{常系数矩阵 $D_B$ 正是 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedcoefficientpresentation} 中的 $D_w$。该定理说明其像为闭像，且商等距于 $\mathcal C_w(\Gamma,M)$。Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientconvolution} 给出 $B$-作用。系数生成映射先在有限支撑上为 $B$-线性，再由有界性及稠密性在整个空间上为 $B$-线性；所得代数同构恰为 $\Phi_M$。因此在任何完备化之前，已经识别了实际关系模。}

\bi{For any finite tensor expression, contractivity of the $A$-action on $M$ gives}
{对任意有限张量表示，由 $M$ 上 $A$-作用的 contractivity，}
\[
 \left\|\Phi_M\left(\sum_jb_j\otimes m_j\right)\right\|_w
 \leq\max_j\|b_j\|_w\|m_j\|.
\]
\bi{Taking infima proves $\|\Phi_M(z)\|_w\leq\|z\|_\pi$. Conversely, let $e_i=q(\mathbf e_i)$ be the generator images, of norm at most one. Every lift $(b_i)\in B^m$ of $\Phi_M(z)$ gives the tensor expression $z=\sum_i b_i\otimes e_i$, hence $\|z\|_\pi\leq\max_i\|b_i\|_w$. The infimum of these lift norms is $\|\Phi_M(z)\|_w$ by the preceding quotient isometry. Thus $\Phi_M$ is isometric. Weighted coefficient completeness, or the closed quotient already obtained, proves that separated completion changes neither the elements nor the norm.}
{取下确界，得到 $\|\Phi_M(z)\|_w\leq\|z\|_\pi$。反之，记生成元的像为 $e_i=q(\mathbf e_i)$，其范数至多为一。$\Phi_M(z)$ 在 $B^m$ 中的每个提升 $(b_i)$ 都给出张量表示 $z=\sum_i b_i\otimes e_i$，所以 $\|z\|_\pi\leq\max_i\|b_i\|_w$。由前面的商等距同构，这些提升范数的下确界就是 $\|\Phi_M(z)\|_w$。因此 $\Phi_M$ 等距。系数空间的完备性，或者已经得到的闭商完备性，说明分离完备化不改变元素或范数。}

\bi{A bounded $A$-linear map commutes with $\Phi_M$ on pure tensors by its coefficient formula. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules} makes every map between finite modules bounded and every short exact sequence norm-strict. Proposition~\ref{fnd:weightedc0exact} then gives coefficient exactness with the asserted unchanged constants. Transporting through the natural isomorphisms proves the two tensor exactness assertions. Finally, uniform inequalities between two norms on $M$ induce the same inequalities between their coefficient norms and between their projective norms. This proves the bounded-isomorphism statement for all canonical choices. The tensor assertion is specific to this coefficient algebra, and does not concern an arbitrary Banach target.}
{对有界 $A$-线性映射，由系数公式可知它在纯张量上与 $\Phi_M$ 交换。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules} 保证有限模之间的每个映射有界，每个短正合列 norm-strict。Proposition~\ref{fnd:weightedc0exact} 因而给出系数正合性，并保持所述常数。沿自然同构搬运，即得两个张量正合性结论。最后，$M$ 上两个范数之间的一致不等式，在对应系数范数之间及 projective 范数之间同样成立。这证明了全部典范范数选择下的有界同构断言。这里的张量结论针对这个系数代数，不涉及任意 Banach 目标。}
\end{proof}
