Actual skeletal fibres and residual strata

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 904

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Actual skeletal fibres and residual strata}
{实际骨架纤维与剩余分层}]
\label{cand:actualskeletalfibrestrata}
\bi{For $w\in\operatorname{relint}(\Delta_I)$, specialization
induces a bijection}
{当 $w\in\operatorname{relint}(\Delta_I)$ 时，特化诱导双射}
\[
 f^{-1}(z_w)\ \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\ Q_I.
\]
\bi{For the point $x_{\eta,w}$ labelled by $\eta\in Q_I$,
put $E_w=\mathcal H(z_w)$ and $l_\eta=\kappa(\eta)$.
Then $\mathcal H(x_{\eta,w})/E_w$ is the unramified
finite extension lifting the finite separable residue extension}
{对于由 $\eta\in Q_I$ 标记的点 $x_{\eta,w}$，置
$E_w=\mathcal H(z_w)$ 及 $l_\eta=\kappa(\eta)$。
则 $\mathcal H(x_{\eta,w})/E_w$ 是提升以下有限可分
剩余扩张的非分歧有限扩张：}
\[
 l_\eta\otimes_{F_I}h_w\,/\,h_w.
\]
\bi{Its degree is $[l_\eta:F_I]$, and its value group
is that of $E_w$.}
{其次数为 $[l_\eta:F_I]$，其值群与 $E_w$ 相同。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use Lemma~\ref{cand:formaletaleboundedpresentation}
to write $B_0=\widehat C$ with
$C=A_0[Y,Q]/(F,Q\det J-1)$. The square-zero correction
in that proof and \cite[Tag 00UR]{StacksAlg} show that
$C$ is an \etale $A_0$-algebra. Base change along the
bounded evaluation $A_0\to E_w^\circ$ gives an \etale
algebra $C_E$ over $E_w^\circ$ by
\cite[Tag 00U2, part (3)]{StacksAlg}. It is flat by part (6)
of the same lemma, so multiplication by $\pi$ is injective.
Its $\pi$-adic completion $D_0$ is an admissible
$E_w^\circ$-algebra by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion} and
Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation}. The
explicit restricted presentation gives}
{利用 Lemma~\ref{cand:formaletaleboundedpresentation}
写 $B_0=\widehat C$，其中
$C=A_0[Y,Q]/(F,Q\det J-1)$。该证明中的平方零修正
及 \cite[Tag 00UR]{StacksAlg} 说明 $C$ 是
$A_0$ 上的 \etale 代数。沿有界取值
$A_0\to E_w^\circ$ 作基变换，由
\cite[Tag 00U2, part (3)]{StacksAlg} 得到
$E_w^\circ$ 上的 \etale 代数 $C_E$。同一引理的
第 (6) 项说明它平坦，故 $\pi$ 的乘法为单射。
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion} 及
Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation}，其
$\pi$-进完备化 $D_0$ 为 admissible
$E_w^\circ$-代数。明确的受限表示给出}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 D&=D_0[1/\pi]
       =B\widehat\otimes_A E_w,\\
 D_0/E_w^{\circ\circ}D_0
   &=(B_0\otimes_O k)\otimes_{A_0\otimes_O k}h_w.
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{For the second identity one simply reduces the finite
list of equations in the restricted presentation: reduction
of an integral restricted series is a polynomial, and
reduction of the ideal is generated by the reductions of
its displayed generators. The first identity is the
completed-tensor fibre presentation of
Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres}.}
{第二个恒等式由受限表示中有限个方程的约化直接
得到：整受限级数的约化是多项式，所列理想的约化
由其生成元的约化生成。第一个恒等式是
Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres} 的完备张量
积纤维表示。}

\bi{The special-fibre map is \etale, and its fibre over
$\xi_I$ is a finite disjoint union
$\coprod_{\eta\in Q_I}\operatorname{Spec}(l_\eta)$
with each $l_\eta/F_I$ finite separable, by
\cite[Tag 02GL]{StacksAlg}. Here the fibre points are
exactly the generic points of $S_I$. Indeed $S_I$ is
smooth over $k$ and hence normal by
\cite[Tags 036U, 056T]{StacksAlg}; flat going down,
\cite[Tag 00HS]{StacksAlg}, makes every component dominate
the integral $D_I$, and the zero-dimensional generic
fibre has no proper chains of primes. The preceding lemma
now identifies the special fibre of $D_0$ with}
{特殊纤维映射为 \etale，由 \cite[Tag 02GL]{StacksAlg}，
其在 $\xi_I$ 上的纤维是有限不交并
$\coprod_{\eta\in Q_I}\operatorname{Spec}(l_\eta)$，
各 $l_\eta/F_I$ 均有限可分。这里的纤维点恰为
$S_I$ 的泛点。事实上，$S_I$ 在 $k$ 上光滑，由
\cite[Tags 036U, 056T]{StacksAlg} 为正规；平坦
going down（\cite[Tag 00HS]{StacksAlg}）使每个
分量支配整簇 $D_I$，零维泛纤维又不含严格素链。
于是上一引理将 $D_0$ 的特殊纤维识别为}
\[
             \prod_{\eta\in Q_I}
                      (l_\eta\otimes_{F_I}h_w),
\]
\bi{a finite product of fields. This is in particular
reduced, so Lemma~\ref{cand:reducedspecialcanonical}
identifies it with the canonical reduction of $D$.
Every prime of this reduction is minimal.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:canonicalgenericshilov} therefore gives
one and only one actual analytic point over each factor,
and these exhaust the fibre. If the product is empty,
the integral quotient norm would have $1$ of norm less
than one unless $D=0$; thus the analytic fibre is empty
in exactly that case. This proves the bijection and its
specialization labels.}
{它是有限个域之积，特别地约化。因此
Lemma~\ref{cand:reducedspecialcanonical} 将它与
$D$ 的典范约化识别。此约化中的每个素理想均极小，
故 Theorem~\ref{fnd:canonicalgenericshilov} 在每个
因子上方给出唯一的实际解析点，且这些点穷尽纤维。
若乘积为空，则除非 $D=0$，整商范数会使 $1$ 的
范数严格小于一，矛盾。因此解析纤维恰在此情形为空。
这证明双射及其特化标记。}

\bi{For the valued-field assertion, fix a factor
$\lambda=l_\eta\otimes_{F_I}h_w$ and apply
Lemma~\ref{cand:residueextensionlift} over the complete
field $E_w$. It supplies a finite complete field $L/E_w$
of degree $[\lambda:h_w]$, residue field $\lambda$, and
unchanged value group. Lift the coordinates of the
corresponding special-fibre point arbitrarily into
$L^\circ$. The finitely many equation errors have norms
strictly below one and the Jacobian determinant has norm
one. Lemma~\ref{cand:banachcenterhensel} corrects them
to an exact bounded solution in $L$. This gives a point
of $\mathcal M(D)$ with that residue label, hence the
point already identified, and an isometric embedding}
{为证明赋值域断言，固定一个因子
$\lambda=l_\eta\otimes_{F_I}h_w$，在完备域 $E_w$
上应用 Lemma~\ref{cand:residueextensionlift}，得到
有限完备扩域 $L/E_w$，其次数为 $[\lambda:h_w]$，
剩余域为 $\lambda$，值群不变。任意把相应特殊
纤维点的坐标提升到 $L^\circ$。有限个方程误差的
范数严格小于一，而 Jacobian 行列式的范数为一。
由 Lemma~\ref{cand:banachcenterhensel}，可将它们
修正为 $L$ 中的精确有界解。这给出
$\mathcal M(D)$ 中具有该剩余标记的一点，故恰为
已识别的点，并给出等距嵌入}
\[
                  \mathcal H(x_{\eta,w})\hookrightarrow L.
\]
\bi{The embedding first holds for the fraction field of
the evaluated affinoid algebra and then for its completion.
Its residue field contains $\lambda$. Lifts of a basis of
$\lambda/h_w$ are $E_w$-linearly independent: normalize
any putative relation by a coefficient of largest absolute
value and reduce. Hence the left field has degree at least
$[\lambda:h_w]$, while the embedding into $L$ gives the
opposite inequality. It equals $L$, proving the assertion.}
{先在取值后的 affinoid 代数的分式域上得到此嵌入，
再延拓到完备化。其剩余域包含 $\lambda$。
$\lambda/h_w$ 的一组基的提升在 $E_w$ 上线性
无关：将任何假设的关系除以绝对值最大的系数后
约化，即得矛盾。因此左侧域的次数至少为
$[\lambda:h_w]$，而嵌入 $L$ 给出相反不等式。
故它等于 $L$，证明断言。}
\end{proof}
