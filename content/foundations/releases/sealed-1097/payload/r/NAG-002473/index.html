Comparison of the two final topologies

8 Intrinsic smooth forms, integration and Stokes

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1166

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Comparison of the two final topologies]
\label{f8cont:topology}
In every bidegree,
\[
             \tau_{\mathrm{cut}}=\tau_{\mathrm{ord}}.
\]
More precisely, each $R_s$ is a finite sum of cutoff presentation maps
composed with continuous linear maps, and each $Q_i$ factors continuously
through one ordinary presentation space $H_s$. The charts, cutoffs and
factorizing maps in each assertion are fixed before the variable ambient
forms are chosen.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Fix $s$. Apply \hyperref[v037:forms:compactpartition]{\base{v037:forms:compactpartition}} to $K_s$ and $(U_j)$,
obtaining fixed $\chi_j$ supported compactly in $U_j$, with sum one near
$K_s$. If $\eta=(\eta_j)\in H_s$, then
\[
        R_s\eta=\sum_j Q_{(V_j,f_j,\Omega_j,\chi_j)}\eta_j.
\]
On $K_s$ this follows from the partition and compatibility; off $K_s$ all
relevant sections are zero. The coordinate projections from $H_s$ are
continuous. Thus every $R_s$ is continuous for $\tau_{\mathrm{cut}}$.

Conversely, fix $i=(V,f,\Omega,\chi)$ and put $K_s=\supp\chi$.
The empty-support case is zero. Otherwise choose finitely many affinoid
neighbourhoods $W_j\subset\Int(V,X)$ whose interiors cover $K_s$, such
that $\chi$ is represented on $W_j$ by a fixed smooth scalar coefficient
$c_j$ in a fixed unit tuple $g_j$. To do this, shrink inside an ordinary
representing neighbourhood of $\chi$ before taking $W_j$; the neighbourhood
basis and restriction assertion are part of the proof of
\base{v037:forms:germsheaf}. On $W_j$ use the joint tuple
$h_j=(f|_{W_j},g_j)$ and the ambient open set
$\Omega\times\Omega_{g_j}$, where $\Omega_{g_j}\supset t_{g_j}(W_j)$.
Set
\[
       L_j\eta=\pi_g^*c_j\,\pi_f^*\eta.
\]
For every compact $L$ in this product and every $m$, the product and chain
rules give
\[
       p_{L,m}(L_j\eta)\leq C_{j,L,m}\,p_{\pi_f(L),m}(\eta).
\]
The derivatives of the fixed coefficient $c_j$ are absorbed in
$C_{j,L,m}$. The tuple
$(L_j\eta)_j$ belongs to the fixed space $H_s$: its components represent
the same ordinary section $\chi t_f^*\eta$ and vanish off $K_s$.
It depends continuously on $\eta$, and its glued section is $Q_i\eta$.
Therefore $Q_i$ is continuous for $\tau_{\mathrm{ord}}$.

A seminorm is continuous for either final topology exactly when its
composites with that topology's generating maps are continuous. The two
factorizations prove equality of these families of seminorms.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\begin{definition}[The two generating families]\label{f8cont:def:stages}
A cutoff presentation $i=(V,f,\Omega,\chi)$ consists of an affinoid
domain $V\subset X$, a unit tuple $f$ on $V$, an open set
$\Omega\supset t_f(V)$, and
$\chi\in\A_c^{0,0}(X)$ with $\supp\chi\subset\Int(V,X)$.
Here $t_f=-\log|f|$. Its map is
\[
 Q_i:E^{p,q}(\Omega)\longrightarrow\E^{p,q}(X),\qquad
 Q_i\eta=\ext(\chi t_f^*\eta).
\]
Let $\tau_{\mathrm{cut}}$ be the locally convex topology generated by all
seminorms $P$ on $\E^{p,q}(X)$ for which $P Q_i$ is continuous for every $i$.
This is the definition F8-T1 in the supplied candidate.

An ordinary presentation $s$ consists of finitely many such charts
$(V_j,f_j,\Omega_j)$, without cutoffs, and a compact set
$K_s\subset\bigcup_j U_j$, where $U_j=\Int(V_j,X)$.
Let
\[
 H_s\subset\prod_j E^{p,q}(\Omega_j)
\]
be the linear subspace of tuples $(\eta_j)$ for which the induced ordinary
sections agree on $U_j\cap U_k$ and vanish on $U_j\setminus K_s$.
Give $H_s$ its coefficient subspace topology. The sections glue with zero
on $X\setminus K_s$ to a section $R_s\eta\in\E^{p,q}(X)$.
Give $B_s=H_s/\ker R_s$ its quotient topology and let
$\tau_{\mathrm{ord}}$ be the final locally convex topology for the maps
$B_s\to\E^{p,q}(X)$, equivalently for all $R_s$.
\end{definition}
