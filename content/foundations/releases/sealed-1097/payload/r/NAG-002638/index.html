Coherent bounded projection

I Descent of the polarized mixed tower

Open in the English reader · PDF page 2122

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Coherent bounded projection]
\label{f9lh:boundedprojection}
Let $q:Y\to X$ be finite between good Hausdorff spaces pure of
the same dimension $d$, with $X$ compact. Let $\mathcal G$
be coherent on $Y_G$, let $u\in\cA_X^{0,0}(X)$, and let
$\theta\in\cA_X^{d,d}(X)$. Put
\[
             V_{X,\mathcal F}(u;\theta)=\int_{X,\mathcal F}u\theta .
\]
Then $Y$ is compact and
\begin{equation}\label{ex68:height:eq:boundedprojection}
 V_{Y,\mathcal G}(q^*u;q^*\theta)
                 =V_{X,q_*\mathcal G}(u;\theta).
\end{equation}
If $q_*\mathcal G$ is locally free of constant rank $\rho$ on
the analytic G-site, this is $\rho V_{X,\cO_X}(u;\theta)$.

If $\theta$ is positive, then, for every coherent $\mathcal F$,
\begin{equation}\label{ex68:height:eq:bound}
 |V_{X,\mathcal F}(u;\theta)|
       \leq \|u\|_\infty\,m_{X,\mathcal F}(\theta),
 \qquad m_{X,\mathcal F}(\theta)=\int_{X,\mathcal F}\theta\geq0.
\end{equation}
This functional extends uniquely and continuously to
$u\in\mathcal B(X)$, retaining \eqref{ex68:height:eq:bound}.
For positive $\theta$, pullback sends $\mathcal B(X)$ into
$\mathcal B(Y)$ and \eqref{ex68:height:eq:boundedprojection}, including
the rank case, holds for these extensions.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
A finite affinoid cover of compact $X$ has full inverse images
that are affinoid; their finite union is $Y$, so $Y$ is compact.
Multiplication and pullback in NAG-002424 give
$q^*(u\theta)=q^*u\,q^*\theta$.
Apply NAG-002430 to the compactly supported form $u\theta$.
Its rank clause gives the second assertion.

Let $C=\|u\|_\infty$. The two forms $(C+u)\theta$ and
$(C-u)\theta$ are positive. Linearity and positivity in
NAG-002425 therefore give
$-C m_{X,\mathcal F}(\theta)\leq V_{X,\mathcal F}(u;\theta)
\leq C m_{X,\mathcal F}(\theta)$.
Applying this inequality to differences shows that the integral
values of a uniformly Cauchy smooth sequence are Cauchy.
Their limits depend only on the uniform limit, by the same bound.
This constructs the unique continuous extension to the specified
closure, including the zero-mass case.

Pullback is continuous for the uniform norm since
$\|q^*u\|_\infty\leq\|u\|_\infty$.
The pullback of a positive top form is positive: on a common
moment presentation the coefficient on a source tangent
$d$-plane is the corresponding target coefficient multiplied by
the square of the real determinant of the induced tangent map;
a rank drop gives zero. This is a pointwise superform calculation.
Thus the same bounds apply to $q^*\theta$ on $Y$.
Taking limits in the proved smooth identity gives the asserted
identity for $\mathcal B(X)$ and its rank specialization.
\end{proof}
