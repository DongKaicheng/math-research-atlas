\begin{proposition}[Separation for functions]\label{f8cont:scalar}
The space $(\E^{0,0}(X),\tau_{\mathrm{cut}})$ is Hausdorff.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
For $x\in X$, evaluation on a cutoff stage is either zero or
$\chi(x)\eta(t_f(x))$. It is continuous in the ambient coefficient space.
Thus $\alpha\mapsto|\alpha(x)|$ is one of the defining continuous
seminorms. By \base{v037:forms:smoothcutoff}, ordinary scalar sections
embed in continuous functions, so these seminorms separate distinct
sections. Given a nonzero difference, disjoint intervals around its two
values pull back to disjoint neighbourhoods. This proves Hausdorffness.
\end{proof}
