Normal loci over arbitrary complete fields

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 351

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Normal loci over arbitrary complete fields}{任意完备域上的正规点集}]
\label{fnd:generalnormalcomparisonlocus}
\bi{Let $X=\mathcal M(A)$ over an arbitrary complete field, and put $\mathfrak p=\ker(x)$ for $x\in X$. Then $A_{\mathfrak p}$ is normal if and only if $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is normal. These points form a Zariski open subset of $X$. Consequently the ordinary normal-point set of every good analytic space is ordinarily open.}
{设 $X=\mathcal M(A)$ 定义于任意完备域上，对 $x\in X$ 令 $\mathfrak p=\ker(x)$。则 $A_{\mathfrak p}$ 正规当且仅当 $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 正规；这些点组成 $X$ 的 Zariski 开子集。因此，每个 good 解析空间的通常正规点集对普通拓扑开放。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $D=A_{\mathfrak p}$ and $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedordinaryregularcomparison}, $D\to R$ is a faithfully flat regular local map. Its fibres are normal by \cite[Tag 0567]{StacksAlg}, so normality of $D$ implies normality of $R$ by \cite[Tag 0C22]{StacksAlg}. Conversely, if $R$ is normal, faithful flatness embeds $D$ into the domain $R$. For $u/v\in\operatorname{Frac}(D)$ integral over $D$, normality of $R$ gives $u/v\in R$, hence $u\in vR$. Faithful flatness contracts $vR$ to $vD$, so $u/v\in D$. This proves the equivalence.}
{记 $D=A_{\mathfrak p}$、$R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedordinaryregularcomparison}，$D\to R$ 为忠实平坦的正则局部映射。其纤维由 \cite[Tag 0567]{StacksAlg} 正规，所以由 \cite[Tag 0C22]{StacksAlg}，$D$ 正规蕴含 $R$ 正规。反之，若 $R$ 正规，则忠实平坦性将 $D$ 嵌入整环 $R$。对在 $D$ 上整的 $u/v\in\operatorname{Frac}(D)$，$R$ 的正规性给出 $u/v\in R$，故 $u\in vR$。忠实平坦性将 $vR$ 收缩为 $vD$，所以 $u/v\in D$。所述等价得证。}

\bi{Let $N$ be the product of the integral closures of the minimal-prime quotients of $A$ in their fraction fields. It is finite by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalaffinoidexcellence}. Integral closure commutes with localization, by clearing the finitely many denominators in a monic equation. Thus $A_{\mathfrak p}$ is normal exactly when $A_{\mathfrak p}\to N_{\mathfrak p}$ is an isomorphism: in the normal case the localization is reduced with one integral component and already integrally closed, and in the converse case it is a localization of the normal ring $N$. The kernel and cokernel of $A\to N$ are finite, so their supports are closed. Their complement is precisely the normal locus in $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$. Its pullback by $x\mapsto\ker(x)$ is Zariski open in $X$, since a principal open pulls back to $|a(x)|>0$. The equivalence already proved identifies it with the normal-point set; ordinary affinoid neighborhoods give the assertion for every good space.}
{令 $N$ 为 $A$ 的各极小素理想商在其分式域中的整闭包之乘积。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalaffinoidexcellence}，它在 $A$ 上有限。在首一方程中清除有限个分母，便知整闭包与局部化交换。因此，$A_{\mathfrak p}$ 正规当且仅当 $A_{\mathfrak p}\to N_{\mathfrak p}$ 为同构：正规时该局部化约化、只有一个整分量且已整闭；反之，该同构将它识别为正规环 $N$ 的局部化。$A\to N$ 的核与余核均有限，故其支撑闭；这两个支撑的补集正是 $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ 的正规点集。它沿 $x\mapsto\ker(x)$ 的拉回在 $X$ 中 Zariski 开，因为主开集的拉回为 $|a(x)|>0$。已经证明的等价将此拉回识别为正规点集；再取普通 affinoid 邻域，即得每个 good 空间上的断言。}
\end{proof}
