The residue field over a relative open face

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 904

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The residue field over a relative open face}
{相对开面上的剩余域}]
\label{cand:facegaussresidueextension}
\bi{Let $w\in\operatorname{relint}(\Delta_I)$ and let
$z_w\in\Sigma_a$ be its standard Gauss point. Specialization
sends $z_w$ to $\xi_I$, and the induced extension}
{设 $w\in\operatorname{relint}(\Delta_I)$，并以
$z_w\in\Sigma_a$ 记其标准 Gauss 点。特化将 $z_w$
送到 $\xi_I$，且诱导扩张}
\[
 F_I:=\kappa(\xi_I)=k(X_j:j\notin I,T_1,\ldots,T_q)
       \ \longrightarrow\ h_w:=\widetilde{\mathcal H(z_w)}
\]
\bi{is purely transcendental. In particular, for every
finite extension $l/F_I$, the tensor product $l\otimes_{F_I}h_w$
is a field, of degree $[l:F_I]$ over $h_w$.}
{是纯超越扩张。特别地，对每个有限扩张 $l/F_I$，
张量积 $l\otimes_{F_I}h_w$ 是域，在 $h_w$ 上的
次数为 $[l:F_I]$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose $i_0\in I$ and eliminate $X_{i_0}$ using the
product relation. The independent Gauss variables are the
remaining $X_i$ and the $T_j$. The variables $X_j$ with
$j\notin I$ and all the $T_j$ have weight zero; all other
original $X_i$ have strictly positive weight. Their
residues therefore give exactly the generic point $\xi_I$:
every nonzero polynomial in the zero-weight variables has
Gauss norm one after normalizing an integral coefficient.}
{选取 $i_0\in I$，利用乘积关系消去 $X_{i_0}$。
其余 $X_i$ 及 $T_j$ 为独立 Gauss 变量。$j\notin I$
时的 $X_j$ 及全部 $T_j$ 的权为零，其余原 $X_i$
的权严格为正。因此它们的剩余类恰给出泛点
$\xi_I$：把一个整系数归一化后，零权变量的每个
非零多项式均具有 Gauss 范数一。}

\bi{In the residue-lattice proof of
Proposition~\ref{new:gauss_residue_formula}, let $L_w$
be the lattice of exponent vectors whose weight belongs
to $v(K^\times)$. The subgroup generated by the
zero-weight coordinate vectors is a direct summand of
the full exponent lattice, hence is saturated in $L_w$.
Lemma~\ref{cand:elementarylatticesplitting} extends its
displayed coordinate basis to a basis of $L_w$. The
Gauss residue formula with this basis expresses $h_w$
as the rational function field over $k$ in the residues
of the zero-weight variables and finitely many additional
normalized Laurent monomials. It is therefore purely
transcendental over the indicated subfield $F_I$.}
{在 Proposition~\ref{new:gauss_residue_formula} 的
剩余格证明中，以 $L_w$ 记权属于 $v(K^\times)$ 的
指数向量格。零权坐标向量生成的子群是整个指数格的
直和因子，因而在 $L_w$ 中饱和。由
Lemma~\ref{cand:elementarylatticesplitting}，可将其
所列坐标基延拓为 $L_w$ 的基。使用此基的 Gauss
剩余公式把 $h_w$ 写为一个有理函数域，其在 $k$ 上的
变量为零权变量的剩余类及有限个额外的归一化 Laurent
单项式。因此它在所指子域 $F_I$ 上纯超越。}

\bi{Finally, write $h_w=F_I(U_1,\ldots,U_s)$. The
ring $l\otimes_{F_I}h_w$ is the localization of
$l[U_1,\ldots,U_s]$ by the nonzero polynomials over
$F_I$. It is an integral domain and is finite-dimensional
over $h_w$, hence is a field: multiplication by a nonzero
element is an injective endomorphism of a finite-dimensional
vector space and is therefore surjective. Tensoring a basis
of $l/F_I$ gives its stated degree.}
{最后，写 $h_w=F_I(U_1,\ldots,U_s)$。
环 $l\otimes_{F_I}h_w$ 是 $l[U_1,\ldots,U_s]$ 关于
$F_I$ 上非零多项式的局部化。它是整环，并在 $h_w$
上有限维，因此为域：非零元素的乘法是有限维向量
空间的单射自同态，因而满射。把 $l/F_I$ 的一组基
作张量积即给出所称次数。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Let $A_0=O\langle X_0,\ldots,X_r,T_1,\ldots,T_q\rangle/
(X_0\cdots X_r-a)$, where $0<|a|<1$, and let
$A_0\to B_0$ be a topologically finitely presented formally
\etale map of admissible algebras. Write $f:X=\mathcal M(B)
\to\mathcal M(A)$ for the generic map, $\bar f$ for the
special-fibre map, and $k=\widetilde K$. For a nonempty
subset $I\subseteq\{0,\ldots,r\}$ put}
{设 $A_0=O\langle X_0,\ldots,X_r,T_1,\ldots,T_q\rangle/
(X_0\cdots X_r-a)$，其中 $0<|a|<1$，并设
$A_0\to B_0$ 为 admissible 代数之间拓扑有限表示的
形式 \etale 映射。以 $f:X=\mathcal M(B)\to\mathcal M(A)$
记泛纤维映射，以 $\bar f$ 记特殊纤维映射，并记
$k=\widetilde K$。对于非空子集
$I\subseteq\{0,\ldots,r\}$，置}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \Delta_I&=\{w\in\Delta_a:w_j=0\text{ for }j\notin I\},\\
 D_I&=\{X_i=0\ (i\in I),\quad X_j\ne0\ (j\notin I)\}
                \subseteq\operatorname{Spec}(A_0\otimes_O k),\\
 S_I&=\bar f^{-1}(D_I).
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{The stratum $D_I$ is the integral smooth variety with
coordinates $X_j^{\pm1}$ for $j\notin I$ and $T_1,\ldots,T_q$.
Denote its generic point by $\xi_I$ and the finite set of
generic points of $S_I$ by $Q_I$. The empty inverse image
has $Q_I=\varnothing$.}
{分层 $D_I$ 是整光滑簇，其坐标为 $j\notin I$ 时的
$X_j^{\pm1}$ 以及 $T_1,\ldots,T_q$。以 $\xi_I$ 记其
泛点，以 $Q_I$ 记 $S_I$ 的有限泛点集；若逆像为空，
则 $Q_I=\varnothing$。}
