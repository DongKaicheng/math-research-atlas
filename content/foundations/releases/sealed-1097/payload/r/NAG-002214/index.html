Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{A finite simplex realization of the chart skeleton}
{坐标片骨架的有限单纯形实现}]
\label{cand:etaleskeletonfinitefaces}
\bi{For each $\eta\in Q_I$, the points
$x_{\eta,w}$ on $\operatorname{relint}(\Delta_I)$
extend uniquely to a continuous map}
{对每个 $\eta\in Q_I$，相对内部
$\operatorname{relint}(\Delta_I)$ 上的点
$x_{\eta,w}$ 唯一延拓为连续映射}
\[
 s_\eta:\Delta_I\longrightarrow X,
 \qquad s_\eta(w)=x_{\eta_J,w}
       \quad\text{if }w\in\operatorname{relint}(\Delta_J).
\]
\bi{It is a homeomorphism onto its image. The finite
collection of these closed simplices covers
$\Sigma_B=f^{-1}(\Sigma_a)$, and the restriction to a
face $\Delta_J$ is $s_{\eta_J}$. More precisely,
$\Sigma_B$ is homeomorphic to the quotient of the
finite disjoint union of the $\Delta_I$, one copy for
each $\eta\in Q_I$, by exactly these face identifications.
In these charts all face maps are the coordinate face
inclusions, integral affine for the lattices}
{它是到其像的同胚。这些有限个闭单纯形覆盖
$\Sigma_B=f^{-1}(\Sigma_a)$，且在面 $\Delta_J$
上的限制为 $s_{\eta_J}$。更精确地，$\Sigma_B$
同胚于以下商：对每个 $\eta\in Q_I$ 取一个
$\Delta_I$ 的副本，再对这个有限不交并恰按上述
面识别取商。在这些坐标中，全部面映射都是坐标
面包含，关于以下格为整仿射：}
\[
               N_I=\{(n_i)_{i\in I}\in\mathbb Z^I:
                                      \textstyle\sum_i n_i=0\}.
\]
\bi{The intersection with the generic fibre of a formal
open subset is a union of closed faces in this finite
realization.}
{与一个形式开子集的泛纤维之交，在此有限实现中为
一些闭面的并。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First work over the relative open face. The primes
in $Q_I$ are distinct and incomparable. Choose an
integral function $g\in B_0$ whose reduction is outside
$\eta$ and inside every other member of $Q_I$, by
choosing one element for each other prime and multiplying.
If there is no other prime, take $g=1$. By the actual
fibre description, the locus $|g|=1$ selects exactly
$x_{\eta,w}$ over each $w$ in this open face. For any
compact subset $W\subset\operatorname{relint}(\Delta_I)$,
the set}
{先在相对开面上工作。$Q_I$ 中的素理想两两不同且
不可比较。对其余每个素理想选取一个属于它而不属于
$\eta$ 的元素，再相乘并提升，可得整函数
$g\in B_0$，其约化不属于 $\eta$ 而属于其余每个
$Q_I$ 成员。若没有其余素理想，取 $g=1$。
由实际纤维的描述，集合 $|g|=1$ 在这个开面的每个
$w$ 上方恰选出 $x_{\eta,w}$。对任意紧子集
$W\subset\operatorname{relint}(\Delta_I)$，集合}
\[
       \{x\in f^{-1}(\{z_w:w\in W\}):|g(x)|=1\}
\]
\bi{is closed in the compact affinoid space $X$, and
maps continuously and bijectively onto $W$. It is
therefore homeomorphic to $W$. Every point of the
relative open face has a compact relative neighborhood
contained in that face, proving continuity of
$w\mapsto x_{\eta,w}$ throughout the relative open face.}
{在紧 affinoid 空间 $X$ 中闭，并连续双射到 $W$，
因而与 $W$ 同胚。相对开面的每一点都有包含于该
面内的紧相对邻域，由此证明
$w\mapsto x_{\eta,w}$ 在整个相对开面上连续。}

\bi{Consider the closure of its graph in the compact
space $\Delta_I\times X$. Let $(w,x)$ be a point of
that closure and let $J=\{j:w_j>0\}$. Continuity of
$f$ gives $f(x)=z_w$, so the actual fibre theorem
labels $x$ by some $\theta\in Q_J$. If an integral
function $h$ has reduction outside $\eta$, then
$|h(x_{\eta,u})|=1$ at every interior $u$.
Continuity makes $|h(x)|=1$. Thus the reduction prime
$\theta$ is contained in $\eta$. Since $\sum_i w_i=v(a)>0$,
$J$ is nonempty. By \hyperref[cand:nonemptyfacegenerization]{nonempty face generization},
$\theta=\eta_J$. Every $w\in\Delta_I$ is a limit of
interior points; compactness supplies a graph limit above
it. We have proved that this limit is unique. The closed
graph therefore projects continuously and bijectively
onto the compact Hausdorff simplex $\Delta_I$, hence
homeomorphically. Its second projection is the desired
continuous extension. Its composition with the base
coordinate map is the identity, so it is injective and
is a homeomorphism onto its compact image.}
{在紧空间 $\Delta_I\times X$ 中取其图的闭包。
设 $(w,x)$ 为该闭包的一点，并令
$J=\{j:w_j>0\}$。由 $f$ 的连续性有 $f(x)=z_w$，
因此实际纤维定理将 $x$ 标记为某个 $\theta\in Q_J$。
若整函数 $h$ 的约化不属于 $\eta$，则在每个内部点
$u$ 处均有 $|h(x_{\eta,u})|=1$；连续性给出
$|h(x)|=1$。所以约化素理想 $\theta$ 包含于
$\eta$。由于 $\sum_i w_i=v(a)>0$，$J$ 非空。由\hyperref[cand:nonemptyfacegenerization]{非空面的一般化}，
$\theta=\eta_J$。每个 $w\in\Delta_I$ 都是内部
点的极限，紧性在其上方给出一个图极限；前述论证
证明此极限唯一。因此这个闭图连续双射到紧 Hausdorff
单纯形 $\Delta_I$，从而为同胚。其第二投影即为
所需连续延拓。它与基底坐标映射的复合是恒等映射，
所以它单射，并为到其紧像的同胚。}

\bi{The boundary formula and \hyperref[cand:nonemptyfacegenerization]{transitivity for nonempty faces}
prove the face restriction assertion.
There are finitely many $I$ and finitely many
$\eta\in Q_I$, and the fibre theorem shows that their
relative interiors partition $\Sigma_B$. Two closed
cells meet at a point of support $J$ exactly when their
$J$-generizations agree; hence their intersection is a
union of the indicated common closed faces. The induced
map from the finite face quotient to $\Sigma_B$ is
therefore a continuous bijection. Its source is compact
and its target Hausdorff, so it is a homeomorphism.
Coordinate inclusions preserve the displayed integer
lattices, giving the final affine assertion.}
{边界公式及\hyperref[cand:nonemptyfacegenerization]{非空面一般化的传递性}证明面限制断言。
$I$ 只有有限多个，各 $Q_I$ 也有限，而纤维定理
说明其相对内部划分 $\Sigma_B$。两个闭胞腔在
支撑为 $J$ 的一点处相交，当且仅当它们的
$J$-一般化相同；故其交是所指公共闭面之并。因此
从有限面商到 $\Sigma_B$ 的诱导映射为连续双射。
源紧而目标 Hausdorff，所以它为同胚。坐标包含
保持所列整数格，给出最后的仿射断言。}

\bi{On a relative open cell the specialization label
is constant. If this label belongs to a given formal
open, all its generizations belong to that open as well.
Consequently inclusion of an open cell includes its
whole closed simplex. The specialization description
of formal-open generic fibres then gives the asserted
union of closed faces.}
{每个相对开胞腔上的特化标记恒定。若此标记属于
一个给定形式开集，则其全部一般化也属于该开集。
因此，一个开胞腔被包含便意味着其整个闭单纯形
被包含。形式开集泛纤维的特化描述于是给出所称
闭面之并。}
\end{proof}
