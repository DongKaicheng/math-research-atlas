Projective descent in the exact tame Kummer class

E Projective descent in the tame Kummer class

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1980

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Projective descent in the exact tame Kummer class]
\label{cand:f5proj:projectivedescent}
Assume (K). Let \(Y'/O'\) be a supplied projective, flat,
finitely presented nodal scheme of relative dimension one,
equipped with a semilinear \(G\)-action. Then the following
are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(G\) acts identically on the entire special-fibre scheme \(Y'_k\).
\item There is a projective, flat, finitely presented nodal
\(O\)-scheme \(Y\) of relative dimension one and a
\(G\)-equivariant isomorphism \(Y\times_O O'\simeq Y'\).
\end{enumerate}
The descent is unique with the specified equivariant identification.
For every relatively ample invertible sheaf \(\mathcal A'\) on
\(Y'\), its canonically linearized orbit tensor
\[
             \mathcal N'=\bigotimes_{j=0}^{e-1}(\sigma^j)^*\mathcal A'
 \tag{8}
\]
is the equivariant pullback of a relatively ample invertible
sheaf \(\mathcal N\) on \(Y\).
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Assume condition 1. The affine base \(\operatorname{Spec}O'\) has
an ample structure sheaf. By \mbox{\href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B45}{Stacks Tag 0B45, \((1)\Rightarrow(6)\)}}, the supplied
projective scheme \(Y'/O'\) is proper and has a relatively ample
invertible sheaf. Fix any such \(\mathcal A'\). Theorem~\ref{cand:f5glue:globaldescent}
supplies a separated, flat, finitely presented nodal \(O\)-scheme
\(Y\) with the required equivariant identification and descends
properness to \(Y/O\).

Apply Proposition~\ref{cand:f5proj:orbittensor} with
\(X=Y'\), \(S=\operatorname{Spec}O'\), and \(Z=Y'_k\).
It makes (8) relatively ample and gives a canonical
linearization whose restriction to the full special fibre is
the identity. Under the supplied identification
\(Y'=Y\times_O O'\), Proposition~\ref{cand:f5proj:linebundledescent}
therefore produces the invertible sheaf
\[
                            \mathcal N=(p_*\mathcal N')^G
\]
and its equivariant pullback identification; it also proves
that \(\mathcal N\) is relatively ample over \(O\).
The base \(\operatorname{Spec}O\) has the ample invertible
sheaf \(\mathcal O\). Applying \mbox{\href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B45}{Stacks Tag 0B45, \((6)\Rightarrow(1)\)}} to the proper
scheme \(Y/O\) with the descended relatively ample invertible sheaf
\(\mathcal N\) makes \(Y/O\) projective. The flatness, finite
presentation, nodality, relative dimension and uniqueness are
those of Theorem~\ref{cand:f5glue:globaldescent}.

Conversely, an equivariant presentation in condition 2 has
special fibre \(Y\times_O k\), with coefficient action.
That action is the identity because \(G\) acts identically
on \(k\). This proves condition 1.
\end{proof}
