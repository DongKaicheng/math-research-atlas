The matrix criterion for a Jacobian presentation

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 398

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The matrix criterion for a Jacobian presentation}{Jacobian 表示的矩阵判据}]
\label{fnd:jacobianmatrixcriterion}
\bi{Let $V$ be an affinoid domain of $\mathbb A_X^{n,\mathrm{an}}$, and let $W\hookrightarrow V$ be the closed immersion defined by the ideal $(f_1,\ldots,f_m)$. Write}
{设 $V$ 为 $\mathbb A_X^{n,\mathrm{an}}$ 的 affinoid 域，且 $W\hookrightarrow V$ 为理想 $(f_1,\ldots,f_m)$ 定义的闭浸入。写成}
\[
 df_i=\sum_{j=1}^n J_{ij}\,dT_j.
\]
\bi{For every $y\in W$,}
{对每个 $y\in W$，有}
\[
 \dim_{\mathcal H(y)}(\Omega_{W/X}\otimes\mathcal H(y))
          =n-\operatorname{rank}_{\mathcal H(y)}J(y).
\]
\bi{Consequently this presentation is Jacobian at $y$, with $m=n-\operatorname{rk}_y\Omega_{W/X}$, if and only if an $m\times m$ minor is nonzero at $y$. In that case $m$ is the minimum number of generators of the ideal. There is an affinoid neighborhood $V'\subseteq V$ of $y$ on which such a minor is invertible; on $W\cap V'$ the differential sheaf is free of rank $n-m$ and the restricted presentation is Jacobian at every point.}
{因此，此表示在 $y$ 处为 Jacobian 表示且 $m=n-\operatorname{rk}_y\Omega_{W/X}$，当且仅当某个 $m\times m$ 子式在 $y$ 处非零。这时 $m$ 是该理想的最少生成元个数。存在 $y$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $V'\subseteq V$，使此子式在其上可逆；在 $W\cap V'$ 上，微分层为秩 $n-m$ 的自由模，且限制表示在每一点都是 Jacobian 表示。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:analyticconormaldescriptions} gives a presentation $\mathcal O_W^m\xrightarrow{J}\mathcal O_W^n\to\Omega_{W/X}\to0$. Tensoring with $\mathcal H(y)$ is right exact, so elementary linear algebra gives the rank formula. Full row rank is equivalent to the existence of the indicated nonzero minor. If the same ideal had $q<m$ generators, the same cokernel computation would give $\operatorname{rk}_y\Omega_{W/X}\ge n-q>n-m$, a contradiction.}
{Proposition~\ref{fnd:analyticconormaldescriptions} 给出表示 $\mathcal O_W^m\xrightarrow{J}\mathcal O_W^n\to\Omega_{W/X}\to0$。与 $\mathcal H(y)$ 张量为右正合，因此基本线性代数给出秩公式。满行秩等价于存在所述非零子式。若同一个理想可由 $q<m$ 个元素生成，则相同的余核计算给出 $\operatorname{rk}_y\Omega_{W/X}\ge n-q>n-m$，产生矛盾。}

\bi{Let $\Delta$ be a nonzero minor at $y$ and choose $0<c<|\Delta(y)|$. The Laurent domain $V'=\{|\Delta|\ge c\}$ is a neighborhood of $y$, and $\Delta$ is a unit on it. Permuting the coordinate columns, its invertible block expresses the first $m$ basis vectors modulo the relations as linear combinations of the remaining $n-m$. Those remaining vectors are independent, since a relation among them would have zero first $m$ coordinates and invertibility forces every relation coefficient to vanish. They form a free basis of the cokernel. The same invertible minor at every point proves the final assertion. If $m=0$, take $\Delta=1$ and $V'=V$.}
{取在 $y$ 处非零的子式 $\Delta$，并选 $0<c<|\Delta(y)|$。Laurent 域 $V'=\{|\Delta|\ge c\}$ 是 $y$ 的邻域，且 $\Delta$ 在其上可逆。适当重排坐标列后，此可逆块使前 $m$ 个基向量模关系后表示为其余 $n-m$ 个基向量的线性组合。后者线性无关：若它们之间有关系，其前 $m$ 个坐标为零，可逆性迫使所有关系系数均为零。因此它们组成余核的自由基。同一个可逆子式在每一点均非零，证明最后一项。若 $m=0$，取 $\Delta=1$ 与 $V'=V$。}
\end{proof}
