Affinoid tests over a free extension

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 362

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Affinoid tests over a free extension}{自由扩张上的 affinoid 测试}]
\label{fnd:freefieldaffinoidtests}
\bi{Let $r$ be a finite tuple free over $K$, and put $L=K_r$. Every norm-admissible $L$-affinoid algebra $C$ is a norm-admissible generalized $K$-affinoid algebra. Its bounded multiplicative seminorms over $K$ are exactly those over $L$:}
{设有限元组 $r$ 在 $K$ 上自由，令 $L=K_r$。每个 norm-admissible $L$-affinoid 代数 $C$ 也是 norm-admissible generalized $K$-affinoid 代数。它在 $K$ 上的有界乘性半范数恰为它在 $L$ 上的这些半范数：}
\[
 \mathcal M_K(C)=\mathcal M_L(C).
\]
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:multilaurentactualpresentation} presents $L$ as a quotient of a generalized Tate algebra over $K$. Apply Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation} to a presentation $C=L\{s^{-1}Z\}/J$. It presents $L\{s^{-1}Z\}$ over $K$, and its further quotient by the closed ideal $J$ is again norm-admissible over $K$. Uniformly equivalent admissible norms give the same conclusion.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:multilaurentactualpresentation} 将 $L$ 表示为 $K$ 上一个 generalized Tate 代数的商。对表示 $C=L\{s^{-1}Z\}/J$ 应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation}：先得到 $L\{s^{-1}Z\}$ 在 $K$ 上的表示，再模闭理想 $J$ 取商，仍在 $K$ 上 norm-admissible。一致等价的 admissible 范数保持此结论。}

\bi{A bounded multiplicative seminorm $z$ on $C$ restricts along the bounded map $L\to C$ to a bounded multiplicative seminorm on the valued field $L$. For $a\in L^\times$, a bound $D$ gives $|a|_z^n\leq D|a|^n$ for all $n$, hence $|a|_z\leq|a|$. Applying the same argument to $a^{-1}$ gives the reverse inequality. Thus $z$ extends the specified absolute value of $L$. Conversely an $L$-point is a $K$-point. Both spectra have the topology generated by evaluation on the same algebra $C$, so the identification is topological as well. If $C=0$, both spaces are empty.}
{在 $C$ 上的有界乘性半范数 $z$，沿有界映射 $L\to C$ 限制为赋值域 $L$ 上的有界乘性半范数。对 $a\in L^\times$，某个有界常数 $D$ 对所有 $n$ 给出 $|a|_z^n\leq D|a|^n$，故 $|a|_z\leq|a|$。对 $a^{-1}$ 应用同一论证，得到反向不等式。因此 $z$ 延拓 $L$ 的指定绝对值。反之，$L$-谱点当然也是 $K$-谱点。两个谱的拓扑都由同一代数 $C$ 上的取值函数生成，因此此识别也保持拓扑。若 $C=0$，两个空间均为空。}
\end{proof}
