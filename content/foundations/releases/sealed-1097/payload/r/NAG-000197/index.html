Result 2.2.4

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 126

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}\label{fnd:localunit}
\bi{If $f(x)\ne0$ on an affinoid space, there is an ordinary neighborhood of $x$ on which $f$ is an analytic unit. On any analytic domain where $f$ is a unit, the function $-\log|f|$ is finite and continuous.}
{若 affinoid space 上 $f(x)\ne0$，则 $x$ 有一个 ordinary neighborhood，其上 $f$ 为 analytic unit。在 $f$ 为 unit 的任何 analytic domain 上，$-\log|f|$ 为 finite continuous function。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose $0<r<|f(x)|$. The Laurent domain $|f|\geq r$ contains the ordinary open neighborhood $|f|>r$ of $x$, and its defining algebra contains $f^{-1}$. Restrict that inverse to the open neighborhood. Continuity follows from continuity of evaluation and of the logarithm on positive real numbers.}
{取 $0<r<|f(x)|$。Laurent domain $|f|\geq r$ 包含 $x$ 的 ordinary open neighborhood $|f|>r$，且其 defining algebra 含有 $f^{-1}$。将 inverse 限制到该 open neighborhood 即可。Continuity 来自 evaluation 的 continuity 及 logarithm 在正实数上的 continuity。}
\end{proof}
