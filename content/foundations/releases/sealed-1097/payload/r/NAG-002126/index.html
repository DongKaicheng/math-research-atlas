A split marked model gives a punctured skeleton

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 718

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{A split marked model gives a punctured skeleton}
{分裂标记模型给出穿孔骨架}]
\label{cand:generalmarkedmodelskeleton}
\bi{Let $\mathcal C/O$ be a projective curve model satisfying
either the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{cand:splitmodelskeleton}
(finite separable residue fields at smooth closed points)
or those of Corollary~\ref{cand:charzerosplitmodelskeleton}
(characteristic-zero $K$, with arbitrary residue fields
at smooth closed points).
Let $P_1,\ldots,P_m$ be $K$-rational points of its
smooth generic fibre whose sections have distinct
smooth special points $q_1,\ldots,q_m$. Put}
{设射影曲线模型 $\mathcal C/O$ 或者满足
Theorem~\ref{cand:splitmodelskeleton} 的假设
（光滑闭点的剩余域有限可分），或者满足
Corollary~\ref{cand:charzerosplitmodelskeleton} 的假设
（$K$ 特征零，允许光滑闭点具有任意剩余域）。
设 $P_1,\ldots,P_m$ 为其光滑泛纤维上的 $K$-有理点，
相关截面具有互异的光滑特殊点 $q_1,\ldots,q_m$。
令}
\[
 U=(\mathcal C_K)^{\an}\setminus\{P_1,\ldots,P_m\}.
\]
\bi{Adjoin to the compact model skeleton one infinite
ray for every $P_i$, contained in $]q_i[$ and attached
to its component vertex. Their union
$\Sigma(\mathcal C;P_1,\ldots,P_m)\subset U$ is a finite
metric graph with finitely many infinite rays and is
a jointly continuous strong deformation retract of
$U$. The entire homotopy and all edge lengths are
invariant under isomorphisms of marked models,
including semilinear isometric isomorphisms.}
{对每个 $P_i$，向紧模型骨架添入一条位于 $]q_i[$
且附着到对应分量顶点的无限射线。
它们的并
$\Sigma(\mathcal C;P_1,\ldots,P_m)\subset U$
是带有限条无限射线的有限度量图，
并为 $U$ 的联合连续强形变收缩像。
整个同伦及全部边长在标记模型的同构下不变，
包括半线性等距同构。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The formal completion has generic fibre
$(\mathcal C_K)^{\an}$ by
Theorem~\ref{cand:projectiveformalcompletion}.
Each $q_i$ is rational because it is the special
point of an $O$-section. Its tube is a rational open
disc by Corollary~\ref{cand:smoothpointpolydisctube}.
Translate a disc coordinate by its value at $P_i$
to put that point at zero. Use
Lemma~\ref{cand:punctureddischomotopy} on the completed
tube with $P_i$ removed.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{cand:projectiveformalcompletion}，
形式完备化的泛纤维为 $(\mathcal C_K)^{\an}$。
每个 $q_i$ 是 $O$-截面的特殊点，故为有理点。
由 Corollary~\ref{cand:smoothpointpolydisctube}，
其管域为有理开圆盘。将圆盘坐标减去它在 $P_i$
处的值，把该点移到零。在删去 $P_i$ 的完备管域上
使用 Lemma~\ref{cand:punctureddischomotopy}。}

\bi{We justify the global pasting using a finite closed
cover. For every component vertex $v$, start with its
compact star $A_v$ from
Proposition~\ref{cand:compactcomponentstars}, using when necessary the
arbitrary-residue extension of
Corollary~\ref{cand:charzerosplitmodelskeleton}.
Remove from $A_v$ all the finitely many marked open
tubes incident to it; the resulting $A_v^0$ is closed
in $A_v$ and thus compact and closed in $U$.
The original star homotopy restricts to $A_v^0$.
Add the finitely many punctured completed marked
tubes $C_{q_i}\setminus\{P_i\}$, which are closed
in $U$, and the finitely many completed nodal tubes.
These sets cover $U$ and their intersections consist
of fixed vertices. Their local homotopies therefore
paste to a jointly continuous map on
$U\times[0,1]$ by the finite closed-cover pasting
lemma. This argument retains the uniform control of
all unmarked tubes already proved for each $A_v$.}
{通过有限闭覆盖验证全局粘合。对每个分量顶点 $v$，
从 Proposition~\ref{cand:compactcomponentstars}
的紧星形空间 $A_v$ 出发，并在需要时使用
Corollary~\ref{cand:charzerosplitmodelskeleton}
给出的任意剩余域推广。从 $A_v$ 删去与其相接的
有限个标记开管域；所得 $A_v^0$ 在 $A_v$ 中闭，
故紧且在 $U$ 中闭。原星形同伦限制到 $A_v^0$。
再添入有限个在 $U$ 中闭的穿孔完备标记管域
$C_{q_i}\setminus\{P_i\}$，以及有限个完备结点
管域。这些集合覆盖 $U$，交集只包含固定顶点。
由有限闭覆盖粘合引理，局部同伦粘合成
$U\times[0,1]$ 上的联合连续映射。
此论证保留了已对各 $A_v$ 证明的全部未标记
管域的一致控制。}

\bi{At time one, unmarked stars map to their vertices,
nodal tubes to their compact Gauss edges, and each
marked punctured tube to its ray. Every point of this
union is fixed at every time. It is therefore the
strong retraction image. The graph topology is the
one induced from $U$: its compact core and each of
its finitely many rays are closed subspaces, and
they meet only at their attached endpoints.
On a ray this follows from
Lemma~\ref{cand:punctureddischomotopy}; on the compact
core it is Theorem~\ref{cand:splitmodelskeleton}.
For a finite closed cover these subspace topologies
determine the topology on their union. The length
coordinate on each ray is $-\log|T|$, and the compact
edge lengths are the previously established annular
lengths. The local homotopy invariances give all the
asserted functoriality.}
{在时刻一，未标记星形空间映到其顶点，结点管域
映到其紧 Gauss 边，每个标记穿孔管域映到其射线。
此并集的每一点在每个时刻固定，故它就是强收缩像。
图拓扑恰为从 $U$ 诱导的拓扑：紧核及有限条射线
都是闭子空间，且只在各自附着端点相交。
射线部分来自
Lemma~\ref{cand:punctureddischomotopy}，
紧核部分来自 Theorem~\ref{cand:splitmodelskeleton}。
有限闭覆盖的各子空间拓扑决定其并集的拓扑。
每条射线的长度坐标为 $-\log|T|$，紧边长度则为
已建立的圆环长度。各局部同伦的不变性给出全部
所述函子性。}
\end{proof}
