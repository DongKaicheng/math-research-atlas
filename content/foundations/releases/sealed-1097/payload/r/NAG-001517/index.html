Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Strict Gerritzen--Grauert}{Strict Gerritzen--Grauert 定理}]\label{fnd:strictgerritzengrauert}
\bi{Let $K$ be complete and nontrivially valued. Let $\varphi:A\to B$ be a strict-test affinoid-domain presentation of $U\subset\mathcal M(A)$. There is a finite rational cover $\mathcal M(A)=\bigcup_iR_i$ such that $U\cap R_i$ is a Weierstrass domain in $R_i$, represented by $B_i=B\widehat\otimes_AA_i$, where $A_i$ is the rational algebra of $R_i$. In particular, $U$ is a finite union of rational domains of $\mathcal M(A)$. These are assertions about all Berkovich points, with bounded algebra identifications and the fixed absolute value on $K$.}
{设 $K$ 完备且非平凡赋值，$\varphi:A\to B$ 是 $U\subset\mathcal M(A)$ 的 strict-test affinoid-domain 表示。则存在有限有理覆盖 $\mathcal M(A)=\bigcup_iR_i$，使 $U\cap R_i$ 是 $R_i$ 中的 Weierstrass 域，并由 $B_i=B\widehat\otimes_AA_i$ 表示，其中 $A_i$ 为 $R_i$ 的有理代数。特别地，$U$ 是 $\mathcal M(A)$ 中有限多个有理域的并。这些结论涉及全部 Berkovich 点、相应的有界代数识别以及 $K$ 上固定的绝对值。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{As observed after Definition~\ref{fnd:stricttestaffinoiddomain}, $\varphi$ is a strict-affinoid epimorphism. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictgerritzengrauertlocaldense} supplies a finite rational cover for which every $A_i\to B_i$ has dense image. This map is again a strict-test domain presentation of $U\cap R_i$. Indeed, Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology} identifies $\mathcal M(B_i)$ with the inverse image of $R_i$ in $\mathcal M(B)$. If a strict test map from $A_i$ has image in $U\cap R_i$, its restriction from $A$ factors uniquely through $B$; the two compatible maps from $B$ and $A_i$ then give the required unique map from the completed pushout $B_i$.}
{如 Definition~\ref{fnd:stricttestaffinoiddomain} 后所述，$\varphi$ 是 strict-affinoid 上态。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictgerritzengrauertlocaldense}，存在有限有理覆盖，使每个 $A_i\to B_i$ 的像稠密。这个映射仍是 $U\cap R_i$ 的 strict-test domain 表示。事实上，Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology} 将 $\mathcal M(B_i)$ 识别为 $\mathcal M(B)$ 中 $R_i$ 的原像。若一个从 $A_i$ 出发的 strict 测试映射的谱像位于 $U\cap R_i$，则其从 $A$ 出发的限制唯一通过 $B$ 分解；来自 $B$ 与 $A_i$ 的两个相容映射，再从完备推出 $B_i$ 给出所需的唯一映射。}

\bi{Apply Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictdenserunge} to factor the dense map as}
{应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictdenserunge}，将该稠密映射分解为}
\[
 A_i\longrightarrow C_i
   =A_i\langle S_1,\ldots,S_t\rangle/(S_j-a_j)
 \twoheadrightarrow B_i,
 \qquad a_j\in A_i.
\]
\bi{The first map represents a Weierstrass domain $W_i\subset R_i$, and the second map is surjective. The latter is itself a strict-test domain presentation. To check its factorization property, take a bounded map $C_i\to E$, where $E$ is a strict test algebra, whose spectrum image lies in $\mathcal M(B_i)\subset\mathcal M(C_i)$. Composing with $A_i$ gives a map with image in $U\cap R_i$, hence a unique map $B_i\to E$. Its composite from $C_i$ equals the original map because both extend the same map from $A_i$ through the rational domain $C_i$, whose bounded factorization is unique by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaldomainup}. The point identification follows from the already established identifications into $R_i$.}
{第一个映射表示 Weierstrass 域 $W_i\subset R_i$，第二个映射为满射。后者本身也是 strict-test domain 表示。为检查其分解性质，取到 strict 测试代数的有界映射 $C_i\to E$，其谱像位于 $\mathcal M(B_i)\subset\mathcal M(C_i)$。与 $A_i$ 复合后，所得映射的像位于 $U\cap R_i$，故唯一给出映射 $B_i\to E$。它从 $C_i$ 出发的复合等于原映射，因为二者通过有理域 $C_i$ 延拓同一个从 $A_i$ 出发的映射；由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaldomainup}，这种有界分解唯一。点集识别则来自已经建立的到 $R_i$ 的识别。}

\bi{By Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictcloseddomainweierstrass}, the map $C_i\twoheadrightarrow B_i$ is a Weierstrass-domain map. It remains to retain the stronger conclusion that its composite with $A_i\to C_i$ is Weierstrass, not merely rational. Let $e\in C_i$ be the kernel idempotent. The image of $A_i$ is dense in $C_i$, because polynomials in the adjoined variables reduce to elements of $A_i$. Choose $a\in A_i$ such that $\|a-e\|_{C_i}<|c|^{-1}$ for some $c\in K$ with $|c|>1$. Since $|e(x)|$ is zero or one, the ultrametric inequality gives, for every $x\in\mathcal M(C_i)$,}
{由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictcloseddomainweierstrass}，映射 $C_i\twoheadrightarrow B_i$ 是 Weierstrass-domain 映射。还需保留更强的结论：它与 $A_i\to C_i$ 的复合为 Weierstrass，而不只是有理。令 $e\in C_i$ 为生成核的幂等元。$A_i$ 在 $C_i$ 中的像稠密，因为所添变量的多项式都可化为 $A_i$ 中的元素。取某个 $c\in K$ 满足 $|c|>1$，再选取 $a\in A_i$ 使 $\|a-e\|_{C_i}<|c|^{-1}$。由于 $|e(x)|$ 只能为零或一，超度量不等式给出每个 $x\in\mathcal M(C_i)$ 都满足}
\[
 |ca(x)|\leq1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad e(x)=0.
\]
\bi{Consequently $U\cap R_i$ is the Weierstrass subset of $R_i$ defined by the original inequalities $|a_j|\leq1$ together with $|ca|\leq1$. Its standard strict rational algebra and $B_i$ satisfy the same strict-test universal property, so they are uniquely boundedly isomorphic. Finally, each of these Weierstrass subsets is rational in $R_i$; rational transitivity makes it rational in $\mathcal M(A)$. Their finite union is $U$ because the $R_i$ cover the entire spectrum.}
{因此，$U\cap R_i$ 是由原有不等式 $|a_j|\leq1$ 及附加不等式 $|ca|\leq1$ 定义的 $R_i$ 中的 Weierstrass 子集。其标准 strict 有理代数与 $B_i$ 满足相同的 strict-test 泛性质，故存在唯一的有界同构。最后，每个这样的 Weierstrass 子集都在 $R_i$ 中有理；由有理传递性，它在 $\mathcal M(A)$ 中也有理。由于各个 $R_i$ 覆盖整个谱，这些子集的有限并恰为 $U$。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{We now use the relative Weierstrass results to obtain a finite rational description of affinoid domains. The argument first treats bounded categorical epimorphisms of strict affinoid algebras. It produces rational pieces on which the image is dense. The domain universal property then removes the closed equations from the resulting Weierstrass factorization. Except in the following definition and its immediate observations, $K$ is complete and its fixed absolute value is nontrivial. All algebra homomorphisms are unital and bounded.}
{现在利用相对 Weierstrass 结果，为 affinoid domains 建立有限有理描述。首先处理 strict affinoid 代数的有界范畴上态，得到其像稠密的有理块；再使用 domain 泛性质，消去所得 Weierstrass 分解中的闭方程。除紧接着的定义及其直接说明外，本节中 $K$ 完备，且给定绝对值非平凡。所有代数同态均保单位且有界。}

\begin{definition}\label{fnd:stricttestaffinoiddomain}
\bi{For any complete non-Archimedean field $K$, including trivial valuation, let $A$ and $B$ be strict $K$-affinoid algebras. A \emph{strict-test affinoid-domain presentation} of a subset $U\subset\mathcal M(A)$ is a bounded map $\varphi:A\to B$ such that the map $\mathcal M(B)\to\mathcal M(A)$ identifies its points bijectively with $U$, and every bounded $K$-algebra map $A\to C$, with $C$ a strict $K$-affinoid and with spectrum image contained in $U$, factors uniquely through a bounded map $B\to C$. The zero algebra represents the empty subset.}
{对任意完备非阿基米德域 $K$，允许赋值平凡，设 $A,B$ 为 strict $K$-affinoid 代数。子集 $U\subset\mathcal M(A)$ 的一个 \emph{strict-test affinoid-domain 表示} 是有界映射 $\varphi:A\to B$，满足：映射 $\mathcal M(B)\to\mathcal M(A)$ 将其点与 $U$ 双射识别；且对每个 strict $K$-affinoid 代数 $C$，每个谱像包含于 $U$ 的有界 $K$-代数同态 $A\to C$ 都唯一通过某个有界映射 $B\to C$ 分解。零代数表示空子集。}
Standing context 3 · exact TeX
\bi{The empty-set assertion follows from Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}, which says that a strict algebra with empty spectrum is zero. Every such presentation is a strict-affinoid epimorphism in the sense of Definition~\ref{fnd:strictcategoricalepimorphism}: two maps from $B$ agreeing on $A$ are two factorizations of the same map into a strict test algebra. Also, the point identification is a homeomorphism onto $U$, since the source spectrum is compact and the target spectrum is Hausdorff by Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact}.}
{空集断言来自 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}：谱为空的 strict 代数必为零。每个这样的表示都是 Definition~\ref{fnd:strictcategoricalepimorphism} 意义下的 strict-affinoid 上态：从 $B$ 出发且在 $A$ 上相同的两个映射，正是同一个到 strict 测试代数的映射的两个分解。此外，由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact}，源谱紧而目标谱 Hausdorff，所以点集识别是到 $U$ 的同胚。}
