4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Separating marked sections}
{分离标记截面}]
\label{cand:separatemarkedsectionsdvr}
\bi{Let $\mathcal Y/R$ be a regular split nodal model and
let $P_1,\ldots,P_m$ be distinct $F$-rational points of
its generic fibre. Their closures are sections meeting
the smooth locus. Blowing up, at each stage, the reduced
union of points at which at least two marked sections
meet terminates in a regular split nodal model on which
the marked sections are disjoint. Projectivity is
preserved. If a finite semilinear group acts on the
model and permutes the marked generic points, it acts
on every stage and on the final marked model.}
{设 $\mathcal Y/R$ 为正则分裂结点模型，
$P_1,\ldots,P_m$ 为其泛纤维上互异的 $F$-有理点。
它们的闭包是经过光滑轨迹的截面。每一步沿至少两个
标记截面相交的点的约化并作 blowup；此过程有限步
后终止，得到各标记截面两两不交的正则分裂结点
模型。射影性保持。若有限半线性群作用于原模型并
置换标记泛点，则它作用于每一阶段及最终标记模型。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Properness extends each $P_i$ uniquely to a section
by \cite[Tag 0BX5]{StacksAlg}. Its special point is
$l$-rational. At a split node the regular completed
local equation is $xy=\epsilon\varpi$, with $\epsilon$
a unit, by the preceding local computation.
A section specializing to the node would give
$x,y\in\varpi R$, contradicting
$xy=\epsilon\varpi$. One may evaluate this completed
equation because a section through the point induces
a continuous local map to the complete ring $R$.
Consequently every marked section meets the smooth locus.}
{由 properness 与 \cite[Tag 0BX5]{StacksAlg}，
每个 $P_i$ 唯一延拓为截面。其特殊点是 $l$-有理点。
由上一局部计算，分裂结点处的正则完成局部方程为
$xy=\epsilon\varpi$，其中 $\epsilon$ 为单位元。
若截面特化到该结点，则 $x,y\in\varpi R$，与
$xy=\epsilon\varpi$ 矛盾。这里可以代入完成局部
方程，因为经过该点的截面诱导到完备环 $R$ 的连续
局部映射。因此每个标记截面都经过光滑轨迹。}

\bi{At a collision point choose a smooth \'etale coordinate
$T$ reducing to zero. The completed local ring is
$R[[T]]$, by the smooth pointed chart and
\cite[Tag 0AGX]{StacksAlg}. A section through this point
has $T=a_i\in\varpi R$. Distinct sections have distinct
$a_i$: the rational smooth-tube isomorphism of
Corollary~\ref{cand:smoothpointpolydisctube} is injective
on their generic analytic points.
For two sections with the same special point set
$c_{ij}=\operatorname{ord}_{\varpi}(a_i-a_j)$, and set
$c_{ij}=0$ if their special points differ.
This nonnegative integer is coordinate independent.
Indeed a change of smooth coordinate in $R[[T]]$
has the form $g(T)=b_0+b_1T+\cdots$, with
$b_0\in\varpi R$, $b_1\in R^\times$, and $b_n\in R$.
For $a_i,a_j\in\varpi R$ the quotient
$(g(a_i)-g(a_j))/(a_i-a_j)$ is a unit.}
{在交点选择约化为零的光滑 \'etale 坐标 $T$。
由光滑带点坐标图及 \cite[Tag 0AGX]{StacksAlg}，
完成局部环为 $R[[T]]$。经过该点的截面满足
$T=a_i\in\varpi R$。互异截面的 $a_i$ 不同：
Corollary~\ref{cand:smoothpointpolydisctube}
中的有理光滑管域同构在这些泛纤维解析点上单射。
对特殊点相同的两个截面，令
$c_{ij}=\operatorname{ord}_{\varpi}(a_i-a_j)$；
特殊点不同时令 $c_{ij}=0$。这个非负整数不依赖坐标。
事实上，$R[[T]]$ 中的光滑坐标变换形如
$g(T)=b_0+b_1T+\cdots$，其中
$b_0\in\varpi R$、$b_1\in R^\times$、$b_n\in R$。
对 $a_i,a_j\in\varpi R$，商
$(g(a_i)-g(a_j))/(a_i-a_j)$ 是单位元。}

\bi{Blow up the collision point, whose ideal is
$(\varpi,T)$. By \cite[Tag 0805]{StacksAlg} its two
local charts are the pullbacks of}
{对理想为 $(\varpi,T)$ 的交点作 blowup。
由 \cite[Tag 0805]{StacksAlg}，其两个局部坐标片
是以下坐标片的拉回：}
\[
 \operatorname{Spec}R[U],\quad T=\varpi U;
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Spec}R[T,V]/(TV-\varpi).
\]
\bi{They are regular, and their special fibres are smooth
or split nodal. Every marked section through the center
lifts to the first chart, where $U=a_i/\varpi$.
Existence and uniqueness also follow from the blowup
universal property \cite[Tag 0806]{StacksAlg}, since
the pulled-back center ideal is $(\varpi)$.
Thus marked sections remain smooth. For every pair
meeting at the center, the new contact number is exactly
$c_{ij}-1$; this includes $c_{ij}=1$, when their new
special points are distinct. All other contact numbers
are unchanged. The finite sum $\sum_{i<j}c_{ij}$ strictly
decreases whenever a collision remains. It proves
termination with disjoint sections.}
{这两个坐标片均正则，其特殊纤维光滑或分裂结点型。
每个经过中心的标记截面都提升到第一坐标片，满足
$U=a_i/\varpi$。由于中心理想拉回为 $(\varpi)$，
存在性与唯一性也来自 blowup 泛性质
\cite[Tag 0806]{StacksAlg}。因此标记截面仍经过
光滑点。每个在中心相交的截面对，其新接触数恰为
$c_{ij}-1$；这也包括 $c_{ij}=1$ 的情形，此时新的
特殊点不同。其余接触数不变。只要尚有交点，
有限和 $\sum_{i<j}c_{ij}$ 就严格下降，
从而证明最终截面两两不交。}

\bi{The collision locus is a finite set of rational smooth
points, invariant under any automorphism permuting the
marked sections. Blowup of its reduced union therefore
lifts the group action by the Rees-algebra argument in
Lemma~\ref{cand:equivariantnodaldvrresolution}.
Projectivity follows again from
\cite[Tags 02NS, 0C4P]{StacksAlg}.
The generic marked points remain unchanged throughout,
and uniqueness of their extensions keeps their labels
and their permutation action compatible.}
{交点轨迹为有限个有理光滑点组成的集合，并在每个
置换标记截面的自同构下不变。因此，由
Lemma~\ref{cand:equivariantnodaldvrresolution}
中的 Rees 代数论证，对其约化并作 blowup 可提升
群作用。射影性同样来自
\cite[Tags 02NS, 0C4P]{StacksAlg}。
整个过程中标记泛点不变，而其延拓的唯一性
保证标记及其置换作用始终相容。}
\end{proof}
