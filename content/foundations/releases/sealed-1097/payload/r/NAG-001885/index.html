The valuation space of punctured affine space

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 608

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The valuation space of punctured affine space}
{去掉原点的仿射空间的赋值空间}]
\label{fnd:puncturedaffinevaluationsnonaffine}
\bi{Let $k$ be a field, let $n\ge2$, and put
$R=k[t_1,\ldots,t_n]$, $F=\operatorname{Frac}(R)$.
Inside the space of valuation rings of $F$ containing $k$, set}
{设 $k$ 为域，$n\ge2$，并记
$R=k[t_1,\ldots,t_n]$、$F=\operatorname{Frac}(R)$。
在 $F$ 中包含 $k$ 的赋值环空间内，置}
\[
 Q=\bigcup_{i=1}^n
       \{V:R[t_i^{-1}]\subset V\}.
\]
\bi{Then $Q$ is not affine as a birational space. In fact,
$\bigcap_{V\in Q}V=R$, but $Q$ does not contain every valuation
ring of $F$ containing $R$.}
{则 $Q$ 作为 birational 空间并不仿射。更精确地，
$\bigcap_{V\in Q}V=R$，但 $Q$ 不包含 $F$ 中所有包含 $R$
的赋值环。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Regard $k$ as a complete trivially valued field. Its strict
Tate algebra in $n$ variables is exactly $R$: a convergent
series has only finitely many nonzero coefficients.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:stricttatefactorial} therefore proves that
$R$ is a unique factorization domain. If $h\in F\setminus R$,
write it as a reduced fraction and choose an irreducible
factor $P$ of its denominator. The exponent of $P$ in a
nonzero fraction defines a discrete valuation $v_P$ of $F$;
its valuation ring $V_P$ contains $R$ and does not contain $h$.
The irreducible $P$ cannot divide both $t_1$ and $t_2$.
Hence some $t_i$ is a unit of $V_P$, and $V_P\in Q$.
Every member of $Q$ contains $R$, so this proves}
{将 $k$ 视为完备的平凡赋值域。其 $n$ 元 strict Tate 代数
恰为 $R$，因为收敛级数只能有有限个非零系数。因此
Theorem~\ref{fnd:stricttatefactorial} 证明 $R$ 是唯一分解整环。
若 $h\in F\setminus R$，将其写成既约分式，并取分母的一个
不可约因子 $P$。非零分式中 $P$ 的指数给出 $F$ 上的离散赋值
$v_P$；其赋值环 $V_P$ 包含 $R$ 而不包含 $h$。
不可约元 $P$ 不可能同时整除 $t_1$ 与 $t_2$，所以某个
$t_i$ 在 $V_P$ 中可逆，从而 $V_P\in Q$。又因为 $Q$ 的每个
成员都包含 $R$，故}
\[
                         \bigcap_{V\in Q}V=R.
\]

\bi{For a nonzero polynomial $f$, let $w(f)$ be the least total
degree of a nonzero homogeneous component of $f$.
The product of the lowest homogeneous components is nonzero,
so $w(fg)=w(f)+w(g)$, and
$w(f+g)\ge\min(w(f),w(g))$ whenever the sum is nonzero.
Thus $w$ extends by subtraction to a valuation of $F$.
Its valuation ring $V_0$ contains $R$, while
$w(t_i)=1$ for every $i$. Consequently $V_0\notin Q$.}
{对非零多项式 $f$，以 $w(f)$ 记其非零齐次分量的最低总次数。
最低齐次分量的乘积非零，所以
$w(fg)=w(f)+w(g)$，且当 $f+g\ne0$ 时
$w(f+g)\ge\min(w(f),w(g))$。因此，通过取差，$w$ 延伸为
$F$ 上的赋值。其赋值环 $V_0$ 包含 $R$，而每个
$w(t_i)=1$，故 $V_0\notin Q$。}

\bi{If $Q$ were affine, its inclusion in the fixed valuation
space would identify it with
$\{V:S\subset V\}$ for a finitely generated $k$-subalgebra
$S\subset F$. Every element of $S$ would belong to
$\bigcap_{V\in Q}V=R$. But then $S\subset R\subset V_0$,
which would force $V_0\in Q$, a contradiction.}
{若 $Q$ 仿射，其在固定赋值空间中的嵌入将它识别为
$\{V:S\subset V\}$，其中 $S\subset F$ 是有限生成的
$k$-子代数。此时 $S$ 的每个元素均属于
$\bigcap_{V\in Q}V=R$。于是 $S\subset R\subset V_0$，
迫使 $V_0\in Q$，矛盾。}
\end{proof}
