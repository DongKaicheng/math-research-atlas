Ordinary regular maps at quasi-smooth points in characteristic zero

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 334

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Ordinary regular maps at quasi-smooth points in characteristic zero}{特征零准光滑点处的通常正则同态}]
\label{fnd:charzeroquasismoothordinaryregular}
\bi{Let $K$ be any complete field of characteristic zero, let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good $K$-analytic spaces, and let $f$ be quasi-smooth at $y$, with $x=f(y)$. Then the ordinary local homomorphism}
{设 $K$ 为任意特征零完备域，$f:Y\to X$ 为 good $K$-解析空间之间的态射，且 $f$ 在 $y$ 处准光滑，记 $x=f(y)$。则通常局部同态}
\[
                       \mathcal O_{X,x}\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{Y,y}
\]
\bi{is regular.}
{为正则同态。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ and $S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$. These rings are Noetherian, and the homomorphism is flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion}. We prove regularity of its fibre at every prime $\mathfrak P\subset R$. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization}, after an ordinary affinoid restriction of $X$, the domain $C=R/\mathfrak P$ has a realization}
{记 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$。这些环均 Noetherian，且该同态由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion} 平坦。下面对每个素理想 $\mathfrak P\subset R$ 证明其纤维正则。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization}，在对 $X$ 作普通 affinoid 限制后，整环 $C=R/\mathfrak P$ 可实现为}
\[
                 B\lhook\joinrel\longrightarrow
                 C=\mathcal O_{Z,z},\qquad Z=\mathcal M(B)\longrightarrow X
\]
\bi{with $B$ integral and $Z\to X$ finite. There is a unique point $z$ above $x$. The base change $T=Y\times_X Z\to Y$ is finite. List its finitely many points $t_i$ above $y$, and write $S_i=\mathcal O_{T,t_i}$. Finite branch separation gives}
{其中 $B$ 整且 $Z\to X$ 有限，$x$ 上方恰有一个点 $z$。基变换 $T=Y\times_X Z\to Y$ 有限。列出 $y$ 上方的有限个点 $t_i$，记 $S_i=\mathcal O_{T,t_i}$。有限分支分离给出}
\[
           S\otimes_R\operatorname{Frac}(C)
              \simeq
           \left(\prod_i S_i\right)\otimes_C\operatorname{Frac}(C).
\]
\bi{The map $T\to Z$ is quasi-smooth at each $t_i$, by the Jacobian base-change theorem. Choose an ordinary affinoid neighborhood $T_i=\mathcal M(D_i)$ of each $t_i$ on which this morphism is quasi-smooth everywhere. Such a neighborhood exists by the strengthened ordinary-neighborhood presentation in Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion}: the equations generate on that neighborhood, and a nonzero Jacobian minor stays invertible on a smaller rational neighborhood.}
{由 Jacobian 基变换定理，映射 $T\to Z$ 在每个 $t_i$ 处准光滑。为每个 $t_i$ 选择普通 affinoid 邻域 $T_i=\mathcal M(D_i)$，使该态射在其上处处准光滑。这由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion} 中加强的普通邻域表示保证：方程在该邻域上生成理想，再缩到较小 rational 邻域，可使非零 Jacobian 子式处处可逆。}

\bi{Apply Proposition~\ref{fnd:charzerouniformregularopen} to $B$, obtaining $0\ne a\in B$ such that, after every complete scalar extension, the ordinary local rings of $Z$ are regular at all points where $a$ is nonzero. We claim first that every ordinary local ring of $T_i$ at a point $v$ with $|a(v)|>0$ is regular. Choose a nontrivially valued complete field $\Omega\supset\mathcal H(v)$ and the rational lift $w$ of $v$ in $(T_i)_\Omega$. Its image $u$ in $Z_\Omega$ is rational and has $|a(u)|>0$. Hence $R'=\mathcal O_{Z_\Omega,u}$ is regular. The lifted morphism is quasi-smooth at $w$, and Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationaljacobiancompletion} gives}
{将 Proposition~\ref{fnd:charzerouniformregularopen} 用于 $B$，得到 $0\ne a\in B$，使任意完备扩标量后，$Z$ 中所有 $a$ 非零的点处的通常局部环均正则。首先证明：$T_i$ 中每个满足 $|a(v)|>0$ 的点 $v$ 处的通常局部环都正则。选择赋值非平凡的完备域 $\Omega\supset\mathcal H(v)$，并取 $(T_i)_\Omega$ 中 $v$ 的有理提升 $w$。其在 $Z_\Omega$ 中的像 $u$ 也为有理点，且 $|a(u)|>0$，所以 $R'=\mathcal O_{Z_\Omega,u}$ 正则。提升后的态射在 $w$ 准光滑，由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationaljacobiancompletion}，}
\[
            \widehat{\mathcal O_{(T_i)_\Omega,w}}
                         \simeq \widehat{R'}[[V_1,\ldots,V_d]].
\]
\bi{The right side is regular. Indeed, $\widehat{R'}$ is regular by Tag 07NY; its power-series extension is flat by the completion argument in that proposition, with regular closed fibre $\kappa(u)[[V_1,\ldots,V_d]]$ by Tag 032C. Tag 031E gives regularity of the power-series ring. Tag 07NY then makes the local ring at $w$ regular, and the faithfully flat scalar-stalk map descends regularity to $\mathcal O_{T_i,v}$ by Tag 00OF. This proves the claim.}
{右侧正则。事实上，$\widehat{R'}$ 由 Tag 07NY 正则；根据所引 proposition 中的完备化论证，其幂级数扩张平坦，而闭纤维 $\kappa(u)[[V_1,\ldots,V_d]]$ 由 Tag 032C 正则，所以 Tag 031E 给出该幂级数环正则。再由 Tag 07NY，$w$ 处的局部环正则；通过忠实平坦的基域茎映射，Tag 00OF 将正则性下降到 $\mathcal O_{T_i,v}$。断言得证。}

\bi{Every prime of $D_i$ avoiding $a$ is the kernel of an analytic point, by Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity}. At that point the ordinary local ring is regular by the claim. Its faithfully flat local map from the corresponding localization of $D_i$ descends regularity. Thus $D_i[1/a]$ is regular. Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroregularcomparison} makes $(D_i)_{\ker(t_i)}\to S_i$ regular. For any prime of $S_i$ avoiding $a$, its contracted localization in $D_i$ is regular, and the local map has regular closed fibre. Tag 031E therefore gives regularity of every such localization of $S_i$. Hence $S_i[1/a]$ is regular.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity}，$D_i$ 的每个避开 $a$ 的素理想都是某个解析点的核。刚才的断言说明该点处的通常局部环正则；从对应 $D_i$ 的局部化到该茎的忠实平坦局部映射，使正则性下降。因此 $D_i[1/a]$ 正则。Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroregularcomparison} 说明 $(D_i)_{\ker(t_i)}\to S_i$ 正则。对 $S_i$ 中任意避开 $a$ 的素理想，其在 $D_i$ 中收缩所对应的局部化正则，且该局部映射的闭纤维正则。Tag 031E 因而给出 $S_i$ 在该素理想处正则，所以 $S_i[1/a]$ 正则。}

\bi{Finally $a$ stays nonzero in $C$, since $B\hookrightarrow C$. The displayed algebraic fibre is therefore a localization of the finite product of regular rings $\prod_i S_i[1/a]$, and is regular. Its ground field $\operatorname{Frac}(C)$ has characteristic zero, so Tag 0381 makes it geometrically regular. Since $\mathfrak P$ was arbitrary, all algebraic fibres of the flat homomorphism $R\to S$ are geometrically regular.}
{最后，由于 $B\hookrightarrow C$，$a$ 在 $C$ 中仍非零。因此所列代数纤维是有限个正则环的乘积 $\prod_iS_i[1/a]$ 的局部化，故正则。其底域 $\operatorname{Frac}(C)$ 的特征为零，由 Tag 0381 得几何正则性。因为 $\mathfrak P$ 任意，平坦同态 $R\to S$ 的全部代数纤维都几何正则。}
\end{proof}
