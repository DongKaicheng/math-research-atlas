Finite change of calibration

C Native scalar extension and finite restriction of constants

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1766

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Finite change of calibration]
\label{f9cand:finite-calibration}
Let $L/K$ be finite of degree $n$. If $Y$ is good Hausdorff and pure
of dimension $d$ over $L$, $\mathcal G$ is coherent, and
$\beta\in\A^{d,d}_{Y/L,c}(Y)$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:calibration}
       \int_{Y_{[K]}/K,\mathcal G}\beta
                   =n\int_{Y/L,\mathcal G}\beta .
\end{equation}
For $Y=X_L$ and $\mathcal G=\F_L$, this gives
\eqref{eq:restricted}. The factors multiply correctly in finite towers.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
First let $Y=\M_L(B)$ be affinoid with one unit tuple $f$, and let
$N$ be the finite $B$-module representing $\mathcal G$.
By Lemma~\ref{f9cand:constants}, its actual level algebra $D_w$,
module $P=N\otimes_B D_w$ on that level, real moment image and tangent lattice
are identical in the two field interpretations. Choose a common
subdivision avoiding both boundary images at generic top-cell points,
as in Proposition~\ref{f9cand:chain}. There $D_w$ and $P$ are finite
canonical over $R_{L,r}$. They are also finite canonical over
$R_{K,r}$ by the finite free coefficient decomposition and composed
finite presentations. Lemma~\ref{f9cand:finite-gauss} gives
\[
 P\otimes_{R_{K,r}}H_{K,r}
    =P\otimes_{R_{L,r}}H_{L,r}
\]
as the same module with different scalar fields. Its dimension over
$H_{K,r}$ is $n$ times its dimension over $H_{L,r}$.
Dividing by the same lattice index proves
$\T_f^K(N)=n\T_f^L(N)$, using the \hyperref[fnd:nag002405:global-finite-module-chain-weights]{closed finite-module chains}.
Integrating by the \hyperref[fnd:nag002405:finite-cell-functional]{finite-cell functional}
and then applying the identical finite covers and
\hyperref[v037:integration:compactintegral]{inclusion--exclusion formula}
proves \eqref{eq:calibration} for the
\hyperref[v037:forms:compactintegral]{ordinary compact integrals}.

There is also a direct check of \eqref{eq:restricted}.
The basis of $L/K$ gives
\[
 p_*p^*\F\simeq \F^{\oplus n}
\]
on every full affinoid inverse image, compatibly with restriction.
The map $p:(X_L)_{[K]}\to X$ is finite with its canonical module norm.
The spaces have the required purity and Hausdorffness by the preceding
lemmas. Thus the exact finite projection theorem [\ref{v037:forms:finiteprojection}]
and coherent additivity give
\[
 \int_{(X_L)_{[K]}/K,\F_L}p^*\alpha
      =\int_{X/K,p_*\F_L}\alpha
      =n\int_{X/K,\F}\alpha .
\]
This uses the actual coherent direct image [\ref{fnd:finitecoherentdirectimagebasechange}], with
no local-freeness assumption on $\F$. Finite tower factors multiply
because $[E:L][L:K]=[E:K]$, while Theorem~\ref{f9cand:native-global}
has factor one in every tower.
\end{proof}
