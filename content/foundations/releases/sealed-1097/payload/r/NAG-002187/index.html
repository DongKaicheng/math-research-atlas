The index along a facet

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 874

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The index along a facet}{沿余维一面的格指数}]
\label{cand:facetindexidentity}
\bi{Let $f:N\to N'$ be a lattice homomorphism, and let
$\tau\prec\sigma$ be rational polyhedra of dimensions $d-1,d$.
Suppose $f$ is injective on $N_{\sigma,\mathbf R}$ and that
$\tau'=f(\tau)$ is a facet of $\sigma'=f(\sigma)$. Put}
{设 $f:N\to N'$ 为格同态，$\tau\prec\sigma$ 是维数分别为
$d-1,d$ 的有理多面体。假设 $f$ 在 $N_{\sigma,\mathbf R}$
上单射，且 $\tau'=f(\tau)$ 为 $\sigma'=f(\sigma)$ 的余维一面。
置}
\[
 a_\sigma=[N'_{\sigma'}:f(N_\sigma)],\qquad
 a_\tau=[N'_{\tau'}:f(N_\tau)].
\]
\bi{There is a positive integer $m_{\sigma/\tau}$ such that}
{存在正整数 $m_{\sigma/\tau}$ 使}
\[
 f(u_{\sigma/\tau})=m_{\sigma/\tau}u_{\sigma'/\tau'}
       \quad\bmod N'_{\tau'},\qquad
 a_\sigma=a_\tau m_{\sigma/\tau}.
\]
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Injectivity and saturation give
$f(N_\sigma)\cap N'_{\tau'}=f(N_\tau)$: the inverse image of
$N'_{\tau',\mathbf R}$ inside $N_{\sigma,\mathbf R}$ is
$N_{\tau,\mathbf R}$. The image of the primitive outward
generator in the rank-one quotient is a positive integer
$m_{\sigma/\tau}$ times the target generator. The finite groups
fit into the exact sequence}
{由单射性及饱和性，有
$f(N_\sigma)\cap N'_{\tau'}=f(N_\tau)$：在
$N_{\sigma,\mathbf R}$ 中，$N'_{\tau',\mathbf R}$ 的逆像
就是 $N_{\tau,\mathbf R}$。本原向外生成元在秩一商格中的
像是目标生成元的某个正整数倍 $m_{\sigma/\tau}$。有限群
组成正合列}
\[
 0\longrightarrow N'_{\tau'}/f(N_\tau)
 \longrightarrow N'_{\sigma'}/f(N_\sigma)
 \longrightarrow N'_{\sigma'}/(N'_{\tau'}+f(N_\sigma))
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
\bi{The final group has order $m_{\sigma/\tau}$, so taking
orders proves the equality.}
{最后一群的阶为 $m_{\sigma/\tau}$，取阶便得等式。}
\end{proof}
