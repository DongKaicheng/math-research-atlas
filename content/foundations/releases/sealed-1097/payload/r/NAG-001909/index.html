The spectral norm of a coefficient expansion

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 514

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{The spectral norm of a coefficient expansion}
{系数展开的谱半范数}]
\label{fnd:gradedcoefficientspectral}
\bi{Let $A$ be an affinoid algebra and $r$ a finite tuple of positive
radii. For a series in $A\{r^{-1}T\}$,}
{设 $A$ 为 affinoid 代数，$r$ 为有限个正半径组成的元组。
对 $A\{r^{-1}T\}$ 中的级数，有}
\[
 \rho\left(\sum_{\nu\in\mathbf N^m}a_\nu T^\nu\right)
       =\max_\nu\rho_A(a_\nu)r^\nu.
\]
\bi{If $r$ is free over $K$, the same formula, with indices in
$\mathbf Z^m$, holds in $A_r=A\widehat\otimes_K K_r$.}
{若 $r$ 在 $K$ 上自由，则在
$A_r=A\widehat\otimes_K K_r$ 中也有同一公式，此时指标属于
$\mathbf Z^m$。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{At every spectral point of either coefficient algebra, restriction
to $A$ and the coordinate bounds give the upper bound on the right.
The series tails tend uniformly to zero in the coefficient norm, so this
estimate passes from polynomials to series. Conversely, for every
$x\in\mathcal M(A)$ the Gauss seminorm on the polynomial algebra over
$\mathcal H(x)$ extends by that same tail estimate and takes the value
$\max_\nu|a_\nu(x)|r^\nu$. In the Laurent case this is precisely the
section of Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusgausssection}.
Taking the supremum over $x$ and interchanging two suprema proves the
formula, by Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedspectralradiusformula}.
The coefficient weights tend to zero, so every positive supremum is
a maximum; when all weights vanish, both sides are zero.}
{在任一个系数代数的谱点处，限制到 $A$ 并使用坐标界，就得到右端
所给的上界。级数的尾部在系数范数中一致趋于零，因此该估计从多项式
传递到级数。反之，对每个 $x\in\mathcal M(A)$，在
$\mathcal H(x)$ 上的多项式代数取 Gauss 半范数，同一尾部估计使它
延拓到级数，并取值 $\max_\nu|a_\nu(x)|r^\nu$。
Laurent 情形正是 Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusgausssection} 的截面。
对 $x$ 取上确界并交换两个上确界，由
Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedspectralradiusformula} 得到公式。
系数权重趋于零，故正的上确界实际达到；若全部权重为零，则两边均为零。}
\end{proof}
