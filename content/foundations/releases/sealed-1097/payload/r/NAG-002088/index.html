The Hilbert function of an initial ideal

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 869

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The Hilbert function of an initial ideal}{初始理想的 Hilbert 函数}]
\label{cand:initialhilbertfunction}
\bi{For every degree $d$, the degree-$d$ component of $\operatorname{in}_\omega(H)$ is the reduction of the unit ball of the translated subspace $H_d$. Consequently}
{对每个次数 $d$，$\operatorname{in}_\omega(H)$ 的 $d$ 次部分等于平移后子空间 $H_d$ 的单位球约化。因此}
\[
 \dim_{\widetilde L}\bigl(\widetilde L[x]/\operatorname{in}_\omega(H)\bigr)_d
 =\dim_K(S/H)_d.
\]
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The reduction of the unit ball of $H_d$ is a vector subspace. Multiplying a normalized homogeneous relation of degree $e\le d$ by a monomial of degree $d-e$ gives a normalized relation in $H_d$. Conversely, every reduced vector in $H_d$ is one of the homogeneous initial relations defining the ideal. Thus its degree-$d$ part is exactly this reduced subspace. After translating, Lemma~\ref{cand:weightedcoordinatesubspace} with unit weights gives its dimension $\dim_KH_d$. Subtract this from the number of degree-$d$ monomials.}
{$H_d$ 单位球的约化是向量子空间。将次数 $e\le d$ 的归一化齐次关系乘以次数 $d-e$ 的单项式，得到 $H_d$ 中的归一化关系。反过来，$H_d$ 中的每个约化向量本身就是定义该理想的齐次初始关系。因此理想的 $d$ 次部分恰为此约化子空间。平移后，对单位权重应用 Lemma~\ref{cand:weightedcoordinatesubspace}，其维数为 $\dim_KH_d$；从 $d$ 次单项式数中减去此数即得结论。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\label{cand:groebnercomplexsection}
\bi{Write $S=K[x_0,\ldots,x_n]$ with its usual grading, and let $H\subset S$ be homogeneous. For $\omega\in\mathbf R^{n+1}$, pass to a complete isometric extension realizing the coordinate weights, translate the variables, and reduce normalized homogeneous relations. This gives a homogeneous ideal $\operatorname{in}_\omega(H)$ in a polynomial ring over the residue field. The arguments of Proposition~\ref{cand:unitinitialbasechange} apply degree by degree to polynomial rings as well. We compare ideals after residue-field extension and diagonal change of coordinates.}
{令 $S=K[x_0,\ldots,x_n]$ 具有通常分次，$H\subset S$ 为齐次理想。对 $\omega\in\mathbf R^{n+1}$，取实现各坐标权重的完备等距扩域，平移变量并约化归一化后的齐次关系，得到剩余域上多项式环中的齐次理想 $\operatorname{in}_\omega(H)$。Proposition~\ref{cand:unitinitialbasechange} 的论证也逐次适用于多项式环。以下允许剩余域扩张及对角坐标变换来比较这些理想。}
