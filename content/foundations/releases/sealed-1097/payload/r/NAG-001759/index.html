Realization of prime and finite-field fibre tests

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 326

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Realization of prime and finite-field fibre tests}{素理想与有限域纤维测试的实现}]
\label{fnd:finitefieldtestrealization}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good spaces, let $x=f(y)$, and put $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$, $S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$. For a prime $\mathfrak p\subset R$ and a finite extension $E/\operatorname{Frac}(R/\mathfrak p)$, there are, after ordinary neighborhood restrictions, an integral finite analytic space $Z\to X$ with a unique point $z$ above $x$, and finitely many points $t_1,\ldots,t_m$ above $y$ in $T=Y\times_X Z$, such that}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 为 good 空间态射，$x=f(y)$，并令 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$。对素理想 $\mathfrak p\subset R$ 及有限扩张 $E/\operatorname{Frac}(R/\mathfrak p)$，经过普通邻域限制后，存在整的有限解析空间 $Z\to X$，其在 $x$ 上方恰有一个点 $z$；在 $T=Y\times_X Z$ 中，$y$ 上方有有限个点 $t_1,\ldots,t_m$，且}
\[
 C:=\mathcal O_{Z,z}\ \text{is a domain},\qquad
 \operatorname{Frac}(C)=E,\qquad
 \ker(R\to C)=\mathfrak p,
\]
\[
       S\otimes_R E
           \simeq
       \left(\prod_{i=1}^{m}\mathcal O_{T,t_i}\right)
                                      \otimes_C E .
\]
\bi{Every local ring of $\operatorname{Spec}(S\otimes_R E)$ is therefore a localization of some $\mathcal O_{T,t_i}$ at a prime whose contraction to the affinoid domain algebra of $Z$ is zero.}
{因此，$\operatorname{Spec}(S\otimes_R E)$ 的每个局部环，都等于某个 $\mathcal O_{T,t_i}$ 在一个素理想处的局部化，且该素理想收缩到 $Z$ 的 affinoid 整环时为零。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Write $R_0=R/\mathfrak p$ and $F=\operatorname{Frac}(R_0)$. Choose finitely many generators $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_s$ of the finite field extension $E/F$. Each satisfies a monic equation over $F$. For each $j$, choose a nonzero $d_j\in R_0$ clearing the finitely many denominators in that equation. Then $\beta_j=d_j\alpha_j$ is integral over $R_0$: in the transformed monic equation, its $i$th coefficient is the old $i$th coefficient multiplied by $d_j^i$, which lies in $R_0$. Thus $C=R_0[\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_s]$ is a finite integral domain over $R_0$, and its fraction field is exactly $E$. This construction uses no integral-closure finiteness theorem.}
{写 $R_0=R/\mathfrak p$、$F=\operatorname{Frac}(R_0)$。为有限域扩张 $E/F$ 选择有限个生成元 $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_s$。每个生成元满足 $F$ 上的一个首一方程。对每个 $j$ 取非零 $d_j\in R_0$，清除该方程全部系数的有限个分母，则 $\beta_j=d_j\alpha_j$ 在 $R_0$ 上整：变换后的首一方程中，第 $i$ 个系数等于原第 $i$ 个系数乘以 $d_j^i$，属于 $R_0$。因此 $C=R_0[\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_s]$ 是 $R_0$ 上的有限整环，且其分式域恰为 $E$。此构造无需整闭包的有限性定理。}

\bi{Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization} to $C$, obtaining an ordinary affinoid neighborhood $X_0=\mathcal M(A)$ and a finite domain $B$ over $A$, with $Z=\mathcal M(B)$, a unique point $z$ above $x$, and $B\otimes_A R=C=\mathcal O_{Z,z}$. The construction there may and does arrange $B\hookrightarrow C$, by quotienting the kernel of this map. Choose an affinoid neighborhood $Y_0$ of $y$ mapping to $X_0$; this changes neither $R$ nor $S$. The projection $T=Y_0\times_{X_0}Z\to Y_0$ is finite. Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation} and finite algebra base change give}
{将 Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization} 用于 $C$，得到普通 affinoid 邻域 $X_0=\mathcal M(A)$、$A$ 上的有限整环 $B$、$Z=\mathcal M(B)$ 及 $x$ 上方的唯一点 $z$，满足 $B\otimes_A R=C=\mathcal O_{Z,z}$。该构造通过除去映射的核，还可并且确实安排 $B\hookrightarrow C$。选择 $y$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $Y_0$ 映入 $X_0$；这不改变 $R,S$。投影 $T=Y_0\times_{X_0}Z\to Y_0$ 有限。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation} 及有限代数基变换，得到}
\[
     \prod_i\mathcal O_{T,t_i}
            \simeq B\otimes_A S
            \simeq (B\otimes_A R)\otimes_R S
            =C\otimes_R S .
\]
\bi{Localize this identity by $C\setminus\{0\}$. Since $\operatorname{Frac}(C)=E$, it becomes the asserted isomorphism. Every prime of a finite product belongs to exactly one factor: the complementary factor idempotents have product zero and sum one. A prime in the localization avoids every nonzero element of $C$, and hence every nonzero element of its subring $B$. Localizing once more at that prime gives precisely the local ring of the original factor at its corresponding prime. This proves the last assertion and explicitly realizes every finite-field test in an algebraic fibre of the ordinary-stalk morphism.}
{将此恒等式关于 $C\setminus\{0\}$ 局部化。因 $\operatorname{Frac}(C)=E$，即得所述同构。有限乘积的每个素理想恰属于一个因子，因为互补因子的幂等元乘积为零且和为一。局部化中的素理想避开 $C$ 的每个非零元素，因此也避开子环 $B$ 的每个非零元素。再在该素理想处局部化，恰得到原因子在对应素理想处的局部环。这证明最后断言，并显式实现通常茎映射的代数纤维中每个有限域测试。}
\end{proof}
