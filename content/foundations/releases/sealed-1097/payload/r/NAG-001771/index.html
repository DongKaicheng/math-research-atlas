\begin{lemma}[\bi{Cofinite subfields of a topological \(p\)-basis}{拓扑 \(p\)-基的余有限子域}]
\label{fnd:cofinitepbasisfields}
\bi{Let $(a_i)_{i\in I}$ be a topological $p$-basis of a complete exponent-one extension $E/F$. For $J\subset I$, let $E_J$ be the closure of $F(a_j:j\in J)$ in $E$. It consists precisely of the convergent $p$-monomial expansions supported on $J$. If $I\setminus J$ is finite, then}
{设 $(a_i)_{i\in I}$ 为完备指数一扩张 $E/F$ 的拓扑 $p$-基。对 $J\subset I$，令 $E_J$ 为 $F(a_j:j\in J)$ 在 $E$ 中的闭包。它恰由只支撑在 $J$ 上的收敛 $p$-单项式展开组成。若 $I\setminus J$ 有限，则}
\[
 E=\bigoplus_{\substack{0\leq \nu_i<p\\i\in I\setminus J}}
        E_J a^\nu,\qquad
 [E:E_J]=p^{|I\setminus J|},\qquad
 \Omega_{E/E_J}=\bigoplus_{i\in I\setminus J}E\,da_i .
\]
\bi{Moreover $\bigcap_{I\setminus J\text{ finite}}E_J=F$.}
{此外，$\bigcap_{I\setminus J\text{ 有限}}E_J=F$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The subspace of expansions supported on $J$ is closed, because each coordinate functional in a topological basis is continuous. Finite such expansions form the field $F(a_j:j\in J)$: the $p$th power of every element lies in $F$, so the inverse of a nonzero element $b$ is $b^{p-1}/b^p$. Its closure is again a field, since inversion is continuous away from zero. Thus the claimed description of $E_J$ follows. Regrouping the expansion by the finitely many exponents outside $J$ gives the displayed direct sum; bounded coordinate maps justify regrouping and convergence. Independence follows from uniqueness of the full expansion. The presentation}
{只支撑在 $J$ 上的展开组成闭子空间，因为拓扑基的每个坐标泛函连续。有限的这种展开组成域 $F(a_j:j\in J)$：每个元素的 $p$ 次幂属于 $F$，故非零元素 $b$ 的逆为 $b^{p-1}/b^p$；其闭包仍为域，因为求逆在非零处连续。由此得到 $E_J$ 的描述。按 $J$ 外有限多个指数重新归组，即得所列直和；坐标映射有界保证重新归组及收敛合法，而线性无关性来自完整展开的唯一性。表示}
\[
 E_J[U_i:i\in I\setminus J]/(U_i^p-a_i^p)_i
                       \ \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\ E
\]
\bi{then computes the differentials: the defining equations have zero differential and the indicated monomials form a basis on both sides. Finally, membership in every $E_J$ forces all coefficients of nonconstant monomials to vanish, since each such monomial involves an index excluded by some cofinite $J$. Only the constant coefficient remains.}
{计算出微分模：定义方程的微分为零，且指定单项式在两侧均为基。最后，属于每个 $E_J$ 使每个非常数单项式的系数为零，因为这种单项式总涉及某个可被余有限 $J$ 排除的指标，故只剩常数系数。}
\end{proof}
