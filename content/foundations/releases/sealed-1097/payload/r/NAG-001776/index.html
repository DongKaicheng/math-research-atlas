\begin{lemma}[\bi{Free Gauss extensions and preservation of \(p\)-bases}{自由 Gauss 扩张与 \(p\)-基保持}]
\label{fnd:gaussanalyticseparable}
\bi{The identity extension and every free Gauss extension $K_r/K$ are analytically separable. If $L/K$ is analytically separable and $K_0$ is a complete intermediate field $K^p\subset K_0\subset K$ with a topological $p$-basis $a$ over $K^p$, then $a$ remains topologically $p$-independent over $L^p$. For every complete subfield $F\subset L$ containing $L^pK_0$ and of countable type over $L^p$, and every cofinite subset $b\subset a$, there is a complete subfield $F^\sharp\subset F$ containing the closure $K_b$ of $K^p(b)$ such that}
{恒等扩张及每个自由 Gauss 扩张 $K_r/K$ 均解析可分。若 $L/K$ 解析可分，而 $K_0$ 是完备中间域 $K^p\subset K_0\subset K$，在 $K^p$ 上具有拓扑 $p$-基 $a$，则 $a$ 在 $L^p$ 上仍拓扑 $p$-无关。对包含 $L^pK_0$、在 $L^p$ 上为可数型的任意完备子域 $F\subset L$，以及任意余有限子集 $b\subset a$，存在完备子域 $F^\sharp\subset F$，包含 $K^p(b)$ 的闭包 $K_b$，并使}
\[
                  K_0\otimes_{K_b}F^\sharp
                               \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }F.
\]
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The assertion for the identity is immediate. If $L=K_r$, the coefficient description gives $L^p=K^p_{r^p}$. The map $L^p\widehat\otimes_{K^p}K\to L$ identifies with the subspace of Laurent expansions whose exponents are divisible by $p$. The coefficient formula of Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients} makes this an isometric embedding with closed image. For general analytically separable $L/K$, Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonisometrictensor} embeds $L^p\widehat\otimes_{K^p}K_0$ into $L^p\widehat\otimes_{K^p}K$. A bounded topological basis and its inverse remain bounded after completed tensor product. Thus the $p$-monomials in $a$ form a topological $L^p$-basis of the closure $F_0$ of $L^pK_0$ in $L$.}
{恒等情形显然。若 $L=K_r$，系数描述给出 $L^p=K^p_{r^p}$，而映射 $L^p\widehat\otimes_{K^p}K\to L$ 的像正好是指数均被 $p$ 整除的 Laurent 展开所组成的子空间。Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients} 的系数公式说明它是等距嵌入且像闭。对一般解析可分的 $L/K$，Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonisometrictensor} 将 $L^p\widehat\otimes_{K^p}K_0$ 嵌入 $L^p\widehat\otimes_{K^p}K$。有界拓扑基映射及其逆在完成张量后仍有界，因此 $a$ 的 $p$-单项式组成 $L^pK_0$ 在 $L$ 中闭包 $F_0$ 的拓扑 $L^p$-基。}

\bi{The extension $F/F_0$ has exponent one and countable type. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:topologicalpbasis} it has a topological $p$-basis $c$. Combining the two bounded basis isomorphisms shows that $a\cup c$ is a topological $p$-basis of $F/L^p$. Let $F^\sharp$ be the closed subfield generated by $L^p$ and $b\cup c$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:cofinitepbasisfields} shows that $a\setminus b$ is a finite algebraic $p$-basis of $F/F^\sharp$. It is also a $p$-basis of $K_0/K_b$. The multiplication map in the statement therefore carries the same monomial basis to the same monomial basis, and is an isomorphism.}
{扩张 $F/F_0$ 的指数为一且为可数型，故由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:topologicalpbasis}，它具有拓扑 $p$-基 $c$。复合两个有界的基同构，说明 $a\cup c$ 是 $F/L^p$ 的拓扑 $p$-基。令 $F^\sharp$ 为 $L^p$ 与 $b\cup c$ 生成的闭子域。Lemma~\ref{fnd:cofinitepbasisfields} 说明 $a\setminus b$ 是 $F/F^\sharp$ 的有限代数 $p$-基，它同时也是 $K_0/K_b$ 的 $p$-基。因此，陈述中的乘法映射把同一单项式基映到同一单项式基，故为同构。}
\end{proof}
