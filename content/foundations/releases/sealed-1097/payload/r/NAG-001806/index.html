Analytic flatness descends from a Gauss lift

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 470

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Analytic flatness descends from a Gauss lift}{解析平坦性从 Gauss 提升下降}]
\label{fnd:analyticgaussliftdescent}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good analytic spaces over any complete field, let $\mathcal F$ be coherent, and let $r>0$. Form the simultaneous affine-line base change $Y^*\to X^*$ and let $y^*$ be the radius-$r$ Gauss point above $y$. Then $\mathcal F$ is analytically $X$-flat at $y$ if and only if its pullback $\mathcal F^*$ is analytically $X^*$-flat at $y^*$.}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 为任意完备域上 good 解析空间之间的态射，$\mathcal F$ 凝聚，$r>0$。同时作仿射直线基变换得到 $Y^*\to X^*$，并令 $y^*$ 为 $y$ 上方的半径 $r$ Gauss 点。则 $\mathcal F$ 在 $y$ 处解析 $X$-平坦，当且仅当其拉回 $\mathcal F^*$ 在 $y^*$ 处解析 $X^*$-平坦。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The forward implication is a base-change test in the definition. Conversely, fix an arbitrary complete extension $L/K$, a good $L$-analytic test $X_1\to X$, and a point $y_1\in Y_1=Y\times_X X_1$ above $y$, with image $x_1$. Form $X_1^*=X_1\times_L\mathbb A_L^{1,\mathrm{an}}$ and $Y_1^*=Y_1\times_L\mathbb A_L^{1,\mathrm{an}}$. The radius-$r$ Gauss point $y_1^*$ maps to $y^*$: the completed-residue embeddings are isometric and the coefficientwise Gauss norm restricts to the same norm on every polynomial over the smaller field. Its base point $x_1^*$ likewise maps to the chosen Gauss point in $X^*$.}
{正向断言就是定义中的一个基变换测试。反之，固定任意完备扩张 $L/K$、good $L$-解析测试 $X_1\to X$，以及 $y$ 上方的点 $y_1\in Y_1=Y\times_X X_1$，其像记为 $x_1$。令 $X_1^*=X_1\times_L\mathbb A_L^{1,\mathrm{an}}$、$Y_1^*=Y_1\times_L\mathbb A_L^{1,\mathrm{an}}$。半径 $r$ 的 Gauss 点 $y_1^*$ 映到 $y^*$：完备剩余域嵌入等距，而逐系数定义的 Gauss 范数在较小域上的多项式上限制为同一范数。其底点 $x_1^*$ 也映到 $X^*$ 中所选 Gauss 点。}

\bi{The assumed analytic flatness at $y^*$ therefore makes the pulled-back coherent stalk at $y_1^*$ flat over $\mathcal O_{X_1^*,x_1^*}$. The horizontal maps in the local square}
{因此，$y^*$ 处所假设的解析平坦性，使 $y_1^*$ 处的拉回凝聚茎在 $\mathcal O_{X_1^*,x_1^*}$ 上平坦。在局部环方块}
\[
 \begin{matrix}
 \mathcal O_{X_1,x_1}&\longrightarrow&\mathcal O_{X_1^*,x_1^*}\\
 \big\downarrow&&\big\downarrow\\
 \mathcal O_{Y_1,y_1}&\longrightarrow&\mathcal O_{Y_1^*,y_1^*}
 \end{matrix}
\]
\bi{are flat local maps by Theorem~\ref{fnd:relativepolydiscanalyticflat}: take relative closed discs of radius greater than $r$, whose ordinary interiors contain the Gauss points and have the same ordinary stalks as the affine-line products. In particular the lower map is faithfully flat. Coherent pullback is ordinary tensor product on these stalks. Lemma~\ref{fnd:flatlocalsquaredescent} proves naive flatness at $y_1$. Since the complete extension, the good test and the point above $y$ were arbitrary, this is analytic flatness at $y$.}
{中，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:relativepolydiscanalyticflat}，两个横向映射均为平坦局部映射：取半径大于 $r$ 的相对闭圆盘，其普通内部包含 Gauss 点，且普通茎与仿射直线乘积的普通茎相同。特别地，下方映射忠实平坦。凝聚拉回在这些茎上为普通张量积，故 Lemma~\ref{fnd:flatlocalsquaredescent} 给出 $y_1$ 处的朴素平坦性。由于完备扩张、good 测试及 $y$ 上方的点均任意，这恰是 $y$ 处的解析平坦性。}
\end{proof}
