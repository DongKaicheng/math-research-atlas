Bounded coefficients of a minimal polynomial

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 80

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Bounded coefficients of a minimal polynomial}{最小多项式的系数界}]\label{fnd:strictminimalpolynomialbounds}
\bi{Let $B$ be a nonzero strict $K$-affinoid algebra which is a domain, and let $R_d\hookrightarrow B$ be a bounded finite injection. For $f\in B$, its monic minimal polynomial over $\operatorname{Frac}(R_d)$ belongs to $R_d[T]$, say}
{设 $B$ 是非零 strict $K$-affinoid 整环，且 $R_d\hookrightarrow B$ 为有界有限单射。对 $f\in B$，它在 $\operatorname{Frac}(R_d)$ 上的首一最小多项式属于 $R_d[T]$，记为}
\[
 p_f(T)=T^r+a_1T^{r-1}+\cdots+a_r.
\]
\bi{If $|f\bmod\mathfrak q|\leq1$ for every maximal ideal $\mathfrak q$ of $B$, using the unique extending residue absolute values, then $\|a_i\|\leq1$ for $1\leq i\leq r$.}
{若对 $B$ 的每个极大理想 $\mathfrak q$，使用唯一的剩余域延拓绝对值都有 $|f\bmod\mathfrak q|\leq1$，则每个 $1\leq i\leq r$ 都满足 $\|a_i\|\leq1$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Finite ring maps are integral by \cite[Tag 00GK, Lemma 10.36.3]{StacksAlg}. The preceding factorization theorem makes $R_d$ normal, so \cite[Tag 00H7, Lemma 10.38.6]{StacksAlg} places the minimal polynomial in $R_d[T]$. Polynomial division by this monic polynomial gives}
{由 \cite[Tag 00GK, Lemma 10.36.3]{StacksAlg}，有限环映射为整映射。前面的唯一分解定理说明 $R_d$ 整闭，因此由 \cite[Tag 00H7, Lemma 10.38.6]{StacksAlg}，最小多项式属于 $R_d[T]$。对此首一多项式作多项式除法，得到}
\[
 S:=R_d[f]\ \simeq\ R_d[T]/(p_f).
\]
\bi{Indeed, any polynomial relation has remainder of degree less than $r$; viewed in the fraction field of the domain $B$, minimality forces that remainder to be zero.}
{事实上，任何多项式关系除以 $p_f$ 后的余式次数都小于 $r$；将其视为整环 $B$ 的分式域中的关系，由最小性可知余式为零。}

\bi{Fix a maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ of $R_d$ and put $k_x=R_d/\mathfrak m$, a finite extension of $K$. By \cite[Tag 09HU, Lemma 9.16.1]{StacksAlg}, choose a splitting field $E$ of the specialization $\overline p_f\in k_x[T]$. It is finite over $k_x$, since it is generated by finitely many algebraic roots; adjoining them successively gives a finite tower. Equip it with the extending absolute value from Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}. Let $\alpha\in E$ be any root. Evaluation at $\alpha$ defines a maximal ideal $\mathfrak n$ of $S$, with $S/\mathfrak n=k_x[\alpha]$. The algebra $B$ is finite over $S$: any list generating it over $R_d$ also generates it over $S$. Consequently $S\subset B$ is integral. By \cite[Tag 00GQ, Lemma 10.36.17]{StacksAlg}, there is a prime $\mathfrak q\subset B$ over $\mathfrak n$. Its quotient is an integral domain integral over the field $S/\mathfrak n$, so \cite[Tag 00GS(2), Lemma 10.36.19]{StacksAlg} makes $\mathfrak q$ maximal.}
{固定 $R_d$ 的极大理想 $\mathfrak m$，并记 $k_x=R_d/\mathfrak m$，这是 $K$ 的有限扩张。由 \cite[Tag 09HU, Lemma 9.16.1]{StacksAlg}，选择特化多项式 $\overline p_f\in k_x[T]$ 的分裂域 $E$。它由有限多个代数根生成，逐次添入这些根得到有限扩张塔，因此 $E/k_x$ 有限。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 为其配备延拓绝对值。任取一个根 $\alpha\in E$。在 $\alpha$ 处取值给出 $S$ 的极大理想 $\mathfrak n$，且 $S/\mathfrak n=k_x[\alpha]$。代数 $B$ 在 $S$ 上有限：任何在 $R_d$ 上生成 $B$ 的列表也在 $S$ 上生成 $B$。因此 $S\subset B$ 为整扩张。由 \cite[Tag 00GQ, Lemma 10.36.17]{StacksAlg}，存在位于 $\mathfrak n$ 上方的素理想 $\mathfrak q\subset B$。商 $B/\mathfrak q$ 为整环，且整于域 $S/\mathfrak n$，所以由 \cite[Tag 00GS(2), Lemma 10.36.19]{StacksAlg}，$\mathfrak q$ 为极大理想。}

\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} gives a bounded point of $B$ at $\mathfrak q$, and $B/\mathfrak q$ is finite over $K$. The inclusion $k_x[\alpha]\hookrightarrow B/\mathfrak q$ identifies $\alpha$ with the residue of $f$. Uniqueness in Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} therefore gives $|\alpha|=|f\bmod\mathfrak q|\leq1$. This argument applies to every root, including repeated roots; separability is unnecessary. In $E$, each coefficient $\overline a_i$ is a sum of products of $i$ roots. Its absolute value is consequently at most one.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}，$\mathfrak q$ 给出 $B$ 的有界谱点，且 $B/\mathfrak q$ 有限于 $K$。包含 $k_x[\alpha]\hookrightarrow B/\mathfrak q$ 将 $\alpha$ 识别为 $f$ 的剩余类。因此，由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 中的唯一性，$|\alpha|=|f\bmod\mathfrak q|\leq1$。这个论证适用于每个根，包括重根，无须可分性。在 $E$ 中，每个系数 $\overline a_i$ 都是若干个由 $i$ 个根组成的乘积之和，故其绝对值不超过一。}

\bi{We have proved $|a_i\bmod\mathfrak m|\leq1$ for every maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ of $R_d$. Apply Lemma~\ref{fnd:tategaussmaximalpoint} separately to each $a_i$ to conclude $\|a_i\|\leq1$. The boundedness of the residue points came from their finite residue fields; no comparison between the norm on $B$ and a finite-$R_d$-module norm has been assumed.}
{现已证明：对 $R_d$ 的每个极大理想 $\mathfrak m$，都有 $|a_i\bmod\mathfrak m|\leq1$。分别对各个 $a_i$ 应用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:tategaussmaximalpoint}，即得 $\|a_i\|\leq1$。剩余域点的有界性来自其剩余域的有限性；这里没有假定 $B$ 上的范数与某个有限 $R_d$-模范数等价。}
\end{proof}
