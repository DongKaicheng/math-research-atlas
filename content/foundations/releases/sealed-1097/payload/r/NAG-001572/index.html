Nonemptiness and the unit-ideal test

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 121

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Nonemptiness and the unit-ideal test}{非空性与单位理想判据}]
\label{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion}
\bi{Let $A$ be a $K$-affinoid algebra with arbitrary positive presentation radii. Then $\mathcal M(A)=\varnothing$ if and only if $A=0$. For every algebraic ideal $J\subseteq A$,}
{设 $A$ 为允许任意正表示半径的 $K$-affinoid 代数。则 $\mathcal M(A)=\varnothing$ 当且仅当 $A=0$。对每个代数理想 $J\subseteq A$，有}
\[
 J\ne A
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \text{there is }x\in\mathcal M(A)\text{ with }|j(x)|=0
 \text{ for every }j\in J.
\]
\bi{In particular, $f\in A$ is a unit if and only if $|f(x)|>0$ at every point. If $A\ne0$ and $f$ is a unit, $|f|$ has a positive minimum on $\mathcal M(A)$. No finiteness of maximal residue fields over $K$ is asserted in this generality.}
{特别地，$f\in A$ 为单位，当且仅当它在每个谱点的值都大于零。若 $A\ne0$ 且 $f$ 为单位，则 $|f|$ 在 $\mathcal M(A)$ 上有正的最小值。在这一一般范围内，不断言极大理想的剩余域在 $K$ 上有限。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For $A\ne0$, Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification} supplies a free extension $L=K_r$ for which $A_r$ is strict norm-admissible. The constant embedding is isometric by Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients}, so $A_r\ne0$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} gives a bounded $L$-spectral point of $A_r$. Its restriction along the bounded map $A\to A_r$ is a point of $\mathcal M_K(A)$. Conversely, a seminorm on the zero algebra cannot send its unit to one. Notice that this argument needs only restriction of a point, not surjectivity of the whole spectral map.}
{若 $A\ne0$，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification}，存在自由扩域 $L=K_r$，使 $A_r$ 为 strict norm-admissible。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients}，常系数嵌入等距，故 $A_r\ne0$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} 给出 $A_r$ 的一个有界 $L$-谱点。沿有界映射 $A\to A_r$ 限制，即得到 $\mathcal M_K(A)$ 中的点。反之，零代数上的半范数不可能将单位元送到一。注意，此论证仅需限制一个点，不需要整个谱映射的满射性。}

\bi{Every ideal $J$ is closed by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}, and $A/J$ with its quotient norm is affinoid by Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients}. If $J$ is proper, the preceding nonemptiness argument applied to $A/J$ gives the required point of $A$. Conversely, a point killing $J$ rules out $1\in J$. Applying this equivalence to $J=fA$ proves the unit criterion; a unit cannot vanish because $|f(x)||f^{-1}(x)|=1$. Finally Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact} makes the nonempty spectrum compact, and $|f|$ is continuous and positive on it. Its minimum is therefore attained and positive.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}，每个理想 $J$ 都闭；由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients}，$A/J$ 配备商范数后为 affinoid。若 $J$ 为真理想，对 $A/J$ 应用刚证的非空性，便得到 $A$ 中所需的点。反之，零化 $J$ 的点排除了 $1\in J$。对 $J=fA$ 使用这一等价性，得到单位判据；单位不能取值为零，因为 $|f(x)||f^{-1}(x)|=1$。最后，由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact}，非空谱是紧空间，而 $|f|$ 在其上连续且为正，所以最小值实际取到并为正。}
\end{proof}
