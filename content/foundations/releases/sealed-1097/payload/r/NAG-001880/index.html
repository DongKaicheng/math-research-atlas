Nonrigid ordinary germs on strict curves

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 476

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Nonrigid ordinary germs on strict curves}
{Strict 曲线上非 rigid 点的通常芽}]
\label{fnd:nonrigidstrictcurvestalkfield}
\bi{Let $T$ be a reduced good strictly $K$-analytic space of
dimension at most one. At every nonrigid point $t$,
$\mathcal O_{T,t}$ is a field.}
{设 $T$ 为约化 good strictly $K$-analytic 空间，维数至多一。
在每个非 rigid 点 $t$ 处，$\mathcal O_{T,t}$ 是域。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose a reduced strict affinoid neighborhood of $t$.
By Lemmas~\ref{fnd:strictordinaryneighborhoods} and
\ref{fnd:strictreduceddomainstalks}, every germ at $t$ can
be represented on a reduced strict affinoid neighborhood
$V=\mathcal M(B)$. By
Corollary~\ref{fnd:affinoiddimensiondomaincover},
$\dim B\leq1$. Its point kernel $\mathfrak p=\ker(t)$
cannot be maximal, since every strict affinoid maximal point
has finite residue field over $K$, whereas $t$ is not rigid.
It is therefore a minimal prime: a strictly smaller prime and
a strictly larger maximal prime would give a chain of length two.
The localization $B_{\mathfrak p}$ is a reduced ring with
exactly one prime, and hence is a field.}
{先选择 $t$ 的约化 strict affinoid 邻域。由
Lemmas~\ref{fnd:strictordinaryneighborhoods} 与
\ref{fnd:strictreduceddomainstalks}，$t$ 处每个芽都可在一个
约化 strict affinoid 邻域 $V=\mathcal M(B)$ 上表示。
由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:affinoiddimensiondomaincover}，
$\dim B\leq1$。其点核 $\mathfrak p=\ker(t)$ 不能极大，
因为 strict affinoid 的每个极大点的剩余域都在 $K$ 上有限，
而 $t$ 非 rigid。因此 $\mathfrak p$ 为极小素理想：
否则一个严格更小的素理想与一个严格更大的极大理想，会构成
长度为二的素理想链。
局部化 $B_{\mathfrak p}$ 约化且只有一个素理想，因而为域。}

\bi{Let a germ $h$ vanish at $t$, and represent it by
$b\in B$ on such a neighborhood. Then $b\in\mathfrak p$
and $b/1=0$ in the field $B_{\mathfrak p}$. There exists
$c\notin\mathfrak p$ with $cb=0$. The germ of $c$ is a
unit, since $c(t)\ne0$, so $h=0$. Thus the maximal ideal of
$\mathcal O_{T,t}$ is zero, as required.}
{若芽 $h$ 在 $t$ 处消失，在这样的邻域上用 $b\in B$ 表示它。
则 $b\in\mathfrak p$，所以 $b/1$ 在域
$B_{\mathfrak p}$ 中为零。存在 $c\notin\mathfrak p$ 使
$cb=0$。由于 $c(t)\ne0$，$c$ 的芽是单位元，故 $h=0$。
因此 $\mathcal O_{T,t}$ 的极大理想为零，正是所需结论。}
\end{proof}
