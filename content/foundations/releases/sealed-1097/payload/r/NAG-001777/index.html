Countable descent of completed ordinary germs

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 343

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Countable descent of completed ordinary germs}{完成通常芽的可数下降}]
\label{fnd:countablecompletedgermdescent}
\bi{Let $L$ be complete of characteristic $p>0$, let $F_0\subset L$ be complete with $L^p\subset F_0$, and let $X_0$ be an affinoid $F_0$-space. Put $X=(X_0)_L$, choose $x\in X$, and denote its image in $(X_0)_F$ by $x_F$. If $F$ ranges over the complete intermediate fields $F_0\subset F\subset L$ of countable type over $F_0$, then}
{设 $L$ 为特征 $p>0$ 的完备域，$F_0\subset L$ 为包含 $L^p$ 的完备子域，$X_0$ 为 affinoid $F_0$-空间。令 $X=(X_0)_L$，取 $x\in X$，记其在 $(X_0)_F$ 中的像为 $x_F$。当 $F$ 遍历 $F_0\subset F\subset L$ 中在 $F_0$ 上为可数型的完备中间域时，有}
\[
 \underset{F}{\operatorname{colim}}\,
       \widehat{\mathcal O_{(X_0)_F,x_F}}
             \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\widehat{\mathcal O_{X,x}} .
\]
\bi{All transition maps, and all maps into the ring on the right, are faithfully flat local maps.}
{所有转移映射及到右侧环的映射均为忠实平坦局部映射。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The ordinary local maps are faithfully flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}; their completions are flat by Tag 0C4G, and local, hence faithfully flat. In particular they are injective. Every individual germ descends to some $F$ in the stated family. Indeed represent it on a rational affinoid ordinary neighborhood, using finitely many inequalities. Lift the finitely many functions in those inequalities, and then the represented analytic function on the rational algebra, to convergent weighted series. Only countably many scalar coefficients of $L$ occur. Adjoining those coefficients to $F_0$ and taking the closure gives such an $F$: each coefficient has its $p$th power in $L^p\subset F_0$, and the finitely supported $p$-monomials in countably many generators have countable dense linear span. The inequalities defining the ordinary neighborhood hold in a neighborhood of $x_F$, since their strict versions hold at $x_F$ exactly when they hold at $x$.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}，通常局部映射忠实平坦；其完备化由 Tag 0C4G 平坦，又为局部映射，所以忠实平坦，特别地单射。每个单独的芽都下降到上述族中的某个 $F$。事实上，在由有限个不等式定义的 rational affinoid 普通邻域上表示该芽；把不等式中的有限个函数及 rational 代数上的解析函数提升到收敛加权级数，只涉及 $L$ 中可数多个标量系数。将它们添入 $F_0$ 并取闭包，便得到所需 $F$：每个系数的 $p$ 次幂都属于 $L^p\subset F_0$，可数生成元的有限支撑 $p$-单项式具有可数稠密线性张成。定义普通邻域的不等式在 $x_F$ 的邻域仍成立，因为相应严格不等式在 $x_F$ 与 $x$ 处同时成立。}

\bi{Write $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$, choose finitely many generators $f_1,\ldots,f_d$ of its maximal ideal, and descend them to some $F_1$. For $g\in\widehat R$, choose $g_n\in R$ with $g-g_n\in(f_1,\ldots,f_d)^n\widehat R$. Descend all $g_n$ and the $f_i$ to one field $F$; a countable union of the coefficient sets above is still countable. Put $R_F=\mathcal O_{(X_0)_F,x_F}$, and denote the descended elements by $f_i'$ and $g_n'$. The faithfully flat map}
{记 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$，选择其极大理想的有限生成元 $f_1,\ldots,f_d$，并将它们下降到某个 $F_1$。对 $g\in\widehat R$，选择 $g_n\in R$ 使 $g-g_n\in(f_1,\ldots,f_d)^n\widehat R$。把所有 $g_n$ 及 $f_i$ 下降到同一个域 $F$；上述系数集合的可数并仍可数。记 $R_F=\mathcal O_{(X_0)_F,x_F}$，将下降后的元素记为 $f_i'$、$g_n'$。忠实平坦映射}
\[
                 R_F/(f_i')\longrightarrow R/(f_i)
\]
\bi{has a field as target, so its source is a field. Thus the $f_i'$ generate the maximal ideal of $R_F$. Faithful flatness contracts every power of this ideal, and therefore $g_{n+1}'-g_n'\in(f_i')^n$. The compatible residues of the $g_n'$ define an element of $\widehat{R_F}$ whose image is $g$. This proves surjectivity of the colimit map; injectivity was proved at the start.}
{的目标为域，所以其源也为域；因此 $f_i'$ 生成 $R_F$ 的极大理想。忠实平坦性使该理想的每个幂都正确收缩，故 $g_{n+1}'-g_n'\in(f_i')^n$。于是 $g_n'$ 的相容剩余类定义 $\widehat{R_F}$ 的元素，其像为 $g$。这证明直极限映射满射，单射性已在开头证明。}
\end{proof}
