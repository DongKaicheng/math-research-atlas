The strict reduction criterion for finiteness

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 526

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The strict reduction criterion for finiteness}
{有限性的 strict 约化判据}]
\label{fnd:strictcanonicalfinitecriterion}
\bi{A bounded homomorphism $A\to B$ of strict $K$-affinoid
algebras is finite if and only if the induced homomorphism
$\widetilde A\to\widetilde B$ is finite.}
{strict $K$-affinoid 代数之间的有界同态 $A\to B$ 为有限同态，
当且仅当诱导同态 $\widetilde A\to\widetilde B$ 为有限同态。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Suppose first that $B$ is finite over $A$. For each minimal
prime $\mathfrak q$ of $B$, the domain $B/\mathfrak q$ is finite
over $A/(A\cap\mathfrak q)$. Normalize the latter using a fixed
strict presentation of $A$. The resulting normalizing Tate algebra
$R_{\mathfrak q}$ lifts contractively to that presentation, and
$B/\mathfrak q$ is finite over $R_{\mathfrak q}$.
Proposition~\ref{fnd:normalizationcanonicalfinite} shows that
$\widetilde{B/\mathfrak q}$ is finite over
$\widetilde R_{\mathfrak q}$, hence finite over $\widetilde A$.
The injection
$\widetilde B\hookrightarrow\prod_{\mathfrak q}
\widetilde{B/\mathfrak q}$ and the Noetherian property of
$\widetilde A$ (Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictcanonicalfinitegeneration}) prove this direction.}
{先设 $B$ 在 $A$ 上有限。对 $B$ 的每个极小素理想
$\mathfrak q$，整环 $B/\mathfrak q$ 在
$A/(A\cap\mathfrak q)$ 上有限。使用 $A$ 的一个固定 strict
表示对后者正规化，所得正规化 Tate 代数 $R_{\mathfrak q}$
可压缩地提升到该表示，且 $B/\mathfrak q$ 在
$R_{\mathfrak q}$ 上有限。
Proposition~\ref{fnd:normalizationcanonicalfinite} 说明
$\widetilde{B/\mathfrak q}$ 在 $\widetilde R_{\mathfrak q}$ 上有限，
因而在 $\widetilde A$ 上有限。再使用单射
$\widetilde B\hookrightarrow\prod_{\mathfrak q}
\widetilde{B/\mathfrak q}$ 及 $\widetilde A$ 的 Noetherian 性（Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictcanonicalfinitegeneration}），
即得此方向。}

\bi{Conversely assume $\widetilde B$ is finite over $\widetilde A$.
Choose the images $b_1,\ldots,b_m$ of the coordinates in a strict
presentation of $B$; they belong to $B^\circ$. Each residue
$\widetilde b_i$ satisfies a monic equation over $\widetilde A$:
one may obtain it by applying the determinant trick to multiplication
by $\widetilde b_i$ on a finite generating module. Lift its
coefficients to $c_{ij}\in A^\circ$. Then}
{反之，设 $\widetilde B$ 在 $\widetilde A$ 上有限。
选取 $B$ 的 strict 表示中各坐标的像 $b_1,\ldots,b_m$，它们
属于 $B^\circ$。每个剩余类 $\widetilde b_i$ 都满足
$\widetilde A$ 上的首一方程：在有限生成模上对
$\widetilde b_i$ 的乘法应用行列式技巧即可。将其系数提升为
$c_{ij}\in A^\circ$，于是}
\[
 w_i=b_i^{d_i}+\sum_{j<d_i}c_{ij}b_i^j,
 \qquad \rho_B(w_i)<1.
\]
\bi{Append variables mapping to the finitely many power-bounded
$c_{ij}$ to a strict presentation of $A$. Bounded substitution
gives an admissible surjection $R\twoheadrightarrow A$ from a
Tate algebra for which these coefficients have lifts of norm at
most one. The induced map
$q:R\langle Y_1,\ldots,Y_m\rangle\twoheadrightarrow B$ is
admissible: the chosen presentation of $B$, using the $Y_i$ alone,
already supplies uniformly bounded lifts. Let
$W_i\in R[Y_i]$ be the lifted monic polynomial. Since $w_i$ is
topologically nilpotent, choose $e_i>0$ and $G_i$ of norm less
than one with $q(G_i)=w_i^{e_i}$. Thus
$W_i^{e_i}-G_i\in\ker q$.}
{在 $A$ 的 strict 表示中添入映到有限个幂有界元素 $c_{ij}$
的变量。有界代换给出 Tate 代数的 admissible 满射
$R\twoheadrightarrow A$，使这些系数具有范数至多为一的提升。
诱导映射
$q:R\langle Y_1,\ldots,Y_m\rangle\twoheadrightarrow B$
也是 admissible，因为仅用 $Y_i$ 的既定 $B$ 的表示已经提供
一致有界的提升。以 $W_i\in R[Y_i]$ 记提升的首一多项式。
由于 $w_i$ 拓扑幂零，可取 $e_i>0$ 及范数小于一的 $G_i$，使
$q(G_i)=w_i^{e_i}$，从而
$W_i^{e_i}-G_i\in\ker q$。}

\bi{Apply Lemma~\ref{fnd:simultaneousmonicperturbation} to the
univariate polynomials $W_i^{e_i}$. It shows that their perturbed
quotient, and hence $B$, is finite over $R$. The action of $R$
on $B$ factors through $A$, so the same finite list of generators
generates $B$ as an $A$-module.}
{对单变量多项式 $W_i^{e_i}$ 应用
Lemma~\ref{fnd:simultaneousmonicperturbation}，可知相应扰动商，
因而 $B$，在 $R$ 上有限。由于 $R$ 在 $B$ 上的作用经过
$A$ 分解，同一组有限生成元也在 $A$ 上生成 $B$。}
\end{proof}
