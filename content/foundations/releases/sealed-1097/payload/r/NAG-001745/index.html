Rational points lie inside analytic domains

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 314

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Rational points lie inside analytic domains}{有理点位于解析域的通常内部}]
\label{fnd:rationalpointdomaininterior}
\bi{If $U$ is an analytic domain of a good $K$-analytic space $X$ and $x\in U$ satisfies $\mathcal H(x)=K$, then $U$ contains an ordinary neighborhood of $x$ in $X$. In particular the ordinary stalks on $U$ and $X$ agree at $x$.}
{若 $U$ 是 good $K$-解析空间 $X$ 的解析域，且 $x\in U$ 满足 $\mathcal H(x)=K$，则 $U$ 包含 $x$ 在 $X$ 中的一个普通邻域。特别地，$U$ 与 $X$ 在 $x$ 处的通常茎相同。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose an affinoid neighborhood $X_0$ of $x$ in $X$ and an affinoid domain of $U\cap X_0$ containing $x$. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert}, this domain has a finite cover by domains rational in $X_0$. Choose one containing $x$, given by $|f_i|\le r_i|g|$. The unit-ideal condition implies $g(x)\ne0$. Write $a_i=f_i(x)\in K$ and $b=g(x)\in K^\times$. On the ordinary neighborhood $|g-b|<|b|$, we have $|g|=|b|$. If $a_i\ne0$, further impose $|f_i-a_i|<|a_i|$; then $|f_i|=|a_i|\le r_i|b|$. If $a_i=0$, impose $|f_i|<r_i|b|$. These finitely many open conditions hold at $x$ and force all the defining rational inequalities. Intersect also with the ordinary interior of $X_0$. The resulting ordinary neighborhood is contained in $U$.}
{选择 $x$ 在 $X$ 中的 affinoid 邻域 $X_0$，并选择 $U\cap X_0$ 中包含 $x$ 的一个 affinoid 域。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert}，此域有由 $X_0$ 中 rational 域组成的有限覆盖。取一个包含 $x$ 的成员，由 $|f_i|\le r_i|g|$ 定义。单位理想条件保证 $g(x)\ne0$。记 $a_i=f_i(x)\in K$、$b=g(x)\in K^\times$。在普通邻域 $|g-b|<|b|$ 上，有 $|g|=|b|$。若 $a_i\ne0$，再要求 $|f_i-a_i|<|a_i|$，则 $|f_i|=|a_i|\le r_i|b|$；若 $a_i=0$，要求 $|f_i|<r_i|b|$。这有限个开条件均在 $x$ 成立，并强制全部 rational 定义不等式。再与 $X_0$ 的普通内部相交，即得到包含于 $U$ 的普通邻域。}
\end{proof}
