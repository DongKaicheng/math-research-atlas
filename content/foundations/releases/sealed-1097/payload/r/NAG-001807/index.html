Inner coherent flatness in arbitrary characteristic-zero valuation

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 471

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Inner coherent flatness in arbitrary characteristic-zero valuation}{任意特征零赋值下的内点凝聚平坦性}]
\label{fnd:charzeroallresidueinnerflatness}
\bi{Let $K$ be any complete field of characteristic zero, let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good analytic spaces, and let $\mathcal F$ be coherent. At every $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$, naive $X$-flatness of $\mathcal F$ is equivalent to analytic $X$-flatness.}
{设 $K$ 为任意特征零完备域，$f:Y\to X$ 为 good 解析空间之间的态射，$\mathcal F$ 凝聚。在每个 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$ 处，$\mathcal F$ 的朴素 $X$-平坦性等价于解析 $X$-平坦性。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Only the implication from naive to analytic flatness requires proof. Put $x=f(y)$. If $\mathcal H(x)$ is nontrivially valued, this is Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroinnercoherentflatness}. Otherwise choose $0<r<1$ and make the simultaneous affine-line base change. Lemma~\ref{fnd:charzeronaivegausslift} gives naive flatness of the pullback at $y^*$. The point $y^*$ is inner over $X^*$ because interior is stable under base change, and $\mathcal H(x^*)=\mathcal H(x)_r$ is nontrivially valued. The same nontrivially valued theorem makes the pullback analytically flat at $y^*$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:analyticgaussliftdescent} then descends analytic flatness to $y$.}
{只须证明从朴素到解析平坦性的方向。置 $x=f(y)$。若 $\mathcal H(x)$ 非平凡赋值，这就是 Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroinnercoherentflatness}。否则选择 $0<r<1$，同时作仿射直线基变换。Lemma~\ref{fnd:charzeronaivegausslift} 给出拉回在 $y^*$ 处的朴素平坦性。内部在基变换下保持，所以 $y^*$ 在 $X^*$ 上为内点；且 $\mathcal H(x^*)=\mathcal H(x)_r$ 非平凡赋值。上述非平凡赋值定理使拉回在 $y^*$ 处解析平坦，再由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:analyticgaussliftdescent} 将解析平坦性下降到 $y$。}
\end{proof}
