Rational factorization for strict targets

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 199

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Rational factorization for strict targets}{Strict 目标的 rational 分解}]\label{fnd:strictrationaldomainup}
\bi{Let $B=A\langle f/g\rangle$, and let}
{设 $B=A\langle f/g\rangle$，并令}
\[
 V=\{x\in\mathcal M(A):|f_j(x)|\leq|g(x)|
                         \text{ for every }j\}.
\]
\bi{For every strict norm-admissible affinoid $C$ and bounded unital $K$-algebra map $\phi:A\to C$, there is a bounded factorization $A\to B\to C$ if and only if the induced map $\mathcal M(C)\to\mathcal M(A)$ has image in $V$. The factorization is unique. Zero targets and zero quotients are included. Consequently two strict rational presentations of the same subset of $\mathcal M(A)$ have a unique bounded $A$-algebra isomorphism with bounded inverse; an inclusion of such subsets gives the unique bounded restriction map in the reverse direction. These maps commute with composition. No assertion about generalized non-strict targets is made.}
{对每个 strict norm-admissible affinoid $C$ 和有界含幺 $K$-代数同态 $\phi:A\to C$，存在有界分解 $A\to B\to C$，当且仅当诱导映射 $\mathcal M(C)\to\mathcal M(A)$ 的像包含于 $V$。该分解唯一。结论包括零目标及零商。因此，$\mathcal M(A)$ 中同一子集的两个 strict rational 表示之间，存在唯一的有界 $A$-代数同构，其逆也有界；这种子集的包含关系给出反方向唯一的有界限制映射。这些映射与复合相容。这里不对 generalized non-strict 目标作出断言。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A bounded homomorphism pulls a bounded multiplicative seminorm back to one, so the stated map on spectra is defined. If the factorization exists, Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology} gives its image condition. Conversely, assume this condition and first take $C\ne0$. At a point $y\in\mathcal M(C)$, the inequalities and the image of the unit-ideal identity imply $|\phi(g)(y)|>0$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} therefore makes $\phi(g)$ a unit of $C$. Put $b_j=\phi(f_j)\phi(g)^{-1}$. For every $y$ one has $|b_j(y)|\leq1$; Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointwisepowerbounded} gives a finite bound for all $\|b_j^n\|$, $n\geq0$. Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictpowercontrolledrational} constructs the unique bounded factorization. If $C=0$, the unique map has the stated properties directly. In particular, a zero $B$ cannot factor into a nonzero $C$; the preceding construction would contradict its unit equation.}
{有界同态将 bounded multiplicative seminorm 拉回为同类半范数，所以所述谱映射有定义。若分解存在，Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology} 给出其像的条件。反之，假设该条件，先取 $C\ne0$。对 $y\in\mathcal M(C)$，由不等式及单位理想恒等式的像，得到 $|\phi(g)(y)|>0$。因此由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}，$\phi(g)$ 是 $C$ 中的单位。令 $b_j=\phi(f_j)\phi(g)^{-1}$。对每个 $y$，有 $|b_j(y)|\leq1$；Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointwisepowerbounded} 给出所有 $n\geq0$ 对应的 $\|b_j^n\|$ 的有限统一界。由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictpowercontrolledrational}，得到唯一的有界分解。若 $C=0$，唯一映射直接具有所述性质。特别地，零的 $B$ 不可能向非零 $C$ 作分解，否则前述构造将与单位等式矛盾。}

\bi{For two presentations with the same subset, apply the first assertion to each presentation as a target of the other. The composites and identity maps agree on $A$, so uniqueness makes the composites identities. The same uniqueness proves composition compatibility for inclusions. The isomorphisms need not preserve the chosen presentation norms; boundedness in both directions is the conclusion.}
{对同一子集的两个表示，分别以其中一个作为另一个的目标，应用第一个断言。两个复合与恒等映射在 $A$ 上相同，故由唯一性，复合均为恒等映射。对包含关系，同一唯一性给出复合相容性。所得同构不必保持所选表示范数；结论是两个方向都有界。}
\end{proof}
