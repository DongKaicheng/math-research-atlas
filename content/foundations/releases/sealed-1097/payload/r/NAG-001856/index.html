A field germ at an auxiliary Gauss point

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 463

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A field germ at an auxiliary Gauss point}{辅助 Gauss 点处的域芽}]
\label{fnd:charzerogaussfieldgerm}
\bi{Let $X$ be good over a complete field of characteristic zero, let $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ be a field, and suppose that $F=\mathcal H(x)$ is trivially valued. Choose $0<r<1$ and let $x^*$ be the radius-$r$ Gauss point above $x$ in $X^*=X\times_K\mathbb A_K^{1,\mathrm{an}}$. Then $\mathcal H(x^*)=F_r$ is nontrivially valued and $\mathcal O_{X^*,x^*}$ is a field.}
{设 $X$ 为特征零完备域上的 good 空间，$\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 为域，并假设 $F=\mathcal H(x)$ 平凡赋值。选择 $0<r<1$，令 $x^*$ 为 $X^*=X\times_K\mathbb A_K^{1,\mathrm{an}}$ 中 $x$ 上方的半径 $r$ Gauss 点。则 $\mathcal H(x^*)=F_r$ 非平凡赋值，且 $\mathcal O_{X^*,x^*}$ 为域。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The completed residue field is $F_r$ by the Gauss evaluation and the completed-residue-field universal property. To prove the local-ring assertion, take a germ $h$ with $h(x^*)=0$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:freegaussproductneighborhood} gives an ordinary product neighborhood $U\times_KD$ of $x^*$ on which $h$ is represented, with $D$ an open annulus containing radius $r$. Since $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is a field, it is normal. Corollary~\ref{fnd:charzeronormallocus} and Lemma~\ref{fnd:normalcomponentidentity} give a normal integral affinoid ordinary neighborhood $\mathcal M(A)\subset U$ of $x$. The map $A\to\mathcal O_{X,x}\hookrightarrow F$ is injective: it is a nonzero flat map from a domain followed by the residue-field embedding.}
{由 Gauss 取值及完备剩余域泛性质，完备剩余域为 $F_r$。为证明局部环断言，取满足 $h(x^*)=0$ 的芽 $h$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:freegaussproductneighborhood}，存在 $x^*$ 的普通乘积邻域 $U\times_KD$，使 $h$ 在其上表示，其中 $D$ 为包含半径 $r$ 的开圆环。因 $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 为域，它正规；Corollary~\ref{fnd:charzeronormallocus} 与 Lemma~\ref{fnd:normalcomponentidentity} 给出 $x$ 的正规整 affinoid 普通邻域 $\mathcal M(A)\subset U$。映射 $A\to\mathcal O_{X,x}\hookrightarrow F$ 单射：前一映射是从整环出发的非零平坦映射，后一映射为剩余域嵌入。}

\bi{Choose a closed annulus $D_0\subset D$ with $r$ strictly between its two radii. The restriction of $h$ to $\mathcal M(A)\times_KD_0$ has a unique convergent Laurent expansion $\sum_{j\in\mathbf Z}a_jT^j$, with $a_j\in A$. This is the weighted Laurent description of the annulus algebra; uniqueness is preserved under restriction to smaller annuli. At the Gauss point $x^*$ its value is}
{在 $D$ 中选择闭圆环 $D_0$，使 $r$ 严格位于其两半径之间。$h$ 在 $\mathcal M(A)\times_KD_0$ 上的限制有唯一收敛 Laurent 展开 $\sum_{j\in\mathbf Z}a_jT^j$，其中 $a_j\in A$。这是圆环代数的加权 Laurent 描述，而向较小圆环限制保持唯一性。在 Gauss 点 $x^*$ 处，其值为}
\[
                         |h(x^*)|=\max_j |a_j(x)|r^j.
\]
\bi{Thus every $a_j(x)$ is zero. Injectivity of $A\to F$ makes all $a_j$ zero simultaneously, so $h$ vanishes on this product, which is an ordinary neighborhood of $x^*$. The maximal ideal of $\mathcal O_{X^*,x^*}$ is exactly the kernel of evaluation in its completed residue field. We have proved that kernel zero, so this local ring is a field.}
{故每个 $a_j(x)$ 均为零。由 $A\to F$ 的单射性，全部 $a_j$ 同时为零，所以 $h$ 在该乘积上消失；此乘积是 $x^*$ 的普通邻域。$\mathcal O_{X^*,x^*}$ 的极大理想恰是向其完备剩余域取值的核。刚才已证明此核为零，因此该局部环为域。}
\end{proof}
