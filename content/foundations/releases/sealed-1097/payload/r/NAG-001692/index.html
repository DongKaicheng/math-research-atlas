Affinoid fibres at all Berkovich points

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 275

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Affinoid fibres at all Berkovich points}{全部 Berkovich 点上的 affinoid 纤维}]
\label{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres}
\bi{Let $\phi:A\to B$ be a bounded homomorphism of generalized $K$-affinoid algebras, with spectral map $f:\mathcal M(B)\to\mathcal M(A)$. For $x\in\mathcal M(A)$ put $F=\mathcal H(x)$ and}
{设 $\phi:A\to B$ 为广义 $K$-affinoid 代数的有界同态，其谱映射为 $f:\mathcal M(B)\to\mathcal M(A)$。对 $x\in\mathcal M(A)$，令 $F=\mathcal H(x)$，并记}
\[
 B_x=B\widehat\otimes_A F .
\]
\bi{Then $B_x$ is a generalized $F$-affinoid algebra. Restriction induces a homeomorphism}
{则 $B_x$ 为广义 $F$-affinoid 代数。限制映射诱导同胚}
\[
 \mathcal M_F(B_x)\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }f^{-1}(x).
\]
\bi{For the point $z$ corresponding to $y\in f^{-1}(x)$, the structural map gives a canonical isometric isomorphism $\mathcal H_B(y)\simeq\mathcal H_{B_x}(z)$ compatible with the embedding $F\hookrightarrow\mathcal H_B(y)$.}
{若点 $z$ 对应于 $y\in f^{-1}(x)$，则结构映射给出典范等距同构 $\mathcal H_B(y)\simeq\mathcal H_{B_x}(z)$，且与嵌入 $F\hookrightarrow\mathcal H_B(y)$ 相容。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The map $A\to F$ is bounded, and $F$ is affinoid over itself. Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout} proves the affinoid assertion; in a relative presentation $B=A\{r^{-1}T\}/I$, it gives $B_x=F\{r^{-1}T\}/I_F$.}
{映射 $A\to F$ 有界，而 $F$ 是自身之上的 affinoid。Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout} 证明了 affinoid 断言；若使用相对表示 $B=A\{r^{-1}T\}/I$，则得到 $B_x=F\{r^{-1}T\}/I_F$。}

\bi{For $y\in f^{-1}(x)$, Proposition~\ref{new:residueuniversal} supplies an isometric embedding $j:F\hookrightarrow\mathcal H_B(y)$ agreeing with $A\to B\to\mathcal H_B(y)$. The compatible maps from $B$ and $F$ extend uniquely to a bounded homomorphism $\rho_y:B_x\to\mathcal H_B(y)$. Its absolute value defines a point $z_y\in\mathcal M_F(B_x)$ restricting to $y$. Conversely, a point $z\in\mathcal M_F(B_x)$ restricts to a point $y\in\mathcal M(B)$ with $f(y)=x$, since its values on the factor $F$ are the prescribed absolute value of $F$.}
{对 $y\in f^{-1}(x)$，由 Proposition~\ref{new:residueuniversal}，存在等距嵌入 $j:F\hookrightarrow\mathcal H_B(y)$，与 $A\to B\to\mathcal H_B(y)$ 相容。来自 $B,F$ 的相容映射唯一延拓为有界同态 $\rho_y:B_x\to\mathcal H_B(y)$。它的绝对值定义一个限制为 $y$ 的点 $z_y\in\mathcal M_F(B_x)$。反之，点 $z\in\mathcal M_F(B_x)$ 限制为某个 $y\in\mathcal M(B)$，且 $f(y)=x$，因为其在因子 $F$ 上的取值就是 $F$ 的指定绝对值。}

\bi{To prove uniqueness, let $i:\mathcal H_B(y)\hookrightarrow\mathcal H_{B_x}(z)$ be the residue embedding induced by $B\to B_x$. The structural embedding $F\to\mathcal H_{B_x}(z)$ and $i\circ j$ agree on $A$, hence on $\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$, and then on $F$ by density. Consequently evaluation at $z$ equals $i\circ\rho_y$ on $B\otimes_A F$ and, by continuity, on $B_x$. Its seminorm is therefore $z_y$. Moreover the image $i(\mathcal H_B(y))$ is a closed field containing the evaluated image of $B_x$. The closure of its fraction field is $\mathcal H_{B_x}(z)$; hence $i$ is surjective. This proves the residue-field assertion as well as bijectivity.}
{为证明唯一性，令 $i:\mathcal H_B(y)\hookrightarrow\mathcal H_{B_x}(z)$ 为 $B\to B_x$ 诱导的剩余域嵌入。结构嵌入 $F\to\mathcal H_{B_x}(z)$ 与 $i\circ j$ 在 $A$ 上一致，故在 $\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$ 上一致，再由稠密性在 $F$ 上一致。因此 $z$ 处的取值在 $B\otimes_A F$ 上等于 $i\circ\rho_y$，再由连续性在 $B_x$ 上相等，所以其半范数就是 $z_y$。此外，像 $i(\mathcal H_B(y))$ 是包含 $B_x$ 取值像的闭子域。后者的分式域之闭包为 $\mathcal H_{B_x}(z)$，故 $i$ 满。这同时证明剩余域断言及双射性。}

\bi{Restriction is continuous, since evaluation on $b\in B$ is evaluation on $b\otimes1$. The source is compact and the target is a subspace of the Hausdorff space $\mathcal M(B)$ by Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact}. A continuous bijection from a compact space to a Hausdorff space is a homeomorphism: closed subsets of the source are compact, and their images are compact and therefore closed. The argument includes the empty fibre, when $B_x=0$.}
{限制映射连续，因为对 $b\in B$ 取值就是对 $b\otimes1$ 取值。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact}，源空间紧，而目标是 Hausdorff 空间 $\mathcal M(B)$ 的子空间。从紧空间到 Hausdorff 空间的连续双射为同胚：源空间的闭集紧，其像也紧，因而闭。该论证包括 $B_x=0$ 时的空纤维。}
\end{proof}
