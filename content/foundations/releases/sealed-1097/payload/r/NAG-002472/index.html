\begin{definition}[The two generating families]\label{f8cont:def:stages}
A cutoff presentation $i=(V,f,\Omega,\chi)$ consists of an affinoid
domain $V\subset X$, a unit tuple $f$ on $V$, an open set
$\Omega\supset t_f(V)$, and
$\chi\in\A_c^{0,0}(X)$ with $\supp\chi\subset\Int(V,X)$.
Here $t_f=-\log|f|$. Its map is
\[
 Q_i:E^{p,q}(\Omega)\longrightarrow\E^{p,q}(X),\qquad
 Q_i\eta=\ext(\chi t_f^*\eta).
\]
Let $\tau_{\mathrm{cut}}$ be the locally convex topology generated by all
seminorms $P$ on $\E^{p,q}(X)$ for which $P Q_i$ is continuous for every $i$.
This is the definition F8-T1 in the supplied candidate.

An ordinary presentation $s$ consists of finitely many such charts
$(V_j,f_j,\Omega_j)$, without cutoffs, and a compact set
$K_s\subset\bigcup_j U_j$, where $U_j=\Int(V_j,X)$.
Let
\[
 H_s\subset\prod_j E^{p,q}(\Omega_j)
\]
be the linear subspace of tuples $(\eta_j)$ for which the induced ordinary
sections agree on $U_j\cap U_k$ and vanish on $U_j\setminus K_s$.
Give $H_s$ its coefficient subspace topology. The sections glue with zero
on $X\setminus K_s$ to a section $R_s\eta\in\E^{p,q}(X)$.
Give $B_s=H_s/\ker R_s$ its quotient topology and let
$\tau_{\mathrm{ord}}$ be the final locally convex topology for the maps
$B_s\to\E^{p,q}(X)$, equivalently for all $R_s$.
\end{definition}
