Structure sheaf and rational comparison

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 376

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Structure sheaf and rational comparison}{结构层与 rational 比较}]
\label{fnd:generalizeddomainsheafcomparison}
\bi{The finite coverings define a pretopology on $\mathcal D_X$. The displayed assignments are a ring sheaf and module sheaves. The functor $M\mapsto\widetilde M^{\mathrm{aff}}$ is exact and fully faithful, with $\Gamma(X,\widetilde M^{\mathrm{aff}})=M$. Inclusion of the arbitrary-radius rational site induces an equivalence of ringed topoi}
{这些有限覆盖定义 $\mathcal D_X$ 上的预拓扑。上述赋值分别是环层与模层。函子 $M\mapsto\widetilde M^{\mathrm{aff}}$ 正合且全忠实，并满足 $\Gamma(X,\widetilde M^{\mathrm{aff}})=M$。任意半径 rational site 的包含诱导赋环 topoi 的等价}
\[
 (\mathcal R_X,\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}})
 \simeq (\mathcal D_X,\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{aff}}),
\]
\bi{identifying the sheaves associated to the same finite module.}
{并识别同一个有限模所给的层。}
\end{theorem}
Selected proof part 1 · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Affinoid-domain intersections and restrictions exist and are compatible. Finite covers are stable under restriction and composition, proving the pretopology axioms. A finite cover of an object $V$ is a finite affinoid-domain cover over $A_V$. Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidcovercech} to $A_V\otimes_A M$; tensor associativity gives exactly the equalizer for the displayed presheaf. This proves the sheaf axiom, and $M=A$ gives the ring sheaf. Restriction flatness proves exactness. Evaluation at $X$ gives $M$. A module-sheaf map is determined on $V$ by $b\otimes m\mapsto b\otimes u(m)$, where $u$ is its map at $X$; conversely this formula defines a sheaf map for every $A$-linear $u$. Thus the functor is fully faithful.}
{Affinoid 域的交及限制已经构造，并彼此相容。有限覆盖在限制与复合下保持，故满足预拓扑公理。对象 $V$ 的有限覆盖是 $A_V$ 上的有限 affinoid 域覆盖。对 $A_V\otimes_A M$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidcovercech}；由张量积结合律，所得等化子恰为上述预层的等化子。这证明层公理；取 $M=A$ 得到环层。限制平坦性给出正合性。在 $X$ 上取值为 $M$。模层映射在 $V$ 上由 $b\otimes m\mapsto b\otimes u(m)$ 决定，其中 $u$ 是它在 $X$ 上的映射；反之，每个 $A$-线性 $u$ 都以此公式定义层映射。因此函子全忠实。}
Selected proof part 2 · exact TeX
\bi{For the comparison apply \cite[Tag 03A0, Lemma 7.29.1]{StacksAlg}. The inclusion is fully faithful and preserves covers and finite intersections, so restriction of sheaves is continuous. Every object of $\mathcal D_X$ has a finite covering by original-$A$ rational objects, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert}. For a cover of a rational object by affinoid domains, refine each member by such a cover; their finite union is a rational refinement. This verifies cocontinuity as well as the covering condition in the comparison lemma. Local fullness and faithfulness follow from full faithfulness of the inclusion. On rational objects the representing algebras and restrictions agree by uniqueness, so the resulting equivalence identifies the ring sheaves and the finite-module sheaves as stated.}
{对 site 比较，应用 \cite[Tag 03A0, Lemma 7.29.1]{StacksAlg}。包含全忠实，并保持覆盖及有限交，所以层的限制是 continuous。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert}，$\mathcal D_X$ 的每个对象都有有限个原 $A$-rational 对象组成的覆盖。若 rational 对象由 affinoid 域覆盖，则用这样的 rational 覆盖加细每个成员，合并后的有限族就是 rational 加细。这同时核验 cocontinuity 及比较引理的覆盖条件。局部满性与忠实性来自包含的全忠实性。在 rational 对象上，表示代数及限制由唯一性相同。因此所得等价按所述方式识别环层和有限模层。}
\end{proof}
