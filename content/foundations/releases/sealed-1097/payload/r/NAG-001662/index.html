Scalar invariance of strict affinoid dimension

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 599

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Scalar invariance of strict affinoid dimension}
{Strict affinoid 维数的扩标量不变性}]
\label{fnd:strictdimensionbasechange}
\bi{Let $A$ be a nonzero strict $K$-affinoid algebra and let $L/K$
be any complete valued extension. Then $A_L$ is strict and
$\dim A_L=\dim A$.}
{设 $A$ 为非零 strict $K$-affinoid 代数，$L/K$ 为任意完备赋值扩域。
则 $A_L$ 为 strict，且 $\dim A_L=\dim A$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{By Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization}, choose a finite
injection $R=K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_d\rangle\hookrightarrow A$.
Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttatedimension} gives $d=\dim A$.
The finite $R$-module topology on $A$ is its affinoid topology.
Indeed finite generators give a bounded surjection $R^m\to A$;
when $K$ is nontrivially valued, Lemma~\ref{fnd:nonarchopenmapping}
makes it norm-strict. When $K$ is trivially valued, the defining
strict quotient norms on $R$ and $A$, and the finite-module
quotient norm, take only the values zero and one, so they agree.
The injection is now norm-strict: the image of the finite $R$-submodule
$R$ is closed with its canonical topology by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}.
The strict exactness of completed scalar tensoring in
Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonstrictexact} therefore gives an injection
$R_L\hookrightarrow A_L$. The finite-module base-change theorem
\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange} shows that this is a finite map.
The scalar presentation theorem~\ref{fnd:generalscalarpresentation}
identifies $R_L$ with $L\langle T_1,\ldots,T_d\rangle$ and makes
$A_L$ strict. Applying Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttatedimension}
over $L$ gives $\dim A_L=d$.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization}，可选有限单射
$R=K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_d\rangle\hookrightarrow A$。
Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttatedimension} 给出 $d=\dim A$。
$A$ 的有限 $R$-模拓扑就是其 affinoid 拓扑。确实，有限生成元
给出有界满射 $R^m\to A$；当 $K$ 的赋值非平凡时，
Lemma~\ref{fnd:nonarchopenmapping} 说明它为 norm-strict。
当 $K$ 的赋值平凡时，$R,A$ 的定义 strict 商范数以及有限模
商范数都只取零和一，因而相同。于是原单射为 norm-strict：
由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}，有限
$R$-子模 $R$ 的像闭且带有典范拓扑。
因此 Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonstrictexact} 中完备扩标量的严格
正合性给出单射 $R_L\hookrightarrow A_L$。
有限模基变换定理~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange}
说明它仍为有限映射。扩标量表示定理
\ref{fnd:generalscalarpresentation} 将 $R_L$ 识别为
$L\langle T_1,\ldots,T_d\rangle$，并说明 $A_L$ 为 strict。
在 $L$ 上应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttatedimension}，
即得 $\dim A_L=d$。}
\end{proof}
