Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Kiehl and Theorem B on the analytic G-site}{解析 G-site 上的 Kiehl 与 Theorem B}]
\label{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb}
\bi{The functors}
{函子}
\[
 \operatorname{Mod}^{\mathrm{fin}}(A)
 \ \rightleftarrows\ \operatorname{Coh}(X_G),
 \qquad M\longmapsto\widetilde M,
 \qquad\mathcal F\longmapsto\Gamma(X,\mathcal F)
\]
\bi{are inverse exact equivalences, where $\widetilde M(V)=A_V\otimes_A M$ on affinoid domains. Every coherent sheaf has a global finite free presentation. For every affinoid domain $V$ and every $q>0$,}
{互逆且正合，其中在 affinoid 域上有 $\widetilde M(V)=A_V\otimes_A M$。每个凝聚层都有整体有限自由表示。对每个 affinoid 域 $V$ 及每个 $q>0$，均有}
\[
 H^q(V_G,\mathcal F|_{V_G})=0.
\]
\bi{The cohomology is derived sheaf cohomology on the full analytic G-site.}
{这里的上同调是在完整解析 G-site 上的导出层上同调。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidfullgcomparison} is the cocontinuous ringed-site comparison of \cite[Tag 03A0]{StacksAlg}, so \cite[Tag 03DM]{StacksAlg} preserves coherence and finite presentation. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainkiehl} therefore gives the equivalence and the global presentation. The equivalence preserves evaluation on each common affinoid object. On the slice at $V$, Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidanalyticdomaincalculus} identifies the site with $V_G$. The corresponding equivalence of abelian sheaf categories is exact and has an exact inverse. It preserves injective objects: apply the inverse equivalence to a monomorphism and use the defining extension property of an injective. Thus an injective resolution may be transported across the equivalence, and its section complexes agree. Their cohomology groups agree as well. Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidtheoremb} now gives the asserted vanishing.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidfullgcomparison} 是 \cite[Tag 03A0]{StacksAlg} 的 cocontinuous 赋环 site 比较，所以 \cite[Tag 03DM]{StacksAlg} 保持凝聚性及有限表示。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainkiehl} 因此给出等价与整体表示。该等价保持在每个公共 affinoid 对象上的取值。在 $V$ 的 slice 上，Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidanalyticdomaincalculus} 将 site 识别为 $V_G$。相应阿贝尔层范畴的等价正合，且逆也正合。它保持内射对象：对一个单射应用逆等价，再使用内射对象的延拓性质即可。因此内射分解可以沿等价转移，所得截面复形相同，其上同调群也相同。最后由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidtheoremb} 得到所述消失。}
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[\bi{Quasi-nets and analytic domains}{Quasi-net 与解析域}]
\label{fnd:affinoidanalyticquasinet}
\bi{A family $(V_i)$ of subsets of a topological space $V$ is a quasi-net if, for every $x\in V$, some finite subfamily consists of sets containing $x$ and has union containing a neighborhood of $x$ in $V$. Let $X=\mathcal M(A)$. A subset $V\subseteq X$ is an analytic domain if it has a quasi-net covering by affinoid domains of $X$, with the induced topology on $V$. A family of analytic subdomains of $V$ is an admissible covering if it is a quasi-net on $V$. The empty family covers the empty domain.}
{若拓扑空间 $V$ 的一个子集族 $(V_i)$ 满足：对每个 $x\in V$，存在一个有限子族，其所有成员都包含 $x$，且其并包含 $x$ 在 $V$ 中的一个邻域，则称该族为 quasi-net。令 $X=\mathcal M(A)$。若子集 $V\subseteq X$ 具有由 $X$ 的 affinoid 域组成的 quasi-net 覆盖，则称它为解析域，并采用诱导拓扑。$V$ 的解析子域族若为 $V$ 上的 quasi-net，则称为 admissible 覆盖。空族覆盖空域。}
\end{definition}
