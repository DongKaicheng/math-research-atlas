The strict-domain structure sheaf and rational comparison

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 255

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The strict-domain structure sheaf and rational comparison}{Strict 域结构层与 rational 比较}]\label{fnd:strictdomainsheafcomparison}
\bi{The coverings of $\mathcal D_X$ form a pretopology. The displayed assignments are a ring sheaf and module sheaves, independent up to canonical isomorphism of the representing algebras. The functor $M\mapsto\widetilde M^{\mathrm{str}}$ is exact and fully faithful, and its global sections are $M$. Inclusion $\mathcal R_X\hookrightarrow\mathcal D_X$ induces an equivalence of ringed topoi, carrying $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{str}}$ to $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}}$ and $\widetilde M^{\mathrm{str}}$ to $\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$.}
{上述覆盖构成 $\mathcal D_X$ 上的预拓扑。所列赋值分别为环层与模层，并且在典范同构意义下与表示代数的选择无关。函子 $M\mapsto\widetilde M^{\mathrm{str}}$ 正合且全忠实，其整体截面为 $M$。包含 $\mathcal R_X\hookrightarrow\mathcal D_X$ 诱导赋环 topoi 的等价，将 $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{str}}$ 对应于 $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}}$，并将 $\widetilde M^{\mathrm{str}}$ 对应于 $\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Intersections exist by Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttestdomainintersections}. Pulling back and composing finite set-theoretic covers proves the pretopology axioms. For a covering of an object $V$, its members and all intersections are strict-test domains over $A_V$ by Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalrestrictiondomainbridge}. Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictaffinoidcovercech} over $A_V$ to the finite module $A_V\otimes_A M$. Tensor associativity identifies its terms with the displayed presheaf. Degree-zero exactness is precisely the sheaf equalizer condition. The empty value is zero. Taking $M=A$ gives the ring sheaf. Uniqueness of all restriction maps proves independence of the choices.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttestdomainintersections}，交叠存在。拉回及复合有限集合覆盖，便核验了预拓扑公理。对对象 $V$ 的一个覆盖，由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalrestrictiondomainbridge}，其成员及全部交叠均为 $A_V$ 上的 strict-test 域。将 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictaffinoidcovercech} 应用于 $A_V$ 及有限模 $A_V\otimes_A M$。由张量积结合律，其各项与所列预层一致。零次正合性恰为层的 equalizer 条件；空对象上的值为零。取 $M=A$ 得到环层。全部限制映射的唯一性证明与选择无关。}

\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictdomainflatness} gives objectwise exactness for finite-module short exact sequences. At $X$ the value is $M$. A sheaf map between two such module sheaves is determined by its map $u$ at $X$, because on each $V$ it must send $b\otimes m$ to $b\otimes u(m)$. Conversely this formula defines a sheaf map for every $A$-linear $u$. Thus the functor is exact and fully faithful.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictdomainflatness}，有限模短正合列逐对象正合。在 $X$ 上取值为 $M$。两个这样的模层之间的层映射，由它在 $X$ 上的映射 $u$ 决定，因为它在每个 $V$ 上必须将 $b\otimes m$ 送到 $b\otimes u(m)$。反之，每个 $A$-线性映射 $u$ 都由此公式定义层映射。因此该函子正合且全忠实。}

\bi{For the site comparison apply the authorized comparison lemma \cite[Tag 03A0, Lemma 7.29.1]{StacksAlg}. Its hypotheses can be checked directly here. Inclusion is fully faithful, so both local faithfulness and local fullness hold. It preserves covers and finite intersections; restriction of a sheaf is therefore a sheaf, so it is continuous. For a covering of a rational object by strict-test domains, refine each member by its finite $A$-rational covering from Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis}. Their union is a covering in $\mathcal R_X$ refining the given one, which is cocontinuity. The same corollary covers every object of $\mathcal D_X$ by objects from $\mathcal R_X$. These are exactly the five hypotheses of the comparison lemma, including the empty object. It gives an equivalence of topoi whose restriction functor is the inclusion-induced restriction of sheaves. On rational objects the chosen representing algebras and restrictions agree by domain uniqueness. Thus the ring sheaves and the finite-module sheaves restrict as asserted, making this an equivalence of ringed topoi.}
{对 site 比较，应用已获准的比较引理 \cite[Tag 03A0, Lemma 7.29.1]{StacksAlg}。其假设在这里可以直接核验。包含函子全忠实，故局部忠实与局部满性都成立。它保持覆盖与有限交叠，因此层的限制仍为层，即它是 continuous。若某个 rational 对象由 strict-test 域覆盖，用 Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis} 给出的有限 $A$-rational 覆盖分别加细每个成员；合并后便得到 $\mathcal R_X$ 中细化原覆盖的覆盖，这证明 cocontinuity。同一 corollary 也用 $\mathcal R_X$ 的对象覆盖 $\mathcal D_X$ 的每个对象。这恰好核验了比较引理的五项假设，包括空对象。于是得到 topoi 的等价，其限制函子就是沿包含函子限制层。在 rational 对象上，由域的唯一性，所选表示代数及限制映射相同。因此环层与有限模层按所述方式限制，从而得到赋环 topoi 的等价。}
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[\bi{The finite strict-domain site}{有限 strict 域 site}]\label{fnd:finitestrictdomainsite}
\bi{For $K$ complete, with trivial valuation allowed, and $X=\mathcal M(A)$ strict, let $\mathcal D_X$ be the partially ordered category of strict-test domains in $X$, with inclusions as arrows. Coverings are finite set-theoretic coverings by objects, including the empty covering of the empty set. Choose representing algebras $A_V$, with $A_X=A$ and $A_\varnothing=0$. Use the unique bounded restrictions of Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalrestrictiondomainbridge}, and set}
{设 $K$ 完备，允许平凡赋值，$X=\mathcal M(A)$ 为 strict。令 $\mathcal D_X$ 为 $X$ 中 strict-test 域构成的偏序范畴，箭头为包含。覆盖为由对象组成的有限集合覆盖，包括空集的空覆盖。选择表示代数 $A_V$，其中 $A_X=A$、$A_\varnothing=0$。使用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalrestrictiondomainbridge} 给出的唯一有界限制，并记}
\[
 \mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{str}}(V)=A_V,
 \qquad \widetilde M^{\mathrm{str}}(V)=A_V\otimes_A M
 \quad(M\text{ finite over }A).
\]
\end{definition}
