GAGA for projective schemes

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 408

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{GAGA for projective schemes}{射影概形的 GAGA}]
\label{fnd:projectiveschemegaga}
\bi{Let \(Z\) be a projective scheme over a complete
non-Archimedean field \(K\). Analytification induces an exact
equivalence between coherent algebraic sheaves on \(Z\)
and coherent sheaves on \(Z^{\an}_G\). For every coherent
algebraic sheaf \(\mathcal E\), the canonical maps}
{设 \(Z\) 为完备非阿基米德域 \(K\) 上的射影概形。
解析化在 \(Z\) 上的代数凝聚层与 \(Z^{\an}_G\) 上的
凝聚层之间诱导正合范畴等价。对每个代数凝聚层
\(\mathcal E\)，典范映射}
\[
 H^q(Z,\mathcal E)\longrightarrow
 H^q(Z^{\an}_G,\mathcal E^{\an})
\]
\bi{are isomorphisms in every degree.}
{在每个次数都是同构。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose a closed immersion \(i:Z\hookrightarrow Q=\mathbf P^n_K\)
with coherent ideal \(\mathcal I\), and write \(P=Q^{\an}\).
On each affine chart, modules over the quotient ring are exactly
modules over the original ring annihilated by the ideal. This
identification, and its analogue for finite affinoid modules,
commute with restriction. Thus algebraic closed pushforward
and the analytic closed pushforward of
Lemma~\ref{fnd:closedanalyticcoherentextension} identify
coherent sheaves on \(Z\) and \(Z^{\an}_G\) with the
respective ideal-annihilated subcategories on \(Q\) and \(P_G\).}
{选择闭浸入 \(i:Z\hookrightarrow Q=\mathbf P^n_K\)，
凝聚理想记为 \(\mathcal I\)，并令 \(P=Q^{\an}\)。
在每个仿射片上，商环上的模就是原环上被该理想消灭的模。
此识别及其有限 affinoid 模的对应识别均与限制相容。
因此，代数闭推前及
Lemma~\ref{fnd:closedanalyticcoherentextension}
中的解析闭推前，将 \(Z\) 和 \(Z^{\an}_G\) 上的
凝聚层分别识别为 \(Q\) 和 \(P_G\) 上被相应理想消灭的
凝聚层子范畴。}

\bi{For an analytic coherent sheaf \(\mathcal F\) on \(Z^{\an}_G\),
Theorem~\ref{fnd:projectivegagainternal} algebraizes
\(i^{\an}_*\mathcal F\) to a coherent \(\mathcal G\) on \(Q\).
The multiplication map
\(\mathcal I\otimes\mathcal G\to\mathcal G\) becomes zero
after analytification. Analytification commutes with this tensor
product, as follows from finite presentations on affine and
affinoid charts. Full faithfulness on \(Q\) therefore makes the
algebraic multiplication map zero as well. Hence
\(\mathcal G=i_*\mathcal E\) for a coherent \(\mathcal E\)
on \(Z\), and \(\mathcal E^{\an}\simeq\mathcal F\).
Full faithfulness restricts to these subcategories, and exactness
is Theorem~\ref{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact}.}
{对 \(Z^{\an}_G\) 上的解析凝聚层 \(\mathcal F\)，
Theorem~\ref{fnd:projectivegagainternal} 将
\(i^{\an}_*\mathcal F\) 代数化为 \(Q\) 上的凝聚层
\(\mathcal G\)。乘法映射
\(\mathcal I\otimes\mathcal G\to\mathcal G\)
在解析化后为零。由仿射与 affinoid 片上的有限表示，
解析化与这个张量积相容；故 \(Q\) 上的全忠实性说明
代数乘法映射也为零。因此 \(\mathcal G=i_*\mathcal E\)，
其中 \(\mathcal E\) 是 \(Z\) 上的凝聚层，且
\(\mathcal E^{\an}\simeq\mathcal F\)。
全忠实性限制到这两个子范畴，正合性由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact} 给出。}

\bi{Finally, closed pushforward does not change the relevant
cohomology. Analytically this is
Lemma~\ref{fnd:closedanalyticcoherentextension}.
Algebraically, use the standard finite affine cover of \(Q\)
and its pullback to \(Z\); both have affine intersections.
By \cite[Tag 01XB]{StacksAlg} and
\cite[Tag 03F7]{StacksAlg}, their ordered \v Cech complexes
compute cohomology. The two complexes are identical for
\(i_*\mathcal E\) and \(\mathcal E\).
The comparison maps commute with this identification because
all maps are induced by restriction of the same sections.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:projectivecoherentcomparison} on \(Q\)
now gives the asserted comparisons on \(Z\).}
{最后，闭推前不改变这里的上同调。解析一侧已由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:closedanalyticcoherentextension} 证明。
代数一侧使用 \(Q\) 的标准有限仿射覆盖及其在 \(Z\)
上的拉回；二者的各交均仿射。由
\cite[Tag 01XB]{StacksAlg} 和 \cite[Tag 03F7]{StacksAlg}，
其有序 \v Cech 复形计算上同调，而对
\(i_*\mathcal E\) 和 \(\mathcal E\)，这两个复形相同。
所有映射都由同一截面的限制诱导，故比较映射与此识别相容。
在 \(Q\) 上应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:projectivecoherentcomparison}，
即得 \(Z\) 上的比较同构。}
\end{proof}
