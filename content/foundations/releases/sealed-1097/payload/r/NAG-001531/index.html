Kiehl equivalence on the fixed rational site

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 242

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Kiehl equivalence on the fixed rational site}{固定 rational site 上的 Kiehl 等价}]\label{fnd:fixedrationalkiehl}
\bi{Let $K$ be complete, with trivial valuation allowed, and let $A$ be strict norm-admissible. On the ringed site $(\mathcal R_X,\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}})$, the following conditions on an $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}}$-module sheaf $\mathcal F$ are equivalent:}
{设 $K$ 完备，允许赋值平凡，且 $A$ 为 strict norm-admissible。在赋环 site $(\mathcal R_X,\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}})$ 上，关于 $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}}$-模层 $\mathcal F$ 的下列条件等价：}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \bi{$\mathcal F$ is coherent in the ringed-site sense of \cite[Tag 03DL, Definition 18.23.1(8)]{StacksAlg}: it is of finite type, and on each slice the kernel of every map from a finite free module to it is of finite type.}
{$\mathcal F$ 在 \cite[Tag 03DL, Definition 18.23.1(8)]{StacksAlg} 的赋环 site 意义下凝聚：它是有限型的，且在每个 slice 上，从有限自由模到它的任一映射的核都是有限型的。}
\item \bi{$\mathcal F$ is of finite presentation in the sense of the same definition, part~(7).}
{$\mathcal F$ 在同一定义的第~(7) 项意义下具有有限表示。}
\item \bi{Some finite covering of $X$ in $\mathcal R_X$ admits finite module models for $\mathcal F$ on every member.}
{$\mathcal R_X$ 中存在 $X$ 的某个有限覆盖，使 $\mathcal F$ 在每个成员上都有有限模模型。}
\item \bi{There is a finite $A$-module $M$ with $\mathcal F\simeq\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$.}
{存在有限 $A$-模 $M$，使 $\mathcal F\simeq\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$。}
\end{enumerate}
\bi{Consequently $M\mapsto\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$ is an exact equivalence from finite $A$-modules to coherent modules on this ringed site, with inverse $\mathcal F\mapsto\Gamma(X,\mathcal F)$. Every such sheaf has a global finite presentation by finite free $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}}$-modules. For any finite covering in this site, its augmented \v Cech complex is norm-strict exact with the induced canonical finite-module norms. This is the stated fixed-rational-site result; no identification with the full analytic G-site or with all generalized affinoid domains is included.}
{因此，$M\mapsto\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$ 给出从有限 $A$-模到该赋环 site 上凝聚模的正合范畴等价，其逆为 $\mathcal F\mapsto\Gamma(X,\mathcal F)$。每个这样的层都具有由有限自由 $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}}$-模给出的整体有限表示。对该 site 中的任意有限覆盖，其增广 \v Cech 复形在诱导的 canonical 有限模范数下 norm-strict 正合。这是所述固定 rational site 的结论，不包含它与完整 analytic G-site 或全部 generalized affinoid 域的识别。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{We first compare the local conditions without using descent. If $\mathcal F$ is coherent, finite type gives a finite covering of $X$ on whose members there are surjections $\mathcal O_V^n\to\mathcal F|_V$. By coherence each kernel is of finite type. A further finite covering supplies finitely many generators for that kernel, hence presentations $\mathcal O_W^m\to\mathcal O_W^n\to\mathcal F|_W\to0$. The finite composite covering proves (2). More generally, covers of any object are obtained by restriction of these covers, so this is the ringed-site definition, not only a condition at $X$.}
{先比较局部条件，而不使用下降。若 $\mathcal F$ 凝聚，则由有限型条件，$X$ 存在有限覆盖，使每个成员上都有满射 $\mathcal O_V^n\to\mathcal F|_V$。由凝聚性，每个核均为有限型。在进一步的有限覆盖上，可以为该核选择有限生成元，从而得到表示 $\mathcal O_W^m\to\mathcal O_W^n\to\mathcal F|_W\to0$。有限复合覆盖证明 (2)。对任意对象限制这些覆盖，可得到同样的结论，因此这满足赋环 site 的定义，而不只是 $X$ 上的条件。}

\bi{For (2)$\Rightarrow$(3), take a finite covering with the displayed finite presentations. A morphism between finite free sheaves on a slice is a matrix with entries in $\mathcal O_W(W)=A_W$: its columns are the images of the standard global basis sections. Let $P$ be the cokernel of this matrix over $A_W$. It is finite. By exactness of Lemma~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalslicemodules}, the sheaf cokernel is precisely $\widetilde P_W$. Thus these are the required finite models.}
{对 (2)$\Rightarrow$(3)，选取具有上述有限表示的有限覆盖。Slice 上有限自由层之间的态射由 $\mathcal O_W(W)=A_W$ 中的矩阵给出：各列就是标准整体基截面的像。令 $P$ 为该矩阵在 $A_W$ 上的余核，则 $P$ 有限。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalslicemodules} 的正合性，层余核正是 $\widetilde P_W$。因此得到所需的有限模型。}

\bi{Assume (3), first with nontrivial valuation. Proposition~\ref{fnd:originalrationaltreerefinement} gives a subordinate finite Laurent tree whose every node remains in $\mathcal R_X$. A leaf contained in a model object inherits its finite model by Lemma~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalslicemodules}. Proposition~\ref{fnd:fixedrationaltreedescent} then gives $\mathcal F\simeq\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$ with $M=\mathcal F(X)$ finite. This proves (4) in this valuation case.}
{假设 (3) 成立，先考虑非平凡赋值。Proposition~\ref{fnd:originalrationaltreerefinement} 给出从属的有限 Laurent 树，且每个节点都保留在 $\mathcal R_X$ 中。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalslicemodules}，包含于一个模型对象中的叶节点继承有限模型。于是 Proposition~\ref{fnd:fixedrationaltreedescent} 给出 $\mathcal F\simeq\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$，其中 $M=\mathcal F(X)$ 有限。这证明此赋值情形下的 (4)。}

\bi{For trivial valuation choose a defining strict quotient norm. The finite-tail condition makes each unit-radius Tate algebra an ordinary polynomial algebra, and each nonzero quotient element has norm one. For a covering member with rational tuple $(g_i;f^{(i)}_1,\ldots,f^{(i)}_{s_i})$, the unit certificate makes $g_i$ invertible in its actual relation quotient. Substitution $T_j=f^{(i)}_j/g_i$ therefore gives}
{对平凡赋值，选取定义 strict 商范数。有限尾条件使每个单位半径的 Tate 代数成为普通多项式代数，且每个非零商元素的范数为一。对具有 rational tuple $(g_i;f^{(i)}_1,\ldots,f^{(i)}_{s_i})$ 的覆盖成员，单位理想证书使 $g_i$ 在实际关系商中可逆。因此代入 $T_j=f^{(i)}_j/g_i$ 给出}
\[
 B_i=A_{V_i}\simeq A[1/g_i],\qquad
 A_{V_i\cap V_j}\simeq A[1/(g_ig_j)]\simeq B_i\otimes_A B_j,
\]
\bi{and likewise for triple intersections, with the actual restriction maps. This is the discrete-norm computation also used in Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfiniterationalcech}; here it verifies ordinary, not merely completed, tensor products. Put $S=\prod_i B_i$. Corollary~\ref{fnd:rationalcoverfaithfullyflat} makes $A\to S$ faithfully flat. The finite local model modules $P_i$ give the finite $S$-module $P=\prod_iP_i$. Their sheaf identifications on pairwise intersections, evaluated there, give an ordinary descent datum for $A\to S$: the decomposition of $S\otimes_A S$ by its finitely many factor idempotents has components $B_i\otimes_A B_j$, and the cocycle identity is the identity of the three restriction identifications on each triple intersection.}
{三重交叠也有相同结论，且与实际限制映射相容。这也是 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfiniterationalcech} 中使用的离散范数计算；此处它核验的是普通张量积，而不只是完备张量积。令 $S=\prod_i B_i$。由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:rationalcoverfaithfullyflat}，$A\to S$ 忠实平坦。有限局部模型模 $P_i$ 给出有限 $S$-模 $P=\prod_iP_i$。在两两交叠上对层同构取值，即得到 $A\to S$ 的普通下降数据：$S\otimes_A S$ 按有限多个因子幂等元分解后，各部分为 $B_i\otimes_A B_j$；cocycle 恒等式就是各三重交叠上三个限制识别的恒等式。}

\bi{Apply the authorized algebraic descent theorem \cite[Tag 023N, Proposition 35.3.9]{StacksAlg}. It produces an $A$-module $M$ with $S\otimes_A M\simeq P$, whose restrictions and descent datum recover the given ones. By finite-type descent, \cite[Tag 03C4, Lemma 10.83.2(1)]{StacksAlg}, $M$ is finite. In particular $B_i\otimes_A M\simeq P_i$. These module isomorphisms give compatible sheaf isomorphisms on the covering slices by Lemma~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalslicemodules}. To see the global isomorphism explicitly, their compatibility identifies each $m\in M$ with a matching family in $\mathcal F(V_i)$, hence with a section of $\mathcal F(X)$. Restriction and multiplication define $\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}\to\mathcal F$. For every object $W$, use the covering $(W\cap V_i)$ and the two sheaf equalizer diagrams; the morphism is an isomorphism on all its members and intersections, hence on $W$. This proves (4) also for trivial valuation. The use of algebraic faithfully flat descent is confined to the case in which the ordinary tensor intersection was just checked.}
{应用已获准的代数下降定理 \cite[Tag 023N, Proposition 35.3.9]{StacksAlg}，得到 $A$-模 $M$，使 $S\otimes_A M\simeq P$，且其限制与下降数据恢复原数据。由有限型下降 \cite[Tag 03C4, Lemma 10.83.2(1)]{StacksAlg}，$M$ 有限。特别地，$B_i\otimes_A M\simeq P_i$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalslicemodules}，这些模同构在覆盖的 slices 上给出相容的层同构。可以直接说明整体同构：相容性将每个 $m\in M$ 对应到 $\mathcal F(V_i)$ 中的一个匹配族，因而对应到 $\mathcal F(X)$ 的截面。通过限制与相乘，定义 $\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}\to\mathcal F$。对每个对象 $W$，使用覆盖 $(W\cap V_i)$ 及两个层 equalizer 图表；该态射在所有成员和交叠上均为同构，因此在 $W$ 上为同构。由此也在平凡赋值时证明 (4)。这里仅在已经核对普通张量积交叠的情形下使用代数忠实平坦下降。}

\bi{Finally suppose (4). The finite $A$-module $M$ is finitely presented by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}; applying the exact functor of Corollary~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalmodulesheaf} gives a global finite free presentation of its sheaf. In particular the sheaf is of finite type. On any slice $\mathcal R_X/V$, it has the finite model $M_V=A_V\otimes_A M$. Given any finite list of its sections over $V$, the associated map $\mathcal O_V^n\to\widetilde{M_V}_V$ is induced by $A_V^n\to M_V$. Its kernel is the sheaf associated to the algebraic kernel, by Lemma~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalslicemodules}. The algebraic kernel is finite since $A_V$ is Noetherian. Its sheaf is generated by a finite list of global sections on that slice. Thus the kernel is of finite type for every such map and every object $V$, which is exactly coherence in (1).}
{最后假设 (4)。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}，有限 $A$-模 $M$ 具有有限表示；应用 Corollary~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalmodulesheaf} 的正合函子，便得到其层的整体有限自由表示。特别地，该层为有限型。在任意 slice $\mathcal R_X/V$ 上，它具有有限模型 $M_V=A_V\otimes_A M$。给定它在 $V$ 上的任意有限截面列，对应映射 $\mathcal O_V^n\to\widetilde{M_V}_V$ 由 $A_V^n\to M_V$ 诱导。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalslicemodules}，其核是代数核所对应的层。由于 $A_V$ Noetherian，代数核有限，其层在该 slice 上由有限个整体截面生成。因此，对每个这样的映射及每个对象 $V$，核均为有限型，这恰好是 (1) 中的凝聚性。}

\bi{Full faithfulness, exactness and the inverse by global sections are now Corollary~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalmodulesheaf}, together with the essential surjectivity just proved. On each object the section module is finite and therefore has a canonical norm up to uniform equivalence. The norm-strict \v Cech statement follows from Lemma~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalslicemodules}, or directly from Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfiniterationalcech} after the global model has been obtained. None of these conclusions changes the object category of the site.}
{现在，由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalmodulesheaf} 及刚证明的本质满性，得到全忠实、正合以及由整体截面给出的逆函子。每个对象上的截面模有限，因此具有在一致等价意义下确定的 canonical 范数。Norm-strict \v Cech 断言来自 Lemma~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalslicemodules}，也可在取得整体模型后直接应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfiniterationalcech}。这些结论均未改变 site 的对象范畴。}
\end{proof}

\section{\bi{Finite modules and Laurent descent}{有限模与 Laurent 下降}}\label{fnd:finitemodulelaurentdescent}

\bi{Tate exactness concerns the localizations of a module that is already defined over the original algebra. For coherence we must also construct a global module from finite modules given separately on a covering. The additional analytic step is a bounded lifting argument for arbitrary finite Laurent gluing data. We then use maximal residue fields to obtain generators and apply the lifting argument once more to the relations. This reconstructs the method of Kiehl's theorem as developed in \cite[\S9.4.3, Lemmas 5--6, pp.~378--382]{BGR84}, with the finite-module norms and the restricted site specified explicitly.}
{Tate 正合性处理的是已经定义在原代数上的模的局部化。凝聚性还要求从覆盖各部分分别给出的有限模构造整体模。额外的解析步骤，是对任意有限模 Laurent 粘合数据证明有界提升。随后借助极大剩余域取得生成元，并对关系模再次应用提升论证。下面重构 \cite[\S9.4.3, Lemmas 5--6, pp.~378--382]{BGR84} 所展开的 Kiehl 定理的方法，同时明确有限模范数及所用的受限 site。}

\bi{Unless an additional hypothesis is stated, $K$ is a complete real-valued non-Archimedean field, its valuation may be trivial, and $A$ is a strict $K$-affinoid algebra with a norm-admissible Banach norm. Relation ideals are actual ideals; their closedness and the canonical norms of finite modules are supplied by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}. The zero algebra and its zero module are allowed separately. Radii belong to $|K^\times|$ and can be scaled to one. Ordinary tensor products in this section are algebraic tensor products. A completed-tensor assertion is made only after its finite-module comparison has been established.}
{除非另行声明，$K$ 是完备的实值非阿基米德域，允许赋值平凡；$A$ 为配备 norm-admissible Banach 范数的 strict $K$-affinoid 代数。关系理想均指实际理想；其闭性及有限模的 canonical 范数由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules} 给出。零代数及其零模作为独立情形允许在内。半径属于 $|K^\times|$，可缩放为一。本节的普通张量积均为代数张量积；只有在建立相应有限模比较之后，才作出完备张量积的断言。}

\bi{Write $X=\mathcal M(A)$, and use exactly the site $\mathcal R_X$ of Definition~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalpretopology}: its objects are rational subsets presented over the original $A$, and its coverings are finite set-theoretic coverings by such objects. In particular, the algebra of an object $V$ is denoted by $A_V$.}
{记 $X=\mathcal M(A)$，并严格采用 Definition~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalpretopology} 中的 site $\mathcal R_X$：其对象是在原始 $A$ 上表示的 rational 子集，其覆盖是这些对象组成的有限集合覆盖。特别地，将对象 $V$ 的代数记为 $A_V$。}

\begin{definition}[\bi{A finite Laurent tree in the fixed site}{固定 site 内的有限 Laurent 树}]\label{fnd:fixedrationaltree}
\bi{A finite Laurent tree in this site means a finite rooted binary tree with root $X$, such that each node $V$ is an object of $\mathcal R_X$ and each nonterminal node is covered by an elementary Laurent pair for some $h\in A_V$ and radius in $|K^\times|$. Its two child algebras and their intersection algebra must be the corresponding actual elementary quotients, up to the canonical bounded isomorphisms. Empty nodes may be terminal. This definition imposes the presentation requirement; it does not assert its existence.}
{本 site 内的有限 Laurent 树，是指以 $X$ 为根的有限二叉树，其中每个节点 $V$ 均属于 $\mathcal R_X$，且每个非终端节点由某个 $h\in A_V$ 及半径 $r\in|K^\times|$ 定义的 elementary Laurent 对覆盖。两个子节点代数及其交叠代数，须在典范有界同构意义下等于相应的实际 elementary 商。空节点可以作为终端节点。这个定义提出了表示要求，并未断言这种表示存在。}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[\bi{A finite module model on a rational slice}{Rational slice 上的有限模模型}]\label{fnd:fixedrationalfinitemodel}
\bi{For an object $V\in\mathcal R_X$, denote the slice site by $\mathcal R_X/V$ and its restricted ring sheaf by $\mathcal O_V$. If $P$ is a finite $A_V$-module, put}
{对对象 $V\in\mathcal R_X$，将 slice site 记为 $\mathcal R_X/V$，将其限制环层记为 $\mathcal O_V$。若 $P$ 为有限 $A_V$-模，记}
\[
 \widetilde P_V(W)=A_W\otimes_{A_V}P\qquad(W\subseteq V, W\in\mathcal R_X).
\]
\bi{A finite module model for a sheaf $\mathcal F$ on $V$ means an isomorphism $\widetilde P_V\simeq\mathcal F|_{\mathcal R_X/V}$ for such a $P$. It includes all objects of the slice site, not just the value at $V$.}
{$\mathcal F$ 在 $V$ 上的有限模模型，是指对某个这样的 $P$，存在同构 $\widetilde P_V\simeq\mathcal F|_{\mathcal R_X/V}$。它涉及整个 slice site 的所有对象，而不只是在 $V$ 上的取值。}
\end{definition}
