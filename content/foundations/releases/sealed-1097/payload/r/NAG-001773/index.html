A triangular presentation on a finite generic open

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 341

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A triangular presentation on a finite generic open}{有限态射泛开集上的三角表示}]
\label{fnd:finitegenerictriangularpresentation}
\bi{Let $D\subset A$ be a finite extension of Noetherian integral domains. There is a nonzero $d\in D$ such that}
{设 $D\subset A$ 为 Noetherian 整环的有限扩张。则存在非零 $d\in D$，使}
\[
 A[1/d]\simeq
 D[1/d][U_1,\ldots,U_m]/(P_1,\ldots,P_m),
\]
\bi{where $P_j$ is monic in $U_j$, has coefficients in $D[1/d][U_1,\ldots,U_{j-1}]$, and the reduced monomials form a free $D[1/d]$-basis.}
{其中 $P_j$ 关于 $U_j$ 首一，系数属于 $D[1/d][U_1,\ldots,U_{j-1}]$，且约化单项式组成自由的 $D[1/d]$-基。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $F=\operatorname{Frac}(D)$ and $E=\operatorname{Frac}(A)$. Since $A$ is finite and integral over $D$, $A\otimes_DF=E$. Choose a tower $F\subset F(u_1)\subset\cdots\subset F(u_1,\ldots,u_m)=E$ of simple finite extensions, with the $u_j$ chosen among elements of $A$ generating $E$. The minimal polynomial of $u_j$ over the preceding field is monic. Express its coefficients in the reduced power basis of that preceding field. Clearing the finitely many denominators in $D$ gives triangular monic polynomials over $D[1/d]$. Their quotient is free with the reduced monomials as basis, by successive division by monic polynomials. Its evaluation map into $A[1/d]$ becomes an isomorphism over $F$, so its kernel is zero because this free module is torsion-free. The cokernel is a finite $D[1/d]$-module whose localization at $F$ vanishes. A further nonzero multiplier in $D$ kills that cokernel. Replacing $d$ by its product with this multiplier proves the assertion.}
{记 $F=\operatorname{Frac}(D)$、$E=\operatorname{Frac}(A)$。因为 $A$ 在 $D$ 上有限且整，故 $A\otimes_DF=E$。从 $A$ 中选取生成 $E$ 的元素，组成有限单扩张塔 $F\subset F(u_1)\subset\cdots\subset F(u_1,\ldots,u_m)=E$。每个 $u_j$ 在前一域上的极小多项式首一；将其系数用前一域的约化幂基表示，并清除 $D$ 中有限个分母，就得到 $D[1/d]$ 上的三角首一多项式。逐次首一除法说明其商以约化单项式为自由基。求值到 $A[1/d]$ 的映射在 $F$ 上为同构，故其核为零，因为该自由模无挠。余核是有限 $D[1/d]$-模，且在 $F$ 上为零，因此可被 $D$ 的另一个非零元素消灭。把该元素乘入 $d$，即得结论。}
\end{proof}
