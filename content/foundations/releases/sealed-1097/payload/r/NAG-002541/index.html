Pulled-back Cartier sections

I Descent of the polarized mixed tower

Open in the English reader · PDF page 2067

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[Pulled-back Cartier sections]\label{f9ds:cartier}
Let $s$ be a section of a line bundle $\cM$ which is a non-zero-divisor
in every local trivialization. Then $q^*s$ is again a Cartier section.
The determinant of multiplication by $q^*s$ gives a section of
$\Nm_q(q^*\cM)$ which, under \eqref{eq:ex68:f9ds:norm-projection}, is $s^{\otimes n}$.
Its effective Cartier divisor is $n\,\operatorname{div}(s)$.
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Choose an affinoid neighbourhood $V=\cM(A)$ on which $\cM$
and $q_*\cO_Y$ are trivial. The frame coefficient $a$ of $s$
is a non-zero-divisor in $A$: its multiplication kernel sheaf
has zero ordinary stalks, so the affinoid coherent equivalence
makes its section kernel zero. The free $A$-algebra module
$B=\Gamma(q^{-1}V,\cO_Y)$ preserves this injection.
The action of $a$ on the rank-$n$ module
$q_*\cO_Y$ is $a I_n$, with determinant $a^n$. These equations
agree on changes of frame, proving the assertion on sections.
The local Cartier equation $a^n$ is precisely that of
$n\,\operatorname{div}(s)$.
\end{proof}
