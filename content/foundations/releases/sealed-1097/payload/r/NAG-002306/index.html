Positive intrinsic weights at arbitrary real flat germs

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 963

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Positive intrinsic weights at arbitrary real flat germs]
\label{v036:flatgermweight}
Let $X=\mathcal M(B)$ be nonempty and pure of analytic dimension $d$
over the original complete field $K$. Let $f_1,\ldots,f_n\in B^\times$
and put $t(x)=(-\log|f_i(x)|)_i$. Fix $w\in t(X)$ and a saturated
rank-$d$ sublattice $N_0\subset\mathbb Z^n$. Suppose that, for some
open neighborhood $W$ of $w$,
\[
 t(X)\cap W\subset w+(N_0)_{\mathbb R},\qquad
 F_w=t^{-1}(w)\subset\operatorname{Int}(X/K).
\]
Let $D_w$ be the actual affinoid algebra of the whole fibre $F_w$.
For every integral map $Q:\mathbb Z^n\to\mathbb Z^d$ whose restriction
to $N_0$ is injective, the monomials $f^{q_j}$ make $D_w$ finite,
with its canonical module norm, over the exact-radius polytorus
algebra at $Qw$. Its Gauss degree $\delta_Q(w)$ is positive, and
\[
                 m_{X,f}(w)=
                 \frac{\delta_Q(w)}{[\mathbb Z^d:Q(N_0)]}
                     \in\mathbb Z_{>0}
\]
is independent of $Q$. This assertion allows arbitrary real $w$ and
the trivial valuation. It uses the actual fibre algebra, including
its nilpotents.

More precisely, after any complete nontrivial isometric extension
$L/K$ realizing the numbers $e^{-w_i}$, choose
$a_i\in L^\times$ of these absolute values. Form the residual moment
cycle of $D_w\widehat\otimes_KL$ with characters $f_i/a_i$.
If $\rho:\mathbb T_{\mathbb Z^n,\widetilde L}
\to\mathbb T_{\mathbb Z^n/N_0,\widetilde L}$ is the quotient, that
cycle has the form $\sum_s c_s[\rho^{-1}(s)]$, and
\[
                       m_{X,f}(w)=
                       \sum_s c_s[\kappa(s):\widetilde L].
\]
This integer is independent of the extension, normalizing elements,
and compatible admissible model.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Transverse integral maps $Q$ exist by the saturated-lattice splitting
in Lemma~\ref{cand:elementarylatticesplitting}.
Choose a closed rectangular box $P$ containing $w$ in its interior
and contained in $W$. Its inverse image $Y=t^{-1}(P)$ is the actual
simultaneous Laurent domain, by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal}. At every point of
$F_w$, the finitely many coordinate inequalities defining $Y$ are
strict. Its relative presentation therefore puts
$F_w\subset\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$. Proposition
\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus} and the hypothesis give
$F_w\subset\operatorname{Int}(Y/K)$.

The linear map $Q$ is injective on $(N_0)_{\mathbb R}$.
Since $t(Y)\subset w+(N_0)_{\mathbb R}$, the entire level of
$Q\circ t|_Y$ at $Qw$ is $F_w$. Theorem
\ref{v035:analytic:levelisfinite}, applied to $Y$ and its units
$f^{q_j}$, makes its actual level algebra finite with the canonical
module norm over the target polytorus. The level is the same
affinoid domain as $F_w\subset X$: the rational universal property
gives bounded inverse identifications of their algebras. Thus the
finite algebra is precisely $D_w$.

Theorem~\ref{v036:analytic:domainpurity} makes the nonempty domain
$F_w$ pure of analytic dimension $d$. Apply Proposition
\ref{v036:analytic:polyannulusdegree} to its finite map to the
exact-radius polytorus, regarded as a polyannulus with degenerate
intervals. Its Gauss degree is positive. This construction works
for every indicated $Q$ over $K$ itself.

Choose a complete extension $L$ as in
Theorem~\ref{v036:gaussmodeldescent}, realizing all $e^{-w_i}$ and
one radius strictly between zero and one. The actual full fibre of
$X_L$ at $w$ is $(F_w)_L$, by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange}. Every point of $X_L$
restricts to a point of $X$ with the same character values, so
$t(X_L)\cap W\subset w+(N_0)_{\mathbb R}$. Every point of $(F_w)_L$
is interior over $L$ by the base-change assertion of
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus}.
Proposition~\ref{v036:gaussbasechange} preserves each finite map,
its canonical module norm, and its Gauss degree.

Normalize by the $a_i$ and apply Theorem
\ref{v036:projection:flatgermcomparison} over $L$. Its hypotheses
have just been verified for the same actual fibre algebra and for
every $Q$. It identifies the residual supports with the indicated
cosets and gives
\[
 \delta_{Q,L}(w)
   =[\mathbb Z^d:Q(N_0)]
                        \sum_s c_s[\kappa(s):\widetilde L].
\]
Scalar extension preserves $\delta_Q(w)$, and its positivity has
already been proved over $K$. This proves integrality, positivity,
and independence of the projection. The left side divided by its
fixed lattice index is defined over the original field and absolute
value; it proves independence of $L$ and the $a_i$. Independence of
the compatible model also follows from
Theorem~\ref{v036:projection:intrinsiccycle}.
\end{proof}
