A finite free locus

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 925

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[A finite free locus]
\label{v035:degreefinitefreelocus}
Let $f:X\to Y$ be a dominant morphism of affine finite-type
$K$-schemes. Suppose that $Y$ is integral and that the generic
fibre is nonempty and zero-dimensional. Put $F=K(Y)$ and
\[
 \delta=\dim_F\Gamma(X_F,\mathcal O_{X_F}).
\]
There is a nonzero $h\in\Gamma(Y,\mathcal O_Y)$ such that
$f^{-1}(Y_h)\to Y_h$ is finite free of rank $\delta$.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Write $Y=\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ and $X=\operatorname{Spec}(B)$.
Lemma~\ref{cand:finiteprimefiltration} makes the generic
algebra $B_F$ finite-dimensional. Each of a finite set of
$A$-algebra generators of $B$ satisfies a monic polynomial
over $F$. Clear the finitely many coefficient denominators.
Each resulting polynomial equality holds in $B_F$, so its
error in the localized $B$ is killed by a further nonzero
element of $A$. After inverting the product of these finitely
many elements, all the monic equalities hold. The bounded
monomials in the generators then give a finite set of module
generators for this localization of $B$.

Choose $c_1,\ldots,c_\delta$ among those monomials whose
images form an $F$-basis of $B_F$. Express the other module
generators in that basis. Clear their finitely many
coefficient denominators and then kill their finitely many
errors by another localization in $A\setminus\{0\}$. The
$c_i$ now generate the localized module. They are independent:
a relation maps to an $F$-linear relation between their images,
and its coefficients vanish because the localized $A$ injects
into $F$. The product of all elements inverted is the required
$h$, and the displayed basis has size $\delta$.
\end{proof}
