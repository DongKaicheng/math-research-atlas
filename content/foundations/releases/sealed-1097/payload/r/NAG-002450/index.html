Local openness for graded-independent moments

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1056

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Local openness for graded-independent moments]
\label{v038:pointed:independentopenness}
Let $V=\mathcal M(A)$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid
space, let $x\in\operatorname{Int}(V/K)$, and let
$f_1,\ldots,f_s\in A^\times$ have algebraically
independent homogeneous initial forms over $k$ in
$F_x$. Put
\[
 t_f(y)=(-\log|f_1(y)|,\ldots,-\log|f_s(y)|),
 \qquad w=t_f(x).
\]
For every ordinary neighbourhood $U$ of $x$ in $V$,
the set $t_f(U)$ contains an ordinary Euclidean
neighbourhood of $w$ in $\mathbf R^s$.
In particular, for every ordinary affinoid neighbourhood
$W$ of $x$ contained in $V$, its actual finite
polyhedral moment image has dimension $s$.
The assertion holds for the trivial valuation,
arbitrary real radii and every analytic dimension of $V$.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
For $s=0$ the image is the one-point space $\mathbf R^0$,
so there is nothing to prove. Assume $s>0$.
Use Lemma~\ref{v038:pointed:gaussnormalization} and
write $h_i=f_i/S_i$ on $V_L$. The point $x_L$ is
interior, every $|h_i(x_L)|=1$, and the reductions
of the $h_i$ are algebraically independent over
$\widetilde L$. Thus the actual residue algebra
\[
 \widetilde L[\widetilde h_1^{\pm1},\ldots,
                  \widetilde h_s^{\pm1}]
       \simeq\widetilde L[T_1^{\pm1},\ldots,T_s^{\pm1}]
\]
is a Laurent polynomial algebra. Its tropicalization
over the trivially valued residue field is all of
$\mathbf R^s$: for any real vector $v$, the weighted
Gauss seminorm on its Laurent ring has moment $v$.

Let $C$ be a nonempty open rational homogeneous
polyhedral cone in $\mathbf R^s$. Choose a vector
$v\in C$ and integer vectors $m_1,\ldots,m_a$
such that the strict inequalities
$\langle m_j,z\rangle>0$ define a nonempty
smaller cone contained in $C$ and contain $v$.
The \hyperref[v037:analytic:normalizedsectors]{normalized sector argument} applied to
$(V_L,x_L)$ and $(h_i)$ says that every ordinary
neighbourhood of $x_L$ contains a point where
$\langle m_j,t_h\rangle>0$ for all $j$.
For any $\delta>0$ apply it to the neighbourhood
\[
       p^{-1}(U)\cap t_h^{-1}(B_\delta(0))
\]
of $x_L$, where $p:V_L\to V$ is projection.
The projected point belongs to $U$ and its moment
displacement $t_f(y)-w=t_h(y_L)$ belongs to
$C\cap B_\delta(0)$.

Choose an ordinary affinoid neighbourhood
$W\subseteq U$ of $x$ by the affinoid-neighbourhood
basis established in the proof of
Theorem~\ref{v037:forms:germsheaf}. Apply the previous
paragraph with $U$ replaced by the ordinary interior
of $W$. Theorem~\ref{v037:globaltrop:finiteimage}
gives a finite integral affine polyhedral complex
whose support is exactly $t_f(W)$. Because $w\in t_f(W)$,
the cells containing $w$ determine a finite union
$C_W$ of closed rational polyhedral tangent cones,
and for some $\epsilon>0$ one has
\[
 t_f(W)\cap B_\epsilon(w)
     =(w+C_W)\cap B_\epsilon(w).
\]
If $C_W\ne\mathbf R^s$, its closed conical support
has a complementary nonempty open rational homogeneous
cone. The preceding sector lifting then produces a
moment displacement in this cone arbitrarily close
to zero, contradicting the displayed equality.
Hence $C_W=\mathbf R^s$ and the displayed equality
puts $B_\epsilon(w)$ inside $t_f(W)\subseteq t_f(U)$.
The dimension assertion follows because the moment
image is a subset of $\mathbf R^s$ containing a ball.
\end{proof}
