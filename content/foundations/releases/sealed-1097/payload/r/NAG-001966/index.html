Laurent refinement in valuation spaces

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 563

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Laurent refinement in valuation spaces}
{赋值空间中的 Laurent 加细}]
\label{fnd:gradedrzlaurentrefinement}
\bi{Every finite covering of a subset $W\subset\mathscr P_k(F)$
by sets $W\{a_1,\ldots,a_n\}$ admits a refinement by a Laurent
covering $W\{b_1^{\epsilon_1},\ldots,b_m^{\epsilon_m}\}$,
$\epsilon_i\in\{1,-1\}$, with homogeneous nonzero $b_i$.}
{以 $W\{a_1,\ldots,a_n\}$ 型集合组成的
$W\subset\mathscr P_k(F)$ 的每个有限覆盖，都具有由
$W\{b_1^{\epsilon_1},\ldots,b_m^{\epsilon_m}\}$ 组成的
Laurent 加细，其中 $\epsilon_i\in\{1,-1\}$，各 $b_i$
为非零齐次元素。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Write the given cover as $W_i=W\{a_{i1},\ldots,a_{in}\}$,
removing zero entries and appending entries $1$ so that
$a_{in}=1$ for every $i$. Let $J$ consist of tuples
$j=(j_1,\ldots,j_s)$ with at least one $j_i=n$, and set
$c_j=\prod_i a_{ij_i}$. The rational sets}
{将覆盖写成 $W_i=W\{a_{i1},\ldots,a_{in}\}$；删去零项并
补入 $1$，使每个 $i$ 都有 $a_{in}=1$。
令 $J$ 由至少一个 $j_i=n$ 的元组
$j=(j_1,\ldots,j_s)$ 组成，设 $c_j=\prod_i a_{ij_i}$。
则 rational 集合}
\[
 V_j=W\{c_{j'}/c_j:j'\in J\}
\]
\bi{cover $W$, since finitely many nonzero homogeneous elements
in a valuation field have a largest one for divisibility.
Fix $i$ with $j_i=n$ and a valuation in $V_j$.
It belongs to some $W_\ell$. If $\ell=i$ there is nothing
to prove. Otherwise replacing $j_\ell$ by $n$ shows that
$a_{\ell j_\ell}^{-1}$ belongs to the valuation ring.
It also contains $a_{\ell j_\ell}$, so this is a unit.
Replacing $j_i$ by $h$ and $j_\ell$ by $n$ now gives
$a_{ih}/a_{\ell j_\ell}$ in the ring for every $h$.
Thus $V_j\subset W_i$.}
{覆盖 $W$，因为赋值域中有限个非零齐次元素按整除关系有
最大者。固定满足 $j_i=n$ 的 $i$，并取 $V_j$ 中一个赋值。
它属于某个 $W_\ell$。若 $\ell=i$ 则无需证明；否则将
$j_\ell$ 改为 $n$，便知该赋值环包含
$a_{\ell j_\ell}^{-1}$。它也包含 $a_{\ell j_\ell}$，
故后者为单位。再同时将 $j_i$ 改为 $h$、$j_\ell$ 改为 $n$，
得到环中元素 $a_{ih}/a_{\ell j_\ell}$。因此对每个 $h$
都有 $a_{ih}$ 属于环，即 $V_j\subset W_i$。}

\bi{A rational covering defined by finitely many $c_j$ has
a Laurent refinement using all ratios $c_j/c_{j'}$.
On a chosen Laurent cell these ratio conditions orient the
complete finite graph. Contract the strongly connected
components and choose a terminal component. Products along
directed paths show that the ratio of every $c_j$ to any
chosen member of this terminal component belongs to all
valuation rings in the cell; ratios inside a component are
units. The cell is therefore contained in its rational chart.
This proves the desired refinement.}
{由有限个 $c_j$ 定义的 rational 覆盖，可用所有比值
$c_j/c_{j'}$ 的 Laurent 覆盖加细。
在一个固定 Laurent 片中，这些比值条件给有限完全图定向。
缩并强连通分支并选取末端分支。沿有向路径相乘，说明每个
$c_j$ 与末端分支中一个固定元素的比值，都属于该片中的全部
赋值环；同一个强连通分支内部的比值则为单位。
因此该 Laurent 片包含于相应 rational 片，证明所需加细。}
\end{proof}
