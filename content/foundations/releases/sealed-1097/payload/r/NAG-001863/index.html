Lifting a fibrewise non-zero-divisor

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 440

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Lifting a fibrewise non-zero-divisor}{提升纤维上的非零因子}]
\label{fnd:flatnonzerodivisorlifting}
\bi{Let $(R,\mathfrak m)\to(S,\mathfrak n)$ be a flat local homomorphism of Noetherian local rings. If multiplication by $a\in S$ is injective on $S/\mathfrak mS$, then it is injective on $S$.}
{设 $(R,\mathfrak m)\to(S,\mathfrak n)$ 是 Noetherian 局部环之间的平坦局部同态。若乘以 $a\in S$ 在 $S/\mathfrak mS$ 上为单射，则在 $S$ 上也为单射。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Flatness identifies the successive quotients with}
{平坦性给出逐级商的识别}
\[
 \mathfrak m^jS/\mathfrak m^{j+1}S
  \simeq (\mathfrak m^j/\mathfrak m^{j+1})
      \otimes_{R/\mathfrak m}(S/\mathfrak mS).
\]
\bi{The first factor is a vector space, so multiplication by $a$ is injective on every displayed quotient. If $ab=0$, induction gives $b\in\mathfrak m^jS$ for every $j\geq0$. Since the map is local, these submodules lie in $\mathfrak n^jS$. Corollary~\ref{fnd:maximaladicseparation}, applied to the local Noetherian ring $S$, gives $b=0$.}
{第一个因子是向量空间，因此乘以 $a$ 在每个所列商上均为单射。若 $ab=0$，归纳即得 $b\in\mathfrak m^jS$ 对所有 $j\geq0$ 成立。由于同态局部，这些子模包含于 $\mathfrak n^jS$。对 Noetherian 局部环 $S$ 应用 Corollary~\ref{fnd:maximaladicseparation}，便得 $b=0$。}
\end{proof}
