\begin{lemma}[\bi{Generic support and rational approximation over $K$}
{$K$ 上的泛支撑与有理逼近}]
\label{cand:originalfieldrationalgerm}
\bi{Every $x\in\Sigma$ has generic algebraic support. If $g$
is an analytic germ at $x$ with $g(x)\ne0$, some
$f\in K(X)^\times$ satisfies $|f|=|g|$ on an ordinary
neighborhood of $x$. If $|g(x)|=1$, the residues of $f(x)$ and
$g(x)$ can also be made equal.}
{每个 $x\in\Sigma$ 的代数支撑都是泛点。若 $g$ 为 $x$
处解析函数芽且 $g(x)\ne0$，则存在 $f\in K(X)^\times$，
使 $|f|=|g|$ 在 $x$ 的通常邻域上成立。若 $|g(x)|=1$，
还可使 $f(x)$ 与 $g(x)$ 的剩余类相等。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For a finite extension $E/K$, Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}
makes $E$ complete, and its proof shows that every value in
$v(E^\times)$ has a positive integer multiple in $v(K^\times)$.
Moreover $\widetilde E/\widetilde K$ is algebraic: for
$b\in E$ of norm one, take a polynomial relation over $K$,
divide its coefficients by a coefficient of maximal norm, and
reduce. The resulting polynomial over $\widetilde K$ is nonzero
and annihilates the residue of $b$. Thus the residual
transcendence degree and relative rational value-group rank of
a finite extension are both zero.}
{对有限扩张 $E/K$，Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}
说明 $E$ 完备，其证明还表明 $v(E^\times)$ 中每个元素
都有正整数倍属于 $v(K^\times)$。此外
$\widetilde E/\widetilde K$ 代数：对范数为一的 $b\in E$，
取一个 $K$ 上多项式关系，将系数同除以一个最大范数的
系数后约化。所得 $\widetilde K$ 上的多项式非零，并消去
$b$ 的剩余类。因此有限扩张的剩余超越次数与相对值群
有理秩均为零。}

\bi{At a component vertex, Proposition~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield}
gives residual transcendence degree one. At an annular Gauss
point, Proposition~\ref{new:gauss_residue_formula} gives the
sum of these two invariants equal to one. If a skeletal point
had closed algebraic support, its residue field would be a finite
extension of $K$ by \cite[Tag 00P0]{StacksAlg}, and
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineanalytificationpoints} would identify
its completed residue field with that same finite field. This
contradicts the preceding invariants. An integral algebraic curve
has only generic and closed points, so its support is generic.}
{在分量顶点处，Proposition~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield}
给出剩余超越次数一。在圆环 Gauss 点处，
Proposition~\ref{new:gauss_residue_formula} 说明上述两个
不变量之和为一。若骨架点的代数支撑是闭点，则由
\cite[Tag 00P0]{StacksAlg}，其剩余域是 $K$ 的有限扩张；
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineanalytificationpoints} 将其完备
剩余域识别为该有限域，与上述不变量矛盾。整代数曲线
只有泛点与闭点，故其支撑必为泛点。}

\bi{Consequently $\mathcal H(x)$ is the completion of $K(X)$
for the norm of $x$. Choose $f\in K(X)$ with
$|f(x)-g(x)|<|g(x)|$. The rational function and its inverse
are regular on a Zariski open containing the generic support;
their analytifications are defined near $x$. Continuity preserves
the strict inequality on an ordinary neighborhood, and the
ultrametric inequality gives equality of the norms there. For
norm-one values the error has norm less than one, which also
identifies the residues.}
{因此 $\mathcal H(x)$ 是 $K(X)$ 对 $x$ 的范数的完备化。
选择 $f\in K(X)$ 使 $|f(x)-g(x)|<|g(x)|$。此有理函数
及其逆在包含泛支撑的某个 Zariski 开集上正则，故其
解析化在 $x$ 附近有定义。连续性使严格不等式在通常邻域
上仍成立，而超度量不等式在那里给出范数相等。对于范数
一的取值，误差小于一，故剩余类也相同。}
\end{proof}
