Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[Smooth double integration by parts; F8F7-IBP]
\label{lem:ibp}
Under these hypotheses, for an ordinary smooth real scalar \(u\) on a
neighbourhood of \(K_0\),
\[
             I_{\mathcal F}(u\,\dd\eta)
                 =I_{\mathcal F}(\dd u\wedge\eta).                 \tag{1.2}
\]
Products defined near \(K_0\) are extended by zero away from that
neighbourhood.
\end{lemma}
