Spectrum and spectral radius

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 60

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Spectrum and spectral radius}{Spectrum 与 spectral radius}]\label{fnd:nonempty}
\bi{For a nonzero $K$-affinoid algebra, the spectrum is nonempty. For any submultiplicative Banach norm uniformly equivalent to its defining affinoid norm,}
{对非零 $K$-affinoid algebra，spectrum 非空。对与其 defining affinoid norm uniformly equivalent 的任意 submultiplicative Banach norm，}
\[
 \rho_A(a):=\lim_{m\to\infty}\|a^m\|^{1/m}
       =\max_{x\in\mathcal M(A)}|a(x)|.
\]
\bi{The function $\rho_A$ is a power-multiplicative seminorm. Its kernel is the nilradical. If $A$ is reduced, it is a complete norm equivalent to the given Banach norm.}
{函数 $\rho_A$ 为 power-multiplicative seminorm，其 kernel 是 nilradical。若 $A$ reduced，则它是与给定 Banach norm equivalent 的 complete norm。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Nonemptiness is Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion}, and the displayed limit and maximum formula are Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedspectralradiusformula}. Their value is unchanged under a uniformly equivalent Banach norm, since the fixed comparison constants have $m$-th roots tending to one. The maximum formula implies the ultrametric and submultiplicative inequalities and $\rho_A(a^j)=\rho_A(a)^j$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:spectralnilradicalinternal} identifies the kernel with the nilradical. For reduced $A$, Theorem~\ref{fnd:reducedspectralcomplete} proves completeness and equivalence with the given norm.}
{非空性为 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion}，所列极限与最大值公式为 Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedspectralradiusformula}。固定范数比较常数的 $m$ 次方根趋于一，因此换成一致等价的 Banach 范数不改变极限。最大值公式给出超度量不等式、次乘不等式及 $\rho_A(a^j)=\rho_A(a)^j$。Proposition~\ref{fnd:spectralnilradicalinternal} 将其核识别为幂零根基。对约化的 $A$，Theorem~\ref{fnd:reducedspectralcomplete} 证明其完备且与给定范数等价。}
\end{proof}
