Scalar recurrence over a normed residue field

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 162

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Scalar recurrence over a normed residue field}{赋范剩余域上的标量递推}]\label{fnd:residuefieldlaurentinjective}
\bi{Let $F$ be a field which is a Banach $K$-algebra with a definite submultiplicative norm. Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional $F$-vector space with a Banach $K$-norm uniformly equivalent to the maximum coordinate norm for an $F$-basis. For every $\lambda\in F$ and every $r>0$, the operator $L_\lambda$ on $E_r(V)$ is bounded and injective. No multiplicativity of the norm on $F$, and no finiteness of $F/K$, is assumed.}
{设 $F$ 为一个域，同时为带有正定次乘范数的 Banach $K$-代数。设 $V$ 为有限维 $F$-向量空间，配备 Banach $K$-范数，并且该范数与某个 $F$-基对应的最大坐标范数 uniformly equivalent。则对每个 $\lambda\in F$ 和每个 $r>0$，$E_r(V)$ 上的算子 $L_\lambda$ 有界且单射。不要求 $F$ 的范数具有乘法性，也不要求扩张 $F/K$ 有限。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose a basis and comparison constants $C,D>0$ such that, for $v=\sum_j a_je_j$,}
{选择一个基以及比较常数 $C,D>0$，使对 $v=\sum_j a_je_j$ 有}
\[
 \max_j\|a_j\|_F\leq C\|v\|,
 \qquad
 \|v\|\leq D\max_j\|a_j\|_F .
\]
\bi{These inequalities identify $E_r(V)$, by a bounded isomorphism with bounded inverse, with a finite power of $E_r(F)$. Multiplication by $\lambda$ has bound $DC\|\lambda\|_F$, so $L_\lambda$ is bounded. If $\lambda=0$, it is the identity. Suppose $\lambda\ne0$ and $L_\lambda v=0$. Each coordinate sequence then satisfies}
{这些不等式将 $E_r(V)$ 与 $E_r(F)$ 的一个有限次幂识别为具有有界逆的有界同构。乘以 $\lambda$ 的界为 $DC\|\lambda\|_F$，所以 $L_\lambda$ 有界。若 $\lambda=0$，该算子为恒等算子。设 $\lambda\ne0$ 且 $L_\lambda v=0$。每个坐标序列于是满足}
\[
 a_n=\lambda a_{n+1},
 \qquad a_n=\lambda^{-n}a_0 \quad(n\in\mathbb Z).
\]
\bi{If $a_0\ne0$, then $a_na_{-n}=a_0^2\ne0$, whereas submultiplicativity gives}
{若 $a_0\ne0$，则 $a_na_{-n}=a_0^2\ne0$，而由次乘性得到}
\[
 0<\|a_0^2\|_F
 \leq
   \bigl(\|a_n\|_Fr^n\bigr)
   \bigl(\|a_{-n}\|_Fr^{-n}\bigr)
 \longrightarrow0
 \qquad(n\longrightarrow+\infty).
\]
\bi{This is impossible. Thus every coordinate has $a_0=0$, and the recurrence makes every coefficient zero. The argument also covers $V=0$.}
{这不可能。因此每个坐标均有 $a_0=0$，再由递推关系得到所有系数均为零。该论证也包括 $V=0$。}
\end{proof}

\section{\bi{The ground field and convergence}{Ground field 与 convergence}}\label{fnd:valuedfields}
\begin{definition}\label{fnd:fieldconventions}
\bi{Throughout the foundations, $K$ is a field complete for a real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value. We permit the trivial absolute value unless a statement explicitly excludes it. Set}
{基础篇中，$K$ 是关于 real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value 完备的 field。除非明确排除，否则允许 trivial absolute value。记}
\[
\begin{gathered}
v(a)=-\log|a|,\qquad \Gamma=v(K^\times),\\
K^\circ=\{a\in K:|a|\leq1\},\qquad K^{\circ\circ}=\{a\in K:|a|<1\},\\
\widetilde K=K^\circ/K^{\circ\circ}.
\end{gathered}
\]
\bi{A complete extension $L/K$ always carries an absolute value restricting to the specified one on $K$. We do not rescale it when changing the ground field.}
{Complete extension $L/K$ 始终配备限制为指定 $K$ 上 absolute value 的 absolute value。改变 ground field 时不重新缩放它。}
Standing context 2 · exact TeX
\begin{definition}\label{fnd:banachalgebra}
\bi{A Banach $K$-algebra is a commutative unital $K$-algebra $A$ with a complete non-Archimedean norm satisfying $\|ca\|=|c|\|a\|$, $\|1\|=1$, and $\|ab\|\leq\|a\|\|b\|$. A homomorphism is bounded if $\|\phi(a)\|\leq C\|a\|$ for some constant $C$. Equivalent norms define the same Banach topology. A surjective bounded map is admissible if its target norm is uniformly equivalent to the quotient norm; equivalently, it admits lifts with a uniform norm bound. Equality of topologies alone is insufficient at trivial valuation; see Definition~\ref{fnd:normstrictconvention} and Example~\ref{fnd:normtopologycounterexample}.}
{Banach $K$-algebra 是 commutative unital $K$-algebra $A$，配备一个 complete norm。此 norm 为 non-Archimedean，且满足 $\|ca\|=|c|\|a\|$、$\|1\|=1$ 和 $\|ab\|\leq\|a\|\|b\|$。若存在常数 $C$ 使 $\|\phi(a)\|\leq C\|a\|$，则 homomorphism 称为 bounded。Equivalent norms 定义相同的 Banach topology。Surjective bounded map 若使 target norm 与 quotient norm uniformly equivalent，则称为 admissible；等价地，它具有 uniform norm bound 的 lifts。赋值平凡时，仅有 topologies 相同还不够；见 Definition~\ref{fnd:normstrictconvention} 及 Example~\ref{fnd:normtopologycounterexample}。}
\end{definition}

\bi{Keep the fixed complete non-Archimedean field $K$, with trivial valuation permitted. For a Banach $K$-space $V$ and $r>0$, put}
{保持固定的完备非阿基米德域 $K$，允许赋值平凡。对 Banach $K$-space $V$ 和 $r>0$，记}
\[
 E_r(V)=
 \left\{(v_n)_{n\in\mathbb Z}:
       \|v_n\|r^n\longrightarrow0\ \text{as }|n|\longrightarrow\infty\right\},
 \qquad
 \|v\|_r=\sup_{n\in\mathbb Z}\|v_n\|r^n .
\]
\bi{We also write its elements as Laurent series $\sum_n v_nT^n$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness} gives completeness. For a bounded operator $f$ on $V$, the operator $L_f=1-fT^{-1}$ is defined by}
{也将其元素写为 Laurent series $\sum_n v_nT^n$。Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness} 给出完备性。对 $V$ 上的有界算子 $f$，定义算子 $L_f=1-fT^{-1}$ 为}
\[
 (L_fv)_n=v_n-fv_{n+1}.
\]

\bi{Throughout, $K$ is complete for its fixed real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value, which may be trivial. A Banach $K$-space has a definite, complete, non-Archimedean, $K$-homogeneous norm. Banach algebras are commutative and unital, with a submultiplicative norm and $\|1\|=1$; Banach module actions satisfy $\|am\|\leq\|a\|\|m\|$. All finite powers have the maximum norm; the maximum over an empty family is zero. Norm-strictness has the quantitative meaning of Definition~\ref{fnd:normstrictconvention}.}
{本节中，$K$ 关于固定的 real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value 完备，允许该 absolute value 平凡。Banach $K$-space 的 norm 为 definite、complete、non-Archimedean 且 $K$-homogeneous。Banach algebras 均 commutative、unital，其 norm submultiplicative 且 $\|1\|=1$；Banach module actions 满足 $\|am\|\leq\|a\|\|m\|$。所有 finite powers 使用 maximum norm，并约定空 family 的 maximum 为零。Norm-strictness 采用 Definition~\ref{fnd:normstrictconvention} 的定量含义。}

\bi{For a countable set $I$ and positive weights $w=(w_i)_{i\in I}$, write $\mathcal C_w(I,E)=c_0(I,E;w)$ as in Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness}; thus}
{对可数集合 $I$ 和正权重 $w=(w_i)_{i\in I}$，按照 Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness} 的定义，记 $\mathcal C_w(I,E)=c_0(I,E;w)$，即}
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \mathcal C_w(I,E)=
 \left\{(e_i)_{i\in I}:
   \text{for every }\varepsilon>0,
   \#\{i:w_i\|e_i\|\geq\varepsilon\}<\infty\right\},\\
 \|(e_i)\|_w=\sup_{i\in I}w_i\|e_i\|.
 \end{gathered}
\]
\bi{We put $\sup\varnothing=0$. The cited lemma, with coefficient algebra $K$ if necessary, proves completeness and density of finite-support families. A bounded linear map $u:E\to F$ with bound $U$ induces a coefficient map with the same bound, since $w_i\|u(e_i)\|\leq U w_i\|e_i\|$. There is also a canonical isometry}
{约定 $\sup\varnothing=0$。上述引理给出完备性以及有限支撑族的稠密性；对 Banach $K$-space，只需取系数代数为 $K$。若有界线性映射 $u:E\to F$ 的界为 $U$，则逐系数映射保持同一个界，因为 $w_i\|u(e_i)\|\leq U w_i\|e_i\|$。此外，对有限 $m$，有 canonical isometry}
\[
 \mathcal C_w(I,E^m)\simeq\mathcal C_w(I,E)^m
 \qquad(m<\infty).
\]
\bi{It merely rearranges coefficients: a supremum commutes with a finite maximum, and a finite union of finite sets is finite, so both the norms and the finite-tail conditions agree.}
{该映射只重新排列系数：supremum 与有限 maximum 可交换，且有限个有限集的并仍有限，所以两种范数相等，有限尾条件也等价。}
