Strict fibres near an inner point

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 437

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Strict fibres near an inner point}{内点附近的 strict 纤维}]
\label{fnd:innerstrictfibrerefinement}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good $K$-analytic spaces, let $x=f(y)$, and assume $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$ and $|\mathcal H(x)^\times|\ne\{1\}$. There are affinoid neighbourhoods $X_0$ of $x$ and $V$ of $y$, with $V\subset f^{-1}(X_0)$, such that $V_x$ is strictly $\mathcal H(x)$-affinoid. The source neighbourhood may be chosen inside any prescribed ordinary neighbourhood of $y$.}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 是 good $K$-解析空间之间的态射，$x=f(y)$，并假设 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$ 且 $|\mathcal H(x)^\times|\ne\{1\}$。则存在 $x$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $X_0$ 及 $y$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $V\subset f^{-1}(X_0)$，使 $V_x$ 是 strict $\mathcal H(x)$-affinoid。源邻域还可包含于预先指定的 $y$ 的普通邻域。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose affinoid neighbourhoods $Y_0\to X_0$ within the prescribed neighbourhoods. Proposition~\ref{fnd:goodinteriorlocaltesting} makes $y$ an inner point of this affinoid morphism. There is therefore a norm-admissible relative presentation $B=A\{r^{-1}T\}/I$ with $|T_i(y)|<r_i$. Put $F=\mathcal H(x)$. Since $F$ is nontrivially valued, $\sqrt{|F^\times|}$ is dense in $\mathbb R_{>0}$: the rational powers of any fixed element of absolute value different from one already form a dense subgroup. Choose}
{在指定邻域内选择 affinoid 邻域 $Y_0\to X_0$。Proposition~\ref{fnd:goodinteriorlocaltesting} 说明 $y$ 是该 affinoid 态射的内点，因此存在 norm-admissible 相对表示 $B=A\{r^{-1}T\}/I$，满足 $|T_i(y)|<r_i$。令 $F=\mathcal H(x)$。因为 $F$ 的赋值非平凡，$\sqrt{|F^\times|}$ 在 $\mathbb R_{>0}$ 中稠密：任取一个绝对值不为一的元素，其绝对值的有理次幂已经构成稠密子群。选择}
\[
 |T_i(y)|<s_i<r_i,\qquad s_i\in\sqrt{|F^\times|}.
\]
\bi{The Weierstrass domain $V=\{|T_i|\leq s_i\text{ for all }i\}$ contains an ordinary neighbourhood of $y$. Relative presentations and base change identify its fibre with a quotient of $F\{s^{-1}T\}$. It remains to exhibit this last algebra as strict.}
{Weierstrass 域 $V=\{|T_i|\leq s_i\text{ 对所有 }i\}$ 包含 $y$ 的普通邻域。由相对表示及基变换，其纤维代数是 $F\{s^{-1}T\}$ 的商。只须明确证明后者为 strict。}

\bi{Write $s_i^{e_i}=|a_i|$ with $e_i>0$ and $a_i\in F^\times$, and choose $c_i\in F^\times$ with $|c_i|\geq s_i$. Then}
{写成 $s_i^{e_i}=|a_i|$，其中 $e_i>0$、$a_i\in F^\times$，再选取 $c_i\in F^\times$，满足 $|c_i|\geq s_i$。于是有}
\[
 F\{s^{-1}T\}\simeq
 F\langle U_1,W_1,\ldots,U_m,W_m\rangle/
       (c_i^{e_i}U_i^{e_i}-a_iW_i)_{i=1}^m,
 \quad T_i\longleftrightarrow c_iU_i.
\]
\bi{Indeed the map to the left sends $U_i$ to $T_i/c_i$ and $W_i$ to $T_i^{e_i}/a_i$, both power bounded by one. Conversely the relation gives a uniform bound for $(c_iU_i)^n/s_i^n$: divide $n$ by $e_i$ and use the finitely many possible remainders. The generalized Tate universal property therefore defines the map to the right. The maps are inverse on the polynomial generators and then on the completed algebras by density. This also proves norm admissibility. Quotients of a strict affinoid are strict, proving the assertion.}
{从右到左的映射把 $U_i$ 送到 $T_i/c_i$，把 $W_i$ 送到 $T_i^{e_i}/a_i$；这两个元素的各次幂的范数均至多为一。反过来，由所列关系，将 $n$ 除以 $e_i$ 并使用有限个可能的余数，可得到 $(c_iU_i)^n/s_i^n$ 的统一界。因此 generalized Tate 泛性质定义从左到右的映射。两者在多项式生成元上互逆，再由稠密性知在完备代数上互逆，同时得到 norm-admissibility。Strict affinoid 的商仍为 strict，故结论成立。}
\end{proof}
