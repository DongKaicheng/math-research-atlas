The reduction of an active Weierstrass inequality

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 565

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The reduction of an active Weierstrass inequality}
{有效 Weierstrass 不等式的约化}]
\label{fnd:gradedweierstrassgerm}
\bi{For a good germ $(X,x)$, let $f_1,\ldots,f_m$ be analytic
near $x$, with $r_i=|f_i(x)|>0$, and write $\lambda_i\in F_x$
for their initial values. Then}
{对 good 芽 $(X,x)$，设 $f_1,\ldots,f_m$ 在 $x$ 附近解析，
$r_i=|f_i(x)|>0$，并以 $\lambda_i\in F_x$ 记其首项值。则}
\[
 \mathscr R\bigl(X\{r^{-1}f\},x\bigr)
          =\mathscr R(X,x)\{\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_m\}.
\]
\bi{Every subdomain germ of a good germ has a finite cover
by germs of this form. Every nonzero homogeneous element
of $F_x$ is the initial value of an invertible analytic
function on some neighbourhood of $x$.}
{Good 芽的每个子域芽都可由有限个这种芽覆盖。
$F_x$ 的每个非零齐次元素，都是 $x$ 的某个邻域上一可逆解析
函数的首项值。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Take an affinoid representative $A$ on which the functions
are defined and put $D=A\{r^{-1}f\}$. Choose an admissible absolute
weighted Tate presentation of $A$. Combine it with the actual
quotient $A\{r^{-1}T\}\twoheadrightarrow D$ by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation} to obtain an absolute
presentation of $D$. In the free-polyradius comparisons used for
this presentation, Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedquotientbasechange}
identifies the extended quotients naturally for the given
bounded maps. Apply
\hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{the fixed absolute presentation result}
and evaluate at $x$ in the original field $F_x$.
The old variables give evaluated classes from $R_{A,x}$ in
their assigned degrees. The new variable for $f_i$ has assigned
radius $r_i=|f_i(x)|$ and evaluates in that degree to $\lambda_i$.
The resulting evaluated polynomial image is contained in
$R_{A,x}[\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_m]\subset R_{D,x}$.
The finite module generators obtained by evaluation therefore
make $R_{D,x}$ finite over $R_{A,x}[\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_m]$.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedvaluationintegrality} gives the formula.}
{取一个定义全部函数的 affinoid 代表 $A$，置 $D=A\{r^{-1}f\}$。
选取 $A$ 的一个 admissible 绝对加权 Tate 表示，用
Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation} 将其与实际商
$A\{r^{-1}T\}\twoheadrightarrow D$ 合并，得到 $D$ 的绝对表示。
在此表示所用的自由多半径比较中，
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedquotientbasechange} 对给定有界映射
自然地识别扩张后的商。应用
\hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{固定绝对表示的结果}，
在原来的域 $F_x$ 中于 $x$ 求值。旧变量在指定次数的求值类来自
$R_{A,x}$。对应于 $f_i$ 的新变量的指定半径是 $r_i=|f_i(x)|$，
在该次数求值为 $\lambda_i$。求值后的多项式像包含于
$R_{A,x}[\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_m]\subset R_{D,x}$。
因此求值所得的有限模生成元使 $R_{D,x}$ 在
$R_{A,x}[\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_m]$ 上有限。
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedvaluationintegrality} 给出公式。}

\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert} gives a finite rational
cover of any affinoid subdomain, and a subdomain germ is
covered by finitely many affinoid subdomain germs.
Discard rational members not containing $x$ after shrinking
the ambient neighbourhood. Every remaining denominator is
nonzero at $x$, so becomes invertible after another shrinking.
The rational inequalities then become Weierstrass inequalities.
Those strict at $x$ can be omitted on a neighbourhood, leaving
precisely the displayed active inequalities.
Finally $\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$ is dense in
$\mathcal H(x)$. Approximate a lift of a nonzero homogeneous
element with relative error less than one. It has the same
initial value as a fraction $a/b$, with $b(x)\ne0$ and
$a(x)\ne0$; on a smaller neighbourhood this fraction is
analytic and invertible.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert} 给出每个 affinoid 子域的有限
rational 覆盖，而一个子域芽由有限个 affinoid 子域芽覆盖。
缩小环境邻域后可丢弃不包含 $x$ 的 rational 成员。
剩余分母在 $x$ 处非零，进一步缩小后都可逆；因此 rational
不等式变为 Weierstrass 不等式。在 $x$ 处严格的不等式可在
邻域上省略，剩下的正是所列有效不等式。
最后，$\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$ 在
$\mathcal H(x)$ 中稠密。以相对误差小于一逼近一个非零齐次
元素的提升，便得到具有同一首项的分式 $a/b$，其中
$b(x)\ne0$ 且 $a(x)\ne0$；在较小邻域上，此分式解析且可逆。}
\end{proof}
