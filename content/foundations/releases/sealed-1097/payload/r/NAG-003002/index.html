\begin{proposition}[Internal continuity and differentials; F8MG-T]
\label{prop:topology}
The pairings in Proposition~\ref{prop:orderzero} are continuous for
\(\tau_{\rm cut}=\tau_{\rm ord}\). On these ordinary test spaces,
\(d'\), \(d''\), multiplication by a fixed ordinary smooth function,
and extension by zero from an ordinary open subspace are continuous.
So is cutoff restriction \(\eta\mapsto(\rho\eta)|_W\) when
\(\rho\) has compact support in an ordinary open \(W\).

Put \(\mathcal D^{p,q}(U)=(\E^{n-p,n-q}(U),\tau_{\rm cut})'\),
the continuous real dual. For \(T\in\mathcal D^{p,q}\), define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:dual}
 (d'T)(\eta)=(-1)^{p+q+1}T(d'\eta),\qquad
 (d''T)(\eta)=(-1)^{p+q+1}T(d''\eta).
\end{equation}
These are well-defined continuous functionals, satisfy square-zero
and anticommutation, and for a degree \((0,0)\) pairing satisfy
\((\dd T)(\eta)=T(\dd\eta)\). In particular
\(\dd T_h=T_{\dd h}\) for ordinary smooth \(h\) on \(U\).
\end{proposition}
