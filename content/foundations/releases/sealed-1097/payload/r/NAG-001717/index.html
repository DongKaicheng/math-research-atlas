A rational-point completion calculation

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 291

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A rational-point completion calculation}{有理点处的完备化计算}]
\label{fnd:tatepolynomiallocalcompletion}
\bi{Let $P=K[T_1,\ldots,T_n]$, $C=K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle$, and $a\in K^n$ with $|a_i|\le1$. For $\mathfrak p=(T_1-a_1,\ldots,T_n-a_n)\subset P$ and $\mathfrak n=\mathfrak p C$, the local map $P_{\mathfrak p}\to C_{\mathfrak n}$ is faithfully flat. Both maximal-ideal completions are canonically $K[[U_1,\ldots,U_n]]$, where $U_i=T_i-a_i$.}
{设 $P=K[T_1,\ldots,T_n]$、$C=K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle$，并取满足 $|a_i|\le1$ 的 $a\in K^n$。令 $\mathfrak p=(T_1-a_1,\ldots,T_n-a_n)\subset P$，$\mathfrak n=\mathfrak p C$，则局部映射 $P_{\mathfrak p}\to C_{\mathfrak n}$ 忠实平坦。两边的极大理想完备化均典范等于 $K[[U_1,\ldots,U_n]]$，其中 $U_i=T_i-a_i$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Translation by $a$ is a polynomial automorphism and a bounded Tate-algebra automorphism with bounded inverse: substitution by $T_i+a_i$ and by $T_i-a_i$ is contractive. We may therefore assume $a=0$. For every integer $q\ge1$, truncation in total degree gives}
{平移 $a$ 既是多项式自同构，也是有界且逆有界的 Tate 代数自同构：以 $T_i+a_i$ 及 $T_i-a_i$ 代入均为压缩映射。因此可设 $a=0$。对每个整数 $q\ge1$，按总次数截断得到}
\[
 P/(T)^q \simeq C/(T)^q
       \simeq \bigoplus_{|\nu|<q} K T^\nu .
\]
\bi{The kernel in $C$ is the actual ideal $(T)^q$: partition all terms of total degree at least $q$ among the finitely many monomials of degree $q$ which divide them. Dividing each group by its chosen monomial leaves a convergent series. Every element with nonzero constant term is a unit in this truncated ring, by a finite geometric sum. Localization at $(T)$ therefore leaves the displayed quotients unchanged. Taking their inverse limits proves the completion identity.}
{$C$ 中的核为实际理想 $(T)^q$：将总次数至少为 $q$ 的所有项，按能整除它们的有限个次数 $q$ 的单项式分组；每组除去所选单项式后仍为收敛级数。在上述截断环中，具有非零常数项的元素由有限几何和给出逆元。因此在 $(T)$ 处局部化不改变这些商。取逆极限，就证明完备化识别。}

\bi{Both local rings are Noetherian: for $P$ use \cite[Tag 00FO, Lemma~10.31.3]{StacksAlg}, and for $C$ use Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}; localization preserves finite generation of ideals by clearing denominators. By \cite[Tag 00MC, Lemma~10.97.3]{StacksAlg}, each local ring maps faithfully flatly to the common completion $D=K[[T]]$. We spell out the descent step. For a finitely generated ideal $I\subset P_{(T)}$, let $N$ be the kernel of $I\otimes_{P_{(T)}}C_{(T)}\to C_{(T)}$. Tensoring with the flat $C_{(T)}$-algebra $D$ identifies $N\otimes D$ with the kernel of $I\otimes D\to D$, which is zero because $D$ is flat over $P_{(T)}$. Faithfulness gives $N=0$. The ideal criterion, \cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg}, proves flatness. The map is local with nonzero residue field $K$, so \cite[Tag 00HP]{StacksAlg} makes it faithfully flat.}
{两边的局部环均 Noetherian：对 $P$ 使用 \cite[Tag 00FO, Lemma~10.31.3]{StacksAlg}，对 $C$ 使用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}；清分母说明局部化保持理想的有限生成性。由 \cite[Tag 00MC, Lemma~10.97.3]{StacksAlg}，两个局部环到共同完备化 $D=K[[T]]$ 的映射均忠实平坦。以下写出下降步骤。对有限生成理想 $I\subset P_{(T)}$，令 $N$ 为 $I\otimes_{P_{(T)}}C_{(T)}\to C_{(T)}$ 的核。与平坦的 $C_{(T)}$-代数 $D$ 张量，将 $N\otimes D$ 识别为 $I\otimes D\to D$ 的核；因为 $D$ 在 $P_{(T)}$ 上平坦，此核为零。由忠实性，$N=0$。理想判据 \cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg} 证明平坦性。该映射局部且剩余域 $K$ 非零，所以 \cite[Tag 00HP]{StacksAlg} 又给出忠实平坦性。}
\end{proof}
