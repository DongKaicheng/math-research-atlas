Local constancy on an interior flat germ

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 965

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Local constancy on an interior flat germ]
\label{v036:flatgermlocalconstancy}
Under the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{v036:flatgermweight}, there is
an open neighborhood $V$ of $w$ such that
\[
                  t(X)\cap V=(w+(N_0)_{\mathbb R})\cap V.
\]
Every $v$ in this set has its whole fibre in
$\operatorname{Int}(X/K)$, satisfies the same flat-germ hypotheses
with lattice $N_0$, and has
\[
                              m_{X,f}(v)=m_{X,f}(w).
\]
The value at a weight is unchanged by restriction to an affinoid
domain containing its whole moment fibre, whenever the two
weighted germs satisfy these hypotheses.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The boundary $\partial_KX$ is compact, by openness of interior and
compactness of $X$ in Propositions
\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus} and
\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact}. Its tropical image is compact and
does not contain $w$. Choose the box $P$ in the preceding proof
also disjoint from that image. Then $Y=t^{-1}(P)$ lies in
$\operatorname{Int}(X/K)$ and is pure of analytic dimension $d$,
by Theorem~\ref{v036:analytic:domainpurity}.

Fix a transverse integral $Q$; one exists by choosing a basis of
$N_0$ extending to a basis of $\mathbb Z^n$, using
Lemma~\ref{cand:elementarylatticesplitting}, and projecting onto
the first $d$ coordinates. The entire level of $Q\circ t|_Y$ at
$Qw$ is $F_w\subset\operatorname{Int}(Y/K)$, as above. Theorem
\ref{v036:analytic:finitecollar} therefore supplies a closed
rectangular neighborhood $P'$ of $Qw$ for which
\[
               Y'=(Q\circ t|_Y)^{-1}(P')\longrightarrow\mathbb T_{P'}
\]
is finite and surjective, with a fixed positive Gauss degree
$\delta$ at every $s\in P'$.

The affine restriction
$Q:w+(N_0)_{\mathbb R}\to\mathbb R^d$ is an isomorphism. Its
inverse sends $P'$ exactly to $t(Y')$: inclusion in one direction
follows from $t(Y)\subset w+(N_0)_{\mathbb R}$, and the other
follows from surjectivity of $Y'\to\mathbb T_{P'}$.
Take an open neighborhood
\[
                    V\subset\operatorname{Int}(P)
                                  \cap Q^{-1}(\operatorname{Int}(P'))
\]
of $w$. The preceding equality proves
$t(X)\cap V=(w+(N_0)_{\mathbb R})\cap V$.
For $v$ in this set, its whole fibre in $X$ belongs to $Y'$,
since $v\in\operatorname{Int}(P)$ and $Qv\in P'$; in particular
it is interior over $K$. The local flat-germ condition at $v$
follows by restricting to a sufficiently small neighborhood
inside $W$, since $v+(N_0)_{\mathbb R}=w+(N_0)_{\mathbb R}$.

Restriction of the finite polyannulus map to its exact level at
$s=Qv$ has algebra $D_v$, by
Theorem~\ref{v036:analytic:finitecollar} and injectivity of $Q$ on
the affine plane. Laurent polynomials are dense both in this
target's exact-radius algebra and in the polyannulus algebra
evaluated at $\eta_s$. Their completed Gauss residue fields are
therefore the same completion of $K(T_1,\ldots,T_d)$ with its
weighted Gauss norm. Finite base change consequently identifies
the Gauss fibre of $D_v$ with that of $Y'$ over $\eta_s$.
Thus $\delta_Q(v)=\delta$ for every such $v$, including $w$.
Theorem~\ref{v036:flatgermweight} gives
\[
                 m_{X,f}(v)=\frac{\delta}{[\mathbb Z^d:Q(N_0)]}
                            =m_{X,f}(w).
\]

Finally an affinoid domain containing the whole fibre has the
same actual fibre domain, with the same analytic structure.
This follows from domain restriction and the two universal
factorizations in Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange}.
The local image equality just proved, applied to both domains,
and inclusion of their moment images identify their tangent
planes: both have dimension $d$. Their saturated tangent lattices
are consequently equal.
The fibre algebra and its monomial maps to each target polytorus are
unchanged, so their Gauss degrees, and hence their calibrated
weights, agree.
\end{proof}
