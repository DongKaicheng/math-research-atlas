Rational approximation of a nonzero germ

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 914

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Rational approximation of a nonzero germ}
{非零函数芽的有理逼近}]
\label{cand:rationalnormgerm}
\bi{Every point of $\Sigma$ has algebraic support the generic point
of $X$. If $x\in\Sigma$ and $g$ is an analytic germ with $g(x)\ne0$,
there is $f\in K(X)^\times$ such that $|f|=|g|$ on an ordinary
neighborhood of $x$ on which both are defined. If $|g(x)|=1$,
$f(x)$ and $g(x)$ can be required to have the same residue.}
{$\Sigma$ 的每一点在 $X$ 上的代数支撑均为泛点。若
$x\in\Sigma$，且解析函数芽 $g$ 满足 $g(x)\ne0$，则存在
$f\in K(X)^\times$，使 $|f|=|g|$ 在 $x$ 的某个通常邻域上
成立，其中两函数都有定义。若 $|g(x)|=1$，还可使 $f(x)$
与 $g(x)$ 具有相同剩余类。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{At a component vertex, Proposition~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield}
gives residual transcendence degree one. At an interior annular
Gauss point, Proposition~\ref{new:gauss_residue_formula} gives either
residual transcendence degree one or a nonzero relative value-group
rank. A point supported at an algebraic closed point would have
completed residue field $K$, by algebraic closedness and
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineanalytificationpoints}. These alternatives
exclude closed algebraic support; an integral algebraic curve has
only generic and closed points.}
{在分量顶点，Proposition~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield}
给出剩余超越次数一。在圆环内部的 Gauss 点，
Proposition~\ref{new:gauss_residue_formula} 给出剩余超越次数一，
或非零的相对值群秩。若解析点的代数支撑是闭点，则由
$K$ 代数闭及 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineanalytificationpoints}，
其完备剩余域为 $K$。前述两种情况均排除这种可能；整代数
曲线只有泛点与闭点。}

\bi{By the same description of analytification, $\mathcal H(x)$
is the completion of $K(X)$ for the norm of $x$. Choose
$f\in K(X)$ with $|f(x)-g(x)|<|g(x)|$. This makes $f\ne0$.
The rational function and its inverse are regular on a Zariski open
containing the generic point, so their analytifications are defined
near $x$. Continuity preserves the displayed strict inequality on a
smaller ordinary neighborhood. The ultrametric inequality gives
$|f|=|g|$ there. When $|g(x)|=1$, the same inequality says that
the residues agree.}
{由同一解析化描述，$\mathcal H(x)$ 是 $K(X)$ 对 $x$ 的范数
的完备化。选择 $f\in K(X)$，使
$|f(x)-g(x)|<|g(x)|$，则 $f\ne0$。有理函数及其逆在包含
泛点的某个 Zariski 开集上正则，因此其解析化在 $x$ 附近
有定义。由连续性，所列严格不等式在较小的通常邻域上仍
成立；超度量不等式给出 $|f|=|g|$。若 $|g(x)|=1$，同一
不等式说明二者剩余类相同。}
\end{proof}
