Mixed Gauss displacement

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Mixed Gauss displacement}
{混合 Gauss 位移}]
\label{cand:mixedgaussdisplacement}
\bi{For $x\in V$, write $E=\mathcal H(x)$,
$\xi_i=X_i(x)\in E^\times$ and $b_j=T_j(x)\in E$.
For $0<t\leq1$, take the Gauss absolute value of radius $t$
in all variables $S_1,\ldots,S_r,Z_1,\ldots,Z_q$ over $E$,
and extend it to the completed rational function field $E_t$.
The substitutions}
{对 $x\in V$，写 $E=\mathcal H(x)$、
$\xi_i=X_i(x)\in E^\times$ 及 $b_j=T_j(x)\in E$。
当 $0<t\leq1$ 时，在 $E$ 上对全部变量
$S_1,\ldots,S_r,Z_1,\ldots,Z_q$ 取半径 $t$ 的 Gauss
绝对值，并延拓到完备有理函数域 $E_t$。代换}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 X_i&\longmapsto\xi_i(1+S_i) &&(1\leq i\leq r),\\
 X_0&\longmapsto\xi_0\prod_{i=1}^r(1+S_i)^{-1},\\
 T_j&\longmapsto b_j+Z_j &&(1\leq j\leq q).
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{define a bounded homomorphism $A\to E_t$ and hence a
point $H(x,t)\in V$. Set $H(x,0)=x$. For every $t\in[0,1]$,}
{定义有界同态 $A\to E_t$，从而定义点 $H(x,t)\in V$。
置 $H(x,0)=x$。对每个 $t\in[0,1]$，有}
\[
 |X_i(H(x,t))|=|X_i(x)|\quad(0\leq i\leq r),\qquad
 |T_j(H(x,t))|=\max\{|T_j(x)|,t\}.
\]
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The Gauss absolute value is multiplicative by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm}. For $t>0$ the polynomial
$1+S_i$ has norm $1$, so it is nonzero and invertible in the
rational function field, also when $t=1$. All displayed
coordinate images have norm at most one, and their product
in the first $r+1$ coordinates is $a$. Convergent restricted
series can therefore be evaluated in the complete field $E_t$.
This yields a homomorphism from the Tate algebra, contractive
for its Gauss norm, which kills the defining relation. It
descends contractively for the quotient norm on $A$.
Multiplicativity and the exact Gauss norms of $1+S_i$ and
$b_j+Z_j$ give the stated coordinate values. At $t=0$ they
are the values at $x$, as required.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm}，Gauss 绝对值具有
乘性。当 $t>0$ 时，多项式 $1+S_i$ 的范数为 $1$，
故它在有理函数域中非零且可逆，$t=1$ 时也如此。
所列坐标像的范数均至多为一，且前 $r+1$ 个坐标之积
等于 $a$。因此收敛受限级数可在完备域 $E_t$ 中取值，
得到从 Tate 代数出发、关于其 Gauss 范数压缩的同态，
并消去定义关系。它下降为关于 $A$ 的商范数压缩的同态。
乘性及 $1+S_i$、$b_j+Z_j$ 的精确 Gauss 范数给出所述
坐标值。当 $t=0$ 时，它们就是 $x$ 处的值。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Let $K$ be complete and nontrivially valued, with its original
absolute value. Fix $0<|a|<1$ and integers $r,q\geq0$. Put}
{设 $K$ 关于原绝对值完备且非平凡赋值。固定
$0<|a|<1$ 及整数 $r,q\geq0$，置}
\[
 A=K\langle X_0,\ldots,X_r,T_1,\ldots,T_q\rangle
                /(X_0\cdots X_r-a),\qquad V=\mathcal M(A).
\]
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:standardmonomialchartgeometry} gives
the continuous Gauss section $s:\Delta_a\to V$, where}
{Proposition~\ref{fnd:standardmonomialchartgeometry} 给出
连续 Gauss 截面 $s:\Delta_a\to V$，其中}
\[
 \Delta_a=\{(w_0,\ldots,w_r)\in\mathbb R_{\geq0}^{r+1}:
                         \sum_iw_i=v(a)\}.
\]
\bi{After eliminating $X_0=a/(X_1\cdots X_r)$, $s(w)$ is the
Gauss point of radii $e^{-w_i}$ in $X_i$, $1\leq i\leq r$,
and radius $1$ in every $T_j$. Let $\Sigma_a=s(\Delta_a)$ and
write $\operatorname{val}:V\to\Delta_a$ for the values of
$X_0,\ldots,X_r$.}
{消去 $X_0=a/(X_1\cdots X_r)$ 后，$s(w)$ 是在
$X_i$、$1\leq i\leq r$ 上半径为 $e^{-w_i}$、在每个
$T_j$ 上半径为 $1$ 的 Gauss 点。令
$\Sigma_a=s(\Delta_a)$，并以
$\operatorname{val}:V\to\Delta_a$ 记
$X_0,\ldots,X_r$ 的赋值映射。}
