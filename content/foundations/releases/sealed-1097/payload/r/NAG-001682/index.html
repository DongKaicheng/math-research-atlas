Exactness of completed tensors over a field

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 270

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Exactness of completed tensors over a field}{域上完备张量积的正合性}]
\label{fnd:grusonstrictexact}
\bi{Let $0\to E\to F\to G\to0$ be an exact sequence of Banach $K$-spaces, with $E$ carrying the induced norm of a closed subspace of $F$ and $G$ the quotient norm. For every Banach $K$-space $H$, the sequence}
{设 $0\to E\to F\to G\to0$ 为 Banach $K$-空间的正合列，其中 $E$ 配备作为 $F$ 的闭子空间的诱导范数，$G$ 配备商范数。对每个 Banach $K$-空间 $H$，序列}
\[
 0\longrightarrow E\widehat\otimes_K H
 \longrightarrow F\widehat\otimes_K H
 \longrightarrow G\widehat\otimes_K H
 \longrightarrow0
\]
\bi{is exact, its injection is isometric, and its surjection induces an isometry on the quotient. In particular completed tensoring preserves norm-strict short exact sequences with any equivalent Banach norms.}
{正合，其中单射等距，满射在商上诱导等距同构。特别地，对任意等价的 Banach 范数，完备张量积保持 norm-strict 短正合列。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Algebraic tensoring over $K$ is exact, as follows by choosing an algebraic linear splitting of $F\to G$. On the algebraic quotient $(F\otimes_K H)/(E\otimes_K H)$, compare its quotient projective norm with the projective norm on $G\otimes_K H$. Contractivity of $F\to G$ gives one inequality. For the other, take an expression $z=\sum_j g_j\otimes h_j$ and $c>1$. Lift each nonzero $g_j$ to $f_j\in F$ with $\|f_j\|\leq c\|g_j\|$, and lift zero by zero. The tensor $\sum_j f_j\otimes h_j$ represents $z$ and has norm at most $c\max_j\|g_j\|\|h_j\|$. Taking both infima and then $c\downarrow1$ proves equality of the two quotient norms.}
{在 $K$ 上的代数张量积正合：对 $F\to G$ 选择一个代数线性分裂即可。比较代数商 $(F\otimes_K H)/(E\otimes_K H)$ 的商 projective 范数与 $G\otimes_K H$ 的 projective 范数。$F\to G$ 为压缩映射，给出一个方向的不等式。对另一方向，取表达式 $z=\sum_j g_j\otimes h_j$ 及 $c>1$。把每个非零 $g_j$ 提升为 $f_j\in F$，使 $\|f_j\|\leq c\|g_j\|$，零元提升为零。张量 $\sum_j f_j\otimes h_j$ 代表 $z$，且范数至多为 $c\max_j\|g_j\|\|h_j\|$。分别取下确界，再令 $c\downarrow1$，即得两个商范数相等。}

\bi{By Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonisometrictensor}, $E\widehat\otimes_K H$ embeds isometrically in $F\widehat\otimes_K H$. Its image is exactly the closure of $E\otimes_K H$. Quotienting by this closed image gives a Banach space by Lemma~\ref{fnd:completequotient}. The image of the algebraic quotient is dense and has the quotient norm just calculated: for an algebraic tensor, taking distance to a subspace or to its closure gives the same infimum. Consequently this Banach quotient is the completion of $G\otimes_K H$ for its projective norm, hence is $G\widehat\otimes_K H$. This proves all three assertions. Uniform inequalities between equivalent norms pass to projective norms, quotient norms and completions, giving the last statement.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonisometrictensor}，$E\widehat\otimes_K H$ 等距嵌入 $F\widehat\otimes_K H$，其像恰为 $E\otimes_K H$ 的闭包。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:completequotient}，对此闭像取商得到 Banach 空间。代数商的像稠密，且具有刚计算出的商范数，因为一个代数张量到子空间及其闭包的距离具有相同下确界。因此所得 Banach 商就是 $G\otimes_K H$ 关于 projective 范数的完备化，即 $G\widehat\otimes_K H$。这证明全部断言。等价范数之间的一致不等式传递到 projective 范数、商范数及完备化，给出最后一项。}
\end{proof}
