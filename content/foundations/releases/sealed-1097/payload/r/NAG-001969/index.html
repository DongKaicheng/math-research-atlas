Finite coverings and inverse images of germ reductions

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 566

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Finite coverings and inverse images of germ reductions}
{芽约化的有限覆盖与逆像}]
\label{fnd:gradedgermcovercomparison}
\bi{Let $(X,x)$ be a good germ and let $(X_i,x)$ be finitely
many good subdomain germs covering it. Then}
{设 $(X,x)$ 为 good 芽，有限多个 good 子域芽 $(X_i,x)$
覆盖它。则}
\[
 \mathscr R(X,x)=\bigcup_i\mathscr R(X_i,x).
\]
\bi{For a morphism of good germs $(Y,y)\to(X,x)$ and a
subdomain germ $X'\subset X$, reduction of the inverse image
is the inverse image of $\mathscr R(X',x)$ under restriction
of graded valuations. Intersections and finite unions of
subdomain germs are consequently compatible with reduction.
Here the reduction of a general subdomain germ of a good
germ is the union of the reductions in any finite affinoid
cover; this union is independent of that cover.}
{对 good 芽态射 $(Y,y)\to(X,x)$ 及子域芽 $X'\subset X$，
逆像的约化等于 $\mathscr R(X',x)$ 在分次赋值限制映射下的
逆像。因此，子域芽的交及有限并均与约化相容。
这里，good 芽的一般子域芽的约化定义为任一有限 affinoid
覆盖中各片约化的并；这个并与覆盖选择无关。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedweierstrassgerm} to refine the
cover and shrink an affinoid representative of $X$ so that
the members have the form}
{利用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedweierstrassgerm} 加细覆盖，并
缩小 $X$ 的一个 affinoid 代表，使覆盖成员形如}
\[
 X_i=\{z:|f_{ij}(z)|\leq r_{ij},\ 1\leq j\leq n\},
 \qquad r_{ij}=|f_{ij}(x)|,\qquad f_{in}=1.
\]
\bi{All the functions may be made invertible near $x$.
Let $J$ be the tuples $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_m)$ with at least
one $a_i=n$, and put $g_a=\prod_i f_{ia_i}$,
$s_a=\prod_i r_{ia_i}$. The rational domains}
{在 $x$ 附近可令全部函数可逆。令 $J$ 为至少一个 $a_i=n$
的元组 $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_m)$，设
$g_a=\prod_i f_{ia_i}$、$s_a=\prod_i r_{ia_i}$。
Rational 域}
\[
 V_a=\{z:|g_{a'}(z)/g_a(z)|\leq s_{a'}/s_a
                                      \text{ for every }a'\in J\}
\]
\bi{cover $X$, by choosing a largest $|g_a(z)|/s_a$.
They refine the $X_i$. Indeed fix $i$ with $a_i=n$ and
$z\in V_a$, and choose $\ell$ with $z\in X_\ell$.
If $\ell\ne i$, the tuples replacing $a_\ell$ by $n$
first show $|f_{\ell a_\ell}(z)|=r_{\ell a_\ell}$.
Replacing also $a_i$ by $j$ then gives
$|f_{ij}(z)|\leq r_{ij}$ for every $j$. If $\ell=i$ this
already holds. Thus $V_a\subset X_i$.
Write $\gamma_a\in F_x$ for the initial value of $g_a$.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedweierstrassgerm} identifies the
reductions of the $V_a$ with}
{覆盖 $X$：在每个 $z$ 处选取最大的 $|g_a(z)|/s_a$ 即可。
它们也加细各 $X_i$。事实上，固定 $a_i=n$ 的 $i$ 及
$z\in V_a$，再取满足 $z\in X_\ell$ 的 $\ell$。
若 $\ell\ne i$，先把 $a_\ell$ 改为 $n$，得到
$|f_{\ell a_\ell}(z)|=r_{\ell a_\ell}$；再同时将 $a_i$
改为 $j$，便得到每个 $j$ 的
$|f_{ij}(z)|\leq r_{ij}$。若 $\ell=i$，该结论已成立。
因此 $V_a\subset X_i$。以 $\gamma_a\in F_x$ 记 $g_a$
的首项值。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedweierstrassgerm}，
各 $V_a$ 的约化为}
\[
 \mathscr R(X,x)\{\gamma_{a'}/\gamma_a:a'\in J\}.
\]
\bi{These rational valuation sets cover $\mathscr R(X,x)$:
any finite list of nonzero homogeneous elements admits a
largest member in the divisibility preorder of a valuation
ring. Since each lies in the reduction of the corresponding
$X_i$, the covering assertion follows.}
{这些 rational 赋值集合覆盖 $\mathscr R(X,x)$：赋值环的
整除预序使有限多个非零齐次元素具有最大成员。
每个这样的集合又包含于相应 $X_i$ 的约化，所以得到覆盖断言。}

\bi{Define the reduction of a subdomain germ by the stated
finite union. Each affinoid piece gives an open containment locus
by Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedaffinoidgerminvariance}, quasi-compact
by Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedrzcompact}. The finite union is thus
a quasi-compact open. Two such covers have a common finite refinement
by affinoid subdomain germs: refine their pairwise intersections.
The proved covering assertion on each original affinoid
piece identifies all three unions. Thus the definition is
independent of the cover.
For an active Weierstrass inequality, inverse image simply
replaces its defining function by its pullback. The isometric
embedding $\mathcal H(x)\hookrightarrow\mathcal H(y)$ preserves
its initial value and degree. The displayed formula of
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedweierstrassgerm} therefore proves the
inverse-image assertion for each such inequality, and hence
for finite intersections of them. Refine a general subdomain
by these pieces and use the proved finite-cover assertion.
Intersection is the particular case of inverse image by a
subdomain inclusion; finite union follows from finite covers.}
{按所述有限并定义子域芽的约化。依
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedaffinoidgerminvariance}，每个 affinoid 片
给出开包含集，由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedrzcompact} 知其拟紧，
故有限并为拟紧开集。两个覆盖可通过加细各成员的两两交，
得到共同有限 affinoid 子域芽加细。将已证的覆盖断言用于每个
原 affinoid 片，就识别了三个并。因此定义与覆盖无关。
对有效 Weierstrass 不等式，取逆像只是把定义函数换为拉回。
等距嵌入 $\mathcal H(x)\hookrightarrow\mathcal H(y)$ 保持
其首项值与次数。因此 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedweierstrassgerm}
中的公式证明单个不等式及它们有限交的逆像断言。
对一般子域，按这种片加细，再使用已证的有限覆盖断言。
交是子域包含下逆像的特例，而有限并来自有限覆盖。}
\end{proof}
