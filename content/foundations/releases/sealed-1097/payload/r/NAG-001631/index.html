Ordinary local testing for good spaces

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 417

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Ordinary local testing for good spaces}{Good 空间上的普通局部测试}]
\label{fnd:goodinteriorlocaltesting}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good analytic spaces. Let $X_0$ be an affinoid neighborhood of $x=f(y)$ and $Y_0\subset f^{-1}(X_0)$ an affinoid neighborhood of $y$ in $Y$. Then}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 是 good 解析空间间的态射。令 $X_0$ 为 $x=f(y)$ 的 affinoid 邻域，$Y_0\subset f^{-1}(X_0)$ 为 $y$ 在 $Y$ 中的 affinoid 邻域。则}
\[
 y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)
       \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y_0/X_0).
\]
\bi{In particular, relative interior is open for morphisms of good spaces.}
{特别地，good 空间间的态射的相对内部为开集。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{If $y$ satisfies the general definition, take a witnessing affinoid neighborhood $V\subset f^{-1}(X_0)$ with $y\in\operatorname{Int}(V/X_0)$. Choose an affinoid neighborhood $W$ of $y$ inside $V\cap Y_0$, for example a rational neighborhood in $Y_0$ contained in this ordinary neighborhood. It is an affinoid domain of both $V$ and $Y_0$. Since $W$ is a neighborhood in $V$, the domain-interior theorem and the composition formula give $y\in\operatorname{Int}(W/X_0)$, and the same formula for $W\to Y_0\to X_0$ gives the desired condition.}
{若 $y$ 满足一般定义，则取见证的 affinoid 邻域 $V\subset f^{-1}(X_0)$，满足 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(V/X_0)$。在 $V\cap Y_0$ 中选取 $y$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $W$，例如在 $Y_0$ 中取包含于此普通邻域的 rational 邻域。它同时是 $V$ 与 $Y_0$ 的 affinoid 域。因为 $W$ 是 $V$ 中的邻域，解析域内部定理与复合公式给出 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(W/X_0)$；再对 $W\to Y_0\to X_0$ 用同一公式，得到所需条件。}

\bi{For the converse, fix any affinoid domain $U\subset X$ containing $x$. Choose a rational neighborhood $U'\subset U$ of $x$ contained in the open set $U\cap\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(X_0,X)$. Then $U'$ is also an affinoid domain of $X_0$. The inverse image $V'=Y_0\times_{X_0}U'$ is affinoid and is a neighborhood of $y$ in $f^{-1}(U)$. The base-change inclusion gives $y\in\operatorname{Int}(V'/U')$; the domain-interior theorem gives $x\in\operatorname{Int}(U'/U)$. Composition proves $y\in\operatorname{Int}(V'/U)$, which is the required witness. Finally, on the ordinary interiors of $Y_0$ and $X_0$, the displayed criterion identifies the relative interior with an open affinoid-interior locus. These neighborhoods cover $Y$, proving openness.}
{反之，固定任意包含 $x$ 的 affinoid 域 $U\subset X$。选择 $x$ 在 $U$ 中的 rational 邻域 $U'$，使其包含于开集 $U\cap\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(X_0,X)$。于是 $U'$ 也是 $X_0$ 的 affinoid 域。逆像 $V'=Y_0\times_{X_0}U'$ 为 affinoid，且是 $y$ 在 $f^{-1}(U)$ 中的邻域。基变换的正向包含给出 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(V'/U')$，解析域内部定理给出 $x\in\operatorname{Int}(U'/U)$。复合得到 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(V'/U)$，正是所需见证。最后，在 $Y_0$ 与 $X_0$ 的普通内部上，所列判据将相对内部识别为开 affinoid 内部轨迹。这些邻域覆盖 $Y$，因此相对内部为开集。}
\end{proof}
