Weighted coefficient completeness

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 145

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Weighted coefficient completeness}{加权系数空间的完备性}]\label{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness}
\bi{Let $I$ be a countable set and let $(w_i)_{i\in I}$ be positive real numbers. Put}
{设 $I$ 为可数集合，$(w_i)_{i\in I}$ 为正实数族。记}
\[
 \begin{split}
 c_0(I,M;w)
   &=\{(m_i)_{i\in I}:
       \text{for every }\varepsilon>0,\ 
       \#\{i:\|m_i\|w_i\geq\varepsilon\}<\infty\},\\
 \|(m_i)\|_w&=\sup_{i\in I}\|m_i\|w_i .
 \end{split}
\]
\bi{We put $\sup\varnothing=0$ if $I$ is empty. This is a Banach $A$-module, and its finitely supported families are dense. Each nonzero family attains its norm at a coordinate. The coordinate map at $i$ has bound $w_i^{-1}$.}
{当 $I$ 为空时，约定 $\sup\varnothing=0$。这是 Banach $A$-模，其中有限支撑族稠密。每个非零族的范数都在某个坐标处达到。第 $i$ 个坐标映射的界为 $w_i^{-1}$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The finite-tail condition makes the supremum finite. The ultrametric inequality, homogeneity, definiteness and the contractive $A$-action follow coordinatewise. To see that a nonzero family attains its norm, choose a coordinate of positive weighted norm $t$. Only finitely many coordinates have weighted norm at least $t$, and the maximum among them is the supremum of all coordinates.}
{有限尾条件保证上确界有限。逐坐标应用范数的性质，即得超度量不等式、齐次性、正定性以及压缩的 $A$-作用。对非零族，选取一个加权范数为 $t>0$ 的坐标。加权范数至少为 $t$ 的坐标只有有限个；其中的最大值便是所有坐标的上确界。}

\bi{Let $x^{(j)}=(m_i^{(j)})$ be Cauchy. For each $i$, the inequality}
{设 $x^{(j)}=(m_i^{(j)})$ 为 Cauchy 序列。对每个 $i$，不等式}
\[
 \|m_i^{(j)}-m_i^{(k)}\|
 \leq w_i^{-1}\|x^{(j)}-x^{(k)}\|_w
\]
\bi{gives a limit $m_i\in M$. Given $\varepsilon>0$, choose $j$ so that the norm on the right before multiplication by $w_i^{-1}$ is less than $\varepsilon/2$ for all sufficiently large $k$. Passing to the limit in each coordinate gives}
{给出极限 $m_i\in M$。给定 $\varepsilon>0$，选取 $j$，使所有充分大的 $k$ 都满足 $\|x^{(j)}-x^{(k)}\|_w<\varepsilon/2$。逐坐标取极限，得到}
\[
 \|m_i-m_i^{(j)}\|w_i\leq\varepsilon/2
 \qquad(i\in I).
\]
\bi{Outside a finite set, $\|m_i^{(j)}\|w_i<\varepsilon/2$, so $\|m_i\|w_i\leq\varepsilon/2$ there. Thus $x=(m_i)$ belongs to the displayed space, and the same estimate proves convergence to $x$. Finally, retaining all coordinates with weighted norm at least $\varepsilon/2$ gives a finite truncation with error at most $\varepsilon/2$. This proves density and completeness. The coordinate bound is part of the definition of the supremum norm.}
{在某个有限集合之外，有 $\|m_i^{(j)}\|w_i<\varepsilon/2$，因而 $\|m_i\|w_i\leq\varepsilon/2$。所以 $x=(m_i)$ 属于上述空间，同一个估计证明原序列收敛到 $x$。最后，保留所有加权范数至少为 $\varepsilon/2$ 的坐标，便得到误差不超过 $\varepsilon/2$ 的有限截断。这证明稠密性和完备性。坐标映射的界直接来自上确界范数的定义。}
\end{proof}
