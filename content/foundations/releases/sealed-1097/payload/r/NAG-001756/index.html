The overconvergent restriction interface

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 324

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{The overconvergent restriction interface}{超收敛限制的接口}]
\label{fnd:overconvergentflatnessreduction}
\bi{Let $Y\to X$ and $Y'\to X$ be morphisms of good spaces. Suppose an ordinary analytic neighborhood $W$ of $y$ in $Y$ is identified over $X$ with a good analytic domain in $Y'$, and suppose coherent sheaves $\mathcal F$ on $Y$ and $\mathcal G$ on $Y'$ have identified restrictions to $W$. Then $\mathcal F$ is naively $X$-flat at $y$ if and only if $\mathcal G$ is; the same equivalence holds for analytic $X$-flatness. Both statements are unchanged by restricting $X$ to an ordinary neighborhood of the image point and the sources to its inverse images.}
{设 $Y\to X$、$Y'\to X$ 为 good 空间态射。假设 $y$ 在 $Y$ 中的某个普通解析邻域 $W$，在 $X$ 上识别为 $Y'$ 中的一个 good 解析域；且 $Y$ 上凝聚层 $\mathcal F$ 与 $Y'$ 上凝聚层 $\mathcal G$ 在 $W$ 上的限制已识别。则 $\mathcal F$ 在 $y$ 处朴素 $X$-平坦，当且仅当 $\mathcal G$ 如此；解析 $X$-平坦性亦有同样等价。把 $X$ 限制到像点的普通邻域、把源限制到其逆像，不改变这两个结论。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The ordinary restriction from $Y$ to $W$ preserves the stalk at $y$. After any good analytic base change, its inverse image is again an ordinary neighborhood of every point over $y$, so it preserves all analytic-flatness tests too. On the other side, Corollary~\ref{fnd:sourcedomainflatnessdetection} gives the equivalence of both types of flatness for $\mathcal G$ and its restriction to the source domain $W$. Combine these two facts with the given isomorphism. A target ordinary open restriction changes neither the target stalk nor the source stalk; every base-change test at the point factors locally through that target open set. Hence it also preserves both assertions.}
{从 $Y$ 到普通邻域 $W$ 的限制保持 $y$ 处的茎。在任意 good 解析基变换后，其逆像仍是 $y$ 上方每个点的普通邻域，因此也保持全部解析平坦性测试。另一方面，Corollary~\ref{fnd:sourcedomainflatnessdetection} 给出 $\mathcal G$ 与其在源解析域 $W$ 上的限制之间，两种平坦性的等价。结合给定同构即得结论。目标的普通开限制不改变目标茎或源茎，且该点处的任意基变换测试局部因子分解经过此目标开集，所以它也保持两个断言。}
\end{proof}
