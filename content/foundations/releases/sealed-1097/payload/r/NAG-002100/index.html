An intrinsic centre at a split node

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{An intrinsic centre at a split node}
{分裂节点处的内禀中心}]
\label{cand:nodefittinginsertion}
\bi{Assume $K$ is algebraically closed. Let $q$ be a node of a nodal curve model
$\mathcal X$. Choose a pointed \textup{\'etale} chart with
equation $st=b$, as in
Theorem~\ref{cand:pointednodalvaluationchart}.
For each $\rho\in|K^\times|$ with $|b|<\rho<1$ there is
an admissible blowup of $\widehat{\mathcal X}$, induced by
an algebraic blowup supported at $q$, whose new nodal model
contains the two annular radii $\rho$ and $|b|/\rho$ as
component points, counted only once if they coincide.
All old component points are retained. A projective model
remains projective.}
{假设 $K$ 代数闭，设 $q$ 为节点曲线模型 $\mathcal X$ 的节点。
按照 Theorem~\ref{cand:pointednodalvaluationchart}，
选择方程为 $st=b$ 的带点 \textup{\'etale} 坐标片。
对每个满足 $|b|<\rho<1$ 的 $\rho\in|K^\times|$，
存在 $\widehat{\mathcal X}$ 的 admissible blowup，
由支撑于 $q$ 的代数 blowup 诱导，使新节点模型以圆环中
半径 $\rho$ 与 $|b|/\rho$ 的点为分量点；二者相同时只计
一次。全部旧分量点保留，射影模型仍为射影模型。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Shrink an algebraic neighborhood $U$ of $q$ so that $q$
is its only nonsmooth special point. Set}
{缩小 $q$ 的代数邻域 $U$，使 $q$ 为其中唯一的特殊
纤维非光滑点。令}
\[
 \mathcal J=\operatorname{Fitt}_1\Omega_{U/O}.
\]
\bi{The module of differentials is finitely presented, and
$\mathcal J$ is a finite-type quasi-coherent ideal
\cite[Tag 0C3E]{StacksAlg}. For an \textup{\'etale} map,
differentials pull back isomorphically
\cite[Tag 04BX]{StacksAlg}, and Fitting ideals commute with
pullback \cite[Tag 0C3D]{StacksAlg}. The standard nodal
presentation}
{微分模为有限表示，且 $\mathcal J$ 为有限型拟凝聚理想
\cite[Tag 0C3E]{StacksAlg}。在 \textup{\'etale} 映射下，
微分模按拉回同构变化 \cite[Tag 04BX]{StacksAlg}，
而 Fitting 理想与拉回相容 \cite[Tag 0C3D]{StacksAlg}。
标准节点表示}
\[
 O[s,t]/(st-b)
 \xrightarrow{\ (t,s)\ }
 \bigl(O[s,t]/(st-b)\bigr)^{\oplus2}
 \longrightarrow\Omega_{(O[s,t]/(st-b))/O}\longrightarrow0
\]
\bi{therefore identifies the pullback of $\mathcal J$ with
$(s,t)$ in every chosen pointed nodal chart.}
{因此在每个所选带点节点坐标片中，将 $\mathcal J$ 的拉回
识别为 $(s,t)$。}

\bi{Choose $\lambda\in K^\times$ with
$|\lambda|=\max(\rho,|b|/\rho)<1$. Then
$|b|\leq|\lambda|^2$. On $U$ use the ideal
$\mathcal I=(\lambda)+\mathcal J$. It is the unit ideal on
$U\setminus\{q\}$: on the generic fibre $\lambda$ is
invertible, and at a smooth special point the differential
module is free of rank one, so $\mathcal J$ is the unit
ideal. Hence $\mathcal I$ glues with the unit ideal on
$\mathcal X\setminus\{q\}$. This gives a global finite-type
ideal supported at $q$ and containing $(\lambda)$. A power
of any fixed topologically nilpotent $\pi$ lies in
$(\lambda)$, so its completed ideal is open.}
{选择 $\lambda\in K^\times$，使
$|\lambda|=\max(\rho,|b|/\rho)<1$，则
$|b|\leq|\lambda|^2$。在 $U$ 上使用理想
$\mathcal I=(\lambda)+\mathcal J$。它在
$U\setminus\{q\}$ 上等于单位理想：泛纤维上 $\lambda$
可逆；在光滑特殊点，微分模自由且秩为一，所以
$\mathcal J$ 为单位理想。因此，$\mathcal I$ 与
$\mathcal X\setminus\{q\}$ 上的单位理想粘合，得到支撑
于 $q$、包含 $(\lambda)$ 的全局有限型理想。任意固定
拓扑幂零元 $\pi$ 的某个幂属于 $(\lambda)$，故完备化
理想为开理想。}

\bi{Blowup commutes with flat, in particular \textup{\'etale},
base change \cite[Tag 0805]{StacksAlg}. Thus its pullback
to the auxiliary pointed chart is exactly the blowup of
$(\lambda,s,t)$ in Lemma~\ref{cand:symmetricnodeblowup}.
This use of the Fitting ideal makes the centre descend even
when both local branches belong to the same global component.
The chosen auxiliary neighborhood can be shrunk so its
fibre over $q$ is just the specified rational point.
Its pullback over the exceptional fibre is consequently an
isomorphism on that fibre. The new components and their
attachment points are therefore precisely those computed
in the standard charts.}
{Blowup 与平坦基变换相容，特别与 \textup{\'etale}
基变换相容 \cite[Tag 0805]{StacksAlg}。因此，它在辅助
带点坐标片上的拉回，正是
Lemma~\ref{cand:symmetricnodeblowup} 中
$(\lambda,s,t)$ 的 blowup。使用 Fitting 理想使中心在
两条局部分支属于同一个整体分量时仍能下降。可将辅助邻域
缩小，使其在 $q$ 上方的纤维只有所指定的有理点。
于是，其在例外纤维上的拉回在该纤维上为同构。因此，新增
分量及其连接点恰为标准坐标片中计算出的分量和点。}

\bi{The explicit charts are flat with reduced nodal special
fibres and smooth generic fibres. These properties descend
through the indicated \textup{\'etale} neighborhoods;
flatness is fpqc local on the source
\cite[Tag 036K]{StacksAlg}, and local finite presentation
is fppf local on the source \cite[Tag 036N]{StacksAlg}.
The blowup is quasi-compact and separated over the original
finite-presentation model, hence the new model is finitely
presented. Its formal generic fibre is unchanged by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:formalglobalblowupcalculus}, so remains
Hausdorff. Away from $q$ the model is unchanged, including
each old component generic point. The chart radii and lengths
are those of Lemma~\ref{cand:symmetricnodeblowup}.
Finally the blowup is projective
\cite[Tag 02NS]{StacksAlg}; if $\mathcal X$ is projective
over $O$, the composite is projective by
\cite[Tag 0C4P]{StacksAlg}, since $\operatorname{Spec}O$
is quasi-compact and quasi-separated.}
{显式坐标片平坦，具有约化节点特殊纤维及光滑泛纤维。
这些性质通过上述 \textup{\'etale} 邻域下降；平坦性对源
的 fpqc 拓扑局部 \cite[Tag 036K]{StacksAlg}，
局部有限表示性对源的 fppf 拓扑局部
\cite[Tag 036N]{StacksAlg}。Blowup 在原有限表示模型上
拟紧且分离，因而新模型有限表示。由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:formalglobalblowupcalculus}，形式泛纤维
不变，故仍为 Hausdorff。模型在 $q$ 外不变，特别保留每个
旧分量泛点。坐标半径和边长由
Lemma~\ref{cand:symmetricnodeblowup} 给出。
最后，由 \cite[Tag 02NS]{StacksAlg}，该 blowup 射影；
若 $\mathcal X$ 在 $O$ 上射影，则因
$\operatorname{Spec}O$ 拟紧且拟分离，
\cite[Tag 0C4P]{StacksAlg} 保证复合仍射影。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Let $K$ be complete and nontrivially valued.
We write $O=K^\circ$, $\mathfrak m=K^{\circ\circ}$ and
$k=O/\mathfrak m$. A nodal curve model in this section is a flat
finitely presented $O$-scheme with smooth generic fibre, reduced
nodal special fibre, and Hausdorff formal generic fibre.}
{设 $K$ 完备且非平凡赋值。
记 $O=K^\circ$、$\mathfrak m=K^{\circ\circ}$、
$k=O/\mathfrak m$。本节中的节点曲线模型是平坦有限表示
$O$-概形，其泛纤维光滑、特殊纤维约化且仅有节点，并且
形式泛纤维为 Hausdorff 空间。}
