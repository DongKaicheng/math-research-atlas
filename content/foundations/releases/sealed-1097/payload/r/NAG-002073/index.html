Residual order on a model branch

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 687

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Residual order on a model branch}
{模型分支上的剩余阶数}]
\label{cand:residualbranchslope}
\bi{Let $\mathcal X$ be a flat finitely presented $O$-curve
with reduced nodal special fibre and smooth generic fibre. Assume
that its formal generic fibre $X$ is Hausdorff. Let $C$ be a
special-fibre component, $x=x_C$ its component point, and
$\nu:C^\nu\to C$ its normalization. Let $p\in C^\nu(k)$
lie over either a smooth point of $\mathcal X_s$, or a split
node admitting a pointed chart as in
Proposition~\ref{cand:pointednodalannulustube}. The pointed
disc or annulus chart determines an outgoing Gauss segment
$\gamma_p:[0,\epsilon)\to X$, with $\gamma_p(0)=x$ and
parameter $\tau=-\log|z|$, where $z$ is the coordinate
of the corresponding normalization branch and $|z(x)|=1$.}
{设 $\mathcal X$ 为平坦有限表示的 $O$-曲线，其特殊纤维
约化且只有节点，泛纤维光滑。假设其形式泛纤维 $X$ 为
Hausdorff 空间。设 $C$ 为特殊纤维的一个分量，$x=x_C$
为分量点，$\nu:C^\nu\to C$ 为正规化。设
$p\in C^\nu(k)$ 位于 $\mathcal X_s$ 的光滑点之上，或位于
具有 Proposition~\ref{cand:pointednodalannulustube} 所述
带点坐标片的分裂节点之上。带点圆盘或圆环坐标片确定出射
Gauss 线段 $\gamma_p:[0,\epsilon)\to X$，其中
$\gamma_p(0)=x$，参数为 $\tau=-\log|z|$；$z$ 是相应
正规化分支的坐标，并满足 $|z(x)|=1$。}

\bi{Let $f$ be an analytic germ at $x$ with $f(x)\ne0$.
Choose $c\in K^\times$ such that $|cf(x)|=1$, and let
$\widetilde f_x\in k(C)^\times$ be the residue of $cf(x)$
under Proposition~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield}.
For all sufficiently small $\tau\geq0$,}
{设 $f$ 为 $x$ 处的解析函数芽，且 $f(x)\ne0$。选择
$c\in K^\times$，使 $|cf(x)|=1$；通过
Proposition~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield}，将
$cf(x)$ 的剩余类记为 $\widetilde f_x\in k(C)^\times$。
则对所有充分小的 $\tau\geq0$，}
\[
 -\log|f(\gamma_p(\tau))|
   =-\log|f(x)|+\operatorname{ord}_p(\widetilde f_x)\,\tau.
\]
\bi{In particular the outgoing slope equals
$\operatorname{ord}_p(\widetilde f_x)$, independently of the
scalar $c$ and of the pointed chart.}
{特别地，出射斜率等于 $\operatorname{ord}_p(\widetilde f_x)$，
与标量 $c$ 及带点坐标片的选择无关。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{We first treat an actual pointed chart on a formal affine
neighborhood of the residual point. Its generic fibre is
$\mathcal M(A)$, with reduced integral model $A_0$. The Gauss
segment has endpoint $x$ by
Propositions~\ref{cand:nodaltubeendpoints} and
\ref{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure}. Choose $P,Q\in A$ with
$Q(x)\ne0$ such that the element $r(x)=P(x)/Q(x)$ of
$\mathcal H(x)$ satisfies}
{先考虑剩余点的形式仿射邻域上实际给定的带点坐标片。
其泛纤维为 $\mathcal M(A)$，约化整模型为 $A_0$。由
Propositions~\ref{cand:nodaltubeendpoints} 和
\ref{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure}，Gauss 线段的端点为
$x$。选择 $P,Q\in A$，其中 $Q(x)\ne0$，使
$\mathcal H(x)$ 中的元素 $r(x)=P(x)/Q(x)$ 满足}
\[
 |f(x)-r(x)|<|f(x)|.
\]
\bi{This is possible because the fraction field of
$A/\ker x$ is dense in $\mathcal H(x)$. The strict
inequality persists on a neighborhood of $x$ on which $f$ and
$P/Q$ are analytic. The ultrametric inequality then gives
$|f|=|r|$ there. Moreover $cf(x)$ and $cr(x)$ have the same
residue. It therefore suffices to prove the formula for $P/Q$.}
{这是因为 $A/\ker x$ 的分式域在 $\mathcal H(x)$ 中稠密。
该严格不等式在 $x$ 的某个邻域上仍成立，而 $f$ 和 $P/Q$
在那里都是解析的。由超度量不等式，该邻域上有
$|f|=|r|$。此外，$cf(x)$ 与 $cr(x)$ 具有相同剩余类。
故只需证明 $P/Q$ 的公式。}

\bi{Apply the separator construction in the proof of
Proposition~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield} to $P$ and
$Q$, using the same function $h$ and the same exponent $N$.
For suitable $c_P,c_Q\in K^\times$, the functions}
{将 Proposition~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield} 证明
中的分离函数构造同时应用于 $P,Q$，使用同一函数 $h$ 及
同一指数 $N$。对适当的 $c_P,c_Q\in K^\times$，函数}
\[
 P'=Ph^N/c_P,\qquad Q'=Qh^N/c_Q
\]
\bi{belong to $A_0$ and satisfy $|P'(x)|=|Q'(x)|=1$.
Their restrictions to $C$ are nonzero regular functions near the
selected residual point. Write $d_P,d_Q$ for their orders at
$p$ on $C^\nu$. By Lemmas~\ref{cand:boundedetaletubeexpansion}
and \ref{cand:boundarycoefficientorder}, near the endpoint of
the corresponding Gauss segment we have}
{属于 $A_0$，并满足 $|P'(x)|=|Q'(x)|=1$。它们在 $C$ 上
的限制，是所选剩余点附近的非零正则函数。记它们在
$C^\nu$ 的 $p$ 处阶数为 $d_P,d_Q$。由
Lemmas~\ref{cand:boundedetaletubeexpansion} 和
\ref{cand:boundarycoefficientorder}，在相应 Gauss 线段的
端点附近有}
\[
 |P'(\gamma_p(\tau))|=e^{-d_P\tau},\qquad
 |Q'(\gamma_p(\tau))|=e^{-d_Q\tau}.
\]
\bi{In the nodal case use the branch coordinate that has
absolute value one at $x$; at the other end this exchanges $s$
and $t=a/s$. Since
$P/Q=(c_P/c_Q)P'/Q'$, subtracting the two logarithms proves
the displayed formula. The quotient of the two reductions is
$\widetilde f_x$ up to a nonzero constant, so its order is
$d_P-d_Q$.}
{节点情形中，使用在 $x$ 处绝对值为一的分支坐标；在另一
端即交换 $s$ 与 $t=a/s$。因
$P/Q=(c_P/c_Q)P'/Q'$，将两式的对数相减即可得到公式。
两约化之商与 $\widetilde f_x$ 只相差非零常数，故其阶数
为 $d_P-d_Q$。}

\bi{For a pointed \textup{\'etale} diagram, pull the calculation back
to its source $\mathcal U$. The component point selected by
the chosen branch maps to $x$: its reduction is the corresponding
generic point, whose analytic inverse image is unique by
Proposition~\ref{cand:reducedmodelcomponentpoints}. Normalization
commutes with this \textup{\'etale} map by \cite[Tag 03GV]{StacksAlg}.
On the normalized curves the local map at the rational branch
point is therefore \textup{\'etale}. Its fibre is a finite separable residue
field by \cite[Tag 02GL]{StacksAlg}, so the upstairs maximal
ideal is generated by the downstairs maximal ideal. A uniformizer
downstairs is consequently a uniformizer upstairs. Thus the order
of the pulled-back residual function
is exactly $\operatorname{ord}_p(\widetilde f_x)$. The pointed
tube map is an analytic isomorphism, so the calculated norm is the
original norm along $\gamma_p$.}
{对带点 \textup{\'etale} 图，将计算拉回其源 $\mathcal U$。所选
分支的分量点映到 $x$：其约化为相应泛点，而由
Proposition~\ref{cand:reducedmodelcomponentpoints}，该泛点
只有一个解析逆像。由 \cite[Tag 03GV]{StacksAlg}，正规化
与此 \textup{\'etale} 映射相容。因此，在正规化曲线的有理分支点处，
相应局部映射仍是 \textup{\'etale}。由 \cite[Tag 02GL]{StacksAlg}，
其纤维为有限可分剩余域，故上方极大理想由下方极大理想的
像生成。因此下方的一致化参数在上方仍为一致化参数。
故拉回剩余函数的
阶数恰为 $\operatorname{ord}_p(\widetilde f_x)$。带点管域
映射为解析同构，因而所得范数正是沿 $\gamma_p$ 的原范数。}

\bi{Multiplying $c$ by a scalar of absolute value one multiplies
the residue by an element of $k^\times$, which has order zero.
The right hand side is intrinsic to the normalized residual point;
the same computation in any other pointed chart gives that same
number. The logarithmic parameter is the annular length parameter
of Proposition~\ref{cand:openannuluscoordinateisometry}.}
{将 $c$ 乘以绝对值为一的标量，只将剩余类乘以
$k^\times$ 中的元素，其阶数为零。右边仅取决于正规化
剩余点；在另一带点坐标片中进行同一计算，仍得到相同
数值。对数参数正是
Proposition~\ref{cand:openannuluscoordinateisometry} 中的
圆环长度参数。}
\end{proof}
