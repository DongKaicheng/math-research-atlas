A cofinal pointed overlap retains whole fibres

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1865

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[A cofinal pointed overlap retains whole fibres]
\label{mpw:finiteoverlap}
In Definition~\ref{mpw:chartclass}, for every ordinary neighbourhood
$U$ of $x$ in $Y$ there are $B_U$ and $H_U$ as in
Lemma~\ref{mpw:torusoverlap} such that
\[
                 p^{-1}(\mathbb T_s)\subset U
                        \qquad(s\in B_U\setminus H_U).
\]
This also holds for an ordinary affinoid neighbourhood inside $U$,
and for a common smaller ordinary affinoid neighbourhood inside
any pair of such neighbourhoods.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
First replace $U$ by an ordinary open neighbourhood of $x$ contained
in it. The compact set $C=Y\setminus U$ has compact image in the
Hausdorff space $\mathbb T_P$. This image misses $\eta_{s_0}$,
because its entire analytic fibre is $\{x\}$. Thus
$O=\mathbb T_P\setminus p(C)$ is an ordinary open neighbourhood of
$\eta_{s_0}$ with $p^{-1}(O)\subset U$.
Lemma~\ref{mpw:torusoverlap} gives the assertion.

The ordinary affinoid-neighbourhood basis used in
\Dep{v037:forms:germsheaf} supplies an affinoid neighbourhood in
$U$, or in the ordinary intersection of two given neighbourhoods.
Apply the same argument there. Compactness was used on the whole
source; no chosen sheet replaces $p^{-1}(O)$.
\end{proof}
