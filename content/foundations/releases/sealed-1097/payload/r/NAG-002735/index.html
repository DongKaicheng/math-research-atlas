Full degree in the common class

I Descent of the polarized mixed tower

Open in the English reader · PDF page 2129

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Full degree in the common class]
\label{f9tm:powerprojection}
Let $q:X'=(\mathbf P^1_K)^d\to X$ be given by $T_i=S_i^{n_i}$, where
$n_i\geq1$, and put $N=\prod_i n_i$. The map is finite locally free
of rank $N$. Its analytic map, with full inverse images and their
canonical finite norms, has the same rank. The pulled-back profiles
are $\phi_i(n_i s_i)$, where $s_i=-\log|S_i|$; they belong to the class
of Section~\ref{ex68:radial:sec:class} with degrees $n_i m_i$. For every
$F\in C(\overline{\mathbf R}^{,d})$,
\begin{equation}\label{ex68:radial:eq:power}
 \begin{split}
 I_K(q^*u_F\,q^*\theta)
 &=\int_{X'^\an}q^*u_F\,
                  d\mu_{{\rm GK},q^*h_1,\ldots,q^*h_d}\\
 &=N\int_{\mathbf R^d}F(t)W(t)\,dt.
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
The common absolute value is at most $ND\|F\|_\infty$.
Here $N$ includes inseparable degree, without a division by it.

For a line bundle $M$ on $X^\an$ with continuous metric $h_0$, give
$\Nm_q(q^*M)$ the projection-compatible metric from its canonical
isomorphism with $M^{\otimes N}$. If $h'_0=e^{-u_F}h_0$, the logarithmic
ratio of the two norm metrics is $Nu_F$. Its bounded pairing against
$\theta$ is precisely the value in \eqref{ex68:radial:eq:power}.
This assertion uses the specified pulled-back metric class only.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
On any product of the finite and infinite standard coordinate charts,
the algebraic map is a tensor product of maps $K[T_i]\to K[S_i]$,
$T_i\mapsto S_i^{n_i}$ (using inverse coordinates at infinity).
Division of exponents gives the free basis
$\prod_i S_i^{e_i}$, $0\leq e_i<n_i$. These charts cover the target
and their whole inverse images, proving finite local freeness of
rank $N$ in every characteristic.

The same assertion is analytic, including its norm. On a closed
polydisc, or on a closed polyannulus in these coordinates, expand a
convergent series upstairs and group its exponent vectors modulo
$(n_1,\ldots,n_d)$. This gives a unique sum
$\sum_{0\leq e_i<n_i}S^e a_e(T)$ of $N$ convergent downstairs series.
Convergence follows by grouping the weighted coefficient families;
shifting by one of the finitely many exponents $e$ changes the
coefficient norms by fixed positive factors only. Thus both this
map and its inverse are bounded. It is the canonical finite-module
topology of NAG-001701. Its spectra use the whole inverse domains;
for \eqref{ex68:radial:eq:collar} the inverse box is
$\prod_i[a_i'/n_i,b_i'/n_i]$. No point or branch is dropped.

For the pulled-back metrics, direct differentiation gives
\[
 q^*\theta_i=n_i^2\phi_i''(n_i s_i)\,d's_i\wedge d''s_i.
\]
Their affine-tail degrees are $n_i m_i$. Theorem~\ref{f9tm:radialcomparison}
applied upstairs gives, for smooth tests first,
\[
 \int_{\mathbf R^d}F(n_1s_1,\ldots,n_ds_d)
            \prod_i n_i^2\phi_i''(n_i s_i)\,ds.
\]
Substitute $t_i=n_i s_i$. The ordinary Jacobian removes one factor
$\prod_i n_i$ from $\prod_i n_i^2$, leaving exactly $N$.
The continuous-test assertion follows either from the same integral
or from the already proved uniform approximation and bound.
If some $n_i$ is divisible by the characteristic, this algebraic rank
and real substitution are unchanged. In particular $T=S^p$ has factor
$p$, although it is purely inseparable and has only one geometric
point over each point. Nilpotents in inseparable fibres are retained
by the free algebra calculation.

NAG-002539 identifies $\Nm_q(q^*M)$ with $M^{\otimes N}$.
In a local frame the chosen metric is the tensor-power metric, so
changing its downstairs weight by $u_F$ changes the norm weight by
$Nu_F$, exactly as in NAG-002554. Applying the bounded functional of
Theorem~\ref{f9tm:radialcomparison} gives $N I_K(u_F\theta)$, equal
to \eqref{ex68:radial:eq:power}. This uses no determinant section with a zero or
pole, and does not construct a norm metric for an arbitrary upstairs
metrized line.
\end{proof}
