Metric equality and compatible powered extensions

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1933

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Metric equality and compatible powered extensions]
\label{f9mi:metric}
Root model norms are unchanged by generic-identity pullback.
Consider two root model presentations
$(\cT,\cL,e,\alpha)$ and $(\cT',\cL',e',\alpha')$ of $L$,
with norms $h,h'$. Let $p:\cM\to\cT$ and $p':\cM\to\cT'$
be any common normal model, and let $n$ be a positive common multiple
of $e,e'$. Put
\[
 \cA=p^*\cL^{\otimes n/e},\qquad
 \cB=p'^*\cL'^{\otimes n/e'}.
\]
The generic identifications define
$\theta_K:\cB|_X\xrightarrow{\sim}\cA|_X$ and its rational
section $\tau$ of $\cA\otimes\cB^{-1}$. The following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $h=h'$ on $L$ at every analytic point of $X$;
\item $h=h'$ at the finitely many $\xi_E$ for the special components
      of $\cM$;
\item $\theta_K$ extends to a unique isomorphism
      $\theta:\cB\xrightarrow{\sim}\cA$ on $\cM$.
\end{enumerate}
In this case $\theta$ identifies the rational sections induced by
$s^{\otimes n}$ for every nonzero rational $s$ of $L$.
The conclusion is a compatible isomorphism of actual extensions
after pullback and tensor powers. It is not equality of prescribed
closed supports.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
For a generic-identity map of models, composition of the centre on
the source with that map is the centre on the target, by uniqueness
in \st{0BX5}. Pulling back the actual line gives the same lattice
in the fibre over $F_x$; hence the same norm. This also proves
pointwise compatibility with tensor powers and duals.

Clearly (1) implies (2). At a special component, a frame of
$\cA\otimes\cB^{-1}$ and the local rational equation of $\tau$ give
\[
 -\log\|\tau(\xi_E)\|
       =\lambda\,\frac{\ord_E(\tau)}{b_E}.
\]
The norm of $\tau$ is the ratio of the two $n$th-power norms.
Thus (2) implies $\ord_E(\tau)=0$ for every vertical prime divisor.
At a horizontal height-one point, $\tau$ already generates the line
on the generic fibre, so its order is zero there too.

The model is Noetherian by \st{01T6}. In a local frame at any point
$y$ of $\cM$, write $\tau=f$. Its local ring is a Noetherian normal
domain; all its height-one localizations are the relevant model
height-one DVRs. Both $f$ and $f^{-1}$ belong to all of them.
Their intersection is the local ring by \st{031T}(2). Hence $f$
is a unit. These local extensions agree on the dense generic fibre
and glue, proving (3) and uniqueness. This is the exact additional
normal-ring input; the codimension-one vanishing alone was not
silently treated as an isomorphism.

Finally an isomorphism in (3) identifies the pulled-back lattices at
every analytic point. The frame rule proves (1). Its generic
restriction is the specified identity on $L^{\otimes n}$, so it
also identifies the stated rational sections.
\end{proof}
