\begin{theorem}[\bi{Intrinsic residual curve and geometric metric}
{内禀剩余曲线与几何度量}]
\label{cand:intrinsiccharzeroresidualcurve}
\bi{Let $K$ be complete, nontrivially valued and of
characteristic zero. Suppose that $x$ is a component
vertex of a proper curve model satisfying
Corollary~\ref{cand:charzerosplitmodelskeleton}.
There is a canonical smooth proper residual curve $C_x$
over $k$ with function field $\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}$.
For every such model of the same analytic curve $X$
having $x$ as a component vertex, it identifies canonically
with the normalization of the component corresponding to $x$.}
{设 $K$ 完备、非平凡赋值且特征为零。假设 $x$ 是满足
Corollary~\ref{cand:charzerosplitmodelskeleton} 的某个
适当曲线模型的分量顶点。存在典范光滑适当剩余曲线
$C_x/k$，其函数域为 $\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}$。
对同一解析曲线 $X$ 的每个以 $x$ 为分量顶点的
这样的模型，它都典范地识别为与 $x$ 对应分量的正规化。}

\bi{The correspondence between the intrinsic outgoing
directions $T_xX$ and the closed points of $C_x$ is
independent of the model. So are the geometric radius
germ $R_p$, the Gauss-ray germ and its parameter
$\ell=-\log r$ in every direction. For every analytic
germ $f$ nonzero at $x$, the intrinsic geometric slopes
are}
{内禀出射方向 $T_xX$ 与 $C_x$ 闭点之间的对应与模型
无关。每个方向的几何半径芽 $R_p$、Gauss 射线芽
及其参数 $\ell=-\log r$ 也与模型无关。对每个在
$x$ 处非零的解析芽 $f$，内禀几何斜率为}
\[
 \sigma_p(f)=[\kappa(p):k]_{\mathrm{insep}}\,
            \operatorname{ord}_p(\widetilde{cf}_x),
 \qquad |cf(x)|=1 ,
\]
\bi{and satisfy the finite sum}
{且满足有限和恒等式}
\[
        \sum_{p\in C_x\ {\rm closed}}
                [\kappa(p):k]_{\mathrm{sep}}\,
                     \sigma_p(f)=0.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\bi{The component-residue identification
Proposition~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield} gives
the function field. The finite normalization of a
proper residual component is proper by
\cite[Tags 0BXR, 01WM, 01W3]{StacksAlg};
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothnodalnormalizationbasechange}
makes it smooth. The normal proper curve/function-field
equivalence \cite[Tag 0BY1]{StacksAlg} identifies these
curves uniquely by the identity of
$\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}$. Call the common curve $C_x$.}
{Proposition~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield} 的
分量剩余域识别给出所需函数域。由
\cite[Tags 0BXR, 01WM, 01W3]{StacksAlg}，适当
剩余分量的有限正规化仍适当；由
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothnodalnormalizationbasechange}，
它还光滑。正规适当曲线与函数域之间的等价
\cite[Tag 0BY1]{StacksAlg}，通过
$\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}$ 上的恒等映射唯一识别
这些曲线。将公共曲线记为 $C_x$。}

\bi{Use the inverse limit of connected components of
punctured ordinary open neighborhoods to define $T_xX$.
The cofinal branch stars in
Corollary~\ref{cand:charzerosplitmodelskeleton} identify
this set, for either model, with its residual points.
Fix one intrinsic direction, and let $p,p'$ be its two
labels on $C_x$. We first identify these labels without
presupposing equality of the two metric parameters.}
{用去点通常开邻域连通分支的逆极限定义 $T_xX$。
Corollary~\ref{cand:charzerosplitmodelskeleton} 中的
共尾分支星形邻域，将这个集合分别识别为两个模型的
剩余点集。固定一个内禀方向，以 $p,p'$ 表示它在
$C_x$ 上的两个标记。先识别这些标记，而不预设两个
度量参数已经相同。}

\bi{Every $\psi\in k(C_x)^\times$ lifts to a norm-one
analytic unit germ $g$ at $x$: the proof of
Proposition~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield}
represents it by an affinoid fraction $P/Q$ with
$P(x)Q(x)\ne0$, and Proposition~\ref{fnd:localunit} makes
$P,Q$ invertible near $x$. By
Lemma~\ref{cand:geometriccollarnormprofile}, the sign
of $-\log|g|$ on a sufficiently thin collar in the first
model is the sign of $\operatorname{ord}_p(\psi)$.
Cofinality of the two branch-star systems puts every
sufficiently thin collar in the second model inside
that collar. Its Gauss ray therefore has the same
sign, and its slope formula gives}
{每个 $\psi\in k(C_x)^\times$ 都可提升为 $x$ 处范数
为一的解析单位芽 $g$：
Proposition~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield} 的
证明将它表示为满足 $P(x)Q(x)\ne0$ 的 affinoid
分式 $P/Q$，再由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:localunit} 使 $P,Q$ 在 $x$ 附近
可逆。由 Lemma~\ref{cand:geometriccollarnormprofile}，
在第一个模型的充分薄领形域上，$-\log|g|$ 的符号就是
$\operatorname{ord}_p(\psi)$ 的符号。两组分支星形
邻域的共尾性，将第二个模型中每个充分薄领形域都放入
这个领形域。因此其 Gauss 射线具有相同符号，再由
其斜率公式得到}
\[
 \operatorname{sgn}\operatorname{ord}_p(\psi)
      =\operatorname{sgn}\operatorname{ord}_{p'}(\psi)
                 \qquad(\psi\in k(C_x)^\times).
\]
\bi{Thus the two valuation rings in the common function
field are identical. Their centres on the separated
proper curve $C_x$ are equal, by the closed-diagonal
argument in Theorem~\ref{cand:intrinsicresidualcurve}.
Consequently $p=p'$, and their residue fields, separable
degrees and inseparable degrees coincide.}
{因此，公共函数域中的两个赋值环相同。由
Theorem~\ref{cand:intrinsicresidualcurve} 中的闭对角线
论证，它们在分离适当曲线 $C_x$ 上的中心相同。
故 $p=p'$，且其剩余域、可分次数与不可分次数均相同。}

\bi{Choose now a uniformizer
$\psi\in k(C_x)^\times$ at this common point $p$, and
lift it to a norm-one unit germ $g$. Write
$e=[\kappa(p):k]_{\mathrm{insep}}$.
The all-point collar formula gives, on the intersection
of sufficiently thin collars,}
{现在选择这个公共点 $p$ 处的一致化元
$\psi\in k(C_x)^\times$，并提升为范数为一的单位芽
$g$。写 $e=[\kappa(p):k]_{\mathrm{insep}}$。
对所有点成立的领形域公式在充分薄领形域之交上给出}
\[
                       |g|=R_p^e=(R'_p)^e.
\]
\bi{Hence $R_p=R'_p$ there. By alternating the two
cofinal stars one can choose a collar of each contained
in a fixed collar of the other. After increasing both
inner radii beyond these bounds, the equality of radius
functions gives equality of every thinner collar as a
subset of $X$. Their analytic structures agree by the
analytic-domain universal property. Thus the entire
radius germ is intrinsic.}
{故其上 $R_p=R'_p$。交替使用两组共尾星形邻域，
可分别选择一个领形域，使其包含于另一组的某个固定
领形域。将两侧内半径提高到这些界之上，半径函数的
相等便给出每个更薄领形域作为 $X$ 子集的相等。由
解析域的泛性质，它们的解析结构也相同。因此整个
半径芽是内禀的。}

\bi{Finally choose a common finite Galois extension
rationalizing the residual-point supports in both
models. The inverse image of an agreed thin collar is,
in either presentation, a finite disjoint union of
standard open annuli. These annuli are path connected,
so their decomposition is the intrinsic connected
component decomposition. Corresponding components are
therefore the same analytic domains. On each, the two
annular coordinates have the same radius function, namely
the pullback of $R_p$. Their identity is a
radius-preserving annular isomorphism, and
Corollary~\ref{cand:openannuluscoordinateisometry}
identifies the Gauss points of every radius. Projection
gives the same Gauss-ray germ on $X$, with the same
parameter $-\log r$.}
{最后，选择一个共同有限 Galois 扩张，使两个模型的
剩余点支撑均有理化。已识别的薄领形域之逆像，在任一
表示下都是有限个标准开圆环的不交并。这些圆环道路
连通，故该分解就是内禀的连通分支分解。因此，对应
分支是同一个解析域。在每个分支上，两个圆环坐标具有
相同半径函数，即 $R_p$ 的拉回。其恒等映射为保持
半径的圆环同构，而
Corollary~\ref{cand:openannuluscoordinateisometry}
将每个半径的 Gauss 点识别起来。投影便在 $X$ 上
给出同一个 Gauss 射线芽及同一个参数 $-\log r$。}

\bi{The slope formula is
Theorem~\ref{cand:charzeroresidualslope}, now with all
its geometric data intrinsic. The finite-support
weighted identity is
Corollary~\ref{cand:charzeroweightedslopebalance}.
All constructions use the given absolute value.}
{斜率公式来自 Theorem~\ref{cand:charzeroresidualslope}，
其中全部几何数据现在均已成为内禀对象。有限支撑加权
恒等式来自 Corollary~\ref{cand:charzeroweightedslopebalance}。
全部构造均使用给定绝对值。}
\end{proof}
