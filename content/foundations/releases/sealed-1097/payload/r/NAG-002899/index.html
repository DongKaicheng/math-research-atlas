\begin{theorem}[Conditional finite-cover scalar identity; F8AG-P]
\label{thm:application}
Let \(X\) be good, Hausdorff, and pure of dimension \(n\), let \(f\)
be regular analytic near a compact set \(K\subset\Int(X/K)\), and
let \(\eta\) be an ordinary \((n-1,n-1)\)-test supported in \(K\).
Suppose the actual zero space \(D\) is empty or pure of dimension
\(n-1\) near \(K\). Assume the following separately identified inputs:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=*]
\item fixed-test support avoidance and the scalar logarithmic pairing
of the unchanged \tagg{F8RC-L} candidate;
\item on the finite affinoid intersections used below, the actual
calibrated product assertion \tagg{F8RC-P}, with \(P_I=\TT_{g_i}(\cO_{D_I})\);
\item R-F7 for the complementary test away from \(D\).
\end{enumerate}
Then the proposed primary identity is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:PL}
               L_f(\dd\eta)=\int_D\eta,
\end{equation}
with the full actual quotient integration. The annular Green and
finite-cover cancellation used in this implication are proved above;
they are no longer additional black-box premises of the implication.
Inputs (i)--(ii) remain under independent audit, and this theorem does
not mark them, or general PL, as admitted. To invoke (ii) by the
submitted rigid-zero theorem, its affinoid-domain \(S_1\) hypothesis
must also hold; the separately reviewed \tagg{F8ST-A} supplies it
from intrinsic analytic \(S_1\) in the exact good-space scope. A
regular nonunit is not used to infer \(S_1\).
\end{theorem}
