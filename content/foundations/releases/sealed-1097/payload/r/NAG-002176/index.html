Restriction to formal open subsets

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 901

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Restriction to formal open subsets}
{限制到形式开子集}]
\label{cand:semistableformalopenrestriction}
\bi{Let $\mathfrak U=\operatorname{Spf}(B_0)$ admit a
topologically finitely presented formal \etale map to a
standard semistable formal chart. Its homotopy preserves
the generic fibre of every formal open subset. On an
admissible affine formal open $\mathfrak V\subseteq\mathfrak U$,
its restriction equals the homotopy obtained from the
restricted chart, independently of its bounded
presentation.}
{设 $\mathfrak U=\operatorname{Spf}(B_0)$ 具有到标准
半稳定形式坐标片的拓扑有限表示形式 \etale 映射。
其同伦保持每个形式开子集的泛纤维。在 admissible 仿射形式开集
$\mathfrak V\subseteq\mathfrak U$ 上，其限制等于由
限制后的坐标片得到的同伦，并且与有界表示无关。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose a bounded presentation by
Lemma~\ref{cand:formaletaleboundedpresentation}.
A principal formal open $D(g)$, $g\in B_0$, has generic
fibre $|g|=1$ by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalprincipalopenflat}.
The integral function $g$ has values at most one.
The constant coefficient of its displacement gives
$|g(H_B(x,t))|\geq|g(x)|$, so a point where $|g|=1$
stays in that domain at every time. General formal opens
are unions of principal formal opens, and their generic
fibres are the corresponding unions by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}; hence
they are preserved as well.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{cand:formaletaleboundedpresentation}
选择有界表示。主形式开集 $D(g)$（$g\in B_0$）的
泛纤维由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalprincipalopenflat}
给为 $|g|=1$。整函数 $g$ 处处取值至多为一，而
其位移的常数系数给出
$|g(H_B(x,t))|\geq|g(x)|$，所以 $|g|=1$ 的点在
每个时刻都留在该域。一般形式开集是主形式开集之
并，由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}，
其泛纤维为相应的并，故也被保持。}

\bi{Write $\mathfrak V=\operatorname{Spf}(C_0)$.
An open immersion is formally \etale: a square-zero
thickening does not change underlying points, so a map
whose reduction factors through that open factors through
it uniquely. Composing unique lifting properties shows
that the restricted map to the standard base is formally
\etale. We also verify relative finite presentation.
By Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation}, choose
finite presentations $B_0=O\langle W\rangle/I$ and
$C_0=O\langle Z\rangle/J$. Lift the images of the finitely
many $W_i$ to integral restricted series $h_i(Z)$.
The two substitution maps give the presentation}
{写 $\mathfrak V=\operatorname{Spf}(C_0)$。开浸入为
形式 \etale：平方零增厚不改变底层点，因此一个映射
若在约化后经过该开集因子分解，则它自身唯一经过该
开集因子分解。复合唯一提升性质可知，到标准基底的
限制映射为形式 \etale。还须验证相对有限表示。
由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation}，
选取有限表示 $B_0=O\langle W\rangle/I$ 及
$C_0=O\langle Z\rangle/J$。把有限个 $W_i$ 的像
提升为整受限级数 $h_i(Z)$。两个代换映射给出表示}
\[
 C_0\simeq B_0\langle Z\rangle/
                  (J(Z),\,W_i-h_i(Z):i).
\]
\bi{The relations are finite; the same construction applies
directly to the map from the standard base to $C_0$.
Thus Lemma~\ref{cand:formaletaleboundedpresentation}
applies over that original base. The integral map
$B_0\to C_0$ gives a contractive map on the displayed
generic algebras: each integral presentation coordinate
of $B$ maps into $C_0$, whose elements have norm at most
one by Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations};
bounded substitution and quotient infima give
contractivity. Thus
Proposition~\ref{cand:etaledisplacementfunctoriality} and
Theorem~\ref{cand:etaleendpointjointcontinuity} identify
the restricted homotopy with that on $\mathfrak V_\eta$.
Theorem~\ref{cand:semistablechartindependence} removes
the chosen presentation.}
{这些关系有限；同样的构造也直接适用于从标准基底
到 $C_0$ 的映射。因此
Lemma~\ref{cand:formaletaleboundedpresentation} 可在
原基底上应用。整映射 $B_0\to C_0$
在所列泛纤维代数上给出压缩映射：$B$ 的每个整
表示坐标都映入 $C_0$，由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations}，
$C_0$ 的元素范数至多为一；有界代换及商下确界
于是给出压缩性。因此
Proposition~\ref{cand:etaledisplacementfunctoriality}
及 Theorem~\ref{cand:etaleendpointjointcontinuity}
将限制同伦与 $\mathfrak V_\eta$ 上的同伦识别。
Theorem~\ref{cand:semistablechartindependence} 再消除
表示的选择。}
\end{proof}

\bi{Throughout this section $K$ is complete and nontrivially
valued, $O=K^\circ$, and $\pi\in O$ satisfies
$0<|\pi|<1$. Formal algebras carry their $\pi$-adic
topology. A formally \etale map below has the unique
lifting property for square-zero extensions of discrete
algebras annihilated by a power of $\pi$.}
{本节中 $K$ 完备且非平凡赋值，$O=K^\circ$，并取
$\pi\in O$ 满足 $0<|\pi|<1$。形式代数配备
$\pi$-进拓扑。下文的形式 \etale 映射具有以下唯一
提升性质：对于被 $\pi$ 的某个幂消灭的离散代数，
可沿平方零扩张唯一提升。}
