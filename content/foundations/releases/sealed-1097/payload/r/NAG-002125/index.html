The punctured-disc homotopy

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 717

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The punctured-disc homotopy}
{穿孔圆盘同伦}]
\label{cand:punctureddischomotopy}
\bi{Let $D$ be an open unit disc, let $P\in D(K)$, and
adjoin its outer endpoint $v$ as in
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure}. Choose a disc
coordinate $T$ with $T(P)=0$. On
$D_P^*=(D\setminus\{P\})\cup\{v\}$ there is a canonical
jointly continuous strong deformation retraction onto}
{设 $D$ 为开单位圆盘，$P\in D(K)$，并如
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure}
添入其外端点 $v$。选择满足 $T(P)=0$ 的圆盘坐标
$T$。在 $D_P^*=(D\setminus\{P\})\cup\{v\}$ 上，
存在到以下集合的典范联合连续强形变收缩：}
\[
 S_P=\{v\}\cup\{\zeta_{0,r}:0<r<1\}.
\]
\bi{The parameter $\ell=-\log r$, with $\ell(v)=0$,
identifies $S_P$ with the metric ray $[0,\infty)$.
The homotopy preserves $|T|$, is independent of the
coordinate centered at $P$, and commutes with complete
isometric scalar extension and semilinear disc
isomorphisms carrying the marked point to the marked point.}
{参数 $\ell=-\log r$，并令 $\ell(v)=0$，
将 $S_P$ 识别为度量射线 $[0,\infty)$。
同伦保持 $|T|$，与以 $P$ 为中心的坐标选择无关，
并与完备等距标量扩张及将标记点映到标记点的半线性
圆盘同构相容。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Using Theorem~\ref{cand:opendisccanonicalhomotopy},
define}
{使用 Theorem~\ref{cand:opendisccanonicalhomotopy}，
定义}
\[
 H_P(x,u)=H_D\bigl(x,u|T(x)|\bigr)
       \quad(x\ne v),\qquad H_P(v,u)=v.
\]
\bi{The parameter on the right is less than one.
The weighted Gauss formula gives
$|T(H_P(x,u))|=|T(x)|>0$. On $D\setminus\{P\}$ joint
continuity is composition of continuous maps. At $v$
the entire outer tail $|T|>r$ is preserved for every
$u$, so the full-tail neighborhood basis gives joint
continuity there, uniformly in $u$. At $u=1$ the
radius-$|T(x)|$ Gauss norm is unchanged by translation
by $T(x)$, giving
$H_P(x,1)=\zeta_{0,|T(x)|}$. The same formula fixes
every centered Gauss point for every $u$, as in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:annulusretraction}. It starts at
$x$, and hence is the asserted strong retraction.}
{右侧参数严格小于一。加权 Gauss 公式给出
$|T(H_P(x,u))|=|T(x)|>0$。
在 $D\setminus\{P\}$ 上，联合连续性来自连续映射
的复合。在 $v$ 处，对每个 $u$，完整外尾部
$|T|>r$ 都保持不变，因此完整尾部邻域基给出此处
关于 $u$ 一致的联合连续性。$u=1$ 时，按 $T(x)$
平移不改变半径 $|T(x)|$ 的 Gauss 范数，故
$H_P(x,1)=\zeta_{0,|T(x)|}$。
如 Proposition~\ref{fnd:annulusretraction}，
同一公式在每个 $u$ 固定每个中心 Gauss 点。
它从 $x$ 出发，因而是所述强收缩。}

\bi{A centered change of coordinate has the form
$g(T)=a_1T+a_2T^2+\cdots$ with $|a_1|=1$ and
$|a_n|\leq1$. Thus $|g(x)|=|T(x)|$ throughout the
punctured disc. The full coordinate invariance of
$H_D$ now proves that $H_P$ is coordinate independent,
not just its value at $u=1$. The same argument proves
semilinear compatibility and scalar-extension
compatibility. Finally the map
$\ell\mapsto\zeta_{0,\exp(-\ell)}$ is continuous for
$\ell>0$ and extends to $v$ at zero by the tube endpoint
theorem. Its inverse is the continuous function
$-\log|T|$, extended by zero at $v$. This proves the
topological and metric ray identification.}
{以标记点为中心的坐标变换形如
$g(T)=a_1T+a_2T^2+\cdots$，其中 $|a_1|=1$、
$|a_n|\leq1$，因此在整个穿孔圆盘上
$|g(x)|=|T(x)|$。由 $H_D$ 的整个同伦的坐标
不变性，$H_P$ 与坐标无关，而不只是它在 $u=1$
的取值无关。同一论证给出半线性相容性与标量扩张
相容性。最后，映射
$\ell\mapsto\zeta_{0,\exp(-\ell)}$ 在 $\ell>0$
时连续，并由管域端点定理在零处延伸为 $v$。
其逆为连续函数 $-\log|T|$，在 $v$ 处延拓为零。
这证明拓扑与度量射线识别。}
\end{proof}
