3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 493

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}\label{fnd:powerboundspectral}
\bi{For an affinoid algebra one has}
{对 affinoid algebra，有}
\[
A^\circ=\{a:\rho_A(a)\leq1\},\qquad
A^{\circ\circ}=\{a:\rho_A(a)<1\}.
\]
\bi{The first is a subring, the second an ideal in it, and $\widetilde A=A^\circ/A^{\circ\circ}$ is reduced.}
{第一项为 subring，第二项为其 ideal，且 $\widetilde A=A^\circ/A^{\circ\circ}$ 为 reduced。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{If $\rho_A(a)<1$, Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedspectralradiusformula} implies $\|a^m\|\to0$. Conversely, if the powers tend to zero, some power has norm less than one and hence $\rho_A(a)<1$. A power-bounded element has spectral radius at most one. For the reverse inclusion use Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointwisepowerbound}, which gives $\|a^m\|\leq C_a\rho_A(a)^m$ when $\rho_A(a)>0$. If $\rho_A(a)=0$, Proposition~\ref{fnd:spectralnilradicalinternal} makes $a$ nilpotent. The ring and ideal assertions follow from the spectral seminorm inequalities. If $\widetilde a^m=0$, then $\rho_A(a)^m<1$, hence $\rho_A(a)<1$ and $\widetilde a=0$.}
{若 $\rho_A(a)<1$，Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedspectralradiusformula} 推出 $\|a^m\|\to0$。反之，若各次幂趋于零，则某次幂的范数小于一，从而 $\rho_A(a)<1$。幂有界元素的谱半径至多为一。反向包含使用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointwisepowerbound}：当 $\rho_A(a)>0$ 时，它给出 $\|a^m\|\leq C_a\rho_A(a)^m$；当 $\rho_A(a)=0$ 时，Proposition~\ref{fnd:spectralnilradicalinternal} 说明 $a$ 幂零。环与理想的断言由谱半范数不等式得到。若 $\widetilde a^m=0$，则 $\rho_A(a)^m<1$，故 $\rho_A(a)<1$，从而 $\widetilde a=0$。}
\end{proof}
