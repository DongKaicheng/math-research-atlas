Stability under a small change of a rational tuple

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 210

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Stability under a small change of a rational tuple}{Rational tuple 在小扰动下的稳定性}]\label{fnd:rationaltuplestability}
\bi{Let $E$ be a nonzero strict norm-admissible affinoid algebra. Fix $a_0,\ldots,a_t,b_0,\ldots,b_t\in E$ with}
{设 $E$ 为非零 strict norm-admissible affinoid 代数。固定 $a_0,\ldots,a_t,b_0,\ldots,b_t\in E$，满足}
\[
 \sum_{j=0}^t b_ja_j=1,\qquad
 H=\max(1,\|b_0\|,\ldots,\|b_t\|).
\]
\bi{Suppose $a'_0,\ldots,a'_t\in E$ and put}
{设 $a'_0,\ldots,a'_t\in E$，并记}
\[
 \delta=\max_{0\leq j\leq t}\|a'_j-a_j\|<H^{-1}.
\]
\bi{Then $(a'_0,\ldots,a'_t)=E$, with a unit-ideal certificate whose coefficient norms are at most $H$. The two rational subsets defined by the denominators $a_0$ and $a'_0$ are equal. Their actual presentation algebras}
{则 $(a'_0,\ldots,a'_t)=E$，并且存在系数范数均不超过 $H$ 的单位理想证书。分别以 $a_0$ 与 $a'_0$ 为分母定义的两个 rational 子集相同。它们的实际表示代数}
\[
 Q=E\{S_1,\ldots,S_t\}/(a_0S_j-a_j)_j,
 \qquad
 Q'=E\{S'_1,\ldots,S'_t\}/(a'_0S'_j-a'_j)_j
\]
\bi{have a unique bounded $E$-algebra isomorphism, and this isomorphism is isometric for the displayed quotient norms. For a finite $E$-module with a fixed finite free presentation, the induced comparison of its two relation-quotient modules is also isometric. These assertions include zero presentation algebras.}
{之间存在唯一的有界 $E$-代数同构，并且它对所列商范数等距。对配备固定有限自由表示的有限 $E$-模，所得两个关系商模之间的比较也为等距。结论允许表示代数为零。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Write $\epsilon_j=a'_j-a_j$ and $u=\sum_jb_ja'_j=1+\sum_jb_j\epsilon_j$. Since $\|u-1\|\leq H\delta<1$, Lemma~\ref{fnd:geometricinverse} gives $u^{-1}$ with $\|u^{-1}\|\leq1$. Thus $b'_j=u^{-1}b_j$ satisfies $\sum_jb'_ja'_j=1$ and $\|b'_j\|\leq H$. All the ideals and quotients appearing here are actual closed ideals and complete quotients by Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictweightedquotient}.}
{写 $\epsilon_j=a'_j-a_j$，并令 $u=\sum_jb_ja'_j=1+\sum_jb_j\epsilon_j$。由于 $\|u-1\|\leq H\delta<1$，由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:geometricinverse} 得到 $u^{-1}$，且 $\|u^{-1}\|\leq1$。因此 $b'_j=u^{-1}b_j$ 满足 $\sum_jb'_ja'_j=1$ 及 $\|b'_j\|\leq H$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictweightedquotient}，这里所有理想均为实际闭理想，商均为完备商。}

\bi{In $Q$, put $s_j=\overline S_j$. The certificate gives $a_0^{-1}=b_0+\sum_{j\geq1}b_js_j$, hence $\|a_0^{-1}\|\leq H$. Consequently $a'_0=a_0(1+\epsilon_0a_0^{-1})$ is a unit and}
{在 $Q$ 中记 $s_j=\overline S_j$。单位理想证书给出 $a_0^{-1}=b_0+\sum_{j\geq1}b_js_j$，故 $\|a_0^{-1}\|\leq H$。因此 $a'_0=a_0(1+\epsilon_0a_0^{-1})$ 为单位，并且}
\[
 \frac{a'_j}{a'_0}
   =\bigl(s_j+\epsilon_ja_0^{-1}\bigr)
      \bigl(1+\epsilon_0a_0^{-1}\bigr)^{-1},
 \qquad \left\|\frac{a'_j}{a'_0}\right\|\leq1.
\]
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictquotientboundedup} therefore gives a contractive map $Q'\to Q$ sending $S'_j$ to the displayed ratio. In $Q'$, use the certificate $(b'_j)$ and the differences $-\epsilon_j$. The same calculation gives a contractive map $Q\to Q'$. The composites fix $E$, and each variable is forced to be the corresponding numerator divided by its unit denominator. Thus the composites fix the variables and, by polynomial density, are identities. Two inverse contractions are isometric. The same argument shows uniqueness among bounded $E$-algebra maps. It also applies when a quotient is zero: unit equations are interpreted with $1=0$, and the two constructed maps force both quotients to be zero together.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictquotientboundedup}，得到将 $S'_j$ 送到所列比值的 contractive 映射 $Q'\to Q$。在 $Q'$ 中使用证书 $(b'_j)$ 及差值 $-\epsilon_j$，同一计算给出 contractive 映射 $Q\to Q'$。两个复合固定 $E$，而每个变量都被关系强制为相应分子除以单位分母。因此复合固定全部变量，再由多项式稠密性可知它们为恒等映射。互逆的 contractions 给出等距同构。同一论证给出有界 $E$-代数映射的唯一性。若某个商为零，单位等式按 $1=0$ 理解；所构造的两个映射迫使两个商同时为零。}

\bi{For completeness, equality of the point sets can also be checked without an algebra comparison. At $x\in\mathcal M(E)$ put $m(x)=\max_j|a_j(x)|$. The certificate and Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive} give $m(x)\geq H^{-1}>\delta$. A coordinate attaining this maximum retains its absolute value after perturbation, and every other perturbed coordinate has value at most $m(x)$. If $|a_0(x)|=m(x)$, the same holds for $a'_0$; if $|a_0(x)|<m(x)$, then $|a'_0(x)|<m(x)$. This is exactly the equivalence of the two rational conditions. Finally choose $E^v\xrightarrow{F}E^w\to N\to0$; such a finite presentation exists by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}. The two induced modules are $Q^w/F_QQ^v$ and $(Q')^w/F_{Q'}(Q')^v$ by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule}. The coordinatewise isometry carries one actual relation image to the other, so it induces an isometry of their quotient norms.}
{也可以不借助代数比较而直接核对点集。对 $x\in\mathcal M(E)$，令 $m(x)=\max_j|a_j(x)|$。由单位理想证书及 Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}，有 $m(x)\geq H^{-1}>\delta$。达到此最大值的坐标在扰动后保持绝对值，其他扰动后的坐标均不超过 $m(x)$。若 $|a_0(x)|=m(x)$，则 $a'_0$ 也达到该值；若 $|a_0(x)|<m(x)$，则 $|a'_0(x)|<m(x)$。这正是两个 rational 条件的等价性。最后选择 $E^v\xrightarrow{F}E^w\to N\to0$；由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}，这样的有限表示存在。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule}，两个诱导模分别为 $Q^w/F_QQ^v$ 与 $(Q')^w/F_{Q'}(Q')^v$。逐坐标等距同构将一个实际关系像送到另一个，因而在商范数上也诱导等距同构。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Throughout, $K$ is a complete real-valued non-Archimedean field; trivial valuation is allowed. Nonzero affinoid algebras are strict and norm-admissible, with submultiplicative norm and unit of norm one. Quotients use their actual quotient norms. The zero algebra and its zero modules have the separate zero-norm interpretation of Section~\ref{fnd:r1quotientbridge}. A rational presentation has a unit-ideal tuple. We use unit radii in the lemmas; radii in $|K^\times|$ are reduced to this case by scaling variables.}
{全文固定完备实值非阿基米德域 $K$，允许平凡赋值。非零 affinoid 代数均为 strict 且 norm-admissible，范数次乘，单位元范数为一。商配备实际商范数。零代数及其零模按 Section~\ref{fnd:r1quotientbridge} 的约定单独使用零范数。Rational 表示的定义 tuple 必须生成单位理想。各引理使用单位半径；属于 $|K^\times|$ 的半径通过缩放变量化为这一情形。}
