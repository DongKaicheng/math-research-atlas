Lifting finitely generated residue fields

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 530

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Lifting finitely generated residue fields}{有限生成剩余域的提升}]
\label{fnd:residuefieldrealization}
\bi{Let $K$ be complete and let $l/\widetilde K$ be a finitely
generated field extension. There is a complete valued extension
$L/K$ with a $\widetilde K$-embedding $l\hookrightarrow\widetilde L$.}
{设 $K$ 完备，$l/\widetilde K$ 为有限生成域扩张。存在完备赋值扩张
$L/K$ 及 $\widetilde K$-嵌入 $l\hookrightarrow\widetilde L$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose a transcendence basis $t_1,\ldots,t_e$ such that
$l/\widetilde K(t_1,\ldots,t_e)$ is finite.
The completion of $\operatorname{Frac}(K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_e\rangle)$
for the Gauss norm has residue field $\widetilde K(t_1,\ldots,t_e)$
by Lemma~\ref{fnd:gaussfibreresiduefields}.
Express the remaining finite extension as a tower of simple
extensions. At one step, lift the coefficients of the monic
irreducible residue polynomial to the valuation ring of the
current complete field. A root in a finite extension has absolute
value at most one, by Lemma~\ref{fnd:monicmaximumroot}.
Its residue satisfies the chosen irreducible polynomial, so the
resulting residue field contains the required simple extension.
The extending absolute value and completeness are supplied by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}. Repeating the finitely many
steps proves the assertion, including inseparable residue extensions.}
{选取超越基 $t_1,\ldots,t_e$，使
$l/\widetilde K(t_1,\ldots,t_e)$ 有限。
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gaussfibreresiduefields}，
$\operatorname{Frac}(K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_e\rangle)$ 关于
Gauss 范数的完备化具有剩余域 $\widetilde K(t_1,\ldots,t_e)$。
把余下有限扩张写成单扩张塔。在每一步，把首一不可约剩余多项式
的系数提升到当前完备域的赋值环。
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:monicmaximumroot}，它在某个有限扩张中的根
具有绝对值不超过一；其剩余类满足所选不可约多项式，故新的剩余域
包含所需的单扩张。延拓绝对值和完备性由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 给出。重复有限步即得结论，
这也包括不可分剩余域扩张。}
\end{proof}
