Exact scalar extension on finite modules

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Exact scalar extension on finite modules}{Finite modules 上的 exact scalar extension}]\label{fnd:weightedfiniteexact}
\bi{With $A,B$ as above, let $0\to M'\to M\to M''\to0$ be an exact sequence of finite $A$-modules with canonical norms. Then both its ordinary tensor sequence and its separated completed tensor sequence with $B$ are exact. Under their coefficient identifications they are norm-strict. If the original injection has inverse bound $D$ on its image and the original surjection has lifting bound $C$, these same constants work for the coefficient sequence.}
{设 $A,B$ 如上，$0\to M'\to M\to M''\to0$ 为配备 canonical norms 的 finite $A$-modules 的 exact sequence。则其与 $B$ 的 ordinary tensor sequence 和 separated completed tensor sequence 均 exact。在 coefficient identifications 下，它们均 norm-strict。若原 injection 对其 image 具有 inverse bound $D$，原 surjection 具有 lifting bound $C$，则同样的常数适用于 coefficient sequence。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The original maps are bounded and norm-strict by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}. Proposition~\ref{fnd:weightedc0exact} gives exactness and the stated constants for coefficient families. Apply the natural identifications of Theorem~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficienttensor}. For quotient norms these are isometric. For other canonical norms they are uniformly bounded isomorphisms by the preceding comparison, which preserves norm-strictness; the stated constants continue to refer to the coefficient norms themselves.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}，原 maps bounded 且 norm-strict。Proposition~\ref{fnd:weightedc0exact} 给出 coefficient families 的 exactness 及所列常数。再应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficienttensor} 的 natural identifications。对 quotient norms，这些 identifications 为 isometric；对其他 canonical norms，由前面的 comparison，它们为 uniformly bounded isomorphisms，故保持 norm-strictness。所列常数仍指 coefficient norms 本身的常数。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Throughout, $K$ is complete for its fixed real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value, which may be trivial. A Banach $K$-space has a definite, complete, non-Archimedean, $K$-homogeneous norm. Banach algebras are commutative and unital, with a submultiplicative norm and $\|1\|=1$; Banach module actions satisfy $\|am\|\leq\|a\|\|m\|$. All finite powers have the maximum norm; the maximum over an empty family is zero. Norm-strictness has the quantitative meaning of Definition~\ref{fnd:normstrictconvention}.}
{本节中，$K$ 关于固定的 real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value 完备，允许该 absolute value 平凡。Banach $K$-space 的 norm 为 definite、complete、non-Archimedean 且 $K$-homogeneous。Banach algebras 均 commutative、unital，其 norm submultiplicative 且 $\|1\|=1$；Banach module actions 满足 $\|am\|\leq\|a\|\|m\|$。所有 finite powers 使用 maximum norm，并约定空 family 的 maximum 为零。Norm-strictness 采用 Definition~\ref{fnd:normstrictconvention} 的定量含义。}
