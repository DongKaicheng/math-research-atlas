Surjectivity after an arbitrary complete extension

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 277

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Surjectivity after an arbitrary complete extension}{任意完备扩域后的谱满射性}]
\label{fnd:scalarextensionspectralsurjective}
\bi{For every generalized $K$-affinoid $A$ and every complete valued extension $L/K$, the natural map}
{对每个广义 $K$-affinoid $A$ 及每个完备赋值扩域 $L/K$，自然映射}
\[
 \pi:\mathcal M_L(A_L)\longrightarrow\mathcal M_K(A)
\]
\bi{is a continuous closed surjection.}
{为连续闭满射。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Continuity follows from the evaluation topology, and closedness follows from compactness of the source and the Hausdorff property of the target, Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact}. Fix $x\in\mathcal M(A)$. Consider all the closed rational domains}
{连续性来自取值拓扑；由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact}，源空间紧而目标 Hausdorff，故映射闭。固定 $x\in\mathcal M(A)$。考虑以下全部闭 rational 域：}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 U^+(a,r)&=\{v:|a(v)|\leq r\}&&\quad(r>|a(x)|),\\
 U^-(a,r)&=\{v:|a(v)|\geq r\}&&\quad(0<r<|a(x)|).
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{Every finite intersection $U$ of these domains is rational by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedjointpresentations} and contains $x$. Its algebra $A_U$ is nonzero. Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedscalartensor} gives an isometric embedding $A_U\hookrightarrow(A_U)_L$, so the latter is nonzero. It is $L$-affinoid by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalscalarpresentation}, and hence has a point by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion}. By Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalcompletebasechange}, this supplies a point of $\pi^{-1}(U)$. Thus the inverse images of the displayed domains have the finite intersection property in the compact space $\mathcal M_L(A_L)$.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedjointpresentations}，这些域的每个有限交 $U$ 仍为 rational，且含有 $x$，所以其代数 $A_U$ 非零。Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedscalartensor} 给出等距嵌入 $A_U\hookrightarrow(A_U)_L$，因此后者非零。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalscalarpresentation}，它为 $L$-affinoid，故 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion} 保证其具有谱点。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalcompletebasechange}，这给出 $\pi^{-1}(U)$ 中的点。因此所列闭域的逆像在紧空间 $\mathcal M_L(A_L)$ 中具有有限交性质。}

\bi{Choose a point $y$ in their intersection. For every $a\in A$, all upper inequalities give $|a(\pi(y))|\leq|a(x)|$. If $|a(x)|>0$, all lower inequalities give the reverse inequality; if $|a(x)|=0$, the upper inequalities already force equality. Hence $\pi(y)=x$. This proves surjectivity. If $A=0$, both spectra are empty and the assertion is immediate.}
{在这些逆像的交中选取一点 $y$。对每个 $a\in A$，全部上界不等式给出 $|a(\pi(y))|\leq|a(x)|$。若 $|a(x)|>0$，全部下界不等式给出反向不等式；若 $|a(x)|=0$，上界不等式已迫使等号成立。所以 $\pi(y)=x$，证明满射性。若 $A=0$，两个谱均为空，断言直接成立。}
\end{proof}
