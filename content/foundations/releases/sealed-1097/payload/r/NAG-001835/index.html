Kiehl equivalence and effective affinoid gluing

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 377

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Kiehl equivalence and effective affinoid gluing}{Kiehl 等价与 affinoid 有效粘合}]
\label{fnd:generalizeddomainkiehl}
\bi{Finite $A$-modules and coherent $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{aff}}$-modules are equivalent through $M\mapsto\widetilde M^{\mathrm{aff}}$ and global sections. The equivalence is exact, and coherence is equivalent to finite presentation. Every coherent sheaf has a global finite free presentation and norm-strict exact augmented \v Cech complexes for all finite affinoid-domain coverings. Finite modules with compatible gluing isomorphisms on such a covering descend effectively to a finite $A$-module, given by their matching kernel.}
{有限 $A$-模与凝聚 $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{aff}}$-模，通过 $M\mapsto\widetilde M^{\mathrm{aff}}$ 及整体截面构成范畴等价。该等价正合，且凝聚性等价于有限表示。每个凝聚层都有整体有限自由表示，并对所有有限 affinoid 域覆盖具有 norm-strict 正合的增广 \v Cech 复形。在这类覆盖上，带有相容粘合同构的有限模能够有效下降到有限 $A$-模，该模由其匹配核给出。}
\end{theorem}
Selected proof part 1 · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The comparison just proved satisfies the cocontinuous comparison hypotheses of \cite[Tag 03DM, Lemma 18.23.2]{StacksAlg}, so it preserves coherence and finite presentation. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalkiehl} identifies the coherent sheaves on the rational site with finite $A$-modules. Under comparison their extensions are exactly $\widetilde M^{\mathrm{aff}}$; hence the asserted equivalence, exactness and inverse follow from Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainsheafcomparison}. A finite presentation over the Noetherian ring $A$ gives the global presentation. Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidcovercech} to obtain the \v Cech assertion.}
{刚证明的比较满足 \cite[Tag 03DM, Lemma 18.23.2]{StacksAlg} 的 cocontinuous 比较假设，因而保持凝聚性与有限表示。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalkiehl}，rational site 上的凝聚层与有限 $A$-模相对应。在比较下，它们的延拓恰为 $\widetilde M^{\mathrm{aff}}$。因此由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainsheafcomparison} 得到所述等价、正合性及逆函子。Noetherian 环 $A$ 上的有限表示给出整体表示，再应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidcovercech} 得到 \v Cech 断言。}
Selected proof part 2 · exact TeX
\bi{For the gluing statement, attach to each local finite module its sheaf on the corresponding domain slice. On an arbitrary object take matching sections on its intersections with the covering members. Local sheaf gluing and the cocycle identity give a sheaf whose restriction to each member is the prescribed local sheaf. Thus it is locally finitely presented and hence coherent. The equivalence gives a finite global module, and the sheaf equalizer identifies that module with the matching kernel. The identifications on each member recover the given gluing maps by construction. All section-module comparisons and inverses are bounded by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}.}
{对粘合断言，先将每个局部有限模关联到相应域 slice 上的模层。在任意对象上，取它与各覆盖成员的交上的匹配截面。局部层的粘合及 cocycle 恒等式给出一个层，且在每个成员上的限制就是指定的局部层。因此该层局部有限表示，从而凝聚。范畴等价给出有限整体模，而层的等化子将其识别为匹配核。根据构造，在各成员上的识别恢复原粘合映射。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}，各截面模的比较及其逆均有界。}
\end{proof}
