A literal-union common refinement

F Descent of an algebraized polarized tower

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1987

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[A literal-union common refinement]
\label{cand:f5sub:radialcomparison}
Let \(\mathcal X,\mathcal Y\) be two given models of \(C\), with
the same \(D\) when marked. Let \(\Sigma_{\mathcal X,\Omega}\)
be the embedded compact geometric skeleton of \(\mathcal X\),
including its component vertices. Assume
\[
             V(\mathcal Y_{\Omega^\circ})
                     \subset\Sigma_{\mathcal X,\Omega}. \tag{3}
\]
There is then a least common dominating model \(\mathcal Z\) with
\[
                  V(\mathcal Z)
                       =V(\mathcal X)\cup V(\mathcal Y).
\]
It is the exact subdivision of \(\mathcal X\) in
Theorem~\ref{cand:f5sub:exactsubdivision}, with the full tube
description of Proposition~\ref{cand:f5sub:tubedata}.
Condition (3) holds, in particular, if the two embedded geometric
skeleta agree.
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Choose a common finite unramified Galois splitting field for the
nodes of both models. Every geometric vertex of \(\mathcal Y\)
not already a geometric vertex of \(\mathcal X\) lies on the
radial skeleton of one of the split \(\mathcal X\)-annuli.
Its image \(w\in C_L^{\mathrm{an}}\) is a component vertex of
\(\mathcal Y_{L^\circ}\), by \dep{v036:f5:vertexarithmetic}.
The annular coordinate is analytic on its entire \(L\)-tube,
so its evaluation lies in \(\mathcal H(w)\). Consequently
\[
 v(x_e(w))\in v(\mathcal H(w)^\times)
                   =v(L^\times)=\Gamma .
\]
The finite cut sets obey the branch compatibility because both
models are defined over \(K\). The theorem constructs
\(\mathcal X[S]\) whose geometric vertices are their union.
The entire-inverse-image assertion \dep{v036:f5:vertexarithmetic}
identifies its original vertex set with the asserted original union.
The two domination maps and least-common property follow from
\dep{v037:f5:algebraicdomination}. Marking compatibility follows
from \dep{v037:f5:intrinsicuniqueness}.
\end{proof}
