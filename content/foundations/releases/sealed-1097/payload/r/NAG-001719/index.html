Exact analytification of coherent modules

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 293

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Exact analytification of coherent modules}{凝聚模解析化的正合性}]
\label{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact}
\bi{For a finite-type $K$-scheme $Z$, the natural map of locally ringed spaces $\pi:Z^{\an}\to Z$ is flat on local rings. Its stalk map at $x$, over $\xi=\pi(x)$, is faithfully flat. The functor $\mathcal F\mapsto\mathcal F^{\an}=\pi^*\mathcal F$ sends coherent modules to coherent modules and is exact. It commutes with tensor products and, for coherent modules $\mathcal F,\mathcal G$, with the sheaf of homomorphisms.}
{对有限型 $K$-概形 $Z$，自然局部环空间映射 $\pi:Z^{\an}\to Z$ 在局部环上平坦。其在 $x$ 处、位于 $\xi=\pi(x)$ 上方的茎映射忠实平坦。函子 $\mathcal F\mapsto\mathcal F^{\an}=\pi^*\mathcal F$ 将凝聚模送至凝聚模，并且正合。它与张量积交换；对凝聚模 $\mathcal F,\mathcal G$，也与同态层交换。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{On an affine scheme chart $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$, the map $\pi$ sends a seminorm to its prime kernel. It is continuous because the inverse image of $D(g)$ is $|g|>0$. A fraction in $R_g$ defines an analytic function there, compatibly with further restriction, giving the structural sheaf map. At $x$, a fraction whose value is nonzero is invertible in an analytic neighbourhood by a Laurent localization. Thus the map on stalks is local.}
{在仿射概形图 $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ 上，$\pi$ 将半范数送至其素核。由于 $D(g)$ 的逆像为 $|g|>0$，该映射连续。$R_g$ 中的分式在此开集上定义解析函数，并与进一步限制相容，因而给出结构层映射。在 $x$ 处，值非零的分式可通过 Laurent 局部化，在解析邻域中取得逆元，因此茎映射是局部的。}

\bi{Choose a radius neighbourhood $X_{R,r}$ of $x$. Its algebra $A_r$ is flat over $R$ by Theorem~\ref{fnd:polynomialtateflatness}. On each smaller rational neighbourhood $V$ of $x$, the algebra $A_V$ is flat over $A_r$ by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness}. The analytic stalk is the filtered colimit of these $A_V$, by Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks}. Filtered colimits of flat modules are flat: a tensor relation uses finitely many elements, and if it becomes zero in the colimit it becomes zero at some stage, where injectivity follows from flatness. Equivalently one applies the finite-ideal criterion to this finite-stage argument. Thus $\mathcal O_{Z^{\an},x}$ is flat over $R$ and over $R_{\mathfrak p}=\mathcal O_{Z,\xi}$. Its maximal ideal lies above $\mathfrak p$, and its residue field is nonzero. The faithful-flat criterion for this local map proves the stalk assertion.}
{选取 $x$ 的半径邻域 $X_{R,r}$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:polynomialtateflatness}，其代数 $A_r$ 在 $R$ 上平坦。对 $x$ 的每个更小 rational 邻域 $V$，Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness} 说明 $A_V$ 在 $A_r$ 上平坦。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks}，解析茎为这些 $A_V$ 的滤过直极限。平坦模的滤过直极限平坦：一个张量关系只使用有限个元素；若它在直极限中变为零，就会在某个阶段为零，而该阶段的平坦性给出单射性。也可对这一有限阶段论证应用有限理想判据。因此 $\mathcal O_{Z^{\an},x}$ 在 $R$ 以及 $R_{\mathfrak p}=\mathcal O_{Z,\xi}$ 上平坦。其极大理想位于 $\mathfrak p$ 上方，且剩余域非零。对这个局部映射应用忠实平坦判据，就得到茎断言。}

\bi{By \cite[Tag 01XZ, Lemma~30.9.1]{StacksAlg}, $\mathcal F$ is represented on $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ by a finite module $M$. Thus its pullback to $X_{R,r}$ is the coherent module associated with $A_r\otimes_R M$. These descriptions agree on overlaps by the tensor mapping property, so they give the stated coherent analytification. Flatness of $R\to A_r$ preserves exact sequences of finite modules on every such chart, proving exactness on the analytic $G$-site as well as on the ordinary topology. Tensor compatibility is algebraic associativity. For homomorphisms, take a finite presentation $R^a\to R^b\to M\to0$ and apply $\operatorname{Hom}_R(-,N)$. Its first three terms identify $\operatorname{Hom}_R(M,N)$ with the kernel of a matrix $N^b\to N^a$. Flat scalar extension to $A_r$ preserves this kernel and gives exactly $\operatorname{Hom}_{A_r}(A_r\otimes M,A_r\otimes N)$. These natural chartwise identifications glue to the asserted sheaf isomorphism.}
{由 \cite[Tag 01XZ, Lemma~30.9.1]{StacksAlg}，$\mathcal F$ 在 $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ 上由有限模 $M$ 表示。因此其在 $X_{R,r}$ 上的拉回为与 $A_r\otimes_R M$ 对应的凝聚模。由张量积映射性质，这些描述在重叠部分上一致，故给出所述凝聚解析化。$R\to A_r$ 的平坦性在每个此类图上保持有限模的正合列，因此同时证明解析 $G$-site 及普通拓扑中的正合性。与张量积的相容性就是代数结合律。对同态，取有限表示 $R^a\to R^b\to M\to0$ 并作用 $\operatorname{Hom}_R(-,N)$；所得前三项将 $\operatorname{Hom}_R(M,N)$ 识别为矩阵映射 $N^b\to N^a$ 的核。平坦扩标量到 $A_r$ 保持此核，并恰好得到 $\operatorname{Hom}_{A_r}(A_r\otimes M,A_r\otimes N)$。这些自然的图上识别粘合为所需层同构。}
\end{proof}
