Descent of the smooth-tube contraction

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 678

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Descent of the smooth-tube contraction}
{光滑管域收缩的下降}]
\label{cand:separablesmoothtubehomotopy}
\bi{Let $q$ be a closed point where a curve model $\mathcal X/O$
is smooth of relative dimension one, and suppose
$\kappa(q)/k$ is finite separable. Let $\eta$ be its component
generic point. Then}
{设曲线模型 $\mathcal X/O$ 在闭点 $q$ 处相对维数为一且光滑，
并假设 $\kappa(q)/k$ 有限可分。以 $\eta$ 表示其分量泛点，则}
\[
 \overline{]q[}=]q[\cup\{v_\eta\}.
\]
\bi{This compact space admits a canonical jointly continuous
strong deformation retraction onto $v_\eta$. After any finite
Galois residue-field lift which splits $q$, it is the descent of
the canonical open-disc contractions on the finitely many rational
tubes. It is independent of that lift and is functorial for
isomorphisms of curve models and isometric automorphisms of the
ground field.}
{此紧空间具有到 $v_\eta$ 的典范联合连续强形变收缩。在任意
使 $q$ 分裂的有限 Galois 剩余域提升后，它是有限多个有理
管域上的典范开圆盘收缩的下降。它不依赖所选提升，并与曲线
模型的同构及基域等距自同构相容。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Take a finite Galois extension $l/k$ containing the normal
closure of $\kappa(q)/k$, and use
Lemma~\ref{cand:galoisresidueextensionlift} to obtain $L/K$.
The separable polynomial factorization gives
$\kappa(q)\otimes_k l\simeq\prod_{\iota:\kappa(q)\hookrightarrow l}l$.
Consequently the points $q_1,\ldots,q_m$ over $q$ are rational
smooth points of the extended model. The preceding lemma
identifies the generic-fibre projection $p:X_L\to X$ and gives}
{取有限 Galois 扩张 $l/k$ 包含 $\kappa(q)/k$ 的正规闭包，
并由 Lemma~\ref{cand:galoisresidueextensionlift} 得到 $L/K$。
可分多项式的分解给出
$\kappa(q)\otimes_k l\simeq\prod_{\iota:\kappa(q)\hookrightarrow l}l$。
因此 $q$ 上方的点 $q_1,\ldots,q_m$ 都是扩张模型的有理
光滑点。上一引理识别泛纤维投影 $p:X_L\to X$，并给出}
\[
 p^{-1}(D_q)=\coprod_{j=1}^m D_{q_j}.
\]
\bi{Each $D_{q_j}$ has compact closure
$C_j=D_{q_j}\cup\{v_j\}$ by
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure}. By flat going down
(\cite[Tag 00HS]{StacksAlg}), each generic point above the unique
component through $q$ maps to a component generic point; its
image is necessarily $\eta$. Thus $p(v_j)=v_\eta$.
Continuity puts this point in $\overline{D_q}$, and
Lemma~\ref{cand:closedtubeboundary} gives
$C_q=D_q\cup\{v_\eta\}$.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure}，每个
$D_{q_j}$ 的闭包都是紧集 $C_j=D_{q_j}\cup\{v_j\}$。
由平坦映射的 going down（\cite[Tag 00HS]{StacksAlg}），
相应分量泛点映到分量泛点；其像必为 $\eta$，故
$p(v_j)=v_\eta$。连续性把该点放在 $\overline{D_q}$ 中，再由
Lemma~\ref{cand:closedtubeboundary} 得到
$C_q=D_q\cup\{v_\eta\}$。}

\bi{The projection $p$ is open by
Theorem~\ref{cand:galoisanalyticquotient}. Any continuous open
surjection satisfies $p^{-1}(\overline E)=\overline{p^{-1}(E)}$:
an open neighborhood upstairs maps to an open neighborhood
downstairs, which meets $E$ whenever its centre lies in $\overline E$.
Apply this to $E=D_q$ to obtain}
{由 Theorem~\ref{cand:galoisanalyticquotient}，投影 $p$ 为开映射。
任何连续开满射都满足
$p^{-1}(\overline E)=\overline{p^{-1}(E)}$：上方的开邻域映到
下方的开邻域，只要中心属于 $\overline E$，后者便与 $E$ 相交。
将此应用于 $E=D_q$，得到}
\[
 p^{-1}(C_q)=\bigcup_{j=1}^m C_j.
\]
\bi{The canonical homotopies of
Theorem~\ref{cand:opendisccanonicalhomotopy} agree on intersections
of these closed sets, which consist only of fixed component
points. Finite closed pasting gives a continuous homotopy on
$p^{-1}(C_q)$. Its full coordinate and semilinear invariance makes
it $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$-equivariant. The compact-to-Hausdorff
surjection $p^{-1}(C_q)\to C_q$ remains a quotient map after
product with $[0,1]$. Hence the homotopy descends, and its endpoint
and fixed-point identities give the asserted strong contraction.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{cand:opendisccanonicalhomotopy} 给出的典范同伦
在这些闭集的交上相同，因为交只含固定的分量点。有限闭集
粘合给出 $p^{-1}(C_q)$ 上的连续同伦。其完整坐标及半线性
不变性使它为 $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$-等变。从紧空间到
Hausdorff 空间的满射 $p^{-1}(C_q)\to C_q$ 与 $[0,1]$ 作乘积
后仍为商映射，因此同伦下降，且其端值和固定点恒等式给出
所需强收缩。}

\bi{To compare two choices of lift, take a finite Galois extension
of $k$ containing both residue fields and lift it in the same way.
The simple roots of each of the two defining polynomials lift in
this common complete field; sending the original generators to
these roots gives compatible isometric embeddings of both lifted
fields. The ground-field compatibility in
Theorem~\ref{cand:opendisccanonicalhomotopy} makes the two
homotopies equal after this common extension. Surjectivity of
projection makes their descents equal. The same argument together
with coordinate and semilinear invariance proves functoriality.}
{比较两个提升时，取包含两个剩余域的有限 Galois 扩张，并按
同一方法提升。两个定义多项式的单根均可在这个共同完备域中
提升；将原生成元送到这些根，得到两个提升域的相容等距
嵌入。Theorem~\ref{cand:opendisccanonicalhomotopy} 的基域相容性
使两个同伦在共同扩域后相等，由投影满射性，其下降也相等。
同一论证结合坐标及半线性不变性，给出函子性。}
\end{proof}
