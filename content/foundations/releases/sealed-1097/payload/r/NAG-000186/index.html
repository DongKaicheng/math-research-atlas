\begin{theorem}[\bi{Reduction of strict analytic germs}{Strict 解析芽的约化}]\label{fnd:germtheorem}
\bi{For strictly analytic spaces, germ reduction is functorial and independent of the model and neighborhood. Germ subdomains correspond to birational subspaces, compatibly with inclusions, intersections and finite unions. A germ is good precisely when its reduction is affine in the birational category. A morphism is separated at a point precisely when the corresponding reduction morphism is separated; the point lies in relative interior precisely when that reduction morphism is proper.}
{对 strictly analytic spaces，germ reduction 为 functorial，且与 model 和 neighborhood 无关。Germ subdomains 与 birational subspaces 对应，并与 inclusions、intersections 和 finite unions 相容。Germ 为 good 当且仅当其 reduction 在 birational category 中为 affine。Morphism 在一个 point 处 separated，当且仅当相应 reduction morphism separated；该 point 位于 relative interior，当且仅当相应 reduction morphism proper。}
\end{theorem}
