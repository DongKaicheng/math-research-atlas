\begin{lemma}[\bi{The diagonal for a good Hausdorff source}
{Good Hausdorff 源空间的对角线}]
\label{fnd:goodhausdorffseparated}
\bi{If $X$ is affinoid and $Y$ is good and Hausdorff, every
analytic morphism $f:Y\to X$ is separated.}
{若 $X$ 为 affinoid、$Y$ 为 good 且 Hausdorff，则每个解析
态射 $f:Y\to X$ 均分离。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $P=Y\times_XY$, with projections $p_1,p_2$.
The diagonal is a topological embedding onto its image because
$p_1\Delta$ is the identity. We describe its closed-subspace
structure on an ordinary open covering of $P$.
Fix $t\in P$ and let $y_i=p_i(t)$.}
{令 $P=Y\times_XY$，其投影为 $p_1,p_2$。
由于 $p_1\Delta$ 为恒等，对角线是到其像的拓扑嵌入。
下面在 $P$ 的一个通常开覆盖上给出其闭子空间结构。
固定 $t\in P$，并记 $y_i=p_i(t)$。}

\bi{If $y_1\ne y_2$, choose disjoint ordinary open neighbourhoods
$N_1,N_2$ in the Hausdorff space $Y$. The open neighbourhood
$p_1^{-1}(N_1)\cap p_2^{-1}(N_2)$ of $t$ is disjoint from the
diagonal image; there its closed-subspace ideal is the unit ideal.}
{若 $y_1\ne y_2$，在 Hausdorff 空间 $Y$ 中选择不交的通常
开邻域 $N_1,N_2$。于是 $t$ 的开邻域
$p_1^{-1}(N_1)\cap p_2^{-1}(N_2)$ 与对角线像不交；
其上的闭子空间理想为单位理想。}

\bi{If $y_1=y_2=y$, goodness supplies an affinoid neighbourhood
$V=\mathcal M(B)$ of $y$. Then $V\times_XV$ is an affinoid
domain of $P$ containing the open neighbourhood
$p_1^{-1}(\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}V)\cap
p_2^{-1}(\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}V)$ of $t$.
For $X=\mathcal M(A)$, the diagonal over this domain is exactly
the multiplication quotient}
{若 $y_1=y_2=y$，goodness 给出 $y$ 的 affinoid 邻域
$V=\mathcal M(B)$。于是 $V\times_XV$ 是 $P$ 的 affinoid
域，并包含 $t$ 的开邻域
$p_1^{-1}(\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}V)\cap
p_2^{-1}(\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}V)$。
若 $X=\mathcal M(A)$，则在此域上的对角线恰由乘法商给出：}
\[
            B\widehat\otimes_A B\longrightarrow B.
\]
\bi{It is bounded and has the bounded section $b\mapsto b\otimes1$,
so it is norm-admissible. Its kernel is a finite closed ideal by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}.
The bounded pushout property identifies its spectrum with the
diagonal, including the analytic points with their residue-field
data; no identification of the analytic product with an ordinary
topological product is used.}
{该映射有界，且具有有界截面 $b\mapsto b\otimes1$，因此
norm-admissible。由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}，其核是有限的
闭理想。有界推出泛性质将其谱识别为对角线，同时保留解析点
的剩余域数据；此处不把解析纤维积等同于通常拓扑纤维积。}

\bi{The two cases cover every point of $P$ by ordinary open
neighbourhoods on which the diagonal is a closed subspace.
Its image is consequently closed, and the ideals agree on
overlaps since they are the kernels of the same restriction map.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:closedimmersiontargetglocal}, applied to this
ordinary open covering, proves that the full diagonal is a closed
immersion. Thus $f$ is separated.}
{上述两种情形给出 $P$ 的通常开邻域覆盖，使对角线在每块上
均为闭子空间。因此其像闭；各理想在交叠上相容，因为它们是
同一限制映射的核。对这个通常开覆盖应用
Lemma~\ref{fnd:closedimmersiontargetglocal}，便知整个对角线
为闭浸入，故 $f$ 分离。}
\end{proof}
