Extending an open ideal with controlled support

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 538

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Extending an open ideal with controlled support}
{带支撑控制的开理想延拓}]
\label{fnd:formalidealextension}
\bi{Let $\mathfrak X$ be a quasi-paracompact admissible formal
scheme and $\mathfrak U\subset\mathfrak X$ a quasi-compact open.
Every coherent open ideal $\mathcal I_{\mathfrak U}$ extends
to a coherent open ideal $\mathcal I$ on $\mathfrak X$ that is
the unit ideal on every open disjoint from $\mathfrak U$.
Consequently a finite composition of admissible blowups on
$\mathfrak U$ extends to such a composition on $\mathfrak X$,
unchanged over opens disjoint from $\mathfrak U$.}
{设 $\mathfrak X$ 为 quasi-paracompact admissible 形式概形，
$\mathfrak U\subset\mathfrak X$ 为拟紧开集。
每个凝聚开理想 $\mathcal I_{\mathfrak U}$ 都延拓为
$\mathfrak X$ 上的凝聚开理想 $\mathcal I$，且在每个与
$\mathfrak U$ 不交的开集上等于单位理想。
因此，$\mathfrak U$ 上有限次 admissible blowup 的复合延拓为
$\mathfrak X$ 上的这种复合，并在与 $\mathfrak U$ 不交的开集
上保持不变。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A finite-type quasi-compact open cover of $\mathfrak X$
contains a finite subfamily covering $\mathfrak U$.
The union $\mathfrak W$ of these members and all members
meeting them is quasi-compact and contains
$\overline{\mathfrak U}$. Indeed, any member containing a
point of this closure meets $\mathfrak U$ and hence one of
the chosen finitely many members.
The space $\mathfrak W$ is Noetherian topologically, by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}.
In particular $\mathfrak W\setminus\overline{\mathfrak U}$
and $\mathfrak U$ are quasi-compact opens in it.}
{取 $\mathfrak X$ 的有限型拟紧开覆盖，其中有限个成员覆盖
$\mathfrak U$。将这些成员以及与它们相交的全部成员取并，得到
拟紧开集 $\mathfrak W$，它包含 $\overline{\mathfrak U}$。
确实，包含闭包中一点的任何覆盖成员都与 $\mathfrak U$ 相交，
因而与所选有限个成员中的一个相交。
由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}，
$\mathfrak W$ 的拓扑 Noetherian，故
$\mathfrak W\setminus\overline{\mathfrak U}$ 和
$\mathfrak U$ 都是其中的拟紧开集。}

\bi{Choose $N$ with $\pi^N\mathcal O_{\mathfrak U}
\subset\mathcal I_{\mathfrak U}$.
On the disjoint open union
$\mathfrak U\cup(\mathfrak W\setminus\overline{\mathfrak U})$
of the scheme $\mathfrak W_N=(\mathfrak W,
\mathcal O_{\mathfrak W}/\pi^N)$, prescribe the finite-type
ideal $\mathcal I_{\mathfrak U}/\pi^N$ on the first part
and the unit ideal on the second.
Apply \cite[Tag 01PF]{StacksAlg} to extend this as a finite-type
quasi-coherent ideal on $\mathfrak W_N$.
Its inverse image in $\mathcal O_{\mathfrak W}$ is locally
generated by lifts of its finite generators and $\pi^N$.
Flatness of principal formal restrictions, proved in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalprincipalopenflat}, identifies these
ideals on overlaps; coherence follows from
Corollary~\ref{fnd:formalringcoherence}.
Glue it with the unit ideal on
$\mathfrak X\setminus\overline{\mathfrak U}$.
The two prescriptions agree on their intersection and give
the required $\mathcal I$.}
{选择 $N$ 使
$\pi^N\mathcal O_{\mathfrak U}\subset\mathcal I_{\mathfrak U}$。
在概形
$\mathfrak W_N=(\mathfrak W,\mathcal O_{\mathfrak W}/\pi^N)$
的不交开并
$\mathfrak U\cup(\mathfrak W\setminus\overline{\mathfrak U})$
上，在第一部分指定有限型理想
$\mathcal I_{\mathfrak U}/\pi^N$，在第二部分指定单位理想。
由 \cite[Tag 01PF]{StacksAlg}，它延拓为 $\mathfrak W_N$
上的有限型拟凝聚理想。其在 $\mathcal O_{\mathfrak W}$ 中的
逆像局部由有限个生成元的提升和 $\pi^N$ 生成。
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalprincipalopenflat} 中的主形式限制
平坦性识别重叠部分上的这些理想，凝聚性由
Corollary~\ref{fnd:formalringcoherence} 给出。
再与 $\mathfrak X\setminus\overline{\mathfrak U}$ 上的单位理想
粘合；两种规定在交上相同，给出所需的 $\mathcal I$。}

\bi{For a composition of blowups, extend the first center,
then the second center on the inverse image of $\mathfrak U$,
and so on. At every stage the modification is an isomorphism
on opens disjoint from that inverse image. Blowups of
quasi-paracompact formal schemes remain quasi-paracompact:
over each member of a finite-type cover their finitely many
affine blowup charts form a finite refinement of the pulled-back
cover, and nonintersecting original members remain disjoint.
This justifies the induction.}
{对 blowup 的复合，先延拓第一个中心，再在
$\mathfrak U$ 的逆像上延拓第二个中心，依次进行。
每一步在与该逆像不交的开集上都是同构。
Quasi-paracompact 形式概形的 blowup 仍为 quasi-paracompact：
有限型覆盖每个成员上有限个仿射 blowup 片构成拉回覆盖的有限加细，
原来不交的成员仍然不交。这说明该归纳可以进行。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
open coverings are the strict affinoid coverings and their
composites. A covering is of finite type if each member meets
only finitely many others. Quasi-paracompact means admitting
such a covering by quasi-compact admissible opens. The same
definition, with ordinary open subsets, is used for formal schemes.}
