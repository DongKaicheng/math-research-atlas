Rational intersections with their actual presentations

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 200

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Rational intersections with their actual presentations}{Rational 交叠及其实际表示}]\label{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation}
\bi{Let $B=A\langle f/g\rangle$ and $C=A\langle e/h\rangle$, with both displayed tuples generating the unit ideal. The joint quotient}
{设 $B=A\langle f/g\rangle$、$C=A\langle e/h\rangle$，其中两个所列 tuples 均生成单位理想。联合商}
\[
 H=A\{T_1,\ldots,T_s,S_1,\ldots,S_t\}/
          (gT_i-f_i,\ hS_j-e_j)_{i,j}
\]
\bi{is isometric to the rational presentation with denominator $gh$ and numerator list}
{等距同构于以下 rational 表示：分母为 $gh$，分子列表为}
\[
 (hf_i)_i,\qquad (ge_j)_j,\qquad (f_ie_j)_{i,j}.
\]
\bi{Its point set is the intersection of the point sets for $B$ and $C$, and $H\simeq B\widehat\otimes_A C$. Finite iteration gives the analogous statement for every finite intersection, including repeated members. The intersection and restriction maps are independent of the chosen rational presentations up to the unique bounded isomorphisms of Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaldomainup}. For a finite $A$-module, the corresponding maps on relation-quotient modules are bounded and satisfy the same composition identities.}
{其谱点集合是 $B$ 与 $C$ 对应谱点集合的交，且 $H\simeq B\widehat\otimes_A C$。有限次迭代给出每个有限交叠的对应结论，包括重复成员。交叠及限制映射不依赖于 rational 表示的选择，准确地说，不同选择由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaldomainup} 中唯一的有界同构比较。对有限 $A$-模，相应关系商模上的映射均有界，并满足同样的复合恒等式。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Multiplying unit-ideal identities for $(g,f_i)$ and $(h,e_j)$ shows that $gh$ and the listed numerators generate the unit ideal. Let $P$ be their rational presentation, with variables $Y_i,Z_j,W_{ij}$ in the listed order. In $H$, both $g$ and $h$ are units by their separate unit-ideal identities. Send}
{将 $(g,f_i)$ 与 $(h,e_j)$ 的单位理想恒等式相乘，可见 $gh$ 和所列分子生成单位理想。记相应 rational 表示为 $P$，依所列顺序使用变量 $Y_i,Z_j,W_{ij}$。在 $H$ 中，分别利用两个单位理想恒等式可知 $g,h$ 均为单位。令}
\[
 Y_i\longmapsto\bar T_i,\qquad
 Z_j\longmapsto\bar S_j,\qquad
 W_{ij}\longmapsto\bar T_i\bar S_j.
\]
\bi{The images have norm at most one and satisfy all relations, so this gives a contractive map $P\to H$. In $P$, the product $gh$ is a unit; hence $g$ and $h$ are units separately. Dividing the defining equations gives $gY_i=f_i$, $hZ_j=e_j$, and $W_{ij}=Y_iZ_j$. Thus $T_i\mapsto\bar Y_i$, $S_j\mapsto\bar Z_j$ gives a contractive map $H\to P$. The two maps are inverse on coefficients and variables, hence on the complete quotients by density. They are isometric.}
{这些像的范数至多为一，并满足所有关系，所以得到 contractive 映射 $P\to H$。在 $P$ 中，乘积 $gh$ 为单位，因而 $g,h$ 分别为单位。将定义等式除以相应单位，得到 $gY_i=f_i$、$hZ_j=e_j$ 及 $W_{ij}=Y_iZ_j$。于是 $T_i\mapsto\bar Y_i$、$S_j\mapsto\bar Z_j$ 给出 contractive 映射 $H\to P$。两个映射在系数和变量上互逆，再由稠密性在完备商上互逆，所以为等距同构。}

\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology} identifies the point set of $P$. On that set $gh$ is nonzero. The inequalities for $hf_i$ and $ge_j$ can therefore be divided by $|h|$ and $|g|$, giving the two original sets of inequalities. Conversely the original inequalities imply all three product inequalities. The completed-tensor assertion is Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpresentationpushout}. Repeat this construction to treat finite intersections; each step uses actual ideals and the quotient norms, by Lemma~\ref{fnd:iteratedstrictquotients}. A repeated member is redundant by Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalredundantconstraint}.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology} 识别 $P$ 的谱点集合。在该集合上 $gh$ 非零，所以可以将 $hf_i$ 和 $ge_j$ 的不等式分别除以 $|h|$ 与 $|g|$，得到原来的两组不等式。反之，原来的不等式蕴含全部三组乘积不等式。Completed-tensor 断言来自 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpresentationpushout}。重复此构造即可处理有限交叠；由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:iteratedstrictquotients}，每一步都使用实际理想及商范数。重复成员由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalredundantconstraint} 识别为冗余约束。}

\bi{The strict-target universal property gives the asserted representation independence and makes every composition identity a consequence of uniqueness. A bounded algebra map induces a bounded map on the finite free modules in a fixed presentation of $M$, hence on their actual quotient modules. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} identifies these with the ordinary and completed extensions. Their maps coincide on the images of the fixed generators, which proves the same compatibility for modules.}
{Strict-target universal property 给出所述表示无关性，并由唯一性给出每个复合恒等式。有界代数映射在 $M$ 的固定表示所用的有限自由模上诱导有界映射，因而在其实际商模上诱导有界映射。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} 将这些模与普通、完备延伸识别。它们在固定生成元的像上相同，因此模的映射也具有相同的相容性。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX

\bi{We work over a complete non-Archimedean field $K$, allowing trivial valuation except in the small-scalar lemmas where nontriviality is stated. Every nonzero algebra has a strict norm-admissible Banach norm, and every relation quotient has its actual quotient norm. The zero algebra is admitted separately with its zero norm. All radii in this section are one; a radius in $|K^\times|$ can first be reduced to this convention by scaling the corresponding numerator. These conventions concern the displayed presentations, not a spectral norm.}
{本节在完备非阿基米德域 $K$ 上工作，允许赋值平凡；使用小标量的引理会明确要求赋值非平凡。每个非零代数配备 strict norm-admissible Banach 范数，每个关系商配备实际商范数。零代数另以零范数允许在内。本节所有半径均为一；值群 $|K^\times|$ 中的半径可先通过缩放相应分子化为这一约定。这些约定涉及所列表示，并不使用 spectral 范数。}

\bi{For a tuple $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$ generating the unit ideal of $A$, write}
{对生成 $A$ 的单位理想的 tuple $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$，记}
\[
 A\langle f/g\rangle
   =A\{T_1,\ldots,T_s\}/(gT_j-f_j)_{1\leq j\leq s}.
\]
\bi{For a unit-ideal tuple $f_0,\ldots,f_r$, its \emph{standard rational family} consists of the $r+1$ presentations}
{对生成单位理想的 tuple $f_0,\ldots,f_r$，其\emph{标准 rational 族}由以下 $r+1$ 个表示组成：}
\[
 R_i=A\{T_j:j\ne i\}/(f_iT_j-f_j)_{j\ne i},
 \qquad 0\leq i\leq r.
\]
\bi{We put $T_i=1$ when using a formula involving all indices. The joint presentation of a finite family means the quotient with disjoint variable blocks and all their relations. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpresentationpushout} identifies it with the separated completed tensor product. Its module attached to a finite $A$-module $M$ is its ordinary tensor with $M$, equipped with the quotient norm from a fixed finite free presentation of $M$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} gives its comparison with the completed tensor. These choices specify the arrows and norms before a geometric sheaf is available.}
{若某个公式涉及全部指标，约定 $T_i=1$。有限族的联合表示，是指添入互不相交的变量组并施加全部关系后所得的商。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpresentationpushout} 将它与 separated completed tensor product 识别。对有限 $A$-模 $M$，相应模是该商与 $M$ 的普通张量积，并配备来自 $M$ 的一个固定有限自由表示的商范数。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} 给出它与 completed tensor 的比较。因此，在尚未构造几何层之前，所有箭头及范数已有明确含义。}
