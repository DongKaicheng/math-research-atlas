Relative strict presentations

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 84

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Relative strict presentations}{相对 strict 表示}]
\label{fnd:relativeaffinoidpresentation}
\bi{Every bounded homomorphism $A\to B$ of strict $K$-affinoid algebras extends to a bounded norm-strict surjection $A\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle\twoheadrightarrow B$. Its kernel is an actual closed ideal. In particular, the norm of $B$ is equivalent to the displayed relative quotient norm. Trivial valuation and zero quotients are allowed.}
{strict $K$-affinoid 代数的每个有界同态 $A\to B$ 都可延拓为有界且范数严格的满射 $A\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle\twoheadrightarrow B$。其核是实际闭理想。特别地，$B$ 的范数与所列相对商范数等价。允许平凡赋值及零商。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose a strict presentation $K\langle T\rangle\twoheadrightarrow B$ and first use its quotient norm on $B$ and a presentation norm on $A$. The variable classes have norm at most one. The bounded map on coefficients and Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundedsubstitution} give $A\langle T\rangle\to B$. This map is surjective because it contains the original presentation. That presentation gives lifts of norm less than twice the norm of any nonzero element of $B$, and its coefficient inclusion into $A\langle T\rangle$ is isometric. These lifts and the bound on the map prove equivalence of its quotient norm and the norm on $B$. The coefficient algebra is strict by Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictweightedquotient}, so its ideal kernel is closed by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}. Equivalent initial norms only change constants. Zero targets are immediate.}
{选择一个 strict 表示 $K\langle T\rangle\twoheadrightarrow B$，先在 $B$ 上使用其商范数，并在 $A$ 上使用表示范数。变量的像范数不超过一。系数映射的有界性及 Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundedsubstitution} 给出 $A\langle T\rangle\to B$。这个映射包含原表示的像，因而满射。对 $B$ 中任意非零元素，原表示给出范数小于其范数两倍的提升，而系数包含到 $A\langle T\rangle$ 是等距的。由这些提升以及映射本身的有界性，得到商范数与 $B$ 上范数的等价性。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictweightedquotient}，系数代数为 strict，故 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules} 使其核理想闭。换成等价的初始范数只改变常数。零目标的情形直接成立。}
\end{proof}
