The scalar-extension quotient

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 642

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The scalar-extension quotient}{标量扩张的商拓扑}]
\label{cand:galoisanalyticquotient}
\bi{For every $K$-analytic space $X$, the scalar-extension projection is a continuous, open, closed surjection and induces a homeomorphism}
{对每个 $K$-解析空间 $X$，标量扩张投影是连续、开且闭的满射，并诱导同胚}
\[
 X_L/G\ \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\ X.
\]
\bi{For every $G$-invariant subset $S\subseteq X_L$, its induced map $S/G\to p(S)$ is also a homeomorphism, with the subspace topologies. For every topological space $T$, the map $p\times\mathrm{id}_T$ is an open quotient map.}
{对每个 $G$-不变子集 $S\subseteq X_L$，诱导映射 $S/G\to p(S)$ 关于子空间拓扑也为同胚。对任意拓扑空间 $T$，映射 $p\times\mathrm{id}_T$ 是开商映射。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{On an affinoid chart $V=\mathcal M(A)$, its inverse image is $V_L=\mathcal M_L(A\widehat\otimes_K L)$. The field automorphisms of $L$ are isometries, so their coefficient actions extend to bounded algebra automorphisms and hence continuous maps of $V_L$. These actions agree on overlaps and give a continuous $G$-action on $X_L$. By Lemma~\ref{cand:finitegaloisfibres}, the projection is surjective and its fibres are exactly the orbits.}
{在 affinoid 图 $V=\mathcal M(A)$ 上，其逆像为 $V_L=\mathcal M_L(A\widehat\otimes_K L)$。$L$ 的这些域自同构均为等距映射，因此它们在系数上的作用延拓为有界代数自同构，从而给出 $V_L$ 的连续映射。这些作用在交叠上相容，得到 $X_L$ 上的连续 $G$-作用。由 Lemma~\ref{cand:finitegaloisfibres}，投影满射，且其纤维恰为轨道。}

\bi{We verify closedness using the affinoid quasi-net atlas. Let $C$ be closed in $X_L$. For each affinoid chart $V$, the set $C\cap V_L$ is closed in the compact space $V_L$; its image $p(C)\cap V$ is compact and thus closed in the Hausdorff space $V$. The quasi-net condition now makes $p(C)$ closed in $X$. To see this directly, for $x\notin p(C)$ choose finitely many chart members $V_i$ containing $x$ whose union contains a neighborhood $N$ of $x$. Each relative open complement $V_i\setminus p(C)$ contains $x$, so it contains $O_i\cap V_i$ for an ambient open neighborhood $O_i$ of $x$. The neighborhood $N\cap\bigcap_i O_i$ is disjoint from $p(C)$. This argument does not require $X$ itself to be Hausdorff or good.}
{利用 affinoid quasi-net atlas 验证闭性。设 $C$ 在 $X_L$ 中闭。对每个 affinoid 图 $V$，集合 $C\cap V_L$ 在紧空间 $V_L$ 中闭；其像 $p(C)\cap V$ 紧，故在 Hausdorff 空间 $V$ 中闭。Quasi-net 条件于是使 $p(C)$ 在 $X$ 中闭。具体地，对 $x\notin p(C)$，选择有限个包含 $x$ 的图 $V_i$，使其并包含 $x$ 的邻域 $N$。每个相对开补集 $V_i\setminus p(C)$ 都包含 $x$，故包含某个环境开邻域 $O_i$ 与 $V_i$ 的交。邻域 $N\cap\bigcap_iO_i$ 便与 $p(C)$ 不交。此论证无需 $X$ 本身 Hausdorff 或 good。}

\bi{A closed continuous surjection is a quotient map. Since its fibres are the $G$-orbits, it gives the stated homeomorphism. It is open as well: for any open $U\subset X_L$, the set $p^{-1}(p(U))=\bigcup_{g\in G}gU$ is open, so $p(U)$ is open by the quotient property.}
{闭的连续满射为商映射。由于其纤维为 $G$-轨道，便得到所述同胚。它也是开映射：对任意开集 $U\subset X_L$，集合 $p^{-1}(p(U))=\bigcup_{g\in G}gU$ 开，故由商性质，$p(U)$ 开。}

\bi{A $G$-invariant subset is saturated for $p$. If $F\subset S$ is relatively closed, write $F=C\cap S$ with $C$ closed in $X_L$. Saturation gives $p(F)=p(C)\cap p(S)$, which is relatively closed. Thus $S\to p(S)$ is a quotient map with orbit fibres and yields the second homeomorphism. Finally, $p\times\mathrm{id}_T$ sends a basic open product $U\times W$ to the open product $p(U)\times W$. It is a continuous open surjection and therefore a quotient map.}
{$G$-不变子集关于 $p$ 饱和。若 $F\subset S$ 相对闭，可写 $F=C\cap S$，其中 $C$ 在 $X_L$ 中闭。饱和性给出 $p(F)=p(C)\cap p(S)$，故此像相对闭。因此 $S\to p(S)$ 是以轨道为纤维的商映射，给出第二个同胚。最后，$p\times\mathrm{id}_T$ 将基本开积 $U\times W$ 送到开积 $p(U)\times W$。它是连续开满射，所以为商映射。}
\end{proof}
