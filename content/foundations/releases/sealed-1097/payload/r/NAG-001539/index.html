Kiehl equivalence on the finite strict-domain site

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Kiehl equivalence on the finite strict-domain site}{有限 strict 域 site 上的 Kiehl 等价}]\label{fnd:strictdomainkiehl}
\bi{For a complete $K$, with trivial valuation allowed, and a strict $K$-affinoid $X=\mathcal M(A)$, the functors}
{设 $K$ 完备，允许平凡赋值，$X=\mathcal M(A)$ 为 strict $K$-affinoid。则函子}
\[
 M\longmapsto\widetilde M^{\mathrm{str}},
 \qquad \mathcal F\longmapsto\Gamma(X,\mathcal F)
\]
\bi{give inverse exact equivalences between finite $A$-modules and coherent $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{str}}$-modules on $\mathcal D_X$. Coherence here is Definition 18.23.1(8), Tag 03DL, of the Stacks Project. It is also equivalent to finite presentation on this site. Every coherent module has a global finite free presentation, and every finite strict-domain covering has a norm-strict exact augmented \v Cech complex for it, with the canonical norms on its finite section modules.}
{给出有限 $A$-模与 $\mathcal D_X$ 上凝聚 $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{str}}$-模之间互逆的正合范畴等价。这里凝聚性采用 Stacks Project 的 Tag 03DL、Definition 18.23.1(8)。在这个 site 上，它也等价于有限表示。每个凝聚模都有整体有限自由表示，且对每个有限 strict 域覆盖，它的增广 \v Cech 复形在各有限截面模的 canonical 范数下 norm-strict 正合。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictdomainsheafcomparison} identifies the ringed topoi of $\mathcal D_X$ and $\mathcal R_X$. Coherence and finite presentation are intrinsic under this equivalence, by \cite[Tag 03DM, Lemma 18.23.2]{StacksAlg}; the special cocontinuous comparison required in that lemma is precisely the one just verified. Theorem~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalkiehl} therefore identifies the coherent modules with the sheaves $\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$ for finite $A$-modules $M$. Under the equivalence these are exactly $\widetilde M^{\mathrm{str}}$. Full faithfulness and the global-sections inverse were already proved in Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictdomainsheafcomparison}, as was exactness. A finite presentation of $M$ over the Noetherian ring $A$ gives a global finite free presentation of its sheaf. Finally apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictaffinoidcovercech} to $M$. Its terms are exactly the section modules of $\mathcal F\simeq\widetilde M^{\mathrm{str}}$, proving the last assertion.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictdomainsheafcomparison}，$\mathcal D_X$ 与 $\mathcal R_X$ 的赋环 topoi 等价。由 \cite[Tag 03DM, Lemma 18.23.2]{StacksAlg}，凝聚性与有限表示在此等价下保持；该引理要求的 special cocontinuous 比较，恰是刚才核验的比较。因此，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalkiehl}，凝聚模恰好对应于有限 $A$-模 $M$ 所给的 $\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$。在上述等价下，它们恰好对应于 $\widetilde M^{\mathrm{str}}$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictdomainsheafcomparison} 已证明全忠实性、整体截面逆函子及正合性。由于 $A$ Noetherian，$M$ 的有限表示给出其层的整体有限自由表示。最后对 $M$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictaffinoidcovercech}；复形的各项恰为 $\mathcal F\simeq\widetilde M^{\mathrm{str}}$ 的截面模，故得到最后的断言。}
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[\bi{The finite strict-domain site}{有限 strict 域 site}]\label{fnd:finitestrictdomainsite}
\bi{For $K$ complete, with trivial valuation allowed, and $X=\mathcal M(A)$ strict, let $\mathcal D_X$ be the partially ordered category of strict-test domains in $X$, with inclusions as arrows. Coverings are finite set-theoretic coverings by objects, including the empty covering of the empty set. Choose representing algebras $A_V$, with $A_X=A$ and $A_\varnothing=0$. Use the unique bounded restrictions of Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalrestrictiondomainbridge}, and set}
{设 $K$ 完备，允许平凡赋值，$X=\mathcal M(A)$ 为 strict。令 $\mathcal D_X$ 为 $X$ 中 strict-test 域构成的偏序范畴，箭头为包含。覆盖为由对象组成的有限集合覆盖，包括空集的空覆盖。选择表示代数 $A_V$，其中 $A_X=A$、$A_\varnothing=0$。使用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalrestrictiondomainbridge} 给出的唯一有界限制，并记}
\[
 \mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{str}}(V)=A_V,
 \qquad \widetilde M^{\mathrm{str}}(V)=A_V\otimes_A M
 \quad(M\text{ finite over }A).
\]
\end{definition}
