\begin{corollary}[\bi{Theorem B on the specified affinoid sites}{所述 affinoid sites 上的 Theorem B}]\label{fnd:strictsitetheoremb}
\bi{Let $K$ be complete, with trivial valuation allowed, and let $A$ be strict. If $\mathcal F$ is a coherent module on the fixed rational ringed site $\mathcal R_X$, then}
{设 $K$ 完备，允许平凡赋值，且 $A$ 为 strict。若 $\mathcal F$ 是固定 rational 赋环 site $\mathcal R_X$ 上的凝聚模，则}
\[
 H^q(V,\mathcal F)=0
 \qquad(V\in\mathcal R_X,\ q>0).
\]
\bi{For the same complete field $K$, including trivial valuation, the same assertion holds for every coherent module on $\mathcal D_X$ and every $V\in\mathcal D_X$. Here $H^q$ denotes the right derived functors of sections in the category of abelian sheaves of the specified site, applied to the underlying abelian sheaf.}
{对同一个完备域 $K$，包括平凡赋值，相同结论对 $\mathcal D_X$ 上的每个凝聚模及每个 $V\in\mathcal D_X$ 成立。这里 $H^q$ 指所述 site 的阿贝尔层范畴中截面函子的右导出函子，作用于相应的底层阿贝尔层。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For $\mathcal R_X$, Theorem~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalkiehl} writes $\mathcal F=\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$ for a finite $A$-module. Lemma~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalslicemodules} gives vanishing of higher \v Cech cohomology for every covering of every object. For $\mathcal D_X$, Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictdomainkiehl} gives the same conclusion. Apply \cite[Tag 03F9, Lemma 21.10.9]{StacksAlg}, with its set $\mathcal B$ equal to all objects and its family $\mathrm{Cov}$ equal to all the finite coverings in the chosen pretopology. These coverings are a cofinal system by definition of the site. All iterated fibre products required by that lemma are intersections and remain in the site, by Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation} or Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttestdomainintersections}, respectively. Thus all three hypotheses of the cited lemma hold, and it gives the claimed derived vanishing in every positive degree. This application uses the general site lemma, not the different theorem about open covers of a topological space.}
{对 $\mathcal R_X$，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalkiehl}，可写 $\mathcal F=\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$，其中 $M$ 为有限 $A$-模。Lemma~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalslicemodules} 给出每个对象的每个覆盖上的高次 \v Cech 上同调消失。对 $\mathcal D_X$，Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictdomainkiehl} 给出相同结论。应用 \cite[Tag 03F9, Lemma 21.10.9]{StacksAlg}，其中 $\mathcal B$ 取全部对象，$\mathrm{Cov}$ 取所选预拓扑中的全部有限覆盖。由 site 的定义，这些覆盖构成余终系统。该引理要求的全部迭代纤维积就是交叠，并分别由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation} 或 Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttestdomainintersections} 保留在该 site 中。因此，所引引理的三项假设全部成立，得到每个正次数上的导出消失。此处使用的是一般 site 引理，而不是拓扑空间开覆盖的另一版本。}
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[\bi{The finite strict-domain site}{有限 strict 域 site}]\label{fnd:finitestrictdomainsite}
\bi{For $K$ complete, with trivial valuation allowed, and $X=\mathcal M(A)$ strict, let $\mathcal D_X$ be the partially ordered category of strict-test domains in $X$, with inclusions as arrows. Coverings are finite set-theoretic coverings by objects, including the empty covering of the empty set. Choose representing algebras $A_V$, with $A_X=A$ and $A_\varnothing=0$. Use the unique bounded restrictions of Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalrestrictiondomainbridge}, and set}
{设 $K$ 完备，允许平凡赋值，$X=\mathcal M(A)$ 为 strict。令 $\mathcal D_X$ 为 $X$ 中 strict-test 域构成的偏序范畴，箭头为包含。覆盖为由对象组成的有限集合覆盖，包括空集的空覆盖。选择表示代数 $A_V$，其中 $A_X=A$、$A_\varnothing=0$。使用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalrestrictiondomainbridge} 给出的唯一有界限制，并记}
\[
 \mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{str}}(V)=A_V,
 \qquad \widetilde M^{\mathrm{str}}(V)=A_V\otimes_A M
 \quad(M\text{ finite over }A).
\]
\end{definition}
