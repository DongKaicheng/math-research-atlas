Geometric multiplicity and its invariance

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 884

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Geometric multiplicity and its invariance}{几何重数及其不变性}]
\label{cand:geometricmultiplicity}
\bi{The weight is a positive integer. It is independent of the chosen weight-realizing field, translating point, and lattice splitting. It equals the sum of the generic lengths of the irreducible components of the initial scheme after a residue extension splitting its finite transverse algebra. In particular it equals the usual sum of geometric generic lengths over an algebraic closure.}
{该权重为正整数，与实现权重的扩域、平移点及格分裂的选择无关。将剩余域扩张到使有限横截代数分裂的域后，它等于初始概形各不可约分支的一般点局部环长度之和。特别地，它等于代数闭包上通常的几何一般长度之和。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Let $A=\Gamma(Z_\sigma,\mathcal O)$, a nonzero finite-dimensional $k$-algebra. Finite-dimensional scalar extension preserves its dimension. A finite field extension $k'/k$ splits the characteristic polynomials of a finite set of algebra generators of $A$. Every residue field of $A\otimes_k k'$ is then $k'$, because the images of those generators are roots in $k'$. Choose a composition series by successively taking a proper submodule of maximal vector-space dimension. Its factors are the one-dimensional residue fields. Localizing the series at each maximal ideal counts each factor exactly once, and gives}
{令 $A=\Gamma(Z_\sigma,\mathcal O)$，它是非零有限维 $k$-代数。有限维标量扩张保持其维数。取有限扩域 $k'/k$，使 $A$ 的有限组代数生成元的乘法特征多项式均分裂；则 $A\otimes_k k'$ 的每个剩余域都等于 $k'$，因为各生成元的像均为 $k'$ 中的根。逐次取向量空间维数最大的真子模，得到组成列，其因子均为一维剩余域。在各极大理想处局部化此列，每个因子恰被计算一次，于是}
\[
 \dim_kA=\dim_{k'}(A\otimes_k k')
   =\sum_z\operatorname{length}\bigl((A\otimes_k k')_z\bigr).
\]
\bi{Adjoining Laurent variables and localizing at a generic point tensors the local Artinian factor with the rational function field of the corresponding torus. Its composition series tensors to a composition series with the same number of simple factors. Thus the displayed summands are exactly the generic lengths of $Y_{k'}$. The intrinsic quotient description in Proposition~\ref{cand:initialtangenttorus} makes the lattice splitting irrelevant. Theorem~\ref{cand:realinitialdegeneration} compares all other choices by residue extension and a torus translation; translation preserves the tangent subtorus and induces a translation on its quotient. The same finite dimension is therefore obtained from every choice.}
{添加 Laurent 变量并在一般点处局部化，将局部 Artinian 因子与相应环面的有理函数域张量。其组成列随之成为具有相同单因子数目的组成列。因此上述各项恰为 $Y_{k'}$ 的一般长度。由 Proposition~\ref{cand:initialtangenttorus} 的内在商描述，格分裂不影响结果。Theorem~\ref{cand:realinitialdegeneration} 以剩余扩域及环面平移比较其余选择；平移保持切向子环面，并诱导商上的平移。因此所有选择均给出相同有限维数。}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[\bi{Finite filtrations and zero-dimensional algebras}{有限滤过与零维代数}]
\label{cand:finiteprimefiltration}
\bi{Every finite module over a Noetherian ring has a finite filtration whose nonzero successive quotients are $A/\mathfrak p$ for prime ideals $\mathfrak p$. A zero-dimensional algebra of finite type over a field $k$ is finite-dimensional over $k$.}
{Noetherian 环上的每个有限模具有有限滤过，其非零相继商均形如 $A/\mathfrak p$，其中 $\mathfrak p$ 为素理想。域 $k$ 上的有限型零维代数在 $k$ 上有限维。}
\end{lemma}
