The full finite flat cycle

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1837

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[The full finite flat cycle]\label{f9be:transfer}
Let $q:Y\to Z$ be finite locally free of constant rank $n$, with
$Z$ integral and Noetherian. Every irreducible component of $Y$
dominates $Z$. For its generic points $\eta_j$, write
\[
 m_j=\len_{\cO_{Y,\eta_j}}\cO_{Y,\eta_j}.
\]
Then, with full field degrees,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cycle}
 \sum_j m_j[\kappa(\eta_j):K(Z)]=n,\qquad
 q_*[Y]=n[Z].
\end{equation}
Here $[Y]=\sum_jm_j[\overline{\{\eta_j\}}]$ is the full
fundamental cycle.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
On an affine open put $Z=\Spec B$ and $Y=\Spec C$.
The finite locally free $B$-module $C$ is torsion free.
If a nonzero $b\in B$ belonged to a minimal prime $\mathfrak q$
of $C$, its image in the zero-dimensional Noetherian local ring
$C_{\mathfrak q}$ would be nilpotent; this ring is Artinian
(\st{00KH}). Clearing a denominator gives $s b^r=0$ for some
$s\notin\mathfrak q$, contradicting torsion freeness.
Thus every minimal prime lies over $(0)$.
Conversely a prime of $C$ over $(0)$ is minimal: after localization
it is a prime in the finite-dimensional $K(Z)$-algebra
$C\otimes_BK(Z)$, whose primes are all maximal.
Its local factors are precisely $C_{\mathfrak q}$.

This finite-dimensional algebra has dimension $n$ and is the product
of these Artinian local factors (\st{00JB}). A composition series of
one factor has $m_j$ quotients equal to $\kappa(\eta_j)$.
Taking dimensions proves the sum in \eqref{eq:cycle}.
The definition of proper cycle pushforward, \st{02R4}, proves the
second identity. In particular, inseparable degrees and generic
nilpotent lengths are separate factors and both are retained.
\end{proof}
