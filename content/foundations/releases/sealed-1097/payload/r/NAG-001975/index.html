Complete scalar extension of a germ reduction

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 571

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Complete scalar extension of a germ reduction}
{芽约化的完备标量扩张}]
\label{fnd:gradedgermscalar}
\bi{Let $L/K$ be a complete valued extension and let
$y\in X_L$ map to $x\in X$. Put
$F=\widetilde{\mathcal H(y)}^{\rm gr}$. Then the reduction of
$(X_L,y)$ is the pullback of $\mathscr R(X,x)$ along}
{设 $L/K$ 为完备赋值扩张，$y\in X_L$ 映到 $x\in X$，
置 $F=\widetilde{\mathcal H(y)}^{\rm gr}$。则
$(X_L,y)$ 的约化是 $\mathscr R(X,x)$ 沿下列映射的拉回：}
\[
 \mathscr P_{\widetilde L^{\rm gr}}(F)
       \longrightarrow
 \mathscr P_{\widetilde K^{\rm gr}}
                  (\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}^{\rm gr}).
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{On an affinoid piece, the second finite comparison map of
Corollary~\ref{fnd:gradedtensorfinitecomparison} identifies,
up to a finite integral extension, the image of
$\widetilde{A_L}^{\rm gr}$ in $F$ with the subalgebra generated
by the images of $\widetilde A^{\rm gr}$ and
$\widetilde L^{\rm gr}$. Every valuation under consideration
already contains the latter field. The integral criterion
therefore gives exactly the displayed inverse image.
Affinoid subdomain-germ covers and their overlaps commute
with scalar extension, so
Theorem~\ref{fnd:allgradedgermreduction} glues the identities.}
{在 affinoid 片上，
Corollary~\ref{fnd:gradedtensorfinitecomparison} 的第二个
有限比较映射说明：$\widetilde{A_L}^{\rm gr}$ 在 $F$ 中的
像，是 $\widetilde A^{\rm gr}$ 与 $\widetilde L^{\rm gr}$
的像生成的子代数的有限整扩张。所考虑的赋值已包含后一个域，
因此整性判据恰好给出上述逆像。
Affinoid 子域芽覆盖及其重叠与标量扩张相容，故可用
Theorem~\ref{fnd:allgradedgermreduction} 粘合这些恒等式。}
\end{proof}
