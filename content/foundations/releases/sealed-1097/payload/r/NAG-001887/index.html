Flat relative Tate algebras

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 428

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Flat relative Tate algebras}{相对 Tate 代数的平坦性}]
\label{fnd:relativepolydiscalgebraflat}
\bi{Let $A$ be a generalized affinoid algebra over a complete
non-Archimedean field, and let $r=(r_1,\ldots,r_n)$ be positive real
radii. Then $A\to B=A\{r^{-1}T\}$ is faithfully flat as a map of rings.}
{设 $A$ 为完备非阿基米德域上的广义 affinoid 代数，
$r=(r_1,\ldots,r_n)$ 为正实半径。则环同态
$A\to B=A\{r^{-1}T\}$ 忠实平坦。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For every finite $A$-module $M$, Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedcoefficienttensor}
identifies $B\otimes_A M$ with the convergent weighted coefficient
module $C_r(\mathbf N^n,M)$, functorially in $M$. The same theorem
preserves injections of finite modules. Since $A$ is Noetherian by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}, every finitely generated ideal
$I$ is a finite module; applying the assertion to $I\hookrightarrow A$
proves that $I\otimes_A B\to B$ is injective. The finite-ideal
criterion \cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg} gives flatness.}
{对每个有限 $A$-模 $M$，Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedcoefficienttensor}
函子性地将 $B\otimes_A M$ 识别为收敛加权系数模
$C_r(\mathbf N^n,M)$，且同一定理保持有限模的单射。由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}，$A$ 是 Noetherian 环，
故每个有限生成理想 $I$ 为有限模。应用于 $I\hookrightarrow A$，
得到 $I\otimes_A B\to B$ 单射。由有限理想判别准则
\cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg}，得到平坦性。}

\bi{The constant coefficient map $p_0:B\to A$ is an $A$-linear
retraction of the coefficient inclusion. For every $A$-module $N$,
the map $N\to B\otimes_A N$, $m\mapsto1\otimes m$, is consequently
split by $p_0\otimes1$. Thus tensoring with $B$ detects nonzero
modules, which proves faithfulness. Only the linear retraction is
used here; multiplicativity is unnecessary.}
{常数项映射 $p_0:B\to A$ 是系数嵌入的 $A$-线性回缩。
所以对每个 $A$-模 $N$，映射 $N\to B\otimes_A N$，
$m\mapsto1\otimes m$，由 $p_0\otimes1$ 分裂。
因此与 $B$ 张量可以检测非零模，得到忠实性。此处只使用线性回缩，
无须乘法相容性。}
\end{proof}
