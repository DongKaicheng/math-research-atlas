Presentations after a free-radius extension

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 104

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Presentations after a free-radius extension}{自由半径扩张后的表示}]\label{fnd:freeextensionpresentation}
\bi{Let $A$ be a norm-admissible generalized $K$-affinoid with a specified presentation $D=K\{s^{-1}X\}\twoheadrightarrow A$ and closed kernel $I$. Let $r$ be any finite tuple free over $K$, put $L=K_r$, and put $A_r=A\widehat\otimes_K L$, $D_r=D\widehat\otimes_K L$. Then}
{设 $A$ 为 norm-admissible generalized $K$-affinoid，指定表示为 $D=K\{s^{-1}X\}\twoheadrightarrow A$，其核 $I$ 闭。设 $r$ 为任意在 $K$ 上自由的有限元组，令 $L=K_r$、$A_r=A\widehat\otimes_K L$、$D_r=D\widehat\otimes_K L$。则}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 D_r&\simeq L\{s^{-1}X\},\\
 \ker(D_r\to A_r)&=\mathcal C_{r^\nu}(\mathbf Z^d,I)
                       =\overline{I D_r},\\
 A_r&\simeq L\{s^{-1}X\}/\overline{I L\{s^{-1}X\}}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{The first map is isometric. The quotient map induces precisely the coefficient norm on $A_r$ if $A$ has the displayed quotient norm; for any given uniformly equivalent norm the comparison has the same two constants. Thus $A_r$ is generalized $L$-affinoid. It is also generalized $K$-affinoid and has the actual relative presentation}
{第一个映射等距。若 $A$ 使用所列商范数，则商映射在 $A_r$ 上诱导的恰为系数范数；对任意给定的一致等价范数，比较保留相同的两个常数。因此 $A_r$ 为 generalized $L$-affinoid。它也为 generalized $K$-affinoid，且具有实际相对表示}
\[
 A_r\simeq A\{r^{-1}T,rU\}/(T_iU_i-1)_{1\leq i\leq d}.
\]
\bi{This last identification is isometric for the specified norm of $A$. These constructions are natural for bounded $K$-algebra maps, so every finite commutative diagram of such maps extends to a commutative diagram of bounded $L$-algebra maps. No strictification of $A_r$ is asserted for an arbitrary choice of $r$.}
{最后这一识别对 $A$ 的指定范数等距。这些构造对有界 $K$-代数映射自然，因而这样的每个有限交换图都扩张为有界 $L$-代数映射的交换图。对任意选择的 $r$，这里不声称 $A_r$ 已为 strict。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients} identifies $D_r$ with $\mathcal C_{r^\nu}(\mathbf Z^d,D)$. Applying Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedc0rearrangement} to the $X$- and Laurent coefficients gives}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients}，$D_r$ 与 $\mathcal C_{r^\nu}(\mathbf Z^d,D)$ 等距。对 $X$ 系数与 Laurent 系数应用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedc0rearrangement}，得到}
\[
 \mathcal C_{r^\nu}\bigl(\mathbf Z^d,
      \mathcal C_{s^\alpha}(\mathbf N^n,K)\bigr)
 \simeq
 \mathcal C_{s^\alpha}\bigl(\mathbf N^n,
      \mathcal C_{r^\nu}(\mathbf Z^d,K)\bigr)
 =L\{s^{-1}X\}.
\]
\bi{On finite coefficient polynomials this is the identity on the monomials $X^\alpha T^\nu$. The convolution bounds show that it is an isometric algebra isomorphism. In particular it agrees with the algebra structure on the separated completed tensor: the product on finite tensors has the projective bound, and the coefficient description sends it to the same product on a dense subspace. The $L$-action and its homogeneous norm are those of Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients}.}
{在有限系数多项式上，这是单项式 $X^\alpha T^\nu$ 上的恒等映射。卷积界说明它为等距代数同构。特别地，它与分离完备张量积上的代数结构一致：有限张量上的乘积满足 projective 范数界，而系数描述在稠密子空间上将其送到同一乘积。$L$-作用及其齐次范数正是 Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients} 所给的结构。}

\bi{First give $A$ its quotient norm from $D$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation}, applied with $\Gamma=\mathbf Z^d$ and weight $r^\nu$, proves the kernel equality and exact quotient norm. The same coefficient isometry identifies the target with $A_r$. Restoring the original norm of $A$ preserves the kernel and changes norms by precisely its uniform comparison constants. This gives the displayed generalized $L$-affinoid presentation without assuming any ideal of $D$ other than the specified $I$ to be closed.}
{先给 $A$ 配备来自 $D$ 的商范数。对 $\Gamma=\mathbf Z^d$、权重 $r^\nu$ 应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation}，即得核等式及精确商范数。同一系数等距同构将目标识别为 $A_r$。换回 $A$ 的原范数时，核不变，而范数比较恰由原先的一致比较常数控制。这样得到所列 generalized $L$-affinoid 表示，并未假设 $D$ 的其他理想已闭。}

\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:multilaurentactualpresentation}, with coefficient algebra $A$, gives the actual relative relation quotient, including its closed kernel and exact norm. By Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation}, $A\{r^{-1}T,rU\}$ is generalized $K$-affinoid and its quotient by this closed ideal is again generalized. Finally, a bounded algebra map acts on every Laurent coefficient with its original bound, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients}. It respects products on finite Laurent sums and therefore on their limits. Identities and compositions are coefficientwise, proving the diagram assertion.}
{以 $A$ 为系数代数，Theorem~\ref{fnd:multilaurentactualpresentation} 给出实际相对关系商，包括其闭核及精确范数。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation}，$A\{r^{-1}T,rU\}$ 为 generalized $K$-affinoid，按这一闭理想取商后仍为 generalized。最后，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients}，有界代数映射逐 Laurent 系数作用，并保有原范数界。它在有限 Laurent 和上保持乘积，因此在其极限上也保持。恒等映射与复合均逐系数计算，由此得到交换图断言。}
\end{proof}
