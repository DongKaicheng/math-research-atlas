Global generation and GAGA on projective space

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 406

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Global generation and GAGA on projective space}
{射影空间上的全局生成与 GAGA}]
\label{fnd:projectivegagainternal}
\bi{For every complete non-Archimedean field \(K\),
analytification is an equivalence}
{对每个完备非阿基米德域 \(K\)，解析化给出范畴等价}
\[
 \operatorname{Coh}(\mathbf P^n_K)
       \ \simeq\ \operatorname{Coh}((\mathbf P^n_K)^{\an}_G).
\]
\bi{For every coherent analytic \(\mathcal F\), the twist
\(\mathcal F(m)\) is generated by finitely many global
sections for all sufficiently large \(m\), and
\(H^q(P_G,\mathcal F(m))=0\) for every \(q>0\) and all
sufficiently large \(m\).}
{对每个凝聚解析层 \(\mathcal F\)，当 \(m\) 充分大时，
\(\mathcal F(m)\) 由有限个全局截面生成；并且对所有
\(q>0\)，当 \(m\) 充分大时，
\(H^q(P_G,\mathcal F(m))=0\)。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{We prove the three assertions together by induction on
\(n\), for all complete fields simultaneously. For \(n=0\)
they are the finite-module description of the one-point
affinoid \(\mathcal M(K)\) and its Theorem B. Suppose
\(n>0\) and that the assertions hold in dimension \(n-1\).}
{对所有完备域同时按 \(n\) 归纳，证明三个断言。
\(n=0\) 时，它们就是单点 affinoid \(\mathcal M(K)\)
的有限模描述及 Theorem B。以下设 \(n>0\)，并假设
\(n-1\) 维的三个断言成立。}

\bi{\textbf{Generation at a rational point.}
Fix a \(K\)-rational point \(x\) and a hyperplane \(H\)
through it, defined by a nonzero linear form \(h\).
Set \(\mathcal C=\ker(\mathcal F(-1)\xrightarrow{h}\mathcal F)\)
and \(\mathcal B=\mathcal F/h\mathcal F(-1)\).
Both are coherent and killed by the ideal of \(H\).
Lemma~\ref{fnd:closedanalyticcoherentextension} identifies
them with coherent sheaves on \(H^{\an}\).
Writing \(\mathcal R_m=h\mathcal F(m-1)\), we have
short exact sequences}
{\textbf{有理点处的生成。}
固定一个 \(K\)-有理点 \(x\)，并取经过它的超平面 \(H\)，
由非零线性形式 \(h\) 定义。置
\(\mathcal C=\ker(\mathcal F(-1)\xrightarrow{h}\mathcal F)\)、
\(\mathcal B=\mathcal F/h\mathcal F(-1)\)。
二者均凝聚且被 \(H\) 的理想消灭；由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:closedanalyticcoherentextension}，
它们可视为 \(H^{\an}\) 上的凝聚层。
记 \(\mathcal R_m=h\mathcal F(m-1)\)，则有短正合列}
\[
\begin{split}
 0&\longrightarrow\mathcal C(m)\longrightarrow\mathcal F(m-1)
       \longrightarrow\mathcal R_m\longrightarrow0,\\
 0&\longrightarrow\mathcal R_m\longrightarrow\mathcal F(m)
       \longrightarrow\mathcal B(m)\longrightarrow0 .
\end{split}
\]
\bi{By induction, \(H^2(\mathcal C(m))=H^1(\mathcal B(m))=0\)
for large \(m\). The cohomology groups here can be computed
either on \(H^{\an}\) or after direct image on \(P\), by
the preceding lemma. Their long exact sequences give
surjections}
{由归纳假设，当 \(m\) 充分大时，
\(H^2(\mathcal C(m))=H^1(\mathcal B(m))=0\)。
由前一引理，这里的群可以在 \(H^{\an}\) 上计算，也可以
直接像后在 \(P\) 上计算。两个长正合列给出满射}
\[
 H^1(\mathcal F(m-1))\twoheadrightarrow H^1(\mathcal R_m)
                 \twoheadrightarrow H^1(\mathcal F(m)).
\]
\bi{All these spaces are finite dimensional by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:projectivecoherentfiniteness}.
The nonincreasing sequence
\(\dim_K H^1(\mathcal F(m))\) is eventually constant.
For those \(m\), both displayed surjections are isomorphisms.
The second short exact sequence then gives a surjection
\(H^0(\mathcal F(m))\twoheadrightarrow H^0(\mathcal B(m))\).
By induction the latter sections generate \(\mathcal B(m)\)
for large \(m\), so their lifts generate the fibre of
\(\mathcal F(m)\) at \(x\).}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:projectivecoherentfiniteness}，
这些空间均有限维。非增整数序列
\(\dim_K H^1(\mathcal F(m))\) 最终为常数。
因此当 \(m\) 足够大时，上述两个满射都为同构。
第二个短正合列于是给出满射
\(H^0(\mathcal F(m))\twoheadrightarrow H^0(\mathcal B(m))\)。
由归纳假设，右边的截面在充分大的扭转后生成
\(\mathcal B(m)\)，所以其提升生成 \(\mathcal F(m)\)
在 \(x\) 处的纤维。}

\bi{\textbf{All analytic points and a uniform twist.}
For an arbitrary \(x\in P\), set \(L=\mathcal H(x)\).
The canonical \(L\)-rational point of \(P_L\) above \(x\)
is defined by the valued residue-field map. Apply the
rational-point argument over \(L\). By
Proposition~\ref{fnd:projectivecoherentbasechange},
all global sections upstairs are \(L\)-linear combinations
of global sections downstairs. The fibre of the evaluation
cokernel upstairs is exactly its downstairs fibre tensored
with \(L=\mathcal H(x)\). It is zero. Thus a sufficiently
large twist is generated at every analytic point \(x\),
with the twist initially depending on \(x\).}
{\textbf{所有解析点与统一扭转。}
对任意 \(x\in P\)，置 \(L=\mathcal H(x)\)。
赋值剩余域映射在 \(P_L\) 中给出位于 \(x\) 上方的典范
\(L\)-有理点。对它应用 \(L\) 上的有理点论证。
由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:projectivecoherentbasechange}，
上方的所有全局截面都是下方全局截面的 \(L\)-线性组合。
上方取值余核的纤维，就是下方余核与 \(L=\mathcal H(x)\)
张量所得的纤维，因而为零。故充分大的扭转在每个解析点
\(x\) 处生成，但此时扭转次数可能依赖于 \(x\)。}

\bi{We justify the passage from fibres to open generation
loci. For a finite module \(M\) over an affinoid algebra
\(A\), its fibre at a point with prime kernel
\(\mathfrak p\) vanishes exactly when \(M_{\mathfrak p}=0\):
extension from \(\kappa(\mathfrak p)\) to \(\mathcal H(x)\)
is faithful, and the finite-module Nakayama lemma applies
to \(A_{\mathfrak p}\). This lemma follows directly from
the determinant argument: if finitely many generators
are linear combinations of themselves with coefficients
in the maximal ideal, the determinant of one minus that
coefficient matrix is a unit annihilating the module.
If \(M_{\mathfrak p}=0\), clearing denominators of a finite
generating list gives \(a\notin\mathfrak p\) with \(aM=0\).
Thus the zero-fibre locus is open.}
{以下说明怎样从纤维得到开生成轨迹。对 affinoid 代数
\(A\) 上的有限模 \(M\)，在素核为 \(\mathfrak p\) 的点处，
其纤维为零当且仅当 \(M_{\mathfrak p}=0\)：
由 \(\kappa(\mathfrak p)\) 到 \(\mathcal H(x)\) 的扩域
忠实，再对 \(A_{\mathfrak p}\) 使用有限模 Nakayama 引理。
此引理直接来自行列式论证：若有限个生成元均可用极大理想
中的系数线性表示为自身，则一减此系数矩阵的行列式是一个
消灭该模的单位。若 \(M_{\mathfrak p}=0\)，对有限个生成元
清分母，可取 \(a\notin\mathfrak p\) 使 \(aM=0\)；
因此零纤维轨迹为开集。}

\bi{The evaluation map uses a finite basis of
\(H^0(P_G,\mathcal F(m))\), so its cokernel is coherent.
On each member of the finite closed unit cover its
nonzero-fibre locus is a closed subset; the finite union
is closed in \(P\). Therefore the generation locus \(G_m\)
is open in \(P\). Moreover \(G_m\subseteq G_{m+1}\):
at any point choose \(X_i\) nonzero, and multiply generating
sections by \(X_i\), which is a unit after trivializing
the twist there. The \(G_m\) cover the compact space \(P\).
A finite subcover and this monotonicity give one \(m_0\)
with \(G_m=P\) for every \(m\geq m_0\).
The evaluation cokernel is then zero on each affinoid,
since a nonzero finite module has a nonzero localization
at some maximal ideal. This proves global generation.}
{取 \(H^0(P_G,\mathcal F(m))\) 的有限基，取值映射的余核
便是凝聚层。在有限闭单位覆盖的每个成员上，其非零纤维
轨迹闭；有限并在 \(P\) 中仍闭。因此生成轨迹 \(G_m\)
在 \(P\) 中开。并且 \(G_m\subseteq G_{m+1}\)：
在每点选择不为零的 \(X_i\)，将生成截面乘以 \(X_i\)；
在该处平凡化扭转层后，它就是一个单位。
\(G_m\) 覆盖紧空间 \(P\)，故有限子覆盖及单调性给出一个
\(m_0\)，使所有 \(m\geq m_0\) 都满足 \(G_m=P\)。
此时取值余核在每个 affinoid 上为零，因为非零有限模总在
某个极大理想处具有非零局部化。全局生成得证。}

\bi{\textbf{Algebraization and vanishing.}
Global generation gives a surjection
\(\mathcal O_P(-a)^s\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal F\).
Its kernel is coherent by the affinoid finite-module
theorem. Apply global generation again to that kernel,
obtaining a presentation}
{\textbf{代数化与消失。}
全局生成给出满射
\(\mathcal O_P(-a)^s\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal F\)。
由 affinoid 有限模定理，其核凝聚。再次对这个核应用
全局生成，得到表示}
\[
 \mathcal O_P(-b)^t\xrightarrow{u}
       \mathcal O_P(-a)^s\longrightarrow\mathcal F
       \longrightarrow0 .
\]
\bi{By Corollary~\ref{fnd:projectiveanalytificationfullyfaithful},
the map \(u\) is the analytification of a unique algebraic
map between the corresponding sums of twists. Its
algebraic cokernel \(\mathcal E\) is coherent and satisfies
\(\mathcal E^{\an}\simeq\mathcal F\) by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact}.
This proves essential surjectivity; full faithfulness was
already proved in that corollary. Finally, apply
\cite[Tag 01YS, Lemma 30.14.1(4)]{StacksAlg} to
\(\mathcal E\), and transport the resulting vanishing
by Theorem~\ref{fnd:projectivecoherentcomparison}.
This proves the remaining assertion and completes the
simultaneous induction.}
{由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:projectiveanalytificationfullyfaithful}，
\(u\) 是相应扭转层直和之间唯一代数映射的解析化。
其代数余核 \(\mathcal E\) 凝聚，且由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact}，
\(\mathcal E^{\an}\simeq\mathcal F\)。这证明本质满射；
全忠实性已由上述推论证明。最后，对 \(\mathcal E\) 应用
\cite[Tag 01YS, Lemma 30.14.1(4)]{StacksAlg}，
再由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:projectivecoherentcomparison}
搬运所得消失结论，即得剩余断言，同时归纳完成。}
\end{proof}
