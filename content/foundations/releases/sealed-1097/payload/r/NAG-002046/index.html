Extension of an isomorphism of stable generic fibres

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 658

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Extension of an isomorphism of stable generic fibres}{稳定泛纤维同构的延拓}]
\label{cand:stableisomvaluation}
\bi{Let $R$ be a valuation ring with fraction field $K$, and let $\mathcal C_1/R$ and $\mathcal C_2/R$ be stable families of the same genus $g\geq2$. Every isomorphism $(\mathcal C_1)_K\simeq(\mathcal C_2)_K$ extends uniquely to an $R$-isomorphism $\mathcal C_1\simeq\mathcal C_2$.}
{设 $R$ 为分式域为 $K$ 的赋值环，$\mathcal C_1/R$ 与 $\mathcal C_2/R$ 为同一亏格 $g\geq2$ 的稳定族。每个同构 $(\mathcal C_1)_K\simeq(\mathcal C_2)_K$ 都唯一延拓为 $R$-同构 $\mathcal C_1\simeq\mathcal C_2$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The moduli stack $\overline{\mathcal M}_g$ is separated by \cite[Tag 0E9A]{StacksAlg}. By the definition of separatedness for stacks, \cite[Tag 04YW]{StacksAlg}, its diagonal is proper. Pulling this diagonal back by the pair $(\mathcal C_1,\mathcal C_2)$ gives the algebraic space}
{由 \cite[Tag 0E9A]{StacksAlg}，模叠 $\overline{\mathcal M}_g$ 分离。按照代数叠分离性的定义 \cite[Tag 04YW]{StacksAlg}，其对角态射 proper。沿 $(\mathcal C_1,\mathcal C_2)$ 拉回该对角态射，得到代数空间}
\[
 I=\operatorname{Isom}_R(\mathcal C_1,\mathcal C_2)
       \longrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}R;
\]
\bi{the identification with the isomorphism sheaf is the fibre-product description in \cite[Tag 04XS]{StacksAlg}. The morphism $I\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ is proper by base change. A specified generic isomorphism is a $K$-point of $I$. The proper algebraic-space valuative criterion, \cite[Tag 0A40]{StacksAlg}, extends it uniquely to an $R$-point of $I$, which is precisely the required isomorphism.}
{它与同构层的识别由 \cite[Tag 04XS]{StacksAlg} 中的纤维积描述给出。由基变换，$I\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ proper。指定的泛纤维同构是 $I$ 的一个 $K$-点。Proper 代数空间的赋值判据 \cite[Tag 0A40]{StacksAlg} 将它唯一延拓为 $I$ 的 $R$-点，这恰好就是所需同构。}
\end{proof}
