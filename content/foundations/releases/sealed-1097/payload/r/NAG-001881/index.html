An outer collar at the Gauss point

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 476

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{An outer collar at the Gauss point}
{Gauss 点处的外侧邻域}]
\label{fnd:gausspointoutercollar}
\bi{Let $D=\{|T|\leq1\}\subset\mathbb A_K^{1,\mathrm{an}}$
and let $\eta$ be its Gauss point. For every ordinary open
neighborhood $U$ of $\eta$, there is $R>1$ with
$D_R=\{|T|\leq R\}\subset D\cup U$.
If $f$ is analytic on $D$ and an analytic function on $U$
agrees with it on $U\cap D$, then $f$ extends analytically
to some such $D_R$.}
{设 $D=\{|T|\leq1\}\subset\mathbb A_K^{1,\mathrm{an}}$，
其 Gauss 点记为 $\eta$。对 $\eta$ 的每个普通开邻域 $U$，
存在 $R>1$ 使 $D_R=\{|T|\leq R\}\subset D\cup U$。
若 $f$ 在 $D$ 上解析，且 $U$ 上某个解析函数在 $U\cap D$
上等于 $f$，则 $f$ 解析延拓到某个这样的 $D_R$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose $R_0>1$ and work inside the closed disc $D_{R_0}$.
Polynomials are uniformly dense in its affinoid algebra.
The evaluation topology and this uniform approximation imply
that finitely many strict inequalities involving polynomial
absolute values define a neighborhood of $\eta$ contained
in $U\cap D_{R_0}$. For a nonzero polynomial
$P=\sum_{i=0}^m a_iT^i$, put $M=\max_i|a_i|$ and choose
the largest index $j$ with $|a_j|=M$. For every $s>1$
sufficiently close to one,}
{选择 $R_0>1$，在闭圆盘 $D_{R_0}$ 中工作。
多项式在其 affinoid 代数中一致稠密。
取值拓扑与此一致逼近说明，可用有限个多项式绝对值的严格
不等式定义 $\eta$ 的邻域，并使其包含于 $U\cap D_{R_0}$。
对非零多项式 $P=\sum_{i=0}^m a_iT^i$，令
$M=\max_i|a_i|$，并选取满足 $|a_j|=M$ 的最大指标 $j$。
当 $s>1$ 充分接近一时，有}
\[
               |a_i|s^i<Ms^j\qquad(i\ne j).
\]
\bi{Consequently every analytic point $z$ with $|T(z)|=s$
has $|P(z)|=Ms^j$. This tends to $M=|P(\eta)|$ as
$s\downarrow1$, uniformly over all such $z$.
Apply this observation to the finitely many polynomials in
the chosen inequalities. For some $1<R<R_0$, all points with
$1<|T|\leq R$ belong to $U$, proving the first assertion.}
{因此每个满足 $|T(z)|=s$ 的解析点都满足 $|P(z)|=Ms^j$。
当 $s\downarrow1$ 时，该值趋于 $M=|P(\eta)|$，且对此类
全部点 $z$ 一致成立。将此观察用于所选不等式中有限个多项式，
得到某个 $1<R<R_0$，使所有满足 $1<|T|\leq R$ 的点都在
$U$ 中。这证明第一个断言。}

\bi{The disc $D$ is a pure one-dimensional strict affinoid,
and its Shilov boundary is $\{\eta\}$. By
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictcurveboundaryshilov}, its absolute
boundary is also $\{\eta\}$. The affine line is boundaryless
over $K$: a disc of radius strictly greater than $|T(z)|$
is an inner presentation at each of its points $z$.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcompositionequality} and
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generaldomainordinaryinterior} show that
every point of $D\setminus\{\eta\}$ has an ordinary
neighborhood in the affine line contained in $D$.
Together with $U$ this proves that the two analytic domains
$D$ and $U$ form an admissible covering of $D\cup U$.
The structure sheaf glues the given functions, and restriction
to $D_R$ gives the required extension.}
{$D$ 是纯一维 strict affinoid，其 Shilov 边界为
$\{\eta\}$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictcurveboundaryshilov}，
其绝对边界也是 $\{\eta\}$。仿射直线在 $K$ 上无边界：
在每个点 $z$ 处，半径严格大于 $|T(z)|$ 的圆盘给出 inner 表示。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcompositionequality} 与
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generaldomainordinaryinterior}
于是说明 $D\setminus\{\eta\}$ 的每个点在仿射直线中都有
包含于 $D$ 的普通邻域。结合 $U$，这证明两个解析域 $D,U$
构成 $D\cup U$ 的 admissible 覆盖。
结构层将给定函数粘合，再限制到 $D_R$ 即得到所需延拓。}
\end{proof}
