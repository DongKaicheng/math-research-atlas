Simultaneous monic division with a small perturbation

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 525

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Simultaneous monic division with a small perturbation}
{带小扰动的同时首一除法}]
\label{fnd:simultaneousmonicperturbation}
\bi{Let $R$ be a complete non-Archimedean Banach algebra. For
$1\leq i\leq m$, let $P_i\in R[Y_i]$ be monic of degree $d_i>0$
with all lower coefficients of norm at most one, and let
$G_i\in R\langle Y_1,\ldots,Y_m\rangle$ satisfy $\|G_i\|<1$.
Then the quotient by the $P_i-G_i$ is generated over $R$ by the
monomials $Y^\nu$ with $0\leq\nu_i<d_i$. These generators admit
coefficient lifts bounded by the norm of a representative.}
{设 $R$ 为完备非阿基米德 Banach 代数。对 $1\leq i\leq m$，
设 $P_i\in R[Y_i]$ 为次数 $d_i>0$ 的首一多项式，各低次系数
范数至多为一，并设
$G_i\in R\langle Y_1,\ldots,Y_m\rangle$ 满足 $\|G_i\|<1$。
则模掉所有 $P_i-G_i$ 所得的商，在 $R$ 上由
$0\leq\nu_i<d_i$ 的单项式 $Y^\nu$ 生成，且相应系数提升的
范数由代表元的范数控制。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Successively divide by the univariate monic polynomials $P_i$,
using Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachweierstrassdivision}. Since the
coefficients of $P_i$ lie in $R$, division in $Y_i$ preserves the
degree bounds already obtained in the earlier variables. This gives
bounded $R$-linear maps $D_i$ and $r$, all of norm at most one, with}
{逐次使用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachweierstrassdivision}，对各
单变量首一多项式 $P_i$ 作除法。由于 $P_i$ 的系数属于 $R$，
在 $Y_i$ 中除法保留此前变量已获得的次数界。于是得到范数均
至多为一的有界 $R$-线性映射 $D_i,r$，满足}
\[
 u=r(u)+\sum_iP_iD_i(u),\qquad
 r(u)\in\sum_{0\leq\nu_i<d_i}RY^\nu.
\]
\bi{Put $E(u)=\sum_iG_iD_i(u)$ and
$\theta=\max_i\|G_i\|<1$. Then $\|E(u)\|\leq\theta\|u\|$.
Substituting $P_i=(P_i-G_i)+G_i$ and iterating yields the convergent
identity}
{令 $E(u)=\sum_iG_iD_i(u)$，并令
$\theta=\max_i\|G_i\|<1$，则
$\|E(u)\|\leq\theta\|u\|$。代入
$P_i=(P_i-G_i)+G_i$ 后反复迭代，得到收敛恒等式}
\[
 u=\sum_{n\geq0}r(E^nu)
       +\sum_i(P_i-G_i)\sum_{n\geq0}D_i(E^nu).
\]
\bi{The first sum remains in the finite free monomial module and
has coefficient norm at most $\|u\|$. This proves both assertions.}
{第一个和仍在有限自由的单项式模中，且系数范数至多为
$\|u\|$，从而得到两个断言。}
\end{proof}
