The affinoid Shilov boundary

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{The affinoid Shilov boundary}{Affinoid Shilov 边界}]
\label{fnd:affinoidshilovinternal}
\bi{Every nonempty affinoid spectrum has a unique finite Shilov boundary.
The Shilov boundary of the empty spectrum is empty.}
{每个非空 affinoid 谱都具有唯一的有限 Shilov 边界。
空谱的 Shilov 边界为空集。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalfiniteboundary} and
Lemma~\ref{fnd:minimalfiniteboundary}, using the compact Hausdorff
spectrum of Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact}.}
{使用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact} 的紧 Hausdorff
谱，依次应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalfiniteboundary} 及
Lemma~\ref{fnd:minimalfiniteboundary} 即得。}
\end{proof}
