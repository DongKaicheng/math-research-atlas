Functoriality and the composition law

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 879

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Functoriality and the composition law}
{函子性与复合律}]
\label{cand:etaledisplacementfunctoriality}
\bi{A contractive homomorphism between two presentations
\eqref{eq:candetalejacobianpresentation} over the same
standard base commutes with their coefficient operators
$D_\nu$. The lifts therefore commute with such morphisms,
with integral Laurent restrictions, and with complete
isometric ground-field extension. They satisfy}
{同一标准基底上两个
\eqref{eq:candetalejacobianpresentation} 型表示之间的
压缩同态，与其系数算子 $D_\nu$ 相容。因此这些提升
与此类态射、整 Laurent 限制及完备等距基域扩张相容。
它们满足}
\[
 H_B(H_B(x,s),t)=H_B(x,\max(s,t))
                      \qquad(0\leq s,t<1).
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Let $\psi:B\to C$ be the homomorphism in question.
Applying $\psi$ to the lifted $Y$-tuple gives a root of the
base-displaced $B$-equations in $C\{\delta^{-1}U\}$.
Applying the $C$-displacement to $\psi(Y)$ gives another
such root. Both differ from $\psi(Y)$ by norm at most
$\delta$, and the original Jacobian inverse has norm at
most one. Lemma~\ref{cand:banachcenterhensel} identifies
them. The inverse determinant and then every element of
$B$ agree by bounded substitution. Comparing coefficients
proves the assertion. The same proof treats a complete
isometric scalar extension using the identical equations.}
{设所给同态为 $\psi:B\to C$。对提升后的 $Y$ 元组
施加 $\psi$，在 $C\{\delta^{-1}U\}$ 中得到基底位移
后的 $B$ 方程组之根；对 $\psi(Y)$ 施加 $C$ 的位移
则得到另一个根。二者与 $\psi(Y)$ 的差范数都至多为
$\delta$，原 Jacobian 逆范数也至多为一。由
Lemma~\ref{cand:banachcenterhensel}，两根相同。
逆行列式随后相同，再由有界代换得到 $B$ 中每个元素
的像相同。比较系数即得断言。对完备等距扩标量，使用
同一组方程的相同证明成立。}

\bi{For an integral Laurent restriction adjoining $g^{-1}$,
both $g$ and $g^{-1}$ have norm at most one. The displacement
of $g$ differs from $g$ by norm at most $\delta$; factoring
out $g$ and using geometric inversion extends the map
uniquely to the inverse, with the same bound. This proves
the restriction assertion. It also shows that two bounded
presentations identified over the fixed base give the same
displacement.}
{对于添入 $g^{-1}$ 的整 Laurent 限制，$g$ 与
$g^{-1}$ 的范数均至多为一。$g$ 的位移与 $g$ 相差
至多 $\delta$；提取因子 $g$ 并用几何求逆，便唯一
延拓到其逆元，并保留同样的界。这证明限制相容性。
同一论证也说明，在固定基底上被有界识别的两个表示
给出同一个位移。}

\bi{For the composition law, take independent variables
$(S,Z)$ and $(S',Z')$ of radii $s,t<1$. The two successive
lifts solve the base-displaced equations whose relative
coordinates are}
{为证明复合律，取半径分别为 $s,t<1$ 的独立变量
$(S,Z)$ 与 $(S',Z')$。两次相继提升所解的基底位移
方程，其相对坐标为}
\[
 W_i=S_i+S'_i+S_iS'_i,\qquad V_j=Z_j+Z'_j.
\]
\bi{The resulting $Y$-tuple differs from the original one
by norm at most $\max(s,t)<1$. The single lift evaluated
at $(W,V)$ has the same property and solves the same
equations. Hensel uniqueness identifies the two tuples.
The isometric Gauss-coordinate change in
Proposition~\ref{cand:standardhomotopysemigroup} then
identifies their norms with the single displacement of
radius $\max(s,t)$. This gives the identity for all $b\in B$,
and the zero-parameter cases are immediate.}
{所得 $Y$ 元组与原元组的差范数至多为
$\max(s,t)<1$。在 $(W,V)$ 处取值的单次提升有同样
性质，并解同一方程。Hensel 唯一性识别二者。随后由
Proposition~\ref{cand:standardhomotopysemigroup} 中的
等距 Gauss 坐标变换，它们的范数与半径
$\max(s,t)$ 的单次位移范数相同。这对全部 $b\in B$
给出恒等式，参数为零的情形立即成立。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Retain the standard algebra $A$ and its integral coordinates
from Lemma~\ref{cand:mixedgaussdisplacement}. Let
$F=(F_1,\ldots,F_m)$ be represented by restricted series in
$X_0,\ldots,X_r,T,Y_1,\ldots,Y_m$ with coefficients in
$K^\circ$. Write $J=\partial F/\partial Y$ and consider the
nonzero affinoid algebra}
{保留 Lemma~\ref{cand:mixedgaussdisplacement} 中的标准代数
$A$ 及其整坐标。设 $F=(F_1,\ldots,F_m)$ 由变量
$X_0,\ldots,X_r,T,Y_1,\ldots,Y_m$ 的受限级数表示，
系数属于 $K^\circ$。写 $J=\partial F/\partial Y$，并考虑
非零 affinoid 代数}
\[
 B=A\langle Y_1,\ldots,Y_m,Q\rangle/
                  (F_1,\ldots,F_m,Q\det J-1).
 \tag{J}\label{eq:candetalejacobianpresentation}
\]
\bi{We use its displayed quotient norm. In particular the
coordinate images and $J^{-1}=Q\operatorname{adj}(J)$ have
norm at most one. All the statements below keep this
presentation over the original valued field.}
{使用所列表示的商范数。特别地，坐标像及
$J^{-1}=Q\operatorname{adj}(J)$ 的范数至多为一。下述
全部陈述保留原赋值域上的这个表示。}
