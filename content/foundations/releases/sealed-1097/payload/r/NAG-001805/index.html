Naive flatness lifts to an auxiliary Gauss point

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 470

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Naive flatness lifts to an auxiliary Gauss point}{朴素平坦性提升到辅助 Gauss 点}]
\label{fnd:charzeronaivegausslift}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good spaces over a complete characteristic-zero field, and let $y\in Y$ with image $x$ have trivially valued $F=\mathcal H(x)$. Let $\mathcal F$ be coherent and naively $X$-flat at $y$. For $0<r<1$, form $X^*=X\times_K\mathbb A_K^{1,\mathrm{an}}$ and $Y^*=Y\times_K\mathbb A_K^{1,\mathrm{an}}$, and let $x^*,y^*$ be their radius-$r$ Gauss points above $x,y$. Then the pullback $\mathcal F^*$ is naively $X^*$-flat at $y^*$.}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 为特征零完备域上 good 空间之间的态射，点 $y\in Y$ 映到 $x$，并假设 $F=\mathcal H(x)$ 平凡赋值。设凝聚层 $\mathcal F$ 在 $y$ 处朴素 $X$-平坦。对 $0<r<1$，令 $X^*=X\times_K\mathbb A_K^{1,\mathrm{an}}$、$Y^*=Y\times_K\mathbb A_K^{1,\mathrm{an}}$，并以 $x^*,y^*$ 记其分别位于 $x,y$ 上方的半径 $r$ Gauss 点。则拉回 $\mathcal F^*$ 在 $y^*$ 处朴素 $X^*$-平坦。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Write $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$, $S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$, $R^*=\mathcal O_{X^*,x^*}$, $S^*=\mathcal O_{Y^*,y^*}$ and $M=\mathcal F_y$. The two affine-line projections are smooth, hence their ordinary local maps $R\to R^*$ and $S\to S^*$ are flat. Coherent pullback gives $M^*=\mathcal F^*_{y^*}=M\otimes_SS^*$. Since $M$ is $R$-flat and $S^*$ is $S$-flat, $M^*$ is $R$-flat: tensoring an injection of $R$-modules with $M$ gives an injection of $S$-modules, which remains injective after tensoring with $S^*$.}
{记 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$、$R^*=\mathcal O_{X^*,x^*}$、$S^*=\mathcal O_{Y^*,y^*}$ 及 $M=\mathcal F_y$。两个仿射直线投影都光滑，故普通局部同态 $R\to R^*$、$S\to S^*$ 平坦。凝聚拉回给出 $M^*=\mathcal F^*_{y^*}=M\otimes_SS^*$。因 $M$ 在 $R$ 上平坦且 $S^*$ 在 $S$ 上平坦，$M^*$ 在 $R$ 上平坦：任一 $R$-模单射与 $M$ 张量后成为 $S$-模单射，再与 $S^*$ 张量仍单射。}

\bi{Let $\mathfrak m$ be the maximal ideal of $R$ and put $I=\mathfrak mR^*$. Apply Corollary~\ref{fnd:charzeroallresidueclosedfibercomparison} to the inner smooth projection $X^*\to X$ at $x^*$. Its analytic fibre is $\mathbb A_F^{1,\mathrm{an}}$, so the preceding lemma gives a faithfully flat local map}
{以 $\mathfrak m$ 记 $R$ 的极大理想，并置 $I=\mathfrak mR^*$。在 $x^*$ 处对内光滑投影 $X^*\to X$ 应用 Corollary~\ref{fnd:charzeroallresidueclosedfibercomparison}。其解析纤维为 $\mathbb A_F^{1,\mathrm{an}}$，所以上一引理给出忠实平坦局部映射}
\[
                             R^*/I\longrightarrow F((T)).
\]
\bi{Its source is a field. Indeed its maximal ideal extends to the zero ideal of the target field, and faithful flatness contracts that zero ideal to zero. Hence $M^*/IM^*$ is flat over $R^*/I$.}
{其源为域：源的极大理想延拓到目标域后为零，而忠实平坦性将零理想收缩为零。因此 $M^*/IM^*$ 在 $R^*/I$ 上平坦。}

\bi{There is also no relative Tor obstruction. Choose a free $R$-resolution of $R/\mathfrak m$. Since $R^*$ is $R$-flat, its tensor with $R^*$ is a free $R^*$-resolution of $R^*/I$. Tensoring that resolution with $M^*$ is the same as tensoring the original resolution over $R$ with $M^*$, and the latter has zero positive homology by $R$-flatness. Thus}
{相对 Tor 障碍也消失。取 $R/\mathfrak m$ 的一个自由 $R$-分解。由于 $R^*$ 在 $R$ 上平坦，与 $R^*$ 张量后得到 $R^*/I$ 的自由 $R^*$-分解。再与 $M^*$ 张量，等同于将原分解在 $R$ 上与 $M^*$ 张量；后者因 $R$-平坦性而正次同调为零。因此}
\[
                  \operatorname{Tor}^{R^*}_1(M^*,R^*/I)=0.
\]
\bi{The local rings $R^*,S^*$ are Noetherian and $M^*$ is finite over $S^*$. The local criterion \cite[Tag 00ML]{StacksAlg} now makes $M^*$ flat over $R^*$, proving the assertion.}
{局部环 $R^*,S^*$ 为 Noetherian，且 $M^*$ 在 $S^*$ 上有限。由局部判据 \cite[Tag 00ML]{StacksAlg}，$M^*$ 在 $R^*$ 上平坦，即得结论。}
\end{proof}
