Regularity along an inner generic fibre in characteristic zero

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 461

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Regularity along an inner generic fibre in characteristic zero}{特征零内泛纤维的正则比较}]
\label{fnd:charzeroinnergenericfibreregularity}
\bi{Let $K$ be a complete field of characteristic zero and let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good analytic spaces. Let $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$ and put $x=f(y)$. If $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is a field and $\mathcal H(x)$ is nontrivially valued, then the canonical local homomorphism}
{设 $K$ 为特征零完备域，$f:Y\to X$ 为 good 解析空间之间的态射。令 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$、$x=f(y)$。若 $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 为域且 $\mathcal H(x)$ 的赋值非平凡，则典范局部同态}
\[
                 \mathcal O_{Y,y}\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{Y_x,y}
\]
\bi{is regular: it is flat and all its algebraic prime fibres are geometrically regular.}
{正则，即它平坦，且其全部代数素理想纤维均几何正则。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{We induct on an upper bound $n$ for the dimension of a strict affinoid fibre of an ordinary affinoid representative of the source germ. Such a bound exists by Proposition~\ref{fnd:innerstrictfibrerefinement}. The induction assertion concerns every morphism satisfying the stated hypotheses that has such a representative with fibre dimension at most $n$. Thus later changes of representatives do not require a comparison with a minimal local-dimension invariant.}
{对源芽的普通 affinoid 代表的 strict affinoid 纤维维数的一个上界 $n$ 作归纳。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:innerstrictfibrerefinement}，这样的上界存在。归纳断言涉及满足所列假设、且存在纤维维数至多为 $n$ 的此类代表的所有态射。因此，后续更换代表时，无须将其与某个最小局部维数不变量比较。}

\bi{For such a representative let $d$ be its strict fibre dimension, so $d\leq n$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfibrefiniteprojection} gives, on ordinary neighborhoods, a finite map to an affinoid ordinary neighborhood $V$ of $t$ in $\mathbb A_X^{d,\mathrm{an}}$. Finite branch separation, followed by shrinking $V$, selects the branch containing $y$ and makes $y$ the only point above $t$. Put}
{对此代表，令其 strict 纤维维数为 $d$，故 $d\leq n$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfibrefiniteprojection} 在普通邻域上给出有限映射，其目标为 $\mathbb A_X^{d,\mathrm{an}}$ 中 $t$ 的 affinoid 普通邻域 $V$。有限分支分离及再次缩小 $V$，可选出含 $y$ 的分支，并使 $y$ 为 $t$ 上方的唯一点。记}
\[
 R_0=\mathcal O_{V,t},\quad T_0=\mathcal O_{V_x,t},\quad
 S=\mathcal O_{Y,y},\quad T=\mathcal O_{Y_x,y}.
\]
\bi{The finite direct-image stalk formulas identify $S$ with a finite $R_0$-algebra and give $T=S\otimes_{R_0}T_0$. To check the latter identity, apply finite affinoid base change and then take ordinary stalks. The unique point above $t$ is still the unique point in the corresponding analytic fibre, so both direct-image stalk products have just one factor. It is enough to prove that $R_0\to T_0$ is regular: flatness is preserved by this base change, and the fibre at a prime of $S$ is a field extension of the corresponding geometrically regular fibre of $R_0\to T_0$.}
{有限直像的茎公式将 $S$ 识别为有限 $R_0$-代数，并给出 $T=S\otimes_{R_0}T_0$。后一识别由有限 affinoid 基变换后取普通茎得到：$t$ 上方的唯一点在相应解析纤维中仍是唯一点，所以两个直像茎的乘积均只有一个因子。只须证明 $R_0\to T_0$ 正则：平坦性在该基变换下保持，而 $S$ 的一个素理想处的纤维，是 $R_0\to T_0$ 的相应几何正则纤维的域扩张。}

\bi{Both $R_0$ and $T_0$ are regular local rings. For $R_0$, use the smooth affine-space projection on the ordinary interior of $V$, Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroquasismoothordinaryregular}, and regularity of the base field $\mathcal O_{X,x}$. For $T_0$, use Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomainregular}. The regularity ascent used here is \cite[Tag 031E]{StacksAlg}. If $R_0$ is a field, its map to the regular ring $T_0$ is flat and geometrically regular by characteristic zero and \href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0381}{Stacks 0381}; this finishes that case. In particular it proves the induction when $n=0$, since then $d=0$ and $V$ is an ordinary neighborhood of $x$ in $X$, so $R_0=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is a field.}
{环 $R_0,T_0$ 都是正则局部环。对 $R_0$，在 $V$ 的普通内部使用光滑仿射空间投影、Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroquasismoothordinaryregular} 及底域 $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 的正则性；对 $T_0$，使用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomainregular}。这里的正则性上升是 \cite[Tag 031E]{StacksAlg}。若 $R_0$ 为域，则其到正则环 $T_0$ 的映射平坦，并由特征零及 \cite[Tag 0381]{StacksAlg} 知它几何正则，故此种情形完成。特别地，这证明了 $n=0$ 的归纳起点：此时 $d=0$，$V$ 是 $x$ 在 $X$ 中的普通邻域，因而 $R_0=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 为域。}

\bi{Suppose now that $R_0$ is not a field, and write $\mathfrak m$ for its maximal ideal. Every power $\mathfrak m^q$, $q\geq1$, is nonzero because a regular local ring is a domain by \href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00NP}{Stacks 00NP}. Lemma~\ref{fnd:charzeroclosedfiberdimensiondrop}, applied to $I=\mathfrak m^q$, represents the resulting closed-subspace germ by an inner affinoid with strict fibre dimension at most $d-1\leq n-1$. The induction hypothesis makes}
{现假设 $R_0$ 不是域，并以 $\mathfrak m$ 记其极大理想。由于正则局部环为整环，对每个 $q\geq1$，幂 $\mathfrak m^q$ 都非零。将 Lemma~\ref{fnd:charzeroclosedfiberdimensiondrop} 应用于 $I=\mathfrak m^q$，得到该闭子空间芽的内 affinoid 代表，其 strict 纤维维数至多为 $d-1\leq n-1$。归纳假设说明}
\[
                  R_0/\mathfrak m^q\longrightarrow
                         T_0/\mathfrak m^qT_0
\]
\bi{regular, and in particular flat. The rings $R_0,T_0$ are Noetherian and $\mathfrak mT_0$ lies in the maximal ideal of $T_0$. Apply the adic flatness criterion \cite[Tag 0523]{StacksAlg}, with module $T_0$ over itself, to all these quotients. It follows that $R_0\to T_0$ is flat.}
{正则，特别地平坦。环 $R_0,T_0$ 为 Noetherian，且 $\mathfrak mT_0$ 位于 $T_0$ 的极大理想中。对全部这些商应用 adic 平坦性判据 \cite[Tag 0523]{StacksAlg}，其中模取 $T_0$ 自身，遂得 $R_0\to T_0$ 平坦。}

\bi{It remains to examine its algebraic prime fibres. At the zero prime, the fibre is a localization of the regular ring $T_0$, hence regular and, by characteristic zero and \href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0381}{Stacks 0381}, geometrically regular. If $\mathfrak p\ne0$ is a prime of $R_0$, apply the dimension-lowering lemma to $I=\mathfrak p$. Induction makes $R_0/\mathfrak p\to T_0/\mathfrak pT_0$ regular. Its fibre at the zero prime is exactly $T_0\otimes_{R_0}\kappa(\mathfrak p)$, and is geometrically regular. This proves regularity of $R_0\to T_0$ and then of $S\to T$, completing the induction.}
{最后检验代数素理想纤维。在零素理想处，纤维是正则环 $T_0$ 的局部化，故正则，并由特征零知其几何正则。若 $\mathfrak p\ne0$ 是 $R_0$ 的素理想，对 $I=\mathfrak p$ 应用维数下降引理。归纳说明 $R_0/\mathfrak p\to T_0/\mathfrak pT_0$ 正则，其零素理想处的纤维恰为 $T_0\otimes_{R_0}\kappa(\mathfrak p)$，因而几何正则。这证明了 $R_0\to T_0$ 的正则性，继而得到 $S\to T$ 正则，归纳完成。}
\end{proof}
