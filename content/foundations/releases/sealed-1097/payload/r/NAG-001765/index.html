Generic regularity of ordinary germs and their completions

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 333

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Generic regularity of ordinary germs and their completions}{通常芽及其完备化的泛正则性}]
\label{fnd:charzerogenericgermregularity}
\bi{Under the hypotheses of Proposition~\ref{fnd:charzerouniformregularopen}, one may choose $0\ne a\in A$ such that, for every $x\in X=\mathcal M(A)$, both of the following rings are regular:}
{在 Proposition~\ref{fnd:charzerouniformregularopen} 的假设下，可选择 $0\ne a\in A$，使对每个 $x\in X=\mathcal M(A)$，以下两个环均正则：}
\[
                     \mathcal O_{X,x}[1/a],
             \qquad\widehat{\mathcal O_{X,x}}[1/a].
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Take $a$ from Proposition~\ref{fnd:charzerouniformregularopen} and a nontrivial free strictification $F=K_r$. Write $B=A_r$, take any $z\in\mathcal M(B)$ above $x$, and put $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ and $S=\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(B),z}$. Such a $z$ exists by the Gauss section. The ring $B[1/a]$ is regular. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictcharzeroregularcomparison} says that $B_{\ker(z)}\to S$ and $S\to\widehat S$ are regular homomorphisms.}
{取 Proposition~\ref{fnd:charzerouniformregularopen} 中的 $a$，并取赋值非平凡的自由 strictification $F=K_r$。写 $B=A_r$，选择 $x$ 上方的任一点 $z\in\mathcal M(B)$，令 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$S=\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(B),z}$。Gauss 截面保证这样的 $z$ 存在。环 $B[1/a]$ 正则。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictcharzeroregularcomparison}，$B_{\ker(z)}\to S$ 及 $S\to\widehat S$ 均为正则同态。}

\bi{Let $\mathfrak Q$ be a prime of $S$ avoiding $a$, and let $\mathfrak q$ be its contraction to $B$. The local ring $B_{\mathfrak q}$ is regular. The map $B_{\mathfrak q}\to S_{\mathfrak Q}$ is flat and has regular closed fibre, since that fibre is a localization of a geometrically regular fibre of $B_{\ker(z)}\to S$. The ascent lemma \cite[Tag 031E]{StacksAlg} makes $S_{\mathfrak Q}$ regular. Therefore $S[1/a]$ is regular. Applying exactly the same local argument to the regular map $S\to\widehat S$ gives regularity of $\widehat S[1/a]$.}
{取 $S$ 中避开 $a$ 的素理想 $\mathfrak Q$，令 $\mathfrak q$ 为其在 $B$ 中的收缩。局部环 $B_{\mathfrak q}$ 正则。映射 $B_{\mathfrak q}\to S_{\mathfrak Q}$ 平坦，且闭纤维正则，因为该纤维是 $B_{\ker(z)}\to S$ 的一个几何正则纤维的局部化。由上升引理 \cite[Tag 031E]{StacksAlg}，$S_{\mathfrak Q}$ 正则。因此 $S[1/a]$ 正则。对正则映射 $S\to\widehat S$ 使用完全相同的局部论证，得到 $\widehat S[1/a]$ 正则。}

\bi{The map $R\to S$ is faithfully flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}. All local rings are Noetherian, so \cite[Tag 0C4G]{StacksAlg} makes $\widehat R\to\widehat S$ flat; it is local, hence faithfully flat by \cite[Tag 00HP]{StacksAlg}. Inverting $a$ preserves faithful flatness for both maps. Every prime of either source then lifts to the corresponding target by \cite[Tag 00HQ]{StacksAlg}. Localization yields a flat local map with regular target, and Tag 00OF descends regularity. This proves both assertions.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}，映射 $R\to S$ 忠实平坦。所有局部环均 Noetherian，因此 \cite[Tag 0C4G]{StacksAlg} 使 $\widehat R\to\widehat S$ 平坦；它为局部映射，再由 \cite[Tag 00HP]{StacksAlg} 得忠实平坦。两个映射在可逆化 $a$ 后均保持忠实平坦。由 \cite[Tag 00HQ]{StacksAlg}，各源环的每个素理想都提升到相应目标；局部化给出目标正则的平坦局部映射，再用 Tag 00OF 下降正则性，即得两个断言。}
\end{proof}
