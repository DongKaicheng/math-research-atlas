Small Laurent coordinate changes

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 420

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Small Laurent coordinate changes}{Laurent 坐标的小扰动}]
\label{fnd:smalllaurentcoordinatechange}
\bi{Let $r_1,\ldots,r_n,p,q>0$, with $p\le q$, and set}
{设 $r_1,\ldots,r_n,p,q>0$，其中 $p\le q$，并令}
\[
 E=K\{r^{-1}T,q^{-1}S,pS^{-1}\}.
\]
\bi{Give $E$ its Laurent Gauss norm. Suppose $H_i,H\in E$ satisfy, for a real number $0<\varepsilon<1$,}
{赋予 $E$ 以 Laurent Gauss 范数。设 $H_i,H\in E$ 对某个实数 $0<\varepsilon<1$ 满足}
\[
 \|H_i-T_i\|\le\varepsilon r_i,
 \qquad H=S(1+u),\quad \|u\|\le\varepsilon.
\]
\bi{Then $T_i\mapsto H_i$, $S\mapsto H$ defines an isometric $K$-algebra automorphism of $E$.}
{则 $T_i\mapsto H_i$、$S\mapsto H$ 定义 $E$ 的等距 $K$-代数自同构。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The coefficient normal form in Theorem~\ref{fnd:laurentcoefficientnormalform}, with coefficients in $K\{r^{-1}T\}$, identifies $E$ isometrically with its weighted convergent Laurent series; the weights are those displayed below.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:laurentcoefficientnormalform} 的系数正规形，以 $K\{r^{-1}T\}$ 为系数，可将 $E$ 等距识别为加权收敛 Laurent 级数，其权重如下所列。}
\bi{The geometric series gives $(1+u)^{-1}$ and $\|(1+u)^{-1}-1\|\le\varepsilon$. Consequently $H$ and $H^{-1}$ satisfy the defining radius bounds, so the substitution defines a bounded algebra endomorphism $\Phi$. The Laurent monomials $T^\nu S^j$, $\nu\in\mathbb N^n$, $j\in\mathbb Z$, have weights}
{几何级数给出 $(1+u)^{-1}$，且 $\|(1+u)^{-1}-1\|\le\varepsilon$。于是 $H$ 与 $H^{-1}$ 满足定义中的半径界，因此代入给出有界代数自同态 $\Phi$。Laurent 单项式 $T^\nu S^j$，其中 $\nu\in\mathbb N^n$、$j\in\mathbb Z$，具有权重}
\[
 w_{\nu,j}=r^\nu
  \begin{cases}q^j&j\ge0,\\p^j&j<0.\end{cases}
\]
\bi{Expanding one factor at a time and using $|m|\le1$ for integers $m$ shows}
{逐个展开因子，并使用整数 $m$ 满足 $|m|\le1$，得到}
\[
 \|\Phi(T^\nu S^j)-T^\nu S^j\|
       \le\varepsilon w_{\nu,j}.
\]
\bi{For negative $j$ use the already established estimate for $(1+u)^{-1}-1$. Passing from finite sums to convergent Laurent series yields $\|\Phi-1\|\le\varepsilon$. The operator series $\sum_{m\ge0}(1-\Phi)^m$ is therefore a continuous inverse to $\Phi$. A bijective algebra homomorphism has an algebra-homomorphic inverse. Finally, $\|\Phi(a)-a\|<\|a\|$ for $a\ne0$ proves $\|\Phi(a)\|=\|a\|$.}
{当 $j<0$ 时，使用已得的 $(1+u)^{-1}-1$ 的估计。从有限和过渡到收敛 Laurent 级数，得到 $\|\Phi-1\|\le\varepsilon$。因此算子级数 $\sum_{m\ge0}(1-\Phi)^m$ 给出 $\Phi$ 的连续逆。双射代数同态的逆仍是代数同态。最后，当 $a\ne0$ 时有 $\|\Phi(a)-a\|<\|a\|$，从而 $\|\Phi(a)\|=\|a\|$。}
\end{proof}
