Uniform approximation implies density

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 611

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Uniform approximation implies density}
{统一逼近推出稠密性}]
\label{fnd:uniformapproximationdensity}
\bi{Let $E$ be a real-valued field and let $V\subset E$ be
an additive subgroup. If some $0<q<1$ has the property that
every $a\in E$ admits $b\in V$ with $|a-b|\le q|a|$,
then $V$ is dense in $E$.}
{设 $E$ 为实值赋值域，$V\subset E$ 为加法子群。若存在
$0<q<1$，使每个 $a\in E$ 都有 $b\in V$ 满足
$|a-b|\le q|a|$，则 $V$ 在 $E$ 中稠密。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Fix $a$. Start with $b_0=0$. Having chosen $b_j\in V$,
choose $c_j\in V$ with
$|a-b_j-c_j|\le q|a-b_j|$ and put $b_{j+1}=b_j+c_j$.
Induction gives $|a-b_j|\le q^j|a|$, hence $b_j\to a$.}
{固定 $a$，从 $b_0=0$ 开始。选定 $b_j\in V$ 后，
选择 $c_j\in V$ 满足
$|a-b_j-c_j|\le q|a-b_j|$，并令 $b_{j+1}=b_j+c_j$。
归纳得到 $|a-b_j|\le q^j|a|$，所以 $b_j\to a$。}
\end{proof}
