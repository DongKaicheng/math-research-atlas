The maximal-order locus

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 92

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The maximal-order locus}{最大阶数的轨迹}]\label{fnd:relativemaximalorderlocus}
\bi{Let $s\geq0$ be an integer. Suppose $F\in A\langle T',T_n\rangle$ is $T_n$-distinguished of some order at most $s$ at every maximal ideal of $A$. Write $F=\sum_jF_j(T')T_n^j$, and let $a_j\in A$ be the constant coefficient of $F_j$. Then $a_0,\ldots,a_s$ generate the unit ideal, and}
{设 $s\geq0$ 为整数，且 $F\in A\langle T',T_n\rangle$ 在 $A$ 的每个极大理想处都是阶数至多为 $s$ 的 $T_n$-distinguished 级数。写 $F=\sum_jF_j(T')T_n^j$，并以 $a_j\in A$ 表示 $F_j$ 的常数项系数。则 $a_0,\ldots,a_s$ 生成单位理想，且}
\[
 R_s=\{x\in\mathcal M(A):|a_j(x)|\leq|a_s(x)|\ (0\leq j<s)\}
\]
\bi{is a strict rational domain. Its maximal residue points are exactly those at which $F$ has order $s$. On the rational quotient algebra $A_s$, the image of $F$ has order $s$ at every maximal residue point. For $s=0$ this domain is all of $\mathcal M(A)$.}
{是 strict 有理域。其极大剩余域点恰好是 $F$ 的阶数等于 $s$ 的点。在有理商代数 $A_s$ 上，$F$ 的像在每个极大剩余域点的阶数均为 $s$。当 $s=0$ 时，该域为整个 $\mathcal M(A)$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{At a maximal point $x$ of order $r\leq s$, Lemma~\ref{fnd:tateunitconstantcriterion} gives}
{在阶数为 $r\leq s$ 的极大点 $x$ 处，Lemma~\ref{fnd:tateunitconstantcriterion} 给出}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 |a_j(x)|&\leq\|F_{j,x}\|\leq|a_r(x)| &&(j\leq r),\\
 |a_j(x)|&\leq\|F_{j,x}\|<|a_r(x)| &&(j>r),
 \end{aligned}
\qquad a_r(x)\ne0.
\]
\bi{Thus the $a_0,\ldots,a_s$ have no common maximal zero and generate the unit ideal by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}. The strict rational construction therefore applies. The displayed inequalities show that a maximal point belongs to $R_s$ exactly when its order is $s$. Every maximal residue field of $A_s$ is finite over $K$; its restriction to $A$ has image a finite-dimensional domain over $K$, hence a field by \cite[Tag 00GS(1), Lemma 10.36.19]{StacksAlg}. It therefore gives a maximal point of $A$ in $R_s$. The resulting residue-field extension is finite and preserves the absolute value by Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}. Extension of this field does not change any coefficient norm or the strict inequalities defining the order. This proves the final assertion. If $s=0$, the same argument makes $a_0$ a unit and the rational quotient is $A$.}
{因此 $a_0,\ldots,a_s$ 没有共同的极大零点，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} 生成单位理想，故可施加 strict 有理商构造。上面的不等式说明：极大点属于 $R_s$ 当且仅当其阶数为 $s$。$A_s$ 的每个极大剩余域都有限于 $K$；其到 $A$ 的限制的像是有限维 $K$-整环，由 \cite[Tag 00GS(1), Lemma 10.36.19]{StacksAlg} 为域，所以给出 $A$ 中属于 $R_s$ 的极大点。所得剩余域扩张有限，且由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 保持绝对值。此域扩张不改变任何系数范数，也不改变定义阶数的严格不等式。这证明了最后的断言。若 $s=0$，同一论证说明 $a_0$ 为单位元，且有理商就是 $A$。}
\end{proof}
