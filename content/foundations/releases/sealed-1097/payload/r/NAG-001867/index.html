\begin{theorem}[\bi{Flatness along a given d\'evissage}{沿给定 d\'evissage 的平坦性}]
\label{fnd:devissageanalyticflatcriterion}
\bi{For an $X$-d\'evissage as above, $\mathcal F$ is analytically $X$-flat at $y$ if and only if $u_i$ is injective at $t_i$ for every $1\leq i\leq r$. When these conditions hold, every $u_i$ is universally injective at $t_i$ in the sense of Theorem~\ref{fnd:genericfibreuniversalinjectivity}, and $u_r$ is an isomorphism at $t_r$.}
{对上述 $X$-d\'evissage，$\mathcal F$ 在 $y$ 处解析 $X$-平坦，当且仅当每个 $1\leq i\leq r$ 的 $u_i$ 在 $t_i$ 处单射。若这些条件成立，则每个 $u_i$ 在 $t_i$ 处都满足 Theorem~\ref{fnd:genericfibreuniversalinjectivity} 意义的普遍单射性，且 $u_r$ 在 $t_r$ 处为同构。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Restriction to the ordinary neighbourhood $V$ preserves all pointwise flatness tests. Regarding a sheaf as a sheaf on its closed support also preserves these tests: the closed immersion is finite, its point fibre is a singleton, and Theorem~\ref{fnd:finitepushforwardflatness} applies. The same theorem, now applied to $\pi_i$, gives}
{限制到普通邻域 $V$ 保持全部逐点平坦性测试。把层视为其闭支撑上的层也保持这些测试：闭浸入有限，其逐点纤维为单点，因此可应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:finitepushforwardflatness}。再对 $\pi_i$ 应用同一定理，得到}
\[
 Q_{i-1}\text{ is analytically }X\text{-flat at }t_{i-1}
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 N_i\text{ is analytically }X\text{-flat at }t_i.
\]
\bi{The spaces $T_i$ are quasi-smooth over $X$ by smoothness and domain invariance. Corollary~\ref{fnd:genericfibreflatcokernel} therefore identifies the condition on the right with the conjunction of injectivity of $u_i$ at $t_i$ and analytic $X$-flatness of $Q_i$ there. Start with the zero sheaf $Q_r$, which is flat after every base change, and iterate these equivalences backwards. This proves the stated equivalence. The universal injectivity follows separately for each arrow from Theorem~\ref{fnd:genericfibreuniversalinjectivity}. Finally $Q_r=0$ makes $u_r$ surjective; its injectivity gives the last assertion.}
{由光滑性及域不变性，各 $T_i$ 在 $X$ 上 quasi-smooth。因此 Corollary~\ref{fnd:genericfibreflatcokernel} 将右侧条件识别为两个条件的合取：$u_i$ 在 $t_i$ 处单射，以及 $Q_i$ 在该处解析 $X$-平坦。从任意基变换后均平坦的零层 $Q_r$ 开始，逆向迭代这些等价，便得到所述等价。每个箭头的普遍单射性分别由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:genericfibreuniversalinjectivity} 给出。最后，$Q_r=0$ 使 $u_r$ 满射，再结合其单射性即得最后断言。}
\end{proof}
