Finite comparison maps for graded reductions

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 557

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Finite comparison maps for graded reductions}
{分次约化的有限比较映射}]
\label{fnd:gradedtensorfinitecomparison}
\bi{Let $A\to B,A\to C$ be bounded affinoid maps, and let
$L/K$ be any complete valued extension. The natural maps}
{设 $A\to B,A\to C$ 为有界 affinoid 映射，$L/K$ 为任意
完备赋值扩域。以下自然映射均有限：}
\[
 \widetilde B^{\rm gr}\otimes_{\widetilde A^{\rm gr}}
     \widetilde C^{\rm gr}
       \longrightarrow\widetilde{B\widehat\otimes_A C}^{\rm gr},
 \qquad
 \widetilde A^{\rm gr}\otimes_{\widetilde K^{\rm gr}}
     \widetilde L^{\rm gr}
       \longrightarrow\widetilde{A_L}^{\rm gr}
\]
\bi{are finite.}
{。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Present $B$ and $C$ by weighted Tate algebras over $K$.
Their combined variables give an admissible quotient
presentation of $D=B\widehat\otimes_A C$, by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout}.
The \hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{fixed absolute presentation result} makes
$\widetilde D^{\rm gr}$ finite over the image of the
corresponding graded polynomial algebra.
The image of each polynomial variable either vanishes or
is the image of the initial form of its image in $B$ or $C$.
Indeed nonvanishing means that its spectral value has remained
equal to its prescribed radius, so this equality already held
in the appropriate factor. Consequently the image of the
first comparison map contains the image of that polynomial
algebra. The same finite module generators therefore work
over this larger image.}
{以 $K$ 上的加权 Tate 代数表示 $B$ 和 $C$。
由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout}，合并这些
变量得到 $D=B\widehat\otimes_A C$ 的 admissible 商表示。
\hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{固定绝对表示的结果}说明
$\widetilde D^{\rm gr}$ 在相应分次多项式代数的像上有限。
每个多项式变量的像或者为零，或者来自其在 $B$ 或 $C$ 中像的
首项：非零意味着谱值仍等于规定半径，所以该等式在相应因子中
已经成立。因此第一个比较映射的像包含该多项式代数的像，
同一组有限模生成元也在这个更大的像上生成。}

\bi{For the second map, extend an admissible presentation
$K\{r^{-1}T\}\twoheadrightarrow A$ to
$L\{r^{-1}T\}\twoheadrightarrow A_L$.
The same argument applies, with $\widetilde L^{\rm gr}$
as coefficient ring. Any nonzero initial coordinate after
extension comes from its original initial coordinate in
$\widetilde A^{\rm gr}$.}
{对第二个映射，把 admissible 表示
$K\{r^{-1}T\}\twoheadrightarrow A$ 扩张为
$L\{r^{-1}T\}\twoheadrightarrow A_L$。
以 $\widetilde L^{\rm gr}$ 为系数环应用同一论证即可。
扩张后的非零坐标首项必来自其在
$\widetilde A^{\rm gr}$ 中的原首项。}
\end{proof}
