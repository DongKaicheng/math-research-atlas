Residual tensor lengths and orders

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 715

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Residual tensor lengths and orders}
{剩余张量积的长度与阶数}]
\label{cand:inseparableresidualordermultiplicity}
\bi{Let $C$ be a smooth integral curve over an arbitrary
field $k$, let $p$ be a closed point, and write}
{设 $C$ 为任意域 $k$ 上的光滑整曲线，$p$ 为闭点，并记}
\[
 \kappa=\kappa(p),\qquad
 s=[\kappa:k]_{\mathrm{sep}},\qquad
 e=[\kappa:k]_{\mathrm{insep}}.
\]
\bi{Choose a field extension $l/k$ containing the images
of all $k$-embeddings of $\kappa$ into an algebraic closure
of $k$. Then $p_l$ has exactly $s$ distinct support points,
all $l$-rational, and its local ring at each support point
has length $e$. If $p'$ is any such point of the smooth
curve $C_l$, then for every $\varphi\in k(C)^\times$,}
{选择域扩张 $l/k$，使其包含 $\kappa$ 到 $k$ 的某个
代数闭包中的全部 $k$-嵌入像。则 $p_l$ 的支撑恰有
$s$ 个不同的点，均为 $l$-有理点，且它在每个支撑点处
的局部环长度均为 $e$。若 $p'$ 为光滑曲线 $C_l$ 上
这样的任一点，则对每个 $\varphi\in k(C)^\times$，有}
\[
                \operatorname{ord}_{p'}(\varphi)
                    =e\,\operatorname{ord}_p(\varphi).
\]
\bi{Here $\varphi$ is pulled back to the component of $C_l$
through $p'$.}
{这里将 $\varphi$ 拉回到 $C_l$ 中经过 $p'$ 的分量。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Let $\kappa_s$ be the maximal separable subextension;
then $\kappa/\kappa_s$ is purely inseparable by
\cite[Tag 030K]{StacksAlg}. Since $l$ contains all the
embeddings of $\kappa_s$, the usual factorization of a
separable minimal polynomial gives}
{令 $\kappa_s$ 为最大可分子扩张。由
\cite[Tag 030K]{StacksAlg}，$\kappa/\kappa_s$ 纯不可分。
因为 $l$ 包含 $\kappa_s$ 的全部嵌入，分解可分极小
多项式即得}
\[
 \kappa\otimes_k l
   =\prod_{\sigma:\kappa_s\hookrightarrow l}
       \bigl(\kappa\otimes_{\kappa_s,\sigma}l\bigr).
\]
\bi{Each factor has dimension $e$ over $l$. Let
$\widehat\sigma:\kappa\hookrightarrow l$ be the unique
extension of $\sigma$. It gives a surjection from that
factor to $l$. In positive characteristic, choose finitely
many generators $\beta_i$ of $\kappa/\kappa_s$ and powers
$\beta_i^{p^{n_i}}\in\kappa_s$. The kernel is generated
by $\beta_i\otimes1-1\otimes\widehat\sigma(\beta_i)$,
each of which is nilpotent. Thus this is the unique
maximal ideal. In characteristic zero each factor is
simply $l$. Every composition factor of the resulting
Artin local ring is $l$, so its length is its
$l$-dimension $e$. There are precisely $s$ factors.}
{每个因子在 $l$ 上的维数为 $e$。令
$\widehat\sigma:\kappa\hookrightarrow l$ 为 $\sigma$
的唯一延伸。它给出该因子到 $l$ 的满射。在正特征时，
选择 $\kappa/\kappa_s$ 的有限个生成元 $\beta_i$ 及满足
$\beta_i^{p^{n_i}}\in\kappa_s$ 的幂。满射之核由
$\beta_i\otimes1-1\otimes\widehat\sigma(\beta_i)$ 生成，
而这些元素均幂零，因此该核是唯一极大理想。特征零时，
每个因子就是 $l$。所得 Artin 局部环的每个合成因子
都是 $l$，故其长度等于 $l$-维数 $e$。因子数恰为 $s$。}

\bi{Put $R=\mathcal O_{C,p}$ and
$R'=\mathcal O_{C_l,p'}$. Both rings are discrete valuation
rings, since the two curves are smooth. If $u$ is a
uniformizer of $R$, then}
{令 $R=\mathcal O_{C,p}$、
$R'=\mathcal O_{C_l,p'}$。两条曲线光滑，故这两个环
均为离散赋值环。若 $u$ 是 $R$ 的一致化元，则}
\[
 R'/uR'=(\kappa\otimes_k l)_{p'},\qquad
 \operatorname{ord}_{R'}(u)
       =\operatorname{length}_{R'}(R'/uR')=e.
\]
\bi{The local ring map $R\to R'$ carries units to units
and is injective by flatness. Writing
$\varphi=u^m v$ with $v\in R^\times$ therefore gives
$\operatorname{ord}_{p'}(\varphi)=em$ for every
$m\in\mathbf Z$, as asserted.}
{局部环映射 $R\to R'$ 将单位映为单位，并由平坦性为
单射。将 $\varphi$ 写为 $u^m v$，其中
$v\in R^\times$，便对每个 $m\in\mathbf Z$ 得到
$\operatorname{ord}_{p'}(\varphi)=em$，即所需公式。}
\end{proof}
