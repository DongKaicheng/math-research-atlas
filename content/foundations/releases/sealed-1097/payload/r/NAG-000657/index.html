\begin{proposition}\label{new:shilov_singleton}
\bi{The Shilov boundary of $V$ consists of one point if and only if its spectral seminorm is multiplicative. When these conditions hold, the unique boundary point is the spectral seminorm.}{ $V$ 的 Shilov boundary 为 singleton，当且仅当其 spectral seminorm multiplicative。此时唯一的 boundary point 就是 spectral seminorm。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{If the Shilov boundary is $\{\xi\}$, its maximum property gives the following identity for every element of $A$:}
{若 Shilov boundary 为 $\{\xi\}$，其 maximum property 对每个 $A$ 中的 element 给出}

\[
\|f\|_{\mathrm{sp}}=|f(\xi)|.
\]
\bi{The right side is multiplicative. Conversely, a multiplicative spectral seminorm is a bounded multiplicative seminorm extending the absolute value of the ground field, hence a point $\xi$ of $V$. It attains the supremum of every function. Thus $\{\xi\}$ is a boundary. Corollary~\ref{fnd:affinoidshilovinternal} gives the smallest closed boundary, which must therefore be $\{\xi\}$.}
{右端具有乘性。反之，具有乘性的谱半范数是延拓基域绝对值的有界乘性半范数，因而给出 $V$ 中的点 $\xi$。它实现每个函数的上确界，所以 $\{\xi\}$ 为边界。Corollary~\ref{fnd:affinoidshilovinternal} 给出最小闭边界，它因此必为 $\{\xi\}$。}

\end{proof}
