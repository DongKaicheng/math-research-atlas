Finite binary splitting trees

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Finite binary splitting trees}{有限二元分裂树}]\label{fnd:finitebinarytreecech}
\bi{Let $N$ be a Banach coefficient diagram as above. Take a finite rooted binary tree whose nodes are elements $U\in\mathscr R$, with root $X$ and children $U_+,U_-\leq U$. Assume that for every internal node $U$ and every $V\in\mathscr R$ the sequence}
{设 $N$ 为上述 Banach 系数图表。取一棵有限有根二叉树，结点为 $\mathscr R$ 中的元素 $U$，根为 $X$，每个分裂结点的两个子结点满足 $U_+,U_-\leq U$。假设对每个内部结点 $U$ 及每个 $V\in\mathscr R$，序列}
\[
 0\longrightarrow N(U\cap V)
 \longrightarrow N(U_+\cap V)\oplus N(U_-\cap V)
 \xrightarrow{(a,b)\mapsto b|-a|}
 N(U_+\cap U_-\cap V)\longrightarrow0
\]
\bi{is strictly exact. Then the augmented ordered \v Cech complex of the leaves, restricted to any $V\in\mathscr R$, is strictly acyclic in every degree. Its bounds depend only on finitely many restriction bounds and the assumed binary inverse and lifting bounds. The assertion is also valid for the complex using all tuples. It concerns the specified coefficient diagram: verifying the displayed hypothesis on all node intersections is part of applying the theorem.}
{均严格正合，则叶结点组成的族限制到任意 $V\in\mathscr R$ 后，其增广有序 \v Cech 复形在所有次数严格无环。其界只依赖有限多个限制映射界，以及假设中的二元逆映射界与提升界。使用全部多重指标的复形也有相同结论。这个断言针对所指定的系数图表；应用定理时必须验证所有结点交叠上的所列假设。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{We prove the ordered assertion simultaneously for all $V$, by induction on the number of internal nodes. With no internal node the leaf is $X$, and the complex is the identity $N(V)\to N(V)$. For the induction step, let $\mathcal P=(P_i)$ and $\mathcal Q=(Q_j)$ be the leaves of the two subtrees rooted at $U_+$ and $U_-$. Order all $P_i$ before all $Q_j$. The induction hypothesis applies to either subtree after restriction by any finite intersection in $\mathscr R$.}
{对内部结点数归纳，同时证明所有 $V$ 对应的有序结论。若没有内部结点，则唯一叶结点为 $X$，复形就是恒等映射 $N(V)\to N(V)$。归纳步骤中，记以 $U_+$、$U_-$ 为根的两棵子树的叶族分别为 $\mathcal P=(P_i)$ 与 $\mathcal Q=(Q_j)$，并将所有 $P_i$ 排在所有 $Q_j$ 之前。归纳假设适用于任一子树在 $\mathscr R$ 中任意有限交下的限制。}

\bi{Write $W=V\cap U_+\cap U_-$. The mixed terms are organized by the finite double complex}
{记 $W=V\cap U_+\cap U_-$。混合项组成有限双复形}
\[
 D^{p,q}=
 \prod_{i_0<\cdots<i_p}\prod_{j_0<\cdots<j_q}
 N(V\cap P_{i_0}\cap\cdots\cap P_{i_p}
        \cap Q_{j_0}\cap\cdots\cap Q_{j_q}).
\]
\bi{For a fixed $j$-tuple, its horizontal augmentation is the \v Cech complex of the left subtree restricted to $V\cap Q_{j_0}\cap\cdots\cap Q_{j_q}$. By induction this row is strictly exact, with augmentation term $N(U_+\cap V\cap Q_{j_0}\cap\cdots\cap Q_{j_q})$. These augmentation terms form $L^\bullet=C^\bullet(\mathcal Q,N;W)$, which is strictly acyclic after augmentation by $N(W)$, again by induction. Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictfinitecechcomparison} therefore proves that $N(W)\to\operatorname{Tot}(D)$ is strictly acyclic. The constants are finite because there are finitely many rows and terms.}
{固定一个 $j$ 多重指标后，其水平增广行是左子树限制到 $V\cap Q_{j_0}\cap\cdots\cap Q_{j_q}$ 上的 \v Cech 复形。由归纳假设，该行严格正合，增广项为 $N(U_+\cap V\cap Q_{j_0}\cap\cdots\cap Q_{j_q})$。这些增广项组成 $L^\bullet=C^\bullet(\mathcal Q,N;W)$；再次由归纳假设，加上 $N(W)$ 的增广后，它严格无环。故由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictfinitecechcomparison}，$N(W)\to\operatorname{Tot}(D)$ 严格无环。行与项的个数有限，因而所得常数有限。}

\bi{To see exactly how this total complex occurs, let $C^\bullet$ be the ordered complex of all leaves, without its augmentation, and let $K^\bullet$ be the kernel of restriction to the two pure subcomplexes. A mixed tuple has $p+1$ left indices followed by $q+1$ right indices and lies in degree $p+q+1$. The identification}
{为准确识别这个总复形的作用，记 $C^\bullet$ 为全部叶结点的有序复形，暂时不含增广；记 $K^\bullet$ 为向两个纯子复形作限制的核。一个混合多重指标先有 $p+1$ 个左指标，再有 $q+1$ 个右指标，位于 $p+q+1$ 次。识别}
\[
 \operatorname{Tot}(D)[-1]\ \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\ K^\bullet,
 \qquad a^{p,q}\longmapsto(-1)^p a^{p,q}
\]
\bi{is isometric and respects the differential. Indeed, deleting a left index gives $d_h$, while deleting a right index contributes $(-1)^{p+1}d_v$ in the leaf complex. Conjugation by $(-1)^p$ gives $-d_h-(-1)^pd_v$, which is the differential of the shift $[-1]$. This also fixes the sign of the degree-one obstruction.}
{为保持微分的等距同构。事实上，删去左指标给出 $d_h$，删去右指标在叶复形中给出 $(-1)^{p+1}d_v$。用 $(-1)^p$ 共轭后，得到 $-d_h-(-1)^pd_v$，恰为平移 $[-1]$ 的微分。这也确定了第一次障碍的符号。}

\bi{Let $z\in C^k$ be a cocycle with $k\geq1$. Its two pure restrictions have bounded primitives by induction. Extend these primitives by zero on mixed tuples and subtract their differential. The remaining cocycle $z'$ belongs to $K^k$, and its norm is bounded by a fixed multiple of $\|z\|$. If $k\geq2$, strict acyclicity of $\operatorname{Tot}(D)$ supplies a bounded primitive in $K^{k-1}$. If $k=1$, exactness at degree zero of the augmented total complex gives a unique $w\in N(W)$ whose restrictions are the mixed components of $z'$, with a bound for $\|w\|$. The assumed binary surjection at the root gives $a\in N(U_+\cap V)$ and $b\in N(U_-\cap V)$ with $b|-a|=w$ and a bounded maximum norm. Restrict $a,b$ to the leaves of their respective subtrees. Their differential is zero on pure tuples and equals $z'$ on mixed pairs. This supplies the required primitive also in degree one.}
{设 $z\in C^k$ 为上闭链，$k\geq1$。由归纳假设，两个纯限制都有范数受控的原像。将这些原像在混合多重指标上延伸为零，再减去其微分。余下的上闭链 $z'$ 属于 $K^k$，且其范数被 $\|z\|$ 的固定倍数控制。若 $k\geq2$，总复形的严格无环性在 $K^{k-1}$ 中给出有界原像。若 $k=1$，增广总复形在零次的正合性给出唯一 $w\in N(W)$，其限制恰为 $z'$ 的混合分量，且 $\|w\|$ 有界。根处假设的二元满射给出 $a\in N(U_+\cap V)$、$b\in N(U_-\cap V)$，满足 $b|-a|=w$，并且二者范数的最大值受控。将 $a,b$ 分别限制到各自子树的叶结点。所得微分在纯多重指标上为零，在混合指标对上等于 $z'$。这就在第一次也给出了所需原像。}

\bi{In degree zero, a compatible family on the leaves gives unique elements $a\in N(U_+\cap V)$ and $b\in N(U_-\cap V)$ by induction. Their difference in $N(W)$ restricts to zero on every mixed pair. The augmentation $N(W)\to\operatorname{Tot}^0(D)$ is injective, so $b|-a|=0$. Binary exactness gives a unique element of $N(V)$ inducing the family, with its norm bounded by the binary inverse bound and the two subtree bounds. If an element of $N(V)$ restricts to zero on all leaves, the same injectivity argument makes it zero. This proves strict acyclicity of the ordered complex.}
{在零次中，叶结点上的相容族由归纳假设给出唯一的 $a\in N(U_+\cap V)$ 与 $b\in N(U_-\cap V)$。它们在 $N(W)$ 中的差限制到每个混合指标对后均为零。由于增广 $N(W)\to\operatorname{Tot}^0(D)$ 单射，得 $b|-a|=0$。二元正合性便给出诱导该族的唯一元素，属于 $N(V)$；其范数由二元逆映射界及两棵子树的界控制。若 $N(V)$ 中一个元素在所有叶结点上限制为零，同样的单射性论证说明它为零。这证明有序复形严格无环。}

\bi{Finally, \cite[Tag 01FM, Lemma 20.23.6]{StacksAlg} identifies the ordered (equivalently alternating) complex and the complex using all tuples by homotopy equivalences. The finite-poset presheaf above verifies its scope. The sorting and repeated-index homotopies in equations (20.23.6.1)--(20.23.6.2) use only finite sums with coefficients $\pm1$ and the identity identifications of repeated intersections. Hence they are bounded in each degree, with norm at most one for the maximum norms here. The projection to increasing tuples and alternating extension are also contractive. If $f,g$ are the resulting comparison maps and $gf-1=dh+hd$, a positive-degree cocycle $z$ with $fz=dy$ has primitive $gy-hz$. This proves the required norm bounds for the full complex. The maps preserve the augmentation and the degree-zero cycles, so exactness and the inverse bound at the augmentation are preserved as well.}
{最后，由 \cite[Tag 01FM, Lemma 20.23.6]{StacksAlg}，有序复形（等价地，交错复形）与使用全部多重指标的复形同伦等价。上面的有限偏序集预层验证了该结果的适用范围。其公式 (20.23.6.1)--(20.23.6.2) 中的排序与去重复同伦只使用系数为 $\pm1$ 的有限和，以及重复交叠的恒等识别。因此它们在每个次数有界，对这里的最大范数，其范数至多为一。向递增多重指标的投影及交错延伸也为 contractive。若所得比较映射为 $f,g$，且 $gf-1=dh+hd$，则满足 $fz=dy$ 的正次数上闭链 $z$ 有原像 $gy-hz$。这给出全部指标复形所需的范数界。这些映射保持增广及零次上闭链，因而也保持增广处的正合性与逆映射界。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{These definitions do not assert that a member of $\mathscr R$ is an analytic domain. They also do not require a sheaf. The formal \v Cech results for presheaves apply to the intersection terms as follows. Give $\mathscr R$ the topology of downward closed subsets and write $O_U=\{V:V\leq U\}$. Then $O_U\cap O_V=O_{U\cap V}$. The prescription}
{这些定义没有断言 $\mathscr R$ 的元素为解析域，也不要求已经有一个层。对预层成立的形式 \v Cech 结果可如下应用于这些交叠项。给 $\mathscr R$ 配备向下闭子集组成的拓扑，记 $O_U=\{V:V\leq U\}$，则 $O_U\cap O_V=O_{U\cap V}$。规定}
\[
 \mathcal F(W)=\varprojlim_{O_U\subseteq W}N(U),\qquad
 \mathcal F(\varnothing)=0.
\]
\bi{Compatibility in this finite limit defines a presheaf. On $O_U$ its sections are naturally $N(U)$: the $U$-component determines every other component by restriction. These identifications are bounded with bounded inverses, and are isometric when restrictions are contractive. The family $(O_{U_i})$ is an open covering of its union, with exactly the required intersection terms. Its augmentation from $N(V)$ is the restriction from $O_V$ and is preserved by the comparison maps below. Thus the formal comparisons require no unproved geometric localization statement.}
{这个有限极限中的相容条件定义一个预层。在 $O_U$ 上，其截面自然等于 $N(U)$：$U$ 分量通过限制决定其余所有分量。这些识别及其逆有界；若限制均为 contractive，则它们等距。族 $(O_{U_i})$ 是其并集的开覆盖，交叠项恰好为所需各项。从 $N(V)$ 出发的增广来自 $O_V$ 上的限制，且被以下比较映射保持。因此这些形式比较不需要任何尚未证明的几何局部化结论。}
