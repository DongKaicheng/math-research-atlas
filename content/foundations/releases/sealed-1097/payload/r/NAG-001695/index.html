Affinoid products and pairs of points

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 278

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Affinoid products and pairs of points}{Affinoid 积与点对}]
\label{fnd:affinoidproducttopology}
\bi{For bounded maps $A\to B,A\to C$ of generalized $K$-affinoid algebras, $\mathcal M(B\widehat\otimes_A C)$ is their fibre product in the affinoid category. The natural map to the ordinary topological fibre product}
{对广义 $K$-affinoid 代数的有界映射 $A\to B,A\to C$，$\mathcal M(B\widehat\otimes_A C)$ 是 affinoid 范畴中的纤维积。到普通拓扑纤维积的自然映射}
\[
 \mathcal M(B\widehat\otimes_A C)
 \longrightarrow
 \mathcal M(B)\times_{\mathcal M(A)}^{\mathrm{top}}\mathcal M(C)
\]
\bi{is continuous, closed and surjective.}
{连续、闭且满。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The categorical assertion is the opposite of the bounded pushout property in Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout}. Restriction gives the displayed continuous map. Its target is a closed subspace of the compact Hausdorff product $\mathcal M(B)\times\mathcal M(C)$: it is the inverse image of the diagonal of $\mathcal M(A)$. Compactness of the source therefore proves closedness.}
{范畴断言就是 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout} 的有界推出性质在反范畴中的表述。限制给出所列连续映射。其目标是紧 Hausdorff 积 $\mathcal M(B)\times\mathcal M(C)$ 的闭子空间，因为它是 $\mathcal M(A)$ 的对角线的逆像。因此源空间的紧性给出闭性。}

\bi{Take $y\in\mathcal M(B)$ and $z\in\mathcal M(C)$ mapping to $x\in\mathcal M(A)$, and put $F=\mathcal H(x)$, $E=\mathcal H(y)$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres} identifies $z$ with a point of the $F$-affinoid $C_x=C\widehat\otimes_A F$. The isometric embedding $F\hookrightarrow E$ makes $E/F$ a complete valued extension. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarextensionspectralsurjective}, the point $z$ lifts to a point $w$ of $C_x\widehat\otimes_F E$. In $\mathcal H(w)$ the maps from $C$ and $B\to E$ agree on $A$. Their bounded product therefore gives a point of $\mathcal M(B\widehat\otimes_A C)$. Its restrictions are $z$ and $y$: the first follows from the choice of $w$, and the second because evaluation on $E$ is its prescribed absolute value. This proves surjectivity.}
{取 $y\in\mathcal M(B)$、$z\in\mathcal M(C)$，设二者都映到 $x\in\mathcal M(A)$，并令 $F=\mathcal H(x)$、$E=\mathcal H(y)$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres} 将 $z$ 识别为 $F$-affinoid $C_x=C\widehat\otimes_A F$ 的一个点。等距嵌入 $F\hookrightarrow E$ 使 $E/F$ 成为完备赋值扩域。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarextensionspectralsurjective}，点 $z$ 提升为 $C_x\widehat\otimes_F E$ 的一个点 $w$。在 $\mathcal H(w)$ 中，来自 $C$ 及 $B\to E$ 的映射在 $A$ 上一致。因此其有界乘积给出 $\mathcal M(B\widehat\otimes_A C)$ 的一个点。它的限制为 $z,y$：前者来自 $w$ 的选择，后者因为在 $E$ 上的取值就是其指定绝对值。这证明满射性。}
\end{proof}
