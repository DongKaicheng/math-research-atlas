Doubling at a rational point in any characteristic

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 743

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Doubling at a rational point in any characteristic}
{任意特征下沿有理点倍接}]
\label{cand:rationalgenusonestabledouble}
\bi{Let $F$ be a field of any characteristic and let $C/F$ be a
smooth projective geometrically connected genus-one curve with
a specified point $P\in C(F)$. Gluing two labelled copies of $C$
at $P$ gives a stable curve of genus two whose normalization is
$C\amalg C$.}
{设 $F$ 为任意特征的域，$C/F$ 为光滑射影几何连通的亏格一曲线，
并指定 $P\in C(F)$。沿 $P$ 粘合 $C$ 的两个带标签副本，
得到亏格二稳定曲线，其正规化为 $C\amalg C$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use the pushout and integral-closure construction in the proof
of Lemma~\ref{cand:genusonestabledouble}, with $E=F$ and $d=1$.
That proof used characteristic zero only to ensure separability
of the chosen closed-point residue field. Here the residue
extension is the identity. The same fibre-product local ring,
finite normalization map, exact cohomology sequence, and
no-rational-tail-or-bridge criterion therefore apply over $F$
and after every field extension. The computed genus is $d+1=2$.}
{在 Lemma~\ref{cand:genusonestabledouble} 的证明中，取
$E=F$、$d=1$，使用同一个推出与整闭包构造。
该证明只在保证所选闭点剩余扩张可分时使用特征零；
此处剩余扩张为恒等扩张。因此同一个纤维积局部环、有限正规化
映射、正合上同调列，以及无有理尾和有理桥的判据，都在 $F$
及每个域扩张后适用。计算所得亏格为 $d+1=2$。}
\end{proof}
