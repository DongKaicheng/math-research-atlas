Bounded lifting for Banach surjections

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 83

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Bounded lifting for Banach surjections}{Banach 满射的有界提升}]
\label{fnd:nonarchopenmapping}
\bi{Assume that the absolute value on the complete field $K$ is nontrivial. If $u:E\to F$ is a bounded surjective $K$-linear map between non-Archimedean Banach spaces, there is $C>0$ such that every $y\in F$ has a lift $x\in E$ with $\|x\|\leq C\|y\|$. In particular, $u$ is open and norm-strict.}
{设完备域 $K$ 的绝对值非平凡。若 $u:E\to F$ 是非阿基米德 Banach 空间之间的有界满 $K$-线性映射，则存在 $C>0$，使每个 $y\in F$ 都有提升 $x\in E$ 满足 $\|x\|\leq C\|y\|$。特别地，$u$ 为开映射且范数严格。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose $\pi\in K$ with $0<|\pi|<1$, and put $E_1=\{x:\|x\|\leq1\}$. Surjectivity gives $F=\bigcup_{n\geq0}u(\pi^{-n}E_1)$. The additive group of $F$ is linearly topologized by its norm balls, complete, and has a countable neighbourhood basis. Hence the Baire theorem in \cite[Tag 0CQU, Lemma 15.37.3]{StacksAlg} shows that the closure of one of these images has nonempty interior. The images are additive subgroups, so their closures are subgroups. Translation and scalar multiplication therefore give some $r>0$ such that}
{选择 $\pi\in K$ 满足 $0<|\pi|<1$，并令 $E_1=\{x:\|x\|\leq1\}$。满射性给出 $F=\bigcup_{n\geq0}u(\pi^{-n}E_1)$。$F$ 的加法群以范数球为线性拓扑的邻域基，完备且具有可数邻域基。因此，\cite[Tag 0CQU, Lemma 15.37.3]{StacksAlg} 中的 Baire 定理说明某个像的闭包具有非空内部。这些像都是加法子群，其闭包亦然。平移并作标量乘法后，得到某个 $r>0$ 使}
\[
 \{y:\|y\|\leq r\}\subset\overline{u(E_1)}.
\]
\bi{For nonzero $y$, choose an integer $k$ with $|\pi|r<\|\pi^k y\|\leq r$. Approximate $\pi^ky$ by $u(z)$, $z\in E_1$, so closely that the error is at most $|\pi|\,\|\pi^ky\|$. Then $x=\pi^{-k}z$ satisfies}
{对非零 $y$，选取整数 $k$ 使 $|\pi|r<\|\pi^k y\|\leq r$。用某个 $z\in E_1$ 的像 $u(z)$ 逼近 $\pi^ky$，使误差不超过 $|\pi|\,\|\pi^ky\|$。于是 $x=\pi^{-k}z$ 满足}
\[
 \|x\|\leq (|\pi|r)^{-1}\|y\|,
 \qquad \|y-u(x)\|\leq |\pi|\,\|y\|.
\]
\bi{Repeat this approximation for the successive errors. The resulting corrections $x_j$ have norms at most $(|\pi|r)^{-1}|\pi|^j\|y\|$, so their sum converges in $E$ and maps to $y$. The ultrametric inequality gives the asserted bound on the sum. Zero has the zero lift. A small ball in $F$ consequently lifts into any prescribed ball in $E$, proving openness and the quotient-norm assertion.}
{对逐次误差重复这个逼近。所得修正项 $x_j$ 的范数不超过 $(|\pi|r)^{-1}|\pi|^j\|y\|$，所以它们在 $E$ 中的和收敛且映到 $y$。超度量不等式给出该和所需的范数界。零元素取零提升。因此 $F$ 中充分小的球可提升到 $E$ 中任意指定的球，开映射性与商范数断言随之成立。}
\end{proof}
