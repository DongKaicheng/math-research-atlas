Retraction to the inverse-image skeleton

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 893

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Retraction to the inverse-image skeleton}
{到逆像骨架的收缩}]
\label{cand:etalechartskeletonretraction}
\bi{For the structure map $f:\mathcal M(B)\to V$, put
$\Sigma_B=f^{-1}(\Sigma_a)$. Then $H_B$ is a strong
deformation retraction onto $\Sigma_B$. It satisfies the
maximum-parameter composition law for all $s,t\in[0,1]$.
Every fibre of $\Sigma_B\to\Sigma_a$ is finite.}
{对结构映射 $f:\mathcal M(B)\to V$，置
$\Sigma_B=f^{-1}(\Sigma_a)$。则 $H_B$ 是到
$\Sigma_B$ 的强形变收缩，并对全部 $s,t\in[0,1]$
满足参数取最大值的复合律。映射
$\Sigma_B\to\Sigma_a$ 的每个纤维有限。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The fibre over any $z\in V$ is the affinoid spectrum
of $B\widehat\otimes_A\mathcal H(z)$ by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres}. The equations
$F=0$, $Q\det J-1=0$ in the $m+1$ variables $(Y,Q)$
have the block Jacobian}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres}，任意
$z\in V$ 上的纤维为
$B\widehat\otimes_A\mathcal H(z)$ 的 affinoid 谱。
关于 $m+1$ 个变量 $(Y,Q)$，方程 $F=0$、
$Q\det J-1=0$ 的分块 Jacobian 为}
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}
 J&0\\ Q\,\partial(\det J)/\partial Y&\det J
 \end{pmatrix},
 \qquad \det=(\det J)^2\in B^\times.
\]
\bi{The matrix criterion
Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianmatrixcriterion} therefore gives
a rank-zero quasi-smooth presentation at every fibre point.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasismoothrankzeroisolated} makes every
such point isolated. The fibre is compact, so this discrete
space is finite.}
{因此 Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianmatrixcriterion} 的
矩阵判据在每个纤维点给出秩零准光滑表示。
Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasismoothrankzeroisolated} 说明每个
这样的点都孤立。纤维紧，故此离散空间有限。}

\bi{If $x\in\Sigma_B$, the projected trajectory is
constant because the standard homotopy fixes $\Sigma_a$.
Thus the continuous trajectory $H_B(x,t)$ lies in a finite
discrete fibre; connectedness of $[0,1]$ makes it constant.
Conversely every endpoint projects to
$s(\operatorname{val}(f(x)))\in\Sigma_a$, so the endpoint
image is precisely $\Sigma_B$. The time-zero identity,
joint continuity and skeletal fixedness prove the strong
deformation retraction assertion. The composition law was
proved for strict times and, when a parameter is one, in
Lemma~\ref{cand:etaledisplacementpointwiseendpoint}.
The preceding fibre calculation gives the final statement.}
{若 $x\in\Sigma_B$，标准同伦固定 $\Sigma_a$，故
投影后的轨迹恒定。因此连续轨迹 $H_B(x,t)$ 落在
一个有限离散纤维内，区间 $[0,1]$ 的连通性使其
恒定。反之，每个终点都投影到
$s(\operatorname{val}(f(x)))\in\Sigma_a$，所以终点
像恰为 $\Sigma_B$。时刻零的恒等性、联合连续性及
骨架固定性，给出强形变收缩。严格小于一的时刻的
复合律已证，而某个参数为一的情形见
Lemma~\ref{cand:etaledisplacementpointwiseendpoint}。
前述纤维计算给出最后一项。}
\end{proof}
