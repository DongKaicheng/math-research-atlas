Equivariant algebraization of a supplied nodal tower

D Polarized towers with compatible cyclic actions

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1977

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Equivariant algebraization of a supplied nodal tower]
\label{cand:f5eq:equivariantalgebraization}
Let $F$ be a complete nontrivially real-valued field,
$R=F^\circ$, $\mathfrak m_R=F^{\circ\circ}$, and
$k_R=R/\mathfrak m_R$. Let a cyclic group $G$ of order $e\geq1$
act isometrically on $F$, and put
$b_g=\operatorname{Spec}(g^{-1}|_R)$. Fix
$0\ne\pi\in\mathfrak m_R$, set $R_n=R/\pi^nR$, and suppose given
proper, flat, finitely presented $R_n$-schemes $X_n$ with cartesian
transition closed immersions $i_n:X_n\hookrightarrow X_{n+1}$.
Suppose compatible semilinear $G$-actions on these schemes are
specified, covering the induced $b_{g,n}$.
Assume that $C=X_1\times_{R_1}k_R$ is at-worst-nodal of pure
dimension one and that the action on the \emph{entire scheme} $C$
is the identity.

For any ample invertible sheaf $\mathcal A_C$ on $C$, one can choose
compatible ample lifts $\mathcal A_n$ and form the orbit tensors
\[
 \mathcal N_n=\bigotimes_{j=0}^{e-1}(\tau_n^j)^*\mathcal A_n,
 \tag{4}
\]
where $\tau_n$ is the action of a generator of $G$.
These form a compatible $G$-linearized ample tower, with identity
action on $\mathcal N_n|_C\simeq\mathcal A_C^{\otimes e}$.
There exist a projective, flat, finitely presented nodal
$R$-scheme $Y'$ of relative dimension one, an ample invertible
sheaf $\mathcal N'$ on $Y'$, a semilinear $G$-action on $Y'$,
and a compatible linearization of $\mathcal N'$, together with
compatible equivariant polarized identifications
\[
              (Y',\mathcal N')\times_R R_n
                         \simeq (X_n,\mathcal N_n).
 \tag{5}
\]
The actions on both $Y'_{k_R}$ and $\mathcal N'|_{Y'_{k_R}}$
are the identity. For a fixed polarized tower (4), this
algebraization is unique up to the unique compatible equivariant
polarized isomorphism. Invertibility of $e$ is not required here.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The isometries preserve $R$ and every ideal $\pi^nR$: membership
in this ideal is the inequality $v(a)\geq n v(\pi)$.
Hence all indicated actions on quotient bases exist. The rank-one
completeness argument in \dep{cand:f5alg:algebraization} gives
$R=\varprojlim R_n$.

The exact result \dep{cand:f5alg:towerpolarization} gives the
compatible ample $\mathcal A_n$, with transition isomorphisms
$\varphi_n:i_n^*\mathcal A_{n+1}\simeq\mathcal A_n$ and a
specified restriction to $\mathcal A_C$. It also supplies at
least one ample $\mathcal A_C$, if none was prescribed.
Apply \dep{cand:f5proj:orbittensor} at each level. Its hypotheses
hold because $X_n$ is separated over the affine base
$\operatorname{Spec}R_n$. It gives ampleness of (4), its canonical
permutation linearization, and identity on the full restricted
line bundle on $C$.

We check the extra tower compatibility explicitly. Equivariance
of $i_n$ gives canonical identifications
\[
 i_n^*(\tau_{n+1}^j)^*\mathcal A_{n+1}
       \simeq(\tau_n^j)^*i_n^*\mathcal A_{n+1}.
\]
Tensor the maps $(\tau_n^j)^*\varphi_n$ over all $j$ to obtain
$\nu_n:i_n^*\mathcal N_{n+1}\simeq\mathcal N_n$.
Applying the cyclic permutation linearization before or after
this tensor map permutes the same indexed factors and applies the
same maps on them. Thus $\nu_n$ is equivariant. Composing these
maps gives compatibility at all levels. Their restrictions to
$C$ identify (4) with $\mathcal A_C^{\otimes e}$, with the
identity action already given by the orbit-tensor result.

Now apply \dep{cand:f5alg:algebraization} to the ordinary polarized
tower $(X_n,\mathcal N_n,\nu_n)$. It gives the projective flat
finitely presented pair $(Y',\mathcal N')$ and the underlying
polarized identifications (5). The nodal hypothesis on $C$ gives
nodality of relative dimension one. At this stage no action on
$Y'$ has been asserted.

Let $b$ be the base automorphism corresponding to the chosen
generator. Via (5), its tower action and linearization are
compatible polarized $R_n$-isomorphisms from
$(Y',\mathcal N')_{R_n}$ to its $b_n$-twist. Both algebraic
pairs $(Y',\mathcal N')$ and $b^*(Y',\mathcal N')$ satisfy
Lemma~\ref{cand:f5eq:polarizedfullfaithfulness}. That lemma gives
a unique polarized $R$-isomorphism between them. Composing its
scheme map with the first projection from the twist gives a
semilinear automorphism $\tau:Y'\to Y'$ covering $b$, and its
line-bundle map is $\lambda:\tau^*\mathcal N'\simeq\mathcal N'$.

The $e$-fold composite of this polarized semilinear automorphism
covers $b^e=\operatorname{id}$. At every level it is the identity
polarized automorphism, by the given action and its linearization.
Injectivity in Lemma~\ref{cand:f5eq:polarizedfullfaithfulness}
therefore makes it the identity on the algebraic pair. The iterates
of $(\tau,\lambda)$ give the required $G$-action and linearization,
and (5) is equivariant. Restricting (5) for $n=1$ further to $C$
proves the full residual identities. Finally, the same lemma gives
a unique polarized comparison of any two algebraizations with
(5). Its reductions commute with the specified actions, so
injectivity makes the comparison equivariant.
\end{proof}
