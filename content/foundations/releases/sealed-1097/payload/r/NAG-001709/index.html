Products and extension of the ground field

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 286

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Products and extension of the ground field}{乘积与基域扩张}]
\label{fnd:analytificationproducts}
\bi{For finite-type $K$-schemes, analytification commutes with fibre products. For every complete valued extension $L/K$, there is a natural analytic isomorphism}
{对有限型 $K$-概形，解析化与纤维积交换。对每个完备赋值扩域 $L/K$，有自然解析同构}
\[
 (Z\times_K L)^{\an}\simeq Z^{\an}\widehat\otimes_K L .
\]
\bi{Both identifications respect all scheme morphisms and iterated complete extensions.}
{这两个识别均与全部概形态射及迭代完备扩域相容。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For affine schemes defined by $R\to S$ and $R\to T$, the mapping property of Theorem~\ref{fnd:affineanalytificationconstruction} gives, for every analytic $Y$,}
{对环映射 $R\to S$ 与 $R\to T$ 定义的仿射概形，Theorem~\ref{fnd:affineanalytificationconstruction} 的映射性质对每个解析空间 $Y$ 给出}
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}(Y,X_{S\otimes_R T})
 \simeq
 \operatorname{Hom}(Y,X_S)\mathop{\times}_{\operatorname{Hom}(Y,X_R)}
 \operatorname{Hom}(Y,X_T).
\]
\bi{The middle algebraic step is the defining universal property of the ordinary tensor product of commutative algebras. Thus $X_{S\otimes_R T}$ is the analytic fibre product. To pass to schemes, cover the target by affine opens and both sources over these opens by affine opens. Their pairwise fibre products cover the scheme fibre product by affine opens. Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationzariskiopens} identifies the overlap maps, so the affine isomorphisms glue, with the universal property checked on the same cover.}
{其中的代数步骤就是交换代数普通张量积的定义泛性质。因此 $X_{S\otimes_R T}$ 为解析纤维积。对一般概形，用仿射开集覆盖目标，再分别用仿射开集覆盖两个源空间在这些开集上的逆像。两两纤维积构成概形纤维积的仿射开覆盖。Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationzariskiopens} 识别重叠映射，所以这些仿射同构可粘合，并可在同一覆盖上核验泛性质。}

\bi{If $R=K[T]/I$, its scalar extension is $L[T]/IL[T]$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalscalarpresentation} gives, on every radius piece,}
{若 $R=K[T]/I$，则其扩标量为 $L[T]/IL[T]$。Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalscalarpresentation} 在每个半径分片上给出}
\[
 \bigl(K\{r^{-1}T\}/I K\{r^{-1}T\}\bigr)
       \widehat\otimes_K L
 \simeq L\{r^{-1}T\}/I L\{r^{-1}T\}.
\]
\bi{The transition maps agree on polynomials and hence agree everywhere by continuity. Thus these isomorphisms glue over the exhaustion; they further glue over affine Zariski covers. The formulas on polynomial coordinates also prove compatibility with morphisms. Iterated extension agrees by Corollary~\ref{fnd:iteratedaffinoidbasechange}, applied piecewise.}
{过渡映射在多项式上相同，故由连续性处处相同。因此这些同构沿穷竭粘合，并进一步沿仿射 Zariski 覆盖粘合。多项式坐标公式也证明与态射相容。对每个分片应用 Corollary~\ref{fnd:iteratedaffinoidbasechange}，即得与迭代扩域的相容性。}
\end{proof}
