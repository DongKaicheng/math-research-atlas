The integral-affine skeleton of a formal model

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The integral-affine skeleton of a formal model}
{形式模型的整仿射骨架}]
\label{cand:globalintegralaffineskeleton}
\bi{Let $\mathfrak X$ satisfy
Theorem~\ref{cand:globalsemistablehomotopy}. Its canonical
fixed set $\Sigma(\mathfrak X)$ is a finite
$\Gamma$-integral-affine face-glued cell complex of closed
simplices. Distinct cells may have the same vertex set.
Each affine
formal semistable chart supplies exactly the cells of
Theorem~\ref{cand:etaleskeletonfinitefaces}. The cell
structure and its integral-affine transition maps are
independent of the chosen formal chart atlas.
The homotopy of Theorem~\ref{cand:globalsemistablehomotopy}
is a jointly continuous strong deformation retraction
onto this finite complex.}
{设 $\mathfrak X$ 满足
Theorem~\ref{cand:globalsemistablehomotopy} 的假设。
其典范不动点集 $\Sigma(\mathfrak X)$ 为有限个闭
$\Gamma$-整仿射单纯形沿面粘合而成的胞腔复形。
不同胞腔允许具有同一顶点集。每个仿射形式半稳定
坐标片恰给出 Theorem~\ref{cand:etaleskeletonfinitefaces}
中的胞腔。胞腔结构及其整仿射过渡映射与所选形式坐标图册
无关。Theorem~\ref{cand:globalsemistablehomotopy} 中的
同伦是到此有限复形的联合连续强形变收缩。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Write $\operatorname{sp}$ for specialization to
$\mathfrak X_s$. In one chart, the relative interior
of the cell labelled by $\eta$ is precisely}
{以 $\operatorname{sp}$ 记到 $\mathfrak X_s$ 的特化。
在一个坐标片中，带标签 $\eta$ 的胞腔的相对内部恰为}
\[
 E_\eta=\{x\in\Sigma(\mathfrak X):
                              \operatorname{sp}(x)=\eta\}.
\]
\bi{This is the actual-fibre classification in
Proposition~\ref{cand:actualskeletalfibrestrata}.
There is no restriction to the chart on the right:
the inverse image under specialization of its formal
open set is its generic domain, by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}.
Thus whenever a second chart contains $\eta$, the
same set $E_\eta$ is one of its relative open cells.
For clarity, choose $x\in E_\eta$. The canonical fixed-set
identity makes its image in the second standard base a
Gauss point. Its specialization is the generic point of
the unique coordinate stratum whose vanishing coordinates
are those belonging to $\eta$. The actual-fibre classification
therefore puts $\eta$ in that chart's corresponding $Q_I$.
The same classification identifies its whole relative
open cell with $E_\eta$. This proves intrinsically which
residual generic points label cells, without choosing a
global ordering of the special-fibre branches.
Its closure in the global Hausdorff generic fibre is
the closed simplex supplied by either chart, since
each generic affinoid chart is compact and closed.
Consequently the cells themselves are independent of
charts.}
{这就是 Proposition~\ref{cand:actualskeletalfibrestrata}
的实际纤维分类。右侧无需限制到该坐标片：由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}，其形式开集
在特化映射下的逆像就是其泛纤维域。因此，只要第二个
坐标片包含 $\eta$，同一个集合 $E_\eta$ 就是其中一个
相对开胞腔。具体地，选取 $x\in E_\eta$。
典范不动点集恒等式说明，它在第二个标准基底中的像为
Gauss 点。其特化为唯一坐标分层的泛点，该分层的消失
坐标恰为属于 $\eta$ 的坐标。因此实际纤维分类把 $\eta$
放入第二片相应的 $Q_I$，并将整个相对开胞腔识别为
$E_\eta$。这内禀地确定了哪些剩余泛点标记胞腔，无须
为特殊纤维的分支选择全局排序。它在全局 Hausdorff 泛纤维中的闭包，就是
任一坐标片给出的闭单纯形，因为每个泛纤维 affinoid
坐标片均紧且闭。所以胞腔本身与坐标片无关。}

\bi{Compare two coordinate systems on such a cell.
Choose a common principal formal neighbourhood of
$\eta$ in their overlap. Its generic domain contains
the entire closed cell: it contains $E_\eta$ and is
closed, or equivalently one may use the formal-open
subcomplex assertion of
Theorem~\ref{cand:etaleskeletonfinitefaces}.
It is admissible by Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalprincipalopenflat},
and its map to either standard base has a bounded
presentation by Proposition~\ref{cand:semistableformalopenrestriction}.
Every monomial coordinate of the second chart is an
analytic unit on this common domain, since the product
of those coordinates is a nonzero scalar.
Theorem~\ref{cand:etalecellunitsaffine} therefore makes
each of its logarithmic coordinates a
$\Gamma$-integral-affine function of the first ones.}
{比较此胞腔上的两组坐标。在两片交叠内，选取 $\eta$
的公共主形式邻域。其泛纤维域包含整个闭胞腔：它包含
$E_\eta$ 且为闭；也可直接应用
Theorem~\ref{cand:etaleskeletonfinitefaces} 的形式开集
子复形断言。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalprincipalopenflat}，
它 admissible；由 Proposition~\ref{cand:semistableformalopenrestriction}，
它到任一标准基底的映射均有有界表示。
第二个坐标片的每个单项式坐标在此公共域上都是解析单位元，
因为这些坐标的乘积为非零标量。
Theorem~\ref{cand:etalecellunitsaffine} 因而说明，其每个
对数坐标都是第一组坐标的 $\Gamma$-整仿射函数。}

\bi{Reverse the two charts to obtain the same assertion
for the inverse coordinate change. The linear parts are
mutually inverse maps of the integer tangent lattices
$N_I=\{(n_i)\in\mathbf Z^I:\sum n_i=0\}$.
They are therefore unimodular isomorphisms. Indeed,
the coordinate compositions are identities on a
relative open simplex, so their derivatives are inverse
real linear maps; both preserve the displayed lattices.
The affine constants belong to $\Gamma$, and all face
maps are the already proved coordinate face inclusions.}
{交换两个坐标片，对逆坐标变换得到相同结论。
它们的线性部分是整数切格
$N_I=\{(n_i)\in\mathbf Z^I:\sum n_i=0\}$ 之间的
互逆映射，因而为幺模同构。事实上，坐标复合在相对开
单纯形上为恒等映射，所以其导数为互逆实线性映射，且
二者都保持所列整数格。仿射常数属于 $\Gamma$，全部
面映射则为已经证明的坐标面嵌入。}

\bi{There are finitely many cells because the given
formal atlas and the cell lists in its charts are finite.
Their intersections are unions of common faces. To see
this for cells in different charts, first intersect the
second cell with the first formal open set.
The formal-open subcomplex assertion expresses this
intersection as a finite union of its closed faces.
Each such face now lies in the first chart, where the
common-face property has already been proved. These
faces are the same global cells by the intrinsic
description $E_\eta$ above. Finally the map from the
finite face-gluing quotient of the compact simplices
to $\Sigma(\mathfrak X)$ is a continuous bijection
from a compact space to a Hausdorff space, hence is a
homeomorphism. This proves the asserted finite complex
and its intrinsic integral-affine structure. The
previously proved global homotopy has exactly this
fixed set and remains the claimed strong deformation
retraction.}
{所给形式图册有限，每个坐标片的胞腔列表也有限，故胞腔
总数有限。胞腔的交为公共面的并。对位于不同坐标片中的
胞腔，先将第二个胞腔与第一个形式开集相交。
形式开集子复形断言把此交写为其有限个闭面的并。
每个这样的面都位于第一个坐标片中，那里已经证明公共面
性质。由上述 $E_\eta$ 的内禀描述，这些面就是同一批全局
胞腔。最后，从紧单纯形的有限沿面粘合商到
$\Sigma(\mathfrak X)$ 的映射为从紧空间到 Hausdorff
空间的连续双射，故为同胚。这证明所称有限复形及其内禀
整仿射结构。已经证明的全局同伦恰以此集合为不动点集，
仍为所述强形变收缩。}
\end{proof}
