Actual initial models along a projective skeleton

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1082

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Actual initial models along a projective skeleton]
\label{v038:faithful:wholefibreinitial}
In the stated model setting, the tuple $F$ of
Lemma~\ref{v038:faithful:chartpreparation} can be chosen so
that the resulting closed immersion
$\varphi:U\hookrightarrow\mathbf G_{m,K}^N$ has the
following additional property. Let $s\in S$, put
$\omega=\trop_\varphi(s)$, and choose a refined chart
containing its specialization, with label $(I,\eta)$.
For every complete isometric $L/K$ realizing the coordinates
of $\omega$ in its value group, the actual initial scheme
of $U_L$ at $\omega$ is isomorphic to a principal open of
\[
             E_{\lambda,I,\eta,\widetilde L}
                          \times\mathbf G_{m,\widetilde L}^{|I|-1}
\]
which meets every geometric irreducible component.
In particular this initial scheme is smooth and geometrically
reduced. The quotient norm of the normalized tropical-fibre
presentation is its spectral norm, and its integral quotient
model is the spectral unit ball.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Use precisely the finite lists constructed in
Lemma~\ref{v038:faithful:chartpreparation}. Fix $s$ and its
chart, and write $h=h_{\lambda,I,\eta}$. The normalization
field $L$ exists by Theorem~\ref{cand:realinitialdegeneration};
the argument below works for every such $L$.
Choose the standard-coordinate normalizers with product
$a_\lambda$ as in Proposition~\ref{v038:faithful:weightedchart}.
Let $B_0$ be that proposition's selected weighted model.

We first locate the entire tropical fibre. Every point of
the actual fibre
\[
                  Y_\omega=\trop_\varphi^{-1}(\omega)
                            \subset U^{\an}
\]
has, on every algebra generator of the selected chart, the
same absolute value as $s$, which is at most one. Algebraically
$U$ is contained in this chart by construction. Hence the
actual bounded-coordinate description of
Lemma~\ref{cand:affineflatformalcompletion} puts the entire
fibre inside the completed chart. The coordinates $X_i$
impose its weights $w_i$, and the coordinate $h$ imposes
$|h|=|h(s)|=1$. The same argument over $L$ places the entire
base-changed fibre in $\mathcal M_L(B_0[1/\pi])$.
This also shows explicitly how points outside the selected
formal chart are excluded: their chart-generator norms
cannot satisfy all these equalities.

For each $p\in P$, choose $b_p\in L^\times$ with
$|b_p|=|p(s)|$. The function $p$ is regular on this chart
because $\mathcal V_\lambda\subset\mathcal W$.
Proposition~\ref{v038:faithful:weightedchart} gives this same
norm at every Shilov point of $B_0[1/\pi]$.
Lemma~\ref{v038:faithful:peaklocalization} therefore gives
\[
 B'_0=B_0\langle V_p:p\in P\rangle/
                                  ((p/b_p)V_p-1:p\in P).
\]
Its reduction is a principal open of the stated smooth
product and meets every geometric component.

Its generic fibre is exactly $Y_{\omega,L}$. In one direction
this follows from the preceding containment and the
equalities $|p|=|p(s)|$. In the other direction those
equalities make all members of $P$ invertible and thus
put a point in $U_L^{\an}$. Every remaining coordinate of
$F$ is $p_f/q_f$ with $p_f,q_f\in P$, so it has the
required absolute value as well. The presentations of
rational domains identify the analytic structures, not only
the sets of points; equivalently, use
Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalcompletebasechange} and the
universal property of these displayed equations.

It remains to identify the integral model with the actual
initial quotient. Choose normalizers $b_f$ for all
coordinates of $F$. Changing them by norm-one constants
only changes torus coordinates. Put
\[
       T_0=L^\circ\langle Z_f^{\pm1}:f\in F\rangle,
       \qquad T=T_0[1/\pi],
       \qquad Z_f\longmapsto f/b_f.
\]
All these images and their inverses belong to $B'_0$.
For $f=p_f/q_f$ this follows by using the normalized
$p_f,q_f$ and a norm-one scalar. The map $T_0\to B'_0$
is surjective, as can be checked on its actual restricted
presentation. Every chart algebra generator $u$ is the
image of $b_u Z_u$, with $b_u\in L^\circ$ because
$|u(s)|\leq1$. Every $U_i^{\pm1}$ is the image of
$Z_{X_i}^{\pm1}$ multiplied by a norm-one scalar.
The generator $h^{-1}$ is the image of a norm-one scalar
times $Z_h^{-1}$. Finally the generators $(p/b_p)^{-1}$
are the images of $Z_p^{-1}$. These are all the generators
in the displayed construction of $B'_0$.
Their lifts are integral Laurent polynomials of Gauss norm
at most one. Substitution lifts every integral restricted
series in them to $T_0$, by Proposition~\ref{fnd:substitution}.
Thus the map is surjective, rather than merely having
dense image.

Let $\mathfrak a$ be the algebraic ideal of the closed
immersion $U_L\hookrightarrow\mathbf G_{m,L}^N$, in these
normalized coordinates. Proposition~\ref{cand:torusfibrealgebra}
identifies the generic map with
\[
                         T/\mathfrak aT\ \simeq\ B'_0[1/\pi].
\]
Since $B'_0$ is $L^\circ$-flat and is embedded in its
generic algebra, the kernel of $T_0\to B'_0$ is precisely
$\mathfrak aT\cap T_0$. Consequently
\[
                   T_0/(\mathfrak aT\cap T_0)\ \simeq\ B'_0.
\]
Proposition~\ref{cand:initialcompletionreduction} identifies
the reduction of this quotient with the actual initial
scheme of the algebraic immersion. Its special fibre was
computed above. Lemma~\ref{cand:reducedspecialcanonical}
now identifies the quotient norm with the spectral norm
and the quotient model with the spectral unit ball.
The arbitrary-real-weight interpretation and independence
of normalization are those of
Theorem~\ref{cand:realinitialdegeneration}; the calculation
has retained every component throughout.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
Let $K$ be complete for its specified nontrivial real-valued
absolute value. Write $O=K^\circ$, $k=\widetilde K$, and
$v=-\log|\cdot|$, and fix $\pi\in O$ with $0<|\pi|<1$.
We use the algebraic model setting of
Theorem~\ref{v035:faithfulglobal}: $\mathcal X$ is a given
projective, flat, finitely presented $O$-scheme, its generic
fibre $X$ is smooth and integral, and a finite collection of
affine opens covering $\mathcal X_s$ has \etale maps to
\[
 \operatorname{Spec}R_\lambda,\qquad
 R_\lambda=
 O[X_0,\ldots,X_{r_\lambda},T_1,\ldots,T_{q_\lambda}]/
                 (X_0\cdots X_{r_\lambda}-a_\lambda),
 \quad 0<|a_\lambda|<1.
\]
Let $S\subset X^{\an}$ be the compact skeleton of the completed
model. All rational functions below belong to the original
field $K(X)$.

For such a chart $\mathcal V=\operatorname{Spec}C$ and a
nonempty $I\subset\{0,\ldots,r\}$, write $S_I$ for its
residual stratum over
\[
 D_I=\{X_i=0\ (i\in I),\ X_j\ne0\ (j\notin I)\}
\]
and $Q_I$ for the finite set of generic points of $S_I$.
These are the strata and labels of
Proposition~\ref{cand:actualskeletalfibrestrata}. If
$s=x_{\eta,w}$, then $I=\{i:w_i>0\}$ and
$\eta\in Q_I$. An irreducible residual component in this
section is a component of this locally closed stratum.
