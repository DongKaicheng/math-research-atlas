Components under a free-radius extension

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 951

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[Components under a free-radius extension]
\label{v036:analytic:freecomponents}
Let $B$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra, let $r$ be a finite
$K$-free polyradius, and put $L=K_r$ and
$B_L=B\widehat\otimes_KL$. If $B$ is integral, then $B_L$ is integral.
For arbitrary nonzero $B$, extension of ideals gives a bijection
\[
 \operatorname{Min}(B)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Min}(B_L),
 \qquad \mathfrak p\longmapsto\mathfrak pB_L.
\]
Moreover
\[
 B_L/\mathfrak pB_L\simeq(B/\mathfrak p)_L,
 \qquad
 \dim_L\mathcal M(B_L/\mathfrak pB_L)
     =\dim_K\mathcal M(B/\mathfrak p).
\]
Consequently $B$ is pure of analytic dimension $d$ if and only if
$B_L$ is pure of analytic dimension $d$.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Suppose first that $B$ is integral. By
Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity}, there is
$x\in\mathcal M(B)$ with kernel zero. In the coefficient description
of Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients}, its Gauss lift is
\[
 \left|\sum_\nu b_\nu T^\nu\right|_{\sigma_r(x)}
          =\max_\nu |b_\nu(x)|r^\nu
\]
by Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusgausssection}. A nonzero coefficient
family has a nonzero coefficient, whose value at $x$ is positive.
Thus this multiplicative seminorm has kernel zero, and $B_L$ is a
domain. No freeness over $\mathcal H(x)$ is used.

The actual quotient identity follows from
Theorem~\ref{fnd:freeextensionfaithfullyflat}. Applied to
$B/\mathfrak p$, the preceding argument shows that
$\mathfrak pB_L$ is prime. Its contraction is $\mathfrak p$, by the
constant embedding in $(B/\mathfrak p)_L$. Every prime of $B_L$
contracts to a prime of $B$ containing a minimal prime, by
\cite[Tag 00E0(4)]{StacksAlg}. It therefore contains one of the primes
$\mathfrak pB_L$. These extended primes cannot contain one another,
since their contractions are distinct minimal primes. They are
exactly the minimal primes of $B_L$. Finally apply
Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticdimensionindependence} to each quotient.
This proves the dimension formula and both purity implications.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
Throughout this section $K$ is complete for its specified real-valued
non-Archimedean absolute value. Generalized affinoid algebras and the
trivial valuation are allowed. An affinoid algebra $B$ is pure of
analytic dimension $d$ if
$\dim_K\mathcal M(B/\mathfrak p)=d$ for every minimal prime
$\mathfrak p$ of $B$. We use analytic dimension before strictification;
it need not equal the Krull dimension of a generalized affinoid algebra.
