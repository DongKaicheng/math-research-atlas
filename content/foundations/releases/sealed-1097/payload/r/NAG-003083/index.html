Extension away from polyhedral junctions

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1911

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Extension away from polyhedral junctions]\label{f8it:sector-extension}
Let $P\subset\Omega$ be a finite compact polyhedral complex. Choose
finitely many maximal faces $\sigma$ and compact subsets
$K_\sigma\subset\relint(\sigma)$, and put $K=\bigcup_\sigma K_\sigma$.
Let $C_K(P)\subset C(P)$ consist of the forms supported in $K$, and give
its ambient preimage the coefficient subspace topology. There is a
linear extension
\[
 S:C_K(P)\longrightarrow E^{p,q}(\Omega),\qquad [Sa]=a,
\]
with compact support in a fixed neighbourhood of $K$ and, for every
ambient compact $L$ and every $m$, a bound
\begin{equation}\label{eq:extension}
 p_{L,m}(Sa)\leq C_{L,m}\max_\sigma r_{\sigma,m}(a).
\end{equation}
Hence the quotient topology on this support sector equals its intrinsic
topology. The same conclusion holds for final spaces generated by
compatible tuples of these sectors.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Write $L_\sigma$ for the affine span of $\sigma$, choose a fixed affine
projection $\pi_\sigma$ onto it and complementary normal coordinates.
The tangential coefficients of $a|_\sigma$ are smooth on
$\relint(\sigma)$ and vanish off $K_\sigma$. Extending them by zero to
$L_\sigma$ is smooth, since $K_\sigma$ stays away from the relative
boundary. Extend the tangential covectors by $\pi_\sigma^*$.

The distance from $K_\sigma$ to the other maximal faces and to the
complement of $\Omega$ is positive. Choose a normal smooth cutoff
$\zeta_\sigma$, equal to one near zero, with sufficiently small fixed
support, and form
\[
 S_\sigma a=\zeta_\sigma\,\pi_\sigma^*(a|_{L_\sigma}).
\]
Its support lies in a fixed compact tube over $K_\sigma$; shrink the
normal cutoff so that this tube misses every other maximal face and
stays in $\Omega$. Define $S=\sum_\sigma S_\sigma$.
On $P$ this is exactly $a$, including the lower-dimensional faces, where
$a$ vanishes. For a zero-dimensional face the same construction is the
constant coefficient times a bump; inadmissible bidegrees are zero.

The product and chain rules express each derivative of order at most
$m$ using derivatives of $a|_{L_\sigma}$ of order at most $m$.
All cutoff derivatives and all covector/projection matrices are fixed.
Their finitely many constants give \eqref{eq:extension}.
The section $S$ gives the reverse quotient bound on this sector; the
forward bound was already proved. For compatible tuples, apply the
sections componentwise. Their pullbacks have not changed, so all
ordinary overlap and support constraints remain satisfied. The two
continuous factorizations give the asserted final-topology comparison.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
Write $U$ for a good Hausdorff analytic space over a complete
non-Archimedean field, with arbitrary valuation. For an application with
interior tests, take $U$ to be an ordinary open subset of the true analytic
interior of the ambient space. Put $E=\A_c^{p,q}(U)$, with the frozen
ordinary moment sheaf and actual ordinary compact supports.
The two final topologies of \tagref{NAG-002472} are equal by
\tagref{NAG-002473}; denote their common value by $\tau_a$.
A cutoff generator has the form
\[
 Q_i:E^{p,q}(\Omega_i)\longrightarrow E,\qquad
 Q_i\eta=\operatorname{ext}_0(\chi_i t_{f_i}^*\eta),
 \qquad t_{f_i}=-\log|f_i|.
\]
Here $V_i$ is affinoid, $f_i$ is a unit tuple, $\Omega_i$ contains
$t_{f_i}(V_i)$, and $\supp\chi_i$ is compact in
$\Int_{\rm top}(V_i,U)$. The coefficient space has all compact $C^\infty$
seminorms. A seminorm on $E$ is $\tau_a$-continuous exactly when all its
composites with $Q_i$ are continuous. The images of the $Q_i$ span $E$.
Standing context 2 · exact TeX
We now define a matched intrinsic model, so that no external sheaf
identification is hidden. For a finite compact polyhedral set
$P\subset\Omega\subset\R^N$, let
\[
 C(P)=E^{p,q}(\Omega)/N_P,
\]
where $N_P$ consists of forms whose tangential restrictions to all faces
vanish. The underlying vector space consists of ambiently smooth
restrictions. Its \emph{intrinsic} topology is generated by the suprema
$r_{\sigma,m}$ of tangential coefficient derivatives through order $m$
on the compact faces $\sigma$, in fixed affine tangent bases.
Using all compact subfaces gives the same model after the corresponding
restrictions. This definition does not add arbitrary piecewise smooth
forms that lack an ambient representative.

For a finite ordinary presentation $s$, set $P_j=t_{f_j}(V_j)$ and
use these $C(P_j)$. We require the usual native nullity implication:
zero tangential restriction on $P_j$ pulls back to the zero ordinary
section. Let $J_s\subset\prod_j C(P_j)$ consist of the tuples whose
pullbacks agree on ordinary open overlaps and vanish off the fixed
compact support $K_s\subset\bigcup_j\Int_{\rm top}(V_j,U)$.
Give $J_s$ the intrinsic subspace topology. Its gluing map is $G_s$.
Let $\tau_t$ be the final locally convex topology for these $G_s$.
The ambient preimage of $J_s$ is exactly the frozen space $H_s$.
Thus both topologies in this comparison are on the same native test
space, with the same compact supports and overlap relations.
