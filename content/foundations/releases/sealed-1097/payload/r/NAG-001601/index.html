\begin{lemma}[\bi{A discrete complete field with its coefficient field}
{具有系数域的离散完备域}]
\label{fnd:discretecoefficientlaurent}
\bi{Let $E$ be a complete valued field with nontrivial cyclic
value group. Suppose that $K\subset E$ is trivially valued
and that reduction identifies $K$ with $\widetilde E$.
For a uniformizer $\tau\in E$, put $r=|\tau|\in(0,1)$.
Then substitution defines an isometric isomorphism
$K_r=K((T))\xrightarrow{\sim}E$, sending $T$ to $\tau$.}
{设完备赋值域 $E$ 的值群为非平凡循环群。假设
$K\subset E$ 平凡赋值，且约化将 $K$ 与 $\widetilde E$ 识别。
对一致化元 $\tau\in E$，令 $r=|\tau|\in(0,1)$。
则代换 $T\mapsto\tau$ 给出等距同构
$K_r=K((T))\xrightarrow{\sim}E$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\paragraph{NAG-001601. A discrete field with its coefficient field.}
Let \(E\) be complete with nontrivial cyclic value group, and let
\(K\subset E\) be trivially valued with reduction \(K\simeq\widetilde E\).
For a uniformizer \(\tau\), set \(r=|\tau|<1\). Then \(T\mapsto\tau\)
defines an isometric isomorphism \(K_r=K((T))\simeq E\).
Indeed, the terms of each nonzero Laurent polynomial in \(\tau\)
have distinct norms, so substitution extends isometrically. Weighted
nullity at negative exponents is exactly the requirement of finitely
many negative terms. For \(a_0\in E^\circ\) choose \(c_j\in K\) recursively
with the residue of \(a_j\), and set \(a_{j+1}=(a_j-c_j)/\tau\).
Cyclicity ensures \(|a_{j+1}|\le1\). The remainder after \(N\) steps
has norm at most \(r^N\). Multiplying any element of \(E\) by a power
of \(\tau\) brings it into \(E^\circ\), which gives surjectivity.
