\begin{lemma}[A necessary inertia condition for original-field existence]
\label{cand:f5exist:stableinertia}
Let $C/K$ be as above, of genus $g\geq2$. Let $L/K$ be finite Galois,
with group $G$, and give $L$ its unique extending absolute value.
Put $O'=L^\circ$, let $k'$ be its residue field, and set
\[
 I=\ker\bigl(G\longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}(k'/k)\bigr).
\]
Suppose a stable model $\mathcal Z/O'$ of $C_L$ is supplied with its
generic-fibre identification. Equip it with the unique semilinear
$G$-action of \dep{cand:stablegaloisaction}. If $C$ admits an F5
model over $O$, then every element of $I$ induces the identity
morphism on the entire $k'$-scheme $\mathcal Z_{k'}$.
No tameness or residue-separability hypothesis is needed for this
necessary condition.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Suppose $f:\mathcal X\to\operatorname{Spec}O$ is an F5 model.
A nontrivial rank-one valuation ring has only its generic and closed
points. Hence all geometric fibres of $f$ are reduced, connected
curves of dimension one: the generic fibre is smooth and geometrically
connected, and the special fibre has the stated properties.
The criterion in \hypertarget{foundations.nag002913.stacks.0E6T.prestable}{}\stacks{0E6T}, with \hypertarget{foundations.nag002913.stacks.0E6L.universal-h0-r1}{}\stacks{0E6L}, makes $f$ a
prestable family. The same lemma gives a finite locally free
$R^1f_*\mathcal O_{\mathcal X}$ with arbitrary base change. Its rank
is constant on the connected spectrum of $O$ and equals $g$ at the
generic point. Thus this is a prestable family of genus $g$ in the
sense of \hypertarget{foundations.nag002913.stacks.0E6V.fixed-genus}{}\stacks{0E6V}.

Apply the arbitrary-base stabilization theorem \hypertarget{foundations.nag002913.stacks.0E8A.stabilization-and-generic}{}\stacks{0E8A}.
It gives a stable family $\mathcal Y/O$ and a morphism
$\mathcal X\to\mathcal Y$ whose fibres contract rational tails
and bridges. The smooth geometrically connected generic curve of
genus at least two has none, so this morphism identifies
$\mathcal Y_K$ with $C$. This use of the stack of stable families
requires no new projectivity theorem for $\mathcal Y$.

% BEGIN detached scheme-category bridge
For the scheme convention, the absence of rational tails and bridges
on the stable closed fibre gives an ample relative dualizing sheaf
on a neighbourhood of the closed point by \hypertarget{foundations.nag002913.stacks.0E74.stable-dualizing}{}\stacks{0E74}.
As the base is local, that neighbourhood is the whole base.
The ample sheaf makes \(\mathcal Y\) a scheme by \hypertarget{foundations.nag002913.stacks.0D32.scheme-over-local-base}{}\stacks{0D32}.
% END detached scheme-category bridge

Both $\mathcal Y_{O'}$ and $\mathcal Z$ \hypertarget{foundations.nag002913.stacks.0E78.stable-genus-after-base-change}{}are stable families of genus
$g$. Their specified generic identifications give a unique isomorphism
\[
 \alpha:\mathcal Y_{O'}\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\mathcal Z
\]
by \dep{cand:stableisomvaluation}, which applies to arbitrary valuation
rings. The canonical semilinear action on the base change
$\mathcal Y_{O'}$ extends the canonical action on $C_L$.
Transporting it by $\alpha$ must therefore give the specified action
on $\mathcal Z$, by the uniqueness in
\dep{cand:stablegaloisaction}.

On the full special fibre
$\mathcal Y_{k'}=\mathcal Y_k\times_k\operatorname{Spec}k'$, the
canonical action of $\sigma\in G$ is
$\operatorname{id}_{\mathcal Y_k}\times
 \operatorname{Spec}(\overline{\sigma}^{-1})$.
For $\sigma\in I$, this is the identity morphism, including the
structure sheaf. The equivariant isomorphism $\alpha_{k'}$ transfers
that identity to $\mathcal Z_{k'}$, as required.
\end{proof}
