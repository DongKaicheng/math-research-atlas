The completed Jacobian calculation

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 317

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{The completed Jacobian calculation}{完备局部环上的 Jacobian 计算}]
\label{fnd:rationaljacobiancompletion}
\bi{Let $K$ be nontrivially valued, let $X$ be good, and let $D$ be an analytic domain of $\mathbb A_X^{n,\an}$. Suppose a closed analytic subspace $Z\subset D$ is defined near a $K$-rational point $z$ by $r$ functions $g_1,\ldots,g_r$ whose relative Jacobian has rank $r$ at $z$. Write $x$ for its image and $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$, $S=\mathcal O_{Z,z}$. Then, as complete local $\widehat R$-algebras,}
{设 $K$ 赋值非平凡，$X$ good，且 $D$ 是 $\mathbb A_X^{n,\an}$ 的解析域。假设闭解析子空间 $Z\subset D$ 在一个 $K$-有理点 $z$ 附近由 $r$ 个函数 $g_1,\ldots,g_r$ 定义，其相对 Jacobian 在 $z$ 处的秩为 $r$。记 $x$ 为其像，并令 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$S=\mathcal O_{Z,z}$。则存在完备局部 $\widehat R$-代数同构}
\[
 \widehat S\simeq\widehat R[[V_1,\ldots,V_{n-r}]].
\]
\bi{The local homomorphism $R\to S$ is flat. The fibre local ring at $z$ is regular of dimension $n-r$.}
{局部同态 $R\to S$ 平坦。$z$ 处的纤维局部环正则，维数为 $n-r$。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The point $x$ is $K$-rational, since its completed residue field embeds into $\mathcal H(z)=K$ over $K$. By Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalpointdomaininterior}, the domain $D$ is an ordinary neighborhood of $z$ in the relative affine space. Choose local affinoid coordinates $X_0=\mathcal M(K\{s^{-1}U\}/J)$ at $x$ and translate $x,z$ to zero. Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalanalyticformalpresentation}, applied also to the relative polydisc, gives}
{由于 $\mathcal H(x)$ 在 $K$ 上嵌入 $\mathcal H(z)=K$，点 $x$ 也为 $K$-有理点。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalpointdomaininterior}，域 $D$ 是相对仿射空间中 $z$ 的普通邻域。在 $x$ 附近选择 affinoid 坐标 $X_0=\mathcal M(K\{s^{-1}U\}/J)$，并将 $x,z$ 平移至零。对底空间及相对多圆盘应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalanalyticformalpresentation}，得到}
\[
 \widehat R=K[[U]]/\widehat J,\qquad
 \widehat S=K[[U,T]]/(\widehat J,\widehat g_1,\ldots,\widehat g_r).
\]
\bi{Here each $g_i$ is a germ modulo $J$; choose any convergent representative in $(U,T)$. The relative derivatives at the origin are unaffected by this choice, because the added ideal consists of functions of $U$ times other germs, and all elements of $J$ vanish at $U=0$. After reordering the $T_i$, the first $r$ columns give an invertible minor. The substitution in the formal ring which fixes all $U_i$, sends $T_i$ to $\widehat g_i$ for $i\le r$, and fixes $T_i$ for $i>r$ has invertible linear part. It is a formal automorphism. Explicitly, invert its linear matrix; then correct an inverse modulo degree $q$ by solving the degree-$q$ discrepancy with this same invertible matrix. The corrections have increasing degree, so their sum converges in the formal maximal-ideal topology. The same recursion gives uniqueness, which makes the resulting left and right inverses equal. This argument works in every characteristic.}
{这里每个 $g_i$ 是模 $J$ 的一个芽；在 $(U,T)$ 中选任意收敛代表元。相对导数在原点的值不依赖此选择：增加的项由只依赖 $U$ 的 $J$ 中元素乘以其他芽组成，而 $J$ 的所有元素在 $U=0$ 消失。排列 $T_i$ 后，可设前 $r$ 列给出可逆子式。在形式环中固定所有 $U_i$、将 $i\le r$ 时的 $T_i$ 送到 $\widehat g_i$、并固定 $i>r$ 时的 $T_i$；此代入的线性部分可逆，所以它是形式自同构。具体地，先求线性矩阵的逆，再用同一可逆矩阵解出次数 $q$ 的误差，逐次修正模次数 $q$ 的逆。修正项次数严格增加，故其和在形式极大理想拓扑中收敛。同一递推给出唯一性，从而所得左逆与右逆一致。这一论证适用于任意特征。}

\bi{This automorphism fixes $K[[U]]$ and hence its extended ideal $\widehat J$. It carries the ideal generated by $T_1,\ldots,T_r$ to that generated by $\widehat g_1,\ldots,\widehat g_r$. Passing to the quotient gives the asserted $\widehat R$-algebra isomorphism.}
{此自同构固定 $K[[U]]$，因此也固定其延拓理想 $\widehat J$；它将 $T_1,\ldots,T_r$ 生成的理想送到 $\widehat g_1,\ldots,\widehat g_r$ 生成的理想。取商即得所述 $\widehat R$-代数同构。}

\bi{Both $R$ and $S$ are Noetherian by Theorem~\ref{fnd:ordinaryanalyticstalknoetherian}; their completions are Noetherian by \cite[Tag 0316]{StacksAlg}. The ring $\widehat R[[V]]$ is the $(V)$-adic completion of the Noetherian polynomial ring $\widehat R[V]$. It is flat over $\widehat R$ by \cite[Tag 00MB]{StacksAlg} and the freeness of a polynomial ring over its coefficient ring. Also $\widehat R$ is flat over $R$, and $\widehat S$ is faithfully flat over $S$, by \cite[Tag 00MC]{StacksAlg}. For each finitely generated ideal $I\subset R$, the kernel of $I\otimes_R S\to S$ becomes zero after tensoring with $\widehat S$, because $\widehat S$ is $R$-flat. Faithfulness makes the original kernel zero; \cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg} proves the required flatness.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:ordinaryanalyticstalknoetherian}，$R,S$ 均 Noetherian；由 \cite[Tag 0316]{StacksAlg}，其完备化亦 Noetherian。环 $\widehat R[[V]]$ 是 Noetherian 多项式环 $\widehat R[V]$ 的 $(V)$-进完备化。由 \cite[Tag 00MB]{StacksAlg} 及多项式环在系数环上的自由性，它在 $\widehat R$ 上平坦。又由 \cite[Tag 00MC]{StacksAlg}，$\widehat R$ 在 $R$ 上平坦，而 $\widehat S$ 在 $S$ 上忠实平坦。对每个有限生成理想 $I\subset R$，$I\otimes_R S\to S$ 的核与 $\widehat S$ 张量后为零，因为 $\widehat S$ 在 $R$ 上平坦。忠实性使原核为零；\cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg} 即给出所需平坦性。}

\bi{Finally Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalfibrestalkquotient} and exact completion identify the completed fibre local ring with $K[[V_1,\ldots,V_{n-r}]]$. This is a regular local ring of dimension $n-r$, by \cite[Tag 032C]{StacksAlg}. Regularity passes to the original local ring by \cite[Tag 07NY]{StacksAlg}, and its embedding dimension remains $n-r$ because completion preserves the quotient of the maximal ideal by its square. For a regular local ring dimension equals embedding dimension, proving the last assertion.}
{最后，Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalfibrestalkquotient} 及完备化正合性将纤维局部环的完备化识别为 $K[[V_1,\ldots,V_{n-r}]]$。由 \cite[Tag 032C]{StacksAlg}，它是维数 $n-r$ 的正则局部环。由 \cite[Tag 07NY]{StacksAlg}，原局部环亦正则；其嵌入维数仍为 $n-r$，因为完备化保持极大理想对其平方的商。正则局部环的维数等于嵌入维数，得到最后断言。}
\end{proof}
