Closed submodules and controlled matrix images

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 109

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Closed submodules and controlled matrix images}{闭子模与有界矩阵提升}]\label{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}
\bi{Let $A$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra. For every finite $m\geq0$, every algebraic submodule $N\subset A^m$ is finitely generated and closed. For every chosen finite generating list $n_1,\ldots,n_t$ of $N$, there is a constant $C>0$ such that}
{设 $A$ 为 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数。对每个有限 $m\geq0$，$A^m$ 的每个代数子模 $N$ 均有限生成且为闭子模。对 $N$ 的任一给定有限生成元组 $n_1,\ldots,n_t$，存在常数 $C>0$，使得}
\[
 \forall n\in N\quad \exists a_1,\ldots,a_t\in A:
 \quad n=\sum_i a_in_i,
 \qquad \max_i\|a_i\|\leq C\|n\|.
\]
\bi{In particular, every finite matrix over $A$ has closed image and a uniform bound for actual lifts from the induced image norm. The constants may depend on the matrix and the chosen norms, but not on the element lifted. Moreover, $A$ is Noetherian.}
{特别地，$A$ 上每个有限矩阵都具有闭像，并且对像上的诱导范数存在统一的实际提升界。常数可以依赖矩阵与所选范数，但不依赖被提升的元素。此外，$A$ 为 Noetherian。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{We may suppose $A\ne0$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification} supplies a finite free tuple $r$ such that $R=A_r$, with its coefficient norm, is a strict norm-admissible $K_r$-affinoid algebra. This step uses the defining presentation of $A$, not Noetherianity of $A$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules} therefore applies to $R$ with the indicated norm.}
{可设 $A\ne0$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification}，存在有限自由元组 $r$，使配备系数范数的 $R=A_r$ 成为 strict 且范数可容许的 $K_r$-affinoid 代数。此步骤使用 $A$ 的定义表示，不使用 $A$ 的 Noetherian 性。因此可对带上述范数的 $R$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}。}

\bi{Let $N_R$ be the algebraic $R$-span of $\iota_A^m(N)$ in $R^m$. It is finite and closed by the strict finite-module theorem. Choose finitely many generators of $N_R$ and expand each as a finite $R$-linear combination of elements of $N$. The union of these finite lists gives $n_1,\ldots,n_t\in N$ that generate $N_R$ over $R$. If $n\in N$, write $\iota_A^m(n)=\sum_i b_i\iota_A^m(n_i)$ and apply $p_0$ coordinatewise. Lemma~\ref{fnd:freecoefficientretraction} gives}
{令 $N_R$ 为 $\iota_A^m(N)$ 在 $R^m$ 中的代数 $R$-线性包。由 strict 有限模定理，$N_R$ 有限且闭。选择其有限个生成元，并将每个生成元写成 $N$ 中元素的有限 $R$-线性组合。合并这些有限列表，得到 $n_1,\ldots,n_t\in N$，它们在 $R$ 上生成 $N_R$。若 $n\in N$，写成 $\iota_A^m(n)=\sum_i b_i\iota_A^m(n_i)$，再逐坐标取 $p_0$。Lemma~\ref{fnd:freecoefficientretraction} 给出}
\[
 n=\sum_i p_0^A(b_i)n_i.
\]
\bi{Thus $N$ is finite. The same argument applied to any constant vector lying in $N_R$ proves}
{所以 $N$ 有限生成。对落在 $N_R$ 中的任一常系数向量使用同一论证，得到}
\[
 N=(\iota_A^m)^{-1}(N_R).
\]
\bi{Since $N_R$ is closed and $\iota_A^m$ is continuous, $N$ is closed. Taking $m=1$ proves Noetherianity.}
{由于 $N_R$ 闭且 $\iota_A^m$ 连续，$N$ 也闭。取 $m=1$ 即得 Noetherian 性。}

\bi{Now let $D:A^t\to A^m$ be any chosen generating matrix for $N$. Its scalar extension $D_r:R^t\to R^m$ has image $N_R$. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}, there is $C>0$ such that each $\iota_A^m(n)$ has a lift $b\in R^t$ with $\|b\|\leq C\|n\|$. Put $a=p_0^t(b)$. The coefficient lemma gives $Da=n$ and $\|a\|\leq\|b\|$. This is the asserted actual lifting bound. Finite matrices are bounded by the maximum of the norms of their entries; the reverse bound just proved is precisely norm-strictness onto the induced image. No algebraic flatness or completed-tensor exactness was used in the descent.}
{再令 $D:A^t\to A^m$ 为 $N$ 的任一给定生成矩阵。其标量扩张 $D_r:R^t\to R^m$ 的像为 $N_R$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}，存在 $C>0$，使每个 $\iota_A^m(n)$ 都有提升 $b\in R^t$，满足 $\|b\|\leq C\|n\|$。令 $a=p_0^t(b)$。系数引理给出 $Da=n$ 及 $\|a\|\leq\|b\|$，即所需实际提升界。有限矩阵由其元素范数的最大值控制，故有界；刚证明的反向界正是到诱导像范数的 norm-strictness。此下降没有使用代数平坦性或完备张量积的正合性。}
\end{proof}
