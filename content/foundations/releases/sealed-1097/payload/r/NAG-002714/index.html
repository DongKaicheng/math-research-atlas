NAG-002714 · Historical whole-proof gap

Local compatible collars and their intrinsic common Gauss set

Records without a chapter binding

Remaining historical proof gap

The sealed ledger records a gap in the proof of the historical whole statement.

Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Local compatible collars and their intrinsic common Gauss set]
\label{gci:localcollars}
Let \(X=\mathcal M(B)\) be nonempty and pure of analytic
dimension \(d\geq1\), with actual units
\(f=(f_1,\ldots,f_n)\) and moment map \(t=t_f\).
Assume
\[
             w\in t(X),\qquad F=t^{-1}(w)
                          \subset\operatorname{Int}(X/K).
 \tag{8}\label{gci:wholeinner}
\]
This hypothesis concerns the entire original moment fibre.

There are a finite pure \(d\)-dimensional rational fan
\(\Sigma\) and a real neighbourhood \(O\) of \(w\) such that
\[
                         t(X)\cap O=(w+|\Sigma|)\cap O.
 \tag{9}\label{gci:star}
\]
Choose a closed rectangular box \(W\subset O\) with
\(w\in\operatorname{int}(W)\), and set
\[
                              Z=t^{-1}(W)\subset X.
 \tag{10}\label{gci:localizedsource}
\]
Call an integral \(Q:\mathbb Z^n\to\mathbb Z^d\)
admissible here when it is injective on the span of every
top cone of \(\Sigma\). Such maps exist.
For any finite family of admissible maps \(Q_i\), including
any two distinct ones, there are boxes \(P_i\) about \(Q_iw\),
with \(Q_iw\in\operatorname{int}(P_i)\), for which
\[
 C_i=(Q_it|_Z)^{-1}(P_i),\qquad
 \psi_i=f^{Q_i}:C_i\longrightarrow\mathbb T_{P_i}
                  \quad\hbox{is norm-admissible finite}.
 \tag{11}\label{gci:constructedcollars}
\]
The boxes may be chosen inside any prescribed real
neighbourhoods of their centres.

Every projection has the same \emph{entire} central analytic
Gauss set, namely
\[
 \psi_i^{-1}(\eta_{Q_iw})
       = S:=\{x\in F:r_f(x)=d\},\qquad 1\leq |S|<\infty .
 \tag{12}\label{gci:intrinsicset}
\]
All points of \(S\) are absolutely interior in \(Z\).
The full pointed fans at these points admit a common
subdivision on whose top-cone spans every \(Q_i\) is injective.
Thus any pair of these collars satisfies the exact hypotheses
of the independently reviewed \Dep{mso:setup}.
For any finite \(\mathcal O(Z)\)-module the reviewed
\Dep{mso:unionoverlap} and \Dep{mso:sectorcomparison} apply.

The saturation in (11) is in \(Z\).
It is also saturation in the original \(X\), after shrinking
the \(P_i\) if needed, \emph{if and only if}
\[
        Q_iw\notin
          \overline{\,Q_it(X\setminus Z)\,}
                              \quad\hbox{for every }i.
 \tag{13}\label{gci:originalcompletion}
\]
Without (13), no completeness assertion over \(X\) is made
for the projected collars.
Nevertheless the original moment fibre \(F\), and every
original moment fibre with value in \(W\), is retained in
its entirety by (10).
\end{theorem}
