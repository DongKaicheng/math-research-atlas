Finite tensor comparison with an affinoid target

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 74

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Finite tensor comparison with an affinoid target}{Affinoid target 下的 finite tensor comparison}]\label{fnd:strictfinitetensor}
\bi{Let $A\to B$ be a bounded homomorphism of strict $K$-affinoid algebras and $M$ a finite $A$-module with its canonical norm. The projective tensor seminorm on $M\otimes_A B$ is equivalent to its complete finite-$B$-module norm. In particular, the canonical map}
{设 $A\to B$ 为 strict $K$-affinoid algebras 的 bounded homomorphism，$M$ 为带有 canonical norm 的 finite $A$-module。$M\otimes_A B$ 上的 projective tensor seminorm 与其 complete finite-$B$-module norm 等价。特别地，canonical map}
\[
 M\otimes_A B\longrightarrow M\widehat\otimes_A B
\]
\bi{is a bounded isomorphism. This assertion does not imply flatness of $A\to B$.}
{为 bounded isomorphism。此断言不推出 $A\to B$ 的 flatness。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose a finite presentation $A^s\to A^m\to M\to0$. The generators-and-relations definition of tensor product gives $M\otimes_A B=\operatorname{coker}(B^s\to B^m)$: the images of the standard generators generate the tensor product, and its relations are exactly the $B$-linear combinations of the displayed relations of $M$. The relation image is closed by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}, so this quotient norm is complete.}
{选择 finite presentation $A^s\to A^m\to M\to0$。由 tensor product 的 generators-and-relations 定义，$M\otimes_A B=\operatorname{coker}(B^s\to B^m)$：standard generators 的 images 生成 tensor product，而 relations 正是所列 $M$ 的 relations 的 $B$-linear combinations。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}，relation image 为 closed，所以该 quotient norm complete。}

\bi{Use the quotient norm from $A^m$ on $M$, and write $e_i$ for its standard generator images. Since $\|e_i\|\leq1$, the expression $\sum e_i\otimes b_i$ bounds the projective norm by the quotient norm. Conversely, write $z=\sum_j m_j\otimes b_j$ and lift each $m_j$ to $A^m$ with coefficient norms at most $2\|m_j\|$. If $\|\phi(a)\|\leq C_\phi\|a\|$, this expresses $z=\sum_i e_i\otimes c_i$ with}
{在 $M$ 上使用来自 $A^m$ 的 quotient norm，记 $e_i$ 为 standard generators 的 images。因为 $\|e_i\|\leq1$，表示 $\sum e_i\otimes b_i$ 说明 projective norm 不超过 quotient norm。反之，写出 $z=\sum_j m_j\otimes b_j$，将每个 $m_j$ 提升到 $A^m$，使 coefficient norms 不超过 $2\|m_j\|$。若 $\|\phi(a)\|\leq C_\phi\|a\|$，则得到表示 $z=\sum_i e_i\otimes c_i$，满足}
\[
 \max_i\|c_i\|\leq2C_\phi\max_j\bigl(\|m_j\|\|b_j\|\bigr).
\]
\bi{Taking the infimum over tensor representations gives the reverse norm comparison. Hence the projective seminorm is definite and complete, and separated completion makes no further change.}
{对所有 tensor representations 取 infimum，得到反方向的 norm comparison。因此 projective seminorm 为 definite 且 complete，作 separated completion 不再改变该 module。}
\end{proof}
