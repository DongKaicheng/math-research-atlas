\begin{lemma}[Primitive cuts and determinant recursion]
\label{f9mi:primitivecut}
Assume $r\geq1$. There is an $R$-basis $e_0,\ldots,e_r$ of $E$ with
$u(e_0)=1$ and $E'=\ker u=Re_1\oplus\cdots\oplus Re_r$.
The horizontal model cut is $\mathbf P(E')\simeq\mathbf P_R^{r-1}$.
For $r\geq1$, a section $s=\ell^m$ with metric shift $c$ and clearing
exponent $e$ as above has normalized model divisor
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ex68:f9mi-projective:divisor}
 \frac1e\cyc\bigl(\divi_{\cO(em)}(s^{\otimes e})\bigr)
        =m[\mathbf P(E')]+(ma+c)[V].
\end{equation}
The generic divisor is $m[\mathbf P(E'_K)]$. A negative $ma+c$ is
allowed as a rational vertical coefficient. All coefficients are
computed by ordinary algebraic DVR orders, with actual quotient lengths.

If $\ell=\ell_i$ is a row of an invertible $(r+1)$-row matrix $A$,
the other rows restrict to an invertible $r$-row matrix $A_i$ on $E'_K$,
and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ex68:f9mi-projective:detrec}
                         v(\det A)=a_i+v(\det A_i).
\end{equation}
The restricted standard metric is the standard metric for the lattice
$E'$, with the same shift $c_j$ and exponent $m_j$ for every remaining
line. No primitivity of these restricted rows is assumed.
\end{lemma}
\F9MIProofInputs{Elementary integral row reduction; ordinary DVR length convention
of NAG-002552. The proof does not invoke the pending model log/divisor
packet or a general intersection product.}
\begin{proof}
Permute the coordinates to make a unit coefficient first, divide that
coordinate vector by the unit, and subtract multiples of it from the
other coordinate vectors. These are invertible $R$-operations and give
the displayed basis. The content is the fractional ideal generated by
the row coefficients, so this ideal and $a$ are invariant under an
invertible $R$-basis change. The primitive equation is now $z_0=0$.
Its model quotient is a polynomial projective hyperplane over $R$,
which proves the claim about $\mathbf P(E')$.

In a model frame, the coefficient of $s^{\otimes e}$ has a factor
$\pi^{e(ma+c)}u^{em}$. At the generic point of the horizontal
hyperplane its order is $em$, and at the generic point of $V$ its
order is $e(ma+c)$. These are the only components of the divisor.
At a DVR with parameter $t$, the successive powers in $A/(t^n)$
give length $n$; for a rational coefficient one subtracts the lengths
of numerator and denominator. Thus no nilpotent layer in $u^{em}=0$
is removed. Divide the coefficients by $e$ to obtain
\eqref{eq:ex68:f9mi-projective:divisor}.

Move row $i$ to the first position and use the constructed basis.
The matrix becomes
\[
                    \begin{pmatrix}\pi^{a_i}&0\\ *&A_i\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Row permutation and the basis matrix have determinant a unit up to
sign, proving \eqref{eq:ex68:f9mi-projective:detrec}; its nonzero determinant also proves
that $A_i$ is invertible over $K$. Finally, a matrix in
$\mathrm{GL}_{r+1}(R)$ and its inverse both have operator norm at most
one for the maximum norm over every valued extension of $K$.
Consequently the maximum norm is unchanged. Restriction to the direct
summand $E'$ gives exactly the standard metric in its basis.
\end{proof}
