Finite compositions of elementary localizations

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 183

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Finite compositions of elementary localizations}{Elementary 局部化的有限复合}]\label{fnd:finiteelementaryflatness}
\bi{Start with a nonzero strict norm-admissible $C_0=A$. For $1\leq j\leq n$, choose $f_j\in C_{j-1}$ and $r_j\in|K^\times|$, and let $C_j$ be either}
{从非零 strict norm-admissible 的 $C_0=A$ 出发。对 $1\leq j\leq n$，选择 $f_j\in C_{j-1}$ 和 $r_j\in|K^\times|$，令 $C_j$ 为下列两种商之一：}
\[
 C_{j-1}\{r_j^{-1}T_j\}/(T_j-f_j)
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 C_{j-1}\{r_jU_j\}/(1-f_jU_j),
\]
\bi{with the quotient norm; once a zero algebra occurs, every subsequent algebra is zero. Every map in the sequence and every composite is algebraically flat. For finite modules over any nonzero intermediate algebra, extension to a later algebra preserves short exact sequences, and the ordinary and separated completed tensor sequences agree by bounded isomorphisms and are norm-strict for canonical norms. In particular, the maps $A(S)\to A(T)$ for $S\subseteq T$ in Proposition~\ref{fnd:elementaryintersectiondiagram} are flat, as are the maps from $A$ to $A_+,A_-,A_0$ in Theorem~\ref{fnd:laurentlocalizationmodules}.}
{各商均使用商范数；一旦出现零代数，其后所有代数均为零。序列中的每个映射及其每个复合都是代数平坦的。对任一非零中间代数上的有限模，向后续代数作延伸保持短正合列，普通与 separated completed tensor 序列通过有界同构相同，并且对 canonical 范数为 norm-strict。特别地，Proposition~\ref{fnd:elementaryintersectiondiagram} 中 $S\subseteq T$ 对应的映射 $A(S)\to A(T)$ 均平坦，Theorem~\ref{fnd:laurentlocalizationmodules} 中从 $A$ 到 $A_+,A_-,A_0$ 的映射也均平坦。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{At each nonzero stage Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictweightedquotient} shows that the next algebra is a strict quotient or zero, and Theorem~\ref{fnd:elementarystrictflatness} proves flatness of the next map. The zero module is flat because its ordinary tensor functor is zero. Flatness of every finite composite now follows directly from \cite[Tag 00HC, Lemma 10.39.4]{StacksAlg}.}
{在每个非零阶段，Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictweightedquotient} 说明下一个代数是 strict 商或零商，Theorem~\ref{fnd:elementarystrictflatness} 则证明下一映射平坦。零模的普通张量积函子为零函子，故零模平坦。由 \cite[Tag 00HC, Lemma 10.39.4]{StacksAlg}，每个有限复合均平坦。}

\bi{Every resulting map is bounded, being a composite of contractive coefficient inclusions followed by quotient maps. For finite modules, algebraic flatness gives exactness, Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitetensor} identifies ordinary and completed tensors by bounded isomorphisms, and Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules} over the final nonzero algebra gives norm-strictness. The zero target is immediate. This proves the assertion for any pair of intermediate stages.}
{所得每个映射都有界，因为它由 contractive 的系数包含及其后的商映射复合而成。对有限模，代数平坦性给出正合性，Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitetensor} 通过有界同构识别普通与完备张量积，再对最后的非零代数应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}，得到 norm-strictness。零目标的情形直接成立。这就证明了任意两个中间阶段之间的结论。}

\bi{For $A(S)\to A(T)$, impose the constraints in $T\setminus S$ one at a time and use Proposition~\ref{fnd:elementaryintersectiondiagram} to identify the result with the simultaneous presentation. Finally, after forming $A_+$, impose the negative relation $1-\bar fU$ with $U$ of radius $r^{-1}$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:iteratedstrictquotients} and the ideal equality in the proof of Theorem~\ref{fnd:laurentlocalizationmodules} identify this two-stage quotient isometrically with}
{对 $A(S)\to A(T)$，逐个施加 $T\setminus S$ 中的约束，再用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:elementaryintersectiondiagram} 将所得代数与同时表示识别。最后，在形成 $A_+$ 后，以变量半径 $r^{-1}$ 施加负关系 $1-\bar fU$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:iteratedstrictquotients} 及 Theorem~\ref{fnd:laurentlocalizationmodules} 证明中的理想等式，这个两阶段商等距识别为}
\[
 A\{r^{-1}T,rU\}/(T-f,1-fU)
   =A\{r^{-1}T,rU\}/(T-f,TU-1)=A_0 .
\]
\bi{This proves flatness of the stated Laurent intersection map.}
{这证明所述 Laurent 交叠映射的平坦性。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Here an algebraic $R$-module $N$ is flat if the ordinary tensor functor $-\otimes_R N$ preserves exact sequences; a ring map $R\to S$ is flat when $S$ is flat as an $R$-module. These are the definitions in \cite[Tag 00HB, Definition 10.39.1(1),(3)]{StacksAlg}. No completion or norm is part of this definition.}
{这里称代数 $R$-模 $N$ 为平坦的，是指普通张量积函子 $-\otimes_R N$ 保持正合列；称环映射 $R\to S$ 平坦，是指 $S$ 作为 $R$-模平坦。这采用 \cite[Tag 00HB, Definition 10.39.1(1),(3)]{StacksAlg} 的定义。该定义不涉及完备化或范数。}
