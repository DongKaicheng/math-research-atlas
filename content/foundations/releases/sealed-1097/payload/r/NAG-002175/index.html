Independence of the semistable chart

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Independence of the semistable chart}
{与半稳定坐标片无关}]
\label{cand:semistablechartindependence}
\bi{Suppose the same affinoid algebra $B$ has two bounded
presentations of type (J), possibly over different standard
semistable algebras. Their homotopies on $\mathcal M(B)$
are equal at every time in $[0,1]$.}
{假设同一个 affinoid 代数 $B$ 具有两个 (J) 型有界
表示，二者可位于不同的标准半稳定代数之上。则它们
在 $\mathcal M(B)$ 上的同伦在 $[0,1]$ 的每个时刻
都相同。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use unprimed and primed notation for the two
presentations. Fix $x$, put $E=\mathcal H(x)$, and fix
$0<\delta<1$. In $E\{\delta^{-1}U\}$ define}
{以不带撇及带撇记号表示两个表示。固定 $x$，置
$E=\mathcal H(x)$，再固定 $0<\delta<1$。
在 $E\{\delta^{-1}U\}$ 中定义}
\[
 \Phi_i=\frac{\sigma_{\delta,x}(X'_i)}{X'_i(x)}-1
                     \quad(1\leq i\leq r'),\qquad
 \Psi_j=\sigma_{\delta,x}(T'_j)-T'_j(x).
\]
\bi{The functions $X'_i$ are global analytic units because
their product is the nonzero scalar $a'$. The $T'_j$ and
the primed implicit coordinates have values at most one
everywhere. Lemma~\ref{cand:intrinsicdisplacementbounds}
therefore gives $\|\Phi_i\|,\|\Psi_j\|\leq\delta$.
Substitution $U'=(S',Z')\mapsto(\Phi,\Psi)$ defines a
contractive $E$-homomorphism}
{由于带撇单项式坐标之积是非零标量 $a'$，各
$X'_i$ 都是全局解析单位元。而 $T'_j$ 及带撇隐式
坐标处处取值至多为一。因此由
Lemma~\ref{cand:intrinsicdisplacementbounds}，有
$\|\Phi_i\|,\|\Psi_j\|\leq\delta$。代换
$U'=(S',Z')\mapsto(\Phi,\Psi)$ 定义压缩
$E$-同态}
\[
 \theta:E\{\delta^{-1}U'\}\longrightarrow
                         E\{\delta^{-1}U\}.
\]
\bi{The two maps $\sigma_{\delta,x}$ and
$\theta\sigma'_{\delta,x}$ have identical images on the
primed base coordinates: this is the definition for
$X'_i$ with $i\geq1$ and for $T'_j$, and the product
relation gives it for $X'_0$. Their images of the primed
implicit tuple solve the same displaced equations and
both lie within $\delta$ of $Y'(x)$. At that center the
new Jacobian differs by at most $\delta$ from the original
invertible Jacobian of inverse norm at most one.
The geometric inverse formula and
Lemma~\ref{cand:banachcenterhensel} therefore identify the
two roots. Their inverse determinants coincide as well.}
{两个映射 $\sigma_{\delta,x}$ 与
$\theta\sigma'_{\delta,x}$ 在带撇基底坐标上相同：
对 $i\geq1$ 的 $X'_i$ 及 $T'_j$，这是定义；对
$X'_0$，则由乘积关系得到。它们给出的带撇隐式
元组解同一组位移后方程，并且都距 $Y'(x)$ 至多
$\delta$。在此中心，新 Jacobian 与原来的、逆范数
至多为一的可逆 Jacobian 相差至多 $\delta$。
因此几何求逆公式及
Lemma~\ref{cand:banachcenterhensel} 识别两根，
它们的逆行列式也相同。}

\bi{The primed presentation topologically generates $B$.
Both maps are bounded for its topology, so equality on
its coordinates implies equality on every $b\in B$.
Contractivity of $\theta$ now gives}
{带撇表示在拓扑上生成 $B$。两个映射对该拓扑都有
界，所以它们在坐标上的相等意味着在每个 $b\in B$
上相等。现在 $\theta$ 的压缩性给出}
\[
 \|\sigma_{\delta,x}(b)\|_\delta
       \leq\|\sigma'_{\delta,x}(b)\|_\delta.
\]
\bi{Reversing the two presentations gives the opposite
inequality. These Gauss norms are precisely evaluation
at the two time-$\delta$ points, so the points agree.
They agree at zero by definition and at one by the
pointwise endpoint formula.}
{交换两个表示得到相反不等式。这两个 Gauss 范数
恰是在两个时刻 $\delta$ 的点处取值，故两点相同。
时刻零由定义相同，时刻一由逐点终点公式相同。}
\end{proof}
