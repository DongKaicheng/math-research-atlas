Residue fields of rank-zero quasi-smooth morphisms

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 472

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Residue fields of rank-zero quasi-smooth morphisms}{秩零准光滑态射的剩余域}]
\label{fnd:rankzeroquasismoothseparableresidue}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good analytic spaces over any complete field. If $f$ is quasi-smooth at $y$ and $\operatorname{rk}_y\Omega_{Y/X}=0$, then $\mathcal H(y)/\mathcal H(f(y))$ is finite separable.}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 为任意完备域上 good 解析空间之间的态射。若 $f$ 在 $y$ 处准光滑，且 $\operatorname{rk}_y\Omega_{Y/X}=0$，则 $\mathcal H(y)/\mathcal H(f(y))$ 为有限可分扩张。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $x=f(y)$ and $F=\mathcal H(x)$. Quasi-smoothness and its differential rank survive passage to the analytic fibre. Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasismoothrankzeroisolated} therefore gives an ordinary open affinoid neighborhood of $y$ in $Y_x$ whose algebra is finite-dimensional over $F$. Its local factor $D$ at $y$ is the ordinary local ring of this finite space. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion} that ring is regular. A zero-dimensional regular local ring is a field, so $D=E=\mathcal H(y)$, and $E/F$ is finite.}
{令 $x=f(y)$、$F=\mathcal H(x)$。准光滑性及其微分秩在取解析纤维后保持。因此 Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasismoothrankzeroisolated} 给出 $y$ 在 $Y_x$ 中的普通开 affinoid 邻域，其代数在 $F$ 上有限维。它在 $y$ 处的局部因子 $D$ 就是此有限空间的普通局部环。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion}，该环正则。零维正则局部环为域，所以 $D=E=\mathcal H(y)$，且 $E/F$ 有限。}

\bi{For every finite field extension $L/F$, the extending absolute value makes $L$ complete, and finite base change identifies the corresponding algebra with $E\otimes_F L$. All its local factors are regular by geometric regularity of the analytic fibre, hence fields. Thus $E\otimes_F L$ is reduced. If some $a\in E$ were inseparable over $F$, its minimal polynomial would acquire a repeated root over a finite splitting field $L/F$. The algebra $F[a]\otimes_F L$ would then contain a nonzero nilpotent. Its injection into $E\otimes_F L$, obtained by tensoring the injection $F[a]\hookrightarrow E$ with the field $L$, contradicts reducedness. Every element of $E$ is therefore separable over $F$.}
{对每个有限域扩张 $L/F$，延拓绝对值使 $L$ 完备，而有限基变换将相应代数识别为 $E\otimes_F L$。由解析纤维的几何正则性，其全部局部因子都正则，因而为域；故 $E\otimes_F L$ 约化。若某个 $a\in E$ 在 $F$ 上不可分，其极小多项式在某个有限分裂域 $L/F$ 上有重根，故 $F[a]\otimes_F L$ 含非零幂零元。把单射 $F[a]\hookrightarrow E$ 与域 $L$ 张量，得到该代数到 $E\otimes_F L$ 的单射，与后者约化矛盾。因此 $E$ 的每个元素都在 $F$ 上可分。}
\end{proof}
