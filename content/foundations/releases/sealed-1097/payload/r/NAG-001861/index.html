Regular affinoids and their components

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 439

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Regular affinoids and their components}{正则 affinoid 及其分量}]
\label{fnd:regularaffinoidcomponents}
\bi{Let $Z=\mathcal M(C)$ be a generalized affinoid space whose ordinary local rings are regular. Then $C$ is a regular Noetherian ring and a finite product of integral affinoid algebras. The corresponding analytic components are open and closed. In each component, the analytic points with kernel equal to its minimal prime are ordinarily dense.}
{设 $Z=\mathcal M(C)$ 为 generalized affinoid 空间，其普通局部环均正则。则 $C$ 为正则 Noetherian 环，并且是有限个 affinoid 整环的乘积。相应的解析分量既开又闭。在每个分量中，以该分量的极小素理想为核的解析点对普通拓扑稠密。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For every prime $\mathfrak p\subset C$, the preceding lemma supplies $z\in Z$ with kernel $\mathfrak p$. The map $C_{\mathfrak p}\to\mathcal O_{Z,z}$ is faithfully flat: the ordinary stalk is a filtered colimit of flat rational-neighbourhood algebras by Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks}, and the map is local with maximal-ideal contraction $\mathfrak p$. Both rings are Noetherian. The regularity descent theorem, \cite[Tag 00OF]{StacksAlg}, therefore makes $C_{\mathfrak p}$ regular.}
{对每个素理想 $\mathfrak p\subset C$，上一引理给出核为 $\mathfrak p$ 的点 $z\in Z$。映射 $C_{\mathfrak p}\to\mathcal O_{Z,z}$ 忠实平坦：由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks}，普通茎是平坦 rational 邻域代数的滤过直极限；该映射又是局部映射，其极大理想收缩为 $\mathfrak p$。两环均 Noetherian，因此正则性的下降定理 \cite[Tag 00OF]{StacksAlg} 说明 $C_{\mathfrak p}$ 正则。}

\bi{By \cite[Tag 00NP]{StacksAlg}, every such local ring is a domain. In particular $C$ is reduced, and its distinct minimal primes are pairwise comaximal: a maximal ideal containing two of them would yield a local ring with two distinct minimal primes. The Chinese remainder theorem and the finite number of minimal primes give $C\simeq\prod_i C/\mathfrak p_i$. The idempotents of this product give precisely the open-and-closed analytic decomposition. Each factor is integral, and Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity} proves the density assertion.}
{由 \cite[Tag 00NP]{StacksAlg}，这些局部环均为整环。特别地，$C$ 约化，且其不同极小素理想两两互素：若某极大理想包含两个不同极小素理想，局部化后便得到具有两个不同极小素理想的局部环。极小素理想的有限性与中国剩余定理给出 $C\simeq\prod_i C/\mathfrak p_i$。此乘积的幂等元恰给出既开又闭的解析分解。每个因子为整环，而 Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity} 证明了所称稠密性。}
\end{proof}
