Split marked models and skeleta in every genus

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Split marked models and skeleta in every genus}
{任意亏格的分裂标记模型与骨架}]
\label{cand:algebraicallyclosedmarkedmodels}
\bi{Let $K$ be algebraically closed, complete and nontrivially
non-Archimedean valued, of any characteristic. Let $C/K$ be a
smooth projective connected curve, and let $D\subset C$ be a
finite reduced closed subscheme, possibly empty.
There is a projective flat finitely presented split nodal
model $\mathcal C/K^\circ$ of $C$ in which the marked sections
have pairwise distinct smooth specializations.
Its formal completion gives a finite metric skeleton, with one
infinite ray for each point of $D$, and a jointly continuous
strong deformation retraction of $(C\setminus D)^{\an}$ onto
that skeleton.}
{设 $K$ 为任意特征的代数闭、完备、非平凡非阿基米德赋值域。
设 $C/K$ 为光滑射影连通曲线，$D\subset C$ 为有限约化闭子概形，
允许为空。存在 $C$ 的射影、平坦、有限呈示的分裂结点模型
$\mathcal C/K^\circ$，使标记截面具有两两互异的光滑特殊化。
其形式完备化给出有限度量骨架，对 $D$ 中每个点添入一条无限射线，
并给出 $(C\setminus D)^{\an}$ 到此骨架的联合连续强形变收缩。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Every closed point of $C$ is $K$-rational by
\cite[Tag 00FV]{StacksAlg}. If $g(C)=0$, choose one such point
$P$. It is an effective Cartier divisor of degree one by
\cite[Tag 0B8Y]{StacksAlg}. The projective-line characterization
\cite[Tag 0C6U, implication (7) to (1)]{StacksAlg} gives
$C\simeq\mathbf P^1_K$. Take the smooth projective model
$\mathbf P^1_{K^\circ}$.}
{由 \cite[Tag 00FV]{StacksAlg}，$C$ 的每个闭点均为 $K$-有理点。
若 $g(C)=0$，选取其中一个点 $P$。由
\cite[Tag 0B8Y]{StacksAlg}，它是次数一的有效 Cartier 除子。
由射影直线的刻画
\cite[Tag 0C6U, implication (7) to (1)]{StacksAlg}，
$C\simeq\mathbf P^1_K$。取光滑射影模型
$\mathbf P^1_{K^\circ}$。}

\bi{If $g(C)\geq2$, Theorem~\ref{cand:stablevaluationextension}
gives a projective stable model after a finite extension of $K$.
Since $K$ is algebraically closed, that extension is $K$ itself.
If $g(C)=1$, choose a rational point and form the genus-two
stable double of Lemma~\ref{cand:rationalgenusonestabledouble}.
Apply Lemma~\ref{cand:properstackfinitevaluation} to its object
of the proper stable-curve stack. Again its finite extension is
$K$ itself. The projectivity subargument of
Theorem~\ref{cand:stablevaluationextension}, as isolated in
Lemma~\ref{cand:stablenodalgenericextension}, depends only on
stability and the local valuation base. It gives a projective
stable model $\mathcal Y/K^\circ$ of this nodal double.}
{若 $g(C)\geq2$，Theorem~\ref{cand:stablevaluationextension}
给出有限扩域后的射影稳定模型。由于 $K$ 代数闭，此扩张就是
$K$ 自身。若 $g(C)=1$，选取一个有理点，并构造
Lemma~\ref{cand:rationalgenusonestabledouble} 的亏格二稳定倍接。
对它在 proper 稳定曲线模叠中的对象应用
Lemma~\ref{cand:properstackfinitevaluation}；所得有限扩域同样
就是 $K$。Theorem~\ref{cand:stablevaluationextension} 的射影性
子论证，也即 Lemma~\ref{cand:stablenodalgenericextension}
中单独说明的部分，只使用稳定性与局部赋值基底。
它给出此结点倍接的射影稳定模型 $\mathcal Y/K^\circ$。}

\bi{In the positive-genus cases, apply
Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting} to the finitely many
special nodes. Its finite extension is still $K$, so all nodes
already have the required rational split pointed charts over
$K^\circ$. In genus one,
Proposition~\ref{cand:nodalmodelgenericnormalization}, which is
valid in every characteristic, separates the two labelled
generic components of $\mathcal Y$. Choose either resulting
projective split nodal model of $C$. This finishes model
existence in all three genus cases.}
{在正亏格情形，对有限个特殊结点应用
Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting}。
其有限扩域仍为 $K$，故全部结点已经在 $K^\circ$ 上具有所需
有理分裂带点坐标。在亏格一情形，任意特征均有效的
Proposition~\ref{cand:nodalmodelgenericnormalization}
将 $\mathcal Y$ 的两个带标签泛分量分离。
选取其中任一所得的 $C$ 的射影分裂结点模型。
至此三个亏格情形的模型存在性均已得到。}

\bi{Properness extends the finitely many rational points of
$D$ to sections. Apply Theorem~\ref{cand:equivariantmarkedrefinement}
with the trivial group to make their specializations smooth and
pairwise distinct. Finally the residue field $k$ of $K$ is
algebraically closed: lift any monic polynomial over $k$ to a
monic polynomial over $K^\circ$ and take a root in $K$; a root
of norm greater than one is excluded by strict domination of the
leading term, so reduction gives a root of the original polynomial.
Thus every smooth closed residual point has residue field $k$.
The perfect-residue alternative of
Theorem~\ref{cand:generalmarkedmodelskeleton} supplies the stated
skeleton and the entire jointly continuous homotopy.}
{由 properness，$D$ 中有限个有理点延拓为截面。
对平凡群应用 Theorem~\ref{cand:equivariantmarkedrefinement}，
使其特殊化光滑且两两互异。最后，$K$ 的剩余域 $k$ 代数闭：
将 $k$ 上任一首一多项式提升到 $K^\circ$ 上，再在 $K$ 中取根；
若根的范数大于一，则首项严格支配其余项，矛盾。
所以根可约化，给出原多项式的根。因此每个光滑剩余闭点的
剩余域都是 $k$。
Theorem~\ref{cand:generalmarkedmodelskeleton} 中完美剩余域的
情形给出所述骨架与整个联合连续同伦。}
\end{proof}
