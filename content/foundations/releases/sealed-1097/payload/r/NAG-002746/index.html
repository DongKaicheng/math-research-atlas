When radial Radon data determines the full measure

G Stable inertia and original-field models

Open in the English reader · PDF page 2044

Statement and full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[When radial Radon data determines the full measure]
\label{f9fm:radondetermination}
Let $\nu$ be a finite positive Radon measure on
$\overline{\mathbf R}^{,d}$ and $\mu$ a finite positive Radon measure
on $X^\an$ with $\bar t_*\mu=\nu$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:f9fm:toric-radon-concentration}
 \mu=\bar\rho_*\nu
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \mu(X^\an\setminus\bar\Gamma)=0.
\end{equation}
If $\nu$ gives zero mass to the tropical boundary and to every proper
affine hyperplane in $\mathbf R^d$ with nonzero integer normal, the
right-hand condition follows automatically. In particular it follows
for $\nu=W(t)dt$ from \eqref{eq:f9fm:toric-product-curvature}. In this case for every
$u\in C(X^\an)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:f9fm:toric-radon-integrals}
                  \int u\,d\mu=\int u(\rho(t))W(t)\,dt.
\end{equation}
No countability condition on the value group or the set of analytic
points is needed. Positivity and Radon regularity are hypotheses.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On $\bar\Gamma$ the map $\bar\rho\bar t$ is the identity. If $\mu$
is concentrated there, then for every continuous $u$,
\[
 \int u\,d\mu=\int u\circ\bar\rho\circ\bar t\,d\mu
             =\int u\circ\bar\rho\,d\nu.
\]
This proves the forward identification of measures; the converse
concentration is immediate from the image of $\bar\rho$.

Now impose the additional hypotheses on $\nu$. The analytic toric
boundary has $\mu$-mass zero because its image is in the tropical
boundary. For each $x\in T^\an\setminus\Gamma$, the neighbourhood in
Lemma~\ref{f9fm:gausssupport} has mass zero: positivity gives
\[
 0\leq\mu(U_x)\leq\mu(t^{-1}(H_x))=\nu(H_x)=0.
\]
Every compact subset of the open set $T^\an\setminus\Gamma$ is covered
by finitely many such $U_x$, hence has mass zero. Inner regularity
therefore makes the entire open set have mass zero. This finite-cover
argument is essential; it does not take a countable union over all
Laurent polynomials. Equation \eqref{eq:f9fm:toric-radon-concentration} now applies.
An absolutely continuous measure $Wdt$ assigns zero mass to all the
specified hyperplanes, which proves \eqref{eq:f9fm:toric-radon-integrals}.
\end{proof}
