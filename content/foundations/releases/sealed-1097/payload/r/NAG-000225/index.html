Result 3.3.2

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 495

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}\label{fnd:shilovreduction}
\bi{An affinoid space has a finite Shilov boundary. If $A$ is strictly affinoid over a nontrivially valued field, $\widetilde A$ is a finite-type $\widetilde K$-algebra, the canonical reduction map is surjective, and each generic point of $\operatorname{Spec}\widetilde A$ has a unique analytic preimage. Those preimages are exactly the Shilov boundary.}
{Affinoid space 有 finite Shilov boundary。若 $A$ 在 nontrivially valued field 上 strictly affinoid，则 $\widetilde A$ 是 finite-type $\widetilde K$-algebra，canonical reduction map 为 surjective，且 $\operatorname{Spec}\widetilde A$ 的每个 generic point 有唯一 analytic preimage。这些 preimages 恰好组成 Shilov boundary。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Corollary~\ref{fnd:affinoidshilovinternal} proves finite Shilov boundaries for all radii. In the strict case, Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictcanonicalfinitegeneration} proves finite generation of canonical reduction, and Theorem~\ref{fnd:canonicalreductionsurjective} realizes every prime ideal by an analytic point. Theorem~\ref{fnd:canonicalgenericshilov} identifies the minimal primes with the Shilov points and proves uniqueness of each preimage.}
{Corollary~\ref{fnd:affinoidshilovinternal} 对任意半径证明有限 Shilov 边界的存在性。在 strict 情形，Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictcanonicalfinitegeneration} 证明典范约化的有限生成性，Theorem~\ref{fnd:canonicalreductionsurjective} 将每个素理想实现为一个解析点的约化。Theorem~\ref{fnd:canonicalgenericshilov} 将极小素理想与 Shilov 点一一对应，并证明每个原像唯一。}
\end{proof}
