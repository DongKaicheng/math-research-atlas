Strict exactness for a coefficient Laurent pair

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Strict exactness for a coefficient Laurent pair}{系数 Laurent pair 的 strict exactness}]\label{fnd:strictcoefficientlaurentpair}
\bi{Let $A,M,f,r$ satisfy Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictlaurentbound}. Define $E_+,E_-,E$, the relation operators, and $P_+,P_-,P_0$ exactly as in Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachlaurentcriterion}, with these $M,f,r$. All three quotients are Banach $A$-modules for their quotient norms, and}
{设 $A,M,f,r$ 满足 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictlaurentbound}。对这些 $M,f,r$，完全按照 Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachlaurentcriterion} 定义 $E_+,E_-,E$、关系算子以及 $P_+,P_-,P_0$。三个商配备商范数后均为 Banach $A$-模，且}
\[
 0\longrightarrow M
 \xrightarrow{m\mapsto([m],[m])}P_+\oplus P_-
 \xrightarrow{([u],[v])\mapsto[u-v]}P_0
 \longrightarrow0
\]
\bi{is strict exact. With $c$ supplied by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictlaurentbound}, the augmentation estimate and the final quotient isometry of Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachlaurentcriterion} hold. This applies to every $f\in A$, without a condition that $f$ be a coordinate or a unit.}
{为 strict exact。取 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictlaurentbound} 提供的 $c$，则 Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachlaurentcriterion} 的 augmentation estimate 及最后的 quotient isometry 均成立。此结论适用于每个 $f\in A$，无须 $f$ 为坐标或单位。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictlaurentbound} verifies the sole additional lower-bound hypothesis of Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachlaurentcriterion}. Apply that theorem to the specified coefficient modules and their actual relation images. Its proof gives closedness of all relation images, completeness of the quotients, both exactness assertions, and the stated quantitative estimates.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictlaurentbound} 验证了 Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachlaurentcriterion} 所需的额外范数下界。将该定理应用于所指定的系数模及其实际关系像，即由其证明得到所有关系像 closed、各商完备、两处正合性以及所述定量估计。}
\end{proof}

\section{\bi{The ground field and convergence}{Ground field 与 convergence}}\label{fnd:valuedfields}
\begin{definition}\label{fnd:fieldconventions}
\bi{Throughout the foundations, $K$ is a field complete for a real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value. We permit the trivial absolute value unless a statement explicitly excludes it. Set}
{基础篇中，$K$ 是关于 real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value 完备的 field。除非明确排除，否则允许 trivial absolute value。记}
\[
\begin{gathered}
v(a)=-\log|a|,\qquad \Gamma=v(K^\times),\\
K^\circ=\{a\in K:|a|\leq1\},\qquad K^{\circ\circ}=\{a\in K:|a|<1\},\\
\widetilde K=K^\circ/K^{\circ\circ}.
\end{gathered}
\]
\bi{A complete extension $L/K$ always carries an absolute value restricting to the specified one on $K$. We do not rescale it when changing the ground field.}
{Complete extension $L/K$ 始终配备限制为指定 $K$ 上 absolute value 的 absolute value。改变 ground field 时不重新缩放它。}

\begin{definition}\label{fnd:banachalgebra}
\bi{A Banach $K$-algebra is a commutative unital $K$-algebra $A$ with a complete non-Archimedean norm satisfying $\|ca\|=|c|\|a\|$, $\|1\|=1$, and $\|ab\|\leq\|a\|\|b\|$. A homomorphism is bounded if $\|\phi(a)\|\leq C\|a\|$ for some constant $C$. Equivalent norms define the same Banach topology. A surjective bounded map is admissible if its target norm is uniformly equivalent to the quotient norm; equivalently, it admits lifts with a uniform norm bound. Equality of topologies alone is insufficient at trivial valuation; see Definition~\ref{fnd:normstrictconvention} and Example~\ref{fnd:normtopologycounterexample}.}
{Banach $K$-algebra 是 commutative unital $K$-algebra $A$，配备一个 complete norm。此 norm 为 non-Archimedean，且满足 $\|ca\|=|c|\|a\|$、$\|1\|=1$ 和 $\|ab\|\leq\|a\|\|b\|$。若存在常数 $C$ 使 $\|\phi(a)\|\leq C\|a\|$，则 homomorphism 称为 bounded。Equivalent norms 定义相同的 Banach topology。Surjective bounded map 若使 target norm 与 quotient norm uniformly equivalent，则称为 admissible；等价地，它具有 uniform norm bound 的 lifts。赋值平凡时，仅有 topologies 相同还不够；见 Definition~\ref{fnd:normstrictconvention} 及 Example~\ref{fnd:normtopologycounterexample}。}
\end{definition}
Standing context 3 · exact TeX
\begin{definition}\label{fnd:normstrictconvention}
\bi{For a bounded linear map $u:E\to F$, give $\operatorname{im}u$ both its induced norm from $F$ and its quotient seminorm from $E$. The map is norm-strict, or admissible, when these are uniformly equivalent. Since $u$ is bounded, the additional requirement is a constant $C>0$ such that every $y\in\operatorname{im}u$ has a lift $x$ with $\|x\|\leq C\|y\|$. The constant may be enlarged by any fixed factor greater than one to approximate an unattained infimum.}
{对 bounded linear map $u:E\to F$，在 $\operatorname{im}u$ 上同时考虑来自 $F$ 的 induced norm 与来自 $E$ 的 quotient seminorm。当两者 uniformly equivalent 时，称该 map 为 norm-strict，或 admissible。由于 $u$ 已 bounded，额外条件等价于存在常数 $C>0$，使每个 $y\in\operatorname{im}u$ 都有 lift $x$ 满足 $\|x\|\leq C\|y\|$。当 infimum 不取到时，可将常数乘以任意固定的大于一的因子。}
\end{definition}
