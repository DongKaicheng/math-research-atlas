NAG-002204 · Accepted whole

The stable double of a genus-one curve

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 737

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The stable double of a genus-one curve}
{亏格一曲线的稳定倍接}]
\label{cand:genusonestabledouble}
\bi{Let $K$ be a characteristic-zero field and let $C/K$ be a
smooth projective geometrically connected curve of genus one.
There is a stable curve $Y/K$ of genus $d+1\geq2$ whose normalization
is the disjoint union of two labelled copies $C_1,C_2$ of $C$.
The construction is defined over $K$; consequently every scalar-extension
Galois action preserves the two labels.}
{设 $K$ 为特征零域，$C/K$ 为光滑射影几何连通的亏格一曲线。
存在亏格 $d+1\geq2$ 的稳定曲线 $Y/K$，其正规化为 $C$ 的两个
带标签副本 $C_1,C_2$ 的不交并。构造在 $K$ 上定义，故每个
标量扩张所带的 Galois 作用均保持这两个标签。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose a closed point $q\in C$ with residue field $E$ finite
over $K$, using \cite[Tag 00FV]{StacksAlg} on a nonempty affine open.
Put $d=[E:K]$. The extension is separable, since $K$ has
characteristic zero. Glue the two copies of $q$ by the identity on
$\operatorname{Spec}E$. This is the pushout of}
{在一个非空仿射开集上使用 \cite[Tag 00FV]{StacksAlg}，选取
闭点 $q\in C$，使剩余域 $E/K$ 有限。令 $d=[E:K]$。
由于 $K$ 特征零，此扩张可分。沿 $\operatorname{Spec}E$ 的恒等映射
粘合 $q$ 的两个副本；这给出以下图的推出：}
\[
 \operatorname{Spec}E\amalg\operatorname{Spec}E
 \longrightarrow C_1\amalg C_2,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Spec}E\amalg\operatorname{Spec}E
 \longrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}E.
\]
\bi{The first map is a closed immersion and the second is finite.
An affine neighborhood of $q$ in each copy supplies the affine-neighborhood
condition of \cite[Tag 0E25]{StacksAlg}, so the pushout is a scheme.
It is separated and of finite type over $K$ by
\cite[Tags 0E26, 0E27]{StacksAlg}. The natural map
$\nu:C_1\amalg C_2\to Y$ is finite and surjective: on the glued
affine chart it is the inclusion}
{第一个映射为闭浸入，第二个为有限态射。每个副本中 $q$ 的一个
仿射邻域满足 \cite[Tag 0E25]{StacksAlg} 的仿射邻域条件，故推出
是概形。由 \cite[Tags 0E26, 0E27]{StacksAlg}，它在 $K$ 上分离且
有限型。自然映射 $\nu:C_1\amalg C_2\to Y$ 有限满射：
在粘合仿射片上，它对应于包含映射}
\[
 R=A\times_E A\ \longrightarrow\ A\oplus A,
 \qquad A\oplus A=R(1,0)+R(0,1).
\]
\bi{Properness of $C_1\amalg C_2$ and
\cite[Tag 03GN]{StacksAlg} show that $Y$ is proper.
The fibre-product description commutes with field extensions, since
tensoring over $K$ is exact. Over an algebraic closure the construction
therefore glues the two smooth connected copies at $d$ distinct pairs
of rational points. At each glued point the completed local ring is}
{由 $C_1\amalg C_2$ 的 properness 及 \cite[Tag 03GN]{StacksAlg}，
$Y$ proper。因为在 $K$ 上张量是正合的，纤维积描述与域扩张相容。
故在代数闭包上，此构造将两个光滑连通副本沿 $d$ 对互异有理点粘合。
每个粘合点处的完备局部环为}
\[
 \overline K[[u]]\times_{\overline K}\overline K[[v]]
       \simeq\overline K[[u,v]]/(uv),
\]
\bi{by \cite[Tag 0E36]{StacksAlg} and the smooth rational-point
completion calculation already used in
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure};
\cite[Tag 0CBW]{StacksAlg} identifies these as split nodes.
Thus $Y$ is geometrically connected and nodal. The finite map $\nu$
is an isomorphism off the glued points and has normal source, so
it is the normalization: an element of the common total function
ring integral over a target affine ring is also integral over its
finite normal source ring, and hence belongs to the latter.}
{这里使用 \cite[Tag 0E36]{StacksAlg}，以及
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure} 已使用的光滑有理点
完备化计算；\cite[Tag 0CBW]{StacksAlg} 将这些点识别为分裂结点。
因此 $Y$ 几何连通且只有结点。有限映射 $\nu$
在粘合点外为同构，且其源正规，因此它是正规化：共同全函数环
中的元素若在目标仿射环上整，也在对应的有限正规源环上整，
故属于后者。}

\bi{The ring description gives the exact sequence}
{由环的描述得到正合列}
\[
 0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_Y\longrightarrow
 \nu_*(\mathcal O_{C_1}\oplus\mathcal O_{C_2})
 \xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{res}_1-\mathrm{res}_2\ }
 \mathcal O_{\operatorname{Spec}E}\longrightarrow0.
\]
\bi{Here the last sheaf is extended by zero along the closed immersion.
The cohomology of the finite pushforward equals that on $C_1\amalg C_2$
by \cite[Tag 089W]{StacksAlg}; the same result and affine
acyclicity, \cite[Tag 01XB]{StacksAlg}, give vanishing of higher
cohomology of the last sheaf.
The smooth geometrically connected curve $C$ is geometrically integral:
its geometric local rings are normal domains by Tag 033C, so distinct
geometric irreducible components cannot meet, and connectedness
leaves just one component. Hence $H^0(C,\mathcal O_C)=K$ by
\cite[Tag 04L2, part (4)]{StacksAlg}, the cohomology sequence is}
{末项沿闭浸入作零延拓。由 \cite[Tag 089W]{StacksAlg}，
有限推前的上同调等于 $C_1\amalg C_2$ 上的上同调；
同一结论及 \cite[Tag 01XB]{StacksAlg} 的仿射非循环性
给出末项的高阶上同调消失。
光滑几何连通曲线 $C$ 几何整：由 Tag 033C，其几何局部环
为正规整环，所以互异几何不可约分量不相交；连通性说明只有
一个分量。因此由 \cite[Tag 04L2, part (4)]{StacksAlg}，
$H^0(C,\mathcal O_C)=K$，故上同调列为}
\[
 0\longrightarrow H^0(Y,\mathcal O_Y)\longrightarrow K^2
 \xrightarrow{(a,b)\mapsto a-b}E
 \longrightarrow H^1(Y,\mathcal O_Y)\longrightarrow K^2
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
\bi{It follows that $H^0(Y,\mathcal O_Y)=K$ and
$\dim_KH^1(Y,\mathcal O_Y)=d+1$.
Every geometric irreducible component has genus one, so there is no
rational tail or bridge. Hence $Y$ is stable by
\cite[Tag 0E75]{StacksAlg}. All maps and both component labels are
defined over $K$, which proves the equivariance assertion.}
{因此 $H^0(Y,\mathcal O_Y)=K$ 且
$\dim_KH^1(Y,\mathcal O_Y)=d+1$。
每个几何不可约分量的亏格均为一，故不存在有理尾或有理桥。
由 \cite[Tag 0E75]{StacksAlg}，$Y$ 稳定。
全部映射与两个分量标签都在 $K$ 上定义，由此得到等变断言。}
\end{proof}
