Finite polyhedral strata for initial degenerations

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Finite polyhedral strata for initial degenerations}{初始退化的有限多面体分层}]
\label{cand:finitegroebnercomplex}
\bi{There is a finite polyhedral complex covering $\mathbf R^{n+1}$, defined by integer linear forms and constants in $v(K^\times)$, such that on the relative interior of each cell the schemes defined by $\operatorname{in}_\omega(H)$ are isomorphic after residue extension and diagonal torus translation. For an ideal $I\subset K[M]$, there is consequently a finite integral $v(K^\times)$-affine polyhedral complex on $\mathbf R^n$ for which $\operatorname{Trop}(\operatorname{Spec}K[M]/I)$ is the support of a subcomplex.}
{存在覆盖 $\mathbf R^{n+1}$ 的有限多面体复形，由整数线性形式及 $v(K^\times)$ 中的常数定义，使每个胞腔相对内部上的 $\operatorname{in}_\omega(H)$ 在剩余扩域及对角环面平移后定义同构的概形。因此，对理想 $I\subset K[M]$，$\mathbf R^n$ 上存在有限整 $v(K^\times)$-仿射多面体复形，使 $\operatorname{Trop}(\operatorname{Spec}K[M]/I)$ 为其某个子复形的支集。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Take $D$ from Proposition~\ref{cand:initialuniformdegree}. For each $d\le D$ apply Lemma~\ref{cand:pluckerpivotweights} to $H_d$. Cut weight space by all equalities between the finitely many affine functions $p_B$ occurring in these degrees. Their normals are integral and their constant differences belong to $v(K^\times)$. The resulting finite hyperplane arrangement is the required polyhedral complex.}
{取 Proposition~\ref{cand:initialuniformdegree} 中的 $D$。对每个 $d\le D$，将 Lemma~\ref{cand:pluckerpivotweights} 用于 $H_d$。用这些次数中有限个仿射函数 $p_B$ 之间的全部等式切分权重空间。其法向量为整数向量，常数差属于 $v(K^\times)$，故所得有限超平面排列给出所需复形。}

\bi{Fix weights $\omega,\omega'$ in the same relative interior. In each degree choose one common minimizing pivot set $B$; the same coefficients in its row basis survive at both weights. Realize both weights in a common valued extension, with translating points $t,t'$. For every surviving exponent difference $m-b$, the unit ratio $(t'/t)^{m-b}$ has norm one. On the subgroup $A\subset\mathbf Z^{n+1}$ generated by all these differences, their residues define a character $\rho:A\to\widetilde L^\times$. This character extends to the full exponent lattice after a finite algebraic residue extension. To see this directly, adjoin the standard basis vectors successively. When adjoining $e_i$, if no positive multiple of $e_i$ belongs to the current subgroup, give $e_i$ any nonzero value. Otherwise let $q>0$ be least with $qe_i$ in that subgroup, adjoin a root $b$ of $T^q-\rho(qe_i)$, and set $\rho(a+me_i)=\rho(a)b^m$. This is well defined and extends $\rho$. A root is obtained by quotienting by an irreducible factor of that polynomial. After finitely many steps, translation by the resulting torus point sends every surviving coefficient in every row at $\omega$ to its coefficient at $\omega'$. The ideals are generated by these rows in degrees at most $D$, so the translation identifies the ideals.}
{固定同一相对内部中的 $\omega,\omega'$。每个次数选取共同的最小主元集 $B$，则相同行基中的相同系数在两处保留。在共同赋值扩域中以 $t,t'$ 实现两个权重。每个保留的指数差 $m-b$ 均使 $(t'/t)^{m-b}$ 的范数为一。在这些差生成的子群 $A\subset\mathbf Z^{n+1}$ 上，其剩余类给出特征 $\rho:A\to\widetilde L^\times$。在有限代数剩余扩域后，此特征可延拓到整个指数格。具体地，逐次添加标准基向量。当添加 $e_i$ 时，若当前子群不含它的正倍数，可赋予 $e_i$ 任意非零值；否则，取使 $qe_i$ 属于当前子群的最小 $q>0$，添加 $T^q-\rho(qe_i)$ 的根 $b$，并定义 $\rho(a+me_i)=\rho(a)b^m$。此定义良好且延拓原特征。取上述多项式的不可约因子并作商即可获得所需根。有限步后，所得环面点的平移将 $\omega$ 处每个保留系数变为 $\omega'$ 处的对应系数。这些次数至多 $D$ 的行生成初始理想，故平移识别两个理想。}

\bi{For the Laurent ideal, take the homogeneous kernel}
{对 Laurent 理想，取齐次核}
\[
 H=\ker\bigl(K[x_0,\ldots,x_n]\longrightarrow
                 (K[M]/I)[z,z^{-1}]\bigr),
 \qquad x_0\mapsto z,\quad x_i\mapsto z\chi^{e_i}.
\]
\bi{Localizing all the variables and putting $x_0=1$ recovers $I$. Initial relations commute with this operation: multiplication by a monomial shifts every term valuation by the same amount, and any Laurent relation can first be multiplied by a monomial and homogenized. Restrict the constructed complex to $\omega_0=0$. Nonemptiness of the resulting initial scheme is constant on every relative interior. By Theorem~\ref{cand:realinitialdegeneration} this is exactly membership in $\operatorname{Trop}(X)$. That set is closed by Proposition~\ref{cand:tropicalpropermap}; hence it contains the closure of every selected relative interior. It is therefore a union of closed cells, together with all their faces.}
{将所有变量局部化并令 $x_0=1$，便恢复 $I$。初始关系与此操作相容：乘单项式使各项赋值平移同一个数，而每个 Laurent 关系可先乘单项式再齐次化。将所构造复形限制到 $\omega_0=0$，所得初始概形的非空性在每个相对内部上恒定。由 Theorem~\ref{cand:realinitialdegeneration}，这恰为属于 $\operatorname{Trop}(X)$ 的条件。该集合由 Proposition~\ref{cand:tropicalpropermap} 闭，因而包含每个选定相对内部的闭包，故为闭胞腔及其全部面的并。}
\end{proof}
