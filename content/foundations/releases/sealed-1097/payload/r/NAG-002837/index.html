Norm integration under a component-degree criterion

G Stable inertia and original-field models

Open in the English reader · PDF page 2012

Statement and full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Norm integration under a component-degree criterion]
\label{f9ml:normcriterion}
Let $q:\cY\to\cX$ be finite locally free of constant rank $N\geq1$,
with both models in the class of \hyperref[f9ml:finite-model-data]{the standing finite-model data} and with integral generic
fibres $Y,X$. Give $q^*L_i$ and $q^*M$ their pulled-back model metrics.
For each $V$, set
\[
 B_V=(q_*\cO_{\cY})_{\eta_V},\qquad A_V=\cO_{\cX,\eta_V}.
\]
Retain the ordinary algebraic local descriptions: $B_V$ is the whole
finite free rank-$N$ algebra, and all its maximal localizations are the
DVRs $\cO_{\cY,\eta_W}$ for the components $W$ over $V$.
Let $t_V$ be a uniformizer of $A_V$, and put
\[
 r_W=\ord_W(t_V),\quad
 f_W=[\kappa(W):\kappa(V)],\quad
 \delta_W=\deg_{q^*\cL_1|_W,\ldots,q^*\cL_d|_W,k}(W).
\]
Assume the finite list of component-degree identities
\begin{equation}\label{eq:f9ml:degreecriterion}
                         \delta_W=f_W\delta_V\quad(W\mid V).
\end{equation}
For a nonzero rational section $s$ of $q^*M$, use the determinant section
$\Nm_q(s)$ of $\Nm_q(q^*M)\simeq M^{\otimes N}$, with the metric
induced by the model line $\cM^{\otimes N}$ and root exponent $e_0$.
Then
\begin{align}
 q^\an_*\mu_{\cY}&=N\mu_{\cX},\label{eq:f9ml:modelprojection}\\
 \int_{X^\an}-\log\|\Nm_q(s)\|\,d\mu_{\cX}
   &=\int_{Y^\an}-\log\|s\|\,d\mu_{\cY}.\label{eq:f9ml:normintegral}
\end{align}
Both logarithmic integrals are absolutely convergent with respect to
total variation. All components, full residue degrees and unreduced
DVR quotients are retained, including in inseparable cases.
The criterion holds for the coordinate power map
$T_i=S_i^{n_i}$ on $(\mathbf P^1_R)^d$, with $n_i\geq1$,
$\cL_i=\operatorname{pr}_i^*\cO(1)$, and $e_i=1$.
\end{theorem}
\par\smallskip\noindent{\small\textit{Direct inputs} \hyperref[f9ds:lengthprojection]{\texttt{NAG-002543}}, \hyperref[f9nm:dvrlength]{\texttt{NAG-002552}}, Theorem~\ref{f9ml:atomiclog}, \hyperref[f9ml:finite-model-mixed-degree]{the mixed algebraic degree convention}.}\par\smallskip
\begin{proof}
Each local DVR extension gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:f9ml:ramification}
 b_W=r_Wb_V,
 \qquad \sum_{W\mid V}f_Wr_W=N.
\end{equation}
The first equality follows by writing $\pi=u t_V^{b_V}$ with $u$ a
unit. For the second, apply \hyperref[f9ds:lengthprojection]{\texttt{NAG-002543}} to the regular sequence $(t_V)$
in $A_V$ and the full algebra $B_V$. Its summand at $W$ is $f_Wr_W$
because $\length_{\cO_{\cY,\eta_W}}
(\cO_{\cY,\eta_W}/t_V)=r_W$. This proof does not require a separable
residue extension. Equations \eqref{eq:f9ml:point} and
\eqref{eq:f9ml:ramification} give $q^\an(\xi_W)=\xi_V$.
Thus the mass pushed to $\xi_V$ is
\[
 \sum_{W\mid V}\frac{b_W\delta_W}{E}
       =\frac{b_V\delta_V}{E}\sum_{W\mid V}r_Wf_W
       =N\frac{b_V\delta_V}{E},
\]
which proves \eqref{eq:f9ml:modelprojection}.

Let $F=K(X)$ and $L=K(Y)$. The generic algebra $B_V\otimes_{A_V}F$
is $L$, a field extension of full degree $N$. For $a\in L^\times$,
\hyperref[f9nm:dvrlength]{\texttt{NAG-002552}} gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:f9ml:ordnorm}
              \ord_V\Nm_{L/F}(a)=\sum_{W\mid V}f_W\ord_W(a).
\end{equation}
To include a rational $a$, choose a nonzero $z\in A_V$ with
$za\in B_V$, possible because $B_V$ spans $L$ over $F$. Apply that
lemma to $za$ and to $z$, and subtract. Multiplication by $a$ on $L$
is invertible even for an inseparable extension; its determinant, rather
than a product over distinct embeddings, is the norm used here.

In a local frame $e$ of $\cM$, write $s^{\otimes e_0}=a q^*e$.
The coefficient of $(\Nm_qs)^{\otimes e_0}$ in $e^{\otimes N}$ is
$\Nm_{L/F}(a)$. Indeed, exterior multiplication on the rank-$N$
module gives $\det(m_a)$, while changing $e$ by a unit $u$ multiplies
the determinant frame by $u^N$. These changes are exactly those of
$\cM^{\otimes N}$, so the construction glues and has the stated
pulled-back tensor-power metric. Consequently, if $c_W=\ord_W(a)$,
then the downstairs coefficient is $\sum_{W\mid V}f_Wc_W$.
Theorem~\ref{f9ml:atomiclog} now gives
\[
 \int-\log\|\Nm_q(s)\|\,d\mu_{\cX}
 =\frac{\lambda}{e_0E}\sum_V\delta_V\sum_{W\mid V}f_Wc_W
 =\frac{\lambda}{e_0E}\sum_W\delta_Wc_W.
\]
The last expression is the upstairs integral. Absolute convergence is
part of the same finite-sum theorem.

For the coordinate power map, the exponent classes
$\prod_iS_i^{a_i}$, $0\leq a_i<n_i$, are a free basis on every
coordinate chart. They give finite flat rank $N=\prod_i n_i$ and
the full special-fibre field degree $N$, including inseparable degrees.
The single reduced component has mixed degree one downstairs. Upstairs
the pullback of the $i$th coordinate line is
$\operatorname{pr}_i^*\cO(n_i)$. Choose the coordinate section with
divisor $n_i\{S_i=0\}$ for each $i$. Their proper intersection at
the rational product origin has local algebra
\[
                 k[S_1,\ldots,S_d]/(S_1^{n_1},\ldots,S_d^{n_d}),
\]
whose monomial basis has length $N$. This computes the mixed degree
as $N$ and verifies \eqref{eq:f9ml:degreecriterion} in the stated example.
\end{proof}
