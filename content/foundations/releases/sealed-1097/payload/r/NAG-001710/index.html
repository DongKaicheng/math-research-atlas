Finite maps and closed immersions under analytification

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 286

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Finite maps and closed immersions under analytification}{解析化下的有限映射与闭浸入}]
\label{fnd:analytificationfiniteclosed}
\bi{Analytification preserves finite morphisms and closed immersions. More precisely, for a finite ring map $R\to S$ of finite-type $K$-algebras and an affinoid domain $V=\mathcal M(A)\subset X_R$, the inverse image of $V$ in $X_S$ is the finite norm-admissible affinoid space}
{解析化保持有限态射与闭浸入。更精确地，对有限型 $K$-代数间的有限环映射 $R\to S$，以及 affinoid 域 $V=\mathcal M(A)\subset X_R$，$V$ 在 $X_S$ 中的逆像为有限 norm-admissible affinoid 空间}
\[
 \mathcal M(A\otimes_R S),
\]
\bi{where the algebra has its canonical finite-$A$-module topology. If $S=R/J$, its algebra is $A/JA$.}
{这里的代数配备典范有限 $A$-模拓扑。若 $S=R/J$，则该代数为 $A/JA$。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Set $D=A\otimes_R S$. It is finite over $A$, so Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras} gives its canonical affinoid algebra structure. Let $\mathcal M(C)$ be any affinoid test space. A pair of maps to $V$ and to $X_S$ with the same composite to $X_R$ is a bounded homomorphism $A\to C$ and an algebra homomorphism $S\to C$ agreeing on $R$. The algebraic tensor property gives a unique $A$-linear algebra map $D\to C$. It is bounded: choose finitely many generators $e_j$ of $D$ as an $A$-module and controlled lifts of its elements from $A^m$; the finite numbers $\|e_j\mapsto C\|$ and the bound on $A\to C$ give a uniform operator bound. Conversely any bounded $D\to C$ supplies the pair.}
{置 $D=A\otimes_R S$。它在 $A$ 上有限，因此 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras} 给出其典范 affinoid 代数结构。取任意 affinoid 测试空间 $\mathcal M(C)$。到 $V$ 与 $X_S$ 的一对映射，若复合到 $X_R$ 相同，就等价于有界同态 $A\to C$ 与代数同态 $S\to C$ 在 $R$ 上一致。代数张量积性质给出唯一的 $A$-线性代数映射 $D\to C$。它有界：取 $D$ 作为 $A$-模的有限生成元 $e_j$，并从 $A^m$ 对其元素作受控提升；各像的有限范数与 $A\to C$ 的界共同给出统一算子界。反之，有界映射 $D\to C$ 给出所需的一对映射。}

\bi{The bijection is natural and glues on affinoid covers of any analytic test space. Hence $\mathcal M(D)$ is $V\times_{X_R}X_S$. The domain mapping property identifies this fibre product with the inverse-image analytic domain. The affinoid domains $V$ cover $X_R$, proving finiteness of the analytified morphism. When $S=R/J$, the algebraic tensor quotient is $A/JA$. The ideal $JA$ is finitely generated and closed by the affinoid finite-module theorem, so this is an actual closed immersion. Both properties are local on the affine-open cover of a scheme target, which proves the global assertion.}
{该双射自然，并可在任意解析测试空间的 affinoid 覆盖上粘合。因此 $\mathcal M(D)$ 就是 $V\times_{X_R}X_S$。域映射性质将此纤维积识别为逆像解析域。Affinoid 域 $V$ 覆盖 $X_R$，从而证明解析化态射有限。若 $S=R/J$，则代数张量商为 $A/JA$。由 affinoid 有限模定理，$JA$ 有限生成且闭，故得到实际闭浸入。这两个性质都可在概形目标的仿射开覆盖上检验，由此得到整体断言。}
\end{proof}
