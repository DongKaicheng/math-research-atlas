Affine algebraic models

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 653

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Affine algebraic models}{仿射代数模型}]
\label{cand:affineflatformalcompletion}
\bi{Let $B$ be a finitely presented flat $O$-algebra, with a
presentation}
{设 $B$ 为有限表示的平坦 $O$-代数，并给定表示}
\[
 B=O[T_1,\ldots,T_n]/(f_1,\ldots,f_s).
\]
\bi{Its completion $\widehat B$ is an admissible $O$-algebra, and
the natural maps give}
{其完备化 $\widehat B$ 是 admissible $O$-代数，自然映射给出}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \widehat B&=O\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle/(f_1,\ldots,f_s),\\
 \widehat B[1/\pi]
   &=K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle/(f_1,\ldots,f_s),\\
 B/\pi^m B&\simeq\widehat B/\pi^m\widehat B\qquad(m\geq1).
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{The generic fibre of $\operatorname{Spf}\widehat B$ is
canonically the analytic domain $|T_i|\leq1$ in
$(\operatorname{Spec}B[1/\pi])^{\an}$, with the displayed affinoid
algebra. For $b\in B$, the completion of $B[1/b]$ is the principal
formal-open algebra}
{形式概形 $\operatorname{Spf}\widehat B$ 的泛纤维典范地等于
$(\operatorname{Spec}B[1/\pi])^{\an}$ 中的解析域 $|T_i|\leq1$，
其 affinoid 代数为上式所列代数。对 $b\in B$，
$B[1/b]$ 的完备化为主形式开集的代数}
\[
 \widehat{B[1/b]}
   \simeq\widehat B\langle U\rangle/(bU-1).
\]
\bi{Its generic fibre is the domain $|b|=1$ in the preceding
bounded-coordinate domain. These identifications respect the
polynomial coordinate maps and their restrictions.}
{其泛纤维为上述有界坐标域中的 $|b|=1$ 域。这些识别与
多项式坐标映射及其限制相容。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The completion of $O[T]$ is $P=O\langle T\rangle$:
a compatible polynomial system modulo $\pi^m$ gives one coefficient
in the complete ring $O$ at each monomial, and for every $m$ only
finitely many coefficients survive modulo $\pi^m$. Conversely such
a restricted coefficient family gives the compatible polynomial
system. The powers of $\pi$ define the given topology on $O$.}
{$O[T]$ 的完备化为 $P=O\langle T\rangle$：模 $\pi^m$ 的
相容多项式族在每个单项式处给出完备环 $O$ 中的一个系数，
且对每个 $m$ 只有有限个系数在模 $\pi^m$ 后非零。反之，
这样的受限系数族给出相容多项式族。$\pi$ 的各次幂定义
$O$ 上给定的拓扑。}

\bi{Apply Lemma~\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion} to
$O[T]\twoheadrightarrow B$. It makes $P\to\widehat B$ surjective,
with kernel the closure of $(f_1,\ldots,f_s)$ from $O[T]$.
The ideal $(f_1,\ldots,f_s)P$ is closed by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalmoduletopology}. Polynomial approximation
of the finitely many coefficients in a sum $\sum_i f_i g_i$ shows
that this ideal is exactly the stated closure. This proves the
restricted quotient presentation.}
{对 $O[T]\twoheadrightarrow B$ 应用
Lemma~\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion}，得到满射
$P\to\widehat B$，其核为 $O[T]$ 中理想
$(f_1,\ldots,f_s)$ 的闭包。由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalmoduletopology}，
$(f_1,\ldots,f_s)P$ 是闭理想。对有限和 $\sum_i f_i g_i$
中的各 $g_i$ 作多项式逼近，说明该理想恰为上述闭包，
从而得到受限级数商表示。}

\bi{Flatness of $B$ preserves injectivity of multiplication by
$\pi$ on $O$. Hence $B$ has no $\pi$-torsion.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion} gives the same assertion
for $\widehat B$ and gives all the reduction isomorphisms.
The displayed quotient is topologically of finite type;
Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation} makes it
topologically finitely presented, so it is admissible.
By Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations}, inverting
$\pi$ gives the displayed strict affinoid algebra.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affineanalytificationconstruction}, with all
radii equal to one, identifies it with the asserted analytic
domain, including its analytic structure.}
{$B$ 的平坦性保持 $O$ 上乘以 $\pi$ 的单射性，故 $B$
无 $\pi$-挠。Lemma~\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion}
给出 $\widehat B$ 的同一无挠性，以及全部约化同构。
所列商拓扑有限型，由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation} 为拓扑有限表示，
因此是 admissible 代数。由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations}，逆转 $\pi$
得到所列 strict affinoid 代数。对
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affineanalytificationconstruction} 取全部半径
为一，即将它连同解析结构识别为所述解析域。}

\bi{Localization preserves $O$-flatness. Apply the quotient
calculation to $B[1/b]=B[U]/(bU-1)$ to obtain the last
presentation. Equivalently, its reductions modulo every
$\pi^m$ are $(B/\pi^mB)[1/b]$, so it is the principal open
restriction of $\operatorname{Spf}\widehat B$.
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalprincipalopenflat} identifies its
generic domain by $|b|\geq1$; every $b\in\widehat B$ already
has absolute value at most one there, giving $|b|=1$.
All these maps agree on polynomial coordinates and their
inverses, hence agree on the completions by continuity.}
{局部化保持 $O$-平坦性。对
$B[1/b]=B[U]/(bU-1)$ 应用上述商计算，得到最后一个表示。
等价地，它对每个 $\pi^m$ 的约化为
$(B/\pi^mB)[1/b]$，所以就是
$\operatorname{Spf}\widehat B$ 的主开集限制。
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalprincipalopenflat}
将其泛纤维识别为 $|b|\geq1$ 域；而
$b\in\widehat B$ 的绝对值在原泛纤维上已经至多为一，
故该域为 $|b|=1$。这些映射在多项式坐标及其逆上相同，
因而由连续性在完备化上相同。}
\end{proof}
