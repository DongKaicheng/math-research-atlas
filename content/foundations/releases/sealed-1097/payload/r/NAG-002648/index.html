Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Residual identity suffices in the nondiscrete class]
\label{cand:f5res:residualidentity}
Assume (K) and the stated flatness and semilinear action on \(B'\).
If the additive quotient \(C=B'/O'[(B')^G]\) is a finitely
generated \(S=O'[(B')^G]\)-module, then the following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(G\) acts identically on the full special-fibre algebra
      \(B'/\mathfrak m'B'\);
\item the given semilinear algebra has effective descent over \(O\);
\item the multiplication map (5) is an isomorphism;
\item for every \(0\leq i<e\),
\[
  s^{-i}\{b\in B':\sigma(b)=\zeta^i b\}
                =I_i(B')^G
       \quad\hbox{inside }K\otimes_O(B')^G .
 \tag{6}
\]
\end{enumerate}
The finiteness assumption on \(C\) holds whenever \(B'\) is of
finite type over \(O'\). In particular, the equivalence applies
to every flat finitely presented nodal affine chart in (K).
For such a chart the invariant algebra \(B\) is flat, finitely
presented and nodal over \(O\), and (5) gives its effective
descent with the prescribed action.
No split-branch, diagonal-coordinate, or discrete-value-group
hypothesis is added.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The zero algebra is immediate, so assume \(B'\ne0\).
Flatness then makes \(O'\to B'\) injective.
The equivalence of conditions 2, 3 and 4, and the implication
\(2\Rightarrow1\), are the exact passing result
\dep{cand:f5tame:charactercriterion}. We prove \(1\Rightarrow3\)
under the specified finite \(S\)-module assumption.

First \(C\otimes_{O'}L=0\). Here is the generic calculation using
the same exact input, without any integral torsor identity.
The Fourier projectors give
\(B'=\bigoplus_i M_i\), with
\(M_i=\{b:\sigma(b)=\zeta^i b\}\).
The localization statement in the passing theorem gives
\[
                 s^{-i}M_i\subset B_K,\qquad
                 B_K=K\otimes_O B .
\]
For \(m\in M_i\), write \(s^{-i}m=b/d\) with
\(b\in B\), \(d\in O\setminus\{0\}\). Then
\(m=s^i b/d\) lies in \(S\otimes_{O'}L\), because
\(s^i\in O'\subset S\). Summing the character components shows
\[
                   S\otimes_{O'}L=B'\otimes_{O'}L .
\]
Localization is exact, so \(C\otimes_{O'}L=0\).

Next, residual identity gives \(C=\mathfrak m'C\).
For every \(z\in B'\), the average
\[
                  P_0(z)=\frac1e\sum_{g\in G}g(z)
\]
belongs to \(B\subset S\). Since \(z-g(z)\in\mathfrak m'B'\)
for every \(g\), we have
\(z-P_0(z)\in\mathfrak m'B'\). Consequently
\[
                  B'=S+\mathfrak m'B',
                  \qquad C=\mathfrak m'C .
\]
The ring \(S\) contains the coefficient ring \(O'\), so these
are precisely the module and scalar actions needed in
Lemma~\ref{cand:f5res:finiteverticalmodule}.
Apply that lemma with \(R=O'\), \(F=L\) to obtain \(C=0\).
Thus \(S=B'\), proving surjectivity of (5); its injectivity
is already supplied by the passing criterion. This proves
condition 3.

If \(B'\) is of finite type over \(O'\), choose a finite set of
algebra generators. Lemma~\ref{cand:f5res:finitedefect} applies
with \(R'=O'\) and gives the required finite \(S\)-module \(C\).
There is no claim that \(B'\) or \(C\) is finite over \(O'\).
For \(B'\) finitely presented and nodal, the flatness, finite
presentation and nodality of \(B\) follow from the persistence
part of \dep{cand:f5tame:charactercriterion}, now that the actual
base-change map is an isomorphism.
\end{proof}
