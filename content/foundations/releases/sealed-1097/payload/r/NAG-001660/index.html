The spectral kernel and free scalar extension

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 597

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{The spectral kernel and free scalar extension}
{谱核与自由标量扩张}]
\label{fnd:spectralnilradicalinternal}
\bi{For every generalized $K$-affinoid algebra $A$, the kernel of its
spectral seminorm $\rho_A$ is its nilradical. If $A$ is reduced, every
free-radius scalar extension $A_r$ is reduced as well.}
{对每个 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数 $A$，谱半范数
$\rho_A$ 的核恰为其幂零根。若 $A$ 约化，则每个自由半径标量扩张
$A_r$ 也约化。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For a strict affinoid domain $B$, use the notation of
Proposition~\ref{fnd:normalizationfiniteboundary}. Any maximal ideal
of $D=E\otimes_F H$ gives a field map from the field $E$ to its
residue field, hence an injection. The resulting bounded point
of $B$ has zero kernel. For a general strict $A$, apply this
observation to each quotient by a minimal prime. Thus any element
vanishing at all analytic points belongs to every minimal prime
and is nilpotent by \cite[Tag 00E0(4),(7)]{StacksAlg}.
Conversely every multiplicative seminorm kills nilpotents.}
{对 strict affinoid 整环 $B$，采用
Proposition~\ref{fnd:normalizationfiniteboundary} 的记号。
$D=E\otimes_F H$ 的任一极大理想给出从域 $E$ 到其剩余域的域
同态，因而是单射。所以对应的 $B$ 的有界谱点具有零核。
对一般 strict $A$，将此论证应用于每个极小素理想商。
于是在全部解析点处为零的元素属于每个极小素理想，由
\cite[Tag 00E0(4),(7)]{StacksAlg} 为幂零元素。
反之，乘性半范数均消去幂零元素。}

\bi{For arbitrary radii choose a free strictification $A_r$.
The Gauss section of Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusgausssection}
shows $\rho_{A_r}(a\otimes1)=\rho_A(a)$. If this value is zero,
the strict case makes $a\otimes1$ nilpotent. The coefficient
inclusion $A\hookrightarrow A_r$ is injective, so $a$ is nilpotent.
Finally, if $A$ is reduced and $z=\sum_\nu a_\nu T^\nu\in A_r$
is nilpotent, evaluation at every Gauss-section point gives
$\max_\nu|a_\nu(x)|r^\nu=0$. Each $a_\nu$ therefore has spectral
seminorm zero and is zero by the result just proved. Thus $z=0$.}
{对任意半径，选择自由 strictification $A_r$。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusgausssection} 的 Gauss 截面给出
$\rho_{A_r}(a\otimes1)=\rho_A(a)$。若此值为零，strict 情形说明
$a\otimes1$ 幂零；系数包含 $A\hookrightarrow A_r$ 为单射，
故 $a$ 幂零。最后，设 $A$ 约化且
$z=\sum_\nu a_\nu T^\nu\in A_r$ 幂零。在每个 Gauss 截面点求值，
得到 $\max_\nu|a_\nu(x)|r^\nu=0$。每个 $a_\nu$ 的谱半范数均为
零，由刚证结果，$a_\nu=0$。于是 $z=0$。}
\end{proof}
