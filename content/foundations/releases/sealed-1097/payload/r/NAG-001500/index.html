Approximation by fractions with one denominator

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 212

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Approximation by fractions with one denominator}{以同一分母的分式作逼近}]\label{fnd:rationalfractiondensity}
\bi{Let $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$ generate the unit ideal of $A$, and set $B=A\langle f/g\rangle$. The element $g$ is a unit in $B$, and the image of $A[g^{-1}]$ is dense in $B$. More explicitly, given $v_0,\ldots,v_t\in B$ and $\eta>0$, there exist $N\geq0$ and $p_0,\ldots,p_t\in A$ such that}
{设 $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$ 生成 $A$ 的单位理想，并令 $B=A\langle f/g\rangle$。则 $g$ 在 $B$ 中为单位，且 $A[g^{-1}]$ 的像在 $B$ 中稠密。具体而言，给定 $v_0,\ldots,v_t\in B$ 及 $\eta>0$，存在 $N\geq0$ 和 $p_0,\ldots,p_t\in A$ 使}
\[
 \|v_j-g^{-N}p_j\|_B<\eta\qquad(0\leq j\leq t).
\]
\bi{If $K$ is trivially valued and $A$ carries a defining strict quotient norm, all these approximations may be chosen to be equalities. In that case every nonzero element of $A$ or $B$ has norm one, and $B$ is the ordinary localization $A[g^{-1}]$ with that discrete norm.}
{若 $K$ 赋值平凡，且 $A$ 配备定义 strict 商范数，则上述逼近可全部取为等式。此时 $A$ 与 $B$ 中的每个非零元素范数均为一，且 $B$ 就是配备该离散范数的普通局部化 $A[g^{-1}]$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose $c_0g+\sum_i c_if_i=1$. In $B$, with $x_i=\overline X_i$, one has $g^{-1}=c_0+\sum_i c_ix_i$. Polynomial images are dense in $B$ by Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness} and the quotient construction of Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictweightedquotient}. For a polynomial $P(X)=\sum_\alpha e_\alpha X^\alpha$ and an integer $N$ at least its total degree, put}
{选择 $c_0g+\sum_i c_if_i=1$。在 $B$ 中记 $x_i=\overline X_i$，则 $g^{-1}=c_0+\sum_i c_ix_i$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness} 及 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictweightedquotient} 的商构造，多项式的像在 $B$ 中稠密。对多项式 $P(X)=\sum_\alpha e_\alpha X^\alpha$，以及不小于其总次数的整数 $N$，令}
\[
 p_P=\sum_\alpha e_\alpha f^\alpha g^{N-|\alpha|}\in A.
\]
\bi{The defining relations give $g^NP(x)=p_P$ in the actual quotient. Choose polynomial approximations to the finitely many $v_j$, and then choose one $N$ at least all their degrees. This proves the claimed simultaneous approximation. No injectivity of $A\to B$ or of $A[g^{-1}]\to B$ has been assumed.}
{定义关系在实际商中给出 $g^NP(x)=p_P$。对有限多个 $v_j$ 选取多项式逼近，再取一个不小于这些多项式总次数的 $N$，即得所述同时逼近。这里没有假定 $A\to B$ 或 $A[g^{-1}]\to B$ 单射。}

\bi{For trivial valuation, a strict Tate algebra over $K$ is a polynomial ring: a coefficient family tending to zero has finite support. Its norm is one on nonzero elements. A defining quotient norm on $A$ therefore has the same property. Consequently $A\{X\}=A[X]$, and every class of $B$ already has a polynomial representative. The quotient norm of a nonzero class is one. The unit equation for $g$ and the maps $X_i\mapsto f_i/g$ identify this polynomial quotient with $A[g^{-1}]$. The assertions for a zero quotient have their stated degenerate meaning.}
{赋值平凡时，$K$ 上的 strict Tate 代数为多项式环，因为趋于零的系数族只能有有限支撑；其每个非零元素范数为一。因此 $A$ 的定义商范数也有这一性质。于是 $A\{X\}=A[X]$，$B$ 中每个类已经具有多项式代表元，非零类的商范数为一。利用 $g$ 的单位等式及 $X_i\mapsto f_i/g$，便将该多项式商与 $A[g^{-1}]$ 识别。零商按上述退化约定解释。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Throughout, $K$ is a complete real-valued non-Archimedean field; trivial valuation is allowed. Nonzero affinoid algebras are strict and norm-admissible, with submultiplicative norm and unit of norm one. Quotients use their actual quotient norms. The zero algebra and its zero modules have the separate zero-norm interpretation of Section~\ref{fnd:r1quotientbridge}. A rational presentation has a unit-ideal tuple. We use unit radii in the lemmas; radii in $|K^\times|$ are reduced to this case by scaling variables.}
{全文固定完备实值非阿基米德域 $K$，允许平凡赋值。非零 affinoid 代数均为 strict 且 norm-admissible，范数次乘，单位元范数为一。商配备实际商范数。零代数及其零模按 Section~\ref{fnd:r1quotientbridge} 的约定单独使用零范数。Rational 表示的定义 tuple 必须生成单位理想。各引理使用单位半径；属于 $|K^\times|$ 的半径通过缩放变量化为这一情形。}
