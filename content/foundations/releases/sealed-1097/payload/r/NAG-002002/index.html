\begin{theorem}[\bi{Product patches after a finite \'etale base change}{有限 \'etale 基变换后的乘积开片}]
\label{fnd:nontrivialresidueproductpatch}
\bi{Let $Y\to X$ be smooth of pure relative dimension $n$ between good spaces, let $x\in X$, and suppose $\mathcal H(x)$ is nontrivially valued. For every nonempty ordinary open subset $W\subset Y_x$, there exist a finite \'etale morphism $X'\to U$ over an ordinary neighborhood $U$ of $x$, its unique point $x'$ above $x$, and a nonempty ordinary open subset $V\subset Y\times_X X'$ such that $V_{x'}$ maps into $W$ and}
{设 $Y\to X$ 是 good 空间之间纯相对维数为 $n$ 的光滑态射，$x\in X$，并假设 $\mathcal H(x)$ 的赋值非平凡。对每个非空普通开集 $W\subset Y_x$，存在 $x$ 的某普通邻域 $U$ 上的有限 \'etale 态射 $X'\to U$、其在 $x$ 上方的唯一点 $x'$，以及非空普通开集 $V\subset Y\times_X X'$，使 $V_{x'}$ 映入 $W$，且}
\[
                         V\simeq U'\times_K D
\]
\bi{over $X'$, for an ordinary neighborhood $U'$ of $x'$ and an $n$-dimensional open polydisc $D$.}
{此同构在 $X'$ 上成立，其中 $U'$ 是 $x'$ 的普通邻域，$D$ 是 $n$ 维开多圆盘。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The preceding lemma gives $y\in W$ with $E=\mathcal H(y)$ finite separable over $F=\mathcal H(x)$. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:goodetalegermresidueequivalence}, realize $E/F$ by a finite \'etale morphism $X'\to U$ with one point $x'$ above $x$ and $\mathcal H(x')=E$. In the fibre product the evaluation at $y$ gives an $E$-rational point $y'$ over $x'$. The ordinary topology of an analytic fibre is the subspace topology by Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres} and its gluing form. Hence one can choose an ordinary neighborhood of $y'$ whose intersection with the fibre maps into $W$. Restrict to that neighborhood and apply Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalfibresmoothproductpatch}. Its product patch has precisely the required properties.}
{上一引理给出 $y\in W$，使 $E=\mathcal H(y)$ 在 $F=\mathcal H(x)$ 上有限可分。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:goodetalegermresidueequivalence}，可用有限 \'etale 态射 $X'\to U$ 实现 $E/F$，在 $x$ 上方只有一个点 $x'$，且 $\mathcal H(x')=E$。纤维积中，在 $y$ 处求值给出 $x'$ 上方的 $E$-有理点 $y'$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres} 及其粘合形式，解析纤维的普通拓扑等于子空间拓扑。因此可取 $y'$ 的普通邻域，使其与纤维的交映入 $W$。限制到此邻域，再应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalfibresmoothproductpatch}，所得乘积开片恰有全部所需性质。}
\end{proof}
