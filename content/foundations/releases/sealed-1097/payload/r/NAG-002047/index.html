The semilinear action on a stable model

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 658

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{The semilinear action on a stable model}{稳定模型上的半线性作用}]
\label{cand:stablegaloisaction}
\bi{Let $K$ be a complete non-Archimedean field, let $L/K$ be finite Galois with group $G$, and give $L$ its unique extending absolute value. Let $C/K$ be a smooth projective geometrically connected curve of genus $g\geq2$, and let $\mathcal C/L^\circ$ be a stable model of $C_L$ with its generic-fibre identification. The canonical semilinear $G$-action on $C_L$ extends uniquely to a semilinear $G$-action on $\mathcal C$. More explicitly, with $S=\operatorname{Spec}L^\circ$ and $b_\sigma=\operatorname{Spec}(\sigma^{-1}|_{L^\circ})$, there are unique automorphisms $\rho_\sigma:\mathcal C\to\mathcal C$ satisfying}
{设 $K$ 为完备非阿基米德域，$L/K$ 为 Galois 群为 $G$ 的有限 Galois 扩张，且 $L$ 配备唯一的延拓绝对值。设 $C/K$ 为亏格 $g\geq2$ 的光滑射影几何连通曲线，$\mathcal C/L^\circ$ 为 $C_L$ 的稳定模型，并指定其泛纤维同构。$C_L$ 上典范的半线性 $G$-作用唯一延拓为 $\mathcal C$ 上的半线性 $G$-作用。具体地，记 $S=\operatorname{Spec}L^\circ$、$b_\sigma=\operatorname{Spec}(\sigma^{-1}|_{L^\circ})$，则存在唯一的自同构 $\rho_\sigma:\mathcal C\to\mathcal C$，满足}
\[
 f\circ\rho_\sigma=b_\sigma\circ f,
 \qquad \rho_1=\operatorname{id},
 \qquad \rho_{\sigma\tau}=\rho_\sigma\circ\rho_\tau,
\]
\bi{and inducing the canonical action on the generic fibre.}
{且在泛纤维上诱导典范作用。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Uniqueness in Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} gives $|\sigma(a)|=|a|$ for every $a\in L$ and $\sigma\in G$, so $G$ preserves $L^\circ$. Our inverse convention gives $b_\sigma b_\tau=b_{\sigma\tau}$. The scheme $C_L=C\times_K\operatorname{Spec}L$ has the corresponding canonical left action induced by $\operatorname{id}_C\times\operatorname{Spec}(\sigma^{-1})$.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 的唯一性，对每个 $a\in L$ 及 $\sigma\in G$ 均有 $|\sigma(a)|=|a|$，所以 $G$ 保持 $L^\circ$。所采用的逆元约定给出 $b_\sigma b_\tau=b_{\sigma\tau}$。概形 $C_L=C\times_K\operatorname{Spec}L$ 具有由 $\operatorname{id}_C\times\operatorname{Spec}(\sigma^{-1})$ 诱导的对应典范左作用。}

\bi{For a fixed $\sigma$, form $b_\sigma^*\mathcal C=\mathcal C\times_{S,b_\sigma}S$, with its structure map given by the second projection. Both this twist and $\mathcal C$ are stable families over $S$. The generic action gives an $L$-isomorphism $\mathcal C_L\to(b_\sigma^*\mathcal C)_L$: its two components are the generic semilinear automorphism and the structure map to $\operatorname{Spec}L$. Lemma~\ref{cand:stableisomvaluation} extends it uniquely to an $S$-isomorphism $a_\sigma:\mathcal C\to b_\sigma^*\mathcal C$. Composing with the first projection defines $\rho_\sigma$. Since $b_\sigma$ is an automorphism, that projection is an isomorphism of schemes, and $\rho_\sigma$ is an automorphism covering $b_\sigma$.}
{固定 $\sigma$，构造 $b_\sigma^*\mathcal C=\mathcal C\times_{S,b_\sigma}S$，以第二投影为其结构态射。此扭曲族与 $\mathcal C$ 都是 $S$ 上的稳定族。泛纤维作用给出 $L$-同构 $\mathcal C_L\to(b_\sigma^*\mathcal C)_L$：其两个分量分别是泛纤维上的半线性自同构与到 $\operatorname{Spec}L$ 的结构态射。Lemma~\ref{cand:stableisomvaluation} 将它唯一延拓为 $S$-同构 $a_\sigma:\mathcal C\to b_\sigma^*\mathcal C$。再与第一投影复合，定义 $\rho_\sigma$。由于 $b_\sigma$ 为自同构，该投影是概形同构，因此 $\rho_\sigma$ 为覆盖 $b_\sigma$ 的自同构。}

\bi{The maps $\rho_\sigma\rho_\tau$ and $\rho_{\sigma\tau}$ cover the same base map and agree on the generic fibre. Reinterpreting them as $S$-isomorphisms $\mathcal C\to b_{\sigma\tau}^*\mathcal C$, uniqueness in Lemma~\ref{cand:stableisomvaluation} makes them equal. The same argument gives $\rho_1=\operatorname{id}$ and proves uniqueness of the action.}
{映射 $\rho_\sigma\rho_\tau$ 与 $\rho_{\sigma\tau}$ 覆盖相同的基底映射，且在泛纤维上相同。将它们视为 $S$-同构 $\mathcal C\to b_{\sigma\tau}^*\mathcal C$，由 Lemma~\ref{cand:stableisomvaluation} 的唯一性即知二者相等。同一论证给出 $\rho_1=\operatorname{id}$，并证明该作用的唯一性。}
\end{proof}
