Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The strict-time analytic lift}
{严格小于终点时刻的解析提升}]
\label{cand:etalestricttimehomotopy}
\bi{For $x\in\mathcal M(B)$ and $0\leq t<1$, the formula}
{对于 $x\in\mathcal M(B)$ 及 $0\leq t<1$，公式}
\[
 |b(H_B(x,t))|
       =\max_\nu |D_\nu(b)(x)|t^{|\nu|},
 \qquad 0^0=1,
 \tag{E}\label{eq:candetaledisplacementvalue}
\]
\bi{defines a jointly continuous map
$H_B:\mathcal M(B)\times[0,1)\to\mathcal M(B)$ with
$H_B(x,0)=x$. For the structure map $f:\mathcal M(B)\to V$,}
{定义联合连续映射
$H_B:\mathcal M(B)\times[0,1)\to\mathcal M(B)$，满足
$H_B(x,0)=x$。对结构映射 $f:\mathcal M(B)\to V$，有}
\[
 f(H_B(x,t))=H(f(x),t).
\]
\bi{If $b$ has quotient norm at most one, then
$|b(H_B(x,t))|<1$ if and only if $|b(x)|<1$. Thus every
strict-time trajectory preserves its reduction prime in the
displayed integral model.}
{若 $b$ 的商范数至多为一，则
$|b(H_B(x,t))|<1$ 当且仅当 $|b(x)|<1$。
因而每条严格小于终点时刻的轨迹，都保持所列整模型
中的约化素理想。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For $t>0$, evaluate the coefficients of
$\sigma_\delta(b)$ in $\mathcal H(x)$ with $t<\delta<1$
and then use the multiplicative radius-$t$ Gauss norm.
This gives a bounded homomorphism into a complete Gauss
field and hence an actual analytic point. Its norm is
\eqref{eq:candetaledisplacementvalue}. At $t=0$ the constant
coefficient gives $x$. The base-coordinate formulas identify
the composite with $f$.}
{当 $t>0$ 时，选 $t<\delta<1$，将
$\sigma_\delta(b)$ 的系数在 $\mathcal H(x)$ 中取值，
再使用乘性的半径 $t$ Gauss 范数。这给出到完备 Gauss
域的有界同态，因而给出实际解析点；其范数就是
\eqref{eq:candetaledisplacementvalue}。当 $t=0$ 时，
常数系数给出 $x$。基底坐标公式识别与 $f$ 的复合。}

\bi{On $\mathcal M(B)\times[0,\delta]$, choose
$\delta<\delta'<1$. The restricted series
$\sigma_{\delta'}(b)$ has uniformly small tails on this
product. Each finite truncation of
\eqref{eq:candetaledisplacementvalue} is a finite maximum
of continuous functions, so its uniform limit is continuous.
These intervals cover $[0,1)$ and prove joint continuity.
For a norm-at-most-one element $b$, its constant coefficient
is $b(x)$ and its nonconstant terms have norms at most $t$.
Thus a constant coefficient of norm one stays of norm one;
if it is smaller than one, every term is bounded by
$\max(|b(x)|,t)<1$. This proves the reduction assertion.}
{在 $\mathcal M(B)\times[0,\delta]$ 上，选取
$\delta<\delta'<1$。受限级数 $\sigma_{\delta'}(b)$
在此直积上有一致趋于零的尾项。
\eqref{eq:candetaledisplacementvalue} 的每个有限截断
都是有限个连续函数的最大值，其一致极限因而连续。
这些区间覆盖 $[0,1)$，证明联合连续性。对于范数至多
为一的 $b$，常数系数为 $b(x)$，全部非常数项的范数
至多为 $t$。所以范数为一的常数系数保持范数一；若
它小于一，则全部项均被 $\max(|b(x)|,t)<1$ 控制。
这证明约化断言。}
\end{proof}

\bi{Retain the standard algebra $A$ and its integral coordinates
from Lemma~\ref{cand:mixedgaussdisplacement}. Let
$F=(F_1,\ldots,F_m)$ be represented by restricted series in
$X_0,\ldots,X_r,T,Y_1,\ldots,Y_m$ with coefficients in
$K^\circ$. Write $J=\partial F/\partial Y$ and consider the
nonzero affinoid algebra}
{保留 Lemma~\ref{cand:mixedgaussdisplacement} 中的标准代数
$A$ 及其整坐标。设 $F=(F_1,\ldots,F_m)$ 由变量
$X_0,\ldots,X_r,T,Y_1,\ldots,Y_m$ 的受限级数表示，
系数属于 $K^\circ$。写 $J=\partial F/\partial Y$，并考虑
非零 affinoid 代数}
\[
 B=A\langle Y_1,\ldots,Y_m,Q\rangle/
                  (F_1,\ldots,F_m,Q\det J-1).
 \tag{J}\label{eq:candetalejacobianpresentation}
\]
\bi{We use its displayed quotient norm. In particular the
coordinate images and $J^{-1}=Q\operatorname{adj}(J)$ have
norm at most one. All the statements below keep this
presentation over the original valued field.}
{使用所列表示的商范数。特别地，坐标像及
$J^{-1}=Q\operatorname{adj}(J)$ 的范数至多为一。下述
全部陈述保留原赋值域上的这个表示。}
