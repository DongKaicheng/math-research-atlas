Finite modules on an affinoid product

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 623

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Finite modules on an affinoid product}{Affinoid 乘积上的有限模}]
\label{cand:groundfieldcoherenttensor}
\bi{Let $B,C$ be generalized $K$-affinoid algebras, let $M$ be a finite canonical $B$-module, and put $D=B\widehat\otimes_K C$. There is a natural bounded $D$-linear isomorphism with bounded inverse}
{设 $B,C$ 为 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数，$M$ 为有限典范 $B$-模，并令 $D=B\widehat\otimes_K C$。存在自然的有界 $D$-线性同构，且逆有界：}
\[
 D\otimes_B M\ \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\ M\widehat\otimes_K C,
 \qquad (b\otimes c)\otimes m\longmapsto bm\otimes c.
\]
\bi{The left side has its canonical finite-$D$-module norm. This module is flat over $C$.}
{左端采用其典范有限 $D$-模范数。该模在 $C$ 上平坦。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout} makes $D$ a generalized affinoid algebra. Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonstrictexact} to a finite $B$-module presentation of $M$, split into its two norm-strict kernel sequences as in the preceding proof. It identifies $M\widehat\otimes_K C$ with the quotient of $D^b$ by the image of the same presentation matrix on $D^a$. This is $D\otimes_B M$ as an algebraic module. The quotient is norm-strict, so its norm is equivalent to the canonical finite-$D$-module norm by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}. The formula on pure tensors identifies the $D$-actions and gives naturality. Lemma~\ref{cand:groundfieldbanachtensorflat}, applied to the Banach $K$-space $M$, gives $C$-flatness.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout}，$D$ 为 generalized affinoid 代数。对 $M$ 的有限 $B$-模表示使用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonstrictexact}，并像前一证明一样将其拆成两个 norm-strict 核序列。这把 $M\widehat\otimes_K C$ 识别为 $D^b$ 对同一表示矩阵在 $D^a$ 上的像所取的商。作为代数模，这正是 $D\otimes_B M$。该商映射为 norm-strict，所以由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}，其范数与典范有限 $D$-模范数等价。纯张量公式识别两端的 $D$-作用并给出自然性。对 Banach $K$-空间 $M$ 应用 Lemma~\ref{cand:groundfieldbanachtensorflat}，便得 $C$-平坦性。}
\end{proof}
