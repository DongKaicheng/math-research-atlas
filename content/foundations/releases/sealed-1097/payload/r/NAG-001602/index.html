Uniformization after a finite extension of constants

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 614

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Uniformization after a finite extension of constants}
{有限常数扩张后的一致化}]
\label{fnd:laurentconstantuniformization}
\bi{Let $F$ be any field, endowed with the trivial absolute
value, let $0<s<1$, and let $L/F_s=F((t))$ be finite
separable. There are a finite extension $K/F$, a number
$0<r<1$, and a surjective $F$-algebra homomorphism}
{设 $F$ 为任意域，赋予平凡绝对值；令 $0<s<1$，
并设 $L/F_s=F((t))$ 为有限可分扩张。
则存在有限扩张 $K/F$、数 $0<r<1$ 及满的 $F$-代数同态}
\[
                     L\otimes_F K\twoheadrightarrow K_r
\]
\bi{whose restriction to $L$ is isometric for its given
absolute value. The restriction to $K$ is the constant-field
embedding. Here the tensor product is the ordinary algebraic
tensor product.}
{其在 $L$ 上的限制保持给定绝对值，并在 $K$ 上限制为
常数域嵌入。这里的张量积是普通代数张量积。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{\emph{Step 1: algebraize the discrete valuation.}
The dense-coefficient lemma
\ref{fnd:separablecoefficientapproximation} supplies
$Q\in F(t)[U]$ such that
$L=F((t))[U]/(Q)$. Let $E=F(t)[U]/(Q)$, identified
with its image in $L$. It is a field, since $Q$ is
irreducible even over $F((t))$. Moreover $E$ is dense
in $L$: approximate the finitely many coefficients of
an expression in the basis $1,U,\ldots,U^{\deg Q-1}$.}
{\emph{第一步：代数化离散赋值。}
稠密系数引理 \ref{fnd:separablecoefficientapproximation}
给出 $Q\in F(t)[U]$，使
$L=F((t))[U]/(Q)$。令 $E=F(t)[U]/(Q)$，
并将其识别为 $L$ 中的像。因为 $Q$ 甚至在 $F((t))$
上不可约，$E$ 是域。并且 $E$ 在 $L$ 中稠密：
只须对基 $1,U,\ldots,U^{\deg Q-1}$ 下的有限个系数作逼近。}

\bi{Let $B$ be the integral closure of $F[t]$ in $E$.
It is finite over $F[t]$, by
\cite[Tag 0335]{StacksAlg} and the definition of a
Japanese domain, \cite[Tag 032F]{StacksAlg}.
Thus $Y=\operatorname{Spec}(B)$ is a
normal affine curve; its dimension is one by
\cite[Tag 00OK]{StacksAlg}. Every $b\in B$ has $|b|\le1$,
since it satisfies a monic equation whose coefficients
have norm at most one. Hence
$P=\{b\in B:|b|<1\}$ is a prime over $(t)$ and is
maximal, with residue field finite over $F$.
By \cite[Tag 0B8Y]{StacksAlg}, $B_P$ is a discrete
valuation ring. Its units have norm one and its
uniformizer has norm less than one. Consequently its
valuation, scaled by the prescribed value $|t|=s$,
is exactly the restriction of the given absolute value
on $L$. In particular $L$ is the completion of $E$
at this closed point $P$.}
{令 $B$ 为 $F[t]$ 在 $E$ 中的整闭包。
由 \cite[Tag 0335]{StacksAlg} 及
\cite[Tag 032F]{StacksAlg} 中 Japanese 整环的定义，
$B$ 在 $F[t]$ 上有限。因此
$Y=\operatorname{Spec}(B)$ 为正规仿射曲线；
由 \cite[Tag 00OK]{StacksAlg}，其维数为一。
每个 $b\in B$ 都满足 $|b|\le1$，因为它满足一个系数范数
至多为一的首一方程。因此
$P=\{b\in B:|b|<1\}$ 是 $(t)$ 上方的素理想，
并为极大理想，其剩余域在 $F$ 上有限。
由 \cite[Tag 0B8Y]{StacksAlg}，$B_P$ 是离散赋值环。
其单位范数为一，一致化元范数小于一。所以用给定值
$|t|=s$ 确定尺度后，其赋值恰为 $L$ 上给定绝对值的限制。
特别地，$L$ 是 $E$ 在闭点 $P$ 处的完备化。}

\bi{\emph{Step 2: obtain a smooth rational center.}
Apply \cite[Tag 0GK5]{StacksAlg} to $Y$.
After a finite purely inseparable extension $F'/F$,
the normalization of $Y_{F'}$ is a finite disjoint union
of curves smooth and geometrically irreducible over
finite extensions of $F'$. The normalization is finite
and surjective, by \cite[Tag 0BXR]{StacksAlg}; choose
a closed point $Q_0$ above $P$ on one component $Z$.
Write $F_0$ for its field of definition in that statement
and put $K=\kappa(Q_0)$, which is finite over $F_0$ by
\cite[Tag 00FV]{StacksAlg}, and hence finite over $F$.
The base change $Z_K$ is a smooth integral
$K$-curve, and $Q_0$ gives a $K$-rational point $Q$ on it.}
{\emph{第二步：获得光滑有理中心。}
把 \cite[Tag 0GK5]{StacksAlg} 应用于 $Y$。
经过某个有限纯不可分扩张 $F'/F$ 后，$Y_{F'}$ 的
正规化是有限条曲线的不交并，各曲线在 $F'$ 的某个有限
扩张上光滑且几何不可约。由 \cite[Tag 0BXR]{StacksAlg}，
此正规化有限且满射；在某分支 $Z$ 上选择位于 $P$ 上方的
闭点 $Q_0$。将该分支在上述定理中的定义域记为 $F_0$，
并令 $K=\kappa(Q_0)$，由 \cite[Tag 00FV]{StacksAlg}，
它在 $F_0$ 上有限，因而在 $F$ 上有限。
基变换 $Z_K$ 为光滑整 $K$-曲线，且 $Q_0$ 在其中给出
一个 $K$-有理点 $Q$。}

\bi{The function field $E'$ of $Z_K$ is generated as a field
by the images of $E$ and $K$. Indeed normalization does
not change the function field of the chosen irreducible
component of $Y_{F'}$; this field is a quotient of
$E\otimes_F F'$. After the indicated base change its
function field is the corresponding quotient of
$E\otimes_F K$. Since this tensor product is finite
over $E$, its domain quotient is already a field.
Thus $E'$ is precisely the compositum of those two images.}
{曲线 $Z_K$ 的函数域 $E'$ 由 $E$ 与 $K$ 的像作为域生成。
事实上，正规化不改变 $Y_{F'}$ 所选不可约分支的函数域，
后者是 $E\otimes_F F'$ 的商。在所列基变换后，其函数域
是 $E\otimes_F K$ 的相应商。由于此张量积在 $E$ 上有限，
其整商已是域。因此 $E'$ 恰为这两个像的合成域。}

\bi{\emph{Step 3: complete and check algebraic surjectivity.}
The local ring at the smooth rational point $Q$ is a
discrete valuation ring by \cite[Tag 0B8Y]{StacksAlg},
with residue field $K$. Choose a uniformizer $\tau$ and
normalize its real absolute value so that the image of
$t$ still has value $s$. Let $M$ be the completion
of $E'$ for this valuation. Completion changes neither
the value group nor the residue field, as proved in
Lemma~\ref{fnd:valuedfielddimensioncalculus}. Therefore
Lemma~\ref{fnd:discretecoefficientlaurent} identifies
$M$ with $K_r$, where $r=|\tau|$.}
{\emph{第三步：完备化并检查代数满射。}
由 \cite[Tag 0B8Y]{StacksAlg}，光滑有理点 $Q$ 的局部环
是剩余域为 $K$ 的离散赋值环。选择一致化元 $\tau$，
并正规化实绝对值，使 $t$ 的像仍具有值 $s$。
令 $M$ 为 $E'$ 关于此赋值的完备化。
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:valuedfielddimensioncalculus}，
完备化不改变值群或剩余域。所以
Lemma~\ref{fnd:discretecoefficientlaurent}
将 $M$ 识别为 $K_r$，其中 $r=|\tau|$。}

\bi{The valuation on $E'$ restricts to the one centered
at $P$ on $E$: the local map $B_P\to\mathcal O_{Z_K,Q}$
sends units to units and the maximal ideal into the maximal
ideal, and the normalization of $|t|$ fixes the scale.
It consequently extends to an isometric embedding
$L\hookrightarrow M$. Together with the constant inclusion
$K\hookrightarrow M$ it gives
$L\otimes_F K\to M$. Its image is a finite-dimensional
$L$-algebra and a domain, hence a field: multiplication
by a nonzero element is an injective endomorphism of a
finite-dimensional vector space and is therefore surjective.
By Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}, this image is
complete for its restricted absolute value and hence closed
in $M$. It contains $E'$, which is dense in $M$.
The image is therefore all of $M=K_r$, proving the assertion
for the algebraic tensor product itself.}
{$E'$ 上的赋值限制到 $E$ 后，等于以 $P$ 为中心的赋值：
局部映射 $B_P\to\mathcal O_{Z_K,Q}$ 将单位送到单位、
极大理想送入极大理想，而 $|t|$ 的正规化确定了尺度。
因此它延拓为等距嵌入 $L\hookrightarrow M$。
将其与常数嵌入 $K\hookrightarrow M$ 结合，
得到 $L\otimes_F K\to M$。其像是有限维 $L$-代数且为整环，
所以是域：非零元素的乘法是有限维向量空间上的单射自同态，
因而满射。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}，
此像对于限制绝对值完备，故在 $M$ 中闭。
但它又包含在 $M$ 中稠密的 $E'$，
所以其像为整个 $M=K_r$。
这就对普通代数张量积本身证明了满射断言。}
\end{proof}
