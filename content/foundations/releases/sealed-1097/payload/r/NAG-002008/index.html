\begin{proposition}[\bi{Finite \'etale maps are quasi-smooth}{有限 \'etale 态射准光滑}]
\label{fnd:finiteetalequasismooth}
\bi{A norm-admissible finite flat affinoid morphism with zero relative differential module is analytically flat and has geometrically regular analytic fibres. Consequently it is quasi-smooth, and these assertions hold after every complete-field extension and analytic base change. The same conclusions hold locally for \'etale analytic morphisms.}
{若 norm-admissible 的有限平坦 affinoid 态射的相对微分模为零，则它解析平坦，且解析纤维几何正则。因此它准光滑；这些结论在任意完备扩域及解析基变换后均成立。同样结论局部适用于 \'etale 解析态射。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Finite-module base change preserves finite flatness. On ordinary local rings, the finite-branch product decomposition makes each source stalk a direct summand of the finite flat algebra tensored with the base stalk. These stalks are therefore flat. This reasoning applies after every analytic base change, proving analytic flatness. Algebraic and bounded analytic differentials agree for finite algebras, by the finite-module argument in Proposition~\ref{fnd:localfiniteetalecriterion}; their vanishing is preserved by base change.}
{有限模基变换保持有限平坦性。对普通局部环，有限分支乘积分解将每个源茎写成有限平坦代数与底空间茎张量后的直和因子，故这些茎均平坦。同一论证适用于任意解析基变换，因此得到解析平坦性。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:localfiniteetalecriterion} 中的有限模论证，有限代数的代数微分与有界解析微分一致，其消失在基变换下保持。}

\bi{Consider an analytic fibre and any complete extension $E$ of its ground field. Its algebra $D$ is finite-dimensional over $E$ and satisfies $\Omega^1_{D/E}=0$. Fix a point $v$ and put $L=\mathcal H(v)$, a finite complete extension of $E$. Lift $v$ to an $L$-rational point $u$ of $\mathcal M(D\otimes_E L)$. The local ring $S$ at $u$ is a finite-dimensional local $L$-algebra with residue field $L$, by the finite-branch stalk product. Its ordinary algebraic differential module over $L$ is zero. If $\mathfrak n$ is its maximal ideal, the augmentation $S\to L$ identifies}
{考虑一个解析纤维，以及其基域的任意完备扩域 $E$。纤维代数 $D$ 在 $E$ 上有限维，且满足 $\Omega^1_{D/E}=0$。固定一点 $v$，令 $L=\mathcal H(v)$；它为 $E$ 的有限完备扩张。将 $v$ 提升为 $\mathcal M(D\otimes_E L)$ 的 $L$-有理点 $u$。由有限分支的茎乘积分解，$u$ 处局部环 $S$ 是有限维局部 $L$-代数，其剩余域为 $L$，且其在 $L$ 上的普通代数微分模为零。若以 $\mathfrak n$ 记其极大理想，则增广映射 $S\to L$ 给出}
\[
 \mathfrak n/\mathfrak n^2
       \simeq\Omega^1_{S/L}\otimes_S L=0.
\]
\bi{For completeness, the isomorphism sends the class of $b$ to $db$ for $b\in\mathfrak n$. Its inverse is induced by the derivation $s\mapsto s-\overline s$ modulo $\mathfrak n^2$, with $\overline s\in L$ the residue; the product rule is immediate modulo $\mathfrak n^2$. The finite module $\mathfrak n$ satisfies $\mathfrak n=\mathfrak n^2$, so the determinant proof of Nakayama gives $\mathfrak n=0$. Hence $S=L$ is a field. The scalar-stalk map from $\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(D),v}$ to $S$ is faithfully flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}. As in the previous lemma, its source is a field as well, and is therefore regular. Since $E$ and $v$ were arbitrary, the fibres are geometrically regular. Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion} proves quasi-smoothness. Ordinary-local restriction gives the final assertion.}
{此同构将 $b\in\mathfrak n$ 的类送到 $db$；其逆由模 $\mathfrak n^2$ 的导子 $s\mapsto s-\overline s$ 给出，其中 $\overline s\in L$ 为剩余类，乘积法则模 $\mathfrak n^2$ 立即成立。有限模 $\mathfrak n$ 满足 $\mathfrak n=\mathfrak n^2$，故 Nakayama 引理的行列式证明给出 $\mathfrak n=0$。因此 $S=L$ 为域。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}，从 $\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(D),v}$ 到 $S$ 的基域茎映射忠实平坦。与前一个引理相同，其源也为域，故为正则环。由于 $E,v$ 任意，纤维几何正则。Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion} 遂证明准光滑性。最后一项由普通局部限制得到。}
\end{proof}
