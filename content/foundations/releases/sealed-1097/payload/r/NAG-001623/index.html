\begin{theorem}[\bi{Banach sections on compact analytic domains}{紧解析域上的 Banach 截面}]
\label{fnd:compactdomainbanachsections}
\bi{On compact analytic domains, $\mathcal O_{X_G}$ is a sheaf of Banach $K$-algebras with bounded restrictions for finite domain coverings. For a finite affinoid covering $V=\bigcup_i V_i$, its Banach algebra of sections is}
{在紧解析域上，$\mathcal O_{X_G}$ 对有限域覆盖构成 Banach $K$-代数层，限制映射有界。对有限 affinoid 覆盖 $V=\bigcup_i V_i$，其 Banach 截面代数为}
\[
 \mathcal O_{X_G}(V)=
 \ker\left(\prod_i A_{V_i}\rightrightarrows
                         \prod_{i,j}A_{V_i\cap V_j}\right),
\]
\bi{with the induced maximum norm. Different finite affinoid covers and admissible norms give canonical bounded isomorphisms and uniformly equivalent section norms.}
{并配备由最大值范数诱导的范数。不同有限 affinoid 覆盖及 admissible 范数给出典范有界同构，并使截面范数一致等价。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidanalyticdomaincalculus} supplies a finite affinoid cover. The preceding theorem identifies the section ring with the displayed matching kernel. The difference of the two restriction maps is bounded, so its kernel is closed in a finite product of Banach algebras and is complete. Multiplication is componentwise and is continuous.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidanalyticdomaincalculus}，存在有限 affinoid 覆盖。上一条定理将截面环识别为所列匹配核。两个限制映射之差有界，所以其核在 Banach 代数的有限乘积中闭，从而完备。乘法逐分量定义，并且连续。}

\bi{For two covers $(V_i)$ and $(W_j)$, their intersections give a common finite affinoid cover. Restriction from the first matching kernel to this refinement is bounded. On each $V_i$, Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidcovercech} applied to $(V_i\cap W_j)_j$ identifies $A_{V_i}$ with its matching kernel by a bounded inverse. Taking the maximum of the finitely many inverse bounds shows that the map between the global matching kernels has bounded inverse. The same argument for $(W_j)$ proves uniform equivalence and cover independence. Changing finitely many initial norms to equivalent ones does not affect this conclusion.}
{对两个覆盖 $(V_i),(W_j)$，其交叠构成共同的有限 affinoid 覆盖。从第一个匹配核到这一加细的限制有界。在每个 $V_i$ 上，对覆盖 $(V_i\cap W_j)_j$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidcovercech}，便将 $A_{V_i}$ 与其匹配核识别，并具有有界逆。取有限多个逆映射界的最大值，说明整体匹配核间的映射也有有界逆。对 $(W_j)$ 使用相同论证，得到一致等价性及与覆盖无关。将有限个初始范数换成等价范数，不改变此结论。}

\bi{For compact domains $W\subseteq V$, choose finite affinoid covers of both. Restrict a matching family on $V$ to all pairwise intersections of these covers, and on each affinoid member of the cover of $W$ use the same bounded gluing inverse. There are only finitely many restrictions and inverses, proving that $\mathcal O(V)\to\mathcal O(W)$ is bounded. Every finite covering of a compact domain by compact analytic domains has finite affinoid refinements. The sheaf equalizer is therefore the one already established on $X_G$, with its bounded Banach realization.}
{对紧解析域 $W\subseteq V$，分别选取有限 affinoid 覆盖。将 $V$ 上的匹配族限制到两个覆盖的全部两两交，再对 $W$ 覆盖中的每个 affinoid 成员使用同一个有界粘合逆映射。这里只有有限个限制与逆映射，因此 $\mathcal O(V)\to\mathcal O(W)$ 有界。紧域由紧解析域组成的每个有限覆盖，都可进一步作有限 affinoid 加细。因此其层等化子就是在 $X_G$ 上已经证明者，并带有上述有界 Banach 实现。}
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[\bi{Quasi-nets and analytic domains}{Quasi-net 与解析域}]
\label{fnd:affinoidanalyticquasinet}
\bi{A family $(V_i)$ of subsets of a topological space $V$ is a quasi-net if, for every $x\in V$, some finite subfamily consists of sets containing $x$ and has union containing a neighborhood of $x$ in $V$. Let $X=\mathcal M(A)$. A subset $V\subseteq X$ is an analytic domain if it has a quasi-net covering by affinoid domains of $X$, with the induced topology on $V$. A family of analytic subdomains of $V$ is an admissible covering if it is a quasi-net on $V$. The empty family covers the empty domain.}
{若拓扑空间 $V$ 的一个子集族 $(V_i)$ 满足：对每个 $x\in V$，存在一个有限子族，其所有成员都包含 $x$，且其并包含 $x$ 在 $V$ 中的一个邻域，则称该族为 quasi-net。令 $X=\mathcal M(A)$。若子集 $V\subseteq X$ 具有由 $X$ 的 affinoid 域组成的 quasi-net 覆盖，则称它为解析域，并采用诱导拓扑。$V$ 的解析子域族若为 $V$ 上的 quasi-net，则称为 admissible 覆盖。空族覆盖空域。}
\end{definition}
