Strict acyclic refinement and common refinement

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 192

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Strict acyclic refinement and common refinement}{严格无环细化与共同细化}]\label{fnd:strictacyclicrefinement}
\bi{Let $N$ be a finite intersection coefficient diagram, and let $\mathcal U=(U_i)_{i\in I}$ and $\mathcal V=(V_j)_{j\in J}$ be nonempty finite families. Suppose that for every nonempty tuple of $\mathcal U$-indices, the augmented ordered complex of $\mathcal V$ restricted to $U_{i_0}\cap\cdots\cap U_{i_p}$ is strictly acyclic. No coefficient term is discarded on the basis of point-set emptiness.}
{设 $N$ 为有限交叠系数图表，$\mathcal U=(U_i)_{i\in I}$ 与 $\mathcal V=(V_j)_{j\in J}$ 为非空有限族。假设对每个含至少一个 $\mathcal U$ 指标的多重指标，$\mathcal V$ 限制到 $U_{i_0}\cap\cdots\cap U_{i_p}$ 上的增广有序复形严格无环。不能依据点集为空而删去任何系数项。}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \bi{If $\mathcal V$ refines $\mathcal U$, meaning that a map $t:J\to I$ satisfies $V_j\leq U_{t(j)}$, then the augmented complexes of $\mathcal U$ and $\mathcal V$ are strictly acyclic simultaneously. The usual refinement map is a quasi-isomorphism, compatible with augmentation; its cohomology map is independent of $t$.}
{若 $\mathcal V$ 细化 $\mathcal U$，即存在映射 $t:J\to I$ 使 $V_j\leq U_{t(j)}$，则 $\mathcal U$ 与 $\mathcal V$ 的增广复形同时严格无环。通常的细化映射为与增广相容的准同构，其诱导的上同调映射不依赖 $t$。}
\item \bi{Without a refinement assumption, put $\mathcal W=(U_i\cap V_j)_{(i,j)\in I\times J}$. Then the augmented complexes of $\mathcal U$ and $\mathcal W$ are strictly acyclic simultaneously. The hypotheses on every $\mathcal U$-intersection remain necessary in this assertion.}
{不要求细化时，令 $\mathcal W=(U_i\cap V_j)_{(i,j)\in I\times J}$。则 $\mathcal U$ 与 $\mathcal W$ 的增广复形同时严格无环。在这个断言中，仍须保留对每个 $\mathcal U$ 交叠的假设。}
\end{enumerate}
\bi{All conclusions are valid for all-tuples complexes as well, with bounded comparison maps and degreewise bounded homotopies. Only finitely many of the stipulated local inverse and lifting bounds are needed.}
{以上结论也适用于全部指标复形，比较映射有界，同伦在每个次数有界。只需假设中的有限多个局部逆映射界与提升界。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For part (1), form the ordered double complex}
{对 (1)，构造有序双复形}
\[
 D^{p,q}=\prod_{i_0<\cdots<i_p}\prod_{j_0<\cdots<j_q}
 N(U_{i_0}\cap\cdots\cap U_{i_p}\cap V_{j_0}\cap\cdots\cap V_{j_q}).
\]
\bi{The assumed vertical augmented rows compare $C^\bullet(\mathcal U,N)$ with $\operatorname{Tot}(D)$ by Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictfinitecechcomparison}. In the other direction, fix a nonempty $j$-tuple and choose any one of its indices $j_0$. Since $V_{j_0}\cap\cdots\cap V_{j_q}\leq U_{t(j_0)}$, the restricted $\mathcal U$-family has a member equal to the entire intersection. Its augmented complex has the insertion contraction of \cite[Tag 0G6T, Lemma 20.23.7]{StacksAlg}. With the maximum norms, insertion has norm at most one; no local sheaf theorem is used. The same comparison lemma now compares $C^\bullet(\mathcal V,N)$ with $\operatorname{Tot}(D)$. The two applications, preserving the common augmentation $N(X)$, prove strict acyclicity in both directions.}
{假设中的纵向增广行通过 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictfinitecechcomparison} 将 $C^\bullet(\mathcal U,N)$ 与 $\operatorname{Tot}(D)$ 比较。反方向，固定非空的 $j$ 多重指标，并选择其中一个指标 $j_0$。由于 $V_{j_0}\cap\cdots\cap V_{j_q}\leq U_{t(j_0)}$，限制后的 $\mathcal U$ 族有一个成员等于整个交叠。因此其增广复形具有 \cite[Tag 0G6T, Lemma 20.23.7]{StacksAlg} 的插入收缩。对最大范数，插入的范数至多为一；这里没有使用任何局部层定理。再次应用比较引理，将 $C^\bullet(\mathcal V,N)$ 与 $\operatorname{Tot}(D)$ 比较。两个应用均保持公共增广 $N(X)$，从而在两个方向证明严格无环性。}

\bi{We record the actual maps. In alternating notation the refinement map is}
{下面记录实际映射。用交错记号表示，细化映射为}
\[
 (T_tc)_{j_0\ldots j_k}
 =c_{t(j_0)\ldots t(j_k)}\big|_{V_{j_0}\cap\cdots\cap V_{j_k}}.
\]
\bi{Repeated indices on the right give zero. Let $a$ and $b$ be the augmentations of the two complexes into the total double complex, placing them in bidegrees $(k,0)$ and $(0,k)$ respectively. The bounded homotopy}
{右边若有重复指标则取零。记 $a,b$ 为两个复形进入双复形总复形的增广，分别将其放入双次数 $(k,0)$ 与 $(0,k)$。下列有界同伦}
\[
 (Hc)^{p,q}_{i_0\ldots i_p;j_0\ldots j_q}
 =(-1)^p c_{i_0\ldots i_p\,t(j_0)\ldots t(j_q)}\big|,
 \qquad p+q=k-1,
\]
\bi{satisfies $dH+Hd=bT_t-a$. All restrictions are to the indicated mixed intersection. To check the sign, the terms obtained by deleting an $i$-index cancel between $dH$ and $Hd$ in positive horizontal degree; the terms obtained by deleting a $t(j)$-index cancel in positive vertical degree. The boundary terms are $bT_t$ at $p=0$ and $-a$ at $q=0$. Thus the quasi-isomorphism supplied by the double complex is induced by the usual refinement map. The prism formula in \cite[Tag 01FP, Section 20.25, paragraph ``Refinements'', following (20.25.0.2)]{StacksAlg} gives a homotopy for two choices $t,t'$. It consists of finitely many signed restrictions and is bounded in each degree. In the finite-poset realization, one may restrict the first open family to the union of the refining family before applying that formula; pulling it back gives precisely the displayed coefficient maps. This verifies the presheaf scope without identifying $N(X)$ with sections of an unproved sheaf.}
{满足 $dH+Hd=bT_t-a$。所有限制均取到所指定的混合交叠。核对符号时，在正水平次数中，删去 $i$ 指标的项在 $dH$ 与 $Hd$ 之间抵消；在正纵向次数中，删去 $t(j)$ 指标的项也抵消。边缘项在 $p=0$ 为 $bT_t$，在 $q=0$ 为 $-a$。因此双复形给出的准同构由通常的细化映射诱导。\cite[Tag 01FP, Section 20.25, paragraph ``Refinements'', following (20.25.0.2)]{StacksAlg} 中的棱柱公式给出两个选择 $t,t'$ 之间的同伦；它由有限个带符号的限制组成，在每个次数有界。在有限偏序集实现中，可先将第一组开集限制到细化族的并集，再应用该公式；将其拉回后恰好得到所列系数映射。这验证了预层版本的适用范围，而没有将 $N(X)$ 识别为一个尚未证明存在的层的截面。}

\bi{For part (2), $\mathcal W$ refines $\mathcal U$. Fix an intersection $Y=U_{i_0}\cap\cdots\cap U_{i_p}$ and choose $i_0$ among its indices. On $Y$, the family $\mathcal W|_Y$ refines $\mathcal V|_Y$ by $(i,j)\mapsto j$, while $\mathcal V|_Y$ refines $\mathcal W|_Y$ by $j\mapsto(i_0,j)$ because $Y\cap V_j=Y\cap U_{i_0}\cap V_j$. The composites are homotopic to the identity by the same prism formula. These bounded homotopies preserve augmentation. For clarity, if $f,g$ are such maps with $gf-1=dh+hd$ and $fz=dy$, then $gy-hz$ is a primitive of $z$; its norm is controlled by the norms of $g,h$ and the given primitive. The analogous degree-zero assertion follows from preservation of augmentation and injectivity. Hence strict acyclicity of $\mathcal V|_Y$ implies that of $\mathcal W|_Y$. Apply part (1) to $\mathcal W\to\mathcal U$. Finally apply Tag 01FM as in Theorem~\ref{fnd:finitebinarytreecech} to obtain the all-tuples versions.}
{对 (2)，$\mathcal W$ 细化 $\mathcal U$。固定一个交 $Y=U_{i_0}\cap\cdots\cap U_{i_p}$，并选择其中指标 $i_0$。在 $Y$ 上，族 $\mathcal W|_Y$ 通过 $(i,j)\mapsto j$ 细化 $\mathcal V|_Y$；同时，由 $Y\cap V_j=Y\cap U_{i_0}\cap V_j$，$\mathcal V|_Y$ 通过 $j\mapsto(i_0,j)$ 细化 $\mathcal W|_Y$。由相同的棱柱公式，两个复合均同伦于恒等映射。这些有界同伦保持增广。具体而言，若 $f,g$ 为这些映射，$gf-1=dh+hd$，且 $fz=dy$，则 $gy-hz$ 是 $z$ 的原像，其范数由 $g,h$ 的范数及已有原像界控制。零次中的相应断言来自增广的保持与单射性。因此 $\mathcal V|_Y$ 严格无环蕴含 $\mathcal W|_Y$ 严格无环。再对 $\mathcal W\to\mathcal U$ 应用 (1)。最后，如 Theorem~\ref{fnd:finitebinarytreecech} 中一样应用 Tag 01FM，得到全部指标版本。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{These definitions do not assert that a member of $\mathscr R$ is an analytic domain. They also do not require a sheaf. The formal \v Cech results for presheaves apply to the intersection terms as follows. Give $\mathscr R$ the topology of downward closed subsets and write $O_U=\{V:V\leq U\}$. Then $O_U\cap O_V=O_{U\cap V}$. The prescription}
{这些定义没有断言 $\mathscr R$ 的元素为解析域，也不要求已经有一个层。对预层成立的形式 \v Cech 结果可如下应用于这些交叠项。给 $\mathscr R$ 配备向下闭子集组成的拓扑，记 $O_U=\{V:V\leq U\}$，则 $O_U\cap O_V=O_{U\cap V}$。规定}
\[
 \mathcal F(W)=\varprojlim_{O_U\subseteq W}N(U),\qquad
 \mathcal F(\varnothing)=0.
\]
\bi{Compatibility in this finite limit defines a presheaf. On $O_U$ its sections are naturally $N(U)$: the $U$-component determines every other component by restriction. These identifications are bounded with bounded inverses, and are isometric when restrictions are contractive. The family $(O_{U_i})$ is an open covering of its union, with exactly the required intersection terms. Its augmentation from $N(V)$ is the restriction from $O_V$ and is preserved by the comparison maps below. Thus the formal comparisons require no unproved geometric localization statement.}
{这个有限极限中的相容条件定义一个预层。在 $O_U$ 上，其截面自然等于 $N(U)$：$U$ 分量通过限制决定其余所有分量。这些识别及其逆有界；若限制均为 contractive，则它们等距。族 $(O_{U_i})$ 是其并集的开覆盖，交叠项恰好为所需各项。从 $N(V)$ 出发的增广来自 $O_V$ 上的限制，且被以下比较映射保持。因此这些形式比较不需要任何尚未证明的几何局部化结论。}
