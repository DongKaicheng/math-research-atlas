The relative Schwarz perturbation lemma

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 410

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The relative Schwarz perturbation lemma}{相对 Schwarz 扰动引理}]
\label{fnd:relativeschwarzlemma}
\bi{Let $f,g:E\to F$ be bounded $A$-linear maps of Banach modules, with $f$ surjective and $g$ completely continuous. Then $(f+g)(E)$ is closed in $F$, and the quotient is a finite $A$-module whose quotient norm is canonical.}
{设 $f,g:E\to F$ 是 Banach 模间的有界 $A$-线性映射，$f$ 满射，$g$ 完全连续。则 $(f+g)(E)$ 在 $F$ 中闭，且商是有限 $A$-模，其商范数为典范范数。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Lemma~\ref{fnd:nonarchopenmapping} gives a lift bound $C$ for $f$. If a bounded map $u$ satisfies $C\|u\|<1$, successive lifts of the errors show that $f+u$ remains surjective with a uniform lift bound: starting with $y_0=y$, choose $f(x_j)=y_j$, $\|x_j\|\leq C\|y_j\|$, and put $y_{j+1}=y_j-(f+u)(x_j)$. The norms decrease geometrically and $\sum_jx_j$ is a lift of $y$. Choose a finite-image approximant $h$ of $g$ with $C\|g-h\|<1$, and set $f'=f+(g-h)$. Thus $f'$ is a norm-strict surjection.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:nonarchopenmapping}，$f$ 有提升界 $C$。若有界映射 $u$ 满足 $C\|u\|<1$，逐次提升误差便知 $f+u$ 仍满射且有统一提升界：令 $y_0=y$，选择 $f(x_j)=y_j$、$\|x_j\|\leq C\|y_j\|$，并令 $y_{j+1}=y_j-(f+u)(x_j)$。范数几何递减，$\sum_jx_j$ 就是 $y$ 的提升。选择 $g$ 的有限像逼近 $h$，使 $C\|g-h\|<1$，并置 $f'=f+(g-h)$。于是 $f'$ 为 norm-strict 满射。}

\bi{Let $N=\ker h$. It is closed, and $Q=E/N$ is a complete finite $A$-module. Its norm is canonical: an algebraic finite generating set defines a bounded surjection $A^m\to Q$, which is norm-strict by the same open-mapping lemma. The image of $\ker f'$ in $Q$ is an algebraic submodule of a finite module, hence closed by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}. Consequently $N+\ker f'$ is closed in $E$. Since $f'$ is a quotient map, $f'(N)$ is closed in $F$, and $F/f'(N)$ is a finite module with its canonical norm. The image of $f'+h$ contains $f'(N)$; its image modulo that closed submodule is a submodule of the finite module $F/f'(N)$, hence closed and finite. Its inverse image is therefore closed in $F$, and its cokernel is finite with its canonical quotient norm. Since $f'+h=f+g$, this proves the assertion.}
{令 $N=\ker h$。它是闭模，$Q=E/N$ 是完备的有限 $A$-模。其范数是典范的：任取代数有限生成元，得到有界满射 $A^m\to Q$，由同一个开映射引理可知它为 norm-strict。$\ker f'$ 在 $Q$ 中的像是有限模的代数子模，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules} 为闭模。所以 $N+\ker f'$ 在 $E$ 中闭。由于 $f'$ 是商映射，$f'(N)$ 在 $F$ 中闭，且 $F/f'(N)$ 是带有典范范数的有限模。$f'+h$ 的像包含 $f'(N)$；模去此闭子模后的像是有限模 $F/f'(N)$ 的子模，因而闭且有限。取逆像可知它在 $F$ 中闭，余核有限且带有典范商范数。因 $f'+h=f+g$，断言得证。}
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[\bi{Relative complete continuity}{相对完全连续性}]
\label{fnd:relativecompletecontinuity}
\bi{A bounded $A$-linear map $g:E\to F$ between Banach modules is completely continuous if it is an operator-norm limit of bounded $A$-linear maps $h_i$ with finite $A$-module image. It is strictly completely continuous if these approximants can be chosen with a fixed $c\in O\setminus\{0\}$ such that $c h_i(E_{\leq1})$ is contained in a finite $A_0$-submodule of $F_{\leq1}$ for every $i$. The finite integral submodule may depend on $i$.}
{Banach 模间的有界 $A$-线性映射 $g:E\to F$ 称为完全连续，如果它是具有有限 $A$-模像的有界 $A$-线性映射 $h_i$ 的算子范数极限。如果可以选择这些逼近以及一个固定的 $c\in O\setminus\{0\}$，使每个 $c h_i(E_{\leq1})$ 都包含在 $F_{\leq1}$ 的某个有限 $A_0$-子模中，则称为严格完全连续。该有限整系数子模可以依赖于 $i$。}
\end{definition}
Standing context 2 · exact TeX
\bi{A restriction to a smaller relative polydisc has finite-rank approximations over the coefficient algebra. To retain these approximations after passing to a closed module of matching sections, one needs a uniform integral bound. We establish that bound first. Throughout the first five results, $K$ is nontrivially valued, $O=K^\circ$, and $A$ is a strict $K$-affinoid algebra with a defining quotient norm. Write $A_0=A_{\leq1}$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations} identifies $A_0$ with a quotient of $O\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle$. Banach module actions are taken to be contractive. An initially bounded action can be made contractive by the equivalent norm $\|m\|_* =\sup_{a\ne0}\|am\|/\|a\|$.}
{缩小相对多圆盘后，限制映射具有系数代数上的有限秩逼近。若要把这些逼近限制到匹配截面组成的闭模，就需要统一的整系数界。下面先建立这个界。前五个结果中，$K$ 的赋值非平凡，$O=K^\circ$，$A$ 为配备定义商范数的 strict $K$-affinoid 代数。记 $A_0=A_{\leq1}$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations}，$A_0$ 是某个 $O\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle$ 的商。Banach 模的作用取为压缩作用；原本有界的作用可通过等价范数 $\|m\|_* =\sup_{a\ne0}\|am\|/\|a\|$ 化为压缩作用。}
