Residue fields of the normalization Gauss fibre

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Statement and full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Residue fields of the normalization Gauss fibre}
{正规化 Gauss 纤维的剩余域}]
\label{fnd:gaussfibreresiduefields}
\bi{Let $R=K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_d\rangle$, and let $H$ be
the completion of $\operatorname{Frac}(R)$ for the Gauss absolute
value. Then $\widetilde H=\widetilde K(T_1,\ldots,T_d)$.
If $L/H$ is finite with its extending absolute value, then
$[\widetilde L:\widetilde H]\leq[L:H]$.}
{令 $R=K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_d\rangle$，以 $H$ 记
$\operatorname{Frac}(R)$ 关于 Gauss 绝对值的完备化，则
$\widetilde H=\widetilde K(T_1,\ldots,T_d)$。
若 $L/H$ 为有限扩张并配备延拓绝对值，则
$[\widetilde L:\widetilde H]\leq[L:H]$。}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
\bi{An element of $H^\circ$ can be approximated to error less than
one by a fraction $f/g$ with $f,g\in R$ and $\|f\|\leq\|g\|$.
The Gauss norm of a nonzero series belongs to $|K^\times|$;
divide both $f$ and $g$ by a scalar of size $\|g\|$. Their
reductions are polynomials, and the denominator has nonzero
reduction. Thus every residue of $H$ lies in $\widetilde K(T)$.
Conversely, lift the numerator and denominator of a rational
function over $\widetilde K$ to polynomials of Gauss norm at most
one, with the denominator of norm one. Their quotient in $H$
has the prescribed residue.}
{$H^\circ$ 中的元素可由分式 $f/g$ 以小于一的误差逼近，
其中 $f,g\in R$ 且 $\|f\|\leq\|g\|$。非零级数的 Gauss 范数
属于 $|K^\times|$，将 $f,g$ 同除以大小为 $\|g\|$ 的标量后，
二者的约化都是多项式，且分母约化非零。所以 $H$ 的每个剩余类
都属于 $\widetilde K(T)$。反之，将 $\widetilde K$ 上有理函数
的分子分母提升为 Gauss 范数至多为一的多项式，并使分母范数为一，
它们在 $H$ 中的商即具有所需剩余类。}

\bi{Lift a finite linearly independent family of elements of
$\widetilde L$ to norm-one elements $u_1,\ldots,u_q\in L$.
If they satisfied a nonzero $H$-linear relation, divide its
coefficients by a coefficient of maximal absolute value. Reduction
would give a nonzero $\widetilde H$-linear relation among the
chosen residues. Hence the $u_i$ are linearly independent over
$H$, so $q\leq[L:H]$. This bounds the dimension of the entire
residue extension and proves the claim.}
{将 $\widetilde L$ 中一个有限线性无关族提升为
$L$ 中范数为一的元素 $u_1,\ldots,u_q$。若它们满足非零的
$H$-线性关系，将关系系数除以一个绝对值最大的系数，再约化，就会
得到所选剩余类之间非零的 $\widetilde H$-线性关系。因此 $u_i$
在 $H$ 上线性无关，故 $q\leq[L:H]$。这控制了整个剩余扩张的维数。}
\end{proof}
