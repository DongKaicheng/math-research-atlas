Exact logarithmic integral on a proper model

G Stable inertia and original-field models

Open in the English reader · PDF page 2007

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Exact logarithmic integral on a proper model]
\label{f9ml:atomiclog}
Under \hyperref[f9ml:finite-model-data]{the standing finite-model data}, every $\xi_V$ lies in the analytification of every
nonempty Zariski open subset of $X$. In particular $g_s$ is finite at
all the atoms of $\mu_{\cX}$ and is integrable with respect to its total
variation. The exact formulas are
\begin{align}
 g_s(\xi_V)&=\frac{\lambda c_V}{e_0b_V},\label{eq:f9ml:logvalue}\\
 \int_{X^\an}g_s\,d\mu_{\cX}
   &=\frac{\lambda}{e_0E}\sum_V c_V\delta_V,\label{eq:f9ml:atomicint}\\
 \int_{X^\an}|g_s|\,d|\mu_{\cX}|
   &=\frac{\lambda}{e_0E}\sum_V|c_V\delta_V|.\label{eq:f9ml:absolute}
\end{align}
Every proper algebraic closed subset of $X$ has $|\mu_{\cX}|$-measure
zero. For $a\in K^\times$, replacing $s$ by $as$ changes
\eqref{eq:f9ml:atomicint} by $-\log|a|\,\mu_{\cX}(X^\an)$.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
On an affine model neighbourhood of $\eta_V$, the valuation in
\eqref{eq:f9ml:point} is nonnegative on the model coordinate ring and extends
the given absolute value on $K$. Indeed, $\ord_V(u)=0$ for
$u\in R^\times$, and $\ord_V(\pi)=b_V$. The valuation inequality
and multiplicativity therefore give an analytic point by \hyperref[fnd:affineanalytificationconstruction]{\texttt{NAG-001707}}.
Its kernel in the generic-fibre coordinate ring is zero, since the
valuation of each nonzero rational function is finite. Its underlying
scheme point is the generic point of $X$. Hence it belongs to every
nonempty Zariski open, including the locus where $s$ is regular and
nonvanishing. Formula \eqref{eq:f9ml:point} and the root metric give
\eqref{eq:f9ml:logvalue}. This also shows that an arbitrary value assigned
on the omitted divisor does not affect the integral.

A finite sum of distinct Dirac masses has total variation obtained by
taking the absolute values of its coefficients. Substituting
\eqref{eq:f9ml:logvalue} into \eqref{eq:f9ml:measure} proves both integral
formulas. The positive integer $b_V$ cancels between the mass and the
value; it has not been set to one. The zero-mass assertion follows
from the generic scheme point of every atom. Finally
\[
 c_V(as)=c_V(s)+e_0\ord_V(a)
        =c_V(s)+e_0b_V\ord_R(a).
\]
Since $-\log|a|=\lambda\ord_R(a)$, substitution proves the last
assertion with its stated sign.
\end{proof}
