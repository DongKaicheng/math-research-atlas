Twists on analytic projective space

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 402

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Twists on analytic projective space}
{解析射影空间上的扭转层}]
\label{fnd:analyticprojectivetwists}
\bi{Let \(P=(\mathbf P^n_K)^{\an}\), and let
\(\mathcal O_P(m)\) be the line bundle obtained by analytifying
the usual homogeneous transition functions. For every integer
\(m\), the canonical map}
{设 \(P=(\mathbf P^n_K)^{\an}\)，并令 \(\mathcal O_P(m)\)
为将通常齐次过渡函数解析化所得的线丛。对每个整数 \(m\)，
典范映射}
\[
 H^q(\mathbf P^n_K,\mathcal O(m))
       \longrightarrow H^q(P_G,\mathcal O_P(m))
\]
\bi{is an isomorphism for every \(q\geq0\), compatibly with
complete extensions of \(K\). If \(n\geq1\), the only possible
nonzero groups have bases}
{对每个 \(q\geq0\) 都是同构，且与 \(K\) 的完备扩域相容。
若 \(n\geq1\)，可能非零的群及其基为}
\[
\begin{aligned}
 H^0(P_G,\mathcal O_P(m))
   &=\bigoplus_{\substack{\nu_j\geq0\\\sum_j\nu_j=m}}KX^\nu,\\
 H^n(P_G,\mathcal O_P(m))
   &=\bigoplus_{\substack{\nu_j<0\\\sum_j\nu_j=m}}KX^\nu.
\end{aligned}
\]
\bi{For \(n=0\), \(H^0=K\) for all \(m\), and all higher groups
are zero. In the standard finite affinoid \v Cech complex, the
projection onto these finite-dimensional representatives and a
homotopy on its complement can be chosen with norm at most one.}
{若 \(n=0\)，对所有 \(m\) 都有 \(H^0=K\)，且高次群为零。
在标准有限 affinoid \v Cech 复形中，可以选择到这些有限维
代表元的投影及其补部上的同伦，使范数均至多为一。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use the finite affinoid covering
\(U_i=\{|X_j|\leq|X_i|\text{ for all }j\}\), constructed in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationprojectivecompact}. On
\(U_I=\bigcap_{i\in I}U_i\), fix \(i\in I\). The ratios
\(X_j/X_i\) have norm at most one, and the ratios with
\(j\in I\) are units with inverse of norm at most one.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:unitlaurentnormalform}, using the frame \(X_i^m\),
identifies the section space of \(\mathcal O_P(m)\) with the
maximum-norm space of coefficient families tending to zero on}
{使用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationprojectivecompact}
构造的有限 affinoid 覆盖
\(U_i=\{|X_j|\leq|X_i|\text{ 对所有 }j\}\)。
在 \(U_I=\bigcap_{i\in I}U_i\) 上固定 \(i\in I\)。
比值 \(X_j/X_i\) 的范数至多为一，其中 \(j\in I\) 的比值
还是逆元范数至多为一的单位。以 \(X_i^m\) 为标架，
Lemma~\ref{fnd:unitlaurentnormalform} 将
\(\mathcal O_P(m)\) 的截面空间识别为下述指标集上趋于零的
系数族，并配备最大范数：}
\[
 \Lambda_I(m)=
 \{\nu\in\mathbf Z^{n+1}:\textstyle\sum\nu_j=m,\
                              \nu_j\geq0\text{ if }j\notin I\}.
\]
\bi{Restrictions are the inclusions of these coefficient spaces.
Every \(U_I\) is affinoid, so
Proposition~\ref{fnd:finiteaffinoidcohomology} computes the
required cohomology by this ordered complex.}
{限制就是这些系数空间的包含。每个 \(U_I\) 都是 affinoid，
故由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:finiteaffinoidcohomology}，
所需上同调由这个有序复形计算。}

\bi{For a fixed exponent vector \(\nu\), put
\(N(\nu)=\{j:\nu_j<0\}\). Its coefficient occurs exactly in the
components indexed by nonempty \(I\) containing \(N(\nu)\).
The differential is the alternating simplex differential. If
\(N(\nu)\) is nonempty and proper, choose the least index
\(k\notin N(\nu)\). The insertion operator
\((hc)_I=(-1)^{\#\{i\in I:i<k\}}c_{I\cup\{k\}}\) when
\(k\notin I\), and zero otherwise, satisfies \(dh+hd=1\).
The identity follows by cancelling the two terms deleting and
inserting each index different from \(k\); the term deleting
\(k\) has positive sign and equals \(c_I\).
The condition on \(N(\nu)\) is preserved on both sides because
\(k\notin N(\nu)\).}
{对固定指数向量 \(\nu\)，记
\(N(\nu)=\{j:\nu_j<0\}\)。其系数恰好出现在包含
\(N(\nu)\) 的非空指标集 \(I\) 所对应的分量中；微分为
单纯形的交错微分。若 \(N(\nu)\) 非空且不是全部指标，
取不属于 \(N(\nu)\) 的最小指标 \(k\)。
当 \(k\notin I\) 时令
\((hc)_I=(-1)^{\#\{i\in I:i<k\}}c_{I\cup\{k\}}\)，
否则令其为零，则 \(dh+hd=1\)。
事实上，对每个不等于 \(k\) 的指标，先删后插与先插后删的
两项符号相反而消去；删去 \(k\) 的项符号为正，恰为
\(c_I\)。因为 \(k\notin N(\nu)\)，两边都保留包含
\(N(\nu)\) 的条件。}

\bi{If \(N(\nu)\) is empty, the full simplex complex has just
the constant degree-zero class; the same insertion at \(k=0\)
contracts the kernel of evaluation at its zeroth vertex.
If \(N(\nu)\) is the entire vertex set, only the degree-\(n\)
component remains. All the insertion maps have norm at most
one. Applied simultaneously to the coefficient vectors, they
preserve convergence to zero and therefore define bounded maps
on the completed analytic complex. Their homotopy identities hold
first on finite Laurent sums and then by continuity.}
{若 \(N(\nu)\) 为空，则完整单纯形复形只有常值的零次
上同调；在 \(k=0\) 处作同样的插入，收缩在第零顶点取值
之核。若 \(N(\nu)\) 为全部顶点，则只剩第 \(n\) 次分量。
所有插入映射的范数都至多为一，同时作用于各指数的系数时
保持趋于零的条件，故定义完备解析复形上的有界映射。
同伦恒等式先在有限 Laurent 和上成立，再由连续性处处成立。}

\bi{The surviving exponent vectors are exactly the two finite
sets in the statement. For \(n=0\), the single exponent is \(m\)
and the complex has one term. The algebraic computation
\cite[Tag 01XT, Lemma 30.8.1]{StacksAlg} uses the same coefficient
complex with finite-support families. The natural map is its
inclusion in the analytic complex. Since the surviving finite
representatives and their projections are identical, it induces
the asserted canonical isomorphism. The formulas have coefficients
\(0,1,-1\); scalar extension therefore preserves all projections,
representatives and comparison maps.}
{存留下来的指数向量恰为陈述中的两个有限集合。若 \(n=0\)，
唯一的指数为 \(m\)，复形只有一项。代数计算
\cite[Tag 01XT, Lemma 30.8.1]{StacksAlg} 使用相同的系数
复形，但只取有限支撑的系数族；自然映射就是它到解析复形的
包含。两边存留的有限代表元及投影完全相同，故诱导所需典范
同构。各公式的系数均为 \(0,1,-1\)，因此扩标量保留全部
投影、代表元与比较映射。}
\end{proof}
