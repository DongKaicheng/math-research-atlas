Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Tate acyclicity}{Tate acyclicity}]\label{fnd:tateacyclicity}
\bi{Let $X=\mathcal M(A)$ and let $X=\bigcup_{i=1}^m V_i$ be a finite affinoid-domain cover. For a finite $A$-module $M$, the augmented \v Cech complex of the corresponding localized modules is exact. In particular, for $M=A$,}
{设 $X=\mathcal M(A)$，$X=\bigcup_{i=1}^m V_i$ 为 finite affinoid-domain cover。对 finite $A$-module $M$，相应 localized modules 的 augmented \v Cech complex 为 exact。特别地，对 $M=A$，}
\[
0\longrightarrow A\longrightarrow\prod_i A_{V_i}
 \longrightarrow\prod_{i,j}A_{V_i\cap V_j}\longrightarrow\cdots
\]
\bi{is exact, with the restriction maps and alternating differentials. The maps are admissible for the natural Banach-module topologies.}
{为 exact，其中 maps 是 restrictions 与 alternating differentials。对于自然的 Banach-module topologies，这些 maps 为 admissible。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidcovercech} to $A$, $M$ and the stated cover. Its all-tuples version gives exactly the displayed differentials. Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor} identifies the localized ordinary modules with their completed tensors and canonical Banach topologies. Norm-strict exactness is therefore precisely the asserted admissibility and exactness.}
{对 $A$、$M$ 及给定覆盖应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidcovercech}。其中的全部指标版本恰给出所列微分。Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor} 将局部化的普通模与其完备张量积及典范 Banach 拓扑识别。因此 norm-strict 正合性正是所需的 admissibility 与正合性。}
\end{proof}
