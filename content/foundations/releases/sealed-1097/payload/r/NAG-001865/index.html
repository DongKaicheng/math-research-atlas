Analytic flatness and the generic-fibre cokernel

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 442

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Analytic flatness and the generic-fibre cokernel}{解析平坦性与泛纤维余核}]
\label{fnd:genericfibreflatcokernel}
\bi{In the setting of Theorem~\ref{fnd:genericfibreuniversalinjectivity}, put $P=\operatorname{Coker}(u)$. The sheaf $N$ is analytically $X$-flat at $t$ if and only if $u$ is injective at $t$ and $P$ is analytically $X$-flat at $t$.}
{在 Theorem~\ref{fnd:genericfibreuniversalinjectivity} 的情形，令 $P=\operatorname{Coker}(u)$。则 $N$ 在 $t$ 处解析 $X$-平坦，当且仅当 $u$ 在 $t$ 处单射，且 $P$ 在 $t$ 处解析 $X$-平坦。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Suppose first that $N$ is analytically flat. Make an arbitrary good analytic base change over a complete field, and choose a point $t'$ above $t$, with image $x'$. The fibre hypothesis persists by Lemma~\ref{fnd:regularfibredensitybasechange}. Enlarge the coefficient field once more to a nontrivially valued complete extension $E$ of $\mathcal H(t')$, and choose the $E$-rational point $t''$ induced by the embedding $\mathcal H(t')\hookrightarrow E$. Its image $x''$ is also $E$-rational. If $\mathcal H(t')$ was trivially valued, a free-radius extension supplies such an $E$.}
{先假设 $N$ 解析平坦。在某完备域上作任意 good 解析基变换，并选择位于 $t$ 上方的点 $t'$，记其像为 $x'$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:regularfibredensitybasechange}，纤维假设保持。再将系数域扩大为 $\mathcal H(t')$ 的一个赋值非平凡的完备扩域 $E$，并选择由嵌入 $\mathcal H(t')\hookrightarrow E$ 诱导的 $E$-有理点 $t''$；其像 $x''$ 亦为 $E$-有理点。若 $\mathcal H(t')$ 的赋值平凡，可用自由半径扩张得到这样的 $E$。}

\bi{Set $R=\mathcal O_{X'',x''}$ and $S=\mathcal O_{T'',t''}$, and let $\mathfrak m$ be the maximal ideal of $R$. By Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalfibrestalkquotient}, the fibre stalk is $S/\mathfrak mS$. On its regular integral component the fibre map from the free sheaf $L$ is injective: its kernel is a submodule of a free module over a domain and becomes zero over that domain's fraction field, because the fibre map is generically bijective. Thus the map of stalks $L_{t''}\to N_{t''}$ is injective modulo $\mathfrak m$. The ring $S$ is Noetherian, and $N_{t''}$ is flat over $R$ by analytic flatness. Apply \cite[Tag 00ME]{StacksAlg}, with its finite source module equal to $L_{t''}$ and its flat target module equal to $N_{t''}$. It gives both injectivity and $R$-flatness of the cokernel $P_{t''}$.}
{令 $R=\mathcal O_{X'',x''}$、$S=\mathcal O_{T'',t''}$，并令 $\mathfrak m$ 为 $R$ 的极大理想。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalfibrestalkquotient}，纤维茎为 $S/\mathfrak mS$。在其正则整分量上，从自由层 $L$ 出发的纤维映射单射：其核是整环上自由模的子模，而因纤维映射泛双射，该核在分式域上变为零。因此茎映射 $L_{t''}\to N_{t''}$ 模掉 $\mathfrak m$ 后单射。环 $S$ Noetherian，且由解析平坦性，$N_{t''}$ 在 $R$ 上平坦。应用 \cite[Tag 00ME]{StacksAlg}，其中有限源模取 $L_{t''}$，平坦目标模取 $N_{t''}$，便同时得到单射性及余核 $P_{t''}$ 的 $R$-平坦性。}

\bi{The two scalar-extension maps on the base and source ordinary local rings are faithfully flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}; coherent stalks pull back by ordinary tensor product. Injectivity therefore descends to $t'$, and Lemma~\ref{fnd:flatlocalsquaredescent} descends the naive flatness of $P$ to $t'$. Since the original test and $t'$ were arbitrary, $P$ is analytically flat at $t$. Taking the identity test also proves that $u$ is injective there.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}，底空间与源空间普通局部环上的两个扩标量同态都忠实平坦，而且凝聚茎通过普通张量积拉回。因此单射性下降到 $t'$，而 Lemma~\ref{fnd:flatlocalsquaredescent} 将 $P$ 的 naive 平坦性下降到 $t'$。由于原测试及 $t'$ 均任意，$P$ 在 $t$ 处解析平坦；取恒等测试还说明 $u$ 在该处单射。}

\bi{Conversely, suppose $u$ is injective and $P$ analytically flat at $t$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:genericfibreuniversalinjectivity} makes $u$ injective after every required base change at every point above $t$. At such a point the short exact sequence $0\to L\to N\to P\to0$ has outer terms flat over the base local ring: this holds for $P$ by assumption, and for $L$ because it is free over the quasi-smooth, hence analytically flat, source. The ordinary-module argument in the proof of Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion}, applied to this sequence of modules over the base ordinary local ring, shows that the middle term is flat. This holds after every test, proving analytic flatness of $N$.}
{反之，假设 $u$ 在 $t$ 处单射，且 $P$ 在该处解析平坦。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:genericfibreuniversalinjectivity}，任意所需基变换后，$u$ 在每个位于 $t$ 上方的点处仍单射。在这样的点处，短正合列 $0\to L\to N\to P\to0$ 的两端在底空间局部环上平坦：$P$ 的平坦性来自假设，而 $L$ 是 quasi-smooth、因而解析平坦的源上的自由模。将底模的短正合列张量过去，或使用相应的核判据，即知中间项平坦。这对每个测试都成立，故 $N$ 解析平坦。}
\end{proof}
