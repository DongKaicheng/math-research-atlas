Finite tensors with generalized affinoid targets

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 114

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Finite tensors with generalized affinoid targets}{以 generalized affinoid 为目标的有限张量积}]\label{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor}
\bi{Let $L/K$ be any complete valued extension preserving the given absolute value. Let $A$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra, $B$ a generalized $L$-affinoid algebra, and $\phi:A\to B$ a bounded unital $K$-homomorphism. For every finite canonical $A$-module $M$, the projective seminorm on $B\otimes_A M$ is uniformly equivalent to its complete canonical finite-$B$-module norm. Consequently}
{设 $L/K$ 为保持给定绝对值的任意完备赋值扩域。设 $A$ 为 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数，$B$ 为 generalized $L$-affinoid 代数，$\phi:A\to B$ 为有界含幺 $K$-同态。对每个有限典范 $A$-模 $M$，$B\otimes_A M$ 上的 projective 半范数与其完备的典范有限 $B$-模范数一致等价。因此}
\[
 B\otimes_A M\longrightarrow B\widehat\otimes_A M
\]
\bi{is a bounded isomorphism with bounded inverse, natural in $M$. More precisely, if $M$ has the quotient norm from $q:A^m\twoheadrightarrow M$, let $Q_B$ be the quotient norm from the corresponding surjection $B^m\twoheadrightarrow B\otimes_A M$. If $\|\phi(a)\|\leq C_\phi\|a\|$, then}
{为自然于 $M$ 的有界同构，且逆有界。更精确地，若 $M$ 使用 $q:A^m\twoheadrightarrow M$ 的商范数，并以 $Q_B$ 表示对应满射 $B^m\twoheadrightarrow B\otimes_A M$ 的商范数，则当 $\|\phi(a)\|\leq C_\phi\|a\|$ 时，有}
\[
 \|z\|_\pi\leq Q_B(z)\leq C_\phi\|z\|_\pi.
\]
\bi{In particular the comparison is isometric when $\phi$ is contractive and these specified quotient norms are used. This proposition does not assert flatness of $A\to B$.}
{特别地，当 $\phi$ 为 contractive 且采用这些指定商范数时，比较为等距同构。本命题不声称 $A\to B$ 平坦。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The zero target is immediate. Choose a finite presentation $A^s\xrightarrow{D}A^m\to M\to0$. By \cite[Tag 00DF, Lemma 10.12.10]{StacksAlg},}
{零目标的情形直接成立。选取有限表示 $A^s\xrightarrow{D}A^m\to M\to0$。由 \cite[Tag 00DF, Lemma 10.12.10]{StacksAlg}，}
\[
 B\otimes_A M=B^m/D_BB^s.
\]
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}, applied over the complete field $L$, makes the relation image closed. Lemma~\ref{fnd:completequotient} makes $Q_B$ complete, and Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules} identifies it as a canonical finite-$B$-module norm. No comparison between ordinary and completed tensors is used to obtain this closedness.}
{在完备域 $L$ 上应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}，可知关系像为闭像。Lemma~\ref{fnd:completequotient} 保证 $Q_B$ 完备，Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules} 则说明它为典范有限 $B$-模范数。取得这个闭性时没有使用普通与完备张量积之间的比较。}

\bi{Let $e_i$ be the images of the standard generators under $q$; they have norm at most one. Any vector $(b_i)$ representing $z$ gives $z=\sum_i b_i\otimes e_i$, so taking the infimum of its maximum norm gives $\|z\|_\pi\leq Q_B(z)$. For the reverse inequality, write $z=\sum_j b_j\otimes m_j$. Fix $\lambda>1$ and lift each nonzero $m_j$ to a vector $(a_{ij})_i$ of norm at most $\lambda\|m_j\|$; lift zero by zero. Then $z$ is represented in $B^m$ by $c_i=\sum_jb_j\phi(a_{ij})$, and}
{记 $e_i$ 为标准生成元在 $q$ 下的像，其范数至多为一。代表 $z$ 的每个向量 $(b_i)$ 给出 $z=\sum_i b_i\otimes e_i$，对其最大值范数取下确界，即得 $\|z\|_\pi\leq Q_B(z)$。为证明反向不等式，写出 $z=\sum_j b_j\otimes m_j$。固定 $\lambda>1$，将每个非零 $m_j$ 提升为范数至多 $\lambda\|m_j\|$ 的向量 $(a_{ij})_i$，零元提升为零。则 $z$ 在 $B^m$ 中由 $c_i=\sum_jb_j\phi(a_{ij})$ 表示，并且}
\[
 Q_B(z)\leq\max_i\|c_i\|
 \leq\lambda C_\phi\max_j\bigl(\|b_j\|\|m_j\|\bigr).
\]
\bi{Take the infimum over all finite tensor representations and then let $\lambda\downarrow1$. This proves the stated bound. A seminorm uniformly equivalent to a definite complete norm is itself definite and complete, so its separated completion changes nothing. Equivalent canonical choices preserve the result by the same norm comparisons. Naturality follows on pure tensors, and the maps are bounded because maps between finite modules are bounded.}
{对全部有限张量表示取下确界，再令 $\lambda\downarrow1$，即得所述界。与正定完备范数一致等价的半范数本身也正定且完备，因此其分离完备化不改变对象。同样的范数比较说明，换用等价的典范范数仍保持结论。自然性在纯张量上直接成立，而有限模之间的映射有界，故相应映射均有界。}
\end{proof}
