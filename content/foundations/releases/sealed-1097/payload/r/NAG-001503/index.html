\begin{proposition}[\bi{The rational site of a rational member}{Rational 成员自身的 rational site}]\label{fnd:rationalslicecomparison}
\bi{Let $U\subseteq X=\mathcal M(A)$ be represented by a strict rational algebra $B$ with value-group radii. Under $\mathcal M(B)\simeq U$, the rational subsets defined over $B$ are exactly the subsets of $U$ which are rational over $A$. Hence the rational site of $\mathcal M(B)$ is identified with the slice $\mathcal R_X/U$ of Definition~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalpretopology}, with finite set-theoretic covers on both sides. The structure sheaves agree under this identification, independently up to their unique bounded isomorphisms of all presentation choices. For every finite $A$-module $M$, the restriction of $\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$ corresponds to the rational module sheaf associated to $B\otimes_A M$. The coefficient and finite-module comparison maps are bounded in both directions.}
{设 $U\subseteq X=\mathcal M(A)$ 由半径属于值群的 strict rational 代数 $B$ 表示。在 $\mathcal M(B)\simeq U$ 的识别下，在 $B$ 上定义的 rational 子集，恰好是包含于 $U$ 且在 $A$ 上为 rational 的子集。因此，$\mathcal M(B)$ 的 rational site 与 Definition~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalpretopology} 中的 slice $\mathcal R_X/U$ 识别，两边均使用有限集合意义覆盖。此识别下结构层相同；所有表示选择均由唯一的有界同构相容比较。对每个有限 $A$-模 $M$，$\widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}$ 的限制对应于 $B\otimes_A M$ 的 rational 模层。系数与有限模的比较映射在两个方向均有界。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Every rational subset over $B$ is rational over $A$ by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaltransitivity}. Conversely, let $V\subseteq U$ have a rational presentation $A_V$ over $A$. Map its unit-ideal tuple into $B$. This gives a rational presentation over $B$, and Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology} identifies its point set with the inverse image of $V$, which is $V$ under $\mathcal M(B)\simeq U$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation} identifies the joint algebra with $B\widehat\otimes_A A_V$. Its point set is $U\cap V=V$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaldomainup} therefore gives its unique bounded $A$-algebra isomorphism with $A_V$, with bounded inverse. The coefficient map from $B$ agrees under this isomorphism by uniqueness of the factorization through $U$.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaltransitivity}，每个在 $B$ 上的 rational 子集都在 $A$ 上为 rational。反之，设 $V\subseteq U$ 在 $A$ 上具有 rational 表示 $A_V$。将其单位理想 tuple 映入 $B$，得到 $B$ 上的 rational 表示。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology}，其谱点集合是 $V$ 的逆像，在 $\mathcal M(B)\simeq U$ 下就是 $V$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation}，联合代数与 $B\widehat\otimes_A A_V$ 识别，其点集为 $U\cap V=V$。因此由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaldomainup}，它与 $A_V$ 之间存在唯一的有界 $A$-代数同构，且逆有界。由于通过 $U$ 的分解唯一，来自 $B$ 的系数映射也在该同构下相容。}

\bi{The two categories have now been identified as partially ordered sets: arrows on both sides are the same inclusions of subsets. Their finite covering families are the same families of subsets with the same union, including the empty covering of the empty object. On every object the coefficient algebras have just been identified. Restriction maps commute with these comparisons, since each is the unique bounded factorization for the corresponding inclusion. Hence the ring presheaves, and therefore the sheaves of Corollary~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalmodulesheaf}, agree. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaltransitivity} supplies the compatible finite-module comparison}
{现在两个范畴已经作为偏序集识别：两边的箭头都是相同的子集包含关系。有限覆盖族也相同，即具有指定并集的有限子集族，包括空对象的空覆盖。每个对象上的系数代数已被识别。限制映射与这些比较交换，因为它们都是相应包含关系所确定的唯一有界分解。因此环预层相同，从而 Corollary~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalmodulesheaf} 中的层相同。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaltransitivity}，还有相容的有限模比较}
\[
 C\otimes_B(B\otimes_A M)\simeq C\otimes_A M
       \simeq A_V\otimes_A M
\]
\bi{whenever $C$ is a chosen $B$-rational presentation of $V$. These maps agree on pure tensors, so their compatibility with restrictions and with morphisms of $M$ follows directly. This proves the assertion for module sheaves and their canonical norms.}
{其中 $C$ 是 $V$ 的任意所选 $B$-rational 表示。这些映射在纯张量上相同，故直接得到它们与限制映射及 $M$ 的同态的相容性。这就证明了模层及其 canonical 范数的断言。}
\end{proof}

\section{\bi{Rational domains inside rational domains}{Rational 域中的 rational 域}}\label{fnd:rationaltransitivitysection}

\bi{The fixed rational site can be compared with the rational site of one of its members only after a transitivity theorem has been proved. The issue is that a function on a rational domain need not come from the original algebra. We first control small changes of its defining functions, and then replace finitely many functions by fractions with one common denominator. The resulting algebra comparison will be proved on the actual relation quotients.}
{只有先证明传递性，才能将固定 rational site 与其某个成员自身的 rational site 比较。这里的困难是，rational 域上的函数未必来自原代数。我们先控制定义函数的小扰动，再将有限多个函数替换成具有共同分母的分式。所得代数比较直接在实际关系商上证明。}

\bi{Throughout, $K$ is a complete real-valued non-Archimedean field; trivial valuation is allowed. Nonzero affinoid algebras are strict and norm-admissible, with submultiplicative norm and unit of norm one. Quotients use their actual quotient norms. The zero algebra and its zero modules have the separate zero-norm interpretation of Section~\ref{fnd:r1quotientbridge}. A rational presentation has a unit-ideal tuple. We use unit radii in the lemmas; radii in $|K^\times|$ are reduced to this case by scaling variables.}
{全文固定完备实值非阿基米德域 $K$，允许平凡赋值。非零 affinoid 代数均为 strict 且 norm-admissible，范数次乘，单位元范数为一。商配备实际商范数。零代数及其零模按 Section~\ref{fnd:r1quotientbridge} 的约定单独使用零范数。Rational 表示的定义 tuple 必须生成单位理想。各引理使用单位半径；属于 $|K^\times|$ 的半径通过缩放变量化为这一情形。}
