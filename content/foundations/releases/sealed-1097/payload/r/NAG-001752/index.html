Coherent direct image under a finite morphism

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 321

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Coherent direct image under a finite morphism}{有限态射的凝聚直接像}]
\label{fnd:finitecoherentdirectimagebasechange}
\bi{Let $\pi:Y\to T$ be a norm-admissible finite analytic morphism and let $\mathcal F$ be coherent on $Y_G$. Then $\pi_*\mathcal F$ is coherent on $T_G$, and formation of this direct image is exact and commutes with every analytic base change, including base change over an arbitrary complete extension of the ground field.}
{设 $\pi:Y\to T$ 为 norm-admissible 的有限解析态射，且 $\mathcal F$ 在 $Y_G$ 上凝聚。则 $\pi_*\mathcal F$ 在 $T_G$ 上凝聚，直接像函子正合，并与每个解析基变换交换，包括任意完备基域扩张上的基变换。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For an affinoid domain $V=\mathcal M(A)\subset T$, write $\pi^{-1}(V)=\mathcal M(B)$, where $B$ is a finite $A$-module with its canonical norm. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb}, the restriction of $\mathcal F$ is represented by a finite $B$-module $M$. It is also finite over $A$. For every affinoid domain $V'\subset V$, finite base change and the finite-module description of coherent restriction identify its inverse-image sections with $M\otimes_A A_{V'}$. Thus the direct image on $V_G$ is the coherent module sheaf associated to $M$ over $A$. These identifications agree with the natural restriction maps and hence glue on the full analytic site. Exactness follows on each $V$ from affinoid coherent acyclicity, or equivalently from exactness of the finite-module correspondence and restriction of scalars.}
{对目标 affinoid 域 $V=\mathcal M(A)\subset T$，写 $\pi^{-1}(V)=\mathcal M(B)$，其中 $B$ 是带典范范数的有限 $A$-模。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb}，$\mathcal F$ 的限制由有限 $B$-模 $M$ 表示；它也在 $A$ 上有限。对每个 affinoid 域 $V'\subset V$，有限基变换及凝聚限制的有限模描述，将其逆像上的截面识别为 $M\otimes_A A_{V'}$。因此 $V_G$ 上的直接像，就是 $A$-模 $M$ 对应的凝聚模层。这些识别与自然限制映射相容，因而在完整解析 site 上粘合。正合性可在每个 $V$ 上由 affinoid 凝聚无环性得到，等价地也可由有限模对应的正合性及限制标量得到。}

\bi{Now take an affinoid piece $\mathcal M(C)$ of any analytic base change, with $C$ an $L$-affinoid algebra and $L/K$ complete. Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange} identifies the new finite algebra with $D=B\otimes_A C$, with the canonical finite $C$-module norm; the ordinary tensor product already gives its completed analytic pushout. The pulled-back coherent module on this finite space is $M\otimes_BD=M\otimes_A C$, by the finite-module pullback construction. This is precisely the module representing the pullback of $\pi_*\mathcal F$. These canonical identifications commute with restrictions and with the original base-change map, so they prove that map is an isomorphism globally.}
{现在取任意解析基变换的 affinoid 分片 $\mathcal M(C)$，其中 $C$ 为 $L$-affinoid 代数，$L/K$ 完备。Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange} 将新的有限代数识别为带典范有限 $C$-模范数的 $D=B\otimes_A C$；此普通张量积已给出完备解析推出。由有限模的拉回构造，此有限空间上的拉回凝聚模为 $M\otimes_BD=M\otimes_A C$，恰为 $\pi_*\mathcal F$ 的拉回所对应的模。这些典范识别与限制及原基变换映射交换，故在整体上证明该映射为同构。}
\end{proof}
