NAG-001657 · Accepted whole

Dimension of a strict normalization

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 594

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Dimension of a strict normalization}
{Strict 正规化的维数}]
\label{fnd:stricttatedimension}
\bi{For every complete non-Archimedean field $K$,
$\dim K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle=n$. If
$K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_d\rangle\hookrightarrow A$ is a finite injection
into a nonzero strict affinoid algebra, then $d=\dim A$.}
{对每个完备非阿基米德域 $K$，有
$\dim K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle=n$。若
$K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_d\rangle\hookrightarrow A$ 是到非零
strict affinoid 代数的有限单射，则 $d=\dim A$。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $R_n=K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle$. The coordinate ideals
$(0)$, $(T_1)$, \ldots, $(T_1,\ldots,T_n)$ form a strictly increasing chain
of primes: each quotient is a Tate algebra and its Gauss norm is
multiplicative. Thus $\dim R_n\geq n$. For the reverse inequality
induct on $n$, starting with the field $R_0=K$. Given a nonzero prime
$\mathfrak p\subset R_n$, choose $0\ne f\in\mathfrak p$.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:distinguishedcoordinates} and
Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachweierstrassdivision} show, after an isometric
coordinate change, that $R_n/(f)$ is finite over $R_{n-1}$.
The same holds for $R_n/\mathfrak p$. The dimension inequality for an
integral map, \cite[Tag 00OJ]{StacksAlg}, gives
$\dim(R_n/\mathfrak p)\leq n-1$. Any prime chain in the domain $R_n$
can be preceded by $(0)$; after its first nonzero member its length
is at most $n-1$. Its total length is therefore at most $n$.
Finally, an integral injection preserves dimension by
\cite[Tag 00OK]{StacksAlg}. Apply this to the displayed finite injection.}
{令 $R_n=K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle$。坐标理想
$(0)$、$(T_1)$、\ldots、$(T_1,\ldots,T_n)$ 构成严格递增的素理想链，
因为各商均为 Gauss 范数乘性的 Tate 代数。因此 $\dim R_n\geq n$。
对反向不等式，关于 $n$ 归纳，从域 $R_0=K$ 开始。
给定非零素理想 $\mathfrak p\subset R_n$，选取
$0\ne f\in\mathfrak p$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:distinguishedcoordinates}
及 Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachweierstrassdivision}，经等距坐标变换后，
$R_n/(f)$ 在 $R_{n-1}$ 上有限，其商 $R_n/\mathfrak p$ 亦然。
整映射的维数不等式 \cite[Tag 00OJ]{StacksAlg} 给出
$\dim(R_n/\mathfrak p)\leq n-1$。整环 $R_n$ 的任意素理想链都可以
在前面添入 $(0)$；从首个非零成员开始，其后链长至多为 $n-1$，
故总链长至多为 $n$。最后，由 \cite[Tag 00OK]{StacksAlg}，
整单射保持维数。将其应用于所列有限单射即可。}
\end{proof}
