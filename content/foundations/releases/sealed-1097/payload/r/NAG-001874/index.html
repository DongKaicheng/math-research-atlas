Two Laurent charts form an affinoid space

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 420

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Two Laurent charts form an affinoid space}{两个 Laurent 图构成 affinoid 空间}]
\label{fnd:doublelaurentaffinoidgluing}
\bi{Let $X=Y\cup Z$ be an analytic space covered by two affinoid domains $Y=\mathcal M(B)$ and $Z=\mathcal M(C)$, with affinoid intersection $Y\cap Z=\mathcal M(D)$. Suppose $0<p<t<q$, $r_i>0$, and bounded norm-strict epimorphisms}
{设解析空间 $X=Y\cup Z$ 由两个 affinoid 域 $Y=\mathcal M(B)$、$Z=\mathcal M(C)$ 覆盖，其交为 affinoid 空间 $Y\cap Z=\mathcal M(D)$。设 $0<p<t<q$、$r_i>0$，并有有界的范数严格满同态}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 K\{r^{-1}T,t^{-1}S,pS^{-1}\}&\longrightarrow B,
       &T_i&\longmapsto f_i,&S&\longmapsto f,\\
 K\{r^{-1}T,q^{-1}S,tS^{-1}\}&\longrightarrow C,
       &T_i&\longmapsto g_i,&S&\longmapsto g.
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{Assume $Y\cap Z=Y\{|f|\ge t\}=Z\{|g|\le t\}$. Give $D$ the quotient norm induced by}
{假设 $Y\cap Z=Y\{|f|\ge t\}=Z\{|g|\le t\}$。在 $D$ 上取由下列满同态诱导的商范数：}
\[
 K\{r^{-1}T,t^{-1}S,tS^{-1}\}\longrightarrow D,
       \qquad T_i\longmapsto f_i,\ S\longmapsto f.
\]
\bi{If $\|f_i-g_i\|_D<r_i$ and $\|f-g\|_D<t$, then $X$ is affinoid. More precisely, for every $\delta>0$ there are an affinoid algebra $A$, an isomorphism $X\simeq\mathcal M(A)$, and a bounded norm-strict epimorphism}
{若 $\|f_i-g_i\|_D<r_i$ 且 $\|f-g\|_D<t$，则 $X$ 为 affinoid。更精确地，对每个 $\delta>0$，存在 affinoid 代数 $A$、同构 $X\simeq\mathcal M(A)$，及有界的范数严格满同态}
\[
 K\{r^{-1}T,q^{-1}S,pS^{-1}\}\longrightarrow A,
        \qquad T_i\longmapsto h_i,\ S\longmapsto h,
\]
\bi{such that, for the quotient norms of the displayed presentations of $B$ and $C$,}
{使得对上述 $B$、$C$ 的表示所给出的商范数，有}
\[
 \begin{split}
 \|h_i-f_i\|_B,\ \|h_i-g_i\|_C
       &<\|f_i-g_i\|_D+\delta,\\
 \|h-f\|_B,\ \|h-g\|_C
       &<\|f-g\|_D+\delta.
 \end{split}
\]
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First suppose the two epimorphisms are isomorphisms. The restrictions identify $B$ and $C$ with subalgebras of $D$. In the $f$-coordinates the latter is the Laurent Gauss algebra of the circle $|S|=t$. The hypotheses and Lemma~\ref{fnd:smalllaurentcoordinatechange}, applied with equal inner and outer radius $t$, identify its $g$-coordinates isometrically as well. Choose $\varepsilon<1$ exceeding all ratios $\|f_i-g_i\|_D/r_i$ and $\|f-g\|_D/t$.}
{先设两个满同态都是同构。限制映射把 $B$、$C$ 识别为 $D$ 的子代数。在 $f$ 坐标中，后者是圆周 $|S|=t$ 的 Laurent Gauss 代数。由假设及内外半径同为 $t$ 时的 Lemma~\ref{fnd:smalllaurentcoordinatechange}，$g$ 坐标也给出等距识别。选择 $\varepsilon<1$，大于所有比值 $\|f_i-g_i\|_D/r_i$ 及 $\|f-g\|_D/t$。}

\bi{Write a Laurent series $a=\sum a_{\nu,j}f^\nu f^j$ on the circle, where $f^\nu=f_1^{\nu_1}\cdots f_n^{\nu_n}$. Retain its terms with $j>0$ in the $f$-coordinates, and write the terms with $j\le0$ in the $g$-coordinates. The first sum $b_0$ converges in $B$, the second sum $c_0$ converges in $C$, and}
{把圆周上的 Laurent 级数写成 $a=\sum a_{\nu,j}f^\nu f^j$，其中 $f^\nu=f_1^{\nu_1}\cdots f_n^{\nu_n}$。保留 $j>0$ 的项的 $f$ 坐标表达，并把 $j\le0$ 的项改用 $g$ 坐标。第一部分 $b_0$ 在 $B$ 中收敛，第二部分 $c_0$ 在 $C$ 中收敛，而且}
\[
 \|b_0\|_B,\|c_0\|_C\le\|a\|_D,
 \qquad \|a-b_0-c_0\|_D\le\varepsilon\|a\|_D.
\]
\bi{Iterating on the error gives $a=b+c$, where $b$ is a series in strictly positive powers of $f$ and $c$ is a series in nonpositive powers of $g$, with}
{对误差迭代，得到 $a=b+c$，其中 $b$ 是 $f$ 的严格正幂级数，$c$ 是 $g$ 的非正幂级数，且}
\[
 \|b\|_B,\|c\|_C\le\|a\|_D,
 \qquad
 \|b/f\|_B,\|c/g\|_C\le t^{-1}\|a\|_D.
\]
\bi{The divided estimates hold term by term: in $B$ the largest radius of $f$ is $t$, whereas in $C$ the smallest radius of $g$ is $t$.}
{最后两个除法估计逐项成立：在 $B$ 中 $f$ 的最大半径为 $t$，在 $C$ 中 $g$ 的最小半径为 $t$。}

\bi{Apply this splitting to $f_i-g_i=b_i+c_i$ and $f-g=b+c$. The matching pairs}
{把此分解应用于 $f_i-g_i=b_i+c_i$ 及 $f-g=b+c$。匹配的二元组}
\[
 h_i=f_i-b_i=g_i+c_i,\qquad h=f-b=g+c
\]
\bi{define sections on $X$. The inequalities $\|b/f\|_B<1$ and $\|c/g\|_C<1$ show that $h$ is invertible and has the same modulus as $f$ on $Y$ and as $g$ on $Z$. On each chart, the change to the $h_i,h$ coordinates satisfies Lemma~\ref{fnd:smalllaurentcoordinatechange}; hence it is an isomorphism of the corresponding Laurent Gauss algebra. Therefore $Y$ and $Z$, with these coordinates, are the standard two Laurent domains $|S|\le t$ and $|S|\ge t$ in}
{不等式 $\|b/f\|_B<1$、$\|c/g\|_C<1$ 表明 $h$ 可逆，并且在 $Y$ 上与 $f$、在 $Z$ 上与 $g$ 具有相同模长。在每个图上，改用 $h_i,h$ 坐标满足 Lemma~\ref{fnd:smalllaurentcoordinatechange} 的条件，因而给出相应 Laurent Gauss 代数的自同构。因此在这些坐标下，$Y$、$Z$ 是下列空间中的标准两个 Laurent 域 $|S|\le t$ 与 $|S|\ge t$：}
\[
 \mathcal M\bigl(K\{r^{-1}T,q^{-1}S,pS^{-1}\}\bigr).
\]
\bi{Their identifications agree on the overlap. Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticspacegluing} identifies their union with this affinoid space. In this free case the coordinate errors are bounded above by the corresponding overlap errors, with no added $\delta$.}
{这些识别在交叠上相容。Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticspacegluing} 将其并识别为上述 affinoid 空间。在此自由情形下，坐标误差不超过对应的交叠误差，无须添加 $\delta$。}

\bi{For the general case put $E=K\{r^{-1}T,t^{-1}S,tS^{-1}\}$. By the defining quotient norm on $D$, choose lifts $G_i,G\in E$ of $g_i,g$ such that}
{在一般情形，令 $E=K\{r^{-1}T,t^{-1}S,tS^{-1}\}$。由 $D$ 上的定义商范数，选择 $g_i,g$ 的提升 $G_i,G\in E$，使得}
\[
 \begin{split}
 \|T_i-G_i\|_E&<\min\{r_i,\|f_i-g_i\|_D+\delta\},\\
 \|S-G\|_E&<\min\{t,\|f-g\|_D+\delta\}.
 \end{split}
\]
\bi{Because $S$ has constant modulus $t$ on $E$, the substitution $T_i\mapsto G_i$, $S\mapsto G$ is an isometric automorphism by the lemma. Let $B'$ be the lower Laurent algebra in the $T_i,S$ coordinates and $C'$ the upper Laurent algebra in the $G_i,G$ coordinates, both regarded as subalgebras of $E$. Their spectra glue along $\mathcal M(E)$ to an affinoid space $X'=\mathcal M(A')$ by the free case. There are compatible epimorphisms $B'\to B$, $C'\to C$, $E\to D$.}
{因为 $S$ 在 $E$ 上的模长恒为 $t$，由引理，代入 $T_i\mapsto G_i$、$S\mapsto G$ 是等距自同构。设 $B'$ 为 $T_i,S$ 坐标中的下侧 Laurent 代数，$C'$ 为 $G_i,G$ 坐标中的上侧 Laurent 代数，均视为 $E$ 的子代数。由自由情形，它们的谱沿 $\mathcal M(E)$ 粘合成 affinoid 空间 $X'=\mathcal M(A')$。有相容满同态 $B'\to B$、$C'\to C$、$E\to D$。}

\bi{Let $I_B,I_C,I_D$ be their kernels. The stated descriptions of the overlap and the quotient formula for rational localization give $I_BE=I_D=I_CE$. The ideals are finite modules, so they glue to a coherent ideal sheaf on $X'$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainkiehl} identifies this sheaf with $\widetilde I$ for an ideal $I\subset A'$, and identifies its two restrictions with $I_B,I_C$. It follows that $\mathcal M(A'/I)$ has affinoid domains with algebras $B,C$ and overlap $D$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticspacegluing} therefore identifies $X$ with $\mathcal M(A'/I)$. Finally the free-case coordinates on $A'$ descend to $A'/I$; quotient maps do not increase the displayed quotient norms. The chosen lifting estimates give all required bounds.}
{令 $I_B,I_C,I_D$ 为其核。由所给的交叠描述及 rational 局部化的商公式，有 $I_BE=I_D=I_CE$。这些理想为有限模，故粘合成 $X'$ 上的凝聚理想层。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainkiehl} 把此层识别为某个理想 $I\subset A'$ 对应的 $\widetilde I$，并把其两个限制识别为 $I_B,I_C$。于是 $\mathcal M(A'/I)$ 具有代数分别为 $B,C$、交叠代数为 $D$ 的 affinoid 域。Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticspacegluing} 因此给出 $X\simeq\mathcal M(A'/I)$。最后，把自由情形中 $A'$ 的坐标下降到 $A'/I$；商映射不增大上述商范数。所选的提升估计给出全部所需不等式。}
\end{proof}
