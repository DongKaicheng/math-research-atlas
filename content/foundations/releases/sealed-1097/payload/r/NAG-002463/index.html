Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Proper projection from generic coefficient agreement]
\label{v038:degree:propergenericprojection}
Let $p:X\to Y$ be proper between good Hausdorff analytic spaces
pure of the same dimension $d$. Let $\mathcal F$ and
$\mathcal G$ be coherent on $X_G$ and $Y_G$, respectively,
and fix $\Delta\in\mathbb Q_{\geq0}$. Let $D\subseteq Y$
be a closed analytic subspace whose defining ideal, on every
affinoid domain of $Y$, is contained in no minimal prime.
The empty subspace is permitted.

Suppose that whenever $W=\mathcal M(A)$ is an affinoid domain
of $Y\setminus D$ for which $p^{-1}(W)=\mathcal M(B)\to W$
is finite, the modules
\[
          M=\Gamma(p^{-1}(W),\mathcal F),\qquad
          L=\Gamma(W,\mathcal G)
\]
satisfy, for every $\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Min}(A)$,
\begin{equation}
\label{v038:degree:genericcoefficientcondition}
  \operatorname{length}_{A_{\mathfrak p}}(M_{\mathfrak p})
          =\Delta\operatorname{length}_{A_{\mathfrak p}}
                                             (L_{\mathfrak p}).
\end{equation}
Then for every affinoid domain $V\subseteq Y$ and unit tuple
$f$ on $V$,
\[
        \mathcal P_{p,\mathcal F,f}
               =\Delta\mathcal T_f\bigl(\Gamma(V,\mathcal G)\bigr).
\]
For every ordinary smooth moment form
$\alpha\in\mathcal A^{d,d}_{Y,c}(Y)$, the pullback has compact
support and
\[
               \int_{X,\mathcal F}p^*\alpha
                                  =\Delta\int_{Y,\mathcal G}\alpha.
\]
These scalar formulas multiply under composition when the
corresponding coefficient conditions hold for the two maps.

For $\mathcal F=\mathcal O_X$ and
$\mathcal G=\mathcal O_Y$, condition
\eqref{v038:degree:genericcoefficientcondition} uses the ratio
of the full generic local lengths. If $A$ is reduced, its
denominator is one, so a nonzero such ratio is a positive
integer. If an actual finite restriction has integral rings
$A,B$ and an injective structural map, its numerator is
$[\operatorname{Frac}(B):\operatorname{Frac}(A)]$, with the
full inseparable degree retained.
\end{theorem}
