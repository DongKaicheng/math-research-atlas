\begin{proposition}[\bi{Separating the generic components of a nodal model}
{分离结点模型的泛分量}]
\label{cand:nodalmodelgenericnormalization}
\bi{Let $F$ be a complete nontrivially valued field, put
$O=F^\circ$, and let $\mathcal Y/O$ be a projective flat finitely
presented nodal curve family. Suppose that all special nodes admit
the split pointed charts of Theorem~\ref{cand:pointednodalvaluationchart}
over $O$. Suppose that the normalization of the generic fibre is
a disjoint union of finitely many smooth proper geometrically
connected curves $C_1,\ldots,C_m$.
The relative normalization of $\mathcal Y$ in
$\coprod_i C_i$ is a finite projective morphism
$\mathcal N\to\mathcal Y$, and}
{设 $F$ 为完备非平凡赋值域，令 $O=F^\circ$。
设 $\mathcal Y/O$ 为射影、平坦、有限呈示的结点曲线族，且
所有特殊结点都在 $O$ 上具有
Theorem~\ref{cand:pointednodalvaluationchart} 的分裂带点坐标。
设泛纤维的正规化为有限个光滑 proper 几何连通曲线
$C_1,\ldots,C_m$ 的不交并。
$\mathcal Y$ 在 $\coprod_iC_i$ 中的相对正规化给出有限射影态射
$\mathcal N\to\mathcal Y$，并且}
\[
             \mathcal N=\coprod_{i=1}^m\mathcal C_i,
             \qquad (\mathcal C_i)_F=C_i.
\]
\bi{Each $\mathcal C_i/O$ is normal, projective, flat and of finite
presentation, with reduced nodal special fibre and split special
nodes. Every semilinear isometric automorphism of $\mathcal Y$
which preserves the labels of the generic normalization induces
automorphisms of all $\mathcal C_i$, compatibly with composition.}
{每个 $\mathcal C_i/O$ 正规、射影、平坦且有限呈示，其特殊纤维
约化且只有分裂结点。$\mathcal Y$ 上保持泛正规化标签的每个
半线性等距自同构均诱导各 $\mathcal C_i$ 上的自同构，并与复合相容。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose $\pi\in O$ with $0<|\pi|<1$.
The rank-one property gives $O[1/\pi]=F$, so the generic fibre is
the quasi-compact open $D(\pi)$. The normalization map
$\coprod_i C_i\to\mathcal Y_F$ is finite by
\cite[Tag 0BXR, part (1)]{StacksAlg}, since $\mathcal Y_F$ is
of finite type over $F$. Followed by this open immersion, it
is quasi-compact and quasi-separated. Its relative normalization
is therefore defined by the integral-closure algebra and has the
universal property of \cite[Tag 035I]{StacksAlg}.}
{取 $\pi\in O$ 满足 $0<|\pi|<1$。秩一性给出
$O[1/\pi]=F$，故泛纤维是拟紧开集 $D(\pi)$。
因为 $\mathcal Y_F$ 在 $F$ 上有限型，由
\cite[Tag 0BXR, part (1)]{StacksAlg}，正规化映射
$\coprod_iC_i\to\mathcal Y_F$ 有限。再接此开浸入后，
所得映射拟紧且拟分离。因此可以通过整闭包代数定义相对正规化，
并具有 \cite[Tag 035I]{StacksAlg} 的泛性质。}

\bi{Relative normalization commutes with smooth, hence with
\textup{\'etale}, base change by \cite[Tag 03GV]{StacksAlg}.
On an actual pointed nodal chart
$\mathcal Y\leftarrow E\to\operatorname{Spec}R_a$,
the generic normalization is the pullback of that of $(R_a)_F$;
one can apply the same result over $F$, or identify the two normal
branches directly. Thus the pullback of $\mathcal N$ to $E$ is}
{由 \cite[Tag 03GV]{StacksAlg}，相对正规化与光滑基变换，
从而与 \textup{\'etale} 基变换相容。
在实际带点结点坐标图
$\mathcal Y\leftarrow E\to\operatorname{Spec}R_a$ 上，
泛正规化是 $(R_a)_F$ 的正规化的拉回；这可以在 $F$ 上再次
应用同一结论，也可直接识别两个正规分支。
因此 $\mathcal N$ 在 $E$ 上的拉回为}
\[
                 E\times_{\operatorname{Spec}R_a}
                          \operatorname{Spec}R_a'.
\]
\bi{Lemma~\ref{cand:valuationnodenormalization} gives its finite
presentation and finiteness over $E$. At smooth points the
normalization changes nothing: a smooth algebra $A$ over the normal
ring $O$ is normal by \cite[Tag 033C]{StacksAlg}, and its
$O$-flatness embeds $A[1/\pi]$ in its total quotient ring.
The images of the finitely many nodal charts and the smooth locus
cover $\mathcal Y$: they cover the closed fibre, and any omitted
generic point would have a closed specialization by properness,
whereas this covering is open. Choose finitely many affine charts
in the smooth locus to obtain a quasi-compact surjective
\textup{\'etale} cover. Finiteness now descends by
\cite[Tag 02LA]{StacksAlg}.}
{Lemma~\ref{cand:valuationnodenormalization} 给出它在 $E$ 上
有限且有限呈示。在光滑点处，正规化不作改变：由
\cite[Tag 033C]{StacksAlg}，正规环 $O$ 上的光滑代数 $A$ 正规，
而 $O$-平坦性将 $A[1/\pi]$ 嵌入其全商环。
有限个结点坐标片的像与光滑处覆盖 $\mathcal Y$：它们覆盖闭纤维；
若遗漏泛纤维上的点，properness 给出其闭纤维中的特殊化，而上述
覆盖为开集，产生矛盾。在光滑处再选有限个仿射坐标片，便得到
拟紧满射 \textup{\'etale} 覆盖。
由 \cite[Tag 02LA]{StacksAlg}，有限性下降。}

\bi{On the induced \textup{\'etale} cover of $\mathcal N$,
each ring is smooth over one of the normal rings $R_a'$ and is
therefore normal by Tag 033C. Normality descends along faithfully
flat local maps by \cite[Tag 033G]{StacksAlg}.
Flatness and local finite presentation over $O$ descend on this
source cover by \cite[Tags 036K, 036N]{StacksAlg}.
Since $\mathcal N\to\mathcal Y$ is finite and $\mathcal Y/O$
is projective, $\mathcal N/O$ is quasi-compact and separated;
it is consequently of finite presentation.
The same charts show that its geometric fibres are reduced and
nodal of pure dimension one. Above an $a=0$ node the two branches
become smooth; above an $a\ne0$ node the normalization is locally
the identity, so the remaining special nodes are split.
By \cite[Tags 0B3I, 0C4P]{StacksAlg}, finiteness and the projectivity
of $\mathcal Y/O$ also make $\mathcal N/O$ projective.}
{在 $\mathcal N$ 上诱导的 \textup{\'etale} 覆盖中，每个环在
某个正规环 $R_a'$ 上光滑，故由 Tag 033C 正规。
由 \cite[Tag 033G]{StacksAlg}，正规性沿忠实平坦局部同态下降。
由 \cite[Tags 036K, 036N]{StacksAlg}，在 $O$ 上的平坦性与
局部有限呈示性也沿此源覆盖下降。
由于 $\mathcal N\to\mathcal Y$ 有限且 $\mathcal Y/O$ 射影，
$\mathcal N/O$ 拟紧分离，因而有限呈示。
同一批坐标还表明其几何纤维约化、纯一维且至多具有结点。
$a=0$ 结点上方的两个分支变为光滑；在 $a\ne0$ 结点上方，
正规化局部为恒等映射，因此剩余特殊结点仍分裂。
由 \cite[Tags 0B3I, 0C4P]{StacksAlg}，有限性与
$\mathcal Y/O$ 的射影性还给出 $\mathcal N/O$ 射影。}

\bi{It remains to separate the labels. On any affine open
$\operatorname{Spec}A\subset\mathcal N$, $O$-flatness makes
$\pi$ a nonzerodivisor, so $A[1/\pi]$ embeds in the total quotient
ring of $A$. Each labelled generic idempotent satisfies
$e_i^2-e_i=0$. A normal ring is integrally closed in its total
quotient ring by \cite[Tag 034M]{StacksAlg}; hence $e_i\in A$.
The extensions agree on overlaps because multiplication by $\pi$
is injective on every stalk. They remain orthogonal and sum to one,
and therefore give the stated clopen decomposition.
Each piece inherits all the preceding geometric properties.
Finally the relative integral-closure construction is functorial
under semilinear isomorphisms; the generic label idempotents are
preserved, so their unique extensions are preserved as well.
This proves equivariance and the composition law.}
{最后分离标签。对 $\mathcal N$ 的任一仿射开集
$\operatorname{Spec}A$，$O$-平坦性使 $\pi$ 成为非零因子，
故 $A[1/\pi]$ 嵌入 $A$ 的全商环。
每个带标签泛幂等元都满足 $e_i^2-e_i=0$。
由 \cite[Tag 034M]{StacksAlg}，正规环在其全商环中整闭，
所以 $e_i\in A$。由于在每个茎上乘以 $\pi$ 都单射，
这些延拓在交上相同。它们仍两两正交且和为一，
因而给出所述既开又闭的分解；每个部分继承上述全部几何性质。
最后，相对整闭包构造在半线性同构下函子性成立。
泛标签幂等元被保持，故其唯一延拓也被保持。
这证明等变性与复合律。}
\end{proof}

\section{\bi{Equivariant genus-one models by doubling}
{由倍接构造等变亏格一模型}}

\bi{We use stable reduction of an auxiliary nodal curve and then
separate its two labelled generic components. The local normalization
calculation works over a valuation ring without a discreteness assumption.}
{我们对一个辅助结点曲线应用稳定约化，再分离其两个带标签的泛分量。
所用的局部正规化计算适用于赋值环，无需离散性假设。}
