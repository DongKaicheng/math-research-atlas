The compactification of a rational smooth tube

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 674

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The compactification of a rational smooth tube}
{有理光滑管域的紧化}]
\label{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure}
\bi{Let $q\in\mathcal X_s(k)$ be a point where a curve model
$\mathcal X$ is smooth of relative dimension one. If $\eta$ is the
unique component generic point specializing to $q$, then}
{设曲线模型 $\mathcal X$ 在点 $q\in\mathcal X_s(k)$ 处相对
维数为一且光滑。若 $\eta$ 为特化到 $q$ 的唯一分量泛点，则}
\[
 D_q=]q[\simeq\{x:|T(x)|<1\},\qquad
 C_q=\overline{D_q}=D_q\cup\{v_\eta\}.
\]
\bi{This closure is the one-point compactification of $D_q$.
In each open-unit-disc coordinate $T$, the sets}
{此闭包是 $D_q$ 的一点紧化。对每个开单位圆盘坐标 $T$，集合}
\[
 \{v_\eta\}\cup\{x\in D_q:|T(x)|>r\},\qquad 0<r<1,
\]
\bi{form a neighborhood basis of $v_\eta$ in $C_q$.}
{构成 $C_q$ 中 $v_\eta$ 的邻域基。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Corollary~\ref{cand:smoothpointpolydisctube} gives the disc
isomorphism. Smoothness makes the local ring of the special-fibre
curve at $q$ a regular one-dimensional local ring, hence a domain;
equivalently its completed pointed \textup{\'etale} coordinate ring
is $k[[T]]$ and faithful flatness of completion gives a unique
minimal prime. Thus exactly one component passes through $q$.
Lemma~\ref{cand:closedtubeboundary} shows that the compact closure
can add only $v_\eta$. The open unit disc is not compact: the
continuous function $|T|$ has supremum $1$ and does not attain it,
as witnessed by its Gauss points of radii $r<1$. Therefore the
closure must add $v_\eta$. A compact Hausdorff space obtained by
adjoining one point to an open locally compact space is its
one-point compactification: the complements of neighborhoods of
the added point are compact subsets of the open space, and
conversely every such compact subset is closed in the compactification.}
{Corollary~\ref{cand:smoothpointpolydisctube} 给出圆盘同构。
光滑性使特殊纤维曲线在 $q$ 处的局部环成为一维正则局部环，
因而为整环；等价地，其带点 \textup{\'etale} 坐标的完备局部环
是 $k[[T]]$，由完备化的忠实平坦性可得唯一极小素理想。因此
恰有一个分量经过 $q$。Lemma~\ref{cand:closedtubeboundary}
说明紧闭包只能增加 $v_\eta$。开单位圆盘不紧：连续函数
$|T|$ 的上确界为 $1$ 却不能达到，这由半径 $r<1$ 的 Gauss
点可见。因此闭包必增加 $v_\eta$。若在开局部紧空间上添入
一点而得到紧 Hausdorff 空间，这就是其一点紧化：新增点的
邻域补集是原开空间中的紧子集，反过来每个这样的紧子集
都在紧化中闭。}

\bi{For $r<1$, the closed subdisc $|T|\leq r$ is compact, so its
complement in $C_q$ is a neighborhood of $v_\eta$. Conversely,
every compact subset of $D_q$ has a maximum $|T|<1$. These
subdiscs are therefore cofinal among compact subsets, proving
the asserted neighborhood basis.}
{对 $r<1$，闭子圆盘 $|T|\leq r$ 紧，故其在 $C_q$ 中的补集
是 $v_\eta$ 的邻域。反过来，$D_q$ 的每个紧子集都有严格
小于 $1$ 的 $|T|$ 最大值。因此这些闭子圆盘在紧子集中共尾，
从而得到所述邻域基。}
\end{proof}
