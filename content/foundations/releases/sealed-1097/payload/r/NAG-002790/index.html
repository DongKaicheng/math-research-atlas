Additivity in the verified scope; F8CA-C

F Descent of an algebraized polarized tower

Open in the English reader · PDF page 2000

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Additivity in the verified scope; F8CA-C]
\label{fnd:nag002790:antichain-additivity}
Let \(A\) be Noetherian and let \(a,b\) be non-zero-divisors. Assume
\(\Min_A(a)\cup\Min_A(b)\) is an antichain. Define the effective
cycle of a regular equation by its actual components and the ambient
lengths
\[
 \divis(a)=\sum_{p\in\Min_A(a)}
       \length_{A_p}(A_p/aA_p)[\Spec(A/p)].
\]
Then \(\divis(ab)=\divis(a)+\divis(b)\). For generalized affinoids,
the same assertion holds for the actual analytic cycles and their
verified effective coefficient transport. In particular it holds
under Lemma~\ref{lem:pure}'s hypotheses.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{lem:minimal} identifies all components on both sides,
including the converse inclusion missing from the original proof.
At a prime \(p\) in this union, each of \(A_p/(a)\) and \(A_p/(b)\)
is either zero (when the factor is a unit in \(A_p\)) or Artinian
(when \(p\) is minimal over that factor). It is minimal over every
factor it contains: otherwise a smaller minimal prime of that factor
would contradict the antichain condition. The regularity of \(b\)
makes the sequence
\[
 0\longrightarrow A/(a)\xrightarrow{\,b\,}A/(ab)
      \longrightarrow A/(b)\longrightarrow0
\]
exact. Localize at \(p\) and add finite lengths. This gives the
coefficient equality on the now identical component sets, the selected \hyperref[f8ca:algebraic-regular-antichain-additivity]{algebraic antichain clause}.
The effective analytic comparison and actual-domain compatibility
are the unchanged, independently verified portions of \hyperref[fnd:nag002772:effective-coefficient]{NAG-002772 effective coefficients} and \hyperref[fnd:nag002772:effective-domain-transport]{actual domain transport};
no new length comparison is being inferred from flatness.
\end{proof}
