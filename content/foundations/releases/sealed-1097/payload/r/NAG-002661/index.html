Support avoidance from the exact F7 support lemma; F8F7-S

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1805

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[Support avoidance from the exact F7 support lemma; F8F7-S]
\label{lem:avoid}
For the test \(\eta\) above,
\[
                   \supp(d''\eta)\cap D=\varnothing.              \tag{2.1}
\]
In particular the extension by zero
\[
       \operatorname{ext}_0\bigl(\log|f|\,\dd\eta|_{X\setminus D}\bigr)
                                                                    \tag{2.2}
\]
is an ordinary smooth compactly supported top form on \(X\).
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
At a point \(x\) of \(\supp(d''\eta)\), choose an ordinary affinoid
neighbourhood \(V=\mathcal M(A)\) carrying one unit-moment representative
of \(d''\eta\), and on which \(f\in A\) is a non-zero-divisor.
It can be chosen inside the analytic interior around \(x\).
The exact F7 lemma \src{f7gg:minimal}, lines 165--225, applies to this
\((n-1,n)\)-form: its germ is nonzero, and the evaluation kernel
\(\ker(x)\) is a minimal prime of \(A\).

A non-zero-divisor in a Noetherian ring avoids every minimal prime.
Indeed, localization at a minimal prime is a nonzero zero-dimensional
Noetherian local ring, hence Artinian by \href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00KH}{Stacks 00KH, forward direction}; an element of its maximal ideal
is nilpotent and cannot act injectively. Localization preserves
injectivity. Thus \(f\notin\ker(x)\), so \(|f(x)|>0\), proving (2.1).
Here Noetherianity is the same \src{NAG-001560} used in the F7 source.

Locality of \(d'\) gives
\(\supp(\dd\eta)\subseteq\supp(d''\eta)\).
This compact support is disjoint from \(D\). On \(X\setminus D\), the
logarithm is a smooth function of the unit \(f\); near \(D\) the tested
top form is identically zero. Gluing gives the smooth extension (2.2).
\end{proof}
