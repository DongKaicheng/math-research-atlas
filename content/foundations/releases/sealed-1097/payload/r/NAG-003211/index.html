\begin{proposition}[A supplied smooth realization]\label{f9be:smoothscope}
Suppose, in addition to the preceding algebraic data, that a good
Hausdorff pure $d$-dimensional $K$-analytic space $U$, a coherent
module $\mathcal F$ and an ordinary smooth compactly supported
$(d,d)$-form $\alpha$ are supplied, together with a proved identity
\[
                 J_R(\cX)=\int_{U/K,\mathcal F}\alpha .
\]
Let the upstairs analytic datum be its actual scalar pullback.
Then for every finite $L/K$ as above,
\[
 J_{R'}(\cY)=
       \int_{U_L/L,p^*\mathcal F}p^*\alpha,\qquad
 J_{\cY/R}=
       \int_{(U_L)_{[K]}/K,p^*\mathcal F}p^*\alpha .
\]
\end{proposition}
