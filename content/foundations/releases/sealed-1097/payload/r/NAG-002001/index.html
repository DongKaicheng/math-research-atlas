Separable rigid points in smooth spaces

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 452

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Separable rigid points in smooth spaces}{光滑空间中的可分 rigid 点}]
\label{fnd:smoothseparablerigiddensity}
\bi{Over a nontrivially valued complete field $F$, every nonempty ordinary open subset of a good smooth $F$-analytic space contains a point whose completed residue field is finite separable over $F$.}
{在赋值非平凡的完备域 $F$ 上，good 光滑 $F$-解析空间的每个非空普通开集都包含一个完备剩余域在 $F$ 上有限可分的点。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First consider a nonempty ordinary open set in $\mathbb A_F^{n,\an}$. Finite polynomial-value inequalities describe a smaller nonempty open neighborhood of any chosen point. The group $\sqrt{|F^\times|}$ is dense in $\mathbb R_{>0}$: powers with rational exponents of any fixed value different from one already are dense. Choose all upper and lower bounds for these inequalities, and the ambient polydisc radii, in this group, strictly outside the values at the point. This produces a nonempty affinoid domain inside the open set. After a finite extension realizing the finitely many radii as absolute values, its presentation is strict. A maximal ideal of this strict algebra gives, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}, a point whose coordinates are algebraic over $F$. Surjectivity of complete scalar extension ensures that the chosen domain remains nonempty.}
{先考虑 $\mathbb A_F^{n,\an}$ 中的非空普通开集。在其中任取一点，有限个多项式值不等式可描述更小的非空开邻域。群 $\sqrt{|F^\times|}$ 在 $\mathbb R_{>0}$ 中稠密，因为任一不等于一的固定值的有理指数幂已构成稠密集。把这些不等式的所有上下界及环境多圆盘半径都选在此群中，并使该点处的不等式严格，便在原开集中得到非空 affinoid 域。作有限扩域以把有限个半径实现为绝对值后，其表示变为 strict。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}，此 strict 代数的极大理想给出一个坐标在 $F$ 上代数的点；完备扩标量的满射性保证所选域仍非空。}

\bi{Separable algebraic elements are dense among algebraic elements over $F$. To see the only nontrivial case, let $a$ have inseparable monic minimal polynomial $P$ of degree $d$. Then $P'=0$. For arbitrarily small $\varepsilon\in F^\times$, the polynomial $P(T)+\varepsilon T$ is separable, as its derivative is the nonzero constant $\varepsilon$. In a finite splitting extension write its roots as $b_1,\ldots,b_d$. Evaluation at $a$ gives $\prod_i|a-b_i|=|\varepsilon a|$, so one root approaches $a$ as $\varepsilon\to0$; the case $a=0$ is already separable. Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} supplies compatible absolute values in the finite splitting extensions. Approximating the finitely many coordinates simultaneously preserves the finitely many strict polynomial inequalities. Their joint field is finite separable over $F$. This proves the assertion for affine space.}
{可分代数元素在 $F$ 上的代数元素中稠密。只须处理如下情形：元素 $a$ 的首一最小多项式 $P$ 不可分，次数为 $d$，故 $P'=0$。对任意充分小的 $\varepsilon\in F^\times$，多项式 $P(T)+\varepsilon T$ 的导数为非零常数 $\varepsilon$，所以可分。在某有限分裂扩张中记其根为 $b_1,\ldots,b_d$。在 $a$ 处求值得 $\prod_i|a-b_i|=|\varepsilon a|$，故当 $\varepsilon\to0$ 时，至少一个根趋近 $a$；$a=0$ 已可分。Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 给出有限分裂扩张上相容的绝对值。同时逼近有限个坐标，可保持有限个严格多项式不等式，所得坐标共同生成 $F$ 的有限可分扩张。这证明了仿射空间的断言。}

\bi{Now take an \'etale coordinate chart of the given smooth open set. An \'etale map is ordinary-open. Indeed, near any point it is finite flat; separate the finite fibre into branches over a small ordinary target neighborhood. A source ordinary neighborhood contains the relevant branch after a further target shrinking, by compactness and the closed-map property. The finite flat branch has open-and-closed image by Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteflatclopenimage}, so the image contains a neighborhood of the image point. Apply the affine-space assertion to this nonempty open image. Over the resulting finite separable point the \'etale fibre is a nonempty finite union of finite separable fields. Any point of it gives a finite separable extension of $F$, as required.}
{现在取给定光滑开集的一个 \'etale 坐标图。\'Etale 映射对普通拓扑是开映射。确实，它在每一点附近有限平坦；在目标的小普通邻域上将有限纤维分离为各分支。利用紧性与闭映射性质，再缩小目标后，源的给定普通邻域包含相关分支。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteflatclopenimage}，此有限平坦分支的像既开又闭，所以原像包含像点的一个邻域。将仿射空间的结论用于这个非空开像。在所得有限可分点上，\'etale 纤维是有限可分域的非空有限并，任取其中一点，其完备剩余域便在 $F$ 上有限可分。}
\end{proof}
