Complementary fans and displacement

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 887

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Complementary fans and displacement}
{互补维数扇与位移}]
\label{cand:complementaryfandisplacement}
\bi{Let $A,B$ be finite balanced fans in $N_{\mathbf R}$,
of dimensions $p,q$ with $p+q=n$. For a vector $b$ outside
the finitely many lower-dimensional images of faces under
$(x,y)\mapsto x-y$, the coefficient of their stable product
at the origin is}
{设 $A,B$ 为 $N_{\mathbf R}$ 中有限平衡扇，维数分别为
$p,q$，且 $p+q=n$。若向量 $b$ 避开
$(x,y)\mapsto x-y$ 下有限个低维面之像，则它们的稳定
乘积在原点的系数为}
\[
 \sum_{\substack{\sigma\in A^{(p)},\ \tau\in B^{(q)}\\
                 b\in\operatorname{relint}(\sigma-\tau)\\
                 N_{\sigma,\mathbf R}+N_{\tau,\mathbf R}=N_{\mathbf R}}}
       w_A(\sigma)w_B(\tau)[N:N_\sigma+N_\tau].
 \tag{F}\label{eq:candfandisplacement}
\]
\bi{In particular this sum is independent of the generic vector
$b$, including for signed real weights.}
{特别地，该和与一般向量 $b$ 无关，此结论也适用于带符号
的实权重。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $Z=A\times B$ and $f(x,y)=x-y$. Its dimension is
$n$. By Corollary~\ref{cand:polyhedralconstantdegree},
$f_*Z=c[N_{\mathbf R}]$, where $c$ is the generic weighted
degree. On a full-rank product facet, $f$ is injective because
the two direction spaces have dimensions adding to $n$.
Its lattice image is $N_\sigma+N_\tau$, and a generic inverse
image exists precisely when $b\in\operatorname{relint}(\sigma-\tau)$.
Thus $c$ is the displayed sum.}
{令 $Z=A\times B$、$f(x,y)=x-y$，则 $\dim Z=n$。
由 Corollary~\ref{cand:polyhedralconstantdegree}，有
$f_*Z=c[N_{\mathbf R}]$，其中 $c$ 为一般加权度数。在
满秩直积胞腔上，两个方向空间的维数之和为 $n$，故 $f$
单射。其格像为 $N_\sigma+N_\tau$；一般逆像存在当且仅当
$b\in\operatorname{relint}(\sigma-\tau)$。所以 $c$ 正是
所列总和。}

\bi{On $N_{\mathbf R}$ let $g_i(z)=\max(0,z_i)$.
Repeated use of Theorem~\ref{cand:cornerprojectionformula}
gives}
{在 $N_{\mathbf R}$ 上令 $g_i(z)=\max(0,z_i)$。反复应用
Theorem~\ref{cand:cornerprojectionformula} 得}
\[
 f_*\bigl(D_{g_1\circ f}\cdots D_{g_n\circ f}Z\bigr)
   =D_{g_1}\cdots D_{g_n}\bigl(c[N_{\mathbf R}]\bigr)
   =c[0].
\]
\bi{The final equality is the successive unit slope jumps
of the coordinate hyperplanes. The source is a zero-dimensional
fan supported on the diagonal, so it is a multiple of the
origin. Pushforward does not change a zero-dimensional
point weight. By the definition of the diagonal product this
multiple is also the coefficient of $A\cdot B$. It is therefore
$c$, as required.}
{最后的等式来自各坐标超平面连续的单位斜率跳跃。源循环
是支集位于对角线的零维扇，故为原点的倍数。推前不改变
零维点的权重。按对角线乘积的定义，该倍数也就是
$A\cdot B$ 的系数，因此等于 $c$。}
\end{proof}
