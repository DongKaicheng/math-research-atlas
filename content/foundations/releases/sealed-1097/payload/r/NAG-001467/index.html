A Laurent refinement of a unit-generated standard family

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 196

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A Laurent refinement of a unit-generated standard family}{单位生成的标准族的 Laurent 加细}]\label{fnd:unitstandardlaurentrefinement}
\bi{Suppose $f_0,\ldots,f_r$ are units of $A$. Form the all-sign Laurent family for the fixed elements $q_{ij}=f_i/f_j$, $0\leq i<j\leq r$, at radius one. Thus each branch $D_\epsilon$ imposes, for each pair, either $X_{ij}=q_{ij}$ or $q_{ij}X_{ij}=1$, with $\|X_{ij}\|\leq1$. For every branch there is an index $k=k(\epsilon)$ for which all $f_i/f_k$ have norm at most one in $D_\epsilon$. In particular, $R_k\to D_\epsilon$ is a contractive map and}
{假设 $f_0,\ldots,f_r$ 都是 $A$ 中的单位。对固定元素 $q_{ij}=f_i/f_j$，$0\leq i<j\leq r$，在半径一处构造 all-sign Laurent 族。具体而言，每个分支 $D_\epsilon$ 对每一对指标施加 $X_{ij}=q_{ij}$ 或 $q_{ij}X_{ij}=1$，并有 $\|X_{ij}\|\leq1$。对每个分支，都存在指标 $k=k(\epsilon)$，使 $D_\epsilon$ 中的全部 $f_i/f_k$ 的范数至多为一。因此 $R_k\to D_\epsilon$ 为 contractive 映射，且}
\[
 D_\epsilon\widehat\otimes_A R_k\simeq D_\epsilon
\]
\bi{isometrically. The same conclusion holds after imposing any additional elementary constraints on that branch.}
{为等距同构。对该分支再施加任意额外 elementary 约束之后，同一结论仍然成立。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The assertion for the zero branch has its stated degenerate meaning. Otherwise, direct an edge $i\to j$ when the chosen relation gives $\|f_i/f_j\|\leq1$, and direct $j\to i$ for the other sign. This defines one directed edge between each pair of vertices. There is a vertex reachable from every vertex by a directed path. To prove this, begin with one vertex $k$. When adding a new vertex $v$, keep $k$ if the edge is $v\to k$, and replace $k$ by $v$ if the edge is $k\to v$. The previously constructed paths followed by this last edge establish the assertion inductively.}
{零分支按上述退化约定理解。否则，若所选关系给出 $\|f_i/f_j\|\leq1$，则定向一条边 $i\to j$；另一符号对应 $j\to i$。这样，每一对顶点之间恰有一条定向边。存在一个顶点，可以从每个顶点沿有向路径到达。证明如下：从单个顶点 $k$ 开始；添入新顶点 $v$ 时，若边为 $v\to k$，则保留 $k$；若边为 $k\to v$，则将所选顶点替换为 $v$。已有路径接上这条新边，即归纳证明了断言。}

\bi{For a path $i=i_0\to i_1\to\cdots\to i_m=k$, multiply its bounded ratios. Their product is $f_i/f_k$, so submultiplicativity gives $\|f_i/f_k\|\leq1$. The bounded universal property gives the map from $R_k$, and Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalredundantconstraint} gives the isometry. Subsequent quotient maps are contractive, preserve the unit equations, and preserve the inequalities just established. Thus the conclusion persists on every further joint presentation. This is a norm calculation; it does not infer a power bound from a pointwise inequality.}
{对路径 $i=i_0\to i_1\to\cdots\to i_m=k$，将各边对应的有界比值相乘。所得积正是 $f_i/f_k$，故由次乘性，$\|f_i/f_k\|\leq1$。Bounded universal property 给出从 $R_k$ 出发的映射，Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalredundantconstraint} 给出等距同构。后续商映射均为 contractive，保持单位等式和刚得到的范数不等式，所以结论在每个后续联合表示上仍成立。这里进行的是范数计算，并未从点值不等式推断幂的界。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{We work over a complete non-Archimedean field $K$, allowing trivial valuation except in the small-scalar lemmas where nontriviality is stated. Every nonzero algebra has a strict norm-admissible Banach norm, and every relation quotient has its actual quotient norm. The zero algebra is admitted separately with its zero norm. All radii in this section are one; a radius in $|K^\times|$ can first be reduced to this convention by scaling the corresponding numerator. These conventions concern the displayed presentations, not a spectral norm.}
{本节在完备非阿基米德域 $K$ 上工作，允许赋值平凡；使用小标量的引理会明确要求赋值非平凡。每个非零代数配备 strict norm-admissible Banach 范数，每个关系商配备实际商范数。零代数另以零范数允许在内。本节所有半径均为一；值群 $|K^\times|$ 中的半径可先通过缩放相应分子化为这一约定。这些约定涉及所列表示，并不使用 spectral 范数。}

\bi{For a tuple $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$ generating the unit ideal of $A$, write}
{对生成 $A$ 的单位理想的 tuple $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$，记}
\[
 A\langle f/g\rangle
   =A\{T_1,\ldots,T_s\}/(gT_j-f_j)_{1\leq j\leq s}.
\]
\bi{For a unit-ideal tuple $f_0,\ldots,f_r$, its \emph{standard rational family} consists of the $r+1$ presentations}
{对生成单位理想的 tuple $f_0,\ldots,f_r$，其\emph{标准 rational 族}由以下 $r+1$ 个表示组成：}
\[
 R_i=A\{T_j:j\ne i\}/(f_iT_j-f_j)_{j\ne i},
 \qquad 0\leq i\leq r.
\]
\bi{We put $T_i=1$ when using a formula involving all indices. The joint presentation of a finite family means the quotient with disjoint variable blocks and all their relations. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpresentationpushout} identifies it with the separated completed tensor product. Its module attached to a finite $A$-module $M$ is its ordinary tensor with $M$, equipped with the quotient norm from a fixed finite free presentation of $M$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} gives its comparison with the completed tensor. These choices specify the arrows and norms before a geometric sheaf is available.}
{若某个公式涉及全部指标，约定 $T_i=1$。有限族的联合表示，是指添入互不相交的变量组并施加全部关系后所得的商。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpresentationpushout} 将它与 separated completed tensor product 识别。对有限 $A$-模 $M$，相应模是该商与 $M$ 的普通张量积，并配备来自 $M$ 的一个固定有限自由表示的商范数。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} 给出它与 completed tensor 的比较。因此，在尚未构造几何层之前，所有箭头及范数已有明确含义。}
