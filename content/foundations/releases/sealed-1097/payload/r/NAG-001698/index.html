Ordinary affinoid stalks

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 279

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Ordinary affinoid stalks}{Affinoid 的通常茎}]
\label{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks}
\bi{Let $X=\mathcal M(A)$, and restrict $\mathcal O_{X_G}$ to ordinary open subsets to obtain $\mathcal O_X$. For every $x\in X$,}
{设 $X=\mathcal M(A)$，将 $\mathcal O_{X_G}$ 限制到通常开集，得到 $\mathcal O_X$。对每个 $x\in X$，}
\[
 \mathcal O_{X,x}
   \simeq\varinjlim_{\substack{V\text{ rational in }X\\
                              x\in\operatorname{int}_{\rm top}(V)}} A_V,
\]
\bi{where the system is directed by shrinking. Evaluation defines a local ring structure: its maximal ideal consists of germs vanishing at $x$. Its residue field $\kappa_X(x)$ embeds isometrically in $\mathcal H_A(x)$ and has completion $\mathcal H_A(x)$.}
{其中按缩小邻域构成有向系统。取值使此茎成为局部环，其极大理想由在 $x$ 处取值为零的芽组成。剩余域 $\kappa_X(x)$ 等距嵌入 $\mathcal H_A(x)$，且其完备化为 $\mathcal H_A(x)$。}
\end{proposition}
Selected proof part 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidanalyticdomaincalculus} constructs, inside every ordinary neighborhood of $x$, a rational domain containing an ordinary neighborhood of $x$. Finite intersections have the same property. Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidfullgcomparison} identifies the sections on these domains with $A_V$. A section on an ordinary open neighborhood restricts to one of them. Conversely, a section on such a $V$ restricts to its ordinary interior near $x$. These restrictions induce mutually inverse maps on germs, proving the displayed formula.}
{Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidanalyticdomaincalculus} 在 $x$ 的每个通常邻域中构造一个包含 $x$ 的通常邻域的 rational 域。有限交叠仍具有同样性质。Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidfullgcomparison} 将这些域上的截面识别为 $A_V$。通常开邻域上的截面可限制到其中一个域；反之，这样一个 $V$ 上的截面可限制到 $x$ 附近的通常内部。它们在芽上给出互逆映射，证明所列公式。}
Selected proof part 2 · exact TeX
\bi{By Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalresiduefields}, the evaluations on all $A_V$ take values compatibly in $\mathcal H_A(x)$, so they define a ring map $\mathcal O_{X,x}\to\mathcal H_A(x)$. If a germ is represented by $b\in A_V$ with $b(x)\ne0$, choose $0<c<|b(x)|$. The Laurent domain $|b|\geq c$ in $V$ contains a neighborhood of $x$ in $X$ and has an inverse for $b$ in its algebra. Hence the germ is a unit. A germ vanishing at $x$ cannot be a unit, since evaluation is a unital map into a field. The kernel of evaluation is consequently exactly the unique maximal ideal.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalresiduefields}，全部 $A_V$ 的取值相容地落在 $\mathcal H_A(x)$ 中，因此定义环映射 $\mathcal O_{X,x}\to\mathcal H_A(x)$。若某个芽由 $b\in A_V$ 表示，且 $b(x)\ne0$，选择 $0<c<|b(x)|$。$V$ 中的 Laurent 域 $|b|\geq c$ 包含 $x$ 在 $X$ 中的一个邻域，且其代数包含 $b$ 的逆元，所以该芽为单位。若芽在 $x$ 消失，则它不可能为单位，因为取值是到域的保单位映射。因此取值的核恰为唯一极大理想。}

\bi{It follows that $\kappa_X(x)$ is identified with the evaluated image, a subfield of $\mathcal H_A(x)$. It contains $\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$: each denominator nonzero at $x$ has the local inverse just constructed. This fraction field is dense in $\mathcal H_A(x)$ by definition. The intermediate field $\kappa_X(x)$ is therefore dense as well, proving the completion assertion.}
{于是，取值将 $\kappa_X(x)$ 识别为 $\mathcal H_A(x)$ 的子域。每个在 $x$ 非零的分母都有上述局部逆元，故 $\kappa_X(x)$ 包含 $\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$。按定义，这个分式域在 $\mathcal H_A(x)$ 中稠密，所以中间域 $\kappa_X(x)$ 也稠密，得到完备化断言。}
