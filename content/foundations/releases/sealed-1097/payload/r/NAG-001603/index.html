Gauss or rational points after finite constants

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 616

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Gauss or rational points after finite constants}
{有限常数扩张后的 Gauss 点或有理点}]
\label{fnd:trivialfieldsmoothgausspoint}
\bi{Let $F$ be trivially valued, and let $X$ be a nonempty
smooth $F$-analytic space. There exist a finite extension
$K/F$ and $z\in X_K$ such that $\mathcal H(z)$ is
isometrically $K$ or $K_r$ for some $0<r<1$.
The image of $z$ can be required to lie in any prescribed
nonempty ordinary open subset of $X$.}
{设 $F$ 平凡赋值，$X$ 为非空光滑 $F$-解析空间。
则存在有限扩张 $K/F$ 及点 $z\in X_K$，
使 $\mathcal H(z)$ 等距同构于 $K$ 或某个 $K_r$，
其中 $0<r<1$。还可要求 $z$ 的像属于 $X$ 中任意预定的
非空普通开集。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Replace $X$ by the prescribed open subset.
Fix $0<s<1$. Scalar extension is surjective,
and smoothness is preserved by base change; hence
$X_{F_s}$ is nonempty and smooth.
By Lemma~\ref{fnd:smoothseparablerigiddensity},
it has a point $y$ for which
$L=\mathcal H(y)$ is finite separable over $F_s$.
Write $x$ for its image in $X$ and
$E=\mathcal H(x)\subset L$. If $E$ is trivially valued,
it is finite over $F$: its residue field is a subfield
of $\widetilde L$, which is finite over $F$ by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitevaluedindexbound}.
Take $K=E$; the diagonal evaluation gives a
$K$-rational point over $x$.}
{先将 $X$ 替换为预定开集。固定 $0<s<1$。
扩标量满射，且光滑性在基变换下保持，
故 $X_{F_s}$ 非空且光滑。
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:smoothseparablerigiddensity}，
其中有一点 $y$，使
$L=\mathcal H(y)$ 在 $F_s$ 上有限可分。
令 $x$ 为其在 $X$ 中的像，并记
$E=\mathcal H(x)\subset L$。
若 $E$ 平凡赋值，则 $E/F$ 有限，因为其剩余域是
$\widetilde L$ 的子域，而由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitevaluedindexbound}，
$\widetilde L/F$ 有限。此时取 $K=E$，
对角取值给出 $x$ 上方的一个 $K$-有理点。}

\bi{Suppose now that $E$ is nontrivially valued.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:laurentconstantuniformization} gives
a finite $K/F$ and a quotient
$L\otimes_F K\twoheadrightarrow M=K_{r_0}$
whose map on $L$ is isometric.
Let $E_K$ be the image of $E\otimes_F K$ in $M$.
This image is a finite-dimensional $E$-algebra and a domain,
so is a field and is complete by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}.
Its value group is a nontrivial subgroup of
the cyclic group $|M^\times|$. Its residue field is
exactly $K$, since it is a subfield of $\widetilde M=K$
and contains the constant field $K$.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:discretecoefficientlaurent} therefore gives
$E_K=K_r$ isometrically, for some $0<r<1$.}
{再设 $E$ 非平凡赋值。由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:laurentconstantuniformization}，
存在有限 $K/F$ 及商映射
$L\otimes_F K\twoheadrightarrow M=K_{r_0}$，
其在 $L$ 上等距。令 $E_K$ 为 $E\otimes_F K$ 在 $M$ 中的像。
此像为有限维 $E$-代数且为整环，故为域，
并由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 完备。
它的值群是循环群 $|M^\times|$ 的非平凡子群。
其剩余域恰为 $K$，因为它是 $\widetilde M=K$ 的子域，
且包含常数域 $K$。因此
Lemma~\ref{fnd:discretecoefficientlaurent}
给出某个 $0<r<1$，使 $E_K=K_r$ 等距成立。}

\bi{It remains to check that this field is an actual
completed residue field on $X_K$.
On an affinoid domain $\mathcal M(A)$ through $x$,
compose evaluation with $A\otimes_F K\to E_K$.
The map is bounded and extends to the completed tensor
product: if $e_1,\ldots,e_d$ is an $F$-basis of $K$,
then the norm of $\sum a_i e_i$ in the target is at most
$\max_i|a_i(x)|$, since the nonzero constants have norm one;
this is bounded by a finite-module tensor norm.
It defines a point $z$ over $x$.
The fraction field of its evaluation image contains the
image of $\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$ and $K$.
The first is dense in $E$; finite sums then show that
the generated field is dense in $E_K$.
Conversely $E_K$ is already complete. Thus
$\mathcal H(z)=E_K=K_r$ as asserted.}
{最后验证该域确实是 $X_K$ 上某点的完备剩余域。
在包含 $x$ 的 affinoid 域 $\mathcal M(A)$ 上，
将取值与 $A\otimes_F K\to E_K$ 复合。
此映射有界，并延拓到完备张量积：
若 $e_1,\ldots,e_d$ 是 $K$ 的 $F$-基，
则 $\sum a_i e_i$ 在目标中的范数至多为
$\max_i|a_i(x)|$，因为每个非零常数的范数为一；
此值受一个有限模张量范数控制。
它定义 $x$ 上方的点 $z$。
其取值像的分式域包含 $\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$
的像及 $K$。前者在 $E$ 中稠密，取有限和即知
这些元素生成的域在 $E_K$ 中稠密。
反之，$E_K$ 已经完备。
所以 $\mathcal H(z)=E_K=K_r$，如所需。}
\end{proof}
