A reduced special fibre determines the unit ball

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 651

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A reduced special fibre determines the unit ball}
{约化特殊纤维确定单位球}]
\label{cand:reducedspecialcanonical}
\bi{Let $A_0$ be an admissible $O$-algebra, let
$A=A_0[1/\pi]$, and assume that $A_0/\mathfrak mA_0$ is reduced.
Then the quotient norm associated to any admissible integral
presentation of $A_0$ is power-multiplicative and equals the spectral
radius. Moreover,}
{设 $A_0$ 为 admissible $O$-代数，$A=A_0[1/\pi]$，并假设
$A_0/\mathfrak mA_0$ 约化。则 $A_0$ 的任一 admissible
整系数表示给出的商范数具有幂乘性，且等于谱半径。此外，}
\[
 A^\circ=A_0,\qquad A^{\circ\circ}=\mathfrak mA_0,
 \qquad \widetilde A=A_0/\mathfrak mA_0.
\]
\bi{In particular, both $A$ and $A_0$ are reduced.}
{特别地，$A$ 与 $A_0$ 均约化。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations}, including
the norm-attaining lifts in its proof. It identifies $A_0$ with
the quotient closed unit ball. For nonzero $a\in A$, its norm is
attained by a restricted power-series lift, whose nonzero norm is
the maximum of its coefficient absolute values. Thus
$\|a\|\in|K^\times|$. It follows that}
{使用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations} 及其证明
中达到商范数的提升。该命题将 $A_0$ 识别为商范数的闭单位球。
对非零 $a\in A$，其范数由某个受限幂级数提升达到，而该提升
的非零范数是其系数绝对值的最大值。因此
$\|a\|\in|K^\times|$。于是}
\[
 \mathfrak mA_0=\{a\in A:\|a\|<1\}.
\]
\bi{One inclusion follows from the ultrametric inequality for
finite sums. For the other, choose $c\in K$ with
$|c|=\|a\|<1$; then $a/c\in A_0$. The case $a=0$ is immediate.}
{一个包含来自有限和的超度量不等式。反向包含中，选择
$c\in K$ 使 $|c|=\|a\|<1$，则 $a/c\in A_0$；$a=0$
的情形直接成立。}

\bi{For any nonzero $a$, choose $c\in K^\times$ with
$|c|=\|a\|$ and put $b=a/c$. Its class in
$A_0/\mathfrak mA_0$ is nonzero. Reducedness implies that every
positive power of this class is nonzero. Since $\|b^j\|\leq1$,
the displayed characterization of $\mathfrak mA_0$ gives
$\|b^j\|=1$ for all $j\geq1$. Consequently}
{对任意非零 $a$，选取满足 $|c|=\|a\|$ 的
$c\in K^\times$，并令 $b=a/c$。它在
$A_0/\mathfrak mA_0$ 中的类非零。由约化性，此类的每个
正整数次幂均非零。又因 $\|b^j\|\leq1$，上述
$\mathfrak mA_0$ 的刻画给出所有 $j\geq1$ 时
$\|b^j\|=1$。所以}
\[
 \|a^j\|=\|a\|^j,\qquad
 \rho_A(a)=\lim_{j\to\infty}\|a^j\|^{1/j}=\|a\|.
\]
\bi{A nonzero nilpotent is incompatible with this identity.
Power-bounded elements are exactly those of norm at most one,
and topologically nilpotent elements are exactly those of norm
less than one. This proves all three identifications.}
{该恒等式排除非零幂零元。幂有界元素恰为范数至多为一的
元素，拓扑幂零元素恰为范数小于一的元素，由此得到全部
三个识别。}
\end{proof}
