The correspondence for pointed semistable formal models

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 761

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[The correspondence for pointed semistable formal models]
\label{v035:f5:formalcorrespondence}
Let $K$ be algebraically closed, complete and nontrivially valued, and
let $C/K$ be a smooth connected projective curve. The vertex-set
functor is an equivalence between the pointedly semistable formal
models of Definition~\ref{v035:formal:modelclass} and the partially
ordered set of semistable vertex sets, with a unique arrow
$W\to V$ when $V\subset W$.
Every such formal model is isomorphic over $C^{\an}$ to the completion
of a split nodal algebraic model. Its skeleton and logarithmic edge
lengths are those of its semistable vertex set.
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
By Corollary~\ref{v035:formal:partition}, a pointedly semistable
formal model has a semistable vertex set $V$ and the exact tube
boundaries required in Proposition~\ref{v035:f5:vertexuniqueness}.
Theorem~\ref{v035:f5:exactrealization} constructs a split nodal
algebraic model $\mathcal C_V$ with that vertex set.
Its completion belongs to the pointed formal class. Indeed, complete
the pointed algebraic smooth and nodal charts $\pi$-adically.
Their rings are admissible by Lemma~\ref{cand:affineflatformalcompletion}.
The completed maps are topologically finitely presented. They are
formally \'etale because, on a discrete target annihilated by a power
of $\pi$, maps from a completed ring factor through its corresponding
algebraic quotient, where the algebraic \'etale lifting property
\cite[Tag 00UR]{StacksAlg} gives the unique lift.

Proposition~\ref{v035:f5:vertexuniqueness} now identifies the given
formal model with $\widehat{\mathcal C_V}$ over $C^{\an}$.
The existence and uniqueness of arrows are consequently
Theorem~\ref{v035:f5:modelvertexcorrespondence}; alternatively the
necessary vertex inclusion is already
Lemma~\ref{v035:f5:dominationvertices}.
The skeletal statement follows from the same identification and
the final assertion of that theorem.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\begin{definition}[Semistable formal curve models with pointed charts]
\label{v035:formal:modelclass}
Suppose $K$ is algebraically closed and $C/K$ is a smooth
connected projective curve. A point of $C^{\an}$ is of
\emph{type II} when its completed residue field has residual
transcendence degree one and relative rational value-group
rank zero. A \emph{pointedly semistable
formal model} of $C^{\an}$ is a quasi-compact admissible
formal $O$-scheme with an identification of its generic
fibre with $C^{\an}$, reduced pure one-dimensional special
fibre, and a diagram as in
Proposition~\ref{v035:formal:tubeends} at each closed
special-fibre point. Morphisms induce the identity on
$C^{\an}$. Its vertex set consists of its component
points $v_\eta$.
\end{definition}
