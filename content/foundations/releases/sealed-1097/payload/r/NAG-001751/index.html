Quasi-smoothness, flatness and geometric regularity

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 318

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Quasi-smoothness, flatness and geometric regularity}{准光滑、平坦与几何正则性}]
\label{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion}
\bi{For a morphism $f:Y\to X$ of analytic spaces, quasi-smoothness at $y$ is equivalent to analytic flatness at $y$ together with geometric regularity at $y$ of the $\mathcal H(f(y))$-analytic fibre. For good $Y,X$, whenever these conditions hold one may choose the Jacobian presentation with its source an ordinary affinoid neighborhood of $y$. More precisely, after fixing any relative affinoid presentation near $y$, one can restrict it to an ordinary rational neighborhood of $y$ in its ambient polydisc so that the relation ideal is generated by $n-\operatorname{rk}_y\Omega_{Y/X}$ elements with independent relative differentials.}
{对解析空间态射 $f:Y\to X$，$y$ 处准光滑等价于 $y$ 处解析平坦，并且 $\mathcal H(f(y))$-解析纤维在 $y$ 处几何正则。若 $Y,X$ good，且这些条件成立，则可将 Jacobian 表示的源取为 $y$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域。更精确地，固定 $y$ 附近的任一相对 affinoid 表示后，可将其限制到环境多圆盘中 $y$ 的普通 rational 邻域，使关系理想由 $n-\operatorname{rk}_y\Omega_{Y/X}$ 个相对微分独立的元素生成。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{\textbf{A Jacobian chart is analytically flat.}
Start with a presentation $W\hookrightarrow D\subset\mathbb A_X^{n,\an}$ whose ideal is generated by $r$ functions with independent relative differentials at $y$. First treat the good chart itself, without assuming that $W$ is an ordinary neighborhood in any larger source. After any good analytic base change over any complete extension, the same equations and an invertible Jacobian minor give a presentation at each point over $y$, by Proposition~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialbasechange} and the actual domain base-change theorem. At such a point $v$, extend the ground field to a nontrivially valued complete field $\Omega$ containing $\mathcal H(v)$. Evaluation gives an $\Omega$-rational lift $w$. The base point is then $\Omega$-rational as well. Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationaljacobiancompletion} proves naive flatness at $w$. Both scalar-stalk maps are faithfully flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}, so Lemma~\ref{fnd:flatlocalsquaredescent} descends naive flatness to $v$. All the tests in Definition~\ref{fnd:analyticflatness} have now been verified.}
{\textbf{Jacobian 图解析平坦。}
从表示 $W\hookrightarrow D\subset\mathbb A_X^{n,\an}$ 出发，其理想由 $r$ 个在 $y$ 处相对微分独立的函数生成。先处理此 good 图本身，不假设 $W$ 在某个更大源空间中为普通邻域。在任意完备扩域上作任意 good 解析基变换后，由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialbasechange} 及实际域基变换定理，同样的方程与可逆 Jacobian 子式在 $y$ 上方每个点仍给出表示。对这样的点 $v$，再把基域扩张到一个包含 $\mathcal H(v)$ 且赋值非平凡的完备域 $\Omega$；取值得到 $\Omega$-有理提升 $w$，其底点也为 $\Omega$-有理点。Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationaljacobiancompletion} 证明 $w$ 处的朴素平坦性。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}，两个基域茎映射均忠实平坦，故 Lemma~\ref{fnd:flatlocalsquaredescent} 将朴素平坦性下降到 $v$。至此已核验 Definition~\ref{fnd:analyticflatness} 的全部测试。}

\bi{\textbf{Its fibres are geometrically regular.}
Let $F$ be any complete extension of $\mathcal H(f(y))$, and take any point $v$ of the extended fibre above $y$. The pulled-back chart is still a Jacobian presentation over $F$. Choose a nontrivially valued complete $\Omega\supset\mathcal H(v)$ and its rational lift. The completed local ring at that lift is $\Omega[[T_1,\ldots,T_{n-r}]]$ by Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationaljacobiancompletion}. Thus its local ring is regular. Its faithfully flat local map from the original fibre stalk has Noetherian source and target, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:ordinaryanalyticstalknoetherian}. Regularity descends by \cite[Tag 00OF]{StacksAlg}. This proves geometric regularity with every complete-extension test.}
{\textbf{其纤维几何正则。}
取 $\mathcal H(f(y))$ 的任意完备扩张 $F$，以及扩张后纤维中位于 $y$ 上方的任一点 $v$。拉回后的图仍是 $F$ 上的 Jacobian 表示。选择赋值非平凡的完备域 $\Omega\supset\mathcal H(v)$ 及相应有理提升。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationaljacobiancompletion}，该提升处的完备局部环为 $\Omega[[T_1,\ldots,T_{n-r}]]$，所以其局部环正则。从原纤维茎到此局部环的局部映射忠实平坦，且由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:ordinaryanalyticstalknoetherian}，源与目标均 Noetherian。\cite[Tag 00OF]{StacksAlg} 因而使正则性下降。这核验了几何正则性的全部完备扩域测试。}

\bi{If the original quasi-smooth presentation uses only an affinoid domain $W\subset Y$ containing $y$, analytic flatness descends from $W$ to the good source $Y$ by Corollary~\ref{fnd:sourcedomainflatnessdetection}. On each complete-extension fibre, the inverse image of $W$ is a domain containing every point over $y$. Its ordinary local map from the ambient fibre is faithfully flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:alldomainanalyticflatness}. Applying \cite[Tag 00OF]{StacksAlg} again descends regularity from this domain to that fibre. Thus the preceding conclusions hold for the original source, not only for the chosen presentation.}
{若原准光滑表示仅使用包含 $y$ 的 affinoid 域 $W\subset Y$，则由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:sourcedomainflatnessdetection}，解析平坦性从 $W$ 下降到 good 源 $Y$。在每个完备扩域纤维上，$W$ 的逆像是包含 $y$ 上方全部点的域。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:alldomainanalyticflatness}，从环境纤维的通常局部环到该域局部环的映射忠实平坦。再次应用 \cite[Tag 00OF]{StacksAlg}，便将正则性从此域下降到纤维。因此前面的结论适用于原源空间，而不只是所选表示。}

\bi{\textbf{Recovering a Jacobian presentation.}
Now suppose $Y,X$ good, $f$ analytically flat at $y$, and its analytic fibre geometrically regular at $y$. Choose ordinary affinoid neighborhoods $Y_0=\mathcal M(B)$, $X_0=\mathcal M(A)$ with $f(Y_0)\subset X_0$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepresentation} gives $B=P/I$, where $P=A\{s^{-1}T_1,\ldots,s_n^{-1}T_n\}$. Let $r$ be the rank at $y$ of the relative differentials of $I$, so that $r=n-\operatorname{rk}_y\Omega_{B/A}$ by Proposition~\ref{fnd:analyticconormaldescriptions}. Choose $g_1,\ldots,g_r\in I$ with independent such differentials, put $J=(g_1,\ldots,g_r)$, and let $Z=\mathcal M(P/J)$. The image point, still denoted $y$, has the same completed residue field. An invertible Jacobian minor makes $Z\to X_0$ quasi-smooth at $y$, so the forward part already proved applies to it.}
{\textbf{恢复 Jacobian 表示。}
现设 $Y,X$ good，$f$ 在 $y$ 处解析平坦，且其解析纤维在 $y$ 处几何正则。选择普通 affinoid 邻域 $Y_0=\mathcal M(B)$、$X_0=\mathcal M(A)$，使 $f(Y_0)\subset X_0$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepresentation}，可写 $B=P/I$，其中 $P=A\{s^{-1}T_1,\ldots,s_n^{-1}T_n\}$。令 $r$ 为 $I$ 的相对微分在 $y$ 处的秩；由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:analyticconormaldescriptions}，有 $r=n-\operatorname{rk}_y\Omega_{B/A}$。选取 $g_1,\ldots,g_r\in I$，使这些相对微分独立，令 $J=(g_1,\ldots,g_r)$，并记 $Z=\mathcal M(P/J)$。对应像点仍记 $y$，其完备剩余域不变。可逆 Jacobian 子式说明 $Z\to X_0$ 在 $y$ 处准光滑，因此可使用已证明的正向结论。}

\bi{Choose a nontrivially valued complete $\Omega\supset\mathcal H(y)$, with rational lifts $u\in Y_{0,\Omega}\subset Z_\Omega$ and $v\in X_{0,\Omega}$. Put}
{选择赋值非平凡的完备域 $\Omega\supset\mathcal H(y)$，并取有理提升 $u\in Y_{0,\Omega}\subset Z_\Omega$、$v\in X_{0,\Omega}$。记}
\[
 R=\mathcal O_{X_{0,\Omega},v},\quad
 S=\mathcal O_{Z_\Omega,u},\quad
 T=\mathcal O_{Y_{0,\Omega},u},\quad
 N=\ker(S\longrightarrow T).
\]
\bi{These rings are Noetherian, and $N$ is a finite $S$-module. The $R$-module $T$ is flat, by the assumed analytic flatness. The two fibre local rings}
{这些环均 Noetherian，且 $N$ 是有限 $S$-模。由所假设的解析平坦性，$T$ 作为 $R$-模平坦。两个纤维局部环}
\[
 S/\mathfrak m_R S,\qquad T/\mathfrak m_R T
\]
\bi{are regular. For the first this follows from Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationaljacobiancompletion}; for the second it is the assumed geometric regularity, using the identification of this fibre with the scalar extension of $Y_{f(y)}$ by associativity of completed base change. Both have embedding dimension $n-r$, by Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalfibrestalkquotient} and invariance of the Jacobian rank under the field embedding. Their dimensions therefore both equal $n-r$. The surjection between them has zero kernel by \cite[Tag 00NR]{StacksAlg}: an ideal with regular quotient of the same dimension is generated by zero members of a regular parameter system.}
{均正则。第一个环由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationaljacobiancompletion} 正则；第二个环由所假设的几何正则性正则，这里利用完备基变换的结合律，将此纤维识别为 $Y_{f(y)}$ 的基域扩张。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalfibrestalkquotient} 及域嵌入下 Jacobian 秩的不变性，二者的嵌入维数均为 $n-r$，所以维数也都等于 $n-r$。\cite[Tag 00NR]{StacksAlg} 说明二者之间的满射核为零：若正则局部环的商仍正则且同维，其核由正则参数系中零个元素生成。}

\bi{Let \(R\) be a commutative ring and let
\(0\to A\to B\to C\to0\) be an exact sequence of \(R\)-modules
with \(C\) flat. For every \(R\)-module \(X\), tensoring the sequence
with \(X\) is exact. To check injectivity on the left, choose a free
module \(F\) mapping onto \(X\), with kernel \(G\). Lift an element of
\(X\otimes_R A\) that dies in \(X\otimes_R B\) to \(v\in F\otimes_R A\).
Its image in \(F\otimes_R B\) comes from some \(w\in G\otimes_R B\).
The image of \(w\) in \(G\otimes_R C\) is zero because it is zero in
\(F\otimes_R C\) and \(C\) is flat. Right exactness lifts \(w\) to
\(G\otimes_R A\). Subtract the image of this lift from \(v\).
The difference vanishes in \(F\otimes_R B\), hence in \(F\otimes_R A\)
since \(F\) is free. Thus \(v\) comes from \(G\otimes_R A\), and the
original element of \(X\otimes_R A\) is zero.

If \(A\) is also flat, then \(B\) is flat. Given an injection
\(X\hookrightarrow Y\), tensor the short exact sequence with \(X\)
and with \(Y\), using the first assertion for the left injections.
An element of \(X\otimes_R B\) that dies in \(Y\otimes_R B\) maps to
zero in \(X\otimes_R C\), by flatness of \(C\). It comes from
\(X\otimes_R A\). The image of this preimage in \(Y\otimes_R A\) is zero
by the left injection in the row for \(Y\); flatness of \(A\) kills
the preimage. Hence the induced map on tensoring with \(B\) is
injective for every injection, which is flatness of \(B\).

Apply the first assertion to $0\to N\to S\to T\to0$ and $X=R/\mathfrak m_R$. The quotient $T$ is flat over $R$, so $N/\mathfrak m_RN$ injects into the zero fibre kernel just computed. Hence $N=\mathfrak m_RN$. Because $\mathfrak m_RS\subset\mathfrak m_S$ and $N$ is finite, $N=0$. Explicitly, writing finite generators in terms of themselves with coefficients in $\mathfrak m_RS$ gives a matrix $C$ with entries in $\mathfrak m_S$; the adjugate identity for $1-C$ and the unit $\det(1-C)$ annihilate every generator.}
{将 $0\to N\to S\to T\to0$ 在 $R$ 上与 $R/\mathfrak m_R=\Omega$ 张量。由于 $T$ 在 $R$ 上平坦，其一阶 Tor 群为零，因此 $N/\mathfrak m_RN$ 单射到刚才算得的零核中，故 $N=\mathfrak m_RN$。又因 $\mathfrak m_RS\subset\mathfrak m_S$ 且 $N$ 有限，所以 $N=0$。具体地，将有限组生成元写为系数属于 $\mathfrak m_RS$ 的自身线性组合，得到元素属于 $\mathfrak m_S$ 的矩阵 $C$；对 $1-C$ 使用伴随矩阵恒等式，而 $\det(1-C)$ 为单位，即迫使每个生成元为零。}

\bi{The coherent ideal $I/J$ on $Z$ thus has zero scalar-pullback stalk at $u$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat} identifies that stalk with its tensor product over $\mathcal O_{Z,y}$ with the faithfully flat $\mathcal O_{Z_\Omega,u}$. Consequently $(I/J)_y=0$. Its finite generators vanish on an ordinary neighborhood $N_y$ in $Z$. Since $Z$ is closed in the ambient polydisc, choose an ordinary open neighborhood of $y$ in that polydisc whose intersection with $Z$ is contained in $N_y$, and then a rational affinoid neighborhood $D'$ inside it. On $D'$, the two closed subspaces $Y_0$ and $Z$ coincide, with ideal generated by $g_1,\ldots,g_r$. Their intersection with $D'$ is an ordinary affinoid neighborhood of $y$ in $Y$, and the minor stays nonzero at $y$. This is the asserted strengthened Jacobian presentation.}
{因此，$Z$ 上的凝聚理想 $I/J$ 在 $u$ 处的基域拉回茎为零。Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat} 将该茎识别为其在 $\mathcal O_{Z,y}$ 上与忠实平坦的 $\mathcal O_{Z_\Omega,u}$ 的张量积，所以 $(I/J)_y=0$。它的有限生成元在 $Z$ 中的某个普通邻域 $N_y$ 上消失。由于 $Z$ 在环境多圆盘中闭，可在该多圆盘中选择 $y$ 的普通开邻域，使其与 $Z$ 的交包含于 $N_y$，再在其中选择 rational affinoid 邻域 $D'$。在 $D'$ 上，两个闭子空间 $Y_0$ 与 $Z$ 相同，其理想由 $g_1,\ldots,g_r$ 生成。它们与 $D'$ 的交是 $y$ 在 $Y$ 中的普通 affinoid 邻域，而所选子式在 $y$ 仍非零。这就是所述加强的 Jacobian 表示。}

\bi{For general analytic spaces, choose affinoid domains in source and target and use the $G$-local definition of analytic flatness. Quasi-smoothness is invariant under these restrictions by Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobiansmoothdomainbasechange}. Completed residue fields on domains agree, so the local analytic fibres are precisely analytic domains in the original fibre. In the forward direction the chart argument above proves regularity on all such domains after all complete extensions. In the reverse direction geometric regularity of the fibre supplies regularity on its affinoid charts, while analytic flatness supplies the good-space tests there. The good case therefore applies on every required chart and proves the general equivalence.}
{对一般解析空间，在源与目标中选择 affinoid 域，并使用解析平坦性的 $G$-局部定义。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobiansmoothdomainbasechange}，准光滑性在这些限制下保持。域上的完备剩余域相同，因此局部解析纤维恰是原纤维中的解析域。正向论证在每次完备扩域后，对所有这样的域证明正则性。反向论证中，纤维的几何正则性提供其 affinoid 图上的正则性，而解析平坦性提供这些图上的 good 空间测试。因此可在全部所需图上应用已证 good 情形，得到一般等价。}
\end{proof}
