Regular comparison over an affinoid base

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 357

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Regular comparison over an affinoid base}{Affinoid 底上的正则比较}]
\label{fnd:relativealgebraicregularcomparison}
\bi{Let $R$ be a finite-type algebra over a generalized affinoid algebra $A$, let $Z=\operatorname{Spec}(R)$, and let $V=\mathcal M(C)$ be an affinoid domain in $Z^{\an}$. For $x\in V$, put $\xi=\ker_R(x)$ and $\mathfrak c=\ker_C(x)$. Every arrow in the following ring diagram is regular:}
{设 $R$ 为 generalized affinoid 代数 $A$ 上的有限型代数，$Z=\operatorname{Spec}(R)$，且 $V=\mathcal M(C)$ 为 $Z^{\an}$ 中的 affinoid 域。对 $x\in V$，令 $\xi=\ker_R(x)$、$\mathfrak c=\ker_C(x)$。以下环图中的每个箭头都正则：}
\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
 R_{\xi}&\longrightarrow&C_{\mathfrak c}\\
 \big\downarrow&&\big\downarrow\\
 \mathcal O_{Z^{\an},x}&\longrightarrow&\mathcal O_{V,x},
\end{array}
\qquad
                 R_{\xi}\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{V,x}.
\]
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{All four rings are Noetherian. The relative analytification construction proves flatness of the upper and left arrows, and the right arrow is regular by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedordinaryregularcomparison}. The bottom arrow is a flat local map by analytic-domain stalk flatness. It remains to verify geometric regularity; throughout we test the residue field at every algebraic prime, not only primes supplied by analytic fibres.}
{四个环都 Noetherian。相对解析化构造给出上箭头及左箭头的平坦性；右箭头由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedordinaryregularcomparison} 正则。下箭头由解析域茎平坦性为平坦局部映射。还需核验几何正则性；以下始终测试每个代数素理想处的剩余域，而不只测试解析纤维提供的素理想。}

\bi{First consider the diagonal map $R_{\xi}\to S=\mathcal O_{V,x}$. Choose $\mathfrak p\subset\xi$ in $R$ and a finite purely inseparable extension $E/\operatorname{Frac}(R/\mathfrak p)$. Clearing denominators in the power equations gives a finite radicial domain $B$ over $R/\mathfrak p$ with fraction field $E$. The finite analytification of $\operatorname{Spec}(B)\to Z$ has a unique point $y$ above $x$; the inverse image $W$ of $V$ is affinoid, and finite branch separation gives}
{先考虑对角映射 $R_{\xi}\to S=\mathcal O_{V,x}$。选择 $R$ 中的 $\mathfrak p\subset\xi$ 及有限纯不可分扩张 $E/\operatorname{Frac}(R/\mathfrak p)$。清除幂方程中的分母，得到 $R/\mathfrak p$ 上分式域为 $E$ 的有限根式整环 $B$。有限态射 $\operatorname{Spec}(B)\to Z$ 的解析化在 $x$ 上方只有一个点 $y$；$V$ 的逆像 $W$ 为 affinoid，且有限分支分离给出}
\[
                  S\otimes_R E
                       =\mathcal O_{W,y}\otimes_B E.
\]
\bi{Write $W=\mathcal M(D)$ and let $\mathfrak Q$ be any prime of $\mathcal O_{W,y}$ surviving this localization. Its contraction to $B$ is zero. Its contraction $\mathfrak q$ to $D$ therefore lies above the regular generic point of the integral scheme $\operatorname{Spec}(B)$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:relativeanalyticdomainregularlocalizations} proves that $D_{\mathfrak q}$ is regular. The regular map from $D$ to the ordinary local ring at $y$, localized at $\mathfrak Q$, has regular source and regular closed fibre. Tag 031E shows that $(\mathcal O_{W,y})_{\mathfrak Q}$ is regular. Thus the displayed $E$-fibre is regular at every prime. Tag 0381 proves geometric regularity of all fibres of the diagonal map.}
{写 $W=\mathcal M(D)$，并取 $\mathcal O_{W,y}$ 中在此局部化后仍存在的任意素理想 $\mathfrak Q$。它收缩到 $B$ 为零。因此，它在 $D$ 中的收缩 $\mathfrak q$ 位于整概形 $\operatorname{Spec}(B)$ 的正则泛点上方。Lemma~\ref{fnd:relativeanalyticdomainregularlocalizations} 说明 $D_{\mathfrak q}$ 正则。把 $D$ 到 $y$ 处通常局部环的正则映射在 $\mathfrak Q$ 处局部化，所得映射具有正则的源和正则的闭纤维。Tag 031E 给出 $(\mathcal O_{W,y})_{\mathfrak Q}$ 正则。因此所列 $E$-纤维在每个素理想处都正则。Tag 0381 遂证明对角映射的全部纤维几何正则。}

\bi{Next consider the bottom arrow. Replace $Z^{\an}$ by an ordinary affinoid neighborhood $X_0=\mathcal M(A_0)$ of $x$, and replace $V$ by an ordinary affinoid neighborhood of $x$ inside $V\cap X_0$. These replacements do not change either ordinary stalk. Put $R_0=\mathcal O_{X_0,x}$. For a prime $\mathfrak P\subset R_0$ and a finite purely inseparable extension $E/\kappa(\mathfrak P)$, Proposition~\ref{fnd:finitefieldtestrealization}, applied to the domain inclusion, realizes the test as}
{再考虑下箭头。以 $x$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域 $X_0=\mathcal M(A_0)$ 替换 $Z^{\an}$，并以 $V\cap X_0$ 中 $x$ 的某个普通 affinoid 邻域替换 $V$；这些替换不改变两个通常茎。令 $R_0=\mathcal O_{X_0,x}$。对素理想 $\mathfrak P\subset R_0$ 及有限纯不可分扩张 $E/\kappa(\mathfrak P)$，将 Proposition~\ref{fnd:finitefieldtestrealization} 用于域包含，便将测试实现为}
\[
 S\otimes_{R_0}E
       =\left(\prod_i\mathcal O_{T,t_i}\right)\otimes_{C_0}E,
 \qquad B_0\hookrightarrow C_0=\mathcal O_{Y,z},
 \qquad Y=\mathcal M(B_0),
\]
\bi{where $B_0$ and $C_0$ are domains, $C_0$ is finite over $R_0$, $\operatorname{Frac}(C_0)=E$, and $T$ is the inverse-image domain of $V$ in $Y$. A prime in any factor surviving the displayed localization contracts to zero in $B_0$. The diagonal comparison already proved, now with the affinoid base $B_0$ and its finite-type algebra $B_0$ itself, makes $B_0\to\mathcal O_{T,t_i}$ regular at the relevant localizations. Their localizations at primes above zero are regular, because the source localization is the field $\operatorname{Frac}(B_0)$ and Tag 031E applies. Hence every local ring of $S\otimes_{R_0}E$ is regular. Tag 0381 proves that the bottom arrow is regular.}
{其中 $B_0,C_0$ 均为整环，$C_0$ 在 $R_0$ 上有限，$\operatorname{Frac}(C_0)=E$，且 $T$ 为 $V$ 在 $Y$ 中的逆像域。各因子中在所列局部化后仍存在的素理想，都在 $B_0$ 中收缩为零。将已证明的对角比较用于 affinoid 底 $B_0$ 及其有限型代数 $B_0$ 本身，便知 $B_0\to\mathcal O_{T,t_i}$ 在有关局部化处正则。它们在零理想上方素理想处的局部化都正则，因为源的局部化为域 $\operatorname{Frac}(B_0)$，可应用 Tag 031E。因此 $S\otimes_{R_0}E$ 的每个局部环都正则。Tag 0381 说明下箭头正则。}

\bi{The upper arrow is regular by descent, Tag 07NT: its composite with the faithfully flat right arrow is the regular diagonal. Finally, the diagonal assertion applied to an ordinary affinoid neighborhood of $x$ in $Z^{\an}$ proves regularity of the left arrow. This establishes every map of the diagram.}
{由下降 Tag 07NT，上箭头正则，因为它与忠实平坦的右箭头之复合是正则对角映射。最后，把对角断言用于 $Z^{\an}$ 中 $x$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域，就得到左箭头正则。至此图中每个映射都已证明。}
\end{proof}
