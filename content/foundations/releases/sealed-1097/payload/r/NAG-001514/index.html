A kernel series with unit coefficient ideal

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A kernel series with unit coefficient ideal}{系数生成单位理想的核级数}]\label{fnd:strictprimitiveepikernel}
\bi{Let $\varphi:A\to B$ be a strict-affinoid epimorphism, let $A\ne0$, and let $\pi:A\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle\twoheadrightarrow B$ be a bounded admissible relative presentation with $n\geq1$. There is an element $F\in\ker\pi$ whose coefficients generate the unit ideal of $A$.}
{设 $\varphi:A\to B$ 为 strict-affinoid 上态，$A\ne0$，且 $\pi:A\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle\twoheadrightarrow B$ 是有界 admissible 相对表示，其中 $n\geq1$。则存在 $F\in\ker\pi$，其系数生成 $A$ 的单位理想。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Fix a maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ of $A$ and put $\kappa=A/\mathfrak m$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} makes $\kappa$ a complete finite extension of $K$. Coefficientwise quotient exactness, Proposition~\ref{fnd:weightedc0exact} and Theorem~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientpresentation}, gives}
{固定 $A$ 的极大理想 $\mathfrak m$，令 $\kappa=A/\mathfrak m$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}，$\kappa$ 是 $K$ 的完备有限扩张。逐系数的取商正合性，即 Proposition~\ref{fnd:weightedc0exact} 与 Theorem~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientpresentation}，给出}
\[
 \kappa\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle\twoheadrightarrow
 D=B/\mathfrak mB,
 \qquad
 D=B\widehat\otimes_A\kappa.
\]
\bi{Here the equality with the completed pushout also follows from Proposition~\ref{fnd:relativeaffinoidpushouts}; all quotients are by actual closed ideals. In particular, $D$ is a strict $\kappa$-affinoid. The map $\kappa\to D$ is a strict-affinoid epimorphism over $\kappa$. Indeed, a strict $\kappa$-affinoid test algebra is strict over $K$ by Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictfiniterestrictionofscalars}; two bounded maps from $D$ agreeing on $\kappa$ give maps from $B$ agreeing on $A$, hence are equal. By Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictfieldmonomorphism}, either $D=0$ or $D=\kappa$. In either case the displayed map from $\kappa\langle T\rangle$ has nonzero kernel: for $n\geq1$ this Tate algebra has the two distinct maximal evaluation ideals at $(0,\ldots,0)$ and $(1,0,\ldots,0)$ and cannot be a field.}
{与完备推出的识别也可由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:relativeaffinoidpushouts} 得到；所有取商均模实际闭理想进行。特别地，$D$ 为 strict $\kappa$-affinoid。映射 $\kappa\to D$ 是 $\kappa$ 上的 strict-affinoid 上态：事实上，由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictfiniterestrictionofscalars}，strict $\kappa$-affinoid 测试代数也在 $K$ 上 strict；从 $D$ 出发且在 $\kappa$ 上相同的两个有界映射，给出从 $B$ 出发且在 $A$ 上相同的两个映射，因而相等。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictfieldmonomorphism}，或有 $D=0$，或有 $D=\kappa$。两种情况下，从 $\kappa\langle T\rangle$ 出发的上述满射均具有非零核：当 $n\geq1$ 时，该 Tate 代数有在 $(0,\ldots,0)$ 与 $(1,0,\ldots,0)$ 处取值所得的两个不同极大理想，因此不是域。}

\bi{The map $\ker\pi\to\ker(\kappa\langle T\rangle\to D)$ is surjective. To see this, lift a relation to $G\in A\langle T\rangle$. Its image under $\pi$ lies in $\mathfrak mB$, which is the image of $\mathfrak m A\langle T\rangle$ because $\pi$ is surjective. Subtract a lift in that ideal. Thus, at every maximal $\mathfrak m$, some series in $\ker\pi$ has a coefficient outside $\mathfrak m$. The ideal of $A$ generated by all coefficients of all these series is the unit ideal, by \cite[Tag 00E0(2), Lemma 10.17.2]{StacksAlg}. A finite unit certificate uses finitely many series $G_1,\ldots,G_r\in\ker\pi$.}
{映射 $\ker\pi\to\ker(\kappa\langle T\rangle\to D)$ 为满射。为此，将一个关系提升为 $G\in A\langle T\rangle$。其在 $\pi$ 下的像属于 $\mathfrak mB$；由于 $\pi$ 满射，该理想就是 $\mathfrak m A\langle T\rangle$ 的像。减去此理想中的一个提升即可。因此，对每个极大理想 $\mathfrak m$，总有 $\ker\pi$ 中的一个级数，其某个系数不属于 $\mathfrak m$。由 \cite[Tag 00E0(2), Lemma 10.17.2]{StacksAlg}，所有这些级数的全部系数生成 $A$ 的单位理想。取一个有限的单位证书，其中只涉及有限多个级数 $G_1,\ldots,G_r\in\ker\pi$。}

\bi{The algebra $A$ is Noetherian by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}. Choose $d\geq1$ such that, for every $i$, the coefficients of $G_i$ of total degree less than $d$ generate the same ideal as all its coefficients. Set}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}，$A$ 为 Noetherian。选择 $d\geq1$，使对每个 $i$，$G_i$ 中总次数小于 $d$ 的系数与全部系数生成相同的理想。令}
\[
 F=G_1+T_n^dG_2+\cdots+T_n^{(r-1)d}G_r\in\ker\pi.
\]
\bi{At a maximal point $x$, let $i$ be the first index with $G_{i,x}\ne0$. Such an index exists by the unit certificate. Some coefficient of $G_{i,x}$ of total degree less than $d$ is nonzero. After the indicated shift, its degree is less than $id$. Earlier summands vanish at $x$, while every later summand has total degree at least $id$, so that coefficient cannot cancel. Hence $F_x\ne0$ for every maximal $x$. Its coefficients have no common maximal zero and therefore generate the unit ideal, as required.}
{在极大点 $x$ 处，取使 $G_{i,x}\ne0$ 的最小下标 $i$；单位证书保证这样的下标存在。$G_{i,x}$ 中必有一个总次数小于 $d$ 的非零系数。经过所列平移后，该项的次数小于 $id$。更早的各项在 $x$ 处消失，而所有更晚的项总次数均至少为 $id$，所以该系数不能被抵消。因此每个极大点 $x$ 都满足 $F_x\ne0$。其系数没有共同的极大零点，故生成单位理想。}
\end{proof}

\section{\bi{Gerritzen--Grauert in the strict category}{Strict 范畴中的 Gerritzen--Grauert 定理}}\label{fnd:strictggsection}
\bi{We now use the relative Weierstrass results to obtain a finite rational description of affinoid domains. The argument first treats bounded categorical epimorphisms of strict affinoid algebras. It produces rational pieces on which the image is dense. The domain universal property then removes the closed equations from the resulting Weierstrass factorization. Except in the following definition and its immediate observations, $K$ is complete and its fixed absolute value is nontrivial. All algebra homomorphisms are unital and bounded.}
{现在利用相对 Weierstrass 结果，为 affinoid domains 建立有限有理描述。首先处理 strict affinoid 代数的有界范畴上态，得到其像稠密的有理块；再使用 domain 泛性质，消去所得 Weierstrass 分解中的闭方程。除紧接着的定义及其直接说明外，本节中 $K$ 完备，且给定绝对值非平凡。所有代数同态均保单位且有界。}
