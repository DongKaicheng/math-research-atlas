The local fan displacement formula

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 888

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The local fan displacement formula}
{局部扇位移公式}]
\label{cand:stablefandisplacement}
\bi{Let $C,E$ be finite balanced real cycles in $N_{\mathbf R}$,
of dimensions $p,q$, and put $r=p+q-n\geq0$. Choose a common
polyhedral refinement of their intersection and let $\gamma$
be an $r$-dimensional face. At $a\in\operatorname{relint}(\gamma)$,
take the two local stars and quotient by
$L=N_{\gamma,\mathbf R}$. Their dimensions are complementary
in $N_{\mathbf R}/L$. The weight of $C\cdot E$ on $\gamma$
is the sum~\eqref{eq:candfandisplacement} for these quotient
fans. Equivalently it is the weighted small-displacement limit
of the transverse intersections of $C$ and $E+\varepsilon b$,
with lattice factors $[N:N_\sigma+N_\tau]$.}
{设 $C,E$ 为 $N_{\mathbf R}$ 中维数分别为 $p,q$ 的有限
平衡实循环，置 $r=p+q-n\geq0$。为其交选择共同多面细分，
令 $\gamma$ 为一个 $r$ 维面。在
$a\in\operatorname{relint}(\gamma)$ 处取两个局部星形复形，
再商去 $L=N_{\gamma,\mathbf R}$；所得扇在
$N_{\mathbf R}/L$ 中维数互补。$C\cdot E$ 在 $\gamma$
上的权重就是这些商扇的和~\eqref{eq:candfandisplacement}。
等价地，它是 $C$ 与 $E+\varepsilon b$ 的横截交的加权小
位移极限，格因子为 $[N:N_\sigma+N_\tau]$。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Stars commute with corners and products. Both stars at
$a$ have $L$ as a lineality space: the minimal face of either
original complex containing $a$ contains the local directions
of $\gamma$. Since $N_\gamma$ is saturated,
Lemma~\ref{cand:elementarylatticesplitting} allows us to choose
$N=N_\gamma\oplus Q$. Write the two stars as
$L\times A$ and $L\times B$. The diagonal cuts in the $L$
coordinates give the diagonal copy of $L$ with weight one,
by the unit calculation in Theorem~\ref{cand:stableproductring}.
The remaining cuts give $A\cdot B$. Their dimensions satisfy}
{取星形复形与角点及直积交换。$a$ 处两个星形复形都以
$L$ 为线性空间，因为各原复形中包含 $a$ 的极小面包含
$\gamma$ 的局部方向。由于 $N_\gamma$ 饱和，由
Lemma~\ref{cand:elementarylatticesplitting} 可选分解
$N=N_\gamma\oplus Q$。将两个星形复形写为
$L\times A$、$L\times B$。由
Theorem~\ref{cand:stableproductring} 中单位元的计算，
$L$ 坐标上的对角线角点给出权重为一的 $L$ 的对角线副本。
其余角点给出 $A\cdot B$。二者的维数满足}
\[
 \dim A+\dim B=(p-r)+(q-r)=n-r=\operatorname{rank}Q.
\]
\bi{The preceding lemma computes the coefficient at their
origin, which is the desired weight on $\gamma$. For every
contributing pair of original facets, $N_\gamma$ is contained
in both direction lattices, so}
{前一引理计算它们在原点的系数，这就是 $\gamma$ 上的所需
权重。对每对有贡献的原极大胞腔，$N_\gamma$ 均包含在
两个方向格中，故}
\[
 [N/N_\gamma:(N_\sigma+N_\tau)/N_\gamma]
       =[N:N_\sigma+N_\tau].
\]
\bi{Finally choose a small neighborhood of $a$ meeting only
the faces that contain its minimal faces. In this neighborhood
the original cells are translates of their local tangent cones.
For a generic $b$ and sufficiently small $\varepsilon>0$, a
transverse displaced pair approaching $a$ is therefore exactly
a pair counted by the quotient-fan formula; the signs agree
because $x\in C\cap(E+\varepsilon b)$ means $x-y=\varepsilon b$.
Only finitely many cells occur, so the incidence conditions are
constant for all sufficiently small positive $\varepsilon$.
Summing their weights gives the claimed weighted limit.
There is no additional support in the relative interior of an
intersection cell of dimension $s>r$: quotienting its common
$s$-dimensional lineality gives a product of dimension $r-s<0$,
which is zero. Cells of dimension less than $r$ cannot carry
an $r$-cycle. Thus refining the $r$-faces gives all coefficients
of the cycle.}
{最后选取 $a$ 的小邻域，使其仅遇到包含有关极小面的胞腔。
在该邻域中，原胞腔是其局部切锥的平移。因此，对一般 $b$
及充分小的 $\varepsilon>0$，趋向 $a$ 的横截位移胞腔对
恰为商扇公式计数的胞腔对。符号一致，因为
$x\in C\cap(E+\varepsilon b)$ 等价于
$x-y=\varepsilon b$。只涉及有限个胞腔，故对全部充分小
正 $\varepsilon$，相交条件不变。将权重相加便得到所称的
加权极限。维数 $s>r$ 的交胞腔的相对内部没有额外支集：
商去共同的 $s$ 维线性空间后，乘积维数为 $r-s<0$，因而
为零。维数小于 $r$ 的胞腔也不能承载 $r$-循环。因此细分
这些 $r$ 维面即可确定循环的全部系数。}
\end{proof}
