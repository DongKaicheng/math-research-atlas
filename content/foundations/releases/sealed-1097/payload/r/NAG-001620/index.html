Quasi-nets and analytic domains

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 379

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{definition}[\bi{Quasi-nets and analytic domains}{Quasi-net 与解析域}]
\label{fnd:affinoidanalyticquasinet}
\bi{A family $(V_i)$ of subsets of a topological space $V$ is a quasi-net if, for every $x\in V$, some finite subfamily consists of sets containing $x$ and has union containing a neighborhood of $x$ in $V$. Let $X=\mathcal M(A)$. A subset $V\subseteq X$ is an analytic domain if it has a quasi-net covering by affinoid domains of $X$, with the induced topology on $V$. A family of analytic subdomains of $V$ is an admissible covering if it is a quasi-net on $V$. The empty family covers the empty domain.}
{若拓扑空间 $V$ 的一个子集族 $(V_i)$ 满足：对每个 $x\in V$，存在一个有限子族，其所有成员都包含 $x$，且其并包含 $x$ 在 $V$ 中的一个邻域，则称该族为 quasi-net。令 $X=\mathcal M(A)$。若子集 $V\subseteq X$ 具有由 $X$ 的 affinoid 域组成的 quasi-net 覆盖，则称它为解析域，并采用诱导拓扑。$V$ 的解析子域族若为 $V$ 上的 quasi-net，则称为 admissible 覆盖。空族覆盖空域。}
\end{definition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Restriction follows by intersecting a neighborhood and its finitely many covering members with the subset. For transitivity, fix $x$ and choose finitely many $V_i$ containing $x$ whose union contains a neighborhood $N$ of $x$. For each chosen $i$, select finitely many refining members containing $x$ whose union contains a relative neighborhood $N_i$ of $x$ in $V_i$. Write $N_i\supseteq O_i\cap V_i$ with $O_i$ a neighborhood of $x$ in the ambient space. Then $N\cap\bigcap_iO_i$ is a neighborhood covered by the finitely many selected refining members, all of which contain $x$.}
{限制性来自将邻域及覆盖它的有限个成员与子集相交。对传递性，固定 $x$，选择有限个包含 $x$ 的 $V_i$，使其并包含 $x$ 的邻域 $N$。对每个选定的 $i$，再选择有限个包含 $x$ 的加细成员，使其并包含 $x$ 在 $V_i$ 中的相对邻域 $N_i$。可取环境空间中 $x$ 的邻域 $O_i$，使 $N_i\supseteq O_i\cap V_i$。于是 $N\cap\bigcap_iO_i$ 是一个邻域，被所选有限个加细成员覆盖，且每个成员均包含 $x$。}

\bi{For a finite closed covering, delete the members not containing $x$; their union is closed and misses $x$, so the remaining members cover a neighborhood of $x$. Finally, the neighborhoods supplied by a quasi-net cover a compact space. A finite subcover of these neighborhoods uses only finitely many of the original members and proves the compactness assertion.}
{对于有限闭覆盖，删去所有不包含 $x$ 的成员。被删去者的并闭且不含 $x$，所以其余成员覆盖 $x$ 的一个邻域。最后，quasi-net 给出的各邻域覆盖紧空间；选取其中有限个邻域即可只用原族的有限个成员覆盖整个空间。}
\end{proof}
