Noetherian ordinary local rings

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 315

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Noetherian ordinary local rings}{通常局部环的 Noetherian 性}]
\label{fnd:ordinaryanalyticstalknoetherian}
\bi{For every good analytic space over any complete non-Archimedean field, every ordinary local ring is Noetherian.}
{对任意完备非阿基米德域上的 good 解析空间，每个通常局部环均为 Noetherian 环。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Fix $x\in X$ and choose an affinoid neighborhood. Put $E=\mathcal H(x)$. If $E$ is nontrivially valued, take $L=E$; otherwise use Lemma~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusfield} to adjoin one radius different from $1$, obtaining a nontrivially valued complete extension $L/E$. Evaluation $A\to E\to L$, together with the identity on $L$, defines an $L$-rational point $u\in X_L$ above $x$. The completed-residue-field and relative pushout properties verify this point directly.}
{固定 $x\in X$，并选择一个 affinoid 邻域。令 $E=\mathcal H(x)$。若 $E$ 赋值非平凡，取 $L=E$；否则由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusfield} 添入一个不同于 $1$ 的半径，得到赋值非平凡的完备扩张 $L/E$。取值 $A\to E\to L$ 与 $L$ 上的恒等映射共同定义 $x$ 上方的一个 $L$-有理点 $u\in X_L$；完备剩余域及相对推出的泛性质直接核验这一点。}

\bi{By Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalanalyticformalpresentation}, $S=\mathcal O_{X_L,u}$ is Noetherian. The map $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}\to S$ is faithfully flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}. Noetherianity descends here by an elementary ideal argument. For any ideal $J\subset R$, the ideal $JS$ has finitely many generators. Their expressions use finitely many elements $a_1,\ldots,a_m\in J$, so $JS=(a_1,\ldots,a_m)S$. If $J_0=(a_1,\ldots,a_m)\subset R$, flatness identifies $(J/J_0)\otimes_R S$ with $JS/J_0S=0$, and faithfulness gives $J=J_0$. Thus every ideal of $R$ is finitely generated.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalanalyticformalpresentation}，$S=\mathcal O_{X_L,u}$ 为 Noetherian。Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat} 说明 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}\to S$ 忠实平坦。此处 Noetherian 性由基本理想论证下降：对任意理想 $J\subset R$，理想 $JS$ 有限生成。这些生成元的表达式仅涉及有限个 $a_1,\ldots,a_m\in J$，故 $JS=(a_1,\ldots,a_m)S$。令 $J_0=(a_1,\ldots,a_m)\subset R$，平坦性将 $(J/J_0)\otimes_R S$ 识别为 $JS/J_0S=0$，忠实性遂给出 $J=J_0$。因此 $R$ 的每个理想均有限生成。}
\end{proof}
