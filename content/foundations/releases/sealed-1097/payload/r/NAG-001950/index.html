Existence of quasi-paracompact formal models

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 543

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Existence of quasi-paracompact formal models}
{Quasi-paracompact 形式模型的存在性}]
\label{fnd:quasiparacompactformalmodel}
\bi{Every quasi-separated quasi-paracompact strict analytic
$G$-space over $K$ has a quasi-paracompact admissible formal
$K^\circ$-model.}
{每个 $K$ 上 quasi-separated、quasi-paracompact 的 strict
解析 $G$-空间都有 quasi-paracompact admissible 形式 $K^\circ$-模型。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{An affinoid space has a model by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations}.
Suppose two quasi-compact analytic opens $U_1,U_2$ have
models $\mathfrak U_1,\mathfrak U_2$.
Their intersection $W$ is quasi-compact by quasi-separatedness.
Apply \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:formalcoverrealizationsuppliedatlas]{cover realization on supplied atlases}} to the
cover $(W,U_i)$ of $U_i$: after a blowup
$\mathfrak U_i'\to\mathfrak U_i$, a quasi-compact formal
open $\mathfrak W_i'\subset\mathfrak U_i'$ represents $W$.
By \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:commonformalmodificationonsuppliedatlases]{common modification on supplied atlases}}, the two models
of $W$ have a common modification $\mathfrak W''$.
Extend its two sequences of centers to $\mathfrak U_i'$
by Lemma~\ref{fnd:formalidealextension}.
The resulting models $\mathfrak U_i''$ have the same formal
open $\mathfrak W''$ and glue along it.
Their generic fibres glue along exactly $W$, giving
$U_1\cup U_2$. Induction on a finite affinoid cover proves
existence for every quasi-compact space.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations}，affinoid
空间有模型。设两个拟紧解析开集 $U_1,U_2$ 有模型
$\mathfrak U_1,\mathfrak U_2$。由 quasi-separatedness，交 $W$ 拟紧。
对 $U_i$ 的覆盖 $(W,U_i)$ 应用
\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:formalcoverrealizationsuppliedatlas]{cover realization on supplied atlases}}：作 blowup
$\mathfrak U_i'\to\mathfrak U_i$ 后，存在表示 $W$ 的拟紧形式开集
$\mathfrak W_i'\subset\mathfrak U_i'$。
由 \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:commonformalmodificationonsuppliedatlases]{common modification on supplied atlases}}，这两个 $W$ 的模型
有共同修改 $\mathfrak W''$。
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:formalidealextension} 把两列中心延拓到
$\mathfrak U_i'$。所得 $\mathfrak U_i''$ 包含同一个形式开集
$\mathfrak W''$，可沿它粘合。泛纤维恰沿 $W$ 粘合，给出
$U_1\cup U_2$。对有限 affinoid 覆盖归纳，即得每个拟紧空间的结论。}

\bi{In general choose a finite-type cover by quasi-compact
analytic opens. Its intersection graph is locally finite.
Each graph component is countable, and distinct components
give disjoint admissible open pieces, which can be treated
separately. In one component fix a vertex, and let $U_n$
be the union of the finitely many covering members at graph
distance $n$ from it. Each $U_n$ is quasi-compact,
the $U_n$ cover that piece, and
$U_n\cap U_m=\varnothing$ whenever $|n-m|>1$.}
{一般地，选择有限型拟紧解析开覆盖。其相交图局部有限，每个图分支
可数，不同分支给出不交的 admissible 开分解，可分别处理。
在一个分支中固定顶点，以 $U_n$ 记到它的图距离为 $n$ 的有限个
覆盖成员之并。每个 $U_n$ 拟紧，它们覆盖该部分，且
$|n-m|>1$ 时 $U_n\cap U_m=\varnothing$。}

\bi{Inductively construct a model $\mathfrak V_n$ of
$V_n=U_0\cup\cdots\cup U_n$, containing formal opens
representing each $U_i$, $i\leq n$.
To add $U_{n+1}$, apply the two-piece gluing construction
above to the current formal open representing $U_n$
and a model of $U_{n+1}$.
Their analytic intersection equals $V_n\cap U_{n+1}$.
Extend the modifications on the $U_n$ side to $\mathfrak V_n$,
with support in the closure of that formal open, by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:formalidealextension}.
This leaves each formal open representing $U_i$, $i<n-1$,
unchanged, because it is disjoint from the $U_n$ open.
Glue in the modified model of $U_{n+1}$.
The preimages of the existing formal opens and the new open
give the required cover of $\mathfrak V_{n+1}$.}
{归纳构造 $V_n=U_0\cup\cdots\cup U_n$ 的模型 $\mathfrak V_n$，
其中含有表示各 $U_i$、$i\leq n$ 的形式开集。
添入 $U_{n+1}$ 时，对当前表示 $U_n$ 的形式开集和一个
$U_{n+1}$ 的模型应用上述两片粘合构造。
它们的解析交等于 $V_n\cap U_{n+1}$。
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:formalidealextension}，把 $U_n$ 一侧的修改
延拓到 $\mathfrak V_n$，支撑控制在该形式开集的闭包中。
当 $i<n-1$ 时，表示 $U_i$ 的形式开集与 $U_n$ 的开集不交，
因而完全不变。再粘入修改后的 $U_{n+1}$ 模型；原开集的逆像
与新的开集给出 $\mathfrak V_{n+1}$ 的所需覆盖。}

\bi{For fixed $i$, its formal open is unchanged after finitely
many stages. Write $\mathfrak U_i^\infty$ for this stabilized
open. Overlap identifications are read at any sufficiently
late common stage; stabilization makes them independent of
the stage, and a common stage for three indices proves the
cocycle identity. Glue these opens.
Each $\mathfrak U_i^\infty$ is quasi-compact, having undergone
only finitely many quasi-compact blowups, and meets only its
two neighbors, since generic fibres detect disjointness.
The glued model is therefore quasi-paracompact and has the
desired generic fibre. Taking disjoint unions over graph
components finishes the proof.}
{对固定 $i$，其形式开集在有限步后不再改变，记稳定后的开集为
$\mathfrak U_i^\infty$。重叠识别可从任意充分晚的共同阶段读出；
稳定性说明它不依赖阶段，对三个指标取共同阶段则证明 cocycle
恒等式。因此可以粘合这些开集。
每个 $\mathfrak U_i^\infty$ 只经过有限次拟紧 blowup，故拟紧；
泛纤维检测不交性，故它只与相邻两个成员相交。
所得模型因而 quasi-paracompact，并具有所需的泛纤维。
最后对图的各分支取不交并即可。}
\end{proof}
