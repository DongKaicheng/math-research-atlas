Intrinsic torus and factorization

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1883

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Intrinsic torus and factorization]\label{prop:torus}
Choose a basis of $M_A$ and lifts of its elements to $A^\times$. Extending multiplicatively gives a section $s_A:M_A\to A^\times$, a torus
\[
            T_A=\Spec K[M_A],\qquad
            N_A=\Hom_\Z(M_A,\Z),
\]
and a morphism $\varphi_A:U\to T_A$. Changing the lifts translates this morphism by a point of $T_A(K)$. Every algebraic moment morphism $U\to\Gm^r$ factors through $\varphi_A$ by an affine homomorphism of tori, meaning a torus homomorphism followed by a constant translation. The morphism $\varphi_A$ is a closed immersion if and only if $A$ is generated as a $K$-algebra by its units; this is equivalent to $U$ being very affine.

For a $K$-algebra map $A\to C$ between integral finite-type $K$-algebras, the induced map $M_A\to M_C$ gives an affine torus map $T_C\to T_A$ compatible with the moment morphisms. No injectivity of this lattice map is asserted for an arbitrary map $A\to C$.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Finite freeness supplies the section: lift a basis and use the unique integral expression of any lattice element. The resulting map $K[M_A]\to A$ sends its basis character $\chi^m$ to $s_A(m)$. For another section $s'_A$, the ratio $s'_A/s_A:M_A\to K^\times$ is a character-valued constant, that is, a point of $T_A(K)$; this gives the translation assertion.

An algebraic moment morphism is a finite tuple $u_i\in A^\times$. Each $u_i$ is uniquely a constant times a monomial in the chosen basis lifts. This proves its factorization. The image of $K[M_A]\to A$ is the subalgebra generated by all units of $A$, so the map is surjective exactly under the stated generation condition, equivalently the morphism is a closed immersion. A closed embedding into a finite-dimensional torus implies generation by the coordinate units and their inverses. Conversely, if all units generate $A$, finite generation of $A$ lets one select finitely many units generating it and gives a closed embedding into a torus.

A $K$-algebra map preserves units and constants. Its induced lattice map and the chosen sections express every pulled-back basis lift as a constant times a monomial in the target basis lifts. These expressions define the asserted affine homomorphism and prove the compatibility on coordinate rings.
\end{proof}
