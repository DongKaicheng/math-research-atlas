Intrinsic realization and local common refinements

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1888

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Intrinsic realization and local common refinements]\label{prop:intrinsic}
The preceding $V$ is an intrinsic chart for the actual algebraic lattice $M_U$. Every selected approximant is a constant times a character of $T_U$, and \eqref{eq:norm} holds on the entire original $C$. Intrinsic charts form a basis of ordinary neighborhoods in $X^\an$ and admit common intrinsic refinements near every point of a finite intersection.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Apply \src{F7U44R1-L1} to the integral finite-type coordinate ring of $U$, and \src{F7U44R1-T1} to its selected units. Their tropicalizations are integral affine functions of $t_U$. In particular the explicit unit embedding $\psi$ of R1 factors through $\varphi_U$ by an affine torus map, with tropical affine map $F$. If $V=\psi_{\rm trop}^{-1}(\Omega_0)$, then
\[
                  V=t_U^{-1}\bigl(F^{-1}(\Omega_0)\bigr).
\]
The inverse image is real open. This does not shrink or change $C$ and does not change the approximants.

Applying R1 to the empty tuple inside any prescribed ordinary neighborhood constructs a chart $V$ there. For common refinements of charts $(V_j,U_j)$ at $x$, choose an affine Zariski neighborhood of the scheme point underlying $x$ contained in $\bigcap_j U_j$. Apply R1 to the ordinary neighborhood $\bigcap_j V_j$ within this affine analytification. Its output $U'$ lies in every $U_j$ and its $V'$ lies in every $V_j$. Restriction of algebraic units gives the compatible maps $M_{U_j}\to M_{U'}$ and hence affine torus maps by \src{F7U44R1-T1}. Their tropical maps are the required refinement maps. This construction also refines any prescribed ordinary neighborhood of $x$.

All unit groups here are $\cO(U)^\times/K^\times$ or the corresponding groups on genuine algebraic localizations. The stability used is \src{F7U44R1-L3}; no normality or saturation assertion is required. In particular neither a coordinate-generated subgroup nor the unit group of the completed algebra $B$ is substituted for $M_U$.
\end{proof}
