A jointly continuous strong deformation retraction

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 865

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{A jointly continuous strong deformation retraction}
{联合连续的强形变收缩}]
\label{cand:standardhigherskeletonhomotopy}
\bi{The map $H:V\times[0,1]\to V$ of
Lemma~\ref{cand:mixedgaussdisplacement} is jointly continuous
and is a strong deformation retraction onto $\Sigma_a$. More
precisely,}
{Lemma~\ref{cand:mixedgaussdisplacement} 的映射
$H:V\times[0,1]\to V$ 联合连续，并为到 $\Sigma_a$
的强形变收缩。更精确地，}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 H(x,0)&=x,& H(x,1)&=s(\operatorname{val}(x)),\\
 H(s(w),t)&=s(w),&
 \operatorname{val}(H(x,t))&=\operatorname{val}(x).
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{It commutes with complete isometric ground-field extension.}
{它与完备等距基域扩张相容。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{We first give a formula proving joint continuity, including
at $t=0,1$. Let
$P\in K[X_1^{\pm1},\ldots,X_r^{\pm1},T]$ and choose
$D\in\mathbb N^r$ so that $Q=X^DP$ is a polynomial.
Expand the finite polynomial}
{先给出证明联合连续性的公式，包括 $t=0,1$ 处。
设 $P\in K[X_1^{\pm1},\ldots,X_r^{\pm1},T]$，选取
$D\in\mathbb N^r$ 使 $Q=X^DP$ 为多项式。展开有限多项式}
\[
 Q\bigl(X_1(1+S_1),\ldots,X_r(1+S_r),T+Z\bigr)
       =\sum_{\beta,\gamma}Q_{\beta\gamma}(X,T)S^\beta Z^\gamma.
\]
\bi{Since the factors $1+S_i$ have norm one, the exact formula is}
{由于因子 $1+S_i$ 的范数为一，有精确公式}
\[
 |P(H(x,t))|
   =\frac{\displaystyle\max_{\beta,\gamma}
            |Q_{\beta\gamma}(x)|\,t^{|\beta|+|\gamma|}}
          {\displaystyle\prod_{i=1}^r|X_i(x)|^{D_i}},
 \tag{H}\label{eq:candmixedgausscontinuous}
\]
\bi{where the term of degree zero uses $0^0=1$. Its right
side is continuous in $(x,t)$: it is a finite maximum divided
by a continuous positive function. Every $X_i$ is invertible
on $V$. The Laurent polynomial algebra is dense in $A$ by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:standardmonomialchartgeometry}.
All evaluations $H(x,t)$ are contractive for one fixed quotient
norm, so approximating an arbitrary $f\in A$ by Laurent
polynomials gives uniform approximation of the real functions
$|f(H(x,t))|$ on $V\times[0,1]$. Thus all such evaluation
functions are continuous, which is precisely continuity into
the Berkovich spectrum.}
{零次项使用约定 $0^0=1$。右侧关于 $(x,t)$ 连续，因为
它是有限个连续函数的最大值除以连续正函数；每个 $X_i$
在 $V$ 上可逆。由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:standardmonomialchartgeometry}，
Laurent 多项式代数在 $A$ 中稠密。全部点 $H(x,t)$ 的
取值都关于同一个固定商范数压缩。因此用 Laurent 多项式
逼近任意 $f\in A$，便在 $V\times[0,1]$ 上一致逼近
实函数 $|f(H(x,t))|$。故全部这样的取值函数连续，
这正是到 Berkovich 谱的连续性。}

\bi{At $t=1$, translation of any variable of the unit Gauss
norm by a scalar of norm at most one is isometric. Indeed,
the ultrametric inequality makes substitution contractive on
polynomials, and the inverse translation gives the reverse
bound; the assertion then extends to fractions. Apply this
to $S_i\mapsto1+S_i$ and $Z_j\mapsto b_j+Z_j$. For every
Laurent polynomial $P=\sum c_{\alpha\mu}X^\alpha T^\mu$,}
{当 $t=1$ 时，将单位 Gauss 范数的某个变量按一个范数
至多为一的标量平移，是等距的。事实上，超度量不等式
使该代换在多项式上压缩，而逆平移给出反向估计；再将
结论延拓到分式。将其用于 $S_i\mapsto1+S_i$ 与
$Z_j\mapsto b_j+Z_j$。对任意 Laurent 多项式
$P=\sum c_{\alpha\mu}X^\alpha T^\mu$，有}
\[
 |P(H(x,1))|=\max_{\alpha,\mu}
       |c_{\alpha\mu}|\prod_{i=1}^r|X_i(x)|^{\alpha_i}.
\]
\bi{This is the defining Gauss norm of $s(\operatorname{val}(x))$,
including radius one for every free coordinate $T_j$. Density
gives the same equality on $A$.}
{这就是 $s(\operatorname{val}(x))$ 的定义 Gauss 范数，
包括每个自由坐标 $T_j$ 的半径一。由稠密性，等式在
$A$ 上仍成立。}

\bi{Let $x=s(w)$. The norm of every monomial after displacement
is at most its original Gauss norm, so
$|P(H(x,t))|\leq|P(x)|$. In
\eqref{eq:candmixedgausscontinuous}, the constant coefficient
is $Q(x)$ and has norm equal to the Gauss norm of $Q$ at
$s(w)$. Consequently the same formula gives the reverse
inequality. Thus $H$ fixes every skeletal point. The identity
at time zero is its definition, and preservation of
$\operatorname{val}$ is the coordinate formula in the lemma.
These are all strong deformation retraction identities.}
{设 $x=s(w)$。位移后每个单项式的范数至多为其原 Gauss
范数，故 $|P(H(x,t))|\leq|P(x)|$。在
\eqref{eq:candmixedgausscontinuous} 中，常数系数为
$Q(x)$，其范数等于 $Q$ 在 $s(w)$ 处的 Gauss 范数。
因此同一公式给出反向不等式。于是 $H$ 固定每个骨架点。
时刻零的恒等式来自定义，而 $\operatorname{val}$ 的保持性
由引理中的坐标公式给出。这些就是强形变收缩的全部恒等式。}

\bi{For a complete isometric extension $L/K$ and a point
$x'\in V_L$ over $x$, the completed residue-field embedding
$\mathcal H(x)\hookrightarrow\mathcal H(x')$ is isometric.
Formula~\eqref{eq:candmixedgausscontinuous} therefore gives
the same value on every original Laurent polynomial before
and after projection. Density proves commutation on $A$.
At the endpoint the standard Gauss section over $L$ restricts
to that over $K$, with unchanged real radii.}
{对于完备等距扩张 $L/K$ 及 $x$ 上方的点
$x'\in V_L$，完备剩余域嵌入
$\mathcal H(x)\hookrightarrow\mathcal H(x')$ 等距。
因此 \eqref{eq:candmixedgausscontinuous} 对每个原
Laurent 多项式在投影前后给出相同取值；由稠密性，得到
$A$ 上的相容性。在终点处，$L$ 上的标准 Gauss 截面
限制为 $K$ 上的截面，实半径保持不变。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Let $K$ be complete and nontrivially valued, with its original
absolute value. Fix $0<|a|<1$ and integers $r,q\geq0$. Put}
{设 $K$ 关于原绝对值完备且非平凡赋值。固定
$0<|a|<1$ 及整数 $r,q\geq0$，置}
\[
 A=K\langle X_0,\ldots,X_r,T_1,\ldots,T_q\rangle
                /(X_0\cdots X_r-a),\qquad V=\mathcal M(A).
\]
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:standardmonomialchartgeometry} gives
the continuous Gauss section $s:\Delta_a\to V$, where}
{Proposition~\ref{fnd:standardmonomialchartgeometry} 给出
连续 Gauss 截面 $s:\Delta_a\to V$，其中}
\[
 \Delta_a=\{(w_0,\ldots,w_r)\in\mathbb R_{\geq0}^{r+1}:
                         \sum_iw_i=v(a)\}.
\]
\bi{After eliminating $X_0=a/(X_1\cdots X_r)$, $s(w)$ is the
Gauss point of radii $e^{-w_i}$ in $X_i$, $1\leq i\leq r$,
and radius $1$ in every $T_j$. Let $\Sigma_a=s(\Delta_a)$ and
write $\operatorname{val}:V\to\Delta_a$ for the values of
$X_0,\ldots,X_r$.}
{消去 $X_0=a/(X_1\cdots X_r)$ 后，$s(w)$ 是在
$X_i$、$1\leq i\leq r$ 上半径为 $e^{-w_i}$、在每个
$T_j$ 上半径为 $1$ 的 Gauss 点。令
$\Sigma_a=s(\Delta_a)$，并以
$\operatorname{val}:V\to\Delta_a$ 记
$X_0,\ldots,X_r$ 的赋值映射。}
