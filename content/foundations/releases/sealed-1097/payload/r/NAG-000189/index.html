Result 3.9.2

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 503

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}\label{fnd:gradedgauss}
\bi{The graded reduction of the weighted Gauss polynomial algebra is the graded polynomial algebra over $\widetilde K^{\mathrm{gr}}$ with $\deg(\widetilde T_i)=r_i$. The same assertion holds for the generalized Tate algebra with its Gauss norm.}
{Weighted Gauss polynomial algebra 的 graded reduction 是 $\widetilde K^{\mathrm{gr}}$ 上满足 $\deg(\widetilde T_i)=r_i$ 的 graded polynomial algebra。对带有 Gauss norm 的 generalized Tate algebra，同样成立。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A homogeneous leading class of size $s$ is represented by the finitely many monomials $a_\nu T^\nu$ having $|a_\nu|r^\nu=s$. The remaining terms have strictly smaller norm. For a convergent series there are still only finitely many terms at the maximal size, and its tail is strictly smaller. This proves surjectivity. A nonzero homogeneous polynomial cannot map to zero, because its Gauss norm is exactly its specified size; this proves injectivity. Multiplication is compatible by Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm}.}
{Size $s$ 的 homogeneous leading class 由满足 $|a_\nu|r^\nu=s$ 的有限个 monomials $a_\nu T^\nu$ 表示，其余项的 norm 严格更小。对 convergent series，最大 size 的项仍只有有限个，tail 的 norm 严格更小，故得到 surjectivity。非零 homogeneous polynomial 的 Gauss norm 恰为其指定 size，不能映为零，故得 injectivity。Multiplication 的 compatibility 由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm} 得到。}
\end{proof}
