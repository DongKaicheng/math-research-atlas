The supported comparison rules needed here

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1930

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[The supported comparison rules needed here]
\label{f9mi:operations}
For computing data, the following changes leave \eqref{eq:f9mi:value}
unchanged:
\begin{enumerate}
\item pullback of the actual data along a proper generic-identity
      map of integral models, using the inverse-image supports;
\item replacement of $(\cA_i,\sigma_i,e_i)$ by
      $(\cA_i^{\otimes m_i},\sigma_i^{\otimes m_i},m_i e_i)$,
      with $m_i\geq1$;
\item replacement of signed presentations on one common support;
\item enlargement of model supports, provided their total generic
      intersection is still empty.
\end{enumerate}
In (1), before degree one has equality in
$\CH_0(T_{\rm red})$ under the proper map of the final supports.
The source need not be normal.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Let $q:\cY\to\cT$ be as in (1). Repeated supported projection gives
\[
 q_*\bigl(I_{q^*0}\cdots I_{q^*d}[\cY]\bigr)
       =I_0\cdots I_d q_*[\cY]
       =I_0\cdots I_d[\cT].
\]
The last equality uses the actual function-field degree one.
At every step the target is the prescribed intersection, and the
final map is between proper vertical supports. Its zero-cycle
pushforward preserves degree over $k$, including residue degrees.
This proves (1).

Signed tensor additivity on the same support gives
$I_{\sigma_i^{m_i}}=m_i I_{\sigma_i}$. The factors $\prod_i m_i$
in the numerator and denominator of \eqref{eq:f9mi:value} cancel,
proving (2).

For (3), present one datum as $(A,a)/(B,b)$ and $(A',a')/(B',b')$.
The actual line identities and equality of their rational ratios
give the global equality $a\otimes b'=a'\otimes b$ on the integral
model. Supported Gysin additivity therefore gives
\[
                   G_a+G_{b'}=G_{a'}+G_b
\]
in the group on the common prescribed support, and subtraction
identifies the signed operations. Naturality for each intermediate
closed support permits their iteration with all other operations.
The comparison precedes degree; it is not merely equality of
ambient Chow classes. Finally the same naturality proves (4).
The larger final support is still proper over $k$, so its inclusion
also preserves degree.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
Let $R$ be a complete DVR, with uniformizer $\pi$, fraction field $K$,
and residue field $k$. Equip $K$ with a nontrivial non-Archimedean
absolute value whose valuation ring is $R$. Put
$\lambda=-\log|\pi|>0$. Let $X$ be a normal integral proper
$K$-variety of dimension $d\geq2$. A model is a proper flat
finite-type $R$-scheme with a specified identification of its generic
fibre with $X$. Maps called generic-identity respect these
identifications. Such models are integral by Theorem~\ref{f9cn:common};
their special fibres may be nonreduced.
Standing context 2 · exact TeX
A root model presentation of a metric $h$ on a line bundle $L$ is
$(\cT,\cL,e,\alpha)$, with $e\geq1$ and
$\alpha:\cL|_X\xrightarrow{\sim}L^{\otimes e}$ specified. If $s$
is a nonzero rational section of $L$, its model section is
$\sigma=\alpha^{-1}(s^{\otimes e})$. The identification $\alpha$ is
part of the datum. An abstract isomorphism of the underlying line
bundles does not replace it.
Standing context 3 · exact TeX
Fix line bundles $L_i$, root algebraic model metrics $h_i$, nonzero
rational sections $s_i$, and prescribed closed subsets $S_i\subset X$,
for $0\leq i\leq d$. Require that $s_i$ is a regular generator off
$S_i$ and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:f9mi:empty}
                         \bigcap_{i=0}^d S_i=\varnothing.
\end{equation}
Standing context 4 · exact TeX
A computing datum consists of an integral model $\cT$, extensions
$\cA_i|_X\simeq L_i^{\otimes e_i}$ inducing $h_i$, and signed
presentations of their model sections on closed sets $Z_i\subset\cT$
such that $Z_i\cap X=S_i$. Every factor zero support must lie in its
own $Z_i$. A support is not replaced by the image of its Chow group
in the Chow group of $\cT$.
Standing context 5 · exact TeX
Use the dimension function of the supported-calculus packet:
$\delta(\Spec K)=1$ and $\delta(\Spec k)=0$. Thus $[\cT]$
has dimension $d+1$. For a closed support $Z$ put
$C_r(\cT;Z)=\CH_r(Z_{\rm red})$. Write $I_i$ for the signed
operation supplied by Theorem~\ref{f9sc:refined-supported} and Proposition~\ref{f9sc:signed}.
Then
\[
 \beta_{\cT}=I_0\cdots I_d[\cT]\in
       \CH_0(T_{\rm red}),\qquad T=\bigcap_i Z_i.
\]
By \eqref{eq:f9mi:empty}, $T_{\rm red}$ is vertical and proper over $k$,
even if it has positive dimension. Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:f9mi:value}
 J_{\cT}=\frac{\lambda}{\prod_{i=0}^d e_i}
                            \deg_k(\beta_{\cT}).
\end{equation}
Degree is proper pushforward to $\Spec k$: a closed point contributes
its full residue degree, including its inseparable part. The original
coherent and divisor lengths stay in the cycle coefficients.
There is no extra factorial and no normalization of $\lambda$ to one.
