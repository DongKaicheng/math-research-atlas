Special fibres of admissible modifications

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 583

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Special fibres of admissible modifications}
{Admissible 修正的特殊纤维}]
\label{fnd:formalmodificationspecialproper}
\bi{Let $K$ be nontrivially valued. An admissible formal blowup
induces a locally projective morphism of special fibres over
$\widetilde K$, and hence a proper morphism. A finite composite
of admissible formal blowups induces a proper morphism as well.
For a fixed point of the common generic fibre, the induced map
between the reduced closures of its specializations is proper.}
{设 $K$ 非平凡赋值。Admissible 形式 blowup 在
$\widetilde K$ 上的特殊纤维之间诱导局部射影态射，因而诱导
适当态射。有限个 admissible 形式 blowup 的复合同样在特殊
纤维之间诱导适当态射。固定共同 generic fibre 的一个点后，
其各特殊化的约化闭包之间的诱导映射也适当。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Work on an affine formal open $\operatorname{Spf}(A_0)$,
and write the open ideal as $I=(f_0,\ldots,f_m)$.
The algebraic blowup of $\operatorname{Spec}A_0$ in $I$
is projective by \cite[Tag 02NS]{StacksAlg}.
In the notation of Theorem~\ref{fnd:formalblowupcharts},
its affine chart is $D_i=A_0[I/f_i]$ and the formal chart is
$B_i=\widehat D_i$. Both are $\pi$-torsion-free.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion} identifies
$D_i/\pi D_i$ with $B_i/\pi B_i$.
Since $\pi\in K^{\circ\circ}$, taking the further quotient
by $K^{\circ\circ}/(\pi)$ gives}
{在仿射形式开集 $\operatorname{Spf}(A_0)$ 上工作，并将开
理想写为 $I=(f_0,\ldots,f_m)$。
由 \cite[Tag 02NS]{StacksAlg}，沿 $I$ 对
$\operatorname{Spec}A_0$ 作代数 blowup 得到射影态射。
在 Theorem~\ref{fnd:formalblowupcharts} 的记号下，其仿射图
为 $D_i=A_0[I/f_i]$，形式图为 $B_i=\widehat D_i$，二者均
无 $\pi$-挠。
Lemma~\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion} 将 $D_i/\pi D_i$
与 $B_i/\pi B_i$ 识别。由于 $\pi\in K^{\circ\circ}$，
再模掉 $K^{\circ\circ}/(\pi)$，得到}
\[
  D_i/K^{\circ\circ}D_i\simeq B_i/K^{\circ\circ}B_i.
\]
\bi{These identifications respect the fractional chart transitions.
Thus the special fibre of the formal blowup is the special fibre
of the algebraic blowup. A local closed embedding of the latter
into a projective space remains a closed embedding after reduction.
The special-fibre map is consequently locally projective and is
proper by \cite[Tag 01WC]{StacksAlg}.
Finite composites are proper by \cite[Tag 01W3]{StacksAlg}.}
{这些识别与分式图册转移相容，因此形式 blowup 的特殊纤维恰为
代数 blowup 的特殊纤维。后者局部到射影空间的闭嵌入在约化后
仍为闭嵌入，所以特殊纤维映射局部射影，再由
\cite[Tag 01WC]{StacksAlg} 得到适当性。
有限复合由 \cite[Tag 01W3]{StacksAlg} 仍适当。}

\bi{Let $Z'$ and $Z$ be the reduced closures of the two
specializations. The map $Z'\to\mathfrak X_s$ factors through
$Z$: a function vanishing on $Z$ vanishes at the specified generic
point of $Z'$, and therefore vanishes on the reduced integral $Z'$.
Locally on $Z$, the scheme $Z'$ is a closed subscheme of the
base change of the preceding projective morphism.
It is therefore locally projective over $Z$, hence proper by the
same criterion. Applying this at each blowup gives the assertion
for any finite admissible modification.}
{令 $Z'$、$Z$ 为两次特殊化的约化闭包。映射
$Z'\to\mathfrak X_s$ 经由 $Z$ 分解：在 $Z$ 上消失的函数
在 $Z'$ 的指定泛点消失，因而在约化整概形 $Z'$ 上消失。
在 $Z$ 上局部地，$Z'$ 是此前射影态射的基变换的闭子概形，
所以 $Z'\to Z$ 局部射影，再由同一判据得到适当性。
逐个 blowup 应用此论证，即得任意有限 admissible 修正的结论。}
\end{proof}
