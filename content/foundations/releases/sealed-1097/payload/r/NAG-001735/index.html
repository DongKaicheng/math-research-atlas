Separating the branches of a finite morphism

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 307

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Separating the branches of a finite morphism}
{分离有限态射的分支}]
\label{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a norm-admissible finite morphism of
good analytic spaces. Its fibre over any $x\in X$ is a
finite set $\{y_1,\ldots,y_m\}$. Given neighbourhoods
$N_i$ of these points, there is an ordinary open
neighbourhood $U$ of $x$ such that}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 是 good 解析空间之间 norm-admissible 的有限
态射。它在任意 $x\in X$ 上的纤维为有限集
$\{y_1,\ldots,y_m\}$。给定这些点的邻域 $N_i$，存在
$x$ 的通常开邻域 $U$，使}
\[
 f^{-1}(U)=\coprod_{i=1}^m W_i,\qquad
 y_i\in W_i\subset N_i,
\]
\bi{where each $W_i$ is open and closed in $f^{-1}(U)$,
and $W_i\to U$ is finite. On ordinary local rings there
is a canonical product decomposition}
{其中每个 $W_i$ 在 $f^{-1}(U)$ 中开闭，且 $W_i\to U$
有限。在通常局部环上，有典范乘积分解}
\[
 (f_*\mathcal O_Y)_x
      \simeq \prod_{i=1}^m\mathcal O_{Y,y_i}.
\]
\bi{If $X=\mathcal M(A)$ and $Y=\mathcal M(B)$, its
left side is $B\otimes_A\mathcal O_{X,x}$.}
{若 $X=\mathcal M(A)$、$Y=\mathcal M(B)$，则左边为
$B\otimes_A\mathcal O_{X,x}$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Work over an affinoid neighbourhood $V$ of $x$.
The fibre has algebra $D=B\otimes_A\mathcal H(x)$,
finite dimensional over the complete field $\mathcal H(x)$,
by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange} and
Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres}.
Every prime of a finite-dimensional algebra over a field
is maximal, since a finite-dimensional domain is a field:
multiplication by a nonzero element is injective and hence
surjective. There are only finitely many maximal ideals.
Indeed, the Chinese remainder map for any finite collection
of distinct maximal ideals is surjective onto their residue
fields, so their number is at most $\dim_{\mathcal H(x)}D$.
Each residue field is finite over $\mathcal H(x)$ and
has exactly one extending absolute value by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}; the quotient map
is bounded for the canonical finite-dimensional norms.
Consequently $\mathcal M(D)$ has exactly one point for
each of these finitely many maximal ideals.}
{在 $x$ 的一个 affinoid 邻域 $V$ 上工作。由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange} 和
Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres}，纤维代数为
$D=B\otimes_A\mathcal H(x)$，在完备域 $\mathcal H(x)$
上有限维。域上有限维代数的每个素理想都是极大理想：
有限维整环中的非零元素乘法单射，故满射，所以该整环为域。
极大理想也只有有限个。确实，对任意有限组不同的极大理想，
中国剩余映射满射到其剩余域的乘积，所以个数至多为
$\dim_{\mathcal H(x)}D$。每个剩余域在 $\mathcal H(x)$
上有限，由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}，其绝对值
延拓唯一；商映射对典范有限维范数有界。因此，
$\mathcal M(D)$ 的点恰好与这有限个极大理想一一对应。}

\bi{The space $f^{-1}(V)$ is affinoid, hence compact
Hausdorff, and its map to $V$ is closed. Choose pairwise
disjoint ordinary open neighbourhoods $O_i\subset N_i$
of its fibre points. The complement of their union is
compact and has closed image not containing $x$.
Remove that image and intersect with the ordinary interior
of $V$ to obtain $U$. Then
$W_i=O_i\cap f^{-1}(U)$ give the stated open-and-closed
partition. For any affinoid domain $V'\subset U$,
$f^{-1}(V')=\mathcal M(B')$ is affinoid and finite
over $V'$. The function equal to one on
$W_i\cap f^{-1}(V')$ and zero on its complement is
analytic by sheaf gluing on this ordinary open partition.
It is an idempotent $e_i\in B'$, since the global
sections on an affinoid are its algebra.
Thus $W_i\cap f^{-1}(V')=\mathcal M(e_iB')$.
The summand $e_iB'$ is a finite module with its canonical
norm: projection by $e_i$ is bounded and finite-module
maps are norm-strict. This proves finiteness of $W_i\to U$.}
{$f^{-1}(V)$ 为 affinoid，因而紧 Hausdorff，且到 $V$ 的映射
闭。为纤维中的各点选择两两不交的通常开邻域
$O_i\subset N_i$。它们的并的补集紧，其像闭且不含 $x$。
去掉此像，再与 $V$ 的通常内部相交，得到 $U$。
于是 $W_i=O_i\cap f^{-1}(U)$ 给出所需的开闭分拆。
对任意 affinoid 域 $V'\subset U$，
$f^{-1}(V')=\mathcal M(B')$ 为 affinoid 且在 $V'$ 上有限。
在 $W_i\cap f^{-1}(V')$ 上等于一、在其补集上等于零的函数，
由此通常开分拆上的层粘合而解析。Affinoid 上的全局截面就是
其代数，因此该函数是 $B'$ 中的幂等元 $e_i$。
从而 $W_i\cap f^{-1}(V')=\mathcal M(e_iB')$。
此直和因子 $e_iB'$ 带有典范有限模范数：乘以 $e_i$ 的投影
有界，而有限模映射为 norm-strict。这证明 $W_i\to U$ 有限。}

\bi{Restriction of germs defines the displayed product
map on local rings. Given a germ at each $y_i$, choose
representatives on $N_i$, separate the neighbourhoods,
and shrink $U$ as just done. Their restrictions glue on
the disjoint $W_i$ to a section over $f^{-1}(U)$.
This proves surjectivity. If all germs of such a section
vanish, choose neighbourhoods where they vanish and
shrink once more; the section then vanishes on an entire
inverse-image neighbourhood. This proves injectivity.
In the affinoid case, take the filtered colimit over
affinoid neighbourhoods $V'$ of $x$. Finite base change
gives $\mathcal O(f^{-1}(V'))=B\otimes_A A_{V'}$;
ordinary tensor commutes with this colimit, which is
$\mathcal O_{X,x}$ by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks}.
This gives the final identification.}
{芽的限制给出所列局部环乘积映射。对每个 $y_i$ 给定一个芽，
在 $N_i$ 上选代表元，分离这些邻域，再按上述方法缩小 $U$。
它们在不交的 $W_i$ 上的限制粘合成 $f^{-1}(U)$ 上的截面，
故映射满。若一个截面在各 $y_i$ 的芽都为零，则选择使其为零
的邻域，再次缩小后，该截面在整个逆像邻域上为零，故映射单。
Affinoid 情形，对 $x$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $V'$ 取滤过余极限。
有限基变换给出
$\mathcal O(f^{-1}(V'))=B\otimes_A A_{V'}$；
普通张量与此余极限交换，而由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks}，余极限为
$\mathcal O_{X,x}$，得到最后的识别。}
\end{proof}
