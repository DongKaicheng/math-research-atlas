Generalized relative presentations

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 273

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Generalized relative presentations}{广义相对表示}]
\label{fnd:generalizedrelativepresentation}
\bi{Every bounded homomorphism $A\to B$ of generalized $K$-affinoid algebras extends to a bounded norm-strict surjection}
{广义 $K$-affinoid 代数之间的每个有界同态 $A\to B$ 都可延拓为有界且 norm-strict 的满射}
\[
 A\{r_1^{-1}T_1,\ldots,r_n^{-1}T_n\}\twoheadrightarrow B
\]
\bi{for suitable positive radii $r_i$. Its kernel is an actual closed ideal, and its quotient norm is equivalent to the given admissible norm on $B$.}
{其中 $r_i$ 为适当的正实数。其核是实际闭理想，商范数与 $B$ 上给定的 admissible 范数等价。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose an admissible presentation $K\{r^{-1}T\}\twoheadrightarrow B$, and temporarily use its quotient norm on $B$ and a presentation norm on $A$. The variable images $b_i$ satisfy $\|b_i^m\|\leq r_i^m$. The given bounded map on coefficients therefore extends by Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundedsubstitution} to the displayed bounded map. It is surjective because it extends the original presentation. For a fixed $c>1$, every nonzero $b\in B$ has a lift in $K\{r^{-1}T\}$ of norm at most $c\|b\|$. Its image in $A\{r^{-1}T\}$ has the same norm, since the coefficient inclusion $K\hookrightarrow A$ is isometric. These lifts and boundedness of the map compare its quotient norm with the norm of $B$ in both directions. Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation} makes the coefficient algebra affinoid, and Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules} makes its kernel ideal closed. Equivalent initial norms only change the constants. Zero algebras are immediate.}
{选择 admissible 表示 $K\{r^{-1}T\}\twoheadrightarrow B$，暂时在 $B$ 上采用其商范数，在 $A$ 上采用表示范数。变量的像 $b_i$ 满足 $\|b_i^m\|\leq r_i^m$，所以由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundedsubstitution}，给定的有界系数映射延拓为所列有界映射。它延拓原来的表示，因而满。固定 $c>1$，每个非零 $b\in B$ 在 $K\{r^{-1}T\}$ 中有范数至多为 $c\|b\|$ 的提升。由于系数包含 $K\hookrightarrow A$ 等距，其在 $A\{r^{-1}T\}$ 中的像具有相同范数。这些提升以及映射的有界性，从两个方向比较所得商范数与 $B$ 上的范数。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation}，系数代数为 affinoid；由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}，其核理想闭。等价的初始范数只改变常数。零代数的情形直接成立。}
\end{proof}
