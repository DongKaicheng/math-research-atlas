Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[The first continuous-metric Chern current]\label{f9si:chern}
Let $X$ be good, Hausdorff, and pure of analytic dimension $n\geq1$, and let $U\subseteq\Int(X/K)$ be ordinary open. Let $L$ be an analytic line bundle on $U$ with a supplied continuous metric. In a local frame $e_a$, put $u_a=-\log\|e_a\|$. The local currents
\begin{equation}\label{eq:chern}
 C(L,\|\cdot\|)|_{U_a}=\DD J_{u_a}
 \quad\text{of bidimension }(n-1,n-1)
\end{equation}
glue to a closed native current independent of frames, evaluated on every ordinary compact test in that bidegree. They are additive in tensor products. If a metric on $L^{\otimes e}$ is given and its positive real $e$-th root is used on $L$, the resulting current is $1/e$ of the current of the power metric. For a smooth potential it equals the current $\alpha\mapsto\int_U(\DD u_a)\wedge\alpha$ locally.

The same assertion holds against a supplied weighted analytic cycle in Theorem~\ref{f9si:cycle}, with $n$ replaced by its component dimension and its weights retained. No positivity, order-zero property of $C(L,\|\cdot\|)$, or second continuous multiplication is claimed.
\end{theorem}
