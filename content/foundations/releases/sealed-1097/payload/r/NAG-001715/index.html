Proper algebraic maps remain proper analytically

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 290

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Proper algebraic maps remain proper analytically}{适当代数映射的解析化仍适当}]
\label{fnd:analytificationproperpreservation}
\bi{If $f:Y\to Z$ is a proper morphism of finite-type $K$-schemes, then $f^{\an}:Y^{\an}\to Z^{\an}$ is proper. This includes nonreduced schemes and trivially valued complete ground fields.}
{若 $f:Y\to Z$ 是有限型 $K$-概形的适当态射，则 $f^{\an}:Y^{\an}\to Z^{\an}$ 适当。这也包括非既约概形及带平凡赋值的完备基域。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The target is Noetherian by \cite[Tag 01T6]{StacksAlg}. Apply Chow's lemma, \cite[Tag 0200, Lemma~30.18.1]{StacksAlg}, to $f$. It gives a proper surjective $\pi:Y'\to Y$ and an immersion $j:Y'\to\mathbf P^n_Z$. Since $f\pi$ is proper by \cite[Tag 01W3, Lemma~29.42.4]{StacksAlg}, and $\mathbf P^n_Z\to Z$ is separated, \cite[Tag 01W6, Lemma~29.42.7]{StacksAlg} shows that $j$ is proper. Its image is closed. An immersion with closed image is a closed immersion: factor it as a closed immersion into an open subspace of the target, and add the open complement of its closed image; the two local defining ideals glue on this open cover. Thus $f\pi$ is projective.}
{由 \cite[Tag 01T6]{StacksAlg}，目标为 Noetherian。对 $f$ 应用 Chow 引理 \cite[Tag 0200, Lemma~30.18.1]{StacksAlg}，得到适当满射 $\pi:Y'\to Y$ 及浸入 $j:Y'\to\mathbf P^n_Z$。由 \cite[Tag 01W3, Lemma~29.42.4]{StacksAlg}，$f\pi$ 适当；又因为 $\mathbf P^n_Z\to Z$ 分离，\cite[Tag 01W6, Lemma~29.42.7]{StacksAlg} 说明 $j$ 适当，因此其像闭。具有闭像的浸入是闭浸入：将其分解为到目标某个开子空间的闭浸入，再添上闭像的开补集，就可在这个开覆盖上粘合两边的局部定义理想。因此 $f\pi$ 射影。}

\bi{By Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationvaluedfibres}, $\pi^{\an}$ is surjective. Let $C$ be any compact Hausdorff subset of $Z^{\an}$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationprojectivecompact} makes $(f\pi)^{\an,-1}(C)$ compact. Its continuous image under $\pi^{\an}$ is exactly $f^{\an,-1}(C)$, so the latter is quasi-compact. The map $f$ is separated; Lemma~\ref{fnd:analytificationseparatedtopology} therefore makes this inverse image Hausdorff. Hence $f^{\an}$ is compact. Proposition~\ref{fnd:algebraicmorphismboundaryless} proves that it is boundaryless. These are the two defining conditions of an analytic proper morphism.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationvaluedfibres}，$\pi^{\an}$ 满射。取 $Z^{\an}$ 中任意紧 Hausdorff 子集 $C$。Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationprojectivecompact} 说明 $(f\pi)^{\an,-1}(C)$ 紧。其在 $\pi^{\an}$ 下的连续像恰好是 $f^{\an,-1}(C)$，因此后者拟紧。由于 $f$ 分离，Lemma~\ref{fnd:analytificationseparatedtopology} 说明此逆像为 Hausdorff，故 $f^{\an}$ 为紧映射。Proposition~\ref{fnd:algebraicmorphismboundaryless} 又证明它无边界；这就是解析适当态射的两项定义条件。}
\end{proof}
