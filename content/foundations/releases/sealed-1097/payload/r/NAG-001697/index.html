Completed residue fields on affinoid domains

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 278

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Completed residue fields on affinoid domains}{Affinoid 域上的完备剩余域}]
\label{fnd:domainresiduefields}
\bi{If $A\to B$ represents an affinoid domain $U\subset\mathcal M(A)$, then at every $x\in U$ the canonical map $\mathcal H_A(x)\to\mathcal H_B(x)$ is an isometric isomorphism.}
{若 $A\to B$ 表示 affinoid 域 $U\subset\mathcal M(A)$，则对每个 $x\in U$，典范映射 $\mathcal H_A(x)\to\mathcal H_B(x)$ 为等距同构。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert} gives a finite cover of $U$ by domains rational both over $A$ and over $B$. Choose a member containing $x$, with algebra $C$. Both maps $\mathcal H_A(x)\to\mathcal H_C(x)$ and $\mathcal H_B(x)\to\mathcal H_C(x)$ are isomorphisms by Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalresiduefields}. Their triangle commutes on the evaluated elements of $A$ and therefore on its completed fraction field, which proves the assertion.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert} 给出 $U$ 的有限覆盖，其各成员同时在 $A$ 与 $B$ 上 rational。选取一个包含 $x$ 的成员，记其代数为 $C$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalresiduefields}，映射 $\mathcal H_A(x)\to\mathcal H_C(x)$、$\mathcal H_B(x)\to\mathcal H_C(x)$ 均为同构。相应三角形在 $A$ 的取值元素上交换，因此在其完备分式域上交换，得到所需断言。}
\end{proof}
