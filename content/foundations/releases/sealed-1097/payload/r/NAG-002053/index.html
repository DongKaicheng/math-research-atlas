NAG-002053 · Accepted whole

All lifts of a finite set of nodes

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 668

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{All lifts of a finite set of nodes}
{有限结点组的全部提升}]
\label{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting}
\bi{Let $\mathcal X\to\operatorname{Spec}O$ be finitely
presented and at-worst-nodal of relative dimension one, and let
$q_1,\ldots,q_s$ be a finite set of nodes of $\mathcal X_k$.
There is a finite Galois extension $L/K$ such that every point
$q'$ of $\mathcal X_{\widetilde L}$ over one of the $q_i$
is a $\widetilde L$-rational split node. Writing $O'=L^\circ$,
each such point admits an affine $O'$-scheme $U$, a
$\widetilde L$-rational point $u$, and pointed \'etale maps}
{设 $\mathcal X\to\operatorname{Spec}O$ 有限表示、相对
维数为一且至多具有结点，并设 $q_1,\ldots,q_s$ 为
$\mathcal X_k$ 的有限结点组。存在有限 Galois 扩张
$L/K$，使 $\mathcal X_{\widetilde L}$ 中位于任意 $q_i$
上方的每个点 $q'$ 都是 $\widetilde L$-有理 split node。
令 $O'=L^\circ$，则每个这样的点都具有一个仿射
$O'$-概形 $U$、一个 $\widetilde L$-有理点 $u$ 及
带点 \'etale 态射}
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 (\mathcal X_{O'},q')\ \longleftarrow\ (U,u)
   \ \longrightarrow\
 \bigl(\operatorname{Spec}O'[S,T]/(ST-a),\,\overline 0\bigr),\\
 a\in L^{\circ\circ}.
 \end{gathered}
\]
\bi{Here $\overline 0$ is the special-fibre origin, whose prime
ideal is $(L^{\circ\circ},S,T)$. If $\mathcal X_K$ is smooth,
every resulting parameter
satisfies $0<|a|<1$. One may take
$|L^\times|=|K^\times|$ and
$\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)\simeq
\operatorname{Gal}(\widetilde L/k)$ by reduction.}
{这里 $\overline 0$ 为特殊纤维原点，其素理想为
$(L^{\circ\circ},S,T)$。若 $\mathcal X_K$ 光滑，
则所得每个参数均满足
$0<|a|<1$。还可使 $|L^\times|=|K^\times|$，且约化诱导
$\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)\simeq
\operatorname{Gal}(\widetilde L/k)$。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{If the node set is empty, take $L=K$. Otherwise, for each
$q_i$, choose the original pointed normal-form
diagram and point $u_i$ used at the beginning of the proof of
Theorem~\ref{cand:pointednodalvaluationchart}. Put
$k_i=\kappa(q_i)$ and $l_i=\kappa(u_i)$.
That proof shows that $k_i/k$ and $l_i/k_i$ are finite separable.
Embed these fields compatibly into one separable closure of $k$.
Choose primitive generators for the finitely many $l_i/k$
by \cite[Tag 030N]{StacksAlg},
and let $l/k$ be the splitting field of their minimal
polynomials. It is finite and normal by
\cite[Tag 09HU]{StacksAlg}. Its generators are separable,
so \cite[Tag 09HC]{StacksAlg} makes it separable as well.
Thus $l/k$ is finite Galois and contains every $l_i$ and its
normal closure.}
{若结点组为空，则取 $L=K$。否则，对每个 $q_i$，选择
Theorem~\ref{cand:pointednodalvaluationchart} 证明开始时
使用的带点正规形图及点 $u_i$。令
$k_i=\kappa(q_i)$、$l_i=\kappa(u_i)$。
该证明说明 $k_i/k$ 与 $l_i/k_i$ 均有限可分。将这些域
相容嵌入 $k$ 的同一个可分闭包。由
\cite[Tag 030N]{StacksAlg}，对有限多个 $l_i/k$
选择本原元，并取其极小多项式的共同分裂域 $l/k$。
由 \cite[Tag 09HU]{StacksAlg}，它有限且正规；其生成元
均可分，由 \cite[Tag 09HC]{StacksAlg}，整个扩张也可分。
因此 $l/k$ 有限 Galois，并包含每个 $l_i$ 及其正规闭包。}

\bi{Apply Lemma~\ref{cand:galoisresidueextensionlift} to obtain
$L/K$ with $\widetilde L=l$. The points over $q_i$ are the
points of $\operatorname{Spec}(k_i\otimes_k l)$, and}
{应用 Lemma~\ref{cand:galoisresidueextensionlift}，得到
满足 $\widetilde L=l$ 的 $L/K$。位于 $q_i$ 上方的点就是
$\operatorname{Spec}(k_i\otimes_k l)$ 的点，并且}
\[
 k_i\otimes_k l\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
     \prod_{\sigma\in\operatorname{Hom}_k(k_i,l)}l,
 \qquad b\otimes c\longmapsto(\sigma(b)c)_\sigma.
\]
\bi{Indeed, a primitive-element presentation writes the left
side as $l[T]/p_i(T)$, where $p_i$ splits into distinct linear
factors. Evaluation at its roots is invertible by the
Lagrange interpolation polynomials. Thus every point over
$q_i$ is rational and is indexed by an embedding
$\sigma:k_i\hookrightarrow l$.}
{事实上，本原元表示将左端写为 $l[T]/p_i(T)$，其中
$p_i$ 分裂为互不相同的一次因子。用 Lagrange 插值多项式
可逆转在各根处的取值映射。因此 $q_i$ 上方的每个点均有理，
并由某个嵌入 $\sigma:k_i\hookrightarrow l$ 标记。}

\bi{Fix such an embedding. By
\cite[Tag 0BME, part (2)]{StacksAlg}, it extends to a
$k$-automorphism of $l$, since $l/k$ is normal. Restriction
to the chosen subfield $l_i$ gives an embedding
$\tau:l_i\hookrightarrow l$ extending $\sigma$.
The map $\operatorname{Spec}l\to U_i$ defined by $\tau$
gives a rational point of $(U_i)_{O'}$ lying over the selected
point $q'$ indexed by $\sigma$. Its image in the \'etale
base neighbourhood $(V_i)_{O'}$ is rational as well.}
{固定这样一个嵌入。由于 $l/k$ 正规，
\cite[Tag 0BME, part (2)]{StacksAlg} 将它延拓为 $l$ 的
$k$-自同构。将此自同构限制到所选子域 $l_i$，便得到
延拓 $\sigma$ 的嵌入 $\tau:l_i\hookrightarrow l$。
由 $\tau$ 定义的映射 $\operatorname{Spec}l\to U_i$
给出 $(U_i)_{O'}$ 的一个有理点，它位于由 $\sigma$
标记的所选点 $q'$ 上方；其在 \'etale 基底邻域
$(V_i)_{O'}$ 中的像也为有理点。}

\bi{Now reuse the henselian-section step of the proof of
Theorem~\ref{cand:pointednodalvaluationchart}, with this
rational point and the fixed field $L$. Lemma
\ref{cand:completevaluationhenselian} supplies a section
of $(V_i)_{O'}\to\operatorname{Spec}O'$ through the point.
Its image is open. Pulling the original diagram back along
that section and then taking an affine neighbourhood of the
rational point gives exactly the two pointed \'etale maps
displayed in the statement. The remaining local arguments
of that theorem apply unchanged: they identify the image in
the standard chart with its origin, give
$a\in L^{\circ\circ}$, identify the completed special local
ring at $q'$ with $l[[S,T]]/(ST)$, and prove $a\ne0$ when
the generic fibre is smooth. No enlargement of $L$ occurs
in any of those steps.}
{现在对这个有理点及已固定的域 $L$，复用
Theorem~\ref{cand:pointednodalvaluationchart} 证明中的
henselian 截面步骤。Lemma
\ref{cand:completevaluationhenselian} 给出
$(V_i)_{O'}\to\operatorname{Spec}O'$ 的经过该点的截面，
其像为开集。沿此截面拉回原图，再取所选有理点的仿射邻域，
便得到陈述中的两个带点 \'etale 态射。该定理余下的局部
论证原样适用：标准坐标片中的像为原点，
$a\in L^{\circ\circ}$，$q'$ 处特殊纤维局部环的完备化
同构于 $l[[S,T]]/(ST)$，并且泛纤维光滑时 $a\ne0$。
这些步骤均不再扩大 $L$。}

\bi{There are only finitely many $i$ and finitely many
embeddings for each $i$. Applying the preceding construction
to each one proves the assertion for every lift of every
specified node, all over the same $L$. The value-group and
Galois assertions were already supplied by the first lemma.}
{指标 $i$ 只有有限多个，每个 $i$ 的嵌入也只有有限多个。
对它们分别应用上述构造，便在同一个 $L$ 上处理了每个
指定结点的全部提升。值群与 Galois 断言已由第一引理给出。}
\end{proof}
