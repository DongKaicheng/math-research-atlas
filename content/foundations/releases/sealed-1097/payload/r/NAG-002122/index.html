Equivariant resolution of a split nodal model

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 711

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Equivariant resolution of a split nodal model}
{分裂结点模型的等变解消}]
\label{cand:equivariantnodaldvrresolution}
\bi{Let $\mathcal Y/R$ be a split nodal model. Repeatedly
blow up the reduced union of all nonregular points of the
total space. This process terminates with a regular split
nodal model $\mathcal Y'/R$. It preserves projectivity and
is functorial for semilinear isomorphisms preserving $R$.
In particular every finite semilinear group action on
$\mathcal Y$ lifts to $\mathcal Y'$.}
{设 $\mathcal Y/R$ 为分裂结点模型。反复沿全空间全部
非正则点的约化并作 blowup。此过程有限步后终止，
得到正则分裂结点模型 $\mathcal Y'/R$。它保持射影性，
并与保持 $R$ 的半线性同构相容。特别地，
$\mathcal Y$ 上每个有限半线性群作用都提升到
$\mathcal Y'$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Nonregular points lie among the finitely many special
nodes. By the pointed charts of
Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting}, after absorbing
a unit into one coordinate their local equation is
$xy=\varpi^n$, $n\geq1$, in an \'etale neighborhood with
the same residue field. The completed local ring is
$R[[x,y]]/(xy-\varpi^n)$ by
\cite[Tag 0AGX]{StacksAlg}; it is regular precisely for
$n=1$. For $n\geq2$, blow up $(x,y,\varpi)$.
The $\varpi$-chart has equation
$x_1y_1=\varpi^{n-2}$, where $x=\varpi x_1$ and
$y=\varpi y_1$. On the $x$-chart put
$u=\varpi/x$; after removing the cancelled torsion the
equation is $xu=\varpi$, with $y=x^{n-1}u^n$.
The $y$-chart is symmetric. These are the local charts
computed in the proof of \cite[Tag 0CDE]{StacksAlg};
flat base change of blowups is
\cite[Tag 0805]{StacksAlg}.}
{非正则点位于特殊纤维有限个结点之中。由
Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting} 的带点
坐标图，将单位元吸收到一个坐标后，其局部方程在
保持剩余域的 \'etale 邻域内为
$xy=\varpi^n$，$n\geq1$。由
\cite[Tag 0AGX]{StacksAlg}，完成局部环为
$R[[x,y]]/(xy-\varpi^n)$，它恰在 $n=1$ 时正则。
当 $n\geq2$ 时，对 $(x,y,\varpi)$ 作 blowup。
$\varpi$-坐标片的方程为
$x_1y_1=\varpi^{n-2}$，其中
$x=\varpi x_1$、$y=\varpi y_1$。
在 $x$-坐标片令 $u=\varpi/x$；消去所得挠关系后，
方程为 $xu=\varpi$，且 $y=x^{n-1}u^n$。
$y$-坐标片对称。这些就是
\cite[Tag 0CDE]{StacksAlg} 的证明中计算的局部坐标片；
blowup 的平坦基变换见 \cite[Tag 0805]{StacksAlg}。}

\bi{All new special nodes are rational and split. The two
outer charts are regular. In the middle chart, $n=2$
gives a smooth chart, $n=3$ a regular node, and $n\geq4$
gives a unique remaining nonregular point with exponent
$n-2$. Around each center choose an affine or \'etale
neighborhood omitting the other finitely many centers.
The other center ideals are units there, so blowup of the
finite reduced union restricts to exactly the local
calculation just made. Every round strictly decreases
each surviving exponent; after finitely many rounds no
nonregular point remains. The center ideal is of finite
type on this Noetherian scheme, so blowups are projective
by \cite[Tag 02NS]{StacksAlg}; composition preserves
projectivity over the affine base by
\cite[Tag 0C4P]{StacksAlg}. They leave the generic fibre
unchanged. Finally the
nonregular locus and its reduced ideal are invariant
under every semilinear isomorphism. Such an isomorphism
acts on all powers of that ideal, hence on its Rees
algebra and its Proj. These induced maps commute with
composition, giving the asserted functoriality.}
{全部新特殊结点仍为有理分裂结点。两个外侧坐标片
正则。在中间坐标片，$n=2$ 给出光滑坐标片，$n=3$
给出正则结点，$n\geq4$ 则留下唯一一个指数为
$n-2$ 的非正则点。在每个中心附近选择仿射或
\'etale 邻域，使其避开其余有限个中心。
其余中心的理想在此为单位理想，因此沿有限约化并
作 blowup 的限制恰为上述局部计算。
每轮都严格降低仍存在的指数，因此有限轮后不再有
非正则点。中心理想在此 Noetherian 概形上有限型，
故 \cite[Tag 02NS]{StacksAlg} 说明 blowup 射影；
\cite[Tag 0C4P]{StacksAlg} 说明在仿射基底上复合
保持射影性。Blowup 不改变泛纤维。最后，
非正则点集及其约化理想在每个半线性同构下不变。
这样的同构作用于该理想的全部幂，因而作用于其
Rees 代数及 Proj；所诱导的映射与复合相容，
即得所述函子性。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{For the next three results, $R$ is a complete discrete
valuation ring with fraction field $F$, uniformizer $\varpi$,
and residue field $l$. A \emph{split nodal model} is a flat,
proper, finitely presented relative curve with smooth
generic fibre, whose special-fibre singularities are
$l$-rational split nodes. Its other special-fibre points
are smooth. At each node we include a rational pointed
\'etale chart over $R$ of the form supplied by
Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting}.
The word regular refers to its total space.}
{下三个结果中，$R$ 为完备离散赋值环，分式域为 $F$，
一致化参数为 $\varpi$，剩余域为 $l$。
\emph{分裂结点模型} 指平坦、proper、有限表示的相对
曲线，其泛纤维光滑，特殊纤维的奇点都是 $l$-有理
split nodes，特殊纤维其余点均光滑。并在每个结点配备
Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting}
所给形式的 $R$ 上有理带点 \'etale 坐标图。
正则性指全空间的正则性。}
