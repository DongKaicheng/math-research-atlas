Effective finite-module descent for a rational covering

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 372

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Effective finite-module descent for a rational covering}{Rational 覆盖的有限模有效下降}]
\label{fnd:generalizedrationaldescent}
\bi{Let $V_1,\ldots,V_n$ be a finite rational covering of $X=\mathcal M(A)$, with algebras $A_i$ and joint algebras $A_{ij},A_{ijk}$. Suppose finite $A_i$-modules $N_i$ are given, together with isomorphisms}
{设 $V_1,\ldots,V_n$ 是 $X=\mathcal M(A)$ 的有限 rational 覆盖，其代数及联合代数记作 $A_i,A_{ij},A_{ijk}$。设给定有限 $A_i$-模 $N_i$ 及同构}
\[
 \alpha_{ij}: A_{ij}\otimes_{A_i}N_i
        \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ } A_{ij}\otimes_{A_j}N_j
\]
\bi{satisfying the identity and cocycle conditions on all triple intersections. Define the matching $A$-module}
{它们在全部三重交叠上满足恒等及 cocycle 条件。定义匹配 $A$-模}
\[
 N=\left\{(n_i)\in\prod_iN_i:
      \alpha_{ij}(1\otimes n_i)=1\otimes n_j 
                         \text{in }A_{ij}\otimes_{A_j}N_j\right\}.
\]
\bi{Then $N$ is finite over $A$. Its subspace norm from $\prod_iN_i$ is canonical up to uniform equivalence, and the maps}
{则 $N$ 在 $A$ 上有限生成。从 $\prod_iN_i$ 继承的子空间范数在一致等价意义下为典范范数，且映射}
\[
 \theta_i:A_i\otimes_A N\longrightarrow N_i,
 \qquad b\otimes(n_j)_j\longmapsto b n_i,
\]
\bi{are isomorphisms with bounded inverses and recover the prescribed overlap identifications. The construction is functorial and gives an equivalence between finite $A$-modules and these finite-module gluing data. It also identifies $N$ with the global sections of the sheaf obtained by gluing the $\widetilde{N_i}_{V_i}$.}
{是具有有界逆的同构，并恢复给定的交叠识别。此构造具有函子性，给出有限 $A$-模与这些有限模粘合数据之间的范畴等价。它还将 $N$ 识别为粘合各 $\widetilde{N_i}_{V_i}$ 所得层的整体截面。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{\textbf{Step 1: the matching Banach module.} Choose the defining rational quotient norms, so that $A\to A_i$ is contractive, and quotient canonical norms on the $N_i$. The module actions are contractive. The overlap modules are finite Banach modules and the $\alpha_{ij}$ are bounded by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}. Thus $N$ is the kernel of a bounded $A$-linear map between finite products of Banach spaces. It is a Banach $A$-module with contractive action; finite generation is not used here. Uniformly equivalent choices of the finite local norms give uniformly equivalent norms on $N$.}
{\textbf{步骤 1：匹配 Banach 模。} 选择定义 rational 商范数，使 $A\to A_i$ 为 contractive，并给 $N_i$ 配备商典范范数；模作用也为 contractive。交叠模是有限 Banach 模，而各 $\alpha_{ij}$ 由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules} 有界。因此 $N$ 是 Banach 空间有限直积之间一个有界 $A$-线性映射的核。它是具有 contractive 作用的 Banach $A$-模；这里尚未使用有限生成性。对有限个局部范数作一致等价的改变，只会使 $N$ 的范数一致等价地改变。}

\bi{\textbf{Step 2: strictify the finite gluing diagram.} Choose one free extension $L=K_r$ by Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification} for the presentation of $A$ and the finitely many covering radii. We may require $L$ to be nontrivially valued: if $K$ is trivial, include any additional radius different from $1$ in the finite list. The extended cover is a unit-radius rational cover after the bounded comparisons of Proposition~\ref{fnd:radicalrationalpresentation}. Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedquotientbasechange} identifies its actual intersection algebras and the extended finite modules, including the overlap maps. The cocycle conditions persist. Lemma~\ref{fnd:coefficientmatchingkernel} gives a bounded identification}
{\textbf{步骤 2：严格化有限粘合图表。} 对 $A$ 的表示及有限个覆盖半径，按 Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification} 选择同一个自由扩域 $L=K_r$。还可要求 $L$ 的赋值非平凡：当 $K$ 赋值平凡时，在有限列表中另加入任一不同于 $1$ 的半径即可。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:radicalrationalpresentation} 的有界比较，扩张后的覆盖可写成单位半径 rational 覆盖。Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedquotientbasechange} 识别其实际交叠代数、扩张后的有限模以及交叠映射；cocycle 条件保持。Lemma~\ref{fnd:coefficientmatchingkernel} 给出有界识别}
\[
 N_r\simeq
 \ker\left(\prod_i (N_i)_r\longrightarrow
        \prod_{i,j}(A_{ij}\otimes_{A_j}N_j)_r\right).
\]

\bi{The strict local module sheaves glue to a sheaf $\mathcal F_r$ on the strict rational site. Explicitly, on a rational object $W$ take the matching families of sections on $W\cap (V_i)_r$, using the prescribed overlap maps. The sheaf axiom follows by gluing in each local module sheaf and then checking the overlap identities after restriction to a cover. On a slice contained in $(V_i)_r$, projection to the $i$th component is an isomorphism: the other components are its restrictions transported by $\alpha_{ij}$, and the cocycle identity is precisely their compatibility. Consequently $\mathcal F_r$ has finite local models, and its global sections are the displayed kernel. The strict Kiehl theorem, Theorem~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalkiehl}, now proves that $N_r$ is finite over $A_r$ and that its restriction maps recover $(N_i)_r$.}
{Strict 局部模层粘合为 strict rational site 上的层 $\mathcal F_r$。具体地，在 rational 对象 $W$ 上取 $W\cap(V_i)_r$ 上、相对于指定交叠映射的匹配截面族。先在每个局部模层中粘合，再在覆盖上核对交叠等式，即得层公理。在包含于 $(V_i)_r$ 的 slice 上，到第 $i$ 个分量的投影为同构：其他分量就是其限制经 $\alpha_{ij}$ 转移所得，而 cocycle 恒等式恰好保证相容。因此 $\mathcal F_r$ 具有有限局部模型，且整体截面就是所列的核。此时 strict Kiehl 定理 Theorem~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalkiehl} 说明 $N_r$ 在 $A_r$ 上有限生成，并且其限制映射恢复各 $(N_i)_r$。}

\bi{\textbf{Step 3: descend finite generation and the restriction maps.} Since $L$ is nontrivially valued, the last clause of Theorem~\ref{fnd:freebanachfinitegenerationdescent} applies to the Banach module $N$. It proves finite generation over $A$ and equivalence of its matching norm with a canonical norm. The maps $\theta_i$ are therefore bounded maps between finite canonical $A_i$-modules. By Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedquotientbasechange}, their free extensions are exactly the isomorphisms obtained in Step 2. The reflection assertion of Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusstrictexactness} implies that each $\theta_i$ is an isomorphism with bounded inverse. Their overlap identities hold by the definition of $N$.}
{\textbf{步骤 3：下降有限生成性及限制映射。} 由于 $L$ 的赋值非平凡，可以将 Theorem~\ref{fnd:freebanachfinitegenerationdescent} 的最后一项用于 Banach 模 $N$。它给出 $N$ 在 $A$ 上的有限生成性，以及匹配范数与典范范数的一致等价性。因此各 $\theta_i$ 是有限典范 $A_i$-模之间的有界映射。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedquotientbasechange}，它们的自由扩张正是步骤 2 中得到的同构。Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusstrictexactness} 的反射断言于是说明每个 $\theta_i$ 都是具有有界逆的同构。交叠恒等式由 $N$ 的定义成立。}

\bi{A morphism of gluing data maps matching families to matching families, which gives functoriality. Starting with a finite $A$-module $M$, the degree-zero equalizer in Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfiniterationalcech} identifies its matching module with $M$. Starting with gluing data, the maps $\theta_i$ identify their reconstruction with the original data, compatibly with morphisms. These are the two inverse natural isomorphisms. The same explicit sheaf gluing construction as in Step 2, now over $K$, and Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalslicemodules} identify its global sections with $N$. Empty components contribute zero modules. If $X$ is empty, Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion} gives $A=0$ and every assertion is immediate.}
{粘合数据的态射把匹配族送到匹配族，故得到函子性。从有限 $A$-模 $M$ 出发，Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfiniterationalcech} 的零次等化子把其匹配模识别为 $M$。从粘合数据出发，各 $\theta_i$ 将重构与原数据识别，并与态射相容。这就是两个互逆的自然同构。在 $K$ 上使用步骤 2 的同一显式层粘合构造，再由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalslicemodules}，整体截面便识别为 $N$。空成员只贡献零模；若 $X$ 为空，则 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion} 给出 $A=0$，全部断言直接成立。}
\end{proof}
