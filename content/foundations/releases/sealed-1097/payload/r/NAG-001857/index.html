Generic-fibre comparison for all characteristic-zero residue fields

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 464

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Generic-fibre comparison for all characteristic-zero residue fields}{任意特征零剩余域的泛纤维比较}]
\label{fnd:charzeroallresiduegenericcomparison}
\bi{Let $K$ be any complete field of characteristic zero and let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good analytic spaces. If $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$ and $\mathcal O_{X,f(y)}$ is a field, then}
{设 $K$ 为任意特征零完备域，$f:Y\to X$ 为 good 解析空间之间的态射。若 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$ 且 $\mathcal O_{X,f(y)}$ 为域，则}
\[
                    \mathcal O_{Y,y}\longrightarrow
                                      \mathcal O_{Y_{f(y)},y}
\]
\bi{is a regular local homomorphism.}
{为正则局部同态。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $x=f(y)$ and $F=\mathcal H(x)$. If $F$ is nontrivially valued, use Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroinnergenericfibreregularity}. Suppose that $F$ is trivially valued. Form $X^*=X\times_K\mathbb A_K^{1,\mathrm{an}}$ and its point $x^*$ as in Lemma~\ref{fnd:charzerogaussfieldgerm}; its ordinary local ring is a field and its completed residue field is $F_r$. Put $Y^*=Y\times_X X^*=Y\times_K\mathbb A_K^{1,\mathrm{an}}$, and choose the radius-$r$ Gauss point $y^*$ above $y$. Here the Gauss seminorm on $\mathcal H(y)[T]$ is multiplicative for any radius; $r$ need not be free over $\mathcal H(y)$. Its restriction to $F[T]$ is the chosen point $x^*$.}
{置 $x=f(y)$、$F=\mathcal H(x)$。若 $F$ 非平凡赋值，应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroinnergenericfibreregularity}。现设 $F$ 平凡赋值。构造 $X^*=X\times_K\mathbb A_K^{1,\mathrm{an}}$ 及 Lemma~\ref{fnd:charzerogaussfieldgerm} 中的点 $x^*$；其普通局部环为域，完备剩余域为 $F_r$。令 $Y^*=Y\times_X X^*=Y\times_K\mathbb A_K^{1,\mathrm{an}}$，并选择 $y$ 上方的半径 $r$ Gauss 点 $y^*$。这里对任意半径，$\mathcal H(y)[T]$ 上的 Gauss 半范数都可乘；无需要求 $r$ 在 $\mathcal H(y)$ 上自由。它限制到 $F[T]$ 后恰为所选点 $x^*$。}

\bi{Interior survives the base change. Write}
{内部条件在基变换下保持。记}
\[
 S=\mathcal O_{Y,y},\quad S^*=\mathcal O_{Y^*,y^*},\quad
 T=\mathcal O_{Y_x,y},\quad T^*=\mathcal O_{Y^*_{x^*},y^*}.
\]
\bi{The smooth projection $Y^*\to Y$ makes $S\to S^*$ regular by Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroquasismoothordinaryregular}. The nontrivially valued comparison just proved makes $S^*\to T^*$ regular. Moreover $Y^*_{x^*}=(Y_x)_{F_r}$ by complete base-change associativity, so $T\to T^*$ is faithfully flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}. These are maps of Noetherian local rings and form the expected commutative square with $S\to T$.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroquasismoothordinaryregular}，光滑投影 $Y^*\to Y$ 使 $S\to S^*$ 正则。刚才证明的非平凡赋值比较使 $S^*\to T^*$ 正则。此外，完备基变换的结合性给出 $Y^*_{x^*}=(Y_x)_{F_r}$，所以由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}，$T\to T^*$ 忠实平坦。这些都是 Noetherian 局部环之间的同态，并与 $S\to T$ 构成相应交换方块。}

\bi{We spell out composition and descent of regularity in this square. The composite $S\to T^*$ is flat. For a prime $\mathfrak p\subset S$ and a finite field extension $E/\kappa(\mathfrak p)$, the algebra $S^*\otimes_SE$ is regular. Base change of the regular map $S^*\to T^*$ is flat with geometrically regular fibres; localization and \cite[Tag 031E]{StacksAlg} make $T^*\otimes_SE$ regular. Thus $S\to T^*$ is regular. Faithful flatness of $T\to T^*$ descends $S$-flatness: tensor any injection of $S$-modules first with $T$ and then with the faithfully flat $T$-module $T^*$, and detect its kernel. Finally}
{在此方块中，将正则性的复合与下降展开如下。复合 $S\to T^*$ 平坦。对素理想 $\mathfrak p\subset S$ 及有限域扩张 $E/\kappa(\mathfrak p)$，代数 $S^*\otimes_SE$ 正则。正则映射 $S^*\to T^*$ 的基变换平坦且具有几何正则纤维；局部化并应用 \cite[Tag 031E]{StacksAlg}，知 $T^*\otimes_SE$ 正则。因此 $S\to T^*$ 正则。$T\to T^*$ 的忠实平坦性使 $S$-平坦性下降：对任意 $S$-模单射，先与 $T$ 张量，再与忠实平坦 $T$-模 $T^*$ 张量，即可检测其核。最后，}
\[
                         T\otimes_SE\longrightarrow T^*\otimes_SE
\]
\bi{is faithfully flat and its target is regular. At every prime of the source choose a prime above it and apply regular-local-ring descent, \cite[Tag 00OF]{StacksAlg}. Hence every $T\otimes_SE$ is regular. This proves all geometrically regular prime fibres of $S\to T$, and completes the proof.}
{忠实平坦，且目标正则。在源的每个素理想上方选择一个素理想，再应用正则局部环下降 \cite[Tag 00OF]{StacksAlg}，知每个 $T\otimes_SE$ 正则。因此 $S\to T$ 的全部素理想纤维均几何正则，证明完成。}
\end{proof}
