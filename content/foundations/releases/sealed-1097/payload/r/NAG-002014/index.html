\begin{theorem}[\bi{Smooth envelopes of good quasi-smooth germs}{Good 准光滑芽的光滑环境}]
\label{fnd:quasismoothsmoothenvelope}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good analytic spaces over any complete field. It is quasi-smooth at $y$ if and only if an ordinary affinoid neighborhood $V$ of $y$ is, over an ordinary neighborhood of $x=f(y)$, an affinoid domain of a smooth analytic $X$-space. If the differential rank at $y$ is $d$, the smooth ambient space may be chosen of relative dimension $d$.}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 为任意完备域上 good 解析空间之间的态射。它在 $y$ 处准光滑，当且仅当 $y$ 的某个普通 affinoid 邻域 $V$，在 $x=f(y)$ 的普通邻域上，是某个光滑解析 $X$-空间的 affinoid 域。若 $y$ 处的微分秩为 $d$，则光滑环境空间可取为相对维数 $d$。}
\end{theorem}
