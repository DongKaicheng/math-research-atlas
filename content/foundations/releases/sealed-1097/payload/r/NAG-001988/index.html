Formal-model realization of a strict germ

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 589

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Formal-model realization of a strict germ}
{Strict 芽的形式模型实现}]
\label{fnd:formalmodelgermrealization}
\bi{Let $U$ be a quasi-compact quasi-separated strictly analytic
neighbourhood of $x$ over a nontrivially valued field $K$, and let
$\mathfrak U$ be any admissible formal model of $U$.
Write $F_0=\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}$, and let $Z$ be the reduced
closure in $\mathfrak U_s$ of the image of
$\operatorname{Spec}F_0\to\mathfrak U_s$. Then there is a
canonical isomorphism}
{设 $U$ 为非平凡赋值域 $K$ 上的拟紧拟分离 strict 解析邻域，
其中 $x\in U$，并设 $\mathfrak U$ 为 $U$ 的任意 admissible
形式模型。记 $F_0=\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}$，以 $Z$ 记
$\operatorname{Spec}F_0\to\mathfrak U_s$ 的像在
$\mathfrak U_s$ 中的约化闭包。则存在典范同构}
\[
       \operatorname{Val}(Z,F_0)\simeq\mathscr R_0(U,x).
\]
\bi{It is independent of the formal model and the neighbourhood.
Common admissible modifications realize the comparison through
proper pointed residual schemes, inducing the identity on $F_0$.}
{该同构与形式模型及邻域无关。共同 admissible 修正通过适当的
带点剩余概形实现比较，并在 $F_0$ 上诱导恒等映射。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A formal model of \(U\), in the standing convention of
\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:formalmodels]{Formal models and their two fibres}},
is given with an identification of its analytic generic fibre with
\(U\). The generic fibre in that convention is the gluing of the
actual formal-affine generic charts along their analytic domains.
Transport this gluing to \(U\) by the specified identification.
Its formal-affine generic charts thus give the compatible
admissible chart family on \(U\). On the formal principal opens the
restriction maps are the bounded maps of
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalprincipalopenflat}; on intersections they
are checked on common principal refinements. The underlying
specialization maps on the affine charts are those of
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affineformalspecializationsurjective} and agree
there by contraction. This uses the generic fibre of the given
model as data. Its finite chart cover and the atlases for its
modifications are obtained by the compact common-model atlas
argument.
The \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:formalopenstracesonsuppliedatlases]{principal traces and contraction}}
identify the full inverse images of the formal affine opens and
the specialization maps on their overlaps.}
{依照 \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:formalmodels]{形式模型及其两种纤维}}
中的约定，$U$ 的形式模型带有其解析泛纤维与 $U$ 的指定同构。
该泛纤维由实际形式仿射泛纤维图沿解析域粘合而成。
沿指定同构把这种粘合传到 $U$，便得到相容的 admissible 图族。
主形式开集上的限制是
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalprincipalopenflat}
给出的有界映射；交叠上的限制在共同主开加细上核对。
各仿射图上的特化由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affineformalspecializationsurjective} 给出，
并通过收缩在交叠上相符。模型的泛纤维是已给数据；
紧模型图的论证给出有限覆盖以及修正所需的图册。
\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:formalopenstracesonsuppliedatlases]{主开迹与收缩}}
识别形式仿射开集的完整逆像及其交叠上的特化映射。}

\bi{Apply the
\mbox{\hyperref[prop:formal-supplied-model-pointed-descent]{pointed reduction of a compact formal model}}
to this actual atlas and evaluation at \(x\).
It identifies the ordinary strict chart gluing with the valuation
space of the reduced pointed closure \(Z\), over the specified
field \(F_0\). The
\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:ordinarystrictgermreductionconstruction]{ordinary neighbourhood invariance}}
identifies the ordinary chart gluings after shrinking representatives
of the analytic germ at \(x\). The identifications use the same
completed residue field at \(x\) and compose on further common
refinements.}
{将\mbox{\hyperref[prop:formal-supplied-model-pointed-descent]{紧形式模型的带点约化}}
应用于此实际图册以及在 $x$ 处的取值。
它在指定域 $F_0$ 上识别通常 strict 图粘合与约化带点闭包 $Z$
的赋值空间。
\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:ordinarystrictgermreductionconstruction]{通常邻域不变性}}
给出缩小 $x$ 处解析芽代表后通常图粘合之间的识别。
这些识别使用 $x$ 处同一个完备剩余域，并在进一步的共同加细上相容。}

\bi{For two models of the same \(U\), use the
\mbox{\hyperref[lem:compact-formal-common-atlas]{compact-model atlas and common modification}}
with the analytic isomorphism specified by their identifications
with \(U\). It supplies the compatible atlases on both legs and
their common source, and the two actual generic maps from that
source agree as isomorphisms to \(U\). Thus the two legs lift \(x\)
to the same point with the same \(F_0\). The supplied-leg part
of the pointed reduction gives proper maps of the reduced pointed
closures. On valuations they keep the field and the valuation and
contract the centre. By the common principal refinements they are
the ordinary chart comparisons already used above, including after
further such modifications.}
{对同一 $U$ 的两个模型，将
\mbox{\hyperref[lem:compact-formal-common-atlas]{紧模型图册与共同修正}}
应用于由两者与 $U$ 的识别给出的解析同构。
这给出两支及其共同源上的相容图册，且共同源到 $U$ 的两个实际
泛纤维映射作为同构相等。因此两支把 $x$ 提升为同一个点，并具有
同一个 $F_0$。带点约化的已给两支部分给出约化带点闭包间的适当映射。
在赋值上，这些映射保留域与赋值并收缩中心。在共同主开加细上，
这些映射正是前面使用的通常图比较，进一步作这样的修正仍然如此。}
\end{proof}
