Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Pointed formal \texorpdfstring{\'etale}{etale} maps]
\label{v035:formal:etaletubes}
Let $f:\mathfrak U\to\mathfrak X$ be an adic, topologically
finitely presented, formally \'etale morphism. Suppose that
$q'\in\mathfrak U_s(k)$ maps to $q\in\mathfrak X_s(k)$.
Then the generic-fibre morphism restricts to an analytic isomorphism
\[
                  ]q'[_{\mathfrak U}\ \xrightarrow{\sim}\ ]q[_{\mathfrak X}.
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
A formal open containing a special point contains its whole tube,
by Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}. Choose affine
formal neighbourhoods of $q$ and $q'$ so that the latter maps into
the former. Write them as $\operatorname{Spf}A_0$ and
$\operatorname{Spf}B_0$. Lemma~\ref{cand:formaletaleboundedpresentation}
gives a square Jacobian presentation
\[
 B_0\simeq
 \widehat{A_0[Z_1,\ldots,Z_m,Q]/(F_1,\ldots,F_m,Q\det J-1)},
 \qquad J=\left(\frac{\partial F_i}{\partial Z_j}\right),
\]
with $F_i\in A_0[Z_1,\ldots,Z_m]$. The lemma's presentation
statement applies to every admissible base $A_0$.
The completion and admissible-presentation results
\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion} and \ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations}
identify this with the corresponding separated restricted-series
quotient. Its coefficients have norm at most one in the generic
quotient norm.

Choose $z_{0i}\in O$ lifting $Z_i(q')\in k$. Reduction gives
$\overline F(\overline z_0)(q)=0$ and
$\det\overline J(\overline z_0)(q)\ne0$. Thus
Corollary~\ref{cand:standardetaletubeisomorphism} applies to the
entire tube. Its inverse is the unique analytic solution with
$|Z_i-z_{0i}|<1$; it identifies the analytic structure as well as
the points. The open restrictions made above retain both tubes,
so this is the claimed map.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
Let $K$ be complete and nontrivially valued, with its given absolute
value. Write $O=K^\circ$, $\mathfrak m=K^{\circ\circ}$ and
$k=O/\mathfrak m$, and choose $\pi\in O$ with $0<|\pi|<1$.
All formal schemes in this section are admissible and topologically
finitely presented over $O$. They carry the $\pi$-adic topology.
For a special-fibre point $q$, write $]q[$ for its entire inverse
image under specialization.
