NAG-001849 · Accepted whole

Normal affinoid components and the identity principle

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 457

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Normal affinoid components and the identity principle}{正规 affinoid 分量与恒等原理}]
\label{fnd:normalcomponentidentity}
\bi{Let $X=\mathcal M(A)$ be a generalized affinoid space whose ordinary local rings are normal. Then $A$ is a finite product of normal domains. The spectra of these factors are exactly the connected components of $X$, and they are open and closed. On each component, an analytic function that vanishes on a nonempty ordinary open set is zero.}
{设 $X=\mathcal M(A)$ 为 generalized affinoid 空间，其普通局部环均正规。则 $A$ 为有限个正规整环的乘积；这些因子的谱恰是 $X$ 的连通分量，且均既开又闭。在每个分量上，若解析函数在某个非空普通开集上为零，则该函数恒为零。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For each prime $\mathfrak p\subset A$, Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity} gives an analytic point with kernel $\mathfrak p$. Its ordinary local ring $R$ is faithfully flat over $A_{\mathfrak p}$, by rational-domain flatness and the ordinary-neighborhood colimit. Since $R$ is a normal local ring, it is a domain. Faithful flatness makes $A_{\mathfrak p}\to R$ injective, so $A_{\mathfrak p}$ is also a domain. If $u/v$ in its fraction field is integral, normality of $R$ gives $u\in vR$; faithful flatness contracts $vR$ to $vA_{\mathfrak p}$, proving that $u/v\in A_{\mathfrak p}$. Thus every $A_{\mathfrak p}$ is normal.}
{对每个素理想 $\mathfrak p\subset A$，Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity} 给出核为 $\mathfrak p$ 的解析点。由 rational 域平坦性及普通邻域的直极限，其普通局部环 $R$ 在 $A_{\mathfrak p}$ 上忠实平坦。因 $R$ 为正规局部环，它是整环；忠实平坦性使 $A_{\mathfrak p}\to R$ 单射，所以 $A_{\mathfrak p}$ 也是整环。若其分式域中的 $u/v$ 整于 $A_{\mathfrak p}$，则由 $R$ 正规得到 $u\in vR$；忠实平坦性把 $vR$ 收缩为 $vA_{\mathfrak p}$，故 $u/v\in A_{\mathfrak p}$。因此每个 $A_{\mathfrak p}$ 正规。}

\bi{The Noetherian ring $A$ is reduced, and distinct minimal primes are pairwise comaximal: otherwise their localizations at a maximal ideal containing both would contradict the domain property of that local ring. The Chinese remainder theorem gives a finite product of normal domains. Its idempotents supply open-and-closed affinoid factors. An integral factor has connected analytic spectrum: a partition into two nonempty open-and-closed sets would glue the functions zero and one to a nontrivial idempotent in its global-section ring, which is that integral factor by the structure-sheaf theorem. Finally, a nonempty ordinary open subset of an integral affinoid contains a kernel-zero point by Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity}. A function vanishing there is therefore zero in the algebra.}
{Noetherian 环 $A$ 约化，且不同极小素理想两两互素；否则，在同时包含二者的极大理想处局部化，会与局部环为整环矛盾。中国剩余定理给出有限个正规整环的乘积，其幂等元给出既开又闭的 affinoid 因子。整因子的解析谱连通：若可分成两个非空既开又闭的集合，则把函数零与一粘合，得到全局截面环中的非平凡幂等元；但结构层定理说明该截面环就是此整因子，矛盾。最后，由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity}，整 affinoid 的每个非空普通开集都包含核为零的点；在该点取值为零的函数必在代数中为零。}
\end{proof}
