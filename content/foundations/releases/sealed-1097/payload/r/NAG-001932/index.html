Adjoining power-bounded functions

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 528

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Adjoining power-bounded functions}{添入幂有界函数}]
\label{fnd:powerboundedmodelblowup}
\bi{Let $A_0$ be an admissible $K^\circ$-algebra and
$A=A_0[1/\pi]$. If $f_1,\ldots,f_s\in A^\circ$, then
$B_0=A_0[f_1,\ldots,f_s]\subset A$ is a finite admissible
$A_0$-algebra, with $B_0[1/\pi]=A$.
There is a nonzero $c\in K^{\circ\circ}$ with $cf_i\in A_0$,
and $\operatorname{Spf}(B_0)\to\operatorname{Spf}(A_0)$ is the
admissible blowup of the open ideal $(c,cf_1,\ldots,cf_s)$.}
{设 $A_0$ 为 admissible $K^\circ$-代数，$A=A_0[1/\pi]$。
若 $f_1,\ldots,f_s\in A^\circ$，则
$B_0=A_0[f_1,\ldots,f_s]\subset A$ 为有限 admissible
$A_0$-代数，且 $B_0[1/\pi]=A$。存在非零
$c\in K^{\circ\circ}$，使全部 $cf_i\in A_0$，并且
$\operatorname{Spf}(B_0)\to\operatorname{Spf}(A_0)$ 为开理想
$(c,cf_1,\ldots,cf_s)$ 的 admissible blowup。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose an admissible presentation of $A_0$.
By Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations}, $A_0$ is the
closed unit ball for the induced quotient norm on $A$.
The monic equations in Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointwisepowerbounded}
therefore show that each $f_i$ is integral over $A_0$.
Successively reducing powers by those equations makes $B_0$ a
finite $A_0$-module. It is complete by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalmoduletopology}. Sending restricted-series
variables to the $f_i$ is well defined, since all monomials in them
belong to the bounded set $B_0$; this gives a topologically finite
type presentation over $A_0$, and hence over $K^\circ$.
As a subring of $A$, $B_0$ is $\pi$-torsion free, and
Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation} makes it admissible.}
{选择 $A_0$ 的一个 admissible 表示。由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations}，$A_0$ 恰为
$A$ 的相应商范数闭单位球。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointwisepowerbounded} 中的首一方程
说明各 $f_i$ 在 $A_0$ 上整。依次用这些方程降低幂次可知
$B_0$ 为有限 $A_0$-模；由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalmoduletopology}，它完备。
把限制幂级数变量送到 $f_i$ 是良定义的，因为所有单项式都属于
有界集合 $B_0$；由此得到 $B_0$ 在 $A_0$ 上、继而在
$K^\circ$ 上的拓扑有限型表示。作为 $A$ 的子环，$B_0$ 无
$\pi$-挠，故由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation}
它是 admissible 的。}

\bi{Clear the finitely many denominators to choose $c$, and put
$I=(c,cf_1,\ldots,cf_s)$. A power of $\pi$ belongs to $cA_0$,
so $I$ is open. Its extension to $B_0$ is $(c)$.
Consider an admissible formal scheme $\mathfrak Y$ over
$\operatorname{Spf}(A_0)$ on which $I$ becomes invertible.
Locally one of the displayed generators generates $I\mathcal O_{\mathfrak Y}$.
If $c$ does, every quotient $cf_j/c=f_j$ is a section.
If $cf_i$ does, the equation $c=(cf_i)d$ gives
$d=f_i^{-1}$ on the generic algebra. The monic integral equation
for $f_i$, multiplied by $f_i^{1-r}$ when its degree is $r$,
expresses $f_i$ as a polynomial in $d$ with coefficients in $A_0$.
Thus $f_i$ is a unit in $\mathcal O_{\mathfrak Y}$ and $c$ also
generates $I\mathcal O_{\mathfrak Y}$. In either case all $f_j$
extend uniquely. The extensions glue, since admissible formal
rings inject into their generic algebras. They give the unique
factorization through $\operatorname{Spf}(B_0)$.}
{清去有限个分母以选择 $c$，并令
$I=(c,cf_1,\ldots,cf_s)$。某个 $\pi$ 的幂属于 $cA_0$，
故 $I$ 为开理想；其在 $B_0$ 中的扩张为 $(c)$。
设 $\mathfrak Y$ 为 $\operatorname{Spf}(A_0)$ 上的 admissible
形式概形，且 $I$ 的拉回可逆。局部地，上述某个生成元生成
$I\mathcal O_{\mathfrak Y}$。若生成元为 $c$，则每个商
$cf_j/c=f_j$ 都是截面。若生成元为 $cf_i$，由
$c=(cf_i)d$ 可知在泛纤维代数上 $d=f_i^{-1}$。
把 $f_i$ 的次数为 $r$ 的首一整方程乘以 $f_i^{1-r}$，
就把 $f_i$ 表成 $d$ 的一个系数在 $A_0$ 中的多项式。
于是 $f_i$ 在 $\mathcal O_{\mathfrak Y}$ 中为单位元，且
$c$ 也生成 $I\mathcal O_{\mathfrak Y}$。两种情形下各 $f_j$
都唯一延拓；由于 admissible 形式环嵌入其泛纤维代数，这些延拓
相容并给出经过 $\operatorname{Spf}(B_0)$ 的唯一分解。}

\bi{The actual blowup is admissible by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:formalblowupcharts}. Its universal property
and the property just proved give inverse morphisms between it
and $\operatorname{Spf}(B_0)$. This proves the last assertion.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:formalblowupcharts}，实际的 blowup 是
admissible 的。其泛性质与刚才证明的性质在它和
$\operatorname{Spf}(B_0)$ 之间给出互逆态射，从而得到最后的断言。}
\end{proof}
