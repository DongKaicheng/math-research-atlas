Factorization and normality of Tate algebras

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 78

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Factorization and normality of Tate algebras}{Tate 代数的唯一分解与整闭性}]\label{fnd:stricttatefactorial}
\bi{For every integer $n\geq0$, the ring $R_n$ is a unique factorization domain and is integrally closed in its fraction field.}
{对每个整数 $n\geq0$，环 $R_n$ 都是唯一分解整环，并且在其分式域中整闭。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The Gauss norm is multiplicative by Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm}, so $R_n$ is a domain. Induct on $n$, starting with the field $R_0=K$. Assume $R_{n-1}$ is a unique factorization domain. Its polynomial ring $R_{n-1}[X_n]$ is a unique factorization domain by \cite[Tag 0BC1, Lemma 10.120.10]{StacksAlg}.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm}，Gauss 范数具有乘性，所以 $R_n$ 是整环。对 $n$ 归纳，初始情形 $R_0=K$ 为域。假设 $R_{n-1}$ 为唯一分解整环。由 \cite[Tag 0BC1, Lemma 10.120.10]{StacksAlg}，多项式环 $R_{n-1}[X_n]$ 也是唯一分解整环。}

\bi{Let $0\ne f\in R_n$ be a nonunit. Lemma~\ref{fnd:distinguishedcoordinates} and Corollary~\ref{fnd:banachpreparation} supply an isometric coordinate automorphism after which $f$, up to multiplication by a scalar and a unit, is a monic polynomial $P\in R_{n-1}[X_n]$ of positive degree with $\|P\|=1$. Factor $P$ in that polynomial ring. Since the product of the leading coefficients is one, each leading coefficient is a unit; after adjusting the factors by units we obtain}
{设 $0\ne f\in R_n$ 不是单位元。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:distinguishedcoordinates} 及 Corollary~\ref{fnd:banachpreparation}，经过等距坐标自同构，并乘以某个标量和单位元后，可将 $f$ 写成正次数首一多项式 $P\in R_{n-1}[X_n]$，且 $\|P\|=1$。在该多项式环中分解 $P$。各因子的首项系数乘积为一，因此每个首项系数都是单位元；用单位元调整因子后，可写成}
\[
 P=P_1\cdots P_s,
 \qquad P_j\in R_{n-1}[X_n]\text{ monic and irreducible}.
\]
\bi{Each $P_j$ has Gauss norm at least one. Multiplicativity and $\|P\|=1$ therefore give $\|P_j\|=1$ for every $j$. Thus each $P_j$ is a monic polynomial with all lower coefficients of norm at most one. The division theorem identifies}
{每个 $P_j$ 的 Gauss 范数至少为一。由乘性及 $\|P\|=1$，每个 $j$ 都满足 $\|P_j\|=1$。因此，各 $P_j$ 均为首一多项式，且低次项系数范数不超过一。除法定理给出识别}
\[
 R_{n-1}[X_n]/(P_j)\ \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\ R_n/(P_j).
\]
\bi{Indeed, both quotients have the same unique polynomial representatives of degree less than $\deg P_j$, and the map is induced by the algebra inclusion. The left ring is a domain because $P_j$ is prime in the polynomial unique factorization domain. Consequently $P_j$ is prime in $R_n$. Reversing the coordinate automorphism gives a factorization of $f$ into prime elements. A domain in which every nonzero nonunit factors into primes is a unique factorization domain, by cancellation of prime factors. Finally, \cite[Tag 0AFV, Lemma 10.120.11]{StacksAlg} gives integral closedness.}
{两边的商都有次数小于 $\deg P_j$ 的唯一多项式代表元，且此映射由代数包含诱导。由于 $P_j$ 在多项式唯一分解整环中为素元，左边是整环。因此 $P_j$ 在 $R_n$ 中也是素元。逆向施加坐标自同构，即得到 $f$ 的素元分解。整环中若每个非零非单位元都可分解为素元，则通过逐个消去素因子可得唯一分解性。最后，由 \cite[Tag 0AFV, Lemma 10.120.11]{StacksAlg} 得到整闭性。}
\end{proof}
