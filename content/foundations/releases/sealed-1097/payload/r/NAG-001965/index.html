Compactness of graded valuation spaces

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 563

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Compactness of graded valuation spaces}
{分次赋值空间的拟紧性}]
\label{fnd:gradedrzcompact}
\bi{For every graded subalgebra $R\subset F$,
$\mathscr P_k(F)\{R\}$ is quasi-compact. In particular
$\mathscr P_k(F)$ is quasi-compact and quasi-separated.
Every nonempty open subset contains the point $F$, so these
spaces are irreducible.}
{对每个分次子代数 $R\subset F$，
$\mathscr P_k(F)\{R\}$ 拟紧。特别地，
$\mathscr P_k(F)$ 拟紧且拟分离。每个非空开集都包含点 $F$，
因此这些空间不可约。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Encode a graded subring by the membership functions of
the homogeneous elements of $F$ in the product of copies of
$\{0,1\}$. The conditions of containing $R$ and $k$,
being closed under products and sums in one degree, and
containing $a$ or $a^{-1}$ are closed finite-coordinate
conditions. The product is compact by
\cite[Tag 08ZU]{StacksAlg}; it is Hausdorff since distinct
points are separated by a coordinate cylinder. Hence its
closed solution set is compact: add the open complement
to any cover of that set and use the finite-subcover property.
Its valuation topology is coarser, proving
quasi-compactness. Finite intersections of the basic opens
are of the same kind, hence quasi-separatedness. Finally
$F$ satisfies every membership condition, proving irreducibility.}
{在若干份 $\{0,1\}$ 的乘积中，用 $F$ 的齐次元素的归属函数
编码一个分次子环。包含 $R$ 及 $k$、对乘法及同次数加法
封闭、并包含 $a$ 或 $a^{-1}$，都是涉及有限坐标的闭条件。
该乘积由 \cite[Tag 08ZU]{StacksAlg} 紧；不同点可用一个
坐标柱集分离，故它 Hausdorff。其闭解集因而紧：对解集的
任意开覆盖加入其开补集，再使用有限子覆盖性质即可。
赋值拓扑更弱，故它拟紧。基本开集的有限交仍为同类集合，
所以空间拟分离。最后，$F$ 满足每个归属条件，证明不可约性。}
\end{proof}
