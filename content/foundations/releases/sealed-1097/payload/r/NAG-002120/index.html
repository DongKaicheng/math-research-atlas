Balancing with actual initial multiplicities

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 898

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Balancing with actual initial multiplicities}{实际初始重数下的平衡定理}]
\label{cand:tropicalbalancing}
\bi{Let $K$ be any complete real-valued non-Archimedean field, and let $X\subset\operatorname{Spec}K[M]$ be a pure $d$-dimensional closed subscheme. Its tropicalization, endowed with the geometric initial multiplicities of Definition~\ref{cand:tropicalmultiplicitydefinition}, is a balanced integral weighted polyhedral complex. Thus at every codimension-one cell $\tau$,}
{设 $K$ 为任意完备实值非阿基米德域，$X\subset\operatorname{Spec}K[M]$ 为纯 $d$ 维闭子概形。其热带化配以 Definition~\ref{cand:tropicalmultiplicitydefinition} 的几何初始重数后，是平衡整权多面复形。因此，在每个余维一胞腔 $\tau$ 处，}
\[
 \sum_{\sigma\supset\tau}m_X(\sigma)u_{\sigma/\tau}=0
         \quad\text{in }N/N_\tau,
\]
\bi{where $u_{\sigma/\tau}$ denotes the primitive generator towards $\sigma$. The assertion is stable under subdivision and complete isometric base extension.}
{其中 $u_{\sigma/\tau}$ 表示指向 $\sigma$ 的本原生成元。该断言与细分及完备等距扩域相容。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The case $d=0$ has no balancing condition. If $d>0$, choose $w\in\operatorname{relint}(\tau)$. Proposition~\ref{cand:weightedinitialstar} expresses the initial scheme as $T_{N_\tau}\times C$ with $C$ a pure one-dimensional closed torus subscheme over a trivially valued residue field. Its tropical rays are precisely the images of the cells $\sigma\supset\tau$ in the quotient lattice. Their primitive vectors are $u_{\sigma/\tau}$, and their actual geometric weights are $m_X(\sigma)$ by that proposition. Theorem~\ref{cand:trivialcurvebalancing} gives exactly the displayed equation. Refinement and base-change compatibility are those of Proposition~\ref{cand:tropicalweightlocalconstancy}.}
{当 $d=0$ 时无需平衡条件。设 $d>0$，取 $w\in\operatorname{relint}(\tau)$。由 Proposition~\ref{cand:weightedinitialstar}，初始概形写成 $T_{N_\tau}\times C$，其中 $C$ 是平凡赋值剩余域上纯一维环面闭子概形。其热带射线恰为胞腔 $\sigma\supset\tau$ 在商格中的像，本原向量为 $u_{\sigma/\tau}$；由同一命题，其实际几何权重为 $m_X(\sigma)$。应用 Theorem~\ref{cand:trivialcurvebalancing} 即得所列等式。细分及基变换相容性由 Proposition~\ref{cand:tropicalweightlocalconstancy} 给出。}
\end{proof}
