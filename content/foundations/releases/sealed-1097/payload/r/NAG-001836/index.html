\begin{corollary}[\bi{Affinoid Theorem B}{Affinoid Theorem B}]
\label{fnd:generalizedaffinoidtheoremb}
\bi{For every coherent module $\mathcal F$ on $\mathcal D_X$ and every object $V$, one has $H^q(V,\mathcal F)=0$ for $q>0$. The same holds on $\mathcal R_X$. These are the right derived functors of sections in the corresponding categories of abelian sheaves.}
{对 $\mathcal D_X$ 上的每个凝聚模 $\mathcal F$ 及每个对象 $V$，当 $q>0$ 时均有 $H^q(V,\mathcal F)=0$。相同结论在 $\mathcal R_X$ 上成立。这里采用相应阿贝尔层范畴中截面函子的右导出函子。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Apply \cite[Tag 03F9, Lemma 21.10.9]{StacksAlg}. Take its basis to be all objects and its covering system to be all finite coverings of the chosen site. Intersections remain in the site. Every covering of every object has vanishing higher \v Cech cohomology, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidcovercech} and the Kiehl identification; for the rational site use Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalslicemodules}. The covering system is cofinal by the definition of the generated topology. These verify the hypotheses of the cited lemma, which gives the asserted derived vanishing.}
{应用 \cite[Tag 03F9, Lemma 21.10.9]{StacksAlg}，其中基取全部对象，覆盖系统取所选 site 的全部有限覆盖。交叠仍在该 site 中。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidcovercech} 及 Kiehl 识别，每个对象的每个覆盖均具有高次 \v Cech 上同调消失；rational site 的情形使用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalslicemodules}。按所生成拓扑的定义，该覆盖系统余终。因此所引引理的假设全部成立，得到所述导出消失。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\begin{definition}[\bi{The finite affinoid-domain site}{有限 affinoid 域 site}]
\label{fnd:generalizedfinitedomainsite}
\bi{For $X=\mathcal M(A)$, let $\mathcal D_X$ be the category of all affinoid domains in $X$, with inclusions as arrows. Give it finite set-theoretic coverings by its objects, including the empty covering of the empty domain. Choose representing algebras $A_V$, with the unique bounded restriction maps, and define}
{对 $X=\mathcal M(A)$，令 $\mathcal D_X$ 为 $X$ 中所有 affinoid 域组成的范畴，以包含为箭头。覆盖取由其对象组成的有限集合覆盖，并包括空域的空覆盖。选择各表示代数 $A_V$ 及其唯一有界限制映射，定义}
\[
 \mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{aff}}(V)=A_V,
 \qquad \widetilde M^{\mathrm{aff}}(V)=A_V\otimes_A M
 \quad(M\text{ finite over }A).
\]
\end{definition}
