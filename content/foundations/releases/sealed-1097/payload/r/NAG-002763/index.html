\begin{lemma}[Serre comparison for finite modules; F8ST-S]
\label{lem:serre}
Let \(R\to S\) be a regular map of Noetherian rings and \(M\) a finite
\(R\)-module. If \(M\) is \(S_1\), then \(M\otimes_RS\) is \(S_1\).
If the map is also faithfully flat, the converse holds. The converse
in fact needs only faithful flatness, not regular fibres.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For ascent, fix \(\mathfrak q\in\Supp(M\otimes_RS)\), put
\(\mathfrak p=\mathfrak q\cap R\), and work with the flat local map
\(R_{\mathfrak p}\to S_{\mathfrak q}\). Write \(M_0=M_{\mathfrak p}\)
and \(N=M_0\otimes S_{\mathfrak q}\). We need a non-zero-divisor on
\(N\) in its maximal ideal only when \(\dim\Supp N>0\).
If \(\dim\Supp M_0>0\), the \(S_1\) condition supplies such an element
\(a\in\mathfrak pR_{\mathfrak p}\) on \(M_0\); flatness preserves its
injectivity on \(N\).

Otherwise \(M_0\) is nonzero of finite length. Its composition series
tensors to a filtration of \(N\) by copies of
\(F=S_{\mathfrak q}/\mathfrak pS_{\mathfrak q}\). Hence
\(\Supp N=\Spec F\). The ring \(F\) is regular local. If its
dimension is positive, its maximal ideal contains a nonzero element
that is regular on \(F\), because \(F\) is a domain
(\tagg{STACKS-00NP}). Lift that element to
\(\mathfrak qS_{\mathfrak q}\). It is injective on each filtration
quotient, and therefore on \(N\). This proves ascent.

For descent, suppose \(M\) is not \(S_1\). Choose a prime
\(\mathfrak p\) for which \(M_{\mathfrak p}\) has positive support
dimension and depth zero. There is an injection
\(\kappa(\mathfrak p)\hookrightarrow M_{\mathfrak p}\): equivalently,
the maximal ideal is associated to this finite local module. In
\((R\setminus\mathfrak p)^{-1}S\), choose a prime minimal over the
extension of \(\mathfrak p\). Such a prime exists by faithful flatness;
let \(\mathfrak q\) be its preimage in \(S\). Its contraction is
\(\mathfrak p\), and
\(F=S_{\mathfrak q}/\mathfrak pS_{\mathfrak q}\) is a nonzero Artinian
local ring. Tensoring the injection gives \(F\hookrightarrow N\).
The socle of \(F\) is nonzero, so \(\depth N=0\).

Choose \(\mathfrak p_0\subsetneq\mathfrak p\) in \(\Supp M\).
Flat going down (\tagg{STACKS-00HS}) gives
\(\mathfrak q_0\subsetneq\mathfrak q\) over \(\mathfrak p_0\).
The localized map is faithfully flat, so
\((M\otimes_RS)_{\mathfrak q_0}\ne0\). Consequently
\(\dim\Supp N>0\), contradicting \(S_1\) upstairs.
\end{proof}
