Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Conditional degree comparison; F8SC-D]
\label{prop:degree}
Assume the finite-flat assertion (2.3) on the indicated neighbourhood,
and use the same actual analytic fibres in both integrations.
At a generic transverse weight let
\[
 e_C=[\Z^{n-1}:p_{\mathrm{trop}}(N_C)]
\]
for the saturated tangent lattice \(N_C\). If \(E_C^D\) is the actual
whole transverse Gauss algebra of \(D\) over its transverse Gauss field
\(H_C\), then
\[
       \dim_{H_C}E_C^D=\delta_C,\qquad
       m_D(C)=\frac{\delta_C}{e_C},\qquad
       m_{C\times[a,b]}=\frac{\delta_C}{e_C}.                       \tag{2.4}
\]
For \(n=1\) the empty transverse tuple has index one.
\end{proposition}
