\begin{lemma}[\bi{Coordinate changes in a free Gauss field}
{自由 Gauss 域中的坐标变换}]
\label{fnd:freegausscoordinatechange}
\bi{Let $r=(r_1,\ldots,r_m)$ be free over a complete field
$K$, and let $S_i\in K_r$ satisfy $|S_i|=r_i$.
The isometric substitution $T_i\mapsto S_i$ is an
automorphism of $K_r$. Thus the complete subfield generated
by $K,S_1,\ldots,S_m$ is all of $K_r$.}
{设 $r=(r_1,\ldots,r_m)$ 在完备域 $K$ 上自由，
$S_i\in K_r$ 满足 $|S_i|=r_i$。则等距代换
$T_i\mapsto S_i$ 是 $K_r$ 的自同构。因此由
$K,S_1,\ldots,S_m$ 生成的完备子域恰为整个 $K_r$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\paragraph{NAG-001598. Coordinate changes in a free Gauss field.}
Let \(K\) be complete, let \(r=(r_i)\) be free over \(K\), and let
\(S_i\in K_r\) have \(|S_i|=r_i\). Then \(T_i\mapsto S_i\) induces
an isometric automorphism of \(K_r\). In particular the complete
subfield generated by the \(S_i\) over \(K\) is \(K_r\).

The weighted-null Laurent expansion has a unique dominant monomial.
By freeness the dominant monomial of \(S_i\) is \(\alpha_i T_i\)
with \(\alpha_i\in K\) and \(|\alpha_i|=1\). Composing with a coordinate
scaling, assume \(\alpha_i=1\). Choose \(q<1\) with
\(|S_i-T_i|\le q r_i\) for all \(i\). Such a bound follows from weighted
nullity and finiteness of the tuple. The inverse difference has
\(|S_i^{-1}-T_i^{-1}|\le q/r_i\). A telescoping product for each signed
monomial \(\nu\) therefore gives \(|S^\nu-T^\nu|\le q r^\nu\).
For any Laurent polynomial \(f\) this gives
\(|f(S)-f(T)|\le q|f(T)|\). Consequently substitution is isometric
and extends to a \(K\)-algebra endomorphism \(U\) of \(K_r\).
The operator \(I-U\) has norm at most \(q\), so for each \(a\) the
convergent sum \(\sum_{j\ge0}(I-U)^j a\) maps to \(a\). This gives
surjectivity. Laurent polynomials in the new coordinates are dense.
