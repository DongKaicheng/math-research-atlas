Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Local calculus of analytic domains}{解析域的局部运算}]
\label{fnd:generalnetdomaincalculus}
\bi{Analytic domains are stable under finite intersections and under passage to analytic domains inside them. Admissible coverings define a pretopology. Every open subset is an analytic domain. Every point has a compact Hausdorff analytic neighborhood contained in any prescribed open Hausdorff neighborhood. On a chart $V\in\bar\tau$, the domain structure and site are precisely those of $\mathcal M(A_V)$ in Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidanalyticdomaincalculus}.}
{解析域在有限交及进一步取解析子域下保持。Admissible 覆盖定义一个预拓扑。每个开子集都是解析域。对每个点及其任意指定的开 Hausdorff 邻域，都存在包含于其中的紧 Hausdorff 解析邻域。对 chart $V\in\bar\tau$，域结构和 site 恰与 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidanalyticdomaincalculus} 中 $\mathcal M(A_V)$ 的结构相同。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For intersections, intersect the finitely many chart members supplying the quasi-net condition at a point, and refine each resulting chart intersection by the net $\bar\tau$. Restriction and transitivity of quasi-nets, proved in Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasinetcalculus}, give the assertions about subdomains, pullbacks of covers, and composition of covers. Chart transport in Proposition~\ref{fnd:analyticatlassaturation} identifies each local refinement with an affinoid domain in the chart under consideration; this proves the assertion about its site.}
{对交集，在一点处将给出 quasi-net 条件的有限个 chart 成员相交，再用 $\bar\tau$ 加细所得的各 chart 交叠。Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasinetcalculus} 中 quasi-net 的限制性及传递性给出子域、覆盖拉回及覆盖复合的断言。Proposition~\ref{fnd:analyticatlassaturation} 的 chart 传递将每个局部加细识别为当前 chart 的 affinoid 域，因此也得到该 chart 的 site 比较。}

\bi{Let $x\in O$ with $O$ open. Choose finitely many charts $V_i$ containing $x$ whose union contains a neighborhood of $x$. Inside each $V_i$, the rational-neighborhood construction in Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidanalyticdomaincalculus} gives an affinoid domain $W_i\subseteq O\cap V_i$ containing a relative neighborhood of $x$. Their union contains a neighborhood of $x$ in $O$. Thus $O$ is analytic. If $O$ is Hausdorff, the same union $D=\bigcup_iW_i$ is compact Hausdorff, is an analytic domain by its finite closed covering, and is a neighborhood of $x$ in $X$.}
{设 $x\in O$ 且 $O$ 开。选取有限个均包含 $x$ 的 chart $V_i$，使其并包含 $x$ 的邻域。在每个 $V_i$ 内，Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidanalyticdomaincalculus} 的 rational 邻域构造给出 affinoid 域 $W_i\subseteq O\cap V_i$，它包含 $x$ 的一个相对邻域。其并包含 $x$ 在 $O$ 中的一个邻域，所以 $O$ 为解析域。若 $O$ 为 Hausdorff，则同一个并 $D=\bigcup_iW_i$ 紧且 Hausdorff；其有限闭覆盖说明它是解析域，并且它是 $x$ 在 $X$ 中的邻域。}
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[\bi{An affinoid atlas}{Affinoid atlas}]
\label{fnd:analyticnetatlas}
\bi{Let $X$ be a locally Hausdorff topological space. A net $\tau$ of compact Hausdorff subsets is a quasi-net such that $\tau|_{U\cap V}=\{W\in\tau:W\subseteq U\cap V\}$ is a quasi-net of $U\cap V$ for every $U,V\in\tau$. An affinoid atlas consists of such a net, affinoid algebras $A_V$, homeomorphisms $V\simeq\mathcal M(A_V)$, and bounded homomorphisms $A_V\to A_U$ for inclusions $U\subseteq V$ in $\tau$. The latter identify $U$ with an affinoid domain of $V$, and are compatible with composition.}
{设 $X$ 为局部 Hausdorff 拓扑空间。由紧 Hausdorff 子集组成的 net $\tau$ 是一个 quasi-net，且对任意 $U,V\in\tau$，族 $\tau|_{U\cap V}=\{W\in\tau:W\subseteq U\cap V\}$ 都是 $U\cap V$ 的 quasi-net。一个 affinoid atlas 由这样的 net、affinoid 代数 $A_V$、同胚 $V\simeq\mathcal M(A_V)$，以及对 $\tau$ 中包含关系 $U\subseteq V$ 给出的有界同态 $A_V\to A_U$ 组成。后者将 $U$ 识别为 $V$ 的 affinoid 域，并与复合相容。}
\end{definition}
Standing context 2 · exact TeX
\begin{definition}[\bi{Analytic domains and the analytic site}{解析域与解析 site}]
\label{fnd:generalnetanalyticdomain}
\bi{An analytic domain $Y\subseteq X$ is a subset for which $\bar\tau|_Y$ is a quasi-net, equipped with the induced topology and the restricted atlas. A covering by analytic domains is admissible if it is a quasi-net. The associated site is denoted $X_G$.}
{若子集 $Y\subseteq X$ 上的族 $\bar\tau|_Y$ 是 quasi-net，则称 $Y$ 为解析域，并配备诱导拓扑及限制 atlas。若解析域覆盖为 quasi-net，则称它为 admissible 覆盖。对应 site 记为 $X_G$。}
\end{definition}
