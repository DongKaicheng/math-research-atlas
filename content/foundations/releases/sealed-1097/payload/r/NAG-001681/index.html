Isometric tensor embeddings

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 269

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Isometric tensor embeddings}{张量积的等距嵌入}]
\label{fnd:grusonisometrictensor}
\bi{For any normed $K$-spaces $E,F$, the projective seminorm on $E\otimes_K F$ is a norm and satisfies $\|e\otimes f\|_\pi=\|e\|\|f\|$. If $E\hookrightarrow E'$ and $F\hookrightarrow F'$ are isometric linear embeddings, then}
{对任意赋范 $K$-空间 $E,F$，$E\otimes_K F$ 上的 projective 半范数为范数，且满足 $\|e\otimes f\|_\pi=\|e\|\|f\|$。若 $E\hookrightarrow E'$、$F\hookrightarrow F'$ 为等距线性嵌入，则}
\[
 E\otimes_K F\longrightarrow E'\otimes_K F',
 \qquad
 E\widehat\otimes_K F\longrightarrow E'\widehat\otimes_K F'
\]
\bi{are isometric embeddings; the image of the second map is closed.}
{均为等距嵌入；第二个映射的像闭。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Algebraic injectivity follows by extending vector-space bases. We first compare seminorms. Fix $z\in E\otimes_K F$ and finite-dimensional subspaces $U\subset E$, $V\subset F$ containing the factors of one expression for $z$. An arbitrary competing expression}
{代数单射性可由延长向量空间基得到。先比较半范数。固定 $z\in E\otimes_K F$，选择有限维子空间 $U\subset E$、$V\subset F$，包含 $z$ 的某个表达式中的全部因子。任取另一个表达式}
\[
 z=\sum_{j=1}^m e'_j\otimes f'_j
       \quad\text{in }E'\otimes_K F'.
\]
\bi{Let $U'\subset E'$ be spanned by $U$ and the $e'_j$, and let $V'\subset F'$ be spanned by $V$ and the $f'_j$. The equality holds already in $U'\otimes_K V'$, by algebraic injectivity. For a fixed $c>1$, Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitealmostprojection} gives projections $P:U'\to U$, $Q:V'\to V$ of norm at most $c$. Applying $P\otimes Q$ yields}
{令 $U'\subset E'$ 由 $U$ 及全部 $e'_j$ 张成，令 $V'\subset F'$ 由 $V$ 及全部 $f'_j$ 张成。由代数单射性，此等式已在 $U'\otimes_K V'$ 中成立。固定 $c>1$，由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitealmostprojection}，存在范数至多为 $c$ 的投影 $P:U'\to U$、$Q:V'\to V$。应用 $P\otimes Q$ 得}
\[
 z=\sum_jP(e'_j)\otimes Q(f'_j),
 \qquad
 \|z\|_{\pi,E,F}
 \leq c^2\max_j\|e'_j\|\|f'_j\|.
\]
\bi{Taking the infimum over competing expressions and then letting $c\downarrow1$ gives $\|z\|_{\pi,E,F}\leq\|z\|_{\pi,E',F'}$. The reverse inequality follows from the inclusion of the sets of expressions. Only projections between the displayed finite-dimensional spaces have been used.}
{对所有候选表达式取下确界，再令 $c\downarrow1$，得到 $\|z\|_{\pi,E,F}\leq\|z\|_{\pi,E',F'}$。反向不等式来自两类表达式集合的包含关系。这里使用的投影均位于所列有限维空间之间。}

\bi{On a finite-dimensional tensor $U\otimes_K V$, choose bases and their coordinate functionals. These functionals are bounded by Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}. If the $(i,j)$-coordinate of $z$ is nonzero, their tensor gives}
{在有限维张量积 $U\otimes_K V$ 上，选择两组基及其坐标泛函。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}，这些泛函有界。若 $z$ 的第 $(i,j)$ 个坐标非零，则两个坐标泛函的张量给出}
\[
 0<|(\lambda_i\otimes\mu_j)(z)|
       \leq\|\lambda_i\|_{\rm op}\|\mu_j\|_{\rm op}\|z\|_\pi.
\]
\bi{Thus the finite-dimensional projective seminorm is definite. Every nonzero tensor lies in such a subspace, whose inclusion is isometric by the preceding argument; this proves definiteness in general. For one-dimensional factors the norm is the product norm, and the same isometric inclusion proves the pure-tensor formula. An isometry extends to an isometry of metric completions. Its image is complete, hence closed.}
{因此有限维 projective 半范数正定。每个非零张量都位于这样的子空间中，而前面的论证说明其包含映射等距，所以一般情形也正定。一维因子的张量积具有乘积范数，同样的等距包含给出纯张量公式。等距映射延拓为度量完备化之间的等距映射；其像完备，因而闭。}
\end{proof}
