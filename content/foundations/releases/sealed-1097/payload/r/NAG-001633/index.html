Jacobian presentations of algebraically smooth morphisms

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 419

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Jacobian presentations of algebraically smooth morphisms}{代数光滑态射的 Jacobian 表示}]
\label{fnd:algebraicsmoothjacobian}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a smooth morphism of finite-type $K$-schemes. Its analytification admits a Jacobian presentation at every point and is boundaryless. The analytic differential rank is the algebraic relative differential rank at the corresponding scheme point.}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 为有限型 $K$-概形的光滑态射。其解析化在每个点都具有 Jacobian 表示，并且无边界。解析微分的秩等于对应概形点处的代数相对微分秩。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{By \cite[Tag 01V7, Lemma 29.35.11]{StacksAlg}, near each scheme point the morphism has a standard smooth presentation}
{由 \cite[Tag 01V7, Lemma 29.35.11]{StacksAlg}，在每个概形点附近，态射具有标准光滑表示}
\[
 S=R[T_1,\ldots,T_n]/(f_1,\ldots,f_c),
 \qquad
 \det\left(\frac{\partial f_i}{\partial T_j}\right)_{1\le i,j\le c}
      \in S^\times.
\]
\bi{The determinant condition is the definition in \cite[Tag 00T6]{StacksAlg}. Choose an affinoid neighborhood of the image point in the analytification of $\operatorname{Spec}R$, and choose finite radii for the coordinates strictly larger than their values at the selected analytic point. The relative analytification construction identifies the resulting affinoid source piece with the quotient of the corresponding relative polydisc by these same $c$ equations. The determinant remains invertible in its quotient algebra. Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianmatrixcriterion} therefore gives a Jacobian presentation, with differential rank $n-c$. The equality with algebraic rank follows directly from Proposition~\ref{fnd:algebraicdifferentialanalytification}, or from the same invertible matrix. Proposition~\ref{fnd:algebraicmorphismboundaryless} supplies boundarylessness, with its all-target-domain interpretation.}
{此行列式条件正是 \cite[Tag 00T6]{StacksAlg} 中的定义。在 $\operatorname{Spec}R$ 的解析化中选择像点的 affinoid 邻域，并为各坐标选择严格大于所选解析点处取值的有限半径。相对解析化构造将所得 affinoid 源分片识别为相应相对多圆盘对这同样的 $c$ 个方程的商。该行列式在商代数中仍可逆。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianmatrixcriterion}，得到 Jacobian 表示，微分秩为 $n-c$。其与代数秩的相等可直接由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:algebraicdifferentialanalytification} 或同一个可逆矩阵得到。Proposition~\ref{fnd:algebraicmorphismboundaryless} 给出按全部目标域解释的无边界性。}
\end{proof}
