Joint presentations and completed pushouts

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 260

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Joint presentations and completed pushouts}{联合表示与完备 pushout}]
\label{fnd:generalizedjointpresentations}
\bi{For any two arbitrary-radius affinoid quotient presentations over $A$, put}
{对 $A$ 上任意两个允许任意半径的 affinoid 商表示，记}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 B&=A\{\rho^{-1}T\}/I,\qquad C=A\{\tau^{-1}S\}/J,\\
 H&=A\{\rho^{-1}T,\tau^{-1}S\}/(I,J),
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{where $(I,J)$ is the sum of the actual extended ideals. With the displayed quotient norms there is a canonical isometric algebra isomorphism $B\widehat\otimes_A C\simeq H$. This is the separated completed tensor product, not an assertion that the ordinary algebraic tensor is already complete. It has the bounded pushout universal property for commutative Banach targets.}
{其中 $(I,J)$ 为实际延伸理想之和。对所列商范数，存在典范等距代数同构 $B\widehat\otimes_A C\simeq H$。这里使用分离完备张量积，并不断言普通代数张量积已经完备。它对交换 Banach 目标具有有界 pushout 泛性质。}

\bi{If $I=(gT_i-f_i)$ and $J=(hS_j-e_j)$ are rational relation ideals, then $H$ is isometric to the rational presentation with denominator $gh$ and the following numerator-radius pairs:}
{若 $I=(gT_i-f_i)$、$J=(hS_j-e_j)$ 为 rational 关系理想，则 $H$ 与如下 rational 表示等距：其分母为 $gh$，分子与半径分别为}
\[
 (hf_i,\rho_i),\qquad (ge_j,\tau_j),\qquad
 (f_ie_j,\rho_i\tau_j).
\]
\bi{Its spectrum is the intersection of the two original rational subsets. Finite iteration gives all intersections, including repeated members, with compatible restriction maps. Empty intersections have zero algebra.}
{它的谱是原来两个 rational 子集的交。有限次迭代给出全部有限交叠，包括重复成员，并给出相容的限制映射。空交叠具有零代数。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{All weighted coefficient algebras here are affinoid by Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation}; their actual ideals are closed and their quotients are complete by Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients}. On the ordinary balanced tensor product, the projective seminorm is submultiplicative by multiplying finite representations and taking their two infima. Its null space is an ideal. Separation and completion thus give a Banach algebra $G=B\widehat\otimes_A C$, or zero. The structural maps are contractive. The two contractive maps into $H$ give a contractive map $G\to H$ by their balanced product.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation}，这里全部加权系数代数均为 affinoid；由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients}，其实际理想闭，商完备。在普通平衡张量积上，将有限表示相乘并对两个表示分别取下确界，可知 projective 半范数为次乘的，且零半范数子空间为理想。因此分离并完备化得到 Banach 代数 $G=B\widehat\otimes_A C$，或零代数。结构映射为 contractive。通过两个到 $H$ 的 contractive 映射的平衡乘积，得到 contractive 映射 $G\to H$。}

\bi{Conversely the elements $\bar T_i\otimes1$ and $1\otimes\bar S_j$ have all powers bounded by $\rho_i^m$ and $\tau_j^m$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundedsubstitution} gives a contractive map from the joint coefficient algebra to $G$. It kills $I$ and $J$, first on each whole coefficient algebra by bounded polynomial approximation, hence on their extended ideals. It descends to a contractive $H\to G$. The composites fix all coefficients and variables in $H$ and all pure tensors in $G$. Density makes them identities, proving the isometry. More generally, two bounded maps from $B,C$ to a Banach algebra agreeing on $A$ have a bounded balanced product; its finite-representation bound extends it uniquely to $G$. This proves the bounded pushout property.}
{反方向，元素 $\bar T_i\otimes1$、$1\otimes\bar S_j$ 的全部幂，范数分别至多为 $\rho_i^m$、$\tau_j^m$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundedsubstitution}，得到从联合系数代数到 $G$ 的 contractive 映射。利用有界的多项式逼近，它在每个整体系数代数上零化 $I$、$J$，因而零化它们的延伸理想，并下降为 contractive 映射 $H\to G$。两个复合固定 $H$ 中的全部系数与变量，以及 $G$ 中的全部纯张量；由稠密性，它们均为恒等映射，故所得同构等距。更一般地，若来自 $B,C$ 的两个有界映射在 $A$ 上相同，则其乘积为有界平衡双线性映射；有限张量表示的估计将其唯一延拓到 $G$。这证明有界 pushout 泛性质。}

\bi{For the rational assertion, multiply the two unit-ideal identities. They show that $gh$ and the listed numerators generate the unit ideal. Call their rational algebra $P$, with variables $Y_i,Z_j,W_{ij}$ of the listed radii. In $H$, both $g$ and $h$ are units. The assignments}
{对 rational 断言，将两个单位理想恒等式相乘，可知 $gh$ 及所列分子生成单位理想。记相应 rational 代数为 $P$，变量 $Y_i,Z_j,W_{ij}$ 采用所列半径。在 $H$ 中，$g,h$ 均为单位。代入}
\[
 Y_i\longmapsto\bar T_i,\qquad Z_j\longmapsto\bar S_j,
 \qquad W_{ij}\longmapsto\bar T_i\bar S_j
\]
\bi{respect all bounds and relations and give a contractive $P\to H$. In $P$, the product $gh$ is a unit, so both factors are units. Dividing its relations gives $gY_i=f_i$, $hZ_j=e_j$, and $W_{ij}=Y_iZ_j$. Sending $T_i$ to $Y_i$ and $S_j$ to $Z_j$ therefore gives a contractive $H\to P$, inverse on all variables. The point theorem identifies the resulting inequalities with the two original sets: divide the $hf_i$ and $ge_j$ inequalities by the nonzero factors; conversely the two original sets imply all product inequalities.}
{保持全部范数界与关系，因此给出 contractive 映射 $P\to H$。在 $P$ 中，乘积 $gh$ 为单位，所以每个因子均为单位。对其关系作除法，得到 $gY_i=f_i$、$hZ_j=e_j$、$W_{ij}=Y_iZ_j$。因此 $T_i\mapsto Y_i$、$S_j\mapsto Z_j$ 给出 contractive 映射 $H\to P$，且两个映射在所有变量上互逆。由谱点定理，所得不等式与原来的两组不等式相同：将 $hf_i$、$ge_j$ 的不等式除以非零因子的值；反之，两组原不等式蕴含全部乘积不等式。}

\bi{Iteration gives finite intersections. Their comparison and restriction maps agree by the uniqueness in Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal}; a repeated member is consequently redundant by bounded inverse maps. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion} makes an empty intersection algebra zero. Every zero-quotient case in the pushout assertion also follows directly: if one factor ideal contains one, so does the joint ideal.}
{迭代即得有限交叠。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal} 的唯一性，各比较与限制映射相容；特别地，重复成员可通过有界互逆映射删去。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion}，空交叠的代数为零。Pushout 断言中的零商情形也可直接处理：若某个因子理想包含一，则联合理想也包含一。}
\end{proof}
