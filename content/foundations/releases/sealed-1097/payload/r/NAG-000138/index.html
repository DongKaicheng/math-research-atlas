Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Affinoid algebra theorems}{Affinoid algebra theorems}]\label{fnd:affinoidalgebratheorems}
\bi{A $K$-affinoid algebra is noetherian, and its ideals are closed. Quotients by ideals, with quotient topology, are again affinoid. Finite modules carry their canonical complete module topology, and homomorphisms between finite modules are continuous and strict. For a strictly affinoid integral algebra $A$, there is a finite injective map $K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_d\rangle\to A$, where $d=\dim A$. Residue fields at maximal ideals of a strictly affinoid algebra are finite over $K$.}
{$K$-affinoid algebra 为 noetherian，且其 ideals 都 closed。对 ideal 的 quotient 配备 quotient topology 后仍为 affinoid。Finite modules 带有 canonical complete module topology，它们之间的 homomorphisms 为 continuous 且 strict。对 strictly affinoid integral algebra $A$，存在 finite injective map $K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_d\rangle\to A$，其中 $d=\dim A$。Strictly affinoid algebra 的 maximal ideals 对应的 residue fields 是 $K$ 的 finite extensions。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}, applied to submodules of $A$, proves Noetherianity and closedness of ideals. Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients} constructs the actual affinoid quotients. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules} gives the complete canonical module norms, their independence of the finite free presentation, and norm-strictness of finite-module maps. For a strict integral algebra, Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization} gives a finite injective map from a Tate algebra; Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttatedimension} identifies its number of variables with $\dim A$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} proves the assertion on maximal residue fields.}
{将 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules} 用于 $A$ 的子模，得到 Noetherian 性与理想的闭性。Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients} 构造实际的 affinoid 商。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules} 给出完备的典范模范数，证明其与有限自由表示无关，并证明有限模映射的 norm-strict 性。对 strict 整环，Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization} 给出来自 Tate 代数的有限单射，Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttatedimension} 将其变量数识别为 $\dim A$。极大剩余域的断言由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} 证明。}
\end{proof}
