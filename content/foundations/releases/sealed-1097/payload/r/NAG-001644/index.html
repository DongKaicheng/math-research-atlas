Base change and transitivity of differentials

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 395

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Base change and transitivity of differentials}{微分的基变换与传递序列}]
\label{fnd:analyticdifferentialbasechange}
\bi{Let $L/K$ be complete and let $A\to C$ be a bounded map to an $L$-affinoid algebra. Put $B'=B\widehat\otimes_A C$. Then}
{设 $L/K$ 完备，且 $A\to C$ 是到某个 $L$-affinoid 代数的有界映射。置 $B'=B\widehat\otimes_A C$，则}
\[
 B'\otimes_B\Omega^{\mathrm{an}}_{B/A}
        \simeq\Omega^{\mathrm{an}}_{B'/C}.
\]
\bi{For bounded affinoid maps $A\to B\to D$ there is a right-exact sequence of finite $D$-modules}
{对有界 affinoid 映射 $A\to B\to D$，存在有限 $D$-模的右正合序列}
\[
 D\otimes_B\Omega^{\mathrm{an}}_{B/A}
       \longrightarrow\Omega^{\mathrm{an}}_{D/A}
       \longrightarrow\Omega^{\mathrm{an}}_{D/B}\longrightarrow0.
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout} gives $B'=C\{r^{-1}T\}/(f_1,\ldots,f_m)$. The Jacobian matrix of the base-changed equations is the image of the original matrix. Tensoring a finite cokernel is right exact, so Theorem~\ref{fnd:boundeduniversaldifferentials} proves the first formula. These are ordinary tensor products of finite modules: Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor} shows that completion does not change them.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout}，有 $B'=C\{r^{-1}T\}/(f_1,\ldots,f_m)$。基变换后方程的 Jacobian 矩阵就是原矩阵的像。有限余核的张量积右正合，因此 Theorem~\ref{fnd:boundeduniversaldifferentials} 给出第一式。这些是有限模的普通张量积；Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor} 说明完备化不改变它们。}

\bi{For transitivity let $Q$ be the cokernel of the first map. It is finite and complete. A $D$-linear map $Q\to N$ is the same as a bounded $A$-derivation $D\to N$ that vanishes on $B$, hence the same as a bounded $B$-derivation. The universal property identifies $Q$ with $\Omega^{\mathrm{an}}_{D/B}$ and identifies the quotient map with the canonical differential map. This proves the exact sequence.}
{对传递性，令 $Q$ 为第一映射的余核。它有限且完备。一个 $D$-线性映射 $Q\to N$ 等价于一个在 $B$ 上为零的有界 $A$-导子 $D\to N$，也就是一个有界 $B$-导子。泛性质将 $Q$ 识别为 $\Omega^{\mathrm{an}}_{D/B}$，并将商映射识别为典范微分映射，故得到所列正合序列。}
\end{proof}
