Ordinary regular comparison in every characteristic

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 349

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Ordinary regular comparison in every characteristic}{任意特征中的通常正则比较}]
\label{fnd:generalizedordinaryregularcomparison}
\bi{Let $K$ be any complete non-Archimedean field, let $A$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra, and let $x\in X=\mathcal M(A)$. Put $\mathfrak p=\ker(x)$ and $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$. Then}
{设 $K$ 为任意完备非阿基米德域，$A$ 为 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数，且 $x\in X=\mathcal M(A)$。令 $\mathfrak p=\ker(x)$、$R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$。则}
\[
                    A_{\mathfrak p}\longrightarrow R,
                           \qquad R\longrightarrow\widehat R
\]
\bi{are regular homomorphisms of Noetherian rings.}
{均为 Noetherian 环之间的正则同态。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The characteristic-zero case was established in Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroregularcomparison}. In positive characteristic, the flatness and Noetherianity argument of Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictordinaryregularcomparison} is unchanged: the ordinary-neighborhood colimit uses arbitrary rational domains, and completion is faithfully flat. We retain its finite purely inseparable fibre tests, but replace strict finite normalization by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedgenericgermregularity}. Here are the two substitutions in full.}
{特征零情形已在 Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroregularcomparison} 中建立。在正特征中，Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictordinaryregularcomparison} 的平坦性及 Noetherian 性论证保持不变：普通邻域的直极限使用任意 rational 域，且完备化忠实平坦。保留其有限纯不可分纤维测试，只把 strict 有限正规化替换为 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedgenericgermregularity}。以下完整写出这两处替换。}

\bi{For $\mathfrak q\subset\mathfrak p$ and a finite purely inseparable extension $E/\operatorname{Frac}(A/\mathfrak q)$, construct the finite radicial domain $B\subset E$ over $A/\mathfrak q$ by the same denominator-clearing power equations. The finite affinoid norm exists by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras}. With $Z=\mathcal M(B)$, there is a unique $z$ above $x$, and}
{对 $\mathfrak q\subset\mathfrak p$ 及有限纯不可分扩张 $E/\operatorname{Frac}(A/\mathfrak q)$，使用相同的清分母幂方程，在 $E$ 中构造 $A/\mathfrak q$ 上的有限根式整环 $B$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras}，其有限 affinoid 范数存在。令 $Z=\mathcal M(B)$，则 $x$ 上方有唯一的 $z$，并且}
\[
                       R\otimes_A E
                             =\mathcal O_{Z,z}\otimes_B E.
\]
\bi{The new generic-germ proposition gives $0\ne b\in B$ with $\mathcal O_{Z,z}[1/b]$ regular. Since $\operatorname{Frac}(B)=E$, the displayed fibre is its further localization and is regular. Tag 0381 proves geometric regularity of every fibre of the first homomorphism.}
{新的泛芽命题给出 $0\ne b\in B$，使 $\mathcal O_{Z,z}[1/b]$ 正则。因为 $\operatorname{Frac}(B)=E$，所列纤维是它的进一步局部化，故正则。Tag 0381 因而证明第一个同态的每个纤维几何正则。}

\bi{For $\mathfrak P\subset R$ and a finite purely inseparable extension $E/\operatorname{Frac}(R/\mathfrak P)$, construct a finite domain $C$ over $R/\mathfrak P$ with fraction field $E$, and realize it using Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization} as $B\hookrightarrow C=\mathcal O_{Z,z}$, where $B$ is now an arbitrary integral generalized affinoid algebra. The finite-local completion calculation in the strict proof applies without change and gives}
{对 $\mathfrak P\subset R$ 及有限纯不可分扩张 $E/\operatorname{Frac}(R/\mathfrak P)$，构造 $R/\mathfrak P$ 上分式域为 $E$ 的有限整环 $C$，再用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization} 将其实现为 $B\hookrightarrow C=\mathcal O_{Z,z}$；此时 $B$ 是任意整 generalized affinoid 代数。Strict 证明中的有限局部环完备化计算原样适用，给出}
\[
                       \widehat R\otimes_R E
                              =\widehat C\otimes_C E.
\]
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedgenericgermregularity} supplies $0\ne b\in B$ with $\widehat C[1/b]$ regular. Its image in $C$ is nonzero by the injection $B\hookrightarrow C$, hence invertible in $E$. Again the fibre is a localization of a regular ring. Tag 0381 treats all primes $\mathfrak P$ and proves geometric regularity of every completion fibre. This completes the proof over arbitrary radii and with either trivial or nontrivial valuation.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedgenericgermregularity}，存在 $0\ne b\in B$，使 $\widehat C[1/b]$ 正则。单射 $B\hookrightarrow C$ 保证其在 $C$ 中的像非零，故在 $E$ 中可逆。因此该纤维仍是正则环的局部化。Tag 0381 处理全部素理想 $\mathfrak P$，给出每个完备化纤维的几何正则性。至此，任意半径以及平凡、非平凡两种赋值的情形均已覆盖。}
\end{proof}
