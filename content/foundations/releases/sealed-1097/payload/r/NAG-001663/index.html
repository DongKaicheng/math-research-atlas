Comparing a free strictification with any extension

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 599

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Comparing a free strictification with any extension}
{将自由 strictification 与任意扩域比较}]
\label{fnd:gausscommonextension}
\bi{Let $s$ be a finite $K$-free polyradius and let $K_s$ be its
Gauss field. For every complete extension $L/K$, there is a complete
field $M$ with compatible isometric embeddings $L\hookrightarrow M$
and $K_s\hookrightarrow M$. One can take the completion of
$L(T_1,\ldots,T_m)$ for the Gauss absolute value of polyradius $s$.}
{设 $s$ 为有限的 $K$-自由多半径，$K_s$ 为相应 Gauss 域。
对每个完备扩域 $L/K$，存在完备域 $M$，具有相容的等距嵌入
$L\hookrightarrow M$ 及 $K_s\hookrightarrow M$。
可以取 $L(T_1,\ldots,T_m)$ 关于多半径 $s$ 的 Gauss 绝对值
的完备化。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The weighted Gauss absolute value on a polynomial ring over a
valued field is multiplicative by Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm}.
It extends to the rational function field by taking quotients.
The inclusion $K(T)\hookrightarrow L(T)$ is isometric because
the coefficientwise maximum defining the Gauss value is unchanged.
It therefore extends to an isometric map from the completion of
$K(T)$ into $M$. This completion is $K_s$: the Laurent monomials
are an orthogonal basis in the free-radius case, and a nonzero
polynomial has a unique dominant monomial, so its reciprocal is
a convergent Laurent geometric series, as in
Lemma~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusfield}.
The constant-field inclusion $L\hookrightarrow M$ is also isometric.
Both maps restrict to the given inclusion of $K$, which proves
compatibility. No independence of $s$ over the larger value group
of $L$ is required.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm}，赋值域上多项式环的加权
Gauss 绝对值可乘，因此可用商延拓到有理函数域。
包含 $K(T)\hookrightarrow L(T)$ 等距，因为定义 Gauss 值的
系数最大值不变。故它延拓为 $K(T)$ 的完备化到 $M$ 的等距映射。
此完备化就是 $K_s$：在自由半径情形，Laurent 单项式构成
正交基，且每个非零多项式有唯一占优单项式，所以其逆可写成
收敛的 Laurent 几何级数，见 Lemma~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusfield}。
常数域包含 $L\hookrightarrow M$ 也等距；两个映射在 $K$ 上
都等于给定包含，因此相容。这里不要求 $s$ 相对于 $L$ 的较大
值群仍然自由。}
\end{proof}
