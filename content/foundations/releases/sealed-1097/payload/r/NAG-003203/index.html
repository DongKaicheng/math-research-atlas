Pullback of the data and of the root norms

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1950

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[Pullback of the data and of the root norms]\label{f9be:pullback}
The scheme $\cY$ is proper, flat and of finite type over $R'$;
$q$ is finite locally free of rank $n$.
Its generic fibre is $X_L=X\times_K L$.
The actual pulled-back lines, sections and identifications have the
same exponents $a_i$. With $Z'_i=q^{-1}Z_i$ and $T'=q^{-1}T$,
their supported operators $I'_i$ define
\[
 \beta_{\cY}=I'_0\cdots I'_d[\cY]\in\CH_0(T'_{\red}).
\]
The support $T'_{\red}$ is proper over $k'$.
Their pointwise root model norms are the pullbacks of the original
norms, using the original extending absolute value.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
On an affine chart $B$ of $\cX$ the fibre product has ring
$B\otimes_RR'$, locally free of rank $n$ over $B$ by
Lemma~\ref{f9be:dvr}. Properness and finite type persist by base
change. Flatness over $R'$ follows by tensoring an exact sequence
of $R'$-modules with the flat $R$-module $B$.
Localizing at $\varpi$ gives $B\otimes_RL$; these identifications
glue to the stated generic fibre.

Pullback preserves all specified line and cross-product identities,
and the zero schemes of the factor sections pull back to their
scheme-theoretic inverse images. This is exactly the arbitrary-source
pullback scope of \texttt{f9sc:signed}, not a new assertion about
arbitrary rational data on a nonreduced scheme.
Every component of $\cY$ dominates $\cX$ by
Lemma~\ref{f9be:transfer}; hence it is horizontal and has dimension
$d+1$. The total generic support is empty because it is the inverse
image of $\bigcap_i S_i$. Thus the final reduced support is vertical
and proper over $k'$, and the supported operations give the stated
zero-class.

For the norm statement let $y\in X_L^{\an}$ and $x=q(y)$.
The valued residue field at $y$ contains the valued residue field
at $x$ isometrically. The centre on $\cX$ extended to the valuation
ring at $y$, together with its map from $R'$, gives the centre on
$\cY$ by the fibre-product property. Uniqueness is the valuative
criterion for properness, \st{0BX5}, as in \cite{MI}.
A local model frame pulls back to a frame. If a vector to the
$a_i$th power has coefficient $c$ in this frame, both norms are
$|c|^{1/a_i}$. Units have norm one. This also handles the actual
generic identifications and their tensor powers.
\end{proof}
