Result 3.10.2

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 504

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}\label{fnd:rzcompact}
\bi{The space $\operatorname{RZ}_k(F)$ is quasi-compact and quasi-separated. Each displayed basic open is quasi-compact.}
{$\operatorname{RZ}_k(F)$ 为 quasi-compact 且 quasi-separated。上式每个 basic open 都 quasi-compact。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{We use product compactness in \cite[Tag 08ZU]{StacksAlg}.}
{使用 \cite[Tag 08ZU]{StacksAlg} 的乘积紧性。}
\bi{Encode a subset $V\subset F$ by its characteristic function in $\{0,1\}^F$, with the product of discrete topologies. The conditions that $V$ contain $k$, be closed under addition, multiplication and negatives, and satisfy $a\in V$ or $a^{-1}\in V$ for every $a\ne0$, are closed conditions on finitely many coordinates at a time. They characterize valuation subrings with fraction field $F$. Thus these subrings form a closed subset of the compact product.}
{用 $\{0,1\}^F$ 中的 characteristic function 编码 subset $V\subset F$，其中使用 discrete topologies 的 product。要求 $V$ 包含 $k$，对 addition、multiplication 和 negatives 封闭，且每个 $a\ne0$ 满足 $a\in V$ 或 $a^{-1}\in V$，均为每次只涉及有限 coordinates 的 closed conditions。这些条件恰好刻画 fraction field 为 $F$ 的 valuation subrings。因此它们构成 compact product 的 closed subset。}

\bi{Each $U(f_1,\ldots,f_m)$ is clopen in this finer topology and therefore compact there. The Riemann--Zariski topology is coarser, so the same set is quasi-compact in it. These sets form a basis closed under finite intersections; hence the space is also quasi-separated. This proof does not assume that an arbitrary inverse limit of quasi-compact topological spaces is quasi-compact.}
{每个 $U(f_1,\ldots,f_m)$ 在此 finer topology 中都是 clopen，因而 compact。Riemann--Zariski topology 较粗，所以该 set 在其中 quasi-compact。这些 sets 构成对 finite intersections 封闭的 basis，故该 space 也 quasi-separated。此证明不假设任意 quasi-compact topological spaces 的 inverse limit 仍 quasi-compact。}
\end{proof}
