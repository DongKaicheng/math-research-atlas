The full ordinary boundary functional; F8AG-T

I Descent of the polarized mixed tower

Open in the English reader · PDF page 2093

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[The full ordinary boundary functional; F8AG-T]
\label{thm:trace}
For \(\beta\in\cA^{n-1,n}_{X,c}(X)\), the finite facet construction
below gives a canonical linear functional \(\BB_{X,\mathcal F}(\beta)\)
with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fullstokes}
             \BB_{X,\mathcal F}(\beta)=\int_{X,\mathcal F}d'\beta.
\end{equation}
With R-F7, it factors through the actual ordinary trace map
\begin{equation}\label{eq:actualtrace}
 r_{\partial X}:\cA^{n-1,n}_{X,c}(X)\longrightarrow
       \prod_{z\in\partial_KX}\cA^{n-1,n}_{X,z},\qquad
                 \beta\longmapsto(\beta_z)_z.
\end{equation}
That is, there is a unique functional on \(\operatorname{im}r_{\partial X}\)
whose composite is \(\BB_{X,\mathcal F}\). This is a full boundary-germ
functional, not an assertion that a boundary trace vanishes.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Let \(S=\Supp\beta\). Choose finitely many ordinary affinoid
neighbourhoods \(V_i\), whose ordinary interiors cover \(S\), such
that \(\beta|_{V_i}=t_{g_i}^*\widetilde\beta_i\). Their union
\(Q\) is a compact analytic neighbourhood of \(S\). For each
nonempty index set \(I\), put \(V_I=\bigcap_{i\in I}V_i\), and use
one \(i\in I\) to restrict its tuple and representative to \(V_I\).
The coefficient module is the actual
\(M_I=\Gamma(V_I,\mathcal F)\). Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tracerecipe}
 \BB_{X,\mathcal F}(\beta)
  =\sum_{I\ne\varnothing}(-1)^{|I|+1}
       \Phi_{\TT_{g_i|V_I}(M_I)}(\widetilde\beta_i).
\end{equation}
Empty intersections contribute zero. Domain purity supplies the correct
analytic dimension for every nonempty intersection, even if its moment
image has lower dimension.

Apply \eqref{eq:facetstokes} term by term. Exterior differentiation is
well-defined on ordinary forms by \tagg{NAG-002424}, and
\(\Supp(d'\beta)\subset S\). The finite compact integration theorem
\tagg{NAG-002416} identifies the sum with
\(\mathcal I_{Q,\mathcal F}(d'\beta)\), and
\tagg{NAG-002425} identifies this with the right side of
\eqref{eq:fullstokes}. Those theorems prove independence of the choice
of \(i\), all representatives, subdivisions, the presenting cover,
and the compact neighbourhood. Thus the explicit facet construction
has the claimed intrinsic value. A single global moment representative
on an affinoid recovers \eqref{eq:facet} itself.

The kernel of \eqref{eq:actualtrace} consists exactly of the compact
sections zero on an ordinary neighbourhood of the actual analytic
boundary. Such a section has compact support in \(\Int(X/K)\).
R-F7 makes its right side in \eqref{eq:fullstokes} zero. Elementary
linear factorization proves the boundary-germ assertion. This factor
is defined only on the image of the trace map; it does not assign
values to arbitrary incompatible germs along the boundary.
\end{proof}
