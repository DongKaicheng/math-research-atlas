Generic points of strict canonical reduction

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 527

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Generic points of strict canonical reduction}
{Strict 典范约化的泛点}]
\label{fnd:canonicalgenericshilov}
\bi{For a strict affinoid algebra $A$, the reduction kernels}
{对 strict affinoid 代数 $A$，约化核}
\[
 P_\xi=\ker\bigl(\widetilde A\longrightarrow
                \widetilde{\mathcal H(\xi)}\bigr),
                   \qquad \xi\in\Gamma_A,
\]
\bi{are exactly the minimal prime ideals of $\widetilde A$.
They are distinct, and each has the unique analytic preimage $\xi$.}
{恰为 $\widetilde A$ 的极小素理想。它们两两不同，且每个都
具有唯一的解析原像 $\xi$。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{These are prime ideals and their intersection is zero, by
the finite boundary maximum formula. For each $\xi$, choose
$a_\xi$ peaking at $\xi$ among $\Gamma_A$. If its maximum is
$m$, Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictspectralvaluegroup} gives $e>0$ and
$c\in K^\times$ with $|c|=m^e$. Replacing $a_\xi$ by
$a_\xi^e/c$ produces a power-bounded function with value one at
$\xi$ and value strictly less than one at every other Shilov point.
Its reduction is in every $P_\eta$ except $P_\xi$.
Thus these primes are pairwise incomparable. Their zero intersection
implies that every prime contains at least one of them, by taking
a product of elements outside that prime if none were contained.
They are therefore exactly the minimal primes.}
{这些都是素理想，且由有限边界最大值公式，它们的交为零。
对每个 $\xi$，选取在 $\Gamma_A$ 上只于 $\xi$ 达到最大值的
$a_\xi$。若其最大值为 $m$，由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictspectralvaluegroup}，存在 $e>0$ 及
$c\in K^\times$，使 $|c|=m^e$。将 $a_\xi$ 替换为
$a_\xi^e/c$，便得到在 $\xi$ 处取值一、在其余每个 Shilov 点
处取值严格小于一的幂有界函数。其约化属于除 $P_\xi$ 外的全部
$P_\eta$。故这些素理想两两不可比较。它们的交为零又说明任意
素理想至少包含其中一个：否则，对每个 $P_\xi$ 选取不属于该
素理想的元素并相乘，就会产生矛盾。因此它们恰为全部极小素理想。}

\bi{Suppose an analytic point $y$ has reduction kernel $P_\xi$,
and use the normalized peaking function $a=a_\xi$. Then
$|a(y)|=1$. If $|b(\xi)|>0$, choose $n$ large enough that
$\rho(a^nb)=|b(\xi)|$, then choose $e>0$ and $c\in K^\times$
with $|c|=|b(\xi)|^e$. The power-bounded element
$a^{ne}b^e/c$ has value one at $\xi$; its reduction is outside
$P_\xi$ and hence has value one at $y$. It follows that
$|b(y)|=|b(\xi)|$. If $b(\xi)=0$, put
$q=\max_{\eta\ne\xi}|a(\eta)|<1$, with empty maxima equal to zero. The estimates
$|b(y)|\leq q^n\max_{\eta\ne\xi}|b(\eta)|$ for all $n$
give $b(y)=0$. Thus $y$ and $\xi$ agree on every element of $A$,
and are the same analytic point.}
{若解析点 $y$ 的约化核为 $P_\xi$，取正规化的峰值函数
$a=a_\xi$，则 $|a(y)|=1$。若 $|b(\xi)|>0$，先取充分大
的 $n$，使 $\rho(a^nb)=|b(\xi)|$，再取 $e>0$ 及
$c\in K^\times$，使 $|c|=|b(\xi)|^e$。幂有界元素
$a^{ne}b^e/c$ 在 $\xi$ 处取值一，其约化不属于 $P_\xi$，
故在 $y$ 处也取值一，从而 $|b(y)|=|b(\xi)|$。
若 $b(\xi)=0$，记
$q=\max_{\eta\ne\xi}|a(\eta)|<1$，空集上的最大值约定为零。对所有 $n$ 都有
$|b(y)|\leq q^n\max_{\eta\ne\xi}|b(\eta)|$，故 $b(y)=0$。
于是 $y$ 与 $\xi$ 在 $A$ 的每个元素上取值相同，是同一个解析点。}
\end{proof}
