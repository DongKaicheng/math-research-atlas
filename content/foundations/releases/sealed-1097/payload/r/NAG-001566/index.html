Free-radius extension is algebraically faithfully flat

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 115

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Free-radius extension is algebraically faithfully flat}{自由半径扩张的代数忠实平坦性}]\label{fnd:freeextensionfaithfullyflat}
\bi{Let $A$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra and let $r$ be any finite tuple free over $K$, not necessarily one that makes $A_r$ strict. For every finite canonical $A$-module $M$ there are natural bounded $A_r$-linear isomorphisms}
{设 $A$ 为 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数，$r$ 为在 $K$ 上自由的任意有限元组，不要求它使 $A_r$ 成为 strict。对每个有限典范 $A$-模 $M$，有自然的有界 $A_r$-线性同构}
\[
 A_r\otimes_A M\simeq A_r\widehat\otimes_A M
       \simeq M\widehat\otimes_K K_r.
\]
\bi{They are isometric for a specified finite-presentation quotient norm on $M$. The ring map $A\to A_r$ is faithfully flat for ordinary tensor products on all algebraic $A$-modules. On finite canonical modules, its ordinary and separated completed tensor sequences are identified with the coefficient sequences and preserve norm-strict short exactness. This statement uses no flatness claim for completed tensors of arbitrary Banach modules.}
{对 $M$ 上指定的有限表示商范数，这些同构等距。环映射 $A\to A_r$ 对所有代数 $A$-模的普通张量积为忠实平坦映射。在有限典范模上，其普通张量序列及分离完备张量序列均识别为系数序列，并保持 norm-strict 短正合性。此处没有使用任意 Banach 模完备张量积的平坦性断言。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{By Lemma~\ref{fnd:freecoefficientretraction}, $A_r$ is the actual coefficient algebra $\mathcal C_r(\mathbf Z^s,A)$, with its multiplicative weight.\par
Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedcoefficienttensor} to that algebra and then Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients} to $M$. These give the displayed natural isomorphisms, including their norm statements and their compatibility with maps of finite modules. Thus the finite-module tensor comparison has been established before flatness is tested.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:freecoefficientretraction}，$A_r$ 就是采用乘性权重的实际系数代数 $\mathcal C_r(\mathbf Z^s,A)$。\par
对该代数应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedcoefficienttensor}，再对 $M$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients}，即得所列自然同构、范数断言及其与有限模映射的相容性。因此，在检验平坦性之前，已经建立了有限模张量积比较。}

\bi{For a finitely generated ideal $J\subset A$, the natural map $A_r\otimes_A J\to A_r\otimes_A A=A_r$ is identified with the coefficientwise inclusion of $J$ into $A$. This map is injective. The finite-module norms needed here are supplied by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}; the induced norm on $J$ is one of its canonical norms. The finitely generated ideal criterion, \cite[Tag 00HD, Lemma 10.39.5(4)]{StacksAlg}, now proves ordinary flatness on all $A$-modules.}
{对有限生成理想 $J\subset A$，自然映射 $A_r\otimes_A J\to A_r\otimes_A A=A_r$ 被识别为将 $J$ 逐系数包含到 $A$ 的映射，因此为单射。这里所需的有限模范数由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules} 给出；$J$ 的诱导范数为其典范范数之一。由有限生成理想判别准则 \cite[Tag 00HD, Lemma 10.39.5(4)]{StacksAlg}，得到所有 $A$-模上的普通平坦性。}

\bi{Let $N$ now be any algebraic $A$-module, with no norm or finite-generation assumption. The map}
{再令 $N$ 为任意代数 $A$-模，不假设它有范数或有限生成。映射}
\[
 A_r\otimes_A N\longrightarrow N,
 \qquad b\otimes n\longmapsto p_0^A(b)n
\]
\bi{is well-defined because $p_0^A$ is $A$-linear. It retracts $n\mapsto1\otimes n$. Thus every nonzero $N$ has nonzero scalar extension. Having already proved flatness, we apply \cite[Tag 00HP, Lemma 10.39.15(2)]{StacksAlg} to obtain faithful flatness. This retraction need not be $A_r$-linear. Finally, the finite-module norm-strict exactness is the corresponding assertion of Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedcoefficienttensor}; it was proved coefficientwise, independently of the algebraic flatness criterion.}
{由 $p_0^A$ 的 $A$-线性，该映射良定义，并且是 $n\mapsto1\otimes n$ 的收缩。因此每个非零 $N$ 的标量扩张都非零。平坦性已经证明，故可应用 \cite[Tag 00HP, Lemma 10.39.15(2)]{StacksAlg} 得到忠实平坦性。这个收缩无须为 $A_r$-线性。最后，有限模的 norm-strict 正合性就是 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedcoefficienttensor} 的相应结论；它已通过逐系数论证独立于代数平坦性判别而得到证明。}
\end{proof}
