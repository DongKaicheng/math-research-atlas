NAG-002095 · Accepted whole

Lifting an arbitrary finite residue extension

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 704

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Lifting an arbitrary finite residue extension}
{提升任意有限剩余域扩张}]
\label{cand:arbitraryresidueextensionlift}
\bi{For every finite extension $l/k$, there is a finite valued
extension $F/K$ with $\widetilde F\simeq l$ over $k$ and}
{对每个有限扩张 $l/k$，存在有限赋值扩张 $F/K$，使
$\widetilde F\simeq l$ 为 $k$ 上同构，且}
\[
 [F:K]=[l:k],\qquad |F^\times|=|K^\times|.
\]
\bi{If $\operatorname{char}K=0$, the extension $F/K$ is separable.}
{若 $\operatorname{char}K=0$，则 $F/K$ 可分。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For a simple extension $l=k(\beta)$, use the construction
in Lemma~\ref{cand:residueextensionlift}: lift the monic
irreducible polynomial of $\beta$, adjoin a root, and apply
the value-group and residue-degree inequality. Every step
through the displayed chain of degree inequalities in that
proof uses only irreducibility, so it gives the asserted
residue field, degree, and value group for this simple
extension. A general finite
extension has a finite tower of simple extensions. Apply
the construction successively, using completeness and the
unique extending absolute value from
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} at every step. Degrees
multiply and the value group does not change. Finally, in
characteristic zero an irreducible polynomial has nonzero
derivative relatively prime to it, so all finite extensions
are separable.}
{对单扩张 $l=k(\beta)$，使用
Lemma~\ref{cand:residueextensionlift} 的构造：提升
$\beta$ 的首一不可约多项式，添入一个根，再应用值群及
剩余次数不等式。该证明直到所列次数不等式链的每一步，
都只使用不可约性，因此对这个单扩张给出所需剩余域、
次数及值群。一般有限扩张具有由单扩张
组成的有限塔。逐步应用此构造，每一步都由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 使用完备性及唯一
延拓绝对值。次数相乘，值群始终不变。最后，在特征零
时，不可约多项式的导数非零且与原多项式互素，因此
所有有限扩张均可分。}
\end{proof}
