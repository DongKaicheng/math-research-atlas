The diagonal relation

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[The diagonal relation]\label{lem:diagonal}
Suppose $f$ and $g$ are two equal-length moment tuples on one compact affinoid $C$ and $|f_i|=|g_i|$ everywhere on $C$. For every ambient smooth superform $\alpha$ defined near their common moment image, the two pullbacks of $\alpha$ to the actual joint image $t_{(f,g)}(C)$ agree tangentially on every cell and face. The assertion remains true after adjoining any finite tuple of further moment coordinates.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The actual joint image lies in the diagonal $\{(u,u)\}$. On this diagonal, the two coordinate projections agree on points and on tangent vectors. Their pullbacks therefore agree in both exterior factors of a superform. Every cell tangent of the joint image lies in the diagonal tangent. Adjoining coordinates preserves the equality of the first two projections. Finite actual-image subdivisions are available by \tagid{002358}. Thus the assertion applies to the finite-image germ relations of \tagid{002424}.
\end{proof}
