Extension of semilinear actions

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 787

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[Extension of semilinear actions]
\label{v037:f5:intrinsicactions}
Let a group $G$ act on $C$ and an unramified marking
datum $D$ by compatible semilinear isometric automorphisms.
If $V$ is an invariant admissible vertex set and $C$
admits a semistable algebraic model over $O$, the model
corresponding to $V$ carries a unique $G$-action inducing
the prescribed generic action and preserving the marking.
All morphisms arising from inclusions between invariant
admissible vertex sets are equivariant.
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
An isometric field automorphism preserves $O$. For
$g\in G$, pull the model back by its base automorphism
and use its prescribed generic curve isomorphism to
identify the new generic fibre with $C$. This is again
a semistable algebraic model. Functorial specialization
identifies its vertices with the corresponding image
of $V$, which is $V$. The pulled-back marking is
identified with $D$ by its given semilinear action.
Corollary~\ref{v037:f5:intrinsicuniqueness} therefore
gives the unique semilinear model isomorphism extending
$g$. Two composites which have the same generic action
are equal by the schematic-density equalizer argument in
the proof of Theorem~\ref{v037:f5:algebraicdomination}. Thus these
isomorphisms satisfy the group law and form the asserted
action. The same uniqueness, applied to the two
composites of a vertex-inclusion morphism with $g$, proves
equivariance of every such morphism.
\end{proof}
