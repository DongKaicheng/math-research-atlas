Rational-local density for strict-affinoid epimorphisms

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 227

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Rational-local density for strict-affinoid epimorphisms}{Strict-affinoid 上态的有理局部稠密性}]\label{fnd:strictgerritzengrauertlocaldense}
\bi{Let $\varphi:A\to B$ be a strict-affinoid epimorphism over the complete nontrivially valued field $K$. There is a finite rational cover $\mathcal M(A)=\bigcup_iR_i$ such that, writing $A_i$ for the rational algebra of $R_i$ and $B_i=B\widehat\otimes_AA_i$ for its pullback, every map $A_i\to B_i$ has dense image. All the algebras $A_i,B_i$ are strict; zero members are permitted. The cover concerns all bounded multiplicative seminorms.}
{设 $\varphi:A\to B$ 为完备非平凡赋值域 $K$ 上的 strict-affinoid 上态。则存在有限有理覆盖 $\mathcal M(A)=\bigcup_iR_i$，使若以 $A_i$ 表示 $R_i$ 的有理代数，并以 $B_i=B\widehat\otimes_AA_i$ 表示其拉回，则每个映射 $A_i\to B_i$ 的像均稠密。所有代数 $A_i,B_i$ 均为 strict，允许零成员。此覆盖涉及全部有界乘性半范数。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{We first record the base-change facts used in the induction. A categorical epimorphism remains an epimorphism under the completed pushouts of Proposition~\ref{fnd:relativeaffinoidpushouts}: two maps from the pushout agreeing on its new base agree on $B$ by the old epimorphism property, and therefore agree everywhere by the pushout property. For a rational base change, this pushout is precisely the rational quotient of $B$ with the pulled-back defining functions, by its universal property. An admissible relative presentation $A\langle T\rangle\twoheadrightarrow B$ gives an admissible relative presentation $A'\langle T\rangle\twoheadrightarrow B\widehat\otimes_AA'$ by the same actual-ideal presentation formula. Every maximal point of a strict rational algebra over $A$ restricts to a maximal point of $A$, and the finite residue extension preserves absolute values, as proved in Lemma~\ref{fnd:relativemaximalorderlocus}. Consequently fibrewise distinguished orders are preserved under these base changes.}
{先列出归纳中使用的基变换事实。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:relativeaffinoidpushouts}，范畴上态在完备推出下仍为上态：从推出出发且在新基底上相同的两个映射，由原上态性质在 $B$ 上相同，再由推出泛性质处处相同。对有理基变换，由其泛性质，该推出正是用拉回后的定义函数构造的 $B$ 的有理商。相同的实际理想表示公式，将 admissible 相对表示 $A\langle T\rangle\twoheadrightarrow B$ 变成 admissible 相对表示 $A'\langle T\rangle\twoheadrightarrow B\widehat\otimes_AA'$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:relativemaximalorderlocus} 中的证明，$A$ 上 strict 有理代数的每个极大点都限制为 $A$ 的极大点，且有限剩余域扩张保持绝对值。因此逐纤维的 distinguished 阶数在这些基变换下保持不变。}

\bi{Induct on the number $n$ of variables in an admissible relative presentation, which exists by Lemma~\ref{fnd:relativeaffinoidpresentation}. For $n=0$ the map is surjective, and the one-member cover suffices. If $A=0$ or $B=0$, the same conclusion is immediate. For $n\geq1$, Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictprimitiveepikernel} supplies a series in the kernel with unit coefficient ideal. By Lemma~\ref{fnd:relativeuniformdistinguished}, an isometric coordinate change makes it $T_n$-distinguished of order at most some integer $s$ at every maximal point. The relative presentation remains admissible.}
{对 admissible 相对表示中的变量数 $n$ 作归纳；这种表示由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:relativeaffinoidpresentation} 保证存在。当 $n=0$ 时映射为满射，单成员覆盖已经足够。若 $A=0$ 或 $B=0$，同样直接得到结论。当 $n\geq1$ 时，Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictprimitiveepikernel} 给出核中一个系数生成单位理想的级数。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:relativeuniformdistinguished}，经过等距坐标变换，可使其在每个极大点处都是阶数至多为某个整数 $s$ 的 $T_n$-distinguished 级数。相对表示仍为 admissible。}

\bi{Assume the theorem proved for presentations with fewer than $n$ variables. For the fixed number $n$, we use a second induction on $s$ for presentations whose kernel contains such a series $F$. If $s=0$, Proposition~\ref{fnd:relativepointwisedivisionfinite} gives $A\langle T\rangle/(F)=0$, so $B=0$. Suppose $s>0$. Let $R_s\subset\mathcal M(A)$ be the rational maximal-order locus of Lemma~\ref{fnd:relativemaximalorderlocus}, with algebra $A_s$, and put $B_s=B\widehat\otimes_AA_s$. If $A_s=0$, all maximal orders are at most $s-1$, and the inner induction applies directly. Otherwise, over $A_s$ the image of $F$ has order exactly $s$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:relativepointwisedivisionfinite} makes}
{假设定理已对少于 $n$ 个变量的表示成立。对固定的变量数 $n$，再按照上述核级数 $F$ 的阶数界 $s$ 作第二重归纳。若 $s=0$，则由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:relativepointwisedivisionfinite}，有 $A\langle T\rangle/(F)=0$，所以 $B=0$。设 $s>0$。令 $R_s\subset\mathcal M(A)$ 为 Lemma~\ref{fnd:relativemaximalorderlocus} 给出的最大阶数有理域，其代数记为 $A_s$，并令 $B_s=B\widehat\otimes_AA_s$。若 $A_s=0$，则所有极大点处的阶数都至多为 $s-1$，可直接应用内层归纳。否则，在 $A_s$ 上 $F$ 的像的阶数处处恰为 $s$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:relativepointwisedivisionfinite}，映射}
\[
 C_s=A_s\langle T_1,\ldots,T_{n-1}\rangle\longrightarrow B_s
\]
\bi{finite, since $B_s$ is a quotient of $A_s\langle T\rangle/(F)$. It is also a strict-affinoid epimorphism: two maps agreeing on $C_s$ agree on $A_s$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictfiniteepimorphism} makes it surjective, and Lemma~\ref{fnd:nonarchopenmapping} makes this surjection admissible. The outer induction therefore yields a finite rational cover $R_s=\bigcup_{i=1}^rU_i$ on which the corresponding maps have dense image. By rational transitivity, Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaltransitivity}, each $U_i$ is rational in $\mathcal M(A)$, with the same algebra and pullback algebra up to the unique bounded comparison maps.}
{为有限映射，因为 $B_s$ 是 $A_s\langle T\rangle/(F)$ 的商。它同时也是 strict-affinoid 上态：在 $C_s$ 上相同的两个映射必在 $A_s$ 上相同。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictfiniteepimorphism}，该映射为满射；再由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:nonarchopenmapping}，此满射为 admissible。因此，外层归纳给出有限有理覆盖 $R_s=\bigcup_{i=1}^rU_i$，使其上相应映射的像稠密。由有理传递性 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaltransitivity}，每个 $U_i$ 也在 $\mathcal M(A)$ 中有理，且其代数与拉回代数在唯一的有界比较映射下保持一致。}

\bi{Choose presentations}
{选择表示}
\[
 U_i=\{x:|f_{ij}(x)|\leq|g_i(x)|\ (1\leq j\leq m_i)\},
 \qquad (g_i,f_{i1},\ldots,f_{im_i})=A.
\]
\bi{The extension theorem, Theorem~\ref{fnd:rationaldenseextension}, supplies $\epsilon_i\in\sqrt{|K^\times|}$ with $\epsilon_i>1$ such that the map on}
{由延拓定理 Theorem~\ref{fnd:rationaldenseextension}，存在 $\epsilon_i\in\sqrt{|K^\times|}$，$\epsilon_i>1$，使定义在}
\[
 U_i^+=\{x:|f_{ij}(x)|\leq\epsilon_i|g_i(x)|\text{ for all }j\}
\]
\bi{still has dense image. These sets have strict rational presentations by Proposition~\ref{fnd:radicalrationalpresentation}. We construct a finite rational family covering what remains, and disjoint from $R_s$. For each $i$ and $1\leq j\leq m_i$, put}
{上的映射仍具有稠密像。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:radicalrationalpresentation}，这些集合具有 strict 有理表示。下面构造一个有限有理族，覆盖余下部分且与 $R_s$ 不交。对每个 $i$ 及 $1\leq j\leq m_i$，令}
\[
 V_{ij}=\{x:\epsilon_i|g_i(x)|\leq|f_{ij}(x)|,
 \quad |f_{i\ell}(x)|\leq|f_{ij}(x)|\ (\ell\ne j)\}.
\]
\bi{To see that this is strict rational, choose $N_i\geq1$ and $c_i\in K^\times$ with $|c_i|=\epsilon_i^{N_i}$ and raise the inequalities to the $N_i$th power. The denominator is $f_{ij}^{N_i}$ and the numerators are $c_ig_i^{N_i}$ and the other $f_{i\ell}^{N_i}$. Their ideal is the unit ideal, since any maximal ideal containing them would contain $g_i$ and all the $f_{i\ell}$. The displayed inequalities show $V_{ij}\cap U_i=\varnothing$: otherwise $(\epsilon_i-1)|g_i(x)|\leq0$, so all entries of a unit tuple vanish at $x$. If $x\notin U_i^+$, choose $j$ attaining the maximum of the $|f_{i\ell}(x)|$. Then $x\in V_{ij}$.}
{为说明这是 strict 有理域，选择 $N_i\geq1$ 与 $c_i\in K^\times$，使 $|c_i|=\epsilon_i^{N_i}$，再将不等式提升至 $N_i$ 次幂。分母为 $f_{ij}^{N_i}$，分子为 $c_ig_i^{N_i}$ 及其余 $f_{i\ell}^{N_i}$。它们生成单位理想，因为若某个极大理想包含这些元素，就会包含 $g_i$ 及全部 $f_{i\ell}$。所列不等式还给出 $V_{ij}\cap U_i=\varnothing$：否则 $(\epsilon_i-1)|g_i(x)|\leq0$，于是该单位列表的各元素均在 $x$ 处消失。若 $x\notin U_i^+$，选择使 $|f_{ij}(x)|$ 达到全部 $|f_{i\ell}(x)|$ 最大值的 $j$，便有 $x\in V_{ij}$。}

\bi{Take all finite intersections $V=\bigcap_iV_{ij_i}$. They are strict rational by Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation}, they are disjoint from every $U_i$ and hence from $R_s$, and together with the $U_i^+$ they cover all of $\mathcal M(A)$. If some $m_i=0$, then $U_i=\mathcal M(A)$ and the already dense map on $U_i$ settles the assertion; thus the intersection family is only needed when every $m_i>0$. On each nonzero algebra $A_V$, every maximal point restricts to a maximal point of $A$ outside $R_s$. The restricted kernel series consequently has distinguished order at most $s-1$. The inner induction gives a finite rational cover of $V$ with dense maps. By transitivity these final members are rational in $\mathcal M(A)$. They, together with the $U_i^+$, give the desired finite cover. Both induction parameters are nonnegative integers and strictly decrease at their indicated steps.}
{取全部有限交 $V=\bigcap_iV_{ij_i}$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation}，它们均为 strict 有理域；它们与每个 $U_i$ 都不交，因而与 $R_s$ 不交，并且与各个 $U_i^+$ 一同覆盖整个 $\mathcal M(A)$。若某个 $m_i=0$，则 $U_i=\mathcal M(A)$，其上已经稠密的映射直接给出所需结论；因此只有当每个 $m_i>0$ 时才需要上述交族。在每个非零代数 $A_V$ 上，每个极大点都限制为 $A$ 中位于 $R_s$ 之外的极大点。因此，限制后的核级数具有至多为 $s-1$ 的 distinguished 阶数。内层归纳给出 $V$ 的有限有理覆盖，其上各映射的像稠密。由传递性，最终这些成员在 $\mathcal M(A)$ 中有理。将其与各个 $U_i^+$ 合并，即得所需的有限覆盖。两个归纳参数都是非负整数，并且在各自所述步骤严格下降。}
\end{proof}
