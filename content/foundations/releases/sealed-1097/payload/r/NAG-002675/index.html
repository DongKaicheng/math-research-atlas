\begin{theorem}[Reduction to an \(S_1\) scalar PL statement; F8U-R]
\label{thm:reduction}
Assume (SC), the frozen interfaces specified above, and the exact F7
inputs used by F8F7-S. Suppose the scalar PL identity is known on good
Hausdorff pure \(n\)-dimensional \(S_1\) analytic spaces for regular
analytic functions and ordinary compact tests in the analytic interior.
Then it holds on good Hausdorff pure \(n\)-dimensional analytic spaces
for regular meromorphic functions and the same test class.

The corresponding implication holds with both premise and conclusion
restricted to boundaryless spaces.
\end{theorem}
