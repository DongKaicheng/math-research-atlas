Finite covers and closed supports on proper analytifications

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 301

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Finite covers and closed supports on proper analytifications}
{适当解析化上的有限覆盖与闭支撑}]
\label{fnd:properanalyticfinitecovers}
\bi{Let $X$ be a proper finite-type $K$-scheme. Its analytification
has a finite affinoid covering all of whose finite intersections
are affinoid. If $i:T\hookrightarrow X$ is a closed subscheme,
closed direct image preserves coherent modules and their cohomology
on both the algebraic and the analytic sites; the resulting
cohomology identifications commute with analytification.}
{设 $X$ 是适当的有限型 $K$-概形。其解析化具有有限 affinoid
覆盖，且所有有限交仍为 affinoid。若 $i:T\hookrightarrow X$
为闭子概形，则在代数及解析 site 上，闭直接像都保持凝聚模及其
上同调；所得上同调识别与解析化相容。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The space $X^{\an}$ is good by the radius construction,
compact by Theorem~\ref{fnd:analytificationproperpreservation},
and has a closed analytic diagonal by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:analytificationseparatedtopology}.
Choose affinoid neighbourhoods and a finite subcover of their
interiors. If $V_1,V_2$ are two of these domains, their fibre
product over $X^{\an}$ is the inverse image of its closed
diagonal in the affinoid space $V_1\times_K V_2$.
It is therefore affinoid, by the quotient presentation of a
closed analytic subspace. The universal properties of domains
identify this fibre product with $V_1\cap V_2$.
Iteration proves the assertion for every finite intersection.}
{由半径构造，$X^{\an}$ 是 good；由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:analytificationproperpreservation}，它紧；
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:analytificationseparatedtopology}，
其解析对角为闭浸入。选择 affinoid 邻域，再从它们的内部选取
有限子覆盖。两个这样的域 $V_1,V_2$ 在 $X^{\an}$ 上的
纤维积，是其闭对角在 affinoid 空间 $V_1\times_K V_2$
中的逆像，所以由闭解析子空间的商表示而为 affinoid。
域的泛性质将此纤维积识别为 $V_1\cap V_2$。
迭代即得全部有限交的结论。}

\bi{The inverse images of this covering on $T^{\an}$ are
closed affinoid subspaces. Thus
Lemma~\ref{fnd:closedanalyticcoherentextension} proves the
analytic assertions by the identical finite \v Cech complexes.
Algebraically, a finite affine cover of the separated scheme
$X$ also has affine finite intersections: apply its closed
diagonal to products of affine opens. A finite module over a
quotient $R/J$ is the same as a finite $R$-module killed by $J$,
so closed direct image is exact and coherent. The same affine
\v Cech complex computes cohomology before and after that
direct image, by \cite[Tags 01XB and 03F7]{StacksAlg}.
On every affine--affinoid chart the comparison sends a regular
section to its analytic section. The identical-complex
identifications therefore commute with comparison.}
{此覆盖在 $T^{\an}$ 上的逆像是闭 affinoid 子空间，故
Lemma~\ref{fnd:closedanalyticcoherentextension} 通过相同的
有限 \v Cech 复形给出解析侧的断言。
代数侧，分离概形 $X$ 的有限仿射覆盖的有限交也仿射：
将其闭对角拉回到仿射开集的乘积即可。有限 $R/J$-模与
被 $J$ 消灭的有限 $R$-模等同，所以闭直接像正合且保持凝聚性。
由 \cite[Tags 01XB and 03F7]{StacksAlg}，闭直接像前后的
同一个仿射 \v Cech 复形计算上同调。
在每个仿射--affinoid 坐标片上，比较把正则截面送到相应
解析截面，因此这些相同复形给出的识别保持比较映射。}
\end{proof}
