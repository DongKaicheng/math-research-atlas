2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Fibre products of analytic spaces}{解析空间的纤维积}]
\label{fnd:generalanalyticproducts}
\bi{The category of analytic spaces over $K$ has fibre products. If $Y\to X\leftarrow Z$ are morphisms, the product $P=Y\times_XZ$ has an affinoid chart net consisting of}
{底域 $K$ 上解析空间的范畴具有纤维积。若给定态射 $Y\to X\leftarrow Z$，则纤维积 $P=Y\times_XZ$ 具有由下列空间组成的 affinoid chart net：}
\[
 V\times_UW=
 \mathcal M\bigl(A_V\widehat\otimes_{A_U}A_W\bigr),
\]
\bi{where $U,V,W$ are affinoid analytic domains of $X,Y,Z$ and both $V,W$ map into $U$. Each such product is the analytic domain $p_Y^{-1}(V)\cap p_Z^{-1}(W)$ in $P$. The canonical map $|P|\to |Y|\times_{|X|}|Z|$ is surjective. These constructions are compatible with restricting any of the three spaces to analytic domains.}
{其中 $U,V,W$ 分别为 $X,Y,Z$ 的 affinoid 解析域，且 $V,W$ 均映到 $U$。每个这样的乘积都是 $P$ 中的解析域 $p_Y^{-1}(V)\cap p_Z^{-1}(W)$。典范映射 $|P|\to |Y|\times_{|X|}|Z|$ 为满射。上述构造与将任一空间限制到解析域相容。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{\emph{Step 1: compact Hausdorff spaces.} Suppose first that $X,Y,Z$ are compact Hausdorff. Choose a finite affinoid cover $(U_a)$ of $X$. Its inverse images in $Y$ and $Z$ are compact analytic domains: they are analytic by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing} and closed because $U_a$ is compact in the Hausdorff space $X$. Choose finite affinoid covers $(V_{ai})$ and $(W_{aj})$ of these inverse images. Form the finitely many affinoid products $P_{aij}=V_{ai}\times_{U_a}W_{aj}$ using Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout}.}
{\emph{第一步：紧 Hausdorff 空间。} 先设 $X,Y,Z$ 都紧且 Hausdorff。选择 $X$ 的有限 affinoid 覆盖 $(U_a)$。其在 $Y,Z$ 中的逆像是紧解析域：由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing}，逆像解析；而 $U_a$ 在 Hausdorff 空间 $X$ 中紧，故闭，逆像也闭。分别为这些逆像选择有限 affinoid 覆盖 $(V_{ai})$、$(W_{aj})$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout} 构造有限多个 affinoid 纤维积 $P_{aij}=V_{ai}\times_{U_a}W_{aj}$。}

\bi{For two triples $\alpha=(a,i,j)$ and $\beta=(b,i',j')$, define $P_{\alpha\beta}\subseteq P_\alpha$ by requiring its projections to lie in $V_{ai}\cap V_{bi'}$ and $W_{aj}\cap W_{bj'}$. It is a compact analytic domain, since these are closed analytic domains of the Hausdorff spaces $Y,Z$. To compare it with $P_{\beta\alpha}$, cover $U_a\cap U_b$ by finitely many affinoid domains $R_l$. Cover the inverse images of each $R_l$ in $V_{ai}\cap V_{bi'}$ and $W_{aj}\cap W_{bj'}$ by finite affinoid families $(E_{lm})$ and $(F_{ln})$. The spaces}
{对两个三元指标 $\alpha=(a,i,j)$、$\beta=(b,i',j')$，令 $P_{\alpha\beta}\subseteq P_\alpha$ 为投影分别落在 $V_{ai}\cap V_{bi'}$、$W_{aj}\cap W_{bj'}$ 中的子域。由于后两者是 Hausdorff 空间 $Y,Z$ 的闭解析域，$P_{\alpha\beta}$ 为紧解析域。为将它与 $P_{\beta\alpha}$ 比较，用有限个 affinoid 域 $R_l$ 覆盖 $U_a\cap U_b$，再用有限 affinoid 族 $(E_{lm})$、$(F_{ln})$ 覆盖每个 $R_l$ 在 $V_{ai}\cap V_{bi'}$、$W_{aj}\cap W_{bj'}$ 中的逆像。空间}
\[
 Q_{lmn}=E_{lm}\times_{R_l}F_{ln}
\]
\bi{are affinoid domains in both $P_\alpha$ and $P_\beta$, by repeated use of Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange}. They cover the two designated overlaps: for a point of either overlap, first choose $R_l$ containing its base point and then pieces containing its two projections. Their affinoid algebras are the same completed tensor products on both sides. On two such pieces, refine the base and both source intersections by common affinoid domains; associativity of the bounded pushout, Corollary~\ref{fnd:iteratedaffinoidbasechange}, identifies the resulting tensor products. Thus the identifications agree on overlaps and give $P_{\alpha\beta}\simeq P_{\beta\alpha}$ by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing}. The same argument on triple intersections proves the cocycle condition.}
{由反复应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange}，同时是 $P_\alpha,P_\beta$ 的 affinoid 域。它们覆盖两边指定的交叠：对交叠中的一点，先选包含其底空间像的 $R_l$，再选包含其两个投影的块。两边对应的 affinoid 代数是同一个完备张量积。对两个这样的块，再将底空间及两侧源交叠细分为共同 affinoid 域；Corollary~\ref{fnd:iteratedaffinoidbasechange} 中有界推出的结合律识别所得张量积。因此这些识别在交叠上一致，并由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing} 粘合为 $P_{\alpha\beta}\simeq P_{\beta\alpha}$。在三重交叠上作相同检查，得到 cocycle 条件。}

\bi{The finite closed gluing of Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticspacegluing} now produces a compact Hausdorff analytic space $P$. Its projections glue. Given any analytic $T$ with compatible maps to $Y,Z$, pull back the finite covers just used and refine by affinoid charts of $T$. On each resulting affinoid piece the bounded-pushout property gives the unique map to an appropriate $P_\alpha$. On overlaps these maps agree after the common refinements $Q_{lmn}$, again by the bounded-pushout property. They therefore glue uniquely. This proves the product universal property in the compact Hausdorff case, with arbitrary analytic test spaces $T$.}
{于是 Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticspacegluing} 的有限闭粘合给出紧 Hausdorff 解析空间 $P$，其投影也粘合。给定任意解析空间 $T$ 及其到 $Y,Z$ 的相容态射，拉回刚使用的有限覆盖，并用 $T$ 的 affinoid chart 加细。在每个所得 affinoid 块上，有界推出的泛性质给出到适当 $P_\alpha$ 的唯一映射。在交叠上进一步取公共加细 $Q_{lmn}$，同一个推出泛性质保证映射一致，因此它们唯一粘合。这就对任意解析测试空间 $T$ 证明了紧 Hausdorff 情形的纤维积泛性质。}

\bi{\emph{Step 2: a compact Hausdorff base.} Keep $X$ compact Hausdorff and allow arbitrary $Y,Z$. By Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalnetdomaincalculus}, cover $Y,Z$ by open domains $O_i,O'_j$ contained respectively in compact Hausdorff analytic domains $D_i,D'_j$. Step 1 constructs $D_i\times_XD'_j$. Restrict it to the open domain where the projections lie in $O_i,O'_j$. The result represents the product of these two open domains over $X$, by the analytic-domain universal property. For two index pairs, the corresponding open subdomains over $O_i\cap O_{i'}$ and $O'_j\cap O'_{j'}$ have the same universal property. Their unique comparison isomorphisms satisfy the cocycle condition. Open gluing constructs $Y\times_XZ$. The universal property follows by restricting a test space to the inverse images of these open domains and gluing its local maps.}
{\emph{第二步：紧 Hausdorff 底空间。} 保持 $X$ 紧且 Hausdorff，而允许 $Y,Z$ 任意。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalnetdomaincalculus}，可用开域 $O_i,O'_j$ 覆盖 $Y,Z$，并使它们分别包含于紧 Hausdorff 解析域 $D_i,D'_j$。第一步构造 $D_i\times_XD'_j$；将其限制到投影落在 $O_i,O'_j$ 的开域。由解析域的泛性质，所得空间表示这两个开域在 $X$ 上的纤维积。对两对指标，它们在 $O_i\cap O_{i'}$、$O'_j\cap O'_{j'}$ 上的开子域具有同一个泛性质，所以存在唯一比较同构，且自动满足 cocycle 条件。开粘合构造出 $Y\times_XZ$。将测试空间限制到这些开域的逆像并粘合局部映射，便得到整体泛性质。}

\bi{\emph{Step 3: an arbitrary base.} Cover $X$ by open domains $S_l$ contained in compact Hausdorff analytic domains $C_l$. Put $Y_l=f^{-1}(S_l)$ and $Z_l=g^{-1}(S_l)$. Step 2 constructs $Y_l\times_{C_l}Z_l$. Because both maps have image in $S_l$, its universal property is also that of $Y_l\times_{S_l}Z_l$. The products over two base opens agree on the open subdomains over their intersection. Open gluing therefore gives the required $P$ for arbitrary $X,Y,Z$.}
{\emph{第三步：任意底空间。} 用包含于紧 Hausdorff 解析域 $C_l$ 的开域 $S_l$ 覆盖 $X$，并令 $Y_l=f^{-1}(S_l)$、$Z_l=g^{-1}(S_l)$。第二步构造 $Y_l\times_{C_l}Z_l$。因两侧态射的像均包含于 $S_l$，它也满足 $Y_l\times_{S_l}Z_l$ 的泛性质。两个底空间开域上的纤维积，在其交叠上对应的开子域具有唯一比较同构，因此开粘合给出一般 $X,Y,Z$ 的纤维积 $P$。}

\bi{Finally take affinoid $U,V,W$ as in the statement. The analytic domain $p_Y^{-1}(V)\cap p_Z^{-1}(W)$ has the same universal property as the affinoid bounded pushout $V\times_UW$, since two maps into $U$ agree whenever their composites into $X$ agree. Thus they are canonically isomorphic. The construction shows that these domains form a chart quasi-net, and the same observation proves compatibility with arbitrary analytic-domain restriction. Surjectivity on underlying fibre-product sets is local on these charts and follows from Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidproducttopology}.}
{最后，取陈述中的 affinoid 域 $U,V,W$。解析域 $p_Y^{-1}(V)\cap p_Z^{-1}(W)$ 与 affinoid 有界推出 $V\times_UW$ 具有同一个泛性质，因为两个到 $U$ 的映射只要在复合到 $X$ 后相等，就已经相等。因此二者典范同构。构造说明这些域形成 chart quasi-net；同一观察也给出与任意解析域限制的相容性。底层集合纤维积上的满射性可在这些 chart 上检查，并由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidproducttopology} 得到。}
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[\bi{Locally bounded morphisms}{局部有界态射}]
\label{fnd:generalnetmorphisms}
\bi{For two affinoid atlases, a morphism consists of a continuous map $f:X\to X'$ and, for every pair of charts $U\in\bar\tau$, $V\in\bar\tau'$ with $f(U)\subseteq V$, a bounded homomorphism $f_{U,V}:A_V\to A_U$ inducing $f|_U$. These maps commute with restrictions in both charts. We require that the charts $U$ admitting such a target chart $V$ form a quasi-net of $X$. An analytic space is an affinoid atlas considered up to the canonical identifications under chart refinement of Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf}.}
{对两个 affinoid atlas，一个态射由连续映射 $f:X\to X'$ 及下述资料组成：对每对满足 $f(U)\subseteq V$ 的 chart $U\in\bar\tau$、$V\in\bar\tau'$，给出诱导 $f|_U$ 的有界同态 $f_{U,V}:A_V\to A_U$。这些映射与源、目标 chart 的限制均相容，并要求具有这样一个目标 chart $V$ 的源 chart $U$ 构成 $X$ 的 quasi-net。将 affinoid atlas 按 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf} 中 chart 加细所给的典范识别视为同一对象，得到解析空间。}
\end{definition}
