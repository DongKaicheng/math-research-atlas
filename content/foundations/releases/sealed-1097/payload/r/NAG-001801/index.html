The elementary formal blowup and its weight

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 593

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{The elementary formal blowup and its weight}
{基本形式 blowup 及其权函数}]
\label{fnd:standarddiscblowupcalculation}
\bi{For $0<|a|<1$, the admissible blowup of $(T,a)$ on
$\operatorname{Spf}O\langle T\rangle$ has generic domains
$|T|\leq|a|$ and $|a|\leq|T|\leq1$. Its special fibre
consists of the original affine line and one projective line
meeting at a point. The invertible pulled-back ideal has
canonically trivial generic fibre, and its model weight for
the section $1$ is $\log\max(|T|,|a|)$.}
{当 $0<|a|<1$ 时，
$\operatorname{Spf}O\langle T\rangle$ 上 $(T,a)$ 的
admissible blowup 具有泛纤维域 $|T|\leq|a|$ 及
$|a|\leq|T|\leq1$。其特殊纤维由原仿射直线与一条射影
直线组成，二者交于一点。拉回的理想可逆，泛纤维典范平凡；
相对于截面 $1$ 的模型权函数为 $\log\max(|T|,|a|)$。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The ideal is open since $a$ divides a sufficiently large
power of any fixed topologically nilpotent $\pi$.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:formalblowupcharts} gives the charts
$O\langle U\rangle$, with $T=aU$, and
$O\langle T,V\rangle/(TV-a)$, with $V=a/T$.
Their formal overlap has $U$ and $V$ inverse units. The
first special chart is $\mathbb A^1_k$ with coordinate $u$;
the second is $tv=0$, as in
Lemma~\ref{fnd:standardannuluscanonical}. Gluing the $t=0$ component to the first
chart by $u=v^{-1}$ gives $\mathbb P^1_k$. The $v=0$
component remains the original affine line, meeting it at
$t=v=0$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:affineblowupgenericinvariance}
identifies the two generic domains and their gluing with the
original disc. Proposition~\ref{fnd:formallinebundlegeneric}
constructs the generic line bundle of the invertible ideal, and
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalmetricwell} gives its model metric.
On the first chart the ideal is generated by
$a$, and on the second by $T$. Its generic ideal is the unit
ideal because $a\in K^\times$. Thus $1=a^{-1}a$ has norm
$|a|^{-1}$ on the first chart and $1=T^{-1}T$ has norm
$|T|^{-1}$ on the second. Taking minus the logarithm gives
the stated weight, and the formulas agree on the overlap.}
{该理想为开理想，因为 $a$ 整除固定拓扑幂零元 $\pi$ 的
充分高次幂。Theorem~\ref{fnd:formalblowupcharts} 给出
坐标片 $O\langle U\rangle$（$T=aU$）及
$O\langle T,V\rangle/(TV-a)$（$V=a/T$）。形式交叠中
$U,V$ 互为逆单位元。第一特殊图为以 $u$ 为坐标的
$\mathbb A^1_k$；由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:standardannuluscanonical}，
第二特殊图为 $tv=0$。将 $t=0$ 分量
通过 $u=v^{-1}$ 与第一图粘合，得到 $\mathbb P^1_k$；
$v=0$ 分量保留为原仿射直线，在 $t=v=0$ 处与之相交。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affineblowupgenericinvariance} 将两片
泛纤维域及其粘合识别为原圆盘。
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formallinebundlegeneric} 构造该可逆理想
的泛纤维线丛，Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalmetricwell} 给出
其模型度量。理想在第一图由 $a$ 生成，
在第二图由 $T$ 生成。由于 $a\in K^\times$，其泛纤维
理想为单位理想。因此 $1=a^{-1}a$ 在第一图的范数为
$|a|^{-1}$，$1=T^{-1}T$ 在第二图的范数为 $|T|^{-1}$。
取负对数即得到所列权函数，且两个公式在交叠处一致。}
\end{proof}

Standing context 2 · exact TeX
\bi{Let $\mathfrak X$ be an admissible formal model of $X$ and let $\mathfrak L$ be an invertible sheaf on it. Its generic fibre is an analytic line bundle $L$. On a formal open chart where $\mathfrak L$ has a frame $e$, prescribe $\|e(x)\|=1$ on the associated generic domain. For a local section $s=fe$, set $\|s(x)\|=|f(x)|$.}
{设 $\mathfrak X$ 为 $X$ 的 admissible formal model，$\mathfrak L$ 为其上的 invertible sheaf。其 generic fibre 是 analytic line bundle $L$。在 $\mathfrak L$ 有 frame $e$ 的 formal open chart 上，于相应 generic domain 规定 $\|e(x)\|=1$。对 local section $s=fe$，令 $\|s(x)\|=|f(x)|$。}
