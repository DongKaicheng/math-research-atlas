Flatness detected on source domains

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 427

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Flatness detected on source domains}{由源解析域检测平坦性}]
\label{fnd:sourcedomainflatnessdetection}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good spaces, let $\mathcal F$ be coherent on $Y_G$, and let $U\subset Y$ be a good analytic domain containing $y$. Then $\mathcal F$ is naively $X$-flat at $y$ if and only if its restriction to $U$ is. The same equivalence holds for analytic $X$-flatness.}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 为 good 空间态射，$\mathcal F$ 是 $Y_G$ 上的凝聚层，且 $U\subset Y$ 为包含 $y$ 的 good 解析域。则 $\mathcal F$ 在 $y$ 处朴素 $X$-平坦，当且仅当其在 $U$ 上的限制如此。对于解析 $X$-平坦性，同样的等价成立。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $A=\mathcal O_{X,f(y)}$, $R=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$, $S=\mathcal O_{U,y}$, and $M=\mathcal F_y$. By the preceding results $R\to S$ is faithfully flat and the restricted module is $M\otimes_RS$. If $M$ is $A$-flat, tensoring any injective map of $A$-modules first with $M$ and then with the flat $R$-module $S$ preserves injectivity; hence $M\otimes_RS$ is $A$-flat. Conversely, for an injective $A$-linear map $N_1\to N_2$, the kernel of $N_1\otimes_AM\to N_2\otimes_AM$ is an $R$-module. Its tensor product with $S$ vanishes if $M\otimes_RS$ is $A$-flat, and faithfulness makes the kernel zero. This proves naive-flatness equivalence.}
{令 $A=\mathcal O_{X,f(y)}$、$R=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$、$S=\mathcal O_{U,y}$，并记 $M=\mathcal F_y$。由前述结果，$R\to S$ 忠实平坦，限制后的模为 $M\otimes_RS$。若 $M$ 在 $A$ 上平坦，则任意 $A$-模单射先与 $M$ 张量，再与平坦 $R$-模 $S$ 张量，均保持单射，所以 $M\otimes_RS$ 在 $A$ 上平坦。反之，对 $A$-线性单射 $N_1\to N_2$，映射 $N_1\otimes_AM\to N_2\otimes_AM$ 的核是 $R$-模。若 $M\otimes_RS$ 在 $A$ 上平坦，则该核与 $S$ 的张量积为零，忠实性遂使该核为零。这证明朴素平坦性的等价。}

\bi{A fibre product of good spaces is good: at a product point choose affinoid neighborhoods of its three images, with the two source neighborhoods mapping into the target neighborhood. Their affinoid product contains the intersection of the inverse images of the two ordinary source interiors, hence is a neighborhood of the product point. After any good base change over a complete extension, the inverse image of $U$ is therefore a good analytic domain in a good source, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:alldomainanalyticflatness}. Coherent pullback commutes with domain restriction, as follows from its local finite-module presentations. Apply the naive equivalence at every point above $y$. These are exactly the tests defining analytic flatness.}
{Good 空间的纤维积仍 good：在乘积点处，对其三个像选择 affinoid 邻域，使两个源邻域都映入目标邻域。它们的 affinoid 乘积包含两个源普通内部的逆像之交，因而是乘积点的邻域。因此在完备扩域上作任意 good 基变换后，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:alldomainanalyticflatness}，$U$ 的逆像是 good 源中的 good 解析域。凝聚拉回与域限制交换，这可由局部有限模表示直接检查。在每个映到 $y$ 的点处应用朴素平坦性的等价；这些正是解析平坦性的定义测试。}
\end{proof}
