Separable extensions and dense coefficient fields

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 448

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Separable extensions and dense coefficient fields}{可分扩张与稠密系数域}]
\label{fnd:separablecoefficientapproximation}
\bi{Let $F$ be a complete valued field and $k_0\subset F$ a dense subfield. Every finite separable extension $E/F$ has a presentation $E\simeq F[T]/(Q)$ with $Q\in k_0[T]$ monic and separable.}
{设 $F$ 为完备赋值域，$k_0\subset F$ 为稠密子域。每个有限可分扩张 $E/F$ 都有表示 $E\simeq F[T]/(Q)$，其中 $Q\in k_0[T]$ 为首一可分多项式。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{By \cite[Tag 030N]{StacksAlg}, choose $\alpha$ with $E=F(\alpha)$, and let $P\in F[T]$ be its monic minimal polynomial of degree $d$. The powers $1,\alpha,\ldots,\alpha^{d-1}$ form an $F$-basis. By Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}, its coordinate maximum norm is equivalent to the absolute value of $E$. Hence, if $\beta$ is sufficiently close to $\alpha$, the coordinate matrix of $1,\beta,\ldots,\beta^{d-1}$ relative to this basis has the form $I+M$ with $\|M\|<1$. The convergent matrix series $\sum_{j\geq0}(-M)^j$ makes it invertible, so these powers are also a basis.}
{由 \cite[Tag 030N]{StacksAlg}，可选 $\alpha$ 使 $E=F(\alpha)$，并记其首一最小多项式为 $P\in F[T]$，次数为 $d$。幂 $1,\alpha,\ldots,\alpha^{d-1}$ 是一组 $F$-基。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}，相应坐标最大范数与 $E$ 的绝对值等价。因此，当 $\beta$ 充分接近 $\alpha$ 时，$1,\beta,\ldots,\beta^{d-1}$ 关于此基的坐标矩阵为 $I+M$，其中 $\|M\|<1$。收敛矩阵级数 $\sum_{j\geq0}(-M)^j$ 给出其逆，所以这些幂也是一组基。}

\bi{Approximate the coefficients of $P$ by elements of $k_0$, retaining the leading coefficient $1$, to obtain $Q$. Since $P'(\alpha)\ne0$, sufficiently close approximation makes $Q(\alpha)$ arbitrarily small and keeps $Q'(\alpha)$ nonzero. The contraction argument in the preceding lemma, now in the complete field $E$ and centered at $\alpha$, produces a root $\beta$ of $Q$ as close to $\alpha$ as desired. The basis conclusion gives $F(\beta)=E$. The degree of $Q$ is $d$, so $F[T]/(Q)\to E$ is an isomorphism. In particular $Q$ is irreducible and separable over $F$, and therefore is separable over $k_0$.}
{把 $P$ 的系数用 $k_0$ 中的元素逼近，保持最高次系数为 $1$，得到 $Q$。由于 $P'(\alpha)\ne0$，充分接近的逼近使 $Q(\alpha)$ 任意小且保持 $Q'(\alpha)\ne0$。将上一引理的不动点论证用于完备域 $E$、以 $\alpha$ 为中心，即得 $Q$ 的一个根 $\beta$，并可使它任意接近 $\alpha$。由上述基的结论，$F(\beta)=E$。又因 $Q$ 次数为 $d$，映射 $F[T]/(Q)\to E$ 为同构。因此 $Q$ 在 $F$ 上不可约且可分，进而在 $k_0$ 上可分。}
\end{proof}
