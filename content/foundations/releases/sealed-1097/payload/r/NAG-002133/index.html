Geometric slope formula in characteristic zero

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 716

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Geometric slope formula in characteristic zero}
{特征零下的几何斜率公式}]
\label{cand:charzeroresidualslope}
\bi{Let $K$ be complete, nontrivially valued and of
characteristic zero, and retain its original absolute
value. Let $\mathcal X/O$ be a curve model satisfying
Corollary~\ref{cand:charzerosplitmodelskeleton}.
Let $x$ be the component vertex associated with $C$.
For a closed point $p\in C^\nu$, put}
{设 $K$ 完备、非平凡赋值且特征为零，并保持其原绝对值。
设 $\mathcal X/O$ 为满足
Corollary~\ref{cand:charzerosplitmodelskeleton} 的曲线
模型，$x$ 为与分量 $C$ 关联的顶点。对闭点
$p\in C^\nu$，记}
\[
 e_p=[\kappa(p):k]_{\mathrm{insep}},\qquad
 s_p=[\kappa(p):k]_{\mathrm{sep}}.
\]
\bi{Use the geometric Gauss-ray germ $\gamma_p$ supplied
by that corollary, parameterized by
$\ell=-\log r$ after a finite valued extension making
the residual point rational. If $f$ is an analytic germ
at $x$ with $f(x)\ne0$, choose $c\in K^\times$ such
that $|cf(x)|=1$, and let}
{使用该推论给出的几何 Gauss 射线芽 $\gamma_p$：
在使该剩余点有理的有限赋值扩张上，取参数
$\ell=-\log r$。若 $f$ 是 $x$ 处满足 $f(x)\ne0$
的解析芽，选择 $c\in K^\times$ 使 $|cf(x)|=1$，
并令}
\[
 \varphi=\widetilde{cf}_x
       \in k(C)^\times=k(C^\nu)^\times.
\]
\bi{Then for all sufficiently small $\ell\geq0$,}
{则对全部充分小的 $\ell\geq0$，有}
\[
 -\log|f(\gamma_p(\ell))|
   =-\log|f(x)|+
      e_p\,\operatorname{ord}_p(\varphi)\ell .
\]
\bi{The formula and its parameter are independent of the
chosen finite valued extension. In particular the
geometric outgoing slope is the integer}
{该公式及其参数与所选有限赋值扩张无关。特别地，
几何出射斜率为整数}
\[
                 \sigma_p(f)
                    =e_p\,\operatorname{ord}_p(\varphi).
\]
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Lemma~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield} gives
the normalizing scalar $c$ and the displayed residual
function field. If $p$ lies over a node, it is rational
by Lemma~\ref{cand:normalizedmodelbranches}; hence
$e_p=1$ and the assertion is
Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope} in its actual
pointed node chart.}
{Lemma~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield} 给出
归一化标量 $c$ 及所列剩余函数域。若 $p$ 位于节点
上方，则由 Lemma~\ref{cand:normalizedmodelbranches}
它有理，故 $e_p=1$；所需结论就是其实际带点节点
坐标图中的 Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope}。}

\bi{Otherwise $p$ corresponds to a smooth point $q$
of the special fibre, with $\kappa(p)=\kappa(q)$.
Choose the finite Galois extension $N/K$ in
Lemma~\ref{cand:charzeroresiduepointsplitting}, with
$l=\widetilde N$ containing all the residue embeddings.
By Lemma~\ref{cand:algebraicmodelscalar} the algebraic
model base change has special fibre $(\mathcal X_s)_l$,
and its formal generic fibre is the analytic scalar
extension. Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothnodalnormalizationbasechange}
shows that this special fibre is reduced and that its
normalization is $(\mathcal X_s^\nu)_l$.}
{否则，$p$ 对应特殊纤维的一个光滑点 $q$，且
$\kappa(p)=\kappa(q)$。选择
Lemma~\ref{cand:charzeroresiduepointsplitting} 中的
有限 Galois 扩张 $N/K$，其中 $l=\widetilde N$
包含全部剩余域嵌入像。由
Lemma~\ref{cand:algebraicmodelscalar}，代数模型基变换
的特殊纤维为 $(\mathcal X_s)_l$，其形式泛纤维为
解析标量扩张。由
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothnodalnormalizationbasechange}，
这个特殊纤维约化，且其正规化为
$(\mathcal X_s^\nu)_l$。}

\bi{Choose a rational point $p'$ above $p$, and let
$x'$ be the component endpoint of its rational smooth
tube. It maps to $x$, so $|cf(x')|=1$.
The component-residue identifications commute with
pullback: on integral affine functions both are ordinary
special-fibre reduction, and the fraction-field
identifications extend these maps. Thus the residue of
$cf$ at $x'$ is the pullback of $\varphi$ to the component
through $p'$. Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope}
applied upstairs gives a geometric slope
$\operatorname{ord}_{p'}(\varphi)$. By
Lemma~\ref{cand:inseparableresidualordermultiplicity},
this equals $e_p\operatorname{ord}_p(\varphi)$.}
{选择 $p$ 上方的有理点 $p'$，并令 $x'$ 为其有理
光滑管域的分量端点。它映到 $x$，故 $|cf(x')|=1$。
分量剩余域识别与拉回相容：在整仿射函数上，两者都是
通常特殊纤维约化，而分式域识别延伸这些映射。因此，
$cf$ 在 $x'$ 处的剩余就是 $\varphi$ 到经过 $p'$ 的
分量的拉回。在上方应用
Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope}，几何斜率为
$\operatorname{ord}_{p'}(\varphi)$。由
Lemma~\ref{cand:inseparableresidualordermultiplicity}，
它等于 $e_p\operatorname{ord}_p(\varphi)$。}

\bi{Theorem~\ref{cand:charzerosmoothtube} identifies the
projection of the upstairs Gauss-ray germ with
$\gamma_p$, using the same radius $r$ and the same
extending absolute value. Evaluation of a function
pulled back from $X$ is unchanged by projection.
The upstairs equality therefore gives exactly the
displayed equality on $X$.
The independence of extension and coordinate follows
from the ray-germ comparison in that theorem.
Multiplying $c$ by a norm-one scalar changes $\varphi$
by a nonzero constant and leaves all orders unchanged.}
{Theorem~\ref{cand:charzerosmoothtube} 将上方 Gauss
射线芽的投影识别为 $\gamma_p$，使用同一个半径 $r$
及同一个延拓绝对值。从 $X$ 拉回的函数，其取值在
投影下不变。因此，上方的等式恰好给出 $X$ 上所列
等式。扩张与坐标的选择无关性来自该定理的射线芽
比较。将 $c$ 乘以范数为一的标量，只将 $\varphi$
乘以一个非零常数，不改变所有阶数。}
\end{proof}
