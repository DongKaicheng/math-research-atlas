Strict weighted adjunction and relation quotients

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 167

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Strict weighted adjunction and relation quotients}{Strict 加权添元与关系商}]\label{fnd:strictweightedquotient}
\bi{Let $A$ be a nonzero strict $K$-affinoid algebra with a Banach norm uniformly equivalent to a defining quotient norm. Let $n\geq0$ and choose $\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_n\in|K^\times|$. Put}
{设 $A$ 是非零 strict $K$-affinoid 代数，其 Banach 范数与一个定义商范数一致等价。设 $n\geq0$，选取 $\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_n\in|K^\times|$，记}
\[
 D=A\{\rho^{-1}X\},\qquad
 \left\|\sum_{\alpha\in\mathbb N^n}a_\alpha X^\alpha\right\|_\rho
       =\sup_\alpha\|a_\alpha\|\rho^\alpha.
\]
\bi{Then $D$ is strict $K$-affinoid in the norm-admissible sense. Every algebraic ideal $J\subset D$ is finitely generated and closed. With the quotient norm, $B=D/J$ is a strict affinoid quotient, or the zero quotient under the convention above. If $J=(h_1,\ldots,h_t)$, the map}
{则 $D$ 在范数 admissibility 意义下为 strict $K$-affinoid。每个代数理想 $J\subset D$ 均有限生成且闭。配备商范数后，$B=D/J$ 是 strict affinoid 商，或按上述约定解释的零商。若 $J=(h_1,\ldots,h_t)$，则映射}
\[
 D^t\longrightarrow D,\qquad (d_j)\longmapsto\sum_jh_jd_j
\]
\bi{has actual image $J$ and is norm-strict onto that image. No unit-ideal hypothesis on other elements of $A$ is needed for this assertion.}
{的实际像为 $J$，且对该像为 norm-strict。这个结论不需要对 $A$ 中其他元素另加生成单位理想的假设。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose a bounded admissible surjection $p:R_d=K\langle Z_1,\ldots,Z_d\rangle\to A$. There are constants $C_p,L_p>0$ such that $\|p(z)\|\leq C_p\|z\|$ and every $a\in A$ has a lift of norm at most $L_p\|a\|$, choosing zero for $a=0$. Applying the coefficient lifting argument of Proposition~\ref{fnd:weightedc0exact}, with index set $\mathbb N^n$ and unit weights, gives a surjection}
{选取 bounded admissible 满射 $p:R_d=K\langle Z_1,\ldots,Z_d\rangle\to A$。存在常数 $C_p,L_p>0$，使 $\|p(z)\|\leq C_p\|z\|$，且每个 $a\in A$ 都有范数不超过 $L_p\|a\|$ 的提升；$a=0$ 时取零提升。对指标集 $\mathbb N^n$ 和单位权重应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:weightedc0exact} 的逐系数提升论证，得到满射}
\[
 R_d\langle Y_1,\ldots,Y_n\rangle
          \longrightarrow A\langle Y_1,\ldots,Y_n\rangle
\]
\bi{with the same upper bound and lifting bound. This is an algebra homomorphism: the assertion holds on polynomial coefficient families and passes to their limits by Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientconvolution}. Regrouping the joint coefficients identifies its source isometrically with $R_{d+n}$. Indeed, a joint family tends to zero outside finite subsets exactly when each row does so and the row norms tend to zero; finite unions of the finitely many relevant rows prove the reverse implication.}
{其 upper bound 和 lifting bound 与原来相同。该映射是代数同态：先对有限多项式系数族成立，再由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientconvolution} 延拓到极限。对联合系数重新分组，将源代数与 $R_{d+n}$ 等距识别。具体而言，联合系数族在有限集之外趋于零，等价于每一行具有这一性质且行范数趋于零；在反方向上，对有限个有关行中的有限集合取并即可。}

\bi{Choose $c_i\in K^\times$ with $|c_i|=\rho_i$. The substitution $X_i=c_iY_i$ is an isometric algebra isomorphism from $D$ to $A\langle Y_1,\ldots,Y_n\rangle$: it sends $a_\alpha$ to $c^\alpha a_\alpha$, preserving both the weighted norm and the finite-tail condition. Thus the displayed surjection supplies a bounded admissible unit-radius Tate presentation of $D$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}, now applied to $D$, proves finite generation and closedness of $J$, as well as norm-strictness of the displayed relation map.}
{选取 $c_i\in K^\times$ 使 $|c_i|=\rho_i$。代换 $X_i=c_iY_i$ 给出从 $D$ 到 $A\langle Y_1,\ldots,Y_n\rangle$ 的等距代数同构：它将系数 $a_\alpha$ 送到 $c^\alpha a_\alpha$，同时保持加权范数与有限尾条件。因此，上述满射为 $D$ 提供了 bounded admissible 的单位半径 Tate 表示。对 $D$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}，得到 $J$ 的有限生成性、闭性，以及所列关系映射的 norm-strictness。}

\bi{Lemma~\ref{fnd:completequotient} makes $B$ complete with a definite quotient norm. Approximating lifts in $D$ shows that this norm is submultiplicative. If $B\ne0$, its unit has norm at most one; writing $u=\|1_B\|>0$, submultiplicativity gives $u\leq u^2$, hence $u=1$. The quotient map has lifts bounded by any fixed factor $\lambda>1$. Composing these lifts with the preceding Tate-presentation lifts gives a bounded admissible surjection from $R_{d+n}$ to $B$. This proves strictness when $B\ne0$; when $J=D$, all quotient assertions reduce to the zero space.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:completequotient}，$B$ 的商范数为正定且完备。逼近 $D$ 中的提升，得到这个范数的次乘性。若 $B\ne0$，其单位元范数不超过一；令 $u=\|1_B\|>0$，由次乘性有 $u\leq u^2$，故 $u=1$。商映射具有由任意固定因子 $\lambda>1$ 控制的提升。将这些提升与前面 Tate 表示的提升复合，得到从 $R_{d+n}$ 到 $B$ 的 bounded admissible 满射。因此 $B\ne0$ 时它是 strict；当 $J=D$ 时，所有商的结论都化为零空间的陈述。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{The coefficient construction is useful for localization only after its relation quotients are controlled. In this section the new radii lie in the value group. This allows the finite-module theorem over strict affinoids to control the actual images of the relation maps. No statement about a structure sheaf is used.}
{系数构造要用于局部化，还须控制加入关系后的商。本节新增变量的半径均位于值群中，因此可以利用 strict affinoid 上的有限模定理控制关系映射的实际像。这里不使用结构层的任何结论。}

\bi{Fix a complete real-valued non-Archimedean field $K$, allowing the trivial absolute value. A nonzero algebra below carries the submultiplicative norm and norm-admissibility convention of Definition~\ref{fnd:banachalgebra}. We also allow a relation ideal to be the whole algebra: its quotient is the zero ring with its zero norm, and every unital module over it is zero. Statements about such a quotient have this explicit degenerate interpretation; the normalization $\|1\|=1$ is required only for nonzero algebras.}
{固定一个关于实值非阿基米德绝对值完备的域 $K$，允许平凡绝对值。下文的非零代数采用 Definition~\ref{fnd:banachalgebra} 中的次乘范数与范数 admissibility 约定。也允许关系理想为整个代数：相应商是配备零范数的零环，其所有幺模均为零。涉及这种商的陈述均按此明确的退化意义理解；$\|1\|=1$ 的归一化只要求于非零代数。}
