Finite rational Tate acyclicity

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 264

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Finite rational Tate acyclicity}{有限 rational Tate 无环性}]
\label{fnd:generalizedfiniterationalcech}
\bi{Let $A$ be any norm-admissible $K$-affinoid algebra and let $M$ be any finite $A$-module with a canonical norm. Let $(V_i)_{i=1}^n$ be any finite covering of $\mathcal M(A)$ by rational domains with arbitrary positive radii. For each finite intersection, let $A_{i_0\cdots i_q}$ be its actual joint rational algebra, with any admissible norm. Then the augmented complex}
{设 $A$ 为任意 norm-admissible $K$-affinoid 代数，$M$ 为任意配备典范范数的有限 $A$-模。设 $(V_i)_{i=1}^n$ 是 $\mathcal M(A)$ 的任意有限 rational 域覆盖，其半径可以为任意正实数。对每个有限交叠，记 $A_{i_0\cdots i_q}$ 为其实际联合 rational 代数，并配备任意 admissible 范数。则增广复形}
\[
 0\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow
 \prod_i A_i\otimes_A M\longrightarrow
 \prod_{i_0<i_1}A_{i_0i_1}\otimes_A M\longrightarrow\cdots
\]
\bi{is norm-strict exact in every degree. The all-tuples complex, with repeated indices and the usual alternating differential, has the same conclusion. Each finite-module ordinary tensor agrees boundedly with its separated completed tensor. For every fixed degree, cycles have primitives with a uniform norm bound depending on the finite cover, $M$, that degree and the chosen norms. No bound uniform over all covers or all degrees is asserted.}
{在每个次数均为 norm-strict 正合。允许重复指标、采用通常交错微分的全部指标复形具有同样结论。每个有限模的普通张量积都通过有界同构与分离完备张量积相同。在每个固定次数，循环均有受统一范数界控制的原像；该界可以依赖于有限覆盖、$M$、该次数及所选范数，不要求对所有覆盖或所有次数一致。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{\textbf{Step 1: one strictification for the whole finite diagram.} Choose rational presentations for the $V_i$. Take a free extension $L=K_r$ as in Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification}, using the finite set consisting of the presentation radii of $A$ and all radii in these rational presentations. Thus $A_r$ is strict and all these rational radii belong to $\sqrt{|L^\times|}$. All products of them that arise in finite joint intersections have the same property. Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedquotientbasechange} identifies every extended intersection algebra with its actual joint presentation over $A_r$.}
{\textbf{步骤 1：对整个有限图表同时严格化。} 为各 $V_i$ 选择 rational 表示。按照 Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification}，对由 $A$ 的表示半径及全部 rational 表示半径组成的有限集合，选择自由扩域 $L=K_r$。于是 $A_r$ 为 strict，且全部这些 rational 半径属于 $\sqrt{|L^\times|}$。有限联合交叠中出现的半径乘积也具有同样性质。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedquotientbasechange}，每个扩张后的交叠代数都与 $A_r$ 上的实际联合表示识别。}

\bi{Every $L$-spectral point of $A_r$ restricts to a $K$-spectral point of $A$. The original covering therefore implies that the extended rational subsets cover $\mathcal M_L(A_r)$. This only uses restriction of points. By Proposition~\ref{fnd:radicalrationalpresentation}, each extended rational algebra has a unit-radius rational presentation over $A_r$, with bounded inverse comparison maps. This step uses powers of the inequalities: belonging to the divisible closure of the value group does not permit direct rescaling of the original variable. The comparison maps on all intersections commute with restrictions by the uniqueness of rational factorization.}
{$A_r$ 的每个 $L$-谱点都限制为 $A$ 的 $K$-谱点，因此原覆盖保证扩张后的 rational 子集覆盖 $\mathcal M_L(A_r)$。这里仅使用谱点的限制。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:radicalrationalpresentation}，每个扩张后的 rational 代数都具有 $A_r$ 上的单位半径 rational 表示，并由有界互逆映射比较。这一步使用不等式的幂：半径属于值群的可除闭包，并不允许直接缩放原变量。由 rational 分解的唯一性，全部交叠的比较映射与限制映射相容。}

\bi{The module $M_r$ is finite canonical over $A_r$ by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedcoefficienttensor}. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfiniterationalcech} now gives norm-strict exactness of the strict rational covering complex over $L$, in both versions. This uses no generalized Gerritzen--Grauert theorem.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedcoefficienttensor}，$M_r$ 是 $A_r$ 上的有限 canonical 模。此时 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfiniterationalcech} 给出 $L$ 上 strict rational 覆盖复形的 norm-strict 正合性，并同时适用于两种复形。这里没有使用一般的 Gerritzen--Grauert 定理。}

\bi{\textbf{Step 2: identify the extended complex, including its arrows.} Write $C^{-1}=M$ and let $C^q$ be the displayed finite product in degree $q\geq0$, with maximum norm. Each factor is a Banach space by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor}. Coefficient families commute isometrically with finite products: a supremum commutes with a finite maximum, and the finite-tail condition is preserved by finite unions. Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedquotientbasechange} consequently identifies $(C^q)_r$, by bounded inverse maps, with the corresponding term of the upper complex. These maps identify restrictions and augmentation, not just their modules. Thus the extended differential is exactly the coefficientwise extension of $d^q:C^q\to C^{q+1}$, including each alternating sign. Each fixed degree has only finitely many factors, so the comparison constants can be absorbed into norm-strictness.}
{\textbf{步骤 2：识别扩张后的复形及其箭头。} 记 $C^{-1}=M$，并以 $C^q$ 表示 $q\geq0$ 次的所列有限直积，采用最大范数。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor}，每个因子均为 Banach 空间。系数族与有限直积等距交换：上确界与有限最大值交换，而有限尾条件由有限并保持。因此，Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedquotientbasechange} 通过有界互逆映射，将 $(C^q)_r$ 与上层复形相应的项识别。这些映射同时识别限制及增广，而不只是识别其模。因此扩张后的微分恰好是 $d^q:C^q\to C^{q+1}$ 的逐系数扩张，包括每个交错符号。在每个固定次数只有有限个因子，所以范数比较常数可吸收到 norm-strictness 的常数中。}

\bi{\textbf{Step 3: descend cycles and their norm bounds.} If $z\in C^q$ is a cycle with $q\geq0$, its constant embedding is an upper cycle. The strict exactness there supplies $w\in(C^{q-1})_r$ satisfying $d_r w=\iota(z)$ and $\|w\|\leq L_q\|z\|$ for a fixed $L_q$. Apply the zeroth-coefficient map of Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients}. Its naturality for bounded $K$-linear maps gives}
{\textbf{步骤 3：下降循环及其范数界。} 若 $z\in C^q$ 是 $q\geq0$ 次的循环，则其常系数嵌入是上层循环。上层的严格正合性给出 $w\in(C^{q-1})_r$，满足 $d_rw=\iota(z)$，且对某个固定 $L_q$ 有 $\|w\|\leq L_q\|z\|$。应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients} 的零次系数映射；由其对有界 $K$-线性映射的自然性，得到}
\[
 d(p_0w)=z,\qquad \|p_0w\|\leq L_q\|z\|.
\]
\bi{Thus exactness and actual bounded lifts descend without presupposing that a lower differential has closed image. Its image is now the kernel of the next bounded differential and hence is closed. At the augmentation, upper injectivity and the isometric constant embedding give injectivity of $M\to C^0$; applying its upper inverse bound to constant families gives the lower inverse bound. This proves norm-strict exactness in every degree. The same argument works degree by degree for all tuples, since each degree still has a finite product. If the cover is empty, its covering condition forces $\mathcal M(A)=\varnothing$, hence $A=M=0$ by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion}. All assertions are then immediate.}
{所以正合性及实际的有界提升一起下降，并未预先假设下层微分的像闭。现在它的像等于下一个有界微分的核，因而闭。在增广处，上层单射性及等距的常系数嵌入给出 $M\to C^0$ 的单射性；再将上层逆映射界用于常系数族，即得下层逆映射界。这证明所有次数的 norm-strict 正合性。对全部指标复形，同一论证逐次数成立，因为每个次数仍然只涉及有限直积。若覆盖族为空，其覆盖条件迫使 $\mathcal M(A)=\varnothing$；由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion}，有 $A=M=0$，所有断言直接成立。}
\end{proof}
