General finite base change

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 281

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{General finite base change}{一般有限基变换}]
\label{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange}
\bi{Let $A\to B$ be a bounded map of generalized $K$-affinoid algebras, where $B$ is finite over $A$ and its given norm is equivalent to its canonical finite-$A$-module norm. For every bounded map $A\to C$ to an affinoid over a complete extension $L/K$, the natural map}
{设 $A\to B$ 为广义 $K$-affinoid 代数的有界映射，其中 $B$ 在 $A$ 上有限，且给定范数与典范有限 $A$-模范数等价。对每个到完备扩域 $L/K$ 上 affinoid 的有界映射 $A\to C$，自然映射}
\[
 B\otimes_A C\longrightarrow B\widehat\otimes_A C
\]
\bi{is a bounded algebra isomorphism with bounded inverse when the source has its canonical finite-$C$-module norm. The result is finite and norm-admissible over $C$.}
{在源空间配备典范有限 $C$-模范数时，为有界代数同构且逆有界。所得代数在 $C$ 上有限且 norm-admissible。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Apply Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor} to the finite canonical $A$-module $B$ and the target $C$. It identifies the projective tensor norm with the complete canonical finite-$C$-module norm up to uniform equivalence. The separated completion consequently changes no elements. Multiplication agrees on the algebraic tensor and its completion by continuity. Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout} gives the affinoid structure, and the norm comparison just obtained gives finite admissibility.}
{对有限典范 $A$-模 $B$ 及目标 $C$ 应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor}。它在一致等价意义下，将 projective 张量范数与完备的典范有限 $C$-模范数识别。因此分离完备化不增加元素。由连续性，代数张量积及其完备化上的乘法一致。Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout} 给出 affinoid 结构，而刚得到的范数比较给出有限 admissibility。}
\end{proof}
