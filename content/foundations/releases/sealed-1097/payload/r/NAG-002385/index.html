Analytic cycles on whole interior weight domains

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1012

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Analytic cycles on whole interior weight domains]
\label{v037:analytic:interiorcycle}
Let $Y=\mathcal M(A)$ be a generalized affinoid space, pure
of analytic dimension $d$, over any complete non-Archimedean
field with its specified absolute value. Let $f_Y$ be a finite
tuple of analytic units, let $t_Y:Y\to\mathbb R^{n'}$ be its
moment map, and put
\[
                  U_Y=\mathbb R^{n'}\setminus t_Y(\partial_KY).
\]
There is a unique local positive integral balanced $d$-cycle
\[
                         \operatorname{Trop}_{f_Y}(Y)|_{U_Y}
\]
whose support is $t_Y(Y)\cap U_Y$ and whose weights at flat
$d$-dimensional germs are the actual Gauss-degree weights.
For every finite $A$-module $M$ there is a local nonnegative
integral balanced cycle $\operatorname{Trop}_{f_Y}(M)|_{U_Y}$
with the module weights of
Theorem~\ref{v037:degree:moduleweight}. It is additive in short
exact sequences and is unchanged by common subdivision.
The structure module gives the preceding positive cycle.

Let $p:X=\mathcal M(B)\to Y$ be finite, with $X$ pure of
analytic dimension $d$. Let $f_X$ be a unit tuple and let
$a:\mathbb Z^n\to\mathbb Z^{n'}$ satisfy the actual character
identities $p^*f_{Y,j}=\prod_i f_{X,i}^{a_{ji}}$, so that
$t_Y\circ p=a\circ t_X$. The entire source support over $U_Y$
lies in its whole interior weight domain, and
\begin{equation}
\label{v037:eq:interiorcyclepushforward}
 a_*\bigl(\operatorname{Trop}_{f_X}(X)|_{a^{-1}(U_Y)}\bigr)
                   =\operatorname{Trop}_{f_Y}(B)|_{U_Y}.
\end{equation}
In particular, adjoining any finite list of analytic unit
coordinates to a moment tuple and projecting back to that
tuple preserves its local analytic cycle.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The set $U_Y$ is open: the absolute boundary is closed in the
compact affinoid spectrum by
Propositions~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus} and
\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact}. At a point of
$t_Y(Y)\cap U_Y$, Theorem~\ref{v037:analytic:interiorlocalstar}
gives a translated finite pure rational fan presenting the
actual image on a sufficiently small open convex neighbourhood
$W\subset U_Y$. Theorem~\ref{v037:analytic:polyhedralcycle}
gives its positive integral balanced weights. At a point of
$U_Y\setminus t_Y(Y)$ take a neighbourhood disjoint from the
compact image and use the zero cycle.

These local presentations agree on overlaps. Cut along the
finitely many defining hyperplanes of two presentations. On
the relative interior of every resulting $d$-cell meeting the
overlap, the actual image has the same flat germ and saturated
tangent lattice. Both weights are therefore
$m_{Y,f_Y}$ of that same whole fibre. The common refinement
has equal weights, which is the required agreement. This also
proves uniqueness. Theorem~\ref{v037:degree:modulecycle} on
each of these neighbourhoods gives the module cycles.
Their weights agree on overlaps by
Theorem~\ref{v037:degree:moduleweight}; its exact-sequence
formula proves the stated additivity.

For the finite morphism, any point $x$ with
$a(t_X(x))\in U_Y$ maps to an absolute interior point of $Y$.
Finite morphisms are everywhere relatively inner by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras}. Composition in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus} makes $x$ an
absolute interior point of $X$. Thus
\[
                  a^{-1}(U_Y)\cap t_X(\partial_KX)=\varnothing.
\]
Every whole source moment fibre above this set is interior.
Theorem~\ref{v037:analytic:interiorlocalstar} therefore applies
at each of its weights.

Fix $w\in U_Y$. Apply
Lemma~\ref{v037:analytic:finitepolyhedralpatching} to the compact
set $S=t_X(X)$ and its complete inverse image over $w$.
It supplies a finite pure source complex on $a^{-1}(W)$ for
some open convex neighbourhood $W\subset U_Y$ of $w$.
If $w\in t_Y(Y)$, shrink $W$ inside one target fan neighbourhood
already constructed. The two finite complexes then satisfy
exactly the hypotheses of
Theorem~\ref{v037:degree:modulecycle}. Its equality proves
\eqref{v037:eq:interiorcyclepushforward} over $W$.
If the source inverse image over $w$ is empty, the compactness
part of the lemma allows $W$ to miss $a(t_X(X))$; the empty-fibre
argument in that same theorem makes the module cycle zero
there. If $w\notin t_Y(Y)$, both sides are zero on a smaller
neighbourhood disjoint from $t_Y(Y)$.

The map on the actual source support is proper over $U_Y$:
the inverse image of a compact subset of $U_Y$ is closed in
the compact set $t_X(X)$. These finite local presentations
therefore define the lattice-index pushforward, and their
equalities glue to the displayed identity. Finally take
$p=\operatorname{id}_Y$, $B=A$, and the coordinate projection
from the enlarged moment tuple. Its finite module is the
structure module, giving the last assertion.
\end{proof}
