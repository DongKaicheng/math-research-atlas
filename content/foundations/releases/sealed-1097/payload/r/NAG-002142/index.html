The maximum-parameter composition law

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 867

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{The maximum-parameter composition law}
{参数取最大值的复合律}]
\label{cand:standardhomotopysemigroup}
\bi{For all $x\in V$ and $s,t\in[0,1]$,}
{对所有 $x\in V$ 及 $s,t\in[0,1]$，有}
\[
 H(H(x,s),t)=H(x,\max(s,t)).
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{When one parameter is zero the assertion is immediate.
For positive parameters, realize the completed residue field
of $H(x,s)$ inside the completed Gauss rational field used
in its definition. The injection comes from its actual
evaluation homomorphism, after quotienting its kernel,
taking fractions, and completing. Gauss extension preserves
the norm of every coefficient. Iterating the construction
therefore uses independent variables $(S,Z)$ of radius $s$
and $(S',Z')$ of radius $t$, and substitutes}
{若某个参数为零，断言立即成立。对于正参数，将
$H(x,s)$ 的完备剩余域实现为其定义所用完备 Gauss
有理函数域的子域：先由实际取值同态商去核，再取分式
并完备化，即得此等距嵌入。Gauss 扩张保持每个系数的
范数。因此迭代构造使用半径为 $s$ 的独立变量 $(S,Z)$
和半径为 $t$ 的独立变量 $(S',Z')$，并作代换}
\[
 X_i\mapsto\xi_i(1+S_i)(1+S'_i),\qquad
 T_j\mapsto b_j+Z_j+Z'_j.
\]
\bi{The two sets of variables are symmetric, so assume
$s\leq t$. Set}
{两组变量对称，故可设 $s\leq t$。置}
\[
 W_i=S_i+S'_i+S_iS'_i,\qquad V_j=Z_j+Z'_j.
\]
\bi{The rational coordinate change from $(S,Z,S',Z')$ to
$(S,Z,W,V)$ is an isometry of Gauss fields with radii
$(s,s,t,t)$. To see this directly, its inverse formulas are}
{从 $(S,Z,S',Z')$ 到 $(S,Z,W,V)$ 的有理坐标变换，是
半径为 $(s,s,t,t)$ 的 Gauss 域之间的等距映射。直接
证明如下，其逆公式为}
\[
 S'_i=(W_i-S_i)/(1+S_i),\qquad Z'_j=V_j-Z_j.
\]
\bi{The displayed images have their assigned norm bounds,
because $s\leq t$ and $|1+S_i|=1$, including when $s=t=1$.
Hence substitution of an old polynomial has norm at most its
original Gauss norm. Its image is a new polynomial divided
by a product of powers of $1+S_i$, of norm one. Applying
the reverse substitution to that numerator gives the reverse
bound. This proves equality for polynomials and then for
their fractions. The same argument for $V=Z+Z'$ is linear
and has no denominator.}
{由于 $s\leq t$ 且 $|1+S_i|=1$，所列各像均满足指定
范数界，$s=t=1$ 时也成立。因此，原多项式经代换后的
范数至多为其原 Gauss 范数。其像是新多项式除以若干
$(1+S_i)$ 的幂之积，此分母范数为一。对分子作反向
代换即得反向估计。由此得到多项式上的等式，再得到
分式上的等式。对于 $V=Z+Z'$，同样论证为线性的，
且没有分母。}

\bi{After this isometry the iterated substitution is simply
$X_i\mapsto\xi_i(1+W_i)$ and $T_j\mapsto b_j+V_j$.
It does not involve $(S,Z)$; its Gauss norm is the single
displacement norm of radius $t$. This proves the identity
on Laurent polynomials, and uniform approximation proves it
on all of $A$.}
{经过此等距变换后，迭代代换就是
$X_i\mapsto\xi_i(1+W_i)$、$T_j\mapsto b_j+V_j$，
不再使用 $(S,Z)$。其 Gauss 范数就是半径为 $t$ 的
单次位移范数。这在 Laurent 多项式上证明恒等式，
再由一致逼近得到整个 $A$ 上的恒等式。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Let $K$ be complete and nontrivially valued, with its original
absolute value. Fix $0<|a|<1$ and integers $r,q\geq0$. Put}
{设 $K$ 关于原绝对值完备且非平凡赋值。固定
$0<|a|<1$ 及整数 $r,q\geq0$，置}
\[
 A=K\langle X_0,\ldots,X_r,T_1,\ldots,T_q\rangle
                /(X_0\cdots X_r-a),\qquad V=\mathcal M(A).
\]
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:standardmonomialchartgeometry} gives
the continuous Gauss section $s:\Delta_a\to V$, where}
{Proposition~\ref{fnd:standardmonomialchartgeometry} 给出
连续 Gauss 截面 $s:\Delta_a\to V$，其中}
\[
 \Delta_a=\{(w_0,\ldots,w_r)\in\mathbb R_{\geq0}^{r+1}:
                         \sum_iw_i=v(a)\}.
\]
\bi{After eliminating $X_0=a/(X_1\cdots X_r)$, $s(w)$ is the
Gauss point of radii $e^{-w_i}$ in $X_i$, $1\leq i\leq r$,
and radius $1$ in every $T_j$. Let $\Sigma_a=s(\Delta_a)$ and
write $\operatorname{val}:V\to\Delta_a$ for the values of
$X_0,\ldots,X_r$.}
{消去 $X_0=a/(X_1\cdots X_r)$ 后，$s(w)$ 是在
$X_i$、$1\leq i\leq r$ 上半径为 $e^{-w_i}$、在每个
$T_j$ 上半径为 $1$ 的 Gauss 点。令
$\Sigma_a=s(\Delta_a)$，并以
$\operatorname{val}:V\to\Delta_a$ 记
$X_0,\ldots,X_r$ 的赋值映射。}
