\begin{corollary}[\bi{Normality and regularity along quasi-smooth maps}{准光滑映射上的正规性与正则性}]
\label{fnd:quasismoothnormalascent}
\bi{Under the hypotheses of the preceding theorem, $\mathcal O_{Y,y}$ is normal if and only if $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is normal, and it is regular if and only if $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is regular. In particular a quasi-smooth good space over a normal good analytic space is normal. These assertions hold after every analytic base change and complete extension for which the spaces are good.}
{在前一定理的假设下，$\mathcal O_{Y,y}$ 正规当且仅当 $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 正规；它正则当且仅当 $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 正则。特别地，正规 good 解析空间上的准光滑 good 空间正规。只要所涉空间 good，这些断言在每个解析基变换及完备扩张后都成立。}
\end{corollary}
