Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A common modification of isomorphic generic fibres}
{泛纤维同构的共同修改}]
\label{fnd:commonformalmodification}
\bi{Let $\mathfrak X,\mathfrak Y$ be quasi-paracompact
admissible formal schemes with a specified generic-fibre
isomorphism. There is a quasi-paracompact admissible formal
scheme $\mathfrak Z$ and morphisms to both, each a finite
composition of admissible blowups, inducing that isomorphism.}
{设 quasi-paracompact admissible 形式概形
$\mathfrak X,\mathfrak Y$ 具有指定的泛纤维同构。
存在 quasi-paracompact admissible 形式概形 $\mathfrak Z$
及到二者的态射，它们均为有限次 admissible blowup 的复合，
并诱导指定的同构。}
\end{lemma}
