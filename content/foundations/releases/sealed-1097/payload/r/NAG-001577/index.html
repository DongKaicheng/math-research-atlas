Bounded and geometric rational factorization

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Bounded and geometric rational factorization}{有界与几何的 rational 分解}]
\label{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal}
\bi{Keep the rational presentation $A\to B$ and subset $V$ above. For every bounded unital $K$-algebra homomorphism $\phi:A\to C$ to a Banach $K$-algebra, a bounded $K$-algebra factorization $A\to B\to C$ exists if and only if $\phi(g)$ is a unit and, for each $i$, there is $C_i\geq1$ with}
{沿用上述 rational 表示 $A\to B$ 及子集 $V$。对映入 Banach $K$-代数的每个有界保单位 $K$-代数同态 $\phi:A\to C$，存在有界 $K$-代数分解 $A\to B\to C$，当且仅当 $\phi(g)$ 为单位，且对每个 $i$ 都存在 $C_i\geq1$，使}
\[
 \|\bigl(\phi(f_i)\phi(g)^{-1}\bigr)^m\|\leq C_i\rho_i^m
 \qquad(m\geq0).
\]
\bi{The factorization is unique. If $C$ is a norm-admissible affinoid over any specified complete extension $L/K$, this condition is equivalent to the image of $\mathcal M_L(C)\to\mathcal M_K(A)$ being contained in $V$. In particular the rational presentation has the domain universal property for all such complete-extension affinoid tests, not only for strict tests over $K$. Zero targets are allowed.}
{此分解唯一。若 $C$ 是某个指定完备扩域 $L/K$ 上的 norm-admissible affinoid，则这一条件等价于 $\mathcal M_L(C)\to\mathcal M_K(A)$ 的像包含于 $V$。特别地，该 rational 表示对于所有这样的完备扩域 affinoid 测试都具有域泛性质，而不只是对于 $K$ 上的 strict 测试如此。允许零目标。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{In $B$, the preceding unit identity makes $g$ invertible and forces any factorization to send $\bar T_i$ to $b_i=\phi(f_i)\phi(g)^{-1}$. Since $\|\bar T_i^m\|\leq\rho_i^m$, boundedness of the factorization gives the required estimates. Conversely, Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundedsubstitution} gives a bounded evaluation $A\{\rho^{-1}T\}\to C$ at the $b_i$. It kills the actual ideal generated by $gT_i-f_i$. If its bound is $L_0$, taking the infimum over lifts of $b\in B$ gives $\|\psi(b)\|\leq L_0\|b\|$. Thus it descends boundedly. Its values on the dense polynomial image are forced, proving uniqueness. This proof uses the explicit power estimates for an arbitrary Banach target.}
{在 $B$ 中，前面的单位恒等式使 $g$ 可逆，并迫使每个分解将 $\bar T_i$ 送到 $b_i=\phi(f_i)\phi(g)^{-1}$。由于 $\|\bar T_i^m\|\leq\rho_i^m$，分解的有界性给出所需估计。反之，由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundedsubstitution}，在 $b_i$ 处取值得到有界映射 $A\{\rho^{-1}T\}\to C$。它零化由 $gT_i-f_i$ 生成的实际理想。若其有界常数为 $L_0$，对 $b\in B$ 的全部提升取下确界，便得 $\|\psi(b)\|\leq L_0\|b\|$，所以该映射有界地下降。在稠密多项式像上的取值已被唯一确定，故分解唯一。对任意 Banach 目标，此证明使用的是明确的幂估计。}

\bi{Now let $C$ be affinoid over $L$. If a factorization exists, restricting its spectral points and using Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints} gives the image condition. Conversely, at every $y\in\mathcal M_L(C)$ the inequalities and the image of the unit-ideal identity imply $|\phi(g)(y)|>0$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion}, over $L$, makes $\phi(g)$ a unit. Its ratios have $|b_i(y)|\leq\rho_i$ at every such point. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointwisepowerbound}, again over $L$ and with the actual radii $\rho_i$, gives the required scaled power estimates. The Banach factorization just proved applies because $C$ is also a Banach $K$-algebra. The zero target has its unique factorization directly; the equivalence of spectral emptiness and zero prevents an exceptional nonzero target with vacuous hypotheses.}
{现在设 $C$ 为 $L$ 上的 affinoid。若分解存在，限制其谱点并使用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints}，得到像的条件。反之，对每个 $y\in\mathcal M_L(C)$，由不等式及单位理想恒等式的像，得到 $|\phi(g)(y)|>0$。在 $L$ 上使用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion}，可知 $\phi(g)$ 为单位。相应比值在所有这样的点都满足 $|b_i(y)|\leq\rho_i$。再次在 $L$ 上使用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointwisepowerbound}，并取实际半径 $\rho_i$，得到所需的缩放幂估计。由于 $C$ 也是 Banach $K$-代数，刚证明的 Banach 分解适用。零目标直接具有唯一分解；谱空当且仅当代数为零，排除了非零目标因没有谱点而使条件空真的例外。}
\end{proof}
