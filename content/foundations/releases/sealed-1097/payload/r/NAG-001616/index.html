Arbitrary complete ground-field extension

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 391

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Arbitrary complete ground-field extension}{任意完备底域扩张}]
\label{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension}
\bi{Let $L/K$ be any complete valued extension, with its specified isometric embedding. There is a scalar-extension functor $X\mapsto X_L$ on analytic spaces, characterized locally by}
{设 $L/K$ 为任意完备赋值扩域，并固定其等距嵌入。解析空间上存在底域扩张函子 $X\mapsto X_L$，它局部由下式刻画：}
\[
 \mathcal M(A)_L=\mathcal M(A\widehat\otimes_KL).
\]
\bi{The canonical continuous map $\pi:X_L\to X$ is surjective. For every analytic domain $Y\subseteq X$, its inverse image is canonically $Y_L$. Scalar extension commutes with fibre products and with successive complete field extensions.}
{典范连续映射 $\pi:X_L\to X$ 为满射。对每个解析域 $Y\subseteq X$，其逆像典范等于 $Y_L$。底域扩张与纤维积及连续的完备扩域相容。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{We first make the local mapping characterization explicit. A bounded map over $K$ from an $L$-analytic space to a $K$-analytic space is defined by the chart condition in Definition~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphisms}, using bounded $K$-homomorphisms from the target $K$-affinoid algebra to the source $L$-affinoid algebra. The proof of Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing} applies to these maps as well: all pullbacks of chart domains use Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainextensiontests} and Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange}. For an affinoid target, the bounded-pushout property gives}
{先明确局部映射刻画。从 $L$-解析空间到 $K$-解析空间的 $K$ 上有界映射，按 Definition~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphisms} 的 chart 条件定义，但使用从目标 $K$-affinoid 代数到源 $L$-affinoid 代数的有界 $K$-同态。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing} 的证明同样适用：所有 chart 域的拉回都由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainextensiontests} 及 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange} 给出。对 affinoid 目标，有界推出的泛性质给出}
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_L(T,\mathcal M(A\widehat\otimes_KL))
 \simeq \operatorname{Hom}_{K,\mathrm{loc}}(T,\mathcal M(A)),
\]
\bi{first on affinoid charts of $T$ and then by gluing.}
{先在 $T$ 的 affinoid chart 上成立，再由粘合得到一般情形。}

\bi{If $X$ is compact Hausdorff, take a finite affinoid cover $(U_i)$. Each intersection $U_i\cap U_j$ is a compact analytic domain and has a finite affinoid cover. Its inverse image in $(U_i)_L$ is therefore a finite union of the corresponding scalar-extended affinoid domains, hence a compact closed analytic domain. The analogous inverse image in $(U_j)_L$ is canonically isomorphic to it: identify the scalar extensions of common affinoid pieces, and use the completed base-change identity on their intersections. These isomorphisms satisfy the cocycle condition by local uniqueness. Finite closed gluing produces $X_L$. The displayed local mapping characterization glues to}
{若 $X$ 紧且 Hausdorff，选取有限 affinoid 覆盖 $(U_i)$。每个交叠 $U_i\cap U_j$ 是紧解析域，具有有限 affinoid 覆盖。它在 $(U_i)_L$ 中的逆像是相应扩域后 affinoid 域的有限并，故为紧闭解析域。它与 $(U_j)_L$ 中的对应逆像典范同构：在共同 affinoid 块上识别其底域扩张，再在交叠上使用完备基变换恒等式。局部唯一性保证这些同构满足 cocycle 条件。有限闭粘合得到 $X_L$。上述局部映射刻画粘合为}
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_L(T,X_L)
 \simeq\operatorname{Hom}_{K,\mathrm{loc}}(T,X).
\]

\bi{For general $X$, cover it by open domains $O_i$ contained in compact Hausdorff analytic domains $D_i$. Construct $(D_i)_L$ and restrict to the open inverse image of $O_i$. On the inverse images of overlaps, the same mapping characterization gives unique comparison isomorphisms satisfying the cocycle condition. Open gluing constructs $X_L$ and proves the displayed characterization for all $X$. It also makes $X\mapsto X_L$ functorial.}
{对一般 $X$，用包含于紧 Hausdorff 解析域 $D_i$ 的开域 $O_i$ 覆盖它。构造 $(D_i)_L$，再限制到 $O_i$ 的开逆像。在交叠的逆像上，同一个映射刻画给出唯一比较同构，并满足 cocycle 条件。开粘合构造出 $X_L$，并对所有 $X$ 证明上式刻画。这同时使 $X\mapsto X_L$ 成为函子。}

\bi{The inverse image of an analytic domain is analytic by the mixed-base version of the local pullback argument. Its mapping characterization is that of $Y_L$, so the two spaces are canonically isomorphic. In particular, the inverse image of any affinoid chart is its affinoid scalar extension. Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarextensionspectralsurjective} now proves surjectivity of $\pi$. For successive extensions, the canonical bounded isomorphism of the iterated completed tensor products in Corollary~\ref{fnd:iteratedaffinoidbasechange} gives the local comparison, and uniqueness glues it. The same corollary identifies the scalar extension of each affinoid product chart with the product of the extended charts. Since such charts form a quasi-net by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticproducts}, these local identifications glue and prove compatibility with fibre products.}
{解析域的逆像由混合底域版本的局部拉回论证可知为解析域。其映射刻画就是 $Y_L$ 的刻画，因此二者典范同构。特别地，每个 affinoid chart 的逆像就是其 affinoid 底域扩张；Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarextensionspectralsurjective} 因而证明 $\pi$ 满射。对连续扩域，Corollary~\ref{fnd:iteratedaffinoidbasechange} 中迭代完备张量积的典范有界同构给出局部比较，再由唯一性粘合。同一推论将每个 affinoid 纤维积 chart 的底域扩张识别为扩域后 chart 的纤维积。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticproducts}，这些 chart 构成 quasi-net，故局部识别粘合，并证明与纤维积相容。}
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[\bi{Locally bounded morphisms}{局部有界态射}]
\label{fnd:generalnetmorphisms}
\bi{For two affinoid atlases, a morphism consists of a continuous map $f:X\to X'$ and, for every pair of charts $U\in\bar\tau$, $V\in\bar\tau'$ with $f(U)\subseteq V$, a bounded homomorphism $f_{U,V}:A_V\to A_U$ inducing $f|_U$. These maps commute with restrictions in both charts. We require that the charts $U$ admitting such a target chart $V$ form a quasi-net of $X$. An analytic space is an affinoid atlas considered up to the canonical identifications under chart refinement of Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf}.}
{对两个 affinoid atlas，一个态射由连续映射 $f:X\to X'$ 及下述资料组成：对每对满足 $f(U)\subseteq V$ 的 chart $U\in\bar\tau$、$V\in\bar\tau'$，给出诱导 $f|_U$ 的有界同态 $f_{U,V}:A_V\to A_U$。这些映射与源、目标 chart 的限制均相容，并要求具有这样一个目标 chart $V$ 的源 chart $U$ 构成 $X$ 的 quasi-net。将 affinoid atlas 按 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf} 中 chart 加细所给的典范识别视为同一对象，得到解析空间。}
\end{definition}
