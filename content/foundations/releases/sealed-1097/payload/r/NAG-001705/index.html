Affinoid diagonals and finite proper maps

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 283

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Affinoid diagonals and finite proper maps}{Affinoid 对角线与有限适当映射}]
\label{fnd:affinoiddiagonalfiniteproper}
\bi{Every affinoid morphism is separated. A finite norm-admissible affinoid morphism is proper, and remains finite, separated and proper after every affinoid base change over a complete valued extension.}
{每个 affinoid 态射都分离。有限 norm-admissible affinoid 态射适当，并且在完备赋值扩域上的每个 affinoid 基变换后仍然有限、分离且适当。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For $A\to B$, the diagonal is represented by multiplication $\mu:B\widehat\otimes_A B\to B$. It is bounded, and $b\mapsto b\otimes1$ is a bounded linear section. Thus $\mu$ is a norm-strict surjection: the section gives a controlled lift for every $b$. Its kernel is a closed ideal, so the diagonal is a closed immersion. This is separatedness.}
{对 $A\to B$，对角线由乘法 $\mu:B\widehat\otimes_A B\to B$ 表示。该映射有界，且 $b\mapsto b\otimes1$ 是一个有界线性截面。因此 $\mu$ 为 norm-strict 满射，因为此截面为每个 $b$ 提供受控提升。其核为闭理想，所以对角线为闭浸入，即得到分离性。}

\bi{If $B$ is finite and norm-admissible over $A$, Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras} gives empty relative boundary, and its conclusion is preserved by the equivalent given norm. The map of spectra is continuous between compact Hausdorff spaces. The inverse image of any compact analytic domain in the target is closed in the compact source and hence compact. Together with boundarylessness this is properness. Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange} preserves finite admissibility under each specified base change, so the same argument applies after base change.}
{若 $B$ 在 $A$ 上有限且 norm-admissible，则由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras}，其相对边界为空；换成等价的给定范数保留该结论。谱映射为紧 Hausdorff 空间之间的连续映射。目标中任一紧解析域的逆像在紧源空间中闭，因此紧。结合无边界性，即得适当性。Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange} 在每个指定基变换下保持有限 admissibility，所以基变换后采用相同论证。}
\end{proof}
