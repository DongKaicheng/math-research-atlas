The integral affinoid case

Chapter 8 supplement: Interior Stokes with coherent coefficients

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1786

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[The integral affinoid case]
\label{f7v38:integral}
Under the hypotheses of \eqref{eq:target}, assume that \(A\) is
integral. Then \eqref{eq:target} holds for every finite \(A\)-module
\(M\).
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Let \(S=\supp(\beta)\). This support is the complement of the open
zero-germ locus in the ordinary sheaf; it is compact because \(V\)
is compact and Hausdorff. The hypothesis implies
\(S\subset\Int(V/K)\). If \(S\) is empty, the ordinary section and
its differential are zero, and
\src{v037:forms:compactintegral} gives the assertion.

Assume \(S\ne\varnothing\). Each point of \(S\) has nonzero germ.
Lemma~\ref{f7v38:generic} shows that every such point has kernel \(0\).
Apply Lemma~\ref{f7v38:separator}. The two compact sets
\[
       K_0=t_F(S),\qquad K_\partial=t_F(\partial_KW)
\]
are disjoint. Compactness of the boundary follows from compactness
of \(W\) and openness of affinoid interior.

Let \(O\) be an open neighborhood of \(t_f(V)\) on which \(\alpha\)
is defined, and let \(\pi\) denote projection to the original \(f\)
coordinates. On \(\pi^{-1}O\) put \(\widetilde\alpha=\pi^*\alpha\).
Ordinary real bump functions give a single
\[
 \chi\in C_c^\infty\bigl(\pi^{-1}O\setminus K_\partial\bigr),
          \qquad \chi=1\text{ on a neighborhood of }K_0.
\]
For example, a finite collection of real balls with closures in
that open set covers \(K_0\); standard smooth bumps and their finite
product complement give such a function.
Extend \(\eta=\chi\widetilde\alpha\) by zero to the entire enlarged
moment space. It is a smooth compactly supported superform, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:representative}
                  \beta|_W=t_F^*\eta
\end{equation}
as an ordinary section: near \(S\) the factor is \(1\); off \(S\)
the germ of \(\beta\) is zero, so multiplication preserves zero.
Unit-adjunction faithfulness and the ordinary germ sheaf justify
this identity without any equality of ambient coefficients away
from the actual source support.

The domain \(W\) is pure of analytic dimension \(d\) by
\src{v036:analytic:domainpurity}. Put
\(M_W=M\otimes_A\mathcal O(W)\).
The support of \(d'\beta\) is contained in \(S\), and \(W\) is an
ordinary neighborhood of \(S\) in \(V\). Compact-neighborhood and
chart independence in \src{v037:forms:compactintegral} give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:integralchain}
 \I_{M,f}(d'\alpha)
   =\int_{V,\widetilde M}d'\beta
   =\int_{W,\widetilde {M_W}}d'(\beta|_W)
   =\I_{M_W,F}(d'\eta).
\end{equation}
The support of \(\eta\) is compact in
\(\R^{n+N}\setminus t_F(\partial_KW)\).
Write \(U_F=\R^{n+N}\setminus t_F(\partial_KW)\).
For the primitive \(\eta\) of bidegree
\((d-1,d)\), the support of \(d'\eta\) is also compact in \(U_F\).
Use the finite-module chain and the finite-cell sum
\hyperref[fnd:nag002405:global-finite-module-chain-weights]{for its weights}
and \hyperref[fnd:nag002405:finite-cell-functional]{for its integral}.
On \(U_F\) the local module cycle of
\src{v037:analytic:interiorcycle} uses the same weights:
on each common top cell the generic point lies in the same
flat tangent plane and its entire \(F\)-fibre in \(W\) is
absolutely interior. Both coefficients are the weight of
\(M_W\) on that fibre by \src{v037:degree:moduleweight}.
A finite refinement on the compact part of the actual image
meeting the test support therefore gives identical top-cell
integrals, with the same saturated tangent lattices and no
contribution from smaller cells
(\src{v037:integration:polyhedralcalculus}). Thus the last
quantity in \eqref{eq:integralchain} equals the whole-interior
integral \(I_{M_W,F}(d'\eta)\), which is zero by
\src{v037:integration:momenttests}.
\end{proof}
