Initial multiplicity and an actual fibre degree

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 924

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Initial multiplicity and an actual fibre degree]
\label{v035:degreeinitialweight}
Let $X\subset T_N$ be a pure $d$-dimensional closed subscheme
over a complete real-valued non-Archimedean field $K$. Let $w$ belong to the relative
interior of a maximal cell $\sigma$ of an initial-stratum
complex, and suppose that $q:N\to\mathbf Z^d$ restricts to an
injective map on $N_\sigma$ with finite index
\[
 e=[\mathbf Z^d:q(N_\sigma)].
\]
After a complete isometric extension realizing $w$, made
nontrivially valued, translate $w$ to zero. The induced
monomial map from the actual tropical fibre $X_w$ to the
$d$-dimensional unit polycircle is finite and norm-admissible.
Its fibre algebra over the completed Gauss residue field $H$
has dimension $e\,m_X(\sigma)$. Multiplying the target
coordinates by constants of absolute value one preserves
these conclusions.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{cand:realinitialdegeneration} supplies the required
complete extension and translation, and
Proposition~\ref{cand:tropicalweightlocalconstancy} preserves
the weight. The quotient model of
Lemma~\ref{v035:degreeinitialmodel} has the full initial
algebra as its special algebra. By
Proposition~\ref{cand:initialtangenttorus} and
Theorem~\ref{cand:tropicalpuredimension}, a lattice splitting
identifies it with
\[
 D[N_\sigma^\vee],\qquad \dim_kD=m_X(\sigma),
\]
where $D$ is a finite $k$-algebra.

In this presentation the target characters reduce to
$d_j\chi^{a_j}$, with $d_j\in D^\times$. The $a_j$ form a
basis of a sublattice of index $e$ in $N_\sigma^\vee$.
Choose representatives for its cosets. Every character has a
unique expression as one of these representatives times a
product of the $\chi^{a_j}$. Substitution of
$\chi^{a_j}=d_j^{-1}U_j$ consequently makes
$D[N_\sigma^\vee]$ free of rank $e$ over $D[U^{\pm1}]$.
The asserted independence follows by comparing character
exponents, since the $d_j$ are units. A $k$-basis of $D$ then
gives a basis of size $e\dim_kD$ over $k[U^{\pm1}]$.

The normalized monomials and their inverses belong to the
quotient formal model, so this is the reduction of a formal
homomorphism from the unit-polycircle model. Apply
Lemma~\ref{v035:degreegaussrank}. It proves the finiteness,
norm comparison and actual Gauss fibre dimension. A unit
constant changes only one of the coefficients $d_j$ in the
preceding calculation. The calculation uses a basis of the
entire finite algebra $D$, including its nilpotents.
\end{proof}
