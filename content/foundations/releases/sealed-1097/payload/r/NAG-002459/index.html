Finite moment collars on compact analytic spaces

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Finite moment collars on compact analytic spaces]
\label{v038:degree:compactmomentcollar}
Let $Z$ be a compact good Hausdorff analytic space of analytic
dimension at most $d$, and let $f$ be a finite tuple of global
analytic units on $Z$. Choose a finite affinoid-domain cover
$(U_i)_{i=1}^r$ whose ordinary interiors cover $Z$, and put
\[
                 E=\bigcup_{i=1}^r t_f(\partial_K U_i).
\]
Then $E$ is a compact finite integral-affine polyhedral set of
dimension at most $d-1$; it is empty if $d=0$.
For every $w\notin E$ there is a closed rectangular box $P$
about $w$, with $w$ in its interior, for which
\[
                   Z_P=t_f^{-1}(P)\longrightarrow\mathbb T_P
\]
is finite. In particular $Z_P$ is an actual affinoid domain of
$Z$. The box can be chosen in any prescribed neighbourhood of
$w$. The inverse image here is the entire inverse image, and
restriction to any exact polytorus in $P$ gives the entire
original moment fibre with its analytic structure.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The cover exists by
Lemma~\ref{v037:integration:separatedintersections}. Each $U_i$
has dimension at most $d$ by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticabhyankar}. The boundary-image
assertion of Theorem~\ref{v037:globaltrop:finiteimage} proves the
claims about $E$. Each boundary is closed by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus} and compact by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact}, so its image and
their finite union are compact.

Let $q:Z\to\mathbb G_m^{n,\mathrm{an}}$ be the actual analytic
map defined by $f$, and let $\mathbb T_w$ be the exact polytorus
at $w$. The map $q$ is compact: the inverse image of a compact
analytic domain in the Hausdorff torus is a closed subset of the
compact Hausdorff space $Z$.

For each $i$ with $w\in t_f(U_i)$, the entire fibre in $U_i$
avoids $\partial_K U_i$. Proposition
\ref{v037:analytic:wholemomentcollar} supplies a finite map from
its full moment neighbourhood to a closed target polyannulus
about $w$. If $w\notin t_f(U_i)$, compactness of this image
supplies a box about $w$ with empty inverse image in $U_i$.
Intersect these finitely many boxes and shrink slightly. We
obtain a box $Q$ about $w$ such that every restriction
\[
                    U_i\cap t_f^{-1}(Q)\longrightarrow\mathbb T_Q
\]
is finite, including the empty restrictions.

Fix a point $a\in\mathbb T_w$. Its entire fibre in $Z$ is a
finite union of fibres of these finite maps, and is therefore
finite. At a point $z$ of this fibre, choose $i$ for which
$z\in\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(U_i,Z)$. Since $w$ satisfies
all inequalities of $Q$ strictly, the affinoid
$U_i\cap t_f^{-1}(Q)$ is an ordinary neighbourhood of $z$,
and $\mathbb T_Q$ is an ordinary neighbourhood of $a$.
Finite morphisms are everywhere interior by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras}. Ordinary local
testing, Proposition~\ref{fnd:goodinteriorlocaltesting}, gives
$z\in\operatorname{Int}(Z/\mathbb G_m^{n,\mathrm{an}})$.
Lemma~\ref{v038:degree:wholefinitefibre} now gives an ordinary
neighbourhood $N_a$ of $a$ over which the full map $q$ is finite.
The union $N=\bigcup_{a\in\mathbb T_w}N_a$ is an ordinary open
neighbourhood of the whole exact polytorus, and $q^{-1}(N)\to N$
is finite by target locality.

Choose a closed box $Q'$ about $w$ inside the prescribed real
neighbourhood. The complement $\mathbb T_{Q'}\setminus N$ is
compact and its tropical image does not contain $w$. A smaller
closed box $P$ about $w$ can therefore be chosen so that
$\mathbb T_P\subset N$. The full restriction
$q^{-1}(\mathbb T_P)\to\mathbb T_P$ is finite, proving the
assertion. The analytic-domain inverse-image construction
\ref{fnd:generalanalyticproducts} identifies this source with
$t_f^{-1}(P)$. Finite base change gives the actual exact-level
algebras. No point or factor of the original inverse image has
been removed.
\end{proof}
