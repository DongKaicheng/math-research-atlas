Localization at the spectral maximum

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 548

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Localization at the spectral maximum}
{在谱最大值处的局部化}]
\label{fnd:spectralmaximumlocalization}
\bi{Let $A$ be affinoid, let $f\in A$, and suppose
$r=\rho_A(f)>0$. Set $B=A\{rf^{-1}\}$ and write $\bar f$
for the initial form of $f$. For $a\in A$,}
{设 $A$ 为 affinoid，$f\in A$，且 $r=\rho_A(f)>0$。
令 $B=A\{rf^{-1}\}$，以 $\bar f$ 记 $f$ 的首项。
对 $a\in A$，有}
\[
 \rho_B(a)=\lim_{n\longrightarrow\infty}r^{-n}\rho_A(f^na).
\]
\bi{If this value is positive, the sequence is eventually constant.
The canonical homomorphism is an isomorphism}
{若这个值为正，则此序列最终常值。典范同态为同构}
\[
 (\widetilde A^{\rm gr})_{\bar f}
       \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\widetilde B^{\rm gr}.
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Let $\Gamma$ be the finite Shilov boundary from
Corollary~\ref{fnd:affinoidshilovinternal}, and set
$\Gamma_f=\{x\in\Gamma:|f(x)|=r\}$. This set is nonempty.
The rational point theorem identifies $\mathcal M(B)$ with
$\{|f|=r\}\subset\mathcal M(A)$. For every $n$ its points satisfy}
{取 Corollary~\ref{fnd:affinoidshilovinternal} 的有限 Shilov
边界 $\Gamma$，令 $\Gamma_f=\{x\in\Gamma:|f(x)|=r\}$。
这个集合非空。Rational 谱点定理将 $\mathcal M(B)$ 识别为
$\mathcal M(A)$ 中的 $\{|f|=r\}$。对任意 $n$，其谱点满足}
\[
 |a(x)|\leq r^{-n}\rho_A(f^na)
   =\max_{y\in\Gamma}\left(\frac{|f(y)|}{r}\right)^n|a(y)|.
\]
\bi{The finite maximum on the right decreases to
$m=\max_{\Gamma_f}|a|$. Conversely $\Gamma_f$ consists of points
of $B$, so $\rho_B(a)\geq m$. This proves the formula.
If $m>0$, every term with $|f(y)|<r$ is smaller than $m$
for all sufficiently large $n$, giving eventual equality.}
{右端的有限最大值递减到 $m=\max_{\Gamma_f}|a|$。
反之，$\Gamma_f$ 中的点都是 $B$ 的谱点，故 $\rho_B(a)\geq m$。
这证明公式。若 $m>0$，则当 $n$ 充分大时，全部
$|f(y)|<r$ 的项都小于 $m$，从而得到最终恒等式。}

\bi{On $B$ one has $|f|=r$, hence
$\rho_B(f^jb)=r^j\rho_B(b)$ for all integers $j$.
If a homogeneous class represented by $a$ dies in $\widetilde B^{\rm gr}$,
the limit formula gives $\rho_A(f^na)<r^n\rho_A(a)$ for some $n$.
Thus it is already killed by a power of $\bar f$, proving
injectivity after localization. Every nonzero homogeneous class in
$\widetilde B^{\rm gr}$ has a representative of the form $a/f^j$:
the polynomial algebra $A[f^{-1}]$ is dense in $B$, so approximate
a representative with error strictly below its positive spectral
size. Increase $j$ if necessary. Eventual equality gives
$\rho_A(f^na)=r^n\rho_B(a)$, and the initial class of
$f^na/f^{j+n}$ then comes from the displayed localization.
This proves surjectivity.}
{在 $B$ 上 $|f|=r$，故对所有整数 $j$ 有
$\rho_B(f^jb)=r^j\rho_B(b)$。
若 $a$ 所代表的齐次类在 $\widetilde B^{\rm gr}$ 中消失，
极限公式给出某个 $n$ 满足
$\rho_A(f^na)<r^n\rho_A(a)$。因此这个类已被 $\bar f$ 的
某个幂消去，证明局部化后的单射性。
$\widetilde B^{\rm gr}$ 中每个非零齐次类都可以由 $a/f^j$
代表：因为 $A[f^{-1}]$ 在 $B$ 中稠密，可以把代表元逼近到
误差严格小于其正谱大小。必要时增加 $j$。最终恒等式给出
$\rho_A(f^na)=r^n\rho_B(a)$，于是
$f^na/f^{j+n}$ 的首项类来自所列局部化。这证明满射性。}
\end{proof}
