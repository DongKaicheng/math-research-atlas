Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Whole-fibre overlap away from boundary walls]
\label{bdg:cofinalboundary}
For every $x\in X$, with $w=t(x)$, there is an ordinary affinoid
neighbourhood $Y_0$ of $x$ such that the germs
\[
 C_{x,f}(M):=[\mathcal T_{f|U}(M_U)]_w
 \tag{B1}\label{bdg:eqgerm}
\]
are independent of the ordinary affinoid neighbourhood $U\subset Y_0$.
They are nonnegative integral $d$-fan germs and are additive in $M$.
They vanish if $r_f(x)<d$; they may also vanish when $r_f(x)=d$.
No interior assumption on $x$ is made.

More precisely, if $r_f(x)=d$, one can choose $Y_0$ with
$S_w\cap Y_0=\{x\}$. For every finite list $U_1,\ldots,U_a\subset Y_0$
of ordinary affinoid neighbourhoods of $x$, there are $\epsilon>0$ and a
finite rational fan germ $E$ at $w$, of dimension at most $d-1$, such that
for $v\in B_\epsilon(w)\setminus E$,
\[
 F_v(Y_0)=F_v(U_i)\subset
 \operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(U_i,Y_0)\cap\operatorname{Int}(Y_0/K)
                         \cap\operatorname{Int}(U_i/K)\quad(1\leq i\leq a).
 \tag{B2}\label{bdg:eqfibres}
\]
Empty fibres are allowed. These are entire fibres in the indicated source.

For every $w$ the set $S_w$ is finite and the original source chain satisfies
\[
 [\mathcal T_f(M)]_w=\sum_{x\in S_w}C_{x,f}(M).
 \tag{B3}\label{bdg:eqsum}
\]
Finally let $L:\mathbf Z^n\to\mathbf Z^m$ be integral and let
$g_j=c_j\prod_i f_i^{L_{ji}}$, where $c_j\in K^\times$.
Writing $b_j=-\log|c_j|$, one has, for \emph{every} $x\in X$,
\[
 (b+L)_*C_{x,f}(M)=C_{x,g}(M).
 \tag{B4}\label{bdg:eqpush}
\]
Thus the directional weights are intrinsic under admissible projections,
with the usual saturated-lattice pushforward indices, including at boundary
points. This statement is about weighted fans, not finite maps through $x$.
\end{theorem}
