A discrete-residue point over a trivially valued field

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 613

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A discrete-residue point over a trivially valued field}
{平凡赋值域上具有离散剩余域的点}]
\label{fnd:trivialfielddiscretepoint}
\bi{Let $F$ be a trivially valued field and let $X$ be a
nonempty boundaryless $F$-analytic space. There is $x\in X$
such that $\mathcal H(x)$ is either finite over $F$, or
is finite over an isometrically embedded $F_r$ for some
$0<r<1$.}
{设 $F$ 为平凡赋值域，$X$ 为非空且无边界的 $F$-解析空间。
则存在 $x\in X$，使 $\mathcal H(x)$ 或者在 $F$ 上有限，
或者在某个等距嵌入的 $F_r$ 上有限，其中 $0<r<1$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Fix $0<s<1$. The field $F_s$ is the Laurent-series
field $F((T))$, with $|T|=s$: the weighted coefficient
condition allows only finitely many negative powers and
arbitrary nonnegative powers. In particular its value
group is $s^{\mathbf Z}$ and its residue field is $F$.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension} gives
surjectivity of complete scalar extension on analytic
spaces, so $X_{F_s}$ is nonempty.
By Definition~\ref{fnd:alltargetrelativeinterior}, each point of
the boundaryless space \(X\) has an ordinary affinoid
neighbourhood \(V\) on which it is interior over \(\mathcal M(F)\).
Indeed the only affinoid target domain containing its image is
\(\mathcal M(F)\) itself. For a point of \(X_{F_s}\) over that
point, the scalar extension \(V_{F_s}\) is an ordinary affinoid
neighbourhood, by the analytic-domain preimage assertion of
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension}. On the affinoid
map \(V\to\mathcal M(F)\), the forward base-change inclusion
of Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus} makes that lift
interior in \(V_{F_s}\) over \(\mathcal M(F_s)\). Since the new
target is also a one-point spectrum, the same definition makes
\(X_{F_s}\) boundaryless. The neighbourhoods just exhibited also
make it good.}
{固定 $0<s<1$。域 $F_s$ 正是 Laurent 级数域
$F((T))$，且 $|T|=s$：加权系数条件只允许有限个负次幂，
而允许任意非负次幂。特别地，其值群为 $s^{\mathbf Z}$，
剩余域为 $F$。由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension}，
完备扩标量在解析空间上满射，所以 $X_{F_s}$ 非空。
由 Definition~\ref{fnd:alltargetrelativeinterior}，在无边界空间 \(X\)
中，每一点都有普通 affinoid 邻域 \(V\)，在 \(V\) 中该点相对于
\(\mathcal M(F)\) 为内点，因为包含像点的 affinoid 目标域只有
\(\mathcal M(F)\) 本身。对 \(X_{F_s}\) 中位于该点上方的一点，
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension} 的解析域逆像结论说明
\(V_{F_s}\) 是它的普通 affinoid 邻域。对 affinoid 态射
\(V\to\mathcal M(F)\) 应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus}
的基变换正向包含，得到提升点在 \(V_{F_s}\) 中相对于
\(\mathcal M(F_s)\) 为内点。新目标仍是单点谱，所以同一定义说明
\(X_{F_s}\) 无边界。上述邻域也说明它是 good 空间。}

\bi{At any point of $X_{F_s}$ apply
Proposition~\ref{fnd:innerstrictfibrerefinement} to its
structure map. This gives a nonempty strict affinoid
domain. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} supplies
a point $y$ whose completed residue field $L$ is finite
over $F_s$. Let $x$ be its image in $X$ and identify
the complete field $E=\mathcal H(x)$ with its isometric
image in $L$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitevaluedindexbound}
makes $|L^\times|$ nontrivial cyclic and
$\widetilde L/F$ finite. Therefore
$\widetilde E/F$ is finite and $|E^\times|$ is a subgroup
of a cyclic group.}
{在 $X_{F_s}$ 的任一点处，将
Proposition~\ref{fnd:innerstrictfibrerefinement} 用于
结构态射，得到一个非空 strict affinoid 域。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} 给出点 $y$，
使其完备剩余域 $L$ 在 $F_s$ 上有限。
令 $x$ 为它在 $X$ 中的像，并把完备域
$E=\mathcal H(x)$ 识别为其在 $L$ 中的等距像。
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitevaluedindexbound}，
$|L^\times|$ 为非平凡循环群，且 $\widetilde L/F$ 有限。
所以 $\widetilde E/F$ 有限，而 $|E^\times|$ 是循环群的子群。}

\bi{If $E$ is trivially valued, it equals its residue field
and is finite over $F$. Otherwise choose
$\lambda\in E$ with $0<r=|\lambda|<1$.
Since $F$ is trivially valued, distinct powers of
$\lambda$ have distinct sizes; evaluation on
$F[T,T^{-1}]$ is therefore isometric for the radius-$r$
Gauss norm. Its completion is an isometric copy of $F_r$
inside $E$, with residue field $F$.
The group $|E^\times|$ is nontrivial cyclic and
$\widetilde E/F$ is finite. Apply
Lemma~\ref{fnd:discretegradedfiniteextension} to
$F_r\subset E$. It gives the required finiteness.}
{若 $E$ 平凡赋值，则其等于自己的剩余域，因而在 $F$ 上有限。
否则，选择 $\lambda\in E$，满足
$0<r=|\lambda|<1$。因 $F$ 平凡赋值，
$\lambda$ 的不同次幂具有不同大小；故
$F[T,T^{-1}]$ 上的取值映射对半径 $r$ 的 Gauss 范数等距。
其完备化给出 $E$ 内一个等距的 $F_r$，其剩余域为 $F$。
此时 $|E^\times|$ 是非平凡循环群，且
$\widetilde E/F$ 有限。对 $F_r\subset E$ 应用
Lemma~\ref{fnd:discretegradedfiniteextension}，
得到所需有限性。}
\end{proof}
