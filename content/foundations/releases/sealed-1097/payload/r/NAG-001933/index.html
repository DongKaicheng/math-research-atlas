Lifting an affine analytic morphism

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 529

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Lifting an affine analytic morphism}{仿射解析态射的提升}]
\label{fnd:affineformalmorphismlifting}
\bi{Let $A_0,B_0$ be admissible $K^\circ$-algebras.
Every bounded $K$-homomorphism
$\varphi:B_0[1/\pi]\to A_0[1/\pi]$ extends to
$B_0\to A_0'$ after a finite admissible blowup
$\operatorname{Spf}(A_0')\to\operatorname{Spf}(A_0)$.
Two homomorphisms between admissible formal algebras that agree
after inverting $\pi$ are equal.}
{设 $A_0,B_0$ 为 admissible $K^\circ$-代数。每个有界
$K$-同态 $\varphi:B_0[1/\pi]\to A_0[1/\pi]$ 都在一次
有限 admissible blowup
$\operatorname{Spf}(A_0')\to\operatorname{Spf}(A_0)$ 后
延拓为 $B_0\to A_0'$。两个 admissible 形式代数之间的同态
若在逆转 $\pi$ 后相同，则它们相同。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose finitely many topological generators $b_i$ of $B_0$.
They are power bounded in its generic algebra. A bounded map
takes power-bounded elements to power-bounded elements, since
it takes the bounded set of their powers to a bounded set.
Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:powerboundedmodelblowup} to
$f_i=\varphi(b_i)$. Evaluation of restricted series now gives
$B_0\to A_0'$. All defining relations vanish because they
vanish in $A_0'[1/\pi]$ and $A_0'$ has no $\pi$-torsion.
The same injectivity proves the last assertion.}
{选取 $B_0$ 的有限个拓扑生成元 $b_i$。它们在泛纤维代数中
幂有界；有界映射把其幂构成的有界集合映为有界集合，故
$\varphi(b_i)$ 也幂有界。对 $f_i=\varphi(b_i)$ 应用
Theorem~\ref{fnd:powerboundedmodelblowup}。限制幂级数求值
给出 $B_0\to A_0'$，各定义关系在 $A_0'[1/\pi]$ 中为零，
又因 $A_0'$ 无 $\pi$-挠，它们在 $A_0'$ 中也为零。
同一个单射性论证给出最后的断言。}
\end{proof}
