Joint continuity for restricted-series Jacobian charts

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 891

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Joint continuity for restricted-series Jacobian charts}
{受限级数 Jacobian 坐标片上的联合连续性}]
\label{cand:etaleendpointjointcontinuity}
\bi{For every presentation
\eqref{eq:candetalejacobianpresentation}, the pointwise
endpoint of Lemma~\ref{cand:etaledisplacementpointwiseendpoint}
makes}
{对每个 \eqref{eq:candetalejacobianpresentation} 型表示，
Lemma~\ref{cand:etaledisplacementpointwiseendpoint} 的
逐点终点使映射}
\[
 H_B:\mathcal M(B)\times[0,1]\longrightarrow\mathcal M(B)
\]
\bi{jointly continuous. Its full homotopy commutes with the
contractive morphisms, integral Laurent restrictions and complete
isometric extensions of
Proposition~\ref{cand:etaledisplacementfunctoriality}.}
{联合连续。其整个同伦与
Proposition~\ref{cand:etaledisplacementfunctoriality}
中的压缩态射、整 Laurent 限制及完备等距扩张相容。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{We first treat a polynomial representative
$b=P(X,T,Y,Q)\in B$. Choose polynomial systems
$F^{(n)}\in K^\circ[X,T,Y]^m$ with
$\|F^{(n)}-F\|\leq\epsilon_n<1$ and
$\epsilon_n\to0$, using the fixed restricted-series
representatives. Differentiation is contractive for the
unit-radius Gauss norms, so the Jacobians are within the
same bound. At the original tuple $Y\in B^m$, the system
$F^{(n)}$ has error at most $\epsilon_n$ and an inverse
Jacobian of norm at most one. Thus
Lemma~\ref{cand:banachcenterhensel}, now in $B$, gives
an exact root $Y_n$ with}
{先考虑一个多项式代表 $b=P(X,T,Y,Q)\in B$。
利用固定的受限级数代表，选择多项式方程组
$F^{(n)}\in K^\circ[X,T,Y]^m$，使
$\|F^{(n)}-F\|\leq\epsilon_n<1$，且
$\epsilon_n\to0$。对单位半径 Gauss 范数，微分算子
压缩，故 Jacobian 之差也满足同样的界。在原元组
$Y\in B^m$ 处，$F^{(n)}$ 的误差至多为
$\epsilon_n$，Jacobian 的逆范数至多为一。因此在
$B$ 中应用 Lemma~\ref{cand:banachcenterhensel}，
得到精确根 $Y_n$，满足}
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \|Y_n-Y\|\leq\epsilon_n,
 \qquad Q_n=\det((F^{(n)})'(Y_n))^{-1},\\
 \|Q_n\|\leq1,\qquad \|Q_n-Q\|\leq\epsilon_n.
 \end{gathered}
\]

\bi{Displace the base and lift the two roots, obtaining
$Y_\delta$ and $Y_{n,\delta}$ in
$B\{\delta^{-1}U\}$ for $\delta<1$.
Both roots lie at distance at most
$\max(\delta,\epsilon_n)<1$ from the original tuple $Y$.
Their displaced systems differ by at most $\epsilon_n$,
since base substitution is contractive. The perturbation
part of Lemma~\ref{cand:banachcenterhensel} therefore gives,
uniformly for every $\delta<1$,}
{对基底作位移并提升这两个根，当 $\delta<1$ 时，
在 $B\{\delta^{-1}U\}$ 中得到 $Y_\delta$ 与
$Y_{n,\delta}$。两根距原元组 $Y$ 都至多为
$\max(\delta,\epsilon_n)<1$。基底代换压缩，故其
位移后的方程组相差至多 $\epsilon_n$。于是
Lemma~\ref{cand:banachcenterhensel} 的扰动部分对
每个 $\delta<1$ 一致给出}
\[
 \|Y_{n,\delta}-Y_\delta\|\leq\epsilon_n.
\]
\bi{The determinant difference has the same bound, by
telescoping and the derivative bound. Both inverses have
norm at most one, so the identity
$u^{-1}-v^{-1}=u^{-1}(v-u)v^{-1}$ gives
$\|Q_{n,\delta}-Q_\delta\|\leq\epsilon_n$.
Let $P_n(U)$ denote $P$ evaluated on the displaced base,
$Y_{n,\delta}$ and $Q_{n,\delta}$. This definition is
compatible with all radii. If $C_P$ is the maximum absolute
value of the coefficients of $P$, telescoping its finitely
many monomials yields}
{由望远镜分解及导数界，行列式之差满足同样的界。
两个逆元的范数都至多为一，因此恒等式
$u^{-1}-v^{-1}=u^{-1}(v-u)v^{-1}$ 给出
$\|Q_{n,\delta}-Q_\delta\|\leq\epsilon_n$。
以 $P_n(U)$ 记把 $P$ 在位移后的基底、
$Y_{n,\delta}$ 及 $Q_{n,\delta}$ 处取值的结果；
其定义与全部半径相容。若 $C_P$ 是 $P$ 的系数
绝对值最大值，则对其有限个单项式作望远镜分解得到}
\[
 \|P_n(U)-\sigma_\delta(b)\|_\delta
                          \leq C_P\epsilon_n
                       \qquad(0<\delta<1).
 \tag{A}\label{eq:canduniformjacobianapproximation}
\]

\bi{By Lemma~\ref{cand:polynomialimplicitendpoint}, the
norm functions defined by $P_n(U)$, with coefficient
suprema at time one, are jointly continuous on the full
product. Inequality~\eqref{eq:canduniformjacobianapproximation}
bounds the differences of their real-valued norms from
$|b(H_B(x,t))|$ by $C_P\epsilon_n$ for all $x$ and $t<1$.
Passing to the already established pointwise limits gives
the same bound at $t=1$. Thus $|b(H_B(x,t))|$ is a uniform
limit of continuous functions on the full product and is
continuous there.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{cand:polynomialimplicitendpoint}，
$P_n(U)$ 所定义的范数函数在时刻一取系数上确界后，
在整个直积上联合连续。
不等式 \eqref{eq:canduniformjacobianapproximation}
将它们的实值范数与 $|b(H_B(x,t))|$ 之差，对全部
$x$ 及 $t<1$ 一致控制在 $C_P\epsilon_n$ 内。
传到已经建立的逐点极限后，在 $t=1$ 仍有同样的界。
因此 $|b(H_B(x,t))|$ 是整个直积上的连续函数的一致
极限，故在其上连续。}

\bi{Finally polynomial representatives are dense in the
quotient Banach algebra $B$. Contractivity of every
strict-time evaluation and of the endpoint seminorm gives}
{最后，多项式代表在商 Banach 代数 $B$ 中稠密。
每个严格小于一时刻的取值及终点半范数都压缩，故}
\[
 \bigl||b(H_B(x,t))|-|b'(H_B(x,t))|\bigr|
                                  \leq\|b-b'\|
                              \quad(0\leq t\leq1).
\]
\bi{Uniform polynomial approximation proves continuity
for every $b\in B$, hence joint continuity into its
spectrum. The previously proved strict-time functorial
identities pass to the endpoint by pointwise convergence.}
{一致多项式逼近对每个 $b\in B$ 给出连续性，从而
给出到其谱的联合连续性。此前严格小于一时刻的函子
恒等式，由逐点收敛传到终点。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Retain the standard algebra $A$ and its integral coordinates
from Lemma~\ref{cand:mixedgaussdisplacement}. Let
$F=(F_1,\ldots,F_m)$ be represented by restricted series in
$X_0,\ldots,X_r,T,Y_1,\ldots,Y_m$ with coefficients in
$K^\circ$. Write $J=\partial F/\partial Y$ and consider the
nonzero affinoid algebra}
{保留 Lemma~\ref{cand:mixedgaussdisplacement} 中的标准代数
$A$ 及其整坐标。设 $F=(F_1,\ldots,F_m)$ 由变量
$X_0,\ldots,X_r,T,Y_1,\ldots,Y_m$ 的受限级数表示，
系数属于 $K^\circ$。写 $J=\partial F/\partial Y$，并考虑
非零 affinoid 代数}
\[
 B=A\langle Y_1,\ldots,Y_m,Q\rangle/
                  (F_1,\ldots,F_m,Q\det J-1).
 \tag{J}\label{eq:candetalejacobianpresentation}
\]
\bi{We use its displayed quotient norm. In particular the
coordinate images and $J^{-1}=Q\operatorname{adj}(J)$ have
norm at most one. All the statements below keep this
presentation over the original valued field.}
{使用所列表示的商范数。特别地，坐标像及
$J^{-1}=Q\operatorname{adj}(J)$ 的范数至多为一。下述
全部陈述保留原赋值域上的这个表示。}
