Analytic domains and their coverings

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 379

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Analytic domains and their coverings}{解析域及其覆盖}]
\label{fnd:affinoidanalyticdomaincalculus}
\bi{Analytic domains in $X$ are closed under finite intersections. Their admissible coverings define a pretopology, denoted $X_G$. Every analytic domain is locally compact and Hausdorff. A subset is a compact analytic domain exactly when it is a finite union of affinoid domains of $X$. Every ordinary open subset of $X$ is an analytic domain. If $V$ is affinoid, the analytic domains and coverings inside $V$ are precisely those obtained by applying Definition~\ref{fnd:affinoidanalyticquasinet} to $\mathcal M(A_V)$.}
{解析域在有限交下保持，其 admissible 覆盖定义一个预拓扑，记为 $X_G$。每个解析域都局部紧且 Hausdorff。一个子集是紧解析域，当且仅当它是 $X$ 中有限个 affinoid 域的并。每个通常开子集都是解析域。若 $V$ 为 affinoid，则其中的解析域及覆盖恰与对 $\mathcal M(A_V)$ 使用 Definition~\ref{fnd:affinoidanalyticquasinet} 所得者相同。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Take affinoid quasi-net coverings $(U_i)$ of $U$ and $(V_j)$ of $V$. At $x\in U\cap V$, choose the finite subfamilies containing $x$ and covering relative neighborhoods. The finitely many intersections $U_i\cap V_j$ contain $x$ and cover a neighborhood in $U\cap V$. They are affinoid domains by Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainintersections}; hence $U\cap V$ is analytic. The same argument, together with Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasinetcalculus}, proves stability of admissible covers under pullback and composition, and the identity cover is admissible.}
{分别取 $U,V$ 的 affinoid quasi-net 覆盖 $(U_i),(V_j)$。在 $x\in U\cap V$ 处，选择包含 $x$ 且覆盖相对邻域的有限子族。所得有限个交集 $U_i\cap V_j$ 均包含 $x$，并覆盖 $x$ 在 $U\cap V$ 中的一个邻域。由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainintersections}，这些交集均为 affinoid 域，故 $U\cap V$ 为解析域。同样的论证结合 Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasinetcalculus}，证明 admissible 覆盖在拉回及复合下保持；恒等覆盖显然 admissible。}

\bi{An affinoid quasi-net gives every point a compact neighborhood, namely a finite union of affinoid pieces. These pieces are compact and closed in the Hausdorff space $X$, so every analytic domain is locally compact and Hausdorff. If the domain is compact, the last assertion of Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasinetcalculus} gives a finite affinoid cover. Conversely a finite union of affinoid domains is compact, and its finite closed covering is a quasi-net by that lemma.}
{Affinoid quasi-net 为每个点提供一个紧邻域，即有限个 affinoid 块之并。这些块在 Hausdorff 空间 $X$ 中紧且闭，所以每个解析域局部紧且 Hausdorff。若解析域本身紧，Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasinetcalculus} 的最后一项给出有限 affinoid 覆盖。反之，有限个 affinoid 域的并紧，且由该引理，其有限闭覆盖为 quasi-net。}

\bi{For an open set $O$ and $x\in O$, the evaluation topology gives finitely many strict upper or lower bounds on values of elements of $A$ whose common locus contains $x$ and is contained in $O$. Choose weak bounds strictly between the value at $x$ and each prescribed strict bound. They define finitely many Weierstrass or Laurent domains; their intersection is rational by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedjointpresentations}, is contained in $O$, and contains a neighborhood of $x$. These rational neighborhoods form a quasi-net covering of $O$. Finally, affinoid domains of $X$ contained in $V$ are affinoid over $A_V$, and affinoid domains over $A_V$ are affinoid over $A$, by restriction and transitivity in Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainrestriction}. The induced topologies and quasi-net conditions coincide. This proves the last assertion.}
{对开集 $O$ 及 $x\in O$，取值拓扑给出有限个关于 $A$ 中元素取值的严格上界或下界，使其公共区域包含 $x$ 且包含于 $O$。在 $x$ 处的值与每个指定严格界之间选择一个弱界，得到有限个 Weierstrass 或 Laurent 域。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedjointpresentations}，其交为 rational，包含于 $O$，并包含 $x$ 的一个邻域。这些 rational 邻域构成 $O$ 的 quasi-net 覆盖。最后，由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainrestriction} 的限制及传递性，$X$ 中包含于 $V$ 的 affinoid 域恰为 $A_V$ 上的 affinoid 域。诱导拓扑和 quasi-net 条件均相同，故得到最后一项。}
\end{proof}
