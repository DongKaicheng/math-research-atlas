2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{An \'etale map with unchanged completed residue field}{完备剩余域不变的 \'etale 态射}]
\label{fnd:etalesameresiduelocalisomorphism}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be \'etale between good spaces, and let $y\mapsto x$. If $\mathcal H(x)\to\mathcal H(y)$ is an isomorphism, then $f$ induces an isomorphism between ordinary neighborhoods of $y$ and $x$.}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 是 good 空间之间的 \'etale 态射，$y\mapsto x$。若 $\mathcal H(x)\to\mathcal H(y)$ 为同构，则 $f$ 在 $y$ 与 $x$ 的某些普通邻域之间诱导同构。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{An \'etale morphism is locally finite at $y$ by Proposition~\ref{fnd:localfiniteetalecriterion}. Separate the branch at $y$ by Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation}. Its finite \'etale germ has fibre $\mathcal H(x)$, so Theorem~\ref{fnd:goodetalegermresidueequivalence} identifies it with the identity germ. The inverse morphism and the two inverse equalities involve finitely many affinoid algebra generators; representing them and shrinking the ordinary neighborhoods makes these equalities hold as analytic morphisms.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:localfiniteetalecriterion}，\'etale 态射在 $y$ 处局部有限。利用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation} 分离 $y$ 处的分支，其有限 \'etale 芽的纤维为 $\mathcal H(x)$，故由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:goodetalegermresidueequivalence}，它与恒等芽同构。逆态射及两个互逆恒等式只涉及有限个 affinoid 代数生成元；把它们表示在普通邻域上并缩小，即可使这些等式作为解析态射成立。}
\end{proof}
