Rational domains under complete-extension base change

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 276

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Rational domains under complete-extension base change}{完备扩域基变换下的 rational 域}]
\label{fnd:rationalcompletebasechange}
\bi{Let $U\subset\mathcal M(A)$ be a generalized rational domain, let $C$ be a generalized affinoid over a complete extension $L/K$, and let $\phi:A\to C$ be bounded. Then}
{设 $U\subset\mathcal M(A)$ 为广义 rational 域，$C$ 为完备扩域 $L/K$ 上的广义 affinoid，$\phi:A\to C$ 有界。则}
\[
 A_U\widehat\otimes_A C\simeq C_{\phi^{-1}(U)}
\]
\bi{as bounded $C$-algebras, where the domain on the right is the inverse image under the spectral map. The comparison commutes with restriction to nested rational domains and finite intersections. Finite rational covers pull back to finite rational covers. In particular, for $\pi:\mathcal M(A_L)\to\mathcal M(A)$,}
{为有界 $C$-代数同构，其中右边的域为谱映射下的逆像。该比较与嵌套 rational 域的限制及有限交叠相容。有限 rational 覆盖的拉回仍为有限 rational 覆盖。特别地，对 $\pi:\mathcal M(A_L)\to\mathcal M(A)$，有}
\[
 (A_U)_L\simeq (A_L)_{\pi^{-1}(U)}.
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For the presentation $A_U=A\{\rho^{-1}T\}/(gT_i-f_i)$, Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout} gives the actual algebra}
{对表示 $A_U=A\{\rho^{-1}T\}/(gT_i-f_i)$，Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout} 给出实际代数}
\[
 C\{\rho^{-1}T\}/\bigl(\phi(g)T_i-\phi(f_i)\bigr).
\]
\bi{The images of $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$ still generate the unit ideal. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints}, its spectrum is precisely the locus $|\phi(f_i)|\leq\rho_i|\phi(g)|$, namely the inverse image of $U$. The uniqueness in Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal} gives compatibility with restriction. The joint presentations of Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedjointpresentations} give the same statement for intersections. The pointwise inverse images of a cover cover the target. For $C=A_L$, both $(A_U)_L$ and $A_U\widehat\otimes_A A_L$ are the pushout of $A_U$ and $L$ over $K$; uniqueness of the bounded pushout identifies them and proves the final formula.}
{这里 $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$ 的像仍生成单位理想。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints}，其谱恰为 $|\phi(f_i)|\leq\rho_i|\phi(g)|$ 定义的集合，即 $U$ 的逆像。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal} 的唯一性给出与限制的相容性；Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedjointpresentations} 的联合表示给出有限交叠的相应断言。一个覆盖的逐点逆像覆盖目标。取 $C=A_L$ 时，$(A_U)_L$ 与 $A_U\widehat\otimes_A A_L$ 均为 $A_U$ 和 $L$ 在 $K$ 上的推出；由有界推出的唯一性，将两者识别，得到最后的公式。}
\end{proof}
