Adic topology on finite formal modules

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 518

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Adic topology on finite formal modules}
{有限形式模上的 adic 拓扑}]
\label{fnd:formalmoduletopology}
\bi{Let $A_0$ be topologically finite type over $O$, let $M$ be a
finite $A_0$-module, and let $N\subset M$ be any submodule. Its
$\pi$-saturation}
{设 $A_0$ 为 $O$ 上的拓扑有限型代数，$M$ 为有限 $A_0$-模，
$N\subset M$ 为任意子模。其 $\pi$-饱和化}
\[
 N^{\mathrm{sat}}=\{m\in M:\pi^e m\in N\text{ for some }e\geq0\}
\]
\bi{is finitely generated. There is an $e\geq0$ such that, for all
$n\geq0$,}
{有限生成，并且存在 $e\geq0$，使对全部 $n\geq0$ 有}
\[
 \pi^{n+e}M\cap N\ \subset\ \pi^nN\ \subset\ \pi^nM\cap N.
\]
\bi{Every finite $A_0$-module is $\pi$-adically complete and
separated. In particular every submodule of a finite module is closed.}
{每个有限 $A_0$-模都是 $\pi$-adic 完备且分离的。因此，有限模的
每个子模均为闭子模。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose $P=O\langle T\rangle\twoheadrightarrow A_0$ and a
finite free $P$-module mapping onto $M$. Its composite map to
$M/N^{\mathrm{sat}}$ has finitely generated kernel by the proof of
Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation}, since the target is
$O$-torsion-free. The image of this kernel is $N^{\mathrm{sat}}$,
which is therefore finite. A common exponent $e$ sends its finite
generators into $N$. If $x=\pi^{n+e}m\in N$, then
$m\in N^{\mathrm{sat}}$ and $\pi^em\in N$, giving the first inclusion.}
{选取 $P=O\langle T\rangle\twoheadrightarrow A_0$，以及到 $M$
的有限自由 $P$-模满射。其到 $M/N^{\mathrm{sat}}$ 的复合映射具有
有限生成的核，因为目标无 $O$-挠，可用
Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation} 的证明。该核的像
正是 $N^{\mathrm{sat}}$，故后者有限。选择同一个指数 $e$，将它的
有限生成元都送入 $N$。若 $x=\pi^{n+e}m\in N$，则
$m\in N^{\mathrm{sat}}$ 且 $\pi^em\in N$，给出第一个包含关系。}

\bi{To prove completeness of a finite module, present it as a quotient
of $P^s$. A compatible sequence of classes modulo $\pi^n$ has lifts
$x_n\in P^s$ with $x_{n+1}-x_n\in\pi^nP^s$: recursively adjust a
lift by an element of the presentation kernel. The $x_n$ converge
in the complete module $P^s$, yielding a lift of all the classes.
For separatedness take $x\in\bigcap_n\pi^nM$ and apply the first
inclusion to the cyclic submodule $A_0x$. It gives $x\in\pi A_0x$,
so $x=\pi ax$ for some $a\in A_0$. The element $1-\pi a$ is a
unit: lift $a$ to $P$ and use the convergent geometric series there.
Hence $x=0$. Finally $M/N$ is finite and separated, which says
exactly that $N$ is closed in $M$.}
{为证明有限模完备，将其表示为 $P^s$ 的商。模 $\pi^n$ 的相容
类序列可提升为 $x_n\in P^s$，且
$x_{n+1}-x_n\in\pi^nP^s$：递归地用表示核中的元素调整提升即可。
序列 $x_n$ 在完备模 $P^s$ 中收敛，其极限提升全部相容类。
为证明分离性，取 $x\in\bigcap_n\pi^nM$，并对子模 $A_0x$
应用第一个包含式，得到 $x\in\pi A_0x$，即某个 $a\in A_0$
满足 $x=\pi ax$。元素 $1-\pi a$ 可逆：将 $a$ 提升到 $P$，
在其中使用收敛几何级数即可。因此 $x=0$。最后，$M/N$ 有限且
分离，这恰好说明 $N$ 在 $M$ 中闭。}
\end{proof}
