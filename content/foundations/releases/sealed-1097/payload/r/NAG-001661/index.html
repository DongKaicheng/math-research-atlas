Completeness of the reduced spectral norm

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 597

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Completeness of the reduced spectral norm}
{约化谱范数的完备性}]
\label{fnd:reducedspectralcomplete}
\bi{Let $A$ be a reduced generalized $K$-affinoid algebra with its
given norm-admissible affinoid norm. Then $\rho_A$ is a complete
norm, uniformly equivalent to the given norm. The statement holds
for arbitrary positive radii and for a trivially valued ground field.}
{设 $A$ 为约化 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数，配备给定的
norm-admissible affinoid 范数，则 $\rho_A$ 为完备范数，且与
给定范数一致等价。此结论允许任意正半径及平凡赋值基域。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First let $A$ be a nonzero strict domain. Choose a finite
normalization $R\hookrightarrow A$, put $F=\operatorname{Frac}(R)$,
and let $H$ be its Gauss completion. Write $E=A\otimes_R F$.
By Theorem~\ref{fnd:gaussfractionseparable}, the finite-dimensional
algebra $D=E\otimes_F H$ is reduced. Every prime of a
finite-dimensional algebra over a field is maximal, and there are
finitely many maximal ideals; the Chinese remainder theorem and
the nilradical formula therefore give
$D=\prod_{s=1}^t L_s$ with finite field extensions $L_s/H$.
Its maximum absolute-value norm is complete and equivalent to
every $H$-coordinate norm by Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}.}
{先设 $A$ 为非零 strict 整环。选择有限正规化 $R\hookrightarrow A$，
令 $F=\operatorname{Frac}(R)$，以 $H$ 记其 Gauss 完备化，并写
$E=A\otimes_R F$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:gaussfractionseparable}，
有限维代数 $D=E\otimes_F H$ 约化。域上的有限维代数的素理想
均极大，且只有有限个极大理想；所以中国剩余定理及幂零根公式给出
$D=\prod_{s=1}^tL_s$，其中 $L_s/H$ 均为有限扩域。
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}，其最大绝对值范数完备，
且与任一 $H$-坐标范数等价。}

\bi{Choose an $F$-basis $e_1,\ldots,e_m$ of $E$. Since $A$ is
finite over $R$, one common nonzero denominator $b\in R$ makes
all coordinates of $bA$ belong to $R$. We obtain an injective
$R$-linear map $j:A\to R^m$. The canonical finite-module theorem,
and its compatibility with the affinoid norm in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebras}, imply}
{选择 $E$ 的一组 $F$-基 $e_1,\ldots,e_m$。由于 $A$ 在 $R$ 上
有限，可选取同一个非零分母 $b\in R$，使 $bA$ 的全部坐标属于 $R$。
于是得到单射 $R$-线性映射 $j:A\to R^m$。典范有限模定理，以及
Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebras} 给出的与 affinoid
范数的相容性，说明}
\[
 \|a\|_A\leq C\|j(a)\|_{R^m}
       \leq C'\max_s|a|_{L_s}\qquad(a\in A)
\]
\bi{for constants independent of $a$. The second inequality is
the finite-dimensional coordinate bound on $D$, including the fixed
factor $|b|$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:normalizationfiniteboundary}
identifies the last maximum with $\rho_A(a)$. The reverse inequality
$\rho_A(a)\leq\|a\|_A$ is the spectral-radius formula. This proves
norm equivalence for a strict domain.}
{其中常数与 $a$ 无关。第二个不等式来自 $D$ 上的有限维坐标界，
并计入固定因子 $|b|$。
Proposition~\ref{fnd:normalizationfiniteboundary} 将最后的最大值
识别为 $\rho_A(a)$。反向不等式
$\rho_A(a)\leq\|a\|_A$ 来自谱半径公式。这证明 strict 整环情形
的范数等价性。}

\bi{For a reduced strict $A$, the diagonal map into the finite product
of its minimal-prime quotients is an injection of finite $A$-modules.
It is norm-strict by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}.
The domain case on the factors and the fact that every point kills
a minimal prime give the same two-sided estimate for $A$.
For arbitrary radii choose a free strictification $A_r$.
It is reduced by Proposition~\ref{fnd:spectralnilradicalinternal}.
Apply the strict estimate upstairs and restrict to the isometric
coefficient inclusion $A\hookrightarrow A_r$.
The Gauss section again gives
$\rho_{A_r}(a\otimes1)=\rho_A(a)$, proving the desired estimate.
Completeness and definiteness follow from equivalence with the given
Banach norm. The zero algebra has the zero Banach space and causes
no exception.}
{对约化 strict $A$，它到有限个极小素理想商之乘积的对角映射
是有限 $A$-模的单射，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}
为 norm-strict。在各因子上使用整环情形，并利用每个谱点均消去
某个极小素理想，即得 $A$ 的相同双向估计。
对任意半径，选择自由 strictification $A_r$。由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:spectralnilradicalinternal}，它约化。
在上层应用 strict 估计，再限制到等距系数包含
$A\hookrightarrow A_r$。Gauss 截面再次给出
$\rho_{A_r}(a\otimes1)=\rho_A(a)$，从而得到所需估计。
完备性与正定性来自与给定 Banach 范数的等价性。零代数对应零
Banach 空间，同样满足结论。}
\end{proof}
