Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[Actual source image; F8GP-2]\label[lemma]{lem:image}
On \(V_0\setminus\{f_0=0\}\), the moment tuple
\[
 h=(T_1-1,\ldots,T_k-1,Z_1,\ldots,Z_d,T_1,\ldots,T_k)
\]
has image exactly \(P\). Its restriction to \(V_{0,S}\) has image
exactly \(P_S\). These are statements about all analytic points over
the original field.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
For one variable, the strong triangle inequality applied to
\(T=(T-1)+1\) gives three possibilities. If \(|T|>1\), then
\(|T-1|=|T|\). If \(|T|<1\), then \(|T-1|=1\).
If \(|T|=1\), then \(|T-1|\leq1\).
Since \(T\ne0\) and \(T-1\) is a unit, these are precisely the
three edges in (1.4), with the stated outer bounds.

Conversely, every point of an edge is attained by a Gauss seminorm:
on \(D_i\) or \(E_i\), use the variable \(T_i\) with radius
\(e^{-s_i}\); on \(H_i\), use \(T_i-1\) with radius \(e^{-y_i}\).
At the common vertex, the radius-one Gauss seminorm in \(T_i\)
has both \(|T_i|=|T_i-1|=1\).
Together with radii \(e^{-x_\nu}\) for the \(Z_\nu\), the
multivariable Gauss seminorm is multiplicative over \(K\).
Its values satisfy all inequalities of (1.1), so the actual rational
domain property gives a point of \(V_0\).
All \(T_i\) have positive value, as required.
This proves both inclusions for \(P\).
Finally \(-\log|f_0|=\lambda+\sum_i m_i s_i+b\cdot x\)
on every analytic point. Imposing its upper bound \(S\) proves the
second equality.
\end{proof}
