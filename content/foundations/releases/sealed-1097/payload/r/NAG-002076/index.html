A punctured smooth tube

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 695

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{A punctured smooth tube}
{带穿孔的光滑管域}]
\label{cand:puncturedsmoothtube}
\bi{Let $\mathcal X/O$ be a curve model, smooth of relative
dimension one at a rational special-fibre point $q$. Let
$D=]q[$, let $v$ be its component point, and let $a\in D(K)$.
There is a pointed disc coordinate $z$ with $z(a)=0$,
identifying $D$ with $|z|<1$. The ray}
{设曲线模型 $\mathcal X/O$ 在特殊纤维的有理点 $q$ 处
相对维数为一且光滑。令 $D=]q[$，以 $v$ 表示其分量点，
并取 $a\in D(K)$。存在满足 $z(a)=0$ 的带点圆盘坐标 $z$，
将 $D$ 识别为 $|z|<1$。射线}
\[
 R_a=\{v\}\cup\{\zeta_{0,\rho}:0<\rho<1\},
 \qquad \tau=-\log\rho\in[0,\infty)
\]
\bi{is intrinsic to the pointed tube $(D,a)$. The radial
annulus homotopy on $D\setminus\{a\}$ extends by fixing $v$
to a jointly continuous strong deformation retraction of
$(D\cup\{v\})\setminus\{a\}$ onto $R_a$.}
{仅由带标记的管域 $(D,a)$ 决定。
$D\setminus\{a\}$ 上的径向圆环同伦，通过固定 $v$，
延拓为 $(D\cup\{v\})\setminus\{a\}$ 到 $R_a$ 的
联合连续强形变收缩。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Take the pointed smooth coordinate supplied by
Proposition~\ref{cand:smoothpointpolydisctube}. Its value at
$a$ belongs to $K^{\circ\circ}$, so subtracting that value
retains the unit-disc coordinate and puts $a$ at zero. The
complement is the standard open annulus $0<|z|<1$.
Corollary~\ref{cand:openannuluscoordinateisometry} supplies its
radial homotopy. It preserves $|z|$ at every time. By
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure}, every net in $D$
whose radii tend to one converges to $v$, uniformly over each
outer radial tail. This proves joint continuity at all
$(v,u)$, $0\leq u\leq1$, after setting $H(v,u)=v$.}
{取 Proposition~\ref{cand:smoothpointpolydisctube} 给出的
带点光滑坐标。其在 $a$ 处的值属于 $K^{\circ\circ}$，
所以减去该值后仍为单位圆盘坐标，且把 $a$ 放在零点。
补集为标准开圆环 $0<|z|<1$。由
Corollary~\ref{cand:openannuluscoordinateisometry}，它具有
径向同伦，且在每个时刻保持 $|z|$。由
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure}，$D$ 中半径
趋于一的任意网都收敛到 $v$，并在外侧径向尾部上一致。
定义 $H(v,u)=v$ 后，这就证明每个 $(v,u)$ 处的联合连续性。}

\bi{A change between two centered disc coordinates restricts
to an isomorphism of $0<|z|<1$. The radius transformation in
Corollary~\ref{cand:openannuluscoordinateisometry} must be
$\rho\mapsto\rho$: it has the form $|c|\rho^{\pm1}$ and maps
$(0,1)$ onto itself. That corollary identifies both the Gauss
ray and the entire homotopy. The logarithmic parameter is
therefore unchanged.}
{两个以 $a$ 为中心的圆盘坐标之间的变换，限制为
$0<|z|<1$ 的同构。由
Corollary~\ref{cand:openannuluscoordinateisometry}，半径
变换为 $|c|\rho^{\pm1}$；它将 $(0,1)$ 映满自身，故必为
$\rho\mapsto\rho$。该推论同时识别 Gauss 射线与整个同伦，
所以对数参数保持不变。}
\end{proof}
