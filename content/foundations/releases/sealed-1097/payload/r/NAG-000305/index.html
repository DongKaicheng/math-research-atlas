\begin{proposition}\label{new:affinoidcompact}
\bi{The space $\mathcal M(A)$ is compact and Hausdorff. A bounded homomorphism $A\to B$ induces a continuous map $\mathcal M(B)\to\mathcal M(A)$ and, at each point $y$ over $x$, an isometric embedding $\mathcal H(x)\hookrightarrow\mathcal H(y)$.}
{空间 $\mathcal M(A)$ 是 compact Hausdorff。Bounded homomorphism $A\to B$ 诱导 continuous map $\mathcal M(B)\to\mathcal M(A)$，并在每个位于 $x$ 之上的点 $y$ 处诱导 isometric embedding $\mathcal H(x)\hookrightarrow\mathcal H(y)$。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The product used below is compact by \cite[Tag 08ZU]{StacksAlg}.}
{下述乘积的紧性由 \cite[Tag 08ZU]{StacksAlg} 给出。}
\bi{Embed $\mathcal M(A)$ in $\prod_{a\in A}[0,\|a\|]$ by evaluation. The conditions on the coordinates $s_a$ are $s_0=0$, $s_1=1$, $s_{ab}=s_as_b$, $s_{a+b}\leq\max(s_a,s_b)$, and $s_c=|c|$ for $c\in K$. They are closed conditions. Conversely, a point satisfying them is a bounded multiplicative seminorm. Thus $\mathcal M(A)$ is a closed subspace of a compact Hausdorff product. Its subspace topology is precisely the evaluation topology. This argument proves compactness; nonemptiness for a nonzero affinoid algebra is the separate analytic existence theorem.}
{将 $\mathcal M(A)$ 通过 evaluation 嵌入 $\prod_{a\in A}[0,\|a\|]$。Coordinates $s_a$ 满足 $s_0=0$、$s_1=1$、$s_{ab}=s_as_b$、$s_{a+b}\leq\max(s_a,s_b)$，以及对 $c\in K$ 有 $s_c=|c|$。这些都是 closed conditions。反过来，满足这些条件的点是 bounded multiplicative seminorm。因此 $\mathcal M(A)$ 是 compact Hausdorff product 的 closed subspace；其 subspace topology 正是 evaluation topology。该论证证明 compactness；非零 affinoid algebra 的 spectrum 非空则是另一个 analytic existence theorem。}

\bi{Composition with $A\to B$ preserves the seminorm conditions and is bounded. Each evaluation on $A$ becomes an evaluation on $B$, which proves continuity. Applying Proposition~\ref{new:residueuniversal} to $A\to B\to\mathcal H(y)$ gives the completed-residue-field embedding.}
{与 $A\to B$ 复合后仍满足 seminorm conditions，且仍 bounded。对 $A$ 的每个 evaluation 都变为对 $B$ 的 evaluation，故 map continuous。将 Proposition~\ref{new:residueuniversal} 用于 $A\to B\to\mathcal H(y)$，得到 completed-residue-field embedding。}
\end{proof}

\section{\bi{Seminorms, residue fields and compactness}{Seminorms、residue fields 与 compactness}}\label{new:affinoidfoundations}
\bi{The pointwise language of Berkovich geometry is useful in later proofs only if it is connected to the Banach-algebra constructions. We record that connection in an affinoid domain. Throughout this section, $A$ is a nonzero $K$-affinoid algebra with a submultiplicative Banach norm defining its topology. A bounded multiplicative seminorm $x$ means a multiplicative map $|\cdot|_x:A\to\R_{\geq0}$ extending $|\cdot|_K$, satisfying the ultrametric inequality and an estimate $|a|_x\leq C\|a\|$ for some constant $C$.}
{Berkovich geometry 的 pointwise language 要用于后续证明，就需要与 Banach-algebra constructions 联系起来。本节在 affinoid domain 上建立这一联系。设 $A$ 为非零 $K$-affinoid algebra，配备定义其 topology 的 submultiplicative Banach norm。Bounded multiplicative seminorm $x$ 指延拓 $|\cdot|_K$、满足 ultrametric inequality 的 multiplicative map $|\cdot|_x:A\to\R_{\geq0}$，且存在常数 $C$ 使 $|a|_x\leq C\|a\|$。}

\begin{lemma}\label{new:seminormcontractive}
\bi{Every such seminorm satisfies $|a|_x\leq\|a\|$. Its kernel $\mathfrak p_x$ is prime, and the induced norm on $A/\mathfrak p_x$ extends uniquely to an absolute value on its fraction field.}
{每个这样的 seminorm 都满足 $|a|_x\leq\|a\|$。其 kernel $\mathfrak p_x$ 为 prime，且在 $A/\mathfrak p_x$ 上诱导的 norm 唯一延拓为其 fraction field 上的 absolute value。}

\begin{proposition}\label{new:residueuniversal}
\bi{Let $F/K$ be a complete valued extension, and let $\phi:A\to F$ be a bounded homomorphism. The seminorm $a\mapsto|\phi(a)|$ defines $x\in\mathcal M(A)$. There is a unique isometric embedding $\mathcal H(x)\to F$ compatible with $\phi$. Its image is the closure of the fraction field generated by $\phi(A)$.}
{设 $F/K$ 为 complete valued extension，$\phi:A\to F$ 为 bounded homomorphism。Seminorm $a\mapsto|\phi(a)|$ 定义 $x\in\mathcal M(A)$。存在唯一与 $\phi$ 相容的 isometric embedding $\mathcal H(x)\to F$，其 image 是由 $\phi(A)$ 生成的 fraction field 的 closure。}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\bi{The kernel of $\phi$ is $\mathfrak p_x$, so $\phi$ gives an injective isometry from $A/\mathfrak p_x$ into $F$. It extends to its fraction field by division and then to its completion because $F$ is complete. Isometry gives injectivity after completion. Uniqueness follows from density, as does the description of the image.}
{由定义，$\phi$ 的 kernel 是 $\mathfrak p_x$，因而给出从 $A/\mathfrak p_x$ 到 $F$ 的 injective isometry。先用 division 将其延拓到 fraction field，再利用 $F$ 的 completeness 延拓到 completion。Isometry 保证 completion 后仍 injective；density 给出 uniqueness 及 image 的描述。}
\end{proof}
