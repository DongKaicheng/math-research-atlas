Cohomology comparison for algebraic coherent sheaves

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 403

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Cohomology comparison for algebraic coherent sheaves}
{代数凝聚层的上同调比较}]
\label{fnd:projectivecoherentcomparison}
\bi{For every coherent sheaf \(\mathcal F\) on \(\mathbf P^n_K\),
analytification induces natural isomorphisms}
{对 \(\mathbf P^n_K\) 上的每个凝聚层 \(\mathcal F\)，
解析化诱导自然同构}
\[
 H^q(\mathbf P^n_K,\mathcal F)
   \ \simeq\ H^q((\mathbf P^n_K)^{\an}_G,\mathcal F^{\an})
 \qquad(q\geq0).
\]
\bi{They respect connecting homomorphisms of short exact sequences.}
{这些同构与短正合列的连接同态相容。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use the algebraic standard affine cover \(D_+(X_i)\) and the
analytic unit cover \(U_i\subset D_+(X_i)^{\an}\). Restriction and
scalar extension on each intersection give a map of their ordered
\v Cech complexes. The algebraic complex computes cohomology by
affine acyclicity, and the analytic complex does so by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:finiteaffinoidcohomology}. On every intersection,
sections preserve short exact sequences of coherent sheaves:
on the algebraic side this is the module description on an affine;
on the analytic side it follows from
Theorem~\ref{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact} and affinoid
Theorem B. The resulting comparison therefore commutes with
connecting homomorphisms. These constructions give the usual
cohomology map induced by analytification, since in degree zero
they are restriction of sections and the acyclic resolutions give
their derived maps.}
{使用代数标准仿射覆盖 \(D_+(X_i)\) 与解析单位覆盖
\(U_i\subset D_+(X_i)^{\an}\)。在每个交叠上，限制及扩标量
给出两个有序 \v Cech 复形之间的映射。由仿射无环性，代数
复形计算上同调；由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:finiteaffinoidcohomology}，
解析复形也计算上同调。在每个交叠上，取截面保持凝聚层的
短正合列：代数一侧由仿射上的模描述给出，解析一侧由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact} 与 affinoid
Theorem B 给出。因此所得比较与连接同态相容。零次时这些映射
就是截面限制，而无环解消给出其导出映射，所以这正是解析化
诱导的通常上同调比较。}

\bi{We prove bijectivity by descending induction on \(q\),
simultaneously for all coherent \(\mathcal F\). Both groups vanish
for \(q>n\), by \cite[Tag 01YS, Lemma 30.14.1(2)]{StacksAlg}
and Proposition~\ref{fnd:finiteaffinoidcohomology}. Choose a
surjection \(\mathcal L\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal F\), where
\(\mathcal L\) is a finite direct sum of twists, using
\cite[Tag 01YS, Lemma 30.14.1(1)]{StacksAlg}, and let
\(\mathcal R\) be its coherent kernel. Analytification preserves
this short exact sequence. In its two long exact sequences,
the comparison maps for \(H^q(\mathcal L)\),
\(H^{q+1}(\mathcal R)\), and \(H^{q+1}(\mathcal L)\) are
isomorphisms, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticprojectivetwists}
and the induction hypothesis.}
{对所有凝聚层同时按 \(q\) 作下降归纳。由
\cite[Tag 01YS, Lemma 30.14.1(2)]{StacksAlg}
及 Proposition~\ref{fnd:finiteaffinoidcohomology}，
当 \(q>n\) 时两边均为零。使用
\cite[Tag 01YS, Lemma 30.14.1(1)]{StacksAlg}，选取满射
\(\mathcal L\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal F\)，其中
\(\mathcal L\) 是有限个扭转层的直和，并记其凝聚核为
\(\mathcal R\)。解析化保持这个短正合列。在相应的两个长
正合列中，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticprojectivetwists}
及归纳假设，\(H^q(\mathcal L)\)、
\(H^{q+1}(\mathcal R)\)、\(H^{q+1}(\mathcal L)\)
上的比较均为同构。}

\bi{Here is the required diagram chase. Given an analytic class
\(a\) in degree \(q\) for \(\mathcal F\), lift its connecting
class to the algebraic \(H^{q+1}(\mathcal R)\). Its image in
\(H^{q+1}(\mathcal L)\) is zero by injectivity there, so it
comes from an algebraic class \(b\) of \(\mathcal F\).
The difference between \(a\) and the image of \(b\) comes from
analytic \(H^q(\mathcal L)\), hence from algebraic
\(H^q(\mathcal L)\). This proves surjectivity for every
\(\mathcal F\). Apply that surjectivity also to \(\mathcal R\).
If an algebraic class of \(\mathcal F\) maps to zero analytically,
injectivity in degree \(q+1\) makes it the image of a class
of \(H^q(\mathcal L)\). Analytically this latter class comes
from \(H^q(\mathcal R)\). Lift that class by the surjectivity
just proved and subtract it; injectivity for \(\mathcal L\)
then proves that the original class is zero. This completes
the induction.}
{下面写出所需的图追踪。对 \(\mathcal F\) 的解析 \(q\) 次类
\(a\)，将其连接类提升到代数 \(H^{q+1}(\mathcal R)\)。
由 \(H^{q+1}(\mathcal L)\) 上的单射性，该提升在此群中的像为零，
故来自 \(\mathcal F\) 的一个代数类 \(b\)。
\(a\) 与 \(b\) 的解析像之差来自解析 \(H^q(\mathcal L)\)，
因而也来自代数 \(H^q(\mathcal L)\)，这就证明了对每个
\(\mathcal F\) 的满射性。再将此满射性用于 \(\mathcal R\)。
若 \(\mathcal F\) 的一个代数类在解析一侧为零，由 \(q+1\) 次的
单射性，它来自 \(H^q(\mathcal L)\) 的某个类；后一个类的解析像
来自 \(H^q(\mathcal R)\)。用刚证的满射性提升这一类并相减，
再用 \(\mathcal L\) 上的单射性，就说明原代数类为零。
归纳完成。}
\end{proof}
