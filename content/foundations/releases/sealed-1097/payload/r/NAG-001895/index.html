Full faithfulness on projective space

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 404

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Full faithfulness on projective space}
{射影空间上的全忠实性}]
\label{fnd:projectiveanalytificationfullyfaithful}
\bi{For coherent algebraic sheaves \(\mathcal F,\mathcal G\)
on \(\mathbf P^n_K\), the canonical map}
{对 \(\mathbf P^n_K\) 上的代数凝聚层
\(\mathcal F,\mathcal G\)，典范映射}
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal F,\mathcal G)
 \longrightarrow
 \operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal F^{\an},\mathcal G^{\an})
\]
\bi{is bijective.}
{为双射。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The sheaf \(\mathcal H\!om(\mathcal F,\mathcal G)\)
is coherent: locally a finite presentation of \(\mathcal F\)
expresses it as the kernel of a map between finite sums of
\(\mathcal G\), over a Noetherian ring. Its analytification
is \(\mathcal H\!om(\mathcal F^{\an},\mathcal G^{\an})\)
by Theorem~\ref{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact}. Apply
Theorem~\ref{fnd:projectivecoherentcomparison} in degree zero.
Global sections of these Hom sheaves are precisely the stated
sheaf morphisms, by the definition of the sheaf Hom.}
{层 \(\mathcal H\!om(\mathcal F,\mathcal G)\) 凝聚：
局部地，\(\mathcal F\) 的有限表示将它写成 Noetherian 环上
有限个 \(\mathcal G\) 的直和之间的映射之核。由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact}，
其解析化为
\(\mathcal H\!om(\mathcal F^{\an},\mathcal G^{\an})\)。
对它应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:projectivecoherentcomparison}
的零次情形。由同态层的定义，这两层的全局截面恰为
所列层态射。}
\end{proof}
