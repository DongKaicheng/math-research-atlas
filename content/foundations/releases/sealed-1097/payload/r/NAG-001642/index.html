Lifting rational domains through a closed immersion

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 393

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Lifting rational domains through a closed immersion}{沿闭浸入提升 rational 域}]
\label{fnd:rationalliftclosedimmersion}
\bi{Let $B=R/I$ be a norm-admissible quotient of affinoid algebras. Every rational domain $W\subseteq\mathcal M(B)$ is the inverse image of a rational domain $V\subseteq\mathcal M(R)$. Moreover $B_W=R_V/IR_V$ as Banach algebras.}
{设 $B=R/I$ 为 affinoid 代数的 norm-admissible 商。每个 rational 域 $W\subseteq\mathcal M(B)$ 都是某个 rational 域 $V\subseteq\mathcal M(R)$ 的逆像，且作为 Banach 代数有 $B_W=R_V/IR_V$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Write $W=\{|b_i|\le\rho_i|b_0|\}$, with $(b_0,\ldots,b_m)=B$. Choose lifts $g_i\in R$ and coefficients $a_i\in R$ such that $h=1-\sum_i a_ig_i$ belongs to $I$. For any $\tau>0$, let $V$ be defined by}
{写成 $W=\{|b_i|\le\rho_i|b_0|\}$，其中 $(b_0,\ldots,b_m)=B$。选取提升 $g_i\in R$ 与系数 $a_i\in R$，使 $h=1-\sum_i a_ig_i\in I$。对任意 $\tau>0$，用下列条件定义 $V$：}
\[
 |g_i|\le\rho_i|g_0|\quad(1\le i\le m),\qquad
 |h|\le\tau|g_0|.
\]
\bi{The denominator and all numerators generate the unit ideal, because $1=h+\sum_i a_ig_i$. On $\mathcal M(B)$ the final numerator vanishes and the other conditions become precisely those defining $W$. The base-change formula of Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange} gives the displayed quotient; the ideal $IR_V$ is finitely generated and closed.}
{分母与全部分子生成单位理想，因为 $1=h+\sum_i a_ig_i$。在 $\mathcal M(B)$ 上，最后一个分子为零，其余条件恰好成为 $W$ 的定义条件。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange} 的基变换公式给出所列商；理想 $IR_V$ 有限生成且闭。}
\end{proof}
