A common affine formal model

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 529

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{A common affine formal model}{共同的仿射形式模型}]
\label{fnd:commonaffineformalmodel}
\bi{Two admissible affine formal models of the same strict
$K$-affinoid algebra are dominated by a common admissible affine
model whose morphisms to both are finite admissible blowups.}
{同一个 strict $K$-affinoid 代数的两个 admissible 仿射形式
模型被同一个 admissible 仿射模型支配，且到这两个模型的态射
均为有限 admissible blowup。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Regard the two models $A_0,B_0$ as subrings of their common
generic algebra $A$. If $b_1,\ldots,b_s$ topologically generate
$B_0$, put $C_0=A_0[b_1,\ldots,b_s]$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:powerboundedmodelblowup}
makes this a complete finite model containing $B_0$: any
restricted series in the $b_i$ converges in $C_0$ to its value
in $A$. Thus $C_0=A_0[B_0]$. Reversing the roles gives
$D_0=B_0[A_0]$ by the same argument. Both are the subring
of $A$ generated by $A_0$ and $B_0$, so $C_0=D_0$.
The cited theorem gives both required blowups.}
{把两个模型 $A_0,B_0$ 看作共同泛纤维代数 $A$ 的子环。
若 $b_1,\ldots,b_s$ 拓扑生成 $B_0$，令
$C_0=A_0[b_1,\ldots,b_s]$。由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:powerboundedmodelblowup}，这是一个完备有限
模型，且包含 $B_0$：关于 $b_i$ 的每个限制幂级数都在 $C_0$
中收敛，并具有它在 $A$ 中的值。故 $C_0=A_0[B_0]$。
交换角色同样得到 $D_0=B_0[A_0]$。二者都是 $A$ 中由
$A_0$ 和 $B_0$ 生成的子环，故 $C_0=D_0$。上述定理给出
所需的两个 blowup。}
\end{proof}
