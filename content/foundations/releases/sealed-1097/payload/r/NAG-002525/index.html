Coherent tensor projection

I Descent of the polarized mixed tower

Open in the English reader · PDF page 2117

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Coherent tensor projection]\label{f9ds:tensorprojection}
Let $q:Y\to X$ be norm-admissible and finite. For
$\cF\in\Coh(Y_G)$ and $\cE\in\Coh(X_G)$ there is a canonical isomorphism
\begin{equation}\label{ex68:tensor:eq:tensorprojection}
 q_*(\cF\otimes_{\cO_Y}q^*\cE)
       \simeq q_*\cF\otimes_{\cO_X}\cE.
\end{equation}
It commutes with affinoid-domain restriction and analytic base change.
Neither $\cF$ nor $\cE$ is required to be locally free.
If $X,Y$ are pure of the same analytic dimension $d$ and
$\alpha\in\cA^{d,d}_{X,c}(X)$ is an ordinary smooth moment form, then
\begin{equation}\label{ex68:tensor:eq:integralprojection}
 \int_{Y/K,\,\cF\otimes q^*\cE}q^*\alpha
   =\int_{X/K,\,q_*\cF\otimes\cE}\alpha.
\end{equation}
Both integrals use the fixed ground field $K$.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
On $V=\cM(A)$ and its full inverse image $\cM(B)$, let $M$ and $N$
represent $\cE$ and $\cF$. The map
\[
 N\otimes_B(B\otimes_A M)\longrightarrow N\otimes_A M,\qquad
 n\otimes(b\otimes m)\longmapsto bn\otimes m
\]
has inverse $n\otimes m\mapsto n\otimes(1\otimes m)$.
These are maps of finite $A$-modules, so they are bounded, with bounded
inverses, for the canonical norms. The finite-tensor theorem identifies
the modules with the analytic completed constructions.

For a bounded affinoid base change $A\to C$, put $D=B\otimes_A C$.
The same formulas identify both base changes with
$N\otimes_A M\otimes_A C$. In particular they respect domain restrictions.
The finite coherent direct-image theorem and affinoid coherent equivalence
therefore glue them to \eqref{ex68:tensor:eq:tensorprojection}. This argument concerns
an isomorphism of tensors, and asserts no exactness for tensoring with
an arbitrary $\cE$.
Finally apply the \hyperref[v037:forms:finiteprojection]{fixed-field finite projection theorem} to the coherent module
$\cF\otimes q^*\cE$. It supplies compact support of $q^*\alpha$ and the
integral equality with coefficient $q_*(\cF\otimes q^*\cE)$.
Substitute \eqref{ex68:tensor:eq:tensorprojection}.
\end{proof}
