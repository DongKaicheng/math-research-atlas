Prescribing finitely many residual orders

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 914

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Prescribing finitely many residual orders}
{指定有限个剩余阶数}]
\label{cand:residualfiniteorders}
\bi{Let $C$ be an irreducible component of the special fibre of
$\mathcal X$, and let $p_1,\ldots,p_m$ be distinct closed points of its
normalization $C^\nu$. For each $i$ there is
$u_i\in k(C)^\times$ with}
{设 $C$ 为 $\mathcal X$ 特殊纤维的不可约分量，
$p_1,\ldots,p_m$ 是其正规化 $C^\nu$ 上的互异闭点。对每个
$i$，存在 $u_i\in k(C)^\times$ 使}
\[
 \operatorname{ord}_{p_j}(u_i)=\delta_{ij}.
\]
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The field $k=\widetilde K$ is algebraically closed: lift a
monic polynomial to $K^\circ$ and factor it over $K$; dominance
of the leading term makes every root integral, and reduction
gives the factorization over $k$. Choose a
projective embedding of $C$. A hyperplane avoiding the finitely
many images of the $p_j$ exists: the forbidden coefficients form
a finite union of proper linear subspaces over the infinite field
$k$. Its complement on $C$ is affine. The normalization is finite
by \cite[Tag 0BXR]{StacksAlg}, so its inverse image is an affine
normal curve $\operatorname{Spec}B$ containing all $p_j$. Its
local rings at these points are discrete valuation rings by
\cite[Tag 0B8Y]{StacksAlg}.}
{域 $k=\widetilde K$ 代数闭：将首一多项式提升到
$K^\circ$ 并在 $K$ 上分解，最高次项的严格支配说明其根
全部整，约化便得 $k$ 上的分解。取 $C$ 的 projective 嵌入。
存在避开有限个 $p_j$ 的像的超平面：被排除的系数构成无限域
$k$ 上有限个真线性子空间的并。该超平面在 $C$ 上的补集
仿射。由 \cite[Tag 0BXR]{StacksAlg}，正规化有限，所以
其逆像是包含全部 $p_j$ 的仿射正规曲线
$\operatorname{Spec}B$。由 \cite[Tag 0B8Y]{StacksAlg}，这些
点处的局部环均为离散赋值环。}

\bi{Write $\mathfrak m_j$ for their maximal ideals. A local
uniformizer at $p_i$ can be written $a_i/b_i$, with $a_i,b_i\in B$
and $b_i\notin\mathfrak m_i$; hence $a_i$ has order one at
$p_i$. The ideals $\mathfrak m_i^2$ and $\mathfrak m_j$,
$j\ne i$, are pairwise comaximal. Indeed, powers of comaximal
ideals remain comaximal by expanding a sufficiently high power
of $1=a+b$. For two comaximal ideals $I,J$, write $a+b=1$
with $a\in I,b\in J$; then $br+as$ has prescribed residues
$r$ modulo $I$ and $s$ modulo $J$. Induction gives the same
assertion for finitely many such ideals. Choose $u_i\in B$
congruent to $a_i$ modulo $\mathfrak m_i^2$ and to $1$ modulo
each $\mathfrak m_j$, $j\ne i$. Its orders are exactly those
claimed, so it is a nonzero rational function.}
{记相应极大理想为 $\mathfrak m_j$。$p_i$ 处的局部一致化
参数可写成 $a_i/b_i$，其中 $a_i,b_i\in B$ 且
$b_i\notin\mathfrak m_i$，故 $a_i$ 在 $p_i$ 处阶数为一。
理想 $\mathfrak m_i^2$ 及 $j\ne i$ 的 $\mathfrak m_j$
两两互素：对互素理想中的 $a,b$，将 $1=a+b$ 的充分高次
幂展开，即知它们的任意正整数次幂仍互素。若 $I,J$ 互素，
写 $a+b=1$、$a\in I,b\in J$，则 $br+as$ 在模 $I$、
模 $J$ 下分别具有指定的剩余类 $r,s$。归纳即得有限多个
互素理想的相应结论。选择 $u_i\in B$，使它模
$\mathfrak m_i^2$ 同余于 $a_i$，并在每个 $j\ne i$ 处
模 $\mathfrak m_j$ 同余于 $1$。其阶数恰为所要求者，
故它是非零有理函数。}
\end{proof}
