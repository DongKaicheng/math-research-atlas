\begin{theorem}[The mixed inertia criterion]
\label{cand:f5mix:transitivity}
Assume \hyperref[fnd:mixedtamevaluationringcontext]{(T)}, and let $Y_B/B$ be a supplied projective, flat,
finitely presented nodal curve of relative dimension one with a
semilinear $G$-action. Then the following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ acts identically on the entire scheme $(Y_B)_l$.
\item There is a projective, flat, finitely presented nodal
$O$-scheme $Y$ of relative dimension one with a $G$-equivariant
isomorphism $Y\times_O B\simeq Y_B$, for the coefficient action.
\end{enumerate}
This descent, with its comparison, is unique up to the unique
compatible isomorphism. If a $G$-linearized relatively ample
invertible sheaf $\mathcal L_B$ is supplied, it descends with
its linearization to a relatively ample invertible sheaf on $Y$
if and only if its entire special-fibre $I$-action is the identity.
There is no requirement of identity for the residual $H$-action.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Assume condition 1. Lemma~\ref{cand:f5mix:quotientaction}
constructs $Y_A$ and its semilinear $H$-action. Equation \hyperref[fnd:mixedtamevaluationringcontext]{(10)}
turns this action into an actual descent datum for
$\operatorname{Spec}A\to\operatorname{Spec}O$: the two pullbacks
are identified on the components indexed by $H$, and the group
law is exactly the cocycle condition on the triple product.
Since this base map is surjective finite locally free and
$Y_A/A$ is projective, \hypertarget{foundations.nag003009.stacks.0CCJ.projective-scheme-effectivity}{}\stacks{0CCJ} makes the datum effective
as an ordinary scheme $Y/O$.

Properness and finite presentation descend by \hypertarget{foundations.nag003009.stacks.02L1.properness}{}\stacks{02L1}
and \hypertarget{foundations.nag003009.stacks.02L0.finite-presentation}{}\stacks{02L0}. Flatness and nodality of relative dimension
one descend by \hypertarget{foundations.nag003009.stacks.0C5E.flat-nodal-relative-dimension-one}{}\stacks{0C5E}. To retain projectivity, note that
$q:Y_A\to Y$ is finite locally free and surjective, as a base
change of $\operatorname{Spec}A\to\operatorname{Spec}O$.
The affine base and projectivity give an ample invertible sheaf
on $Y_A$. The finite locally free case of \hypertarget{foundations.nag003009.stacks.0BD4.finite-free-cover-ampleness}{}\stacks{0BD4} gives
an ample invertible sheaf on $Y$. Properness over the affine
base now gives projectivity by \hypertarget{foundations.nag003009.stacks.0B45.projectivity-over-affine-base}{}\stacks{0B45}. No division by
$|H|$ occurs.

Combining $Y\times_O A\simeq Y_A$ with \hyperlink{foundations.nag003008.scheme_component}{(13)} gives the required
$G$-equivariant comparison over $B$. Conversely, in any such
comparison the special fibre is $Y_k\times_k l$. The subgroup
$I$ acts trivially on $l$ by \hyperref[fnd:mixedtamevaluationringcontext]{(9)}, and acts on the coefficient
factor only. Hence it is the identity on the whole special
scheme. This proves necessity.

Suppose now that $\mathcal L_B$ has the stated residual
$I$-identity. \hyperlink{foundations.nag003008.same_bundle_component}{The preceding lemma} gives an $H$-linearized
relatively ample $\mathcal L_A$ on $Y_A$. Equation \hyperref[fnd:mixedtamevaluationringcontext]{(10)}, after
base change to $Y$, makes this a genuine fpqc sheaf descent
datum. Effectivity and full faithfulness in \hypertarget{foundations.nag003009.stacks.023T.same-bundle-effectivity}{}\stacks{023T}
give a quasi-coherent sheaf $\mathcal L$ on $Y$ with the
specified pullback. By \hypertarget{foundations.nag003009.stacks.05B2.same-bundle-finite-local-freeness}{}\stacks{05B2} it is finite locally free;
its rank is one because rank is unchanged by residue-field
extension and $q$ is surjective. It is therefore invertible.
Relative ampleness descends by \hypertarget{foundations.nag003009.stacks.0D2P.same-bundle-relative-ampleness}{}\stacks{0D2P}. Conversely, a
bundle pulled back from $Y$ has identity $I$-action on its full
special-fibre module, for the same coefficient reason as above.
Thus the actual supplied bundle descends, with no further power.

The inertia comparison is unique by the cited invariant descent
and isomorphism functoriality; unramified descent is fully faithful
by \hypertarget{foundations.nag003009.stacks.02W0.scheme-comparison}{}\stacks{02W0}, and sheaf descent by \hypertarget{foundations.nag003009.stacks.023T.same-bundle-comparison}{}\stacks{023T}. Any two
final descents therefore have a unique compatible comparison.
In particular the special scheme $C$ has the resulting full
semilinear $H$-descent datum over $l/k$, and
$Y_k\times_k l\simeq C$ respects it. This datum, rather than
identity of the $H$-action or mere invariance of vertices,
is what is used in the unramified step.
\end{proof}
