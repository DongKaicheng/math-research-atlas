Stabilization and purity of pointed tropical germs

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1062

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Stabilization and purity of pointed tropical germs]
\label{v038:pointed:fullstabilization}
Let $V=\mathcal M(A)$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid
space, $x\in\operatorname{Int}(V/K)$, and
$f=(f_1,\ldots,f_n)$ a tuple of analytic units.
Put $w=t_f(x)$ and
\[
 s=\operatorname{trdeg}_{\widetilde K^{\rm gr}}
   \widetilde K^{\rm gr}
     (\widetilde{f_1(x)},\ldots,\widetilde{f_n(x)}).
\]
There is a finite pure $s$-dimensional rational
polyhedral fan $C_x\subseteq\mathbf R^n$ and an
ordinary affinoid neighbourhood $W_0$ of $x$ such that
for \emph{every} ordinary neighbourhood $U$ of $x$
contained in $W_0$, there is $\varepsilon_U>0$ with
\[
 t_f(U)\cap B_{\varepsilon_U}(w)
                 =(w+C_x)\cap B_{\varepsilon_U}(w).
\]
Thus the pointed moment-image germ is independent of
the choice of sufficiently small ordinary neighbourhood.
Its dimension is exactly $s$, for every complete real-valued
ground field, including the trivial valuation, with the
original absolute value fixed. The statement allows
nonreduced or nonpure $A$.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Let $C_x$ and $W_0$ be supplied by
Lemmas~\ref{v038:pointed:gradedfinitebasis}
and~\ref{v038:pointed:pointedupper}.
The finite upper relation applies to every smaller
neighbourhood $U\subseteq W_0$.

For the opposite inclusion, use
Lemma~\ref{v038:pointed:gaussrelationmodel} to make the
simultaneous weighted Gauss extension with all $n$
radii and its specified point $x_L$ above $x$.
That lemma identifies the ordinary residual Laurent
algebra generated by the normalized initial forms
$\widetilde{f_i/S_i}(x_L)$ with the algebra $B$ of
Lemma~\ref{v038:pointed:gradedfinitebasis}.
Its tropicalization is therefore exactly $C_x$,
and $x_L$ is interior over $L$.

Fix a nonzero vector $q\in C_x$ and a strict rational
open homogeneous sector $S$ containing $q$.
The \hyperref[v037:analytic:normalizedsectors]{normalized sector argument}, applied to
the normalized tuple $h_i=f_i/S_i$ at $x_L$, supplies
points with $t_h$ in $S$ in every ordinary neighbourhood
of $x_L$. To require arbitrarily small displacements,
intersect this neighbourhood with
$t_h^{-1}(B_\delta(0))$ for any $\delta>0$.
For any prescribed ordinary neighbourhood $U$ of $x$
the inverse image of $U$ under $V_L\to V$ is an
ordinary neighbourhood of $x_L$. Projection then gives
points $y\in U$ with $t_f(y)-w\in S$ and
$\|t_f(y)-w\|<\delta$.

Choose an ordinary affinoid neighbourhood $W$ of $x$
contained in $U\cap W_0$. The actual image $t_f(W)$
is a finite integral affine polyhedral set by
Theorem~\ref{v037:globaltrop:finiteimage}. In a
sufficiently small Euclidean ball about $w$, it equals
$w+D_W$ for the finite closed rational polyhedral
tangent fan $D_W$ formed by the cells containing $w$.
By the upper inclusion, $D_W\subseteq C_x$.
If some nonzero $q\in C_x$ were absent from $D_W$,
the finite closed conical set $D_W$ would have a
strict rational open sector about $q$ disjoint from it.
The preceding residue-sector lifting into $W$ would
contradict the local equality with $w+D_W$.
Since $0\in D_W$, this proves $D_W=C_x$.
Hence $t_f(W)$ agrees near $w$ with $w+C_x$.
It is contained in $t_f(U)$, while the upper inclusion
for $U\subseteq W_0$ gives the reverse containment
near $w$. Shrinking the Euclidean ball once more proves
the displayed equality. The finite pure-dimensional
fan calculation in
Lemma~\ref{v038:pointed:gradedfinitebasis} gives
the dimension assertion. Every field extension was
isometric and every projected moment used the original
norm on $K$.
\end{proof}
