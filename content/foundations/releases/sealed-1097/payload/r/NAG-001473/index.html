Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Strict \v Cech exactness for a standard rational family}{标准 rational 族的 strict \v Cech 正合性}]\label{fnd:standardrationalcech}
\bi{Assume that $K$ is nontrivially valued. For every unit-ideal tuple $f_0,\ldots,f_r\in A$ and every finite canonical $A$-module $M$, the augmented ordered \v Cech complex of its standard rational family is strict exact in every degree. The same is true after replacing $A$ by any strict relation quotient and using the image tuple and any finite module over that quotient.}
{假设 $K$ 的赋值非平凡。对每个生成单位理想的 tuple $f_0,\ldots,f_r\in A$ 及每个有限 canonical $A$-模 $M$，其标准 rational 族的 ordered 增广 \v Cech 复形在所有次数都 strict exact。将 $A$ 替换为任意 strict 关系商，并使用 tuple 的像及该商上的任意有限模之后，同一结论仍成立。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First assume that all $f_i$ are units. Use the all-sign Laurent family $\mathcal W$ on the ratios $f_i/f_j$ from Lemma~\ref{fnd:unitstandardlaurentrefinement}. Theorem~\ref{fnd:allsignelementarycech} gives strict acyclicity of $\mathcal W$. It also gives strict acyclicity of $\mathcal W$ restricted to every joint rational presentation $R_J$: the images of the ratios are elements of that strict algebra, and the restricted family is their all-sign family. The module at the base of this restricted complex is the finite $R_J$-module $R_J\otimes_A M$. No tensoring of an exact Banach complex by an arbitrary module is involved.}
{先假设全部 $f_i$ 都是单位。使用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:unitstandardlaurentrefinement} 中对比值 $f_i/f_j$ 构造的 all-sign Laurent 族 $\mathcal W$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:allsignelementarycech} 给出 $\mathcal W$ 的 strict 无环性。它也给出 $\mathcal W$ 在每个联合 rational 表示 $R_J$ 上的限制的 strict 无环性：这些比值的像是该 strict 代数中的元素，限制后的族正是它们的 all-sign 族。此限制复形底部的模为有限 $R_J$-模 $R_J\otimes_A M$。这里没有将正合 Banach 复形与任意模作张量积。}

\bi{On every nonempty joint presentation of members of $\mathcal W$, select one of its members $W$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:unitstandardlaurentrefinement} supplies $k(W)$, and Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalredundantconstraint} identifies adjoining $R_{k(W)}$ with the identity on this joint presentation, isometrically. Thus the restricted rational family contains an identity member. Inserting its index contracts its augmented \v Cech complex; the maps are bounded, and in the matched quotient norms have bound one. The common-refinement comparison, Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictfinitecechcomparison}, now applies in both directions: the Laurent columns are strict exact, and the rational rows on their intersections are contracted. It identifies the two augmentations through the common double complex and proves strict exactness for $(R_i)_i$. These are the two local acyclicity inputs required for the comparison. Equivalently, one can apply Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictacyclicrefinement}(1) to this refinement.}
{对 $\mathcal W$ 中成员的每个非空联合表示，选取其中一个成员 $W$。Lemma~\ref{fnd:unitstandardlaurentrefinement} 给出 $k(W)$；由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalredundantconstraint}，再添入 $R_{k(W)}$ 与该联合表示上的恒等操作等距识别。因此，限制后的 rational 族包含一个恒等成员。插入该指标即可收缩其增广 \v Cech 复形；这些映射均有界，在匹配的商范数下其 bound 为一。现在向两个方向应用 common-refinement 比较，即 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictfinitecechcomparison}：Laurent 列 strict exact，而其交叠上的 rational 行具有上述收缩。通过共同的双复形识别两个增广，得到 $(R_i)_i$ 的 strict exactness。这正是比较所需的两个局部无环性输入。等价地，可对该加细应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictacyclicrefinement}(1)。}

\bi{For a general unit-ideal tuple, take the first-level all-sign family $\mathcal V$ of Lemma~\ref{fnd:standardsmallscalarrefinement}. It and its restriction to every $R_J$ are strict acyclic by Theorem~\ref{fnd:allsignelementarycech}, applied afresh over that strict algebra and its finite module. On any nonempty intersection of members of $\mathcal V$, choose one member and its sets $P,N$. The explicit zero identities from Lemma~\ref{fnd:standardsmallscalarrefinement} remain valid there. The terms indexed by $P$ can therefore be discarded, and every term involving one of those indices is zero. The remaining rational family is the standard family on the units $(f_i)_{i\in N}$, by the same lemma and its intersection compatibility; some of its members may also be zero. Its complex is strict exact by the unit case just proved. If the intersection algebra is zero, all its modules and complexes are zero. The same common-refinement comparison therefore proves the theorem for the original tuple.}
{对一般的单位理想 tuple，使用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:standardsmallscalarrefinement} 的第一层 all-sign 族 $\mathcal V$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:allsignelementarycech}，它及其在每个 $R_J$ 上的限制都 strict acyclic；在后者情形，重新对该 strict 代数及其有限模应用定理。对 $\mathcal V$ 中成员的任意非空交叠，选取其中一个成员及其指标集 $P,N$。Lemma~\ref{fnd:standardsmallscalarrefinement} 中的显式零商恒等式在该交叠上仍有效，因此可以移除由 $P$ 标记的 rational 项，且每个包含这些指标的项均为零。由同一引理及其交叠相容性，剩余 rational 族是单位 $(f_i)_{i\in N}$ 的标准族；其中仍可能有零成员。它的复形由刚证明的单位情形 strict exact。若交叠代数为零，其全部模及复形均为零。因此再应用同一个 common-refinement 比较，即证明原 tuple 的结论。}

\bi{Every application uses finitely many complexes in each relevant degree. The comparison theorem supplies uniform bounds for their successive lifts, which is the norm-strictness assertion; algebraic exactness alone would not supply those bounds. All auxiliary algebras are verified strict actual quotients by Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictweightedquotient}. Replacing $A$ by such an algebra merely repeats the argument, and equivalent canonical module norms preserve strict exactness.}
{每次应用在每个相关次数中都只涉及有限个复形。比较定理为逐次提升给出统一界，这正是 norm-strictness 的来源；仅有代数正合性并不能给出这些界。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictweightedquotient}，全部辅助代数都是已验证的 strict 实际商。将 $A$ 替换为这样的代数，只是重复同一论证；等价的 canonical 模范数保持 strict exactness。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX

\bi{We work over a complete non-Archimedean field $K$, allowing trivial valuation except in the small-scalar lemmas where nontriviality is stated. Every nonzero algebra has a strict norm-admissible Banach norm, and every relation quotient has its actual quotient norm. The zero algebra is admitted separately with its zero norm. All radii in this section are one; a radius in $|K^\times|$ can first be reduced to this convention by scaling the corresponding numerator. These conventions concern the displayed presentations, not a spectral norm.}
{本节在完备非阿基米德域 $K$ 上工作，允许赋值平凡；使用小标量的引理会明确要求赋值非平凡。每个非零代数配备 strict norm-admissible Banach 范数，每个关系商配备实际商范数。零代数另以零范数允许在内。本节所有半径均为一；值群 $|K^\times|$ 中的半径可先通过缩放相应分子化为这一约定。这些约定涉及所列表示，并不使用 spectral 范数。}

\bi{For a tuple $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$ generating the unit ideal of $A$, write}
{对生成 $A$ 的单位理想的 tuple $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$，记}
\[
 A\langle f/g\rangle
   =A\{T_1,\ldots,T_s\}/(gT_j-f_j)_{1\leq j\leq s}.
\]
\bi{For a unit-ideal tuple $f_0,\ldots,f_r$, its \emph{standard rational family} consists of the $r+1$ presentations}
{对生成单位理想的 tuple $f_0,\ldots,f_r$，其\emph{标准 rational 族}由以下 $r+1$ 个表示组成：}
\[
 R_i=A\{T_j:j\ne i\}/(f_iT_j-f_j)_{j\ne i},
 \qquad 0\leq i\leq r.
\]
\bi{We put $T_i=1$ when using a formula involving all indices. The joint presentation of a finite family means the quotient with disjoint variable blocks and all their relations. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpresentationpushout} identifies it with the separated completed tensor product. Its module attached to a finite $A$-module $M$ is its ordinary tensor with $M$, equipped with the quotient norm from a fixed finite free presentation of $M$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} gives its comparison with the completed tensor. These choices specify the arrows and norms before a geometric sheaf is available.}
{若某个公式涉及全部指标，约定 $T_i=1$。有限族的联合表示，是指添入互不相交的变量组并施加全部关系后所得的商。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpresentationpushout} 将它与 separated completed tensor product 识别。对有限 $A$-模 $M$，相应模是该商与 $M$ 的普通张量积，并配备来自 $M$ 的一个固定有限自由表示的商范数。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} 给出它与 completed tensor 的比较。因此，在尚未构造几何层之前，所有箭头及范数已有明确含义。}
