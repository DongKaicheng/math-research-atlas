Closed reduction points and absolute interior

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 617

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Closed reduction points and absolute interior}
{约化闭点与绝对内点}]
\label{fnd:strictabsoluteinteriorreduction}
\bi{Let $A$ be a strict affinoid algebra over a complete
non-Archimedean field $K$, with its specified absolute value.
For $X=\mathcal M(A)$, the canonical reduction map satisfies}
{设 $A$ 为完备非阿基米德域 $K$ 上的 strict affinoid 代数，
保持指定绝对值。对 $X=\mathcal M(A)$，典范约化映射满足}
\[
 \operatorname{Int}(X/K)=
 \operatorname{red}^{-1}\bigl((\operatorname{Spec}\widetilde A)_{
 \mathrm{closed}}\bigr).
\]
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Fix $x\in X$, put $k=\widetilde K$ and $R=\widetilde A$,
and let $S$ be the image of $\widetilde A^{\rm gr}$ in
$\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}^{\rm gr}$. Its degree-one part is
$S_1=R/\mathfrak p$, where $\mathfrak p=\operatorname{red}(x)$.
The \hyperref[fnd:gradedabsoluteevaluationforward]{forward} and
\hyperref[fnd:gradedabsoluteevaluationreverse]{reverse} implications
for absolute evaluation show that $A\to\mathcal H(x)$ is inner over
$K$ precisely when $S$ is finite over $\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$.
We show this is equivalent to $S_1$ being finite-dimensional over $k$.}
{固定 $x\in X$，令 $k=\widetilde K$、$R=\widetilde A$，
并以 $S$ 记 $\widetilde A^{\rm gr}$ 在
$\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}^{\rm gr}$ 中的像。其一次部分为
$S_1=R/\mathfrak p$，其中 $\mathfrak p=\operatorname{red}(x)$。
绝对求值的\hyperref[fnd:gradedabsoluteevaluationforward]{正向}和
\hyperref[fnd:gradedabsoluteevaluationreverse]{反向}蕴涵表明：
$A\to\mathcal H(x)$ 在 $K$ 上为 inner，当且仅当 $S$ 在
$\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$ 上有限。下面证明这等价于 $S_1$ 在 $k$ 上有限维。}

\bi{A finite graded module has finitely many homogeneous
generators. Every homogeneous component of the graded field
$\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$ has dimension at most one over $k$.
Thus finiteness of $S$ implies finite dimension of $S_1$.
Conversely, choose an admissible absolute weighted Tate presentation of $A$.
The \hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{fixed absolute presentation result}
gives finitely many homogeneous module generators over the image of the
initial-coordinate polynomial algebra. Taking their images in $S$ together
with the initial coordinates gives finitely many homogeneous algebra generators
$u_j$ over $\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$. Discard the zero generators.
The degree $r_j$ of each remaining generator is the spectral
radius of a representative in $A$, since its initial form has
nonzero image. Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictspectralvaluegroup} gives
$e_j>0$ and $c_j\in K^\times$ with $r_j^{e_j}=|c_j|$.
Hence $u_j^{e_j}/\widetilde c_j\in S_1$.
If $S_1$ is finite-dimensional over $k$, the algebra
$\widetilde K^{\rm gr}S_1$ is finite over
$\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$, and every $u_j$ is integral over it.
Adjoining these finitely many integral generators proves that
$S$ is finite over $\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$.}
{有限分次模可取有限个齐次生成元，而分次域
$\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$ 的每个齐次分量在 $k$ 上至多一维。
因此 $S$ 有限推出 $S_1$ 有限维。反之，取 $A$ 的一个 admissible
绝对带权 Tate 表示。\hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{固定绝对表示的结论}
给出首项坐标多项式代数的像上的有限个齐次模生成元。
它们在 $S$ 中的像与首项坐标一起给出 $S$ 在
$\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$ 上有限个齐次代数生成元 $u_j$。删去零生成元。其余 $u_j$ 的次数 $r_j$ 等于 $A$ 中一个代表元
的谱半径，因为其首项的像非零。由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictspectralvaluegroup}，存在 $e_j>0$ 及
$c_j\in K^\times$，使 $r_j^{e_j}=|c_j|$。于是
$u_j^{e_j}/\widetilde c_j\in S_1$。
若 $S_1$ 在 $k$ 上有限维，则
$\widetilde K^{\rm gr}S_1$ 在 $\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$ 上有限，
且各 $u_j$ 都在它上面整。依次添入有限个这样的整生成元，
就得到 $S$ 在 $\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$ 上有限。}

\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictcanonicalfinitegeneration} makes
$R$ a finite-type $k$-algebra. By the Nullstellensatz
\cite[Tag 00FV]{StacksAlg}, $R/\mathfrak p$ is finite over
$k$ when $\mathfrak p$ is maximal. Conversely, a
finite-dimensional domain over a field is a field: multiplication
by any nonzero element is an injective linear endomorphism,
hence surjective. Thus $S_1$ is finite-dimensional exactly
when $\mathfrak p$ is a closed point.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictcanonicalfinitegeneration} 说明 $R$
为有限型 $k$-代数。由 Nullstellensatz
\cite[Tag 00FV]{StacksAlg}，若 $\mathfrak p$ 极大，则
$R/\mathfrak p$ 在 $k$ 上有限。反之，域上的有限维整环必为域：
任何非零元素的乘法是单射线性自同态，因而满射。
所以 $S_1$ 有限维恰好等价于 $\mathfrak p$ 为闭点。}
\end{proof}
