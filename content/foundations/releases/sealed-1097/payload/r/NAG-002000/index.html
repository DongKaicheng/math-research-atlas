A product patch from a rational fibre point

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 451

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{A product patch from a rational fibre point}{由有理纤维点获得乘积开片}]
\label{fnd:rationalfibresmoothproductpatch}
\bi{Let $Y\to X$ be smooth of pure relative dimension $n$ between good spaces. If the fibre over $x$ has a point $y$ with $\mathcal H(y)=\mathcal H(x)$, then $Y$ has a nonempty ordinary open subset, over an ordinary neighborhood $U$ of $x$, that is $X$-isomorphic to $U\times_K D$, where $D$ is an $n$-dimensional open polydisc. The subset can be chosen inside any prescribed ordinary neighborhood of $y$, but need not contain $y$.}
{设 $Y\to X$ 是 good 空间之间纯相对维数为 $n$ 的光滑态射。若 $x$ 处纤维含有点 $y$，满足 $\mathcal H(y)=\mathcal H(x)$，则在 $x$ 的某普通邻域 $U$ 上，$Y$ 有非空普通开集在 $X$ 上同构于 $U\times_K D$，其中 $D$ 为 $n$ 维开多圆盘。此开集可选择在 $y$ 的任意预定普通邻域内，但不要求包含 $y$。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Restrict to the prescribed neighborhood and take an \'etale coordinate map $Y\to\mathbb A_X^{n,\an}$ near $y$, as supplied by Theorem~\ref{fnd:goodsmoothquasismoothinterior}. If $z$ is the image of $y$, the inclusions $\mathcal H(x)\subset\mathcal H(z)\subset\mathcal H(y)$ force equality. Corollary~\ref{fnd:etalesameresiduelocalisomorphism} therefore identifies an ordinary neighborhood of $y$ with an ordinary open set $W$ in relative affine space. The fibre $W_x$ contains a point with coordinates in $F=\mathcal H(x)$. Since $\kappa(x)$ is dense in $F$, there is a point $z'$ in this same fibre open set all of whose coordinates belong to $\kappa(x)$: only finitely many strict polynomial-value conditions need to be preserved. Represent these coordinates by germs on $X$, shrink to an ordinary neighborhood where they are defined, and translate the corresponding relative affine coordinates. The translated open set contains the fibre origin. Lemma~\ref{fnd:freegaussproductneighborhood}, with $m=0$, now supplies the desired product. Translating back and using the \'etale local isomorphism completes the proof.}
{先限制到给定邻域，并在 $y$ 附近取 Theorem~\ref{fnd:goodsmoothquasismoothinterior} 给出的 \'etale 坐标映射 $Y\to\mathbb A_X^{n,\an}$。若 $z$ 为 $y$ 的像，则包含关系 $\mathcal H(x)\subset\mathcal H(z)\subset\mathcal H(y)$ 强迫三者相等。因此，由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:etalesameresiduelocalisomorphism}，$y$ 的某普通邻域与相对仿射空间中的普通开集 $W$ 同构。纤维 $W_x$ 含有一个坐标属于 $F=\mathcal H(x)$ 的点。由于 $\kappa(x)$ 在 $F$ 中稠密，$W_x$ 还含有一个全部坐标属于 $\kappa(x)$ 的点 $z'$：只须保持有限个严格的多项式值条件。用 $X$ 上的芽表示这些坐标，缩小到其有定义的普通邻域，再平移相应相对仿射坐标。平移后的开集包含纤维原点，故 Lemma~\ref{fnd:freegaussproductneighborhood} 在 $m=0$ 的情形给出所需乘积。最后平移回来并使用 \'etale 局部同构，即得结论。}
\end{proof}
