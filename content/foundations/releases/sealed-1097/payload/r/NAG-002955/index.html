Fixed-test stabilization; F8SA-E

I Descent of the polarized mixed tower

Open in the English reader · PDF page 2068

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Fixed-test stabilization; F8SA-E]\label{prop:cuts}
Assume IL. For every fixed compact ordinary top form \(\beta\) supported
in \(C\), there is \(R_\beta\) such that the continuous truncation
\(\ell_R=\max(\log|f|,-R)\), extended by \(-R\) at \(D\), satisfies
\[
 \ell_R\beta=\log|f|\,\beta\quad(R\geq R_\beta),
\]
where the right side is extended by zero across a neighbourhood on which
\(\beta=0\). These sufficiently deep products are ordinary smooth
compact forms, and their scalar integrals are exactly equal.

More specifically, fix a smooth \(\chi\) equal to one sufficiently
far left and zero for \(x\geq b_0\). Set
\(\eta_1=\chi(\log|f|)\eta\), extending \(\chi(\log|f|)\) by one
at \(D\). Given \(b>b_0\), there exists finite \(a_*<b_0\) such
that for every \(a\leq a_*\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:finitecut}
 L_f(\dd\eta_1)=\int_{Q\cap\{e^a\leq|f|\leq e^b\}}
                          \log|f|\,\dd\eta_1.
\end{equation}
Both \(d''\eta_1\) and \(\dd\eta_1\) vanish on an ordinary
neighbourhood of the removed lower cap.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
IL [\hyperref[prop:log]{NAG-002806}] makes the closed compact support of \(\beta\) disjoint from
\(D\). Hence \(|f|\) has a positive minimum on that support.
Choose the cut strictly below this minimum. Outside the support the
form is locally zero, so the asserted equality is equality of ordinary
forms after zero extension, not just of integrals.

The cut scalar is smooth near \(D\), because it is locally constant
there; away from \(D\), \(f\) is a unit and its logarithm is an
ordinary smooth moment function. Define its product with \(\eta\)
on \(U\), and extend by zero on \(X\setminus C\). These definitions
agree on their ordinary open overlap. All resulting supports remain
in \(C\). Apply IL [\hyperref[prop:log]{NAG-002806}] to the two indicated derivatives. Their compact
supports avoid \(D\), so one positive lower bound for \(|f|\)
works for both. Choose \(a_*\) strictly below its logarithm and
in the constant-one range of \(\chi\). They vanish on a neighbourhood
of \(Q\cap\{|f|\leq e^{a_*}\}\). They also vanish wherever
\(\log|f|>b_0\). Compact-neighbourhood independence of the frozen
integral [\hyperref[v037:forms:compactintegral]{NAG-002425}] now gives \eqref{eq:finitecut}, including its closed endpoints.
\end{proof}
