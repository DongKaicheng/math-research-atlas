Boundary points of a closed-point tube

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 671

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Boundary points of a closed-point tube}{闭点管域的边界点}]
\label{cand:closedtubeboundary}
\bi{Let $q$ be a closed point of the special fibre of a curve model
$\mathcal X$, and put $D_q=\operatorname{sp}^{-1}(q)$ in
$X=\widehat{\mathcal X}_\eta$. Then $D_q$ is open in $X$, its closure
$C_q$ is compact, and}
{设 $q$ 为曲线模型 $\mathcal X$ 的特殊纤维闭点，并在
$X=\widehat{\mathcal X}_\eta$ 中令
$D_q=\operatorname{sp}^{-1}(q)$。则 $D_q$ 在 $X$ 中开，
其闭包 $C_q$ 紧，且}
\[
 C_q\setminus D_q\ \subseteq\
 \{v_\eta:q\in\overline{\{\eta\}}\}.
\]
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose an affine open $\mathcal U$ containing $q$. Formal-open
compatibility in Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}
puts the entire tube $D_q$ inside the compact affinoid
$U=\widehat{\mathcal U}_\eta$. Since $X$ is Hausdorff, $U$ is closed
in $X$, so $C_q$ is a closed subset of $U$ and is compact.
On every formal affine chart, the inverse image of the closed residual
point $q$, when it is present, is given by finitely many inequalities
$|f_i|<1$; use Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalstratumtubes} and the
Noetherian special fibre. These inverse images are relatively open.
The affinoid net topology of
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful} therefore makes
$D_q$ open in $X$.}
{取包含 $q$ 的仿射开集 $\mathcal U$。
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful} 的形式开集相容性
把整个 $D_q$ 放在紧 affinoid 域
$U=\widehat{\mathcal U}_\eta$ 中。由于 $X$ Hausdorff，$U$
在 $X$ 中闭，故 $C_q$ 为 $U$ 的闭子集，从而紧。在每个形式
仿射图上，若闭点 $q$ 属于该图，其逆像由有限个不等式
$|f_i|<1$ 给出；这里使用
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalstratumtubes} 及特殊纤维的
Noetherian 性。这些逆像相对开，因而
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful} 的 affinoid 网拓扑
说明 $D_q$ 在 $X$ 中开。}

\bi{For $x\in C_q$, write $p=\operatorname{sp}(x)$ as a prime of
the affine special fibre. If $p\not\subseteq\mathfrak m_q$, choose
$\bar f\in p\setminus\mathfrak m_q$ and an integral lift $f$.
Then $|f(x)|<1$, whereas $|f(y)|=1$ for every $y\in D_q$.
The relative open set $|f|<1$ separates $x$ from $D_q$, a contradiction.
Thus $p\subseteq\mathfrak m_q$. In a pure one-dimensional
Noetherian scheme the proper generalizations of a closed point are
component generic points. Their analytic fibres are the singletons
$\{v_\eta\}$ by Proposition~\ref{cand:reducedmodelcomponentpoints}.
This proves the inclusion.}
{对 $x\in C_q$，把 $p=\operatorname{sp}(x)$ 看作仿射特殊纤维
中的素理想。若 $p\not\subseteq\mathfrak m_q$，选择
$\bar f\in p\setminus\mathfrak m_q$ 及其整系数提升 $f$。
此时 $|f(x)|<1$，但对全部 $y\in D_q$ 均有 $|f(y)|=1$。
相对开集 $|f|<1$ 将 $x$ 与 $D_q$ 分开，矛盾。因此
$p\subseteq\mathfrak m_q$。在纯一维 Noetherian 概形中，闭点的
真泛化都是分量泛点。由
Proposition~\ref{cand:reducedmodelcomponentpoints}，其解析纤维
恰为单点 $\{v_\eta\}$，从而得到所需包含关系。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Throughout this section $K$ is complete and nontrivially valued,
$O=K^\circ$ and $k=\widetilde K$. A curve model is a flat finitely
presented $O$-scheme whose special fibre is a reduced pure
one-dimensional scheme. Its formal generic fibre is assumed Hausdorff.
We write $v_\eta$ for the unique analytic point above a component
generic point $\eta$, as in Proposition~\ref{cand:reducedmodelcomponentpoints}.}
{本节中 $K$ 完备且非平凡赋值，$O=K^\circ$、$k=\widetilde K$。
曲线模型是特殊纤维为约化纯一维概形的平坦有限表示 $O$-概形，
并假设其形式泛纤维为 Hausdorff 空间。按照
Proposition~\ref{cand:reducedmodelcomponentpoints}，以 $v_\eta$
表示分量泛点 $\eta$ 上方的唯一解析点。}
