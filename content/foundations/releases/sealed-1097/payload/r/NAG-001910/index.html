Graded polynomial and free scalar extension

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 515

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Graded polynomial and free scalar extension}
{分次多项式与自由标量扩张}]
\label{fnd:gradedfreebasechange}
\bi{There are canonical graded algebra isomorphisms}
{存在典范分次代数同构}
\[
 \widetilde A^{\mathrm{gr}}[t_1,\ldots,t_m]
     \simeq\widetilde{A\{r^{-1}T\}}^{\mathrm{gr}},
 \qquad \deg t_i=r_i.
\]
\bi{When $r$ is free over $K$, there are also canonical isomorphisms}
{当 $r$ 在 $K$ 上自由时，另有典范同构}
\[
 \widetilde A^{\mathrm{gr}}\otimes_{\widetilde K^{\mathrm{gr}}}
                  \widetilde {K_r}^{\mathrm{gr}}
 \simeq \widetilde A^{\mathrm{gr}}[t_1^{\pm1},\ldots,t_m^{\pm1}]
 \simeq\widetilde{A_r}^{\mathrm{gr}}.
\]
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{In a homogeneous class of degree $s$, only finitely many terms of a
convergent series have spectral size $s$; all remaining terms together
have spectral size strictly less than $s$. This follows because their
coefficient norms tend to zero. Taking the initial classes of the
maximizing coefficients therefore proves surjectivity of the displayed
polynomial or Laurent map. Conversely, a nonzero homogeneous polynomial
of degree $s$ has a coefficient with nonzero class at its prescribed
degree. The formula in Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedcoefficientspectral}
makes the spectral radius of any corresponding lift exactly $s$.
Its image cannot vanish. This proves injectivity, and multiplication
respects the initial classes by the filtered-algebra construction.}
{次数为 $s$ 的齐次类中，一个收敛级数只有有限项具有谱大小 $s$，
其余各项之和的谱大小严格小于 $s$；这是因为系数范数趋于零。
取达到最大值的系数的首项类，便证明所列多项式或 Laurent 映射满射。
反之，次数为 $s$ 的非零齐次多项式至少有一个系数，在其规定次数上
具有非零首项类。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedcoefficientspectral}，
任何对应提升的谱半径恰为 $s$，故其像不可能为零。
这就证明单射性；乘法相容性来自滤过代数的构造。}

\bi{For $K_r$ itself, the same coefficient calculation gives}
{对 $K_r$ 本身，同一系数计算给出}
\[
 \widetilde {K_r}^{\mathrm{gr}}
 =\widetilde K^{\mathrm{gr}}[t_1^{\pm1},\ldots,t_m^{\pm1}].
\]
\bi{
The freeness condition is the one used to construct the complete field
$K_r$. Tensoring this polynomial description with
$\widetilde A^{\mathrm{gr}}$ gives the first isomorphism in the second line.}
{这里的自由性正是构造完备域 $K_r$ 时的条件。
将这一 Laurent 多项式描述与 $\widetilde A^{\mathrm{gr}}$ 张量，
得到第二行的第一个同构。}
\end{proof}
