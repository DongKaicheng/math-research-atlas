Regular localizations on relative analytic domains

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Regular localizations on relative analytic domains}{相对解析域上的正则局部化}]
\label{fnd:relativeanalyticdomainregularlocalizations}
\bi{Let $A$ be generalized affinoid, let $R$ be a finite-type $A$-algebra, and let $V=\mathcal M(C)$ be an affinoid domain in $(\operatorname{Spec}R)^{\an}$. For a prime $\mathfrak q\subset C$, write $\mathfrak p=\mathfrak q\cap R$. If $R_{\mathfrak p}$ is regular, then $C_{\mathfrak q}$ is regular.}
{设 $A$ 为 generalized affinoid 代数，$R$ 为有限型 $A$-代数，$V=\mathcal M(C)$ 为 $(\operatorname{Spec}R)^{\an}$ 中的 affinoid 域。对素理想 $\mathfrak q\subset C$，令 $\mathfrak p=\mathfrak q\cap R$。若 $R_{\mathfrak p}$ 正则，则 $C_{\mathfrak q}$ 正则。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose an analytic point $v\in V$ with kernel $\mathfrak q$, by Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity}. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalaffinoidexcellence} makes $A$ excellent, so its $J$-2 property says that the regular locus of $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ is open. Choose $g\notin\mathfrak p$ such that $R[1/g]$ is regular. Choose a nontrivially valued free extension $L=K_r$ making both $A_L$ and $C_L$ strict, and lift $v$ to a point $w$ of $V_L$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:gaussanalyticseparable} and Proposition~\ref{fnd:analyticseparablescalarregular} show that $R_L[1/g]$, where $R_L=R\otimes_A A_L$, is regular.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity}，选取核为 $\mathfrak q$ 的解析点 $v\in V$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalaffinoidexcellence} 说明 $A$ 优秀，故其 $J$-2 性给出 $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ 的正则点集开放。选择 $g\notin\mathfrak p$，使 $R[1/g]$ 正则。选取赋值非平凡的自由扩张 $L=K_r$，使 $A_L,C_L$ 都 strict，再把 $v$ 提升为 $V_L$ 中的点 $w$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gaussanalyticseparable} 及 Proposition~\ref{fnd:analyticseparablescalarregular}，$R_L[1/g]$ 正则，其中 $R_L=R\otimes_A A_L$。}

\bi{By Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictordinaryneighborhoods}, take a strict affinoid ordinary neighborhood $W=\mathcal M(D)$ of $w$ in the open set $V_L\cap D(g)^{\an}$. For every maximal ideal $\mathfrak n\subset D$, the strict point theorem gives an $L$-rigid point $t\in W$ with that kernel. Lemma~\ref{fnd:relativerigidcompletioncomparison} identifies $\widehat{D_{\mathfrak n}}$ with the completion of the local ring of $\operatorname{Spec}(R_L[1/g])$ at the image of $t$. This algebraic local ring is regular. The regularity/completion criterion, Tag 07NY, shows first that its completion is regular and then that $D_{\mathfrak n}$ is regular. Since every prime lies under a maximal ideal and localization preserves regularity, $D$ is regular.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictordinaryneighborhoods}，可在开集 $V_L\cap D(g)^{\an}$ 中选取 $w$ 的 strict affinoid 普通邻域 $W=\mathcal M(D)$。对每个极大理想 $\mathfrak n\subset D$，strict 点定理给出核为它的 $L$-rigid 点 $t\in W$。Lemma~\ref{fnd:relativerigidcompletioncomparison} 将 $\widehat{D_{\mathfrak n}}$ 识别为 $\operatorname{Spec}(R_L[1/g])$ 在 $t$ 的像处的局部环完备化。该代数局部环正则。由正则性与完备化判据 Tag 07NY，其完备化正则，进而 $D_{\mathfrak n}$ 正则。每个素理想都包含于某个极大理想，且局部化保持正则性，因此 $D$ 正则。}

\bi{The composite $C\to C_L\to D$ is flat by scalar flatness and domain flatness. At the kernel $\mathfrak n_w$ of $w$ on $D$, its contraction to $C$ is $\mathfrak q$. Thus $C_{\mathfrak q}\to D_{\mathfrak n_w}$ is a flat local map with regular target. Tag 00OF descends regularity and proves the assertion.}
{复合 $C\to C_L\to D$ 由标量平坦性及域平坦性为平坦映射。$w$ 在 $D$ 上的核 $\mathfrak n_w$ 收缩到 $C$ 恰为 $\mathfrak q$，所以 $C_{\mathfrak q}\to D_{\mathfrak n_w}$ 是目标正则的平坦局部映射。Tag 00OF 下降正则性，即得结论。}
\end{proof}
