A model determined by its specialization map

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 750

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[A model determined by its specialization map]
\label{v035:f5:specializationdeterminesmodel}
Let $\mathfrak X_1$ and $\mathfrak X_2$ be admissible formal models
of the same analytic space $X$, with reduced special fibres. Suppose
there is a homeomorphism $h:|\mathfrak X_{1,s}|\to|\mathfrak X_{2,s}|$
such that $h\circ\operatorname{sp}_1=\operatorname{sp}_2$.
There is a unique formal isomorphism inducing $h$ and the identity on $X$.
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The underlying formal topology equals the special-fibre topology:
on an affine chart, rank one gives
$\sqrt{\pi A_0}=\sqrt{\mathfrak m A_0}$, as in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}.
Corresponding formal opens under $h$ have the same generic domain.
Proposition~\ref{v035:f5:boundedsections} identifies their rings of
sections, restrictions, and $\pi^n$ ideals. These identifications give
an isomorphism of locally ringed spaces and recover the local adic
topologies. Its generic fibre is the identity. Uniqueness follows
from the faithfulness in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
Let $K$ be a complete nontrivially real-valued non-Archimedean field,
with its specified absolute value. Write $O=K^\circ$,
$\mathfrak m=K^{\circ\circ}$, and $k=O/\mathfrak m$, and choose
$\pi\in O$ with $0<|\pi|<1$. All formal schemes below are
$\pi$-adic. An admissible formal algebra is understood to be
$\pi$-torsion-free and topologically finitely presented over $O$.
For a formal open $\mathfrak U\subset\mathfrak X$, put
$D_{\mathfrak U}=\operatorname{sp}^{-1}(\mathfrak U)$, with its
analytic $G$-domain structure.
