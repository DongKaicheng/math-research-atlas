A punctured closed polydisc is not good

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 608

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{A punctured closed polydisc is not good}
{去掉开多圆盘后的闭多圆盘不是 good 的}]
\label{fnd:puncturedpolydiscnongood}
\bi{Let $K$ be any complete non-Archimedean field, possibly
trivially valued, let $n\ge2$, and let $r_1,\ldots,r_n>0$.
In}
{设 $K$ 为任意完备非阿基米德域，允许平凡赋值，$n\ge2$，
且 $r_1,\ldots,r_n>0$。在}
\[
 X=\mathcal M(K\{r_1^{-1}T_1,\ldots,r_n^{-1}T_n\})
\]
\bi{consider the subset}
{中考虑子集}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 U&=X\setminus\{z:|T_i(z)|<r_i\text{ for every }i\}
   =\bigcup_{i=1}^n U_i,\\
 U_i&=\{z\in X:|T_i(z)|=r_i\}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{Then $U$ is a compact Hausdorff analytic domain of $X$.
At the weighted Gauss point
$x(\sum_\nu a_\nu T^\nu)=\max_\nu|a_\nu|r^\nu$,
the space $U$ has no affinoid neighbourhood.}
{则 $U$ 是 $X$ 的紧 Hausdorff 解析域。在加权 Gauss 点
$x(\sum_\nu a_\nu T^\nu)=\max_\nu|a_\nu|r^\nu$ 处，
空间 $U$ 不具有 affinoid 邻域。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{\emph{Step 1: the domain structure.}
Each $U_i$ is the Laurent domain $|T_i|\ge r_i$ in $X$;
the opposite inequality already holds throughout $X$.
All finite intersections are Laurent affinoid domains.
Their finite cover of $U$ is an analytic net: at a point
$z\in U$, remove the finitely many closed members which do
not contain $z$. The remaining relative open neighbourhood
is covered by the members containing $z$.
The domain and net constructions in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidanalyticdomaincalculus} and
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalnetdomaincalculus} therefore give the
analytic-domain structure on $U$. The displayed complement
is open in $X$, so $U$ is closed in the compact Hausdorff
space $X$, hence compact Hausdorff.}
{\emph{第一步：解析域结构。}
每个 $U_i$ 都是 $X$ 中由 $|T_i|\ge r_i$ 定义的 Laurent
域，因为相反的不等式在整个 $X$ 上已经成立。其有限交仍为
Laurent affinoid 域。这组有限覆盖构成解析 net：在
$z\in U$ 处，删去不包含 $z$ 的有限个闭成员，剩下的相对开邻域
由包含 $z$ 的成员覆盖。于是
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidanalyticdomaincalculus} 及
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalnetdomaincalculus} 中的域与 net
构造给出 $U$ 的解析域结构。被删去的多圆盘在 $X$ 中开，
所以 $U$ 在紧 Hausdorff 空间 $X$ 中闭，因而紧且 Hausdorff。}

\bi{\emph{Step 2: unit radii.}
Assume first that every $r_i=1$, put $k=\widetilde K$,
and let $x$ be the unit Gauss point.
Polynomials are dense in the Tate algebra. Approximating
the numerator and denominator of a fraction, with error
smaller than the nonzero denominator, shows that
$\mathcal H(x)$ is the completion of $K(T_1,\ldots,T_n)$
for the Gauss absolute value.
Every nonzero Gauss norm of a polynomial belongs to
$|K^\times|$. For a norm-one fraction, divide its numerator
and denominator by coefficients of their maximal norms.
Reduction then identifies the residue field with
$k(t_1,\ldots,t_n)$; completion has the same residue field
by Lemma~\ref{fnd:valuedfielddimensioncalculus}. The graded
image of the Tate algebra in
$F_x=\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}^{\,\mathrm{gr}}$ is
$\widetilde K^{\,\mathrm{gr}}[t_1,\ldots,t_n]$:
only finitely many terms of a convergent series can have
its maximal norm, and these give precisely its initial form.}
{\emph{第二步：单位半径。}
先设所有 $r_i=1$，记 $k=\widetilde K$，并令 $x$ 为单位
Gauss 点。多项式在 Tate 代数中稠密；将分式的分子与分母以小于
非零分母绝对值的误差逼近，可知 $\mathcal H(x)$ 是
$K(T_1,\ldots,T_n)$ 关于 Gauss 绝对值的完备化。
非零多项式的 Gauss 范数均属于 $|K^\times|$。
对范数一的分式，将分子与分母分别除以具有最大绝对值的系数，
取约化即可将剩余域识别为 $k(t_1,\ldots,t_n)$；由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:valuedfielddimensioncalculus}，完备化保持剩余域。
Tate 代数在
$F_x=\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}^{\,\mathrm{gr}}$ 中的分次像为
$\widetilde K^{\,\mathrm{gr}}[t_1,\ldots,t_n]$：收敛级数只有
有限项能够取得最大范数，这些项恰好给出其首项形式。}

\bi{All the $U_i$ contain $x$. Near $x$, $T_i$ is invertible;
the additional active inequality defining $U_i$ is
$|T_i^{-1}|\le1$. By
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedweierstrassgerm}, its reduction adds
$t_i^{-1}$ to the graded image just computed. Gluing the
domain germs by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:allgradedgermreduction} and
Theorem~\ref{fnd:allgermdomainclassification}, the reduction
of $(U,x)$ is therefore the union of the graded valuation
subspaces containing}
{所有 $U_i$ 都包含 $x$。在 $x$ 附近，$T_i$ 可逆，而定义
$U_i$ 的新增有效不等式为 $|T_i^{-1}|\le1$。由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedweierstrassgerm}，其约化将
$t_i^{-1}$ 加入刚才计算的分次像。应用
Theorem~\ref{fnd:allgradedgermreduction} 与
Theorem~\ref{fnd:allgermdomainclassification} 粘合这些域芽，
可知 $(U,x)$ 的约化为包含下列分次子环的赋值子空间之并：}
\[
 \widetilde K^{\,\mathrm{gr}}
                  [t_1,\ldots,t_n,t_i^{-1}],\qquad 1\le i\le n.
\]
\bi{It is exactly the pullback, by restriction to degree one,
of the ordinary birational space $Q$ in
Lemma~\ref{fnd:puncturedaffinevaluationsnonaffine}.
That lemma says $Q$ is not affine.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradeddegreeonevaluation} shows that its
graded pullback is not affine either, including when the
valuation of $K$ is trivial.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:goodgermiffaffinereduction} now shows that
$x$ has no affinoid neighbourhood in $U$.}
{它恰为
Lemma~\ref{fnd:puncturedaffinevaluationsnonaffine} 中通常
birational 空间 $Q$ 沿限制到次数一的映射之拉回。该引理说明
$Q$ 不仿射；Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradeddegreeonevaluation} 则说明
其分次拉回也不仿射，包括 $K$ 平凡赋值的情形。于是
Theorem~\ref{fnd:goodgermiffaffinereduction} 表明 $x$
在 $U$ 中没有 affinoid 邻域。}

\bi{\emph{Step 3: arbitrary radii.}
Equip the rational function field $K(S_1,\ldots,S_n)$
with the Gauss absolute value of polyradius $r$ and let
$L$ be its completion. Multiplicativity and completion are
the Gauss-field construction used in
Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalgaussfieldbasis}; no independence of
the radii is required. The images $c_i$ of $S_i$ satisfy
$|c_i|=r_i$. Scalar extension identifies}
{\emph{第三步：任意半径。}
赋予有理函数域 $K(S_1,\ldots,S_n)$ 多半径为 $r$ 的
Gauss 绝对值，并记其完备化为 $L$。这里的乘法性及完备化正是
Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalgaussfieldbasis} 所使用的 Gauss
域构造，不要求半径无关。$S_i$ 的像 $c_i$ 满足
$|c_i|=r_i$。标量扩张给出}
\[
 X_L=\mathcal M(L\{r^{-1}T\})
       \simeq\mathcal M(L\{Z_1,\ldots,Z_n\}),
                 \qquad T_i=c_iZ_i .
\]
\bi{The exact scalar presentation and domain base-change
statements
(\ref{fnd:generalscalarpresentation},
\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange},
\ref{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension})
identify $(U_i)_L$ with $|T_i|=r_i$, hence with $|Z_i|=1$.
Since the finite domain cover and its intersections base
change, $U_L=\bigcup_i(U_i)_L$ is exactly the unit-radius
punctured polydisc. Equivalently its underlying set is the
inverse image of $U$: the projection preserves every
number $|T_i|$, so it preserves the displayed complement
condition as well.}
{精确的标量表示及解析域基变换结论
(\ref{fnd:generalscalarpresentation},
\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange},
\ref{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension})
将 $(U_i)_L$ 识别为 $|T_i|=r_i$，即 $|Z_i|=1$。
由于该有限域覆盖及其交都可基变换，
$U_L=\bigcup_i(U_i)_L$ 恰为单位半径的去开多圆盘。
等价地，其底层集合就是 $U$ 的逆像：投影保持每个数
$|T_i|$，所以也保持所显示的补集条件。}

\bi{Let $y$ be its unit Gauss point in the $Z$-coordinates.
For a polynomial with coefficients in $K$, evaluation at
$y$ is $\max_\nu|a_\nu c^\nu|
=\max_\nu|a_\nu|r^\nu$; by continuity the same holds for
every element of $K\{r^{-1}T\}$. Thus $y$ lies over $x$.
If $x$ had an affinoid neighbourhood $W$ in $U$, then
$W_L$ would be an affinoid domain of $U_L$. Moreover,
an ordinary open neighbourhood of $x$ contained in $W$
has an open inverse image containing $y$ and contained
in $W_L$. Hence $W_L$ would be an affinoid neighbourhood
of $y$, contradicting Step 2 over $L$. This proves the
assertion for every positive polyradius.}
{令 $y$ 为 $Z$ 坐标中的单位 Gauss 点。对系数属于 $K$ 的
多项式，在 $y$ 处的值为
$\max_\nu|a_\nu c^\nu|=\max_\nu|a_\nu|r^\nu$；
由连续性，这对 $K\{r^{-1}T\}$ 的所有元素成立。因此 $y$
位于 $x$ 上方。若 $x$ 在 $U$ 中具有 affinoid 邻域 $W$，
则 $W_L$ 是 $U_L$ 中的 affinoid 域。此外，包含于 $W$
的 $x$ 的通常开邻域，其逆像为包含 $y$ 且包含于 $W_L$
的开集。因此 $W_L$ 将是 $y$ 的 affinoid 邻域，
与在 $L$ 上应用第二步所得结论矛盾。这就处理了每个正多半径。}
\end{proof}
