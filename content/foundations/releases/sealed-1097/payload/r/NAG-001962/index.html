\begin{theorem}[\bi{The graded innerness criterion}
{分次 innerness 判据}]
\label{fnd:gradedinnernesscriterion}
\bi{Let $A$ be affinoid, let $B$ be an $A$-affinoid algebra,
and let $\varphi:B\to D$ be a bounded $A$-homomorphism to a
commutative Banach $A$-algebra. Call $\varphi$ inner over $A$
if some admissible surjection $A\{r^{-1}T\}\twoheadrightarrow B$
has $\rho_D(\varphi(T_i))<r_i$ for every coordinate.
Then $\varphi$ is inner over $A$ if and only if}
{设 $A$ 为 affinoid，$B$ 为 $A$-affinoid 代数，
$\varphi:B\to D$ 为到交换 Banach $A$-代数的有界
$A$-同态。若存在 admissible 满射
$A\{r^{-1}T\}\twoheadrightarrow B$，使每个坐标都满足
$\rho_D(\varphi(T_i))<r_i$，则称 $\varphi$ 在 $A$ 上为
inner。此时 $\varphi$ 在 $A$ 上为 inner 当且仅当}
\[
 \operatorname{im}(\widetilde B^{\rm gr}\to\widetilde D^{\rm gr})
 \quad\hbox{is finite over}\quad
 \operatorname{im}(\widetilde A^{\rm gr}\to\widetilde D^{\rm gr}).
\]
\end{theorem}

\label{fnd:gradedrelativepointevaluation}%
\textbf{Relative innerness at an affinoid point.}
\makeatother
Let \(A\to B\) be a bounded map of norm-admissible generalized
affinoid algebras over the same complete non-Archimedean field \(K\).
For \(y\in\mathcal M(B)\), let \(E=\mathcal H(y)\), and for
\(D=A,B\) let \(R_D\) be the image of \(\widetilde D^{\rm gr}\)
in \(\widetilde E^{\rm gr}\) under evaluation at \(y\).
Then \(y\in\operatorname{Int}(\mathcal M(B)/\mathcal M(A))\)
if and only if \(R_B\) is finite over \(R_A\).

\begin{proof}
Fix an admissible absolute weighted Tate presentation
\(P=K\{r^{-1}T\}\twoheadrightarrow A\).
In any admissible relative presentation
\(A\{s^{-1}X\}\twoheadrightarrow B\), adjoining \(X\)
coefficientwise to \(P\twoheadrightarrow A\) and composing
gives an admissible absolute presentation
\(P\{s^{-1}X\}\twoheadrightarrow B\), by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation}.
If this relative presentation witnesses innerness at \(y\), the
initial classes of the \(X_j\) at the assigned degrees \(s_j\)
evaluate to zero in \(\widetilde E^{\rm gr}\).
The \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{fixed absolute}}
presentation result gives a finite module
over the initial-coordinate polynomial image. Evaluating the
finite generators, only the images of the coefficient field
and the \(T_i\) are needed, and these belong to \(R_A\).
Thus \(R_B\) is finite over \(R_A\).

For the converse, choose the coordinates \(b_j\) of a relative
presentation and shrink to radii \(s_j=\rho_B(b_j)\) when these
are positive. For zero spectral radius choose any positive
\(s_j\) at most the original radius. The presentation stays
admissible by Lemma~\ref{fnd:spectralgeneratorradii}.
Let \(\beta_j\) denote the class of \(b_j\) at degree \(s_j\),
allowing the zero class. If \(R_B\) is finite over \(R_A\),
the image of \(\beta_j\) satisfies a monic equation over
\(R_A\). Take its homogeneous component of degree
\(s_j^{d_j}\), where \(d_j\) is the monic degree. Lift its
coefficient of degree \(s_j^i\) through
\(\widetilde A^{\rm gr}\) to \(a_{ji}\in A\) with
\(\rho_A(a_{ji})\leq s_j^i\), using zero for a zero
coefficient. Put
\[
 p_j(Z)=Z^{d_j}+\sum_{i=1}^{d_j}a_{ji}Z^{d_j-i},
 \qquad t_j=s_j^{d_j},\qquad w_j=p_j(b_j).
\]
For zero evaluated class one may take \(p_j(Z)=Z\).
Then \(\rho_B(w_j)\leq t_j\) and \(|w_j(y)|<t_j\).
Positive-radius power bounds
\ref{fnd:generalizedpointwisepowerbound} give the bounded
substitution \(C=A\{t^{-1}U\}\to B\), with \(U_j\mapsto w_j\).

In \(\widetilde B^{\rm gr}\), each \(\beta_j\) is integral over
the image of \(\widetilde C^{\rm gr}\): its monic equation is
\(\overline p_j(\beta_j)-\overline w_j=0\) in degree \(t_j\),
with \(\overline w_j\) the image of the class of \(U_j\).
The fixed absolute presentation of \(B\) obtained above gives
finiteness over the algebra generated by the image of
\(\widetilde A^{\rm gr}\) and the \(\beta_j\).
Indeed, if an old coefficient coordinate survives at its degree
in \(B\), spectral contraction shows it survives at that
degree in \(A\). Its class in \(B\) therefore comes from \(A\).
It follows by the monic equations that
\(\widetilde B^{\rm gr}\) is finite over the image of
\(\widetilde C^{\rm gr}\).

Apply coefficientwise presentation once more to obtain the
admissible absolute presentation \(Q=P\{t^{-1}U\}\twoheadrightarrow C\).
The \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{fixed absolute}} result makes \(\widetilde C^{\rm gr}\)
finite over the image of \(\widetilde Q^{\rm gr}\).
Transitivity, with both rings mapped to \(\widetilde B^{\rm gr}\),
makes \(\widetilde B^{\rm gr}\) finite over the image of
\(\widetilde Q^{\rm gr}\). The selected \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedabsolutereversefinite]{reverse finite}}
admissibility result applies to the actual absolute map \(Q\to B\).
Choose its finite module generators \(v_i\) with bounded
coefficient lifts. Sending the coefficients through
\(Q\twoheadrightarrow C\) yields lifts with a uniform bound
for the same generators over \(C\).
Choose radii \(u_i>\rho_B(v_i)\), so weighted substitution
defines \(C\{u^{-1}V\}\to B\), \(V_i\mapsto v_i\).
Linear polynomials in \(V_i\) give bounded lifts:
their norms are bounded by the common coefficient-lift bound
times \(\max_i u_i\). This is therefore an admissible relative
presentation over \(A\) with coordinates \(w_j,v_i\).
Their values at \(y\) are strictly below \(t_j,u_i\)
respectively, proving relative innerness.
\end{proof}
