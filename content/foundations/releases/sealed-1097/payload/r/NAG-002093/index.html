Initial degenerations describe local stars

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 872

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Initial degenerations describe local stars}{初始退化描述局部星形}]
\label{cand:initiallocalstar}
\bi{Let $X\subset\operatorname{Spec}K[M]$ be a closed subscheme and $w\in\operatorname{Trop}(X)$. Give the residue field of an initial degeneration $Y=\operatorname{in}_w(X)$ the trivial absolute value. Then}
{设 $X\subset\operatorname{Spec}K[M]$ 为闭子概形，$w\in\operatorname{Trop}(X)$。将初始退化 $Y=\operatorname{in}_w(X)$ 的剩余域赋予平凡绝对值，则}
\[
 \operatorname{Trop}(Y)=\operatorname{Star}_w\operatorname{Trop}(X).
\]
\bi{Here the right side consists of those $u$ for which $w+\varepsilon u\in\operatorname{Trop}(X)$ for all sufficiently small positive $\varepsilon$. More precisely, for every fixed $u$, the schemes $\operatorname{in}_{w+\varepsilon u}(X)$ and $\operatorname{in}_u(Y)$ are equivalent after residue extension and translation for all sufficiently small positive $\varepsilon$.}
{右侧由满足如下条件的 $u$ 构成：对所有充分小的正数 $\varepsilon$，有 $w+\varepsilon u\in\operatorname{Trop}(X)$。更准确地，对每个固定 $u$，在所有充分小的正数 $\varepsilon$ 下，$\operatorname{in}_{w+\varepsilon u}(X)$ 与 $\operatorname{in}_u(Y)$ 在剩余扩域及平移后等价。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Homogenize as in Theorem~\ref{cand:finitegroebnercomplex}, and write $\omega=(0,w)$ and $\upsilon=(0,u)$. Choose the common degree bound $D$ of Proposition~\ref{cand:initialuniformdegree}, also valid for the further initial ideals. In every degree at most $D$, minimize the pair}
{按 Theorem~\ref{cand:finitegroebnercomplex} 齐次化，记 $\omega=(0,w)$、$\upsilon=(0,u)$。选取 Proposition~\ref{cand:initialuniformdegree} 的共同次数界 $D$，它也适用于进一步的初始理想。在每个至多 $D$ 的次数中，按字典序最小化数对}
\[
 \left(p_B(\omega),\ \sum_{b\in B}\langle b,\upsilon\rangle\right).
\]
\bi{There are finitely many pairs. Thus a lexicographically minimizing basis is minimizing for $p_B(\omega+\varepsilon\upsilon)$ whenever $\varepsilon>0$ is sufficiently small. Cramer's rule then says that its surviving row entries are exactly those which first survive at $\omega$ and then have minimal $\upsilon$-weight. After the first reduction these rows are a basis of the degree piece of $\operatorname{in}_\omega(H)$; their second reduction is therefore its degree piece at $\upsilon$, by Lemma~\ref{cand:pluckerpivotweights}.}
{数对只有有限个，因此字典序最小的主元集在充分小的 $\varepsilon>0$ 下也使 $p_B(\omega+\varepsilon\upsilon)$ 最小。由 Cramer 法则，其保留行项恰好是先在 $\omega$ 处保留、再使 $\upsilon$ 权重最小的项。第一次约化后，这些行构成 $\operatorname{in}_\omega(H)$ 相应次数的基；由 Lemma~\ref{cand:pluckerpivotweights}，第二次约化就是它在 $\upsilon$ 处的相应次数部分。}

\bi{We spell out the coefficient comparison. Fix a complete field $L$ and $t$ of value $\omega$. Adjoin Gauss variables $q_i$ of values $\varepsilon\upsilon_i$ to $L$ and complete their fraction field, obtaining $E$. Use $tq$ for the one-step translation. For a row entry $c_{bm}$ put $a=m-b$. An entry survives both operations precisely when}
{具体比较系数如下。固定完备域 $L$ 及赋值为 $\omega$ 的 $t$。向 $L$ 添加赋值为 $\varepsilon\upsilon_i$ 的 Gauss 变量 $q_i$，并完备化其分式域，得到 $E$。一次平移采用 $tq$。对行系数 $c_{bm}$，记 $a=m-b$。它在两步中均保留，当且仅当}
\[
 v(c_{bm})+\langle a,\omega\rangle=0,
 \qquad \langle a,\upsilon\rangle=0.
\]
\bi{Its coefficient after the first reduction is $\overline{c_{bm}t^a}\in\widetilde L$. The ordinary weight initial form over the trivially valued field retains this coefficient. The one-step coefficient in $\widetilde E$ is $\overline{c_{bm}t^a}\,\overline{q^a}$. Thus the discrepancy is exactly the character $a\mapsto\overline{q^a}$ on the subgroup generated by the surviving exponent differences. Extend this character to the whole exponent lattice by the finite root-adjunction argument in the preceding theorem. A single torus translation then compares all the row generators.}
{第一次约化后的系数为 $\overline{c_{bm}t^a}\in\widetilde L$。平凡赋值域上的通常权重初始形式保留此系数；而在 $\widetilde E$ 中，一次平移后的系数为 $\overline{c_{bm}t^a}\,\overline{q^a}$。因此差异恰为保留指数差所生成子群上的特征 $a\mapsto\overline{q^a}$。用上一命题中逐次添加有限个根的论证将其延拓到整个指数格，一个环面平移便同时比较所有行生成元。}

\bi{For completeness, the ordinary initial ideal obtained by retaining the terms of least $\upsilon$-weight agrees, up to this same kind of translation, with the valued-extension definition of $\operatorname{in}_{\upsilon}$ over a trivially valued field. Indeed, introduce Gauss variables $s_i$ of values $\upsilon_i$ and divide each selected row by its pivot monomial. For a retained exponent difference $a$ one has $\langle a,\upsilon\rangle=0$, so the only new coefficient factor is the character $a\mapsto\overline{s^a}$. This also extends after algebraic residue extension. The common degree bound identifies the full homogeneous ideals from the compared rows. Localizing and dehomogenizing gives the claimed scheme equivalence. The nonemptiness criterion of Theorem~\ref{cand:realinitialdegeneration}, over both fields, gives the equality of supports.}
{另说明通常初始理想与平凡赋值域上通过赋值扩域定义的 $\operatorname{in}_{\upsilon}$ 的相容性。引入赋值为 $\upsilon_i$ 的 Gauss 变量 $s_i$，并将所选各行除以主元单项式。对每个保留指数差 $a$，有 $\langle a,\upsilon\rangle=0$，因此新增系数因子仅为特征 $a\mapsto\overline{s^a}$；它同样可在代数剩余扩域后延拓。共同次数界使已比较的行确定完整齐次理想。局部化并去齐次化得到所述概形等价；对两个域应用 Theorem~\ref{cand:realinitialdegeneration} 的非空判据，即得支集等式。}
\end{proof}
