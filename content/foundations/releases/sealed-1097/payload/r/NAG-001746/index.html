Formal presentations at rational analytic points

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 314

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Formal presentations at rational analytic points}{有理解析点处的形式表示}]
\label{fnd:rationalanalyticformalpresentation}
\bi{Let $K$ be nontrivially valued and $X=\mathcal M(K\{r^{-1}T\}/I)$. At a $K$-rational point $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_n)$, put $U_i=T_i-a_i$ and let $I_a$ denote the ideal of $C_n$ generated by the translated elements of $I$. Then}
{设 $K$ 的赋值非平凡，且 $X=\mathcal M(K\{r^{-1}T\}/I)$。在 $K$-有理点 $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_n)$ 处，令 $U_i=T_i-a_i$，并以 $I_a$ 记 $I$ 的元素平移后在 $C_n$ 中生成的理想。则}
\[
 \mathcal O_{X,a}\simeq C_n/I_a,\qquad
 \widehat{\mathcal O_{X,a}}\simeq K[[U_1,\ldots,U_n]]/
                                    I_aK[[U_1,\ldots,U_n]].
\]
\bi{If $X$ is replaced near $a$ by an analytic domain, these identifications are unchanged. If $Z\hookrightarrow X$ is a closed analytic subspace with coherent ideal $\mathcal J$, then $\mathcal O_{Z,a}=\mathcal O_{X,a}/\mathcal J_a$.}
{若在 $a$ 附近将 $X$ 换为解析域，这些识别不变。若 $Z\hookrightarrow X$ 为凝聚理想 $\mathcal J$ 定义的闭解析子空间，则 $\mathcal O_{Z,a}=\mathcal O_{X,a}/\mathcal J_a$。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Small polydiscs centered at $a$ form an ordinary neighborhood basis in the ambient polydisc. To verify cofinality, an ordinary neighborhood is specified by finitely many evaluations of analytic functions. On a sufficiently small centered polydisc, each such function differs uniformly from its value at $a$ by an arbitrarily small amount, by its convergent translated series. For a nonzero value this preserves its absolute value; for a zero value it gives any prescribed positive upper bound. These conditions realize every finite evaluation neighborhood. All sufficiently small centered polydiscs lie in the original polydisc, since $|a_i|\le r_i$.}
{以 $a$ 为中心的小多圆盘在环境多圆盘中构成普通邻域基。为核验余终性，普通邻域由有限个解析函数的取值条件给出。在充分小的中心多圆盘上，由收敛的平移级数，每个这样的函数都与其在 $a$ 处的值一致地任意接近。若此值非零，则绝对值保持不变；若此值为零，则得到任意指定的正上界。这些条件实现全部有限取值邻域。所有充分小的中心多圆盘均位于原多圆盘中，因为 $|a_i|\le r_i$。}

\bi{Intersecting them with $X$ gives a cofinal system of affinoid neighborhoods. Closed-subspace base change and finite-module exactness identify their algebras with the corresponding Tate algebras modulo the extended ideal $I$. The ideal $I$ is finitely generated by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}. Passing to the filtered colimit therefore gives $C_n/I_a$: tensoring a finite ideal presentation, or lifting a finite equality to a smaller polydisc, verifies both its surjectivity and its kernel. The same argument applies to any coherent closed ideal $\mathcal J$ after choosing finite generators on an affinoid neighborhood.}
{将这些多圆盘与 $X$ 相交，得到 affinoid 邻域的余终系统。闭子空间的基变换及有限模正合性，将它们的代数识别为相应 Tate 代数对延拓理想 $I$ 的商。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}，理想 $I$ 有限生成。因此传到滤过直极限就得到 $C_n/I_a$：对有限理想表示作张量，或把有限个等式提升到更小多圆盘，均可核验满射性与其核。对任意凝聚闭理想 $\mathcal J$，在 affinoid 邻域上选有限生成元后，同一论证仍适用。}

\bi{Lemma~\ref{fnd:convergentgermnoetherian} makes $C_n$ Noetherian and computes its completion. Exactness of completion for finite modules, \cite[Tag 00MA]{StacksAlg}, identifies the completion of its quotient with the displayed formal quotient. Finally Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalpointdomaininterior} says that any analytic domain containing $a$ is an ordinary neighborhood there, and hence does not change the stalk.}
{Lemma~\ref{fnd:convergentgermnoetherian} 给出 $C_n$ 的 Noetherian 性并计算其完备化。有限模完备化的正合性 \cite[Tag 00MA]{StacksAlg} 将商的完备化识别为所列形式商。最后，由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalpointdomaininterior}，包含 $a$ 的任意解析域在该点都是普通邻域，故不改变茎。}
\end{proof}
