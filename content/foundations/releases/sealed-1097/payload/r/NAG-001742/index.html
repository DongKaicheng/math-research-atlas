Descent from one scalar-extension point

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 312

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Descent from one scalar-extension point}{由一个扩域点下降解析平坦性}]
\label{fnd:analyticflatonescalardescent}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good $K$-analytic spaces, let $\mathcal F$ be coherent on $Y$, and let $L/K$ be a complete valued extension. For $y\in Y$ and any point $u\in Y_L$ above $y$, $\mathcal F$ is analytically $X$-flat at $y$ if and only if $\mathcal F_L$ is analytically $X_L$-flat at $u$.}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 为 good $K$-解析空间之间的态射，$\mathcal F$ 为 $Y$ 上的凝聚层，$L/K$ 为完备赋值扩张。对 $y\in Y$ 及其上方任一点 $u\in Y_L$，$\mathcal F$ 在 $y$ 处解析 $X$-平坦，当且仅当 $\mathcal F_L$ 在 $u$ 处解析 $X_L$-平坦。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The forward implication follows directly by composing the base changes quantified in Definition~\ref{fnd:analyticflatness}. For the converse, let $F/K$ be any complete valued extension, let $X'\to X$ be a good $F$-analytic base change, and let $y'\in Y'=Y\times_X X'$ map to $y$. Write $x'$ for its image in $X'$. We must prove that $\mathcal F_{Y',y'}$ is flat over $\mathcal O_{X',x'}$.}
{正向断言直接来自复合 Definition~\ref{fnd:analyticflatness} 中量化的基变换。反之，取任意完备赋值扩张 $F/K$、good $F$-解析基变换 $X'\to X$，以及映到 $y$ 的点 $y'\in Y'=Y\times_X X'$。记 $x'$ 为其在 $X'$ 中的像。需要证明 $\mathcal F_{Y',y'}$ 在 $\mathcal O_{X',x'}$ 上平坦。}

\bi{Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:completevaluedamalgamation} to the complete extensions $\mathcal H(u)$ and $\mathcal H(y')$ of $\mathcal H(y)$. It gives a complete field $\Omega$ containing both compatibly. In particular $\Omega$ contains $L$ and $F$ over $K$. The inclusion $\mathcal H(y')\hookrightarrow\Omega$ defines an $\Omega$-rational point $\omega$ of $Y'_\Omega$ above $y'$, by the pointwise fibre description in Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres}, applied locally. Its image in $Y_L$ is $u$: on any affinoid chart the evaluations of its analytic functions and of the coefficients in $L$ are exactly those obtained through $\mathcal H(u)\hookrightarrow\Omega$. Denote its image in $X'_\Omega$ by $v$.}
{对 $\mathcal H(y)$ 的完备扩张 $\mathcal H(u)$ 与 $\mathcal H(y')$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:completevaluedamalgamation}，得到相容包含二者的完备域 $\Omega$。特别地，$\Omega$ 在 $K$ 上包含 $L$ 与 $F$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres} 的逐点纤维描述，在局部应用可知，嵌入 $\mathcal H(y')\hookrightarrow\Omega$ 定义 $Y'_\Omega$ 中位于 $y'$ 上方的 $\Omega$-有理点 $\omega$。它在 $Y_L$ 中的像为 $u$：在任一 affinoid chart 上，其解析函数及 $L$ 中系数的取值，恰为经过 $\mathcal H(u)\hookrightarrow\Omega$ 得到的取值。记其在 $X'_\Omega$ 中的像为 $v$。}

\bi{Associativity of the analytic fibre products identifies $Y'_\Omega$ with the pullback of $Y_L$ along $X'_\Omega\to X_L$. The assumed analytic flatness at $u$ therefore makes $(\mathcal F_{Y'_\Omega})_\omega$ flat over $\mathcal O_{X'_\Omega,v}$. In the commutative local square}
{解析纤维积的结合律将 $Y'_\Omega$ 识别为 $Y_L$ 沿 $X'_\Omega\to X_L$ 的拉回。因此，所假设的 $u$ 处解析平坦性说明 $(\mathcal F_{Y'_\Omega})_\omega$ 在 $\mathcal O_{X'_\Omega,v}$ 上平坦。在局部环交换方块}
\[
 \begin{matrix}
 \mathcal O_{X',x'}&\longrightarrow&\mathcal O_{X'_\Omega,v}\\
 \big\downarrow&&\big\downarrow\\
 \mathcal O_{Y',y'}&\longrightarrow&\mathcal O_{Y'_\Omega,\omega}
 \end{matrix}
\]
\bi{both horizontal maps are faithfully flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}. The same theorem identifies the pullback coherent stalk with the ordinary tensor product. Lemma~\ref{fnd:flatlocalsquaredescent} now proves the required naive flatness at $y'$. Since the complete extension and analytic base change were arbitrary, $\mathcal F$ is analytically flat at $y$.}
{中，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}，两个横向映射均忠实平坦。同一定理将拉回后的凝聚茎识别为普通张量积。Lemma~\ref{fnd:flatlocalsquaredescent} 于是证明所需的 $y'$ 处 naive 平坦性。由于完备扩域与解析基变换均任意，$\mathcal F$ 在 $y$ 处解析平坦。}
\end{proof}
