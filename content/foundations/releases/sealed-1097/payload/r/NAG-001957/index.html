Finite admissible maps descend from Gauss fields

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 554

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Finite admissible maps descend from Gauss fields}
{有限 admissible 映射的 Gauss 域下降}]
\label{fnd:gaussfiniteadmissibledescent}
\bi{Let $A\to B$ be a bounded affinoid homomorphism and
$L/K$ a finite iterated Gauss-field extension. Then $A\to B$
is finite and admissible if and only if $A_L\to B_L$ is finite
and admissible.}
{设 $A\to B$ 为有界 affinoid 同态，$L/K$ 为有限次迭代的
Gauss 域扩张。则 $A\to B$ 有限且 admissible 当且仅当
$A_L\to B_L$ 有限且 admissible。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{In an orthogonal scalar basis, all completed tensor products
are weighted coefficient $c_0$-spaces. A bounded admissible
surjection $A^m\to B$ therefore remains such a surjection:
lift each coefficient with a fixed common norm bound, which
preserves coefficient decay. This proves the forward direction.}
{选取正交标量基后，各完备张量积都是加权系数 $c_0$-空间。
因此，有界 admissible 满射 $A^m\to B$ 扩张后仍为同类满射：
逐系数以同一个范数界作提升即可，且系数衰减保持。
这证明正向结论。}

\bi{Conversely let $b_1',\ldots,b_m'$ generate $B_L$ over $A_L$
with a bounded lifting constant $C$. Approximate these generators
by finite scalar sums $b_i''$ so closely that
$C\max_i\|b_i'-b_i''\|<1$.
Let \(M\) be a bound for the action \(A_L\times B_L\to B_L\).
In choosing the approximations require \(MC\max_i\|b_i'-b_i''\|<1\).
They still generate with a bounded
lifting constant. Indeed lift an element using the original
generators, replace them by the approximations, and repeat on
the resulting error. The errors decrease geometrically, and
the coefficient corrections converge in $A_L^m$.
Collect the finitely many elements $b_j\in B$ occurring in
the finite scalar expansions of the $b_i''$. They generate
$B_L$, again with a bounded lifting constant.
For $b\in B$ write $b=\sum_j c_jb_j$, $c_j\in A_L$, with
$\max\|c_j\|\leq C'\|b\|$. Let $p_0=p_{A,0}:A_L\to A$ be the constant-coordinate projection
from Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalgaussfieldbasis}. It is $A$-linear,
has norm at most one, and fixes $A$. The corresponding projection
$p_{B,0}:B_L\to B$ fixes $B$.
In the final equality \(b=\sum_j\varphi_L(c_j)(b_j\otimes 1)\),
apply the \(B\)-valued constant-coordinate projection \(p_{B,0}\).
On each product it equals \(\varphi(p_{A,0}(c_j))b_j\) by the
coefficient formula, first on finite sums and then by continuity.
Hence}
{反之，设 $b_1',\ldots,b_m'$ 在 $A_L$ 上生成 $B_L$，并具有
有界提升常数 $C$。以有限标量和 $b_i''$ 充分逼近这些生成元，
使 $C\max_i\|b_i'-b_i''\|<1$。新元素仍生成，并具有有界
提升常数：先用原生成元提升一个元素，再换成逼近元素，然后
对误差重复这一过程。误差按几何级数衰减，系数修正在
$A_L^m$ 中收敛。收集各 $b_i''$ 的有限标量展开中出现的有限个
$b_j\in B$，它们在 $A_L$ 上生成 $B_L$，且仍有有界提升常数。
对 $b\in B$ 写成 $b=\sum_jc_jb_j$，其中 $c_j\in A_L$，
且 $\max\|c_j\|\leq C'\|b\|$。应用由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalgaussfieldbasis} 逐系数延拓的常数坐标
投影 $p_0:A_L\to A$。它是 $A$-线性的，范数至多一，并在
$A$ 上为恒等映射。因此}
\[
 b=\sum_jp_0(c_j)b_j,\qquad
       \max_j\|p_0(c_j)\|\leq C'\|b\|.
\]
\bi{This proves finite generation and admissibility over $A$.
Only linearity of $p_0$ is used.}
{这同时证明在 $A$ 上的有限生成性与 admissibility。
这里仅使用 $p_0$ 的线性性质。}
\end{proof}
