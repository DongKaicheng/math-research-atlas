\begin{proposition}[Product boundary sign; F8RC-S]
\label{prop:sign}
For a calibrated integral \((n-1)\)-chain \(P\), with the standard
product lattice, the facet convention in \eqref{eq:green} gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sign}
 \int_{\partial(P\times[\sigma,\tau])}
           d''x\wedge\chi(x)\alpha=-\int_P\alpha,
\end{equation}
provided \(\chi=1\) near \(\sigma\), \(\chi=0\) near \(\tau\), and
\(\alpha\) is pulled back from the horizontal factor. This is a
polyhedral identity and does not supply R-GREEN.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Subdivide into products of \((n-1)\)-cells with the interval. On the
upper face the coefficient is zero. On a lateral face the horizontal
primed degree is \(n-1\) but its horizontal tangent space has dimension
at most \(n-2\), so the restriction is zero. On the lower face the
outward primitive interval normal is \(-\partial_x\). With the same
superform boundary convention as \eqref{eq:green}, contraction leaves
\(-\alpha\) with its original horizontal calibration. Equivalently,
on one product cell the one-variable boundary contribution is
\((\chi(\tau)-\chi(\sigma))\int_C\alpha=-\int_C\alpha\).
For an explicit sign check, put \(d=n-1\) and
\(s_d=(-1)^{d(d-1)/2}\). If
\(\alpha=s_d A\,d'y_1\wedge\cdots\wedge d'y_d
\wedge d''y_1\wedge\cdots\wedge d''y_d\), then the vertical
term of \(d'(d''x\wedge\chi\alpha)\) is
\(\chi'(x)d'x\wedge d''x\wedge\alpha\). Its coefficient
in the positive product generator is \(\chi'(x)A\), since
\(s_{d+1}=(-1)^d s_d\). One-dimensional integration gives the
displayed endpoint difference. Weights and shared facets add linearly.
This checks both the outward normal and the cutoff sign; no limiting
operation is involved.
\end{proof}
