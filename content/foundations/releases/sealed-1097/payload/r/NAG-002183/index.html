Well-defined corner weights

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 862

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Well-defined corner weights}{角点权重的良定义性}]
\label{cand:cornerwelldefined}
\bi{Formula~\eqref{eq:candcornerweight} is independent of all
normal lifts, linear extensions, and adapted subdivisions. It is
linear in $C$ and in $\varphi$, and $D_\ell C=0$ for every
globally affine function $\ell$. It commutes with integral affine
lattice isomorphisms.}
{公式~\eqref{eq:candcornerweight} 与法向提升、线性延拓及适配
细分的选择均无关。它关于 $C$ 与 $\varphi$ 分别线性；对每个
全局仿射函数 $\ell$，有 $D_\ell C=0$。它与整仿射格同构相容。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Changing a lift adds a vector in $N_{\tau,\mathbf R}$, on
which $\varphi_\sigma-\ell_\tau$ vanishes by continuity.
Changing $\ell_\tau$ by a linear form $h$ vanishing on that space
changes the sum by
$-h(\sum_\sigma w_\sigma n_{\sigma/\tau})=0$, by balancing.
At a new face inside an old affine piece, the two contributions
cancel; in fact the same linear extension can be used on both
sides. At a subdivided old face, the formula has the old value.
Lemma~\ref{cand:polyhedralrefinementproduct} now compares any two
adapted subdivisions. The formula gives both linearities. For a
globally affine function choose its common linear part as every
$\ell_\tau$; all summands vanish. A lattice isomorphism transports
the saturated lattices, their primitive generators, and this
same formula, proving the last assertion.}
{改变提升只会增加 $N_{\tau,\mathbf R}$ 中的向量；由连续性，
$\varphi_\sigma-\ell_\tau$ 在该空间上为零。若将
$\ell_\tau$ 改为 $\ell_\tau+h$，其中 $h$ 在该空间上为零，
则总和的改变量为
$-h(\sum_\sigma w_\sigma n_{\sigma/\tau})=0$，这里使用
平衡性。在原仿射片内部新增的面处，两侧贡献相消；实际上
可在两侧使用相同的线性延拓。在原面的细分处，公式保持原值。
由 Lemma~\ref{cand:polyhedralrefinementproduct}，可比较任意
两个适配细分。两种线性性直接来自公式。若函数全局仿射，
将其共同线性部分选作全部 $\ell_\tau$，每项均为零。格同构
输送饱和格、本原生成元及同一公式，从而给出最后的相容性。}
\end{proof}
