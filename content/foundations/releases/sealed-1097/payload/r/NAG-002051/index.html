A rational pointed nodal chart after finite extension

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 660

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{A rational pointed nodal chart after finite extension}{有限扩域后的有理带点结点坐标}]
\label{cand:pointednodalvaluationchart}
\bi{Let $\mathcal X\to\operatorname{Spec}O$ be a morphism of schemes which is at-worst-nodal of relative dimension one, and let $x$ be a node of its closed fibre. There are a finite separable extension $L/K$, an affine $L^\circ$-scheme $U$, \textup{\'etale} morphisms}
{设 $\mathcal X\to\operatorname{Spec}O$ 为相对维数一、至多具有结点的概形态射，并设 $x$ 为其闭纤维上的结点。存在有限可分扩张 $L/K$、一个仿射 $L^\circ$-概形 $U$，以及 \textup{\'etale} 态射}
\[
 \mathcal X_{L^\circ}\ \longleftarrow\ U
 \ \longrightarrow\ W_a=\operatorname{Spec}\bigl(L^\circ[s,t]/(st-a)\bigr)
 \qquad(a\in L^{\circ\circ}),
\]
\bi{and a $\widetilde L$-rational point $u$ of the closed fibre of $U$ such that its image $x'$ in $\mathcal X_{\widetilde L}$ lies over $x$ and its image in $(W_a)_{\widetilde L}$ is the origin $s=t=0$. The point $x'$ is a split node, with}
{以及 $U$ 的闭纤维上的一个 $\widetilde L$-有理点 $u$，使其在 $\mathcal X_{\widetilde L}$ 中的像 $x'$ 位于 $x$ 上方，而其在 $(W_a)_{\widetilde L}$ 中的像为原点 $s=t=0$。点 $x'$ 是 split node，并有}
\[
 \widehat{\mathcal O}_{\mathcal X_{\widetilde L},x'}
       \simeq \widetilde L[[s,t]]/(st).
\]
\bi{If the generic fibre $\mathcal X_K$ is smooth over $K$, the parameter satisfies $a\ne0$, and consequently $0<|a|<1$.}
{若泛纤维 $\mathcal X_K$ 在 $K$ 上光滑，则参数满足 $a\ne0$，因而 $0<|a|<1$。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Apply the nodal normal-form theorem \cite[Tag 0CBY]{StacksAlg}. It gives \textup{\'etale} maps $\mathcal X\leftarrow U_0$, $V\to\operatorname{Spec}O$ and}
{应用结点正规形定理 \cite[Tag 0CBY]{StacksAlg}，得到 \textup{\'etale} 态射 $\mathcal X\leftarrow U_0$、$V\to\operatorname{Spec}O$ 以及}
\[
 U_0\longrightarrow
 V\times_{\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Z[A]}
       \operatorname{Spec}\bigl(\mathbf Z[S,T,A]/(ST-A)\bigr),
\]
\bi{and a point $u_0\in U_0$ mapping to $x$. Let $v_0\in V$ be its image. A nodal point of a curve has finite separable residue field over the ground field by \cite[Tag 0C4D]{StacksAlg}. The \textup{\'etale} fibre description \cite[Tag 02GL]{StacksAlg} makes $\kappa(u_0)/\kappa(x)$ finite separable as well. Hence $l=\kappa(u_0)$ is finite separable over $k$.}
{并得到映到 $x$ 的点 $u_0\in U_0$。记其在 $V$ 中的像为 $v_0$。由 \cite[Tag 0C4D]{StacksAlg}，曲线结点的剩余域在基域上有限可分；由 \textup{\'etale} 纤维的描述 \cite[Tag 02GL]{StacksAlg}，$\kappa(u_0)/\kappa(x)$ 也有限可分。因此 $l=\kappa(u_0)$ 在 $k$ 上有限可分。}

\bi{Choose $L/K$ by Lemma~\ref{cand:residueextensionlift}, with a specified identification $l=\widetilde L$. Base change the diagram to $O'=L^\circ$. The maps $\operatorname{Spec}\widetilde L\to U_0$ and $\operatorname{Spec}\widetilde L\to\operatorname{Spec}O'$ define a rational point $u_1$ of $(U_0)_{O'}$, whose image $v_1$ in $V_{O'}$ is rational. Replace $V_{O'}$ by an affine open containing $v_1$ and $(U_0)_{O'}$ by its inverse image. Since $L$ is complete, Lemma~\ref{cand:completevaluationhenselian} gives a section}
{由 Lemma~\ref{cand:residueextensionlift} 选择 $L/K$，并指定识别 $l=\widetilde L$。将此图基变换到 $O'=L^\circ$。映射 $\operatorname{Spec}\widetilde L\to U_0$ 与 $\operatorname{Spec}\widetilde L\to\operatorname{Spec}O'$ 给出 $(U_0)_{O'}$ 的有理点 $u_1$；其在 $V_{O'}$ 中的像 $v_1$ 也为有理点。将 $V_{O'}$ 替换为包含 $v_1$ 的仿射开集，并将 $(U_0)_{O'}$ 替换为其逆像。由于 $L$ 完备，Lemma~\ref{cand:completevaluationhenselian} 给出经过 $v_1$ 的截面}
\[
 j:\operatorname{Spec}O'\longrightarrow V_{O'}
\]
\bi{through $v_1$. Pull back the diagram along $j$. Its image is open, so the resulting $U_1$ is open in $(U_0)_{O'}$; in particular $U_1\to\mathcal X_{O'}$ remains \textup{\'etale}. The other map becomes \textup{\'etale} over $W_a=\operatorname{Spec}(O'[s,t]/(st-a))$, where $a\in O'$ is the pullback of the parameter $A$. These uses of composition and base change are \cite[Tags 02GN, 02GO]{StacksAlg}. The point $u_1$ belongs to this pullback because its image is the special point of the section. Choose an affine open neighbourhood $U\subset U_1$ of it and denote the point by $u$.}
{沿 $j$ 拉回上述图。截面的像为开集，故所得 $U_1$ 是 $(U_0)_{O'}$ 的开子概形；特别地，$U_1\to\mathcal X_{O'}$ 仍为 \textup{\'etale}。另一个态射变成到 $W_a=\operatorname{Spec}(O'[s,t]/(st-a))$ 的 \textup{\'etale} 态射，其中 $a\in O'$ 为参数 $A$ 的拉回。这里使用的复合与基变换性质见 \cite[Tags 02GN, 02GO]{StacksAlg}。点 $u_1$ 属于此拉回，因为其像为截面的闭点。取它的一个仿射开邻域 $U\subset U_1$，并将该点记为 $u$。}

\bi{Let $x'$ and $w$ be the images of $u$ in $\mathcal X_{\widetilde L}$ and $(W_a)_{\widetilde L}$. Both are rational: their residue fields are intermediate fields between $\widetilde L$ and $\kappa(u)=\widetilde L$. The point $x'$ remains a node by the base-change invariance in \cite[Tag 0C56]{StacksAlg}. Applying the \textup{\'etale} invariance of nodes, \cite[Tag 0C57]{StacksAlg}, to the two maps from $U_{\widetilde L}$ shows that $u$ and $w$ are nodes. The curve $st-\overline a=0$ is smooth wherever $s$ or $t$ is invertible; its only possible nodal point is therefore $s=t=0$. Thus $w$ is that origin and $\overline a=0$.}
{记 $u$ 在 $\mathcal X_{\widetilde L}$ 与 $(W_a)_{\widetilde L}$ 中的像分别为 $x'$、$w$。二者均为有理点，因为其剩余域是 $\widetilde L$ 与 $\kappa(u)=\widetilde L$ 之间的中间域。由 \cite[Tag 0C56]{StacksAlg} 的基变换不变性，$x'$ 仍为结点。对从 $U_{\widetilde L}$ 出发的两个态射应用结点的 \textup{\'etale} 不变性 \cite[Tag 0C57]{StacksAlg}，可知 $u$、$w$ 均为结点。曲线 $st-\overline a=0$ 在 $s$ 或 $t$ 可逆处光滑，所以其唯一可能的结点是 $s=t=0$。因此 $w$ 是原点，且 $\overline a=0$。}

\bi{The local rings at $x'$, $u$ and $w$ of these special fibres are Noetherian, since the fibres are locally of finite type over $\widetilde L$. Each of the two \textup{\'etale} local maps is flat, has the same residue field $\widetilde L$, and carries the source maximal ideal onto the target maximal ideal; flatness and the fibre assertion follow from \cite[Tags 02GS, 02GL]{StacksAlg}. Thus \cite[Tag 0AGX]{StacksAlg} identifies their completions. The completion at $w$ is $\widetilde L[[s,t]]/(st)$, giving the displayed isomorphism for $x'$. The split-node criterion \cite[Tag 0CBW]{StacksAlg} completes this assertion.}
{这些闭纤维在 $x'$、$u$、$w$ 处的局部环均为 Noetherian 环，因为纤维在 $\widetilde L$ 上局部有限型。两个 \textup{\'etale} 局部同态均平坦、剩余域均为 $\widetilde L$，并将源的极大理想扩张为目标的极大理想；平坦性与纤维断言来自 \cite[Tags 02GS, 02GL]{StacksAlg}。所以 \cite[Tag 0AGX]{StacksAlg} 识别它们的完备化。$w$ 处的完备化是 $\widetilde L[[s,t]]/(st)$，由此得到 $x'$ 的所述同构。再由 \cite[Tag 0CBW]{StacksAlg} 的判据，$x'$ 为 split node。}

\bi{Finally assume that $\mathcal X_K$ is smooth, and suppose $a=0$. Then $W_a$ has the section $s=t=0$ over $\operatorname{Spec}O'$. The image of $U\to W_a$ is open by \cite[Tag 03WT]{StacksAlg} and contains the closed point of this section. Its inverse image on $\operatorname{Spec}O'$ is an open neighbourhood of the closed point, hence the whole local spectrum. In particular the image contains the generic point of the section, the node $(s,t)=(0,0)$ of $(W_0)_L$. Any point of $U_L$ mapping to this node is itself a node by \cite[Tag 0C57]{StacksAlg}. On the other hand $U_L\to\mathcal X_L$ is \textup{\'etale} and $\mathcal X_L$ is smooth, so $U_L$ is smooth; preservation of smoothness under \textup{\'etale} precomposition is included in \cite[Tag 036U]{StacksAlg}. This contradiction proves $a\ne0$. Together with $a\in L^{\circ\circ}$ it gives $0<|a|<1$.}
{最后假设 $\mathcal X_K$ 光滑，并反设 $a=0$。此时 $W_a$ 在 $\operatorname{Spec}O'$ 上具有截面 $s=t=0$。由 \cite[Tag 03WT]{StacksAlg}，$U\to W_a$ 的像为开集，且包含该截面的闭点。将此像拉回到 $\operatorname{Spec}O'$，得到包含闭点的开邻域，因而等于整个局部谱。特别地，像包含该截面的泛点，即 $(W_0)_L$ 的结点 $(s,t)=(0,0)$。由 \cite[Tag 0C57]{StacksAlg}，$U_L$ 中映到此结点的任一点也是结点。另一方面，$U_L\to\mathcal X_L$ 为 \textup{\'etale}，而 $\mathcal X_L$ 光滑，所以 $U_L$ 光滑；光滑性在 \textup{\'etale} 前复合下保持包含于 \cite[Tag 036U]{StacksAlg}。矛盾证明 $a\ne0$。再结合 $a\in L^{\circ\circ}$，得到 $0<|a|<1$。}
\end{proof}
