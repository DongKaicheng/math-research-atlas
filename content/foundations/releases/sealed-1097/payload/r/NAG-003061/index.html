Uniform norm preservation

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1832

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[Uniform norm preservation]\label{lem:uniform}
Let $B$ be a unital non-Archimedean Banach $K$-algebra with submultiplicative norm, and let $R\subset B$ be a norm-dense $K$-subalgebra. Given $f_1,\ldots,f_m\in B^\times$, there are $g_i\in R$ such that
\[
 \|g_i-f_i\|<\varepsilon,
 \qquad 0<\varepsilon<\min_i\|f_i^{-1}\|^{-1}.
\]
For every $z\in\mathcal M(B)$, $|g_i(z)|=|f_i(z)|$. Each $g_i$ is also a unit of $B$.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Choose one positive $\varepsilon$ satisfying the displayed finite list of inequalities and then approximate each $f_i$ by density. By contractivity of bounded multiplicative seminorms (\hyperref[fnd:boundedspectrumcompact]{\texttt{NAG-001496}}),
\[
 |f_i(z)|\geq\|f_i^{-1}\|^{-1}>\varepsilon>|g_i(z)-f_i(z)|.
\]
The ultrametric inequality gives equality of the first two absolute values. The element $u_i=f_i^{-1}(g_i-f_i)$ has norm less than one. The convergent series $\sum_{j\geq0}(-u_i)^j$ is the inverse of $1+u_i$, proving invertibility of $g_i$. For an empty tuple there is nothing to approximate.
\end{proof}
