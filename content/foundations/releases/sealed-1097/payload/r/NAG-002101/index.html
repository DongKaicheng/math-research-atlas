Insertion inside a smooth residual tube

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Insertion inside a smooth residual tube}
{在光滑剩余管域内插入顶点}]
\label{cand:smoothtubeinsertion}
\bi{Let $q$ be a rational smooth special point of a nodal
curve model $\mathcal X$, and let $z$ be the open-disc
coordinate supplied by an algebraic smooth chart in
Corollary~\ref{cand:smoothpointpolydisctube}. If
$a,\lambda\in K$ satisfy}
{设 $q$ 为节点曲线模型 $\mathcal X$ 的有理光滑特殊点，
且 $z$ 为 Corollary~\ref{cand:smoothpointpolydisctube}
中代数光滑坐标片所给出的开圆盘坐标。若
$a,\lambda\in K$ 满足}
\[
 |a|<1,\qquad 0<|\lambda|<1,
\]
\bi{then an algebraic blowup supported at $q$ gives a new
nodal model containing $\zeta_{a,|\lambda|}$ as a component
point. It retains all old component points and adds one
projective-line special component, attached at the old
component through $q$. Its new edge has length
$-\log|\lambda|$. Projectivity of $\mathcal X/O$ is preserved.}
{则存在支撑于 $q$ 的代数 blowup，给出以
$\zeta_{a,|\lambda|}$ 为分量点的新节点模型。它保留全部旧
分量点，并增加一个射影直线特殊分量，连接于经过 $q$
的旧分量。新增边长为 $-\log|\lambda|$。
$\mathcal X/O$ 的射影性保持。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Translate the algebraic smooth coordinate by a lift of its
residue value at $q$, so that its tube coordinate is $z$.
Shrink its Zariski chart until $q$ is the only special point
with $\bar z=0$; the fibre of an \textup{\'etale} morphism
over that point is finite on the chosen affine chart.
The ideal $(\lambda,z-a)$ is then the unit ideal off $q$.
Glue it with the unit ideal on $\mathcal X\setminus\{q\}$.
It is finite type and its formal completion is open.}
{将代数光滑坐标平移其在 $q$ 处剩余值的一个提升，使所得
管域坐标为 $z$。缩小其 Zariski 坐标片，使 $q$ 成为唯一
满足 $\bar z=0$ 的特殊点；\textup{\'etale} 态射在该点
上方的纤维，在所选仿射片上有限。此时
$(\lambda,z-a)$ 在 $q$ 外为单位理想，因此可与
$\mathcal X\setminus\{q\}$ 上的单位理想粘合。所得理想
有限型，其形式完备化为开理想。}

\bi{Flat base change of the algebraic blowup
\cite[Tag 0805]{StacksAlg} reduces the calculation to
Corollary~\ref{fnd:standarddiscblowupcalculation}, with
$T=z-a$ and scale $\lambda$. The charts are}
{由代数 blowup 的平坦基变换
\cite[Tag 0805]{StacksAlg}，计算归结为
Corollary~\ref{fnd:standarddiscblowupcalculation}，
其中 $T=z-a$、尺度为 $\lambda$。两个坐标片是}
\[
 T=\lambda u,\quad O[u],
 \qquad\text{\bi{and}{以及}}\qquad
 O[T,v]/(Tv-\lambda).
\]
\bi{Their completions, exceptional projective line, reduced
nodal special fibre and generic-fibre identification are
exactly those proved there. Pullback to the chosen
\textup{\'etale} chart introduces no extra exceptional
components: its fibre over the selected rational point is a
single rational point after the preceding shrinking.
The exceptional component point is the Gauss point of
$|z-a|\leq|\lambda|$, and its attaching annulus is
$|\lambda|<|z-a|<1$. The old model is unchanged away
from $q$. Flatness and finite presentation follow as in
Lemma~\ref{cand:nodefittinginsertion}, while
\cite[Tags 02NS and 0C4P]{StacksAlg} preserves projectivity.}
{这些坐标片的完备化、例外射影直线、约化节点特殊纤维及
泛纤维识别，正是该推论已证明的结论。拉回到所选
\textup{\'etale} 坐标片不引入额外例外分量，因为经过上述
缩小，所选有理点上方的纤维只有一个有理点。例外分量点为
$|z-a|\leq|\lambda|$ 的 Gauss 点，其连接圆环为
$|\lambda|<|z-a|<1$。旧模型在 $q$ 外不变。平坦性及
有限表示性如 Lemma~\ref{cand:nodefittinginsertion}
中所证，而 \cite[Tags 02NS and 0C4P]{StacksAlg}
保持射影性。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Let $K$ be complete and nontrivially valued.
We write $O=K^\circ$, $\mathfrak m=K^{\circ\circ}$ and
$k=O/\mathfrak m$. A nodal curve model in this section is a flat
finitely presented $O$-scheme with smooth generic fibre, reduced
nodal special fibre, and Hausdorff formal generic fibre.}
{设 $K$ 完备且非平凡赋值。
记 $O=K^\circ$、$\mathfrak m=K^{\circ\circ}$、
$k=O/\mathfrak m$。本节中的节点曲线模型是平坦有限表示
$O$-概形，其泛纤维光滑、特殊纤维约化且仅有节点，并且
形式泛纤维为 Hausdorff 空间。}
