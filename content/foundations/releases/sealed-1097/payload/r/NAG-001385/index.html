Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Kiehl coherence input}{Kiehl coherence input}]\label{fnd:kiehlcoherence}
\bi{With the preceding affinoid structure sheaf, finite $A$-modules are equivalent to coherent $\mathcal O_{X_G}$-modules. The functor sends $M$ to the sheaf with sections $M\otimes_A A_V$ on affinoid domains; a quasi-inverse is global sections. Here a coherent module is locally finitely generated, and for every analytic domain $U$, the kernel of every map $\mathcal O_{U_G}^n\to\mathcal F|_{U_G}$ is locally finitely generated.}
{对上述 affinoid structure sheaf，finite $A$-modules 与 coherent $\mathcal O_{X_G}$-modules 等价。该 functor 将 $M$ 送到在 affinoid domains 上 sections 为 $M\otimes_A A_V$ 的 sheaf；global sections 给出 quasi-inverse。这里 coherent module 指 locally finitely generated，且对每个 analytic domain $U$，每个 map $\mathcal O_{U_G}^n\to\mathcal F|_{U_G}$ 的 kernel 都 locally finitely generated。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainkiehl} proves the equivalence on the finite affinoid-domain site, including effective gluing of arbitrary finite local modules. Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb} transports it to the full analytic $G$-site through the ringed-topos comparison. Its coherence condition is local finite generation together with local finite generation of the stated kernels, so it is exactly the condition here. Evaluating on an affinoid domain gives $M\otimes_A A_V$; evaluating on $X$ recovers $M$. Restriction to the compact-domain basis preserves both the sheaf and these local properties.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainkiehl} 在有限 affinoid 域 site 上证明范畴等价，其中包括任意有限局部模的有效粘合。Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb} 通过赋环 topoi 比较，将等价传到完整的解析 $G$-site。其凝聚性条件为局部有限生成以及所述核的局部有限生成，恰与此处条件一致。在 affinoid 域上取截面得到 $M\otimes_A A_V$，在 $X$ 上取截面恢复 $M$。限制到紧域构成的基保留该层及这些局部性质。}
\end{proof}
