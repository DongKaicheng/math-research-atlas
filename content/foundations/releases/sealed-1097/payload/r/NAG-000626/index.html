Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}\label{new:rationalpoints}
\bi{The map $\mathcal M(B)\to\mathcal M(A)$ is a homeomorphism onto $D$. For $x\in D$, the corresponding evaluation sends $T_i$ to $f_i(x)/g(x)$ in $\mathcal H(x)$. In particular $g$ is a unit in $B$.}
{Map $\mathcal M(B)\to\mathcal M(A)$ 是到 $D$ 的 homeomorphism。对 $x\in D$，相应 evaluation 将 $T_i$ 送到 $\mathcal H(x)$ 中的 $f_i(x)/g(x)$。特别地，$g$ 在 $B$ 中为 unit。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Write $a_0g+\sum_i a_if_i=1$ in $A$. In $B$ this becomes $g(a_0+\sum_i a_iT_i)=1$. Thus $g$ is invertible. A point of $B$ satisfies $|T_i|\leq r_i$, and hence its image belongs to $D$. Conversely, if $x\in D$ and $g(x)=0$, then every $f_i(x)$ is zero, contradicting the displayed unit-ideal relation. Therefore $g(x)\ne0$ and the quotients $f_i(x)/g(x)$ have sizes at most $r_i$. Substitution into a convergent power series defines a bounded homomorphism to $\mathcal H(x)$, which kills the defining relations of $B$. This gives a point over $x$.}
{在 $A$ 中写出 $a_0g+\sum_i a_if_i=1$。在 $B$ 中这变为 $g(a_0+\sum_i a_iT_i)=1$，故 $g$ invertible。$B$ 的每个 point 满足 $|T_i|\leq r_i$，所以其 image 属于 $D$。反之，若 $x\in D$ 且 $g(x)=0$，则所有 $f_i(x)$ 均为零，与上述 unit-ideal relation 矛盾。因此 $g(x)\ne0$，且 quotients $f_i(x)/g(x)$ 的 sizes 不超过 $r_i$。将它们代入 convergent power series 得到到 $\mathcal H(x)$ 的 bounded homomorphism，且该 map 消去 $B$ 的 defining relations，从而给出 $x$ 之上的点。}

\bi{For any point over $x$, the relations force those same values of the variables in the completed residue field. Values on polynomial expressions are therefore fixed by the seminorm of $x$ on fractions with denominator a power of $g$. Such polynomial expressions are dense in $B$, and bounded seminorms are continuous, so the point is unique. Finally, the map is continuous, its source is compact and its target is Hausdorff. A continuous bijection onto $D$ is consequently a homeomorphism.}
{对 $x$ 之上的任何点，relations 都强制 variables 在 completed residue field 中取上述值。因此 polynomial expressions 的 seminorm values 由 $x$ 对分母为 $g$ 的幂的 fractions 的 seminorm 唯一确定。这些 polynomial expressions 在 $B$ 中 dense，而 bounded seminorms continuous，所以点唯一。最后，该 map continuous，source compact，target Hausdorff；故到 $D$ 的 continuous bijection 是 homeomorphism。}
\end{proof}

{Rational-domain presentation 将有限个 inequalities 转化为 Banach algebra。设 $g,f_1,\ldots,f_m\in A$ 生成 unit ideal，且 $r_i>0$。令}
\[
 D=\{x\in\mathcal M(A):|f_i(x)|\leq r_i|g(x)|\ (1\leq i\leq m)\},
\]
\[
 B=A\{r_1^{-1}T_1,\ldots,r_m^{-1}T_m\}/(gT_i-f_i)_{i=1}^m.
\]
