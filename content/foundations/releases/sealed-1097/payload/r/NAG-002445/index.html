A nonrational component cannot disappear

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 825

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[A nonrational component cannot disappear]
\label{v038:f5:essentialcomponentobstruction}
Let $C/K$ be a smooth projective geometrically connected curve.
Suppose $\mathcal Y/\Omega^\circ$ is a semistable algebraic
model of $C_\Omega$, and let $w$ be the component vertex of
a special component $E$ whose function field
$\overline k(E)$ is not rational.
Then every semistable algebraic model $\mathcal X/O$ of $C$
satisfies
\[
             w\in V(\mathcal X_{\Omega^\circ}),
                  \qquad p(w)\in V(\mathcal X).
\]
Consequently, if $C$ has such an original-field model, the
point $v=p(w)$ must satisfy
\[
 \begin{aligned}
   |\mathcal H(v)^\times|&=|K^\times|,\\
   \widetilde{\mathcal H(v)}/k
       &\text{ is separably generated of transcendence degree one}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Failure of either condition at $v$ rules out every original-field
semistable algebraic model, including every such marked model.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Assume $\mathcal X$ exists. Its coefficient extension
$\mathcal X_{\Omega^\circ}$ is a semistable algebraic model
of the same curve as $\mathcal Y$, by
Lemma~\ref{cand:algebraicmodelscalar} and preservation
of the nodal relative-curve condition under base change,
\cite[Tag 0C5B]{StacksAlg}.
Apply Theorem~\ref{v037:f5:commondomination} over the complete
field $\Omega$, with empty marking. It supplies an actual
semistable model $\mathcal T/\Omega^\circ$ and
generic-identity morphisms
\[
  \mathcal T\longrightarrow\mathcal Y,
             \qquad
  \mathcal T\longrightarrow\mathcal X_{\Omega^\circ}.
\]
The first morphism and
Proposition~\ref{v036:f5:forestnecessity} show that
$w\in V(\mathcal T)$. Let $F$ be its special component
in $\mathcal T$. The actual residue-field identifications
of Proposition~\ref{v036:f5:vertexarithmetic}, applied
over $\Omega$, give
\[
       \overline k(F)=\widetilde{\mathcal H(w)}
                                  =\overline k(E).
\]
If $w$ were absent from $V(\mathcal X_{\Omega^\circ})$,
the necessity of the geometric forest condition for the
second morphism would make $F$ a smooth
$\mathbf P^1_{\overline k}$. Its function field would be
rational, contrary to the displayed equality. Hence $w$
is a vertex of the coefficient extension of $\mathcal X$.
The geometric vertex identification in
Proposition~\ref{v036:f5:vertexarithmetic} projects it to
the original vertex $v=p(w)$ and gives the two asserted
arithmetic conditions. Forgetting a marking retains a
semistable algebraic model, so the same obstruction applies
to every prescribed marking.
\end{proof}
