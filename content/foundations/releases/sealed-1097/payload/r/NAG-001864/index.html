Universal injectivity from a generic fibre isomorphism

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 440

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Universal injectivity from a generic fibre isomorphism}{由泛纤维同构判定普遍单射}]
\label{fnd:genericfibreuniversalinjectivity}
\bi{Let $T\to X$ be quasi-smooth between good analytic spaces, let $t\in T$ and $x=f(t)$, and let $u:L\to N$ be a morphism of coherent sheaves with $L$ free of finite rank. Suppose that}
{设 $T\to X$ 是 good 解析空间之间的 quasi-smooth 态射，$t\in T$、$x=f(t)$，并设 $u:L\to N$ 为凝聚层之间的态射，其中 $L$ 有限秩自由。假设}
\[
 t\in\overline{\operatorname{Bij}(u_{T_x})}^{\,T_x},
\]
\bi{where closure means ordinary topological closure. Then the following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $u$ is injective at every point over $t$ after every good analytic base change over every complete extension of the ground field;
\item $u$ is injective at $t$;
\item $t\in\overline{\operatorname{Bij}(u)\cap T_x}^{\,T_x}$.
\end{enumerate}}
{其中闭包指普通拓扑闭包。则以下条件等价：
\begin{enumerate}
\item 在原基域的任意完备扩域上作任意 good 解析基变换后，$u$ 在每个位于 $t$ 上方的点处均为单射；
\item $u$ 在 $t$ 处为单射；
\item $t\in\overline{\operatorname{Bij}(u)\cap T_x}^{\,T_x}$。
\end{enumerate}}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{All assertions are ordinary-local near the points, so take $T=\mathcal M(B)$ and $X=\mathcal M(A)$ affinoid. Put $F=\mathcal H(x)$ and $C=B\widehat\otimes_A F$. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion}, the fibre is geometrically regular. Lemma~\ref{fnd:regularaffinoidcomponents} gives an integral component $C_0=C/\mathfrak q$ containing $t$. Choose an analytic point $z$ of this component whose kernel on $C$ is $\mathfrak q$. If $\mathfrak p,\mathfrak p'\subset B$ are the kernels of $t,z$, then $\mathfrak p'\subset\mathfrak p$. The assumption says that $u_C$ is an isomorphism at $\mathfrak q$: a Zariski-open subset of this integral component is ordinarily dense exactly when it is nonempty, by Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity}.}
{各断言在所论点附近对普通拓扑局部，因此可取 $T=\mathcal M(B)$、$X=\mathcal M(A)$ 为 affinoid。令 $F=\mathcal H(x)$、$C=B\widehat\otimes_A F$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion}，纤维几何正则。Lemma~\ref{fnd:regularaffinoidcomponents} 给出包含 $t$ 的整分量 $C_0=C/\mathfrak q$，并可在此分量中选择解析点 $z$，使其在 $C$ 上的核为 $\mathfrak q$。若 $\mathfrak p,\mathfrak p'\subset B$ 分别是 $t,z$ 的核，则 $\mathfrak p'\subset\mathfrak p$。假设说明 $u_C$ 在 $\mathfrak q$ 处为同构：由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity}，此整分量的 Zariski 开集普通稠密当且仅当它非空。}

\bi{The ordinary stalk at any analytic point is faithfully flat over the localization of its affinoid algebra at the point's kernel, by the same rational-neighbourhood argument used in Lemma~\ref{fnd:regularaffinoidcomponents}. Therefore injectivity and bijectivity of our finite-module maps can be tested at these algebraic localizations. Their bijectivity locus is Zariski open: its complement is the union of the supports of the finite kernel and cokernel.}
{任意解析点的普通茎，在 affinoid 代数对该点之核的局部化上忠实平坦；证明与 Lemma~\ref{fnd:regularaffinoidcomponents} 中的 rational 邻域论证相同。因此有限模映射的单射性与双射性可以在这些代数局部化处检测。双射 locus 为 Zariski 开集，因为其补集是有限核与有限余核的支撑之并。}

\bi{Assume (2). The map $u_{B_{\mathfrak p}}$ is injective, hence so is $u_{B_{\mathfrak p'}}$. At $z$ the fibre map is bijective, so the cokernel of $u$ tensored with $\mathcal H(z)$ vanishes. Nakayama's lemma at $B_{\mathfrak p'}$ implies that this cokernel is zero there. Thus $u_{B_{\mathfrak p'}}$ is an isomorphism. Its Zariski-open bijectivity locus contains the generic point of the fibre component, hence is ordinarily dense in that component. It contains $t$ in its closure, proving (3).}
{假设 (2) 成立。映射 $u_{B_{\mathfrak p}}$ 单射，局部化后 $u_{B_{\mathfrak p'}}$ 亦单射。在 $z$ 处纤维映射双射，所以 $u$ 的余核与 $\mathcal H(z)$ 张量后为零。在 $B_{\mathfrak p'}$ 处应用 Nakayama 引理，说明该余核在那里为零。于是 $u_{B_{\mathfrak p'}}$ 为同构，其 Zariski 开的双射 locus 包含纤维分量的泛点，故在该分量中普通稠密，其闭包包含 $t$，即得 (3)。}

\bi{For the converse, both the hypothesis and (3) remain valid after a complete scalar extension at every point above $t$. Indeed Lemma~\ref{fnd:regularfibredensitybasechange} preserves the relevant fibre closures; scalar extension is flat on finite affinoid modules, so it identifies the fibre bijectivity locus with its pullback, and pullback of the total bijectivity locus is contained in the new one. Extend the ground field to $F=\mathcal H(x)$, choose the canonical $F$-rational lift of $x$ and a compatible lift of $t$, and work there. Injectivity descends back by Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}. We may therefore assume $x$ is rational.}
{为证明反向断言，先说明假设与 (3) 在任意完备扩标量后、在每个位于 $t$ 上方的点处仍成立。确实，Lemma~\ref{fnd:regularfibredensitybasechange} 保持所需纤维闭包；扩标量对有限 affinoid 模平坦，所以纤维双射 locus 等于其拉回，而原总空间的双射 locus 的拉回包含于新双射 locus。将基域扩到 $F=\mathcal H(x)$，选择 $x$ 的典范 $F$-有理提升及 $t$ 的相容提升，并在这些点处工作。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}，单射性可以下降回原处。因此可假设 $x$ 为有理点。}

\bi{Write $\mathfrak m=\ker(A\to F)$. Then $C=B/\mathfrak mB$. The map $A_{\mathfrak m}\to B_{\mathfrak p}$ is flat. To see this directly from analytic flatness, the local ring $\mathcal O_{T,t}$ is flat over $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ and hence over $A_{\mathfrak m}$; it is faithfully flat over $B_{\mathfrak p}$. Tensoring kernels with this latter faithfully flat algebra descends the asserted flatness. The ring $B_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak mB_{\mathfrak p}=C_{\mathfrak p/\mathfrak mB}$ is a regular local domain. Each element of $B_{\mathfrak p}\setminus\mathfrak p'B_{\mathfrak p}$ has nonzero image in that domain. Lemma~\ref{fnd:flatnonzerodivisorlifting} shows that it is a non-zero-divisor in $B_{\mathfrak p}$. Hence}
{记 $\mathfrak m=\ker(A\to F)$，则 $C=B/\mathfrak mB$。映射 $A_{\mathfrak m}\to B_{\mathfrak p}$ 平坦，可直接从解析平坦性看出：局部环 $\mathcal O_{T,t}$ 在 $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 上平坦，故也在 $A_{\mathfrak m}$ 上平坦；它又在 $B_{\mathfrak p}$ 上忠实平坦。将待检验的核与后一忠实平坦代数张量，即可下降所称平坦性。环 $B_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak mB_{\mathfrak p}=C_{\mathfrak p/\mathfrak mB}$ 是正则局部整环。$B_{\mathfrak p}\setminus\mathfrak p'B_{\mathfrak p}$ 中的每个元素在该整环中的像非零，因此由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:flatnonzerodivisorlifting}，它在 $B_{\mathfrak p}$ 中是非零因子。于是}
\[
 B_{\mathfrak p}\longrightarrow B_{\mathfrak p'}
 \quad\hbox{and}\quad
 L_{\mathfrak p}\longrightarrow L_{\mathfrak p'}
\]
\bi{are injective. By (3), $u_{\mathfrak p'}$ is bijective. The commutative localization square then makes $u_{\mathfrak p}$ injective, and faithful flat comparison with the ordinary stalk proves (2).}
{均为单射。由 (3)，$u_{\mathfrak p'}$ 为双射。局部化的交换方块遂说明 $u_{\mathfrak p}$ 为单射，再与普通茎作忠实平坦比较，得到 (2)。}

\bi{Finally (1) implies (2) by the identity base change. Conversely assume (2), hence (3), and make any good analytic base change, over any complete extension. At an image $x'$ the new fibre is the complete scalar extension of $T_x$ to $\mathcal H(x')$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:regularfibredensitybasechange} preserves the initial fibre hypothesis and the closure in (3); bijectivity on the total space is preserved by pullback. Quasi-smoothness is stable under this base change by Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobiansmoothdomainbasechange}. The implication (3)$\Rightarrow$(2), already proved, therefore gives injectivity at each point above $t$. This is (1).}
{最后，取恒等基变换便知 (1) 蕴含 (2)。反之，假设 (2)，因而 (3) 成立，并在任意完备扩域上作任意 good 解析基变换。在新的像点 $x'$ 处，纤维是 $T_x$ 到 $\mathcal H(x')$ 的完备扩标量。Lemma~\ref{fnd:regularfibredensitybasechange} 保持初始纤维假设及 (3) 中的闭包；总空间上的双射性也在拉回后保持。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobiansmoothdomainbasechange}，quasi-smooth 性在此基变换下保持。因此已证明的 (3)$\Rightarrow$(2) 给出每个位于 $t$ 上方的点处的单射性，正是 (1)。}
\end{proof}
