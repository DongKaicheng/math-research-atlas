2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Algebraic comparison used in this book}{本书使用的 algebraic comparison}]\label{fnd:gaga}
\bi{Analytification of finite-type $K$-schemes commutes with fibre products and complete scalar extension. It preserves closed immersions, finite morphisms, smooth morphisms and proper morphisms. Analytifications have no analytic boundary over $K$. For a proper $K$-scheme $Z$, analytification gives an equivalence between algebraic and analytic coherent sheaves, compatible with coherent cohomology. In particular algebraic line bundles and their homomorphisms are recovered from the analytic ones.}
{Finite-type $K$-schemes 的 analytification 与 fibre products 及 complete scalar extension 相容，并保持 closed immersions、finite morphisms、smooth morphisms 和 proper morphisms。Analytifications 在 $K$ 上无 analytic boundary。对 proper $K$-scheme $Z$，analytification 给出 algebraic 与 analytic coherent sheaves 的 equivalence，且与 coherent cohomology 相容。特别地，algebraic line bundles 及其 homomorphisms 可从 analytic ones 恢复。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:analytificationproducts} proves compatibility
with fibre products and every complete scalar extension.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:analytificationfiniteclosed} proves preservation
of closed immersions and finite morphisms.
Corollary~\ref{fnd:analytificationsmoothpreservation} proves
preservation of smooth morphisms, using the ordinary-open
\'etale local definition of analytic smoothness.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:analytificationproperpreservation} proves
preservation of proper morphisms.
Applying Proposition~\ref{fnd:algebraicmorphismboundaryless}
to the structure morphism gives the assertion about the boundary.
Finally Theorem~\ref{fnd:propergagainternal} is the asserted
equivalence for a proper scheme, including its restriction
to line bundles and full faithfulness on their homomorphisms;
its cohomology comparison is
Theorem~\ref{fnd:propercoherentcomparison}.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:analytificationproducts} 证明与纤维积及任意
完备扩标量的相容性。Theorem~\ref{fnd:analytificationfiniteclosed}
证明闭浸入及有限态射的保持。
Corollary~\ref{fnd:analytificationsmoothpreservation} 使用
通常开集上的 \'etale 局部定义证明解析化保持光滑态射。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:analytificationproperpreservation} 证明
适当态射的保持。将 Proposition~\ref{fnd:algebraicmorphismboundaryless}
用于结构态射，即得无边界断言。
最后，Theorem~\ref{fnd:propergagainternal} 给出适当概形上的
所述等价，包括在线丛上的限制及对线丛同态的全忠实性；
上同调比较为 Theorem~\ref{fnd:propercoherentcomparison}。}
\end{proof}
