Dimension as a maximum over analytic points

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 605

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Dimension as a maximum over analytic points}
{由解析点的最大值恢复维数}]
\label{fnd:affinoidabhyankarmaximum}
\bi{For every nonempty generalized affinoid space $X=\mathcal M(A)$
over any complete non-Archimedean field with its specified absolute
value,}
{对任意完备非阿基米德域上的非空广义 affinoid 空间
$X=\mathcal M(A)$，保持基域上指定的绝对值，有}
\[
             \dim_K X=\max_{x\in X}d_K(x).
\]
\bi{In particular every $d_K(x)$ is finite. This includes
nonreduced algebras, trivial valuations, and arbitrary real radii.}
{特别地，每个 $d_K(x)$ 均有限。该结论包括非约化代数、
平凡赋值及任意实半径。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{\textbf{Step 1: the strict upper bound.}
Suppose $A$ is strict and fix $x$. Its kernel $\mathfrak p$ is
prime, and $B=A/\mathfrak p$ is a strict integral affinoid algebra.
The evaluated fraction fields of $A$ and $B$ at this point
coincide, so their completed residue fields coincide.
Choose a finite normalization}
{\textbf{步骤 1：strict 情形的上界。}
设 $A$ 为 strict，并固定 $x$。其核 $\mathfrak p$ 是素理想，
$B=A/\mathfrak p$ 为 strict affinoid 整环。
$A,B$ 在该点求值后的分式域相同，故其完备剩余域相同。
选择有限正规化}
\[
 R=K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_e\rangle\hookrightarrow B,\qquad
 e=\dim B\le\dim A
\]
\bi{using Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization} and
Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttatedimension}. Let $z$ be the image of $x$ in
$\mathcal M(R)$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitemorphismresiduefinite}
gives $d_K(x)=d_K(z)$.}
{其中使用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization} 及
Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttatedimension}。以 $z$ 记 $x$ 在
$\mathcal M(R)$ 中的像。由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitemorphismresiduefinite}，
$d_K(x)=d_K(z)$。}

\bi{The field $\mathcal H_R(z)$ is the completion of
$\operatorname{Frac}(K[T_1,\ldots,T_e]/\mathfrak q)$,
where $\mathfrak q$ is the kernel on the polynomial subalgebra.
Indeed polynomials are dense in $R$ for its Gauss norm and
evaluation at $z$ is contractive. After approximating numerator
and denominator, with denominator error smaller than its
nonzero value, the same assertion holds for fractions.
This proves the claimed density in the completed residue field.
The polynomial fraction field has transcendence degree at most
$e$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:valuedfieldabhyankar} therefore gives
$d_K(z)\le e\le\dim A$. This argument uses the polynomial
fraction field, not the generally much larger fraction field
of the Tate algebra.}
{域 $\mathcal H_R(z)$ 是
$\operatorname{Frac}(K[T_1,\ldots,T_e]/\mathfrak q)$ 的完备化，
其中 $\mathfrak q$ 是在多项式子代数上的求值核。
事实上，多项式相对于 Gauss 范数在 $R$ 中稠密，
而在 $z$ 处求值为 contractive。对分子、分母分别逼近，
并使分母误差小于其非零值，同样得到分式的逼近。
这证明了它在完备剩余域中的稠密性。
该多项式分式域的超越次数至多为 $e$。
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:valuedfieldabhyankar}，
$d_K(z)\le e\le\dim A$。这里使用的是多项式分式域，
而不是通常大得多的 Tate 代数分式域。}

\bi{\textbf{Step 2: a strict point attaining the bound.}
Choose a prime quotient $B=A/\mathfrak p$ with
$\dim B=\dim A=d$. Such a quotient exists: a prime chain
of maximal length starts at such a prime. The dimension is finite:
a strict presentation $R_n\twoheadrightarrow A$ identifies every
prime chain of $A$ with a prime chain of $R_n$, whose dimension
is $n$ by Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttatedimension}.
Normalize $B$ finitely over $R_d$. Proposition
\ref{fnd:normalizationfiniteboundary} gives a point $x$ above
the unit Gauss point $\eta$ of $R_d$.
Polynomial density identifies $\mathcal H(\eta)$ with the
completion of the unit Gauss field $K(T_1,\ldots,T_d)$.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gaussvaluedfielddimension} gives $d_K(\eta)=d$,
and Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitemorphismresiduefinite} gives
$d_K(x)=d$. Its image in $\mathcal M(A)$ has the same residue
field, proving attainment.}
{\textbf{步骤 2：达到上界的 strict 点。}
选择素商 $B=A/\mathfrak p$，使 $\dim B=\dim A=d$。
这样的商存在：一条最大长度的素理想链的起点即满足此条件。
其维数有限：strict 表示 $R_n\twoheadrightarrow A$
将 $A$ 中的每条素理想链识别为 $R_n$ 中的素理想链，
而由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttatedimension}，后者的维数为 $n$。
将 $B$ 有限正规化到 $R_d$ 上。
Proposition~\ref{fnd:normalizationfiniteboundary} 给出
$R_d$ 的单位 Gauss 点 $\eta$ 上方的点 $x$。
多项式稠密性将 $\mathcal H(\eta)$ 识别为
单位 Gauss 域 $K(T_1,\ldots,T_d)$ 的完备化。
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gaussvaluedfielddimension}，
$d_K(\eta)=d$，再由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitemorphismresiduefinite}，
$d_K(x)=d$。它在 $\mathcal M(A)$ 中的像具有相同剩余域，
因此上界实际取到。}

\bi{\textbf{Step 3: arbitrary radii.}
Choose $L=K_r$ making $A_L$ strict by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification}. Its dimension
is $d=\dim_KX$ by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticdimensionindependence}.
For every $x\in X$, the Gauss-section equality in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:freegaussresidualdimension} and Step 1 give}
{\textbf{步骤 3：任意半径。}
由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification}，
选择 $L=K_r$ 使 $A_L$ 为 strict。
根据 Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticdimensionindependence}，
其维数为 $d=\dim_KX$。
对每个 $x\in X$，由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:freegaussresidualdimension} 的 Gauss
截面等式及步骤 1，得到}
\[
             d_K(x)=d_L(\sigma_r(x))\le d.
\]
\bi{By Step 2 choose $y\in\mathcal M_L(A_L)$ with $d_L(y)=d$,
and let $x$ be its image. The other inequality in that same
proposition gives $d\le d_K(x)$. Combining both bounds gives
$d_K(x)=d$. Every construction used the original absolute values.}
{由步骤 2，选择 $y\in\mathcal M_L(A_L)$，使 $d_L(y)=d$，
并以 $x$ 记其像。同一命题中的另一不等式给出
$d\le d_K(x)$。结合两边即得 $d_K(x)=d$。
所有构造均使用原来的绝对值。}
\end{proof}
