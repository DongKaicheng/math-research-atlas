Completed residue fields of a finite morphism

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 604

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Completed residue fields of a finite morphism}
{有限态射的完备剩余域}]
\label{fnd:finitemorphismresiduefinite}
\bi{Let $A\to B$ be a bounded homomorphism of affinoid algebras
with $B$ finite as an $A$-module. If $y\in\mathcal M(B)$ lies
above $x\in\mathcal M(A)$, then $\mathcal H(y)/\mathcal H(x)$
is finite. Hence $d_K(y)=d_K(x)$.}
{设 $A\to B$ 是 affinoid 代数的有界同态，且 $B$ 作为
$A$-模有限。若 $y\in\mathcal M(B)$ 位于
$x\in\mathcal M(A)$ 上方，则
$\mathcal H(y)/\mathcal H(x)$ 有限，因而 $d_K(y)=d_K(x)$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $F=\mathcal H(x)$ and view it as a closed subfield of
$\mathcal H(y)$. If $b_1,\ldots,b_q$ generate $B$ over $A$,
the $F$-span of the evaluated $B$ is generated by their images.
This span is a finite-dimensional $F$-subalgebra and a domain.
Multiplication by a nonzero element is an injective endomorphism
of a finite-dimensional space, hence surjective, so this
subalgebra is a field. It is complete and closed, by the
finite-dimensional norm comparison in
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}. It contains the evaluated
fraction field of $B/\ker y$, which is dense in $\mathcal H(y)$.
It is therefore all of $\mathcal H(y)$. The invariant equality
follows from Lemma~\ref{fnd:valuedfielddimensioncalculus}.}
{令 $F=\mathcal H(x)$，将其视为 $\mathcal H(y)$ 中的闭子域。
若 $b_1,\ldots,b_q$ 在 $A$ 上生成 $B$，则求值后的 $B$ 的
$F$-线性包由这些元素的像生成。这个线性包是有限维
$F$-子代数且为整环。非零元素的乘法是有限维空间的单射，
故为满射，因此该子代数是域。由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 的有限维范数比较，
它完备且闭。它包含求值后的 $B/\ker y$ 的分式域，
而后者在 $\mathcal H(y)$ 中稠密，所以它就是
$\mathcal H(y)$。不变量相等由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:valuedfielddimensioncalculus} 得到。}
\end{proof}
