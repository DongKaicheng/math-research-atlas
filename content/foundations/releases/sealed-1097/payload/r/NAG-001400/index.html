NAG-001400 · Accepted whole

Ratios must be tested at the same point

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 64

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{example}[\bi{Ratios must be tested at the same point}{Ratios 必须在同一点检验}]\label{fnd:spectralquotientcounterexample}
\bi{Let $0<|\pi|<1$, $A=K\times K$, $f=(1,1)$ and $g=(1,\pi)$. For the maximum norm, $\rho(f)=\rho(g)=1$ but $\rho(f/g)=|\pi|^{-1}>1$. The domain $|f|\leq|g|$ contains only the first component. Its rational algebra is $K$, so the identity of $A$ cannot factor through it.}
{设 $0<|\pi|<1$，$A=K\times K$，$f=(1,1)$、$g=(1,\pi)$。对 maximum norm，$\rho(f)=\rho(g)=1$，但 $\rho(f/g)=|\pi|^{-1}>1$。Domain $|f|\leq|g|$ 只包含第一个 component。其 rational algebra 为 $K$，因此 $A$ 的恒等映射不能经它分解。}
\end{example}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The two spectral radii are read componentwise. The rational quotient $A\langle U\rangle/(gU-f)$ is the product of $K\langle U\rangle/(U-1)=K$ and $K\langle U\rangle/(\pi U-1)=0$. The first equality follows by monic division, and the second because $1-\pi U$ is a unit by geometric inversion. A factorization of the identity would kill the nonzero second idempotent of $A$, which is impossible.}
{两个 spectral radii 逐 component 计算。Rational quotient $A\langle U\rangle/(gU-f)$ 是 $K\langle U\rangle/(U-1)=K$ 与 $K\langle U\rangle/(\pi U-1)=0$ 的 product。前一个等式来自 monic division；后一个等式来自 geometric inversion，因为 $1-\pi U$ 为 unit。若恒等映射可如此分解，则 $A$ 的非零第二个 idempotent 会被消去，矛盾。}
\end{proof}
