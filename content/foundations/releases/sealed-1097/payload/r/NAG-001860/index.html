Generic analytic points in ordinary open sets

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 439

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Generic analytic points in ordinary open sets}{普通开集中的泛解析点}]
\label{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity}
\bi{Let $C$ be an integral generalized $F$-affinoid algebra. Every nonempty ordinary open subset of $\mathcal M(C)$ contains a point $z$ for which $C\to\mathcal H(z)$ is injective. More generally every prime ideal of a generalized affinoid algebra is the kernel of an analytic point.}
{设 $C$ 为整 generalized $F$-affinoid 代数。$\mathcal M(C)$ 的每个非空普通开集都包含一个点 $z$，使 $C\to\mathcal H(z)$ 为单射。更一般地，generalized affinoid 代数的每个素理想都是某个解析点的核。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First let $D$ be any nonzero integral affinoid algebra. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalfiniteboundary}, choose a finite spectral boundary $S\subset\mathcal M(D)$. If every point of $S$ had nonzero kernel, choose a nonzero element in each kernel and take their product $a$. The algebra $D$ is integral, so $a\ne0$, whereas $a$ vanishes on the whole finite boundary. Thus $\rho_D(a)=0$, contradicting Proposition~\ref{fnd:spectralnilradicalinternal}. At least one point has zero kernel. Applied to $D=C/\mathfrak p$, this also proves the last assertion for every prime $\mathfrak p$.}
{先设 $D$ 为任意非零 affinoid 整环。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalfiniteboundary}，选择有限谱边界 $S\subset\mathcal M(D)$。若其中每个点的核都非零，则从各核中选一个非零元素并取其乘积 $a$。由于 $D$ 为整环，有 $a\ne0$，但 $a$ 在整个有限边界上为零，故 $\rho_D(a)=0$，与 Proposition~\ref{fnd:spectralnilradicalinternal} 矛盾。因此至少一个点的核为零。对 $D=C/\mathfrak p$ 应用此结论，也证明了任意素理想 $\mathfrak p$ 的最后断言。}

\bi{Now choose a nonempty rational domain $V=\mathcal M(B)$ inside the given ordinary open set. The algebra $B$ is nonzero and flat over $C$ by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness}. Let $\mathfrak q$ be a minimal prime of $B$. Its contraction to $C$ is zero. Indeed, a nonzero $a\in C$ acts injectively on $B$ by flatness and hence on the nonzero ring $B_{\mathfrak q}$. If $a\in\mathfrak q$, its image in $B_{\mathfrak q}$ is nilpotent, since the only prime ideal of that ring is its maximal ideal; a nilpotent element cannot act injectively on a nonzero ring. The preceding paragraph supplies a point of $\mathcal M(B/\mathfrak q)$ with zero kernel. Its image in $V$ has kernel $\mathfrak q$ on $B$ and therefore zero kernel on $C$, as required.}
{现在在给定普通开集中选择非空 rational 域 $V=\mathcal M(B)$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness}，非零代数 $B$ 在 $C$ 上平坦。取 $B$ 的一个极小素理想 $\mathfrak q$，其在 $C$ 中的收缩为零。确实，任意非零 $a\in C$ 由平坦性在 $B$ 上单射作用，因而在非零环 $B_{\mathfrak q}$ 上也单射作用。若 $a\in\mathfrak q$，则其在 $B_{\mathfrak q}$ 中的像幂零，因为该环唯一的素理想是极大理想；非零环上的幂零元素不可能单射作用。上一段给出 $\mathcal M(B/\mathfrak q)$ 中核为零的点。它在 $V$ 中的像在 $B$ 上的核为 $\mathfrak q$，所以在 $C$ 上的核为零，正是所需结论。}
\end{proof}
