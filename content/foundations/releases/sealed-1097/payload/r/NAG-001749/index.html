The fibre over a rational base point

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 316

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The fibre over a rational base point}{有理底点上的纤维}]
\label{fnd:rationalfibrestalkquotient}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good spaces over a nontrivially valued field $K$, and suppose $x=f(y)$ is $K$-rational. Put $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$, $S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$. Then}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 为赋值非平凡的域 $K$ 上的 good 空间态射，且 $x=f(y)$ 为 $K$-有理点。令 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$，则}
\[
 \mathcal O_{Y_x,y}\simeq S/\mathfrak m_R S.
\]
\bi{If also $y$ is $K$-rational and a local relative presentation has $n$ variables and relation ideal $I$, then the cotangent-space dimension of this fibre local ring is $n$ minus the rank at $y$ of the relative Jacobian matrix of any finite generators of $I$.}
{若 $y$ 亦为 $K$-有理点，且一个局部相对表示具有 $n$ 个变量与关系理想 $I$，则该纤维局部环的余切空间维数，等于 $n$ 减去 $I$ 的任意有限组生成元的相对 Jacobian 矩阵在 $y$ 处的秩。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose affinoid neighborhoods $X_0=\mathcal M(A)$ and $Y_0=\mathcal M(B)$ with $f(Y_0)\subset X_0$. Evaluation at $x$ is a bounded quotient $A\to K$. Since $K$ is a finite $A$-module, Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange} identifies the fibre algebra with the ordinary quotient $B/\mathfrak m_xB$. In an affinoid presentation $A=K\{r^{-1}Z\}/J$, translate $x$ to $Z=0$. Monomial partition, as in Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalanalyticformalpresentation}, shows both that $\mathfrak m_x$ is generated by the $Z_i$ and that their germs generate $\mathfrak m_R$.}
{选择 affinoid 邻域 $X_0=\mathcal M(A)$、$Y_0=\mathcal M(B)$，使 $f(Y_0)\subset X_0$。$x$ 处取值是有界商映射 $A\to K$。因 $K$ 为有限 $A$-模，Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange} 将纤维代数识别为普通商 $B/\mathfrak m_xB$。在 affinoid 表示 $A=K\{r^{-1}Z\}/J$ 中，将 $x$ 平移至 $Z=0$。如 Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalanalyticformalpresentation} 中的单项式分组论证表明，$\mathfrak m_x$ 由 $Z_i$ 生成，其芽也生成 $\mathfrak m_R$。}

\bi{The point $y$ need not be rational. Take a cofinal system of ordinary affinoid neighborhoods $V$ of $y$ in $Y_0$. Since the fibre is a closed analytic subspace with its subspace topology, the intersections $V\cap Y_x$ are cofinal ordinary affinoid neighborhoods of $y$ in $Y_x$: any ordinary neighborhood in the closed subspace is the intersection with an ambient open neighborhood, inside which goodness supplies such a $V$. On every $V$, closed-subspace base change identifies the fibre algebra with $\mathcal O(V)/(Z_1,\ldots,Z_d)\mathcal O(V)$. Passing to the filtered colimit gives $\mathcal O_{Y_x,y}=S/(Z_1,\ldots,Z_d)S$: every representative lifts on the same $V$, and a representative vanishing in the quotient is, on some smaller $V$, a finite combination of these generators. Because their germs generate $\mathfrak m_R$, this is the asserted quotient.}
{此处 $y$ 不必为有理点。在 $Y_0$ 中取 $y$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域 $V$ 的一个余终系统。由于纤维是带子空间拓扑的闭解析子空间，交 $V\cap Y_x$ 在纤维中也构成 $y$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域余终系统：闭子空间中的任意普通邻域可写为与环境开邻域的交，而 good 性可在该环境邻域中选取这样的 $V$。在每个 $V$ 上，闭子空间基变换将纤维代数识别为 $\mathcal O(V)/(Z_1,\ldots,Z_d)\mathcal O(V)$。取滤过直极限便得 $\mathcal O_{Y_x,y}=S/(Z_1,\ldots,Z_d)S$：每个代表元在同一 $V$ 上可提升，在商中为零的代表元则在某个更小 $V$ 上为这些生成元的有限线性组合。因这些芽生成 $\mathfrak m_R$，即得所述商。}

\bi{At a rational $y$, its completed fibre ring is $K[[T_1,\ldots,T_n]]/\widehat I_x$, after translating its relative coordinates to zero. The maximal ideal modulo its square is consequently $K^n$ modulo the linear terms of the relations. Those terms are the rows of the relative Jacobian evaluated at $y$. Completion does not change this vector space: its quotient modulo the square of the maximal ideal is the second finite truncation in the definition of completion.}
{在有理点 $y$ 处，将相对坐标平移至零后，纤维环的完备化为
\[
 K[[T_1,\ldots,T_n]]/\widehat I_x.
\]
因此极大理想对其平方的商，等于 $K^n$ 对关系式线性项所张空间的商。这些线性项正是相对 Jacobian 在 $y$ 处取值后的各行。完备化不改变此向量空间，因为对极大理想平方的商就是完备化定义中的第二阶有限截断。}
\end{proof}
