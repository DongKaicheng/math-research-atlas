Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Finite \'etale germs and completed residue fields}{有限 \'etale 芽与完备剩余域}]
\label{fnd:goodetalegermresidueequivalence}
\bi{For a good analytic space $X$ and $x\in X$, passage to the analytic fibre induces an equivalence between finite \'etale germs over $(X,x)$ and finite \'etale $\mathcal H(x)$-algebras, with arrows reversed on the algebra side. In particular, any finite separable extension of $\mathcal H(x)$ is realized by a finite \'etale morphism over an ordinary neighborhood of $x$, with exactly one point above $x$.}
{对 good 解析空间 $X$ 及点 $x\in X$，取解析纤维在 $(X,x)$ 上的有限 \'etale 芽与有限 \'etale $\mathcal H(x)$-代数之间给出反变范畴等价。特别地，$\mathcal H(x)$ 的任一有限可分扩张，都由 $x$ 的某普通邻域上的有限 \'etale 态射实现，且在 $x$ 上方恰有一个点。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$, $k_0=\kappa(x)$ and $F=\mathcal H(x)$. Finite analytic maps near $x$ correspond to finite $R$-algebras: existence is Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization}, and morphisms descend after shrinking because the algebras have finite presentations. Explicitly, represent the images of finitely many algebra generators on a common neighborhood and shrink again to make their finitely many relations hold. All the resulting maps are bounded by finite-module automatic boundedness. Equality of two maps is tested on the same finite list. Finite \'etaleness corresponds to finite flatness and the vanishing of relative differentials, by Proposition~\ref{fnd:localfiniteetalecriterion}; these conditions spread from the germ by finite presentations and finite branch separation.}
{令 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$k_0=\kappa(x)$、$F=\mathcal H(x)$。$x$ 附近的有限解析映射对应有限 $R$-代数：存在性由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization} 给出，而因这些代数有限表示，态射经过缩小可下降。具体地，把有限个代数生成元的像在公共邻域上表示，再缩小使其有限组关系成立。有限模的自动有界性保证所得映射均有界；两个映射是否相等也只须检查这有限组生成元。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:localfiniteetalecriterion}，有限 \'etale 性对应有限平坦性与相对微分消失；有限表示与有限分支分离使这两个条件从芽延伸到邻域。}

\bi{We describe maps explicitly. A finite \'etale $R$-algebra is a finite product of local algebras by Corollary~\ref{fnd:ordinaryanalyticstalkhenselian}. A local factor $C$ is finite free over $R$, and its residue algebra is a finite separable field over $k_0$ by \href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02GL}{Stacks 02GL(1)}. Choose a primitive residue element, using \cite[Tag 030N]{StacksAlg}, and lift it to $c\in C$. If the residue degree is $d$, the elements $1,c,\ldots,c^{d-1}$ are an $R$-basis: Nakayama gives a surjection from $R^d$, and flatness makes its finite kernel vanish after reduction and hence vanish. The expression of $c^d$ in this basis gives}
{先显式描述态射。由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:ordinaryanalyticstalkhenselian}，有限 \'etale $R$-代数是有限个局部代数的乘积。局部因子 $C$ 在 $R$ 上有限自由，其剩余代数是 $k_0$ 的有限可分域扩张。利用 \cite[Tag 030N]{StacksAlg} 选择剩余域的本原元，并提升为 $c\in C$。若剩余次数为 $d$，则 $1,c,\ldots,c^{d-1}$ 是 $R$-基：Nakayama 给出 $R^d$ 到 $C$ 的满射，而平坦性说明其有限核模极大理想为零，从而该核为零。将 $c^d$ 用此基表示，得到}
\[
             C=R[T]/(P),\qquad P\ \text{monic},
             \qquad P'(c)\in C^\times.
\]
\bi{For a second local factor $D$, realized as an ordinary local ring $\mathcal O_{Z,z}$, an $F$-algebra map $C\otimes_R F\to D\otimes_R F=\mathcal H(z)$ is determined by a simple root of $P$ in $\mathcal H(z)$. The last equality follows from the finite \'etale fibre description in \href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02GL}{Stacks 02GL(1)} and the fact that this local germ has a single point over $x$; its finite reduced fibre algebra has one field factor. Lemma~\ref{fnd:analyticcompletedroots}, applied on $Z$, lifts this root uniquely to $D$. Thus it lifts the given homomorphism uniquely. Products cause no difficulty: an algebra map into a local factor selects exactly one source factor, as its orthogonal idempotents must map to zero or one. This proves full faithfulness.}
{对另一局部因子 $D$，将其实现为通常局部环 $\mathcal O_{Z,z}$。一个 $F$-代数同态 $C\otimes_R F\to D\otimes_R F=\mathcal H(z)$ 由 $P$ 在 $\mathcal H(z)$ 中的一个单根决定。最后的等式来自有限 \'etale 纤维结构以及此局部芽在 $x$ 上方只有一个点：其有限约化纤维代数只有一个域因子。把 Lemma~\ref{fnd:analyticcompletedroots} 应用于 $Z$，便把这个单根唯一提升到 $D$，从而唯一提升给定同态。有限乘积的情形同样成立：映入局部因子的代数映射恰选择一个源因子，因为其正交幂等元只能映为零或一。因此得到完全忠实性。}

\bi{For essential surjectivity it suffices to realize a finite separable field $E/F$. The preceding lemma supplies a monic $Q\in k_0[T]$ with $F[T]/(Q)=E$. Lift its finitely many coefficients to $R$, obtaining $\widehat Q\in R[T]$, and set $C=R[T]/(\widehat Q)$. The algebra $C$ is finite free. Its derivative is a unit: modulo the maximal ideal of $R$, the coprime pair $Q,Q'$ has a polynomial Bezout identity; lifting this identity shows that multiplication by $\widehat Q'$ is surjective modulo that maximal ideal, hence surjective on the finite module $C$ by Nakayama. Thus $C$ is finite \'etale. Realize it on an ordinary neighborhood by the first paragraph. Its fibre is exactly $E$, so there is one point above $x$ with completed residue field $E$. Finite products realize all finite \'etale algebras.}
{为证明本质满，只须实现有限可分域 $E/F$。上一引理给出首一 $Q\in k_0[T]$，使 $F[T]/(Q)=E$。将其有限个系数提升到 $R$，得到 $\widehat Q\in R[T]$，并令 $C=R[T]/(\widehat Q)$。此代数有限自由，且其导数为单位：模掉 $R$ 的极大理想后，互素的 $Q,Q'$ 满足多项式 Bezout 恒等式；提升此恒等式便知乘以 $\widehat Q'$ 模极大理想为满射，因而由 Nakayama，在有限模 $C$ 上也为满射。所以 $C$ 有限 \'etale，由第一段可在普通邻域上实现。其纤维恰为 $E$，故在 $x$ 上方恰有一个点，完备剩余域为 $E$。取有限乘积便实现全部有限 \'etale 代数。}
\end{proof}
