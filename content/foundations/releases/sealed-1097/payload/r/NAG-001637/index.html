Isolated points of a strict affinoid space

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 618

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Isolated points of a strict affinoid space}
{Strict affinoid 空间的孤立点}]
\label{fnd:strictisolatedpointdimension}
\bi{An isolated point of a strict affinoid space is rigid and
has local analytic dimension zero. In particular a pure
one-dimensional strict affinoid space has no isolated points.}
{Strict affinoid 空间的孤立点是 rigid 点，且其局部解析维数为零。
特别地，纯一维 strict affinoid 空间没有孤立点。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Let $X=\mathcal M(A)$ and suppose $\{x\}$ is open.
It is also closed because $X$ is Hausdorff. Glue the constant
function one on $\{x\}$ and zero on its complement using
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf}. The resulting
global function is an idempotent $e\in A$. The direct factor
$B=A/(1-e)$ is a strict affinoid algebra whose spectrum is
$\{x\}$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} supplies
a rigid point of its nonempty spectrum, necessarily $x$.
Consequently $\mathcal H(x)/K$ is finite and $d_K(x)=0$ by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitevaluedindexbound}.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidabhyankarmaximum} now gives
$\dim_K\mathcal M(B)=0$. Since this is an open neighborhood
of $x$, its local dimension in $X$ is zero.}
{设 $X=\mathcal M(A)$，并假设 $\{x\}$ 为开集。由于 $X$
是 Hausdorff，它也是闭集。利用
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf}，将 $\{x\}$ 上的
常函数一与其补集上的常函数零粘合，得到全局幂等元 $e\in A$。
直因子 $B=A/(1-e)$ 是 strict affinoid 代数，其谱为 $\{x\}$。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} 在这个非空谱中给出
rigid 点，只能是 $x$。因此 $\mathcal H(x)/K$ 有限，且由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitevaluedindexbound}，有 $d_K(x)=0$。
于是 Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidabhyankarmaximum} 给出
$\dim_K\mathcal M(B)=0$。这是 $x$ 的开邻域，故 $x$ 在 $X$
中的局部维数为零。}
\end{proof}
