Shrinking generator radii to spectral bounds

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 557

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Shrinking generator radii to spectral bounds}
{将生成元半径缩至谱界}]
\label{fnd:spectralgeneratorradii}
\bi{Let $A\{r^{-1}T\}\twoheadrightarrow B$ be an admissible
affinoid presentation with $T_i\mapsto b_i$.
If $0<s_i\leq r_i$ and $\rho_B(b_i)\leq s_i$, substitution
gives an admissible surjection $A\{s^{-1}T\}\twoheadrightarrow B$.}
{设 $A\{r^{-1}T\}\twoheadrightarrow B$ 为 admissible
affinoid 表示，$T_i\mapsto b_i$。若
$0<s_i\leq r_i$ 且 $\rho_B(b_i)\leq s_i$，则代入给出
admissible 满射 $A\{s^{-1}T\}\twoheadrightarrow B$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointwisepowerbound} gives
$\|b_i^n\|\leq C_is_i^n$ for all $n$.
Multiplying these finitely many estimates proves bounded
convergence of the substituted series. The original series
space maps contractively into the smaller-radius series space,
and its composite with substitution is the original
surjection. Thus surjectivity and the original bounded
lifting estimate both persist.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointwisepowerbound} 给出
对所有 $n$ 的估计 $\|b_i^n\|\leq C_is_i^n$。
将这有限多个估计相乘，就证明代入后级数有界收敛。
原级数空间压缩地映到较小半径的级数空间，且再作代入的复合
为原满射。因此满射性及原有的有界提升估计都保持。}
\end{proof}
