Principal formal opens and flat restriction

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 534

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Principal formal opens and flat restriction}
{主形式开集与平坦限制}]
\label{fnd:formalprincipalopenflat}
\bi{Let $A_0$ be admissible and $f\in A_0$. Write
$A_0\{f^{-1}\}$ for the $\pi$-adic completion of $A_0[1/f]$.
It is an admissible algebra,}
{设 $A_0$ 为 admissible，$f\in A_0$。以 $A_0\{f^{-1}\}$
记 $A_0[1/f]$ 的 $\pi$-进完备化。它是 admissible 代数，且}
\[
 A_0\{f^{-1}\}=A_0\langle U\rangle/(fU-1),\qquad
 A_0\{f^{-1}\}/\pi^n=(A_0/\pi^n)[1/f].
\]
\bi{The homomorphism $A_0\to A_0\{f^{-1}\}$ is flat.
On $\operatorname{Spf}(A_0)$, it is the restriction ring
of the principal open $D(f)$. Its generic fibre is the
strict rational domain $\{x:|f(x)|=1\}$.}
{同态 $A_0\to A_0\{f^{-1}\}$ 平坦。在
$\operatorname{Spf}(A_0)$ 上，它是主开集 $D(f)$ 的限制环。
其泛纤维为 strict rational 域 $\{x:|f(x)|=1\}$。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The polynomial quotient $A_0[U]/(fU-1)$ is $A_0[1/f]$
and is $\pi$-torsion free. Lemma~\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion}
gives the completion and reduction formulas. The ideal
generated by $fU-1$ in $A_0\langle U\rangle$ is closed by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalmoduletopology}, so its completion
has the displayed quotient presentation. It is torsion free
by the same completion lemma and hence admissible.}
{多项式商 $A_0[U]/(fU-1)$ 为 $A_0[1/f]$，且无
$\pi$-挠。Lemma~\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion} 给出完备化
及约化公式。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalmoduletopology}，
$A_0\langle U\rangle$ 中由 $fU-1$ 生成的理想闭，故完备化具有
上述商表示。由同一个完备化引理它无挠，因而是 admissible 的。}

\bi{Here is a direct flatness proof. Put $R=A_0[1/f]$ and
$B_0=\widehat R$. Let $I\subset A_0$ be a finitely generated
ideal, choose a surjection $\alpha:A_0^s\to I$, and let
$N=\ker\alpha$. The module $N$ is finite by
Corollary~\ref{fnd:formalringcoherence}.
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalmoduletopology} gives $e\geq0$ with}
{下面直接证明平坦性。令 $R=A_0[1/f]$，$B_0=\widehat R$。
对有限生成理想 $I\subset A_0$，选取满射
$\alpha:A_0^s\to I$，令 $N=\ker\alpha$。
由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:formalringcoherence}，$N$ 有限。
由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalmoduletopology}，存在 $e\geq0$ 使}
\[
       I\cap\pi^{n+e}A_0\subset\pi^n I\qquad(n\geq0).
\]
\bi{Algebraic localization preserves this containment.
For $v\in\ker(B_0^s\xrightarrow{\alpha}B_0)$ choose
$w_n\in R^s$ with $v-w_n\in\pi^{n+e}B_0^s$.
The reduction formula shows
$\alpha(w_n)\in I R\cap\pi^{n+e}R\subset\pi^n I R$.
Thus $\alpha(w_n)=\pi^n\alpha(u_n)$ for some $u_n\in R^s$,
and $w_n-\pi^n u_n\in NR$. These vectors approach $v$.
The image $NB_0\subset B_0^s$ is closed by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalmoduletopology}, so $v\in NB_0$.
Hence $I\otimes_{A_0}B_0\to B_0$ is injective.
The finite-ideal flatness criterion \cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg}
proves flatness.}
{代数局部化保留此包含关系。对
$v\in\ker(B_0^s\xrightarrow{\alpha}B_0)$，选取 $w_n\in R^s$
使 $v-w_n\in\pi^{n+e}B_0^s$。约化公式说明
$\alpha(w_n)\in IR\cap\pi^{n+e}R\subset\pi^n IR$。
故存在 $u_n\in R^s$ 使
$\alpha(w_n)=\pi^n\alpha(u_n)$，从而
$w_n-\pi^n u_n\in NR$。这些向量趋于 $v$。
由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalmoduletopology}，像
$NB_0\subset B_0^s$ 闭，因此 $v\in NB_0$。
所以 $I\otimes_{A_0}B_0\to B_0$ 为单射；有限理想平坦性判据
\cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg} 给出平坦性。}

\bi{The formal structure sheaf is the inverse limit of the
structure sheaves on $\operatorname{Spec}(A_0/\pi^n)$.
Taking sections on $D(f)$ gives precisely the displayed inverse
limit. After inverting $\pi$, the quotient presentation is
$A\langle U\rangle/(fU-1)$, the domain $|f|\geq1$.
Since $f\in A_0$ has value at most one everywhere, this is
the asserted equality domain.}
{形式结构层是 $\operatorname{Spec}(A_0/\pi^n)$ 的结构层的逆极限。
在 $D(f)$ 上取截面恰得到上述逆极限。逆转 $\pi$ 后，商表示为
$A\langle U\rangle/(fU-1)$，即域 $|f|\geq1$。
由于 $f\in A_0$ 处处取值不超过一，这就是所述的等值域。}
\end{proof}
