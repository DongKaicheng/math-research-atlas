\begin{proposition}[Cancellation for compatible artificial cuts]
\label{f7v38:cuts}
On a domain \(W\) constructed in Proposition~\ref{f7v38:integral},
retain the one tuple \(F\) and the one compact representative \(\eta\).
For any finite affinoid-domain cover \(W_1,\ldots,W_q\) of \(W\),
put \(W_I=\bigcap_{i\in I}W_i\) and restrict \(F\), \(\eta\), and
\(M_W\) to these domains. Then
\[
 \B_{\T_F(M_W)}(\eta)
  =\sum_{\varnothing\ne I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,q\}}
        (-1)^{|I|+1}\B_{\T_{F|W_I}(M_W|W_I)}(\eta)=0.
\]
On a common subdivision the corresponding signed normal densities
agree facetwise. The individual terms need not vanish.
\end{proposition}
