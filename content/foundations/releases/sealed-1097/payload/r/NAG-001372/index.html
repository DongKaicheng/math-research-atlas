Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Affinoid structure-sheaf input}{Affinoid structure-sheaf input}]\label{fnd:affinoidsheafinput}
\bi{On the site of compact analytic domains of $X=\mathcal M(A)$ with finite domain coverings, the affinoid assignment $V\mapsto A_V$ extends to a sheaf of Banach algebras with bounded restrictions. For a compact domain covered by affinoid domains, its sections are the compatible tuples of affinoid sections, independently of the chosen finite cover up to canonical bounded isomorphism and uniformly equivalent norms.}
{在 $X=\mathcal M(A)$ 的 compact analytic domains 组成、以 finite domain coverings 为 coverings 的 site 上，affinoid assignment $V\mapsto A_V$ 延拓为具有 bounded restrictions 的 Banach algebras sheaf。对被 affinoid domains 覆盖的 compact domain，其 sections 是相容的 affinoid sections tuples，在 canonical bounded isomorphism 与 uniformly equivalent norms 的意义下，与所选 finite cover 无关。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Restrict the ring sheaf of Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidfullgcomparison} to compact analytic domains. Theorem~\ref{fnd:compactdomainbanachsections} realizes its sections as the closed matching kernel for every finite affinoid cover, proves boundedness of restrictions, and identifies the kernels obtained from two covers by mutually bounded maps. Those maps are induced by the unique restriction maps on common affinoid refinements, so the comparison is canonical. Their two boundedness estimates give uniform equivalence of the Banach norms.}
{将 Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidfullgcomparison} 的环层限制到紧解析域。Theorem~\ref{fnd:compactdomainbanachsections} 对每个有限 affinoid 覆盖，将截面实现为闭的匹配核，证明限制有界，并以双向有界映射识别不同覆盖所得的核。这些映射由共同 affinoid 加细上的唯一限制映射诱导，因此比较典范；双向有界性给出 Banach 范数的一致等价。}
\end{proof}
