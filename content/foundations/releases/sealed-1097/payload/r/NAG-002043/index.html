Finite extensions in the valuative criterion

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 655

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Finite extensions in the valuative criterion}{赋值判据中的有限扩域}]
\label{cand:properstackfinitevaluation}
\bi{Let $R$ be a valuation ring with fraction field $K$, let $\mathcal X$ be an algebraic stack proper over $\operatorname{Spec}R$, and let $x:\operatorname{Spec}K\to\mathcal X$ be an $R$-morphism. There is a finite field extension $L/K$ such that, for every valuation ring $R'\subset L$ dominating $R$ and having fraction field $L$, the object $x_L$ extends to an object of $\mathcal X(R')$. Such valuation rings $R'$ exist.}
{设 $R$ 为分式域为 $K$ 的赋值环，$\mathcal X$ 为 $\operatorname{Spec}R$ 上的 proper 代数叠，并设 $x:\operatorname{Spec}K\to\mathcal X$ 为 $R$-态射。存在有限域扩张 $L/K$，使得对每个分式域为 $L$、支配 $R$ 的赋值环 $R'\subset L$，对象 $x_L$ 都延拓为 $\mathcal X(R')$ 的对象。这样的赋值环 $R'$ 存在。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The scheme $\operatorname{Spec}R$ is quasi-compact and quasi-separated, and $\mathcal X\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ is separated and of finite type. Chow's lemma for algebraic stacks, \cite[Tag 0CQ8]{StacksAlg}, gives an algebraic space $P$ and a proper surjective morphism $P\to\mathcal X$. Proper morphisms compose by \cite[Tag 0CL7]{StacksAlg}; hence $P\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ is proper. The compatibility of properness for algebraic spaces and stacks is recorded in \cite[Tag 0CL4]{StacksAlg}.}
{$\operatorname{Spec}R$ 拟紧且拟分离，而 $\mathcal X\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ 分离且为有限型。代数叠的 Chow 引理 \cite[Tag 0CQ8]{StacksAlg} 给出一个代数空间 $P$ 及 proper 满射 $P\to\mathcal X$。由 \cite[Tag 0CL7]{StacksAlg}，proper 态射可复合，所以 $P\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ proper。代数空间与代数叠的 properness 定义之间的相容性见 \cite[Tag 0CL4]{StacksAlg}。}

\bi{The fibre product $P_x=P\times_{\mathcal X}\operatorname{Spec}K$ is an algebraic space. Indeed, its map to $P\times_R\operatorname{Spec}K$ is the base change of the representable diagonal of $\mathcal X$; see \cite[Tag 04XS]{StacksAlg}. Properness and surjectivity survive this base change, so $P_x$ is a nonempty proper algebraic space of finite type over $K$. Choose a nonempty affine open $W=\operatorname{Spec}B$ in an \textup{\'etale} scheme presentation of $P_x$. The morphism $W\to\operatorname{Spec}K$ is locally of finite type and quasi-compact, so $B$ is a finite-type $K$-algebra. A maximal ideal of $B$ has residue field $L$ finite over $K$ by \cite[Tag 00FV]{StacksAlg}. The resulting point of $P_x(L)$ gives a point $p_L:\operatorname{Spec}L\to P$ and a specified isomorphism between its image in $\mathcal X(L)$ and $x_L$.}
{纤维积 $P_x=P\times_{\mathcal X}\operatorname{Spec}K$ 为代数空间。事实上，它到 $P\times_R\operatorname{Spec}K$ 的态射是 $\mathcal X$ 的可表对角态射的基变换；见 \cite[Tag 04XS]{StacksAlg}。Properness 与满射性在此基变换下保持，因此 $P_x$ 是 $K$ 上非空、proper、有限型的代数空间。在 $P_x$ 的一个 \textup{\'etale} 概形表示中选取非空仿射开集 $W=\operatorname{Spec}B$。态射 $W\to\operatorname{Spec}K$ 局部有限型且拟紧，故 $B$ 为有限型 $K$-代数。由 \cite[Tag 00FV]{StacksAlg}，$B$ 的任一极大理想的剩余域 $L$ 在 $K$ 上有限。得到的 $P_x(L)$ 中的点给出 $p_L:\operatorname{Spec}L\to P$，以及其在 $\mathcal X(L)$ 中的像与 $x_L$ 之间的指定同构。}

\bi{Fix any valuation ring $R'\subset L$ as in the statement. Apply the ordinary valuative criterion for the proper algebraic space $P/R$, \cite[Tag 0A40]{StacksAlg}, to}
{固定陈述中的任一赋值环 $R'\subset L$。对 proper 代数空间 $P/R$ 应用通常的赋值判据 \cite[Tag 0A40]{StacksAlg}，得到}
\[
 \operatorname{Spec}L\longrightarrow P,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Spec}R'\longrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}R.
\]
\bi{It supplies a unique extension $\operatorname{Spec}R'\to P$ of $p_L$. Its composite with $P\to\mathcal X$, together with the preceding generic isomorphism, is the required extension of $x_L$. Finally, \cite[Tag 00IA]{StacksAlg} applied to the local subring $R\subset L$ supplies such an $R'$. Domination ensures $R'\cap K=R$: if $a\in K\setminus R$, then $a^{-1}\in\mathfrak m_R\subset\mathfrak m_{R'}$, which excludes $a\in R'$.}
{判据给出 $p_L$ 的唯一延拓 $\operatorname{Spec}R'\to P$。将它与 $P\to\mathcal X$ 复合，并配以上述泛纤维同构，即得 $x_L$ 的所需延拓。最后，对局部子环 $R\subset L$ 应用 \cite[Tag 00IA]{StacksAlg}，可得这样的 $R'$。支配关系保证 $R'\cap K=R$：若 $a\in K\setminus R$，则 $a^{-1}\in\mathfrak m_R\subset\mathfrak m_{R'}$，所以 $a\notin R'$。}
\end{proof}
