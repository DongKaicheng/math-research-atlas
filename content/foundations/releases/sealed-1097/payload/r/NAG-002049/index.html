Simple roots over a complete valued field

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 659

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Simple roots over a complete valued field}{完备赋值域上的单根}]
\label{cand:completevaluationhenselian}
\bi{The valuation ring $O$ is henselian. If $V\to\operatorname{Spec}O$ is \textup{\'etale} and $v\in V$ lies over the closed point with $\kappa(v)=k$, then an affine open neighbourhood of $v$ admits a section over $\operatorname{Spec}O$ through $v$. Its image is open in $V$.}
{赋值环 $O$ 是 henselian 的。若 $V\to\operatorname{Spec}O$ 为 \textup{\'etale}，且闭纤维中的点 $v\in V$ 满足 $\kappa(v)=k$，则 $v$ 的某个仿射开邻域具有一个经过 $v$ 的 $\operatorname{Spec}O$ 上截面。该截面的像在 $V$ 中为开集。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Let $F\in O[T]$ have a simple root $\overline b\in k$ after reduction, and choose a lift $b\in O$. Then $|F(b)|<1$ and $|F'(b)|=1$. Expanding at $b$ gives}
{设 $F\in O[T]$ 的约化具有单根 $\overline b\in k$，并选择提升 $b\in O$。此时 $|F(b)|<1$ 且 $|F'(b)|=1$。在 $b$ 处展开得到}
\[
 \frac{F(b+Z)}{F'(b)}=c_0+Z+\sum_{i=2}^d c_iZ^i,
 \qquad |c_0|<1,\quad |c_i|\leq1.
\]
\bi{Choose a real number $\delta$ with $|c_0|<\delta<1$. On the complete closed ball $|Z|\leq\delta$, the map $\Phi(Z)=-c_0-\sum_{i\geq2}c_iZ^i$ takes values in the same ball and satisfies}
{选择实数 $\delta$ 满足 $|c_0|<\delta<1$。在完备闭球 $|Z|\leq\delta$ 上，映射 $\Phi(Z)=-c_0-\sum_{i\geq2}c_iZ^i$ 仍取值于该球，且满足}
\[
 |\Phi(Z)-\Phi(Z')|\leq\delta\,|Z-Z'|.
\]
\bi{Starting with $Z_0=0$, its iterates satisfy $|Z_{n+1}-Z_n|\leq\delta^n|Z_1|$; the ultrametric inequality makes them Cauchy. Their limit $Z$ is a fixed point. Thus $b+Z\in O$ lifts $\overline b$ and satisfies $F(b+Z)=0$. This is the one-variable contraction used in the proof of Lemma~\ref{fnd:analyticcompletedroots}, now applied to a residue root. The simple-root characterization in \cite[Tag 04GG]{StacksAlg} proves that $O$ is henselian.}
{从 $Z_0=0$ 开始迭代，有 $|Z_{n+1}-Z_n|\leq\delta^n|Z_1|$；超度量不等式说明该序列为 Cauchy 序列。其极限 $Z$ 是不动点。因此 $b+Z\in O$ 提升 $\overline b$ 且满足 $F(b+Z)=0$。这正是 Lemma~\ref{fnd:analyticcompletedroots} 的证明所用的一元压缩论证，此处应用于剩余域中的根。由 \cite[Tag 04GG]{StacksAlg} 的单根刻画，$O$ 为 henselian 环。}

\bi{Choose an affine open $V_0=\operatorname{Spec}B$ containing $v$. The map $O\to B$ is \textup{\'etale}. Part~(8) of \cite[Tag 04GG]{StacksAlg} supplies the $O$-algebra retraction $B\to O$ whose inverse image of $\mathfrak m$ is the prime of $v$. The corresponding section passes through $v$. A section of an unramified morphism is an open immersion by \cite[Tag 024T]{StacksAlg}; hence its image is open in $V_0$ and in $V$.}
{取包含 $v$ 的仿射开集 $V_0=\operatorname{Spec}B$。映射 $O\to B$ 为 \textup{\'etale}。由 \cite[Tag 04GG]{StacksAlg} 的第~(8)~项，存在 $O$-代数回缩 $B\to O$，使 $\mathfrak m$ 的逆像恰为 $v$ 对应的素理想。对应截面经过 $v$。由 \cite[Tag 024T]{StacksAlg}，unramified 态射的截面是开浸入，因此其像在 $V_0$ 及 $V$ 中都是开集。}
\end{proof}
