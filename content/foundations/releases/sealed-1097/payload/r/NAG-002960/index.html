Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[An arbitrary-dimensional class with checked purity; F8SA-M]
\label{prop:monomial}
On the strict \(n\)-polydisc \(V=\cM(K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle)\),
let \(f=u\prod_{j=1}^rT_j^{m_j}\), where \(u\) is an analytic unit,
\(1\leq r\leq n\), and \(m_j\) are positive integers. Then S1--S3
hold on \(V\). The irreducible components of its actual zero space
are the coordinate hyperplanes, and its module length at the generic
prime \((T_j)\) is \(m_j\).
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The polydisc algebra is a domain, and its ordinary local rings and
all actual affinoid-domain algebras are regular by
\tagg{NAG-001760}; in particular they satisfy \(S_1\). Multiplication
by \(f\) is injective, also after domain restriction by the flatness of
\tagg{NAG-001832}. A prime containing
the displayed product contains one \(T_j\). Each \((T_j)\) is prime,
its quotient is a strict \((n-1)\)-polydisc algebra, and these distinct
coordinate primes are incomparable. They are therefore exactly the
minimal primes over \((f)\). Their analytic dimensions are \(n-1\)
by \tagg{NAG-001657}/\tagg{001664}. This verifies S3 without an
unqualified prime-union rule in a mixed-component ring.

At \((T_j)\), the other coordinates and \(u\) are units. The maximal
ideal is generated by the non-zero-divisor \(T_j\); successive powers
in the quotient by \(T_j^{m_j}\) have exactly \(m_j\) residue-field
factors. The length is thus \(m_j\).
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{S\arabic*.},leftmargin=*]
\item Every ordinary local ring of \(U\) satisfies \(S_1\).
\item Multiplication by \(f\) is injective on \(\cO_{U_G}\).
Thus ``regular'' means an actual non-zero-divisor, not merely a
nonzero function on one component.
\item The actual zero space \(D=V(f)\subset U\), with its quotient
structure, is empty or pure of analytic dimension \(n-1\).
\end{enumerate}
