\begin{theorem}[\bi{Rational-domain input}{Rational-domain input}]\label{fnd:rationaluniversalinput}
\bi{Let $A$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra and let $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$ generate the unit ideal. For positive radii $r_i$, the norm-admissible quotient}
{设 $A$ 为 generalized $K$-affinoid algebra，且 $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$ 生成 unit ideal。对正 radii $r_i$，norm-admissible quotient}
\[
 A\{r^{-1}T\}/(gT_i-f_i)_i
\]
\bi{represents the domain $|f_i(x)|\leq r_i|g(x)|$. Every bounded map $A\to B$ to a generalized affinoid algebra whose spectrum maps into this domain factors uniquely through that quotient as a bounded map.}
{表示 domain $|f_i(x)|\leq r_i|g(x)|$。对每个到 generalized affinoid algebra 的 bounded map $A\to B$，若其 spectrum 的 image 位于此 domain，则该 map 唯一地作为 bounded map 经此 quotient 分解。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints} constructs the displayed actual quotient as a norm-admissible affinoid algebra and identifies its spectrum homeomorphically with the stated subset. Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal} to the given generalized affinoid target $B$. Its geometric criterion is exactly the image condition in the statement, and yields the unique bounded factorization. Both results allow all positive radii and a trivially valued ground field; zero targets have the unique zero factorization. No strictness or rational-cover acyclicity hypothesis is added.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints} 将所列实际商构造成 norm-admissible affinoid 代数，并将其谱同胚识别为所述子集。对给定的 generalized affinoid 目标 $B$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal}；其中的几何判据恰好是本陈述中的像条件，因而给出唯一有界分解。这两个结果都允许任意正半径及平凡赋值基域；零目标具有唯一的零分解。这里没有增加 strictness 或 rational-cover acyclicity 假设。}
\end{proof}

\subsection{\bi{The exact complex and its scope}{精确的 complex 及其范围}}\label{fnd:cechscope}
\bi{Let $K$ be complete for its fixed real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value, possibly trivial. Let $A$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra in the norm-admissible convention, $X=\mathcal M(A)$, and $V_1,\ldots,V_m$ affinoid domains whose union is $X$. The theorem allows nonreduced $A$, arbitrary positive radii and boundary points. It assumes neither smoothness nor algebraic closedness. Before forming the complex one needs representing algebras $A_I$ for all finite intersections $V_I$ and compatible bounded restriction maps. Empty intersections contribute the zero module.}
{设 $K$ 关于其固定的 real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value 完备，允许 trivial valuation。令 $A$ 为采用 norm-admissible 约定的 generalized $K$-affinoid algebra，$X=\mathcal M(A)$，且 affinoid domains $V_1,\ldots,V_m$ 的并为 $X$。定理允许 nonreduced $A$、任意正 radii 和 boundary points，不假设 smoothness 或 algebraic closedness。形成 complex 之前，需要为每个 finite intersection $V_I$ 构造 representing algebra $A_I$ 及相容的 bounded restriction maps；empty intersections 对应 zero module。}
