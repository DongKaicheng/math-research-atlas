Scalar extension of algebraic formal models

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 703

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Scalar extension of algebraic formal models}
{代数形式模型的标量扩张}]
\label{cand:algebraicmodelscalar}
\bi{Let $L/K$ be a complete isometric valued extension and let
$\mathcal U$ be a flat finitely presented $O$-scheme. Put
$O'=L^\circ$, $l=\widetilde L$, and
$\mathcal U'=\mathcal U\times_O O'$. Completion uses the same
$\pi\in O$, $0<|\pi|<1$, on both sides. There are canonical
identifications}
{设 $L/K$ 为完备等距赋值扩张，$\mathcal U$ 为平坦有限表示
$O$-概形。令 $O'=L^\circ$、$l=\widetilde L$，以及
$\mathcal U'=\mathcal U\times_O O'$。两侧完备化都使用
同一个满足 $0<|\pi|<1$ 的 $\pi\in O$。存在典范识别}
\[
 (\widehat{\mathcal U'}_\eta)\simeq
       (\widehat{\mathcal U}_\eta)_L,
 \qquad \mathcal U'_s=\mathcal U_s\times_k l,
\]
\bi{compatible with specialization, formal-open restriction,
and successive scalar extensions. In particular a geometrically
reduced special fibre remains reduced after this operation.}
{它们与特化、形式开集限制及相继标量扩张相容。特别地，
几何约化特殊纤维在此运算后仍约化。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Work first with
$B=O[T_1,\ldots,T_n]/(f_1,\ldots,f_s)$.
Base change preserves flatness and finite presentation.
Lemma~\ref{cand:affineflatformalcompletion}, applied over
$O$ and over $O'$, gives generic algebras}
{先考虑
$B=O[T_1,\ldots,T_n]/(f_1,\ldots,f_s)$。
基变换保持平坦性与有限表示性。在 $O$ 与 $O'$ 上分别
应用 Lemma~\ref{cand:affineflatformalcompletion}，得到泛纤维
代数}
\[
 A=K\langle T\rangle/(f_1,\ldots,f_s),\qquad
 A'=L\langle T\rangle/(f_1,\ldots,f_s).
\]
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalscalarpresentation} identifies
$A'$ with $A\widehat\otimes_K L$, with the actual extended
relation ideal. Reduction of a restricted series modulo
the maximal ideal leaves a polynomial: its coefficients tend
to zero, so only finitely many have nonzero reduction.
Conversely a restricted series with zero reduction has a
largest coefficient absolute value less than one and belongs
to the maximal ideal times the restricted series ring.
Thus the special-fibre quotients are $k[T]/(\bar f_i)$ and
$l[T]/(\bar f_i)$, which gives the second identification.}
{Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalscalarpresentation} 通过实际
延伸关系理想将 $A'$ 识别为 $A\widehat\otimes_K L$。
受限级数模极大理想约化后是多项式：其系数趋于零，故
只有有限个系数具有非零约化。反过来，约化为零的受限
级数，其最大系数绝对值小于一，因而属于极大理想与
受限级数环的乘积。因此特殊纤维商分别为
$k[T]/(\bar f_i)$ 及 $l[T]/(\bar f_i)$，得到第二个识别。}

\bi{Principal affine restrictions have the common relation
$bU-1$ by Lemma~\ref{cand:affineflatformalcompletion}; their
generic domains are $|b|=1$ on both sides. The local
identifications therefore agree on restrictions and glue,
using Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension}.
Specialization commutes because its prime is defined by
the integral functions with value less than one, and those
values are unchanged by the isometric coefficient map.
All comparisons agree on polynomial coordinates, so their
composites for successive extensions agree as well. The
last assertion is the definition of geometric reducedness.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{cand:affineflatformalcompletion}，主仿射
限制在两侧都具有关系 $bU-1$，其泛纤维域都为 $|b|=1$。
因此局部识别与限制相容，并借助
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension} 粘合。
特化由取值小于一的整函数定义其素理想，而等距系数
映射不改变这些取值，故特化相容。全部比较映射在
多项式坐标上相同，因此相继扩域时其复合也相同。
最后一个断言来自几何约化的定义。}
\end{proof}
