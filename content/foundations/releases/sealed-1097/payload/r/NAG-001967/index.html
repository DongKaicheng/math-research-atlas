Affinoid neighbourhood invariance

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 564

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Affinoid neighbourhood invariance}
{Affinoid 邻域不变性}]
\label{fnd:gradedaffinoidgerminvariance}
\bi{If $Y=\mathcal M(B)\subset X$ is an affinoid domain
containing $x$, its reduction is an open subspace of
$\mathscr R(X,x)$. If $Y$ is a neighbourhood of $x$ in $X$,
this open subspace equals $\mathscr R(X,x)$. Consequently
$\mathscr R(X,x)$ depends only on the good analytic germ
$(X,x)$, with its actual completed residue field.}
{若 $Y=\mathcal M(B)\subset X$ 是包含 $x$ 的 affinoid 域，
其约化为 $\mathscr R(X,x)$ 的开子空间。若 $Y$ 是 $x$
在 $X$ 中的邻域，则这个开子空间等于 $\mathscr R(X,x)$。
因此，$\mathscr R(X,x)$ 只依赖于 good 解析芽 $(X,x)$，
并保留其实际完备剩余域。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Corollary~\ref{fnd:domainresiduefields} identifies the
completed residue fields. Under this identification
$R_{A,x}\subset R_{B,x}\subset F_x$.
Choose an admissible absolute weighted Tate presentation of $B$.
Apply \hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{the fixed absolute presentation result}
and evaluate in $F_x$, as for $A$. Finiteness over the evaluated
polynomial image makes $R_{B,x}$ a finitely generated graded
$\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$-algebra. Containment of homogeneous generators
is an open condition in $\mathscr R(X,x)$, and
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedrzcompact} makes this open quasi-compact.}
{Corollary~\ref{fnd:domainresiduefields} 识别完备剩余域。
在这个识别下，$R_{A,x}\subset R_{B,x}\subset F_x$。
选取 $B$ 的一个 admissible 绝对加权 Tate 表示。
应用 \hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{固定绝对表示的结果}，
再如对 $A$ 那样在 $F_x$ 中求值。$R_{B,x}$ 在求值后的多项式像上
有限，因而是有限生成的分次 $\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$-代数。在
$\mathscr R(X,x)$ 中包含其齐次生成元是开条件，该开集由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedrzcompact} 知为拟紧。}

\bi{If $Y$ is a neighbourhood, the topology defined by
function values gives a Laurent affinoid neighbourhood
$W\subset Y$ of $x$ cut out by finitely many inequalities
$|f_i|\leq r_i$ and $|g_j|\geq s_j$ that are all strict at
$x$. Put $D=\mathcal O(W)$. Its presentation over $A$ adjoins
$f_i$ at radius $r_i$ and $g_j^{-1}$ at radius $s_j^{-1}$.
Choose an admissible absolute weighted Tate presentation of $A$.
Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation} combines it with
the displayed relative presentation into an admissible absolute
weighted Tate presentation of $D$.
Apply \hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{the fixed absolute presentation result}
and evaluate in the common field $F_x$. The resulting finite
module generators for $R_{D,x}$ are taken over the evaluated
polynomial image. Each old variable has the same evaluated class
in its assigned degree as the class from $A$. Each new variable
has evaluated norm strictly less than its assigned radius, so its
class evaluates to zero. Thus the coefficient image lies in
$R_{A,x}$, and the same generators make $R_{D,x}$ finite over
$R_{A,x}$. The intermediate $R_{B,x}$ is integral over $R_{A,x}$
and finitely generated as an algebra, hence finite.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedvaluationintegrality} gives equality of
the valuation spaces. Any two affinoid neighbourhoods of the
good germ contain such a common Laurent affinoid neighbourhood,
with its domain structures identified by
Corollary~\ref{fnd:intrinsicaffinoidatlas}. The identifications
are canonical and satisfy the cocycle condition.}
{若 $Y$ 为邻域，由函数值定义的拓扑给出包含 $x$ 的 Laurent
affinoid 邻域 $W\subset Y$，它由有限个 $|f_i|\leq r_i$、
$|g_j|\geq s_j$ 给出，且诸不等式在 $x$ 处均为严格不等式。
置 $D=\mathcal O(W)$。它在 $A$ 上的表示加入半径为 $r_i$ 的
$f_i$ 和半径为 $s_j^{-1}$ 的 $g_j^{-1}$。选取 $A$ 的一个
admissible 绝对加权 Tate 表示，依
Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation} 将其与上述相对表示
合并为 $D$ 的 admissible 绝对加权 Tate 表示。
应用 \hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{固定绝对表示的结果}，
再在共同的域 $F_x$ 中求值，得到 $R_{D,x}$ 在求值后的多项式像上
的有限模生成元。每个旧变量在其指定次数的求值类与从 $A$ 得到的
类相同；每个新变量的求值范数严格小于其指定半径，故其类求值为零。
因此系数像包含在 $R_{A,x}$ 中，同一组生成元使 $R_{D,x}$ 在
$R_{A,x}$ 上有限。中间代数 $R_{B,x}$ 在 $R_{A,x}$ 上整，
且有限生成为代数，故有限。由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedvaluationintegrality}，赋值空间相等。
Good 芽的任意两个 affinoid 邻域均包含这样的共同 Laurent
affinoid 邻域，其域结构由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:intrinsicaffinoidatlas}
识别。所得识别典范，并满足 cocycle 条件。}
\end{proof}
