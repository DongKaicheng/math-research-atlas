A spectral unit ball need not be an admissible model

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 622

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A spectral unit ball need not be an admissible model}
{谱单位球未必是 admissible 模型}]
\label{fnd:freegaussunitballnonadmissible}
\bi{Assume $K$ is nontrivially valued and
$r\notin\sqrt{|K^\times|}$. For the one-radius field $K_r$,
its valuation ring $K_r^\circ$ is not topologically of finite
type over $K^\circ$.}
{设 $K$ 非平凡赋值且 $r\notin\sqrt{|K^\times|}$。
对单半径域 $K_r$，其赋值环 $K_r^\circ$ 在 $K^\circ$
上不是拓扑有限型。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose $\pi\in K$ with $0<|\pi|<1$. Every element of
$K_r$ becomes power-bounded after multiplication by a power
of $\pi$, so $K_r^\circ[1/\pi]=K_r$.
If the torsion-free ring $K_r^\circ$ were topologically of
finite type, Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation}
and Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations} would
make $K_r$ a strict $K$-affinoid algebra. But its Laurent
coordinate has spectral norm $r$, contrary to
Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictspectralvaluegroup}, which puts every
nonzero spectral value of a strict affinoid algebra in
$\sqrt{|K^\times|}$.}
{取 $\pi\in K$ 满足 $0<|\pi|<1$。每个 $K_r$ 中元素
乘以 $\pi$ 的某个幂后都成为幂有界元素，所以
$K_r^\circ[1/\pi]=K_r$。若无挠环 $K_r^\circ$ 在
$K^\circ$ 上拓扑有限型，则
Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation} 及
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations} 会使
$K_r$ 成为 strict $K$-affinoid 代数。但其 Laurent 坐标
的谱范数为 $r$，这与 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictspectralvaluegroup}
所断言的 strict affinoid 代数的非零谱值均属于
$\sqrt{|K^\times|}$ 矛盾。}
\end{proof}
