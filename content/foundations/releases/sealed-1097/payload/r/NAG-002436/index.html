Numerical vertex recognition in residue characteristic zero

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 815

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Numerical vertex recognition in residue characteristic zero]
\label{v038:f5:residuezerorecognition}
Let $K$ be a complete nontrivially real-valued field with residue
characteristic zero, and let $C/K$ be a smooth projective
geometrically connected curve. If $v\in C^{\an}$ satisfies
\[
 \begin{aligned}
   |\mathcal H(v)^\times|&=|K^\times|,\\
   \widetilde{\mathcal H(v)}/\widetilde K
     &\text{ is separably generated of transcendence degree one},
 \end{aligned}
\]
then $v$ is the unique component point of an actual integral
\textup{\'etale} Gauss chart in $C^{\an}$ and has an unramified
branch certificate.

If $C$ admits one semistable algebraic model, in the sense of
Definition~\ref{v036:f5:modelclass}, over the original
$K^\circ$, the two displayed conditions characterize the points
which occur as component vertices of such models. In this
existence class, a finite nonempty set satisfying those conditions
is realized exactly with a specified unramified marking datum
precisely when its entire geometric inverse image satisfies the
marked semistable-vertex condition of
Definition~\ref{v037:f5:admissiblevertices}.

For arbitrary residue characteristic, the chart and branch-certificate
conclusions, and realization under original-model existence, hold
at a point satisfying the displayed conditions if one separating
rational Gauss germ supplied by Lemma~\ref{v038:f5:separatinggaussgerm}
has completed degree $n=[\mathcal H(v):H_t]$ with $|n|=1$.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Choose the rational Gauss germ by
Lemma~\ref{v038:f5:separatinggaussgerm}. Put $L=\mathcal H(v)$
and $l=\widetilde L$. The Gauss calculation in
Proposition~\ref{v037:f5:integralgaussdegree} gives
\[
          |H_t^\times|=|K^\times|,\qquad
                         \widetilde{H_t}=k(\bar t).
\]
Thus $L/H_t$ has unchanged value group and finite separable
residue extension. In residue characteristic zero every positive
integer has absolute value one. Proposition~\ref{v038:f5:unitdegreeunramified}
therefore gives
\[
                           [L:H_t]=[l:k(\bar t)].
\]
In arbitrary residue characteristic the stated hypothesis $|n|=1$
gives exactly the same conclusion by that proposition.
Proposition~\ref{v037:f5:gaussgermintegralchart} supplies the actual
integral chart, and Theorem~\ref{v037:f5:gausschartcertificate}
supplies its branch certificate.

Under original-model existence,
Theorem~\ref{v037:f5:vertexcertificate} realizes the point.
The converse follows from Proposition~\ref{v036:f5:vertexarithmetic}.
For a finite nonempty set, the pointwise certificates and the
entire geometric marked-complement condition are precisely the
hypotheses and conclusions of
Theorem~\ref{v037:f5:intrinsiccorrespondence}.
All coordinates, completions and traces use the original absolute
value; the argument does not require a discrete value group.
\end{proof}
