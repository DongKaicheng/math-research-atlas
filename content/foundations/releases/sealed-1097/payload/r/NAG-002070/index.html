The valued field of a component point

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 684

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{The valued field of a component point}
{分量点的赋值域}]
\label{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield}
\bi{Let $A_0$ be an admissible $O$-algebra with reduced special
fibre, let $A=A_0[1/\pi]$, and let $x=x_\eta$ be the component
point corresponding to a minimal prime $P\subset\widetilde A$.
The reduction map induces an isomorphism}
{设 $A_0$ 为特殊纤维约化的 admissible $O$-代数，令
$A=A_0[1/\pi]$，并设 $x=x_\eta$ 为极小素理想
$P\subset\widetilde A$ 对应的分量点。约化映射诱导同构}
\[
 \operatorname{Frac}(\widetilde A/P)
       \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)},
 \qquad |\mathcal H(x)^\times|=|K^\times|.
\]
\bi{If $\eta$ is the generic point of a residual curve component
$C$, the left hand field is $k(C)$. In particular $x$ has residual
transcendence degree one and relative rational value-group rank zero.}
{若 $\eta$ 是剩余曲线分量 $C$ 的泛点，则左边的域为
$k(C)$。特别地，$x$ 的剩余超越次数为一，相对值群有理秩为零。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{By Proposition~\ref{cand:reducedmodelcomponentpoints}, the
Shilov boundary consists of finitely many component points
$x=x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_r$, with distinct minimal reduction primes
$P=P_1,P_2,\ldots,P_r$. For each $j>1$, choose
$\overline h_j\in P_j\setminus P$ and lift their product to
$h\in A_0$. If $r=1$, take $h=1$. Then}
{由 Proposition~\ref{cand:reducedmodelcomponentpoints}，Shilov
边界由有限个分量点 $x=x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_r$ 组成，其极小
约化素理想 $P=P_1,P_2,\ldots,P_r$ 两两不同。对每个
$j>1$，选择 $\overline h_j\in P_j\setminus P$，并将它们的
乘积提升为 $h\in A_0$。若 $r=1$，取 $h=1$。于是}
\[
 |h(x)|=1,\qquad |h(x_j)|<1\quad(j>1).
\]
\bi{For $a\in A$ with $a(x)\ne0$, choose $N$ so large that
$|a(x_j)h(x_j)^N|<|a(x)|$ for every $j>1$. The boundary
maximum formula gives $\rho_A(ah^N)=|a(x)|$.
Lemma~\ref{cand:reducedspecialcanonical}, including the
norm-attaining lift used there, implies that this nonzero spectral
value belongs to $|K^\times|$. Choose $c\in K^\times$ of this
absolute value. Then $ah^N/c\in A_0$ and its reduction is outside
$P$.}
{对满足 $a(x)\ne0$ 的 $a\in A$，选择充分大的 $N$，使
每个 $j>1$ 都满足 $|a(x_j)h(x_j)^N|<|a(x)|$。边界最大值
公式给出 $\rho_A(ah^N)=|a(x)|$。由
Lemma~\ref{cand:reducedspecialcanonical} 及其中达到范数的
提升，此非零谱取值属于 $|K^\times|$。取具有此绝对值的
$c\in K^\times$，便有 $ah^N/c\in A_0$，且其约化不属于
$P$。}

\bi{The homomorphism $\widetilde A/P\to
\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}$ is injective by the definition of $P$,
so it extends to its fraction field. To prove surjectivity, first
consider $a/b$ with $a,b\in A$, $a(x)b(x)\ne0$, and
$|a(x)/b(x)|=1$. Choose a single $N$ large enough for both $a$
and $b$ in the preceding argument, and choose
$c_a,c_b\in K^\times$ with $|c_a|=|a(x)|$ and
$|c_b|=|b(x)|$. Both}
{根据 $P$ 的定义，同态 $\widetilde A/P\to
\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}$ 单射，因而延拓到分式域。
为证明满射，先考虑 $a/b$，其中 $a,b\in A$、
$a(x)b(x)\ne0$ 且 $|a(x)/b(x)|=1$。在上一段同时适用于
$a,b$ 的范围内选择同一个充分大的 $N$，再取
$c_a,c_b\in K^\times$，使 $|c_a|=|a(x)|$、
$|c_b|=|b(x)|$。于是}
\[
 a'=ah^N/c_a,\qquad b'=bh^N/c_b
\]
\bi{belong to $A_0$ and have nonzero residues on $C$. Since
$|c_a/c_b|=1$, the residue of $a/b=(c_a/c_b)a'/b'$ lies in
$\operatorname{Frac}(\widetilde A/P)$. Every unit of
$\mathcal H(x)$ is within distance strictly less than one of such
a fraction, because $\mathcal H(x)$ is the completion of
$\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$. It therefore has the same residue
as that fraction. This proves surjectivity.}
{均属于 $A_0$，且在 $C$ 上具有非零剩余类。因
$|c_a/c_b|=1$，$a/b=(c_a/c_b)a'/b'$ 的剩余类属于
$\operatorname{Frac}(\widetilde A/P)$。由于
$\mathcal H(x)$ 是 $\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$ 的
完备化，其中每个范数为一的元素都可由这样的分式以严格
小于一的误差逼近，故与该分式具有相同剩余类。这证明满射。}

\bi{The first paragraph gives the value-group assertion for
fractions. A nonzero limit in a non-Archimedean valued field has
the same absolute value as every sufficiently accurate
approximant, so completion does not enlarge that value group.
The final assertion follows when $\widetilde A/P$ has dimension
one.}
{第一段给出分式的值群断言。非阿基米德赋值域中的非零
极限，与每个充分精确的逼近项具有相同绝对值，因此完备化
不扩大此值群。当 $\widetilde A/P$ 的维数为一时，最后的
断言随即成立。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Let $K$ be complete and nontrivially valued. We retain its given
absolute value and write $O=K^\circ$, $\mathfrak m=K^{\circ\circ}$,
and $k=O/\mathfrak m$.}
{设 $K$ 完备且非平凡赋值。始终保持给定绝对值，并记
$O=K^\circ$、$\mathfrak m=K^{\circ\circ}$、$k=O/\mathfrak m$。}
