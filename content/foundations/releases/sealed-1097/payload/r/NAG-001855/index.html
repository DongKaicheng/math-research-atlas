Ordinary closed fibres and analytic fibres

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 463

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Ordinary closed fibres and analytic fibres}{普通闭纤维与解析纤维}]
\label{fnd:charzeroclosedfibercomparison}
\bi{Let $K$ have characteristic zero, let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good analytic spaces, and let $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$ with $x=f(y)$ and nontrivially valued $\mathcal H(x)$. Put $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$, $S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$ and let $\mathfrak m$ be the maximal ideal of $R$. Then}
{设 $K$ 为特征零域，$f:Y\to X$ 为 good 解析空间之间的态射，$y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$，并令 $x=f(y)$，假设 $\mathcal H(x)$ 的赋值非平凡。记 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$，以 $\mathfrak m$ 记 $R$ 的极大理想。则}
\[
                 S/\mathfrak mS\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{Y_x,y}
\]
\bi{is a regular faithfully flat local homomorphism.}
{为正则的忠实平坦局部同态。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The Noetherian ideal $\mathfrak m$ has finitely many generators. Represent them on an ordinary affinoid neighborhood of $x$ and let $X_0$ be the closed analytic subspace they define. It contains $x$, and coherent closed-subspace restriction gives $\mathcal O_{X_0,x}=R/\mathfrak m$. Set $Y_0=Y\times_X X_0$. The corresponding point has ordinary local ring $S/\mathfrak mS$, while its analytic fibre is unchanged because all the chosen generators vanish in $\mathcal H(x)$. Interior is preserved by base change. Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroinnergenericfibreregularity} applies to $Y_0\to X_0$, giving the claimed regular map. It is local and flat between nonzero local rings, hence faithfully flat.}
{Noetherian 理想 $\mathfrak m$ 有有限个生成元。将它们实现在 $x$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域上，以 $X_0$ 记其定义的闭解析子空间。该子空间包含 $x$，且凝聚闭子空间的限制给出 $\mathcal O_{X_0,x}=R/\mathfrak m$。令 $Y_0=Y\times_X X_0$。相应点的普通局部环为 $S/\mathfrak mS$，而因所选生成元在 $\mathcal H(x)$ 中均为零，其解析纤维不变。内部条件在基变换下保持。对 $Y_0\to X_0$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroinnergenericfibreregularity}，得到所述正则映射。它是非零局部环之间的平坦局部映射，故忠实平坦。}
\end{proof}
