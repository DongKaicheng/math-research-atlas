Surjectivity of formal specialization

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 532

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Surjectivity of formal specialization}{形式特化的满射性}]
\label{fnd:affineformalspecializationsurjective}
\bi{Let $A_0$ be an admissible $K^\circ$-algebra and
$A=A_0[1/\pi]$. The map}
{设 $A_0$ 为 admissible $K^\circ$-代数，$A=A_0[1/\pi]$。
映射}
\[
 \operatorname{sp}:\mathcal M(A)\longrightarrow
       \operatorname{Spec}(A_0/K^{\circ\circ}A_0),\qquad
 x\longmapsto\{a\in A_0:|a(x)|<1\}/K^{\circ\circ}A_0
\]
\bi{is well defined and surjective onto all prime ideals.}
{良定义，且满射到全部素理想。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Every analytic point has value at most one on $A_0$,
by Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations} and
Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}. Its elements of value
less than one form a prime ideal containing
$K^{\circ\circ}A_0$, which proves well-definedness.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations} 和
Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}，每个解析点在 $A_0$
上的值都不超过一。值严格小于一的元素构成一个包含
$K^{\circ\circ}A_0$ 的素理想，故映射良定义。}

\bi{First suppose $A_0$ is a domain, and let
$\mathfrak q\subset A_0$ represent a given special-fibre point.
By Lemma~\ref{fnd:integralformalnormalization}, choose
a finite injection $R_0=K^\circ\langle Y\rangle\hookrightarrow A_0$.
The contraction of $\mathfrak q$ defines a prime of
$\widetilde K[Y]$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:polydiscprimecenter}
gives a multiplicative norm on $K\langle Y\rangle$ centered
there. Extend it to the fraction field of $R_0$ by quotients.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:normalbaseprescribedcenter} extends this
absolute value to $\operatorname{Frac}(A_0)$ with center
$\mathfrak q$. It is bounded on $A$: every element of $A_0$
is integral over $R_0$, hence has value at most one, and
the quotient-unit-ball description gives contractivity on $A$.
It therefore defines an analytic point specializing to
$\mathfrak q$.}
{先设 $A_0$ 为整环，令 $\mathfrak q\subset A_0$ 表示给定的
特殊纤维点。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:integralformalnormalization}，
选取有限单射
$R_0=K^\circ\langle Y\rangle\hookrightarrow A_0$。
$\mathfrak q$ 的收缩定义 $\widetilde K[Y]$ 的一个素理想。
Lemma~\ref{fnd:polydiscprimecenter} 给出以它为中心的
$K\langle Y\rangle$ 上的乘性范数。通过取商把它延拓到
$R_0$ 的分式域，再由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:normalbaseprescribedcenter} 延拓到
$\operatorname{Frac}(A_0)$，使中心为 $\mathfrak q$。
所得绝对值在 $A$ 上有界：$A_0$ 的每个元素在 $R_0$ 上整，
因而其值不超过一，商单位球的描述于是给出有界性。
它定义一个特化到 $\mathfrak q$ 的解析点。}

\bi{For general $A_0$, let $P_1,\ldots,P_t$ be the finitely
many minimal primes of the Noetherian algebra $A$, and put
$Q_i=P_i\cap A_0$. Their intersection is the nilradical of $A_0$,
since $A_0\hookrightarrow A$. A prime $\mathfrak q$ of $A_0$
therefore contains some $Q_i$: otherwise a product of elements
of the $Q_i$ outside $\mathfrak q$ gives a contradiction.
The quotient $A_0/Q_i$ is complete by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalmoduletopology}, topologically finite type,
and torsion free as a subring of $A/P_i$. It is admissible by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation}.
Apply the domain case to $\mathfrak q/Q_i$, then compose
the resulting point with $A\to A/P_i$. The zero algebra
has empty source and target.}
{对一般 $A_0$，令 $P_1,\ldots,P_t$ 为 Noetherian 代数 $A$
的有限个极小素理想，记 $Q_i=P_i\cap A_0$。
由于 $A_0\hookrightarrow A$，这些 $Q_i$ 的交为 $A_0$ 的幂零根。
故 $A_0$ 的素理想 $\mathfrak q$ 至少包含一个 $Q_i$：否则从
各 $Q_i$ 中取不属于 $\mathfrak q$ 的元素并相乘即得矛盾。
由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalmoduletopology}，商 $A_0/Q_i$ 完备；
它拓扑有限型，且作为 $A/P_i$ 的子环无挠，因此由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation} 为 admissible。
对 $\mathfrak q/Q_i$ 应用整环情形，并把所得点与
$A\to A/P_i$ 复合即可。零代数的源和目标都为空。}
\end{proof}
