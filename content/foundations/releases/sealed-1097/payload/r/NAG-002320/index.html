Standing definition · exact TeX
\label{v037:f5:certificatesection}

Fix the isometric algebraically closed comparison field
$\Omega$ of Lemma~\ref{v036:f5:comparisonfield}, and write
$p:C_\Omega^{\an}\to C^{\an}$ for the projection.

\begin{definition}[Unramified branch certificates]
\label{v037:f5:branchcertificate}
A point $v\in C^{\an}$ has an \emph{unramified branch
certificate} if there are a point $w\in p^{-1}(v)$,
an unramified marking datum $P$, and three pairwise
disjoint open analytic discs $B_1,B_2,B_3\subset
C_\Omega^{\an}$, with $w\notin\bigcup_iB_i$, such that
\[
          \overline{B_i}=B_i\cup\{w\},
                 \qquad B_i\cap P_K(\Omega)\ne\varnothing
                         \quad(1\leq i\leq3).
\]
Closures are taken in $C_\Omega^{\an}$.
\end{definition}
