An algebraic thickening captures analytic support

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 301

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{An algebraic thickening captures analytic support}
{代数增厚承载解析支撑}]
\label{fnd:analyticcoherentsupportthickening}
\bi{Let $X$ be proper over $K$, let $Z\hookrightarrow X$
be a closed subscheme with ideal $\mathcal I$, and let
$\mathcal F$ be a coherent module on $X^{\an}_G$.
If $\mathcal F$ vanishes on $X^{\an}\setminus Z^{\an}$,
then $(\mathcal I^{\an})^N\mathcal F=0$ for some integer
$N\ge1$. Hence $\mathcal F$ is the closed direct image of a
coherent module on the analytification of
$Z_N=(X,\mathcal O_X/\mathcal I^N)$.}
{设 $X$ 在 $K$ 上适当，$Z\hookrightarrow X$ 是理想为
$\mathcal I$ 的闭子概形，$\mathcal F$ 是 $X^{\an}_G$ 上的
凝聚模。如果 $\mathcal F$ 在 $X^{\an}\setminus Z^{\an}$
上为零，则对某个整数 $N\ge1$ 有
$(\mathcal I^{\an})^N\mathcal F=0$。
因此 $\mathcal F$ 是
$Z_N=(X,\mathcal O_X/\mathcal I^N)$ 的解析化上某个凝聚模
的闭直接像。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use a finite affinoid cover from
Lemma~\ref{fnd:properanalyticfinitecovers}. On one member
$V=\mathcal M(A)$ write $M=\mathcal F(V)$ and
$J=\mathcal I^{\an}(V)$; both are finite over $A$.
For $x\in V$, with prime $\mathfrak p=\ker|\cdot|_x$,
the fibre $M\otimes_A\mathcal H(x)$ is zero exactly when
$M_{\mathfrak p}=0$. Indeed, the residue-field extension
$\kappa(\mathfrak p)\subset\mathcal H(x)$ is faithful,
and Nakayama's lemma applies to the finite module
$M_{\mathfrak p}$. Moreover
$M_{\mathfrak p}=0$ exactly when
$\operatorname{Ann}_A(M)\not\subset\mathfrak p$:
if the localization vanishes, the product of finitely many
denominators killing a generating list of $M$ lies in the
annihilator and outside $\mathfrak p$; the converse is immediate.}
{取 Lemma~\ref{fnd:properanalyticfinitecovers} 的有限
affinoid 覆盖。在其中一个成员 $V=\mathcal M(A)$ 上记
$M=\mathcal F(V)$、$J=\mathcal I^{\an}(V)$；两者都在
$A$ 上有限。对 $x\in V$，记
$\mathfrak p=\ker|\cdot|_x$。纤维
$M\otimes_A\mathcal H(x)$ 为零当且仅当 $M_{\mathfrak p}=0$：
剩余域扩张 $\kappa(\mathfrak p)\subset\mathcal H(x)$
忠实，再对有限模 $M_{\mathfrak p}$ 使用 Nakayama 引理。
此外，$M_{\mathfrak p}=0$ 当且仅当
$\operatorname{Ann}_A(M)\not\subset\mathfrak p$。
确实，若局部化为零，可分别选择分母消灭 $M$ 的有限组生成元，
其乘积属于消灭理想而不属于 $\mathfrak p$；反向显然。}

\bi{Put $B=A/\operatorname{Ann}_A(M)$ with its affinoid
quotient norm. Every point of $\mathcal M(B)$ gives a
nonzero fibre of $M$, so the support hypothesis forces every
element of $J$ to vanish there. By the spectral-radius formula
and Proposition~\ref{fnd:spectralnilradicalinternal}, its
image in $B$ is nilpotent. If $a_1,\ldots,a_s$ generate $J$
and $a_i^{e_i}$ annihilates $M$, then
$J^{1+\sum_i(e_i-1)}M=0$: each monomial of this total degree
has some exponent at least $e_i$. Take the maximum of these
finitely many exponents over the cover. The resulting global
annihilation and Lemma~\ref{fnd:closedanalyticcoherentextension}
give the stated factorization through $Z_N^{\an}$.}
{给 $B=A/\operatorname{Ann}_A(M)$ 配备 affinoid 商范数。
$\mathcal M(B)$ 的每个点都给出 $M$ 的非零纤维，所以支撑
假设迫使 $J$ 的每个元素在其上取值为零。
由谱半径公式及 Proposition~\ref{fnd:spectralnilradicalinternal}，
它在 $B$ 中的像幂零。若 $a_1,\ldots,a_s$ 生成 $J$，且
$a_i^{e_i}$ 消灭 $M$，则
$J^{1+\sum_i(e_i-1)}M=0$：每个具有这个总次数的单项式
都有某个指数至少为 $e_i$。在有限覆盖上取这些指数的最大值，
得到全局消灭关系。再由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:closedanalyticcoherentextension}，便得经过
$Z_N^{\an}$ 的所述分解。}
\end{proof}
