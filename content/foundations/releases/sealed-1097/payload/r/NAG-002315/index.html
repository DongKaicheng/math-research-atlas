Descent of invariant ideals

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 780

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[Descent of invariant ideals]
\label{v037:f5:idealdescent}
Let $L/K$ be the finite unramified Galois lift of a finite
Galois extension of $k$, and put $O'=L^\circ$ and
$G=\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$. Let $\mathcal X$ be an
$O$-scheme. Every $G$-invariant quasi-coherent ideal
$\mathcal J$ of finite type on $\mathcal X_{O'}$, for the
canonical coefficient action, is the extension of a unique
quasi-coherent ideal $\mathcal I$ of finite type on
$\mathcal X$. Its blowup is the coefficient extension of
the blowup of $\mathcal I$. If $\mathcal J$ contains
$\pi^n$, then $\mathcal I$ contains $\pi^n$.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Use the basis $1,\alpha,\ldots,\alpha^{d-1}$ of $O'/O$
from Lemma~\ref{v036:f5:unramifiedcoefficients}.
The matrix $(g(\alpha^i))_{g,i}$ is invertible over $O'$,
by its unit Vandermonde determinant. On an affine open
$\operatorname{Spec}A\subset\mathcal X$, put
$A'=A\otimes_O O'$ and let $J\subset A'$ be the given
invariant ideal. If
$z=\sum_{i=0}^{d-1}a_i\alpha^i\in J$, then
\[
                  g(z)=\sum_{i=0}^{d-1}a_i g(\alpha^i)
                         \qquad(g\in G).
\]
Inverting the matrix expresses each $a_i$ as an $O'$-linear
combination of the $g(z)$. Thus each $a_i$ belongs to
$I=J\cap A$, and $J=IA'$.

Choose finitely many generators of $J$. All their coefficients
belong to $I$, and generate an ideal $I_0\subset A$ with
$I_0A'=J$. The coefficient-of-$1$ map $A'\to A$ is
$A$-linear and is the identity on $A$. Applying it to a
finite expression for any $a\in J\cap A$ proves
$a\in I_0$. Hence $I=I_0$ is finitely generated.
The same coefficient map shows that extension of any ideal
of $A$ contracts back to itself. This proves uniqueness.

On a principal open of $\operatorname{Spec}A$, coefficient
extraction commutes with localization. The ideals just
constructed therefore agree on an affine basis of every
overlap and glue to $\mathcal I$. The assertion about
$\pi^n$ follows by contraction. Finally $O'/O$ is faithfully
flat by Lemma~\ref{v036:f5:unramifiedcoefficients}, and
\cite[Tag 0805]{StacksAlg} identifies the two blowups.
This identification is induced by the coefficient map on
their Rees algebras, and respects the canonical $G$-actions.
\end{proof}
