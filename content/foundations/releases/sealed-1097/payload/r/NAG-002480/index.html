A logarithmic current in the retraction scope

G Stable inertia and original-field models

Open in the English reader · PDF page 2026

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[A logarithmic current in the retraction scope]
\label{f8cont:log}
Let $X$ be good, Hausdorff and pure of analytic dimension $n\geq1$,
and let $\mathcal F$ be a coherent $\mathcal O_{X_G}$-module.
Let $g,h\in\mathcal O_X(X)$ be non-zero-divisors in every local ring
and put
\[
       Z=\{|g|=0\}\cup\{|h|=0\},\qquad
       u=\log|g/h|\quad\text{on }X\setminus Z.
\]
Assume $(\mathrm R_{n-1})$ for $Z$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{f8cont:eq:logcurrent}
       T_{g/h,\mathcal F}(\alpha)=
              \int_{X\setminus Z,\,\mathcal F|_{X\setminus Z}}u\alpha
\end{equation}
is well-defined and continuous on
$(\E^{n,n}(X),\tau_{\mathrm{cut}})=
 (\E^{n,n}(X),\tau_{\mathrm{ord}})$.
The displayed functional is independent of the representatives of the
ordinary test form. It is a current of bidimension $(n,n)$ for this
fixed regular quotient.

For each cutoff presentation $Q_i$, there is $C_i<\infty$ with
\begin{equation}\label{f8cont:eq:C0}
 |T_{g/h,\mathcal F}(Q_i\eta)|
 \leq C_i\max_{I,J}\sup_{t_{f_i}(V_i)}|\eta_{I,J}|.
\end{equation}
There are smooth functions $u_M\in\A^{0,0}(X)$, $M\geq0$, formed by
constant smooth truncation of $\log|g|$ and $\log|h|$ near their zeros,
such that for each $i$ one number $M_i$ satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{f8cont:eq:stationary}
 \int_{X,\mathcal F}u_M Q_i\eta=T_{g/h,\mathcal F}(Q_i\eta)
       \quad(M\geq M_i,\ \eta\in E^{n,n}(\Omega_i)).
\end{equation}
The threshold is uniform over the whole fixed presentation, with no
bound on $\eta$ required.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Fix a cutoff presentation $Q_i$ and put $K=\supp\chi_i$.
For each $z\in K\cap Z$, Lemma~\ref{f8cont:retraction} applied to
its fixed tuple makes every pulled-back $(n,n)$-form zero on one
ordinary neighbourhood of $z$. A finite union $O$ of these
neighbourhoods covers $K\cap Z$. Therefore
$K_i=K\setminus O$ is compact in $X\setminus Z$ and contains
$\supp Q_i\eta$ for every $\eta$. The cutoff presentations span the
test space, so every test has compact support in the complement.
There $g$ and $h$ are locally analytic units, using
\base{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion} on smaller affinoids as in
\base{v037:forms:smoothcutoff}. Thus $u$ is an ordinary smooth moment
function. The product $u\alpha$ has compact support in the complement.
The integral exists and is independent of its chart representatives by
\base{v037:forms:compactintegral}. Open restrictions are pure of dimension
$n$ by \base{v037:integration:puritylocality}. These facts also prove
linearity of \eqref{f8cont:eq:logcurrent}.

For the fixed $i$ and $K_i$, by
\base{v037:forms:finitepartition}, choose $\theta\in\A_c^{0,0}(X)$
supported in $X\setminus Z$ and equal to one near $K_i$.
The function $b=\ext(\theta u)$ is ordinary smooth on $X$, and locality
of the baseline integral gives
\[
       T_{g/h,\mathcal F}(Q_i\eta)=\int_{X,\mathcal F}b\,Q_i\eta.
\]
Compute this integral on a finite affinoid cover of the fixed support
of $\chi_i$ inside $\Int(V_i,X)$, adjoining $f_i$ to representatives
of $b\chi_i$. The finite inclusion--exclusion formula
\base{v037:integration:eqcompactintegral} and
\base{v037:integration:globalchain} give fixed cells, actual coherent
module weights and fixed coefficient multipliers. On each cell, the
absolute integral is at most its lattice-normalized volume times the
absolute weight times a fixed constant times the right-hand coefficient
supremum in \eqref{f8cont:eq:C0}, by
\base{v037:integration:polyhedralcalculus}. The projected coefficient
arguments lie in $t_{f_i}(V_i)$. Summing these finitely many bounds proves
\eqref{f8cont:eq:C0} and continuity by the definition of the final topology.
No positivity or integration by parts enters this estimate.

Choose a smooth real function $\rho$ with $\rho=0$ on $(-\infty,0]$
and $\rho=1$ on $[1,\infty)$, as explicitly constructed in
\base{v037:forms:smoothcutoff}. Put
\[
 \lambda(s)=s\rho(s+1),\qquad
 \lambda_M(t)=-M+\lambda(t+M).
\]
Thus $\lambda_M(t)=t$ for $t\geq-M$ and is $-M$ for $t\leq-M-1$.
For an analytic function $a$ set
\[
 \ell_M(a)(x)=
 \begin{cases}
   \lambda_M(\log|a(x)|),& |a(x)|>0,\\
   -M,& |a(x)|=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
This is an ordinary smooth function: near a zero it is constant on
$\{|a|<e^{-M-1}\}$, and on the nonzero locus it is a smooth function of
a unit moment coordinate. The scalar sheaf in
\base{v037:forms:smoothcutoff} glues these representatives.
Set $u_M=\ell_M(g)-\ell_M(h)$.

If $K_i$ is empty, every $Q_i\eta$ is zero and stationarity is immediate.
Otherwise, the positive continuous functions $|g|$ and $|h|$ have positive minima
on $K_i$. Choose $M_i\geq0$ strictly larger than both negatives of the
minimum logarithms there. For $M\geq M_i$, $u_M=u$ on a neighbourhood
of $K_i$. Since every $Q_i\eta$ is supported in $K_i$, the two products
agree as ordinary forms after extension by zero. This proves
\eqref{f8cont:eq:stationary}.
It also gives absolute finiteness on every finite cell presentation:
all coefficients and the logarithms are bounded on the relevant compact
supports. No separate representation by Radon measures is required.

\end{proof}
