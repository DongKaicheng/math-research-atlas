Coherent embedded torsion quotient; F8U-G

G Stable inertia and original-field models

Open in the English reader · PDF page 2018

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Coherent embedded torsion quotient; F8U-G]
\label{prop:gluing}
Assume (SC) and the stated v0.38 coherent-domain interfaces.
On a good Hausdorff pure \(n\)-dimensional analytic \(X\), the ideals
\(T(A)\) on affinoid domains glue to a coherent ideal \(\mathcal T\).
The closed subspace
\[
                 i:X^{\mathrm u}=(X,\OO_X/\mathcal T)\longrightarrow X
                                                                    \tag{3.1}
\]
is finite, is a homeomorphism on underlying points, is pure of analytic
dimension \(n\), and satisfies \(S_1\).
Every regular analytic local function on \(X\) remains regular on
\(X^{\mathrm u}\). Pullback sends \(\Int(X/K)\) into
\(\Int(X^{\mathrm u}/K)\).
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Let \(A\to B\) represent an actual nonempty affinoid domain of
\(\M(A)\). Restriction is exact on coherent sheaves, and
\src{NAG-001624} identifies it with \(B\otimes_A-\) on finite modules.
In particular \(TB=T(A)\otimes_A B\) injects into \(B\).
The lower-dimensional support bound of \hyperref[lem:dimension]{NAG-002669} applies to the original torsion ideal. Its support is the restriction of \(\Supp T(A)\), so has analytic
dimension at most \(n-1\), by domain base change
\src{NAG-001846} and dimension locality. The domain is pure of
dimension \(n\) by \src{NAG-002413}. Therefore \(TB\subseteq T(B)\):
a module with smaller-dimensional support has zero localization
at every minimal prime of \(B\).

The quotient \(B/TB\) is the actual domain algebra obtained by
base change from \(A^{\mathrm u}\), using
\src{NAG-001565} and \src{NAG-001846}.
By Lemma~\ref{lem:algebra}, \(A^{\mathrm u}\) is \(S_1\);
(SC) makes \(B/TB\) \(S_1\) as well.
Its underlying space is unchanged by the nilpotent ideal \(TB\)
and remains pure of dimension \(n\).
The image of \(T(B)\) in \(B/TB\) has support of dimension at most
\(n-1\). A nonzero submodule of a Noetherian \(S_1\) pure structure
module has an associated prime among its minimal primes, so cannot
have smaller-dimensional support. The image is zero, proving
\[
                       T(B)=T(A)\otimes_A B.                      \tag{3.2}
\]
Thus the coherent ideals coincide on all actual affinoid intersections
and satisfy the cocycle condition. The coherent sheaf equivalence
\src{NAG-001624} glues them, without assuming a finite global cover.

Each local quotient is by a finite nilpotent ideal, as in the actual closed quotient of \hyperref[fnd:closedimmersiontargetglocal]{NAG-001653}, so (3.1) is a
finite closed immersion and a homeomorphism. Its analytic dimension
and purity agree with those of \(X\); (SC) gives the asserted \(S_1\)
property. Regularity follows locally from Lemma~\ref{lem:algebra}.
Finite morphisms are relatively interior by \src{NAG-001701};
composition of interiors, \src{NAG-001704}, proves the last claim.
In particular a boundaryless \(X\) gives boundaryless \(X^{\mathrm u}\).
\end{proof}
