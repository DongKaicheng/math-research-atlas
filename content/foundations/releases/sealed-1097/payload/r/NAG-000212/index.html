Result 1.8.1

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 61

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}\label{fnd:radiusfieldprop}
\bi{The algebra $K_r$ is a complete valued field and a $K$-affinoid algebra. Its spectrum consists of one point. It admits the presentation}
{$K_r$ 是 complete valued field，也是 $K$-affinoid algebra，其 spectrum 只有一个点。它具有 presentation}
\[
K_r\simeq K\{r^{-1}T,rS\}/(TS-1).
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The norm-strict quotient presentation is the one-variable case of Theorem~\ref{fnd:multilaurentactualpresentation}.}
{范数严格的商表示是 Theorem~\ref{fnd:multilaurentactualpresentation} 的单变量情形。}
\bi{For distinct $i,j$, nonzero terms cannot have equal sizes: $|a_i|r^i=|a_j|r^j$ would imply $r^{i-j}\in|K^\times|$. A nonzero Laurent sum therefore has a unique largest term $a_mT^m$. It can be written $a_mT^m(1+h)$ with $\|h\|_r<1$, and geometric inversion gives its inverse. The norm remains multiplicative on completion. The presentation follows by reducing monomials using $TS=1$, with positive powers assigned to $T$ and negative powers to $S$; the convergence conditions on the resulting Laurent coefficients are exactly the stated ones.}
{不同的 $i,j$ 对应的非零项不可能有相同的 size：$|a_i|r^i=|a_j|r^j$ 会推出 $r^{i-j}\in|K^\times|$。因此非零 Laurent sum 有唯一最大项 $a_mT^m$，可写为 $a_mT^m(1+h)$，其中 $\|h\|_r<1$；geometric inversion 给出 inverse。Norm 在 completion 后仍为 multiplicative。利用 $TS=1$ 化简 monomials，正次幂归给 $T$、负次幂归给 $S$，所得 Laurent coefficients 的 convergence conditions 正是上述条件，从而得到 presentation。}

\bi{At any bounded point, the bounds on $T,S$ and $TS=1$ force $|T|=r$. Every Laurent sum then has the same unique largest term as above, so its value is forced. Thus there is only the Gauss point.}
{在任何 bounded point 处，$T,S$ 的 bounds 及 $TS=1$ 强制 $|T|=r$。每个 Laurent sum 都具有上述唯一最大项，所以它的 value 被唯一确定。因此只有 Gauss point。}
\end{proof}
