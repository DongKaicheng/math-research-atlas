Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Pointed projection independence in finite Gauss charts]
\label{mpw:pointedweight}
In Definition~\ref{mpw:chartclass}, ordinary affinoid neighbourhoods
$U$ of $x$ in $Y$ have the same germ at $w_0$ of their existing
top-dimensional module chains. This germ is the affine $d$-plane
$w_0+N_{\mathbb R}$ with coefficient
\[
                               \mu_x(M,f)=j_A D_M.
\]
For $M=B$ the coefficient is positive; a zero module coefficient
denotes the zero chain.

For each $U$, outside finitely many proper hyperplanes in a
neighbourhood of $s_0$, its entire moment fibre at $v=b+As$ has
the actual algebra $D_s=B\widehat\otimes_{R_P}R_s$.
For every admissible $Q$, that same algebra is norm-admissible
finite over $R_{Q,v}$ and
\begin{equation}
\label{mpw:eqcalibration}
 \frac{\dim_{H_{Q,v}}
     \bigl(M\otimes_B(D_s\widehat\otimes_{R_{Q,v}}H_{Q,v})\bigr)}
      {e_Q}=j_A D_M.
\end{equation}
The entire fibre and module, including their scheme structure,
occur in this formula. Its value is independent of the projection
and of the ordinary affinoid neighbourhood.
\end{theorem}
