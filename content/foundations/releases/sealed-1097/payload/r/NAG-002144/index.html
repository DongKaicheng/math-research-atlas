An intrinsic blowup for a nodal cluster

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 723

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{An intrinsic blowup for a nodal cluster}
{结点标记簇的内禀 blowup}]
\label{cand:nodalmarkcluster}
\bi{Let $q$ be a node of a split nodal model $\mathcal X$,
and let a nonempty finite set $P(q)$ of marked sections
specialize to $q$. There is a finite-type ideal
$\mathcal I_q$, equal to the unit ideal away from $q$ and
open after formal completion, such that its blowup is again
a split nodal model, retains every old component point, and
moves at least one member of $P(q)$ to a smooth special point.
No member of $P(q)$ moves to an outer attaching node.
The ideal depends only on the marked cluster and the model,
so every semilinear isometric model isomorphism carrying
one marked cluster to another carries their ideals to one
another. Projectivity over $O$ is preserved.}
{设 $q$ 为分裂结点模型 $\mathcal X$ 的结点，
且非空有限标记截面组 $P(q)$ 专化到 $q$。
存在有限型理想 $\mathcal I_q$，在 $q$ 外为单位理想，
形式完备化后为开理想，使其 blowup 仍为分裂结点模型，
保留全部旧分量点，并将 $P(q)$ 中至少一个标记移至
光滑特殊点。$P(q)$ 中没有标记移至外侧连接结点。
此理想只依赖标记簇和模型，故将一个标记簇映到另一个的
每个半线性等距模型同构，都将对应理想映到对应理想。
模型在 $O$ 上的射影性保持。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use a pointed chart $st=b$ at $q$, with $0<|b|<1$.
The rational-pointed tube comparison identifies $]q[$
with $|b|<|s|<1$. A section specializing to $q$ lifts
uniquely to the pointed auxiliary chart: its pullback
along that chart is étale over the henselian ring $O$
and has the specified rational closed point, so the
henselian property of
Lemma~\ref{cand:completevaluationhenselian} supplies the lift.
Write}
{在 $q$ 处取带点坐标 $st=b$，其中 $0<|b|<1$。
有理带点管域比较将 $]q[$ 识别为 $|b|<|s|<1$。
专化到 $q$ 的截面唯一提升到指定辅助带点坐标片：
沿该坐标片拉回后，所得态射在 Hensel 环 $O$ 上
étale，并有指定的有理闭点，故由
Lemma~\ref{cand:completevaluationhenselian}
所给 Hensel 性得到此提升。记}
\[
 a_P=s(P),\qquad d_P=t(P),\qquad
 a_Pd_P=b,\qquad
 \delta_q=\max_{P\in P(q)}\max(|a_P|,|d_P|).
\]
\bi{Then $0<\delta_q<1$, $\delta_q\in|K^\times|$,
and $|b|\leq\delta_q^2$. Choose $\lambda\in O$ with
$|\lambda|=\delta_q$. Near $q$ put}
{则 $0<\delta_q<1$、$\delta_q\in|K^\times|$，
且 $|b|\leq\delta_q^2$。选取满足
$|\lambda|=\delta_q$ 的 $\lambda\in O$，在 $q$ 附近令}
\[
 \mathcal I_q=(\lambda)+
                 \operatorname{Fitt}_1\Omega_{\mathcal X/O}.
\]
\bi{Shrink this neighborhood to omit every other node.
The construction and proof of
Lemma~\ref{cand:nodefittinginsertion} apply verbatim to these
rational pointed charts. That proof uses algebraic
closedness only to supply the rational split chart, which
is a hypothesis here: finite generation, gluing with the
unit ideal, étale descent of the charts, retention of old
vertices, and projectivity are all proved over the displayed
valuation ring. Its local pullback is
$(\lambda,s,t)$, so
Lemma~\ref{cand:symmetricnodeblowup} gives the actual
three charts.}
{将此邻域缩小到不包含其他结点。
Lemma~\ref{cand:nodefittinginsertion}
的构造和证明逐字适用于这些有理带点坐标片。
该证明使用代数闭性，只是为了得到有理分裂坐标，
而这里已将其作为假设；有限生成性、与单位理想的
粘合、坐标片的 étale 下降、旧顶点的保留及射影性，
均是在所列赋值环上证明的。局部拉回为
$(\lambda,s,t)$，故
Lemma~\ref{cand:symmetricnodeblowup}
给出实际的三个坐标片。}

\bi{Every section in the cluster lies in the $\lambda$-chart:
its coordinates there are
$u=a_P/\lambda$, $v=d_P/\lambda$, both in $O$.
At least one section has $u$ or $v$ a unit by the definition
of $\delta_q$. If $|b|=\delta_q^2$, the entire middle special
chart $uv=\overline{b/\lambda^2}$ is smooth.
If $|b|<\delta_q^2$, its only node is $u=v=0$.
Thus at least one marked section becomes smooth, and the
only possible remaining nodal marked specialization is the
middle node. The outer attaching nodes are outside the
middle chart.}
{簇内每个截面都位于 $\lambda$-片，因为其中坐标为
$u=a_P/\lambda$、$v=d_P/\lambda$，均属于 $O$。
由 $\delta_q$ 的定义，至少一个截面的 $u$ 或 $v$
为单位元。若 $|b|=\delta_q^2$，整个中间特殊片
$uv=\overline{b/\lambda^2}$ 光滑。
若 $|b|<\delta_q^2$，其中唯一结点为 $u=v=0$。
故至少一个标记截面变为光滑，仍为结点的标记专化
只可能在中间结点。两个外侧连接结点均在中间坐标片外。}

\bi{It remains to check that the centre is intrinsic.
Between two normalized annular coordinates
$A(|b|,1)$ and $A(|b'|,1)$,
Corollary~\ref{cand:openannuluscoordinateisometry} gives
$|b'|=|b|$ and either $|s'|=|s|$ or
$|s'|=|b|/|s|$. It therefore preserves the unordered pair
$(|a_P|,|d_P|)$ and hence $\delta_q$.
This also holds semilinearly, since the field automorphism
is isometric. A principal ideal of $O$ depends only on the
absolute value of its nonzero generator, and the Fitting
ideal is intrinsic. The glued ideal is consequently
independent of all choices and has the asserted covariance.}
{还需检查中心的内禀性。对两个归一化圆环坐标
$A(|b|,1)$、$A(|b'|,1)$，
Corollary~\ref{cand:openannuluscoordinateisometry}
给出 $|b'|=|b|$，并且或者 $|s'|=|s|$，
或者 $|s'|=|b|/|s|$。因此无序对
$(|a_P|,|d_P|)$ 保持，从而 $\delta_q$ 保持。
半线性情形同样成立，因为域自同构等距。
$O$ 的主理想只依赖非零生成元的绝对值，
而 Fitting 理想是内禀的。因此粘合所得理想与全部
选择无关，并具有所述协变性。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Throughout this section $K$ is a complete nontrivially
valued field, $O=K^\circ$, and $k=\widetilde K$.
A split nodal model is a separated flat finitely presented $O$-scheme
with smooth generic fibre and reduced nodal special fibre,
whose nodes are $k$-rational and admit the actual rational
pointed charts of Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting}.
Its formal generic fibre is assumed Hausdorff.
A marking is a finite set of distinct $K$-rational generic
points. In the proper case each extends uniquely to an
$O$-section by \cite[Tag 0BX5]{StacksAlg}.}
{本节中 $K$ 为完备非平凡赋值域，$O=K^\circ$、
$k=\widetilde K$。分裂结点模型指分离、平坦、有限表示
$O$-概形，其泛纤维光滑、特殊纤维约化且仅有结点，
并且结点均为 $k$-有理点，具有
Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting}
中的实际有理带点坐标片。假设其形式泛纤维为 Hausdorff。
标记指有限个互异的 $K$-有理泛纤维点。在 proper 情形，
由 \cite[Tag 0BX5]{StacksAlg}，每个标记唯一延拓为
$O$-截面。}
