\begin{lemma}[\bi{Roots of a monic polynomial}{首一多项式的根界}]
\label{fnd:monicmaximumroot}
\bi{Let $H$ be a complete non-Archimedean field and let
$P(Z)=Z^d+c_1Z^{d-1}+\cdots+c_d$ be monic of positive degree.
In a finite splitting field, endowed with the extending absolute value,}
{设 $H$ 为完备非阿基米德域，
$P(Z)=Z^d+c_1Z^{d-1}+\cdots+c_d$ 为正次数首一多项式。
在配备延拓绝对值的有限分裂域中，有}
\[
 \max_{P(\alpha)=0}|\alpha|
     =\max_{1\leq i\leq d}|c_i|^{1/i}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
\bi{A finite splitting field exists by \cite[Tag 09HU]{StacksAlg};
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} gives its extending absolute value.
Write $R$ for the maximum on the right. If $|\alpha|>R$, the leading
term $\alpha^d$ has strictly larger absolute value than every other term
of $P(\alpha)$, so $P(\alpha)\ne0$. Thus every root has size at most $R$.
Conversely, if $S$ is the maximum size of the roots, the elementary symmetric
expressions for the coefficients give $|c_i|\leq S^i$. This proves $R\leq S$.
The argument includes repeated roots and the polynomial $Z^d$.}
{由 \cite[Tag 09HU]{StacksAlg} 存在有限分裂域，
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 给出其延拓绝对值。
以 $R$ 记右端最大值。若 $|\alpha|>R$，则 $P(\alpha)$ 的首项
$\alpha^d$ 的绝对值严格大于其余各项，故 $P(\alpha)\ne0$。
因此所有根的大小至多为 $R$。反之，若 $S$ 为所有根的最大绝对值，
系数的初等对称表达式给出 $|c_i|\leq S^i$，从而 $R\leq S$。
此论证包括重根及多项式 $Z^d$。}
\end{proof}
