Scaled power bounds for arbitrary positive radii

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 121

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Scaled power bounds for arbitrary positive radii}{任意正半径的缩放幂界}]
\label{fnd:generalizedpointwisepowerbound}
\bi{Let $A$ be any norm-admissible $K$-affinoid algebra, let $f\in A$, and let $s>0$ be any real number. Then}
{设 $A$ 为任意 norm-admissible $K$-affinoid 代数，$f\in A$，且 $s>0$ 为任意实数。则}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 &\bigl(|f(x)|\leq s\text{ for every }x\in\mathcal M(A)\bigr)\\
 &\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \bigl(\exists C\geq1\ \forall m\geq0:\ \|f^m\|\leq Cs^m\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{The constant may depend on $A$, its chosen norm, $f$ and $s$. It is not asserted to be uniform in $s$. Neither reducedness nor a restriction on the residue characteristic is needed.}
{常数可以依赖于 $A$、所选范数、$f$ 和 $s$；并不要求它关于 $s$ 一致。不要求约化性，也不限制剩余特征。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The zero algebra is immediate. Otherwise choose, by Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitebatchradiuschoice} and Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification}, a single free extension $L=K_r$ for which $A_r$ is strict and $s\in\sqrt{|L^\times|}$. Thus there are $n\geq1$ and $c\in L^\times$ with $|c|=s^n$. Give $A_r$ its actual coefficient norm, which is admissible for its strict structure. Every point $y$ of $\mathcal M_L(A_r)$ restricts to a point of $\mathcal M_K(A)$. If the left-hand condition holds, $h=(f\otimes1)^n/c$ has $|h(y)|\leq1$ for every such $y$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointwisepowerbounded} supplies $C_0\geq1$ with $\|h^q\|\leq C_0$ for all $q\geq0$, also for this equivalent admissible norm. Homogeneity and the isometric constant embedding give}
{零代数的情形直接成立。否则，由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitebatchradiuschoice} 及 Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification}，选择同一个自由扩域 $L=K_r$，使 $A_r$ 为 strict，且 $s\in\sqrt{|L^\times|}$。因此，存在 $n\geq1$ 及 $c\in L^\times$，满足 $|c|=s^n$。给 $A_r$ 使用实际系数范数；该范数相对于其 strict 结构为 admissible。$\mathcal M_L(A_r)$ 的每个点 $y$ 都限制为 $\mathcal M_K(A)$ 的点。若左侧条件成立，则 $h=(f\otimes1)^n/c$ 在每个这样的 $y$ 上均满足 $|h(y)|\leq1$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointwisepowerbounded}，存在 $C_0\geq1$，使所有 $q\geq0$ 都满足 $\|h^q\|\leq C_0$；这一结论也适用于此处等价的 admissible 范数。由齐次性及常系数嵌入的等距性，得到}
\[
 \|f^{nq}\|\leq C_0s^{nq}.
\]
\bi{Write $m=nq+j$, where $0\leq j<n$. Submultiplicativity gives $\|f^m\|\leq C_0s^{nq}\|f^j\|$, so the required bound holds with}
{写 $m=nq+j$，其中 $0\leq j<n$。次乘性给出 $\|f^m\|\leq C_0s^{nq}\|f^j\|$，因此可取}
\[
 C=C_0\max_{0\leq j<n}s^{-j}\|f^j\|.
\]
\bi{Conversely, contractivity of bounded multiplicative seminorms, proved by the elementary power argument in Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}, gives $|f(x)|^m\leq Cs^m$. Taking $m$th roots and letting $m$ increase proves $|f(x)|\leq s$. All absolute values used on auxiliary fields restrict to the specified one on $K$.}
{反之，Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive} 中的初等取幂论证说明有界乘性半范数为 contractive，故 $|f(x)|^m\leq Cs^m$。取 $m$ 次方根并令 $m$ 增大，即得 $|f(x)|\leq s$。所有辅助域上的绝对值都限制为 $K$ 上原先指定的绝对值。}
\end{proof}
