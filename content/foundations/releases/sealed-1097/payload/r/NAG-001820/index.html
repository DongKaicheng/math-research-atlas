Naive flatness and a trivial-residue Gauss lift

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 486

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Naive flatness and a trivial-residue Gauss lift}{朴素平坦性与平凡剩余域的 Gauss 提升}]
\label{fnd:generalnaivegausslift}
\bi{For a morphism $Y\to X$ of good analytic spaces over any complete field, let $\mathcal F$ be coherent and naively $X$-flat at $y$, and suppose $\mathcal H(x)$ is trivially valued for $x=f(y)$. Its pullback under the simultaneous affine-line base change is naively $X^*$-flat at the radius-$r$ Gauss lift $y^*$ for any $0<r<1$.}
{对任意完备域上 good 解析空间间的态射 $Y\to X$，设凝聚层 $\mathcal F$ 在 $y$ 处朴素 $X$-平坦，并假设 $x=f(y)$ 处的 $\mathcal H(x)$ 平凡赋值。则对每个 $0<r<1$，其沿同时仿射直线基变换的拉回，在半径 $r$ 的 Gauss 提升 $y^*$ 处朴素 $X^*$-平坦。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$, $S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$, $R^*=\mathcal O_{X^*,x^*}$, $S^*=\mathcal O_{Y^*,y^*}$ and $M=\mathcal F_y$. Choose relative closed discs of radius greater than $r$ around the two Gauss points. Their ordinary stalks agree with those of the affine-line products, so Theorem~\ref{fnd:relativepolydiscanalyticflat} makes $R^*$ flat over $R$ and $S^*$ flat over $S$. Coherent pullback gives $M^*=M\otimes_SS^*$; tensoring injections twice shows that $M^*$ is $R$-flat. Put $I=\mathfrak m_RR^*$. Represent a finite generating family of \(\mathfrak m_R\) on an
ordinary affinoid neighbourhood of \(x\), and let \(X_0\) be
the closed quotient it defines. The ordinary quotient stalk
is \(\mathcal O_{X_0,x}=R/\mathfrak m_R\). Its completed residue
field at \(x\) is still \(\mathcal H(x)\), since all generators
vanish at \(x\). On the simultaneous affine-line space the
pullback closed quotient is \(X_0^*=X_0\times_K\mathbb A_K^{1,\mathrm{an}}\).
On the actual affinoid product charts its ideal is the extension
of the original ideal, by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticproducts} and
Corollary~\ref{cand:groundfieldcoherenttensor}. Since the generators evaluate to zero at \(x\), their
pullbacks also evaluate to zero at \(x^*\). The Gauss formula
on the quotient coefficients gives that same point in \(X_0^*\).
The local ring
of this quotient at the radius-\(r\) Gauss point is \(R^*/I\).
These ordinary quotient stalk identities are the single-point
finite stalk formula of Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation},
applied to the affinoid quotient maps with their quotient norms.
By Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalgaussfieldgerm} on the good space \(X_0\),
this ordinary ring is a field. Thus \(I\) is proper and
\(M^*/IM^*\) is \(R^*/I\)-flat.}
{记 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$、$R^*=\mathcal O_{X^*,x^*}$、$S^*=\mathcal O_{Y^*,y^*}$ 及 $M=\mathcal F_y$。在两个 Gauss 点周围选择半径大于 $r$ 的相对闭圆盘；它们的普通茎与仿射直线乘积的普通茎相同，所以 Theorem~\ref{fnd:relativepolydiscanalyticflat} 说明 $R^*$ 在 $R$ 上平坦、$S^*$ 在 $S$ 上平坦。凝聚拉回给出 $M^*=M\otimes_SS^*$；连续两次张量单射，便知 $M^*$ 在 $R$ 上平坦。置 $I=\mathfrak m_RR^*$。在 \(x\) 的普通 affinoid 邻域上表示 \(\mathfrak m_R\) 的一组有限
生成元，并以 \(X_0\) 记其定义的闭商。其普通商茎为
\(\mathcal O_{X_0,x}=R/\mathfrak m_R\)。因这些生成元在 \(x\)
处均为零，\(X_0\) 在 \(x\) 处的完备剩余域仍为 \(\mathcal H(x)\)。
同步作仿射直线乘积后，拉回的闭商为
\(X_0^*=X_0\times_K\mathbb A_K^{1,\mathrm{an}}\)。
由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticproducts} 及
Corollary~\ref{cand:groundfieldcoherenttensor}，在实际 affinoid
乘积图上其理想是原理想的扩张。生成元在 \(x\) 处取值为零，
故其拉回在 \(x^*\) 处也取值为零。商系数上的 Gauss 公式给出
\(X_0^*\) 中的同一点。此商在半径 \(r\) 的 Gauss 点
处的局部环为 \(R^*/I\)。这些普通商茎公式来自
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation} 的单点有限茎公式，
应用于采用商范数的 affinoid 商映射。对 good 空间 \(X_0\) 应用
Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalgaussfieldgerm}，此普通环为域。因此 \(I\)
是真理想，且 \(M^*/IM^*\) 在 \(R^*/I\) 上平坦。}

\bi{Tensor a free $R$-resolution of $R/\mathfrak m_R$ with the flat algebra $R^*$. It is a free resolution of $R^*/I$, and its tensor with $M^*$ has no positive homology because $M^*$ is $R$-flat. Hence $\operatorname{Tor}^{R^*}_1(M^*,R^*/I)=0$. The rings $R^*,S^*$ are Noetherian and $M^*$ is finite over $S^*$. The exact local criterion \cite[Tag 00ML]{StacksAlg} makes $M^*$ flat over $R^*$, as required.}
{将 $R/\mathfrak m_R$ 的自由 $R$-分解与平坦代数 $R^*$ 张量，得到 $R^*/I$ 的自由分解。因 $M^*$ 在 $R$ 上平坦，此分解再与 $M^*$ 张量后没有正次同调，故 $\operatorname{Tor}^{R^*}_1(M^*,R^*/I)=0$。环 $R^*,S^*$ 为 Noetherian，且 $M^*$ 在 $S^*$ 上有限。精确局部判据 \cite[Tag 00ML]{StacksAlg} 遂给出 $M^*$ 在 $R^*$ 上平坦。}
\end{proof}
