Rank of the finite direct image

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1958

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[Rank of the finite direct image]\label{f9ds:normrank}
For an analytic line bundle $\cL$ on $Y$, the module $q_*\cL$ is
locally free of rank $n$ on ordinary opens of $X$.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Choose an affinoid neighbourhood on which $B=\Gamma(q^{-1}V,\cO_Y)$
is free of rank $n$ over $A=\Gamma(V,\cO_X)$.
The module $P=\Gamma(q^{-1}V,\cL)$ is invertible over $B$.
The dual-basis argument makes $P$ a direct summand of a finite free
$B$-module, so it is finite projective over $A$.
For any residue field $k$ of $A$, the algebra $B_k$ is Artinian of
$k$-dimension $n$. An invertible module on each local Artinian factor
is free of rank one: lift a nonzero residue generator and apply
Nakayama; projectivity splits the resulting surjection, and its
kernel has zero residue and hence is zero. It follows that
$\dim_k P_k=\dim_k B_k=n$. Thus $P$ is projective of constant rank
$n$. Its coherent sheaf has flat ordinary stalks, and
\tagref{NAG-001753} gives ordinary local freeness of that rank.
\end{proof}
