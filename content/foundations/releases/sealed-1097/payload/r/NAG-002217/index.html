\begin{theorem}[\bi{Units along an \etale skeletal simplex}
{\etale 骨架单纯形上的单位元}]
\label{cand:etalecellunitsaffine}
\bi{Use the notation and hypotheses of
Theorem~\ref{cand:etaleskeletonfinitefaces}. Put
$\Gamma=v(K^\times)$. For every $\eta\in Q_I$ and every
$g\in B^\times$, the function}
{使用 Theorem~\ref{cand:etaleskeletonfinitefaces} 的记号与
假设，置 $\Gamma=v(K^\times)$。对于每个
$\eta\in Q_I$ 及 $g\in B^\times$，函数}
\[
                 w\longmapsto v(g(x_{\eta,w}))
                                  \qquad(w\in\Delta_I)
\]
\bi{is $\Gamma$-integral-affine: after eliminating any
one coordinate in $I$, it has an integral linear part
and a constant term in $\Gamma$.}
{为 $\Gamma$-整仿射函数：消去 $I$ 中任一个坐标后，
其线性部分为整，常数项属于 $\Gamma$。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Let $H_0$ be the completion of
$K(X_j:j\notin I,T_1,\ldots,T_q)$ in the unit Gauss
norm. Its residue field is $F_I$, by
Proposition~\ref{new:gauss_residue_formula}, and its
value group is $|K^\times|$: a nonzero polynomial attains
its Gauss norm at a coefficient, fractions have the same
property for their values, and completion does not change
the value group since a nonzero convergent limit has the
same norm as every sufficiently close term.}
{令 $H_0$ 为
$K(X_j:j\notin I,T_1,\ldots,T_q)$ 关于单位 Gauss 范数的
完备化。由 Proposition~\ref{new:gauss_residue_formula}，
其剩余域为 $F_I$，值群则为 $|K^\times|$：非零多项式的
Gauss 范数在某个系数处达到，分式的值具有同一性质，而
完备化不改变值群，因为非零收敛极限与充分接近的每一项
具有相同范数。}

\bi{Send the zero-weight variables to these fixed
elements of $H_0^\circ$. The remaining formal base is}
{把零权变量送到 $H_0^\circ$ 中上述固定元素。
剩余的形式基底为}
\[
 H_0^\circ\langle X_i:i\in I\rangle/
       \left(\prod_{i\in I}X_i-b\right),
 \qquad b=a\left(\prod_{j\notin I}X_j\right)^{-1},
 \qquad |b|=|a|.
\]
\bi{Base-change the algebraic square-Jacobian
presentation of Lemma~\ref{cand:formaletaleboundedpresentation}
and then complete. The result is an admissible formally
\etale algebra over this base. The justification is the
same explicit completion argument in
Proposition~\ref{cand:actualskeletalfibrestrata}: the
algebraic presentation is flat, so it has no scalar
torsion; Lemma~\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion} preserves
this under completion, and
Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation} gives the
admissible finite presentation.}
{基变换 Lemma~\ref{cand:formaletaleboundedpresentation}
中的代数平方 Jacobian 表示，然后完备化。
所得代数在此基底上 admissible 且形式 \etale。
其理由就是 Proposition~\ref{cand:actualskeletalfibrestrata}
中显式的完备化论证：代数表示平坦，故无标量挠元；
Lemma~\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion} 使此性质在完备化后
保持，而 Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation}
给出 admissible 有限表示。}

\bi{Above the all-zero special point this model has the
residual point with field $l_\eta=\kappa(\eta)$, a finite
separable extension of $F_I$.
Lemma~\ref{cand:residueextensionlift} supplies one finite
extension $L_0/H_0$ with residue field $l_\eta$ and
unchanged value group. Base-change the presentation once
more to $L_0^\circ$. The multiplication map
$l_\eta\otimes_{F_I}l_\eta\to l_\eta$ chooses a rational
residual point $q$ above the origin. Thus all fields,
models, and the point $q$ have been fixed independently
of $w$.}
{在全零特殊点上方，此模型具有剩余域为
$l_\eta=\kappa(\eta)$ 的点，该域为 $F_I$ 的有限可分
扩张。Lemma~\ref{cand:residueextensionlift} 给出一个
有限扩张 $L_0/H_0$，其剩余域为 $l_\eta$，值群保持不变。
把表示再基变换到 $L_0^\circ$。乘法映射
$l_\eta\otimes_{F_I}l_\eta\to l_\eta$ 选取原点上方的
有理剩余点 $q$。至此，所有域、模型及点 $q$ 都已选定，
且不依赖于 $w$。}

\bi{Lemma~\ref{cand:rationaloriginpolyannulustube}
now gives an analytic map from the entire open simplex
polyannulus over $L_0$ to $X$, by its inverse section
followed by the two base-change projections. Its Gauss
point with logarithms $w$ maps to $z_w$ on the original
standard base. Indeed, after eliminating one $X_i$ with
$i\in I$, the coefficient Gauss norm in the zero-weight
variables followed by the Gauss norm in the remaining
positive-weight variables is exactly the original Gauss
norm. This identity first holds for Laurent polynomials
and then for all functions by bounded continuity.
The specialization of the image is $\eta$, by the
chosen multiplication map of residue fields.
Proposition~\ref{cand:actualskeletalfibrestrata} therefore
identifies this image with the actual point $x_{\eta,w}$.}
{现在 Lemma~\ref{cand:rationaloriginpolyannulustube}
给出从 $L_0$ 上整个开单纯形多圆环到 $X$ 的解析映射：
先取其逆截面，再复合两次基变换投影。其对数为 $w$ 的
Gauss 点在原标准基底上映到 $z_w$。事实上，消去一个
$i\in I$ 时的 $X_i$ 后，先对零权变量取系数 Gauss 范数，
再对其余正权变量取 Gauss 范数，恰等于原 Gauss 范数。
此恒等式先对 Laurent 多项式成立，再由有界连续性推广
到全部函数。由所选剩余域乘法映射，像点的特化为 $\eta$。
于是 Proposition~\ref{cand:actualskeletalfibrestrata}
将此像识别为实际点 $x_{\eta,w}$。}

\bi{The pullback of $g$ is an analytic unit on this
single open polyannulus. Lemma~\ref{cand:openpolyannulusunits}
gives the required integral-affine formula on
$\operatorname{relint}(\Delta_I)$, with constant term
in $v(L_0^\times)=\Gamma$. The closed-cell section is
continuous by Theorem~\ref{cand:etaleskeletonfinitefaces},
and $g$ is nowhere zero. Hence both sides extend
continuously and agree on all of $\Delta_I$. The proof
also covers a zero-dimensional face.}
{把 $g$ 拉回，得到此单一开多圆环上的解析单位元。
Lemma~\ref{cand:openpolyannulusunits} 在
$\operatorname{relint}(\Delta_I)$ 上给出所需整仿射公式，
其常数项属于 $v(L_0^\times)=\Gamma$。
由 Theorem~\ref{cand:etaleskeletonfinitefaces}，闭胞腔
截面连续，且 $g$ 处处非零。因此等式两边连续延拓，并在
整个 $\Delta_I$ 上相等。此证明也包括零维面。}
\end{proof}
