Fibrewise density after complete scalar extension

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 440

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Fibrewise density after complete scalar extension}{完备扩标量后的逐纤维稠密性}]
\label{fnd:regularfibredensitybasechange}
\bi{Let $Z=\mathcal M(C)$ be geometrically regular over a complete field $F$, let $S\subset Z$ be Zariski open, and suppose $z$ belongs to the ordinary closure of $S$. For every complete extension $E/F$, every point $z'\in Z_E$ above $z$ belongs to the ordinary closure of the inverse image $S_E$.}
{设 $Z=\mathcal M(C)$ 在完备域 $F$ 上几何正则，$S\subset Z$ 为 Zariski 开集，且 $z$ 属于 $S$ 的普通闭包。则对任意完备扩域 $E/F$，$Z_E$ 中每个位于 $z$ 上方的点 $z'$ 都属于逆像 $S_E$ 的普通闭包。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use the preceding lemma to select the integral factor $C_0$ containing $z$. It is open and closed, so $S$ meets this factor. Write $S$ as the complement of the zero-locus of an ideal and choose, on $C_0$, a nonzero element $a$ of that ideal. Then $D(a)\subset S\cap\mathcal M(C_0)$. The homomorphism $C_0\to(C_0)_E$ is flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalscalarfaithful}; multiplication by $a$ therefore remains injective. Geometric regularity and the preceding lemma make $(C_0)_E$ a product of integral affinoid algebras. The element $a$ is nonzero on each factor, since it acts injectively. The generic-point density lemma shows that $D(a)$ is ordinarily dense in each factor, and hence in their union. The point $z'$ belongs to this union, proving the assertion.}
{由上一引理选择包含 $z$ 的整因子 $C_0$。该分量既开又闭，所以 $S$ 与其相交。将 $S$ 写成某理想零点集的补集，在 $C_0$ 中选择该理想的一个非零元素 $a$，便有 $D(a)\subset S\cap\mathcal M(C_0)$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalscalarfaithful}，同态 $C_0\to(C_0)_E$ 平坦，所以乘以 $a$ 仍为单射。几何正则性与上一引理说明 $(C_0)_E$ 是若干 affinoid 整环的乘积；由于 $a$ 单射作用，它在每个因子中都非零。泛点稠密性引理说明 $D(a)$ 在每个因子中普通稠密，因而在其并中稠密。点 $z'$ 属于这个并，故断言成立。}
\end{proof}
