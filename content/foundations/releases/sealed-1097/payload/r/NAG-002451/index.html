Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[The two extreme ranks of pointed tropical germs]
\label{v038:pointed:extremeranks}
Let $V,x$ be as in
Theorem~\ref{v038:pointed:independentopenness},
and let $f=(f_1,\ldots,f_n)$ be any finite unit tuple.
Put
\[
 s=\operatorname{trdeg}_{\widetilde K^{\rm gr}}
   \widetilde K^{\rm gr}
      (\widetilde{f_1(x)},\ldots,\widetilde{f_n(x)}).
\]
If $s=0$, there is an ordinary neighbourhood $U_0$
of $x$ on which $t_f$ is constant. If $s=n$, for
every ordinary neighbourhood $U$ of $x$, the germ
$(t_f(U),t_f(x))$ is the full Euclidean germ
$(\mathbf R^n,t_f(x))$.
More generally, if $s$ of the initial forms are
algebraically independent, the projection of
$t_f(U)$ to those $s$ coordinates contains an
ordinary Euclidean neighbourhood of its base point.
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
When $s=0$, each initial form is algebraic over
$\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$, so apply
Lemma~\ref{v038:pointed:rigidity} to the finite tuple.
When $s=n$ the tuple itself satisfies
Theorem~\ref{v038:pointed:independentopenness}.
The projection assertion follows by applying the same
theorem to the indicated subtuple.
\end{proof}
