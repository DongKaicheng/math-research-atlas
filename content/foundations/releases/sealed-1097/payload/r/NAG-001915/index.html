Torsion-free finite modules are finitely presented

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 518

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Torsion-free finite modules are finitely presented}
{无挠有限模具有有限表示}]
\label{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation}
\bi{Let $A_0$ be a topologically finite type $O$-algebra and let
$M$ be a finite $A_0$-module with no $O$-torsion. Then $M$ is finitely
presented over $A_0$. In particular, a topologically finite type
$O$-algebra with no $\pi$-torsion is topologically finitely presented.}
{设 $A_0$ 为拓扑有限型 $O$-代数，$M$ 为无 $O$-挠的有限
$A_0$-模，则 $M$ 在 $A_0$ 上具有有限表示。特别地，无
$\pi$-挠的拓扑有限型 $O$-代数自动为拓扑有限表示代数。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $P=O\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle$ and choose a
surjection $P\twoheadrightarrow A_0$. Since $M$ is finite over $P$,
take a surjection $P^s\twoheadrightarrow M$ with kernel $N$.
Inside $K\langle T\rangle^s=P^s[1/\pi]$, torsion-freeness gives}
{令 $P=O\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle$，并选取满射
$P\twoheadrightarrow A_0$。模 $M$ 在 $P$ 上有限，故可选取
满射 $P^s\twoheadrightarrow M$，记核为 $N$。
在 $K\langle T\rangle^s=P^s[1/\pi]$ 中，无挠性给出}
\[
 N=N[1/\pi]\cap P^s.
\]
\bi{The equality $P[1/\pi]=K\langle T\rangle$ follows by bounding
the coefficients of a restricted series. Apply
Theorem~\ref{fnd:exactunitballgenerators} to $N[1/\pi]$. Its norm-one
generators lie in $N$, and its coefficient bound shows that they
generate $N$ over $P$. Thus $M$ is finitely presented over $P$.
Their images also generate the kernel of $A_0^s\twoheadrightarrow M$,
so $M$ is finitely presented over $A_0$. Finally, in a real-valued
rank-one field, $\pi$-torsion-freeness implies $O$-torsion-freeness:
for $0\ne a\in O$ choose $m$ with $|\pi|^m\leq|a|$, so that
$\pi^m\in aO$. Apply the result to $M=A_0$ as a quotient of $P$;
its defining ideal is then finitely generated.}
{等式 $P[1/\pi]=K\langle T\rangle$ 来自受限级数系数的有界性。
对 $N[1/\pi]$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:exactunitballgenerators}。
其中范数为一的生成元属于 $N$，而系数界说明它们在 $P$ 上生成 $N$。
所以 $M$ 在 $P$ 上具有有限表示；这些生成元的像也生成
$A_0^s\twoheadrightarrow M$ 的核，故 $M$ 在 $A_0$ 上具有有限表示。
最后，在实值秩一域上，无 $\pi$-挠蕴含无 $O$-挠：对
$0\ne a\in O$ 选择 $m$ 使 $|\pi|^m\leq|a|$，便有
$\pi^m\in aO$。将结论用于作为 $P$ 的商的 $M=A_0$，即知其
定义理想有限生成。}
\end{proof}
