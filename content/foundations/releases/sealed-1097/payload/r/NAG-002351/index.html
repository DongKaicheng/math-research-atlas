A finite analytic image around a whole moment fibre

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 972

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[A finite analytic image around a whole moment fibre]
\label{v037:analytic:wholemomentcollar}
Let $X=\mathcal M(B)$ be a generalized affinoid space over a
complete field $K$ with its specified absolute value, let
$f_1,\ldots,f_n\in B^\times$, and put $t=t_f$.
If $w\in t(X)$ and
$F_w=t^{-1}(w)\subset\operatorname{Int}(X/K)$, there is a
closed box $P$ about $w$, with $w$ in its interior, such that
$Y=t^{-1}(P)$ lies in $\operatorname{Int}(X/K)$ and its moment
map to the closed polyannulus $\mathbb T_P$ is finite.
For $A_P=\mathcal O(\mathbb T_P)$, $D_P=\mathcal O(Y)$, and
$I_P=\ker(A_P\to D_P)$, one has
\[
       t(X)\cap P
          =t\bigl(\mathcal M(A_P/I_P)\bigr).
\]
Every point of the right-hand analytic space has an actual lift
to $Y$. If $X$ is pure of analytic dimension $d$, the closed
analytic image $\mathcal M(A_P/I_P)$ is pure of dimension $d$.
For every $v\in P$, restriction to the exact-radius polytorus
at $v$ gives the actual algebra of the whole fibre $F_v$.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Apply the first assertion of Theorem~\ref{v036:analytic:finitecollar}
to the complete tuple of $n$ moment functions. That assertion
places no equality between $n$ and the dimension of $X$.
It gives the stated domain, finite map, canonical module norm,
and the exact-level identifications.
Lemma~\ref{v037:analytic:finiteimage} gives the actual image and
the pointwise lifting assertion. In the pure case,
Theorem~\ref{v036:analytic:domainpurity} makes $Y$ pure of dimension
$d$, and the last assertion of the same lemma gives the purity
of its finite image.
\end{proof}
