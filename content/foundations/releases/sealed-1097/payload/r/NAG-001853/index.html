Dimension lowering by a nonzero ordinary ideal

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 460

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Dimension lowering by a nonzero ordinary ideal}{非零普通理想使纤维维数下降}]
\label{fnd:charzeroclosedfiberdimensiondrop}
\bi{Let $K$ have characteristic zero, let $X$ be good, and let $V$ be an ordinary affinoid neighborhood of a point $t$ in $\mathbb A_X^{d,\mathrm{an}}$. Put $x=f(t)$, assume that $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is a field and that $F=\mathcal H(x)$ is nontrivially valued, and let $0\ne I\subsetneq\mathcal O_{V,t}$ be an ideal. The closed-subspace germ defined by $I$ has an ordinary affinoid representative $Z$ whose fibre $Z_x$ is strict and has dimension at most $d-1$. The maps $Z\to X$ are inner at $t$, and their ordinary local rings are}
{设 $K$ 为特征零域，$X$ 为 good 空间，$V$ 为 $\mathbb A_X^{d,\mathrm{an}}$ 中点 $t$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域。令 $x=f(t)$，假设 $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 为域且 $F=\mathcal H(x)$ 的赋值非平凡，并给定理想 $0\ne I\subsetneq\mathcal O_{V,t}$。由 $I$ 定义的闭子空间芽有一个普通 affinoid 代表 $Z$，其纤维 $Z_x$ strict 且维数至多为 $d-1$。态射 $Z\to X$ 在 $t$ 处为内态射，且普通局部环为}
\[
 \mathcal O_{Z,t}=\mathcal O_{V,t}/I,
 \qquad
 \mathcal O_{Z_x,t}=\mathcal O_{V_x,t}/I\mathcal O_{V_x,t}.
\]
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The ordinary local ring is Noetherian, so finitely many generators of $I$ extend to functions on an ordinary affinoid neighborhood of $t$. Choose a nonzero germ $h\in I$ and represent it there as well. Its image in the fibre germ is nonzero. Indeed, otherwise its zero set would contain an ordinary fibre neighborhood; Proposition~\ref{fnd:charzerogenericfibreneighborhood}, applied to the smooth map from the ordinary interior of $V$ to $X$, would make $h$ zero on an ordinary neighborhood of $t$, contrary to its choice.}
{普通局部环为 Noetherian，因此 $I$ 的有限个生成元可延拓为 $t$ 的某个普通 affinoid 邻域上的函数。选择非零芽 $h\in I$，也将其在该邻域上表示。它在纤维芽中的像非零。否则其零点集将包含一个普通纤维邻域；将 Proposition~\ref{fnd:charzerogenericfibreneighborhood} 应用于从 $V$ 的普通内部到 $X$ 的光滑态射，便知 $h$ 在 $t$ 的普通邻域上为零，与选择矛盾。}

\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:innerstrictfibrerefinement} now gives an ordinary affinoid neighborhood with strict fibre algebra $C$. This fibre is an affinoid domain of $\mathbb A_F^{d,\mathrm{an}}$. Its ordinary local rings are regular by Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomainregular}. Lemma~\ref{fnd:regularaffinoidcomponents} therefore writes $C$ as a finite product of regular domains. Use Lemma~\ref{fnd:originalfiberidempotentcut} to retain the factor $C_0$ containing $t$, over the original field and without changing the ordinary germs. The image of $h$ in $C_0$ is nonzero, since its fibre germ was nonzero. Lemma~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomaindimensionbound} gives $\dim C_0\leq d$.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:innerstrictfibrerefinement}，可取得普通 affinoid 邻域，使其纤维代数 $C$ strict。该纤维是 $\mathbb A_F^{d,\mathrm{an}}$ 的 affinoid 域；由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomainregular}，其普通局部环正则。因此 Lemma~\ref{fnd:regularaffinoidcomponents} 将 $C$ 写成有限个正则整环的乘积。应用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:originalfiberidempotentcut}，在原基域上保留含 $t$ 的因子 $C_0$，且不改变普通芽。由于 $h$ 的纤维芽非零，它在 $C_0$ 中的像也非零。Lemma~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomaindimensionbound} 给出 $\dim C_0\leq d$。}

\bi{Cut out $Z$ by the chosen generators of $I$. Its fibre algebra is the strict quotient $C_0/I_C$, where $I_C$ contains the nonzero image of $h$. Every chain of primes containing $I_C$ can be extended strictly at its lower end by the zero prime of the domain $C_0$. Hence $\dim(C_0/I_C)\leq d-1$. Coherent closed-subspace restriction gives the two displayed local-ring identifications. A closed immersion is finite and inner; composition with the inner map from the chosen ordinary neighborhood to $X$ proves the final assertion.}
{用所选 $I$ 的生成元定义 $Z$。其纤维代数是 strict 商 $C_0/I_C$，其中 $I_C$ 包含 $h$ 的非零像。每一条包含 $I_C$ 的素理想链，都可在下端严格添入整环 $C_0$ 的零素理想，故 $\dim(C_0/I_C)\leq d-1$。凝聚闭子空间的限制给出所列两个局部环识别。闭浸入有限，因而为内态射；与所选普通邻域到 $X$ 的内态射复合，便得到最后的结论。}
\end{proof}
