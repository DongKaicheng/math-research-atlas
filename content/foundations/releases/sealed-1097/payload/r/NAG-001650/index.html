Algebraic differentials under analytification

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 400

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Algebraic differentials under analytification}{解析化下的代数微分}]
\label{fnd:algebraicdifferentialanalytification}
\bi{Let $Y\to X$ be a morphism of finite-type $K$-schemes. The pullback of the algebraic differential sheaf to $Y^{\mathrm{an}}$ is canonically isomorphic to $\Omega_{Y^{\mathrm{an}}/X^{\mathrm{an}}}$, compatibly with universal derivations. More concretely, if $S=R[T_1,\ldots,T_n]/(f_1,\ldots,f_m)$, $V=\mathcal M(A)\subseteq(\operatorname{Spec}R)^{\mathrm{an}}$, and $D=A\{r^{-1}T\}/(f_1,\ldots,f_m)$ is a relative radius piece, then}
{设 $Y\to X$ 为有限型 $K$-概形态射。代数微分层在 $Y^{\mathrm{an}}$ 上的拉回与 $\Omega_{Y^{\mathrm{an}}/X^{\mathrm{an}}}$ 典范同构，且与泛导子相容。更具体地，若 $S=R[T_1,\ldots,T_n]/(f_1,\ldots,f_m)$，$V=\mathcal M(A)\subseteq(\operatorname{Spec}R)^{\mathrm{an}}$，且 $D=A\{r^{-1}T\}/(f_1,\ldots,f_m)$ 为相对半径分片，则}
\[
 D\otimes_S\Omega_{S/R}\simeq\Omega^{\mathrm{an}}_{D/A}.
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For polynomial rings the Leibniz rule shows directly that an $R$-derivation is determined arbitrarily by its values on the $T_j$. It descends to $S$ precisely when it kills the $f_i$. Thus $\Omega_{S/R}$ is the cokernel of the polynomial Jacobian matrix $(\partial f_i/\partial T_j)$. Tensoring this finite presentation with $D$ gives exactly the presentation of Theorem~\ref{fnd:boundeduniversaldifferentials}, proving the formula. Both maps send the differential of a polynomial to the differential of its analytic image. Uniqueness makes them compatible with algebraic localization, changes of generators and analytic domain restriction. The relative radius pieces cover the inverse images of affine scheme charts by Proposition~\ref{fnd:algebraicmorphismboundaryless}; hence these isomorphisms glue to the asserted sheaf isomorphism.}
{对多项式环，Leibniz 法则直接说明一个 $R$-导子由各 $T_j$ 上任意指定的值唯一确定。它下降到 $S$ 当且仅当消灭各 $f_i$。因此 $\Omega_{S/R}$ 是多项式 Jacobian 矩阵 $(\partial f_i/\partial T_j)$ 的余核。将此有限表示与 $D$ 张量，得到的恰好是 Theorem~\ref{fnd:boundeduniversaldifferentials} 的表示，从而证明公式。两边的映射都将多项式的微分送到其解析像的微分。唯一性保证它们与代数局部化、改变生成元及解析域限制相容。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:algebraicmorphismboundaryless} 的相对半径分片构造，这些分片覆盖仿射概形 chart 的逆像，所以所得同构粘合为所求层同构。}
\end{proof}
