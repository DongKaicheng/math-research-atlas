Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[Ordinary local dimension bound; F8RC-D]
\label{lem:defect}
Let \(X\) be good, Hausdorff, and pure of analytic dimension \(n\). If
\(d_K(x)\geq n-1\), then
\(\dim\cO_{X,x}\leq1\).
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The ordinary local ring \(R=\cO_{X,x}\) is Noetherian. If its dimension
were at least two, choose primes
\(P_0\subsetneq P_1\subsetneq\mathfrak m_R\), and choose
\(a\in P_1\setminus P_0\) and \(b\in\mathfrak m_R\setminus P_1\).
Represent both germs on one ordinary affinoid neighbourhood
\(U=\cM(C)\) of \(x\), using \tagg{NAG-001698}. Contract the three
primes to \(q_0\subsetneq q_1\subsetneq q_2\) of \(C\); strictness
is witnessed by \(a,b\), and \(q_2=\ker(C\to\cH(x))\).
Choose a minimal prime \(p\subset q_0\). The affinoid \(C/p\) is
integral and pure of dimension \(n\), by purity of \(U\).
Twice applying the dimension-drop theorem \tagg{NAG-002374}, first
to \(C/p\to C/q_1\) and then to the integral algebra
\(C/q_1\to C/q_2\), gives
\(\dim_K\cM(C/q_2)\leq n-2\). The intermediate integral affinoid
is pure of its own analytic dimension, so the second application has
its stated hypothesis. The point \(x\) has the same completed residue
field on this quotient. Its Abhyankar dimension is therefore at most
\(n-2\), a contradiction. This also covers \(n=1\), where a second
nonempty strict dimension drop is impossible.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
Fix a complete non-Archimedean field \(K\) with its specified absolute
value. Arbitrary positive real radii, trivial valuation, and positive
characteristic are allowed. Dimensions of analytic spaces are analytic
dimensions. Ordinary analytic local rings are denoted by \(\cO_{X,x}\).
Put
\[
 d_K(x)=\operatorname{trdeg}_{\widetilde K}\widetilde{\cH(x)}
 +\dim_{\mathbb Q}\bigl((|\cH(x)^\times|/|K^\times|)
                         \otimes_{\ZZ}\mathbb Q\bigr).
\]
