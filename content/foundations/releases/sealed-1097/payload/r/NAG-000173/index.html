Result 2.6.4

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 131

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}\label{fnd:finitefibrelength}
\bi{Suppose a finite affinoid morphism is given by a finite locally free $A$-algebra $B$ of constant rank $d$. For $x\in\mathcal M(A)$ put $F=\mathcal H(x)$ and $E=B\otimes_A F$. Then $E$ is an Artinian $F$-algebra of dimension $d$. Writing it as the product of its local factors $E_j$ with residue fields $F_j$,}
{设 finite affinoid morphism 由 constant rank $d$ 的 finite locally free $A$-algebra $B$ 给出。对 $x\in\mathcal M(A)$，令 $F=\mathcal H(x)$、$E=B\otimes_A F$。则 $E$ 是 dimension $d$ 的 Artinian $F$-algebra。将其写成 local factors $E_j$ 的乘积，并记 residue fields 为 $F_j$，则}
\[
 d=\sum_j\length_{E_j}(E_j)[F_j:F].
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Finite projectivity commutes with scalar extension, giving $\dim_FE=d$; finite-dimensional algebras are complete by Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}. The Artinian product decomposition and finite length follow from \cite[Tag 00JB]{StacksAlg}. A composition series of $E_j$ as an $E_j$-module has $\length_{E_j}(E_j)$ factors, each isomorphic to $F_j$. Taking $F$-dimensions gives the formula.}
{有限投射性与标量扩张相容，故 $\dim_FE=d$；Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 保证有限维代数完备。Artinian 乘积分解及有限长度来自 \cite[Tag 00JB]{StacksAlg}。将 $E_j$ 视为 $E_j$-模的组成列有 $\length_{E_j}(E_j)$ 个因子，每个都同构于 $F_j$。取 $F$-维数即得公式。}
\end{proof}
