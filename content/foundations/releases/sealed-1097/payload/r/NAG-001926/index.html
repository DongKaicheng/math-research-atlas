Finite canonical reduction over a normalization

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 524

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Finite canonical reduction over a normalization}
{相对于正规化的典范约化有限性}]
\label{fnd:normalizationcanonicalfinite}
\bi{Let $B$ be a strict affinoid domain with a bounded finite
injection $R=K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_d\rangle\hookrightarrow B$.
Then the induced map
$\widetilde K[T_1,\ldots,T_d]=\widetilde R\to\widetilde B$
is finite.}
{设 strict affinoid 整环 $B$ 带有有界有限单射
$R=K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_d\rangle\hookrightarrow B$，则诱导映射
$\widetilde K[T_1,\ldots,T_d]=\widetilde R\to\widetilde B$
是有限映射。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use the finite extensions $L_1,\ldots,L_s$ of the Gauss
field $H$ constructed in Proposition~\ref{fnd:normalizationfiniteboundary}.
They give an injective map}
{使用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:normalizationfiniteboundary} 构造的
Gauss 域 $H$ 的有限扩张 $L_1,\ldots,L_s$，得到单射}
\[
 \widetilde B\longrightarrow\prod_{i=1}^s\widetilde L_i.
\]
\bi{Indeed, a power-bounded element maps to zero in every residue
field exactly when all its finitely many boundary values are less
than one, which is exactly $\rho_B(b)<1$. Put
$k=\widetilde K$ and $S=k[T_1,\ldots,T_d]$.
For $b\in B^\circ$, its minimal polynomial over
$\operatorname{Frac}(R)$ has coefficients in $R$, as in
Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictminimalpolynomialbounds}. The root calculation
in Proposition~\ref{fnd:normalizationfiniteboundary} gives
$\|c_j\|^{1/j}\leq\rho_B(b)\leq1$ for each coefficient $c_j$.
Reducing that monic equation shows that $\widetilde b$ is integral
over $S$.}
{事实上，幂有界元素在每个剩余域中的像均为零，当且仅当其有限个
边界取值都小于一，这恰好等价于 $\rho_B(b)<1$。
记 $k=\widetilde K$，$S=k[T_1,\ldots,T_d]$。
对 $b\in B^\circ$，其在 $\operatorname{Frac}(R)$ 上的最小多项式
的系数属于 $R$，见 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictminimalpolynomialbounds}。
Proposition~\ref{fnd:normalizationfiniteboundary} 中的根计算给出
每个系数 $c_j$ 的界 $\|c_j\|^{1/j}\leq\rho_B(b)\leq1$。
约化这个首一方程，便知 $\widetilde b$ 在 $S$ 上整。}

\bi{By Lemma~\ref{fnd:gaussfibreresiduefields}, each
$\widetilde L_i$ is a finite extension of $\operatorname{Frac}(S)$.
The polynomial ring $S$ is Nagata by
\cite[Tag 0335, Proposition 10.162.16]{StacksAlg}; hence its
integral closure $S_i$ in $\widetilde L_i$ is finite over $S$.
The preceding injection is $S$-linear and every coordinate is
integral over $S$. Its image is therefore an $S$-submodule of
the finite $S$-module $\prod_{i=1}^s S_i$. The Nagata property
already gives Noetherianity of $S$ by definition
\cite[Tag 032R(2)]{StacksAlg}. The submodule assertion of
\cite[Tag 00IK, Lemma 10.51.1]{StacksAlg} now makes
$\widetilde B$ finite over $S$.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gaussfibreresiduefields}，每个
$\widetilde L_i$ 都是 $\operatorname{Frac}(S)$ 的有限扩张。
由 \cite[Tag 0335, Proposition 10.162.16]{StacksAlg}，
多项式环 $S$ 为 Nagata 环，故其在 $\widetilde L_i$ 中的整闭包
$S_i$ 在 $S$ 上有限。前述单射将 $\widetilde B$ 放入有限
$S$-模 $\prod_iS_i$ 中。由于 $S$ 为 Noetherian 环，其子模
$\widetilde B$ 也有限。}
\end{proof}
