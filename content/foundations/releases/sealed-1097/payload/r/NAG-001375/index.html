\begin{corollary}[\bi{Preparation and the quotient norm}{Preparation 与 quotient norm}]\label{fnd:banachpreparation}
\bi{In Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachweierstrassdivision}, take $M=A$ and suppose that $h$ has only terms of degree greater than $d$. There is a factorization}
{在 Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachweierstrassdivision} 中取 $M=A$，并假设 $h$ 只含 degree 大于 $d$ 的 terms。则存在 factorization}
\[
 f=uW,\qquad
 W=T^d+\sum_{i<d}w_iT^i,\qquad
 \|w_i\|r^i\leq r^d,
 \qquad \|u-1\|_r\leq\theta<1.
\]
\bi{The element $u$ is a unit. The monic degree-$d$ polynomial $W$ is unique among such factorizations by analytic units. The quotient $E_r(A)/(f)$, with its quotient norm, is isometric as an $A$-module to $P_{<d}(A)$; it is finite free with basis $1,T,\ldots,T^{d-1}$.}
{$u$ 为 unit。在通过 analytic unit 作出的这类 factorizations 中，monic degree-$d$ polynomial $W$ 唯一。配备 quotient norm 的 $E_r(A)/(f)$ 作为 $A$-module 与 $P_{<d}(A)$ isometric，因而为 finite free，其 basis 为 $1,T,\ldots,T^{d-1}$。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Divide $T^d$ by $f$, obtaining $T^d=qf+s$. Since $Q_P(T^d)=1$, the construction of the preceding theorem gives $q=S(1)$ and $\|q-1\|_r\leq\theta$. The geometric series $\sum_{n\geq0}(1-q)^n$ converges and is an inverse of $q$, as is checked by multiplying finite sums and passing to the limit. Its difference from one has norm at most $\theta$. Set $u=q^{-1}$ and $W=T^d-s$. Then $f=uW$, and $\|s\|_r\leq r^d$ gives the lower-coefficient bounds.}
{将 $T^d$ 除以 $f$，得到 $T^d=qf+s$。由于 $Q_P(T^d)=1$，前一个 theorem 的构造给出 $q=S(1)$ 和 $\|q-1\|_r\leq\theta$。Geometric series $\sum_{n\geq0}(1-q)^n$ 收敛；对 finite sums 作乘法后取 limit，可知它是 $q$ 的 inverse，且与一的差的 norm 不超过 $\theta$。令 $u=q^{-1}$、$W=T^d-s$，则 $f=uW$；由 $\|s\|_r\leq r^d$ 得到 lower-coefficient bounds。}

\bi{If also $f=vW'$ with $v$ a unit and $W'$ monic of degree $d$, then $W'=v^{-1}uW$. Apply Lemma~\ref{fnd:banachpolynomialdivision} to $W$. Polynomial division of $W'$ by $W$ has quotient one and remainder $W'-W$, whereas the displayed analytic representation has quotient $v^{-1}u$ and remainder zero. Uniqueness gives $W'=W$ and $v=u$. The quotient and its norm were computed in Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachweierstrassdivision}; its unique polynomial representatives give both spanning and independence of the stated basis.}
{若另有 $f=vW'$，其中 $v$ 为 unit，$W'$ 为 monic degree-$d$ polynomial，则 $W'=v^{-1}uW$。对 $W$ 应用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:banachpolynomialdivision}。Polynomial division 将 $W'$ 除以 $W$ 时，quotient 为一，remainder 为 $W'-W$；而上述 analytic representation 的 quotient 为 $v^{-1}u$，remainder 为零。由 uniqueness 得 $W'=W$ 和 $v=u$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachweierstrassdivision} 已经确定 quotient 及其 norm；唯一的 polynomial representatives 同时证明所述 basis 的 spanning 与 independence。}
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}\label{fnd:banachalgebra}
\bi{A Banach $K$-algebra is a commutative unital $K$-algebra $A$ with a complete non-Archimedean norm satisfying $\|ca\|=|c|\|a\|$, $\|1\|=1$, and $\|ab\|\leq\|a\|\|b\|$. A homomorphism is bounded if $\|\phi(a)\|\leq C\|a\|$ for some constant $C$. Equivalent norms define the same Banach topology. A surjective bounded map is admissible if its target norm is uniformly equivalent to the quotient norm; equivalently, it admits lifts with a uniform norm bound. Equality of topologies alone is insufficient at trivial valuation; see Definition~\ref{fnd:normstrictconvention} and Example~\ref{fnd:normtopologycounterexample}.}
{Banach $K$-algebra 是 commutative unital $K$-algebra $A$，配备一个 complete norm。此 norm 为 non-Archimedean，且满足 $\|ca\|=|c|\|a\|$、$\|1\|=1$ 和 $\|ab\|\leq\|a\|\|b\|$。若存在常数 $C$ 使 $\|\phi(a)\|\leq C\|a\|$，则 homomorphism 称为 bounded。Equivalent norms 定义相同的 Banach topology。Surjective bounded map 若使 target norm 与 quotient norm uniformly equivalent，则称为 admissible；等价地，它具有 uniform norm bound 的 lifts。赋值平凡时，仅有 topologies 相同还不够；见 Definition~\ref{fnd:normstrictconvention} 及 Example~\ref{fnd:normtopologycounterexample}。}
\end{definition}

\chapter{\bi{Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points}{Valued fields、Banach algebras 与 analytic points}}\label{ch:1}\label{vol:I}\label{part:tropical}\label{fnd:algebras}
\bi{A non-Archimedean analytic space is locally described by convergent functions, but its points need not be evaluations at elements of the ground field. The purpose of this chapter is to construct both parts of this description: the algebras of functions and the valued fields in which their values lie. The next chapter supplies domains and sheaves. This order is important for multiplicities: a seminorm detects the value of a function, whereas the algebra also retains nilpotents and finite-module lengths.}
{Non-Archimedean analytic space 局部由 convergent functions 描述，但其 points 未必来自 ground field 中元素的 evaluation。本章构造这一描述的两部分：functions 的 algebras，以及函数值所在的 valued fields。下一章再建立 domains 与 sheaves。这个顺序对 multiplicities 很重要：seminorm 检测函数值，而 algebra 还保留 nilpotents 与 finite-module lengths。}

\section{\bi{The ground field and convergence}{Ground field 与 convergence}}\label{fnd:valuedfields}
\begin{definition}\label{fnd:fieldconventions}
\bi{Throughout the foundations, $K$ is a field complete for a real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value. We permit the trivial absolute value unless a statement explicitly excludes it. Set}
{基础篇中，$K$ 是关于 real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value 完备的 field。除非明确排除，否则允许 trivial absolute value。记}
\[
\begin{gathered}
v(a)=-\log|a|,\qquad \Gamma=v(K^\times),\\
K^\circ=\{a\in K:|a|\leq1\},\qquad K^{\circ\circ}=\{a\in K:|a|<1\},\\
\widetilde K=K^\circ/K^{\circ\circ}.
\end{gathered}
\]
\bi{A complete extension $L/K$ always carries an absolute value restricting to the specified one on $K$. We do not rescale it when changing the ground field.}

{Theorem~\ref{fnd:weierstrassdivision} 的证明可推广到一般 Banach coefficients 和任意 positive radius。这一推广给出按 Tate variables 数目归纳时需要的 division 步骤，并显式确定 quotient norm；因此证明不调用 open-mapping theorem 或 finite-module completeness theorem。下面保留 classical Weierstrass division 论证中的全部 norm estimates；这一步尚不构成一般 Tate acyclicity 的证明。}

\bi{Throughout this section, $A$ is a commutative unital Banach $K$-algebra as in Definition~\ref{fnd:banachalgebra}. A Banach $A$-module $M$ means an $A$-module with a complete, definite, non-Archimedean norm, homogeneous over $K$, such that $\|am\|\leq\|a\|\|m\|$. No algebraic finiteness is assumed. Fix $r>0$ and put}
{本节中，$A$ 为 Definition~\ref{fnd:banachalgebra} 中的 commutative unital Banach $K$-algebra。Banach $A$-module $M$ 指具有 complete、definite、non-Archimedean norm 的 $A$-module；该 norm 在 $K$ 上 homogeneous，且满足 $\|am\|\leq\|a\|\|m\|$。不要求 $M$ algebraically finite。固定 $r>0$，记}
\[
\begin{split}
 E_r(M)&=\left\{\sum_{j\geq0}m_jT^j:\|m_j\|r^j\longrightarrow0\right\},\\
 \left\|\sum_{j\geq0}m_jT^j\right\|_r&=\sup_j\|m_j\|r^j.
\end{split}
\]
\bi{For a nonzero series the supremum is attained. Write $P_{<d}(M)$ for the polynomials of degree less than $d$, with the same norm; $P_{<0}(M)=0$. The field may be trivially valued, and $r$ need not belong to $|K^\times|$.}
{对 nonzero series，上述 supremum 可达到。以 $P_{<d}(M)$ 记 degree 小于 $d$ 的 polynomials，并使用同一 norm；约定 $P_{<0}(M)=0$。允许 $K$ trivially valued，也不要求 $r\in|K^\times|$。}
