A rational-origin tube over a semistable chart

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 910

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A rational-origin tube over a semistable chart}
{半稳定坐标片中有理原点的管域}]
\label{cand:rationaloriginpolyannulustube}
\bi{Let $L$ be complete and nontrivially valued, and let
$0<|b|<1$. Put}
{设 $L$ 完备且非平凡赋值，并取 $0<|b|<1$。置}
\[
 D_0=L^\circ\langle Z_0,\ldots,Z_d\rangle/
                         (Z_0\cdots Z_d-b).
\]
\bi{Let $D_0\to E_0$ be a topologically finitely presented
formally \etale map of admissible algebras, and let $q$
be a rational point of its special fibre lying over
$Z_0=\cdots=Z_d=0$. The generic map induces an analytic
isomorphism}
{设 $D_0\to E_0$ 为 admissible 代数之间拓扑有限表示的
形式 \etale 映射，并设 $q$ 为其特殊纤维中位于
$Z_0=\cdots=Z_d=0$ 上方的有理点。泛纤维映射诱导
解析同构}
\[
 ]q[\ \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
 P_b:=\{z\in\mathcal M(D):|Z_i(z)|<1\text{ for every }i\}.
\]
\bi{After eliminating $Z_0$, this is the open polyannulus
with logarithmic domain
$w_j>0$ and $\sum_{j=1}^d w_j<v(b)$.}
{消去 $Z_0$ 后，这是对数定义域为
$w_j>0$、$\sum_{j=1}^d w_j<v(b)$ 的开多圆环。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose the bounded presentation of
Lemma~\ref{cand:formaletaleboundedpresentation}, with
coordinates $Y,Q$, equations $F(Y)=0$ and
$Q\det F'(Y)=1$. Lift the residue coordinates of $q$
to constants $y_0,q_0$ in $L^\circ$.
Choose integral restricted-series lifts of all the
coefficients in the variables $Z_0,\ldots,Z_d$.
Their evaluations at $Z=0,Y=y_0$ have $F$-error of
norm less than one, and the Jacobian determinant has
norm one. These assertions follow by reduction at the
specified rational point $q$.}
{取 Lemma~\ref{cand:formaletaleboundedpresentation} 的有界
表示，坐标为 $Y,Q$，方程为 $F(Y)=0$ 及
$Q\det F'(Y)=1$。把 $q$ 的剩余坐标提升为
$L^\circ$ 中的常数 $y_0,q_0$。对全部系数选取关于
$Z_0,\ldots,Z_d$ 的整受限级数提升。在 $Z=0,Y=y_0$
处，$F$ 的误差范数小于一，而 Jacobian 行列式的范数为一。
这些断言由在指定有理点 $q$ 处约化得到。}

\bi{Let $W$ be a compact logarithmic box inside the
displayed open simplex, with affinoid inverse image $P_W$.
All $|Z_i|$ are at most some $\rho<1$ there.
Every nonconstant monomial in their integral series has
norm at most $\rho$. Thus, in the Banach algebra
$\mathcal O(P_W)$, the error $F(y_0)$ has norm at most
some $\delta<1$, and $F'(y_0)$ has inverse of norm at
most one. Indeed its determinant differs by a term of
norm less than one from its constant unit, so the
geometric inverse applies. Lemma~\ref{cand:banachcenterhensel}
produces the unique root $y_W$ with $|y_W-y_0|\leq\delta$;
set $q_W=(\det F'(y_W))^{-1}$. This gives a bounded
analytic section $P_W\to\mathcal M(E)$, specializing to
$q$. Uniqueness makes the sections agree on overlaps,
so they glue on all of $P_b$. When $d=0$ use the same
argument in $L$, where $Z_0=b$.}
{设 $W$ 为所列开单纯形内的紧对数长方体，其 affinoid
逆像为 $P_W$。在此处，全部 $|Z_i|$ 均不超过某个
$\rho<1$。其整级数中的每个非常数单项式的范数至多为
$\rho$。所以在 Banach 代数 $\mathcal O(P_W)$ 中，
$F(y_0)$ 的范数不超过某个 $\delta<1$，且
$F'(y_0)$ 的逆范数至多为一：行列式与其常数单位项之差
的范数小于一，可用几何求逆。
Lemma~\ref{cand:banachcenterhensel} 给出满足
$|y_W-y_0|\leq\delta$ 的唯一根 $y_W$；令
$q_W=(\det F'(y_W))^{-1}$。这给出特化到 $q$ 的有界
解析截面 $P_W\to\mathcal M(E)$。由唯一性，各截面在
交叠上一致，故粘合到整个 $P_b$。当 $d=0$ 时，
在 $L$ 中使用同一论证，此时 $Z_0=b$。}

\bi{We check the inverse as an analytic map. Over $P_W$,
cover the tube by affinoid subdomains on which
$|Y-y_0|\leq\lambda<1$, increasing $\lambda$ if necessary
so that $\lambda\geq\delta$; one may impose the analogous
bound on $Q-q_0$. The original coordinate tuple and the
pullback of $y_W$ are roots of the same system in the
Banach algebra of such a domain. Both lie within a
radius less than one of $y_0$, where the same Jacobian
inverse estimate holds. Hensel uniqueness makes them
equal as algebra elements, and then the inverse-determinant
equation identifies $Q$. Consequently the section and
the generic map are inverse on these subdomains.
Every point of the tube lies in one of them, since its
residue coordinates are those of $q$. This proves the
analytic isomorphism, including its compatibility for
every compact box in the open simplex.}
{下面在解析映射层面核对逆映射。在 $P_W$ 上，以满足
$|Y-y_0|\leq\lambda<1$ 的 affinoid 子域覆盖管域，
必要时增大 $\lambda$，使 $\lambda\geq\delta$；还可
对 $Q-q_0$ 施加同类界。在这样的子域的 Banach 代数中，
原坐标元组及 $y_W$ 的拉回都是同一方程组的根。
二者距 $y_0$ 都小于一，且同样的 Jacobian 逆估计成立。
Hensel 唯一性使它们作为代数元素相等，随后逆行列式方程
识别 $Q$。因此截面与泛纤维映射在这些子域上互逆。
管域的每一点都属于某个这样的子域，因为其剩余坐标为
$q$ 的坐标。这证明解析同构，以及它在开单纯形全部紧
长方体上的相容性。}
\end{proof}
