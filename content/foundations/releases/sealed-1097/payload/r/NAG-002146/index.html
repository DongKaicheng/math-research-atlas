Equivariant finite marked refinement

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 725

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Equivariant finite marked refinement}
{等变的有限标记细化}]
\label{cand:equivariantmarkedrefinement}
\bi{Let $\mathcal X/O$ be a proper split nodal model, and
let $P$ be a finite set of distinct $K$-rational points of
its generic fibre. A finite sequence of algebraic blowups
with open completed centres produces a split nodal model
$\mathcal X'$ in which the marked sections have pairwise
distinct smooth specializations. Every old component point
is retained. If $\mathcal X$ is projective, so is
$\mathcal X'$. If a finite group $G$ acts semilinearly
and isometrically on $\mathcal X$ and preserves $P$, the
sequence can be chosen $G$-equivariant, with the given
generic action unchanged.}
{设 $\mathcal X/O$ 为 proper 分裂结点模型，
$P$ 为其泛纤维上有限个互异的 $K$-有理点。
存在有限次代数 blowup，中心完备化后均为开理想，
得到分裂结点模型 $\mathcal X'$，
其中各标记截面具有两两互异的光滑专化。
全部旧分量点保留。若 $\mathcal X$ 射影，
则 $\mathcal X'$ 也射影。
若有限群 $G$ 在 $\mathcal X$ 上半线性等距地作用
并保持 $P$，则可选择整个序列等变，
且给定泛纤维作用不变。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First treat all nodes containing marked specializations.
For each such node take the intrinsic ideal of
Lemma~\ref{cand:nodalmarkcluster}, and blow up the product
of these finitely many ideals. In a neighborhood of one
centre that omits the other centres, every other ideal is
the unit ideal. Thus this simultaneous blowup has exactly
the local charts already proved there. This argument uses
a finite product of finite-type ideals, not an intersection.
The number of marked sections meeting nodes strictly
decreases: at each treated node at least one becomes
smooth, while every previously smooth section is untouched.
After at most $|P|$ rounds all marked sections are smooth.}
{先处理含有标记专化的全部结点。
对每个此类结点取
Lemma~\ref{cand:nodalmarkcluster}
中的内禀理想，然后对这些有限个理想的乘积作 blowup。
在包含某一个中心而避开其余中心的邻域中，
其他各理想均为单位理想。
因此同时 blowup 的局部坐标恰为此前已证明的坐标。
这里使用有限型理想的有限乘积，而不是其交。
与结点相交的标记截面数严格减少：
在每个所处理结点，至少一个标记变为光滑，
而此前光滑的截面完全不受影响。
至多 $|P|$ 轮后，全部标记截面均光滑。}

\bi{Now treat every smooth point containing at least two
marked specializations, again by blowing up the finite
product of the ideals in
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothmarkcluster}. All marks stay smooth.
The partition of $P$ by specializations strictly refines
whenever a collision is present, and different old classes
never merge because their centres are distinct and each
blowup is an isomorphism off its centre. A partition of a
finite set has at most $|P|$ classes, so this second process
also terminates after finitely many rounds.
Each blowup preserves properness, split nodality, finite
presentation, old component points, and projectivity
when present, by the two preceding lemmas.}
{再处理每个含至少两个标记专化的光滑点，
仍对 Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothmarkcluster}
中各理想的有限乘积作 blowup。
所有标记保持光滑。只要仍有碰撞，按专化给出的
$P$ 的划分就严格细化。
不同旧类不会合并，因为其中心互异，
且每次 blowup 在中心外均为同构。
有限集合的划分至多有 $|P|$ 个类，
故第二过程也在有限轮后结束。
由前两个引理，每次 blowup 保持 properness、
分裂结点性、有限表示性、旧分量点，
以及已有的射影性。}

\bi{The group permutes the relevant marked clusters at every
round and preserves their intrinsic ideals. It therefore
preserves their product. An automorphism of a scheme
preserving an ideal acts on its graded Rees algebra and
hence on its relative Proj. The multiplication and cocycle
identities are inherited from the original action on that
algebra; semilinear base automorphisms are handled by the
same graded construction. Thus every stage is
$G$-equivariant. No integer-valued contact function or
discreteness of the value group enters either termination
argument.}
{群在每一轮置换相关标记簇，并保持其内禀理想，
因而保持这些理想的乘积。
保持某理想的概形自同构作用于其分次 Rees 代数，
从而作用于其相对 Proj。
乘法和余圈恒等式直接继承自原代数上的作用；
半线性基底自同构也由同一分次构造处理。
因此每一步都等变。
两个终止性论证都不使用整数值接触函数，
也不要求值群离散。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Throughout this section $K$ is a complete nontrivially
valued field, $O=K^\circ$, and $k=\widetilde K$.
A split nodal model is a separated flat finitely presented $O$-scheme
with smooth generic fibre and reduced nodal special fibre,
whose nodes are $k$-rational and admit the actual rational
pointed charts of Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting}.
Its formal generic fibre is assumed Hausdorff.
A marking is a finite set of distinct $K$-rational generic
points. In the proper case each extends uniquely to an
$O$-section by \cite[Tag 0BX5]{StacksAlg}.}
{本节中 $K$ 为完备非平凡赋值域，$O=K^\circ$、
$k=\widetilde K$。分裂结点模型指分离、平坦、有限表示
$O$-概形，其泛纤维光滑、特殊纤维约化且仅有结点，
并且结点均为 $k$-有理点，具有
Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting}
中的实际有理带点坐标片。假设其形式泛纤维为 Hausdorff。
标记指有限个互异的 $K$-有理泛纤维点。在 proper 情形，
由 \cite[Tag 0BX5]{StacksAlg}，每个标记唯一延拓为
$O$-截面。}
