Domain flatness on ordinary local rings

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 279

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Domain flatness on ordinary local rings}{通常局部环上的域平坦性}]
\label{fnd:analyticdomainstalkflatness}
\bi{Let $U\subset X=\mathcal M(A)$ be an affinoid domain. For every $x\in U$, the local homomorphism}
{设 $U\subset X=\mathcal M(A)$ 为 affinoid 域。对每个 $x\in U$，局部同态}
\[
 \mathcal O_{X,x}\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{U,x}
\]
\bi{is flat. This assertion remains true after every bounded affinoid base change over every complete valued extension of $K$.}
{平坦。在 $K$ 的任意完备赋值扩域上作任意有界 affinoid 基变换后，此断言仍然成立。}
\end{theorem}
Selected proof part 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Write $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ and $S=\mathcal O_{U,x}$. Fix a rational neighborhood $V$ of $x$ in $X$. Affinoid neighborhoods $W$ of $x$ in $U$ contained in $V$ form a cofinal system: intersect an ordinary neighborhood with $V$, then choose a rational neighborhood inside the affinoid space $U$, as in Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidanalyticdomaincalculus}. By transitivity and restriction of affinoid domains, each such $W$ is an affinoid domain of $V$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness} makes $A_V\to A_W$ flat.}
{记 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$S=\mathcal O_{U,x}$。固定 $x$ 在 $X$ 中的一个 rational 邻域 $V$。包含于 $V$ 的、$x$ 在 $U$ 中的 affinoid 邻域 $W$ 构成余终系统：把通常邻域与 $V$ 相交，再如 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidanalyticdomaincalculus} 那样，在 affinoid 空间 $U$ 中选取 rational 邻域。由 affinoid 域的传递性与限制性，每个这样的 $W$ 都是 $V$ 的 affinoid 域。Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness} 保证 $A_V\to A_W$ 平坦。}
Selected proof part 2 · exact TeX
\bi{We verify flatness of $S=\varinjlim_W A_W$ over this fixed $A_V$ by the finite-ideal criterion \cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg}. For a finitely generated ideal $J\subset A_V$, every element of $J\otimes_{A_V}S$ is represented by a finite sum at some stage $A_W$. If it maps to zero in $S$, its image vanishes at a smaller neighborhood $W'$. Flatness of $A_{W'}$ implies that its representative in $J\otimes_{A_V}A_{W'}$ is zero. Thus $J\otimes_{A_V}S\to S$ is injective. This also spells out the exactness of the filtered passage being used.}
{对这个固定的 $A_V$，用有限理想判别准则 \cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg} 检验 $S=\varinjlim_W A_W$ 的平坦性。对有限生成理想 $J\subset A_V$，$J\otimes_{A_V}S$ 的每个元素均由某个阶段 $A_W$ 上的有限和表示。若它映到 $S$ 中的零元，其像就在更小邻域 $W'$ 上消失。由 $A_{W'}$ 的平坦性，其在 $J\otimes_{A_V}A_{W'}$ 中的代表元为零。因此 $J\otimes_{A_V}S\to S$ 单射。这也明确给出了此处经过有向极限时使用的正合性论证。}
Selected proof part 3 · exact TeX
\bi{Now take a finitely generated ideal $I\subset R$ and $z\in I\otimes_R S$ mapping to zero. Choose generators $a_1,\ldots,a_m$ of $I$ and write $z=\sum_i a_i\otimes s_i$ with $s_i\in S$. By Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks}, the $a_i$ lift to elements $\widetilde a_i$ of one $A_V$. Put $J=(\widetilde a_1,\ldots,\widetilde a_m)$. Then $z_V=\sum_i\widetilde a_i\otimes s_i\in J\otimes_{A_V}S$ maps to $z$ and has zero image in $S$. We have just proved $S$ flat over $A_V$, so $z_V=0$, and hence $z=0$. Again applying \cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg} proves $R$-flatness. The map is local because both maximal ideals are defined by vanishing at $x$, and their residue evaluations are compatible by Corollary~\ref{fnd:domainresiduefields}.}
{现在取有限生成理想 $I\subset R$，以及映到零元的 $z\in I\otimes_R S$。选择 $I$ 的生成元 $a_1,\ldots,a_m$，并写成 $z=\sum_i a_i\otimes s_i$，其中 $s_i\in S$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks}，$a_i$ 可提升为同一个 $A_V$ 中的元素 $\widetilde a_i$。令 $J=(\widetilde a_1,\ldots,\widetilde a_m)$。于是 $z_V=\sum_i\widetilde a_i\otimes s_i\in J\otimes_{A_V}S$ 映到 $z$，且其在 $S$ 中的像为零。刚才已经证明 $S$ 在 $A_V$ 上平坦，所以 $z_V=0$，从而 $z=0$。再次应用 \cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg}，得到 $R$-平坦性。该同态为局部同态，因为两边的极大理想都由在 $x$ 消失定义，并且由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:domainresiduefields}，剩余域取值彼此相容。}
Selected proof part 4 · exact TeX
\bi{For a bounded map $A\to C$ with $C$ affinoid over a complete extension, Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange} identifies the inverse image of $U$ with an affinoid domain of $\mathcal M(C)$. Apply the same proof over the complete coefficient field of $C$.}
{若 $A\to C$ 有界，且 $C$ 是某个完备扩域上的 affinoid，则 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange} 将 $U$ 的逆像识别为 $\mathcal M(C)$ 的 affinoid 域。在 $C$ 的完备系数域上应用同一个证明即可。}
