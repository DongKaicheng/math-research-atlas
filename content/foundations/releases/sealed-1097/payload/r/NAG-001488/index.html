An affinoid epimorphism over a field

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 88

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{An affinoid epimorphism over a field}{域上的 affinoid 上态}]
\label{fnd:strictfieldmonomorphism}
\bi{Let $\kappa$ be any complete non-Archimedean field with its fixed absolute value, and let $D\ne0$ be a strict $\kappa$-affinoid algebra. If $\kappa\to D$ is a strict-affinoid epimorphism, then it is a bounded isomorphism. No reducedness or separability is assumed.}
{设 $\kappa$ 为任意赋予固定绝对值的完备非阿基米德域，$D\ne0$ 为 strict $\kappa$-affinoid 代数。若 $\kappa\to D$ 是 strict-affinoid 上态，则它是有界同构。不假设约化性或可分性。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For each maximal ideal $\mathfrak n$ of $D$, the residue field $L=D/\mathfrak n$ is finite over $\kappa$, and the quotient map $D\to L$ is bounded by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}. The finite-dimensional algebra $L\otimes_\kappa L$ is strict by Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebras}, and its two maps from $L$ are bounded by finite-dimensional norm equivalence. Compose them with the surjection $D\to L$. The epimorphism condition shows that $a\otimes1=1\otimes a$ for all $a\in L$. Multiplication $L\otimes_\kappa L\to L$ is consequently an isomorphism, so $[L:\kappa]^2=[L:\kappa]$ and $L=\kappa$.}
{对 $D$ 的每个极大理想 $\mathfrak n$，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}，剩余域 $L=D/\mathfrak n$ 在 $\kappa$ 上有限，且商映射 $D\to L$ 有界。有限维代数 $L\otimes_\kappa L$ 由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebras} 为 strict，来自 $L$ 的两个映射则由有限维范数等价性有界。将它们与满射 $D\to L$ 复合，上态条件说明对所有 $a\in L$ 都有 $a\otimes1=1\otimes a$。于是乘法 $L\otimes_\kappa L\to L$ 为同构，所以 $[L:\kappa]^2=[L:\kappa]$，即 $L=\kappa$。}

\bi{The bounded residue maps $D\to\kappa$ for any two maximal ideals must now agree; their kernels agree as well. Hence $D$ has exactly one maximal ideal. Apply finite normalization, Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization}, to obtain a finite injection $\kappa\langle T_1,\ldots,T_d\rangle\hookrightarrow D$. If $d>0$, the two evaluation ideals at $(0,\ldots,0)$ and $(1,0,\ldots,0)$ are distinct maximal ideals in the Tate algebra. Lying over and the maximal-ideal criterion for integral extensions, \cite[Tags 00GQ and 00GS]{StacksAlg}, give maximal ideals of $D$ above both of them, a contradiction. Therefore $d=0$ and $D$ is finite over $\kappa$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictfiniteepimorphism} makes $\kappa\to D$ surjective. It is injective because $D\ne0$, and its inverse is bounded by finite-dimensional norm equivalence.}
{任意两个极大理想所给的有界剩余映射 $D\to\kappa$ 此时必须相同，故其核也相同。因此 $D$ 恰有一个极大理想。应用有限正规化 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization}，得到有限单射 $\kappa\langle T_1,\ldots,T_d\rangle\hookrightarrow D$。若 $d>0$，则在 $(0,\ldots,0)$ 与 $(1,0,\ldots,0)$ 处取值给出 Tate 代数中两个不同的极大理想。由整扩张的 lying-over 及极大理想判据 \cite[Tags 00GQ and 00GS]{StacksAlg}，$D$ 中存在分别位于二者之上的极大理想，矛盾。因此 $d=0$，$D$ 在 $\kappa$ 上有限。Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictfiniteepimorphism} 使 $\kappa\to D$ 为满射；因 $D\ne0$，它也是单射。有限维范数等价性保证其逆有界。}
\end{proof}
