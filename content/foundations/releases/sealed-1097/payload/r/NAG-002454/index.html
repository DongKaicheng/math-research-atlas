A graded residual fan bounds a pointed moment germ

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1061

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[A graded residual fan bounds a pointed moment germ]
\label{v038:pointed:pointedupper}
Let $V=\mathcal M(A)$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid
space, $x\in V$, and let $f=(f_1,\ldots,f_n)$ be a
tuple of analytic units. Put $w=t_f(x)$, set
$u_i=\widetilde{f_i(x)}\in F_x$, and let $C_x$ be the
graded tropical fan of
Lemma~\ref{v038:pointed:gradedfinitebasis}.
There are an ordinary affinoid neighbourhood $W_0$ of
$x$ and $\varepsilon>0$ such that
\[
 t_f(W_0)\cap B_\varepsilon(w)
                 \subseteq(w+C_x)\cap B_\varepsilon(w).
\]
This upper bound has pure fan dimension
$s=\operatorname{trdeg}_k k(u_1,\ldots,u_n)$ and
requires no interior or purity hypothesis on $V$.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Choose the finite homogeneous relations $P_j=\sum_m
\bar c_{jm}Z^m$ from
Lemma~\ref{v038:pointed:gradedfinitebasis}, and lift each
nonzero $\bar c_{jm}$ to $c_{jm}\in K$ with its exact
absolute value. Because $P_j$ is homogeneous, there is
an $R_j>0$ such that
\[
             |c_{jm}|\prod_i|f_i(x)|^{m_i}=R_j
\]
for every monomial of $P_j$. Put
$F_j=\sum_mc_{jm}f^m\in A$. The graded relation gives
$|F_j(x)|<R_j$. Choose real $b_j$ with
$|F_j(x)|<b_j<R_j$. By continuity, all the
inequalities $|F_j(y)|<b_j$ hold on one ordinary
affinoid neighbourhood $W_0$ of $x$.
Shrink $\varepsilon>0$ so that for every
$v\in B_\varepsilon(w)$, every monomial of the finite
list has size
\[
            |c_{jm}|\exp(-\langle m,v\rangle)>b_j.
\]
If $v=t_f(y)$ does not belong to $w+C_x$, then
$q=v-w\notin C_x$ and some $P_j$ has one unique
least $q$-weight exponent. All terms of $F_j(y)$
have sizes $R_j\exp(-\langle m,q\rangle)$;
the unique least exponent is therefore the unique
largest term, and the strong triangle inequality gives
$|F_j(y)|>b_j$. This contradicts the choice of $W_0$.
The fan and dimension assertions are precisely those of
Lemma~\ref{v038:pointed:gradedfinitebasis}.
\end{proof}
