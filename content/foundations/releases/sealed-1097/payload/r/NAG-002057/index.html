The complete nodal homotopy

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{The complete nodal homotopy}{结点管域上的完整同伦}]
\label{cand:nodaltubehomotopy}
\bi{Under the hypotheses of Proposition~\ref{cand:nodaltubeendpoints},
the radial homotopy on $]q[$ extends uniquely by fixing the attached
component points to a jointly continuous strong deformation
retraction of $C_q$ onto a closed edge of length $-\log|a|$.
If the endpoint images coincide, this edge is a circle with two
distinct incident half-edges at its vertex. The construction is
independent of the chosen pointed nodal chart and annular coordinate.}
{在 Proposition~\ref{cand:nodaltubeendpoints} 的假设下，$]q[$
上的径向同伦通过固定所附着的分量点，唯一延拓为 $C_q$ 到一条
长度为 $-\log|a|$ 的闭边的联合连续强形变收缩。若两个端点像
相同，此边为一个圆，在其顶点仍有两条不同的入射半边。该
构造不依赖所选的带点结点坐标图及圆环坐标。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First work in $C=D\cup\{v_t,v_s\}$ of the proposition.
The annulus homotopy of Proposition~\ref{fnd:annulusretraction}
preserves $|s|$. The full-tail assertion shows that fixing $v_t,v_s$
extends this homotopy continuously at both endpoints, uniformly in
its parameter. The resulting map $C\times[0,1]\to C$ is therefore
jointly continuous. The Gauss radius map extends to a continuous
injection of $[|a|,1]$ into $C$, with endpoints $v_s,v_t$;
compactness identifies its image with a closed interval. The
extended homotopy fixes this interval and retracts onto it.}
{先在该命题中的 $C=D\cup\{v_t,v_s\}$ 上工作。
Proposition~\ref{fnd:annulusretraction} 的圆环同伦保持 $|s|$。
完整尾部断言说明：固定 $v_t,v_s$ 后，同伦在两个端点均连续
延拓，且控制对同伦参数一致。因此得到的
$C\times[0,1]\to C$ 联合连续。Gauss 半径映射延拓为
$[|a|,1]$ 到 $C$ 的连续单射，其端点为 $v_s,v_t$；由紧性，
其像同胚于闭区间。延拓后的同伦固定此区间并收缩到它。}

\bi{The map $C\to C_q$ is a compact-to-Hausdorff surjection, as
is its product with $[0,1]$, so both are quotient maps. The only
possible identified pair consists of the two fixed endpoints.
The homotopy therefore descends to a continuous map on
$C_q\times[0,1]$, with all strong-retraction identities unchanged.
The quotient of the Gauss interval is an interval or, when its
endpoints coincide, a circle. Its logarithmic length is
$-\log|a|$ by Corollary~\ref{cand:annulusmodulusinvariance}.
The full homotopy commutes with every open-annulus coordinate
change by Theorem~\ref{cand:openannuluscoordinateisometry};
continuity on the dense tube gives the same equality at the ends.
This proves independence and retains both half-edges of a loop.}
{映射 $C\to C_q$ 是从紧空间到 Hausdorff 空间的满射，它与
$[0,1]$ 的乘积也是如此，故二者均为商映射。唯一可能被识别
的一对点是两个固定端点，因此同伦下降为
$C_q\times[0,1]$ 上的连续映射，并保持全部强收缩恒等式。
Gauss 区间的商是区间，或在端点相同时成为圆。由
Corollary~\ref{cand:annulusmodulusinvariance}，其对数长度为
$-\log|a|$。根据 Theorem~\ref{cand:openannuluscoordinateisometry}，
完整同伦与每个开圆环坐标变换相容；由稠密管域上的连续性，
同一等式在端点成立。这证明了独立性，并保留自环的两条半边。}
\end{proof}
