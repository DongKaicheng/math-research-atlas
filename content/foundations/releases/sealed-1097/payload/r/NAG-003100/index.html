Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Weighted meromorphic comparison; F8NM-Q]\label{prop:quotient}
On a pure affinoid \(V\), let \(f=a/b\) with \(a,b\) ambient regular,
and retain the reviewed pure-zero-space premises on actual refinements.
Writing \(f_i=a_i/b_i\), one has
\begin{align}
 L_f(\alpha)&=\sum_i m_i L_{f_i}(j_i^*\alpha),\label{eq:logsplit}\\
 \divis_V(f)&=\sum_i m_i(j_i)_*\divis_{V_i}(f_i),\label{eq:cycles}
\end{align}
for ordinary compact top tests \(\alpha\). The second identity includes
the actual analytic Cartier coefficients and their native compact-test
pairings. Local regular quotient atlases glue both sides independently
of their presentations. No ambient \(S_1\) is needed.
\end{proposition}
