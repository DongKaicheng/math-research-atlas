\begin{corollary}[Proper modifications and generic finite free degree]
\label{v038:degree:propermodification}
Keep $p:X\to Y$ and $D\subseteq Y$ as in
Theorem~\ref{v038:degree:propergenericprojection}.
If $p$ is an isomorphism over $Y\setminus D$ and coherent
modules $\mathcal F,\mathcal G$ are identified there by
$\mathcal F\simeq p^*\mathcal G$, then
\[
                    \int_{X,\mathcal F}p^*\alpha
                                         =\int_{Y,\mathcal G}\alpha
\]
for every compactly supported ordinary smooth top form.
In particular a proper modification which is an isomorphism
away from such a closed subspace preserves the structure-module
integral. Additional source components mapped into $D$ are
allowed, provided $X$ remains pure of dimension $d$.

More generally, suppose that over $Y\setminus D$ the map is
finite and its coherent direct image of $\mathcal F$ is
locally free of constant rank $r$ on the analytic G-site.
Then
\[
                    \int_{X,\mathcal F}p^*\alpha
                                      =r\int_{Y,\mathcal O_Y}\alpha.
\]
The rank may be the full degree of a finite inseparable map;
separability is not required.
\end{corollary}
