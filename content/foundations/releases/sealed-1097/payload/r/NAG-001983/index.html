Restriction to degree one

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 578

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Restriction to degree one}
{限制到次数一}]
\label{fnd:gradeddegreeonevaluation}
\bi{For an extension of graded fields $k\subset F$, the map}
{对分次域扩张 $k\subset F$，映射}
\[
 \pi:\mathscr P_k(F)\longrightarrow\mathscr P_{k_1}(F_1),
                         \qquad V\longmapsto V\cap F_1
\]
\bi{is surjective. If $Q_0$ is an ordinary birational space
with field $F_1$, its pullback $Q$ along $\pi$ is affine
if and only if $Q_0$ is affine.}
{为满射。若 $Q_0$ 是具有域 $F_1$ 的通常 birational 空间，
而 $Q$ 是它沿 $\pi$ 的拉回，则 $Q$ 仿射当且仅当 $Q_0$
仿射。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Let $V_0\subset F_1$ be a valuation ring containing $k_1$.
In the graded subring $C=kV_0\subset F$, its degree-$r$
part, for $r$ in the support of $k$, is $c_rV_0$ for any
nonzero $c_r\in k_r$. Distinct degrees are independent.
Consequently $\mathfrak m_{V_0}C$ is proper and its contraction
to $V_0$ is $\mathfrak m_{V_0}$.
Choose an ordinary maximal ideal above it by
\cite[Tag 00E0(2)]{StacksAlg}, take its homogeneous part,
and localize homogeneously. Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedvaluationintegrality}
gives a graded valuation ring $V$ dominating this local ring.
It contains $k$ and contracts to $V_0$: if $a\in F_1\setminus V_0$,
then $a^{-1}\in\mathfrak m_{V_0}$ lies in the maximal ideal
of $V$, so $a\notin V$. This proves surjectivity.}
{设 $V_0\subset F_1$ 为包含 $k_1$ 的赋值环。
在分次子环 $C=kV_0\subset F$ 中，对 $k$ 支撑内的次数
$r$，其次数 $r$ 部分为 $c_rV_0$，其中任取非零
$c_r\in k_r$。不同次数独立，因此
$\mathfrak m_{V_0}C$ 是真理想，且收缩到 $V_0$ 为
$\mathfrak m_{V_0}$。由 \cite[Tag 00E0(2)]{StacksAlg}
选取其上方的通常极大理想，取齐次部分并作齐次局部化。
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedvaluationintegrality} 给出支配此局部环
的分次赋值环 $V$。它包含 $k$，且收缩为 $V_0$：
若 $a\in F_1\setminus V_0$，则
$a^{-1}\in\mathfrak m_{V_0}$ 属于 $V$ 的极大理想，
从而 $a\notin V$。这证明满射性。}

\bi{If $Q_0=\mathscr P_{k_1}(F_1)\{b_1,\ldots,b_m\}$,
then $Q=\mathscr P_k(F)\{b_1,\ldots,b_m\}$, proving
one direction. Conversely suppose $Q$ is affine.
Surjectivity of $\pi$ shows that the structural local
homeomorphism of $Q_0$ is injective; hence $Q_0$ is an
open subspace of $\mathscr P_{k_1}(F_1)$.
Write $Q=\mathscr P_k(F)\{a_1,\ldots,a_n\}$ with
nonzero homogeneous $a_i$.
Let $H$ be the subgroup of degrees whose positive powers
belong to the support of $k$. If $\deg(a_i)\notin H$,
then $F_H[a_i^{-1}]$ is a polynomial graded algebra over
the graded subfield $F_H$: distinct powers have distinct
degree classes modulo $H$. Localize at the homogeneous
prime $(a_i^{-1})$ and use graded domination.
The resulting valuation contains $F_H$, hence $F_1$ and $k$,
but excludes $a_i$. Its restriction is the trivial valuation
$F_1$, which belongs to every nonempty open $Q_0$.
This contradicts $Q=\pi^{-1}(Q_0)$.
Thus $\deg(a_i)^{d_i}=\deg(c_i)$ for some $d_i>0$ and
nonzero homogeneous $c_i\in k$. Put $b_i=a_i^{d_i}/c_i\in F_1$.
A valuation containing $k$ contains $a_i$ exactly when
it contains $b_i$. Hence}
{若 $Q_0=\mathscr P_{k_1}(F_1)\{b_1,\ldots,b_m\}$，
则 $Q=\mathscr P_k(F)\{b_1,\ldots,b_m\}$，证明一个方向。
反之设 $Q$ 仿射。由 $\pi$ 满射，$Q_0$ 的结构局部同胚
单射，因此 $Q_0$ 是 $\mathscr P_{k_1}(F_1)$ 的开子空间。
写成 $Q=\mathscr P_k(F)\{a_1,\ldots,a_n\}$，其中
$a_i$ 非零齐次。令 $H$ 为某个正整数次幂属于 $k$ 支撑的
全部次数组成的子群。若 $\deg(a_i)\notin H$，则
$F_H[a_i^{-1}]$ 是分次子域 $F_H$ 上的多项式分次代数，
因为不同幂在模 $H$ 的次数类中不同。
在齐次素理想 $(a_i^{-1})$ 处局部化，再应用分次支配。
所得赋值包含 $F_H$，因而包含 $F_1$ 与 $k$，却不包含
$a_i$。其限制为平凡赋值 $F_1$，它属于每个非空开集 $Q_0$，
这与 $Q=\pi^{-1}(Q_0)$ 矛盾。
因此存在 $d_i>0$ 及非零齐次 $c_i\in k$，满足
$\deg(a_i)^{d_i}=\deg(c_i)$。置
$b_i=a_i^{d_i}/c_i\in F_1$。包含 $k$ 的赋值包含 $a_i$
当且仅当它包含 $b_i$。于是}
\[
 Q=\pi^{-1}\bigl(\mathscr P_{k_1}(F_1)\{b_1,\ldots,b_n\}\bigr).
\]
\bi{Surjectivity gives the same expression for $Q_0$,
proving that it is affine.}
{由满射性，$Q_0$ 等于括号中的集合，因此仿射。}
\end{proof}
