1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 59

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}\label{new:seminormcontractive}
\bi{Every such seminorm satisfies $|a|_x\leq\|a\|$. Its kernel $\mathfrak p_x$ is prime, and the induced norm on $A/\mathfrak p_x$ extends uniquely to an absolute value on its fraction field.}
{每个这样的 seminorm 都满足 $|a|_x\leq\|a\|$。其 kernel $\mathfrak p_x$ 为 prime，且在 $A/\mathfrak p_x$ 上诱导的 norm 唯一延拓为其 fraction field 上的 absolute value。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For every positive integer $m$, multiplicativity and boundedness give $|a|_x^m\leq C\|a^m\|\leq C\|a\|^m$. Taking $m$th roots and letting $m$ tend to infinity gives the first assertion. If $ab\in\mathfrak p_x$, then $|a|_x|b|_x=0$, so at least one factor belongs to $\mathfrak p_x$. The quotient norm is therefore an absolute value on an integral domain. On fractions it must be $|a/b|=|a|_x/|b|_x$; cross multiplication proves that this is well-defined.}
{对每个正整数 $m$，multiplicativity 与 boundedness 给出 $|a|_x^m\leq C\|a^m\|\leq C\|a\|^m$。取 $m$th roots 并令 $m$ 趋于无穷，得到第一项。若 $ab\in\mathfrak p_x$，则 $|a|_x|b|_x=0$，故至少一个因子属于 $\mathfrak p_x$。因此 quotient norm 是 integral domain 上的 absolute value。对 fractions，延拓只能为 $|a/b|=|a|_x/|b|_x$；cross multiplication 证明其 well-defined。}
\end{proof}
