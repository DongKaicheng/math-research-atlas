Local extension and gluing of morphisms

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 386

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Local extension and gluing of morphisms}{态射的局部延拓与粘合}]
\label{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing}
\bi{Locally bounded morphisms form a category independent of atlas refinement. It is enough to specify compatible bounded affinoid maps on a chart quasi-net. Morphisms glue uniquely over admissible coverings. The inverse image of an analytic domain is analytic. The inclusion of an analytic domain $Y\subseteq X$ has the universal property}
{局部有界态射构成一个与 atlas 加细无关的范畴。只须在一个 chart quasi-net 上给出相容的有界 affinoid 映射，即可确定态射。态射可沿 admissible 覆盖唯一粘合。解析域的逆像仍为解析域，且解析域 $Y\subseteq X$ 的包含满足泛性质}
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}(Z,Y)=
 \{f\in\operatorname{Hom}(Z,X):f(|Z|)\subseteq Y\}.
\]
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Here compatibility of a collection of local maps means that, after refining a source overlap by common affinoid charts and refining the common target image by affinoid charts in the target overlap, the induced bounded homomorphisms coincide. Such refinements exist: target overlaps have their chart net, and an affinoid map pulls back each chart domain by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange}. Pulling back a quasi-net along a continuous map preserves the quasi-net condition on its inverse image. Intersecting source chart nets then gives the required common source refinements.}
{这里局部映射族的相容性是指：将源的交叠加细为公共 affinoid chart，并将共同目标像加细到目标交叠内的 affinoid chart 后，所诱导的有界同态相同。这样的加细确实存在：目标交叠具有 chart net，而 affinoid 映射由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange} 将每个 chart 域拉回为域。连续映射拉回 quasi-net 后，在逆像上保持 quasi-net 条件。再将源 chart net 相交，即得到所需公共源加细。}

\bi{Suppose the local maps have been specified, and take arbitrary charts $U,V$ with $f(U)\subseteq V$. Intersect $U$ with the source charts on which the maps are given. On each piece, refine the target overlap between its original target chart and $V$, then pull back those refinements. These are affinoid domains of $U$ and form a quasi-net of $U$. Compactness gives a finite subcover. The resulting maps from $A_V$ agree on all overlaps by the prescribed compatibility, so Lemma~\ref{fnd:nettopologyboundedgluing} gives $f_{U,V}:A_V\to A_U$. Any second finite refinement gives the same map after their common refinement. This proves existence, uniqueness and restriction compatibility of all maps required in Definition~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphisms}. The topological part glues by Lemma~\ref{fnd:nettopologyboundedgluing}.}
{设已经给定局部映射，并任取满足 $f(U)\subseteq V$ 的 chart $U,V$。将 $U$ 与已经给定映射的源 chart 相交。对所得每块，加细其原目标 chart 与 $V$ 的交叠，再拉回这些加细。所得集合是 $U$ 的 affinoid 域，并构成 $U$ 的 quasi-net；紧性给出有限子覆盖。相容性保证从 $A_V$ 出发的这些映射在所有交叠上一致，故 Lemma~\ref{fnd:nettopologyboundedgluing} 给出 $f_{U,V}:A_V\to A_U$。任何第二个有限加细，都在共同加细上给出相同映射。这证明 Definition~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphisms} 所需全部映射的存在、唯一性及限制相容性。拓扑映射也由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:nettopologyboundedgluing} 粘合。}

\bi{The inverse image assertion is local on a presenting source chart and a target chart. Cover the target domain by chart domains and pull them back using the affinoid-domain base-change theorem. The resulting quasi-net of affinoid domains proves the assertion. For a composite $g\circ f$, refine source charts by inverse images of presenting charts for $g$, compose the bounded algebra maps there, and use the preceding extension construction. Common refinement and associativity of algebra homomorphisms prove associativity. The identity homomorphisms give identities. This construction also shows that replacing either atlas by a chart quasi-net does not change the morphism set.}
{逆像断言可在表示态射的一个源 chart 和一个目标 chart 上检查。用 chart 域覆盖目标解析域，再由 affinoid 域基变换定理拉回，所得 affinoid 域 quasi-net 证明逆像解析。对复合 $g\circ f$，用表示 $g$ 的 chart 的逆像加细源 chart，在其上复合有界代数同态，再应用刚才的延拓构造。公共加细及代数同态的结合律证明复合的结合律；恒等同态给出恒等态射。这也证明将任一 atlas 换成 chart quasi-net 不改变态射集合。}

\bi{For gluing along an admissible covering, present each local morphism on chart pieces contained in the relevant covering member. Their union is a quasi-net by transitivity. Agreement on pairwise intersections is exactly the compatibility used above, so extension gives a unique global morphism. Finally, if $f(|Z|)\subseteq Y$, pull back the chart net of $Y$. The local maps factor through its chart domains by the affinoid-domain universal property and glue to $Z\to Y$. Local uniqueness proves the displayed universal property.}
{对 admissible 覆盖上的粘合，在每个覆盖成员内的 chart 块上表示相应局部态射。由传递性，合并后的族为 quasi-net；两两交叠上的一致性正是上文所需的相容性，因此延拓构造给出唯一整体态射。最后，若 $f(|Z|)\subseteq Y$，则拉回 $Y$ 的 chart net。Affinoid 域的泛性质将局部映射分解到这些 chart 域，并粘合为 $Z\to Y$。局部唯一性即给出所列泛性质。}
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[\bi{Locally bounded morphisms}{局部有界态射}]
\label{fnd:generalnetmorphisms}
\bi{For two affinoid atlases, a morphism consists of a continuous map $f:X\to X'$ and, for every pair of charts $U\in\bar\tau$, $V\in\bar\tau'$ with $f(U)\subseteq V$, a bounded homomorphism $f_{U,V}:A_V\to A_U$ inducing $f|_U$. These maps commute with restrictions in both charts. We require that the charts $U$ admitting such a target chart $V$ form a quasi-net of $X$. An analytic space is an affinoid atlas considered up to the canonical identifications under chart refinement of Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf}.}
{对两个 affinoid atlas，一个态射由连续映射 $f:X\to X'$ 及下述资料组成：对每对满足 $f(U)\subseteq V$ 的 chart $U\in\bar\tau$、$V\in\bar\tau'$，给出诱导 $f|_U$ 的有界同态 $f_{U,V}:A_V\to A_U$。这些映射与源、目标 chart 的限制均相容，并要求具有这样一个目标 chart $V$ 的源 chart $U$ 构成 $X$ 的 quasi-net。将 affinoid atlas 按 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf} 中 chart 加细所给的典范识别视为同一对象，得到解析空间。}
\end{definition}
