A regular open preserved by analytically separable extensions

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 348

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{A regular open preserved by analytically separable extensions}{解析可分扩张保持的正则开集}]
\label{fnd:generalanalyticseparableregularopen}
\bi{Let $K$ be any complete field and let $A$ be an integral generalized $K$-affinoid algebra. There is $0\ne a\in A$ such that, for every analytically separable complete $L/K$, the ring $A_L[1/a]$ is regular and all ordinary local rings of $\mathcal M(A_L)$ at points where $a$ is nonzero are regular.}
{设 $K$ 为任意完备域，$A$ 为整 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数。则存在 $0\ne a\in A$，使对每个解析可分完备扩张 $L/K$，环 $A_L[1/a]$ 正则，且 $\mathcal M(A_L)$ 中所有 $a$ 非零的点处的通常局部环均正则。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{In characteristic zero this is Proposition~\ref{fnd:charzerouniformregularopen}. In positive characteristic, choose a finite $K$-free polyradius $r$ such that $K_r$ is nontrivially valued and $A_r$ is strict. The kernel-zero point and its Gauss lift used in the proof of that proposition also show, in every characteristic, that $A_r$ is integral: the Gauss value of a nonzero Laurent coefficient family is positive. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization} there is a finite inclusion $K_r\{T_1,\ldots,T_l\}\subset A_r$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:positivecharacteristicgenericgerms}, applied over the original field $K$ with this polyradius $r$, gives a nonzero $d\in A_r$ having its stated regularity properties after every analytically separable extension of $K$. Write}
{特征零情形即 Proposition~\ref{fnd:charzerouniformregularopen}。在正特征中，选择有限的 $K$-自由多半径 $r$，使 $K_r$ 赋值非平凡且 $A_r$ 为 strict。该命题证明中所用的核为零的点及其 Gauss 提升，在任意特征中都说明 $A_r$ 为整环：非零 Laurent 系数族的 Gauss 值为正。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization}，有有限包含 $K_r\{T_1,\ldots,T_l\}\subset A_r$。在原域 $K$ 上、用此多半径 $r$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:positivecharacteristicgenericgerms}，得到非零 $d\in A_r$，它在 $K$ 的每个解析可分扩张之后具有该定理所述正则性。写}
\[
                 d=\sum_{\nu\in\mathbb Z^{|r|}}a_\nu U^\nu,
            \qquad a=a_{\nu_0}\ne0
\]
\bi{for one nonzero coefficient. Fix such an extension $L/K$ and $y\in\mathcal M(A_L)$ with $|a(y)|>0$. Associativity of completed tensor products and the coefficient description identify}
{并选择一个非零系数 $a=a_{\nu_0}$。固定这样的扩张 $L/K$ 及满足 $|a(y)|>0$ 的点 $y\in\mathcal M(A_L)$。完成张量积的结合律与系数描述给出}
\[
           (A_r)_L=A_L\{r^{-1}U,rU^{-1}\}.
\]
\bi{The right side is the algebra of the relative polyannulus $P\to\mathcal M(A_L)$ on which $|U_i|=r_i$. Evaluate its coefficients in $\mathcal H(y)$ and then in the completion of $\mathcal H(y)(U)$ for the weighted Gauss absolute value. This defines a point $z$ of $P$ above $y$ and gives}
{右侧为相对多圆环 $P\to\mathcal M(A_L)$ 的代数，其上 $|U_i|=r_i$。将其系数先在 $\mathcal H(y)$ 中取值，再映入 $\mathcal H(y)(U)$ 关于加权 Gauss 绝对值的完备化，便定义 $P$ 中位于 $y$ 上方的点 $z$，并有}
\[
                       |d(z)|=\max_\nu |a_\nu(y)|r^\nu>0.
\]
\bi{The multiplicativity and extension of this weighted Gauss norm were proved in Lemma~\ref{fnd:gausscommonextension}; no freedom assumption over $L$ is used. The ring $\mathcal O_{P,z}$ is regular by the choice of $d$. Moreover $P$ is the Laurent domain $|U_i|\ge r_i$ in the relative polydisc $|U_i|\le r_i$. Corollary~\ref{fnd:relativepolydiscanalyticflat} therefore makes $\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(A_L),y}\to\mathcal O_{P,z}$ a flat local map. Tag 00OF descends regularity to the source.}
{这个加权 Gauss 范数的乘性及其扩张已在 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gausscommonextension} 中证明，不需要半径在 $L$ 上自由。由 $d$ 的选取，环 $\mathcal O_{P,z}$ 正则。此外，$P$ 是相对多圆盘 $|U_i|\le r_i$ 中由 $|U_i|\ge r_i$ 定义的 Laurent 域。因此 Corollary~\ref{fnd:relativepolydiscanalyticflat} 说明 $\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(A_L),y}\to\mathcal O_{P,z}$ 为平坦局部映射；Tag 00OF 将正则性下降到源。}

\bi{Finally, every prime of $A_L$ avoiding $a$ is the kernel of an analytic point $y$, by Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity}. Its local ring maps faithfully flatly to the ordinary local ring at $y$, which is regular by the preceding paragraph. Another application of Tag 00OF proves regularity at that prime. This proves regularity of $A_L[1/a]$ and all the assertions with a single coefficient $a$ independent of $L$.}
{最后，由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity}，$A_L$ 中每个避开 $a$ 的素理想都是某个解析点 $y$ 的核；该处的代数局部环忠实平坦地映入 $y$ 处的通常局部环，而后者已由上一段证明正则。再次应用 Tag 00OF，就得到该素理想处正则。因此 $A_L[1/a]$ 正则，且全部断言使用同一个不依赖 $L$ 的系数 $a$。}
\end{proof}
