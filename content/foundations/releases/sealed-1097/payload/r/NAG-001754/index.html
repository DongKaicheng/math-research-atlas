Finite pushforward detects flatness

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 322

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Finite pushforward detects flatness}{有限推前检测平坦性}]
\label{fnd:finitepushforwardflatness}
\bi{Let $Y\xrightarrow{\pi}T\to X$ be analytic morphisms with $\pi$ finite, let $t\in T$, and write $\pi^{-1}(t)=\{y_1,\ldots,y_m\}$. For a coherent sheaf $\mathcal F$ on $Y_G$, if the spaces are good there is a canonical decomposition}
{设 $Y\xrightarrow{\pi}T\to X$ 为解析态射，$\pi$ 有限，$t\in T$，且 $\pi^{-1}(t)=\{y_1,\ldots,y_m\}$。对 $Y_G$ 上的凝聚层 $\mathcal F$，若各空间 good，则存在典范分解}
\[
       (\pi_*\mathcal F)_t
                   \simeq \bigoplus_{i=1}^{m}\mathcal F_{y_i}.
\]
\bi{Consequently $\pi_*\mathcal F$ is naively $X$-flat at $t$ if and only if $\mathcal F$ is naively $X$-flat at every $y_i$. The analogous equivalence for analytic $X$-flatness holds for arbitrary analytic spaces.}
{因此，$\pi_*\mathcal F$ 在 $t$ 处朴素 $X$-平坦，当且仅当 $\mathcal F$ 在每个 $y_i$ 处朴素 $X$-平坦。解析 $X$-平坦性的相应等价对任意解析空间均成立。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For good spaces, use Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation}. Representatives of finitely many section germs at the $y_i$ may be restricted to its disjoint open-and-closed branches above a smaller ordinary neighborhood of $t$; they glue to a section of $\mathcal F$ on that full inverse image. If all germs of such a section vanish, shrinking the same way makes it vanish on a full inverse-image neighborhood. This proves the displayed isomorphism for module sheaves just as for structure sheaves. It respects the action of $\mathcal O_{X,f(t)}$. A finite direct sum of modules is flat exactly when every summand is flat: tensor an injection and inspect its component kernels. This gives the naive equivalence.}
{对 good 空间应用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation}。在各 $y_i$ 处给定有限个截面芽的代表元后，可把它们限制到 $t$ 的某个更小普通邻域上方两两不交的开闭分支，再粘合成整个逆像上的 $\mathcal F$ 截面。若此截面的全部这些芽为零，同样缩小后，它就在某个完整逆像邻域上消失。这像结构层情形一样，证明模层的所列同构，并保持 $\mathcal O_{X,f(t)}$ 的作用。有限直和模平坦，当且仅当每个直和因子平坦：对单射作张量，再逐个检查分量的核即可。这给出朴素平坦性的等价。}

\bi{Take any good analytic base change $X'\to X$ over any complete extension. The map $\pi':Y'\to T'$ is still finite, and Proposition~\ref{fnd:finitecoherentdirectimagebasechange} identifies $(\pi_*\mathcal F)_{T'}$ with $\pi'_*(\mathcal F_{Y'})$. At any $t'$ above $t$, the naive equivalence already proved applies to its finite fibre. Every point of that fibre lies over one of the $y_i$, and every point above a $y_i$ is in the fibre of its own image $t'$. Thus the complete collection of naive-flatness tests on the two sides is the same. This proves the analytic equivalence in the good case. For general spaces, restrict to affinoid domains in $X,T$ and their finite inverse-image domains in $Y$. The general $G$-local definition and the canonical base-change isomorphism give the same equivalence.}
{在任意完备扩域上取任意 good 解析基变换 $X'\to X$。映射 $\pi':Y'\to T'$ 仍有限，而 Proposition~\ref{fnd:finitecoherentdirectimagebasechange} 将 $(\pi_*\mathcal F)_{T'}$ 识别为 $\pi'_*(\mathcal F_{Y'})$。对 $t$ 上方的任意 $t'$，已证朴素等价适用于其有限纤维。该纤维的每个点都位于某个 $y_i$ 上方，而每个 $y_i$ 上方的点都属于其像 $t'$ 的纤维。因此，两边全部朴素平坦性测试完全对应，证明 good 情形的解析平坦性等价。对一般空间，限制到 $X,T$ 中的 affinoid 域及 $Y$ 中相应的有限逆像域，再用一般 $G$-局部定义及典范基变换同构，即得相同结论。}
\end{proof}
