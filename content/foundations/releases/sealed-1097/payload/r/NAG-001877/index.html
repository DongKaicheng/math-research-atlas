\begin{proposition}[\bi{An ordinary local criterion for \'etaleness}{\'Etale 性的普通局部判据}]
\label{fnd:localfiniteetalecriterion}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good analytic spaces. Suppose $y\in Y$ is isolated in its analytic fibre and belongs to $\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$. Then $f$ is \'etale at $y$ if and only if the local map $\mathcal O_{X,x}\to\mathcal O_{Y,y}$ is flat and $(\Omega^1_{Y/X})_y=0$, where $x=f(y)$. In this case one can choose ordinary neighbourhoods on which $f$ is finite locally free and has zero relative differential sheaf.}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 为 good 解析空间间的态射。假设 $y\in Y$ 在其解析纤维中孤立，且属于 $\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$。则 $f$ 在 $y$ 处 \'etale，当且仅当局部映射 $\mathcal O_{X,x}\to\mathcal O_{Y,y}$ 平坦且 $(\Omega^1_{Y/X})_y=0$，其中 $x=f(y)$。此时可以选择普通邻域，使限制后的态射有限局部自由，且相对微分层为零。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The local finite criterion and Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation} reduce the converse to a finite morphism $\mathcal M(B)\to\mathcal M(A)$ with fibre over $x$ consisting only of $y$, where these affinoids are neighbourhoods of the original points. Put $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$, $S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$, and $\mathfrak p=\ker(A\to\mathcal H(x))$. The finite-branch stalk identity is}
{局部有限性判据与 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation} 将反向断言归结为有限态射 $\mathcal M(B)\to\mathcal M(A)$，其在 $x$ 上的纤维仅有 $y$，且这两个 affinoid 是原点的邻域。令 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$，并令 $\mathfrak p=\ker(A\to\mathcal H(x))$。有限分支的茎恒等式为}
\[
 B\otimes_A R\simeq S.
\]
\bi{The local map $A_{\mathfrak p}\to R$ is faithfully flat. Indeed, Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks} writes $R$ as a filtered colimit of rational-neighbourhood algebras, all flat over $A$ by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness}. It is consequently flat over $A_{\mathfrak p}$. The maximal ideal contracts to $\mathfrak p A_{\mathfrak p}$ by evaluation, so faithful flatness follows from \cite[Tag 00HP]{StacksAlg}.}
{局部映射 $A_{\mathfrak p}\to R$ 忠实平坦。确实，Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks} 将 $R$ 写成 rational 邻域代数的滤过直极限；由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness}，这些代数均在 $A$ 上平坦。因此 $R$ 在 $A_{\mathfrak p}$ 上平坦。由取值可知，其极大理想的收缩为 $\mathfrak p A_{\mathfrak p}$，故 \cite[Tag 00HP]{StacksAlg} 给出忠实平坦性。}

\bi{If $S$ is $R$-flat, then $B_{\mathfrak p}$ is $A_{\mathfrak p}$-flat. For any injection of $A_{\mathfrak p}$-modules, the kernel after tensoring with $B_{\mathfrak p}$ becomes zero after the faithfully flat scalar extension to $R$, because the extended module is $S$. Thus that kernel is zero. As $B$ is finite over the Noetherian ring $A$, \cite[Tag 00NX]{StacksAlg} makes $B_{\mathfrak p}$ free of finite rank. Lift a basis and its inverse finite presentation to a localization $A_a$ with $a\notin\mathfrak p$; clearing the finitely many kernel and cokernel generators after a further such localization gives $B_a\simeq A_a^m$.}
{若 $S$ 在 $R$ 上平坦，则 $B_{\mathfrak p}$ 在 $A_{\mathfrak p}$ 上平坦：对任意 $A_{\mathfrak p}$-模单射，与 $B_{\mathfrak p}$ 张量后的核，在忠实平坦扩张到 $R$ 后变为零，因为扩张后的模为 $S$；故该核原本即为零。由于 $B$ 是 Noetherian 环 $A$ 上的有限模，\cite[Tag 00NX]{StacksAlg} 说明 $B_{\mathfrak p}$ 为有限秩自由模。将一组基及相应有限表示提升到某个局部化 $A_a$，其中 $a\notin\mathfrak p$；再作这样的局部化以消去核与余核的有限组生成元，便得 $B_a\simeq A_a^m$。}

\bi{Let $P=\Omega^1_{B/A}$, a finite $B$-module and hence a finite $A$-module. Here algebraic and analytic differentials agree. Indeed an algebraic $A$-derivation from the finite $A$-module $B$ into a finite $B$-module is $A$-linear and automatically bounded for the canonical finite-module topologies. Thus the two finite differential modules represent the same derivations, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:boundeduniversaldifferentials}. The finite-branch stalk argument, applied to a finite presentation of $P$, gives $P\otimes_A R\simeq(\Omega^1_{Y/X})_y$. The assumed vanishing and faithful flatness imply $P_{\mathfrak p}=0$. After enlarging $a\notin\mathfrak p$, finite generation gives $P_a=0$. On the ordinary open neighbourhood $\{|a|>0\}$ of $x$, the finite morphism is therefore locally free and has zero relative differential sheaf. To verify the first assertion analytically, restrict to a rational neighbourhood where $|a|$ is bounded below. Its algebra contains $a^{-1}$, so tensoring $B_a\simeq A_a^m$ gives a free finite algebra module there. Its ordinary local rings are the factors in the finite-branch decomposition; those factors are direct summands of a free module after passage to the base stalk. They are thus flat. The same argument applies after every analytic base change, since finite-module base change is ordinary tensor product. This proves the claimed \'etaleness and analytic flatness.}
{令 $P=\Omega^1_{B/A}$；它是有限 $B$-模，因而也是有限 $A$-模。此处代数微分与解析微分一致：从有限 $A$-模 $B$ 到有限 $B$-模的代数 $A$-导子为 $A$-线性映射，故对典范有限模拓扑自动有界。因此由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:boundeduniversaldifferentials}，这两个有限微分模代表相同的导子。将有限分支的茎论证应用于 $P$ 的有限表示，得到 $P\otimes_A R\simeq(\Omega^1_{Y/X})_y$。所假设的消失及忠实平坦性给出 $P_{\mathfrak p}=0$。扩大所选的 $a\notin\mathfrak p$ 后，有限生成性使 $P_a=0$。所以在 $x$ 的普通开邻域 $\{|a|>0\}$ 上，有限态射局部自由且相对微分层为零。其解析解释如下：限制到 $|a|$ 具有正下界的 rational 邻域后，域代数包含 $a^{-1}$，因而将 $B_a\simeq A_a^m$ 张量过去，得到自由有限代数模。普通局部环是有限分支分解中的各因子；在底空间茎上，它们是自由模的直和因子，故平坦。有限模的解析基变换就是普通张量积，因此同一论证在任意解析基变换后仍成立。这证明了所称的 \'etale 性与解析平坦性。}

\bi{The forward implication follows from the local finite flat definition of an \'etale morphism and the same finite-branch stalk identity.}
{正向断言来自 \'etale 态射的局部有限平坦定义，以及同一个有限分支的茎恒等式。}
\end{proof}
