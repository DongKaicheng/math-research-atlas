\begin{lemma}[\bi{Stable extension of a nodal generic curve}
{结点泛曲线的稳定延拓}]
\label{cand:stablenodalgenericextension}
\bi{Let $K$ be a complete nontrivially valued characteristic-zero
field, and let $Y/K$ be a stable curve of genus $g\geq2$.
There is a finite Galois extension $L/K$, with the original
absolute value uniquely extended, and a projective stable
model $\mathcal Y/L^\circ$ carrying the canonical semilinear
$\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$-action on its generic fibre.
After enlarging $L$ finitely and Galois, all special nodes admit
split pointed charts over $L^\circ$.}
{设 $K$ 为完备非平凡赋值的特征零域，$Y/K$ 为亏格
$g\geq2$ 的稳定曲线。存在配备原绝对值唯一延拓的有限
Galois 扩张 $L/K$，以及射影稳定模型 $\mathcal Y/L^\circ$，
其上带有泛纤维典范半线性 $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$-作用的延拓。
将 $L$ 再作有限 Galois 扩张后，可使全部特殊结点在
$L^\circ$ 上具有分裂带点坐标。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Apply Lemma~\ref{cand:properstackfinitevaluation} to the
$K$-object $Y$ of the proper stack
$\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,K^\circ}$,
using \cite[Tags 0E9C, 0CL6]{StacksAlg}.
This gives a stable family after a finite extension.
The projectivity argument in the proof of
Theorem~\ref{cand:stablevaluationextension} applies without change:
Tag 0E74 makes the dualizing line bundle relatively ample over
an open neighborhood of the closed point of the local base;
that neighborhood is the whole base. Tags 0D32 and 0B5N then give
a projective scheme. This argument uses stability of the fibres,
not smoothness of the generic fibre.}
{对 proper 模叠 $\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,K^\circ}$ 的
$K$-对象 $Y$ 应用 Lemma~\ref{cand:properstackfinitevaluation}，
其中使用 \cite[Tags 0E9C, 0CL6]{StacksAlg}，得到有限扩域后的
稳定族。Theorem~\ref{cand:stablevaluationextension} 的证明中
射影性论证原样适用：Tag 0E74 使对偶化线丛在局部基底闭点的
某个开邻域上相对 ample，而该邻域就是整个基底。
随后 Tags 0D32 与 0B5N 给出射影概形。
此论证使用纤维的稳定性，并不要求泛纤维光滑。}

\bi{Take a finite normal closure as in
Corollary~\ref{cand:stablecompletevaluation}; characteristic zero
makes it Galois, and uniqueness of the absolute-value extension
preserves the specified valuation. Apply
Lemma~\ref{cand:stableisomvaluation} to the semilinear twists of
the model. Its statement already permits nodal generic fibres.
The twist construction and uniqueness argument of
Corollary~\ref{cand:stablegaloisaction} therefore supply the group
action and its composition law. Finally use
Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting} and take a further
normal closure. Stability persists under this base change; the
same uniqueness argument supplies the action of the enlarged group.
Parameters $a=0$ are permitted in the pointed charts.}
{如 Corollary~\ref{cand:stablecompletevaluation}，取有限正规包；
特征零使其成为 Galois 扩张，绝对值延拓的唯一性保持指定赋值。
对模型的半线性扭曲应用 Lemma~\ref{cand:stableisomvaluation}；
它的陈述已经允许结点泛纤维。因此
Corollary~\ref{cand:stablegaloisaction} 的扭曲构造和唯一性论证
给出群作用与复合律。最后使用
Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting}，再取正规包。
此基变换保持稳定性，同一个唯一性论证给出扩大后的群作用。
带点坐标中允许参数 $a=0$。}
\end{proof}
