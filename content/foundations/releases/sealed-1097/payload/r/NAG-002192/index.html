NAG-002192 · Accepted whole

A basis-independent diagonal operator

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 886

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A basis-independent diagonal operator}
{与格基无关的对角线算子}]
\label{cand:diagonalcorneroperator}
\bi{For every balanced cycle $Z$ in
$N_{\mathbf R}\times N_{\mathbf R}$, the cycle
$\mathcal D_{xy}Z$ is balanced, is supported in
$|Z|\cap\Delta$, and is independent of the chosen basis of
$N^\vee$. Interchanging $x$ and $y$ preserves this operator.}
{对 $N_{\mathbf R}\times N_{\mathbf R}$ 中每个平衡循环 $Z$，
循环 $\mathcal D_{xy}Z$ 平衡，支集包含于 $|Z|\cap\Delta$，
且与 $N^\vee$ 的格基选择无关。交换 $x,y$ 保持此算子。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{By Theorem~\ref{cand:cornerbalancecommute}, successive
corners are balanced and their order does not matter. A corner
by $\max(0,\ell)$ is supported where $\ell=0$, since off that
hyperplane the function is affine. It follows inductively that
the final support lies in $\Delta$.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{cand:cornerbalancecommute}，逐次角点平衡
且次序无关。对 $\max(0,\ell)$ 取角点后，支集包含于
$\ell=0$，因为在该超平面之外函数仿射。归纳可知最终支集
包含于 $\Delta$。}

\bi{Every change of integral basis is a composition of row
interchanges, sign changes, and addition of an integer multiple
of one row to another. Row interchanges use commutation of
corners. A sign change replaces $\max(0,\ell)$ by
$\max(0,-\ell)=\max(0,\ell)-\ell$, which has the same corner
operator. For a row operation $\ell_i\mapsto\ell_i+a\ell_j$,
perform the $j$-th corner first. Its support is contained in
$\ell_j=0$, where the old and new $i$-th functions coincide.
Restriction locality, Lemma~\ref{cand:cornerproductlocality},
proves equality. The elementary row operations generate
$\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbf Z)$ by Lemma~\ref{cand:elementarylatticesplitting}, so this
proves basis independence. Interchanging $x,y$ changes each
linear form to its negative and is already covered.}
{每个整格基变换均由行交换、变号及将一行的整数倍加到另一
行生成。行交换使用角点交换性。变号将 $\max(0,\ell)$
替换为 $\max(0,-\ell)=\max(0,\ell)-\ell$，角点算子不变。
对行操作 $\ell_i\mapsto\ell_i+a\ell_j$，先执行第 $j$
个角点；其支集位于 $\ell_j=0$，在此处新的第 $i$ 个函数
与原函数相同。由 Lemma~\ref{cand:cornerproductlocality}
的限制局部性，算子相同。Lemma~\ref{cand:elementarylatticesplitting}
说明这些初等行操作
生成 $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbf Z)$，从而得到格基独立性。
交换 $x,y$ 使每个线性形式变号，已包含在上述讨论中。}
\end{proof}
