Module sheaves on a rational slice

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 371

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Module sheaves on a rational slice}{Rational slice 上的模层}]
\label{fnd:generalizedrationalslicemodules}
\bi{Let $X=\mathcal M(A)$ and let $\mathcal R_X$ be its arbitrary-radius finite rational site. For $V\in\mathcal R_X$ and a finite $A_V$-module $P$, put}
{设 $X=\mathcal M(A)$，令 $\mathcal R_X$ 为其任意半径的有限 rational site。对 $V\in\mathcal R_X$ 及有限 $A_V$-模 $P$，记}
\[
 \widetilde P_V(W)=A_W\otimes_{A_V}P
 \qquad(W\in\mathcal R_X, W\subseteq V).
\]
\bi{This is a sheaf on the slice $\mathcal R_X/V$. The functor $P\mapsto\widetilde P_V$ is exact and fully faithful, its sections over $V$ are $P$, and it commutes with restriction to smaller rational objects. The augmented \v Cech complex for every finite covering in this slice is norm-strict exact in all degrees.}
{它是 slice $\mathcal R_X/V$ 上的层。函子 $P\mapsto\widetilde P_V$ 正合且全忠实，在 $V$ 上的截面为 $P$，并与向较小 rational 对象的限制相容。对该 slice 中的每个有限覆盖，其增广 \v Cech 复形在所有次数均为 norm-strict 正合。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A rational object $W\subseteq V$ is rational over $A_V$ with algebra $A_W$: pull back its original defining tuple and apply uniqueness in Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal}. Finite intersections have the same identifications. Applying Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfiniterationalcech} over $A_V$, and then over each $A_W$, proves the sheaf and \v Cech assertions. Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalflatness} proves exactness. At $V$ the value is $P$. A sheaf homomorphism is determined by its map on $P$, since its value on $b\otimes p$ must be $b$ times the restriction of that map on $p$. Conversely every $A_V$-linear map defines these maps on all objects. Ordinary tensor associativity proves the restriction assertion, with the bounded finite-module comparisons of Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor}.}
{Rational 对象 $W\subseteq V$ 在 $A_V$ 上仍为 rational，且代数是 $A_W$：拉回它原来的定义 tuple，再应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal} 的唯一性即可。有限交叠具有相同识别。在 $A_V$ 上，再在每个 $A_W$ 上应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfiniterationalcech}，得到层性及 \v Cech 断言。Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalflatness} 给出正合性。在 $V$ 上其值为 $P$。一个层同态由其在 $P$ 上的映射决定，因为它在 $b\otimes p$ 上的值必须等于 $b$ 乘以该映射在 $p$ 上取值的限制。反之，每个 $A_V$-线性映射均由这些公式定义全部对象上的映射。普通张量积结合律给出限制相容性；相应有界比较由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor} 保证。}
\end{proof}
