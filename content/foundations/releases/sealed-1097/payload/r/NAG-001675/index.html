Approximating fibre functions by original coefficients

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 436

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Approximating fibre functions by original coefficients}{以原系数逼近纤维函数}]
\label{fnd:originalcoefficientfibredensity}
\bi{Let $A\to B$ be a bounded homomorphism of generalized $K$-affinoid algebras, let $x\in\mathcal M(A)$, put $F=\mathcal H(x)$ and $C=B\widehat\otimes_A F$. For finitely many $c_1,\ldots,c_m\in C$ and $\varepsilon>0$, there are a rational neighbourhood $U$ of $x$ in $\mathcal M(A)$ and elements $b_1,\ldots,b_m\in B\widehat\otimes_A A_U$ whose images in $C$ differ from the $c_i$ by norm less than $\varepsilon$. The domain $U$ may be chosen inside any prescribed ordinary neighbourhood of $x$.}
{设 $A\to B$ 为 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数之间的有界同态，$x\in\mathcal M(A)$，并令 $F=\mathcal H(x)$、$C=B\widehat\otimes_A F$。给定有限个 $c_1,\ldots,c_m\in C$ 及 $\varepsilon>0$，存在 $x$ 在 $\mathcal M(A)$ 中的 rational 邻域 $U$，以及 $b_1,\ldots,b_m\in B\widehat\otimes_A A_U$，使它们在 $C$ 中的像与各 $c_i$ 的差的范数小于 $\varepsilon$。还可要求 $U$ 包含于预先指定的 $x$ 的普通邻域。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The image of $B\otimes_A F$ is dense in its completed tensor product. Approximate each $c_i$ first by a finite sum $\sum_j\beta_{ij}\otimes\lambda_{ij}$. By the definition of the completed residue field, $\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$ is dense in $F$. Approximate all the finitely many $\lambda_{ij}$ by fractions $a_{ij}(x)/d_{ij}(x)$ with $a_{ij},d_{ij}\in A$ and $d_{ij}(x)\ne0$. Their errors can be chosen small enough that the finite sums have error less than $\varepsilon$, since the completed tensor seminorm is bounded by the product of the two factor norms.}
{代数张量积 $B\otimes_A F$ 的像在其完备张量积中稠密。先用有限和 $\sum_j\beta_{ij}\otimes\lambda_{ij}$ 逼近各 $c_i$。由完备剩余域的定义，$\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$ 在 $F$ 中稠密。因此可同时用分式 $a_{ij}(x)/d_{ij}(x)$ 逼近有限个 $\lambda_{ij}$，其中 $a_{ij},d_{ij}\in A$ 且 $d_{ij}(x)\ne0$。完备张量半范数不超过两因子范数之积，所以可将各系数误差取得充分小，使有限和的总误差小于 $\varepsilon$。}

\bi{Choose positive numbers $r_{ij}<|d_{ij}(x)|$. The finitely many Laurent conditions $|d_{ij}|\geq r_{ij}$ define a rational neighbourhood of $x$ on which all denominators are invertible. Intersect with a rational neighbourhood inside the prescribed ordinary neighbourhood, using Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictrationalinteriorneighborhood} and rational transitivity. On the resulting $U$, set $b_i=\sum_j\beta_{ij}a_{ij}/d_{ij}$. Completed base-change associativity identifies the fibre algebra over $x$ with the original $C$, and the preceding estimates give the assertion.}
{选择正数 $r_{ij}<|d_{ij}(x)|$。有限个 Laurent 条件 $|d_{ij}|\geq r_{ij}$ 定义 $x$ 的 rational 邻域，所有分母在其上可逆。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictrationalinteriorneighborhood} 与 rational 域的传递性，再与包含于指定普通邻域的 rational 邻域相交。对所得的 $U$，令 $b_i=\sum_j\beta_{ij}a_{ij}/d_{ij}$。完备基变换的结合性将 $x$ 上的纤维代数识别为原来的 $C$，上述估计遂给出结论。}
\end{proof}
