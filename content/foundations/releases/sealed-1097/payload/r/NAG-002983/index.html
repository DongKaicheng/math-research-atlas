\begin{proposition}[The actual finite-family CP application; F8ZI-CP]
Assume the target hypotheses above and, pending review of ZP, its
zero-space-purity conclusion. Choose the finite presenting affinoids
$V_i\subset U$ for a fixed ordinary test, with representatives
$\eta|_{V_i}=t_{g_i}^*\alpha_i$, where $t_g=-\log|g|$.
For each nonempty actual intersection $V_I$, choose an index $i(I)\in I$
and restrict that unit tuple and representative. There exists a real
$B$ such that, for every $a<b<B$, the actual annulus
\[
 A_I[a,b]=V_I\cap\{e^a\leq|f|\leq e^b\}
\]
satisfies CP for this chosen representative:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:CP}
 \TT_{(t_{g_{i(I)}},\,\log|f|)}(\OO_{A_I[a,b]})
       =\TT_{g_{i(I)}}(\OO_{D_I})\times[a,b],
       \qquad D_I=V(f)\cap V_I.
\end{equation}
The notation on the left denotes the image of the actual joint moment
chain under reflection of its final coordinate. The equality includes
full quotient coefficients and saturated tangent lattices. An empty
$D_I$ gives an empty annulus after the common bound is decreased.
Any further \emph{finite} list of joint tuples needed on actual
overlaps can be included before choosing $B$.
\end{proposition}
