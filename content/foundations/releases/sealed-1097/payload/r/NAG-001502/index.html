NAG-001502 · Accepted whole

Transitivity of strict rational presentations

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 214

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Transitivity of strict rational presentations}{Strict rational 表示的传递性}]\label{fnd:strictrationaltransitivity}
\bi{Let $A$ be a strict norm-admissible $K$-affinoid algebra. Let $B$ be a rational presentation over $A$, and let $C$ be a rational presentation over $B$, with all radii in $|K^\times|$. Then there is a rational presentation $D$ over $A$ and a unique bounded $A$-algebra isomorphism $D\simeq C$ with bounded inverse. Under the point homeomorphisms of Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology}, the image of $\mathcal M(C)$ in $\mathcal M(A)$ is precisely the rational subset represented by $D$.}
{设 $A$ 为 strict norm-admissible $K$-affinoid 代数，$B$ 为 $A$ 上的 rational 表示，$C$ 为 $B$ 上的 rational 表示，所有半径均属于 $|K^\times|$。则存在 $A$ 上的 rational 表示 $D$，以及唯一的有界 $A$-代数同构 $D\simeq C$，其逆也有界。通过 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology} 的谱点同胚，$\mathcal M(C)$ 在 $\mathcal M(A)$ 中的像恰好是 $D$ 表示的 rational 子集。}

\bi{If the valuation of $K$ is nontrivial, $D$ can be chosen so that this isomorphism is isometric for the given actual presentation norms. For trivial valuation, the same holds after replacing the norm on $A$ by a defining strict quotient norm and using the induced quotient norms on the presentations; for the original norms the conclusion is a bounded isomorphism in both directions. The construction and conclusion allow zero quotients.}
{若 $K$ 的赋值非平凡，可选择 $D$ 使该同构对给定的实际表示范数等距。若赋值平凡，则先将 $A$ 的范数替换为定义 strict 商范数，并在各表示上使用诱导商范数，便得到相同的等距结论；对于原范数，结论是两个方向都有界的同构。构造及结论均允许零商。}

\bi{For every finite canonical $A$-module $M$, the resulting identifications}
{对每个有限 canonical $A$-模 $M$，所得识别}
\[
 D\otimes_A M\ \simeq\ C\otimes_A M
       \ \simeq\ C\otimes_B(B\otimes_A M)
\]
\bi{are natural bounded isomorphisms with bounded inverses. The corresponding assertions hold with separated completed tensors. With norms from one fixed finite free presentation of $M$, the module comparisons are isometric whenever the algebra comparison is isometric. This completed-tensor assertion concerns these finite modules.}
{为自然的有界同构，且逆映射有界。将张量积换成 separated completed tensors 后，同样成立。若范数来自 $M$ 的同一个固定有限自由表示，则代数比较等距时，模比较也等距。这里关于 completed tensor 的断言限于这些有限模。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{By the weighted change of variables in Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictweightedquotient}, reduce every radius to one. This change is isometric. Write}
{由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictweightedquotient} 中的加权变量变换，将每个半径化为一；该变换等距。写成}
\[
 B=A\langle f/g\rangle,
 \qquad C=B\langle h/k\rangle,
 \qquad h=(h_1,\ldots,h_t).
\]
\bi{If $A=0$ or $B=0$, then $C=0$; the rational presentation $A\{V\}/(0V-1)$ is zero and gives the conclusion, including the module assertions. We may therefore assume $A,B\ne0$. Choose certificates}
{若 $A=0$ 或 $B=0$，则 $C=0$；rational 表示 $A\{V\}/(0V-1)$ 为零，给出所需结论及模的断言。因此以下可设 $A,B\ne0$。选择证书}
\[
 c_0g+\sum_i c_if_i=1,
 \qquad b_0k+\sum_{j=1}^t b_jh_j=1,
\]
\bi{and define $L=\max(1,\|c_i\|)$ and $H=\max(1,\|b_j\|)$. Choose $0<\eta<H^{-1}$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalfractiondensity} supplies a common $N$ and $p_0,\ldots,p_t\in A$ such that}
{并令 $L=\max(1,\|c_i\|)$、$H=\max(1,\|b_j\|)$。选择 $0<\eta<H^{-1}$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalfractiondensity}，存在共同的 $N$ 及 $p_0,\ldots,p_t\in A$，使}
\[
 a'_0=g^{-N}p_0,\qquad a'_j=g^{-N}p_j,
 \qquad \|k-a'_0\|<\eta,\quad \|h_j-a'_j\|<\eta.
\]
\bi{Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationaltuplestability} gives a unit-ideal certificate for $(a'_j)$ with coefficient norms at most $H$, and an isometric $B$-algebra identification}
{由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationaltuplestability}，$(a'_j)$ 具有系数范数不超过 $H$ 的单位理想证书，并有等距的 $B$-代数识别}
\[
 C\simeq C'=B\langle a'_1/a'_0,\ldots,a'_t/a'_0\rangle.
\]
\bi{If $K$ is nontrivially valued, powers of a scalar of absolute value less than one give $\gamma\in K^\times$ with $|\gamma|L^{N+1}H\leq1$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalfractionflattening} then gives the required $D$, with denominator $gp_0$ and numerator list}
{若 $K$ 的赋值非平凡，取绝对值小于一的标量的高次幂，便得到满足 $|\gamma|L^{N+1}H\leq1$ 的 $\gamma\in K^\times$。此时由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalfractionflattening} 得到所需 $D$，其分母为 $gp_0$，分子列表为}
\[
 (p_0f_i)_{1\leq i\leq s},\qquad
 (gp_j)_{1\leq j\leq t},\qquad \gamma.
\]
\bi{Both comparisons are isometric, so $D\simeq C$ is isometric. Notice that adding $\gamma$ is indispensable to this construction: clearing denominators only proves a unit-ideal statement in $B$, and does not establish $(p_0,\ldots,p_t)=A$.}
{两个比较均等距，故 $D\simeq C$ 等距。这里添入 $\gamma$ 是该构造的重要部分：消去分母只在 $B$ 中给出单位理想断言，不能由此推出 $(p_0,\ldots,p_t)=A$。}

\bi{For trivial valuation, replace the norm on $A$ by a defining strict quotient norm. Uniformly equivalent coefficient norms give uniformly equivalent Tate and quotient norms, with the same underlying complete algebras: the coefficient convergence condition is unchanged, and taking infima over representatives preserves both norm comparisons. By Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalfractiondensity}, every nonzero element of $A$ and $B$ now has norm one. Hence $L=H=1$, the approximations may be exact, and $\gamma=1$ meets the required inequality. The same two lemmas give an isometric comparison for these norms. Returning to the original uniformly equivalent norms gives the asserted bounded inverse maps.}
{赋值平凡时，将 $A$ 的范数替换为定义 strict 商范数。一致等价的系数范数给出一致等价的 Tate 范数及商范数，并保持底层完备代数不变：系数收敛条件不变，而对代表元取下确界保留两个方向的范数比较。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalfractiondensity}，此时 $A$ 与 $B$ 中每个非零元素范数为一。因此 $L=H=1$，逼近可取为等式，且 $\gamma=1$ 满足所需不等式。同样的两个引理便给出这些范数下的等距比较。再回到原来一致等价的范数，即得到所述有界互逆映射。}

\bi{The isomorphism is over $A$. Applying Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology} first to $A\to B$ and then to $B\to C$ identifies $\mathcal M(C)$ homeomorphically with its image in $\mathcal M(A)$. The same theorem for $A\to D$, together with the constructed isomorphism, identifies this image with the rational subset of the displayed tuple. This proves the point assertion from the algebra comparison; no spectral-radius or maximum principle is needed in this proof. Any bounded $A$-algebra endomorphism of $C$ restricts on $B$ to its unique bounded rational factorization and then has its values on the second-stage variables forced by their unit denominator. Thus it is the identity. The same argument after composing with the constructed inverse proves the asserted uniqueness. Different choices of approximations and of $\gamma$ are consequently compared by unique bounded $A$-algebra maps.}
{所得同构在 $A$ 上相容。先对 $A\to B$、再对 $B\to C$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology}，便将 $\mathcal M(C)$ 同胚识别为它在 $\mathcal M(A)$ 中的像。将同一定理应用于 $A\to D$，并使用已经构造的代数同构，即将该像与所列 tuple 的 rational 子集识别。因此谱点断言来自代数比较；这个证明不需要 spectral-radius 或 maximum principle。$C$ 的任意有界 $A$-代数自同态在 $B$ 上必须等于唯一的有界 rational 分解，随后第二层变量的像又被单位分母强制确定，因此该自同态为恒等映射。将任意待比较映射与已构造的逆映射复合，同一论证便给出所述唯一性。因此，不同逼近及不同 $\gamma$ 的选择之间，也由唯一的有界 $A$-代数映射比较。}

\bi{For the module assertion choose $A^v\xrightarrow{F}A^w\to M\to0$. Ordinary tensor associativity, \cite[Tag 00D2, Lemma~10.12.7]{StacksAlg}, gives $C\otimes_B(B\otimes_A M)\simeq C\otimes_A M$, sending $c\otimes(b\otimes m)$ to $cb\otimes m$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} identifies the successive relation-quotient module with $C^w/F_CC^v$, with its quotient norm. The algebra comparison gives a bounded inverse comparison with $D^w/F_DD^v$, and it is isometric if the coefficient comparison is isometric. All these maps agree on the images of the chosen generators and are therefore natural in $M$. Finally, Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} and Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitetensor} identify each finite ordinary tensor here with its separated completion. No passage from algebraic flatness to an assertion about arbitrary completed tensors is involved.}
{对模的断言，选择 $A^v\xrightarrow{F}A^w\to M\to0$。普通张量积的结合律，即 \cite[Tag 00D2, Lemma~10.12.7]{StacksAlg}，给出 $C\otimes_B(B\otimes_A M)\simeq C\otimes_A M$，将 $c\otimes(b\otimes m)$ 送到 $cb\otimes m$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule}，逐次关系商模与配备商范数的 $C^w/F_CC^v$ 识别。代数比较给出它与 $D^w/F_DD^v$ 之间的有界互逆比较；若系数比较等距，则模比较也等距。这些映射在所选生成元的像上相同，因而对 $M$ 自然。最后，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} 及 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitetensor}，这里每个有限普通张量积均与其 separated completion 识别。证明没有从代数平坦性推断任意 completed tensor 的性质。}
\end{proof}
