Spreading a closed neighborhood of a generic fibre

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 481

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Spreading a closed neighborhood of a generic fibre}{泛纤维闭邻域的延展}]
\label{fnd:generalgenericfibreneighborhood}
\bi{Let $Y\to X$ be a morphism of good analytic spaces over any complete field, smooth at $y$, and put $x=f(y)$. If $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is Artinian, then every Zariski-closed subset of $Y$ containing an ordinary neighborhood of $y$ in $Y_x$ contains an ordinary neighborhood of $y$ in $Y$.}
{设 $Y\to X$ 为任意完备域上 good 解析空间之间的态射，在 $y$ 处光滑，并令 $x=f(y)$。若 $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 为 Artinian 环，则 $Y$ 中每个包含 $y$ 在 $Y_x$ 中的普通邻域的 Zariski 闭子集，都包含 $y$ 在 $Y$ 中的普通邻域。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First reduce the base. On an ordinary affinoid neighborhood $\mathcal M(A)$ of $x$, put $\mathfrak p=\ker(x)$, $D=A_{\mathfrak p}$ and $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$. The faithfully flat map $D\to R$ and the Artinian hypothesis imply that $D$ is zero-dimensional. Its reduction is a field. If $N$ is the nilradical of $A$, Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedordinaryregularcomparison}, after quotienting by the nilradical of $D$, makes $R/NR$ geometrically regular over that field. It is also local Artinian, hence is a field. The nilpotent closed base change $X_{\rm red}\to X$ and its pullback to $Y$ are homeomorphisms and do not alter the analytic fibre. Thus it suffices to treat the case in which $R$ is a field.}
{首先约化底空间。在 $x$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域 $\mathcal M(A)$ 上，令 $\mathfrak p=\ker(x)$、$D=A_{\mathfrak p}$、$R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$。忠实平坦映射 $D\to R$ 与 Artinian 假设说明 $D$ 零维，所以其约化为域。若 $N$ 为 $A$ 的幂零根，则在除去 $D$ 的幂零根后，Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedordinaryregularcomparison} 说明 $R/NR$ 在该域上几何正则。它又为局部 Artinian 环，故为域。幂零闭基变换 $X_{\rm red}\to X$ 及其到 $Y$ 的拉回，均为同胚且不改变解析纤维。因此只须处理 $R$ 为域的情形。}

\bi{Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalnormalcomparisonlocus} gives a normal ordinary neighborhood of $x$. Normality ascends along the smooth map by \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:smoothordinarynormalascent]{smooth normality ascent}}. Choose an ordinary affinoid neighborhood $Y_0$ of $y$ and its normal integral component through $y$, using Lemma~\ref{fnd:normalcomponentidentity}. Write the closed subset there as $V(h_1,\ldots,h_s)$. The identity principle reduces the assertion to finding a nonempty ordinary open subset of $Y_0$ on which every $h_j$ vanishes. Restrict inside the ordinary interior of $Y_0$ and the locus where the relative dimension of the smooth map is constant.}
{由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalnormalcomparisonlocus}，$x$ 有正规普通邻域。\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:smoothordinarynormalascent]{光滑态射的正规性上升}}说明正规性沿该光滑映射上升。利用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:normalcomponentidentity}，选择 $y$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域 $Y_0$，并取其中经过 $y$ 的正规整分量。将给定闭子集在此处写成 $V(h_1,\ldots,h_s)$。恒等原理将问题归约为：寻找 $Y_0$ 的非空普通开子集，使全部 $h_j$ 在其上消失。限制到 $Y_0$ 的普通内部及光滑态射相对维数恒定的点集。}

\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalresidueproductpatch} applies to the nonempty ordinary fibre open set in the hypothesis. It gives a finite flat base change $X'\to U$, with a unique point $x'$ above $x$ and reduced ordinary closed fibre there, and a nonempty ordinary open product patch in $Y'=Y\times_XX'$. Its factors are an ordinary base neighborhood $U'$ of $x'$, an open annulus $D$ or a point, and an open polydisc $\Delta$; its fibre at $x'$ lies in the given closed subset. The ordinary ring $R'=\mathcal O_{X',x'}$ is finite over the field $R$, by finite branch separation and the uniqueness of the fibre point. Its ordinary closed fibre is itself, and is reduced. Thus $R'$ is a field. Apply Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalnormalcomparisonlocus} at $x'$ and then Lemma~\ref{fnd:normalcomponentidentity} to choose a normal integral affinoid ordinary neighborhood $\mathcal M(A')\subset U'$. The map}
{对假设中的非空普通纤维开集应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalresidueproductpatch}。它给出有限平坦基变换 $X'\to U$，其中 $x$ 上方只有一点 $x'$，且该点的普通闭纤维约化；同时得到 $Y'=Y\times_XX'$ 中一个非空普通乘积开片。其因子为 $x'$ 的普通底邻域 $U'$、一个开圆环 $D$ 或一点，以及一个开多圆盘 $\Delta$；它在 $x'$ 处的纤维位于所给闭子集中。有限分支分离及纤维点的唯一性说明普通环 $R'=\mathcal O_{X',x'}$ 在域 $R$ 上有限。它的普通闭纤维就是自身，且约化，故 $R'$ 为域。在 $x'$ 处应用 Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalnormalcomparisonlocus}，再用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:normalcomponentidentity}，选择正规整 affinoid 普通邻域 $\mathcal M(A')\subset U'$。映射}
\[
                       A'\longrightarrow R'\longrightarrow\mathcal H(x')
\]
\bi{is injective: its first map is flat and nonzero with integral source, and its second is the evaluation of a field germ.}
{单射：前一映射平坦且非零，其源为整环；后一映射是域芽的取值映射。}

\bi{Restrict each $h_j$ to $\mathcal M(A')\times_KD\times_K\Delta$. The weighted Laurent and Tate expansions on closed subannuli and closed subpolydiscs give unique coefficients in $A'$. They agree under all restrictions, so we may write}
{将各 $h_j$ 限制到 $\mathcal M(A')\times_KD\times_K\Delta$。在闭子圆环及闭子多圆盘上，加权 Laurent 与 Tate 展开给出 $A'$ 中的唯一系数；这些系数在全部限制下保持一致，因此可写成}
\[
                 h_j=\sum_{(a,I)\in\mathbf Z^m\times\mathbf N^{n-m}}
                              c_{a,I,j}S^aT^I,\qquad m\in\{0,1\}.
\]
\bi{The fibre function vanishes at every point of $D_{\mathcal H(x')}\times\Delta_{\mathcal H(x')}$. Evaluation at its Gauss points, with radii in the corresponding intervals, makes every coefficient $c_{a,I,j}(x')$ zero. The displayed injection therefore makes all coefficients zero in $A'$ simultaneously. Every $h_j$ vanishes on the whole product, and hence on a nonempty ordinary open subset of $Y'$. By Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteflatordinaryopen}, its image in $Y_0$ is nonempty and ordinary-open. The functions vanish on this image, and the identity principle makes them zero throughout $Y_0$. This proves the assertion.}
{纤维函数在 $D_{\mathcal H(x')}\times\Delta_{\mathcal H(x')}$ 的每一点均为零。取半径位于相应区间内的 Gauss 点进行取值，便知每个系数 $c_{a,I,j}(x')$ 为零。上式的单射性使全部系数在 $A'$ 中同时为零。因此各 $h_j$ 在整个乘积上消失，继而在 $Y'$ 的某个非空普通开子集上消失。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteflatordinaryopen}，它在 $Y_0$ 中的像非空且普通开。各函数在该像上为零，恒等原理于是使它们在整个 $Y_0$ 上为零，证明完成。}
\end{proof}
