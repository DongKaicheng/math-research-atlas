A rational basis for strict-test domains over any complete field

\begin{corollary}[\bi{A rational basis for strict-test domains over any complete field}{任意完备域上 strict-test 域的 rational 基}]\label{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis}
\bi{Let $K$ now be any complete real-valued non-Archimedean field, with its fixed absolute value, possibly trivial. Let $A\to B$ be a strict-test affinoid-domain presentation of $U\subseteq\mathcal M(A)$. Then $U$ has a finite covering by rational subsets $R_1,\ldots,R_t$ defined over the original $A$, with unit radii or, equivalently after scaling, radii in $|K^\times|$. For each $R_i$, and for each intersection $R$ indexed by a nonempty finite list of these members, there is a canonical bounded isomorphism}
{现在允许 $K$ 为任意完备实值非阿基米德域，固定其给定绝对值，并允许该绝对值平凡。设 $A\to B$ 为 $U\subseteq\mathcal M(A)$ 的 strict-test affinoid-domain 表示。则 $U$ 具有有限覆盖 $R_1,\ldots,R_t$，各成员在原始 $A$ 上为 rational，并采用单位半径，或等价地在缩放后采用 $|K^\times|$ 中的半径。对每个 $R_i$，以及由这些成员的任意非空有限指标列所指的交 $R$，存在典范有界同构}
\[
 B\widehat\otimes_A A_R\ \simeq\ A_R
\]
\bi{with bounded inverse. It identifies $R$ as a rational domain over $B$ with the same algebra $A_R$. All identifications commute with the restriction maps between these members and intersections. Empty resulting intersections have algebra zero; the empty domain uses the empty covering. This statement concerns finite rational coverings of the domain, and does not assert ambient local Weierstrass for trivial valuation.}
{且其逆有界。该同构将 $R$ 识别为 $B$ 上的 rational 域，并使用同一个代数 $A_R$。全部识别都与这些成员及交之间的限制映射相容。若所得交为空，其代数为零；空域使用空覆盖。此陈述涉及域自身的有限 rational 覆盖，并不对平凡赋值断言环境局部 Weierstrass 性。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{If $U$ is empty, the point correspondence and Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} give $B=0$, and the empty covering suffices. For nontrivial valuation, Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictgerritzengrauert} supplies a finite original-$A$ rational covering of $U$. For trivial valuation, Theorem~\ref{fnd:trivialstrictdomainrationalcover} supplies one. Thus in both cases the members have the asserted scope.}
{若 $U$ 为空，则由点的对应及 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}，有 $B=0$，使用空覆盖即可。赋值非平凡时，Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictgerritzengrauert} 给出 $U$ 的有限原始 $A$ 上 rational 覆盖。赋值平凡时，Theorem~\ref{fnd:trivialstrictdomainrationalcover} 给出这样的覆盖。因此，两种情形中的成员都具有所述范围。}

\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation} makes every indicated finite intersection rational over $A$. It is contained in $U$. Apply Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalrestrictiondomainbridge} to this inclusion: the image of its unit-ideal tuple in $B$ defines the same domain, and the actual completed pushout is $A_R$. This gives the displayed bounded isomorphisms for both valuation cases without using an intersection theorem for arbitrary strict-test domains. Their compatibility follows from the same lemma, or directly from uniqueness in the strict-test factorization property: after a further restriction, the two maps from $B$ extend the same map from $A$, and the rational variables are prescribed. No arbitrary Banach completed-tensor exactness is used.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation}，所指的每个有限交都在 $A$ 上为 rational，并且包含在 $U$ 中。对这个包含关系应用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalrestrictiondomainbridge}：其单位理想 tuple 在 $B$ 中的像定义同一个域，实际 completed pushout 就是 $A_R$。由此在两种赋值情形中均得到所列有界同构，而不使用任意 strict-test 域的交定理。相容性来自同一引理，也可直接由 strict-test 分解性质中的唯一性得到：进一步限制后，两个从 $B$ 出发的映射延拓同一个从 $A$ 出发的映射，而 rational 变量已有指定的像。这里没有使用任意 Banach 完备张量积的正合性。}
\end{proof}
