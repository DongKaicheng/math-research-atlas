Finite rank stabilization over descending fields

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 340

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Finite rank stabilization over descending fields}{下降域族上的有限秩稳定}]
\label{fnd:descendingfieldrank}
\bi{Let $(F_\lambda)$ be a downward directed family of subfields of a field $L$, and put $F_\infty=\bigcap_\lambda F_\lambda$. For every finite family $v_1,\ldots,v_d\in L$, its rank over $F_\infty$ equals its rank over some $F_{\lambda_0}$. In particular, a family independent over $F_\infty$ is independent over one member of the family.}
{设 $(F_\lambda)$ 为域 $L$ 中向下有向的子域族，并令 $F_\infty=\bigcap_\lambda F_\lambda$。对任意有限族 $v_1,\ldots,v_d\in L$，其在 $F_\infty$ 上的秩等于其在某个 $F_{\lambda_0}$ 上的秩。特别地，在 $F_\infty$ 上线性无关的有限族，在该域族的某个成员上已经线性无关。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose $\lambda_0$ at which the integer rank is maximal, and choose from the $v_i$ a basis $w_1,\ldots,w_r$ of their span over $F_{\lambda_0}$. For every $\lambda$ with $F_\lambda\subset F_{\lambda_0}$, these elements stay independent, and maximality makes them a basis of the span over $F_\lambda$. The coefficients expressing any $v_i$ in this basis are unique among coefficient tuples in $F_{\lambda_0}$, hence independent of $\lambda$, and lie in every such $F_\lambda$. This subfamily is cofinal by downward directedness, so those coefficients lie in $F_\infty$. Thus the same $w_j$ span over $F_\infty$ and remain independent over it.}
{选择使整数秩达到最大值的 $\lambda_0$，并从 $v_i$ 中选出其在 $F_{\lambda_0}$ 上张成空间的基 $w_1,\ldots,w_r$。对每个满足 $F_\lambda\subset F_{\lambda_0}$ 的 $\lambda$，这些元素仍线性无关，而秩的极大性使它们仍为 $F_\lambda$ 上张成空间的基。任意 $v_i$ 在此基下的系数在取值于 $F_{\lambda_0}$ 的系数组中唯一，故不依赖 $\lambda$，并属于所有这些 $F_\lambda$。由向下有向性，此子族共尾，所以各系数属于 $F_\infty$。因此同一组 $w_j$ 在 $F_\infty$ 上张成原族，且仍线性无关。}
\end{proof}
