Separation of analytic germs

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 573

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Separation of analytic germs}
{解析芽的分离性}]
\label{fnd:gradedgermseparation}
\bi{A morphism of analytic germs is separated if and only if
its reduction is separated. In particular, an analytic germ
whose reduction is affine admits a compact separated
neighbourhood representative.}
{解析芽态射分离，当且仅当其约化分离。特别地，约化仿射的
解析芽具有紧分离邻域代表。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{\emph{The diagonal is locally a closed immersion into a
subdomain.} Choose finite affinoid subdomain-germ covers of
source and target, with each source piece $Y_i$ mapping to
one target piece $X_i$. In $Z=Y\times_XY$ the spaces
$Z_i=Y_i\times_{X_i}Y_i$ are analytic domains.
On $Z_i$ the diagonal is given by the admissible quotient
$B_i\widehat\otimes_{A_i}B_i\twoheadrightarrow B_i$;
the bounded section $b\mapsto b\otimes1$ proves admissibility.
The finitely generated kernel ideals agree on overlaps:
after a common affinoid refinement they are the kernels
of the same restricted quotient. The finite-module
equivalence of Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb} glues
these ideals to a closed subspace of $\bigcup_i Z_i$,
whose charts are the $Y_i$. Thus the diagonal factors,
as a germ, through this subdomain by a closed immersion.}
{\emph{对角线局部为到一个子域的闭浸入。}
选取源与目标的有限 affinoid 子域芽覆盖，使每个源片
$Y_i$ 映入某个目标片 $X_i$。在 $Z=Y\times_XY$ 中，
$Z_i=Y_i\times_{X_i}Y_i$ 是解析域。
在 $Z_i$ 上，对角线由 admissible 商
$B_i\widehat\otimes_{A_i}B_i\twoheadrightarrow B_i$ 给出；
有界截面 $b\mapsto b\otimes1$ 保证 admissibility。
有限生成的核理想在重叠上相等，因为在共同 affinoid 加细上，
它们都是同一个限制商的核。由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb} 的有限模等价，
这些理想粘合为 $\bigcup_i Z_i$ 的闭子空间，其各局部图
恰为 $Y_i$。因此对角线作为芽，经该子域分解为闭浸入。}

\bi{Let $z=\Delta(y)$ and $F=\widetilde{\mathcal H(y)}^{\rm gr}$.
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermproducts} identifies
$\mathscr R(Z,z)$ with the fibre product of two copies of
$\mathscr R(Y,y)$ over
$\mathscr R(X,x)\times_{\mathscr P(F_x)}\mathscr P(F)$.
Its diagonal is an isomorphism precisely when the canonical
map from $\mathscr R(Y,y)$ to that last space is injective.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedgermclosedimmersion} therefore identifies
separatedness of the reduction with the diagonal being a
closed immersion on a neighbourhood of $z$.}
{令 $z=\Delta(y)$、$F=\widetilde{\mathcal H(y)}^{\rm gr}$。
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermproducts} 将
$\mathscr R(Z,z)$ 识别为两份 $\mathscr R(Y,y)$ 在
$\mathscr R(X,x)\times_{\mathscr P(F_x)}\mathscr P(F)$ 上的
纤维积。其对角线为同构，当且仅当
$\mathscr R(Y,y)$ 到后一个空间的典范映射单射。
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedgermclosedimmersion}，约化的分离性
于是等价于对角线在 $z$ 的一个邻域上为闭浸入。}

\bi{We spell out the neighbourhood step. First restrict the
target to a compact Hausdorff neighbourhood of $x$ and
the source to a compact Hausdorff neighbourhood of $y$
mapping into it; these exist by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalnetdomaincalculus}.
Their fibre product is compact Hausdorff by the construction
in Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticproducts}.
Suppose the diagonal is a closed immersion over an open
neighbourhood $O$ of $z$. Its inverse image is an open
neighbourhood of $y$. Choose a compact analytic neighbourhood
$Y'$ of $y$ inside that inverse image.
The diagonal of $Y'$ is a closed immersion over the inverse
image of $O$ in $Y'\times_XY'$. Its full image is closed,
because $Y'$ is compact and this product is Hausdorff.
The open complement of that image, together with the inverse
image of $O$, covers the product; on the complement the
closed subspace is empty. Thus the diagonal of $Y'$ is a
closed immersion everywhere. This proves separatedness
of a representative, rather than merely a condition at
one point of its diagonal. Conversely a separated
representative has a closed diagonal, giving the reduction
condition above. The last assertion follows since an
affine birational space embeds into its valuation space.}
{明确说明邻域步骤。先将目标限制到 $x$ 的一个紧 Hausdorff
邻域，再将源限制到映入它的 $y$ 的紧 Hausdorff 邻域；
这些邻域由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalnetdomaincalculus}
存在。根据 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticproducts} 的构造，
其纤维积紧且 Hausdorff。假设对角线在 $z$ 的开邻域 $O$ 上
为闭浸入。$O$ 的逆像是 $y$ 的开邻域，在其中选取紧解析
邻域 $Y'$。$Y'$ 的对角线在 $O$ 于 $Y'\times_XY'$ 中的
逆像上为闭浸入。其完整像是闭集，因为 $Y'$ 紧而该乘积
Hausdorff。此像的开补集与 $O$ 的逆像覆盖乘积；在补集上，
闭子空间为空。因此 $Y'$ 的对角线处处为闭浸入。
这证明了一个代表的分离性，而不只是对角线上一点的性质。
反之，分离代表具有闭对角线，故满足上面的约化条件。
最后一项来自仿射 birational 空间嵌入其赋值空间。}
\end{proof}
