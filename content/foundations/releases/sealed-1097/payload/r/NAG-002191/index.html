A constant weighted degree

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 876

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{A constant weighted degree}{加权度数为常数}]
\label{cand:polyhedralconstantdegree}
\bi{Let $C$ be a finite balanced $d$-cycle and
$f:N_{\mathbf R}\to\mathbf R^d$ an integral affine map. For
a point $b$ outside the images of all cells of dimension at
most $d-1$ and all rank-deficient facets, the number}
{设 $C$ 为有限平衡 $d$-循环，
$f:N_{\mathbf R}\to\mathbf R^d$ 为整仿射映射。若点 $b$
避开所有维数至多为 $d-1$ 的胞腔之像及全部秩不足的极大
胞腔之像，则数}
\[
 \deg_f(C;b)=\sum_{x\in |C|\,,\ f(x)=b}
     w_C(\sigma_x)[\mathbf Z^d:f(N_{\sigma_x})]
\]
\bi{is finite and independent of $b$. It is the constant weight
of $f_*C$ on $\mathbf R^d$, allowing zero.}
{为有限数且与 $b$ 无关。它就是 $f_*C$ 在 $\mathbf R^d$
上的常权重，允许等于零。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Each permitted preimage is in a full-rank facet, and each
such facet contains at most one preimage. Formula~
\eqref{eq:candpushforwardweight} gives the displayed sum.
Extend a target subdivision to a subdivision of all of
$\mathbf R^d$, assigning weight zero to the omitted regions.
Across every codimension-one face, the two full-dimensional
normal vectors are opposite primitive vectors, so balancing
equates their weights. Any two generic points can be joined
by a polygonal path avoiding the codimension-two faces and
crossing only finitely many codimension-one faces: choose its
intermediate vertices outside the finitely many affine
subspaces causing a nontransverse crossing. For $d=1$ an
interval suffices; for $d=0$ there is only one target point.
The weight is therefore constant throughout the target.}
{每个允许的逆像位于满秩极大胞腔中，每个这样的胞腔至多
包含一个逆像。公式~\eqref{eq:candpushforwardweight} 给出
所列总和。将目标细分扩充为整个 $\mathbf R^d$ 的细分，
对缺少的区域赋零权。跨过每个余维一面，两侧满维本原法向
相反，故平衡性使两侧权重相等。任意两个一般点可用避开
余维二面、仅横穿有限个余维一面的折线连接：选择中间顶点
避开导致非横截相交的有限个仿射子空间即可。$d=1$ 时使用
区间，$d=0$ 时目标仅有一点。因此权重在整个目标上恒定。}
\end{proof}
