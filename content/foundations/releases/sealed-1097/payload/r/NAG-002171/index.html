Marked genus-one models after finite extension

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 736

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Marked genus-one models after finite extension}
{有限扩域后的亏格一标记模型}]
\label{cand:genusonepotentialmarkedmodel}
\bi{Let $K$ be complete of characteristic zero with its
given nontrivial non-Archimedean absolute value.
Let $C/K$ be a smooth projective geometrically connected
genus-one curve, and let $D\subset C$ be finite and reduced.
There is a finite Galois extension $L/K$ and a projective
split nodal model of $C_L$ such that all points of $D_L$
are rational and have distinct smooth specializations.
Consequently $(C_L\setminus D_L)^{\an}$ has a jointly
continuous strong deformation retraction onto a finite
metric graph with one infinite ray for every point of $D_L$.}
{设 $K$ 为特征零的完备域，
配备给定非平凡非阿基米德绝对值。
设 $C/K$ 为光滑射影几何连通亏格一曲线，
$D\subset C$ 为有限约化闭子概形。
存在有限 Galois 扩张 $L/K$ 及 $C_L$ 的射影
分裂结点模型，使 $D_L$ 中各点均有理，
且具有互异光滑专化。
因此 $(C_L\setminus D_L)^{\an}$ 具有到有限度量图的
联合连续强形变收缩，
对 $D_L$ 中每个点恰有一条无限射线。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A nonempty affine open of $C$ has a closed point of
finite residue degree by \cite[Tag 00FV]{StacksAlg}.
After that finite extension the curve has a rational
point. Flat cohomology base change
\cite[Tag 02KH]{StacksAlg} preserves genus one;
geometric integrality is preserved as well.
Apply Proposition~\ref{cand:legendresemistablevaluation}
or Proposition~\ref{cand:deuringsemistablevaluation}
according to the residue characteristic.}
{$C$ 的非空仿射开集由 \cite[Tag 00FV]{StacksAlg}
知具有有限剩余次数的闭点。
作此有限扩域后，曲线便有有理点。
平坦上同调基变换
\cite[Tag 02KH]{StacksAlg} 保持亏格一，
几何整性也保持。
按照剩余特征，应用
Proposition~\ref{cand:legendresemistablevaluation}
或 Proposition~\ref{cand:deuringsemistablevaluation}。}

\bi{Take a finite Galois closure over $K$ that also splits
the finitely many residue fields of $D$.
The constructed projective nodal model survives this base
change: flatness and finite presentation are preserved,
and the geometric special fibres remain nodal.
Apply Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting} to
its finite node set, then take a further finite Galois
closure over $K$.
The actual rational split charts persist.
Theorem~\ref{cand:equivariantmarkedrefinement}, used
without a group-action hypothesis, separates the finite
rational marking set by admissible algebraic blowups.
Projectivity is preserved throughout.
Finally Theorem~\ref{cand:generalmarkedmodelskeleton},
using its characteristic-zero arbitrary-residue branch,
gives the asserted homotopy over the final field.
Every extension carries the unique absolute value extending
the originally specified one.}
{取在 $K$ 上的有限 Galois 正规包，
并使它同时分裂 $D$ 的有限多个剩余域。
所构造的射影结点模型在此基变换后仍成立：
平坦性与有限表示性保持，
几何特殊纤维仍然只有结点。
对有限结点组应用
Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting}，
再取一次在 $K$ 上的有限 Galois 正规包，
实际有理分裂坐标片仍成立。
不加入群作用假设，应用
Theorem~\ref{cand:equivariantmarkedrefinement}，
用 admissible 代数 blowup 分离有限有理标记组。
射影性始终保持。
最后使用 Theorem~\ref{cand:generalmarkedmodelskeleton}
中特征零、任意剩余域的分支，
得到最终基域上的所述同伦。
每个扩域均配备原指定绝对值的唯一延拓。}
\end{proof}
