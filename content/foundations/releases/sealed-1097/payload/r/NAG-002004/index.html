Lifting a finite extension of a trivial residue field

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 455

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Lifting a finite extension of a trivial residue field}{提升平凡剩余域的有限扩张}]
\label{fnd:trivialresiduefiniteflatlift}
\bi{Let $X$ be good, let $x\in X$ have trivially valued $F=\mathcal H(x)$, and let $E/F$ be finite. There are an ordinary neighborhood $U$ of $x$ and a finite flat morphism $X'\to U$ whose fibre at $x$ is $\mathcal M(E)$. In particular it has a unique point $x'$ over $x$, with $\mathcal H(x')=E$, and the closed fibre of $\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal O_{X',x'}\to\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is reduced. If $E/F$ is separable, the morphism may be chosen finite \'etale.}
{设 $X$ good，点 $x\in X$ 的完备剩余域 $F=\mathcal H(x)$ 平凡赋值，且 $E/F$ 有限。则存在 $x$ 的普通邻域 $U$ 及有限平坦态射 $X'\to U$，其在 $x$ 处的纤维为 $\mathcal M(E)$。特别地，$x$ 上方只有一个点 $x'$，满足 $\mathcal H(x')=E$，且 $\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal O_{X',x'}\to\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 的闭纤维约化。若 $E/F$ 可分，可使此态射有限 \'etale。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ and let $\mathfrak m$ be its maximal ideal. The injection $\kappa(x)\hookrightarrow F$ has dense image. As $F$ is trivially valued and hence discrete, it is surjective, so $R/\mathfrak m=F$. Write the finite extension as a tower generated by finitely many elements. At each step lift the coefficients of the monic minimal polynomial from the preceding residue field to the preceding ring, starting with $R$. Successive monic quotients give a finite free $R$-algebra $C$ with $C/\mathfrak mC=E$. Every maximal ideal of the integral finite algebra $C$ lies over $\mathfrak m$, and this residue quotient is a field, so $C$ is local.}
{令 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$，以 $\mathfrak m$ 记其极大理想。单射 $\kappa(x)\hookrightarrow F$ 的像稠密；而 $F$ 平凡赋值，故拓扑离散，所以此映射满射，即 $R/\mathfrak m=F$。将有限扩张写成由有限个元素逐次生成的塔。从 $R$ 开始，在每一步把首一最小多项式的系数从前一剩余域提升到前一环。逐次取首一多项式商，得到有限自由 $R$-代数 $C$，满足 $C/\mathfrak mC=E$。有限整代数 $C$ 的每个极大理想都位于 $\mathfrak m$ 上方，而此剩余商是域，因此 $C$ 为局部环。}

\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization} realizes $C$ by a finite analytic map over an ordinary neighborhood of $x$. Its ordinary direct-image stalk is $C$ and its analytic fibre algebra is $C\otimes_R F=E$. To obtain flatness on a neighborhood, lift a basis of the free $R$-module $C$ to finitely many sections of the finite direct-image module. The resulting map from a free coherent module is an isomorphism at $x$. Its coherent kernel and cokernel vanish on a common ordinary neighborhood, since finitely many local generators vanish after one shrink. On that neighborhood the direct-image algebra is finite free. The finite-module base-change theorem then gives finite flatness, and the residue quotient proves the closed-fibre assertion. If $E/F$ is separable, one may instead use Theorem~\ref{fnd:goodetalegermresidueequivalence}, which gives the required finite \'etale realization directly.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization}，可在 $x$ 的普通邻域上用有限解析映射实现 $C$。其普通直像茎是 $C$，解析纤维代数为 $C\otimes_R F=E$。为在邻域上得到平坦性，将自由 $R$-模 $C$ 的一组基提升为有限直像模的有限组截面。由此得到从自由凝聚模出发的映射，它在 $x$ 处为同构；其凝聚核与余核在某共同普通邻域上为零，因为有限组局部生成元经一次缩小后同时消失。在该邻域上，直像代数有限自由，有限模基变换定理遂给出有限平坦性，而剩余商给出闭纤维断言。若 $E/F$ 可分，也可直接使用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:goodetalegermresidueequivalence}，取得所需有限 \'etale 实现。}
\end{proof}
