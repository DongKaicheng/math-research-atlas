Stable models for the fixed absolute value

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 657

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Stable models for the fixed absolute value}{固定绝对值的稳定模型}]
\label{cand:stablecompletevaluation}
\bi{Let $K$ be a complete non-Archimedean field with its given nontrivial absolute value, and let $C/K$ be a smooth projective geometrically connected curve of genus at least two. There is a finite extension $L/K$, endowed with the unique extending absolute value, for which $C_L$ has a projective stable model over $L^\circ$. If $\operatorname{char}K=0$, the extension $L/K$ may be chosen Galois.}
{设 $K$ 为配备给定非平凡绝对值的完备非阿基米德域，$C/K$ 为亏格至少二的光滑射影几何连通曲线。存在有限扩张 $L/K$，配备唯一的延拓绝对值，使 $C_L$ 在 $L^\circ$ 上有射影稳定模型。若 $\operatorname{char}K=0$，还可选择 $L/K$ 为 Galois 扩张。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Apply Theorem~\ref{cand:stablevaluationextension} to $R=K^\circ$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} supplies the unique extending absolute value on the finite extension $L$; its valuation ring $L^\circ$ dominates $K^\circ$, and $L$ is complete. We may therefore take $R'=L^\circ$ in that theorem.}
{对 $R=K^\circ$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{cand:stablevaluationextension}。Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 为有限扩张 $L$ 给出唯一的延拓绝对值；其赋值环 $L^\circ$ 支配 $K^\circ$，且 $L$ 完备。因此可以在该定理中取 $R'=L^\circ$。}

\bi{If $\operatorname{char}K=0$, take a finite normal closure $M/K$ of $L/K$ using \cite[Tag 09DT]{StacksAlg}. Every irreducible polynomial over a characteristic-zero field has nonzero derivative and is relatively prime to its derivative, so $M/K$ is separable and hence Galois. Uniqueness of the extended absolute values identifies the restriction from $M$ with the chosen value on $L$, giving $L^\circ\subset M^\circ$. Base change of the stable model to $M^\circ$ remains stable by \cite[Tag 0E76]{StacksAlg}, and remains projective, flat and of finite presentation. Replacing $L$ by $M$ proves the assertion.}
{若 $\operatorname{char}K=0$，由 \cite[Tag 09DT]{StacksAlg}，取 $L/K$ 的有限正规包 $M/K$。特征零域上的不可约多项式具有非零导数，且与导数互素，因此 $M/K$ 可分，从而为 Galois 扩张。延拓绝对值的唯一性说明 $M$ 上绝对值限制到 $L$ 后即为已选绝对值，得到 $L^\circ\subset M^\circ$。根据 \cite[Tag 0E76]{StacksAlg}，稳定模型基变换到 $M^\circ$ 后仍稳定，并且仍射影、平坦、有限呈示。以 $M$ 代替 $L$ 即得断言。}
\end{proof}
