Units on standard open domains

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 645

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Units on standard open domains}{标准开域上的可逆函数}]
\label{cand:standardopenunitnorms}
\bi{An analytic unit on a standard open annulus $r<|T|<R$, where $0\leq r<R\leq\infty$, has}
{标准开圆环 $r<|T|<R$ 上的解析可逆函数满足下式，其中 $0\leq r<R\leq\infty$：}
\[
 |f(x)|=|c|\,|T(x)|^m\qquad(c\in K^\times,\ m\in\Z).
\]
\bi{Thus its absolute value factors through the radial retraction. An analytic unit on a standard open or closed disc of positive radius with centre $a\in K$ has constant absolute value $|f(a)|$.}
{因此其绝对值经径向 retraction 分解。以 $a\in K$ 为中心、半径为正的标准开或闭圆盘上的解析可逆函数，则具有恒定绝对值 $|f(a)|$。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Restrict an annular unit and its inverse to closed subannuli of positive modulus. Proposition~\ref{cand:annulusunitnormalform} applies to each restriction. When two radius intervals overlap in a nonempty open interval, the affine functions $\log|c|+m\log\rho$ agree there, so their slope and constant agree. Any two compact radius intervals lie in a common larger compact subinterval of $(r,R)$; comparing with that subinterval gives the asserted global formula.}
{将圆环上的可逆函数及其逆限制到具有正 modulus 的闭子圆环。Proposition~\ref{cand:annulusunitnormalform} 适用于每个限制。当两个半径区间的交含有非空开区间时，仿射函数 $\log|c|+m\log\rho$ 在该区间相等，因此其斜率与常数项相等。任意两个紧半径区间均包含于 $(r,R)$ 的同一个更大的紧子区间，与该子区间比较便得到全局公式。}

\bi{For a closed disc of radius $q>0$, translate the centre to zero and write $f=\sum_{n\geq0}a_nT^n$ and its inverse $g=\sum_{n\geq0}b_nT^n$. Restriction to the radius-$q$ circle identifies these coefficient series with elements of $A_{q,q}$. Their greatest maximizing exponents are nonnegative and sum to zero by Lemma~\ref{cand:annulusdominantexponents}. Both are zero. Consequently $a_0\ne0$ and $\|f/a_0-1\|_q<1$, which gives $|f(x)|=|a_0|$ throughout the disc. Restricting an open-disc unit to each closed subdisc centred at $a$ proves the open-disc assertion.}
{对半径 $q>0$ 的闭圆盘，平移中心到零，写 $f=\sum_{n\geq0}a_nT^n$，并将其逆写为 $g=\sum_{n\geq0}b_nT^n$。限制到半径为 $q$ 的圆周，将这些系数级数识别为 $A_{q,q}$ 中的元素。其达到最大值的最大指数均非负，且由 Lemma~\ref{cand:annulusdominantexponents}，两者之和为零，所以两者均为零。于是 $a_0\ne0$，且 $\|f/a_0-1\|_q<1$，故在整个圆盘上 $|f(x)|=|a_0|$。将开圆盘上的可逆函数限制到每个以 $a$ 为中心的闭子圆盘，便得到开圆盘的结论。}
\end{proof}
