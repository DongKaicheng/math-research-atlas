The boundary of a strict affinoid curve

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 619

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The boundary of a strict affinoid curve}
{Strict affinoid 曲线的边界}]
\label{fnd:strictcurveboundaryshilov}
\bi{Let $X$ be a nonempty strict affinoid space of dimension
at most one with no isolated points. Then its absolute
analytic boundary equals its Shilov boundary. In particular
this holds for every pure one-dimensional strict affinoid space.}
{设 $X$ 为非空、维数至多一且没有孤立点的 strict affinoid
空间。则其绝对解析边界等于其 Shilov 边界。
特别地，每个纯一维 strict affinoid 空间都满足此结论。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Write $X=\mathcal M(A)$ and $R=\widetilde A$.
By Lemma~\ref{fnd:canonicalreductiondimensionbound},
$R$ has dimension at most one. Theorem~\ref{fnd:canonicalgenericshilov}
identifies its minimal primes with the Shilov points, each
having a unique analytic preimage. A minimal prime
$\mathfrak p$ cannot also be maximal. Indeed, choose finitely
many generators $\bar f_i$ of $\mathfrak p$ and lifts
$f_i\in A^\circ$. If $\mathfrak p$ were maximal, then
$V(\mathfrak p)=\{\mathfrak p\}$ and}
{写成 $X=\mathcal M(A)$，令 $R=\widetilde A$。由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:canonicalreductiondimensionbound}，$R$ 的维数
至多为一。Theorem~\ref{fnd:canonicalgenericshilov} 将其极小
素理想与 Shilov 点一一对应，每个这样的素理想只有一个解析原像。
极小素理想 $\mathfrak p$ 不可能同时极大。事实上，取
$\mathfrak p$ 的有限个生成元 $\bar f_i$ 及其提升
$f_i\in A^\circ$。若 $\mathfrak p$ 极大，则
$V(\mathfrak p)=\{\mathfrak p\}$，且}
\[
 \operatorname{red}^{-1}(\mathfrak p)
 =\{y\in X:|f_i(y)|<1\text{ for every }i\}
\]
\bi{would be an open singleton, contrary to the hypothesis.
In a ring of dimension at most one, a nonmaximal prime is
minimal: otherwise a strictly smaller minimal prime and a
strictly larger maximal prime would form a chain of length
two. Thus the nonclosed points of $\operatorname{Spec}R$
are exactly its generic points.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictabsoluteinteriorreduction} says that
their inverse image is the analytic boundary, while
Theorem~\ref{fnd:canonicalgenericshilov} says that the same
inverse image is the Shilov boundary.
The last assertion follows from
Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictisolatedpointdimension}.}
{就会是一个单点开集，与假设矛盾。
维数至多一的环中，非极大素理想必为极小素理想：否则一个严格
更小的极小素理想与一个严格更大的极大素理想将构成长度为二
的素理想链。因此 $\operatorname{Spec}R$ 的非闭点恰为其泛点。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictabsoluteinteriorreduction} 说明其逆像为
解析边界，而 Theorem~\ref{fnd:canonicalgenericshilov} 说明
同一逆像为 Shilov 边界。
最后一个断言由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictisolatedpointdimension} 得到。}
\end{proof}
