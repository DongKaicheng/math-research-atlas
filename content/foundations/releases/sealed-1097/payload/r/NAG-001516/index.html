A closed strict-test domain is Weierstrass

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 229

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A closed strict-test domain is Weierstrass}{闭 strict-test domain 是 Weierstrass 域}]\label{fnd:strictcloseddomainweierstrass}
\bi{Suppose a bounded surjection $q:C\twoheadrightarrow D$ of strict $K$-affinoid algebras is a strict-test affinoid-domain presentation. Then its kernel is generated by an idempotent $e\in C$. For any $c\in K$ with $|c|>1$, its image is the Weierstrass domain}
{设 strict $K$-affinoid 代数的有界满射 $q:C\twoheadrightarrow D$ 是 strict-test affinoid-domain 表示。则其核由某个幂等元 $e\in C$ 生成。对任意满足 $|c|>1$ 的 $c\in K$，其像为 Weierstrass 域}
\[
 \{x\in\mathcal M(C):|ce(x)|\leq1\},
\]
\bi{and the natural map $C\langle S\rangle/(S-ce)\to D$ is a bounded isomorphism.}
{且自然映射 $C\langle S\rangle/(S-ce)\to D$ 为有界同构。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $I=\ker q$. The algebra $C$ is Noetherian and $I,I^2$ are closed by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}. A multiplicative seminorm on $C$ annihilates $I^2$ if and only if it annihilates $I$, since $a^2\in I^2$ for every $a\in I$. A bounded seminorm annihilating an ideal descends boundedly to its quotient: take the infimum of its bound over representatives. Thus $\mathcal M(C/I^2)$ and $\mathcal M(C/I)$ have the same image in $\mathcal M(C)$. The quotient $C/I$ is boundedly isomorphic to $D$ by Lemma~\ref{fnd:nonarchopenmapping}. Apply the strict-test domain property to the map $C\to C/I^2$. Its factorization through $C/I$ forces $I\subset I^2$, hence $I=I^2$. The authorized algebraic lemma \cite[Tag 00EH, Lemma 10.21.5]{StacksAlg} gives an idempotent $e$ with $I=(e)$.}
{令 $I=\ker q$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}，$C$ 为 Noetherian，且 $I,I^2$ 均闭。$C$ 上的乘性半范数消去 $I^2$ 当且仅当它消去 $I$，因为每个 $a\in I$ 都满足 $a^2\in I^2$。消去某个理想的有界半范数在商上仍有界：对所有代表元的界取下确界即可。因此，$\mathcal M(C/I^2)$ 与 $\mathcal M(C/I)$ 在 $\mathcal M(C)$ 中有相同的像。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:nonarchopenmapping}，商 $C/I$ 与 $D$ 有界同构。将 strict-test domain 性质应用于映射 $C\to C/I^2$。该映射通过 $C/I$ 的分解强迫 $I\subset I^2$，所以 $I=I^2$。由获准使用的代数引理 \cite[Tag 00EH, Lemma 10.21.5]{StacksAlg}，存在幂等元 $e$ 使 $I=(e)$。}

\bi{At every multiplicative point, $|e|$ is zero or one. Thus $|ce|\leq1$ holds exactly where $e$ vanishes, which is the image of $\mathcal M(D)$. To identify the algebras, put $E=C\langle S\rangle/(S-ce)$. In $E$ the element $e$ satisfies $e^m=e$ and $S^m=c^me$. Since the variable $S$ is power bounded in its actual quotient norm, these identities give $\|e\|\leq |c|^{-m}\|S^m\|\to0$, so $e=0$. Consequently $C\to E$ factors through $D=C/(e)$. Conversely $C\to D$ sends $ce$ to zero, so bounded substitution gives $E\to D$ with $S\mapsto0$. The two maps are inverse on $C$ and on the variable $S$, hence on the completions. Their boundedness follows from quotient norms and the admissible comparison with $D$.}
{在每个乘性点处，$|e|$ 只能为零或一。因此，$|ce|\leq1$ 恰在 $e$ 消失的点成立，也就是 $\mathcal M(D)$ 的像。为识别代数，令 $E=C\langle S\rangle/(S-ce)$。在 $E$ 中，元素 $e$ 满足 $e^m=e$ 及 $S^m=c^me$。变量 $S$ 在实际商范数下幂有界，所以这些恒等式给出 $\|e\|\leq|c|^{-m}\|S^m\|\to0$，故 $e=0$。因此 $C\to E$ 通过 $D=C/(e)$ 分解。反过来，$C\to D$ 将 $ce$ 送到零，所以有界代换给出 $E\to D$，并将 $S$ 送到零。这两个映射在 $C$ 与变量 $S$ 上互逆，因而在完成上互逆。它们的有界性来自商范数及与 $D$ 的 admissible 比较。}
\end{proof}
