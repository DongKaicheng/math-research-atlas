An original-ring symmetric insertion

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1903

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[An original-ring symmetric insertion]
\label{cand:f5sub:originalsymmetric}
Let \(q\) be a closed node of \(\mathcal X_s\), and let \(\ell\)
be the common length of its split geometric tubes.
For \(\delta\in\Gamma\) with \(0<\delta\leq\ell/2\), there is a
projective semistable modification of \(\mathcal X\), an isomorphism
away from \(q\), inserting precisely \(\delta,\ell-\delta\) in each
geometric tube over \(q\). A coincident pair is counted once.
The modification preserves \(D\) and all old vertices and is the
blowup of an explicit finite-type ideal on \(\mathcal X\).
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Choose \(\lambda\in K\) with \(v(\lambda)=\delta\).
On a Zariski neighbourhood \(U\) of \(q\) omitting the other special
nodes, set
\[
                 \mathcal I_U=(\lambda)
                    +\operatorname{Fitt}_1\Omega_{U/O}.
\]
The differential module is finitely presented, so its Fitting ideal
is of finite type. At a smooth point that module is locally free
of rank one, and its first Fitting ideal is the unit ideal.
On the generic fibre \(\lambda\) is invertible.
Thus \(\mathcal I_U\) equals the unit ideal on \(U\setminus\{q\}\)
and glues with that ideal on \(\mathcal X\setminus\{q\}\).
We have constructed a quasi-coherent finite-type ideal
\(\mathcal I_q\) on the original model.
It contains \((\lambda)\); for any fixed
\(0\ne\pi\in K^{\circ\circ}\), some power of \(\pi\) belongs to
\((\lambda)\), since the valuation has rank one.

Extend to \(O'=L^\circ\).
Differentials and Fitting ideals commute with coefficient extension
and the indicated etale charts
(Stacks tags \stacks{04BX}, \stacks{0C3D}, \stacks{0C3E}).
The ideal in every selected chart is \((\lambda,x_e,y_e)\).
Blowup commutes with these flat base changes by \stacks{0805}.
The accepted \dep{cand:symmetricnodeblowup} applies because
\(c_e=a_e/\lambda^2\in O'\). Its three actual torsion-free charts are
\[
 O'[u,z]/(uz-c_e),\qquad
 O'[x,w]/(xw-\lambda),\qquad
 O'[y,w']/(yw'-\lambda).
\]
We reuse that calculation, including its gluing and component count.
It inserts two smooth geometric projective lines if \(2\delta<\ell\),
and one if \(2\delta=\ell\), at exactly the asserted radii.
They form a chain between the two old branch ends.

Shrink each auxiliary pointed etale neighbourhood so its fibre over
each selected node in either target is exactly the specified rational
point. These charts cover the exceptional fibre.
With the unchanged complement, they prove flatness and local finite
presentation as in \dep{cand:nodefittinginsertion}, using
\stacks{036K} and \stacks{036N}. Nodality descends through the finite
faithfully flat coefficient extension by \stacks{0C5E}.
The actual global blowup is projective by \stacks{02NS};
composition over the affine base is projective by \stacks{0C4P}.
Quasi-compactness, separatedness and local finite presentation give
finite presentation. Replacing each geometric edge by a connected
chain preserves geometric connectedness and reducedness.
The generic fibre is unchanged, and the smooth marking is disjoint
from the centre.

This constructs the integral model before using geometric vertex
invariance. The Fitting ideal is defined even at a nonsplit node
and when both branches belong to one global component.
\end{proof}
