Global semistable deformation

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 902

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Global semistable deformation}
{全局半稳定形变}]
\label{cand:globalsemistablehomotopy}
\bi{Let $\mathfrak X$ be a quasi-compact admissible formal
$O$-scheme with Hausdorff generic fibre $X$. Suppose it
has a finite affine formal open cover $\mathfrak U_i$
with topologically finitely presented formal \etale maps}
{设 $\mathfrak X$ 为拟紧 admissible 形式 $O$-概形，
其泛纤维 $X$ 为 Hausdorff。假设它有有限仿射形式
开覆盖 $\mathfrak U_i$，并具有拓扑有限表示形式
\etale 映射}
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 f_i:\mathfrak U_i\longrightarrow
 \operatorname{Spf}
 O\langle X_{i0},\ldots,X_{ir_i},T_{i1},\ldots,T_{iq_i}\rangle/
                 (X_{i0}\cdots X_{ir_i}-a_i),\\
 0<|a_i|<1.
 \end{gathered}
\]
\bi{There is a canonical jointly continuous homotopy
$H_{\mathfrak X}:X\times[0,1]\to X$ whose restriction
to each $U_i=(\mathfrak U_i)_\eta$ is the constructed
chart homotopy. It is a strong deformation retraction onto
the closed compact subset}
{存在典范联合连续同伦
$H_{\mathfrak X}:X\times[0,1]\to X$，其在每个
$U_i=(\mathfrak U_i)_\eta$ 上的限制为所构造的坐标片
同伦。它是到以下闭紧子集的强形变收缩：}
\[
 \Sigma(\mathfrak X)=H_{\mathfrak X}(X,1),\qquad
 \Sigma(\mathfrak X)\cap U_i=(f_i)_\eta^{-1}(\Sigma_{a_i}).
\]
\bi{It satisfies the maximum composition law, is independent
of the affine cover and the semistable coordinate maps,
and restricts to the corresponding homotopy on every
quasi-compact formal open. Under a complete isometric
extension $L/K$, the projection $X_L\to X$ commutes with
these homotopies.}
{它满足最大值复合律，与仿射覆盖及半稳定坐标映射
无关，并在每个拟紧形式开集上限制为相应同伦。
对于完备等距扩张 $L/K$，投影 $X_L\to X$ 与这些
同伦相容。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The preceding presentation lemma and
Theorem~\ref{cand:etalechartskeletonretraction} give a
jointly continuous strong deformation $H_i$ on each
$U_i$. On an overlap of two formal charts, choose a
principal formal open of the first chart contained in the
second. It is admissible by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalprincipalopenflat}. Its generic fibre
is preserved by both homotopies, and
Proposition~\ref{cand:semistableformalopenrestriction}
identifies their restrictions with the two homotopies of
that affine algebra. Those homotopies coincide by
Theorem~\ref{cand:semistablechartindependence}.
Such formal affine opens cover the overlap, and their
generic fibres cover the analytic overlap by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}.
Thus all $H_i$ agree on overlaps.}
{前面的表示引理及
Theorem~\ref{cand:etalechartskeletonretraction}
在每个 $U_i$ 上给出联合连续强形变 $H_i$。在两个
形式坐标片的交叠内，取第一个坐标片的一个包含于
第二个中的主形式开集。由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalprincipalopenflat}，它为
admissible。其泛纤维被两个同伦保持，而
Proposition~\ref{cand:semistableformalopenrestriction}
将二者的限制识别为该仿射代数的两个同伦。由
Theorem~\ref{cand:semistablechartindependence}，
这两个同伦相同。此类仿射形式开集覆盖交叠，由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}，
其泛纤维覆盖解析交叠。因此全部 $H_i$ 在交叠上一致。}

\bi{Each $U_i$ is compact and hence closed in the
Hausdorff space $X$, and the finite family covers $X$.
The product sets $U_i\times[0,1]$ consequently form a
finite closed cover. The maps $H_i$ glue to a continuous
map: the inverse image of any closed subset of $X$ is
the finite union of its closed inverse images in these
closed product sets. All homotopy and composition
identities hold on each preserved $U_i$, hence globally.}
{每个 $U_i$ 紧，因此在 Hausdorff 空间 $X$ 中闭，
且这个有限族覆盖 $X$。所以各
$U_i\times[0,1]$ 组成有限闭覆盖。映射 $H_i$ 粘合为
连续映射：$X$ 中任一闭集的逆像是它在这些闭直积
片中的闭逆像之有限并。全部同伦及复合恒等式在每个
被保持的 $U_i$ 上成立，故全局成立。}

\bi{A point in $U_i$ is fixed by the global endpoint
exactly when it is fixed by the local endpoint, namely
when it lies over $\Sigma_{a_i}$. The endpoint is
idempotent, so its image is precisely its fixed set.
Since $X$ is a finite union of compact sets, it is compact;
its endpoint image is compact and hence closed. This
proves the displayed local description and strong
deformation assertion. A second formal atlas has common
affine formal refinements with the first one; the same
overlap argument proves independence. It also proves
restriction to a quasi-compact formal open. Finally,
base change of a chart retains its integral Jacobian
presentation. The local scalar-extension compatibility
of Theorem~\ref{cand:etaleendpointjointcontinuity}
therefore gives the asserted projection identity on the
cover, and hence globally.}
{位于 $U_i$ 的一点被全局终点固定，当且仅当它被
局部终点固定，即位于 $\Sigma_{a_i}$ 之上。终点为
幂等映射，所以其像恰为不动点集。由于 $X$ 是有限
个紧集之并，$X$ 紧；其终点像紧，因而闭。这证明
所列局部描述及强形变断言。第二个形式图册与第一个
有共同仿射形式加细，相同的交叠论证证明独立性，
也证明限制到拟紧形式开集的相容性。最后，坐标片
的基变换保留其整 Jacobian 表示。
Theorem~\ref{cand:etaleendpointjointcontinuity} 的
局部扩标量相容性因此在覆盖上给出所称投影恒等式，
从而全局成立。}
\end{proof}
