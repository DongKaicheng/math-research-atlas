Proper reductions and base change

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 571

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Proper reductions and base change}
{适当约化与基变换}]
\label{fnd:propergermreductionbasechange}
\bi{If the reduction of a morphism of analytic germs is proper,
then its reduction after an analytic base change over any
complete valued extension is proper. Properness of birational
morphisms is local on an open covering of the target.}
{若解析芽态射的约化适当，则在任意完备赋值扩张上作解析
基变换后，其约化仍适当。Birational 态射的适当性可在目标的
开覆盖上检查。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A proper birational map is a homeomorphism to the specified
pullback of its target. A further pullback is again a
homeomorphism. For analytic products this is
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermproducts}, and for change of
ground field it is Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermscalar}.
Factor a general change into these two changes.
For target locality, a local homeomorphism is bijective
if its inverse images over every member of an open cover
are bijective. This proves the final assertion.}
{适当 birational 映射是到所指定目标拉回的同胚，再作拉回
仍为同胚。解析乘积由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermproducts} 处理，基域变换由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermscalar} 处理；一般变换分解
为这两步即可。关于目标局部性，局部同胚若在目标一个开覆盖
的各成员上均为双射，则全局为双射，证明最后的断言。}
\end{proof}
