Affinoid intersections in a separated space

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 574

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Affinoid intersections in a separated space}
{分离空间中的 affinoid 交}]
\label{fnd:separatedaffinoidintersections}
\bi{The intersection of two affinoid domains in a separated
analytic space is affinoid.}
{分离解析空间中两个 affinoid 域的交仍为 affinoid。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For domains $U,V\subset X$, their intersection represents
$U\times_XV$ by the domain universal property. It is the
inverse image of the closed diagonal of $X$ in the affinoid
space $U\times_KV$. A closed analytic subspace of an affinoid
is affinoid: its coherent ideal is $\widetilde I$ for a finite
ideal $I$ by Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb}, and the
space is represented by the admissible quotient $A/I$.
Completed tensor products carry that quotient to its
base-changed quotient. These observations prove the assertion.}
{由域的泛性质，$U,V\subset X$ 的交表示 $U\times_XV$。
它是 $X$ 的闭对角线在 affinoid 空间 $U\times_KV$ 中的
逆像。Affinoid 的闭解析子空间仍为 affinoid：由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb}，其凝聚理想为某有限
理想 $I$ 对应的 $\widetilde I$，而该空间由 admissible 商
$A/I$ 表示。完备张量积把这个商送到其基变换商。
这些观察证明所需结论。}
\end{proof}
