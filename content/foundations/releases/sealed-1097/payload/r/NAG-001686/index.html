Finite modules and faithful scalar extension

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 272

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Finite modules and faithful scalar extension}{有限模与忠实标量扩张}]
\label{fnd:generalscalarfaithful}
\bi{For every complete valued extension $L/K$ and generalized $K$-affinoid $A$, the ring map $A\to A_L$ is faithfully flat. For every finite canonical $A$-module $M$ there are natural bounded isomorphisms}
{对每个完备赋值扩域 $L/K$ 及广义 $K$-affinoid $A$，环映射 $A\to A_L$ 忠实平坦。对每个有限典范 $A$-模 $M$，存在自然的有界同构}
\[
 A_L\otimes_A M
 \simeq A_L\widehat\otimes_A M
 \simeq M\widehat\otimes_K L .
\]
\bi{Their inverses are bounded. For the quotient norm from a fixed finite free presentation of $M$, and the corresponding quotient norm after extension, the comparisons are isometric. They identify short exact sequences of finite modules with norm-strict short exact sequences after either ordinary or completed scalar extension.}
{这些同构的逆有界。若使用 $M$ 的某个固定有限自由表示的商范数，以及扩张后相应的商范数，则各比较等距。它们将有限模的短正合列识别为普通或完备标量扩张后 norm-strict 的短正合列。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose a finite presentation $A^s\xrightarrow{D}A^m\xrightarrow{q}M\to0$, and give $M$ its quotient norm. Its relation module $R=\ker q$ is closed and finite; both $A^s\to R$ and $q$ are norm-strict by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}. Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonstrictexact} to these two kernel sequences. Finite coordinate sums commute isometrically with completed scalar extension, either by their coordinate projections or by Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedscalartensor} with finite $I$. We obtain}
{选择有限表示 $A^s\xrightarrow{D}A^m\xrightarrow{q}M\to0$，给 $M$ 配备商范数。关系模 $R=\ker q$ 闭且有限；由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}，$A^s\to R$ 与 $q$ 均为 norm-strict。对这两个核序列应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonstrictexact}。有限坐标直和与完备标量扩张等距交换，可由坐标投影验证，也可对有限 $I$ 使用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedscalartensor}。于是得到}
\[
 A_L^s\xrightarrow{D_L}A_L^m
       \longrightarrow M\widehat\otimes_K L\longrightarrow0,
 \qquad
 M\widehat\otimes_K L\simeq A_L^m/D_LA_L^s
\]
\bi{with closed relation image and the indicated quotient norm. Ordinary tensor right exactness, \cite[Tag 00DF]{StacksAlg}, identifies this algebraic quotient with $A_L\otimes_A M$. The map is the natural one on pure tensors. Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor}, with target $A_L$, identifies its ordinary and completed tensors over $A$, including the isometry for the chosen norms. Naturality follows on generators and then by continuity. Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonstrictexact} and the finite-module theorem give the exact-sequence assertion.}
{其中关系像闭，并具有所列商范数。由普通张量积的右正合性 \cite[Tag 00DF]{StacksAlg}，这个代数商识别为 $A_L\otimes_A M$；在纯张量上即为自然映射。以 $A_L$ 为目标应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor}，将关于 $A$ 的普通与完备张量积识别，并得到所选范数下的等距性。自然性先在生成元上成立，再由连续性延拓。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonstrictexact} 及有限模定理，得到关于正合列的断言。}

\bi{For a finitely generated ideal $J\subset A$, the algebraic map $J\otimes_A A_L\to A_L$ is now identified with the injective map $J\widehat\otimes_K L\to A\widehat\otimes_K L$. The induced norm on $J$ is canonical, so Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonisometrictensor} applies. The finitely generated ideal criterion \cite[Tag 00HD, Lemma 10.39.5(4)]{StacksAlg} proves flatness on all algebraic $A$-modules. If $J$ is proper, the isometric map}
{对有限生成理想 $J\subset A$，代数映射 $J\otimes_A A_L\to A_L$ 现已识别为单射 $J\widehat\otimes_K L\to A\widehat\otimes_K L$。$J$ 上的诱导范数为典范范数，因此 Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonisometrictensor} 适用。由有限生成理想判别准则 \cite[Tag 00HD, Lemma 10.39.5(4)]{StacksAlg}，得到所有代数 $A$-模上的平坦性。若 $J$ 为真理想，则等距映射}
\[
 A/J\lhook\joinrel\longrightarrow
 (A/J)\widehat\otimes_K L
       \simeq A_L/JA_L
\]
\bi{shows that the quotient on the right is nonzero. In particular the fibre at every maximal ideal is nonzero. The faithful-flatness criterion \cite[Tag 00HP, Lemma 10.39.15]{StacksAlg} completes the proof. For the zero algebra the module category is zero and the same assertions hold directly.}
{说明右边的商非零。特别地，每个极大理想处的纤维非零。由忠实平坦性判别准则 \cite[Tag 00HP, Lemma 10.39.15]{StacksAlg}，证明完成。对零代数，模范畴为零范畴，相同断言直接成立。}
\end{proof}
