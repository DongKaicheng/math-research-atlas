Intersections of strict-test domains

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 253

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Intersections of strict-test domains}{Strict-test 域的交}]\label{fnd:stricttestdomainintersections}
\bi{Let $K$ be complete, with trivial valuation allowed. Let $A\to B$ and $A\to C$ represent strict-test domains $V,W\subseteq\mathcal M(A)$. Then}
{设 $K$ 完备，允许平凡赋值。设 $A\to B$ 与 $A\to C$ 分别表示 $\mathcal M(A)$ 中的 strict-test 域 $V,W$。则}
\[
 D=B\widehat\otimes_A C
\]
\bi{is strict, with zero allowed, and represents the strict-test domain $V\cap W$. Its spectrum maps homeomorphically onto that intersection. Iterated intersections are canonically associative, and their bounded algebra maps agree with restriction. In particular, an empty intersection has the zero algebra.}
{为 strict，允许零代数，并且表示 strict-test 域 $V\cap W$。它的谱同胚映到该交集。迭代交叠在典范意义下满足结合律，其有界代数映射与限制映射一致。特别地，空交集具有零代数。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:relativeaffinoidpushouts} makes $D$ strict and gives its bounded pushout property. Every point of $\mathcal M(D)$ restricts to points of $\mathcal M(B)$ and $\mathcal M(C)$, so its image lies in $V\cap W$. To prove that this map is bijective, rather than assuming a general theorem about Berkovich fibre products, take the finite rational covering $V=\bigcup_\alpha R_\alpha$ over $A$ supplied by Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis}. Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalrestrictiondomainbridge} identifies the algebra of $R_\alpha$ as a rational algebra over $B$ with $A_{R_\alpha}$. Its pullback to $D$ is rational and has algebra}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:relativeaffinoidpushouts}，$D$ 为 strict，且具有有界 pushout 泛性质。$\mathcal M(D)$ 中的每个点限制为 $\mathcal M(B)$ 与 $\mathcal M(C)$ 中的点，故其像属于 $V\cap W$。为证明该映射双射，而不预先假设一般 Berkovich 纤维积定理，应用 Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis}，取在 $A$ 上的有限 rational 覆盖 $V=\bigcup_\alpha R_\alpha$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalrestrictiondomainbridge}，$R_\alpha$ 作为 $B$ 上 rational 域的代数与 $A_{R_\alpha}$ 识别。它拉回到 $D$ 后仍为 rational，且具有代数}
\[
 D_\alpha
   =D\widehat\otimes_B A_{R_\alpha}
   \simeq C\widehat\otimes_A A_{R_\alpha}.
\]
\bi{The bounded isomorphism follows either from the two pushout properties or from the common relative presentation; it is not an assertion that an ordinary tensor product is already complete. The right-hand side is a rational quotient of $C$. Its spectrum identifies with $W\cap R_\alpha$, by the explicit rational point theorem. The $D_\alpha$ cover $\mathcal M(D)$, because the $R_\alpha$ cover $\mathcal M(B)$. For any $x\in V\cap W$, choose $\alpha$ with $x\in R_\alpha$; the point in $\mathcal M(C)$ above $x$ gives a point of $\mathcal M(D_\alpha)$ and hence of $\mathcal M(D)$. If two points of $\mathcal M(D)$ map to $x$, both lie in this rational piece, and the injectivity of $\mathcal M(D_\alpha)\to\mathcal M(C)$ makes them equal. This proves the bijection.}
{由两个 pushout 泛性质，或共同的相对表示，得到上述有界同构；这不是断言普通张量积已经完备。右边是 $C$ 的 rational 商，由显式 rational 点定理，其谱与 $W\cap R_\alpha$ 识别。由于 $R_\alpha$ 覆盖 $\mathcal M(B)$，各 $D_\alpha$ 覆盖 $\mathcal M(D)$。对任意 $x\in V\cap W$，选取使 $x\in R_\alpha$ 的指标；$\mathcal M(C)$ 中位于 $x$ 上方的点给出 $\mathcal M(D_\alpha)$ 中的点，进而给出 $\mathcal M(D)$ 中的点。若 $\mathcal M(D)$ 中两个点映到 $x$，则它们都位于这个 rational 部分；由 $\mathcal M(D_\alpha)\to\mathcal M(C)$ 的单射性，两者相等。这证明双射。}

\bi{The map is continuous, and compactness and Hausdorffness from Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact} make it a homeomorphism onto its image. If a bounded $A\to E$ with $E$ strict has spectral image in $V\cap W$, the two domain properties supply unique compatible maps $B,C\to E$, and the pushout supplies a unique $D\to E$. This is exactly the required domain property. Iterating it gives canonical restriction and association isomorphisms by uniqueness. Finally, a nonzero strict affinoid has a point by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}; hence an empty spectrum forces $D=0$.}
{该映射连续；由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact} 的紧性与 Hausdorff 性，它到其像为同胚。若某个有界映射 $A\to E$ 的谱像包含于 $V\cap W$，其中 $E$ 为 strict，则两个域泛性质给出唯一相容映射 $B,C\to E$，pushout 又给出唯一映射 $D\to E$。这恰为所需的域泛性质。迭代使用唯一性，便得到典范限制与结合同构。最后，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}，非零 strict affinoid 总有点；因此谱为空迫使 $D=0$。}
\end{proof}
