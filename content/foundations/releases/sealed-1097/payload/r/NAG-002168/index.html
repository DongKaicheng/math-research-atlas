The integral plane-cubic family

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 733

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The integral plane-cubic family}
{整平面三次曲线族}]
\label{cand:integralplanecubicfamily}
\bi{Let $O=F^\circ$ for a complete nontrivially valued
field. For coefficients $a_i\in O$, the projective cubic}
{设 $O=F^\circ$，其中 $F$ 为完备非平凡赋值域。
对系数 $a_i\in O$，射影三次曲线}
\[
 Y^2Z+a_1XYZ+a_3YZ^2
       =X^3+a_2X^2Z+a_4XZ^2+a_6Z^3
\]
\bi{is projective, flat and finitely presented over $O$,
and its section $(0:1:0)$ is relatively smooth.
If its generic fibre is smooth and its geometric special
fibre is smooth or nodal, it is a nodal model of that
generic curve.}
{在 $O$ 上射影、平坦、有限表示，
并且截面 $(0:1:0)$ 相对光滑。
若其泛纤维光滑，而几何特殊纤维光滑或仅有结点，
则它是该泛纤维曲线的结点模型。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The homogeneous coordinate ring is a free
$O[Y,Z]$-module with basis $1,X,X^2$, because the equation
is monic of degree three in $X$.
It is therefore $O$-flat.
Localization at each homogeneous coordinate remains flat;
its degree-zero submodule is a direct summand and hence
flat. These degree-zero rings are the standard affine
charts of the projective cubic.
The single homogeneous equation gives finite presentation,
and the closed embedding in $\mathbf P^2_O$ gives
projectivity.
In the affine chart $Y=1$, the partial derivative with
respect to $Z$ is one at $(X,Z)=(0,0)$, so the indicated
section lies in the relative smooth locus.
A nontrivial real-valued valuation ring has only generic
and closed points. The stated two fibre conditions thus
verify the nodal-model hypotheses.}
{齐次坐标环是以 $1,X,X^2$ 为基的自由
$O[Y,Z]$-模，因为方程关于 $X$ 首一且次数为三。
因此它在 $O$ 上平坦。
对任一齐次坐标局部化后仍平坦，
而零次子模为直和项，故也平坦。
这些零次环正是射影三次曲线的标准仿射片。
一个齐次方程给出有限表示性，
在 $\mathbf P^2_O$ 中的闭嵌入给出射影性。
在仿射片 $Y=1$ 中，对 $Z$ 的偏导数在
$(X,Z)=(0,0)$ 处为一，故所列截面位于相对光滑处。
非平凡实值赋值环的谱只有泛点和闭点，
所以所述两个纤维条件验证了结点模型的假设。}
\end{proof}
