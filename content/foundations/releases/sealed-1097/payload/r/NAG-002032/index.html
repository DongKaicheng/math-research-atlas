Open annuli and their radial homotopies

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 669

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Open annuli and their radial homotopies}
{开圆环及其径向同伦}]
\label{cand:openannuluscoordinateisometry}
\bi{Let $K$ be a complete non-Archimedean field with its specified
absolute value. Let}
{设 $K$ 是配备指定绝对值的完备非阿基米德域，并设}
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 X=A_K(r,R),\qquad X'=A_K(r',R'),\\
 0\leq r<R\leq\infty,\qquad
 0\leq r'<R'\leq\infty
 \end{gathered}
\]
\bi{be standard open annuli with coordinates $T,S$. Their radial
strong homotopies are obtained by restricting and gluing the
closed-annulus homotopies of Proposition~\ref{fnd:annulusretraction}.
For any analytic isomorphism $\phi:X\to X'$, there are
$c\in K^\times$ and $\varepsilon\in\{1,-1\}$ such that}
{为标准开圆环，坐标分别为 $T,S$。其径向强同伦由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:annulusretraction} 的闭圆环同伦经限制
与粘合得到。对任意解析同构 $\phi:X\to X'$，存在
$c\in K^\times$、$\varepsilon\in\{1,-1\}$，使}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 |S(\phi(x))|&=|c|\,|T(x)|^\varepsilon&& (x\in X),\\
 \phi(\zeta_{0,\rho})&=\zeta'_{0,|c|\rho^\varepsilon}
       &&(r<\rho<R),\\
 \phi\bigl(H_X(x,u)\bigr)&=H_{X'}\bigl(\phi(x),u\bigr)
       &&(x\in X,\ 0\leq u\leq1).
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{In particular, $\phi$ preserves the radial retractions and
induces a skeleton isometry
$t'=\varepsilon t+v(c)$ for $t=-\log|T|$ and
$v(c)=-\log|c|$. When both boundary radii are positive and finite,
the logarithmic modulus $\log(R/r)$ is invariant under analytic
isomorphism.}
{特别地，$\phi$ 保持径向收缩，并在骨架上诱导等距同构
$t'=\varepsilon t+v(c)$，其中 $t=-\log|T|$、
$v(c)=-\log|c|$。当两端半径均为有限正数时，对数模
$\log(R/r)$ 在解析同构下保持不变。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Every point of $X$ belongs to the ordinary interior of some
closed subannulus $A_K[a,b]$, with $r<a<b<R$. In the formula of
Proposition~\ref{fnd:annulusretraction}, the homotopy keeps
$|T|$ fixed at every parameter value. Its seminorm formula at a
point depends only on that point and $u$, not on the chosen
end radii. Consequently the homotopies on closed subannuli agree
on overlaps. Their restrictions to the ordinary interiors give
a continuous homotopy on $X\times[0,1]$. The initial map, final
radial retraction, and fixed-point identity on the Gauss skeleton
hold on each subannulus, hence on $X$. The same construction
applies to $X'$.}
{$X$ 中每一点均属于某个闭子圆环 $A_K[a,b]$ 的普通内部，
其中 $r<a<b<R$。在
Proposition~\ref{fnd:annulusretraction} 的公式中，同伦在每个
参数值都保持 $|T|$ 不变，而且一点处的半范数公式只依赖该点
与 $u$，不依赖所选端点半径。因此各闭子圆环上的同伦在重叠处
相同。将它们限制到普通内部，便得到 $X\times[0,1]$ 上的连续
同伦。起始恒等映射、终止径向收缩以及 Gauss 骨架逐点固定的
性质在每个子圆环成立，因而在 $X$ 上成立。对 $X'$ 作同一构造。}

\bi{Apply Corollary~\ref{cand:standardopenunitnorms} to
$\phi^*S$ and $(\phi^{-1})^*T$. It gives constants
$c,d\in K^\times$ and exponents $m,n\in\mathbb Z$ with}
{将 Corollary~\ref{cand:standardopenunitnorms} 应用于
$\phi^*S$ 及 $(\phi^{-1})^*T$，得到常数
$c,d\in K^\times$ 与指数 $m,n\in\mathbb Z$，满足}
\[
 |S(\phi(x))|=|c|\,|T(x)|^m,\qquad
 \rho=|d|\,|c|^n\rho^{mn}\quad(r<\rho<R).
\]
\bi{The second identity may be evaluated at Gauss points of any
two distinct radii. Thus $mn=1$, so
$m=n=\varepsilon\in\{1,-1\}$. Surjectivity and the existence of
all Gauss radii show that
$\rho\mapsto|c|\rho^\varepsilon$ maps $(r,R)$ onto $(r',R')$.}
{第二个恒等式可在任意两个不同半径的 Gauss 点处取值，因此
$mn=1$，即 $m=n=\varepsilon\in\{1,-1\}$。由满性以及全部
Gauss 半径的存在性，映射 $\rho\mapsto|c|\rho^\varepsilon$
将 $(r,R)$ 映满 $(r',R')$。}

\bi{Fix $r<a<b<R$ and let $[a',b']$ be the increasing interval
whose endpoints are $|c|a^\varepsilon$ and $|c|b^\varepsilon$.
The norm identities for $\phi$ and its inverse identify the inverse
image of $A_K[a',b']\subset X'$ with $A_K[a,b]$ as a set.
The universal property of analytic domains, proved in
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing}, restricts both
morphisms and their inverse identities to these domains. Hence}
{固定 $r<a<b<R$，并以 $[a',b']$ 表示由
$|c|a^\varepsilon$、$|c|b^\varepsilon$ 两个端点按递增顺序
组成的区间。$\phi$ 与逆映射的范数恒等式表明，
$A_K[a',b']\subset X'$ 的集合论逆像恰为 $A_K[a,b]$。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing} 所证解析域的
泛性质将两个态射及其逆恒等式限制到这些域上，故有}
\[
             \phi|_{A_K[a,b]}:A_K[a,b]\xrightarrow{\sim}A_K[a',b'].
\]
\bi{Theorem~\ref{cand:annuluscoordinateisometry} applies to this
nondegenerate closed-annulus isomorphism. Its Gauss-point and
homotopy identities are the restrictions of the asserted ones:
both homotopies preserve their radii and remain in the respective
closed subannuli. These subannuli cover $X$, proving all the
identities. Negative logarithms give the skeleton isometry;
its slope has absolute value one, so finite logarithmic interval
lengths are preserved.}
{Theorem~\ref{cand:annuluscoordinateisometry} 适用于这个
非退化闭圆环同构。其 Gauss 点与同伦恒等式就是所需恒等式
的限制，因为两个同伦保持半径，并始终位于相应闭子圆环内。
这些子圆环覆盖 $X$，所以全部恒等式成立。取负对数得到骨架
等距同构；其斜率绝对值为一，故有限对数区间长度保持不变。}
\end{proof}
