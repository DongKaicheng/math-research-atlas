Failure of ambient local Weierstrass over trivial valuation

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 250

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Failure of ambient local Weierstrass over trivial valuation}{平凡赋值下环境局部 Weierstrass 性的失败}]\label{fnd:trivialweierstrassobstruction}
\bi{Let $A=K[T]$ and $B=K[T,T^{-1}]$, both with their discrete norms. The rational map $A\to B=A\langle Z\rangle/(TZ-1)$ presents the nonempty proper strict-test domain}
{令 $A=K[T]$ 及 $B=K[T,T^{-1}]$，均采用离散范数。Rational 映射 $A\to B=A\langle Z\rangle/(TZ-1)$ 表示非空真 strict-test 域}
\[
 U=\{x\in\mathcal M(A):|T(x)|=1\}.
\]
\bi{There is no finite strict rational covering $\mathcal M(A)=\bigcup_iV_i$ for which every $U\cap V_i$ is a Weierstrass domain in $V_i$ with radii in $|K^\times|$. The same obstruction applies if such radii are allowed in $\sqrt{|K^\times|}$, which is also $\{1\}$.}
{不存在有限 strict rational 覆盖 $\mathcal M(A)=\bigcup_iV_i$，使每个 $U\cap V_i$ 都是 $V_i$ 中半径属于 $|K^\times|$ 的 Weierstrass 域。即使允许半径属于 $\sqrt{|K^\times|}$，同样存在此障碍，因为该群也等于 $\{1\}$。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The rational point and domain universal properties, Theorems~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology} and~\ref{fnd:strictrationaldomainup}, prove the asserted domain presentation. The seminorm $\eta$ that assigns one to every nonzero polynomial and zero to zero is multiplicative and bounded, and belongs to $U$. Evaluation at $T=0$ gives another bounded point $x_0$, which does not belong to $U$.}
{由 rational 点与域的泛性质，即 Theorems~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology} 与~\ref{fnd:strictrationaldomainup}，得到所述域表示。将每个非零多项式送到一、将零送到零的半范数 $\eta$ 为有界乘性半范数，且属于 $U$。在 $T=0$ 处取值给出另一个有界点 $x_0$，它不属于 $U$。}

\bi{Every nonempty strict rational subset of $\mathcal M(A)$ contains $\eta$. Indeed, write it with a unit-ideal tuple $(g;f_1,\ldots,f_s)$ and inequalities $|f_j|\leq|g|$. If $g=0$, these inequalities force every $f_j$ to vanish at a point, contradicting the unit certificate, so the subset is empty. Otherwise $|g(\eta)|=1$ and $|f_j(\eta)|\leq1$ for every $j$, so $\eta$ belongs to it.}
{$\mathcal M(A)$ 中每个非空 strict rational 子集都包含 $\eta$。事实上，将其写成单位理想 tuple $(g;f_1,\ldots,f_s)$ 及不等式 $|f_j|\leq|g|$。若 $g=0$，这些不等式会迫使某点处全部 $f_j$ 消失，与单位理想证书矛盾，故该子集为空。否则，$|g(\eta)|=1$，且每个 $j$ 都满足 $|f_j(\eta)|\leq1$，所以 $\eta$ 属于该子集。}

\bi{For any nonzero strict affinoid algebra $C$ over this $K$, all elements have values at most one at every bounded point, by Lemma~\ref{fnd:trivialstrictalgebraicbridge} and Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}. Every unit-radius Weierstrass condition $|h_j|\leq1$ is therefore automatic. Algebraically, its presentation is the polynomial quotient $C[S_1,\ldots,S_m]/(S_j-h_j)$, canonically equal to $C$. Thus a nonempty such Weierstrass domain is the entire spectrum of $C$.}
{对这个 $K$ 上的任意非零 strict affinoid 代数 $C$，由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:trivialstrictalgebraicbridge} 与 Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}，每个元素在每个有界点的取值都至多为一。因此，每个单位半径 Weierstrass 条件 $|h_j|\leq1$ 都自动成立。从代数上看，其表示为多项式商 $C[S_1,\ldots,S_m]/(S_j-h_j)$，典范等于 $C$。所以这样的非空 Weierstrass 域就是 $C$ 的整个谱。}

\bi{In any rational covering of $\mathcal M(A)$, choose a member $V$ containing $x_0$. It is nonempty, so it also contains $\eta$. Hence $U\cap V$ is nonempty and proper in $V$. The algebra representing $V$ is strict; the preceding paragraph shows that this intersection cannot be a unit-radius Weierstrass domain in $V$. This proves the contradiction, even if an empty domain is admitted separately by convention.}
{在 $\mathcal M(A)$ 的任意 rational 覆盖中，选择一个包含 $x_0$ 的成员 $V$。它非空，所以也包含 $\eta$。因此，$U\cap V$ 是 $V$ 的非空真子集。$V$ 的 affinoid 代数为 strict，故前一段说明该交不可能是 $V$ 中单位半径的 Weierstrass 域。由此得到矛盾；即使另按约定允许空域，论证仍然成立。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Except in Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis}, $K$ is complete with its fixed \emph{trivial} absolute value. Algebras are commutative and unital, and a strict affinoid algebra is equipped with a norm-admissible Banach norm. The term strict-test affinoid domain has exactly the meaning of Definition~\ref{fnd:stricttestaffinoiddomain}; its targets are strict $K$-affinoid algebras. In the trivial-valuation assertions, all strict rational and Weierstrass radii lie in $|K^\times|=\{1\}$. We allow the zero algebra with its usual conventions.}
{除 Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis} 外，本节假设 $K$ 完备，并固定其\emph{平凡}绝对值。代数均交换含幺，strict affinoid 代数配备 norm-admissible Banach 范数。Strict-test affinoid 域严格采用 Definition~\ref{fnd:stricttestaffinoiddomain} 的含义，其测试目标是 strict $K$-affinoid 代数。在平凡赋值的断言中，所有 strict rational 与 Weierstrass 半径都属于 $|K^\times|=\{1\}$。允许零代数，并采用通常约定。}
