Annulus isomorphisms and radial homotopies

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 662

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Annulus isomorphisms and radial homotopies}
{圆环同构与径向同伦}]
\label{cand:annuluscoordinateisometry}
\bi{Let $0<r<R<\infty$ and $0<r'<R'<\infty$, and let}
{设 $0<r<R<\infty$、$0<r'<R'<\infty$，并设}
\[
 \phi:X=A_K[r,R]\xrightarrow{\sim}X'=A_K[r',R']
\]
\bi{be an analytic isomorphism. Write $T,S$ for the coordinates of
$X,X'$. There are $c\in K^\times$, $\varepsilon\in\{1,-1\}$ and
$h\in\mathcal O(X)$ with $\|h\|_{r,R}<1$ such that}
{为解析同构，以 $T,S$ 分别表示 $X,X'$ 的坐标。则存在
$c\in K^\times$、$\varepsilon\in\{1,-1\}$ 以及满足
$\|h\|_{r,R}<1$ 的 $h\in\mathcal O(X)$，使}
\[
 \phi^*S=cT^\varepsilon(1+h).
\]
\bi{For every $r\leq\rho\leq R$,}
{对每个 $r\leq\rho\leq R$，均有}
\[
 \phi(\zeta_{0,\rho})=\zeta'_{0,|c|\rho^\varepsilon}.
\]
\bi{Thus $\phi$ restricts to an isometry of Gauss skeleta for their
logarithmic metrics. In coordinates $t=-\log|T|$ and $t'=-\log|S|$,
this isometry is $t'=\varepsilon t+v(c)$, where $v(c)=-\log|c|$.
Moreover, for every $x\in X$ and $0\leq u\leq1$,}
{因此 $\phi$ 在 Gauss 骨架上限制为对数度量的等距同构。在坐标
$t=-\log|T|$、$t'=-\log|S|$ 中，该同构为
$t'=\varepsilon t+v(c)$，其中 $v(c)=-\log|c|$。此外，对每个
$x\in X$ 及 $0\leq u\leq1$，均有}
\[
 \phi\bigl(H_X(x,u)\bigr)=H_{X'}\bigl(\phi(x),u\bigr),
 \qquad \phi\circ\tau_X=\tau_{X'}\circ\phi.
\]
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $g=\phi^*S$. Proposition~\ref{cand:annulusunitnormalform},
applied to $g$ and to the inverse isomorphism, gives}
{令 $g=\phi^*S$。将 Proposition~\ref{cand:annulusunitnormalform}
应用于 $g$ 及逆同构，得到}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 g&=cT^m(1+h),&\qquad&\|h\|_{r,R}<1,\\
 (\phi^{-1})^*T&=dS^n(1+k),&&\|k\|_{r',R'}<1,
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{with $c,d\in K^\times$ and $m,n\in\mathbb Z$. The norm identity
in that proposition holds at every analytic point. Composing the two
identities at a point of radius $\rho$ gives}
{其中 $c,d\in K^\times$、$m,n\in\mathbb Z$。该命题的范数恒等式
在每个解析点成立。对半径为 $\rho$ 的点，复合两个恒等式得}
\[
 \rho=|d|\,|c|^n\rho^{mn}\qquad(r\leq\rho\leq R).
\]
\bi{Comparing two distinct radii gives $mn=1$. Hence
$m=n=\varepsilon\in\{1,-1\}$. Surjectivity of $\phi$ and the existence
of the Gauss point at every radius show that
$\rho\mapsto|c|\rho^\varepsilon$ maps $[r,R]$ onto $[r',R']$.}
{比较两个不同的半径得 $mn=1$，故
$m=n=\varepsilon\in\{1,-1\}$。由 $\phi$ 的满性以及每个半径处
Gauss 点的存在性，映射 $\rho\mapsto|c|\rho^\varepsilon$ 将
$[r,R]$ 映满 $[r',R']$。}

\bi{We identify the image of each Gauss point. Set
$q=\|h\|_{r,R}<1$, $\sigma=|c|\rho^\varepsilon$, and take a nonzero
Laurent polynomial $P(S)=\sum_j a_jS^j$. For every integer $j$, the
geometric inverse and the integer binomial formula give}
{下面识别每个 Gauss 点的像。令 $q=\|h\|_{r,R}<1$、
$\sigma=|c|\rho^\varepsilon$，并取非零 Laurent 多项式
$P(S)=\sum_j a_jS^j$。对每个整数 $j$，由几何级数求逆及整数
二项式公式，有}
\[
 \bigl|(1+h)^j-1\bigr|_{\zeta_{0,\rho}}\leq q.
\]
\bi{Consequently}
{因此}
\[
 \left|P(g)-\sum_j a_jc^jT^{\varepsilon j}\right|_{\zeta_{0,\rho}}
 \leq q\max_j|a_j|\sigma^j.
\]
\bi{The exponents $\varepsilon j$ are distinct. The Gauss norm of the
Laurent polynomial on the right inside the difference is therefore
$\max_j|a_j|\sigma^j>0$, while the error has strictly smaller norm.
Thus $|P(g)|_{\zeta_{0,\rho}}=\max_j|a_j|\sigma^j$.
Laurent polynomials are dense in the target affinoid algebra, so bounded
multiplicative seminorms agreeing on them agree on the entire algebra.
This proves the asserted equality of actual analytic points.}
{各指数 $\varepsilon j$ 两两不同，所以差式中该 Laurent 多项式
的 Gauss 范数为 $\max_j|a_j|\sigma^j>0$，而误差范数严格更小。
因此 $|P(g)|_{\zeta_{0,\rho}}=\max_j|a_j|\sigma^j$。
Laurent 多项式在目标 affinoid 代数中稠密，故在其上相等的有界
乘性半范数在整个代数上相等。这证明了实际解析点的所述等式。}

\bi{For an arbitrary $x$, the norm formula gives
$|S(\phi(x))|=|c|\,|T(x)|^\varepsilon$. Combining this with the
Gauss-point equality proves $\phi\tau_X=\tau_{X'}\phi$. Taking
negative logarithms gives the stated affine formula and its preservation
of logarithmic distance.}
{对任意 $x$，范数公式给出
$|S(\phi(x))|=|c|\,|T(x)|^\varepsilon$。将它与 Gauss 点等式
结合，得到 $\phi\tau_X=\tau_{X'}\phi$。取负对数便得到所列
仿射公式及其对数距离保持性。}

\bi{It remains to compare the homotopies for $0<u<1$. Work in
$F=\mathcal H(x)$ and put}
{剩下比较 $0<u<1$ 时的同伦。在 $F=\mathcal H(x)$ 中工作，令}
\[
 \alpha=T(x),\quad \rho=|\alpha|,\quad
 \beta=g(x),\quad \sigma=|\beta|=|c|\rho^\varepsilon,
 \qquad \delta=u\rho.
\]
\bi{For a Laurent series $h=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}h_nT^n$, its
substitution on the disc $|Z|\leq\delta<\rho$ is}
{对 Laurent 级数 $h=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}h_nT^n$，在圆盘
$|Z|\leq\delta<\rho$ 上的代入为}
\[
 h(\alpha+Z)=\sum_{j\geq0}e_jZ^j,
 \qquad
 e_j=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}h_n\binom nj\alpha^{n-j},
 \qquad |e_j|\rho^j\leq q.
\]
\bi{To check the convergence, the weighted terms are bounded by
$|h_n|\rho^n u^j$. The first factor tends to zero as $|n|\to\infty$,
and $u^j$ tends to zero as $j\to\infty$. Above any fixed positive
threshold only finitely many pairs remain. Lemma~\ref{fnd:seriescriterion}
therefore permits the indicated sums and their interchange. The negative
powers use the convergent geometric inverse of $\alpha+Z$.
All integer binomial coefficients have absolute value at most one, which
gives the displayed bound.}
{为验证收敛，各加权项被 $|h_n|\rho^n u^j$ 控制。第一因子在
$|n|\to\infty$ 时趋于零，而 $u^j$ 在 $j\to\infty$ 时趋于零。
超过任意固定正阈值的指标对只有有限个。因此
Lemma~\ref{fnd:seriescriterion} 允许上述求和及交换次序。
负次幂使用 $\alpha+Z$ 的收敛几何级数逆。所有整数二项式系数的
绝对值至多为一，从而得到所列估计。}

\bi{Expand $g=cT^\varepsilon(1+h)$ at $\alpha$ as}
{将 $g=cT^\varepsilon(1+h)$ 在 $\alpha$ 处展开为}
\[
 g(\alpha+Z)=\beta+\sum_{j\geq1}b_jZ^j.
\]
\bi{The binomial coefficients of $(\alpha+Z)^\varepsilon$ have the
same bound, so $|b_j|\rho^j\leq\sigma$ for $j\geq1$.
The first coefficient satisfies}
{$(\alpha+Z)^\varepsilon$ 的二项式系数具有同一估计，所以对
$j\geq1$ 有 $|b_j|\rho^j\leq\sigma$。第一个系数满足}
\[
 b_1=c\alpha^{\varepsilon-1}
       \bigl(\varepsilon(1+e_0)+\alpha e_1\bigr),
 \qquad |b_1|=\frac{\sigma}{\rho}.
\]
\bi{Indeed $\varepsilon=\pm1$ has absolute value one, and both
$e_0$ and $\alpha e_1$ have size at most $q<1$. Hence, in the
radius-$\delta$ Tate algebra over $F$,}
{事实上 $\varepsilon=\pm1$ 的绝对值为一，而 $e_0$ 及
$\alpha e_1$ 的大小都不超过 $q<1$。因此在 $F$ 上半径为
$\delta$ 的 Tate 代数中，}
\[
 g(\alpha+Z)-\beta=b_1Z(1+E(Z)),\qquad
 E(Z)=\sum_{j\geq2}\frac{b_j}{b_1}Z^{j-1},\qquad
 \|E\|_\delta\leq u<1.
\]
\bi{The bound follows termwise from
$|b_j/b_1|\delta^{j-1}\leq u^{j-1}$; the same estimate proves
convergence.}
{估计逐项来自
$|b_j/b_1|\delta^{j-1}\leq u^{j-1}$，同一估计也证明收敛。}

\bi{For a nonzero polynomial $Q(S)\in K[S]$, write
$Q(\beta+W)=\sum_{j=0}^N q_jW^j$ over $F$, and put}
{对非零多项式 $Q(S)\in K[S]$，在 $F$ 上写成
$Q(\beta+W)=\sum_{j=0}^N q_jW^j$，并令}
\[
 M=\max_j|q_j|(u\sigma)^j>0.
\]
\bi{Replacing $W=b_1Z(1+E)$ by $b_1Z$ changes the resulting
polynomial by a series of radius-$\delta$ norm at most $uM<M$.
The Gauss norm of $\sum_jq_jb_1^jZ^j$ is $M$. Consequently}
{将 $W=b_1Z(1+E)$ 换为 $b_1Z$，所得误差级数的半径
$\delta$ 范数至多为 $uM<M$，而
$\sum_jq_jb_1^jZ^j$ 的 Gauss 范数为 $M$。故}
\[
 \bigl\|Q\bigl(g(\alpha+Z)\bigr)\bigr\|_\delta
       =\max_j|q_j|(u\sigma)^j.
\]
\bi{The left side is the evaluation of $Q$ at
$\phi(H_X(x,u))$. This follows from the construction of $H_X$ and
the bounded substitution $T\mapsto\alpha+Z$, whose coordinate and
inverse have norms $\rho$ and $\rho^{-1}$. The right side is the
evaluation at $H_{X'}(\phi(x),u)$; the induced embedding
$\mathcal H(\phi(x))\hookrightarrow F$ is isometric and hence
preserves the Gauss norm. Thus these two points agree on $K[S]$.
Their common nonzero value on $S$ gives agreement on Laurent
polynomials, and density gives agreement on $\mathcal O(X')$.
This proves the homotopy identity for $0<u<1$. At $u=0$ it is the
identity map, and at $u=1$ it is the already proved compatibility of
the radial retractions.}
{左边是 $Q$ 在 $\phi(H_X(x,u))$ 的取值。这来自 $H_X$ 的构造
及有界代入 $T\mapsto\alpha+Z$；该坐标及其逆的范数分别为
$\rho$、$\rho^{-1}$。右边是 $H_{X'}(\phi(x),u)$ 处的取值；
诱导嵌入 $\mathcal H(\phi(x))\hookrightarrow F$ 等距，因而保持
Gauss 范数。所以这两个点在 $K[S]$ 上相等。它们在 $S$ 上具有
相同的非零取值，故在 Laurent 多项式上相等，再由稠密性，在
$\mathcal O(X')$ 上相等。这证明 $0<u<1$ 时的同伦恒等式。
$u=0$ 时为恒等映射；$u=1$ 时则是已证明的径向收缩相容性。}
\end{proof}
