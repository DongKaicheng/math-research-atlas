Restriction, products, and quotient directions

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 864

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Restriction, products, and quotient directions}
{限制、直积与商方向}]
\label{cand:cornerproductlocality}
\bi{The corner depends only on the restricted function on the
support of its cycle. If $p$ is projection from a product, then}
{角点仅依赖函数在循环支集上的限制。若 $p$ 是直积投影，则}
\[
 D_{\varphi\circ p}(C\times E)=(D_\varphi C)\times E.
\]
\bi{Taking a star commutes with the corner after replacing the
function by its local piecewise linear part. If a fan has a
rational lineality space $L$, and the function is pulled back
from its quotient by $L$, the corner is the pullback of the
corner on that quotient, with the same weights.}
{将函数替换成局部分片线性部分后，取星形复形与角点交换。
若扇具有有理线性空间 $L$，且函数来自对 $L$ 的商，则角点
是商上角点的拉回，权重不变。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{All terms of~\eqref{eq:candcornerweight} are determined by
the restricted affine functions. A codimension-one face of
$C\times E$ belonging to the $E$ factor has zero weight for this
corner, since the function has a common affine extension there.
At a face belonging to the $C$ factor, the formula is the corner
weight in $C$ multiplied by the weight in $E$. Stars leave the
normal quotient lattices unchanged. For the last assertion,
$N\cap L$ is saturated; Lemma~\ref{cand:elementarylatticesplitting}
gives a lattice splitting of $N$ over
$N/(N\cap L)$. The fan is then a product of $L$ with its quotient,
so the product calculation applies.}
{公式~\eqref{eq:candcornerweight} 的各项均由限制后的仿射
函数确定。对 $C\times E$ 中来自 $E$ 因子的余维一面，函数
在该处具有共同仿射延拓，故角点权重为零。对来自 $C$ 因子
的面，公式等于 $C$ 中的角点权重乘以 $E$ 的权重。取星形
复形不改变法向商格。最后，$N\cap L$ 饱和，故由
Lemma~\ref{cand:elementarylatticesplitting} 可为
$N\to N/(N\cap L)$ 选择格分裂。此时扇是 $L$ 与商扇的
直积，因此应用上述直积计算即可。}
\end{proof}
