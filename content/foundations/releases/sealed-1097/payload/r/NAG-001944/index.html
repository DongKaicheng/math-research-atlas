\begin{proposition}[\bi{The global generic fibre and its faithfulness}
{全局泛纤维及其忠实性}]
\label{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}
\bi{The affine generic-fibre construction glues to a functor
from admissible formal $K^\circ$-schemes to strict analytic
$G$-spaces. For a formal open $\mathfrak U\subset\mathfrak X$,}
{仿射泛纤维构造粘合为从 admissible 形式 $K^\circ$-概形到
strict 解析 $G$-空间的函子。对形式开集
$\mathfrak U\subset\mathfrak X$，有}
\[
                  \mathfrak U_\eta=\operatorname{sp}^{-1}(\mathfrak U).
\]
\bi{The specialization map is surjective and functorial.
The generic-fibre functor is faithful; it detects equality and
covering of formal open subspaces. A quasi-paracompact formal
scheme has a quasi-separated quasi-paracompact generic fibre.}
{特化映射满射且具有函子性。泛纤维函子忠实，能检测形式开子空间
的相等及覆盖。Quasi-paracompact 形式概形具有 quasi-separated、
quasi-paracompact 的泛纤维。}
\end{proposition}
