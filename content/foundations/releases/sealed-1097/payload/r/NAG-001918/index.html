3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Affinoid generic fibres and integral presentations}
{Affinoid generic fibres 与整系数表示}]
\label{fnd:affineformalpresentations}
\bi{Let $A_0$ be a topologically finite type $O$-algebra without
$\pi$-torsion. Then $A=A_0[1/\pi]$ is naturally a strict
$K$-affinoid algebra. A presentation
$O\langle T\rangle\twoheadrightarrow A_0$ gives a complete
quotient norm on $A$ whose closed unit ball is precisely $A_0$.
Conversely every strict $K$-affinoid algebra has such an admissible
integral presentation. Moreover,}
{设 $A_0$ 为无 $\pi$-挠的拓扑有限型 $O$-代数，则
$A=A_0[1/\pi]$ 自然为 strict $K$-affinoid 代数。
一个表示 $O\langle T\rangle\twoheadrightarrow A_0$ 在 $A$ 上给出
完备的商范数，其闭单位球恰为 $A_0$。反之，每个 strict
$K$-affinoid 代数都具有这样的 admissible 整系数表示。此外，}
\[
 A_0\langle U_1,\ldots,U_m\rangle[1/\pi]
       \simeq A\langle U_1,\ldots,U_m\rangle.
\]
\bi{A homomorphism of admissible integral algebras induces a bounded
homomorphism of these generic affinoid algebras.}
{admissible 整系数代数之间的同态诱导这些 generic affinoid 代数之间
的有界同态。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Write $P=O\langle T\rangle$ and let $J$ be the presentation
kernel. Torsion-freeness gives
$J=J[1/\pi]\cap P$. The orthonormal basis constructed in the proof
of Theorem~\ref{fnd:exactunitballgenerators}, applied to the submodule
$J[1/\pi]\subset K\langle T\rangle$, splits that Banach vector
space as the orthogonal sum of $J[1/\pi]$ and a closed complement.
Every quotient class therefore has a lift attaining its quotient norm.
In particular the quotient unit ball is $P/J=A_0$. The quotient is
the affinoid algebra $A=A_0[1/\pi]$. This also proves the converse:
starting with $K\langle T\rangle/I$, use
$P/(I\cap P)$, which is torsion-free and hence topologically finitely
presented by Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation}.}
{记 $P=O\langle T\rangle$，以 $J$ 记表示核。无挠性给出
$J=J[1/\pi]\cap P$。对
$J[1/\pi]\subset K\langle T\rangle$ 应用
Theorem~\ref{fnd:exactunitballgenerators} 证明中构造的正交基，
将该 Banach 向量空间分解为 $J[1/\pi]$ 与一个闭补空间的正交直和。
所以每个商类都有达到其商范数的提升，商的单位球正是
$P/J=A_0$。该商就是 affinoid 代数 $A=A_0[1/\pi]$。
反向结论也随之成立：对 $K\langle T\rangle/I$ 取
$P/(I\cap P)$；它无挠，故由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation} 为拓扑有限表示代数。}

\bi{The $\pi$-adic topology on $A_0$ is the topology induced by
this quotient norm. A restricted series with coefficients in $A$
has bounded coefficients, so multiplication by a single power of
$\pi$ puts all of them in $A_0$; their convergence to zero is then
equivalent to $\pi$-adic convergence. This proves the displayed
identity. Norm-attaining coefficient lifts also show that
$O\langle T,U\rangle\twoheadrightarrow A_0\langle U\rangle$ is
surjective. The latter ring is torsion-free and topologically finite
type, hence admissible. Finally a map sending $A_0$ into $B_0$
is bounded after inverting $\pi$: scaling by an appropriate power
of $\pi$ gives a bound with constant $|\pi|^{-1}$ in the two quotient
norms.}
{$A_0$ 的 $\pi$-adic 拓扑正是此商范数的诱导拓扑。系数属于 $A$
的受限级数具有有界系数，故乘以同一个 $\pi$ 的幂后可使系数全部属于
$A_0$；此时系数趋于零等价于 $\pi$-adic 趋于零。这证明所列等式。
系数的范数达到提升还说明
$O\langle T,U\rangle\twoheadrightarrow A_0\langle U\rangle$
满射。后一个环无挠且拓扑有限型，因而 admissible。最后，将
$A_0$ 映入 $B_0$ 的映射在倒转 $\pi$ 后有界：用适当的
$\pi$ 的幂缩放，即可在两商范数之间得到常数 $|\pi|^{-1}$ 的界。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{For the remainder of this section $K$ is nontrivially valued,
$O=K^\circ$, and $\pi\in O$ satisfies $0<|\pi|<1$. An algebra of
topologically finite type means a quotient of $O\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle$
with its quotient $\pi$-adic topology.}
{本节以下设 $K$ 的赋值非平凡，$O=K^\circ$，并固定
$\pi\in O$ 使 $0<|\pi|<1$。拓扑有限型代数是
$O\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle$ 的商，配备商 $\pi$-adic 拓扑。}
