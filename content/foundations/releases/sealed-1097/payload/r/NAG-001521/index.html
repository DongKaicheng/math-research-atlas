\begin{definition}[\bi{Finite Laurent gluing data}{有限模 Laurent 粘合数据}]\label{fnd:laurentfinitegluingdatum}
\bi{For the next three results fix $f\in A$ and use the following actual quotient algebras with their quotient norms:}
{在下面三个结果中固定 $f\in A$，并使用下列实际商代数及其商范数：}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 A_+&=A\{T\}/(T-f),& A_-&=A\{U\}/(1-fU),\\
 A_0&=A\{T,U\}/(T-f,TU-1).
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{A finite Laurent gluing datum means finite modules $N_+$ over $A_+$ and $N_-$ over $A_-$, together with an $A_0$-linear isomorphism between $A_0\otimes_{A_+}N_+$ and $A_0\otimes_{A_-}N_-$. Denote the common module by $N_0$, the two maps by $\rho_+,\rho_-$, and their difference by $\delta$. Equip the finite modules with canonical norms. A morphism of such data is a pair of local module maps commuting with the given overlap isomorphisms.}
{有限模 Laurent 粘合数据是指 $A_+$ 上的有限模 $N_+$、$A_-$ 上的有限模 $N_-$，以及 $A_0\otimes_{A_+}N_+$ 与 $A_0\otimes_{A_-}N_-$ 之间的一个 $A_0$-线性同构。将共同的模记作 $N_0$，将两个映射记作 $\rho_+,\rho_-$，其差记作 $\delta$。给各有限模配备 canonical 范数。这类数据之间的态射，是与给定交叠同构交换的一对局部模映射。}
\end{definition}

\bi{The gluing isomorphism and its inverse are bounded by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}. Thus boundedness follows from the finite canonical module structure.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}，粘合同构及其逆有界。因此有界性由有限 canonical 模结构推出。}
