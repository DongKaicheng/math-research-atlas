Relative Schwarz with a presentation and a closed target

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 412

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Relative Schwarz with a presentation and a closed target}{带表示及闭目标的相对 Schwarz 引理}]
\label{fnd:presentedrelativeschwarz}
\bi{Let $f,g:E\to F$ be bounded $A$-linear maps, with $f$ surjective. Suppose there are a bounded surjection $p:E^\flat\to E$ and a closed embedding $j:F\to F^\sharp$ of Banach modules such that $jgp$ is strictly completely continuous. Then $(f+g)(E)$ is closed and its cokernel is a finite $A$-module with its canonical quotient norm.}
{设 $f,g:E\to F$ 是有界 $A$-线性映射，且 $f$ 满射。假设存在 Banach 模的有界满射 $p:E^\flat\to E$ 和闭嵌入 $j:F\to F^\sharp$，使 $jgp$ 严格完全连续。则 $(f+g)(E)$ 闭，其余核是带有典范商范数的有限 $A$-模。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Every contractive Banach $A$-module $M$ admits a bounded norm-strict surjection $q:c_0(I,A)\to M$. Take $I=M_{\leq1}$ and send its basis element indexed by $m$ to $m$. The map is contractive. For fixed $\pi\in K$ with $0<|\pi|<1$, any nonzero $x\in M$ can be divided by a power of $\pi$ to have norm in $(|\pi|,1]$; the corresponding single basis vector gives a lift of $x$ of norm at most $|\pi|^{-1}\|x\|$.}
{每个压缩 Banach $A$-模 $M$ 都有有界 norm-strict 满射 $q:c_0(I,A)\to M$。取 $I=M_{\leq1}$，将以 $m$ 标记的标准基向量送到 $m$。该映射为压缩映射。固定满足 $0<|\pi|<1$ 的 $\pi\in K$；任意非零 $x\in M$ 除以某个 $\pi$ 的幂后，其范数可落在 $(|\pi|,1]$ 中。相应的单个标准基向量便给出范数至多为 $|\pi|^{-1}\|x\|$ 的提升。}

\bi{Apply this to $M=E^\flat$. The composite $jgpq$ is strictly completely continuous by bounded precomposition. Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictcompactclosedtarget} makes $gpq$ completely continuous into $F$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:relativeschwarzlemma}, applied to the surjection $fpq$ and this map, proves the conclusion, because $pq$ is surjective and hence does not change the image of $f+g$.}
{将此应用于 $M=E^\flat$。有界前复合保持严格完全连续性，故 $jgpq$ 严格完全连续。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictcompactclosedtarget}，$gpq$ 进入 $F$ 的映射完全连续。对满射 $fpq$ 与该映射应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:relativeschwarzlemma}，即得结论，因为 $pq$ 满射，因而不改变 $f+g$ 的像。}
\end{proof}
