Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The intrinsic interior criterion}{内禀的内部判据}]
\label{fnd:generalproperreductioninterior}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of analytic spaces, let $y\in Y$, and put $x=f(y)$. With the all-target-domain definition of relative interior,}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 为解析空间态射，$y\in Y$，并令 $x=f(y)$。采用对全部目标域进行测试的相对内部定义，则}
\[
 y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \mathscr R(Y,y)\longrightarrow\mathscr R(X,x)
       \text{ is proper}.
\]
\end{theorem}
