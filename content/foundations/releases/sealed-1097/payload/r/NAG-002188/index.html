Balanced pushforward

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 874

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Balanced pushforward}{平衡推前}]
\label{cand:balancedpolyhedralpushforward}
\bi{Let $C$ be a finite balanced real $d$-cycle in $N_{\mathbf R}$
and let $f:N_{\mathbf R}\to N'_{\mathbf R}$ be an integral affine
map. There is a well-defined balanced $d$-cycle $f_*C$. After
subdivision so that images are cells, its weight on a
$d$-dimensional target cell $\sigma'$ is}
{设 $C$ 为 $N_{\mathbf R}$ 中有限平衡实 $d$-循环，
$f:N_{\mathbf R}\to N'_{\mathbf R}$ 为整仿射映射。存在
良定义的平衡 $d$-循环 $f_*C$。经细分使像成为胞腔后，
它在 $d$ 维目标胞腔 $\sigma'$ 上的权重为}
\[
 w_{f_*C}(\sigma')=
   \sum_{\substack{\dim\sigma=d\,,\ f(\sigma)=\sigma'}}
       w_C(\sigma)[N'_{\sigma'}:f(N_\sigma)].
 \tag{P}\label{eq:candpushforwardweight}
\]
\bi{Facets on which the dimension drops contribute zero.
Pushforward is linear and functorial under composition of
integral affine maps.}
{维数下降的极大胞腔贡献为零。推前线性，并与整仿射映射的
复合相容。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Images of rational polyhedra are rational polyhedra: eliminate
one coordinate at a time from their finite systems of affine
inequalities, pairing a lower and an upper bound for that
coordinate. Equalities are eliminated by substitution. This
finite elimination also permits real constants. Choose a common
target subdivision of all images and pull its defining
hyperplanes back to the source. On every facet where $f$ is
injective, source cells correspond bijectively to the target
cells lying in its image. Formula~\eqref{eq:candpushforwardweight}
therefore defines a finite weighted complex. Adding more
hyperplanes merely subdivides it with the same generic weights;
two choices are compared by their combined hyperplanes. This
also proves invariance under a source subdivision.}
{有理多面体的线性像仍为有理多面体：在有限个仿射不等式中
逐一消去坐标，将该坐标的每个下界与上界配对；等式则以代入
消去。此有限消元也允许实常数。为全部像选择共同目标细分，
再将其定义超平面拉回源空间。在 $f$ 单射的每个极大胞腔上，
源胞腔与其像中包含的目标胞腔一一对应。因此
公式~\eqref{eq:candpushforwardweight} 定义有限带权复形。
增加超平面只产生保持一般权重的细分；合并两组超平面即可
比较不同选择。这同时证明了源复形细分下的不变性。}

\bi{Fix a target face $\tau'$ of dimension $d-1$ and sum over
all source faces $\tau$ of dimension $d-1$ with $f(\tau)=\tau'$.
Multiply the balancing equation at $\tau$, after applying the
linear part of $f$, by
$a_\tau=[N'_{\tau'}:f(N_\tau)]$. For an incident full-rank
facet $\sigma$, Lemma~\ref{cand:facetindexidentity} replaces its
term by $w_C(\sigma)a_\sigma u_{f(\sigma)/\tau'}$. If $f$
has rank less than $d$ on $\sigma$, its normal maps into
$N'_{\tau',\mathbf R}$ and gives zero in the normal quotient.
Each full-rank $\sigma$ with $f(\sigma)\supset\tau'$ occurs
exactly once: the inverse image of the target facet is its
unique facet mapping to $\tau'$. The sum is consequently the
balancing equation for the weights~\eqref{eq:candpushforwardweight}.
This proves balancedness, including faces bordering a zero-weight
region.}
{固定维数为 $d-1$ 的目标面 $\tau'$，对满足
$f(\tau)=\tau'$ 的全部源余维一面 $\tau$ 求和。先对
$\tau$ 处的平衡等式施加 $f$ 的线性部分，再乘以
$a_\tau=[N'_{\tau'}:f(N_\tau)]$。对相邻满秩极大胞腔
$\sigma$，Lemma~\ref{cand:facetindexidentity} 将其项化为
$w_C(\sigma)a_\sigma u_{f(\sigma)/\tau'}$。若 $f$ 在
$\sigma$ 上的秩小于 $d$，其法向映入
$N'_{\tau',\mathbf R}$，故在法向商空间中贡献为零。
满足 $f(\sigma)\supset\tau'$ 的每个满秩 $\sigma$ 恰好
出现一次，因为目标余维一面的逆像是其中唯一映到 $\tau'$
的余维一面。故总和正是权重~\eqref{eq:candpushforwardweight}
的平衡等式。这也覆盖与零权区域相邻的面。}

\bi{Linearity follows from the sum. For a second map $g$, a
facet contributing to $(g\circ f)_*C$ has full rank at both
stages, while a dimension drop cannot be reversed. On a
contributing facet the two finite indices multiply, since $g$
is injective on the relevant direction space. After a common
subdivision, summing these products proves
$(g\circ f)_*=g_*f_*$.}
{线性性来自求和公式。对第二个映射 $g$，向
$(g\circ f)_*C$ 作贡献的极大胞腔在两个阶段均满秩，而已
下降的维数不可能恢复。在有贡献的胞腔上，$g$ 在有关方向
空间上单射，故两个有限格指数相乘。取共同细分后对这些乘积
求和，即得 $(g\circ f)_*=g_*f_*$。}
\end{proof}
