Completion retains the initial relations

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 859

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Completion retains the initial relations}{完备化保留初始关系}]
\label{cand:initialcompletionreduction}
\bi{Let $\widehat I=I\mathcal T_0$, let $\mathcal T_0^{\le1}$ denote the Gauss unit ball, and reduce this unit ball coefficientwise to $k[M]$. Then}
{设 $\widehat I=I\mathcal T_0$，以 $\mathcal T_0^{\le1}$ 记 Gauss 单位球，将其逐系数约化到 $k[M]$。则}
\[
 \operatorname{red}(\widehat I\cap\mathcal T_0^{\le1})=\overline J.
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{One inclusion follows from $I\subset\widehat I$. Given $h\in\widehat I$ with $\|h\|_0\le1$, approximate it by $g\in I$ with $\|g-h\|_0<1$, using Proposition~\ref{cand:torusfibrealgebra}. Then $\|g\|_0\le1$ and $g,h$ have identical reductions. This proves the reverse inclusion. Coefficientwise reduction is a Laurent polynomial because only finitely many coefficients of a convergent Laurent series can have absolute value one.}
{一侧包含来自 $I\subset\widehat I$。给定 $h\in\widehat I$ 且 $\|h\|_0\le1$，由 Proposition~\ref{cand:torusfibrealgebra}，可取 $g\in I$ 使 $\|g-h\|_0<1$。于是 $\|g\|_0\le1$ 且 $g,h$ 约化相同，得到反向包含。逐系数约化是 Laurent 多项式，因为收敛 Laurent 级数仅有有限个系数的绝对值为一。}
\end{proof}
