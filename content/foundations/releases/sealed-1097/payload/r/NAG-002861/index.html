Whole-fibre capture in a boundaryless affinoid exhaustion

I Descent of the polarized mixed tower

Open in the English reader · PDF page 2086

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Whole-fibre capture in a boundaryless affinoid exhaustion]
\label{wfi:exhaustioncapture}
Suppose that \(\Omega\) is boundaryless over \(K\) and has
increasing affinoid domains \(U_1\subset U_2\subset\cdots\)
whose ordinary interiors in \(\Omega\) cover \(\Omega\).
Let \(f\) be any tuple of global analytic units and let
\(w\in t(\Omega)\).
If \(\Omega_{P_0}\) is compact for some closed
full-dimensional box \(P_0\) with
\(w\in\operatorname{int}(P_0)\), then there are an index
\(m\) and arbitrarily small closed full-dimensional boxes
\(P\subset\operatorname{int}(P_0)\), with
\(w\in\operatorname{int}(P)\), such that
\[
 \Omega_P\subset\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(U_m,\Omega),
 \qquad
 \Omega_P\longrightarrow T_P\quad\hbox{is norm-admissible finite},
 \qquad
 F_w^\Omega\subset\operatorname{Int}(\Omega_P/K).
 \tag{4}\label{wfi:exhaustionresult}
\]
The inverse images are full inverse images in \(\Omega\).
The fibre-finiteness assertion in condition 2 of
Theorem~\ref{wfi:wholecollarcriterion} is a consequence here,
not an additional hypothesis.

This applies, in particular, to the analytification of an
affine finite-type \(K\)-scheme, provided the whole weight-box
inverse image is compact. The unit functions may be global
analytic functions; no algebraicity of the tuple is required.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The fibre \(F_w^\Omega\) is a closed subset of the compact
\(\Omega_{P_0}\). Finitely many of the interiors of the
\(U_j\) cover it. Taking the largest index gives
\[
 F_w^\Omega\subset\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(U_m,\Omega).
\]
Boundarylessness and (1) imply
\(F_w^\Omega\subset\operatorname{Int}(U_m/K)\).
Theorem~\ref{wfi:wholecollarcriterion} applies and gives a full
finite collar in \(\Omega\).
To put the collar inside the specified \(U_m\), use the compact
set
\[
 C=\Omega_{P_0}\setminus\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(U_m,\Omega).
\]
Its tropical image is compact and misses \(w\).
Shrink the finite collar box to avoid \(t(C)\), still keeping
\(w\) in its interior. This proves every assertion of (4).
In particular, the same compact argument controls escape
outside \(U_m\); no completeness over \(\Omega\) is inferred
merely from a collar inside \(U_m\).

For \(\Omega=(\operatorname{Spec}B)^{\rm an}\), choose a finite
algebra presentation \(B=K[X_1,\ldots,X_N]/I\).
The exact radius-piece construction
\Dep{fnd:affineanalytificationconstruction} gives affinoid
domains with \(|X_j|\le R_m\), for any increasing sequence
\(R_m\to\infty\). Every point has all coordinates strictly
below some \(R_m\), so their ordinary interiors cover.
The construction is good and Hausdorff, and
\Dep{fnd:algebraicmorphismboundaryless} supplies absolute
boundarylessness. The preceding argument uses only these
properties and the stated compactness of the full inverse
image. It places no extra restriction on the global analytic
unit tuple.
\end{proof}
