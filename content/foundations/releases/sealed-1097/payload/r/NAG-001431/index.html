A lower Laurent bound at value-group radii

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 165

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{A lower Laurent bound at value-group radii}{值群半径下的 Laurent 范数下界}]\label{fnd:strictlaurentbound}
\bi{Under the hypotheses on $A,M$ in Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictlaurentinjectivity}, let $f\in A$ and $r\in|K^\times|$. There exists a real constant $c=c(A,M,f,r,\|\cdot\|)>0$ such that}
{在 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictlaurentinjectivity} 对 $A,M$ 的假设下，取 $f\in A$ 和 $r\in|K^\times|$。则存在实常数 $c=c(A,M,f,r,\|\cdot\|)>0$，使}
\[
 \|L_fw\|_r\geq c\|w\|_r\qquad(w\in E_r(M)).
\]
\bi{The constant is independent of $w$. Consequently $L_f$ has closed image and is norm-strict onto its image. For a trivially valued ground field this theorem includes the radius $r=1$.}
{此常数与 $w$ 无关。因此 $L_f$ 的像为 closed，且它到其像的映射为 norm-strict。对平凡赋值基域，此定理包含半径 $r=1$。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First let $r=1$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictunitlaurentalgebra} makes $E_1(M)$ a finite module with canonical norm over the strict affinoid algebra $B=E_1(A)$. The operator $L_f$ is multiplication by $1-fZ^{-1}\in B$, hence is $B$-linear. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}, it has closed image and is norm-strict. Thus some $C>0$ has the property that every $y$ in the image has a preimage of norm at most $C\|y\|_1$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictlaurentinjectivity} makes that preimage unique. Substituting $y=L_fw$ gives $\|w\|_1\leq C\|L_fw\|_1$ and hence the required bound with $c=C^{-1}$.}
{先取 $r=1$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictunitlaurentalgebra}，$E_1(M)$ 是 strict affinoid algebra $B=E_1(A)$ 上配备 canonical norm 的有限模。算子 $L_f$ 是乘以 $B$ 中的元素 $1-fZ^{-1}$，故为 $B$-线性。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}，其像 closed，且该映射为 norm-strict。因此存在 $C>0$，使像中的每个 $y$ 都有范数不超过 $C\|y\|_1$ 的原像。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictlaurentinjectivity} 保证此原像唯一。取 $y=L_fw$，即得 $\|w\|_1\leq C\|L_fw\|_1$，从而 $c=C^{-1}$ 给出所需下界。}

\bi{For general $r\in|K^\times|$, choose $s\in K^\times$ with $|s|=r$. The substitution $T=sZ$ gives an isometry}
{对一般的 $r\in|K^\times|$，选取满足 $|s|=r$ 的 $s\in K^\times$。代换 $T=sZ$ 给出等距同构}
\[
 \Phi_s:E_r(M)\longrightarrow E_1(M),
 \qquad (m_n)_n\longmapsto(s^nm_n)_n .
\]
\bi{The norms and vanishing tails agree term by term. A coefficient calculation gives}
{范数与趋零尾条件逐项一致。直接计算系数得到}
\[
 \Phi_s L_f=L_{f/s}\Phi_s .
\]
\bi{Applying the unit-radius bound to $f/s$ proves the assertion. The image and norm-strictness statements follow either by this isometry or directly from the lower bound and completeness: convergence of images makes preimages Cauchy, and continuity identifies their limit. No scalar extension or change in the absolute value is involved.}
{将单位半径的下界应用于 $f/s$，即得结论。像的 closedness 和 norm-strictness 可由该等距同构推出，也可由下界与完备性直接得到：像收敛使原像成为 Cauchy 序列，再由连续性识别其极限。这里没有扩张基域或改变绝对值。}
\end{proof}

\section{\bi{The ground field and convergence}{Ground field 与 convergence}}\label{fnd:valuedfields}
\begin{definition}\label{fnd:fieldconventions}
\bi{Throughout the foundations, $K$ is a field complete for a real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value. We permit the trivial absolute value unless a statement explicitly excludes it. Set}
{基础篇中，$K$ 是关于 real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value 完备的 field。除非明确排除，否则允许 trivial absolute value。记}
\[
\begin{gathered}
v(a)=-\log|a|,\qquad \Gamma=v(K^\times),\\
K^\circ=\{a\in K:|a|\leq1\},\qquad K^{\circ\circ}=\{a\in K:|a|<1\},\\
\widetilde K=K^\circ/K^{\circ\circ}.
\end{gathered}
\]
\bi{A complete extension $L/K$ always carries an absolute value restricting to the specified one on $K$. We do not rescale it when changing the ground field.}
{Complete extension $L/K$ 始终配备限制为指定 $K$ 上 absolute value 的 absolute value。改变 ground field 时不重新缩放它。}

\begin{definition}\label{fnd:banachalgebra}
\bi{A Banach $K$-algebra is a commutative unital $K$-algebra $A$ with a complete non-Archimedean norm satisfying $\|ca\|=|c|\|a\|$, $\|1\|=1$, and $\|ab\|\leq\|a\|\|b\|$. A homomorphism is bounded if $\|\phi(a)\|\leq C\|a\|$ for some constant $C$. Equivalent norms define the same Banach topology. A surjective bounded map is admissible if its target norm is uniformly equivalent to the quotient norm; equivalently, it admits lifts with a uniform norm bound. Equality of topologies alone is insufficient at trivial valuation; see Definition~\ref{fnd:normstrictconvention} and Example~\ref{fnd:normtopologycounterexample}.}
{Banach $K$-algebra 是 commutative unital $K$-algebra $A$，配备一个 complete norm。此 norm 为 non-Archimedean，且满足 $\|ca\|=|c|\|a\|$、$\|1\|=1$ 和 $\|ab\|\leq\|a\|\|b\|$。若存在常数 $C$ 使 $\|\phi(a)\|\leq C\|a\|$，则 homomorphism 称为 bounded。Equivalent norms 定义相同的 Banach topology。Surjective bounded map 若使 target norm 与 quotient norm uniformly equivalent，则称为 admissible；等价地，它具有 uniform norm bound 的 lifts。赋值平凡时，仅有 topologies 相同还不够；见 Definition~\ref{fnd:normstrictconvention} 及 Example~\ref{fnd:normtopologycounterexample}。}
\end{definition}
Standing context 3 · exact TeX
\begin{definition}\label{fnd:normstrictconvention}
\bi{For a bounded linear map $u:E\to F$, give $\operatorname{im}u$ both its induced norm from $F$ and its quotient seminorm from $E$. The map is norm-strict, or admissible, when these are uniformly equivalent. Since $u$ is bounded, the additional requirement is a constant $C>0$ such that every $y\in\operatorname{im}u$ has a lift $x$ with $\|x\|\leq C\|y\|$. The constant may be enlarged by any fixed factor greater than one to approximate an unattained infimum.}
{对 bounded linear map $u:E\to F$，在 $\operatorname{im}u$ 上同时考虑来自 $F$ 的 induced norm 与来自 $E$ 的 quotient seminorm。当两者 uniformly equivalent 时，称该 map 为 norm-strict，或 admissible。由于 $u$ 已 bounded，额外条件等价于存在常数 $C>0$，使每个 $y\in\operatorname{im}u$ 都有 lift $x$ 满足 $\|x\|\leq C\|y\|$。当 infimum 不取到时，可将常数乘以任意固定的大于一的因子。}
\end{definition}

\bi{Keep the fixed complete non-Archimedean field $K$, with trivial valuation permitted. For a Banach $K$-space $V$ and $r>0$, put}
{保持固定的完备非阿基米德域 $K$，允许赋值平凡。对 Banach $K$-space $V$ 和 $r>0$，记}
\[
 E_r(V)=
 \left\{(v_n)_{n\in\mathbb Z}:
       \|v_n\|r^n\longrightarrow0\ \text{as }|n|\longrightarrow\infty\right\},
 \qquad
 \|v\|_r=\sup_{n\in\mathbb Z}\|v_n\|r^n .
\]
\bi{We also write its elements as Laurent series $\sum_n v_nT^n$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness} gives completeness. For a bounded operator $f$ on $V$, the operator $L_f=1-fT^{-1}$ is defined by}
{也将其元素写为 Laurent series $\sum_n v_nT^n$。Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness} 给出完备性。对 $V$ 上的有界算子 $f$，定义算子 $L_f=1-fT^{-1}$ 为}
\[
 (L_fv)_n=v_n-fv_{n+1}.
\]

\begin{remark}\label{fnd:strictclusterboundary}
\bi{For arbitrary polyradii, the coefficient description of a free-radius extension replaces the strict coordinate changes used here. Theorems~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules} and~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules} descend closedness and the canonical finite-module topology through that extension, giving the general affinoid statements.}
{对任意多半径，自由半径扩张的系数描述替代这里使用的 strict 坐标变换。Theorems~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules} 与~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules} 沿该扩张下降闭性及有限模的典范拓扑，得到一般 affinoid 陈述。}
\end{remark}
