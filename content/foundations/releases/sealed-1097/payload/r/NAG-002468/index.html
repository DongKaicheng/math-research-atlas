A weighted chart with one residual component

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1079

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[A weighted chart with one residual component]
\label{v038:faithful:weightedchart}
Fix a chart $\operatorname{Spec}C$, a label $\eta\in Q_I$,
and its separator $h$, and put $E=(S_I)_{\bar h}$.
Let $w\in\operatorname{relint}\Delta_I$ and
$s=x_{\eta,w}$. Let $L/K$ be a complete isometric extension
in whose value group all $w_i$ occur. Choose $c_i\in L^\times$
with $v(c_i)=w_i$ and $\prod_i c_i=a$, taking $c_j=1$ for
$j\notin I$. Write $l=\widetilde L$.

There is an admissible $L^\circ$-model $B_0$ of the actual
domain in the completed chart over $L$ given by
\[
                   |X_i|=|c_i|\quad(0\leq i\leq r),
                         \qquad |h|=1.
\]
Its special fibre is
\[
              \operatorname{Spec}(B_0/\mathfrak m_LB_0)
                    \simeq E_l\times\mathbf G_{m,l}^{|I|-1}.
\]
This fibre is smooth and geometrically reduced, and $B_0$
is the spectral unit ball of $B=B_0[1/\pi]$.
Every Shilov point of $\mathcal M_L(B)$ restricts to $s$.
Consequently, for every $g\in C$ nonzero as a rational
function on $X$,
\[
       \rho_B(g)=|g(s)|>0,
       \qquad |g(\xi)|=|g(s)|
                    \quad(\xi\in\Gamma_B).
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Use the separator supplied by
Lemma~\ref{v038:faithful:chartpreparation}.
Choose $i_0\in I$. Choose the $c_i$ for $i\ne i_0$ first
and then put $c_{i_0}=a/\prod_{i\ne i_0}c_i$.
The relation $\sum_iw_i=v(a)$ gives its required norm.
Consider the algebraic base change
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 A_w&=L^\circ[U_0^{\pm1},\ldots,U_r^{\pm1},T_1,\ldots,T_q]
                                      /(\prod_iU_i-1),\\
 C_w&=(C\otimes_R A_w)[1/h],\\
 B_0&=\widehat{C_w},
 \end{aligned}
\]
where $X_i\mapsto c_iU_i$ and completion is $\pi$-adic.
Eliminating $U_{i_0}$ makes $A_w$ a Laurent polynomial
algebra with polynomial variables over $L^\circ$; it is
flat by the coefficientwise ideal criterion
\cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg}. The map $A_w\to C_w$ is
\etale by \cite[Tag 00U2]{StacksAlg}. Thus $C_w$ is
finitely presented and flat over $L^\circ$, and
Lemma~\ref{cand:affineflatformalcompletion} applies.

Its explicit bounded-coordinate presentation imposes the
original chart bounds, $|U_i|=1$, and $|h^{-1}|\leq1$.
As $h$ was already integral, these are exactly the displayed
domain conditions. This proves the assertion about the
actual analytic domain, with its analytic structure.
On reduction, $c_i$ vanishes for $i\in I$ and equals one
for $i\notin I$. The coordinates $U_j$ for $j\notin I$
are therefore the invertible stratum coordinates $X_j$.
After eliminating $U_{i_0}$, the remaining $U_i$, $i\in
I\setminus\{i_0\}$, are independent invertible variables.
Reducing the displayed finite presentation gives precisely
$E_l\times\mathbf G_{m,l}^{|I|-1}$, also for nondiscrete
valuations: integral restricted series reduce to polynomials.
Smoothness and geometric reducedness follow from
\cite[Tags 00U2, 036U, 056T]{StacksAlg}.
Lemma~\ref{cand:reducedspecialcanonical} identifies $B_0$
with $B^\circ$ and its special fibre with $\widetilde B$.

Let $\xi\in\Gamma_B$. Its reduction is a component generic
point by Theorem~\ref{fnd:canonicalgenericshilov}.
The reduced algebra is \etale, hence flat, over
$l[U_i^{\pm1}:i\ne i_0,T]$ after the above identification.
Flat going down, \cite[Tag 00HS]{StacksAlg}, makes its
minimal prime contract to the zero prime of this Laurent
polynomial algebra. The image of $\xi$ in its analytic
unit polytorus times polydisc therefore reduces to the
generic point. It is the Gauss point: divide any nonzero
restricted series by a coefficient of maximum norm; its
nonzero polynomial reduction cannot vanish at that generic
point. Polynomial approximation, or
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm}, gives the Gauss value for
every series.

Restriction to $K$ gives the standard point $z_w$.
Moreover the reduction of $\xi$ contracts to $\eta$ in
the original stratum. Indeed the field extension $k\to l$
is flat by \cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg}, and going down
applied to $E_l\to E$ contracts a minimal prime to the
unique minimal prime of $E$; adjoining the independent
Laurent variables has the same property. Compatibility
of algebraic completion, scalar extension and specialization
is the explicit presentation comparison of
Lemma~\ref{cand:algebraicmodelscalar}.
Proposition~\ref{cand:actualskeletalfibrestrata} now says
that the restricted point is exactly $x_{\eta,w}=s$.
Taking maxima on the finite Shilov boundary gives the
last formula. Its value is positive by
Proposition~\ref{v035:faithfulrationalpl}.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
Let $K$ be complete for its specified nontrivial real-valued
absolute value. Write $O=K^\circ$, $k=\widetilde K$, and
$v=-\log|\cdot|$, and fix $\pi\in O$ with $0<|\pi|<1$.
We use the algebraic model setting of
Theorem~\ref{v035:faithfulglobal}: $\mathcal X$ is a given
projective, flat, finitely presented $O$-scheme, its generic
fibre $X$ is smooth and integral, and a finite collection of
affine opens covering $\mathcal X_s$ has \etale maps to
\[
 \operatorname{Spec}R_\lambda,\qquad
 R_\lambda=
 O[X_0,\ldots,X_{r_\lambda},T_1,\ldots,T_{q_\lambda}]/
                 (X_0\cdots X_{r_\lambda}-a_\lambda),
 \quad 0<|a_\lambda|<1.
\]
Let $S\subset X^{\an}$ be the compact skeleton of the completed
model. All rational functions below belong to the original
field $K(X)$.

For such a chart $\mathcal V=\operatorname{Spec}C$ and a
nonempty $I\subset\{0,\ldots,r\}$, write $S_I$ for its
residual stratum over
\[
 D_I=\{X_i=0\ (i\in I),\ X_j\ne0\ (j\notin I)\}
\]
and $Q_I$ for the finite set of generic points of $S_I$.
These are the strata and labels of
Proposition~\ref{cand:actualskeletalfibrestrata}. If
$s=x_{\eta,w}$, then $I=\{i:w_i>0\}$ and
$\eta\in Q_I$. An irreducible residual component in this
section is a component of this locally closed stratum.
