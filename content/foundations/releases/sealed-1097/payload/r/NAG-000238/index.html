Result 1.7.2

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 61

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}\label{fnd:unitcriterion}
\bi{An element $f\in A$ is a unit if and only if $f(x)\ne0$ for every $x\in\mathcal M(A)$. In that case $|f|$ has a positive minimum on the spectrum.}
{$f\in A$ 为 unit 当且仅当对每个 $x\in\mathcal M(A)$ 都有 $f(x)\ne0$。此时 $|f|$ 在 spectrum 上有正的 minimum。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A unit cannot vanish at a multiplicative seminorm. If $f$ is not a unit, $A/(f)$ is a nonzero affinoid algebra by closedness of ideals. Its nonempty spectrum maps to a point of $\mathcal M(A)$ at which $f$ vanishes. Finally a positive continuous function on a compact space has a positive minimum.}
{Unit 不会在 multiplicative seminorm 处消失。若 $f$ 不是 unit，由 ideals 的 closedness，$A/(f)$ 是非零 affinoid algebra。其非空 spectrum 映到 $\mathcal M(A)$ 中使 $f$ 消失的点。最后，compact space 上的 positive continuous function 有正的 minimum。}
\end{proof}
