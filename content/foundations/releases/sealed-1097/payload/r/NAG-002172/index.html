\begin{lemma}[\bi{A bounded presentation for a formal \etale chart}
{形式 \etale 坐标片的有界表示}]
\label{cand:formaletaleboundedpresentation}
\bi{Let $A_0\to B_0$ be an adic, topologically finitely
presented, formally \etale homomorphism of complete
admissible $O$-algebras. There are polynomials
$F_1,\ldots,F_m\in A_0[Y_1,\ldots,Y_m]$ and an isomorphism}
{设 $A_0\to B_0$ 为完备 admissible $O$-代数之间的
进、拓扑有限表示、形式 \etale 同态。存在多项式
$F_1,\ldots,F_m\in A_0[Y_1,\ldots,Y_m]$ 及同构}
\[
 B_0\simeq
 \widehat{A_0[Y_1,\ldots,Y_m,Q]/
             (F_1,\ldots,F_m,Q\det(\partial F/\partial Y)-1)}.
 \tag{FJ}\label{eq:candformaljacobianpresentation}
\]
\bi{Here completion is $\pi$-adic. If $A_0$ is a standard
semistable algebra, the generic fibre has a presentation
\eqref{eq:candetalejacobianpresentation}, with all
coefficients integral and all coordinates bounded by one.}
{这里取 $\pi$-进完备化。若 $A_0$ 为标准半稳定代数，
则其泛纤维具有
\eqref{eq:candetalejacobianpresentation} 型表示，全部
系数为整，全部坐标的范数至多为一。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $\bar A=A_0/\pi A_0$ and
$\bar B=B_0/\pi B_0$. Topological finite presentation
makes $\bar B$ finitely presented over $\bar A$.
Its unique square-zero lifting property follows from that
of $B_0$: a target $\bar A$-algebra is annihilated by
$\pi$, and every lifted $A_0$-map therefore factors through
$\bar B$. By \cite[Tag 00UR]{StacksAlg},
$\bar A\to\bar B$ is \etale. The global presentation
\cite[Tag 00U9]{StacksAlg} gives}
{置 $\bar A=A_0/\pi A_0$ 及
$\bar B=B_0/\pi B_0$。拓扑有限表示说明 $\bar B$
在 $\bar A$ 上有限表示。其唯一平方零提升性质来自
$B_0$：目标 $\bar A$-代数被 $\pi$ 消灭，因此提升后的
$A_0$-映射因子分解经过 $\bar B$。由
\cite[Tag 00UR]{StacksAlg}，$\bar A\to\bar B$ 为
\etale。全局表示 \cite[Tag 00U9]{StacksAlg} 给出}
\[
 \bar B=\bar A[Y_1,\ldots,Y_m]/
                  (\bar F_1,\ldots,\bar F_m),
 \qquad \det(\partial\bar F/\partial Y)\in\bar B^\times.
\]
\bi{Lift the finitely many polynomial coefficients to
$A_0$, and write $C$ for the uncompleted algebra on the
right of \eqref{eq:candformaljacobianpresentation}.
Then $C/\pi C\simeq\bar B$. We check its algebraic
unique lifting property directly. Across a square-zero ideal
$I$ in a target ring, choose any lifts $y$ of a prescribed
solution modulo $I$. Its determinant is a unit: a lift of
a unit modulo a nilpotent ideal is a unit, by a finite
geometric inverse. The error $F(y)$ lies in $I^m$.
The correction}
{把有限个多项式系数提升到 $A_0$，以 $C$ 记
\eqref{eq:candformaljacobianpresentation} 右侧尚未
完备化的代数。于是 $C/\pi C\simeq\bar B$。
直接检验其代数唯一提升性质。在目标环的平方零理想
$I$ 上，任取所给模 $I$ 解的提升 $y$。其行列式是
单位元：模幂零理想的单位元提升仍为单位元，这由
有限几何求逆可见。误差 $F(y)$ 属于 $I^m$，修正}
\[
                   h=-F'(y)^{-1}F(y)\in I^m
\]
\bi{gives $F(y+h)=0$, since all terms quadratic in $h$
vanish. The same linear identity proves uniqueness; $Q$
must be the inverse determinant at the corrected root.
Thus $C$ has the required unique lifting property.}
{使 $F(y+h)=0$，因为关于 $h$ 的二次及以上项全为零。
同一线性恒等式证明唯一性，而 $Q$ 必须取为修正后
根处的逆行列式。因此 $C$ 具有所需唯一提升性质。}

\bi{The identity modulo $\pi$ now lifts successively to
mutually inverse maps}
{模 $\pi$ 的恒等识别现在逐次提升为互逆映射}
\[
             C/\pi^n C\ \simeq\ B_0/\pi^n B_0
                         \qquad(n\geq1).
\]
\bi{Indeed the kernels of the maps from level $n+1$ to
level $n$ are square-zero. The lifting properties of $C$
and $B_0$ respectively lift the two directions, and
uniqueness makes both composites the identity and makes
the isomorphisms compatible in $n$. Taking inverse limits,
and using the assumed completeness of $B_0$, proves
\eqref{eq:candformaljacobianpresentation}. Finally
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations} and
Lemma~\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion} identify the generic
algebra with the stated restricted-series quotient.
Coefficients in a standard $A_0$ have integral
restricted-series lifts in its defining coordinates, so
this is exactly a bounded presentation of type (J).}
{从第 $n+1$ 层到第 $n$ 层的核为平方零。分别用
$C$ 及 $B_0$ 的提升性质提升两个方向，唯一性使两种
复合都是恒等映射，并使这些同构关于 $n$ 相容。
取逆极限，并使用 $B_0$ 的完备性，得到
\eqref{eq:candformaljacobianpresentation}。最后由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations} 及
Lemma~\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion}，泛纤维代数
就是所称受限级数商。标准 $A_0$ 中的系数在其定义
坐标中具有整受限级数提升，故这恰为 (J) 型有界表示。}
\end{proof}

\section{\bi{The homotopy of a strictly semistable formal model}
{严格半稳定形式模型的同伦}}

\bi{Throughout this section $K$ is complete and nontrivially
valued, $O=K^\circ$, and $\pi\in O$ satisfies
$0<|\pi|<1$. Formal algebras carry their $\pi$-adic
topology. A formally \etale map below has the unique
lifting property for square-zero extensions of discrete
algebras annihilated by a power of $\pi$.}
{本节中 $K$ 完备且非平凡赋值，$O=K^\circ$，并取
$\pi\in O$ 满足 $0<|\pi|<1$。形式代数配备
$\pi$-进拓扑。下文的形式 \etale 映射具有以下唯一
提升性质：对于被 $\pi$ 的某个幂消灭的离散代数，
可沿平方零扩张唯一提升。}
