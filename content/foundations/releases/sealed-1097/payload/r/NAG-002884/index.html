Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Full trace inclusion--exclusion; F8AG-C]
\label{fnd:nag002884:full-trace-finite-cover}
Let \(Q\) be a finite union of affinoid domains in a good Hausdorff
pure \(n\)-dimensional analytic space. Let \(\mathcal F\) be coherent
on \(Q_G\) and let \(\beta\) be compatible finite moment-chart data
of degree \((n-1,n)\) on \(Q\). Its facet construction defines
\(\BB_{Q,\mathcal F}(\beta)=\mathcal I_{Q,\mathcal F}(d'\beta)\).
For any finite affinoid-domain cover \(V_1,\ldots,V_s\) of \(Q\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cover}
 \BB_{Q,\mathcal F}(\beta)
  =\sum_{\varnothing\ne I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,s\}}
         (-1)^{|I|+1}\BB_{V_I,\mathcal F|V_I}(\beta|V_I),
       \qquad V_I=\bigcap_{i\in I}V_i.
\end{equation}
This holds with different unit tuples on different members. Every
term is a full trace on its own actual domain. It does not require
or imply that the individual terms vanish. It is unchanged under
finite refinements and applies to the flux in Green.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
All intersections are actual affinoid domains, by
\tagg{NAG-002411}; all nonempty ones are pure of dimension \(n\),
by \tagg{NAG-002413}. The coefficient on an intersection is the
single actual restriction of \(\mathcal F\), equivalently
\(M_i\otimes_{\cO(V_i)}\cO(V_I)\) in a finite-module presentation.
It is not an independently chosen multiplicity.

For a cover on which \(\beta\) has one representative per member,
apply frozen finite inclusion--exclusion \tagg{NAG-002416} to
\(d'\beta\), then \eqref{eq:facetstokes} on each intersection. This
is \eqref{eq:cover}. On \(V_I\), the two unit tuples from any two
chosen members are both defined and can be adjoined. Their joint
pullbacks represent the same ordinary form; their derivatives have
equal tangential top restrictions by \tagg{NAG-002422}/\tagg{002424}.
Global chain coordinate augmentation \tagg{NAG-002405} gives the
same integral. No unit is extended outside its actual domain.

For two arbitrary finite covers \((V_i)\) and \((W_j)\), refine on
\(V_I\cap W_J\). The same frozen formula gives the finite double
sum with coefficient \((-1)^{|I|+|J|}\). Its forms, derivatives, and
coherent modules are restrictions of the same data on every actual
intersection. Interchanging these finite sums recovers either cover.
This also permits a nonpresenting member to be replaced by its finite
presenting cover, proving the formula as stated. Triple overlaps
are handled by the same actual restrictions; no unmatched jets are
identified across unrelated closed pieces.

If one tuple and representative are defined on all of an affinoid
\(Q\), \tagg{NAG-002401} gives equality of the weighted top chains
on one common real subdivision. Applying the linear normal expression
\eqref{eq:normal} gives facetwise cancellation there. With different
tuples, the invariant assertion is \eqref{eq:cover}; joint-coordinate
comparisons are made on actual overlaps. Intersecting unrelated
moment images is not a substitute for intersecting the source domains.
\end{proof}
