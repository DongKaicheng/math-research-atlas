A generic basis lifted from original sections

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 466

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A generic basis lifted from original sections}{从原截面提升泛基}]
\label{fnd:originalgenericbasisdimensiondrop}
\bi{Let $T=\mathcal M(B)\to X=\mathcal M(A)$ be affinoid, let $x\in X$, and suppose that $C=B\widehat\otimes_A\mathcal H(x)$ is a nonzero integral strict affinoid algebra. For every finite $B$-module $N$, there are a nonnegative integer $r$ and a $B$-linear map $u:B^r\to N$ such that $u_C$ is injective and is an isomorphism on a nonempty Zariski-open subset of $\mathcal M(C)$. If $Q=\operatorname{Coker}(u)$ and $d=\dim C$, then $Q_C=0$ when $d=0$, and otherwise}
{设 $T=\mathcal M(B)\to X=\mathcal M(A)$ 为 affinoid 态射，$x\in X$，并假设 $C=B\widehat\otimes_A\mathcal H(x)$ 是非零的整 strict affinoid 代数。对每个有限 $B$-模 $N$，存在非负整数 $r$ 及 $B$-线性映射 $u:B^r\to N$，使 $u_C$ 单射，且在 $\mathcal M(C)$ 的非空 Zariski 开集上为同构。若 $Q=\operatorname{Coker}(u)$、$d=\dim C$，则 $d=0$ 时 $Q_C=0$，而在其余情形下有}
\[
 \dim\operatorname{Supp}(Q_C)\leq d-1
 \qquad\text{whenever }Q_C\ne0.
\]
\bi{The bijectivity locus of $u_C$ is ordinarily dense in the fibre. The columns of $u$ may be chosen from any fixed finite generating family of $N$.}
{映射 $u_C$ 的双射 locus 在纤维中普通稠密。还可从 $N$ 的任意预先固定的有限生成组中选取 $u$ 的列。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Let $E=\operatorname{Frac}(C)$ and $r=\dim_E(N_C\otimes_C E)$. The images of the given generators span this vector space. Choose $r$ of them which form a basis and use the corresponding original elements of $N$ to define $u$; if $r=0$, use the zero module as source. The map $u_C$ becomes an isomorphism over $E$. Its kernel is a submodule of the free module $C^r$ and becomes zero over $E$. Since $C$ is a domain, a submodule of $C^r$ has no nonzero element killed by a nonzero element of $C$. Thus that kernel is zero.}
{令 $E=\operatorname{Frac}(C)$、$r=\dim_E(N_C\otimes_C E)$。给定生成元的像张成该向量空间，从中选择 $r$ 个构成一组基，再用相应的原模 $N$ 中的元素定义 $u$；若 $r=0$，取零模作为源。映射 $u_C$ 在 $E$ 上成为同构，其核为自由模 $C^r$ 的子模，并在 $E$ 上消失。由于 $C$ 是整环，$C^r$ 的子模没有被非零元素杀掉的非零元素，故此核为零。}

\bi{Right exactness gives $Q_C=\operatorname{Coker}(u_C)$. This is a finite torsion $C$-module. Kill a finite generating family by nonzero elements and multiply those elements, obtaining $a\in C\setminus\{0\}$ with $aQ_C=0$; when $Q_C=0$, take $a=1$. Thus $u_C$ is an isomorphism on $D(a)$, which is nonempty and ordinarily dense by Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity}. If $d=0$, the Noetherian domain $C$ is a field and every finite torsion module is zero. If $d>0$ and $Q_C\ne0$, every prime in its support contains $a$. Prepending the zero prime to a chain of primes containing $a$ shows that its Krull dimension is at most $d-1$. The algebra $C/\operatorname{Ann}(Q_C)$ is strict; its analytic dimension equals its Krull dimension by Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttatedimension} and strict finite normalization. This proves the asserted fibre-dimension bound.}
{由右正合性，$Q_C=\operatorname{Coker}(u_C)$，这是有限挠 $C$-模。用非零元素分别杀掉一组有限生成元，再取这些元素的乘积，得到 $a\in C\setminus\{0\}$，满足 $aQ_C=0$；若 $Q_C=0$，取 $a=1$。于是 $u_C$ 在 $D(a)$ 上为同构，而由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity}，$D(a)$ 非空且普通稠密。若 $d=0$，Noetherian 整环 $C$ 为域，因而有限挠模为零。若 $d>0$ 且 $Q_C\ne0$，则支撑中的每个素理想都包含 $a$；在这样的素理想链前添加零素理想，便知支撑的 Krull 维数至多为 $d-1$。代数 $C/\operatorname{Ann}(Q_C)$ 为 strict，其解析维数由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttatedimension} 及 strict 有限正规化等于 Krull 维数。这就证明纤维维数估计。}
\end{proof}
