Closed immersions and reductions

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 572

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Closed immersions and reductions}
{闭浸入与约化}]
\label{fnd:gradedgermclosedimmersion}
\bi{A closed immersion of analytic germs induces an isomorphism
on reductions. Suppose a germ morphism factors as a closed
immersion into a subdomain germ of its target. It is a closed
immersion near its distinguished point if and only if its
reduction is an isomorphism.}
{解析芽的闭浸入诱导约化同构。设某芽态射分解为到目标的
一个子域芽的闭浸入。该态射在指定点附近为闭浸入，当且仅当
其约化为同构。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For an admissible quotient $A\twoheadrightarrow B$ and
a point of $\mathcal M(B)$, the completed residue fields on
the two sides are identical. Compose an admissible absolute
weighted Tate presentation of $A$ with $A\twoheadrightarrow B$.
The \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{fixed absolute}} presentation result
gives a finite module over the evaluated image of the graded
polynomial algebra. By spectral contraction each coordinate
surviving at its prescribed degree in $B$ already survives at
that degree in $A$. Thus the image of $\widetilde B^{\rm gr}$
is finite over the image of $\widetilde A^{\rm gr}$ in the same field. The valuation criterion
then identifies the reductions. This proves the assertion
on affinoid pieces; finite subdomain covers prove it for
closed immersions of arbitrary germs.}
{对 admissible 商 $A\twoheadrightarrow B$ 及
$\mathcal M(B)$ 中一点，两边的完备剩余域相同。
将 $A$ 的绝对加权 Tate admissible 表示与
$A\twoheadrightarrow B$ 复合。
\hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{固定绝对表示的结果}
给出在分次多项式代数的取值像上的有限模。由谱收缩，在 $B$
中于指定次数存留的坐标在 $A$ 中也于该次数存留。因此
$\widetilde B^{\rm gr}$ 在此域中的像在
$\widetilde A^{\rm gr}$ 的像上有限；赋值判据随即识别两个约化。这先证明 affinoid 片上的断言，再用有限子域覆盖得到
任意芽的闭浸入情形。}

\bi{For the converse write the factorization as
$(Y,y)\hookrightarrow(X',x)\hookrightarrow(X,x)$.
The first part identifies $\mathscr R(Y,y)$ with
$\mathscr R(X',x)$. Hence an isomorphism to $\mathscr R(X,x)$
means that the subdomain germ $X'$ has full reduction.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:allgermdomainclassification} gives
$(X',x)=(X,x)$, so the factorization is a closed immersion
on an ordinary neighbourhood. The forward direction follows
from the first part.}
{反向写出分解
$(Y,y)\hookrightarrow(X',x)\hookrightarrow(X,x)$。
第一部分识别 $\mathscr R(Y,y)$ 与 $\mathscr R(X',x)$。
因此它到 $\mathscr R(X,x)$ 为同构，意味着子域芽 $X'$
具有全约化。Theorem~\ref{fnd:allgermdomainclassification}
给出 $(X',x)=(X,x)$，故该分解在一个通常邻域上为闭浸入。
正向已经由第一部分证明。}
\end{proof}
