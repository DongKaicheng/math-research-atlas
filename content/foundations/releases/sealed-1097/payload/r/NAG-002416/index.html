The integral on a compact good analytic space

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1037

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[The integral on a compact good analytic space]
\label{v037:integration:compactintegral}
Let $X$ be a compact good $K$-analytic space pure of
analytic dimension $d$, let $\mathcal F$ be a coherent
$\mathcal O_{X_G}$-module, and let $\alpha$ be finite
moment-chart data of bidegree $(d,d)$.
For a presenting cover $(V_i)_{i=1}^s$ put
\[
 V_I=\bigcap_{i\in I}V_i,
       \qquad M_I=\Gamma(V_I,\mathcal F).
\]
For nonempty $I$, choose any $i\in I$. Then
\begin{equation}
\label{v037:integration:eqcompactintegral}
 \mathcal I_{X,\mathcal F}(\alpha)
   =\sum_{\varnothing\ne I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,s\}}
        (-1)^{|I|+1}
        \mathcal I_{M_I,f_i|_{V_I}}(\alpha_i)
\end{equation}
is independent of these choices and of the representative
of the finite chart data. Empty intersections contribute
zero. The integral is linear in $\alpha$, additive in
short exact sequences of coherent modules, and nonnegative
if the chart representatives have nonnegative tangential
top coefficients. It may equivalently be constructed from
compatible finite module data as in
Lemma~\ref{v037:integration:coherentcoverdata}.
For affinoid $X$ it agrees with
Theorem~\ref{v037:integration:finitechartintegral}.

More generally, let $Y$ be good, Hausdorff and pure of
analytic dimension $d$, and let $Q\subseteq Y$ be the
union of finitely many affinoid domains of $Y$.
The same construction and all these conclusions hold
for coherent modules and finite moment-chart data on
the compact analytic domain $Q$, including when $Q$
is not good.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The intersections $V_I$ are actual affinoid domains by
Lemma~\ref{v037:integration:separatedintersections}, and
their nonempty members are pure of dimension $d$ by
Lemma~\ref{v037:integration:puritylocality}.
Lemma~\ref{v037:integration:coherentcoverdata} makes
$M_I$ a finite module, identified with the restriction
of any $M_i$ for $i\in I$. Thus every nonzero term is
given by the \hyperref[fnd:nag002405:finite-cell-functional]{finite-cell functional} on the actual affinoid.
Compatibility of the chart forms restricts to every
further domain by
Theorem~\ref{v037:integration:globalchartfaithfulness}.
On $V_I$, adjoining two chosen tuples identifies their
integrals with the integrals of their joint pullbacks,
by the \hyperref[fnd:nag002405:same-affinoid-coordinate-augmentation]{same-affinoid coordinate augmentation}.
The pullbacks have the same tangential top coefficients.
Their cell integrals agree, proving independence of $i$.

Consider two compatible presentations on finite covers
$(V_i)$ and $(W_j)$. On each $V_I$ the domains
$V_I\cap W_j$ form a finite affinoid cover. Apply
Theorem~\ref{v037:localization:globalcoveradditivity}
to the chosen single-chart representative on $V_I$
and the module $\Gamma(V_I,\mathcal F)$.
Inserting its formula in \eqref{v037:integration:eqcompactintegral}
gives the finite double sum
\[
 \sum_{I\ne\varnothing}\sum_{J\ne\varnothing}
       (-1)^{|I|+|J|}
       \mathcal I_{\mathcal F|_{V_I\cap W_J}}
                         (\alpha|_{V_I\cap W_J}).
\]
The modules are the same restrictions of $\mathcal F$,
and the two local form representatives have equal
integrals by the joint-coordinate argument just given.
Interchange the finite sums and apply the cover formula
on each $W_J$. This recovers the expression for the
second presentation. If two data sets agree only after
a common refinement, compare each with that refinement.
Lemma~\ref{v037:integration:separatedintersections}
provides the needed finite intersections. Restriction preserves tangential equality by Theorem~\ref{v037:integration:globalchartfaithfulness}, and the \hyperref[fnd:nag002405:same-affinoid-coordinate-augmentation]{same-affinoid coordinate augmentation} preserves the integral.
This proves all the asserted independences.

Linearity follows after passing to a common cover and
adjoining the two tuples on each member, where addition
is ordinary addition of their pulled-back superforms.
For a short exact sequence of coherent modules, the
affinoid exact equivalence
\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb} makes its section sequence
on every $V_I$ exact. Additivity of the affinoid global
chains in the \hyperref[fnd:nag002405:global-finite-module-chain-weights]{finite-module chain and weights}
therefore proves additivity of the displayed finite sum.

For positivity, order the cover and use the largest
index $j$ of $I$ as the representative in each term.
The $j$-th group is the integral against $\alpha_j$ of
the finite $d$-chain
\[
 C_j=\sum_{J\subseteq\{1,\ldots,j-1\}}(-1)^{|J|}
   \mathcal T_{f_j|_{V_j\cap V_J}}
                   \bigl(\Gamma(V_j\cap V_J,\mathcal F)\bigr),
\]
where $V_j\cap V_\varnothing$ means $V_j$.
All moment tuples in this formula are restrictions of
the single tuple $f_j$ on the affinoid $V_j$.
By Theorem~\ref{v037:globaltrop:finiteimage} and
the \hyperref[cand:polyhedralrefinementproduct]{common finite subdivision lemma}, choose
a finite common subdivision of these actual images
and their boundary images. The boundary images have
dimension at most $d-1$, and are empty for $d=0$.

At the relative interior of any $d$-cell, its whole
fibre in $V_j$ and all its parts in $V_j\cap V_i$
are interior. Near that point the actual image lies
in the cell's saturated $d$-plane. Apply
Theorem~\ref{v037:localization:fibrecover} on $V_j$
to the family consisting of $V_j$ itself and the
$V_j\cap V_i$, $i<j$. The member $V_j$ ensures the
cover hypothesis. Write $D$ for its whole-fibre
algebra, $e_i\in D$ for the overlap idempotents,
$E_Q$ for a transverse Gauss algebra, and $c_Q$ for
the common positive lattice index. The coefficient
of $C_j$ on this cell is
\[
  \frac{1}{c_Q}\dim_{H_Q}
   \left(\prod_{i<j}(1-e_i)\right)
       \bigl(M_j\otimes_{\mathcal O(V_j)}E_Q\bigr)
                                                    \geq0.
\]
Indeed, expansion of the product gives exactly the
alternating sum of the idempotent-restricted module
dimensions in $C_j$. This retains all nilpotent
lengths. The coefficient is already an integer, being
an alternating sum of the integer module weights.
Thus $C_j$ is nonnegative on every $d$-cell.
If its images have dimension less than $d$, the chain
is zero and the assertion is immediate.
The \hyperref[v037:integration:polyhedralcalculus]{nonnegative cell integral} on each cell makes its
integral against $\alpha_j$ nonnegative.
Summing over $j$ proves positivity.

The coherent-module formulation and compatible-module
formulation coincide by
Lemma~\ref{v037:integration:coherentcoverdata}.
When $X$ itself is affinoid, its defining expression
is the one in
Theorem~\ref{v037:integration:finitechartintegral},
so the affinoid comparison follows.

Finally consider $Q$ in the second assertion. Each
of its finitely many affinoid pieces is compact and
closed in the Hausdorff space $Y$. Their finite cover
is a quasi-net by Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasinetcalculus},
so the analytic-domain definition
\ref{fnd:generalnetanalyticdomain} gives $Q$ its
actual analytic-domain structure. It is compact.
By the transitivity in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalnetdomaincalculus}, every
affinoid domain of $Q$ is also an affinoid domain of
$Y$. Its finite intersections are therefore affinoid
by Lemma~\ref{v037:integration:separatedintersections}
applied to $Y$, and every nonempty one is pure of
dimension $d$ by Lemma~\ref{v037:integration:puritylocality}
applied to $Y$.

A coherent sheaf on $Q_G$ restricts to finite modules
on these affinoids by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb}. Conversely,
compatible finite modules glue on $Q_G$ by the same
section-family construction as in
Lemma~\ref{v037:integration:coherentcoverdata}: for
each analytic $T\subseteq Q$, take compatible
sections on $T\cap V_i$. The finite cover is
admissible, so the sheaf axiom and coherence of its
chart restrictions give the coherent glued sheaf, with
the stated affinoid restriction tensors.
Thus all modules, intersections and finite cover
comparisons in the preceding proof are available
on $Q$. Each invocation of the affinoid cover formula
occurs on a single affinoid intersection, and the
complementary-idempotent positivity calculation occurs
on a single affinoid $V_j$. Neither uses goodness of
$Q$. Those arguments prove precisely the same
independence, exact-sequence additivity and positivity
on $Q$, as claimed.
\end{proof}
