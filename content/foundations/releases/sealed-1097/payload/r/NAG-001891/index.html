Cohomology on a finite acyclic cover

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 401

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Cohomology on a finite acyclic cover}
{有限无环覆盖上的上同调}]
\label{fnd:finiteaffinoidcohomology}
\bi{Let \(X\) be an analytic space with an admissible finite cover
\((U_i)_{0\leq i\leq n}\) such that all nonempty finite
intersections are affinoid domains. For a coherent
\(\mathcal O_{X_G}\)-module \(\mathcal F\), the ordered
\v Cech complex of this cover computes \(H^q(X_G,\mathcal F)\).
In particular these groups vanish for \(q>n\).}
{设解析空间 \(X\) 具有 admissible 有限覆盖
\((U_i)_{0\leq i\leq n}\)，其所有非空有限交均为 affinoid 域。
对凝聚的 \(\mathcal O_{X_G}\)-模 \(\mathcal F\)，此覆盖的
有序 \v Cech 复形计算 \(H^q(X_G,\mathcal F)\)。
特别地，这些群在 \(q>n\) 时为零。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The restriction of \(\mathcal F\) to every intersection is
coherent. Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb} gives zero higher
cohomology on that intersection. Thus the hypotheses of
\cite[Tag 03F7, Lemma 21.10.7]{StacksAlg} hold for the covering
on \(X_G\), including repeated-index intersections, and the
all-tuples \v Cech complex computes derived section cohomology.
The ordered-to-all-tuples homotopy comparison used in
Theorem~\ref{fnd:finitebinarytreecech} applies to this finite
intersection coefficient diagram. It identifies the same groups
with the ordered complex, which has no terms above degree \(n\).}
{在每个交叠上，\(\mathcal F\) 的限制仍凝聚。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb} 给出该交叠上的高次
上同调为零。因此，此 \(X_G\) 上的覆盖满足
\cite[Tag 03F7, Lemma 21.10.7]{StacksAlg} 的假设，包括
指标重复的交叠；全部指标的 \v Cech 复形计算导出截面上同调。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:finitebinarytreecech} 中使用的有序复形与
全部指标复形之间的同伦比较，适用于此有限交叠系数图表。
故同一上同调也由有序复形计算，而该复形在 \(n\) 次以上没有项。}
\end{proof}
