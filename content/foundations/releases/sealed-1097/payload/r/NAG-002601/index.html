The full tubes of a subdivision

F Descent of an algebraized polarized tower

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1986

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[The full tubes of a subdivision]
\label{cand:f5sub:tubedata}
In a geometric node tube \(A_e\), list the cuts
\(0<s_1<\cdots<s_m<\ell_e\), and set
\[
 r_0=1,\qquad r_j=\exp(-s_j),\qquad r_{m+1}=|a_e|.
\]
The closed-point tubes of \(\mathcal X[S]_{\Omega^\circ}\) inside
\(A_e\) are precisely the open annuli
\[
              \{r_{j+1}<|x_e|<r_j\},\quad 0\leq j\leq m, \tag{1}
\]
and the open discs, for \(1\leq j\leq m\),
\[
 \{|x_e/b_j-\widehat\alpha|<1\},
 \quad \alpha\in\overline k^\times,\quad
             b_j\in K^\times,\quad v(b_j)=s_j.           \tag{2}
\]
Here \(\widehat\alpha\in\Omega^\circ\) is any lift of \(\alpha\).
The omitted point on \(|x_e|=r_j\) is its Gauss vertex.
The old smooth tubes are unchanged as analytic subsets.

The two ends of (1) carry consecutive component labels, allowing
coincident endpoint labels at an original self-loop.
Each disc (2) has the inserted vertex as its sole boundary point.
On an inserted geometric component the two annular directions
are the normalization points \(0,\infty\); the other residual
points give (2).
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
On \(|x_e|=r_j\), the coordinate \(z=x_e/b_j\) gives
\(\mathcal M(\Omega\langle z,z^{-1}\rangle)\).
Its canonical reduction is \(\overline k[z,z^{-1}]\), with
one component point, by \dep{fnd:standardannuluscanonical}.
The inverse images of its closed points are exactly (2), by
\dep{fnd:formalstratumtubes}; these are open unit discs with
coordinate \(z-\widehat\alpha\).
The intervals between cut circles give the standard open annuli
(1). These open connected sets partition the complement of the
inserted vertices in \(A_e\), so they are its connected components.

No new vertex lies in an old smooth tube.
The specialization partition \dep{v035:formal:partition}, applied
over \(\Omega\), identifies precisely these components with the
closed-point tubes of \(\mathcal X[S]\).
The boundary assertions follow from \dep{v035:formal:tubeends}.
The two ends are retained before any identification of component
labels, so this includes self-loops. The accepted contraction
theorem only removes chains between these retained endpoints.
\end{proof}
