Polynomial-to-Tate flatness over an affinoid base

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 353

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Polynomial-to-Tate flatness over an affinoid base}{Affinoid 底上的多项式到 Tate 平坦性}]
\label{fnd:relativepolynomialtateflatness}
\bi{For a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra $A$ and any positive polyradius $r$, the ordinary ring homomorphism}
{对 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数 $A$ 及任意正多半径 $r$，通常环同态}
\[
                         A[T_1,\ldots,T_n]
                         \longrightarrow A\{r^{-1}T\}
\]
\bi{is flat. At a $K$-rational point of the target, the induced map of local rings identifies their maximal-ideal completions.}
{平坦。在目标的 $K$-有理点处，所诱导的局部环映射将两者的极大理想完备化识别起来。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{We first prove the completion assertion, without strictness assumptions. Write $A=K\{s^{-1}S\}/I$, and let the rational point have coordinates $S=a$, $T=b$. Translation by these coordinates is a bounded automorphism of the relevant Tate algebras, since $|a_i|\le s_i$ and $|b_j|\le r_j$. After translation the point is the origin. Grouping every monomial of total degree at least $N$ by one of the finitely many degree-$N$ monomials proves that reduction modulo $(S,T)^N$ is precisely Taylor truncation. The grouped series remain convergent: division by a fixed monomial multiplies their weighted coefficient norms by a fixed positive constant. Consequently the completion of the localized target is}
{先证明完备化断言，不假设 strict。写 $A=K\{s^{-1}S\}/I$，并设有理点的坐标为 $S=a,T=b$。因为 $|a_i|\le s_i$、$|b_j|\le r_j$，按这些坐标平移给出有关 Tate 代数的有界自同构。平移后该点即原点。把总次数至少为 $N$ 的每个单项式按有限个 $N$ 次单项式之一分组，便知模 $(S,T)^N$ 的约化恰为 Taylor 截断。分组后的级数仍收敛，因为除以一个固定单项式仅将加权系数范数乘以固定正常数。因此，目标局部环的完备化为}
\[
                     K[[S,T]]/I K[[S,T]].
\]
\bi{For the polynomial source, its maximal ideal is generated by the maximal ideal $(S)$ of $A$ together with the $T_j$. The same finite Taylor truncations, first in $T$ and then in $S$, give exactly the same quotients by every power of that maximal ideal. Finite-module exactness of completion, Tag 00MA, passes to the quotient by $I$. This proves the claimed canonical isomorphism. Both local rings are Noetherian; completion is faithfully flat by Tag 00MB. The kernel argument in Lemma~\ref{fnd:tatepolynomiallocalcompletion} therefore proves flatness of the local map at every rational point.}
{对多项式源，其极大理想由 $A$ 的极大理想 $(S)$ 及各 $T_j$ 生成。先对 $T$、再对 $S$ 作相同的有限 Taylor 截断，得到每个极大理想幂商完全相同。有限模完备化的正合性 Tag 00MA 允许通过理想 $I$ 取商，因而得到所称典范同构。两个局部环均 Noetherian，且由 Tag 00MB，完备化忠实平坦。因此 Lemma~\ref{fnd:tatepolynomiallocalcompletion} 中的核论证证明每个有理点处的局部映射平坦。}

\bi{Suppose next that $K$ is nontrivially valued, $A$ is strict, and $r=(1,\ldots,1)$. Put $P=A[T]$ and $B=A\{T\}$. For a maximal ideal $\mathfrak m\subset B$, Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} gives the finite complete residue field $E=B/\mathfrak m$. Evaluation defines an $E$-rational maximal ideal $\mathfrak n$ of $B_E$, lying over $\mathfrak m$. The ordinary extension $B\to B_E=B\otimes_K E$ is finite free, so $B_{\mathfrak m}\to(B_E)_{\mathfrak n}$ is faithfully flat. The first paragraph shows that $(B_E)_{\mathfrak n}$ is flat over the corresponding localization of $A_E[T]$. Since $A_E[T]=P\otimes_K E$ is flat over $P$, the same kernel descent proves that $B_{\mathfrak m}$ is flat over $P$. Testing the kernel of $J\otimes_P B\to B$ at every maximal ideal of $B$, for a finitely generated ideal $J\subset P$, proves that it vanishes by Tag 00HN. The finite-ideal criterion gives flatness of $P\to B$.}
{再设 $K$ 赋值非平凡、$A$ strict，且 $r=(1,\ldots,1)$。令 $P=A[T]$、$B=A\{T\}$。对极大理想 $\mathfrak m\subset B$，Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} 给出有限完备剩余域 $E=B/\mathfrak m$。取值在 $B_E$ 上定义一个 $E$-有理极大理想 $\mathfrak n$，它位于 $\mathfrak m$ 上方。通常扩张 $B\to B_E=B\otimes_K E$ 有限自由，所以 $B_{\mathfrak m}\to(B_E)_{\mathfrak n}$ 忠实平坦。第一段说明 $(B_E)_{\mathfrak n}$ 在 $A_E[T]$ 的相应局部化上平坦。因为 $A_E[T]=P\otimes_K E$ 在 $P$ 上平坦，相同的核下降说明 $B_{\mathfrak m}$ 在 $P$ 上平坦。对 $P$ 的有限生成理想 $J$，在 $B$ 的每个极大理想处测试 $J\otimes_P B\to B$ 的核，Tag 00HN 说明该核为零。有限理想判据便给出 $P\to B$ 平坦。}

\bi{Finally choose a complete extension $L/K$ containing elements whose absolute values are all the radii in a fixed presentation of $A$ and all the $r_j$, and one element of absolute value different from one. Such a field is obtained by completing a rational function field for the weighted Gauss absolute value, as in Lemma~\ref{fnd:gausscommonextension}. Rescaling coordinates makes $A_L$ strict and $B_L=A_L\{r^{-1}T\}$ a unit-radius Tate algebra over it. The preceding case makes $B_L$ flat over $A_L[T]$, hence over $A[T]$, because $A\to A_L$ is flat. The map $B\to B_L$ is faithfully flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalscalarfaithful}. Descent of the same finite-ideal kernels proves that $B$ is flat over $A[T]$.}
{最后，选取完备扩域 $L/K$，使其中存在绝对值等于 $A$ 的某个固定表示中全部半径及所有 $r_j$ 的元素，并存在一个绝对值不为一的元素。按 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gausscommonextension}，将有理函数域关于加权 Gauss 绝对值完备化，即可得到这样的域。重标坐标后，$A_L$ 为 strict，且 $B_L=A_L\{r^{-1}T\}$ 成为其上的单位半径 Tate 代数。前一情形说明 $B_L$ 在 $A_L[T]$ 上平坦，而 $A\to A_L$ 平坦，故它也在 $A[T]$ 上平坦。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalscalarfaithful}，$B\to B_L$ 忠实平坦。下降相同的有限理想核，便得 $B$ 在 $A[T]$ 上平坦。}
\end{proof}
