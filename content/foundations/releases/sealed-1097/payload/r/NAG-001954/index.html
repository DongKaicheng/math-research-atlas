Orthogonal coordinates on one Gauss field

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 549

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Orthogonal coordinates on one Gauss field}
{单变量 Gauss 域的正交坐标}]
\label{fnd:generalgaussfieldbasis}
\bi{Let $K(t)_r$ be $K(t)$ with the Gauss absolute value of
radius $r>0$, and let $K(r)$ be its completion. It has an
orthogonal $K$-basis $(e_i)$, in the Banach sense, containing
$1$. The basis remains orthogonal for Gauss evaluation over
every complete extension of $K$. The constant-coordinate
projection is a bounded $K$-linear retraction $K(r)\to K$.}
{设 $K(t)_r$ 为带半径 $r>0$ 的 Gauss 绝对值的 $K(t)$，
以 $K(r)$ 记其完备化。它在 Banach 意义下具有包含 $1$ 的
正交 $K$-基 $(e_i)$。在任意完备扩域上作 Gauss 求值时，
该基仍正交。常数坐标投影是有界 $K$-线性收缩 $K(r)\to K$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First take $r=1$. For each monic irreducible polynomial
$\bar q\in\widetilde K[t]$, choose a monic lift
$q\in K^\circ[t]$. Use the family}
{先取 $r=1$。对 $\widetilde K[t]$ 中每个首一不可约多项式
$\bar q$，选取首一提升 $q\in K^\circ[t]$。取如下族：}
\[
 1,t,t^2,\ldots,\qquad
 \frac{t^j}{q(t)^m}\quad
       (m\geq1,\quad0\leq j<\deg q).
\]
\bi{All its members have norm one. Their reductions are the
partial-fraction basis of $\widetilde K(t)$ over $\widetilde K$.
For a finite linear combination, divide by a coefficient of
largest norm. A nonzero reduced combination remains, so the
norm is exactly the largest coefficient norm. The same argument
works after any field extension: these rational functions remain
linearly independent over the extended residue field, as is seen
by their distinct principal parts and their polynomial parts.}
{所有成员的范数均为一，其约化为
$\widetilde K(t)$ 在 $\widetilde K$ 上的部分分式基。
对有限线性组合，除以范数最大的一个系数后，仍有非零约化组合，
故其范数恰为最大系数范数。同一论证在扩域后仍成立：
这些有理函数在扩大的剩余域上仍线性无关，可通过不同极点处的
主部和多项式部分直接看出。}

\bi{Their span is dense in $K(t)_1$. To check this, normalize
the denominator of a rational function to have Gauss norm one.
Factor its nonzero reduction into irreducible factors and lift
the product, including its leading scalar, to a polynomial $D$.
The denominator differs from $D$ by a polynomial of norm
strictly less than one. Its inverse is therefore a convergent
geometric series with terms in the algebra generated by
$t$ and the chosen $q^{-1}$'s. Ordinary polynomial division and
partial fractions put each term in the displayed span.
Completion gives the asserted orthonormal basis.}
{此线性包在 $K(t)_1$ 中稠密。为验证这一点，将有理函数分母
正规化为 Gauss 范数一。把其非零约化分解为不可约因子，并把
因子的乘积及首项常数提升为多项式 $D$。原分母与 $D$ 的差的
范数严格小于一，故分母的倒数是收敛的几何级数，各项属于
$t$ 及所选 $q^{-1}$ 生成的代数。通常的多项式除法和部分分式
分解将每项写成所列线性包中的元素。完备化即给出正交归一基。}

\bi{If $r$ is free over $\sqrt{|K^\times|}$, use the Laurent
basis $(t^n)_{n\in\mathbf Z}$ from
Lemma~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusfield}; every nonzero polynomial
has one dominant monomial, so its inverse is a convergent
Laurent geometric series. Otherwise choose the least $d>0$
with $r^d=|c|$, $c\in K^\times$, and put $s=t^d/c$.
Apply the radius-one construction to $K(s)$ and multiply its
basis by $1,t,\ldots,t^{d-1}$. The decomposition into powers
of $t$ modulo $d$ shows both that this spans $K(t)$ over
$K(s)$ and that its weighted Gauss norm is the maximum of
the coordinate norms. After changing the coefficient field,
the same assertion follows by clearing a common polynomial
denominator in $s$: the remaining polynomials in $t$ have
disjoint exponent classes modulo $d$. This also proves
the claimed extension-stability. In every case the coefficient
of the basis vector $1$ gives the stated retraction.}
{若 $r$ 在 $\sqrt{|K^\times|}$ 上自由，使用
Lemma~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusfield} 中的 Laurent 基
$(t^n)_{n\in\mathbf Z}$；每个非零多项式有唯一占优单项式，
故其逆由收敛的 Laurent 几何级数给出。
否则取最小的 $d>0$ 满足 $r^d=|c|$，其中 $c\in K^\times$，
令 $s=t^d/c$。对 $K(s)$ 应用半径一的构造，再把所得基分别乘以
$1,t,\ldots,t^{d-1}$。按 $t$ 的幂模 $d$ 分解，既说明它们
在 $K(s)$ 上张成 $K(t)$，又说明加权 Gauss 范数是坐标范数的
最大值。改变系数域后，清去关于 $s$ 的一个公分母即可得到同一
结论：剩余关于 $t$ 的多项式具有互不相交的模 $d$ 指数类。
这也证明扩域稳定性。各种情形中，基向量 $1$ 的系数投影均给出
所述收缩。}
\end{proof}
