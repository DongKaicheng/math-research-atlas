Faithful flatness of scalar-extension stalks

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 309

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Faithful flatness of scalar-extension stalks}{基域扩张茎的忠实平坦性}]
\label{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}
\bi{Let $X$ be a good $K$-analytic space, let $L/K$ be any complete valued extension, and let $u\in X_L$ map to $x\in X$. The local homomorphism
$\mathcal O_{X,x}\to\mathcal O_{X_L,u}$ is faithfully flat. For every coherent $\mathcal F$ on $X$, its analytic scalar pullback satisfies}
{设 $X$ 为 good $K$-解析空间，$L/K$ 为任意完备赋值扩张，且 $u\in X_L$ 映到 $x\in X$。局部同态
$\mathcal O_{X,x}\to\mathcal O_{X_L,u}$ 忠实平坦。对 $X$ 上每个凝聚层 $\mathcal F$，其解析基域拉回满足}
\[
 (\mathcal F_L)_u
   \simeq \mathcal F_x\otimes_{\mathcal O_{X,x}}\mathcal O_{X_L,u}.
\]
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ and $S=\mathcal O_{X_L,u}$. Fix an affinoid neighborhood $V=\mathcal M(A)$ of $x$. Its inverse image $V_L$ is an affinoid neighborhood of $u$: the inverse image of the ordinary interior of $V$ is an ordinary neighborhood contained in $V_L$. Rational neighborhoods $W$ of $u$ inside $V_L$ form a cofinal family. Each composite}
{令 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$S=\mathcal O_{X_L,u}$。固定 $x$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $V=\mathcal M(A)$。其逆像 $V_L$ 是 $u$ 的 affinoid 邻域，因为 $V$ 的普通内部的逆像是包含于 $V_L$ 的普通邻域。$V_L$ 内的 $u$ 的 rational 邻域 $W$ 构成余终族。每个复合}
\[
 A\longrightarrow A_L\longrightarrow A_W
\]
\bi{is flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalscalarfaithful} and Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness}. Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks} identifies $S$ with the filtered colimit of these $A_W$. The finite-relation proof used in Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticdomainstalkflatness} therefore proves that $S$ is flat over this fixed $A$.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalscalarfaithful} 与 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness} 平坦。Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks} 将 $S$ 识别为这些 $A_W$ 的滤过直极限。因此 Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticdomainstalkflatness} 中的有限关系论证说明，$S$ 在这个固定的 $A$ 上平坦。}

\bi{To pass from $A$ to $R$, let $I\subset R$ be finitely generated and let $z\in I\otimes_R S$ have zero image in $S$. Choose generators $a_i$ of $I$ and write $z=\sum_i a_i\otimes s_i$ with $s_i\in S$. Lift the $a_i$ to the algebra $A$ of one sufficiently small affinoid neighborhood $V$. If $J\subset A$ is the ideal generated by the lifts, the corresponding tensor in $J\otimes_A S$ maps to $z$ and has zero image in $S$. Since $S$ is $A$-flat, this element is zero. The ideal criterion \cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg} gives $R$-flatness. Evaluation is compatible with the isometric embedding $\mathcal H(x)\hookrightarrow\mathcal H(u)$, so this map is local. It is faithfully flat by \cite[Tag 00HP]{StacksAlg}.}
{从 $A$ 过渡到 $R$：设 $I\subset R$ 有限生成，且 $z\in I\otimes_R S$ 在 $S$ 中的像为零。取 $I$ 的生成元 $a_i$，并写 $z=\sum_i a_i\otimes s_i$，其中 $s_i\in S$。将 $a_i$ 提升到一个足够小的 affinoid 邻域 $V$ 的代数 $A$。若 $J\subset A$ 由这些提升生成，则 $J\otimes_A S$ 中相应的张量映到 $z$，且在 $S$ 中的像为零。由于 $S$ 在 $A$ 上平坦，此元素为零。理想判据 \cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg} 给出 $R$-平坦性。取值与等距嵌入 $\mathcal H(x)\hookrightarrow\mathcal H(u)$ 相容，故此映射为局部同态；由 \cite[Tag 00HP]{StacksAlg}，它忠实平坦。}

\bi{On $V$, Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb} represents $\mathcal F|_V$ by a finite $A$-module $M$. Pullback sends a finite free presentation to its cokernel presentation with the same matrix over $A_L$. Thus its scalar pullback is represented by $M\otimes_A A_L$, with the finite scalar comparison of Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalscalarfaithful}. Taking stalks gives $M\otimes_A S$, while $\mathcal F_x=M\otimes_A R$. The displayed identity is now the associativity of ordinary tensor products.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb}，$\mathcal F|_V$ 由有限 $A$-模 $M$ 表示。拉回把有限自由表示变为同一矩阵在 $A_L$ 上的余核表示。因此其基域拉回由 $M\otimes_A A_L$ 表示，并具有 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalscalarfaithful} 的有限标量比较。取茎得到 $M\otimes_A S$，而 $\mathcal F_x=M\otimes_A R$。所列恒等式于是来自普通张量积的结合律。}
\end{proof}
