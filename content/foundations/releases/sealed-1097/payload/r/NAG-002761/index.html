\begin{lemma}[Closed-fibre length; F8ST-L]
\label{lem:length}
Let \((R,\mathfrak m)\to(S,\mathfrak n)\) be a flat local map of
Noetherian rings. Suppose \(S/\mathfrak mS\) is Artinian. For every
finite-length \(R\)-module \(M\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:f8st:closed-fibre-length}
 \length_S(M\otimes_RS)
 =\length_R(M)\,\length_S(S/\mathfrak mS).
\end{equation}
If the map is regular and the closed fibre is zero-dimensional, its
closed fibre is a field and the last factor is one. In particular a
regular local map between Artinian rings preserves these lengths.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Tensor a composition series of \(M\) with \(S\). Flatness preserves
its injections and quotients; every quotient is \(S/\mathfrak mS\).
Additivity of finite length gives \eqref{eq:f8st:closed-fibre-length}. A zero-dimensional
regular local ring has zero maximal ideal, hence is a field. Thus
\(\mathfrak mS=\mathfrak n\) in the regular case. A local map is
faithfully flat when it is flat, so a nonzero composition factor is
never lost.
\end{proof}
