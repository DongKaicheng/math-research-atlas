Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Component scope and support; F8CR-S]
\label{fnd:nag002966:component-support}
In \eqref{fnd:nag002966:component-data}, every \(X_i\) is integral and pure of dimension
\(n\), every \(f_i\) is regular, and every actual good affinoid domain
in \(X_i\) has an \(S_1\) coordinate ring. For an ordinary compactly
supported form \(\eta\) on \(X\),
\[
 \Supp(i_i^*\eta)\subseteq i_i^{-1}\Supp\eta.
\]
If \(\Supp\eta\subset\Int(X/K)\), its pullback has compact support
in \(\Int(X_i/K)\). Moreover
\(i_i^*(\dd\eta)=\dd(i_i^*\eta)\).
The support and functoriality assertions also hold for an arbitrary
closed immersion of good Hausdorff analytic spaces.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The quotients by minimal primes have their actual affinoid structures
by \tagg{NAG-001561} and \tagg{NAG-001562}. Purity of \(X\) gives their
analytic dimension \(n\). Proposition~\ref{prop:length}'s avoidance
argument makes \(f_i\ne0\) in the domain \(A/\mathfrak q_i\).
Every domain is \(S_1\): at a nonzero prime its local ring has a
nonzero element of the maximal ideal which is regular, and the
zero-prime localization is a field. The finite coherent comparison
\hyperref[thm:analyticserre]{NAG-002765} applies to the structure module of this affinoid
and then to its actual good analytic domains. It gives the asserted
analytic stalk and affinoid \(S_1\) statements. It is never applied
to infer \(S_1\) for the original possibly embedded-component ring.

Closed immersions are finite locally with the canonical quotient
norm. By \tagg{NAG-001701}, every point is relatively interior;
\tagg{NAG-001704} and the atlas definition of relative interior give
\[
 i_i^{-1}\Int(X/K)\subseteq\Int(X_i/K).
\]
Their underlying maps identify the source with a closed subspace.
The preimage of the compact support is therefore compact. Pullback
of a zero germ is zero, proving the support inclusion. Finally
\tagg{NAG-002424} gives pullback commuting with both differentials,
with their fixed order unchanged. These statements are about the
true analytic interior; compactness alone does not remove an
analytic boundary.
\end{proof}
