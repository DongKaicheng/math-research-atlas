Affinoid compatibility and arbitrary analytic domains

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 417

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Affinoid compatibility and arbitrary analytic domains}{Affinoid 相容性及任意解析域}]
\label{fnd:generaldomainordinaryinterior}
\bi{Definition~\ref{fnd:alltargetrelativeinterior} agrees with the original affinoid definition. For any analytic domain $V\subset X$, with no goodness or global Hausdorff hypothesis, it gives}
{Definition~\ref{fnd:alltargetrelativeinterior} 与原 affinoid 定义相容。对任意解析域 $V\subset X$，无需 good 或整体 Hausdorff 假设，它给出}
\[
 \operatorname{Int}(V/X)=\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(V,X).
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For an affinoid map, the forward base-change inclusion proves that an affinoid interior point satisfies the new definition, taking $V$ to be the entire affinoid inverse image of each $U$. Conversely, take $U=X$ in the definition. A witnessing affinoid neighborhood $V\subset Y$ has $y\in\operatorname{Int}(V/X)$. Apply Proposition~\ref{fnd:goodinteriorlocaltesting} first with the affinoid pair $(V,X)$ and then with the whole pair $(Y,X)$. Both tests compute the same general relative interior at $y$, so $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$ in the original affinoid sense.}
{对 affinoid 映射，基变换的正向包含说明 affinoid 内点满足新定义：对每个 $U$，取整个 affinoid 逆像作为 $V$ 即可。反之，在定义中取 $U=X$，得到见证的 affinoid 邻域 $V\subset Y$，满足 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(V/X)$。先对 affinoid 对 $(V,X)$、再对整体 $(Y,X)$ 应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:goodinteriorlocaltesting}。两个测试都计算 $y$ 处的一般相对内部，故 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$，这里采用原 affinoid 定义。}

\bi{For a domain inclusion, an ordinary neighborhood of $y$ contained in $V$ meets each affinoid $U\ni y$ in an open neighborhood in $U$. Choose a strict-inequality rational neighborhood inside that intersection, using Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictrationalinteriorneighborhood}. It is the required witness. Conversely, if the definition holds, its witness $W$ over any affinoid $U\ni y$ is an affinoid domain of $U$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoiddomainordinaryinterior} makes $W$, and hence $V\cap U$, a neighborhood of $y$ in $U$.}
{对解析域嵌入，若 $V$ 包含 $y$ 的普通邻域，则它与每个包含 $y$ 的 affinoid 域 $U$ 相交后，包含 $U$ 中的开邻域。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictrationalinteriorneighborhood}，在此交集中选择严格不等式给出的 rational 邻域，便是所需见证。反之，若定义成立，则在任意 affinoid 域 $U\ni y$ 上的见证 $W$ 是 $U$ 的 affinoid 域。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoiddomainordinaryinterior}，$W$ 因而也是 $V\cap U$ 都包含 $y$ 在 $U$ 中的一个邻域。}

\bi{To pass from these neighborhoods to $X$, use its analytic net. Finitely many affinoid domains $U_1,\ldots,U_s$ containing $y$ have union containing a neighborhood of $y$. For each $i$ choose an open subset $O_i\subset X$ with $y\in O_i$ and $O_i\cap U_i\subset V$. Intersect the finitely many $O_i$ with an open neighborhood contained in $\bigcup_iU_i$. The resulting open neighborhood lies in $V$. This proves the assertion for arbitrary analytic domains and also shows that the construction is unchanged by saturated atlas refinements.}
{从这些邻域传到 $X$ 时使用解析 net：存在有限个包含 $y$ 的 affinoid 域 $U_1,\ldots,U_s$，其并包含 $y$ 的一个邻域。对每个 $i$ 选择 $X$ 的开集 $O_i$，使 $y\in O_i$ 且 $O_i\cap U_i\subset V$。将有限个 $O_i$ 与包含于 $\bigcup_iU_i$ 的一个开邻域相交，所得开邻域包含于 $V$。这证明了任意解析域的结论，也说明饱和图册的加细不改变该构造。}
\end{proof}
