Tangential equality on a finite affinoid cover

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1043

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[Tangential equality on a finite affinoid cover]
\label{v037:forms:finitecoverfaithfulness}
Let $V$ be a generalized affinoid space, let $f$ be a finite
tuple of analytic units, and let $V=\bigcup_{i=1}^s V_i$ be
a finite affinoid-domain cover. A smooth ambient superform
$\eta$ near $t_f(V)$ has zero tangential restriction on
every face of $t_f(V)$ if and only if the same holds on
each $t_f(V_i)$.

Tangential equality on a finite polyhedral set is preserved
by $d'$, $d''$, $J$, and multiplication by another superform.
These assertions hold in every bidegree and in every dimension.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
All the actual images are finite polyhedral by
Theorem~\ref{v037:globaltrop:finiteimage}, and
$t_f(V)=\bigcup_i t_f(V_i)$ as point sets. Cut their
complexes by the finitely many supporting hyperplanes of
all their cells, as in Lemma~\ref{cand:polyhedralrefinementproduct}.
This makes every subimage a subcomplex of the resulting
complex on the union. Each cell of that union is contained
in one subimage: a point of its relative interior belongs
to a cell of one subcomplex, and the common face property
then puts the entire cell in that subcomplex.
Thus vanishing on the subimages implies vanishing on every
cell of the common refinement. Independence of subdivision,
proved in Theorem~\ref{v037:integration:globalchartfaithfulness},
gives vanishing on the original image. The converse is
restriction to a polyhedral subset, as in that theorem.

Fix a cell and choose affine coordinates on its span.
Restriction of both cotangent factors commutes with
differentiation along that span. If a tangential coefficient
vanishes on the relative interior, all its derivatives
in tangent directions vanish there. Their continuity
extends the assertion to its faces. This proves the
claims for $d'$ and $d''$. The operations $J$ and wedge
product commute directly with restriction of alternating
covectors. Applying these observations to the difference
of two forms proves preservation of tangential equality.
\end{proof}
