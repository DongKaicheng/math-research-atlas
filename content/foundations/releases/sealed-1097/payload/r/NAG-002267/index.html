Generic normalization of a nodal family

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 766

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Generic normalization of a nodal family]
\label{v036:f5:genericnormalization}
Let $\mathcal Y/O$ be a projective flat finitely presented
nodal curve family. Its relative normalization in the
normalization of $\mathcal Y_K$ is finite over $\mathcal Y$,
normal, projective, flat and finitely presented over $O$,
with reduced nodal geometric fibres. A decomposition of the
generic normalization into open-and-closed pieces extends
uniquely to such a decomposition of this relative normalization.
The construction is functorial for semilinear isometric
isomorphisms, including permutations of the generic pieces.

In particular a nodal $O$-model with smooth geometrically
integral generic fibre is normal and integral.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The generic normalization is finite by
\cite[Tag 0BXR]{StacksAlg}. Since $O[1/\pi]=K$, its composite
with the generic open immersion is quasi-compact and
quasi-separated. The relative integral-closure construction
of \cite[Tag 035I]{StacksAlg} therefore defines
$\nu:\mathcal N\to\mathcal Y$.

Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting} supplies a
finite unramified Galois extension $L/K$ giving split pointed
charts at every special node. Its valuation ring is finite
\textup{\'etale} and faithfully flat over $O$ by
Lemma~\ref{v036:f5:unramifiedcoefficients}.
Relative normalization commutes with this base change and
with the pointed \textup{\'etale} charts by
\cite[Tag 03GV]{StacksAlg}. On a chart
$R_a=O'[u,v]/(uv-a)$ its actual algebra is
\[
 R_a'=R_a\quad(a\ne0),\qquad
 R_0'=O'[u]\oplus O'[v],
\]
by Lemma~\ref{cand:valuationnodenormalization}.
At smooth points it is the identity, since a smooth algebra
over the normal valuation ring is normal
(\cite[Tag 033C]{StacksAlg}). The finitely many nodal charts
and the smooth locus cover the family: an omitted generic
point would have a specialization in the proper closed
fibre, which these open sets cover. These formulas prove,
on an actual \textup{\'etale} cover, finiteness over
$\mathcal Y_{O'}$, normality, and flat finite presentation
over $O'$, with reduced nodal geometric fibres. They require
no choice of labels for the generic components.

Finiteness descends by \cite[Tag 02LA]{StacksAlg}. Normality
descends on local rings by \cite[Tag 033G]{StacksAlg}; flatness
and local finite presentation descend on the induced source
cover by \cite[Tags 036K, 036N]{StacksAlg}. The finite
morphism to projective $\mathcal Y$ gives quasi-compactness
and separatedness, hence finite presentation. Nodality
descends by \cite[Tag 0C5E]{StacksAlg}. Finally finiteness
and projectivity of $\mathcal Y/O$ give projectivity of
$\mathcal N/O$ by \cite[Tags 0B3I, 0C4P]{StacksAlg}.

On every affine open of $\mathcal N$, flatness makes $\pi$
a nonzerodivisor. A labelled generic idempotent is an
element of the total quotient ring satisfying $e^2-e=0$.
Normality and \cite[Tag 034M]{StacksAlg} put it in the
original affine ring. These extensions agree on overlaps
by $\pi$-torsion-freeness; they remain orthogonal and sum
to one. This proves the asserted unique decomposition.
Integral closure and these idempotents are carried to their
counterparts by every indicated isomorphism, proving
functoriality.

For the last assertion, a flat model of the integral generic
curve is integral: each affine coordinate ring embeds in
its generic coordinate ring, a domain. In the preceding
local charts its smoothing parameter is nonzero, because
the generic fibre is smooth. Thus the relative normalization
is locally the identity after the faithfully flat cover.
The original model is consequently normal as well.
\end{proof}
