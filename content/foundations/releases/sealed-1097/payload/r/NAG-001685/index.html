Scalar extension and actual relation ideals

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 271

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Scalar extension and actual relation ideals}{标量扩张与实际关系理想}]
\label{fnd:generalscalarpresentation}
\bi{Let $L/K$ be a complete valued extension, and let $A=K\{r^{-1}T\}/I$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid presentation with its quotient norm. Then}
{设 $L/K$ 为完备赋值扩域，$A=K\{r^{-1}T\}/I$ 为广义 $K$-affinoid 表示，配备商范数。则}
\[
 A_L:=A\widehat\otimes_K L
 \simeq L\{r^{-1}T\}/I\,L\{r^{-1}T\}
\]
\bi{is an isometric algebra isomorphism for the displayed quotient norm. The ideal on the right is the actual extended ideal, which is closed. In particular $A_L$ is a generalized $L$-affinoid algebra, and $A\to A_L$ is isometric. With any equivalent admissible norm the same maps are bounded isomorphisms with bounded inverses.}
{对于所列商范数，这是等距代数同构。右边的理想是实际延伸理想，且为闭理想。特别地，$A_L$ 是广义 $L$-affinoid 代数，而 $A\to A_L$ 等距。对任意等价的 admissible 范数，同样的映射为有界同构，且逆有界。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $D=K\{r^{-1}T\}$. Theorems~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules} and~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules} make $I$ a closed finite $D$-module. Choose generators $f_1,\ldots,f_s$; the surjection $D^s\to I$ is norm-strict for the induced norm on $I$. Applying Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonstrictexact} to its kernel sequence and to $0\to I\to D\to A\to0$ gives}
{令 $D=K\{r^{-1}T\}$。Theorems~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules} 和~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules} 说明 $I$ 为闭的有限 $D$-模。选择生成元 $f_1,\ldots,f_s$；对 $I$ 的诱导范数，满射 $D^s\to I$ 为 norm-strict。对其核序列及 $0\to I\to D\to A\to0$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonstrictexact}，得到}
\[
 (D\widehat\otimes_K L)^s\twoheadrightarrow
 I\widehat\otimes_K L
 \lhook\joinrel\longrightarrow D\widehat\otimes_K L
 \longrightarrow A\widehat\otimes_K L\longrightarrow0 .
\]
\bi{The injection is isometric and closed; the last map is a quotient isometry. Under Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedscalartensor}, the first map is the row $(f_1,\ldots,f_s)$ over $L\{r^{-1}T\}$. Its image is therefore exactly the algebraic ideal generated by the $f_i$, not just its closure. The last quotient is the asserted presentation, and the algebra operations agree on dense polynomial tensors. The scalar embedding is isometric by the same lemma. Equivalent norms give the final assertion by taking the corresponding projective and quotient infima.}
{其中单射等距且像闭，最后一个映射为商等距映射。在 Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedscalartensor} 的识别下，第一个映射就是 $L\{r^{-1}T\}$ 上的行矩阵 $(f_1,\ldots,f_s)$。所以其像恰为 $f_i$ 生成的代数理想，而不只是其闭包。最后的商就是所述表示；代数运算在稠密的多项式张量上相同。由同一引理，标量嵌入等距。对等价范数分别取 projective 与商下确界，即得最后一项。}
\end{proof}
