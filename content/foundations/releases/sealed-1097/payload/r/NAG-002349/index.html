A finite tropical basis over a trivially valued field

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 970

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[A finite tropical basis over a trivially valued field]
\label{v037:analytic:finitetropicalbasis}
Let $k$ be a field with the trivial absolute value, and let
$J\subset k[T_1^{\pm1},\ldots,T_n^{\pm1}]$ be an ideal.
There are finitely many nonzero $p_1,\ldots,p_a\in J$ such that
\[
 \operatorname{Trop}(\operatorname{Spec}k[T^{\pm1}]/J)
   =\bigcap_{j=1}^a\operatorname{Trop}(V(p_j)).
\]
For $J=0$ the empty list is allowed. The set on either side is
the support of a finite rational polyhedral fan. At every weight
outside this set, some $p_j$ has a unique term of least weight.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Form the homogeneous ideal $H\subset k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]$ used
in Theorem~\ref{cand:finitegroebnercomplex}; localization in all
variables followed by $x_0=1$ recovers $J$.
Use ordinary weight initials, taking terms of least weight.
In the present trivial valuation, the hyperplane arrangement in
that theorem is central. On each relatively open cone $C$ its
proof gives one fixed ordinary homogeneous ideal
$H_C=\operatorname{in}_\omega(H)$ over $k$: the minimizing pivot
sets and the surviving entries in their row bases are fixed on
$C$, and these rows generate the ideal in the uniformly bounded
degrees of Proposition~\ref{cand:initialuniformdegree}.
Restrict this finite arrangement to $\omega_0=0$.

For any cone $C$ outside the tropicalization, localization of
$H_C$ in all variables is the unit ideal, by
Theorem~\ref{cand:realinitialdegeneration} and the localization
argument in Theorem~\ref{cand:finitegroebnercomplex}.
Hence $H_C$ contains a monomial $x^{\alpha_C}$, of some degree
$e_C$. In the finite-dimensional space $H_{e_C}$, choose a fixed
row matrix and consider every column set $B$ with a nonzero
maximal minor. There are finitely many such sets. For every one
include all members of its pivot row basis in a list $\mathcal G_C$.

Fix $\omega\in C$ and choose a minimizing pivot set $B$ for
this degree. Lemma~\ref{cand:pluckerpivotweights} and
Lemma~\ref{cand:initialhilbertfunction} show that the initial
rows form a basis of $(H_C)_{e_C}$ and have the identity matrix
in the pivot columns. Since the single monomial vector
$x^{\alpha_C}$ belongs to this space, its column must be a pivot:
otherwise its pivot coordinates would all be zero, forcing the
vector to be zero. Its pivot coordinates then show that the
corresponding initial row is exactly $x^{\alpha_C}$.
Thus a member of the finite list $\mathcal G_C$ has a monomial
initial at every $\omega\in C$.

Dehomogenize the lists for the finitely many excluded cones.
These give nonzero Laurent polynomials in $J$. A homogeneous
polynomial of fixed degree remains nonzero after $x_0=1$, since
its remaining exponent vector determines the exponent of $x_0$.
Every weight outside the tropicalization has a unique least term
in one of the resulting Laurent polynomials. Such a weight is
outside that polynomial's hypersurface tropicalization by the
strong triangle inequality. Conversely, an actual analytic point
of the subscheme is an analytic point of every one of these
hypersurfaces, with the same moment value.
This proves the equality. The fan assertion is the central
arrangement already constructed. If $J$ is the unit ideal,
the polynomial $1$ gives the assertion directly.
\end{proof}
