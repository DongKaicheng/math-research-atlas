Generic-fibre invariance of an affine admissible blowup

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 522

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Generic-fibre invariance of an affine admissible blowup}
{仿射 admissible blowup 的 generic-fibre 不变性}]
\label{fnd:affineblowupgenericinvariance}
\bi{In Theorem~\ref{fnd:formalblowupcharts}, let $A=A_0[1/\pi]$.
There are canonical bounded isomorphisms}
{在 Theorem~\ref{fnd:formalblowupcharts} 的情形，令
$A=A_0[1/\pi]$，则存在典范有界同构}
\[
 B_i[1/\pi]\simeq
 A\langle U_j:j\ne i\rangle/(f_iU_j-f_j:j\ne i).
\]
\bi{The associated spectra are the rational domains}
{对应谱恰为 rational domains}
\[
 V_i=\{x\in\mathcal M(A): |f_j(x)|\leq|f_i(x)|
                                      \text{ for all }j\}.
\]
\bi{They cover $\mathcal M(A)$, and the formal chart transitions
induce exactly their rational-domain intersections. Thus the generic
fibre of the completed blowup, glued from these charts, is canonically
$\mathcal M(A)$.}
{它们覆盖 $\mathcal M(A)$，且形式图册的转移映射恰好诱导这些
rational domains 的交。因此，从这些图册粘合所得的完备 blowup
的 generic fibre 典范地等于 $\mathcal M(A)$。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The final presentation in Theorem~\ref{fnd:formalblowupcharts}
and Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations} give the displayed
algebra isomorphism. It is bounded in both directions: all coordinate
images lie in the respective integral unit balls, and the finite
affinoid quotient topologies agree. Equation $(*)$ in the preceding
proof makes $f_i$ invertible in the generic chart. Its inverse is
$\pi^{-N}(a_i+\sum_{j\ne i}a_jU_j)$. Thus the bounded universal
property of this quotient is exactly that of the rational domain
$V_i$, with $U_j=f_j/f_i$.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:formalblowupcharts} 的最后一个表示及
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations} 给出所列代数同构。
它在两个方向都有界：各坐标的像均落在相应整系数单位球中，且有限
affinoid 商拓扑相容。前一证明的等式 $(*)$ 使 $f_i$ 在 generic
图册中可逆，其逆元为
$\pi^{-N}(a_i+\sum_{j\ne i}a_jU_j)$。因此，该商的有界泛性质
恰为 $V_i$ 的 rational-domain 泛性质，其中 $U_j=f_j/f_i$。}

\bi{At every point of $\mathcal M(A)$ at least one $f_i$ is
nonzero, since $\pi^N\in I$. An index of maximal absolute value
therefore supplies a chart containing that point. On the overlap of
the $i$th and $\ell$th algebraic blowup charts, $f_\ell/f_i$ is
invertible. Its formal completion has generic algebra obtained by
adjoining its inverse with norm at most one. Since $|f_\ell/f_i|\leq1$
already on $V_i$, this cuts out $|f_\ell/f_i|=1$, precisely
$V_i\cap V_\ell$. The inverse-coordinate substitutions agree on
triple intersections. The rational-domain structure sheaf consequently
identifies the gluing of all generic charts with $\mathcal M(A)$.}
{在 $\mathcal M(A)$ 的每个点，至少有一个 $f_i$ 非零，因为
$\pi^N\in I$。取其中绝对值最大的一个，就得到包含该点的图册。
在第 $i$ 与第 $\ell$ 个代数 blowup 图册的交上，$f_\ell/f_i$
可逆。其形式完备化的 generic 代数通过添入范数至多为一的逆元得到。
由于在 $V_i$ 上已经有 $|f_\ell/f_i|\leq1$，这恰好切出
$|f_\ell/f_i|=1$，也就是 $V_i\cap V_\ell$。
逆坐标代换在三重交上相容。因此 rational-domain 结构层将全部
generic 图册的粘合识别为 $\mathcal M(A)$。}
\end{proof}
