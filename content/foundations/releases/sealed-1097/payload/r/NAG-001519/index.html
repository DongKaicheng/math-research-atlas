Maximal fibers of a rational localization

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 232

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Maximal fibers of a rational localization}{Rational 局部化的极大纤维}]\label{fnd:rationalmaximalfibers}
\bi{Let $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$ generate the unit ideal of $A$, put}
{设 $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$ 生成 $A$ 的单位理想，记}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 B&=A\{T_1,\ldots,T_s\}/(gT_j-f_j)_j,\\
 V&=\{x\in\mathcal M(A): |f_j(x)|\leq|g(x)|\text{ for all }j\}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{For a maximal ideal $\mathfrak m\subset A$, let $L=A/\mathfrak m$ and let $x_{\mathfrak m}$ be its bounded residue-field seminorm. There is a canonical bounded isomorphism}
{对极大理想 $\mathfrak m\subset A$，令 $L=A/\mathfrak m$，并令 $x_{\mathfrak m}$ 为其有界剩余域半范数。存在典范有界同构}
\[
 B/\mathfrak m B\ \simeq\ B\otimes_A L\ \simeq\
 \begin{cases}
 L,&x_{\mathfrak m}\in V,\\
 0,&x_{\mathfrak m}\notin V.
 \end{cases}
\]
\bi{The nonzero isomorphism has bounded inverse for canonical norms and is evaluation at $f_j(x_{\mathfrak m})/g(x_{\mathfrak m})$. Every maximal ideal $\mathfrak n\subset B$ is of the form $\mathfrak mB$ for a unique maximal ideal $\mathfrak m\subset A$ with $x_{\mathfrak m}\in V$, and $A/\mathfrak m\to B/\mathfrak n$ is an isomorphism. These fiber identifications commute with rational restriction maps and with finite intersections.}
{非零情形的同构在 canonical 范数下具有有界逆，且正是在 $f_j(x_{\mathfrak m})/g(x_{\mathfrak m})$ 处取值。$B$ 的每个极大理想 $\mathfrak n$ 都唯一写成 $\mathfrak mB$，其中 $\mathfrak m\subset A$ 极大且 $x_{\mathfrak m}\in V$；映射 $A/\mathfrak m\to B/\mathfrak n$ 为同构。这些纤维识别与 rational 限制映射及有限交叠相容。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Theorems~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} and~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} identify the fiber, with its canonical norm, with}
{由 Theorems~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} 与~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule}，该纤维及其 canonical 范数可识别为}
\[
 L\{T_1,\ldots,T_s\}/(\bar gT_j-\bar f_j)_j.
\]
\bi{The coefficient map is the quotient $A\to L$, so this identification respects constants and all displayed variables. The field $L$ is finite over $K$. By Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}, its extending absolute value is equivalent to its canonical quotient norm.}
{系数映射是商映射 $A\to L$，所以该识别保持常数及全部所列变量。域 $L$ 在 $K$ 上有限。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}，其延伸绝对值与 canonical 商范数一致等价。}

\bi{Suppose first that $x_{\mathfrak m}\in V$. The unit-ideal relation forces $\bar g\ne0$. Set $b_j=\bar f_j/\bar g$. Since $|b_j|_L\leq1$, the sequences $(b_j^n)_{n\geq0}$ are bounded for the canonical norm on $L$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictquotientboundedup} therefore permits substitution $T_j\mapsto b_j$. Because $\bar g$ is a unit, the relation ideal equals $(T_j-b_j)_j$. Substitution and the inclusion of constants are bounded inverse maps, by polynomial density. This proves the nonzero case without identifying the canonical norm with the field absolute value.}
{先设 $x_{\mathfrak m}\in V$。单位理想关系迫使 $\bar g\ne0$。令 $b_j=\bar f_j/\bar g$。由 $|b_j|_L\leq1$，序列 $(b_j^n)_{n\geq0}$ 在 $L$ 的 canonical 范数下有界。因此 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictquotientboundedup} 允许代入 $T_j\mapsto b_j$。由于 $\bar g$ 为单位，关系理想等于 $(T_j-b_j)_j$。由多项式稠密性，代入映射与常数包含为有界互逆映射。这证明非零情形，并未将 canonical 范数与域的绝对值混同。}

\bi{If $x_{\mathfrak m}\notin V$ and $\bar g=0$, at least one $\bar f_j$ is nonzero, so the relation ideal contains a unit. Otherwise some $|\bar f_j/\bar g|_L>1$. Put $\lambda=\bar g/\bar f_j$. The norm equivalence gives $\|\lambda^n\|\leq C|\lambda|_L^n\to0$, so $\sum_{n\geq0}\lambda^nT_j^n$ belongs to $L\{T_1,\ldots,T_s\}$ and is the inverse of $1-\lambda T_j$, as is checked by its finite partial sums. The relation $\bar gT_j-\bar f_j=-\bar f_j(1-\lambda T_j)$ is thus a unit. This proves that the actual fiber algebra is zero.}
{若 $x_{\mathfrak m}\notin V$ 且 $\bar g=0$，则至少有一个 $\bar f_j$ 非零，故关系理想含有单位。否则，存在 $j$ 使 $|\bar f_j/\bar g|_L>1$。令 $\lambda=\bar g/\bar f_j$。范数等价给出 $\|\lambda^n\|\leq C|\lambda|_L^n\to0$，所以 $\sum_{n\geq0}\lambda^nT_j^n$ 属于 $L\{T_1,\ldots,T_s\}$；通过有限部分和可核对，它正是 $1-\lambda T_j$ 的逆。于是关系 $\bar gT_j-\bar f_j=-\bar f_j(1-\lambda T_j)$ 为单位。这证明实际纤维代数为零。}

\bi{Now take a maximal ideal $\mathfrak n$ of $B$. Its residue field $E$ is finite over $K$ by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}. The image of $A$ in $E$ is a finite-dimensional $K$-domain and hence a field by \cite[Tag 00GS, Lemma 10.36.19(1)]{StacksAlg}. Thus $\mathfrak m=\mathfrak n\cap A$ is maximal. The absolute value on this subfield is the unique extending one by Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}. The bounded point of $B$ defined by $E$ restricts to $x_{\mathfrak m}$, which belongs to $V$ by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology}. The already computed fiber $B/\mathfrak mB=L$ has no proper nonzero ideal, and its quotient $B/\mathfrak n$ is nonzero. Consequently $\mathfrak n=\mathfrak mB$ and $E=L$. Conversely, for $x_{\mathfrak m}\in V$, the computed fiber shows that $\mathfrak mB$ is maximal. All nonzero fiber maps fix $L$ and substitute the prescribed ratios; maps involving a zero fiber have their unique possible value. This proves compatibility with the restriction and joint-presentation maps of Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation}.}
{再取 $B$ 的极大理想 $\mathfrak n$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}，其剩余域 $E$ 在 $K$ 上有限。$A$ 在 $E$ 中的像为有限维 $K$-整环，故由 \cite[Tag 00GS, Lemma 10.36.19(1)]{StacksAlg} 它是域。因此 $\mathfrak m=\mathfrak n\cap A$ 极大。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}，这个子域上的绝对值是唯一的延伸绝对值。$E$ 定义的 $B$ 的有界点限制为 $x_{\mathfrak m}$；由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology}，该点属于 $V$。已经算出的纤维 $B/\mathfrak mB=L$ 没有非零真理想，而其商 $B/\mathfrak n$ 非零。因此 $\mathfrak n=\mathfrak mB$ 且 $E=L$。反之，当 $x_{\mathfrak m}\in V$ 时，纤维计算说明 $\mathfrak mB$ 极大。所有非零纤维映射均固定 $L$ 并代入指定比值；涉及零纤维的映射只有唯一可能的值。这证明它们与 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation} 的限制及联合表示映射相容。}
\end{proof}
