Differentials on analytic domains

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 395

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Differentials on analytic domains}{解析域上的微分}]
\label{fnd:analyticdifferentialdomains}
\bi{If $C$ is the algebra of an affinoid domain in $\mathcal M(B)$, then for every bounded affinoid map $A\to B$ the natural map is an isomorphism}
{若 $C$ 为 $\mathcal M(B)$ 中某个 affinoid 域的代数，则对每个有界 affinoid 映射 $A\to B$，自然映射为同构}
\[
 C\otimes_B\Omega^{\mathrm{an}}_{B/A}
        \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\Omega^{\mathrm{an}}_{C/A}.
\]
\bi{In particular $\Omega^{\mathrm{an}}_{C/B}=0$. For an arbitrary analytic morphism $f:Y\to X$, these modules determine a coherent sheaf $\Omega_{Y/X}$ on $Y_G$, compatible with restriction to analytic domains and with every analytic base change over a complete ground-field extension.}
{特别地，$\Omega^{\mathrm{an}}_{C/B}=0$。对任意解析态射 $f:Y\to X$，这些微分模在 $Y_G$ 上确定一个凝聚层 $\Omega_{Y/X}$，并与解析域限制及完备基域扩张上的任意解析基变换相容。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First suppose $C=B\{\rho^{-1}S\}/(gS_i-f_i)$ is rational. The unit-ideal condition makes $g$ invertible in $C$. For a finite $C$-module $N$, every bounded $A$-derivation $D:B\to N$ has a unique possible extension to $C$, namely}
{先设 $C=B\{\rho^{-1}S\}/(gS_i-f_i)$ 为 rational 代数。单位理想条件使 $g$ 在 $C$ 中可逆。对有限 $C$-模 $N$，每个有界 $A$-导子 $D:B\to N$ 若延拓到 $C$，只能满足}
\[
 D(S_i)=g^{-1}\bigl(D(f_i)-S_iD(g)\bigr).
\]
\bi{These values do give an extension. On a series $\sum_\nu b_\nu S^\nu$, differentiate coefficients using $D$ and variables using the displayed values. The coefficientwise derivative term converges because $D$ is bounded; the variable term converges by the weighted derivative estimate used above and the uniform power bounds on the images of the $S_i$. Both estimates are uniform in the series norm. The resulting bounded derivation kills $gS_i-f_i$ and hence their ideal, so it descends through the norm-admissible quotient. Thus restriction induces a natural bijection on derivations, which proves the differential isomorphism by the universal property. Taking $A=B$ proves the vanishing.}
{这些值确实给出延拓。对级数 $\sum_\nu b_\nu S^\nu$，分别以 $D$ 微分系数，并按所列公式微分变量。由于 $D$ 有界，系数微分项收敛；由前面的加权微分估计及各 $S_i$ 像的一致幂界，变量微分项也收敛。两个估计均以级数范数一致控制。所得有界导子消灭 $gS_i-f_i$，因而消灭其生成理想，并通过 norm-admissible 商下降。因此限制在导子之间给出自然双射；泛性质证明微分同构。取 $A=B$ 得到消失结论。}

\bi{For a general domain, Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert} supplies a finite cover by domains rational in $\mathcal M(B)$. They are also rational in $\mathcal M(C)$: pull back their defining inequalities and use the domain universal property. On each covering algebra $C_i$, the rational result identifies both sides of the proposed map with $\Omega^{\mathrm{an}}_{C_i/A}$. The finite affinoid cover is faithfully flat on finite modules, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidcovercech} and Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness}; applying it to the kernel and cokernel proves that the original map is an isomorphism.}
{对一般域，Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert} 给出由 $\mathcal M(B)$ 中 rational 域组成的有限覆盖。它们在 $\mathcal M(C)$ 中也为 rational 域：拉回定义不等式，再用域的泛性质即可。在每个覆盖代数 $C_i$ 上，rational 情形将所求映射两端都识别为 $\Omega^{\mathrm{an}}_{C_i/A}$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidcovercech} 与 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness}，有限 affinoid 覆盖在有限模上忠实平坦。对核与余核应用这一事实，便知原映射为同构。}

\bi{For sheaf gluing, choose affinoid chart pairs $V\to U$ of $f$. The finite modules just constructed restrict compatibly under source-chart refinement. If $U'\subseteq U$ is an affinoid domain and $V\to U$ factors through $U'$, then $V=V\times_UU'$ by the domain property; Proposition~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialbasechange} gives the same differential module with base $U'$. Thus target-chart refinement is compatible as well. Common affinoid refinements and uniqueness of the universal derivation give the cocycle identities. Coherent-module gluing from Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf} and the affinoid Kiehl equivalence produces the coherent sheaf. The base-change formula holds on every affinoid chart pair by Proposition~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialbasechange}; these canonical isomorphisms glue.}
{对层粘合，选择 $f$ 的 affinoid chart 对 $V\to U$。刚构造的有限模与源 chart 的加细相容。若 $U'\subseteq U$ 为 affinoid 域，且 $V\to U$ 经 $U'$ 分解，则域的泛性质给出 $V=V\times_UU'$；Proposition~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialbasechange} 说明换成基底 $U'$ 后得到相同微分模。因此它也与目标 chart 加细相容。公共 affinoid 加细及泛导子的唯一性给出 cocycle 恒等式。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf} 的模层粘合及 affinoid Kiehl 等价由此产生凝聚层。Proposition~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialbasechange} 在每个 affinoid chart 对上给出基变换公式，而这些典范同构可以粘合。}
\end{proof}
