Separation of a compact generic interior support

Chapter 8 supplement: Interior Stokes with coherent coefficients

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1785

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[Separation of a compact generic interior support]
\label{f7v38:separator}
Let \(A\) be integral, and let \(S\) be a nonempty compact subset of
\(\Int(V/K)\), \(V=\mathcal M(A)\), such that \(\ker(x)=0\) for
every \(x\in S\). Given any global unit tuple \(f\) on \(V\),
there are a single affinoid domain \(W\subseteq V\) containing an
ordinary neighborhood of \(S\) and a finite tuple of units \(g\)
on \(W\), obtained by restricting nonzero elements of \(A\), such that
for \(F=(f|_W,g)\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:separate}
          t_F(S)\cap t_F(\partial_KW)=\varnothing .
\end{equation}
More precisely, every point of \(W\) with the same \(F\)-moment
as a point of \(S\) is interior in \(W/K\).
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
By the affinoid definition \src{fnd:relativeinterior}, for each
\(x\in S\) there is an admissible presentation of the \emph{whole}
algebra \(A\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:presentation}
 K\{r_{x,1}^{-1}T_1,\ldots,r_{x,m_x}^{-1}T_{m_x}\}
      \twoheadrightarrow A,\qquad T_j\longmapsto a_{x,j},
 \qquad |a_{x,j}(x)|<r_{x,j}.
\end{equation}
Delete any coordinate whose image is \(0\) in \(A\).
Setting these coordinates to zero is a contractive projection of
the Tate algebra and does not change its map to \(A\).
Every lift can be so projected without increasing its norm;
hence the induced quotient norm and admissibility of the remaining
presentation are preserved. Thus every listed \(a_{x,j}\) may be
taken nonzero.

The strict loci
\[
 G_x=\{y\in V:\ |a_{x,j}(y)|<r_{x,j}\text{ for every }j\}
\]
are open, contained in \(\Int(V/K)\), and cover \(S\).
Choose a finite subcover \(G_{x_1},\ldots,G_{x_q}\). Let
\(g_1,\ldots,g_N\) list all nonzero generators in these finitely
many presentations; repetitions may be retained. Every \(g_l\)
is nonzero at \emph{every} point of \(S\), since these points have
kernel \(0\). Compactness and continuity give
\[
 0<\delta_l<\min_{x\in S}|g_l(x)|.
\]
The actual Laurent domain
\begin{equation}\label{eq:W}
 W=\bigcap_{l=1}^N\{y\in V:\ |g_l(y)|\geq\delta_l\}
\end{equation}
is affinoid by the generalized rational point, universal-property,
and joint-presentation results
\src{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints},
\src{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal}, and
\src{fnd:generalizedjointpresentations}.
Its algebra is the actual joint quotient adjoining inverses
\(S_l=g_l^{-1}\) with bounds \(|S_l|\leq\delta_l^{-1}\).
Thus the restrictions of the \(g_l\) are units on \(W\).
The strict lower-bound locus contains \(S\), so \(W\) contains
an ordinary neighborhood of \(S\).

Let \(x\in S\), \(y\in W\), and \(t_F(y)=t_F(x)\).
Then \(|g_l(y)|=|g_l(x)|>\delta_l\) for every \(l\).
Choose \(i\) with \(x\in G_{x_i}\).
Equality of the generator values puts \(y\) in the same strict
presentation locus \(G_{x_i}\), and hence \(y\in\Int(V/K)\).
The strict lower bounds also put \(y\) in the ordinary interior
of \(W\) in \(V\). By \src{fnd:affinoiddomainordinaryinterior},
\(y\in\Int(W/V)\). The composition inclusion now gives
\(y\in\Int(W/K)\). This proves the stronger assertion and
\eqref{eq:separate}.
\end{proof}
