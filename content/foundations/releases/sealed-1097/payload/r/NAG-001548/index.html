The free-polyradius field

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 95

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The free-polyradius field}{自由多半径域}]\label{fnd:freepolyradiusfield}
\bi{For every finite free tuple $r$, the space $K_r$ is a complete valued extension field of $K$. Its multiplication extends Laurent-polynomial multiplication, its absolute value is the displayed weighted supremum norm, and $|T^\nu|=r^\nu$. Every nonzero $a=\sum_\nu a_\nu T^\nu$ has a unique dominant term, and if this term is $a_\mu T^\mu$, then}
{对每个有限自由元组 $r$，空间 $K_r$ 是 $K$ 的完备赋值扩域。其乘法延拓 Laurent 多项式乘法，其绝对值是上述加权上确界范数，且 $|T^\nu|=r^\nu$。每个非零 $a=\sum_\nu a_\nu T^\nu$ 具有唯一的主导项；若该项为 $a_\mu T^\mu$，则}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 a&=a_\mu T^\mu(1+h),\qquad \|h\|<1,\\
 a^{-1}&=a_\mu^{-1}T^{-\mu}\sum_{n\geq0}(-h)^n,
 \qquad \|a^{-1}\|=\|a\|^{-1}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{No spherical completeness, nontrivial valuation, or restriction on the characteristic is required.}
{无须球完备性、非平凡赋值或特征限制。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{We first check the underlying normed space. The vanishing condition implies boundedness: there are only finitely many terms of weight at least one, and all other terms have weight less than one. Finite-support families are dense, by retaining only the finitely many terms above a prescribed error threshold. If $(a^{(j)})$ is Cauchy for the weighted norm, each coefficient is Cauchy in $K$ and has a limit $a_\nu$. Given $\epsilon>0$, choose $j$ so that $\|a^{(j)}-a^{(k)}\|<\epsilon/2$ for every sufficiently large $k$. Passing to each coefficient limit gives $|a_\nu-a^{(j)}_\nu|r^\nu\leq\epsilon/2$. Outside a finite set the terms of $a^{(j)}$ have weight less than $\epsilon/2$. Thus the limiting family has weight at most $\epsilon/2<\epsilon$ outside that set. It belongs to $K_r$, and the same estimate proves convergence in norm. This proves completeness. The identical argument proves completeness of $c_0(\mathbf Z^s,r;E)$ whenever $E$ is complete.}
{先检查底层赋范空间。趋零条件蕴含有界性：权重至少为一的项只有有限个，其余项的权重都小于一。通过保留超过给定误差阈值的有限多个项可见，有限支撑族稠密。若 $(a^{(j)})$ 关于加权范数为 Cauchy 列，则每个系数在 $K$ 中为 Cauchy 列，因而有极限 $a_\nu$。给定 $\epsilon>0$，选择 $j$，使对所有充分大的 $k$ 都有 $\|a^{(j)}-a^{(k)}\|<\epsilon/2$。逐系数取极限得 $|a_\nu-a^{(j)}_\nu|r^\nu\leq\epsilon/2$。在某个有限集之外，$a^{(j)}$ 的各项权重小于 $\epsilon/2$，所以极限族在该集之外的权重至多为 $\epsilon/2<\epsilon$。它属于 $K_r$，同一估计也证明范数收敛。这证明完备性。只要 $E$ 完备，同一论证也证明 $c_0(\mathbf Z^s,r;E)$ 完备。}

\bi{For Laurent polynomials, the ultrametric inequality and $r^{\nu+\mu}=r^\nu r^\mu$ give $\|fg\|\leq\|f\|\|g\|$. Approximate each of two elements by Laurent polynomials. This inequality makes the polynomial products a Cauchy sequence and makes its limit independent of the approximations. It defines a bounded bilinear multiplication on $K_r$. Associativity, commutativity, distributivity and the unit law extend from the dense Laurent-polynomial subalgebra by continuity. In particular, this constructs the product without assuming an infinite convolution or its rearrangement in advance.}
{对 Laurent 多项式，超度量不等式及 $r^{\nu+\mu}=r^\nu r^\mu$ 给出 $\|fg\|\leq\|f\|\|g\|$。用 Laurent 多项式分别逼近两个元素。此不等式使多项式乘积成为 Cauchy 列，并使其极限与逼近选择无关。由此在 $K_r$ 上定义有界双线性乘法。结合律、交换律、分配律及单位律由稠密的 Laurent 多项式子代数通过连续性延拓。因此，我们没有预先假设无限卷积及其重排的合法性。}

\bi{Let $a\ne0$. Its positive supremum is attained: choose a nonzero term of weight $d$ and maximize over the finite nonempty set of terms of weight at least $d$. Two distinct nonzero terms cannot have the same weight. Such an equality would put $r^{\nu-\mu}=|a_\mu/a_\nu|$ in $|K^\times|$, contrary to freeness. Let $a_\mu T^\mu$ be the unique maximizing term. After deleting it, the remaining terms have supremum strictly less than its weight. Indeed, terms of weight at least half the maximal weight form a finite set, and the others are uniformly smaller. Multiplication by the invertible monomial $a_\mu^{-1}T^{-\mu}$ shifts the coefficients and rescales the norm exactly, so it gives $a=a_\mu T^\mu(1+h)$ with $\|h\|<1$.}
{设 $a\ne0$。其正的上确界可以取到：选择一个权重为 $d$ 的非零项，再在权重至少为 $d$ 的有限非空项集上取最大值。两个不同的非零项不能具有相同权重，否则 $r^{\nu-\mu}=|a_\mu/a_\nu|$ 属于 $|K^\times|$，与自由性矛盾。设 $a_\mu T^\mu$ 为唯一最大项。删去它后，其余项的上确界严格小于其权重：权重至少为最大权重一半的项构成有限集，其余项则有统一的更小界。乘以可逆单项式 $a_\mu^{-1}T^{-\mu}$ 恰好平移系数并按确定因子缩放范数，于是得到 $a=a_\mu T^\mu(1+h)$ 且 $\|h\|<1$。}

\bi{Submultiplicativity implies $\|h^n\|\leq\|h\|^n$, so the geometric series converges. Its finite-sum identity proves that it is the inverse of $1+h$. This inverse equals $1+u$ with $\|u\|<1$, hence has norm one. The asserted formula for $a^{-1}$ and its norm follow. Thus every nonzero element is invertible. Finally, for $a,b\ne0$, submultiplicativity applied to $a^{-1}(ab)=b$ gives}
{次乘性给出 $\|h^n\|\leq\|h\|^n$，故几何级数收敛。有限和恒等式证明其为 $1+h$ 的逆。此逆形如 $1+u$ 且 $\|u\|<1$，所以范数为一。由此得到 $a^{-1}$ 的公式及范数，故每个非零元素都可逆。最后，对 $a,b\ne0$，将次乘性用于 $a^{-1}(ab)=b$ 得}
\[
 \|a\|\|b\|\leq\|ab\|\leq\|a\|\|b\|.
\]
\bi{The norm is therefore multiplicative. Constant families embed $K$ isometrically, as claimed.}
{因此范数具有乘法性。常系数族等距嵌入 $K$，得到全部断言。}
\end{proof}
