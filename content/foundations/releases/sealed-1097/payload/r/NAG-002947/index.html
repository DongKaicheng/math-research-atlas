Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1971

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Generalized affinoid principal zero purity; F8ZP-G]
\label{f8:zero-purity-general}
Let $A$ be a nonzero generalized $K$-affinoid algebra pure of analytic
dimension $n\geq1$. If $f$ avoids every minimal prime of $A$, then
$\M(A/fA)$ is empty or pure of analytic dimension $n-1$.
In particular this holds for every non-zero-divisor $f\in A$.
For $n=0$, every element avoiding the minimal primes is a unit.
Moreover, in the original algebra $A$, for every
$\mathfrak q\in\Min_A(fA)$ and $\mathfrak p\in\Min(A)$ contained in
$\mathfrak q$, the ordinary height of $\mathfrak q/\mathfrak p$ in
$A/\mathfrak p$ is one.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Put $B=A/fA$. This is an actual affinoid quotient by
\tagg{NAG-001561}--\tagg{001562}. If $B=0$, there is nothing to prove.
Choose one finite $K$-free polyradius $r$, and put $L=K_r$, such that
$A_L$ and $B_L$ are strict; \tagg{NAG-001557} permits this simultaneous
choice. If $K$ is trivially valued, include any additional radius
other than $1$ in the strictification request, so that $L$ is
nontrivially valued. The quotient comparison and faithful flatness
in \tagg{NAG-001566} give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:f8:zero-purity-quotient}
       B_L=(A/fA)_L\simeq A_L/fA_L\ne0.
\end{equation}

By \tagg{NAG-002291}, the minimal primes of $A_L$ are exactly
$\mathfrak pA_L$ for $\mathfrak p\in\Min(A)$, with contraction
$\mathfrak p$ and with the same componentwise analytic dimension.
Thus $A_L$ is pure of dimension $n$, and $f$ avoids each of its minimal
primes. Analytic and Krull dimensions agree for these strict algebras
by \tagg{NAG-001664}. Proposition~\ref{prop:strictprincipal} makes
$B_L$ pure of dimension $n-1$ (and rules out $B\ne0$ when $n=0$).

Apply \tagg{NAG-002291} again, now to the full quotient $B$.
For every $\mathfrak b\in\Min(B)$, the prime $\mathfrak bB_L$ is
minimal and
\[
 \dim_K\M(B/\mathfrak b)
   =\dim_L\M\bigl(B_L/\mathfrak bB_L\bigr)=n-1.
\]
This is the required descent of componentwise analytic purity.
Nonzero affinoids have nonempty spectra by \tagg{NAG-001572}, so the
empty-space and zero-algebra alternatives agree. The regular case
follows from Lemma~\ref{lem:avoid}; its regularity also persists
upstairs by the flatness in \tagg{NAG-001566}. The ordinary height
assertion in the original $A$ follows by the minimal-prime and \href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00KV}{Tag 00KV}
argument of Proposition~\ref{prop:strictprincipal}: it uses only that
$A/\mathfrak p$ is a Noetherian domain and that $f$ is nonzero there.
It is the strictification argument, not this ordinary height statement
alone, that supplies the asserted analytic dimension.

Only this finite free-radius extension, with its exact minimal-prime
bijection, was used. No assertion of purity ascent by arbitrary flat
extension, of freeness over completed residue fields, or of equality
between generalized analytic dimension and ordinary Krull dimension
has entered the argument.
\end{proof}
