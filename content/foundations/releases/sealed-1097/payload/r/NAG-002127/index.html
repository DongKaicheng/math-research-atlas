Punctured curves over the original discrete field

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 719

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Punctured curves over the original discrete field}
{原离散赋值域上的穿孔曲线}]
\label{cand:puncturedcurvedvrretraction}
\bi{Let $K,C,D$ be as in
Theorem~\ref{cand:allgenusmarkeddvrmodel}.
After its finite Galois extension $L/K$,
$(C_L\setminus D_L)^{\an}$ has a strong deformation
retraction onto a finite metric graph with one
infinite ray for each point of $D_L$.
If $g(C)>0$, the homotopy descends to
$(C\setminus D)^{\an}$ over the original field and
the original absolute value. Its image is the
finite metric quotient graph, with one infinite
ray for each closed point of $D$.}
{设 $K,C,D$ 如
Theorem~\ref{cand:allgenusmarkeddvrmodel}。
在该定理的有限 Galois 扩张 $L/K$ 后，
$(C_L\setminus D_L)^{\an}$ 强形变收缩到一个
有限度量图，对 $D_L$ 的每个点有一条无限射线。
若 $g(C)>0$，此同伦下降到原基域及原绝对值上的
$(C\setminus D)^{\an}$；其像为有限度量商图，
对 $D$ 的每个闭点有一条无限射线。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Apply Theorem~\ref{cand:generalmarkedmodelskeleton} to the
split marked projective model just constructed.
For $g(C)>0$ that model has the compatible Galois
action; the full marked homotopy is equivariant.
Corollary~\ref{cand:galoisretractiondescent}, applied
to the analytic open space $(C\setminus D)^{\an}$,
descends it. This descent is valid without compactness:
Theorem~\ref{cand:galoisanalyticquotient} gives the
open quotient property after product with $[0,1]$.}
{对刚构造的分裂标记射影模型应用
Theorem~\ref{cand:generalmarkedmodelskeleton}。
若 $g(C)>0$，模型具有相容的 Galois 作用，
整个标记同伦等变。对解析开空间
$(C\setminus D)^{\an}$ 应用
Corollary~\ref{cand:galoisretractiondescent}，
即得下降。此下降无需紧性：
Theorem~\ref{cand:galoisanalyticquotient}
给出与 $[0,1]$ 作乘积后的开商映射性质。}

\bi{The quotient of the compact core is a finite metric
graph by Corollary~\ref{cand:galoissplitmodelskeleton}.
A group element carrying one marked disc to another
carries its center to the other center. Hence it
preserves the radius on the entire ray, by
Lemma~\ref{cand:punctureddischomotopy}.
Thus stabilizers act trivially on each ray, and
quotient rays are indexed by Galois orbits of $D_L$,
equivalently by the closed points of $D$.
All parameters are computed with the extending
absolute value; no degree factor is introduced.}
{由 Corollary~\ref{cand:galoissplitmodelskeleton}，
紧核的商为有限度量图。将一个标记圆盘映到另一个
的群元素，也将其中心映到另一个中心，因此由
Lemma~\ref{cand:punctureddischomotopy}，
它在整条射线上保持半径。于是稳定子在每条射线上
平凡作用，商射线由 $D_L$ 的 Galois 轨道编号，
也就是由 $D$ 的闭点编号。全部参数都使用原绝对值
的延拓计算，不引入次数因子。}
\end{proof}
