Pure dimension of algebraic tropicalization

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 884

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Pure dimension of algebraic tropicalization}{代数热带化的纯维性}]
\label{cand:tropicalpuredimension}
\bi{If $X\subset\operatorname{Spec}K[M]$ is nonempty and pure of dimension $d$, then $\operatorname{Trop}(X)$ is a nonempty finite pure $d$-dimensional integral $v(K^\times)$-affine polyhedral complex. At the relative interior of each maximal cell $\sigma$, the quotient $Z_\sigma$ of Proposition~\ref{cand:initialtangenttorus} is a nonempty finite scheme over the residue field.}
{若 $X\subset\operatorname{Spec}K[M]$ 非空且纯 $d$ 维，则 $\operatorname{Trop}(X)$ 是非空有限纯 $d$ 维整 $v(K^\times)$-仿射多面复形。在每个极大胞腔 $\sigma$ 的相对内部，Proposition~\ref{cand:initialtangenttorus} 中的商 $Z_\sigma$ 是剩余域上的非空有限概形。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The Gauss-field construction in Lemma~\ref{cand:zerotropicaldimension}, with arbitrary radii and allowing an empty transcendence basis, gives an analytic point on each nonempty integral component. Thus the tropicalization is nonempty. The finite complex is Theorem~\ref{cand:finitegroebnercomplex}. Fix a maximal cell $\sigma$, a relative-interior point $w$, and put $s=\dim\sigma$. The local star at $w$ is the vector space $L_\sigma$. By Theorem~\ref{cand:initiallocalstar}, $Y=\operatorname{in}_w(X)$ has tropical support $L_\sigma$. By Proposition~\ref{cand:initialtangenttorus}, $Y=T_{N_\sigma}\times Z_\sigma$. Every analytic point of $Z_\sigma$ gives a point of this product by assigning value one to the first torus coordinates. It follows that $\operatorname{Trop}(Z_\sigma)\subseteq\{0\}$. The quotient is nonempty, since $Y$ is nonempty. Lemma~\ref{cand:zerotropicaldimension} makes $Z_\sigma$ finite.}
{在 Lemma~\ref{cand:zerotropicaldimension} 的 Gauss 域构造中取任意半径，并允许超越基为空，即在每个非空整分支上得到解析点，故热带化非空。有限复形由 Theorem~\ref{cand:finitegroebnercomplex} 给出。固定极大胞腔 $\sigma$、其相对内部中的点 $w$，置 $s=\dim\sigma$。$w$ 处的局部星形为向量空间 $L_\sigma$。由 Theorem~\ref{cand:initiallocalstar}，$Y=\operatorname{in}_w(X)$ 的热带支集为 $L_\sigma$；由 Proposition~\ref{cand:initialtangenttorus}，有 $Y=T_{N_\sigma}\times Z_\sigma$。为第一个环面因子的坐标赋值一，便将 $Z_\sigma$ 的每个解析点变为直积上的点，因此 $\operatorname{Trop}(Z_\sigma)\subseteq\{0\}$。由于 $Y$ 非空，商也非空。由 Lemma~\ref{cand:zerotropicaldimension}，$Z_\sigma$ 有限。}

\bi{Every component of $T_{N_\sigma}\times Z_\sigma$ has dimension $s$: a prime quotient of its finite coefficient algebra is a finite field extension, and its Laurent polynomial ring has transcendence degree $s$, so \cite[Tag 00P0]{StacksAlg} applies. Proposition~\ref{cand:initialpuredimension} gives dimension $d$ for the same components. Thus $s=d$. This holds for every maximal cell.}
{直积 $T_{N_\sigma}\times Z_\sigma$ 的每个分支维数均为 $s$：有限系数代数的素商为有限扩域，其 Laurent 多项式环的超越次数为 $s$，故可应用 \cite[Tag 00P0]{StacksAlg}。而 Proposition~\ref{cand:initialpuredimension} 说明同一分支的维数为 $d$，因此 $s=d$。对每个极大胞腔均如此。}
\end{proof}

{初始概形采用 Theorem~\ref{cand:realinitialdegeneration} 的定义。称概形纯 $d$ 维，是指其底空间的每个不可约分支均为 $d$ 维；允许嵌入分支。}

\begin{lemma}[\bi{Finite filtrations and zero-dimensional algebras}{有限滤过与零维代数}]
\label{cand:finiteprimefiltration}
\bi{Every finite module over a Noetherian ring has a finite filtration whose nonzero successive quotients are $A/\mathfrak p$ for prime ideals $\mathfrak p$. A zero-dimensional algebra of finite type over a field $k$ is finite-dimensional over $k$.}
{Noetherian 环上的每个有限模具有有限滤过，其非零相继商均形如 $A/\mathfrak p$，其中 $\mathfrak p$ 为素理想。域 $k$ 上的有限型零维代数在 $k$ 上有限维。}
\end{lemma}
