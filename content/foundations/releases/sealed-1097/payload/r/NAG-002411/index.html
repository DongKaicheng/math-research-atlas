Affinoid intersections in a separated good space

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1034

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[Affinoid intersections in a separated good space]
\label{v037:integration:separatedintersections}
Let $X$ be a good Hausdorff $K$-analytic space. Any finite
intersection of affinoid domains of $X$ is an affinoid domain,
with the empty space allowed. Two finite affinoid-domain covers
therefore have a common finite affinoid refinement. If $X$ is
compact, it has a finite affinoid cover whose ordinary interiors
cover $X$.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{fnd:goodhausdorffseparated}, applied to
$X\to\mathcal M(K)$, makes the analytic diagonal
$\Delta:X\to X\times_KX$ a closed immersion.
Let $U=\mathcal M(A)$ and $V=\mathcal M(B)$ be affinoid
domains of $X$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticproducts}
makes
\[
                  U\times_KV=\mathcal M(A\widehat\otimes_KB)
\]
an actual affinoid domain of $X\times_KX$.
The pullback of $\Delta$ to this domain is an affinoid
closed subspace. Indeed, the coherent defining ideal restricts
to a finite ideal by Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb},
and its quotient is the actual affinoid quotient of
Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients}.
The arbitrary-domain restriction argument in
Lemma~\ref{fnd:closedimmersiontargetglocal} identifies this
quotient with the entire diagonal pullback, including its
analytic structure.

This pullback is $U\times_XV$. By the domain universal
property of Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing}, a map
to it is exactly a map to $X$ whose image lies in both $U$
and $V$. Thus it is the actual analytic domain $U\cap V$.
There is no identification of an analytic product with an
ordinary product of point sets in this argument.
Iteration proves the finite-intersection assertion, using
domain transitivity in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalnetdomaincalculus}.
The pairwise intersections of two finite covers form the
claimed common refinement. Affinoid domains are compact by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact}, and hence closed
in the Hausdorff space $X$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasinetcalculus}
makes every finite cover by such domains admissible.
Finally, goodness supplies an
affinoid neighbourhood of each point. Compactness gives
a finite subcover of their ordinary interiors.
\end{proof}
