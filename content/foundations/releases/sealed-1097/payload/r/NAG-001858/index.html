Closed-fibre comparison in arbitrary characteristic-zero valuation

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 465

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Closed-fibre comparison in arbitrary characteristic-zero valuation}{任意特征零赋值下的闭纤维比较}]
\label{fnd:charzeroallresidueclosedfibercomparison}
\bi{For a morphism $f:Y\to X$ of good analytic spaces over any complete characteristic-zero field and an inner point $y$ with image $x$, put $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ and $S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$. If $\mathfrak m$ is the maximal ideal of $R$, then}
{对任意特征零完备域上 good 解析空间之间的态射 $f:Y\to X$，以及像为 $x$ 的内点 $y$，令 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$。若 $\mathfrak m$ 是 $R$ 的极大理想，则}
\[
                    S/\mathfrak mS\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{Y_x,y}
\]
\bi{is regular and faithfully flat.}
{正则且忠实平坦。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Realize a finite generating family of $\mathfrak m$ on an ordinary base neighborhood and form the associated closed analytic base change. As in Corollary~\ref{fnd:charzeroclosedfibercomparison}, the new ordinary base ring is the field $R/\mathfrak m$, the ordinary source ring is $S/\mathfrak mS$, and the analytic fibre and the inner point are unchanged. Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroallresiduegenericcomparison}. Its regular map is local and flat, hence faithfully flat.}
{将 $\mathfrak m$ 的有限生成组实现在底的普通邻域上，并作其定义的闭解析基变换。与 Corollary~\ref{fnd:charzeroclosedfibercomparison} 相同，新的普通底环为域 $R/\mathfrak m$，普通源环为 $S/\mathfrak mS$，而解析纤维及内点不变。应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroallresiduegenericcomparison}；所得正则映射局部且平坦，故忠实平坦。}
\end{proof}
