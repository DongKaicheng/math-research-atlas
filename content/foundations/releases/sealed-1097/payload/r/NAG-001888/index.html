Relative polydisc flatness on ordinary stalks

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 428

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Relative polydisc flatness on ordinary stalks}
{相对多圆盘的茎平坦性}]
\label{fnd:relativepolydiscstalkflat}
\bi{Let $P=\mathcal M(A\{r^{-1}T\})\to X=\mathcal M(A)$ be the
relative closed polydisc, and let $y\mapsto x$. Then the local map
$\mathcal O_{X,x}\to\mathcal O_{P,y}$ is faithfully flat.}
{设 $P=\mathcal M(A\{r^{-1}T\})\to X=\mathcal M(A)$ 为相对闭多圆盘，
且 $y\mapsto x$。则局部同态
$\mathcal O_{X,x}\to\mathcal O_{P,y}$ 忠实平坦。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Write $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ and $S=\mathcal O_{P,y}$.
Fix a rational domain $V$ in $X$ containing an ordinary neighborhood
of $x$. By the bounded tensor universal property, the inverse image
$P_V$ has algebra $A_V\{r^{-1}T\}$. It contains an ordinary
neighborhood of $y$. The affinoid neighborhoods $W$ of $y$ in $P$
contained in $P_V$ therefore form a cofinal system. Each is an
affinoid domain of $P_V$, by the domain restriction calculus.
The preceding proposition and Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness}
give flat maps}
{记 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$S=\mathcal O_{P,y}$。
固定 $X$ 中包含 $x$ 的通常邻域的 rational 域 $V$。
由有界张量的泛性质，逆像 $P_V$ 的代数为
$A_V\{r^{-1}T\}$，并且 $P_V$ 包含 $y$ 的通常邻域。
所以 $P$ 中包含于 $P_V$ 的、$y$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $W$ 构成余终系统。
由域的限制运算，每个 $W$ 均为 $P_V$ 的 affinoid 域。
前一命题及 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness} 给出平坦映射}
\[
 A_V\longrightarrow A_V\{r^{-1}T\}\longrightarrow A_W.
\]
\bi{Their composite is flat because the two consecutive tensor
functors preserve injections. Ordinary stalks are the filtered
colimits of these $A_W$ by Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks}.
For a finitely generated ideal $J\subset A_V$, any element of
$J\otimes_{A_V}S$ is a finite sum represented at one stage.
If its image in $S$ is zero, that image vanishes after shrinking
$W$ once more. Flatness of the new stage then makes the tensor
itself zero. The finite-ideal criterion proves that $S$ is flat
over each fixed $A_V$.}
{两个相继的张量函子均保持单射，因此复合映射平坦。由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks}，通常茎是这些 $A_W$
的滤过直极限。给定有限生成理想 $J\subset A_V$，
$J\otimes_{A_V}S$ 的任一元素可由一个阶段上的有限和表示。
若其在 $S$ 中的像为零，再缩小一次 $W$ 后其像就为零。
新阶段的平坦性使该张量本身为零。由有限理想判别准则，
$S$ 在每个固定的 $A_V$ 上平坦。}

\bi{Finally, take a finitely generated ideal $I\subset R$ and
$z\in I\otimes_R S$ whose image in $S$ is zero. Choose generators
$a_1,\ldots,a_m$ of $I$ and write $z=\sum_i a_i\otimes s_i$ with
$s_i\in S$. Lift the $a_i$ to elements $\widetilde a_i$ of one
$A_V$, and put $J=(\widetilde a_1,\ldots,\widetilde a_m)$.
Then $z_V=\sum_i\widetilde a_i\otimes s_i\in J\otimes_{A_V}S$
maps to $z$ and has zero image in $S$. The flatness just proved
gives $z_V=0$, hence $z=0$.
Again \cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg} gives $R$-flatness. Evaluations
at $x$ and $y$ are compatible through the isometric map
$\mathcal H(x)\hookrightarrow\mathcal H(y)$, so the map is local.
A nonzero module contains a cyclic submodule $R/I$ with $I$ proper;
its tensor $S/IS$ is nonzero because $IS$ lies in the maximal ideal
of $S$. Flatness preserves the cyclic injection and therefore
proves faithful flatness.}
{最后，取有限生成理想 $I\subset R$ 以及在 $S$ 中的像为零的
$z\in I\otimes_R S$。选择 $I$ 的生成元 $a_1,\ldots,a_m$，并写成
$z=\sum_i a_i\otimes s_i$，其中 $s_i\in S$。将 $a_i$ 提升为同一个
$A_V$ 中的元素 $\widetilde a_i$，并令
$J=(\widetilde a_1,\ldots,\widetilde a_m)$。于是
$z_V=\sum_i\widetilde a_i\otimes s_i\in J\otimes_{A_V}S$
映到 $z$，且其在 $S$ 中的像为零。刚才的平坦性给出 $z_V=0$，
从而 $z=0$。再次应用
\cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg}，得到 $R$-平坦性。在 $x,y$ 处的取值
通过等距映射 $\mathcal H(x)\hookrightarrow\mathcal H(y)$ 相容，
因此该同态为局部同态。每个非零模包含一个循环子模 $R/I$，
其中 $I$ 为真理想；其张量积 $S/IS$ 非零，因为 $IS$ 位于 $S$
的极大理想中。平坦性保持此循环子模的单射，因而给出忠实平坦性。}
\end{proof}
