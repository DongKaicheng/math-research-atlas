Principal slopes at arbitrary type II points

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 709

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Principal slopes at arbitrary type II points}
{任意第二类点处的主函数斜率}]
\label{cand:alltypeiiprincipalslopes}
\bi{Let $K$ be algebraically closed, complete and
nontrivially valued. Let $Y$ be a smooth integral projective
$K$-curve possessing a projective nodal $O$-model, and
put $X=Y^{\mathrm{an}}$. Every type II point $x\in X$
has the intrinsic residual curve, metric directions and
local slope formula of
Theorem~\ref{cand:intrinsicresidualcurve}.
For every $f\in K(Y)^\times$, there is a finite marked
skeleton of $X\setminus|\operatorname{div}(f)|$ such that
$-\log|f|$ factors through its retraction, is integral
affine on every open edge and ray, and has outgoing sum
zero at every finite vertex. On a ray ending at
$a\in|\operatorname{div}(f)|$ its slope towards $a$ is
$\operatorname{ord}_a(f)$. Any prescribed finite set of
type II points can be included among the vertices.}
{设 $K$ 代数闭、完备且非平凡赋值，$Y$ 为具有射影
节点 $O$-模型的光滑整射影 $K$-曲线，并令
$X=Y^{\mathrm{an}}$。每个第二类点 $x\in X$ 都具有
Theorem~\ref{cand:intrinsicresidualcurve} 中的内禀
剩余曲线、度量方向与局部斜率公式。
对每个 $f\in K(Y)^\times$，存在
$X\setminus|\operatorname{div}(f)|$ 的有限标记骨架，
使 $-\log|f|$ 经其收缩分解，在每条开边和射线上
整斜率仿射，且在每个有限顶点处出射斜率之和为零。
在通向 $a\in|\operatorname{div}(f)|$ 的射线上，朝向
$a$ 的斜率为 $\operatorname{ord}_a(f)$。
任意预先指定的有限个第二类点均可纳入顶点集。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The generic-fibre comparison of
Proposition~\ref{cand:projectiveformalcompletion} identifies
the formal generic fibre with $X$.
Theorem~\ref{cand:finitetypeiirealization} realizes any
chosen type II point, or any finite set of them, as component
vertices of a projective nodal model.
Theorem~\ref{cand:intrinsicresidualcurve} applies at each
such vertex and proves that the resulting objects do not
depend on that choice.}
{Proposition~\ref{cand:projectiveformalcompletion} 的
泛纤维比较将形式泛纤维识别为 $X$。
Theorem~\ref{cand:finitetypeiirealization} 将任意所选
第二类点或其有限集实现为射影节点模型的分量顶点。
Theorem~\ref{cand:intrinsicresidualcurve} 在每个这样的
顶点适用，并证明所得对象与该选择无关。}

\bi{The support $P=|\operatorname{div}(f)|$ is finite and
consists of $K$-rational points. Apply
Theorem~\ref{cand:adaptedmarkedmodel} while retaining
the prescribed vertices. The marked skeleton and every
stated norm, affine and slope identity then follow from
Theorem~\ref{cand:markedmodelskeleton} and
Theorem~\ref{cand:markedmodelslopeformula}.}
{支撑 $P=|\operatorname{div}(f)|$ 有限，且由
$K$-有理点组成。保留指定顶点，并应用
Theorem~\ref{cand:adaptedmarkedmodel}。
随后，Theorem~\ref{cand:markedmodelskeleton} 和
Theorem~\ref{cand:markedmodelslopeformula} 给出标记骨架
以及所有所述范数、仿射性和斜率恒等式。}
\end{proof}
