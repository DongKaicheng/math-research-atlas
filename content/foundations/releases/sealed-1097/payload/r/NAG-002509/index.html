NAG-002509 · Accepted whole

Finite generic pushforward coefficients

C Native scalar extension and finite restriction of constants

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1760

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Finite generic pushforward coefficients]
\label{f9cand:finite-cycle}
Suppose $n=[L:K]<\infty$ and $B=A_L$. For
$\mathfrak p\in\Min(A)$ one has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:finite-length-sum}
 \sum_{\substack{\mathfrak q\in\Min(B)\\\mathfrak q\cap A=\mathfrak p}}
   \len_{B_{\mathfrak q}}(M_L)_{\mathfrak q}
             [\kappa(\mathfrak q):\kappa(\mathfrak p)]
       =n\,\len_{A_{\mathfrak p}}M_{\mathfrak p}.
\end{equation}
Thus the algebraic generic cycle pushforward of the pullback coefficient
is $n[M]$, with the indicated full residue-field degrees.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Finite-dimensional norm equivalence [\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}] gives bounded
coordinate maps for a $K$-basis $e_1,\ldots,e_n$ of $L$. Tensoring these
maps with the identity of any Banach $K$-space identifies its completed
tensor with $L$ with its $n$-fold direct sum. In particular
\[
                A_L=A\otimes_K L=\bigoplus_{i=1}^n Ae_i .
\]
The finite-dimensional $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$-algebra
$C=B\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ has dimension $n$.
Its local factors are $B_{\mathfrak q}/\mathfrak pB_{\mathfrak q}$
for the primes in the displayed sum. Indeed they lie over the minimal
prime $\mathfrak p$ in a finite flat map; a prime over it is minimal,
since $A_{\mathfrak p}$ is Artinian and its finite algebra $B_{\mathfrak p}$
has finite length as an $A_{\mathfrak p}$-module, so is Artinian as a ring. A composition series of each factor gives
\[
 n=\sum_{\mathfrak q\mid\mathfrak p}
     \len_{B_{\mathfrak q}}
       (B_{\mathfrak q}/\mathfrak pB_{\mathfrak q})
       [\kappa(\mathfrak q):\kappa(\mathfrak p)] .
\]
Multiply by $\len_{A_{\mathfrak p}}M_{\mathfrak p}$ and apply
Lemma~\ref{f9cand:generic-length}.
\end{proof}
