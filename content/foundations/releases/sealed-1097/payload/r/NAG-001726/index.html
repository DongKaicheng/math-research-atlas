The flat analytification morphism of ringed sites

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 298

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{The flat analytification morphism of ringed sites}
{解析化给出的平坦赋环 site 态射}]
\label{fnd:analytificationgsiteflat}
\bi{For a finite-type $K$-scheme $X$, inverse image of Zariski opens
defines a flat morphism of ringed topoi
$\alpha_X:X^{\an}_G\to X_{\mathrm{Zar}}$.
If $W=\operatorname{Spec}(R)\subset X$ and $V=\mathcal M(A)$ is any
affinoid domain in $W^{\an}$, then $R\to A$ is flat.
On coherent modules $\alpha_X^*$ is the analytification already
constructed, and $\alpha_X^*$ is exact on all modules.}
{对有限型 $K$-概形 $X$，Zariski 开集的逆像定义平坦的赋环拓扑斯态射
$\alpha_X:X^{\an}_G\to X_{\mathrm{Zar}}$。
若 $W=\operatorname{Spec}(R)\subset X$，且
$V=\mathcal M(A)$ 是 $W^{\an}$ 中任意 affinoid 域，则
$R\to A$ 平坦。在凝聚模上，$\alpha_X^*$ 就是已构造的解析化；
它在全部模上都是正合的。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The rule $W\mapsto W^{\an}$ preserves finite intersections, the
final object and coverings, by Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationzariskiopens}.
It therefore defines the stated morphism of sites, with structural
ring map given by evaluation of regular functions. Choose a finite
generating list of $R$ over $K$. Its values on the compact space $V$
are bounded. Consequently $V$ lies in a sufficiently large radius
affinoid $X_{R,r}$. It is an affinoid domain there by the domain
restriction property. The factorization}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationzariskiopens}，
对应 $W\mapsto W^{\an}$ 保持有限交、终对象和覆盖，因而定义所述
site 态射，其结构环映射由正则函数的解析取值给出。
取 $R$ 在 $K$ 上的一组有限生成元。它们在紧空间 $V$ 上的取值有界，
所以 $V$ 包含在足够大的半径 affinoid $X_{R,r}$ 中。
由域的限制性质，$V$ 也是其中的 affinoid 域。因此分解}
\[
 R\longrightarrow A_r\longrightarrow A
\]
\bi{is flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:polynomialtateflatness} and
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness}.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:polynomialtateflatness} 与
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness} 平坦。}

\bi{Here is a sheaf-level verification of flatness, including modules
which are not coherent. A finite relation with coefficients in
$\alpha_X^{-1}\mathcal O_X$ and values in $\mathcal O_{X^{\an}_G}$
can, locally for the analytic site, be represented by coefficients
$r_1,\ldots,r_t$ in one affine ring $R$ and values $a_1,\ldots,a_t$
in one affinoid ring $A$, with $\sum r_i a_i=0$. To obtain this
representation, first use the local definition of an inverse-image
sheaf for the finitely many coefficients, and then refine to affinoids.
Since $R$ is Noetherian, the relation module
$N=\ker(R^t\to R)$ is finite. Flatness of $R\to A$ gives
$\ker(A^t\to A)=A\otimes_R N$. Thus the vector $(a_i)$ is a finite
$A$-linear combination of relations already over $R$. This is the
finite-relation criterion for flatness, equivalently the finite-ideal
criterion \cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg}, applied locally to the ring
sheaves. It proves that $\mathcal O_{X^{\an}_G}$ is flat over
$\alpha_X^{-1}\mathcal O_X$. Inverse image of sheaves is exact;
tensoring with this flat ring sheaf is exact as well. On finite
presentations the result is $A\otimes_R M$, agreeing with
Theorem~\ref{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact}.}
{以下在层的层面核验平坦性，也包括非凝聚模。
系数属于 $\alpha_X^{-1}\mathcal O_X$、取值属于
$\mathcal O_{X^{\an}_G}$ 的有限关系，在解析 site 上局部可表示为
同一仿射环 $R$ 中的系数 $r_1,\ldots,r_t$，以及同一 affinoid
环 $A$ 中的元素 $a_1,\ldots,a_t$，满足 $\sum r_i a_i=0$。
具体地，先对有限个系数使用逆像层的局部定义，再加细到 affinoid。
因为 $R$ Noetherian，关系模 $N=\ker(R^t\to R)$ 有限；
$R\to A$ 的平坦性给出 $\ker(A^t\to A)=A\otimes_R N$。
因此向量 $(a_i)$ 是原来 $R$ 上关系的有限 $A$-线性组合。
这就是局部应用于环层的有限关系平坦判据，等价于
\cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg} 中的有限理想判据。
故 $\mathcal O_{X^{\an}_G}$ 在
$\alpha_X^{-1}\mathcal O_X$ 上平坦。层的逆像正合，再与此平坦
环层张量仍正合。对有限表示，结果为 $A\otimes_R M$，
与 Theorem~\ref{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact} 一致。}
\end{proof}
