Comparison with the full analytic G-site

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 380

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Comparison with the full analytic G-site}{与完整解析 G-site 的比较}]
\label{fnd:affinoidfullgcomparison}
\bi{The inclusions of the finite affinoid-domain site and the arbitrary-radius rational site into $X_G$ induce equivalences of topoi. The structure sheaf $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{aff}}$ therefore extends uniquely, up to its unique comparison isomorphism, to a ring sheaf $\mathcal O_{X_G}$. For an analytic domain $V$, its sections are compatible families of sections on affinoid domains contained in $V$. On an affinoid object $V$ they equal $A_V$, with the original restriction homomorphisms.}
{有限 affinoid 域 site 及任意半径 rational site 到 $X_G$ 的包含，都诱导 topoi 等价。因此结构层 $\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{aff}}$ 唯一延拓为环层 $\mathcal O_{X_G}$，其中唯一性按唯一的比较同构理解。对解析域 $V$，其截面是包含于 $V$ 的各 affinoid 域上的相容截面族。对 affinoid 对象 $V$，其截面就是 $A_V$，且限制为原来的同态。}
\end{theorem}
Selected proof part 1 · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A finite affinoid-domain cover is a finite closed cover, so it is admissible by Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasinetcalculus}. Conversely, take an admissible cover $(V_i)$ of an affinoid object $V$. Choose an affinoid quasi-net covering of each $V_i$. Transitivity makes their combined family a quasi-net of $V$, and compactness gives a finite affinoid refinement of the original cover. Every object of $X_G$ is covered by affinoid objects by definition. Intersections of such objects remain affinoid.}
{有限 affinoid 域覆盖是有限闭覆盖，所以由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasinetcalculus} 为 admissible。反之，给定 affinoid 对象 $V$ 的 admissible 覆盖 $(V_i)$，为每个 $V_i$ 选择 affinoid quasi-net 覆盖。由传递性，合并后的族是 $V$ 的 quasi-net；紧性再给出原覆盖的有限 affinoid 加细。按定义，$X_G$ 的每个对象均由 affinoid 对象覆盖，且 affinoid 对象的交仍为 affinoid。}
Selected proof part 2 · exact TeX
\bi{These statements verify the comparison hypotheses of \cite[Tag 03A0, Lemma 7.29.1]{StacksAlg}: full faithfulness of the inclusion, continuity, cocontinuity by the finite refinement just constructed, and covering of every target object. Thus restriction gives an equivalence of topoi. Combining it with Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainsheafcomparison} gives the rational comparison. The inverse equivalence extends the ring sheaf. Concretely, its value on $V$ is the compatible-family limit over all affinoid domains contained in $V$. Indeed, for an affinoid cover, compatibility on all intersections is the sheaf equalizer condition; refining two such covers compares them on their affinoid intersections. On an affinoid $V$ the family has final section datum at $V$ itself, recovering $A_V$. These identifications determine the restriction maps and the unique comparison isomorphism.}
{这些断言核验了 \cite[Tag 03A0, Lemma 7.29.1]{StacksAlg} 的比较假设：包含全忠实且 continuous；刚构造的有限加细给出 cocontinuity；每个目标对象都有来自源 site 的覆盖。因此限制给出 topoi 等价。与 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainsheafcomparison} 复合便得到 rational 比较。逆等价将环层延拓。具体地，其在 $V$ 上的值是所有包含于 $V$ 的 affinoid 域上的相容族之极限：对于 affinoid 覆盖，各交上的相容性就是层的等化子条件，而两个覆盖可通过其 affinoid 交叠比较。若 $V$ 本身为 affinoid，则整个相容族由它在 $V$ 上的数据决定，恢复 $A_V$。这些识别决定限制映射及唯一的比较同构。}
\end{proof}
