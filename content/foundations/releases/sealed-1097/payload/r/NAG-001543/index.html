NAG-001543 · Accepted whole

A strict-test domain gives an étale ring epimorphism

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 246

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{A strict-test domain gives an étale ring epimorphism}{Strict-test 域给出 étale 环上态}]\label{fnd:trivialstrictdomainetale}
\bi{Let $\varphi:A\to B$ present a strict-test affinoid domain $U\subseteq\mathcal M(A)$. As a map of abstract rings, $\varphi$ is an epimorphism and is formally étale: for every commutative $A$-algebra $C$, every ideal $J\subset C$ with $J^2=0$, and every $A$-algebra map $B\to C/J$, there is a unique lift $B\to C$. No topology or finite-type condition is imposed on $C$ in this assertion. Consequently $A\to B$ is an étale ring map of finite presentation, and is algebraically flat.}
{设 $\varphi:A\to B$ 表示 strict-test affinoid 域 $U\subseteq\mathcal M(A)$。作为抽象环映射，$\varphi$ 为上态，并且形式 étale：对任意交换 $A$-代数 $C$、任意满足 $J^2=0$ 的理想 $J\subset C$，以及任意 $A$-代数映射 $B\to C/J$，都存在唯一提升 $B\to C$。这个断言并不给 $C$ 施加拓扑或有限型条件。因此，$A\to B$ 是具有有限表示的 étale 环映射，并且代数平坦。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The strict-test domain property makes $A\to B$ an epimorphism in the strict-affinoid category. Indeed, if $u,v:B\to E$ agree on $A$, their common composite has spectrum image in $U$, and the uniqueness part of the domain property gives $u=v$. By Lemma~\ref{fnd:trivialstrictalgebraicbridge}, the ordinary algebra $D=B\otimes_A B$ is an allowed strict target, and the two maps $b\mapsto b\otimes1$ and $b\mapsto1\otimes b$ are bounded. They therefore agree. Now let $u,v:B\to E$ be any two homomorphisms of abstract commutative rings agreeing on $A$. The balanced map}
{Strict-test 域性质说明 $A\to B$ 在 strict-affinoid 范畴中为上态。事实上，若 $u,v:B\to E$ 在 $A$ 上相同，则共同复合的谱像位于 $U$，域性质中的唯一性给出 $u=v$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:trivialstrictalgebraicbridge}，普通代数 $D=B\otimes_A B$ 是允许的 strict 目标，且两个映射 $b\mapsto b\otimes1$ 与 $b\mapsto1\otimes b$ 均有界，所以它们相同。现在取任意两个在 $A$ 上相同的抽象交换环同态 $u,v:B\to E$。平衡映射}
\[
 D\longrightarrow E,\qquad b\otimes b'\longmapsto u(b)v(b')
\]
\bi{takes their equality to $u(b)=v(b)$. Thus $\varphi$ is an epimorphism against arbitrary ring targets, not just strict targets.}
{将上述相等送为 $u(b)=v(b)$。因此，$\varphi$ 对任意环目标都是上态，而不只对 strict 目标如此。}

\bi{Choose the finite presentation $\pi:P=A[X_1,\ldots,X_n]\twoheadrightarrow B$ supplied by the preceding lemma, put $I=\ker\pi$, and set $Q=P/I^2$. The algebra $Q$ is finite type over $K$, so it is an allowed strict test algebra. Every multiplicative seminorm on $Q$ annihilates $I/I^2$, since each element of this ideal has square zero. It therefore factors through $B=P/I$. Boundedness descends by taking the infimum over quotient representatives, or directly from the discrete defining norms. Conversely, a bounded point of $B$ pulls back to a bounded point of $Q$. Consequently the maps $A\to Q$ and $A\to B$ have the same spectrum image.}
{选择前一引理给出的有限表示 $\pi:P=A[X_1,\ldots,X_n]\twoheadrightarrow B$，令 $I=\ker\pi$，并记 $Q=P/I^2$。代数 $Q$ 在 $K$ 上有限型，所以是允许的 strict 测试代数。$Q$ 上的每个乘性半范数都消去 $I/I^2$，因为此理想中的每个元素的平方为零。因此，它通过 $B=P/I$ 分解。对商代表元取下确界即可使有界性下降，也可直接使用离散定义范数。反之，$B$ 的有界点拉回为 $Q$ 的有界点。因此，映射 $A\to Q$ 与 $A\to B$ 具有相同的谱像。}

\bi{The domain property now supplies an $A$-algebra map $\sigma:B\to Q$. If $q:Q\to B$ is the quotient map, the endomorphisms $q\sigma$ and $\mathrm{id}_B$ agree on $A$. The ring-epimorphism property just proved gives $q\sigma=\mathrm{id}_B$. This single section is obtained using only a finite-type strict test.}
{域性质于是给出 $A$-代数映射 $\sigma:B\to Q$。若 $q:Q\to B$ 为商映射，则自同态 $q\sigma$ 与 $\mathrm{id}_B$ 在 $A$ 上相同。由刚证明的环上态性质，$q\sigma=\mathrm{id}_B$。这个截面只使用了一个有限型 strict 测试目标。}

\bi{To pass to an arbitrary square-zero diagram, let $\psi:B\to C/J$ be as in the statement. Choose in $C$ one lift of each of the finitely many elements $\psi(\pi(X_i))$. They define an $A$-algebra map $\alpha:P\to C$ whose reduction modulo $J$ is $\psi\pi$. Hence $\alpha(I)\subset J$, and $\alpha(I^2)=0$. There is an induced map $\bar\alpha:Q\to C$, and $\bar\alpha\sigma$ lifts $\psi$ because $q\sigma=\mathrm{id}_B$. Any two lifts agree on $A$, so the ring-epimorphism property gives uniqueness. This proves the asserted lifting property without assuming that the arbitrary target $C$ itself is a strict affinoid.}
{为处理任意平方零图，取陈述中的 $\psi:B\to C/J$。在 $C$ 中为有限多个元素 $\psi(\pi(X_i))$ 各选择一个提升，它们定义 $A$-代数映射 $\alpha:P\to C$，其模 $J$ 的约化为 $\psi\pi$。因此 $\alpha(I)\subset J$，并且 $\alpha(I^2)=0$。于是得到诱导映射 $\bar\alpha:Q\to C$；由 $q\sigma=\mathrm{id}_B$，复合 $\bar\alpha\sigma$ 提升 $\psi$。任意两个提升在 $A$ 上相同，所以环上态性质给出唯一性。由此证明所述提升性质，并没有假设任意目标 $C$ 本身是 strict affinoid。}

\bi{This is formal étaleness in \cite[Tag 00UQ, Definition 10.150.1]{StacksAlg}. Finite presentation was proved in Lemma~\ref{fnd:trivialstrictalgebraicbridge}. Apply \cite[Tag 00UR, Lemma 10.150.3]{StacksAlg} to obtain étaleness, and \cite[Tag 00U2, Lemma 10.143.3(6)]{StacksAlg} to obtain algebraic flatness. These are authorized external algebraic inputs, not additional analytic assumptions.}
{这正是 \cite[Tag 00UQ, Definition 10.150.1]{StacksAlg} 中的形式 étale 性。有限表示已经由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:trivialstrictalgebraicbridge} 证明。应用 \cite[Tag 00UR, Lemma 10.150.3]{StacksAlg} 得到 étale 性，再应用 \cite[Tag 00U2, Lemma 10.143.3(6)]{StacksAlg} 得到代数平坦性。它们是获准使用的外部代数输入，并非附加的解析假设。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{The nontrivial-valuation hypothesis in the local Weierstrass form of Gerritzen--Grauert cannot simply be removed. Over a trivially valued field, strict affinoid algebras carry discrete defining norms. This makes an algebraic route available for finite rational coverings of a domain, but it also makes the stronger ambient local Weierstrass conclusion false. We prove the positive statement and the obstruction separately.}
{Gerritzen--Grauert 的局部 Weierstrass 表述中的非平凡赋值假设不能直接删去。平凡赋值域上的 strict affinoid 代数具有离散的定义范数。这使域自身的有限 rational 覆盖可以通过代数方法构造，同时也使更强的、相对于环境覆盖的局部 Weierstrass 结论不成立。下面分别证明正面结论与这一障碍。}

\bi{Except in Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis}, $K$ is complete with its fixed \emph{trivial} absolute value. Algebras are commutative and unital, and a strict affinoid algebra is equipped with a norm-admissible Banach norm. The term strict-test affinoid domain has exactly the meaning of Definition~\ref{fnd:stricttestaffinoiddomain}; its targets are strict $K$-affinoid algebras. In the trivial-valuation assertions, all strict rational and Weierstrass radii lie in $|K^\times|=\{1\}$. We allow the zero algebra with its usual conventions.}
{除 Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis} 外，本节假设 $K$ 完备，并固定其\emph{平凡}绝对值。代数均交换含幺，strict affinoid 代数配备 norm-admissible Banach 范数。Strict-test affinoid 域严格采用 Definition~\ref{fnd:stricttestaffinoiddomain} 的含义，其测试目标是 strict $K$-affinoid 代数。在平凡赋值的断言中，所有 strict rational 与 Weierstrass 半径都属于 $|K^\times|=\{1\}$。允许零代数，并采用通常约定。}
