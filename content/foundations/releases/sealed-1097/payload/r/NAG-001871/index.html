Lifting fibre injectivity at an inner point

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 467

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Lifting fibre injectivity at an inner point}{在内点处提升纤维单射性}]
\label{fnd:charzeroinnerflatcokernel}
\bi{Let $K$ have characteristic zero, let $T\to X$ be a morphism of good analytic spaces, and let $t\in\operatorname{Int}(T/X)$ with image $x$ and nontrivially valued $\mathcal H(x)$. Let $u:L\to N$ be a morphism of coherent sheaves. If $N_t$ is flat over $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ and the induced map on the analytic fibre is injective at $t$, then $u_t$ is injective and $\operatorname{Coker}(u)_t$ is flat over $\mathcal O_{X,x}$.}
{设 $K$ 为特征零域，$T\to X$ 为 good 解析空间之间的态射，$t\in\operatorname{Int}(T/X)$ 映到 $x$，且 $\mathcal H(x)$ 的赋值非平凡。设 $u:L\to N$ 为凝聚层态射。若 $N_t$ 在 $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 上平坦，且解析纤维上的诱导映射在 $t$ 处单射，则 $u_t$ 单射，且 $\operatorname{Coker}(u)_t$ 在 $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 上平坦。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$, $S=\mathcal O_{T,t}$, and let $\mathfrak m$ be the maximal ideal of $R$. Coherent pullback identifies the fibre stalk map with $u_t\otimes_S\mathcal O_{T_x,t}$. Corollary~\ref{fnd:charzeroclosedfibercomparison} says that $S/\mathfrak mS\to\mathcal O_{T_x,t}$ is faithfully flat. Thus fibre injectivity descends to}
{令 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$S=\mathcal O_{T,t}$，以 $\mathfrak m$ 记 $R$ 的极大理想。凝聚拉回把纤维茎映射识别为 $u_t\otimes_S\mathcal O_{T_x,t}$。Corollary~\ref{fnd:charzeroclosedfibercomparison} 说明 $S/\mathfrak mS\to\mathcal O_{T_x,t}$ 忠实平坦，所以纤维单射性下降为}
\[
 L_t/\mathfrak m L_t\longrightarrow N_t/\mathfrak m N_t
 \quad\text{injective}.
\]
\bi{The ring $S$ is Noetherian, $L_t$ is a finite $S$-module, and $N_t$ is flat over $R$. The local flat-map criterion \cite[Tag 00ME]{StacksAlg}, applied with these modules, gives both conclusions. Formation of the coherent cokernel commutes with ordinary stalks, identifying its module with the quotient in that criterion.}
{环 $S$ Noetherian，$L_t$ 为有限 $S$-模，且 $N_t$ 在 $R$ 上平坦。将局部平坦映射判据 \cite[Tag 00ME]{StacksAlg} 用于这两个模，即得两个结论。凝聚余核与普通茎交换，因此其模恰为该判据中的商模。}
\end{proof}
