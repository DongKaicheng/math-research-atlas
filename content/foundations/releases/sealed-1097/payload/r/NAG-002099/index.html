A symmetric subdivision of a nodal annulus

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 698

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A symmetric subdivision of a nodal annulus}
{节点圆环的对称细分}]
\label{cand:symmetricnodeblowup}
\bi{Let $b,\lambda\in\mathfrak m\setminus\{0\}$ with
$|b|\leq|\lambda|^2$, and put $c=b/\lambda^2\in O$.
The blowup of $(\lambda,s,t)$ in
$\operatorname{Spec}O[s,t]/(st-b)$ is covered by the three
flat finitely presented charts}
{设 $b,\lambda\in\mathfrak m\setminus\{0\}$ 满足
$|b|\leq|\lambda|^2$，并令 $c=b/\lambda^2\in O$。
$\operatorname{Spec}O[s,t]/(st-b)$ 中
$(\lambda,s,t)$ 的 blowup 由以下三个平坦有限表示坐标片覆盖：}
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{\bi{generator}{生成元}}&
\text{\bi{chart ring}{坐标环}}&
\text{\bi{map to the original chart}{到原坐标片的映射}}\\ \hline
\lambda&O[u,v]/(uv-c)&s=\lambda u,\quad t=\lambda v\\
s&O[s,w]/(sw-\lambda)&t=\lambda c w\\
t&O[t,w']/(tw'-\lambda)&s=\lambda c w'.
\end{array}
\]
\bi{Its formal completion has the same analytic generic fibre
as the original model. On the original annular skeleton it
inserts the radii $|s|=|\lambda|$ and
$|s|=|b|/|\lambda|$. These are two new component points if
$|b|<|\lambda|^2$, and one if $|b|=|\lambda|^2$. The
resulting edge lengths are, respectively,}
{其形式完备化与原模型具有相同的解析泛纤维。在原圆环骨架上，
它插入半径 $|s|=|\lambda|$ 和
$|s|=|b|/|\lambda|$。当 $|b|<|\lambda|^2$ 时，它们是
两个新的分量点；当 $|b|=|\lambda|^2$ 时，它们合为一个。
相应的边长分别为}
\[
 -\log|\lambda|,\quad
 \log\frac{|\lambda|^2}{|b|},\quad
 -\log|\lambda|,
 \qquad\text{\bi{or}{或}}\qquad
 -\log|\lambda|,\quad-\log|\lambda|.
\]
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Set $A=O[s,t]/(st-b)$. Its normal form with monomials
$1,s^i,t^j$ for $i,j>0$ embeds $A$ into $K[s,s^{-1}]$.
The affine blowup chart for $\lambda$ is
$A[s/\lambda,t/\lambda]\subset A[1/\lambda]$.
It is exactly $O[u,v]/(uv-c)$: the displayed map is surjective
onto that subring, and its injectivity follows from the same
normal form, now with $u=s/\lambda$ and $v=c/u$.}
{令 $A=O[s,t]/(st-b)$。以 $1,s^i,t^j$（$i,j>0$）
为单项式的正规形将 $A$ 嵌入 $K[s,s^{-1}]$。
生成元 $\lambda$ 对应的仿射 blowup 片是
$A[s/\lambda,t/\lambda]\subset A[1/\lambda]$。
它恰为 $O[u,v]/(uv-c)$：所列映射满射到该子环，而
单射性由同一正规形得到，此时 $u=s/\lambda$、$v=c/u$。}

\bi{For the $s$-chart, put $w=\lambda/s$.
Inside $A[1/s]$ one has}
{对 $s$-片，置 $w=\lambda/s$。在 $A[1/s]$ 中有}
\[
 t=\frac{b}{s}=\lambda c w,\qquad
 \frac{t}{s}=c w^2 .
\]
\bi{Consequently $A[\lambda/s,t/s]$ equals the subring
generated by $s,w$, with sole relation $sw=\lambda$.
The normal form again proves injectivity. Interchanging $s,t$
gives the third chart. Each chart is a free $O$-module with
the indicated normal-form basis, so is flat; its displayed
presentation is finite. These computations identify the
actual blowup subrings, not unsaturated presentations with
extra vertical torsion. Their completions are the admissible
charts of Theorem~\ref{fnd:formalblowupcharts}.}
{故 $A[\lambda/s,t/s]$ 等于由 $s,w$ 生成、唯一关系为
$sw=\lambda$ 的子环；正规形再次证明单射性。
交换 $s,t$ 得第三片。每片以所列正规形单项式为基，是自由
$O$-模，因而平坦，且所列表示有限。这一计算识别的是实际
blowup 子环，而非含有额外垂直挠元的未饱和表示。它们的
完备化正是 Theorem~\ref{fnd:formalblowupcharts}
中的 admissible 坐标片。}

\bi{If $|c|<1$, the middle special chart is $uv=0$.
The two outer special charts are $sw=0$ and $tw'=0$.
On the first overlap $u=w^{-1}$; on the second
$v=(w')^{-1}$. Thus the two new components are projective
lines, joined to each other and to the two old branches in a
chain. If $|c|=1$, the middle special chart is
$uv=\bar c$, and its two ends glue to the two outer affine
pieces to give a single projective line between the old
branches. The special fibres are reduced and nodal in both
cases.}
{若 $|c|<1$，中间特殊坐标片为 $uv=0$，两个外侧特殊片
分别为 $sw=0$ 和 $tw'=0$。第一交叠中 $u=w^{-1}$，
第二交叠中 $v=(w')^{-1}$。因此新增两个射影直线分量，
与彼此及两条旧分支构成一条链。若 $|c|=1$，中间特殊
坐标片为 $uv=\bar c$，其两端分别与外侧仿射片粘合，在
两条旧分支之间形成一个射影直线分量。两种情形的特殊纤维
均约化且仅有节点。}

\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:affineblowupgenericinvariance} identifies
the completed generic charts with their rational domains.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:standardannuluscanonical} then identifies the
new component points with the stated Gauss radii. In the
strict-inequality case the middle annulus has parameter $c$;
the outer annuli have parameter $\lambda$. In the equality
case the middle generic chart is a circle. The logarithmic
length formulas follow, and their sum is $-\log|b|$.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:affineblowupgenericinvariance} 将完备化
泛纤维坐标片识别为相应的有理域。
Lemma~\ref{fnd:standardannuluscanonical} 随后将新增
分量点识别为所列 Gauss 半径。严格不等号情形的中间圆环
参数为 $c$，两个外侧圆环参数为 $\lambda$；等号情形的
中间泛纤维片为圆周。对数边长公式由此得到，且其和为
$-\log|b|$。}
\end{proof}
