Finiteness from a fixed fibrewise order

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 93

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Finiteness from a fixed fibrewise order}{由固定的逐纤维阶数推出有限性}]\label{fnd:relativepointwisedivisionfinite}
\bi{Let $n\geq1$ and $C=A\langle T_1,\ldots,T_{n-1}\rangle$. If $F\in C\langle Z\rangle$ is $Z$-distinguished of the same order $s$ at every maximal ideal of $A$, then the natural map}
{设 $n\geq1$，并令 $C=A\langle T_1,\ldots,T_{n-1}\rangle$。若 $F\in C\langle Z\rangle$ 在 $A$ 的每个极大理想处都是相同阶数 $s$ 的 $Z$-distinguished 级数，则自然映射}
\[
 C\longrightarrow D=C\langle Z\rangle/(F)
\]
\bi{is finite. The quotient norm on $D$ is uniformly equivalent to its canonical finite-$C$-module norm. If $s=0$, then $D=0$.}
{是有限映射。$D$ 的商范数与其典范有限 $C$-模范数一致等价。若 $s=0$，则 $D=0$。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The zero coefficient algebra causes no difficulty. Write $F=\sum_{j\geq0}F_jZ^j$. Any maximal residue field of $C$ is finite over $K$, and its restriction to $A$ is a maximal residue point, by the finite-dimensional-domain argument in the preceding proof. Evaluation of $F_s$ at such a point factors through the unit $F_{s,x}\in k_x\langle T'\rangle$. It is therefore nonzero. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} makes $F_s$ a unit of $C$. Replacing $F$ by $F_s^{-1}F$, assume $F_s=1$. In every maximal residue field of $C$ one then has}
{零系数代数的情形直接成立。写 $F=\sum_{j\geq0}F_jZ^j$。由前一证明中的有限维整环论证，$C$ 的任一极大剩余域都有限于 $K$，其到 $A$ 的限制为极大剩余域点。在这样的点处对 $F_s$ 取值，通过单位元 $F_{s,x}\in k_x\langle T'\rangle$ 分解，因此不为零。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}，$F_s$ 是 $C$ 的单位元。将 $F$ 替换为 $F_s^{-1}F$，可设 $F_s=1$。于是，在 $C$ 的每个极大剩余域中都有}
\[
 |F_j|\leq1\quad(j<s),\qquad |F_j|<1\quad(j>s).
\]
\bi{Here normalization of each fibre is legitimate because Lemma~\ref{fnd:tateunitconstantcriterion} gives $\|F_{s,x}^{-1}\|=\|F_{s,x}\|^{-1}$. The normalized coefficients still tend to zero in $C$. If $s=0$, each fibre $F_x$ is a unit in $k_x\langle T',Z\rangle$; the same maximal-residue argument makes $F$ a unit globally, so $D=0$. We may henceforth assume $s\geq1$ and $D\ne0$.}
{这里可以对每个纤维作正规化，因为 Lemma~\ref{fnd:tateunitconstantcriterion} 给出 $\|F_{s,x}^{-1}\|=\|F_{s,x}\|^{-1}$。正规化后的系数在 $C$ 中仍趋于零。若 $s=0$，则每个纤维 $F_x$ 都是 $k_x\langle T',Z\rangle$ 中的单位元；同一个极大剩余域论证使 $F$ 在全局也是单位元，故 $D=0$。以下可设 $s\geq1$ 且 $D\ne0$。}

\bi{Write $z$ for the image of $Z$ in $D$ and put $w=z^s+\sum_{j<s}F_jz^j$. At every maximal residue point $y$ of $D$, the residue of $z$ has absolute value at most one and the restriction to $C$ is maximal. The relation $F(z)=0$ gives}
{以 $z$ 表示 $Z$ 在 $D$ 中的像，并令 $w=z^s+\sum_{j<s}F_jz^j$。在 $D$ 的每个极大剩余域点 $y$ 处，$z$ 的剩余类绝对值不超过一，且到 $C$ 的限制为极大点。关系 $F(z)=0$ 给出}
\[
 w(y)=-\sum_{j>s}F_j(y)z(y)^j,\qquad |w(y)|<1.
\]
\bi{The strict inequality follows because each summand has absolute value less than one, and their absolute values tend to zero; hence their maximum is less than one. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictmaximalpointtopnilpotent} shows that $w$ is topologically nilpotent.}
{严格不等式成立的理由是：每一项的绝对值都小于一，而且这些绝对值趋于零，所以其最大值小于一。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictmaximalpointtopnilpotent}，$w$ 为拓扑幂零元素。}

\bi{By Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointwisepowerbounded}, the elements $F_0,\ldots,F_{s-1}$ of $C$ are power bounded. Start with an admissible strict presentation of $C$, and append variables $Y_0,\ldots,Y_{s-1}$ mapping to these elements. Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundedsubstitution} gives a bounded surjection $p:R\twoheadrightarrow C$, where $R$ is a Tate algebra over $K$. It is admissible: the old presentation already supplies lifts with a uniform norm bound, while boundedness of $p$ supplies the reverse norm inequality. Proposition~\ref{fnd:weightedc0exact}, applied coefficientwise, then gives an admissible surjection}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointwisepowerbounded}，$C$ 中的元素 $F_0,\ldots,F_{s-1}$ 均幂有界。从 $C$ 的一个 admissible strict 表示出发，添加映到这些元素的变量 $Y_0,\ldots,Y_{s-1}$。Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundedsubstitution} 给出有界满射 $p:R\twoheadrightarrow C$，其中 $R$ 是 $K$ 上的 Tate 代数。该满射为 admissible：原表示已经提供统一范数界的提升，而 $p$ 的有界性提供反向范数不等式。逐系数应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:weightedc0exact}，再得到 admissible 满射}
\[
 \pi:R\langle Z\rangle\twoheadrightarrow D.
\]
\bi{The polynomial $W=Z^s+\sum_{j<s}Y_jZ^j$ maps to $w$. Topological nilpotence and admissibility give an integer $r\geq1$ for which $\|\pi(W^r)\|_\pi<1$. By the definition of a quotient norm, choose $G\in R\langle Z\rangle$ of norm less than one with $\pi(G)=\pi(W^r)$. Then}
{多项式 $W=Z^s+\sum_{j<s}Y_jZ^j$ 映到 $w$。由拓扑幂零性与 admissibility，存在整数 $r\geq1$，使 $\|\pi(W^r)\|_\pi<1$。根据商范数的定义，选择范数小于一的 $G\in R\langle Z\rangle$，使 $\pi(G)=\pi(W^r)$。于是}
\[
 H=W^r-G\in\ker\pi.
\]
\bi{The polynomial $W^r$ is monic of degree $rs$ in $Z$ and all its lower coefficients have norm at most one. Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachweierstrassdivision}, applied with perturbation $-G$, expresses every element of $R\langle Z\rangle/(H)$ as a polynomial in $Z$ of degree less than $rs$, with the remainder norm no larger than the original norm. Since $H$ lies in the kernel of $\pi$, the elements $1,z,\ldots,z^{rs-1}$ generate $D$ over $C$.}
{$W^r$ 是关于 $Z$ 的 $rs$ 次首一多项式，各低次系数的范数不超过一。将扰动 $-G$ 代入 Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachweierstrassdivision}，可将 $R\langle Z\rangle/(H)$ 的每个元素表示为次数小于 $rs$ 的 $Z$-多项式，且余式范数不超过原元素范数。由于 $H$ 位于 $\pi$ 的核中，元素 $1,z,\ldots,z^{rs-1}$ 在 $C$ 上生成 $D$。}

\bi{For the norm assertion, lift $d\in D$ through the admissible map $\pi$ with norm at most a fixed constant times $\|d\|_D$, divide the lift by $H$, and apply the bounded coefficient map $p$ to the remainder. This gives an expression $d=\sum_{j<rs}c_jz^j$ with $\max_j\|c_j\|_C\leq C_1\|d\|_D$, where $C_1$ is independent of $d$. Conversely the finite generator map $C^{rs}\to D$ is bounded. Its quotient norm is therefore uniformly equivalent to $\|\cdot\|_D$, and this quotient norm is a canonical finite-$C$-module norm.}
{为证明范数断言，通过 admissible 映射 $\pi$ 提升 $d\in D$，使提升的范数不超过固定常数乘以 $\|d\|_D$；将提升除以 $H$，并对余式的系数施加有界映射 $p$。这样得到表示 $d=\sum_{j<rs}c_jz^j$，满足 $\max_j\|c_j\|_C\leq C_1\|d\|_D$，其中 $C_1$ 与 $d$ 无关。反之，有限生成元映射 $C^{rs}\to D$ 有界。因此其商范数与 $\|\cdot\|_D$ 一致等价，而该商范数正是一种典范有限 $C$-模范数。}
\end{proof}
