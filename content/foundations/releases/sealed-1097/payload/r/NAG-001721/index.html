Bounded splittings in countable type

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 294

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Bounded splittings in countable type}{可数型空间中的有界分裂}]
\label{fnd:countablebanachsplitting}
\bi{Let $E$ be a Banach $K$-space of countable type, meaning that a countable set has dense linear span. Every closed subspace $N\subset E$ is of countable type and admits a bounded linear projection $E\to N$. More generally, every norm-strict surjection of Banach spaces onto a space of countable type admits a bounded linear section. Finite modules with their canonical norms over generalized $K$-affinoid algebras are of countable type.}
{设 $E$ 为可数型 Banach $K$-空间，即某个可数集合的线性包稠密。每个闭子空间 $N\subset E$ 都是可数型，并具有有界线性投影 $E\to N$。更一般地，每个到可数型空间的 Banach 空间范数严格满射，都具有有界线性截面。广义 $K$-affinoid 代数上的有限模配备典范范数后均为可数型。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Let $p:B\to Q$ be the asserted norm-strict surjection. Replace the norm on $Q$ by the equivalent quotient norm. Choose a family $(e_i)$ as in Lemma~\ref{fnd:countablebanachcoordinates}, with lower constant $0<c<1$. For each $i$, choose a lift $\widehat e_i$ with $\|\widehat e_i\|\le2\|e_i\|$. If $q=\sum_i a_ie_i$, then $|a_i|\|e_i\|\to0$. Hence $\sum_i a_i\widehat e_i$ converges in $B$, and defines a linear section $s:Q\to B$ satisfying $\|s(q)\|\le2c^{-1}\|q\|$. This proves the splitting assertion.}
{设 $p:B\to Q$ 为所述范数严格满射。以等价的商范数替换 $Q$ 的范数。利用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:countablebanachcoordinates} 选取族 $(e_i)$，其下界常数为 $0<c<1$。对每个 $i$，选取满足 $\|\widehat e_i\|\le2\|e_i\|$ 的提升 $\widehat e_i$。若 $q=\sum_i a_ie_i$，则 $|a_i|\|e_i\|\to0$，所以 $\sum_i a_i\widehat e_i$ 在 $B$ 中收敛，并定义线性截面 $s:Q\to B$，满足 $\|s(q)\|\le2c^{-1}\|q\|$。这就证明分裂断言。}

\bi{Now take $Q=E/N$. It is Banach by Lemma~\ref{fnd:completequotient}. The images of a countable dense spanning set of $E$ span densely in $Q$, so the preceding argument gives a bounded section $s$. The map $P=1-sp:E\to N$ is a bounded projection. Applying $P$ to that same countable set gives a dense spanning set in $N$. Finally, a generalized affinoid algebra is a quotient of a finite-variable Tate algebra, whose polynomial monomials form a countable dense spanning set. A finite module is a bounded quotient of a finite power of this algebra by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}. Images of the corresponding spanning sets prove the last assertion.}
{现在取 $Q=E/N$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:completequotient}，它是 Banach 空间。$E$ 的一个可数稠密生成集的像在 $Q$ 中仍稠密生成，因此上述论证给出有界截面 $s$。映射 $P=1-sp:E\to N$ 为有界投影。将同一个可数集合用 $P$ 映出，就在 $N$ 中得到稠密生成集。最后，广义 affinoid 代数是有限变量 Tate 代数的商，而后者的多项式单项式构成可数稠密生成集。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}，有限模是该代数有限次幂的有界商；取上述生成集的像，就得到最后一项。}
\end{proof}
