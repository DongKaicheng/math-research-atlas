The corner projection formula

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 875

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The corner projection formula}{角点投影公式}]
\label{cand:cornerprojectionformula}
\bi{Let $f$ and $C$ be as in
Theorem~\ref{cand:balancedpolyhedralpushforward}, and let
$\varphi$ be a continuous piecewise affine function on the
target. Then}
{设 $f,C$ 如 Theorem~\ref{cand:balancedpolyhedralpushforward}，
$\varphi$ 为目标空间上的连续分片仿射函数。则}
\[
 f_*(D_{\varphi\circ f}C)=D_\varphi(f_*C).
\]
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Refine the complexes so that all images are cells and
$\varphi$ is affine on each image. For a target $(d-1)$-cell
$\tau'$, extend the linear part of $\varphi|_{\tau'}$ to a
linear form $\ell$ on the target. On any source face
$\tau$ mapping onto $\tau'$, use $\ell\circ f$ in the corner
formula. For an incident facet $\sigma$ of full image dimension,
the difference $\varphi_{f(\sigma)}-\ell$ vanishes tangentially
to $\tau'$. Lemma~\ref{cand:facetindexidentity} therefore gives}
{细分复形，使所有像为胞腔且 $\varphi$ 在每个像上仿射。
对目标 $(d-1)$-胞腔 $\tau'$，将
$\varphi|_{\tau'}$ 的线性部分延拓为目标空间上的线性形式
$\ell$。对映满 $\tau'$ 的源面 $\tau$，在角点公式中
选用 $\ell\circ f$。若相邻极大胞腔 $\sigma$ 的像满维，
则 $\varphi_{f(\sigma)}-\ell$ 沿 $\tau'$ 的切向为零，
由 Lemma~\ref{cand:facetindexidentity} 得}
\[
 a_\tau\bigl(\varphi_{f(\sigma)}-\ell\bigr)
                   (f(n_{\sigma/\tau}))
 =a_\sigma\bigl(\varphi_{f(\sigma)}-\ell\bigr)
                   (n_{f(\sigma)/\tau'}).
\]
\bi{A facet collapsed to dimension $d-1$ has
$f(N_{\sigma,\mathbf R})=N'_{\tau',\mathbf R}$, so its term
vanishes. A lower-rank facet cannot contain such a $\tau$.
Multiply by $w_C(\sigma)$ and sum over $\tau$ and its incident
facets. As in the pushforward proof, every full-rank facet over
a target facet containing $\tau'$ occurs once. The resulting
sum is exactly the corner weight of $f_*C$ at $\tau'$. If no
full-rank facet occurs, both sides have weight zero there. This
proves equality of the cycles.}
{若极大胞腔降到维数 $d-1$，则
$f(N_{\sigma,\mathbf R})=N'_{\tau',\mathbf R}$，其项为零；
更低秩的胞腔不能包含这样的 $\tau$。乘以 $w_C(\sigma)$，
对 $\tau$ 及其相邻极大胞腔求和。与推前证明相同，每个映到
包含 $\tau'$ 的目标极大胞腔的满秩源胞腔恰好出现一次。
所得总和正是 $f_*C$ 在 $\tau'$ 处的角点权重。若无满秩
胞腔，两侧在该处权重均为零，故循环相等。}
\end{proof}
