A model adapted to finitely many marked points

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 702

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{A model adapted to finitely many marked points}
{适配有限个标记点的模型}]
\label{cand:adaptedmarkedmodel}
\bi{Under the hypotheses of
Theorem~\ref{cand:finitetypeiirealization}, let
$P\subset X(K)$ be finite. There is a model obtained by
finitely many of the preceding admissible blowups such that
the points of $P$ specialize to pairwise distinct smooth
closed points of the special fibre. The construction can
retain any finite set of previously specified type II
vertices. It preserves projectivity.}
{在 Theorem~\ref{cand:finitetypeiirealization} 的假设下，
设 $P\subset X(K)$ 有限。经过前述有限次 admissible
blowup，可得到一个模型，使 $P$ 中各点特化到特殊纤维上
两两不同的光滑闭点。构造可保留预先指定的任意有限个
第二类顶点，并保持射影性。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A $K$-rational analytic point cannot specialize to a
component generic point, since
Lemma~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield} gives the latter
a residual function field of transcendence degree one,
whereas the rational point has completed residue field $K$.
Thus every point of $P$ lies in a closed-point tube. If a
marked point belongs to a nodal tube $st=b$, insert the
radial point with radius $|s(a)|$. Its reduction on the new
component has nonzero finite scaled coordinate, so is smooth.
Points already specializing to smooth points are unchanged
by a blowup supported at a node. Repeating at most $|P|$
times puts every marked point in a smooth tube.}
{一个 $K$-有理解析点不能特化到分量泛点：由
Lemma~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield}，后者的
剩余域为超越次数一的函数域，而有理点的完备剩余域是 $K$。
因此 $P$ 中各点均位于某个闭点管域。若标记点 $a$
属于节点管域 $st=b$，插入半径 $|s(a)|$ 的径向点。
它在新分量上的约化具有非零有限的缩放坐标，故为光滑点。
支撑于节点的 blowup 不改变已经特化到光滑点的标记点。
至多重复 $|P|$ 次，可使所有标记点均位于光滑管域中。}

\bi{Suppose a smooth tube contains distinct marked points
with coordinates $a_1,\ldots,a_m$, where $m\geq2$. Set}
{设一个光滑管域包含坐标为 $a_1,\ldots,a_m$ 的不同标记点，
其中 $m\geq2$。令}
\[
 R=\max_{i,j}|a_i-a_j|\in|K^\times|,\qquad 0<R<1.
\]
\bi{Insert $\zeta_{a_1,R}$. Every marked point lies in the
closed disc $|z-a_1|\leq R$, so none lies in the new
attaching annulus. On the exceptional affine line their
specializations are the residues of
$(a_i-a_1)/\lambda$, where $|\lambda|=R$.
At least two of these residues differ: one is zero and
another has absolute value one. The points are therefore
partitioned into at least two nonempty groups, each lying
in a distinct smooth residual tube. Repeat on any group
of size greater than one. Each step increases the number
of nonempty groups and never exceeds $|P|$, so the process
terminates with distinct smooth specializations.
All preceding blowups retain old vertices; insert any
additional prescribed type II vertices first.}
{插入 $\zeta_{a_1,R}$。所有标记点均位于闭圆盘
$|z-a_1|\leq R$ 中，所以没有标记点位于新增连接圆环中。
取 $|\lambda|=R$ 后，它们在例外仿射直线上的特化为
$(a_i-a_1)/\lambda$ 的剩余类。至少两个剩余类不同：
一个为零，另一个的绝对值为一。因此，标记点分成至少两个
非空组，每组位于不同的光滑剩余管域中。对仍含多个点的组
重复此过程。每步都增加非空组数，而其总数不超过 $|P|$，
故最终得到两两不同的光滑特化。前述所有 blowup 均保留
旧顶点；如另有指定第二类顶点，先将它们插入即可。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Let $K$ be complete and nontrivially valued.
We write $O=K^\circ$, $\mathfrak m=K^{\circ\circ}$ and
$k=O/\mathfrak m$. A nodal curve model in this section is a flat
finitely presented $O$-scheme with smooth generic fibre, reduced
nodal special fibre, and Hausdorff formal generic fibre.}
{设 $K$ 完备且非平凡赋值。
记 $O=K^\circ$、$\mathfrak m=K^{\circ\circ}$、
$k=O/\mathfrak m$。本节中的节点曲线模型是平坦有限表示
$O$-概形，其泛纤维光滑、特殊纤维约化且仅有节点，并且
形式泛纤维为 Hausdorff 空间。}
