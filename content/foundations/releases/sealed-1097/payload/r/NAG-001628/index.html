Interior of an affinoid domain

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 416

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Interior of an affinoid domain}{Affinoid 域的内部}]
\label{fnd:affinoiddomainordinaryinterior}
\bi{If $U$ is an affinoid domain of an affinoid space $X$, then $\operatorname{Int}(U/X)$ is the ordinary topological interior of $U$ in $X$.}
{若 $U$ 是 affinoid 空间 $X$ 的 affinoid 域，则 $\operatorname{Int}(U/X)$ 等于 $U$ 在 $X$ 中的普通拓扑内部。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Suppose $x$ belongs to the ordinary interior. Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictrationalinteriorneighborhood} gives a rational domain $W\subset U$ containing a neighborhood of $x$ and satisfying $x\in\operatorname{Int}(W/X)$. By Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainrestriction}, $W$ is also an affinoid domain of $U$. The composition formula for $W\to U\to X$ then gives $x\in\operatorname{Int}(U/X)$.}
{先设 $x$ 属于普通拓扑内部。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictrationalinteriorneighborhood}，存在 rational 域 $W\subset U$，它包含 $x$ 的一个邻域，且满足 $x\in\operatorname{Int}(W/X)$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainrestriction}，$W$ 也是 $U$ 的 affinoid 域。对 $W\to U\to X$ 使用内部的复合公式，得到 $x\in\operatorname{Int}(U/X)$。}

\bi{Conversely, let $x\in\operatorname{Int}(U/X)$. The relative boundary $D=U\setminus\operatorname{Int}(U/X)$ is compact, by openness of affinoid relative interior. The inclusion $U\hookrightarrow X$ is a topological embedding, so its compact image $D$ is closed in the Hausdorff space $X$ and does not contain $x$. Choose a rational domain $V$ that is a neighborhood of $x$ and is contained in $X\setminus D$. The base change $U_V=U\cap V$ is an affinoid domain of $V$. Every point of $U_V$ was interior in $U/X$, so the forward base-change inclusion in Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus} shows that $U_V\to V$ is boundaryless. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidboundarylessfinite}, its algebra is finite and norm-admissible over that of $V$. It is flat by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness}. Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteflatclopenimage} now says that $U\cap V$ is open in $V$. As $V$ is a neighborhood of $x$ in $X$, this provides an ordinary neighborhood of $x$ contained in $U$.}
{反之，设 $x\in\operatorname{Int}(U/X)$。由于 affinoid 相对内部为开集，相对边界 $D=U\setminus\operatorname{Int}(U/X)$ 为紧集。嵌入 $U\hookrightarrow X$ 是拓扑嵌入，故其紧像 $D$ 在 Hausdorff 空间 $X$ 中闭，且不含 $x$。选择包含于 $X\setminus D$ 的 rational 域 $V$，使它是 $x$ 的邻域。基变换 $U_V=U\cap V$ 是 $V$ 的 affinoid 域。$U_V$ 的每个点原本都属于 $U/X$ 的内部，因此 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus} 中基变换的正向包含说明 $U_V\to V$ 无边界。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidboundarylessfinite}，其代数在 $V$ 的代数上有限且 norm-admissible；由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness}，它也平坦。Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteflatclopenimage} 于是说明 $U\cap V$ 在 $V$ 中开。因为 $V$ 是 $x$ 在 $X$ 中的邻域，这就给出包含于 $U$ 的 $x$ 的普通拓扑邻域。}
\end{proof}
