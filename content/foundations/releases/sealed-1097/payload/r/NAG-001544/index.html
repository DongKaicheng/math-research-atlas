A finite rational covering in the trivial-valuation case

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 247

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{A finite rational covering in the trivial-valuation case}{平凡赋值情形的有限 rational 覆盖}]\label{fnd:trivialstrictdomainrationalcover}
\bi{Let $A\to B$ present a strict-test affinoid domain $U\subseteq\mathcal M(A)$. There exist finitely many elements $g_1,\ldots,g_t\in A$ such that, with $W$ the image of the ordinary prime spectrum $\operatorname{Spec}(B)\to\operatorname{Spec}(A)$,}
{设 $A\to B$ 表示 strict-test affinoid 域 $U\subseteq\mathcal M(A)$。存在有限多个元素 $g_1,\ldots,g_t\in A$，使得若以 $W$ 表示普通素谱映射 $\operatorname{Spec}(B)\to\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ 的像，则}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 W&=\bigcup_{i=1}^t D_A(g_i),&
 A[1/g_i]&\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ } B[1/\varphi(g_i)],\\
 U&=\bigcup_{i=1}^t R_i,&
 R_i&=\{x\in\mathcal M(A):|g_i(x)|=1\}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{The elements $\varphi(g_i)$ generate the unit ideal of $B$. Each $R_i$ is rational over the original $A$, with strict algebra $A\langle Z\rangle/(g_iZ-1)=A[1/g_i]$. The displayed algebra isomorphisms and}
{元素 $\varphi(g_i)$ 生成 $B$ 的单位理想。每个 $R_i$ 都在原始 $A$ 上为 rational，其 strict 代数为 $A\langle Z\rangle/(g_iZ-1)=A[1/g_i]$。所列代数同构以及}
\[
 B\widehat\otimes_A A[1/g_i]\ \simeq\ B[1/\varphi(g_i)]
 \ \simeq\ A[1/g_i]
\]
\bi{are bounded with bounded inverses; they are isometric for the discrete defining norms. They commute with restriction to finite intersections, obtained by inverting products of the $g_i$. Thus the domain itself has a finite original-$A$ rational covering with the stated algebra identifications. No local Weierstrass assertion on a covering of the whole ambient spectrum is made. The empty domain is represented by $B=0$ and uses the empty family.}
{及其逆均有界；对离散定义范数，它们为等距同构。这些同构与向有限交的限制相容，有限交通过逆化 $g_i$ 的乘积得到。因此，域自身具有所述代数识别下的有限原始 $A$ 上 rational 覆盖。这里不对整个环境谱的某个覆盖断言局部 Weierstrass 性。空域由 $B=0$ 表示，并使用空族。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{If $B=0$, all assertions have the indicated empty meaning. Suppose $B\ne0$. By Proposition~\ref{fnd:trivialstrictdomainetale}, $A\to B$ is a flat ring map of finite presentation. The authorized openness theorem \cite[Tag 00I1, Proposition 10.41.8]{StacksAlg} therefore makes $W$ open in $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$. By the definition of the Zariski topology, every point of $W$ lies in some principal open $D_A(g)$ contained in $W$: write the closed complement as $V(J)$ and choose $g\in J$ outside that prime.}
{若 $B=0$，全部断言具有所述空情形的含义。设 $B\ne0$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:trivialstrictdomainetale}，$A\to B$ 是具有有限表示的平坦环映射。因此，获准使用的开性定理 \cite[Tag 00I1, Proposition 10.41.8]{StacksAlg} 说明 $W$ 在 $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ 中开。由 Zariski 拓扑的定义，$W$ 的每个点都属于一个包含于 $W$ 的主开集 $D_A(g)$：将闭补集写作 $V(J)$，再选择不属于该素理想的 $g\in J$ 即可。}

\bi{Let $G$ be the set of all $g\in A$ with $D_A(g)\subseteq W$. Their images generate the unit ideal in $B$. Otherwise that ideal lies in a maximal ideal $\mathfrak n$ of $B$, by \cite[Tag 00E0, Lemma 10.17.2(2)]{StacksAlg} applied to its nonzero quotient. The contraction of $\mathfrak n$ belongs to $W$, and a principal neighbourhood as above supplies $g\in G$ whose image is not in $\mathfrak n$, a contradiction. Hence there is a finite certificate}
{令 $G$ 为所有满足 $D_A(g)\subseteq W$ 的 $g\in A$ 构成的集合。它们在 $B$ 中的像生成单位理想。否则，对相应非零商应用 \cite[Tag 00E0, Lemma 10.17.2(2)]{StacksAlg}，可将此理想包含在 $B$ 的某个极大理想 $\mathfrak n$ 中。$\mathfrak n$ 的收缩属于 $W$，上述主开邻域便给出某个 $g\in G$，使其像不属于 $\mathfrak n$，矛盾。因此存在有限证书}
\[
 \sum_{i=1}^t b_i\varphi(g_i)=1,\qquad b_i\in B,\quad g_i\in G.
\]
\bi{This also proves $W=\bigcup_iD_A(g_i)$. Indeed, choose a prime of $B$ over any point of $W$; at least one term $\varphi(g_i)$ lies outside that prime. The reverse inclusion holds by the choice of $G$.}
{这也证明 $W=\bigcup_iD_A(g_i)$。事实上，对 $W$ 中任一点选择其上的 $B$ 的素理想，则至少有一个 $\varphi(g_i)$ 不属于该素理想。反向包含来自 $G$ 的选择。}

\bi{Fix any $g\in G$. The ring map $A_g\to B_g$, where $B_g$ abbreviates $B[1/\varphi(g)]$, is étale by the base-change assertion \cite[Tag 00U2, Lemma 10.143.3(3)]{StacksAlg}, hence flat by part~(6). It remains an epimorphism: two maps from $B_g$ agreeing on $A_g$ agree on $B$ by Proposition~\ref{fnd:trivialstrictdomainetale}, and then agree on the inverse of $\varphi(g)$. Every maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ of $A_g$ has nonzero fiber in $B_g$. To verify this, contract it to a prime $\mathfrak p$ of $A$. Since $g\notin\mathfrak p$ and $D_A(g)\subseteq W$, there is a prime $\mathfrak q$ of $B$ over $\mathfrak p$. The fraction ideal $\mathfrak qB_g$ is prime and contracts to $\mathfrak m$; the fraction description verifies both assertions directly. Its residue field consequently receives a unital map from $B_g\otimes_{A_g}\kappa(\mathfrak m)$, so this fiber is nonzero.}
{固定任意 $g\in G$，并以 $B_g$ 简记 $B[1/\varphi(g)]$。由基变换断言 \cite[Tag 00U2, Lemma 10.143.3(3)]{StacksAlg}，环映射 $A_g\to B_g$ 为 étale，因而由第~(6) 项平坦。它仍然是上态：从 $B_g$ 出发且在 $A_g$ 上相同的两个映射，由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:trivialstrictdomainetale} 在 $B$ 上相同，随后也在 $\varphi(g)$ 的逆上相同。$A_g$ 的每个极大理想 $\mathfrak m$ 在 $B_g$ 中具有非零纤维。为核验这一点，将其收缩到 $A$ 的素理想 $\mathfrak p$。由于 $g\notin\mathfrak p$ 且 $D_A(g)\subseteq W$，存在 $B$ 中位于 $\mathfrak p$ 之上的素理想 $\mathfrak q$。分式理想 $\mathfrak qB_g$ 为素理想，且收缩为 $\mathfrak m$；这两个断言都可从分式表示直接核对。因此，它的剩余域接收一个从 $B_g\otimes_{A_g}\kappa(\mathfrak m)$ 出发的含幺映射，所以该纤维非零。}

\bi{By \cite[Tag 00HP, Lemma 10.39.15, (1)$\Leftrightarrow$(4)]{StacksAlg}, the map $A_g\to B_g$ is faithfully flat. The authorized epimorphism criterion \cite[Tag 04VU, Lemma 10.107.7]{StacksAlg} makes it an isomorphism. If $A_g=0$, its unital target $B_g$ is also zero and the same conclusion holds. Thus the open-immersion consequence is obtained by actual principal-localization isomorphisms; no separate analytic local-ring theorem is involved. All these maps and their inverses are bounded by Lemma~\ref{fnd:trivialstrictalgebraicbridge}.}
{由 \cite[Tag 00HP, Lemma 10.39.15, (1)$\Leftrightarrow$(4)]{StacksAlg}，$A_g\to B_g$ 忠实平坦。获准使用的上态判据 \cite[Tag 04VU, Lemma 10.107.7]{StacksAlg} 于是说明它为同构。若 $A_g=0$，其含幺目标 $B_g$ 也为零，同样得到结论。因此，这里的开浸入结论由实际主局部化同构给出，不涉及额外的解析局部环定理。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:trivialstrictalgebraicbridge}，这些映射及其逆均有界。}

\bi{We now compare the two spectra carefully. For $x\in\mathcal M(A)$, contractivity for the discrete norm, Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}, gives $|a(x)|\leq1$ for every $a\in A$. The subset}
{下面仔细比较两种谱。对 $x\in\mathcal M(A)$，由离散范数下的 contractivity，即 Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}，每个 $a\in A$ 都满足 $|a(x)|\leq1$。子集}
\[
 \mathfrak r_x=\{a\in A:|a(x)|<1\}
\]
\bi{is a prime ideal: the ultrametric inequality and the bound by one give the ideal axioms, while $|ab(x)|<1$ implies $|a(x)|<1$ or $|b(x)|<1$. It is a proper ideal because $|1|=1$. Hence}
{是素理想：超度量不等式及范数至多为一给出理想公理，而 $|ab(x)|<1$ 蕴含 $|a(x)|<1$ 或 $|b(x)|<1$。它是真理想，因为 $|1|=1$。因此}
\[
 \mathfrak r_x\in D_A(g)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad |g(x)|=1.
\]
\bi{This prime is not in general the kernel of $x$. Thus the comparison uses the value-one condition, not merely nonvanishing of $g$ at $x$.}
{这个素理想一般并不是 $x$ 的核。因此，此处比较使用取值为一的条件，而不只是 $g$ 在 $x$ 处非零。}

\bi{The rational algebra for $\{|g|=1\}$ is $A\langle Z\rangle/(gZ-1)$. Its tuple $(g;1)$ generates the unit ideal, and the inequality $1\leq|g|$ is equivalent to equality because all values are at most one. By the discrete bridge, this actual quotient is $A[1/g]$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology} identifies its spectrum with that rational subset. The already constructed bounded map $B\to B_g\simeq A_g$ shows that this subset lies in $U$ whenever $g\in G$. Conversely, for $y\in\mathcal M(B)$, the displayed unit certificate and the bounds $|b_i(y)|\leq1$ give $|\varphi(g_i)(y)|=1$ for at least one $i$. Thus every point of $U$ lies in some $R_i$. Equivalently, $U$ is precisely the set of $x$ for which $\mathfrak r_x\in W$, as follows from the finite principal covering just proved.}
{$\{|g|=1\}$ 的 rational 代数为 $A\langle Z\rangle/(gZ-1)$。其 tuple $(g;1)$ 生成单位理想；由于全部取值至多为一，不等式 $1\leq|g|$ 等价于等号。由离散桥，这个实际商就是 $A[1/g]$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology} 将其谱识别为该 rational 子集。当 $g\in G$ 时，已经构造的有界映射 $B\to B_g\simeq A_g$ 说明此子集包含在 $U$ 中。反之，对 $y\in\mathcal M(B)$，上述单位理想证书及界 $|b_i(y)|\leq1$ 说明至少有一个 $i$ 满足 $|\varphi(g_i)(y)|=1$。因此，$U$ 的每个点都属于某个 $R_i$。等价地，由刚证明的有限主开覆盖，$U$ 恰由满足 $\mathfrak r_x\in W$ 的点 $x$ 构成。}

\bi{Finally, localization gives the ordinary algebra isomorphism $B\otimes_A A_g\simeq B_g$: the mutually inverse maps send $b\otimes a/g^n$ to $ba/\varphi(g)^n$ and send $b/\varphi(g)^n$ to $b\otimes1/g^n$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:trivialstrictalgebraicbridge} identifies the ordinary tensor with its separated completion. Repeating this construction for products of the $g_i$ proves the finite-intersection assertions. Every map is fixed on coefficients and on the specified inverses, so the restriction maps commute.}
{最后，局部化给出普通代数同构 $B\otimes_A A_g\simeq B_g$：两个互逆映射分别将 $b\otimes a/g^n$ 送到 $ba/\varphi(g)^n$，并将 $b/\varphi(g)^n$ 送到 $b\otimes1/g^n$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:trivialstrictalgebraicbridge}，普通张量积与其 separated completion 相同。对 $g_i$ 的乘积重复此构造，即得有限交的断言。每个映射在系数及指定的逆元上都已确定，所以限制映射相互交换。}
\end{proof}
