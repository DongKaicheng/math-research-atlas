Galois action on completed fibres

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 641

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Galois action on completed fibres}{完备纤维上的 Galois 作用}]
\label{cand:finitegaloisfibres}
\bi{For a $K$-analytic space $X$ and $x\in X$, the fibre $p^{-1}(x)$ is a nonempty finite $G$-orbit. More precisely, putting $H=\mathcal H(x)$, there is a finite product decomposition}
{对 $K$-解析空间 $X$ 及 $x\in X$，纤维 $p^{-1}(x)$ 是一个非空有限 $G$-轨道。更准确地，令 $H=\mathcal H(x)$，则存在有限乘积分解}
\[
 H\widehat\otimes_K L=H\otimes_K L=\prod_{j=1}^s E_j,
 \qquad
 \sum_{j=1}^s[E_j:H]=[L:K],
\]
\bi{where the $E_j$ are finite field extensions of $H$, equipped with their unique extending absolute values. The factors correspond to the points $y_j$ of the fibre, with $\mathcal H(y_j)\simeq E_j$. The $G$-action permutes the factors transitively and preserves their valued-field structures.}
{其中 $E_j$ 是 $H$ 的有限域扩张，配备各自唯一的延拓绝对值。各因子对应纤维中的点 $y_j$，且 $\mathcal H(y_j)\simeq E_j$。$G$ 传递地置换这些因子，并保持其赋值域结构。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose a $K$-basis of $L$. Its coordinate maps are bounded by Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}. Tensoring these finitely many maps with $H$ compares the projective tensor norm with a coordinate maximum norm on $H^{[L:K]}$ in both directions. The latter is complete. Thus the algebraic tensor is already complete and is the displayed completed tensor.}
{选择 $L$ 的一组 $K$-基。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}，对应的坐标映射均有界。将这有限个映射与 $H$ 作张量积，便从两个方向将投射张量范数与 $H^{[L:K]}$ 上的坐标最大范数比较。后者完备，因此代数张量积已经完备，并等于所示的完备张量积。}

\bi{For a $K$-affinoid algebra $A$, the same coordinate comparison identifies $A\widehat\otimes_K L$ with the finite free $A$-algebra $A\otimes_K L$ in its canonical finite-module topology. It is $K$-affinoid by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras}. Every bounded $K$-point restricts on $L$ to its unique extending absolute value, so its $K$-spectrum and $L$-spectrum agree. The affinoid fibre formula therefore applies to the projection and gives precisely $H\otimes_K L$. These identifications glue on the chart atlas.}
{对 $K$-affinoid 代数 $A$，同一坐标比较将 $A\widehat\otimes_K L$ 识别为配备典范有限模拓扑的有限自由 $A$-代数 $A\otimes_K L$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras}，它为 $K$-affinoid。每个有界 $K$-点在 $L$ 上都限制为唯一的延拓绝对值，因此其 $K$-谱与 $L$-谱相同。于是 affinoid 纤维公式适用于此投影，并恰好给出 $H\otimes_K L$。这些识别沿图册粘合。}

\bi{By the primitive-element lemma, \cite[Tag 030N]{StacksAlg}, write $L=K[\alpha]$ with separable minimal polynomial $P$. The coprimality of $P$ and $P'$ persists in $H[T]$ by the same polynomial Bezout identity. Factoring $P$ into distinct monic irreducibles over $H$ and applying the Chinese remainder construction gives the product of fields $E_j=H[T]/(P_j)$. The sum of their degrees is $\deg P=[L:K]$. Each field is complete and has precisely one extending absolute value by Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}. The fibre construction of Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticfibres} now gives the point and completed-residue-field assertions.}
{由本原元引理 \cite[Tag 030N]{StacksAlg}，可写 $L=K[\alpha]$，其极小多项式 $P$ 可分。同一个多项式 Bezout 恒等式说明 $P,P'$ 在 $H[T]$ 中仍互素。将 $P$ 在 $H$ 上分解为互异首一不可约多项式，再作中国剩余构造，得到域的乘积，其中 $E_j=H[T]/(P_j)$。各次数之和等于 $\deg P=[L:K]$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}，每个域均完备，并恰有一个延拓绝对值。此时 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticfibres} 的纤维构造给出点及其完备剩余域的断言。}

\bi{The fixed algebra is $(H\otimes_K L)^G=H$. Indeed, write an invariant element as $\sum_i h_i\otimes\ell_i$, with the finitely many $h_i$ linearly independent over $K$. Applying each $\sigma-1$ shows that every $\ell_i$ lies in $L^G=K$, where the last equality is \cite[Tag 09DW]{StacksAlg}. If the primitive idempotents of the product had two or more $G$-orbits, the sum of the idempotents in one orbit would be a nontrivial idempotent in this fixed field. This is impossible. Hence the action is transitive. An induced $H$-isomorphism between two factors preserves their absolute values by uniqueness, which completes the proof.}
{不变代数为 $(H\otimes_K L)^G=H$。事实上，将一个不变元素写为 $\sum_i h_i\otimes\ell_i$，使有限多个 $h_i$ 在 $K$ 上线性无关。逐一施加 $\sigma-1$，即知每个 $\ell_i$ 属于 $L^G=K$，其中最后的等式来自 \cite[Tag 09DW]{StacksAlg}。若乘积代数的本原幂等元分成两个或更多 $G$-轨道，则任一轨道中的幂等元之和会给出此不变域中的非平凡幂等元，矛盾。因此作用传递。因子之间由此诱导的 $H$-同构根据唯一性保持绝对值，证明完毕。}
\end{proof}
