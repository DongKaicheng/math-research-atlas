Flatness along a nontrivially valued inner generic fibre

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 483

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Flatness along a nontrivially valued inner generic fibre}{非平凡赋值的内泛纤维平坦性}]
\label{fnd:nontrivialinnergenericfibreflatness}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good analytic spaces over any complete field, let $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$, and put $x=f(y)$. If $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is a field and $\mathcal H(x)$ is nontrivially valued, then $\mathcal O_{Y,y}\to\mathcal O_{Y_x,y}$ is faithfully flat.}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 为任意完备域上 good 解析空间之间的态射，$y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$，置 $x=f(y)$。若 $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 为域且 $\mathcal H(x)$ 的赋值非平凡，则 $\mathcal O_{Y,y}\to\mathcal O_{Y_x,y}$ 忠实平坦。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Induct on an upper bound $n$ for the Krull dimension of a strict fibre algebra of an ordinary affinoid representative of the source germ. Such a representative exists by Proposition~\ref{fnd:innerstrictfibrerefinement}. For a representative of fibre dimension $d\leq n$, Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfibrefiniteprojection} gives, on ordinary neighborhoods, a finite map to an ordinary affinoid neighborhood $V$ of a point $t$ in $\mathbb A_X^d$. Separate the finite branches and retain the one through $y$, so that $y$ is the only point above $t$. Put}
{对源芽的普通 affinoid 代表之 strict 纤维代数的 Krull 维数上界 $n$ 作归纳。这样的代表由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:innerstrictfibrerefinement} 给出。若某代表的纤维维数为 $d\leq n$，则 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfibrefiniteprojection} 在普通邻域上给出有限映射，目标为 $\mathbb A_X^d$ 中一点 $t$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域 $V$。分离有限分支并保留经过 $y$ 的分支，使 $y$ 成为 $t$ 上方的唯一点。记}
\[
 R_0=\mathcal O_{V,t},\quad T_0=\mathcal O_{V_x,t},\quad
 S=\mathcal O_{Y,y},\quad T=\mathcal O_{Y_x,y}.
\]
\bi{The finite direct-image stalk formulas give $S$ finite over $R_0$ and $T=S\otimes_{R_0}T_0$. This is an ordinary tensor identity: take finite affinoid base change, where completed and ordinary tensor products agree, and then take ordinary stalks. The unique point over $t$ means that the two finite stalk products each have one factor. Therefore flatness of $R_0\to T_0$ suffices.}
{有限直像的茎公式给出 $S$ 在 $R_0$ 上有限，且 $T=S\otimes_{R_0}T_0$。这是普通张量积恒等式：先作有限 affinoid 基变换，此时完备张量积与普通张量积一致，再取普通茎。$t$ 上方点的唯一性使两个有限茎乘积都只有一个因子。因此只须证明 $R_0\to T_0$ 平坦。}

\bi{The map from the ordinary interior of $V$ to $X$ is smooth. Since the base ordinary ring is a field, \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:smoothordinarynormalascent]{smooth normality ascent}} makes $R_0$ a normal local domain. If it is a field, its map to $T_0$ is flat. This includes $n=0$, for then $d=0$ and $R_0=\mathcal O_{X,x}$. Otherwise every power $\mathfrak m^q$ of its maximal ideal is nonzero. Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalclosedfiberdimensiondrop} represents the closed germ defined by $\mathfrak m^q$ by an inner affinoid with strict fibre dimension at most $d-1\leq n-1$. Induction gives flatness of}
{从 $V$ 的普通内部到 $X$ 的映射光滑。因普通底环为域，\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:smoothordinarynormalascent]{光滑态射的正规性上升}}使 $R_0$ 成为正规局部整环。若它为域，则其到 $T_0$ 的映射平坦。这包括 $n=0$ 的情形：此时 $d=0$，且 $R_0=\mathcal O_{X,x}$。否则其极大理想的每个幂 $\mathfrak m^q$ 均非零。Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalclosedfiberdimensiondrop} 用一个内 affinoid 代表 $\mathfrak m^q$ 所定义的闭芽，其 strict 纤维维数至多为 $d-1\leq n-1$。归纳假设给出}
\[
                     R_0/\mathfrak m^q\longrightarrow
                                  T_0/\mathfrak m^qT_0
\]
\bi{for all $q\geq1$. Both rings $R_0,T_0$ are Noetherian and the extended ideal lies in the maximal ideal of $T_0$. The adic criterion \cite[Tag 0523]{StacksAlg} makes $T_0$ flat over $R_0$. Base change gives flatness of $S\to T$. This is a local map of nonzero local rings, so it is faithfully flat. The argument applies to every representative with strict fibre dimension at most $n$, completing the induction.}
{对每个 $q\geq1$ 都平坦。环 $R_0,T_0$ 均 Noetherian，且扩张后的理想位于 $T_0$ 的极大理想中。Adic 判据 \cite[Tag 0523]{StacksAlg} 说明 $T_0$ 在 $R_0$ 上平坦，基变换遂给出 $S\to T$ 平坦。该映射为非零局部环之间的局部同态，故忠实平坦。此论证适用于每个 strict 纤维维数至多为 $n$ 的代表，归纳完成。}
\end{proof}
