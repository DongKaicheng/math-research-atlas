Permitted computations upstairs

Remaining historical proof gap

The sealed ledger records a gap in the proof of the historical whole statement.

Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[Permitted computations upstairs]\label{f9be:descent}
Write $[\cY]=\sum_j m_j[Y_j]$ with reduced irreducible components
$Y_j$. For each $j$, let
$r_j:\widetilde Y_j\to Y_j$ be a supplied proper birational map
from an integral proper flat $R'$-scheme.
Use all actual pulled-back data and supports. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:weighted}
 J_R(\cX)=
 \sum_j m_j\,\frac{\lambda_L}{A}
       \deg_{k'}\bigl(I_{j,0}\cdots I_{j,d}
                                  [\widetilde Y_j]\bigr).
\end{equation}
There is no assertion here that the supplied modifications exist.

If the reduced generic components of $X_L$ are normal, each summand
may instead be computed on any computing model in the class of
\texttt{f9mi:independence} with those pulled-back metrics, sections
and generic supports. In particular, when $X_L$ is normal integral,
its model-independent value equals the downstairs value.

For two downstairs systems with the same $(X,L_i,s_i,S_i)$ in the
inherited model-independent class, equality of their pulled-back
root model norms at every point of $X_L^{\an}$ implies equality of
their downstairs supported values.
\end{corollary}
