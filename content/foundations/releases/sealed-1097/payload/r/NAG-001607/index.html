\begin{proposition}[\bi{Transport and saturation of charts}{Chart 的传递与饱和}]
\label{fnd:analyticatlassaturation}
\bi{If $U,V\in\tau$ and $W$ is an affinoid domain of $U$ contained in $V$, then its affinoid algebra has a canonical bounded $A_V$-algebra structure representing the same subset as an affinoid domain of $V$. These structures are transitive. Consequently the family $\bar\tau$ of affinoid domains in members of $\tau$ is a net with a unique extending affinoid atlas.}
{若 $U,V\in\tau$，而 $W$ 是 $U$ 的 affinoid 域且包含于 $V$，则其 affinoid 代数具有典范的有界 $A_V$-代数结构，并将同一子集表示为 $V$ 的 affinoid 域。这些结构具有传递性。因此，所有 $\tau$ 成员中的 affinoid 域组成的族 $\bar\tau$ 是一个 net，并具有唯一延拓原 atlas 的 affinoid atlas。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose finitely many $C_i\in\tau|_{U\cap V}$ whose intersections with $W$ cover $W$. This is possible by the quasi-net condition and compactness of $W$. Put $D_i=W\cap C_i$, using the intersection algebra inside $U$. Each $D_i$ is an affinoid domain of $W$ and of $C_i$, hence of $V$ by transitivity. The maps $A_V\to A_{C_i}\to A_{D_i}$ agree on $D_i\cap D_j$: cover this compact intersection by sets $W\cap E$ with $E\in\tau|_{C_i\cap C_j}$. On these sets the two maps agree by the original atlas functoriality. Affinoid Tate injectivity on the finite refinement proves their agreement on the intersection. Lemma~\ref{fnd:nettopologyboundedgluing} now gives $A_V\to A_W$, whose spectrum map is the inclusion of $W$.}
{由 quasi-net 条件及 $W$ 的紧性，可选择有限个 $C_i\in\tau|_{U\cap V}$，使 $W\cap C_i$ 覆盖 $W$。在 $U$ 中取交叠代数，记 $D_i=W\cap C_i$。每个 $D_i$ 是 $W$ 及 $C_i$ 的 affinoid 域，由传递性也是 $V$ 的 affinoid 域。各映射 $A_V\to A_{C_i}\to A_{D_i}$ 在 $D_i\cap D_j$ 上一致：用 $E\in\tau|_{C_i\cap C_j}$ 所给的 $W\cap E$ 覆盖该紧交叠，两个映射在这些集合上一致，原因是原 atlas 的函子性。在有限加细上使用 affinoid Tate 的单射性，即知它们在整个交叠上一致。于是 Lemma~\ref{fnd:nettopologyboundedgluing} 给出 $A_V\to A_W$，其谱映射就是 $W$ 的包含。}

\bi{We verify the domain property. Let $T$ be affinoid and let $T\to V$ have image in $W$. The inverse images $T_i$ of the domains $D_i\subseteq V$ form a finite affinoid cover of $T$. Each gives a map $T_i\to D_i\to W$. On $T_i\cap T_j$, refine by inverse images of the common atlas pieces $E\subseteq C_i\cap C_j$ used above. These pieces cover because the common point image lies in $D_i\cap D_j$. On every refined piece, both maps to $U$ are the map through $E\to U$ prescribed by the original atlas. They therefore agree, and their factorizations through the original affinoid domain $W\subseteq U$ agree by its universal property. Tate gluing yields a bounded map $T\to W$. Its composite with $W\to V$ is the given map, as can be checked on the $T_i$. Any other factorization restricts uniquely to $T_i\to D_i$, since $D_i$ is an affinoid domain both of $W$ and of $V$; hence it is equal to the constructed factorization.}
{核验域的泛性质。设 $T$ 为 affinoid，且映射 $T\to V$ 的像包含于 $W$。各域 $D_i\subseteq V$ 的逆像 $T_i$ 构成 $T$ 的有限 affinoid 覆盖，并给出映射 $T_i\to D_i\to W$。在 $T_i\cap T_j$ 上，进一步取上述公共 atlas 块 $E\subseteq C_i\cap C_j$ 的逆像。共同点像属于 $D_i\cap D_j$，故这些块覆盖交叠。在每个加细块上，两个到 $U$ 的映射均为原 atlas 指定的、经过 $E\to U$ 的映射，因此相等。再由原 affinoid 域 $W\subseteq U$ 的泛性质，它们到 $W$ 的分解相等。Tate 粘合给出有界映射 $T\to W$。其与 $W\to V$ 的复合在各 $T_i$ 上等于原映射，因此在 $T$ 上相等。由于 $D_i$ 同时是 $W,V$ 的 affinoid 域，任何其他分解限制到 $T_i$ 后，都必为唯一的映射 $T_i\to D_i$，所以与所构造分解相同。}

\bi{The same universal property proves independence of the finite cover and transitivity under inclusions. For the net assertion take $W\subseteq U$ and $W'\subseteq V$ in $\bar\tau$ and a point $x\in W\cap W'$. Choose finitely many $C_i\in\tau|_{U\cap V}$ containing $x$ whose union contains a relative neighborhood of $x$. The domains $W\cap W'\cap C_i$, formed inside $C_i$, belong to $\bar\tau$, contain $x$, and cover a neighborhood in $W\cap W'$. This proves the net condition. The established transport gives all inclusion maps in $\bar\tau$ and proves their uniqueness.}
{同一个泛性质证明结果与有限覆盖无关，并与包含关系的复合相容。对 net 条件，取 $\bar\tau$ 中的 $W\subseteq U$、$W'\subseteq V$ 及 $x\in W\cap W'$。在 $\tau|_{U\cap V}$ 中选取有限个均包含 $x$ 的 $C_i$，使其并包含 $x$ 的一个相对邻域。在 $C_i$ 中构造的域 $W\cap W'\cap C_i$ 都属于 $\bar\tau$，均包含 $x$，且覆盖 $x$ 在 $W\cap W'$ 中的一个邻域。这证明 net 条件。已经建立的传递性给出 $\bar\tau$ 中所有包含映射，并证明其唯一性。}
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[\bi{An affinoid atlas}{Affinoid atlas}]
\label{fnd:analyticnetatlas}
\bi{Let $X$ be a locally Hausdorff topological space. A net $\tau$ of compact Hausdorff subsets is a quasi-net such that $\tau|_{U\cap V}=\{W\in\tau:W\subseteq U\cap V\}$ is a quasi-net of $U\cap V$ for every $U,V\in\tau$. An affinoid atlas consists of such a net, affinoid algebras $A_V$, homeomorphisms $V\simeq\mathcal M(A_V)$, and bounded homomorphisms $A_V\to A_U$ for inclusions $U\subseteq V$ in $\tau$. The latter identify $U$ with an affinoid domain of $V$, and are compatible with composition.}
{设 $X$ 为局部 Hausdorff 拓扑空间。由紧 Hausdorff 子集组成的 net $\tau$ 是一个 quasi-net，且对任意 $U,V\in\tau$，族 $\tau|_{U\cap V}=\{W\in\tau:W\subseteq U\cap V\}$ 都是 $U\cap V$ 的 quasi-net。一个 affinoid atlas 由这样的 net、affinoid 代数 $A_V$、同胚 $V\simeq\mathcal M(A_V)$，以及对 $\tau$ 中包含关系 $U\subseteq V$ 给出的有界同态 $A_V\to A_U$ 组成。后者将 $U$ 识别为 $V$ 的 affinoid 域，并与复合相容。}
\end{definition}
