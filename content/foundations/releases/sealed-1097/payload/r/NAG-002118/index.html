Balancing for a trivially valued curve

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 896

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Balancing for a trivially valued curve}{平凡赋值曲线的平衡性}]
\label{cand:trivialcurvebalancing}
\bi{For every pure one-dimensional closed torus subscheme $C$ over a trivially valued field, its tropicalization with the geometric initial multiplicities is a balanced one-dimensional fan. In a pointed subdivision,}
{对平凡赋值域上每个纯一维环面闭子概形 $C$，其热带化配以几何初始重数后是平衡一维扇。在尖锥细分中，}
\[
 \sum_{\rho}m_C(\rho)u_\rho=0\quad\text{in }N,
\]
\bi{where $u_\rho$ is the primitive generator of the ray $\rho$.}
{其中 $u_\rho$ 是射线 $\rho$ 的本原生成元。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Since $v(k^\times)=0$, all hyperplanes in the finite construction of Theorem~\ref{cand:finitegroebnercomplex} pass through zero. Theorem~\ref{cand:tropicalpuredimension} therefore makes the tropicalization a finite pure one-dimensional fan. For every component $C_i$, only finitely many points of $\overline C_i$ have a nonzero vector $a_{i,p}$, because each of the finitely many coordinate rational functions has only finitely many zeros and poles. Every such vector lies in $\operatorname{Trop}(C_i)$: the absolute value $f\mapsto\exp(-\operatorname{ord}_p(f))$ on its function field is trivial on $k$ and, after completion, gives an analytic point of $C_i$ with that tropical vector. Its positive rescalings give the same ray.}
{由于 $v(k^\times)=0$，Theorem~\ref{cand:finitegroebnercomplex} 的有限构造中的全部超平面均经过零点。因此 Theorem~\ref{cand:tropicalpuredimension} 使热带化成为有限纯一维扇。对每个分支 $C_i$，仅有限个 $\overline C_i$ 上的点具有非零向量 $a_{i,p}$，因为有限个坐标有理函数各自仅有有限个零点及极点。每个非零向量均属于 $\operatorname{Trop}(C_i)$：函数域上的绝对值 $f\mapsto\exp(-\operatorname{ord}_p(f))$ 在 $k$ 上平凡，完备化后给出热带向量为 $a_{i,p}$ 的 $C_i$ 解析点。正倍缩放该赋值给出同一射线。}

\bi{Conversely, Proposition~\ref{cand:curveraymultiplicity} expresses the positive weight on each tropical ray as a sum over these points, so none is missing. For every character $m\in M$, the degree of its principal divisor on each proper integral curve is zero by \cite[Tag 02RU]{StacksAlg}:}
{反过来，Proposition~\ref{cand:curveraymultiplicity} 将每条热带射线上的正权重表示为这些点的总和，因此没有遗漏。对每个特征 $m\in M$，由 \cite[Tag 02RU]{StacksAlg}，其在每条适当整曲线上的主除子次数为零：}
\[
 \sum_p[\kappa(p):k]\langle m,a_{i,p}\rangle=0.
\]
\bi{Multiply by $\nu_i$, sum over $i$, and group the nonzero vectors by their primitive rays. Formula~\eqref{eq:candrayboundaryweight} turns the result into $\langle m,\sum_\rho m_C(\rho)u_\rho\rangle=0$. Since this holds for every $m$, it is the required lattice-vector equality.}
{乘以 $\nu_i$ 后对 $i$ 求和，再按本原射线对非零向量分组。由公式~\eqref{eq:candrayboundaryweight}，得到 $\langle m,\sum_\rho m_C(\rho)u_\rho\rangle=0$。此式对每个 $m$ 成立，因此就是所需格向量等式。}
\end{proof}
