Completely continuous restriction

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 295

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Completely continuous restriction}{完全连续的限制}]
\label{fnd:shrinkingcompactrestriction}
\bi{Let $A=K\{r^{-1}T\}/I$ and let $A\to B$ be a bounded homomorphism of generalized affinoid algebras. Suppose that the image $b_i$ of every $T_i$ has spectral radius strictly smaller than $r_i$. For a finite $A$-module $M$, the natural restriction map}
{设 $A=K\{r^{-1}T\}/I$，$A\to B$ 为广义 affinoid 代数的有界同态。假设每个 $T_i$ 的像 $b_i$ 的谱半径均严格小于 $r_i$。对有限 $A$-模 $M$，自然限制映射}
\[
 M\longrightarrow B\otimes_A M
\]
\bi{between the canonical Banach modules is completely continuous. This assertion holds also over a trivially valued field.}
{在两边的典范 Banach 范数下完全连续。该断言在平凡赋值基域上也成立。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose numbers $\rho_B(b_i)<\gamma_i<r_i$. By the spectral-radius formula, Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedspectralradiusformula}, there are constants $C_i$ with $\|b_i^d\|\le C_i\gamma_i^d$ for every $d\ge0$. Choose finite generators $m_1,\ldots,m_s$ of $M$, and write $N=B\otimes_A M$ and $n_j=1\otimes m_j$. The composite}
{选择 $\rho_B(b_i)<\gamma_i<r_i$。由谱半径公式 Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedspectralradiusformula}，存在常数 $C_i$，使所有 $d\ge0$ 都满足 $\|b_i^d\|\le C_i\gamma_i^d$。选取 $M$ 的有限生成元 $m_1,\ldots,m_s$，并记 $N=B\otimes_A M$、$n_j=1\otimes m_j$。复合映射}
\[
 K\{r^{-1}T\}^{\,s}\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow N
\]
\bi{sends $\sum_\nu a_{\nu j}T^\nu$ in component $j$ to $\sum_\nu a_{\nu j}b^\nu n_j$. Let $0<\theta=\max_i(\gamma_i/r_i)<1$, and put $C=(\prod_iC_i)\max_j\|n_j\|$, enlarged if necessary for the module-action bound. Terms with $|\nu|\ge d$ have norm at most $C\theta^d|a_{\nu j}|r^\nu$. Truncation to $|\nu|<d$ is a bounded finite-rank map, and its operator-norm error is at most $C\theta^d$. Hence this composite is completely continuous. The first arrow is norm-strict by the quotient presentation and Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}; its target is of countable type. Lemma~\ref{fnd:countablebanachsplitting} gives a bounded linear section. Composing with it proves the assertion for $M\to N$. If there are no Tate variables, the source module is finite dimensional over $K$ and the assertion is immediate.}
{将第 $j$ 个分量中的 $\sum_\nu a_{\nu j}T^\nu$ 送至 $\sum_\nu a_{\nu j}b^\nu n_j$。令 $0<\theta=\max_i(\gamma_i/r_i)<1$，并令 $C=(\prod_iC_i)\max_j\|n_j\|$，必要时扩大以包含模作用的有界常数。次数 $|\nu|\ge d$ 的项，其范数至多为 $C\theta^d|a_{\nu j}|r^\nu$。截断到 $|\nu|<d$ 给出有界有限秩映射，且算子范数误差至多为 $C\theta^d$，故这个复合完全连续。第一箭头由商表示及 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules} 为范数严格，其目标又为可数型。Lemma~\ref{fnd:countablebanachsplitting} 给出有界线性截面，与之复合，就证明 $M\to N$ 的断言。若没有 Tate 变量，则源模在 $K$ 上有限维，断言直接成立。}
\end{proof}
