Separating the small members of a standard family

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 197

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Separating the small members of a standard family}{分离标准族中的小元素}]\label{fnd:standardsmallscalarrefinement}
\bi{Assume the valuation of $K$ is nontrivial. Let $f_0,\ldots,f_r$ generate the unit ideal and fix $\sum_i a_if_i=1$. Choose $\kappa\in K^\times$ with}
{假设 $K$ 的赋值非平凡。设 $f_0,\ldots,f_r$ 生成单位理想，并固定 $\sum_i a_if_i=1$。选择 $\kappa\in K^\times$ 使}
\[
 \theta=|\kappa|\max_i\|a_i\|<1.
\]
\bi{Form the all-sign Laurent family for $f_i/\kappa$. For a branch $C$, let $P$ be the positive indices and $N$ the negative indices, and write its relations as}
{对 $f_i/\kappa$ 构造 all-sign Laurent 族。对某个分支 $C$，记正号指标集为 $P$，负号指标集为 $N$，并将其关系写为}
\[
 f_p=\kappa X_p\quad(p\in P),\qquad
 f_nX_n=\kappa\quad(n\in N),\qquad \|X_i\|\leq1.
\]
\bi{If $N$ is empty, $C$ is the actual zero quotient. If $i\in P$, the joint quotient $C\widehat\otimes_A R_i$ is zero. For $i\in N$, the element $f_i$ is a unit in $C$, and there is an isometric identification}
{若 $N$ 为空，则 $C$ 为实际零商。若 $i\in P$，则联合商 $C\widehat\otimes_A R_i$ 为零。若 $i\in N$，则 $f_i$ 在 $C$ 中为单位，且有等距识别}
\[
 C\widehat\otimes_A R_i
 \simeq C\{T_j:j\in N\setminus\{i\}\}/(T_j-f_j/f_i)_j.
\]
\bi{These identifications commute with all intersection maps. They persist after further elementary constraints on $C$. Consequently the restricted standard rational family is, after removing actual zero terms, the standard family generated by the units $(f_i)_{i\in N}$.}
{这些识别与全部交叠映射交换，并且在 $C$ 上再施加 elementary 约束之后仍成立。因此，删去实际零项后，限制所得的标准 rational 族正是由单位 $(f_i)_{i\in N}$ 生成的标准族。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{If every index is positive, the relation ideal contains}
{若每个指标都是正号，则关系理想包含}
\[
 1-\kappa\sum_i a_iX_i
      =\sum_i a_i(f_i-\kappa X_i).
\]
\bi{The subtracted element has norm at most $\theta<1$ in the coefficient algebra. Hence this displayed element is a unit by Lemma~\ref{fnd:geometricinverse}. The relation ideal is the whole coefficient algebra. This proves the actual zero assertion without using an empty-spectrum implication.}
{在系数代数中，被减去的元素的范数至多为 $\theta<1$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:geometricinverse}，所列元素为单位，故关系理想等于整个系数代数。这直接证明实际商为零，而不使用空谱蕴含零代数的推断。}

\bi{For $i\in P$, work in the coefficient algebra for the joint presentation with $R_i$, and again put $T_i=1$. Its relation ideal contains}
{若 $i\in P$，在与 $R_i$ 的联合表示所用的系数代数中工作，并仍约定 $T_i=1$。其关系理想包含}
\[
 1-\kappa X_i\sum_j a_jT_j
 =\sum_j a_j(f_j-f_iT_j)
      +(f_i-\kappa X_i)\sum_j a_jT_j.
\]
\bi{The norm of $\kappa X_i\sum_j a_jT_j$ is at most $\theta$. The same geometric inverse proves that this joint ideal is the whole algebra. In particular, every further intersection involving such a term is an actual zero quotient.}
{$\kappa X_i\sum_j a_jT_j$ 的范数至多为 $\theta$。同一个几何级数逆元论证表明，该联合理想等于整个代数。因此，每个包含这样一项的后续交叠也都是实际零商。}

\bi{If $i\in N$, then $f_i^{-1}=X_i/\kappa$ in $C$. Multiplying the rational relations by this unit replaces them by $T_j-f_j/f_i$ without changing the actual ideal. For $p\in P$ one has}
{若 $i\in N$，则 $C$ 中有 $f_i^{-1}=X_i/\kappa$。将 rational 关系乘以该单位，可将其替换为 $T_j-f_j/f_i$，实际理想不变。对 $p\in P$，有}
\[
 f_p/f_i=X_pX_i,\qquad \|X_pX_i\|\leq1.
\]
\bi{Thus all variables indexed by $P$ can be eliminated by the two contractive substitution maps of Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalredundantconstraint}. What remains is precisely the displayed presentation. The maps fix coefficients and send each surviving variable to the same ratio, so they commute with adding further index blocks. Their norm inequalities and the explicit unit equations survive all later contractive quotient maps. This proves the assertions about intersections and further constraints.}
{因此，由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalredundantconstraint} 中的两个 contractive 代入映射，可以消去全部由 $P$ 标记的变量。剩下的恰好是所列表示。这些映射固定系数，并将每个保留变量送到同一比值，所以与添入其他指标组相交换。范数不等式及显式单位等式在后续 contractive 商映射下保持成立。这就证明了交叠与进一步约束的断言。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{We work over a complete non-Archimedean field $K$, allowing trivial valuation except in the small-scalar lemmas where nontriviality is stated. Every nonzero algebra has a strict norm-admissible Banach norm, and every relation quotient has its actual quotient norm. The zero algebra is admitted separately with its zero norm. All radii in this section are one; a radius in $|K^\times|$ can first be reduced to this convention by scaling the corresponding numerator. These conventions concern the displayed presentations, not a spectral norm.}
{本节在完备非阿基米德域 $K$ 上工作，允许赋值平凡；使用小标量的引理会明确要求赋值非平凡。每个非零代数配备 strict norm-admissible Banach 范数，每个关系商配备实际商范数。零代数另以零范数允许在内。本节所有半径均为一；值群 $|K^\times|$ 中的半径可先通过缩放相应分子化为这一约定。这些约定涉及所列表示，并不使用 spectral 范数。}

\bi{For a tuple $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$ generating the unit ideal of $A$, write}
{对生成 $A$ 的单位理想的 tuple $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$，记}
\[
 A\langle f/g\rangle
   =A\{T_1,\ldots,T_s\}/(gT_j-f_j)_{1\leq j\leq s}.
\]
\bi{For a unit-ideal tuple $f_0,\ldots,f_r$, its \emph{standard rational family} consists of the $r+1$ presentations}
{对生成单位理想的 tuple $f_0,\ldots,f_r$，其\emph{标准 rational 族}由以下 $r+1$ 个表示组成：}
\[
 R_i=A\{T_j:j\ne i\}/(f_iT_j-f_j)_{j\ne i},
 \qquad 0\leq i\leq r.
\]
\bi{We put $T_i=1$ when using a formula involving all indices. The joint presentation of a finite family means the quotient with disjoint variable blocks and all their relations. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpresentationpushout} identifies it with the separated completed tensor product. Its module attached to a finite $A$-module $M$ is its ordinary tensor with $M$, equipped with the quotient norm from a fixed finite free presentation of $M$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} gives its comparison with the completed tensor. These choices specify the arrows and norms before a geometric sheaf is available.}
{若某个公式涉及全部指标，约定 $T_i=1$。有限族的联合表示，是指添入互不相交的变量组并施加全部关系后所得的商。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpresentationpushout} 将它与 separated completed tensor product 识别。对有限 $A$-模 $M$，相应模是该商与 $M$ 的普通张量积，并配备来自 $M$ 的一个固定有限自由表示的商范数。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} 给出它与 completed tensor 的比较。因此，在尚未构造几何层之前，所有箭头及范数已有明确含义。}
