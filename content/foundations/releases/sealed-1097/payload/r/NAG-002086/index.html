\begin{proposition}[\bi{Properness of toric tropicalization}{环面热带化的拓扑适当性}]
\label{cand:tropicalpropermap}
\bi{The map $\operatorname{trop}:X^{\an}\to\mathbf R^n$ is continuous and topologically proper. Its image is closed.}
{映射 $\operatorname{trop}:X^{\an}\to\mathbf R^n$ 连续且拓扑适当，其像闭。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Continuity follows from evaluation on the invertible characters. The inverse image of a closed box is the closed analytic subspace defined by $I$ in the corresponding affinoid polyannulus; Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact} gives its compactness. For a compact subset of $\mathbf R^n$, choose a box containing it; its inverse image is closed in that compact polyannulus, hence compact. Closedness of the image also follows directly from Theorem~\ref{cand:tropicalfundamental}: its complement is the union, over $f\in I$, of the open loci where one term of $f$ is uniquely active.}
{连续性来自对可逆特征的取值。闭盒子的逆像是相应 affinoid 多重圆环中由 $I$ 定义的闭解析子空间；由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact}，它是紧的。对 $\mathbf R^n$ 的紧子集，选择包含它的盒子；其逆像在该紧多重圆环中闭，故紧。像的闭性也直接由 Theorem~\ref{cand:tropicalfundamental} 得出：其补集是对 $f\in I$，某项唯一活跃的开集之并。}
\end{proof}

\section{\bi{Initial ideals and analytic tropical fibres}{初始理想与解析热带纤维}}
\label{cand:initialfibressection}

\bi{Let $K$ be a complete non-Archimedean field, put $v=-\log|\cdot|$, and write $M=\mathbf Z^n$. For $w\in\mathbf R^n$ and $f=\sum_m a_m\chi^m\in K[M]$, put}
{设 $K$ 为完备非阿基米德域，记 $v=-\log|\cdot|$、$M=\mathbf Z^n$。对 $w\in\mathbf R^n$ 及 $f=\sum_m a_m\chi^m\in K[M]$，置}
\[
 \|f\|_w=\max_m |a_m|e^{-\langle m,w\rangle},
 \qquad
 \mu_w(f)=\min_{a_m\ne0}\bigl(v(a_m)+\langle m,w\rangle\bigr).
\]
\bi{A term is active if it attains this maximum, or equivalently this minimum. Denote by $\mathcal T_w$ the completion of $K[M]$ for $\|\cdot\|_w$. The multilaurent presentation of Theorem~\ref{fnd:multilaurentactualpresentation} identifies it, as a normed algebra, with}
{达到上述最大值（等价地，最小值）的项称为活跃项。记 $\mathcal T_w$ 为 $K[M]$ 关于 $\|\cdot\|_w$ 的完备化。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:multilaurentactualpresentation} 的多重 Laurent 表示，它作为赋范代数等于}
\[
 K\{r_1^{-1}T_1,r_1S_1,\ldots,r_n^{-1}T_n,r_nS_n\}/(T_iS_i-1)_i,
 \qquad r_i=e^{-w_i}.
\]
