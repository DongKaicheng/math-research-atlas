Actual quotients by arbitrary ideals

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 111

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Actual quotients by arbitrary ideals}{任意理想的实际商}]\label{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients}
\bi{Every algebraic ideal $J$ of a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra $A$ is finitely generated and closed. The actual quotient $A/J$, with its quotient norm, is again a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra or the zero algebra. The quotient map is norm-strict.}
{Generalized $K$-affinoid 代数 $A$ 的每个代数理想 $J$ 都有限生成且闭。实际商 $A/J$ 配备商范数后仍为 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数或零代数，且商映射为 norm-strict。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Closedness and finite generation follow from Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}; completeness and controlled quotient lifts follow from Lemma~\ref{fnd:completequotient}. Choose a norm-admissible presentation $D=K\{t^{-1}X\}\twoheadrightarrow A$. The inverse image $J'$ of $J$ is closed, and the algebraic quotient is $D/J'$. Successive quotient infima are the single infimum over all lifts in $D$. Consequently the quotient norm on $A/J$ is uniformly equivalent to the quotient norm from $D$: inequalities comparing the two norms on $A$ remain valid after taking their quotient infima. This is the required admissible affinoid presentation.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}，$J$ 闭且有限生成；由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:completequotient}，商完备且有受控提升。选择范数可容许表示 $D=K\{t^{-1}X\}\twoheadrightarrow A$。$J$ 的逆像 $J'$ 闭，代数商为 $D/J'$。逐次取商的下确界等于对 $D$ 中全部提升取一次下确界。因此 $A/J$ 的商范数与来自 $D$ 的商范数一致等价：比较 $A$ 上两个范数的不等式，在分别取商下确界后仍然成立。这就是所需可容许 affinoid 表示。}
\end{proof}
