Cutting out a fibre component over the original field

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 460

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Cutting out a fibre component over the original field}{在原基域上切出纤维分量}]
\label{fnd:originalfiberidempotentcut}
\bi{Let $Y=\mathcal M(B)\to X=\mathcal M(A)$ be an affinoid morphism, let $x\in X$, and set $F=\mathcal H(x)$ and $C=B\widehat\otimes_A F$. If $e\in C$ is idempotent and $y\in Y_x$ satisfies $e(y)=1$, then, after replacing $X$ by an ordinary affinoid neighborhood of $x$, a Weierstrass domain $W$ of the resulting $Y$ is an ordinary neighborhood of $y$ and satisfies $W_x=\mathcal M(eC)$. In particular a strict fibre remains strict after this operation.}
{设 $Y=\mathcal M(B)\to X=\mathcal M(A)$ 为 affinoid 态射，$x\in X$，并令 $F=\mathcal H(x)$、$C=B\widehat\otimes_A F$。若 $e\in C$ 为幂等元，且 $y\in Y_x$ 满足 $e(y)=1$，则将 $X$ 换为 $x$ 的某个普通 affinoid 邻域后，所得 $Y$ 的一个 Weierstrass 域 $W$ 是 $y$ 的普通邻域，并满足 $W_x=\mathcal M(eC)$。特别地，此操作保持纤维的 strict 性。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose $0<\delta<\varepsilon<1$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:originalcoefficientfibredensity} supplies, after the stated base restriction, a function $b$ on $Y$ whose image in $C$ differs from $e$ by norm less than $\delta$. Define $W$ by $|b-1|\leq\varepsilon$. At $y$ the inequality is strict, so $W$ is an ordinary neighborhood there. On $\mathcal M(eC)$ one has $|b-1|<\delta$, whereas on $\mathcal M((1-e)C)$ one has $|b-1|=1$. Thus its fibre has exactly the first factor as underlying domain. Its domain algebra is also that factor: on $eC$ the Weierstrass condition is automatic by the norm bound, while on $(1-e)C$ the element $b-1$ has inverse of spectral norm one, so the condition $|b-1|\leq\varepsilon<1$ gives the zero algebra. Equivalently, the affinoid-domain universal property identifies $W_x$ with the open-and-closed affinoid factor $\mathcal M(eC)$. This factor is a quotient of $C$, so it is strict when $C$ is strict.}
{选择 $0<\delta<\varepsilon<1$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:originalcoefficientfibredensity}，作所述底限制后，存在 $Y$ 上的函数 $b$，其在 $C$ 中的像与 $e$ 之差的范数小于 $\delta$。以 $|b-1|\leq\varepsilon$ 定义 $W$。在 $y$ 处不等式严格，故 $W$ 是该点的普通邻域。在 $\mathcal M(eC)$ 上有 $|b-1|<\delta$，而在 $\mathcal M((1-e)C)$ 上有 $|b-1|=1$，故纤维的底层域恰为第一个因子。其域代数也是该因子：在 $eC$ 上，范数界使 Weierstrass 条件自动成立；在 $(1-e)C$ 上，$b-1$ 的逆的谱范数为一，因此条件 $|b-1|\leq\varepsilon<1$ 给出零代数。等价地，由 affinoid 域泛性质，$W_x$ 就是既开又闭的 affinoid 因子 $\mathcal M(eC)$。此因子为 $C$ 的商，所以若 $C$ strict，则它也 strict。}
\end{proof}
