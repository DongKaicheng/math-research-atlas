Generalized Gerritzen--Grauert

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 366

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Generalized Gerritzen--Grauert}{广义 Gerritzen--Grauert 定理}]
\label{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert}
\bi{Every affinoid domain $U\subseteq\mathcal M_K(A)$ has a finite covering by rational domains defined over the original $A$, with positive real radii. If $B$ represents $U$, each member $R_i$ and each finite intersection $R$ of these members is rational over $B$ with algebra canonically boundedly isomorphic to $A_R$. In particular}
{每个 affinoid 域 $U\subseteq\mathcal M_K(A)$ 都可由定义在原 $A$ 上、允许正实半径的有限个 rational 域覆盖。若 $B$ 表示 $U$，则各成员 $R_i$ 及这些成员的每个有限交 $R$ 都在 $B$ 上 rational，其代数与 $A_R$ 典范有界同构。特别地，}
\[
 B\widehat\otimes_A A_R\simeq A_R,
\]
\bi{compatibly with all restriction maps. The empty domain admits the empty covering and has algebra zero.}
{并且这些同构与全部限制映射相容。空域采用空覆盖，其代数为零。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{If $U$ is empty, the point identification and Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion} give $B=0$. Otherwise choose the simultaneous free strictification of Proposition~\ref{fnd:freedomainstrictification}. Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis}, over $L=K_r$, gives a finite covering $U_r=\bigcup_j R'_j$, where the $R'_j$ are rational in $\mathcal M_L(A_r)$. By that proposition and Lemma~\ref{fnd:gaussrationalfinitecover},}
{若 $U$ 为空，则由谱点识别及 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion} 得 $B=0$。否则，采用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:freedomainstrictification} 的同时自由 strictification。在 $L=K_r$ 上应用 Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis}，得到有限覆盖 $U_r=\bigcup_jR'_j$，其中 $R'_j$ 在 $\mathcal M_L(A_r)$ 中 rational。由该 proposition 及 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gaussrationalfinitecover}，有}
\[
 U=\sigma_{r,A}^{-1}(U_r)
   =\bigcup_j\sigma_{r,A}^{-1}(R'_j),
\]
\bi{and each inverse image is a finite union of original-$A$ rational domains. Their combined family is the required finite covering. Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainrestriction} supplies all algebra identifications, their bounded inverses and their compatibility on intersections.}
{且每个逆像都是有限个原 $A$-rational 域的并。将这些有限族合并，就得到所需覆盖。Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainrestriction} 给出全部代数识别、有界逆及其在交上的相容性。}
\end{proof}
