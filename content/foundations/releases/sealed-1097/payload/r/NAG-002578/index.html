Minimal evaluation kernels on the relevant support

Chapter 8 supplement: Interior Stokes with coherent coefficients

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1788

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[Minimal evaluation kernels on the relevant support]
\label{f7gg:minimal}
Let \(V=\mathcal M(A)\) be pure of analytic dimension \(d\geq1\),
let \(x\in\Int(V/K)\), and let \(t_f^*\alpha\) have bidegree
\((d-1,d)\). If its ordinary germ at \(x\) is nonzero, then
\[
 s_f(x)=d,\qquad \ker(x)\in\Min(A),
\]
where \(s_f(x)\) is the graded transcendence degree of the field
generated by the initial forms of the \(f_i(x)\).
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The interior-only theorem \src{NAG-002455},
\src{v038:pointed:fullstabilization}, gives the germ of the
actual moment image as a pure \(s_f(x)\)-dimensional fan.
After shrinking an ordinary affinoid neighborhood into a
small inverse moment ball, its entire image has this dimension.
If \(s_f(x)<d\), all tangential restrictions of the second
alternating degree \(d\) vanish, so the ordinary germ is zero.
The global finite-image dimension bound
\src{v037:globaltrop:finiteimage} gives the reverse bound
\(s_f(x)\leq d\). Thus \(s_f(x)=d\).

Put \(\mathfrak q=\ker(x)\).
The algebra \(A\) is Noetherian by
\src{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}, so it has finitely
many minimal primes, say \(\mathfrak p_1,\ldots,\mathfrak p_r\).
We use the elementary prime-existence and finiteness facts
\href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00E0}{Stacks 00E0(4)}
and \href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00FR}{Stacks 00FR}.
Suppose that \(\mathfrak q\) is none of the \(\mathfrak p_i\).
Then \(\mathfrak q\not\subseteq\mathfrak p_i\) for every \(i\);
otherwise minimality, using a minimal prime in \(\mathfrak q\),
would force equality.
Choose \(a_i\in\mathfrak q\setminus\mathfrak p_i\).
By incomparability of the minimal primes choose
\[
 c_i\in\bigcap_{j\ne i}\mathfrak p_j\setminus\mathfrak p_i
\]
as a product of elements of \(\mathfrak p_j\setminus\mathfrak p_i\);
use \(c_i=1\) when \(r=1\).
Then \(a=\sum_i a_ic_i\) belongs to \(\mathfrak q\) and to
none of the minimal primes: modulo \(\mathfrak p_i\), only
the nonzero product \(a_ic_i\) remains.

The actual quotient \(Z=\mathcal M(A/(a))\) contains \(x\) and
has analytic dimension at most \(d-1\), by
\src{v037:degree:exceptionalimage}.
The finite closed immersion \(Z\to V\) is everywhere relatively
interior, and composition gives \(x\in\Int(Z/K)\).
Here we use \src{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras} and
\src{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus}.
The completed residue field at this point of \(Z\), and all
initial forms of the restricted \(f_i\), are unchanged.
The same interior pointed-fan theorem, which permits nonpure
\(Z\), therefore gives a pointed image dimension \(s_f(x)=d\)
on \(Z\). This contradicts the dimension bound for \(t_f(Z)\)
in the exceptional-image theorem. Hence \(\mathfrak q\) is
a minimal prime.
\end{proof}
