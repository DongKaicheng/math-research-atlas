Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A zero tropical support forces dimension zero}{零热带支集迫使维数为零}]
\label{cand:zerotropicaldimension}
\bi{Let $Z$ be a nonempty closed subscheme of a torus over a complete field $k$. If $\operatorname{Trop}(Z)\subseteq\{0\}$, then $Z$ is zero-dimensional and its coordinate algebra is finite over $k$.}
{设 $Z$ 为完备域 $k$ 上环面的非空闭子概形。若 $\operatorname{Trop}(Z)\subseteq\{0\}$，则 $Z$ 零维，且其坐标代数在 $k$ 上有限。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Suppose an integral component has positive dimension, with function field $F$. By \cite[Tag 00P0]{StacksAlg}, $F/k$ has positive transcendence degree. At least one of its torus-coordinate generators, say $z_1$, is transcendental over $k$. Extend $z_1$ to a maximal algebraically independent subset $z_1,\ldots,z_e$ of a finite field-generating set. Then $F$ is finite over $k(z_1,\ldots,z_e)$. Give the latter field its Gauss absolute value with $|z_1|\ne1$ and arbitrary positive radii on the other variables, and complete it to $E$. The Gauss norm is multiplicative by Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm}.}
{假设某个整分支具有正维数，函数域记为 $F$。由 \cite[Tag 00P0]{StacksAlg}，$F/k$ 的超越次数为正。其环面坐标生成元中至少一个，例如 $z_1$，在 $k$ 上超越。从一个有限域生成集中，将 $z_1$ 扩充为极大代数无关子集 $z_1,\ldots,z_e$；于是 $F$ 在 $k(z_1,\ldots,z_e)$ 上有限。赋予后者满足 $|z_1|\ne1$、其余变量具有任意正半径的 Gauss 绝对值，并将其完备化为 $E$。Gauss 范数由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm} 具有乘法性。}

\bi{The finite nonzero $E$-algebra $D=F\otimes_{k(z_1,\ldots,z_e)}E$ has a maximal proper ideal: choose one of maximal $E$-dimension. Its quotient is a finite field extension $E'/E$, which has the extending complete absolute value by Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}. The induced map $F\to E'$ is injective, since it is a unital map from a field. Restricting its norm to the coordinate algebra gives an analytic point of $Z$ by Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineanalytificationpoints}, with $|z_1|\ne1$. This contradicts the tropical-support hypothesis. All components therefore have dimension zero. Apply Lemma~\ref{cand:finiteprimefiltration} to obtain finiteness over $k$.}
{有限非零 $E$-代数 $D=F\otimes_{k(z_1,\ldots,z_e)}E$ 有极大真理想：选择 $E$-维数最大的真理想即可。其商是有限扩域 $E'/E$，由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 具有延拓的完备绝对值。诱导映射 $F\to E'$ 为域的保单位映射，故单射。将此范数限制到坐标代数，由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineanalytificationpoints} 得到 $Z$ 的解析点，且 $|z_1|\ne1$，与热带支集假设矛盾。因此所有分支均零维，再由 Lemma~\ref{cand:finiteprimefiltration} 得到在 $k$ 上的有限性。}
\end{proof}
