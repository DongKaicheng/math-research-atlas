Componentwise strict dimension; F8ZP-E

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1814

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Componentwise strict dimension; F8ZP-E]
\label{prop:strictdimension}
Let $A$ be a nonzero strict $K$-affinoid algebra pure of dimension $n$.
For primes $\mathfrak p\in\Min(A)$ and $\mathfrak q\supseteq\mathfrak p$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:dimension}
 \htp_{A/\mathfrak p}(\mathfrak q/\mathfrak p)
       +\dim(A/\mathfrak q)=n.
\end{equation}
In particular, for each $\mathfrak q\in\Spec(A)$ the ordinary algebraic
localization $A_{\mathfrak q}$ is equidimensional, of dimension
$n-\dim(A/\mathfrak q)$. This assertion concerns $A_{\mathfrak q}$,
not an identification with an ordinary analytic stalk.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Choose a maximal ideal $\mathfrak m\supseteq\mathfrak q$. Both
$A/\mathfrak p$ and $A/\mathfrak q$ are strict integral affinoids.
Apply the \hyperref[f8:strict-purity-dimensions]{prescribed maximal-dimension calculation} to these two rings separately. With
$d=\dim(A/\mathfrak q)$ this gives
\[
 \dim(A/\mathfrak p)_{\mathfrak m/\mathfrak p}=n,
 \qquad
 \dim(A/\mathfrak q)_{\mathfrak m/\mathfrak q}=d.
\]
The first equality uses the dimension of the particular component
$A/\mathfrak p$, not merely $\dim A_{\mathfrak m}=n$.
The integral local ring $R=(A/\mathfrak p)_{\mathfrak m/\mathfrak p}$
is catenary by the \hyperref[f8:strict-purity-algebra]{strict quotient calculation}. Write
$\mathfrak r=(\mathfrak q/\mathfrak p)R$ and let $\mathfrak n$ be its
maximal ideal. A saturated chain from $0$ to $\mathfrak r$, followed by
one from $\mathfrak r$ to $\mathfrak n$, is saturated from $0$ to
$\mathfrak n$. Finite dimension permits such chains. Catenarity makes
their lengths respectively $h$, $d$, and $n$, where $h$ is the height
of $\mathfrak r$. Thus $h+d=n$.

Localization at $\mathfrak m$ does not remove any prime contained in
$\mathfrak q$, so $h=\htp_{A/\mathfrak p}(\mathfrak q/\mathfrak p)$.
This proves \eqref{eq:dimension}. The minimal primes of
$A_{\mathfrak q}$ are exactly $\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak q}$ for
$\mathfrak p\in\Min(A)$ contained in $\mathfrak q$: use prime
correspondence for localization and choose a minimal prime below a
contracted prime. Each corresponding component localization has
dimension $h=n-d$, independently of $\mathfrak p$. Their prime-chain
spectra cover the spectrum of $A_{\mathfrak q}$, proving the last
assertion. Equidimensionality has therefore been derived at the precise
algebraic localizations used, rather than assumed for analytic stalks.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
The dimension of a strict affinoid is the Krull dimension of its
algebra by Definition~\ref{fnd:analyticdimension}. Purity here
means that every actual minimal-prime quotient has this dimension
equal to the given \(n\).
