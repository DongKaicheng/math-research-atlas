Equal differential ranks and regularity

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 341

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Equal differential ranks and regularity}{相等微分秩与正则性}]
\label{fnd:equaldifferentialrankregular}
\bi{Let $S\to B$ be a ring map with $B$ Noetherian and regular, and suppose that $\Omega_{B/S}$ is free of rank $N$. Let}
{设 $S\to B$ 为环映射，$B$ Noetherian 且正则，并假设 $\Omega_{B/S}$ 自由且秩为 $N$。设}
\[
 C=B[U_1,\ldots,U_m]/(P_1,\ldots,P_m)
\]
\bi{is finite over $B$, with the displayed triangular monic presentation. If $\Omega_{C/S}$ is free of rank $N$, then $C$ is regular. The same conclusion holds for each direct factor of $C$.}
{在 $B$ 上有限，且具有所列三角首一表示。若 $\Omega_{C/S}$ 自由且秩为 $N$，则 $C$ 正则；其每个直因子也正则。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The differential presentation is}
{微分模具有表示}
\[
 C^m\xrightarrow{(dP_j)} C^{N+m}\longrightarrow
                         \Omega_{C/S}\longrightarrow0.
\]
\bi{The last module is free of rank $N$, so its kernel is projective of constant rank $m$. The first map surjects onto this kernel. After localization at any prime of $C$, a surjection between free modules of rank $m$ is an isomorphism: reduce modulo the maximal ideal, then its determinant is a unit. Hence the $dP_j$ are independent modulo every such prime. Let $\mathfrak q$ be its inverse image in $B[U]$. This polynomial ring is regular by \cite[Tag 07NF]{StacksAlg}, so its localization $R=B[U]_{\mathfrak q}$ is regular local. A relation $\sum c_jP_j\in\mathfrak q^2R$ would, on differentiating and reducing modulo $\mathfrak q$, imply $\sum\overline c_j\,dP_j=0$. Therefore the classes of the $P_j$ are independent in $\mathfrak qR/(\mathfrak qR)^2$. Extend them to a minimal set of generators; \cite[Tag 00NQ]{StacksAlg} shows that $R/(P_1,\ldots,P_m)$ is regular local. These are all the local rings of $C$, proving regularity. Local rings of a direct factor are among them.}
{末项自由且秩为 $N$，故其核为常秩 $m$ 的投射模；第一箭头满射到该核。在 $C$ 的任意素理想处局部化后，秩均为 $m$ 的自由模之间的满射必为同构：模极大理想后取行列式，即知其为单位。因此，$dP_j$ 模每个此类素理想均线性无关。令 $\mathfrak q$ 为该素理想在 $B[U]$ 中的逆像。由 \cite[Tag 07NF]{StacksAlg}，这个多项式环正则，所以 $R=B[U]_{\mathfrak q}$ 为正则局部环。若有关系 $\sum c_jP_j\in\mathfrak q^2R$，求微分并模去 $\mathfrak q$，会得到 $\sum\overline c_j\,dP_j=0$。因此 $P_j$ 在 $\mathfrak qR/(\mathfrak qR)^2$ 中的类线性无关；将它们补成极大理想的极小生成组，\cite[Tag 00NQ]{StacksAlg} 给出 $R/(P_1,\ldots,P_m)$ 正则。这些正是 $C$ 的全部局部环，故 $C$ 正则；直因子的局部环也在其中。}
\end{proof}
