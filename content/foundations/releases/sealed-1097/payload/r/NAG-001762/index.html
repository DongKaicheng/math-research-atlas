Strict ordinary neighborhoods

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 330

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Strict ordinary neighborhoods}{Strict 的普通邻域}]
\label{fnd:strictordinaryneighborhoods}
\bi{Suppose the valuation of $K$ is nontrivial and $X=\mathcal M(A)$ is strict. Every point of $X$ has a basis of ordinary neighborhoods consisting of strict affinoid domains. Consequently, in Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization}, when $X$ is strict, the neighborhood algebra and the finite algebra may both be chosen strict.}
{假设 $K$ 的赋值非平凡，且 $X=\mathcal M(A)$ 为 strict。则 $X$ 的每个点都有由 strict affinoid 域组成的普通邻域基。因此，当 $X$ 为 strict 时，Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization} 中的邻域代数及有限代数都可选为 strict。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The topology of the spectrum is generated by evaluation inequalities. A prescribed open neighborhood of $x$ therefore contains a finite intersection of conditions $|f_i|<a_i$ and $|g_j|>b_j$ holding at $x$. Since the valuation is nontrivial, $\sqrt{|K^\times|}$ is dense in $\mathbb R_{>0}$: the rational powers of any element of absolute value different from one already have logarithms dense in $\mathbb R$. Choose $r_i,s_j$ in this group with}
{谱的拓扑由取值不等式生成。因此，$x$ 的给定开邻域包含一个有限交，由在 $x$ 成立的条件 $|f_i|<a_i$ 及 $|g_j|>b_j$ 定义。由于赋值非平凡，$\sqrt{|K^\times|}$ 在 $\mathbb R_{>0}$ 中稠密：任一绝对值不等于一的元素的有理次幂（允许负指数），其对数在 $\mathbb R$ 中稠密。可在该群中选择 $r_i,s_j$，满足}
\[
 |f_i(x)|<r_i<a_i,\qquad b_j<s_j<|g_j(x)|.
\]
\bi{The domain defined by $|f_i|\le r_i$ and $|g_j|\ge s_j$ is an ordinary neighborhood of $x$ contained in the prescribed open set. Write $r_i^{m_i}=|c_i|$ and $s_j^{n_j}=|d_j|$, with nonzero scalars $c_i,d_j\in K$. The inequalities are equivalent to}
{由 $|f_i|\le r_i$ 及 $|g_j|\ge s_j$ 定义的域是 $x$ 的普通邻域，并包含于给定开集。写 $r_i^{m_i}=|c_i|$、$s_j^{n_j}=|d_j|$，其中 $c_i,d_j\in K$ 非零；这些不等式等价于}
\[
       |c_i^{-1}f_i^{m_i}|\le1,\qquad
       |d_j^{-1}g_j^{n_j}|\ge1 .
\]
\bi{They give a finite succession of unit-radius Weierstrass and Laurent domains, hence a strict affinoid domain by the established presentation and intersection theorems. The finite list of germs in the proof of Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization} can thus be represented on such a strict neighborhood. Its finite algebra is strict by Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebras}; taking the quotient by the kernel used there preserves strictness.}
{它们给出有限次单位半径的 Weierstrass 与 Laurent 域构造，由已有的表示及交集定理得到 strict affinoid 域。因而 Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization} 证明中的有限组芽可在这样的 strict 邻域上表示。所得有限代数由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebras} 为 strict；最后除去该证明中使用的核仍保持 strict。}
\end{proof}
