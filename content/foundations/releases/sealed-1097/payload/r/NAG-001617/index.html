Points and fibres on general analytic spaces

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 392

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Points and fibres on general analytic spaces}{一般解析空间上的点与纤维}]
\label{fnd:generalanalyticfibres}
\bi{For a point $x$ of an analytic space, the completed residue fields computed in affinoid domains containing $x$ identify canonically and isometrically with a field $\mathcal H(x)$. For a morphism $f:Y\to X$, the fibre at $x$ is a canonical $\mathcal H(x)$-analytic space $Y_x$. On affinoid domains $V\subseteq Y$, $U\subseteq X$ with $f(V)\subseteq U$ and $x\in U$, it has the affinoid chart}
{对解析空间中的一点 $x$，在包含 $x$ 的不同 affinoid 域中计算的完备剩余域，都典范且等距地识别为同一个域 $\mathcal H(x)$。对态射 $f:Y\to X$，点 $x$ 上的纤维是典范的 $\mathcal H(x)$-解析空间 $Y_x$。若 affinoid 域 $V\subseteq Y$、$U\subseteq X$ 满足 $f(V)\subseteq U$ 且 $x\in U$，则纤维具有下述 affinoid chart：}
\[
 V_x=\mathcal M\bigl(A_V\widehat\otimes_{A_U}\mathcal H(x)\bigr).
\]
\bi{The canonical map $Y_x\to Y$ identifies its underlying topological space with $f^{-1}(x)$. It preserves the completed residue field at every point.}
{典范映射 $Y_x\to Y$ 将其底层拓扑空间识别为 $f^{-1}(x)$，并保持每个点的完备剩余域。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Any two affinoid domains containing $x$ have a common affinoid domain containing $x$, by the net condition. Corollary~\ref{fnd:domainresiduefields} identifies the fields along each inclusion. The isomorphisms satisfy the cocycle condition because they agree on evaluations, so they define $F=\mathcal H(x)$. A morphism induces an isometric embedding of these fields, checked on any presenting affinoid chart.}
{由 net 条件，包含 $x$ 的任意两个 affinoid 域，都有一个包含 $x$ 的公共 affinoid 子域。Corollary~\ref{fnd:domainresiduefields} 沿每个包含识别其剩余域。这些同构在取值上映射相同，因此满足 cocycle 条件，定义出 $F=\mathcal H(x)$。一个态射诱导这些域的等距嵌入；这可以在任一表示态射的 affinoid chart 上检查。}

\bi{Evaluation on affinoid domains containing $x$ defines a bounded map over $K$ from the $F$-affinoid point $\mathcal M(F)$ to $X$. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension}, it corresponds to an $F$-analytic point map $i_x:\mathcal M(F)\to X_F$. Define}
{在包含 $x$ 的 affinoid 域上取值，给出从 $F$-affinoid 点 $\mathcal M(F)$ 到 $X$ 的 $K$ 上有界映射。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension}，它对应一个 $F$-解析点映射 $i_x:\mathcal M(F)\to X_F$。定义}
\[
 Y_x=Y_F\times_{X_F,i_x}\mathcal M(F).
\]
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticproducts} and the affinoid pushout identity give the displayed chart formula. Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres} identifies each $V_x$ homeomorphically with $V\cap f^{-1}(x)$ and preserves the completed residue fields. The charts $V_x$ form a quasi-net of $Y_x$, while the corresponding subsets of $f^{-1}(x)$ are the restriction of a presenting chart quasi-net of $Y$. These local homeomorphisms agree on overlaps. The topology criterion of Lemma~\ref{fnd:nettopologyboundedgluing}, applied on both sides, makes their glued bijection a homeomorphism. The field identifications glue by the same restriction compatibility.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticproducts} 及 affinoid 推出恒等式给出陈述中的 chart 公式。Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres} 将每个 $V_x$ 同胚地识别为 $V\cap f^{-1}(x)$，并保持完备剩余域。各 $V_x$ 构成 $Y_x$ 的 quasi-net，而 $f^{-1}(x)$ 中对应的子集族，是 $Y$ 的表示 chart quasi-net 的限制。这些局部同胚在交叠上一致。对两边使用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:nettopologyboundedgluing} 的拓扑判据，说明所得整体双射是同胚。剩余域识别也由同一个限制相容性粘合。}
\end{proof}
