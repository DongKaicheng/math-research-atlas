Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Rigid completions in relative analytification}{相对解析化中的 rigid 完备化}]
\label{fnd:relativerigidcompletioncomparison}
\bi{In the preceding construction, let $x\in Z^{\an}$ be rigid over $K$, let $\xi\in Z$ be its image, and let $V=\mathcal M(C)$ be any affinoid domain containing $x$, with $\mathfrak m=\ker_C(x)$. The natural maps identify all four complete local rings}
{在上述构造中，设 $x\in Z^{\an}$ 在 $K$ 上 rigid，$\xi\in Z$ 为其像，且 $V=\mathcal M(C)$ 为任意包含 $x$ 的 affinoid 域，令 $\mathfrak m=\ker_C(x)$。自然映射将以下四个完备局部环全部识别：}
\[
 \widehat{\mathcal O_{Z,\xi}},\qquad
 \widehat{\mathcal O_{Z^{\an},x}},\qquad
 \widehat{\mathcal O_{V,x}},\qquad
 \widehat{C_{\mathfrak m}}.
\]
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Assume first that $x$ is $K$-rational. For a presentation $A=K\{s^{-1}S\}/J$ and $R=A[T]/I$, translate the coordinates to their values at $x$. The finite Taylor calculation of Proposition~\ref{fnd:relativepolynomialtateflatness}, followed by the finite-ideal completion formula, identifies the first ring and the local completion of any radius piece containing $x$ with}
{先设 $x$ 为 $K$-有理点。取表示 $A=K\{s^{-1}S\}/J$、$R=A[T]/I$，并按坐标在 $x$ 的值平移。Proposition~\ref{fnd:relativepolynomialtateflatness} 的有限 Taylor 计算及有限理想完备化公式，将第一个环和每个包含 $x$ 的半径分片的局部完备化识别为}
\[
                         K[[S,T]]/(J,I).
\]
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalanalyticformalpresentation} gives the same ring for the ordinary analytic germ. Lemma~\ref{fnd:rigidpointdomaininterior} identifies the two ordinary germs, since $V$ contains an ordinary neighborhood of $x$. Applying the rational Taylor calculation to a presentation of $C$ identifies its algebraic local completion with its ordinary germ completion. This proves all four comparisons in the rational case.}
{Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalanalyticformalpresentation} 为通常解析芽给出相同的环。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rigidpointdomaininterior}，$V$ 包含 $x$ 的普通邻域，故两个通常芽相同。再对 $C$ 的一个表示应用有理 Taylor 计算，将其代数局部完备化识别为其通常芽完备化。因此有理情形中的四个比较全部成立。}

\bi{In general $E=\mathcal H(x)$ is finite over $K$. The residue fields of both $R$ and $C$ at the indicated ideals are $E$: their valued fraction fields are dense in $E$, while every $K$-subspace of the finite-dimensional complete field $E$ is closed. Choose a finite extension $L/K$ splitting $E/K$. All residue fields of $E\otimes_K L$ are $L$, so all points over $x$ are $L$-rational. Tensoring any of the four complete local rings with $L$ decomposes it into the product of the complete local rings at these points. For the ordinary analytic rings this is Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation}. For the algebraic rings, the same assertion follows by decomposing the finite algebra modulo the maximal ideal into its local Artinian factors and lifting the resulting idempotents through the complete adic tower; the geometric-series correction solves an approximate idempotent modulo each next power, and Tag 00MA identifies the completed finite algebra. In each factor the maximal-ideal and original-ideal topologies agree because the residue algebra is local Artinian.}
{一般地，$E=\mathcal H(x)$ 在 $K$ 上有限。$R$ 和 $C$ 在所指理想处的剩余域都等于 $E$：它们的赋值分式域在 $E$ 中稠密，而有限维完备域 $E$ 的每个 $K$-子空间都闭。选择分裂 $E/K$ 的有限扩域 $L/K$。$E\otimes_K L$ 的全部剩余域均为 $L$，故 $x$ 上方的全部点均为 $L$-有理点。将四个完备局部环中的任何一个与 $L$ 张量，都将它分解为这些点处完备局部环之积。通常解析环的情形即 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation}。代数环的情形则先把模极大理想的有限代数分解成局部 Artinian 因子，再沿完备进塔提升相应幂等元：几何级数修正在每个下一次幂模下解出近似幂等元，而 Tag 00MA 将所得环识别为有限代数的完备化。各因子中的极大理想拓扑与原理想拓扑相同，因为剩余代数局部且 Artinian。}

\bi{The maps in every factor are exactly the rational comparison maps already proved, since finite scalar extension commutes with the relative analytification and domain constructions. They are therefore isomorphisms. Tensoring with the finite field extension $L/K$ is faithfully flat; the kernels and cokernels of each original comparison map vanish after this tensor product and hence vanish. This descends all four canonical isomorphisms.}
{因有限扩标量与相对解析化及域的构造相容，每个因子中的映射恰为已证明的有理比较映射，所以均为同构。与有限扩域 $L/K$ 张量忠实平坦；原来每个比较映射的核与余核在此张量后均消失，故原本就消失。这便下降了四个典范同构。}
\end{proof}
