Subdomains of an arbitrary analytic germ

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 570

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Subdomains of an arbitrary analytic germ}
{任意解析芽的子域}]
\label{fnd:allgermdomainclassification}
\bi{For every analytic germ $(X,x)$, reduction is an
order-preserving bijection between its subdomain germs and
the nonempty quasi-compact open subspaces of its graded
birational reduction. This includes arbitrary positive
radii and trivially valued ground fields.}
{对每个解析芽 $(X,x)$，约化在其子域芽与其分次 birational
约化的非空拟紧开子空间之间给出保持包含关系的双射。
该结论包括任意正半径及平凡赋值基域。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Take a finite affinoid subdomain-germ cover $(X_i,x)$.
For a quasi-compact open $W$ in the reduction, the intersections
$W\cap\mathscr R(X_i,x)$ are quasi-compact by
quasi-separatedness. Theorem~\ref{fnd:goodgermdomainclassification}
lifts them to subdomain germs $Y_i\subset X_i$.
On overlaps the two lifts coincide, because their reductions
coincide and the good-germ theorem reflects equality on every
affinoid piece of the overlap. Hence their union is a subdomain
germ of $X$ with reduction $W$. This proves surjectivity.
If two subdomain germs have equal reductions, intersect with
each $X_i$ and use the good-germ theorem to obtain equality
there. The pieces cover the original germ, so the subdomains
are equal. The same argument with inclusions proves order
reflection.}
{取有限 affinoid 子域芽覆盖 $(X_i,x)$。
对约化中的拟紧开集 $W$，由拟分离性，各
$W\cap\mathscr R(X_i,x)$ 拟紧。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:goodgermdomainclassification} 将它们提升
为子域芽 $Y_i\subset X_i$。在重叠上，两种提升相等：其约化
相等，而 good 芽定理在重叠的每个 affinoid 片上反映相等性。
因此各 $Y_i$ 之并给出 $X$ 的子域芽，并具有约化 $W$，
证明满射性。若两个子域芽的约化相等，则与每个 $X_i$ 相交，
利用 good 芽定理得到在该片上的相等性；这些片覆盖原芽，故
两个子域相等。对包含关系应用同一论证，得到包含关系的反映性。}
\end{proof}
