\begin{proposition}[Both ends and the lateral trace; F8AG-E]
\label{prop:producttrace}
Under CP, let \(\beta\) be represented on the indicated joint moment
chart by \(\widetilde\beta\). Then its full trace is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:productboundary}
 \BB_A(\beta)=\int_P R_b\widetilde\beta-\int_P R_a\widetilde\beta
                         +\mathcal L_{P,[a,b]}(\widetilde\beta),
\end{equation}
where \(\mathcal L\) is exactly the sum in \eqref{eq:facet} over all
lateral facets \(\tau\times[a,b]\), with their signed normal
\(b_P(\tau)\) in the horizontal direction. All weights and normal
indices are retained. Under the vanishing hypothesis of
Proposition~\ref{prop:ends}, this lateral term is zero and the two
terms are the intrinsic end traces there. Otherwise \(\mathcal L\)
must not be discarded without a separate argument.
\end{proposition}
