Spreading a closed fibre neighborhood in characteristic zero

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 457

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Spreading a closed fibre neighborhood in characteristic zero}{特征零中闭纤维邻域的延展}]
\label{fnd:charzerogenericfibreneighborhood}
\bi{Let $K$ be complete of characteristic zero, let $Y\to X$ be a morphism of good $K$-analytic spaces smooth at $y$, and put $x=f(y)$. Suppose that $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is Artinian and $\mathcal H(x)$ is nontrivially valued. If a Zariski-closed subset $Z\subset Y$ contains an ordinary neighborhood of $y$ in $Y_x$, then $Z$ contains an ordinary neighborhood of $y$ in $Y$.}
{设 $K$ 为特征零完备域，$Y\to X$ 为 good $K$-解析空间之间的态射，并在 $y$ 处光滑，令 $x=f(y)$。假设 $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 为 Artinian 环且 $\mathcal H(x)$ 的赋值非平凡。若 Zariski 闭子集 $Z\subset Y$ 包含 $y$ 在 $Y_x$ 中的一个普通邻域，则 $Z$ 包含 $y$ 在 $Y$ 中的一个普通邻域。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The assertion concerns underlying sets. We first reduce the base near $x$. On an ordinary affinoid neighborhood $\mathcal M(A)$ put $\mathfrak p=\ker(x)$, $D=A_{\mathfrak p}$ and $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$. The map $D\to R$ is faithfully flat, so its map on spectra is surjective. Since $R$ is local Artinian, $D$ has only one prime and is zero-dimensional. Its reduction is therefore a field: its unique prime is the nilradical, which is nilpotent because the ring is Noetherian.}
{此断言只涉及底层集合。先在 $x$ 附近约化底空间。在普通 affinoid 邻域 $\mathcal M(A)$ 上，令 $\mathfrak p=\ker(x)$、$D=A_{\mathfrak p}$、$R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$。映射 $D\to R$ 忠实平坦，故其谱映射满射。因 $R$ 为局部 Artinian 环，$D$ 只有一个素理想，因而零维；其约化为域，因为唯一素理想是幂零根，而 Noetherian 环的幂零根幂零。}

\bi{Let $N\subset A$ be the nilradical. The ordinary local ring of $X_{\mathrm{red}}$ at $x$ is $R/NR$, by coherent closed-subspace restriction. The regular homomorphism $D\to R$ of Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroregularcomparison}, after quotienting by the nilradical of $D$, shows that $R/NR$ is geometrically regular over the field $D_{\mathrm{red}}$. It is also local Artinian, being a quotient of $R$. A zero-dimensional regular local ring is a field, since its maximal ideal has zero-dimensional cotangent space and Nakayama makes that ideal zero. The base change $Y\times_X X_{\mathrm{red}}\to Y$ is a homeomorphism on underlying spaces, since it is cut out by a nilpotent ideal. It preserves smoothness and the analytic fibre. Thus we may replace the base by its reduction and assume $R$ is a field.}
{令 $N\subset A$ 为幂零根。由凝聚闭子空间的限制，$X_{\mathrm{red}}$ 在 $x$ 处的普通局部环为 $R/NR$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroregularcomparison} 的正则同态 $D\to R$ 在除去 $D$ 的幂零根后，说明 $R/NR$ 在域 $D_{\mathrm{red}}$ 上几何正则。它又是 $R$ 的商，因而为局部 Artinian 环。零维正则局部环为域：其极大理想的余切空间维数为零，Nakayama 遂使极大理想为零。基变换 $Y\times_X X_{\mathrm{red}}\to Y$ 由幂零理想定义，故在底层空间上为同胚；它保持光滑性与解析纤维。因此可用约化底空间替换原底，假设 $R$ 为域。}

\bi{By Corollary~\ref{fnd:charzeronormallocus}, shrink to an ordinary neighborhood of $x$ on which $X$ is normal. Smoothness and the same corollary make $Y$ normal near $y$. Choose a normal affinoid ordinary neighborhood $Y_0=\mathcal M(B)$ of $y$ and select its component containing $y$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:normalcomponentidentity} makes its algebra a normal domain. Write $Z\cap Y_0$ as the common zero set of finitely many functions $h_1,\ldots,h_s\in B$. It suffices to show that these functions vanish on some nonempty ordinary open subset of $Y_0$, for the identity principle will then make them zero on all of $Y_0$.}
{由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:charzeronormallocus}，可缩小到 $x$ 的一个普通邻域，使 $X$ 正规。光滑性与同一推论使 $Y$ 在 $y$ 附近正规。选择 $y$ 的正规 affinoid 普通邻域 $Y_0=\mathcal M(B)$，并取包含 $y$ 的分量；由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:normalcomponentidentity}，其代数为正规整环。将 $Z\cap Y_0$ 写成有限个函数 $h_1,\ldots,h_s\in B$ 的公共零点集。只须证明这些函数在 $Y_0$ 的某个非空普通开集上为零；恒等原理随后使其在整个 $Y_0$ 上为零。}

\bi{Work in an ordinary open neighborhood of $y$ inside $Y_0$, small enough that the morphism is smooth of constant relative dimension there. The hypothesis supplies a nonempty fibre open subset contained in $Z$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:nontrivialresidueproductpatch} gives a finite \'etale map $X'\to U$ near $x$, one point $x'$ above $x$, and a nonempty ordinary open product patch in $Y'=Y\times_X X'$ whose fibre at $x'$ lies in $Z'$. Its base is an ordinary neighborhood of $x'$ and its other factor is an open polydisc $D$. The local ring $R'=\mathcal O_{X',x'}$ is a field: finite branch separation identifies it with a local factor of a finite \'etale algebra over the field $R$. The space $X'$ is normal near $x'$ by quasi-smooth ascent.}
{在 $Y_0$ 内取 $y$ 的普通开邻域，足够小以使态射在其上光滑且相对维数恒定。假设给出一个包含于 $Z$ 的非空纤维开子集。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:nontrivialresidueproductpatch}，存在 $x$ 附近的有限 \'etale 映射 $X'\to U$、$x$ 上方的唯一点 $x'$，以及 $Y'=Y\times_X X'$ 中的非空普通乘积开片，使其在 $x'$ 处的纤维包含于 $Z'$。此乘积的底是 $x'$ 的普通邻域，另一因子为开多圆盘 $D$。局部环 $R'=\mathcal O_{X',x'}$ 为域：有限分支分离把它识别为域 $R$ 上某有限 \'etale 代数的一个局部因子。由准光滑正规性上升，$X'$ 在 $x'$ 附近正规。}

\bi{Inside the base of the product patch choose a normal affinoid ordinary neighborhood $X'_0=\mathcal M(A')$ of $x'$ and retain its component through $x'$. Then $A'$ is a domain, and $A'\to R'\hookrightarrow\mathcal H(x')$ is injective. Indeed $A'\to R'$ is a nonzero flat map from a domain, so multiplication by every nonzero element remains injective. Restrict the pulled-back $h_j$ to $X'_0\times_KD$. Exhausting $D$ by nested closed polydiscs identifies each such function with a power series}
{在乘积开片的底中，选择 $x'$ 的正规 affinoid 普通邻域 $X'_0=\mathcal M(A')$，并保留含 $x'$ 的分量。则 $A'$ 为整环，且 $A'\to R'\hookrightarrow\mathcal H(x')$ 为单射。确实，$A'\to R'$ 是整环到非零环的平坦映射，所以每个非零元素的乘法仍为单射。将 $h_j$ 的拉回限制到 $X'_0\times_KD$。用递增闭多圆盘穷竭 $D$，便将每个函数识别为幂级数}
\[
                      h_j=\sum_I a_{I,j}T^I,\qquad a_{I,j}\in A'.
\]
\bi{The coefficients agree on the exhaustion because Tate-series expansions are unique, and restrictions to smaller polydiscs preserve their coefficients. On the fibre at $x'$ this function vanishes at every point. In particular it vanishes at every Gauss point of a sufficiently small closed polydisc in $D$, so every coefficient $a_{I,j}(x')$ is zero by the Gauss norm formula. Injectivity of $A'\to\mathcal H(x')$ gives $a_{I,j}=0$ for all $I,j$. Thus every $h_j$ vanishes on $X'_0\times_KD$, which contains a nonempty ordinary open subset of the original product patch because $X'_0$ is an ordinary neighborhood of $x'$.}
{因 Tate 级数展开唯一，且向较小闭多圆盘的限制保持系数，这些系数在穷竭过程中一致。在 $x'$ 处的纤维上，该函数在每一点均为零。特别地，它在 $D$ 中任意充分小闭多圆盘的 Gauss 点处为零；由 Gauss 范数公式，每个系数 $a_{I,j}(x')$ 都为零。单射性 $A'\to\mathcal H(x')$ 便给出全部 $a_{I,j}=0$。因此每个 $h_j$ 都在 $X'_0\times_KD$ 上为零；由于 $X'_0$ 是 $x'$ 的普通邻域，此乘积包含原乘积开片中的非空普通开集。}

\bi{The map $Y'\to Y$ is \'etale over the chosen base neighborhood and is ordinary-open by the finite-branch argument in Lemma~\ref{fnd:smoothseparablerigiddensity}. Hence the image of that open subset is a nonempty ordinary open subset of $Y_0$ contained in $Z$. The identity principle proves $Z\supset Y_0$. Since $Y_0$ was an ordinary neighborhood of $y$, this is the required assertion.}
{在所选底邻域上，映射 $Y'\to Y$ 为 \'etale，且由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:smoothseparablerigiddensity} 中的有限分支论证，它对普通拓扑开放。所以该开集的像是 $Y_0$ 中包含于 $Z$ 的非空普通开集。恒等原理给出 $Z\supset Y_0$；因 $Y_0$ 是 $y$ 的普通邻域，即得结论。}
\end{proof}
