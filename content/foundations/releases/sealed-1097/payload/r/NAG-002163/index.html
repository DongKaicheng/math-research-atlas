Polynomial implicit equations have finite endpoint control

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 890

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Polynomial implicit equations have finite endpoint control}
{多项式隐方程的终点有有限控制}]
\label{cand:polynomialimplicitendpoint}
\bi{Let $C$ be an affinoid algebra receiving the standard
algebra $A$ contractively. Suppose the system
$G\in K^\circ[X_0,\ldots,X_r,T,Y]^m$ has a root
$y\in C^m$ of norm at most one and its determinant has an
inverse $q\in C$ of norm at most one. Lift this root under
the base displacement by Lemma~\ref{cand:banachcenterhensel}.
For every polynomial $P\in K[X_0,\ldots,X_r,T,Y,Q]$, the
norm of $P$ evaluated at the displaced base coordinates,
the lifted root, and the inverse determinant extends jointly
continuously to $\mathcal M(C)\times[0,1]$ by taking its
coefficient supremum at time one.}
{设 affinoid 代数 $C$ 接受从标准代数 $A$ 出发的压缩
映射。假设方程组
$G\in K^\circ[X_0,\ldots,X_r,T,Y]^m$ 有范数至多为
一的根 $y\in C^m$，其行列式有范数至多为一的逆
$q\in C$。利用 Lemma~\ref{cand:banachcenterhensel}
将此根沿基底位移提升。对任意多项式
$P\in K[X_0,\ldots,X_r,T,Y,Q]$，将其在位移后的
基底坐标、提升后的根及逆行列式处取值；其范数在
时刻一取系数上确界后，联合连续延拓到
$\mathcal M(C)\times[0,1]$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First assume the coefficients of $P$ belong to
$K^\circ$. Let $R\subseteq C$ be the ring generated over
the image of $\mathbb Z$ by the finitely many coefficients
of $G,P$, the base-coordinate images, the coordinates of
$y$, and $q$. Every generator has norm at most one, hence
so does every element of $R$. The ring $\mathbb Z$ is
Noetherian, since each ideal is generated by its least
positive element, or is zero. Its image is a quotient, and
\cite[Tag 00FN]{StacksAlg} therefore makes $R$ Noetherian.}
{先设 $P$ 的系数属于 $K^\circ$。令 $R\subseteq C$
为由 $G,P$ 的有限个系数、基底坐标像、$y$ 的坐标及
$q$ 在 $\mathbb Z$ 的像上生成的环。每个生成元范数
至多为一，因而 $R$ 的全部元素范数都至多为一。
环 $\mathbb Z$ 为 Noetherian，因为其非零理想由最小
正元素生成，零理想也有限生成。其像为一个商环，
故由 \cite[Tag 00FN]{StacksAlg}，$R$ 为 Noetherian。}

\bi{We check that every formal displacement coefficient lies
in this single ring. The base displacement has coefficients
in $R$: the inverse factors $(1+S_i)^{-1}$ have integral
coefficients. Write the lifted root as $y+h(U)$, with
$h(0)=0$. In each positive total degree, the coefficient
of an as-yet-unknown term of $h$ in $G_\sigma(y+h)$ is
the original matrix $G'(y)$ times that coefficient; all
remaining terms use only already determined lower degrees.
The inverse matrix $q\operatorname{adj}(G'(y))$ has its
entries in $R$. Induction on total degree therefore puts
every coefficient of $h$ in $R$. This formal recursion
agrees with the analytic root at each radius less than one
by uniqueness. Recursively inverting its determinant puts
every coefficient of the new inverse determinant in $R$
as well. Polynomial substitution then does the same for $P$.}
{验证每个形式位移系数都落在这同一个环中。基底位移
的系数属于 $R$，因为逆因子 $(1+S_i)^{-1}$ 的系数
都是整数。把提升后的根写成 $y+h(U)$，其中 $h(0)=0$。
在每个正总次数中，$G_\sigma(y+h)$ 里尚未确定的
$h$ 的某项系数，总以原矩阵 $G'(y)$ 乘该系数的形式
出现；其余项只涉及已经确定的较低次数。
逆矩阵 $q\operatorname{adj}(G'(y))$ 的元素属于 $R$。
对总次数归纳，得到 $h$ 的每个系数均属于 $R$。
由唯一性，此形式递推在每个小于一的半径上与解析根
一致。对其行列式逐次求逆，也使新逆行列式的每个
系数属于 $R$。最后，多项式代换对 $P$ 给出同样结论。}

\bi{Lemma~\ref{cand:noetherianboundedcoefficients} now
applies to this coefficient family and proves the assertion.
For arbitrary $P$, multiply it by a nonzero scalar making
its finitely many coefficients integral, apply the result,
and divide the real-valued norm function by the absolute
value of that scalar. Such a scalar exists because the
ground-field valuation is nontrivial.}
{现在对这个系数族应用
Lemma~\ref{cand:noetherianboundedcoefficients}，即可
证明断言。对于任意 $P$，乘以某个非零标量使其
有限个系数均为整，应用上述结论，再把实值范数函数
除以该标量的绝对值。基域赋值非平凡保证存在这样的
标量。}
\end{proof}
