Strictification of a domain presentation

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 363

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Strictification of a domain presentation}{域表示的 strictification}]
\label{fnd:freedomainstrictification}
\bi{Let $A\to B$ represent an affinoid domain $U\subseteq\mathcal M_K(A)$. There is a finite free tuple $r$ such that $L=K_r$ is nontrivially valued, $A_r=A\widehat\otimes_K L$ and $B_r=B\widehat\otimes_K L$ are strict $L$-affinoid, and $A_r\to B_r$ is a strict-test affinoid-domain presentation of its spectral image $U_r$. Writing $\varphi_r:\mathcal M_L(B_r)\to\mathcal M_L(A_r)$ and $\varphi:\mathcal M_K(B)\to\mathcal M_K(A)$, one has}
{设 $A\to B$ 表示 affinoid 域 $U\subseteq\mathcal M_K(A)$。存在有限自由元组 $r$，使 $L=K_r$ 赋值非平凡，$A_r=A\widehat\otimes_K L$ 与 $B_r=B\widehat\otimes_K L$ 都在 $L$ 上 strict affinoid，且 $A_r\to B_r$ 是其谱像 $U_r$ 的 strict-test affinoid-domain 表示。记 $\varphi_r:\mathcal M_L(B_r)\to\mathcal M_L(A_r)$ 及 $\varphi:\mathcal M_K(B)\to\mathcal M_K(A)$，则有}
\[
 \varphi_r\circ\sigma_{r,B}=\sigma_{r,A}\circ\varphi,
 \qquad \sigma_{r,A}^{-1}(U_r)=U.
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose $r$ by Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification} for presentations of $A$ and $B$. If $K$ is trivially valued and the chosen tuple is empty, append one radius greater than one. It is free over $K$, and the original presentation radii remain in $\sqrt{|L^\times|}$, so the same strictification applies. All these algebras are also generalized $K$-affinoid by Proposition~\ref{fnd:freeextensionpresentation}.}
{对 $A,B$ 的表示应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification}，选择 $r$。若 $K$ 的赋值平凡且所得元组为空，就添入一个大于一的半径。它在 $K$ 上自由，而原表示半径仍属于 $\sqrt{|L^\times|}$，故同一 strictification 仍然适用。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:freeextensionpresentation}，这些代数也都是 generalized $K$-affinoid。}

\bi{The map $A_r\to B_r$ is an epimorphism among strict $L$-affinoid algebras. Indeed, two bounded $L$-maps $B_r\to C$ agreeing on $A_r$ restrict to maps $B\to C$ agreeing on $A$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:freefieldaffinoidtests} makes $C$ a permitted generalized $K$-affinoid test, and both restrictions factor the same map with spectral image in $U$. Domain uniqueness makes them equal. The original $B$ and the scalar field $L$ generate a dense subalgebra of $B_r$, so the two maps agree on $B_r$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictepispectralinjectivity} now gives injectivity on spectra. Compactness and Hausdorffness, Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact}, identify $\mathcal M_L(B_r)$ homeomorphically with $U_r$.}
{映射 $A_r\to B_r$ 在 strict $L$-affinoid 代数范畴中为上态。事实上，若两个有界 $L$-映射 $B_r\to C$ 在 $A_r$ 上相同，则它们限制为在 $A$ 上相同的映射 $B\to C$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:freefieldaffinoidtests}，$C$ 是允许的 generalized $K$-affinoid 测试代数；两个限制都是同一个谱像包含于 $U$ 的映射的分解，所以域泛性质中的唯一性使它们相等。原来的 $B$ 与标量域 $L$ 在 $B_r$ 中生成稠密子代数，故两个映射在 $B_r$ 上相同。现在 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictepispectralinjectivity} 给出谱单射性。再由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact} 的紧性和 Hausdorff 性，将 $\mathcal M_L(B_r)$ 与 $U_r$ 同胚识别。}

\bi{Suppose a bounded $L$-map $A_r\to C$, with $C$ strict over $L$, has spectral image in $U_r$. Its restriction $A\to C$ has image in $U$, since restricting any point of $B_r$ to $B$ gives a point over $U$. By the same lemma, the original domain property provides a unique bounded map $B\to C$. Together with the given scalar map $L\to C$, its bounded balanced product extends to $B\widehat\otimes_K L\to C$. This is the required $L$-factorization; its uniqueness follows by restriction to $B$ and $L$ and density. Thus $A_r\to B_r$ has the strict-test domain property.}
{设有界 $L$-映射 $A_r\to C$ 的谱像包含于 $U_r$，其中 $C$ 在 $L$ 上 strict。其限制 $A\to C$ 的谱像包含于 $U$，因为将 $B_r$ 的任一谱点限制到 $B$，便得到映入 $U$ 的点。由同一引理，原域泛性质提供唯一有界映射 $B\to C$。将它与给定的标量映射 $L\to C$ 相乘，所得有界平衡映射延拓为 $B\widehat\otimes_K L\to C$，即所需的 $L$-分解。限制到 $B,L$ 再用稠密性，得到唯一性。因此 $A_r\to B_r$ 满足 strict-test 域泛性质。}

\bi{The first displayed identity is the naturality in Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusgausssection}. It implies $\sigma_{r,A}(U)\subseteq U_r$. Conversely the projection of $U_r$ to $\mathcal M_K(A)$ is contained in $U$, and $\pi_r\sigma_{r,A}$ is the identity. These two inclusions prove the second displayed equality.}
{所列第一个恒等式是 Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusgausssection} 的自然性。它推出 $\sigma_{r,A}(U)\subseteq U_r$。反之，$U_r$ 到 $\mathcal M_K(A)$ 的投影包含于 $U$，且 $\pi_r\sigma_{r,A}$ 为恒等映射。这两个包含关系证明所列第二个等式。}
\end{proof}
