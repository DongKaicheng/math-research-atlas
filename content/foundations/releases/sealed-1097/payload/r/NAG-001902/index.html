A boundary above a normalization Gauss point

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 510

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{A boundary above a normalization Gauss point}
{正规化 Gauss 点上方的边界}]
\label{fnd:normalizationfiniteboundary}
\bi{Let $B$ be a nonzero strict $K$-affinoid domain, and let
$R=K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_d\rangle\hookrightarrow B$ be a bounded finite
injection. Set $F=\operatorname{Frac}(R)$ and let $H$ be the completion of
$F$ for the extending Gauss absolute value. The finite-dimensional
$H$-algebra $D=B\otimes_R H$ is nonzero. Its finitely many maximal ideals,
with the absolute values on their residue fields, give a finite set
$S\subset\mathcal M(B)$, and}
{设 $B$ 为非零 strict $K$-affinoid 整环，
$R=K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_d\rangle\hookrightarrow B$ 为有界有限单射。
令 $F=\operatorname{Frac}(R)$，并令 $H$ 为 $F$ 关于 Gauss 绝对值延拓的
完备化。有限维 $H$-代数 $D=B\otimes_R H$ 非零。
它的有限多个极大理想及其剩余域绝对值给出有限集合
$S\subset\mathcal M(B)$，且}
\[
 \max_{x\in\mathcal M(B)}|b(x)|=\max_{s\in S}|b(s)|
 \qquad(b\in B).
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The multiplicative Gauss norm extends to the fraction field by
$|u/v|=\|u\|/\|v\|$. Its metric completion is again a valued field:
if a nonzero Cauchy class is represented by $u_n$, then $|u_n|$ is
eventually constant and positive, and
$|u_n^{-1}-u_m^{-1}|=|u_n-u_m|/(|u_n||u_m|)$ proves convergence of
the inverses. Put $E=B\otimes_R F$. This is a finite-dimensional
$F$-domain, hence a field. Consequently $D=E\otimes_F H$ has positive
finite dimension over $H$.}
{乘性的 Gauss 范数通过 $|u/v|=\|u\|/\|v\|$ 延拓到分式域。
其度量完备化仍为赋值域：若非零 Cauchy 类由 $u_n$ 表示，则
$|u_n|$ 最终为正的常数，而等式
$|u_n^{-1}-u_m^{-1}|=|u_n-u_m|/(|u_n||u_m|)$ 保证逆元序列收敛。
令 $E=B\otimes_R F$。它是有限维 $F$-整环，故为域。
所以 $D=E\otimes_F H$ 在 $H$ 上具有正的有限维数。}

\bi{A finite-dimensional algebra has only finitely many maximal ideals.
Indeed, any $r$ distinct maximal ideals give, by the Chinese remainder
theorem, a quotient which is a product of $r$ nonzero fields, forcing
$r\leq\dim_H D$. Each residue field $L$ is finite over $H$ and has the
absolute value of Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}.
The resulting maps $B\to L$ are bounded. To check this last assertion,
choose finite $R$-module generators $b_1,\ldots,b_m$ of $B$.
Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebras} identifies the given norm of
$B$ with its canonical finite-module norm. Thus every $b$ has an
expression $b=\sum a_i b_i$ with $\max_i\|a_i\|\leq C\|b\|$,
where $C$ is independent of $b$. Evaluation in $L$ then gives
$|b|\leq C\|b\|\max_i|b_i|$. These characters therefore define the
asserted points.}
{有限维代数只有有限多个极大理想。事实上，由中国剩余定理，任意
$r$ 个不同极大理想给出一个由 $r$ 个非零域组成的乘积商，故
$r\leq\dim_H D$。每个剩余域 $L$ 都有限于 $H$，由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 配备绝对值。
所得映射 $B\to L$ 有界：选择有限 $R$-模生成元 $b_1,\ldots,b_m$，
由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebras}，$B$ 的给定范数与
典范有限模范数等价。因此，每个 $b$ 都有表达式
$b=\sum a_i b_i$，其中 $\max_i\|a_i\|\leq C\|b\|$，且 $C$
与 $b$ 无关。在 $L$ 中取值便有
$|b|\leq C\|b\|\max_i|b_i|$，故这些 character 确实定义谱点。}

\bi{Fix $b\in B$. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:stricttatefactorial} and the
minimal-polynomial argument in Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictminimalpolynomialbounds},
the monic minimal polynomial of $b$ over $F$ belongs to $R[Z]$;
write it as $Z^e+a_1Z^{e-1}+\cdots+a_e$. Put
$r=\max_i\|a_i\|^{1/i}$. Every analytic point $x$ of $B$ restricts to
a bounded point of $R$, so $|a_i(x)|\leq\|a_i\|$. The dominant-term
argument from the preceding lemma gives $|b(x)|\leq r$.}
{固定 $b\in B$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:stricttatefactorial} 以及
Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictminimalpolynomialbounds} 中的最小多项式论证，
$b$ 在 $F$ 上的首一最小多项式属于 $R[Z]$，可写成
$Z^e+a_1Z^{e-1}+\cdots+a_e$。令 $r=\max_i\|a_i\|^{1/i}$。
$B$ 的每个解析点 $x$ 都限制为 $R$ 的有界谱点，故
$|a_i(x)|\leq\|a_i\|$。上一引理的主导项论证给出 $|b(x)|\leq r$。}

\bi{At the Gauss point the coefficients have sizes exactly $\|a_i\|$,
so the same lemma supplies a root $\alpha$ of size $r$ in a finite
splitting field over $H$. The algebra
$F[b]\otimes_F H=H[Z]/(Z^e+a_1Z^{e-1}+\cdots+a_e)$ has the maximal
ideal defined by this root. The extension}
{在 Gauss 点，各系数的大小恰为 $\|a_i\|$，所以上一引理在 $H$ 的
某个有限分裂域中给出大小为 $r$ 的根 $\alpha$。
代数 $F[b]\otimes_F H=H[Z]/(Z^e+a_1Z^{e-1}+\cdots+a_e)$ 因此具有
由该根定义的极大理想。扩张}
\[
 F[b]\otimes_F H\longrightarrow E\otimes_F H=D
\]
\bi{is finite free of positive rank, since $E$ is a finite-dimensional
vector space over the field $F[b]$. It is in particular integral and
faithfully flat. Lying over, \cite[Tag 00GQ]{StacksAlg}, gives a maximal
ideal of $D$ above the chosen one. Its residue field contains $H(\alpha)$;
uniqueness of the extending absolute value implies that the corresponding
point $s\in S$ satisfies $|b(s)|=r$. This proves the maximum identity.}
{是正秩有限自由扩张，因为 $E$ 是域 $F[b]$ 上的有限维向量空间。
特别地，它整且忠实平坦。由 lying over，\cite[Tag 00GQ]{StacksAlg}，
$D$ 中存在位于所选极大理想上方的极大理想。其剩余域包含
$H(\alpha)$，延拓绝对值的唯一性说明相应点 $s\in S$ 满足
$|b(s)|=r$，从而得到最大值恒等式。}
\end{proof}
