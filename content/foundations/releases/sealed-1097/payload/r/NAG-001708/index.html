Principal opens and global analytification

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 285

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Principal opens and global analytification}{主开集与整体解析化}]
\label{fnd:analytificationzariskiopens}
\bi{For $g\in R$, the natural map $X_{R_g}\to X_R$ identifies its source with the open analytic domain $|g|>0$. Consequently affine analytification glues uniquely to a functor from finite-type $K$-schemes to good $K$-analytic spaces. Open immersions of schemes give open analytic immersions.}
{对 $g\in R$，自然映射 $X_{R_g}\to X_R$ 将源空间识别为开解析域 $|g|>0$。因此仿射解析化唯一地粘合为从有限型 $K$-概形到 good $K$-解析空间的函子。概形的开浸入给出解析空间的开浸入。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Multiplicativity gives a unique extension of a seminorm with $|g|>0$ to $R_g$, by $|a/g^m|=|a|/|g|^m$. On the bounded piece $X_{R,r}$, a bound $|g^{-1}|\le t$ is represented by}
{乘性给出满足 $|g|>0$ 的半范数到 $R_g$ 的唯一延拓，其公式为 $|a/g^m|=|a|/|g|^m$。在有界分片 $X_{R,r}$ 上，界 $|g^{-1}|\le t$ 由下述代数表示：}
\[
 \bigl(K\{r^{-1}T\}/I\bigr)\{t^{-1}V\}/(gV-1).
\]
\bi{This is exactly its Laurent domain $|g|\ge t^{-1}$ by the rational universal property. These domains, as $r$ and $t$ grow, form neighbourhoods of every point with $|g|>0$. They are also the affinoid pieces for the presentation $R_g=R[V]/(gV-1)$. Thus the pointwise bijection is an analytic isomorphism onto that open domain.}
{由 rational 泛性质，这恰好是其中的 Laurent 域 $|g|\ge t^{-1}$。随着 $r,t$ 增大，这些域在每个满足 $|g|>0$ 的点处构成邻域；同时，它们也是表示 $R_g=R[V]/(gV-1)$ 的 affinoid 分片。因此上述逐点双射是到该开域的解析同构。}

\bi{Every open subset of an affine scheme is covered by principal opens. The identifications just constructed agree on intersections, since localizing successively at $g$ and $h$ gives $R_{gh}$ and the same seminorm and ring maps. An affine open cover of a finite-type scheme can therefore be analytified and glued along its open overlaps by Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticspacegluing}. Any two covers have a common affine-open refinement, and the same local identifications give the unique comparison isomorphism. Ring maps on affine neighbourhoods similarly glue to the map attached to a scheme morphism; their composites agree on coordinates. Goodness holds locally on these open affine analytifications.}
{仿射概形的每个开子集都由主开集覆盖。上述识别在交上相容，因为先后在 $g,h$ 处局部化给出 $R_{gh}$ 以及同一半范数和环映射。因此可将有限型概形的一个仿射开覆盖逐片解析化，再由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticspacegluing} 沿开重叠部分粘合。任意两个覆盖都有共同的仿射开加细；相同的局部识别给出唯一比较同构。概形态射在仿射邻域上的环映射也同样粘合，且复合在坐标上一致，从而得到函子性。Goodness 在这些仿射解析化开集上局部成立。}
\end{proof}
