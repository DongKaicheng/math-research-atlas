A Gauss extension containing all spectral values

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 554

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A Gauss extension containing all spectral values}
{包含全部谱值的 Gauss 扩张}]
\label{fnd:spectralvaluenormalizinggauss}
\bi{For finitely many $K$-affinoid algebras $A_j$, one can
choose a finite iterated Gauss extension $L/K$ such that every
$(A_j)_L$ is strict and all its nonzero spectral values belong
to $|L^\times|$. One may also require $L$ to be nontrivially valued.}
{对有限多个 $K$-affinoid 代数 $A_j$，可选择有限次迭代的
Gauss 扩张 $L/K$，使每个 $(A_j)_L$ 都为 strict，且其全部
非零谱值属于 $|L^\times|$。还可要求 $L$ 的赋值非平凡。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First make the algebras strict by a common free-radius
extension $K_s/K$. For each strict algebra, the proof of
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalfiniteboundary} gives a finite
boundary consisting of points whose residue fields are finite
extensions of Gauss fields of strict polydiscs over $K_s$.
The latter Gauss fields have value group $|K_s^\times|$.
In a finite valued-field extension the value-group quotient
is finite: vectors with values in distinct cosets are linearly
independent over the base field, since a sum with a uniquely
largest term cannot vanish. Its cardinality is therefore
bounded by the field degree. Choose finitely many positive
numbers representing generators of all these finite quotient
groups. The spectral values of every original $A_j$ lie in
the group generated by $|K^\times|$, the entries of $s$, and
these finitely many numbers, because they are attained on
the specified finite boundaries.}
{先作一个共同自由半径扩张 $K_s/K$，使全部代数变成 strict。
对每个 strict 代数，Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalfiniteboundary}
的证明给出有限边界，其中各点的剩余域为 $K_s$ 上 strict
多圆盘 Gauss 域的有限扩张。后者的值群为 $|K_s^\times|$。
有限赋值域扩张的值群商有限：值位于不同陪集的向量在基域上
线性无关，因为唯一占优项的和不可能为零。因此陪集数不超过
域扩张次数。选取有限个正数，代表这些有限商群的生成元。
每个原 $A_j$ 的谱值均在指定有限边界上达到，因而属于
$|K^\times|$、$s$ 的各分量及上述有限个正数所生成的群。}

\bi{Adjoin Gauss variables with precisely these radii, and, if
necessary, one radius different from one. The resulting $L$
has this generated value group and still makes all $A_j$ strict.
By Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalgaussgradedbasechange}, every positive
spectral value on $(A_j)_L$ is the maximum of finitely many
products of an original spectral value and a basis-vector norm.
Both factors belong to $|L^\times|$, proving the assertion.}
{加入具有这些半径的 Gauss 变量；必要时再加入一个不等于一的
半径。所得 $L$ 的值群为相应生成群，且仍使所有 $A_j$ 为
strict。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalgaussgradedbasechange}，
$(A_j)_L$ 的每个正谱值都是有限多个乘积的最大值，每个乘积
由一个原谱值和一个基向量范数组成。两个因子都属于
$|L^\times|$，结论随之成立。}
\end{proof}
