Complete base change for projective coherent cohomology

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 405

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Complete base change for projective coherent cohomology}
{射影凝聚上同调的完备基变换}]
\label{fnd:projectivecoherentbasechange}
\bi{Let \(P=(\mathbf P^n_K)^{\an}\), let \(\mathcal F\) be
coherent on \(P_G\), and let \(L/K\) be any complete valued
extension. For the coherent pullback \(\mathcal F_L\) there
are natural isomorphisms of finite-dimensional \(L\)-spaces}
{设 \(P=(\mathbf P^n_K)^{\an}\)，\(\mathcal F\) 为 \(P_G\)
上的凝聚层，\(L/K\) 为任意完备赋值扩域。对凝聚拉回
\(\mathcal F_L\)，存在有限维 \(L\)-空间的自然同构}
\[
 L\otimes_K H^q(P_G,\mathcal F)
   \simeq H^q((P_L)_G,\mathcal F_L).
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use the standard finite unit cover and its Banach \v Cech
complex \(C^\bullet\). Put \(Z^q=\ker d^q\) and
\(B^q=\operatorname{im}d^{q-1}\). By
Theorem~\ref{fnd:projectivecoherentfiniteness}, every \(B^q\)
is closed and \(Z^q/B^q\) is finite dimensional. Thus all
these spaces are Banach. If \(K\) is nontrivially valued,
Lemma~\ref{fnd:nonarchopenmapping} makes the surjections
\(C^q\to B^{q+1}\) norm-strict. If \(K\) is trivially valued,
the coefficient algebras and their finite modules on this
cover have the discrete \(0/1\) quotient norms; the same
surjections are norm-strict directly. The sequences}
{使用标准有限单位覆盖及其 Banach \v Cech 复形
\(C^\bullet\)。记 \(Z^q=\ker d^q\)、
\(B^q=\operatorname{im}d^{q-1}\)。由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:projectivecoherentfiniteness}，
每个 \(B^q\) 都闭，且 \(Z^q/B^q\) 有限维，因此这些空间均
完备。若 \(K\) 的赋值非平凡，
Lemma~\ref{fnd:nonarchopenmapping} 说明满射
\(C^q\to B^{q+1}\) 范数严格；若赋值平凡，此覆盖上的系数
代数及其有限模具有离散的 \(0/1\) 商范数，故这些满射直接
满足范数严格性。因此下列序列都是范数严格正合列：}
\[
 0\longrightarrow Z^q\longrightarrow C^q
       \longrightarrow B^{q+1}\longrightarrow0,\qquad
 0\longrightarrow B^q\longrightarrow Z^q
       \longrightarrow H^q\longrightarrow0 .
\]
\bi{Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonstrictexact} to both sequences.
The completed tensor complex is the \v Cech complex on \(P_L\):
this follows on each intersection from
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalscalarpresentation} and finite-module
base change, Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor}.
The two exact sequences identify its cohomology with
\(H^q\widehat\otimes_K L\). Since \(H^q\) is finite
dimensional, its completed tensor product is its ordinary
tensor product: choose a basis and use equivalence of
finite-dimensional norms. The intersection identifications
respect restrictions, so the resulting isomorphism is natural.}
{对两个序列应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonstrictexact}。
完备张量后的复形就是 \(P_L\) 上的 \v Cech 复形：在每个
交叠上，这来自 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalscalarpresentation}
及有限模基变换 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor}。
两个正合列将其上同调识别为
\(H^q\widehat\otimes_K L\)。由于 \(H^q\) 有限维，取一组基
并使用有限维范数等价性，便知其完备张量积就是普通张量积。
交叠上的识别与限制相容，因此所得同构自然。}
\end{proof}
