The reduction functor on all analytic germs

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 569

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The reduction functor on all analytic germs}
{全部解析芽上的约化函子}]
\label{fnd:allgradedgermreduction}
\bi{There is a canonical functor from analytic germs over
$K$ to graded birational spaces over $\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$,
extending $(X,x)\mapsto\mathscr R(X,x)$ for affinoid germs.
It carries subdomain inclusions to open embeddings,
finite subdomain coverings to open coverings, and commutes
with intersections, finite unions and inverse images.
Its field at $(X,x)$ is $\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}^{\rm gr}$.}
{存在从 $K$ 上解析芽到 $\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$ 上分次
birational 空间的典范函子，延拓 affinoid 芽上的
$(X,x)\mapsto\mathscr R(X,x)$。它将子域包含送到开嵌入，
将有限子域覆盖送到开覆盖，并与交、有限并及逆像相容。
它在 $(X,x)$ 处的域为 $\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}^{\rm gr}$。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use the domain chart quasi-nets of
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalnetdomaincalculus} and their restriction
and refinement properties in Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasinetcalculus}.
Choose finitely many affinoid subdomain germs $(X_i,x)$ covering
a neighbourhood of $x$. Refine each pairwise intersection by
finitely many affinoid subdomain germs. By
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedaffinoidgerminvariance} each reduced
piece of the overlap is open in the corresponding reduced chart,
with the canonical identification of the completed residue field.
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermcovercomparison} identifies the
overlap with their finite union. Compute in either chart using
the common finite refinement and those same field identifications.
On triple intersections the maps agree, since each is the identity
on the same field and is checked on the common affinoid refinement.
Glue these open subspaces.}
{使用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalnetdomaincalculus} 的域 chart
quasi-net 及 Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasinetcalculus} 的限制与加细性质。
选取覆盖 $x$ 的某个邻域的有限个 affinoid 子域芽 $(X_i,x)$，
并用有限个 affinoid 子域芽加细每个两两交。由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedaffinoidgerminvariance}，每个约化交叠片
在对应约化 chart 中开，并典范地识别完备剩余域。
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermcovercomparison} 将交叠识别为这些
片的有限并。在任一 chart 内，用共同有限加细及同样的域识别计算。
三重交上的映射相容，因为它们在同一域上为恒等映射，且可在共同
affinoid 加细上检查。因此可以沿这些开子空间粘合。}

\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedrzcompact} makes each affine
piece and each basic overlap piece quasi-compact, so the finite
overlap unions are quasi-compact. The gluing is quasi-compact and
quasi-separated and has a local homeomorphism to
$\mathscr P_{\widetilde K^{\rm gr}}(F_x)$.
Each affine piece is irreducible by the same proposition, and
their overlap identifications identify the common trivial
graded valuation $F_x$. Thus the gluing is connected.
A refinement of the affinoid cover gives the same gluing by the
finite-cover assertion of Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermcovercomparison}.
Any two choices have a common refinement, proving independence
of the atlas and of neighbourhood representatives.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedrzcompact}，各仿射片及基本交叠片
均拟紧，故有限交叠并也拟紧。粘合所得空间拟紧且拟分离，并带有
到 $\mathscr P_{\widetilde K^{\rm gr}}(F_x)$ 的局部同胚。
由同一命题，每个仿射片不可约，且交叠识别将共同的平凡分次赋值
$F_x$ 识别，故粘合所得空间连通。由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermcovercomparison} 的有限覆盖断言，
加细 affinoid 覆盖给出同一粘合。任意两个选择有共同加细，故构造
与 atlas 及邻域代表无关。}

\bi{By Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing}, a morphism
of germs is represented after finite affinoid refinements by maps
of good germs. On each such map use the
isometric embedding of completed residue fields and restriction
of valuations. These local maps agree on intersections by
the inverse-image assertion of
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermcovercomparison}, hence glue.
The same assertion proves all stated compatibilities; identities
and compositions are checked on the common affinoid refinements.
This also proves uniqueness of the extension.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing}，芽态射在作有限
affinoid 加细后由 good 芽之间的态射表示。在每个这样的态射上使用
完备剩余域的等距嵌入及赋值
限制映射。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermcovercomparison}
的逆像断言，这些局部映射在交上相等，因而粘合。
同一断言证明所列全部相容性；恒等态射与复合可在共同
affinoid 加细上检查。这也证明函子延拓的唯一性。}
\end{proof}
