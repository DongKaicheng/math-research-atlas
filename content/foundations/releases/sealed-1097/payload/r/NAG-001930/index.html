Spectral values in the strict case

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 527

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Spectral values in the strict case}
{Strict 情形的谱取值}]
\label{fnd:strictspectralvaluegroup}
\bi{For a strict $K$-affinoid algebra $A$, every nonzero spectral
radius $\rho_A(a)$ belongs to $\sqrt{|K^\times|}$. The same holds
for every nonzero value of $a$ at a Shilov point.}
{对 strict $K$-affinoid 代数 $A$，每个非零谱半径
$\rho_A(a)$ 都属于 $\sqrt{|K^\times|}$；$a$ 在 Shilov 点处
每个非零取值也具有此性质。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{In the finite-boundary construction, each point is obtained
from a finite extension $L/H$ of a completed Gauss field.
The value group of $H$ is $|K^\times|$: a nonzero limit has
the absolute value of any sufficiently accurate approximant, and
fractions of Gauss series have values in $|K^\times|$.
For $0\ne u\in L$, a polynomial equation over $H$ has at least
two terms of maximal absolute value. Equality of their sizes gives
$|u|^e\in|H^\times|$ for some nonzero integer $e$.
Thus all nonzero values at the constructed finite boundary lie
in $\sqrt{|K^\times|}$. Their finite maximum is $\rho_A(a)$.
The minimal Shilov boundary is a subset of this boundary by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:minimalfiniteboundary}, proving both assertions.}
{有限边界的构造中，每个点都来自一个完备 Gauss 域 $H$ 的有限
扩张 $L/H$。域 $H$ 的值群为 $|K^\times|$：非零极限的
绝对值等于任何充分精确的逼近项的绝对值，而 Gauss 级数分式的
值属于 $|K^\times|$。对 $0\ne u\in L$，其在 $H$ 上的
多项式方程至少有两个绝对值最大的项；这两项的大小相等给出
$|u|^e\in|H^\times|$，其中 $e$ 为某个非零整数。
因此，所构造有限边界上的全部非零值都属于
$\sqrt{|K^\times|}$，它们的有限最大值正是 $\rho_A(a)$。
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:minimalfiniteboundary}，极小 Shilov 边界
是这个边界的子集，从而得到两个断言。}
\end{proof}
