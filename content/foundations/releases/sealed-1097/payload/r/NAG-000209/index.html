Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}\label{fnd:quasismooth}
\bi{A morphism $f:Y\to X$ is quasi-smooth at $y$ if and only if it is analytically flat there and its $\mathcal H(f(y))$-analytic fibre is geometrically regular at $y$. For morphisms between good spaces, it is smooth at $y$ if and only if it is quasi-smooth and boundaryless there. Analytic-domain inclusions are flat. For a morphism between good spaces, naive flatness of a coherent sheaf at an interior point implies analytic flatness there.}
{Morphism $f:Y\to X$ 在 $y$ quasi-smooth 当且仅当它在该点 analytically flat，且其 $\mathcal H(f(y))$-analytic fibre 在 $y$ geometrically regular。对 good spaces 之间的 morphism，它在 $y$ smooth 当且仅当它在该点 quasi-smooth 且 boundaryless。Analytic-domain inclusions 为 flat。对 good spaces 之间的 morphism，coherent sheaf 在 interior point 处的 naive flatness 推出该点处的 analytic flatness。}
\end{theorem}
