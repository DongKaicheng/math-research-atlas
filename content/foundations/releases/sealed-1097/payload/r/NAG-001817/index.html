A generic Gauss field germ in arbitrary characteristic

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 484

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A generic Gauss field germ in arbitrary characteristic}{任意特征中的泛 Gauss 域芽}]
\label{fnd:generalgaussfieldgerm}
\bi{Let $X$ be good over any complete field, with $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ a field and $F=\mathcal H(x)$ trivially valued. If $0<r<1$ and $x^*$ is the radius-$r$ Gauss point above $x$ in $X\times_K\mathbb A_K^1$, then $\mathcal H(x^*)=F_r$ and $\mathcal O_{X\times_K\mathbb A_K^1,x^*}$ is a field.}
{设 $X$ 为任意完备域上的 good 空间，$\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 为域，且 $F=\mathcal H(x)$ 平凡赋值。若 $0<r<1$，$x^*$ 为 $X\times_K\mathbb A_K^1$ 中 $x$ 上方的半径 $r$ Gauss 点，则 $\mathcal H(x^*)=F_r$，且 $\mathcal O_{X\times_K\mathbb A_K^1,x^*}$ 为域。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The Gauss completed-residue calculation gives $\mathcal H(x^*)=F_r$. To test the kernel of ordinary evaluation, represent a germ $h$ on a product neighborhood $U\times D$ supplied by Lemma~\ref{fnd:freegaussproductneighborhood}, with $D$ an open annulus around radius $r$. Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalnormalcomparisonlocus} and Lemma~\ref{fnd:normalcomponentidentity} give a normal integral affinoid ordinary neighborhood $\mathcal M(A)\subset U$ of $x$. Its flat nonzero map to the field $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ and then to $F$ is injective. On a closed subannulus about $r$, write $h=\sum_{j\in\mathbf Z}a_jT^j$ with $a_j\in A$. If $h(x^*)=0$, the Gauss norm formula forces all $a_j(x)=0$, so the single injection $A\hookrightarrow F$ makes every coefficient zero. Thus $h$ is the zero germ. The kernel of ordinary evaluation is the maximal ideal, so that maximal ideal is zero and the ordinary local ring is a field. This coefficient argument uses no restriction on characteristic.}
{Gauss 点的完备剩余域计算给出 $\mathcal H(x^*)=F_r$。为检验普通取值的核，在 Lemma~\ref{fnd:freegaussproductneighborhood} 给出的乘积邻域 $U\times D$ 上表示一个芽 $h$，其中 $D$ 是半径 $r$ 周围的开圆环。Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalnormalcomparisonlocus} 与 Lemma~\ref{fnd:normalcomponentidentity} 给出 $x$ 的正规整 affinoid 普通邻域 $\mathcal M(A)\subset U$。它到域 $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 的映射平坦且非零，再复合到 $F$ 后仍单射。在包含半径 $r$ 的闭子圆环上，写 $h=\sum_{j\in\mathbf Z}a_jT^j$，其中 $a_j\in A$。若 $h(x^*)=0$，则 Gauss 范数公式使全部 $a_j(x)=0$，而同一个单射 $A\hookrightarrow F$ 使每个系数均为零。因此 $h$ 是零芽。普通取值的核就是极大理想，故该极大理想为零，普通局部环为域。此系数论证不限制特征。}
\end{proof}
