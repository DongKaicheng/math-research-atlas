Recovery from bounded analytic sections

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 750

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Recovery from bounded analytic sections]
\label{v035:f5:boundedsections}
Let $\mathfrak X$ be an admissible formal $O$-scheme with reduced
special fibre and generic fibre $X$. The generic-fibre map identifies
\[
 \Gamma(\mathfrak U,\mathcal O_{\mathfrak X})
 =\{f\in\Gamma(D_{\mathfrak U},\mathcal O_X):
                         |f(x)|\leq1\text{ for all }x\in D_{\mathfrak U}\}
\]
for every formal open $\mathfrak U$. These identifications commute with
restriction. For each $n\geq0$, the ideal
$\pi^n\Gamma(\mathfrak U,\mathcal O_{\mathfrak X})$ corresponds to the
same condition with bound $|\pi|^n$.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
On $\mathfrak U=\operatorname{Spf}A_0$, put $A=A_0[1/\pi]$.
Lemma~\ref{cand:reducedspecialcanonical} gives $A_0=A^\circ$ and
identifies its quotient norm with the spectral norm.
Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedspectralradiusformula} then gives
\[
                  A_0=\{a\in A:\max_{x\in\mathcal M(A)}|a(x)|\leq1\}.
\]
The map $A_0\to A$ is injective by $\pi$-torsion-freeness.

For general $\mathfrak U$, cover it by formal affines
$\mathfrak U_i$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}
makes their generic fibres an admissible analytic $G$-cover of
$D_{\mathfrak U}$. A bounded analytic section restricts to an integral
section on each $\mathfrak U_i$ by the affine equality. On each affine
subopen of $\mathfrak U_i\cap\mathfrak U_j$, the two integral sections
agree after inverting $\pi$, hence agree before inverting it.
They therefore glue in $\mathcal O_{\mathfrak X}$. Conversely, the
restrictions of an integral section have norm at most one and their
generic domains cover $D_{\mathfrak U}$. This proves the equality and
its restriction compatibility. Applying it to $f/\pi^n$ proves the
last assertion.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
Let $K$ be a complete nontrivially real-valued non-Archimedean field,
with its specified absolute value. Write $O=K^\circ$,
$\mathfrak m=K^{\circ\circ}$, and $k=O/\mathfrak m$, and choose
$\pi\in O$ with $0<|\pi|<1$. All formal schemes below are
$\pi$-adic. An admissible formal algebra is understood to be
$\pi$-torsion-free and topologically finitely presented over $O$.
For a formal open $\mathfrak U\subset\mathfrak X$, put
$D_{\mathfrak U}=\operatorname{sp}^{-1}(\mathfrak U)$, with its
analytic $G$-domain structure.
