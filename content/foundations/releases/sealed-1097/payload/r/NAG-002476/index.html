The available direction of the tangential comparison

8 Intrinsic smooth forms, integration and Stokes

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1167

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[The available direction of the tangential comparison]
\label{f8cont:tangential}
Let $P\subset\Omega\subset\R^N$ be a compact finite polyhedral set,
and let $N_P$ be the ambient smooth forms with zero tangential restriction
on every face. Give $E^{p,q}(\Omega)/N_P$ its quotient topology.
For each face $\sigma$, fix an affine tangent basis. The suprema, on
compact subsets of $\sigma$, of tangential coefficient derivatives of
any fixed finite order are well-defined continuous seminorms on this
quotient. Consequently the topology they generate is no finer than the
ambient quotient topology.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
In a tangent basis, restriction of each covector factor is a fixed linear
map. Each tangential derivative is a fixed linear combination of ambient
partial derivatives. Its coefficient supremum is bounded by a constant
times the ambient seminorm of the same order on the same compact set.
If a form belongs to $N_P$, its restricted coefficients vanish on the
relative interior of every face. Their derivatives along that face
vanish there and, by continuity, on its boundary. Thus the seminorms
annihilate $N_P$ and descend. The defining property of the quotient gives
continuity of each descended seminorm.
\end{proof}
