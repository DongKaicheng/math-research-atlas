Dimension of domains and affinoid covers

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 606

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Dimension of domains and affinoid covers}
{解析域及 affinoid 覆盖的维数}]
\label{fnd:affinoiddimensiondomaincover}
\bi{If $V$ is a nonempty affinoid domain of an affinoid space
$X$, then $\dim_KV\le\dim_KX$. If $(V_i)$ is any cover of a
nonempty affinoid $X$ by nonempty affinoid domains, then}
{若 $V$ 是 affinoid 空间 $X$ 的非空 affinoid 域，则
$\dim_KV\le\dim_KX$。若 $(V_i)$ 是非空 affinoid $X$ 的
任意非空 affinoid 域覆盖，则}
\[
                  \dim_KX=\sup_i\dim_KV_i.
\]
\bi{A finite cover has a maximum in place of the supremum.}
{有限覆盖时，上确界就是最大值。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Corollary~\ref{fnd:domainresiduefields} identifies the
completed residue fields of a point on $V$ and on $X$.
The preceding theorem therefore gives}
{Corollary~\ref{fnd:domainresiduefields} 识别同一点在
$V$ 与 $X$ 中的完备剩余域。因此前一定理给出}
\[
 \dim_KV=\max_{x\in V}d_K(x)\le\max_{x\in X}d_K(x)=\dim_KX.
\]
\bi{The union of the $V_i$ is $X$, so taking the supremum of
these equalities proves the covering formula. No flat-ring
dimension inequality or boundary hypothesis is used.}
{由于 $V_i$ 的并为 $X$，对这些等式取上确界即得覆盖公式。
这里不需要平坦环的维数不等式或边界假设。}
\end{proof}
