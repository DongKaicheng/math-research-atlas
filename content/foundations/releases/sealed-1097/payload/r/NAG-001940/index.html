Surjectivity of strict canonical reduction

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 533

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Surjectivity of strict canonical reduction}
{Strict 典范约化的满射性}]
\label{fnd:canonicalreductionsurjective}
\bi{For every strict $K$-affinoid algebra, including a trivially
valued ground field, the canonical reduction map
$\mathcal M(A)\to\operatorname{Spec}(\widetilde A)$ is surjective.}
{对每个 strict $K$-affinoid 代数，包括基域赋值平凡的情形，
典范约化映射
$\mathcal M(A)\to\operatorname{Spec}(\widetilde A)$ 都满射。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Suppose first that $K$ is nontrivially valued.
Choose an admissible model $A_0\subset A$ by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations}.
The algebra $\widetilde A$ is finitely generated by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictcanonicalfinitegeneration}.
Lift a finite generating list to $A^\circ$ and adjoin it to
$A_0$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:powerboundedmodelblowup} gives a new
admissible model $B_0$ for which $B_0\to\widetilde A$ is
surjective. A prime $\mathfrak p\subset\widetilde A$ pulls
back to a special-fibre prime $\mathfrak q$ of $B_0$.
Choose $x$ with center $\mathfrak q$ by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affineformalspecializationsurjective}.
For any $b\in A^\circ$, choose $a\in B_0$ with
$b-a\in A^{\circ\circ}$. Then $|b(x)|<1$ if and only if
$|a(x)|<1$, because $|b(x)-a(x)|<1$.
The reduction kernel of $x$ is therefore exactly $\mathfrak p$.}
{先设 $K$ 的赋值非平凡。由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations} 选择
admissible 模型 $A_0\subset A$。由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictcanonicalfinitegeneration}，
$\widetilde A$ 为有限生成代数。把它的一组有限生成元提升到
$A^\circ$，并添入 $A_0$。由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:powerboundedmodelblowup}，得到新的 admissible
模型 $B_0$，使 $B_0\to\widetilde A$ 满射。
$\widetilde A$ 的素理想 $\mathfrak p$ 拉回为 $B_0$ 的特殊纤维
素理想 $\mathfrak q$。由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affineformalspecializationsurjective}，选取以
$\mathfrak q$ 为中心的点 $x$。
对每个 $b\in A^\circ$，可选 $a\in B_0$ 使
$b-a\in A^{\circ\circ}$。由于 $|b(x)-a(x)|<1$，
$|b(x)|<1$ 当且仅当 $|a(x)|<1$。
因此 $x$ 的约化核恰为 $\mathfrak p$。}

\bi{If $K$ is trivially valued, a strict Tate algebra is a
polynomial algebra with the trivial norm, and its quotient norm
is trivial. Thus $A^\circ=A$, and a sequence of powers tends
to zero exactly when it is eventually zero. Consequently
$A^{\circ\circ}=\sqrt{(0)}$.
For a prime of $\widetilde A=A/\sqrt{(0)}$, give the corresponding
prime quotient of $A$ the trivial multiplicative norm.
It is bounded for the quotient norm on $A$ and has precisely
the requested reduction kernel.}
{若 $K$ 的赋值平凡，strict Tate 代数就是配备平凡范数的多项式环，
其商范数也平凡。于是 $A^\circ=A$，且一个幂序列趋于零当且仅当
它最终为零。因此 $A^{\circ\circ}=\sqrt{(0)}$。
给定 $\widetilde A=A/\sqrt{(0)}$ 的素理想，在 $A$ 的相应
素商上赋予平凡乘性范数。它对 $A$ 的商范数有界，且具有所需的
约化核。}
\end{proof}
