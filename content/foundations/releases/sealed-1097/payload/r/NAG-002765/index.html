Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Analytic Serre comparison; F8ST-A]
\label{thm:analyticserre}
Let \(A\) be a generalized affinoid algebra, \(X=\cM(A)\), \(M\) a
finite \(A\)-module, and \(\widetilde M\) its coherent sheaf. For
\(x\in X\), put \(\mathfrak p=\ker_A(x)\). Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:serrepoint}
 M_{\mathfrak p}\text{ is }S_1
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \widetilde M_x\text{ is }S_1
 \text{ as a module over }\cO_{X,x}.
\end{equation}
Thus \(M\) is \(S_1\) if and only if all these analytic stalk modules
are \(S_1\). If \(V\) is a good analytic domain in a good analytic
space \(X\), and \(\mathcal F\) is coherent, then at every \(x\in V\)
\begin{equation}\label{eq:serredomain}
 \mathcal F_x\text{ is }S_1
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 (\mathcal F|_V)_x\text{ is }S_1.
\end{equation}
The same comparison applies to a finite-type scheme over an affinoid
base, its analytification, and an affinoid domain therein, with the
corresponding algebraic and analytic stalks.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
By \tagg{NAG-001624}/\tagg{001666},
\(\widetilde M_x=M\otimes_A\cO_{X,x}\).
The map \(A_{\mathfrak p}\to\cO_{X,x}\) is regular by
\tagg{NAG-001782} and is local, hence faithfully flat.
Lemma~\ref{lem:serre} proves \eqref{eq:serrepoint}.
Every prime of \(A\) is the kernel of an analytic point by
\tagg{NAG-001860}, proving the assertion for all of \(M\).
For \eqref{eq:serredomain}, use the tensor identity
\tagg{NAG-001666} and the regular local map \tagg{NAG-001792}.
For the last assertion use the regular comparison square
\tagg{NAG-001791} and the same finite-presentation stalk calculation.
All localizations required in Lemma~\ref{lem:serre} are Noetherian.
\end{proof}
