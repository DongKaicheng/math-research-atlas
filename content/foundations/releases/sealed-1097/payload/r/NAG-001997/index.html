Boundaryless affinoid morphisms are finite

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 413

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Boundaryless affinoid morphisms are finite}{无边界 affinoid 态射的有限性}]
\label{fnd:affinoidboundarylessfinite}
\bi{Let $K$ be any complete non-Archimedean field and $A\to B$ a bounded homomorphism of generalized $K$-affinoid algebras. The following conditions are equivalent:}
{设 $K$ 为任意完备非阿基米德域，$A\to B$ 为 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数的有界同态。以下条件等价：}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \bi{$\operatorname{Int}(\mathcal M(B)/\mathcal M(A))=\mathcal M(B)$.}
{$\operatorname{Int}(\mathcal M(B)/\mathcal M(A))=\mathcal M(B)$。}
\item \bi{$B$ is a finite $A$-module and its given norm is equivalent to its canonical finite-module norm.}
{$B$ 是有限 $A$-模，且其给定范数与典范有限模范数等价。}
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The zero algebra is immediate. Suppose first that every point is interior. For $y\in\mathcal M(B)$ choose a witnessing norm-admissible relative presentation $A\{r_y^{-1}T_y\}\twoheadrightarrow B$. Choose radii $s_{yj}$ with $|T_{yj}(y)|<s_{yj}<r_{yj}$. The Weierstrass domain}
{零代数的情形直接成立。先假设所有点都是内点。对 $y\in\mathcal M(B)$，选择证明其为内点的 norm-admissible 相对表示 $A\{r_y^{-1}T_y\}\twoheadrightarrow B$。选择半径 $s_{yj}$，使 $|T_{yj}(y)|<s_{yj}<r_{yj}$。Weierstrass 域}
\[
 V_y=\{z\in\mathcal M(B): |T_{yj}(z)|\leq s_{yj}\text{ for every }j\}
\]
\bi{contains an ordinary neighborhood of $y$. Compactness gives a finite family of these domains covering $\mathcal M(B)$. Only finitely many presentation radii and witness radii now occur. Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification} with all these radii, adjoining one additional nonunit radius if $K$ is trivially valued. We obtain a finite free-radius extension $F=K_u$, nontrivially valued, over which $A_F$ is strict and all witness radii $r_{yj}$ belong to $\sqrt{|F^\times|}$.}
{包含 $y$ 的一个普通拓扑邻域。由紧性可选有限个这样的域覆盖 $\mathcal M(B)$。此时只涉及有限个表示半径及见证半径。对这些半径应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification}；若 $K$ 的赋值平凡，则额外加入一个不等于一的半径。得到有限自由半径扩张 $F=K_u$，其赋值非平凡，且 $A_F$ 为 strict，所有见证半径 $r_{yj}$ 都属于 $\sqrt{|F^\times|}$。}

\bi{For each such radius choose an integer $m>0$ and $c\in F^\times$ with $r_{yj}^m=|c|$. Adjoin a root of $X^m-c$, successively for the finitely many radii. Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} makes the resulting finite field extension $L/F$ complete, and multiplicativity gives an element of absolute value $r_{yj}$ in $L$. After this base change each witness presentation can be rescaled to unit radii. The inverse-image domains still cover by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange}; on each such domain the rescaled variables have spectral radius at most $s_{yj}/r_{yj}<1$. Relative presentations commute with this base change by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout}. Thus Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteinnercovercriterion} shows that $B_L$ is finite over $A_L$.}
{对每个这样的半径，选择整数 $m>0$ 及 $c\in F^\times$，使 $r_{yj}^m=|c|$。依次添入多项式 $X^m-c$ 的一个根，只需有限次。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}，所得有限域扩张 $L/F$ 完备；绝对值的乘法性说明 $L$ 中有绝对值为 $r_{yj}$ 的元素。作此基变换后，每个见证表示都可缩放为单位半径。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange}，各逆像域仍构成覆盖；在相应的域上，缩放后变量的谱半径至多为 $s_{yj}/r_{yj}<1$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout}，相对表示与此基变换相容。因此 Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteinnercovercriterion} 说明 $B_L$ 在 $A_L$ 上有限。}

\bi{We descend this finiteness in two steps. Choose an $F$-basis of $L$ containing $1$. Equivalence of finite-dimensional norms shows directly that, for every Banach $F$-space $M$, the completed tensor $M\widehat\otimes_F L$ is a finite direct sum of copies of $M$, with equivalent coefficient norms. Consequently $A_L$ is finite over $A_F$, and the coefficient projection $L\to F$ taking $1$ to $1$ induces a bounded $A_F$-linear retraction $B_L\to B_F$. Hence $B_F$ is a direct summand of the finite $A_F$-module $B_L$ and is finite. Since $F$ is nontrivially valued, its given Banach norm is canonical by the open-mapping argument. Theorem~\ref{fnd:freebanachfinitegenerationdescent}, applied to the original Banach $A$-module $B$, now gives finite generation and the canonical norm over the original field $K$. A bounded action can first be made contractive by the equivalent norm specified at the start of this section; the conclusion is unchanged.}
{分两步下降有限性。选择包含 $1$ 的一个 $F$-基。有限维范数的等价性直接说明：对任意 Banach $F$-空间 $M$，完备张量积 $M\widehat\otimes_F L$ 是有限份 $M$ 的直和，带等价的系数范数。因此 $A_L$ 在 $A_F$ 上有限，而把 $1$ 送到 $1$ 的系数投影 $L\to F$ 诱导有界 $A_F$-线性收缩 $B_L\to B_F$。所以 $B_F$ 是有限 $A_F$-模 $B_L$ 的直和因子，因而有限。由于 $F$ 的赋值非平凡，开映射论证说明其给定 Banach 范数为典范范数。最后对原 Banach $A$-模 $B$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:freebanachfinitegenerationdescent}，便在原基域 $K$ 上得到有限生成及典范范数。有界作用可先用本节开头的等价范数化为压缩作用，这不改变结论。}

\bi{Conversely, if $B$ is finite with its canonical norm, Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras} supplies one relative affinoid presentation whose generator radii strictly exceed their norms. It witnesses interiority at every point.}
{反之，若 $B$ 有限且带有典范范数，则 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras} 给出一个相对 affinoid 表示，使每个生成元的半径都严格大于其范数。该表示同时证明所有点都是内点。}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[\bi{Almost finite generation over the integral coefficient ring}{整系数环上的几乎有限生成}]
\label{fnd:integralalmostfinitegeneration}
\bi{Let $E$ be a finite $A_0$-module and $N\subset E$ any submodule. For every real $0<\alpha<1$ there is a finite $A_0$-submodule $N_0\subset N$ such that $aN\subset N_0$ for every $a\in O$ with $|a|\leq\alpha$.}
{设 $E$ 为有限 $A_0$-模，$N\subset E$ 为任意子模。对每个实数 $0<\alpha<1$，存在有限 $A_0$-子模 $N_0\subset N$，使所有满足 $|a|\leq\alpha$ 的 $a\in O$ 都满足 $aN\subset N_0$。}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
\bi{It suffices to prove the assertion over $P_n=O\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle$: take a finite free presentation above $E$ and then pass to its image. We use the Gauss norm inherited from $K\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle$. The coordinate changes in Lemma~\ref{fnd:distinguishedcoordinates} preserve $P_n$. The norm bounds in Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachweierstrassdivision} therefore give division within $P_n$ by a normalized distinguished element.}
{只需在 $P_n=O\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle$ 上证明：在 $E$ 上方取有限自由模的满射，最后取像即可。使用从 $K\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle$ 继承的 Gauss 范数。Lemma~\ref{fnd:distinguishedcoordinates} 的坐标变换保持 $P_n$；Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachweierstrassdivision} 的范数估计因而使归一化 distinguished 元素的除法保持在 $P_n$ 中。}
