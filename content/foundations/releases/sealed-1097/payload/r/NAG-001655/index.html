Proper analytic morphisms are separated

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 447

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Proper analytic morphisms are separated}
{适当解析态射分离}]
\label{fnd:properanalyticseparated}
\bi{A compact boundaryless morphism of arbitrary analytic
spaces is separated.}
{任意解析空间之间的紧且无边界的态射均分离。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose an affinoid chart covering $(X_i)$ of the target and
put $Y_i=f^{-1}(X_i)$. Since each $X_i$ is compact Hausdorff,
compactness makes $Y_i$ compact Hausdorff. For each \(y\in
Y_i\), test the boundarylessness of \(f\) on the actual affinoid
domain \(X_i\) in Definition~\ref{fnd:alltargetrelativeinterior}.
It gives an affinoid neighbourhood of \(y\) in \(Y_i\), so
\(Y_i\) is good. Lemma~\ref{fnd:goodhausdorffseparated}
therefore makes $Y_i\to X_i$ separated.}
{选取目标的 affinoid chart 覆盖 $(X_i)$，并令
$Y_i=f^{-1}(X_i)$。每个 $X_i$ 紧且 Hausdorff，因此紧性
说明 $Y_i$ 也紧且 Hausdorff。对每个 \(y\in Y_i\)，
在 Definition~\ref{fnd:alltargetrelativeinterior} 中用实际
affinoid 域 \(X_i\) 测试 \(f\) 的无边界性，可得到 \(y\) 在
\(Y_i\) 中的 affinoid 邻域，故 \(Y_i\) 为 good。
Lemma~\ref{fnd:goodhausdorffseparated} 于是给出
$Y_i\to X_i$ 分离。}

\bi{Let $P=Y\times_XY$. The domains
$P_i=Y_i\times_{X_i}Y_i$ form an admissible covering of $P$:
they are the inverse images of the target chart covering under
$P\to X$, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticproducts} and
analytic-domain base change. The pullback of the full diagonal
to $P_i$ is the closed diagonal of $Y_i\to X_i$.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:closedimmersiontargetglocal} glues these closed
immersions over the whole product, proving the assertion.}
{令 $P=Y\times_XY$。各域
$P_i=Y_i\times_{X_i}Y_i$ 构成 $P$ 的 admissible 覆盖：
由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticproducts} 及解析域基变换，
它们就是目标 chart 覆盖沿 $P\to X$ 的逆像。
整个对角线向 $P_i$ 的拉回，正是 $Y_i\to X_i$ 的闭对角线。
Lemma~\ref{fnd:closedimmersiontargetglocal} 在整个乘积上
粘合这些闭浸入，证明结论。}
\end{proof}
