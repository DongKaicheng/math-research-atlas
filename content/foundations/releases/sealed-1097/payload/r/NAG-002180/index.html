Refinement and products of balanced complexes

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 862

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Refinement and products of balanced complexes}
{平衡复形的细分与直积}]
\label{cand:polyhedralrefinementproduct}
\bi{Finite rational polyhedral complexes admit common rational
refinements. Balancing is invariant under refinement, and the
Cartesian product of two balanced cycles, with product weights,
is balanced. The star at a face and the quotient of that star by
the direction space of the face are balanced.}
{有限有理多面复形存在共同有理细分。平衡性在细分下不变；
两个平衡循环的直积配以权重之积后仍平衡。一个面的星形复形，
以及它对该面方向空间的商，均平衡。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Take all affine supporting hyperplanes of the finitely many
faces, and cut every polyhedron by them. The resulting sign cells
and their faces form a common refinement. At a new codimension-one
face lying inside an old facet, there are two incident pieces with
the same weight and opposite primitive normals. At a subdivision
of an old codimension-one face, the old normal equation is
unchanged. All other new faces have smaller dimension. These
observations prove refinement invariance in both directions.}
{收集有限个面的全部仿射支撑超平面，以它们切割每个多面体。
所得符号胞腔及其各面构成共同细分。若新增余维一面位于原
极大胞腔内部，则它的两侧权重相同而本原法向相反。若它细分
原余维一面，则原法向等式保持不变。其余新增面维数更低。
这些观察同时证明平衡性在细分前后的等价。}

\bi{A codimension-one face of a product is either
$\tau\times\rho$ or $\sigma\times\nu$, where exactly one factor
has codimension one. Its normal equation is the normal equation
in that factor multiplied by the weight of the other factor.
For a star, the normals and weights are the original ones at the
faces containing the chosen face. Its direction lattice is
saturated, so passage to the quotient preserves the primitive
normal quotient lattices and the balancing equations.}
{直积中的余维一面形如 $\tau\times\rho$ 或
$\sigma\times\nu$，其中恰有一个因子余维为一。其法向等式
是该因子的等式乘以另一因子的权重。对星形复形，法向与权重
就是原复形中包含所选面的那些数据。该面的方向格饱和，故
取商保持本原法向所在的商格及平衡等式。}
\end{proof}
