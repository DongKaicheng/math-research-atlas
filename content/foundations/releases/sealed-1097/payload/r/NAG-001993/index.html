Strict complete continuity into a closed target

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 411

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Strict complete continuity into a closed target}{进入闭目标的严格完全连续性}]
\label{fnd:strictcompactclosedtarget}
\bi{Let $E=c_0(I,A)$ with its maximum norm and standard basis $(e_\lambda)_{\lambda\in I}$. Let $j:N\to F$ identify a Banach module with a closed submodule, with equivalent induced norm. If $g:E\to N$ is bounded and $jg$ is strictly completely continuous, then $g$ is strictly completely continuous.}
{令 $E=c_0(I,A)$，带最大范数和标准基 $(e_\lambda)_{\lambda\in I}$。设 $j:N\to F$ 将一个 Banach 模识别为闭子模，且诱导范数与原范数等价。若 $g:E\to N$ 有界，且 $jg$ 严格完全连续，则 $g$ 也严格完全连续。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use the induced norm on $N$. Multiplication of the maps by one fixed nonzero scalar, followed by division by that scalar at the end, reduces to the case that $g$ is contractive and approximants $h:E\to F$ satisfy $h(E_{\leq1})\subset L\subset F_{\leq1}$ for a finite $A_0$-module $L$. This rescaling also shows directly that equivalent norms and bounded pre- or postcomposition preserve strict complete continuity: multiply the fixed scalar by a sufficiently small scalar to absorb each uniform operator bound.}
{在 $N$ 上使用诱导范数。先把映射都乘一个固定非零标量，最后再除回去，就可归约到 $g$ 为压缩映射，且逼近 $h:E\to F$ 满足 $h(E_{\leq1})\subset L\subset F_{\leq1}$，其中 $L$ 为有限 $A_0$-模。这种缩放也直接说明，等价范数以及有界的前后复合都保持严格完全连续性：只需再乘一个足够小的固定标量以吸收相应的统一算子界。}

\bi{Fix $a\in O$ with $0<|a|<1$. Given $\varepsilon>0$, choose such an $h$ with $\|jg-h\|\leq\varepsilon$. Apply Lemma~\ref{fnd:integralalmostfinitegeneration} to $h(E_{\leq1})\subset L$. It gives generators $y_1,\ldots,y_s\in h(E_{\leq1})$ of a finite submodule containing $a h(E_{\leq1})$. Choose $x_j\in E_{\leq1}$ with $h(x_j)=y_j$ and put $z_j=g(x_j)\in N_{\leq1}$. Then $\|jz_j-y_j\|\leq\varepsilon$. For each $\lambda$, choose coefficients $b_{j\lambda}\in A$ satisfying}
{固定 $a\in O$，使 $0<|a|<1$。给定 $\varepsilon>0$，选择上述 $h$，使 $\|jg-h\|\leq\varepsilon$。对 $h(E_{\leq1})\subset L$ 应用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:integralalmostfinitegeneration}，得到有限子模的生成元 $y_1,\ldots,y_s\in h(E_{\leq1})$，该子模包含 $a h(E_{\leq1})$。选择 $x_j\in E_{\leq1}$，满足 $h(x_j)=y_j$，并置 $z_j=g(x_j)\in N_{\leq1}$。于是 $\|jz_j-y_j\|\leq\varepsilon$。对每个 $\lambda$ 选择系数 $b_{j\lambda}\in A$，使}
\[
 h(e_\lambda)=\sum_j b_{j\lambda}y_j,
 \qquad \|b_{j\lambda}\|\leq |a|^{-1}.
\]
\bi{Define $g_0(e_\lambda)=\sum_jb_{j\lambda}z_j$. The uniform coefficient bound extends this rule to a bounded $A$-linear map on $c_0(I,A)$, with image in the finite module generated by the $z_j$. The basis estimate gives $\|g-g_0\|\leq |a|^{-1}\varepsilon$. If $x=\sum_\lambda d_\lambda e_\lambda$ has norm at most one, the series $\sum_\lambda a d_\lambda b_{j\lambda}$ converge in $A_0$, so}
{定义 $g_0(e_\lambda)=\sum_jb_{j\lambda}z_j$。统一系数界将它延拓为 $c_0(I,A)$ 上的有界 $A$-线性映射，其像包含在由 $z_j$ 生成的有限模中。标准基上的估计给出 $\|g-g_0\|\leq |a|^{-1}\varepsilon$。若 $x=\sum_\lambda d_\lambda e_\lambda$ 的范数不超过一，则级数 $\sum_\lambda a d_\lambda b_{j\lambda}$ 在 $A_0$ 中收敛，因此}
\[
 a g_0(E_{\leq1})\subset\sum_j A_0z_j\subset N_{\leq1}.
\]
\bi{The same fixed $a$ works for every $\varepsilon$, proving strict complete continuity.}
{同一个固定的 $a$ 适用于所有 $\varepsilon$，故严格完全连续性得证。}
\end{proof}
