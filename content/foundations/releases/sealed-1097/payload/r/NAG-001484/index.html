Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Finite base change}{有限基变换}]
\label{fnd:strictfinitealgebrabasechange}
\bi{Let $A\to B$ and $A\to C$ be bounded maps of strict $K$-affinoid algebras, with $B$ finite as an $A$-module. Then the ordinary algebra $D=B\otimes_A C$ is a strict affinoid, finite over $C$, and the natural map}
{设 $A\to B$ 与 $A\to C$ 为 strict $K$-affinoid 代数之间的有界映射，且 $B$ 为有限 $A$-模。则普通代数 $D=B\otimes_A C$ 为 strict affinoid，且在 $C$ 上有限；自然映射}
\[
 B\otimes_A C\longrightarrow B\widehat\otimes_A C
\]
\bi{is a bounded algebra isomorphism when the left side has its canonical finite-$C$-module norm. Both structural arrows into $D$ are bounded.}
{在左边配备典范有限 $C$-模范数时，是有界代数同构。映入 $D$ 的两个结构映射均有界。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebras} identifies the given norm on $B$ with its finite-$A$-module norm. Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitetensor}, applied to that module and the target $C$, shows that the ordinary tensor product is already complete with its canonical finite-$C$-module norm. As a finite $C$-algebra it is strict by Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebras}. The tensor maps $b\mapsto b\otimes1$ and $c\mapsto1\otimes c$ are bounded for the projective tensor norm, hence for the equivalent norm just identified. The multiplication is the ordinary tensor-algebra multiplication and extends without change to its completion.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebras}，$B$ 的给定范数与有限 $A$-模范数等价。对该模及目标 $C$ 应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitetensor}，说明普通张量积在典范有限 $C$-模范数下已经完备。作为有限 $C$-代数，由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebras} 它为 strict。张量映射 $b\mapsto b\otimes1$ 与 $c\mapsto1\otimes c$ 在投射张量范数下有界，因而在刚才识别的等价范数下也有界。其乘法就是普通张量代数的乘法，完成后不发生改变。}
\end{proof}
