Coherent restriction on ordinary stalks

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 426

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Coherent restriction on ordinary stalks}{通常茎上的凝聚限制}]
\label{fnd:coherentdomainstalkrestriction}
\bi{Let $U\subset Y$ be an analytic domain, with $U,Y$ good, and let $\mathcal F$ be coherent on $Y_G$. For every $y\in U$, the canonical map gives}
{设 $U\subset Y$ 为解析域，$U,Y$ 均 good，且 $\mathcal F$ 是 $Y_G$ 上的凝聚层。对每个 $y\in U$，典范映射给出}
\[
 (\mathcal F|_{U_G})_y
       \simeq\mathcal F_y\otimes_{\mathcal O_{Y,y}}\mathcal O_{U,y}.
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose affinoid neighborhoods $Y_0$ in $Y$ and $U_0$ in $U$ with $U_0\subset Y_0$. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb}, $\mathcal F|_{Y_0}$ comes from a finite module $M$ over $A=\mathcal O(Y_0)$. On every affinoid domain $V\subset Y_0$ its sections are $M\otimes_AA_V$. Ordinary stalks are filtered limits over affinoid neighborhoods, by Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks}; the same proof applies to these module sheaves. Tensor products commute with filtered colimits, so the two stalks are respectively $M\otimes_A\mathcal O_{Y,y}$ and $M\otimes_A\mathcal O_{U,y}$. Associativity of tensor products gives the displayed isomorphism.}
{选择 $Y$ 中的 affinoid 邻域 $Y_0$ 及 $U$ 中的 affinoid 邻域 $U_0$，使 $U_0\subset Y_0$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb}，$\mathcal F|_{Y_0}$ 来自 $A=\mathcal O(Y_0)$ 上的有限模 $M$。在每个 affinoid 域 $V\subset Y_0$ 上，其截面为 $M\otimes_AA_V$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks}，通常茎是在 affinoid 邻域上的滤过直极限；同一证明适用于这些模层。张量积与滤过直极限交换，所以两个茎分别为 $M\otimes_A\mathcal O_{Y,y}$ 及 $M\otimes_A\mathcal O_{U,y}$。张量积结合律即给出所列同构。}
\end{proof}
