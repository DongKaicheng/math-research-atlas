Gluing across a circle

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 127

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Gluing across a circle}{跨过一个 circle 的 gluing}]\label{fnd:laurentgluing}
\bi{Let $0<s<1$. Cover the closed unit disc by $V_- =\{|T|\leq s\}$ and $V_+=\{s\leq|T|\leq1\}$. With}
{设 $0<s<1$。用 $V_- =\{|T|\leq s\}$ 和 $V_+=\{s\leq|T|\leq1\}$ 覆盖 closed unit disc。记}
\[
A_-=K\{s^{-1}T\},\quad A_+=K\{T,sT^{-1}\},\quad
C=K\{s^{-1}T,sT^{-1}\}.
\]
\bi{The sequence $0\to K\langle T\rangle\to A_-\oplus A_+\to C\to0$, with last map $(f_-,f_+)\mapsto f_--f_+$, is exact.}
{Sequence $0\to K\langle T\rangle\to A_-\oplus A_+\to C\to0$ 为 exact，其中最后的 map 为 $(f_-,f_+)\mapsto f_--f_+$。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A Laurent series in $C$ splits into its nonnegative and negative powers. The nonnegative part converges on $V_-$; the negative part converges on $V_+$ because its worst bound is attained at radius $s$. This proves surjectivity of the difference map. If two restrictions agree on the circle, uniqueness of Laurent coefficients shows that the annular series has no negative powers. Its nonnegative coefficients tend to zero at radius one, since it belongs to $A_+$. They therefore define an element of $K\langle T\rangle$ restricting to both functions. Injectivity follows from uniqueness of the coefficients.}
{$C$ 中的 Laurent series 可分成 nonnegative powers 与 negative powers。Nonnegative part 在 $V_-$ 上收敛；negative part 在 $V_+$ 上收敛，因为其最强 bound 出现在 radius $s$。这证明 difference map 的 surjectivity。若两个 restrictions 在 circle 上一致，Laurent coefficients 的 uniqueness 说明 annular series 没有 negative powers。由于它属于 $A_+$，其 nonnegative coefficients 在 radius one 处趋于零，故定义 $K\langle T\rangle$ 的一个元素，限制到两侧正是给定 functions。Injectivity 同样由 coefficients 的 uniqueness 得到。}
\end{proof}
