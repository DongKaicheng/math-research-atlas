One-sided relation operators

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 181

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{One-sided relation operators}{单边关系算子}]\label{fnd:elementaryrelationinjectivity}
\bi{Let $A$ be a nonzero strict norm-admissible $K$-affinoid algebra, let $M$ be a finite $A$-module with a canonical Banach norm, and let $f\in A$ and $r>0$. On the one-sided coefficient spaces}
{设 $A$ 为非零 strict norm-admissible $K$-affinoid 代数，$M$ 为配备 canonical Banach 范数的有限 $A$-模，且 $f\in A$、$r>0$。在单边系数空间}
\[
 E_+(M)=M\{r^{-1}T\},\qquad
 E_-(M)=M\{rU\}
\]
\bi{the bounded $A$-linear operators}
{上，有界 $A$-线性算子}
\[
 Q_+=T-f:E_+(M)\to E_+(M),\qquad
 Q_-=1-fU:E_-(M)\to E_-(M)
\]
\bi{are injective. No value-group restriction on $r$ is needed for this injectivity statement.}
{均为单射。这个单射性结论不需要 $r$ 属于值群。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First give $M$ a quotient norm from a finite free presentation, so that its $A$-action is contractive. Embed both spaces isometrically into the bilateral coefficient module $E_r(M)$ of Section~\ref{fnd:t02laurentoperators}, by sending $T$ to $Z$ and $U$ to $Z^{-1}$. Their images consist of the nonnegative and the nonpositive powers of $Z$, respectively. Multiplication by $Z$ is a bounded automorphism of $E_r(M)$; its norm scales by $r$ and its inverse scales by $r^{-1}$. Under these embeddings the two operators become}
{先给 $M$ 配备一个有限自由表示的商范数，使其 $A$-作用为 contractive。将两个空间等距嵌入 Section~\ref{fnd:t02laurentoperators} 中的双边系数模 $E_r(M)$，分别令 $T$ 对应 $Z$、$U$ 对应 $Z^{-1}$。其像分别由 $Z$ 的非负次幂与非正次幂组成。乘以 $Z$ 是 $E_r(M)$ 的有界自同构：它将范数乘以 $r$，其逆将范数乘以 $r^{-1}$。在这些嵌入下，两个算子分别成为}
\[
 Z-f=Z(1-fZ^{-1}),\qquad 1-fZ^{-1}.
\]
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictlaurentinjectivity} makes $1-fZ^{-1}$ injective for every $r>0$, so both restrictions are injective. Their boundedness follows from the shift bounds and the bounded action of $f$. Finally, replacing the quotient norm on $M$ by a uniformly equivalent canonical norm leaves each coefficient space unchanged as a set and gives a bounded identity map with bounded inverse. It therefore preserves boundedness and injectivity of the same algebraic operators.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictlaurentinjectivity}，对每个 $r>0$，$1-fZ^{-1}$ 都为单射，所以两个限制算子均单射。其有界性来自 shift 的界与 $f$ 的有界作用。最后，将 $M$ 的商范数换成一致等价的 canonical 范数，不改变每个系数空间作为集合的内容，且恒等映射及其逆都有界。因此同一代数算子的有界性与单射性保持成立。}
\end{proof}

\bi{Here an algebraic $R$-module $N$ is flat if the ordinary tensor functor $-\otimes_R N$ preserves exact sequences; a ring map $R\to S$ is flat when $S$ is flat as an $R$-module. These are the definitions in \cite[Tag 00HB, Definition 10.39.1(1),(3)]{StacksAlg}. No completion or norm is part of this definition.}
{这里称代数 $R$-模 $N$ 为平坦的，是指普通张量积函子 $-\otimes_R N$ 保持正合列；称环映射 $R\to S$ 平坦，是指 $S$ 作为 $R$-模平坦。这采用 \cite[Tag 00HB, Definition 10.39.1(1),(3)]{StacksAlg} 的定义。该定义不涉及完备化或范数。}
