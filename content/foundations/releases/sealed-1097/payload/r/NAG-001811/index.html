\begin{corollary}[\bi{The universal good-space test}{普适的 good 空间测试}]
\label{fnd:universalboundarylesstest}
\bi{A morphism $f:Y\to X$ is boundaryless if and only if, for every complete valued extension $L/K$ and every morphism $X'\to X$ from a good $L$-analytic space, the space $Y'=Y\times_X X'$ is good and the induced good-space morphism $Y'\to X'$ is boundaryless.}
{态射 $f:Y\to X$ 无边界，当且仅当对每个完备赋值扩张 $L/K$ 及每个来自 good $L$-解析空间的态射 $X'\to X$，空间 $Y'=Y\times_X X'$ 都是 good，且所得 good 空间态射 $Y'\to X'$ 无边界。}
\end{corollary}
