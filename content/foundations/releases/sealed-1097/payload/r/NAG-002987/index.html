A descended polarization in the exact tame Kummer class

F Descent of an algebraized polarized tower

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1982

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[A descended polarization in the exact tame Kummer class]
\label{cand:f5eq:tamedescent}
Let $K$ be complete and nontrivially real-valued, with valuation
ring $O$, residue field $k$, and value group $\Gamma$.
Assume the exact coefficient class of
\dep{cand:f5tame:charactercriterion}: $e\geq2$ is invertible in
$O$, $\zeta\in O$ and its residue have order $e$, and
\[
 L=K(s),\quad s^e=c\in K^\times,\quad \gamma=v(s)>0,
 \qquad i\gamma\notin\Gamma\quad(0<i<e).
 \tag{K}
\]
Put $O'=L^\circ$ and $G=\langle\sigma\rangle$, where
$\sigma(s)=\zeta s$. Choose $0\ne\pi\in\mathfrak m_O$.
Suppose a cartesian proper flat finitely presented tower over
$O'/\pi^nO'$ with compatible semilinear $G$-actions satisfies
the nodal and full special-fibre identity hypotheses of
Theorem~\ref{cand:f5eq:equivariantalgebraization}.
Then there exist a projective, flat, finitely presented nodal
$O$-scheme $Y$ of relative dimension one and a relatively ample
invertible sheaf $\mathcal P$ on $Y$, with compatible equivariant
polarized isomorphisms
\[
 (Y,\mathcal P)\times_O(O'/\pi^nO')
              \simeq (X_n,\mathcal N_n).
 \tag{6}
\]
Here $\mathcal N_n$ is the chosen orbit-tensor tower (4).
The algebraized bundle $\mathcal N'$ itself descends to
$\mathcal P$; no further tensor power of $\mathcal N'$ is needed.
In particular $\mathcal P|_{Y_k}$ identifies with
$\mathcal A_C^{\otimes e}$ under the induced residue identification.
The reductions $(Y,\mathcal P)\times_O O/\pi^nO$ form the
corresponding descended polarized tower.
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The coefficient criterion identifies the residue fields of $O$
and $O'$ with $k$, gives identity coefficient action on $k$, and
uses the actual complete valued field $L$ and its valuation ring.
Thus Theorem~\ref{cand:f5eq:equivariantalgebraization}, applied
over $O'$, first supplies an algebraic projective nodal pair
$(Y',\mathcal N')$ with the specified semilinear action and full
residual identities. This step uses no ramified descent.
For clarity, with $b_g=\operatorname{Spec}(g^{-1})$ the induced
left action on functions is $g\cdot a=\rho_{g^{-1}}^*a$, and on
linearized sections it is
$g\cdot t=\lambda_{g^{-1}}(\rho_{g^{-1}}^*t)$.
Thus its coefficient action on $O'$ is exactly $g$, as in the
cited tame descent results.

Theorem~\ref{cand:f5proj:projectivedescent}
applies to $Y'/O'$. It supplies a projective flat finitely presented
nodal $Y/O$ and an equivariant isomorphism
$Y\times_O O'\simeq Y'$, with the prescribed coefficient action
on the base change. Properness is retained: $Y'$ is projective
and proper, and the cited result descends properness before
concluding projectivity.

Under this identification, $\mathcal N'$ is an already existing
$G$-linearized invertible sheaf on $Y\times_O O'$ whose action
on the entire special-fibre module is the identity. Apply
\dep{cand:f5proj:linebundledescent} to this bundle itself.
For $p:Y\times_O O'\to Y$, it gives
\[
              \mathcal P=(p_*\mathcal N')^G,
                \qquad p^*\mathcal P\simeq\mathcal N',
 \tag{7}
\]
and makes $\mathcal P$ relatively ample. There is no need to
replace $\mathcal N'$ by a second orbit tensor. The scheme and
bundle descents are unique with their specified identifications,
by the two cited results.

Reduce the algebraic isomorphisms just constructed modulo
$\pi^n$ and use the level comparisons (5) of
Theorem~\ref{cand:f5eq:equivariantalgebraization}.
This gives (6), with all transition maps
and linearizations, and then the asserted residue polarization.
The $O/\pi^nO$ reductions of the actual pair $(Y,\mathcal P)$
are automatically cartesian, proper, flat and finitely presented.
Their base changes to $O'/\pi^nO'$ are exactly the supplied
polarized levels. In particular no valuation-ring descent theorem
has been applied directly to the non-valuation quotient rings
$O'/\pi^nO'$.
\end{proof}
