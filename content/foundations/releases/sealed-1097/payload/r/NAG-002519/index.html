The full finite Gauss degree

C Native scalar extension and finite restriction of constants

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1765

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[The full finite Gauss degree]
\label{f9cand:finite-gauss}
For finite $L/K$ and every real positive $r$,
\[
 H_{K,r}\otimes_K L=H_{L,r},\qquad
 [H_{L,r}:H_{K,r}]=[L:K]=n .
\]
The natural maps are bounded isomorphisms with bounded inverses.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The coefficient bounds in Lemma~\ref{f9cand:constants} give
\[
 R_{L,r}=\bigoplus_i e_iR_{K,r},\qquad
 c\max_i\|a_i\|_r\leq\left\|\sum_i e_i a_i\right\|_r
                        \leq C\max_i\|a_i\|_r .
\]
Both tails and uniqueness are controlled by these inequalities.
Both Laurent algebras are domains by multiplicativity of their
Gauss norms [\ref{v036:gaussbasechange}, \ref{v036:flatgermweight}]. Localizing $R_{L,r}$ over
$F=\Frac(R_{K,r})$ gives a finite-dimensional $F$-domain, hence a
field, necessarily $\Frac(R_{L,r})$. The $e_i$ remain a basis.
Clearing a common denominator extends the two bounds to coefficients
in $F$. Completion consequently gives $\bigoplus_i e_iH_{K,r}$,
with the tensor-product multiplication, and this completion is
$H_{L,r}$. This proves both the isomorphism and the full degree.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Finite restriction of constants}\label{sec:finite}
\begin{lemma}[The analytic space with finite constants restricted]
\label{f9cand:constants}
Suppose $n=[L:K]<\infty$. Every generalized $L$-affinoid algebra $B$
is a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra with the same Banach topology.
The two analytic spectra, domains, structure sheaves and completed
residue fields agree. On a good Hausdorff $L$-analytic space $Y$ this
gives $Y_{[K]}$, with the same ordinary moment sheaf and supports.
Purity of analytic dimension $d$ is unchanged.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix a basis $e_1,\ldots,e_n$ of $L/K$. By [\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}] there are
$c,C>0$ such that
\[
 c\max_i|a_i|\leq\left|\sum_i a_ie_i\right|_L
             \leq C\max_i|a_i| \quad(a_i\in K).
\]
