Pushouts of bounded strict-affinoid maps

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 84

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Pushouts of bounded strict-affinoid maps}{有界 strict-affinoid 映射的推出}]
\label{fnd:relativeaffinoidpushouts}
\bi{For any bounded maps $A\to B$, $A\to C$ of strict $K$-affinoids, the separated completed projective tensor product $B\widehat\otimes_A C$ is a strict affinoid, with zero allowed. It is the pushout for bounded homomorphisms into commutative Banach $K$-algebras. In relative presentations}
{对 strict $K$-affinoid 的任意有界映射 $A\to B$、$A\to C$，分离完备投射张量积 $B\widehat\otimes_A C$ 为 strict affinoid，允许零代数。它是以交换 Banach $K$-代数为目标、有界同态为态射的推出。在相对表示}
\[
 B=A\langle T\rangle/I,\qquad C=A\langle S\rangle/J
\]
\bi{it is canonically $A\langle T,S\rangle/(I,J)$, with bounded inverse comparisons for the given norms. If the displayed quotient norms are used, this identification is isometric. No claim that the ordinary tensor product is complete is made.}
{下，它典范地等于 $A\langle T,S\rangle/(I,J)$，且对给定范数比较映射及其逆均有界。若使用所列商范数，则该识别为等距同构。这里不声称普通张量积已经完备。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Lemma~\ref{fnd:relativeaffinoidpresentation} supplies these presentations with equivalent quotient norms. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpresentationpushout} gives exactly the asserted tensor quotient and its isometry for those norms. Uniform equivalence of the original factor norms passes to projective seminorms and separated completions. For the universal property, two bounded maps $B,C\to E$ agreeing on $A$ give a bounded balanced bilinear map $(b,c)\mapsto b c$ into $E$. On a finite tensor expression its norm is bounded by a fixed constant times the maximum of $\|b\|\|c\|$; taking the infimum and completing gives a bounded algebra map. It is unique on the dense algebraic tensor image. This argument also covers a zero tensor quotient: boundedness forces the balanced map to vanish on every seminorm-zero tensor.}
{Lemma~\ref{fnd:relativeaffinoidpresentation} 给出具有等价商范数的上述表示。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpresentationpushout} 恰好给出所述张量商及在这些范数下的等距性。原来两个因子范数的一致等价性传递到投射半范数和分离完备化。对泛性质，两个在 $A$ 上相同的有界映射 $B,C\to E$ 给出有界平衡双线性映射 $(b,c)\mapsto bc$。对有限张量表达式，其像的范数被某个固定常数乘以 $\|b\|\|c\|$ 的最大值控制；取下确界并完备化，得到有界代数映射。稠密的代数张量像保证其唯一性。这个论证也包括零张量商：有界性迫使平衡映射在所有半范数为零的张量上消失。}
\end{proof}
