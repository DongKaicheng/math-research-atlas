Kiehl equivalence on the arbitrary-radius rational site

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 373

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Kiehl equivalence on the arbitrary-radius rational site}{任意半径 rational site 上的 Kiehl 等价}]
\label{fnd:generalizedrationalkiehl}
\bi{On $(\mathcal R_X,\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}})$ the following conditions on a module sheaf $\mathcal F$ are equivalent: coherence in the sense of \cite[Tag 03DL, Definition 18.23.1(8)]{StacksAlg}; local finite presentation; possession of finite module models on some finite rational covering; and existence of a finite $A$-module $M$ with $\mathcal F\simeq\widetilde M$. Thus}
{在 $(\mathcal R_X,\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}})$ 上，模层 $\mathcal F$ 的下列条件等价：按 \cite[Tag 03DL, Definition 18.23.1(8)]{StacksAlg} 的定义为凝聚层；局部有限表示；在某个有限 rational 覆盖上具有有限模模型；以及存在有限 $A$-模 $M$ 使 $\mathcal F\simeq\widetilde M$。因此}
\[
 \operatorname{Mod}^{\mathrm{fin}}(A)
       \ \simeq\ \operatorname{Coh}(\mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}}),
 \qquad M\longmapsto\widetilde M,
 \qquad\mathcal F\longmapsto\mathcal F(X)
\]
\bi{are inverse exact equivalences. Every coherent sheaf has a global finite free presentation. Its augmented \v Cech complex for every finite rational covering is norm-strict exact, with canonical norms on the section modules.}
{给出互逆的正合范畴等价。每个凝聚层都有整体有限自由表示；对任一有限 rational 覆盖，其增广 \v Cech 复形在截面模的典范范数下为 norm-strict 正合。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A coherent sheaf has finite local generators, and the kernels of the resulting maps from finite free sheaves are locally of finite type. Refining the finite cover supplies finite presentations. Each presentation on a slice is a matrix over $A_V$, since its columns are the images of the global basis sections. Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalslicemodules} identifies its cokernel sheaf with the sheaf of the finite matrix cokernel. Thus local finite presentation gives finite local module models. Their overlap sheaf isomorphisms give module isomorphisms by full faithfulness in the same lemma, with the required cocycle condition. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaldescent} produces a global finite module $M$ and compatible local isomorphisms with $\widetilde M$. Applying the sheaf equalizer on the covers $W\cap V_i$ glues these to an isomorphism on every object $W$.}
{凝聚层有有限个局部生成元，所得有限自由层映射的核局部有限型。加细有限覆盖即可取得有限表示。每个 slice 上的表示由 $A_V$ 上的矩阵给出，因为各列就是整体基截面的像。Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalslicemodules} 将其余核层识别为有限矩阵余核的层。因此局部有限表示给出有限局部模模型。由同一引理的全忠实性，交叠层同构对应为满足 cocycle 条件的模同构。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaldescent} 给出整体有限模 $M$ 及与 $\widetilde M$ 的相容局部同构。在各覆盖 $W\cap V_i$ 上应用层等化子，便将其粘合为每个对象 $W$ 上的同构。}

\bi{Conversely, a finite $A$-module has a finite presentation because $A$ is Noetherian by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}. Exactness of the module-sheaf functor gives a global finite free presentation of $\widetilde M$. For any $V$ and any map $\mathcal O_V^t\to\widetilde M|_V$, full faithfulness identifies it with a map $A_V^t\to A_V\otimes_A M$. Its kernel is finite by Noetherianity of $A_V$, and exactness identifies the sheaf kernel with its associated finite-module sheaf. This proves coherence. The same lemma gives exactness and full faithfulness, and global sections recover $M$. The \v Cech assertion follows from Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfiniterationalcech}.}
{反之，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}，$A$ Noetherian，故有限 $A$-模具有有限表示。模层函子的正合性给出 $\widetilde M$ 的整体有限自由表示。对任意 $V$ 及映射 $\mathcal O_V^t\to\widetilde M|_V$，全忠实性将其识别为 $A_V^t\to A_V\otimes_A M$。由 $A_V$ 的 Noetherian 性，其核有限；正合性再将层核识别为该有限模的层。这就证明凝聚性。同一引理给出正合性与全忠实性，整体截面恢复 $M$。\v Cech 断言来自 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfiniterationalcech}。}
\end{proof}
