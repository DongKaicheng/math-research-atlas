Raynaud equivalence

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 546

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Raynaud equivalence}{Raynaud 等价}]
\label{fnd:raynaudequivalenceinternal}
\bi{Let $\mathcal F$ be the category of quasi-paracompact
admissible formal $K^\circ$-schemes, and let $\mathcal S$
be the class of admissible blowups.
The generic-fibre functor induces an equivalence}
{令 $\mathcal F$ 为 quasi-paracompact admissible 形式
$K^\circ$-概形范畴，$\mathcal S$ 为 admissible blowup 类。
泛纤维函子诱导等价}
\[
 \mathcal F[\mathcal S^{-1}]
 \simeq
 \left\{\begin{gathered}
 \text{quasi-separated quasi-paracompact}\\
 \text{strict analytic $G$-spaces over }K
 \end{gathered}\right\}.
\]
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Every blowup is sent to an isomorphism by
\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:blowupinvarianceonsuppliedatlases]{generic invariance on supplied atlases}}.
Replacing $\mathcal S$ by its finite composites does not
change the localization: a functor inverts one class if and
only if it inverts the other.
Use roofs
$\mathfrak X\xleftarrow{s}\mathfrak X'\xrightarrow{f}\mathfrak Y$
with $s$ such a finite composite.
They can be composed by successively blowing up the
pullbacks of the second roof's denominator centers on
$\mathfrak X'$. The universal properties give the middle map,
and the new source map remains a finite composite.}
{由 \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:blowupinvarianceonsuppliedatlases]{generic invariance on supplied atlases}}，每个 blowup
都映为同构。以 $\mathcal S$ 的有限复合替换 $\mathcal S$ 不改变
局部化，因为一个函子逆转其中一类当且仅当它逆转另一类。
使用屋顶图
$\mathfrak X\xleftarrow{s}\mathfrak X'\xrightarrow{f}\mathfrak Y$，
其中 $s$ 为这样的有限复合。
它们可以复合：在 $\mathfrak X'$ 上依次吹起第二个屋顶图的
分母中心的拉回。各泛性质给出中间态射，新的源态射仍为有限复合。}

\bi{Two choices have a common refinement.
Their modified sources have identified generic fibres, so
\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:commonformalmodificationonsuppliedatlases]{common modification on supplied atlases}} gives modifications
from a common source to both. The maps to the original
source agree by generic-fibre faithfulness.
If the roofs have the same analytic map, their target maps
also agree on this refinement. Conversely, equality on a
common refinement implies equality of analytic maps.
\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:globalformalliftingonsuppliedatlases]{lifting on supplied atlases}} ensures that every
analytic map is represented by a roof.
Thus roofs modulo common refinement are in bijection with
the analytic morphisms between generic fibres. Taking common
refinements of the two iterated pullback constructions proves
associativity; the identity roof is the identity morphism.}
{两个选择具有共同加细。其修改后源的泛纤维已被识别，故
\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:commonformalmodificationonsuppliedatlases]{common modification on supplied atlases}} 给出共同源到二者的修改。
到原源的两个映射由泛纤维忠实性相同。若两个屋顶图给出同一解析
映射，则其到目标的映射在此加细上也相同；反之，共同加细上的
相同推出解析映射相同。
\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:globalformalliftingonsuppliedatlases]{lifting on supplied atlases}} 保证每个解析映射
都有屋顶图表示。因此模去共同加细后的屋顶图与泛纤维之间的解析
态射一一对应。对两种迭代拉回构造取共同加细得到结合律，
恒等屋顶图为恒等态射。}

\bi{This roof category has the localization universal property.
If $H:\mathcal F\to\mathcal D$ inverts blowups, send the roof
to $H(f)H(s)^{-1}$. Common refinements leave this expression
unchanged, and the pullback construction proves compatibility
with composition. This extension is unique, since every roof
is the composite of its forward map and its denominator's
inverse. Hence the category is $\mathcal F[\mathcal S^{-1}]$.
The preceding bijection of morphisms proves full faithfulness,
and Theorem~\ref{fnd:quasiparacompactformalmodel} proves
essential surjectivity.}
{这个屋顶图范畴具有局部化泛性质。若
$H:\mathcal F\to\mathcal D$ 逆转 blowup，就把屋顶图送到
$H(f)H(s)^{-1}$。共同加细不改变此表达式，拉回构造说明它与
复合相容。这个延拓唯一，因为每个屋顶图都是其正向态射与分母
逆之复合。故该范畴就是 $\mathcal F[\mathcal S^{-1}]$。
上述态射集合的双射证明全忠实性，
Theorem~\ref{fnd:quasiparacompactformalmodel} 证明本质满射性。}
\end{proof}
