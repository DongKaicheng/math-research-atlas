Geometric pushforward under a finite locally free map

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 966

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Geometric pushforward under a finite locally free map]
\label{v036:analyticgeometricpushforward}
Let $p:X\to Y$ be a finite morphism of generalized $K$-affinoid
spaces, both pure of analytic dimension $d$. Suppose its algebra
is finite locally free of constant rank $\Delta>0$ over the target
algebra, with the canonical module Banach structure. Let unit moment
tuples $f_X$ and $f_Y$ define $t_X:X\to\mathbb R^n$ and
$t_Y:Y\to\mathbb R^{n'}$, and suppose that
\[
                         t_Y\circ p=a\circ t_X
\]
comes from the corresponding identity of characters for an integral
map $a:\mathbb Z^n\to\mathbb Z^{n'}$.
Let $w'\in t_Y(Y)$ have its whole fibre in
$\operatorname{Int}(Y/K)$, and suppose that the local tropical image
at $w'$ is contained in
$w'+(N_Y)_{\mathbb R}$ for a saturated rank-$d$ lattice
$N_Y\subset\mathbb Z^{n'}$.
Assume
\[
                   a^{-1}(w')\cap t_X(X)=\{w_1,\ldots,w_s\}
\]
is finite and nonempty, and that the local tropical image at each
$w_i$ is contained in $w_i+(N_i)_{\mathbb R}$ for a saturated
rank-$d$ lattice $N_i\subset\mathbb Z^n$.

Then $a(N_i)\subset N_Y$ has finite index for every $i$, all the
weights in Theorem~\ref{v036:flatgermweight} are defined, and
\[
              \Delta\,m_{Y,f_Y}(w')
                   =\sum_{i=1}^s m_{X,f_X}(w_i)
                                      [N_Y:a(N_i)].
\]
The locally free rank, the fibre algebras, and their lengths are
taken before reduction. No restriction on the real weights or on
the characteristic or value group of $K$ is imposed.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras} makes $p$ everywhere
interior relative to $Y$. The composition inclusion in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus} therefore gives
\[
                p^{-1}(t_Y^{-1}(w'))\subset\operatorname{Int}(X/K).
\]
In particular the whole fibre at every $w_i$ is interior.
Theorem~\ref{v036:flatgermweight} defines its positive intrinsic
weight and that at $w'$. Theorem~\ref{v036:flatgermlocalconstancy}
shows that the local images fill neighborhoods in their stated
planes.

For $v$ sufficiently near $w_i$ in $w_i+(N_i)_{\mathbb R}$, one
has $v\in t_X(X)$ and $a(v)\in t_Y(Y)$ sufficiently near $w'$.
It follows that $a((N_i)_{\mathbb R})\subset(N_Y)_{\mathbb R}$.
Saturation of $N_Y$ implies $a(N_i)\subset N_Y$. If the restriction
of $a$ to $(N_i)_{\mathbb R}$ had nonzero kernel, a small interval
through $w_i$ in that kernel would give infinitely many elements
of $a^{-1}(w')\cap t_X(X)$. This contradicts the finite-set
hypothesis. Hence this restriction is injective, and $a(N_i)$ has
finite index in $N_Y$.

Choose an integral $Q:\mathbb Z^{n'}\to\mathbb Z^d$ transverse to
$N_Y$. Take a small closed box in the target moment space, with
$w'$ in its interior and with its inverse image
$Y_0\subset Y$ contained in the specified flat-germ neighborhood.
As in the proof of Theorem~\ref{v036:flatgermweight}, the entire
level of $Q\circ t_Y|_{Y_0}$ at $Qw'$ is $t_Y^{-1}(w')$ and is
interior over $K$ in $Y_0$. Put $X_0=p^{-1}(Y_0)$.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange} gives its actual
affinoid algebra, and Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange}
preserves the finite canonical module structure. Ordinary base
change of a locally free module preserves its rank: its local
bases base change to local bases.

The entire level of $Q a\circ t_X|_{X_0}$ at $Qw'$ is
$p^{-1}(t_Y^{-1}(w'))$. Indeed, the target moment image in $Y_0$
lies in the plane on which $Q$ is injective. This level is interior
in $X_0$ by finite relative interior and composition. Its moment
values are exactly $w_1,\ldots,w_s$. Proposition
\ref{v035:analytic:projectedfibrefactors} therefore identifies its
actual algebra with
\[
                               \prod_{i=1}^s D_{w_i}.
\]
Each factor is the original whole moment-fibre algebra on $X$,
since that whole fibre already belongs to $X_0$.

Let $D'_{w'}$ be the actual whole target-fibre algebra, $A$ the
exact-radius target polytorus at $Qw'$, and $H$ its Gauss field.
The algebra of the displayed source level is also the actual
finite base change of the algebra of $X$ along the algebra map
from $Y$ to $D'_{w'}$. It is consequently finite locally free of
rank $\Delta$ over $D'_{w'}$. After tensoring over $A$ with $H$,
it is finite locally free of that rank over the Artinian algebra
\[
                              E'=D'_{w'}\widehat\otimes_A H.
\]
All these finite tensors are ordinary tensors with their complete
canonical norms by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange}.

By \cite[Tag 00JB]{StacksAlg}, $E'$ is a finite product of Artinian
local algebras. On each factor, a local basis of the finite locally
free module at its unique maximal ideal is a global basis: a
principal open neighborhood of that ideal is the entire spectrum,
and its defining element is a unit. Its $H$-dimension is therefore
$\Delta$ times the $H$-dimension of that factor. Summing the
dimensions and using the actual product of source fibres gives
\[
                            \Delta\,\delta_Q(w')
                                =\sum_{i=1}^s\delta_{Qa}(w_i).
\]
Apply Theorem~\ref{v036:flatgermweight} to every term. Since $Q$
is injective on $N_Y$, counting representatives in the subgroup
chain $Qa(N_i)\subset Q(N_Y)\subset\mathbb Z^d$ gives
\[
 [\mathbb Z^d:Qa(N_i)]
      =[\mathbb Z^d:Q(N_Y)]\,[N_Y:a(N_i)].
\]
Cancel the common positive first factor. This proves the stated
geometric pushforward formula with all lengths and full field
degrees already incorporated in the intrinsic weights.
\end{proof}
