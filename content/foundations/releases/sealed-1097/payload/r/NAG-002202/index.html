Local rational coordinates of lattice slope one

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 915

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Local rational coordinates of lattice slope one}
{格斜率为一的局部有理坐标}]
\label{cand:rationalstarcoordinates}
\bi{Every point of $\Sigma$ has a graph neighborhood $W$ and
finitely many rational functions whose negative logarithmic norms
give an injective map $W\to\mathbf R^m$. On each sufficiently
short edge of $W$, one coordinate has slope $1$ or $-1$. At a
model vertex the incident branches can be sent to distinct
positive coordinate axes, after subtracting the value at the
vertex.}
{$\Sigma$ 的每一点都有图邻域 $W$ 及有限个有理函数，使其
范数的负对数给出单射 $W\to\mathbf R^m$。在 $W$ 的每条
充分短的边上，某个坐标具有斜率 $1$ 或 $-1$。在模型
顶点处，减去顶点处的值后，可将入射分支送到互异的正坐标轴。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For a point interior to an edge, its nodal annulus coordinate
$z$ is an analytic unit, and $-\log|z|$ is its length coordinate.
Lemma~\ref{cand:rationalnormgerm} gives a rational function with
the same norm on a neighborhood of the point. Restricting to a
small interval proves the assertion there, for every real radius.}
{对边内部的点，节点圆环坐标 $z$ 是解析单位，且
$-\log|z|$ 就是长度坐标。Lemma~\ref{cand:rationalnormgerm}
给出在该点附近具有相同范数的有理函数；限制到一个小区间
即得结论，此论证适用于任意实半径。}

\bi{At a vertex $v_C$, label its incident branches by the
distinct normalization points $p_1,\ldots,p_m$, counting the
two ends of a loop separately. Choose the residual functions
$u_i$ of Lemma~\ref{cand:residualfiniteorders}. By
Proposition~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield}, each is the
residue of a norm-one element of $\mathcal H(v_C)$. The
density of $K(X)$ in this field gives $f_i\in K(X)^\times$
of norm one with residue $u_i$. The slope formula
Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope} then gives, on the
$j$-th short branch with length parameter $\tau$,}
{在顶点 $v_C$ 处，用互异的正规化点 $p_1,\ldots,p_m$
标记其入射分支，回路的两端分别计算。选择
Lemma~\ref{cand:residualfiniteorders} 中的剩余函数 $u_i$。
由 Proposition~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield}，每个
$u_i$ 是 $\mathcal H(v_C)$ 中某个范数一元素的剩余类。
$K(X)$ 在该域中稠密，故可取范数一、剩余类为 $u_i$ 的
$f_i\in K(X)^\times$。由斜率公式
Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope}，在第 $j$ 条短分支
上，以长度参数 $\tau$ 表示，有}
\[
  \bigl(-\log|f_1|,\ldots,-\log|f_m|\bigr)(\gamma_j(\tau))
       =\tau e_j.
\]
\bi{There are only finitely many pairs $(i,j)$, so one shortening
works for all of them. The resulting star is mapped injectively,
with primitive edge direction vectors. An isolated vertex needs
no local coordinates.}
{因为 $(i,j)$ 只有有限对，一次共同收缩即适用于全部坐标。
所得星形邻域被单射映入，且边方向向量本原。孤立顶点无须
局部坐标。}
\end{proof}
