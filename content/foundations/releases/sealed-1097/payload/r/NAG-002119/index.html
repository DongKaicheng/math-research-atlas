Weighted initial stars and tangent quotients

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 897

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Weighted initial stars and tangent quotients}{带权初始星形与切向商}]
\label{cand:weightedinitialstar}
\bi{Let $X$ be pure of dimension $d$, let $\tau$ be a cell of its initial-stratum complex of dimension $s$, and take $w\in\operatorname{relint}(\tau)$. Write $\operatorname{in}_w(X)=T_{N_\tau}\times Z_\tau$ as in Proposition~\ref{cand:initialtangenttorus}. Give its residue field the trivial valuation. Then $Z_\tau$ is pure of dimension $d-s$, and}
{设 $X$ 纯 $d$ 维，$\tau$ 为其初始分层复形中维数为 $s$ 的胞腔，取 $w\in\operatorname{relint}(\tau)$。按 Proposition~\ref{cand:initialtangenttorus} 写成 $\operatorname{in}_w(X)=T_{N_\tau}\times Z_\tau$，并将剩余域赋予平凡赋值。则 $Z_\tau$ 纯 $d-s$ 维，且}
\[
 \operatorname{Trop}(Z_\tau)
    =\bigl(\operatorname{Star}_w\operatorname{Trop}(X)\bigr)/L_\tau.
\]
\bi{The equality preserves the weights on maximal cells, using the quotient lattice $N/N_\tau$.}
{使用商格 $N/N_\tau$ 时，此等式保持极大胞腔上的权重。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A prime quotient of the coordinate algebra of $Z_\tau$, with $s$ independent Laurent variables adjoined, has dimension increased by $s$, by \cite[Tag 00P0]{StacksAlg}. Proposition~\ref{cand:initialpuredimension} therefore proves the pure-dimension assertion. For every analytic point of $Z_\tau$, its completed residue field admits Gauss extensions with arbitrary values of the additional torus coordinates. Hence the tropical support of the product is exactly $L_\tau\times\operatorname{Trop}(Z_\tau)$. Apply Theorem~\ref{cand:initiallocalstar} to obtain the displayed support equality.}
{对 $Z_\tau$ 坐标代数的每个素商添加 $s$ 个独立 Laurent 变量，由 \cite[Tag 00P0]{StacksAlg}，维数恰增加 $s$。因此 Proposition~\ref{cand:initialpuredimension} 给出纯维性。对 $Z_\tau$ 的每个解析点，其完备剩余域都可作 Gauss 扩张，使新增环面坐标具有任意指定值。故直积的热带支集恰为 $L_\tau\times\operatorname{Trop}(Z_\tau)$；再应用 Theorem~\ref{cand:initiallocalstar}，即得所列支集等式。}

\bi{Take a direction $u$ whose class in $N_{\mathbf R}/L_\tau$ lies in the relative interior of a maximal cell of a common refinement of the quotient star and the initial complex of $Z_\tau$. For small $\varepsilon>0$, Theorem~\ref{cand:initiallocalstar} identifies $\operatorname{in}_{w+\varepsilon u}(X)$ with $\operatorname{in}_u(\operatorname{in}_w(X))$ after residue extension and torus translation. In the split coordinate ring, all equations for $Z_\tau$ use quotient characters only. Taking their initial forms therefore gives}
{取方向 $u$，使其在 $N_{\mathbf R}/L_\tau$ 中的类位于商星形与 $Z_\tau$ 初始复形的某个共同细分之极大胞腔的相对内部。对充分小的 $\varepsilon>0$，Theorem~\ref{cand:initiallocalstar} 在剩余扩域及环面平移后识别 $\operatorname{in}_{w+\varepsilon u}(X)$ 与 $\operatorname{in}_u(\operatorname{in}_w(X))$。在分裂坐标环中，$Z_\tau$ 的全部方程只使用商特征，故取初始形式给出}
\[
 \operatorname{in}_u(\operatorname{in}_w(X))
       \simeq T_{N_\tau}\times\operatorname{in}_{\bar u}(Z_\tau).
\]
\bi{The maximal tangent lattice on the left is the inverse image in $N$ of the maximal tangent lattice in $N/N_\tau$. These are saturated lattices. Quotienting the two schemes by that maximal tangent torus gives the same finite transverse algebra after residue extension. Its dimension is unchanged. Proposition~\ref{cand:geometricmultiplicity} gives equality of the weights; Proposition~\ref{cand:tropicalweightlocalconstancy} removes the chosen refinements.}
{左侧的极大切向格是 $N/N_\tau$ 中极大切向格在 $N$ 中的逆像，两者均为饱和格。将两边概形商去该极大切向环面，便在剩余扩域后得到同一有限横截代数，其维数不变。由 Proposition~\ref{cand:geometricmultiplicity}，权重相等；再由 Proposition~\ref{cand:tropicalweightlocalconstancy}，可去除所选细分。}
\end{proof}
