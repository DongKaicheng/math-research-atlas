Rearranging weighted vanishing families

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 100

Statement · exact TeX

\begin{lemma}[\bi{Rearranging weighted vanishing families}{加权趋零族的重排}]\label{fnd:weightedc0rearrangement}
\bi{Let $I,J$ be countable sets, let $u_i,v_j>0$, and let $E$ be a Banach $K$-space. There is a natural isometric isomorphism}
{设 $I,J$ 为可数集合，$u_i,v_j>0$，且 $E$ 为 Banach $K$-空间。存在自然的等距同构}
\[
 \mathcal C_u\bigl(I,\mathcal C_v(J,E)\bigr)
 \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
 \mathcal C_{u\boxtimes v}(I\times J,E),
 \qquad (u\boxtimes v)_{ij}=u_iv_j,
\]
\bi{which sends a family of rows to its joint coefficient family. Consequently any finite number of such coefficient constructions can be regrouped or permuted, preserving norms and the finite-tail condition. These identifications commute with bounded coefficient maps. For coefficient algebras or modules indexed by products of copies of $\mathbf N$ and $\mathbf Z$, with multiplicative monomial weights, they also preserve the convolution products and module actions. Empty index sets and empty products are allowed.}
{该同构将逐行给出的族送到联合系数族。因此，有限次这样的系数构造可以重新分组或交换次序，同时保持范数及有限尾条件。这些识别与有界系数映射交换。对于指标为有限个 $\mathbf N$ 与 $\mathbf Z$ 的乘积、权重为乘法性单项式权重的系数代数或模，它们还保持卷积乘法及模作用。允许空指标集及空乘积。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Write the joint coefficients as $e_{ij}$ and put $b_i=\sup_j v_j\|e_{ij}\|$. Suppose first that the joint weighted family tends to zero outside finite subsets. Each fixed row belongs to $\mathcal C_v(J,E)$, since $u_i>0$. If $u_ib_i\geq\epsilon>0$, some entry in that row satisfies $u_iv_j\|e_{ij}\|>\epsilon/2$. There are only finitely many such entries, and hence only finitely many such rows. Thus $(\text{row}_i)_i$ satisfies the outer finite-tail condition.}
{将联合系数写为 $e_{ij}$，并令 $b_i=\sup_j v_j\|e_{ij}\|$。先设联合加权族在有限子集之外趋于零。由于 $u_i>0$，每个固定行都属于 $\mathcal C_v(J,E)$。若 $u_ib_i\geq\epsilon>0$，该行中存在一项满足 $u_iv_j\|e_{ij}\|>\epsilon/2$。这样的项只有有限个，因而这样的行也只有有限个。所以逐行族满足外层有限尾条件。}

\bi{Conversely, suppose that every row has a vanishing weighted tail and that $(u_ib_i)_i$ tends to zero outside finite subsets. An entry of joint weight at least $\epsilon$ lies in a row with $u_ib_i\geq\epsilon$. There are finitely many such rows. In each of them only finitely many entries have joint weight at least $\epsilon$, so the union of these finite sets is finite. This proves the converse. Moreover,}
{反之，设每行的加权尾趋于零，且 $(u_ib_i)_i$ 在有限子集之外趋于零。联合权重至少为 $\epsilon$ 的项必在满足 $u_ib_i\geq\epsilon$ 的行中。这样的行只有有限个，而每个这样的行中，联合权重至少为 $\epsilon$ 的项也只有有限个；这些有限集的并仍有限。这证明反向蕴含。此外，}
\[
 \sup_i u_i\sup_j v_j\|e_{ij}\|
   =\sup_{(i,j)\in I\times J}u_iv_j\|e_{ij}\|.
\]
\bi{The isomorphism and norm equality follow. Completeness is supplied by Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness}. Repeated application proves the finite regrouping assertion; permutations preserve the joint weights and the same finite subsets. A coefficient map acts on the same $e_{ij}$ in either description and retains its given bound. Finally, finite coefficient convolution is unchanged by these bijections of multiindices. Finite-support families are dense, and Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientconvolution} bounds products and actions. Passing to limits proves the algebra and module assertions. The zero spaces arising from empty index sets and the one-element index set for an empty product satisfy the same formulas.}
{由此得到同构及范数等式。完备性由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness} 保证。反复应用即得有限次重排的断言；交换指标次序保留联合权重以及相应的有限子集。系数映射在两种描述中作用于同一个 $e_{ij}$，保有原范数界。最后，这些多指标之间的双射不改变有限系数卷积。有限支撑族稠密，而 Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientconvolution} 给出乘法及作用的范数界。取极限便得到代数与模的断言。空指标集对应的零空间以及空乘积对应的单元素指标集也满足相同公式。}
\end{proof}
