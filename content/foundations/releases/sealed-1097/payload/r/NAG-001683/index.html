Scalar extension of weighted coefficient spaces

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 270

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Scalar extension of weighted coefficient spaces}{加权系数空间的标量扩张}]
\label{fnd:weightedscalartensor}
\bi{Let $I$ be a countable set, let $w_i>0$, and let $E$ be a Banach $K$-space. There is a natural isometric linear isomorphism}
{设 $I$ 为可数集合，$w_i>0$，$E$ 为 Banach $K$-空间。存在自然等距线性同构}
\[
 \mathcal C_w(I,K)\widehat\otimes_K E
       \simeq \mathcal C_w(I,E),\qquad
 (a_i)_i\otimes e\longmapsto(a_ie)_i.
\]
\bi{For a complete valued extension $L/K$, this gives the isometric algebra isomorphism $K\{r^{-1}T\}\widehat\otimes_K L\simeq L\{r^{-1}T\}$. Also $E\to E\widehat\otimes_K L$, $e\mapsto e\otimes1$, is isometric.}
{对完备赋值扩域 $L/K$，这给出等距代数同构 $K\{r^{-1}T\}\widehat\otimes_K L\simeq L\{r^{-1}T\}$。此外，$E\to E\widehat\otimes_K L$、$e\mapsto e\otimes1$ 等距。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The displayed bilinear map is contractive for the weighted supremum norm. Write $\epsilon_i$ for the coordinate vector of norm $w_i$. For a finitely supported tensor $z=\sum_i\epsilon_i\otimes e_i$, its displayed expression gives $\|z\|_\pi\leq\max_i w_i\|e_i\|$. Conversely the $i$th coordinate functional on $\mathcal C_w(I,K)$ has norm $w_i^{-1}$. Applying its tensor with $1_E$ to any representation gives $w_i\|e_i\|\leq\|z\|_\pi$. Hence equality holds. Finitely supported vectors are dense on the right. They are also dense on the left: truncate each of the finitely many coefficient families in an algebraic tensor, then use density of algebraic tensors in the completion. This proves the isometry and surjectivity. For Tate algebras it preserves multiplication first on polynomials and then by continuity. Finally, the pure-tensor formula of Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonisometrictensor} gives $\|e\otimes1\|=\|e\|$.}
{所列双线性映射对加权上确界范数为压缩映射。记 $\epsilon_i$ 为范数为 $w_i$ 的坐标向量。对有限支撑张量 $z=\sum_i\epsilon_i\otimes e_i$，该表达式给出 $\|z\|_\pi\leq\max_i w_i\|e_i\|$。反之，$\mathcal C_w(I,K)$ 上第 $i$ 个坐标泛函的范数为 $w_i^{-1}$；对任意张量表达式应用它与 $1_E$ 的张量，得到 $w_i\|e_i\|\leq\|z\|_\pi$，故等号成立。有限支撑向量在右边稠密；它们在左边也稠密，因为可分别截断一个代数张量中出现的有限个系数族，再利用代数张量在完备化中的稠密性。这证明等距性及满射性。对 Tate 代数，先在多项式上检验乘法，再由连续性延拓。最后，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonisometrictensor} 的纯张量公式，$\|e\otimes1\|=\|e\|$。}
\end{proof}
