Valuations and a proper pointed comparison

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 582

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Valuations and a proper pointed comparison}
{赋值与适当的带点比较}]
\label{fnd:properpointedvaluationcomparison}
\bi{Let $Z$ be an integral finite-type $k$-scheme equipped with
a dominant map $\operatorname{Spec}F\to Z$.
The spaces
$\mathscr P_k(F)\{R\}$ for affine opens $\operatorname{Spec}R\subset Z$
glue to a birational space $\operatorname{Val}(Z,F)$.
Its points are valuations of $F/k$ together with a centre on $Z$.
If $Z'\to Z$ is proper and the specified generic point lifts to
$\operatorname{Spec}F\to Z'$, then the induced map}
{设 $Z$ 为整的有限型 $k$-概形，并配备支配映射
$\operatorname{Spec}F\to Z$。
对仿射开集 $\operatorname{Spec}R\subset Z$，各空间
$\mathscr P_k(F)\{R\}$ 粘合为 birational 空间
$\operatorname{Val}(Z,F)$；其点是 $F/k$ 的赋值及其在 $Z$
上的一个中心。若 $Z'\to Z$ 适当，且指定泛点提升为
$\operatorname{Spec}F\to Z'$，则诱导映射}
\[
       \operatorname{Val}(Z',F)\longrightarrow
       \operatorname{Val}(Z,F)
\]
\bi{is an isomorphism, with $Z'$ replaced by the reduced closure
of that generic point when necessary.}
{为同构；必要时，将 $Z'$ 替换为该泛点的约化闭包。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A valuation ring $V\subset F$ containing $R$ has the unique
centre $\mathfrak m_V\cap R$ on $\operatorname{Spec}R$.
Indeed the elements outside that prime are units of $V$, so the
map $R\to V$ factors through the required local homomorphism.
Conversely a centre supplies this containment. For a principal
open $D(h)$, the centre lies in $D(h)$ exactly when $h^{-1}\in V$.
Thus restriction to scheme opens gives the usual basic open
subspaces of the valuation space. Affine coverings of intersections
therefore provide compatible gluing maps. On a nonseparated scheme
the same valuation may have distinct centres; the gluing retains
these as distinct points unless an overlap identifies them.}
{若赋值环 $V\subset F$ 包含 $R$，则它在
$\operatorname{Spec}R$ 上的唯一中心为
$\mathfrak m_V\cap R$。事实上，该素理想以外的元素在 $V$
中可逆，因此 $R\to V$ 经由所需的局部同态分解；反之，中心
给出上述包含。对主开集 $D(h)$，中心属于 $D(h)$ 当且仅当
$h^{-1}\in V$。因此限制到概形开集给出赋值空间的通常基本开
子空间。交的仿射覆盖由此提供相容的粘合映射。在非分离概形上，
同一个赋值可能有不同中心；除非交叠将其识别，粘合保留这些不同点。}

\bi{For a point $(V,z)$ of $\operatorname{Val}(Z,F)$, apply
the valuative criterion \cite[Tag 0BX5]{StacksAlg} to the
specified generic map and $\operatorname{Spec}V\to Z$.
It gives exactly one lift to $Z'$. Its image is contained in the
closure of the specified generic point. The morphism factors through
the reduced closure: a section of its defining radical ideal vanishes
at the generic point of the integral scheme $\operatorname{Spec}V$,
and hence vanishes identically. This proves bijectivity on centred
valuations.}
{对 $\operatorname{Val}(Z,F)$ 的点 $(V,z)$，将赋值判据
\cite[Tag 0BX5]{StacksAlg} 应用于指定泛点映射及
$\operatorname{Spec}V\to Z$，得到唯一的到 $Z'$ 的提升。
其像包含于指定泛点的闭包，且态射经过该约化闭包分解：定义
这个闭包的根理想中的截面在整概形 $\operatorname{Spec}V$
的泛点消失，因此处处消失。这证明带中心赋值之间的双射性。}

\bi{The map is a local homeomorphism. Around any lifted centre
choose affine opens $\operatorname{Spec}R'\subset Z'$ and
$\operatorname{Spec}R\subset Z$ with the first mapping into the
second. The induced valuation map is the inclusion of the open
$\mathscr P_k(F)\{R'\}$ into $\mathscr P_k(F)\{R\}$.
Here the images of the coordinate rings in $F$ are understood;
they are finitely generated, so these are basic quasi-compact opens.
A bijective local homeomorphism is a homeomorphism, and the field
map is the identity. This proves the birational-space isomorphism.}
{此映射是局部同胚：在提升的中心附近，选取仿射开集
$\operatorname{Spec}R'\subset Z'$ 及
$\operatorname{Spec}R\subset Z$，使前者映入后者。
诱导的赋值映射就是开集 $\mathscr P_k(F)\{R'\}$ 到
$\mathscr P_k(F)\{R\}$ 的包含。这里使用坐标环在 $F$ 中的
像；这些像有限生成，故所列集合均为基本拟紧开集。
双射的局部同胚是同胚，而域映射为恒等，因此得到 birational
空间同构。}
\end{proof}
