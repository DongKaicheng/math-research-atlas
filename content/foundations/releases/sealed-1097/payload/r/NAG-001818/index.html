Faithful flatness along every inner generic fibre

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 484

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Faithful flatness along every inner generic fibre}{每个内泛纤维上的忠实平坦性}]
\label{fnd:generalinnergenericfibreflatness}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good analytic spaces over any complete field. If $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$ and $\mathcal O_{X,f(y)}$ is a field, then}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 为任意完备域上 good 解析空间之间的态射。若 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$ 且 $\mathcal O_{X,f(y)}$ 为域，则}
\[
                      \mathcal O_{Y,y}\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{Y_{f(y)},y}
\]
\bi{is faithfully flat.}
{忠实平坦。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The case of nontrivially valued $\mathcal H(x)$, where $x=f(y)$, is Theorem~\ref{fnd:nontrivialinnergenericfibreflatness}. Otherwise choose an affinoid domain $U$ which is an ordinary neighborhood of $x$. The test $U$ in Definition~\ref{fnd:alltargetrelativeinterior} provides an affinoid domain $V$ which is an ordinary neighborhood of $y$ in $Y\times_X U$, with $y\in\operatorname{Int}(V/U)$. Since $x$ is in the ordinary interior of $U$, the source and base ordinary germs and the analytic fibre germ are unchanged. Replace $Y,X$ by $V,U$. Choose $0<r<1$ and form their simultaneous affine-line products $Y^*\to X^*$. These spaces are good by Theorem~\ref{fnd:relativepolydiscanalyticflat}. Take the radius-$r$ Gauss points $y^*,x^*$; the Gauss seminorms are compatible under the isometric inclusion of completed residue fields. Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalgaussfieldgerm} makes $\mathcal O_{X^*,x^*}$ a field and $\mathcal H(x^*)$ nontrivially valued. For every affinoid domain $D\subset X^*$ containing $x^*$, the test inverse image $Y^*\times_{X^*}D=V\times_U D$ is affinoid by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticproducts}. Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus}, applied to the affinoid diagram $V\to U\leftarrow D$, puts $y^*$ in the relative interior of that entire test over $D$. Thus $y^*\in\operatorname{Int}(Y^*/X^*)$ by the same definition, and the nontrivial case applies. With}
{若 $x=f(y)$ 处的 $\mathcal H(x)$ 非平凡赋值，结论由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:nontrivialinnergenericfibreflatness} 给出。否则选取 $x$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域 $U$。在 Definition~\ref{fnd:alltargetrelativeinterior} 中以 $U$ 作测试，得到 $Y\times_X U$ 中 $y$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域 $V$，且 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(V/U)$。由于 $x$ 位于 $U$ 的普通内部，源与底的普通芽及解析纤维芽不变。以 $V,U$ 代替 $Y,X$。取 $0<r<1$，同时作仿射直线乘积 $Y^*\to X^*$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:relativepolydiscanalyticflat}，两空间均为 good。取半径 $r$ 的 Gauss 点 $y^*,x^*$；Gauss 半范数与完备剩余域的等距嵌入相容。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalgaussfieldgerm}，$\mathcal O_{X^*,x^*}$ 为域，且 $\mathcal H(x^*)$ 非平凡赋值。对每个含 $x^*$ 的 affinoid 域 $D\subset X^*$，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticproducts}，测试的逆像 $Y^*\times_{X^*}D=V\times_U D$ 为 affinoid。将 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus} 应用于 affinoid 图 $V\to U\leftarrow D$，知 $y^*$ 位于整个测试逆像相对于 $D$ 的内部。故由同一定义，$y^*\in\operatorname{Int}(Y^*/X^*)$，可应用非平凡赋值的情形。记}
\[
 S=\mathcal O_{Y,y},\quad S^*=\mathcal O_{Y^*,y^*},\quad
 T=\mathcal O_{Y_x,y},\quad T^*=\mathcal O_{Y^*_{x^*},y^*},
\]
\bi{the map $S^*\to T^*$ is flat. Around the Gauss point, the projection $Y^*\to Y$ is the projection from the ordinary interior of a relative closed disc of radius greater than $r$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:relativepolydiscanalyticflat} therefore makes $S\to S^*$ flat. Finally $Y^*_{x^*}=(Y_x)_{\mathcal H(x)_r}$, and Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat} makes $T\to T^*$ faithfully flat. Thus the composite $S\to T^*$ is flat, and faithful-flat descent along $T\to T^*$ gives flatness of $S\to T$. All maps are local and the rings are nonzero, proving faithful flatness.}
{则 $S^*\to T^*$ 平坦。在 Gauss 点附近，投影 $Y^*\to Y$ 是半径大于 $r$ 的相对闭圆盘之普通内部上的投影。因此 Theorem~\ref{fnd:relativepolydiscanalyticflat} 说明 $S\to S^*$ 平坦。最后，$Y^*_{x^*}=(Y_x)_{\mathcal H(x)_r}$，而 Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat} 说明 $T\to T^*$ 忠实平坦。因此复合 $S\to T^*$ 平坦；沿 $T\to T^*$ 忠实平坦下降，得到 $S\to T$ 平坦。全部映射局部且环非零，故得到忠实平坦性。}
\end{proof}
