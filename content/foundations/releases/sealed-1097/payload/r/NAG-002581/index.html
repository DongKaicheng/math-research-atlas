Finite single-representative decomposition

Chapter 8 supplement: Interior Stokes with coherent coefficients

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1792

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Finite single-representative decomposition]
\label{f7gg:decomposition}
Let \(X\) be good, Hausdorff and pure of analytic dimension
\(d\geq1\), and let \(\beta\in\A_c^{d-1,d}(X)\) have support
\(S\subseteq\Int(X/K)\).
There exist finitely many affinoid domains \(W_i\subseteq X\)
and global functions \(0\leq b_i\leq1\) such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the loci on which \(b_i=1\) cover an ordinary
neighborhood of \(S\);
\item on \(W_i\), both \(\beta\) and \(b_i\) have one global
unit-moment representative, and
\(\supp(b_i)\subseteq C_i\) for a compact
\(C_i\subseteq\Int_{\rm top}(W_i,X)\cap\Int(X/K)\);
\item for \(W_I=\bigcap_{i\in I}W_i\), \(I\ne\varnothing\),
the section
\[
       \theta_I=\left(\prod_{i\in I}b_i|_{W_I}\right)\beta|_{W_I}
\]
has one global unit-moment representative, with compact support
in both ordinary interior and absolute analytic interior:
\[
\supp(\theta_I)\subseteq\Int_{\rm top}(W_I,X)\cap\Int(W_I/K).
\]
\item as ordinary sections on \(X\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:decompose}
      \beta=\sum_{\varnothing\ne I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,m\}}
                       (-1)^{|I|+1}e_!\theta_I .
\end{equation}
Empty domains and zero terms are omitted.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
If \(S=\varnothing\), use the empty sum.
For \(x\in S\), choose an ordinary affinoid neighborhood
\(V_x\) on which \(\beta=t_{f_x}^*\alpha_x\).
It is pure of dimension \(d\) by
\src{v037:integration:puritylocality}.
The point is interior in \(V_x/K\) by
\src{fnd:goodinteriorlocaltesting}, since it is interior
in \(X/K\) and \(V_x\) is an ordinary neighborhood.
The germ is nonzero, so Lemma~\ref{f7gg:minimal} applies.
Choose the bump of Lemma~\ref{f7gg:bump}, with its ordinary
support neighborhood contained in \(\Int(X/K)\).
Compactness of \(S\) gives finitely many such bumps,
with domains \(W_i\), which equal one on neighborhoods
covering \(S\). Write
\[
       \beta|_{W_i}=t_{f_i}^*\alpha_i,\qquad
       b_i|_{W_i}=t_{g_i}^*\Phi_i.
\]

Each \(W_I\) is an actual affinoid domain by
\src{v037:integration:separatedintersections}, and every
nonempty \(W_I\) is pure of dimension \(d\).
Choose any \(i_0\in I\). The joint tuple
\[
                     F_I=(f_{i_0},(g_i)_{i\in I})|_{W_I}
\]
consists of units on \(W_I\).
A single ambient representative of \(\theta_I\) is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:onechart}
       \alpha_I=
       \left(\prod_{i\in I}\pi_{g_i}^*\Phi_i\right)
                                      \pi_{f_{i_0}}^*\alpha_{i_0},
\end{equation}
defined near its actual compact image.
No extension of an analytic unit from one domain to another
has been made; all tuples are restricted to the intersection.
The ordinary sheaf and unit-adjunction faithfulness ensure
that this representative is the displayed section, independently
of the choice of \(i_0\).

Put \(C_I=\bigcap_{i\in I}C_i\).
It is compact and contains \(\supp_{W_I}(\theta_I)\).
Every point of \(C_I\) lies in all the ordinary interiors of
the \(W_i\), and hence in \(\Int_{\rm top}(W_I,X)\).
It is also interior in \(X/K\). Applying
\src{fnd:goodinteriorlocaltesting} to this ordinary affinoid
neighborhood gives
\[
                           C_I\subseteq\Int(W_I/K).
\]
Consequently \(\theta_I\) is zero on an ordinary neighborhood
of \(\partial_KW_I\). Indeed \(C_I\) is compact and closed
and is disjoint from that boundary.

The function
\[
                         P=1-\prod_{i=1}^m(1-b_i)
\]
equals one on an ordinary neighborhood of \(S\).
Thus \(\beta=P\beta\) everywhere as an ordinary section:
off \(S\) its germs are zero.
Expand the finite product to obtain \eqref{eq:decompose}.
The global product \((\prod_{i\in I}b_i)\beta\) is
\(e_!\theta_I\), by Lemma~\ref{f7gg:zeroextension}.
This proves all four assertions.
\end{proof}
