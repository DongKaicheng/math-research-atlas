Finite algebras with their canonical norm

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 280

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Finite algebras with their canonical norm}{配备典范范数的有限代数}]
\label{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras}
\bi{Let $A$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra. Every commutative finite $A$-algebra $D$ admits a complete submultiplicative norm, with $\|1\|=1$ when $D\ne0$, equivalent to its canonical finite-$A$-module norm. With this norm it is a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra and $A\to D$ is bounded. The resulting Banach structure is independent, up to bounded isomorphism, of the chosen canonical module norm. Every $A$-linear map between finite algebras with these structures is bounded. Moreover}
{设 $A$ 为广义 $K$-affinoid 代数。每个交换有限 $A$-代数 $D$ 都存在完备次乘范数，当 $D\ne0$ 时满足 $\|1\|=1$，且与典范有限 $A$-模范数等价。配备此范数后，它是广义 $K$-affinoid 代数，且 $A\to D$ 有界。所得 Banach 结构在有界同构意义下不依赖所选典范模范数。配备这些结构的有限代数之间，每个 $A$-线性映射均有界。此外，}
\[
 \operatorname{Int}\bigl(\mathcal M(D)/\mathcal M(A)\bigr)
       =\mathcal M(D).
\]
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Give $A$ a presentation norm, and $D$ a quotient norm from a finite free $A$-module. For $d\in D$, multiplication is an $A$-linear map $L_d:D\to D$ and is bounded by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}. Choose module generators $e_1,\ldots,e_m$. Controlled lifts of $d=\sum_i a_i e_i$ and contractivity of the $A$-action give a constant $C$ independent of $d$ with}
{给 $A$ 配备表示范数，给 $D$ 配备某个有限自由 $A$-模的商范数。对 $d\in D$，乘法给出 $A$-线性映射 $L_d:D\to D$，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules} 有界。选择模生成元 $e_1,\ldots,e_m$。利用 $d=\sum_i a_i e_i$ 的受控提升及 $A$-作用的压缩性，得到与 $d$ 无关的常数 $C$，使}
\[
 \|L_d\|_{\rm op}\leq C\|d\|,\qquad
 \|d\|=\|L_d(1)\|\leq\|L_d\|_{\rm op}\|1\|.
\]
\bi{Thus $\|d\|_*=\|L_d\|_{\rm op}$ is equivalent to the complete module norm. It is homogeneous and submultiplicative, since $L_{dd'}=L_dL_{d'}$, and $\|1\|_*=1$. The map $A\to D$ is contractive for this norm. Treat the zero algebra separately with its zero norm.}
{因此 $\|d\|_*=\|L_d\|_{\rm op}$ 与完备模范数等价。由于 $L_{dd'}=L_dL_{d'}$，该范数齐次且次乘，并满足 $\|1\|_*=1$。对这个范数，映射 $A\to D$ 为压缩映射。零代数另取其零范数。}

\bi{Choose real radii $r_i>\|e_i\|_*$. Substitution gives a bounded surjection $A\{r^{-1}T\}\to D$, $T_i\mapsto e_i$. It is norm-strict: a controlled module lift $d=\sum_i a_i e_i$ is represented by the linear polynomial $\sum_i a_i T_i$, whose coefficient norm is at most $(\max_i r_i)\max_i\|a_i\|$. Its kernel is closed, and the coefficient algebra is affinoid by Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation}. Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients} now realizes $D$ as the required affinoid quotient with an equivalent norm.}
{选择实数半径 $r_i>\|e_i\|_*$。代入给出有界满射 $A\{r^{-1}T\}\to D$、$T_i\mapsto e_i$。它为 norm-strict：受控模提升 $d=\sum_i a_i e_i$ 可由线性多项式 $\sum_i a_i T_i$ 表示，其系数范数至多为 $(\max_i r_i)\max_i\|a_i\|$。该映射的核闭；由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation}，系数代数为 affinoid。由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients}，将 $D$ 实现为所需的 affinoid 商，且商范数与原范数等价。}

\bi{Every point $y\in\mathcal M(D)$ satisfies $|e_i(y)|\leq\|e_i\|_*<r_i$, by Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}. The same relative presentation therefore puts every $y$ in the interior of Definition~\ref{fnd:relativeinterior}. Equivalence of canonical module norms and boundedness of all linear maps follow from Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}，每个 $y\in\mathcal M(D)$ 都满足 $|e_i(y)|\leq\|e_i\|_*<r_i$。所以同一个相对表示把每个 $y$ 都放在 Definition~\ref{fnd:relativeinterior} 的内部中。典范模范数的等价性及全部线性映射的有界性，来自 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}。}
\end{proof}
