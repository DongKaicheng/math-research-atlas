Shilov fibres and geometric generic lengths

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1084

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[Shilov fibres and geometric generic lengths]
\label{v038:faithful:shilovmultiplicity}
For the immersion of Theorem~\ref{v038:faithful:wholefibreinitial},
the original-$K$ tropical fibre at $\trop_\varphi(s)$ has
Shilov boundary $\{s\}$ for every $s\in S$. The inverse of
the faithful map on $S$ is therefore a continuous section
over $\trop_\varphi(S)$ selecting the unique Shilov point.

For $s=x_{\eta,w}$, let $g_\eta$ be the number of
irreducible components of $E_{\lambda,I,\eta,\bar k}$,
where $\bar k$ is an algebraic closure of $k$.
After any normalization and algebraically closed residue
extension, the sum of the generic component lengths of the
actual initial scheme at $\trop_\varphi(s)$ equals $g_\eta$.
In particular this sum is one if the corresponding residual
component is geometrically integral. If every residual
component in these charts is geometrically integral, this
conclusion holds at every point of $S$. At weights lying
in relative interiors of maximal tropical cells, the sum
is the tropical multiplicity of
Proposition~\ref{cand:geometricmultiplicity}.
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Normalize the weights over a complete isometric extension
$L/K$. Theorem~\ref{v038:faithful:wholefibreinitial} and
Lemma~\ref{v038:faithful:peaklocalization} identify the
Shilov points of the base-changed fibre with those of the
selected weighted chart. Every one restricts to $s$ by
Proposition~\ref{v038:faithful:weightedchart}.
For any analytic function $g$ on the original tropical
fibre, its base change consequently satisfies
\[
                   \max_{Y_{\omega,L}}|g|=|g(s)|.
\]
The map $Y_{\omega,L}\to Y_\omega$ is surjective by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarextensionspectralsurjective}, with
scalar compatibility supplied by
Proposition~\ref{cand:torusfibrealgebra}. The same maximum formula
therefore holds on $Y_\omega$. The singleton $\{s\}$ is
a boundary, and the existence and minimality of the
Shilov boundary in Corollary~\ref{fnd:affinoidshilovinternal}
give the assertion. The faithful map on the compact $S$
is a homeomorphism onto its image, so its inverse is
continuous and has the stated values.

Over an algebraically closed residue field the initial
scheme is smooth and hence reduced. Each of its generic
local rings is consequently a field and has length one.
The principal localization in
Theorem~\ref{v038:faithful:wholefibreinitial} removes no
geometric component. Taking the product with a split
torus changes no component count: the Laurent polynomial
ring of an integral component is a domain, and the finite
minimal-prime decomposition gives all components this way.

For completeness, this count is unchanged by extending the
algebraically closed residue field. Every irreducible
component over an algebraically closed field is geometrically
irreducible by \cite[Tag 020J]{StacksAlg}. Thus its base
change remains irreducible. The components stay distinct
and incomparable: contraction of a prime under a field
extension preserves their original defining prime, by
faithful flatness of a nonzero extension of fields and
\cite[Tags 00HD, 00HP, 00HQ]{StacksAlg}. Their finite union
still covers the base change. To compare $k$ with the
residue field $l$ of a chosen normalization, take an
algebraic closure of $l$ and inside it the algebraic closure
of $k$ consisting of elements algebraic over $k$. Applying
the preceding argument gives exactly $g_\eta$.
The asserted length sum follows. For a maximal-cell weight,
Proposition~\ref{cand:geometricmultiplicity} identifies this
sum with its registered tropical weight.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
Let $K$ be complete for its specified nontrivial real-valued
absolute value. Write $O=K^\circ$, $k=\widetilde K$, and
$v=-\log|\cdot|$, and fix $\pi\in O$ with $0<|\pi|<1$.
We use the algebraic model setting of
Theorem~\ref{v035:faithfulglobal}: $\mathcal X$ is a given
projective, flat, finitely presented $O$-scheme, its generic
fibre $X$ is smooth and integral, and a finite collection of
affine opens covering $\mathcal X_s$ has \etale maps to
\[
 \operatorname{Spec}R_\lambda,\qquad
 R_\lambda=
 O[X_0,\ldots,X_{r_\lambda},T_1,\ldots,T_{q_\lambda}]/
                 (X_0\cdots X_{r_\lambda}-a_\lambda),
 \quad 0<|a_\lambda|<1.
\]
Let $S\subset X^{\an}$ be the compact skeleton of the completed
model. All rational functions below belong to the original
field $K(X)$.

For such a chart $\mathcal V=\operatorname{Spec}C$ and a
nonempty $I\subset\{0,\ldots,r\}$, write $S_I$ for its
residual stratum over
\[
 D_I=\{X_i=0\ (i\in I),\ X_j\ne0\ (j\notin I)\}
\]
and $Q_I$ for the finite set of generic points of $S_I$.
These are the strata and labels of
Proposition~\ref{cand:actualskeletalfibrestrata}. If
$s=x_{\eta,w}$, then $I=\{i:w_i>0\}$ and
$\eta\in Q_I$. An irreducible residual component in this
section is a component of this locally closed stratum.
