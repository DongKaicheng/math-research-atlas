Noetherianity of convergent germs

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 313

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Noetherianity of convergent germs}{收敛芽环的 Noetherian 性}]
\label{fnd:convergentgermnoetherian}
\bi{Suppose $K$ is nontrivially valued. Let $C_n$ be the subring of $K[[T_1,\ldots,T_n]]$ consisting of the series convergent on some positive polydisc about $0$. Then $C_n$ is a Noetherian local ring with maximal ideal $(T_1,\ldots,T_n)$, residue field $K$, and maximal-ideal completion $K[[T_1,\ldots,T_n]]$.}
{设 $K$ 的赋值非平凡。令 $C_n$ 为 $K[[T_1,\ldots,T_n]]$ 中在原点的某个正半径多圆盘上收敛的级数组成的子环。则 $C_n$ 是 Noetherian 局部环，其极大理想为 $(T_1,\ldots,T_n)$，剩余域为 $K$，极大理想完备化为 $K[[T_1,\ldots,T_n]]$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A series with nonzero constant term becomes a unit on a sufficiently small polydisc: its nonconstant part has norm smaller than the constant term, and geometric inversion applies. A series with zero constant term is a sum $\sum_iT_i g_i$ with each $g_i$ convergent, by partitioning its monomials according to a variable dividing them. Thus the asserted maximal ideal and residue field follow. Invertible linear changes of the variables preserve $C_n$, since either linear substitution maps a sufficiently small polydisc into any prescribed one.}
{常数项非零的级数在充分小的多圆盘上为单位：其非常数部分的范数严格小于常数项，可施加几何级数求逆。常数项为零的级数可写为 $\sum_iT_i g_i$，其中每个 $g_i$ 收敛，只需按整除各单项式的一个变量分组。因此得到所述极大理想与剩余域。可逆线性坐标变换保持 $C_n$，因为正反两个线性代入都把充分小的多圆盘送入任意给定的多圆盘。}

\bi{We prove Noetherianity by induction on $n$, with $C_0=K$. Let $I\ne0$ be an ideal and choose $0\ne f\in I$. If its constant term is nonzero, $I=C_n$. Otherwise, let $P_d$ be its first nonzero homogeneous part. The field $K$ is infinite because its absolute value is nontrivial. A nonzero polynomial over an infinite field cannot vanish on every point: induct on the number of variables and use the root bound in one variable. Hence, after permuting coordinates if needed, choose $a_1,\ldots,a_{n-1}\in K$ with $P_d(a_1,\ldots,a_{n-1},1)\ne0$. The invertible linear substitution $T_i\mapsto T_i+a_iT_n$ makes}
{关于 $n$ 归纳证明 Noetherian 性，起点为 $C_0=K$。设 $I\ne0$ 为理想，取 $0\ne f\in I$。若其常数项非零，则 $I=C_n$。否则，令 $P_d$ 为其首个非零齐次项。由于赋值非平凡，域 $K$ 无限。无限域上的非零多项式不可能在全部点消失：关于变量个数归纳，并使用一元多项式根数上界即可。因此，必要时排列坐标后，可选 $a_1,\ldots,a_{n-1}\in K$，使 $P_d(a_1,\ldots,a_{n-1},1)\ne0$。可逆线性代入 $T_i\mapsto T_i+a_iT_n$ 使得}
\[
 f(0,\ldots,0,T_n)=cT_n^d+\text{terms of higher degree},
 \qquad c\ne0.
\]
\bi{Write $T'=(T_1,\ldots,T_{n-1})$ and $f=\sum_j a_j(T')T_n^j$. For arbitrarily small $s>0$, one can choose a positive polyradius $r'$ such that $a_d$ is a unit in $A=K\{(r')^{-1}T'\}$ and the normalized series $a_d^{-1}f$ is distinguished of degree $d$ at radius $s$. Here are the norm checks. First choose $s$ small enough that the scalar tail $\sum_{j>d}a_j(0)T_n^j$ has $s$-norm strictly less than $|c|s^d$. At this fixed $s$, convergence leaves only finitely many coefficients outside any prescribed smaller bound for the weighted tail. Shrinking $r'$ makes those finitely many $a_j(T')-a_j(0)$ arbitrarily small, makes $a_d-c$ smaller than $|c|$, and makes $\|a_j\|s^j\le |c|s^d$ for $j<d$, since $a_j(0)=0$. The omitted tail remains uniformly small under this shrinking. Thus $\|a_d^{-1}\|=|c|^{-1}$ and all hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachweierstrassdivision} hold.}
{写 $T'=(T_1,\ldots,T_{n-1})$，并写 $f=\sum_j a_j(T')T_n^j$。对任意充分小的 $s>0$，可选正多半径 $r'$，使 $a_d$ 在 $A=K\{(r')^{-1}T'\}$ 中可逆，而归一化级数 $a_d^{-1}f$ 在半径 $s$ 处 distinguished，次数为 $d$。范数检查如下。先使 $s$ 足够小，令标量尾项 $\sum_{j>d}a_j(0)T_n^j$ 的 $s$-范数严格小于 $|c|s^d$。固定此 $s$ 后，收敛性保证只有有限个系数不满足任意指定的更小加权尾项上界。缩小 $r'$，可使这些有限个 $a_j(T')-a_j(0)$ 任意小，使 $a_d-c$ 小于 $|c|$；又因 $j<d$ 时 $a_j(0)=0$，还可使 $\|a_j\|s^j\le |c|s^d$。被略去的尾项在缩小过程中仍一致地小。因此 $\|a_d^{-1}\|=|c|^{-1}$，并满足 Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachweierstrassdivision} 的全部条件。}

\bi{Given any $g\in C_n$, take these radii small enough that $g$ also converges. Division yields $g=qf+\sum_{j<d}b_j(T')T_n^j$ with $q\in C_n$ and $b_j\in C_{n-1}$. Consequently $C_n/(f)$ is finite as a $C_{n-1}$-module. By induction $C_{n-1}$ is Noetherian, so the submodule $I/(f)$ is finitely generated over $C_{n-1}$. To recall the elementary module step, submodules of a finite free module are finitely generated by induction on its rank, using the first-coordinate ideal and its kernel; the statement for a finite module follows by inverse image under a finite free surjection. Lift generators of $I/(f)$ and adjoin $f$. They generate $I$ over $C_n$. Undoing the coordinate change proves that every ideal is finitely generated.}
{给定任意 $g\in C_n$，可把上述半径取得足够小，使 $g$ 同样收敛。除法给出 $g=qf+\sum_{j<d}b_j(T')T_n^j$，其中 $q\in C_n$、$b_j\in C_{n-1}$。故 $C_n/(f)$ 作为 $C_{n-1}$-模有限。由归纳，$C_{n-1}$ 为 Noetherian，所以子模 $I/(f)$ 在 $C_{n-1}$ 上有限生成。这里的基本模论步骤可直接回顾：按有限自由模的秩归纳，利用第一坐标理想及其核证明其子模有限生成；一般有限模的情形由有限自由满射的逆像得到。提升 $I/(f)$ 的生成元并加入 $f$，即得到 $I$ 的有限组 $C_n$-生成元。撤销坐标变换后，便知每个理想均有限生成。}

\bi{For every $q\ge1$, total-degree truncation identifies $C_n/(T)^q$ with $K[T]/(T)^q$. Its kernel is exactly $(T)^q$: partition the terms of total degree at least $q$ among the finitely many degree-$q$ monomials dividing them; the quotients remain convergent. The inverse limit of these finite polynomial truncations is the formal power-series ring.}
{对每个 $q\ge1$，按总次数截断将 $C_n/(T)^q$ 识别为 $K[T]/(T)^q$。其核恰为 $(T)^q$：将总次数至少为 $q$ 的项，按整除它们的有限个次数 $q$ 的单项式分组，所得商级数仍收敛。这些有限多项式截断的逆极限就是形式幂级数环。}
\end{proof}
