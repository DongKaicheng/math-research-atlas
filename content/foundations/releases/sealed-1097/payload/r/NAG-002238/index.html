The vertex correspondence for algebraic completions

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 760

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[The vertex correspondence for algebraic completions]
\label{v035:f5:modelvertexcorrespondence}
Let $K$ and $C$ be as in Theorem~\ref{v035:f5:exactrealization}.
Consider the formal completions of split nodal algebraic models of
$C$, with their specified identifications of the generic fibre with
$X=C^{\an}$. Their isomorphism classes over $X$ correspond bijectively
to the semistable vertex sets of $X$. For two such models
$\mathfrak C_W$ and $\mathfrak C_V$, there is a formal morphism
$\mathfrak C_W\to\mathfrak C_V$ inducing the identity on $X$ if
and only if $V\subset W$; when it exists, it is unique.
The graph attached to $\mathfrak C_V$ is $\Sigma(X,V)$ with its
original logarithmic edge lengths.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The actual smooth and nodal tube theorems and
Theorem~\ref{cand:splitmodelskeleton} show that every such model
has a semistable vertex set and the asserted graph.
Theorem~\ref{v035:f5:exactrealization} gives every semistable
vertex set. Its uniqueness as a formal model follows from
Proposition~\ref{v035:f5:vertexuniqueness}, using
Proposition~\ref{cand:separablesmoothtubehomotopy} and
Proposition~\ref{cand:nodaltubeendpoints} for the exact boundaries.

If $V\subset W$, apply the contraction in
Theorem~\ref{v035:f5:exactrealization} to an algebraic model
underlying $\mathfrak C_W$ and complete the resulting morphism.
The target has vertices $V$, so the preceding uniqueness identifies
it with $\mathfrak C_V$ over $X$. Conversely,
Lemma~\ref{v035:f5:dominationvertices} gives the required inclusion
for any such formal morphism. Its uniqueness is generic-fibre
faithfulness. Finally, the nodal tubes are exactly the annular
components of $X\setminus V$; their Gauss intervals give
$\Sigma(X,V)$, and Corollary~\ref{cand:annulusmodulusinvariance}
identifies their lengths with their logarithmic moduli.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
For the rest of this section and the next, assume that $K$ is
algebraically closed. Let $C/K$ be a smooth connected projective curve
and put $X=C^{\an}$. A split nodal algebraic model means a projective,
flat, finitely presented $O$-model of $C$ with reduced nodal special
fibre and the split pointed algebraic charts of
Theorem~\ref{cand:pointednodalvaluationchart}. The smoothing parameters
at its nodes are nonzero. Theorems~\ref{cand:projectiveformalcompletion}
and~\ref{cand:splitmodelskeleton} identify its completed generic fibre
with $X$ and supply its embedded finite graph and retraction.
The space $X$ is compact Hausdorff by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationprojectivecompact}.
