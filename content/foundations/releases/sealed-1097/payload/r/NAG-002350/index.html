The actual image of a finite affinoid map

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 971

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[The actual image of a finite affinoid map]
\label{v037:analytic:finiteimage}
Let $A\to D$ be a bounded homomorphism of generalized
$K$-affinoid algebras, finite with its canonical module norm, and
put $I=\ker(A\to D)$. Its analytic image is exactly
$\mathcal M(A/I)$, with the quotient affinoid structure, and
\[
       \dim_K\mathcal M(D)=\dim_K\mathcal M(A/I)
                              \leq\dim_K\mathcal M(A)
\]
when $D\ne0$. If $D$ is pure of analytic dimension $d$, so is
$A/I$.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The quotient is an actual affinoid algebra by
Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients}.
The injection $A/I\hookrightarrow D$ is integral by
\cite[Tag 00GK]{StacksAlg}. For $z\in\mathcal M(A/I)$ with
kernel $\mathfrak p$, lying over,
\cite[Tag 00GQ]{StacksAlg}, makes
$D\otimes_{A/I}\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ nonzero.
It is a finite-dimensional vector space. Extending it to
$\mathcal H(z)$ keeps it nonzero. Proposition
\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor} identifies this algebra with
the actual completed fibre algebra, which has an analytic point
by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion}.
The fibre identification of Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres}
therefore gives a point above $z$. This proves the image assertion.

At every point of $\mathcal M(D)$, the completed residue field
is finite over that of its image, by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitemorphismresiduefinite}.
Taking the maxima in Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidabhyankarmaximum}
on the two spaces gives equality of their analytic dimensions.
The dimension inequality follows from the same maximum formula,
since the quotient space has the same completed residue fields
as its points in $\mathcal M(A)$.

Suppose $D$ is pure of dimension $d$ and let $\mathfrak p$ be
a minimal prime of $A/I$. A prime of $D$ above $\mathfrak p$
contains a minimal prime $\mathfrak q$ of $D$, by
\cite[Tag 00E0(4)]{StacksAlg}. Its contraction equals
$\mathfrak p$ by minimality. Thus
$(A/I)/\mathfrak p\hookrightarrow D/\mathfrak q$ is a finite
injection. These quotient algebras have the canonical finite
module norms by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}.
Apply the dimension equality just proved to this injection.
The right-hand algebra has analytic dimension $d$, and hence so
does the left-hand algebra. This holds for every minimal prime.
\end{proof}
