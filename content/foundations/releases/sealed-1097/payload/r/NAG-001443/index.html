Power-controlled rational factorization

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 178

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Power-controlled rational factorization}{带 powers 控制的 rational factorization}]\label{fnd:strictpowercontrolledrational}
\bi{Keep $A,\rho,C,\phi$ as above. Let $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$ generate the unit ideal of $A$, and put}
{保持上述 $A,\rho,C,\phi$。设 $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$ 生成 $A$ 的 unit ideal，并令}
\[
 B=A\{\rho^{-1}X\}/(gX_i-f_i)_{i=1}^s.
\]
\bi{There is a bounded factorization $A\to B\to C$ if and only if $\phi(g)$ is a unit and, for each $i$, the ratios $b_i=\phi(f_i)\phi(g)^{-1}$ satisfy $\|b_i^n\|\leq C_i\rho_i^n$ for all $n\geq0$ and some $C_i$. Such a factorization is unique.}
{存在 bounded factorization $A\to B\to C$，当且仅当 $\phi(g)$ 为 unit，且对每个 $i$，ratio $b_i=\phi(f_i)\phi(g)^{-1}$ 满足：存在 $C_i$ 使全部 $n\geq0$ 都有 $\|b_i^n\|\leq C_i\rho_i^n$。这样的 factorization 唯一。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose $a_0g+\sum_i a_if_i=1$. In $B$ the equality becomes}
{选择 $a_0g+\sum_i a_if_i=1$。在 $B$ 中，此式变为}
\[
 g\left(a_0+\sum_i a_ix_i\right)=1.
\]
\bi{Thus every factorization makes $\phi(g)$ invertible and forces $\psi(x_i)=\phi(f_i)\phi(g)^{-1}$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictquotientboundedup} supplies the necessary power bounds. Conversely, the stated ratios satisfy $\phi(g)b_i=\phi(f_i)$, so that proposition constructs the unique factorization. In the zero ring, the unit equation is interpreted with $1=0$; the assertion then concerns the unique map to zero.}
{因此，每个 factorization 都使 $\phi(g)$ invertible，并强制 $\psi(x_i)=\phi(f_i)\phi(g)^{-1}$。Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictquotientboundedup} 给出必要的 powers bounds。反之，上述 ratios 满足 $\phi(g)b_i=\phi(f_i)$，所以由该 proposition 构造出唯一的 factorization。在 zero ring 中，unit equation 按 $1=0$ 理解，此时断言涉及到零的唯一 map。}
\end{proof}
