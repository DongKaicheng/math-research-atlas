Residue fields, algebraic fibres and surjectivity

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 288

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Residue fields, algebraic fibres and surjectivity}{剩余域、代数纤维及满射性}]
\label{fnd:analytificationvaluedfibres}
\bi{Let $x\in Z^{\an}$ lie above the scheme point $\xi$. Then $\mathcal H(x)$ is the completion of the valued field $\kappa(\xi)$ defined by $x$. For a morphism $Y\to Z$ of finite-type $K$-schemes, its analytic fibre over $x$ is naturally the analytification, over $\mathcal H(x)$, of $Y\times_Z\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal H(x)$. A surjective morphism of finite-type $K$-schemes induces a surjective map of analytifications.}
{设 $x\in Z^{\an}$ 位于概形点 $\xi$ 上方。则 $\mathcal H(x)$ 为 $x$ 在 $\kappa(\xi)$ 上定义的赋值域的完备化。对有限型 $K$-概形态射 $Y\to Z$，其在 $x$ 处的解析纤维自然等于 $Y\times_Z\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal H(x)$ 在 $\mathcal H(x)$ 上的解析化。有限型 $K$-概形的满射态射诱导解析化之间的满射。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Work first on an affine neighbourhood $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ of $\xi$ and a radius piece $A=K\{r^{-1}T\}/I$ containing an ordinary neighbourhood of $x$. Polynomial density shows that the image of $R$ is dense in $A$, also for the seminorm at $x$. Its fraction field modulo the point kernel is therefore dense in $\mathcal H_A(x)$: approximate each numerator and denominator by polynomials, and keep denominators nonzero by sufficiently close approximation. Corollary~\ref{fnd:domainresiduefields} makes the identification independent of the affinoid neighbourhood. Since $\kappa(\xi)=\operatorname{Frac}(R/\ker x)$, this proves the first assertion.}
{先在 $\xi$ 的仿射邻域 $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ 上，取包含 $x$ 普通邻域的半径分片 $A=K\{r^{-1}T\}/I$。多项式稠密性说明 $R$ 的像在 $A$ 中稠密，对 $x$ 的半范数也如此。因此将点核除去后的分式域在 $\mathcal H_A(x)$ 中稠密：分别用多项式逼近分子和分母，并通过足够精确的逼近保持分母非零。Corollary~\ref{fnd:domainresiduefields} 保证此识别与 affinoid 邻域的选择无关。由于 $\kappa(\xi)=\operatorname{Frac}(R/\ker x)$，第一项得证。}

\bi{Put $F=\mathcal H(x)$. The scheme morphism $\operatorname{Spec}(F)\to Z_F$ determined by $x$ is an $F$-rational point. Analytifying its fibre square over $F$, Theorems~\ref{fnd:analytificationproducts} and \ref{fnd:analytificationfiniteclosed} identify the resulting analytic fibre with the fibre of $Y_F^{\an}\to Z_F^{\an}$ over this rational point. On an affinoid chart $A\to B$ about the original map, that fibre has algebra $B\widehat\otimes_A F$, by Corollary~\ref{fnd:iteratedaffinoidbasechange} and Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres}. The identification agrees on chart intersections, proving the fibre assertion.}
{置 $F=\mathcal H(x)$。由 $x$ 决定的概形态射 $\operatorname{Spec}(F)\to Z_F$ 是一个 $F$-有理点。在 $F$ 上解析化其纤维方块，Theorems~\ref{fnd:analytificationproducts} 与 \ref{fnd:analytificationfiniteclosed} 将得到的解析纤维识别为 $Y_F^{\an}\to Z_F^{\an}$ 在此有理点处的纤维。在原映射附近的 affinoid 图 $A\to B$ 上，由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:iteratedaffinoidbasechange} 与 Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres}，该纤维的代数为 $B\widehat\otimes_A F$。这些识别在图的交上相容，故证明纤维断言。}

\bi{Now assume $Y\to Z$ is surjective. Its scheme fibre over $\xi$ is nonempty. It stays nonempty after extension from $\kappa(\xi)$ to $F$: a nonzero affine algebra in that fibre remains nonzero under a field extension, as follows by extending a vector-space basis. Thus $Y\times_Z\operatorname{Spec}(F)$ has a nonempty affine open of finite type over $F$. Choose a maximal ideal in its nonzero coordinate ring. Its residue field $E$ is finite over $F$ by the Hilbert Nullstellensatz, \cite[Tag 00FV, Theorem~10.34.1(1)]{StacksAlg}. Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} extends the absolute value to $E$ and makes it complete. This valued closed point gives a point of the analytic fibre and hence a point above $x$.}
{现设 $Y\to Z$ 满射。它在 $\xi$ 上的概形纤维非空。由 $\kappa(\xi)$ 扩域至 $F$ 后仍非空：该纤维中的非零仿射代数在扩域后保持非零，这可通过扩张向量空间的一组基看出。因此 $Y\times_Z\operatorname{Spec}(F)$ 有一个在 $F$ 上有限型的非空仿射开集。在其非零坐标环中选择极大理想；由 Hilbert 零点定理 \cite[Tag 00FV, Theorem~10.34.1(1)]{StacksAlg}，其剩余域 $E$ 在 $F$ 上有限。Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 将绝对值延拓到 $E$，并说明 $E$ 完备。此赋值闭点给出解析纤维的一个点，即 $x$ 上方的一个点。}
\end{proof}
