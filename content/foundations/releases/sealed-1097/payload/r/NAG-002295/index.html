A finite polyannulus neighborhood of a level

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 955

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[A finite polyannulus neighborhood of a level]
\label{v036:analytic:finitecollar}
Let $X=\mathcal M(B)$, let $g_1,\ldots,g_d\in B^\times$, and set
\[
 q:X\longrightarrow\mathbb R^d,
 \qquad q(x)=(-\log|g_1(x)|,\ldots,-\log|g_d(x)|).
\]
Suppose $s_0\in q(X)\setminus q(\partial_KX)$. There is a closed
rectangular neighborhood $P$ of $s_0$, with $s_0$ in its interior,
such that $Y=q^{-1}(P)$ is an actual affinoid domain of $X$,
$Y\subset\operatorname{Int}(X/K)$, and
\[
             g:Y\longrightarrow\mathbb T_P
\]
is finite with the canonical finite-module norm on its algebra.

If $X$ is pure of analytic dimension $d$, this map is surjective.
Its Gauss-fibre dimension is one fixed positive integer on $P$.
For every $s\in P$, its restriction to the exact-radius polytorus
at $s$ is the actual algebra of the whole level $q^{-1}(s)$.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The set $\partial_KX$ is closed, by the openness of affinoid
interior in Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus}. It is
compact by Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact}; its image
under $q$ is compact and hence closed. Choose a closed box $P$
around $s_0$ disjoint from that image. The simultaneous inequalities
on $g_j$ and $g_j^{-1}$ give the domain $Y$ with its actual
affinoid algebra, by Theorems~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints}
and~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal}.

Choose a larger closed polyannulus $\mathbb T_{P^+}$ whose coordinate
bounds strictly exceed the norms of all $g_j$ and $g_j^{-1}$ and
which contains $\mathbb T_P$. Bounded substitution, as in
Theorem~\ref{v035:analytic:levelisfinite}, gives
$X\to\mathbb T_{P^+}$. At every point of $Y$, the affinoid
composition formula
\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcompositionequality} makes this morphism
inner, since the point is in $\operatorname{Int}(X/K)$. The actual
relative pushout of Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout}
identifies the algebra of $Y$ with
\[
                  B\widehat\otimes_{A_{P^+}}A_P.
\]
Indeed both presentations impose exactly the stated bounds on the
images of $g_j$ and $g_j^{-1}$; the rational universal maps in both
directions fix these images and the coefficients. Interior is
preserved by base change, by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus}. Hence every point
of $Y$ is inner over $\mathbb T_P$.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidboundarylessfinite} proves finiteness and
the norm assertion.

If $X$ is pure of analytic dimension $d$, then $Y$ is nonempty
and pure of the same dimension by
Theorem~\ref{v036:analytic:domainpurity}. Proposition
\ref{v036:analytic:polyannulusdegree} gives surjectivity and a
constant positive Gauss degree. Base change from $A_P$ to the
exact-radius polytorus algebra imposes the equalities
$|g_j|=e^{-s_j}$, so the same actual rational universal property
identifies it with the whole level algebra. This includes every
real $s$, without changing the original absolute value.
\end{proof}
