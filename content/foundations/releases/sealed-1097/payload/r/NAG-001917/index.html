Coherence of formal coefficient rings

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 519

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Coherence of formal coefficient rings}
{形式系数环的凝聚性}]
\label{fnd:formalringcoherence}
\bi{Every topologically finitely presented $O$-algebra is coherent.}
{每个拓扑有限表示的 $O$-代数都是凝聚环。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For $P=O\langle T\rangle$, the image of a homomorphism
$P^r\to P^s$ is finite and $O$-torsion-free, so its presentation
kernel is finite by Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation}.
Thus all such kernels are finite. Now write $A_0=P/(g_1,\ldots,g_t)$.
Lift a matrix $A_0^r\to A_0^s$ to a matrix over $P$, and augment it
by the $st$ columns $g_j e_i$. The kernel of this augmented matrix
is finite over $P$. Projection to the first $r$ coordinates, followed
by reduction modulo $(g_1,\ldots,g_t)$, maps it onto the kernel of
the original matrix. Hence that kernel is finite over $A_0$, which
is the finite-matrix criterion for coherence.}
{对于 $P=O\langle T\rangle$，同态 $P^r\to P^s$ 的像有限且无
$O$-挠，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation}，
其表示核有限。因此所有此类矩阵的核均有限。现写
$A_0=P/(g_1,\ldots,g_t)$，将矩阵 $A_0^r\to A_0^s$ 提升到
$P$ 上，并添入 $st$ 个列向量 $g_j e_i$。扩充矩阵的核在 $P$
上有限；投影到前 $r$ 个坐标，再模掉 $(g_1,\ldots,g_t)$，便将
该核满射到原矩阵的核。故原矩阵的核在 $A_0$ 上有限，这正是
凝聚性的有限矩阵判据。}
\end{proof}
