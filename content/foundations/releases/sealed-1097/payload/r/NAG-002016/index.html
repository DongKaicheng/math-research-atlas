The monomial simplex and its residual data

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 620

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{The monomial simplex and its residual data}
{单项式单纯形及其剩余数据}]
\label{fnd:standardmonomialchartgeometry}
\bi{Let $K$ be complete and nontrivially valued, let
$0<|a|<1$, and let $0\leq r\leq d$. Put}
{设 $K$ 完备且非平凡赋值，$0<|a|<1$，$0\leq r\leq d$。
令}
\[
 A_0=K^\circ\langle X_0,\ldots,X_r,T_1,\ldots,T_{d-r}\rangle
             /(X_0\cdots X_r-a),\qquad A=A_0[1/a].
\]
\bi{Then $A_0$ is admissible and $\mathcal M(A)$ is the
rational domain $|a|\leq|X_1\cdots X_r|$ in the unit
$d$-dimensional polydisc with coordinates $X_1,\ldots,X_r,T$.
The monomial points parametrized by}
{则 $A_0$ 为 admissible，且 $\mathcal M(A)$ 是以
$X_1,\ldots,X_r,T$ 为坐标的 $d$ 维单位多圆盘中的 rational
域 $|a|\leq|X_1\cdots X_r|$。由下列集合参数化的单项式点}
\[
 \Delta_a=\{(u_0,\ldots,u_r)\in\mathbb R_{\geq0}^{r+1}:
                         u_0+\cdots+u_r=v(a)\}
\]
\bi{form a subspace homeomorphic to $\Delta_a$. At every
such point $x_u$, the completed residue field is the completion
of $K(X_1,\ldots,X_r,T)$ for its Gauss valuation of weights
$u_1,\ldots,u_r,0,\ldots,0$, and}
{组成一个与 $\Delta_a$ 同胚的子空间。在每个这样的点
$x_u$ 处，完备剩余域是 $K(X_1,\ldots,X_r,T)$ 对权
$u_1,\ldots,u_r,0,\ldots,0$ 的 Gauss 赋值的完备化，且}
\[
 s_K(x_u)+t_K(x_u)=\dim_{x_u}\mathcal M(A)=d.
\]
\bi{If all $u_i>0$, specialization is the generic point of
the stratum $X_0=\cdots=X_r=0$, of dimension $d-r$.
Any finite family of Laurent monomials in the $X_i$ with
coefficients in $K$ and value one at $x_u$ becomes integral on
a formal affine neighborhood of its specialization after an
admissible formal modification. Its nonzero residues there are
the original residues in $\widetilde{\mathcal H(x_u)}$.}
{若所有 $u_i>0$，则特化为分层 $X_0=\cdots=X_r=0$ 的
泛点，该分层维数为 $d-r$。任意有限个系数属于 $K$、在
$x_u$ 处绝对值为一的 $X_i$ 的 Laurent 单项式，在某个
admissible 形式修正后，都在其特化点的一个形式仿射邻域
上成为整系数元素；它们在那里得到的非零剩余类就是原来
在 $\widetilde{\mathcal H(x_u)}$ 中的剩余类。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{\emph{Integral normal form.} Write $M=X_0\cdots X_r$.
In a restricted series replace a monomial $X^\nu T^\mu$ by
$a^qX^{\nu-q\mathbf1}T^\mu$, where $q=\min_i\nu_i$.
There is a unique resulting series supported on exponent
vectors whose minimum $X$-exponent is zero. Its coefficient
at such a vector $(\lambda,\mu)$ is}
{\emph{整系数正规形。} 记 $M=X_0\cdots X_r$。在受限
级数中，将单项式 $X^\nu T^\mu$ 替换为
$a^qX^{\nu-q\mathbf1}T^\mu$，其中 $q=\min_i\nu_i$。
所得级数唯一支撑在最小 $X$-指数为零的指数向量上；在
这样的向量 $(\lambda,\mu)$ 处，其系数为}
\[
          \sum_{q\geq0}a^q c_{\lambda+q\mathbf1,\mu}.
\]
\bi{These sums converge and preserve integral coefficients.
The identity}
{这些和收敛并保留整系数。恒等式}
\[
 M^q-a^q=(M-a)\sum_{j=0}^{q-1}a^jM^{q-1-j}
\]
\bi{gives a contractive division operator on polynomials.
The coefficients of its value on a restricted series are
$\sum_{q\geq1}a^{q-1}c_{\nu+q\mathbf1,\mu}$;
the finite-tail condition makes this a restricted series again.
Thus the polynomial division identities extend continuously,
and their kernel is exactly $(M-a)$, not just its closure.
The quotient is the torsion-free module of these normal
forms. Hence $A_0$ is admissible by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation}. Inverting
$a$ gives its complete strict affinoid algebra by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations}. Eliminating
$X_0=a/(X_1\cdots X_r)$ is exactly the rational-localization
presentation asserted in the statement. For $r=0$ the empty
product is one and the domain is the whole $d$-polydisc.}
{在多项式上给出压缩范数的除法算子。对受限级数，其像的
系数为 $\sum_{q\geq1}a^{q-1}c_{\nu+q\mathbf1,\mu}$；
有限尾条件保证所得仍为受限级数。因此多项式除法恒等式
连续延拓，其核恰为 $(M-a)$，不只是该理想的闭包。商为
上述正规形组成的无挠模。于是由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation}，$A_0$
为 admissible。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations}，
逆转 $a$ 得到其完备 strict affinoid 代数。消去
$X_0=a/(X_1\cdots X_r)$，正好给出陈述中的 rational
局部化表示。当 $r=0$ 时，空乘积为一，该域就是整个
$d$ 维多圆盘。}

\bi{\emph{Points and topology.} At $u\in\Delta_a$, give
$X_i$ value $u_i$ and each $T_j$ value zero. After eliminating
$X_0$, the Gauss norm is multiplicative by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm}, and all original coordinates
have norm at most one. It therefore extends to a bounded
multiplicative seminorm on $A$. A polynomial becomes a finite
Laurent polynomial after elimination; its value is a maximum
of finitely many continuous functions of $u$. Restricted-series
tails tend to zero uniformly on $\Delta_a$, because all original
coordinates have norm at most one. Thus the same continuity
holds for every element of $A$. The inverse on the image is
$x\mapsto(v(X_0(x)),\ldots,v(X_r(x)))$; the coordinates are
invertible in $A$, so these functions are continuous. This proves
the claimed homeomorphism.}
{\emph{点与拓扑。} 对 $u\in\Delta_a$，赋予 $X_i$ 值
$u_i$，赋予每个 $T_j$ 值零。消去 $X_0$ 后，由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm}，Gauss 范数具有乘性，
且所有原坐标的范数至多为一，因此它延拓为 $A$ 上的有界
乘性半范数。消元后，多项式变为有限 Laurent 多项式，
其取值为有限个 $u$ 的连续函数的最大值。由于原坐标的
范数都至多为一，受限级数的尾项在 $\Delta_a$ 上一致趋于
零，因此每个 $A$ 中元素的取值也连续。像上的逆映射为
$x\mapsto(v(X_0(x)),\ldots,v(X_r(x)))$；因这些坐标在
$A$ 中可逆，各函数都连续，故得到所述同胚。}

\bi{\emph{Residue fields and dimension.} The Laurent
polynomial algebra $K[X_1^{\pm1},\ldots,X_r^{\pm1},T]$ is
dense in $A$ and its evaluation at $x_u$ is injective. Indeed
distinct normal-form monomials give distinct Laurent exponents,
so their Gauss values cannot cancel. Passing to fractions and
then completion identifies $\mathcal H(x_u)$ with the stated
completed Gauss field; density is preserved on taking fractions
because inversion is continuous at every nonzero element.
Proposition~\ref{new:gauss_residue_formula} gives $d_K(x_u)=d$.
The rational-domain description and
Corollary~\ref{fnd:affinoiddimensiondomaincover} give
$\dim\mathcal M(A)\leq d$. The local Abhyankar inequality
in Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticabhyankar} now gives}
{\emph{剩余域与维数。} Laurent 多项式代数
$K[X_1^{\pm1},\ldots,X_r^{\pm1},T]$ 在 $A$ 中稠密，
且在 $x_u$ 处的取值为单射。事实上，不同正规形单项式
给出不同 Laurent 指数，其 Gauss 取值不能抵消。取分式
再完备化，就将 $\mathcal H(x_u)$ 识别为所述完备 Gauss
域；因为逆运算在每个非零元素处连续，取分式保持这里的
稠密性。Proposition~\ref{new:gauss_residue_formula}
给出 $d_K(x_u)=d$。由 rational 域表示及
Corollary~\ref{fnd:affinoiddimensiondomaincover}，有
$\dim\mathcal M(A)\leq d$。再用
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticabhyankar} 的局部
Abhyankar 不等式，得到}
\[
 d=d_K(x_u)\leq\dim_{x_u}\mathcal M(A)
                  \leq\dim\mathcal M(A)\leq d.
\]

\bi{If all $u_i>0$, every $X_i$ reduces to zero, whereas
the reductions of the $T_j$ are algebraically independent by
the same Gauss calculation. The kernel of special-fibre
evaluation is consequently $(x_0,\ldots,x_r)$. The stratum
is affine $(d-r)$-space, and its dimension is $d-r$ by
\cite[Tag 00P0]{StacksAlg}. This proves the specialization
assertion.}
{若所有 $u_i>0$，则每个 $X_i$ 都约化为零，而由同一
Gauss 计算，各 $T_j$ 的约化代数独立。因此特殊纤维取值
的核为 $(x_0,\ldots,x_r)$。该分层是 $d-r$ 维仿射空间，
由 \cite[Tag 00P0]{StacksAlg}，其维数为 $d-r$。
这证明特化断言。}

\bi{\emph{Making a residue visible on a model.} Write one
of the specified monomials as $h=f/g$ with $f,g\in A_0$
monomials having integral coefficients. This is possible after
multiplication of numerator and denominator by a scalar.
The denominator $g=cX^\nu$ divides a nonzero element of
$K^\circ$: choose $N\geq\max_i\nu_i$ and use
$gX^{N\mathbf1-\nu}=ca^N$. Hence $(f,g)$ is an open ideal.
The $g$-chart of its admissible blowup has $f/g$ integral by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:formalblowupcharts}, and contains the unique
generic lift of $x_u$ since $|f(x_u)|=|g(x_u)|$. For a finite
family of monomials, blow up the product of these open ideals.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:formalglobalblowupcalculus} makes the resulting
modification dominate all the individual blowups. The
intersection of the inverse images of their $g$-charts is a
formal open containing the specialization of the lifted point,
by functoriality of specialization and
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}. Take a
formal affine neighborhood inside that intersection. All the
chosen monomials are integral there, and their value one at
the point makes their reductions nonzero. Generic-fibre
invariance and evaluation identify them with the original
residues.}
{\emph{在模型上显现剩余类。} 将指定单项式之一写为
$h=f/g$，其中 $f,g\in A_0$ 是具有整系数的单项式；
同时以某个标量乘分子、分母即可做到。分母
$g=cX^\nu$ 整除 $K^\circ$ 的某个非零元素：选择
$N\geq\max_i\nu_i$，便有
$gX^{N\mathbf1-\nu}=ca^N$。所以 $(f,g)$ 为开理想。
由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:formalblowupcharts}，其 admissible
blowup 的 $g$-图中 $f/g$ 为整系数元素；因为
$|f(x_u)|=|g(x_u)|$，该图包含 $x_u$ 的唯一泛纤维提升。
对有限个单项式，blow up 这些开理想的乘积。由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:formalglobalblowupcalculus}，所得修正
支配各个单独的 blowup。由特化的函子性及
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}，各
$g$-图逆像的交为包含提升点特化的形式开集。在此交内取
形式仿射邻域，所选单项式都成为整系数元素；其在该点的
绝对值为一，故约化非零。泛纤维不变性及取值将它们识别
为原来的剩余类。}
\end{proof}
