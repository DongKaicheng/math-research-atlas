Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Iterated base change}{迭代基变换}]
\label{fnd:iteratedaffinoidbasechange}
\bi{For compatible bounded affinoid maps $A\to B$, $A\to C\to D$, allowing complete coefficient-field extensions for $C,D$, there is a natural bounded isomorphism with bounded inverse}
{对相容的有界 affinoid 映射 $A\to B$、$A\to C\to D$，允许 $C,D$ 的系数域为完备扩域，存在自然有界同构且其逆有界：}
\[
 (B\widehat\otimes_A C)\widehat\otimes_C D
          \simeq B\widehat\otimes_A D .
\]
\bi{For complete valued extensions $K\subset L\subset L'$, this includes $(A_L)_{L'}\simeq A_{L'}$. All comparison maps commute with the structural homomorphisms.}
{对完备赋值扩域 $K\subset L\subset L'$，这包括 $(A_L)_{L'}\simeq A_{L'}$。各比较映射与结构同态交换。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Both algebras represent compatible bounded homomorphisms from $B$ and $D$ agreeing on $A$, by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout}. Applying that universal property in both directions gives inverse maps; their composites are identities by uniqueness. The same argument applies to the scalar-extension pushouts.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout}，两个代数都表示来自 $B,D$ 且在 $A$ 上一致的相容有界同态。分别向两个方向应用泛性质，得到互逆映射；由唯一性，两个复合为恒等映射。标量扩张的推出采用相同论证。}
\end{proof}
