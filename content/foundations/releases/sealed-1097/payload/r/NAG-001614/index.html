Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Gluing analytic spaces}{解析空间的粘合}]
\label{fnd:analyticspacegluing}
\bi{Let $(X_i)$ be analytic spaces with analytic domains $X_{ij}\subseteq X_i$ and isomorphisms $v_{ij}:X_{ij}\simeq X_{ji}$ satisfying the identity and cocycle conditions. Their analytic gluing exists and is unique in each of the following cases:}
{设解析空间族 $(X_i)$ 配有解析域 $X_{ij}\subseteq X_i$ 及满足恒等与 cocycle 条件的同构 $v_{ij}:X_{ij}\simeq X_{ji}$。在下述每种情形，其解析粘合存在且唯一：}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \bi{all $X_{ij}$ are open in $X_i$;}{所有 $X_{ij}$ 都在 $X_i$ 中开；}
\item \bi{each $X_i$ is Hausdorff, every $X_{ij}$ is closed in $X_i$, and for each $i$ only finitely many $X_{ij}$ are nonempty.}{每个 $X_i$ 为 Hausdorff，每个 $X_{ij}$ 在 $X_i$ 中闭，且对每个 $i$，只有有限个 $X_{ij}$ 非空。}
\end{enumerate}
\bi{The images of the $X_i$ are analytic domains and form an admissible cover. They are open in the first case. In the second case they are closed and locally finite, and the glued space is Hausdorff. The gluing has the usual mapping universal property.}
{各 $X_i$ 的像是解析域，且构成 admissible 覆盖。在第一种情形，这些像为开集；在第二种情形，它们闭且局部有限，而粘合空间为 Hausdorff。粘合满足通常的映射泛性质。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Form the quotient $X$ of the disjoint union $S=\coprod_iX_i$ by the prescribed identifications. In the open case, the saturation of an open subset of one $X_i$ is open in every $X_j$, so the quotient map is open and each $X_i$ embeds as an open subspace. Hence $X$ is locally Hausdorff.}
{将不交并 $S=\coprod_iX_i$ 按指定识别取商，记为 $X$。在开粘合情形，一个 $X_i$ 中开子集的饱和在每个 $X_j$ 中都开，所以商映射为开映射，且每个 $X_i$ 嵌入为开子空间。因此 $X$ 局部 Hausdorff。}

\bi{In the second case, the saturation of a closed subset of $S$ meets each $X_i$ in a finite union of closed sets. Thus the quotient map $q$ is closed, and each $X_i$ embeds as a closed subspace. Each equivalence class is finite, since all its representatives meet any one of its representatives' components. Distinct classes are disjoint finite subsets of the Hausdorff space $S$, so they have disjoint open neighborhoods $P,Q$ in $S$. The complements of $q(S\setminus P)$ and $q(S\setminus Q)$ are disjoint open neighborhoods of the corresponding quotient points. Thus $X$ is Hausdorff. Fix $i$ and let $J_i$ be the finite set of indices meeting $X_i$. The union of the images of $X_j$ for $j\notin J_i$ is closed: on each component its inverse image is either the entire component or a finite union of closed overlaps. Its complement is an open neighborhood of the image of $X_i$ meeting only the finitely many components indexed by $J_i$. This proves local finiteness. A locally finite closed cover is a quasi-net, by applying the finite closed-cover assertion locally.}
{在第二种情形，$S$ 中闭子集的饱和与每个 $X_i$ 的交都是有限个闭集之并。因此商映射 $q$ 为闭映射，每个 $X_i$ 嵌入为闭子空间。每个等价类都是有限的，因为其所有代表元所在的分量都与任意一个代表元所在分量相交。不同等价类为 Hausdorff 空间 $S$ 中不交的有限子集，故具有不交的开邻域 $P,Q$。$q(S\setminus P)$ 与 $q(S\setminus Q)$ 的补集给出对应商点的不交开邻域，所以 $X$ 为 Hausdorff。固定 $i$，令 $J_i$ 为与 $X_i$ 相交的有限指标集。所有 $j\notin J_i$ 对应像的并是闭的：它在每个分量上的逆像或者为整个分量，或者为有限个闭交叠之并。其补集是 $X_i$ 的像的开邻域，只与 $J_i$ 所指标的有限个分量相交。因此这些像局部有限。局部有限闭覆盖在局部归约到有限闭覆盖，故为 quasi-net。}

\bi{Take all images of affinoid chart domains in the $X_i$. Their family is a quasi-net by transitivity. On a pairwise intersection, the given analytic-domain isomorphism identifies the chart nets and their bounded restriction maps; refining inside the overlap gives the net axiom. If a chart from $X_i$ is contained in a chart from $X_j$, transport it through $X_{ij}\simeq X_{ji}$. It is an affinoid analytic domain in $X_j$, so Corollary~\ref{fnd:intrinsicaffinoidatlas} supplies the required bounded affinoid-domain inclusion map. The cocycle identity gives composition compatibility. This is an affinoid atlas on $X$. It identifies each $X_i$ with its analytic-domain image. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing} gives the mapping universal property and uniqueness.}
{取所有 $X_i$ 的 affinoid chart 域在 $X$ 中的像。由 quasi-net 传递性，这个族为 quasi-net。在两两交叠上，给定的解析域同构识别 chart net 及其有界限制映射；在交叠内加细即可得到 net 公理。若来自 $X_i$ 的一个 chart 包含于来自 $X_j$ 的 chart，就通过 $X_{ij}\simeq X_{ji}$ 将它传到 $X_j$。它是 $X_j$ 中同构于 affinoid 的解析域，故 Corollary~\ref{fnd:intrinsicaffinoidatlas} 给出所需有界 affinoid 域包含映射。Cocycle 恒等式保证复合相容。这样得到 $X$ 上的 affinoid atlas，并将每个 $X_i$ 识别为其解析域像。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing} 最后给出映射泛性质及唯一性。}
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[\bi{Locally bounded morphisms}{局部有界态射}]
\label{fnd:generalnetmorphisms}
\bi{For two affinoid atlases, a morphism consists of a continuous map $f:X\to X'$ and, for every pair of charts $U\in\bar\tau$, $V\in\bar\tau'$ with $f(U)\subseteq V$, a bounded homomorphism $f_{U,V}:A_V\to A_U$ inducing $f|_U$. These maps commute with restrictions in both charts. We require that the charts $U$ admitting such a target chart $V$ form a quasi-net of $X$. An analytic space is an affinoid atlas considered up to the canonical identifications under chart refinement of Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf}.}
{对两个 affinoid atlas，一个态射由连续映射 $f:X\to X'$ 及下述资料组成：对每对满足 $f(U)\subseteq V$ 的 chart $U\in\bar\tau$、$V\in\bar\tau'$，给出诱导 $f|_U$ 的有界同态 $f_{U,V}:A_V\to A_U$。这些映射与源、目标 chart 的限制均相容，并要求具有这样一个目标 chart $V$ 的源 chart $U$ 构成 $X$ 的 quasi-net。将 affinoid atlas 按 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf} 中 chart 加细所给的典范识别视为同一对象，得到解析空间。}
\end{definition}
