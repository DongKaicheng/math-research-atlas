Algebraic coordinates on a common affine open

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1078

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[Algebraic coordinates on a common affine open]
\label{v038:faithful:chartpreparation}
There is an affine open $\mathcal W=\operatorname{Spec}D$ of
$\mathcal X$ containing every specialization of $S$. There
is a finite collection of affine \etale semistable charts
$\mathcal V_\lambda=\operatorname{Spec}C_\lambda$ contained
in $\mathcal W$ whose completed generic fibres cover $S$.
For each pair $(I,\eta)$ in a chart one may choose
$h_{\lambda,I,\eta}\in C_\lambda$ such that
\[
 E_{\lambda,I,\eta}:=(S_I)_{\overline h_{\lambda,I,\eta}}
\]
is a nonempty smooth integral open of the component labelled
by $\eta$ and meets no other component of $S_I$.

Fix a faithful rational tuple $F_0$ as in
Theorem~\ref{v035:faithfulglobal}. There are a finite list
$P\subset D\setminus\{0\}$, a dense open
\[
 U=\operatorname{Spec}D_K\left[1/\prod_{p\in P}p\right]
       \subset X,
\]
and a finite tuple $F\subset K(X)^\times$ with the following
properties. The open $U$ is contained in every
$\mathcal V_{\lambda,K}$ and contains $S$ analytically.
The tuple $F$ contains $P$, $F_0$, finite nonzero
$O$-algebra generating sets of $D$ and every $C_\lambda$,
the chart coordinates, and every $h_{\lambda,I,\eta}$.
Each member of $F$ is a ratio of members of $P$, or itself
a member of $P$. All members of $F$ are units on $U$, and
they define a closed immersion
$U\hookrightarrow\mathbf G_{m,K}^{|F|}$. Its restriction
to $S$ is faithful and unimodular on a finite subdivision.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Proposition~\ref{v035:faithfulrationalpl} makes the set $Z$
of skeletal specializations finite. Apply
Lemma~\ref{v035:faithfulaffinepairs} to obtain $\mathcal W$.
For each point of $Z$ in an original chart, choose a principal
open neighbourhood in that chart contained in $\mathcal W$.
There are only finitely many choices. A principal open is
\etale over its chart, and the restricted map to the standard
model is \etale by \cite[Tag 00U2]{StacksAlg}.
At every skeletal point specializing to the chosen point,
the defining function of this principal open has norm one.
Lemma~\ref{cand:affineflatformalcompletion} and
Proposition~\ref{cand:semistableformalopenrestriction}
therefore show that these completed opens cover $S$ and have
the restricted skeletons claimed.

The algebras $D$ and $C_\lambda$ are finitely presented over
$O$: apply \cite[Tag 01TQ]{StacksAlg} to the restrictions of
the finitely presented structural morphism to these affine
opens. They are $O$-flat. Thus they inject into their generic
coordinate rings, which in turn inject into $K(X)$.

For fixed $(\lambda,I)$, the distinct primes in $Q_I$ are
incomparable. For each $\eta'\ne\eta$, choose an element
of the prime of $\eta'$ outside the prime of $\eta$, and
multiply these finitely many elements. They may be chosen
in $C_\lambda\otimes_O k$, by taking the inverse images
of the stratum primes in this ring. Lift the product to
$h_{\lambda,I,\eta}\in C_\lambda$. If $Q_I$ is a singleton,
take $h_{\lambda,I,\eta}=1$. The stratum is smooth over $k$,
by \etale base change and smooth composition
\cite[Tags 00U2, 036U]{StacksAlg}; it is reduced by
\cite[Tag 056T]{StacksAlg}. The principal open thus has
exactly the chosen minimal prime and is integral. It is
nonempty, smooth, and contains $\eta$. In particular each
chosen $h$ is a nonzero element of $K(X)$.

Let $A$ be the finite list consisting of $F_0$, nonzero
algebra generators for the charts, their standard
coordinates, and the chosen $h$'s. For every $f\in A$ write
$f=p_f/q_f$ with nonzero $p_f,q_f\in D$. This is possible
because $\operatorname{Frac}(D)=K(X)$; denominators from $K$
can be cleared by a power of $\pi$.
For each $\lambda$, choose $b_\lambda\in D\setminus\{0\}$
whose principal generic open is contained in the nonempty
open $\mathcal V_{\lambda,K}$ of $\mathcal W_K$.
Such a principal open exists by the affine open basis; again
clear a scalar denominator to put $b_\lambda$ in $D$.
Let $P$ contain these $b_\lambda$, all $p_f,q_f$, and a
finite nonzero generating set for $D$ over $O$, and put
$F=A\cup P$. Zero generators can be omitted.

Every point of $S$ is a norm on $K(X)$ by
Proposition~\ref{v035:faithfulrationalpl}. It consequently
has generic scheme support and belongs to $U^{\an}$.
The elements $b_\lambda$ give $U\subset\mathcal V_{\lambda,K}$.
The Laurent coordinate map associated with $F$ surjects
onto $D_K[1/\prod P]$: its image contains the generators
of $D_K$ and the inverses of every member of $P$.
This proves the closed immersion assertion.
Finally all added rational coordinates have finite
piecewise $v(K^\times)$-integral-affine values on $S$ by
the same proposition. Retaining $F_0$ retains injectivity
and an integral left inverse on every tangent lattice.
A common finite subdivision, followed by the integral graph
argument in Theorem~\ref{v035:faithfulglobal}, gives the
last assertion.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
Let $K$ be complete for its specified nontrivial real-valued
absolute value. Write $O=K^\circ$, $k=\widetilde K$, and
$v=-\log|\cdot|$, and fix $\pi\in O$ with $0<|\pi|<1$.
We use the algebraic model setting of
Theorem~\ref{v035:faithfulglobal}: $\mathcal X$ is a given
projective, flat, finitely presented $O$-scheme, its generic
fibre $X$ is smooth and integral, and a finite collection of
affine opens covering $\mathcal X_s$ has \etale maps to
\[
 \operatorname{Spec}R_\lambda,\qquad
 R_\lambda=
 O[X_0,\ldots,X_{r_\lambda},T_1,\ldots,T_{q_\lambda}]/
                 (X_0\cdots X_{r_\lambda}-a_\lambda),
 \quad 0<|a_\lambda|<1.
\]
Let $S\subset X^{\an}$ be the compact skeleton of the completed
model. All rational functions below belong to the original
field $K(X)$.

For such a chart $\mathcal V=\operatorname{Spec}C$ and a
nonempty $I\subset\{0,\ldots,r\}$, write $S_I$ for its
residual stratum over
\[
 D_I=\{X_i=0\ (i\in I),\ X_j\ne0\ (j\notin I)\}
\]
and $Q_I$ for the finite set of generic points of $S_I$.
These are the strata and labels of
Proposition~\ref{cand:actualskeletalfibrestrata}. If
$s=x_{\eta,w}$, then $I=\{i:w_i>0\}$ and
$\eta\in Q_I$. An irreducible residual component in this
section is a component of this locally closed stratum.
