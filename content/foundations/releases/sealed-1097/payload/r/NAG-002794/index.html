\begin{lemma}[Closed subspace neighbourhood; F8RC-N]
\label{lem:neighbourhood}
Let \(X\) be an analytic space, \(Z\hookrightarrow X\) a closed analytic
subspace, and \(W\subset X\) an analytic domain containing \(|Z|\).
Then \(W\) contains an ordinary open neighbourhood of \(|Z|\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For \(z\in Z\), the \hyperref[fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing]{domain universal property} identifies
\(Z\times_XW\) with \(Z\), since the entire map \(Z\to X\) factors
through \(W\).

For the closed immersion into \(W\), one may check the pullback on
actual affinoid domains of \(W\) subordinate to affinoid charts of \(X\).
On these charts the \hyperref[fnd:generalizedfinitetensor]{canonical finite tensor} of the \hyperref[fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients]{closed ideal quotient} is the actual closed quotient. This verifies the closed
immersion by the target-local criterion \hyperref[fnd:closedimmersiontargetglocal]{NAG-001653}.

Both maps \((Z,z)\to(X,z)\) and \((Z,z)\to(W,z)\)
are closed immersions of analytic germs. By \hyperref[fnd:gradedgermclosedimmersion]{NAG-001978}, their
maps on reductions are isomorphisms. Functoriality makes
\(\mathcal R(W,z)\to\mathcal R(X,z)\) an isomorphism.

The reduction functor \hyperref[fnd:allgradedgermreduction]{NAG-001972} identifies the reduction of
\((W,z)\) with an open subspace of the reduction of \((X,z)\).
The isomorphism above makes this subspace the whole reduction.
By the subdomain classification \hyperref[fnd:allgermdomainclassification]{NAG-001973}, \((W,z)\) and
\((X,z)\) are the same subdomain germ of \((X,z)\). Thus some ordinary
open neighbourhood of \(z\) in \(X\) lies in \(W\). Taking the union
over \(z\) proves the assertion.
\end{proof}
