The ring of balanced cycles

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 887

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The ring of balanced cycles}{平衡循环的环}]
\label{cand:stableproductring}
\bi{The stable product is bilinear, commutative, associative,
and compatible with weighted subdivision and integral affine
lattice isomorphisms. Its unit is $[N_{\mathbf R}]$ with weight
one, and $|C\cdot E|\subseteq |C|\cap|E|$.}
{稳定乘积双线性、交换、结合，并与带权细分及整仿射格同构
相容。其单位元是赋权一的 $[N_{\mathbf R}]$，且
$|C\cdot E|\subseteq |C|\cap|E|$。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Bilinearity and refinement invariance follow from those
of products, corners, and pushforwards. The preceding lemma
gives the support assertion, commutativity, and invariance under
changes of lattice basis. Simultaneous translation of $x,y$
leaves their differences unchanged, proving affine invariance.}
{双线性与细分不变性来自直积、角点及推前的相应性质。前一
引理给出支集断言、交换性及格基变换不变性。同时平移 $x,y$
不改变其差，因此也有仿射不变性。}

\bi{For the unit, begin with $N_{\mathbf R}\times E$. Impose
$x_i=y_i$ one coordinate at a time by its corner. Before the
$i$-th step, $x_i$ is still a free lattice coordinate, so the two
sides of $x_i-y_i=0$ have opposite primitive normals and the
slope jump of $\max(0,x_i-y_i)$ is exactly one. Each facet thus
keeps the weight of its $E$ factor. At a face inherited from
$E$, the function has a common affine extension on either side
away from that equality and contributes zero. The final cycle
is the diagonal copy of $E$ with its original weights. Projection
therefore gives $[N_{\mathbf R}]\cdot E=E$.}
{对单位元，从 $N_{\mathbf R}\times E$ 开始，逐一以角点
施加等式 $x_i=y_i$。在第 $i$ 步之前，$x_i$ 仍为自由的
格坐标，因此 $x_i-y_i=0$ 的两侧有相反的本原法向，而
$\max(0,x_i-y_i)$ 的斜率跳跃恰为一。故每个极大胞腔
保持其 $E$ 因子的权重。在继承自 $E$ 的面处，函数在该
等式之外的每一侧都有共同仿射延拓，贡献为零。最终得到
保留原权重的 $E$ 的对角线副本，投影即得
$[N_{\mathbf R}]\cdot E=E$。}

\bi{For associativity, work on $C\times E\times F$ with
coordinates $x,y,z$. By product locality, imposing the $x=y$
diagonal first gives the diagonal copy of $(C\cdot E)\times F$.
On this support the functions $\max(0,x_i-z_i)$ and
$\max(0,y_i-z_i)$ agree. Hence the lift of $(C\cdot E)\cdot F$
to the small diagonal is}
{对结合性，在坐标为 $x,y,z$ 的 $C\times E\times F$ 上
工作。由直积局部性，先施加 $x=y$ 的对角线角点，得到
$(C\cdot E)\times F$ 的对角线副本。在该支集上，函数
$\max(0,x_i-z_i)$ 与 $\max(0,y_i-z_i)$ 相同。因此
$(C\cdot E)\cdot F$ 在小对角线上的提升为}
\[
 \mathcal D_{yz}\mathcal D_{xy}(C\times E\times F).
\]
\bi{Starting instead with $E\cdot F$ gives
$\mathcal D_{xy}\mathcal D_{yz}(C\times E\times F)$.
The corner operators commute, so the two cycles agree before
projection to $x$. If an intermediate dimension is negative,
both expressions vanish by the same commuting-operator
calculation. This proves associativity in all degrees.}
{若从 $E\cdot F$ 开始，则得到
$\mathcal D_{xy}\mathcal D_{yz}(C\times E\times F)$。
角点算子交换，故投影到 $x$ 之前两个循环已经相同。若某个
中间维数为负，同一交换算子计算说明两式均为零。这证明
全部分次中的结合性。}
\end{proof}
