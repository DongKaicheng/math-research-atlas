Lifting a finite separable residue extension

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 660

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Lifting a finite separable residue extension}{有限可分剩余域扩张的提升}]
\label{cand:residueextensionlift}
\bi{Let $l/k$ be a finite separable extension. There is a finite separable extension $L/K$, with the unique extending absolute value, and a $k$-isomorphism $l\simeq\widetilde L$ such that}
{设 $l/k$ 为有限可分扩张。存在有限可分扩张 $L/K$，配备唯一的延拓绝对值，以及一个 $k$-同构 $l\simeq\widetilde L$，使}
\[
 [L:K]=[\widetilde L:k]=[l:k],
 \qquad |L^\times|=|K^\times|.
\]
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{By the primitive-element theorem, \cite[Tag 030N]{StacksAlg}, write $l=k(\beta)$ with monic irreducible polynomial $p(T)\in k[T]$ of degree $d=[l:k]$. Lift its coefficients to a monic polynomial $P(T)\in O[T]$ of degree $d$. Choose a root $\alpha$ of $P$ in an algebraic extension of $K$, set $L=K(\alpha)$, and use Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} to equip $L$ with its complete extending absolute value. Then $[L:K]\leq d$.}
{由本原元定理 \cite[Tag 030N]{StacksAlg}，写成 $l=k(\beta)$，并令 $p(T)\in k[T]$ 为 $\beta$ 的首一不可约多项式，其次数为 $d=[l:k]$。将其系数提升为次数为 $d$ 的首一多项式 $P(T)\in O[T]$。在 $K$ 的一个代数扩张中选择 $P$ 的根 $\alpha$，令 $L=K(\alpha)$，并由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 为 $L$ 配备完备的延拓绝对值。此时 $[L:K]\leq d$。}

\bi{If $|\alpha|>1$, the leading term of $P(\alpha)$ strictly dominates every other term, which is impossible. Thus $\alpha\in L^\circ$, and $p(\overline\alpha)=0$ defines an embedding $l\hookrightarrow\widetilde L$ sending $\beta$ to $\overline\alpha$. The value-group and residue-degree inequality \cite[Tag 0ASH]{StacksAlg} gives}
{若 $|\alpha|>1$，则 $P(\alpha)$ 的首项绝对值严格大于其余每项，矛盾。因此 $\alpha\in L^\circ$，且 $p(\overline\alpha)=0$ 给出将 $\beta$ 送到 $\overline\alpha$ 的嵌入 $l\hookrightarrow\widetilde L$。值群与剩余次数不等式 \cite[Tag 0ASH]{StacksAlg} 给出}
\[
 d\leq[\widetilde L:k]\leq
 [|L^\times|:|K^\times|]\,[\widetilde L:k]
 \leq[L:K]\leq d.
\]
\bi{All inequalities are equalities. In particular the residue embedding is an isomorphism, the value-group index is one, and $P$ is the minimal polynomial of $\alpha$. Since $p$ is separable, $p'(\overline\alpha)\ne0$, so $P'(\alpha)$ is a unit of $L^\circ$. The minimal polynomial is therefore separable.}
{所有不等式均为等式。因此剩余域嵌入是同构，值群指数为一，且 $P$ 是 $\alpha$ 的极小多项式。由于 $p$ 可分，$p'(\overline\alpha)\ne0$，所以 $P'(\alpha)$ 是 $L^\circ$ 的单位元。故此极小多项式可分。}
\end{proof}
