Perturbing affinoid generators

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 221

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Perturbing affinoid generators}{扰动 affinoid 生成元}]\label{fnd:affinoidgeneratorperturbation}
\bi{Let $\pi_0:A\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle\twoheadrightarrow B$ be a surjective homomorphism, with $A$ strict norm-admissible and $B$ carrying its actual quotient norm. Write $h_i^0=\pi_0(X_i)$. If $h_i\in B$ satisfy}
{设 $\pi_0:A\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle\twoheadrightarrow B$ 为满同态，$A$ 为 strict norm-admissible，$B$ 配备实际商范数。记 $h_i^0=\pi_0(X_i)$。若 $h_i\in B$ 满足}
\[
 \delta=\max_i\|h_i-h_i^0\|<1,
\]
\bi{then substitution $X_i\mapsto h_i$ defines a contractive surjection $\pi:A\langle X\rangle\to B$. The quotient norm induced by $\pi$ equals the given norm on $B$. The case $n=0$ uses $\delta=0$.}
{则代入 $X_i\mapsto h_i$ 定义一个 contractive 满射 $\pi:A\langle X\rangle\to B$。由 $\pi$ 诱导的商范数等于 $B$ 的给定范数。$n=0$ 时约定 $\delta=0$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Each $h_i^0$ and $h_i$ has norm at most one. The bounded substitution criterion gives $\pi$. A telescoping product expansion gives $\|h^\alpha-(h^0)^\alpha\|\leq\delta$ for every nonconstant monomial, and the constant monomial has difference zero. The ultrametric inequality, followed by polynomial approximation, therefore gives}
{每个 $h_i^0$ 及 $h_i$ 的范数至多为一。有界代入判据给出 $\pi$。将乘积差逐因子展开，可得每个非恒定单项式均满足 $\|h^\alpha-(h^0)^\alpha\|\leq\delta$；恒定单项式的差为零。由超度量不等式及多项式逼近，得到}
\[
 \|\pi(F)-\pi_0(F)\|\leq\delta\|F\|
 \qquad(F\in A\langle X\rangle).
\]
\bi{Fix $\lambda>1$ with $q=\lambda\delta<1$. For $b\in B$, choose a $\pi_0$-lift $F_0$ with $\|F_0\|\leq\lambda\|b\|$, taking zero when $b=0$. Then $e_1=b-\pi(F_0)$ has norm at most $q\|b\|$. Repeat for each successive error. This produces $F_j$ with}
{固定满足 $q=\lambda\delta<1$ 的 $\lambda>1$。对 $b\in B$，选择 $\pi_0$-提升 $F_0$，使 $\|F_0\|\leq\lambda\|b\|$，并在 $b=0$ 时取零。则误差 $e_1=b-\pi(F_0)$ 的范数不超过 $q\|b\|$。对逐次误差重复此过程，得到 $F_j$ 满足}
\[
 \|F_j\|\leq\lambda q^j\|b\|,
 \qquad
 \left\|b-\pi\left(\sum_{j=0}^m F_j\right)\right\|
     \leq q^{m+1}\|b\|.
\]
\bi{The sum $F=\sum_jF_j$ converges, has norm at most $\lambda\|b\|$, and satisfies $\pi(F)=b$. Thus $\pi$ is surjective. Its quotient norm is at least $\|b\|$ because it is contractive, and at most $\lambda\|b\|$ for every sufficiently small $\lambda>1$. Letting $\lambda\downarrow1$ proves equality. This proof requires only approximate quotient lifts; it does not assume that a norm-minimizing lift exists. If $B=0$, the statement is immediate.}
{$F=\sum_jF_j$ 收敛，范数不超过 $\lambda\|b\|$，且 $\pi(F)=b$。故 $\pi$ 满射。由于它 contractive，其商范数至少为 $\|b\|$；上述构造又说明，对每个充分接近一且大于一的 $\lambda$，商范数至多为 $\lambda\|b\|$。令 $\lambda\downarrow1$ 即得相等。此证明只需近似的商提升，没有假定存在范数达到下确界的提升。若 $B=0$，断言直接成立。}
\end{proof}
