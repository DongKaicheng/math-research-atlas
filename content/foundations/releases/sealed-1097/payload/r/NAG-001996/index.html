Finiteness from finitely many inner relative presentations

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Finiteness from finitely many inner relative presentations}{由有限个内相对表示得到有限性}]
\label{fnd:finiteinnercovercriterion}
\bi{Assume that $K$ is nontrivially valued and $A$ is strict. Let $A\to B$ be bounded, with $B$ generalized affinoid. Suppose that $\mathcal M(B)$ has a finite affinoid-domain covering $(V_i)$, and for each $i$ there is a norm-admissible surjection $P_i=A\langle T_i\rangle\twoheadrightarrow B$ whose variables have spectral radius less than one on $V_i$. Then $B$ is finite over $A$, and its given norm is equivalent to its canonical finite-$A$-module norm.}
{设 $K$ 的赋值非平凡，$A$ 为 strict。令 $A\to B$ 有界，其中 $B$ 为 generalized affinoid。假设 $\mathcal M(B)$ 有有限 affinoid-domain 覆盖 $(V_i)$，且对每个 $i$ 存在 norm-admissible 满射 $P_i=A\langle T_i\rangle\twoheadrightarrow B$，其变量在 $V_i$ 上的谱半径都小于一。则 $B$ 在 $A$ 上有限，且其给定范数与典范有限 $A$-模范数等价。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Write $B_i=\mathcal O(V_i)$, and let $Z\subset\prod_iB_i$ be the kernel of the first \v Cech differential. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidcovercech} identifies $B$ boundedly with $Z$, with bounded inverse. For comparison, take the outer covering consisting of one copy of $\mathcal M(B)$ for each $i$; its matching module is the diagonal $Z_0\subset B^s$, also boundedly isomorphic to $B$. Restriction therefore induces a bounded isomorphism $r:Z_0\to Z$.}
{记 $B_i=\mathcal O(V_i)$，令 $Z\subset\prod_iB_i$ 为第一个 \v Cech 微分的核。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidcovercech}，$B$ 与 $Z$ 有界同构，且逆有界。作为比较，取一个外覆盖，对每个 $i$ 都使用一份 $\mathcal M(B)$；其匹配模是对角子模 $Z_0\subset B^s$，也与 $B$ 有界同构。因此限制诱导有界同构 $r:Z_0\to Z$。}

\bi{The product presentation $q:\prod_iP_i\twoheadrightarrow B^s$ is norm-strict. Its inverse image $E^\flat=q^{-1}(Z_0)$ is closed, and $p=q|_{E^\flat}:E^\flat\twoheadrightarrow Z_0$ is norm-strict: any controlled lift of an element of $Z_0$ automatically belongs to this inverse image. With $j:Z\hookrightarrow\prod_iB_i$, the map $jrp$ is the restriction of the product of the maps $P_i\to B_i$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictcompactrelativerestriction} makes it strictly completely continuous. Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:presentedrelativeschwarz} with $f=r$ and $g=-r$. The zero map then has finite cokernel $Z$, with its canonical norm. Transporting this norm through $B\simeq Z$ proves the theorem.}
{乘积表示 $q:\prod_iP_i\twoheadrightarrow B^s$ 为 norm-strict。其逆像 $E^\flat=q^{-1}(Z_0)$ 是闭模，且 $p=q|_{E^\flat}:E^\flat\twoheadrightarrow Z_0$ 为 norm-strict：$Z_0$ 中元素的任意受控提升自动属于此逆像。令 $j:Z\hookrightarrow\prod_iB_i$；映射 $jrp$ 是各 $P_i\to B_i$ 的乘积在此逆像上的限制。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictcompactrelativerestriction}，它严格完全连续。在 Theorem~\ref{fnd:presentedrelativeschwarz} 中取 $f=r$、$g=-r$，便知零映射的余核 $Z$ 是带有典范范数的有限模。通过 $B\simeq Z$ 搬运范数，得到结论。}
\end{proof}

{缩小相对多圆盘后，限制映射具有系数代数上的有限秩逼近。若要把这些逼近限制到匹配截面组成的闭模，就需要统一的整系数界。下面先建立这个界。前五个结果中，$K$ 的赋值非平凡，$O=K^\circ$，$A$ 为配备定义商范数的 strict $K$-affinoid 代数。记 $A_0=A_{\leq1}$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations}，$A_0$ 是某个 $O\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle$ 的商。Banach 模的作用取为压缩作用；原本有界的作用可通过等价范数 $\|m\|_* =\sup_{a\ne0}\|am\|/\|a\|$ 化为压缩作用。}

\begin{lemma}[\bi{Almost finite generation over the integral coefficient ring}{整系数环上的几乎有限生成}]
\label{fnd:integralalmostfinitegeneration}
\bi{Let $E$ be a finite $A_0$-module and $N\subset E$ any submodule. For every real $0<\alpha<1$ there is a finite $A_0$-submodule $N_0\subset N$ such that $aN\subset N_0$ for every $a\in O$ with $|a|\leq\alpha$.}
{设 $E$ 为有限 $A_0$-模，$N\subset E$ 为任意子模。对每个实数 $0<\alpha<1$，存在有限 $A_0$-子模 $N_0\subset N$，使所有满足 $|a|\leq\alpha$ 的 $a\in O$ 都满足 $aN\subset N_0$。}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
\bi{It suffices to prove the assertion over $P_n=O\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle$: take a finite free presentation above $E$ and then pass to its image. We use the Gauss norm inherited from $K\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle$. The coordinate changes in Lemma~\ref{fnd:distinguishedcoordinates} preserve $P_n$. The norm bounds in Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachweierstrassdivision} therefore give division within $P_n$ by a normalized distinguished element.}
{只需在 $P_n=O\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle$ 上证明：在 $E$ 上方取有限自由模的满射，最后取像即可。使用从 $K\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle$ 继承的 Gauss 范数。Lemma~\ref{fnd:distinguishedcoordinates} 的坐标变换保持 $P_n$；Theorem~\ref{fnd:banachweierstrassdivision} 的范数估计因而使归一化 distinguished 元素的除法保持在 $P_n$ 中。}
