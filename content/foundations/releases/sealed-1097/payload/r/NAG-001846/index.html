Affinoid domains under arbitrary affinoid base change

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 368

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Affinoid domains under arbitrary affinoid base change}{任意 affinoid 基变换下的 affinoid 域}]
\label{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange}
\bi{Let $A\to B$ represent a generalized $K$-affinoid domain $U$. Let $L/K$ be any complete isometric extension, let $C$ be a generalized norm-admissible $L$-affinoid algebra, and let $\alpha:A\to C$ be bounded over $K$. Write $p:\mathcal M_L(C)\to\mathcal M_K(A)$. Then}
{设 $A\to B$ 表示 generalized $K$-affinoid 域 $U$。设 $L/K$ 为任一完备等距扩域，$C$ 为 generalized norm-admissible $L$-affinoid 代数，$\alpha:A\to C$ 为有界 $K$-映射，并记 $p:\mathcal M_L(C)\to\mathcal M_K(A)$。则}
\[
 D=B\widehat\otimes_A C
\]
\bi{is norm-admissible $L$-affinoid and $C\to D$ represents the affinoid domain $p^{-1}(U)$. For every original-$A$ rational domain $R\subseteq U$, its pullback to $\mathcal M_L(D)$ is rational and has algebra}
{为 norm-admissible $L$-affinoid，且 $C\to D$ 表示 affinoid 域 $p^{-1}(U)$。对包含于 $U$ 的每个原 $A$-rational 域 $R$，其在 $\mathcal M_L(D)$ 中的拉回为 rational，其代数为}
\[
 D\widehat\otimes_B A_R
       \simeq C\widehat\otimes_A A_R,
\]
\bi{the actual rational quotient of $C$ defined by the pulled-back tuple. These are canonical bounded isomorphisms and commute with finite intersections, restriction and further complete-affinoid base change. Finite affinoid-domain coverings pull back to finite affinoid-domain coverings.}
{即由拉回 tuple 定义的 $C$ 的实际 rational 商。这些同构典范且双向有界，并与有限交、限制及进一步完备 affinoid 基变换相容。有限 affinoid 域覆盖的拉回仍为有限 affinoid 域覆盖。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout} gives the actual generalized $L$-affinoid algebra $D$ and its bounded pushout property. Choose $U=\bigcup_iR_i$ by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert}. Each $R_i$ is rational over $B$ with algebra $A_{R_i}$ by Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainrestriction}. Pull its tuple into $D$. By the rational point theorem these rational subsets cover $\mathcal M_L(D)$, since the $R_i$ cover $\mathcal M_K(B)$. Their actual algebras have the displayed bounded identifications: compatible bounded maps out of either side are precisely compatible maps from $C$ and $A_{R_i}$ over $A$, because the intervening map from $B$ is the restriction $B\to A_{R_i}$. The rational universal properties, or the actual joint quotient presentations, therefore give bounded inverse maps.}
{Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout} 给出实际的 generalized $L$-affinoid 代数 $D$ 及其有界 pushout 泛性质。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert}，选择 $U=\bigcup_iR_i$。根据 Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainrestriction}，每个 $R_i$ 在 $B$ 上 rational，其代数为 $A_{R_i}$。将其 tuple 映入 $D$。由 rational 谱点定理，这些 rational 子集覆盖 $\mathcal M_L(D)$，因为各 $R_i$ 覆盖 $\mathcal M_K(B)$。其实际代数具有所列的有界识别：从两边任一代数出发的相容有界映射，恰好对应为从 $C$ 与 $A_{R_i}$ 出发且在 $A$ 上相同的映射，因为中间的 $B$-映射为限制 $B\to A_{R_i}$。因此，rational 泛性质或实际联合商表示给出双向有界的逆映射。}

\bi{The right-hand algebra is the rational quotient of $C$ for $p^{-1}(R_i)$. Every point of $D$ maps into $p^{-1}(U)$ by restricting to $B$. Conversely, for $x\in p^{-1}(U)$ choose $i$ with $p(x)\in R_i$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints} gives a unique point above $x$ in this rational quotient of $C$, hence a point of $D$. If two points of $D$ lie over the same $x$, both belong to that $i$th rational piece; its identification with the rational quotient of $C$ makes them equal. Thus $\mathcal M_L(D)\to\mathcal M_L(C)$ is bijective onto $p^{-1}(U)$; compactness and Hausdorffness in Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact} make this a homeomorphism.}
{右边的代数是定义 $p^{-1}(R_i)$ 的 $C$ 的 rational 商。将谱点限制到 $B$，可知 $D$ 的每个点都映入 $p^{-1}(U)$。反之，对 $x\in p^{-1}(U)$，选择满足 $p(x)\in R_i$ 的 $i$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints} 在该 $C$ 的 rational 商中给出位于 $x$ 上方的唯一点，因而得到 $D$ 的一个点。若 $D$ 中两个点位于同一个 $x$ 上方，则两者都属于该第 $i$ 个 rational 部分；它与 $C$ 的 rational 商的识别使两点相等。因此 $\mathcal M_L(D)\to\mathcal M_L(C)$ 双射到 $p^{-1}(U)$，而 Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact} 的紧性与 Hausdorff 性说明它为同胚。}

\bi{For the domain property, let $C\to E$ be a bounded $L$-map to a generalized $L$-affinoid algebra whose spectral image lies in $p^{-1}(U)$. The composite $A\to E$ has image in $U$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainextensiontests} supplies a unique bounded $K$-map $B\to E$ over $A$. Together with $C\to E$, the pushout gives the required bounded $L$-map $D\to E$. Conversely any such factorization restricts to these two maps, so it is unique. This proves the generalized $L$-domain property.}
{为验证域泛性质，设 $C\to E$ 是到 generalized $L$-affinoid 代数的有界 $L$-映射，且谱像包含于 $p^{-1}(U)$。复合 $A\to E$ 的像包含于 $U$，故 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainextensiontests} 提供唯一的有界 $K$-映射 $B\to E$，并保持 $A$。与 $C\to E$ 一起，由 pushout 得到所需的有界 $L$-映射 $D\to E$。反之，任何这种分解都限制为上述两个映射，所以唯一。这证明 generalized $L$-域泛性质。}

\bi{The algebra identification for any rational $R\subseteq U$ follows by the same pushout argument. Uniqueness of the bounded factorization makes these identifications compatible with restriction, intersections and a further base change. For successive base changes, the two parenthesizations of the completed pushout represent the same bounded-map problem, so their canonical maps are bounded inverses. A finite domain covering remains a covering because inverse images preserve finite unions. Empty pullbacks have zero algebra by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion} over $L$.}
{对任意包含于 $U$ 的 rational 域 $R$，同一 pushout 论证给出所述代数识别。有界分解的唯一性保证这些识别与限制、有限交及进一步基变换相容。对于逐次基变换，完备 pushout 的两种括号顺序表示同一个有界映射问题，因此其典范比较映射互为有界逆。因为逆像保持有限并，有限域覆盖仍拉回为覆盖。在 $L$ 上应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion}，可知空拉回的代数为零。}
\end{proof}
