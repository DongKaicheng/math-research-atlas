Bounded expansions on pointed tubes

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Bounded expansions on pointed tubes}
{带点管域上的有界展开}]
\label{cand:boundedetaletubeexpansion}
\bi{Let $\mathcal U$ be a flat finitely presented $O$-scheme
with a rational special-fibre point $u$, and let
$\mathcal U\to\mathbb A^1_O$ be \'etale at $u$, sending $u$
to the special-fibre origin. Shrink $\mathcal U$ around $u$ and
write $z$ for the target coordinate. Every integral function on
its formal completion restricts to its tube as a power series}
{设 $\mathcal U$ 为平坦有限表示的 $O$-概形，$u$ 为其
特殊纤维上的有理点，且 $\mathcal U\to\mathbb A^1_O$
在 $u$ 处 \'etale，并将 $u$ 送到特殊纤维原点。在 $u$
附近收缩 $\mathcal U$，以 $z$ 表示靶坐标。其形式完备化
上的每个整函数，在管域上均有幂级数表示}
\[
 g(z)=\sum_{j\geq0}b_jz^j,\qquad b_j\in O,
 \quad |z|<1.
\]
\bi{Reducing the coefficients gives the expansion of
$\overline g$ in the completed local ring $k[[\overline z]]$
of the special fibre at $u$.}
{逐系数约化得到 $\overline g$ 在特殊纤维 $u$ 处的完备
局部环 $k[[\overline z]]$ 中的展开。}

\bi{For an \'etale pointed chart}
{对于带点 \'etale 坐标片}
\[
 (\mathcal U,u)\longrightarrow
 \bigl(\operatorname{Spec}O[s,t]/(st-a),o\bigr),
 \qquad 0<|a|<1,
\]
\bi{with $u,o$ rational, the corresponding assertion is that
the restriction to $]u[$ is represented by}
{其中 $u,o$ 均为有理点，相应结论是：函数在 $]u[$ 上
具有表示}
\[
 g=\sum_{i,j\geq0}c_{ij}s^it^j,\qquad c_{ij}\in O,
 \qquad |s|<1,\quad |t|<1,\quad st=a.
\]
\bi{On the normalization branch $t=0$, the reduction of $g$
has completed expansion $\sum_{i\geq0}\overline c_{i0}s^i$;
on $s=0$, it has expansion
$\sum_{j\geq0}\overline c_{0j}t^j$.}
{在正规化分支 $t=0$ 上，$g$ 的约化具有完备展开
$\sum_{i\geq0}\overline c_{i0}s^i$；在 $s=0$ 上则为
$\sum_{j\geq0}\overline c_{0j}t^j$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{After a Zariski shrinking preserving the entire tube, the
square Jacobian presentation of \cite[Tag 02GU]{StacksAlg}
expresses the chart by a system $F(Z,W)=0$ with coefficients in
$O$, where $Z=z$ in the smooth case and $Z=(s,t)$ in the nodal
case. Include the inverses of all localized functions as extra
variables and equations. The Jacobian with respect to $W$ is
invertible at the selected residual point. In the nodal case take
polynomial representatives of the coefficients before imposing
$st=a$.}
{作保持整个管域的 Zariski 收缩后，
\cite[Tag 02GU]{StacksAlg} 的方阵 Jacobian 表示将坐标片
写为系数属于 $O$ 的方程组 $F(Z,W)=0$；光滑情形中
$Z=z$，节点情形中 $Z=(s,t)$。将全部局部化函数的逆作为
附加变量和方程。关于 $W$ 的 Jacobian 在所选剩余点处
可逆。节点情形中，先选取系数的多项式代表，再施加
$st=a$。}

\bi{At $Z=0$, the residual solution of $F(0,W)=0$ has
invertible Jacobian. Lemma~\ref{cand:completevaluationhenselian}
gives a solution $w_0\in O^n$ lifting it. There is a unique
formal solution $W(Z)\in O[[Z]]^n$ with constant term $w_0$.
Indeed, after coefficients of degrees less than $m$ have been
chosen, the equations in degree $m$ are linear equations with
coefficient matrix $J_WF(0,w_0)\in\mathrm{GL}_n(O)$; solve
them for every monomial of degree $m$. This recursive construction
also proves uniqueness.}
{在 $Z=0$ 处，$F(0,W)=0$ 的剩余解具有可逆 Jacobian。
由 Lemma~\ref{cand:completevaluationhenselian}，它提升为
解 $w_0\in O^n$。存在唯一常数项为 $w_0$ 的形式解
$W(Z)\in O[[Z]]^n$：若次数小于 $m$ 的系数已经选定，
则 $m$ 次方程是系数矩阵为
$J_WF(0,w_0)\in\mathrm{GL}_n(O)$ 的线性方程组；对每个
$m$ 次单项式求解即可。此递归构造也证明唯一性。}

\bi{All coefficients lie in $O$, so these series converge on
every closed polydisc of radii strictly less than one. Their values
reduce to the prescribed point. The uniqueness in
Corollary~\ref{cand:standardetaletubeisomorphism} identifies this
analytic solution with the inverse of the pointed tube map.
Choose a polynomial representative for each regular function in
the presentation, including the inverse variables. Substitution
gives its bounded-coefficient expansion. For an element of the
$\pi$-adic completion, choose successively regular functions
$g_n$ such that $g=\sum_{n\geq0}\pi^ng_n$. Expand each $g_n$
in this way and sum the resulting series with the factors $\pi^n$.
The coefficients converge in $O$, uniformly on every closed
smaller polydisc. On the nodal tube the imposed relation $st=a$
ensures equality with the original function, regardless of the
polynomial representatives used.}
{所有系数均在 $O$ 中，所以这些级数在每个半径严格小于一
的闭多圆盘上收敛，其值约化到指定点。由
Corollary~\ref{cand:standardetaletubeisomorphism} 中的
唯一性，此解析解正是带点管域映射的逆。在包含逆变量的
表示中，为每个正则函数选择多项式代表，代入便得到其有界
系数展开。对 $\pi$-进完备化中的元素，逐次选择正则函数
$g_n$，使 $g=\sum_{n\geq0}\pi^ng_n$。按此方式展开各
$g_n$，再带着因子 $\pi^n$ 求和。所得系数在 $O$ 中
收敛，并在每个较小闭多圆盘上一致收敛。在节点管域上施加
$st=a$ 后，无论使用哪一组多项式代表，所得函数都等于原函数。}

\bi{Reduction commutes with this construction. The reduced
recursive solution is the inverse of the pointed \'etale map on
completed special-fibre local rings. In the smooth case this is
$k[[z]]$. In the nodal case impose $st=0$ and then restrict to
either branch, giving the two stated expansions. Equivalently,
these are the corresponding expansions on the normalization;
normalization commutes with \'etale base change by
\cite[Tag 03GV]{StacksAlg}.}
{约化与此构造相容。约化后的递归解，是特殊纤维完备局部环
上带点 \'etale 映射的逆。光滑情形中此环为 $k[[z]]$；
节点情形中先施加 $st=0$，再限制到任一分支，得到所列两种
展开。等价地，它们就是正规化上的相应展开，因为
\cite[Tag 03GV]{StacksAlg} 保证正规化与 \'etale 基变换相容。}
\end{proof}
