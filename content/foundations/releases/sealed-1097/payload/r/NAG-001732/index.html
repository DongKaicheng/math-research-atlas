Hom and extension comparison on proper schemes

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 303

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Hom and extension comparison on proper schemes}
{适当概形上的同态与扩张比较}]
\label{fnd:properextcomparison}
\bi{Let $X$ be proper over $K$, and let $\mathcal E,\mathcal F$
be coherent modules on $X$. For every $q\ge0$, exact
analytification induces an isomorphism}
{设 $X$ 在 $K$ 上适当，$\mathcal E,\mathcal F$ 是 $X$ 上的
凝聚模。对每个 $q\ge0$，正合解析化诱导同构}
\[
 \operatorname{Ext}^q_{\mathcal O_X}(\mathcal E,\mathcal F)
 \ \simeq\
 \operatorname{Ext}^q_{\mathcal O_{X^{\an}_G}}
                    (\mathcal E^{\an},\mathcal F^{\an}).
\]
\bi{For $q=1$ this is the map which analytifies short exact
sequences; for $q=0$ it gives full faithfulness.}
{当 $q=1$ 时，此映射就是将短正合列解析化；当 $q=0$ 时，
它给出全忠实性。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{We first compare sheaf Ext locally. On an affine open
$\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ choose a resolution of the finite
module representing $\mathcal E$ by finite free $R$-modules,
continuing as far as necessary. Each successive kernel is
finite because $R$ is Noetherian. Flat analytification makes
this an exact resolution by finite free analytic modules.
Applying Hom into $\mathcal F$ and its analytification gives
complexes which agree after pullback, since each free term
is finite. In each fixed degree only finitely many terms are
needed to compute kernel modulo image. Exact pullback thus
gives}
{先局部比较层 Ext。在仿射开集 $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ 上，
用有限自由 $R$-模解消表示 $\mathcal E$ 的有限模，并按需
延长解消。由于 $R$ Noetherian，每一步的核仍有限。
平坦解析化把它变成由有限自由解析模组成的正合解消。
分别对 $\mathcal F$ 及其解析化取 Hom；各自由项有限，
故所得复形在拉回后相同。在每个固定次数，只需有限多个项
就能计算核模去像。因此正合拉回给出}
\[
 (\mathcal E\!xt_X^j(\mathcal E,\mathcal F))^{\an}
   \simeq \mathcal E\!xt_{X^{\an}_G}^j
                         (\mathcal E^{\an},\mathcal F^{\an}).
\]
\bi{The algebraic Ext sheaves are coherent by the same
finite-free calculation. These local identifications are
independent of the resolution: they are the cohomology maps
of the canonical derived-Hom pullback morphism
\cite[Tag 08JF]{StacksAlg}. They therefore glue to a
quasi-isomorphism}
{相同的有限自由计算说明代数 Ext 层凝聚。
这些局部识别与所选解消无关：它们是
\cite[Tag 08JF]{StacksAlg} 中典范导出 Hom 拉回映射的
上同调映射，因此粘合为拟同构}
\[
 \alpha_X^*R\mathcal H\!om_X(\mathcal E,\mathcal F)
  \simeq R\mathcal H\!om_{X^{\an}_G}
                      (\mathcal E^{\an},\mathcal F^{\an}).
\]
\bi{Put $C=R\mathcal H\!om_X(\mathcal E,\mathcal F)$.
It has coherent cohomology in nonnegative degrees, although
it need not be bounded above. Fix $q$. The truncation
$\tau_{\le q}C$ is a bounded coherent complex.
The complementary truncation belongs to $D^{\ge q+1}$;
its derived global sections also belong to $D^{\ge q+1}$,
as a bounded below injective resolution may be chosen to
start in that degree. Thus $H^qR\Gamma(C)$ is computed
by $\tau_{\le q}C$. The same holds after exact pullback.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:propercoherentcomparison} and the
truncation triangle now give equality in degree $q$ of
the two derived global Hom complexes. By
\cite[Tag 08JA]{StacksAlg}, applied after shifting the
second argument by $q$, these groups are the stated Ext
groups. Finally \cite[Tag 06XV]{StacksAlg} identifies
derived Ext in degree one with short exact extensions.
Its identification takes an extension to its connecting
morphism. Exact pullback preserves that triangle and
connecting morphism, proving the claimed compatibility.}
{记 $C=R\mathcal H\!om_X(\mathcal E,\mathcal F)$。
它的上同调集中在非负次数且凝聚，但未必有上界。
固定 $q$，则 $\tau_{\le q}C$ 是有界凝聚复形。
互补截断属于 $D^{\ge q+1}$，其导出全局截面也属于
$D^{\ge q+1}$，因为可选择从该次数开始的有下界内射解消。
因此 $H^qR\Gamma(C)$ 可由 $\tau_{\le q}C$ 计算；
正合拉回后同样如此。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:propercoherentcomparison} 与截断三角
给出两个导出全局 Hom 复形在 $q$ 次的同构。
将 \cite[Tag 08JA]{StacksAlg} 用于第二个变量的 $q$ 次移位，
即将这些群识别为所列 Ext 群。
最后，\cite[Tag 06XV]{StacksAlg} 将一次导出 Ext 与短正合扩张
等同，其对应把扩张送到连接态射。正合拉回保持该三角及其
连接态射，因而得到所需相容性。}
\end{proof}
