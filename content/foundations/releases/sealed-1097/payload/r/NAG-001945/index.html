\begin{lemma}[\bi{Products of open ideals and global blowups}
{开理想的乘积与全局 blowup}]
\label{fnd:formalglobalblowupcalculus}
\bi{A coherent open ideal on an admissible formal scheme has
an admissible formal blowup, obtained by gluing the affine
constructions. It is compatible with restriction to formal opens,
has the invertible-ideal universal property, and induces an
isomorphism on generic fibres.
Blowing up a finite product of coherent open ideals makes each
factor invertible, so dominates the blowup of each factor.
For a formal morphism $\mathfrak X\to\mathfrak Y$, every blowup
of $\mathfrak Y$ is dominated over $\mathfrak Y$ by the blowup
of its pulled-back ideal on $\mathfrak X$.}
{Admissible 形式概形上的凝聚开理想具有 admissible 形式 blowup，
由仿射构造粘合得到。它与形式开集上的限制相容，具有可逆理想的
泛性质，并在泛纤维上诱导同构。
对有限个凝聚开理想的乘积作 blowup 使每个因子都可逆，故支配
每个因子的 blowup。对形式态射 $\mathfrak X\to\mathfrak Y$，
$\mathfrak Y$ 的每个 blowup 都被 $\mathfrak X$ 上的拉回理想
的 blowup 在 $\mathfrak Y$ 上支配。}
\end{lemma}
