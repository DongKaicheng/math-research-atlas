Finitely many monomial ideals with a fixed Hilbert function

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 869

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Finitely many monomial ideals with a fixed Hilbert function}{固定 Hilbert 函数的单项式理想只有有限个}]
\label{cand:monomialhilbertfiniteness}
\bi{In a polynomial ring in finitely many variables, there are only finitely many monomial ideals with a prescribed Hilbert function. This finite set depends only on that function and the number of variables, not on the coefficient field.}
{在有限个变量的多项式环中，具有给定 Hilbert 函数的单项式理想只有有限个。此有限集仅依赖该函数及变量数，与系数域无关。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Suppose there were infinitely many distinct such ideals. Enumerate the monomials by nondecreasing degree. Successively retain an infinite subfamily on which membership of the next monomial is constant. The limiting membership choices define a monomial ideal $Q$: divisibility preserves membership because it does so in every ideal of every sufficiently late subfamily. In each degree its standard monomials have the prescribed cardinality. The polynomial ring is Noetherian by \cite[Tag 00FN]{StacksAlg}, so $Q$ has finitely many monomial generators, all of degree at most some $D$.}
{假设存在无穷多个互异的此类理想。按次数不减的顺序列举单项式，逐次保留一个无穷子族，使下一个单项式是否属于理想保持一致。极限选择定义单项式理想 $Q$：成员性在乘单项式后仍保持，因为每个充分靠后的子族中的理想均有此性质。每个次数中，$Q$ 的标准单项式数均为规定值。由 \cite[Tag 00FN]{StacksAlg}，多项式环 Noetherian，故 $Q$ 有有限个单项式生成元，次数至多为某个 $D$。}

\bi{An infinite subfamily agrees with $Q$ on all monomials of degree at most $D$. Each member therefore contains $Q$. Since its standard-monomial count equals that of $Q$ in every degree, the containment is equality. This contradicts the distinctness of the infinite subfamily. All choices involved only sets of exponent vectors.}
{存在一个无穷子族，在次数至多 $D$ 的所有单项式上与 $Q$ 一致，因而其中每个理想都包含 $Q$。由于各次标准单项式数又与 $Q$ 相等，该包含必为等号，与此无穷子族的互异性矛盾。全部选择仅涉及指数向量集合。}
\end{proof}
