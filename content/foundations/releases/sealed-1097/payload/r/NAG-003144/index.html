\begin{proposition}[An actual unramified base change supplies the steps]
\label{f5gt:pdf-derived:unramifiedbridge}
Let \(E/K\) satisfy \eqref{eq:f5gt:pdf-derived:tame-hypotheses}, without assuming it Galois. There is an actual
unramified subextension \(M/K\) in \(E\) with
\(\widetilde M=\widetilde E\) and \([M:K]=f\).
There is a finite Galois actual unramified extension \(N/K\), containing this
\(M\) in a common valued overfield, such that \(EN/N\) is a tower of exact
Kummer steps, of total degree and value index \(e\) and with residue field
\(\widetilde N\). For \(e=1\) this tower is empty.
Neither a Galois action on a supplied \(E\)-model nor a Galois extension
\(EN/K\) is asserted by this conclusion.
\end{proposition}
\par\smallskip\noindent{\small\textit{Direct inputs} \hyperref[cand:completevaluationhenselian]{\texttt{NAG-002049}}, \hyperref[cand:residueextensionlift]{\texttt{NAG-002050}}, \hyperref[cand:galoisresidueextensionlift]{\texttt{NAG-002052}}, \hyperref[v036:f5:unramifiedcoefficients]{\texttt{NAG-002262}}, \hyperref[f5gt:pdf-derived:radicalsteps]{\texttt{NAG-003143}}.}\par\smallskip
\begin{proof}
Write \(k=\widetilde K\) and \(l=\widetilde E\). Use the primitive residue
generator and its lifted monic polynomial in the proof of
\hyperref[cand:residueextensionlift]{\texttt{NAG-002050}}, but lift its simple residue root inside
\(E\). This gives \(\alpha\in E^\circ\) and \(M=K(\alpha)\).
The degree is at most \(f\); the residue degree is at least \(f\).
The fundamental inequality \stacks{0ASH} forces \([M:K]=f\),
\(\Gamma_M=\Gamma_K\) and \(\widetilde M=l\). This is the same actual
residue lift, with its chosen embedding in \(E\). By \eqref{eq:f5gt:pdf-derived:tame-hypotheses}, \(E/M\) has
degree and value index \(e\) and unchanged residue field.

Choose a finite Galois residue extension \(k'/k\) containing \(l\) and all
\(e\)-th roots of unity. It exists by taking the splitting field of a
separable defining polynomial for \(l/k\) together with \(T^e-1\), using
\stacks{09HU}; the latter polynomial is separable because \(e\) is a unit.
First lift \(k'/l\) over \(M\) by \hyperref[cand:residueextensionlift]{\texttt{NAG-002050}},
obtaining \(N/M\) in a common algebraic overfield of \(E\).
Thus \([N:K]=[k':k]\), \(\widetilde N=k'\) and \(\Gamma_N=\Gamma_K\).

To identify \(N/K\) as the actual Galois unramified lift, lift a primitive
generator of \(k'/k\) inside \(N\) by Hensel. Its field over \(K\) has
degree at least \([k':k]\), by its residue, and at most that degree,
by its lifted polynomial. It is therefore \(N\). All roots of the residue
polynomial lift inside \(N\), since its residue is \(k'\); they are distinct
and exhaust the roots of the lifted
% @witness proof_continued 229:4-8
polynomial. Thus \(N/K\) is Galois and is precisely the lift of
\hyperref[cand:galoisresidueextensionlift]{\texttt{NAG-002052}}. Its valuation rings have the
torsor property in \hyperref[v036:f5:unramifiedcoefficients]{\texttt{NAG-002262}}. Hensel also lifts
all roots of \(T^e-1\) from \(k'\) to \(N\). Lemma~\ref{f5gt:pdf-derived:radicalsteps}, applied to \(E/M\)
and \(N/M\), proves all the assertions about \(EN/N\). The construction
supplies field inclusions; it does not supply action maps on a model or
assert normality over \(K\).
\end{proof}
