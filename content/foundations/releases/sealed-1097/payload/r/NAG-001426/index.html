Detection by maximal-ideal powers

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 161

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Detection by maximal-ideal powers}{极大理想幂的检测性质}]\label{fnd:maximaladicseparation}
\bi{Let $R$ be a commutative Noetherian ring with identity and let $M$ be a finite unital $R$-module. Then}
{设 $R$ 为交换含幺 Noetherian 环，$M$ 为有限含幺 $R$-模。则}
\[
 \bigcap_{\mathfrak m\in\operatorname{Max}(R)}
       \ \bigcap_{k\geq1}\mathfrak m^kM=0.
\]
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{If $M=0$ there is nothing to prove; this includes the zero ring. We interpret an empty intersection as $M$. Suppose that a nonzero $x$ belongs to the displayed intersection. Its annihilator $J=\operatorname{Ann}_R(x)$ is proper. Apply \cite[Tag 00E0, Lemma 10.17.2(2)]{StacksAlg} to the nonzero ring $R/J$ and take the inverse image of a maximal ideal. This gives a maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ of $R$ containing $J$. Put $N=\bigcap_{k\geq1}\mathfrak m^kM$.}
{若 $M=0$，结论直接成立；这也包括零环。约定空族子模之交为 $M$。设非零 $x$ 属于所列交。其消去理想 $J=\operatorname{Ann}_R(x)$ 为真理想。对非零环 $R/J$ 应用 \cite[Tag 00E0, Lemma 10.17.2(2)]{StacksAlg}，再取其中一个极大理想的原像，得到包含 $J$ 的 $R$ 的极大理想 $\mathfrak m$。记 $N=\bigcap_{k\geq1}\mathfrak m^kM$。}

\bi{Apply \cite[Tag 00IQ, Lemma 10.51.5(1)]{StacksAlg} with $I=\mathfrak p=\mathfrak m$. It gives $g\notin\mathfrak m$ such that $N_g=0$. Since $x\in N$, the defining equivalence relation for localization gives $g^e x=0$ for some integer $e\geq0$. Hence $g^e\in J\subseteq\mathfrak m$. This is impossible, since no nonnegative power of $g\notin\mathfrak m$ belongs to the prime ideal $\mathfrak m$.}
{在 \cite[Tag 00IQ, Lemma 10.51.5(1)]{StacksAlg} 中取 $I=\mathfrak p=\mathfrak m$，得到 $g\notin\mathfrak m$，使 $N_g=0$。由于 $x\in N$，按局部化的定义等价关系，存在整数 $e\geq0$，使 $g^e x=0$。因此 $g^e\in J\subseteq\mathfrak m$。但 $g\notin\mathfrak m$ 的任何非负次幂都不属于素理想 $\mathfrak m$，矛盾。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{We use the stated parts of two Stacks Project results as declared foundations. Only the short application needed by the Laurent argument is proved below. For an $R$-module $N$ and $g\in R$, the localization $N_g$ consists of fractions $x/g^a$, $a\geq0$, with}
{将 Stacks Project 中两个结果的指定分项作为已声明的基础使用。下面只证明 Laurent 论证需要的短应用。对 $R$-模 $N$ 和 $g\in R$，局部化 $N_g$ 由分式 $x/g^a$（$a\geq0$）构成，其等价关系为}
\[
 \frac{x}{g^a}=\frac{y}{g^b}
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \text{there is }e\geq0\text{ with }g^e(g^bx-g^ay)=0.
\]
\bi{In particular, $x/1=0$ means that $g^ex=0$ for some $e\geq0$. This is the defining equivalence relation, not an additional exactness theorem.}
{特别地，$x/1=0$ 意味着存在 $e\geq0$ 使 $g^ex=0$。这是定义中的等价关系，不是另一个正合性定理。}
