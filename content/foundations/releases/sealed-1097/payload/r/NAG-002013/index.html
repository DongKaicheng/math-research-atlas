Independent functions as relative coordinates

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 474

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Independent functions as relative coordinates}{作为相对坐标的独立函数}]
\label{fnd:independentquasismoothcoordinates}
\bi{Let $Y\to X$ be quasi-smooth of differential rank $d$ at a point $y$ of good analytic spaces. If analytic germs $f_1,\ldots,f_\ell$ have linearly independent differentials in $\Omega_{Y/X}\otimes\mathcal H(y)$, then $(f,f_1,\ldots,f_\ell):Y\to\mathbb A_X^{\ell,\mathrm{an}}$ is quasi-smooth of differential rank $d-\ell$ at $y$.}
{设 good 解析空间间的 $Y\to X$ 在 $y$ 处准光滑且微分秩为 $d$。若解析函数芽 $f_1,\ldots,f_\ell$ 的微分在 $\Omega_{Y/X}\otimes\mathcal H(y)$ 中线性无关，则 $(f,f_1,\ldots,f_\ell):Y\to\mathbb A_X^{\ell,\mathrm{an}}$ 在 $y$ 处准光滑且微分秩为 $d-\ell$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use the ordinary-neighborhood presentation in Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion}: after restrictions, $Y$ is cut out by $r$ functions $g_1,\ldots,g_r$ in an affinoid domain $D$ of $\mathbb A_X^n$, with independent Jacobian rows and $n-r=d$. Represent the $f_i$ on this $Y$ and lift them to the affinoid algebra of $D$, using the surjection defining the closed immersion. Choose an affinoid polydisc neighborhood $P$ of their values in $\mathbb A_X^\ell$. The graph of the new map is cut out, in the inverse image of $D$ inside $\mathbb A_P^n$, by}
{使用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion} 的普通邻域表示：限制后，$Y$ 在 $\mathbb A_X^n$ 的一个 affinoid 域 $D$ 中由 $r$ 个函数 $g_1,\ldots,g_r$ 定义，其 Jacobian 行线性无关，且 $n-r=d$。在此 $Y$ 上表示各 $f_i$，并利用闭浸入的代数满射将其提升到 $D$ 的 affinoid 代数。在 $\mathbb A_X^\ell$ 中选取函数值的 affinoid 多圆盘邻域 $P$。新映射的图像，在 $D$ 于 $\mathbb A_P^n$ 内的逆像中，由下列方程定义：}
\[
             g_1,\ldots,g_r,\quad f_1-Z_1,\ldots,f_\ell-Z_\ell.
\]
\bi{The original $r$ Jacobian rows together with the $\ell$ rows $df_i$ are linearly independent at $y$: this is precisely independence of the latter classes modulo the relation rows. Some $(r+\ell)$-minor is therefore nonzero at $y$, and it is invertible on an ordinary Laurent neighborhood. The graph ideal is generated by the displayed functions, so the Jacobian criterion makes the graph quasi-smooth over $P$ with rank $n-r-\ell=d-\ell$. As $P$ is an ordinary neighborhood of the target point, this is the asserted property over $\mathbb A_X^\ell$.}
{原有的 $r$ 个 Jacobian 行与 $\ell$ 个行 $df_i$ 在 $y$ 处合起来线性无关；这恰好是后者模关系行后的类线性无关。因此某个 $(r+\ell)$ 阶子式在 $y$ 处非零，并在某个普通 Laurent 邻域上可逆。图像理想由所列函数生成，故 Jacobian 判据说明图像在 $P$ 上准光滑，秩为 $n-r-\ell=d-\ell$。因 $P$ 是目标点的普通邻域，便得到相对于 $\mathbb A_X^\ell$ 的所述性质。}
\end{proof}
