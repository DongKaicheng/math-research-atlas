\begin{proposition}[Closed pullback and non-\(S_1\) current transfer; F8NM-T]
\label{prop:topology}
For a closed immersion \(j:Y\hookrightarrow X\) of good Hausdorff
spaces, pullback is continuous on native ordinary compact test spaces
with \(\tau_a\), including after restriction to an ordinary open
\(U\subseteq\Int(X/K)\) and its inverse image. It preserves compact
interior support and commutes with \(d'\) and \(d''\).

Under the reviewed inputs of Theorem~\ref{thm:fixed}, \(L_f\),
\(G_s\), \(\delta_{\divis(f)}\), \(\delta_{D_s}\) and the smooth
curvature pairing are continuous for this topology on \(U\).
The fixed-test identities are therefore native current identities
\[
 \dd L_f=\delta_{\divis(f)},\qquad
 \dd G_s+\delta_{D_s}=T_{c_1(\overline{\cL})}.
\]
This extends ambient scope without enlarging the written \(S_1\)
statements of the reviewed meromorphic source.
\end{proposition}
