NAG-001869 · Accepted whole

Support under analytic pullback

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 465

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Support under analytic pullback}{解析拉回下的支撑}]
\label{fnd:coherentsupportbasechange}
\bi{Let $A\to B$ be a bounded homomorphism of generalized affinoid algebras, allowing a complete extension of the ground field for $B$, and let $M$ be a finite $A$-module. For $z\in\mathcal M(B)$ with image $x\in\mathcal M(A)$,}
{设 $A\to B$ 为 generalized affinoid 代数之间的有界同态，允许 $B$ 的基域是原基域的完备扩张，并设 $M$ 为有限 $A$-模。对像为 $x\in\mathcal M(A)$ 的点 $z\in\mathcal M(B)$，有}
\[
 z\in\operatorname{Supp}(M\otimes_A B)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 x\in\operatorname{Supp}(M).
\]
\bi{Consequently the support of the pullback of a coherent sheaf is the inverse image of its support. The closed support defined by the annihilator and its analytic base change have the same underlying set as the support of the pulled-back sheaf.}
{因此凝聚层拉回后的支撑等于原支撑的逆像。由消去理想定义的闭支撑及其解析基变换，与拉回层的支撑具有相同的底层集合。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $\mathfrak p=\ker x$ and $\mathfrak q=\ker z$. A finite module over a local ring is zero exactly when its residue vector space is zero, by Nakayama's determinant argument. Hence $x$ belongs to the support precisely when $M\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p)\ne0$. The bounded map supplies an injection $\kappa(\mathfrak p)\hookrightarrow\mathcal H(z)$, and extension of vector-space scalars is faithful. The canonical identifications}
{令 $\mathfrak p=\ker x$、$\mathfrak q=\ker z$。由 Nakayama 的行列式论证，局部环上的有限模为零，当且仅当其剩余向量空间为零。因此 $x$ 属于支撑，恰当且仅当 $M\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p)\ne0$。有界映射给出单射 $\kappa(\mathfrak p)\hookrightarrow\mathcal H(z)$，而向量空间的扩标量是忠实的。典范识别}
\[
 (M\otimes_A B)\otimes_B\mathcal H(z)
 \simeq M\otimes_A\mathcal H(z)
 \simeq (M\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p))
                \otimes_{\kappa(\mathfrak p)}\mathcal H(z)
\]
\bi{therefore prove the equivalence. The same argument at $\mathfrak q$ identifies the left side with membership in the support over $B$. The internal finite-module correspondence identifies analytic pullback with the indicated ordinary tensor product of finite modules, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb} and finite presentation. This gives the sheaf assertion on affinoid domains and hence globally. Finally the analytic base change of the annihilator-defined closed subspace has point set exactly the inverse image of its original point set, by the universal property of a closed immersion. That point set is the support just computed; no equality of the two annihilator ideals is asserted or needed.}
{遂证明所述等价。在 $\mathfrak q$ 处应用同一论证，将左侧识别为 $B$ 上的支撑条件。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb} 及有限表示，内部有限模对应把解析拉回识别为所列有限模的普通张量积。因此在 affinoid 域上得到层的断言，再得到整体断言。最后，由闭浸入的泛性质，消去理想所定义闭子空间的解析基变换，其点集恰为原点集的逆像，亦即刚才计算的支撑；这里无需把两种消去理想识别。}
\end{proof}
