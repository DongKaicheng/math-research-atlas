Coherent flatness at an inner point in characteristic zero

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 467

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Coherent flatness at an inner point in characteristic zero}{特征零内点处的凝聚平坦性}]
\label{fnd:charzeroinnercoherentflatness}
\bi{Let $K$ be a complete non-Archimedean field of characteristic zero, let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good $K$-analytic spaces, and let $\mathcal F$ be coherent on $Y_G$. Suppose that $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$ and that $\mathcal H(f(y))$ is nontrivially valued. Then $\mathcal F$ is naively $X$-flat at $y$ if and only if it is analytically $X$-flat there. In particular this holds for every complete nontrivially valued characteristic-zero ground field and every relative inner point.}
{设 $K$ 为特征零完备非阿基米德域，$f:Y\to X$ 为 good $K$-解析空间之间的态射，$\mathcal F$ 在 $Y_G$ 上凝聚。假设 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$，且 $\mathcal H(f(y))$ 的赋值非平凡。则 $\mathcal F$ 在 $y$ 处朴素 $X$-平坦，当且仅当它在该处解析 $X$-平坦。特别地，这适用于每个特征零、非平凡赋值的完备基域上的每个相对内点。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Analytic flatness implies naive flatness by the identity test. For the converse, put $x=f(y)$ and $F=\mathcal H(x)$. If the coherent stalk is zero, it is zero on an ordinary neighborhood: finitely many generators vanish after a common shrink. Every pullback is then zero near every point above $y$, proving analytic flatness. Assume henceforth that $y$ belongs to the support.}
{取恒等测试即知解析平坦蕴含朴素平坦。为证明反向，令 $x=f(y)$、$F=\mathcal H(x)$。若凝聚茎为零，则它在某个普通邻域上为零：有限个生成元经一次共同缩小后同时消失。因此每个拉回在 $y$ 上方每点附近也为零，遂得解析平坦。下文可假设 $y$ 属于支撑。}

\bi{\textbf{Step 1: the induction parameter.}
Proposition~\ref{fnd:innerstrictfibrerefinement} supplies an ordinary affinoid representative with strict $F$-fibre. Let $Z$ be the closed support of $\mathcal F$ on this representative. It is inner over $X$ at $y$, since a closed immersion is finite and inner and interior is preserved under composition. Regard $\mathcal F$ as a coherent sheaf on $Z$. This changes neither kind of flatness, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:finitepushforwardflatness}. The algebra of $Z_x$ is a strict quotient, and Lemma~\ref{fnd:coherentsupportbasechange} identifies its point set with the support of $\mathcal F_x$. We prove the assertion by induction on an upper bound $n\geq0$ for this strict fibre dimension. Restrictions to smaller ordinary affinoid neighborhoods cannot increase the analytic dimension of the support, by Corollary~\ref{fnd:affinoiddimensiondomaincover}; on a strict fibre this is its Krull dimension.}
{\textbf{步骤 1：归纳参数。}
Proposition~\ref{fnd:innerstrictfibrerefinement} 给出一个普通 affinoid 代表，其 $F$-纤维 strict。以 $Z$ 记此代表上 $\mathcal F$ 的闭支撑。闭浸入有限，因而为内态射，再由内部的复合性质，$Z$ 在 $y$ 处为 $X$ 上的内空间。将 $\mathcal F$ 视为 $Z$ 上的凝聚层；由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:finitepushforwardflatness}，这不改变两种平坦性。$Z_x$ 的代数是 strict 商，而 Lemma~\ref{fnd:coherentsupportbasechange} 将其点集识别为 $\mathcal F_x$ 的支撑。对这个 strict 纤维维数的上界 $n\geq0$ 作归纳。由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:affinoiddimensiondomaincover}，限制到较小普通 affinoid 邻域不会增加支撑的解析维数；在 strict 纤维上，这就是其 Krull 维数。}

\bi{\textbf{Step 2: a finite projection with integral fibre target.}
Write $d=\dim Z_x\leq n$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfibrefiniteprojection} gives, on ordinary neighborhoods, a finite map from $Z$ to an affinoid ordinary neighborhood $T$ of $t$ in $\mathbb A_X^{d,\mathrm{an}}$. Separate its finite branches and retain the one through $y$, so that the fibre over $t$ is the singleton $\{y\}$. The point $t$ is inner over $X$, since it is in the ordinary interior of $T$ in relative affine space. Shrink $T$ by Proposition~\ref{fnd:innerstrictfibrerefinement} so that $T_x$ is strict. Its ordinary local rings are regular by Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomainregular}; Lemma~\ref{fnd:regularaffinoidcomponents} therefore decomposes its algebra into integral factors. Lemma~\ref{fnd:originalfiberidempotentcut} retains the factor through $t$ by an ordinary domain over the original field. Pull the finite map back at each restriction. We obtain a finite map $\pi:Z\to T$ with singleton distinguished fibre, whose fibre algebra $C=\mathcal O(T_x)$ is integral and strict and satisfies $\dim C\leq d$ by Lemma~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomaindimensionbound}. All distinguished ordinary germs remain unchanged.}
{\textbf{步骤 2：整纤维目标上的有限投影。}
记 $d=\dim Z_x\leq n$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfibrefiniteprojection} 在普通邻域上给出从 $Z$ 到 $\mathbb A_X^{d,\mathrm{an}}$ 中 $t$ 的某个 affinoid 普通邻域 $T$ 的有限映射。分离有限分支并只保留含 $y$ 的分支，使 $t$ 上的纤维仅为 $\{y\}$。因 $t$ 位于 $T$ 在相对仿射空间中的普通内部，它在 $X$ 上为内点。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:innerstrictfibrerefinement} 缩小 $T$，使 $T_x$ strict。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomainregular}，其普通局部环正则，故 Lemma~\ref{fnd:regularaffinoidcomponents} 将纤维代数分解为整因子。再用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:originalfiberidempotentcut}，在原基域上的一个普通域中保留含 $t$ 的因子。每次限制都拉回有限映射，得到具有单点指定纤维的有限映射 $\pi:Z\to T$；其纤维代数 $C=\mathcal O(T_x)$ 整且 strict，并由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomaindimensionbound} 满足 $\dim C\leq d$。各指定普通芽均保持不变。}

\bi{\textbf{Step 3: the quotient has smaller fibre support.}
Put $N=\pi_*\mathcal F$. It is coherent, and $N_t$ is naively $X$-flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:finitepushforwardflatness}. The finite-module description of $N$ and Lemma~\ref{fnd:originalgenericbasisdimensiondrop} give a morphism $u:L\to N$ with $L$ finite free, whose fibre map is injective and generically bijective on $T_x$. Set $Q=\operatorname{Coker}(u)$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:charzeroinnerflatcokernel} makes $u_t$ injective and $Q_t$ naively $X$-flat. The strict fibre support of $Q$ is either empty or has dimension at most $\dim C-1\leq n-1$. If $Q_t=0$, the initial vanishing argument makes it analytically flat at $t$. Otherwise apply the induction hypothesis to $Q$ on its closed support in $T$: that support is still inner over $X$, and its fibre has the stated strictly smaller bound. Thus $Q$ is analytically $X$-flat at $t$. This also covers the induction start $n=0$, when the generic-basis lemma forces $Q_x=0$ and Nakayama gives $Q_t=0$.}
{\textbf{步骤 3：余核的纤维支撑维数下降。}
令 $N=\pi_*\mathcal F$。它凝聚，且由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:finitepushforwardflatness}，$N_t$ 朴素 $X$-平坦。由 $N$ 的有限模描述及 Lemma~\ref{fnd:originalgenericbasisdimensiondrop}，存在态射 $u:L\to N$，其中 $L$ 有限自由，而纤维映射单射并在 $T_x$ 上泛双射。令 $Q=\operatorname{Coker}(u)$。Lemma~\ref{fnd:charzeroinnerflatcokernel} 使 $u_t$ 单射，且 $Q_t$ 朴素 $X$-平坦。$Q$ 的 strict 纤维支撑或者为空，或者维数至多为 $\dim C-1\leq n-1$。若 $Q_t=0$，开头的消失论证使其在 $t$ 处解析平坦；否则将归纳假设用于 $T$ 中闭支撑上的 $Q$，该支撑在 $X$ 上仍为内空间，而其纤维具有所述严格更小的维数界。因此 $Q$ 在 $t$ 处解析 $X$-平坦。这也处理了归纳起点 $n=0$：此时泛基引理迫使 $Q_x=0$，再由 Nakayama 得到 $Q_t=0$。}

\bi{\textbf{Step 4: recover the original sheaf.}
The space $T$ is quasi-smooth over $X$, and the bijectivity locus of $u_x$ is ordinarily dense. Corollary~\ref{fnd:genericfibreflatcokernel}, applied to the injectivity of $u_t$ and the analytic flatness of $Q_t$, proves analytic $X$-flatness of $N$ at $t$. The finite-pushforward equivalence then gives analytic flatness of $\mathcal F$ at $y$. That equivalence and the generic-fibre criterion include every good analytic base change over every complete extension; thus the conclusion is exactly the universal analytic-flatness condition. Finally a completed residue field over a nontrivially valued ground field is automatically nontrivially valued, proving the last sentence.}
{\textbf{步骤 4：恢复原凝聚层。}
空间 $T$ 在 $X$ 上 quasi-smooth，且 $u_x$ 的双射 locus 普通稠密。将 Corollary~\ref{fnd:genericfibreflatcokernel} 用于 $u_t$ 的单射性及 $Q_t$ 的解析平坦性，得到 $N$ 在 $t$ 处解析 $X$-平坦。有限推前等价随即给出 $\mathcal F$ 在 $y$ 处的解析平坦性。该等价及泛纤维判据涵盖每个完备扩域上的每个 good 解析基变换，因此结论恰为普遍解析平坦条件。最后，非平凡赋值基域上的完备剩余域自动具有非平凡赋值，故得最后一句。}
\end{proof}
