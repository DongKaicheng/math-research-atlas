A Galois-compatible genus-zero model

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

\begin{theorem}[\bi{A Galois-compatible genus-zero model}
{与 Galois 作用相容的亏格零模型}]
\label{cand:genuszerogaloismodel}
\bi{Let $K$ be a complete nontrivially valued field of
characteristic zero, let $C/K$ be a smooth projective
geometrically connected genus-zero curve, and let
$D\subset C$ be finite and reduced.
There is a finite Galois extension $M/K$, with its uniquely
extended absolute value, and an identification
$C_M\simeq\mathbf P^1_M$ for which the canonical semilinear
Galois action extends to $\mathbf P^1_{M^\circ}$.
After equivariant admissible algebraic blowups, this model
can be chosen split nodal and projective, with all points
of $D_M$ rational and specializing to distinct smooth points.}
{设 $K$ 为特征零的完备非平凡赋值域，
$C/K$ 为光滑射影几何连通亏格零曲线，
$D\subset C$ 为有限约化闭子概形。
存在有限 Galois 扩张 $M/K$，配备唯一延拓的绝对值，
及一个识别 $C_M\simeq\mathbf P^1_M$，
使典范半线性 Galois 作用延拓到
$\mathbf P^1_{M^\circ}$。
经过等变 admissible 代数 blowup 后，
可使模型为分裂结点射影模型，
$D_M$ 中各点均有理，并专化到互异光滑点。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Proposition~\ref{cand:genuszerovaluationmodel} gives
a finite splitting field for $C$.
Enlarge it to a finite Galois extension $L/K$ also splitting
the residue fields of $D$, and choose $C_L\simeq\mathbf P^1_L$.
In this coordinate the canonical Galois action is by
semilinear fractional linear transformations.
For completeness, any linear automorphism of the projective
line has this form: the pullback of the affine coordinate
has exactly one simple pole, at the rational inverse image
of infinity; a rational function with that pole divisor
is a ratio of linear polynomials.
Composing a semilinear map with the inverse coefficient
automorphism reduces it to this case.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{cand:genuszerovaluationmodel}，
存在 $C$ 的有限分裂域。
将其扩大为同时分裂 $D$ 各剩余域的有限 Galois 扩张
$L/K$，并选取 $C_L\simeq\mathbf P^1_L$。
在此坐标中，典范 Galois 作用由半线性分式线性变换给出。
具体地，射影直线的任一线性自同构均具有此形式：
仿射坐标的拉回只有一个一阶极点，
位于无穷远点的有理原像；
具有该极点除子的有理函数是两个一次多项式之比。
将半线性映射与逆系数自同构复合，即归结于此情形。}

\bi{Apply Lemma~\ref{cand:finitegaussorbitcentre} to this
finite action. It gives a fixed point
$c=\zeta_{a,r}$, with $a\in L$ and $r^2\in|L^\times|$.
Choose $\gamma\in L^\times$ with $|\gamma|=r^2$,
and let $M/K$ be a finite Galois extension containing
$L(\sqrt\gamma)$. Such a finite Galois closure exists
because the characteristic is zero.
Set $\lambda=\sqrt\gamma$ in $M$.
The canonical lift $c_M=\zeta_{a,r}$ is fixed by
$\operatorname{Gal}(M/K)$: an automorphism restricts to
one of $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$, and its matrix has
coefficients in $L$; the explicit Gauss-disc formulas
of Lemma~\ref{cand:gaussdiscmetriccalculus} commute with
this extension. Automorphisms trivial on $L$ fix both
the centre and the radius.}
{对这个有限作用应用
Lemma~\ref{cand:finitegaussorbitcentre}，
得到固定点 $c=\zeta_{a,r}$，
其中 $a\in L$、$r^2\in|L^\times|$。
选取满足 $|\gamma|=r^2$ 的 $\gamma\in L^\times$，
并取包含 $L(\sqrt\gamma)$ 的有限 Galois 扩张 $M/K$。
因特征为零，所需有限 Galois 正规包存在。
在 $M$ 中令 $\lambda=\sqrt\gamma$。
典范提升 $c_M=\zeta_{a,r}$ 被
$\operatorname{Gal}(M/K)$ 固定：
每个自同构限制为 $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ 的元素，
其矩阵系数属于 $L$；
Lemma~\ref{cand:gaussdiscmetriccalculus}
的显式 Gauss 圆盘公式与该扩张相容。
在 $L$ 上平凡的自同构同时固定中心和半径。}

\bi{Replace the coordinate by $U=(T-a)/\lambda$.
The fixed point becomes the unit Gauss point.
Lemma~\ref{cand:gaussintegralstabilizer} extends every
semilinear Galois transformation to
$\mathbf P^1_{M^\circ}$, and its uniqueness gives the
group identities. This is a projective smooth model with
geometrically integral special fibre.
The finite rational marking set remains invariant.
Apply Theorem~\ref{cand:equivariantmarkedrefinement}
to obtain the asserted projective split marked model.}
{将坐标改为 $U=(T-a)/\lambda$，
固定点就变成单位 Gauss 点。
Lemma~\ref{cand:gaussintegralstabilizer}
将每个半线性 Galois 变换延拓到
$\mathbf P^1_{M^\circ}$，唯一性保证群恒等式。
这是一个射影光滑模型，特殊纤维几何整。
有限有理标记组仍然不变。
应用 Theorem~\ref{cand:equivariantmarkedrefinement}，
即得所述射影分裂标记模型。}
\end{proof}
