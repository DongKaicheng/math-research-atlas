The analytic space with finite constants restricted

C Native scalar extension and finite restriction of constants

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1764

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[The analytic space with finite constants restricted]
\label{f9cand:constants}
Suppose $n=[L:K]<\infty$. Every generalized $L$-affinoid algebra $B$
is a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra with the same Banach topology.
The two analytic spectra, domains, structure sheaves and completed
residue fields agree. On a good Hausdorff $L$-analytic space $Y$ this
gives $Y_{[K]}$, with the same ordinary moment sheaf and supports.
Purity of analytic dimension $d$ is unchanged.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Fix a basis $e_1,\ldots,e_n$ of $L/K$. By [\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}] there are
$c,C>0$ such that
\[
 c\max_i|a_i|\leq\left|\sum_i a_ie_i\right|_L
             \leq C\max_i|a_i| \quad(a_i\in K).
\]
Applying the same bounds to every power-series coefficient identifies
$L\{r^{-1}T\}$ with $K\{r^{-1}T\}\otimes_KL$, finite free with an
equivalent norm. Finite canonical algebras and their quotients are
actual generalized affinoids [\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras}, \ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange}, \ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}]. This gives
the asserted $K$-affinoid realization of $B$.
Every bounded $K$-seminorm on $B$ restricts to the unique extended
absolute value on $L$ [\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}]. The spectra therefore consist
of the same seminorms with the same evaluation topology. Evaluated
fraction fields and their completions are unchanged.

Here is the domain comparison, including its norm condition.
A $K$-affinoid algebra $C$ with a bounded structural map $B\to C$
has an induced bounded $L$-action. A norm-admissible presentation
$K\{s^{-1}U\}\twoheadrightarrow C$ extends coefficientwise to
$L\{s^{-1}U\}\twoheadrightarrow C$. Bounded multiplication gives a
bounded map; the original $K$-lifts give a uniform quotient-norm
bound, so it is norm-admissible. Thus $C$ is also $L$-affinoid.
An $L$-affinoid test object is already $K$-affinoid by the first
paragraph. A $K$-test map into $B$ conversely gives its source this
$L$-structure. Hence the two domain universal properties are equivalent:
each tests exactly the same bounded factorizations over $B$.
Domain algebras and restriction maps agree by uniqueness.
Gluing affinoid charts gives the stated global space and structure sheaf.

The ordinary neighbourhoods, analytic units, absolute values and
joint moment images used in the definition [\ref{v037:forms:germsheaf}, \ref{v037:forms:compactintegral}] now coincide
literally; thus so do the germs and their relations.
At each point $y$, the valued-field tower
$K\subset L\subset\HH(y)$ has
$d(\HH(y)/K)=d(\HH(y)/L)$, since $L/K$ is algebraic
[\ref{fnd:valuedfielddimensioncalculus}]. The maximum formula [\ref{fnd:affinoidabhyankarmaximum}] gives
equality of analytic dimensions, also on every minimal-prime quotient.
This proves the purity assertion.
\end{proof}
