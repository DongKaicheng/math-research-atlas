Hensel lifting at a Banach-algebra center

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 877

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Hensel lifting at a Banach-algebra center}
{Banach 代数中心处的 Hensel 提升}]
\label{cand:banachcenterhensel}
\bi{Let $C$ be a complete non-Archimedean Banach algebra,
let $G\in C\langle V_1,\ldots,V_m\rangle^m$ have norm at
most one, and let $v\in C^m$ have norm at most one. Suppose
$D=G'(v)$ is invertible with $\|D^{-1}\|\leq1$ and
$\|G(v)\|\leq\delta<1$. There is a unique root $v+h$ with
$\|h\|<1$, and it satisfies $\|h\|\leq\delta$. Its Jacobian
inverse has norm at most one. Moreover, if two such systems
differ in coefficient norm by at most $\epsilon$, and their
roots both lie at distance less than one from the same center
with an inverse Jacobian of norm at most one for the first
system at that center, their roots differ by at most
$\epsilon$.}
{设 $C$ 为完备非阿基米德 Banach 代数，
$G\in C\langle V_1,\ldots,V_m\rangle^m$ 的范数至多为
一，$v\in C^m$ 的范数也至多为一。假设
$D=G'(v)$ 可逆，$\|D^{-1}\|\leq1$，且
$\|G(v)\|\leq\delta<1$。存在唯一满足 $\|h\|<1$ 的
根 $v+h$，并且 $\|h\|\leq\delta$；该根处 Jacobian
的逆范数至多为一。此外，若两个这样的方程组在系数
范数下相差至多 $\epsilon$，其根都距离同一中心严格
小于一，且第一个方程组在该中心的 Jacobian 逆范数
至多为一，则两个根相差至多 $\epsilon$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Translation by the bounded tuple $v$ is a contractive
substitution. Expanding gives}
{按有界元组 $v$ 平移是压缩代换。展开得到}
\[
 G(v+H)=e+DH+N(H),\qquad e=G(v),
\]
\bi{where $N$ has terms of degree at least two and coefficient
norms at most one. This assertion follows first for polynomials
by the binomial formula, then for restricted series by uniform
truncation. For $\|h\|,\|k\|\leq\rho<1$, telescoping each
monomial gives}
{其中 $N$ 的项次数至少为二，系数范数至多为一。这先由
二项式公式对多项式成立，再由一致截断对受限级数成立。
若 $\|h\|,\|k\|\leq\rho<1$，逐单项式作望远镜分解
得到}
\[
 \|N(h)\|\leq\|h\|^2,\qquad
 \|N(h)-N(k)\|\leq\rho\|h-k\|.
\]
\bi{Consequently $h\mapsto-D^{-1}e-D^{-1}N(h)$ maps the
closed ball of radius $\delta$ to itself and is a contraction
of ratio at most $\delta$. Starting at zero, successive
differences tend to zero geometrically; completeness gives
a fixed point. Any other root at distance less than one lies
with it in a ball of some radius $\rho<1$, where the same
estimate proves equality. The Jacobian changes by a matrix
of norm at most $\delta$, so a matrix geometric series gives
the asserted inverse bound.}
{因此映射 $h\mapsto-D^{-1}e-D^{-1}N(h)$ 将半径
$\delta$ 的闭球映入自身，压缩比至多为 $\delta$。
从零开始迭代，相邻差按几何速度趋于零，完备性给出
不动点。另一距离中心小于一的根与此根共同落在某个
半径 $\rho<1$ 的球中，同一估计说明二者相等。
Jacobian 的变化矩阵范数至多为 $\delta$，故矩阵几何
级数给出所述逆范数界。}

\bi{For the perturbation assertion, let the two roots be $z,w$,
and write the first system as $G$. Telescoping its translated
monomials gives}
{对于扰动断言，设两根为 $z,w$，第一个方程组为 $G$。
对其平移后的单项式作望远镜分解，得到}
\[
 G(z)-G(w)=(D+E)(z-w),\qquad
 \|E\|\leq\max(\|z-v\|,\|w-v\|)<1.
\]
\bi{The matrix $D+E$ has inverse norm at most one. One term
on the left is zero; the other has norm at most $\epsilon$
because $w$ is a root of the second system. This proves the
bound. All constructions use Banach-algebra operations, so
nilpotents are retained.}
{矩阵 $D+E$ 的逆范数至多为一。左侧一项为零，另一项
由于 $w$ 是第二个方程组的根而范数至多为 $\epsilon$。
由此得到所需界。全部构造都使用 Banach 代数运算，
因此保留幂零元。}
\end{proof}
