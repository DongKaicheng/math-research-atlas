Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Intrinsic affinoid domains}{内禀的 affinoid 域}]
\label{fnd:intrinsicaffinoidatlas}
\bi{The analytic domains isomorphic to affinoid spaces form a canonical maximal affinoid atlas. If two such domains satisfy $U\subseteq V$, then $U$ is an affinoid domain of $V$ in the original affinoid-algebra sense. Their algebras are $\mathcal O_{X_G}(U)$, and all restriction maps are bounded. The analytic site and structure sheaf constructed from this maximal atlas equal those already constructed.}
{所有同构于 affinoid 空间的解析域组成一个典范的极大 affinoid atlas。若其中两个域满足 $U\subseteq V$，则 $U$ 在原 affinoid 代数意义下是 $V$ 的 affinoid 域。其代数为 $\mathcal O_{X_G}(U)$，且所有限制映射有界。由这一极大 atlas 构造的解析 site 和结构层，与已经构造者相同。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The inclusion $U\to V$ is a morphism between affinoid spaces and hence is induced by a bounded homomorphism. Its factorization property for affinoid tests follows from the analytic-domain universal property in Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing}; it is therefore an affinoid-domain map. Every original chart belongs to this family. The intersections of any two members are analytic domains and have affinoid chart quasi-nets, so the family is a net. Sections on each member are its affinoid algebra by the structure-sheaf theorem applied after refinement. Finally, every new chart already has an admissible covering by old chart pieces. The refinement argument of Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf} gives equality of the sites and ring sheaves.}
{包含 $U\to V$ 是 affinoid 空间之间的态射，因此由有界同态诱导。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing} 中解析域的泛性质给出其对 affinoid tests 的分解性质，故它是 affinoid 域映射。每个原 chart 都属于这个族，而任意两个成员的交都是解析域，具有 affinoid chart quasi-net，所以整个族为 net。加细后应用结构层定理，知每个成员上的截面就是其 affinoid 代数。最后，每个新 chart 都已有由旧 chart 块组成的 admissible 覆盖；Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf} 的加细论证给出 site 及环层相同。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\begin{definition}[\bi{Locally bounded morphisms}{局部有界态射}]
\label{fnd:generalnetmorphisms}
\bi{For two affinoid atlases, a morphism consists of a continuous map $f:X\to X'$ and, for every pair of charts $U\in\bar\tau$, $V\in\bar\tau'$ with $f(U)\subseteq V$, a bounded homomorphism $f_{U,V}:A_V\to A_U$ inducing $f|_U$. These maps commute with restrictions in both charts. We require that the charts $U$ admitting such a target chart $V$ form a quasi-net of $X$. An analytic space is an affinoid atlas considered up to the canonical identifications under chart refinement of Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf}.}
{对两个 affinoid atlas，一个态射由连续映射 $f:X\to X'$ 及下述资料组成：对每对满足 $f(U)\subseteq V$ 的 chart $U\in\bar\tau$、$V\in\bar\tau'$，给出诱导 $f|_U$ 的有界同态 $f_{U,V}:A_V\to A_U$。这些映射与源、目标 chart 的限制均相容，并要求具有这样一个目标 chart $V$ 的源 chart $U$ 构成 $X$ 的 quasi-net。将 affinoid atlas 按 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf} 中 chart 加细所给的典范识别视为同一对象，得到解析空间。}
\end{definition}
