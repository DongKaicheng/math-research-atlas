Pointed Jacobian charts preserve entire tubes

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 650

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Pointed Jacobian charts preserve entire tubes}
{带点 Jacobian 坐标片保持整管域}]
\label{cand:standardetaletubeisomorphism}
\bi{Let $K$ be nontrivially valued, put $O=K^\circ$ and
$k=\widetilde K$, and let $\mathfrak X=\operatorname{Spf}A_0$
and $\mathfrak Y=\operatorname{Spf}B_0$ be admissible formal
$O$-schemes with}
{设 $K$ 非平凡赋值，令 $O=K^\circ$、$k=\widetilde K$，
并设 $\mathfrak X=\operatorname{Spf}A_0$、
$\mathfrak Y=\operatorname{Spf}B_0$ 是具有如下表示的
admissible 形式 $O$-概形：}
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 F=(F_1,\ldots,F_n)\in A_0\langle Z_1,\ldots,Z_n\rangle^n,
 \qquad J(Z)=(\partial F_i/\partial Z_j),\\
 B_0=A_0\langle Z_1,\ldots,Z_n,Q\rangle/
             (F_1,\ldots,F_n,Q\det J(Z)-1).
 \end{gathered}
\]
\bi{Let $q\in\mathfrak X_s(k)$ and $z_0\in O^n$ satisfy
$\overline F(\overline z_0)(q)=0$ and
$\det\overline J(\overline z_0)(q)\ne0$. Let
$q'\in\mathfrak Y_s(k)$ be the point over $q$ with
$Z(q')=\overline z_0$ and
$Q(q')=\det\overline J(\overline z_0)(q)^{-1}$.
The generic-fibre map induces a canonical analytic isomorphism}
{设 $q\in\mathfrak X_s(k)$、$z_0\in O^n$ 满足
$\overline F(\overline z_0)(q)=0$ 及
$\det\overline J(\overline z_0)(q)\ne0$。令
$q'\in\mathfrak Y_s(k)$ 为 $q$ 上方满足
$Z(q')=\overline z_0$、
$Q(q')=\det\overline J(\overline z_0)(q)^{-1}$ 的点。
则泛纤维映射诱导典范解析同构}
\[
 ]q'[_{\mathfrak Y}\ \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\ ]q[_{\mathfrak X}.
\]
\bi{The same conclusion holds for a finite composition of such
pointed charts and for formal-open restrictions containing the
chosen special-fibre points.}
{对有限次这样的带点坐标变换的复合，以及包含所选特殊纤维点
的形式开集限制，同一结论仍成立。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations} gives the
generic affinoid algebras and their quotient norms. The coefficients
of each $F_i$ have norm at most one and tend to zero in this norm,
because they lie in $A_0$ and tend to zero $\pi$-adically.
By Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalstratumtubes}, a point of
$]q[_{\mathfrak X}$ satisfies $\max_i|F_i(z_0)|<1$ and
$|\det J(z_0)|=1$. This tube therefore lies in $W$.}
{Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations} 给出泛纤维的
affinoid 代数及其商范数。每个 $F_i$ 的系数范数至多为一，
并在此范数下趋于零，因为这些系数属于 $A_0$ 且
$\pi$-adically 趋于零。由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalstratumtubes}，
$]q[_{\mathfrak X}$ 中的点满足
$\max_i|F_i(z_0)|<1$、$|\det J(z_0)|=1$，所以此管域
包含于 $W$。}

\bi{Over this base tube, specialization to $q'$ is equivalent
to $\max_i|Z_i-z_{0i}|<1$. The base coordinates already specialize
to $q$, these inequalities fix the residues of all $Z_i$, and
$Q\det J(Z)=1$ forces the specified residue of $Q$.
These coordinates generate the special-fibre algebra and hence
determine its specialization point. Thus}
{在此底管域上，特化到 $q'$ 等价于
$\max_i|Z_i-z_{0i}|<1$。底空间坐标已特化到 $q$，这些
不等式固定全部 $Z_i$ 的剩余类，而 $Q\det J(Z)=1$ 强制
$Q$ 具有所列剩余类。这些坐标生成特殊纤维代数，因而确定
特化点。因此}
\[
 ]q'[_{\mathfrak Y}=Y\cap p^{-1}(]q[_{\mathfrak X}).
\]
\bi{Restricting the preceding isomorphism proves the assertion.
Two lifts of $\overline z_0$ differ coordinatewise by elements of
$K^{\circ\circ}$ and define the same strict inequalities;
the unique inverse is consequently independent of the lift.
A formal open containing $q$ contains its entire tube in the
generic fibre. Indeed, the principal-open specialization criterion
applies to a principal neighborhood of $q$ inside that formal open.
Formal-open restrictions therefore preserve the isomorphism,
and composition gives the final assertion.}
{限制前一同构即得结论。$\overline z_0$ 的两个提升逐坐标之差
属于 $K^{\circ\circ}$，因而定义相同的严格不等式；唯一的
逆映射遂与提升无关。包含 $q$ 的形式开集，其泛纤维包含
$q$ 的整管域：在该形式开集内选择 $q$ 的主开邻域，再应用
主开集特化判据即可。因此形式开集限制保持同构，复合则
给出最后的断言。}
\end{proof}

\bi{The simple-root argument of Lemma~\ref{fnd:analyticcompletedroots}
extends to a square system of restricted power series and can be made
uniform on increasing affinoid domains. We use its contraction
construction and the geometric inversion of
Lemma~\ref{fnd:geometricinverse}, with the maximum norm for vectors
and matrices.}
{Lemma~\ref{fnd:analyticcompletedroots} 的单根论证可推广到受限
幂级数的方程数等于变量数的系统，并在递增的 affinoid 域上一致
进行。下面复用其中的不动点构造，以及
Lemma~\ref{fnd:geometricinverse} 的几何级数求逆；向量及矩阵
均使用最大范数。}
