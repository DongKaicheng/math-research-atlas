Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Realizing a finite ordinary-stalk algebra}{实现通常茎上的有限代数}]
\label{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization}
\bi{Let $X$ be good, let $x\in X$, and put $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$. For every finite $R$-algebra $C$ there are an ordinary affinoid neighborhood $V=\mathcal M(A)$ of $x$ and a finite $A$-algebra $B$, with its canonical affinoid norm, such that}
{设 $X$ good，$x\in X$，并令 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$。对每个有限 $R$-代数 $C$，存在 $x$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域 $V=\mathcal M(A)$ 及带典范 affinoid 范数的有限 $A$-代数 $B$，使}
\[
                  C\simeq B\otimes_A R.
\]
\bi{Writing $Z=\mathcal M(B)$ and $\pi^{-1}(x)=\{z_1,\ldots,z_m\}$ for the resulting finite map, one has $C\simeq\prod_i\mathcal O_{Z,z_i}$. If $C$ is a nonzero domain, one can choose $B$ to be a domain; then $m=1$ and $\mathcal O_{Z,z_1}\simeq C$.}
{令 $Z=\mathcal M(B)$，并以 $\pi^{-1}(x)=\{z_1,\ldots,z_m\}$ 记相应有限映射的纤维，则 $C\simeq\prod_i\mathcal O_{Z,z_i}$。若 $C$ 是非零整环，还可令 $B$ 为整环；此时 $m=1$ 且 $\mathcal O_{Z,z_1}\simeq C$。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The ring $R$ is Noetherian by Theorem~\ref{fnd:ordinaryanalyticstalknoetherian}. Choose finitely many algebra generators $c_j$ of $C$; every $c_j$ is integral over $R$, by the determinant argument on a finite generating set of the $R$-module $C$ containing $1$. Thus the kernel of $R[T_1,\ldots,T_s]\to C$ contains a monic polynomial in each $T_j$. It is finitely generated, because the polynomial ring is Noetherian by \cite[Tag 00FN]{StacksAlg}. Adjoin these monic polynomials to a finite generating set of the kernel.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:ordinaryanalyticstalknoetherian}，$R$ Noetherian。为 $C$ 选有限个代数生成元 $c_j$；在包含 $1$ 的有限 $R$-模生成组上使用行列式论证，便知每个 $c_j$ 在 $R$ 上整。因此映射 $R[T_1,\ldots,T_s]\to C$ 的核包含每个 $T_j$ 的一个首一多项式。由 \cite[Tag 00FN]{StacksAlg}，多项式环 Noetherian，所以此核有限生成；将这些首一多项式加入其有限生成组。}

\bi{All coefficients of this finite list of relations are germs represented on a common ordinary affinoid neighborhood $V$ of $x$, by Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks}. Write $A=\mathcal O(V)$ and take the quotient $B$ of $A[T_1,\ldots,T_s]$ by these representatives. The monic relations show that bounded powers of the $T_j$ span $B$ over $A$, so $B$ is finite. Tensoring its presentation with $R$ recovers exactly the chosen presentation of $C$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras} gives $B$ its actual finite affinoid structure. Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation} then identifies $B\otimes_A R$ with the displayed product of ordinary local rings.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks}，这有限组关系式的全部系数芽可在 $x$ 的同一个普通 affinoid 邻域 $V$ 上表示。令 $A=\mathcal O(V)$，并以这些代表元生成的理想对 $A[T_1,\ldots,T_s]$ 取商，记为 $B$。首一关系表明 $T_j$ 的有界指数幂乘积在 $A$ 上生成 $B$，故 $B$ 有限。将此表示与 $R$ 张量，恰恢复 $C$ 的所选表示。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras} 给出 $B$ 的实际有限 affinoid 结构，再由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation} 将 $B\otimes_A R$ 识别为所列通常局部环的乘积。}

\bi{Suppose $C$ is a nonzero domain. Let $I$ be the kernel of $B\to B\otimes_A R=C$. Since $C$ is a domain, $B/I$ is a domain. It is still finite over $A$, and its tensor product with $R$ is $C$: right exactness gives the quotient of $C$ by the image of $I\otimes_A R$, and that image is zero by the definition of $I$. Replace $B$ by $B/I$. The resulting product of nonzero local rings is the domain $C$, so exactly one factor occurs. This gives the last assertion, without normalization or an excellence assumption.}
{若 $C$ 是非零整环，令 $I$ 为 $B\to B\otimes_A R=C$ 的核。因 $C$ 为整环，$B/I$ 亦为整环。它仍在 $A$ 上有限，且与 $R$ 的张量积为 $C$：右正合性给出 $C$ 对 $I\otimes_A R$ 的像的商，而由 $I$ 的定义，此像为零。以 $B/I$ 替换 $B$ 后，所得非零局部环乘积等于整环 $C$，所以恰有一个因子，证明最后断言。这里无需正规化或优秀性假设。}
\end{proof}
