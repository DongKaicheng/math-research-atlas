A simple completed-residue root lifts analytically

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 447

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A simple completed-residue root lifts analytically}{完备剩余域中的单根可解析提升}]
\label{fnd:analyticcompletedroots}
\bi{Let $X$ be good, $x\in X$, $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$, and $F=\mathcal H(x)$. If $P\in R[T]$ and $\alpha\in F$ satisfy $P_x(\alpha)=0$ and $P_x'(\alpha)\ne0$, there is a unique germ $b\in R$ with $P(b)=0$ and $b(x)=\alpha$. Consequently the residue field $\kappa(x)$ is separably algebraically closed in $F$.}
{设 $X$ good，$x\in X$，$R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$，$F=\mathcal H(x)$。若 $P\in R[T]$ 与 $\alpha\in F$ 满足 $P_x(\alpha)=0$ 且 $P_x'(\alpha)\ne0$，则存在唯一芽 $b\in R$，满足 $P(b)=0$ 及 $b(x)=\alpha$。因此剩余域 $\kappa(x)$ 在 $F$ 中可分代数闭。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The image of $R$ in $F$ is dense. Indeed, on an affinoid neighborhood $\mathcal M(A)$ it contains $\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$: a denominator nonzero at $x$ is invertible on a rational ordinary neighborhood. This fraction field is dense in $F$ by the definition of the completed residue field. Choose $a\in R$ whose value is sufficiently close to $\alpha$. Then $P'(a)$ is a unit of $R$. Write}
{环 $R$ 在 $F$ 中的像稠密。确实，在 affinoid 邻域 $\mathcal M(A)$ 上，该像包含 $\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$，因为在 $x$ 处非零的分母可在 rational 普通邻域上求逆；由完备剩余域的定义，此分式域在 $F$ 中稠密。选择 $a\in R$，使其值充分接近 $\alpha$，则 $P'(a)$ 为 $R$ 中的单位。写成}
\[
 \frac{P(a+Z)}{P'(a)}=c_0+Z+\sum_{i=2}^{d}c_iZ^i.
\]
\bi{One can choose $\rho>0$ and $0<q<1$ so that}
{可以选择 $\rho>0$ 及 $0<q<1$，使}
\[
 |\alpha-a(x)|<\rho,\qquad |c_0(x)|<q\rho,
 \qquad |c_i(x)|\rho^{i-1}<q\quad(2\leq i\leq d).
\]
\bi{To justify the simultaneous choice, first use the Taylor coefficients of $P_x$ at its simple root $\alpha$ to choose a sufficiently small $\rho$, and then approximate $\alpha$ by $a(x)$ closely enough; all the finitely many normalized coefficients vary continuously and $c_0(x)$ tends to zero. Represent the coefficients on an ordinary affinoid neighborhood and impose the weak inequalities $|c_0|\leq q\rho$ and $|c_i|\leq q\rho^{1-i}$. They define a Weierstrass domain $U$ that is still an ordinary neighborhood of $x$, because every inequality is strict at $x$. In its defining quotient Banach norm the coefficients satisfy the same weak bounds: each one is the image of the corresponding scaled Weierstrass variable.}
{为说明这些条件可以同时满足，先在单根 $\alpha$ 处利用 $P_x$ 的 Taylor 系数选择充分小的 $\rho$，再令 $a(x)$ 充分接近 $\alpha$；有限个归一化系数均连续变化，且 $c_0(x)$ 趋于零。在普通 affinoid 邻域上表示这些系数，并施加弱不等式 $|c_0|\leq q\rho$ 及 $|c_i|\leq q\rho^{1-i}$。它们定义 Weierstrass 域 $U$，且因所有不等式在 $x$ 处严格，$U$ 仍是 $x$ 的普通邻域。在其定义商 Banach 范数下，系数满足同样的弱界，因为每个系数均是相应带尺度 Weierstrass 变量的像。}

\bi{In $B=\mathcal O(U)$ consider the closed ball $\|z\|\leq\rho$ and the map $\Phi(z)=-c_0-\sum_{i\geq2}c_iz^i$. The displayed bounds give $\|\Phi(z)\|\leq q\rho$ and}
{在 $B=\mathcal O(U)$ 中考虑闭球 $\|z\|\leq\rho$ 及映射 $\Phi(z)=-c_0-\sum_{i\geq2}c_iz^i$。所列界给出 $\|\Phi(z)\|\leq q\rho$，以及}
\[
 \|\Phi(z)-\Phi(w)\|\leq q\|z-w\|.
\]
\bi{Starting with $z_0=0$, the iterates satisfy $\|z_{n+1}-z_n\|\leq q^n\rho$, so completeness gives a unique fixed point $z$ in this ball. The element $b=a+z$ satisfies $P(b)=0$. Evaluation at $x$ commutes with the convergent iteration, and $\alpha-a(x)$ is a fixed point in the same ball over $F$; uniqueness gives $b(x)=\alpha$. If two germs $b,b'$ have the stated properties, factor $P(b)-P(b')=(b-b')Q(b,b')$. The value of $Q(b,b')$ at $x$ is $P'_x(\alpha)\ne0$, so $Q(b,b')$ is a unit and $b=b'$. Finally, lift the coefficients of the separable minimal polynomial of any element of $F$ algebraic separable over $\kappa(x)$ to $R$ and apply the first assertion. Its root is the value of a germ, hence belongs to $\kappa(x)$.}
{从 $z_0=0$ 开始迭代，有 $\|z_{n+1}-z_n\|\leq q^n\rho$，所以完备性给出此闭球中的唯一不动点 $z$。元素 $b=a+z$ 满足 $P(b)=0$。在 $x$ 处求值与收敛迭代可交换，而 $\alpha-a(x)$ 是 $F$ 上同一闭球内的不动点，唯一性遂给出 $b(x)=\alpha$。若两个芽 $b,b'$ 均满足所列条件，则分解 $P(b)-P(b')=(b-b')Q(b,b')$；因 $Q(b,b')$ 在 $x$ 处的值为 $P'_x(\alpha)\ne0$，它是单位，故 $b=b'$。最后，对 $F$ 中任一在 $\kappa(x)$ 上可分代数的元素，把其最小多项式的系数提升到 $R$ 并应用第一项，其根便是某个芽的值，因而属于 $\kappa(x)$。}
\end{proof}
