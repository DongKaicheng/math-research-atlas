Amalgamation of complete valued fields

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 310

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Amalgamation of complete valued fields}{完备赋值域的相容并域}]
\label{fnd:completevaluedamalgamation}
\bi{Let $E$ and $F$ be complete valued extensions of a complete valued field $K$. There is a complete valued field $\Omega$ with isometric embeddings $E\hookrightarrow\Omega$ and $F\hookrightarrow\Omega$ agreeing on $K$.}
{设 $E,F$ 为完备赋值域 $K$ 的完备赋值扩张。则存在完备赋值域 $\Omega$ 及在 $K$ 上一致的等距嵌入 $E\hookrightarrow\Omega$、$F\hookrightarrow\Omega$。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For every finite subset $S\subset F^\times$, consider the bounded evaluation map}
{对每个有限子集 $S\subset F^\times$，考虑有界取值映射}
\[
 K\{(|s|^{-1}T_s)_{s\in S}\}\longrightarrow F,\qquad T_s\longmapsto s,
\]
\bi{and let $A_S$ be its quotient by the kernel. The kernel is closed; the quotient is a nonzero generalized affinoid algebra by Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients}. Evaluation gives a point $x_S\in\mathcal M(A_S)$ and an injective map from $K[S]$ into $A_S$. If $S\subset T$, substitution gives a bounded map $A_S\to A_T$: the variables have norms at most their assigned radii, and an element of the first kernel maps to zero because its evaluation in $F$ is zero. These maps preserve the evaluation points.}
{令 $A_S$ 为源代数对其核的商。该核闭；由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients}，所得商是非零广义 affinoid 代数。取值给出点 $x_S\in\mathcal M(A_S)$，并给出单射 $K[S]\to A_S$。若 $S\subset T$，代入给出有界映射 $A_S\to A_T$：各变量范数不超过指定半径，而第一个核的元素在 $F$ 中取值为零，故映到零。这些映射保持取值点。}

\bi{By Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarextensionspectralsurjective}, the fibre}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarextensionspectralsurjective}，纤维}
\[
 P_S=\{w\in\mathcal M_E((A_S)_E):w|_{A_S}=x_S\}
\]
\bi{is nonempty and compact. The maps $A_S\to A_T$ define an inverse system of these compact spaces. Its inverse limit is nonempty. Indeed, the product $\prod_S P_S$ is compact by \cite[Tag 08ZU]{StacksAlg}, and each compatibility equation is closed in it. A finite collection of equations involves only finitely many subsets; choose a finite $T$ containing their union and restrict any point of $P_T$ to all the subsets involved. Thus these closed conditions have the finite intersection property.}
{非空且紧。映射 $A_S\to A_T$ 使这些紧空间组成逆系统。其逆极限非空：由 \cite[Tag 08ZU]{StacksAlg}，积 $\prod_S P_S$ 紧，而每个相容方程都是其中的闭条件。有限个方程仅涉及有限个子集；选择包含这些子集之并的有限 $T$，将 $P_T$ 的任一点限制到所涉及的各子集，即同时满足这些方程。因此这些闭条件具有有限交性质。}

\bi{Choose a compatible family $(w_S)$. For $S\subset T$, the completed-residue-field construction gives an isometric embedding $\mathcal H(w_S)\hookrightarrow\mathcal H(w_T)$, by agreement of evaluations. These embeddings satisfy the cocycle identity on the dense evaluated fraction fields. Form their directed union as fields and complete it; call the resulting field $\Omega$. Every $\mathcal H(w_S)$ contains $E$ isometrically, and the transition maps fix it. The compatible maps $K[S]\to\mathcal H(w_S)$ are injective and preserve the given absolute value on $F$, because $w_S$ lies above $x_S$. As the finite subsets range over $F^\times$, their union is all of $F$. They therefore give an isometric embedding $F\hookrightarrow\Omega$, agreeing with the embedding of $E$ on $K$. Completion of a valued field is again a valued field: inversion of a Cauchy sequence with nonzero limit is Cauchy once its norms are bounded below. This completes the construction.}
{选择相容族 $(w_S)$。对 $S\subset T$，取值的一致性通过完备剩余域构造给出等距嵌入 $\mathcal H(w_S)\hookrightarrow\mathcal H(w_T)$。这些嵌入在稠密的取值分式域上满足 cocycle 恒等式。取这些域的有向并，再作完备化，得到域 $\Omega$。每个 $\mathcal H(w_S)$ 都等距包含 $E$，且过渡映射固定 $E$。相容映射 $K[S]\to\mathcal H(w_S)$ 单射并保持 $F$ 的给定绝对值，因为 $w_S$ 位于 $x_S$ 之上。当有限子集遍历 $F^\times$ 时，这些子代数之并就是 $F$，因而给出等距嵌入 $F\hookrightarrow\Omega$，并与 $E$ 的嵌入在 $K$ 上一致。赋值域的完备化仍为域：趋于非零极限的 Cauchy 序列，其范数最终有正下界，所以逆元序列仍为 Cauchy。这就完成构造。}
\end{proof}
