Finite flat maps are ordinary-open

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 480

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Finite flat maps are ordinary-open}{有限平坦映射对普通拓扑开放}]
\label{fnd:finiteflatordinaryopen}
\bi{A finite flat morphism between good analytic spaces is open for the ordinary topology.}
{Good 解析空间之间的有限平坦态射对普通拓扑为开映射。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Let \(f:Y\to X\) be finite flat, let \(y\in O\) with \(O\) ordinary open in \(Y\), and put \(x=f(y)\).
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation} gives disjoint
clopen branches over an ordinary neighborhood \(U\) of \(x\),
with the branch through \(y\) contained in \(O\): in its construction
choose the separating neighborhood of \(y\) inside \(O\) before removing
the compact image of the complement of the separating neighborhoods.
Choose an affinoid ordinary neighborhood \(V=\mathcal M(A)\) of \(x\)
in \(U\), and write \(f^{-1}(V)=\mathcal M(B)\).
Set \(R=\mathcal O_{X,x}\). By the same lemma,
\[
 B\otimes_A R\simeq\prod_{f(y_i)=x}\mathcal O_{Y,y_i}
\]
as \(R\)-algebras. The identity base change in the definition of
analytic flatness gives \(R\)-flatness at each \(y_i\). The finite
product is therefore flat over \(R\). The affinoid algebra \(A\) is
Noetherian by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}, so the
finite \(A\)-module \(B\) has a finite presentation. Tensoring the
presentation with \(R\) gives a finite presentation of \(B\otimes_A R\).
Since \(R\) is local, \cite[Tag 00NX(1) and (5)]{StacksAlg} makes
\(B\otimes_A R\) free over \(R\).

Fix such a finite \(A\)-presentation of \(B\).
Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks} expresses \(R\) as the
filtered colimit of the actual algebras \(A_W\) of rational ordinary
neighborhoods of \(x\) in \(V\).
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange} identifies the
finite inverse-image algebra there with \(B_W=B\otimes_A A_W\).
Represent a basis of \(B\otimes_A R\) in one \(B_W\), and
represent the inverse map on the finitely many generators of the
chosen presentation over the same \(A_W\), after shrinking.
The finitely many relation equations hold after a further shrinking,
so the inverse data define an \(A_W\)-linear map. Both inverse
identities on the two finite lists of generators also hold after
shrinking. Thus \(B_W\) is free over \(A_W\).

The branch through \(y\) gives an idempotent factor of \(B_W\) by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation}. This factor is finite
flat over \(A_W\), with its canonical finite-module topology.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteflatclopenimage} makes its image open and closed
in \(W\). The image contains \(x\) and is contained in \(f(O)\).
Intersecting it with the ordinary interior of \(W\) gives an ordinary
neighborhood of \(x\) in \(X\) contained in \(f(O)\).}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 有限平坦，$y\in O$，其中 $O$ 在 $Y$ 中普通开，并令 $x=f(y)$。在 $x$ 的 affinoid 普通邻域上工作。利用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation} 分离 $x$ 上方的有限个点。其构造允许含 $y$ 的分支位于 $O$ 中：先把此点的分离开邻域选在 $O$ 内，再从目标去掉所选各分支邻域之补的紧像。缩小目标的普通邻域后，该分支在有限源空间中既开又闭，故在目标上仍有限平坦；其代数为有限平坦代数的幂等因子。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteflatclopenimage}，其像既开又闭并包含 $x$。此像又包含于 $f(O)$，所以 $f(O)$ 是其每一点的邻域。}
\end{proof}
