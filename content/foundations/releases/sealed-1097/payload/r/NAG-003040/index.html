Divisor and special-fibre pushforward from the common model

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1917

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Divisor and special-fibre pushforward from the common model]
\label{f9cn:pushforward}
Let $p:\cM\to\cT$ be one of the maps of
Theorem~\ref{f9cn:common}, or any proper birational map from a normal
model to a model of the same $X$, inducing the identity on $X$.
For each codimension-one point $v$ of $\cT$, the prime divisors
of $\cM$ mapping onto $\overline{\{v\}}$ correspond exactly to
the branches $\mathfrak q_j$ of the finite normalization of
$\cO_{\cT,v}$. Their local DVRs and residue fields are those branch
DVRs and fields, embedded in the common function field.

Consequently, for every rational Cartier section $\sigma$ of a line
bundle on $\cT$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:f9cn:divpush}
 p_*\divi(p^*\sigma)=\divi(\sigma)
             \quad\text{as coherent divisor cycles on }\cT.
\end{equation}
For the special fibres, of pure dimension $d$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:f9cn:specialpush}
 p_*[\cM_k]_d=[\cT_k]_d,\qquad
 b_V=\sum_{E\mapsto V}
                  [\kappa(E):\kappa(V)]b_E.
\end{equation}
Every other prime divisor upstairs is vertical and has image of
codimension at least two, so contributes zero to these cycle
pushforwards. If $\cT$ is normal, the branch over each prime divisor
is unique, its residue degree is one, and its DVR and $b_V$ are
unchanged. In that case $p$ is an isomorphism over an open containing
all codimension-one points of $\cT$.
These formulas apply both to $\nu:\cM\to C$ and to every
projection $p_i$ in Theorem~\ref{f9cn:common}.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The target normalization $n:\cT^\nu\to\cT$ is finite by the
same Nagata argument. Normality of $\cM$ gives a factorization
$p=n\circ q$. The map $q$ is proper by \st{01W6}, applied over
$\cT$, since $\cT^\nu$ is separated over $\cT$ and $p$ is proper.
It is birational. Localization of the integral closure at $v$ is
exactly the finite algebra $B$ in Lemma~\ref{f9cn:branches}.
Its maximal ideals give the codimension-one points of $\cT^\nu$
over $v$. The morphism $q$ is an isomorphism near every such point
by \st{0BFP}(2): the target is Noetherian, the source is reduced,
and the relevant normal height-one local ring is a DVR.
This proves the precise branch correspondence, including equality of
the embedded local rings. It does not assert that $p$ is finite.

At each $v$, choose a local frame for the given line. The coefficient
of the left side of \eqref{eq:f9cn:divpush} is the branch sum in
\eqref{eq:branches}, because the pulled-back rational function is the
same element of the common function field. That sum is exactly the
coherent coefficient downstairs. All other source divisors are
contracted in codimension-one cycle pushforward. They cannot be
horizontal because the generic-fibre morphism is an isomorphism.

The special fibres are pure $d$-dimensional by \st{00QK}, applied
to the integral affine coordinate algebras containing $R$. Their
generic multiplicities are the lengths of the local quotients by
$\pi$. Apply \eqref{eq:f9cn:branch-length} with $t=\pi$ at each $V$ to obtain
\eqref{eq:f9cn:specialpush}. A contracted special component has image
dimension less than $d$: its image is contained in the special fibre
and contains none of its generic component points. Thus its cycle
pushforward is zero. Normality of $\cT$ reduces its finite local
normalization to its own DVR, giving the final assertions directly
from \st{0BFP}.
\end{proof}
