Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Uniform rigid-zero calibration; F8RC-P]
\label{thm:product}
Under H1--H3, there is \(H\in\RR\) such that, for every pair of real
numbers \(H<a<b\), the actual annular domain
\[
 V_{[a,b]}=\{e^{-b}\leq|f|\leq e^{-a}\}\subset V
\]
has the following equality of integral \(n\)-dimensional moment
chains:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:product}
 \TT_{(g,f)}(\cO_{V_{[a,b]}})
       =\TT_g(\cO_D)\times[a,b].
\end{equation}
On the left the final coordinate is \(-\log|f|\); on the right the
interval has the standard lattice \(\ZZ\) and weight one. All
weights of \(\TT_g(\cO_D)\) are the full actual quotient weights.
The same finite central complex works for every \(a,b\) in this
range. Equality is equality of top-dimensional calibrated chains;
no assertion is made about persistence of every lower-dimensional
stratum or about convergence in a test-space topology.
\end{theorem}
