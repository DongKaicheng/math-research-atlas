Existence of a finite boundary

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 511

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Existence of a finite boundary}{有限边界的存在性}]
\label{fnd:generalfiniteboundary}
\bi{Every nonempty $K$-affinoid spectrum has a finite boundary.}
{每个非空 $K$-affinoid 谱都有有限边界。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First suppose that $A$ is strict. Its finitely many minimal primes
$\mathfrak p_j$ exist by the Noetherian theorem (Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}) and
\cite[Tag 00FR]{StacksAlg}. Each quotient $B_j=A/\mathfrak p_j$ is a
strict affinoid domain. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization}
and Proposition~\ref{fnd:normalizationfiniteboundary} give a finite boundary
of each $\mathcal M(B_j)$. Every point of $\mathcal M(A)$ kills some
$\mathfrak p_j$: its prime kernel contains a minimal prime by
\cite[Tag 00E0]{StacksAlg}. The induced seminorm on $B_j$ is bounded,
as is seen by taking the infimum over the representatives in the quotient
norm. The union of the finite boundaries therefore detects every maximum
on $\mathcal M(A)$.}
{先设 $A$ 为 strict。由 Noetherian 定理（Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}）及
\cite[Tag 00FR]{StacksAlg}，它有有限多个极小素理想 $\mathfrak p_j$。
各商 $B_j=A/\mathfrak p_j$ 都是 strict affinoid 整环。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization} 及
Proposition~\ref{fnd:normalizationfiniteboundary} 给出各
$\mathcal M(B_j)$ 的有限边界。$\mathcal M(A)$ 的每个点都零化某个
$\mathfrak p_j$：由 \cite[Tag 00E0]{StacksAlg}，其素核包含极小素理想。
对商范数中的代表元取下确界可见，诱导到 $B_j$ 上的半范数有界。
因此，这些有限边界之并检测 $\mathcal M(A)$ 上的全部最大值。}

\bi{For arbitrary radii, choose a free extension $L=K_r$ for which
$A_r$ is strict, as in Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification}.
The strict case gives a finite boundary $S_r$ upstairs. The spectral
projection is surjective by Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusgausssection}.
For every $a\in A$, its values upstairs are exactly the values of $a$
at the projected points. It follows that the finite image of $S_r$
is a boundary of $\mathcal M(A)$.}
{对任意半径，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification}，
选择自由扩域 $L=K_r$，使 $A_r$ 为 strict。
Strict 情形给出上层有限边界 $S_r$。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusgausssection} 保证谱投影满射。
对每个 $a\in A$，它在上层的取值恰为在投影点处的取值。
所以 $S_r$ 的有限像就是 $\mathcal M(A)$ 的边界。}
\end{proof}
