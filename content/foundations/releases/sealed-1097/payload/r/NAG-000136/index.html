2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 135

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}\label{fnd:affineanalytificationpoints}
\bi{Points of $Z^{\an}$ are equivalently pairs consisting of a scheme point $\xi\in Z$ and an absolute value on $\kappa(\xi)$ extending that of $K$. A scheme morphism induces the corresponding analytic morphism by restriction of seminorms.}
{$Z^{\an}$ 的 points 等价于以下 pairs：一个 scheme point $\xi\in Z$，以及 $\kappa(\xi)$ 上延拓 $K$ 的 absolute value。Scheme morphism 通过限制 seminorms 诱导相应 analytic morphism。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The kernel of a multiplicative seminorm on $B$ is a prime $\mathfrak p$, and its norm on $B/\mathfrak p$ extends uniquely to $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$. Conversely such a valued field gives a seminorm on $B$. Every chosen finite set of generators has finite sizes, so the point lies in one of the affinoid pieces above. The two constructions are inverse, and composition of algebra homomorphisms gives functoriality.}
{Multiplicative seminorm 在 $B$ 上的 kernel 为 prime $\mathfrak p$，且其在 $B/\mathfrak p$ 上的 norm 唯一延拓到 $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$。反之，这样的 valued field 给出 $B$ 上的 seminorm。所选 finite generators 的 sizes 有限，因此该 point 位于上述某个 affinoid piece 中。两种 constructions 互逆，algebra homomorphisms 的 composition 给出 functoriality。}
\end{proof}
