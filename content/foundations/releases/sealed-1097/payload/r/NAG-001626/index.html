The affinoid composition formula for interior

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 415

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The affinoid composition formula for interior}{Affinoid 内部的复合公式}]
\label{fnd:affinoidinteriorcompositionequality}
\bi{For composable affinoid morphisms $Z\xrightarrow{g}Y\xrightarrow{f}X$ over any complete non-Archimedean field,}
{对任意完备非阿基米德域上的可复合 affinoid 态射 $Z\xrightarrow{g}Y\xrightarrow{f}X$，有}
\[
 \operatorname{Int}(Z/X)=
 \operatorname{Int}(Z/Y)\cap g^{-1}\operatorname{Int}(Y/X).
\]
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The inclusion from right to left is the composition implication of Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus}. Write the algebras as $A\to B\to C$ and fix $z\in Z$, with images $y$ and $x$. The completed residue-field embeddings identify the images of their graded reductions with graded subrings}
{从右到左的包含已由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus} 的复合方向证明。将代数写成 $A\to B\to C$，固定 $z\in Z$，其像为 $y$ 与 $x$。完备剩余域的嵌入将相应分次约化的像识别为分次子环}
\[
 R_A\subset R_B\subset R_C\subset\widetilde{\mathcal H(z)}^{\rm gr}.
\]
\bi{If $z\in\operatorname{Int}(Z/X)$, the \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedrelativepointevaluation]{relative point criterion}} says that $R_C$ is finite over $R_A$. It is then finite over $R_B$, using the same generating set. Each element of $R_B$ is integral over $R_A$: multiplication by this element on the finite $R_A$-module $R_C$, which contains $1$, gives a monic relation by the determinant argument. Furthermore $R_B$ is finitely generated as an $R_A$-algebra, because the \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{fixed absolute}} presentation result makes $\widetilde B^{\rm gr}$ finitely generated over $\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$, whose image is contained in $R_A$. Adjoining these finitely many integral generators successively shows that $R_B$ is finite over $R_A$: the bounded powers below the respective monic degrees form a finite spanning family. Apply the reverse implication of the \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedrelativepointevaluation]{relative point criterion}} to $A\to B$ at $y$ and to $B\to C$ at $z$. It gives $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$ and $z\in\operatorname{Int}(Z/Y)$, as required.}
{若 $z\in\operatorname{Int}(Z/X)$，则 \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedrelativepointevaluation]{相对点判据}} 说明 $R_C$ 在 $R_A$ 上有限。使用同一组生成元可知它在 $R_B$ 上也有限。$R_B$ 的每个元素都在 $R_A$ 上整：将该元素在包含 $1$ 的有限 $R_A$-模 $R_C$ 上的乘法写成矩阵，行列式论证给出首一关系。此外，$R_B$ 是有限生成的 $R_A$-代数，因为 \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{固定绝对表示}}结果说明 $\widetilde B^{\rm gr}$ 在 $\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$ 上有限生成，后者的像包含于 $R_A$。依次加入这些有限个整生成元便知 $R_B$ 在 $R_A$ 上有限：指数分别小于各首一关系次数的幂乘积构成有限生成族。最后在 $y$ 处对 $A\to B$ 并在 $z$ 处对 $B\to C$ 应用\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedrelativepointevaluation]{相对点判据}}的逆向，得到 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$ 及 $z\in\operatorname{Int}(Z/Y)$。}
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[\bi{The affinoid composition formula for interior}{Affinoid 内部的复合公式}]
\label{fnd:affinoidinteriorcompositionequality}
\bi{For composable affinoid morphisms $Z\xrightarrow{g}Y\xrightarrow{f}X$ over any complete non-Archimedean field,}
{对任意完备非阿基米德域上的可复合 affinoid 态射 $Z\xrightarrow{g}Y\xrightarrow{f}X$，有}
\[
 \operatorname{Int}(Z/X)=
 \operatorname{Int}(Z/Y)\cap g^{-1}\operatorname{Int}(Y/X).
\]
\end{theorem}
Selected scope proof · exact TeX
% NAG-001626, selected source-factor implication.
\begin{proof}
Write \(X=\mathcal M(A)\), \(Y=\mathcal M(B)\), and \(Z=\mathcal M(C)\).
For \(z\in\operatorname{Int}(Z/X)\), choose an admissible presentation
\(q_A:A\{r^{-1}T\}\twoheadrightarrow C\) with
\(\lvert q_A(T_i)(z)\rvert<r_i\) for every \(i\).
Give \(C\) the equivalent quotient norm from \(q_A\).
Then \(\|q_A(T_i)^n\|\le r_i^n\).
The bounded coefficient map \(B\to C\) and these bounds give a
bounded map \(q_B:B\{r^{-1}T\}\to C\) by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundedsubstitution}.
The coefficientwise map \(j:A\{r^{-1}T\}\to B\{r^{-1}T\}\)
is bounded by the bound for \(A\to B\).
The identity \(q_Bj=q_A\) holds on polynomials and hence on the
completions. In particular \(q_B\) is surjective.

Write \(\|c\|_{q_B}=\inf_{q_B(v)=c}\|v\|\).
Applying \(j\) to lifts through \(q_A\) and taking infima gives
\(\|c\|_{q_B}\le \|j\|\|c\|\).
Boundedness of \(q_B\) gives
\(\|c\|\le \|q_B\|\|c\|_{q_B}\).
Thus \(q_B\) is admissible. It has the same variable values and
radii as \(q_A\), so Definition~\ref{fnd:relativeinterior} gives
\(z\in\operatorname{Int}(Z/Y)\).
\end{proof}
