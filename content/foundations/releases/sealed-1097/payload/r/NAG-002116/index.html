A ray multiplicity is a boundary-order sum

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 895

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{A ray multiplicity is a boundary-order sum}{射线重数等于边界阶数之和}]
\label{cand:curveraymultiplicity}
\bi{Let $k$ have the trivial absolute value and let $C\subset\operatorname{Spec}k[M]$ be a pure one-dimensional closed subscheme. Write $C_i$ for its integral components, with generic lengths $\nu_i$, and let $\overline C_i$ be normal proper curves with function fields $k(C_i)$. At a closed point $p\in\overline C_i$, define $a_{i,p}\in N$ by}
{设 $k$ 具有平凡绝对值，$C\subset\operatorname{Spec}k[M]$ 为纯一维闭子概形。以 $C_i$ 表示其整分支，$\nu_i$ 表示一般长度，以 $\overline C_i$ 表示函数域为 $k(C_i)$ 的正规适当曲线。对闭点 $p\in\overline C_i$，定义 $a_{i,p}\in N$：}
\[
 \langle m,a_{i,p}\rangle=\operatorname{ord}_p(\chi^m).
\]
\bi{If $u\in N$ is primitive and $\mathbf R_{\ge0}u$ is a ray of $\operatorname{Trop}(C)$, its geometric initial multiplicity is}
{若 $u\in N$ 本原且 $\mathbf R_{\ge0}u$ 是 $\operatorname{Trop}(C)$ 的射线，则其几何初始重数为}
\[
 m_C(u)=\sum_i\nu_i
     \sum_{\substack{p\in\overline C_i\ \mathrm{closed}\\a_{i,p}=e_{i,p}u,\ e_{i,p}>0}}
       [\kappa(p):k]e_{i,p}.
 \tag{B}\label{eq:candrayboundaryweight}
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{By Lemma~\ref{cand:elementarylatticesplitting}, choose torus coordinates $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ in which $u=e_1$. An integral lattice change transports weighted Laurent norms and their initial ideals, so it preserves the multiplicity definition. Let $I\subset k[x_1^{\pm1},\ldots,x_n^{\pm1}]$ define $C$, and put}
{由 Lemma~\ref{cand:elementarylatticesplitting}，可选环面坐标 $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ 使 $u=e_1$。整格变换输送加权 Laurent 范数及其初始理想，因此保持重数定义。设 $I\subset k[x_1^{\pm1},\ldots,x_n^{\pm1}]$ 定义 $C$，置}
\[
 J=I\cap k[x_1,x_2^{\pm1},\ldots,x_n^{\pm1}],\qquad
 B=k[x_1,x_2^{\pm1},\ldots,x_n^{\pm1}]/J.
\]
\bi{This is the coordinate algebra of the schematic closure in $\mathbf A^1\times\mathbf G_m^{n-1}$. Multiplication by $x_1$ is injective, and $B[x_1^{-1}]$ is the coordinate algebra of $C$. Every Laurent relation, multiplied by a suitable power of $x_1$, has lowest $x_1$-exponent zero and belongs to $J$. Its ordinary initial form in direction $e_1$ is its reduction modulo $x_1$. Conversely, every nonzero reduction of an element of $J$ is such an initial form. Thus there is an equality of ideals, and hence of schemes,}
{它是 $\mathbf A^1\times\mathbf G_m^{n-1}$ 中概形论闭包的坐标代数。乘以 $x_1$ 单射，且 $B[x_1^{-1}]$ 是 $C$ 的坐标代数。每个 Laurent 关系乘以适当的 $x_1$ 幂后，最低 $x_1$ 指数为零，因而属于 $J$；其沿 $e_1$ 的通常初始形式就是模 $x_1$ 的约化。反过来，$J$ 中元素的每个非零约化均为这样的初始形式。因此得到理想的等式，以及概形的等式，}
\[
 \operatorname{in}_{e_1}(C)
      \simeq\mathbf G_m\times\operatorname{Spec}(B/x_1B).
\]
\bi{The ordinary initial ideal over a trivially valued field agrees with the initial-degeneration definition after extension and translation, by the explicit coefficient comparison in Theorem~\ref{cand:initiallocalstar}. The initial scheme is pure of dimension one by Proposition~\ref{cand:initialpuredimension}; consequently $B/x_1B$ is zero-dimensional, hence finite by Lemma~\ref{cand:finiteprimefiltration}. Its dimension is exactly $m_C(u)$. The closure $\operatorname{Spec}B$ is pure one-dimensional: each of its minimal components meets $x_1\ne0$, and its function field is that of a component of $C$, of transcendence degree one. Its generic lengths are the same $\nu_i$, since localization at the corresponding minimal primes is unchanged by inverting $x_1$.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{cand:initiallocalstar} 中的显式系数比较，平凡赋值域上的通常初始理想在扩域及平移后与初始退化定义一致。由 Proposition~\ref{cand:initialpuredimension}，初始概形纯一维，故 $B/x_1B$ 零维；Lemma~\ref{cand:finiteprimefiltration} 说明它有限，其维数恰为 $m_C(u)$。闭包 $\operatorname{Spec}B$ 纯一维：每个极小分支均与 $x_1\ne0$ 相交，其函数域是 $C$ 某个分支的函数域，超越次数为一。一般长度仍为 $\nu_i$，因为在相应极小素理想处局部化不因逆化 $x_1$ 而改变。}

\bi{For precision about the completion, take the closure of each $C_i$ in $(\mathbf P^1)^n$ and normalize it. Normalization is finite by \cite[Tag 0BXR]{StacksAlg}. The resulting curve is proper: projective space is proper by \cite[Tag 01WC]{StacksAlg}; finite products follow from proper base change and composition, and closed subspaces from proper closed immersions \cite[Tags 01W3--01W5]{StacksAlg}. A finite morphism is proper because it is of finite type, separated, and universally closed by \cite[Tag 01WM]{StacksAlg}. Here finiteness implies integrality by the determinant trick: multiplication by an algebra element on finitely many module generators satisfies the monic determinant relation. The normal proper model of a one-variable function field is unique up to its unique function-field-preserving isomorphism by \cite[Tag 0BY1]{StacksAlg}; hence this model may be used to compute the stated orders. Normalization commutes with restriction to an open set, since integral closure commutes with localization. Indeed, clearing the finitely many denominators in a monic relation shows that an element integral after localization becomes integral before localization after multiplying by one denominator.}
{为明确完备化，先取各 $C_i$ 在 $(\mathbf P^1)^n$ 中的闭包，再作正规化。由 \cite[Tag 0BXR]{StacksAlg}，正规化有限。所得曲线适当：射影空间由 \cite[Tag 01WC]{StacksAlg} 适当；有限直积由适当基变换与复合得到，闭子空间由闭浸入的适当性得到 \cite[Tags 01W3--01W5]{StacksAlg}。有限态射具有有限型、分离性，并由 \cite[Tag 01WM]{StacksAlg} 普遍闭，故适当。这里有限性借助行列式技巧推出整性：代数元素在有限个模生成元上的乘法满足首一行列式关系。由 \cite[Tag 0BY1]{StacksAlg}，一变量函数域的正规适当模型在唯一的保持函数域的同构意义下唯一，故可在此模型上计算所述阶数。正规化与开集限制相容，因为整闭包与局部化相容：将一个首一关系中的有限个分母清除，可使局部化后整的元素乘以一个分母后在局部化前已整。}

\bi{The normalization of each component of $\operatorname{Spec}B$ is therefore the inverse image of $\mathbf A^1\times\mathbf G_m^{n-1}$ in $\overline C_i$. Its points above $x_1=0$ are exactly those with}
{因此，$\operatorname{Spec}B$ 各分支的正规化就是 $\overline C_i$ 中 $\mathbf A^1\times\mathbf G_m^{n-1}$ 的逆像。它在 $x_1=0$ 上方的点恰好满足}
\[
 \operatorname{ord}_p(x_1)>0,\qquad
 \operatorname{ord}_p(x_j)=0\quad(2\le j\le n),
\]
\bi{equivalently $a_{i,p}=e_{i,p}u$ with $e_{i,p}>0$. Applying Lemma~\ref{cand:curvenormalizationlength} with $s=x_1$ gives~\eqref{eq:candrayboundaryweight}, including the residue degrees and generic component lengths.}
{等价地，$a_{i,p}=e_{i,p}u$ 且 $e_{i,p}>0$。将 Lemma~\ref{cand:curvenormalizationlength} 用于 $s=x_1$，即得~\eqref{eq:candrayboundaryweight}，其中剩余次数及分支一般长度均保留。}
\end{proof}
