Base-domain locality for affinoid morphisms

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 418

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Base-domain locality for affinoid morphisms}{Affinoid 态射的底空间域局部性}]
\label{fnd:affinoidboundaryglocality}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be an affinoid morphism, let $(X_i)$ be a finite affinoid-domain covering of $X$, and put $Y_i=f^{-1}(X_i)$. Then}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 为 affinoid 态射，$(X_i)$ 为 $X$ 的有限 affinoid-domain 覆盖，并令 $Y_i=f^{-1}(X_i)$。则}
\[
 \partial(Y/X)=\bigcup_i\partial(Y_i/X_i).
\]
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The forward base-change inclusion for interior proves that the union on the right is contained in the left side. Conversely, fix $y\in Y$ with image $x$, and suppose $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y_i/X_i)$ for every $i$ with $x\in X_i$. Write $F=\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}^{\rm gr}$, $G=\widetilde{\mathcal H(y)}^{\rm gr}$, and denote by $R_X,R_Y,R_{X_i},R_{Y_i}$ the images of the corresponding graded affinoid reductions in these fields. We regard $F$ as a graded subfield of $G$.}
{内部在基变换下的正向包含说明右边的并包含于左边。反之，固定 $y\in Y$，其像为 $x$，并假设每个满足 $x\in X_i$ 的 $i$ 都有 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y_i/X_i)$。令 $F=\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}^{\rm gr}$、$G=\widetilde{\mathcal H(y)}^{\rm gr}$，并用 $R_X,R_Y,R_{X_i},R_{Y_i}$ 表示相应 affinoid 分次约化在这些域中的像。把 $F$ 看作 $G$ 的分次子域。}

\bi{Each $Y_i$ is the \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange]{actual inverse-image domain}}, and restriction to $X_i$ and $Y_i$ preserves the relevant \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:domainresiduefields]{completed residue fields}}. Take any graded valuation ring $V\subset G$ containing $R_X$. Its intersection with $F$ is a graded valuation ring containing $R_X$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermcovercomparison} applied to the cover of the germ $(X,x)$ supplies an index $i$ such that $V\cap F$ contains $R_{X_i}$. By the assumed interiority and the \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedrelativepointevaluation]{relative point criterion}}, $R_{Y_i}$ is finite over $R_{X_i}$. A valuation ring containing the latter contains the former, by integral closedness on homogeneous elements. In particular $V$ contains $R_Y\subset R_{Y_i}$. Thus every graded valuation of $G$ containing $R_X$ also contains $R_Y$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedvaluationintegrality} and finite generation of $R_Y$ from the \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{fixed absolute}} presentation imply that $R_Y$ is finite over $R_X$. The reverse implication of the \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedrelativepointevaluation]{relative point criterion}} now gives $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$, proving the reverse inclusion of the boundary sets.}
{每个 $Y_i$ 都是\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange]{实际逆像域}}，限制到 $X_i$ 和 $Y_i$ 保持相应的\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:domainresiduefields]{完备剩余域}}。任取包含 $R_X$ 的分次赋值环 $V\subset G$。其与 $F$ 的交是包含 $R_X$ 的分次赋值环。对芽 $(X,x)$ 的覆盖应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermcovercomparison}，得到一个指标 $i$，使 $V\cap F$ 包含 $R_{X_i}$。由所假设的内点性质及\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedrelativepointevaluation]{相对点判据}}，$R_{Y_i}$ 在 $R_{X_i}$ 上有限。赋值环对齐次元素整闭，所以包含后者的赋值环也包含前者。特别地，$V$ 包含 $R_Y\subset R_{Y_i}$。因此 $G$ 中每个包含 $R_X$ 的分次赋值环也包含 $R_Y$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedvaluationintegrality} 及由\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{固定绝对表示}}得到的 $R_Y$ 的有限生成性，$R_Y$ 在 $R_X$ 上有限。应用\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedrelativepointevaluation]{相对点判据}}的逆向便得 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$，证明了边界集合的反向包含。}
\end{proof}
