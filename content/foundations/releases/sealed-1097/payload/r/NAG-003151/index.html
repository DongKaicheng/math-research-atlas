Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Closed integration on interior tests]\label{f9si:start}
Let $X$ be good, Hausdorff, and pure of analytic dimension $d\geq1$, and let $\F$ be coherent on $X_G$. The native functional
\[
 I_{X,\F}(\alpha)=\int_{X,\F}\alpha
 \quad(\alpha\in\E^{d,d}(X))
\]
is a continuous current. On every fixed cutoff stage it has a bound involving only coefficient order zero. For every ordinary open $U\subseteq\Int(X/K)$, its restriction $I_{U,\F|_U}$ is closed. In particular $I_{X,\F}$ is closed if $\partial_KX=\varnothing$.

This assertion includes nilpotent structure and singular support. It requires no smoothness, reducedness, $S_1$ condition, or deletion of a singular locus. It asserts no closedness on tests meeting the analytic boundary.
\end{theorem}
