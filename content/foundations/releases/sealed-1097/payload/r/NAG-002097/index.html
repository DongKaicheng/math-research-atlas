Canonical smooth-tube contraction in characteristic zero

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 705

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Canonical smooth-tube contraction in characteristic zero}
{特征零下光滑管域的典范收缩}]
\label{cand:charzerosmoothtube}
\bi{Assume $\operatorname{char}K=0$, and let $q$ be any
closed point where a curve model $\mathcal X/O$ is smooth
of relative dimension one. Let $\eta$ be the unique
component through $q$. Then}
{假设 $\operatorname{char}K=0$，并设曲线模型
$\mathcal X/O$ 在闭点 $q$ 处相对维数为一且光滑。
以 $\eta$ 表示经过 $q$ 的唯一分量泛点。则}
\[
 C_q=\overline{]q[}=]q[\cup\{v_\eta\}
\]
\bi{has a canonical jointly continuous strong deformation
retraction onto $v_\eta$, functorial for model isomorphisms
and isometric ground-field automorphisms. The tube is path
connected and has the canonical Gauss-ray germ and
path-connected collar neighborhood basis at $v_\eta$
described in Proposition~\ref{cand:residualdirectiongerms}.}
{具有到 $v_\eta$ 的典范联合连续强形变收缩，并与模型
同构及基域等距自同构相容。管域道路连通，并在
$v_\eta$ 处具有
Proposition~\ref{cand:residualdirectiongerms} 所描述的
典范 Gauss 射线芽及道路连通领形域邻域基。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose an affine neighborhood $\mathcal U$ of $q$
which is smooth of relative dimension one over $O$.
Its special fibre is geometrically
reduced by \cite[Tag 056T]{StacksAlg}; smoothness also gives
a unique component through $q$. Apply
Lemma~\ref{cand:charzeroresiduepointsplitting} to
$\kappa=\kappa(q)$. Lemma~\ref{cand:algebraicmodelscalar}
identifies the formal generic fibre of
$\mathcal U_{N^\circ}$ with $U_N$ and its reduced smooth
special fibre with $(\mathcal U_s)_{\widetilde N}$.
The finitely many points $q_j$ above $q$ are rational smooth
points. Thus their tubes $D_j$ are open unit discs and
their compact closures are $C_j=D_j\cup\{v_j\}$ by
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure}.}
{选择 $q$ 的一个在 $O$ 上相对维数为一且光滑的仿射邻域 $\mathcal U$。
由 \cite[Tag 056T]{StacksAlg}，其特殊纤维几何约化；
光滑性也给出经过 $q$ 的唯一分量。对
$\kappa=\kappa(q)$ 应用
Lemma~\ref{cand:charzeroresiduepointsplitting}。
Lemma~\ref{cand:algebraicmodelscalar} 将
$\mathcal U_{N^\circ}$ 的形式泛纤维识别为 $U_N$，
将其约化光滑特殊纤维识别为
$(\mathcal U_s)_{\widetilde N}$。$q$ 上方的有限个点
$q_j$ 都是有理光滑点。因此，由
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure}，其管域
$D_j$ 为开单位圆盘，紧闭包为
$C_j=D_j\cup\{v_j\}$。}

\bi{The projection $p:U_N\to U$ is the open Galois quotient
of Theorem~\ref{cand:galoisanalyticquotient}. Specialization
gives $p^{-1}(D_q)=\coprod_jD_j$.
Flat going down, \cite[Tag 00HS]{StacksAlg}, maps all
$v_j$ to $v_\eta$. Lemma~\ref{cand:closedtubeboundary}
therefore gives the displayed closure, and openness gives}
{投影 $p:U_N\to U$ 是
Theorem~\ref{cand:galoisanalyticquotient} 的开 Galois
商映射。特化给出
$p^{-1}(D_q)=\coprod_jD_j$。由平坦映射的 going down，
\cite[Tag 00HS]{StacksAlg}，全部 $v_j$ 都映到
$v_\eta$。Lemma~\ref{cand:closedtubeboundary} 因此
给出所列闭包，而开映射性给出}
\[
             p^{-1}(C_q)=\bigcup_j C_j.
\]
\bi{The finite closed pasting and compact-product quotient
argument in Proposition~\ref{cand:separablesmoothtubehomotopy}
now applies verbatim to the canonical disc homotopies:
its topological proof requires exactly this equality,
rational disc tubes upstairs, and full semilinear
coordinate invariance. These have just been verified.
It gives the desired jointly continuous contraction.}
{此时 Proposition~\ref{cand:separablesmoothtubehomotopy}
中的有限闭粘合及紧乘积商空间论证可原样应用于典范
圆盘同伦：其拓扑证明恰好需要这个等式、上方的有理
圆盘管域，以及完整的半线性坐标不变性。这些条件
刚刚都已验证，因此得到所需联合连续收缩。}

\bi{Two choices of $N$ embed over $K$ into a common finite
Galois extension, obtained by taking a splitting field of
their defining polynomials. Under further extension,
a rational residual point remains a single rational point,
and its open-disc tube is its analytic scalar extension
by Lemma~\ref{cand:algebraicmodelscalar}. The full
ground-field compatibility in
Theorem~\ref{cand:opendisccanonicalhomotopy} makes the
two constructions identical upstairs; surjectivity makes
their descents equal. Shrinking $\mathcal U$ does not
change the whole tube or its component endpoint, and the
same comparison proves independence of $\mathcal U$,
functoriality, and semilinear invariance.}
{两个 $N$ 的选择都能在 $K$ 上嵌入同一个有限 Galois
扩张：取其定义多项式的共同分裂域即可。在进一步扩张
下，有理剩余点仍是单个有理点；由
Lemma~\ref{cand:algebraicmodelscalar}，其开圆盘管域
正是原管域的解析标量扩张。
Theorem~\ref{cand:opendisccanonicalhomotopy} 中完整的
基域相容性使两个构造在上方相同，再由满射性知其
下降相同。缩小 $\mathcal U$ 不改变整个管域及其
分量端点，同一比较因此证明了与 $\mathcal U$ 的
选择无关、函子性以及半线性不变性。}

\bi{Finally, the Galois group acts transitively on the
$q_j$ by Lemma~\ref{cand:charzeroresiduepointsplitting}.
All the collar and ray arguments of
Proposition~\ref{cand:residualdirectiongerms} use only
this transitivity, finitely many disc-coordinate
transitions, and the topological quotient. They therefore
give path connectedness, the cofinal connected collars,
and the canonical embedded ray with parameter $-\log r$
in the present setting as well.}
{最后，由 Lemma~\ref{cand:charzeroresiduepointsplitting}，
Galois 群在 $q_j$ 上传递作用。
Proposition~\ref{cand:residualdirectiongerms} 中关于
领形域与射线的论证只使用此传递性、有限个圆盘坐标
过渡以及拓扑商空间，因此在当前设定下同样得到道路
连通性、共尾连通领形域，以及参数为 $-\log r$ 的
典范嵌入射线。}
\end{proof}
