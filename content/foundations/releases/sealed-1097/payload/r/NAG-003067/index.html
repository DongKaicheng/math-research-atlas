Finite analytic relations survive replacement

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1886

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Finite analytic relations survive replacement]\label{prop:relations}
For the simultaneous replacement in Proposition~\ref{prop:approx}, every finite ordinary moment-germ relation has the same value after replacing the analytic coordinates by the algebraic coordinates. After passing to a given compatible intrinsic chart contained in the witness neighborhood, a zero relation belongs to the full smooth part of GK's universal kernel.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Use the single $C$ for the complete tuple of coordinates and apply Lemma~\ref{lem:diagonal}. A relation tangentially zero on the original actual joint image is therefore zero on the replaced joint image. On the explicit very affine embedding, the replaced tuple is given by monomials and constants in the selected unit coordinates. The preimage $V$ of the strict box is contained in the original witness. Pullback by the resulting integral affine map preserves tangential vanishing on each refined cell.

For a given intrinsic chart, the selected units factor through its torus by their classes in $\cO(U)^\times/K^\times$; shrinking its real open set by the inverse image of the strict box gives a chart still contained in $V$. The same argument applies to this affine pullback. Lemma~\ref{lem:gk} then gives vanishing under every compatible algebraic test. Two choices of approximation are compared by putting their tuples and the original tuple on one smaller ordinary witness and applying the same diagonal argument. The resulting assertion is local and hence a statement about germ relations.
\end{proof}
