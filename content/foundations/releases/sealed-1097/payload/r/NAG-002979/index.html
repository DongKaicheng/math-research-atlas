Cartier cycles with full generic weights; F8CR-C

I Descent of the polarized mixed tower

Open in the English reader · PDF page 2072

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Cartier cycles with full generic weights; F8CR-C]
\label{prop:cartier}
For \(X,f\) as above, assume ZP\(_{\rm loc}\). Then, with the actual
analytic Cartier coefficients and native pure-cycle integrals,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cartier}
 \divis_X(f)=\sum_i m_i(i_i)_*\divis_{X_i}(f_i),\qquad
 \int_D\eta|_D=\sum_i m_i\int_{D_i}(i_i^*\eta)|_{D_i}
\end{equation}
for every compact ordinary \((n-1,n-1)\)-form \(\eta\).
The effective local length formula itself does not require ZP; its
use here as a common pure-cycle identity does.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
First check that no extra component is hidden in the right side.
If \(\mathfrak r\) is minimal over \((\mathfrak q_i,f)\), choose
\(\mathfrak p\subseteq\mathfrak r\) minimal over \((f)\) [\href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00E0}{Stacks 00E0(4)}].
ZP\(_{\rm loc}\) makes both integral affinoids
\(\cM(A/\mathfrak p)\) and \(\cM(A/\mathfrak r)\) have analytic
dimension \(n-1\). A strict inclusion would contain a nonzero
element of the domain \(A/\mathfrak p\); the dimension-drop bound
\hyperref[v037:degree:exceptionalimage]{NAG-002374} would give dimension at most \(n-2\) for the
latter zero locus (empty when \(n-1=0\)). This is impossible. Thus
\(\mathfrak r=\mathfrak p\). Conversely, if
\(\mathfrak p\in\Min_A(f)\) contains \(\mathfrak q_i\), no prime
strictly between them can contain \(f\), so
\(\mathfrak p/\mathfrak q_i\) is minimal over \((f_i)\).
Every \(\mathfrak p\) contains at least one minimal prime of \(A\).
This establishes the exact component correspondence, allowing the
same Cartier component to occur for several \(i\).

At each such \(\mathfrak p\), Proposition~\ref{prop:length} gives
the coefficient identity with every local Artin length. The frozen
effective Cartier comparison \hyperref[fnd:nag002772:effective-coefficient]{NAG-002772} identifies each of
these algebraic lengths with the length of the actual analytic
quotient stalk at a maximal-Abhyankar point of that zero-space
component. More explicitly, \hyperref[fnd:nag002769:generic-length]{NAG-002769} applies to the pure
quotient affinoids \(A/(f)\) and \((A/\mathfrak q_i)/(f_i)\) and
their finite structure modules; its ordinary and pointed-domain
Artinian length comparisons retain the full closed fibre. Its
regular local comparison, not flatness alone, is what justifies
that bridge. No \(S_1\) property of \(A\) is required by either
of these effective coefficient assertions. On the common reduced
component the closed immersion has identical quotient \(A/\mathfrak p\)
and generic residue field, hence generic degree one.

Finally \hyperref[thm:integration]{NAG-002778} identifies the full quotient integration
with these analytic Cartier coefficients and their intrinsic
component chains. Its pure-zero-space hypothesis is exactly the
one retained here. Finite chart localization and its ordinary
compact-form clause give the second equality in \eqref{eq:cartier}.
No additional tropical multiplicity, lattice index, or reduced-fibre
replacement is allowed.
\end{proof}
