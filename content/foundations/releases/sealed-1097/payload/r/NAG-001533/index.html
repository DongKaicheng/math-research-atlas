\begin{lemma}[\bi{Restriction and rational subdomains}{限制与 rational 子域}]\label{fnd:rationalrestrictiondomainbridge}
\bi{Let $K$ be complete, with trivial valuation allowed, and let $A\to B$ represent a strict-test domain $V\subseteq X=\mathcal M(A)$. If $A\to C$ represents another such domain $W\subseteq V$, there is a unique bounded $A$-homomorphism $B\to C$, and it represents $W$ as a strict-test domain of $\mathcal M(B)$. These restriction maps compose. Composing two strict-test domain morphisms again gives a strict-test domain morphism.}
{设 $K$ 完备，允许平凡赋值，且 $A\to B$ 表示 $X=\mathcal M(A)$ 中的 strict-test 域 $V$。若 $A\to C$ 表示另一个这样的域 $W\subseteq V$，则存在唯一有界 $A$-同态 $B\to C$，且它将 $W$ 表示为 $\mathcal M(B)$ 的 strict-test 域。这些限制映射满足复合律。两个 strict-test 域态射的复合仍为 strict-test 域态射。}

\bi{In particular, if $W$ is rational over $A$ with radii in $|K^\times|$ and $W\subseteq V$, then $W$ is rational over $B$, its rational presentation algebra is canonically boundedly isomorphic to $A_W$, and}
{特别地，若 $W$ 在 $A$ 上为半径属于 $|K^\times|$ 的 rational 域，且 $W\subseteq V$，则 $W$ 在 $B$ 上也为 rational，其 rational 表示代数与 $A_W$ 典范有界同构，且}
\[
 B\widehat\otimes_A A_W\ \simeq\ A_W.
\]
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The domain property for $A\to B$, tested on the strict algebra $C$, gives the unique map $B\to C$. Its map of spectra is the inclusion $W\subseteq V$ under the given identifications. Suppose that a bounded $B\to E$, with $E$ strict, has spectral image in $W$. The domain property for $A\to C$ gives a unique $C\to E$ over $A$. It is also over $B$: the two resulting $A$-maps from $B$ to $E$ agree by the uniqueness clause for $V$. This proves the relative domain property. The same uniqueness proves composition of restrictions. To prove transitivity of domains, factor any eligible $A\to E$ first through $B$ and then through the second domain algebra. Both existence and uniqueness follow in this order, and the spectral identifications compose.}
{将 $A\to B$ 的域泛性质应用于 strict 代数 $C$，得到唯一映射 $B\to C$。在给定谱的识别下，其谱映射为包含 $W\subseteq V$。若某个有界映射 $B\to E$ 的谱像包含于 $W$，其中 $E$ 为 strict，则由 $A\to C$ 的域泛性质，存在唯一的 $A$-映射 $C\to E$。它也是 $B$-映射：所得两个从 $B$ 到 $E$ 的 $A$-映射，由 $V$ 的唯一性条件相等。这证明相对的域泛性质。同一个唯一性证明限制映射的复合律。对域的传递性，将任意符合条件的 $A\to E$ 先通过 $B$ 分解，再通过第二个域代数分解；存在性与唯一性依次成立，谱的识别也相应复合。}

\bi{For the rational assertion use the original tuple defining $W$ over $A$, now in $B$. Its entries still generate the unit ideal. Its actual rational quotient is $D=B\widehat\otimes_A A_W$, by Proposition~\ref{fnd:relativeaffinoidpushouts} and the joint-presentation calculation of Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation}. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology}, its spectrum is the inverse image of $W$ in $\mathcal M(B)$, hence is $W$. The restriction map $B\to A_W$ just constructed, together with the identity of $A_W$, gives $D\to A_W$. This is inverse to the canonical $A_W\to D$. Indeed, the composite fixes $A_W$; on $B$ it agrees with the given map by domain uniqueness, since $\mathcal M(D)$ maps into $W\subseteq V$. The pushout universal property proves equality on $D$. All maps are bounded, so this is the asserted Banach-algebra isomorphism. Zero algebras give the empty-domain case.}
{对 rational 断言，将原来在 $A$ 上定义 $W$ 的 tuple 映入 $B$，其元素仍生成单位理想。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:relativeaffinoidpushouts} 以及 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation} 的联合表示计算，其实际 rational 商为 $D=B\widehat\otimes_A A_W$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology}，它的谱是 $W$ 在 $\mathcal M(B)$ 中的逆像，因此正是 $W$。刚构造的限制映射 $B\to A_W$ 与 $A_W$ 的恒等映射给出 $D\to A_W$。这与典范映射 $A_W\to D$ 互逆：复合固定 $A_W$；在 $B$ 上，由于 $\mathcal M(D)$ 的像包含于 $W\subseteq V$，域的唯一性使复合等于原结构映射。再由 pushout 泛性质得到 $D$ 上的相等。所有映射均有界，故得到所述 Banach 代数同构。零代数对应空域的情形。}
\end{proof}

\section{\bi{Finite affinoid coverings and coherent modules}{有限 affinoid 覆盖与凝聚模}}\label{fnd:strictdomaincoherencesection}

\bi{Throughout this section $K$ is complete, with trivial valuation allowed. The rational calculations now extend to finite coverings by strict affinoid domains. The geometric input is the finite rational covering of Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis}. Its nontrivial-valuation branch comes from Gerritzen--Grauert; its trivial-valuation branch is the separate algebraic argument just proved. The stronger ambient local Weierstrass assertion is not used. We retain the strict-test domain convention of Definition~\ref{fnd:stricttestaffinoiddomain}. Thus every ordinary strict affinoid domain is included, but no assertion about non-strict test algebras is built into the arguments below.}
{本节始终假设 $K$ 完备，允许平凡赋值。现在将 rational 情形的计算推广到 strict affinoid 域的有限覆盖。几何输入为 Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis} 的有限 rational 覆盖：非平凡赋值分支来自 Gerritzen--Grauert，平凡赋值分支则由刚才的独立代数论证给出。这里不使用更强的环境局部 Weierstrass 断言。我们沿用 Definition~\ref{fnd:stricttestaffinoiddomain} 的 strict-test 域约定。因此通常的 strict affinoid 域都包括在内，但下面的论证不预设关于 non-strict 测试代数的结论。}

\begin{definition}\label{fnd:stricttestaffinoiddomain}
\bi{For any complete non-Archimedean field $K$, including trivial valuation, let $A$ and $B$ be strict $K$-affinoid algebras. A \emph{strict-test affinoid-domain presentation} of a subset $U\subset\mathcal M(A)$ is a bounded map $\varphi:A\to B$ such that the map $\mathcal M(B)\to\mathcal M(A)$ identifies its points bijectively with $U$, and every bounded $K$-algebra map $A\to C$, with $C$ a strict $K$-affinoid and with spectrum image contained in $U$, factors uniquely through a bounded map $B\to C$. The zero algebra represents the empty subset.}
{对任意完备非阿基米德域 $K$，允许赋值平凡，设 $A,B$ 为 strict $K$-affinoid 代数。子集 $U\subset\mathcal M(A)$ 的一个 \emph{strict-test affinoid-domain 表示} 是有界映射 $\varphi:A\to B$，满足：映射 $\mathcal M(B)\to\mathcal M(A)$ 将其点与 $U$ 双射识别；且对每个 strict $K$-affinoid 代数 $C$，每个谱像包含于 $U$ 的有界 $K$-代数同态 $A\to C$ 都唯一通过某个有界映射 $B\to C$ 分解。零代数表示空子集。}
