The skeleton of a split curve model

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 681

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The skeleton of a split curve model}
{分裂曲线模型的骨架}]
\label{cand:splitmodelskeleton}
\bi{Let $\mathcal X$ be a curve model. Assume that every closed
point of its special fibre is either a point where $\mathcal X$
is smooth of relative dimension one with finite separable
residue field over $k$, or one of finitely many $k$-rational
nodes $q$ admitting a pointed diagram as in
Proposition~\ref{cand:nodaltubeendpoints}, with parameter
$a_q\in O$, $0<|a_q|<1$. Then $X=\widehat{\mathcal X}_\eta$
has a canonical finite metric graph $\Sigma(\mathcal X)$ with
vertices $v_\eta$ and one edge of length $-\log|a_q|$ for every
node $q$. The ends of this edge attach to the component points
of the two branches at $q$, allowing a self-loop. There is a
jointly continuous strong deformation retraction}
{设 $\mathcal X$ 为曲线模型。假设其特殊纤维的每个闭点，
或者是使 $\mathcal X$ 相对维数为一且光滑、剩余域在 $k$
上有限可分的点，或者属于有限个 $k$-有理结点 $q$；每个
结点都具有 Proposition~\ref{cand:nodaltubeendpoints}
中的带点图，参数为满足 $0<|a_q|<1$ 的 $a_q\in O$。
则 $X=\widehat{\mathcal X}_\eta$ 有典范有限度量图
$\Sigma(\mathcal X)$，其顶点为 $v_\eta$，并对每个结点
$q$ 有一条长度为 $-\log|a_q|$ 的边。该边的两端附着于
$q$ 的两个分支所对应的分量点，允许自环。存在联合连续
强形变收缩}
\[
       H_{\mathcal X}:X\times[0,1]\longrightarrow X,
       \qquad H_{\mathcal X}(X,1)=\Sigma(\mathcal X).
\]
\bi{On each nodal tube it is the radial annulus homotopy; on
each completed smooth tube it is the canonical disc contraction
of Proposition~\ref{cand:separablesmoothtubehomotopy}. The
entire parametrized homotopy is independent of the pointed
charts and commutes with model isomorphisms, including those
semilinear for isometric ground-field automorphisms.}
{在每个结点管域上，它是圆环的径向同伦；在每个完备光滑
管域上，它是 Proposition~\ref{cand:separablesmoothtubehomotopy}
的典范圆盘收缩。整个参数化同伦与带点坐标图的选择无关，
并与模型同构相容，包括关于等距基域自同构的半线性同构。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The vertex set $V=\{v_\eta\}$ is finite by
Proposition~\ref{cand:reducedmodelcomponentpoints}. For every
node $q$, let $I_q=[0,-\log|a_q|]$ and map its interior to the
Gauss skeleton of $D_q$ by the coordinate $-\log|s|$.
Proposition~\ref{cand:nodaltubeendpoints} and
Corollary~\ref{cand:nodaltubehomotopy} continuously extend this map to
the two incident vertices, including when these coincide.
Form a finite graph $G$ from the disjoint union of $V$ and the
$I_q$ by these endpoint identifications. Its map to $X$ is
continuous and injective: distinct interiors specialize to
distinct nodes, and no interior point specializes to a
component generic point. Since $G$ is compact and $X$ is
Hausdorff, this is a homeomorphism onto its image, denoted
$\Sigma(\mathcal X)$. Give each edge its interval length.
Corollary~\ref{cand:annulusmodulusinvariance} makes the length
independent of the pointed coordinate, using the original
absolute value on $K$.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{cand:reducedmodelcomponentpoints}，
顶点集 $V=\{v_\eta\}$ 有限。对每个结点 $q$，令
$I_q=[0,-\log|a_q|]$，并用坐标 $-\log|s|$ 将其内部映到
$D_q$ 的 Gauss 骨架。Proposition~\ref{cand:nodaltubeendpoints}
和 Corollary~\ref{cand:nodaltubehomotopy} 将此映射连续延伸到两个
入射顶点，即使它们重合。由 $V$ 与各 $I_q$ 的不交并，
按这些端点识别形成有限图 $G$。其到 $X$ 的映射连续且
单射：不同的边内部特化到不同结点，而任何内部点都不
特化到分量泛点。由于 $G$ 紧、$X$ Hausdorff，此映射是
到其像的同胚；将该像记为 $\Sigma(\mathcal X)$。给每条
边赋予其区间长度。Corollary~\ref{cand:annulusmodulusinvariance}
说明该长度与带点坐标无关，并使用 $K$ 上原有的绝对值。}

\bi{Every point of the special fibre is a component generic
point or a closed point. Thus $X$ is the finite union of the
component stars $A_\eta$ and the completed nodal tubes $C_q$.
Proposition~\ref{cand:compactcomponentstars} makes the former
compact, and Lemma~\ref{cand:closedtubeboundary} makes the
latter compact. They are therefore closed in $X$. Their
pairwise intersections consist only of incident vertices:
the open tubes have distinct specializations, and their
boundaries were identified in the endpoint propositions.}
{特殊纤维的每一点不是分量泛点就是闭点。因此 $X$ 是分量
星形空间 $A_\eta$ 与完备结点管域 $C_q$ 的有限并。
Proposition~\ref{cand:compactcomponentstars} 保证前者紧，
Lemma~\ref{cand:closedtubeboundary} 保证后者紧，故它们在
$X$ 中均闭。它们两两相交只能发生在入射顶点处：不同开
管域具有不同的特化，而其边界已经在端点命题中确定。}

\bi{On each $A_\eta$ use $H_\eta$, and on each $C_q$ use
Corollary~\ref{cand:nodaltubehomotopy}. These homotopies
fix all intersection vertices. The finite closed pasting
lemma, applied to their products with $[0,1]$, gives a
jointly continuous map $H_{\mathcal X}$. At time zero it is
the identity; at time one it maps each star to its vertex
and each nodal tube to its Gauss edge. It fixes all these
vertices and edges at every time. These are exactly the
strong deformation retraction identities.}
{在每个 $A_\eta$ 上使用 $H_\eta$，在每个 $C_q$ 上使用
Corollary~\ref{cand:nodaltubehomotopy}。这些同伦固定
全部交点顶点。对它们与 $[0,1]$ 的乘积应用有限闭粘合
引理，得到联合连续映射 $H_{\mathcal X}$。时刻零时它
是恒等映射；时刻一时，它将每个星形空间映到相应顶点，
将每个结点管域映到相应 Gauss 边。它在每个时刻固定
所有这些顶点与边。这正是强形变收缩的恒等式。}

\bi{Coordinate independence on smooth tubes is part of
Proposition~\ref{cand:separablesmoothtubehomotopy}; on nodal
tubes it is Corollary~\ref{cand:openannuluscoordinateisometry}.
A model isomorphism permutes residual components and closed
points, sends their completed tubes to the corresponding
completed tubes, and induces the indicated analytic or
semilinear isomorphisms. Hence it commutes with every local
homotopy, and therefore with their global pasting.}
{光滑管域上的坐标无关性包含于
Proposition~\ref{cand:separablesmoothtubehomotopy}，结点
管域上的坐标无关性则由
Corollary~\ref{cand:openannuluscoordinateisometry} 给出。
模型同构置换剩余分量及闭点，将完备管域映到对应的完备
管域，并诱导所述解析或半线性同构。因此它与每个局部
同伦相容，也就与其全局粘合相容。}
\end{proof}
