Tubes of locally closed strata

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 590

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Tubes of locally closed strata}
{局部闭分层的管域}]
\label{fnd:formalstratumtubes}
\bi{Let $\mathfrak X$ be an admissible formal $O$-scheme,
$X=\mathfrak X_\eta$, and $Z\subset\mathfrak X_s$ a locally
closed subset. Then $]\!Z[_{\mathfrak X}=\operatorname{sp}^{-1}(Z)$
is an analytic domain of $X$, canonically with its induced
analytic structure. On an affine formal chart it has a finite
cover by sets of the form}
{设 $\mathfrak X$ 为 admissible 形式 $O$-概形，
$X=\mathfrak X_\eta$，$Z\subset\mathfrak X_s$ 为局部闭子集。
则 $]\!Z[_{\mathfrak X}=\operatorname{sp}^{-1}(Z)$ 是 $X$ 的
解析域，典范配备诱导解析结构。在每个形式仿射图上，它有由
下列形式的集合组成的有限覆盖：}
\[
 \{x:|f_i(x)|<1\ (1\leq i\leq m),\quad |g(x)|=1\},
 \qquad f_i,g\in\mathcal O_{\mathfrak X}(\mathfrak U).
\]
\bi{This construction commutes with intersections and inverse
images under formal morphisms.}
{此构造与交及形式态射下的逆像相容。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{On $\mathfrak U=\operatorname{Spf}(A_0)$, every integral
element has absolute value at most one by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations}. Thus evaluation
induces $A_0/K^{\circ\circ}A_0\to\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}$;
equivalently the source is $A_0\otimes_O k$.
The special fibre is of finite type over $k$, hence Noetherian
by \cite[Tag 00FN]{StacksAlg}.
Write $Z\cap\mathfrak U_s$ as a finite union of
$V(\bar f_1,\ldots,\bar f_m)\cap D(\bar g)$, with lifts in
$A_0$. The definition of specialization gives exactly the
displayed absolute-value conditions.}
{在 $\mathfrak U=\operatorname{Spf}(A_0)$ 上，由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations}，每个整系数
元素的绝对值至多为一。因此取值诱导
$A_0/K^{\circ\circ}A_0\to\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}$。
特殊纤维在 $k$ 上有限型，由 \cite[Tag 00FN]{StacksAlg}
为 Noetherian。将
$Z\cap\mathfrak U_s$ 写为有限个
$V(\bar f_1,\ldots,\bar f_m)\cap D(\bar g)$ 的并，并在
$A_0$ 中选择提升。特化的定义恰好给出所列绝对值条件。}

\bi{The inequalities $|f_i|<1$ define an ordinary open subset
$W$ of $\mathfrak U_\eta$. The locus $|g|=1$ is the Laurent
affinoid domain $|g|\geq1$, because $|g|\leq1$ everywhere.
Their intersection is an analytic domain by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalnetdomaincalculus}. To verify the
finite-union assertion, first take the common open subset $W$
cut out by the equations for the closed part of $Z$, and write
the open part as a finite union of principal opens. Each of
the resulting domains is relatively closed in their union.
At a point, omit the finitely many closed pieces not containing
it, and take small rational neighborhoods inside the remaining
pieces. They give the quasi-net required by
Definition~\ref{fnd:generalnetanalyticdomain}. Hence the union
is an analytic domain with its induced topology.}
{不等式 $|f_i|<1$ 在 $\mathfrak U_\eta$ 中定义通常开子集
$W$。由于处处有 $|g|\leq1$，集合 $|g|=1$ 就是 Laurent
affinoid 域 $|g|\geq1$。由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalnetdomaincalculus}，二者的交
为解析域。验证有限并时，先用 $Z$ 的闭部分的方程取共同的
开集 $W$，再将其开部分写为有限个主开集的并。所得各域在
它们的并中相对闭。在一点处去掉不含该点的有限个闭片，再
在其余各片中选小的 rational 邻域，就得到
Definition~\ref{fnd:generalnetanalyticdomain} 所要求的
quasi-net。因此该并以其诱导拓扑成为解析域。}

\bi{The formal affine generic fibres form the analytic net of
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}. The local
domains just constructed therefore glue: their intersections
agree as subsets, and analytic-domain structures are unique by
Corollary~\ref{fnd:intrinsicaffinoidatlas} and chartwise domain
calculus. The same uniqueness proves compatibility with
intersections. Functoriality of specialization gives the asserted
inverse-image identity, and domain base change supplies its
analytic structure. The equations and the finite covers used
above are local on formal affine charts; no global
quasi-compactness of $Z$ is assumed.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}，
形式仿射图的泛纤维组成解析网。因此刚构造的局部域相容粘合：
它们的交作为子集一致，而
Corollary~\ref{fnd:intrinsicaffinoidatlas} 及逐图的域运算
保证解析域结构唯一。同一唯一性证明与交相容。特化的函子性
给出所述逆像恒等式，域的基变换则给出其解析结构。上述方程
及有限覆盖均只在形式仿射图上选取，不要求 $Z$ 全局拟紧。}
\end{proof}
