The generic coefficient under scalar extension

C Native scalar extension and finite restriction of constants

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1758

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[The generic coefficient under scalar extension]
\label{f9cand:generic-length}
Let $A$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra, $B=A_L$, and $M$ a finite
$A$-module. Then $M_L=B\otimes_A M=M\wt_K L$, with equivalent canonical
complete norms, and $A\to B$ is faithfully flat. If
$\mathfrak q\in\Min(B)$ and $\mathfrak p=\mathfrak q\cap A$, then
$\mathfrak p\in\Min(A)$ and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:length}
 \len_{B_{\mathfrak q}}(M_L)_{\mathfrak q}
 =\len_{A_{\mathfrak p}}M_{\mathfrak p}\,
   \len_{B_{\mathfrak q}}
       (B_{\mathfrak q}/\mathfrak pB_{\mathfrak q}).
\end{equation}
In particular the generic coefficient can acquire a nontrivial length
even when the original component is reduced.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The completed-module identification and faithful flatness are the exact
assertions of [\ref{fnd:generalscalarpresentation}, \ref{fnd:generalscalarfaithful}]; its proof uses closed finite
presentations and strict exactness, not a formal replacement of completed
tensors by algebraic tensors. Flat going down \cite[Tag 00HS, Lemma 10.39.19]{StacksAlg} implies
that a prime strictly below $\mathfrak p$ would lift to a prime strictly
below $\mathfrak q$. Thus $\mathfrak p$ is minimal.

The two local rings are Noetherian of dimension zero, hence Artinian
\cite[Tag 00KH, Lemma 10.60.5]{StacksAlg}; their finite modules have finite length
\cite[Tag 00JB, Lemma 10.53.6]{StacksAlg}. The localized homomorphism
$A_{\mathfrak p}\to B_{\mathfrak q}$ is flat. A composition series of
$M_{\mathfrak p}$ has every simple quotient equal to
$\kappa(\mathfrak p)$. Tensoring this series with $B_{\mathfrak q}$ is
exact, and every successive quotient is
$B_{\mathfrak q}/\mathfrak pB_{\mathfrak q}$. Additivity of length gives
\eqref{eq:length}. The quotient is nonzero because the map is local.
\end{proof}
