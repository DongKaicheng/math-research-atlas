The tube of a rational smooth point

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 666

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{The tube of a rational smooth point}
{有理光滑点的管域}]
\label{cand:smoothpointpolydisctube}
\bi{Let $\mathcal X$ be a flat finitely presented $O$-scheme.
Suppose that $\mathcal X\to\operatorname{Spec}O$ is smooth
of relative dimension $d$ at $q\in\mathcal X_s(k)$.
Then $]q[_{\widehat{\mathcal X}}$ is analytically isomorphic
to the open unit $d$-dimensional polydisc over $K$.}
{设 $\mathcal X$ 为平坦有限表示 $O$-概形，并假设
$\mathcal X\to\operatorname{Spec}O$ 在
$q\in\mathcal X_s(k)$ 处相对维数为 $d$ 且光滑。
则 $]q[_{\widehat{\mathcal X}}$ 解析同构于 $K$ 上
$d$ 维开单位多圆盘。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{By \cite[Tag 054L]{StacksAlg}, a Zariski neighbourhood
$\mathcal U$ of $q$ admits an \'etale morphism
$\mathcal U\to\mathbb A^d_O$. Its image $\bar a$ is a
$k$-rational point. Apply the preceding proposition. The completion
of $\mathbb A^d_O$ is $\operatorname{Spf}O\langle T_1,\ldots,T_d\rangle$;
its generic fibre is the closed unit polydisc. If $a_i\in O$
lift the coordinates of $\bar a$, the specialization criterion
identifies its $\bar a$-tube with $|T_i-a_i|<1$ for every $i$.
Translation by $a$ gives the stated open unit polydisc.}
{由 \cite[Tag 054L]{StacksAlg}，$q$ 的某个 Zariski 邻域
$\mathcal U$ 具有 \'etale 态射
$\mathcal U\to\mathbb A^d_O$，其像 $\bar a$ 是
$k$-有理点。应用上一命题。
$\mathbb A^d_O$ 的完备化为
$\operatorname{Spf}O\langle T_1,\ldots,T_d\rangle$，其泛纤维
是闭单位多圆盘。若 $a_i\in O$ 提升 $\bar a$ 的坐标，
特化判据将 $\bar a$-管域识别为对全部 $i$ 满足
$|T_i-a_i|<1$ 的点集。按 $a$ 平移即得所述开单位多圆盘。}
\end{proof}
