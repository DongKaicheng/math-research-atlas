A minimal finite boundary is the smallest closed boundary

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 512

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A minimal finite boundary is the smallest closed boundary}
{极小有限边界是最小闭边界}]
\label{fnd:minimalfiniteboundary}
\bi{Suppose that a compact space $X$ of multiplicative seminorms on an
algebra has continuous evaluation functions and has a finite boundary.
Every inclusion-minimal finite boundary is contained in every closed
boundary. In particular it is unique.}
{设由某代数上的乘性半范数组成的紧空间 $X$，其取值函数连续，且具有
有限边界。则每个按包含关系极小的有限边界，都包含于每个闭边界。
特别地，这个极小有限边界唯一。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose an inclusion-minimal finite boundary $S$ and a point
$\xi\in S$. Minimality supplies $a$ with}
{选择按包含关系极小的有限边界 $S$ 及点 $\xi\in S$。由极小性，
存在 $a$ 使}
\[
 m=|a(\xi)|>\max_{s\in S\setminus\{\xi\}}|a(s)|=q,
 \qquad m>0,
\]
\bi{where the maximum over the empty set is zero. Let $C$ be any closed
boundary and put $C_0=\{y\in C:|a(y)|=m\}$, a nonempty compact set.
For $b$ in the algebra and $y\in C_0$, the boundary property of $S$ gives}
{其中空集上的最大值约定为零。任取闭边界 $C$，令
$C_0=\{y\in C:|a(y)|=m\}$；它是非空紧集。
对代数中的 $b$ 及 $y\in C_0$，由 $S$ 的边界性质有}
\[
 |b(y)|\leq
 \max_{s\in S}\bigl(|a(s)|/m\bigr)^n|b(s)|
 \xrightarrow[n\to\infty]{}|b(\xi)|.
\]
\bi{If $|b(\xi)|>0$, choose for each sufficiently large $n$ a point
$y_n\in C$ attaining the maximum of $|a^n b|$. The maximum equals
$m^n|b(\xi)|$ for large $n$. Since $|a|\leq m$ and $|b|$ is bounded,
we obtain $|a(y_n)|\to m$ and $|b(y_n)|\geq|b(\xi)|$.
A convergent subnet has limit in $C_0$ and attains $|b(\xi)|$ there.
For finitely many $b_1,\ldots,b_t$ with nonzero values at $\xi$, apply
this conclusion to their product. The preceding upper bound for each
factor forces all their values to equal the respective values at $\xi$
at one common point of $C_0$. Factors vanishing at $\xi$ vanish throughout
$C_0$ by that same upper bound.}
{若 $|b(\xi)|>0$，则对每个足够大的 $n$，选择在 $C$ 上达到
$|a^n b|$ 最大值的点 $y_n$；该最大值为 $m^n|b(\xi)|$。
由于 $|a|\leq m$ 且 $|b|$ 有界，可得 $|a(y_n)|\to m$ 以及
$|b(y_n)|\geq|b(\xi)|$。一个收敛子网的极限属于 $C_0$，
并在该处达到 $|b(\xi)|$。
对有限多个在 $\xi$ 处非零的 $b_1,\ldots,b_t$，将这一结论应用于
它们的乘积。每个因子的上述上界迫使它们同时在 $C_0$ 的同一个点
达到各自在 $\xi$ 处的值。在 $\xi$ 处为零的因子则由同一上界在
整个 $C_0$ 上为零。}

\bi{Thus the closed sets
$\{y\in C_0:|b(y)|=|b(\xi)|\}$, indexed by all $b$, have the finite
intersection property. Compactness produces a point with all evaluations
equal to those of $\xi$. Points are the seminorms themselves, so this
point is $\xi$. Hence $\xi\in C$. This holds for every $\xi\in S$
and every closed boundary $C$, proving the assertion.}
{因此，由所有 $b$ 编号的闭集
$\{y\in C_0:|b(y)|=|b(\xi)|\}$ 具有有限交性质。
由紧性，存在一点，其所有取值均等于 $\xi$ 的相应取值。
由于点就是半范数本身，该点只能是 $\xi$，故 $\xi\in C$。
这对每个 $\xi\in S$ 及每个闭边界 $C$ 成立，因而得到结论。}
\end{proof}
