The affinoid norm on a finite algebra

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 85

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{The affinoid norm on a finite algebra}{有限代数的 affinoid 范数}]
\label{fnd:strictfinitealgebras}
\bi{Let $A$ be a strict $K$-affinoid algebra over a complete field with its fixed absolute value, possibly trivial. Every commutative finite $A$-algebra $D$ has a strict $K$-affinoid structure whose norm is uniformly equivalent to its canonical finite-$A$-module norm; the structural map $A\to D$ is bounded. This structure is unique up to bounded isomorphism. More precisely, if $D$ already has a strict affinoid norm and $A\to D$ is bounded, that norm is equivalent to the canonical finite-$A$-module norm. Every $A$-linear map between such finite algebras is bounded.}
{设 $A$ 为完备域 $K$ 上的 strict affinoid 代数，绝对值固定且允许平凡。每个交换有限 $A$-代数 $D$ 都具有 strict $K$-affinoid 结构，其范数与典范有限 $A$-模范数一致等价，且结构映射 $A\to D$ 有界。此结构在有界同构意义下唯一。更确切地说，若 $D$ 已带有 strict affinoid 范数且 $A\to D$ 有界，则该范数与典范有限 $A$-模范数等价。这类有限代数之间的每个 $A$-线性映射均有界。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use a presentation quotient norm on $A$, and give $D$ its canonical module norm by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}. Multiplication by each $d\in D$ is a bounded $A$-linear operator $L_d$ on $D$. If $e_1,\ldots,e_s$ generate $D$ as an $A$-module, controlled lifts in $A^s$ show that $\|L_d\|_{\rm op}\leq C\|d\|$ for a constant independent of $d$: write $d=\sum a_i e_i$ with $\max\|a_i\|\leq2\|d\|$ and use the finitely many operator bounds for $L_{e_i}$. Conversely $\|d\|=\|L_d(1)\|\leq\|L_d\|_{\rm op}\|1\|$. Thus}
{在 $A$ 上使用表示的商范数，并由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules} 在 $D$ 上赋予典范模范数。每个 $d\in D$ 的乘法给出 $D$ 上的有界 $A$-线性算子 $L_d$。若 $e_1,\ldots,e_s$ 在 $A$ 上生成 $D$，则 $A^s$ 中的受控提升说明 $\|L_d\|_{\rm op}\leq C\|d\|$，其中常数与 $d$ 无关：写成 $d=\sum a_i e_i$，使 $\max\|a_i\|\leq2\|d\|$，再使用有限个 $L_{e_i}$ 的算子界。反之，$\|d\|=\|L_d(1)\|\leq\|L_d\|_{\rm op}\|1\|$。因此}
\[
 \|d\|_*:=\|L_d\|_{\rm op}
\]
\bi{is an equivalent complete submultiplicative norm, with $\|1\|_*=1$, and $A\to D$ is contractive. The zero algebra is treated separately with its unique norm.}
{是等价的完备次乘范数，满足 $\|1\|_*=1$，且 $A\to D$ 为压缩映射。零代数另取其唯一范数。}

\bi{If the valuation is nontrivial, multiply each $e_i$ by a nonzero scalar so that its $*$-norm is at most one; the rescaled elements still generate $D$ as an $A$-module. Substitution gives a bounded surjection $A\langle T_1,\ldots,T_s\rangle\to D$. It is norm-strict without invoking an open mapping theorem: the polynomials of degree at most one already give controlled lifts, by the chosen finite-module presentation and equivalence of norms. Its kernel is an algebraic ideal, hence closed by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}; consequently its quotient norm is equivalent to $\|\cdot\|_*$. Since $A\langle T\rangle$ is strict by the established iterated-presentation construction, this gives the required affinoid structure.}
{若赋值非平凡，将每个 $e_i$ 乘以非零标量，使其 $*$-范数不超过一；缩放后的元素仍在 $A$ 上生成 $D$。代入给出有界满射 $A\langle T_1,\ldots,T_s\rangle\to D$。这里无需开映射定理即可证明范数严格性：由所选有限模表示及范数等价性，次数不超过一的多项式已经给出受控提升。其核是代数理想，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules} 为闭理想，所以商范数与 $\|\cdot\|_*$ 等价。已有的迭代表示构造说明 $A\langle T\rangle$ 为 strict，因而得到所需的 affinoid 结构。}

\bi{If the valuation is trivial, a strict Tate algebra is a polynomial algebra with the discrete norm (one on every nonzero element). Its presentation quotients and their canonical finite-module norms are also discrete. The same construction is then the algebraic surjection $A[T_1,\ldots,T_s]\to D$, and all the indicated norms are discrete. No choice of a small nonzero scalar is made in this case.}
{若赋值平凡，则 strict Tate 代数就是带离散范数（非零元素范数为一）的多项式代数。其表示的商与典范有限模范数也为离散范数。上述构造此时就是代数满射 $A[T_1,\ldots,T_s]\to D$，所述范数均为离散范数。这里不选择范数很小的非零标量。}

\bi{Finally suppose $D$ already has an affinoid norm and the structural map is bounded. The finite generator map $A^s\to D$ is bounded and surjective. Over a nontrivially valued field Lemma~\ref{fnd:nonarchopenmapping} makes it norm-strict, which identifies its quotient norm with the existing norm up to equivalence. Over a trivially valued field both strict presentation norms are equivalent to the discrete norm. The assertion about linear maps now follows from the finite-module theorem with coefficient algebra $A$.}
{最后设 $D$ 已带 affinoid 范数且结构映射有界。有限生成元映射 $A^s\to D$ 有界且满。若赋值非平凡，Lemma~\ref{fnd:nonarchopenmapping} 保证其范数严格性，即商范数与现有范数等价。若赋值平凡，两者的 strict 表示范数均等价于离散范数。线性映射的断言于是来自以 $A$ 为系数代数的有限模定理。}
\end{proof}
