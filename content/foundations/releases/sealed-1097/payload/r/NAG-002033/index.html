Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{A uniform implicit-function domain}{一致隐函数域}]
\label{cand:uniformhenseldomain}
\bi{Let $A$ be a $K$-affinoid algebra over a complete non-Archimedean
field, with an admissible submultiplicative Banach norm. Let $n\geq1$,
let $F=(F_1,\ldots,F_n)\in A\langle Z_1,\ldots,Z_n\rangle^n$
have coefficients of norm at most one, and fix $z_0\in(K^\circ)^n$.
Write $J(Z)=(\partial F_i/\partial Z_j)$ and put}
{设 $A$ 是完备非阿基米德域 $K$ 上的 affinoid 代数，配备
admissible 次乘性 Banach 范数。设 $n\geq1$，
$F=(F_1,\ldots,F_n)\in A\langle Z_1,\ldots,Z_n\rangle^n$
的系数范数至多为一，并固定 $z_0\in(K^\circ)^n$。
记 $J(Z)=(\partial F_i/\partial Z_j)$，并令}
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 e=F(z_0),\qquad J_0=J(z_0),\qquad d=\det J_0,\\
 B=A\langle Z_1,\ldots,Z_n,Q\rangle/
               (F_1,\ldots,F_n,Q\det J(Z)-1),\\
 p:\mathcal M(B)\longrightarrow\mathcal M(A).
 \end{gathered}
\]
\bi{Give the following subsets their analytic-domain structures:}
{为下列子集配备其解析域结构：}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 W&=\{x:|d(x)|=1,\ \max_i|e_i(x)|<1\},\\
 Y&=\{y\in p^{-1}(W):\max_i|Z_i(y)-z_{0i}|<1\}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{Then $p:Y\to W$ is an analytic isomorphism. Its inverse has
$Z$-coordinate equal to the unique vector of analytic functions
$z$ on $W$ satisfying $F(z)=0$ and
$\max_i|z_i(x)-z_{0i}|<1$ at every $x\in W$.}
{则 $p:Y\to W$ 是解析同构。其逆的 $Z$ 坐标为 $W$ 上唯一的
解析函数向量 $z$，满足 $F(z)=0$，且对每个 $x\in W$ 有
$\max_i|z_i(x)-z_{0i}|<1$。}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
\bi{Translation by $z_0$ is a contractive endomorphism of the unit
Tate algebra. Indeed, expand each monomial by the integer binomial
formula. Its coefficients have norm at most the norm of the original
coefficient. For any positive threshold, only finitely many original
coefficients exceed that threshold, and each associated monomial has
finitely many terms. The series criterion
\ref{fnd:seriescriterion} therefore permits the sums to be interchanged
and gives a restricted series with the same bound. Termwise
differentiation is also bounded, since integer coefficients have
absolute value at most one. Consequently}
{按 $z_0$ 平移是单位 Tate 代数的压缩自同态。确实，按整数
二项式公式展开每个单项式，所得各系数的范数至多为原系数
范数。对任意正阈值，超过该阈值的原系数只有有限个，每个
对应单项式的展开又只有有限项。因此级数判据
\ref{fnd:seriescriterion} 允许交换求和次序，得到具有同一界的
受限级数。逐项求导也有界，因为整数系数的绝对值至多为一。
于是}
\[
 F(z_0+H)=e+J_0H+N(H),\qquad
 N_i(H)=\sum_{|\alpha|\geq2}a_{i\alpha}H^\alpha,
 \qquad \|a_{i\alpha}\|\leq1.
\]
\bi{The coefficients of each displayed series tend to zero.
In particular all entries of $J_0$, its adjugate, and $d$ have
norm at most one.}
{各所列级数的系数均趋于零。特别地，$J_0$、其伴随矩阵以及
$d$ 的所有系数范数均至多为一。}

\bi{The equation $|d|=1$ defines the Laurent affinoid domain
$D=\{|d|\geq1\}$, because $\|d\|\leq1$. The set $W$ is open
in $D$, and $Y$ is open in $p^{-1}(W)$; domain calculus and base
change, Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalnetdomaincalculus}, give their
analytic structures. For $0<\delta<1$, put}
{因 $\|d\|\leq1$，等式 $|d|=1$ 定义 Laurent affinoid 域
$D=\{|d|\geq1\}$。集合 $W$ 在 $D$ 中开，$Y$ 在
$p^{-1}(W)$ 中开；Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalnetdomaincalculus}
的域运算及基变换给出其解析结构。对 $0<\delta<1$，令}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 W_\delta&=\{x\in D:\max_i|e_i(x)|\leq\delta\},\\
 A_\delta&=A\langle D_1,\delta^{-1}E_1,\ldots,
                         \delta^{-1}E_n\rangle/
                (dD_1-1,E_1-e_1,\ldots,E_n-e_n).
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{In the defining quotient norm, the original coefficients and
$d^{-1}=D_1$ have norm at most one, $\|e\|\leq\delta$, and
$\|J_0^{-1}\|\leq1$ by the adjugate formula. These domains cover
$W$ admissibly: every point lies in the relative interior of some
$W_\delta$, by choosing $\max_i|e_i(x)|<\delta<1$.}
{在定义商范数下，原有系数及 $d^{-1}=D_1$ 的范数至多为一，
$\|e\|\leq\delta$，并且伴随矩阵公式给出
$\|J_0^{-1}\|\leq1$。这些域 admissibly 覆盖 $W$：对任一点
选择 $\max_i|e_i(x)|<\delta<1$，该点便属于某个
$W_\delta$ 的相对内部。}

\bi{For vectors $h,h'$ with norms at most $\delta$, telescoping
each monomial gives}
{对范数至多为 $\delta$ 的向量 $h,h'$，逐个单项式作差得到}
\[
 \|N(h)\|\leq\delta^2,\qquad
 \|N(h)-N(h')\|\leq\delta\|h-h'\|.
\]
\bi{The same bounds pass to restricted series by convergence.
Thus $\Phi(h)=-J_0^{-1}e-J_0^{-1}N(h)$ preserves the closed
radius-$\delta$ ball in $A_\delta^n$ and has contraction constant
$\delta<1$. The iteration from zero used in
Lemma~\ref{fnd:analyticcompletedroots} gives a fixed point
$h_\delta$ with $\|h_\delta\|\leq\delta$.
Set $z_\delta=z_0+h_\delta$; then $F(z_\delta)=0$ and}
{由收敛性，同一估计适用于受限级数。因此
$\Phi(h)=-J_0^{-1}e-J_0^{-1}N(h)$ 保持 $A_\delta^n$ 中
半径为 $\delta$ 的闭球，且压缩常数为 $\delta<1$。
Lemma~\ref{fnd:analyticcompletedroots} 中从零开始的迭代
给出不动点 $h_\delta$，满足 $\|h_\delta\|\leq\delta$。
令 $z_\delta=z_0+h_\delta$，则 $F(z_\delta)=0$，且}
\[
 J(z_\delta)=J_0(I+V_\delta),\qquad
 \|V_\delta\|\leq\delta<1.
\]
\bi{The matrix geometric inverse gives $J(z_\delta)^{-1}$ of
norm at most one. Its determinant therefore has norm at most one.
Hence $Z\mapsto z_\delta$ and
$Q\mapsto\det J(z_\delta)^{-1}$ define a bounded map
$B\to A_\delta$ and an analytic section $s_\delta:W_\delta\to Y$.}
{矩阵几何级数给出范数至多为一的逆矩阵
$J(z_\delta)^{-1}$，其行列式范数也至多为一。因此
$Z\mapsto z_\delta$、$Q\mapsto\det J(z_\delta)^{-1}$
定义有界同态 $B\to A_\delta$，以及解析截面
$s_\delta:W_\delta\to Y$。}

\bi{For later use, the monomial telescoping also gives a matrix
difference identity}
{为后面使用，单项式逐变量作差还给出矩阵恒等式}
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 F(z_0+h)-F(z_0+h')=(J_0+R(h,h'))(h-h'),\\
 \|R(h,h')\|\leq\max(\|h\|,\|h'\|).
 \end{gathered}
\]
\bi{whenever both norms are less than one. Each entry of the
remainder contains a monomial of positive degree in $h,h'$;
the restricted-series tails converge, so this is an identity
in the Banach algebra, not only at its analytic points.}
{其中要求两个范数都小于一。余项的每个矩阵项至少含一个
关于 $h,h'$ 的正次数单项式；受限级数尾项收敛，所以这是
Banach 代数内的恒等式，而不只是解析点处的等式。}

\bi{If $0<\delta_1\leq\delta_2<1$, the defining presentations
give a contractive restriction $A_{\delta_2}\to A_{\delta_1}$.
Apply the difference identity to $h_{\delta_1}$ and the restricted
$h_{\delta_2}$. The factor
$J_0+R=J_0(I+J_0^{-1}R)$ is invertible, since
$\|J_0^{-1}R\|\leq\delta_2<1$. Hence the roots agree.
Analytic morphism gluing gives a section $s:W\to Y$.}
{若 $0<\delta_1\leq\delta_2<1$，定义表示给出压缩的限制
映射 $A_{\delta_2}\to A_{\delta_1}$。将差恒等式应用于
$h_{\delta_1}$ 及限制后的 $h_{\delta_2}$；由于
$\|J_0^{-1}R\|\leq\delta_2<1$，因子
$J_0+R=J_0(I+J_0^{-1}R)$ 可逆，故两个根相等。
解析态射粘合给出截面 $s:W\to Y$。}

\bi{We check the other inverse identity on analytic functions.
The domains of $Y$ defined by $p(y)\in W_\delta$ and
$\max_i|Z_i(y)-z_{0i}|\leq\gamma$, for $0<\delta,\gamma<1$,
form an admissible affinoid cover. In their defining quotient norms,
$\|Z-z_0\|\leq\gamma$ and the pulled-back $h_\delta$ has norm
at most $\delta$. The difference identity becomes}
{下面在解析函数层面验证另一个逆恒等式。在 $Y$ 中，由
$p(y)\in W_\delta$ 及
$\max_i|Z_i(y)-z_{0i}|\leq\gamma$ 定义的域组成 admissible
affinoid 覆盖，其中 $0<\delta,\gamma<1$。在其定义商范数下，
$\|Z-z_0\|\leq\gamma$，且 $h_\delta$ 的拉回范数至多为
$\delta$。差恒等式变为}
\[
 0=F(Z)-F(z_\delta)=(J_0+R)(Z-z_\delta),\qquad
 \|J_0^{-1}R\|\leq\max(\delta,\gamma)<1.
\]
\bi{Thus $Z=z_\delta$ in the affinoid algebra, and
$Q\det J(Z)=1$ identifies the $Q$ coordinate as well.
The base coordinates already agree, so $s\circ p$ is the identity
as an analytic morphism. The argument preserves nilpotents.}
{所以在 affinoid 代数中有 $Z=z_\delta$，而
$Q\det J(Z)=1$ 也识别 $Q$ 坐标。底空间坐标本已相等，
故 $s\circ p$ 作为解析态射为恒等；论证同时保留幂零元。}

\bi{Finally, let $z'$ be another analytic solution with the stated
strict bound. On $W_\delta$, restrict further to the Weierstrass
domains $\max_i|z'_i-z_{0i}|\leq\gamma$, where $0<\gamma<1$.
As $\delta$ and $\gamma$ vary, these domains cover $W$ admissibly.
Their defining quotient norms make the map from $A_\delta$
contractive and give $\|z'-z_0\|\leq\gamma$; thus all the restricted
series substitutions converge. The same difference identity has an
invertible matrix factor, since its error has norm at most
$\max(\delta,\gamma)<1$. It follows in each domain algebra that
$z'=z_\delta$, and sheaf gluing proves uniqueness on $W$.}
{最后，设 $z'$ 是满足所列严格界的另一个解析解。在 $W_\delta$
上进一步限制到 Weierstrass 域
$\max_i|z'_i-z_{0i}|\leq\gamma$，其中 $0<\gamma<1$。
当 $\delta$、$\gamma$ 变化时，这些域 admissibly 覆盖 $W$。
其定义商范数使来自 $A_\delta$ 的映射压缩，并保证
$\|z'-z_0\|\leq\gamma$，因而全部受限级数代入收敛。
同一差恒等式的矩阵因子可逆，因为其误差范数至多为
$\max(\delta,\gamma)<1$。所以在每个域的代数中
$z'=z_\delta$；层的粘合给出 $W$ 上的唯一性。}
\end{proof}

\bi{The simple-root argument of Lemma~\ref{fnd:analyticcompletedroots}
extends to a square system of restricted power series and can be made
uniform on increasing affinoid domains. We use its contraction
construction and the geometric inversion of
Lemma~\ref{fnd:geometricinverse}, with the maximum norm for vectors
and matrices.}
{Lemma~\ref{fnd:analyticcompletedroots} 的单根论证可推广到受限
幂级数的方程数等于变量数的系统，并在递增的 affinoid 域上一致
进行。下面复用其中的不动点构造，以及
Lemma~\ref{fnd:geometricinverse} 的几何级数求逆；向量及矩阵
均使用最大范数。}
