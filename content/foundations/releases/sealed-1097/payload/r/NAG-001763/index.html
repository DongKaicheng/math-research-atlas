Ordinary regular comparison in the strict characteristic-zero case

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 330

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Ordinary regular comparison in the strict characteristic-zero case}{Strict 特征零情形的通常正则比较}]
\label{fnd:strictcharzeroregularcomparison}
\bi{Let $K$ be nontrivially valued of characteristic zero, let $A$ be strict $K$-affinoid, let $x\in X=\mathcal M(A)$, and put $\mathfrak p=\ker(x)$ and $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$. Both of the following homomorphisms are regular:}
{设 $K$ 的赋值非平凡且特征为零，$A$ 为 strict $K$-affinoid 代数，$x\in X=\mathcal M(A)$，并令 $\mathfrak p=\ker(x)$、$R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$。则以下两个同态均为正则同态：}
\[
                         A_{\mathfrak p}\longrightarrow R,
                 \qquad R\longrightarrow\widehat R .
\]
\bi{Here regular means flat with geometrically regular algebraic fibres; the fibre fields and tensor products in this assertion are those of ordinary commutative algebra.}
{这里正则指平坦且代数纤维几何正则；此断言中的纤维域及张量积均取通常交换代数意义。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{All rings are Noetherian, by the affinoid finite-module theorem, Theorem~\ref{fnd:ordinaryanalyticstalknoetherian}, and \cite[Tag 0316]{StacksAlg}. The map $A_{\mathfrak p}\to R$ is flat: $R$ is the filtered colimit of ordinary rational-neighborhood algebras, all flat over $A$, and elements of $A\setminus\mathfrak p$ are units in $R$. The completion map is faithfully flat by \cite[Tag 00MC]{StacksAlg}. It remains to check their algebraic fibres.}
{由 affinoid 有限模定理、Theorem~\ref{fnd:ordinaryanalyticstalknoetherian} 及 \cite[Tag 0316]{StacksAlg}，全部环均 Noetherian。映射 $A_{\mathfrak p}\to R$ 平坦：$R$ 是普通 rational 邻域代数的滤过直极限，而这些代数均在 $A$ 上平坦，且 $A\setminus\mathfrak p$ 中元素在 $R$ 中可逆。完备化映射由 \cite[Tag 00MC]{StacksAlg} 忠实平坦。因此只需核验代数纤维。}

\bi{Let $\mathfrak q\subset\mathfrak p$ be any prime of $A$, set $B=A/\mathfrak q$, and put $F=\operatorname{Frac}(B)$. The point $x$ lies on $Z=\mathcal M(B)$, and finite branch separation for this closed immersion gives}
{取 $A$ 的任意素理想 $\mathfrak q\subset\mathfrak p$，令 $B=A/\mathfrak q$、$F=\operatorname{Frac}(B)$。点 $x$ 位于 $Z=\mathcal M(B)$ 上；对这个闭浸入使用有限分支分离，得到}
\[
                R/\mathfrak qR=\mathcal O_{Z,x},
       \qquad R\otimes_A F=\mathcal O_{Z,x}\otimes_B F .
\]
\bi{The ring $B$ is strict and integral. Proposition~\ref{fnd:separablenormalizationregularlocus} supplies a nonzero $d\in B$ for which $\mathcal O_{Z,x}[1/d]$ is regular. The displayed fibre is its further localization by the nonzero elements of $B$, so it is a regular Noetherian ring. Its ground field $F$ has characteristic zero. By \cite[Tag 0381]{StacksAlg}, a Noetherian algebra over a characteristic-zero field is geometrically regular once it is regular: the only finite purely inseparable field extension to be tested is the identity extension. This proves geometric regularity of every fibre of $A_{\mathfrak p}\to R$, at all primes $\mathfrak q\subset\mathfrak p$.}
{环 $B$ 为 strict 整环。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:separablenormalizationregularlocus}，存在非零 $d\in B$，使 $\mathcal O_{Z,x}[1/d]$ 正则。所列纤维是它关于 $B$ 的非零元素的进一步局部化，因而为正则 Noetherian 环。其底域 $F$ 的特征为零。由 \cite[Tag 0381]{StacksAlg}，特征零域上的 Noetherian 代数只要正则便几何正则，因为所需测试的有限纯不可分扩张只有恒等扩张。这就在全部素理想 $\mathfrak q\subset\mathfrak p$ 处证明了 $A_{\mathfrak p}\to R$ 的纤维几何正则。}

\bi{Now let $\mathfrak P$ be any prime of $R$ and put $C=R/\mathfrak P$. By Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictordinaryneighborhoods} and Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization}, there is a strict integral affinoid algebra $B$, a point $z\in Z=\mathcal M(B)$, and an identification}
{现取 $R$ 的任意素理想 $\mathfrak P$，令 $C=R/\mathfrak P$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictordinaryneighborhoods} 及 Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization}，存在 strict 整 affinoid 代数 $B$、点 $z\in Z=\mathcal M(B)$ 及识别}
\[
                         B\lhook\joinrel\longrightarrow
                         \mathcal O_{Z,z}=C .
\]
\bi{The injection is part of the kernel-quotient construction in that theorem. Finite-module completion, \cite[Tag 00MA, part (3)]{StacksAlg}, gives $\widehat R\otimes_R C=\widehat C$, since the maximal ideal of the quotient $C$ is the image of the maximal ideal of $R$. Consequently the algebraic fibre of $R\to\widehat R$ at $\mathfrak P$ is}
{此单射来自该定理中除去核的构造。由有限模完备化公式 \cite[Tag 00MA, (3)]{StacksAlg}，有 $\widehat R\otimes_R C=\widehat C$，因为商环 $C$ 的极大理想恰为 $R$ 的极大理想的像。因此 $R\to\widehat R$ 在 $\mathfrak P$ 处的代数纤维为}
\[
       \widehat R\otimes_R\kappa(\mathfrak P)
                 =\widehat C\otimes_C\operatorname{Frac}(C).
\]
\bi{Apply Proposition~\ref{fnd:separablenormalizationregularlocus} to $B$. It gives $0\ne d\in B$ such that $\widehat{\mathcal O_{Z,z}}[1/d]=\widehat C[1/d]$ is regular. Because $B\hookrightarrow C$, the image of $d$ is nonzero in $C$. The last displayed fibre is thus a further localization of this regular ring, and is regular. It is Noetherian and is an algebra over the characteristic-zero field $\operatorname{Frac}(C)$, so the same Tag 0381 makes it geometrically regular. This treats every prime of $R$, and proves the second assertion.}
{将 Proposition~\ref{fnd:separablenormalizationregularlocus} 用于 $B$，得到 $0\ne d\in B$，使 $\widehat{\mathcal O_{Z,z}}[1/d]=\widehat C[1/d]$ 正则。由于 $B\hookrightarrow C$，$d$ 在 $C$ 中的像非零。因此最后所列纤维是该正则环的进一步局部化，故正则。它是 Noetherian 环，并为特征零域 $\operatorname{Frac}(C)$ 上的代数，所以同一 Tag 0381 给出几何正则性。这处理了 $R$ 的全部素理想，并证明第二个断言。}
\end{proof}
