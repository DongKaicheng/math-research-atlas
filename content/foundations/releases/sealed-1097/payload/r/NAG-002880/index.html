Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Full Green identity; F8AG-G]
\label{thm:green}
Let \(\alpha\) and \(\eta\) be ordinary smooth forms of bidegrees
\((p,p)\) and \((q,q)\), with \(p+q=n-1\), satisfying
\(J\alpha=(-1)^p\alpha\), \(J\eta=(-1)^q\eta\). Suppose
\(\Supp\alpha\cap\Supp\eta\) is compact. Define the flux form
\[
        \Gamma(\alpha,\eta)=\alpha\wedge d''\eta-d''\alpha\wedge\eta.
\]
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:green}
 \int_{X,\mathcal F}\bigl(\alpha\wedge\dd\eta-\dd\alpha\wedge\eta\bigr)
                     =\BB_{X,\mathcal F}\bigl(\Gamma(\alpha,\eta)\bigr).
\end{equation}
In particular, for an ordinary smooth scalar \(u\) and symmetric
\((n-1,n-1)\)-test \(\eta\) of compact support,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scalargreen}
 \int_{X,\mathcal F}u\,\dd\eta-\dd u\wedge\eta
               =\BB_{X,\mathcal F}(u\,d''\eta-d''u\wedge\eta).
\end{equation}
The right side is evaluated on the full boundary trace
\(r_{\partial X}\Gamma\). It need not vanish when the test reaches
\(\partial_KX\).
\end{theorem}
