Balancing and commutation of corners

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 863

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Balancing and commutation of corners}
{角点的平衡性与交换性}]
\label{cand:cornerbalancecommute}
\bi{For every balanced real cycle $C$ and continuous piecewise
affine functions $\varphi,\psi$ on it, $D_\varphi C$ is balanced
and}
{对每个平衡实循环 $C$ 及其上的连续分片仿射函数
$\varphi,\psi$，循环 $D_\varphi C$ 平衡，且}
\[
 D_\psi D_\varphi C=D_\varphi D_\psi C.
\]
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Both assertions are local at an original face of codimension
two. Take its star and quotient by its direction space. Subtract
linear extensions of the two functions along that face. By
Proposition~\ref{cand:cornerwelldefined}, this changes neither
corner. We have reduced to a two-dimensional balanced rational
fan, with both functions homogeneous and linear on its cones.
Cut cones with lineality by rational hyperplanes if necessary,
so that the cones are pointed; their two-dimensional cones then
have two extreme rays. Let the distinct primitive ray vectors be
$v_1,\ldots,v_s$. For a cone with extreme rays $v_i,v_j$ put}
{两个断言均可在原复形的余维二面处局部验证。取其星形复形
并商去该面的方向空间，再减去两个函数沿该面的线性延拓。
由 Proposition~\ref{cand:cornerwelldefined}，这不改变角点。
于是归约到二维平衡有理扇，两函数均齐次且逐锥线性。必要时
以有理超平面切割含直线的锥，使锥成为尖锥；此时每个二维锥
有两条极射线。设互异本原射线向量为 $v_1,\ldots,v_s$。
对由 $v_i,v_j$ 生成的锥，置}
\[
 d_{ij}=[N_{\langle v_i,v_j\rangle}:\mathbf Zv_i+\mathbf Zv_j],
 \qquad a_{ij}=a_{ji}=w_{ij}/d_{ij};
\]
\bi{put $a_{ij}=0$ if there is no such cone. Modulo
$\mathbf Rv_i$, its primitive normal is the class of $v_j/d_{ij}$.
Indeed $v_i$ is primitive in the rank-two saturated lattice;
Lemma~\ref{cand:elementarylatticesplitting} completes it to a
basis, in which $v_j=bv_i+d_{ij}e$ with
the appropriate sign of $e$. Balancing at $v_i$ therefore says}
{若不存在该锥则令 $a_{ij}=0$。模 $\mathbf Rv_i$ 后，其本原
法向是 $v_j/d_{ij}$ 的类。事实上，$v_i$ 在秩二饱和格中
本原，由 Lemma~\ref{cand:elementarylatticesplitting} 补成格基
后可写 $v_j=bv_i+d_{ij}e$，其中 $e$ 的符号
取向正确。因此，沿 $v_i$ 的平衡性给出}
\[
 \sum_j a_{ij}v_j=\lambda_i v_i
 \quad\text{for some }\lambda_i\in\mathbf R.
\]
\bi{The weight of the first corner on the ray $i$ is consequently}
{因而第一次角点在第 $i$ 条射线上的权重为}
\[
 b_i(\varphi)=\sum_j a_{ij}\varphi(v_j)-\lambda_i\varphi(v_i).
 \tag{R}\label{eq:candtwodcorner}
\]
\bi{One may use $v_j/d_{ij}$ instead of a lattice lift in
\eqref{eq:candcornerweight}, since their difference is tangential.
Symmetry of $a_{ij}$ and the displayed balancing equations give}
{在~\eqref{eq:candcornerweight} 中可用 $v_j/d_{ij}$ 代替格
提升，因为二者之差为切向向量。由 $a_{ij}$ 的对称性及所示
平衡等式，得到}
\[
 \sum_i b_i(\varphi)v_i
 =\sum_j\varphi(v_j)\sum_i a_{ij}v_i
       -\sum_i\lambda_i\varphi(v_i)v_i=0.
\]
\bi{This is balancing for the corner at the origin. The coefficient
of the second corner at that origin is}
{这正是角点在原点处的平衡性。第二次角点在原点的系数为}
\[
 \sum_i b_i(\varphi)\psi(v_i)
 =\sum_{i,j}a_{ij}\varphi(v_j)\psi(v_i)
       -\sum_i\lambda_i\varphi(v_i)\psi(v_i),
\]
\bi{which is symmetric in $\varphi,\psi$. Hence the two orders
agree. For a cycle of dimension one, its corner is a zero-cycle,
whose further corner is zero; in dimension zero both assertions
are immediate. The local calculation and refinement invariance
prove the theorem in every dimension.}
{此式关于 $\varphi,\psi$ 对称，故两个次序相同。一维循环的
角点为零循环，再取角点为零；零维情形的两个断言直接成立。
局部计算及细分不变性由此证明任意维数的结论。}
\end{proof}
