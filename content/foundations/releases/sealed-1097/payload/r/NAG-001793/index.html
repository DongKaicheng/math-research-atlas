Ordinary regularity of relative affine space

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 359

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Ordinary regularity of relative affine space}{相对仿射空间的通常正则性}]
\label{fnd:relativeaffinespaceordinaryregular}
\bi{Let $X$ be good and let $Y$ be a good analytic domain in $\mathbb A_X^{n,\an}$. At every $y\in Y$, with image $x\in X$, the ordinary local homomorphism $\mathcal O_{X,x}\to\mathcal O_{Y,y}$ is regular.}
{设 $X$ good，且 $Y$ 为 $\mathbb A_X^{n,\an}$ 中的 good 解析域。对每个 $y\in Y$ 及其像 $x\in X$，通常局部同态 $\mathcal O_{X,x}\to\mathcal O_{Y,y}$ 正则。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Flatness follows from relative polydisc flatness and analytic-domain flatness, since one can choose a polydisc ordinary neighborhood of $y$ with radii larger than its coordinate values. Put $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ and $S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$. For every prime $\mathfrak p\subset R$ and finite purely inseparable extension $E/\kappa(\mathfrak p)$, apply Proposition~\ref{fnd:finitefieldtestrealization}. After ordinary restrictions it supplies an integral affinoid space $Z=\mathcal M(B)$, an inclusion $B\hookrightarrow C=\mathcal O_{Z,z}$ with $\operatorname{Frac}(C)=E$, and an inverse-image domain $T$ of $Y$ in $\mathbb A_Z^{n,\an}$ such that}
{在 $y$ 处选择坐标半径严格大于其坐标值的多圆盘普通邻域，则相对多圆盘平坦性和解析域平坦性给出所需平坦性。令 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$。对每个素理想 $\mathfrak p\subset R$ 及有限纯不可分扩张 $E/\kappa(\mathfrak p)$，应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:finitefieldtestrealization}。经过普通限制，它给出整 affinoid 空间 $Z=\mathcal M(B)$、包含 $B\hookrightarrow C=\mathcal O_{Z,z}$ 及 $\operatorname{Frac}(C)=E$，还有 $Y$ 在 $\mathbb A_Z^{n,\an}$ 中的逆像域 $T$，满足}
\[
              S\otimes_R E
                 =\left(\prod_i\mathcal O_{T,t_i}\right)
                                                       \otimes_C E.
\]
\bi{Fix any prime $\mathfrak Q$ in a factor surviving this localization. It contracts to zero in $B$. On an ordinary affinoid neighborhood of $t_i$ in $T$, the polynomial coordinates define a ring map $B[T_1,\ldots,T_n]\to\mathcal O_{T,t_i}$. Let $\mathfrak q$ be the contraction of $\mathfrak Q$ to this polynomial ring. Since $\mathfrak q\cap B=0$, its localization $B[T]_{\mathfrak q}$ is a localization of a polynomial algebra over $\operatorname{Frac}(B)$, and is regular. Theorem~\ref{fnd:relativealgebraicregularcomparison} makes its map to $(\mathcal O_{T,t_i})_{\mathfrak Q}$ regular. Tag 031E proves that this target is regular. Every prime of the displayed $E$-fibre has been treated. Tag 0381 therefore gives geometric regularity of every algebraic fibre of $R\to S$.}
{固定一个因子中在此局部化后仍存在的任意素理想 $\mathfrak Q$；它在 $B$ 中收缩为零。在 $T$ 中 $t_i$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域上，多项式坐标定义映射 $B[T_1,\ldots,T_n]\to\mathcal O_{T,t_i}$。以 $\mathfrak q$ 记 $\mathfrak Q$ 在此多项式环中的收缩。因 $\mathfrak q\cap B=0$，局部化 $B[T]_{\mathfrak q}$ 是 $\operatorname{Frac}(B)$ 上多项式代数的局部化，因而正则。Theorem~\ref{fnd:relativealgebraicregularcomparison} 说明其到 $(\mathcal O_{T,t_i})_{\mathfrak Q}$ 的映射正则，再由 Tag 031E 得到目标正则。所列 $E$-纤维的每个素理想都已处理，因此 Tag 0381 给出 $R\to S$ 每个代数纤维的几何正则性。}
\end{proof}
