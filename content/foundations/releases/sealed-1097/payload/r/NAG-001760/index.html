Regular affine-space domains

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 327

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Regular affine-space domains}{仿射空间中正则的解析域}]
\label{fnd:affinespacedomainregular}
\bi{Let $K$ be any complete non-Archimedean field, and let $V=\mathcal M(A)$ be an affinoid domain in $\mathbb A_K^{n,\an}$. Every ordinary local ring $\mathcal O_{V,v}$ is regular, and $A$ is a regular Noetherian ring. The same assertions hold after every complete valued extension of $K$.}
{设 $K$ 为任意完备非阿基米德域，$V=\mathcal M(A)$ 为 $\mathbb A_K^{n,\an}$ 中的 affinoid 域。每个通常局部环 $\mathcal O_{V,v}$ 都正则，且 $A$ 是正则 Noetherian 环。在 $K$ 的任意完备赋值扩张之后，同样的断言仍成立。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Fix $v\in V$. Choose a nontrivially valued complete extension $L$ of $\mathcal H(v)$; if necessary adjoin a free Gauss radius. The evaluated coordinates give an $L$-rational point $w$ of $V_L$ above $v$. Indeed, the bounded map $A\to\mathcal H(v)\to L$ and the identity of $L$ induce a bounded map $A\widehat\otimes_K L\to L$. By analytic domain base change, $V_L$ is an affinoid domain of $\mathbb A_L^{n,\an}$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalpointdomaininterior} makes $V_L$ an ordinary neighborhood of $w$ in that affine space. Consequently}
{固定 $v\in V$。为 $\mathcal H(v)$ 选择一个绝对值非平凡的完备扩域 $L$；必要时可添加一个自由 Gauss 半径。坐标取值给出 $V_L$ 中位于 $v$ 上方的 $L$-有理点 $w$：有界映射 $A\to\mathcal H(v)\to L$ 与 $L$ 的恒等映射诱导有界映射 $A\widehat\otimes_K L\to L$。由解析域的基变换，$V_L$ 是 $\mathbb A_L^{n,\an}$ 中的 affinoid 域。Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalpointdomaininterior} 表明 $V_L$ 在该仿射空间中是 $w$ 的普通邻域。因此}
\[
 \mathcal O_{V_L,w}=\mathcal O_{\mathbb A_L^{n,\an},w},
 \qquad
 \widehat{\mathcal O_{V_L,w}}\simeq L[[T_1,\ldots,T_n]].
\]
\bi{The completion formula is Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalanalyticformalpresentation}, with the coordinates translated by their values at $w$. The convergent-germ theorem makes the local ring Noetherian. Its completion is regular by \cite[Tag 032C]{StacksAlg}; the regularity/completion criterion \cite[Tag 07NY]{StacksAlg} therefore makes $\mathcal O_{V_L,w}$ regular. Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat} makes $\mathcal O_{V,v}\to\mathcal O_{V_L,w}$ a faithfully flat local homomorphism. Both rings are Noetherian by Theorem~\ref{fnd:ordinaryanalyticstalknoetherian}, so regularity descends by \cite[Tag 00OF]{StacksAlg}.}
{完备化公式来自 Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationalanalyticformalpresentation}，其中将坐标平移其在 $w$ 的取值。收敛芽定理给出局部环的 Noetherian 性。由 \cite[Tag 032C]{StacksAlg}，其完备化正则；再由正则性与完备化的判据 \cite[Tag 07NY]{StacksAlg}，得到 $\mathcal O_{V_L,w}$ 正则。Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat} 说明 $\mathcal O_{V,v}\to\mathcal O_{V_L,w}$ 是忠实平坦局部同态。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:ordinaryanalyticstalknoetherian}，两个环都 Noetherian，所以可用 \cite[Tag 00OF]{StacksAlg} 下降正则性。}

\bi{To pass from ordinary local rings to $A$, take a maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ of $A$. It is closed by the finite-module theorem; the nonzero affinoid quotient $A/\mathfrak m$ has a point by Theorem~\ref{fnd:nonempty}. Its point kernel is zero, since this quotient is a field. It therefore gives $v\in V$ with point kernel exactly $\mathfrak m$. The map $A\to\mathcal O_{V,v}$ is flat: the latter is the filtered colimit of the rational-neighborhood algebras of Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks}, each of which is flat over $A$ by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness}; the finite-ideal criterion commutes with this filtered colimit. Evaluation now gives a local flat map $A_{\mathfrak m}\to\mathcal O_{V,v}$. Hence $A_{\mathfrak m}$ is regular by the same descent lemma. Every $A_{\mathfrak p}$ is a localization of some such $A_{\mathfrak m}$ and is regular by \cite[Tag 0AFS]{StacksAlg}. Thus $A$ is regular.}
{为从通常局部环推广到 $A$，取 $A$ 的极大理想 $\mathfrak m$。由有限模定理它是闭的；非零 affinoid 商 $A/\mathfrak m$ 由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:nonempty} 有点。由于此商为域，该点的核为零，从而给出 $V$ 中点核恰为 $\mathfrak m$ 的点 $v$。映射 $A\to\mathcal O_{V,v}$ 平坦：由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks}，后者是 rational 邻域代数的滤过直极限；每个邻域代数由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness} 在 $A$ 上平坦，而有限理想判据与此滤过直极限相容。取值于是给出平坦局部映射 $A_{\mathfrak m}\to\mathcal O_{V,v}$；再次由正则性下降，$A_{\mathfrak m}$ 正则。每个 $A_{\mathfrak p}$ 都是某个这样的 $A_{\mathfrak m}$ 的局部化，由 \cite[Tag 0AFS]{StacksAlg} 仍正则。因此 $A$ 正则。}

\bi{Finally, affinoid domain base change identifies $V_F$ with an affinoid domain of $\mathbb A_F^{n,\an}$ for every complete $F/K$. Apply the argument over $F$.}
{最后，对任意完备扩域 $F/K$，affinoid 域的基变换将 $V_F$ 识别为 $\mathbb A_F^{n,\an}$ 中的 affinoid 域。在 $F$ 上应用上述论证即可。}
\end{proof}
