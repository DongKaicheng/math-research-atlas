Radii in the divisible closure of the value group

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 219

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Radii in the divisible closure of the value group}{值群可除闭包中的半径}]\label{fnd:radicalradiusaffinoid}
\leavevmode\par
\bi{Let $A$ be strict norm-admissible and let $r_1,\ldots,r_n\in\Gamma_K^{\mathrm{div}}$. Then the weighted coefficient algebra $D=A\{r^{-1}T\}$ is strict norm-admissible. Every algebraic ideal of $D$ is finite and closed, and its actual quotient is strict, with zero allowed. Adjoining another finite block with radii in $\Gamma_K^{\mathrm{div}}$ commutes isometrically with taking such a quotient: for $B=D/I$ and $F=D\{s^{-1}Y\}$,}
{设 $A$ 为 strict norm-admissible，且 $r_1,\ldots,r_n\in\Gamma_K^{\mathrm{div}}$。则加权系数代数 $D=A\{r^{-1}T\}$ 为 strict norm-admissible。$D$ 的每个代数理想均有限生成且闭，其实际商为 strict，允许零商。添入另一组半径属于 $\Gamma_K^{\mathrm{div}}$ 的有限变量，与取上述商等距相容：若 $B=D/I$、$F=D\{s^{-1}Y\}$，则}
\[
 F/IF\simeq B\{s^{-1}Y\}
\]
\bi{isometrically, with actual kernel $IF$. A further finite relation quotient is the direct quotient by the sum of the lifted actual ideals, with the same norm.}
{为等距同构，实际核为 $IF$。再施加有限多个关系，等于直接按提升后的实际理想之和取商，且范数相同。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For each $i$, choose an integer $n_i\geq1$ and $c_i\in K^\times$ with $|c_i|=r_i^{n_i}$. Write $S_i=c_i^{-1}T_i^{n_i}$ and put $R=A\langle S_1,\ldots,S_n\rangle$. Grouping exponents modulo the $n_i$ gives a unique expansion}
{对每个 $i$，选择整数 $n_i\geq1$ 及 $c_i\in K^\times$，使 $|c_i|=r_i^{n_i}$。写 $S_i=c_i^{-1}T_i^{n_i}$，并令 $R=A\langle S_1,\ldots,S_n\rangle$。按指数模 $n_i$ 分组，得到唯一展开}
\[
 D=\bigoplus_{0\leq\beta_i<n_i}R\,T^\beta,
 \qquad
 \left\|\sum_\beta F_\beta(S)T^\beta\right\|_r
     =\max_\beta r^\beta\|F_\beta\|.
\]
\bi{Indeed, the coefficient of $S^\nu T^\beta$ is $a_{n\nu+\beta}c^\nu$, whose weighted norm is $\|a_{n\nu+\beta}\|r^{n\nu+\beta}$. There are only finitely many $\beta$, so the finite-tail conditions on both sides are equivalent. Multiplication is determined by $T_i^{n_i}=c_iS_i$ and agrees on polynomials, hence by continuity on the coefficient algebras. Thus $D$ is a finite free $R$-algebra. Its displayed norm is uniformly equivalent to the maximum norm in this finite basis. Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictweightedquotient} makes $R$ strict, and Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebras} makes $D$ strict with precisely this norm-admissibility. No assertion about arbitrary positive radii follows from this finite regrouping.}
{具体地说，$S^\nu T^\beta$ 的系数为 $a_{n\nu+\beta}c^\nu$，其加权范数为 $\|a_{n\nu+\beta}\|r^{n\nu+\beta}$。由于 $\beta$ 只有有限多个，两边的有限尾条件等价。乘法由 $T_i^{n_i}=c_iS_i$ 确定，在多项式上相同，再由连续性在系数代数上相同。因此 $D$ 为有限自由 $R$-代数，其所列范数与这一有限基下的最大范数一致等价。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictweightedquotient}，$R$ 为 strict；再由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebras}，$D$ 为 strict，且恰好具有上述 norm-admissibility。这个有限分组论证不对任意正半径作出结论。}

\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules} now gives finite generation and closedness of every ideal of $D$. Its quotient is complete by Lemma~\ref{fnd:completequotient}; composing bounded quotient lifts with a strict Tate presentation of $D$ proves that the quotient norm is admissible. This also covers the zero quotient.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}，$D$ 的每个理想均有限生成且闭。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:completequotient}，其商完备；将有界商提升与 $D$ 的 strict Tate 表示的提升复合，即证明商范数 admissible。这也包括零商。}

\bi{For the iterated assertion, regrouping coefficients first identifies the joint coefficient algebra with $F=D\{s^{-1}Y\}$, with equality of norms. Proposition~\ref{fnd:weightedc0exact}, applied to $D\to D/I$, gives a surjective coefficient map with quotient norm equal to the coefficient norm on $B\{s^{-1}Y\}$. Its kernel consists of the convergent coefficient families in $I$. Choose generators $u_1,\ldots,u_q$ of $I$. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}, every $i\in I$ can be written $i=\sum_\ell u_\ell d_\ell$ with $\max_\ell\|d_\ell\|\leq C\|i\|$ for a fixed $C$. Applying these lifts coefficientwise proves that the kernel is the actual ideal $IF$. This repeats the coefficient-lifting argument of Lemma~\ref{fnd:iteratedstrictquotients}, whose needed strictness is now supplied by the preceding paragraph. Finally the inverse image of a finitely generated relation ideal is $IF$ plus its lifted generators: lift the finitely many coefficients of any relation expression and subtract. Taking successive infima over representatives gives the direct quotient norm. No infimum need be attained.}
{对迭代断言，先重排系数，将联合系数代数与 $F=D\{s^{-1}Y\}$ 等距识别。对 $D\to D/I$ 应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:weightedc0exact}，得到满的逐系数映射，其商范数等于 $B\{s^{-1}Y\}$ 上的系数范数。其核由系数属于 $I$ 的收敛族组成。选择 $I$ 的生成元 $u_1,\ldots,u_q$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}，存在固定常数 $C$，使每个 $i\in I$ 均可写成 $i=\sum_\ell u_\ell d_\ell$，且 $\max_\ell\|d_\ell\|\leq C\|i\|$。逐系数应用这一提升，证明核恰为实际理想 $IF$。这重现了 Lemma~\ref{fnd:iteratedstrictquotients} 的逐系数提升论证，所需 strictness 现已由前段提供。最后，有限生成的关系理想的逆像等于 $IF$ 加上其生成元的提升：对任意关系表达式提升有限多个系数，再相减即可。逐次对代表元取下确界，得到直接商范数，无须下确界取到。}
\end{proof}
