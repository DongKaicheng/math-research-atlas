Finite module data on an analytic cover

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1036

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[Finite module data on an analytic cover]
\label{v037:integration:coherentcoverdata}
Let $(V_i)$ be a finite affinoid-domain cover of a good
Hausdorff analytic space $X$. Put $A_i=\mathcal O(V_i)$
and $A_{ij}=\mathcal O(V_i\cap V_j)$.
Finite $A_i$-modules $M_i$, with isomorphisms
\[
       A_{ij}\otimes_{A_i}M_i
                 \simeq A_{ij}\otimes_{A_j}M_j
\]
satisfying the identity and cocycle conditions, determine
a coherent $\mathcal O_{X_G}$-module $\mathcal F$, uniquely
up to the isomorphism inducing the given identifications.
Conversely every coherent module gives such data.
The construction is compatible with morphisms, finite
affinoid refinements and short exact sequences.
For every affinoid domain $W\subseteq V_i$,
\[
             \Gamma(W,\mathcal F)
                   =\mathcal O(W)\otimes_{A_i}M_i
\]
with its canonical finite-module norm.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
All finite intersections are affinoid by
Lemma~\ref{v037:integration:separatedintersections}.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb} associates to $M_i$
a coherent sheaf $\widetilde M_i$ on $(V_i)_G$ and
identifies its restrictions with the displayed finite
tensors. It turns the given module isomorphisms into
sheaf isomorphisms on the intersections, with the same
cocycle condition.

For any analytic domain $T\subseteq X$, define
$\mathcal F(T)$ to be the families of sections of
$\widetilde M_i$ on $T\cap V_i$ whose restrictions agree
under these isomorphisms. The domain and covering
calculus of Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalnetdomaincalculus}
shows that an admissible cover of $T$ restricts to one
of each $T\cap V_i$. Existence and uniqueness of glued
sections for the sheaves $\widetilde M_i$ therefore
prove the sheaf axiom for these families.
On a domain contained in $V_i$, its $i$-th section
determines all the others by restriction and the
transition isomorphisms. The cocycle condition makes
them compatible. Thus $\mathcal F|_{(V_i)_G}$ is
$\widetilde M_i$; in particular it is coherent. Indeed,
finite generation and finite generation of the kernels
of maps from finite free sheaves are both checked on
this admissible cover, where $\widetilde M_i$ is coherent.
The same description proves uniqueness and glues
compatible module morphisms.

Conversely, sections of a coherent sheaf on each $V_i$
are finite modules by Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb}.
That theorem gives both their restriction isomorphisms
and the displayed formula on $W$.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules} gives the
canonical norms and boundedness of all these module
maps, while Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor}
identifies the finite ordinary and completed tensors.
The two constructions are inverse on every chart and
hence inverse globally by the sheaf axiom.
Refinements simply restrict these same sheaves.
Finally exactness is local on the cover, and the
affinoid equivalence in
Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb} is exact.
This proves the assertion for short exact sequences.
\end{proof}
