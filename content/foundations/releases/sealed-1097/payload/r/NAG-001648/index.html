Domain locality and base change of quasi-smoothness

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 398

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Domain locality and base change of quasi-smoothness}{Quasi-smoothness 的域局部性与基变换}]
\label{fnd:jacobiansmoothdomainbasechange}
\bi{Use the Jacobian-presentation definition of quasi-smoothness in Section~\ref{fnd:flatness}. Let $f:Y\to X$, let $y\in Y$, let $D$ be an analytic domain of $Y$ containing $y$, and let $U$ be an analytic domain of $X$ containing $f(D)$. Then $f$ is quasi-smooth at $y$ if and only if $D\to U$ is quasi-smooth there. Quasi-smoothness is preserved by every analytic base change, including complete ground-field extension. Analytic-domain inclusions are quasi-smooth and have zero relative differential sheaf.}
{采用 Section~\ref{fnd:flatness} 中以 Jacobian 表示给出的 quasi-smoothness 定义。设 $f:Y\to X$、$y\in Y$，$D$ 是包含 $y$ 的 $Y$ 的解析域，$U$ 是包含 $f(D)$ 的 $X$ 的解析域。则 $f$ 在 $y$ 处 quasi-smooth，当且仅当 $D\to U$ 在该点 quasi-smooth。Quasi-smoothness 在任意解析基变换下保持，包括完备基域扩张。解析域包含为 quasi-smooth，且其相对微分层为零。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Start with a Jacobian presentation $W\hookrightarrow V\subseteq\mathbb A_X^{n,\mathrm{an}}$ at $y$, with $m$ equations. To restrict the target, choose an affinoid domain $V_0$ in $V\cap\mathbb A_U^{n,\mathrm{an}}$ containing the image of $y$. Its inverse image $W_0$ is affinoid and closed in $V_0$, with the restricted $m$ equations. The differential comparison of Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialdomains} identifies the relevant fibre ranks, so the same minor is nonzero at $y$.}
{先取 $y$ 处的 Jacobian 表示 $W\hookrightarrow V\subseteq\mathbb A_X^{n,\mathrm{an}}$，其中有 $m$ 个方程。为限制目标，在 $V\cap\mathbb A_U^{n,\mathrm{an}}$ 中选取包含 $y$ 的像的 affinoid 域 $V_0$。其逆像 $W_0$ 为 affinoid，且以限制后的 $m$ 个方程闭嵌入 $V_0$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialdomains} 的微分比较识别对应纤维的秩，因此同一个子式在 $y$ 处非零。}

\bi{To restrict the source, choose an affinoid domain $E\subseteq D\cap W_0$ containing $y$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert} gives a finite cover of $E$ by domains rational in $W_0$; choose a member $E_0$ containing $y$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalliftclosedimmersion} lifts $E_0$ to a rational domain $V_1\subseteq V_0$ with $E_0=W_0\times_{V_0}V_1$. Thus $E_0\hookrightarrow V_1\subseteq\mathbb A_U^{n,\mathrm{an}}$ has the same equations and the same nonzero minor at $y$, and is the required presentation. Conversely a Jacobian presentation for $D\to U$ is also one for $Y\to X$: domain inclusions are transitive, $\mathbb A_U^n$ is a domain of $\mathbb A_X^n$, and the differential fibres agree.}
{为限制源，在 $D\cap W_0$ 内选取包含 $y$ 的 affinoid 域 $E$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert} 给出 $E$ 的有限覆盖，其成员在 $W_0$ 中为 rational；选取其中包含 $y$ 的成员 $E_0$。Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalliftclosedimmersion} 将 $E_0$ 提升为 rational 域 $V_1\subseteq V_0$，满足 $E_0=W_0\times_{V_0}V_1$。因此 $E_0\hookrightarrow V_1\subseteq\mathbb A_U^{n,\mathrm{an}}$ 具有相同方程，且同一子式在 $y$ 处非零，给出所求表示。反之，$D\to U$ 的 Jacobian 表示也给出 $Y\to X$ 的表示：域包含具有传递性，$\mathbb A_U^n$ 为 $\mathbb A_X^n$ 的域，而微分纤维彼此识别。}

\bi{For base change by $X'\to X$, let $y'\in Y\times_XX'$ lie over $y$. Pull back $W\hookrightarrow V$ and take an affinoid domain $V'$ in $V\times_XX'$ containing the image of $y'$. The inverse image $W'$ is an affinoid domain of $Y\times_XX'$ closed in $V'$, and $V'$ is a domain of $\mathbb A_{X'}^n$. The $m$ pulled-back equations generate its ideal by the completed-pushout calculation. Proposition~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialbasechange} identifies the differential fibres by extension from $\mathcal H(y)$ to $\mathcal H(y')$; the nonzero minor stays nonzero under this isometric field embedding. This proves base-change stability. The argument works verbatim over any complete extension. Finally an analytic-domain inclusion has locally the presentation $W=W\subseteq\mathbb A_X^0=X$, with no equations, and its differential sheaf vanishes by Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialdomains}.}
{对基变换 $X'\to X$，取位于 $y$ 上方的 $y'\in Y\times_XX'$。拉回 $W\hookrightarrow V$，并在 $V\times_XX'$ 内选取包含 $y'$ 的像的 affinoid 域 $V'$。逆像 $W'$ 是 $Y\times_XX'$ 的 affinoid 域，闭嵌入 $V'$，而 $V'$ 是 $\mathbb A_{X'}^n$ 的域。由完备 pushout 计算，拉回后的 $m$ 个方程生成其理想。Proposition~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialbasechange} 通过从 $\mathcal H(y)$ 到 $\mathcal H(y')$ 扩标量识别微分纤维；非零子式在此等距域嵌入下仍非零。这证明基变换稳定性，同样论证适用于任意完备扩域。最后，解析域包含局部具有无方程表示 $W=W\subseteq\mathbb A_X^0=X$，且由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialdomains}，其相对微分层为零。}
\end{proof}
