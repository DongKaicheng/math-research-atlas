\paragraph{Interior on the actual charts.}
For an affinoid domain \(X\) of the good source \(\Omega\), the formulas used below are
\[
 \operatorname{Int}(\Omega/T)=\operatorname{Int}(\Omega/K),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Int}(X/K)
 =X\cap\operatorname{Int}(\Omega/K)\cap\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(X,\Omega).
 \tag{1}\label{wfi:interiorcalculus}
\]
For the first formula take a Laurent affinoid neighbourhood of the target point
with all bounds strict at that point, and an ordinary affinoid neighbourhood of
the source point mapping into it. The target point is absolutely interior by
this presentation. The
\hyperref[fnd:nag001626:source-factor-interior]{source-factor inclusion}
and \hyperref[fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus]{forward composition}
give the equivalence on these two affinoids.
The \hyperref[fnd:goodinteriorlocaltesting]{ordinary local test} then gives
the asserted equivalence on the good spaces.

For the second formula, let \(y\in X\), choose an ordinary affinoid neighbourhood
\(W\) of \(y\) in \(\Omega\), and choose an affinoid neighbourhood \(V\) of \(y\)
in \(X\) contained in the ordinary interior of \(W\). Both domain presentations
use the induced analytic structure, so \(V\) is an affinoid domain of \(W\).
Absolute interiority in \(X\) is equivalent to absolute interiority in \(V\) by
the ordinary local test. The source-factor inclusion for \(V\to W\to\mathcal M(K)\)
then gives relative interiority in \(V/W\). The
\hyperref[fnd:affinoiddomainordinaryinterior]{affinoid domain criterion}
makes \(V\) an ordinary neighbourhood at \(y\) in \(W\). Thus \(y\) is
ordinary interior in \(X\), and the local test on \(W\) and \(\Omega\) gives
absolute interiority in \(\Omega\). Conversely, if \(y\) is ordinary interior
in \(X\) and absolutely interior in \(\Omega\), take one ordinary affinoid
neighbourhood of \(y\) in \(\Omega\) contained in \(X\) and use the local test
on both spaces.
