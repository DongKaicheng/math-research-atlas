Units on an open polyannulus

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 908

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Units on an open polyannulus}
{开多圆环上的单位元}]
\label{cand:openpolyannulusunits}
\bi{Let $L$ be a complete non-Archimedean field and let
$\Omega\subset\mathbf R^d$ be a nonempty convex open set.
Let $P_\Omega\subset(\mathbf G_{m,L}^d)^{\an}$ be the
analytic domain defined by $v(Z)\in\Omega$, and denote its
Gauss section by $w\mapsto z_w$. For every analytic unit
$g\in\mathcal O(P_\Omega)^\times$ there are
$c\in v(L^\times)$ and $m\in\mathbf Z^d$ such that}
{设 $L$ 为完备非阿基米德域，$\Omega\subset\mathbf R^d$
为非空凸开集。以 $P_\Omega\subset(\mathbf G_{m,L}^d)^{\an}$
记由 $v(Z)\in\Omega$ 定义的解析域，并以 $w\mapsto z_w$
记其 Gauss 截面。对每个解析单位元
$g\in\mathcal O(P_\Omega)^\times$，存在
$c\in v(L^\times)$ 及 $m\in\mathbf Z^d$，使}
\[
                    v(g(z_w))=c+\langle m,w\rangle
                              \qquad(w\in\Omega).
\]
\bi{The assertion includes $d=0$, when $P_\Omega=\mathcal M(L)$.}
{此结论包括 $d=0$，此时 $P_\Omega=\mathcal M(L)$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Suppose first that $d>0$. On the inverse image of a
closed rectangular box $W=\prod_j[\alpha_j,\beta_j]$
contained in $\Omega$, the arbitrary-radius domain
presentations of Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal}
give the product of closed annulus algebras; their actual
analytic sections are these algebras by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidfullgcomparison}. Iterate
Theorem~\ref{fnd:laurentcoefficientnormalform}, with the
other coordinates as Banach coefficients. This gives a
unique Laurent expansion $\sum_{m\in\mathbf Z^d}c_mZ^m$,
and the norm is}
{先设 $d>0$。对于包含于 $\Omega$ 的闭长方体
$W=\prod_j[\alpha_j,\beta_j]$，由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal} 的任意半径
域表示，其逆像具有各闭圆环代数的乘积表示；由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidfullgcomparison}，其实际解析截面
就是这些代数。反复应用
Theorem~\ref{fnd:laurentcoefficientnormalform}，每次把其余
坐标作为 Banach 系数，得到唯一 Laurent 展开
$\sum_{m\in\mathbf Z^d}c_mZ^m$，范数为}
\[
 \max_m |c_m|\max_{w\in W}\exp(-\langle m,w\rangle).
\]
\bi{The weighted coefficients tend to zero outside finite
subsets. To check the multivariable assertion directly,
apply the one-coordinate normal-form operators successively
to polynomial families. Their bounds and commuting actions
on different exponent pairs extend them to the complete
weighted coefficient space. Their composite kernel is the
sum of the coordinate relations, and their common section
is the Laurent monomial representative. The finite-tail
condition is exactly the weighted $c_0$ condition of
Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness}. Thus this
iteration also proves uniqueness and all restriction formulas.}
{加权系数在有限子集之外趋于零。为直接核对多变量断言，
先对多项式族逐次应用单坐标的正规形算子。它们的范数界，
以及不同指数对上的作用可交换这一事实，将它们延拓到完备
加权系数空间。复合算子的核为各坐标关系之和，其公共截面
为 Laurent 单项式代表元。有限尾条件恰为
Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness} 中的加权
$c_0$ 条件。因此此次迭代同时证明唯一性及全部限制公式。}

\bi{Use boxes with nonempty interior. On overlapping
interiors, uniqueness makes their coefficients equal.
Every two such boxes can be connected by a finite chain
of overlapping smaller boxes inside the convex set
$\Omega$. Hence a function on $P_\Omega$ has a single
Laurent coefficient family, converging on every compact
box, and}
{使用具有非空内部的长方体。在相交的内部上，唯一性使其
系数相同。在凸集 $\Omega$ 内，任意两个这样的长方体都可由
有限条内部相交的较小长方体链连接。因此 $P_\Omega$ 上的
函数具有一个统一的 Laurent 系数族，在每个紧长方体上
收敛，且}
\[
       \log|g(z_w)|=\max_m\{\log|c_m|-\langle m,w\rangle\}.
\]
\bi{This is convex in $w$. Apply the same argument to
$g^{-1}$. Multiplicativity of the Gauss seminorm says that
its logarithm is the negative of the displayed function.
A function both convex and concave on an open convex set
is affine: equality in the two convexity inequalities
gives its affine interpolation on every line segment.
At an interior point $w_0$, a nonzero coefficient $c_m$
attains the maximum. The difference between the affine
function and this one affine term is nonnegative on
$\Omega$ and zero at $w_0$; an affine function with these
properties is zero, by varying a small distance in both
directions along every vector. Thus
$v(g(z_w))=v(c_m)+\langle m,w\rangle$. If $d=0$, the
unit is an element of $L^\times$, which proves the same
claim.}
{此函数关于 $w$ 凸。对 $g^{-1}$ 使用同一论证。
Gauss 半范数的乘法性说明其对数恰为上述函数的相反数。
在凸开集上同时凸且凹的函数为仿射函数：两个凸性不等式
迫使每条线段上的仿射插值取等号。在内部点 $w_0$，某个
非零系数 $c_m$ 达到最大值。该仿射函数与这一仿射项之差
在 $\Omega$ 上非负，并在 $w_0$ 为零；沿每个向量作正反
两个方向的小变动，即知此差恒为零。因此
$v(g(z_w))=v(c_m)+\langle m,w\rangle$。若 $d=0$，
单位元属于 $L^\times$，结论相同。}
\end{proof}
