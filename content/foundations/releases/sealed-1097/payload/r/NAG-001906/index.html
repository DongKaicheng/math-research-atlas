Power-bounded elements and spectral reduction

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 513

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Power-bounded elements and spectral reduction}
{幂有界元素与谱约化}]
\label{fnd:powerboundreductioninternal}
\bi{For every affinoid algebra $A$, power-bounded elements are exactly
those with spectral radius at most one; topologically nilpotent elements
are exactly those with spectral radius less than one. The ring
$A^\circ/A^{\circ\circ}$ is reduced.}
{对任意 affinoid 代数 $A$，幂有界元素恰为谱半径至多为一的元素；
拓扑幂零元素恰为谱半径小于一的元素。
环 $A^\circ/A^{\circ\circ}$ 约化。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The first equivalence is Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointwisepowerbound}
with $s=1$, together with Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedspectralradiusformula}.
If $\rho(a)<1$, choose $s$ strictly between $\rho(a)$ and one; the same
power bound gives $\|a^n\|\leq Cs^n\to0$. Conversely, if $a^n\to0$,
some power has norm less than one, whence $\rho(a)<1$ by the spectral
formula. The ultrametric and submultiplicative inequalities for $\rho$
show that the first set is a ring and the second an ideal in it.
Finally, $\widetilde a^n=0$ implies $\rho(a)^n<1$, hence $\rho(a)<1$
and $\widetilde a=0$.}
{第一个等价性来自 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointwisepowerbound}
在 $s=1$ 时的结论，以及 Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedspectralradiusformula}。
若 $\rho(a)<1$，在 $\rho(a)$ 与一之间选择 $s$；同一幂界给出
$\|a^n\|\leq Cs^n\to0$。反之，若 $a^n\to0$，则某次幂的范数
小于一，由谱半径公式得 $\rho(a)<1$。
$\rho$ 的超度量及次乘不等式说明第一集合为环，第二集合为其中的理想。
最后，$\widetilde a^n=0$ 蕴含 $\rho(a)^n<1$，从而 $\rho(a)<1$，
即 $\widetilde a=0$。}
\end{proof}
