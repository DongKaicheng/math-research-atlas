Finite projection from a strict fibre

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 438

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Finite projection from a strict fibre}{从 strict 纤维构造有限投影}]
\label{fnd:strictfibrefiniteprojection}
\bi{Let $Y=\mathcal M(B)\to X=\mathcal M(A)$ be an affinoid morphism, let $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$, and put $x=f(y)$ and $F=\mathcal H(x)$. Assume $C=B\widehat\otimes_A F$ is strict, and set $d=\dim C$. After replacing $X$ by an affinoid neighbourhood of $x$, there are $d$ analytic functions on $Y$ defining an $X$-morphism $g:Y\to\mathbb A_X^{d,\mathrm{an}}$ which restricts to a finite morphism from an ordinary neighbourhood of $y$ to an ordinary neighbourhood of $g(y)$. Consequently there are affinoid neighbourhoods $V$ of $y$ and $T$ of $g(y)$, with $T$ an affinoid domain of $\mathbb A_X^{d,\mathrm{an}}$, for which $V\to T$ is finite.}
{设 $Y=\mathcal M(B)\to X=\mathcal M(A)$ 是 affinoid 态射，$y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$，并令 $x=f(y)$、$F=\mathcal H(x)$。假设 $C=B\widehat\otimes_A F$ 为 strict，记 $d=\dim C$。将 $X$ 换为 $x$ 的某个 affinoid 邻域后，存在 $Y$ 上的 $d$ 个解析函数，定义 $X$-态射 $g:Y\to\mathbb A_X^{d,\mathrm{an}}$，且此态射在 $y$ 的某个普通邻域上，是到 $g(y)$ 的某个普通邻域的有限态射。因此存在 $y$ 与 $g(y)$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $V,T$，其中 $T$ 为 $\mathbb A_X^{d,\mathrm{an}}$ 的 affinoid 域，且 $V\to T$ 有限。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization} and Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttatedimension} provide a bounded finite injection $F\langle U_1,\ldots,U_d\rangle\hookrightarrow C$. Approximate its coordinate images, by Lemma~\ref{fnd:originalcoefficientfibredensity}, with images $v_i$ of functions on $Y_U=Y\times_X U$, where $U$ is an affinoid neighbourhood of $x$. Choose the errors of norm less than one. Lemma~\ref{fnd:smallnormalizationperturbation} makes the resulting homomorphism $F\langle U\rangle\to C$ finite. Replace $X,Y$ by $U,Y_U$; these replacements are ordinary neighbourhoods of the points under consideration.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization} 与 Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttatedimension} 给出有界有限单射 $F\langle U_1,\ldots,U_d\rangle\hookrightarrow C$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:originalcoefficientfibredensity}，可用 $Y_U=Y\times_X U$ 上函数的像 $v_i$ 逼近这些坐标的像，其中 $U$ 为 $x$ 的 affinoid 邻域，并使误差范数小于一。Lemma~\ref{fnd:smallnormalizationperturbation} 说明所得同态 $F\langle U\rangle\to C$ 有限。将 $X,Y$ 分别换成 $U,Y_U$；这些都是所研究点的普通邻域。}

\bi{Let $g$ be the morphism defined by the chosen functions. Its fibre over $g(y)$ is, near $y$, a fibre of the finite $F$-affinoid morphism just constructed, and is therefore a finite set. In particular $y$ is isolated in this fibre. To verify interior, choose an affinoid polydisc over $X$ containing $g(Y)$, with coordinate bounds strictly larger than the norms of the chosen functions. It gives a factorization $Y\to P\to X$. The affinoid composition formula, Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcompositionequality}, implies $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/P)$ because $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$. Since $g(y)$ is in the ordinary interior of $P$ in affine space, ordinary local testing gives $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/\mathbb A_X^{d,\mathrm{an}})$.}
{令 $g$ 为所选函数定义的态射。它在 $g(y)$ 上的纤维，在 $y$ 附近就是刚才构造的有限 $F$-affinoid 态射的一个纤维，故为有限集；特别地，$y$ 在该纤维中孤立。为验证内部条件，选择 $X$ 上包含 $g(Y)$ 的 affinoid 多圆盘 $P$，其坐标界严格大于所选函数的范数，得到分解 $Y\to P\to X$。由 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$ 及 affinoid 复合公式 Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcompositionequality}，得到 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/P)$。又因 $g(y)$ 位于 $P$ 在仿射空间中的普通内部，普通局部测试给出 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/\mathbb A_X^{d,\mathrm{an}})$。}

\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:isolatedinteriorlocalfinite} now produces ordinary open neighbourhoods on which $g$ is finite. Choose an affinoid neighbourhood $T$ of $g(y)$ inside the target open neighbourhood. Its inverse image $V$ is affinoid and finite over $T$, and contains an ordinary neighbourhood of $y$. This proves the final assertion.}
{现在 Theorem~\ref{fnd:isolatedinteriorlocalfinite} 给出普通开邻域，使 $g$ 在其上有限。在目标开邻域内选择 $g(y)$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $T$，其逆像 $V$ 为 affinoid，且在 $T$ 上有限，并包含 $y$ 的普通邻域。这就证明了最后的断言。}
\end{proof}
