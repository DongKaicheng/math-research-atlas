4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 631

Statement and full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}\label{fnd:annulusunitaffine}
\bi{For a nonzero analytic function $f=\sum a_mT^m$ on a closed annulus, its skeletal function is}
{对 closed annulus 上的 nonzero analytic function $f=\sum a_mT^m$，其 skeletal function 为}
\[
F(t)=-\log|f(\zeta_{0,e^{-t}})|=\min_m\{v(a_m)+mt\}.
\]
\bi{It is piecewise affine with integral slopes. If $f$ is an analytic unit on the annulus, $F$ is affine.}
{它为 piecewise affine，且 slopes 为 integral。若 $f$ 是 annulus 上的 analytic unit，则 $F$ 为 affine。}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\bi{The formula is the Gauss norm. Uniform convergence of the Laurent series and the positive minimum of its Gauss norm on the compact radius interval show that only finitely many terms can attain the minimum. Thus $F$ is the minimum of finitely many affine functions with integral slopes. In logarithmic radius $s=\log\rho$, the function $\log|f|$ is convex. If $f$ has an inverse, multiplicativity gives $\log|f^{-1}|=-\log|f|$, and this negative is convex as well. Therefore $\log|f|$, and hence $F$, is affine.}
{此公式就是 Gauss norm。Laurent series 的 uniform convergence，以及其 Gauss norm 在 compact radius interval 上有正 minimum，说明只有有限项可能达到 minimum。因此 $F$ 是有限个具有 integral slopes 的 affine functions 的 minimum。以 logarithmic radius $s=\log\rho$ 为变量时，$\log|f|$ 为 convex。若 $f$ 有 inverse，则 multiplicativity 给出 $\log|f^{-1}|=-\log|f|$，后者也 convex。因此 $\log|f|$ 及 $F$ 都 affine。}
\end{proof}
