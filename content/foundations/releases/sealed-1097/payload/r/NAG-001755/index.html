Naive flatness for a finite morphism

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 323

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Naive flatness for a finite morphism}{有限态射的朴素平坦性}]
\label{fnd:finitecoherentnaiveanalytic}
\bi{For a finite morphism $Y\to X$ of good spaces and a coherent sheaf $\mathcal F$ on $Y_G$, naive $X$-flatness at a point $y$ is equivalent to analytic $X$-flatness there.}
{对 good 空间之间的有限态射 $Y\to X$ 及 $Y_G$ 上的凝聚层 $\mathcal F$，某点 $y$ 处朴素 $X$-平坦等价于该点处解析 $X$-平坦。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The analytic assertion always implies the naive one. For the converse, separate the finite branches over $x=f(y)$ by Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation}, and retain the branch containing $y$. It is an ordinary neighborhood of $y$, finite over an ordinary neighborhood $U$ of $x$, and its fibre over $x$ consists only of $y$. Denote its finite map by $\pi$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:finitepushforwardflatness} makes $(\pi_*\mathcal F)_x$ flat over $\mathcal O_{U,x}$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:coherentflatstalkfree} makes the direct image locally free near $x$, hence analytically $U$-flat. The analytic equivalence in Theorem~\ref{fnd:finitepushforwardflatness} gives analytic flatness at $y$. Ordinary open restrictions do not change any of the pointwise tests, so this is the assertion for the original morphism.}
{解析平坦总蕴含朴素平坦。反向中，用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation} 分离 $x=f(y)$ 上方的有限个分支，只保留含 $y$ 的分支。它是 $y$ 的普通邻域，在 $x$ 的某个普通邻域 $U$ 上有限，并且其 $x$-纤维仅含 $y$；以 $\pi$ 记此有限映射。Theorem~\ref{fnd:finitepushforwardflatness} 使 $(\pi_*\mathcal F)_x$ 在 $\mathcal O_{U,x}$ 上平坦。Lemma~\ref{fnd:coherentflatstalkfree} 使此直接像在 $x$ 附近局部自由，故解析 $U$-平坦。再由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:finitepushforwardflatness} 的解析等价，得到 $y$ 处的解析平坦性。普通开限制不改变任何逐点测试，因此这就是原态射的结论。}
\end{proof}
