Cohomology comparison for proper schemes

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 302

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Cohomology comparison for proper schemes}
{适当概形的上同调比较}]
\label{fnd:propercoherentcomparison}
\bi{For every proper finite-type $K$-scheme $X$ and every
coherent $\mathcal F$ on $X$, the natural maps are isomorphisms}
{对每个适当的有限型 $K$-概形 $X$ 及其上每个凝聚层
$\mathcal F$，下列自然映射均为同构：}
\[
 H^q(X,\mathcal F)\ \longrightarrow\
 H^q(X^{\an}_G,\mathcal F^{\an})\qquad(q\ge0).
\]
\bi{The same comparison is a quasi-isomorphism for every
bounded complex with coherent cohomology sheaves.}
{对每个具有凝聚上同调层的有界复形，相应比较也是拟同构。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{We first record how to use a comparison already known
for the cohomology sheaves of a bounded complex $C$.
The canonical truncation triangles
\cite[Tag 08J5]{StacksAlg} express $C$ by finitely many
successive extensions of $\mathcal H^j(C)[-j]$.
Flatness of $\alpha_X^*$ preserves these triangles and
their cohomology sheaves. Applying derived global sections
and the long exact sequences shows, by induction on the
number of nonzero cohomology sheaves, that comparison for
each $\mathcal H^j(C)$ implies comparison for $C$.
The comparison maps here are the maps induced by regular
sections mapping to analytic sections, or equivalently the
adjunction maps for the flat morphism $\alpha_X$.
They are natural in distinguished triangles.}
{先说明怎样从有界复形 $C$ 的上同调层的比较推出 $C$ 的比较。
\cite[Tag 08J5]{StacksAlg} 中的典范截断三角把 $C$ 表示成
有限多个 $\mathcal H^j(C)[-j]$ 的逐次扩张。
$\alpha_X^*$ 的平坦性保持这些三角及其上同调层。
应用导出全局截面和相应长正合列，再对非零上同调层的个数归纳，
即知每个 $\mathcal H^j(C)$ 的比较蕴含 $C$ 的比较。
这里的比较映射来自正则截面到解析截面的映射，等价地来自
平坦态射 $\alpha_X$ 的伴随映射；它们与区别三角自然相容。}

\bi{Fix $X$ and perform Noetherian induction on closed
subsets $Z\subset|X|$. The induction assertion is the
comparison for every coherent module on $X$ supported in
$Z$. It is clear for the empty set. Let $\mathcal F$ have
support contained in $Z$. If its support is a proper closed
subset, the induction hypothesis applies. Otherwise let
$T\hookrightarrow X$ be defined by
$\operatorname{Ann}(\mathcal F)$; its underlying space
is $Z$. The module $\mathcal F$ is the closed direct image
of a coherent module $\mathcal F_T$ on $T$, and
Lemma~\ref{fnd:properanalyticfinitecovers} identifies the
two comparison problems.}
{固定 $X$，对闭子集 $Z\subset|X|$ 作 Noetherian 归纳。
归纳命题是：$X$ 上每个支撑包含在 $Z$ 内的凝聚模均满足比较。
空集情形显然。设 $\mathcal F$ 的支撑包含在 $Z$ 内。
若其支撑是 $Z$ 的真闭子集，直接应用归纳假设。
否则，令 $T\hookrightarrow X$ 由
$\operatorname{Ann}(\mathcal F)$ 定义，其底空间就是 $Z$。
$\mathcal F$ 是 $T$ 上某个凝聚模 $\mathcal F_T$ 的闭直接像；
Lemma~\ref{fnd:properanalyticfinitecovers} 将两处比较问题等同。}

\bi{Apply the algebraic Chow lemma
\cite[Tag 0200]{StacksAlg} to the proper scheme $T$.
It supplies a proper surjection $\pi:Y\to T$, an
isomorphism above a dense open $U\subset T$, and an
immersion $Y\to\mathbf P^n_K$. Since $Y$ is proper
over $K$, the immersion is closed: its map to
$\mathbf P^n_K$ is proper by \cite[Tag 01W6]{StacksAlg},
and a proper immersion is closed by its open--closed
factorization. Thus $Y$ is projective over $K$.
The graph of $\pi$ is closed because $T$ is separated;
combining it with $Y\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^n_K$
gives a closed immersion $Y\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^n_T$.
Consequently $\pi$ is also projective. Put
$\mathcal E=\pi^*\mathcal F_T$ and $D=R\pi_*\mathcal E$.
Its cohomology sheaves are coherent by
\cite[Tag 02O5]{StacksAlg} and vanish in sufficiently
large degrees, as the finite standard relative-projective
cover computes them.}
{对适当概形 $T$ 应用代数 Chow 引理
\cite[Tag 0200]{StacksAlg}，得到适当满射 $\pi:Y\to T$、
$T$ 的一个稠密开集 $U$ 上的同构，以及浸入
$Y\to\mathbf P^n_K$。由于 $Y$ 在 $K$ 上适当，
此浸入是闭的：由 \cite[Tag 01W6]{StacksAlg}，
它到 $\mathbf P^n_K$ 的映射适当，而适当浸入的开--闭分解
说明它为闭浸入。所以 $Y$ 在 $K$ 上射影。
$T$ 分离，故 $\pi$ 的图像闭；将其与
$Y\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^n_K$ 组合得到闭浸入
$Y\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^n_T$，所以 $\pi$ 也射影。
令 $\mathcal E=\pi^*\mathcal F_T$、$D=R\pi_*\mathcal E$。
由 \cite[Tag 02O5]{StacksAlg}，$D$ 的上同调层凝聚；
有限的标准相对射影覆盖计算它们，因此充分高次时为零。}

\bi{Take the cone $C$ in the natural triangle}
{取自然三角中的锥 $C$：}
\[
 \mathcal F_T\longrightarrow D\longrightarrow C
                 \longrightarrow\mathcal F_T[1].
\]
\bi{The first map is adjunction, followed by the map to
the derived direct image. On $U$ it is the identity,
because $\pi$ is an isomorphism there. Hence every
$\mathcal H^j(C)$ is coherent and supported in the
proper closed subset $T\setminus U$ of $Z$.
Closed direct image to $X$, the induction hypothesis,
and the first paragraph give comparison for $C$.}
{第一箭头是伴随映射与通向导出直接像的映射之复合。
在 $U$ 上，$\pi$ 是同构，因此该箭头是恒等映射。
于是每个 $\mathcal H^j(C)$ 都凝聚，且支撑在 $Z$ 的真闭子集
$T\setminus U$ 中。先闭直接像到 $X$，再用归纳假设与
第一段，即得 $C$ 的比较。}

\bi{For $D$, the derived composition of global sections
and direct image \cite[Tag 0D6E]{StacksAlg}, together
with Theorem~\ref{fnd:relativeprojectivecomparison},
identifies its comparison with}
{对 $D$，由导出全局截面与直接像的复合公式
\cite[Tag 0D6E]{StacksAlg} 及
Theorem~\ref{fnd:relativeprojectivecomparison}，
其比较被识别为}
\[
 R\Gamma(Y,\mathcal E)\longrightarrow
 R\Gamma(Y^{\an}_G,\mathcal E^{\an}).
\]
\bi{This is a quasi-isomorphism by
Corollary~\ref{fnd:projectiveschemegaga}. Compatibility
with adjunction in the relative comparison identifies
the maps in the two triangles, not only their objects.
The long exact cohomology sequences of these triangles
therefore give comparison for $\mathcal F_T$ and hence
for $\mathcal F$. Noetherian induction proves the assertion
for all coherent sheaves. The first paragraph then proves
the assertion for every bounded coherent complex.}
{由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:projectiveschemegaga}，这是拟同构。
相对比较与伴随映射的相容性同时识别两个三角中的箭头，
而不只是对象。因此，两个三角的上同调长正合列给出
$\mathcal F_T$、进而给出 $\mathcal F$ 的比较。
Noetherian 归纳证明了全部凝聚层的断言，再由第一段
推出每个有界凝聚复形的断言。}
\end{proof}
