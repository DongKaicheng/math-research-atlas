Finite Galois descent of the model skeleton

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 683

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Finite Galois descent of the model skeleton}
{模型骨架的有限 Galois 下降}]
\label{cand:galoissplitmodelskeleton}
\bi{Let $L/K$ be a finite Galois extension of complete
nontrivially valued fields, using the extension of the fixed
absolute value on $K$, and let $G=\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$.
Suppose that a curve model $\mathcal Y$ over $L^\circ$
satisfies Theorem~\ref{cand:splitmodelskeleton}, its formal
generic fibre is identified with $X_L$, and the natural
semilinear $G$-action on $X_L$ extends to $\mathcal Y$.
Then the model homotopy descends to a jointly continuous
strong deformation retraction of $X$ onto the embedded
finite graph $\Sigma(\mathcal Y)/G$.}
{设 $L/K$ 为完备非平凡赋值域的有限 Galois 扩张，使用
$K$ 上固定绝对值的延伸，并令
$G=\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$。假设 $L^\circ$ 上的曲线
模型 $\mathcal Y$ 满足 Theorem~\ref{cand:splitmodelskeleton}，
其形式泛纤维已识别为 $X_L$，且 $X_L$ 上自然的半线性
$G$-作用延伸到 $\mathcal Y$。则模型同伦下降为 $X$ 到
嵌入有限图 $\Sigma(\mathcal Y)/G$ 的联合连续强形变收缩。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The model homotopy is $G$-equivariant by the final assertion
of Theorem~\ref{cand:splitmodelskeleton}. Theorem
\ref{cand:galoisanalyticquotient} identifies $X_L/G$ with $X$
and the quotient of its invariant skeleton with the skeleton's
image in $X$. Corollary~\ref{cand:galoisretractiondescent}
then descends the entire homotopy and all strong retraction
identities.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{cand:splitmodelskeleton} 的最后断言，
模型同伦 $G$-等变。Theorem~\ref{cand:galoisanalyticquotient}
将 $X_L/G$ 识别为 $X$，并将不变骨架的商识别为骨架在
$X$ 中的像。Corollary~\ref{cand:galoisretractiondescent}
于是使整个同伦及全部强收缩恒等式下降。}

\bi{For completeness, this quotient is a finite graph. The
$G$-action permutes vertices and edges; on an edge it is an
isometry by annulus-coordinate invariance, hence, after
identifying its interval, is either orientation-preserving
or a reflection. Subdivide every edge at its midpoint.
An element stabilizing a resulting edge cannot reverse it,
because original vertices and new midpoints are distinct
$G$-invariant classes. The quotient is therefore the finite
graph whose vertices and edges are the corresponding
orbits. Its edge lengths are the lengths of these
subdivided intervals. Thus an inverted original edge has
an image of half its length, whereas an edge with no
inversion retains its total length after the unnecessary
subdivision is removed. No field-degree rescaling is
introduced.}
{最后说明此商确为有限图。$G$-作用置换顶点与边；由圆环
坐标无关性，它在每条边上是等距映射，故在相应区间坐标
下或者保持定向，或者为反射。将每条边在中点细分。稳定
某条细分边的元素不能反转它，因为原顶点与新增中点是
两个不同的 $G$-不变类别。因此商图的顶点与边分别是
相应的轨道，仍为有限图。其边长取相应细分区间的长度。
因此，被反转的原边的像长为原长的一半；无反转的原边
在去掉非必要细分后保留总长度。这里不引入域次数缩放。}
\end{proof}
