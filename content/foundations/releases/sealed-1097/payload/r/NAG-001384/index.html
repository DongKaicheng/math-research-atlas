Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Gerritzen--Grauert input}{Gerritzen--Grauert input}]\label{fnd:gerritzengrauert}
\bi{Every affinoid domain $V$ of a generalized $K$-affinoid space $X$ is a finite union of rational domains of $X$. The field may be trivially valued.}
{Generalized $K$-affinoid space $X$ 的每个 affinoid domain $V$ 都是 $X$ 中有限个 rational domains 的并。允许基域赋值平凡。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert} supplies a finite covering by rational domains defined over the original algebra $A$. Its proof allows arbitrary positive radii and both trivial and nontrivial absolute values. The domain convention in the statement agrees with that theorem by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainextensiontests}.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert} 给出由原代数 $A$ 上的 rational 域组成的有限覆盖。其证明允许任意正半径以及平凡或非平凡绝对值。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainextensiontests}，本陈述的域约定与该定理一致。}
\end{proof}
