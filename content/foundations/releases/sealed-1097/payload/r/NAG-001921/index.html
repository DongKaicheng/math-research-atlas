The charts of an admissible formal blowup

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 521

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The charts of an admissible formal blowup}
{Admissible 形式 blowup 的图册}]
\label{fnd:formalblowupcharts}
\bi{Let $A_0$ be an admissible $O$-algebra and let
$I=(f_0,\ldots,f_m)\subset A_0$ contain $\pi^N$ for some $N$.
For each $i$, set}
{设 $A_0$ 为 admissible $O$-代数，理想
$I=(f_0,\ldots,f_m)\subset A_0$ 包含某个 $\pi^N$。
对每个 $i$，令}
\[
 C_i=A_0[U_j:j\ne i]/(f_iU_j-f_j:j\ne i),\qquad
 B_i=\widehat{C_i/(\pi\text{-torsion})}.
\]
\bi{The $B_i$ are admissible $O$-algebras and are the affine charts
of the $\pi$-adic completion of $\operatorname{Bl}_I(\operatorname{Spec}A_0)$.
The ideal $IB_i$ is generated by the nonzerodivisor $f_i$. Equivalently,}
{各 $B_i$ 均为 admissible $O$-代数，并构成
$\operatorname{Bl}_I(\operatorname{Spec}A_0)$ 的 $\pi$-adic
完备化的仿射图册。理想 $IB_i$ 由非零因子 $f_i$ 生成。
等价地，有}
\[
 B_i\simeq
 \left(A_0\langle U_j:j\ne i\rangle/(f_iU_j-f_j:j\ne i)\right)
                         /(\pi\text{-torsion}),
\]
\bi{and in the parenthesized ring its $\pi$-torsion equals its
$f_i$-torsion.}
{而括号中的环的 $\pi$-挠子模恰好等于其 $f_i$-挠子模。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Write $R_i=A_0[U_j:j\ne i]$ and let $L_i$ be the ideal of
the displayed relations. Evaluation $U_j\mapsto f_j/f_i$ maps
$R_i$ onto the subring $D_i=A_0[I/f_i]\subset A_0[1/f_i]$.
Its kernel is $L_i:f_i^\infty$: after localizing at $f_i$, the
relations eliminate all the variables, and clearing denominators
gives the assertion. Choose $a_j\in A_0$ with
$\pi^N=\sum_j a_jf_j$. In $C_i$ this becomes}
{记 $R_i=A_0[U_j:j\ne i]$，以 $L_i$ 记所列关系生成的理想。
求值 $U_j\mapsto f_j/f_i$ 将 $R_i$ 满射到子环
$D_i=A_0[I/f_i]\subset A_0[1/f_i]$。其核为
$L_i:f_i^\infty$：局部化 $f_i$ 后，关系消去全部变量，再清除
分母即可。选取 $a_j\in A_0$ 满足
$\pi^N=\sum_j a_jf_j$，在 $C_i$ 中这变为}
\[
 \pi^N=f_i\left(a_i+\sum_{j\ne i}a_jU_j\right).
 \tag{\(*\)}
\]
\bi{Consequently $f_i$-torsion in $C_i$ is $\pi$-torsion.
Conversely $D_i$, being a subring of $A_0[1/f_i]$, is
$\pi$-torsion-free, so all $\pi$-torsion in $C_i$ lies in that
kernel. Thus $D_i=C_i/(\pi\text{-torsion})$.
By Lemma~\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion}, $B_i=\widehat D_i$
is $\pi$-torsion-free and is a quotient of
$A_0\langle U_j:j\ne i\rangle$. It is topologically finite type,
and Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation} makes it
topologically finitely presented. Equation $(*)$ shows that $f_i$
is a nonzerodivisor on $B_i$ and that $IB_i=(f_i)$.}
{因此 $C_i$ 中的 $f_i$-挠也是 $\pi$-挠。反之，$D_i$ 是
$A_0[1/f_i]$ 的子环，故无 $\pi$-挠，所以 $C_i$ 中的全部
$\pi$-挠都落在该核中。于是
$D_i=C_i/(\pi\text{-torsion})$。由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion}，$B_i=\widehat D_i$
无 $\pi$-挠，且是 $A_0\langle U_j:j\ne i\rangle$ 的商。
它拓扑有限型，再由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:formaltorsionfreepresentation}
得到拓扑有限表示。等式 $(*)$ 说明 $f_i$ 是 $B_i$ 中的非零因子，
并且 $IB_i=(f_i)$。}

\bi{The degree-zero part of the localization of the Rees algebra
$\bigoplus_{d\geq0}I^dt^d$ at $f_it$ is precisely $D_i$:
its elements are $g/f_i^d$ with $g\in I^d$. Equality of two such
fractions in $A_0[1/f_i]$ is the equality obtained in the homogeneous
localization, because both are checked after multiplication by a
power of $f_i$. These standard affine opens cover the Proj of the
Rees algebra. Completing them gives the asserted blowup charts.}
{Rees 代数 $\bigoplus_{d\geq0}I^dt^d$ 在 $f_it$ 处局部化的
零次部分正是 $D_i$：其中元素为 $g/f_i^d$，$g\in I^d$。
这些分式在 $A_0[1/f_i]$ 中相等，当且仅当它们在齐次局部化中相等，
因为两种相等都由乘以某个 $f_i$ 的幂来检验。这些标准仿射开集覆盖
Rees 代数的 Proj；将它们完备化，就得到所述 blowup 图册。}

\bi{It remains to compare the two orders of completion and torsion
removal. Put $C_i'=A_0\langle U\rangle/L_iA_0\langle U\rangle$.
The polynomial $\pi$-torsion in $C_i$ maps into the
$\pi$-torsion $\tau$ of $C_i'$. The latter is finitely generated
by Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalmoduletopology}, hence killed by one
power of $\pi$; its induced topology is therefore discrete.
Its image in $B_i$ vanishes. Conversely the kernel of
$C_i'\to B_i$ is the closure of the image of the polynomial torsion,
by Lemma~\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion}. The submodule $\tau$ is
closed by Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalmoduletopology}, so this closure
is contained in $\tau$. The reverse inclusion holds because $B_i$
is $\pi$-torsion-free. Thus this kernel equals $\tau$.
Its dense polynomial-torsion submodule equals $\tau$ because
$\tau$ is discrete. Every element of $\tau$ is consequently
killed by a power of $f_i$, as is already true polynomially.
Equation $(*)$ gives the reverse torsion inclusion, completing
the comparison.}
{最后比较完备化与去挠的先后顺序。令
$C_i'=A_0\langle U\rangle/L_iA_0\langle U\rangle$。
$C_i$ 中多项式的 $\pi$-挠映入 $C_i'$ 的 $\pi$-挠子模
$\tau$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalmoduletopology}，
$\tau$ 有限生成，因而由同一个 $\pi$ 的幂消去；其诱导拓扑因此是
离散的。它在 $B_i$ 中的像为零。反之，由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:adicquotientcompletion}，$C_i'\to B_i$ 的核
是多项式挠子模之像的闭包。由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalmoduletopology}，子模 $\tau$ 是闭的，
所以该闭包包含于 $\tau$。由于 $B_i$ 无 $\pi$-挠，反向包含也成立。
因此该核等于 $\tau$。
其中稠密的多项式挠子模由于 $\tau$ 离散而等于整个 $\tau$。
于是 $\tau$ 中每个元素都如多项式情形一样被某个 $f_i$ 的幂消去；
等式 $(*)$ 给出反向挠子模包含，完成比较。}
\end{proof}
