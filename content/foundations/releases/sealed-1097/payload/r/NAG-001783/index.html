Excellence of affinoid algebras and ordinary analytic local rings

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 350

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Excellence of affinoid algebras and ordinary analytic local rings}{Affinoid 代数与通常解析局部环的优秀性}]
\label{fnd:generalaffinoidexcellence}
\bi{Every generalized affinoid algebra over a complete non-Archimedean field is excellent. Every ordinary local ring of a good analytic space over such a field is excellent. In particular, these rings are Nagata and have finite algebraic normalization.}
{完备非阿基米德域上的每个 generalized affinoid 代数均为优秀环。该域上 good 解析空间的每个通常局部环也均为优秀环。特别地，这些环为 Nagata 环，且其代数正规化有限。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Let $A$ be affinoid and let $R=\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(A),x}$. Both are Noetherian. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedordinaryregularcomparison} proves that $R\to\widehat R$ is regular. The maximal-ideal criterion \cite[Tag 07PT]{StacksAlg} therefore makes $R$ a $G$-ring. For any prime $\mathfrak p\subset A$, choose an analytic point $x$ with kernel $\mathfrak p$ and put $D=A_{\mathfrak p}$. The same theorem makes $D\to R\to\widehat R$ a composite of regular maps. Since the target is Noetherian, the composite is regular by \cite[Tag 07QI]{StacksAlg}. The completed map $\widehat D\to\widehat R$ is a flat local map by Tag 0C4G, hence faithfully flat. Descent of regularity of ring maps, \cite[Tag 07NT]{StacksAlg}, applied to $D\to\widehat D\to\widehat R$, makes $D\to\widehat D$ regular. This holds for every $\mathfrak p$, so $A$ is a $G$-ring as well.}
{设 $A$ 为 affinoid 代数，$R=\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(A),x}$。两个环都 Noetherian。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedordinaryregularcomparison} 证明 $R\to\widehat R$ 正则，因此由极大理想判据 \cite[Tag 07PT]{StacksAlg}，$R$ 为 $G$-环。对 $A$ 的任意素理想 $\mathfrak p$，选取核为 $\mathfrak p$ 的解析点 $x$，并令 $D=A_{\mathfrak p}$。同一定理说明 $D\to R\to\widehat R$ 是正则映射的复合；目标 Noetherian，所以由 \cite[Tag 07QI]{StacksAlg}，复合也正则。由 Tag 0C4G，完成映射 $\widehat D\to\widehat R$ 是平坦局部映射，故忠实平坦。对 $D\to\widehat D\to\widehat R$ 应用环映射正则性的下降 \cite[Tag 07NT]{StacksAlg}，得到 $D\to\widehat D$ 正则。此结论对每个 $\mathfrak p$ 都成立，因此 $A$ 也是 $G$-环。}

\bi{We verify the $J$-2 property with Tag 07PC(4). For a prime $\mathfrak p\subset A$ and a finite purely inseparable extension $E/\kappa(\mathfrak p)$, clearing monic-equation denominators gives a finite domain $B$ over $A/\mathfrak p$ with fraction field $E$. Its canonical finite affinoid structure is supplied by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras}. The identity-extension case of Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticseparableregularopen} gives a nonempty regular principal open in $\operatorname{Spec}(B)$. Thus $B$ is $J$-0 and the criterion applies to $A$. For a prime $\mathfrak P\subset R$ and a finite purely inseparable extension $E/\kappa(\mathfrak P)$, take a finite domain $C$ over $R/\mathfrak P$ with fraction field $E$. Realize it by Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization} as $B\hookrightarrow C=\mathcal O_{Z,z}$, with $B$ integral affinoid. Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedgenericgermregularity} gives $0\ne b\in B$ for which $C[1/b]$ is regular. Since $B\hookrightarrow C$, this is a nonempty principal open. Thus $C$ is $J$-0, and the same criterion proves that $R$ is $J$-2. Both $A$ and $R$ are now quasi-excellent.}
{用 Tag 07PC(4) 核验 $J$-2 性。对 $\mathfrak p\subset A$ 及有限纯不可分扩张 $E/\kappa(\mathfrak p)$，清除首一方程的分母，得到 $A/\mathfrak p$ 上分式域为 $E$ 的有限整环 $B$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras} 给出其典范有限 affinoid 结构。Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticseparableregularopen} 的恒等扩张情形，给出 $\operatorname{Spec}(B)$ 中非空的正则主开集。因此 $B$ 为 $J$-0，该判据适用于 $A$。对 $\mathfrak P\subset R$ 及有限纯不可分扩张 $E/\kappa(\mathfrak P)$，取 $R/\mathfrak P$ 上分式域为 $E$ 的有限整环 $C$；用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization} 将其实现为 $B\hookrightarrow C=\mathcal O_{Z,z}$，其中 $B$ 整且 affinoid。Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedgenericgermregularity} 给出 $0\ne b\in B$，使 $C[1/b]$ 正则。因 $B\hookrightarrow C$，这是非空主开集，所以 $C$ 为 $J$-0；同一判据证明 $R$ 为 $J$-2。至此 $A,R$ 都拟优秀。}

\bi{It remains to prove universal catenarity. Present $A$ as a quotient of a generalized Tate algebra $D=K\{r^{-1}T\}$. The ring $D$ is regular by Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomainregular}. Its local rings are Cohen--Macaulay by Tag 00NQ; hence $D$ is universally catenary by \cite[Tag 00NM]{StacksAlg}. Quotients preserve universal catenarity by \cite[Tag 0ECE]{StacksAlg}, so $A$ is universally catenary. For $R$, the closed immersion $\mathcal M(A)\hookrightarrow\mathcal M(D)$ identifies $R$ with a quotient of the ordinary local ring $\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(D),x}$ by the extended defining ideal; this is the finite closed-immersion case of Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation}. That local ring is regular by Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomainregular}. The same two catenarity results therefore apply to its quotient $R$. A Noetherian, universally catenary, quasi-excellent ring is excellent by definition. Nagata's property and finite normalization now follow from Tags 07QV and 035S. Ordinary affinoid neighborhoods identify every ordinary local ring of a good space with an $R$ of this form.}
{还需证明普遍链状性。将 $A$ 表示为广义 Tate 代数 $D=K\{r^{-1}T\}$ 的商。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomainregular}，$D$ 正则；其局部环由 Tag 00NQ 为 Cohen--Macaulay，故由 \cite[Tag 00NM]{StacksAlg}，$D$ 普遍链状。由 \cite[Tag 0ECE]{StacksAlg}，商保持普遍链状性，因此 $A$ 普遍链状。对于 $R$，闭浸入 $\mathcal M(A)\hookrightarrow\mathcal M(D)$ 将它识别为通常局部环 $\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(D),x}$ 对扩张定义理想的商；这是 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation} 的有限闭浸入情形。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomainregular}，该局部环正则，所以相同的两个链状性结果适用于其商 $R$。根据定义，Noetherian、普遍链状且拟优秀的环就是优秀环。Nagata 性及正规化有限性随即来自 Tags 07QV 与 035S。普通 affinoid 邻域将 good 空间的每个通常局部环识别为上述形式的某个 $R$。}
\end{proof}
