Integral affine coordinates on the original skeleton

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 919

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Integral affine coordinates on the original skeleton}
{原骨架上的整仿射坐标}]
\label{cand:originalfieldskeletalcoordinates}
\bi{For every $f\in K(X)^\times$, the function $-\log|f|$
is finite and continuous on $\Sigma$ and integral affine on
the edges of a finite subdivision. Every point of $\Sigma$
has a graph neighborhood on which finitely many such functions
give an injective map with a derivative coordinate equal to
$1$ or $-1$ on each short edge.}
{对每个 $f\in K(X)^\times$，函数 $-\log|f|$ 在
$\Sigma$ 上有限且连续，并在某个有限细分的各边上整仿射。
$\Sigma$ 的每个点都有图邻域，使有限个这样的函数给出
单射，且在每条短边上某个导数坐标等于 $1$ 或 $-1$。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{By Lemma~\ref{cand:originalfieldrationalgerm}, $f$ and its
inverse are analytic near every skeletal point. At a vertex,
each incident graph branch is a rational normalization point
above a split node, by Lemma~\ref{cand:normalizedmodelbranches}.
Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope}, over the original field,
makes the logarithmic norm affine with integral slope on a
short initial segment. On the interior of a nodal edge,
express $f=P/Q$ on an algebraic affine neighborhood of the
residual node. Both functions are analytic on the nodal tube.
On each compact radius subinterval their nonzero Gauss norms
are finite maxima of Laurent monomial norms by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:annulusunitaffine}. Hence their logarithmic
difference has finitely many integral affine pieces. The
endpoint segments leave a compact interior interval; doing
this for the finitely many edges proves the first assertion.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{cand:originalfieldrationalgerm}，$f$ 及其
逆在每个骨架点附近解析。在顶点处，由
Lemma~\ref{cand:normalizedmodelbranches}，每条入射图分支
对应分裂节点上方的一个有理正规化点。
Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope} 在原域上说明对数
范数在一小段初始线段上整仿射。在节点边的内部，在剩余
节点的一个代数仿射邻域上写 $f=P/Q$，则两函数在节点
管域上解析。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:annulusunitaffine}，
在每个紧半径子区间上，其非零 Gauss 范数都是有限个
Laurent 单项式范数的最大值。因此二者对数之差只有
有限个整仿射片段。去掉端点线段后剩余内部区间紧；
对有限条边应用此论证，便得第一个断言。}

\bi{At an interior point, approximate its annular coordinate
$z$ by Lemma~\ref{cand:originalfieldrationalgerm}. The rational
norm agrees near that point with $|z|$, so its negative logarithm
is an oriented length coordinate of slope one. At a vertex
$v_C$, list the finitely many distinct normalization points
$p_j$ corresponding to incident edges, counting the two branches
of a loop separately. Lemma~\ref{cand:finiteprojectiveaffineneighborhood}
gives $u_i\in k(C)^\times$ with orders $\delta_{ij}$.
Proposition~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield} identifies
these with norm-one residues in $\mathcal H(v_C)$. Approximate
their lifts by $f_i\in K(X)$ with error of norm less than one.
Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope} gives, after a simultaneous
shortening of finitely many branches,}
{在边的内部点，利用 Lemma~\ref{cand:originalfieldrationalgerm}
逼近圆环坐标 $z$。有理函数的范数在该点附近等于 $|z|$，
故其负对数是斜率一的定向长度坐标。在顶点 $v_C$，
列出对应入射边的有限个互异正规化点 $p_j$，回路的两条
分支分别计算。Lemma~\ref{cand:finiteprojectiveaffineneighborhood}
给出阶数为 $\delta_{ij}$ 的 $u_i\in k(C)^\times$。
由 Proposition~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield}，
它们可识别为 $\mathcal H(v_C)$ 中范数一元素的剩余类。
用 $f_i\in K(X)$ 将其提升逼近到误差范数小于一。
由 Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope}，对有限条分支
同时缩短后，得到}
\[
 (-\log|f_1|,\ldots,-\log|f_m|)(\gamma_j(t))=t e_j.
\]
\bi{These coordinates embed the small graph star in distinct
positive coordinate axes and have primitive derivative vectors.
An isolated vertex requires no coordinates.}
{这些坐标将小图星形嵌入互异的正坐标轴，且导数向量
本原。孤立顶点不需要坐标。}
\end{proof}
