Relative neighbourhoods for algebraic morphisms

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 287

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Relative neighbourhoods for algebraic morphisms}{代数态射的相对邻域}]
\label{fnd:algebraicmorphismboundaryless}
\bi{The analytification of every morphism between finite-type $K$-schemes is boundaryless. More explicitly, for every affinoid domain $W$ in the target and every point $y$ of its inverse image, there is an affinoid neighbourhood $D$ of $y$ in that inverse image with $y\in\operatorname{Int}(D/W)$. In particular every finite-type scheme analytification is boundaryless over $K$.}
{有限型 $K$-概形之间的每个态射，其解析化都无边界。更精确地，对目标中的任意 affinoid 域 $W$ 及其逆像中的任意点 $y$，在该逆像中存在 $y$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $D$，使 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(D/W)$。特别地，每个有限型概形的解析化在 $K$ 上无边界。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Fix an affinoid target domain $W$ and $y\in f^{\an,-1}(W)$, and write $x=f^{\an}(y)$. Choose affine scheme neighbourhoods $\operatorname{Spec}(S)\to\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ of the two underlying scheme points, with $S=R[U_1,\ldots,U_m]/J$. The intersection $W\cap X_R$ is an ordinary open neighbourhood of $x$ in $W$. By Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictrationalinteriorneighborhood}, it contains a rational neighbourhood $V=\mathcal M(A)$ of $x$ in $W$ such that $x\in\operatorname{Int}(V/W)$. Choose positive radii $s_j>|U_j(y)|$. The affinoid algebra}
{固定目标的 affinoid 域 $W$ 及 $y\in f^{\an,-1}(W)$，并记 $x=f^{\an}(y)$。在两个底层概形点处选择仿射概形邻域 $\operatorname{Spec}(S)\to\operatorname{Spec}(R)$，其中 $S=R[U_1,\ldots,U_m]/J$。交集 $W\cap X_R$ 是 $x$ 在 $W$ 中的普通开邻域。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictrationalinteriorneighborhood}，其中包含 $x$ 在 $W$ 中的 rational 邻域 $V=\mathcal M(A)$，且 $x\in\operatorname{Int}(V/W)$。选择正半径 $s_j>|U_j(y)|$。考虑 affinoid 代数}
\[
 D=A\{s^{-1}U\}/J A\{s^{-1}U\}.
\]
\bi{It represents the part of the inverse image of $V$ with $|U_j|\le s_j$. Indeed, for every affinoid test algebra $C$, a map to that part gives $A\to C$ and the coordinate functions from $S$. The pointwise bounds on the latter imply the required power bounds by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointwisepowerbound}; substitution gives the unique bounded map $D\to C$. The converse follows directly from evaluation. The resulting domain identifications agree on overlaps, so this represents the actual analytic domain, not only its set of points.}
{它表示 $V$ 的逆像中满足 $|U_j|\le s_j$ 的部分。事实上，对任意 affinoid 测试代数 $C$，到此部分的映射给出 $A\to C$ 及来自 $S$ 的坐标函数。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointwisepowerbound}，这些函数的逐点界给出所需幂界，于是代入得到唯一有界映射 $D\to C$。反向结论直接由取值得到。所得域识别在重叠部分上一致，故表示实际解析域，而非仅识别点集。}

\bi{The inverse image of the interior of $V$ in $W$, intersected with $X_S$ and the strict inequalities $|U_j|<s_j$, is an open neighbourhood of $y$ in $f^{\an,-1}(W)$ contained in $\mathcal M(D)$. Thus $\mathcal M(D)$ is an affinoid neighbourhood in that inverse image. Its displayed norm-admissible quotient presentation witnesses $y\in\operatorname{Int}(\mathcal M(D)/V)$. Together with $x\in\operatorname{Int}(V/W)$, the composition inclusion of Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus} gives $y\in\operatorname{Int}(\mathcal M(D)/W)$. This verifies the boundaryless condition for every target affinoid domain. Taking the target to be $\operatorname{Spec}(K)$ proves the last assertion.}
{将 $V$ 在 $W$ 中内部的逆像，与 $X_S$ 及严格不等式 $|U_j|<s_j$ 相交，得到 $y$ 在 $f^{\an,-1}(W)$ 中的开邻域，且包含于 $\mathcal M(D)$。因此 $\mathcal M(D)$ 是该逆像中的 affinoid 邻域。所列 norm-admissible 商表示证明 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(\mathcal M(D)/V)$；结合 $x\in\operatorname{Int}(V/W)$，Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus} 的复合包含式给出 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(\mathcal M(D)/W)$。这就在每个目标 affinoid 域上验证无边界条件。令目标为 $\operatorname{Spec}(K)$，即得最后一个断言。}
\end{proof}
