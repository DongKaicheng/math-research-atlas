Rational inverse images under a Gauss section

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 364

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Rational inverse images under a Gauss section}{Gauss 截面下的 rational 逆像}]
\label{fnd:gaussrationalfinitecover}
\bi{Let $A$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra, let $r$ be a finite free tuple, and put $A_r=A\widehat\otimes_K K_r$. For every rational domain $R'$ of $\mathcal M_{K_r}(A_r)$, the subset $\sigma_{r,A}^{-1}(R')$ is a finite union of rational domains defined over the original algebra $A$, with positive real radii.}
{设 $A$ 为 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数，$r$ 为有限自由元组，令 $A_r=A\widehat\otimes_K K_r$。对 $\mathcal M_{K_r}(A_r)$ 中的每个 rational 域 $R'$，子集 $\sigma_{r,A}^{-1}(R')$ 都是定义在原代数 $A$ 上、允许正实半径的有限个 rational 域的并。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The zero algebra gives the empty case. Otherwise choose a rational presentation}
{零代数对应空情形。否则，选择 rational 表示}
\[
 R'=\{z:|f_i(z)|\leq\rho_i|g(z)|\ (1\leq i\leq m)\},
 \qquad h_0g+\sum_{i=1}^m h_if_i=1
\]
\bi{in $A_r$, with $\rho_i>0$. Use the coefficient norm of Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients} and set}
{其中各 $\rho_i>0$。采用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients} 的系数范数，并令}
\[
 C=\max\bigl(\|h_0\|,\rho_1\|h_1\|,\ldots,\rho_m\|h_m\|\bigr)>0,
 \qquad \delta=C^{-1}.
\]
\bi{For $x\in\sigma_{r,A}^{-1}(R')$, evaluating the unit identity gives}
{对 $x\in\sigma_{r,A}^{-1}(R')$，将单位恒等式取值，得到}
\[
 1\leq C\,\sigma_{r,A}(x)(g),
 \qquad \sigma_{r,A}(x)(g)\geq\delta.
\]
\bi{Write $g=\sum_{\nu}g_\nu T^\nu$ and $f_i=\sum_\nu f_{i\nu}T^\nu$. Choose finite index sets $G,F_i\subset\mathbf Z^s$ such that the omitted coefficient weights satisfy}
{写作 $g=\sum_{\nu}g_\nu T^\nu$ 及 $f_i=\sum_\nu f_{i\nu}T^\nu$。选择有限指标集 $G,F_i\subset\mathbf Z^s$，使省去的系数权重满足}
\[
 \|g_\nu\|r^\nu<\delta/2\quad(\nu\notin G),\qquad
 \|f_{i\nu}\|r^\nu<\rho_i\delta/2\quad(\nu\notin F_i).
\]
\bi{Such sets exist by coefficient convergence. For each $\nu\in G$, define $R_\nu\subseteq\mathcal M_K(A)$ by}
{由系数收敛性，这些有限集存在。对每个 $\nu\in G$，用下列条件定义 $R_\nu\subseteq\mathcal M_K(A)$：}
\[
 \begin{split}
 \delta&\leq r^\nu|g_\nu(x)|,\\
 r^\mu|g_\mu(x)|&\leq r^\nu|g_\nu(x)|\quad(\mu\in G),\\
 r^\mu|f_{i\mu}(x)|&\leq\rho_i r^\nu|g_\nu(x)|
       \quad(1\leq i\leq m,\ \mu\in F_i).
 \end{split}
\]
\bi{This is an original-$A$ rational presentation with denominator $g_\nu$: its numerators are $1$, the $g_\mu$, and the $f_{i\mu}$, with respective radii $r^\nu/\delta$, $r^{\nu-\mu}$, and $\rho_i r^{\nu-\mu}$. The numerator $1$ guarantees the unit-ideal condition. Empty members, including those with $g_\nu=0$, are allowed.}
{这是原 $A$ 上的 rational 表示，以 $g_\nu$ 为分母，分子依次为 $1$、各 $g_\mu$ 及各 $f_{i\mu}$，相应半径为 $r^\nu/\delta$、$r^{\nu-\mu}$ 及 $\rho_i r^{\nu-\mu}$。分子中包含 $1$，保证单位理想条件。允许空成员，包括 $g_\nu=0$ 的情形。}

\bi{At a point of $\sigma_{r,A}^{-1}(R')$, the denominator value is at least $\delta$. The omitted $g$-coefficients have smaller value, so some retained coefficient $g_\nu$ attains its Gauss maximum. The rational inequalities and the same Gauss formula then put the point in $R_\nu$. Conversely, on $R_\nu$, the first two conditions and the omitted-coefficient bound give $\sigma_{r,A}(x)(g)=r^\nu|g_\nu(x)|\geq\delta$. The retained $f_i$-coefficients satisfy the required bound by the third condition, while the omitted coefficients are smaller than $\rho_i\delta$. Therefore $\sigma_{r,A}(x)(f_i)\leq\rho_i\sigma_{r,A}(x)(g)$ for every $i$. This proves the exact set equality}
{在 $\sigma_{r,A}^{-1}(R')$ 的点处，分母取值至少为 $\delta$。省去的 $g$-系数取值更小，因此某个保留的系数 $g_\nu$ 达到其 Gauss 最大值。由 rational 不等式与同一 Gauss 公式，该点属于 $R_\nu$。反之，在 $R_\nu$ 上，前两组条件及省去系数的界给出 $\sigma_{r,A}(x)(g)=r^\nu|g_\nu(x)|\geq\delta$。第三组条件控制全部保留的 $f_i$-系数，而省去的系数小于 $\rho_i\delta$。因此，对每个 $i$ 都有 $\sigma_{r,A}(x)(f_i)\leq\rho_i\sigma_{r,A}(x)(g)$。于是得到精确的集合等式}
\[
 \sigma_{r,A}^{-1}(R')=\bigcup_{\nu\in G}R_\nu.
\]
\bi{The proof also allows an empty tuple $r$, zero retained coefficients and an empty inverse image.}
{此证明也允许 $r$ 为空元组、保留的系数为零，以及逆像为空。}
\end{proof}
