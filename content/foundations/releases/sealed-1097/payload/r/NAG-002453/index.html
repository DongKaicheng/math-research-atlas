The actual residual algebra after free Gauss normalization

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1060

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[The actual residual algebra after free Gauss normalization]
\label{v038:pointed:gaussrelationmodel}
Let $V=\mathcal M(A)$, $x\in V$, and
$f_1,\ldots,f_n\in A^\times$ be arbitrary analytic units.
Put $r_i=|f_i(x)|$, $F_x=\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}^{\rm gr}$,
$k=\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$, and
$u_i=\widetilde{f_i(x)}$. Let $L$ be the completion of
$K(S_1,\ldots,S_n)$ for the weighted Gauss norm
$|S_i|=r_i$, without assuming these radii are free
over the value group of $K$. There is a point
$x_L\in V_L$ above $x$ for which the residue algebra
\[
          \widetilde L[\widetilde{f_1/S_1}(x_L)^{\pm1},
                    \ldots,\widetilde{f_n/S_n}(x_L)^{\pm1}]
\]
is precisely the ordinary degree-one algebra $B$
constructed in Lemma~\ref{v038:pointed:gradedfinitebasis}
from the graded relation ideal of the $u_i$.
If $x\in\operatorname{Int}(V/K)$, then
$x_L\in\operatorname{Int}(V_L/L)$.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Put $H=\mathcal H(x)$ and let $E$ be the completion of
$H(S_1,\ldots,S_n)$ for the same weighted Gauss norm.
The formula on finite sums gives an isometric embedding
$L\hookrightarrow E$. It also proves the graded identities
\[
 \widetilde L^{\rm gr}
       =\operatorname{Frac}^{\rm gr}(k[s_1^{\pm1},\ldots,
                                             s_n^{\pm1}]),
 \quad
 \widetilde E^{\rm gr}
       =\operatorname{Frac}^{\rm gr}(F_x[s_1^{\pm1},\ldots,
                                             s_n^{\pm1}]),
\]
where $s_i=\widetilde{S_i}$ and the fraction fields
invert nonzero homogeneous elements. Indeed the maximum
Gauss term of a nonzero polynomial has nonzero initial
polynomial, distinct Laurent monomials do not cancel,
and the initial form of a rational fraction is the
quotient of its numerator and denominator initial
forms. Passing to the completion adds no homogeneous
initial values: approximate every nonzero element
by a rational fraction with smaller error.

The bounded evaluation $A\to H$ and the isometric map
$L\to E$ produce a bounded map
$A\widehat\otimes_KL\to E$ and hence $x_L$ above $x$.
The completed residue field $\mathcal H(x_L)$ embeds
isometrically into $E$. The graded injection
\[
             F_x\otimes_k\widetilde L^{\rm gr}
                              \longrightarrow\widetilde E^{\rm gr}
\]
comes from the algebraic independence of the $s_i$
over $F_x$, followed by homogeneous localization.
Consequently the kernel of evaluation of the
normalized initials $u_i/s_i$ over
$\widetilde L=(\widetilde L^{\rm gr})_1$ is exactly
the degree-one normalization of the scalar extension
of the original homogeneous relation ideal $I$.
By the definition of $B$ in
Lemma~\ref{v038:pointed:gradedfinitebasis}, this is
the asserted ordinary residue algebra. The base-change
interior inclusion in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus} gives the
last assertion.
\end{proof}
