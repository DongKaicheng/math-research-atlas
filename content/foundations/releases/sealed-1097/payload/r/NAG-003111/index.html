\begin{lemma}[Ordinary height-one model local rings]\label{f9scdvr:bridge}
Let $\cT$ be a Noetherian normal integral scheme and $x$ a point of
height one. The ordinary algebraic local ring
$A=\mathcal O_{\cT,x}$ is a DVR. Its normalized order
$\ord_x:K(\cT)^\times\to\mathbf Z$ satisfies
\[
 \length_A(A/fA)=\ord_x(f) \qquad (0\ne f\in A).
\]
For a rational local equation $f/g$, its signed divisor coefficient is
therefore $\ord_x(f/g)=\ord_x(f)-\ord_x(g)$.
\end{lemma}
\noindent\emph{Exact external input:}
\st{00PD}, Lemma 10.119.7, implication $(5)\Rightarrow(1)$ and
its normalized uniformizer expansion.
\begin{proof}
The ring $A$ is local and Noetherian, and normality makes it a normal
domain. Height one means $\dim A=1$. These are exactly condition~(5)
of \st{00PD}, so condition~(1) gives a DVR. For a generator $t$ of
its maximal ideal, every nonzero element is $ut^n$ with $u$ a unit.
The successive quotients of the $t$-adic filtration of $A/(t^n)$
are copies of the residue field; hence their total length is $n$.
Subtracting orders gives the assertion for a rational equation.
\end{proof}
