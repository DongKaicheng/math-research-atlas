Dimension of strict canonical reduction

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 618

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Dimension of strict canonical reduction}
{Strict 典范约化的维数}]
\label{fnd:canonicalreductiondimensionbound}
\bi{For every nonempty strict affinoid space $X=\mathcal M(A)$,}
{对每个非空 strict affinoid 空间 $X=\mathcal M(A)$，有}
\[
                  \dim\widetilde A\leq\dim_KX.
\]
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The ring $R=\widetilde A$ is a finite-type
$k=\widetilde K$-algebra. For every prime $\mathfrak p$ of
$R$, Theorem~\ref{fnd:canonicalreductionsurjective} gives a
point $x$ reducing to $\mathfrak p$. The inclusion
$R/\mathfrak p\hookrightarrow\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}$
extends to its fraction field. Thus}
{$R=\widetilde A$ 是有限型 $k=\widetilde K$-代数。
对 $R$ 的任意素理想 $\mathfrak p$，由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:canonicalreductionsurjective}，可取约化为
$\mathfrak p$ 的点 $x$。单射
$R/\mathfrak p\hookrightarrow\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}$
延拓到其分式域。因此}
\[
 \dim(R/\mathfrak p)
 =\operatorname{trdeg}_{k}\operatorname{Frac}(R/\mathfrak p)
 \leq\operatorname{trdeg}_{k}\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}
 \leq d_K(x)\leq\dim_KX.
\]
\bi{The first equality is \cite[Tag 00P0]{StacksAlg}, and
the last inequality is Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidabhyankarmaximum}.
Every finite chain of prime ideals of the Noetherian ring $R$
lies over a minimal prime. Taking the maximum of the dimensions
of these finitely many components gives the assertion.}
{第一个等式是 \cite[Tag 00P0]{StacksAlg}，最后一个不等式是
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidabhyankarmaximum}。
Noetherian 环 $R$ 的每条有限素理想链都位于一个极小素理想之上。
取这有限多个不可约分支维数的最大值，即得结论。}
\end{proof}
