Composition of Jacobian presentations

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 399

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Composition of Jacobian presentations}{Jacobian 表示的复合}]
\label{fnd:jacobiansmoothcomposition}
\bi{Let $Z\xrightarrow{g}Y\xrightarrow{f}X$ be analytic morphisms and put $y=g(z)$. If $g$ is quasi-smooth at $z$ and $f$ is quasi-smooth at $y$, then $fg$ is quasi-smooth at $z$. The differential fibre ranks satisfy}
{设 $Z\xrightarrow{g}Y\xrightarrow{f}X$ 为解析态射，置 $y=g(z)$。若 $g$ 在 $z$ 处 quasi-smooth，且 $f$ 在 $y$ 处 quasi-smooth，则 $fg$ 在 $z$ 处 quasi-smooth。微分纤维秩满足}
\[
 \operatorname{rk}_z\Omega_{Z/X}
       =\operatorname{rk}_y\Omega_{Y/X}
             +\operatorname{rk}_z\Omega_{Z/Y}.
\]
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Write $d=\operatorname{rk}_y\Omega_{Y/X}$ and $e=\operatorname{rk}_z\Omega_{Z/Y}$. Choose $W\hookrightarrow V\subseteq\mathbb A_X^n$ at $y$ with $n-d$ equations. By domain locality replace the part of $Z$ under consideration by its inverse image over $W$, and choose a Jacobian presentation $T\hookrightarrow S\subseteq\mathbb A_W^m$ at $z$ with $m-e$ equations.}
{记 $d=\operatorname{rk}_y\Omega_{Y/X}$ 与 $e=\operatorname{rk}_z\Omega_{Z/Y}$。在 $y$ 处选择具有 $n-d$ 个方程的表示 $W\hookrightarrow V\subseteq\mathbb A_X^n$。由域局部性，将所考虑的 $Z$ 的部分换为 $W$ 上的逆像，再在 $z$ 处选择具有 $m-e$ 个方程的 Jacobian 表示 $T\hookrightarrow S\subseteq\mathbb A_W^m$。}

\bi{Since $S$ is affinoid, its $m$ coordinate functions are bounded. Hence it lies in a closed relative polydisc $P_W$ over $W$, the inverse image of a closed relative polydisc $P$ over $V$. It is an affinoid domain of $P_W$ by the domain mapping property. Cover $S$ by rational domains of $P_W$ and choose one $S_0$ containing the image of $z$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalliftclosedimmersion} gives a rational domain $P_0\subseteq P$ with $P_0\cap P_W=S_0$. In particular $P_0$ is an affinoid domain of $\mathbb A_X^{n+m}$. Put $T_0=T\times_SS_0$.}
{由于 $S$ 为 affinoid，其 $m$ 个坐标函数有界，故它包含于 $W$ 上某个闭相对多圆盘 $P_W$；后者为 $V$ 上某个闭相对多圆盘 $P$ 的逆像。域映射性质说明 $S$ 是 $P_W$ 的 affinoid 域。用 $P_W$ 中的 rational 域覆盖 $S$，并选择包含 $z$ 的像的成员 $S_0$。Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalliftclosedimmersion} 给出 rational 域 $P_0\subseteq P$，满足 $P_0\cap P_W=S_0$。特别地，$P_0$ 是 $\mathbb A_X^{n+m}$ 的 affinoid 域。置 $T_0=T\times_SS_0$。}

\bi{The ideal of $S_0$ in $P_0$ is generated by the original $n-d$ equations. The ideal of $T_0$ in $S_0$ is generated by the restricted $m-e$ equations; lift these through the surjection of affinoid algebras $\mathcal O(P_0)\to\mathcal O(S_0)$. The union of the two lists generates the ideal of $T_0$ in $P_0$. Thus the rank formula of Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianmatrixcriterion} gives}
{原来的 $n-d$ 个方程生成 $S_0$ 在 $P_0$ 中的理想。限制后的 $m-e$ 个方程生成 $T_0$ 在 $S_0$ 中的理想；沿 affinoid 代数满射 $\mathcal O(P_0)\to\mathcal O(S_0)$ 提升这些方程。两组方程的并生成 $T_0$ 在 $P_0$ 中的理想。因此 Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianmatrixcriterion} 的秩公式给出}
\[
 \operatorname{rk}_z\Omega_{Z/X}
   =\operatorname{rk}_z\Omega_{T_0/X}
   \ge(n+m)-((n-d)+(m-e))=d+e.
\]
\bi{The transitivity sequence of Proposition~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialbasechange}, tensored with $\mathcal H(z)$, gives the opposite inequality. The first term has dimension $d$, because its fibre is obtained from the fibre at $y$ by a field extension, and the final term has dimension $e$. Hence equality holds. The immersion $T_0\hookrightarrow P_0\subseteq\mathbb A_X^{n+m}$ has exactly the required number of equations, so is a Jacobian presentation at $z$.}
{将 Proposition~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialbasechange} 的传递序列与 $\mathcal H(z)$ 张量，给出反向不等式。首项的维数为 $d$，因为它由 $y$ 处纤维扩域得到；末项维数为 $e$。故必为等号。因此闭浸入 $T_0\hookrightarrow P_0\subseteq\mathbb A_X^{n+m}$ 恰好具有定义要求的方程数，是 $z$ 处的 Jacobian 表示。}
\end{proof}
