Reduced strict domains and ordinary stalks

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 475

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Reduced strict domains and ordinary stalks}
{约化 strict 域及其通常茎}]
\label{fnd:strictreduceddomainstalks}
\bi{Let $A$ be a reduced strict $K$-affinoid algebra.
Every ordinary local ring of $\mathcal M(A)$ is reduced.
Every strict affinoid domain in $\mathcal M(A)$ has a reduced
affinoid algebra.}
{设 $A$ 为约化 strict $K$-affinoid 代数。
$\mathcal M(A)$ 的每个通常局部环均约化，且其中每个 strict
affinoid 域的 affinoid 代数均约化。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Fix $x$, set $D=A_{\ker(x)}$ and
$R=\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(A),x}$.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictordinaryregularcomparison} makes
$D\to R$ a regular, faithfully flat homomorphism.
The reduced Noetherian ring $D$ embeds into the product of the
fraction fields of its finitely many minimal-prime quotients.
Tensoring this injection with the flat $D$-module $R$ embeds
$R$ into the product of the corresponding algebraic fibres.
These fibres are geometrically regular by the cited comparison,
hence reduced: every regular local ring is a domain by
\cite[Tag 00NP]{StacksAlg}. Therefore $R$ is reduced.}
{固定 $x$，令 $D=A_{\ker(x)}$ 及
$R=\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(A),x}$。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictordinaryregularcomparison} 说明
$D\to R$ 为正则且忠实平坦的同态。
约化 Noetherian 环 $D$ 嵌入其有限个极小素理想商的分式域的乘积。
与平坦 $D$-模 $R$ 张量，将 $R$ 嵌入相应代数纤维的乘积。
由所引比较定理，这些纤维均几何正则，从而约化：
\cite[Tag 00NP]{StacksAlg} 说明每个正则局部环都是整环。
因此 $R$ 约化。}

\bi{Let $V=\mathcal M(B)$ be a strict affinoid domain and
let $\mathfrak m$ be any maximal ideal of $B$.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} realizes it as a
rigid point $v$. Its residue field is finite over $K$, so its
residue field in degree one is finite over $\widetilde K$ by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitevaluedindexbound}. Its canonical reduction
in $\widetilde B$ is consequently a closed point, and
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictabsoluteinteriorreduction} makes $v$
an absolute inner point of $V$.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcompositionequality}
implies that it is inner for $V\hookrightarrow\mathcal M(A)$.
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generaldomainordinaryinterior} therefore
makes $V$ an ordinary neighborhood of $v$ in $\mathcal M(A)$.
Thus $\mathcal O_{V,v}$ is the reduced local ring just considered.
The map $B_{\mathfrak m}\to\mathcal O_{V,v}$ is faithfully
flat by Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks} and
domain flatness. It is injective, so $B_{\mathfrak m}$ is
reduced. This holds for all maximal ideals of $B$, and hence
$B$ is reduced.}
{设 $V=\mathcal M(B)$ 为 strict affinoid 域，并任取
$B$ 的极大理想 $\mathfrak m$。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} 将其实现为 rigid 点
$v$。其剩余域在 $K$ 上有限，所以由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitevaluedindexbound}，一次剩余域也在
$\widetilde K$ 上有限。因此它在 $\widetilde B$ 中的典范
约化是闭点，Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictabsoluteinteriorreduction}
说明 $v$ 为 $V$ 的绝对内点。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcompositionequality} 说明它也是
$V\hookrightarrow\mathcal M(A)$ 的内点，故由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generaldomainordinaryinterior}，$V$ 是
$v$ 在 $\mathcal M(A)$ 中的普通邻域。
所以 $\mathcal O_{V,v}$ 就是刚才已证约化的局部环。
由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks} 及域平坦性，
$B_{\mathfrak m}\to\mathcal O_{V,v}$ 忠实平坦，因而单射，
故 $B_{\mathfrak m}$ 约化。对 $B$ 的所有极大理想均成立，
于是 $B$ 约化。}
\end{proof}
