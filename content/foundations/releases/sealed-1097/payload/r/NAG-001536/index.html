Flat restriction to a strict affinoid domain

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 255

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Flat restriction to a strict affinoid domain}{向 strict affinoid 域的平坦限制}]\label{fnd:strictdomainflatness}
\bi{Under the same field hypothesis, every strict-test domain map $A\to B$ is flat as an ordinary ring map. A finite covering by such domains gives a faithfully flat ordinary ring map $A\to\prod_i A_{V_i}$. These assertions concern algebraic tensor products of arbitrary modules; they do not assert exactness of completed tensor products of arbitrary Banach modules.}
{在相同的基域假设下，每个 strict-test 域映射 $A\to B$ 作为普通环映射均平坦。这类域的有限覆盖给出忠实平坦的普通环映射 $A\to\prod_i A_{V_i}$。这些断言涉及任意模的代数张量积，并不断言任意 Banach 模的完备张量积正合。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose an $A$-rational covering $(R_j)$ of the domain by Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis}. Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalrestrictiondomainbridge} makes it a rational covering over $B$ with the same algebras. Put $S=\prod_j A_{R_j}$. Corollary~\ref{fnd:rationalcoverfaithfullyflat} makes $B\to S$ faithfully flat, whereas rational flatness makes $A\to S$ flat. If $N'\to N$ is an injective map of $A$-modules and $Q$ is the kernel of $B\otimes_A N'\to B\otimes_A N$, tensoring over $B$ with the flat algebra $S$ identifies $S\otimes_B Q$ with the kernel of $S\otimes_A N'\to S\otimes_A N$, which is zero. Faithfulness gives $Q=0$. Together with right exactness of ordinary tensor products, \cite[Tag 00DF, Lemma 10.12.10]{StacksAlg}, this is flatness of $B$ over $A$. For an empty domain, $B=0$ is flat and the assertion is immediate.}
{由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis} 选取该域的 $A$-rational 覆盖 $(R_j)$。Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalrestrictiondomainbridge} 使它成为 $B$ 上的 rational 覆盖，代数保持相同。令 $S=\prod_j A_{R_j}$。由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:rationalcoverfaithfullyflat}，$B\to S$ 忠实平坦；由 rational 平坦性，$A\to S$ 平坦。若 $N'\to N$ 是 $A$-模单射，令 $Q$ 为 $B\otimes_A N'\to B\otimes_A N$ 的核。在 $B$ 上与平坦代数 $S$ 张量后，$S\otimes_B Q$ 等于 $S\otimes_A N'\to S\otimes_A N$ 的核，因此为零。由忠实性，$Q=0$。结合普通张量积的右正合性，即 \cite[Tag 00DF, Lemma 10.12.10]{StacksAlg}，这证明 $B$ 在 $A$ 上平坦。对空域，有 $B=0$，平坦性直接成立。}

\bi{For a finite covering, refine each member by the preceding rational covers. The resulting $A$-rational cover has faithfully flat product $A\to S$, and $S$ is an algebra over $B'=\prod_iA_{V_i}$. The already proved flatness makes $B'$ flat over $A$. If $B'\otimes_A N=0$, then $S\otimes_A N=0$, so $N=0$. Thus tensoring with $B'$ is faithful as well as exact. This is the asserted faithful flatness.}
{对有限覆盖，分别用上述 rational 覆盖加细其成员。所得 $A$-rational 覆盖的乘积映射 $A\to S$ 忠实平坦，且 $S$ 是 $B'=\prod_iA_{V_i}$ 上的代数。由刚证明的平坦性，$B'$ 在 $A$ 上平坦。若 $B'\otimes_A N=0$，则 $S\otimes_A N=0$，故 $N=0$。因此与 $B'$ 张量不仅正合，而且忠实，得到所述忠实平坦性。}
\end{proof}
