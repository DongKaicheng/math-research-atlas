Quasi-excellence in characteristic zero

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 336

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Quasi-excellence in characteristic zero}{特征零中的拟优秀性}]
\label{fnd:charzeroaffinoidquasiexcellence}
\bi{Every generalized affinoid algebra over a complete field of characteristic zero is a quasi-excellent ring and is Nagata. Its algebraic normalization is finite.}
{特征零完备域上的每个 generalized affinoid 代数都是拟优秀环，也是 Nagata 环；其代数正规化有限。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Let $A$ be such an algebra. It is Noetherian. We first prove that $A$ is a $G$-ring, meaning that for every prime $\mathfrak p\subset A$ the completion map $A_{\mathfrak p}\to\widehat{A_{\mathfrak p}}$ is regular. Choose $x\in\mathcal M(A)$ with kernel $\mathfrak p$, using Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity}, and put $D=A_{\mathfrak p}$ and $R=\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(A),x}$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroregularcomparison} makes $D\to R$ and $R\to\widehat R$ regular.}
{设 $A$ 为所述代数，它是 Noetherian 环。先证明 $A$ 为 $G$-环，即对每个素理想 $\mathfrak p\subset A$，完备化映射 $A_{\mathfrak p}\to\widehat{A_{\mathfrak p}}$ 正则。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity}，选择核为 $\mathfrak p$ 的点 $x\in\mathcal M(A)$，令 $D=A_{\mathfrak p}$、$R=\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(A),x}$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroregularcomparison} 说明 $D\to R$ 及 $R\to\widehat R$ 正则。}

\bi{We verify the composite on its fibres. For $\mathfrak q\in\operatorname{Spec}(D)$, set $F=\kappa(\mathfrak q)$, $R_F=R\otimes_D F$ and $H_F=\widehat R\otimes_D F$. The ring $R_F$ is regular. The map $R_F\to H_F$ is flat. A prime of $R_F$ corresponds to a prime $\mathfrak Q$ of $R$ over $\mathfrak q$, with the same residue field, because $R_F$ is obtained from $R$ by quotient and localization. Its fibre in $H_F$ is therefore $\widehat R\otimes_R\kappa(\mathfrak Q)$, which is regular. All these rings are Noetherian. At each prime of $H_F$, the local source is regular and the local map has regular closed fibre; Tag 031E makes the target local ring regular. Thus $H_F$ is regular, and Tag 0381 makes it geometrically regular over $F$. This proves directly that $D\to\widehat R$ is regular.}
{逐纤维核验复合同态。对 $\mathfrak q\in\operatorname{Spec}(D)$，令 $F=\kappa(\mathfrak q)$、$R_F=R\otimes_DF$、$H_F=\widehat R\otimes_DF$。环 $R_F$ 正则，且映射 $R_F\to H_F$ 平坦。因为 $R_F$ 是 $R$ 的商再局部化，其每个素理想对应于 $R$ 中位于 $\mathfrak q$ 上方的素理想 $\mathfrak Q$，二者剩余域相同。所以该点在 $H_F$ 中的纤维为 $\widehat R\otimes_R\kappa(\mathfrak Q)$，它正则。全部环均 Noetherian。对 $H_F$ 的每个素理想，相应局部源正则且局部映射的闭纤维正则，Tag 031E 使目标局部环正则。因此 $H_F$ 正则，再由 Tag 0381 得其在 $F$ 上几何正则。这直接证明 $D\to\widehat R$ 正则。}

\bi{By Tag 0C4G, $\widehat D\to\widehat R$ is flat, since $D\to R$ is a flat local map; it is local and hence faithfully flat. After tensoring with each $F=\kappa(\mathfrak q)$ over $D$, the resulting map}
{由 Tag 0C4G，因 $D\to R$ 是平坦局部映射，故 $\widehat D\to\widehat R$ 平坦；它又是局部映射，所以忠实平坦。在 $D$ 上与每个 $F=\kappa(\mathfrak q)$ 张量后，所得映射}
\[
                 \widehat D\otimes_D F
                         \longrightarrow \widehat R\otimes_D F
\]
\bi{remains faithfully flat. Its target is regular by the preceding paragraph. Tags 00HQ and 00OF descend regularity to its source, prime by prime. Tag 0381 then gives geometric regularity. Together with flatness of completion, this proves regularity of $D\to\widehat D$ at every algebraic fibre. Hence $A$ is a $G$-ring.}
{仍忠实平坦，且其目标由上一段正则。逐素理想使用 Tags 00HQ 与 00OF，使正则性下降到源，再由 Tag 0381 得几何正则性。结合完备化的平坦性，这就在每个代数纤维证明 $D\to\widehat D$ 正则。因此 $A$ 为 $G$-环。}

\bi{To prove the $J$-2 condition, use \cite[Tag 07PC, equivalence (4)$\Rightarrow$(1)]{StacksAlg}. For any prime $\mathfrak p\subset A$, the field $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ has characteristic zero, so its finite purely inseparable extension is itself. Take the finite $A$-algebra $A/\mathfrak p$. It is a domain, has fraction field $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$, and has a nonempty regular principal open subset by Proposition~\ref{fnd:charzerouniformregularopen}. These are exactly the hypotheses of that criterion. Thus $A$ is $J$-2. By definition, a Noetherian $G$-ring which is $J$-2 is quasi-excellent. The Nagata conclusion follows from \cite[Tag 07QV]{StacksAlg}, and finiteness of normalization from \cite[Tag 035S]{StacksAlg}, applied to $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$.}
{对 $J$-2 条件，使用 \cite[Tag 07PC, (4)$\Rightarrow$(1)]{StacksAlg}。对任意素理想 $\mathfrak p\subset A$，域 $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ 的特征为零，所以其有限纯不可分扩张只有自身。取有限 $A$-代数 $A/\mathfrak p$；它为整环，分式域为 $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$，且由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:charzerouniformregularopen} 具有非空的正则主开子集。这恰好核验该判据的全部假设，所以 $A$ 为 $J$-2。根据定义，Noetherian 的 $G$-环且为 $J$-2，即为拟优秀环。Nagata 结论来自 \cite[Tag 07QV]{StacksAlg}；将 \cite[Tag 035S]{StacksAlg} 用于 $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$，得到正规化有限。}
\end{proof}
