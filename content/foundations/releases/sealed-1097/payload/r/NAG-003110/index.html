Three failures of broader formulations; F8NM-E

Remaining historical proof gap

The sealed ledger records a gap in the proof of the historical whole statement.

Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Three failures of broader formulations; F8NM-E]
\label{prop:obstructions}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)},leftmargin=*]
\item In \(A=K\langle t,e\rangle/(e^2,te)\), the element \(t\) is
nonzero on the sole reduced component but is an ambient zero-divisor.
The ambient quotient \(A/(t)\) has length two, whereas the component
quotient has length one and the ambient generic weight is one.
Thus this weakening cannot recover the full quotient cycle.
\item Even for the reduced disc \(A=K\langle t\rangle\), an arbitrary
full-support coherent module \(F=A\oplus A/(t)\) cannot be inserted
with right side \(F/tF\) in the scalar formula. Its generic top weight
is one, but \(F/tF\) has length two. The function \(t\) is ambient
regular but not \(F\)-regular.
\item For a smooth metrized line bundle, constant truncation of local
\(\log|f_i|\) does not in general glue to a frame-independent smooth
potential, even when the untruncated potential and its current glue.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}
