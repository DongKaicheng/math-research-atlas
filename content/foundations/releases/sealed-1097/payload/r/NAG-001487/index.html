Finite strict-affinoid epimorphisms

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 87

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Finite strict-affinoid epimorphisms}{有限 strict-affinoid 上态}]
\label{fnd:strictfiniteepimorphism}
\bi{A finite strict-affinoid epimorphism $A\to B$ is surjective. Conversely, every bounded surjective homomorphism of strict affinoids is a finite strict-affinoid epimorphism. These assertions hold for trivial as well as nontrivial valuation.}
{有限 strict-affinoid 上态 $A\to B$ 必为满射。反之，strict affinoid 之间每个有界满同态都是有限 strict-affinoid 上态。平凡与非平凡赋值下这两个断言均成立。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{By Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebrabasechange}, the ordinary algebra $D=B\otimes_A B$ is a strict affinoid and its two tensor arrows from $B$ are bounded. The epimorphism condition makes these arrows equal. Therefore every pure tensor satisfies $b\otimes b'=bb'\otimes1$. The multiplication map $D\to B$ and $b\mapsto b\otimes1$ are inverse. Thus $A\to B$ is an epimorphism of rings: any two ring maps agreeing on $A$ factor through the tensor product, and the same equality forces them to agree on $B$. The finite epimorphism theorem \cite[Tag 04VT, Lemma 10.107.6]{StacksAlg} now gives surjectivity. The converse follows immediately from surjectivity; as an $A$-module a quotient algebra is generated by one.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebrabasechange}，普通代数 $D=B\otimes_A B$ 为 strict affinoid，且来自 $B$ 的两个张量映射均有界。上态条件使这两个映射相同。因此每个纯张量满足 $b\otimes b'=bb'\otimes1$，从而乘法映射 $D\to B$ 与 $b\mapsto b\otimes1$ 互逆。于是 $A\to B$ 是环上态：任意两个在 $A$ 上相同的环映射通过张量积给出的映射可见，它们在 $B$ 上也相同。有限上态定理 \cite[Tag 04VT, Lemma 10.107.6]{StacksAlg} 给出满射性。反向断言直接来自满射性；作为 $A$-模，商代数由一生成。}
\end{proof}
