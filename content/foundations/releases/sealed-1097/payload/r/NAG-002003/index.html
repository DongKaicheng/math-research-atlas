A product patch from a free Gauss residue field

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 454

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{A product patch from a free Gauss residue field}{由自由 Gauss 剩余域获得乘积开片}]
\label{fnd:freegausssmoothproductpatch}
\bi{Let $Y\to X$ be smooth of pure relative dimension $n$ between good analytic spaces over any complete field $K$. Suppose that $y\in Y_x$ has an isometric $\mathcal H(x)$-identification $\mathcal H(y)=\mathcal H(x)_r$, where $r=(r_1,\ldots,r_m)$ is free and $0\leq m\leq n$. Inside any prescribed ordinary neighborhood of $y$ there is a nonempty ordinary open subset which, over $X$, is isomorphic to}
{设 $Y\to X$ 为任意完备域 $K$ 上 good 解析空间之间纯相对维数 $n$ 的光滑态射。假设 $y\in Y_x$ 有等距的 $\mathcal H(x)$-识别 $\mathcal H(y)=\mathcal H(x)_r$，其中 $r=(r_1,\ldots,r_m)$ 自由且 $0\leq m\leq n$。则在 $y$ 的任意预定普通邻域内，存在非空普通开集在 $X$ 上同构于}
\[
                         U\times_K D\times_K\Delta,
\]
\bi{where $U$ is an ordinary neighborhood of $x$, $D$ is an $m$-dimensional open polyannulus, and $\Delta$ is an $(n-m)$-dimensional open polydisc.}
{其中 $U$ 是 $x$ 的普通邻域，$D$ 是 $m$ 维开多圆环，$\Delta$ 是 $n-m$ 维开多圆盘。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Restrict to the prescribed neighborhood. The field $\kappa(y)$ is dense in $\mathcal H(y)$, so each free coordinate value of norm $r_i$ can be approximated by an element of $\kappa(y)$ closely enough to preserve its norm. Represent these elements by analytic germs $f_i$ near $y$; a denominator with nonzero value is a unit on a smaller ordinary neighborhood. Proposition~\ref{fnd:freegaussdifferentialindependence}, applied on the analytic fibre, makes $(df_1)(y),\ldots,(df_m)(y)$ independent in the relative differential fibre. Fibre base change of analytic differentials identifies this with the corresponding subspace of $\Omega^{\rm an}_{Y/X}\otimes\mathcal H(y)$. Since the latter has dimension $n$ and is generated by differentials of local analytic functions, add $f_{m+1},\ldots,f_n$ so that their differentials form a basis.}
{先限制到预定邻域。域 $\kappa(y)$ 在 $\mathcal H(y)$ 中稠密，因此每个范数为 $r_i$ 的自由坐标值，都可用 $\kappa(y)$ 中的元素充分逼近而保持范数。用 $y$ 附近的解析芽 $f_i$ 表示这些元素；取值非零的分母在更小普通邻域上为单位。将 Proposition~\ref{fnd:freegaussdifferentialindependence} 应用于解析纤维，便知 $(df_1)(y),\ldots,(df_m)(y)$ 在相对微分纤维中线性无关。解析微分的纤维基变换将其与 $\Omega^{\rm an}_{Y/X}\otimes\mathcal H(y)$ 中的相应子空间识别。后者维数为 $n$，且由局部解析函数的微分生成，所以可添入 $f_{m+1},\ldots,f_n$，使这些微分构成一组基。}

\bi{The map $g=(f_1,\ldots,f_n):Y\to\mathbb A_X^{n,\mathrm{an}}$ is quasi-smooth of relative dimension zero at $y$. Explicitly, take an ordinary Jacobian presentation for $Y/X$, lift the finitely many $f_i$ to its ambient affinoid algebra using the surjective closed-immersion presentation, and adjoin the equations $Z_i-f_i$ for the graph of $g$. Independence of the displayed differential basis says exactly that the original relation rows together with these $n$ new rows have full rank; Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion} gives the assertion. Because $Y\to X$ is inner at $y$, the interior composition formula makes $g$ inner there. Its fibre through $y$ is isolated by Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasismoothrankzeroisolated}; it is ordinarily flat by the Jacobian theorem and its relative differential module vanishes. Proposition~\ref{fnd:localfiniteetalecriterion} therefore makes $g$ \'etale near $y$.}
{映射 $g=(f_1,\ldots,f_n):Y\to\mathbb A_X^{n,\mathrm{an}}$ 在 $y$ 处准光滑且相对维数为零。具体地，取 $Y/X$ 的普通 Jacobian 表示，由闭浸入表示的满射性把有限个 $f_i$ 提升到环境 affinoid 代数中，再为 $g$ 的图添入方程 $Z_i-f_i$。上述微分基的独立性，恰说明原关系的行与这 $n$ 个新行共同满秩；由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion} 得到断言。由于 $Y\to X$ 在 $y$ 处为内态射，内部复合公式说明 $g$ 也在该点为内态射。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasismoothrankzeroisolated}，$y$ 在其纤维中孤立；Jacobian 定理给出普通平坦性，而相对微分模为零。因此 Proposition~\ref{fnd:localfiniteetalecriterion} 使 $g$ 在 $y$ 附近为 \'etale。}

\bi{Put $z=g(y)$. The completed field $\mathcal H(z)$ contains $\mathcal H(x)$ and the first $m$ coordinate values. Lemma~\ref{fnd:freegausscoordinatechange} says that these generate all of $\mathcal H(y)$ as a complete field, so $\mathcal H(z)=\mathcal H(y)$. Corollary~\ref{fnd:etalesameresiduelocalisomorphism} identifies an ordinary neighborhood of $y$ with an ordinary open subset $W\subset\mathbb A_X^{n,\mathrm{an}}$. Let $p$ be projection onto the first $m$ coordinates and put $a=p(z)$. Freeness implies that $a$ is the Gauss point of polyradius $r$ over $x$, and $z$ is rational over $\mathcal H(a)$.}
{置 $z=g(y)$。完备域 $\mathcal H(z)$ 包含 $\mathcal H(x)$ 及前 $m$ 个坐标值；Lemma~\ref{fnd:freegausscoordinatechange} 说明它们生成整个完备域 $\mathcal H(y)$，故 $\mathcal H(z)=\mathcal H(y)$。由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:etalesameresiduelocalisomorphism}，$y$ 的普通邻域与普通开集 $W\subset\mathbb A_X^{n,\mathrm{an}}$ 同构。令 $p$ 为前 $m$ 个坐标的投影，$a=p(z)$。自由性说明 $a$ 是 $x$ 上多半径 $r$ 的 Gauss 点，且 $z$ 在 $\mathcal H(a)$ 上有理。}

\bi{The fibre open set $W_a$ contains a rational point whose last $n-m$ coordinates lie in the dense subfield $\kappa(a)\subset\mathcal H(a)$, by preserving the finitely many polynomial-value conditions of a neighborhood of $z$. Extend these coordinates to germs on the good space $\mathbb A_X^{m,\mathrm{an}}$ and translate the last coordinates over an ordinary neighborhood of $a$. The translated $W$ contains the relative fibre origin. Lemma~\ref{fnd:freegaussproductneighborhood} with no free coordinates, applied over this new base, supplies $U_1\times_K\Delta\subset W$ with $U_1$ an ordinary neighborhood of $a$. Apply the same lemma to $\mathbb A_X^{m,\mathrm{an}}\to X$ at the free Gauss point $a$: inside $U_1$ it gives $U\times_KD$. Restrict the first product to this second one, and undo the coordinate translation and the local \'etale isomorphism. The result is the stated product patch.}
{通过保持 $z$ 的某邻域中的有限个多项式值条件，纤维开集 $W_a$ 含有一个有理点，其后 $n-m$ 个坐标属于稠密子域 $\kappa(a)\subset\mathcal H(a)$。将这些坐标延拓为 good 空间 $\mathbb A_X^{m,\mathrm{an}}$ 上的芽，并在 $a$ 的普通邻域上平移后面的坐标。平移后的 $W$ 包含相对纤维原点。在这个新底上应用没有自由坐标的 Lemma~\ref{fnd:freegaussproductneighborhood}，得到 $U_1\times_K\Delta\subset W$，其中 $U_1$ 是 $a$ 的普通邻域。再在自由 Gauss 点 $a$ 处对 $\mathbb A_X^{m,\mathrm{an}}\to X$ 应用同一引理，便在 $U_1$ 中得到 $U\times_KD$。将前一乘积限制到后一乘积上，再撤销坐标平移及局部 \'etale 同构，即得所述乘积开片。}
\end{proof}
