Completion of a projective model

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 654

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Completion of a projective model}
{射影模型的完备化}]
\label{cand:projectiveformalcompletion}
\bi{Let $\mathcal C$ be a projective, finitely presented, flat
$O$-scheme, and let $\widehat{\mathcal C}$ be its
$\pi$-adic formal completion. Then $\widehat{\mathcal C}$
is an admissible formal model of $(\mathcal C_K)^{\an}$:
there is a canonical analytic isomorphism}
{设 $\mathcal C$ 为射影、有限表示、平坦的 $O$-概形，
以 $\widehat{\mathcal C}$ 记其 $\pi$-进形式完备化。
则 $\widehat{\mathcal C}$ 是 $(\mathcal C_K)^{\an}$
的 admissible 形式模型，即存在典范解析同构}
\[
                 (\widehat{\mathcal C})_\eta
                       \simeq(\mathcal C_K)^{\an}.
\]
\bi{For a closed immersion $\mathcal C\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^N_O$,
the generic fibres of the completed standard affine charts are
the domains}
{对闭浸入 $\mathcal C\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^N_O$，
标准仿射坐标片完备化的泛纤维为下列解析域：}
\[
 V_j=\{x\in(\mathcal C_K)^{\an}:
          |X_i/X_j|(x)\leq1\text{ for every }i\ne j\}.
\]
\bi{Their overlap maps are the usual coordinate-ratio maps.}
{其重叠映射就是通常的坐标比值映射。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose the indicated projective embedding and write
$\mathcal U_j=\mathcal C\cap D_+(X_j)=\operatorname{Spec}B_j$.
These are finitely presented flat affine $O$-schemes:
flatness and local finite presentation restrict to open
subschemes, and each $\mathcal U_j$ is affine and
quasi-compact. Its coordinate algebra is generated by
the ratios $T_i=X_i/X_j$. A finite-type presentation using
these chosen generators has finitely generated kernel.
Indeed, if $B_j=O[Z]/(F)$ is any finite presentation, express
$Z=p(T)$ and $T=q(Z)$ in $B_j$; the finitely many
relations $F(p(T))$ and $T-q(p(T))$ generate the kernel,
by substituting in a polynomial relation.}
{选择所列射影嵌入，并记
$\mathcal U_j=\mathcal C\cap D_+(X_j)=\operatorname{Spec}B_j$。
这些是有限表示的平坦仿射 $O$-概形：平坦性与局部有限表示
限制到开子概形，且每个 $\mathcal U_j$ 仿射、拟紧。
其坐标代数由比值 $T_i=X_i/X_j$ 生成。采用这些指定生成元
的有限型表示具有有限生成的核。事实上，若
$B_j=O[Z]/(F)$ 是任一个有限表示，在 $B_j$ 中写
$Z=p(T)$、$T=q(Z)$；对任意多项式关系作代入，即知有限个
关系 $F(p(T))$、$T-q(p(T))$ 生成该核。}

\bi{By Lemma~\ref{cand:affineflatformalcompletion},
each $\widehat B_j$ is admissible. On
$\mathcal U_j\cap\mathcal U_\ell=D(X_\ell/X_j)$,
the same lemma identifies the completed overlap with
the principal formal open of $\operatorname{Spf}\widehat B_j$.
The reduction formula modulo every $\pi^m$ identifies
these overlaps with the restrictions of
$\mathcal C\times_O O/\pi^m$.
Their inverse limits therefore glue to exactly
$\widehat{\mathcal C}$, proving its admissibility.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{cand:affineflatformalcompletion}，
每个 $\widehat B_j$ 都是 admissible 代数。在
$\mathcal U_j\cap\mathcal U_\ell=D(X_\ell/X_j)$ 上，
同一引理将重叠部分的完备化识别为
$\operatorname{Spf}\widehat B_j$ 的主形式开集。
模每个 $\pi^m$ 的约化公式将这些重叠识别为
$\mathcal C\times_O O/\pi^m$ 的限制，故其逆极限粘合后
恰为 $\widehat{\mathcal C}$，证明它 admissible。}

\bi{The generic fibre of the $j$th completed chart is,
by that lemma and Theorem~\ref{fnd:analytificationfiniteclosed},
the actual closed-unit-coordinate domain $V_j$ of
$(\mathcal C_K)^{\an}$, with the same quotient ideal.
No reduction of that ideal is taken. The completed overlap
adds the bound $|X_j/X_\ell|\leq1$ to
$|X_\ell/X_j|\leq1$, hence gives the domain
$|X_\ell/X_j|=1$ inside $V_j$.
This is exactly $V_j\cap V_\ell$; the remaining coordinates
change by division by $X_\ell/X_j$.
Thus the coordinate identifications agree as bounded
affinoid algebra homomorphisms on every overlap.}
{由该引理与 Theorem~\ref{fnd:analytificationfiniteclosed}，
第 $j$ 个完备化坐标片的泛纤维，是
$(\mathcal C_K)^{\an}$ 中实际的闭单位坐标域 $V_j$，
且具有同一个商理想；此处不对该理想取根。
完备化的重叠在 $|X_\ell/X_j|\leq1$ 之外再加入
$|X_j/X_\ell|\leq1$，故在 $V_j$ 内给出
$|X_\ell/X_j|=1$ 域。这恰好是 $V_j\cap V_\ell$；
其余坐标通过除以 $X_\ell/X_j$ 变换。
因此各坐标识别在每个重叠上作为有界 affinoid 代数同态相同。}

\bi{The domains $V_j$ cover $(\mathcal C_K)^{\an}$:
at any analytic point choose a homogeneous coordinate of
maximal absolute value. This is precisely the projective
affinoid covering in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationprojectivecompact}.
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful} constructs
the generic fibre by gluing the completed affine charts
along the overlaps just identified. The same domains and
the same transition maps consequently give the asserted
analytic isomorphism. On every chart its map is induced
by $B_j\to\widehat B_j[1/\pi]$, so these local maps define
the natural comparison map to $(\mathcal C_K)^{\an}$.
Its definition on affine restrictions is independent of
the projective embedding, which proves canonicity.}
{解析域 $V_j$ 覆盖 $(\mathcal C_K)^{\an}$：
在任意解析点选择绝对值最大的齐次坐标即可。这正是
Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationprojectivecompact}
中的射影 affinoid 覆盖。
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}
通过沿已识别的重叠粘合这些完备仿射坐标片，构造泛纤维。
因此两边由同一批解析域及同一批过渡映射组成，得到所需
解析同构。在每个坐标片上，该映射由
$B_j\to\widehat B_j[1/\pi]$ 诱导，所以这些局部映射给出
到 $(\mathcal C_K)^{\an}$ 的自然比较映射。
它在仿射限制上的定义不依赖射影嵌入，因而此同构典范。}
\end{proof}
