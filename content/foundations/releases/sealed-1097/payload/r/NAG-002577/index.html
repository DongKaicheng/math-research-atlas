Global Stokes with interior compact support

Chapter 8 supplement: Interior Stokes with coherent coefficients

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1782

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Global Stokes with interior compact support]
\label{f7gg:global}
Let \(X\) be a good Hausdorff analytic space, pure of analytic
dimension \(d\geq1\), and let \(\mathcal F\) be coherent on \(X_G\).
For ordinary smooth moment sections
\[
 \beta\in\A_c^{d-1,d}(X),\qquad
 \gamma\in\A_c^{d,d-1}(X)
\]
with
\[
 \supp(\beta),\ \supp(\gamma)\ \subseteq\Int(X/K),
\]
one has
\[
       \int_{X,\mathcal F}d'\beta=0,
       \qquad
       \int_{X,\mathcal F}d''\gamma=0.
\]
In particular the assertion holds for every such compactly
supported section if \(\partial_KX=\varnothing\).
No global paracompactness, reducedness, strictness, or connected-fiber
hypothesis is added.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{f7gg:global}]
Put \(S=\supp(\beta)\) and apply
Proposition~\ref{f7gg:decomposition}. On each nonempty intersection
\(W_I\), the resulting \(\theta_I=t_{F_I}^*\alpha_I\) has compact
support inside both the ordinary interior in \(X\) and the absolute
analytic interior of \(W_I\). Locality keeps the support of
\(d'\theta_I\) in the same collar. All sums are finite. The
commutation in Lemma~\ref{f7gg:zeroextension} gives
\[
 d'\beta=\sum_{I\ne\varnothing}(-1)^{|I|+1}e_!(d'\theta_I).
\]
Let \(M_I=\Gamma(W_I,\mathcal F|_{(W_I)_G})\).
The affinoid coherent-module equivalence
\src{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb} makes this a finite module
with its actual restriction interpretation.
Each \(W_I\) is a compact carrier for its extended derivative.
The compact-carrier and single-chart comparisons in
\src{v037:forms:compactintegral}, with these same restricted
coefficients, give
\[
 \int_{X,\mathcal F}d'\beta
   =\sum_{I\ne\varnothing}(-1)^{|I|+1}
          \int_{W_I,\widetilde M_I}d'\theta_I
   =\sum_{I\ne\varnothing}(-1)^{|I|+1}
                          \I_{M_I,F_I}(d'\alpha_I).
\]
Every summand is zero by Theorem~\ref{f7v38:annihilation}
(\(\mathrm{AFF}\)). The compact support of \(\theta_I\) in
\(\Int(W_I/K)\) gives an ordinary zero neighborhood of the
\emph{actual absolute analytic boundary} of \(W_I\).
Here \(W_I\) is pure of dimension \(d\), \(F_I\) is the restricted
unit tuple, and \(\alpha_I\) is defined near its actual compact
moment image. All generic module lengths are retained.

Put \(P=1-\prod_i(1-b_i)\). If \(b_I=\prod_{i\in I}b_i\),
the scalar product rule gives
\[
 d'(b_I\beta)=d'b_I\wedge\beta+b_I\,d'\beta .
\]
The scalar \(b_I\) has degree zero, so the sign is plus.
Summing with \((-1)^{|I|+1}\) gives
\(d'P\wedge\beta+P\,d'\beta\).
Near \(S\), \(P=1\) and \(d'P=0\); off \(S\), both
\(\beta\) and \(d'\beta\) are locally zero.
This is exactly \(d'\beta\), as used above.
The same check applies to \(d''\).

For the second assertion use the frozen convention
\src{v037:integration:conventions}:
all one-form generators anticommute, \(J\) exchanges
the two copies and fixes functions, and \(d''=Jd'J\).
The positive interleaved top generator
\[
        \omega_N=d'x_1\wedge d''x_1\wedge\cdots
                            \wedge d'x_d\wedge d''x_d
\]
satisfies \(J\omega_N=(-1)^d\omega_N\).
On each top cell \(J\) changes the coefficient in this same
generator by \((-1)^d\) and leaves its weight and lattice measure
fixed. Thus the finite-chart integral satisfies
\(\int J\zeta=(-1)^d\int\zeta\) for top forms \(\zeta\).
The section \(J\gamma\) has bidegree \((d-1,d)\)
and the same support as \(\gamma\).
The proved assertion yields
\[
 0=\int_{X,\mathcal F}d'(J\gamma)
   =\int_{X,\mathcal F}J(d''\gamma)
   =(-1)^d\int_{X,\mathcal F}d''\gamma.
\]
This proves the \(d''\) formula with the exact frozen sign.
\end{proof}
