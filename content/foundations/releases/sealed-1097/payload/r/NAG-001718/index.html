Polynomial-to-Tate flatness in arbitrary radii

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 292

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Polynomial-to-Tate flatness in arbitrary radii}{任意半径下多项式到 Tate 代数的平坦性}]
\label{fnd:polynomialtateflatness}
\bi{For every complete non-Archimedean field $K$ and every positive polyradius $r$, the ring map}
{对每个完备非阿基米德域 $K$ 及正多半径 $r$，环映射}
\[
 K[T_1,\ldots,T_n]\longrightarrow K\{r^{-1}T\}
\]
\bi{is flat. Consequently, if $R=K[T]/I$ and $A_r=K\{r^{-1}T\}/I K\{r^{-1}T\}$, then $R\to A_r$ is flat.}
{平坦。因此，若 $R=K[T]/I$ 且 $A_r=K\{r^{-1}T\}/I K\{r^{-1}T\}$，则 $R\to A_r$ 平坦。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First take $r=(1,\ldots,1)$ and write $P=K[T]$, $C=K\langle T\rangle$. For a maximal ideal $\mathfrak m\subset C$, Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} gives the finite complete residue field $E=C/\mathfrak m$. Let $a_i\in E$ be the images of $T_i$; contractivity gives $|a_i|\le1$. The scalar extension $C_E=E\langle T\rangle$ is $C\otimes_K E$ as an ordinary finite $C$-module, by Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedscalartensor} and a finite $K$-basis of $E$. It is free over $C$. Evaluation at $a$ has maximal kernel $\mathfrak n=(T_i-a_i)$ and restricts to the given quotient $C\to E$, so $\mathfrak n\cap C=\mathfrak m$.}
{先取 $r=(1,\ldots,1)$，记 $P=K[T]$、$C=K\langle T\rangle$。对极大理想 $\mathfrak m\subset C$，Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} 给出有限完备剩余域 $E=C/\mathfrak m$。令 $a_i\in E$ 为 $T_i$ 的像；由压缩性，$|a_i|\le1$。利用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedscalartensor} 及 $E$ 的有限 $K$-基，扩标量 $C_E=E\langle T\rangle$ 作为普通有限 $C$-模就是 $C\otimes_K E$，因此在 $C$ 上自由。在 $a$ 处取值的极大核为 $\mathfrak n=(T_i-a_i)$，其限制就是给定商映射 $C\to E$，故 $\mathfrak n\cap C=\mathfrak m$。}

\bi{Localization of a flat module is flat, since fractions preserve exact sequences: a fraction maps to zero precisely when some denominator annihilates its numerator. Hence $C_{\mathfrak m}\to(C_E)_{\mathfrak n}$ is flat, and it is faithfully flat because it is local. Lemma~\ref{fnd:tatepolynomiallocalcompletion} says that $(C_E)_{\mathfrak n}$ is flat over $E[T]_{(T-a)}$, and therefore over $P$: the polynomial scalar extension is free and the intervening localization is flat. Applying the kernel descent argument of that lemma to $C_{\mathfrak m}\to(C_E)_{\mathfrak n}$ proves that $C_{\mathfrak m}$ is flat over $P$. For a finitely generated ideal $J\subset P$, the kernel of $J\otimes_P C\to C$ vanishes after localization at every maximal ideal of $C$. It is therefore zero by \cite[Tag 00HN, Lemma~10.23.1(2)]{StacksAlg}. The finite-ideal criterion proves $P\to C$ flat.}
{平坦模的局部化仍平坦，因为分式运算保持正合列：一个分式映为零，当且仅当某个分母消去其分子。因此 $C_{\mathfrak m}\to(C_E)_{\mathfrak n}$ 平坦；又因为它是局部映射，所以忠实平坦。Lemma~\ref{fnd:tatepolynomiallocalcompletion} 说明 $(C_E)_{\mathfrak n}$ 在 $E[T]_{(T-a)}$ 上平坦，故在 $P$ 上平坦，因为多项式扩标量自由，而中间局部化平坦。对 $C_{\mathfrak m}\to(C_E)_{\mathfrak n}$ 使用前一引理中的核下降论证，得到 $C_{\mathfrak m}$ 在 $P$ 上平坦。对有限生成理想 $J\subset P$，$J\otimes_P C\to C$ 的核在 $C$ 的每个极大理想处局部化后均为零，故由 \cite[Tag 00HN, Lemma~10.23.1(2)]{StacksAlg} 为零。有限理想判据证明 $P\to C$ 平坦。}

\bi{For arbitrary $r$, the multiplicative Gauss norm of Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm} extends to the fraction field of $K\{r^{-1}S\}$. Let $L$ be its completion. It contains elements $s_i$ with $|s_i|=r_i$. By scalar extension and rescaling $T_i=s_iV_i$,}
{对任意 $r$，Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm} 的乘性 Gauss 范数延拓到 $K\{r^{-1}S\}$ 的分式域。令 $L$ 为其完备化，则它包含满足 $|s_i|=r_i$ 的元素 $s_i$。扩标量并作缩放 $T_i=s_iV_i$，得到}
\[
 K\{r^{-1}T\}\widehat\otimes_K L
     \simeq L\{r^{-1}T\}\simeq L\langle V\rangle .
\]
\bi{The first map from $K\{r^{-1}T\}$ is faithfully flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalscalarfaithful}. The strict case proves that the right side is flat over $L[T]$, hence over $K[T]$ by the free scalar extension. The same kernel descent argument proves flatness before extension. Finally $A_r=K\{r^{-1}T\}\otimes_{K[T]}R$ as ordinary algebras. Tensoring a short exact sequence of $R$-modules through this equality proves the last assertion.}
{从 $K\{r^{-1}T\}$ 出发的第一个映射由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalscalarfaithful} 忠实平坦。Strict 情形证明右边在 $L[T]$ 上平坦，再由自由扩标量，得到在 $K[T]$ 上平坦。同样的核下降论证证明扩域前的平坦性。最后，作为普通代数有 $A_r=K\{r^{-1}T\}\otimes_{K[T]}R$。利用此等式对 $R$-模短正合列张量，就得到最后一项。}
\end{proof}
