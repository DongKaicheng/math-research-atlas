A finite presentation near a compact projected fibre

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1011

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[A finite presentation near a compact projected fibre]
\label{v037:analytic:finitepolyhedralpatching}
Let $S\subset\mathbb R^n$ be compact, let
$a:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ be an integral linear map, and
let $w\in\mathbb R^m$. Suppose that, at each
$u\in S\cap a^{-1}(w)$, there are a neighbourhood $V_u$ and
a finite pure $d$-dimensional rational polyhedral fan $C_u$
such that
\[
                 S\cap V_u=(u+|C_u|)\cap V_u.
\]
There are an open convex neighbourhood $W$ of $w$ and a
finite pure $d$-dimensional integral affine polyhedral complex
$\mathcal D$ such that
\[
       |\mathcal D|\subset S,\qquad
       S\cap a^{-1}(W)=|\mathcal D|\cap a^{-1}(W).
\]
If the fibre is empty, $\mathcal D$ may be empty.
The neighbourhood $W$ may be required to lie in any prescribed
neighbourhood of $w$.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
For each $u$ in the compact fibre, take a closed rectangular
box $P_u$ centred at $u$, contained in $V_u$, and with $u$ in
its interior. The interiors of finitely many such boxes
$P_{u_1},\ldots,P_{u_s}$ cover the fibre. Put
\[
 T=\bigcup_{j=1}^s\bigl((u_j+|C_{u_j}|)\cap P_{u_j}\bigr)
   =\bigcup_{j=1}^s(S\cap P_{u_j}).
\]
For every $d$-cone $\sigma$ of $C_{u_j}$, the polytope
$(u_j+\sigma)\cap P_{u_j}$ has dimension $d$: the box contains
a neighbourhood of its cone vertex. Every smaller-dimensional
cone is a face of a $d$-cone. Thus $T$ is a finite union of
$d$-dimensional integral affine polytopes and their faces.
Cut these polytopes by all the finitely many hyperplanes in
their inequality and affine-hull presentations. Their cells
and faces form a finite common refinement $\mathcal D$ with
support $T$. Each cell is contained in the closure of a
$d$-cell. Indeed, in any original $d$-polytope its relative
interior is dense, and the finitely many proper cutting
hyperplanes have empty relative interior. Hence this complex
is pure of dimension $d$. This is also the explicit refinement
construction of Lemma~\ref{cand:polyhedralrefinementproduct}.

The set
\[
            E=S\setminus\bigcup_{j=1}^s\operatorname{int}(P_{u_j})
\]
is compact and has no point over $w$. Its image $a(E)$ is
compact and misses $w$. Choose a sufficiently small open
convex neighbourhood $W$ of $w$ disjoint from that image.
Every point of $S\cap a^{-1}(W)$ belongs to one of the box
interiors, hence to $T$. Conversely $T\subset S$, proving
both asserted inclusions. If the fibre is empty, apply the
same compactness argument to $E=S$ and take the empty complex.
Shrinking $W$ proves the last assertion.
\end{proof}
