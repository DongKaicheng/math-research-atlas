Weighted component formula for native integrals; F8CR-N

I Descent of the polarized mixed tower

Open in the English reader · PDF page 2070

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Weighted component formula for native integrals; F8CR-N]
\label{prop:native}
For every ordinary compact \((n,n)\)-form \(\omega\) on the affinoid
\(X\) of \eqref{eq:components},
\begin{equation}\label{fnd:nag002967:weighted-native-integrals}
 \int_X\omega=\sum_{i=1}^s m_i\int_{X_i}i_i^*\omega.
\end{equation}
The same formula holds with \(\omega\) replaced by \(\phi\omega\)
for every ordinary smooth scalar \(\phi\) on \(X\). No boundary
support assumption, zero-space purity, or scalar PL is used.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Here is the restriction check needed to keep the original \(m_i\)
when different moment representatives are used. Let \(V=\cM(B)\)
be an actual affinoid domain of \(X\). The map \(A\to B\) is flat
by \hyperref[fnd:nag001832:ordinary-single-domain-flatness]{ordinary single-domain flatness}. If \(\mathfrak r\in\Min B\), then
\(\mathfrak q=\mathfrak r\cap A\) is minimal in \(A\): flat going
down (the already registered algebraic input \href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00HS}{Stacks 00HS}) would
otherwise give a prime properly below \(\mathfrak r\). The local
flat map \(A_{\mathfrak q}\to B_{\mathfrak r}\) has Artinian source
and target. Tensor a composition series of \(A_{\mathfrak q}\) with
\(B_{\mathfrak r}\). Exactness gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:restriction-length}
 \length_{B_{\mathfrak r}}B_{\mathfrak r}
 =m_{\mathfrak q}\,
   \length_{B_{\mathfrak r}}(B_{\mathfrak r}/\mathfrak qB_{\mathfrak r}).
\end{equation}
For every other \(\mathfrak q_i\in\Min A\), choose an element of
\(\mathfrak q_i\setminus\mathfrak q\). It is invertible in
\(B_{\mathfrak r}\), so
\((B/\mathfrak q_iB)_{\mathfrak r}=0\). Consequently
\eqref{eq:restriction-length} is exactly
\[
 \length_{B_{\mathfrak r}}B_{\mathfrak r}
 =\sum_i m_i\length_{B_{\mathfrak r}}
                         (B/\mathfrak q_iB)_{\mathfrak r}.
\]
In particular no assertion that a flat fibre has length one has
entered this calculation.

Apply the generic finite-module weight formula \hyperref[v037:degree:moduleweight]{NAG-002362}
to these modules on \(V\), and then its global chain construction
\hyperref[fnd:nag002405:global-finite-module-chain-weights]{closed finite-module chain}. For any chosen unit tuple \(g\) on \(V\), it gives
\[
 \mathcal T_g(B)=\sum_i m_i\mathcal T_g(B/\mathfrak q_iB).
\]
Actual domain base change \hyperref[fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange]{NAG-001846} and finite tensor
comparison \hyperref[fnd:generalizedfinitetensor]{NAG-001565} identify
\(\cM(B/\mathfrak q_iB)\) with \(V\times_X X_i\), retaining its
full structure. This is an actual domain of \(X_i\), hence pure of
dimension \(n\) when nonempty by \hyperref[v036:analytic:domainpurity]{NAG-002293}. The \hyperref[v037:integration:compactfinitepushforward]{compact finite-pushforward formula}, on this single actual affinoid domain and for this closed immersion and the identity
map on the common moment coordinates, identifies its integral with
the target finite-module integral. There is no lattice or residue
factor in addition to the recorded chain weights.

Represent \(\omega\) on a finite actual affinoid cover of a compact
neighbourhood of its support. Use the same cover pulled back to each
\(X_i\), discarding empty pieces. All actual intersections have the
same calculation. The finite inclusion--exclusion integral
\hyperref[v037:integration:compactintegral]{NAG-002416}, followed by \hyperref[v037:forms:compactintegral]{NAG-002425}, gives
\eqref{fnd:nag002967:weighted-native-integrals}. No unit tuple is extended beyond its chart;
joint tuples and their projection pullbacks are used only on actual
overlaps. The argument applies verbatim to the compact form
\(\phi\omega\).
\end{proof}
