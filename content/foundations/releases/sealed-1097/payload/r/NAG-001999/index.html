Product neighborhoods of free Gauss points

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 451

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Product neighborhoods of free Gauss points}{自由 Gauss 点的乘积邻域}]
\label{fnd:freegaussproductneighborhood}
\bi{Let $X$ be good, $x\in X$, and let $r=(r_1,\ldots,r_m)$ be $\mathcal H(x)$-free, with $0\leq m\leq n$. In $\mathbb A_X^{n,\an}$ let $\xi$ be the fibre point that is Gauss of radii $r$ in the first $m$ coordinates and has its remaining coordinates zero. Every ordinary neighborhood $W$ of $\xi$ contains a product}
{设 $X$ good，$x\in X$，$r=(r_1,\ldots,r_m)$ 是 $\mathcal H(x)$-自由多半径，其中 $0\leq m\leq n$。在 $\mathbb A_X^{n,\an}$ 中，令 $\xi$ 为纤维上的点，其前 $m$ 个坐标是半径 $r$ 的 Gauss 点，其余坐标为零。$\xi$ 的每个普通邻域 $W$ 都包含一个乘积}
\[
                 U\times_K D_1\times_K\cdots\times_K D_n
\]
\bi{that contains $\xi$, where $U$ is an ordinary neighborhood of $x$, the $D_i$ for $i\leq m$ are open annuli around radius $r_i$, and the remaining $D_i$ are open discs centered at zero.}
{且该乘积包含 $\xi$；这里 $U$ 是 $x$ 的普通邻域，$i\leq m$ 时 $D_i$ 是包含半径 $r_i$ 的开圆环，其余 $D_i$ 是以零为中心的开圆盘。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Take an ordinary affinoid neighborhood $X_0=\mathcal M(A)$ of $x$. The topology on relative affine space is generated locally by finitely many conditions $|P_j|\in I_j$ with $P_j\in A[T_1,\ldots,T_n]$ and open intervals $I_j\subset\mathbb R_{\geq0}$. This follows directly on each closed polydisc from polynomial density in its Tate algebra: approximate a finite list of functions sufficiently closely to preserve the strict open inequalities at the point. Choose such conditions defining a neighborhood contained in $W$.}
{取 $x$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域 $X_0=\mathcal M(A)$。相对仿射空间的拓扑局部由有限个条件 $|P_j|\in I_j$ 生成，其中 $P_j\in A[T_1,\ldots,T_n]$，$I_j\subset\mathbb R_{\geq0}$ 为开区间。这可在每个闭多圆盘上直接由多项式在 Tate 代数中的稠密性得到：把有限组函数充分逼近，使该点处的严格开不等式仍成立。选择这样的有限组条件，使所得邻域包含于 $W$。}

\bi{Write $T=(T',T'')$, separating the first $m$ coordinates. In each specialization $P_j(T',0)$, freeness makes the values of distinct nonzero monomials at $\xi$ distinct. If the specialization is nonzero at $\xi$, it therefore has a unique dominant monomial. The finitely many strict inequalities expressing its dominance, and the condition that its value lies in $I_j$, persist when the coefficients vary over a sufficiently small ordinary neighborhood of $x$ and the values $|T_i|$ vary in sufficiently small intervals around $r_i$. Every term divisible by a coordinate of $T''$ can simultaneously be made strictly smaller by taking those coordinates in sufficiently small zero-centered discs; there are only finitely many such terms. If the specialization vanishes at $\xi$, all its coefficients have value zero at $x$; bound them on a small ordinary base neighborhood and make every term have value in a sufficiently small interval $[0,\varepsilon)$ contained in $I_j$. Intersecting these finitely many coefficient neighborhoods and choosing the annulus and disc radii proves the assertion.}
{写 $T=(T',T'')$，分离前 $m$ 个坐标。在每个特化 $P_j(T',0)$ 中，自由性使不同非零单项式在 $\xi$ 处的值两两不同。因此，只要此特化在 $\xi$ 处非零，就有唯一占优单项式。表达其占优性的有限组严格不等式，以及其值属于 $I_j$ 的条件，在系数于 $x$ 的充分小普通邻域中变化、各 $|T_i|$ 于 $r_i$ 附近充分小的区间内变化时仍成立。令 $T''$ 各坐标位于充分小的零心圆盘中，可同时使所有含这些坐标的项严格较小，因为这样的项只有有限个。若特化在 $\xi$ 处为零，则其全部系数在 $x$ 处为零；在小的普通底邻域上控制其值，使每个项都属于包含于 $I_j$ 的充分小区间 $[0,\varepsilon)$。交合这有限组系数邻域，并选择相应圆环与圆盘半径，即得结论。}
\end{proof}
