Slopes at separable residual points

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 689

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Slopes at separable residual points}
{可分剩余点处的斜率}]
\label{cand:separableresidualbranchslope}
\bi{Keep the curve model and component of
Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope}. Let $p$ be a closed point
of $C^\nu$ with $\kappa(p)/k$ separable. Choose a finite
Galois residue-field lift $L/K$ which makes the points above $p$
rational and supplies pointed charts at any node under $p$.
Let $p'$ lie above $p$ on the normalization of a component $C'$
of the extended special fibre, let $x'$ be its component point,
and let $\gamma_{p'}$ be an outgoing Gauss segment at $x'$.
Writing $\varpi:X_L\to X$ for projection, the projected
segment $\gamma=\varpi\circ\gamma_{p'}$ is nonconstant and
injective on a sufficiently short initial interval. For every
analytic germ $f$ at $x$ with $f(x)\ne0$,}
{保持 Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope} 中的曲线模型与
分量。设 $p$ 为 $C^\nu$ 的闭点，且 $\kappa(p)/k$ 可分。
选择有限 Galois 剩余域提升 $L/K$，使 $p$ 上方的点均为
有理点，并在 $p$ 下方可能的节点处给出带点坐标片。设 $p'$
位于扩张特殊纤维的一个分量 $C'$ 的正规化上且映到 $p$；
以 $x'$ 表示该分量点，以 $\gamma_{p'}$ 表示其出射 Gauss
线段。令 $\varpi:X_L\to X$ 为投影，则投影线段
$\gamma=\varpi\circ\gamma_{p'}$ 在某个充分短的初始区间
上非平凡且单射。对 $x$ 处满足 $f(x)\ne0$ 的任意解析
函数芽 $f$，都有}
\[
 -\log|f(\gamma(\tau))|
  =-\log|f(x)|+\operatorname{ord}_{p}(\widetilde f_x)\,\tau
 \qquad(0\leq\tau\ll1).
\]
\bi{The coefficient on the right is independent of $L$, of
$p'$, and of the pointed chart. The parameter uses the original
absolute value on $K$, extended to $L$; no factor $[L:K]$
occurs in this slope.}
{右边的系数与 $L$、$p'$ 及带点坐标片的选择无关。参数
使用 $K$ 上原绝对值在 $L$ 上的延拓；此斜率中不出现
$[L:K]$ 因子。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Take a finite Galois residue extension containing the
normal closure of $\kappa(p)/k$. If $p$ lies over a node,
enlarge it to include the finite residue extension used in
Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting}. Its lift exists
by Lemma~\ref{cand:galoisresidueextensionlift}. The algebraic
and formal base changes agree with the analytic scalar extension
by Lemma~\ref{cand:unramifiedmodelbasechange}. Their special
fibres are obtained by tensoring with $l=\widetilde L$, and
remain reduced.}
{取包含 $\kappa(p)/k$ 正规闭包的有限 Galois 剩余域扩张。
若 $p$ 位于节点之上，再扩大它，使其包含
Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting} 所用的有限
剩余域扩张。由 Lemma~\ref{cand:galoisresidueextensionlift}，
该扩张具有所需提升。Lemma~\ref{cand:unramifiedmodelbasechange}
保证代数与形式基变换同解析系数扩张相符；其特殊纤维由与
$l=\widetilde L$ 张量得到，且仍为约化的。}

\bi{The finite separable morphism
$\operatorname{Spec}l\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ is \'etale.
By \cite[Tag 03GV]{StacksAlg}, the normalization of the
extended residual curve is the base change of the original
normalization. The local map at $p'$ over $p$ is therefore
\'etale. By \cite[Tag 02GL]{StacksAlg}, its maximal ideal
is generated by the image of the maximal ideal at $p$. Both
local rings are discrete valuation rings, by
\cite[Tag 0B8Y]{StacksAlg}. Consequently}
{有限可分态射
$\operatorname{Spec}l\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ 为 \'etale。
由 \cite[Tag 03GV]{StacksAlg}，扩张后剩余曲线的正规化
正是原正规化的基变换。因此，$p'$ 到 $p$ 的局部同态为
\'etale；由 \cite[Tag 02GL]{StacksAlg}，其极大理想由 $p$
处极大理想的像生成。由 \cite[Tag 0B8Y]{StacksAlg}，两
局部环均为离散赋值环。因此}
\[
 \operatorname{ord}_{p'}(u|_{C'})
       =\operatorname{ord}_{p}(u)\qquad(u\in k(C)^\times).
\]
\bi{The generic point of $C'$ maps to that of $C$, so
specialization and the uniqueness of component points give
$\varpi(x')=x$. The residue-field isomorphisms in
Proposition~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield} commute
with these maps: they are induced by evaluating integral model
functions and then taking residues. Thus the normalized residue
of the pullback of $f$ is the pullback of $\widetilde f_x$.
Apply Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope} at $x',p'$ and
use the displayed order equality. Evaluation of a pulled-back
function has exactly its original absolute value. This proves
the formula with the stated parameter and coefficient.}
{$C'$ 的泛点映到 $C$ 的泛点，所以特化相容性与分量点
的唯一性给出 $\varpi(x')=x$。
Proposition~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield} 中的
剩余域同构与这些映射相容，因为它们都通过对整模型函数
取值再取剩余类而定义。因此，$f$ 拉回后的归一化剩余类
正是 $\widetilde f_x$ 的拉回。在 $x',p'$ 处应用
Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope}，再使用上述阶数
等式，即得结论。拉回函数的取值具有原函数的同一绝对值，
所以公式确实采用所述参数与系数。}

\bi{To see that the projected path is nonconstant and has no
initial self-identifications, choose a uniformizer
$u\in k(C)^\times$ at $p$. Write $u=\overline P/\overline Q$
in the fraction field of a residual affine chart, with both
numerator and denominator nonzero on $C$, and lift them to
integral functions $P,Q$. Then $r=P/Q$ is analytic near $x$,
$|r(x)|=1$, and its normalized residue is $u$. The already
proved formula for $r$ gives $|r(\gamma(\tau))|=e^{-\tau}$
on a sufficiently short interval. These values distinguish its
parameters.}
{为证明投影路径非平凡且初始部分无自我识别，选择 $p$ 处
的一致化参数 $u\in k(C)^\times$。在剩余仿射坐标片的
分式域中写成 $u=\overline P/\overline Q$，其中分子与
分母在 $C$ 上均非零，再将它们提升为整函数 $P,Q$。于是
$r=P/Q$ 在 $x$ 附近解析，$|r(x)|=1$，且其归一化剩余类
为 $u$。对 $r$ 应用已证公式，得到在充分短区间上
$|r(\gamma(\tau))|=e^{-\tau}$。这些取值区分路径的参数。}
\end{proof}
