Smooth normalized residual components

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 714

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Smooth normalized residual components}
{光滑的正规化剩余分量}]
\label{cand:smoothnodalnormalizationbasechange}
\bi{Let $D$ be a reduced finite-type curve over a field $k$.
Assume that $D$ is smooth away from finitely many split
$k$-rational nodes. Then its normalization $D^\nu$ is
smooth over $k$. For every field extension $l/k$,
$D_l$ is reduced and the finite morphism}
{设 $D$ 为域 $k$ 上的约化有限型曲线。假设 $D$ 除有限个
分裂 $k$-有理节点外均光滑。则其正规化 $D^\nu$ 在 $k$
上光滑。对每个域扩张 $l/k$，$D_l$ 约化，且有限态射}
\[
                    (D^\nu)_l\longrightarrow D_l
\]
\bi{is the normalization. The same statements hold for
each irreducible component $C$ and its normalization $C^\nu$.}
{就是正规化。对每个不可约分量 $C$ 及其正规化 $C^\nu$，
相应断言也成立。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Normalization is finite by \cite[Tag 0BXR]{StacksAlg}.
Away from the nodes it is an isomorphism, because the
smooth local rings are normal. At a node,
Lemma~\ref{cand:normalizedmodelbranches} identifies the
two normalization points with the origins of the two axes
through actual Cartesian normalization squares for the
pointed \textup{\'etale} charts. These points are rational,
and their local rings on the normalized components are
discrete valuation rings. By \cite[Tag 0B8Y]{StacksAlg},
a normal curve is smooth at a closed point whose residue
field is separable over $k$. Thus $D^\nu$ is smooth
at these points as well. This proves smoothness everywhere,
also when the two node branches belong to the same component.}
{由 \cite[Tag 0BXR]{StacksAlg}，正规化有限。在节点以外，
光滑局部环正规，故正规化为同构。在节点处，
Lemma~\ref{cand:normalizedmodelbranches} 利用实际带点
\textup{\'etale} 坐标图的 Cartesian 正规化方块，将两个
正规化点识别为两条坐标轴的原点。这些点有理，且在正规化
分量上的局部环为离散赋值环。由
\cite[Tag 0B8Y]{StacksAlg}，正规曲线在剩余域于 $k$
上可分的闭点处光滑。因此 $D^\nu$ 在这些点也光滑，
从而处处光滑；两条节点分支属于同一分量时也一样。}

\bi{Consequently $(D^\nu)_l$ is normal and reduced for
every $l/k$, by \cite[Tag 056T]{StacksAlg}. On an affine
open of $D$, write its finite normalization as
$R\hookrightarrow S$. The inclusion is injective because
$R$ is reduced. Tensoring with the flat field extension
$l/k$ gives $R\otimes_k l\hookrightarrow S\otimes_k l$.
The right side is reduced, so $D_l$ is reduced. This
argument applies separately to each $C$ as well, since
$C^\nu$ is a component of the smooth scheme $D^\nu$.}
{因此，由 \cite[Tag 056T]{StacksAlg}，对每个 $l/k$，
$(D^\nu)_l$ 正规且约化。在 $D$ 的仿射开集上，将有限
正规化写为 $R\hookrightarrow S$。因为 $R$ 约化，该映射
单射。与平坦域扩张 $l/k$ 张量，得到
$R\otimes_k l\hookrightarrow S\otimes_k l$。右边约化，
故 $D_l$ 约化。因为 $C^\nu$ 是光滑概形 $D^\nu$ 的
一个分量，此论证也分别适用于每个 $C$。}

\bi{The base-changed finite map is an isomorphism off
the inverse image of the finite node set. This open set
contains every generic point of $D_l$: flat going down
\cite[Tag 00HS]{StacksAlg} sends each minimal prime to
a minimal prime of $D$. The same holds for $C_l$.
Thus on each affine chart the two rings have the same
total ring of fractions. The source ring is normal and
integral over the target ring. An element of their common
total fraction ring integral over the target is also
integral over the source, hence belongs to the source;
the reverse inclusion follows from finiteness. Therefore
the source is exactly the integral closure, proving the
asserted normalization comparison.}
{基变换后的有限映射在有限节点集的逆像之外为同构。
这个开集包含 $D_l$ 的每个泛点：平坦映射的 going down
\cite[Tag 00HS]{StacksAlg} 将每个极小素理想映到 $D$
的极小素理想。对 $C_l$ 也如此。因此，每个仿射坐标片
上的两个环具有同一总分式环。源环正规且在目标环上整。
公共总分式环中任何在目标环上整的元素也在源环上整，
故属于源环；反向包含来自有限性。因此源环恰好是整闭包，
证明所述正规化比较。}
\end{proof}
