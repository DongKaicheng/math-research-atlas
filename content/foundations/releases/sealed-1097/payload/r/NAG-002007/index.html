Rank-zero quasi-smooth fibres

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 432

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Rank-zero quasi-smooth fibres}{秩零准光滑纤维}]
\label{fnd:quasismoothrankzeroisolated}
\bi{Let $Z$ be a good analytic space over a complete non-Archimedean field $F$. If $Z\to\mathcal M(F)$ is quasi-smooth at $z$ and $\operatorname{rk}_z\Omega_{Z/F}=0$, then $z$ has an ordinary open affinoid neighbourhood finite over $F$. In particular, $z$ is isolated in $Z$.}
{设 $Z$ 为完备非阿基米德域 $F$ 上的 good 解析空间。若 $Z\to\mathcal M(F)$ 在 $z$ 处准光滑，且 $\operatorname{rk}_z\Omega_{Z/F}=0$，则 $z$ 有在 $F$ 上有限的普通开 affinoid 邻域。特别地，$z$ 在 $Z$ 中孤立。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose an affinoid neighbourhood $Z_0$ of $z$. By domain locality of Jacobian presentations, take an affinoid domain $U=\mathcal M(C)$ contained in $\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(Z_0,Z)$, containing $z$, with a closed presentation in a domain of $\mathbb A_F^n$ by $n$ equations whose square Jacobian determinant is invertible. Such a domain is obtained from a rank-zero presentation by the Laurent restriction in Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianmatrixcriterion}. All points of $U$ have the same rank-zero Jacobian description.}
{选择 $z$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $Z_0$。由 Jacobian 表示的域局部性，可选包含 $z$、且包含于 $\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(Z_0,Z)$ 的 affinoid 域 $U=\mathcal M(C)$，使其以 $n$ 个方程闭嵌入 $\mathbb A_F^n$ 的一个域，并且对应的方形 Jacobian 行列式可逆：对秩零表示作 Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianmatrixcriterion} 中的 Laurent 限制即可。因此 $U$ 的全部点均具有同样的秩零 Jacobian 描述。}

\bi{Choose a nontrivially valued complete extension $L/F$ such that $C_L$ is strict, using Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification} and, if necessary, one further free-radius extension. Let $\mathfrak m$ be any maximal ideal of $C_L$. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}, $E=C_L/\mathfrak m$ is finite over $L$ with its extending complete absolute value. Evaluation gives an $E$-rational point $u\in U_E$. The pulled-back equations retain their invertible determinant. Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationaljacobiancompletion}, with $n-r=0$, identifies the completion of $S=\mathcal O_{U_E,u}$ with $E$. The ring $S$ is Noetherian by Theorem~\ref{fnd:ordinaryanalyticstalknoetherian}. Since its completion is faithfully flat by \cite[Tag 00MC]{StacksAlg}, its maximal ideal is zero: that ideal becomes zero in its completion, and faithful flatness detects the zero module. Thus $S$ is a field.}
{利用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification}，并在需要时再作一次自由半径扩张，可选赋值非平凡的完备扩域 $L/F$，使 $C_L$ 为 strict。任取 $C_L$ 的极大理想 $\mathfrak m$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}，$E=C_L/\mathfrak m$ 在 $L$ 上有限，并带有延拓的完备绝对值。取值给出 $E$-有理点 $u\in U_E$，拉回后的方程保持其可逆行列式。取 $n-r=0$，Proposition~\ref{fnd:rationaljacobiancompletion} 将 $S=\mathcal O_{U_E,u}$ 的完备化识别为 $E$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:ordinaryanalyticstalknoetherian}，$S$ Noetherian；由 \cite[Tag 00MC]{StacksAlg}，其完备化忠实平坦。因此 $S$ 的极大理想为零：该理想在完备化后为零，而忠实平坦性检测零模。所以 $S$ 为域。}

\bi{The local map $(C_L)_{\mathfrak m}\to S$ is faithfully flat. Indeed $C_L\to C_E$ is finite free by finite-extension base change, and the rational-neighbourhood description of $S$ expresses it as a filtered colimit of algebras flat over $C_E$. The composite is flat, and it is local after localization at $\mathfrak m$; use \cite[Tag 00HP]{StacksAlg}. A local ring with a faithfully flat local map to a field is itself a field: its maximal ideal extends to zero, so is zero. Thus $(C_L)_{\mathfrak m}$ is a field for every maximal $\mathfrak m$. Every prime of $C_L$ is maximal, since a strict inclusion of a prime in a maximal ideal would remain a strict inclusion after localization there. Therefore $\dim C_L=0$. Finite strict normalization and Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttatedimension} give a finite injection from a Tate algebra on zero variables. Consequently $C_L$ is finite-dimensional over $L$.}
{局部映射 $(C_L)_{\mathfrak m}\to S$ 忠实平坦。确实，有限扩域基变换说明 $C_L\to C_E$ 有限自由，而 $S$ 的 rational 邻域描述将其写成在 $C_E$ 上平坦的代数的滤过直极限。因此复合平坦，在 $\mathfrak m$ 处局部化后又是局部同态，可应用 \cite[Tag 00HP]{StacksAlg}。若局部环到域有忠实平坦的局部同态，则它本身就是域：其极大理想延拓后为零，故原本即为零。因此每个 $(C_L)_{\mathfrak m}$ 都是域。$C_L$ 的每个素理想均极大，因为素理想到某个极大理想的严格包含，在该极大理想处局部化后仍严格。因此 $\dim C_L=0$。有限 strict 正规化与 Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttatedimension} 给出从零变量 Tate 代数出发的有限单射，所以 $C_L$ 在 $L$ 上有限维。}

\bi{Every finite $F$-linearly independent family in $C$ remains $L$-linearly independent in $C_L$: algebraic scalar extension preserves linear independence, and Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonisometrictensor} embeds the algebraic tensor product in its completion. Thus $\dim_F C\le\dim_L C_L<\infty$. The canonical finite-dimensional topology is the affinoid topology by Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}, so $U\to\mathcal M(F)$ is norm-admissible finite. It is boundaryless by Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidboundarylessfinite}. The composition formula for $U\to Z_0\to\mathcal M(F)$ implies $\operatorname{Int}(U/Z_0)=U$, and Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoiddomainordinaryinterior} makes $U$ open in $Z_0$. Since $U\subset\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(Z_0,Z)$, it is open in $Z$. A finite-dimensional affinoid algebra has finitely many spectral points, a discrete set by Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation}. This proves the assertion.}
{$C$ 中任意有限的 $F$-线性无关族在 $C_L$ 中仍然 $L$-线性无关：代数扩标量保持线性无关性，而 Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonisometrictensor} 将代数张量积嵌入其完备化。因此 $\dim_F C\le\dim_L C_L<\infty$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}，典范有限维拓扑即为 affinoid 拓扑，所以 $U\to\mathcal M(F)$ 为 norm-admissible 的有限态射。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidboundarylessfinite}，它无边界。对 $U\to Z_0\to\mathcal M(F)$ 的内部复合公式给出 $\operatorname{Int}(U/Z_0)=U$；Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoiddomainordinaryinterior} 因而说明 $U$ 在 $Z_0$ 中开。又因 $U\subset\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(Z_0,Z)$，它也在 $Z$ 中开。有限维 affinoid 代数只有有限个谱点；由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation}，它们组成离散集。这就证明了结论。}
\end{proof}
