Testing a finite rational cover at maximal points

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 208

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Testing a finite rational cover at maximal points}{在极大点检验有限 rational 覆盖}]
\label{fnd:finitecovermaximaltest}
\bi{Let $A$ be a strict $K$-affinoid algebra over any complete non-Archimedean field, possibly trivially valued. A finite family of value-group rational subsets of $\mathcal M(A)$ covers the spectrum if and only if it contains the bounded point associated to every maximal ideal of $A$. The assertion includes the empty family when $A=0$.}
{设 $A$ 为任意完备非阿基米德域 $K$ 上的 strict affinoid 代数，允许赋值平凡。$\mathcal M(A)$ 的有限个值群半径 rational 子集覆盖整个谱，当且仅当该族包含 $A$ 的每个极大理想所对应的有界点。此断言也包含 $A=0$ 时的空覆盖。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Scale the finitely many radii by nonzero elements of $K$, so that the members have presentations}
{用 $K$ 的非零元素将有限个半径归一化，使各成员具有表示}
\[
 U_i=\{x:|f_{ij}(x)|\leq|g_i(x)|\text{ for all }j\},
 \qquad (g_i,f_{i1},\ldots,f_{im_i})=A.
\]
\bi{The forward implication is immediate. For the converse first suppose the valuation is nontrivial, and fix $\pi\in K$ with $0<|\pi|<1$. If $x$ lies outside every $U_i$, choose for each $i$ an index $j_i$ and put $h_i=f_{ij_i}$, so $|h_i(x)|>|g_i(x)|$. Choose an integer $N_i\geq1$ and a nonzero scalar $c_i$ with}
{正向断言直接成立。反向先设赋值非平凡，并固定 $\pi\in K$ 满足 $0<|\pi|<1$。若某个 $x$ 不属于任何 $U_i$，则对每个 $i$ 选取下标 $j_i$，记 $h_i=f_{ij_i}$，使 $|h_i(x)|>|g_i(x)|$。选择整数 $N_i\geq1$ 及非零标量 $c_i$ 使}
\[
 |g_i(x)|^{N_i}\leq|\pi|\,|h_i(x)|^{N_i},
 \qquad |c_i|\leq|h_i(x)|^{N_i}.
\]
\bi{This is possible because the ratio is strictly less than one and $|h_i(x)|>0$. Let $V_i$ be the rational subset with denominator $h_i^{N_i}$ and numerators $\pi^{-1}g_i^{N_i},c_i$. Its defining tuple generates the unit ideal since $c_i\ne0$. It contains $x$, and every $y\in V_i$ satisfies}
{这是可能的，因为前述比值严格小于一，且 $|h_i(x)|>0$。令 $V_i$ 为分母 $h_i^{N_i}$、分子 $\pi^{-1}g_i^{N_i},c_i$ 所定义的 rational 子集。由于 $c_i\ne0$，其定义列表生成单位理想。它包含 $x$，且每个 $y\in V_i$ 满足}
\[
 |h_i(y)|>0,\qquad
 |g_i(y)|\leq|\pi|^{1/N_i}|h_i(y)|<|h_i(y)|.
\]
\bi{Hence $V_i\cap U_i=\varnothing$. The finite intersection $V=\bigcap_iV_i$ has a strict rational presentation by Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation}. Its presentation algebra $A_V$ is nonzero: the point $x\in V$ lifts to its spectrum by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology}. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} supplies a bounded maximal-residue evaluation $A_V\to L$, with $L/K$ finite. Its restriction to $A$ has maximal kernel: its image is a finite-dimensional $K$-domain, hence a field by \cite[Tag 00GS(1)]{StacksAlg}. Uniqueness of the extending absolute value, Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}, identifies the resulting point with the maximal point of that kernel. This point belongs to $V$ and to none of the $U_i$, contrary to the hypothesis.}
{因此 $V_i\cap U_i=\varnothing$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation}，有限交 $V=\bigcap_iV_i$ 具有 strict rational 表示。其表示代数 $A_V$ 非零：由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology}，点 $x\in V$ 提升到它的谱。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} 给出有界极大剩余域取值 $A_V\to L$，其中 $L/K$ 有限。限制到 $A$ 后其核为极大理想：像是有限维 $K$-整环，故由 \cite[Tag 00GS(1)]{StacksAlg} 为域。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 中绝对值延拓的唯一性，所得点恰为该核的极大点。此点属于 $V$ 而不属于任何 $U_i$，与假设矛盾。}

\bi{If the valuation is trivial, equip $A$ with a strict presentation quotient norm, which is discrete. Every element has pointwise value at most one. A unit-ideal certificate for $(g_i,f_{i1},\ldots,f_{im_i})$ consequently shows that}
{若赋值平凡，在 $A$ 上使用 strict 表示的商范数，此范数为离散范数。每个元素的逐点值均不超过一。因此，$(g_i,f_{i1},\ldots,f_{im_i})$ 的单位理想证书说明}
\[
 U_i=\{x:|g_i(x)|=1\}.
\]
\bi{Indeed, all the numerators have value at most one, and if the denominator and numerators all had value strictly less than one, their unit-ideal relation would contradict $|1|=1$. At a maximal point $|g_i|=1$ is equivalent to $g_i\notin\mathfrak m$, since finite extensions of a trivially valued field have the trivial extending absolute value. The assumed covering of maximal points therefore gives $(g_1,\ldots,g_t)=A$ by \cite[Tag 00E0(2)]{StacksAlg}. Evaluating a unit certificate at any $x$ shows that some $|g_i(x)|=1$, so that $x\in U_i$. The empty family is dealt with by the nonemptiness criterion of Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}.}
{事实上，所有分子的值都不超过一；若分母及全部分子的值都严格小于一，则单位理想关系会与 $|1|=1$ 矛盾。在极大点处，$|g_i|=1$ 等价于 $g_i\notin\mathfrak m$，因为平凡赋值域的有限扩张具有平凡的延拓绝对值。因此，由覆盖全部极大点的假设及 \cite[Tag 00E0(2)]{StacksAlg}，有 $(g_1,\ldots,g_t)=A$。在任意 $x$ 处对单位理想证书取值，可见某个 $|g_i(x)|=1$，从而 $x\in U_i$。空族的情形由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} 的非空性判据处理。}
\end{proof}
