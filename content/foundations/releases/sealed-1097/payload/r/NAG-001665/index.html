All analytic-domain inclusions are analytically flat

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 425

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{All analytic-domain inclusions are analytically flat}{全部解析域包含均解析平坦}]
\label{fnd:alldomainanalyticflatness}
\bi{Every analytic-domain inclusion $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ is analytically flat, over any complete non-Archimedean ground field. If $U$ and $X$ are good, the ordinary local homomorphism}
{在任意完备非阿基米德基域上，每个解析域包含 $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ 都解析平坦。若 $U$、$X$ 均 good，则通常局部同态}
\[
       \mathcal O_{X,x}\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{U,x}
          \qquad(x\in U)
\]
\bi{is faithfully flat. In this good case, after every base change from a good space over every complete valued extension, the resulting domain is good and the same faithful-flat assertion holds.}
{忠实平坦。在此 good 情形，在任意完备赋值扩域上，从任意 good 空间作基变换后，所得域仍 good，并且同样的忠实平坦断言成立。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First assume $U$ and $X$ good. Choose an affinoid neighborhood $X_0$ of $x$ in $X$, and then an affinoid neighborhood $U_0$ of $x$ in $U$ contained in $X_0$. By Corollary~\ref{fnd:intrinsicaffinoidatlas}, $U_0$ is an affinoid domain of $X_0$. Ordinary neighborhoods do not change stalks, so Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticdomainstalkflatness} gives the claimed flatness. The homomorphism is local by Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks} and residue-field compatibility.}
{先设 $U$、$X$ good。选择 $x$ 在 $X$ 中的 affinoid 邻域 $X_0$，再选择 $x$ 在 $U$ 中的 affinoid 邻域 $U_0\subset X_0$。由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:intrinsicaffinoidatlas}，$U_0$ 是 $X_0$ 的 affinoid 域。普通邻域不改变茎，因此 Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticdomainstalkflatness} 给出所需平坦性。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:ordinaryaffinoidstalks} 及剩余域相容性，该同态为局部同态。}

\bi{For clarity, a flat local homomorphism $R\to S$ of nonzero local rings is faithful for the following elementary reason. If $N\ne0$, a nonzero element of $N$ generates a submodule $R/I$ with $I$ proper. Flatness embeds $S/IS$ into $S\otimes_RN$, and $IS$ is contained in the maximal ideal of $S$, so this tensor product is nonzero. Apply this to the two stalks.}
{明确地说，非零局部环间的平坦局部同态 $R\to S$ 忠实，理由如下。若 $N\ne0$，则其中一个非零元素生成子模 $R/I$，其中 $I$ 为真理想。平坦性把 $S/IS$ 嵌入 $S\otimes_RN$，而 $IS$ 包含于 $S$ 的极大理想，故此张量积非零。将其用于上述两个茎即可。}

\bi{Let $X'\to X$ be a morphism from a good $L$-analytic space, with $L/K$ complete. Its inverse image $U'$ is an analytic domain by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing} and the construction of analytic products. To verify that $U'$ is good in the current good-source case, fix $u'\in U'$, with images $u,x'$. Choose affinoid neighborhoods $X_0$ of $u$ in $X$, $U_0$ of $u$ in $U$ contained in $X_0$, and $X'_0$ of $x'$ in $X'$ contained in the inverse image of the ordinary interior of $X_0$. Then}
{设 $X'\to X$ 来自 good $L$-解析空间，其中 $L/K$ 完备。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing} 及解析乘积的构造，其逆像 $U'$ 是解析域。为检验当前 good 源情形下 $U'$ 也 good，固定 $u'\in U'$，其像记为 $u,x'$。选择 $u$ 在 $X$ 中的 affinoid 邻域 $X_0$、$u$ 在 $U$ 中且包含于 $X_0$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $U_0$，以及 $x'$ 在 $X'$ 中的 affinoid 邻域 $X'_0$，使后者包含于 $X_0$ 普通内部的逆像。则}
\[
                  U'_0=U_0\times_{X_0}X'_0
\]
\bi{is an affinoid domain of $X'_0$, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange}, and contains an ordinary neighborhood of $u'$ in $U'$: intersect the inverse images of the ordinary interiors of $U_0$ and $X'_0$. Thus $U'$ is good. The first part, applied over $L$, proves faithful flatness on its ordinary stalks. This verifies every good base-change test in Definition~\ref{fnd:analyticflatness}.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange}，它是 $X'_0$ 的 affinoid 域，并且包含 $u'$ 在 $U'$ 中的普通邻域：取 $U_0$ 与 $X'_0$ 的普通内部的逆像之交即可。因此 $U'$ good。在 $L$ 上应用第一部分，得到其通常茎的忠实平坦性。这检验了 Definition~\ref{fnd:analyticflatness} 中全部 good 基变换测试。}

\bi{For a general domain inclusion, choose an affinoid chart of the target and an affinoid chart of its inverse-image domain. The latter is an affinoid domain of the former. What was just proved supplies all universal good-space flatness tests for this local map. Such charts form the analytic nets on source and target. The general $G$-local definition of analytic flatness therefore proves the assertion without assuming that an arbitrary domain of a good space is itself good.}
{对一般的域包含，选择目标的一个 affinoid 图，以及其逆像域的一个 affinoid 图。后者是前者的 affinoid 域，刚才的证明为这个局部映射提供全部普适 good 空间平坦性测试。这些图在源与目标上构成解析 net。由解析平坦性的一般 $G$-局部定义，便得到断言，无须假设 good 空间的任意域本身都 good。}
\end{proof}
