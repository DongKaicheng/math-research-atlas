Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[The discarded Cartier contribution; F8U-D]
\label{lem:dimension}
The coherent module \(T=T(A)\) has analytic support of dimension
at most \(n-1\). If \(f\in A\) is regular, then
\[
                    \dim_K\Supp(T/fT)\leq n-2,                    \tag{2.1}
\]
where the right side means empty support when \(n=1\).
Consequently
\[
            [A/fA]_{n-1}=[A^{\mathrm u}/fA^{\mathrm u}]_{n-1}       \tag{2.2}
\]
as integer cycles, with generic local lengths, on the common reduced
zero support.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Since \(T\) is finite and \(T_{\mathfrak p}=0\) at all minimal primes,
\(\operatorname{Ann}(T)\) is contained in none of them.
The frozen exceptional-image theorem \src{NAG-002374}, applied to
this ideal, gives
\(\dim_K\M(A/\operatorname{Ann}(T))\leq n-1\).

Let \(\mathfrak q\) be a minimal prime of \(\Supp(T)\).
The nonzero module \(T_{\mathfrak q}\) has finite length.
If \(f\in\mathfrak q\), some power of \(f\) annihilates this module,
contradicting injectivity of \(f\) on \(T_{\mathfrak q}\).
Hence \(f\notin\mathfrak q\).
Each reduced irreducible support component
\(Z_{\mathfrak q}=\M(A/\mathfrak q)\) is pure of its analytic
dimension \(d_{\mathfrak q}\leq n-1\).
Apply \src{NAG-002374} on this component to the nonzero image of \(f\).
Its zero set has dimension at most
\(d_{\mathfrak q}-1\leq n-2\), and is empty if \(d_{\mathfrak q}=0\).
The finite union of these sets contains \(\Supp(T/fT)\), proving (2.1).

At every generic point of an \((n-1)\)-dimensional zero component,
the first term in (1.2) localizes to zero.
The other two localized quotients are therefore isomorphic, not just
equal after reduction. Their Artinian lengths agree. This proves (2.2).
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
Let \(A\) be a nonzero Noetherian ring. Set
\[
 S=A\setminus\bigcup_{\mathfrak p\in\Min(A)}\mathfrak p,\qquad
 T(A)=\ker\!\left(A\longrightarrow
                    \prod_{\mathfrak p\in\Min(A)}A_{\mathfrak p}\right),
 \qquad A^{\mathrm u}=A/T(A).                                    \tag{1.1}
\]
We call \(T(A)\) the embedded torsion ideal. No reduction of the
generic Artinian local rings is performed.
Standing context 2 · exact TeX

Now let \(A\) be generalized \(K\)-affinoid and
\(V=\M(A)\) pure of analytic dimension \(n\geq1\).
All dimensions in the following lemma are \emph{analytic} dimensions.
We do not identify \(\dim_{\mathrm{Krull}} A\) with \(n\) for arbitrary
generalized radii.
