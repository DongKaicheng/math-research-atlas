\begin{proposition}[A scoped intrinsic adaptation]\label{prop:adapt}
Let $E_{\rm ext}$ be an ordinary compact-test space with chart/support
final topology of the intrinsic type described in Section~1. Assume:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=*]
\item There is a specified algebraic identification with the native
ordinary test sheaf, compatible with local moment pullbacks, nullity,
restriction, ordinary gluing and support. The support family is the same
compact subsets of the chosen $U$, not compact sets meeting an ambient
analytic boundary.
\item On every fixed external chart/support stage, the compact support
has a finite cover by ordinary interiors of compact affinoid
refinements, with fixed unit moments and finite polyhedral images.
Restriction to those images is continuous for the intrinsic seminorms.
Fixed affine coordinate changes, including reflection between $\log$
and $-\log$, induce the indicated pullbacks. Conversely a native
cutoff presentation, after adjoining fixed cutoff moments, is
represented by one fixed external stage.
\item On each finite refinement needed in (ii), the coefficient
quotient map onto the relevant intrinsic compatible-tuple space is
strict (a quotient map). This follows, for example, from
\eqref{eq:reverse}, or from the supported extension in
Proposition~\ref{prop:extension} when that is the exact sector in use.
\end{enumerate}
Then the identification is a homeomorphism for $\tau_a$ and the
external final topology. Under these hypotheses the strong-dual
conclusions of Theorem~\ref{thm:log} transfer, with the same underlying
bounded sets. Without (iii) only the native-to-external continuity
provided by the coefficient restriction argument is obtained from
these assumptions.
\end{proposition}
