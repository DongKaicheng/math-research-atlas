Compactness of analytified projective maps

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 289

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Compactness of analytified projective maps}{射影映射解析化的紧性}]
\label{fnd:analytificationprojectivecompact}
\bi{The analytification of a projective morphism of finite-type $K$-schemes is compact: the inverse image of every compact Hausdorff subset of the target is compact Hausdorff. In particular $(\mathbf P^n_K)^{\an}$ is compact.}
{有限型 $K$-概形的射影态射，其解析化是紧映射：目标中每个紧 Hausdorff 子集的逆像都紧且 Hausdorff。特别地，$(\mathbf P^n_K)^{\an}$ 紧。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First consider $\mathbf P^n_R\to\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ and an affinoid domain $V=\mathcal M(A)\subset X_R$. For each homogeneous coordinate $X_i$, the domain}
{先考虑 $\mathbf P^n_R\to\operatorname{Spec}(R)$，以及 affinoid 域 $V=\mathcal M(A)\subset X_R$。对每个齐次坐标 $X_i$，域}
\[
 V_i=\{\,y\text{ above }V:\ |X_j/X_i|(y)\le1
                              \text{ for }j\ne i\,\}
 \simeq\mathcal M\bigl(A\{T_0,\ldots,\widehat T_i,\ldots,T_n\}\bigr)
\]
\bi{is compact. These $n+1$ domains cover the inverse image of $V$. Indeed, at a scheme point choose homogeneous coordinates in its residue field, not all zero, and evaluate them using its analytic absolute value. Choosing a coordinate of maximal absolute value puts the analytic point in the corresponding domain. The identifications of the domains follow either from their displayed coordinate ratios or from Theorem~\ref{fnd:analytificationproducts}. The union is Hausdorff by Lemma~\ref{fnd:analytificationseparatedtopology}, because projective space has closed diagonal. It is a finite union of compact spaces and hence compact. A closed subscheme of projective space gives a closed analytic subspace, so its inverse image over $V$ is compact as well.}
{是紧的。这 $n+1$ 个域覆盖 $V$ 的逆像。事实上，在概形点的剩余域中选取不全为零的齐次坐标，再用该解析点的绝对值取值；选择绝对值最大的坐标，就使该点属于相应域。所列坐标比值直接给出这些域的识别，也可应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:analytificationproducts}。由于射影空间有闭对角线，由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:analytificationseparatedtopology}，此并为 Hausdorff。它又是有限个紧空间的并，因而紧。射影空间的闭子概形给出闭解析子空间，因此其在 $V$ 上的逆像也紧。}

\bi{For a compact subset $C\subset X_R$, finitely many affinoid neighbourhoods cover $C$. Their inverse images form a finite compact union. Since $X_R$ is Hausdorff, $C$ is closed, and its inverse image is a closed subset of that union. This proves compactness over an affine scheme base. For a general scheme base, cover a given compact Hausdorff subset $C$ by finitely many analytified affine opens. A compact Hausdorff space admits a finite closed refinement of any finite open cover: choose, at each point, an open neighbourhood whose closure lies in one chosen cover member, using regularity of compact Hausdorff spaces, and take a finite subcover. Apply the affine result to these finitely many closed subsets of $C$. Their inverse images are compact and cover the required inverse image. The latter is Hausdorff by the preceding lemma. Hence it is compact Hausdorff.}
{对紧子集 $C\subset X_R$，可用有限个 affinoid 邻域覆盖 $C$；它们的逆像构成有限紧并。由于 $X_R$ 为 Hausdorff，$C$ 闭，因此其逆像是该并中的闭子集。这证明了仿射概形底上的紧性。对一般概形底，用有限个仿射开集的解析化覆盖给定的紧 Hausdorff 子集 $C$。紧 Hausdorff 空间的有限开覆盖具有有限闭加细：利用其正则性，在每点选择闭包包含于某一覆盖成员的开邻域，再选有限子覆盖即可。对 $C$ 的这些有限个闭子集应用仿射情形，其紧逆像覆盖所需逆像；该逆像又由前一引理为 Hausdorff，因而紧且 Hausdorff。}
\end{proof}
