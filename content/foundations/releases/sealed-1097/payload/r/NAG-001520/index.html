Algebraic faithful flatness of a finite rational covering

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Algebraic faithful flatness of a finite rational covering}{有限 rational 覆盖的代数忠实平坦性}]\label{fnd:rationalcoverfaithfullyflat}
\bi{If $V_1,\ldots,V_t$ are rational subsets covering $\mathcal M(A)$, with their strict value-group presentation algebras $B_i$, then $A\to\prod_{i=1}^t B_i$ is a faithfully flat ring map. Faithfulness here tests arbitrary algebraic $A$-modules. It does not identify $B_i\otimes_A B_j$ with the completed intersection algebra.}
{设 rational 子集 $V_1,\ldots,V_t$ 覆盖 $\mathcal M(A)$，其 strict 值群半径表示代数为 $B_i$，则 $A\to\prod_{i=1}^t B_i$ 为忠实平坦环映射。此处忠实性检测任意代数 $A$-模，并不将 $B_i\otimes_A B_j$ 与完备的交叠代数相识别。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalfiniteelementary} gives algebraic flatness of each $B_i$. To spell out its trivial-valuation branch, choose a defining strict quotient norm on $A$. Every nonzero element then has norm one, so every coefficient of a unit certificate has norm at most one; the scalar in that lemma can be taken to be $a=1$. Its elementary chain is therefore available, and Corollary~\ref{fnd:finiteelementaryflatness}, which allows trivial valuation, proves the same algebraic flatness. A finite product is a finite direct sum as an $A$-module, so tensoring by it preserves exactness. For every maximal $\mathfrak m\subset A$, its residue point belongs to some $V_i$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalmaximalfibers} then gives $B_i/\mathfrak mB_i=A/\mathfrak m\ne0$. The product therefore has nonzero fiber at every maximal ideal. Apply \cite[Tag 00HP, Lemma 10.39.15, equivalence of (1) and (4)]{StacksAlg}. If $A=0$, all modules under consideration are zero and the assertion has its usual degenerate meaning.}
{Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalfiniteelementary} 给出每个 $B_i$ 的代数平坦性。为明确其中的平凡赋值分支，给 $A$ 选择一个定义 strict 商范数。此时每个非零元素的范数为一，因此单位理想证书的每个系数范数至多为一，该引理中的标量可以取 $a=1$。故其 elementary 链仍然可用，再由允许平凡赋值的 Corollary~\ref{fnd:finiteelementaryflatness} 得到相同的代数平坦性。作为 $A$-模，有限直积就是有限直和，故与它张量保持正合性。对每个极大理想 $\mathfrak m\subset A$，其剩余点属于某个 $V_i$。Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalmaximalfibers} 于是给出 $B_i/\mathfrak mB_i=A/\mathfrak m\ne0$。因此该直积在每个极大理想处都有非零纤维。应用 \cite[Tag 00HP, Lemma 10.39.15, (1) 与 (4) 的等价性]{StacksAlg} 即得结论。若 $A=0$，所考虑的全部模均为零，结论按通常的退化情形理解。}
\end{proof}

\section{\bi{Finite modules and Laurent descent}{有限模与 Laurent 下降}}\label{fnd:finitemodulelaurentdescent}

\bi{Tate exactness concerns the localizations of a module that is already defined over the original algebra. For coherence we must also construct a global module from finite modules given separately on a covering. The additional analytic step is a bounded lifting argument for arbitrary finite Laurent gluing data. We then use maximal residue fields to obtain generators and apply the lifting argument once more to the relations. This reconstructs the method of Kiehl's theorem as developed in \cite[\S9.4.3, Lemmas 5--6, pp.~378--382]{BGR84}, with the finite-module norms and the restricted site specified explicitly.}
{Tate 正合性处理的是已经定义在原代数上的模的局部化。凝聚性还要求从覆盖各部分分别给出的有限模构造整体模。额外的解析步骤，是对任意有限模 Laurent 粘合数据证明有界提升。随后借助极大剩余域取得生成元，并对关系模再次应用提升论证。下面重构 \cite[\S9.4.3, Lemmas 5--6, pp.~378--382]{BGR84} 所展开的 Kiehl 定理的方法，同时明确有限模范数及所用的受限 site。}

\bi{Unless an additional hypothesis is stated, $K$ is a complete real-valued non-Archimedean field, its valuation may be trivial, and $A$ is a strict $K$-affinoid algebra with a norm-admissible Banach norm. Relation ideals are actual ideals; their closedness and the canonical norms of finite modules are supplied by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}. The zero algebra and its zero module are allowed separately. Radii belong to $|K^\times|$ and can be scaled to one. Ordinary tensor products in this section are algebraic tensor products. A completed-tensor assertion is made only after its finite-module comparison has been established.}
{除非另行声明，$K$ 是完备的实值非阿基米德域，允许赋值平凡；$A$ 为配备 norm-admissible Banach 范数的 strict $K$-affinoid 代数。关系理想均指实际理想；其闭性及有限模的 canonical 范数由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules} 给出。零代数及其零模作为独立情形允许在内。半径属于 $|K^\times|$，可缩放为一。本节的普通张量积均为代数张量积；只有在建立相应有限模比较之后，才作出完备张量积的断言。}
