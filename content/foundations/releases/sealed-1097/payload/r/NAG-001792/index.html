Regular ordinary local maps of analytic domains

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 359

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Regular ordinary local maps of analytic domains}{解析域的正则通常局部映射}]
\label{fnd:analyticdomainordinaryregular}
\bi{If $V$ is a good analytic domain in a good analytic space $X$, then $\mathcal O_{X,x}\to\mathcal O_{V,x}$ is regular for every $x\in V$. The same holds after every complete extension and every analytic base change for which the spaces under consideration are good.}
{若 $V$ 是 good 解析空间 $X$ 中的 good 解析域，则对每个 $x\in V$，$\mathcal O_{X,x}\to\mathcal O_{V,x}$ 正则。在每个完备扩张及每个所涉空间均 good 的解析基变换后，同样成立。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Restrict $X$ to an ordinary affinoid neighborhood of $x$, and $V$ to an ordinary affinoid neighborhood of $x$ inside its inverse image. For the corresponding affinoid algebra $A$, apply the bottom-arrow assertion of Theorem~\ref{fnd:relativealgebraicregularcomparison} to $R=A$. Domain base change remains an analytic domain, so the same argument applies after each of the stated base changes.}
{把 $X$ 限制到 $x$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域，并把 $V$ 限制到其逆像中 $x$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域。对相应的 affinoid 代数 $A$，取 $R=A$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:relativealgebraicregularcomparison} 的下箭头断言。解析域的基变换仍为解析域，因此同一论证适用于所述每次基变换之后。}
\end{proof}
