Remaining historical proof gap

The sealed ledger records a gap in the proof of the historical whole statement.

Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Conditional local weak Poincar\'e--Lelong; F8LA-A2R]
\label{thm:analyticpl}
Under \cref{hyp:graph,hyp:flux},
\[
 L_f(\dd\eta_X)=\int_D (t_g|_D)^*\eta.
 \tag{4.11}
\]
Here \(L_f\) is the stabilized logarithmic excision from
\cref{prop:analytic}. The sign is positive for \(\log|f|\)
and negative for \(-\log|f|\).

The function \(h_R(t_f)\) from (3.2) extends smoothly across \(Z\)
by the constant value \(-R-\mu\). For \(R\geq H\),
\[
 \int_X\operatorname{ext}_0
       \bigl(\dd(h_R(t_f))\wedge\eta_X|_V\bigr)
       =\int_D(t_g|_D)^*\eta .
 \tag{4.12}
\]
The integrand on the left is supported in the annular shell
\(R<t_f<R+1\) and the fixed support of \(\eta_X\).
\end{theorem}
