Restriction of scalars through a finite field extension

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Restriction of scalars through a finite field extension}{沿有限域扩张限制标量}]
\label{fnd:strictfiniterestrictionofscalars}
\bi{Let $L/K$ be a finite extension, endowed with the unique extending absolute value. Every strict $L$-affinoid algebra, with its given admissible norm, is also a strict $K$-affinoid algebra. Bounded $L$-algebra homomorphisms remain bounded after restriction of scalars. For every Banach $K$-space $E$ and every $K$-basis $\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_d$ of $L$, the map}
{设 $L/K$ 为有限扩张，配备唯一的延拓绝对值。每个带给定 admissible 范数的 strict $L$-affinoid 代数也是 strict $K$-affinoid 代数。有界 $L$-代数同态在限制标量后仍有界。对每个 Banach $K$-空间 $E$ 及 $L$ 的每组 $K$-基 $\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_d$，映射}
\[
 E^d\longrightarrow E\widehat\otimes_K L,
 \qquad (e_1,\ldots,e_d)\longmapsto\sum_i e_i\otimes\ell_i
\]
\bi{is a bounded linear isomorphism with bounded inverse; the algebraic tensor product is already complete. In particular $L\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle$ is a finite free algebra of rank $d$ over $K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle$. No separability hypothesis is needed.}
{为有界线性同构，且逆有界；代数张量积已经完备。特别地，$L\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle$ 在 $K\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle$ 上是秩为 $d$ 的有限自由代数。不要求可分性。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} gives the absolute value on $L$ and constants bounding its coordinate maps $\lambda_i:L\to K$. On the algebraic tensor product, the displayed basis map is an algebraic isomorphism. Its forward bound follows from the finite expression $\sum e_i\otimes\ell_i$. In the other direction $1_E\otimes\lambda_i$ has bound $\|\lambda_i\|$: apply the ultrametric inequality to an arbitrary finite tensor expression and take its projective infimum. Thus the projective norm is definite and equivalent to the maximum norm on $E^d$, which is complete. Completing changes nothing and gives the claimed bounded inverse.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}，$L$ 上存在所述绝对值，且坐标映射 $\lambda_i:L\to K$ 具有固定的界。在代数张量积上，所列基映射为代数同构。正向的界来自有限表达式 $\sum e_i\otimes\ell_i$。反方向，$1_E\otimes\lambda_i$ 的界为 $\|\lambda_i\|$：对任意有限张量表达式使用超度量不等式，再取投射下确界即可。因此投射范数正定，且与 $E^d$ 的最大范数等价；后者完备。所以完成不改变该空间，并给出所需的有界逆。}

\bi{Apply the same coordinate bounds to every coefficient of a power series. A coefficient family in $L$ tends to zero exactly when all its $d$ coordinate families in $K$ do, with the same uniform bounds on their supremum norms. Coefficientwise multiplication then identifies $L\langle T\rangle$ as a Banach algebra with $L\otimes_K K\langle T\rangle$. This algebra is finite free over the strict $K$-affinoid $K\langle T\rangle$, and Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebras} supplies its strict structure with the same norm up to equivalence. A strict $L$-affinoid is a quotient of $L\langle T\rangle$ by an actual ideal. The ideal is closed, and composing its quotient with a strict $K$-presentation gives a strict $K$-presentation, by the successive-quotient argument of Lemma~\ref{fnd:iteratedstrictquotients}. The quotient norms remain equivalent. The last assertion about homomorphisms follows because the norm inequality is unchanged when scalars are restricted.}
{对幂级数的每个系数使用相同坐标界。$L$ 中的系数族趋于零，当且仅当它的全部 $d$ 个 $K$-坐标族趋于零，且各上确界范数之间具有相同的统一界。逐系数核对乘法，便将 $L\langle T\rangle$ 作为 Banach 代数与 $L\otimes_K K\langle T\rangle$ 识别。该代数在 strict $K$-affinoid $K\langle T\rangle$ 上有限自由；由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebras}，它具有 strict 结构，且范数在等价意义下不变。Strict $L$-affinoid 是 $L\langle T\rangle$ 模某个实际理想的商。该理想闭，将其商映射与一个 strict $K$-表示复合，由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:iteratedstrictquotients} 的逐次取商论证，得到 strict $K$-表示；商范数仍等价。限制标量并不改变范数不等式，故最后关于同态的断言成立。}
\end{proof}
