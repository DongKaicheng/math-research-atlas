The discrete algebraic bridge

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 245

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The discrete algebraic bridge}{离散代数桥}]\label{fnd:trivialstrictalgebraicbridge}
\bi{Every strict $K$-affinoid algebra is a finite-type $K$-algebra, and each of its defining strict quotient norms is the discrete norm: every nonzero element has norm one. Conversely, every finite-type $K$-algebra, equipped with this norm, is strict affinoid. Every $K$-algebra homomorphism between such algebras is bounded for their admissible norms and is of finite presentation. If $A\to B$ and $A\to C$ are such maps, the ordinary algebra $B\otimes_A C$ is strict affinoid, and}
{每个 strict $K$-affinoid 代数都是有限型 $K$-代数，其任一定义 strict 商范数都是离散范数，即每个非零元素的范数为一。反之，每个有限型 $K$-代数配备此范数后都是 strict affinoid。这样的代数之间的每个 $K$-代数同态对 admissible 范数均有界，并且具有有限表示。若 $A\to B$ 与 $A\to C$ 是这样的映射，则普通代数 $B\otimes_A C$ 是 strict affinoid，且}
\[
 B\otimes_A C\ \longrightarrow\ B\widehat\otimes_A C
\]
\bi{is a bounded isomorphism. For discrete defining norms, its projective tensor norm is exactly the discrete norm and the map is an isometry.}
{为有界同构。对离散定义范数，其 projective 张量积范数恰为离散范数，该映射为等距同构。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A family of coefficients in $K$ tending to zero has only finitely many nonzero terms. Thus $K\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle=K[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$, and the Gauss norm is discrete. Every ideal is closed for this norm. In a nonzero quotient class, every representative has norm one, so the quotient norm is one. These observations prove both statements about the algebras and their defining norms. A given norm-admissible norm is uniformly equivalent to a defining norm, so every algebra map, contractive for the discrete norms, remains bounded.}
{一个趋于零的 $K$ 中系数族只能有有限多个非零项。因此 $K\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle=K[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$，且 Gauss 范数离散。每个理想在此范数下均闭。一个非零商类的每个代表元的范数都为一，故其商范数为一。这证明有关代数及定义范数的两个断言。给定的 norm-admissible 范数与某个定义范数一致等价，所以每个在离散范数下 contractive 的代数映射仍然有界。}

\bi{A finite set of $K$-algebra generators of $B$ also generates it over $A$. Choose a surjection $P=A[X_1,\ldots,X_n]\to B$. The algebra $P$ is itself a finite-type $K$-algebra and hence strict. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules} makes it Noetherian, so the kernel is finitely generated. This proves finite presentation of the map.}
{$B$ 的一组有限 $K$-代数生成元也在 $A$ 上生成 $B$。选择满射 $P=A[X_1,\ldots,X_n]\to B$。代数 $P$ 本身是有限型 $K$-代数，因此为 strict。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}，它是 Noetherian 的，所以核有限生成。这证明该映射具有有限表示。}

\bi{The tensor algebra is a quotient of $B\otimes_K C$, hence is of finite type over $K$. For its projective seminorm, every pure-tensor representation has maximum factor norm at most one. If the represented element is nonzero, at least one term in every representation has both factors nonzero; its factor norm is then one. The infimum over representations is therefore exactly one on every nonzero element. The seminorm is separated, and this discrete norm is complete. Passing to the separated completion changes nothing. Uniformly equivalent norms on $B,C$ give uniformly equivalent projective tensor norms, by applying the two fixed factor bounds to every representation. This proves the assertion for all stipulated norms.}
{张量代数是 $B\otimes_K C$ 的商，所以在 $K$ 上有限型。对于其 projective 半范数，每个纯张量表示中的因子范数乘积的最大值至多为一。若所表示的元素非零，则每个表示至少有一项的两个因子都非零，此项的因子范数乘积就是一。因此，对所有表示取下确界后，每个非零元素的范数仍恰为一。该半范数已分离，且离散范数完备，所以取 separated completion 不改变它。若替换 $B,C$ 的范数，只须对每个表示应用两个固定的因子界，就得到 projective 范数的一致等价性。这证明全部指定范数下的结论。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{The nontrivial-valuation hypothesis in the local Weierstrass form of Gerritzen--Grauert cannot simply be removed. Over a trivially valued field, strict affinoid algebras carry discrete defining norms. This makes an algebraic route available for finite rational coverings of a domain, but it also makes the stronger ambient local Weierstrass conclusion false. We prove the positive statement and the obstruction separately.}
{Gerritzen--Grauert 的局部 Weierstrass 表述中的非平凡赋值假设不能直接删去。平凡赋值域上的 strict affinoid 代数具有离散的定义范数。这使域自身的有限 rational 覆盖可以通过代数方法构造，同时也使更强的、相对于环境覆盖的局部 Weierstrass 结论不成立。下面分别证明正面结论与这一障碍。}

\bi{Except in Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis}, $K$ is complete with its fixed \emph{trivial} absolute value. Algebras are commutative and unital, and a strict affinoid algebra is equipped with a norm-admissible Banach norm. The term strict-test affinoid domain has exactly the meaning of Definition~\ref{fnd:stricttestaffinoiddomain}; its targets are strict $K$-affinoid algebras. In the trivial-valuation assertions, all strict rational and Weierstrass radii lie in $|K^\times|=\{1\}$. We allow the zero algebra with its usual conventions.}
{除 Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictdomainrationalbasis} 外，本节假设 $K$ 完备，并固定其\emph{平凡}绝对值。代数均交换含幺，strict affinoid 代数配备 norm-admissible Banach 范数。Strict-test affinoid 域严格采用 Definition~\ref{fnd:stricttestaffinoiddomain} 的含义，其测试目标是 strict $K$-affinoid 代数。在平凡赋值的断言中，所有 strict rational 与 Weierstrass 半径都属于 $|K^\times|=\{1\}$。允许零代数，并采用通常约定。}
