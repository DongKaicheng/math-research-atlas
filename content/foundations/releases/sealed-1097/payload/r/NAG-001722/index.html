The completely continuous perturbation lemma

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 294

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The completely continuous perturbation lemma}{完全连续扰动引理}]
\label{fnd:absoluteschwarzlemma}
\bi{Assume that $K$ is nontrivially valued. A bounded linear map is called completely continuous if it is an operator-norm limit of bounded maps with finite-dimensional image. Let $f:E\to F$ be a surjective bounded map of Banach $K$-spaces, and let $g:E\to F$ be completely continuous. Then $(f+g)(E)$ is closed and has finite codimension in $F$. Completely continuous maps remain such after bounded pre- or postcomposition. If their image lies in a closed subspace of a countable-type target, they are completely continuous as maps to that subspace.}
{设 $K$ 的赋值非平凡。若一个有界线性映射是有限维像有界映射在算子范数下的极限，就称其完全连续。设 $f:E\to F$ 为 Banach $K$-空间间的有界满射，$g:E\to F$ 完全连续，则 $(f+g)(E)$ 闭且在 $F$ 中余维有限。完全连续性在左右复合有界映射后保持。若完全连续映射的像包含在可数型目标空间的某个闭子空间中，则以该子空间为目标时仍完全连续。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The open mapping theorem, Lemma~\ref{fnd:nonarchopenmapping}, gives a constant $C>0$ such that every $y\in F$ has a lift $x$ with $\|x\|\le C\|y\|$. Choose a finite-rank map $h$ with $C\|g-h\|<1$, and put $f'=f+(g-h)$. This map is surjective. Indeed, lift $y$ by $f$; the error under $f'$ has norm at most $C\|g-h\|\|y\|$. Lift that error and repeat. The errors decrease geometrically, so the successive lifts converge and give a preimage under $f'$. The same estimates give a bounded lifting constant for $f'$, hence it is open.}
{由开映射定理 Lemma~\ref{fnd:nonarchopenmapping}，存在常数 $C>0$，使每个 $y\in F$ 都具有满足 $\|x\|\le C\|y\|$ 的提升 $x$。选择有限秩映射 $h$，使 $C\|g-h\|<1$，并令 $f'=f+(g-h)$。这个映射满射：先在 $f$ 下提升 $y$，则其在 $f'$ 下的误差范数至多为 $C\|g-h\|\|y\|$；再提升这个误差并重复。误差几何衰减，因此各次提升的和收敛，给出 $f'$ 下的原像。同样的估计给出 $f'$ 的有界提升常数，故它是开映射。}

\bi{Write $N=\ker h$. This is closed, and $E/N$ is finite dimensional. The image of $\ker f'$ in $E/N$ is a finite-dimensional subspace, hence closed by Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}. Consequently $N+\ker f'$ is closed in $E$. Since $f'$ is an open quotient map, $f'(N)$ is closed in $F$. The vector space $F/f'(N)$ is finite dimensional, because it is a quotient of $E/N$. The image of $f'+h$ contains $f'(N)$ and corresponds to a subspace of this finite-dimensional Hausdorff quotient. It is therefore closed and has finite codimension. As $f'+h=f+g$, this proves the first assertion.}
{记 $N=\ker h$。它闭，且 $E/N$ 有限维。$\ker f'$ 在 $E/N$ 中的像是有限维子空间，因而由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} 闭。所以 $N+\ker f'$ 在 $E$ 中闭。由于 $f'$ 是开商映射，$f'(N)$ 在 $F$ 中闭。向量空间 $F/f'(N)$ 是 $E/N$ 的商，因此有限维。$f'+h$ 的像包含 $f'(N)$，并对应于此有限维 Hausdorff 商中的一个子空间；故它闭且余维有限。由于 $f'+h=f+g$，第一项得证。}

\bi{Bounded composition preserves convergence in operator norm and finite rank, proving the second assertion. For the last one, use Lemma~\ref{fnd:countablebanachsplitting} to project the target boundedly onto the closed subspace and compose each finite-rank approximant with that projection.}
{有界复合保持算子范数收敛和有限秩，故第二项成立。最后一项中，利用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:countablebanachsplitting} 取到该闭子空间的有界投影，再将每个有限秩逼近与这个投影复合即可。}
\end{proof}
