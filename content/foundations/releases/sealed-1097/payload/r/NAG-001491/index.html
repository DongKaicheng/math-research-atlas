Units in a field Tate algebra

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 90

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Units in a field Tate algebra}{域上 Tate 代数中的单位元}]\label{fnd:tateunitconstantcriterion}
\bi{For a complete non-Archimedean field $L$, an element $H=\sum_\nu h_\nu U^\nu\in L\langle U_1,\ldots,U_m\rangle$ is a unit if and only if}
{对完备非阿基米德域 $L$，元素 $H=\sum_\nu h_\nu U^\nu\in L\langle U_1,\ldots,U_m\rangle$ 为单位元，当且仅当}
\[
 |h_0|>\sup_{\nu\ne0}|h_\nu|.
\]
\bi{For a unit one has $\|H\|=|h_0|$ and $\|H^{-1}\|=|h_0|^{-1}$.}
{对单位元，有 $\|H\|=|h_0|$ 及 $\|H^{-1}\|=|h_0|^{-1}$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The strict inequality gives the convergent geometric inverse of $h_0^{-1}H=1+(h_0^{-1}H-1)$ by Lemma~\ref{fnd:geometricinverse}. Conversely suppose $HG=1$. Choose the lexicographically largest exponent $\mu$ attaining $\|H\|$ and the corresponding exponent $\nu$ for $G$; these exist because the coefficients tend to zero. In the coefficient of $U^{\mu+\nu}$ in $HG$, the product of the chosen coefficients is the unique term of norm $\|H\|\|G\|$: another term of that norm would require two maximizing exponents, and decreasing the first would increase the second. It cannot cancel. Hence $\mu+\nu=0$, since $HG$ is constant. Both exponents are nonnegative, so $\mu=\nu=0$. No nonconstant exponent can then attain $\|H\|$. Coefficient decay makes the supremum of their absolute values strictly smaller than $|h_0|$. The inverse-norm assertion follows from multiplicativity of the Gauss norm, Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm}.}
{严格不等式使 $h_0^{-1}H=1+(h_0^{-1}H-1)$ 可由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:geometricinverse} 的收敛几何级数求逆。反之，设 $HG=1$。在达到 $\|H\|$ 的指数中取字典序最大的 $\mu$，并对 $G$ 取相应指数 $\nu$；系数趋于零保证二者存在。在乘积 $HG$ 的 $U^{\mu+\nu}$ 系数中，所选系数之积是唯一范数为 $\|H\|\|G\|$ 的项：另一项若具有相同范数，其两个指数也都必须达到最大范数，而减小第一个指数会增大第二个指数。因此该项不能被抵消。因 $HG$ 为常数，必有 $\mu+\nu=0$。两者均为非负指数，所以 $\mu=\nu=0$，从而没有非常数指数能达到 $\|H\|$。系数衰减又保证所有非常数系数绝对值的上确界严格小于 $|h_0|$。逆元的范数公式来自 Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm} 的 Gauss 范数乘性。}
\end{proof}
