NAG-002031 · Accepted whole

The original absolute value and edge length

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 665

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{The original absolute value and edge length}
{原绝对值与边长}]
\label{cand:annulusmodulusinvariance}
\bi{Isomorphic nondegenerate closed annuli over $K$ have equal
logarithmic moduli. For the nodal chart}
{在 $K$ 上同构的非退化闭圆环具有相同的对数模。对节点坐标片}
\[
 \operatorname{Spf}K^\circ\langle U,V\rangle/(UV-a),
 \qquad 0<|a|<1,
\]
\bi{the radial edge has length $-\log|a|$, and this length and the
preceding coordinate-change identities are preserved by complete
isometric extensions of $K$.}
{其径向边长度为 $-\log|a|$；该长度及上面的坐标变换恒等式
在 $K$ 的完备等距扩域下保持不变。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The two logarithmic radius intervals are related by an affine
map of slope $\pm1$, so $\log(R/r)=\log(R'/r')$.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:standardannuluscanonical} and
Proposition~\ref{fnd:standardmonomialchartgeometry}, with $r=d=1$
in the latter's notation, identify the nodal generic fibre with
$A_K[|a|,1]$. Its length is therefore $-\log|a|$. A complete
isometric field extension leaves the coefficients' absolute values,
the Laurent coefficient norms and $|a|$ unchanged. Base change of an
analytic isomorphism is an isomorphism by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension}; reapplying the theorem
over the extended field gives the asserted identities.}
{两个对数半径区间由斜率为 $\pm1$ 的仿射映射联系，故
$\log(R/r)=\log(R'/r')$。由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:standardannuluscanonical} 及
Proposition~\ref{fnd:standardmonomialchartgeometry}，在后者
记号中取 $r=d=1$，节点泛纤维就是 $A_K[|a|,1]$，其长度因而
为 $-\log|a|$。完备等距扩域不改变系数的绝对值、Laurent 系数
范数及 $|a|$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension}，
解析同构的基变换仍为同构；在扩域后应用该定理，就得到所述
恒等式。}
\end{proof}
