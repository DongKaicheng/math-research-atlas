\begin{lemma}[\bi{The norm profile on an entire residual collar}
{整个剩余领形域上的范数形状}]
\label{cand:geometriccollarnormprofile}
\bi{Assume Theorem~\ref{cand:charzeroresidualslope}.
For every residual direction $p$ there are an open
collar $B_p(r_0)$ and a continuous radius function}
{假设 Theorem~\ref{cand:charzeroresidualslope} 的条件
成立。对每个剩余方向 $p$，存在开领形域 $B_p(r_0)$
及连续半径函数}
\[
 R_p:B_p(r_0)\longrightarrow(r_0,1),\qquad
 B_p(r)=\{y\in B_p(r_0):R_p(y)>r\},
\]
\bi{whose Gauss-ray representative has radius $r$.
The germ of this radius function is independent of the
rationalizing extension and coordinates. For every
analytic germ $f$ at the component vertex $x$ with
$f(x)\ne0$, after increasing $r_0$ one has on all actual
points of the collar}
{其 Gauss 射线代表的半径为 $r$。该半径函数的芽
与有理化扩张及坐标选择无关。对每个分量顶点 $x$
处满足 $f(x)\ne0$ 的解析芽，提高 $r_0$ 后，领形域
上所有实际点均满足}
\[
 |f(y)|=|f(x)|\,R_p(y)^{\,e_p\operatorname{ord}_p
                  (\widetilde{cf}_x)},
 \qquad |cf(x)|=1 .
\]
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{At a rational node branch use its standard annular
coordinate. At a smooth residual point, the construction
of Proposition~\ref{cand:residualdirectiongerms}, extended
by Theorem~\ref{cand:charzerosmoothtube}, produces finitely
many rational open discs after a finite Galois extension.
Lemma~\ref{cand:discradialgerm} makes the finitely many
semilinear transition maps preserve the entire radius
functions on sufficiently thin collars. These functions
therefore descend through the analytic Galois quotient to
$R_p$. The same lemma and a common finite Galois extension
compare two constructions. They also show that
$B_p(r)$ is exactly the image of any upstairs radius-$r$
outer collar.}
{对于有理节点分支，使用其标准圆环坐标。对于光滑剩余点，
Proposition~\ref{cand:residualdirectiongerms} 的构造经
Theorem~\ref{cand:charzerosmoothtube} 推广，在一个有限
Galois 扩张后得到有限个有理开圆盘。由
Lemma~\ref{cand:discradialgerm}，有限个半线性过渡映射
在充分薄的领形域上保持整个半径函数。因此，这些函数
经解析 Galois 商下降为 $R_p$。同一引理及共同有限
Galois 扩张比较任意两个构造。它们还说明 $B_p(r)$
恰为上方任一半径 $r$ 外侧领形域的像。}

\bi{By Proposition~\ref{fnd:localunit}, $f$ and $f^{-1}$ are
analytic on a neighborhood of $x$. Cofinality of the
branch stars puts a sufficiently thin collar inside
that neighborhood. On every rational annular collar
upstairs, Corollary~\ref{cand:standardopenunitnorms}
therefore gives an all-point identity
$|f|=a r^n$. Along its Gauss ray,
Theorem~\ref{cand:charzeroresidualslope} identifies
$a=|f(x)|$ and $n=e_p\operatorname{ord}_p(\widetilde{cf}_x)$.
These constants are the same on every conjugate collar.
Evaluation and the radius function descend, proving the
identity on the entire collar downstairs.}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:localunit}，$f$ 与 $f^{-1}$ 在 $x$ 的某个邻域上
解析。分支星形邻域的共尾性将充分薄的领形域放入该邻域。
因此，在上方每个有理圆环领形域上，
Corollary~\ref{cand:standardopenunitnorms} 给出对所有点
成立的恒等式 $|f|=a r^n$。沿其 Gauss 射线，
Theorem~\ref{cand:charzeroresidualslope} 将常数识别为
$a=|f(x)|$、
$n=e_p\operatorname{ord}_p(\widetilde{cf}_x)$。
这些常数在所有共轭领形域上相同。将取值及半径函数下降，
便得到下方整个领形域上的恒等式。}
\end{proof}
