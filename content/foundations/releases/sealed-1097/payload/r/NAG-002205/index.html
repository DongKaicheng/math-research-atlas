Normalizing a nodal chart over a valuation ring

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 739

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Normalizing a nodal chart over a valuation ring}
{赋值环上结点坐标片的正规化}]
\label{cand:valuationnodenormalization}
\bi{Let $O$ be a valuation ring with fraction field $F$, and let
$a\in O$. Put $R_a=O[u,v]/(uv-a)$. The integral closure of $R_a$
in the normalization of $(R_a)_F$ is}
{设 $O$ 为分式域为 $F$ 的赋值环，$a\in O$，
并令 $R_a=O[u,v]/(uv-a)$。
$R_a$ 在 $(R_a)_F$ 的正规化中的整闭包为}
\[
 R_a'=
 \begin{cases}
 R_a,&a\ne0,\\
 O[u]\oplus O[v],&a=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
\bi{The ring $R_a'$ is normal, finite and finitely presented over
$R_a$, and flat and finitely presented over $O$.
Its geometric fibres over $O$ are smooth or nodal curves.}
{环 $R_a'$ 正规，在 $R_a$ 上有限且有限呈示，在 $O$ 上平坦且
有限呈示；其在 $O$ 上的几何纤维为光滑曲线或结点曲线。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A valuation ring is integrally closed in its fraction field:
if $x\notin O$ satisfied a monic equation over $O$, divide it by its
leading power of $x$; all other terms lie in the maximal ideal,
whereas the constant term is one. For $a\ne0$, substitution
$v=a/u$ identifies $R_a$ with the Laurent polynomials
$\sum_nc_nu^n$ satisfying}
{赋值环在其分式域中整闭：若 $x\notin O$ 满足 $O$ 上的首一方程，
用 $x$ 的最高次幂相除，则其余各项都属于极大理想，而常数项为一，
矛盾。对 $a\ne0$，代入 $v=a/u$ 将 $R_a$ 识别为满足以下条件
的 Laurent 多项式 $\sum_nc_nu^n$：}
\[
 c_n\in O\quad(n\geq0),\qquad
 c_n\in a^{-n}O\quad(n<0).
\]
\bi{Indeed the monomials $1,u,u^2,\ldots,v,v^2,\ldots$
span the quotient and their Laurent images are linearly independent
over $O$. Inside $F[u,u^{-1}]$ we therefore have}
{事实上，单项式 $1,u,u^2,\ldots,v,v^2,\ldots$
张成商环，其 Laurent 像在 $O$ 上线性无关。
因此在 $F[u,u^{-1}]$ 内有}
\[
 R_a=O[u,u^{-1}]\cap O[v,v^{-1}],\qquad v=a/u.
\]
\bi{Both rings on the right are normal domains by
\cite[Tags 030A, 00GY]{StacksAlg}; their intersection is integrally
closed in the common fraction field $F(u)$, since a monic equation
with coefficients in the intersection is a monic equation over
each ring. Hence $R_a$ is normal and is already integrally closed
in its generic ring $F[u,u^{-1}]$. The displayed monomial basis
also proves its $O$-flatness.}
{由 \cite[Tags 030A, 00GY]{StacksAlg}，右侧两个环均为正规整环。
它们的交在共同分式域 $F(u)$ 中整闭，因为系数在交中的首一方程
同时也是每个环上的首一方程。因此 $R_a$ 正规，在其泛环
$F[u,u^{-1}]$ 中已经整闭。所示单项式基还证明其 $O$-平坦性。}

\bi{For $a=0$, identify $R_0$ with the pairs in
$O[u]\oplus O[v]$ having the same constant term.
The larger ring is generated as an $R_0$-module by the two
idempotents $(1,0),(0,1)$, and has the finite presentation}
{对 $a=0$，将 $R_0$ 识别为 $O[u]\oplus O[v]$ 中常数项相同
的有序对。较大的环作为 $R_0$-模由两个幂等元
$(1,0),(0,1)$ 生成，并具有有限呈示}
\[
 R_0'=R_0[e]/\bigl(e^2-e,(1-e)u,ev\bigr),
          \qquad e=(1,0).
\]
\bi{It is normal, as a product of two normal polynomial rings.
If an element of $F[u]\oplus F[v]$ is integral over $R_0$,
each projection is integral over the corresponding polynomial
ring over $O$, so belongs to that ring. This proves the asserted
integral-closure identity. It is free as an $O$-module and has an
explicit finite presentation over $O$. Finally the fibres of
$R_a$ are $uv=\overline a$, and those of $R_0'$ are two disjoint
affine lines. These equations prove the geometric-fibre assertion.}
{它是两个正规多项式环的积，故正规。若
$F[u]\oplus F[v]$ 中的元素在 $R_0$ 上整，则每个投影都在
对应的 $O$ 上多项式环上整，因而属于该多项式环。
这证明所述整闭包等式。它作为 $O$-模自由，并在 $O$ 上具有
显式有限呈示。最后，$R_a$ 的纤维为 $uv=\overline a$，
$R_0'$ 的纤维为两条不交仿射直线；这些方程证明几何纤维断言。}
\end{proof}
