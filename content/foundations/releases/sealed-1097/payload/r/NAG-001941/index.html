Surjectivity of homogeneous graded reduction

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 533

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Surjectivity of homogeneous graded reduction}
{齐次分次约化的满射性}]
\label{fnd:gradedreductionsurjective}
\bi{For an arbitrary generalized $K$-affinoid algebra $A$, every
homogeneous prime ideal of $\operatorname{gr}A$ is the reduction
kernel of a point of $\mathcal M(A)$.}
{对任意广义 $K$-affinoid 代数 $A$，$\operatorname{gr}A$ 的
每个齐次素理想都是 $\mathcal M(A)$ 中某点的约化核。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First let $A$ be strict and let $P\subset\operatorname{gr}A$
be homogeneous prime. Its degree-one part is a prime
$P_1\subset\widetilde A$; realize it by $x$ using
Theorem~\ref{fnd:canonicalreductionsurjective}.
If the initial class of $a$ is nonzero of degree $r=\rho_A(a)$,
Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictspectralvaluegroup} supplies $e>0$ and
$c\in K^\times$ with $|c|=r^e$. The class of $a^e/c$ has
degree one. Primality shows that the initial class of $a$
belongs to $P$ exactly when this degree-one class belongs
to $P_1$, equivalently $|a(x)|<r$.
This proves that the full graded kernel of $x$ is $P$.}
{先设 $A$ 为 strict，令 $P\subset\operatorname{gr}A$
为齐次素理想。其一度部分为 $\widetilde A$ 的素理想 $P_1$；
由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:canonicalreductionsurjective} 用点 $x$
实现它。若 $a$ 的初始类非零且次数为 $r=\rho_A(a)$，由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictspectralvaluegroup}，存在 $e>0$ 和
$c\in K^\times$ 使 $|c|=r^e$。$a^e/c$ 的类具有次数一。
由素性，$a$ 的初始类属于 $P$ 当且仅当这个一度类属于 $P_1$，
也即 $|a(x)|<r$。故 $x$ 的整个分次核就是 $P$。}

\bi{In general choose a free polyradius $\boldsymbol r$ making
$A_{\boldsymbol r}$ strict.
By Theorem~\ref{fnd:gradedfreebasechange}, its graded reduction
is the Laurent polynomial algebra
$\operatorname{gr}A[t_1^{\pm1},\ldots,t_m^{\pm1}]$ with
the stated degrees. Extend $P$ to this Laurent polynomial
algebra; the resulting homogeneous ideal is prime because
$(\operatorname{gr}A)/P$ is a domain.
The strict case gives a point of $\mathcal M(A_{\boldsymbol r})$
with that kernel. Its restriction to $A$ has graded kernel $P$,
because the Gauss extension is spectrally isometric on $A$.}
{一般地，选择一个自由多半径 $\boldsymbol r$，使
$A_{\boldsymbol r}$ 为 strict。
由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:gradedfreebasechange}，其分次约化为赋予
所述次数的 Laurent 多项式代数
$\operatorname{gr}A[t_1^{\pm1},\ldots,t_m^{\pm1}]$。
把 $P$ 扩张到该 Laurent 多项式代数；由于
$(\operatorname{gr}A)/P$ 为整环，所得齐次理想仍为素理想。
Strict 情形给出 $\mathcal M(A_{\boldsymbol r})$ 中以它为核的点。
该点限制到 $A$ 后的分次核为 $P$，因为 Gauss 扩张在 $A$
上对谱范数等距。}
\end{proof}
