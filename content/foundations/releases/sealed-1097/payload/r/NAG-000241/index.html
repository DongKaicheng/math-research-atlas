1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}\label{fnd:weierstrassdivision}
\bi{Suppose $f=P+h$, where $P=T^d+\sum_{i<d}p_iT^i$ has $|p_i|\leq1$ and $\|h\|<1$, with $h$ involving only powers larger than $d$. For each $g\in K\langle T\rangle$ there are unique $q\in K\langle T\rangle$ and $s\in K[T]$, $\deg s<d$, such that $g=qf+s$. They satisfy $\|q\|,\|s\|\leq\|g\|$. For $d=0$ the remainder is zero.}
{设 $f=P+h$，其中 $P=T^d+\sum_{i<d}p_iT^i$ 满足 $|p_i|\leq1$，而 $\|h\|<1$，且 $h$ 只含高于 $d$ 次的项。对每个 $g\in K\langle T\rangle$，存在唯一 $q\in K\langle T\rangle$ 和 $s\in K[T]$，$\deg s<d$，使 $g=qf+s$，且 $\|q\|,\|s\|\leq\|g\|$。当 $d=0$ 时 remainder 为零。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Polynomial division by the monic polynomial $P$ gives operators $Q,R$ with $g=PQ(g)+R(g)$. Each elimination step has coefficients of size at most one, so both operators have norm at most one. They extend to the completion, with $R$ valued in the finite-dimensional space of polynomials of degree less than $d$.}
{对 monic polynomial $P$ 作 polynomial division，得到 operators $Q,R$，满足 $g=PQ(g)+R(g)$。每次 elimination 的 coefficients 的 size 不超过一，所以这两个 operators 的 norm 都不超过一。它们延拓到 completion，且 $R$ 的值位于 degree 小于 $d$ 的 polynomials 构成的 finite-dimensional space 中。}

\bi{Applying $Q$ to the desired identity gives $(I+E)q=Q(g)$, where $E(q)=Q(hq)$ and $\|E\|\leq\|h\|<1$. The convergent operator series $\sum_{j\geq0}(-E)^j$ is the inverse of $I+E$. Define $q$ by this inverse and put $s=R(g-hq)$. Then $Q(g-hq)=q$, so $g-hq=Pq+s$, as required. The inverse has norm at most one; hence $\|q\|\leq\|g\|$ and $\|s\|\leq\|g\|$. Any other division satisfies the same invertible operator equation and therefore has the same quotient and remainder.}
{对所需恒等式施加 $Q$，得到 $(I+E)q=Q(g)$，其中 $E(q)=Q(hq)$，且 $\|E\|\leq\|h\|<1$。收敛的 operator series $\sum_{j\geq0}(-E)^j$ 给出 $I+E$ 的 inverse。用此 inverse 定义 $q$，并令 $s=R(g-hq)$。于是 $Q(g-hq)=q$，故 $g-hq=Pq+s$。该 inverse 的 norm 不超过一，因此 $\|q\|\leq\|g\|$，且 $\|s\|\leq\|g\|$。任何其他 division 都满足同一个 invertible operator equation，故 quotient 与 remainder 唯一。}
\end{proof}
