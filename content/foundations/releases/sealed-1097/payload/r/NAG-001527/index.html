A Laurent refinement tree over the original algebra

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 239

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{A Laurent refinement tree over the original algebra}{原代数上的 Laurent 加细树}]\label{fnd:originalrationaltreerefinement}
\bi{Assume that the valuation of $K$ is nontrivial. Every finite covering of $X$ by objects of $\mathcal R_X$ has a subordinate finite Laurent tree in the sense just specified: each leaf is contained in one covering member. All nodes, including those obtained by comparing ratios in intermediate algebras, remain rational over the original $A$. The child and intersection maps agree with the actual elementary quotient maps.}
{假设 $K$ 的赋值非平凡。$X$ 的每个由 $\mathcal R_X$ 对象组成的有限覆盖，都有上述意义的从属有限 Laurent 树，即每个叶节点包含在某个覆盖成员中。全部节点，包括在中间代数中比较比值而产生的节点，仍在原始 $A$ 上为 rational。子节点及交叠映射与实际 elementary 商映射一致。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{If $A=0$, use the single empty node. Otherwise Proposition~\ref{fnd:finiterationalstandardrefinement} gives a standard rational refinement generated by a unit-ideal tuple $f_0,\ldots,f_s\in A$. Choose a unit certificate $\sum_i a_if_i=1$ and $\kappa\in K^\times$ with $|\kappa|\max_i\|a_i\|<1$. Split successively by the elementary Laurent pairs for $f_0/\kappa,\ldots,f_s/\kappa$. Each partial branch is an intersection of rational subsets over $A$: its positive condition has denominator $1$, and its negative condition has tuple $(f_i;\kappa)$, which generates the unit ideal because $\kappa\ne0$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation} and Lemma~\ref{fnd:iteratedstrictquotients} identify its algebra with the simultaneous relation quotient. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology} identifies each binary pair with a covering of its parent.}
{若 $A=0$，只取一个空节点。否则，Proposition~\ref{fnd:finiterationalstandardrefinement} 给出由单位理想 tuple $f_0,\ldots,f_s\in A$ 生成的标准 rational 加细。选择单位理想证书 $\sum_i a_if_i=1$ 及 $\kappa\in K^\times$，使 $|\kappa|\max_i\|a_i\|<1$。依次用 $f_0/\kappa,\ldots,f_s/\kappa$ 的 elementary Laurent 对作二分。每个部分分支都是 $A$ 上 rational 子集的交：其正条件的分母为 $1$，负条件的 tuple 为 $(f_i;\kappa)$，由于 $\kappa\ne0$ 而生成单位理想。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation} 及 Lemma~\ref{fnd:iteratedstrictquotients}，其代数与同时施加关系所得的商相识别。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology} 将每个二元对识别为其父节点的覆盖。}

\bi{Consider a completed first-stage branch with algebra $C$. Let $P$ and $N$ be its positive and negative index sets. Lemma~\ref{fnd:standardsmallscalarrefinement} says that $C=0$ when $N$ is empty. For $i\in N$, the element $f_i$ is a unit in $C$ and $\|\kappa/f_i\|\leq1$. For a nonzero branch, split further, successively, by the finitely many ratios $q_{ij}=f_i/f_j$ with $i<j$ in $N$. We verify that this second-stage operation stays in $\mathcal R_X$, rather than assuming rational transitivity.}
{考虑第一阶段完成后的一个分支，记其代数为 $C$，正负指标集分别为 $P$ 和 $N$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:standardsmallscalarrefinement}，当 $N$ 为空时有 $C=0$。对 $i\in N$，$f_i$ 在 $C$ 中为单位，且 $\|\kappa/f_i\|\leq1$。对非零分支，再依次按照 $N$ 中满足 $i<j$ 的有限多个比值 $q_{ij}=f_i/f_j$ 作二分。下面核对这个第二阶段仍保留在 $\mathcal R_X$ 内，而不预先假设 rational 传递性。}

\bi{The two rational subsets of the original $X$}
{原始 $X$ 中的以下两个 rational 子集}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 W_{ij}^+&=\{|f_i|\leq|f_j|,\ |\kappa|\leq|f_j|\},\\
 W_{ij}^-&=\{|f_j|\leq|f_i|,\ |\kappa|\leq|f_i|\}
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{have unit-ideal tuples $(f_j;f_i,\kappa)$ and $(f_i;f_j,\kappa)$. Let $D$ be $C$ or any partial second-stage branch. By induction its quotient map from $C$ is contractive, so both $f_i,f_j$ remain units and the bounds $\|\kappa/f_i\|,\|\kappa/f_j\|\leq1$ persist. Over $D$, intersection with $W_{ij}^+$ has the joint presentation}
{具有单位理想 tuple $(f_j;f_i,\kappa)$ 和 $(f_i;f_j,\kappa)$。令 $D$ 为 $C$ 或第二阶段的任意部分分支。由归纳，从 $C$ 出发的商映射为 contractive，因此 $f_i,f_j$ 仍为单位，且界 $\|\kappa/f_i\|,\|\kappa/f_j\|\leq1$ 保持成立。在 $D$ 上，与 $W_{ij}^+$ 的交具有联合表示}
\[
 D\{Z,Y\}/(f_jZ-f_i,f_jY-\kappa)
 \simeq D\{Z,Y\}/(Z-q_{ij},Y-\kappa/f_j)
 \simeq D\{Z\}/(Z-q_{ij}).
\]
\bi{The first equality uses a unit and does not change the actual ideal. In the second, inclusion and substitution $Y=\kappa/f_j$ are inverse contractions by Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalredundantconstraint} and the iterated-quotient lemma. Thus this is the positive elementary quotient with its actual quotient norm. The negative intersection is similarly $D\{Z\}/(Z-f_j/f_i)$, whose actual ideal equals $(1-q_{ij}Z)$ because $q_{ij}$ is a unit. Imposing both signs gives the same joint presentation as their elementary intersection. All maps fix $D$ and the indicated ratios, so these isometries commute with restriction. This proves the inductive presentation requirement for every partial branch. The subsets cover their parent because any two nonnegative real numbers are comparable and the scalar lower bounds already hold there.}
{第一个相等使用单位元，不改变实际理想。第二个识别中，包含映射与代入 $Y=\kappa/f_j$ 由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalredundantconstraint} 及迭代商引理互为 contractions。因此，这正是配备实际商范数的正 elementary 商。同理，负交叠为 $D\{Z\}/(Z-f_j/f_i)$；由于 $q_{ij}$ 为单位，其实际理想等于 $(1-q_{ij}Z)$。同时施加两种符号，得到的联合表示恰好等于 elementary 交叠。所有映射固定 $D$ 及指定比值，所以这些等距同构与限制映射交换。由此归纳核对了每个部分分支的表示要求。这两个子集覆盖其父节点，因为任意两个非负实数可比较，而且标量下界在父节点上已经成立。}

\bi{At a second-stage leaf, Lemma~\ref{fnd:unitstandardlaurentrefinement} chooses $k\in N$ with $\|f_i/f_k\|\leq1$ for every $i\in N$. For $p\in P$, write $f_p=\kappa X_p$ and $\kappa/f_k=X_k$ from the first-stage relations; then $f_p/f_k=X_pX_k$ also has norm at most one. Hence the leaf factors contractively through the $k$th member of the standard rational family. That member is contained in an original covering member. The construction has finitely many stages and finitely many branches. Empty branches use the zero algebra, as follows either from their explicit relation or from Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}. This completes the required finite tree.}
{在第二阶段的叶节点，Lemma~\ref{fnd:unitstandardlaurentrefinement} 选出 $k\in N$，使每个 $i\in N$ 都满足 $\|f_i/f_k\|\leq1$。对 $p\in P$，由第一阶段关系写出 $f_p=\kappa X_p$ 和 $\kappa/f_k=X_k$，则 $f_p/f_k=X_pX_k$ 的范数也至多为一。因此该叶节点 contractively 通过标准 rational 族的第 $k$ 个成员分解，而这个成员包含于原覆盖的某个成员中。该构造的阶段与分支均有限。空分支使用零代数，这可由显式关系或 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} 得到。至此构造出所需的有限树。}
\end{proof}

\section{\bi{Finite modules and Laurent descent}{有限模与 Laurent 下降}}\label{fnd:finitemodulelaurentdescent}

\bi{Tate exactness concerns the localizations of a module that is already defined over the original algebra. For coherence we must also construct a global module from finite modules given separately on a covering. The additional analytic step is a bounded lifting argument for arbitrary finite Laurent gluing data. We then use maximal residue fields to obtain generators and apply the lifting argument once more to the relations. This reconstructs the method of Kiehl's theorem as developed in \cite[\S9.4.3, Lemmas 5--6, pp.~378--382]{BGR84}, with the finite-module norms and the restricted site specified explicitly.}
{Tate 正合性处理的是已经定义在原代数上的模的局部化。凝聚性还要求从覆盖各部分分别给出的有限模构造整体模。额外的解析步骤，是对任意有限模 Laurent 粘合数据证明有界提升。随后借助极大剩余域取得生成元，并对关系模再次应用提升论证。下面重构 \cite[\S9.4.3, Lemmas 5--6, pp.~378--382]{BGR84} 所展开的 Kiehl 定理的方法，同时明确有限模范数及所用的受限 site。}

\bi{Unless an additional hypothesis is stated, $K$ is a complete real-valued non-Archimedean field, its valuation may be trivial, and $A$ is a strict $K$-affinoid algebra with a norm-admissible Banach norm. Relation ideals are actual ideals; their closedness and the canonical norms of finite modules are supplied by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules}. The zero algebra and its zero module are allowed separately. Radii belong to $|K^\times|$ and can be scaled to one. Ordinary tensor products in this section are algebraic tensor products. A completed-tensor assertion is made only after its finite-module comparison has been established.}
{除非另行声明，$K$ 是完备的实值非阿基米德域，允许赋值平凡；$A$ 为配备 norm-admissible Banach 范数的 strict $K$-affinoid 代数。关系理想均指实际理想；其闭性及有限模的 canonical 范数由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules} 给出。零代数及其零模作为独立情形允许在内。半径属于 $|K^\times|$，可缩放为一。本节的普通张量积均为代数张量积；只有在建立相应有限模比较之后，才作出完备张量积的断言。}

\bi{Write $X=\mathcal M(A)$, and use exactly the site $\mathcal R_X$ of Definition~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalpretopology}: its objects are rational subsets presented over the original $A$, and its coverings are finite set-theoretic coverings by such objects. In particular, the algebra of an object $V$ is denoted by $A_V$.}
{记 $X=\mathcal M(A)$，并严格采用 Definition~\ref{fnd:fixedrationalpretopology} 中的 site $\mathcal R_X$：其对象是在原始 $A$ 上表示的 rational 子集，其覆盖是这些对象组成的有限集合覆盖。特别地，将对象 $V$ 的代数记为 $A_V$。}

\begin{definition}[\bi{A finite Laurent tree in the fixed site}{固定 site 内的有限 Laurent 树}]\label{fnd:fixedrationaltree}
\bi{A finite Laurent tree in this site means a finite rooted binary tree with root $X$, such that each node $V$ is an object of $\mathcal R_X$ and each nonterminal node is covered by an elementary Laurent pair for some $h\in A_V$ and radius in $|K^\times|$. Its two child algebras and their intersection algebra must be the corresponding actual elementary quotients, up to the canonical bounded isomorphisms. Empty nodes may be terminal. This definition imposes the presentation requirement; it does not assert its existence.}
{本 site 内的有限 Laurent 树，是指以 $X$ 为根的有限二叉树，其中每个节点 $V$ 均属于 $\mathcal R_X$，且每个非终端节点由某个 $h\in A_V$ 及半径 $r\in|K^\times|$ 定义的 elementary Laurent 对覆盖。两个子节点代数及其交叠代数，须在典范有界同构意义下等于相应的实际 elementary 商。空节点可以作为终端节点。这个定义提出了表示要求，并未断言这种表示存在。}
\end{definition}
