Short Weierstrass equations and their parameter

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 732

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Short Weierstrass equations and their parameter}
{短 Weierstrass 方程及其参数}]
\label{cand:shortweierstrassreconstruction}
\bi{Let $F$ be a field of characteristic zero and let
$(E,O_E)$ be a smooth projective geometrically connected
genus-one curve with an $F$-rational point.
It has a pointed plane equation}
{设 $F$ 为特征零域，$(E,O_E)$ 为带有
$F$-有理点的光滑射影几何连通亏格一曲线。
它具有以下带点平面方程：}
\[
 y^2=x^3+Ax+B,\qquad
 O_E=(0:1:0),\qquad 4A^3+27B^2\ne0 .
\]
\bi{The value}
{数值}
\[
             j=1728\,\frac{4A^3}{4A^3+27B^2}
\]
\bi{is independent of the pointed short equation.
Two such equations with the same $j$ become pointed
isomorphic after a finite extension of $F$.}
{与带点短方程的选择无关。
具有相同 $j$ 的两个这样的方程，
在 $F$ 的某个有限扩张后带点同构。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Geometric connectedness and smoothness imply geometric
integrality, and \cite[Tag 04L2(4)]{StacksAlg} gives
$H^0(E,\mathcal O_E)=F$.
The curve is regular and hence Gorenstein by
\cite[Tags 056S and 0DWG]{StacksAlg}.
The Riemann--Roch and duality formulas
\cite[Tag 0BS6]{StacksAlg} give
$\deg\omega_E=0$ and}
{光滑性及几何连通性推出几何整性，
由 \cite[Tag 04L2(4)]{StacksAlg} 得
$H^0(E,\mathcal O_E)=F$。
由 \cite[Tags 056S and 0DWG]{StacksAlg}，
曲线正则，因而为 Gorenstein。
Riemann--Roch 与对偶公式
\cite[Tag 0BS6]{StacksAlg} 给出
$\deg\omega_E=0$，并且}
\[
        h^0(E,\mathcal O_E(nO_E))=n
                         \qquad(n\geq1).
\]
\bi{Indeed the dual term has degree $-n$ and no nonzero
section. A nonzero section of an invertible sheaf injects
$\mathcal O_E$ with a finite-length quotient, so its degree
is the nonnegative length of that quotient; this excludes
negative degree. Choose functions $x,y$ with exact pole
orders two and three at $O_E$ and no other poles.
The six functions $1,x,y,x^2,xy,x^3$ have distinct pole
orders and form a basis of $H^0(E,\mathcal O_E(6O_E))$.
Consequently $y^2$ is their linear combination, with a
nonzero $x^3$ coefficient. Scaling both $x,y$ by that
coefficient makes it one. This gives}
{因为对偶项的次数为 $-n$，故无非零截面。
可逆层的非零截面给出从 $\mathcal O_E$ 的单射，
商为有限长度层，所以该层次数为商的非负长度；
这排除了负次数。
选取仅在 $O_E$ 有极点、极点阶分别恰为二和三的函数
$x,y$。六个函数 $1,x,y,x^2,xy,x^3$
具有不同极点阶，构成
$H^0(E,\mathcal O_E(6O_E))$ 的一组基。
因此 $y^2$ 是它们的线性组合，
其中 $x^3$ 的系数非零。
将 $x,y$ 同时乘以该系数，可使它等于一，得到}
\[
         y^2+a_1xy+a_3y
               =x^3+a_2x^2+a_4x+a_6 .
\]
\bi{The linear system $|3O_E|$, with basis $1,x,y$,
is very ample by \cite[Tag 0H2V]{StacksAlg}.
Its image is therefore the indicated plane cubic.
Indeed the image is an integral plane curve, and its
homogeneous ideal is principal by
\cite[Tag 0BYB]{StacksAlg}.
If its homogeneous generator has degree $e$, the genus
formula \cite[Tag 0BYD]{StacksAlg} gives
$(e-1)(e-2)/2=1$, whence $e=3$.
The cubic already displayed lies in this ideal and is
nonzero, so it is a scalar multiple of the generator.
Completing the square in $y$ and eliminating the $x^2$
term by translation gives the stated short equation.
These coordinate changes fix $(0:1:0)$.
The partial derivatives of the short equation show
smoothness precisely when $4A^3+27B^2\ne0$.}
{线性系 $|3O_E|$ 以 $1,x,y$ 为基，
由 \cite[Tag 0H2V]{StacksAlg} 知其 very ample，
故其像就是所列平面三次曲线。
具体而言，像为整平面曲线，由
\cite[Tag 0BYB]{StacksAlg} 知其齐次理想为主理想。
若齐次生成元的次数为 $e$，则亏格公式
\cite[Tag 0BYD]{StacksAlg} 给出
$(e-1)(e-2)/2=1$，所以 $e=3$。
已列出的三次式属于该理想且非零，
故为生成元的非零常数倍。
对 $y$ 配方，再平移 $x$ 消去二次项，
即得所述短方程。坐标变化均固定 $(0:1:0)$。
计算短方程的偏导数可知，
光滑性恰等价于 $4A^3+27B^2\ne0$。}

\bi{A pointed isomorphism between short equations pulls
back the second $x$ to $\alpha x+r$ and the second $y$
to $\beta y+sx+t$, since it preserves the pole-order
filtration, with $\alpha\beta\ne0$.
Comparing the coefficients of $xy,y,x^3,x^2$ gives
$s=t=r=0$ and $\beta^2=\alpha^3$.
Thus with $u=\beta/\alpha$ one has
$\alpha=u^2$, $\beta=u^3$, $A'=u^4A$, and $B'=u^6B$.
This preserves $j$. Conversely, for $j\ne0,1728$ put
$r=A'/A$, $s=B'/B$. Equality of $j$ gives $r^3=s^2$.
After choosing $u$ with $u^2=s/r$, one has
$u^4=r$ and $u^6=s$, giving the isomorphism.
For $j=0$ take a sixth root of $B'/B$; for $j=1728$
take a fourth root of $A'/A$.
All these extensions are finite.}
{短方程之间的带点同构保持极点阶过滤，
故将第二个 $x$ 拉回为 $\alpha x+r$，
将第二个 $y$ 拉回为 $\beta y+sx+t$，
其中 $\alpha\beta\ne0$。
比较 $xy,y,x^3,x^2$ 的系数，
得到 $s=t=r=0$、$\beta^2=\alpha^3$。
因此令 $u=\beta/\alpha$，有
$\alpha=u^2$、$\beta=u^3$、
$A'=u^4A$、$B'=u^6B$，从而 $j$ 保持。
反之，若 $j\ne0,1728$，令
$r=A'/A$、$s=B'/B$。
$j$ 相等给出 $r^3=s^2$。
选择满足 $u^2=s/r$ 的 $u$ 后，
便有 $u^4=r$、$u^6=s$，给出所需同构。
若 $j=0$，取 $B'/B$ 的六次根；
若 $j=1728$，取 $A'/A$ 的四次根。
这些扩张均有限。}
\end{proof}
