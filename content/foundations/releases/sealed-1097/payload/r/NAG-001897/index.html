Coherent modules on a closed analytic subspace

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 405

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Coherent modules on a closed analytic subspace}
{闭解析子空间上的凝聚模}]
\label{fnd:closedanalyticcoherentextension}
\bi{Let \(i:Z\hookrightarrow X\) be a closed analytic subspace
defined by a coherent ideal \(\mathcal I\). Direct image
identifies coherent modules on \(Z_G\) with coherent modules
on \(X_G\) annihilated by \(\mathcal I\), and is exact.
If \(X\) has a finite affinoid cover with affinoid finite
intersections, then}
{设 \(i:Z\hookrightarrow X\) 是由凝聚理想
\(\mathcal I\) 定义的闭解析子空间。直接像把 \(Z_G\) 上的
凝聚模与 \(X_G\) 上被 \(\mathcal I\) 消灭的凝聚模等同，
并且是正合函子。若 \(X\) 有一个有限 affinoid 覆盖，
其有限交仍 affinoid，则}
\[
 H^q(Z_G,\mathcal E)=H^q(X_G,i_*\mathcal E).
\]
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{On an affinoid domain \(V=\mathcal M(A)\) the inverse
image is \(\mathcal M(A/J)\), where \(J\) is the finite
ideal representing \(\mathcal I|_V\). By the affinoid
Kiehl equivalence, a coherent module on it is a finite
\(A/J\)-module \(M\). Restriction of scalars identifies
such modules, with their morphisms and exact sequences,
with finite \(A\)-modules killed by \(J\). On a smaller
affinoid domain with algebra \(A'\), both restrictions are
\(A'\otimes_A M\), viewed over \(A'/JA'\). Thus these
identifications agree on overlaps and glue uniquely.
For the cohomology assertion, the inverse images of the
given affinoid cover and its intersections are affinoid,
and the two \v Cech coefficient complexes are literally
the same. Proposition~\ref{fnd:finiteaffinoidcohomology}
computes both derived cohomology groups from that complex.}
{在 affinoid 域 \(V=\mathcal M(A)\) 上，逆像为
\(\mathcal M(A/J)\)，其中 \(J\) 是表示
\(\mathcal I|_V\) 的有限理想。由 affinoid Kiehl 等价，
其上的凝聚模就是有限 \(A/J\)-模 \(M\)。限制标量将这些
模及其态射、正合列，分别与被 \(J\) 消灭的有限 \(A\)-模
及其态射、正合列等同。在代数为 \(A'\) 的较小 affinoid
域上，两种限制都是 \(A'\otimes_A M\)，视为
\(A'/JA'\)-模。因此这些识别在重叠上一致并唯一粘合。
对上同调断言，给定覆盖及其交叠的逆像仍 affinoid，
且两个 \v Cech 系数复形完全相同。
Proposition~\ref{fnd:finiteaffinoidcohomology}
用这个复形同时计算两边的导出上同调。}
\end{proof}
