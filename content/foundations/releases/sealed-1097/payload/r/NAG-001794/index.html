\begin{lemma}[\bi{Ordinary local rings of \'etale morphisms}{\'Etale 态射的通常局部环}]
\label{fnd:etaleordinaryregular}
\bi{For an \'etale morphism of good analytic spaces, every induced ordinary local homomorphism is regular.}
{对 good 解析空间之间的 \'etale 态射，每个诱导的通常局部同态都正则。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use the ordinary finite neighborhoods in the definition of \'etaleness. For the corresponding finite \'etale affinoid algebra $A\to B$ and $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$, Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation} expresses each source stalk as a local factor of $B\otimes_A R$. Finite flatness and vanishing of algebraic relative differentials survive this base change and passage to a factor, as in Proposition~\ref{fnd:finiteetalequasismooth}. Hence the local map is flat. At every algebraic prime of $R$, its fibre is a factor of a finite \'etale algebra over the residue field. After any field extension it remains finite \'etale: finite flatness and the standard \'etale presentations are preserved by tensor product. A finite \'etale algebra over a field is a product of finite separable fields. Indeed its standard presentations have an invertible polynomial derivative, so after an algebraic closure all their local factors are copies of that field, with no nilpotents; descent gives the asserted finite separable factors. Thus all the algebraic fibres and all their finite field extensions are regular. This proves geometric regularity.}
{在 \'etale 定义中的普通有限邻域上工作。对相应的有限 \'etale affinoid 代数 $A\to B$ 及 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$，Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation} 将每个源茎表示为 $B\otimes_A R$ 的一个局部因子。如 Proposition~\ref{fnd:finiteetalequasismooth} 中一样，有限平坦性及代数相对微分的消失在此基变换及取因子后保持，所以局部映射平坦。在 $R$ 的每个代数素理想处，其纤维是剩余域上有限 \'etale 代数的一个因子。任意域扩张后它仍有限 \'etale，因为有限平坦性及 standard \'etale 表示在张量积后保持。域上的有限 \'etale 代数是有限可分域的乘积：其 standard 表示具有可逆多项式导数，所以到代数闭包之后，其全部局部因子都等于该域，且无幂零元；再下降即可得到所述有限可分因子。因此全部代数纤维及其所有有限域扩张都正则，这就是几何正则性。}
\end{proof}
