Admissibility of a finite closed cover

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 137

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Admissibility of a finite closed cover}{Finite closed cover 的 admissibility}]\label{fnd:finiteclosedcover}
\bi{Let $F_1,\ldots,F_m$ be closed subsets covering a topological space $X$. For each $x\in X$, the union of those $F_i$ containing $x$ contains an ordinary open neighborhood of $x$.}
{设 closed subsets $F_1,\ldots,F_m$ 覆盖 topological space $X$。对每个 $x\in X$，所有包含 $x$ 的 $F_i$ 之并包含 $x$ 的一个 ordinary open neighborhood。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The finite union of those $F_j$ not containing $x$ is closed and misses $x$. Its complement is the required open neighborhood, since the entire family covers $X$.}
{所有不包含 $x$ 的 $F_j$ 的有限并为 closed，且不含 $x$。其 complement 是所需 open neighborhood，因为整个 family 覆盖 $X$。}
\end{proof}
