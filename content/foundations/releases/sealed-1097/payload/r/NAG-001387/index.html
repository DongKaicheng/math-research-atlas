The topology alone is insufficient

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 62

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{example}[\bi{The topology alone is insufficient}{仅有 topology 不够}]\label{fnd:normtopologycounterexample}
\bi{Let $K$ be trivially valued. The two norms on $K[T]$ given by}
{设 $K$ 带 trivial valuation。在 $K[T]$ 上定义两个 norms：}
\[
 \Big\|\sum a_nT^n\Big\|_1=\max_n|a_n|,
 \qquad
 \Big\|\sum a_nT^n\Big\|_w=\max_n(n+1)|a_n|.
\]
\bi{They make $K[T]$ complete Banach algebras with the same discrete topology, but they are not uniformly equivalent. The identity from the second norm to the first is bounded and has no bounded inverse.}
{它们使 $K[T]$ 成为具有相同 discrete topology 的 complete Banach algebras，但并不 uniformly equivalent。从第二个 norm 到第一个 norm 的恒等映射 bounded，却没有 bounded inverse。}
\end{example}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The coefficientwise ultrametric inequality gives the triangle rules and $K$-homogeneity. Submultiplicativity of the second norm follows from $i+j+1\leq(i+1)(j+1)$; its unit has norm one. Every nonzero element has norm at least one for both norms, so both topologies are discrete and both spaces complete. But $\|T^n\|_w=n+1$ and $\|T^n\|_1=1$, which disproves a uniform reverse bound.}
{Coefficientwise ultrametric inequality 给出 triangle rules 与 $K$-homogeneity。第二个 norm 的 submultiplicativity 来自 $i+j+1\leq(i+1)(j+1)$，且其 unit 的 norm 为一。对两个 norms，每个非零元素的 norm 均至少为一，所以两种 topologies 都 discrete，空间也都 complete。但 $\|T^n\|_w=n+1$ 而 $\|T^n\|_1=1$，不存在统一的反向 bound。}

\bi{For $r>1$, the generalized Tate algebra $K\{r^{-1}S\}$ is $K[S]$. The map $S\mapsto T$ to the second normed algebra is bounded, because $(n+1)/r^n$ is bounded, and is a topological quotient isomorphism. Its quotient norm, however, assigns $r^n$ to $T^n$, and is not equivalent to $n+1$. Thus the topology-only convention even admits presentations outside the intended affinoid category.}
{当 $r>1$ 时，generalized Tate algebra $K\{r^{-1}S\}$ 等于 $K[S]$。到第二个 normed algebra 的 map $S\mapsto T$ 是 bounded 的，因为 $(n+1)/r^n$ 有界，并且它是 topological quotient isomorphism。但其 quotient norm 将 $T^n$ 的 norm 定为 $r^n$，与 $n+1$ 并不等价。因此仅用 topology 的约定甚至会接受不属于预期 affinoid category 的 presentations。}
\end{proof}
