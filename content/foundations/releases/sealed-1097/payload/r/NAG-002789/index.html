\begin{lemma}[Analytic purity supplies incomparability; F8CA-P]
\label{lem:pure}
Let \(A\) be a generalized affinoid over a complete non-Archimedean
field with its specified absolute value, and let \(a,b\in A\).
Suppose the nonempty actual zero spaces \(\cM(A/(a))\) and
\(\cM(A/(b))\) are both pure of one common analytic dimension \(d\).
Empty zero spaces are allowed. Then their minimal-prime union is
an antichain, \eqref{eq:union} holds, and the product zero space is
empty or pure of dimension \(d\).
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Every prime in the union defines an integral affinoid component of
analytic dimension \(d\). If distinct members \(q\subsetneq p\)
were comparable, the nonzero ideal \(p/q\) in the integral pure
\(d\)-dimensional algebra \(A/q\) would, by frozen
\tagg{NAG-002374}, give
\[
 \dim\cM(A/p)\leq d-1
\]
(or an empty quotient when \(d=0\)). This contradicts its dimension
\(d\). Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:minimal}. The listed primes are exactly
the irreducible components of the product zero space, so all its
components have dimension \(d\). This argument uses analytic dimension,
not an unproved equality with the Krull dimension of a generalized
affinoid ring.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
For a Noetherian ring \(A\), write \(\Min_A(a)\) for the primes of
\(A\) minimal over \((a)\), and set \(\Min_A(a)=\varnothing\) when
\(a\) is a unit. A finite collection of primes is an antichain if
no two distinct members are comparable by inclusion.
