Preservation and reflection of strict exactness

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 98

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Preservation and reflection of strict exactness}{严格正合性的保持与反射}]\label{fnd:freepolyradiusstrictexactness}
\bi{Let $r$ be finite and free over $K$. For any composable bounded maps $E\xrightarrow{i}F\xrightarrow{q}G$ of Banach $K$-spaces with $qi=0$, the sequence}
{设 $r$ 为在 $K$ 上自由的有限元组。对 Banach $K$-空间之间任意满足 $qi=0$ 的可复合有界映射 $E\xrightarrow{i}F\xrightarrow{q}G$，序列}
\[
 0\longrightarrow E\xrightarrow{i}F\xrightarrow{q}G\longrightarrow0
\]
\bi{is norm-strict exact if and only if its separated completed scalar extension to $K_r$ is norm-strict exact. More precisely, suppose $C,D>0$, $\|e\|\leq D\|i(e)\|$, and every $g\in G$ has a lift $f\in F$ with $\|f\|\leq C\|g\|$. The same constants then work after scalar extension. Conversely, any such constants after scalar extension work before extension. In particular scalar extension detects zero spaces and reflects isomorphisms with bounded inverse. No statement about uncompleted tensor flatness is part of this theorem.}
{严格正合，当且仅当它向 $K_r$ 的 separated completed 标量扩张严格正合。更精确地，设 $C,D>0$，有 $\|e\|\leq D\|i(e)\|$，且每个 $g\in G$ 都具有满足 $\|f\|\leq C\|g\|$ 的提升 $f\in F$。则同样的常数在标量扩张后仍然适用。反之，扩张后的任意这样的常数在扩张前也适用。特别地，标量扩张检测零空间，并反射具有有界逆的同构。此定理不包含未完备张量积的平坦性断言。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{By the definition of norm-strictness, a strict exact sequence admits positive constants of the stated kind. If a quotient-norm infimum is not attained, enlarge the norm-comparison constant to obtain actual lifts. No open-mapping theorem is needed for this passage.}
{由 norm-strictness 的定义，严格正合列具有所述正数常数。若商范数的下确界不能取到，则放大范数比较常数即可得到实际提升。这个步骤不需要开映射定理。}

\bi{Use the coefficient isometries of Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients}. Suppose first that the original sequence is norm-strict exact. A coefficient family in the kernel of $q_r$ has all its coefficients in $\ker q=\operatorname{im}i$. Their unique lifts to $E$ have weighted norms bounded by $D$ times the corresponding original weighted norms. They therefore form an element of $c_0(\mathbf Z^s,r;E)$. This proves middle exactness and the bound for the inverse of $i_r$ on its image; injectivity follows coefficientwise. For $(g_\nu)\in c_0(\mathbf Z^s,r;G)$, choose $f_\nu$ with $q(f_\nu)=g_\nu$ and $\|f_\nu\|\leq C\|g_\nu\|$, choosing $f_\nu=0$ when $g_\nu=0$. The same estimate shows that $(f_\nu)$ belongs to $c_0(\mathbf Z^s,r;F)$. This proves surjectivity of $q_r$ with the stated lifting bound. Since its kernel is closed, these facts give norm-strict exactness.}
{使用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients} 的系数等距同构。先假设原序列严格正合。$q_r$ 的核中的系数族，其各系数都属于 $\ker q=\operatorname{im}i$。这些系数在 $E$ 中的唯一提升，其加权范数至多为原加权范数的 $D$ 倍，所以构成 $c_0(\mathbf Z^s,r;E)$ 中的元素。这证明中间正合性及 $i_r$ 在其像上的逆的界；单射性逐系数成立。对 $(g_\nu)\in c_0(\mathbf Z^s,r;G)$，选择满足 $q(f_\nu)=g_\nu$ 及 $\|f_\nu\|\leq C\|g_\nu\|$ 的 $f_\nu$，并在 $g_\nu=0$ 时取 $f_\nu=0$。同一估计说明 $(f_\nu)$ 属于 $c_0(\mathbf Z^s,r;F)$。这证明 $q_r$ 满射且具有所述提升界。由于其核闭，以上事实给出严格正合性。}

\bi{Conversely, suppose the extended sequence is norm-strict exact. Constant-family embeddings show that $i$ is injective. If $f\in\ker q$, exactness after extension gives an $e'\in E\widehat\otimes_K K_r$ with $i_r(e')=\iota_F(f)$. Apply the zeroth-coefficient map: naturality gives $i(p_0e')=f$. Thus $\ker q=\operatorname{im}i$. A lift of $\iota_G(g)$ through $q_r$, followed by $p_0$, similarly lifts any $g$ through $q$. If the extended lift has norm at most $C\|g\|$, its zeroth coefficient has norm at most the same quantity. Applying an extended inverse bound for $i_r$ to a constant family yields $\|e\|\leq D\|i(e)\|$. These are precisely the required bounds. Again $\operatorname{im}i=\ker q$ is closed.}
{反之，设扩张后的序列严格正合。常系数嵌入说明 $i$ 单射。若 $f\in\ker q$，则扩张后的正合性给出 $e'\in E\widehat\otimes_K K_r$，使 $i_r(e')=\iota_F(f)$。应用零次系数映射，由自然性得 $i(p_0e')=f$，所以 $\ker q=\operatorname{im}i$。同样地，先通过 $q_r$ 提升 $\iota_G(g)$，再取 $p_0$，就通过 $q$ 提升任意 $g$。若扩张后的提升范数至多为 $C\|g\|$，其零次系数的范数也至多为同一量。将 $i_r$ 的扩张后逆映射界用于常系数族，则得 $\|e\|\leq D\|i(e)\|$。这些正是所需的界。此时 $\operatorname{im}i=\ker q$ 同样为闭子空间。}

\bi{The isometric constant-family embedding detects zero spaces. To see the last assertion directly for a bounded map $u:E\to F$ whose extension is an isomorphism with bounded inverse, lift every constant $\iota_F(f)$ through $u_r$ and apply $p_0$; this proves surjectivity and the inverse bound for $u$. Its injectivity follows from the constant embedding and injectivity of $u_r$. This argument uses the explicit Banach-space retraction, not an unproved algebraic faithful-flatness statement.}
{等距的常系数嵌入检测零空间。最后，设有界映射 $u:E\to F$ 的扩张为具有有界逆的同构。通过 $u_r$ 提升每个常系数族 $\iota_F(f)$ 再取 $p_0$，即得 $u$ 的满射性及逆映射界。其单射性来自常系数嵌入及 $u_r$ 的单射性。此论证使用明确的 Banach 空间收缩，而不使用尚未证明的代数忠实平坦性。}
\end{proof}
