Intersections and restrictions of affinoid domains

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 369

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Intersections and restrictions of affinoid domains}{Affinoid 域的交与限制}]
\label{fnd:generalizeddomainintersections}
\bi{For affinoid domains $U,V\subseteq\mathcal M_K(A)$ represented by $B,C$, their intersection is affinoid and is represented by $B\widehat\otimes_A C$. All finite intersections have the corresponding iterated completed pushout algebras, independently of parenthesization. If $U\subseteq V$, the canonical restriction $C\to B$ represents an affinoid domain and}
{设 $B,C$ 分别表示 $\mathcal M_K(A)$ 中的 affinoid 域 $U,V$。则 $U\cap V$ 为 affinoid，由 $B\widehat\otimes_A C$ 表示。所有有限交都由相应的迭代完备 pushout 代数表示，且不依赖括号顺序。若 $U\subseteq V$，则典范限制 $C\to B$ 表示 affinoid 域，并且}
\[
 B\widehat\otimes_A C\simeq B.
\]
\bi{All these identifications are canonical and bounded in both directions.}
{全部识别均典范且双向有界。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange} with $L=K$ to the domain $U$ and the map $A\to C$ representing $V$. Its inverse image is $U\cap V$, viewed as a domain of $V$. Domain transitivity in Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainrestriction} makes it a domain of $\mathcal M_K(A)$. Iteration and uniqueness give the finite-intersection and parenthesization assertions. If $U\subseteq V$, the restriction part of that lemma and the same uniqueness identify the pushout with $B$.}
{在 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange} 中取 $L=K$，对域 $U$ 及表示 $V$ 的映射 $A\to C$ 作基变换。其逆像就是作为 $V$ 中域的 $U\cap V$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainrestriction} 的域传递性，它也是 $\mathcal M_K(A)$ 中的域。迭代与唯一性给出有限交及括号顺序的断言。若 $U\subseteq V$，同一引理的限制断言及相同的唯一性将 pushout 与 $B$ 识别。}
\end{proof}
