Stable reduction over a valuation ring

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 656

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Stable reduction over a valuation ring}{赋值环上的稳定约化}]
\label{cand:stablevaluationextension}
\bi{Let $R$ be a valuation ring with fraction field $K$, and let $C/K$ be a smooth projective geometrically connected curve of genus $g\geq2$. There is a finite field extension $L/K$ such that, for every valuation ring $R'\subset L$ with fraction field $L$ extending $R$, the curve $C_L$ has a stable model $\mathcal C/R'$ which is a projective scheme, flat and of finite presentation over $R'$. If $R$ has rank one, then $R'$ has rank one.}
{设 $R$ 为分式域为 $K$ 的赋值环，$C/K$ 为亏格 $g\geq2$ 的光滑射影几何连通曲线。存在有限域扩张 $L/K$，使得对每个分式域为 $L$、延拓 $R$ 的赋值环 $R'\subset L$，曲线 $C_L$ 都有稳定模型 $\mathcal C/R'$；它为 $R'$ 上的射影概形，且平坦、有限呈示。若 $R$ 的秩为一，则 $R'$ 的秩也为一。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The curve $C$ gives a $K$-object of the stack $\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,R}$ of stable curves of genus $g$, by \cite[Tags 0E75, 0E78]{StacksAlg}. This stack is proper over $R$: \cite[Tag 0E9C]{StacksAlg} proves properness over $\mathbf Z$, and \cite[Tag 0CL6]{StacksAlg} preserves it under base change. Lemma~\ref{cand:properstackfinitevaluation} therefore gives a finite extension $L/K$ and, over each $R'$ as stated, a stable family of algebraic spaces $f:\mathcal C\to\operatorname{Spec}R'$ whose generic fibre is identified with $C_L$. As an object of the stack of curves, this family is proper, flat and of finite presentation by \cite[Tag 0D4Z]{StacksAlg}.}
{由 \cite[Tags 0E75, 0E78]{StacksAlg}，曲线 $C$ 给出亏格 $g$ 稳定曲线模叠 $\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,R}$ 的一个 $K$-对象。此模叠在 $R$ 上 proper：\cite[Tag 0E9C]{StacksAlg} 证明它在 $\mathbf Z$ 上 proper，而 \cite[Tag 0CL6]{StacksAlg} 说明此性质在基变换下保持。因此，Lemma~\ref{cand:properstackfinitevaluation} 给出有限扩张 $L/K$，以及每个所述 $R'$ 上的稳定代数空间族 $f:\mathcal C\to\operatorname{Spec}R'$，并将其泛纤维与 $C_L$ 识别。作为曲线模叠的对象，根据 \cite[Tag 0D4Z]{StacksAlg}，这个族 proper、平坦且有限呈示。}

\bi{We verify that this family is a projective scheme. Let $s$ be the closed point of $\operatorname{Spec}R'$. Stability of $\mathcal C_s$ and \cite[Tag 0E74]{StacksAlg} give an open neighbourhood $U$ of $s$ on which $\omega_{\mathcal C/R'}$ is relatively ample. Every open neighbourhood of the closed point of a local spectrum is the whole spectrum: a basic open $D(a)$ containing that point has $a$ a unit. Hence $U=\operatorname{Spec}R'$. By \cite[Tag 0D32]{StacksAlg}, relative ampleness over the scheme $\operatorname{Spec}R'$ implies that $\mathcal C$ is a scheme. Properness and the ample invertible sheaf make $f$ locally projective by \cite[Tag 0B5N]{StacksAlg}. Apply the same local-spectrum observation to the projective neighbourhood of $s$; thus $f$ is projective.}
{下面验证所得族为射影概形。设 $s$ 为 $\operatorname{Spec}R'$ 的闭点。由 $\mathcal C_s$ 的稳定性及 \cite[Tag 0E74]{StacksAlg}，存在 $s$ 的开邻域 $U$，使 $\omega_{\mathcal C/R'}$ 在其上相对 ample。局部环谱中包含闭点的开邻域必为整个谱：包含该点的基本开集 $D(a)$ 中，$a$ 必为单位元。因此 $U=\operatorname{Spec}R'$。由 \cite[Tag 0D32]{StacksAlg}，在概形 $\operatorname{Spec}R'$ 上的相对 ampleness 说明 $\mathcal C$ 是概形。根据 \cite[Tag 0B5N]{StacksAlg}，properness 与此 ample 可逆层使 $f$ 局部射影。再对 $s$ 的射影邻域应用同一个局部谱观察，即得 $f$ 射影。}

\bi{For the last assertion, the value group of $R$ has finite index in that of $R'$ by \cite[Tag 0ASH]{StacksAlg}. Write the groups additively as $\Gamma\subset\Gamma'$. Every element of $\Gamma'$ has a positive integer multiple in $\Gamma$. If $H'\subset\Gamma'$ is a nonzero convex subgroup, multiplying one of its nonzero elements shows that $H'\cap\Gamma$ is nonzero and convex in $\Gamma$. Rank one of $\Gamma$ gives $H'\cap\Gamma=\Gamma$. For any positive $\gamma'\in\Gamma'$, some $n\gamma'$ lies in $\Gamma\subset H'$; convexity then gives $\gamma'\in H'$. Thus $H'=\Gamma'$, proving that $\Gamma'$ has rank one.}
{对最后的断言，由 \cite[Tag 0ASH]{StacksAlg}，$R$ 的值群在 $R'$ 的值群中具有有限指数。将二者以加法记号写为 $\Gamma\subset\Gamma'$。$\Gamma'$ 中每个元素都有正整数倍落入 $\Gamma$。若 $H'\subset\Gamma'$ 为非零凸子群，则将其中一个非零元素乘以适当正整数可知，$H'\cap\Gamma$ 是 $\Gamma$ 的非零凸子群。由 $\Gamma$ 的秩为一，$H'\cap\Gamma=\Gamma$。对任意正元素 $\gamma'\in\Gamma'$，某个 $n\gamma'$ 属于 $\Gamma\subset H'$，由凸性便有 $\gamma'\in H'$。因此 $H'=\Gamma'$，即 $\Gamma'$ 的秩为一。}
\end{proof}
