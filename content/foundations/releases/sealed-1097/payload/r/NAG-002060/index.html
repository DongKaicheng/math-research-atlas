The integral algebra of a residue-field lift

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 677

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The integral algebra of a residue-field lift}
{剩余扩张提升的整系数代数}]
\label{cand:unramifiedmodelbasechange}
\bi{Let $l/k$ be finite separable and let
$L=K(\alpha)$ be the lift constructed in
Lemma~\ref{cand:residueextensionlift}: the minimal polynomial
$P\in O[T]$ of $\alpha$ is monic and reduces to the separable
minimal polynomial of a generator of $l/k$. Put $d=[l:k]$.
Then}
{设 $l/k$ 有限可分，并令 $L=K(\alpha)$ 为
Lemma~\ref{cand:residueextensionlift} 构造的提升：$\alpha$ 的
极小多项式 $P\in O[T]$ 首一，且约化为 $l/k$ 的一个生成元的
可分极小多项式。令 $d=[l:k]$，则}
\[
 \left|\sum_{i=0}^{d-1}c_i\alpha^i\right|=\max_i|c_i|,
 \qquad L^\circ=\bigoplus_{i=0}^{d-1}O\alpha^i=O[T]/(P),
 \qquad L^{\circ\circ}=K^{\circ\circ}L^\circ.
\]
\bi{For every flat finitely presented $O$-scheme $\mathcal X$, its
formal completion and analytic generic fibre commute with this
coefficient extension. On a completed affine algebra $A_0$ the
corresponding formulas are}
{对每个平坦有限表示 $O$-概形 $\mathcal X$，其形式完备化和
解析泛纤维与此系数扩张相容。在完成后的仿射代数 $A_0$ 上，
对应公式为}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 B_0&=A_0\otimes_O L^\circ=\bigoplus_{i=0}^{d-1}A_0\alpha^i,\\
 B_0[1/\pi]&=A\widehat\otimes_K L,\\
 B_0/L^{\circ\circ}B_0&=(A_0/K^{\circ\circ}A_0)\otimes_k l.
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{If the original special fibre is reduced, so is the extended
special fibre. Specialization commutes with the projection, and
the inverse image of a closed-point tube is the disjoint union of
the tubes of the points above that closed point.}
{若原特殊纤维约化，则扩张后的特殊纤维也约化。特化与投影
相容，且闭点管域的逆像为该闭点上方各点的管域的不交并。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For a nonzero coefficient vector, divide by a coefficient of
largest absolute value. All normalized coefficients lie in $O$,
and at least one has nonzero reduction. Linear independence of
$1,\bar\alpha,\ldots,\bar\alpha^{d-1}$ over $k$ makes the
reduction of this normalized sum nonzero, hence its absolute value
is one. This proves the norm formula. It proves the two integral
identifications as well, since membership in the closed or open
unit ball is equivalent to that property for every coefficient.}
{对非零系数向量，用绝对值最大的一个系数相除。全部归一化
系数属于 $O$，且至少一个约化非零。由
$1,\bar\alpha,\ldots,\bar\alpha^{d-1}$ 在 $k$ 上线性无关，
归一化之和的约化非零，所以其绝对值等于一。这证明范数公式。
闭单位球或开单位球中的归属逐系数判定，因而也证明两个
整系数识别。}

\bi{A finite free direct sum commutes with every quotient by
$\pi^n$ and with the inverse limit defining completion. Thus for
an affine algebraic chart, completing its base change gives
exactly $B_0$; Lemma~\ref{cand:affineflatformalcompletion} makes
this an admissible model. After inverting $\pi$, the finite
coordinate comparison in the proof of
Lemma~\ref{cand:finitegaloisfibres}, which uses only finiteness of
$L/K$, identifies the algebraic tensor with the completed tensor.
The displayed special-fibre formula follows from
$L^{\circ\circ}=K^{\circ\circ}L^\circ$. These constructions
respect affine restriction, so they glue.}
{有限自由直和与每个模 $\pi^n$ 商及定义完备化的逆极限相容。
因此对仿射代数坐标片，基变换后的完备化恰为 $B_0$；由
Lemma~\ref{cand:affineflatformalcompletion}，它为 admissible
模型。逆转 $\pi$ 后，Lemma~\ref{cand:finitegaloisfibres} 证明中
只使用 $L/K$ 有限性的有限坐标比较，将代数张量积识别为
完备张量积。特殊纤维公式来自
$L^{\circ\circ}=K^{\circ\circ}L^\circ$。这些构造与仿射限制
相容，故可粘合。}

\bi{For completeness, separability preserves the required
reducedness directly. A reduced finite-type $k$-algebra $R$ injects
into the finite product of the fraction fields of its minimal-prime
quotients. Tensoring with $l$, which is flat over $k$, preserves
this injection. Each field tensor with $l=k[T]/(p)$ is reduced:
$p$ remains relatively prime to its derivative and hence factors
into distinct irreducible factors. Thus $R\otimes_k l$ embeds in
a product of reduced rings and is reduced. Finally, specialization
is defined by the elements of absolute value less than one.
The displayed algebra maps therefore make it commute with the
projection. Taking inverse images of a singleton residual point
gives the asserted tube decomposition.}
{为完整起见，可直接验证可分性保持所需约化性。约化有限型
$k$-代数 $R$ 注入其有限个极小素商的分式域之积。与 $k$ 上
平坦的 $l$ 作张量积保持此单射。每个域与 $l=k[T]/(p)$ 的
张量积都约化：$p$ 与其导数在扩张后仍互素，故分解为互异
不可约因子。因此 $R\otimes_k l$ 嵌入约化环之积，从而约化。
最后，特化由绝对值小于一的元素定义，所列代数映射因此使
特化与投影相容。对单个剩余点取逆像即得所述管域分解。}
\end{proof}
