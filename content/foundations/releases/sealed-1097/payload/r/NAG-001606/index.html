\begin{lemma}[\bi{Topology and bounded gluing}{拓扑与有界粘合}]
\label{fnd:nettopologyboundedgluing}
\bi{A quasi-net determines the topology: a subset $O\subseteq X$ is open if and only if $O\cap V$ is relatively open for every member $V$. Let $Z=\mathcal M(B)$ and let $Z_1,\ldots,Z_m$ be an affinoid-domain cover. Compatible bounded homomorphisms $C\to B_{Z_i}$ from an affinoid algebra $C$ glue uniquely to a bounded homomorphism $C\to B$. Their induced maps on spectra glue to the induced map on $Z$.}
{Quasi-net 决定拓扑：子集 $O\subseteq X$ 开，当且仅当它与每个成员 $V$ 的交在 $V$ 中相对开。令 $Z=\mathcal M(B)$，并取 affinoid 域覆盖 $Z_1,\ldots,Z_m$。来自某个 affinoid 代数 $C$ 的相容有界同态 $C\to B_{Z_i}$ 唯一粘合为有界同态 $C\to B$；所诱导的谱映射也粘合为 $Z$ 上的对应映射。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Suppose all $O\cap V$ are open and take $x\in O$. Choose $V_1,\ldots,V_m$ containing $x$ whose union contains a neighborhood $N$ of $x$. Write $O\cap V_i=O_i\cap V_i$ near $x$, with $O_i$ open in $X$ and containing $x$. An open neighborhood of $x$ contained in $N\cap\bigcap_iO_i$ lies in $O$. This proves the topological assertion. For bounded gluing, the degree-zero statement of Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidcovercech} identifies $B$, by a bounded isomorphism, with the matching kernel of the finite diagram of the $B_{Z_i}$. The given homomorphisms take values in that kernel. Composition with its bounded inverse gives the required map; addition, multiplication and the unit can be checked after the injective restriction to the product. Evaluation on each $Z_i$ proves the assertion about spectra.}
{设所有 $O\cap V$ 都开，并取 $x\in O$。选取均包含 $x$ 的 $V_1,\ldots,V_m$，使其并包含 $x$ 的邻域 $N$。在 $x$ 附近可写 $O\cap V_i=O_i\cap V_i$，其中 $O_i$ 在 $X$ 中开且包含 $x$。取包含于 $N\cap\bigcap_iO_i$ 的开邻域，便得到 $x$ 的一个包含于 $O$ 的开邻域。对于有界粘合，Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidcovercech} 的零次断言以有界同构将 $B$ 识别为各 $B_{Z_i}$ 有限图表的匹配核。给定同态落入这个核，再与其有界逆复合即可。加法、乘法及单位的保持，都可通过到乘积的单射限制检查。在每个 $Z_i$ 上取值即得到谱映射断言。}
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[\bi{An affinoid atlas}{Affinoid atlas}]
\label{fnd:analyticnetatlas}
\bi{Let $X$ be a locally Hausdorff topological space. A net $\tau$ of compact Hausdorff subsets is a quasi-net such that $\tau|_{U\cap V}=\{W\in\tau:W\subseteq U\cap V\}$ is a quasi-net of $U\cap V$ for every $U,V\in\tau$. An affinoid atlas consists of such a net, affinoid algebras $A_V$, homeomorphisms $V\simeq\mathcal M(A_V)$, and bounded homomorphisms $A_V\to A_U$ for inclusions $U\subseteq V$ in $\tau$. The latter identify $U$ with an affinoid domain of $V$, and are compatible with composition.}
{设 $X$ 为局部 Hausdorff 拓扑空间。由紧 Hausdorff 子集组成的 net $\tau$ 是一个 quasi-net，且对任意 $U,V\in\tau$，族 $\tau|_{U\cap V}=\{W\in\tau:W\subseteq U\cap V\}$ 都是 $U\cap V$ 的 quasi-net。一个 affinoid atlas 由这样的 net、affinoid 代数 $A_V$、同胚 $V\simeq\mathcal M(A_V)$，以及对 $\tau$ 中包含关系 $U\subseteq V$ 给出的有界同态 $A_V\to A_U$ 组成。后者将 $U$ 识别为 $V$ 的 affinoid 域，并与复合相容。}
\end{definition}
