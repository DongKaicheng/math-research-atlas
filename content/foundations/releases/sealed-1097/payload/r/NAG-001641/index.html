Inner rational neighborhoods

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 393

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Inner rational neighborhoods}{内点 rational 邻域}]
\label{fnd:strictrationalinteriorneighborhood}
\bi{Let $x\in X=\mathcal M(A)$ and let $O$ be an open neighborhood of $x$. There is a rational domain $U\subseteq O$ which is a neighborhood of $x$ and for which $x\in\operatorname{Int}(U/X)$. It can be chosen as a finite intersection of Weierstrass and Laurent domains whose defining inequalities are all strict at $x$.}
{设 $x\in X=\mathcal M(A)$，且 $O$ 为 $x$ 的开邻域。存在包含于 $O$ 的 rational 域 $U$，它是 $x$ 的邻域且满足 $x\in\operatorname{Int}(U/X)$。可以将 $U$ 取为有限个 Weierstrass 域与 Laurent 域的交，并使全部定义不等式在 $x$ 处严格成立。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The evaluation topology gives finitely many open conditions $|f_i|<a_i$ and $|g_j|>b_j$ whose common locus contains $x$ and is contained in $O$. Choose radii $|f_i(x)|<r_i<a_i$ and $b_j<s_j<|g_j(x)|$, omitting lower conditions with no effective restriction. The domain}
{由取值拓扑，可取有限个开条件 $|f_i|<a_i$ 与 $|g_j|>b_j$，使其公共轨迹包含 $x$ 并包含于 $O$。选择半径 $|f_i(x)|<r_i<a_i$ 与 $b_j<s_j<|g_j(x)|$，并略去没有实际限制的下界条件。所求域为}
\[
 U=\{|f_i|\le r_i,\ |g_j|\ge s_j\},\qquad
 A_U=A\{r^{-1}T,sS\}/(T_i-f_i,\ g_jS_j-1).
\]
\bi{The generalized rational-domain theorem identifies this actual quotient with the domain algebra. The joint quotient is norm-admissible, and its variables satisfy $|T_i(x)|<r_i$ and $|S_j(x)|<s_j^{-1}$. It therefore witnesses the claimed relative interior. The same strict inequalities define an open neighborhood of $x$ contained in $U$.}
{广义 rational 域定理将此实际商识别为域代数。联合商映射为 norm-admissible，且变量满足 $|T_i(x)|<r_i$ 与 $|S_j(x)|<s_j^{-1}$，所以此表示证明所需相对内点性质。相同的严格不等式定义一个包含于 $U$ 的 $x$ 的开邻域。}
\end{proof}
