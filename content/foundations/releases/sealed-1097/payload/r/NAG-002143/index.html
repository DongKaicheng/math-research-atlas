Monomial and bounded affine covariance

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 868

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Monomial and bounded affine covariance}
{单项式与有界仿射坐标变换相容性}]
\label{cand:standardhomotopymonomialcovariance}
\bi{On a torus times a unit polydisc, the mixed displacement
construction is unchanged by a torus coordinate change
$Y_i=c_iX^{m_i}$ whose exponent matrix lies in
$\mathrm{GL}_r(\mathbb Z)$, together with a free-coordinate
change $U=BT+b$ with $B,B^{-1}$ having entries in $K^\circ$
and $b\in(K^\circ)^q$. Whenever such a change identifies two
standard chart domains, it intertwines their entire homotopies.
In particular the construction is independent of which monomial
coordinate is eliminated from $X_0\cdots X_r=a$.}
{在环面与单位多圆盘的直积上，混合位移构造在如下坐标
变换下保持：环面坐标变换为 $Y_i=c_iX^{m_i}$，其指数
矩阵属于 $\mathrm{GL}_r(\mathbb Z)$；自由坐标变换为
$U=BT+b$，其中 $B,B^{-1}$ 的元素均属于 $K^\circ$，
且 $b\in(K^\circ)^q$。若这样的变换识别两个标准坐标片
域，则它与二者的整个同伦相容。特别地，构造与从
$X_0\cdots X_r=a$ 中消去哪一个单项式坐标无关。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For $0<t<1$, the normalized relative displacement of
$Y_i$ is}
{当 $0<t<1$ 时，$Y_i$ 的归一化相对位移为}
\[
 W_i=\prod_j(1+S_j)^{m_{ij}}-1.
\]
\bi{Integer binomial coefficients have norm at most one,
including those for negative exponents. The binomial series
therefore converge for radius $t$, and $|W_i|\leq t$.
The inverse integral matrix gives the inverse substitutions
with the same bounds. The two maps of radius-$t$ Tate
algebras are contractive inverses, hence isometric. At $t=1$
use instead the variables $R_i=1+S_i$. Their radius-one
Gauss norm is unchanged by translation. A unimodular monomial
change permutes the Laurent monomial basis, so preserves
the Gauss norm and its extension to fractions. At time zero
both constructions are evaluation at the original point.}
{整二项式系数的范数至多为一，负指数的二项式系数也
如此。因此二项式级数在半径 $t$ 时收敛，且
$|W_i|\leq t$。逆整矩阵给出具有相同界的逆代换。
两个半径 $t$ 的 Tate 代数之间的映射互逆且均压缩，
故等距。当 $t=1$ 时，改用变量 $R_i=1+S_i$。
其半径一的 Gauss 范数在平移下不变，而幺模单项式
变换排列 Laurent 单项式基，故保持 Gauss 范数及其
在分式上的延拓。时刻零时，两种构造都是原点取值。}

\bi{For the free variables, the relative displacement of
$BT+b$ is $BZ$. Both $B$ and its inverse are contractive
for the radius-$t$ coordinate maximum norm, so their induced
Gauss substitutions are isometric. The constant $b$ merely
changes the center. These isometries identify all polynomial
evaluations defining the homotopies; density identifies the
analytic evaluations. Finally, changing the eliminated
coordinate expresses the new independent monomial variables
in the old ones by an integral invertible exponent matrix
and powers of $a$, precisely the torus changes just treated.}
{对于自由变量，$BT+b$ 的相对位移为 $BZ$。
$B$ 及其逆对半径 $t$ 的坐标最大范数均压缩，因而诱导
的 Gauss 代换等距。常数 $b$ 仅改变中心。这些等距
映射识别定义同伦的全部多项式取值，再由稠密性识别
解析取值。最后，更换被消去的坐标，就是通过一个可逆
整指数矩阵及 $a$ 的幂，将新独立单项式变量表示为旧
变量，正好属于已经处理的环面变换。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Let $K$ be complete and nontrivially valued, with its original
absolute value. Fix $0<|a|<1$ and integers $r,q\geq0$. Put}
{设 $K$ 关于原绝对值完备且非平凡赋值。固定
$0<|a|<1$ 及整数 $r,q\geq0$，置}
\[
 A=K\langle X_0,\ldots,X_r,T_1,\ldots,T_q\rangle
                /(X_0\cdots X_r-a),\qquad V=\mathcal M(A).
\]
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:standardmonomialchartgeometry} gives
the continuous Gauss section $s:\Delta_a\to V$, where}
{Proposition~\ref{fnd:standardmonomialchartgeometry} 给出
连续 Gauss 截面 $s:\Delta_a\to V$，其中}
\[
 \Delta_a=\{(w_0,\ldots,w_r)\in\mathbb R_{\geq0}^{r+1}:
                         \sum_iw_i=v(a)\}.
\]
\bi{After eliminating $X_0=a/(X_1\cdots X_r)$, $s(w)$ is the
Gauss point of radii $e^{-w_i}$ in $X_i$, $1\leq i\leq r$,
and radius $1$ in every $T_j$. Let $\Sigma_a=s(\Delta_a)$ and
write $\operatorname{val}:V\to\Delta_a$ for the values of
$X_0,\ldots,X_r$.}
{消去 $X_0=a/(X_1\cdots X_r)$ 后，$s(w)$ 是在
$X_i$、$1\leq i\leq r$ 上半径为 $e^{-w_i}$、在每个
$T_j$ 上半径为 $1$ 的 Gauss 点。令
$\Sigma_a=s(\Delta_a)$，并以
$\operatorname{val}:V\to\Delta_a$ 记
$X_0,\ldots,X_r$ 的赋值映射。}
