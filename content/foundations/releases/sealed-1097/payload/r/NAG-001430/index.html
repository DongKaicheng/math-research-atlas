The unit Laurent coefficient algebra is strict

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 164

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The unit Laurent coefficient algebra is strict}{单位 Laurent 系数代数的 strictness}]\label{fnd:strictunitlaurentalgebra}
\bi{For $A$ as in Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictlaurentinjectivity}, the convolution algebra $B=E_1(A)$ is strict $K$-affinoid with its coefficient norm. More precisely, the normal-form map gives an isometric algebra isomorphism}
{对 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictlaurentinjectivity} 中的 $A$，卷积代数 $B=E_1(A)$ 配备系数范数后为 strict $K$-affinoid。更精确地，normal-form map 给出等距代数同构}
\[
 A\langle T,U\rangle/(TU-1)\ \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\ B,
 \qquad T\longmapsto Z,\quad U\longmapsto Z^{-1}.
\]
\bi{For every finite $A$-module $M$ with a canonical norm, $E_1(M)$ is a finite $B$-module and its coefficient norm is uniformly equivalent to its canonical finite-$B$-module norm. If $M$ has the quotient norm from a chosen $A^m\to M$, then $E_1(M)$ has exactly the quotient norm from the induced $B^m\to E_1(M)$.}
{对每个带有 canonical norm 的有限 $A$-模 $M$，$E_1(M)$ 为有限 $B$-模，其系数范数与 canonical finite-$B$-module norm uniformly equivalent。若 $M$ 的范数来自所选 $A^m\to M$ 的商范数，则 $E_1(M)$ 的系数范数恰为诱导映射 $B^m\to E_1(M)$ 的商范数。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:laurentcoefficientnormalform} and Proposition~\ref{fnd:laurentcoefficientfunctoriality}, with $a=b=1$, give the displayed algebra isomorphism, the actual kernel $(TU-1)A\langle T,U\rangle$, and equality of the quotient and coefficient norms. To verify strictness rather than assume it, choose an admissible bounded surjection}
{取 $a=b=1$，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:laurentcoefficientnormalform} 与 Proposition~\ref{fnd:laurentcoefficientfunctoriality} 得到所示代数同构、实际的核 $(TU-1)A\langle T,U\rangle$，以及商范数与系数范数相等。为验证而不是预设 strictness，选取 admissible bounded surjection}
\[
 \pi:R_d=K\langle X_1,\ldots,X_d\rangle\longrightarrow A .
\]
\bi{Its kernel is closed, and admissibility supplies a uniform bound for lifts of elements of $A$. Applying Proposition~\ref{fnd:weightedc0exact} to $0\to\ker\pi\to R_d\to A\to0$, with index set $\mathbb N^2$ and unit weights, extends $\pi$ to a bounded surjection with the same lifting bound}
{其核为 closed，且 admissibility 为 $A$ 中元素的提升提供统一范数界。对序列 $0\to\ker\pi\to R_d\to A\to0$ 应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:weightedc0exact}，取指标集 $\mathbb N^2$ 和单位权重，即将 $\pi$ 延伸为具有同一 lifting bound 的 bounded surjection}
\[
 R_d\langle T,U\rangle\longrightarrow A\langle T,U\rangle .
\]
\bi{Regrouping coefficients gives an isometric algebra identification}
{重排系数给出等距代数识别}
\[
 R_d\langle T,U\rangle=K\langle X_1,\ldots,X_d,T,U\rangle.
\]
\bi{To check the convergence condition, joint coefficients tending to zero outside finite subsets imply that each $(T,U)$-row has the same property and that the row norms tend to zero. Conversely, only finitely many rows can have norm at least a fixed $\varepsilon>0$, and in each of those rows only finitely many coefficients can have norm at least $\varepsilon$. The norms are equal because the two suprema commute. Multiplication agrees first for polynomials and then by continuity.}
{收敛条件可直接核对：联合系数在有限集之外趋于零，蕴含每个 $(T,U)$-行的系数趋于零且行范数趋于零。反之，对固定 $\varepsilon>0$，只有有限个行的范数至少为 $\varepsilon$，而在这些行中又各自只有有限个系数的范数至少为 $\varepsilon$。两次 supremum 可交换，故范数相等。乘法先在多项式上相容，再由连续性推广。}

\bi{The normal-form quotient onto $B$ has an isometric $A$-linear section. Composing it with the preceding surjection gives a bounded surjection from this unit-radius Tate algebra to $B$, with uniform lifts. Thus the coefficient norm on $B$ is uniformly equivalent to its defining quotient norm, as required by the definition of strict affinoid. Finally Theorem~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficienttensor}, for $\mathbb Z$ and unit weights, identifies $E_1(M)$ with the finite $B$-module $B\otimes_A M$. The coefficient presentation in Theorem~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientpresentation} gives the asserted equality with the quotient norm from $B^m$. Replacing the chosen canonical norm on $M$ by an equivalent one preserves uniform equivalence of coefficient norms.}
{到 $B$ 的 normal-form quotient 有一个等距的 $A$-线性 section。将其与前述满射复合，得到从这个单位半径 Tate algebra 到 $B$ 的有界满射，且提升有统一范数界。因此 $B$ 的系数范数与定义它的商范数 uniformly equivalent，满足 strict affinoid 的定义。最后，对指标集 $\mathbb Z$ 和单位权重应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficienttensor}，将 $E_1(M)$ 识别为有限 $B$-模 $B\otimes_A M$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientpresentation} 中的系数表示给出所述系数范数与 $B^m$ 商范数的相等。若将 $M$ 的所选 canonical norm 换为等价范数，则对应系数范数仍 uniformly equivalent。}
\end{proof}

\section{\bi{The ground field and convergence}{Ground field 与 convergence}}\label{fnd:valuedfields}
\begin{definition}\label{fnd:fieldconventions}
\bi{Throughout the foundations, $K$ is a field complete for a real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value. We permit the trivial absolute value unless a statement explicitly excludes it. Set}
{基础篇中，$K$ 是关于 real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value 完备的 field。除非明确排除，否则允许 trivial absolute value。记}
\[
\begin{gathered}
v(a)=-\log|a|,\qquad \Gamma=v(K^\times),\\
K^\circ=\{a\in K:|a|\leq1\},\qquad K^{\circ\circ}=\{a\in K:|a|<1\},\\
\widetilde K=K^\circ/K^{\circ\circ}.
\end{gathered}
\]
\bi{A complete extension $L/K$ always carries an absolute value restricting to the specified one on $K$. We do not rescale it when changing the ground field.}
{Complete extension $L/K$ 始终配备限制为指定 $K$ 上 absolute value 的 absolute value。改变 ground field 时不重新缩放它。}

\begin{definition}\label{fnd:banachalgebra}
\bi{A Banach $K$-algebra is a commutative unital $K$-algebra $A$ with a complete non-Archimedean norm satisfying $\|ca\|=|c|\|a\|$, $\|1\|=1$, and $\|ab\|\leq\|a\|\|b\|$. A homomorphism is bounded if $\|\phi(a)\|\leq C\|a\|$ for some constant $C$. Equivalent norms define the same Banach topology. A surjective bounded map is admissible if its target norm is uniformly equivalent to the quotient norm; equivalently, it admits lifts with a uniform norm bound. Equality of topologies alone is insufficient at trivial valuation; see Definition~\ref{fnd:normstrictconvention} and Example~\ref{fnd:normtopologycounterexample}.}
{Banach $K$-algebra 是 commutative unital $K$-algebra $A$，配备一个 complete norm。此 norm 为 non-Archimedean，且满足 $\|ca\|=|c|\|a\|$、$\|1\|=1$ 和 $\|ab\|\leq\|a\|\|b\|$。若存在常数 $C$ 使 $\|\phi(a)\|\leq C\|a\|$，则 homomorphism 称为 bounded。Equivalent norms 定义相同的 Banach topology。Surjective bounded map 若使 target norm 与 quotient norm uniformly equivalent，则称为 admissible；等价地，它具有 uniform norm bound 的 lifts。赋值平凡时，仅有 topologies 相同还不够；见 Definition~\ref{fnd:normstrictconvention} 及 Example~\ref{fnd:normtopologycounterexample}。}
\end{definition}
Standing context 3 · exact TeX
\begin{definition}\label{fnd:normstrictconvention}
\bi{For a bounded linear map $u:E\to F$, give $\operatorname{im}u$ both its induced norm from $F$ and its quotient seminorm from $E$. The map is norm-strict, or admissible, when these are uniformly equivalent. Since $u$ is bounded, the additional requirement is a constant $C>0$ such that every $y\in\operatorname{im}u$ has a lift $x$ with $\|x\|\leq C\|y\|$. The constant may be enlarged by any fixed factor greater than one to approximate an unattained infimum.}
{对 bounded linear map $u:E\to F$，在 $\operatorname{im}u$ 上同时考虑来自 $F$ 的 induced norm 与来自 $E$ 的 quotient seminorm。当两者 uniformly equivalent 时，称该 map 为 norm-strict，或 admissible。由于 $u$ 已 bounded，额外条件等价于存在常数 $C>0$，使每个 $y\in\operatorname{im}u$ 都有 lift $x$ 满足 $\|x\|\leq C\|y\|$。当 infimum 不取到时，可将常数乘以任意固定的大于一的因子。}
\end{definition}

{保持固定的完备非阿基米德域 $K$，允许赋值平凡。对 Banach $K$-space $V$ 和 $r>0$，记}
\[
 E_r(V)=
 \left\{(v_n)_{n\in\mathbb Z}:
       \|v_n\|r^n\longrightarrow0\ \text{as }|n|\longrightarrow\infty\right\},
 \qquad
 \|v\|_r=\sup_{n\in\mathbb Z}\|v_n\|r^n .
\]
\bi{We also write its elements as Laurent series $\sum_n v_nT^n$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness} gives completeness. For a bounded operator $f$ on $V$, the operator $L_f=1-fT^{-1}$ is defined by}
{也将其元素写为 Laurent series $\sum_n v_nT^n$。Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness} 给出完备性。对 $V$ 上的有界算子 $f$，定义算子 $L_f=1-fT^{-1}$ 为}
\[
 (L_fv)_n=v_n-fv_{n+1}.
\]

\bi{Throughout, $K$ is complete for its fixed real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value, which may be trivial. A Banach $K$-space has a definite, complete, non-Archimedean, $K$-homogeneous norm. Banach algebras are commutative and unital, with a submultiplicative norm and $\|1\|=1$; Banach module actions satisfy $\|am\|\leq\|a\|\|m\|$. All finite powers have the maximum norm; the maximum over an empty family is zero. Norm-strictness has the quantitative meaning of Definition~\ref{fnd:normstrictconvention}.}
{本节中，$K$ 关于固定的 real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value 完备，允许该 absolute value 平凡。Banach $K$-space 的 norm 为 definite、complete、non-Archimedean 且 $K$-homogeneous。Banach algebras 均 commutative、unital，其 norm submultiplicative 且 $\|1\|=1$；Banach module actions 满足 $\|am\|\leq\|a\|\|m\|$。所有 finite powers 使用 maximum norm，并约定空 family 的 maximum 为零。Norm-strictness 采用 Definition~\ref{fnd:normstrictconvention} 的定量含义。}

\bi{For a countable set $I$ and positive weights $w=(w_i)_{i\in I}$, write $\mathcal C_w(I,E)=c_0(I,E;w)$ as in Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness}; thus}
{对可数集合 $I$ 和正权重 $w=(w_i)_{i\in I}$，按照 Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness} 的定义，记 $\mathcal C_w(I,E)=c_0(I,E;w)$，即}
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \mathcal C_w(I,E)=
 \left\{(e_i)_{i\in I}:
   \text{for every }\varepsilon>0,
   \#\{i:w_i\|e_i\|\geq\varepsilon\}<\infty\right\},\\
 \|(e_i)\|_w=\sup_{i\in I}w_i\|e_i\|.
 \end{gathered}
\]
\bi{We put $\sup\varnothing=0$. The cited lemma, with coefficient algebra $K$ if necessary, proves completeness and density of finite-support families. A bounded linear map $u:E\to F$ with bound $U$ induces a coefficient map with the same bound, since $w_i\|u(e_i)\|\leq U w_i\|e_i\|$. There is also a canonical isometry}
{约定 $\sup\varnothing=0$。上述引理给出完备性以及有限支撑族的稠密性；对 Banach $K$-space，只需取系数代数为 $K$。若有界线性映射 $u:E\to F$ 的界为 $U$，则逐系数映射保持同一个界，因为 $w_i\|u(e_i)\|\leq U w_i\|e_i\|$。此外，对有限 $m$，有 canonical isometry}
\[
 \mathcal C_w(I,E^m)\simeq\mathcal C_w(I,E)^m
 \qquad(m<\infty).
\]
\bi{It merely rearranges coefficients: a supremum commutes with a finite maximum, and a finite union of finite sets is finite, so both the norms and the finite-tail conditions agree.}
{该映射只重新排列系数：supremum 与有限 maximum 可交换，且有限个有限集的并仍有限，所以两种范数相等，有限尾条件也等价。}
Standing context 6 · exact TeX
\bi{The isometry uses the specified quotient norm on $M$. With another uniformly equivalent norm $\|\cdot\|_*$, choose $0<a\leq b$ such that $a\|m\|\leq\|m\|_*\leq b\|m\|$. The corresponding coefficient and projective norms each satisfy the same comparisons with the original ones. Consequently}
{上述 isometry 使用 $M$ 上指定的 quotient norm。若改用另一 uniformly equivalent norm $\|\cdot\|_*$，取 $0<a\leq b$ 使 $a\|m\|\leq\|m\|_*\leq b\|m\|$。相应的 coefficient norms 和 projective norms 相对于原 norms 都满足同样的 comparisons。因此}
\[
 \frac{a}{b}\|\Phi(z)\|_{w,*}
       \leq\|z\|_{\pi,*}
       \leq\frac{b}{a}\|\Phi(z)\|_{w,*}.
\]
\bi{Thus the bounded isomorphism and completeness are independent of the chosen canonical norm. Uniform equivalence, rather than equality of topologies, is the relevant hypothesis.}

\begin{remark}\label{fnd:strictclusterboundary}
\bi{For arbitrary polyradii, the coefficient description of a free-radius extension replaces the strict coordinate changes used here. Theorems~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules} and~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules} descend closedness and the canonical finite-module topology through that extension, giving the general affinoid statements.}
{对任意多半径，自由半径扩张的系数描述替代这里使用的 strict 坐标变换。Theorems~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules} 与~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules} 沿该扩张下降闭性及有限模的典范拓扑，得到一般 affinoid 陈述。}
\end{remark}
