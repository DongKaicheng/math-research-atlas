Lifting a residual basis with a uniform gap

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 516

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Lifting a residual basis with a uniform gap}
{利用一致间隙提升剩余基}]
\label{fnd:gaporthonormallifting}
\bi{Let $V=c_0(I,K)$ with its maximum norm, where $I$ is finite or
countable, and let $e_i$ be its coordinate basis. Let $S\subset K^\circ$
have the properties of Lemma~\ref{fnd:baldcoefficientring}. Suppose
$y_j\in c_0(I,S)$ and that their residues form a basis of
$\widetilde V=\widetilde K^{(I)}$. Then the $y_j$ are an orthonormal
basis of $V$.}
{令 $V=c_0(I,K)$ 配备最大范数，其中 $I$ 有限或可数，以 $e_i$ 记
坐标基。设 $S\subset K^\circ$ 满足 Lemma~\ref{fnd:baldcoefficientring}
的性质。若 $y_j\in c_0(I,S)$ 的剩余类构成
$\widetilde V=\widetilde K^{(I)}$ 的一组基，则 $y_j$ 是 $V$ 的
正交归一基。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For a finite linear combination of the $y_j$, divide the
coefficients by one of maximal absolute value. Residual linear
independence then proves that its norm is the maximum coefficient
norm. Passing to convergent series gives an isometric map
$c_0(J,K)\to V$ with closed image. The same argument identifies the
closed $S$-span $W_S$ of the $y_j$ isometrically with $c_0(J,S)$.}
{对 $y_j$ 的有限线性组合，将所有系数除以其中绝对值最大的一个。
剩余类的线性无关性说明组合的范数正是最大系数范数。传递到收敛级数，
得到具有闭像的等距映射 $c_0(J,K)\to V$。同一论证将 $y_j$ 的闭
$S$-线性包 $W_S$ 等距识别为 $c_0(J,S)$。}

\bi{The residue field $k_S$ of $S$ embeds in $\widetilde K$. The
residual vectors of the $y_j$ have finite support and coordinates in
$k_S$. They form a basis already over $k_S$: linear independence
descends immediately, and spanning descends by row reduction of the
finite matrix involved in any expression for a coordinate vector.
Thus, for every $i$, there is a finite $S$-linear combination $z_i$ of
the $y_j$ whose residue equals that of $e_i$. The gap gives
$\|e_i-z_i\|\leq\theta$ and $\|z_i\|=1$.}
{环 $S$ 的剩余域 $k_S$ 嵌入 $\widetilde K$。各 $y_j$ 的剩余向量
具有有限支撑，且坐标属于 $k_S$。它们已在 $k_S$ 上构成一组基：
线性无关性立即下降；生成性则对每个坐标向量所涉及的有限矩阵作行化简
即可下降。因此，对每个 $i$，存在 $y_j$ 的有限 $S$-线性组合 $z_i$，
其剩余类等于 $e_i$ 的剩余类。间隙给出
$\|e_i-z_i\|\leq\theta$，并且 $\|z_i\|=1$。}

\bi{For $x=\sum_i a_i e_i\in c_0(I,S)$, put
$P(x)=\sum_i a_i z_i\in W_S$. The series converges and satisfies
$\|x-P(x)\|\leq\theta\|x\|$. Repeating this construction on the
error gives a convergent sum in $W_S$ equal to $x$. Hence
$c_0(I,S)=W_S$. In particular every $e_i$ belongs to the closed
$K$-span of the $y_j$. Since these coordinate vectors span a dense
subspace of $V$, that closed span is all of $V$.}
{对 $x=\sum_i a_i e_i\in c_0(I,S)$，令
$P(x)=\sum_i a_i z_i\in W_S$。该级数收敛，且
$\|x-P(x)\|\leq\theta\|x\|$。对误差反复作同一构造，得到
$W_S$ 中一个和为 $x$ 的收敛级数。因此 $c_0(I,S)=W_S$。
特别地，每个 $e_i$ 都属于 $y_j$ 的闭 $K$-线性包，而坐标向量张成
$V$ 的稠密子空间，故该闭线性包就是整个 $V$。}
\end{proof}
