The canonical residual curve at a component vertex

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 708

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The canonical residual curve at a component vertex}
{分量顶点处的典范剩余曲线}]
\label{cand:intrinsicresidualcurve}
\bi{Let $K$ be algebraically closed, complete and
nontrivially valued. Suppose that $x$ is a component vertex
of a proper nodal curve model of an analytic curve $X$.
There is a normal proper $k$-curve $C_x$, canonically
characterized by}
{设 $K$ 代数闭、完备且非平凡赋值。假设 $x$ 是解析
曲线 $X$ 的某个适当节点曲线模型的分量顶点。存在正规
适当 $k$-曲线 $C_x$，由下式典范刻画：}
\[
                         k(C_x)=\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}.
\]
\bi{For every such model, $C_x$ is canonically the
normalization of its component corresponding to $x$.
The bijection between $T_xX$ and the closed points of $C_x$,
the outgoing Gauss-ray germ in each direction, and its
parameter $\ell=-\log r$ are independent of the model.}
{对每个这样的模型，$C_x$ 典范地等于与 $x$ 对应分量
的正规化。$T_xX$ 与 $C_x$ 闭点之间的双射、每个
方向的出射 Gauss 射线芽，以及参数 $\ell=-\log r$，
都与模型无关。}

\bi{If $f$ is an analytic germ at $x$ with $f(x)\ne0$,
choose $c\in K^\times$ with $|cf(x)|=1$ and put
$\varphi=\widetilde{cf}_x\in k(C_x)^\times$. Then for the
direction corresponding to $p\in C_x$ one has, for all
sufficiently small $\ell\geq0$,}
{若 $f$ 是 $x$ 处满足 $f(x)\ne0$ 的解析芽，选择
$c\in K^\times$ 使 $|cf(x)|=1$，并令
$\varphi=\widetilde{cf}_x\in k(C_x)^\times$。则对
$p\in C_x$ 对应的方向，在全部充分小的
$\ell\geq0$ 上有}
\[
 -\log|f(\gamma_p(\ell))|
 =-\log|f(x)|+\operatorname{ord}_p(\varphi)\ell .
\]
\bi{Only finitely many of these slopes are nonzero, and
their sum is zero.}
{这些斜率只有有限个非零，且其和为零。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Let $C$ be the special component corresponding to $x$.
Lemma~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield} identifies its
function field with $\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}$ and gives
$|\mathcal H(x)^\times|=|K^\times|$. The normalization
$C^\nu$ is finite over $C$ by \cite[Tag 0BXR]{StacksAlg},
hence is proper by \cite[Tags 01WM, 01W3]{StacksAlg}.
Here $C$ is a closed component of the proper special fibre;
properness is preserved by base change and closed immersion
\cite[Tags 01W4, 01W5]{StacksAlg}.
The equivalence between normal proper
curves and their function fields
\cite[Tag 0BY1]{StacksAlg} identifies any two such
normalizations by the unique isomorphism inducing the
identity of $\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}$. This defines $C_x$.}
{设 $C$ 为与 $x$ 对应的特殊分量。
Lemma~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield} 将其函数域
识别为 $\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}$，并给出
$|\mathcal H(x)^\times|=|K^\times|$。
由 \cite[Tag 0BXR]{StacksAlg}，正规化 $C^\nu$ 在
$C$ 上有限，因而由 \cite[Tags 01WM, 01W3]{StacksAlg}
适当。此处 $C$ 是适当特殊纤维的闭分量；基变换与闭浸入
保持适当性，见 \cite[Tags 01W4, 01W5]{StacksAlg}。
正规适当曲线与其函数域之间
的等价 \cite[Tag 0BY1]{StacksAlg}，通过在
$\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}$ 上诱导恒等的唯一同构识别
任意两个这样的正规化。这就定义了 $C_x$。}

\bi{The direction set itself is intrinsic by the cofinal
description preceding the lemma. Every closed residual
point is rational, since $k$ is algebraically closed.
Lemma~\ref{cand:commonannularcollars} therefore identifies
the ray germs and their parameters for the same intrinsic
direction in any two models. It remains to check that the
associated residual points correspond under the just
constructed isomorphism of normal proper curves.}
{由上一引理之前的共尾描述，方向集本身是内禀的。
因为 $k$ 代数闭，每个闭剩余点均有理。因此，
Lemma~\ref{cand:commonannularcollars} 将任意两个模型中
同一内禀方向的射线芽及其参数识别起来。还需验证关联
的剩余点在刚刚构造的正规适当曲线同构下相互对应。}

\bi{Every $\psi\in\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}^\times$ is the
residue of a norm-one analytic unit germ at $x$. Indeed,
the proof of Lemma~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield}
represents it by a fraction $P/Q$ of affinoid functions with
$P(x)Q(x)\ne0$; on a small neighborhood $Q$, and also $P$,
are invertible by Proposition~\ref{fnd:localunit}.
Apply Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope} to this germ
in the two models. The rays and length parameters coincide,
so the two residual-point valuations take the same value
on every $\psi$. Thus their discrete valuation rings in the
common function field coincide. They have the same centre
on the identified proper curve: the two maps from the
spectrum of this valuation ring agree at its generic point,
and the closed diagonal of the separated curve makes
them equal. Hence the residual points coincide.}
{每个 $\psi\in\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}^\times$ 都是
$x$ 处范数为一的解析单位芽的剩余。事实上，
Lemma~\ref{cand:componentvaluedresiduefield} 的证明将它
表示为 affinoid 函数的分式 $P/Q$，其中
$P(x)Q(x)\ne0$；由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:localunit}，
在一个小邻域上 $Q$ 和 $P$ 均可逆。对这个芽，在两个
模型中分别应用 Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope}。
射线与长度参数相同，故两个剩余点赋值在每个 $\psi$
上取相同值。因此，它们在公共函数域中的离散赋值环
相同。它们在已识别的适当曲线上具有相同中心：从这个
赋值环的谱出发的两个映射在泛点相同，而分离曲线的
对角线闭，故这两个映射相同。因此剩余点相同。}

\bi{The asserted formula is now
Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope} in any one model.
Finite support and the sum-zero assertion are
Theorem~\ref{cand:weightedcomponentbalance}; here all
closed-point degrees are one. Multiplying the normalizing
constant $c$ by a norm-one scalar multiplies $\varphi$
by a nonzero constant and does not change any order.}
{所述公式就是任一模型中的
Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope}。有限支撑及
零和断言来自 Theorem~\ref{cand:weightedcomponentbalance}；
此处全部闭点次数均为一。将归一化常数 $c$ 乘以范数为一
的标量，只会将 $\varphi$ 乘以非零常数，不改变任何阶数。}
\end{proof}
