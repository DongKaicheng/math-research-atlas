Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Norm replacement gives the ordinary algebraic sheaf]\label{thm:ordinary}
There is a canonical isomorphism of ordinary sheaves of bigraded differential real algebras
\begin{equation}\label{eq:iso}
 \A_{\rm CLD}\xleftarrow[\sim]{\ c\ }\A_{\rm mom}
                  \xrightarrow[\sim]{\ \Phi\ }\A_{\rm alg}.
\end{equation}
On a moment representative, $\Phi$ is obtained by the single simultaneous replacement \eqref{eq:norm} and pullback along the integral affine character map of Proposition~\ref{prop:intrinsic}. It is independent of the replacement, the compact witness, the chosen algebraic chart, and the lifts of a unit-lattice basis. The maps commute with ordinary restriction and with algebraic pullbacks. They preserve ordinary supports exactly.
\end{theorem}
