Spectral injectivity of strict epimorphisms

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 363

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Spectral injectivity of strict epimorphisms}{Strict 上态的谱单射性}]
\label{fnd:strictepispectralinjectivity}
\bi{Let $L$ be complete and nontrivially valued. A bounded categorical epimorphism $D\to E$ of strict $L$-affinoid algebras induces an injective map $\mathcal M(E)\to\mathcal M(D)$.}
{设 $L$ 完备且赋值非平凡。Strict $L$-affinoid 代数之间的有界范畴上态 $D\to E$ 诱导单射 $\mathcal M(E)\to\mathcal M(D)$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictgerritzengrauertlocaldense} supplies a finite rational covering $(W_j)$ of $\mathcal M(D)$ such that $D_j\to E_j=E\widehat\otimes_DD_j$ has dense image. Suppose $z_1,z_2\in\mathcal M(E)$ have the same image $x$, and choose $j$ with $x\in W_j$. By Proposition~\ref{fnd:relativeaffinoidpushouts}, $E_j$ is the actual strict rational quotient over $E$ for the pulled tuple. The strict rational point comparison, Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology}, lifts each $z_i$ uniquely to a point of $E_j$. Their restrictions to $D_j$ are both the unique rational lift of $x$. Thus these two seminorms agree on the dense image of $D_j$. Bounded multiplicative seminorms are continuous, by Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}, so they agree on $E_j$, and hence $z_1=z_2$. The claim is immediate if the source spectrum is empty.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictgerritzengrauertlocaldense}，存在 $\mathcal M(D)$ 的有限 rational 覆盖 $(W_j)$，使 $D_j\to E_j=E\widehat\otimes_DD_j$ 的像稠密。设 $z_1,z_2\in\mathcal M(E)$ 具有相同的像 $x$，选择满足 $x\in W_j$ 的 $j$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:relativeaffinoidpushouts}，$E_j$ 是拉回 tuple 在 $E$ 上的实际 strict rational 商。Strict rational 谱点比较 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology} 将每个 $z_i$ 唯一提升到 $E_j$ 的一个谱点。这两个点在 $D_j$ 上的限制都是 $x$ 的唯一 rational 提升，故相应两个半范数在 $D_j$ 的稠密像上相等。由 Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}，有界乘性半范数连续，所以它们在 $E_j$ 上相等，进而 $z_1=z_2$。源谱为空时断言直接成立。}
\end{proof}
