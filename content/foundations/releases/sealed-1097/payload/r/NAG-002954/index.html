Open in the English reader · PDF page 2024

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[Actual chart hypotheses; F8SA-C]\label{lem:charts}
Under S1--S3, choose finitely many affinoid domains \(V_i\subset U\)
whose ordinary interiors cover \(C\), and unit moment representatives
\(\eta|_{V_i}=t_{g_i}^*\alpha_i\). For every nonempty intersection
\(V_I=\bigcap_{i\in I}V_i\), the hypotheses of IP hold whenever
\(D_I=D\cap V_I\) is nonempty. If \(D_I=\varnothing\), a sufficiently
small \(f\)-tube in \(V_I\) is empty.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Goodness and the ordinary local definition of a smooth moment form [\hyperref[v037:forms:germsheaf]{NAG-002424}]
supply such neighbourhoods; compactness gives a finite family.
\tagg{NAG-002411}/\tagg{002413} make each nonempty \(V_I\) an actual
pure \(n\)-dimensional affinoid domain. Restricting the injective sheaf
map in S2 and taking sections proves that \(f\) is a non-zero-divisor
in its algebra. Quotient/domain base change \hyperref[fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange]{NAG-001846} identifies \(D_I\) with
the actual algebra quotient by \(f\), not its reduction. It is an
affinoid domain of \(D\), hence is pure of dimension \(n-1\) by S3
and domain purity. R-S1 [\hyperref[thm:analyticserre]{NAG-002765}] applied to every affinoid domain of \(V_I\)
supplies IP's full affinoid-domain \(S_1\) condition. A chosen
\(g_i\), \(i\in I\), remains a tuple of units upon restriction.
If \(D_I\) is empty, continuity on compact \(V_I\) gives
\(\min_{V_I}|f|>0\).
\end{proof}
