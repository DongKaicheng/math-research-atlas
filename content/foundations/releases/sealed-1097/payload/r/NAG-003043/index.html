Exact generic criterion for acquiring presentations

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1919

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Exact generic criterion for acquiring presentations]
\label{f9cn:presentation}
Let $\cM$ be a normal model of $X$, and let
$(\cL_i,\sigma_i;Z_i)$ be finitely many Cartier data on it.
There exists a normal model $\cM'$ and a proper generic-identity
map $q:\cM'\to\cM$ such that every pulled-back datum admits a
signed presentation on $q^{-1}Z_i$ if and only if every generic
Cartier datum admits a signed presentation on $Z_i\cap X$.
When these equivalent conditions hold, $q$ can be constructed by
finitely many blowups of nonzero coherent ideals, followed by finite
normalization. It is an isomorphism off $\bigcup_iZ_i$. Each
individual presentation retains its own prescribed inverse-image
support.

In particular the condition holds if signed presentations are already
supplied on another model and pulled back to $\cM$. It also holds
for every collection of rational Cartier data when $X$ is locally
factorial. No factoriality of $\cM$ or $\cM'$ follows or is required.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Necessity follows by restriction to $X$: the map is the identity there,
and each effective Cartier factor remains effective Cartier because
its nonzero section stays nonzero on the integral generic fibre.

For sufficiency first consider one datum, with divisor $D$ and a
generic presentation $D|_X=A_K-B_K$ on $Z\cap X$. Let
$\mathcal I_+$ and $\mathcal I_-$ be the ideals of the
scheme-theoretic closures of $A_K$ and $B_K$ in $\cM$.
They are coherent nonzero ideals, are invertible on $X$, and are
the unit ideal off $Z$. For example, on $\Spec A\subset\cM$
the closure ideal is the contraction of its ideal in $A[1/\pi]$;
Noetherianity gives finite generation and localization gives gluing.

Blow up $\mathcal I_+$ and then the extended ideal
$\mathcal I_-$. These blowups are projective and proper by
\st{02NS} and \st{01WC}. Their affine charts for an ideal
$I=(a_1,\ldots,a_s)$ are $A[I/a_j]\subset\Frac(A)$, for
nonzero $a_j$. On such a chart the extended ideal is $(a_j)$,
an invertible ideal generated by a non-zero-divisor. Thus the
blowups are integral, make the indicated ideals invertible, and are
isomorphisms wherever those ideals were invertible. In particular
they leave $X$ and $\cM\setminus Z$ unchanged. Write
$u:Y\to\cM$ for the composite, and let $A_0,B_0$ be the effective
Cartier divisors of the two extended ideals. Their supports lie in
$u^{-1}Z$, and their generic restrictions are $A_K,B_K$.

The Cartier divisor
\[
                         V=u^*D-(A_0-B_0)
\]
is zero on the generic fibre and off $u^{-1}Z$.
As subsheaves of the constant sheaf of rational functions on the
integral $Y$, form the coherent nonzero ideal
\[
                         \mathcal J=\cO_Y(-V)\cap\cO_Y.
\]
Coherence follows locally by clearing one denominator and intersecting
two finitely generated modules over a Noetherian ring. It is the unit
ideal on $X$ and off $u^{-1}Z$.
Blow up $\mathcal J$, with map $v:Y'\to Y$, and write
$\mathcal J\cO_{Y'}=\cO_{Y'}(-E)$.
The inclusion $\mathcal J\subset\cO_Y(-V)$ gives a nonzero map
between invertible sheaves
\[
                 \cO_{Y'}(-E)\longrightarrow\cO_{Y'}(-v^*V).
\]
Its section has an effective Cartier divisor $F$ satisfying
$E-v^*V=F$. This can also be checked in a local frame: if
$\cO_Y(-V)=f\cO_Y$ and $\mathcal J\cO_{Y'}=(h)$,
then $h/f$ is regular and $F=\divi(h/f)$.
Both $E$ and $F$ vanish off $(uv)^{-1}Z$. Thus
\[
             (uv)^*D=(v^*A_0+E)-(v^*B_0+F)
\]
is the required signed presentation. This third blowup leaves the
generic fibre unchanged because $\mathcal J|_X=\cO_X$.

For finitely many data repeat these steps, pulling earlier effective
Cartier factors along subsequent dominant maps. Their sections remain
nonzero, and their supports remain inside the respective inverse
images of $Z_i$. All stages are integral proper finite-type schemes
over $R$, with generic fibre $X$, and are flat by \st{0539}.
Normalize the final stage. Its normalization is finite by
Theorem~\ref{f9cn:common}'s Nagata argument, is the identity over
normal $X$, and preserves all effective Cartier factors under its
dominant pullback. Outside $\bigcup_iZ_i$ the previous stages
were the original normal $\cM$, so normalization changes nothing
there either. This gives $q$ and all the asserted support properties.

An existing presentation pulls back to any common integral model,
with its actual line and section identities. No factoriality is
needed for this step. Finally on a locally factorial integral
Noetherian $X$, decompose the finite Weil cycle of a Cartier divisor
into its positive and negative parts. Local unique factorization
makes both parts effective Cartier: their local equations differ by
units on overlaps. Their difference is the original Cartier divisor,
and their supports lie in its support. This proves the last sufficient
condition, independently of any pending intersection claim.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
A \emph{Cartier datum on a prescribed closed support $Z$} consists of
an invertible sheaf $\cL$ on an integral scheme and a nonzero rational
section $\sigma$ which is a regular generating section off $Z$.
Equivalently its Cartier divisor $D$ is zero off $Z$. A \emph{signed
presentation on $Z$} is a pair of effective Cartier divisors $A,B$
with $D=A-B$ as Cartier divisors and $|A|\cup|B|\subset Z$.
Equivalently it consists of two line bundles with nonzero global
sections, an actual identification of their ratio with
$(\cL,\sigma)$, and the same support condition. This is stronger
than equality of ordinary Chow classes.

Call a proper birational map of models \emph{generic-identity} when
it is the identity on the specified $X$. The next result constructs
presentations; it does not assume the pending supported-calculus
operations or prove descent of those operations on Chow groups.
