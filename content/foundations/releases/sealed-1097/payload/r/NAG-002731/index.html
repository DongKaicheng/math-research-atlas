The full torus chart has weight one

I Descent of the polarized mixed tower

Open in the English reader · PDF page 2126

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[The full torus chart has weight one]\label{f9tm:torusweight}
For an interior weight $w$ of the box $B$ in \eqref{ex68:radial:eq:collar}, the frozen
structure-module weight of $Q$ with moment coordinates $T_1,\ldots,T_d$
is one. Its saturated tangent lattice is $\mathbf Z^d$. The primary
algebraic tropicalization of $T$ has the same lattice and coefficient
one. Consequently both chart integrals of $a\omega_d$, for every
$a\in C_c^\infty(\Int B)$, equal $\int_B a(t)\,dt$.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Write $r_i=e^{-w_i}$. The entire weight fibre has the actual algebra
\[
 T_w=K\{r^{-1}Z,rZ^{-1}\}
    =\left\{\sum_{\nu\in\mathbf Z^d}a_\nu Z^\nu:
                  |a_\nu|r^\nu\longrightarrow0\right\},
 \qquad\|a\|_w=\max_\nu |a_\nu|r^\nu.
\]
This is the relation quotient, with its exact quotient norm, of
NAG-001553; the fibre identification is NAG-002081. The Laurent bounds
of $Q$ impose no further relation at this weight. All points of the
fibre satisfy the strict upper bounds for both $Z_i$ and $Z_i^{-1}$ in
the presentation of $Q$, hence lie in $\Int(Q/K)$. The image germ is
open in $\mathbf R^d$, whose saturated lattice is $\mathbf Z^d$.
Use the identity lattice projection in NAG-002362. The source fibre
algebra and target polytorus algebra are both $T_w$. If $H_w$ is its
Gauss field, multiplicativity of the Gauss norm and the actual tensor
identification give
\[
 T_w\widehat\otimes_{T_w}H_w=H_w,
 \qquad [\mathbf Z^d:\mathbf Z^d]=1.
\]
Thus the full Gauss-module dimension divided by the lattice index is
one. No reduction, radical quotient, separated quotient losing a
module, or count of analytic points has entered this calculation.

For the primary torus chart the defining ideal in its own Laurent
coordinate ring is zero. Its initial ideal is again zero at every
weight. Over a valued extension realizing that weight, its initial
scheme is a Laurent torus, with one reduced generic component and
length one. Its tangent lattice is exactly $\mathbf Z^d$.
This verifies its tropical coefficient directly, without a general
multiplicity or pushforward theorem. Finally NAG-002405 computes the
frozen integral with these weights, NAG-002389 gives the covolume-one
real integral, and the defining primary chart integral uses the same
coefficient and the same positive generator. This proves the claim.
\end{proof}
