Pushforward on a relative interior weight domain

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1001

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Pushforward on a relative interior weight domain]
\label{v037:degree:relativeinteriorcycle}
Let $p:X=\mathcal M(B)\to Y=\mathcal M(A)$ be an affinoid
morphism, with $X$ and $Y$ pure of analytic dimension $d$.
Let a unit tuple $f_Y$ define $t_Y:Y\to\mathbb R^{n'}$.
Write $\partial(X/Y)=X\setminus\operatorname{Int}(X/Y)$ and put
\[
 U_p=\mathbb R^{n'}\setminus
       \bigl(t_Y(\partial_KY)\cup
                    (t_Y\circ p)(\partial(X/Y))\bigr).
\]
This is open. There is a canonical local nonnegative integral
balanced $d$-cycle $\mathcal C_{p,f_Y}$ on $U_p$, with support
$(t_Y\circ p)(X)\cap U_p$. It is the local analytic cycle
\[
              \mathcal C_{p,f_Y}
                   =\operatorname{Trop}_{p^*f_Y}(X)|_{U_p}.
\]

Every $w\in U_p\cap t_Y(Y)$ has a closed target moment box
$P$ about $w$, with $w$ in its interior, such that
\[
 Y_0=t_Y^{-1}(P),\qquad X_0=p^{-1}(Y_0),\qquad
                         p_0:X_0\longrightarrow Y_0
\]
is an actual finite canonical affinoid map.
Writing $A_0=\mathcal O(Y_0)$ and $B_0=\mathcal O(X_0)$,
there is an open neighbourhood $W\subset\operatorname{int}(P)$
of $w$ on which
\[
            \mathcal C_{p,f_Y}|_W
                    =\operatorname{Trop}_{f_Y|Y_0}(B_0)|_W.
\]
These finite-module descriptions agree on overlaps and under
further target-box restriction. At a flat target germ
$v\in W\cap t_Y(Y)$ their weight is
\[
 m_{\mathcal C_{p,f_Y}}(v)
   =\sum_{\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Min}(A_0)}
       \operatorname{length}_{(A_0)_{\mathfrak p}}
                                  ((B_0)_{\mathfrak p})
                        m_{(Y_0)_{\mathfrak p},f_Y}(v),
\]
where an empty component fibre has weight zero.

Suppose a unit tuple $f_X$ and an integral map
$a:\mathbb Z^n\to\mathbb Z^{n'}$ satisfy the character identity
$p^*f_Y=f_X^{a^*}$. All source weights in
$a^{-1}(U_p)\cap t_X(X)$ have whole interior fibres, and
\[
 a_*\bigl(\operatorname{Trop}_{f_X}(X)|_{a^{-1}(U_p)}\bigr)
                              =\mathcal C_{p,f_Y}.
\]
The pushforward uses the entire actual source support and is
proper over $U_p$.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The absolute boundary of $Y$ and the relative boundary of $X$
are closed, by Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus},
and compact, by Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact}.
Their moment images are compact, so $U_p$ is open.
If $(t_Y\circ p)(x)\in U_p$, then $x$ is interior relative
to $Y$ and $p(x)$ is interior over $K$.
The composition inclusion in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus} makes $x$
interior over $K$. Consequently
\[
                    U_p\cap(t_Y\circ p)(\partial_KX)
                                      =\varnothing.
\]
Theorem~\ref{v037:analytic:interiorcycle}, applied on $X$ to
the pullback unit tuple $p^*f_Y$, defines the asserted local
cycle and its support.

Fix $w\in U_p\cap t_Y(Y)$. The entire inverse image of its
target moment fibre is relatively interior by definition of
$U_p$. Theorem~\ref{v037:degree:finitelevelneighborhood}
therefore supplies the stated actual finite map, and permits
$P$ to be chosen inside $U_p$. Choose an open box
$W\subset\operatorname{int}(P)$ about $w$.
The whole target fibre at every $v\in W$ satisfies the box
inequalities strictly. It is interior in $Y_0$ relative to
$Y$, by the actual rational presentation, and interior in
$Y$ over $K$, by $P\subset U_p$. Composition makes it
interior in $Y_0$ over $K$. Thus $W$ belongs to the whole
interior weight domain of $Y_0$.
Theorem~\ref{v036:analytic:domainpurity} gives purity of
$Y_0$ and of every nonempty $X_0$.

The finite-map assertion of
Theorem~\ref{v037:analytic:interiorcycle}, with the same
moment tuple on source and target and the identity lattice
map, now gives
\[
 \operatorname{Trop}_{p_0^*(f_Y|Y_0)}(X_0)|_W
                      =\operatorname{Trop}_{f_Y|Y_0}(B_0)|_W.
\]
The whole pullback moment fibre at every $v\in W$ is the
same in $X_0$ and $X$, and it is absolute interior in both.
For $X_0$ this follows from finite relative interior and
the target interior just checked. The flat-germ restriction
assertion of Theorem~\ref{v036:flatgermlocalconstancy}
identifies their weights on every local generic cell.
Hence the source cycle in this display is
$\mathcal C_{p,f_Y}|_W$. If $X_0$ is empty, its algebra and
module cycle are zero by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion}, and the same
identity holds.

For completeness, the finite-module descriptions also compare
directly. Intersect two boxes at a weight in the overlap of
their open interiors. The intersection has that weight in
its interior and retains the whole target fibre and its
entire inverse image. The actual domain and finite tensor
comparisons, Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainbasechange} and
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange}, identify
both restricted finite algebras with the algebra of this
same inverse-image domain. The whole-fibre restriction
assertion of Theorem~\ref{v037:degree:moduleweight} gives
equal module weights on all generic cells of a common local
refinement. This proves the overlap and restriction statements.
The generic-length expression is the formula of that theorem
for the actual finite $A_0$-module $B_0$.

Finally, the equality $t_Y\circ p=a\circ t_X$ and the first
paragraph show that every whole $f_X$-fibre above $U_p$ is
absolute interior. Apply the finite-map theorem
\ref{v037:analytic:interiorcycle} to the identity map of $X$,
with source tuple $f_X$, target tuple $p^*f_Y$, and lattice
map $a$, and restrict its equality to $U_p$.
Its target module is the structure module of $X$, so the
result is exactly the asserted pushforward.
For a compact subset $C\subset U_p$, the complete inverse
image $t_X(X)\cap a^{-1}(C)$ is closed in the compact set
$t_X(X)$. This proves the stated properness on the actual
support.
\end{proof}
