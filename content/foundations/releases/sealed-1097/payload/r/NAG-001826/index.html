\begin{lemma}[\bi{Coefficient extension of matching kernels}{匹配核的系数扩张}]
\label{fnd:coefficientmatchingkernel}
\bi{Let $d:E\to F$ be a bounded map of Banach $K$-spaces, let $N=\ker d$ with its induced norm, and let $r$ be a finite $K$-free tuple. Under the coefficient identifications for scalar extension to $K_r$, the natural map identifies}
{设 $d:E\to F$ 是 Banach $K$-空间之间的有界映射，给 $N=\ker d$ 配备诱导范数，并取在 $K$ 上自由的有限元组 $r$。在向 $K_r$ 扩标量的系数识别下，自然映射给出}
\[
 N\widehat\otimes_KK_r\ \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\
 \ker\bigl(E\widehat\otimes_KK_r\longrightarrow
                  F\widehat\otimes_KK_r\bigr)
\]
\bi{isometrically. Coefficient extension also commutes isometrically with finite products equipped with their maximum norms. These identifications respect the actions and maps of Banach modules when the original maps are module-linear.}
{等距同构。系数扩张还与配备最大值范数的有限直积等距交换。当原映射为模线性时，这些识别保持 Banach 模的作用及映射。}
\end{lemma}

\paragraph{Matching kernels before any sheaf theorem (NAG-001826).}
For a bounded map \(d:E\to F\) of Banach \(K\)-spaces,
\(N=\ker d\) is closed and carries its induced norm.
With any one free tuple, NAG-001549 represents extension
as weighted Laurent coefficients, and the extended map
acts coefficientwise. A weighted-null family is in its kernel
exactly when each coefficient is in \(N\), with the same norm
on that family. Over a finite product the maximum and the
coefficient supremum commute, and the finite union of the
exceptions to a fixed tail bound is finite. These identifications
are isometric and natural. Module actions agree on finite
Laurent sums and then by continuity, using the coefficient
algebra and constant maps of NAG-001559. This statement
places no closed-image condition on \(d\).
