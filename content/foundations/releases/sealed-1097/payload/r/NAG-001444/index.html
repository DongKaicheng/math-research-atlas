Points of a strict rational quotient

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 179

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Points of a strict rational quotient}{Strict rational quotient 的 points}]\label{fnd:strictrationalpointtopology}
\bi{With $A,g,f_i,\rho,B$ as in Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictpowercontrolledrational}, the map induced by $A\to B$ is a homeomorphism}
{设 $A,g,f_i,\rho,B$ 如 Corollary~\ref{fnd:strictpowercontrolledrational}。由 $A\to B$ 诱导的 map 为 homeomorphism}
\[
 \mathcal M(B)\xrightarrow{\sim}
 V=\{x\in\mathcal M(A): |f_i|_x\leq\rho_i|g|_x
                       \text{ for }1\leq i\leq s\},
\]
\bi{where both spectra carry the evaluation topology and $V$ the subspace topology. The subset $V$ is closed. This includes $B=0$ and an empty spectrum; it does not assert that an algebra with empty spectrum must be zero.}
{其中两个 spectra 均配备 evaluation topology，$V$ 配备 subspace topology。Subset $V$ 为 closed。该断言包括 $B=0$ 及 spectrum 为空的情形；它并未断言 spectrum 为空的 algebra 必须为零。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{\textbf{Step 1: construct the point over $x$.} The unit-ideal identity in the preceding proof shows that $|g|_x>0$ for every $x\in V$. By Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}, $x$ defines an absolute value on the field $F_x=\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$, with $|a|_x\leq\|a\|$ for $a\in A$. Evaluate polynomials at $X_i=f_i/g$ in $F_x$. The resulting multiplicative seminorm $\alpha_x$ on $A[X]$ satisfies}
{\textbf{Step 1：构造 $x$ 之上的 point。} 前一证明中的 unit-ideal identity 表明，每个 $x\in V$ 都有 $|g|_x>0$。由 Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}，$x$ 在 field $F_x=\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$ 上定义 absolute value，且对 $a\in A$ 有 $|a|_x\leq\|a\|$。在 $F_x$ 中令 $X_i=f_i/g$，对 polynomials 作 evaluation。所得 $A[X]$ 上的 multiplicative seminorm $\alpha_x$ 满足}
\[
 \alpha_x\left(\sum_\nu a_\nu X^\nu\right)
 \leq\max_\nu |a_\nu|_x\prod_i|f_i/g|_x^{\nu_i}
 \leq\max_\nu\|a_\nu\|\rho^\nu.
\]
\bi{For polynomials $P,Q$, the ultrametric inequality also gives}
{对 polynomials $P,Q$，ultrametric inequality 还给出}
\[
 |\alpha_x(P)-\alpha_x(Q)|\leq\alpha_x(P-Q)\leq\|P-Q\|.
\]
\bi{Given $F\in D=A\{\rho^{-1}X\}$, choose polynomial truncations $P_n\to F$. The displayed estimate makes $\alpha_x(P_n)$ a Cauchy sequence of real numbers and makes its limit independent of the truncations. Define $\alpha_x(F)$ to be this limit. Polynomial density follows from Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness}. Addition, multiplication and the scalar rule pass to the limit: for multiplication, polynomial approximations are norm-bounded and their products converge by submultiplicativity. Thus the extension is a multiplicative seminorm with}
{给定 $F\in D=A\{\rho^{-1}X\}$，取 polynomial truncations $P_n\to F$。上述 estimate 使 $\alpha_x(P_n)$ 成为实数的 Cauchy sequence，并使其 limit 与 truncations 无关。将该 limit 定义为 $\alpha_x(F)$。Polynomial density 来自 Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientcompleteness}。Addition、multiplication 与 scalar rule 均可传到 limit；对 multiplication，polynomial approximations 在 norm 下 bounded，且由 submultiplicativity，其 products 收敛。因此，延拓后仍为 multiplicative seminorm，满足}
\[
 \alpha_x(F)\leq\|F\|,\qquad
 |\alpha_x(F)-\alpha_x(G)|\leq\|F-G\|.
\]
\bi{It kills every $gX_i-f_i$ and therefore the actual ideal $J$. If $F-G\in J$, the ultrametric inequality gives $\alpha_x(F)=\alpha_x(G)$. The induced seminorm on $B$ is bounded by its quotient norm, by taking the infimum over lifts. It extends the fixed absolute value on $K$ and defines a point $y_x$ over $x$. In particular, when $B=0$, this construction rules out any $x\in V$. It uses no completion of $F_x$.}
{它消去每个 $gX_i-f_i$，因而消去实际 ideal $J$。若 $F-G\in J$，则由 ultrametric inequality，$\alpha_x(F)=\alpha_x(G)$。对 lifts 取 infimum，可见 $B$ 上诱导的 seminorm 被 quotient norm 控制。它延拓 $K$ 上指定的 absolute value，定义 $x$ 之上的 point $y_x$。特别地，当 $B=0$ 时，此构造排除了 $V$ 中存在 point 的可能性。这里没有使用 $F_x$ 的 completion。}

\bi{\textbf{Step 2: image and uniqueness.} A bounded multiplicative seminorm on the nonzero strict affinoid algebra $B$ is contractive by Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}. Hence $|x_i|_y\leq\rho_i$, and the relations show that its restriction belongs to $V$. For a polynomial $P$ of total degree at most $N$, write}
{\textbf{Step 2：image 与 uniqueness。} 由 Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}，非零 strict affinoid algebra $B$ 上的 bounded multiplicative seminorm 为 contractive。因此 $|x_i|_y\leq\rho_i$，由 relations 可见其 restriction 属于 $V$。对 total degree 至多为 $N$ 的 polynomial $P$，写出}
\[
 Q_P=\sum_\nu a_\nu f^\nu g^{N-|\nu|}\in A,
 \qquad g^N P(x_1,\ldots,x_s)=Q_P\quad\text{in }B.
\]
\bi{For every point $y$ over $x$, this gives $|P|_y=|Q_P|_x/|g|_x^N$. Thus its values on polynomial images are fixed. These images are dense in $B$, and contractivity gives the same continuity estimate used in Step 1. Therefore $y=y_x$. Restriction is continuous because each evaluation on $A$ is an evaluation on $B$.}
{对每个 $x$ 之上的 point $y$，此式给出 $|P|_y=|Q_P|_x/|g|_x^N$。因此其在 polynomial images 上的值已被确定。这些 images 在 $B$ 中 dense，contractivity 给出与 Step 1 相同的 continuity estimate，故 $y=y_x$。Restriction 为 continuous，因为对 $A$ 的每个 evaluation 都是对 $B$ 的一个 evaluation。}

\bi{\textbf{Step 3: continuity of the inverse.} For each polynomial $P$, the function $x\mapsto|Q_P|_x/|g|_x^N$ is continuous on $V$. Fix $v\in B$ and any lift $F\in D$. Polynomial truncations satisfy, uniformly for $x\in V$,}
{\textbf{Step 3：inverse 的 continuity。} 对每个 polynomial $P$，function $x\mapsto|Q_P|_x/|g|_x^N$ 在 $V$ 上 continuous。固定 $v\in B$ 及其任意 lift $F\in D$。Polynomial truncations 满足对 $x\in V$ 一致成立的 estimate：}
\[
 \bigl||v|_{y_x}-|P_n|_{y_x}\bigr|\leq\|F-P_n\|\longrightarrow0.
\]
\bi{It follows that $x\mapsto|v|_{y_x}$ is continuous. Explicitly, given a point of $V$ and $\varepsilon>0$, choose a truncation with error less than $\varepsilon/3$ and use continuity of its evaluation to bound the difference of its values by $\varepsilon/3$ on a neighborhood. The two approximation errors then bound the difference for $v$ by $\varepsilon$. Since the evaluation topology is the weakest topology making all these functions continuous, $x\mapsto y_x$ is continuous. Finally, the finitely many inequalities defining $V$ are closed conditions on continuous real-valued functions.}
{因此 $x\mapsto|v|_{y_x}$ continuous。具体地，给定 $V$ 中的点及 $\varepsilon>0$，选择 error 小于 $\varepsilon/3$ 的 truncation，再利用其 evaluation 的 continuity，在某个 neighborhood 上将两个函数值之差控制在 $\varepsilon/3$ 内。加上两端的 approximation errors，$v$ 的函数值之差即小于 $\varepsilon$。Evaluation topology 是使全部这些 functions 连续的最弱 topology，故 $x\mapsto y_x$ continuous。最后，定义 $V$ 的有限个 inequalities 是 continuous real-valued functions 上的 closed conditions。}
\end{proof}
