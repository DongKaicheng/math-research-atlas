Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Continuous multiplication of a starting structure current]\label{f9si:mult}
Let $Y$ be good, Hausdorff, and pure of analytic dimension $d\geq1$. For every real continuous $u$ on $Y$, define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Ju}
 J_u(\alpha)=\int_Y u\,d\mu_{Y,\alpha},\qquad
 \alpha\in\E^{d,d}(Y),
\end{equation}
where $\mu_{Y,\alpha}$ is the exact frozen native top-form measure of \NAG{002970}. Then $J_u$ is an actual native current. On each cutoff stage there is a constant $A_i\geq0$, independent of $u$ and $\eta$, such that
\begin{align}
 |\mu_{Y,Q_i\eta}|(Y)&\leq A_i p_{t_f(V),0}(\eta),\label{eq:variationbound}\\
 |J_u(Q_i\eta)|&\leq A_i\sup_{C_i}|u|\,p_{t_f(V),0}(\eta).\label{eq:multbound}
\end{align}
For an empty support the expressions are zero. The construction is linear in $u$ and in the test, local on ordinary opens, and agrees with $\int_Yu\alpha$ when $u$ is ordinary smooth. If $u_a\to u$ uniformly on every compact set, then $J_{u_a}\to J_u$ on all tests, uniformly on each fixed stage with bounded indicated order-zero seminorm.

For the affinoid data of Theorem~\ref{f9si:cycle},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:multcomponents}
 J_u^X=\sum_i m_i(j_i)_*J_{u|_{X_i}}^{X_i}.
\end{equation}
The same construction component by component supplies a continuous multiplier on every supplied weighted analytic cycle of that theorem.
\end{theorem}
