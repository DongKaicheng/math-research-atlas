Proper analytic GAGA

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 304

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Proper analytic GAGA}
{适当解析 GAGA}]
\label{fnd:propergagainternal}
\bi{For every proper finite-type scheme $X$ over a complete
non-Archimedean field $K$, analytification is an exact tensor
equivalence}
{对完备非阿基米德域 $K$ 上每个适当的有限型概形 $X$，
解析化给出正合张量范畴等价}
\[
 \operatorname{Coh}(X)\ \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\
 \operatorname{Coh}(X^{\an}_G).
\]
\bi{It preserves coherent cohomology and restricts to an
equivalence on vector bundles, and hence on line bundles.}
{它保持凝聚上同调，并限制为向量丛的范畴等价，
特别地给出线丛的等价。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Full faithfulness is Proposition~\ref{fnd:properextcomparison}.
Exactness and compatibility with tensor products are
Theorem~\ref{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact}; cohomology
comparison is Theorem~\ref{fnd:propercoherentcomparison}.
It remains to prove essential surjectivity. We do this by
Noetherian induction on closed subsets $Z\subset|X|$, with
the assertion that every analytic coherent module supported
in $Z^{\an}$ is algebraizable on $X$. For a closed subset,
$Z^{\an}$ means the analytification of its reduced closed
subscheme; this underlying set is unchanged by a thickening.
The empty case is immediate.}
{全忠实性来自 Proposition~\ref{fnd:properextcomparison}；
正合性及与张量积的相容性来自
Theorem~\ref{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact}；
上同调比较来自 Theorem~\ref{fnd:propercoherentcomparison}。
只需证明本质满射。对闭子集 $Z\subset|X|$ 作 Noetherian
归纳，断言为：每个支撑在 $Z^{\an}$ 中的解析凝聚模都能在
$X$ 上代数化。这里闭子集的 $Z^{\an}$ 指相应约化闭子概形
的解析化；增厚不改变其底集。空集情形显然。}

\bi{Let $\mathcal F$ be supported in $Z^{\an}$.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:analyticcoherentsupportthickening} realizes
it as the closed direct image of a coherent module
$\mathcal F_T$ on $T^{\an}$, for a closed subscheme
$T\hookrightarrow X$ with underlying space $Z$.
Apply Chow as in the preceding proof: there is a projective
surjection $\pi:Y\to T$ with $Y$ projective over $K$,
which is an isomorphism above a dense open $U\subset T$.
The coherent module $(\pi^{\an})^*\mathcal F_T$ is
algebraizable on $Y$ by Corollary~\ref{fnd:projectiveschemegaga};
choose an algebraization $\mathcal E$ and its identifying
isomorphism. Relative projective comparison in degree zero
identifies}
{设 $\mathcal F$ 支撑在 $Z^{\an}$ 中。
Lemma~\ref{fnd:analyticcoherentsupportthickening} 将其表示为
某个底空间为 $Z$ 的闭子概形 $T\hookrightarrow X$ 的解析化上
凝聚模 $\mathcal F_T$ 的闭直接像。
按前一证明使用 Chow 引理，得到射影满射 $\pi:Y\to T$，
其中 $Y$ 在 $K$ 上射影，且 $\pi$ 在稠密开集 $U\subset T$
上为同构。由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:projectiveschemegaga}，
凝聚模 $(\pi^{\an})^*\mathcal F_T$ 可在 $Y$ 上代数化；
选择其代数化 $\mathcal E$ 及相应同构。
零次相对射影比较给出识别}
\[
 \mathcal G:=\pi^{\an}_*(\pi^{\an})^*\mathcal F_T
       \simeq (\pi_*\mathcal E)^{\an}.
\]
\bi{In particular $\mathcal G$ is coherent. The kernel
$\mathcal K$ and cokernel $\mathcal C$ of the adjunction
$\mathcal F_T\to\mathcal G$ are coherent and vanish
on $U^{\an}$. Their closed direct images on $X^{\an}$
are supported in the proper closed subset $Z'=T\setminus U$
of $Z$. By the induction hypothesis they are algebraizations
of coherent modules $K_X,C_X$ on $X$. These algebraizations
are annihilated by the ideal of $T$: the respective
multiplication maps by this ideal analytify to zero, so
full faithfulness makes them zero already on $X$.
Thus they descend to coherent $K_T,C_T$ on $T$, whose
analytifications are $\mathcal K,\mathcal C$.}
{特别地，$\mathcal G$ 凝聚。伴随映射
$\mathcal F_T\to\mathcal G$ 的核 $\mathcal K$ 和余核
$\mathcal C$ 凝聚，且在 $U^{\an}$ 上为零。
它们在 $X^{\an}$ 上的闭直接像支撑在 $Z$ 的真闭子集
$Z'=T\setminus U$ 中。由归纳假设，它们分别是 $X$ 上凝聚模
$K_X,C_X$ 的解析化。这些代数化均被 $T$ 的理想消灭：
相应的理想乘法映射解析化后为零，由全忠实性，它们在 $X$
上已经为零。因此它们下降为 $T$ 上的凝聚模 $K_T,C_T$，
解析化分别为 $\mathcal K,\mathcal C$。}

\bi{The map $\mathcal G\to\mathcal C$ algebraizes by
full faithfulness on the proper scheme $T$. Let $J_T$
be the kernel of the resulting map
$\pi_*\mathcal E\to C_T$. Exactness gives an exact
analytic sequence}
{由适当概形 $T$ 上的全忠实性，映射
$\mathcal G\to\mathcal C$ 可代数化。
令 $J_T$ 为所得映射 $\pi_*\mathcal E\to C_T$ 的核。
正合性给出解析短正合列}
\[
 0\longrightarrow K_T^{\an}\longrightarrow\mathcal F_T
       \longrightarrow J_T^{\an}\longrightarrow0.
\]
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:properextcomparison} in degree one
lifts its class to $\operatorname{Ext}^1_T(J_T,K_T)$.
By \cite[Tag 06XV]{StacksAlg} this class is represented
by an algebraic short exact sequence
$0\to K_T\to F_T\to J_T\to0$; its middle term is coherent.
Indeed, locally take a finite free surjection $P\to J_T$
and lift its finitely many basis sections into $F_T$ after
shrinking. Then $K_T\oplus P\to F_T$ is surjective and
its kernel, by projection to $P$, is isomorphic to the
coherent kernel of $P\to J_T$. Thus $F_T$ is a cokernel
of a map between coherent modules. The analytic extension is isomorphic to
the displayed sequence with identity on its ends.
Hence $F_T^{\an}\simeq\mathcal F_T$.
Closed direct image to $X$ algebraizes $\mathcal F$.
This completes the Noetherian induction and proves the
equivalence.}
{将 Proposition~\ref{fnd:properextcomparison} 用于一次 Ext，
可将该扩张类提升到 $\operatorname{Ext}^1_T(J_T,K_T)$。
由 \cite[Tag 06XV]{StacksAlg}，此类由代数短正合列
$0\to K_T\to F_T\to J_T\to0$ 表示，其中间项凝聚。
确实，局部取有限自由满射 $P\to J_T$，并在缩小后将其有限个
基截面提升到 $F_T$。则 $K_T\oplus P\to F_T$ 满射，
其核通过投影到 $P$ 与 $P\to J_T$ 的凝聚核同构。
所以 $F_T$ 是凝聚模间态射的余核。所得解析扩张与所列序列同构，
且在两端为恒等映射，所以 $F_T^{\an}\simeq\mathcal F_T$。
再闭直接像到 $X$ 即代数化 $\mathcal F$。
Noetherian 归纳完成，范畴等价得证。}

\bi{Finally finite local freeness descends along a
faithfully flat local map. Here is the elementary argument
in the form needed. If $R\to S$ is such a map, $M$ is
finite over the Noetherian local ring $R$, and $S\otimes_R M$
is free of rank $r$, then the residue-field extension shows
$\dim_{\kappa(R)}M/\mathfrak m_RM=r$. Lift a basis;
Nakayama gives a surjection $R^r\to M$. After tensoring
with $S$ this is a surjection between two rank-$r$ free
modules over the local ring $S$, and hence an isomorphism
(reduce its determinant modulo the maximal ideal).
Faithful flatness kills its kernel, so $M$ is free.}
{最后，有限局部自由性沿忠实平坦局部映射下降。
所需的初等论证如下。设 $R\to S$ 为这样的映射，
$M$ 是 Noetherian 局部环 $R$ 上的有限模，且
$S\otimes_R M$ 自由、秩为 $r$。
由剩余域扩张可知
$\dim_{\kappa(R)}M/\mathfrak m_RM=r$。
提升一组基，Nakayama 引理给出满射 $R^r\to M$。
与 $S$ 张量后，它是局部环 $S$ 上两个秩为 $r$ 的自由模之间
的满射，因而为同构：将其行列式模极大理想约化即可。
忠实平坦性检测其核为零，所以 $M$ 自由。}

\bi{For completeness, local freeness in the analytic
$G$-topology also yields free ordinary stalks here.
On an affinoid $\mathcal M(A)$ representing a finite
module $M$, choose a trivializing affinoid domain
$\mathcal M(B)$ containing the point in question.
The domain map $A\to B$ is flat; localizing at the
corresponding primes gives a faithfully flat local map.
The preceding argument makes $M$ free at that prime of
$A$. A finite presentation then makes it free after
inverting one element not in the prime: lift the basis
and clear the finitely many relations for the kernel
and cokernel. A small rational neighbourhood on which
that element is invertible gives the same free ordinary
analytic stalk. Now every scheme point has an analytic
lift by Lemma~\ref{fnd:analytificationsurjectivity},
and its local map is faithfully flat by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact}.
Applying the local descent argument shows that an
algebraization of an analytic vector bundle is locally
free of the same rank. The converse follows by tensoring
a local basis. Rank one gives the line-bundle assertion.}
{补充说明，这里的解析 $G$-拓扑局部自由性也给出通常茎的
自由性。在表示有限模 $M$ 的 affinoid $\mathcal M(A)$ 上，
选择一个包含所考察点且使模平凡化的 affinoid 域
$\mathcal M(B)$。域映射 $A\to B$ 平坦；在相应素理想处
局部化得到忠实平坦局部映射。前述论证说明 $M$ 在 $A$ 的
该素理想处自由。有限表示进一步说明，反演某个不属于该素理想
的元素后 $M$ 已经自由：提升一组基，再清除核与余核的有限多个
关系所需的分母。在使该元素可逆的一个小 rational 邻域上，
所得通常解析茎因而自由。
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:analytificationsurjectivity}，每个概形点
有解析提升；由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact}，
相应局部映射忠实平坦。应用局部下降论证，解析向量丛的
代数化局部自由且秩相同。反向只需将局部基张量。
秩一情形即线丛的断言。}
\end{proof}
