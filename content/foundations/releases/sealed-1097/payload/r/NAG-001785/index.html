Regular algebraic scalar extension

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 352

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Regular algebraic scalar extension}{正则的代数扩标量}]
\label{fnd:analyticseparablescalarregular}
\bi{Let $A$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra and let $L/K$ be an analytically separable complete extension. The ordinary ring map $A\to A_L$ is regular. Consequently, if $B$ is a finite-type $A$-algebra, then $B\to B\otimes_A A_L$ is regular, and regular localizations of $B$ have regular inverse images under this base change.}
{设 $A$ 为 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数，$L/K$ 为解析可分的完备扩张。则通常环映射 $A\to A_L$ 正则。因此，对有限型 $A$-代数 $B$，映射 $B\to B\otimes_A A_L$ 正则，且 $B$ 的正则局部化在此基变换下的逆像也正则。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The map is flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalscalarfaithful}. For any $\mathfrak p\subset A$ and any finite purely inseparable extension $E/\kappa(\mathfrak p)$, clear denominators to find a finite domain $C$ over $A/\mathfrak p$ with $\operatorname{Frac}(C)=E$. Give $C$ its canonical finite affinoid structure. Finite-module scalar extension identifies $C_L$ with $C\otimes_A A_L$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticseparableregularopen} gives $0\ne c\in C$ such that $C_L[1/c]$ is regular. Therefore}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalscalarfaithful}，该映射平坦。对任意 $\mathfrak p\subset A$ 及有限纯不可分扩张 $E/\kappa(\mathfrak p)$，清除分母后可取 $A/\mathfrak p$ 上的有限整环 $C$，使 $\operatorname{Frac}(C)=E$；赋予它典范有限 affinoid 结构。有限模扩标量将 $C_L$ 识别为 $C\otimes_A A_L$。Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticseparableregularopen} 给出 $0\ne c\in C$，使 $C_L[1/c]$ 正则。因此}
\[
                 A_L\otimes_A E
                       =C_L\otimes_C E
\]
\bi{is a localization of a regular ring and is regular. Tag 0381 proves geometric regularity of every algebraic fibre, giving the first assertion. Finite-type base change of regular ring maps is \cite[Tag 07C1]{StacksAlg}; its Noetherian fibre hypothesis holds since $A_L$ and its finite-type algebra $B\otimes_A A_L$ are Noetherian. At a prime above a regular localization of $B$, apply Tag 031E to the flat local map and its regular closed fibre. This proves the last assertion.}
{为正则环的局部化，故正则。Tag 0381 给出每个代数纤维的几何正则性，从而证明第一项。正则环映射在有限型基变换下的保持由 \cite[Tag 07C1]{StacksAlg} 给出；其 Noetherian 纤维条件成立，因为 $A_L$ 及其有限型代数 $B\otimes_A A_L$ 都 Noetherian。在 $B$ 的正则局部化上方的任意素理想处，对平坦局部映射及其正则闭纤维应用 Tag 031E，即得最后断言。}
\end{proof}
