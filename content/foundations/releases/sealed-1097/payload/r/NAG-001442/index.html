The bounded universal property

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{The bounded universal property}{Bounded universal property}]\label{fnd:strictquotientboundedup}
\bi{Let $A$ be a nonzero strict norm-admissible $K$-affinoid algebra, let $\rho=(\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_s)\in|K^\times|^s$, and put $D=A\{\rho^{-1}X\}$. For an ideal $J=(h_1,\ldots,h_t)\subset D$, give $B=D/J$ its quotient norm and write $x_i$ for the image of $X_i$. Let $C$ be any Banach $K$-algebra, with the zero case allowed, and let $\phi:A\to C$ be a bounded unital $K$-algebra homomorphism. Bounded $A$-algebra maps $\psi:B\to C$ are in bijection with tuples $b=(b_1,\ldots,b_s)\in C^s$ satisfying both conditions:}
{设 $A$ 为非零 strict norm-admissible $K$-affinoid algebra，$\rho=(\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_s)\in|K^\times|^s$，并令 $D=A\{\rho^{-1}X\}$。对 ideal $J=(h_1,\ldots,h_t)\subset D$，给 $B=D/J$ 配备 quotient norm，记 $X_i$ 的 image 为 $x_i$。设 $C$ 为任意 Banach $K$-algebra，允许零的情形，且 $\phi:A\to C$ 为有界含幺 $K$-代数同态。Bounded $A$-algebra maps $\psi:B\to C$ 与满足以下两个条件的 tuples $b=(b_1,\ldots,b_s)\in C^s$ 一一对应：}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \bi{For each $i$ there is $C_i\geq1$ such that $\|b_i^n\|\leq C_i\rho_i^n$ for every integer $n\geq0$.}{对每个 $i$，存在 $C_i\geq1$，使每个整数 $n\geq0$ 都满足 $\|b_i^n\|\leq C_i\rho_i^n$。}
\item \bi{The convergent evaluations $h_j(b)$, with coefficients mapped by $\phi$, vanish for $1\leq j\leq t$.}{将 coefficients 通过 $\phi$ 映射后，收敛的 evaluations $h_j(b)$ 对 $1\leq j\leq t$ 均为零。}
\end{enumerate}
\bi{The correspondence sends $\psi$ to $(\psi(x_i))_i$. If $\|\phi(a)\|\leq C_\phi\|a\|$, the map obtained from such a tuple satisfies}
{对应将 $\psi$ 送到 $(\psi(x_i))_i$。若 $\|\phi(a)\|\leq C_\phi\|a\|$，则由上述 tuple 得到的 map 满足}
\[
 \|\psi(v)\|\leq C_\phi\prod_{i=1}^s C_i\,\|v\|
 \qquad(v\in B).
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictweightedquotient} gives a closed actual ideal $J$ and a complete quotient $B$. If $\psi$ is bounded by $C$, then $\|x_i^n\|\leq\rho_i^n$ gives $\|\psi(x_i)^n\|\leq C\rho_i^n$; enlarge $C$ to at least one. Composing with $D\to B$ shows that every defining relation evaluates to zero.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictweightedquotient}，实际 ideal $J$ 为 closed，商 $B$ complete。若 $\psi$ 的 bound 为 $C$，则 $\|x_i^n\|\leq\rho_i^n$ 给出 $\|\psi(x_i)^n\|\leq C\rho_i^n$；可将 $C$ 增大至至少为一。与 $D\to B$ 复合，可见全部 defining relations 的 evaluations 均为零。}

\bi{Conversely, Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundedsubstitution} extends the tuple to a bounded homomorphism $\Phi:D\to C$ with bound $L=C_\phi\prod_iC_i$. The second condition kills the actual ideal $J$, not just its chosen generators. Thus $\Phi$ induces an algebraic map $\psi:B\to C$. For every lift $F$ of $v$ and every $j\in J$,}
{反之，由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundedsubstitution}，该 tuple 延拓为 bound 为 $L=C_\phi\prod_iC_i$ 的 bounded homomorphism $\Phi:D\to C$。第二个条件消去实际 ideal $J$，而不只是所选 generators。因此 $\Phi$ 诱导 algebraic map $\psi:B\to C$。对 $v$ 的每个 lift $F$ 以及每个 $j\in J$，}
\[
 \|\psi(v)\|=\|\Phi(F+j)\|\leq L\|F+j\|.
\]
\bi{Taking the infimum proves the stated quotient-norm bound; attainment of that infimum is unnecessary. The images of polynomials are dense in $B$. Two bounded maps with the same values on $A$ and the $x_i$ therefore agree. All constructions include the zero target; a map from the zero source to a nonzero unital target cannot occur, because it would identify zero and one.}
{对右侧取 infimum，即得所述 quotient-norm bound，无须 infimum 取到。Polynomials 的 images 在 $B$ 中 dense，所以两个 bounded maps 若在 $A$ 及所有 $x_i$ 上相同，就处处相同。这些构造包括 zero target；从 zero source 到非零 unital target 的 map 不可能存在，因为它会将零与一识别。}
\end{proof}
