Finite generation of strict canonical reduction

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 525

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Finite generation of strict canonical reduction}
{Strict 典范约化的有限生成性}]
\label{fnd:strictcanonicalfinitegeneration}
\bi{For every admissible strict presentation
$K\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle\twoheadrightarrow A$, the map}
{对每个 admissible strict 表示
$K\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle\twoheadrightarrow A$，映射}
\[
 \widetilde K[X_1,\ldots,X_n]\longrightarrow\widetilde A
\]
\bi{is finite. In particular $\widetilde A$ is a reduced finite
type $\widetilde K$-algebra.}
{是有限映射。特别地，$\widetilde A$ 是约化的有限型
$\widetilde K$-代数。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The zero algebra is immediate. Otherwise take the finitely
many minimal primes $\mathfrak p_j$ of $A$. The canonical map}
{零代数的情形直接成立。否则，取 $A$ 的有限个极小素理想
$\mathfrak p_j$，典范映射}
\[
 \widetilde A\longrightarrow\prod_j\widetilde{A/\mathfrak p_j}
\]
\bi{is injective: if an element has spectral radius less than one
on every component, the finite maximum of those radii is less than
one on $A$. For each quotient, apply the presentation-compatible
normalization theorem, Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization}.
It gives a bounded finite injection from a Tate algebra $R_j$ and
a contractive lift $R_j\to K\langle X\rangle$.
Proposition~\ref{fnd:normalizationcanonicalfinite} makes
$\widetilde{A/\mathfrak p_j}$ finite over $\widetilde R_j$,
and therefore finite over $\widetilde K[X]$ via that lift.
Their finite product is a finite module over the Noetherian ring
$\widetilde K[X]$. The displayed injection makes $\widetilde A$
a submodule, so it too is finite. Reducedness was proved in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundreductioninternal}.}
{为单射：若一个元素在每个分支上的谱半径都小于一，这些谱半径的
有限最大值仍小于一，也就是它在 $A$ 上的谱半径小于一。
对每个商应用与表示相容的正规化定理
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization}，得到 Tate 代数
$R_j$ 的有界有限单射，以及压缩提升
$R_j\to K\langle X\rangle$。
Proposition~\ref{fnd:normalizationcanonicalfinite} 说明
$\widetilde{A/\mathfrak p_j}$ 在 $\widetilde R_j$ 上有限，
故经由上述提升也在 $\widetilde K[X]$ 上有限。
它们的有限积为 Noetherian 环 $\widetilde K[X]$ 上的有限模，
而所列单射将 $\widetilde A$ 识别为子模，故它也有限。
约化性已由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundreductioninternal} 证明。}
\end{proof}
