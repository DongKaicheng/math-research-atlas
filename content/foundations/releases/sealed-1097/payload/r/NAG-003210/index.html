\begin{example}[Reduction loses a factor]\label{f9be:inseparable}
Let $k=\mathbf F_p$, $K=k((t))$ and $L=k((u))$ with $u^p=t$.
Keep $|t|=c$ with $0<c<1$, so $|u|=c^{1/p}$.
Take $R=k[[t]]$, $R'=k[[u]]$, and regard
$\cX=\mathbf P^d_{k[[v]]}$ as an $R$-model via $t=v^p$,
where $d\geq2$. Grouping power-series exponents modulo $p$
identifies $k[[v]]$ with the free $R$-module on
$1,v,\ldots,v^{p-1}$. Thus $\cX$ is integral, proper and flat over $R$,
and its generic fibre is normal integral over $K$.

Take exponent one throughout, $(\cL_0,\sigma_0)=(\cO,v)$
with vertical support, and use $d$ coordinate hyperplane sections
of $\cO(1)$ for the other factors. Their intersection with
$\operatorname{div}(v)$ is one $k$-point of length one, so
$J_R(\cX)=\lambda_K$.
The full base change is
\[
 \cY=\mathbf P^d_{R'[v]/(v^p-u^p)}
       \simeq\mathbf P^d_{R'[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^p)},\qquad
 v=u+\epsilon .
\]
It has generic length $p$ along its one reduced component.
After the hyperplane operations the quotient by $v$ is
$R'/(u^p)$, of length $p$; multiplication by $v$ is injective
because $v^p=u^p$ and the algebra is $R'$-free.
Equivalently, compute on the full fundamental cycle
$p[\mathbf P^d_{R'}]$. Thus
\[
 \deg_{k'}\beta_{\cY}=p,\qquad
 J_{R'}(\cY)=(\lambda_K/p)p=\lambda_K.
\]
Replacing $\cY$ by its reduction before forming its fundamental
cycle gives degree one and the incorrect value $\lambda_K/p$.
Its reduction is a different scheme, not a permissible deletion
of generic multiplicity.
\end{example}
