\begin{proposition}[Absolute logarithmic integrability and native normalization; F8CR-I]
\label{prop:log}
Let \(f\in A\) be regular and let \(\beta\) be any compact ordinary
\((n,n)\)-form on \(X\). Put \(\beta_i=i_i^*\beta\) and extend
\(\log|f|\) by zero on \(D\). This extension is Borel, is integrable
against \(|\mu_{X,\beta}|\), and satisfies
\begin{align}
 \int_X|\log|f||\,d|\mu_{X,\beta}|
 &\leq\sum_i m_i M_i\,|\mu_{X_i,\beta_i}|(X_i)<\infty,
                                                    \label{eq:logbound}\\
 L_f(\beta)=\int_X\log|f|\,d\mu_{X,\beta}
 &=\sum_i m_i L_{f_i}(\beta_i).             \label{eq:logsplit}
\end{align}
Here \(M_i\) is any bound for \(|\log|f_i||\) on
\(\Supp\beta_i\), and is zero for an empty support. The measure
assigns zero variation mass to \(D\), so its assigned logarithm
value there is immaterial. No scalar PL or ZP hypothesis is needed.
\end{proposition}
