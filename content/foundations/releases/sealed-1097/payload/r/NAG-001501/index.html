Flattening a rational presentation by exact fractions

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 212

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Flattening a rational presentation by exact fractions}{用精确分式展开 rational 表示}]\label{fnd:rationalfractionflattening}
\bi{Let $B=A\langle f/g\rangle$ and fix certificates}
{设 $B=A\langle f/g\rangle$，并固定单位理想证书}
\[
 c_0g+\sum_{i=1}^s c_if_i=1\quad\text{in }A,
 \qquad \sum_{j=0}^t b_ja_j=1\quad\text{in }B.
\]
\bi{Suppose $a_j=g^{-N}p_j$ in $B$ for one integer $N\geq0$ and $p_j\in A$. Put}
{假设对某个整数 $N\geq0$ 及 $p_j\in A$，在 $B$ 中有 $a_j=g^{-N}p_j$。记}
\[
 L=\max(1,\|c_0\|,\ldots,\|c_s\|),\qquad
 H=\max(1,\|b_0\|,\ldots,\|b_t\|),
\]
\bi{and choose $\gamma\in K^\times$ satisfying $|\gamma|L^{N+1}H\leq1$. Set $d=gp_0$. Then}
{并选择满足 $|\gamma|L^{N+1}H\leq1$ 的 $\gamma\in K^\times$。令 $d=gp_0$。则}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 C'&=B\{S_1,\ldots,S_t\}/(a_0S_j-a_j)_j,\\
 D&=A\{Y_1,\ldots,Y_s,Z_1,\ldots,Z_t,V\}/
       (dY_i-p_0f_i,\ dZ_j-gp_j,\ dV-\gamma)_{i,j}
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{are canonically isometric as $A$-algebras. The tuple defining $D$ generates the unit ideal because it contains $\gamma$; thus $D$ is a rational presentation over $A$. The isometry identifies its variables by}
{作为 $A$-代数具有典范等距同构。定义 $D$ 的 tuple 包含 $\gamma$，因而生成单位理想，所以 $D$ 是 $A$ 上的 rational 表示。该等距同构将变量识别为}
\[
 Y_i\longmapsto\overline X_i,\qquad
 Z_j\longmapsto\overline S_j,\qquad
 V\longmapsto\gamma g^{-(N+1)}a_0^{-1}
 \quad\text{in }C'.
\]
\bi{Zero quotients are included. No unit-ideal condition on $(p_0,\ldots,p_t)$ in $A$ is required.}
{结论包括零商。这里不要求 $(p_0,\ldots,p_t)$ 在 $A$ 中生成单位理想。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{All displayed ideals are actual closed ideals by Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictweightedquotient}. In $C'$, the two certificates give}
{由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictweightedquotient}，所列理想均为实际闭理想。在 $C'$ 中，两个证书给出}
\[
 g^{-1}=c_0+\sum_i c_i\overline X_i,
 \qquad a_0^{-1}=b_0+\sum_{j=1}^t b_j\overline S_j.
\]
\bi{Their norms are at most $L$ and $H$, respectively. Since $p_0=g^Na_0$, the element $d=g^{N+1}a_0$ is a unit and $\|\gamma d^{-1}\|\leq |\gamma|L^{N+1}H\leq1$. The proposed images of $Y_i,Z_j,V$ all have norm at most one. The relations for the first two blocks follow from $g\overline X_i=f_i$ and $a_0\overline S_j=a_j=g^{-N}p_j$; the last relation follows from the definition of $\gamma d^{-1}$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictquotientboundedup} therefore gives a contractive map $D\to C'$.}
{它们的范数分别不超过 $L$ 与 $H$。由于 $p_0=g^Na_0$，元素 $d=g^{N+1}a_0$ 为单位，且 $\|\gamma d^{-1}\|\leq |\gamma|L^{N+1}H\leq1$。所指定的 $Y_i,Z_j,V$ 的像范数均至多为一。前两组关系来自 $g\overline X_i=f_i$ 以及 $a_0\overline S_j=a_j=g^{-N}p_j$，最后一个关系来自 $\gamma d^{-1}$ 的定义。因此由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictquotientboundedup} 得到 contractive 映射 $D\to C'$。}

\bi{Conversely, in $D$ the equation $d\overline V=\gamma$ makes $d$ a unit. Therefore $g$ and $p_0$ are units separately. Dividing $d\overline Y_i=p_0f_i$ by $p_0$ gives $g\overline Y_i=f_i$. The images of the $Y_i$ have norm at most one, so Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictquotientboundedup} gives a contractive coefficient map $B\to D$. Under this map the equality $a_j=g^{-N}p_j$ persists. Dividing $d\overline Z_j=gp_j$ by $g$ and multiplying by $g^{-N}$ gives $a_0\overline Z_j=a_j$. The same proposition now gives a contractive map $C'\to D$.}
{反方向，在 $D$ 中，等式 $d\overline V=\gamma$ 使 $d$ 成为单位，因而 $g$ 与 $p_0$ 分别为单位。将 $d\overline Y_i=p_0f_i$ 除以 $p_0$，得到 $g\overline Y_i=f_i$。各 $Y_i$ 的像范数至多为一，故由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictquotientboundedup}，得到 contractive 系数映射 $B\to D$。等式 $a_j=g^{-N}p_j$ 在此映射下保持成立。将 $d\overline Z_j=gp_j$ 除以 $g$，再乘以 $g^{-N}$，便得到 $a_0\overline Z_j=a_j$。再次应用同一 proposition，得到 contractive 映射 $C'\to D$。}

\bi{The composites fix $A$, $X_i$, $S_j$, $Y_i$ and $Z_j$. They also fix $V$: the relation $dV=\gamma$ and the invertibility of $d$ uniquely determine this element. Polynomial density then makes the composites identities, proving the isometry. If $B=0$, the already constructed unital map $B\to D$ forces $D=0$, and $C'=0$ directly; the second universal-property step is then replaced by the unique map of zero algebras. All other zero-quotient cases are covered by the same calculations. Thus the proof never identifies an empty spectrum with a zero ring without an algebraic argument.}
{两个复合固定 $A$、$X_i$、$S_j$、$Y_i$ 及 $Z_j$。它们也固定 $V$，因为关系 $dV=\gamma$ 及 $d$ 的可逆性唯一确定该元素。再由多项式稠密性，复合均为恒等映射，从而得到等距同构。若 $B=0$，已经构造的含幺映射 $B\to D$ 迫使 $D=0$，而 $C'=0$ 也直接成立；此时第二次 universal-property 步骤改用零代数之间的唯一映射。其他零商情形由相同计算处理。因此，证明中没有未经代数论证便将空谱与零环识别。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Throughout, $K$ is a complete real-valued non-Archimedean field; trivial valuation is allowed. Nonzero affinoid algebras are strict and norm-admissible, with submultiplicative norm and unit of norm one. Quotients use their actual quotient norms. The zero algebra and its zero modules have the separate zero-norm interpretation of Section~\ref{fnd:r1quotientbridge}. A rational presentation has a unit-ideal tuple. We use unit radii in the lemmas; radii in $|K^\times|$ are reduced to this case by scaling variables.}
{全文固定完备实值非阿基米德域 $K$，允许平凡赋值。非零 affinoid 代数均为 strict 且 norm-admissible，范数次乘，单位元范数为一。商配备实际商范数。零代数及其零模按 Section~\ref{fnd:r1quotientbridge} 的约定单独使用零范数。Rational 表示的定义 tuple 必须生成单位理想。各引理使用单位半径；属于 $|K^\times|$ 的半径通过缩放变量化为这一情形。}
