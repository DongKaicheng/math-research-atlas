Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}\label{fnd:preparation}
\bi{Under these assumptions, $f=uW$ with $u$ a unit of $K\langle T\rangle$ and $W$ a monic polynomial of degree $d$ whose lower coefficients have size at most one. The quotient $K\langle T\rangle/(f)$ has $K$-basis $1,T,\ldots,T^{d-1}$.}
{在这些假设下，$f=uW$，其中 $u$ 为 $K\langle T\rangle$ 的 unit，而 $W$ 为 degree $d$ 的 monic polynomial，其低次 coefficients 的 size 不超过一。Quotient $K\langle T\rangle/(f)$ 以 $1,T,\ldots,T^{d-1}$ 为 $K$-basis。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Divide $T^d$ by $f$: $T^d=qf+s$. Since $Q(T^d)=1$, the proof above shows $\|q-1\|<1$. Thus $q$ is a unit by Lemma~\ref{fnd:geometricinverse}; take $u=q^{-1}$ and $W=T^d-s$. The norm estimate bounds the coefficients of $s$. Division shows that the listed monomials span the quotient, and uniqueness of the remainder proves independence.}
{将 $T^d$ 除以 $f$：$T^d=qf+s$。因为 $Q(T^d)=1$，上述证明给出 $\|q-1\|<1$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:geometricinverse}，$q$ 是 unit；取 $u=q^{-1}$、$W=T^d-s$。Norm estimate 控制 $s$ 的 coefficients。Division 说明所列 monomials 张成 quotient，而 remainder 的 uniqueness 证明它们 linearly independent。}
\end{proof}
