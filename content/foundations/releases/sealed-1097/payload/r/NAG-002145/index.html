An intrinsic blowup for a smooth cluster

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 724

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{An intrinsic blowup for a smooth cluster}
{光滑标记簇的内禀 blowup}]
\label{cand:smoothmarkcluster}
\bi{Let at least two distinct marked sections of a split
nodal model $\mathcal X$ specialize to the same smooth
point $q$. There is an intrinsic finite-type ideal supported
at $q$ whose blowup leaves all these sections smooth and
splits their specializations into at least two nonempty
classes. It has the same semilinear covariance, retention
of old vertices, and projectivity properties as
Lemma~\ref{cand:nodalmarkcluster}.}
{设分裂结点模型 $\mathcal X$ 上至少两个互异标记截面
专化到同一个光滑点 $q$。存在支撑于 $q$ 的内禀
有限型理想，其 blowup 使这些截面仍然光滑，
并将其专化分成至少两个非空类。
它具有 Lemma~\ref{cand:nodalmarkcluster}
中相同的半线性协变性、旧顶点保留性和射影性。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The point $q$ is rational, being the special point of a
section. Choose an algebraic smooth coordinate $T$ at $q$,
and write $a_P=T(P)$ for the finitely many sections in its
cluster. All $a_P$ have the same residue.
The tube coordinate is injective on its $K$-points by
Corollary~\ref{cand:smoothpointpolydisctube}, so these values
are pairwise distinct. Put}
{$q$ 是截面的特殊点，故为有理点。
在 $q$ 处选取代数光滑坐标 $T$，对簇内各截面
记 $a_P=T(P)$。所有 $a_P$ 具有相同的剩余值。
由 Corollary~\ref{cand:smoothpointpolydisctube}，
管域坐标在其 $K$-点上单射，所以这些值两两互异。令}
\[
 0<\delta_q=\max_{P,Q}|a_P-a_Q|<1,\qquad
 |\lambda|=\delta_q .
\]
\bi{Choose one section $S_0$ in the cluster, and define}
{从簇中选取一个截面 $S_0$，定义}
\[
                  \mathcal I_q=(\lambda)+\mathcal I_{S_0}.
\]
\bi{The section is contained in the smooth locus: both its
generic and special points are smooth, and the spectrum
of the rank-one valuation ring has just those two points.
Its image is an effective Cartier divisor. Indeed it is
finite flat of rank one over $O$, and
\cite[Tag 0B9D]{StacksAlg} applies in the relative smooth
curve locus; away from the image its ideal is the unit
ideal. Thus $\mathcal I_q$ is a finite-type quasi-coherent
ideal. It is the unit ideal away from $q$, since $\lambda$
is invertible on the generic fibre and $S_0$ has only the
one special point. Its completion is open.}
{此截面包含于光滑处：其泛点与特殊点都光滑，
而秩一赋值环的谱只有这两个点。
其像为有效 Cartier 除子。事实上，它在 $O$ 上
有限平坦且秩为一，故可在相对光滑曲线部分应用
\cite[Tag 0B9D]{StacksAlg}；在其像外，理想为单位理想。
因此 $\mathcal I_q$ 是有限型拟凝聚理想。
它在 $q$ 外为单位理想，因为 $\lambda$ 在泛纤维
可逆，而 $S_0$ 仅有这一个特殊点。其完备化为开理想。}

\bi{Near $q$, the ideal of every section $S_P$ in the
cluster is generated by $T-a_P$. To see this, choose a
Cartier generator $f_P$ of its ideal. The function
$T-a_P$ belongs to that ideal, so equals $f_Ph_P$.
On the smooth special fibre both $\overline f_P$ and
$\overline{T-a_P}$ are local parameters at $q$; the former
assertion follows also from the relative Cartier statement
in Tag 0B9D. Hence $h_P(q)\ne0$, and $h_P$ is a unit after
shrinking. Since $a_P-a_Q\in\lambda O$ for every pair,}
{在 $q$ 附近，簇内每个截面 $S_P$ 的理想都由
$T-a_P$ 生成。为此，取该理想的 Cartier 生成元 $f_P$。
函数 $T-a_P$ 属于该理想，故可写成 $f_Ph_P$。
在光滑特殊纤维上，$\overline f_P$ 与
$\overline{T-a_P}$ 都是 $q$ 处的局部参数；
前一断言也来自 Tag 0B9D 的相对 Cartier 结论。
故 $h_P(q)\ne0$，缩小邻域后 $h_P$ 可逆。
又因每对标记均有 $a_P-a_Q\in\lambda O$，所以}
\[
       (\lambda,T-a_P)=(\lambda,T-a_Q).
\]
\bi{These ideals are the unit ideal away from $q$.
Consequently the global centre is independent of $S_0$.
Changing smooth coordinates does not change $\delta_q$:
an open-unit-disc isomorphism has linear coefficient of
norm one and all higher coefficients of norm at most one,
so for $|a|,|a'|<1$ its difference divided by $a-a'$
is a unit of norm one. This is the coefficient calculation
in Theorem~\ref{cand:opendisccanonicalhomotopy}.
Thus the centre is also independent of the coordinate,
and is covariant under the indicated semilinear isomorphisms.}
{这些理想在 $q$ 外均为单位理想，
所以全局中心与 $S_0$ 无关。
更换光滑坐标不会改变 $\delta_q$：
开单位圆盘同构的一次项系数范数为一，
高次项系数范数至多一，因此对 $|a|,|a'|<1$，
函数值之差除以 $a-a'$ 后是范数为一的单位元。
这正是 Theorem~\ref{cand:opendisccanonicalhomotopy}
中的系数计算。故中心也与坐标无关，并在所述
半线性同构下协变。}

\bi{Apply Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothtubeinsertion} with centre
$a_{P_0}$ and scale $\lambda$, reusing its actual blowup
charts. Every marked section belongs to the smooth
$\lambda$-chart, with coordinate
$U=(T-a_{P_0})/\lambda$. At least two of these coordinates
have distinct residues, because one pairwise difference
has norm exactly $\delta_q$. All marked specializations
therefore remain smooth, and the old cluster separates
into at least two nonempty residue classes.}
{应用 Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothtubeinsertion}，
以 $a_{P_0}$ 为中心、$\lambda$ 为尺度，
复用其中已证明的实际 blowup 坐标片。
每个标记截面都位于光滑 $\lambda$-片，
坐标为 $U=(T-a_{P_0})/\lambda$。
至少两个坐标的剩余值不同，
因为某一对的差的范数恰为 $\delta_q$。
故全部标记专化仍光滑，
而原标记簇分成至少两个非空剩余类。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Throughout this section $K$ is a complete nontrivially
valued field, $O=K^\circ$, and $k=\widetilde K$.
A split nodal model is a separated flat finitely presented $O$-scheme
with smooth generic fibre and reduced nodal special fibre,
whose nodes are $k$-rational and admit the actual rational
pointed charts of Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting}.
Its formal generic fibre is assumed Hausdorff.
A marking is a finite set of distinct $K$-rational generic
points. In the proper case each extends uniquely to an
$O$-section by \cite[Tag 0BX5]{StacksAlg}.}
{本节中 $K$ 为完备非平凡赋值域，$O=K^\circ$、
$k=\widetilde K$。分裂结点模型指分离、平坦、有限表示
$O$-概形，其泛纤维光滑、特殊纤维约化且仅有结点，
并且结点均为 $k$-有理点，具有
Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting}
中的实际有理带点坐标片。假设其形式泛纤维为 Hausdorff。
标记指有限个互异的 $K$-有理泛纤维点。在 proper 情形，
由 \cite[Tag 0BX5]{StacksAlg}，每个标记唯一延拓为
$O$-截面。}
