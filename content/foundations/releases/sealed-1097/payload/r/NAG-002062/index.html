Cofinitely many whole tubes in a vertex neighborhood

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 679

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Cofinitely many whole tubes in a vertex neighborhood}
{顶点邻域包含余有限个完整管域}]
\label{cand:componentcofinitetubes}
\bi{Let $\mathcal X$ be a curve model, let $\eta$ be a component
generic point of $\mathcal X_s$, and let $v=v_\eta$. For every
ordinary open neighborhood $N$ of $v$ in
$X=\widehat{\mathcal X}_\eta$, there are only finitely many closed
points $q\in\overline{\{\eta\}}$ for which the whole tube
$D_q=\operatorname{sp}^{-1}(q)$ is not contained in $N$.}
{设 $\mathcal X$ 为曲线模型，$\eta$ 为 $\mathcal X_s$ 的一个
分量泛点，$v=v_\eta$。对 $X=\widehat{\mathcal X}_\eta$ 中
$v$ 的每个通常开邻域 $N$，只有有限个闭点
$q\in\overline{\{\eta\}}$ 的完整管域
$D_q=\operatorname{sp}^{-1}(q)$ 不包含在 $N$ 中。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Take a finite affine cover of $\mathcal X$. Every member meeting
$\overline{\{\eta\}}$ contains $\eta$, since an open set contains
the generalizations of each of its points. Thus it suffices to
prove the assertion inside a formal affine chart
$\mathfrak U=\operatorname{Spf}A_0$ containing $\eta$. Write
$A=A_0[1/\pi]$ and $R=A_0/\mathfrak m A_0$; let
$P\subset R$ be the minimal prime of $\eta$.}
{取 $\mathcal X$ 的有限仿射覆盖。每个与
$\overline{\{\eta\}}$ 相交的覆盖成员都包含 $\eta$，因为
开集包含其每一点的全部泛化。因此，只需在包含 $\eta$ 的
形式仿射图 $\mathfrak U=\operatorname{Spf}A_0$ 内证明。
写 $A=A_0[1/\pi]$、$R=A_0/\mathfrak m A_0$，并以
$P\subset R$ 表示 $\eta$ 对应的极小素理想。}

\bi{Choose $\bar g\in R\setminus P$ which belongs to every other
minimal prime of $R$. Such an element is obtained by multiplying,
for each of the finitely many other minimal primes $P'$, an
element of $P'\setminus P$; if there are no others, take $\bar g=1$.
The reduced ring $R_{\bar g}$ has the unique minimal prime
$P R_{\bar g}$, hence that prime is zero and $R_{\bar g}$ is an
integral domain. The complement of $D(\bar g)$ on the component
of $\eta$ is finite: a nonzero regular function on a finite-type
integral curve vanishes on a zero-dimensional Noetherian closed
subset, which has finitely many points.}
{选取 $\bar g\in R\setminus P$，使它属于 $R$ 的所有其余
极小素理想。具体地，对有限个其余极小素理想 $P'$ 各取一个
$P'\setminus P$ 中的元素并相乘；若无其余极小素理想，则取
$\bar g=1$。约化环 $R_{\bar g}$ 只有极小素理想
$P R_{\bar g}$，故该素理想为零，$R_{\bar g}$ 是整环。
$D(\bar g)$ 在 $\eta$ 的分量中的补集有限：有限型整曲线上
非零正则函数的零点集是零维 Noetherian 闭子集，因而只有
有限个点。}

\bi{Lift $\bar g$ to $g\in A_0$ and use the principal formal open
$B_0=A_0\{g^{-1}\}$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalprincipalopenflat}
identifies its generic fibre with $|g|=1$, and its special fibre
is $\operatorname{Spec}R_{\bar g}$. Put $B=B_0[1/\pi]$.
By Lemma~\ref{cand:reducedspecialcanonical}, the spectral norm on
$B$ is attained by an integral presentation and its nonzero
values belong to $|K^\times|$. By
Proposition~\ref{cand:reducedmodelcomponentpoints}, this affinoid
has the singleton Shilov boundary $\{v\}$. In particular, for
$f\in A$, its restriction $f_B$ satisfies}
{将 $\bar g$ 提升为 $g\in A_0$，并使用主形式开集
$B_0=A_0\{g^{-1}\}$。Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalprincipalopenflat}
将其泛纤维识别为 $|g|=1$，而其特殊纤维为
$\operatorname{Spec}R_{\bar g}$。令 $B=B_0[1/\pi]$。
由 Lemma~\ref{cand:reducedspecialcanonical}，$B$ 上的谱范数
由整系数表示达到，其非零值属于 $|K^\times|$。由
Proposition~\ref{cand:reducedmodelcomponentpoints}，此 affinoid
的 Shilov 边界为单点集 $\{v\}$。特别地，对 $f\in A$，其限制
$f_B$ 满足}
\[
                 \|f_B\|=|f(v)|.
\]
\bi{If this value is zero, $f_B=0$ and the value of $f$ is zero
throughout every tube over $D(\bar g)$. Otherwise choose
$c\in K^\times$ with $|c|=|f(v)|$. Then $b=f_B/c$ belongs to
$B_0$, and its reduction is a nonzero element of the integral
curve ring $R_{\bar g}$. Outside its finitely many zeros,
specialization gives $|b(x)|=1$ for every point of the entire
tube, and therefore}
{若此值为零，则 $f_B=0$，$f$ 在 $D(\bar g)$ 上方的每个
管域内恒取绝对值零。否则选取满足 $|c|=|f(v)|$ 的
$c\in K^\times$。此时 $b=f_B/c$ 属于 $B_0$，其约化是
整曲线环 $R_{\bar g}$ 的非零元素。在其有限个零点之外，
特化判据对整个管域中的每一点给出 $|b(x)|=1$，从而}
\[
          |f(x)|=|f(v)|\qquad(x\in D_q)
\]
\bi{for all but finitely many closed $q$ of this component in the
chart. Here the whole tube lies in the chosen principal formal
open by formal-open compatibility.}
{对该图内这一分量上除有限个以外的全部闭点 $q$ 成立。
这里形式开集相容性保证整个管域都属于所选主形式开集。}

\bi{The topology of $\mathcal M(A)$ is generated by the evaluation
functions $x\mapsto |f(x)|$. Choose a basic neighborhood of $v$
inside $N\cap\mathcal M(A)$, using finitely many such functions.
The preceding equality puts all but finitely many whole tubes
in this basic neighborhood. Finally take the union of the finite
exceptional sets over the chosen finite affine cover.}
{在 $\mathcal M(A)$ 上，拓扑由取值函数 $x\mapsto |f(x)|$
生成。在 $N\cap\mathcal M(A)$ 内选取由有限个这样的函数
定义的 $v$ 的基本邻域。上述等式把除有限个以外的完整管域
全部放入该基本邻域。最后对所选有限仿射覆盖取这些有限
例外集合的并集。}
\end{proof}
