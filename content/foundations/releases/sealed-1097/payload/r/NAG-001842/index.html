Restriction to a rational subdomain

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 365

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Restriction to a rational subdomain}{向 rational 子域的限制}]
\label{fnd:generalizeddomainrestriction}
\bi{Let $A\to B$ represent an affinoid domain $U$. If $A\to C$ represents an affinoid domain $V\subseteq U$, there is a unique bounded $A$-map $B\to C$, representing $V$ as a domain of $\mathcal M(B)$. These restriction maps compose, and compositions of domain embeddings are domain embeddings. If $V=R$ is rational over $A$, with algebra $A_R$, its defining tuple in $B$ presents a rational domain with the same algebra, and}
{设 $A\to B$ 表示 affinoid 域 $U$。若 $A\to C$ 表示 affinoid 域 $V\subseteq U$，则存在唯一有界 $A$-映射 $B\to C$，将 $V$ 表示为 $\mathcal M(B)$ 中的域。这些限制映射满足复合律，域嵌入的复合仍为域嵌入。若 $V=R$ 在 $A$ 上 rational，其代数为 $A_R$，则将其定义 tuple 映入 $B$ 得到的 rational 域具有同一个代数，并且}
\[
 B\widehat\otimes_A A_R\simeq A_R
\]
\bi{by canonical bounded inverse maps. These identifications commute with restriction to finite intersections of original-$A$ rational subsets contained in $U$.}
{其中同构及其逆典范且有界。这些识别与向包含于 $U$ 的原 $A$-rational 子集的有限交作限制相容。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Test the domain property of $A\to B$ on $C$. It supplies the unique map $B\to C$, whose spectral map is $V\subseteq U$. For a generalized affinoid $B$-test $E$ with image in $V$, the $A$-domain property of $C$ supplies $C\to E$. This is a $B$-map because its two maps from $B$ agree over $A$ and the uniqueness for $U$ applies to $E$. The same uniqueness proves composition of restrictions. Factoring a test map successively proves transitivity of domains.}
{将 $A\to B$ 的域泛性质应用于 $C$，便得到唯一映射 $B\to C$，其谱映射为包含 $V\subseteq U$。若 generalized affinoid $B$-测试 $E$ 的像包含于 $V$，则 $C$ 的 $A$-域泛性质提供 $C\to E$。它也是 $B$-映射，因为所得两个从 $B$ 出发的映射在 $A$ 上相同，且 $U$ 的唯一性适用于 $E$。同一个唯一性证明限制映射的复合律。逐次分解测试映射，则证明域的传递性。}

\bi{For $R$, write its actual rational algebra as $A_R=A\{\rho^{-1}T\}/(gT_i-f_i)$. The pulled-back tuple defines the actual quotient $D=B\{\rho^{-1}T\}/(\varphi(g)T_i-\varphi(f_i))$. It is affinoid, and its spectrum is the inverse image of $R$ in $\mathcal M(B)$, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints}. The two bounded rational universal properties identify $D$ with the completed pushout $B\widehat\otimes_A A_R$: the variables have the prescribed powers and images, and their finite polynomials are dense.}
{对 $R$，将其实际 rational 代数写作 $A_R=A\{\rho^{-1}T\}/(gT_i-f_i)$。拉回的 tuple 定义实际商 $D=B\{\rho^{-1}T\}/(\varphi(g)T_i-\varphi(f_i))$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints}，它为 affinoid，且其谱是 $R$ 在 $\mathcal M(B)$ 中的逆像。两个有界 rational 泛性质将 $D$ 与完备 pushout $B\widehat\otimes_A A_R$ 识别：变量的幂界及其像已经指定，而有限多项式稠密。}

\bi{The restriction $B\to A_R$ and the identity on $A_R$ induce $D\to A_R$, inverse to the canonical map $A_R\to D$. One composite fixes $A_R$. For the other, its two maps from $B$ agree over $A$ and the spectral image of $D$ is contained in $R\subseteq U$; hence domain uniqueness makes them equal. The pushout property gives equality on $D$. The same argument on an intersection, which is original-$A$ rational by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedjointpresentations}, proves compatibility. Every comparison is bounded in both directions.}
{限制映射 $B\to A_R$ 与 $A_R$ 的恒等映射诱导 $D\to A_R$，它与典范映射 $A_R\to D$ 互逆。一个复合固定 $A_R$；对于另一个复合，所得两个从 $B$ 出发的映射在 $A$ 上相同，而 $D$ 的谱像包含于 $R\subseteq U$，所以域泛性质中的唯一性使它们相等。再由 pushout 泛性质得到 $D$ 上的相等。根据 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedjointpresentations}，所述有限交仍在原 $A$ 上 rational；在该交上使用同一论证便得相容性。全部比较映射及其逆均有界。}
\end{proof}
