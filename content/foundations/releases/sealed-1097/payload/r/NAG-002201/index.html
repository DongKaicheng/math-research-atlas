Rational functions are piecewise integral affine

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 915

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Rational functions are piecewise integral affine}
{有理函数的分片整仿射性}]
\label{cand:rationalskeletalpiecewise}
\bi{For every $f\in K(X)^\times$, the function
$-\log|f|$ is finite and continuous on $\Sigma$, and is integral
affine on every edge of a finite subdivision.}
{对每个 $f\in K(X)^\times$，函数 $-\log|f|$ 在 $\Sigma$
上有限且连续，并在某个有限细分的每条边上整仿射。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{All skeletal points have generic algebraic support, so $f$
and $f^{-1}$ are analytic near $\Sigma$. At a vertex, the finitely
many incident graph branches are among the residual directions
of Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchstar}. Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope}
makes $-\log|f|$ integral affine on a sufficiently short initial
segment of every one of them.}
{所有骨架点的代数支撑均为泛点，因此 $f$ 及 $f^{-1}$ 在
$\Sigma$ 附近解析。在顶点处，有限条入射图分支属于
Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchstar} 中的剩余方向。
Theorem~\ref{cand:residualbranchslope} 使 $-\log|f|$ 在每条
分支上充分短的初始线段上整仿射。}

\bi{For an open nodal edge choose an algebraic affine neighborhood
of its residual node. Its formal generic fibre contains the entire
nodal tube. Express $f=P/Q$ in the fraction field of the generic
coordinate algebra. The functions $P,Q$ are analytic on that tube.
In its pointed annulus coordinate, their norms on every compact
subinterval of radii are finite maxima of Laurent monomial norms,
by Proposition~\ref{fnd:annulusunitaffine} and its first, nonunit,
assertion. The two nonzero functions have positive Gauss norm at
every radius. Subtracting their logarithmic profiles proves finite
piecewise integral affineness on each compact interior subinterval.
The already affine endpoint segments leave one such compact
subinterval. There are finitely many edges, so one finite common
subdivision works.}
{对一条开节点边，选择其剩余节点的代数仿射邻域。该邻域
的形式泛纤维包含整个节点管域。在其泛纤维坐标代数的分式
域中写 $f=P/Q$；$P,Q$ 在该管域上均解析。在带点圆环
坐标中，由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:annulusunitaffine} 的首个
关于一般非零函数的断言，它们在每个紧半径子区间上的范数
都是有限个 Laurent 单项式范数的最大值。两个非零函数在
每个半径处的 Gauss 范数均为正。将对数相减，便得每个紧
内部子区间上的有限分片整仿射性。去掉已知仿射的端点
线段后，只剩这样一个紧子区间。边数有限，故存在共同的
有限细分。}
\end{proof}
