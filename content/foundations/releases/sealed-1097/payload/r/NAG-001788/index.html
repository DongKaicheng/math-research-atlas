Analytification over an affinoid algebra

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 354

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Analytification over an affinoid algebra}{Affinoid 代数上的解析化}]
\label{fnd:relativeaffinoidalgebraanalytification}
\bi{Let $A$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra and let $R$ be a finite-type $A$-algebra. There is a good Hausdorff analytic space $Z^{\an}$ over $\mathcal M(A)$, where $Z=\operatorname{Spec}(R)$, constructed by the radius pieces}
{设 $A$ 为 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数，$R$ 为有限型 $A$-代数。令 $Z=\operatorname{Spec}(R)$。存在 $\mathcal M(A)$ 上的 good Hausdorff 解析空间 $Z^{\an}$，它由以下半径分片构造：}
\[
 R=A[T]/I,\qquad Z_r=\mathcal M(A\{r^{-1}T\}/I),
                    \qquad Z^{\an}=\bigcup_r Z_r.
\]
\bi{The construction is independent of the presentation, is compatible with finite morphisms, closed immersions, principal open subsets and complete scalar extensions, and glues on finite-type $A$-schemes. If $V=\mathcal M(C)$ is an affinoid domain in $Z^{\an}$, the ring map $R\to C$ is flat. The map from an algebraic local ring to the corresponding ordinary analytic local ring is faithfully flat.}
{此构造与表示无关，与有限态射、闭浸入、主开子集及完备扩标量相容，并可在有限型 $A$-概形上粘合。若 $V=\mathcal M(C)$ 为 $Z^{\an}$ 中的 affinoid 域，则环映射 $R\to C$ 平坦。代数局部环到相应通常解析局部环的映射忠实平坦。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The radius-piece construction and its gluing are those of Theorem~\ref{fnd:affineanalytificationconstruction}, with coefficients in $A$. More explicitly, the intersection of two pieces is the piece obtained by taking the coordinatewise minimum of their radii, and is a Weierstrass domain in both. Every piece is contained in the ordinary interior of a larger piece, by strict coordinate inequalities. Hence the union is good. Two distinct points are separated either by their image in $\mathcal M(A)$ or by the absolute value of a polynomial coordinate expression, so the union is Hausdorff. A morphism from an affinoid space over $\mathcal M(A)$, together with an $A$-algebra map $R$ to its analytic functions, has bounded images of the finitely many $T_i$. It therefore factors through a radius piece. The affinoid universal property gives the unique factorization. Localizing this assertion and gluing morphisms gives the same universal property for arbitrary analytic sources. It also proves presentation independence.}
{半径分片构造及其粘合与 Theorem~\ref{fnd:affineanalytificationconstruction} 相同，只是系数改取于 $A$。具体地，两个分片之交是各坐标半径取最小值所得的分片，并且为两者中的 Weierstrass 域。由严格坐标不等式，每个分片都包含于更大分片的普通内部，故所得并为 good。两个不同点或者由其在 $\mathcal M(A)$ 中的像，或者由某个多项式坐标表达式的绝对值分开，因此此并 Hausdorff。给定一个到 $\mathcal M(A)$ 的 affinoid 解析空间态射，以及一个从 $R$ 到其解析函数环的 $A$-代数映射，有限个 $T_i$ 的像均有界，故该映射分解经过某个半径分片。Affinoid 泛性质给出唯一分解。将此断言局部化并粘合态射，得到任意解析源上的相同泛性质，也证明与表示无关。}

\bi{For a finite $R$-algebra $S$, its inverse image over a radius piece with algebra $B$ is $\mathcal M(B\otimes_R S)$. Indeed this is a finite $B$-algebra with its canonical complete finite-module norm, and the bounded universal property identifies its maps with the maps to $S$ above that piece. Each of the finitely many integral generators satisfies a monic equation, so its value is bounded there; thus every algebraic valuation point above the piece is included. This proves the finite assertion, and quotient algebras give closed immersions. The principal open $D(g)$ is the union of the Laurent domains $|g|\ge\epsilon>0$, with the function $g^{-1}$ supplied by their localization algebras. This proves the open assertion and gives the cocycle identifications required for scheme gluing. Completed tensor associativity identifies every radius piece after $L/K$ with the radius piece for $R\otimes_A A_L$, proving scalar compatibility.}
{对有限 $R$-代数 $S$，代数为 $B$ 的半径分片的逆像为 $\mathcal M(B\otimes_R S)$。事实上，这是一个有限 $B$-代数，带有其典范完备有限模范数；有界泛性质将其态射识别为该分片上方到 $S$ 的态射。有限组整生成元各自满足首一方程，所以它们的值在此处有界，故该分片上方的每个代数赋值点都已包含。这证明有限态射断言，商代数则给出闭浸入。主开集 $D(g)$ 是各 Laurent 域 $|g|\ge\epsilon>0$ 之并，而这些局部化代数给出函数 $g^{-1}$。这证明开子集断言，并给出概形粘合所需的余循环识别。完成张量积的结合律把每个半径分片的 $L/K$ 扩标量识别为 $R\otimes_A A_L$ 的对应半径分片，从而证明标量相容性。}

\bi{For flatness, the finitely many coordinate functions are bounded on the compact affinoid domain $V$. Choosing larger radii places all of $V$ in a single radius piece $Z_r$, as an affinoid domain. Proposition~\ref{fnd:relativepolynomialtateflatness}, followed by the quotient by $I$, makes $R\to B=A\{r^{-1}T\}/I$ flat. Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness} makes $B\to C$ flat. Their composite is flat. Ordinary stalks are filtered colimits over affinoid neighborhoods; the finite-relation argument in Theorem~\ref{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact} makes their maps from $R$ flat. Localizing at the image prime gives a flat local map, hence a faithfully flat map.}
{对平坦性，有限个坐标函数在紧 affinoid 域 $V$ 上有界。选择更大的半径，就把整个 $V$ 放进一个半径分片 $Z_r$ 中，并使它成为其中的 affinoid 域。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:relativepolynomialtateflatness} 再对 $I$ 取商，$R\to B=A\{r^{-1}T\}/I$ 平坦。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness}，$B\to C$ 平坦，因此复合平坦。通常茎是各 affinoid 邻域上截面的滤过直极限；Theorem~\ref{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact} 中的有限关系论证说明从 $R$ 到这些茎的映射平坦。在像素理想处局部化后得到平坦局部映射，故忠实平坦。}
\end{proof}
