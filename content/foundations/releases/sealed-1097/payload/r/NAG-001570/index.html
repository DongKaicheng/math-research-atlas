Two points in a free-extension fibre

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 120

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{example}[\bi{Two points in a free-extension fibre}{自由扩张纤维中的两个点}]
\label{fnd:freeextensionnonsingletonfiber}
\bi{Let $K$ be trivially valued, let $r>1$, and put $A=K_r$, with parameter $S$. Form $B=A\widehat\otimes_K K_r$, using $T$ for the parameter in the second factor. The given absolute value is a point $x\in\mathcal M_K(A)$. Then the fibre of $\mathcal M_{K_r}(B)\to\mathcal M_K(A)$ over $x$ contains two distinct points, and $B$ is not a field.}
{设 $K$ 带平凡赋值，$r>1$，并令 $A=K_r$，其参数记为 $S$。构造 $B=A\widehat\otimes_K K_r$，第二个因子的参数记为 $T$。给定绝对值定义点 $x\in\mathcal M_K(A)$。则 $\mathcal M_{K_r}(B)\to\mathcal M_K(A)$ 在 $x$ 上的纤维至少有两个不同的点，且 $B$ 不是域。}
\end{example}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The coefficient isometry makes $F=T-S$ nonzero, and Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusgausssection} gives $\sigma_r(x)(F)=r$. Identify the two copies of $K_r$ by sending $T$ to $S$. The map $a\otimes b\mapsto ab(S)$ on the ordinary tensor product is contractive for every tensor representation, hence for the projective seminorm. It extends to a unital contractive homomorphism $\mu:B\to A$. The point $\delta=|\mu(-)|$ extends the second $K_r$-absolute value and restricts to $x$, but $\delta(F)=0$. The two points are distinct. A nonzero element in the kernel of a unital map to a nonzero field cannot be a unit, so $B$ is not a field. Here $r$ is free over $K$ but not over $A$, since $|S|=r$; no assertion about zero divisors is being made.}
{由系数等距描述，$F=T-S$ 非零，而 Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusgausssection} 给出 $\sigma_r(x)(F)=r$。通过 $T\mapsto S$ 识别两个 $K_r$。普通张量积上的映射 $a\otimes b\mapsto ab(S)$ 对每个张量表示均为 contractive，因此对 projective 半范数也是如此。它延拓为保单位的 contractive 同态 $\mu:B\to A$。点 $\delta=|\mu(-)|$ 延拓第二个 $K_r$ 的绝对值，并限制为 $x$，但 $\delta(F)=0$。所以这两个点不同。映入非零域的含幺同态之核中的非零元素不可能可逆，故 $B$ 不是域。这里 $r$ 在 $K$ 上自由，却不在 $A$ 上自由，因为 $|S|=r$；此处没有对零因子作出断言。}
\end{proof}
