Banach coefficients over an affinoid algebra

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 622

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Banach coefficients over an affinoid algebra}{Affinoid 代数上的 Banach 系数}]
\label{cand:groundfieldbanachtensorflat}
\bi{Let $C$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra and let $E$ be a Banach $K$-space. Put $T=E\widehat\otimes_K C$. For every finite canonical $C$-module $N$, there is a natural algebraic $C$-linear isomorphism}
{设 $C$ 为 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数，$E$ 为 Banach $K$-空间，并令 $T=E\widehat\otimes_K C$。对每个有限典范 $C$-模 $N$，存在自然的代数 $C$-线性同构}
\[
 T\otimes_C N\ \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\ E\widehat\otimes_K N,
 \qquad (e\otimes c)\otimes n\longmapsto e\otimes cn.
\]
\bi{The algebraic $C$-module $T$ is flat.}
{代数 $C$-模 $T$ 平坦。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The algebra $C$ is Noetherian by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}. Choose a finite presentation $C^a\xrightarrow{D}C^b\to N\to0$, and put $R=\operatorname{im}D$. The image $R$ is closed; the maps $C^a\to R$ and $C^b\to N$ are norm-strict for the induced and canonical norms, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}. Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonstrictexact} to their kernel sequences. The finite coordinate injections and projections identify $E\widehat\otimes_K C^j$ with $T^j$. We obtain an exact sequence}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedclosedsubmodules}，代数 $C$ 为 Noetherian。选择有限表示 $C^a\xrightarrow{D}C^b\to N\to0$，并令 $R=\operatorname{im}D$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}，$R$ 为闭像，且 $C^a\to R$、$C^b\to N$ 对诱导范数与典范范数均为 norm-strict。对其核序列应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonstrictexact}。有限个坐标嵌入与投影将 $E\widehat\otimes_K C^j$ 识别为 $T^j$，于是得到正合列}
\[
 T^a\xrightarrow{D}T^b\longrightarrow E\widehat\otimes_K N
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
\bi{By \cite[Tag 00DF]{StacksAlg}, ordinary tensoring gives the same cokernel presentation for $T\otimes_C N$. The resulting isomorphism is the displayed map: the bounded action on $N$ extends its pure-tensor formula in the first factor. This also proves naturality.}
{由 \cite[Tag 00DF]{StacksAlg}，普通张量积为 $T\otimes_C N$ 给出相同的余核表示。所得同构就是所列映射：$N$ 上的有界作用使其纯张量公式可在第一个因子上连续延拓，同时也给出自然性。}

\bi{For a finitely generated ideal $I\subset C$, its induced norm is a canonical finite-module norm. Under the comparison just proved, the map $I\otimes_C T\to T$ is $E\widehat\otimes_K I\to E\widehat\otimes_K C$. It is injective by Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonisometrictensor}. The finite-ideal criterion \cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg} proves flatness.}
{对有限生成理想 $I\subset C$，其诱导范数为典范有限模范数。在刚证明的比较下，映射 $I\otimes_C T\to T$ 就是 $E\widehat\otimes_K I\to E\widehat\otimes_K C$，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:grusonisometrictensor} 为单射。有限理想判别准则 \cite[Tag 00HD]{StacksAlg} 因而给出平坦性。}
\end{proof}
