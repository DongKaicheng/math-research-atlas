Compactness of the bounded spectrum

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 207

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Compactness of the bounded spectrum}{有界谱的紧性}]
\label{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact}
\bi{Let $K$ be a complete field with its fixed non-Archimedean absolute value, and let $A$ be a commutative unital Banach $K$-algebra with a submultiplicative homogeneous norm. Its bounded multiplicative spectrum $\mathcal M(A)$, with the evaluation topology, is compact and Hausdorff; it may be empty. Every bounded unital homomorphism $A\to B$ induces a continuous map $\mathcal M(B)\to\mathcal M(A)$. Any subset defined by finitely many inequalities $|f_i(x)|\leq r_i|g_i(x)|$, with $r_i>0$, is closed and therefore compact. No nonemptiness theorem is part of this statement.}
{设 $K$ 为带固定非阿基米德绝对值的完备域，$A$ 为具有次乘齐次范数的交换含幺 Banach $K$-代数。其有界乘性谱 $\mathcal M(A)$ 在取值拓扑下紧且 Hausdorff；它可以为空。每个有界保单位同态 $A\to B$ 都诱导连续映射 $\mathcal M(B)\to\mathcal M(A)$。由有限多个不等式 $|f_i(x)|\leq r_i|g_i(x)|$（$r_i>0$）定义的子集为闭集，因而紧。此陈述不包含任何非空性定理。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The power argument of Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive} applies verbatim to a bounded multiplicative seminorm on any such $A$: $|a|_x^n\leq C\|a\|^n$ and taking $n$th roots gives $|a|_x\leq\|a\|$. Hence evaluation embeds the spectrum in}
{Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive} 的取幂论证同样适用于上述任意 $A$ 上的有界乘性半范数：由 $|a|_x^n\leq C\|a\|^n$，取 $n$ 次方根得到 $|a|_x\leq\|a\|$。因此取值映射将谱嵌入}
\[
 P=\prod_{a\in A}[0,\|a\|].
\]
\bi{Each closed real interval is compact. For completeness, if an open cover of an interval had no finite subcover, repeatedly bisect and keep a half without a finite subcover. The nested intervals have lengths tending to zero and a common point, by completeness of the real numbers. An open set of the cover containing that point contains a sufficiently small retained interval, a contradiction. By the authorized Tychonov theorem \cite[Tag 08ZU, Theorem 5.14.4]{StacksAlg}, $P$ is compact. It is Hausdorff because two distinct points differ in a coordinate, where disjoint real neighbourhoods separate them.}
{每个闭实区间都紧。具体地，若一个区间的开覆盖没有有限子覆盖，反复二等分并保留没有有限子覆盖的一半。由实数完备性，所得长度趋于零的嵌套闭区间有公共点。覆盖中含该点的一个开集会包含某个充分小的保留区间，矛盾。由获准使用的 Tychonov 定理 \cite[Tag 08ZU, Theorem 5.14.4]{StacksAlg}，$P$ 紧。若 $P$ 中两点不同，则某个坐标不同；该坐标中的不交实邻域将二者分离，因此 $P$ 为 Hausdorff。}

\bi{The image of the spectrum is cut out by}
{谱的像由以下条件刻画：}
\[
 s_0=0,\quad s_1=1,\quad s_c=|c|\ (c\in K),\quad
 s_{ab}=s_as_b,\quad s_{a+b}\leq\max(s_a,s_b).
\]
\bi{All these conditions are closed, since real addition, multiplication and maximum are continuous. Conversely they give exactly a multiplicative ultrametric seminorm extending the fixed absolute value and bounded by the chosen norm. Thus $\mathcal M(A)$ is a closed subspace of $P$, with exactly its evaluation topology. A closed subspace of a compact space is compact: adjoin its open complement to an open cover and take a finite subcover of the ambient space. Hausdorffness is inherited. Composition with a bounded homomorphism preserves boundedness and turns each evaluation into another evaluation, proving continuity. Finally each stated inequality is the inverse image of a closed subset of $\mathbb R^2$ under the pair of evaluations; their finite intersection is closed.}
{这些条件都是闭条件，因为实数上的加法、乘法及最大值运算连续。反过来，它们恰好给出延拓固定绝对值且被所选范数控制的乘性超度量半范数。因此 $\mathcal M(A)$ 是 $P$ 的闭子空间，其子空间拓扑恰为取值拓扑。紧空间的闭子空间为紧：给定开覆盖，加上其开补集，便可从环境空间取出有限子覆盖。Hausdorff 性由子空间继承。与有界同态复合保持有界性，并将每个取值函数变成另一个取值函数，故诱导映射连续。最后，每个所述不等式都是一对取值函数所给映射下某个 $\mathbb R^2$ 中闭集的原像，有限相交仍为闭集。}
\end{proof}
