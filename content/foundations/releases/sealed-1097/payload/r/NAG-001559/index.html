Coefficient retractions and constant matrices

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 108

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Coefficient retractions and constant matrices}{系数收缩与常系数矩阵}]\label{fnd:freecoefficientretraction}
\bi{Let $A$ be a Banach $K$-algebra, $M$ a Banach $A$-module, and $r$ a finite tuple free over $K$. Write}
{设 $A$ 为 Banach $K$-代数，$M$ 为 Banach $A$-模，$r$ 为在 $K$ 上自由的有限元组。记}
\[
 A_r=A\widehat\otimes_KK_r,\qquad
 M_r=M\widehat\otimes_KK_r.
\]
\bi{The coefficient isometries of Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients} identify $A_r$ with a Banach $K_r$-algebra and $M_r$ with a Banach $A_r$-module, with convolution multiplication and action. The constant embeddings $\iota_A,\iota_M$ are isometric. Their zeroth-coefficient retractions $p_0^A,p_0^M$ are contractive and $A$-linear, and satisfy}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients} 的系数等距同构将 $A_r$ 识别为 Banach $K_r$-代数，将 $M_r$ 识别为 Banach $A_r$-模，其乘法与作用由卷积给出。常系数嵌入 $\iota_A,\iota_M$ 等距，零次系数收缩 $p_0^A,p_0^M$ 为 contractive 且 $A$-线性，并满足}
\[
 p_0^M\iota_M=\mathrm{id}_M,\qquad
 p_0^M\bigl(b\,\iota_M(m)\bigr)=p_0^A(b)m
 \quad(b\in A_r,\ m\in M).
\]
\bi{A bounded $A$-linear map acts coefficientwise after extension and becomes $A_r$-linear. In particular, a finite matrix with entries in $A$ acts coefficientwise on $A_r^m$, and commutes with the constant embeddings and zeroth-coefficient maps. None of the retractions is asserted to be $K_r$-linear or multiplicative.}
{有界 $A$-线性映射扩张后逐系数作用，并成为 $A_r$-线性映射。特别地，元素属于 $A$ 的有限矩阵在 $A_r^m$ 上逐系数作用，并与常系数嵌入及零次系数映射交换。这里不声称收缩为 $K_r$-线性或乘法映射。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For two finite tensor representations, submultiplicativity in $A$ and multiplicativity in $K_r$ bound the product by the product of their maximum tensor bounds. Taking both representation infima proves submultiplicativity for the projective seminorm. The zero-seminorm space is consequently an ideal, and multiplication extends to the separated completion. The same estimate with the module action constructs the completed action on $M_r$. These operations agree with Laurent multiplication and action on finite-support coefficient families. Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientconvolution}, with index group $\mathbf Z^s$ and weight $r^\nu$, and density of finite supports identify them with convolution on all families.}
{对两个有限张量表示，由 $A$ 中的次乘性及 $K_r$ 中的乘法范数，乘积的张量界不超过两个最大张量界之积。对两个表示分别取下确界，即得 projective 半范数的次乘性。因此零半范数空间为理想，乘法可延拓到分离完备化。对模作用使用同一估计，即构造出 $M_r$ 上的完备作用。这些操作在有限支撑系数族上与 Laurent 乘法及作用一致。对指标群 $\mathbf Z^s$、权重 $r^\nu$ 应用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientconvolution}，再利用有限支撑的稠密性，即在全部系数族上得到卷积识别。}

\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients} supplies the isometries, completeness, the homogeneous $K_r$-norms and the coefficientwise rule for bounded maps. Multiplication by a constant $a\in A$ multiplies each coefficient by $a$. Likewise, the zeroth coefficient of $b\iota_M(m)$ is $b_0m$. These observations give all the displayed retraction identities. For a bounded $A$-linear map, $A_r$-linearity is checked on finite Laurent families and passes to limits by boundedness. Rearranging coefficients in a finite power preserves its maximum norm, so the matrix assertions follow.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients} 给出等距同构、完备性、齐次的 $K_r$-范数及有界映射逐系数作用的规则。乘以常数 $a\in A$ 就是将每个系数乘以 $a$；同样，$b\iota_M(m)$ 的零次系数为 $b_0m$。这些事实给出全部收缩恒等式。对有界 $A$-线性映射，先在有限 Laurent 族上验证 $A_r$-线性，再由有界性传到极限。有限幂中的系数重排保持最大值范数，故得到矩阵断言。}
\end{proof}
