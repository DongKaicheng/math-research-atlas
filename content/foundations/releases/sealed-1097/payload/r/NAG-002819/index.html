Cofinal pointed weighted germs

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1965

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Cofinal pointed weighted germs]
\label{sgo:pointedoverlap}
Let $x\in\operatorname{Int}(X/K)$ and $w=t(x)$.
There is an ordinary affinoid neighbourhood $Y_0$ of $x$
such that the germs at $w$ of
\[
                 \mathcal T_{f|U}(M_U)
 \qquad(x\in\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(U,X),\ U\subset Y_0)
\]
are identical for all ordinary affinoid neighbourhoods $U$.
They define a germ $\mathcal C_{x,f}(M)$, independent of all
auxiliary admissible generic projections and their finite
Gauss neighbourhoods. It is additive in $M$.
If $r_f(x)<d$, this germ is zero. If $r_f(x)=d$, it is a
nonnegative integral weighted rational $d$-fan germ on the
pure pointed fan $w+C_x$ of
\Dep{v038:pointed:fullstabilization}; for $M=A$ its support
is the whole of that fan germ.

In the case $r_f(x)=d$, a projection $Q:\mathbb Z^n\to\mathbb Z^d$ is admissible
for this proof if it is injective on the span of every
$d$-cone of $C_x$. For each such $Q$ a finite ordinary Gauss
chart as in Lemma~\ref{sgo:gaussbranches} exists at $x$ for
$\psi=f^Q$. In each such chart $Y_Q$, for every ordinary affinoid
neighbourhood $U\subset Y_Q$ of $x$, there are a real
neighbourhood $B$ of $w$ and finitely many proper affine
hyperplanes $H\subset\mathbf R^d$ such that
\[
 t^{-1}(v)\cap Y_Q=t^{-1}(v)\cap U
 \quad(v\in B,\ Qv\notin H).
\]
Both full fibres are then interior in their respective
source domains. The inverse images of $H$ have dimension
at most $d-1$ on each $d$-cone.

If $C_x$ is the $d$-plane $N_{\mathbb R}$ for a saturated
rank-$d$ lattice $N\subset\mathbb Z^n$, the germ has constant
weight
\begin{equation}
 \mu_{x,f}(M)=\frac{\delta_{f^Q}(M,x)}{[\mathbb Z^d:Q(N)]}
                         \in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}
 \label{sgo:flatcalibration}
\end{equation}
for every integral $Q$ injective on $N$. No analogous single
quotient of the total projected degree is asserted at a
nonflat fan.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Write $r=r_f(x)$. The full pointed-fan theorem supplies
$W_0$ such that every ordinary neighbourhood of $x$ inside
$W_0$ has moment-image germ $w+C_x$, of pure dimension $r$.
If $r<d$, no such image has a $d$-dimensional cell near $w$,
and the support clause of \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:nag002405:global-finite-module-chain-weights]{finite-module chain support}} proves
that the chain germ is zero. This includes the case $n<d$.

Suppose $r=d$. Choose an integral $Q$ injective on the span
of every maximal cone: the failure for each of the finitely
many rational $d$-planes is a proper determinant-zero condition
on a $d\times n$ matrix; a rational matrix outside their finite
union exists, and clearing denominators gives the required
integer matrix. For every sufficiently small ordinary
neighbourhood of $x$, its $f$-image contains the pointed
$d$-cones, and their $Q$-images have dimension $d$.
Apply the full pointed-fan theorem again, now to the $d$ units
$f^Q$. It follows that $r_{f^Q}(x)=d$.
As in the last paragraph of Lemma~\ref{sgo:gaussbranches}, this
means $f^Q(x)=\eta_{Qw}$.

That lemma gives a finite ordinary affinoid chart
$\psi:Y_0\to N_0$, with central Gauss fibre $\{x\}$.
Choose it inside $W_0$ and inside the inverse image of a
sufficiently small real ball where the pointed upper bound
holds. Consequently
\begin{equation}
                         t(Y_0)\subset w+C_x.
 \label{sgo:globalconecontainment}
\end{equation}
These source restrictions are allowed by the arbitrarily
small neighbourhood clause of the local finite criterion.
They do not replace $N_0$ by the entire central polytorus.

Fix an ordinary affinoid neighbourhood $U\subset Y_0$ of $x$.
The compact set
\[
 D=Y_0\setminus\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(U,Y_0)
\]
does not meet the Gauss fibre. Its image is compact and closed
in the Hausdorff torus, so
\[
 O=\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(N_0,T^d)\setminus\psi(D)
\]
is an ordinary open neighbourhood of $\eta_{Qw}$.
Choose a compact full polyannulus with $Qw$ in the interior
of its weight box. Apply the frozen whole-torus overlap lemma
\Dep{mpw:torusoverlap} to $O$ intersected with this polyannulus.
There are a real neighbourhood $B'$ of $Qw$ and finitely many
proper affine hyperplanes $H$ such that
\begin{equation}
 \mathbb T_s\subset O,\qquad
 \psi^{-1}(\mathbb T_s)\cap Y_0
              \subset\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(U,Y_0)
       \quad(s\in B'\setminus H).
 \label{sgo:capture}
\end{equation}
This assertion retains every analytic point over every such
$\mathbb T_s$, including all of its Gauss lifts in $Y_0$.

For $v$ near $w$ with $Qv\in B'\setminus H$, equation
\eqref{sgo:capture} identifies the entire $f$-fibre in $Y_0$
with the entire $f$-fibre in $U$. These are identical analytic
domains, and the restricted modules are identical.
The target polytorus is contained in the ordinary interior
of $N_0$ in the boundaryless torus. Its inverse image is
therefore absolutely interior in $Y_0$, by finite relative
interior and composition. Its points lie in the ordinary
interior of $U$ in $Y_0$, so they are absolutely interior in
$U$ as well. The precise locality inputs are
\Dep{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras},
\Dep{fnd:affinoiddomainordinaryinterior}, and
\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus]{forward composition}} with \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:goodinteriorlocaltesting]{ordinary target testing}}.

The inverse image of every hyperplane in $H$ cuts each
$d$-cone of $C_x$ in dimension at most $d-1$, since $Q$ is
injective on its span. Make a common finite subdivision of
the two image germs, these cuts, and their faces. At generic
points of every $d$-cell, the local image lies in its affine
$d$-plane and the whole fibre is interior on both sides.
The exact-domain identification above and
\Dep{v037:degree:moduleweight} therefore give identical
weights, using the saturated tangent lattice of that cell.
This proves equality of the two $d$-chain germs. Finite
polyhedrality makes each germ a weighted rational fan after
shrinking at $w$; for the structure module, positivity in
\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:nag002405:global-finite-module-chain-weights]{finite-module chain positivity}} and full stabilization give
exactly the asserted support. Restriction $M\mapsto M_U$ is exact by \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:nag001832:ordinary-single-domain-flatness]{ordinary single-domain flatness}}. Additivity then follows from \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:nag002405:global-finite-module-chain-weights]{the finite-module chain}}.

For clarity, the comparison just proved first compares each
$U\subset Y_0$ with $Y_0$. Two such $U$'s can be compared
through a common ordinary affinoid neighbourhood of $x$ inside
their ordinary interiors. Charts constructed using a different
admissible projection can likewise be compared through a
common smaller ordinary affinoid neighbourhood, applying the
same argument in each chart. Thus both the cofinal germ and
the full-fibre comparison off the indicated cuts are independent
of the chosen projection. Different nearby Gauss lifts have
not been identified or removed; they occur in the same whole
fibre being compared.

Finally suppose $C_x=N_{\mathbb R}$. Use the free direct-image
shrinking in Lemma~\ref{sgo:gaussbranches}, retaining
\eqref{sgo:globalconecontainment}. For $s=Qv$ near $Qw$
outside the hyperplanes, the finite inverse image of
$\mathbb T_s$ in this chart equals the single original moment
fibre at $v$: $Q$ is injective on the entire plane containing
the moment image. Its actual Gauss module has dimension
$\delta_{f^Q}(M,x)$, because the direct image is free of that
rank. The interior flat-germ formula
\Dep{v037:degree:moduleweight} divides this number by exactly
$[\mathbb Z^d:Q(N)]$. It determines the weight on a dense
subset of every $d$-cell, and hence the constant weight germ.
Equation~\eqref{sgo:localdegree} keeps the actual local length
and full field degree, while \eqref{sgo:genericlengths} keeps
the generic component lengths.
\end{proof}
