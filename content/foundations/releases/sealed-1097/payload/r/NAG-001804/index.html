The ordinary germ at a free point of a trivial-field line

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 469

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The ordinary germ at a free point of a trivial-field line}{平凡赋值直线上自由点的普通芽}]
\label{fnd:triviallinegaussgermfield}
\bi{Let $F$ be trivially valued and let $0<r<1$. At the radius-$r$ Gauss point $\xi_r$ of $\mathbb A_F^{1,\mathrm{an}}$, evaluation identifies the ordinary local ring with the underlying field of $F_r=F((T))$.}
{设 $F$ 平凡赋值，$0<r<1$。在 $\mathbb A_F^{1,\mathrm{an}}$ 的半径 $r$ Gauss 点 $\xi_r$ 处，取值将普通局部环识别为 $F_r=F((T))$ 的底层域。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Lemma~\ref{fnd:freegaussproductneighborhood}, with base $\mathcal M(F)$, shows that annuli $a\leq|T|\leq b$ with $0<a<r<b<1$ form an ordinary affinoid neighborhood basis. Their analytic functions have unique Laurent expansions $\sum_{j\in\mathbf Z}c_jT^j$, with $|c_j|b^j\to0$ as $j\to+\infty$ and $|c_j|a^j\to0$ as $j\to-\infty$. This is the Laurent coefficient normal form of Theorem~\ref{fnd:laurentcoefficientnormalform}, applied to the annulus algebra. Since every nonzero coefficient has absolute value one, the positive coefficients are arbitrary and only finitely many negative coefficients can be nonzero. Thus the underlying algebra is exactly $F((T))$. Restriction between these annuli preserves every coefficient and is the identity on that field. The filtered colimit giving the ordinary stalk is therefore $F((T))$ as well. Gauss evaluation gives its original radius-$r$ absolute value and is injective.}
{对底空间 $\mathcal M(F)$ 应用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:freegaussproductneighborhood}，可知满足 $0<a<r<b<1$ 的圆环 $a\leq|T|\leq b$ 构成普通 affinoid 邻域基。其解析函数有唯一 Laurent 展开 $\sum_{j\in\mathbf Z}c_jT^j$，满足当 $j\to+\infty$ 时 $|c_j|b^j\to0$，而当 $j\to-\infty$ 时 $|c_j|a^j\to0$；这就是 Theorem~\ref{fnd:laurentcoefficientnormalform} 对圆环代数给出的 Laurent 系数正规形。因每个非零系数的绝对值为一，正次数系数可以任意，而负次数系数只有有限个非零。因此底层代数恰为 $F((T))$。这些圆环之间的限制保持全部系数，所以在此域上为恒等映射。构成普通茎的滤过直极限因而仍是 $F((T))$。Gauss 取值给出它原来的半径 $r$ 绝对值，且为单射。}
\end{proof}
