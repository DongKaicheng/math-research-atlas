A redundant bounded rational constraint

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 196

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A redundant bounded rational constraint}{冗余的有界 rational 约束}]\label{fnd:rationalredundantconstraint}
\bi{Let $B=A\langle f/g\rangle$ and let $C$ be a strict relation quotient over $A$. Suppose $g$ is a unit in $C$ and the elements $b_j=f_jg^{-1}\in C$ satisfy $\|b_j\|\leq1$. Then the canonical map}
{设 $B=A\langle f/g\rangle$，且 $C$ 为 $A$ 上的 strict 关系商。假设 $g$ 在 $C$ 中为单位，且 $b_j=f_jg^{-1}\in C$ 满足 $\|b_j\|\leq1$。则典范映射}
\[
 C\longrightarrow C\{T_1,\ldots,T_s\}/(gT_j-f_j)_j
       \simeq C\widehat\otimes_A B
\]
\bi{is an isometry. More generally, if each $b_j$ is power bounded, with a fixed bound on $\|b_j^n\|$ for all $n\geq0$, the same map is a bounded isomorphism with bounded inverse. The isomorphisms are compatible with subsequent bounded maps and with the associated finite-module quotients.}
{为等距同构。更一般地，若每个 $b_j$ 为 power bounded，即全部 $n\geq0$ 对应的 $\|b_j^n\|$ 有统一上界，则同一映射为有界同构，且其逆也有界。这些同构与后续有界映射及相应有限模商相容。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The image of $g$ is a unit before adjoining the variables, so the actual ideal generated by $gT_j-f_j$ equals the ideal generated by $T_j-b_j$. Substitution at $b_j$ gives an inverse to the coefficient inclusion by Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictquotientboundedup}. In the first case both maps are contractive; in the second their bounds are finite. The two composites fix the coefficients and variables, hence are identities by polynomial density. The completed-tensor comparison is Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpresentationpushout}. The same maps on a fixed finite free presentation of a module descend to its relation quotient by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule}; they retain the stated norm bounds. The formulae show compatibility. The zero case is immediate.}
{在添入变量之前，$g$ 的像已经是单位，故由 $gT_j-f_j$ 生成的实际理想等于由 $T_j-b_j$ 生成的理想。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictquotientboundedup}，在 $b_j$ 处代入得到系数包含的逆映射。第一种情形下两映射都为 contractive；第二种情形下两者均有有限的 bound。两个复合固定系数及变量，所以由多项式稠密性均为恒等映射。Completed-tensor 比较来自 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpresentationpushout}。在模的一个固定有限自由表示上施加相同映射，再由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} 下降到关系商，即得到模的结论，并保留上述范数界。映射公式同时给出相容性。零商情形直接成立。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{We work over a complete non-Archimedean field $K$, allowing trivial valuation except in the small-scalar lemmas where nontriviality is stated. Every nonzero algebra has a strict norm-admissible Banach norm, and every relation quotient has its actual quotient norm. The zero algebra is admitted separately with its zero norm. All radii in this section are one; a radius in $|K^\times|$ can first be reduced to this convention by scaling the corresponding numerator. These conventions concern the displayed presentations, not a spectral norm.}
{本节在完备非阿基米德域 $K$ 上工作，允许赋值平凡；使用小标量的引理会明确要求赋值非平凡。每个非零代数配备 strict norm-admissible Banach 范数，每个关系商配备实际商范数。零代数另以零范数允许在内。本节所有半径均为一；值群 $|K^\times|$ 中的半径可先通过缩放相应分子化为这一约定。这些约定涉及所列表示，并不使用 spectral 范数。}

\bi{For a tuple $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$ generating the unit ideal of $A$, write}
{对生成 $A$ 的单位理想的 tuple $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$，记}
\[
 A\langle f/g\rangle
   =A\{T_1,\ldots,T_s\}/(gT_j-f_j)_{1\leq j\leq s}.
\]
\bi{For a unit-ideal tuple $f_0,\ldots,f_r$, its \emph{standard rational family} consists of the $r+1$ presentations}
{对生成单位理想的 tuple $f_0,\ldots,f_r$，其\emph{标准 rational 族}由以下 $r+1$ 个表示组成：}
\[
 R_i=A\{T_j:j\ne i\}/(f_iT_j-f_j)_{j\ne i},
 \qquad 0\leq i\leq r.
\]
\bi{We put $T_i=1$ when using a formula involving all indices. The joint presentation of a finite family means the quotient with disjoint variable blocks and all their relations. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpresentationpushout} identifies it with the separated completed tensor product. Its module attached to a finite $A$-module $M$ is its ordinary tensor with $M$, equipped with the quotient norm from a fixed finite free presentation of $M$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} gives its comparison with the completed tensor. These choices specify the arrows and norms before a geometric sheaf is available.}
{若某个公式涉及全部指标，约定 $T_i=1$。有限族的联合表示，是指添入互不相交的变量组并施加全部关系后所得的商。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpresentationpushout} 将它与 separated completed tensor product 识别。对有限 $A$-模 $M$，相应模是该商与 $M$ 的普通张量积，并配备来自 $M$ 的一个固定有限自由表示的商范数。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} 给出它与 completed tensor 的比较。因此，在尚未构造几何层之前，所有箭头及范数已有明确含义。}
