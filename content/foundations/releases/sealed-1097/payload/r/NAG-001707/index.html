Affine analytification and its mapping property

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 283

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Affine analytification and its mapping property}{仿射解析化及其映射性质}]
\label{fnd:affineanalytificationconstruction}
\bi{Let $R=K[T_1,\ldots,T_n]/I$ be a finite-type $K$-algebra. The set $X_R$ of multiplicative seminorms on $R$ extending the absolute value of $K$, with its evaluation topology, has a canonical good Hausdorff $K$-analytic structure. For every $r\in\mathbf R_{>0}^n$, its locus $|T_i|\le r_i$ is the affinoid space}
{设 $R=K[T_1,\ldots,T_n]/I$ 为有限型 $K$-代数。$R$ 上延拓 $K$ 绝对值的乘性半范数所成集合 $X_R$，配备取值拓扑后，具有典范的 good Hausdorff $K$-解析结构。对每个 $r\in\mathbf R_{>0}^n$，其中 $|T_i|\le r_i$ 的部分为 affinoid 空间}
\[
 X_{R,r}=\mathcal M\bigl(K\{r^{-1}T\}/I K\{r^{-1}T\}\bigr).
\]
\bi{These pieces form an exhaustion by affinoid neighbourhoods. The construction is independent of the presentation and represents the functor}
{这些分片构成由 affinoid 邻域组成的穷竭。此构造与表示无关，并表示函子}
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_{K\text{-an}}(Y,X_R)
       \simeq \operatorname{Hom}_{K\text{-alg}}
                   \bigl(R,\Gamma(Y_G,\mathcal O_Y)\bigr)
\]
\bi{for every $K$-analytic space $Y$.}
{其中 $Y$ 为任意 $K$-解析空间。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{If $x$ is a seminorm with the stated coordinate bounds, then every polynomial satisfies $|p(x)|\le\max_\nu |a_\nu|r^\nu$. Thus evaluation extends continuously to the weighted Tate algebra, uniquely by polynomial density, and kills the actual ideal $I K\{r^{-1}T\}$. Conversely, a point of the displayed affinoid quotient gives precisely such a seminorm on $R$. This identifies the sets. Its topology agrees with the topology induced by polynomial evaluations: for a convergent series $h$ and a polynomial truncation $p$, the inequality $||h(x)|-|p(x)||\le\|h-p\|$ holds uniformly on this piece. All series evaluations are therefore uniform limits of polynomial evaluations.}
{若 $x$ 为满足所列坐标界的半范数，则每个多项式满足 $|p(x)|\le\max_\nu |a_\nu|r^\nu$。因此取值连续地延拓至加权 Tate 代数；由多项式稠密性，此延拓唯一，并消去实际理想 $I K\{r^{-1}T\}$。反之，所列 affinoid 商的点恰好给出 $R$ 上这类半范数，从而识别两边的集合。其拓扑也等于多项式取值诱导的拓扑：对收敛级数 $h$ 及多项式截断 $p$，不等式 $||h(x)|-|p(x)||\le\|h-p\|$ 在此分片上一致成立。因此全部级数取值都是多项式取值的一致极限。}

\bi{If $r_i\le s_i$ for all $i$, adjoining variables $U_i$ of radii $r_i$ with relations $U_i-T_i=0$ identifies $X_{R,r}$ with a Weierstrass domain of $X_{R,s}$. Both bounded maps between the resulting presentations are given by these variables; they are inverse on the dense polynomials. Arbitrary intersections are the pieces with radii $\min(r_i,s_i)$. Every point has an open neighbourhood $|T_i|<r_i$ contained in one such piece, by increasing all radii past its coordinate values. Thus the pieces and their affinoid subdomains form an affinoid net. Their identifications satisfy the cocycle condition on the coordinates, giving the asserted analytic space by the atlas construction. Its evaluation topology is Hausdorff because distinct seminorms differ on an element of $R$, and the affinoid neighbourhoods show goodness. Scalar radii $r_i=m$, for positive integers $m$, already give an exhaustion.}
{若对全部 $i$ 有 $r_i\le s_i$，则添入半径为 $r_i$ 的变量 $U_i$ 及关系 $U_i-T_i=0$，将 $X_{R,r}$ 识别为 $X_{R,s}$ 的 Weierstrass 域。这两个表示之间的双向有界映射均由上述变量给出，并在稠密的多项式上互逆。任意两个分片的交就是半径为 $\min(r_i,s_i)$ 的分片。将所有半径增大到超过一个点的坐标值，得到包含该点且位于该分片中的开邻域 $|T_i|<r_i$。因此这些分片及其 affinoid 子域构成 affinoid 网。它们之间的识别在坐标上满足余循环条件，由图册构造得到所需解析空间。其取值拓扑为 Hausdorff，因为不同半范数必在 $R$ 的某个元素上不同；这些 affinoid 邻域又说明空间为 good。仅取正整数的共同半径 $r_i=m$，已经得到穷竭。}

\bi{First let $Y=\mathcal M(A)$. Any homomorphism $R\to A$ sends its finitely many generators to elements of finite norm. Choosing radii exceeding those norms gives a bounded map from the corresponding affinoid quotient to $A$, and therefore an analytic map to $X_R$. Increasing the radii does not change the map. Conversely, the compact image of any analytic map $\mathcal M(A)\to X_R$ lies in some $X_{R,m}$: the open sets $|T_i|<m$ form an increasing cover, so compactness supplies one $m$. The domain mapping property of Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing} factors the map through $X_{R,m}$. Its ring map restricts to a homomorphism $R\to A$. Polynomial density proves that these constructions are inverse, and proves uniqueness of an analytic map with the prescribed coordinate functions.}
{先设 $Y=\mathcal M(A)$。任一同态 $R\to A$ 将有限个生成元映为范数有限的元素。选择超过这些范数的半径，就得到相应 affinoid 商到 $A$ 的有界映射，从而得到到 $X_R$ 的解析映射。继续增大半径不会改变映射。反之，任一解析映射 $\mathcal M(A)\to X_R$ 的紧像包含于某个 $X_{R,m}$：开集 $|T_i|<m$ 构成递增覆盖，由紧性可选一个 $m$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing} 的域映射性质使该映射经 $X_{R,m}$ 分解。对应环映射限制后给出 $R\to A$。多项式稠密性证明上述构造互逆，也证明坐标函数给定时解析映射唯一。}

\bi{For general $Y$, restrict a homomorphism $R\to\Gamma(Y_G,\mathcal O_Y)$ to every affinoid chart. The preceding construction gives analytic maps on the charts. They agree on each affinoid subdomain of an overlap because their coordinate functions agree, and hence glue by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing}. Conversely, pullback of the coordinates along an analytic map gives global analytic functions. The affinoid calculation proves both compositions are identities. This mapping property determines $X_R$ up to a unique analytic isomorphism and therefore proves presentation independence.}
{对一般 $Y$，将同态 $R\to\Gamma(Y_G,\mathcal O_Y)$ 限制到每个 affinoid 图。前述构造在各图上给出解析映射。由于坐标函数相同，它们在每个重叠部分的 affinoid 子域上相同，故由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing} 粘合。反之，沿解析映射拉回坐标，得到整体解析函数。Affinoid 情形的计算证明两个复合均为恒等。此映射性质确定 $X_R$ 直至唯一解析同构，因而证明与表示无关。}
\end{proof}
