Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Strict finite rational \v Cech acyclicity}{Strict 有限 rational \v Cech 无环性}]\label{fnd:strictfiniterationalcech}
\bi{Let $K$ be complete, allowing the trivial absolute value. Let $A$ be a strict norm-admissible $K$-affinoid algebra and let $\mathcal U$ be a finite cover of $\mathcal M(A)$ by strict rational subsets with radii in $|K^\times|$. For every finite canonical $A$-module $M$, the augmented ordered \v Cech complex of the rational quotient modules is strict exact in all degrees. The all-tuples complex, with repeated indices, has the corresponding strict exactness. This statement concerns finite rational covers and finite modules; it asserts neither acyclicity for arbitrary affinoid covers nor exactness of completed base change on arbitrary Banach modules.}
{设 $K$ 完备，允许平凡绝对值。设 $A$ 为 strict norm-admissible $K$-affinoid 代数，$\mathcal U$ 是由半径属于 $|K^\times|$ 的 strict rational 子集组成的 $\mathcal M(A)$ 的有限覆盖。对每个有限 canonical $A$-模 $M$，相应 rational 商模的 ordered 增广 \v Cech 复形在全部次数 strict exact。包含重复指标的 all-tuples 复形具有相应的 strict exactness。本结论处理有限 rational 覆盖和有限模；它并未断言任意 affinoid 覆盖的无环性，也未断言任意 Banach 模的 completed base change 保持正合。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Scale the finitely many numerators to reduce the radii to one, using the isometric change of weighted variables. If $A=0$ every term is zero. Assume first that $A\ne0$ and $K$ is nontrivially valued. Proposition~\ref{fnd:finiterationalstandardrefinement} provides a standard rational refinement $\mathcal V$. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:standardrationalcech}, its augmented complex is strict exact. For every finite intersection of members of $\mathcal U$, its joint algebra $B_J$ is strict by Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation}. The pullback of $\mathcal V$ is the standard rational family generated by the images of the same unit-ideal tuple $(F_\lambda)$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:standardrationalcech}, now over $B_J$ and for the finite $B_J$-module $B_J\otimes_A M$, proves strict exactness on that intersection.}
{先缩放有限个分子，通过加权变量的等距变换将半径化为一。若 $A=0$，所有项均为零。先设 $A\ne0$ 且 $K$ 的赋值非平凡。Proposition~\ref{fnd:finiterationalstandardrefinement} 给出一个标准 rational 加细 $\mathcal V$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:standardrationalcech}，它的增广复形 strict exact。对 $\mathcal U$ 中成员的每个有限交叠，由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictrationalintersectionpresentation}，其联合代数 $B_J$ 为 strict。$\mathcal V$ 的拉回是由同一单位理想 tuple $(F_\lambda)$ 的像生成的标准 rational 族。现在对 $B_J$ 及有限 $B_J$-模 $B_J\otimes_A M$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:standardrationalcech}，得到该交叠上的 strict exactness。}

\bi{In the other direction, every nonempty intersection of members of $\mathcal V$ maps into one chosen member of $\mathcal U$, because one of its factors already does. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaldomainup} and Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalredundantconstraint} identify that restricted member with the identity, by bounded inverse maps. Inserting its index gives a bounded contraction of the restricted coarse complex. Thus both local inputs of Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictfinitecechcomparison} are verified, or equivalently all hypotheses of Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictacyclicrefinement}(1). It transfers strict exactness from $\mathcal V$ to $\mathcal U$ with the stated augmentation. This is the common-refinement argument in all degrees, not merely the degree-zero gluing implication.}
{反方向，$\mathcal V$ 中成员的每个非空交叠都映入 $\mathcal U$ 的某个选定成员，因为它的一个因子已经如此。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaldomainup} 及 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalredundantconstraint}，该限制成员通过有界互逆映射与恒等成员识别。插入其指标即得到限制后 coarse 复形的有界收缩。因此，Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictfinitecechcomparison} 所需的两个局部输入都已核对，等价地也核对了 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictacyclicrefinement}(1) 的全部假设。它将带所列增广的 strict exactness 从 $\mathcal V$ 传到 $\mathcal U$。这使用了所有次数的 common-refinement 论证，而不只是零次的粘合推断。}

\bi{It remains to treat trivial valuation. Choose a defining strict quotient norm on $A$. Then $K\langle X_1,\ldots,X_d\rangle=K[X_1,\ldots,X_d]$ with norm one on every nonzero polynomial: the finite-tail condition permits only finitely many nonzero coefficients. The quotient norm of every nonzero class in $A$, and the quotient norm of every nonzero vector in a finite canonical module, is also one. The original admissible norms are uniformly equivalent to these discrete norms. With these choices each rational algebra is the ordinary polynomial quotient}
{还须处理平凡赋值。为 $A$ 选择一个定义 strict 商范数。此时 $K\langle X_1,\ldots,X_d\rangle=K[X_1,\ldots,X_d]$，且每个非零多项式的范数均为一，因为有限尾条件只允许有限个非零系数。$A$ 中每个非零类的商范数、有限 canonical 模中每个非零向量的商范数，也都为一。原 admissible 范数与这些离散范数一致等价。在这些选择下，每个 rational 代数就是普通多项式商}
\[
 B_i=A[T_1,\ldots,T_{r_i}]/(g_iT_j-f^{(i)}_j)_j
       \simeq A[1/g_i],\qquad g_i=f^{(i)}_0.
\]
\bi{Indeed the unit-ideal identity gives an inverse of $g_i$ in the displayed quotient, and the inverse map sends $T_j$ to $f^{(i)}_j/g_i$. These maps are inverse algebraically and bounded for the discrete norms. Joint quotients and their modules are therefore $A[1/\prod_{i\in J}g_i]$ and $M[1/\prod_{i\in J}g_i]$, with the usual localization maps.}
{事实上，单位理想恒等式在所列商中给出 $g_i$ 的逆，而反向映射将 $T_j$ 送到 $f^{(i)}_j/g_i$。两个映射在代数意义下互逆，并且对离散范数有界。因此，联合商及其模分别为 $A[1/\prod_{i\in J}g_i]$ 与 $M[1/\prod_{i\in J}g_i]$，箭头为通常的局部化映射。}

\bi{For every maximal ideal $\mathfrak m\subset A$, Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} provides the bounded point from the finite residue field $A/\mathfrak m$. Its extending absolute value is trivial by Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}. Since this point belongs to some $U_i$, the unit-ideal identity for that row forces $g_i\notin\mathfrak m$. Thus $(g_1,\ldots,g_n)=A$. This argument uses these particular maximal-residue-field points; it does not assert that every point of $\mathcal M(A)$ is trivially valued.}
{对每个极大理想 $\mathfrak m\subset A$，Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion} 给出由有限剩余域 $A/\mathfrak m$ 产生的有界谱点。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}，其延拓绝对值为平凡绝对值。由于该谱点属于某个 $U_i$，对应行的单位理想恒等式迫使 $g_i\notin\mathfrak m$。因此 $(g_1,\ldots,g_n)=A$。这个论证只使用这些特定的极大剩余域谱点，并未声称 $\mathcal M(A)$ 的每个谱点都具有平凡赋值。}

\bi{Apply \cite[Tag 0G6K, Lemma~15.30.5(4)]{StacksAlg} to the ordinary extended alternating localization complex, with $f=1\in(g_1,\ldots,g_n)$. It is exactly our ordered augmented complex, with its degree numbering shifted by one, and is exact in every degree. For discrete norms every linear map is bounded, every image is closed, and the quotient norm of a nonzero image element is one. The complex is consequently norm-strict. Uniform equivalence transfers this conclusion to the original canonical norms. Finally the bounded comparison between ordered and all-tuples complexes at the end of the proof of Theorem~\ref{fnd:finitebinarytreecech} gives the all-tuples assertion in both valuation cases.}
{对普通 extended alternating 局部化复形，应用 \cite[Tag 0G6K, Lemma~15.30.5(4)]{StacksAlg}，取 $f=1\in(g_1,\ldots,g_n)$。它恰好是这里的 ordered 增广复形，只是次数编号相差一，所以在每个次数都正合。对离散范数，每个线性映射有界，每个像闭，且像中每个非零元素的商范数为一。因此该复形为 norm-strict。一致等价将此结论传到原 canonical 范数。最后，利用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:finitebinarytreecech} 证明末尾的 ordered 与 all-tuples 复形有界比较，得到两种赋值情形下的 all-tuples 断言。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX

\bi{We work over a complete non-Archimedean field $K$, allowing trivial valuation except in the small-scalar lemmas where nontriviality is stated. Every nonzero algebra has a strict norm-admissible Banach norm, and every relation quotient has its actual quotient norm. The zero algebra is admitted separately with its zero norm. All radii in this section are one; a radius in $|K^\times|$ can first be reduced to this convention by scaling the corresponding numerator. These conventions concern the displayed presentations, not a spectral norm.}
{本节在完备非阿基米德域 $K$ 上工作，允许赋值平凡；使用小标量的引理会明确要求赋值非平凡。每个非零代数配备 strict norm-admissible Banach 范数，每个关系商配备实际商范数。零代数另以零范数允许在内。本节所有半径均为一；值群 $|K^\times|$ 中的半径可先通过缩放相应分子化为这一约定。这些约定涉及所列表示，并不使用 spectral 范数。}

\bi{For a tuple $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$ generating the unit ideal of $A$, write}
{对生成 $A$ 的单位理想的 tuple $g,f_1,\ldots,f_s$，记}
\[
 A\langle f/g\rangle
   =A\{T_1,\ldots,T_s\}/(gT_j-f_j)_{1\leq j\leq s}.
\]
\bi{For a unit-ideal tuple $f_0,\ldots,f_r$, its \emph{standard rational family} consists of the $r+1$ presentations}
{对生成单位理想的 tuple $f_0,\ldots,f_r$，其\emph{标准 rational 族}由以下 $r+1$ 个表示组成：}
\[
 R_i=A\{T_j:j\ne i\}/(f_iT_j-f_j)_{j\ne i},
 \qquad 0\leq i\leq r.
\]
\bi{We put $T_i=1$ when using a formula involving all indices. The joint presentation of a finite family means the quotient with disjoint variable blocks and all their relations. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpresentationpushout} identifies it with the separated completed tensor product. Its module attached to a finite $A$-module $M$ is its ordinary tensor with $M$, equipped with the quotient norm from a fixed finite free presentation of $M$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} gives its comparison with the completed tensor. These choices specify the arrows and norms before a geometric sheaf is available.}
{若某个公式涉及全部指标，约定 $T_i=1$。有限族的联合表示，是指添入互不相交的变量组并施加全部关系后所得的商。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpresentationpushout} 将它与 separated completed tensor product 识别。对有限 $A$-模 $M$，相应模是该商与 $M$ 的普通张量积，并配备来自 $M$ 的一个固定有限自由表示的商范数。Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictquotientmodule} 给出它与 completed tensor 的比较。因此，在尚未构造几何层之前，所有箭头及范数已有明确含义。}
