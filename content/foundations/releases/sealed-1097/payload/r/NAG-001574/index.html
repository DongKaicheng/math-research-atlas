The spectral-radius formula in the affinoid category

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 122

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{The spectral-radius formula in the affinoid category}{Affinoid 范畴中的谱半径公式}]
\label{fnd:generalizedspectralradiusformula}
\bi{For every nonzero norm-admissible $K$-affinoid algebra $A$ and every $f\in A$, the limit exists and satisfies}
{对每个非零 norm-admissible $K$-affinoid 代数 $A$ 及每个 $f\in A$，以下极限存在，且}
\[
 \lim_{m\to\infty}\|f^m\|^{1/m}
     =\max_{x\in\mathcal M(A)}|f(x)|.
\]
\bi{This assertion is unchanged by a uniformly equivalent admissible norm. It does not yet assert equivalence of the spectral norm and the Banach norm on every reduced affinoid.}
{该结论在用一致等价的 admissible 范数替换原范数后保持不变。这里尚不断言每个约化 affinoid 上的谱范数都与 Banach 范数等价。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion} and Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact} show that the continuous function $|f|$ has a maximum $s_0\geq0$ on a nonempty compact space. Contractivity gives $s_0\leq\|f^m\|^{1/m}$ for every positive $m$. If $s_0>0$, Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointwisepowerbound} gives the reverse bound $\|f^m\|^{1/m}\leq C^{1/m}s_0$, proving convergence to $s_0$. If $s_0=0$, apply the same theorem with any $s>0$; the upper limit is at most every such $s$, hence zero. Multiplicative comparison constants between two equivalent norms disappear after taking $m$th roots.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion} 及 Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact}，连续函数 $|f|$ 在非空紧空间上具有最大值 $s_0\geq0$。Contractivity 给出对每个正整数 $m$ 都有 $s_0\leq\|f^m\|^{1/m}$。若 $s_0>0$，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointwisepowerbound}，有反向估计 $\|f^m\|^{1/m}\leq C^{1/m}s_0$，故极限为 $s_0$。若 $s_0=0$，对任意 $s>0$ 应用同一定理，得到上极限不超过每个这样的 $s$，因而为零。两个等价范数之间的乘法比较常数，在取 $m$ 次方根后趋于一。}
\end{proof}
