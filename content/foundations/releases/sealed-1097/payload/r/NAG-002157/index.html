The bounded displacement expansion

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{The bounded displacement expansion}
{有界位移展开}]
\label{cand:etaledisplacementexpansion}
\bi{Put $U=(S_1,\ldots,S_r,Z_1,\ldots,Z_q)$.
There are bounded $K$-linear maps $D_\nu:B\to B$,
$\nu\in\mathbb N^{r+q}$, with $D_0=\mathrm{id}$ and
$\|D_\nu b\|\leq\|b\|$, such that for every
$0<\delta<1$ the series}
{置 $U=(S_1,\ldots,S_r,Z_1,\ldots,Z_q)$。
存在有界 $K$-线性映射 $D_\nu:B\to B$，
$\nu\in\mathbb N^{r+q}$，满足 $D_0=\mathrm{id}$ 及
$\|D_\nu b\|\leq\|b\|$，使得对每个
$0<\delta<1$，级数}
\[
 \sigma_\delta(b)=\sum_\nu D_\nu(b)U^\nu
             \in B\{\delta^{-1}U\}
\]
\bi{defines a contractive homomorphism, extending the base
displacement}
{定义压缩同态，并延拓基底上的位移}
\[
 X_i\mapsto X_i(1+S_i)\ (i\geq1),\quad
 X_0\mapsto X_0\prod_i(1+S_i)^{-1},\quad
 T_j\mapsto T_j+Z_j.
\]
\bi{It is characterized by this base formula and
$\|\sigma_\delta(Y)-Y\|<1$. In fact}
{它由该基底公式及 $\|\sigma_\delta(Y)-Y\|<1$ 唯一
刻画。事实上有}
\[
 \|\sigma_\delta(b)-b\|\leq\delta\|b\|,
 \qquad
 D_\nu(bc)=\sum_{\alpha+\beta=\nu}D_\alpha(b)D_\beta(c).
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Work in $C_\delta=B\{\delta^{-1}U\}$. The base
formulas define a contractive homomorphism by geometric
inversion and bounded substitution. On each integral
coordinate the difference from its original image has norm
at most $\delta$. Telescoping monomials, passing to restricted
series, and taking quotient infima therefore give
$\|\sigma_\delta(a)-a\|\leq\delta\|a\|$ for $a\in A$.}
{在 $C_\delta=B\{\delta^{-1}U\}$ 中工作。由几何求逆
及有界代换，基底公式定义压缩同态。每个整坐标与其
原像之差的范数至多为 $\delta$。逐单项式作望远镜
分解，再传到受限级数并取商下确界，得到对 $a\in A$
有 $\|\sigma_\delta(a)-a\|\leq\delta\|a\|$。}

\bi{Apply the base displacement to the coefficients of $F$.
At the center $Y\in B^m$ the new error has norm at most
$\delta$, since $F(Y)=0$. Its Jacobian differs from $J(Y)$
by a matrix of norm at most $\delta$. As
$J(Y)^{-1}=Q\operatorname{adj}(J(Y))$ has norm at most one,
the new Jacobian also has inverse norm at most one.
Lemma~\ref{cand:banachcenterhensel} gives the unique root
$Y+h_\delta$, with $\|h_\delta\|\leq\delta$.
The determinant at that root has an inverse $Q_\delta$ of
norm at most one and $\|Q_\delta-Q\|\leq\delta$, again by
the geometric inverse formula. These images kill all
relations in \eqref{eq:candetalejacobianpresentation} and
give a contractive homomorphism $B\to C_\delta$.
The same telescoping and quotient-infimum argument gives
its displayed difference bound on every $b\in B$.}
{对 $F$ 的系数施加基底位移。由于 $F(Y)=0$，新方程组
在中心 $Y\in B^m$ 的误差范数至多为 $\delta$；其
Jacobian 与 $J(Y)$ 的差范数也至多为 $\delta$。
由于 $J(Y)^{-1}=Q\operatorname{adj}(J(Y))$ 的范数至多
为一，新 Jacobian 的逆范数也至多为一。由
Lemma~\ref{cand:banachcenterhensel}，得到唯一的根
$Y+h_\delta$，满足 $\|h_\delta\|\leq\delta$。
该根处行列式有范数至多为一的逆 $Q_\delta$，且几何
求逆公式还给出 $\|Q_\delta-Q\|\leq\delta$。
这些像消去 \eqref{eq:candetalejacobianpresentation} 的
全部关系，定义压缩同态 $B\to C_\delta$。同样的
望远镜分解及商下确界论证，对每个 $b\in B$ 给出
所列差值界。}

\bi{If $\delta'\leq\delta<1$, restriction from radius
$\delta$ to radius $\delta'$ carries this root to a root
at distance less than one from $Y$. Uniqueness identifies it
with the root constructed at radius $\delta'$. Thus all
coefficient expansions agree. Evaluation at $U=0$ is the
identity, by the same uniqueness. Write their coefficients
as $D_\nu(b)$. Contractivity at every radius gives}
{若 $\delta'\leq\delta<1$，从半径 $\delta$ 限制到
半径 $\delta'$ 将上述根送到一个与 $Y$ 距离小于一
的根；唯一性将其与半径 $\delta'$ 的构造识别。
因此各系数展开一致。同样由唯一性，在 $U=0$ 处取值
为恒等映射。以 $D_\nu(b)$ 记其系数。每个半径下的
压缩性给出}
\[
 \|D_\nu(b)\|\delta^{|\nu|}\leq\|b\|.
\]
\bi{Letting $\delta$ increase to one proves
$\|D_\nu(b)\|\leq\|b\|$. The homomorphism identity at any
positive radius, together with uniqueness of coefficients,
gives the convolution identity and $K$-linearity.}
{令 $\delta$ 增至一，得到
$\|D_\nu(b)\|\leq\|b\|$。任一正半径下的同态
恒等式及系数的唯一性，给出卷积恒等式和 $K$-线性。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Retain the standard algebra $A$ and its integral coordinates
from Lemma~\ref{cand:mixedgaussdisplacement}. Let
$F=(F_1,\ldots,F_m)$ be represented by restricted series in
$X_0,\ldots,X_r,T,Y_1,\ldots,Y_m$ with coefficients in
$K^\circ$. Write $J=\partial F/\partial Y$ and consider the
nonzero affinoid algebra}
{保留 Lemma~\ref{cand:mixedgaussdisplacement} 中的标准代数
$A$ 及其整坐标。设 $F=(F_1,\ldots,F_m)$ 由变量
$X_0,\ldots,X_r,T,Y_1,\ldots,Y_m$ 的受限级数表示，
系数属于 $K^\circ$。写 $J=\partial F/\partial Y$，并考虑
非零 affinoid 代数}
\[
 B=A\langle Y_1,\ldots,Y_m,Q\rangle/
                  (F_1,\ldots,F_m,Q\det J-1).
 \tag{J}\label{eq:candetalejacobianpresentation}
\]
\bi{We use its displayed quotient norm. In particular the
coordinate images and $J^{-1}=Q\operatorname{adj}(J)$ have
norm at most one. All the statements below keep this
presentation over the original valued field.}
{使用所列表示的商范数。特别地，坐标像及
$J^{-1}=Q\operatorname{adj}(J)$ 的范数至多为一。下述
全部陈述保留原赋值域上的这个表示。}
