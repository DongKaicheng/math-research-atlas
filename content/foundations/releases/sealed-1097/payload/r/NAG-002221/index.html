The boundary of a formal closed-point tube

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 745

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[The boundary of a formal closed-point tube]
\label{v035:formal:tubeboundary}
Let $\mathfrak X_s$ be a reduced pure one-dimensional scheme
and suppose $X=\mathfrak X_\eta$ is Hausdorff. For a closed point
$q\in\mathfrak X_s$, put $D_q=]q[$ and $C_q=\overline{D_q}$
in $X$. Then $D_q$ is open, $C_q$ is compact, and
\[
 C_q\setminus D_q\ \subseteq\
 \{v_\eta:q\in\overline{\{\eta\}},\ \eta
       \text{ a component generic point of }\mathfrak X_s\},
\]
where $v_\eta$ is the unique component point of
Proposition~\ref{cand:reducedmodelcomponentpoints}.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Choose an affine formal open $\mathfrak V$ containing $q$.
Its generic fibre $V$ is compact, contains the entire tube,
and is closed in Hausdorff $X$. Consequently $C_q$ is compact
and lies in $V$. On each affine formal chart containing $q$,
the ideal of $q$ in the special fibre has finitely many
generators. Integral lifts $f_1,\ldots,f_t$ express its tube
by $|f_j|<1$ for all $j$, by
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalstratumtubes}. The same proposition
and the affinoid net in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful} show that
these relatively open pieces define an open subset of $X$.

For $x\in C_q$, let $\mathfrak p$ be its specialization prime
in $\mathfrak V_s$, and let $\mathfrak n_q$ be the prime of
$q$. If $\mathfrak p\not\subseteq\mathfrak n_q$, choose
$\overline f\in\mathfrak p\setminus\mathfrak n_q$ and an
integral lift $f$. Then $|f(x)|<1$, while $|f(y)|=1$ for every
$y\in D_q$. The open condition $|f|<1$ in $V$ contradicts
$x\in\overline{D_q}$. Hence
$\mathfrak p\subseteq\mathfrak n_q$. The proper
generalizations of a closed point of a pure one-dimensional
Noetherian scheme are component generic points.
Proposition~\ref{cand:reducedmodelcomponentpoints} identifies
each of their analytic fibres with its single point $v_\eta$.
This gives the assertion.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
Let $K$ be complete and nontrivially valued, with its given absolute
value. Write $O=K^\circ$, $\mathfrak m=K^{\circ\circ}$ and
$k=O/\mathfrak m$, and choose $\pi\in O$ with $0<|\pi|<1$.
All formal schemes in this section are admissible and topologically
finitely presented over $O$. They carry the $\pi$-adic topology.
For a special-fibre point $q$, write $]q[$ for its entire inverse
image under specialization.
