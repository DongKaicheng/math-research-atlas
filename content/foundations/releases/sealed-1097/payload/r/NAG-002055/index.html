NAG-002055 · Accepted whole

Two components in a pointed nodal chart

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 671

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Two components in a pointed nodal chart}
{带点结点坐标中的两个分量}]
\label{cand:pointednodalbranches}
\bi{Let $U_k\to\operatorname{Spec}k[s,t]/(st)$ be an \textup{\'etale}
morphism and let $u\in U_k(k)$ map to the origin. In
$R=\mathcal O_{U_k,u}$, the ideals $(s)$ and $(t)$ are the two distinct
minimal prime ideals. They identify the two distinct irreducible
components of $U_k$ through $u$. Under any \textup{\'etale} map from
$U_k$ to a reduced curve, their generic points map to component
generic points; these two images may coincide.}
{设 $U_k\to\operatorname{Spec}k[s,t]/(st)$ 为 \textup{\'etale}
态射，且 $u\in U_k(k)$ 映到原点。在
$R=\mathcal O_{U_k,u}$ 中，理想 $(s)$、$(t)$ 恰为两个不同的
极小素理想，并识别 $U_k$ 中经过 $u$ 的两个不同不可约分量。
在从 $U_k$ 到约化曲线的任意 \textup{\'etale} 态射下，这两个
分量的泛点映到分量泛点，而这两个像允许相同。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The local map from the origin is flat, induces the identity on
$k$, and extends the maximal ideal to the maximal ideal. Thus
\cite[Tag 0AGX]{StacksAlg} identifies}
{从原点局部环出发的局部映射平坦，在 $k$ 上诱导恒等，并将
极大理想扩张为极大理想。因此 \cite[Tag 0AGX]{StacksAlg} 给出}
\[
 \widehat R\simeq k[[s,t]]/(st).
\]
\bi{The special-fibre ring $R$ is Noetherian. By
\cite[Tag 00MC]{StacksAlg}, completion is faithfully flat, so
$R/(s)$ and $R/(t)$ inject into $k[[t]]$ and $k[[s]]$, respectively.
Consequently $(s)$ and $(t)$ are prime. Their extensions to
$\widehat R$ are incomparable, so the original ideals are
incomparable. Their intersection is zero because it becomes zero
in the displayed completion and $R\to\widehat R$ is injective.
Since $st=0$, every prime contains $(s)$ or $(t)$. These are
therefore exactly the minimal primes. The prime correspondence for
localization identifies them with distinct global components through
$u$. Finally, an \textup{\'etale} map is flat. Going down for a flat
map, \cite[Tag 00HS]{StacksAlg}, implies that the contraction of a
minimal prime is minimal: any strictly smaller prime would lift
below the chosen minimal prime. This proves the last assertion.}
{特殊纤维局部环 $R$ 为 Noetherian 环。由
\cite[Tag 00MC]{StacksAlg}，完备化忠实平坦，故 $R/(s)$、
$R/(t)$ 分别注入 $k[[t]]$、$k[[s]]$，所以 $(s)$、$(t)$ 均为
素理想。它们在 $\widehat R$ 中的扩张互不包含，故原理想也
互不包含。其交在所示完备化中变成零，而 $R\to\widehat R$
单射，所以原交为零。由于 $st=0$，每个素理想都包含 $(s)$
或 $(t)$，因此它们恰为全部极小素理想。局部化的素理想对应
把它们识别为通过 $u$ 的两个不同全局分量。最后，
\textup{\'etale} 态射平坦。由平坦映射的 going-down 性质
\cite[Tag 00HS]{StacksAlg}，极小素理想的收缩仍极小：任何严格
更小的素理想都会提升到该极小素理想之下。由此得到最后断言。}
\end{proof}
