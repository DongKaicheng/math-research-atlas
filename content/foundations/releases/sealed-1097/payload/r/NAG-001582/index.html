Algebraic flatness of rational localization

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 266

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Algebraic flatness of rational localization}{Rational 局部化的代数平坦性}]
\label{fnd:generalizedrationalflatness}
\bi{Every arbitrary-radius rational map $A\to B$ above is flat as a homomorphism of ordinary rings. It carries a short exact sequence of finite canonical $A$-modules to a norm-strict exact sequence under ordinary or separated completed tensor product. A finite rational covering gives a faithfully flat ordinary ring map $A\to\prod_i A_i$. These statements do not assert completed-tensor exactness for arbitrary Banach modules.}
{上述每个任意半径的 rational 映射 $A\to B$，作为普通环同态均为平坦。对有限 canonical $A$-模的短正合列，普通或分离完备张量积将其送为 norm-strict 正合列。有限 rational 覆盖给出忠实平坦的普通环映射 $A\to\prod_i A_i$。这些陈述不断言任意 Banach 模的完备张量积保持正合。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose a single free extension strictifying $A$ and the radii of $B$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:radicalrationalpresentation} makes $A_r\to B_r$ boundedly isomorphic to a unit-radius rational map. Its algebraic flatness and finite-module exactness are already proved in Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalfiniteelementary}; no covering theorem is required. A short exact sequence of finite $A$-modules is norm-strict by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}. Its coefficient extension is exact by Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusstrictexactness}, and it consists of finite canonical $A_r$-modules by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedcoefficienttensor}. Tensoring this sequence with $B_r$ is exact.}
{选择同一个自由扩域，使 $A$ 及 $B$ 的半径同时严格化。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:radicalrationalpresentation}，$A_r\to B_r$ 通过有界同构与单位半径 rational 映射相同。其代数平坦性及有限模正合性已经在 Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalfiniteelementary} 中证明，不需要覆盖定理。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}，有限 $A$-模的短正合列是 norm-strict 的；其系数扩张由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusstrictexactness} 保持正合，并由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedcoefficienttensor} 成为有限 canonical $A_r$-模序列。再张量 $B_r$ 后，该序列仍正合。}

\bi{The resulting maps are norm-strict finite-$B_r$-module maps. Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedquotientbasechange} identifies this sequence with the completed free scalar extension of the original tensor sequence over $B$. That original sequence consists of Banach spaces by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor}. Reflection in Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusstrictexactness} therefore proves the finite-module assertion before extension. For every finitely generated ideal $I\subseteq A$, apply it to $0\to I\to A\to A/I\to0$. The map $I\otimes_A B\to B$ is injective. The finite-ideal criterion \cite[Tag 00HD, Lemma~10.39.5(4)]{StacksAlg} now proves ordinary flatness for all algebraic modules.}
{所得映射为 norm-strict 的有限 $B_r$-模映射。Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedquotientbasechange} 将该序列与原 $B$ 上张量序列的完备自由扩标量识别。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitetensor}，原序列的各项是 Banach 空间，因此 Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusstrictexactness} 的反射断言证明扩域之前的有限模结论。对每个有限生成理想 $I\subseteq A$，将其用于 $0\to I\to A\to A/I\to0$，得到 $I\otimes_A B\to B$ 单射。此时由有限理想判据 \cite[Tag 00HD, Lemma~10.39.5(4)]{StacksAlg}，得到对全部代数模的普通平坦性。}

\bi{Write \(B\) for the base of the given finite rational cover, \(B_j\) for its actual row algebras, and \(Q=\prod_j B_j\). The preceding single-row result makes each \(B_j\) flat over \(B\), so \(Q\) is flat by the finite direct-sum identity. If \(B=0\), the assertion is immediate.
For any maximal ideal \(\mathfrak m\) of \(B\), its actual nonzero
quotient is affinoid by Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients}. Nonemptiness in Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion}
gives a bounded point of this quotient, hence a point of the domain
that kills \(\mathfrak m\). Some row in the cover over \(B\)
contains that point. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints} the point lifts to its actual
row algebra, so \(Q/\mathfrak m Q\ne0\). For a nonzero \(B\)-module
choose a cyclic submodule \(B/J\) with \(J\) proper and choose
\(\mathfrak m\supseteq J\). The quotient \(Q/JQ\) surjects onto
\(Q/\mathfrak m Q\) and injects into the tensor of the given module
by flatness. This proves that \(Q\) is faithfully flat over \(B\).}
{以 \(B\) 表示所给有限 rational 覆盖的基环，\(B_j\) 表示各覆盖域的实际代数，令 \(Q=\prod_j B_j\)。上面已证明每个 \(B_j\) 在 \(B\) 上平坦，故有限直积 \(Q\) 在 \(B\) 上平坦。若 \(B=0\)，结论立即成立。
对非零 \(B\) 的任意极大理想 \(\mathfrak m\)，由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients}，实际非零商 \(B/\mathfrak m\) 是 affinoid 代数。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion}，该商有一个有界谱点，在 \(B\) 上取值时消去 \(\mathfrak m\)。取包含该点的一个覆盖域，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints} 将此点提升到其实际代数 \(B_j\)，从而 \(Q/\mathfrak m Q\ne0\)。
对任意非零 \(B\)-模，取一个形如 \(B/J\) 的非零循环子模，并取包含真理想 \(J\) 的极大理想 \(\mathfrak m\)。商 \(Q/JQ\) 满射到非零的 \(Q/\mathfrak m Q\)，且由平坦性嵌入给定模与 \(Q\) 的张量积。故 \(Q\) 在 \(B\) 上忠实平坦。}
\end{proof}
