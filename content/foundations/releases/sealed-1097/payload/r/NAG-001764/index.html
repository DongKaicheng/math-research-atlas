A uniform characteristic-zero regular open set

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 332

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{A uniform characteristic-zero regular open set}{特征零中的统一正则开集}]
\label{fnd:charzerouniformregularopen}
\bi{Let $K$ be any complete field of characteristic zero and let $A$ be an integral generalized $K$-affinoid algebra. There exists $0\ne a\in A$ such that, for every complete $L/K$, the ring $A_L[1/a]$ is regular and every ordinary local ring of $\mathcal M(A_L)$ at a point where $a$ is nonzero is regular.}
{设 $K$ 为任意特征零完备域，$A$ 为整 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数。则存在 $0\ne a\in A$，使对每个完备扩域 $L/K$，环 $A_L[1/a]$ 都正则，并且 $\mathcal M(A_L)$ 中每个 $a$ 非零的点处的通常局部环也都正则。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose a finite free polyradius $r$ such that $F=K_r$ is nontrivially valued and $A_r$ is strict, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification}. The algebra $A_r$ is integral. Indeed, Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity} supplies $x\in\mathcal M(A)$ with kernel zero. Its Gauss lift has value}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification}，选择有限自由多半径 $r$，使 $F=K_r$ 赋值非平凡且 $A_r$ 为 strict。代数 $A_r$ 是整环。事实上，Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity} 给出核为零的点 $x\in\mathcal M(A)$。其 Gauss 提升的取值为}
\[
 \left|\sum_\nu b_\nu T^\nu\right|_{\sigma_r(x)}
                         =\max_\nu |b_\nu(x)|r^\nu.
\]
\bi{This vanishes only when every coefficient is zero. The lift is a multiplicative seminorm by Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusgausssection}; its kernel-zero property makes $A_r$ a domain. Apply Proposition~\ref{fnd:separablenormalizationregularlocus} over $F$, and obtain $0\ne d\in A_r$ with its conclusions over every complete extension of $F$. Write $d=\sum_\nu a_\nu T^\nu$, and choose one nonzero coefficient $a=a_{\nu_0}\in A$.}
{此值为零当且仅当每个系数都为零。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusgausssection}，该提升是乘性半范数，故核为零使 $A_r$ 为整环。在 $F$ 上应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:separablenormalizationregularlocus}，得到 $0\ne d\in A_r$，并有该命题对 $F$ 的每个完备扩域的结论。写 $d=\sum_\nu a_\nu T^\nu$，选择一个非零系数 $a=a_{\nu_0}\in A$。}

\bi{Fix a complete extension $L/K$ and a point $y\in\mathcal M(A_L)$ with $|a(y)|>0$. Let $E$ be the completion of $\mathcal H(y)(T_1,\ldots,T_s)$ for the weighted Gauss absolute value of polyradius $r$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:gausscommonextension} gives compatible isometric embeddings $L\subset\mathcal H(y)\subset E$ and $F\subset E$. Evaluation $A_L\to\mathcal H(y)\subset E$ defines an $E$-rational lift $w$ in $\mathcal M(A_E)$ above $y$. Associativity identifies}
{固定完备扩域 $L/K$ 及点 $y\in\mathcal M(A_L)$，假设 $|a(y)|>0$。令 $E$ 为 $\mathcal H(y)(T_1,\ldots,T_s)$ 关于多半径 $r$ 的加权 Gauss 绝对值的完备化。Lemma~\ref{fnd:gausscommonextension} 给出相容的等距嵌入 $L\subset\mathcal H(y)\subset E$ 及 $F\subset E$。取值 $A_L\to\mathcal H(y)\subset E$ 定义 $\mathcal M(A_E)$ 中位于 $y$ 上方的 $E$-有理提升 $w$。由结合律，}
\[
                      A_E=A_r\widehat\otimes_F E .
\]
\bi{The image of $d$ at this lift has absolute value $\max_\nu|a_\nu(y)|r^\nu>0$. To justify the formula for the infinite expansion, its finite Laurent truncations converge in the Gauss field, since the weighted coefficient values tend to zero; the maximum formula passes to the limit. Thus $d$ is a unit in the ordinary local ring at $w$. The separable regular-locus proposition makes that local ring regular. The local map}
{在此提升处，$d$ 的像的绝对值为 $\max_\nu|a_\nu(y)|r^\nu>0$。对无限展开，公式成立是因为有限 Laurent 截断在 Gauss 域中收敛，其系数加权取值趋于零，所以最大值公式可传至极限。因此 $d$ 在 $w$ 处的通常局部环中为单位。可分正则开集命题使该局部环正则。局部映射}
\[
                \mathcal O_{\mathcal M(A_L),y}
                          \longrightarrow\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(A_E),w}
\]
\bi{is faithfully flat by Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat}. Regularity therefore descends by \cite[Tag 00OF]{StacksAlg}. This proves the assertion about all ordinary local rings over $D(a)$, uniformly in $L$.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:scalarstalkfaithfulflat} 忠实平坦，因此可用 \cite[Tag 00OF]{StacksAlg} 下降正则性。这就在所有 $L$ 上统一证明了 $D(a)$ 上全部通常局部环的正则性。}

\bi{For any prime $\mathfrak q$ of $A_L$ not containing $a$, Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity} gives a point $y$ with kernel $\mathfrak q$. It satisfies $|a(y)|>0$. The map $(A_L)_{\mathfrak q}\to\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(A_L),y}$ is a faithfully flat local map, by the filtered rational-neighborhood description of the stalk and domain flatness. The target is regular by the preceding paragraph, so the source is regular by Tag 00OF. These are all local rings of $A_L[1/a]$, proving its regularity.}
{对 $A_L$ 的任意不含 $a$ 的素理想 $\mathfrak q$，Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity} 给出核为 $\mathfrak q$ 的点 $y$，它满足 $|a(y)|>0$。由茎的 rational 邻域滤过描述及域平坦性，映射 $(A_L)_{\mathfrak q}\to\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(A_L),y}$ 为忠实平坦局部映射。上一段说明目标正则，故由 Tag 00OF，源也正则。这些正是 $A_L[1/a]$ 的全部局部环，因而该环正则。}
\end{proof}
