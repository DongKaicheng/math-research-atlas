\begin{theorem}[Simultaneous directional collars and full Gauss degrees]
\label{bdg:directionalcollars}
Let $x$ have $r_f(x)=d$, choose $Y_0,U_1,\ldots,U_a$ as in
Theorem~\ref{bdg:cofinalboundary}, and let $\sigma$ be a $d$-cone of
$C_{x,f}(A)$ in a common refinement with $C_{x,f}(M)$, with lattice
$N=N_\sigma$. Suppose
$Q_1,\ldots,Q_b:\mathbf Z^n\to\mathbf Z^d$ are integral and injective
on $N$. Refine $\sigma-w$ by a finite rational fan. In every resulting
full-dimensional open sector $\Omega\subset N_{\mathbf R}$ avoiding the exceptional walls,
and at every sufficiently small noncentral $v\in w+\Omega$, the
following assertions hold.

The fibres $F_v(Y_0)=F_v(U_i)$ are entire and interior, and their common
image germ is an affine $d$-plane. Let $D_v$ be their actual algebra,
$R_{j,v}$ the exact-radius polytorus algebra at $Q_jv$, and $H_{j,v}$
its Gauss field. Then $R_{j,v}\to D_v$ is norm-admissible finite. Set
\[
 E_{j,v}=D_v\widehat\otimes_{R_{j,v}}H_{j,v},\quad
 e_j=[\mathbf Z^d:Q_j(N)],\quad P_{j,v}=M_{Y_0}\otimes_{A_{Y_0}}E_{j,v}.
\]
The weight of $M$ in this sector is
\begin{align}
 m_\sigma(M)&=\frac{\dim_{H_{j,v}}P_{j,v}}{e_j}
 =\frac{1}{e_j}\sum_{z\in\mathcal M(E_{j,v})}
 \operatorname{length}_{E_{j,v,z}}P_{j,v,z}\,
             [\mathcal H(z):H_{j,v}]\label{bdg:eqdegree}\\
 &=\frac1{e_j}\sum_{\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Min}(A_{Y_0})}
 \operatorname{length}_{(A_{Y_0})_{\mathfrak p}}(M_{Y_0})_{\mathfrak p}
             \dim_{H_{j,v}}(E_{j,v}/\mathfrak pE_{j,v}).\nonumber
\end{align}
It is independent of $j$, $v$ in the sector, and the listed source
neighbourhoods. Full field degrees, including inseparable degrees, and
all Artinian and generic lengths occur in this formula.

There also exists a full-dimensional closed rational parallelotope $W$
in the affine plane of the sector, with $v\in\operatorname{relint}(W)$
and $W\subset w+\Omega$, such that the \emph{same actual domain}
\[
 Z=t^{-1}(W)\cap Y_0=t^{-1}(W)\cap U_i
\]
is finite, with canonical norms, over the toric affinoid
$\mathbb T_{Q_jW}$ under $f^{Q_j}$ for every $j$.
These are full inverse images under $t$ in $Y_0$ at all weights of $W$.
They need not be full inverse images under $Q_jt$ in $Y_0$: other original
weights may have the same projected weight.
Such collars approach $w$ through the retained sectors; no extension
of a collar or its factor idempotents to $w$ is asserted.
\end{theorem}
