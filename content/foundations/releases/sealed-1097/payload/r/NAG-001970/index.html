A valuative inclusion gives a germ inclusion

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 567

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A valuative inclusion gives a germ inclusion}
{由赋值包含得到芽包含}]
\label{fnd:gradedgermmonicinclusion}
\bi{Let $V$ be an affinoid subdomain germ of a good germ
$(X,x)$, and let
$W=X\{|g_1|\leq s_1,\ldots,|g_m|\leq s_m\}$,
where $s_j=|g_j(x)|>0$. If
$\mathscr R(V,x)\subseteq\mathscr R(W,x)$, then $V\subseteq W$
as subdomain germs.}
{设 $V$ 为 good 芽 $(X,x)$ 的 affinoid 子域芽，
$W=X\{|g_1|\leq s_1,\ldots,|g_m|\leq s_m\}$，其中
$s_j=|g_j(x)|>0$。若
$\mathscr R(V,x)\subseteq\mathscr R(W,x)$，则作为子域芽有
$V\subseteq W$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Write $V=\mathcal M(B)$ after choosing sufficiently small
representatives. By Lemmas~\ref{fnd:gradedvaluationintegrality}
and \ref{fnd:gradedweierstrassgerm}, each initial value
$\mu_j$ of $g_j(x)$ is integral over $R_{B,x}$.
Take a homogeneous monic equation and lift its coefficients
to $a_{ji}\in B$ with $\rho_B(a_{ji})\leq s_j^i$.
The lifted polynomial satisfies}
{选取充分小的代表后写成 $V=\mathcal M(B)$。
由 Lemmas~\ref{fnd:gradedvaluationintegrality} 和
\ref{fnd:gradedweierstrassgerm}，每个 $g_j(x)$ 的首项值
$\mu_j$ 在 $R_{B,x}$ 上整。取齐次首一关系，并把其系数提升
为 $a_{ji}\in B$，满足 $\rho_B(a_{ji})\leq s_j^i$。
提升的多项式满足}
\[
 \left|g_j^{d_j}+\sum_{i=1}^{d_j}a_{ji}g_j^{d_j-i}\right|(x)
                  <s_j^{d_j}.
\]
\bi{This strict inequality persists in an open neighbourhood
of $x$ in $V$. There $|g_j|>s_j$ is impossible: its leading
power would strictly dominate every other term, so the displayed
absolute value would equal $|g_j|^{d_j}>s_j^{d_j}$.
Intersect the finitely many neighbourhoods obtained this way.
It lies in $W$, which is exactly the required germ inclusion.}
{该严格不等式在 $V$ 中 $x$ 的一个开邻域上保持。
在那里不可能有 $|g_j|>s_j$，否则最高次项会严格占优，
使上式绝对值等于 $|g_j|^{d_j}>s_j^{d_j}$。
取这有限多个邻域之交，就得到包含于 $W$ 的 $V$ 中邻域，
这正是所需的芽包含。}
\end{proof}
