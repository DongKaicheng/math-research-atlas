\begin{lemma}[\bi{Coordinates on a Banach space of countable type}
{可数型 Banach 空间的坐标}]
\label{fnd:countablebanachcoordinates}
\bi{Let $V$ be a Banach $K$-space with a countable set whose linear
span is dense. For every $0<c<1$ there is a finite or countable
family $(e_i)$ such that every element has a unique convergent
expansion $\sum_i a_i e_i$ and}
{设 Banach $K$-空间 $V$ 具有一个线性包稠密的可数集合。
对每个 $0<c<1$，存在有限或可数族 $(e_i)$，使每个元素均有唯一的
收敛展开 $\sum_i a_i e_i$，且}
\[
 c\max_i |a_i|\|e_i\|
 \ \leq\ \left\|\sum_i a_i e_i\right\|
 \ \leq\ \max_i |a_i|\|e_i\|.
\]
\bi{In particular its coordinate functionals are bounded and separate
points.}
{特别地，坐标泛函有界且分离点。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\paragraph{Coordinates on a Banach space of countable type (NAG-001658).}
Enumerate a dense linear spanning set. Fix \(0<c<1\) and
choose \(0<c_j<1\) with product \(>c\).
At each new vector \(v_j\) outside the previous finite span
\(W\), let \(\delta=\operatorname{dist}(v_j,W)>0\).
This span is closed by finite-dimensional norm equivalence.
Choose \(w_j\in W\) with
\(\|v_j-w_j\|<\delta/c_j\) and set \(e_j=v_j-w_j\).
If \(\|w\|>|a|\|e_j\|\), then
\(\|w+ae_j\|=\|w\|\). Otherwise for \(a\ne0\) it is at
least \(|a|\delta\), hence at least
\(c_j\max(\|w\|,|a|\|e_j\|)\); the zero case is immediate.
Inductively the finite sums have lower bound \(c\) times
their weighted maximum and upper bound equal to that maximum.
The sum map on the complete weighted-null families therefore
has closed image containing the dense spanning set. It is an
isomorphism, and the bounds give uniqueness and bounded
coordinate maps. The zero space has the empty family.
