NAG-001923 · Accepted whole

The formal universal property

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 523

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{The formal universal property}
{形式泛性质}]
\label{fnd:formalblowupuniversal}
\bi{Let $\mathfrak X=\operatorname{Spf}(A_0)$ and let
$\mathfrak X'\to\mathfrak X$ be the completed blowup above.
Every morphism of adic formal $O$-schemes
$h:\mathfrak Y\to\mathfrak X$ for which $I\mathcal O_{\mathfrak Y}$
is an invertible ideal factors uniquely through $\mathfrak X'$.}
{令 $\mathfrak X=\operatorname{Spf}(A_0)$，并令
$\mathfrak X'\to\mathfrak X$ 为上面的完备 blowup。
对每个使 $I\mathcal O_{\mathfrak Y}$ 成为可逆理想的 adic
形式 $O$-概形态射 $h:\mathfrak Y\to\mathfrak X$，存在唯一的
经由 $\mathfrak X'$ 的分解。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Locally on $\mathfrak Y$, one of the $f_i$ generates the
invertible ideal: after choosing a local generator, the coefficients
of the finitely many $f_i$ generate the unit ideal, so one is a unit
near the given point. On such an affine formal open
$\operatorname{Spf}(B)$, the generator $f_i$ is a nonzerodivisor.
There are unique $u_j\in B$ with $f_iu_j=f_j$. The corresponding
polynomial map $C_i\to B$ kills its $f_i$-torsion, hence factors
through $D_i$. It extends uniquely to the adic completion $B_i$,
since $B$ is complete. The uniqueness of the fractions makes these
maps agree on overlaps. Conversely any factorization must map this
open into the chart where $f_i$ generates the pulled-back ideal and
must have the specified coordinates. This proves global uniqueness.}
{在 $\mathfrak Y$ 上局部地，某个 $f_i$ 生成该可逆理想：选定局部
生成元后，有限个 $f_i$ 的系数生成单位理想，所以在给定点附近至少
有一个系数可逆。在这样的仿射形式开集 $\operatorname{Spf}(B)$ 上，
生成元 $f_i$ 为非零因子。因此存在唯一的 $u_j\in B$，满足
$f_iu_j=f_j$。对应多项式映射 $C_i\to B$ 消去其 $f_i$-挠，
故经由 $D_i$ 分解。由于 $B$ 完备，它唯一延拓到 adic 完备化
$B_i$。分式的唯一性保证这些映射在交上相容。反之，任意分解在
此开集上都必须映入由 $f_i$ 生成拉回理想的图册，并具有所指定的
坐标。这就证明整体唯一性。}
\end{proof}
