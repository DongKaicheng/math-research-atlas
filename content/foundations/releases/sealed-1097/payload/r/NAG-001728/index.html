Relative projective cohomology comparison

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 299

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Relative projective cohomology comparison}
{相对射影上同调比较}]
\label{fnd:relativeprojectivecomparison}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a projective morphism of finite-type
$K$-schemes, and let $\mathcal E$ be coherent on $Y$.
There is a natural quasi-isomorphism}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 是有限型 $K$-概形之间的射影态射，
$\mathcal E$ 为 $Y$ 上的凝聚层，则有自然拟同构}
\[
 \alpha_X^* Rf_*\mathcal E
       \ \simeq\ Rf^{\an}_*(\mathcal E^{\an}).
\]
\bi{In particular
$(R^qf_*\mathcal E)^{\an}\simeq R^qf^{\an}_*\mathcal E^{\an}$
for every $q$. If $X=\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ and
$V=\mathcal M(A)\subset X^{\an}$ is an affinoid domain, the
corresponding comparison is}
{特别地，对所有 $q$ 有
$(R^qf_*\mathcal E)^{\an}\simeq R^qf^{\an}_*\mathcal E^{\an}$。
若 $X=\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ 且
$V=\mathcal M(A)\subset X^{\an}$ 为 affinoid 域，则相应比较为}
\[
 A\otimes_R H^q(Y,\mathcal E)
       \ \simeq\ H^q(f^{\an,-1}(V)_G,\mathcal E^{\an}).
\]
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{\textbf{Projective space.} First take $Y=\mathbf P^n_R$.
For $\mathcal O(m)$ the standard unit-affinoid covering above
$V$ has coordinate algebras consisting of convergent Laurent
series with coefficients in $A$. The proof of
Lemma~\ref{fnd:unitlaurentnormalform} works coefficient by
coefficient over the complete Banach algebra $A$: its reduction,
isometric section, and convergent ideal multipliers have norm
at most one. The exponent sets and the simplex insertion
operators in Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticprojectivetwists} are
unchanged. They yield a bounded contraction onto the finite free
$A$-modules indexed by the same surviving monomials.
The algebraic computation \cite[Tag 01XT]{StacksAlg} over $R$
has the same representatives. Hence the displayed comparison
holds for every twist, and respects all restriction maps in $V$.}
{\textbf{射影空间。} 先取 $Y=\mathbf P^n_R$。
对 $\mathcal O(m)$，$V$ 上方的标准单位 affinoid 覆盖的
坐标代数由系数属于 $A$ 的收敛 Laurent 级数组成。
Lemma~\ref{fnd:unitlaurentnormalform} 的证明可逐系数用于
完备 Banach 代数 $A$：约化、等距截面及收敛的理想倍数的
范数均至多为一。Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticprojectivetwists}
中的指数集和单纯形插入算子不变，给出到由同一组存留单项式
标记的有限自由 $A$-模的有界收缩。
\cite[Tag 01XT]{StacksAlg} 在 $R$ 上的代数计算具有相同代表元，
因此所列比较对每个扭转层成立，且保持 $V$ 的全部限制映射。}

\bi{For an arbitrary coherent $\mathcal E$, choose, by
\cite[Tag 01YS(1)--(2)]{StacksAlg}, an exact sequence
$0\to\mathcal R\to\mathcal L\to\mathcal E\to0$, where
$\mathcal L$ is a finite sum of twists and $\mathcal R$ is coherent.
Both the algebraic standard affine cover and the analytic unit
cover have length $n$, so all cohomology above $n$ vanishes,
using \cite[Tag 01XB]{StacksAlg} and
Proposition~\ref{fnd:finiteaffinoidcohomology}.
Tensor the algebraic long exact sequence with $A$; it stays exact
by Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationgsiteflat}.
Analytification and restriction to $f^{\an,-1}(V)$ also preserve
the short exact sequence. Descend on $q$, simultaneously for
all coherent sheaves. The exact diagram first lifts an analytic
$q$-class of $\mathcal E$ by lifting its connecting
$(q+1)$-class of $\mathcal R$ and correcting by a class of
$\mathcal L$; this proves surjectivity for all $\mathcal E$.
Apply this surjectivity to $\mathcal R$ to lift the correction
of a class of $\mathcal L$ mapping to zero analytically.
Injectivity for $\mathcal L$ and the $(q+1)$-term then proves
injectivity for $\mathcal E$. This is the exact diagram chase
of Theorem~\ref{fnd:projectivecoherentcomparison}, now with
the flat coefficient extension $R\to A$.}
{对任意凝聚层 $\mathcal E$，由
\cite[Tag 01YS(1)--(2)]{StacksAlg} 选择正合列
$0\to\mathcal R\to\mathcal L\to\mathcal E\to0$，
其中 $\mathcal L$ 是扭转层的有限直和，$\mathcal R$ 凝聚。
代数标准仿射覆盖及解析单位覆盖的长度均为 $n$，
所以由 \cite[Tag 01XB]{StacksAlg} 和
Proposition~\ref{fnd:finiteaffinoidcohomology}，
$n$ 次以上的上同调均为零。将代数长正合列与 $A$ 张量；
由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationgsiteflat}，它仍正合。
解析化及向 $f^{\an,-1}(V)$ 的限制也保持短正合列。
对所有凝聚层同时按 $q$ 递降归纳：先提升
$\mathcal E$ 的解析 $q$ 次类所对应的
$\mathcal R$ 的连接 $(q+1)$ 次类，再用 $\mathcal L$
的类修正，得到对所有 $\mathcal E$ 的满射性。
将此满射性用于 $\mathcal R$，便能提升在解析一侧消去
$\mathcal L$ 中原像所需的修正；$\mathcal L$ 及
$(q+1)$ 次项上的单射性随后给出 $\mathcal E$ 的单射性。
这正是 Theorem~\ref{fnd:projectivecoherentcomparison}
的图追踪，只是增加了平坦的系数扩张 $R\to A$。}

\bi{\textbf{Closed projective subspaces and direct images.}
Factor a general projective $f$ as a closed immersion into
$\mathbf P^n_X$ followed by projection. Closed direct image
is exact and coherent on both sides, by finite quotient modules
and Lemma~\ref{fnd:closedanalyticcoherentextension}; it commutes
with analytification on each chart. The same affinoid and affine
\v Cech complexes compute cohomology before and after this closed
direct image. Thus the preceding argument applies to $i_*\mathcal E$.
The algebraic modules $H^q(Y,\mathcal E)$ are finite over $R$
by \cite[Tag 02O5]{StacksAlg}; their localizations are the
sections of $R^qf_*\mathcal E$, as also follows by localizing
the finite affine \v Cech complex. On the analytic side,
\cite[Tag 072W]{StacksAlg} identifies the higher direct image
as the sheafification of the cohomology on inverse images of
objects. The displayed isomorphisms, compatible with affinoid
restriction, therefore give the asserted isomorphisms of
cohomology sheaves.}
{\textbf{闭射影子空间与直接像。}
一般射影 $f$ 分解为到 $\mathbf P^n_X$ 的闭浸入及投影。
由有限商模及 Lemma~\ref{fnd:closedanalyticcoherentextension}，
两边的闭直接像都正合、保持凝聚性，并逐片与解析化相容。
闭直接像前后的仿射及 affinoid \v Cech 复形相同，
所以前述论证适用于 $i_*\mathcal E$。
由 \cite[Tag 02O5]{StacksAlg}，代数模
$H^q(Y,\mathcal E)$ 在 $R$ 上有限；其局部化为
$R^qf_*\mathcal E$ 的截面，也可直接将有限仿射
\v Cech 复形局部化得到。
解析一侧，\cite[Tag 072W]{StacksAlg} 将高次直接像识别为
各对象逆像的上同调所组成预层的层化。因此，与 affinoid
限制相容的所列同构给出所需上同调层同构。}

\bi{Finally the commutative analytification square of ringed
topoi has the canonical derived comparison map of
\cite[Tag 07A7]{StacksAlg}. Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationgsiteflat}
makes its derived pullbacks ordinary pullbacks.
The cohomology maps just calculated are those of this map:
on \v Cech resolutions they send a regular section to its
analytic section, which is the adjunction map used in its
construction. They are isomorphisms in every degree on an
affinoid basis, so the comparison is a quasi-isomorphism.
This also proves its compatibility with the adjunction
$\mathcal F\to Rf_*f^*\mathcal F$ and with restriction.}
{最后，赋环拓扑斯的交换解析化方块具有
\cite[Tag 07A7]{StacksAlg} 的典范导出比较映射。
由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationgsiteflat}，
其中导出拉回就是普通拉回。刚才计算的上同调映射正是此映射：
在 \v Cech 解消上，它将正则截面送到相应解析截面，
也就是构造中的伴随映射。它在 affinoid 基上每次均为同构，
故导出比较为拟同构。这也证明其与伴随映射
$\mathcal F\to Rf_*f^*\mathcal F$ 以及限制的相容性。}
\end{proof}
