Strict inequalities at maximal points

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 89

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Strict inequalities at maximal points}{极大点处的严格不等式}]\label{fnd:strictmaximalpointtopnilpotent}
\bi{Let $q:R_n=K\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle\twoheadrightarrow A$ be an admissible strict presentation, and let $\|\cdot\|_q$ be its quotient norm. For $f\in A$, the following conditions are equivalent:}
{设 $q:R_n=K\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle\twoheadrightarrow A$ 为 admissible strict 表示，以 $\|\cdot\|_q$ 表示其商范数。对 $f\in A$，下列条件等价：}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \bi{$|f\bmod\mathfrak m|<1$ for every maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ of $A$, using the unique extending absolute value on its residue field.}{对 $A$ 的每个极大理想 $\mathfrak m$，在其剩余域上使用唯一的延拓绝对值，都有 $|f\bmod\mathfrak m|<1$。}
\item \bi{$|f(x)|<1$ for every $x\in\mathcal M(A)$.}{每个 $x\in\mathcal M(A)$ 都满足 $|f(x)|<1$。}
\item \bi{There is a monic equation $f^r+b_1f^{r-1}+\cdots+b_r=0$, with $r\geq1$ and $\|b_i\|_q<1$ for every $i$.}{存在首一方程 $f^r+b_1f^{r-1}+\cdots+b_r=0$，其中 $r\geq1$ 且每个 $i$ 都满足 $\|b_i\|_q<1$。}
\item \bi{$\|f^m\|_q\to0$ as $m\to\infty$.}{当 $m\to\infty$ 时，$\|f^m\|_q\to0$。}
\end{enumerate}
\bi{These conditions imply a uniform bound $|f(x)|\leq\rho<1$ on $\mathcal M(A)$. Condition (4) is unchanged under replacement of the norm by a uniformly equivalent norm. The zero algebra and nonreduced algebras are included.}
{这些条件还给出 $\mathcal M(A)$ 上的统一界 $|f(x)|\leq\rho<1$。将范数替换为一致等价的范数不改变条件 (4)。结论包括零代数与非约化代数。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The zero algebra is immediate, using the polynomial $T$. Suppose $A\ne0$. We first retain the strict inequality in the minimal-polynomial argument of Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictminimalpolynomialbounds}. If $B$ is a strict affinoid algebra which is a domain, equipped with a bounded finite injection $R_d\hookrightarrow B$, and $u\in B$ has absolute value less than one at every maximal residue field, write its minimal polynomial as $p(T)=T^r+a_1T^{r-1}+\cdots+a_r\in R_d[T]$. The membership in $R_d[T]$ is already proved in that lemma. Fix a maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ of $R_d$ and a root $\alpha$ of the specialization of $p$ in a finite splitting field of $R_d/\mathfrak m$. The proof of the lemma identifies $R_d[u]$ with $R_d[T]/(p)$ and, by lying over for the finite extension $R_d[u]\subset B$, lifts the evaluation at $\alpha$ to a maximal ideal of $B$. Uniqueness of the extending absolute value then gives $|\alpha|<1$. Every specialized coefficient is a finite sum of products of roots, so its absolute value is strictly less than one. Lemma~\ref{fnd:tategaussmaximalpoint}, applied to each $a_i$, now gives $\|a_i\|<1$. This argument uses precisely the finite splitting-field and integral-extension inputs displayed in Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictminimalpolynomialbounds}; it does not infer a strict norm bound from pointwise bounds by compactness.}
{零代数的情形直接取多项式 $T$。以下设 $A\ne0$。先在 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictminimalpolynomialbounds} 的最小多项式论证中保留严格不等式。若 strict affinoid 整环 $B$ 带有有界有限单射 $R_d\hookrightarrow B$，且 $u\in B$ 在每个极大剩余域的绝对值都小于一，则将其最小多项式写为 $p(T)=T^r+a_1T^{r-1}+\cdots+a_r\in R_d[T]$；该引理已经证明系数属于 $R_d$。固定 $R_d$ 的极大理想 $\mathfrak m$，并在 $R_d/\mathfrak m$ 的一个有限分裂域中任取 $p$ 的特化多项式的根 $\alpha$。上述引理的证明将 $R_d[u]$ 识别为 $R_d[T]/(p)$，并通过有限扩张 $R_d[u]\subset B$ 的 lying-over，将在 $\alpha$ 处的取值提升到 $B$ 的极大理想。延拓绝对值的唯一性于是给出 $|\alpha|<1$。每个特化系数都是有限多个根的乘积之和，因此其绝对值严格小于一。对每个 $a_i$ 应用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:tategaussmaximalpoint}，即得 $\|a_i\|<1$。这里使用的正是 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictminimalpolynomialbounds} 中已列出的有限分裂域与整扩张输入，并未以紧致性从逐点界推断严格的范数界。}

\bi{Assume (1). By Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules} and \cite[Tag 00FR, Lemma 10.31.6]{StacksAlg}, the algebra $A$ has finitely many closed minimal primes $\mathfrak p_1,\ldots,\mathfrak p_t$. Apply the presentation-compatible normalization theorem, Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization}, to each quotient $R_n\twoheadrightarrow A/\mathfrak p_j$. Its contractive lift $R_{d_j}\to R_n$, together with the strict coefficient bound just proved, yields a monic polynomial $P_j\in A[T]$ whose nonleading coefficients have quotient norm less than one and for which $P_j(f)\in\mathfrak p_j$. In a product of monic polynomials with this property, every nonleading coefficient is a finite sum of products containing at least one nonleading coefficient. Thus $P=\prod_jP_j$ has the same strict bounds. Its value $P(f)$ lies in every minimal prime and is nilpotent by \cite[Tag 00E0(4),(7), Lemma 10.17.2]{StacksAlg}. Choose $e\geq1$ with $P(f)^e=0$. The polynomial $P(T)^e$ is monic, still has all nonleading coefficients of norm less than one, and proves (3).}
{假设 (1) 成立。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitemodules} 及 \cite[Tag 00FR, Lemma 10.31.6]{StacksAlg}，$A$ 只有有限多个闭的极小素理想 $\mathfrak p_1,\ldots,\mathfrak p_t$。对每个商 $R_n\twoheadrightarrow A/\mathfrak p_j$ 应用与表示相容的正规化定理 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization}。其压缩提升 $R_{d_j}\to R_n$ 与刚才证明的严格系数界给出首一多项式 $P_j\in A[T]$，各非首项系数的商范数小于一，并满足 $P_j(f)\in\mathfrak p_j$。具有此性质的首一多项式相乘时，每个非首项系数均为有限个乘积之和，且每个乘积至少含一个非首项系数。因此 $P=\prod_jP_j$ 具有相同的严格系数界。其值 $P(f)$ 属于每个极小素理想，故由 \cite[Tag 00E0(4),(7), Lemma 10.17.2]{StacksAlg} 为幂零元素。取 $e\geq1$ 使 $P(f)^e=0$。多项式 $P(T)^e$ 仍首一，各非首项系数的范数仍小于一，从而证明 (3)。}

\bi{Assume (3), choose a real $\delta$ with $0<\delta<1$ and $\|b_i\|_q\leq\delta$ for every $i$, and put $C=\max_{0\leq j<r}\|f^j\|_q$. The recurrence $f^m=-\sum_{i=1}^rb_if^{m-i}$ gives, by induction,}
{假设 (3) 成立，选择实数 $\delta$ 满足 $0<\delta<1$ 且每个 $i$ 都有 $\|b_i\|_q\leq\delta$，并令 $C=\max_{0\leq j<r}\|f^j\|_q$。递推式 $f^m=-\sum_{i=1}^rb_if^{m-i}$ 通过归纳给出}
\[
 \|f^m\|_q\leq C\delta^{\lfloor m/r\rfloor}\qquad(m\geq0).
\]
\bi{Indeed, for $m\geq r$ one has $\lfloor(m-i)/r\rfloor\geq\lfloor m/r\rfloor-1$. This proves (4). By Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}, every bounded multiplicative seminorm satisfies $|f(x)|^m\leq\|f^m\|_q$, so (4) implies (2). Maximal residue absolute values are bounded points by Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}, giving (2)$\Rightarrow$(1). Finally choose $N$ with $\|f^N\|_q<1$; then $|f(x)|\leq\|f^N\|_q^{1/N}<1$ uniformly. Equivalent norms preserve convergence to zero.}
{事实上，当 $m\geq r$ 时有 $\lfloor(m-i)/r\rfloor\geq\lfloor m/r\rfloor-1$，故得到 (4)。由 Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}，每个有界乘性半范数都满足 $|f(x)|^m\leq\|f^m\|_q$，所以 (4) 推出 (2)。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictpointunitcriterion}，极大剩余域绝对值都是有界点，从而有 (2)$\Rightarrow$(1)。最后取 $N$ 使 $\|f^N\|_q<1$，即得到统一界 $|f(x)|\leq\|f^N\|_q^{1/N}<1$。等价范数保持趋于零的收敛性。}
\end{proof}
