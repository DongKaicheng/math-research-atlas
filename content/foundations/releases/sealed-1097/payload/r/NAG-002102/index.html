Realization of finitely many type II points

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 701

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Realization of finitely many type II points}
{有限个第二类点的模型实现}]
\label{cand:finitetypeiirealization}
\bi{Assume $K$ is algebraically closed, complete and
nontrivially valued. Let $\mathcal X$ be a nodal curve model
and put $X=\widehat{\mathcal X}_\eta$. For every finite
set $S$ of type II points of $X$, a finite composition of
admissible blowups of $\widehat{\mathcal X}$, induced by
algebraic blowups of $\mathcal X$, yields a nodal curve
model whose component vertex set contains $S$ and all the
old component vertices. If $\mathcal X$ is projective over
$O$, the new model is projective over $O$.}
{假设 $K$ 代数闭、完备且非平凡赋值。设 $\mathcal X$
为节点曲线模型，并令 $X=\widehat{\mathcal X}_\eta$。
对 $X$ 中任意有限个第二类点组成的集合 $S$，存在
$\widehat{\mathcal X}$ 的有限次 admissible blowup，
由 $\mathcal X$ 的代数 blowup 诱导，所得节点曲线模型
的分量顶点集包含 $S$ 及全部旧分量顶点。若
$\mathcal X$ 在 $O$ 上射影，新模型也在 $O$ 上射影。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{By the tube decomposition in
Theorem~\ref{cand:splitmodelskeleton}, a point not already
a component vertex belongs to a smooth open-disc tube or
to a nodal open-annulus tube. In either chart the exact
classification of Theorem~\ref{fnd:classification} represents
a type II point as $\zeta_{a,r}$ with
$r\in|K^\times|$.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{cand:splitmodelskeleton} 的管域分解，
尚非分量顶点的点属于一个光滑开圆盘管域或节点开圆环
管域。在任一种坐标片中，
Theorem~\ref{fnd:classification} 的精确分类将第二类点
写成 $\zeta_{a,r}$，其中 $r\in|K^\times|$。}

\bi{In a smooth tube, membership in the open unit disc gives
$|a|<1$ and $r<1$. Choose $|\lambda|=r$ and apply
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothtubeinsertion}. In a nodal tube
$|b|<|s|<1$, first suppose that the point is radial,
namely $\zeta_{0,\rho}$. Apply
Lemma~\ref{cand:nodefittinginsertion} at radius $\rho$.}
{在光滑管域中，属于开单位圆盘意味着 $|a|<1$ 且 $r<1$。
选择 $|\lambda|=r$，应用
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothtubeinsertion}。
在节点管域 $|b|<|s|<1$ 中，先设该点为径向点
$\zeta_{0,\rho}$，则在半径 $\rho$ 处应用
Lemma~\ref{cand:nodefittinginsertion}。}

\bi{For a nonradial point $\zeta_{a,r}$ in that annulus one
has $r<|a|$; otherwise the disc is also centred at zero.
Here $|b|<|a|<1$. Insert the radial point of radius
$|a|$ first. In the new component its residue class is a
nonzero finite value of the scaled coordinate, hence is a
smooth point rather than one of the two attaching nodes.
The point $\zeta_{a,r}$ now lies in that smooth residual
tube, so a second application of
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothtubeinsertion} inserts it. Thus each
desired point is inserted in at most two steps, possibly
with one extra reflected annular vertex. Applying this
procedure successively to $S$ terminates; every step
preserves the previously obtained vertices and the stated
model properties.}
{对该圆环中的非径向点 $\zeta_{a,r}$，有 $r<|a|$，
否则该圆盘也以零为中心。此时 $|b|<|a|<1$。先插入
半径 $|a|$ 的径向点。该点在新分量上的剩余类，是适当缩放
坐标的一个非零有限值，因而为光滑点，而非两个连接节点。
于是 $\zeta_{a,r}$ 位于这个光滑剩余管域中，再次应用
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothtubeinsertion} 即可插入它。
因此，每个指定点至多经过两步就可插入，可能附带一个
额外的反射圆环顶点。依次对 $S$ 应用此过程会终止，且
每步都保留已经得到的顶点和所述模型性质。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{Let $K$ be complete and nontrivially valued.
We write $O=K^\circ$, $\mathfrak m=K^{\circ\circ}$ and
$k=O/\mathfrak m$. A nodal curve model in this section is a flat
finitely presented $O$-scheme with smooth generic fibre, reduced
nodal special fibre, and Hausdorff formal generic fibre.}
{设 $K$ 完备且非平凡赋值。
记 $O=K^\circ$、$\mathfrak m=K^{\circ\circ}$、
$k=O/\mathfrak m$。本节中的节点曲线模型是平坦有限表示
$O$-概形，其泛纤维光滑、特殊纤维约化且仅有节点，并且
形式泛纤维为 Hausdorff 空间。}
