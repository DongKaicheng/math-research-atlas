Pushouts with complete-extension affinoids

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 273

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Pushouts with complete-extension affinoids}{以完备扩域 affinoid 为系数的推出}]
\label{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout}
\bi{Let $A\to B$ be a bounded map of generalized $K$-affinoid algebras. Let $L/K$ be a complete valued extension, let $C$ be a generalized $L$-affinoid algebra, and let $A\to C$ be a bounded $K$-homomorphism. Then the separated completed projective tensor algebra $B\widehat\otimes_A C$ is a generalized $L$-affinoid algebra. If}
{设 $A\to B$ 为广义 $K$-affinoid 代数的有界映射。设 $L/K$ 为完备赋值扩域，$C$ 为广义 $L$-affinoid 代数，$A\to C$ 为有界 $K$-同态。则分离完备 projective 张量代数 $B\widehat\otimes_A C$ 是广义 $L$-affinoid 代数。若}
\[
 B=A\{r^{-1}T\}/I
\]
\bi{is a norm-admissible relative presentation, there is a natural bounded algebra isomorphism, with bounded inverse,}
{是 norm-admissible 相对表示，则存在自然的有界代数同构，且逆有界：}
\[
 B\widehat\otimes_A C
       \simeq C\{r^{-1}T\}/I\,C\{r^{-1}T\}.
\]
\bi{The extended ideal is actual and closed. For the displayed quotient norm on $B$, the coefficient norm on $C\{r^{-1}T\}$, and a contractive map $A\to C$, this isomorphism is isometric. The tensor algebra is the pushout for compatible bounded homomorphisms to commutative Banach $K$-algebras. Zero algebras are allowed.}
{延伸理想是实际闭理想。若 $B$ 采用所列商范数，$C\{r^{-1}T\}$ 采用系数范数，且 $A\to C$ 为压缩映射，则此同构等距。该张量代数是以交换 Banach $K$-代数为目标、以相容有界同态为态射的推出。允许零代数。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepresentation} supplies the relative presentation. Put $G=B\widehat\otimes_A C$ and $H=C\{r^{-1}T\}/I\,C\{r^{-1}T\}$. By Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation} and Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients}, applied over $L$, $H$ is a complete actual quotient and is $L$-affinoid. On the algebraic tensor, multiplying finite tensor expressions proves submultiplicativity of the projective seminorm. Its zero seminorm subspace is an ideal, so separation and completion make $G$ a Banach algebra.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepresentation}，存在所需的相对表示。令 $G=B\widehat\otimes_A C$，$H=C\{r^{-1}T\}/I\,C\{r^{-1}T\}$。在 $L$ 上应用 Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation} 和 Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients}，可知 $H$ 是完备的实际商，且为 $L$-affinoid。在代数张量积上，将有限张量表达式相乘，得到 projective 半范数的次乘性；零半范数子空间为理想，所以分离并完备化使 $G$ 成为 Banach 代数。}

\bi{The natural maps $B\to H$, $C\to H$ are bounded and agree on $A$. Their balanced product extends to a bounded map $G\to H$. In the other direction, the elements $\bar T_i\otimes1$ in $G$ have their $m$th powers bounded by $r_i^m$ when $B$ has its relative quotient norm. Substitution, with coefficients in $C$, gives a bounded map $C\{r^{-1}T\}\to G$. The two maps from $A\{r^{-1}T\}$ to $G$ agree on coefficients and variables and hence, by polynomial density, everywhere. Therefore the substitution kills $I$ and its extended ideal. It descends boundedly to $H\to G$ by taking quotient infima. The composites fix every coefficient and variable in $H$ and every pure tensor in $G$. Density makes them identity maps. When $A\to C$ is contractive, every map just constructed is contractive, giving the isometry. Equivalent admissible norms give the stated bounded comparisons.}
{自然映射 $B\to H$、$C\to H$ 有界，且在 $A$ 上一致。其平衡乘积延拓为有界映射 $G\to H$。反方向，若 $B$ 采用相对商范数，则 $G$ 中元素 $\bar T_i\otimes1$ 的 $m$ 次幂范数至多为 $r_i^m$。以 $C$ 为系数代入，得到有界映射 $C\{r^{-1}T\}\to G$。从 $A\{r^{-1}T\}$ 到 $G$ 的两个映射在系数与变量上相同，由多项式稠密性在全代数上相同。因此该代入零化 $I$ 及其延伸理想；对商下确界取界，使它有界地下降为 $H\to G$。两个复合固定 $H$ 的每个系数及变量，也固定 $G$ 的每个纯张量。由稠密性，两者均为恒等映射。若 $A\to C$ 为压缩映射，上述所有映射均为压缩映射，故得到等距性。等价的 admissible 范数给出所述有界比较。}

\bi{Finally, compatible bounded maps $B,C\to D$ to a commutative Banach $K$-algebra have bounded balanced product $(b,c)\mapsto b c$. On any finite tensor expression its norm is bounded by the product of their operator bounds times the projective bound. Taking the infimum, separating and completing gives a unique bounded map $G\to D$. Uniqueness follows from the dense algebraic tensor image.}
{最后，若 $B,C\to D$ 是到交换 Banach $K$-代数的相容有界映射，则 $(b,c)\mapsto bc$ 是有界平衡乘积。对任意有限张量表达式，其范数被两个映射有界常数的乘积乘以 projective 界控制。取下确界、分离并完备化，得到唯一的有界映射 $G\to D$；唯一性来自代数张量像的稠密性。}
\end{proof}
