Every scheme point has an analytic lift

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 299

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Every scheme point has an analytic lift}
{每个概形点都有解析提升}]
\label{fnd:analytificationsurjectivity}
\bi{For every finite-type $K$-scheme $X$, the map
$|X^{\an}|\to |X|$ is surjective. Consequently coherent
analytification detects zero sheaves and isomorphisms.}
{对每个有限型 $K$-概形 $X$，映射
$|X^{\an}|\to|X|$ 满射。因此凝聚层解析化检测零层及同构。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Let $\xi\in X$ and put $E=\kappa(\xi)$, a finitely generated
field extension of $K$. Choose a transcendence basis from a finite
generating list. Then $E$ is finite over $F=K(t_1,\ldots,t_d)$.
The Gauss absolute value on $K[t_1,\ldots,t_d]$ is multiplicative
by Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm}; it extends by quotients to
$F$. Let $H$ be its completion. The finite nonzero $H$-algebra
$E\otimes_F H$ has a maximal ideal, and its quotient $L$ is a
finite field extension of $H$. The map $E\to L$ is injective
because $E$ is a field and $1$ has nonzero image.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} gives $L$ its extending
absolute value. Its restriction to $E$ defines a multiplicative
seminorm on any affine coordinate ring at $\xi$, with kernel
exactly the prime of $\xi$. The radius construction of
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affineanalytificationconstruction}
makes this an analytic point above $\xi$.
At such a point the local ring map is faithfully flat by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact}.
A coherent sheaf whose pullback is zero therefore has every
stalk zero. Apply the same assertion to the kernel and cokernel,
using exactness, to detect an isomorphism.}
{取 $\xi\in X$，令 $E=\kappa(\xi)$；这是 $K$ 的有限生成域扩张。
从有限生成元中选一组超越基，则 $E$ 在
$F=K(t_1,\ldots,t_d)$ 上有限。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:gaussnorm}，
$K[t_1,\ldots,t_d]$ 上的 Gauss 绝对值可乘，故用商延拓到 $F$。
令 $H$ 为其完备化。非零有限 $H$-代数 $E\otimes_F H$ 有极大理想，
其商 $L$ 是 $H$ 的有限域扩张。由于 $E$ 是域且 $1$ 的像非零，
映射 $E\to L$ 单射。Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}
给出 $L$ 上的延拓绝对值，其在 $E$ 上的限制定义 $\xi$ 附近
任意仿射坐标环上的可乘半范数，核恰为 $\xi$ 的素理想。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affineanalytificationconstruction} 的半径构造
使它成为 $\xi$ 上方的解析点。由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:coherentanalytificationexact}，该点的局部环映射
忠实平坦。因此，拉回为零的凝聚层的每个茎都为零。
再用正合性对态射的核、余核应用这一断言，即得同构检测。}
\end{proof}
