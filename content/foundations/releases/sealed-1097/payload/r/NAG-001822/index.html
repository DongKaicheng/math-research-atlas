Lifting analytic-fibre injectivity at an inner point

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 487

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Lifting analytic-fibre injectivity at an inner point}{在内点提升解析纤维单射性}]
\label{fnd:generalinnerflatcokernel}
\bi{Let $T\to X$ be a morphism of good analytic spaces over any complete field, let $t\in\operatorname{Int}(T/X)$ have image $x$, and let $u:L\to N$ be a morphism of coherent sheaves. If $N_t$ is flat over $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ and the analytic-fibre map is injective at $t$, then $u_t$ is injective and $\operatorname{Coker}(u)_t$ is flat over $\mathcal O_{X,x}$.}
{设 $T\to X$ 为任意完备域上 good 解析空间之间的态射，$t\in\operatorname{Int}(T/X)$ 的像为 $x$，并设 $u:L\to N$ 为凝聚层态射。若 $N_t$ 在 $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 上平坦，且解析纤维上的映射在 $t$ 处单射，则 $u_t$ 单射，且 $\operatorname{Coker}(u)_t$ 在 $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 上平坦。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Write $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$, $S=\mathcal O_{T,t}$ and $\mathfrak m=\mathfrak m_R$. Coherent pullback identifies the fibre map with $u_t\otimes_S\mathcal O_{T_x,t}$. Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalclosedfiberfaithfulflat} gives a faithfully flat local map $S/\mathfrak mS\to\mathcal O_{T_x,t}$. Its faithful flatness detects the kernel of the finite map $L_t/\mathfrak mL_t\to N_t/\mathfrak mN_t$, so this map is injective. The ring $S$ is Noetherian, $L_t$ is finite over $S$, and $N_t$ is $R$-flat. Apply \cite[Tag 00ME]{StacksAlg}: it gives injectivity of $u_t$ and $R$-flatness of its cokernel. Ordinary stalks commute with coherent cokernels, proving the statement.}
{记 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$S=\mathcal O_{T,t}$、$\mathfrak m=\mathfrak m_R$。凝聚拉回将纤维映射识别为 $u_t\otimes_S\mathcal O_{T_x,t}$。Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalclosedfiberfaithfulflat} 给出忠实平坦局部映射 $S/\mathfrak mS\to\mathcal O_{T_x,t}$；它检测有限映射 $L_t/\mathfrak mL_t\to N_t/\mathfrak mN_t$ 的核，所以后者单射。环 $S$ Noetherian，$L_t$ 在 $S$ 上有限，而 $N_t$ 在 $R$ 上平坦。应用 \cite[Tag 00ME]{StacksAlg}，得到 $u_t$ 单射及其余核的 $R$-平坦性。普通茎与凝聚余核交换，遂得结论。}
\end{proof}
