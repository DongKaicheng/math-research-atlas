Transport of a split nodal chart

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 666

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Transport of a split nodal chart}
{分裂节点坐标片的传递}]
\label{cand:pointednodalannulustube}
\bi{Let $0<|a|<1$ and put
$\mathcal Z_a=\operatorname{Spec}O[U,V]/(UV-a)$, with
special-fibre origin $o$. Suppose that there is a diagram}
{设 $0<|a|<1$，令
$\mathcal Z_a=\operatorname{Spec}O[U,V]/(UV-a)$，其特殊纤维
原点为 $o$。假设给定图}
\[
 (\mathcal X,q)\ \longleftarrow\ (\mathcal U,u)
                    \ \longrightarrow\ (\mathcal Z_a,o)
\]
\bi{of flat finitely presented $O$-schemes, with both arrows
\'etale and all selected special-fibre points $k$-rational.
Then $]q[_{\widehat{\mathcal X}}$ is an open annulus with
coordinate $T$ satisfying $|a|<|T|<1$.}
{其中各概形均在 $O$ 上平坦且有限表示，两个箭头均
\'etale，且全部所选特殊纤维点均为 $k$-有理点。
则 $]q[_{\widehat{\mathcal X}}$ 是一个具有坐标 $T$、
满足 $|a|<|T|<1$ 的开圆环。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Apply Proposition~\ref{cand:algebraicetaletubes} to both
arrows. The formal completion of $\mathcal Z_a$ is the standard
nodal chart of Proposition~\ref{fnd:standardmonomialchartgeometry}.
Its origin tube is computed in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalstratumtubes}: with $T=U$ and $V=a/T$,
the two inequalities $|U|<1$ and $|V|<1$ become
$|a|<|T|<1$. Transport this already established analytic
identification along the two tube isomorphisms.}
{将 Proposition~\ref{cand:algebraicetaletubes} 应用于两个
箭头。$\mathcal Z_a$ 的形式完备化是
Proposition~\ref{fnd:standardmonomialchartgeometry} 的标准
节点坐标片。其原点管域已由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalstratumtubes} 计算：取 $T=U$、
$V=a/T$ 后，不等式 $|U|<1$、$|V|<1$ 变为
$|a|<|T|<1$。沿两个管域同构传递这一已有的解析识别即可。}
\end{proof}
