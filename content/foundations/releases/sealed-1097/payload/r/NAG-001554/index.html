Closed presentations after adjoining coefficients

1 Valued fields, Banach algebras and analytic points

Open in the English reader · PDF page 103

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Closed presentations after adjoining coefficients}{添入系数变量后的闭表示}]\label{fnd:closedweightedpresentation}
\bi{Let $D$ be a Banach $K$-algebra, let $I\subset D$ be a closed algebraic ideal, and give $A=D/I$ its quotient norm. Let $\Gamma=\mathbf N^p\times\mathbf Z^q$, with finite $p,q\geq0$, and let $w_0=1$, $w_\gamma>0$, $w_{\gamma+\delta}\leq w_\gamma w_\delta$. Applying $D\to A$ coefficientwise gives a norm-strict surjection of Banach algebras, with}
{设 $D$ 为 Banach $K$-代数，$I\subset D$ 为闭的代数理想，并给 $A=D/I$ 配备商范数。设 $\Gamma=\mathbf N^p\times\mathbf Z^q$，其中 $p,q\geq0$ 有限，且 $w_0=1$、$w_\gamma>0$、$w_{\gamma+\delta}\leq w_\gamma w_\delta$。逐系数应用 $D\to A$，得到 Banach 代数的 norm-strict 满射，并且}
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \ker\bigl(\mathcal C_w(\Gamma,D)\to\mathcal C_w(\Gamma,A)\bigr)
    =\mathcal C_w(\Gamma,I)
    =\overline{I\,\mathcal C_w(\Gamma,D)},\\
 \mathcal C_w(\Gamma,D)\Big/
       \overline{I\,\mathcal C_w(\Gamma,D)}
       \ \simeq\ \mathcal C_w(\Gamma,A)
 \quad\text{isometrically}.
 \end{gathered}
\]
\bi{The ideal inside the closure is the algebraic ideal generated by the constant image of $I$; no finite generation of $I$ is assumed. If $A$ is equipped instead with a norm uniformly equivalent to its quotient norm, the induced quotient isomorphism is bounded with bounded inverse, with those norm-comparison constants. In particular, if $D=K\{s^{-1}X\}$ and $\rho$ is any finite tuple of positive radii, then $A\{\rho^{-1}Y\}$ is norm-admissible generalized affinoid, with presentation}
{闭包内的理想是 $I$ 的常系数像生成的代数理想；不假设 $I$ 有限生成。若 $A$ 改用与商范数一致等价的范数，则诱导的商同构及其逆均有界，保有所给范数比较常数。特别地，若 $D=K\{s^{-1}X\}$，$\rho$ 为任意有限正半径元组，则 $A\{\rho^{-1}Y\}$ 为 norm-admissible generalized affinoid，具有表示}
\[
 A\{\rho^{-1}Y\}
 \simeq
 K\{s^{-1}X,\rho^{-1}Y\}\Big/
       \overline{I K\{s^{-1}X,\rho^{-1}Y\}}.
\]
\bi{Any subsequent quotient by a closed ideal is again generalized affinoid with its quotient norm. These statements allow zero quotients, and do not assert that any unclosed extension ideal is already closed.}
{随后再按闭理想取商，配备商范数后仍为 generalized affinoid。这些陈述允许零商，并不声称尚未取闭包的扩张理想已经闭合。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The closed ideal $I$, with its induced norm, is complete. Lemma~\ref{fnd:completequotient} makes $A$ complete. The quotient norm is submultiplicative by approximating representatives of two factors; if $A\ne0$, its unit has norm one, since $0<\|1_A\|\leq1$ and $\|1_A\|\leq\|1_A\|^2$. For each fixed $\lambda>1$, every nonzero quotient coefficient has a lift of norm at most $\lambda$ times its norm; take the zero lift for zero. Proposition~\ref{fnd:weightedc0exact} therefore gives the surjection, its coefficient kernel and the exact quotient norm. Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientconvolution} shows that this map is an algebra homomorphism: it is so on finite-support families, and all maps and products are continuous.}
{闭理想 $I$ 配备诱导范数后完备。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:completequotient}，$A$ 完备。通过逼近两个因子的代表元，商范数具有次乘性；若 $A\ne0$，则其单位元范数为一，因为 $0<\|1_A\|\leq1$ 且 $\|1_A\|\leq\|1_A\|^2$。固定任意 $\lambda>1$，每个非零商系数都有一个范数至多为其范数的 $\lambda$ 倍的提升；零系数取零提升。因此 Proposition~\ref{fnd:weightedc0exact} 给出满射、逐系数的核及精确商范数。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedcoefficientconvolution}，该映射为代数同态：它在有限支撑族上如此，而所有映射与乘法均连续。}

\bi{Its kernel is closed and contains $I\,\mathcal C_w(\Gamma,D)$, so it contains the closure of that ideal. Conversely, every family with coefficients in $I$ is a norm limit of finite sums $\sum_{\gamma\in F}i_\gamma X^\gamma$. Each summand is the product of the constant $i_\gamma\in I$ and the monomial $X^\gamma\in\mathcal C_w(\Gamma,D)$, and hence belongs to the algebraic extension ideal. This proves both inclusions. This argument proves an equality with a closure, not an unproved closed-image assertion.}
{其核闭且包含 $I\,\mathcal C_w(\Gamma,D)$，因而包含该理想的闭包。反之，系数属于 $I$ 的每个族都是有限和 $\sum_{\gamma\in F}i_\gamma X^\gamma$ 的范数极限。每个被加项都是常数 $i_\gamma\in I$ 与单项式 $X^\gamma\in\mathcal C_w(\Gamma,D)$ 的乘积，故属于代数扩张理想。这证明两个方向的包含。该论证得到的是与闭包相等，并未预设关系像已经闭合。}

\bi{Uniform comparison of two coefficient norms follows by multiplying the coefficient inequalities by $w_\gamma$ and taking their supremum. For $D=K\{s^{-1}X\}$ and $\Gamma=\mathbf N^n$ with $w_\beta=\rho^\beta$, Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedc0rearrangement} identifies the source isometrically with the joint Tate algebra in $X,Y$. The displayed quotient is therefore a generalized affinoid presentation. If $J$ is a closed ideal of this quotient, its inverse image under the continuous Tate presentation is closed. The composite quotient is norm-admissible: lift through the two quotient maps with any fixed slack factor greater than one, or take successive infima over representatives. The resulting infimum is the direct quotient norm. This proves the last assertion without a Noetherianity or ordinary flatness hypothesis.}
{将两个系数范数的比较不等式乘以 $w_\gamma$ 再取上确界，即得系数空间范数的一致比较。当 $D=K\{s^{-1}X\}$、$\Gamma=\mathbf N^n$ 且 $w_\beta=\rho^\beta$ 时，由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:weightedc0rearrangement}，源空间等距识别为 $X,Y$ 的联合 Tate 代数。因此所列商给出 generalized affinoid 表示。若 $J$ 是此商的闭理想，则它在连续 Tate 表示下的逆像闭。复合商为 norm-admissible：可通过两个商映射逐次提升，均留出任意固定的大于一的误差因子；也可逐次对代表元取下确界，所得下确界恰为直接商范数。这在不假设 Noetherianity 或普通平坦性的前提下证明最后一项断言。}
\end{proof}
