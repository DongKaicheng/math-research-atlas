3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Strict affinoid germs and model rings}
{Strict affinoid 芽与模型环}]
\label{fnd:strictmodelgradedcomparison}
\bi{Assume $K$ is nontrivially valued. Let $A$ be strict
affinoid, $A_0$ an admissible affine model, and $x\in\mathcal M(A)$.
Put $F=\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}^{\rm gr}$ and let
$R_0\subset F_1$ be the image of
$A_0/K^{\circ\circ}A_0$ under evaluation at $x$. Then}
{设 $K$ 非平凡赋值，$A$ 为 strict affinoid，
$A_0$ 为 admissible 仿射模型，$x\in\mathcal M(A)$。
置 $F=\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}^{\rm gr}$，并令
$R_0\subset F_1$ 为 $A_0/K^{\circ\circ}A_0$ 在 $x$ 处
取值后的像。则}
\[
 \mathscr R(\mathcal M(A),x)
       =\pi^{-1}\bigl(\mathscr P_{\widetilde K}(F_1)\{R_0\}\bigr).
\]
\bi{The ordinary birational space on the right is independent
of $A_0$. It can also be computed using the image of the
canonical reduction $\widetilde A$ in $F_1$.}
{右侧的通常 birational 空间与 $A_0$ 无关，也可用典范约化
$\widetilde A$ 在 $F_1$ 中的像来计算。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The ring $R_0$ is a finitely generated
$\widetilde K$-algebra: reduction of a restricted power
series is a polynomial, and a finite topological presentation
of $A_0$ supplies finitely many generators.
Its image lies in the degree-one part of the image of
$\widetilde A^{\rm gr}$, giving one inclusion of valuation sets.}
{$R_0$ 为有限生成 $\widetilde K$-代数：受限幂级数约化为
多项式，而 $A_0$ 的有限拓扑表示提供有限个生成元。
它的像包含于 $\widetilde A^{\rm gr}$ 的像的次数一部分，
因此得到赋值集合的一个包含。}

\bi{Conversely let a graded valuation contain
$\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$ and $R_0$.
A nonzero homogeneous element of the image of
$\widetilde A^{\rm gr}$ is the initial value of some
$a\in A$ with $|a(x)|=\rho_A(a)=r>0$.
By Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictspectralvaluegroup}, choose
$d>0$, $c\in K^\times$ with $r^d=|c|$.
The element $b=a^d/c$ is power-bounded.
The integral adjunction proved in
Theorem~\ref{fnd:powerboundedmodelblowup} makes $b$
integral over $A_0$. Reduce a monic equation at $x$:
the initial value of $b$ is integral over $R_0$ and therefore
belongs to the valuation ring.
It equals the $d$th power of the initial value of $a$,
divided by the homogeneous unit given by $c$.
The valuation ring thus also contains that initial value
of $a$. This proves equality of the two sets.}
{反之，设某分次赋值包含 $\widetilde K^{\rm gr}$ 与 $R_0$。
$\widetilde A^{\rm gr}$ 的像中每个非零齐次元素都是某个
$a\in A$ 的首项值，其中
$|a(x)|=\rho_A(a)=r>0$。
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictspectralvaluegroup}，
可选 $d>0$、$c\in K^\times$，满足 $r^d=|c|$。
元素 $b=a^d/c$ 幂有界。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:powerboundedmodelblowup} 中已证的整
增添使 $b$ 在 $A_0$ 上整。在 $x$ 处约化一个首一关系，
说明 $b$ 的首项值在 $R_0$ 上整，因而属于该赋值环。
它等于 $a$ 的首项值的 $d$ 次幂除以 $c$ 给出的齐次单位，
故该赋值环也包含 $a$ 的首项值。这证明两集合相等。}

\bi{The identical argument with all power-bounded elements
in place of the model generators gives the canonical
reduction formula. Finally
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradeddegreeonevaluation} makes $\pi$
surjective, so the ordinary valuation sets are identical
for any two models. Their topologies are the induced
valuation topology, giving the asserted independence.}
{将模型生成元换为全部幂有界元素，完全相同的论证给出
典范约化公式。最后，由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradeddegreeonevaluation}，$\pi$ 满射，
故任意两个模型给出的通常赋值集合相同。它们的拓扑都是诱导
的赋值拓扑，因此得到所述独立性。}
\end{proof}

\bi{For an affinoid $X=\mathcal M(A)$ and $x\in X$, put}
{对 affinoid $X=\mathcal M(A)$ 及 $x\in X$，令}
\[
 F_x=\widetilde{\mathcal H(x)}^{\rm gr},\qquad
 R_{A,x}=\operatorname{im}(\widetilde A^{\rm gr}\to F_x),
 \qquad
 \mathscr R(X,x)=\mathscr P_{\widetilde K^{\rm gr}}(F_x)\{R_{A,x}\}.
\]
