Coordinate bases for weighted subspaces

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 857

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Coordinate bases for weighted subspaces}{加权子空间的坐标基}]
\label{cand:weightedcoordinatesubspace}
\bi{Let $E=K^q$ have the norm $\|(a_i)\|=\max_i|a_i|r_i$, where every $r_i>0$. For every $s$-dimensional subspace $W\subset E$ there are distinct indices $b_1,\ldots,b_s$ and a basis $g_1,\ldots,g_s$ of $W$ such that}
{设 $E=K^q$ 配备范数 $\|(a_i)\|=\max_i|a_i|r_i$，其中每个 $r_i>0$。对任意 $s$ 维子空间 $W\subset E$，存在互异的指标 $b_1,\ldots,b_s$ 及 $W$ 的基 $g_1,\ldots,g_s$，使}
\[
 (g_i)_{b_j}=\delta_{ij},\qquad \|g_i\|=r_{b_i},\qquad
 \Bigl\|\sum_i c_i g_i\Bigr\|=\max_i|c_i|r_{b_i}.
\]
\bi{The same assertions hold after every isometric field extension, with the same basis and weights. If all $r_i=1$, reduction of the unit ball of $W$ has dimension $s$ over $\widetilde K$, and its reduction commutes with residue-field extension.}
{在任意等距扩域后，同一组基与权重仍满足这些等式。若所有 $r_i=1$，则 $W$ 单位球的约化在 $\widetilde K$ 上维数为 $s$，且其约化与剩余域扩张相容。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose a nonzero vector in $W$, choose a coordinate at which its weighted norm is attained, and divide by that coordinate. This gives $g_1$ with pivot $b_1$, pivot coefficient one, and norm $r_{b_1}$. Apply induction to the kernel in $W$ of the $b_1$-coordinate. Its basis vectors have zero $b_1$-coordinate. Subtract their pivot multiples from $g_1$ to make all other pivot coordinates of $g_1$ zero. Each subtraction has norm at most $r_{b_1}$: its scalar coefficient $c$ occurs in a pivot coordinate of weight $r_b$ and satisfies $|c|r_b\le r_{b_1}$. The $b_1$-coordinate stays one, so the norm stays $r_{b_1}$. This completes the construction.}
{先选 $W$ 中非零向量，在达到其加权范数的坐标处选取主元，再除以该坐标。这给出主元为 $b_1$、主元系数为一、范数为 $r_{b_1}$ 的 $g_1$。对 $W$ 中 $b_1$ 坐标为零的子空间归纳；所得基向量的 $b_1$ 坐标均为零。再从 $g_1$ 减去这些基向量的主元倍数，使其余主元坐标为零。每次所减项的范数至多为 $r_{b_1}$：其标量系数 $c$ 来自权重为 $r_b$ 的主元坐标，因此 $|c|r_b\le r_{b_1}$。$b_1$ 坐标始终为一，故范数仍为 $r_{b_1}$，构造完成。}

\bi{The ultrametric inequality gives the upper bound for a linear combination. Its pivot coordinates are exactly the $c_i$, giving the reverse bound. These calculations remain valid over an isometric extension. For unit weights the unit ball is therefore $\bigoplus_iK^\circ g_i$, its reduction has basis $\overline g_i$, and the identical description over the extension proves the last assertion.}
{超度量不等式给出线性组合范数的上界，而其主元坐标恰为各 $c_i$，从而给出反向上界。等距扩域不改变这些计算。权重均为一时，单位球就是 $\bigoplus_iK^\circ g_i$，其约化以 $\overline g_i$ 为基；扩域后同一描述给出最后的相容性。}
\end{proof}
