Analytic domains around rigid points

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 353

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Analytic domains around rigid points}{Rigid 点周围的解析域}]
\label{fnd:rigidpointdomaininterior}
\bi{Let $X$ be good and let $x\in X$ have $[\mathcal H(x):K]<\infty$. Every analytic domain $V\subset X$ containing $x$ contains an ordinary neighborhood of $x$.}
{设 $X$ good，且 $x\in X$ 满足 $[\mathcal H(x):K]<\infty$。每个包含 $x$ 的解析域 $V\subset X$ 都包含 $x$ 的普通邻域。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose an ordinary affinoid neighborhood $U$ of $x$ in $X$, and then an affinoid domain $W\subset V\cap U$ containing $x$, using the affinoid domain basis. Choose a finite extension $L/K$ in which the finitely many minimal polynomials of generators of $\mathcal H(x)/K$ split. The unique extension of the complete absolute value makes $L$ a complete valued field. Every residue field of $\mathcal H(x)\otimes_K L$ is then $L$, even when the tensor product has nilpotents. Thus all points of the finite fibre of $\pi:U_L\to U$ over $x$ are $L$-rational. They all lie in $W_L$, by the domain base-change theorem. Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalpointdomaininterior} gives each of them an ordinary neighborhood in $U_L$ contained in $W_L$. Let $O$ be the union of these neighborhoods.}
{先在 $X$ 中选择 $x$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域 $U$，再由 affinoid 域基选取包含 $x$ 的 affinoid 域 $W\subset V\cap U$。选择有限扩域 $L/K$，使 $\mathcal H(x)/K$ 的有限组生成元的极小多项式均在 $L$ 中分裂。完备绝对值的唯一扩张使 $L$ 成为完备赋值域。于是 $\mathcal H(x)\otimes_K L$ 的每个剩余域都为 $L$，即使该张量积含有幂零元。因此有限映射 $\pi:U_L\to U$ 在 $x$ 上方的全部点均为 $L$-有理点。由域的基变换定理，它们全属于 $W_L$。Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalpointdomaininterior} 给出这些点各自在 $U_L$ 中包含于 $W_L$ 的普通邻域；令 $O$ 为这些邻域之并。}

\bi{The space $U_L$ is compact and $U$ is Hausdorff. Hence the continuous image $\pi(U_L\setminus O)$ is closed in $U$ and does not contain $x$. Its complement is an ordinary neighborhood of $x$. Scalar-extension spectral surjectivity shows that this complement is contained in $W$: a point outside $W$ has every lift outside $W_L$, and hence outside $O$. The complement therefore lies in $V$, proving the assertion.}
{$U_L$ 紧且 $U$ Hausdorff，故连续像 $\pi(U_L\setminus O)$ 在 $U$ 中闭，并且不含 $x$。其补是 $x$ 的普通邻域。扩标量谱映射的满射性说明这个补包含于 $W$：位于 $W$ 外的点的每个提升都在 $W_L$ 外，因而在 $O$ 外。所以该补包含于 $V$，证明结论。}
\end{proof}
