The interior criterion for good germs

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 572

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{The interior criterion for good germs}
{Good 芽的内点判据}]
\label{fnd:goodgermproperinterior}
\bi{For a morphism of good germs $(Y,y)\to(X,x)$,
the point $y$ is in the relative analytic interior if and only
if $\mathscr R(Y,y)\to\mathscr R(X,x)$ is proper.}
{对 good 芽态射 $(Y,y)\to(X,x)$，点 $y$ 位于相对解析
内部，当且仅当 $\mathscr R(Y,y)\to\mathscr R(X,x)$ 适当。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose affinoid neighbourhood representatives
$\mathcal M(B)\to\mathcal M(A)$. The \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:goodinteriorlocaltesting]{ordinary local test}}
identifies interiority here with interiority for the given good germs. In
$F=\widetilde{\mathcal H(y)}^{\rm gr}$, let $R_B$ and $R_A$
be the images of the two graded algebras. The canonical
birational map is the inclusion}
{选取 affinoid 邻域代表
$\mathcal M(B)\to\mathcal M(A)$。\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:goodinteriorlocaltesting]{普通局部判据}}将此处的内部与给定 good 芽的内部相识别。在
$F=\widetilde{\mathcal H(y)}^{\rm gr}$ 中，将两个分次代数
的像记为 $R_B,R_A$。典范 birational 映射就是包含}
\[
          \mathscr P(F)\{R_B\}\ \subseteq\
                          \mathscr P(F)\{R_A\}.
\]
\bi{It is bijective exactly when $R_B$ is finite over $R_A$,
by Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedvaluationintegrality} and the finite
generation from the \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{fixed absolute}} presentation.
The \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedrelativepointevaluation]{relative point criterion}}, applied to $A\to B$ at $y$, identifies this with innerness
at $y$. Shrinking the good representatives does not change
their reductions by Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedaffinoidgerminvariance},
so the criterion is a criterion on the original germs.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedvaluationintegrality} 及
\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedfixedabsolutepresentation]{固定绝对表示}}给出的有限生成性，该包含为双射当且仅当 $R_B$ 在 $R_A$ 上有限。
在 $y$ 处对 $A\to B$ 应用\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:gradedrelativepointevaluation]{相对点判据}}，这恰好等价于在 $y$ 处内。
由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedaffinoidgerminvariance}，缩小 good
代表不改变约化，因此所得确实是原芽上的判据。}
\end{proof}
