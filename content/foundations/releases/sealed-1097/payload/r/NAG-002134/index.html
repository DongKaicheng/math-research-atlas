Separable-degree balance

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Separable-degree balance}
{可分次数加权的平衡}]
\label{cand:charzeroweightedslopebalance}
\bi{Under the hypotheses of
Theorem~\ref{cand:charzeroresidualslope}, assume that
$C$ is proper over $k$. Only finitely many geometric
slopes $\sigma_p(f)$ are nonzero, and}
{在 Theorem~\ref{cand:charzeroresidualslope} 的假设下，
再假设 $C$ 在 $k$ 上适当。只有有限个几何斜率
$\sigma_p(f)$ 非零，且}
\[
          \sum_{p\in C^\nu\ {\rm closed}}
                    s_p\,\sigma_p(f)=0.
\]
\bi{Equivalently, the algebraically normalized slopes
$\sigma_p(f)/e_p$ satisfy the residue-degree identity}
{等价地，代数归一化斜率 $\sigma_p(f)/e_p$ 满足
剩余次数恒等式}
\[
 \sum_{p\in C^\nu\ {\rm closed}}
       [\kappa(p):k]\,\frac{\sigma_p(f)}{e_p}=0.
\]
\bi{When $k$ is perfect, $e_p=1$ and this is
Theorem~\ref{cand:weightedcomponentbalance}.}
{当 $k$ 完美时，$e_p=1$，此即
Theorem~\ref{cand:weightedcomponentbalance}。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The finite normalization $C^\nu$ is proper by
\cite[Tags 0BXR, 01WM, 01W3]{StacksAlg}.
The principal divisor of $\varphi$ has finite support.
Its degree is zero by \cite[Tag 02RU]{StacksAlg}, so}
{由 \cite[Tags 0BXR, 01WM, 01W3]{StacksAlg}，
有限正规化 $C^\nu$ 适当。$\varphi$ 的主除子具有
有限支撑。由 \cite[Tag 02RU]{StacksAlg}，其次数
为零，故}
\[
 0=\sum_p[\kappa(p):k]\operatorname{ord}_p(\varphi)
   =\sum_p s_p e_p\operatorname{ord}_p(\varphi)
   =\sum_p s_p\sigma_p(f).
\]
\bi{Both displayed assertions and finite support follow.}
{两条所列恒等式及有限支撑断言均随之得到。}
\end{proof}
