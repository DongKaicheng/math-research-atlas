Pointwise smooth weights

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 889

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Pointwise smooth weights}{逐点光滑权重}]
\label{cand:smoothweightedstableproduct}
\bi{Suppose the weights of $C,E$ are smooth functions on their
cells, extending smoothly to their faces, and that their values
satisfy balancing at every point of every face. Freezing the
weights at each point and applying the local fan formula defines
a smooth-weighted balanced product. It is independent of
subdivision and generic displacement, and is bilinear,
commutative, and associative.}
{设 $C,E$ 的权重是各胞腔上的光滑函数，光滑延拓到各面，
且其值在每个面的每一点满足平衡。逐点固定权重后应用局部
扇公式，定义一个带光滑权重的平衡乘积。它与细分及一般
位移的选择无关，并且双线性、交换且结合。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{At a fixed point, the frozen stars are constant-weight
balanced fans. Theorem~\ref{cand:stablefandisplacement} makes
their product coefficient well-defined. On any fixed prospective
intersection face, this coefficient is a finite sum of products
of restrictions of the original weights multiplied by constant
lattice indices. It is therefore smooth. At a lower face,
freezing again identifies the normal equation with balancing
of the product of the frozen fans. Refinement and the two
bracketings of a triple product are compared by the same frozen
fan calculation, using Theorem~\ref{cand:stableproductring}.
The equalities hold at every point, hence as equalities of
smooth coefficient functions.}
{在固定点处，固定权重后的星形复形为常权平衡扇。由
Theorem~\ref{cand:stablefandisplacement}，其乘积系数良定义。
在每个固定的候选交面上，该系数是原权重限制之积的有限和，
各项乘以常格指数，故光滑。在更低维的面处再次固定权重，
便将法向等式识别为固定权重扇的乘积平衡性。对细分以及三重
乘积的两种括号，同样逐点使用
Theorem~\ref{cand:stableproductring} 比较固定权重的扇。
所得等式逐点成立，因此是光滑系数函数的等式。}
\end{proof}
