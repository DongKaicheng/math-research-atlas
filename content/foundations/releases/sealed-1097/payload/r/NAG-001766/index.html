Characteristic-zero ordinary regular comparison

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 334

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Characteristic-zero ordinary regular comparison}{特征零中的通常正则比较}]
\label{fnd:charzeroregularcomparison}
\bi{Let $K$ be any complete field of characteristic zero, with possibly trivial valuation. Let $A$ be any generalized $K$-affinoid algebra, let $x\in X=\mathcal M(A)$, and put $\mathfrak p=\ker(x)$ and $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$. The following maps are regular homomorphisms:}
{设 $K$ 为任意特征零完备域，赋值允许平凡；$A$ 为任意 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数，$x\in X=\mathcal M(A)$，并令 $\mathfrak p=\ker(x)$、$R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$。则以下映射均正则：}
\[
                 A_{\mathfrak p}\longrightarrow R,
                  \qquad R\longrightarrow\widehat R.
\]
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Flatness and Noetherianity are proved exactly as at the start of Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictcharzeroregularcomparison}; those arguments use no strictness or nontriviality of the valuation. Let $\mathfrak q\subset\mathfrak p$, put $B=A/\mathfrak q$, and let $F=\operatorname{Frac}(B)$. The finite closed immersion $\mathcal M(B)\to X$ gives $R/\mathfrak qR=\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(B),x}$. The integral generalized algebra $B$ satisfies Proposition~\ref{fnd:charzerogenericgermregularity}, so this ordinary local ring becomes regular after inverting one nonzero element of $B$. Its further localization by all of $B\setminus\{0\}$ is the fibre $R\otimes_A F$, hence is regular. By \cite[Tag 0381]{StacksAlg} it is geometrically regular over $F$. This treats every fibre of the first map.}
{平坦性和 Noetherian 性完全沿用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictcharzeroregularcomparison} 开头的论证；这些论证并不使用 strict 性或赋值非平凡性。取 $\mathfrak q\subset\mathfrak p$，令 $B=A/\mathfrak q$、$F=\operatorname{Frac}(B)$。有限闭浸入 $\mathcal M(B)\to X$ 给出 $R/\mathfrak qR=\mathcal O_{\mathcal M(B),x}$。整 generalized 代数 $B$ 满足 Proposition~\ref{fnd:charzerogenericgermregularity}，所以该通常局部环在可逆化 $B$ 的某个非零元素后正则。再可逆化 $B\setminus\{0\}$ 中全部元素，便得到纤维 $R\otimes_A F$，因此它正则。由 \cite[Tag 0381]{StacksAlg}，它在 $F$ 上几何正则。这处理了第一个映射的每个纤维。}

\bi{For a prime $\mathfrak P\subset R$, put $C=R/\mathfrak P$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization} realizes $C$ as $\mathcal O_{Z,z}$ for an integral generalized affinoid $Z=\mathcal M(B)$, with $B\hookrightarrow C$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:charzerogenericgermregularity} gives $0\ne a\in B$ for which $\widehat C[1/a]$ is regular. Since $a$ stays nonzero in $C$, the fibre}
{对素理想 $\mathfrak P\subset R$，令 $C=R/\mathfrak P$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization} 将 $C$ 实现为整 generalized affinoid $Z=\mathcal M(B)$ 的某个 $\mathcal O_{Z,z}$，且 $B\hookrightarrow C$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:charzerogenericgermregularity}，存在 $0\ne a\in B$，使 $\widehat C[1/a]$ 正则。因为 $a$ 在 $C$ 中仍非零，纤维}
\[
             \widehat R\otimes_R\kappa(\mathfrak P)
                     =\widehat C\otimes_C\operatorname{Frac}(C)
\]
\bi{is a localization of a regular ring. The equality follows from finite-module completion, Tag 00MA. The fibre is geometrically regular over its characteristic-zero ground field by Tag 0381. This treats all primes of $R$ and completes the proof.}
{是正则环的局部化。等式来自有限模完备化公式 Tag 00MA。由 Tag 0381，该纤维在其特征零底域上几何正则。这处理了 $R$ 的全部素理想，完成证明。}
\end{proof}
