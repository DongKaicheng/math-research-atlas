Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Horizontal profile with both ends; F8AG-H]
\label{prop:profile}
Under CP, suppose
\(\eta=\chi(x)t_g^*\alpha\), where \(\alpha\) is a symmetric smooth
\((n-1,n-1)\)-form on the horizontal image and \(\chi\) is smooth
near \([a,b]\). Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:profile}
 \int_{A,\mathcal F}x\,\dd\eta
  =\left[(x\chi'(x)-\chi(x))\right]_{a}^{b}\int_P\alpha.
\end{equation}
This is the full Green identity for this profile. No inherited-boundary
vanishing is needed: its lateral flux is zero by tangential degree.
In particular, if \(\chi=1\) near \(a\) and \(\chi=0\) near \(b\),
the right side is \(+\int_P\alpha\).
\end{proposition}
