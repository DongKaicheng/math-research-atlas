Normal loci and quasi-smooth ascent in characteristic zero

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 337

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Normal loci and quasi-smooth ascent in characteristic zero}{特征零中的正规点集与准光滑上升}]
\label{fnd:charzeronormallocus}
\bi{Let $K$ be complete of characteristic zero. For an affinoid $X=\mathcal M(A)$ and $x\in X$, put $\mathfrak p=\ker(x)$. Then $A_{\mathfrak p}$ is normal if and only if $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is normal. The set of such points is a Zariski open subset of $X$. The normal-point set of a good $K$-analytic space is therefore ordinarily open. If $f:Y\to X$ is a morphism of good spaces, is quasi-smooth at $y$, and $X$ is normal at $f(y)$, then $Y$ is normal at $y$.}
{设 $K$ 完备且特征为零。对 affinoid 空间 $X=\mathcal M(A)$ 及点 $x\in X$，令 $\mathfrak p=\ker(x)$。则 $A_{\mathfrak p}$ 正规当且仅当 $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 正规；这样的点组成 $X$ 的 Zariski 开子集。因此，good $K$-解析空间的正规点集对普通拓扑开放。若 good 空间态射 $f:Y\to X$ 在 $y$ 准光滑，且 $X$ 在 $f(y)$ 正规，则 $Y$ 在 $y$ 正规。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The map $A_{\mathfrak p}\to\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is regular by Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroregularcomparison}. Regular rings are normal by \cite[Tag 0567]{StacksAlg}, so all its fibres are normal. Normality ascends by \cite[Tag 0C22]{StacksAlg}. Conversely suppose the target $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is normal. This faithfully flat local map is injective, so $A_{\mathfrak p}$ is a domain, since $R$ is a domain. If $u/v\in\operatorname{Frac}(A_{\mathfrak p})$ is integral over $A_{\mathfrak p}$, it is integral over $R$ and belongs to $R$. Thus $u\in vR$. Faithful flatness contracts the ideal $vR$ to $vA_{\mathfrak p}$: the class of $u$ in $A_{\mathfrak p}/vA_{\mathfrak p}$ becomes zero after tensoring with $R$, hence was already zero. Consequently $u/v\in A_{\mathfrak p}$, proving normality.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroregularcomparison}，映射 $A_{\mathfrak p}\to\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 正则。正则环由 \cite[Tag 0567]{StacksAlg} 正规，所以全部纤维正规；再由 \cite[Tag 0C22]{StacksAlg} 得正规性上升。反之，假设目标 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 正规。该忠实平坦局部映射单射，而 $R$ 为整环，所以 $A_{\mathfrak p}$ 也是整环。若 $u/v\in\operatorname{Frac}(A_{\mathfrak p})$ 在 $A_{\mathfrak p}$ 上整，则其在 $R$ 上也整，从而属于 $R$，故 $u\in vR$。忠实平坦性使理想 $vR$ 收缩为 $vA_{\mathfrak p}$：$u$ 在 $A_{\mathfrak p}/vA_{\mathfrak p}$ 中的类与 $R$ 张量后为零，故原来已为零。因此 $u/v\in A_{\mathfrak p}$，证明正规性。}

\bi{For openness, let $N$ be the algebraic normalization of $A$, namely the product of the integral closures of its finitely many minimal-prime quotients in their fraction fields. It is finite by Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroaffinoidquasiexcellence}. A localization $A_{\mathfrak p}$ is normal exactly when the map $A_{\mathfrak p}\to N_{\mathfrak p}$ is an isomorphism. Indeed normalization commutes with localization: clearing the finitely many denominators of a monic equation proves that integral closure does so, and the minimal-prime factors localize to the surviving components. If $A_{\mathfrak p}$ is normal it is reduced with one integral component and already integrally closed; conversely an isomorphism to the localization of $N$ makes it normal.}
{对开放性，令 $N$ 为 $A$ 的代数正规化，即其有限个极小素理想商在各自分式域中的整闭包的乘积。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroaffinoidquasiexcellence}，$N$ 在 $A$ 上有限。局部化 $A_{\mathfrak p}$ 正规当且仅当映射 $A_{\mathfrak p}\to N_{\mathfrak p}$ 为同构。事实上，正规化与局部化交换：清除首一方程中有限个分母即证明整闭包与局部化交换，而极小素理想的各因子局部化为仍存在的分量。若 $A_{\mathfrak p}$ 正规，则其约化、只有一个整分量且已整闭；反之，若同构于 $N$ 的局部化，它必正规。}

\bi{The kernel and cokernel of $A\to N$ are finite $A$-modules. Their supports are closed: for a finite module $M$, its localization at $\mathfrak p$ vanishes exactly when some element outside $\mathfrak p$ annihilates all members of a fixed finite generating set, equivalently when $\mathfrak p$ avoids $\operatorname{Ann}(M)$. Hence the normal locus of $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ is the complement of these two closed supports. Pulling this open set back by $x\mapsto\ker(x)$ gives a Zariski open subset of $\mathcal M(A)$; a principal open pulls back to $|a(x)|>0$. The normality equivalence already proved identifies it with the ordinary normal-point set. Affinoid ordinary neighborhoods then prove ordinary openness on every good space.}
{映射 $A\to N$ 的核与余核都是有限 $A$-模，其支撑为闭集：对有限模 $M$，其在 $\mathfrak p$ 处的局部化为零，当且仅当某个不属于 $\mathfrak p$ 的元素消灭固定有限生成组的每个成员，等价于 $\mathfrak p$ 不含 $\operatorname{Ann}(M)$。因此 $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ 的正规点集是这两个闭支撑的补集。沿 $x\mapsto\ker(x)$ 拉回，得到 $\mathcal M(A)$ 中的 Zariski 开集；其中主开集的拉回为 $|a(x)|>0$。前面证明的正规性等价将它识别为通常正规点集。在每个 good 空间上使用普通 affinoid 邻域，即得普通开放性。}

\bi{Finally Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroquasismoothordinaryregular} makes $\mathcal O_{X,f(y)}\to\mathcal O_{Y,y}$ regular. Its fibres are normal by Tag 0567. Applying Tag 0C22 gives the last assertion.}
{最后，Theorem~\ref{fnd:charzeroquasismoothordinaryregular} 使 $\mathcal O_{X,f(y)}\to\mathcal O_{Y,y}$ 正则，其纤维由 Tag 0567 正规，再应用 Tag 0C22 即得最后断言。}
\end{proof}
