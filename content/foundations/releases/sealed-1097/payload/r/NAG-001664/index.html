Intrinsic analytic dimension and scalar extension

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 600

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Intrinsic analytic dimension and scalar extension}
{内禀解析维数与扩标量}]
\label{fnd:analyticdimensionindependence}
\bi{Let $A$ be a nonzero generalized $K$-affinoid algebra.
For any complete extension $L/K$ making $A_L$ strict, the integer
$\dim A_L$ is independent of $L$. Denote it by $\dim_K\mathcal M(A)$.
For every complete extension $E/K$,}
{设 $A$ 为非零 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数。
对任意使 $A_L$ 为 strict 的完备扩域 $L/K$，整数 $\dim A_L$
都不依赖 $L$。记此整数为 $\dim_K\mathcal M(A)$。
对每个完备扩域 $E/K$，有}
\[
 \dim_E\mathcal M(A_E)=\dim_K\mathcal M(A).
\]
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose once a free strictification $F=K_s$ using
Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification}, and set
$d=\dim A_F$. Given another strictifying $L$, choose the common
complete extension $M$ of $F$ and $L$ in
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gausscommonextension}.
Associativity of completed affinoid scalar extension
\ref{fnd:iteratedaffinoidbasechange} identifies
$(A_F)_M$ and $(A_L)_M$ with $A_M$.
Both $A_F$ and $A_L$ are strict, so
Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictdimensionbasechange} gives}
{先用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:simultaneousstrictification} 固定一个自由
strictification $F=K_s$，令 $d=\dim A_F$。
给定另一个使代数 strict 的扩域 $L$，按
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gausscommonextension} 选择 $F$ 与 $L$ 的共同
完备扩域 $M$。完备 affinoid 扩标量的结合性
\ref{fnd:iteratedaffinoidbasechange} 将 $(A_F)_M$ 与 $(A_L)_M$
都识别为 $A_M$。由于 $A_F,A_L$ 都为 strict，
Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictdimensionbasechange} 给出}
\[
 d=\dim A_F=\dim A_M=\dim A_L.
\]
\bi{This proves independence, including strictifying extensions
which are not themselves affinoid over $K$.
For arbitrary $E/K$, choose a complete extension $N/E$ making
$(A_E)_N$ strict. It is $A_N$ by the same associativity, so its
dimension is $d$ by the independence just proved over $K$.
The definition over $E$ now gives the displayed equality.
Nonvanishing under all scalar extensions follows from
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalscalarfaithful}.}
{这证明了独立性，也包括那些本身不在 $K$ 上 affinoid 的
strictifying 扩域。对任意 $E/K$，选择使 $(A_E)_N$ strict
的完备扩域 $N/E$。同一结合性将它识别为 $A_N$，故由刚才在
$K$ 上证明的独立性，其维数为 $d$。再使用 $E$ 上的定义，
即得所列等式。任意扩标量后仍非零，由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalscalarfaithful} 保证。}
\end{proof}
