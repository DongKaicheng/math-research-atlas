Independent differentials of free coordinates

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 612

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Independent differentials of free coordinates}
{自由坐标的微分线性无关}]
\label{fnd:freegaussdifferentialindependence}
\bi{Let $Y$ be a $K$-analytic space and let $y\in Y$
with an isometric $K$-identification $\mathcal H(y)=K_r$,
where $r=(r_1,\ldots,r_m)$ is free over $K$.
If analytic functions $g_i$ near $y$ satisfy
$|g_i(y)|=r_i$, then}
{设 $Y$ 为 $K$-解析空间，$y\in Y$，且有等距的
$K$-识别 $\mathcal H(y)=K_r$，其中
$r=(r_1,\ldots,r_m)$ 在 $K$ 上自由。
若 $y$ 附近的解析函数 $g_i$ 满足 $|g_i(y)|=r_i$，
则}
\[
              (dg_1)(y),\ldots,(dg_m)(y)
              \quad\hbox{in}\quad
              \Omega^{\rm an}_{Y/K}\otimes\mathcal H(y)
\]
\bi{are linearly independent. In particular this applies
to quasi-smooth spaces.}
{线性无关。特别地，这适用于准光滑空间。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose an affinoid domain containing $y$ on which the
functions are represented, and write its algebra as $A$.
By Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialdomains}, its
differential fibre is the asserted one.
Set $S_i=g_i(y)$. The preceding lemma makes $S_i$
coordinates of $K_r$. On a Laurent series in these coordinates
the operator}
{选择一个包含 $y$ 的 affinoid 域，使函数均在其上表示，
并记其代数为 $A$。由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialdomains}，
其微分纤维就是所需的纤维。置 $S_i=g_i(y)$。
上一引理说明 $S_i$ 构成 $K_r$ 的坐标。在这些坐标的
Laurent 级数上，算子}
\[
 D_j\left(\sum_{\nu\in\mathbf Z^m} a_\nu S^\nu\right)
       =\sum_\nu \nu_j a_\nu S^{\nu-e_j}
\]
\bi{is bounded with norm at most $r_j^{-1}$, since every
integer has absolute value at most one. The weighted
coefficients still tend to zero, so the formula defines
a continuous operator. The Leibniz identity on Laurent
polynomials extends by continuity; hence $D_j$ is a bounded
$K$-derivation and $D_j(S_i)=\delta_{ij}$.}
{有界，范数至多为 $r_j^{-1}$，因为每个整数的绝对值至多为一。
加权系数仍趋于零，所以此公式定义连续算子。
Laurent 多项式上的 Leibniz 恒等式由连续性延伸，故
$D_j$ 是有界 $K$-导子，且 $D_j(S_i)=\delta_{ij}$。}

\bi{Compose $D_j$ with the bounded evaluation
$A\to\mathcal H(y)=K_r$. The resulting bounded derivation
from $A$ to the complete Banach $A$-module $K_r$ is
represented by Theorem~\ref{fnd:boundeduniversaldifferentials}.
After tensoring with $K_r$, it gives a $K_r$-linear functional
on the differential fibre taking $(dg_i)(y)$ to
$\delta_{ij}$. These $m$ functionals form a left inverse
to the map with columns $(dg_i)(y)$, proving independence.}
{将 $D_j$ 与有界取值映射
$A\to\mathcal H(y)=K_r$ 复合。所得从 $A$ 到完备
Banach $A$-模 $K_r$ 的有界导子，由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:boundeduniversaldifferentials} 代表。
与 $K_r$ 张量后，它给出微分纤维上的 $K_r$-线性泛函，
将 $(dg_i)(y)$ 送到 $\delta_{ij}$。
这 $m$ 个泛函为以 $(dg_i)(y)$ 为列的映射提供左逆，
因此这些微分线性无关。}
\end{proof}
