Power presentations for rational inequalities

\begin{proposition}[\bi{Power presentations for rational inequalities}{Rational 不等式的幂表示}]\label{fnd:radicalrationalpresentation}
\bi{Let $(g,f_1,\ldots,f_s)=A$ and let $r_i\in\Gamma_K^{\mathrm{div}}$. Choose a common integer $n\geq1$ and $c_i\in K^\times$ with $|c_i|=r_i^n$. Then}
{设 $(g,f_1,\ldots,f_s)=A$，且 $r_i\in\Gamma_K^{\mathrm{div}}$。选择共同整数 $n\geq1$ 及 $c_i\in K^\times$，使 $|c_i|=r_i^n$。则}
\[
 Q=A\{r^{-1}T\}/(gT_i-f_i)_i,
 \qquad
 P=A\{W_1,\ldots,W_s\}/(g^nW_i-c_i^{-1}f_i^n)_i
\]
\bi{are canonically isomorphic by bounded inverse $A$-algebra maps. The tuple defining $P$ generates the unit ideal. Thus $Q$ is a strict affinoid, and its spectrum identifies homeomorphically with}
{之间存在典范的有界互逆 $A$-代数映射。定义 $P$ 的 tuple 生成单位理想。因此 $Q$ 为 strict affinoid，且其谱同胚识别为}
\[
 \{x\in\mathcal M(A):|f_i(x)|\leq r_i|g(x)|\text{ for all }i\}.
\]
\bi{It has the strict-target domain universal property of Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaldomainup}. These conclusions concern the stated actual quotient norms, with bounded comparison; an isometry with the power presentation is not asserted.}
{它具有 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaldomainup} 的 strict 目标域泛性质。这些结论涉及所述实际商范数，比较映射有界；并不声称它与幂表示之间等距。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Raise a unit-ideal identity for $(g,f_1,\ldots,f_s)$ to the exponent $(s+1)(n-1)+1$. In every term, at least one of these $s+1$ elements occurs to an exponent at least $n$. Thus $(g^n,f_1^n,\ldots,f_s^n)=A$, and multiplying individual entries by $c_i^{-1}$ preserves that ideal. Lemma~\ref{fnd:radicalradiusaffinoid} makes $Q$ a strict actual quotient; the ordinary strict quotient theorem does the same for $P$. In $Q$ the element $g$ is a unit by the original certificate. Sending $W_i$ to $T_i^n/c_i$, which has norm at most one, gives a contractive map $P\to Q$.}
{将 $(g,f_1,\ldots,f_s)$ 的单位理想恒等式取 $(s+1)(n-1)+1$ 次幂。每一项中，这 $s+1$ 个元素至少有一个出现次数不小于 $n$，故 $(g^n,f_1^n,\ldots,f_s^n)=A$。将相应元素乘以 $c_i^{-1}$ 不改变这一理想。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:radicalradiusaffinoid}，$Q$ 为 strict 实际商；通常的 strict 商定理对 $P$ 给出相同结论。由原单位理想证书，$g$ 在 $Q$ 中为单位。将 $W_i$ 送到范数至多为一的 $T_i^n/c_i$，便得到 contractive 映射 $P\to Q$。}

\bi{In $P$, its unit-ideal certificate makes $g^n$, and hence $g$, a unit. Put $b_i=f_i/g$. The relation gives $b_i^n=c_iW_i$. If $m=qn+j$ with $0\leq j<n$, then}
{在 $P$ 中，由其单位理想证书可知 $g^n$ 为单位，因而 $g$ 也为单位。令 $b_i=f_i/g$，则定义关系给出 $b_i^n=c_iW_i$。若 $m=qn+j$、$0\leq j<n$，则}
\[
 \|b_i^m\|\leq |c_i|^q\|b_i^j\|
    \leq C_i r_i^m,
 \qquad C_i=\max_{0\leq j<n}r_i^{-j}\|b_i^j\|.
\]
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundedsubstitution} therefore defines a bounded map $A\{r^{-1}T\}\to P$ sending $T_i$ to $b_i$. It kills the actual relation ideal and descends boundedly to $Q$ by taking infima over lifts. The two composites fix the variables: in $P$, $f_i^n/(c_ig^n)=W_i$, and in $Q$, $f_i/g=T_i$. Polynomial density proves that they are inverse. Zero targets and zero quotients satisfy the same conclusion directly.}
{因此由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:powerboundedsubstitution}，得到将 $T_i$ 送到 $b_i$ 的有界映射 $A\{r^{-1}T\}\to P$。它零化实际关系理想，对提升取下确界后便有界地下降到 $Q$。两个复合固定变量：在 $P$ 中有 $f_i^n/(c_ig^n)=W_i$，在 $Q$ 中有 $f_i/g=T_i$。多项式稠密性证明两映射互逆。零目标与零商的结论直接成立。}

\bi{The point theorem and the strict-target universal property for the unit-radius presentation $P$ now apply. Its inequalities are $|f_i|^n\leq|c_i|\,|g|^n$, exactly the displayed inequalities for $Q$. Transferring across the bounded inverse isomorphism proves both assertions. In particular, every such domain also has a unit-radius rational presentation over the original field.}
{现在可对单位半径表示 $P$ 应用谱点定理及 strict 目标泛性质。它的不等式为 $|f_i|^n\leq|c_i|\,|g|^n$，恰好等价于 $Q$ 的所列不等式。通过有界互逆同构传递，即得两个断言。特别地，每个这样的域在原基域上都具有单位半径 rational 表示。}
\end{proof}
