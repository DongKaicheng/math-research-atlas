Subdomains of a good germ

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 568

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Subdomains of a good germ}
{Good 芽的子域}]
\label{fnd:goodgermdomainclassification}
\bi{Reduction gives an order-preserving bijection between
subdomain germs of a good germ $(X,x)$ and nonempty
quasi-compact open subspaces of $\mathscr R(X,x)$.
It preserves intersections, finite unions and inverse images.}
{约化在 good 芽 $(X,x)$ 的子域芽与
$\mathscr R(X,x)$ 的非空拟紧开子空间之间给出保持包含关系
的双射，并保持交、有限并及逆像。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Every quasi-compact open is a finite union of basic opens
$\mathscr R(X,x)\{\lambda_{i1},\ldots,\lambda_{in_i}\}$.
Lift the nonzero homogeneous $\lambda_{ij}$ to invertible
analytic functions near $x$, by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedweierstrassgerm}, and impose the
corresponding inequalities with radii $|f_{ij}(x)|$.
These finitely many closed domains in a common affinoid
representative give a finite quasi-net on their union by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasinetcalculus}. The reduction of that union
is the prescribed one by Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermcovercomparison}.
This proves surjectivity.}
{每个拟紧开集都是有限个基本开集
$\mathscr R(X,x)\{\lambda_{i1},\ldots,\lambda_{in_i}\}$ 的并。
利用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedweierstrassgerm}，将非零齐次
$\lambda_{ij}$ 提升为 $x$ 附近的可逆解析函数，并施加半径为
$|f_{ij}(x)|$ 的相应不等式。在共同 affinoid 代表中，由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasinetcalculus}，这些闭域在其并上构成有限
quasi-net。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermcovercomparison}，
此并的约化就是所指定的约化。这证明满射性。}

\bi{First suppose a subdomain germ $Y\subset X$ has full
reduction. Refine $Y$ by finitely many active Weierstrass
germs $Y_i$, using Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedweierstrassgerm}.
Their reductions cover $\mathscr R(X,x)$ and therefore
admit a Laurent refinement by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedrzlaurentrefinement}.
Lift the finitely many homogeneous Laurent functions to
invertible analytic functions near $x$.
The resulting Laurent domains $V_\epsilon$ are actual joint
domains by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints} and
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedjointpresentations}. Each opposite
inequality is active at $x$, and
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedweierstrassgerm} computes its reduction.
The Laurent domains cover a neighbourhood of $x$ in $X$.
Each reduction is contained in the reduction of some $Y_i$, so
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedgermmonicinclusion} gives
$V_\epsilon\subset Y_i$ as germs.
There are only finitely many cells, so one common shrinking
therefore makes their whole union lie in $Y$.
Thus $Y=X$ as germs.}
{先设子域芽 $Y\subset X$ 具有全约化。
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedweierstrassgerm}，将 $Y$ 加细为
有限个有效 Weierstrass 芽 $Y_i$。其约化覆盖
$\mathscr R(X,x)$，因此由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedrzlaurentrefinement} 具有 Laurent 加细。
将有限个齐次 Laurent 函数提升为 $x$ 附近的可逆解析函数。
由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints} 和
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedjointpresentations}，所得 Laurent 域
$V_\epsilon$ 是实际的联合域。每个方向的不等式在 $x$ 处均有效，
其约化由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedweierstrassgerm} 计算。
这些 Laurent 域覆盖 $X$ 中 $x$ 的邻域。每个片的约化包含于
某个 $Y_i$ 的约化，因此 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedgermmonicinclusion}
给出芽包含 $V_\epsilon\subset Y_i$。片数有限，故可作一个共同缩小，
使其整个并位于 $Y$。于是 $Y=X$ 作为芽成立。}

\bi{Now suppose $\mathscr R(Y,x)\subseteq\mathscr R(Z,x)$.
Cover $Y$ and $Z$ by finitely many affinoid subdomain germs
$Y_i$ and $Z_j$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermcovercomparison}
shows that the reductions of the $Y_i\cap Z_j$, for fixed
$i$, cover the reduction of $Y_i$.
The preceding full-reduction assertion, applied inside the
good germ $Y_i$, implies $Y_i\subset Z$.
Thus $Y\subset Z$. This proves injectivity and order
reflection. The compatibility assertions are already
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermcovercomparison}.}
{现设 $\mathscr R(Y,x)\subseteq\mathscr R(Z,x)$。
分别用有限个 affinoid 子域芽 $Y_i,Z_j$ 覆盖 $Y,Z$。
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermcovercomparison} 说明，对固定
$i$，各 $Y_i\cap Z_j$ 的约化覆盖 $Y_i$ 的约化。
在 good 芽 $Y_i$ 中应用刚证的全约化断言，得到
$Y_i\subset Z$，因而 $Y\subset Z$。这证明单射性及包含
关系的反映性。各相容性已经由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:gradedgermcovercomparison} 给出。}
\end{proof}
