A zero-kernel point above a polydisc reduction point

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 531

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A zero-kernel point above a polydisc reduction point}
{多圆盘约化点上方的零核点}]
\label{fnd:polydiscprimecenter}
\bi{Assume $K$ is nontrivially valued. For every prime
$\mathfrak p\subset\widetilde K[Y_1,\ldots,Y_d]$, there is
a bounded multiplicative norm on $K\langle Y\rangle$ whose
reduction kernel is $\mathfrak p$.}
{假设 $K$ 的赋值非平凡。对每个素理想
$\mathfrak p\subset\widetilde K[Y_1,\ldots,Y_d]$，存在
$K\langle Y\rangle$ 上的有界乘性范数，其约化核为 $\mathfrak p$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $l=\operatorname{Frac}(\widetilde K[Y]/\mathfrak p)$
and realize it in $\widetilde L$ by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:residuefieldrealization}.
Choose $a_i\in L^\circ$ lifting the images of $Y_i$.
In $L\langle U_1,\ldots,U_d\rangle$ substitute
$Y_i=a_i+\pi U_i$ and take the Gauss norm.
The resulting homomorphism is injective: scalar extension
$K\langle Y\rangle\hookrightarrow L\langle Y\rangle$ is
coefficientwise injective, translation by the $a_i$ is an
isometric automorphism of the latter Tate algebra, and
$Y_i\mapsto\pi U_i$ is coefficientwise injective.
The map is contractive and the Gauss norm multiplicative.
Modulo elements of norm less than one, the substitution is
$Y_i\mapsto\widetilde a_i$, whose kernel is exactly
$\mathfrak p$. This gives the required norm with zero kernel.}
{令 $l=\operatorname{Frac}(\widetilde K[Y]/\mathfrak p)$，
用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:residuefieldrealization} 将它实现于
$\widetilde L$ 中，并选取 $a_i\in L^\circ$ 提升 $Y_i$ 的像。
在 $L\langle U_1,\ldots,U_d\rangle$ 中作代换
$Y_i=a_i+\pi U_i$，再取 Gauss 范数。
所得同态为单射：系数扩张
$K\langle Y\rangle\hookrightarrow L\langle Y\rangle$
逐系数单射；按 $a_i$ 平移是后一个 Tate 代数的等距自同构；
而 $Y_i\mapsto\pi U_i$ 仍逐系数单射。
整个映射为压缩映射，且 Gauss 范数乘性。模去范数严格小于一的
元素后，代换成为 $Y_i\mapsto\widetilde a_i$，核恰为
$\mathfrak p$。这就得到所需的零核范数。}
\end{proof}
