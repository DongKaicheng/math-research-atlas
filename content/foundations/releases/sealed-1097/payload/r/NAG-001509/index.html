Dense image and a Weierstrass factorization

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 222

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Dense image and a Weierstrass factorization}{稠密像与 Weierstrass 分解}]\label{fnd:strictdenserunge}
\bi{For a bounded homomorphism $\phi:A\to B$ of strict $K$-affinoids, the following are equivalent: $\phi(A)$ is dense in $B$; and there are finitely many $a_1,\ldots,a_n\in A$ and a bounded surjection}
{对 strict $K$-affinoid 之间的有界同态 $\phi:A\to B$，以下两条等价：$\phi(A)$ 在 $B$ 中稠密；存在有限多个 $a_1,\ldots,a_n\in A$ 及有界满射}
\[
 A\langle a_1,\ldots,a_n\rangle
    :=A\{X_1,\ldots,X_n\}/(X_i-a_i)_i
       \twoheadrightarrow B
\]
\bi{whose composite with $A$ is $\phi$. Thus density supplies the algebraic factorization called a Runge immersion in the classical terminology, without using a stalk criterion.}
{使其与 $A$ 的结构映射的复合等于 $\phi$。因此，稠密性给出经典术语中称为 Runge immersion 的代数分解，而不使用茎的判据。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose a relative presentation $\pi_0:A\langle X\rangle\twoheadrightarrow B$ by Lemma~\ref{fnd:relativeaffinoidpresentation}, and use its quotient norm on $B$. This replaces its original norm by a uniformly equivalent one. If $\phi(A)$ is dense, choose $a_i\in A$ so that $\|\pi_0(X_i)-\phi(a_i)\|<1$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:affinoidgeneratorperturbation} makes substitution $X_i\mapsto\phi(a_i)$ surjective. It kills each $X_i-a_i$ and hence factors through the displayed actual quotient as a bounded surjection.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:relativeaffinoidpresentation} 选择相对表示 $\pi_0:A\langle X\rangle\twoheadrightarrow B$，并在 $B$ 上使用其商范数；这只是将原范数换成一致等价的范数。若 $\phi(A)$ 稠密，可选取 $a_i\in A$，使 $\|\pi_0(X_i)-\phi(a_i)\|<1$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:affinoidgeneratorperturbation}，代入 $X_i\mapsto\phi(a_i)$ 为满射。它零化每个 $X_i-a_i$，因而通过所列实际商作有界满射分解。}

\bi{Conversely, in that quotient every polynomial in the variables is the image of an element of $A$, because $X_i=a_i$. Polynomial density therefore makes $A$ dense in the Weierstrass quotient. A bounded surjection from it to $B$ sends this dense image to a dense subset of $B$: approximate any preimage of a given element. Thus $\phi(A)$ is dense. The argument includes zero algebras and the empty list of variables.}
{反之，在该商中有 $X_i=a_i$，所以每个变量多项式都是 $A$ 中某个元素的像。由多项式稠密性，$A$ 的像在 Weierstrass 商中稠密。由该商到 $B$ 的有界满射将此像送到 $B$ 的稠密子集：对给定元素的任意一个原像作逼近即可。因此 $\phi(A)$ 稠密。论证包括零代数及空变量列。}
\end{proof}
