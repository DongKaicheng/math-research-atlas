Domain base change can destroy naive flatness

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 479

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Domain base change can destroy naive flatness}
{解析域基变换可以破坏朴素平坦性}]
\label{fnd:naivecounterexampleinternal}
\bi{Choose $\pi\in K$ with $0<|\pi|<1$ and set}
{选择 $\pi\in K$，满足 $0<|\pi|<1$，并令}
\[
 f(T)=\sum_{n\geq0}\pi^{\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor}T^n,
 \qquad\phi:D\longrightarrow\mathbb A_K^{2,\mathrm{an}},
 \qquad t\longmapsto(t,f(t)).
\]
\bi{The morphism $\phi$ is naively flat at the unit Gauss
point $\eta$. Its base change by the analytic domain
$X=\{|T_1|\leq1\}$ is not naively flat at that point.}
{态射 $\phi$ 在单位 Gauss 点 $\eta$ 处朴素平坦。
沿解析域 $X=\{|T_1|\leq1\}$ 的基变换，在同一点处不朴素平坦。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The coefficients tend to zero, whereas their $n$th-root
absolute values tend to one. Thus $f\in K\langle T\rangle$
has convergence radius exactly one.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:exactradiusgraphfield} makes the ordinary
target local ring at $x=\phi(\eta)$ a field. Every module
over a field is flat, so $\phi$ is naively flat at $\eta$.}
{这些系数趋于零，但其绝对值的 $n$ 次方根趋于一，所以
$f\in K\langle T\rangle$ 的收敛半径恰好为一。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:exactradiusgraphfield} 说明目标在
$x=\phi(\eta)$ 处的通常局部环为域。域上的每个模均平坦，
因此 $\phi$ 在 $\eta$ 处朴素平坦。}

\bi{Because $\phi$ factors through the domain $X$, its
base change to $X$ is its closed graph immersion, with
equation $g=T_2-f(T_1)$. The ordinary local ring
$R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is a domain by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomainregular} and
\cite[Tag 00NP]{StacksAlg}, using an affinoid neighborhood
of $x$ in $X$. The germ $g$ is nonzero: differentiation
in the second coordinate gives $\partial g/\partial T_2=1$.
This derivation on convergent series restricts to the germ
by Theorem~\ref{fnd:boundeduniversaldifferentials} and
Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialdomains}.
It vanishes at $x$, so $R/(g)$ is nonzero.
Tensoring the injective multiplication map
$R\xrightarrow{g}R$ with $R/(g)$ gives the zero map on
the nonzero module $R/(g)$. This quotient is therefore not
$R$-flat. The graph local ring is exactly $R/(g)$ by the
coherent closed-subspace restriction formula, proving the assertion.}
{因为 $\phi$ 因子分解经过解析域 $X$，它到 $X$ 的基变换
就是其闭图像浸入，方程为 $g=T_2-f(T_1)$。
在 $X$ 中取 $x$ 的 affinoid 邻域，应用
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomainregular} 及
\cite[Tag 00NP]{StacksAlg}，得到通常局部环
$R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ 为整环。
芽 $g$ 非零，因为对第二坐标求导得到
$\partial g/\partial T_2=1$。
根据 Theorem~\ref{fnd:boundeduniversaldifferentials} 与
Theorem~\ref{fnd:analyticdifferentialdomains}，此收敛级数
上的导子可限制到通常芽。另一方面，$g$ 在 $x$ 处消失，
故 $R/(g)$ 非零。将单射乘法映射
$R\xrightarrow{g}R$ 与 $R/(g)$ 张量，得到非零模
$R/(g)$ 上的零映射。因此此商不在 $R$ 上平坦。
凝聚闭子空间的限制公式将图像局部环恰好识别为 $R/(g)$，
于是得到结论。}
\end{proof}
