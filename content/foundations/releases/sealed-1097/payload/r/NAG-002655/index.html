Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[A fixed-test substitute; F8SC-L]
\label{lem:localize}
Let \(X\) be good Hausdorff and pure of dimension \(n\), let \(f\) be
regular analytic, and let
\(\eta\in\A_c^{n-1,n-1}(X)\), with compact support \(K_0\) in
\(\Int(X/K)\). Let \(B\) be a closed analytic set such that \(X\setminus B\)
satisfies \(S_1\). Assume
\[
 \dim(D\cap B)\leq n-2,\qquad
 \supp\eta\cap(D\cap B)=\varnothing,                                \tag{4.2}
\]
where \(D=\{f=0\}\); for \(n=1\), require \(D\cap B=\varnothing\).
Let \(W\) be an ordinary open neighbourhood of \(K_0\cap D\) on
which \(f\) is pure of relative dimension \(n-1\).
There is a smooth cutoff \(\chi\), equal to one near \(D\), such that
\(\chi\eta\) has compact support in \(W\setminus B\), and
\((1-\chi)\eta\) has compact support in \(X\setminus D\).
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The compact set \(K_0\cap B\) is disjoint from \(D\), as is
\(K_0\setminus W\). On either nonempty set the continuous function
\(|f|\) has a positive minimum. Choose \(r>0\) below both minima,
ignoring an empty set. Choose a smooth function of \(\log|f|\)
equal to one for sufficiently small \(|f|\), and zero for
\(|f|\geq r'<r\). Extend it by one across \(D\).
This is an ordinary smooth scalar: it is constant near \(D\), and
off \(D\) it uses the unit \(f\).
Its two products with \(\eta\) have the asserted compact supports.
\end{proof}
