Residual dimension under free scalar extension

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 603

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Residual dimension under free scalar extension}
{自由扩标量时的剩余维数}]
\label{fnd:freegaussresidualdimension}
\bi{Let $A$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra, let $r$ be a
finite tuple of $m$ radii free over $K$, and put $L=K_r$.
For $x\in\mathcal M(A)$ and its Gauss lift $\sigma_r(x)$,}
{设 $A$ 为广义 $K$-affinoid 代数，$r$ 为在 $K$ 上自由的
$m$ 个半径组成的有限元组，令 $L=K_r$。
对 $x\in\mathcal M(A)$ 及其 Gauss 提升 $\sigma_r(x)$，有}
\[
 d_L(\sigma_r(x))=d_K(x).
\]
\bi{For every $y\in\mathcal M_L(A_L)$ above $x$,}
{对 $x$ 上方的每个 $y\in\mathcal M_L(A_L)$，有}
\[
 d_L(y)\le d_K(x).
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Write $F=\mathcal H_A(x)$ and $E=\mathcal H_{A_L}(y)$.
The residue-field universal property embeds $F$ and $L$
isometrically in $E$. Denote the images of the $m$ Gauss
parameters by $t_1,\ldots,t_m$. The field $E$ is the completion
of $F(t_1,\ldots,t_m)$ with the induced absolute value.
Here the density assertion is essential: finite Laurent
polynomials with coefficients in $A$ are dense in $A_L$ by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients}; bounded evaluation
therefore puts the entire image of $A_L$ in the closure of
$F(t_1,\ldots,t_m)$. Conversely the completion defining $E$
contains $F$, the parameters, and the closure of their fraction
field. Inverting nonzero limits is continuous, so these two
closures are the same complete valued field.}
{记 $F=\mathcal H_A(x)$、$E=\mathcal H_{A_L}(y)$。
剩余域的泛性质将 $F,L$ 等距嵌入 $E$。记 $m$ 个 Gauss
参数的像为 $t_1,\ldots,t_m$。域 $E$ 是
$F(t_1,\ldots,t_m)$ 关于诱导绝对值的完备化。
这里必须核验稠密性：由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients}，$A$-系数的有限
Laurent 多项式在 $A_L$ 中稠密；有界求值因而将 $A_L$ 的
整个像置于 $F(t_1,\ldots,t_m)$ 的闭包中。反过来，定义 $E$
的完备化包含 $F$、这些参数及其分式域的闭包。
非零极限的求逆连续，所以这两个闭包为同一个完备赋值域。}

\bi{Lemma~\ref{fnd:valuedfieldabhyankar} gives $d(E/F)\le m$.
The free-polyradius field $L$ is the completion of the Gauss
rational field over $K$: the unique dominant term expands every
rational inverse as a convergent Laurent geometric series.
Thus Lemma~\ref{fnd:gaussvaluedfielddimension} gives $d(L/K)=m$.
The two tower formulas yield}
{Lemma~\ref{fnd:valuedfieldabhyankar} 给出 $d(E/F)\le m$。
自由多半径域 $L$ 是 $K$ 上 Gauss 有理函数域的完备化：
唯一占优项使每个有理逆元展开为收敛的 Laurent 几何级数。
因此 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gaussvaluedfielddimension} 给出
$d(L/K)=m$。两个塔式等式给出}
\[
 m+d(E/L)=d(E/K)=d(F/K)+d(E/F).
\]
\bi{If $d(F/K)$ is infinite, the asserted inequality is automatic;
otherwise all terms are finite and cancellation of $m$ proves it.}
{若 $d(F/K)$ 无限，则所需不等式自动成立；否则所有项均有限，
消去 $m$ 即得不等式。}

\bi{At the canonical point $\sigma_r(x)$ the norm of a finite
Laurent polynomial is precisely its weighted Gauss norm over
$F$, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusgausssection}.
The same density argument identifies its completed residue field
with the completion of $F(T_1,\ldots,T_m)$ for that norm.
In particular the parameters are algebraically independent here,
even if their radii are no longer free over $F$.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gaussvaluedfielddimension} gives $d(E/F)=m$,
and the displayed tower equality proves
$d(E/L)=d(F/K)$, with infinity allowed.}
{在典范点 $\sigma_r(x)$ 处，由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusgausssection}，有限 Laurent
多项式的范数正是在 $F$ 上的加权 Gauss 范数。
相同的稠密性论证将其完备剩余域识别为
$F(T_1,\ldots,T_m)$ 关于此范数的完备化。
特别地，这里的参数代数无关，即使其半径在 $F$ 上不再自由。
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gaussvaluedfielddimension} 给出 $d(E/F)=m$，
所列塔式等式遂给出 $d(E/L)=d(F/K)$，也允许无穷大的情形。}
\end{proof}
