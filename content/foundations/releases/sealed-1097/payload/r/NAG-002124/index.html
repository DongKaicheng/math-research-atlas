\begin{theorem}[\bi{Split marked models over a complete discrete field}
{完备离散赋值域上的分裂标记模型}]
\label{cand:allgenusmarkeddvrmodel}
\bi{Let $K$ be a complete nontrivially discretely valued
field of characteristic zero. Let $C/K$ be a smooth
projective geometrically connected curve, and let $D$
be a finite reduced closed subscheme of $C$. There is
a finite Galois extension $L/K$ such that $D_L$ is a
disjoint union of $L$-rational points and $C_L$ has a
regular projective split nodal $L^\circ$-model on which
their sections are disjoint and smooth. If $g(C)>0$,
the model may be chosen with a semilinear
$\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$-action extending the given
action on $C_L$ and permuting these sections.
All absolute values extend the specified one on $K$.}
{设 $K$ 为特征零的非平凡离散赋值完备域，
$C/K$ 为光滑射影几何连通曲线，$D$ 为 $C$ 的有限
约化闭子概形。存在有限 Galois 扩张 $L/K$，
使 $D_L$ 为 $L$-有理点的不交并，且 $C_L$ 有一个
正则射影分裂结点 $L^\circ$-模型，相关标记截面
两两不交并经过光滑点。若 $g(C)>0$，可选模型使之
具有延拓 $C_L$ 上给定作用的半线性
$\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$-作用，并置换这些截面。
全部绝对值均延拓 $K$ 上指定的绝对值。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{After extension to an algebraic closure, smoothness
makes all local rings regular, so distinct irreducible
components cannot meet. Geometric connectedness therefore
makes $C$ geometrically integral.
By \cite[Tag 04L2(4)]{StacksAlg},
$H^0(C,\mathcal O_C)=K$.
Apply the all-genus semistable reduction theorem
\cite[Tag 0CDN]{StacksAlg}. Its extension of discrete
valuation rings is the ring of integers for the unique
extending absolute value: if $\varpi_0$ is a uniformizer
of $K^\circ$ and its order in the extension ring is
$e>0$, the formula
$|b|'=|\varpi_0|^{\operatorname{ord}(b)/e}$
extends the given absolute value. Uniqueness in
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms} identifies it with
the fixed extension. Finite extension preserves a
cyclic nontrivial value group by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitevaluedindexbound}.}
{扩张到代数闭包后，光滑性使全部局部环正则，
故不同不可约分量不能相交。几何连通性于是说明
$C$ 几何整。由 \cite[Tag 04L2(4)]{StacksAlg}，
$H^0(C,\mathcal O_C)=K$。
应用全亏格半稳定约化定理
\cite[Tag 0CDN]{StacksAlg}。其中的离散赋值环扩张
就是唯一延拓绝对值的整数环：若 $\varpi_0$ 为
$K^\circ$ 的一致化参数，在扩张环中的阶为 $e>0$，
则公式
$|b|'=|\varpi_0|^{\operatorname{ord}(b)/e}$
延拓给定绝对值。Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteextensionnorms}
中的唯一性将它识别为固定的延拓。由
Lemma~\ref{fnd:finitevaluedindexbound}，有限扩张
保持值群非平凡且循环。}

\bi{Enlarge this field to a finite Galois extension
$L_0/K$ also splitting all residue fields of $D$.
These fields are separable since $K$ has characteristic
zero. The base-changed semistable model remains nodal.
Choose a minimal regular proper model $\mathcal M$
over $L_0^\circ$, using \cite[Tag 0C2W]{StacksAlg}.
It is nodal by \cite[Tag 0CDG]{StacksAlg}, and it is
projective by \cite[Tag 0C5P]{StacksAlg}, applied to its
regular two-dimensional total space over the Noetherian
base $\operatorname{Spec}L_0^\circ$.}
{将该域扩大为有限 Galois 扩张 $L_0/K$，并使 $D$
的全部剩余域分裂。由 $K$ 特征零，这些域扩张均
可分。半稳定模型基变换后仍为结点型。
由 \cite[Tag 0C2W]{StacksAlg}，选取
$L_0^\circ$ 上的极小正则 proper 模型 $\mathcal M$。
由 \cite[Tag 0CDG]{StacksAlg}，它为结点型；
再对 Noetherian 基底
$\operatorname{Spec}L_0^\circ$ 上的正则二维全空间
应用 \cite[Tag 0C5P]{StacksAlg}，可知它射影。}

\bi{Use Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting}
on the finitely many nodes of $\mathcal M_s$, and
take a finite Galois normal closure $L/K$ of the
resulting extension. Split rational node equations
remain split rational node equations after this
further base change. Thus
$\mathcal M\times_{L_0^\circ}L^\circ$ is a projective
split nodal model. Apply
Lemma~\ref{cand:equivariantnodaldvrresolution} and then
Lemma~\ref{cand:separatemarkedsectionsdvr}.
The result has all the claimed geometric properties.}
{对 $\mathcal M_s$ 的有限个结点应用
Theorem~\ref{cand:simultaneousnodalsplitting}，
再取所得扩张在 $K$ 上的有限 Galois 正规闭包 $L$。
分裂有理结点方程在进一步基变换后仍为分裂有理
结点方程，因此
$\mathcal M\times_{L_0^\circ}L^\circ$ 为射影分裂
结点模型。依次应用
Lemma~\ref{cand:equivariantnodaldvrresolution}
及 Lemma~\ref{cand:separatemarkedsectionsdvr}，
便得到全部所述几何性质。}

\bi{Suppose $g(C)>0$. The unique minimal model theorem
\cite[Tag 0C6B]{StacksAlg} extends the generic
semilinear $\operatorname{Gal}(L_0/K)$-action to
$\mathcal M$: apply uniqueness to $\mathcal M$ and
each coefficient twist with the prescribed generic
identification. The extensions are unique, so their
compositions satisfy the group law. Since $L_0/K$
and $L/K$ are Galois, this gives a semilinear action
of $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ on
$\mathcal M\times_{L_0^\circ}L^\circ$ by restriction
on the first factor and its natural action on the
second. Both subsequent constructions are equivariant
by the two preceding lemmas.}
{若 $g(C)>0$，极小模型唯一性定理
\cite[Tag 0C6B]{StacksAlg} 将泛纤维上的半线性
$\operatorname{Gal}(L_0/K)$-作用延拓到 $\mathcal M$：
对 $\mathcal M$ 与其每个系数扭转，配以指定的泛纤维
识别，应用唯一性。延拓唯一，故其复合满足群律。
由于 $L_0/K$ 与 $L/K$ 均为 Galois 扩张，
$\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ 在第一因子上通过限制作用，
在第二因子上自然作用，从而半线性作用于
$\mathcal M\times_{L_0^\circ}L^\circ$。
由前两个引理，随后两个构造均等变。}
\end{proof}
