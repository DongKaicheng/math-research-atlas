The generic fibre of a formal line bundle

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 591

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{The generic fibre of a formal line bundle}
{形式线丛的泛纤维}]
\label{fnd:formallinebundlegeneric}
\bi{An invertible sheaf $\mathfrak L$ on an admissible formal
scheme $\mathfrak X$ has a canonical invertible
$\mathcal O_{X_G}$-module $\mathfrak L_\eta$ on
$X=\mathfrak X_\eta$. This construction commutes with tensor
products, duals and formal pullback. Its formal transition
functions have absolute value one.}
{Admissible 形式概形 $\mathfrak X$ 上的可逆层
$\mathfrak L$ 在 $X=\mathfrak X_\eta$ 上给出典范的可逆
$\mathcal O_{X_G}$-模 $\mathfrak L_\eta$。此构造与张量积、
对偶及形式拉回相容，其形式过渡函数的绝对值为一。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose a formal affine open cover $\mathfrak U_i$ with
frames $e_i$ of $\mathfrak L$. On overlaps write
$e_j=u_{ij}e_i$, where $u_{ij}$ is a formal unit. By
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}, the domains
$U_i=(\mathfrak U_i)_\eta$ form an admissible analytic cover,
and the functions $u_{ij}$ restrict to analytic units on
$U_i\cap U_j$. They satisfy $u_{ik}=u_{ij}u_{jk}$ on triple
overlaps. For an analytic domain $V\subset X$, let
$\mathcal L(V)$ consist of families of analytic coefficients
$a_i$ on $V\cap U_i$ satisfying $a_i=u_{ij}a_j$ on
$V\cap U_i\cap U_j$. The structure-sheaf gluing theorem
\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf} makes this a sheaf of
$\mathcal O_{X_G}$-modules. On $U_i$ it is freely generated
by $e_i$: a coefficient there determines every other local
coefficient by the displayed transition relation. Thus it is
an invertible sheaf.}
{取形式仿射开覆盖 $\mathfrak U_i$，使 $\mathfrak L$ 在其上
有标架 $e_i$。在交叠处写 $e_j=u_{ij}e_i$，其中 $u_{ij}$
为形式单位元。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}，
各域 $U_i=(\mathfrak U_i)_\eta$ 构成 admissible 解析覆盖，
$u_{ij}$ 在 $U_i\cap U_j$ 上限制为解析单位元，并在三重
交叠处满足 $u_{ik}=u_{ij}u_{jk}$。对解析域 $V\subset X$，
令 $\mathcal L(V)$ 由 $V\cap U_i$ 上的解析系数族 $a_i$
组成，要求在 $V\cap U_i\cap U_j$ 上满足 $a_i=u_{ij}a_j$。
结构层粘合定理 \ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf} 使它成为
$\mathcal O_{X_G}$-模层。在 $U_i$ 上，它由 $e_i$ 自由生成：
该片上的系数通过所列过渡关系唯一决定其余各局部系数。
因此它是可逆层。}

\bi{A change of formal frames multiplies these coefficients by
the corresponding formal units and gives a canonical isomorphism.
Two covers have their common formal refinement; the isomorphisms
just described agree there and hence globally. This proves
independence. Products, inverses and pullbacks of the cocycles
give respectively tensor products, duals and pullback, with the
usual compatibility identities holding coefficientwise. Finally
$u_{ij}$ and $u_{ij}^{-1}$ are integral on every formal affine
subchart of the overlap, so both have absolute value at most one.
It follows that $|u_{ij}|=1$ everywhere on the analytic overlap.}
{形式标架的改变将系数乘以相应形式单位元，给出典范同构。
两个覆盖具有共同的形式加细，上述同构在那里一致，故全局
一致，证明构造独立于选择。对余循环分别取乘积、逆及拉回，
即得到张量积、对偶及拉回；各相容恒等式逐系数成立。最后，
$u_{ij}$ 与 $u_{ij}^{-1}$ 在交叠的每个形式仿射子图上都为
整系数元素，绝对值均至多为一。因此解析交叠上处处有
$|u_{ij}|=1$。}
\end{proof}
