Coherent sheaves are flat over the ground field

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 625

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Coherent sheaves are flat over the ground field}{凝聚层在基域上平坦}]
\label{cand:groundfieldanalyticflatness}
\bi{Let $Y$ be a $K$-analytic space and let $\mathcal F$ be coherent on $Y_G$. Then $\mathcal F$ is analytically flat over $\mathcal M(K)$. More generally, for every $K$-analytic space $X$, the sheaf $p_Y^*\mathcal F$ on $Y\times_KX$ is analytically $X$-flat. In particular the projection $Y\times_KX\to X$ is analytically flat.}
{设 $Y$ 为 $K$-解析空间，$\mathcal F$ 是 $Y_G$ 上的凝聚层。则 $\mathcal F$ 在 $\mathcal M(K)$ 上解析平坦。更一般地，对任意 $K$-解析空间 $X$，$Y\times_KX$ 上的层 $p_Y^*\mathcal F$ 解析 $X$-平坦。特别地，投影 $Y\times_KX\to X$ 解析平坦。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First let $Y,X$ be good. Let $L/K$ be any complete valued extension, let $X'$ be a good $L$-analytic space with a morphism to $X$, and consider any point of the base-changed product. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension} and Corollary~\ref{fnd:iteratedaffinoidbasechange}, the base change and its coherent pullback identify with}
{先设 $Y,X$ good。任取完备赋值扩张 $L/K$、带有到 $X$ 的态射的 good $L$-解析空间 $X'$，以及基变换后乘积中的任意点。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension} 及 Corollary~\ref{fnd:iteratedaffinoidbasechange}，该基变换与其凝聚拉回识别为}
\[
 (Y\times_KX)\times_XX'\simeq Y_L\times_LX',
 \qquad p_{Y_L}^*\mathcal F_L.
\]
\bi{The space $Y_L$ is good: the inverse image of an ordinary affinoid neighborhood is affinoid and contains the inverse image of its ordinary interior. The sheaf $\mathcal F_L$ is coherent by its local finite presentations. Proposition~\ref{cand:groundfieldproductstalkflat}, applied over $L$, proves naive flatness at every point of this product. These are all the good base-change tests, over all complete extensions, in Definition~\ref{fnd:analyticflatness}.}
{空间 $Y_L$ good：普通 affinoid 邻域的逆像仍为 affinoid，且包含其普通内部的逆像。局部有限表示说明 $\mathcal F_L$ 凝聚。在 $L$ 上应用 Proposition~\ref{cand:groundfieldproductstalkflat}，便得到该乘积每一点处的朴素平坦性。这检验了 Definition~\ref{fnd:analyticflatness} 中所有完备扩域上的全部 good 基变换测试。}

\bi{For general $Y,X$, choose affinoid analytic nets on both spaces. Their affinoid products form an analytic atlas of $Y\times_KX$ by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticproducts}. On every such product, the restricted sheaf is the pullback of the finite-module sheaf on the source chart. The good-space case supplies all the required flatness tests there. The $G$-local definition therefore gives the assertion on the general product. Taking $X=\mathcal M(K)$ proves the first statement; taking $\mathcal F=\mathcal O_Y$ proves the projection statement.}
{对一般的 $Y,X$，分别选择 affinoid 解析 net。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticproducts}，相应的 affinoid 乘积构成 $Y\times_KX$ 的解析 atlas。在每个这样的乘积上，限制层是源图上的有限模层的拉回。Good 空间的情形在该处给出全部所需的平坦性测试，于是 $G$-局部定义给出一般乘积上的结论。取 $X=\mathcal M(K)$ 即得第一项；取 $\mathcal F=\mathcal O_Y$ 即得投影的断言。}
\end{proof}
