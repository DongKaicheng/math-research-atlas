Smoothness and interior quasi-smoothness

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 434

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Smoothness and interior quasi-smoothness}{光滑性与内点准光滑性}]
\label{fnd:goodsmoothquasismoothinterior}
\bi{For a morphism $f:Y\to X$ of good analytic spaces and a point $y\in Y$, $f$ is smooth at $y$ if and only if it is quasi-smooth at $y$ and $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$. Here smoothness is the ordinary-open local \'etale factorization through a relative analytic affine space.}
{对 good 解析空间间的态射 $f:Y\to X$ 及一点 $y\in Y$，$f$ 在 $y$ 处光滑，当且仅当它在 $y$ 处准光滑且 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$。这里光滑性指普通开集上经相对解析仿射空间的局部 \'etale 分解。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Suppose first that $f$ is quasi-smooth and interior at $y$. The strengthened presentation in Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion} supplies affinoid neighbourhoods $Y_0$ of $y$ and $X_0$ of $x=f(y)$, together with a closed immersion $Y_0\hookrightarrow D\subset\mathbb A_{X_0}^n$ whose ideal is generated by $r$ functions and whose Jacobian has an invertible $r\times r$ minor near $y$. Apply the ordinary Laurent-neighbourhood restriction to make the minor invertible throughout the chosen source. Permute coordinates so that it uses $T_1,\ldots,T_r$, and put $d=n-r$. The remaining coordinates define}
{先设 $f$ 在 $y$ 处准光滑且为内点。Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion} 的加强表示给出 $y$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $Y_0$、$x=f(y)$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $X_0$，以及闭浸入 $Y_0\hookrightarrow D\subset\mathbb A_{X_0}^n$；其理想由 $r$ 个函数生成，且 Jacobian 在 $y$ 附近有可逆的 $r\times r$ 子式。作普通 Laurent 邻域限制，使该子式在所选源分片上处处可逆。排列坐标，使此子式对应于 $T_1,\ldots,T_r$，并令 $d=n-r$。其余坐标定义}
\[
 g:Y_0\longrightarrow\mathbb A_{X_0}^{d},\qquad
        g=(f,T_{r+1},\ldots,T_n).
\]
\bi{Choose a closed relative polydisc $P\subset\mathbb A_{X_0}^{d}$ whose radii are strictly larger than the corresponding coordinate bounds on the affinoid $D$. Then $g(Y_0)\subset P$, and $P$ is a neighbourhood of $g(y)$. The domain $D$ lies in $\mathbb A_P^r$ and remains an affinoid domain there by the domain mapping property. The same $r$ equations with their invertible square Jacobian make $Y_0\to P$ quasi-smooth with zero relative differential sheaf.}
{选择闭相对多圆盘 $P\subset\mathbb A_{X_0}^{d}$，使各半径严格大于这些坐标在 affinoid $D$ 上的界。于是 $g(Y_0)\subset P$，且 $P$ 是 $g(y)$ 的邻域。域 $D$ 包含于 $\mathbb A_P^r$，并由域映射性质仍是其 affinoid 域。同样的 $r$ 个方程具有可逆的方形 Jacobian，说明 $Y_0\to P$ 准光滑且相对微分层为零。}

\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:goodinteriorlocaltesting} gives $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y_0/X_0)$. The affinoid composition formula for $Y_0\to P\to X_0$ implies $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y_0/P)$. The analytic fibre of $Y_0\to P$ through $y$ is good and quasi-smooth of differential rank zero, by base change. Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasismoothrankzeroisolated} makes $y$ isolated in that fibre. Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion} gives the ordinary local flatness of $Y_0\to P$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:localfiniteetalecriterion} now makes it \'etale near $y$. Shrink its ordinary finite neighbourhoods inside the ordinary interiors of $Y_0$ in $Y$ and $P$ in $\mathbb A_{X_0}^d$; the small-neighbourhood assertion of Theorem~\ref{fnd:isolatedinteriorlocalfinite} permits this. Restrict also inside the ordinary interior of $X_0$ in $X$. We obtain the required ordinary-open \'etale factorization of $f$, proving smoothness.}
{Proposition~\ref{fnd:goodinteriorlocaltesting} 给出 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y_0/X_0)$。对 $Y_0\to P\to X_0$ 应用 affinoid 内部复合公式，得到 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y_0/P)$。由基变换，$Y_0\to P$ 经过 $y$ 的解析纤维为 good 且准光滑，其微分秩为零。Lemma~\ref{fnd:quasismoothrankzeroisolated} 说明 $y$ 在该纤维中孤立。Theorem~\ref{fnd:jacobianflatregularcriterion} 给出 $Y_0\to P$ 的普通局部平坦性。Proposition~\ref{fnd:localfiniteetalecriterion} 于是说明它在 $y$ 附近 \'etale。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:isolatedinteriorlocalfinite} 的任意小邻域结论，可将其普通有限邻域缩入 $Y_0$ 在 $Y$ 中及 $P$ 在 $\mathbb A_{X_0}^d$ 中的普通内部；再限制到 $X_0$ 在 $X$ 中的普通内部，便得到所需的普通开集上 \'etale 分解，证明光滑性。}

\bi{Conversely, a local \'etale map is quasi-smooth by Proposition~\ref{fnd:finiteetalequasismooth}. A relative analytic affine-space projection is quasi-smooth by its presentation with no equations. The composition theorem for Jacobian presentations therefore makes every smooth morphism quasi-smooth. The finite local maps defining \'etaleness are boundaryless by the affinoid finite criterion. Relative affine-space projections are boundaryless because, at each point, one may choose coordinate radii strictly larger than the coordinate values. The composition inclusion for relative interior, applied on good affinoid neighbourhoods, and ordinary-local testing then make the composite boundaryless at $y$.}
{反之，由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:finiteetalequasismooth}，局部 \'etale 态射准光滑。相对解析仿射空间的投影具有无方程表示，故亦准光滑。因此 Jacobian 表示的复合定理说明每个光滑态射均准光滑。定义 \'etale 性的有限局部映射由 affinoid 有限性判据而无边界；相对仿射空间投影也无边界，因为在每点均可选严格大于坐标取值的半径。在 good affinoid 邻域上应用相对内部的复合包含，再用普通局部测试，便知复合在 $y$ 处无边界。}
\end{proof}
