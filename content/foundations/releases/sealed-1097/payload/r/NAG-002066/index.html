The radial germ of an open disc

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 691

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The radial germ of an open disc}
{开圆盘的径向芽}]
\label{cand:discradialgerm}
\bi{Let $D$ be an open unit disc with coordinate $T$, and let
$\zeta_r$, $0<r<1$, be its centered Gauss points. An analytic
isomorphism between two such discs sends $\zeta_r$ to the
centered radius-$r$ Gauss point for all $r$ sufficiently close
to $1$. On a sufficiently thin outer collar it also preserves
the function $|T|$. These assertions hold for semilinear
isometric isomorphisms and commute with complete isometric
ground-field extension. The open disc and each collar
$\{r<|T|<1\}$ are path connected.}
{设 $D$ 为坐标 $T$ 下的开单位圆盘，以 $\zeta_r$，
$0<r<1$，表示中心 Gauss 点。两个这样的圆盘之间的解析
同构，将充分接近 $1$ 的 $\zeta_r$ 映到另一个圆盘的中心
半径 $r$ 的 Gauss 点。在充分薄的外侧领形域上，它还
保持函数 $|T|$。这些断言对半线性等距同构仍成立，并与
完备等距基域扩张相容。开圆盘及每个领形域
$\{r<|T|<1\}$ 都道路连通。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First suppose that the ground valuation is nontrivial. Write an isomorphism as $g(T)=\sum a_nT^n$. The proof of
Theorem~\ref{cand:opendisccanonicalhomotopy} gives
$|a_0|<1$, $|a_1|=1$, and $|a_n|\leq1$ for $n\geq2$.
For $|a_0|<|T(x)|<1$, the linear term uniquely dominates all
others, so $|g(x)|=|T(x)|$. The same statement for the inverse
makes the corresponding collars identical after increasing
their lower radius if necessary. The homotopy identity gives}
{先设底域的赋值非平凡。将同构写为 $g(T)=\sum a_nT^n$。
Theorem~\ref{cand:opendisccanonicalhomotopy} 的证明给出
$|a_0|<1$、$|a_1|=1$ 及 $n\geq2$ 时 $|a_n|\leq1$。
当 $|a_0|<|T(x)|<1$ 时，一次项严格支配其余所有项，
故 $|g(x)|=|T(x)|$。对逆映射应用同样结论，并在必要时
提高领形域的内半径，便得到相应领形域的识别。同伦恒等式给出}
\[
 g(\zeta_r)=g(H_D(0,r))=H_D(g(0),r)=\zeta_r
                    \qquad(r>|a_0|).
\]
\bi{The last equality is invariance of the radius-$r$ Gauss
seminorm under translation by an element of absolute value
at most $r$. It follows directly by applying the ultrametric
inequality first to translation and then to its inverse.
This argument also gives $H_D(x,r)=\zeta_r$ whenever
$|T(x)|\leq r$. Hence any two points of $D$ can be joined,
using $H_D$ up to a common time $r<1$, to the same $\zeta_r$.
For a collar, the radial annulus homotopy joins each point
to the Gauss point of the same radius, and the Gauss interval
joins any two of these points inside the collar. These are
continuous paths by Proposition~\ref{fnd:annulusretraction}.
All formulas preserve the absolute value under semilinear
isometries and agree on original polynomials after scalar
extension, proving the remaining assertions.}
{最后一个等式是：按绝对值至多为 $r$ 的元素平移不改变
半径 $r$ 的 Gauss 半范数。先对平移、再对逆平移应用
超度量不等式，即可直接得到这一点。同一论证还给出
$|T(x)|\leq r$ 时 $H_D(x,r)=\zeta_r$。因此，对圆盘中
任意两点，可将 $H_D$ 运行到同一个小于 $1$ 的时刻 $r$，
从而把它们连到同一个 $\zeta_r$。对于领形域，圆环径向
同伦将每一点连到相同半径的 Gauss 点，再由 Gauss 区间
在领形域内部连接任意两个这样的点。由
Proposition~\ref{fnd:annulusretraction}，这些都是连续道路。
全部公式在半线性等距同构下保持绝对值，并在标量扩张后
对原多项式取同一值，由此得到其余断言。}

It remains to treat a trivially valued \(K\). Choose \(0<\eta<1\).
Then \((\eta)\) is free over \(K\), and
Lemma~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiusfield} supplies a complete isometric
extension \(L=K_\eta\) with a Laurent parameter of absolute value
\(\eta\). The valuation on \(L\) is nontrivial. An isometric
automorphism of \(K\) extends coefficient by coefficient to \(L\),
fixing that parameter. This extension preserves the weighted
Laurent norm and its inverse is constructed in the same way.

For either given coordinate, take real radii \(s_i\uparrow1\),
with \(0<s_i<1\). The interiors of the closed discs of
radii \(s_i\) cover the open disc. In the actual completed
tensor presentation their algebras satisfy
\[
 K\langle s_i^{-1}T\rangle\widehat\otimes_K L
       = L\langle s_i^{-1}T\rangle .
\]
Indeed, the weighted Laurent coefficient description of
Theorem~\ref{fnd:freepolyradiuscoefficients} for the free radius
\(\eta\) on the left is the weighted coefficient description
for the same two indices on the right. In both descriptions
the norm is the supremum with weights \(s_i^n\eta^j\) and the
weighted coefficient family tends to zero. The identifications
commute with restriction to smaller discs and the radii are
cofinal at every point with \(|T|<1\) over both fields.

Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalanalyticscalarextension} to each
open disc with its given coordinate. The scalar projections are
continuous and surjective and commute with the given isomorphism
and its extension. For a semilinear isomorphism, use the
coefficient automorphism of \(L\) just described. Since projection
restricts a seminorm to the original coefficients, the function
\(\lvert T\rvert\) pulls back to the same coordinate norm.
The inverse image of each collar \(q<\lvert T\rvert<1\) is
therefore the corresponding collar. The radius-\(r\) centered
Gauss point over \(L\) projects to the radius-\(r\) centered
Gauss point over \(K\), by its formula on \(K[T]\).

Apply the nontrivially valued case to the lifted isomorphism.
It identifies the two collars and preserves their coordinate
norms as soon as \(q\) bounds the absolute values at zero of
the coordinate series of the isomorphism and its inverse.
Both constants lie in \(K\) and have norm less than one, so
they vanish in the trivially valued case. Surjectivity on
each collar, and the commutative square with the scalar
projections, give these assertions downstairs. Applying the same
square to the centered Gauss points gives their asserted images.
For any two points of the disc or of a collar, choose their two
lifts individually by surjectivity and join the lifts upstairs.
The projection of that path joins the given endpoints. This
uses only the surjectivity of the scalar projection. For any further complete isometric scalar extension
the same projection identities on coordinate norms and on the
Gauss seminorms follow by restriction of seminorms. These
identities give scalar compatibility for the asserted collars
and centered points.
\end{proof}
