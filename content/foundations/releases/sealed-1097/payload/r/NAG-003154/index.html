Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Closed component currents with full generic lengths]\label{f9si:cycle}
Let $j:Y\hookrightarrow X$ be a closed immersion of good Hausdorff analytic spaces, let $Y$ be pure of analytic dimension $r\geq1$, and let $U\subseteq\Int(X/K)$ be ordinary open. Put $V=j^{-1}U$. Then
\[
 j_*I_Y(\alpha)=\int_V j^*\alpha,
 \qquad \alpha\in\E^{r,r}(U),
\]
defines a closed current of bidimension $(r,r)$, with support contained in $j(Y)\cap U$. If $X$ is pure $n$-dimensional its bidegree is $(n-r,n-r)$. Finite supplied real linear combinations of these currents, and supplied locally finite such combinations with one fixed component dimension $r$, are closed with the same degree convention.

For a pure affinoid $X=\mathcal M(A)$ of dimension $n\geq1$, let $\mathfrak q_i$ be its minimal primes, $X_i=\mathcal M(A/\mathfrak q_i)$, and $j_i:X_i\hookrightarrow X$. Then, on interior tests,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cycle}
 I_X=\sum_i m_i(j_i)_*I_{X_i},\qquad
 m_i=\length_{A_{\mathfrak q_i}}A_{\mathfrak q_i}.
\end{equation}
These are the full generic lengths. No general coherent-module component formula is asserted here.
\end{theorem}
