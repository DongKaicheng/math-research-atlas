Descent of continuous metrized line bundles

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1957

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Descent of continuous metrized line bundles]\label{f9ds:metricdescent}
Let $(\cM_Y,\delta)$ be a line bundle with the full analytic descent
datum of Theorem~\ref{f9ds:coherentdescent}. Let $\|\cdot\|_Y$ be a
continuous metric. It is the pullback of a unique continuous metric
on the descended line bundle, relative to its specified descent
isomorphism, if and only if
\[
 \delta:(q_1^*\cM_Y,q_1^*\|\cdot\|_Y)
       \longrightarrow(q_2^*\cM_Y,q_2^*\|\cdot\|_Y)
\]
is an isometry. Tensor products, duals and isometric isomorphisms
are compatible with this descent.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Descend the analytic line bundle first. On an ordinary open set
$U$ with descended frame $e$, let $\widetilde e$ be its frame on
$p^{-1}(U)$ under the specified descent isomorphism. The function
$u=-\log\|\widetilde e\|_Y$ is continuous. The cocycle sends the two
pullbacks of $\widetilde e$ to each other. Its isometry condition
therefore gives $q_1^*u=q_2^*u$ above $U$.
Lemma~\ref{f9ds:topdescent}, also valid on every open restriction,
gives a unique continuous function $v_e$ on $U$ with $p^*v_e=u$.

If $e'=he$ on an overlap, then upstairs
$u_{e'}=u_e-\log|p^*h|$. Surjectivity of $p$ implies
$v_{e'}=v_e-\log|h|$. Hence
$\|a e(x)\|=|a|\exp(-v_e(x))$ is independent of frame and defines
the required continuous metric. The necessity and uniqueness follow
by pulling back frame weights. Sums and negatives of weights give
tensor and dual compatibility. An analytic isomorphism commuting
with descent data descends by Theorem~\ref{f9ds:coherentdescent};
whether it is an isometry is checked by the surjective map $p$.
\end{proof}
