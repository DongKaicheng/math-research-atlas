A flat coherent ordinary stalk is locally free

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 322

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{A flat coherent ordinary stalk is locally free}{平坦凝聚通常茎的局部自由性}]
\label{fnd:coherentflatstalkfree}
\bi{Let $T$ be good and $\mathcal G$ coherent on $T_G$. If $\mathcal G_t$ is flat over $\mathcal O_{T,t}$, then $\mathcal G$ is free of finite rank on an ordinary neighborhood of $t$. In particular it is analytically $T$-flat at $t$.}
{设 $T$ good，$\mathcal G$ 在 $T_G$ 上凝聚。若 $\mathcal G_t$ 在 $\mathcal O_{T,t}$ 上平坦，则 $\mathcal G$ 在 $t$ 的某个普通邻域上为有限秩自由层。特别地，它在 $t$ 处解析 $T$-平坦。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Put $R=\mathcal O_{T,t}$ and $M=\mathcal G_t$. The ring $R$ is Noetherian by Theorem~\ref{fnd:ordinaryanalyticstalknoetherian}, and $M$ is finite. Lift a basis of $M/\mathfrak m_RM$ to $m$ elements of $M$. Nakayama's determinant argument makes the induced map $R^m\to M$ surjective. Its kernel $N$ is finite. Tensoring with the residue field is left exact at $N$, since flatness of $M$ kills the first Tor group; the chosen residue basis therefore gives $N/\mathfrak m_RN=0$. Another application of the determinant argument gives $N=0$. Hence $M$ is free.}
{令 $R=\mathcal O_{T,t}$、$M=\mathcal G_t$。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:ordinaryanalyticstalknoetherian}，$R$ Noetherian，而 $M$ 有限。将 $M/\mathfrak m_RM$ 的一组基提升为 $M$ 中的 $m$ 个元素。Nakayama 的行列式论证使相应映射 $R^m\to M$ 满射。其核 $N$ 有限；与剩余域张量时，$M$ 的平坦性使一阶 Tor 为零，因而在 $N$ 处左正合。所选剩余基于是给出 $N/\mathfrak m_RN=0$，再次用行列式论证即得 $N=0$。所以 $M$ 自由。}

\bi{Represent the finitely many basis germs by local sections, giving a morphism $\mathcal O^m\to\mathcal G$ on an ordinary neighborhood. Its kernel and cokernel are coherent by the local finite-module theorem. Each has zero ordinary stalk at $t$, and finite generators on an affinoid neighborhood therefore vanish after shrinking to an ordinary neighborhood of $t$. The morphism is an isomorphism there. Any analytic pullback of this isomorphism is again a finite-rank free module, whose ordinary stalks are flat. This checks all complete-extension and good-base-change tests.}
{用局部截面表示这有限个基芽，得到某个普通邻域上的态射 $\mathcal O^m\to\mathcal G$。由局部有限模定理，其核与余核凝聚。二者在 $t$ 处的通常茎均为零，所以在一个 affinoid 邻域上选择有限生成元后，再缩小至 $t$ 的普通邻域即可使它们全部消失。所得态射在该邻域上为同构。此同构的任意解析拉回仍为有限秩自由模，其通常茎平坦；这核验了全部完备扩域及 good 基变换测试。}
\end{proof}
