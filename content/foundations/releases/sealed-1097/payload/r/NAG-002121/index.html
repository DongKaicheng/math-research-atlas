An explicit genus-zero model

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 710

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{An explicit genus-zero model}
{亏格零的显式模型}]
\label{cand:genuszerovaluationmodel}
\bi{Let $K$ be complete and nontrivially valued of characteristic
zero, with the fixed absolute value, and let $C/K$ be a smooth
projective geometrically connected curve of genus zero. There
is a finite Galois extension $L/K$ and an isomorphism
$C_L\simeq\mathbf P^1_L$. The smooth projective model
$\mathbf P^1_{L^\circ}$ gives a one-vertex skeleton and a
jointly continuous strong deformation retraction of
$(C_L)^{\an}$ onto that vertex. The value group of $K$
may be nondiscrete.}
{设 $K$ 为配备固定绝对值的特征零非平凡赋值完备域，
$C/K$ 为光滑射影几何连通亏格零曲线。存在有限 Galois
扩张 $L/K$ 及同构 $C_L\simeq\mathbf P^1_L$。
光滑射影模型 $\mathbf P^1_{L^\circ}$ 给出单顶点骨架，
以及 $(C_L)^{\an}$ 到该顶点的联合连续强形变收缩。
这里允许 $K$ 的值群非离散。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{A nonempty affine open of $C$ has a closed point with
finite residue field over $K$, by \cite[Tag 00FV]{StacksAlg}.
Take a finite normal closure of this field over $K$.
Characteristic zero makes the resulting $L/K$ Galois,
and $C_L$ has an $L$-rational point $P$. Smoothness makes
$P$ an effective Cartier divisor by
\cite[Tag 0B8Y]{StacksAlg}. Thus $\mathcal O(P)$ has degree
one. Flat cohomology base change
\cite[Tag 02KH]{StacksAlg} gives
$H^1(C_L,\mathcal O)=H^1(C,\mathcal O)\otimes_K L=0$.
The characterization \cite[Tag 0C6U]{StacksAlg} yields
$C_L\simeq\mathbf P^1_L$.}
{$C$ 的非空仿射开集有剩余域在 $K$ 上有限的闭点，
见 \cite[Tag 00FV]{StacksAlg}。取该域在 $K$ 上的
有限正规闭包。由特征零，所得 $L/K$ 为 Galois 扩张，
且 $C_L$ 有一个 $L$-有理点 $P$。光滑性与
\cite[Tag 0B8Y]{StacksAlg} 说明 $P$ 为有效 Cartier
除子，因此 $\mathcal O(P)$ 的次数为一。平坦上同调
基变换 \cite[Tag 02KH]{StacksAlg} 给出
$H^1(C_L,\mathcal O)=H^1(C,\mathcal O)\otimes_K L=0$。
由刻画定理 \cite[Tag 0C6U]{StacksAlg}，
$C_L\simeq\mathbf P^1_L$。}

\bi{The two standard affine charts of
$\mathbf P^1_{L^\circ}$ are finitely presented and flat;
the special fibre is the smooth integral curve
$\mathbf P^1_{\widetilde L}$. Its completion has analytic
generic fibre $(C_L)^{\an}$ by
Theorem~\ref{cand:projectiveformalcompletion}.
Corollary~\ref{cand:charzerosplitmodelskeleton} applies
with no nodal edges and one component vertex, and gives
the asserted homotopy.}
{$\mathbf P^1_{L^\circ}$ 的两个标准仿射坐标片有限表示
且平坦，特殊纤维为光滑整曲线
$\mathbf P^1_{\widetilde L}$。由
Theorem~\ref{cand:projectiveformalcompletion}，其完备化
的解析泛纤维为 $(C_L)^{\an}$。此时没有结点边而只有
一个分量顶点，应用
Corollary~\ref{cand:charzerosplitmodelskeleton} 即得
所述同伦。}
\end{proof}
