Equivariant marked genus-one models

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

\begin{theorem}[\bi{Equivariant marked genus-one models}
{等变标记亏格一模型}]
\label{cand:genusoneequivariantvaluationmodel}
\bi{Let $K$ be a complete nontrivially valued characteristic-zero
field, let $C/K$ be a smooth projective geometrically connected
curve of genus one, and let $D$ be a finite reduced closed subscheme
of $C$, possibly empty. There is a finite Galois extension $L/K$
and a projective split nodal model $\mathcal C/L^\circ$ of $C_L$
with a semilinear $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$-action extending the
generic action, such that the points of $D_L$ are rational and
have pairwise distinct smooth specializations.
Consequently $(C\setminus D)^{\an}$ has a jointly continuous
strong deformation retraction onto a finite graph with finitely
many infinite rays, obtained from the marked model skeleton by
finite Galois descent with the original absolute value.}
{设 $K$ 为完备非平凡赋值的特征零域，$C/K$ 为光滑射影几何连通
的亏格一曲线，$D$ 为 $C$ 的有限约化闭子概形，允许为空。
存在有限 Galois 扩张 $L/K$，以及 $C_L$ 的射影分裂结点模型
$\mathcal C/L^\circ$，其上具有延拓泛作用的半线性
$\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$-作用，使 $D_L$ 中的点均有理，
且具有两两互异的光滑特殊化。
因此 $(C\setminus D)^{\an}$ 具有到一个带有限条无限射线的
有限图的联合连续强形变收缩；它从标记模型骨架按原绝对值作
有限 Galois 下降得到。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Form the stable double $Y/K$ of
Lemma~\ref{cand:genusonestabledouble} and apply
Lemma~\ref{cand:stablenodalgenericextension}.
Its generic normalization is $C_L\amalg C_L$, with two
Galois-invariant labels. Proposition~\ref{cand:nodalmodelgenericnormalization}
therefore supplies two projective split nodal models over
$L^\circ$, each preserved by the canonical semilinear Galois action.
Choose the first labelled model. Its generic fibre is the
original $C_L$ with its specified action.}
{构造 Lemma~\ref{cand:genusonestabledouble} 的稳定倍接 $Y/K$，
并应用 Lemma~\ref{cand:stablenodalgenericextension}。
其泛正规化是 $C_L\amalg C_L$，两个标签均 Galois 不变。
因此 Proposition~\ref{cand:nodalmodelgenericnormalization}
给出 $L^\circ$ 上的两个射影分裂结点模型，且每个都被典范
半线性 Galois 作用保持。选取第一个带标签模型；其泛纤维正是
带指定作用的原曲线 $C_L$。}

\bi{Enlarge $L/K$ finitely and Galois so that all points of $D_L$
are rational. Such an extension exists because the finite residue
extensions of closed points are separable in characteristic zero.
Base change of a split nodal family remains split nodal; its
projectivity, flatness and finite presentation persist.
The previously constructed action extends to the larger group
through the restriction to the earlier normal extension and the
action on coefficients. Properness extends each rational marked
point uniquely to a section, as in
Theorem~\ref{cand:equivariantmarkedrefinement}.
That theorem applies to this projective separated model and the
Galois-invariant set of marked sections, and yields the required
equivariant model with distinct smooth marked specializations.}
{将 $L/K$ 作进一步有限 Galois 扩张，使 $D_L$ 中所有点有理。
因为特征零下闭点的有限剩余扩张均可分，这样的扩张存在。
分裂结点族的基变换仍为分裂结点族，并保持射影性、平坦性与
有限呈示性。此前构造的作用，通过限制到较早正规扩张及对
系数的作用，延拓为更大群的作用。由 properness，每个有理
标记点唯一延拓为截面，正如
Theorem~\ref{cand:equivariantmarkedrefinement} 中所用。
对这个射影分离模型及 Galois 不变的标记截面集应用该定理，
得到具有互异光滑标记特殊化的所需等变模型。}

\bi{Apply Theorem~\ref{cand:generalmarkedmodelskeleton}, using
its characteristic-zero, arbitrary-residue-field alternative.
The entire homotopy is equivariant, so it descends by
Corollary~\ref{cand:galoisretractiondescent}.
For the noncompact punctured space,
Theorem~\ref{cand:galoisanalyticquotient} gives the open quotient
property after product with $[0,1]$.
The finite graph quotient, after
subdivision at any inverted-edge midpoint, is again a finite
graph with finitely many rays. All field extensions carry the
unique extending absolute value throughout.}
{应用 Theorem~\ref{cand:generalmarkedmodelskeleton} 中
特征零且允许任意剩余域的情形。整个同伦等变，故由
Corollary~\ref{cand:galoisretractiondescent} 下降。
对非紧的穿孔空间，Theorem~\ref{cand:galoisanalyticquotient}
给出与 $[0,1]$ 作积后的开商映射性质。
在每条被反向作用的边的中点细分后，有限图的商仍为
带有限条射线的有限图。全过程中每个域扩张都配备唯一的
延拓绝对值。}
\end{proof}
