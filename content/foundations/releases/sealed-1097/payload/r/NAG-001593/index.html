Local analytic dimension and the Abhyankar inequality

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 606

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Local analytic dimension and the Abhyankar inequality}
{局部解析维数与 Abhyankar 不等式}]
\label{fnd:generalanalyticabhyankar}
\bi{For any nonempty analytic space $X$, the supremum of the
dimensions of its affinoid charts is independent of the chosen
analytic atlas and equals}
{对任意非空解析空间 $X$，其 affinoid 图的维数的上确界与解析
图册的选择无关，且等于}
\[
                         \dim_KX=\sup_{x\in X}d_K(x).
\]
\bi{It agrees with the affinoid dimension already defined.
An analytic domain has dimension at most that of its ambient
space. If $\dim_xX$ is the infimum of the dimensions of the
analytic neighborhoods of $x$, then}
{它与此前定义的 affinoid 维数相同。解析域的维数不超过
环境空间的维数。若 $\dim_xX$ 是 $x$ 的解析邻域维数的
下确界，则}
\[
                         d_K(x)\le\dim_xX.
\]
\bi{These conclusions require neither goodness nor Hausdorffness.
At a good point the infimum can be taken over affinoid
neighborhoods. At every point $\dim_xX$ is finite.}
{这些结论不要求 good 或 Hausdorff。
在 good 点处，可仅对 affinoid 邻域取下确界。
每一点处的 $\dim_xX$ 均有限。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The completed residue field attached to a point is independent
of its affinoid chart: on a common refinement this is
Corollary~\ref{fnd:domainresiduefields}, and the analytic-net
construction identifies the maps on successive refinements.
Thus $d_K(x)$ is an intrinsic point invariant. For any analytic
atlas $(X_i)$, Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidabhyankarmaximum} gives}
{一点所附的完备剩余域与其 affinoid 图无关：在共同加细上，
这是 Corollary~\ref{fnd:domainresiduefields}；
解析 net 的构造识别连续加细中的这些映射。
因此 $d_K(x)$ 是点的内禀不变量。
对任意解析图册 $(X_i)$，由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidabhyankarmaximum}，有}
\[
 \sup_i\dim_KX_i
       =\sup_i\sup_{x\in X_i}d_K(x)
       =\sup_{x\in X}d_K(x).
\]
\bi{This proves atlas independence rather than assuming it in
the definition. It also agrees with the old dimension on an
affinoid chart, by the same theorem. For an analytic domain
$U\subset X$, the residue-field invariance is checked on
affinoid refinements of its domain atlas; it gives
$\dim_KU=\sup_{x\in U}d_K(x)\le\dim_KX$.}
{这直接证明图册独立性，而不是将其假定为定义的一部分。
由同一定理，它也与 affinoid 图上原有的维数相同。
对解析域 $U\subset X$，在其域图册的 affinoid 加细上核验
剩余域不变，便得到
$\dim_KU=\sup_{x\in U}d_K(x)\le\dim_KX$。}

\bi{Every analytic neighborhood $U$ of $x$ contains that point,
so $d_K(x)\le\dim_KU$. Taking their infimum proves the desired
inequality. If $x$ is good, affinoid neighborhoods are cofinal
among analytic neighborhoods: take one inside the ordinary
open neighborhood contained in a given analytic neighborhood.
Finally the net axiom gives finitely many affinoid charts $X_i$
containing $x$ whose union contains an ordinary open
neighborhood $W$ of $x$. Give $W$ its open analytic structure.
For every $w\in W$ one of these charts contains $w$, so the
already proved formula gives}
{每个解析邻域 $U$ 都含有 $x$，所以 $d_K(x)\le\dim_KU$；
取下确界即得所需不等式。
若 $x$ 为 good，则 affinoid 邻域在解析邻域中余终：
在给定解析邻域所含的普通开邻域中选取一个即可。
最后，net 公理给出有限多个含 $x$ 的 affinoid 图 $X_i$，
使其并包含 $x$ 的普通开邻域 $W$。给 $W$ 配备开解析子空间
结构。每个 $w\in W$ 均属于某个这样的图，故已证公式给出}
\[
          \dim_KW=\sup_{w\in W}d_K(w)\le\max_i\dim_KX_i<\infty.
\]
\bi{Thus $W$ is an analytic neighborhood of finite dimension.
This proves finiteness of $\dim_xX$ without a good-space assumption.}
{因此 $W$ 是一个有限维的解析邻域。这证明 $\dim_xX$ 有限，
而不要求空间 good。}
\end{proof}
