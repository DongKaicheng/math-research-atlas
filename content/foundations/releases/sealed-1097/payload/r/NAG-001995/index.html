Strictly compact relative restriction

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 412

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Strictly compact relative restriction}{严格紧的相对限制}]
\label{fnd:strictcompactrelativerestriction}
\bi{Let $B$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra and let $u:A\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle\to B$ be bounded. Suppose that the spectral radius of each $t_i=u(T_i)$ is less than one. Then $u$, regarded as an $A$-linear map, is strictly completely continuous. The same holds for finite direct sums and for the restriction to any closed source submodule with its induced norm.}
{设 $B$ 为 generalized $K$-affinoid 代数，$u:A\langle T_1,\ldots,T_n\rangle\to B$ 有界。假设每个 $t_i=u(T_i)$ 的谱半径都小于一。则把 $u$ 看作 $A$-线性映射时，它严格完全连续。有限直和以及限制到带诱导范数的任意闭源子模，也有相同结论。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose $0<\theta<1$ larger than all spectral radii. The spectral-radius formula supplies a constant $C$ such that $\|t^\nu\|\leq C\theta^{|\nu|}$ for every multi-index. Replace the target norm by an equivalent norm making its $A$-action contractive. The degree-$d$ truncation $u_d$ has image in the finite $A$-module generated by $t^\nu$, $|\nu|<d$, and $\|u-u_d\|\leq C'\theta^d$ for one fixed $C'$. Choose $c\in O\setminus\{0\}$ so small that $\|ct^\nu\|\leq1$ for every $\nu$. Then}
{选择 $0<\theta<1$，使它大于所有谱半径。谱半径公式给出常数 $C$，使每个多重指标都满足 $\|t^\nu\|\leq C\theta^{|\nu|}$。将目标范数换成使 $A$-作用压缩的等价范数。次数小于 $d$ 的截断 $u_d$ 的像包含在 $t^\nu$、$|\nu|<d$ 生成的有限 $A$-模中，且对一个固定的 $C'$ 有 $\|u-u_d\|\leq C'\theta^d$。选择足够小的 $c\in O\setminus\{0\}$，使所有 $\nu$ 都满足 $\|ct^\nu\|\leq1$。于是}
\[
 c u_d\bigl(A\langle T\rangle_{\leq1}\bigr)
   \subset \sum_{|\nu|<d}A_0\,ct^\nu\subset B_{\leq1}.
\]
\bi{This proves the required uniform integral bound. For finitely many maps take a common small $c$; restriction of the approximants to a source submodule preserves the displayed bound and the operator convergence.}
{这就是所需的统一整系数界。对有限个映射取共同的足够小的 $c$；把逼近限制到源子模后，所列界以及算子范数收敛均保持。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\bi{A restriction to a smaller relative polydisc has finite-rank approximations over the coefficient algebra. To retain these approximations after passing to a closed module of matching sections, one needs a uniform integral bound. We establish that bound first. Throughout the first five results, $K$ is nontrivially valued, $O=K^\circ$, and $A$ is a strict $K$-affinoid algebra with a defining quotient norm. Write $A_0=A_{\leq1}$. Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations} identifies $A_0$ with a quotient of $O\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle$. Banach module actions are taken to be contractive. An initially bounded action can be made contractive by the equivalent norm $\|m\|_* =\sup_{a\ne0}\|am\|/\|a\|$.}
{缩小相对多圆盘后，限制映射具有系数代数上的有限秩逼近。若要把这些逼近限制到匹配截面组成的闭模，就需要统一的整系数界。下面先建立这个界。前五个结果中，$K$ 的赋值非平凡，$O=K^\circ$，$A$ 为配备定义商范数的 strict $K$-affinoid 代数。记 $A_0=A_{\leq1}$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affineformalpresentations}，$A_0$ 是某个 $O\langle X_1,\ldots,X_n\rangle$ 的商。Banach 模的作用取为压缩作用；原本有界的作用可通过等价范数 $\|m\|_* =\sup_{a\ne0}\|am\|/\|a\|$ 化为压缩作用。}
Standing context 2 · exact TeX
\begin{definition}[\bi{Relative complete continuity}{相对完全连续性}]
\label{fnd:relativecompletecontinuity}
\bi{A bounded $A$-linear map $g:E\to F$ between Banach modules is completely continuous if it is an operator-norm limit of bounded $A$-linear maps $h_i$ with finite $A$-module image. It is strictly completely continuous if these approximants can be chosen with a fixed $c\in O\setminus\{0\}$ such that $c h_i(E_{\leq1})$ is contained in a finite $A_0$-submodule of $F_{\leq1}$ for every $i$. The finite integral submodule may depend on $i$.}
{Banach 模间的有界 $A$-线性映射 $g:E\to F$ 称为完全连续，如果它是具有有限 $A$-模像的有界 $A$-线性映射 $h_i$ 的算子范数极限。如果可以选择这些逼近以及一个固定的 $c\in O\setminus\{0\}$，使每个 $c h_i(E_{\leq1})$ 都包含在 $F_{\leq1}$ 的某个有限 $A_0$-子模中，则称为严格完全连续。该有限整系数子模可以依赖于 $i$。}
\end{definition}
