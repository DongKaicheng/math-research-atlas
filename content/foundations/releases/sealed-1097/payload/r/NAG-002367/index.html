The free locus of a finite analytic module

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 993

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[The free locus of a finite analytic module]
\label{v037:degree:freelocus}
Let $A$ be a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra and let $M$ be
a finite $A$-module. The set
\[
 U_M=\{\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}(A):
                         M_{\mathfrak p}\text{ is free over }A_{\mathfrak p}\}
\]
is open. It is a finite union of principal opens $D(g_j)$
on each of which $M_{g_j}$ is finite free. If $M$ is free at
all minimal primes, then the ideal $I=(g_1,\ldots,g_t)$ is
contained in no minimal prime. This condition holds for
every finite $M$ when $A$ is reduced.

Let
\[
 U_M^{\rm an}=\{y\in\mathcal M(A):\ker(y)\in U_M\}.
\]
For every affinoid domain $V\subset U_M^{\rm an}$, its
restriction $M\otimes_AA_V$ is finite locally free as an
ordinary $A_V$-module. Its local bases give bounded
isomorphisms with the canonical free-module norms on
affinoid domains where the corresponding $g_j$ is a unit.

Suppose $M$ is free at all minimal primes, and let $E$ be any
actual Gauss algebra in
Proposition~\ref{v037:degree:genericgauss}. Then $IE=E$.
On a local factor $E_j$ whose residue prime contracts to
$\mathfrak p$, the module $M\otimes_AE_j$ is free of rank
$\operatorname{rank}_{A_{\mathfrak p}}M_{\mathfrak p}$.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
The ring $A$ is Noetherian and every finite module is finitely
presented, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}.
Suppose $M_{\mathfrak p}$ is free of rank $r$. A basis can be
represented by elements of $M$: multiply its finitely many
members by their respective denominators, which are units
in $A_{\mathfrak p}$. They give a map $A^r\to M$ whose
localization at $\mathfrak p$ is an isomorphism.
Its kernel and cokernel are finite and become zero at
$\mathfrak p$ by exactness of localization,
\cite[Tag 00HT]{StacksAlg}. For each member of finite
generating lists, vanishing after localization supplies an
annihilator outside $\mathfrak p$. The product of these
finitely many annihilators is an element $g\notin\mathfrak p$
for which $M_g\simeq A_g^r$. This proves openness and gives
a principal free neighborhood of every point of $U_M$.

Let $I$ be the ideal generated by all these elements $g$.
It is generated by finitely many of them, since $A$ is
Noetherian: express a finite generating set of $I$ using
finitely many of the original $g$'s. Thus
$U_M=D(I)=\bigcup_jD(g_j)$ with the asserted free bases.
If every minimal prime belongs to $U_M$, none contains $I$.
When $A$ is reduced, each $A_{\mathfrak p}$ at a minimal
prime is a reduced Artinian local ring, by
Proposition~\ref{v037:degree:genericgauss}. Its maximal ideal
is nilpotent by \cite[Tag 00JB]{StacksAlg} and hence zero;
it is a field. The finite
module $M_{\mathfrak p}$ is therefore free.

Empty domains are immediate. Let
$V=\mathcal M(A_V)\subset U_M^{\rm an}$ be nonempty. There is no
point of $V$ where all $g_j$ vanish. The quotient
$A_V/IA_V$ is an actual affinoid algebra with its canonical
norm, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras}, and has
empty spectrum. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion}
makes it zero. Hence $IA_V=A_V$ and the principal opens
$D(g_j)$ cover $\operatorname{Spec}(A_V)$. The given algebraic
bases base change on these opens and prove that
$M\otimes_AA_V$ is finite locally free.
If $W\subset V$ is an affinoid domain on which $g_j$ is a
unit, its corresponding basis gives an algebraic isomorphism
\[
                         M\otimes_AA_W\simeq A_W^{r_j}.
\]
Both maps are bounded for the canonical norms by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitemodules}. Such domains cover
$V$: compactness, Proposition~\ref{fnd:boundedspectrumcompact},
gives a positive lower bound for
$\max_j|g_j(y)|$, and the resulting finitely many Laurent
domains $|g_j|\geq\varepsilon$ cover. Their actual algebras
are given by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal}.

Finally, $IE=E$ follows from
Proposition~\ref{v037:degree:genericgauss}. For a local factor
over $\mathfrak p$, the map $A\to E_j$ factors through
$A_{\mathfrak p}$. Tensor its prescribed free basis with
$E_j$ to obtain the stated rank.
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
Throughout, $K$ is complete for its specified real-valued
non-Archimedean absolute value. The valuation may be trivial.
All affinoid algebras may be generalized, and finite algebra
maps carry their canonical finite-module Banach structures.
For a positive real polyradius $r$, write $R=A_{K,r}$ and
$H=H_{K,r}$ for the actual Laurent algebra and its Gauss field
in Section~\ref{v036:gaussdegreesection}.
