Finite-module sheaves on the finite rational site

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 267

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Finite-module sheaves on the finite rational site}{有限 rational site 上的有限模层}]
\label{fnd:generalizedrationalmodulesheaf}
\bi{Let $X=\mathcal M(A)$. Use as objects the rational subsets of $X$ with arbitrary positive radii, and as coverings their finite set-theoretic rational coverings. For a finite $A$-module $M$, the assignment}
{设 $X=\mathcal M(A)$。取 $X$ 中允许任意正半径的 rational 子集为对象，以其有限的集合意义 rational 覆盖为覆盖。对有限 $A$-模 $M$，对应}
\[
 \widetilde M(V)=A_V\otimes_A M
\]
\bi{with the unique bounded restrictions is a sheaf on this site. The assignment $M\mapsto\widetilde M$ is exact, $\Gamma(X,\widetilde M)=M$, and $\widetilde A$ is a sheaf of rings. This statement specifies a finite rational site; it does not identify it with the entire analytic G-site or assert a new coherence equivalence.}
{配备唯一有界限制映射后，是该 site 上的层。对应 $M\mapsto\widetilde M$ 为正合函子，且 $\Gamma(X,\widetilde M)=M$；其中 $\widetilde A$ 为环层。此陈述采用明确指定的有限 rational site，并不将它与整个 analytic G-site 识别，也不断言新的凝聚层等价。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The identity object is rational. Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedjointpresentations} gives rational intersections, and finite covers are preserved by pullback through them and by finite composition. These facts define the stated pretopology. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal} defines restrictions independently of the chosen presentation, with the required composition identities. If $V_i\subseteq V$ are rational over $A$, their defining tuples remain unit-generated in $A_V$. Their rational presentations over $A_V$ represent $V_i$ by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints}. The identity and restriction maps, together with uniqueness, identify these algebras boundedly with $A_{V_i}$. Equivalently the rational pullback algebra is $A_V\widehat\otimes_A A_{V_i}\simeq A_{V_i}$.}
{恒等对象是 rational。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedjointpresentations}，rational 子集的交仍为 rational，有限覆盖在通过这些交叠作拉回以及有限复合下保持。因此得到所述 pretopology。Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal} 给出不依赖表示选择的限制映射，并保证所需的复合恒等式。若 $V_i\subseteq V$ 都在 $A$ 上 rational，则它们的定义 tuple 在 $A_V$ 中仍生成单位理想。由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints}，其在 $A_V$ 上的 rational 表示仍表示 $V_i$。利用恒等与限制映射，再用唯一性，可将这些代数有界地识别为 $A_{V_i}$。等价地，相应的 rational 拉回代数满足 $A_V\widehat\otimes_A A_{V_i}\simeq A_{V_i}$。}

\bi{For a finite cover of $V$, apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfiniterationalcech} over the affinoid $A_V$ to its finite module $A_V\otimes_A M$. Ordinary tensor associativity identifies the degree-zero and degree-one terms with the claimed restrictions. Exactness at these degrees is precisely the sheaf equalizer condition. The empty object has zero algebra, so its sheaf value is zero. Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalflatness} proves exactness of the functor on each object, hence exactness as a functor to sheaves. At $V=X$, the identity presentation gives $A_X=A$, so its sections are $M$. Taking $M=A$, the restrictions are algebra homomorphisms and the equalizer respects multiplication, giving the asserted ring sheaf.}
{对 $V$ 的有限覆盖，在 affinoid $A_V$ 上，对有限模 $A_V\otimes_A M$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfiniterationalcech}。普通张量积结合律将零次及一次的项与所述限制识别；这些次数的正合性恰好是层的等化子条件。空对象的代数为零，所以其层值为零。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalflatness}，该函子在每个对象上正合，从而作为到层范畴的函子正合。在 $V=X$ 处，恒等表示给出 $A_X=A$，故截面为 $M$。取 $M=A$ 时，各限制都是代数同态，且等化子与乘法相容，因此得到所述环层。}
\end{proof}
