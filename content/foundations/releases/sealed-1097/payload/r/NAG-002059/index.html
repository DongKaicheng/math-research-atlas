The intrinsic open-disc homotopy

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 675

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The intrinsic open-disc homotopy}
{开圆盘的内在同伦}]
\label{cand:opendisccanonicalhomotopy}
\bi{Let $D$ be the open unit disc over a complete nontrivially
valued field $K$, and let $D^*=D\cup\{v\}$ be its one-point
compactification. Write}
{设 $D$ 为完备非平凡赋值域 $K$ 上的开单位圆盘，
$D^*=D\cup\{v\}$ 为其一点紧化。写成}
\[
 P(T+Z)=\sum_{j\geq0}P_j(T)Z^j\qquad(P\in K[T]).
\]
\bi{There is a jointly continuous strong deformation retraction
$H_D:D^*\times[0,1]\to D^*$ onto $\{v\}$, characterized for
$x\in D$ and $0\leq u<1$ by}
{存在到 $\{v\}$ 的联合连续强形变收缩
$H_D:D^*\times[0,1]\to D^*$，它对 $x\in D$、$0\leq u<1$
由下式刻画：}
\[
 |P(H_D(x,u))|=\max_j |P_j(x)|u^j,
 \qquad H_D(x,1)=v,\qquad H_D(v,u)=v.
\]
\bi{Every analytic isomorphism of open unit discs, including an
isomorphism semilinear for an isometric field automorphism,
commutes with this entire parametrized homotopy. The homotopy also
commutes with projection under any complete isometric ground-field
extension. In particular it gives a coordinate-independent
contraction of $C_q$ in Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure}.}
{开单位圆盘的每个解析同构，包括关于等距域自同构的半线性
同构，都与这一整个参数化同伦相容。同伦也与任意完备等距
基域扩张的投影相容。因此它给出
Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure} 中 $C_q$ 的坐标
无关收缩。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{For $u<1$, evaluate at $T(x)+Z$ over $\mathcal H(x)$ and then
take the radius-$u$ Gauss seminorm. It is multiplicative and has}
{当 $u<1$ 时，在 $\mathcal H(x)$ 上代入 $T(x)+Z$，然后取半径
$u$ 的 Gauss 半范数。这是乘性半范数，并满足}
\[
 |T(H_D(x,u))|=\max\{|T(x)|,u\}<1.
\]
\bi{Thus it defines a point of $D$, and at $u=0$ it gives $x$.
On a neighborhood of any $(x,u)\in D\times[0,1)$, choose
$r<1$ strictly larger than both $|T(x)|$ and $u$. The displayed
formula is continuous on polynomials and remains bounded on the
closed radius-$r$ disc; uniform polynomial approximation proves
continuity for its affinoid algebra. This proves joint continuity
on $D\times[0,1)$. At $v$, the preceding inequality preserves
every tail $|T|>r$, uniformly in $u$. As $u\to1$, it also puts all
images in $|T|>r$ for $u>r$, uniformly in $x$. The neighborhood
basis of Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure}, whose
one-point-compactification argument applies equally to $D^*$,
proves joint continuity at every remaining point. The strong
contraction identities follow from the construction.}
{因此它定义 $D$ 中的点，并在 $u=0$ 时等于 $x$。在任意
$(x,u)\in D\times[0,1)$ 的邻域，选择同时严格大于
$|T(x)|$ 及 $u$ 的 $r<1$。所列公式对多项式连续，并在半径
$r$ 的闭圆盘上有界；一致多项式逼近给出其 affinoid 代数上
的连续性。因此在 $D\times[0,1)$ 上联合连续。在 $v$ 处，
前一不等式对 $u$ 一致地保持每个尾部 $|T|>r$。当 $u\to1$
时，只要 $u>r$，它还使全部像落在 $|T|>r$ 中，且此控制对
$x$ 一致。Lemma~\ref{cand:smoothrationaltubeclosure} 的邻域基
论证同样适用于 $D^*$ 的一点紧化，故在其余各点也联合连续。
强收缩恒等式直接来自构造。}

\bi{We check coordinate independence explicitly. An analytic
isomorphism is represented by a power series
$g(T)=\sum_{n\geq0}a_nT^n$ convergent on every closed disc of
radius less than one. Such an expansion follows by restricting to
those discs and using uniqueness of Tate coefficients on overlaps.
Since $|g|<1$ on $D$, evaluation at its Gauss points and passage of
their radii to $1$ give $|a_n|\leq1$ for every $n$ and
$|a_0|<1$. The inverse has the same coefficient bounds. The
derivative identity for the two inverse series at $0$ implies
$|a_1|=1$: both $|g'(0)|$ and the derivative of the inverse at
$a_0$ are at most one and their product is one.}
{明确验证坐标独立性。解析同构由幂级数
$g(T)=\sum_{n\geq0}a_nT^n$ 表示，它在每个半径小于一的闭
圆盘上收敛。这一展开可由限制到各闭圆盘并使用交叠上 Tate
系数的唯一性得到。因为 $D$ 上 $|g|<1$，在 Gauss 点取值并让
半径趋于 $1$，得到全部 $|a_n|\leq1$ 及 $|a_0|<1$。
逆同构具有相同系数界。两个互逆级数在 $0$ 处的导数恒等式
给出 $|a_1|=1$：$|g'(0)|$ 与逆同构在 $a_0$ 处的导数绝对值
均至多为一，而二者乘积为一。}

\bi{Put $F=\mathcal H(x)$, $\alpha=T(x)$ and $\beta=g(x)$.
For $0<u<1$, expansion over $F$ gives}
{令 $F=\mathcal H(x)$、$\alpha=T(x)$、$\beta=g(x)$。
当 $0<u<1$ 时，在 $F$ 上展开得到}
\[
 g(\alpha+Z)=\beta+\sum_{j\geq1}b_jZ^j,
 \qquad |b_1|=1,\quad |b_j|\leq1\ (j\geq2).
\]
\bi{Indeed, $b_j=\sum_{n\geq j}a_n\binom nj\alpha^{n-j}$;
these sums converge, and for $j=1$ all terms after $a_1$ have
norm at most $|\alpha|<1$. On the radius-$u$ disc,}
{事实上，$b_j=\sum_{n\geq j}a_n\binom nj\alpha^{n-j}$；
这些和收敛，且在 $j=1$ 时，除 $a_1$ 外的各项范数均至多为
$|\alpha|<1$。在半径 $u$ 的圆盘上，}
\[
 g(\alpha+Z)-\beta=b_1Z(1+E(Z)),\qquad \|E\|_u\leq u<1.
\]
\bi{For a polynomial $Q(S)$ write
$Q(\beta+W)=\sum_jq_jW^j$ and put $M=\max_j|q_j|u^j$.
Replacing $W=b_1Z(1+E)$ by $b_1Z$ changes its Gauss norm by an
error at most $uM<M$ when $Q\ne0$. The Gauss norm of
$\sum_jq_jb_1^jZ^j$ is $M$. Thus}
{对多项式 $Q(S)$ 写出 $Q(\beta+W)=\sum_jq_jW^j$，并令
$M=\max_j|q_j|u^j$。将 $W=b_1Z(1+E)$ 换为 $b_1Z$，当
$Q\ne0$ 时所得误差的 Gauss 范数至多为 $uM<M$，而
$\sum_jq_jb_1^jZ^j$ 的 Gauss 范数等于 $M$。故}
\[
 \bigl\|Q(g(\alpha+Z))\bigr\|_u
       =\max_j|q_j|u^j.
\]
\bi{These are precisely the polynomial evaluations at
$g(H_D(x,u))$ and $H_D(g(x),u)$. Points of the open disc are
determined by polynomial evaluations, so the two points agree.
The identities at $u=0,1$ follow from the endpoints and the
extension of a disc isomorphism to its one-point compactification.
An isometric automorphism of the coefficient field preserves all
these formulas. Finally, under any complete isometric extension,
the formulas agree on the original polynomials by the isometric
embedding of completed residue fields. At the added endpoint the
projection sends the endpoint to the endpoint; continuity follows
also from its preservation of the radial tails. This proves all
the claimed compatibilities.}
{这些恰好分别是 $g(H_D(x,u))$ 与 $H_D(g(x),u)$ 处的多项式
取值。开圆盘的点由多项式取值确定，所以两点相等。
$u=0,1$ 的恒等式来自端值，以及圆盘同构到其一点紧化的
延拓。系数域的等距自同构保持全部公式。最后，在任意完备
等距扩域下，由完备剩余域的等距嵌入，上述公式在原多项式上
相同。新增端点在投影下映到新增端点；投影保持径向尾部也
直接给出端点处的连续性。这证明全部相容性。}
\end{proof}
