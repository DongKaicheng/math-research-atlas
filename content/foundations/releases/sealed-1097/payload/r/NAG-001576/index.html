\begin{theorem}[\bi{Points of an arbitrary-radius rational presentation}{任意半径 rational 表示的谱点}]
\label{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints}
\bi{Let $A$ be a norm-admissible $K$-affinoid algebra, let $\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_s>0$, and suppose $(g,f_1,\ldots,f_s)=A$. With the coefficient norm and its quotient norm, put}
{设 $A$ 为 norm-admissible $K$-affinoid 代数，$\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_s>0$，且 $(g,f_1,\ldots,f_s)=A$。采用系数范数及其商范数，记}
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 D&=A\{\rho^{-1}T\},\qquad
 B=D/(gT_i-f_i)_{i=1}^s,\\
 V&=\{x\in\mathcal M(A):|f_i(x)|\leq\rho_i|g(x)|\ \forall i\}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
\bi{Then $B$ is a norm-admissible $K$-affinoid algebra, possibly zero, and restriction gives a homeomorphism $\mathcal M(B)\simeq V$. Moreover $B=0$ if and only if $V$ is empty. The case $s=0$ is allowed and gives $B=A$, since $g$ is then a unit.}
{则 $B$ 为 norm-admissible $K$-affinoid 代数，允许为零；限制映射给出同胚 $\mathcal M(B)\simeq V$。此外，$B=0$ 当且仅当 $V$ 为空。允许 $s=0$，此时 $g$ 为单位，且 $B=A$。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation} makes $D$ affinoid, and Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients} gives the asserted complete actual quotient $B$. Choose a unit-ideal identity $a_0g+\sum_i a_if_i=1$. In $B$ it becomes}
{由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:closedweightedpresentation}，$D$ 为 affinoid；Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients} 给出所述完备的实际商 $B$。选择单位理想恒等式 $a_0g+\sum_i a_if_i=1$。它在 $B$ 中变为}
\[
 g\left(a_0+\sum_i a_i\bar T_i\right)=1.
\]
\bi{Thus $g$ is a unit in $B$, with the usual zero-ring interpretation. At every $x\in V$, the same identity and the inequalities force $|g(x)|>0$.}
{所以 $g$ 在 $B$ 中为单位；零环按通常约定理解。在每个 $x\in V$ 上，同一恒等式及相应不等式迫使 $|g(x)|>0$。}

\bi{For $x\in V$, Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive} gives the valued fraction field $F_x=\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$. Polynomial evaluation at $T_i=f_i/g$ in this field has multiplicative seminorm $p_x$ bounded by the weighted coefficient norm:}
{对 $x\in V$，由 Lemma~\ref{new:seminormcontractive}，可取赋值分式域 $F_x=\operatorname{Frac}(A/\ker x)$。在该域中令 $T_i=f_i/g$，多项式取值所给的乘性半范数 $p_x$ 被加权系数范数控制：}
\[
 p_x\left(\sum_\alpha a_\alpha T^\alpha\right)
 \leq\max_\alpha |a_\alpha(x)|\prod_i|f_i/g|_x^{\alpha_i}
 \leq\max_\alpha\|a_\alpha\|\rho^\alpha.
\]
\bi{For polynomials $P,Q$, $|p_x(P)-p_x(Q)|\leq\|P-Q\|$. Hence norm-convergent polynomial truncations extend $p_x$ uniquely to a seminorm on $D$. Addition, multiplication and its scalar values pass to the limit, because products of bounded convergent sequences converge. It kills all $gT_i-f_i$ and their ideal. Taking the infimum over representatives gives a contractive multiplicative seminorm $y_x$ on $B$ extending $x$. No completion of $F_x$ is needed here.}
{对多项式 $P,Q$，有 $|p_x(P)-p_x(Q)|\leq\|P-Q\|$。因此通过范数收敛的多项式截断，将 $p_x$ 唯一延拓为 $D$ 上的半范数。由于有界收敛列的乘积仍收敛，加法、乘法及标量取值均可传到极限。该半范数零化全部 $gT_i-f_i$ 及其生成的理想。对代表元取下确界，便得到 $B$ 上延拓 $x$ 的 contractive 乘性半范数 $y_x$。这里不需要对 $F_x$ 作完备化。}

\bi{Conversely, a point $y$ of $B$ is contractive for its quotient norm, so $|\bar T_i(y)|\leq\rho_i$. Its restriction belongs to $V$. To prove uniqueness over a given $x$, take a polynomial $P=\sum a_\alpha T^\alpha$ of total degree at most $N$ and form}
{反之，$B$ 的谱点 $y$ 对商范数为 contractive，故 $|\bar T_i(y)|\leq\rho_i$，其限制属于 $V$。为证明给定 $x$ 上方的唯一性，取总次数不超过 $N$ 的多项式 $P=\sum a_\alpha T^\alpha$，并令}
\[
 Q_P=\sum_\alpha a_\alpha f^\alpha g^{N-|\alpha|}\in A.
\]
\bi{In $B$ one has $g^NP(\bar T)=Q_P$, so every point over $x$ must satisfy $|P(\bar T)(y)|=|Q_P(x)|/|g(x)|^N$. The polynomial image is dense in $B$, and contractivity determines the remaining values uniquely. This proves the bijection. For a polynomial, the displayed value is a continuous function of $x\in V$. For any $b\in B$, choose a lift $F\in D$ and polynomial truncations $P_n\to F$. Uniformly in $x\in V$,}
{在 $B$ 中有 $g^NP(\bar T)=Q_P$，所以位于 $x$ 上方的每个点都必须满足 $|P(\bar T)(y)|=|Q_P(x)|/|g(x)|^N$。多项式像在 $B$ 中稠密，contractivity 唯一确定其余元素的值。因此限制映射双射。对多项式，上述取值是 $x\in V$ 的连续函数。对任意 $b\in B$，选取提升 $F\in D$ 及多项式截断 $P_n\to F$，则关于 $x\in V$ 一致有}
\[
 \bigl||b(y_x)|-|P_n(\bar T)(y_x)|\bigr|\leq\|F-P_n\|.
\]
\bi{Thus all evaluations along $x\mapsto y_x$ are continuous. Restriction is also continuous by its definition, proving the homeomorphism. Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion} applied to $B$ proves the assertion about emptiness. Zero algebras and an empty $V$ require no exceptional point construction.}
{因此，沿 $x\mapsto y_x$ 的全部取值函数都连续。限制映射按定义也连续，故为同胚。对 $B$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion}，得到关于空集的断言。零代数和空 $V$ 不需要额外的点构造。}
\end{proof}
