Finite filtrations and zero-dimensional algebras

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 881

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Finite filtrations and zero-dimensional algebras}{有限滤过与零维代数}]
\label{cand:finiteprimefiltration}
\bi{Every finite module over a Noetherian ring has a finite filtration whose nonzero successive quotients are $A/\mathfrak p$ for prime ideals $\mathfrak p$. A zero-dimensional algebra of finite type over a field $k$ is finite-dimensional over $k$.}
{Noetherian 环上的每个有限模具有有限滤过，其非零相继商均形如 $A/\mathfrak p$，其中 $\mathfrak p$ 为素理想。域 $k$ 上的有限型零维代数在 $k$ 上有限维。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Finite free modules over a Noetherian ring are Noetherian by induction on their rank, using the first-coordinate ideal and the kernel of that coordinate; hence their quotients are Noetherian. In a nonzero finite module choose a nonzero element whose annihilator is maximal among annihilators of nonzero elements. Such a maximum exists by the ascending chain condition. Its annihilator $\mathfrak p$ is prime: if $ab$ annihilates the element and $b$ does not, the annihilator of its nonzero $b$-multiple is the same maximal annihilator, so $a\in\mathfrak p$. This yields a submodule $A/\mathfrak p$. Repeat in the quotient. The resulting ascending chain of submodules terminates.}
{先按秩归纳，利用第一个坐标的像理想及其核，说明 Noetherian 环上的有限自由模是 Noetherian 模；故其商也 Noetherian。在非零有限模中，选取一个非零元素，使其零化理想在非零元素的零化理想中极大；升链条件保证可作此选择。该零化理想 $\mathfrak p$ 为素理想：若 $ab$ 消去所选元素而 $b$ 不消去，则其非零 $b$ 倍的零化理想仍为同一极大理想，故 $a\in\mathfrak p$。于是得到子模 $A/\mathfrak p$。在商模中重复；所得子模升链必终止。}

\bi{For the second assertion, finite-type $k$-algebras are Noetherian by \cite[Tag 00FN]{StacksAlg}. Apply the filtration to the algebra itself. Each prime quotient is a zero-dimensional finite-type domain. Its fraction field has transcendence degree zero by \cite[Tag 00P0]{StacksAlg}. Its finitely many algebra generators are therefore algebraic over $k$, so the quotient is a finite-dimensional field. A finite extension of these vector spaces is finite-dimensional.}
{对第二个断言，由 \cite[Tag 00FN]{StacksAlg}，有限型 $k$-代数 Noetherian。将前述滤过用于代数自身。每个素商均为零维有限型整环，由 \cite[Tag 00P0]{StacksAlg}，其分式域在 $k$ 上超越次数为零。因此其有限个代数生成元均在 $k$ 上代数，故此素商是有限维域。有限个此类向量空间的扩张仍有限维。}
\end{proof}
