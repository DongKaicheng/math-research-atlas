Standing definition · exact TeX
\bi{The acyclicity theorem already gives a useful sheaf construction. We specify the site so that no theorem about arbitrary affinoid domains is concealed in its definition. Let $A$ be a strict $K$-affinoid algebra over a complete field with its fixed non-Archimedean absolute value, allowing trivial valuation, and put $X=\mathcal M(A)$.}
{刚证明的无环性定理已经可以给出结构层。下面明确指定所用的 site，从而不在定义中隐藏关于任意 affinoid 域的定理。设 $A$ 是完备域 $K$ 上的 strict affinoid 代数，固定其非阿基米德绝对值，允许赋值平凡，并令 $X=\mathcal M(A)$。}

\begin{definition}[\bi{The fixed rational pretopology}{固定 rational 预拓扑}]\label{fnd:fixedrationalpretopology}
\bi{Let $\mathcal R_X$ be the partially ordered category of subsets of $X$ admitting a rational presentation over the original algebra $A$ with radii in $|K^\times|$. It contains $X$ and the empty set. Arrows are inclusions. A covering family of $V\in\mathcal R_X$ is a finite family $(V_i\subseteq V)$ of objects of $\mathcal R_X$ whose union is $V$. The empty family covers the empty set. Choose one rational presentation algebra $A_V$ for each $V$, with $A_X=A$ and $A_\varnothing=0$, and use the canonical bounded restriction maps of Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaldomainup}. For a finite $A$-module $M$, write}
{令 $\mathcal R_X$ 为如下子集构成的偏序范畴：它们在原始代数 $A$ 上具有半径属于 $|K^\times|$ 的 rational 表示。该范畴包含 $X$ 与空集，态射为包含映射。对象 $V\in\mathcal R_X$ 的一个覆盖是由 $\mathcal R_X$ 中对象组成的有限族 $(V_i\subseteq V)$，且其并等于 $V$。空族覆盖空集。对每个 $V$ 选择一个 rational 表示代数 $A_V$，并取 $A_X=A$、$A_\varnothing=0$；限制映射采用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictrationaldomainup} 给出的典范有界映射。对有限 $A$-模 $M$，记}
\[
 \mathcal O_X^{\mathrm{rat}}(V)=A_V,
 \qquad \widetilde M^{\mathrm{rat}}(V)=A_V\otimes_A M.
\]
\end{definition}
