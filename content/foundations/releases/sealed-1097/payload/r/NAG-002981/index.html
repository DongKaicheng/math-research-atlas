Remaining historical proof gap

The sealed ledger records a gap in the proof of the historical whole statement.

Historical statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Conditional reduction beyond \(S_1\); F8CR-P]
\label{thm:pl}
Let \(X\) be good, Hausdorff, and pure of analytic dimension \(n\geq1\),
let \(f\) be a regular analytic function, and let
\(\eta\in\cA_{X,c}^{n-1,n-1}\) have support in \(\Int(X/K)\).
Assume ZP\(_{\rm loc}\) on the finitely many affinoid neighbourhoods
used below, and RPL on their reduced irreducible components. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pl}
              L_f(\dd\eta)=\int_{\divis_X(f)}\eta.
\end{equation}
In particular, after those inputs are reviewed and admitted, the
ambient \(S_1\) hypothesis is unnecessary for this scalar identity.
This is a scoped implication, not an unconditional assertion of
Poincar\'e--Lelong, continuity of a current, or a boundary trace formula.
\end{theorem}
