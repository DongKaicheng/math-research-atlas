Reduction of a fibre-product germ

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 571

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Reduction of a fibre-product germ}
{纤维积芽的约化}]
\label{fnd:gradedgermproducts}
\bi{Let $T=Y\times_X Z$ and let $t$ have images $y,z,x$.
Put $F=\widetilde{\mathcal H(t)}^{\rm gr}$. Pull the three
reductions back to $\mathscr P_{\widetilde K^{\rm gr}}(F)$,
and denote them by $Y_0,Z_0,X_0$. Then}
{设 $T=Y\times_X Z$，点 $t$ 的像分别为 $y,z,x$。
置 $F=\widetilde{\mathcal H(t)}^{\rm gr}$。
把三个约化拉回到 $\mathscr P_{\widetilde K^{\rm gr}}(F)$，
分别记为 $Y_0,Z_0,X_0$。则}
\[
       \mathscr R(T,t)\simeq Y_0\times_{X_0}Z_0 .
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{First take $X=\mathcal M(A)$, $Y=\mathcal M(B)$ and
$Z=\mathcal M(C)$. Write $R_A,R_B,R_C,R_D$ for the
images in $F$ of the graded reductions, where
$D=B\widehat\otimes_A C$. Corollary
\ref{fnd:gradedtensorfinitecomparison} says that $R_D$
is finite over the graded subalgebra generated by $R_B,R_C$.
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedvaluationintegrality} consequently gives}
{先设 $X=\mathcal M(A)$、$Y=\mathcal M(B)$、
$Z=\mathcal M(C)$。将各分次约化在 $F$ 中的像记为
$R_A,R_B,R_C,R_D$，其中 $D=B\widehat\otimes_A C$。
Corollary~\ref{fnd:gradedtensorfinitecomparison} 说明
$R_D$ 在 $R_B,R_C$ 生成的分次子代数上有限。
因此 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gradedvaluationintegrality} 给出}
\[
 \mathscr P(F)\{R_D\}
   =\mathscr P(F)\{R_B,R_C\}
   =\mathscr P(F)\{R_B\}
           \times_{\mathscr P(F)\{R_A\}}\mathscr P(F)\{R_C\}.
\]
\bi{These are identities of open subspaces with their structural
maps, not just identities of points. In general choose finite
affinoid subdomain-germ covers of the three spaces, refining the
inverse image of the target cover. Their products cover the
product germ by the analytic fibre-product construction.
The displayed identifications agree on overlaps by the
inverse-image compatibility in
Theorem~\ref{fnd:allgradedgermreduction}. They therefore glue
to the asserted isomorphism.}
{这些是带结构映射的开子空间恒等式，而不只是点集相等。
一般情形中，对三个空间选取有限 affinoid 子域芽覆盖，
并使源覆盖加细目标覆盖的逆像。由解析纤维积构造，这些片
的乘积覆盖乘积芽。根据
Theorem~\ref{fnd:allgradedgermreduction} 的逆像相容性，
上述识别在重叠上相等，因而粘合为所需同构。}
\end{proof}
