Dimension of an affinoid domain in affine space

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 459

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Dimension of an affinoid domain in affine space}{仿射空间中 affinoid 域的维数}]
\label{fnd:affinespacedomaindimensionbound}
\bi{Let $F$ be a complete non-Archimedean field and let $V=\mathcal M(C)$ be a generalized affinoid domain of $\mathbb A_F^{d,\mathrm{an}}$. Then $\dim C\leq d$.}
{设 $F$ 为完备非阿基米德域，$V=\mathcal M(C)$ 为 $\mathbb A_F^{d,\mathrm{an}}$ 的 generalized affinoid 域。则 $\dim C\leq d$。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Fix a maximal ideal $\mathfrak q$ of $C$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity} realizes it as the kernel of a point $z\in V$. Choose a nontrivially valued complete extension $E/\mathcal H(z)$, using $E=\mathcal H(z)$ when possible and otherwise adjoining a free radius. Evaluation defines an $E$-rational point $w\in V_E$ above $z$. Rational-domain flatness and scalar-stalk faithful flatness give faithfully flat local maps}
{固定 $C$ 的极大理想 $\mathfrak q$。Lemma~\ref{fnd:genericanalyticpointdensity} 将其实现为某点 $z\in V$ 的核。选择赋值非平凡的完备扩张 $E/\mathcal H(z)$：若可能，取 $E=\mathcal H(z)$，否则添入一个自由半径。取值映射定义 $z$ 上方的 $E$-有理点 $w\in V_E$。Rational 域平坦性与基域扩张的茎忠实平坦性给出忠实平坦局部映射}
\[
 C_{\mathfrak q}\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{V,z}
                    \longrightarrow\mathcal O_{V_E,w}.
\]
\bi{Since $V_E$ is an analytic domain of affine $d$-space and $w$ is rational, Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalpointdomaininterior} identifies the last ring with the ordinary affine-space germ at $w$. After translating the coordinates it is the ring of convergent power series in $d$ variables of Lemma~\ref{fnd:convergentgermnoetherian}; its completion is $E[[T_1,\ldots,T_d]]$. It is a regular local ring of dimension $d$: regularity follows by faithful-flat descent from that formal power-series ring, and its maximal ideal and cotangent space are generated freely to first order by the $d$ coordinate germs. Finally, a chain of primes of $C_{\mathfrak q}$ lifts, successively downward from the maximal ideal, along the displayed flat local maps by \cite[Tag 00HS]{StacksAlg}. Its length is therefore at most $d$. This holds at every maximal ideal of $C$, and every prime chain is contained in one, proving the assertion.}
{因 $V_E$ 为仿射 $d$ 维空间的解析域且 $w$ 有理，Lemma~\ref{fnd:rationalpointdomaininterior} 将最后一个环识别为 $w$ 处的普通仿射空间芽。平移坐标后，它就是 Lemma~\ref{fnd:convergentgermnoetherian} 的 $d$ 变量收敛幂级数环，其完备化为 $E[[T_1,\ldots,T_d]]$。它是维数 $d$ 的正则局部环：正则性从形式幂级数环经忠实平坦下降得到，而其极大理想由 $d$ 个坐标芽生成，且这些坐标在余切空间中线性无关。最后，由 \cite[Tag 00HS]{StacksAlg}，$C_{\mathfrak q}$ 中的任意素理想链，可沿上述平坦局部映射从极大理想开始逐次向下提升，故其长度至多为 $d$。对 $C$ 的每个极大理想均成立，且任意素理想链包含于某个极大理想之下，遂得结论。}
\end{proof}
