Analytification preserves smooth morphisms

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 309

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Analytification preserves smooth morphisms}
{解析化保持光滑态射}]
\label{fnd:analytificationsmoothpreservation}
\bi{If $f:Y\to X$ is a smooth morphism of finite-type
$K$-schemes, then $f^{\an}:Y^{\an}\to X^{\an}$ is smooth.}
{若 $f:Y\to X$ 是有限型 $K$-概形之间的光滑态射，
则 $f^{\an}:Y^{\an}\to X^{\an}$ 光滑。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{At the scheme image of any analytic source point,
\cite[Tag 054L]{StacksAlg} gives Zariski open
neighbourhoods $Y_0,X_0$ and a factorization}
{在任意解析源点的概形像处，\cite[Tag 054L]{StacksAlg}
给出 Zariski 开邻域 $Y_0,X_0$ 及分解}
\[
 Y_0\xrightarrow{\ \text{\'etale}\ }\mathbf A^d_{X_0}
                          \longrightarrow X_0.
\]
\bi{Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationzariskiopens}
makes their analytifications ordinary open
neighbourhoods. By Theorem~\ref{fnd:analytificationproducts},
the analytification of the middle space is the relative
analytic affine space over $X_0^{\an}$; its projection
is the usual analytic projection. The first arrow is
\'etale after analytification by
Theorem~\ref{fnd:analytificationetalepreservation}.
This is exactly the defining local smooth factorization.}
{Proposition~\ref{fnd:analytificationzariskiopens} 使这些
邻域的解析化成为通常开邻域。由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:analytificationproducts}，中间空间的
解析化就是 $X_0^{\an}$ 上的相对解析仿射空间，其投影为
通常的解析投影。由
Theorem~\ref{fnd:analytificationetalepreservation}，
第一箭头解析化后为 \'etale。
这正是定义光滑性所需的局部分解。}
\end{proof}
