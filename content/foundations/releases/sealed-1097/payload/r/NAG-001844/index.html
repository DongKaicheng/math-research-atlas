Complete-extension tests for an affinoid domain

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 367

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Complete-extension tests for an affinoid domain}{Affinoid 域的完备扩域测试}]
\label{fnd:generalizeddomainextensiontests}
\bi{Let $A\to B$ represent an affinoid domain $U$ using generalized tests over $K$. For every complete isometric extension $L/K$ and every norm-admissible $L$-affinoid algebra $C$, a bounded $K$-map $\alpha:A\to C$ whose spectral image lies in $U$ has a unique bounded $K$-factorization through $B$. Thus the same-$K$ generalized domain property and the domain property using all complete-extension affinoid tests are equivalent, with the same representing algebra.}
{设 $A\to B$ 通过 $K$ 上的 generalized 测试表示 affinoid 域 $U$。对每个完备等距扩域 $L/K$ 及每个 norm-admissible $L$-affinoid 代数 $C$，若有界 $K$-映射 $\alpha:A\to C$ 的谱像包含于 $U$，则它具有唯一的、通过 $B$ 的有界 $K$-分解。因此，使用同一 $K$ 上 generalized 测试的域泛性质，与使用全部完备扩域 affinoid 测试的域泛性质等价，且表示代数保持相同。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose the finite original-$A$ rational covering $U=\bigcup_iR_i$ from Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert}, and write $B_i=A_{R_i}$ and $B_{ij}=A_{R_i\cap R_j}$. They form a rational covering of $\mathcal M(B)$ by Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainrestriction}. Pull the original rational tuples into $C$. Their actual quotient algebras $C_i$ and $C_{ij}$ form a finite rational covering of $\mathcal M_L(C)$ and its intersections, by Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints} and Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedjointpresentations}. The covering assertion follows from the spectral-image hypothesis.}
{由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedgerritzengrauert} 选取有限原 $A$-rational 覆盖 $U=\bigcup_iR_i$，记 $B_i=A_{R_i}$ 及 $B_{ij}=A_{R_i\cap R_j}$。根据 Lemma~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainrestriction}，它们构成 $\mathcal M(B)$ 的 rational 覆盖。将原 rational tuples 映入 $C$，所得实际商代数 $C_i,C_{ij}$，由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationalpoints} 及 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedjointpresentations} 给出 $\mathcal M_L(C)$ 的有限 rational 覆盖及其交。覆盖性来自谱像的假设。}

\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal}, with its complete-extension test clause, gives bounded maps $B_i\to C_i$. After restricting to $C_{ij}$, the same theorem extends each map through the rational overlap algebra $B_{ij}$. The two resulting maps $B_{ij}=A_{R_i\cap R_j}\to C_{ij}$ agree: they extend the same map from $A$ and send every rational variable to its prescribed ratio. Composing with $B\to B_i$ consequently gives a bounded map from $B$ to the matching kernel of $\prod_i C_i\rightrightarrows\prod_{i,j}C_{ij}$. Degree-zero norm-strict exactness in Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfiniterationalcech}, applied over $L$ to the finite $C$-module $C$, identifies this kernel with $C$, with bounded inverse. The resulting map $B\to C$ is bounded, preserves multiplication and the unit, and extends $\alpha$, because these assertions hold after the injective restriction into $\prod_iC_i$.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedrationaluniversal} 中的完备扩域测试断言给出有界映射 $B_i\to C_i$。限制到 $C_{ij}$ 后，同一定理使每个映射延拓为从 rational 交叠代数 $B_{ij}$ 出发的映射。所得两个映射 $B_{ij}=A_{R_i\cap R_j}\to C_{ij}$ 相等：它们延拓同一个从 $A$ 出发的映射，并把每个 rational 变量送到指定比值。因此，复合 $B\to B_i$ 后，得到从 $B$ 到 $\prod_i C_i\rightrightarrows\prod_{i,j}C_{ij}$ 的 matching kernel 的有界映射。在 $L$ 上对有限 $C$-模 $C$ 应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedfiniterationalcech}，其中零次的 norm-strict 正合性将该核与 $C$ 识别，且逆映射有界。所得 $B\to C$ 有界、保持乘法及单位元，并延拓 $\alpha$，因为这些断言在单射限制到 $\prod_iC_i$ 后成立。}

\bi{For uniqueness, let $\beta:B\to C$ be any bounded factorization. After restricting to $C_i$, its spectral image in $\mathcal M_K(B)$ lies over $R_i$, hence in the rational $B$-domain represented by $B_i$. The same rational universal property factors $B\to C_i$ through $B_i$. This map $B_i=A_{R_i}\to C_i$ is the unique map determined by $\alpha$ and the original rational ratios. It is therefore the map used in the construction. Injectivity of $C\to\prod_iC_i$ proves uniqueness. If the spectral image is empty, Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion} over $L$ gives $C=0$, for which the factorization is unique. The converse implication follows by taking $L=K$.}
{为证明唯一性，设 $\beta:B\to C$ 是任一有界分解。限制到 $C_i$ 后，其在 $\mathcal M_K(B)$ 中的谱像位于 $R_i$ 上方，因而属于由 $B_i$ 表示的 rational $B$-域。再次使用 rational 泛性质，使 $B\to C_i$ 通过 $B_i$ 分解。这个映射 $B_i=A_{R_i}\to C_i$ 由 $\alpha$ 及原 rational 比值唯一确定，所以正是构造中使用的映射。由 $C\to\prod_iC_i$ 的单射性得到唯一性。若谱像为空，在 $L$ 上使用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointunitcriterion}，可得 $C=0$，此时分解唯一。反方向取 $L=K$ 即得。}
\end{proof}
