Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Meromorphic scalar PL from the reviewed analytic input; F8MG-P]
\label{thm:meromorphic}
Under R-SPL and R-ZP in the category above, every regular meromorphic
\(f\) satisfies, for every ordinary
\(\eta\in\A^{n-1,n-1}_{U,c}\),
\begin{equation}\label{fnd:nag002990:fixed-test-meromorphic-pl}
                    L_f(\dd\eta)=\delta_{\divis(f)}(\eta),
 \qquad \delta_D(\eta):=\int_D\eta.
\end{equation}
This is first an equality of fixed-test pairings, before a continuity
claim is made.
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Use quotient neighbourhoods and a finite compact smooth partition
\(\rho_j\) near \(\Supp\eta\). Put \(\eta_j=\rho_j\eta\).
Each \(\eta_j\) has compact support in its ordinary quotient
neighbourhood and in the true analytic interior. R-SPL applies
separately to \(a_j\) and \(b_j\) on that neighbourhood. Subtract:
\[
 L_f(\dd\eta_j)
   =\int_{V(a_j)}\eta_j-\int_{V(b_j)}\eta_j
   =\delta_{\divis(f)}(\eta_j).
\]
Here \(\Supp\dd\eta_j\subseteq\Supp\eta_j\), so the local pairing
agrees with the global pairing of Proposition~\ref{prop:quotient}.
Since \(\eta=\sum_j\eta_j\) as ordinary sections, differentiation
gives \(\dd\eta=\sum_j\dd\eta_j\). In particular the terms
\[
 \dd(\rho_j\eta)=\rho_j\dd\eta+d'\rho_j\wedge d''\eta
       -d''\rho_j\wedge d'\eta+\dd\rho_j\wedge\eta
\]
are all retained. Their sums, rather than individual terms, use
\(\sum\rho_j=1\) near the support. Adding the finite identities
proves \eqref{fnd:nag002990:fixed-test-meromorphic-pl}. No component reduction, new annular
Green assertion, or limit in a current topology enters this proof.
\end{proof}
