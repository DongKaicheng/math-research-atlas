Codimension-one coefficients under a modification

I Descent of the polarized mixed tower

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[Codimension-one coefficients under a modification]
\label{f9md:codimone}
Let $p$ be a displayed modification. For each prime divisor $V$ of
$\cX$ there is exactly one prime divisor $\widetilde V$ of $\cY$
dominating it, and their ordinary local DVRs are identified inside
$K(X)$. For a Cartier divisor $D$ with rational section presentation,
\begin{equation}\label{ex68:model:eq:divpush}
                    p_*\cyc(p^*D)=\cyc(D).
\end{equation}
The pushforward uses full residue-field degrees on equal-dimensional
images and zero on dimension-decreasing images. In particular, for
special components, $b_{\widetilde V}=b_V$ and
$\xi_{\widetilde V}=\xi_V$. Every exceptional special component has
image of dimension at most $d-1$.

The codimension-one isomorphism hypothesis is checkable for a blowup
of a finite-type ideal $I$ with $I|_X=\cO_X$: it holds wherever $I$ is
locally principal and nonzero, hence on an open containing every
height-one point of the normal model. This assertion concerns the
blowup itself; any additional normalization or domination needs its own
compatibility check.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Over the generic point of a prime divisor $V$, the morphism is an
isomorphism. Taking the closure of its inverse-image point gives
$\widetilde V$ and identifies its local ring and residue field with
those of $V$. No second divisor can dominate $V$, since it would also
meet this isomorphic inverse image. Any other prime divisor lies over
the complement of $U$, so its image has smaller dimension. Properness
makes that image closed. The coefficient of $p^*D$ at
$\widetilde V$ is the coefficient of $D$ at $V$, because the same
rational function is measured in the same DVR. Its pushforward degree
is one, and the other coefficients push forward to zero. This proves
\eqref{ex68:model:eq:divpush}. Apply it locally to $\pi$ and to arbitrary rational
functions to obtain the assertions about $b$ and $\xi$.

At a height-one point $v$, $I_v$ is a nonzero finitely generated ideal
in a DVR and thus is principal, say $(a)$. After shrinking a
neighbourhood, $a$ is a section of $I$ and every chosen finite generator
of $I$ is a multiple of $a$; clearing the finitely many localization
denominators proves this assertion on an open. On that open, the Rees
algebra is $\bigoplus_{n\geq0}(a^n)T^n\simeq A[t]$, since $a$ is
a non-zero-divisor. Its Proj is $\operatorname{Spec}A$. The union of
these opens and the generic fibre gives the required $U$ for the blowup.
\end{proof}
