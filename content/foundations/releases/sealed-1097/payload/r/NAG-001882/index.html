The ambient germ of an exact-radius graph

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 477

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The ambient germ of an exact-radius graph}
{恰好收敛半径图像的环境芽}]
\label{fnd:exactradiusgraphfield}
\bi{Let $f(T)=\sum_{n\geq0}a_nT^n\in K\langle T\rangle$
have convergence radius exactly one. Put
$D=\mathcal M(K\langle T\rangle)$, let $\eta$ be its Gauss
point, and set $x=(\eta,f(\eta))$ in
$\mathbb A_K^{2,\mathrm{an}}$. Then
$\mathcal O_{\mathbb A_K^{2,\mathrm{an}},x}$ is a field.}
{设 $f(T)=\sum_{n\geq0}a_nT^n\in K\langle T\rangle$
的收敛半径恰好为一。令 $D=\mathcal M(K\langle T\rangle)$，
其 Gauss 点记为 $\eta$，并令
$x=(\eta,f(\eta))\in\mathbb A_K^{2,\mathrm{an}}$。
则 $\mathcal O_{\mathbb A_K^{2,\mathrm{an}},x}$ 是域。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{\emph{Step 1: a hypothetical vanishing germ gives a curve.}
Write $p$ for the first projection, put $X=p^{-1}(D)$,
and let $Z\subset X$ be the graph of $f$, with isomorphism
$p|_Z:Z\simeq D$. Its point $x$ has
$\mathcal H(x)=\mathcal H(\eta)$: the evaluated first
coordinate already has dense fraction field in
$\mathcal H(\eta)$, and the second coordinate belongs to
that field. Thus $d_K(x)=1$, by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:gaussvaluedfielddimension},
and $x$ is not rigid.}
{\emph{第一步：假设存在非零消失芽，构造一条曲线。}
以 $p$ 记第一投影，令 $X=p^{-1}(D)$，并以 $Z\subset X$
记 $f$ 的图像，带有同构 $p|_Z:Z\simeq D$。
其点 $x$ 满足 $\mathcal H(x)=\mathcal H(\eta)$：第一坐标
求值后所得的分式域已在 $\mathcal H(\eta)$ 中稠密，而第二
坐标也属于该域。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gaussvaluedfielddimension}，
有 $d_K(x)=1$，所以
$x$ 非 rigid。}

\bi{Suppose a nonzero ordinary germ $h$ at $x$ vanishes
there. Choose a strict affinoid neighborhood
$V=\mathcal M(A)$ representing it. By
Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomainregular} and
Lemma~\ref{fnd:regularaffinoidcomponents}, we may retain
the open-and-closed integral component containing $x$.
The element $h\in A$ remains nonzero. This domain has
dimension at most two, by
Corollary~\ref{fnd:affinoiddimensiondomaincover} applied
inside a sufficiently large strict bidisc.
Therefore the reduced closed subspace
$W=\mathcal M(A/\sqrt{(h)})$ has dimension at most one:
every prime containing $h$ is nonzero, and a chain in that
quotient can be preceded by the zero prime of $A$.
It contains $x$, so its dimension at $x$ is one by the
Abhyankar inequality.}
{假设存在 $x$ 处的非零通常芽 $h$，且它在该点消失。
选择表示它的 strict affinoid 邻域 $V=\mathcal M(A)$。
由 Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinespacedomainregular} 与
Lemma~\ref{fnd:regularaffinoidcomponents}，可只保留包含
$x$ 的开闭整分支。元素 $h\in A$ 仍非零。
在一个充分大的 strict 双圆盘内应用
Corollary~\ref{fnd:affinoiddimensiondomaincover}，可知
这个整域的维数至多二。因此约化闭子空间
$W=\mathcal M(A/\sqrt{(h)})$ 的维数至多一：每个包含 $h$
的素理想都非零，而商中的任何素理想链前面还可以添上 $A$
的零素理想。它包含 $x$，所以 Abhyankar 不等式说明其在
$x$ 处的维数为一。}

\bi{The pullback of $h$ to the curve $D$ vanishes at its
nonrigid point $\eta$. By
Lemma~\ref{fnd:nonrigidstrictcurvestalkfield} its germ there
is zero. The same is true of each generator of
$\sqrt{(h)}$, since its suitable power is a multiple of
$h$ and the graph is reduced. After shrinking $V$, we
therefore have a closed immersion $Z\cap V\subset W\cap X$.
The latter is a reduced strict domain of the curve $W$, by
Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictreduceddomainstalks}; both its ordinary
local ring at $x$ and that of $Z$ are fields by the curve
lemma. Their quotient map is an isomorphism.
The defining coherent ideal consequently has zero stalk
at $x$, so its finitely many local generators vanish on a
smaller ordinary neighborhood in $X$. Restricting $V$ to
a strict ambient neighborhood with that intersection gives}
{$h$ 拉回曲线 $D$ 后，在其非 rigid 点 $\eta$ 处消失。
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:nonrigidstrictcurvestalkfield}，它在那里
的芽为零。$\sqrt{(h)}$ 的每个生成元也如此，因为其某个幂是
$h$ 的倍数，而图像约化。缩小 $V$ 后，得到闭浸入
$Z\cap V\subset W\cap X$。
由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictreduceddomainstalks}，后者为曲线
$W$ 的约化 strict 域；曲线引理说明它及 $Z$ 在 $x$ 处的
通常局部环均为域，故相应商映射为同构。
定义该闭浸入的凝聚理想于 $x$ 的茎为零，所以其有限个局部
生成元在 $X$ 中一个更小普通邻域上全部消失。
将 $V$ 限制到与 $X$ 相交于此邻域中的 strict 环境邻域，便有}
\[
                         W\cap X=Z\cap V
\]
\bi{as closed analytic subspaces of $V\cap X$.}
{作为 $V\cap X$ 中闭解析子空间的等式。}

\bi{\emph{Step 2: the projection is locally finite of rank one.}
For every point $w$ of $W$ lying above $\eta$, the residue-field
tower formula and the affinoid dimension bound give}
{\emph{第二步：投影局部有限且秩为一。}
对 $W$ 中位于 $\eta$ 上方的每个点 $w$，剩余域塔公式与
affinoid 维数界给出}
\[
 1+d_{\mathcal H(\eta)}(w)=d_K(w)\leq1.
\]
\bi{Here the tower formula is
Lemma~\ref{fnd:valuedfielddimensioncalculus} and the last
inequality is Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidabhyankarmaximum}.
The fibre $W_\eta$ is a strict
$\mathcal H(\eta)$-affinoid: in a strict presentation of
$W$, scalar extension and the equation fixing its first
coordinate give such a presentation. The same maximum
formula makes it zero-dimensional.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization} and
Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttatedimension}
then make its algebra finite-dimensional over
$\mathcal H(\eta)$, so its analytic point set is finite
and discrete by Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation}.
In particular $x$ is isolated in this fibre.}
{其中塔公式是 Lemma~\ref{fnd:valuedfielddimensioncalculus}，
最后一个不等式是 Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidabhyankarmaximum}。
纤维 $W_\eta$ 是 strict $\mathcal H(\eta)$-affinoid：
在 $W$ 的 strict 表示中，扩标量并加入固定第一坐标的方程，
就得到这样的表示。同一个最大值公式说明此纤维零维。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization} 与
Proposition~\ref{fnd:stricttatedimension} 于是使其代数在 $\mathcal H(\eta)$ 上
有限维，故由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation}，
其解析点集有限且离散。特别地，$x$ 在该纤维中孤立。}

\bi{The projection $\mathbb A^{2,\mathrm{an}}_K\to
\mathbb A^{1,\mathrm{an}}_K$ is boundaryless: near each
point use a relative disc in the second coordinate with
radius strictly larger than its value. The inclusion of
$V$ is inner at $x$ because $V$ is an ordinary neighborhood,
and the closed immersion of $W$ is finite and boundaryless.
The \mbox{\hyperref[fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus]{forward composition rule}}
applied on these affinoid neighborhoods therefore makes $p|_W$
inner at $x$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:isolatedinteriorlocalfinite}
gives ordinary neighborhoods on which $p|_W$ is finite.
Separate the branch through $x$ and shrink the target about
$\eta$ as in Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation}.
The ordinary target ring at $\eta$ is a field by the curve
lemma. The finite direct image is therefore flat at that
point; Lemma~\ref{fnd:coherentflatstalkfree} makes it free
of some positive rank $m$ after shrinking.
We obtain affinoid neighborhoods $W_0$ of $x$ in $W$ and
$U$ of $\eta$ in the affine line for which
$p:W_0\to U$ is finite free of rank $m$.}
{投影 $\mathbb A^{2,\mathrm{an}}_K\to
\mathbb A^{1,\mathrm{an}}_K$ 无边界：在每个点附近，用
第二坐标构造半径严格大于其取值的相对圆盘。
由于 $V$ 为普通邻域，其包含在 $x$ 处为 inner，而 $W$ 的
闭浸入有限且无边界。在这些 affinoid 邻域上应用
\mbox{\hyperref[fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus]{复合的正向规则}}，因此使
$p|_W$ 在 $x$ 处为 inner。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:isolatedinteriorlocalfinite}
给出使 $p|_W$ 有限的普通邻域。
按 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation} 分离
经过 $x$ 的分支，并在 $\eta$ 附近缩小目标。
曲线引理说明目标在 $\eta$ 处的通常局部环为域，因此有限
直接像在那里平坦；Lemma~\ref{fnd:coherentflatstalkfree}
在缩小后使它成为某个正秩 $m$ 的自由层。
得到 $x$ 在 $W$ 中的 affinoid 邻域 $W_0$ 与 $\eta$ 在
仿射直线中的 affinoid 邻域 $U$，使
$p:W_0\to U$ 为秩 $m$ 的有限自由态射。}

\bi{Base change to $U\cap D$ gives a finite free map of
the same rank from $W_0\cap X$. This source is an analytic
domain of $Z$, by the closed-subspace equality proved above.
Under $Z\simeq D$ it maps isomorphically onto its image.
A positive-rank finite free algebra has nonzero fibres,
so the image is all of $U\cap D$. Consequently this base
change is an isomorphism and $m=1$.
A unital finite free algebra of rank one is the base algebra:
its unit is a basis after every residue-field specialization,
so the unit map is an isomorphism by Nakayama and finite
presentation. Thus $p:W_0\simeq U$.}
{基变换到 $U\cap D$ 后，得到从 $W_0\cap X$ 出发的同秩
有限自由态射。由此前证明的闭子空间等式，此源为 $Z$ 的解析域。
在 $Z\simeq D$ 的识别下，它同构地映到自己的像。
正秩有限自由代数的所有纤维均非零，因此该像是整个
$U\cap D$。所以此基变换为同构，从而 $m=1$。
带单位的秩一有限自由代数就是底代数：其单位元在每个剩余域
特化后都是一组基，由 Nakayama 与有限表示，单位映射为同构。
因此 $p:W_0\simeq U$。}

\bi{\emph{Step 3: contradict the exact radius.}
The second coordinate of the inverse section $U\to W_0$
is an analytic function agreeing with $f$ on $U\cap D$.
Restrict to the ordinary interior of $U$ at $\eta$ and
apply Lemma~\ref{fnd:gausspointoutercollar}. This extends
$f$ to $D_R$ for some $R>1$. Restriction
$K\{R^{-1}T\}\to K\langle T\rangle$ preserves the power-series
coefficients, so such an extension implies
$|a_n|R^n\to0$, contrary to the convergence-radius hypothesis.
Hence no nonzero germ at $x$ can vanish there. The maximal
ideal of the ordinary local ring is zero, proving the theorem.}
{\emph{第三步：与恰好收敛半径矛盾。}
逆截面 $U\to W_0$ 的第二坐标是一个解析函数，在
$U\cap D$ 上等于 $f$。限制到 $U$ 在 $\eta$ 处的普通内部，
再应用 Lemma~\ref{fnd:gausspointoutercollar}，便将 $f$
延拓到某个 $R>1$ 的 $D_R$。
限制 $K\{R^{-1}T\}\to K\langle T\rangle$ 保持幂级数系数，
故此延拓要求 $|a_n|R^n\to0$，与收敛半径假设矛盾。
因此 $x$ 处不存在非零消失芽，通常局部环的极大理想为零，
定理得证。}
\end{proof}
