Ordinary regular comparison for strict affinoids

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 346

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{Ordinary regular comparison for strict affinoids}{Strict affinoid 的通常正则比较}]
\label{fnd:strictordinaryregularcomparison}
\bi{Let $K$ be nontrivially valued, let $A$ be strict $K$-affinoid, let $x\in X=\mathcal M(A)$, and put $\mathfrak p=\ker(x)$ and $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$. The homomorphisms}
{设 $K$ 的赋值非平凡，$A$ 为 strict $K$-affinoid 代数，$x\in X=\mathcal M(A)$，并令 $\mathfrak p=\ker(x)$、$R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$。则同态}
\[
                  A_{\mathfrak p}\longrightarrow R,
                       \qquad R\longrightarrow\widehat R
\]
\bi{are regular: they are flat, and every algebraic fibre is geometrically regular.}
{均正则：它们平坦，且每个代数纤维均几何正则。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The characteristic-zero case is Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictcharzeroregularcomparison}. Assume that $K$ has characteristic $p>0$. Noetherianity and flatness are exactly as in the first paragraph of that proof. By Tag 0381, it is enough to test regularity after every finite purely inseparable extension of the residue field of every prime. We perform those tests explicitly.}
{特征零情形即 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictcharzeroregularcomparison}。以下设 $K$ 的特征为 $p>0$。Noetherian 性和平坦性与该证明的第一段完全相同。由 Tag 0381，只需在每个素理想的剩余域的每个有限纯不可分扩张之后测试正则性。下面显式实施这些测试。}

\bi{First choose $\mathfrak q\subset\mathfrak p$ in $A$, put $A_0=A/\mathfrak q$, and let $E/\operatorname{Frac}(A_0)$ be finite and purely inseparable. Choose generators $\alpha_j$ of this field extension and powers $p^{e_j}$ with $\alpha_j^{p^{e_j}}\in\operatorname{Frac}(A_0)$. Clearing denominators gives nonzero $c_j\in A_0$ such that $\beta_j=c_j\alpha_j$ has $\beta_j^{p^{e_j}}\in A_0$. Thus $B=A_0[\beta_j]$ is a finite integral domain, has fraction field $E$, and is radicial over $A_0$. Equip it with its finite affinoid norm; it is strict by Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebras}. There is a unique analytic point $z$ of $Z=\mathcal M(B)$ above $x$: the finite fibre has one residue field, purely inseparable over $\mathcal H(x)$, whose absolute value is determined by its $p$-power equations. Finite branch separation gives}
{先取 $A$ 中的 $\mathfrak q\subset\mathfrak p$，令 $A_0=A/\mathfrak q$，并取有限纯不可分扩张 $E/\operatorname{Frac}(A_0)$。选择此域扩张的生成元 $\alpha_j$，以及使 $\alpha_j^{p^{e_j}}\in\operatorname{Frac}(A_0)$ 的幂 $p^{e_j}$。清除分母后，存在非零 $c_j\in A_0$，使 $\beta_j=c_j\alpha_j$ 满足 $\beta_j^{p^{e_j}}\in A_0$。因此 $B=A_0[\beta_j]$ 是有限整环，其分式域为 $E$，且在 $A_0$ 上根式。赋予它有限 affinoid 范数；由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:strictfinitealgebras}，它为 strict。$Z=\mathcal M(B)$ 中在 $x$ 上方恰有一个解析点 $z$：其有限纤维只有一个剩余域，它在 $\mathcal H(x)$ 上纯不可分，而绝对值由其 $p$ 幂方程确定。有限分支分离给出}
\[
 R\otimes_A B=\mathcal O_{Z,z},\qquad
 R\otimes_A E=\mathcal O_{Z,z}\otimes_B E .
\]
\bi{Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization} gives a finite inclusion $K\{T_1,\ldots,T_l\}\subset B$. Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:positivecharacteristicgenericgerms} with empty free polyradius and the identity scalar extension. It supplies $0\ne d\in B$ such that $\mathcal O_{Z,z}[1/d]$ is regular. The displayed $E$-fibre is a further localization of this regular ring, since every nonzero element of $B$ is invertible in $E$. Hence it is regular. This treats every finite purely inseparable test of every fibre of $A_{\mathfrak p}\to R$.}
{Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictfinitenormalization} 给出有限包含 $K\{T_1,\ldots,T_l\}\subset B$。以空自由多半径和恒等标量扩张应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:positivecharacteristicgenericgerms}，得到 $0\ne d\in B$，使 $\mathcal O_{Z,z}[1/d]$ 正则。由于 $B$ 的每个非零元素在 $E$ 中可逆，所列 $E$-纤维是该正则环的进一步局部化，故正则。这处理了 $A_{\mathfrak p}\to R$ 的每个纤维的全部有限纯不可分测试。}

\bi{For the completion map, choose any prime $\mathfrak P\subset R$ and any finite purely inseparable extension $E/\operatorname{Frac}(R/\mathfrak P)$. Clearing denominators as in Proposition~\ref{fnd:finitefieldtestrealization} produces a finite domain $C$ over $R/\mathfrak P$ with fraction field $E$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization} and Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictordinaryneighborhoods} realize it as}
{对完备化映射，任取素理想 $\mathfrak P\subset R$ 及有限纯不可分扩张 $E/\operatorname{Frac}(R/\mathfrak P)$。按 Proposition~\ref{fnd:finitefieldtestrealization} 清除分母，得到 $R/\mathfrak P$ 上的有限整环 $C$，其分式域为 $E$。Theorem~\ref{fnd:finiteordinarygermrealization} 及 Lemma~\ref{fnd:strictordinaryneighborhoods} 将其实现为}
\[
                 B\hookrightarrow C=\mathcal O_{Z,z},
                    \qquad Z=\mathcal M(B),
\]
\bi{with $B$ a strict integral affinoid algebra. In particular $C$ is local and finite over $R$. Its maximal-ideal topology equals its $\mathfrak m_R C$-adic topology: the finite algebra $C/\mathfrak m_RC$ is local Artinian, so a power of its maximal ideal is zero. The finite-module completion formula, Tag 00MA, therefore gives}
{其中 $B$ 为 strict 整 affinoid 代数。特别地，$C$ 是局部环且在 $R$ 上有限。它的极大理想拓扑与 $\mathfrak m_R C$-进拓扑相同：有限代数 $C/\mathfrak m_RC$ 局部且 Artinian，所以其极大理想的某个幂为零。因此，有限模完备化公式 Tag 00MA 给出}
\[
       \widehat R\otimes_R C=\widehat C,
       \qquad \widehat R\otimes_R E
                    =\widehat C\otimes_C E .
\]
\bi{Apply the same identity-extension case of Theorem~\ref{fnd:positivecharacteristicgenericgerms} to a strict finite normalization of $B$. It gives $0\ne d\in B$ with $\widehat C[1/d]$ regular. The injection $B\hookrightarrow C$ ensures that $d$ remains nonzero in $C$, and hence becomes invertible in $E$. Thus the last displayed ring is a localization of a regular ring and is regular. Tag 0381 now proves that every algebraic fibre of $R\to\widehat R$ is geometrically regular, completing both assertions.}
{对 $B$ 的 strict 有限正规化，再次使用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:positivecharacteristicgenericgerms} 的恒等扩张情形，得到 $0\ne d\in B$，使 $\widehat C[1/d]$ 正则。单射 $B\hookrightarrow C$ 保证 $d$ 在 $C$ 中仍非零，故在 $E$ 中可逆。因此最后所列环是正则环的局部化，也正则。Tag 0381 于是证明 $R\to\widehat R$ 的每个代数纤维几何正则，从而完成两个断言。}
\end{proof}
