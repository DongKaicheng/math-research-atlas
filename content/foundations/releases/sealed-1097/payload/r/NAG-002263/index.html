Bounded sections after an unramified extension

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 763

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Bounded sections after an unramified extension]
\label{v036:f5:boundeddescent}
Keep the Galois extension and notation of
Lemma~\ref{v036:f5:unramifiedcoefficients}.
Let $\mathfrak X$ be an admissible formal $O$-scheme with
reduced special fibre, let $\mathfrak X'$ be its coefficient
extension to $O'$, and let $X'=X_L$ be the generic fibre.
For a formal open $\mathfrak U\subset\mathfrak X$, write
$\mathfrak U'$ for its inverse image and $D,D'$ for the
corresponding generic domains. Then
\[
 \Gamma(\mathfrak U,\mathcal O_{\mathfrak X})
 =\Gamma(\mathfrak U',\mathcal O_{\mathfrak X'})^G
 =\{f\in\Gamma(D',\mathcal O_{X'})^G: |f|\leq1\}.
\]
These equalities commute with restriction and identify the
$\pi^n$ ideals by the bound $|\pi|^n$.
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
On an affine formal open the first equality is
Lemma~\ref{v036:f5:unramifiedcoefficients} with $M=A_0$.
Its special-fibre extension is
$(A_0/\mathfrak mA_0)\otimes_k l$, which is reduced by the
separable-extension argument in
Lemma~\ref{cand:unramifiedmodelbasechange}.
Proposition~\ref{v035:f5:boundedsections} therefore identifies
the integral sections upstairs with the displayed bounded
analytic sections. The actual generic domain upstairs is $D_L$
by the coefficient presentation and the generic-fibre gluing
of Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful}.

For a general formal open, restrict an invariant integral section
to the inverse images of a formal affine cover. The affine
equalities descend it uniquely on each member. Their restrictions
agree because the upstairs restrictions agree and coefficient
extension is injective. The descended sections consequently glue.
The converse and restriction compatibility follow from the same
affine identifications. Apply the equality to $f/\pi^n$ for the
last assertion. In particular this descent recovers the actual
integral sheaf and its adic topology.
\end{proof}
