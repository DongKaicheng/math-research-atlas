\begin{lemma}[\bi{Closed immersions are target $G$-local}
{闭浸入在目标上 $G$-局部}]
\label{fnd:closedimmersiontargetglocal}
\bi{Let $h:W\to P$ be an analytic morphism and let
$(P_i)$ be an admissible covering of $P$ by analytic domains.
If every pullback $h_i:W\times_PP_i\to P_i$ is a closed
immersion, then $h$ is a closed immersion.}
{设 $h:W\to P$ 为解析态射，$(P_i)$ 为 $P$ 的一个由解析域
组成的 admissible 覆盖。若每个拉回
$h_i:W\times_PP_i\to P_i$ 均为闭浸入，则 $h$ 为闭浸入。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The underlying map is injective: two points with the same
image belong to a common inverse-image domain, where $h_i$ is
injective. Write its image as $S\subset P$.
On each $P_i$ the intersection $S\cap P_i$ is closed.
Refine the covering by affinoid domains. The net topology,
Lemma~\ref{fnd:nettopologyboundedgluing}, tests a set's closedness
on this chart quasi-net, so $S$ is closed in $P$.
The same test proves that $W\to S$ is a homeomorphism.
Indeed the pullbacks of the chart quasi-net form a quasi-net of
$W$, and intersecting it on the target with $S$ gives a quasi-net
of $S$. On every such pair the map is a homeomorphism.}
{底层映射为单射：像相同的两个点属于同一个逆像域，而该域上
的 $h_i$ 单射。以 $S\subset P$ 记其像，则每个
$S\cap P_i$ 在 $P_i$ 中闭。将覆盖加细为 affinoid 域后，
Lemma~\ref{fnd:nettopologyboundedgluing} 的 net 拓扑判据可在
此 chart quasi-net 上检验闭性，因此 $S$ 在 $P$ 中闭。
同一判据说明 $W\to S$ 为同胚：chart quasi-net 的逆像构成
$W$ 的 quasi-net，在目标中与 $S$ 相交则构成 $S$ 的
quasi-net，而在各对应块上映射均为同胚。}

\bi{Let $\mathcal I_i$ be the coherent ideal defining $h_i$.
Choose an affinoid chart $U=\mathcal M(A)$ in $P_i$ on which
the inverse image is the actual quotient $\mathcal M(B)$,
where $B=A/I$ and $I=\Gamma(U,\mathcal I_i)$.
For any affinoid domain $V=\mathcal M(C)$ of $U$, the map
$A\to C$ is a bounded domain restriction. By
Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients}, $I$ is finite
and closed and the quotient norm on $B$ is the canonical
finite-$A$-module norm for the cyclic presentation $A\to B$.
With canonical finite-module norms on the terms,
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness} restricts
$0\to I\to A\to B\to0$ to a norm-strict exact sequence over
$C$. Its left map identifies $I\otimes_A C$ with the ideal
$IC$ in $C$, since its image consists of sums of products of
images of elements of $I$ with elements of $C$. The canonical
identification $B\otimes_A C=C/IC$ is the algebraic quotient
identity. The same corollary over $C$ gives closedness of $IC$
and the actual complete quotient norm; this is the canonical
norm for the cyclic $C$-module presentation. Applying
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange} with $L=K$
identifies $B\widehat\otimes_A C$ with this actual quotient
by a bounded isomorphism with bounded inverse. If $V$ or the
quotient is empty, the corresponding zero algebra gives the
same assertions directly.

The bounded pushout property of the completed affinoid tensor
product, Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout},
represents the analytic inverse image over $V$: on each
affinoid chart of a test space the compatible maps are exactly
the compatible bounded algebra maps, and these identifications
agree on refinements and glue. This uses the given quotient
chart over $U$, not closed-immersion locality for the morphism
being proved. On a common affinoid refinement of $P_i\cap P_j$,
the two restricted quotients thus represent the same inverse
image over the refinement. Their canonical isomorphism over
$C$ makes their kernels in $C$ equal. Consequently the ideals
glue to a coherent ideal $\mathcal I\subset\mathcal O_{P_G}$.
One may construct this sheaf directly as compatible families
of local sections, as in
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf}; finite presentation
and coherence are local on the given admissible covering.}
{以 $\mathcal I_i$ 记定义 $h_i$ 的凝聚理想。取 $P_i$ 中一个
affinoid 图表 $U=\mathcal M(A)$，其逆像是实际商
$\mathcal M(B)$，其中 $B=A/I$、$I=\Gamma(U,\mathcal I_i)$。
设 $V=\mathcal M(C)$ 为 $U$ 中的 affinoid 域，则 $A\to C$
是有界的域限制映射。由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients}，
$I$ 有限且闭，$B$ 的商范数正是循环表示 $A\to B$ 的典范有限
$A$-模范数。各项配备典范有限模范数后，
Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizeddomainflatness} 将短正合列
$0\to I\to A\to B\to0$ 限制为 $C$ 上范数严格正合列。左侧映射
将 $I\otimes_A C$ 与 $C$ 中的理想 $IC$ 识别：其像由 $I$ 中元素
的像与 $C$ 中元素的乘积之和组成。代数商恒等式给出
$B\otimes_A C=C/IC$。将同一推论用于 $C$，得到 $IC$ 闭，
且实际商范数完备；该范数正是循环 $C$-模表示的典范范数。
在 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange} 中取 $L=K$，
得到 $B\widehat\otimes_A C$ 与此实际商之间具有有界逆的有界同构。
若 $V$ 或商空间为空，由相应的零代数直接得到同样的结论。

由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedrelativepushout} 中完备 affinoid
张量积的有界推出泛性质，所得对象表示 $V$ 上的解析逆像：
对检验空间的每个 affinoid 图表，相容的态射恰对应相容的有界
代数同态；这些识别在加细上相容并可粘合。这里只用到 $U$ 上
给定的商图表，不调用待证态射的闭浸入局部性。在
$P_i\cap P_j$ 的共同 affinoid 加细上，两个限制商因而表示同一
解析逆像。二者在 $C$ 上的典范同构说明它们在 $C$ 中的核相等。
故各理想粘合为凝聚理想 $\mathcal I\subset\mathcal O_{P_G}$。
与 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetstructuresheaf} 一样，也可用局部截面
的相容族直接构造该层；有限表示及凝聚性均在给定 admissible
覆盖上局部成立。}

\bi{Fix an arbitrary affinoid domain $Q=\mathcal M(A)$ in $P$.
Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb} identifies
$\mathcal I|_Q$ with the finite ideal
$I=\Gamma(Q,\mathcal I)\subset A$.
By Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients},
$A/I$ is an affinoid quotient with its actual quotient norm.
The space $\mathcal M(A/I)$ and $W\times_PQ$ become the same
closed subspace after an affinoid refinement subordinate to the
$P_i$. Their quotient maps and their inverses agree on overlaps,
so Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing} glues those
identifications. Hence $h$ is represented on every such $Q$ by
the admissible quotient $A\twoheadrightarrow A/I$.
Together with the closed topological embedding, this is a closed
immersion.}
{任取 $P$ 中的 affinoid 域 $Q=\mathcal M(A)$。
Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb} 将
$\mathcal I|_Q$ 识别为有限理想
$I=\Gamma(Q,\mathcal I)\subset A$ 所给的层。
由 Corollary~\ref{fnd:generalizedaffinoidquotients}，
$A/I$ 带实际商范数成为 affinoid 商。
在从属于 $P_i$ 的 affinoid 加细上，
$\mathcal M(A/I)$ 与 $W\times_PQ$ 给出同一个闭子空间。
这些商映射及其逆在交叠上相容，因此
Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalnetmorphismgluing} 将它们粘合为同构。
故 $h$ 在每个这样的 $Q$ 上均由 admissible 商
$A\twoheadrightarrow A/I$ 表示。结合闭的拓扑嵌入，即得
闭浸入。}
\end{proof}
