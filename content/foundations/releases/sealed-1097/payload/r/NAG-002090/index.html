A uniform degree bound for homogeneous initial ideals

5 Tropicalization, multiplicities and balancing

Open in the English reader · PDF page 870

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{A uniform degree bound for homogeneous initial ideals}{齐次初始理想的一致次数界}]
\label{cand:initialuniformdegree}
\bi{There is an integer $D$ depending only on the Hilbert function of $S/H$ such that every $\operatorname{in}_\omega(H)$, over every residue extension, is generated in degrees at most $D$. The same $D$ works for all further ordinary initial ideals of these ideals.}
{存在仅依赖 $S/H$ 的 Hilbert 函数的整数 $D$，使每个 $\operatorname{in}_\omega(H)$ 在任意剩余扩域后均由次数至多 $D$ 的元素生成。同一个 $D$ 也适用于这些理想的所有进一步的通常初始理想。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Fix a degree-compatible monomial order. For a homogeneous ideal $J$ over a field, let $\operatorname{LM}(J)$ be the monomial ideal generated by its leading monomials. Its standard monomials form a basis of the quotient in every degree. Indeed, finitely many leading monomials generate $\operatorname{LM}(J)$ by Noetherianity. Choose homogeneous elements of $J$ having these leading monomials. Polynomial division by them terminates because the monomial order is well ordered and reduces every homogeneous polynomial to a linear combination of standard monomials. Such a combination cannot belong to $J$ unless it is zero, since otherwise its leading monomial would belong to $\operatorname{LM}(J)$. The same division proves that the chosen elements generate $J$.}
{固定与次数相容的单项式序。对域上的齐次理想 $J$，以 $\operatorname{LM}(J)$ 记其首单项式生成的理想。每个次数中的标准单项式构成商的基。确实，由 Noetherian 性，有限个首单项式生成 $\operatorname{LM}(J)$；为它们选取 $J$ 中具有相应首项的齐次元素。多项式除法由于单项式序良序而终止，将每个齐次多项式化为标准单项式的线性组合。若此组合非零且属于 $J$，其首项将属于 $\operatorname{LM}(J)$，矛盾。同一除法也证明所选元素生成 $J$。}

\bi{By Lemma~\ref{cand:initialhilbertfunction}, all ideals under consideration have the Hilbert function of $H$. Their monomial leading ideals therefore belong to the finite list of Lemma~\ref{cand:monomialhilbertfiniteness}. Take $D$ to be the largest degree among the minimal monomial generators in this list. Lifting these generators as in the preceding paragraph proves the bound. An ordinary weight initial ideal has the same Hilbert function, by the same degreewise argument over a trivially valued field, so the bound applies to it as well.}
{由 Lemma~\ref{cand:initialhilbertfunction}，所考虑的理想均具有 $H$ 的 Hilbert 函数。因此其首单项式理想属于 Lemma~\ref{cand:monomialhilbertfiniteness} 的有限列表。取 $D$ 为此列表中所有极小单项式生成元的最高次数，按前段将生成元提升，即得一致界。通常权重初始理想也具有相同 Hilbert 函数：在平凡赋值域上应用同一逐次论证即可。因此同一界亦适用于它。}
\end{proof}
Standing context 1 · exact TeX
\label{cand:groebnercomplexsection}
\bi{Write $S=K[x_0,\ldots,x_n]$ with its usual grading, and let $H\subset S$ be homogeneous. For $\omega\in\mathbf R^{n+1}$, pass to a complete isometric extension realizing the coordinate weights, translate the variables, and reduce normalized homogeneous relations. This gives a homogeneous ideal $\operatorname{in}_\omega(H)$ in a polynomial ring over the residue field. The arguments of Proposition~\ref{cand:unitinitialbasechange} apply degree by degree to polynomial rings as well. We compare ideals after residue-field extension and diagonal change of coordinates.}
{令 $S=K[x_0,\ldots,x_n]$ 具有通常分次，$H\subset S$ 为齐次理想。对 $\omega\in\mathbf R^{n+1}$，取实现各坐标权重的完备等距扩域，平移变量并约化归一化后的齐次关系，得到剩余域上多项式环中的齐次理想 $\operatorname{in}_\omega(H)$。Proposition~\ref{cand:unitinitialbasechange} 的论证也逐次适用于多项式环。以下允许剩余域扩张及对角坐标变换来比较这些理想。}
