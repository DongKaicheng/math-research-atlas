Small perturbations of a finite normalization

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 436

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Small perturbations of a finite normalization}{有限正规化的小扰动}]
\label{fnd:smallnormalizationperturbation}
\bi{Let $C$ be a strict $F$-affinoid algebra over a complete non-Archimedean field. Suppose that $u_1,\ldots,u_d\in C$ define a bounded finite homomorphism $F\langle U_1,\ldots,U_d\rangle\to C$. If $v_i\in C$ satisfy $\rho_C(v_i-u_i)<1$ for every $i$, then $U_i\mapsto v_i$ also defines a bounded finite homomorphism.}
{设 $C$ 是完备非阿基米德域 $F$ 上的 strict affinoid 代数。假设 $u_1,\ldots,u_d\in C$ 定义有界有限同态 $F\langle U_1,\ldots,U_d\rangle\to C$。若 $v_i\in C$ 对每个 $i$ 都满足 $\rho_C(v_i-u_i)<1$，则 $U_i\mapsto v_i$ 也定义有界有限同态。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Boundedness of the original map gives $\rho_C(u_i)\leq1$ by applying it to powers. Hence $\rho_C(v_i)\leq1$. The pointwise power-boundedness theorem, Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointwisepowerbound}, shows that the powers of each $v_i$ are bounded. The finitely many commuting elements therefore define the stated bounded Tate-algebra homomorphism. In the canonical reduction of $C$, the images of $v_i$ and $u_i$ coincide. Thus the two homomorphisms from $\widetilde F[U_1,\ldots,U_d]$ into $\widetilde C$ are identical. Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictcanonicalfinitecriterion} applies to both maps: finiteness of the first reduction map proves finiteness of the second.}
{对原同态应用幂次估计，得到 $\rho_C(u_i)\leq1$，故 $\rho_C(v_i)\leq1$。逐点幂有界定理 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalizedpointwisepowerbound} 说明每个 $v_i$ 的各次幂有界。因此这有限个可交换元素定义所述有界 Tate 代数同态。在 $C$ 的典范约化中，$v_i$ 与 $u_i$ 的像相同，所以从 $\widetilde F[U_1,\ldots,U_d]$ 到 $\widetilde C$ 的两个同态完全相同。对两者应用 Theorem~\ref{fnd:strictcanonicalfinitecriterion}，第一个约化同态的有限性便给出第二个同态的有限性。}
\end{proof}
