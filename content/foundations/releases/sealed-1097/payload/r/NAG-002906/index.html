Line bundles and relative ampleness

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1901

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[Line bundles and relative ampleness]
\label{cand:f5proj:linebundledescent}
Assume (K). Let \(Y\) be a flat, locally finitely presented
\(O\)-scheme, put \(Y'=Y\times_O O'\), and let \(p:Y'\to Y\)
be the projection, with its coefficient \(G\)-action.
Let \(\mathcal L'\) be a \(G\)-linearized invertible sheaf on \(Y'\).
The following conditions are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The restricted action on \(\mathcal L'|_{Y'_k}\) is the identity.
\item The sheaf \(\mathcal L=(p_*\mathcal L')^G\) is invertible,
and evaluation gives a \(G\)-equivariant isomorphism
\[
                         p^*\mathcal L\simeq\mathcal L'.
 \tag{4}
\]
\end{enumerate}
This descent is unique up to a unique isomorphism compatible with
(4). If the conditions hold, \(\mathcal L\) is relatively ample
over \(O\) if and only if \(\mathcal L'\) is relatively ample
over \(O'\).
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Assume condition 1. For any affine open \(V=\operatorname{Spec}B\)
of \(Y\), its inverse image is the invariant affine
\(V'=\operatorname{Spec}B'\), where \(B'=O'\otimes_O B\).
The algebra \(B'\) is flat and finitely presented over \(O'\).
Its coefficient action has full residual identity, and
\((B')^G=B\) by the character-zero recovery in
\dep{cand:f5tame:charactercriterion} for an effective presentation.

By the affine equivalence \stacks{01IB} and the finite-projective
characterization \stacks{00NX}, the module
\(M'=\Gamma(V',\mathcal L')\) is finite projective of rank
one over \(B'\). It is flat over \(O'\), since it is
a direct summand of a finite free \(B'\)-module and \(B'\) is
\(O'\)-flat. Its residual action is the identity: reduction
of sections on this affine identifies it with the sections of
\(\mathcal L'|_{V'_k}\), by \stacks{01I9}. Proposition~\ref{cand:f5proj:modulecriterion}
therefore gives a finite projective rank-one \(B\)-module
\(M=(M')^G\) and the equivariant isomorphism (1).

These affine modules describe the asserted invariant sheaf.
Indeed, for \(f\in B\), the averaging projector
\[
                       P_0=\frac1e\sum_{g\in G}g
\]
is \(B\)-linear and commutes with localization at \(f\).
Its image is the invariant module, both before and after
localization. Hence
\[
                    (M'_f)^G=M_f .
 \tag{5}
\]
On the principal-open basis of \(V\), formula (5) identifies
\((p_*\mathcal L')^G|_V\) with \(\widetilde M\). Thus the
invariant sheaf is quasi-coherent and invertible. The local
multiplication maps (1) are the restrictions of the canonical
evaluation map (4), so they glue and make (4) an equivariant
isomorphism. No independent assumption of an integral fpqc
descent datum has been made.

For any other invertible sheaf \(\mathcal D\) on \(Y\) with an
equivariant identification \(p^*\mathcal D\simeq\mathcal L'\),
local trivializations of \(\mathcal D\), together with
\((O'\otimes_O B)^G=B\), identify
\(\mathcal D\simeq(p_*p^*\mathcal D)^G\). Taking invariants of
the specified identification gives its unique compatible
isomorphism with \(\mathcal L\). Conversely, (4) gives identity
on the special fibre because the coefficient action on \(k\)
is the identity.

Finally, \(\operatorname{Spec}O'\to\operatorname{Spec}O\) is a
faithfully flat affine morphism, hence an fpqc cover. Once the
actual invertible sheaf \(\mathcal L\) and (4) have been constructed,
\stacks{0D2P} applies to this cover and proves the equivalence
of relative ampleness. Its hypotheses require neither a
Noetherian base nor a finite extension of valuation rings.
\end{proof}
