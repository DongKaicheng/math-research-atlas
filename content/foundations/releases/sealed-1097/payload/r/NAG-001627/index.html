The image of a finite flat affinoid map

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 416

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{The image of a finite flat affinoid map}{有限平坦 affinoid 映射的像}]
\label{fnd:finiteflatclopenimage}
\bi{Let $A\to B$ be finite, norm-admissible and flat between generalized affinoid algebras. Its image on Berkovich spectra is open and closed.}
{设 generalized affinoid 代数间的映射 $A\to B$ 有限、norm-admissible 且平坦。则其 Berkovich 谱上的像为开闭集。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The algebra $A$ is Noetherian, so the finite module $B$ is finitely presented. By \cite[Tag 00NX, Lemma 10.78.2(1),(8)]{StacksAlg}, its rank function on $\operatorname{Spec}A$ is locally constant. Hence its positive-rank locus is open and closed. For $x\in\mathcal M(A)$ with kernel $\mathfrak p$, the analytic fibre has algebra $B\otimes_A\mathcal H(x)$, with its complete finite-dimensional norm, by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange} and Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres}. This algebra is nonzero exactly when $B\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ has positive dimension, since $\kappa(\mathfrak p)\to\mathcal H(x)$ is a field embedding. A nonzero finite-dimensional algebra has a bounded character after a finite residue-field extension, so its spectrum is nonempty. Therefore the image is the inverse image of the positive-rank locus. Inverse images of $D(a)$ are $\{|a|>0\}$, and inverse images of Zariski closed subsets are common zero loci of continuous evaluations. The inverse image in question is consequently open and closed.}
{代数 $A$ 为 Noetherian，所以有限模 $B$ 有限表示。由 \cite[Tag 00NX, Lemma 10.78.2(1),(8)]{StacksAlg}，其在 $\operatorname{Spec}A$ 上的秩函数局部常值，因此正秩轨迹为开闭集。对 $x\in\mathcal M(A)$，记其核为 $\mathfrak p$。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange} 和 Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres}，解析纤维的代数为带完备有限维范数的 $B\otimes_A\mathcal H(x)$。由于 $\kappa(\mathfrak p)\to\mathcal H(x)$ 为域嵌入，该代数非零当且仅当 $B\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ 的维数为正。非零有限维代数在有限剩余域扩张后有有界特征，因而其谱非空。因此，谱映射的像就是正秩轨迹的逆像。$D(a)$ 的逆像是 $\{|a|>0\}$，Zariski 闭集的逆像则是连续取值函数的公共零点集。所以该逆像为开闭集。}
\end{proof}
