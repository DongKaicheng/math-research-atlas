The local finite criterion

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 430

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[\bi{The local finite criterion}{局部有限性判据}]
\label{fnd:isolatedinteriorlocalfinite}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good analytic spaces, and let $y\in Y$, $x=f(y)$. The following conditions are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $y$ is isolated in the analytic fibre $Y_x$ and $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$;
\item there are ordinary open neighbourhoods $W\subset Y$ of $y$ and $N\subset X$ of $x$ such that $f(W)\subset N$ and $W\to N$ is norm-admissible finite.
\end{enumerate}
When these conditions hold, $W$ and $N$ may be required to lie in any prescribed ordinary neighbourhoods of $y$ and $x$.}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 为 good 解析空间间的态射，$y\in Y$，$x=f(y)$。以下条件等价：
\begin{enumerate}
\item $y$ 在解析纤维 $Y_x$ 中为孤立点，且 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$；
\item 存在 $y$ 在 $Y$ 中的普通开邻域 $W$ 及 $x$ 在 $X$ 中的普通开邻域 $N$，使 $f(W)\subset N$，且 $W\to N$ 为 norm-admissible 的有限态射。
\end{enumerate}
若这些条件成立，可进一步要求 $W,N$ 包含于预先指定的 $y,x$ 的普通邻域。}
\end{theorem}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Assume the first condition. By the topological identification of analytic fibres, there is an ordinary open neighbourhood of $y$ meeting $f^{-1}(x)$ only in $y$. Choose an affinoid neighbourhood $X_0$ of $x$ inside the prescribed target neighbourhood. Goodness supplies an affinoid neighbourhood $V$ of $y$ contained in this isolating source neighbourhood, in the prescribed source neighbourhood, and in $f^{-1}(\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(X_0,X))$. Thus $V_x=\{y\}$ as a set. Proposition~\ref{fnd:goodinteriorlocaltesting} gives $y\in\operatorname{Int}(V/X_0)$.}
{先设第一条件成立。解析纤维与拓扑纤维的识别说明，存在 $y$ 的普通开邻域，其与 $f^{-1}(x)$ 仅交于 $y$。在预先指定的底空间邻域内选择 $x$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $X_0$。由 goodness，可在该孤立源邻域、预先指定的源邻域及 $f^{-1}(\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(X_0,X))$ 的交中，选择 $y$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $V$。于是作为集合，$V_x=\{y\}$。Proposition~\ref{fnd:goodinteriorlocaltesting} 给出 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(V/X_0)$。}

\bi{The relative boundary $D=\partial(V/X_0)$ is closed in the compact space $V$. Its image in the Hausdorff affinoid space $X_0$ is therefore compact and closed. This image does not contain $x$: the only point of $V_x$ is $y$, which is interior. Choose a rational neighbourhood $U$ of $x$ in $X_0\setminus f(D)$ and inside $\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(X_0,X)$. The inverse image $V_U=V\times_{X_0}U$ is affinoid. Every one of its points comes from $\operatorname{Int}(V/X_0)$, so interior stability under base change makes $V_U\to U$ boundaryless. Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidboundarylessfinite} now makes this morphism norm-admissible finite.}
{相对边界 $D=\partial(V/X_0)$ 在紧空间 $V$ 中闭，故其在 Hausdorff affinoid 空间 $X_0$ 中的像紧且闭。此像不含 $x$，因为 $V_x$ 的唯一点 $y$ 为内点。在 $X_0\setminus f(D)$ 中选择 $x$ 的 rational 邻域 $U$，并使其包含于 $\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(X_0,X)$。逆像 $V_U=V\times_{X_0}U$ 为 affinoid。其每个点均来自 $\operatorname{Int}(V/X_0)$，因此内部在基变换下的保持性说明 $V_U\to U$ 无边界。Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidboundarylessfinite} 于是给出这个态射 norm-admissible 且有限。}

\bi{It remains to obtain ordinary open neighbourhoods. Since both $V$ and $U$ were neighbourhoods of the chosen points, $V_U$ is a neighbourhood of $y$ in $Y$. Set}
{最后取得普通开邻域。因为 $V,U$ 均为所选点的邻域，$V_U$ 是 $y$ 在 $Y$ 中的邻域。令}
\[
 E=V_U\setminus\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(V_U,Y).
\]
\bi{This is a closed subset of the compact space $V_U$, and its image in $U$ is closed. The image avoids $x$, because $(V_U)_x=\{y\}$ and $y\notin E$. Choose an ordinary open neighbourhood $N$ of $x$ in $X$, contained in $\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(U,X)$ and disjoint from $f(E)$. Put $W=V_U\cap f^{-1}(N)$. Then $W$ is contained in $\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(V_U,Y)$, and hence}
{这是紧空间 $V_U$ 的闭子集，其在 $U$ 中的像闭。此像不含 $x$，因为 $(V_U)_x=\{y\}$ 且 $y\notin E$。选择 $x$ 在 $X$ 中的普通开邻域 $N$，使其包含于 $\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(U,X)$ 且避开 $f(E)$。令 $W=V_U\cap f^{-1}(N)$。于是 $W\subset\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(V_U,Y)$，从而}
\[
 W=\operatorname{Int}_{\rm top}(V_U,Y)\cap f^{-1}(N)
\]
\bi{is open in $Y$. The map $W\to N$ is the base change of the finite map $V_U\to U$, so it is finite by Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange} and analytic-domain gluing. It contains $y$ and has all the required neighbourhood restrictions.}
{在 $Y$ 中开。映射 $W\to N$ 是有限映射 $V_U\to U$ 的基变换，故由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange} 及解析域粘合仍有限。它包含 $y$，并满足全部预先指定的邻域限制。}

\bi{Conversely, suppose the finite map is given on ordinary open neighbourhoods $W\to N$. Choose an affinoid neighbourhood $X_0\subseteq N$ of $x$ on a finite chart, so its full inverse image $V$ in $W$ is affinoid and $V\to X_0$ is norm-admissible finite. As $X_0$ is a neighbourhood of $x$ and $W$ is open in $Y$, $V$ is a neighbourhood of $y$ in $Y$. Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidboundarylessfinite} gives $y\in\operatorname{Int}(V/X_0)$, and Proposition~\ref{fnd:goodinteriorlocaltesting} gives $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$. By Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation}, the finite fibre in $W$ is discrete. Since $W$ is open, $y$ is isolated in the original fibre.}
{反之，设普通开邻域间的映射 $W\to N$ 有限。在有限坐标片上取 $x$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $X_0\subseteq N$，使它在 $W$ 中的全逆像 $V$ 为 affinoid，且 $V\to X_0$ 是 norm-admissible 有限态射。由于 $X_0$ 是 $x$ 的邻域且 $W$ 在 $Y$ 中开，$V$ 是 $y$ 在 $Y$ 中的邻域。Theorem~\ref{fnd:affinoidboundarylessfinite} 给出 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(V/X_0)$，Proposition~\ref{fnd:goodinteriorlocaltesting} 于是给出 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation}，$W$ 中的有限纤维离散。因为 $W$ 开，$y$ 在原纤维中孤立。}\end{proof}
