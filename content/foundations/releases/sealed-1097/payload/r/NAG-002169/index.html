The Legendre model when two is a unit

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 734

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{The Legendre model when two is a unit}
{二可逆时的 Legendre 模型}]
\label{cand:legendresemistablevaluation}
\bi{Let $F$ be complete of characteristic zero with
nontrivial absolute value and residue characteristic
different from two. Every pointed smooth projective
genus-one curve over $F$ has, after a finite extension,
a projective nodal model over the resulting valuation ring.
Its special fibre is either smooth or an irreducible cubic
with one ordinary double point.}
{设 $F$ 完备、特征零、绝对值非平凡，
且剩余特征不为二。
每条带点光滑射影亏格一曲线在有限扩域后，
都在所得赋值环上具有射影结点模型。
其特殊纤维或光滑，或为具有一个普通二重点的不可约
三次曲线。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Use Lemma~\ref{cand:shortweierstrassreconstruction}
and split its cubic as
$(x-e_1)(x-e_2)(x-e_3)$ after a finite extension.
The roots are distinct.
Choose the pair labelled $e_1,e_2$ to have maximal
pairwise distance. After scaling $x-e_1$ by $e_2-e_1$
and adjoining a square root for the corresponding
$y$-scaling, the equation becomes}
{使用 Lemma~\ref{cand:shortweierstrassreconstruction}，
有限扩域后将三次式分解为
$(x-e_1)(x-e_2)(x-e_3)$，其中根互异。
选取两两距离最大的一对作为 $e_1,e_2$。
用 $e_2-e_1$ 缩放 $x-e_1$，
并添入相应 $y$ 缩放所需的平方根后，
方程变为}
\[
 Y^2=X(X-1)(X-\lambda),\qquad
 \lambda=\frac{e_3-e_1}{e_2-e_1},\qquad
 |\lambda|\leq1,\quad|1-\lambda|\leq1 .
\]
\bi{At least one of the last two norms is one.
If both are one, the three residual roots are distinct.
Since two is a unit, the affine partial derivatives then
have no common zero on the cubic.
The point at infinity is smooth as in
Lemma~\ref{cand:integralplanecubicfamily}.}
{最后两个范数至少一个等于一。
若二者均为一，则三个剩余根互异。
由于二可逆，仿射偏导数在三次曲线上没有公共零点。
无穷远点由 Lemma~\ref{cand:integralplanecubicfamily}
知其光滑。}

\bi{If $|\lambda|<1$, the residual equation is
$v^2=u^2(u-1)$, with its sole singular point at the origin.
There the derivative ideal is
$(u(2-3u),2v)=(u,v)$.
The local hypersurface ring has depth one: $u$ is a
nonzerodivisor since the equation is not divisible by $u$,
and its dimension is one.
Its first differential Fitting ideal is the derivative
ideal just computed. Its residue field is the base field,
so \cite[Tag 0C4D(5)]{StacksAlg} proves that this point
is a node.
If $|1-\lambda|<1$, translate $u=X-1$; the same computation
uses $v^2=u^2(u+1)$ and derivative
$u(-2-3u)$.
The quadratic terms have two distinct linear factors over
a separable extension, because the characteristic is not two.
The residual cubic is irreducible over the algebraic closure:
in the first case $u-1$ is not a square in $\overline k(u)$,
so $v^2-u^2(u-1)$ is irreducible, and the
second case is identical with $u+1$.
The integral-family lemma now supplies the required model.}
{若 $|\lambda|<1$，剩余方程为
$v^2=u^2(u-1)$，唯一奇点为原点。
此处的导数理想为
$(u(2-3u),2v)=(u,v)$。
局部超曲面环深度为一：
方程不被 $u$ 整除，所以 $u$ 为非零因子，
而环的维数为一。
微分模的第一个 Fitting 理想就是上述导数理想。
剩余域为基域，故
\cite[Tag 0C4D(5)]{StacksAlg} 说明该点为结点。
若 $|1-\lambda|<1$，令 $u=X-1$，
对 $v^2=u^2(u+1)$ 作同样计算，
此时导数为 $u(-2-3u)$。
因为特征不是二，二次项在某个可分扩域后
分成两个不同线性因子。
剩余三次式在代数闭包上不可约：
第一种情形中，$u-1$ 在 $\overline k(u)$ 中不是平方，
所以 $v^2-u^2(u-1)$ 不可约；
第二种情形将 $u-1$ 换成 $u+1$ 即可。
整三次曲线族引理现在给出所需模型。}
\end{proof}
