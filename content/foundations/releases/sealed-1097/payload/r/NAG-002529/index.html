The finite tensor bridge

Proper direct image on historical ordinary compact tests

Open in the English reader · PDF page 1953

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[The finite tensor bridge]\label{f9ds:finitebridge}
For a generalized $K$-affinoid algebra $A$, put $B=A\widehat\otimes_KL$.
There are natural algebra identifications
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cechtensor}
 B=A\otimes_KL,\qquad
 \underbrace{B\widehat\otimes_A\cdots\widehat\otimes_A B}_{r\text{ factors}}
       =A\otimes_K L^{\otimes_K r}\quad(r\geq 1).
\end{equation}
Every algebra on the right has its canonical finite-$A$-module topology.
In particular $A\to B$ is faithfully flat and finite free of rank $n$.
For a finite $A$-module $M$, $B\otimes_A M=M\otimes_KL$ is the actual
analytic pullback. These identifications commute with affinoid
restrictions.
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
Choose a $K$-basis of $L$ containing $1$. The finite-dimensional norm
theorem makes its coordinate maps bounded in both directions. Tensoring
these maps with $A$ identifies $A\widehat\otimes_KL$ with the complete
space $A^n$, with equivalent norms. The maps are inverse on elementary
tensors and hence on their completions. Their multiplication is the
ordinary tensor multiplication. The scalar-presentation theorem gives
the native $L$-affinoid structure, and the finite-algebra theorem gives
the $K$-affinoid structure with the same Banach topology.
A bounded $K$-seminorm on $B$ restricts to the specified absolute value
on $L$ by uniqueness. Thus the two spectra have the same points and
evaluation topology. All sheaves and fibre products in this supplement
are taken on the finite $K$-analytic model just constructed.

Finite base change now identifies the completed tensors over $A$ with
ordinary tensors, proving \eqref{eq:cechtensor} by iteration. The same
finite-module comparison applies to $M$. A free $A$-module of positive
rank is faithfully flat: tensoring an $A$-module with $B$ gives its
$n$-fold direct sum. The formulas and their inverses commute with the
maps $A\to A_V$, which proves restriction compatibility.
\end{proof}
