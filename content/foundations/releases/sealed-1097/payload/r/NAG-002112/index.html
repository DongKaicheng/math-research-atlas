Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{Constancy, refinement, and complete base change}{常值性、细分与完备基变换}]
\label{cand:tropicalweightlocalconstancy}
\bi{The integer $m_X(\sigma)$ is constant on the relative interior of each maximal initial stratum. The resulting weighted polyhedral complex is independent of its choice of initial-stratum refinement, and every complete isometric extension of $K$ preserves its support and weights.}
{整数 $m_X(\sigma)$ 在每个极大初始分层的相对内部上恒定。所得带权多面复形与初始分层细分的选择无关；任意完备等距扩域均保持其支集及权重。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Theorem~\ref{cand:finitegroebnercomplex} compares the initial schemes at two relative-interior points by a single torus translation after residue extension. The cell has the same tangent lattice at both points, so the comparison descends to their transverse finite quotients. Proposition~\ref{cand:geometricmultiplicity} equates the dimensions. A subdivision retains the tangent lattice on its dimension-$d$ pieces and therefore retains the weights. Any two initial-stratum complexes have a common refinement; at a point belonging to both relative interiors their weights are computed from the identical initial scheme and tangent lattice. Finally, the initial scalar-extension identity in Proposition~\ref{cand:unitinitialbasechange}, together with the common-extension comparison in Theorem~\ref{cand:realinitialdegeneration}, gives the same finite quotient after residue extension. Its dimension is unchanged.}
{Theorem~\ref{cand:finitegroebnercomplex} 在剩余扩域后，以一个环面平移比较两个相对内部点处的初始概形。两处具有同一胞腔切向格，因此比较下降到横截有限商。由 Proposition~\ref{cand:geometricmultiplicity}，其维数相等。细分的 $d$ 维片保持同一切向格，故保持权重。任意两个初始分层复形存在共同细分；在两个相对内部的公共点，权重由同一个初始概形及切向格计算。最后，Proposition~\ref{cand:unitinitialbasechange} 的初始标量扩张恒等式及 Theorem~\ref{cand:realinitialdegeneration} 的共同扩域比较，在剩余扩域后给出同一有限商，其维数不变。}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[\bi{Finite filtrations and zero-dimensional algebras}{有限滤过与零维代数}]
\label{cand:finiteprimefiltration}
\bi{Every finite module over a Noetherian ring has a finite filtration whose nonzero successive quotients are $A/\mathfrak p$ for prime ideals $\mathfrak p$. A zero-dimensional algebra of finite type over a field $k$ is finite-dimensional over $k$.}
{Noetherian 环上的每个有限模具有有限滤过，其非零相继商均形如 $A/\mathfrak p$，其中 $\mathfrak p$ 为素理想。域 $k$ 上的有限型零维代数在 $k$ 上有限维。}
\end{lemma}
Standing context 2 · exact TeX
\begin{definition}[\bi{The geometric tropical weight}{几何热带权重}]
\label{cand:tropicalmultiplicitydefinition}
\bi{At $w\in\operatorname{relint}(\sigma)$ as in Theorem~\ref{cand:tropicalpuredimension}, define}
{在 Theorem~\ref{cand:tropicalpuredimension} 的 $w\in\operatorname{relint}(\sigma)$ 处，定义}
\[
 m_X(\sigma)=\dim_k\Gamma(Z_\sigma,\mathcal O_{Z_\sigma}).
\]
\end{definition}
