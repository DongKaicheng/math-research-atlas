Ordinary closed fibres map faithfully flat to analytic fibres

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 485

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{corollary}[\bi{Ordinary closed fibres map faithfully flat to analytic fibres}{普通闭纤维到解析纤维的忠实平坦映射}]
\label{fnd:generalclosedfiberfaithfulflat}
\bi{Let $f:Y\to X$ be a morphism of good analytic spaces over any complete field and let $y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$ with image $x$. Write $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$, $S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$ and let $\mathfrak m$ be the maximal ideal of $R$. Then the canonical local homomorphism}
{设 $f:Y\to X$ 为任意完备域上 good 解析空间之间的态射，$y\in\operatorname{Int}(Y/X)$ 的像为 $x$。记 $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$、$S=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$，并以 $\mathfrak m$ 记 $R$ 的极大理想。则典范局部同态}
\[
                         S/\mathfrak mS\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{Y_x,y}
\]
\bi{is faithfully flat.}
{忠实平坦。}
\end{corollary}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose an ordinary affinoid neighborhood $U=\mathcal M(A)$ of $x$ on which a finite generating family of $\mathfrak m$ is represented by $a_1,\dots,a_q$. Use $U$ in Definition~\ref{fnd:alltargetrelativeinterior} to choose an ordinary affinoid neighborhood $V=\mathcal M(B)$ of $y$ in $Y\times_X U$ with $y\in\operatorname{Int}(V/U)$. The ordinary stalks on $U,V$ at these points are $R,S$. Put $J=(a_1,\dots,a_q)$, $U_0=\mathcal M(A/J)$ and $V_0=\mathcal M(B/JB)$, with their quotient norms. The finite quotient and base-change formulas in Propositions~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras} and~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange} identify $V_0$ with $V\times_U U_0$. Each closed immersion has just one point above its indicated point, since the point seminorm kills the defining ideal and factors through the quotient. Write these points as $x_0,y_0$. Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation} gives their ordinary stalks $R/\mathfrak m$ and $S/\mathfrak m S$. The analytic fibre germ is unchanged: $\mathcal H(x_0)=\mathcal H(x)$ by quotient evaluation, and $a_i$ has zero image in this field for every $i$, so the two affinoid fibre algebras identified by Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres} coincide under finite base change. For each affinoid domain $D\subset U_0$ containing $x_0$, its test inverse image is the affinoid $V_0\times_{U_0}D=V\times_U D$. The forward inclusion of Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus} on $V\to U\leftarrow D$ makes $y_0$ interior over $D$ in that entire test. Thus $y_0\in\operatorname{Int}(V_0/U_0)$ by the same definition. Apply Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalinnergenericfibreflatness} to $V_0\to U_0$ at $y_0$, with the field stalk $R/\mathfrak m$. Under the stated stalk and fibre identifications its faithfully flat map is the canonical map in the assertion.}
{选取 $x$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域 $U=\mathcal M(A)$，使 $\mathfrak m$ 的有限生成组由 $a_1,\dots,a_q$ 表示。在 Definition~\ref{fnd:alltargetrelativeinterior} 中以 $U$ 作测试，取 $Y\times_X U$ 中 $y$ 的普通 affinoid 邻域 $V=\mathcal M(B)$，且 $y\in\operatorname{Int}(V/U)$。两个邻域在相应点的普通茎为 $R,S$。令 $J=(a_1,\dots,a_q)$、$U_0=\mathcal M(A/J)$ 和 $V_0=\mathcal M(B/JB)$，均取商范数。由 Propositions~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitealgebras} 和~\ref{fnd:generalizedfinitebasechange} 的有限商及基变换公式，$V_0=V\times_U U_0$。两个闭浸入在相应点上方各有一点，因为点的半范数在定义理想上为零并经商分解。记这两点为 $x_0,y_0$。由 Lemma~\ref{fnd:finiteanalyticbranchseparation}，其普通茎分别为 $R/\mathfrak m$ 和 $S/\mathfrak m S$。解析纤维芽不变：经商取值给出 $\mathcal H(x_0)=\mathcal H(x)$，各 $a_i$ 在此域的像为零，因此 Theorem~\ref{fnd:allpointaffinoidfibres} 所识别的两个 affinoid 纤维代数在有限基变换下相同。对每个含 $x_0$ 的 affinoid 域 $D\subset U_0$，其测试逆像为 affinoid 空间 $V_0\times_{U_0}D=V\times_U D$。将 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidinteriorcalculus} 的正向包含用于图 $V\to U\leftarrow D$，知 $y_0$ 位于整个测试逆像相对于 $D$ 的内部。故由同一定义，$y_0\in\operatorname{Int}(V_0/U_0)$。底的普通茎 $R/\mathfrak m$ 为域，故可将 Theorem~\ref{fnd:generalinnergenericfibreflatness} 应用于 $V_0\to U_0$ 在 $y_0$ 处的芽。经上述普通茎与纤维的识别，所得忠实平坦映射就是陈述中的典范映射。}
\end{proof}
