Result 3.8.1

3 Reduction, formal models and residual geometry

Open in the English reader · PDF page 501

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}\label{new:gauss_residue_formula}
\bi{The value group of $H_w$ is $\Gamma_w$. The group $L_w$ has rank $n-t$, and the residue field of $H_w$ is a purely transcendental extension of $\widetilde K$ of degree $n-t$. In particular,}
{$H_w$ 的 value group 为 $\Gamma_w$。Group $L_w$ 的 rank 为 $n-t$，且 $H_w$ 的 residue field 是 $\widetilde K$ 的 transcendence degree 为 $n-t$ 的 purely transcendental extension。特别地，}
\[
 \operatorname{trdeg}_{\widetilde K}\widetilde H_w
 +\dim_\Q\bigl((v(H_w^\times)/\Gamma)\otimes\Q\bigr)=n.
\]
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{The value of a nonzero Laurent polynomial is the minimum of its finitely many term values, so it belongs to $\Gamma_w$. Quotients have the same property, and the constants and variables generate the whole group $\Gamma_w$. Completion adds no values: for a nonzero limit $a$, a sufficiently close approximation $a_j$ has $|a-a_j|<|a|$, hence $|a_j|=|a|$. It adds no residue classes either, because an approximation with difference of norm less than one has the same residue.}
{非零 Laurent polynomial 的 value 是其有限个 term values 的 minimum，因此属于 $\Gamma_w$。Quotients 也如此，而 constants 与 variables 生成整个 group $\Gamma_w$。Completion 不增加 values：对非零 limit $a$，充分接近的 approximation $a_j$ 满足 $|a-a_j|<|a|$，故 $|a_j|=|a|$。Completion 也不增加 residue classes，因为 norm 小于一的差具有相同 residue。}

\bi{The homomorphism $\Z^n\to\Gamma_w/\Gamma$, $m\mapsto\langle m,w\rangle$, is surjective with kernel $L_w$. Tensoring with $\Q$ gives $\operatorname{rank}L_w=n-t$. Choose a basis $m_1,\ldots,m_{n-t}$ of this free abelian group and constants $c_j\in K^\times$ with $v(c_j)=-\langle m_j,w\rangle$. The monomials $U_j=c_jZ^{m_j}$ have norm one.}
{Homomorphism $\Z^n\to\Gamma_w/\Gamma$、$m\mapsto\langle m,w\rangle$ surjective，kernel 为 $L_w$。与 $\Q$ tensor 后得到 $\operatorname{rank}L_w=n-t$。选择此 free abelian group 的 basis $m_1,\ldots,m_{n-t}$，以及满足 $v(c_j)=-\langle m_j,w\rangle$ 的 constants $c_j\in K^\times$。Monomials $U_j=c_jZ^{m_j}$ 的 norm 均为一。}

\bi{Their residues are algebraically independent. A nonzero polynomial relation in their residues could be lifted to a Laurent polynomial in distinct exponent vectors $\sum_j a_jm_j$, with all nonzero leading coefficients of norm one. The Gauss norm of this polynomial is one, so its residue cannot be zero. Independence of the $m_j$ ensures that the exponent vectors are distinct.}
{它们的 residues algebraically independent。若 residues 满足 nonzero polynomial relation，可将其 lift 为 Laurent polynomial，其 exponent vectors 为互异的 $\sum_j a_jm_j$，所有 nonzero leading coefficients 的 norm 为一。此 polynomial 的 Gauss norm 为一，故 residue 不可能为零。$m_j$ 的 independence 保证 exponent vectors 互异。}

\bi{They generate the residue field as well. Let $F/G$ be a rational function of norm one. Divide numerator and denominator by one term of $G$ attaining its Gauss norm. Both resulting Laurent polynomials have norm one. Each surviving term in their reductions has exponent difference $m\in L_w$ and is a residue-field scalar times a Laurent monomial in the $U_j$. Terms of smaller norm disappear. The denominator reduction is nonzero by algebraic independence, so the residue of $F/G$ belongs to $\widetilde K(\widetilde U_1,\ldots,\widetilde U_{n-t})$. The completion observation proves the same assertion for $H_w$.}
{它们也生成 residue field。设 rational function $F/G$ 的 norm 为一。将 numerator 与 denominator 同除以 $G$ 中达到其 Gauss norm 的某一 term。所得两个 Laurent polynomials 的 norm 均为一。其 reductions 中每个保留下来的 term 的 exponent difference $m$ 属于 $L_w$，并且是 residue-field scalar 乘上 $U_j$ 的 Laurent monomial。Norm 更小的 terms 消失。由 algebraic independence，denominator reduction 非零，所以 $F/G$ 的 residue 属于 $\widetilde K(\widetilde U_1,\ldots,\widetilde U_{n-t})$。关于 completion 的观察给出 $H_w$ 的相同断言。}
\end{proof}
