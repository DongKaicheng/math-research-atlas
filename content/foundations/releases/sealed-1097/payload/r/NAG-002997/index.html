\begin{theorem}[Metrized line-bundle identity on fixed tests; F8MG-G]
\label{thm:green}
For the smooth metric and regular meromorphic section above, R-SPL
and R-ZP imply
\begin{equation}\label{eq:green}
 G_s(\dd\eta)+\delta_{D_s}(\eta)
                       =\int_Uc_1(\overline{\cL})\wedge\eta
 \qquad(\eta\in\A^{n-1,n-1}_{U,c}).
\end{equation}
With \(s'=f s\), one has
\(D_{s'}=D_s+\divis(f)\) and \(G_{s'}=G_s-L_f\).
If \(\|\cdot\|_2=e^{-\psi}\|\cdot\|_1\), with ordinary smooth
\(\psi\), then
\(G_{s,2}=G_{s,1}+\int\psi(\cdot)\) and
\(c_{1,2}=c_{1,1}+\dd\psi\).
Curvature adds under tensor product and changes sign under duality.
\end{theorem}
