The analytic point of a residual component

4 Analytic curves and skeleta

Open in the English reader · PDF page 652

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{proposition}[\bi{The analytic point of a residual component}
{剩余分支对应的解析点}]
\label{cand:reducedmodelcomponentpoints}
\bi{Let $\mathfrak X$ be an admissible formal $O$-scheme with
reduced special fibre, and let $X=\mathfrak X_\eta$. For each
generic point $\eta$ of an irreducible component of
$\mathfrak X_s$, the fibre $\operatorname{sp}^{-1}(\eta)$
consists of a unique point $x_\eta\in X$.
On each formal affine open, these points are exactly the Shilov
boundary of its generic fibre. They agree under restriction to
formal opens. If $\mathfrak X$ is quasi-compact, the resulting
component-point set is finite.}
{设 $\mathfrak X$ 为特殊纤维约化的 admissible 形式
$O$-概形，$X=\mathfrak X_\eta$。对 $\mathfrak X_s$ 的
每个不可约分支的泛点 $\eta$，纤维
$\operatorname{sp}^{-1}(\eta)$ 恰由一个解析点
$x_\eta\in X$ 组成。在每个形式仿射开集上，这些点恰为
其泛纤维的 Shilov 边界，并在形式开集限制下相容。
若 $\mathfrak X$ 拟紧，则所得分支点集有限。}
\end{proposition}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Choose a formal affine open
$\mathfrak U=\operatorname{Spf}A_0$ containing $\eta$.
The preceding lemma identifies its special fibre with strict
canonical reduction. Theorem~\ref{fnd:canonicalgenericshilov}
therefore gives a unique point above each component generic
point and identifies these points with the Shilov boundary of
$\mathfrak U_\eta$.}
{选取包含 $\eta$ 的形式仿射开集
$\mathfrak U=\operatorname{Spf}A_0$。上一引理将其特殊
纤维识别为 strict 典范约化。因此
Theorem~\ref{fnd:canonicalgenericshilov} 给出每个分支泛点
上方的唯一解析点，并将这些点识别为
$\mathfrak U_\eta$ 的 Shilov 边界。}

\bi{The specialization and formal-open compatibility in
Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful} give
$\operatorname{sp}^{-1}(\mathfrak U_s)=\mathfrak U_\eta$.
Thus every global point above $\eta$ already belongs to this
affine generic fibre. This proves global uniqueness as well as
existence. On two formal affine opens, restriction to an affine
open in their intersection containing $\eta$ shows that the
constructed points agree.}
{Proposition~\ref{fnd:formalglobalgenericfaithful} 中特化与
形式开集的相容性给出
$\operatorname{sp}^{-1}(\mathfrak U_s)=\mathfrak U_\eta$。
因此全局上方位于 $\eta$ 的每一点，本已属于此仿射泛纤维，
由此得到全局存在唯一性。对两个形式仿射开集，在它们的交内
取包含 $\eta$ 的仿射开集作限制，便知构造出的点相同。}

\bi{Finally, a finite formal affine cover exists when
$\mathfrak X$ is quasi-compact. By
Corollary~\ref{fnd:affinoidshilovinternal}, the generic fibre of
each member has a finite Shilov boundary. Every global component
generic point lies in a member of the cover, so its analytic lift
belongs to one of these finitely many finite boundaries. This
proves finiteness.}
{最后，$\mathfrak X$ 拟紧时可取有限形式仿射覆盖。由
Corollary~\ref{fnd:affinoidshilovinternal}，每个覆盖成员的
泛纤维都有有限 Shilov 边界。每个全局分支泛点属于某个
覆盖成员，因而其解析提升属于这些有限多个有限边界之一。
这就证明有限性。}
\end{proof}
