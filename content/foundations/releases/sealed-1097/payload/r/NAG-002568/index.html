Statement · exact TeX
\begin{theorem}[Persistent logarithmic graph; F8GP-1]\label{thm:base}
For every real \(S\), put
\[
 V_{0,S}=\{z\in V_0:|f_0(z)|\geq e^{-S}\},\qquad
 P_S=P\cap\left\{\lambda+\sum_i m_i s_i+b\cdot x\leq S\right\}.
 \tag{1.7}
\]
Every nonempty \(V_{0,S}\) is pure of analytic dimension \(n\).
The following chain is defined once, independently of \(S\):
\[
                         C_0=F_*\mathcal P.                \tag{1.8}
\]
The pushforward counts each full-rank cell with its lattice index
and discards cells on which \(F\) has rank below \(n\).
It is a finite nonnegative integral \(n\)-chain, and
\NextLinkTarget{foundations.nag-002568.eq.1.9}
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \Trop_{(g_0,f_0),V_{0,S}}(\mathcal O_{V_{0,S}})
                    =C_0|_{\{t\leq S\}}
 \quad}                                                    \tag{1.9}\label{fnd:nag002568:closed-cutoff-identity}
\]
for every \(S\), with zero chain for the empty domain.
Its support lies in
\[
 \left(\kappa+M\!\left(\prod_i[-A_i,B_i]\times Q\right)\right)
                            \times[t_{\min},\infty),
 \quad
 t_{\min}=\lambda-\sum_i m_iA_i+\min_Q b\cdot x.              \tag{1.10}
\]
Thus the same finite weighted chain works at all levels, including
levels where a full-dimensional cell lies in \(\{t=S\}\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{thm:base}]
Purity is \cref{lem:purity}. On \(V_{0,S}\), all entries of \(h\)
and \((g_0,f_0)\) are units. Their actual moment maps satisfy
\(t_{(g_0,f_0)}=F\circ t_h\).
If \(V_{0,S}\) is empty, the \hyperref[lem:image]{actual source image}
makes \(\mathcal P|_{\{t\circ F\leq S\}}\) zero. The
\hyperref[lem:proper]{closed-cutoff pushforward} then gives the required
zero chain. Otherwise adjoin the actual constant units \(a,c_1,\ldots,c_r\)
to \(h\). The whole level algebras and the transverse Gauss dimensions
are unchanged, and the saturated tangent lattices embed with zero
constant coordinates and index one. The translated formula is now a
literal character map for the identity finite map of \(V_{0,S}\).
Use \hyperref[v037:analytic:interiorcycle]{whole-interior projection}
off the target absolute boundary image. That lower-dimensional image
does not contain any top-dimensional cell of a full-rank image, even
at \(t=S\). On a common subdivision the
\hyperref[fnd:nag002405:global-finite-module-chain-weights]{finite-module chain and weights}
identify the closed chains from their generic coefficients. This gives
\[
 \Trop_{(g_0,f_0),V_{0,S}}(\mathcal O)
       =F_*\Trop_{h,V_{0,S}}(\mathcal O).
\]
Substitute \hyperref[lem:weight]{(3.2)}, then \hyperref[lem:proper]{(3.3)}, to obtain (1.9).
The bounds in (1.10) follow directly from
\(y_i\in[-A_i,B_i]\), \(x\in Q\), and \(s_i\geq-A_i\).
The finite chain is constructed before any \(S\) is chosen.
\end{proof}
