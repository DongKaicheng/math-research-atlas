Separated algebraic maps and the underlying topology

2 Analytic spaces, morphisms and fibres

Open in the English reader · PDF page 289

Statement · exact TeX
\begin{lemma}[\bi{Separated algebraic maps and the underlying topology}{分离代数映射与底层拓扑}]
\label{fnd:analytificationseparatedtopology}
\bi{If $Y\to Z$ is a separated morphism of finite-type $K$-schemes, then $Y^{\an}\to Z^{\an}$ is separated and is a Hausdorff map of topological spaces. In particular the inverse image of a Hausdorff subspace of $Z^{\an}$ is Hausdorff.}
{若 $Y\to Z$ 为有限型 $K$-概形间的分离态射，则 $Y^{\an}\to Z^{\an}$ 分离，并且是拓扑空间之间的 Hausdorff 映射。特别地，$Z^{\an}$ 中 Hausdorff 子空间的逆像为 Hausdorff。}
\end{lemma}
Full selected proof · exact TeX
\begin{proof}
\bi{Its algebraic diagonal is a closed immersion. Theorems~\ref{fnd:analytificationproducts} and \ref{fnd:analytificationfiniteclosed} identify its analytification with the analytic diagonal and preserve its closed-immersion property. A closed immersion restricts on any affinoid target domain to the quotient by its finite defining ideal: this follows first on defining affinoid charts by the quotient base-change presentation, and then on a finite affinoid refinement by Kiehl gluing, Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb}. Thus pulling the diagonal back to any affinoid product gives an affinoid closed subspace.}
{代数对角线是闭浸入。Theorems~\ref{fnd:analytificationproducts} 与 \ref{fnd:analytificationfiniteclosed} 将其解析化识别为解析对角线，并保持闭浸入性质。闭浸入限制到任意 affinoid 目标域后，都是由有限定义理想给出的商：先在定义它的 affinoid 图上用商的基变换表示证明，再在有限 affinoid 加细上应用 Kiehl 粘合，即 Theorem~\ref{fnd:fullgkiehltheoremb}。因此将对角线拉回任一 affinoid 乘积，就得到 affinoid 闭子空间。}

\bi{Consider two points $y_1,y_2$ above the same point $z$. Goodness permits affinoid neighbourhoods $V$ of $z$ and $U_i$ of $y_i$ with maps $U_i\to V$. The analytic product $P=U_1\times_V U_2$ is compact Hausdorff. Its closed diagonal pullback $D$ is compact. By Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidproducttopology}, the map from $P$ onto the ordinary topological fibre product is closed. The image of $D$ is exactly the ordinary diagonal in $U_1\times_{|V|}U_2$, and is therefore closed there. Restricting to the ordinary interiors of the chosen neighbourhoods proves that the relative ordinary diagonal is closed locally, hence closed. Distinct points in a common fibre can consequently be separated by neighbourhoods in $Y^{\an}$. Points with distinct images in a Hausdorff subspace are separated by inverse images of disjoint neighbourhoods of their images. These two cases give the last assertion.}
{考虑同一 $z$ 上方的两个点 $y_1,y_2$。由 goodness，可取 $z$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $V$ 以及 $y_i$ 的 affinoid 邻域 $U_i$，并使 $U_i\to V$。解析乘积 $P=U_1\times_V U_2$ 紧且 Hausdorff。其对角线闭拉回 $D$ 紧。由 Proposition~\ref{fnd:affinoidproducttopology}，$P$ 到普通拓扑纤维积的映射为闭映射。$D$ 的像恰好是 $U_1\times_{|V|}U_2$ 中的普通对角线，因此在其中闭。限制到所选邻域的普通内部，说明相对普通对角线局部闭，因而闭。于是同一纤维中的不同点可在 $Y^{\an}$ 中用邻域分离。对像位于 Hausdorff 子空间中且像不同的两点，则可拉回像点的不交邻域来分离。这两种情况证明最后一项。}
\end{proof}
