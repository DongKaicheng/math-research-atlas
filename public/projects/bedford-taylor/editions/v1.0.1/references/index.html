The contribution being investigated

The clearest candidate extensions concern the bridge from original current positivity to local regularization, the fixed-neighborhood approximation of locally bounded classical potentials, and curvature-representation independence against arbitrary allowed original backgrounds. The normal mixed-characteristic envelope argument is recorded as a separate foundational contribution requiring its own detailed scrutiny.

The continuous-potential product framework has precise antecedents in Chambert–Loir–Ducros. Relative capacity and non-pluripolar products have close complex counterparts; the first-energy inequality and real radial mass monotonicity also have established antecedents. The comparison therefore tracks the potential class, background, test space, field, domain and continuity quantifiers of each assertion.

The review includes the 2026 local theory of Wu and the tropical Monge–Ampère work of Amini–Piquerez–Wu. Candidate novelty and internal proof completion remain distinct judgments.
