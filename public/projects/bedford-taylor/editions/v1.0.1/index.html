The complex model

For locally bounded plurisubharmonic functions u1,…,uku_1,\ldots,u_ku1​,…,uk​ on a complex manifold, Bedford–Taylor theory defines the mixed current

ddcu1∧⋯∧ddcuk.dd^c u_1\wedge\cdots\wedge dd^c u_k.ddcu1​∧⋯∧ddcuk​.

These products are positive and symmetric, and are continuous under decreasing limits within this class. They provide the basis for pluripotential capacity, comparison principles and complex Monge–Ampère equations. Bedford–Taylor (1982).

A non-Archimedean counterpart

Work over a fixed finite extension K/QpK/\mathbf Q_pK/Qp​ with its given absolute value. On Berkovich spaces, the forms and currents of Chambert–Loir–Ducros provide a differential calculus in which d′d′′d'd''d′d′′ plays the role of ddcdd^cddc. The basic construction is

Classical psh potentials are upper-semicontinuous functions whose pullbacks to analytic curves are subharmonic after every complete isometric field extension. The bounded calculus uses real-valued, locally bounded such functions.

An allowed background is a symmetric CLD current SSS with d′S=d′′S=0d'S=d''S=0d′S=d′′S=0, positive on the signed column-square tests η∧Jη\eta\wedge J\etaη∧Jη defining the CLD middle-positive cone.

The goal is to develop the corresponding product, capacity and Monge–Ampère theory, and use it in the study of singular metrics and arithmetic intersections.

The central problem

When do local curvature formulas define an intrinsic operation?

Here intrinsic means that equal analytic curvature expressions give the same Bedford–Taylor current after insertion of any allowed background. Thus the operation depends on the analytic forms, not on a choice of local potentials or their coordinate presentation. The article defines the permitted piecewise-linear and piecewise-smooth potentials and their action on a background.

The main conjecture asks for this independence on one ordinary neighborhood fixed before the background is chosen, with equality on all original compact smooth CLD tests. Further questions concern the regularity of capacity and the measures that occur in a Monge–Ampère equation.

Current progress

On good, Hausdorff, boundaryless, quasi-smooth Berkovich spaces, for the bounded potentials and backgrounds above, mixed products have been constructed and shown to be symmetric, continuous under the specified decreasing limits, and unchanged when one potential is replaced by another with the same curvature. Further results concern capacity, unbounded potentials, measure equations and special cases of the main conjecture.
