\section{One-sided background order suffices for the restricted Hausdorff theorem}
\paragraph{Status.}
\texttt{PROVED-INTERNAL}, a direct consequence of the already audited
Hausdorff estimate and degree-one background linearity. This appendix
is separate from the proof sources already sent for review.

Retain the original domain, fixed field and absolute value, normalized
tester class, moment map $\tau$, and $S_0=\beta^{n-1}[X]$ with $q=1$.
Let $S'$ be an allowed background on the same ordinary representative.
Suppose that for a constant $C<\infty$ the actual residual current
\[
                       CS_0-S'
\]
is an allowed symmetric middle-positive separately $d'$- and
$d''$-closed background. No positive lower bound for $S'$ is required.
Then for every protected compact $P\Subset Q$ and Borel $B\subset P$
with $\mathcal H^{n-1}(B)<\infty$, the original outer capacity defined
using $S'$ satisfies
\[
 C_{S'}^*(\tau^{-1}B;V)
   =\sup_{H\subset\tau^{-1}B,\ H\text{ compact}}C_{S'}^*(H;V).
 \tag{OH1}
\]
Compact sets of the form $H=\tau^{-1}F$ with $F\subset B$ compact suffice.

\begin{proof}
For every original normalized psh tester $a$, the bounded linear
recursion and positivity of the residual background give
\[
 0\le Da\wedge S'\le C\,Da\wedge S_0.
\]
Taking suprema over the unchanged testers and then infima over the
unchanged original open neighborhoods yields
$C_{S'}^*(E;V)\le C\,C_{S_0}^*(E;V)$ for every relevant set $E$.
The protected-stage Hausdorff estimate therefore gives, for every
Borel $F\subset P$,
\[
 C_{S'}^*(\tau^{-1}F;V)
       \le C M_{n,P}\gamma\mathcal H^{n-1}(F).
\]
Choose compact $F\subset B$ with
$\mathcal H^{n-1}(B\setminus F)<\epsilon$, using inner regularity of
the single finite Radon measure $\mathcal H^{n-1}|_B$. Ordinary
subadditivity for the original $S'$ outer capacity gives
\[
 C_{S'}^*(\tau^{-1}B;V)
 \le C_{S'}^*(\tau^{-1}F;V)+C M_{n,P}\gamma\epsilon.
\]
Properness of $\tau$ over compact real sets makes $\tau^{-1}F$ compact.
Letting $\epsilon\downarrow0$ and using monotonicity proves (OH1).
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Range of the extension.}
The hypothesis is an actual current-order statement with an allowed
positive closed residual, valid on the original representative and all
original tests. It is not inferred from smoothness of $S'$ or comparison
of scalar coefficients in one selected tropicalization. In particular,
the moment form $\beta$ need not control extra directions introduced by
actual refinements. The theorem still requires a protected compact
stage, a moment-saturated Borel set, finite Hausdorff measure, and
remaining degree one. It does not promote these conclusions to arbitrary
allowed backgrounds or arbitrary original Borel sets. General B1
remains OPEN.
