Second-round theorem and status increment

This section updates, rather than replaces, the source-by-source comparison in Section “Theorem-by-theorem comparison with the classical theory”. The first-round statements remain frozen with their original hypotheses. An earlier OPEN entry is closed only by the particular second-round theorem cited below. No claim of literature priority is encoded by NEW_PROVED: that label records a proof supplied in this project, relative to its accepted inputs.

Two independent status axes and the fixed category

The labels EXTERNAL_KNOWN, INTERNAL_INPUT, DIRECT_COROLLARY, and NEW_PROVED record provenance. The labels PROVED, CONDITIONAL, OPEN, and FALSE assess a precisely qualified proposed analogy. A theorem proved under an explicit additional geometric hypothesis is PROVED with that hypothesis; the corresponding general-domain analogy is CONDITIONAL or OPEN. A counterexample to an unrestricted assertion does not change the status of its qualified replacement.

All local assertions retain the finite extension K/QpK/\mathbf Q_pK/Qp​, its prescribed absolute value, and the allowed good, Hausdorff, boundaryless, quasi-smooth pure-dimensional analytic spaces. Unless a narrower theorem states otherwise, SSS is an arbitrary fixed symmetric current of bidegree (p,p)(p,p)(p,p), positive in the original CLD cone and separately d′d'd′- and d′′d''d′′-closed. Products and current equalities are tested on all original compactly supported smooth complementary CLD forms. Neither a change of positivity cone nor an enlargement of the test sheaf is implicit. Put q=n−pq=n-pq=n−p; the relative top-capacity statements below use q≥1q\geq1q≥1.

R and B remain INTERNAL_INPUT. In particular, the bounded classical class and its [1][1][1]-background calculus are not second-round discoveries. The completed geometric adelic nefness theorem A2 also remains an INTERNAL_INPUT under its own number-field, integrability, and boundary hypotheses. The original classical, CLD, GR, and GK theorems recorded in Section “Theorem-by-theorem comparison with the classical theory” remain EXTERNAL_KNOWN in their stated categories.

PROVED / NEW_PROVED: the weighted L1L^1L1 estimate and uniform truncation estimate of Theorem “Uniform truncation estimate over an arbitrary background”, under the specified collar-trace integrability condition. 4

Symmetry and weighted monotone convergence

R1 already proves symmetry and weighted joint decreasing continuity for actual bounded classical limits and fixed SSS. PROVED / NEW_PROVED: bounded increasing sequences converge in outer relative capacity to their actual Borel supremum, Theorem “Increasing sequences are Cauchy in outer capacity” and Corollary “Local increasing convergence without a prescribed collar”; weighted increasing continuity in the completed class is Corollary “Weighted increasing limits with actual representatives”. Ambient no-jump and its common-collar energy equality are closed questions in this record. Replacing the actual supremum by its ambient usc regularization remains FALSE without qualification for arbitrary SSS. Unrestricted varying-SSS weighted stability remains FALSE.

5

Locality and maximum principles

R1 Borel maximum locality and ordinary restriction/locality remain PROVED. Theorem “Positive closed restriction to a complete pole set” proves that 1PS\mathbf1_P S1P​S and 1PcS\mathbf1_{P^c}S1Pc​S are separately closed for a classical pole set PPP, and that BT products commute with this restriction: PROVED / NEW_PROVED. Strict inequality sets of discontinuous potentials are Borel and locally quasi-open, not automatically ordinary open. The polar restriction theorem starts with a current defined on the entire domain; it is not a general extension theorem for a current defined only off the pole set.

6

Bedford–Taylor capacities

PROVED / DIRECT_COROLLARY: outer regularity on quasi-open sets, Proposition “Quasi-open sets are outer regular”, and the elementary outer-capacity properties in Lemma “Elementary outer-capacity facts”. PROVED / NEW_PROVED: two-sided local comparison with one fixed relatively compact affine capacity domain, Theorem “Uniform local comparison with a fixed domain”. For the ambient capacity on an exhaustion domain, compact raw outer regularity and compact continuity are Theorem “Compact continuity and compact outer regularity”; outer-capacity continuity for arbitrary increasing subsets of a fixed relatively compact core is Theorem thm:r2-outer-capacity-increasing-sets. Choquet capacitability and Borel outer regularity on that core are Corollary cor:r2-local-choquet.

Raw compact outer regularity and the corresponding Choquet theory for arbitrary relative backgrounds SSS remain OPEN. The ambient result retains the exhaustion-domain and fixed-core scope. General-domain nondegeneracy remains FALSE; the R1 proper-space example still applies.

7

Quasicontinuity and capacity convergence

R1 bounded quasicontinuity is retained. Theorems “Increasing sequences are Cauchy in outer capacity” and “BT calculus on the bounded capacitary closure” give the increasing and capacitary-completion extensions. Corollary “Quasicontinuity of extended classical psh functions” gives local finite quasicontinuity and lower-truncation outer-capacity convergence for every nontrivial extended classical psh function: PROVED / DIRECT_COROLLARY of the new polar-trace and weighted estimates.

Capacity precompactness of the full normalized continuous psh family, and weighted BT stability under bounded pointwise convergence alone, are FALSE, Proposition “Failure of capacity precompactness and pointwise weighted stability”. This counterexample does not refute compact-set domination by the weak closure of the admissible measure family.

8

Pluripolar and negligible sets

PROVED / NEW_PROVED: every original smooth ambient trace vanishes on a nontrivial classical pole set, Theorem “Vanishing of ambient smooth traces on a classical pole set”. DIRECT_COROLLARY: all ambient bounded BT coefficients avoid such sets, and every locally pluripolar subset has local outer capacity zero, Corollaries “Ambient bounded BT products avoid classical pole sets” and “Local outer-capacity zero of ambient pluripolar sets”. Theorem thm:r2-capacity-zero-polar-witness constructs a nontrivial witness for an outer-zero set. The resulting local equivalence of pluripolar, outer-zero, and negligible sets is Corollary cor:r2-polar-negligible-equivalence. Pole patching on one exhaustion domain is Corollary cor:r2-exhaustion-domain-pole-patching.

These local ambient equivalences are PROVED. Unqualified untwisted pole globalization on every analytic space is FALSE, already on PK1,an\mathbf P^{1,\mathrm{an}}_KPK1,an​. Arbitrary relative backgrounds may charge ambient pluripolar sets; their mass is governed by Theorem “Positive closed restriction to a complete pole set”, not forced to vanish.

9

Comparison and domination

The R1 compact-gap comparison theorem for arbitrary fixed SSS remains PROVED. PROVED / DIRECT_COROLLARY: ambient strict-gap detection and pointwise domination/uniqueness on the stated relatively compact framed affine domains, Theorem “Strict-gap detection for the ambient capacity” and Corollary “Pointwise ambient domination and uniqueness”. Relative atomic and smooth-factor detection criteria and explicit faithful backgrounds are proved in Propositions “Pointwise conclusions under atomic detection” and “A sufficient condition using smooth factors”.

Pointwise domination for arbitrary SSS remains CONDITIONAL on detection; the unrestricted version is FALSE even for nonzero SSS on a connected domain, Example “A nonzero connected-domain counterexample”. Capacity-modulo uniqueness and actual pointwise uniqueness are distinct assertions.

10

Relative extremal functions

PROVED / NEW_PROVED: least upper bounds in the local SSS-quasi-everywhere order, represented in AS,b\mathcal A_{S,b}AS,b​, Theorem “Local relative capacitary envelope”. Ambient bounded usc obstacle envelopes follow directly from B; orthogonality for bounded quasicontinuous usc projective obstacles is Theorem “Orthogonality for bounded quasicontinuous usc obstacles”, PROVED / NEW_PROVED. For the ambient background on an exhaustion domain, compact/open extremal equilibrium formulas are Theorems “The compact extremal and its equilibrium mass” and “Open-set extremals and their total mass”; arbitrary relatively compact sets satisfy the outer-capacity total-mass formula thm:r2-arbitrary-extremal-mass.

The ambient and relative categories remain distinct. General relative equilibrium-measure/contact-set formulas and their capacity identities remain OPEN; unrestricted replacement of an SSS-capacitary envelope by the ambient envelope is FALSE, Proposition “Ambient negligible sets can have positive relative capacity”.

11

Monge–Ampère and Dirichlet theory

PROVED / NEW_PROVED: the ambient local equivalence between maximality and the homogeneous equation, Theorem “Maximality and the homogeneous equation”. The explicit peak-barrier theorem “Homogeneous Dirichlet problem under peak barriers” gives a bounded classical solution of (DHg)n=0(DH_g)^n=0(DHg​)n=0, ordinary boundary values, uniqueness, and the boundary sup-norm stability estimate under Definition “Continuous psh peak barriers”. Corollary “Constant boundary data and one divisorial atom” gives an explicit continuous solution and uniqueness for a single positive divisorial atom and constant boundary data on the domains constructed with its barrier: PROVED / DIRECT_COROLLARY.

As a general-domain analogy the Dirichlet conclusion is CONDITIONAL on those explicit barriers. Their general geometric construction, interior continuity for the Perron solution, and existence for a general prescribed nonzero measure remain OPEN. Bounded negative exhaustion domains are explicitly defined and locally supplied; they are not silently assumed to have peak barriers at every boundary point.

Scope distinctions that survive the second round

Fixed backgrounds and controlled varying backgrounds.

The primary calculus keeps SSS fixed. The order-dominated replacement in Proposition “Order-dominated varying backgrounds” allows 0≤Sj≤CR00\leq S_j\leq C R_00≤Sj​≤CR0​, Sj→SS_j\to SSj​→S, with one fixed positive closed R0R_0R0​ and a fixed differentiated tuple; its multiplier hypotheses must also be retained. The second-round weak-closure multiplier theorem instead requires uniform coefficient bounds by a fixed local capacity and quasicontinuous weights. Neither statement removes the counterexample in Corollary “Failure for varying backgrounds”.

Actual representatives and ambient upper regularization.

An actual increasing supremum is now an element of the bounded capacitary closure, and its BT products are established there. For the ambient background, Theorem “Increasing envelopes have no ambient capacity jump” identifies it with its ordinary usc regularization locally outside outer-zero sets and proves the common-collar energy no-jump equality. More generally, Corollary “The unique classical representative” assigns each a∈A[1],ba\in\mathcal A_{[1],b}a∈A[1],b​ a unique classical representative rar_ara​ modulo such sets. For an arbitrary Borel representative aaa, rar_ara​ need not equal a∗a^*a∗; arbitrary modifications on polar sets are allowed in that capacitary class. This distinction does not affect the actual increasing-supremum theorem. Theorem “Local relative capacitary envelope” obtains a least upper bound without taking an uncountable union of exceptional sets: each competitor has its own quasi-everywhere comparison, and a countable energy construction determines the resulting class. For an arbitrary relative background SSS, the capacitary representative cannot in general be replaced by the ambient usc envelope.

Raw capacity and outer capacity.

For arbitrary SSS, the identity C∗(E)=C(E)C^*(E)=C(E)C∗(E)=C(E) is proved for quasi-open EEE; full raw compact outer regularity remains open. For S=[1]S=[1]S=[1] on an exhaustion domain, it is now proved for compact sets and for every Borel subset of each fixed relatively compact core. The ordinary-open proof of Theorem thm:r2-outer-capacity-increasing-sets supplies the additional continuity axiom needed for the local Choquet theorem; compact continuity of C∗C^*C∗ alone would not supply that proof. The raw-capacity closure criterion in Proposition “Weak closure and compact outer regularity” is therefore resolved for the stated ambient compact stages but remains an exact target for arbitrary relative SSS. The explicit potential-noncompactness example has an admissible unweighted weak limit and does not refute compact-set domination by the admissible measure family’s weak closure.

Polar sets and arbitrary support.

The general ambient pole-trace problem is now PROVED; it is no longer a missing hypothesis in the ambient nonconcentration result. The proof uses the absence of divisorial poles, whole-box model approximation, and the capacity-controlled multiplier passage, as recorded in Theorem “Vanishing of ambient smooth traces on a classical pole set”. For arbitrary SSS, the exact statement is 1PTS,k=T1PS,k\mathbf1_P T_{S,k}=T_{\mathbf1_PS,k}1P​TS,k​=T1P​S,k​ from Theorem “Positive closed restriction to a complete pole set”. In particular, a background already carried by PPP is not made nonpolar by applying bounded BT operators. Ambient outer-zero sets now admit local nontrivial pole witnesses, and the local pluripolar/negligible/outer-zero classes agree by Corollary cor:r2-polar-negligible-equivalence. There is also pole patching on a single connected exhaustion domain. A global untwisted version for all analytic spaces is false on proper connected spaces; these local and qualified global statements are not conflated.

Potential quotients.

Proposition “Actual classical representatives are determined by their original currents” concerns [u][u][u] and [v][v][v], not merely D[u]D[u]D[u] and D[v]D[v]D[v]. General independence under replacing a differentiated potential by another actual bounded classical potential with the same DDD-current remains OPEN. The continuous harmonic-difference result “Continuous harmonic multipliers”, the ambient-mixed and smooth background cases “Ambient mixed backgrounds” and “Smooth closed backgrounds”, and the dominated-closure case “Passage through a dominated weak closure” remain proved partial answers. None is silently promoted to the general assertion.

Comparison with other differential calculi.

Agreement on ordinary CLD tests with compatible model and Chambert–Loir measures is retained where the accepted R/B comparison applies. Equality on every δ\deltaδ-test form is a different statement. The report does not extend arbitrary original currents to an enlarged CLD25 test sheaf, or claim the nonpositive, nonclosed, or nondiagonal parts of the GK calculus. The completed A2 adelic theorem is an input to subsequent geometric applications, not an unresolved item in this local analytic program.

Three minimal targets for the next round

The former ambient no-jump and local polar-witness targets are now proved. The following remaining targets are not assumed anywhere in the second-round arguments.

  1. Compact domination for the raw capacity’s measure closure for arbitrary relative backgrounds. For an arbitrary allowed fixed SSS, on one fixed permitted capacity domain and compact test stage, prove or refute ν(K)≤Cap⁡S(K;Ω)for every compact K\nu(K)\leq\operatorname{Cap}_S(K;\Omega) \quad\text{for every compact }Kν(K)≤CapS​(K;Ω)for every compact K for every weak limit ν\nuν of normalized top BT measures (with the localization conventions of Proposition “Weak closure and compact outer regularity”). It is enough to resolve this precise closure statement; a capacity-convergent subsequence of all normalized potentials is not available. A counterexample must violate the displayed inequality, rather than only potential precompactness or weighted pointwise continuity. The ambient result on exhaustion domains is already closed and is not assigned again as new work.

  2. The general potential-quotient lemma. Given actual u,v∈Cb(U)u,v\in\mathcal C_b(U)u,v∈Cb​(U) with D[u]=D[v]D[u]=D[v]D[u]=D[v] as original currents, establish or refute D((u−v)S)=0D\bigl((u-v)S\bigr)=0D((u−v)S)=0 for every allowed fixed positive closed background SSS. The continuous-difference and specified-background cases are already proved. Equality of the original currents [u][u][u] and [v][v][v], and the unique ambient capacitary representative, do not by themselves settle this statement about equality of their DDD-currents.

  3. Nonzero data on one prescribed domain. Starting with an explicitly qualified domain and boundary barriers, construct and control solutions for a sum of at least two prescribed positive divisorial atoms on that same domain. The single-atom result “Constant boundary data and one divisorial atom” constructs its domain together with the atom and barrier. It does not provide this next fixed-domain step; adding its potentials produces mixed terms. A passage from finite atomic data to more general measures would then require uniform potential bounds, boundary control, and a proved capacity stability estimate for that family of solutions.

The homogeneous peak-barrier theorem yields bounded classical solutions with ordinary boundary limits; interior continuity has not been proved there. General prescribed nonzero measures and general ordinary domains still require additional geometric and analytic hypotheses. The relative equilibrium/contact problem and comparison on enlarged δ\deltaδ-test categories remain separate subsequent tasks.

Excluded routes and the conclusions they actually exclude

  1. FALSE: arbitrary varying-background weighted stability, even at order zero; see Corollary “Failure for varying backgrounds”. Fixed-background monotonicity and the qualified order-dominated replacement survive.

  2. FALSE: relative nonconcentration on every ambient pluripolar set, or universal relative negligibility of the discrepancy between an actual supremum and its ambient usc envelope; see Proposition “Concentration on a smooth divisor” and Proposition “Ambient negligible sets can have positive relative capacity”. The valid replacement is support restriction and the SSS-capacitary envelope.

  3. FALSE: pointwise domination solely from relative measure inequalities for an arbitrary nonzero SSS on a connected domain; see Example “A nonzero connected-domain counterexample”. Relative gap detection is a real additional condition.

  4. FALSE: nondegeneracy of the untwisted capacity on every analytic domain, including proper ones; see Proposition “Compact-domain degeneration”. Local framed domains and projective metric capacities must be stated separately.

  5. FALSE: compactness in capacity of the whole normalized continuous psh family, or weighted BT continuity under bounded pointwise convergence; see Proposition “Failure of capacity precompactness and pointwise weighted stability”. OPEN: the compact raw-capacity weak-closure inequality for arbitrary relative SSS. The latter is not contradicted by this example; the qualified ambient compact inequality is now proved.

  6. FALSE: a uniform first-moment estimate implies uniform integrability of a fixed unbounded multiplier, or universal exponential tail control for arbitrary SSS; see Propositions “A uniform first moment does not give uniform integrability” and “No uniform exponential improvement over arbitrary backgrounds”. The proved weighted estimate is used with its actual trace-integrability and truncation conclusions.

  7. FAILED_ATTEMPT: deriving raw outer regularity by interchanging an infimum over ordinary neighborhoods with a supremum over a measure family. Individual Radon outer regularity does not justify that interchange. For arbitrary SSS, the quasi-open argument and weak-closure characterization remain the available replacements. For the ambient exhaustion-domain capacity, the extremal mass formula and increasing ordinary sublevels supply the additional proof.

  8. FALSE: untwisted pole globalization on every analytic space. The rational point of PK1,an\mathbf P^{1,\mathrm{an}}_KPK1,an​ in Section sec:r2-polar-converse-choquet is locally pluripolar, while nontrivial global classical psh functions there are constant. Corollary cor:r2-exhaustion-domain-pole-patching proves the qualified replacement on one connected exhaustion domain.

  9. FAILED_ATTEMPT: inferring smooth-trace nullity merely from density of divisorial points, or passing an indicator through weak convergence without coefficient control. The successful polar proof additionally uses quasicontinuity of the actual indicator and uniform same-domain capacity estimates.

  10. FAILED_ATTEMPT: swapping two discontinuous moving limits by formal integration by parts, using the desired joint continuity to prove its own energy estimate, or treating an uncountable union of quasi-everywhere exceptional sets as negligible. The established R1 trace argument and R2 countable energy-envelope construction avoid these inferences.

The second-round record therefore closes several analytic gaps while retaining explicit frontier statements. It does not label the full non-Archimedean Bedford–Taylor program complete.

\subsection{Scope distinctions that survive the second round}

\paragraph{Fixed backgrounds and controlled varying backgrounds.}
The primary calculus keeps $S$ fixed. The order-dominated replacement
in Proposition~\ref{prop:dominated-background-stability} allows
$0\leq S_j\leq C R_0$, $S_j\to S$, with one fixed positive closed
$R_0$ and a fixed differentiated tuple; its multiplier hypotheses
must also be retained. The second-round weak-closure multiplier
theorem instead requires uniform coefficient bounds by a fixed local
capacity and quasicontinuous weights. Neither statement removes the
counterexample in Corollary~\ref{cor:varying-background-false}.

\paragraph{Actual representatives and ambient upper regularization.}
An actual increasing supremum is now an element of the bounded
capacitary closure, and its BT products are established there. For the
ambient background, Theorem~\ref{thm:r2-increasing-no-jump} identifies
it with its ordinary usc regularization locally outside outer-zero
sets and proves the common-collar energy no-jump equality. More
generally, Corollary~\ref{cor:r2-ambient-classical-representative}
assigns each $a\in\mathcal A_{[1],b}$ a unique classical representative
$r_a$ modulo such sets. For an arbitrary Borel representative $a$,
$r_a$ need not equal $a^*$; arbitrary modifications on polar sets are
allowed in that capacitary class. This distinction does not affect
the actual increasing-supremum theorem.
Theorem~\ref{thm:r2-local-capacitary-envelope} obtains a least upper
bound without taking an uncountable union of exceptional sets: each
competitor has its own quasi-everywhere comparison, and a countable
energy construction determines the resulting class. For an arbitrary
relative background $S$, the capacitary representative cannot in
general be replaced by the ambient usc envelope.

\paragraph{Raw capacity and outer capacity.}
For arbitrary $S$, the identity $C^*(E)=C(E)$ is proved for quasi-open
$E$; full raw compact outer regularity remains open. For $S=[1]$ on
an exhaustion domain, it is now proved for compact sets and for
every Borel subset of each fixed relatively compact core. The
ordinary-open proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:r2-outer-capacity-increasing-sets}
supplies the additional continuity axiom needed for the local
Choquet theorem; compact continuity of $C^*$ alone would not supply
that proof. The raw-capacity closure criterion in
Proposition~\ref{prop:capacity-weak-closure} is therefore resolved
for the stated ambient compact stages but remains an exact target
for arbitrary relative $S$. The explicit potential-noncompactness
example has an admissible unweighted weak limit and does not refute
compact-set domination by the admissible measure family's weak closure.

\paragraph{Polar sets and arbitrary support.}
The general ambient pole-trace problem is now \texttt{PROVED}; it is
no longer a missing hypothesis in the ambient nonconcentration result.
The proof uses the absence of divisorial poles, whole-box model
approximation, and the capacity-controlled multiplier passage, as
recorded in Theorem~\ref{thm:r2-ambient-polar-traces}. For arbitrary
$S$, the exact statement is
$\mathbf1_P T_{S,k}=T_{\mathbf1_PS,k}$ from
Theorem~\ref{thm:r2-polar-restriction}. In particular, a background
already carried by $P$ is not made nonpolar by applying bounded BT
operators. Ambient outer-zero sets now admit local nontrivial pole
witnesses, and the local pluripolar/negligible/outer-zero classes agree
by Corollary~\ref{cor:r2-polar-negligible-equivalence}. There is also
pole patching on a single connected exhaustion domain. A global
untwisted version for all analytic spaces is false on proper connected
spaces; these local and qualified global statements are not conflated.

\paragraph{Potential quotients.}
Proposition~\ref{prop:r2-current-faithfulness} concerns $[u]$ and
$[v]$, not merely $D[u]$ and $D[v]$. General independence under
replacing a differentiated potential by another actual bounded
classical potential with the same $D$-current remains \texttt{OPEN}.
The continuous harmonic-difference result
\ref{lem:continuous-harmonic-multipliers}, the ambient-mixed and smooth
background cases \ref{lem:potential-independence-ambient-mixed} and
\ref{lem:potential-independence-smooth}, and the dominated-closure
case \ref{prop:potential-independence-dominated-closure} remain proved
partial answers. None is silently promoted to the general assertion.

\paragraph{Comparison with other differential calculi.}
Agreement on ordinary CLD tests with compatible model and
Chambert--Loir measures is retained where the accepted R/B comparison
applies. Equality on every $\delta$-test form is a different statement.
The report does not extend arbitrary original currents to an enlarged
CLD25 test sheaf, or claim the nonpositive, nonclosed, or nondiagonal
parts of the GK calculus. The completed A2 adelic theorem is an input
to subsequent geometric applications, not an unresolved item in this
local analytic program.

\subsection{Three minimal targets for the next round}

The former ambient no-jump and local polar-witness targets are now
proved. The following remaining targets are not assumed anywhere in
the second-round arguments.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Compact domination for the raw capacity's measure
closure for arbitrary relative backgrounds.} For an arbitrary allowed
fixed $S$, on one fixed permitted capacity domain and compact test
stage, prove or refute
\[
 \nu(K)\leq\operatorname{Cap}_S(K;\Omega)
 \quad\text{for every compact }K
\]
for every weak limit $\nu$ of normalized top BT measures (with the
localization conventions of
Proposition~\ref{prop:capacity-weak-closure}). It is enough to resolve
this precise closure statement; a capacity-convergent subsequence of
all normalized potentials is not available. A counterexample must
violate the displayed inequality, rather than only potential
precompactness or weighted pointwise continuity. The ambient result
on exhaustion domains is already closed and is not assigned again
as new work.

\item \textbf{The general potential-quotient lemma.} Given actual
$u,v\in\Cb(U)$ with $D[u]=D[v]$ as original currents, establish
or refute
\[
 D\bigl((u-v)S\bigr)=0
\]
for every allowed fixed positive closed background $S$.
The continuous-difference and specified-background cases are already
proved. Equality of the original currents $[u]$ and $[v]$, and the
unique ambient capacitary representative, do not by themselves
settle this statement about equality of their $D$-currents.

\item \textbf{Nonzero data on one prescribed domain.} Starting with
an explicitly qualified domain and boundary barriers, construct and
control solutions for a sum of at least two prescribed positive
divisorial atoms on that \emph{same} domain. The single-atom result
\ref{cor:r2-dirac-dirichlet} constructs its domain together with the
atom and barrier. It does not provide this next fixed-domain step;
adding its potentials produces mixed terms. A passage from finite
atomic data to more general measures would then require uniform
potential bounds, boundary control, and a proved capacity stability
estimate for that family of solutions.
\end{enumerate}

The homogeneous peak-barrier theorem yields bounded classical
solutions with ordinary boundary limits; interior continuity has not
been proved there. General prescribed nonzero measures and general
ordinary domains still require additional geometric and analytic
hypotheses. The relative equilibrium/contact problem and comparison
on enlarged $\delta$-test categories remain separate subsequent tasks.

\subsection{Excluded routes and the conclusions they actually exclude}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \texttt{FALSE}: arbitrary varying-background weighted stability,
even at order zero; see
Corollary~\ref{cor:varying-background-false}. Fixed-background
monotonicity and the qualified order-dominated replacement survive.

\item \texttt{FALSE}: relative nonconcentration on every ambient
pluripolar set, or universal relative negligibility of the discrepancy
between an actual supremum and its ambient usc envelope; see
Proposition~\ref{prop:polar-relative-mass} and
Proposition~\ref{prop:r2-negligible-positive}. The valid replacement
is support restriction and the $S$-capacitary envelope.

\item \texttt{FALSE}: pointwise domination solely from relative
measure inequalities for an arbitrary nonzero $S$ on a connected
domain; see Example~\ref{ex:r2-nonzero-domination-failure}.
Relative gap detection is a real additional condition.

\item \texttt{FALSE}: nondegeneracy of the untwisted capacity on every
analytic domain, including proper ones; see
Proposition~\ref{prop:compact-capacity-zero}. Local framed domains
and projective metric capacities must be stated separately.

\item \texttt{FALSE}: compactness in capacity of the whole normalized
continuous psh family, or weighted BT continuity under bounded
pointwise convergence; see
Proposition~\ref{prop:r2-capacity-noncompactness}.
\texttt{OPEN}: the compact raw-capacity weak-closure inequality for
arbitrary relative $S$. The latter is not contradicted by this example;
the qualified ambient compact inequality is now proved.

\item \texttt{FALSE}: a uniform first-moment estimate implies uniform
integrability of a fixed unbounded multiplier, or universal exponential
tail control for arbitrary $S$; see
Propositions~\ref{prop:r2-unbounded-weight-failure} and
\ref{prop:r2-no-exponential-tails}. The proved weighted estimate is
used with its actual trace-integrability and truncation conclusions.

\item \texttt{FAILED\_ATTEMPT}: deriving raw outer regularity by
interchanging an infimum over ordinary neighborhoods with a supremum
over a measure family. Individual Radon outer regularity does not
justify that interchange. For arbitrary $S$, the quasi-open argument
and weak-closure characterization remain the available replacements.
For the ambient exhaustion-domain capacity, the extremal mass formula
and increasing ordinary sublevels supply the additional proof.

\item \texttt{FALSE}: untwisted pole globalization on every analytic
space. The rational point of $\mathbf P^{1,\an}_K$ in
Section~\ref{sec:r2-polar-converse-choquet} is locally pluripolar,
while nontrivial global classical psh functions there are constant.
Corollary~\ref{cor:r2-exhaustion-domain-pole-patching} proves the
qualified replacement on one connected exhaustion domain.

\item \texttt{FAILED\_ATTEMPT}: inferring smooth-trace nullity merely
from density of divisorial points, or passing an indicator through
weak convergence without coefficient control. The successful polar
proof additionally uses quasicontinuity of the actual indicator and
uniform same-domain capacity estimates.

\item \texttt{FAILED\_ATTEMPT}: swapping two discontinuous moving
limits by formal integration by parts, using the desired joint
continuity to prove its own energy estimate, or treating an
uncountable union of quasi-everywhere exceptional sets as negligible.
The established R1 trace argument and R2 countable energy-envelope
construction avoid these inferences.
\end{enumerate}

The second-round record therefore closes several analytic gaps while
retaining explicit frontier statements. It does not label the full
non-Archimedean Bedford--Taylor program complete.
