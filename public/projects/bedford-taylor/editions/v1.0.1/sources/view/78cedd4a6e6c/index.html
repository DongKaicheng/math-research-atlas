Remaining gates, false formulations, and the exact delta

The status of a universal assertion is not changed by proving a qualified theorem with a specific boundary class. Conversely, a counterexample to an unqualified assertion should not be recorded merely as an open problem. The following ledger distinguishes the two.

The capacity and unbounded-calculus boundary

Proved unbounded sector.

The original arbitrary-background construction now includes coefficient-finite non-polar closedness on ordinary germs; compact-sublevel outer quasicontinuity; the exact no-loss capacity-tail cone; its comparison, domination and relative uniqueness; all positive power-energy classes; all-degree curvature recursion and graded first-order and energy currents; and their precise capacity and strong-energy continuity statements. On one original affinoid neighborhood, the new analytic tester extension compares the full local capacities on compact cores. On strict analytic boxes with an extending background, the finite-mass boundary tester core computes raw capacity, and the full global E1E^1E1 Green and energy geometry hold. These are NEW_PROVED results with the individual assumptions printed in their modules.

General raw and outer capacities: OPEN.

An unrestricted proof of CS(K;V)=CS∗(K;V)C_S(K;V)=C_S^*(K;V)CS​(K;V)=CS∗​(K;V) for every compact KKK, or of raw/outer equality on every Borel set, is not provided. The elementary continuity of the outer regularization on decreasing compact sets was already frozen and is not the missing assertion. The remaining weak-limit gate is concrete: for a locally weakly convergent family of full normalized original tester measures, one needs control on a specified compact or Borel set by the raw tester capacity, rather than merely the outer-capacity bound inherited on opens. No infimum over neighborhoods has been interchanged with a supremum over testers.

The exact raw equality for the finite-mass core (TC6) concerns two tester families on the same domain. The domain comparisons (AL8)–(AL9) compare two capacities of the same kind. The strict-gap equality (BC17) and the scalar coarea estimates concern specified sets. None of these statements proves the unrestricted raw/outer assertion.

A single auxiliary energy tail: FALSE.

The new fixed-tail example (FT2) compares the full nonlinear capacity of one background with the linear energy capacity of a particular auxiliary tail: its point has positive nonlinear capacity and zero auxiliary energy capacity. The frozen R6 example instead compares the BT and energy capacities of the same degree-one background and gives a BT-null compact set of positive energy capacity. Together they explain why neither identifying the two polar ideals nor controlling an entire nonlinear tester family by one fixed linear form resolves the raw-capacity gate. They are not counterexamples to raw/outer regularity itself.

Unrestricted recursion: FALSE.

The logarithmic example (CR2) has a locally coefficient-integrable multiplier uNq−1uN_{q-1}uNq−1​, yet D(uNq−1)=δoD(uN_{q-1})=\delta_oD(uNq−1​)=δo​ while the non-polar top product is zero. No-loss and the printed integrability condition repair that passage; every positive power class supplies sufficient integrability. The discarded polar residue cannot be removed by redefining the measure on its pole after differentiation.

Unrestricted endpoint continuity: FALSE.

Uniform EσE^\sigmaEσ energy and capacity convergence alone do not give critical weighted continuity. The shell family in (PS7) has a nonzero limiting weighted atom. The proved repairs are fixed-tuple tails, uniform capacity-tail integrability, actual decreasing lower barriers, or the strong E1E^1E1 discrepancy topology with its derived uniform tails.

Poles, envelopes, and admissible representatives

Complete compact-sublevel pole sets.

The logarithmic construction gives one actual representative of each such pole set in every positive power class, simultaneously for all allowed extending backgrounds. This is NEW_PROVED. The ambient R2 pole/negligible statements on their specified exhaustion domains remain INTERNAL_INPUT. The unrestricted claim that a relative capacity-null set must be an ambient or compact-sublevel complete pole set is FALSE without nondegeneracy and domain qualifications. An appropriate converse with explicit nondegeneracy, representation and domain hypotheses remains OPEN.

Ordinary upper regularization: FALSE.

The all-power boundary-core example (PO1)–(PO7) retains a common actual lower barrier, finite mass, unit energies and even an actual classical solution of the prescribed source. Its ordinary upper-regularized Perron supremum nevertheless loses the source. This is a strengthening of the frozen proper-support example, not a new proof of the original bounded failure.

Relative completed envelopes: NEW_PROVED.

The barrier completion constructs original closed currents, derivatives and allowed energy currents before asserting their unbounded recursive identities. Its unbounded relative Perron envelope preserves Borel constraints and a common source lower bound. The completed barrier-constrained variational problem has a unique relative maximizer, a precise Euler inequality, optimal weighted-data stability, and a retraction on its finite-mass operator image. These are actual existence statements in an explicitly enlarged Borel potential class. They do not claim that every completed potential has an ambient classical representative.

Full relative strong completion: NEW_PROVED.

The first-energy strong space removes the prescribed actual lower barrier. Uniform deep tails and global weighted tightness give its complete actual-Borel/current realization. Its full comparison, domination, strict locality, exact truncation carriers and finite-mass approximation retain the encoded strong boundary class. The intrinsic capacity-layer formula characterizes each power moment of an already constructed completed member; its strict-gap raw/outer equality still does not give unrestricted capacity regularity.

The exact intrinsic characterization now requires same-domain bounded capacitary admissibility of every truncation and a finite outer first-energy capacity integral. This removes the need to prescribe a strongly Cauchy defining row, while retaining a genuine analytic admissibility gate. It also gives the precise monotone closure theorem. Every nonempty strong completed family has a relative Perron envelope by energy minimization over finite maxima, preserving source lower bounds and Borel upper constraints.

For positive data dominated by a constant times one actual first-energy top measure, the full strong cone has a unique variational maximizer, sharp energy bounds and quantitative data stability. Total source mass at the boundary may be infinite. Literal datum integrals are proved continuous from the completed tester estimate before maximization is used. The source equation remains distinct even in this explicit dominated class. Within one fixed dominated interval, the actual completed operator image is closed for the proved absolute energy-dual distance. This is an approximation criterion: it requires approximants already in that operator image and does not assert that the image fills the interval.

Maximality implies the source equation: FALSE.

The completed maximizer in (BV13), and even the unconstrained strong-cone maximizer in (VF13), can fail the prescribed current equation. The R10 compact-source rigidity extends through the new degree-zero current convergence to the entire strong completion: a uniform first-energy bound controls local convex Gauss traces by (SO3). Thus a classical representative is not the only missing issue: the constrained admissible variation cone is itself a substantive equation gate. The exact proved condition is (BV8). The current equation follows only under additional valid variations, as explicitly stated in the actual-class conditional corollary.

Dirichlet and obstacle problems

The complete relative linear measure-data problem.

For an arbitrary original degree-(n−1,n−1)(n-1,n-1)(n−1,n−1) background on the printed affinoid part domain, every finite signed energy-diffuse Radon datum has a unique relative renormalized zero-boundary solution. Its original current equation, exact level carriers, bounded-value coarea, hard-curvature global total-variation convergence, and reference independence are NEW_PROVED. The signed finite-diffuse obstacle problem likewise has a unique least majorant and an exact positive contact reaction, under the stated finite-curvature obstacle assumption. The reaction bound and source stability are proved before and during the limiting construction; contact is not inferred from an uncontrolled weak restriction.

The word zero boundary here denotes the specified part-space clipping and radiation conditions. It does not denote arbitrary ordinary pointwise boundary values. Unweighted energy of a measure-data solution is not always Radon locally; the explicit positive-data example proves this assertion FALSE. The chain and coarea theorems use only the finite bounded-value energy measures they actually require.

The nonlinear equation remains appropriately qualified.

For q>1q>1q>1, the new calculus identifies the derivative of every actual admissible power-class segment and solves its relative linearized signed diffuse problem on a small part domain. This is a DIRECT_COROLLARY; it does not realize an arbitrary energy tangent as a classical variation.

An unrestricted assertion that every capacity-controlled or finite-dual-energy datum has an ambient classical solution for every original SSS is already FALSE in the precise remaining-degree-one moment example, now also in its unbounded power classes and full strong completion. The genuinely open nonlinear program is therefore to specify a valid admissible data class, potential class and boundary condition, and prove existence, uniqueness, comparison and suitable regularity there. No theorem here removes those three specifications.

A precise degree-one image criterion.

For q=1q=1q=1 the strong first-energy completion embeds isometrically as the closed cone C\mathcal CC in the original part Hilbert space. The full finite-HHH-dual source equation is solvable in that cone exactly when its Riesz vector lies in C\mathcal CC, equivalently when the stated polar-cone inequalities hold. The source-functional projection, its sharp stability, and its exact polar defect are direct Hilbert consequences. The original smooth core is a difference of actual finite-mass common-collar potentials, so the cone has dense linear span. Its properness shows why dense span does not provide feasible two-sided directions at a specified maximizer. This is an exact criterion in the enlarged relative class, not a constructive classification of all ambient classical data or an identification of the BT and energy polar ideals. Pairings with general signed HHH-dual data remain continuous energy functionals unless their literal measure interpretation is separately justified.

The new quotient of actual power-class potentials modulo relative capacity does not imply (OGQ). No equality on the full GK delta-test category has been silently added.

Low-real-degree CTO: OPEN.

Signed constant-tensor order transfer in 1<b<r1<b<r1<b<r, including the critical b=2<r=3b=2<r=3b=2<r=3 range, is not completed by the new ordinary energy and capacity identities.
