Remaining minimal gates and failed inferences

Status.

This register is a scope audit, not an additional theorem. All original field, norm, topology, carrier, test, refinement and positivity conventions are those in Section sec:r24-main-scope. The positive implications cited below are proved in their exact source modules. No listed open statement is used as an input to an unconditional conclusion.

The unchanged principal gate

KC12: OPEN.

For every actual signed PL-zero on the same original ordinary germ and its correct absolute cap carrier, prove that the exact KC5 carrier-CLN seminorm is zero on every required compact control stage, in every relevant degree. Equivalently, by the frozen full-polar duality, prove annihilation by every genuine full original carrier polar. A zero on the absolute integration background is not itself such a certificate. Neither a finite chosen family of tests nor a representation by an arbitrary real current replaces this universal assertion.

The R17 real primitive theorem remains valid with its radial-hull support. This round does not prove that an actual analytic zero has zero real double derivative on that hull. A primitive lift on the original analytic carrier is not obtained merely by retaining a support in a real target. The same caution applies to convolution: no zero-preserving analytic convolution has been constructed.

ST19 and LG4 in mixed degree b≥2b\ge2b≥2: OPEN.

LC proves an exact conditional arbitrary-companion transfer for one global upstairs unit logarithm. Even under its explicit locality assumption, the further transfer of an arbitrary whole-carrier harmonic trace and of every original local ambient generator is not proved. LG7: OPEN.

The R23 finite-tuple geometry and smooth actual-delta faithfulness give an original Abhyankar model for the chosen analytic relation. The actual-zero relation on that model still has to yield its full carrier certificate. Returning the full seminorm certificate through the finite cover is a second gate if a downstairs seminorm conclusion is required. For all-original-background action, the frozen LG reduction already uses complete finite-etale base change and EC directly from the model certificate and does not require that reverse seminorm theorem. Finite preparation of a unit tuple, numerical positivity, and a smooth pullback identity do not establish either gate.

The coupled cap and the two different scalar obstructions

CM10: OPEN.

For the specified actual normal cap, numerical harmonicity of the PL trace on its intrinsic Gauss fiber gives the known actual coupled zero and arbitrary-background action zero. It has not been shown to give the unchanged full-polar certificate after cap multiplication. HC24.2a now identifies an arbitrary original continuous harmonic trace witness exactly with Picard zero. The completed residue field of the cap may have a function-field residue, so the finite residue field of the original KKK cannot be substituted for it.

CZ24 completes the ordinary zero-product family for this interface and retains a quantitative same-carrier estimate. Its bound still uses positive-tail costs on the larger G∩LG\cap LG∩L. It does not by itself compare the full carrier seminorms. It also does not cancel the exact cutoff commutators in HC. A literal local witness at every point of a compact cap stage and a single global GGG-harmonic factor are different statements.

RV4 and the scalar-boundary action target: OPEN.

For the quadratic PS energy form ZZZ, one still needs the balanced covector lift RV4, or an alternative direct action annihilation. The raw coordinate coefficient of a covector times a cap is not intrinsic, because the frozen normal-factor example has a zero covector product with a nonzero scalar cap. After RV4 the separately closed PS remainder has the exact formula Dδq=A−γ.(RG24.2)D_\delta q=A-\gamma . \tag{RG24.2}Dδ​q=A−γ.(RG24.2) The scalar boundary γ\gammaγ is retained. RV15 asks for γ=0\gamma=0γ=0 as an actual germ. AP24 supplies a weaker sufficient target: one finite continuous ordinary potential vvv, on one representative chosen before SSS and the tests, with [γ]=D[v].(RG24.3)[\gamma]=D[v]. \tag{RG24.3}[γ]=D[v].(RG24.3) The local DC property then follows from the exact degree-one order argument. Existence in (RG24.3) is not proved.

An alternative sufficient interface is transfer of the particular covector relation AP24.9 to the ordinary arbitrary-background identity AP24.10. This is beyond the scalar continuous-coefficient R6 theorem. The compactness theorem for already represented curvature intervals does not supply a potential for every closed current dominated by a represented curvature. Strictly rejected shortcuts

A common-domain harmonic comparator: FALSE.

BC constructs one fixed quadratic finite etale cover and unit such that, for every initial ordinary neighborhood, a later legal upstairs DC weight produces a local strict-defect unit logarithm with no harmonic extension to that whole neighborhood. The fixed-weight preparation remains possible; the refuted claim puts the neighborhood before every later weight. Neither the polynomial involved nor any of its polar values is shown to be a KC counterexample.

Finitely many local coefficient kernels: FALSE.

IK fixes just two original positive cap cycles. On every Gauss neighborhood their intrinsic restriction kernels include the distinct lines a+Nb=0a+Nb=0a+Nb=0, for every integer N≥1N\ge1N≥1. A finite number of fixed additional moment coordinates does not change these intrinsic local equalities. This refutes the literal finite-constant-type approach to coefficient gluing. It does not refute a finite generated object with genuinely variable relations and an appropriate specialization operation. Such an object and operation have not been constructed here. It also does not refute the elementary finite-subfamily detector for a fixed finite-dimensional global vector space.

One scalar for all intersecting branches: FALSE.

AP24.13–AP24.14 exhibit two intersecting original cap currents whose distinct constant coefficients cannot be represented by one continuous coefficient multiplying their sum. This simple diagnostic already has an explicit continuous PL potential and does not have RV15’s centered homogeneity. It is not a new counterexample family for the principal problem.

Fixed-outer harmonic witnesses on shifted caps.

FM24–FQ24 give an exact obstruction to this particular construction. For a fixed finite PL trace and one fixed outer ordinary domain, one sufficiently near negative-level complete-fiber witness already gives a harmonic witness for the original trace on a fixed smaller germ. In particular, a trace with a nonzero residue Picard class cannot acquire a sequence of such witnesses, or uniformly approximate witnesses on the protected complete cap stages, even if their norms are unbounded. The factor-two estimate is first proved on irrational levels; compact-image upper semicontinuity extends it to all nearby levels without Gauss saturation. The sufficient same-domain cap approximation estimate has no witness-norm cost: its remainder is εJh∏Ji\varepsilon J_h\prod J_iεJh​∏Ji​. The failure is therefore geometric extension of the witnesses, not control of their norms.

This is an obstruction only to that stronger witness construction. It neither supplies nor refutes a genuinely coupled certificate, and the positive scalar approximation gap is not a positive lower bound for a full carrier-CLN seminorm. Witnesses on domains depending on the level, and agreement only on a general closed carrier slice, are not covered by the proof. No norm-level/full- Gauss-fiber identification at rational levels is asserted.

Affine coefficients without the full closedness premise.

HB24 computes γ=−hDδh\gamma=-hD_\delta hγ=−hDδ​h for h=max⁡(0,s1,s2)h=\max(0,s_1,s_2)h=max(0,s1​,s2​), with Dγ=−δxD\gamma=-\delta_xDγ=−δx​. It therefore cannot be a finite sum of original smooth harmonic coefficients times separately closed scalar forms. The source A=d′h∧d′′hA=d'h\wedge d''hA=d′h∧d′′h is not separately closed, so this does not refute the RV hypotheses. A supplied representation γ=∑ℓiDδvi\gamma=\sum\ell_iD_\delta v_iγ=∑ℓi​Dδ​vi​ with harmonic ℓi\ell_iℓi​ would suffice for the ordinary arbitrary-background action gate; neither its construction under the genuine RV hypotheses nor the balancing of its separately extracted scalar faces is proved.

Higher nilpotence as quadratic division: FAILED_ATTEMPT.

CA24 retains the exact hypothetical polynomial identities and shows that, for dimension at least two, their degree-three-or-higher relations do not force the desired quadratic product to vanish. The explicitly stated abstract implication is false. The graded algebra used to show this is not an actual polar, analytic relation, or allowed background. The geometric coupled cap remains open. Positive-point certificates are used only for their specified positive presentations and their initial germ preparations; an unbounded family does not thereby receive one common domain.

Original-category pressure checks

No positive conclusion changes the original field or norm. Finite residue-field extensions used to describe an original analytic seminorm are not new fields for arbitrary-background current theory. Ordinary domains are chosen before the subsequent objects quantified in each theorem; BC and IK spell out failures of stronger uniform choices.

Every cap carrier is the complete support supplied by the frozen cap theorem. A real norm level, a chosen Gauss section, and a real radial hull are not substituted for it. All lower dimensional and singular boundary terms remain in HC and AP. The BC example is a scalar continuation obstruction; IK is an intrinsic restriction-kernel obstruction; CA is only a formal failed-inference witness. None supplies the entire actual-zero, same-germ, full-polar, arbitrary-background conjunction needed to label KC12 false.

For a fixed-stage conclusion, KC6 is applied to the tests whose required control stage is that stage. An all-current conclusion uses the premise at every LηL_\etaLη​. The distinction is explicit in CZ24 and remains in the result register. No smooth test equality has been promoted to unrestricted GK delta-test equality, and no top-degree point-cycle result is counted again as an all-degree theorem.

Next proof obligation.

A useful continuation should attack one of the concrete interfaces above: prove original local-unit drift annihilation for a genuine full polar; construct the missing continuous scalar boundary potential or covector transfer; or prove a coupled Picard/cap certificate with an unchanged ordinary representative and quantitative remainder. Merely naming an analytic relation complex, invoking local contractibility, or choosing a finite test family does not address these obligations.
