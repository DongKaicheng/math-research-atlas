Borel inner regularity of relative outer capacity

Result summary

Determine whether relative outer capacity is inner regular on Borel sets and establish the required exact recovery by tests.

Hypotheses and scope

The failures of raw–outer equality and strong subadditivity do not settle this question. The special saturated finite-Hausdorff-measure case is retained.
