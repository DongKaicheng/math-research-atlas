Stability of signed data and obstacle contact

Result summary

Total-variation convergence gives strong convergence of fixed clippings, their energy measures and the qualified smooth scalar-curvature expressions. The finite-curvature obstacle problem has the stated contact reaction.

Hypotheses and scope

Moving hard-level atoms require additional conditions; their unconditional convergence is not asserted.
