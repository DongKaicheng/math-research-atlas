M03 · Research record

The curvature kernel and independence of potentials

Result summary

If bounded classical potentials u and v have equal curvature currents, their difference is represented by an actual continuous pluriharmonic function. Consequently D((u−v)S)=0 for every allowed S, and bounded mixed products depend only on the curvature classes.

Hypotheses and scope

This settles the potential quotient. General relations between higher-degree actual currents are a separate open descent problem.
