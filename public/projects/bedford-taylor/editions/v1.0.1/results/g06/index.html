G06 · Research record

Controlled full-exact approximation as a sufficient criterion

Result summary

An approximation by full-exact representatives with one fixed Q gives zero action for every allowed background. The construction retains the singular component A₀.

Hypotheses and scope

Q is fixed before S, the tests and their refinements. The approximation hypothesis is not established for every actual zero relation.
