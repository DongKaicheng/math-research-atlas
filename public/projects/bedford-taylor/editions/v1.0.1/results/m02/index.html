M02 · Research record

Local CLN bounds, coefficient measures and Green identities

Result summary

Local CLN estimates on finite test families control coefficient measures, Borel multiplication, truncation and the qualified Green-energy identities.

Hypotheses and scope

The constants depend on a fixed larger ordinary neighborhood, the tests and S. These estimates do not assert continuity for arbitrary moving backgrounds with discontinuous potentials.
