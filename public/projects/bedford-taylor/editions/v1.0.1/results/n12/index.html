Countable-measure and nonliteral-dual obstructions

Result summary

A countable family of Radon measures need not detect the full capacity-null ideal. The recorded examples also show nonreflexivity, positive continuous duals with a nonliteral Dirac shadow, and failure of literality under unqualified norm limits.

Hypotheses and scope

The qualified compactness and literal-closure results under fixed domination are preserved.
