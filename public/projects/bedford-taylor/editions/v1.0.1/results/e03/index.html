Capacity characterization of the strong topology

Result summary

Strong energy discrepancy tends to zero exactly when local truncated outer-capacity convergence and convergence of the energies hold. The corresponding monotone closure and critical continuity follow.

Hypotheses and scope

S and the completion are fixed. Energy boundedness on its own is not this convergence criterion.
