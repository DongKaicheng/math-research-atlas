G09 · Research record

Actual curve stars, unit completion and joint Stokes

Result summary

The marked-curve construction, full analytic weights, finite Picard unit completion, final faithful separator and paired Hessian Stokes identity hold in the stated normal-three setting.

Hypotheses and scope

Radii must satisfy the stated commensurability conditions. New marks have zero old projection, and completion is performed before faithful separation.
