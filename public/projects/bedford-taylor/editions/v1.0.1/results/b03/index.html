B03 · Research record

Joint decreasing continuity for bounded classical potentials

Result summary

Mixed currents are jointly continuous under decreasing bounded classical approximations, including an undifferentiated potential in the same decreasing classical psh approximation, using its given Borel representative. In top degree this is local weak convergence against compactly supported continuous tests.

Hypotheses and scope

This result starts with background [1]. Its extension to a fixed arbitrary allowed background is recorded separately as M01.
