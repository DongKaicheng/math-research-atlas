Continuous harmonic potentials and scalar boundary action

Result summary

The source identifies the specified continuous harmonic, classical pluriharmonic and local unit-logarithm cases. When a continuous potential v is given, the action has the form M_S α=D(vS).

Hypotheses and scope

The existence of that continuous potential is a hypothesis. This does not construct one for an arbitrary full-polar relation.
