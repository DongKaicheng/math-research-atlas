N07 · Research record

Failure of continuous smooth-form double primitives

Result summary

An actual full-polar relation need not have the proposed continuous smooth-form double primitive, even after changing the Hessian representative.

Hypotheses and scope

Singular primitives are not excluded by this example. It is not a counterexample to KC12.
