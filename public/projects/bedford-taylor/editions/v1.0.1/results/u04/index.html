U04 · Research record

Degree budgets for mixed, first-order and energy currents

Result summary

The mixed recursion D(u_i N_α)=N_(α+e_i) and the first-order and energy constructions hold under their strict or critical degree and weight budgets.

Hypotheses and scope

Limits at the critical budget require the stated integrability and uniform-tail hypotheses. A uniform energy bound alone is insufficient there.
