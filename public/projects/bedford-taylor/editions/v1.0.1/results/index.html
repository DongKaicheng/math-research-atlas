Regularization and semipositive metrics

Locally bounded classical plurisubharmonic functions admit decreasing semipositive rational piecewise-linear approximations over the original field, on one fixed ordinary neighborhood. On smooth quasi-projective analytifications, continuous functions with positive CLD curvature admit smooth positive approximations on a fixed neighborhood, decreasing after shrinking. These two regularization results supply the bounded and continuous potential classes used in the product construction.

For an ample line bundle on a smooth projective variety admitting the stated regular model, curve positivity, CLD positivity and uniform Fubini–Study approximation are equivalent. The supporting results are contact rigidity, complete-intersection curve approximation and continuity and orthogonality of envelopes; the normal-model envelope theorem is recorded separately.

For rational integrable adelic line bundles with the specified identifications in the boundary completion, positive continuous local CLD curvature gives positive local mixed measures.

Unbounded products and finite energy

Truncation constructs non-polar products, separately closed whenever their coefficient masses are locally finite. On strict Laurent boxes with SSS extending past the closure, compact negative sublevels give an exact capacity-tail criterion for no loss of mass and a capacity characterization of every positive-power energy class. First energy admits a complete space of Borel representatives modulo relative outer-capacity zero, with intrinsic mixed currents and energies.

In this completion, strong convergence is exactly local truncated capacity convergence together with convergence of the energies. It gives the weighted-current limits up to the stated critical degree; mixed Green–Hölder has constant one.

The relative completed order also admits Perron envelopes, finite rooftops and the stated Borel obstacle problems. The unbounded calculus includes level currents and coarea formulas under their integrability hypotheses.

Source equations and Dirichlet problems

On the stated Laurent domains, differences of completed first-energy potentials form a Banach space, and the nonlinear source map has norm-closed image. Every strongly continuous dual datum has a unique variational projection. For positive Radon data acting by bounded, strongly continuous actual integration, a nonnegative scalar defect of this projection vanishes exactly when the source equation has a solution; that solution is unique.

Descent of analytic curvature relations

The constructed action respects degree-one relations and the specified constant-coefficient cycle relations. Quadratic relations descend for finite continuous closed-cell piecewise-linear or piecewise-smooth presentations whose joint residue rank is at most two, with no restriction on ambient dimension. Hence the quadratic action is canonical on germs of residue dimension at most two, including analytic surfaces. This is a statement about relations between expressions, beyond independence of individual potentials.

Higher action also descends on the ordinary-local weak closure of positive closed delta-backgrounds. Full-exact primitives, controlled approximations with one fixed positive current, and the stated carrier estimates provide proved sufficient criteria for descent.

For the specified model germs of residue dimension three, the action vanishes off the central point. It vanishes there below top degree; in top degree the remaining Dirac coefficient has an explicit spherical formula. Its general cancellation is the unresolved step.

Obstructions and remaining questions

The counterexamples separate raw from outer relative capacity, refute relative strong subadditivity and the universal extremal total-mass formula, and show that the rooftop energy constant cannot always be one. Other examples force critical tail control and source compatibility: even small positive smooth density perturbations can leave the nonlinear source image. Further obstructions concern proposed primitive, gluing, lifting and duality arguments. The real finite-fan and critical-mass examples are not counterexamples to an actual analytic zero relation.

The main remaining questions are descent of all analytic relations, critical central cancellation, Borel inner regularity for arbitrary relative outer capacities, strong continuity of general literal energy data, and conditions on a datum that ensure source compatibility. Full delta-test interfaces, larger domains and boundary classes, and classical representatives of completed potentials remain separate obligations.
