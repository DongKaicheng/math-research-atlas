Global Green calculus and mixed-energy constant one

Result summary

On a closed strict Laurent box, the global Green symmetry, mixed-energy Hölder inequality with optimal constant one, and the sharp midpoint estimate hold.

Hypotheses and scope

S extends to an ordinary neighborhood of the closed box and q=n−p. The constant in this inequality is distinct from the rooftop energy constant.
