C01 · Research record

Relative quasicontinuity and capacity completion

Result summary

Bounded classical potentials are locally quasicontinuous for the relative capacity, decreasing sequences converge in capacity, and the Borel completion is independent outside relative capacity-null sets. Strict-maximum locality holds in the stated form.

Hypotheses and scope

Relative capacity-null sets must be distinguished from ambient pluripolar sets for general S.
