The energy-difference Banach space and the closed source image

Result summary

The actual Borel energy-difference space and order-energy lattice have the stated Banach structure. The nonlinear source image is norm closed, and its source metric is complete.

Hypotheses and scope

No weak-star closedness or convexity is inferred. Positive continuous dual functionals need not be literal measure integrals.
