Parameter energy models and radial source inversion

Result summary

In the specified parameter models, the L^(k+1) energy and capacity formulas give the compatible L^((k+1)/k) source inverse. In the radial model, the flux-tail condition characterizes the stated inversion and stability.

Hypotheses and scope

These are results for the prescribed parameter and radial families. The existence and uniqueness scopes are retained separately from the general source problem.
