Hessian-wall drift in PL graph pullback

Result summary

The Jacobian-only strain chain rule fails for piecewise-linear graphs: the Hessian contribution on the wall must be retained.

Hypotheses and scope

The correction includes rank-loss loci. One cannot erase these terms when passing to an actual graph or common refinement.
