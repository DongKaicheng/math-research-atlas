N02 · Research record

Failure of relative strong subadditivity

Result summary

An actual torus model with q=1, n≥2 and S₀=β^(n−1) disproves the proposed strong subadditivity of relative outer capacity.

Hypotheses and scope

This does not disprove ordinary subadditivity or Borel inner regularity.
