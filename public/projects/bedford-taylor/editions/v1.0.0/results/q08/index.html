Q08 · Research record

Small nonradial interfaces and nonlinear source graphs

Result summary

In prescribed smooth toric backgrounds, the small Hölder graph and sphere-interface constructions give normal-trace lifting, a clamped inverse, all harmonic-moment conditions and a convergent nonlinear source graph.

Hypotheses and scope

Keep the separate hypotheses on sphere densities, exterior traces and moving interfaces. These are specified-model results, not an arbitrary-background inverse theorem.
