Bounded Bedford–Taylor products with a fixed background

Result summary

For any fixed allowed background S, the recursive mixed currents exist and are positive, separately closed, symmetric and multilinear in the potential slots. They restrict and glue on ordinary opens and satisfy weighted joint decreasing continuity.

Hypotheses and scope

Potentials are bounded classical psh. S is a fixed symmetric, CLD middle-positive, separately closed current. Every original smooth test and actual finite refinement remains in the test class.
