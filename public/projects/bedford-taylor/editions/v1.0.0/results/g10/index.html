Ordinary localization around the whole central fiber

Result summary

After freezing the required units, finite rational controls and properness cover the whole central fiber. The ordinary product localization gives the stated fixed-Q approximation.

Hypotheses and scope

This covers the entire central fiber, not just the Gauss section. Product valuations retain their joint structure.
