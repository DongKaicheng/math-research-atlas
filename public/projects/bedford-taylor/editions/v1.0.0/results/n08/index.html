N08 · Research record

Real Killing-gluing and fixed-Q obstructions

Result summary

Local strain and D-closedness do not force global Killing gluing. A real finite-fan model gives a positive L¹ gap for the specified fixed-Q approximation problem.

Hypotheses and scope

The real obstruction has not been realized as an actual analytic zero relation and therefore is not a counterexample to the general actual-descent conjecture.
