Q05 · Research record

Signed diffuse Dirichlet data in remaining degree one

Result summary

In remaining degree q=1, every finite signed energy-diffuse Radon datum has the stated unique canonical renormalized zero-boundary solution in the faithful part-energy space, with the original equation, clipping and radiation properties.

Hypotheses and scope

The ambient dimension can be arbitrary. The faithful regular strongly local coercive part form and energy-capacity diffuseness are required. The solution is not automatically in the classical psh cone or nonlinear strong E¹ cone.
