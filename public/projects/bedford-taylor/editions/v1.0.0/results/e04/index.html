Rooftops, Perron envelopes and obstacle problems

Result summary

Relative envelopes, finite rooftops, Borel obstacle constraints and preservation of a common lower source are constructed. The constrained variational problem has its stated unique maximizer.

Hypotheses and scope

The maximizer and its Euler inequality do not by themselves imply a nonlinear source equation; the residual must also vanish.
