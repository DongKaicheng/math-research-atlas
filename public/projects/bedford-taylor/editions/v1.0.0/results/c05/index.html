Inner regularity on saturated sets of finite Hausdorff measure

Result summary

In the specified moment box with S₀=β^(n−1)[X], the saturated Borel sets of finite (n−1)-dimensional Hausdorff measure have the proved inner-regularity property. The stated one-sided background-order extension also holds.

Hypotheses and scope

This is a special class of sets and backgrounds. It does not settle inner regularity for all Borel sets and all S.
