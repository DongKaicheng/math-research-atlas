Literal energy-difference boundedness and strong L¹ continuity

Result summary

For one fixed S, determine whether a literal energy-difference-bounded datum is continuous in L¹ along strong E¹ convergence, including general nonzero limits and the allowed infinite-mass setting.

Hypotheses and scope

Fixed domination and the qualified capacity-control subclasses are known. Counterexamples that change S do not settle this question.
