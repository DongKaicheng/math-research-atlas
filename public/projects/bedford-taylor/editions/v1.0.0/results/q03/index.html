Q03 · Research record

A single geometric probe for source compatibility

Result summary

For a literal positive Radon datum that is strongly continuous, the nonnegative probe Ξ=−nψ₀ vanishes exactly when the full original source equation holds. It controls the residual on every compact test.

Hypotheses and scope

The criterion does not require an assumed subsolution. Conditions checkable directly from the datum that force Ξ=0 remain an open problem.
