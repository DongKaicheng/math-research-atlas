R01 · Research record

Fixed-neighborhood regularization of continuous current-positive functions

Result summary

For a finite continuous function, positivity as an original CLD current is equivalent to local uniform approximation by smooth functions with positive curvature on one fixed ordinary neighborhood. After one further shrinking, the approximation can be chosen decreasing and uniform.

Hypotheses and scope

The ground field is the original finite extension of Q_p with its original absolute value. The space is the analytification of a smooth quasi-projective algebraic space. This statement does not cover arbitrary upper-semicontinuous functions.
