\section{PS coefficient caps and sharp conormal reconstruction}
\label{sec:r10-pws-curvature-reconstruction}

This section uses the intrinsic PS differential module of
Section~\ref{sec:r10-pws-differential-module}.  It removes the
smooth-coefficient restriction from the corrected cap criterion
and from the positive-curvature-generated reconstruction theorem.
It does not remove the independent capped-background relation
premise.  All backgrounds and final tests remain original over
the fixed field and absolute value.

\begin{lemma}[Corrected PS caps, full fibers, and every parameter]
\label{lem:r10-pws-corrected-cap-criterion}
Let $R\in\mathscr N_{\rm PS}^{a,b}$, and let $c\ge1$ satisfy
$\max(a,b)+c\le n$.  For any local original smooth rank-$c$
unit map $f$ and real parameter $\mathbf t$, define
\[
 \mathfrak C_{f,\mathbf t}R=
 \mathfrak B_{(\log|f_c|-t_c)_+}\cdots
 \mathfrak B_{(\log|f_1|-t_1)_+}R.
 \tag{PC1}
\]
For a PS witness $R=\sum_\nu\alpha_\nu\wedge S_\nu$, it has
the intrinsic factorization
\[
 \mathfrak C_{f,\mathbf t}R=j_*Q_R,\qquad
 Q_R=\sum_\nu j^*\alpha_\nu\wedge Q_{S_\nu}
 \tag{PC2}
\]
on the complete fiber from the frozen R9 theorem.
Here PS multiplication on the specified closed cap currents
$Q_{S_\nu}$ is the tangential coefficient product; no
auxiliary-field BT operation is defined.  The current $Q_R$
is independent of the witness and need not be closed.

For every fixed original compact smooth complementary test
$\eta$, the pairing
$\langle\mathfrak C_{f,\mathbf t}R,\eta\rangle$ is continuous
in $\mathbf t$.  For diagonal $R\in\mathscr N_{\rm PS}^{P,P}$
and $1\le c\le n-P$, the following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Every local corrected intrinsic rank-$c$ cap is zero
      for every real parameter, on every original intrinsic test.
\item Every local tuple of original analytic units satisfies
\[
                \omega_{g_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\omega_{g_c}
                                  \wedge R=0
 \tag{PC3}
\]
      on all original complementary tests.
\end{enumerate}
No symmetry, separate closedness, or positivity of $R$ is
required.
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Status: \texttt{NEW\_PROVED}} for the PS
extension of the already printed smooth coefficient criterion.

\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:r10-pws-corrected-bounded-insertion} gives
the finite sum $\sum_\nu\alpha_\nu\wedge
\mathfrak C_{f,\mathbf t}S_\nu$ on the original space.
Each latter cap has the frozen full-fiber factorization
$j_*Q_{S_\nu}$.

For clarity, the tangential product in (PC2) requires no new
complete-field intersection theory.  R9 supplies an actual
positive separately closed coefficient-Radon current $Q_{S_\nu}$
and all original tests on its specified complete fiber.
The elementary R6 level-tangency proof applies to that current:
bounded smooth cutoffs of a level force both conormal factors
to annihilate its coefficient restriction to the level.
Consequently the finite PS face formula defines
$j^*\alpha_\nu\wedge Q_{S_\nu}$ independently of incident
extensions.  Original moment units restrict to fiber units;
the same tangent-restriction argument respects zero PS
representatives and every further actual fiber moment.
On each Borel face the smooth projection formula then gives
the PS projection formula
\[
 j_*(j^*\alpha_\nu\wedge Q_{S_\nu})
                        =\alpha_\nu\wedge j_*Q_{S_\nu}.
\]
Finite summation proves (PC2).  Exact original full-fiber
lifting of every smooth intrinsic test makes this pushforward
injective.  Thus the intrinsic factor is unique, even though
it need not be separately closed.

Fix a compact parameter set and an original compact test.
The positive closed currents
$T_{\nu,\mathbf t}=\mathfrak C_{f,\mathbf t}S_\nu$ have
uniform bounds on each finite family of compact positive
controls: their pairings with those controls are continuous
by the frozen R9 continuous-cap theorem.  The uniform estimate
(P3) therefore approximates the fixed PS pairing
$\langle\alpha_\nu\wedge T_{\nu,\mathbf t},\eta\rangle$
uniformly on this parameter set by smooth coefficient pairings.
Those pairings are continuous by R9, so their uniform limit
is continuous.  This proves the claimed every-parameter
continuity without a continuity assertion for nonclosed
backgrounds imported from outside the PS module.

The original unweighted and compactly weighted parameter
averaging identities extend to these PS pairings in the
same way.  One may first smooth each coefficient, use the
frozen identity, and then pass by (P3), uniform compact
parameter mass controls, and dominated integration.  The
averaged closed currents themselves have finite compact
controls by the R9 parameter-kernel theorem.  Thus their
PS pairings converge on the other side as well.

If every cap is zero, unweighted local averaging gives
the corresponding smooth rank-$c$ wedge zero.
Conversely, if that wedge is zero, its product with every
compact continuous function of the parameter logarithms
is zero.  The weighted identity makes every compactly
weighted integral of each cap pairing zero.  A continuous
function with all those integrals zero is identically
zero.  This gives the cap equality at every parameter,
and exact intrinsic test lifting gives its full-fiber
version.  Finally the frozen original norm-preserving
smoothification converts arbitrary local unit tuples to
smooth parameter tuples with exactly the same logarithms,
hinges, and smooth rank-one forms.  This proves (PC3) and
its converse in the ordinary local topology.  All smoothing
and averaging arguments were made after adjoining the
chosen original test moments, so every actual refinement
is admitted.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[PS-weighted curvature generation]
\label{def:r10-pws-curvature-generated}
Let $\mathscr G_{\rm PS}^{a,b}$ be the PS submodule of
$\mathscr N_{\rm PS}^{a,b}$ locally generated by
$D(vS)$, where $S$ is any allowed original positive closed
background and
$v\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}\downarrow}$ is one of the
explicitly regularizable bounded classical slots defined
earlier.  Thus a member is locally a finite sum
$\alpha_\nu\wedge D(v_\nu S_\nu)$ with arbitrary actual
PS coefficients $\alpha_\nu$.
\end{definition}

Every positive-codimension prescribed pure cycle action,
multiplied by any actual PS coefficient, belongs to this
module: its finite signed PL presentation has at least
one regularizable convex PL slot.  The module is stable
under all PS coefficients and all permitted corrected
bounded insertions, by (P11) and mixed symmetry.  This
statement is a \texttt{DIRECT\_COROLLARY}; it does not
assert that every bounded classical potential is
regularizable.

\begin{lemma}[Conormal annihilation with PS coefficients]
\label{lem:r10-pws-generated-conormal-annihilation}
Let $R\in\mathscr G_{\rm PS}^{P,P}$ and $q=n-P$.
Let $0\le j<q$, $\ell=q-j$, and
$\Omega=\omega_{g_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\omega_{g_j}$
for local original units.  If $n\le j+2\ell$, then
\[
                    \langle R,\Omega\wedge
                              s_{\ell,\ell}\gamma\rangle=0
 \tag{PC4}
\]
for every compact original prime form
$\gamma\in\mathscr A_c^{2\ell,0}$ on that open.
The assertion does not require $R$ to be symmetric or closed.
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Status: \texttt{NEW\_PROVED}} in this
PS coefficient scope.

\begin{proof}
Take one term $\alpha\wedge D(vS)$ and a local smooth
decreasing approximation $v_m\downarrow v$.  The actual
form identity proved in the smooth conormal section says
\[
                    \Omega\wedge Dv_m\wedge
                                  s_{\ell,\ell}\gamma=0.
 \tag{PC5}
\]
On each actual tangent face, rank-deficient conormals
make $\Omega=0$.  Otherwise quotient by their $j$
covectors.  Its dimension is at most $n-j\le2\ell$.
The top alternating shuffle is annihilated by every
symmetric $(1,1)$ tensor, including the Hessian of $v_m$.
This proves (PC5) on the full actual form sheaf, including
the moments of each $v_m$ and of the test.

Canonical PS associativity and the smooth closed-background
rule make the corresponding pairing with
$\alpha\wedge D(v_mS)$ zero.  The fixed-PS version of
(P5), applied to the positive closed currents $D(v_mS)$,
passes this equality to $\alpha\wedge D(vS)$.
This use of PS test convergence is essential: the fixed
coefficient is not assumed smooth and is not differentiated.
Finite sums and ordinary localization prove (PC4).
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The sharp PS-generated equality thresholds]
\label{thm:r10-pws-generated-thresholds}
Let $1\le P<n$, $q=n-P$, and $1\le c\le q$.
For currents in $\mathscr G_{\rm PS}^{P,P}$, let
$\mathscr G_{{\rm PS},c}$ denote the kernel of all local
$c$-fold smooth unit wedges (PC3), with
$\mathscr G_{{\rm PS},0}=\{0\}$.
Without a symmetry hypothesis, the universal kernel jump
from $c-1$ to $c$ is strict exactly when
\[
                              n>2q-c+1.
 \tag{PC6}
\]
For the symmetric submodule it is strict exactly when
both (PC6) holds and $q-c+1$ is even.  Accordingly the
sharp universal zero-detection ranges are
\[
 \begin{array}{ll}
 c\le h_{\rm all}(n,P)=\max(0,n-2P+1),
                      &\text{without symmetry},\\[2pt]
 c\le h_{\rm ex}(n,P)
       =\max\{q\bmod2,\ q-2\lfloor(P-1)/2\rfloor\},
                      &\text{with symmetry}.
 \end{array}
 \tag{PC7}
\]
Within the indicated range, every zero corrected rank-$c$
cap implies $R=0$ on all original tests.  Sharpness means
that the excluded universal implications have explicit
original torus witnesses; strictness is not asserted on
every individual analytic space.
\end{theorem}

\noindent\textbf{Status: \texttt{NEW\_PROVED}} for the
full PS coefficient class.  The threshold arithmetic and
sharp torus witnesses are already proved in the smooth
generated-response theorem and are not counted twice.

\begin{proof}
Put $j=c-1$ and $\ell=q-j$.  If $R$ has zero
$c$-fold wedges, then every $j$-fold compression
$T=\Omega\wedge R$ is Hessian-invisible.  The intrinsic
alternating quotient therefore says that its entire
pairing is determined by its pairings with
$s_{\ell,\ell}\gamma$.  This algebraic factorization
does not require separate closedness or positivity.
When $n\le j+2\ell$, these pairings vanish by (PC4).
Thus every $j$-compression is zero, which proves the
non-jump assertion.  In the symmetric case the same
factorization also vanishes for odd $\ell$ by the
printed prime--double-prime symmetry rule.

The strict-jump examples constructed in (X8)--(X9) and
(G10)--(G11) use smooth coefficients and hence already
belong to $\mathscr G_{\rm PS}$.  Their complete
original-refinement calculations are unchanged; the
nonsymmetric examples allow odd $\ell$ as well.
They prove exactly the opposite implications in (PC6).
Inducting the non-jump steps from $c$ to zero yields
the bounds (PC7), with the same elementary integer
calculation as before.  The cap statement follows from
the equivalence in Lemma
~\ref{lem:r10-pws-corrected-cap-criterion}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A PS variable-coefficient arbitrary-background reduction]
\label{cor:r10-pws-variable-relation-reconstruction}
Fix an allowed arbitrary original background $S$.  Let
$e$ be a finite compatible PS/PL presentation whose
prescribed response has diagonal degree $(P,P)$,
$1\le P<n$, and is a finite sum
\[
                R=\mathcal A_S(e)
                       =\sum_i\alpha_i\wedge\mathcal A(C_i)S,
 \tag{PC8}
\]
where every $\alpha_i$ is an actual PS form and every
constant pure cycle $C_i$ has positive codimension.
Fix $1\le c\le n-P$.  Suppose independently that the
same presentation acts by zero on every local original
pushed rank-$c$ cap background of $S$, at every real
parameter.  Then $R=0$ on every original complementary
test whenever $c\le h_{\rm all}(n,P)$; if $R$ is
symmetric, it suffices that $c\le h_{\rm ex}(n,P)$.
All statements allow every actual moment refinement.
\end{corollary}

\noindent\textbf{Status: \texttt{NEW\_PROVED}} for the
response reconstruction.  As an application to all
actual-zero presentations, it remains
\texttt{CONDITIONAL} on the printed capped-background
premise.

\begin{proof}
Every term of (PC8) belongs to
$\mathscr G_{\rm PS}$ by its positive-codimension PL
presentation.  The corrected operator is PS-linear.
Mixed symmetry of the frozen closed tails therefore
identifies each corrected cap of $R$ with the response
of the same presentation on the original capped $S$.
The premise makes these zero.  Apply the preceding
threshold theorem.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The exact progress and the remaining quotient gate.}
The derivative-Radon and interface gate for finite actual PS
coefficients has now been proved through (P3)--(P13); it
is no longer assumed in (PC8).  This does not identify
ordinary equality with equality on all GK delta tests.
Nor does an actual-zero analytic presentation automatically
supply the independent cap-background premise above.
Individual codimension-zero summands need not belong to
$\mathscr G_{\rm PS}$.  For a compatible actual-zero expression,
however, Proposition~\ref{prop:r10-actual-zero-codim-zero-removal}
uses the frozen global actual trigrading to discard their
already-zero PS sum.  Corollary
~\ref{cor:r10-actual-zero-full-ps-cap-reduction} therefore removes
the positive-cycle-codimension restriction in this actual-zero
application, while retaining the independent cap premise.
Theorem~\ref{thm:r10-ps-module-positivity} also proves middle
positivity on the finite actual PS module by closed conormal
penalties.  The unrestricted analytic relation implication and
reflection of middle order from actual signed cycle inequalities
remain the separate \texttt{OPEN} GQ/CTO gates.
