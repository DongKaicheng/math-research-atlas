# Central degree-one backgrounds: the missing cap comparison

Author: `r13_codim_two_geometry`.

Status: **failure-route and dependency audit, not a new theorem**. The definitions below use retained calculus. No local potential, numerical class, restriction to a curve, or cap representation is constructed for an arbitrary allowed current. The later Round 13 MT--LE--PT results do construct a protected real transpose and its critical tangents, and identify their relevant pairing; Section 7 records that progress separately from the still missing cap comparison. Independent internal review elsewhere is not peer review or formal certification.

## 1. The residual under investigation

In the supplied smooth integral normal-three model and fixed finite closed-cell quadratic class of PA, the new ordinary cover gives zero action away from the original generic model point `y`. PA's separately reviewed current argument shows that, for an arbitrary allowed degree-one background `S`, the remaining action is

\[
\mathcal A_S(P)=c_S(P)\,\delta_y.
\]

The original finite field extension, absolute value, ordinary domains, middle positivity, separate closedness, original compact smooth tests, and all actual refinements remain fixed. The problem is to prove `c_S(P)=0` for every such `S`. Covering the puncture does not determine that scalar.

The finite actual relation and `S` are already given objects on the original analytic space. Temkin reduction, a residue curve, and a tropical source fan are geometric devices used to calculate particular expressions; none is a replacement domain for the arbitrary current.

## 2. What the cap construction permits us to define

Fix finitely many actual projective cap potentials `q_L` supplied by PC/AD/GT on one common ordinary neighborhood of `y`. They are actual continuous locally bounded classical psh functions. The retained fixed-background bounded BT theorem defines the positive top-degree measure

\[
\mu_{S;L,M}=Dq_L\wedge Dq_M\wedge S.
\]

Its atom

\[
m_S(L,M):=\mu_{S;L,M}(\{y\})
\]

is therefore a well-defined nonnegative real number for this chosen pair of actual cap potentials. Symmetry and finite signed multilinearity are inherited from the frozen BT calculus. These assertions do **not** require a current on the residue model, a tangent current, or a restriction of `S` to a curve.

At this stage the notation records the chosen actual caps and their domains. It must not silently be interpreted as a bilinear form on numerical divisor classes of the residue model. In particular, nonnegative entries of a finite cap matrix do not imply that the matrix is positive semidefinite on arbitrary signed coefficient vectors; positivity applies to the stated positive mixed products.

## 3. What AD/GT actually calculate

AD10--AD12 calculate the complete trace-weighted one-cycle output of the specified pair of caps. The calculation retains all actual divisorial sources, all marks, primitive radial stretching, and the protected old differential factor. GT extends that calibration in its stated model dimension and uses whole cap powers and exact polarization for the numerical rows.

Those results calculate known actual cap products and their pairings with the particular trace tensors and original smooth tests allowed in their statements. They do not take an arbitrary degree-one current `S`, restrict it to each marked residue curve, and identify its values with a numerical divisor class. The latter operation has not been defined or justified by those source comparisons.

In particular, the following are different assertions:

1. A known actual cap one-cycle has the calibrated residue intersection weights.
2. An arbitrary allowed `S` has a curve restriction, model normal-star trace, or actual cap representation compatible with all refinements.
3. The atomic quantities `m_S(L,M)` depend only on the numerical complete-intersection class `L·M`.
4. Those numerical quantities determine `c_S(P)` for every actual quadratic zero in the stated class.

The first is a retained theorem in its precise scope. The second through fourth need additional proofs.

## 4. The circularity in a proposed numerical class of `S`

Suppose one tries to assign a class `alpha_S` on a projective model of the residue field by requiring

\[
m_S(L,M)=\alpha_S\cdot L\cdot M.
\]

Before this is a definition, every relation among the proposed numerical complete-intersection classes must be shown to give the same relation among the actual atomic BT quantities. A relation among algebraic intersection rows or normal cap source weights alone does not provide that implication for an arbitrary `S`.

The missing implication transports a quadratic relation among actual curvature factors through the arbitrary background. It is precisely of the form that the intrinsic action-descent problem asks to prove. Declaring numerical factorization in order to prove that descent would therefore assume the missing compatibility.

Finite-field Picard torsion and the same-field finite unit-lifting theorem solve algebraic lifting and finite divisor-row problems. They do not define this current-to-class map. Likewise, a principal or numerical relation on a selected residue model is not an equality against every original complementary test with arbitrary `S` merely because its scalar numerical rows vanish.

## 5. The exact weak-approximation route already available

The frozen R6 source defines

\[
\mathcal W^1(V)=\overline{\{[\sigma]:\sigma\in B^{1,1}(V),
\ [\sigma]\text{ is symmetric, original-positive and separately closed}\}}^{\rm weak}
\]

using **all** original compact smooth tests on one fixed ordinary domain `V`. Nets are allowed, but the domain does not shrink with the net. Membership in `W^1_loc` means such a fixed domain exists near each point.

R6's fixed-continuous-slot lemma proves weak continuity of the BT insertion of any fixed finite continuous psh list when positive closed backgrounds vary on that same domain. Finite signed continuous DSH expansions and the fixed PS test estimates retain the applicable finite signed expressions. Thus weak approximation of the background is a legitimate mechanism for this fixed residual problem; no new weak-continuity theorem is needed here.

R6's full actual delta-test theorem gives presentation-independent action for backgrounds in `W^1_loc`. In particular, the actual zero relation has zero action for that subclass. The frozen manuscript explicitly does **not** identify `W^1_loc` with the whole allowed positive closed cone.

Consequently a sufficient new geometric result would be one of the following, each proved on an original fixed ordinary neighborhood:

- membership of the arbitrary degree-one `S` in `W^1_loc`;
- a decomposition into a background in that class and an error whose pairing with this fixed residual is proved zero;
- an approximation in a weaker, explicitly specified topology which is nevertheless proved to preserve `c_S(P)`.

A finite approximation on each punctured tube does not provide any of these at the center. Pointwise or numerical convergence on a selected source fan is not the required original-test convergence.

## 6. Known geometric subclasses do not settle the arbitrary background

A cap curvature `Dq_L` already has an actual continuous bounded local potential. A first Chern current of an actual locally continuously metrized line bundle likewise has such a potential on an ordinary local frame domain, when its stated semipositivity hypotheses place that potential in the retained classical class. These backgrounds are therefore already handled by the potential or weak-closure results. Producing more such cap or metric examples does not prove that a given arbitrary `S` is among them.

R6 also includes locally effective Cartier backgrounds, with singularities and original multiplicities, through the original Poincaré--Lelong formula and same-domain logarithmic truncations. It includes the displayed smooth flags, integrable-potential subclasses, and admissible diffuse barycenters. Each retains its own hypotheses. None supplies an arbitrary-current potential theorem.

## 7. What the completed critical-tangent comparison proves and leaves open

The subsequent Round 13 MT theorem defines the real bidimension-two current `T` by the original smooth test functional `T(eta)=S(t^*eta)` on a protected moment domain. Properness uses the entire inverse image of each compact target support. This is an exact transpose of original tests; it is not an arbitrary analytic pushforward of currents and does not discard other points in the central moment fiber.

Middle positivity and both closedness equations pass to `T`. Its trace satisfies the proved critical bound `mu_T(B_r)=O(r^2)`, with finite density `nu=lim mu_T(B_r)/r^2`. The critical rescalings `T_delta=delta^2(a_delta)_*T` have subsequential positive closed conic limits `T_0`. The density need not be zero, and `O(r^2)` is not replaced by `o(r^2)`.

LE constructs the complete homogeneous first-jet energy `Z_0` from genuine ambient scalar PL jets. The all-face coefficient error is `O(r)`, so the critical trace estimate removes the higher-jet contribution with error `O_S(delta)`. PT then identifies the remaining original scalar with every realized critical tangent:

\[
c_S(P)=-\langle T_0,Z_0\wedge D\rho\rangle.
\]

This last passage retains wall mass. Its quantitative compatible-PS approximation uses normal diagonal strip mass `O(epsilon)` and mixed normal variation `O(sqrt(epsilon))`; the mechanism is retained R4/R6 machinery, explicitly checked again for the real tangent interface. The full original Borel coefficient module identifies the transpose pairing on all face values. Smooth weak convergence alone would not have justified this step.

The remaining comparison would have to show that these **realized** paired functionals lie in a proved annihilator of the leading class of every actual zero, for example through a same-field actual cap/model realization or a complete approximation theorem. MT--LE--PT do not put an arbitrary `S`, or its critical transpose tangent, in `W^1_loc`; they do not define a numerical divisor class or prove that the available curve-cap rows span every realized paired functional. Nor is every positive closed real cone on the same geometric fan asserted to arise from an original background.

Thus the first unproved geometric step is an **arbitrary-background realization or paired-completeness theorem**. Pullback of an arbitrary current to a marked residue curve remains unavailable merely from positivity and separate closedness. The residue reduction is not an analytic map through which that arbitrary current may be pushed. Another slope normalization does not resolve this remaining gate.

## 10. The spherical reformulation does not supply the missing cap map

The independently reviewed SR corollary now writes every critical real transpose in terms of a finite positive semidefinite tangent matrix measure `M` on the unit sphere. Its trace has total mass `2 nu`, its spherical divergence is zero, and its radial coefficient measure is `r dr M`. Conversely, every such finite real matrix measure defines a middle-positive separately closed conic real current. This is an exact **real** classification, not a theorem that every member is realized by an original analytic background.

For the actual leading scalar jets, the exact central coefficient is

\[
c_S(P)=\sum_\alpha c_\alpha\left(
 \int h_\alpha k_\alpha\,d\operatorname{tr}M
 -\int d_{\mathbb S}h_\alpha\otimes d_{\mathbb S}k_\alpha:dM
\right).
\]

All mixed tensor entries and charged wall traces are present. The same rescaled-test argument proves `D(Z_0 wedge T_0)=-c_S(P) delta_0`, so every smooth cutoff equal to one near zero reads the same scalar. The matrix moment identity and distributional spherical equilibrium are exact real consequences of both closedness equations.

The unresolved condition concerns the **jointly admissible pair** consisting of an actual-zero leading tensor and the angular measure coming from the same original geometry/background under all actual refinements. Neither the real classification nor a numerical list of cap weights proves its mass-minus-Dirichlet expression is zero. The existing real plane/quadrant example has an equilibrated angular measure and a nonzero expression; its coefficient tensor is not an actual analytic zero, so it is not a counterexample to the desired descent.
