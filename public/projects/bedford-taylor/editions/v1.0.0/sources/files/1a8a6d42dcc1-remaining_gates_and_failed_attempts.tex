\section{Remaining minimal gates and failed inferences}
\label{sec:r24-remaining-gates}

\paragraph{Status.}
This register is a scope audit, not an additional theorem.
All original field, norm, topology, carrier, test, refinement and
positivity conventions are those in Section~\ref{sec:r24-main-scope}.
The positive implications cited below are proved in their exact
source modules.  No listed open statement is used as an input to
an unconditional conclusion.

\subsection{The unchanged principal gate}

\paragraph{KC12: \texttt{OPEN}.}
For every actual signed PL-zero on the same original ordinary
germ and its correct absolute cap carrier, prove that the exact
KC5 carrier-CLN seminorm is zero on every required compact control
stage, in every relevant degree.  Equivalently, by the frozen
full-polar duality, prove annihilation by every genuine full
original carrier polar.  A zero on the absolute integration
background is not itself such a certificate.  Neither a finite
chosen family of tests nor a representation by an arbitrary real
current replaces this universal assertion.

The R17 real primitive theorem remains valid with its radial-hull
support.  This round does not prove that an actual analytic zero
has zero real double derivative on that hull.  A primitive lift
on the original analytic carrier is not obtained merely by
retaining a support in a real target.  The same caution applies
to convolution: no zero-preserving analytic convolution has
been constructed.

\subsection{Harmonic localization and finite-cover return}

\paragraph{NC12/HE7/IL20: \texttt{OPEN}.}
For each unchanged original \(U,F,L\), each genuine full
degree-\(b\) polar, and every fixed signed global tail tuple,
prove local harmonic annihilation of its intrinsic first-slot
measure.  By HL and HC, it is enough to establish the exact
local-unit drift assertion.  In the protected affinoid setting
its one-function form is
\[
 \nu_{\ell_{a,r},v_3,\ldots,v_b}\big|_{\{|a|>r\}}=0,
 \qquad \ell_{a,r}=\log\max\{r,|a|\},                 \tag{RG24.1}
\]
for every original analytic \(a\), every \(r>0\), every
full polar, and all tails.  The measure is already defined on
\(U\); it is its Borel restriction that occurs in (RG24.1).
The assertion does not assume a full polar on the smaller
ordinary open.  HL settles the class of continuous harmonic
witnesses, but it supplies no missing global harmonic extension.

\paragraph{ST19 and LG4 in mixed degree \(b\ge2\): \texttt{OPEN}.}
LC proves an exact conditional arbitrary-companion transfer
for one global upstairs unit logarithm.  Even under its explicit
locality assumption, the further transfer of an arbitrary
whole-carrier harmonic trace and of every original local ambient
generator is not proved.  Thus an arbitrary upstairs KC5
decomposition still has no general reverse certificate estimate
through a finite etale cover.  The degree-one full-domain return
is already settled in the frozen FR/LG chain and is not reopened.

One may not apply the assumed harmonic-locality theorem to
\(\Lambda\circ\mathcal T\) before showing that this transported
functional kills the full upstairs relation ideal.  That would
use the required harmonic and ambient descent in order to prove
it.  LC avoids this circularity by working directly with the
existing downstairs full polar and its already constructed
Radon measures.

\paragraph{LG7: \texttt{OPEN}.}
The R23 finite-tuple geometry and smooth actual-delta faithfulness
give an original Abhyankar model for the chosen analytic relation.
The actual-zero relation on that model still has to yield its
full carrier certificate.  Returning the full seminorm certificate
through the finite cover is a second gate if a downstairs
\emph{seminorm} conclusion is required.  For all-original-background
action, the frozen LG reduction already uses complete finite-etale
base change and EC directly from the model certificate and does
not require that reverse seminorm theorem.  Finite preparation
of a unit tuple, numerical positivity, and a smooth pullback
identity do not establish either gate.

\subsection{The coupled cap and the two different scalar obstructions}

\paragraph{CM10: \texttt{OPEN}.}
For the specified actual normal cap, numerical harmonicity of
the PL trace on its intrinsic Gauss fiber gives the known actual
coupled zero and arbitrary-background action zero.  It has not
been shown to give the unchanged full-polar certificate after
cap multiplication.  HC24.2a now identifies an arbitrary original
continuous harmonic trace witness exactly with Picard zero.
The completed residue field of the cap may have a function-field
residue, so the finite residue field of the original \(K\)
cannot be substituted for it.

CZ24 completes the ordinary zero-product family for this
interface and retains a quantitative same-carrier estimate.
Its bound still uses positive-tail costs on the larger
\(G\cap L\).  It does not by itself compare the full carrier
seminorms.  It also does not cancel the exact cutoff commutators
in HC.  A literal local witness at every point of a compact cap
stage and a single global \(G\)-harmonic factor are different
statements.

\paragraph{RV4 and the scalar-boundary action target: \texttt{OPEN}.}
For the quadratic PS energy form \(Z\), one still needs the
balanced covector lift RV4, or an alternative direct action
annihilation.  The raw coordinate coefficient of a covector
times a cap is not intrinsic, because the frozen normal-factor
example has a zero covector product with a nonzero scalar cap.
After RV4 the separately closed PS remainder has the exact
formula
\[
                 D_\delta q=A-\gamma .                    \tag{RG24.2}
\]
The scalar boundary \(\gamma\) is retained.
RV15 asks for \(\gamma=0\) as an actual germ.  AP24 supplies
a weaker sufficient target: one finite continuous ordinary
potential \(v\), on one representative chosen before \(S\)
and the tests, with
\[
                            [\gamma]=D[v].                 \tag{RG24.3}
\]
The local DC property then follows from the exact degree-one
order argument.  Existence in (RG24.3) is not proved.

An alternative sufficient interface is transfer of the particular
covector relation AP24.9 to the ordinary arbitrary-background
identity AP24.10.  This is beyond the scalar continuous-coefficient
R6 theorem.  The compactness theorem for already represented
curvature intervals does not supply a potential for every closed
current dominated by a represented curvature.  A convergent
represented net on the same domain would have to be constructed
first.

\subsection{Strictly rejected shortcuts}

\paragraph{A common-domain harmonic comparator: \texttt{FALSE}.}
BC constructs one fixed quadratic finite etale cover and unit
such that, for every initial ordinary neighborhood, a later
legal upstairs DC weight produces a local strict-defect unit
logarithm with no harmonic extension to that whole neighborhood.
The fixed-weight preparation remains possible; the refuted
claim puts the neighborhood before every later weight.
Neither the polynomial involved nor any of its polar values is
shown to be a KC counterexample.

\paragraph{Finitely many local coefficient kernels: \texttt{FALSE}.}
IK fixes just two original positive cap cycles.  On every Gauss
neighborhood their intrinsic restriction kernels include the
distinct lines \(a+Nb=0\), for every integer \(N\ge1\).
A finite number of fixed additional moment coordinates does
not change these intrinsic local equalities.  This refutes the
literal finite-constant-type approach to coefficient gluing.
It does not refute a finite generated object with genuinely
variable relations and an appropriate specialization operation.
Such an object and operation have not been constructed here.
It also does not refute the elementary finite-subfamily detector
for a fixed finite-dimensional global vector space.

\paragraph{One scalar for all intersecting branches: \texttt{FALSE}.}
AP24.13--AP24.14 exhibit two intersecting original cap currents
whose distinct constant coefficients cannot be represented by
one continuous coefficient multiplying their sum.  This simple
diagnostic already has an explicit continuous PL potential and
does not have RV15's centered homogeneity.  It is not a new
counterexample family for the principal problem.

\paragraph{Fixed-outer harmonic witnesses on shifted caps.}
FM24--FQ24 give an exact obstruction to this particular construction.
For a fixed finite PL trace and one fixed outer ordinary domain,
one sufficiently near negative-level complete-fiber
witness already gives a harmonic witness for the original trace
on a fixed smaller germ.  In particular, a trace with a nonzero
residue Picard class cannot acquire a sequence of such witnesses,
or uniformly approximate witnesses on the protected complete cap
stages, even if their norms are unbounded.  The factor-two estimate
is first proved on irrational levels; compact-image upper
semicontinuity extends it to all nearby levels without Gauss
saturation.  The sufficient same-domain
cap approximation estimate has no witness-norm cost: its remainder
is \(\varepsilon J_h\prod J_i\).  The failure is therefore
geometric extension of the witnesses, not control of their norms.

This is an obstruction only to that stronger witness construction.
It neither supplies nor refutes a genuinely coupled certificate,
and the positive scalar approximation gap is not a positive lower
bound for a full carrier-CLN seminorm.  Witnesses on domains
depending on the level, and agreement only on a general closed
carrier slice, are not covered by the proof.  No norm-level/full-
Gauss-fiber identification at rational levels is asserted.

\paragraph{Affine coefficients without the full closedness premise.}
HB24 computes \(\gamma=-hD_\delta h\) for
\(h=\max(0,s_1,s_2)\), with \(D\gamma=-\delta_x\).
It therefore cannot be a finite sum of original smooth harmonic
coefficients times separately closed scalar forms.  The source
\(A=d'h\wedge d''h\) is not separately closed, so this does
not refute the RV hypotheses.  A supplied representation
\(\gamma=\sum\ell_iD_\delta v_i\) with harmonic \(\ell_i\)
would suffice for the ordinary arbitrary-background action gate;
neither its construction under the genuine RV hypotheses nor
the balancing of its separately extracted scalar faces is proved.

\paragraph{Higher nilpotence as quadratic division:
\texttt{FAILED\_ATTEMPT}.}
CA24 retains the exact hypothetical polynomial identities and
shows that, for dimension at least two, their degree-three-or-higher
relations do not force the desired quadratic product to vanish.
The explicitly stated abstract implication is false.  The graded
algebra used to show this is not an actual polar, analytic relation,
or allowed background.  The geometric coupled cap remains open.
Positive-point certificates are used only for their specified
positive presentations and their initial germ preparations;
an unbounded family does not thereby receive one common domain.

\subsection{Original-category pressure checks}

No positive conclusion changes the original field or norm.
Finite residue-field extensions used to describe an original
analytic seminorm are not new fields for arbitrary-background
current theory.  Ordinary domains are chosen before the
subsequent objects quantified in each theorem; BC and IK spell
out failures of stronger uniform choices.

Every cap carrier is the complete support supplied by the
frozen cap theorem.  A real norm level, a chosen Gauss section,
and a real radial hull are not substituted for it.  All lower
dimensional and singular boundary terms remain in HC and AP.
The BC example is a scalar continuation obstruction; IK is an
intrinsic restriction-kernel obstruction; CA is only a formal
failed-inference witness.  None supplies the entire actual-zero,
same-germ, full-polar, arbitrary-background conjunction needed
to label KC12 false.

For a fixed-stage conclusion, KC6 is applied to the tests whose
required control stage is that stage.  An all-current conclusion
uses the premise at every \(L_\eta\).  The distinction is
explicit in CZ24 and remains in the result register.  No smooth
test equality has been promoted to unrestricted GK delta-test
equality, and no top-degree point-cycle result is counted again
as an all-degree theorem.

\paragraph{Next proof obligation.}
A useful continuation should attack one of the concrete interfaces
above: prove original local-unit drift annihilation for a genuine
full polar; construct the missing continuous scalar boundary
potential or covector transfer; or prove a coupled Picard/cap
certificate with an unchanged ordinary representative and
quantitative remainder.  Merely naming an analytic relation
complex, invoking local contractibility, or choosing a finite
test family does not address these obligations.
