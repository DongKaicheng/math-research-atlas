\section{Constructed ordinary quadratic descent in finite residue rank at most two}
\label{sec:r11-ordinary-residue-two}

\paragraph{Purpose and promotion rule.}
The normal-surface construction is useful for the intrinsic problem
only after it returns to an arbitrary original ordinary germ. This
section supplies that return and the full controlled approximants.
Its status is fixed by the source-bound composition review recorded
in the result register. In particular the NS assembly is a necessary
proved dependency, not an unrecorded conjecture in the statement.
The result is quadratic and belongs to the fixed finite closed-cell
PS class. It leaves higher coupled relations and residue rank at
least three open.

Let \(X\) be good, Hausdorff, boundaryless and quasi-smooth over
the original finite \(K/\mathbf Q_p\) with its original absolute
value. Fix \(x\in X\), and work on an ordinary representative
of pure dimension \(n\). Fix a finite original unit tuple
\(f=(f_1,\ldots,f_N)\). Its graded moment field and ranks are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathscr F_f(x)&=\widetilde K^{\mathrm{gr}}
          (\widetilde f_1(x),\ldots,\widetilde f_N(x)),\\
 d_f(x)&=\operatorname{trdeg}^{\mathrm{gr}}_{\widetilde K^{\mathrm{gr}}}
                         \mathscr F_f(x),\\
 r_f(x)&=\dim_{\mathbf Q}\operatorname{Span}_{\mathbf Q}
  \{-\log|f_j(x)|+\mathbf Q\log|K^\times|\}_{j=1}^N,\\
 s_f(x)&=d_f(x)-r_f(x).
\end{aligned}
\tag{OR1}
\]
The retained FM theorem proves that \(s_f\) is the transcendence
degree of the finitely generated field of normalized old residues.
In particular \(s_f(x)\le s_K(x)\). No finite generation of the
full residue field of \(\mathcal H(x)\) is required.

The scalar family below consists of finitely many continuous PL or
continuous PS functions through this fixed tuple, with smooth
extensions near the closed cells of one finite coefficient complex.
Their complete original moment image, including all lower strata,
is part of the presentation. SE supplies ambient scalar extensions
when the functions are initially given only on that image.

\begin{theorem}[Ordinary quadratic descent in finite residue rank two]
\label{thm:r11-ordinary-quadratic-residue-two}
Suppose the fixed finite scalar presentation
\[
                    P=\sum_\nu c_\nu[h_\nu][k_\nu]
\tag{OR2}
\]
is an actual signed quadratic analytic zero, meaning
\[
             \kappa_2(P)=\sum_\nu c_\nu
                 D_\delta h_\nu\wedge D_\delta k_\nu=0,
                    \qquad s_f(x)\le2.
\tag{OR3}
\]
For every fixed \(1<\kappa<2\), there are an original ordinary
neighborhood \(U_x\), a finite actual original-unit refinement,
and a continuous finite-PS ambient covector on its complete moment
image whose strain is the energy
\[
 Z_P=\tfrac12\sum_\nu c_\nu
       (d'_Ph_\nu\wedge d''_Pk_\nu+
                         d'_Pk_\nu\wedge d''_Ph_\nu).
\tag{OR4}
\]
In particular \(Z_P=d'\beta_Z+d''\zeta_Z\) in the full actual
degree-one module on \(U_x\).

Put
\[
 u=\sum_\nu c_\nu h_\nu k_\nu,\qquad
 A_0=\tfrac12D_\delta u,\qquad \alpha_P=A_0-Z_P.
\tag{OR5}
\]
Then
\[
 \alpha_P=d'\bigl(\tfrac12d''u-\beta_Z\bigr)+d''(-\zeta_Z).
\tag{OR6}
\]
There are one fixed positive represented control \(Q=D[q]\),
actual symmetric full-exact approximants
\(a_j=d'B_j+d''C_j\), and \(\epsilon_j\downarrow0\),
chosen before every subsequent background, test and refinement,
with
\[
\begin{aligned}
 -\epsilon_jQ&\le[\alpha_P]-[a_j]\le\epsilon_jQ,\\
 -\epsilon_jD(qS)&\le M_S(\alpha_P)-M_S(a_j)
                                      \le\epsilon_jD(qS).
\end{aligned}
\tag{OR7}
\]
Here \(S\) ranges over all original symmetric middle-positive
backgrounds separately closed under \(d'\) and \(d''\), in all
legal degrees. The inequalities and identities use all original
compact smooth complementary CLD tests and every actual refinement,
including rank degeneration. Explicit choices are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 a_j&=A_0-e_{\delta_j},&
 \epsilon_j&=C\delta_j^{(2-\kappa)/2},\\
 B_j&=\tfrac12d''u-\beta_{\delta_j},&
 C_j&=-\zeta_{\delta_j},
\end{aligned}
\tag{OR8}
\]
where \(e_\delta=d'\beta_\delta+d''\zeta_\delta\) is the
constructed smooth regular-energy approximation and
\(\delta_j\downarrow0\) is sufficiently small.

For every such original \(S\) of bidegree \((p,p)\),
\(0\le p\le n-2\), the action satisfies
\[
                         \mathcal A_S(P)|_{U_x}=0.
\tag{OR9}
\]
The relation presentation, \(\kappa\), and the chosen ordinary
truncation may affect the construction. Later backgrounds, tests
and refinements do not affect its control or require the inequality
\(s_f\le2\) again.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\emph{Step 1: a regular equation on the exact finite-rank target.}
The retained QA/QP transgression gives
\[
                        D_\delta Z_P=-\kappa_2(P)=0,
                         \qquad D[Z_P]=0.
\tag{OR10}
\]
Apply SE and FM to precisely the fixed finite presentation. They
construct an actual smooth map \(F:U\to Y\), a point \(y=F(x)\),
and a target tuple \(\tau\) such that
\[
 \dim Y=d_f(x),\qquad r_K(y)=r_f(x),\qquad s_K(y)=s_f(x),
                    \qquad t_f=E(t_\tau\circ F)
\tag{OR11}
\]
on every point of an original ordinary source neighborhood.
The map \(E\) is a fixed rational affine map. Composing the
finite scalar extensions with \(E\) gives a regular finite-PS
energy \(Z_Y\) with
\(Z_P=F^*_{\mathrm{coeff}}Z_Y\) on every source face.

The retained SD compact-test operator applies to the actual smooth
map and this regular tensor. It gives \(D[Z_Y]=0\) on an ordinary
target neighborhood. This is the original-test equation, obtained
using compact vertical forms and actual finite etale packets.
No arbitrary current or arbitrary original background is pushed
forward. The relation \(P\) itself need not descend as a target
delta-algebra relation; the regular equation is the required input.

\emph{Step 2: the target has an actual complete-image primitive.}
If \(s_f\le1\), the retained UQ construction already supplies the
primitive, including its zero- and one-dimensional cases. Suppose
\(s_f=2\). The explicit FM construction is
\[
                 Y=\mathbf G_{m,K}^{r_f,\mathrm{an}}
                              \times Y_0^{\mathrm{an}},
\tag{OR12}
\]
where \(Y_0\) is the smooth integral hypersurface lift of the
finite separable residue extension used by FM. After the displayed
finite localizations, it is the generic fiber of a smooth integral
\(K^\circ\)-surface with integral special fiber. Its generic
special-fiber point has residue field exactly the chosen normalized
residue field \(L\), and value group exactly \(|K^\times|\).
The point \(y\) is its Gauss extension at the \(K\)-free values
of the retained old value-basis tuple. These statements are checked
in the separate FM-to-NS landing interface; they are stronger than
merely comparing the two numerical invariants in (OR11).

Adjoin finitely many algebraic target units to make the residue
surface a closed torus subvariety with function field \(L\), and
retain all old scalar coordinates by SA. The new norms agree on
one ordinary target neighborhood, with their exact initial forms
and saturated integral graph. This is an allowed target refinement;
its units pull back to actual original source units. No additional
factorization of arbitrary later source units through \(F\) is
claimed or needed.

The hypotheses of the NS assembly theorem
\ref{thm:r11-ns-finite-ps-strain} now hold. Its conewise primitives
are constructed by CE/SP, its central trace is removed by the
original SS cap calculation and CU, and its sourcewise residual
flux is treated by RCF on the whole actual cycle. Actual normalized
NCB curve blocks, simultaneous Picard units, two complete-source
separations, and all-radius TP/AX invisibility give the full joint
incident-span values. NL then constructs an ambient covector
\(\Lambda_Y\) on the complete image. Thus the premise required by
PR has been proved, including every lower face. It is not supplied
by a current kernel, an unverified normal-star identification, or
an assumption that the old-coordinate obstruction matrix vanishes.

\emph{Step 3: return the finite coefficients to the original source.}
Write the final target tuple as \(H=(H_1,\ldots,H_m)\) and
\(\Lambda_Y=\sum_i b_i(z)\,dz_i\). Put \(t=t_{H\circ F}\),
and define on an ordinary source neighborhood
\[
 \beta_Z=\tfrac12\sum_i b_i(t)d''t_i,
                 \qquad \zeta_Z=-\tfrac12\sum_i b_i(t)d't_i.
\tag{OR13}
\]
The actual source enlargement retains both \(f\) and \(H\circ F\).
Every source moment face maps linearly into a target coefficient
face. The target strain equality restricts along this linear map,
including when its rank drops. The anchored degree-one Leibniz
formula therefore proves the identity in (OR4). This is the exact
PR coefficient return. It involves no pullback of current order.
The full scalar Leibniz formula then gives (OR5)--(OR6).

\emph{Step 4: construct the required control on the source itself.}
Shrink once so that the source moment-image closure lies in a
bounded region inside the ambient domain of \(\Lambda_Y\).
Convolve the fixed continuous finite-PS coefficient functions there.
Substitute the resulting smooth covectors into (OR13), obtaining
\(e_\delta=d'\beta_\delta+d''\zeta_\delta\).
The retained source-side SB/PSO estimate uses the finitely many
coefficient facet equations \(\ell_H\), their fixed potentials
\(|\ell_H|^\kappa\), and a fixed strictly convex quadratic term.
It constructs one continuous psh \(q\) and gives
\[
        \pm([Z_P]-[e_\delta])
                  \le C\delta^{(2-\kappa)/2}D[q],
\tag{OR14}
\]
together with the original all-background module estimate.
All finite closed-cell bounds and all-face identities needed for
that estimate were supplied by the explicit target covector and
its coefficient return. Neither existence of an approximating
sequence nor existence of its fixed positive control is assumed.

Now use (OR8). The exact cancellation
\[
                         \alpha_P-a_j=e_{\delta_j}-Z_P
\tag{OR15}
\]
proves (OR7). Its approximants are full first-order exact, while
retaining the entire possibly singular \(A_0\). The two distinct
requirements of OC7 have therefore been constructed. Alternatively,
apply the retained all-background degree-one action to (OR6);
separate closedness, nilpotence and anticommutation give the action
identity (OR9) directly.

Finally, any later original compact test has a finite actual common
refinement with the constructed source tuple. Each new face maps
into a face of the complete certificate, and the finite-dimensional
semipositive inequalities restrict through its tangent map. The
fixed scalar-module control then gives every allowed original
background. This argument uses the same \(q\), neighborhood and
finite original construction. No bound on the ranks of the enlarged
test or refinement tuple is imposed.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[An intrinsic ordinary locus]
\label{cor:r11-intrinsic-residue-two-locus}
At every original point with \(s_K(x)\le2\), every fixed finite
quadratic actual-zero presentation in the stated continuous PL/PS
class satisfies the theorem. The rational rank and the Abhyankar
defect are unrestricted. In particular the conclusion concerns
ordinary non-saturated analytic germs of rational rank at least
two whenever their fixed relation has \(s_f\le2\).
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
FM proves \(s_f(x)\le s_K(x)\). The theorem is local at the
original point and its construction depends on the finite relation,
not on an ambient saturation assumption. Apply it to each fixed
presentation. This is a pointwise germ conclusion, not a passage
from a dense set to a current identity.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact limits.}
The complete carrier of a prescribed KC12 seminorm has not been
replaced, and this theorem does not assert that stronger full
certificate. One fixed common bounded BT curvature tail may be
absorbed in \(S\) in its legal degrees; a common finite signed PL
tail uses its retained finite positive expansion. Different termwise
tails and arbitrary higher coupled relations are not covered.
The original notions of middle, strong and weak positivity remain
distinct. General residue rank at least three, variable-PS/CTO,
ordinary-to-GK tests, and the whole intrinsic gluing and naturality
interface remain separate tasks. Only \(e_\delta\) is asserted
smooth; the full approximants \(a_j\) retain \(A_0\), consistently
with the R24 VE obstruction.
