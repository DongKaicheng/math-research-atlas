Exact stopping point and failed inference.

The two singletons are individually raw/outer regular, while their union is not. Thus singleton regularity cannot be used to prove finite-compact regularity by an additive argument: the raw capacity is a supremum over one common normalized tester, not a measure. The exact value of CS∗(K;V)C_S^*(K;V)CS∗​(K;V) inside the proved interval [3κ,4κ][3\kappa,4\kappa][3κ,4κ] remains OPEN. \section{Individually regular points and compact discontinuity in the crossed box}
\label{sec:r16-crossed-singleton-outer-bounds}

\paragraph{Scope, role and status.}
This is a \texttt{DIRECT\_COROLLARY} refinement of the single
crossed-box family CB, with zero additional principal-family count.
It proves two exact singleton outer capacities, a finite upper bound
on the already separated two-point outer capacity, and an explicit
failure of decreasing-compact continuity for the raw capacity.
The exact two-point outer value is not determined.

Fix the same original finite extension $K/\mathbf Q_p$, its unchanged
absolute value, a uniformizer $\pi$, $r=|\pi|$, $\lambda=-\log r>0$,
an integer $q\ge2$, and $\kappa=(2\lambda)^{-q}$. Work on the original
affine strict Laurent box
\[
 V=\{|X_i|<r^{-1},\ |Y_j|<r^{-1},\ |X_iY_j|<r^{-1}
                                      \quad(1\le i,j\le q)\}
 \tag{CO1}
\]
with exactly the extending background
\[
 S=2S_1+S_2,\qquad
 S_1=\bigwedge_{j=1}^qD\max(\log|Y_j|,-2\lambda)\wedge[X],
 \qquad
 S_2=\bigwedge_{i=1}^qD\max(\log|X_i|,0)\wedge[X].
 \tag{CO2}
\]
Here $D=d'd''$ and $X=(\mathbf A_K^{2q})^{\rm an}$.
All original compact tests, every ordinary germ and every actual
refinement are retained. The full carriers $F_1,F_2$ are the entire
fibers over the normal joint Gauss points of $Y$ radii $r^2$ and
$X$ radii one, respectively. They are not replaced by norm levels.
The original closed box is compact in the interior of the radius
$r^{-3}$ affinoid, and both backgrounds extend across it.

Keep the original points of CB:
$A$ has $X$ radii all $r$ and $Y$ radii all $r^2$;
$B$ has $X$ radii all one and $Y$ radii $(1,r,\ldots,r)$.
The distinct moving residue points $B_j$ of CB satisfy $B_j\to B$.
Write
\[
       K=\{A,B\},\qquad K_m=\{A,B,B_j:\ j\ge m\}.
 \tag{CO3}
\]
All capacities below use \emph{every} actual original classical
psh tester $a:V\to[0,1]$, including discontinuous upper-semicontinuous
ones, normalized on this entire $V$.

\subsection*{A sharp mass bound on each full cap}

For $i=1$ use free coordinates $Z=(X_1,\ldots,X_q)$ on $F_1$;
for $i=2$ use $Z=(Y_1,\ldots,Y_q)$ on $F_2$.
The coordinate inequalities on either entire fiber are precisely
the free radius-$r^{-1}$ polydisc inequalities. For numbers
$D_1,\ldots,D_q>0$ put
\[
 E_{i,\mathbf D}
   =F_i\cap\{\,|Z_l|\le\exp(\lambda-D_l)\quad(1\le l\le q)\,\},
 \qquad
 b_{\mathbf D}
   =\max\left\{-1,\,
           \frac{\log|Z_l|-\lambda}{D_l}\quad(1\le l\le q)\right\}.
 \tag{CO4}
\]
The latter is a global continuous actual psh function on $V$,
with $-1\le b_{\mathbf D}<0$, and extends across the closed box.
The finite floor removes coordinate poles.

\begin{lemma}[Full-tester inner-polydisc mass bound]
For every original normalized tester $a$ and either $i$,
\[
       \mu_{S_i,a}(E_{i,\mathbf D})
                            \le\prod_{l=1}^qD_l^{-1}.
 \tag{CO5}
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The full-cap toric calibration gives the exact original measure
\[
  \mu_{S_i,b_{\mathbf D}}
       =\left(\prod_lD_l^{-1}\right)\delta_{\zeta_{i,\mathbf D}},
 \tag{CO6}
\]
where the free joint Gauss radii are $\exp(\lambda-D_l)$ and
the normal radii are those of $F_i$. The product of the free slopes
is exactly the $q!$-normalized simplex volume; all normal
multiplicities are one. This calibrates only the displayed actual
toric function, not an arbitrary Gauss pushforward.

Fix $\delta>0$ and $c>1+\delta$, and put
\[
               v=\max\{a-1-\delta,\ c b_{\mathbf D}\}.
 \tag{CO7}
\]
It is a bounded actual classical psh function on the same original
$V$. On $E_{i,\mathbf D}$ one has $b_{\mathbf D}=-1$ and
$a-1-\delta\ge-1-\delta>-c$. The frozen strict Borel maximum
locality theorem therefore gives
\[
             \mathbf1_{E_{i,\mathbf D}}\mu_{S_i,v}
                   =\mathbf1_{E_{i,\mathbf D}}\mu_{S_i,a}.
 \tag{CO8}
\]
This uses no continuity of $a$ and no assertion that its strict
comparison superlevel is ordinary open.

We verify the total mass of $v$ directly. Every mixed tail with
background $S_i$ remains carried by $F_i$. Since $a-1-\delta\le-\delta$,
the coefficient $v-cb_{\mathbf D}$ is zero wherever
$b_{\mathbf D}>-\delta/c$. Its product with any such mixed tail
is thus supported in
\[
                  F_i\cap\{b_{\mathbf D}\le-\delta/c\}\Subset V.
 \tag{CO9}
\]
Indeed every free coordinate is then bounded by
$\exp(\lambda-D_l\delta/c)<r^{-1}$, while all normal radii
are fixed. This is a closed subset of a compact original
polydisc. On $F_1$ the normal $Y$ radii are $r^2<1$;
on $F_2$ the normal $X$ radii are one. In both cases the
individual and product inequalities have a strict uniform
margin, proving the asserted compact containment.

The original bounded mixed BT telescope gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mu_{S_i,v}-c^q\mu_{S_i,b_{\mathbf D}}
  =D\sum_{k=0}^{q-1}
       (v-cb_{\mathbf D})(Dv)^k
                (cDb_{\mathbf D})^{q-1-k}\wedge S_i.
 \end{split}
 \tag{CO10}
\]
Every coefficient current on the right is compactly supported
by (CO9). Testing by an original smooth scalar equal to one on
a neighborhood of those supports and the point in (CO6) gives
zero for the total derivative. Outside that compact the two
positive top measures agree, and the second is supported at
the displayed point. Consequently both total masses are finite and
\[
               \mu_{S_i,v}(V)=c^q\prod_lD_l^{-1}.
 \tag{CO11}
\]
Equations (CO8)--(CO11) imply the bound with the factor $c^q$.
Let $\delta\downarrow0$ and $c\downarrow1$ with $c>1+\delta$.
This proves (CO5).

Only local bounded original BT, strict Borel locality, compact
current differentiation and the exact full-cap calibration are
used. In particular (CO7) is not silently declared to satisfy
a different global energy boundary condition; no completed
boundary assertion or new-field BT theorem is needed.
\end{proof}

The union of the two neighborhoods in (CO14) contains $K$.
For each tester its positive measure on this union is at most
the sum of the two masses, so taking the supremum and using
(CO15) gives $C_S^*(K;V)\le4\kappa$. The exact raw value
and strict outer lower bound in (CO13) are the already proved
CB theorem. No interchange of an infimum and a supremum is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Raw capacity fails decreasing-compact continuity]
The compact sets $K_m$ in (CO3) decrease to $K$, are all contained
in the one compact $K_1\Subset V$, and satisfy
\[
                  C_S(K_m;V)\ge3\kappa
                       >C_S(K;V)\qquad(m\ge1).
 \tag{CO18}
\]
Hence the raw capacity is not continuous from above even along
this explicit compact sequence.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Compactness and the intersection follow from the original
convergence of the distinct $B_j$ to $B$. For any $j\ge m$,
the actual normalized CB tester has source
$\kappa(2\delta_A+\delta_{B_j})$, wholly supported on $K_m$.
Its mass there is $3\kappa$. Taking the supremum proves (CO18).
The common-core CY row may equally be used, with the identical
original source. The strict inequality is CB's positive gap.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact stopping point and failed inference.}
The two singletons are individually raw/outer regular, while their
union is not. Thus singleton regularity cannot be used to prove
finite-compact regularity by an additive argument: the raw capacity
is a supremum over one common normalized tester, not a measure.
The exact value of $C_S^*(K;V)$ inside the proved interval
$[3\kappa,4\kappa]$ remains \texttt{OPEN}.
In particular no atomic bound from CB has been applied to a
whole shrinking ordinary neighborhood. Its moving residue sources
prevent that replacement.
