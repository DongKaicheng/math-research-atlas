Research source and reading edition

This website is based on the complete internal export of 16 November 2026. Its 6,019 payload files were recovered and checked against the original export manifest. The unsigned reading edition is dated 18 November 2026: personal bylines and metadata are omitted, and existing mathematical question marks are placed above the relation in question.

The mathematical statements, proofs and source status are preserved. Revised PDFs are compiled from the corresponding downloadable reading-edition TeX. Each proof-source record identifies the served bytes and the frozen original separately; the original files remain in the private project archive. Older source-download URLs supply the current unsigned reading edition.

The underlying records preserve 74 thematic result groups, 66 formal statements in the core article and 14 formal open statements in the programme. Progress groups these results into six reading sections, while the full catalogue retains every original record. Definitions link to the corresponding numbered definition, equation or defining paragraph in an article.

Mathematical status and review

“Established in source” records that the frozen internal manuscript supplies an argument under its stated hypotheses and foundational inputs. Model cases retain their model restrictions. Counterexamples retain the exact assertion they refute. Open objectives keep their current formal formulation.

The maintainer records source identities, exact statements, contexts and references separately from proof acceptance. The present import and publication checks establish source fidelity and navigation; they do not create an independent mathematical acceptance judgment.

Two distinctions are central to the project: the order completion of currents is different from the relative strong first-energy completion, and the remaining-degree-one renormalized equation uses its faithful part-energy space. Likewise, a punctured normal-three action theorem and its central pairing formula leave the general central cancellation as an open conjecture.
