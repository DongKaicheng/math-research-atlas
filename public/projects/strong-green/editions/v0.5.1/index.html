A research programme in arithmetic geometry

Beilinson–Bloch Heights and the Strong Zero-Curvature Green Conjecture

For degree-zero divisors on a curve, height pairings connect intersection theory with the arithmetic of its Jacobian. The goal here is a canonical Beilinson–Bloch height pairing for homologically trivial cycles of complementary codimension on smooth projective varieties over number fields.

The local conjecture

Over a finite extension of the p-adic numbers with its fixed absolute value, the strong Green conjecture asks for an actual non-Archimedean Green form for every prescribed homologically trivial cycle. The form must be defined on the exact complement of that cycle and satisfy

From local forms to global heights

Turning local forms into a canonical global pairing requires reciprocity, a finite global sum, and independence of representatives and Green choices. Agreement with classical heights, positivity, and arithmetic finiteness are further steps. The programme identifies the main obstacle in each.
