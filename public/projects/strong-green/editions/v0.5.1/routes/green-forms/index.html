Research route

Direct construction of zero-curvature Green forms

This approach begins with a zero-curvature Green form itself, without requiring it to come from a particular Chern-character construction. The main additional task is to identify its integrals against principal cycles with the field norms describing rational equivalence.

What is established

The principal-divisor calculation and the product formula give a conditional passage from local Green forms to a global Chow pairing.

Tropical forms and global primitives

One possible method is to solve for primitives on tropical charts and descend them to the Berkovich space. The comparison must preserve both differentials, integration and compatibility on overlaps. A topological inverse limit alone does not give this statement for forms. A global actual primitive, together with a prescribed curved Green pair, would supply a strong Green form.

The remaining problems

The local existence problem retains the precise cycle complement, actual form sheaf and full test class. The principal-divisor argument also needs restriction along singular carriers and compatible projective-bundle integration. Correspondence adjunction and comparison with arithmetic-intersection heights require further identities with the same normalizations.
