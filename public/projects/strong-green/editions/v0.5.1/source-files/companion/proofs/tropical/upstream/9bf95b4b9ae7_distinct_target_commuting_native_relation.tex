\section{A native commuting relation for two distinct target rows}
\label{sec:r11-distinct-target-commuting}

\paragraph{Status, authorship and scope.}
\textbf{PROVED THIS ROUND}, subject to exact-source independent review.
The construction contributor is \texttt{/root/r11\_challenge}.
The inputs are the exact frozen SHT native shear, the complete CS edge
formula, and full-loop MT.  The argument below does not use the new
Steinberg theorem or the new arbitrary-passive-block lemma.  It concerns
two distinct target rows; it is not a reconstruction or relabeling of
the historically reported, missing-final-source same-target CG/RA
modules.  The latter retain their separate recovery status.

Let $N$ be an ordinary analytic open over the original $K$, and let
$\overline L$ carry the same actual metric as in the frozen CI/SHT
calculus.  Let $f=(f_1,\ldots,f_r)$ be analytic sections of $L$ on $N$,
where $r\ge3$, and put
\[
 W=N\setminus |f=0|,\qquad s=r-1,\qquad R=c_1(\overline L).
 \tag{DTC1}
\]
There is no regular-sequence hypothesis in the local assertion.  Fix
indices and arbitrary analytic coefficient functions $b,c$ on $N$ with
\[
 i\ne j,\quad k\ne l,\quad i\ne k,\quad j\ne k,\quad l\ne i,
 \qquad B=E_{ij}(b),\quad C=E_{kl}(c).
 \tag{DTC2}
\]
Thus $B$ and $C$ commute, their targets are distinct, and both source
rows belong to the common passive block.  The source rows may coincide.
The coefficients may vanish and may have unbounded norm on $N$.
Matrices act on column tuples with the rightmost matrix first.

Denote by $\mathcal P_{ij,b}(f)$ the literal frozen SHT pair.  The
specified path difference is
\[
 \mathscr P_{\rm com}
 =\mathcal P_{ij,b}(f)+\mathcal P_{kl,c}(Bf)
  -\mathcal P_{kl,c}(f)-\mathcal P_{ij,b}(Cf).
 \tag{DTC3}
\]
For a nonvanishing analytic coefficient $t$, the native shear pair is
the sum of the two unit edges
\[
 (\Phi_g,\Phi_{D_a(t)^{-1}g},-v(t)),\qquad
 (\Phi_{D_a(t)^{-1}g},\Phi_{E_{ab}(t)g},v(t)).
 \tag{DTC4}
\]
The second edge uses the equally normed representative
$D_a(t)^{-1}E_{ab}(t)g=E_{ab}(1)D_a(t)^{-1}g$ before the final
diagonal multiplication.  All passive rows stay unchanged in this
representation.  Equal endpoint potentials in (DTC4) make the two
edges exact inverses, not merely pairs with equal boundaries.  SHT
glues this pair by zero across $t=0$ using its vanishing on $|t|<1$.

\subsection{The whole eight-edge presentation}

On $N_{bc}=\{bc\ne0\}$ write $d=v(b)$, $e=v(c)$ and set
\[
 \begin{array}{llll}
 F=\Phi_f,&S=\Phi_{D_i(b)^{-1}f},&V=\Phi_{Bf},
   &T=\Phi_{D_k(c)^{-1}Bf},\\
 H=\Phi_{CBf}=\Phi_{BCf},&J=\Phi_{D_i(b)^{-1}Cf},
   &U=\Phi_{Cf},&K_0=\Phi_{D_k(c)^{-1}f}.
 \end{array}
 \tag{DTC5}
\]
Expanding exactly the two specified paths and reversing the second
one gives
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
 \text{edge}&\text{ordered potentials}&\text{weight}\\\hline
 1&(F,S)&-d\\
 2&(S,V)&d\\
 3&(V,T)&-e\\
 4&(T,H)&e\\
 5&(H,J)&-d\\
 6&(J,U)&d\\
 7&(U,K_0)&-e\\
 8&(K_0,F)&e.
\end{array}
\tag{DTC6}
\]
The word is not replaced by a norm-equivalent unspecified path.

\begin{lemma}[Whole-witness zero domains]
On $O_b=\{|b|<1\}$, respectively $O_c=\{|c|<1\}$, the complete
loop (DTC6), where defined, is a union of four inverse-edge pairs.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
On $O_b$, the shear $B$ is a norm isometry, so $F=V$ and $U=H$.
The diagonal $D_k(c)^{-1}$ commutes with $B$ because $k$ is neither
its source nor its target.  Hence $T=K_0$.  The inverse pairs are
$(1,2),(5,6),(3,8),(4,7)$.  On $O_c$, similarly $F=U$, $V=H$,
and $J=S$, since $D_i(b)^{-1}$ commutes with $C$.  The inverse
pairs are $(3,4),(7,8),(1,6),(2,5)$.

These are identities on the whole ordinary opens.  They therefore
identify every actual derivative and mixed coefficient jet.  At a
zero coefficient the corresponding native shear is already zero by
SHT; no value is assigned to the logarithm of zero.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A common cap and strict top-degree cancellation}

The common passive tuple consists of all rows other than $i,k$.
Write
\[
 M=\|f\|_\infty,\qquad
 P=\|(f_a)_{a\notin\{i,k\}}\|_\infty,\qquad
 \psi=-\log P,
 \tag{DTC7}
\]
with $\psi=+\infty$ at $P=0$.  On the coefficient-unit locus put
\[
 \kappa=\max(0,-d,-e)=\log\max(1,|b|,|c|),\qquad
 \Psi=\min(\psi,\Phi_f+\kappa+\varepsilon),\qquad
 R_*=R-D\Psi,
 \tag{DTC8}
\]
where $\varepsilon>0$ is fixed.  The minima and maxima here have
finite piecewise affine valuation presentations after a common
metric shift.  No closure under arbitrary minima of smooth actual
scalars is asserted.  At $P=0$ the second cap branch holds on an
ordinary neighborhood, so $\Psi$ is an actual scalar everywhere
on $N_{bc}\cap W$.

\begin{lemma}[Strong small-passive cancellation]
On the ordinary open
\[
 \mathcal O_{\rm small}=\{P<e^{-\kappa}M\}
                         =\{\psi>\Phi_f+\kappa\}
 \tag{DTC9}
\]
the four endpoint potentials satisfy $F=V=U=H$.  Each of the four
native pairs in (DTC3) is therefore an exact inverse-edge pair.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Both source rows are passive.  For every analytic point, in its
completed residue field,
\[
 \|Bf-f\|\le |b|P<M,\qquad
 \|Cf-f\|\le |c|P<M,
 \qquad
 \|BCf-f\|\le\max(|b|,|c|)P<M.
 \tag{DTC10}
\]
There is no cross-term in the last expression by (DTC2).  The
non-Archimedean strict triangle identity proves the four norm
equalities.  They hold on an ordinary open, including non-rational
analytic points, so their actual jets agree there.  No assertion
about the middle normalizer norms is necessary: equal endpoints
make each native two-edge pair inverse whatever its middle norm.
\end{proof}

Here and below $\mathcal K_s(e;R_*)$ and $S_{e,s}$ mean exactly
the frozen full CS edge triple and residual with the two endpoint
potentials shifted by $-\Psi$.  In particular the identity used is
\[
        p_e^{[s]}=\partial_2\mathcal K_s(e;R_*)-\tfrac12S_{e,s},
 \qquad
 \partial_2(A,B_0,M_0)=(d'A+d''M_0,d''B_0+d'M_0).
 \tag{DTC11}
\]
The subscripted $B_0,M_0$ are components of a triple, not matrices.
All $D|d|$, $D|e|$, $D\kappa$, and cap-wall terms in that formula
are retained.  Its exact inverse-edge antisymmetry is part of the
explicit polynomial formula, rather than a choice of primitive.

The second ordinary open
\[
 \mathcal O_{\rm pas}=\{\psi<\Phi_f+\kappa+\varepsilon\}
 \tag{DTC12}
\]
together with (DTC9) covers $N_{bc}\cap W$.  On (DTC12), $\Psi=\psi$.
Every unit edge in (DTC6), using the representatives in (DTC4),
leaves the same $r-2$ passive sections unchanged.  The full frozen
CS passive-block calculation therefore gives $S_{e,s}=0$ edgewise.
For precision, its reference curvature is pulled back from the map
defined by those passive sections to $\mathbf P^{r-3}$, hence
$R_*^{r-2}=0$.  After the CS15 reduction, the remaining symbol for a
nonnegative edge weight $c_e$ is
\[
 \mathfrak a(p+c_e,q;h_{s-1}(R_*,C_q)),\qquad C_q=R_*-Dq.
 \tag{DTC13}
\]
The pure reference-curvature term vanishes by that dimension bound.
On every other carrier, either the changed row is active and
$p+c_e=q$ with full restricted first jets, or a passive row is
active and $q=0$ with its restricted first jet zero, or the other
target row alone is active and $C_q$ vanishes on a neighborhood.
This exhausts all rows.  Negative weights are handled by reversing
the edge; on the zero-weight wall use $p=q$ and the restricted
first jets of $(c_e)_\pm$.  This is the full carrier calculation,
with arbitrary passive base directions, not an assertion that a
low-degree coefficient block is independently closed.

On (DTC9) all four native pairs cancel inversely.  Consequently
\[
 \sum_e S_{e,s}=0,\qquad \sum_e\mathcal K_s(e;R_*)=0
                   \quad\hbox{on }\mathcal O_{\rm small}.
 \tag{DTC14}
\]
Actual locality proves $\sum_eS_{e,s}=0$ on the whole unit locus.

\subsection{The explicit metric term and the actual witness}

Use all eight original edges in (DTC6).  If an edge has endpoint
potentials $\Phi_a,\Phi_b$ and weight $c_e$, put
\[
 C_a=R-D\Phi_a,\quad v_e=\Phi_b-\Phi_a,\quad
 \overline\Phi_e=(\Phi_a+\Phi_b)/2,
 \qquad \mathcal L(u,Q)=(u\,d''Q,d'u\wedge Q).
 \tag{DTC15}
\]
Write $h_j$ for the complete homogeneous polynomial of degree $j$,
with $h_0=1$ and $h_j=0$ for $j<0$.  Define the full-loop source
terms, with no independent-closedness assumption,
\[
 \begin{split}
 \Gamma_k&=\tfrac12\sum_e c_e(C_a^k+C_b^k),\\
 f_k&=-\sum_e\left\{
 \mathcal L(\overline\Phi_e,v_eh_{k-1}(C_a,C_b))
 +\mathcal L\left(c_e,\tfrac12
       (\Phi_aC_a^{k-1}+\Phi_bC_b^{k-1})\right)\right\}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{DTC16}
\]
For $h=-\Psi$, put
\[
 \mathcal H_h(\alpha,\beta)
 =(d''h\wedge\alpha,-d'h\wedge\beta,h\,d'\alpha),
 \qquad
 S_j(R_*,R)=\sum_{a=0}^jR_*^aR^{j-a},
 \tag{DTC17}
\]
and use the entire metric-transport triple
\[
 Q_{\rm met}=
 \sum_{k=1}^{s-1}S_{s-k-1}(R_*,R)\mathcal H_h(f_k)
 -\left(0,0,h\sum_{k=1}^s\Gamma_{k-1}R_*^{s-k}\right).
 \tag{DTC18}
\]
This is frozen MT16, not CPT applied to the possibly nonclosed
individual $f_k$.  In particular its final mixed component is not
omitted.  Define the actual finite expression
\[
 q_{\rm com}=\sum_{e=1}^8\mathcal K_s(e;R_*)-Q_{\rm met}
 \quad\hbox{on }N_{bc}\cap W.
 \tag{DTC19}
\]
Here $\mathcal K_s$ is the exact CS8--CS10 finite polynomial
formula in the two normalized potentials, the edge weight, its
absolute value, and their actual differentials.  This definition
is an explicit evaluation of that frozen formula, not an
existential selection from a quotient or closed range.

\begin{theorem}[Distinct-target native commuting relation]
For the data (DTC1)--(DTC2), the expression (DTC19) extends by zero
from $N_{bc}\cap W$ to an actual triple
\[
 q_{\rm com}\in
 B^{r-3,r-1}(W)\oplus B^{r-1,r-3}(W)\oplus B^{r-2,r-2}(W),
 \qquad
             \mathscr P_{\rm com}=\partial_2q_{\rm com}.
 \tag{DTC20}
\]
The error pair is exactly zero.  The construction keeps the whole
specified native presentation, the original metric, all intercept
walls, zero entries, and coefficient zeros, over the original $K$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
CS and the proved top residual cancellation give the shifted loop
$\mathscr P_{\rm com}^*=\partial_2\sum_e\mathcal K_s(e;R_*)$.
Full-loop MT gives
$\mathscr P_{\rm com}^*-\mathscr P_{\rm com}
=\partial_2Q_{\rm met}$, proving (DTC20) on the unit locus with
the sign in (DTC19).

On each of $O_b,O_c$, the inverse-edge pairings already proved
make the entire sum of $\mathcal K_s$ vanish, for the same chosen
cap.  They also make every $f_k$ and $\Gamma_k$ vanish.  Therefore
the whole triple $q_{\rm com}$, including all metric terms, is zero
on $O_b\cap N_{bc}\cap W$ and $O_c\cap N_{bc}\cap W$.
The three ordinary opens $N_{bc},O_b,O_c$ cover $N$.
Gluing with the zero triple on the latter two is actual sheaf
gluing and proves the assertion through $b=0$ and $c=0$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Presentation and topology interface.}
On each of these ordinary opens, the witness has one finite actual
presentation obtained by adjoining the finitely many analytic units,
norm vertices and cap walls used above to the prescribed presentation.
All derivatives and products are the actual operations in that common
presentation.  On refinements the formulas pull back to the same
actual forms, and the literal zero identifications glue them.
This provides an actual element of the stated $B$ spaces.  It does
not supply a global continuous linear right inverse, a uniform
finite-height description of all actual relations, a quotient
topology comparison, or a full $m=n-2$ kernel theorem.

\subsection{The original-support Green interface}

Here is the exact geometric scope in which the local certificate
produces a Green form.  Let $s_0,s_1$ be global regular $r$-tuples
of the same metrized line bundle on the original smooth projective
$X/K$.  Put $Y_a=(s_a=0)$, $Z=[Y_1]-[Y_0]$ and
\[
 U=X^{\rm an}\setminus|Z|,\qquad
 V=X^{\rm an}\setminus(|Y_0|\cup|Y_1|),\qquad
 C=(|Y_0|\cup|Y_1|)\cap U.
 \tag{DTC21}
\]
Do not alter CI0 in the original CI theorem.  Instead suppose that
an ordinary neighborhood $N=N_0\cup N_1\subset U$ of $C$ is
equipped with original-$K$ analytic shear paths from $s_0$ to $s_1$.
Their literal native pairs $p_a=(\alpha_a,\beta_a)$ satisfy
\[
 G_1-G_0=\partial_1p_a\quad\hbox{on }N_a\cap V.
 \tag{DTC22}
\]
On the whole overlap their prescribed difference is one (DTC3)
at the tuple reached after any identical prefix; identical suffixes
cancel because the two commuting words reach exactly the same tuple.
Thus, after indexing the paths consistently,
\[
 p_1-p_0=\partial_2q_{\rm com}\quad\hbox{on }N_0\cap N_1\cap V.
 \tag{DTC23}
\]
This is an independently checkable overlap certificate, not an
assertion that arbitrary local paths have one.

Take the frozen RAG ordinary smooth partition $\theta_0+\theta_1=1$
on $N$, write $\theta=\theta_1$, and let
$q_{\rm com}=(A,B_0,M_0)$.  The complete two-colour partition formula is
\[
 \begin{split}
 \alpha&=\theta_0\alpha_0+\theta_1\alpha_1
                         +d'\theta\wedge A+d''\theta\wedge M_0,\\
 \beta&=\theta_0\beta_0+\theta_1\beta_1
                         +d''\theta\wedge B_0+d'\theta\wedge M_0.
 \end{split}
 \tag{DTC24}
\]
Terms involving $q_{\rm com}$ are formed on the overlap and extended
by their partition supports as in RAG.  Its full mixed calculation
gives $G_1-G_0=d'\alpha+d''\beta$ on $N\cap V$.
Choose the RSP cutoff $\chi$ on $U$, supported in $N$ and equal to
one on an ordinary neighborhood of $C$.  On $V$ set
\[
 g_Z=G_1-G_0-d'(\chi\alpha)-d''(\chi\beta).
 \tag{DTC25}
\]
It is zero on that neighborhood intersected with $V$, so extension
by zero gives an actual $g_Z\in B^{r-1,r-1}(U)$.  The cutoff is
on $U$, not asserted to extend to $X$.

For every actual $\eta\in B_c^{n-r,n-r}(X^{\rm an})$, the frozen
support lemma makes $D\eta$ compactly supported away from
$|Y_0|\cup|Y_1|$.  The frozen CI identities apply separately to
$G_0,G_1$ there, while actual Stokes kills both derivative corrections
in (DTC25).  Hence
\[
       \int_Ug_Z\wedge D\eta=-\int_Z\eta.
 \tag{DTC26}
\]
Thus the actual representative bridge is supplied precisely under
the displayed path and whole-overlap certificate hypotheses.

\paragraph{Pressure tests and boundaries.}
All equalities were established in completed residue fields and on
ordinary analytic opens, not just at rational points.  Analytic
coefficients or refinements involving $(T_1,T_2,T_1+T_2)$ remain
allowed wherever these are units; no independence of unit valuations
was assumed.  Moving punctures and arbitrarily many additional
passive base directions do not affect the pointwise bounds or the
projective passive-block dimension calculation.  There is no base
extension, degree division, general matrix factorization, automatic
path overlap theorem, or use of arbitrary proper pushforward of
actual forms.  In rank two the distinct-target commuting conditions
(DTC2) have no solution; the opposite-root Gauss relation is a
different problem and is not included in this theorem.

The result is a new explicit native path-relation certificate, not a
new claim that every CI difference or every homologically trivial
cycle admits a Green representative.  The registered NR/FL/JR/AQ/QP/
ND/wedge failures, and the LTF prohibition on arbitrary low-degree
closed splitting, remain unchanged.  No failure or success here
settles general Strong G, the entire actual kernel comparison, or
the additional Beilinson--Bloch height axioms.
