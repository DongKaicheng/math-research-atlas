\subsection{A linear companion for a finite native word}

Fix $N,L,f,W,R$ as in GB, with $r\ge2$, and two selected rows.
All matrices in this section change only these two rows, so the
other $r-2$ sections are literally unchanged.  A supplied actual
scalar $\Psi$ on $W$ gives $R_*=R-D\Psi$ and normalized vertex
potentials $p_v=\Phi_v-\Psi$.  For any finite native word $w$,
expand every nonzero shear into its two prescribed SHT unit edges,
retain every unit-row edge, and assign the prescribed zero edge to
each supplied signed-permutation norm isometry.  Define
\[
 \mathcal J_\Psi(w;f)=\sum_{e\in w}\mathcal K_s(e;R_*)
                    -\mathcal M_{-\Psi}(w;f),\qquad s=r-1.
 \tag{TCT1}
\]
Here $\mathcal K_s$ is exactly CS6--CS10.  The symbol
$\mathcal M_h$ means the finite polynomial MT15, with its full
$f_k$ and $\Gamma_k$ edge sums, evaluated on this edge list.
For an open word (TCT1) is a definition of a finite actual triple;
we do \emph{not} assert the closed-loop MT boundary identity for an
individual open word.  The actual formula is meaningful and linear
in edge lists regardless of that boundary identity.

Use the \emph{same} supplied $\Psi$ in every term.  Literal edge
linearity gives
\[
 \mathcal J_\Psi(w_2w_1;f)
 =\mathcal J_\Psi(w_1;f)+\mathcal J_\Psi(w_2;w_1f),
 \qquad \mathcal J_\Psi(w^{-1};wf)=-\mathcal J_\Psi(w;f).
 \tag{TCT2}
\]
On the ordinary small-coefficient open of a native shear, its two
unit edges are exact reverses: the full $\mathcal K_s$, every
$f_k$, and every $\Gamma_k$ cancel.  Thus its complete contribution
to (TCT1) is zero there, for arbitrary $\Psi$.  This glues (TCT1)
through every zero shear coefficient.  It is not an extension of
the two singular edges separately.

\subsection{Admissible caps for one fixed two-target loop}

For $r\ge3$, write $\psi$ for the norm potential of the common
$r-2$ passive sections, with value $+\infty$ at their common zeros.
On a coefficient-unit domain consider a fixed closed native loop
whose endpoint potentials telescope and whose unit weights sum to
zero.  Suppose its complete two-active-entry degree-one calculation
has one of the following already supplied exact identities:
\[
 \sum_e S_{e,1}=0,\qquad\hbox{or}\qquad
 \sum_e S_{e,1}=-\mathfrak a(x,y;1),\quad Dx=Dy=0.
 \tag{TCT3}
\]
The allowed examples here are respectively NSC and WNR, and CS
and GPR, with exactly their original scalars and unit-edge tables.
Their identities hold through all analytic sum-cancellation walls
and are invariant under an arbitrary common scalar shift.
We are not supplying (TCT3) for an unknown matrix loop.

An admissible cap is a supplied actual scalar
\[
 \Psi_j=\min(\psi,b_j),\qquad
 b_j>\max_v\Phi_v
 \tag{TCT4}
\]
on that domain, where the maximum includes \emph{every} genuine
unit-edge vertex of the fixed whole loop.  The minima in (TCT4)
must have a supplied finite actual presentation.  In our applications
they are finite valuation/norm minima with a common metric shift;
no closure of arbitrary smooth scalars under minimum is assumed.
Positive real offsets in the frozen caps give exactly (TCT4).

In the strict case put $q[\Psi_j]=\mathcal J_{\Psi_j}(w;f)$.
In the second case let $d=r-2$ and put
\[
\begin{gathered}
 e_{x,y}=(x\,d''y,y\,d'x),\qquad
 h_j=-\Psi_j\sum_{a=0}^{d-1}R_{*,j}^{a}R^{d-1-a},\\
 q[\Psi_j]=\mathcal J_{\Psi_j}(w;f)
       -(0,0,xyR_{*,j}^{d}/2)+H_{h_j}(e_{x,y}).
 \tag{TCT5}
\end{gathered}
\]
The full operator is
$H_h(\alpha,\beta)=(d''h\wedge\alpha,-d'h\wedge\beta,
h\wedge d'\alpha)$.  It is applied before any external coefficient
is multiplied and only to the independently closed pair $e_{x,y}$.
For $r=2$ omit the cap and the $H_h$ term and use $R^0=1$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact finite transport of these entire witnesses]
For two admissible caps of the same fixed loop and passive block,
\[
             q[\Psi_1]-q[\Psi_0]=\partial_3 T_{01}
 \tag{TCT6}
\]
with the following explicit actual $T_{01}$: interpolate
$\widetilde\Psi=\Psi_0+(3t^2-2t^3)(\Psi_1-\Psi_0)$ in every
term of the entire formula (TCT5) (or its strict version), use the
full CT2 differentials, and apply CT7 including its $-d''J$ term.
The ordinary boundary in either case is the specified native loop
minus its original extending pair, which is unchanged.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let $m=\max_v\Phi_v$.  The two ordinary opens
\[
 W_0=\{\psi<\min(b_0,b_1)\},\qquad W_2=\{\psi>m\}
 \tag{TCT7}
\]
cover the domain by the strict inequalities in (TCT4).
On $W_0$ both caps are exactly $\psi$, hence their difference and
all actual and formal derivatives vanish.  The complete CS21
passive-carrier calculation kills every top-degree edge residual;
$R_*^{r-2}=0$ there because the same original-$K$ passive map
lands in $\mathbf P^{r-3}$.

On $W_2$ all the original norm vertices are determined by the two
selected rows.  The CS17 two-entry recurrence applies coefficientwise
for the formal common normalization, since the old- and new-corner
differences are unchanged.  The full degree-one relation (TCT3)
also persists coefficientwise: its common-shift change is an edge
sum of $\mathfrak a(c_e-(\Phi_q-\Phi_p),h;1)$, and both the
unit weights and endpoint differences telescope.  The particular
analytic first-jet identities used in NSC, WNR and GPR involve only
the unchanged original units; no independent formal variables
are substituted into an unproved analytic relation.

Thus the complete formal edge identity, full nonclosed MT remainder,
scalar correction and the independently closed CPT correction give
\[
 \widetilde\partial_2\widetilde q=P
 \quad\hbox{or}\quad
 \widetilde\partial_2\widetilde q=P-e_{x,y}R^d
 \tag{TCT8}
\]
with no parameter or exterior-variable terms on the right.  This
is stronger than equality of numerical boundaries.  Flat endpoint
derivatives give CT6, so its exact finite coefficient identity CT8
proves (TCT6).  The proof retains the entire two-colour mixed jet.
For $r=2$, the sum of the scalar CS edge terms is invariant under
any common shift by direct endpoint and weight telescoping, and
the displayed formula is already cap independent.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The normalized GB overlap, without losing its original caps}

Take exactly the GB16 overlap word on $\{\alpha\gamma\ne0\}$,
including all nine nested calls listed in GIC13.  Supply a common
two-target cap admissible for every nonzero call on the ordinary
domain under consideration.  Use the above transports, with SAN
for SL/SAR and the SL summands of MX.  Prefixes and suffixes are
the exact GB10/GB15 tuples, and all passive entries are unchanged.
Adding their finite oriented transport quadruples gives
\[
 Q_A^{\rm com}-Q_A^{\rm old}=\partial_3\mathcal H_A.
 \tag{TCT9}
\]
The new full triple is literally
\[
\begin{gathered}
 a=v(\alpha),\quad c=v(\gamma),\quad
 \epsilon_A=e_{c,a-c},\quad b_A=c(c-a)/2,\\
 Q_A^{\rm com}=
 \mathcal J_\Psi(w_\alpha;f)-\mathcal J_\Psi(w_\gamma;f)
 +(0,0,b_A R_*^{r-2})+H_h(\epsilon_A),
 \qquad h=-\Psi\sum_{a=0}^{r-3}R_*^aR^{r-3-a}.
 \tag{TCT10}
\end{gathered}
\]
Indeed all strict subrelations contribute their linear companion
edge sums; only the displayed GB distinct-row CS swap contributes
a scalar and extending-pair correction.  Its weights are
$c,a-c$, so CS36 has scalar $-c(a-c)/2=b_A$.
Every common intermediate word cancels in the full edge and MT
sums by (TCT2).  This is a literal formula, not a boundary-only
identification of $Q_A$.
For $r=2$ the sum defining $h$ is absent, the $H_h$ term is zero,
and $R_*^{r-2}=R^0=1$, exactly as in the rank-two input formulas.

For an ordinary partition $\theta$ used in GB22, if (TCT9) is
