\section{Original source annihilation for projective models with totally degenerate strata}
\label{sc2td:section}

\paragraph{Scope and source.}
This is an application of the frozen FD17 and ED27 source interfaces,
not a new construction of their analytic objects. It supplies every
detector-closure certificate in a geometric class of models and then
kills the original Abel--Jacobi source. In every dimension and codimension,
the resulting Green form is ordinary actual on the originally specified
support complement, with the original absolute value and all compact
actual tests. General G and arbitrary-variety complete unconditional BB
remain \texttt{OPEN}.

The external theorem used is Raskind--Xarles, \emph{On the Etale
Cohomology of Algebraic Varieties with Totally Degenerate Reduction over
$p$-adic Fields}, J. Math. Sci. Univ. Tokyo 14 (2007), 261--291:
Definition 1, conditions (a),(b), printed pp.~266--267;
Proposition 1, p.~275; Proposition 2 and Corollaries 1,2,
pp.~277--279. The exact final publisher PDF is retained and hashed
in the companion dependency record. Only (a) and (b) are used.
The crystalline and ordinary conditions (c),(d) are not inferred or needed.
Their definition permits a regular proper model. Our hypothesis below
explicitly requires a regular \emph{projective} witness, as FD17 does;
we do not assert that properness implies projectivity.

\subsection{Full native assumptions}

Fix $K/\mathbf Q_p$ finite with its original absolute value, and let
$X/K$ be smooth projective geometrically integral of dimension $n\geq1$.
Suppose that the following data are supplied, independently of a cycle:
\[
 L/K\text{ finite valued},\qquad
 g:Y\longrightarrow X_L\text{ projective dominant generically finite},
 \qquad \mathcal Y/O_L.
 \tag{TDR2.1}
\]
Here $Y/L$ is smooth projective geometrically integral, and
$\mathcal Y$ is a regular projective reduced strictly semistable model
of $Y$, of relative dimension $n$. Write $k$ for the native finite
residue field. For every nonempty indexed stratum $D_I$ of the special
fibre put $\overline D_I=D_I\times_k\bar k$ and
$d_I=\dim D_I$. Disconnected geometric strata are retained with all
their connected components.

Assume the following exact Raskind--Xarles conditions.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Each $\operatorname{CH}^c(\overline D_I)$ is finitely generated.
There is a projective embedding of the entire special fibre whose
restricted hyperplane operators $H_I$ satisfy, for $2c\leq d_I$,
\[
 H_I^{d_I-2c+1}x=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 (-1)^c\deg\bigl(x\,H_I^{d_I-2c}x\bigr)>0
 \quad\text{for }0\ne x\in\operatorname{CH}^c(\overline D_I)_{\mathbf Q}.
 \tag{TDR2.2}
\]
The positivity is read componentwise, or on the direct sum with the
sum of component degrees.
\item[(b)] For every prime $\ell\ne p$ the odd integral geometric
etale cohomology of each stratum is torsion, and the cycle map induces
\[
 \bigl(\operatorname{CH}^c(\overline D_I)/\mathrm{tors}\bigr)
       \otimes\mathbf Z_\ell
 \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
 H^{2c}(\overline D_I,\mathbf Z_\ell(c))/\mathrm{tors}.
 \tag{TDR2.3}
\]
\end{enumerate}
These are hypotheses on the specified strata; no standard conjecture
is declared true for arbitrary strata. For the rational argument below,
the rational cycle isomorphisms and the weight--monodromy consequence
at one supplied $\ell\ne p$ would suffice. We state (a),(b) as in the
published theorem, so that the exact external implication is available
without weakening or reproving it.

\begin{theorem}[TDR2-A: original-source vanishing]
\label{sc2td:main}
Under (TDR2.1)--(TDR2.3), for every $1\leq r\leq n$ and every original
rational cycle $Z$ on $X$ with zero geometric $\ell$-adic cycle class,
the original Chow-source map to the FD17 numerical detector dual
vanishes at $[Z]$. In particular all its originally fixed finite
detector periods are exactly zero, and $Z$ has the original ordinary
actual strong-zero-curvature Green form. The assertion holds for every
specified representative of $[Z]$ with its own exact support complement.
It does not assert that the detector kernel or residual quotient is zero.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Splitting and the numerical-to-cohomological comparison}

There are finitely many strata and finitely many degrees. Choose integral
generators of all their finitely generated geometric Chow groups modulo
torsion. Each generator is represented by an actual finite cycle, defined
over a finite extension of $k$. Enlarging that extension also separates
all geometric components. It lifts uniquely to a finite unramified
extension $L'/L$. Replace the supplied witness by its base change to
$O_{L'}$, retaining the complete degree $[L':K]$.
This remains a regular projective reduced strictly semistable witness.
Relabel it $(L,Y,\mathcal Y,k)$ for the following calculation. All of
these choices precede $Z$.

\begin{lemma}[TDR2-B: native numerical realization]
\label{sc2td:native}
For every native component $D_i$ after this finite splitting and every
$0\leq c\leq n$, restriction and cycle class identify
\[
 \operatorname{CH}^c(D_i)_{\mathbf Q}
       \simeq \operatorname{CH}^c(\overline D_i)_{\mathbf Q},
 \qquad
 N^c(D_i)_{\mathbf Q}\otimes\mathbf Q_\ell
       \simeq H^{2c}(\overline D_i,\mathbf Q_\ell(c)).
 \tag{TDR2.4}
\]
The second isomorphism is induced by the actual cycle class map.
It is compatible with native degree pairings, restriction, Gysin,
and the full component-incidence operator $A$. In particular it is
not an assumed cycle map on an arbitrary numerical group.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Surjectivity of the first map follows from the selected generators.
If a native rational Chow class becomes zero over $\bar k$, its
rational equivalence uses finitely many subvarieties and rational
functions, hence is already defined over a finite extension $k'/k$.
Finite flat pullback followed by proper pushforward multiplies the
native Chow class by $[k':k]$. It was zero after pullback, so it was
zero over $k$ with rational coefficients. This proves injectivity.

Condition (b), tensored with $\mathbf Q_\ell$, says that the geometric
cycle maps in both complementary codimensions $c,n-c$ are isomorphisms.
Compatibility of cycle intersection with etale cup product and native
proper trace makes the rational Chow degree pairing nondegenerate:
if a class pairs to zero against every native complementary Chow class,
its geometric cycle class pairs to zero against the entire complementary
geometric cohomology. Poincare duality kills that cohomology class.
The cycle isomorphism and the first assertion then kill the native
rational Chow class. Thus the kernel defining numerical equivalence is
zero on $\operatorname{CH}^c(D_i)_{\mathbf Q}$, proving (TDR2.4).

The cycle-class compatibility used here is for actual Chow cycles.
It commutes with Cartier restriction, proper Gysin, and multiplication
by the diagonal normal class. These are precisely the entries of the
incidence operator in VT17/ED27. The resulting numerical realization
therefore intertwines the full operators, including their fixed signs.
No native residue degree has been divided out: on a zero-cycle the
geometric trace of its native cycle class is its native degree.
\end{proof}

Fix $r$ and put $a=n-r$, $q=a+1$. Write
\[
 C^c=\bigoplus_iN^c(D_i)_{\mathbf Q},\qquad
 H_c=\bigoplus_iH^{2c}(\overline D_i,\mathbf Q_\ell(c)),
 \qquad V=\ker(A_a:C^a\to C^{a+1}).
 \tag{TDR2.5}
\]
For every original numerical detector $v\in V$, choose any actual
native rational representatives $\widetilde v_i$. Their classes
$h=(\operatorname{cl}_\ell(\widetilde v_i))_i$ satisfy
\[
                    A_{\ell,a}h=0.
 \tag{TDR2.6}
\]
Indeed the cohomological tuple in (TDR2.6) is the class of the actual
Cartier restrictions of $\sum_i j_{i*}\widetilde v_i$; their numerical
tuple is $A_av=0$. The injectivity in (TDR2.4), in degree $a+1$,
therefore proves the asserted cohomological zero. Here $A_{\ell,a}$
is ED27's full incidence operator, equivalently $-\gamma\rho_a$ with
the SC2-EA sign convention. Nothing requires $\rho_a h=0$.

ED27's model-weight and component-detection lemma now shows that the
supported detector class
\[
 t_v=\sum_i(j_i)_*\operatorname{cl}_\ell(\widetilde v_i)
       \in H^{2q}_{\mathcal Y_k}(\mathcal Y,\mathbf Q_\ell(q))
 \quad\text{satisfies}\quad \operatorname{forg}(t_v)=0.
 \tag{TDR2.7}
\]
Thus the finite etale-closure certificates missing in general ED27/SC1
have actually been supplied here, for every detector. In the notation
of SC1, its algebraic envelope is $E_c=H_c$, its projection $\nu_c$
is an isomorphism, and the space of genuinely closed lifts maps onto
$V\otimes\mathbf Q_\ell$. This does not set $V$ equal to zero.

\subsection{The odd Galois groups, with both weights retained}

The exact consequence of Raskind--Xarles Corollary 2 is
weight--monodromy for every $H^m(Y_{\bar L},\mathbf Q_\ell)$.
It follows from (a),(b) on the supplied model; this is an established
theorem in this class, not a general weight--monodromy assumption.
Finite unramified splitting preserves all these hypotheses.

\begin{lemma}[The weight calculation used in this application]
\label{sc2td:odd}
For $1\leq t\leq n$ put
$W_t=H^{2t-1}(Y_{\bar L},\mathbf Q_\ell(t))$. Then
\[
                         W_t^{G_L}=0,
 \qquad                  H^1(L,W_t)=0.
 \tag{TDR2.8}
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The monodromy filtration on $W_t$ is centered at weight
$(2t-1)-2t=-1$. Strict semistability makes inertia unipotent, with
nilpotent monodromy $N$. Each monodromy string centered at $-1$ has
a bottom piece in $\ker N$ of weight $-1-j$ and a top piece in
$\operatorname{coker}N$ of weight $-1+j$, with $j\geq0$.
Equivalently these are the primitive-decomposition consequences of
$N^j:\operatorname{gr}_j^M\simeq
\operatorname{gr}_{-j}^M(-j)$. They concern a filtration by pure
subquotients; no Frobenius semisimplicity is needed.

Consequently $W_t^{I_L}=\ker N$ has weights at most $-1$.
Its Frobenius operator has no eigenvalue $1$, so both its invariants
and its coinvariants are zero. The canonical tame inertia formula is
\[
 H^1(I_L,W_t)\simeq\operatorname{coker}
       (N:W_t\to W_t(-1))
       \simeq (W_t/NW_t)(-1),
 \tag{TDR2.9}
\]
where the last expression treats $N$ as the underlying nilpotent
linear operator and retains the indicated Frobenius twist. Its pure
subquotients have weights $(-1+j)+2=1+j\geq1$, so it has no
Frobenius-fixed vectors. In particular the two inertia terms have
different signs of weight; they have not both been called negative.

For completeness the inertia--residue-field Hochschild--Serre sequence
is
\[
 0\to H^1(k,W_t^{I_L})\to H^1(L,W_t)
     \to H^1(I_L,W_t)^{G_k}\to0.
 \tag{TDR2.10}
\]
The left term is the Frobenius coinvariant group just shown to be zero,
and the right term is zero by the positive-weight calculation. This
proves the second assertion. The first follows from
$W_t^{G_L}=(W_t^{I_L})^{G_k}=0$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Proof on the original Chow source and full descent}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem \ref{sc2td:main}]
For the original $z=[Z]$ let $z_Y=g^*z_L$. Its geometric cycle class
is zero by pullback compatibility. Choose any actual rational model
Chow extension $\Theta$ of $z_Y$, as allowed by frozen localization.
For every fixed numerical detector $v$, the closure certificate
(TDR2.7) permits the unique ED27 generic class $\psi_v$ and its
geometric restriction
\[
 b_v\in H^{2q-1}(Y_{\bar L},\mathbf Q_\ell(q))^{G_L}.
\]
The exact original-source ED27 formula reads
\[
 \begin{split}
 P_z(v)
   &=\sum_i\deg_k\bigl(j_i^!\Theta\cdot\widetilde v_i\bigr)\\
   &=\operatorname{ord}_L\bigl(
       \operatorname{AJ}_\ell(z_Y)\cup_{\rm ev}b_v\bigr).
 \end{split}
 \tag{TDR2.11}
\]
The geometric coefficient degrees add to $2n$, and their twists to
$n+1$; the resulting class is in $H^1(L,\mathbf Q_\ell(1))$ with
the original positive valuation normalization.

Equation (TDR2.8) for $t=r$ gives
$\operatorname{AJ}_\ell(z_Y)=0$. Independently, its assertion for
$t=q$ gives $b_v=0$. Either fact annihilates (TDR2.11).
These are used only after the actual closure certificate has been
proved. In particular this argument does not infer numerical-period
zero from an arbitrary cohomological primitive of $s_z$, or substitute
the full Rapoport--Zink boundary space for the original Chow source.

Each $P_z(v)$ was a rational native intersection number. Its zero image
in $\mathbf Q_\ell$ means that rational number is exactly zero.
Choose the FD17 basis before $Z$; all its periods therefore vanish.
The frozen exact-kernel theorem constructs the strong Green form on
the exact support complement of this specified $Z$, against all compact
ordinary actual tests, over the original $K$. This step is the already
proved FD17/VT17 activation and requires no new proper actual push.

More explicitly, for the final finite field $L$ used in the witness,
FD17 normalizes the original tests by
\[
 \eta_j=\frac{1}{[L:K]c_L}
       \operatorname{Tr}_{X_L/X}^{K}\bigl(B_g(\beta_{v_j})\bigr),
 \qquad c_L=-\log|\varpi_L|=c_K/e(L/K).
 \tag{TDR2.12}
\]
The degree is the complete $[L:K]$, including the splitting extension
and every ramification and residue factor. The absolute value is
extended without renormalization. $B_g$ is the frozen graph-adjoint
representative, not a newly assumed proper actual pushforward.
If a finite detector family from an earlier witness had already been
fixed, its periods also vanish: FD17 identifies its kernel with the
same original obstruction kernel. We have proved a zero value for the
original source, not replaced the requested periods by approximate ones.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Concrete scope and boundaries}

\paragraph{No novelty claim for previously covered model families.}
The concrete toric case below was already covered by the R6 criterion
and its frozen R18 reuse. The historical R6 files are
\path{round6_general_g/models_all_dimensions_criterion.tex}
(SHA256 \texttt{64d87a1f5d904d095b56333ffb624c1d3bbde0c864c4f90491898fa84832fa9a})
and \path{round6_general_g/root_all_codimension_consequences.tex}
(SHA256 \texttt{02d54471c113ee35d9f5a53e1c0c9da8d92defa4615e36e4a42f8552dc06c64c}).
Their complete extraction paths are recorded in the companion JSON.
The R6 criterion uses numerical hard Lefschetz and Hodge index on
all strata for the restriction of one relatively ample class on the
total model, and the specified double-stratum numerical-to-homological
comparison. Raskind--Xarles Remark 1(i), p.~268, supplies hard
Lefschetz on Chow groups, and the comparison above identifies the
numerical groups. However its condition (a) specifies a polarization
on the special fibre. Without a lifting or polarization-independence
argument, it has not been shown that the entire (a),(b) class is
literally contained in the R6 hypothesis. That containment does hold
when the specified polarization is the restriction of a relatively
ample class on the total model. The older all-codimension conclusion explicitly
states ordinary actual forms and all compact actual tests; it is not
relabelled as only a PWS assertion.

The general stratum mechanisms thus overlap, but no strictly new
geometric family has been established by this comparison. Accordingly
this module is registered as an exact original-source and etale
comparison certificate under the R32 interfaces, with no new geometric
G-domain counted. Its proof depends on FD17, ED27 and the
published Raskind--Xarles theorem, not on either R6 proof. Historical
R6 review/status flags are preserved; this module does not silently
reaudit or alter their entire historical chain.

\begin{corollary}[TDR2-C: supplied projective toric-stratum witnesses]
\label{sc2td:toric}
The theorem applies if the supplied regular projective reduced strictly
semistable witness has every geometric stratum a disjoint union of
smooth projective toric varieties. In particular it applies to $X$
itself when such a model exists after a finite valued extension, and
more generally when $X$ admits the supplied witness (TDR2.1).
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Raskind--Xarles Example 1(i), printed p.~270, verifies their
conditions (a)--(d) for projective special fibres with these strata.
Its (a),(b) are exactly the hypotheses above; the already supplied
projectivity of the total model supplies the remaining FD17 condition.
Theorem \ref{sc2td:main} then applies.
\end{proof}

This is an arbitrary-dimensional model criterion. The source is still
the actual original geometric-homological Chow group of $X$. There is
no assertion that every $X$ admits such a witness. The positivity in
(a) is a substantive input and cannot be discarded for general
algebraic strata. Even a cycle-class isomorphism on every stratum,
without the cited monodromy theorem or another odd-side annihilation
input, has not been promoted here to the conclusion.

\paragraph{Failed shortcuts explicitly excluded.}
The numerical condition $A_av=0$ alone did not give (TDR2.6): the
native comparison lemma did. Weight--monodromy alone would not give
that comparison, so the general SC1 defect is not solved by merely
asserting an Abel--Jacobi zero. Nor does the sign of an $M_{-1}$ index
replace the weight computation in (TDR2.9). No Frobenius weight-zero
semisimplicity or Liu very-nice condition (N3) is invoked. No claim is
made for all rigid uniformizations or all Shimura varieties from their
names alone. Finally the theorem is a local Green statement: a global
height conclusion needs the separately frozen global assembly and its
hypotheses at every place, and no arbitrary-variety global conclusion
is asserted here.

\paragraph{Nonvanishing of the ambient detector space is allowed.}
For example the standard smooth projective model of $\mathbf P^n$
has a single component, with zero incidence operator because its
special fibre is principal. Hence $\ker A_a=C^a$ is nonzero for
$0\leq a\leq n$. This is consistent with the theorem: the conclusion
is zero on the original homologically trivial source, and does not
ask the whole detector space to vanish.
