\section{Relative angular transgression across a valuation-order wall}
\label{sec:r9-relative-rank-one-transgression}

The constant-matrix angular construction has a relative extension
which does not use a fiberwise compact homotopy.  The latter would
be unsafe: with varying weights, a mixed angular product may have
noncompact angular support and nonzero mixed base coefficients.
We instead keep a finite actual identity and its complete wall
term.  For one varying diagonal entry that wall term vanishes
exactly, giving a global relative transgression.  Finite
factorization then treats every diagonal analytic-unit matrix.

\subsection{The full mixed identity and its actual wall term}

Let $Y$ be a good $K$-analytic space, let $r\ge2$ and $s=r-1$,
and work on $Y\times\mathbf P^s$.  Supply analytic units
$\lambda_i\in\mathcal O(Y)^\times$ and put
$a_i=v(\lambda_i)$.  These are actual pluriharmonic scalars:
$Da_i=0$, although their first differentials need not vanish.
Let $\omega_0$ be the pullback of the standard projective form,
and define
\[
 \begin{split}
 v&=-\log\|\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_i)z\|_\infty
                                  +\log\|z\|_\infty,\\
 \omega_a&=\omega_0-Dv,\qquad
 P_j=\omega_a^j\omega_0^{s-j}\quad(0\le j\le s),\\
 \Xi&=v\sum_{j=0}^{s}P_j,\qquad
 Q_j=-v\sum_{\ell=0}^{j-1}\omega_a^\ell\omega_0^{s-1-\ell}
                          \quad(1\le j\le s),\qquad Q_0=0.
 \end{split}
 \tag{RAT1}
\]
The two projective forms are pullbacks by two genuine maps
to $\mathbf P^s$.  In particular each has $(s+1)$st power
zero; this assertion does not suppress their mixed base
coefficients.  All forms in (RAT1) are global actual forms
on the product, and
\[
                            DQ_j=P_j-P_0.
 \tag{RAT2}
\]

Let $b_1\le\cdots\le b_r$ be the pointwise order statistics
of the $a_i$.  They are actual continuous piecewise smooth
scalars: the sum of the $k$ smallest entries is the minimum
of the finitely many sums over $k$-element subsets, and
$b_k$ is the difference of successive such minima.  These
are finite unit presentations, with all real walls and
actual relations retained.  Write $c=\sum_i a_i=\sum_i b_i$.
The exact actual support calculation is
\[
                         vP_j=b_{j+1}P_j\quad(0\le j\le s).
 \tag{RAT3}
\]
Indeed each individual power vanishes locally unless at
least $j+1$ shifted entries, respectively $r-j$ unshifted
entries, can attain its minimum.  The active-set argument
AAT8--AAT9 then gives $v=b_{j+1}$ on the support of their
product, pointwise in the base as well.  To verify that
this is an actual relation, take a common unit presentation
of all $\lambda_i$ and all relevant projective ratios.
The corner forms are pure polyhedral delta forms, their
products have no derivative-of-Dirac terms, and multiplication
restricts the scalar coefficient to each resulting carrier.
The difference scalar is zero on every such carrier.
The same argument applies after wedging with every refined
tropical cycle.  Thus (RAT3) is equality in the actual
quotient, not merely equality of pointwise integrals or
of one many-to-one pushforward.

Define the literal actual forms
\[
 \sigma=\sum_{j=1}^{s} b_{j+1}\,d''Q_j,
       \qquad \tau=\sum_{j=1}^{s} d'b_{j+1}\wedge Q_j,
       \qquad \mathcal W=\sum_{j=1}^{s} Db_{j+1}\wedge Q_j.
 \tag{RAT4}
\]
The full relative identity, with its wall error, is
\[
                    \Xi=d'\sigma+d''\tau+c\omega_0^s
                                                        +\mathcal W.
 \tag{RAT5}
\]
For proof, (RAT2)--(RAT3) give
$\Xi-cP_0=\sum_{j=1}^{s}b_{j+1}DQ_j$.
For each scalar $b$ and even-degree form $Q$,
\[
          d'(b\,d''Q)+d''(d'b\wedge Q)=bDQ-Db\wedge Q.
 \tag{RAT6}
\]
Substitution proves (RAT5), including every mixed first
derivative and every wall curvature.  If one fixed ordering
of the original pluriharmonic $a_i$ holds on the whole base,
then $Db_i=0$ and the error vanishes.  In general it must
not be dropped by differentiating a fiberwise constant-frame
formula.  The explicit wall object $\mathcal W$ is an actual
form given by finite local presentations; no topology is
assigned to it other than the actual coefficient-final one.

\subsection{One varying entry: the full wall error is zero}

Suppose now that only the first diagonal entry varies:
$\lambda_1=\lambda\in\mathcal O(Y)^\times$ and
$\lambda_2=\cdots=\lambda_r=1$.  Set
$a=v(\lambda)$, $a_+=\max(a,0)$ and $a_-=\min(a,0)$.
The order statistics are $a_-,0,\ldots,0,a_+$, so (RAT3)
gives the simpler exact identity
\[
                          \Xi=a_-\omega_0^s+a_+\omega_a^s
                              =a\omega_0^s+a_+DQ_s.
 \tag{RAT7}
\]
It includes $r=2$, when there are no intermediate zeros.
The actual corner form $Da_+$ is supported on $a=0$.
On that locus the shifted and unshifted minima agree
for every projective vector, so $v=0$.  Since $Q_s$
has the scalar factor $v$, the coefficient relation
\[
                              Da_+\wedge Q_s=0
 \tag{RAT8}
\]
holds on every actual refinement.  More explicitly,
on the unit presentation for $\lambda$ the corner
is supported on the hyperplane $a=0$; restricting
the scalar $v$ to that hyperplane gives the identically
zero function in all projective coordinates.  There
is no derivative-of-Dirac coefficient to retain.
Consequently the globally defined actual forms
\[
                     \sigma_\lambda=a_+d''Q_s,
                 \qquad\tau_\lambda=d'a_+\wedge Q_s
 \tag{RAT9}
\]
satisfy on the whole product, across the order wall,
\[
                 \Xi=d'\sigma_\lambda+d''\tau_\lambda
                                                     +a\omega_0^s.
 \tag{RAT10}
\]
No compact support, angular rectangle, fiberwise integration,
or continuity of a choice of compact primitives has been used.
The term $d'a_+\wedge Q_s$ is essential and retains the
mixed base--angular coefficients.

\subsection{Relative full-metric transgression}

Let $N$ be an analytic open in a smooth space, let $L|_N$
carry a piecewise smooth metric with curvature $R$, and
let $f$ be an $r$-tuple of analytic sections of $L|_N$.
Write $N^\circ$ for the complement of its common zero
locus.  No regularity is needed for the following
identity of actual off-zero forms.  Let $\Phi$ and
$C_0=R-D\Phi$ be its CI2 potential and projective
curvature; for $\widetilde f=(\lambda f_1,f_2,\ldots,f_r)$
write $v=\Phi_{\widetilde f}-\Phi$, $C_1=C_0-Dv$.
Pull (RAT9) back by the actual graph of the projective
section map, with base point in $N$.  Denote the resulting
forms by $\sigma_\lambda,\tau_\lambda$ again.

Define $B_j$ as in AAT15 with $C_A=C_1$ and set
\[
 \begin{split}
 \alpha&=\sum_{j=1}^{r-1}\Phi\,d''B_j R^{r-1-j}
                                                    +\sigma_\lambda,\\
 \beta&=\sum_{j=1}^{r-1}d'\Phi\wedge B_j R^{r-1-j}
                                                    +\tau_\lambda,\\
 P_\Phi&=\Phi\sum_{\ell=0}^{r-2}C_0^\ell R^{r-2-\ell},\\
 \widehat\alpha&=\alpha-a\,d''P_\Phi,\qquad
 \widehat\beta=\beta-d'a\wedge P_\Phi.
 \end{split}
 \tag{RAT11}
\]
The full metric telescoping calculation AAT18--AAT19,
which is purely a graded algebra identity, applies
unchanged to these variable scalar functions.  With
(RAT10), it gives
$G_{\widetilde f}-G_f=d'\alpha+d''\beta+aC_0^{r-1}$.
Moreover $DP_\Phi=R^{r-1}-C_0^{r-1}$ and $Da=0$.
Using (RAT6) for $b=a$ therefore yields
\[
 G_{\widetilde f}-G_f
       =d'\widehat\alpha+d''\widehat\beta+aR^{r-1}
                                             \quad\text{on }N^\circ.
 \tag{RAT12}
\]
In particular the extra correction $-d'a\wedge P_\Phi$
in $\widehat\beta$ cannot be omitted in a varying family.
The residue $aR^{r-1}$ is an actual form on all of $N$,
including the common zero locus, since $\lambda$ is
an analytic unit there.

\subsection{Finite diagonal and isometric factorizations}

An analytic diagonal matrix
$A=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_r)$
on $N$ is a product of $r$ one-entry changes.  Apply
(RAT12) successively to the intermediate analytic
tuples and add the literal identities.  This constructs
actual forms $\widehat\alpha_A,\widehat\beta_A$ on
$N^\circ$ such that
\[
 G_{Af}-G_f=d'\widehat\alpha_A+d''\widehat\beta_A
                              +v(\det A)R^{r-1}.
 \tag{RAT13}
\]
There is no ordering condition on the functions
$v(\lambda_i)$, and all crossings of their valuation
walls are included through the individual formulas
(RAT8)--(RAT12).

More generally suppose a finite factorization of an
analytic invertible matrix $A$ on $N$ is supplied in
which every factor is either an analytic-unit diagonal
matrix or an analytic matrix $U$ with
\[
 \|U(x)\|_\infty\le1,\qquad
 \|U(x)^{-1}\|_\infty\le1
                     \quad\text{for every }x\in N.
 \tag{RAT14}
\]
The latter is a universal residue-field maximum-norm
isometry, so $G_{Uf}=G_f$ literally as actual forms;
its determinant has norm one.  Ignore its zero
contribution and telescope the diagonal steps.
Formula (RAT13) follows again with displayed actual
primitives.  This factorization is a finite input:
its matrices, inverses, analytic-unit entries, and
the inequalities (RAT14) must be verified on the
whole stated domain.  No theorem supplying such a
factorization on an arbitrary analytic neighborhood
is asserted.  Maximal-pivot domains by themselves
are not a proof that different local primitives
glue through all valuation walls.

All equalities above have finite local unit
presentations and use actual operators before
restriction or refinement.  For each fixed whole
presentation and fixed factorization, the products,
piecewise affine functions, and derivatives require
one finite common refinement; full mixed coefficient
seminorms are retained.  The displayed operations
have the resulting finite-stage continuity.  No
topology on all probes, unproved all-wall strict
tower, or native/full analytic kernel comparison
is introduced.

\paragraph{A failed compact-support extrapolation.}
For variable weights it is false that the common-
inactive-coordinate argument AAT5 automatically
confines every mixed angular product to a bounded
angular box.  After omitting an entry, the two
forms can factor through different maps to a
smaller projective space, with nonzero mixed base
directions; they need not factor through one common
map.  Accordingly none of (RAT1)--(RAT14) uses a
relative compact angular homotopy or suppresses
those mixed coefficients.  The successful argument
is the finite full identity and its exact wall
cancellation, not that unsupported extrapolation.
