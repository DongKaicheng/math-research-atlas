\section{A finite block word on the reduced conormal scheme}
\label{sec:r11-reduced-conormal-word}

\paragraph{Status, authorship, and scope.}
\textbf{PROVED THIS ROUND}, pending final exact-source independent
review.  The construction contributors are \texttt{/root} and
\texttt{/root/r11\_challenge}.  The input below is a finite algebraic
word on a reduced scheme.  The output is a genuinely constructed
actual Green form on the original exact complement.  No calculus of
Mihatsch forms is imposed on that reduced scheme, and no arbitrary
analytic matrix factorization is asserted.

This module uses the precise finite-word germ-invariance theorem GIC.
In particular, the positive floor in every capped subtuple norm and
the fixed canonical two-pivot partition are part of that theorem.
Equality just of the endpoint norms would not suffice.

\subsection{Original cycle and the independently checkable certificate}

Let $X/K$ be the original smooth projective geometrically integral
variety, let $n=\dim X$, and let $n\ge r\ge2$.  Fix the original
absolute value and one actual metrized line bundle $\overline L$ of
the type allowed in frozen CI.  Let $s_0,s_1$ be regular $r$-tuples
of global sections of the same $L$, and put
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 Y_i=V(s_i),\qquad Z=[Y_1]-[Y_0],\qquad
 U=X^{\rm an}\setminus|Z|,\\
 W=X^{\rm an}\setminus(|Y_0|\cup|Y_1|),\qquad
 C_U=(|Y_0|\cup|Y_1|)\cap U.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{CBW1}
\]
Supply a Zariski open $V\subset X$ with
\[
 C_U\subset V^{\rm an},\qquad
 \mathcal I_{Y_0}|_V=\mathcal I_{Y_1}|_V=:\mathcal I.
 \tag{CBW2}
\]
The open $V$ need not be affine.  Set $Y=V(\mathcal I)\subset V$
and $Y_{\rm red}=V(\sqrt{\mathcal I})$.  The original zero schemes
and all their cycle multiplicities remain $Y_0,Y_1$, not their
reductions.  The reduction here serves only to formulate a sufficient
finite coefficient certificate.

Each regular tuple identifies the same conormal bundle with
$(L^{-1}|_Y)^{\oplus r}$.  The common scalar line-bundle frame
cancels from their change of basis.  Thus the regular-sequence
calculation in frozen ICC gives a canonical matrix
\[
 \overline M\in\operatorname{GL}_r\bigl(\Gamma(Y,\mathcal O_Y)\bigr),
 \qquad
 \overline M_{\rm red}
   \in\operatorname{GL}_r\bigl(\Gamma(Y_{\rm red},\mathcal O_{Y_{\rm red}})\bigr).
 \tag{CBW3}
\]
Explicitly, every local analytic or algebraic comparison $s_1=M_i s_0$
reduces to $\overline M$, since two comparison rows differ by a
regular-sequence relation whose coefficients lie in $\mathcal I$.

A \emph{reduced conormal block-word certificate} is the following
finite data.  In the commutative original-$K$ ring
$A=\Gamma(Y_{\rm red},\mathcal O_{Y_{\rm red}})$, supply an exact
factorization
\[
                 \overline M_{\rm red}
                           =\overline F_m\cdots\overline F_1,
 \tag{CBW4}
\]
where each factor is one of:
\begin{enumerate}
\item an elementary shear $E_{ij}(b)$ with $b\in A$;
\item one-entry multiplication $D_i(\lambda)$ with $\lambda\in A^\times$;
\item a specified $2\times2$ block in two selected rows, the identity
on the other rows, with entries in $A$ and determinant in $A^\times$.
\end{enumerate}
The word length, row labels, coefficients, unit inverses, and block
determinant inverses are part of the certificate.  Its meaning is an
identity of finite matrices over $A$, not a condition involving
existence of Green forms or actual primitives.  Execution is from
right to left in (CBW4).  A fixed signed permutation could also be
inserted with its zero native primitive; it is unnecessary for the
stated factor list.

The mathematical object does not identify different words: one
specified word is used throughout the construction.  Local lifts
of this same word are considered equivalent when their coefficient
restrictions to $Y_{\rm red}$ agree.  GIC proves the exact actual
germ compatibility for that equivalence.  No word-independence or
classification of all matrix relations is asserted.

\begin{theorem}[Original-support Green form from a reduced conormal word]
Under (CBW1)--(CBW4), there is an explicitly constructed
\[
                         g_Z\in B^{r-1,r-1}(U)
 \tag{CBW5}
\]
over the original $K$, satisfying
\[
 \int_U g_Z\wedge d'd''\eta=-\int_Z\eta
      \qquad\text{for every actual }
      \eta\in B_c^{n-r,n-r}(X^{\rm an}).
 \tag{CBW6}
\]
The cancellation set $C_U$ need not be closed in $X^{\rm an}$,
proper, or separated from the remaining support.  No literal
common tail is required of the two original tuples.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Local lifts and a final small norm-isometric error}

\begin{proof}
The quotient map $\mathcal O_V\to\mathcal O_{Y_{\rm red}}$ is
surjective as a sheaf.  Therefore all the finitely many coefficients
in (CBW4) admit simultaneous lifts on a sufficiently fine affine
open cover of $V$ near $Y$.  The unit and determinant inverses in
the certificate show that these lifted units and determinants are
nonzero at the points of $Y_{\rm red}$.  Shrink their ordinary
analytic neighborhoods so that the lifted elementary, diagonal
and two-row factors $F_{i,j}$ are all invertible.  Define
\[
                         T_i=F_{i,m}\cdots F_{i,1}.
 \tag{CBW7}
\]
This is one fixed finite word on each local neighborhood.  It has
reduction $\overline M_{\rm red}$ on $Y_{\rm red}$.

Independently choose a local exact comparison
\[
                             s_1=M_i s_0.
 \tag{CBW8}
\]
It exists by equality of the ideals.  The same elementary conormal
calculation as ICC shows that $M_i$ is invertible near $Y$.
Both (CBW7) and (CBW8) reduce to the matrix in (CBW3), so every
entry of $M_i-T_i$ vanishes on the reduced support.  Hence
\[
       H_i=M_iT_i^{-1}=I_r+E_i^{\rm mat},\qquad
                       E_i^{\rm mat}|_{Y_{\rm red}}=0.
 \tag{CBW9}
\]
After shrinking to an ordinary neighborhood $N_i\subset U$ of
the relevant portion of $C_U$, continuity gives
\[
            \max_{a,b}|(E_i^{\rm mat})_{ab}|<1
                                  \quad\hbox{on all of }N_i.
 \tag{CBW10}
\]
This is a pointwise neighborhood inequality, not a global boundedness
assumption on the original comparison matrices.  It holds at every
analytic point near the reduced zero set because the displayed
entries vanish there.  Over each completed residue field, the
ultrametric inequality gives
\[
                 \|H_i v\|_\infty=\|v\|_\infty
                                  \quad\text{for every vector }v.
 \tag{CBW11}
\]
Its inverse is equally norm-isometric.  In the common line-bundle
metric this proves literal equality of the two tuple norms, and
therefore of their complete CI expressions,
\[
                  G_{s_1}=G_{T_i s_0}
                                  \quad\hbox{on }N_i\cap W.
 \tag{CBW12}
\]
No isometry-equivariance theorem for arbitrary primitive choices is
used: only the actual CI expression, which is a fixed function of
the tuple norm and common metric, is compared in (CBW12).

Execute the lifted word in (CBW7), using SHT for each shear, the
frozen antisymmetric unit primitive for each diagonal factor, and
the fixed canonical-partition GB construction for each two-row
block.  Fix the same positive cap constants and the same pivot
partition function for every occurrence on every local lift.
Evaluate all factors on their actual intermediate tuples and sum
their finite actual primitives and their extending residues.
Together with (CBW12), this constructs
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 p_i=(p_i',p_i'')\in
 B^{r-2,r-1}(N_i\cap W)\oplus B^{r-1,r-2}(N_i\cap W),\\
 E_i^{\rm form}\in B^{r-1,r-1}(N_i),\qquad
 G_{s_1}-G_{s_0}=\partial_1p_i+E_i^{\rm form}|_W.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{CBW13}
\]
All differentiated pivot-partition terms of GB are retained in
$E_i^{\rm form}$.  It is not replaced by a determinant residue
alone.  The matrix error in (CBW9) is not confused with this
extending differential form.

\subsection{Actual germ gluing, followed by the original-support cutoff}

On an overlap, the two systems of coefficient lifts restrict to
the same prescribed word on $Y_{\rm red}$.  Their differences
therefore vanish on that reduced support.  The GIC finite-word
lemma applies to the entire original tuple $s_0$, all intermediate
invertible matrices, and all positive-floor capped subtuple norms.
For every $x\in C_U\cap N_i\cap N_j$ it proves that on one
ordinary neighborhood of $x$ the pairs $p_i,p_j$ agree literally
on its punctured part, with every actual mixed derivative.
This statement uses the fixed word; it does not infer compatibility
between arbitrary different local factorizations.

Put $N=\bigcup_iN_i$.  Choose an ordinary smooth locally finite
partition $(\rho_i)$ on $N$ subordinate to these neighborhoods,
with each support closed inside its own domain.  Closed-support
zero extension defines the locally finite actual pair
\[
                         p=\sum_i\rho_i p_i
                                   \quad\hbox{on }N\cap W.
 \tag{CBW14}
\]
The actual cross term on $N\cap W$ is
\[
       H=\sum_i\bigl(d'\rho_i\wedge p_i'
                              +d''\rho_i\wedge p_i''\bigr).
 \tag{CBW15}
\]
Near each $x\in C_U$, only finitely many supports occur.
The just-proved literal germ equalities make all their pairs equal
on one common punctured neighborhood.  There (CBW15) is zero,
because $\sum_i d'\rho_i=\sum_i d''\rho_i=0$.
Thus $H$ has a genuine actual zero extension $\widetilde H$ to
$N$; this is equality to zero on ordinary neighborhoods, not a
boundary-value or distributional extension assertion.
Set
\[
              E=\sum_i\rho_iE_i^{\rm form}-\widetilde H
                                         \in B^{r-1,r-1}(N).
 \tag{CBW16}
\]
Differentiating the full locally finite sum in (CBW14) and using
(CBW13) proves
\[
                  G_{s_1}-G_{s_0}=\partial_1p+E|_{N\cap W}.
 \tag{CBW17}
\]
Every compact set encounters only finitely many summands.  No
completion of an actual-form space is being used.

Finally take the frozen RSP cutoff $\zeta$ on the original $U$,
supported in $N$ and equal to one near $C_U$.  On $W$ put
\[
 g_W=G_{s_1}-G_{s_0}
             -d'(\widetilde{\zeta p'})-d''(\widetilde{\zeta p''}).
 \tag{CBW18}
\]
By (CBW17) this agrees with $E$ near $C_U$ and therefore glues
to the actual form (CBW5) on exactly $U$.  For every actual test
in (CBW6), the frozen support lemma places $D\eta$ compactly
inside $W$.  Actual Stokes kills the two correction pairings in
(CBW18).  Subtracting the original CI identities cancels their
identical curvature term and gives (CBW6).  The original cycle,
multiplicities, absolute value and field have never been changed.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Specializations and limits}

For $r=2$, the canonical matrix (CBW3) is itself one allowed
$2\times2$ block.  Thus (CBW2) alone supplies the certificate:
\emph{every such rank-two same-ideal CI difference has the required
original-support actual form, even when its canceled part meets
the remaining support}.  This is not a theorem for arbitrary
codimension-two homologically trivial cycles or for different
common-zero ideals.

For $r>2$, (CBW4) remains an additional finite input.  No theorem
that all matrices over $A$ admit such a word is asserted.  In the
shared-tail setting of GIC, the conormal comparison has block shape
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\overline A&\overline B\\0&I\end{smallmatrix}\right)$,
so its explicit upper shears and its one $2\times2$ block give
the certificate directly.  This is a compatibility check between
the two constructions, not a second count of that existence class.

If the reduced common support is proper and its global-function
ring is a finite product of fields, ordinary finite Gaussian
elimination over each field supplies words of this kind.  This
explains consistency with the already frozen OFC class.  That
class is not registered as a new independent result here, and no
high-order analytic form is descended from one of those fields.
The lifted coefficients in the proof are sections of the original
$K$-scheme's structure sheaf, obtained by local quotient lifting.

All operations used to construct the actual forms are finite on
each local presentation and locally finite only in ordinary sheaf
gluing.  Restrictions and refinements preserve the displayed
identities.  On each fixed compact whole test presentation, the
frozen support argument and finite mixed coefficient estimates
give the same continuous integration interface as RSP.  No
topology is placed on the set of words to infer a continuous
choice, no full inherited-kernel comparison is proved, and no
arbitrary word is identified with another one by an abstract
quotient.

General different-ideal cancellation, a general conormal block-word
existence theorem, general strong G, the entire lower-middle actual
kernel, arbitrary high-order actual descent and the additional
Beilinson--Bloch height axioms remain \textbf{OPEN}.
