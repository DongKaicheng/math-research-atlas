\section{Vertex-stable actual local witnesses for the higher-rank raw cube}
\label{sec:r11-vertex-stable-local-cube}

\paragraph{Status and scope.}
\textbf{PROVED THIS ROUND}, submitted for exact-source independent
review.  The construction contributors are \texttt{/root} and
\texttt{/root/r11\_cube}.  The theorem below constructs actual
third witnesses on a specified finite ordinary projective cover
of the entire punctured tuple domain, including proper-entry zeros.
It does not glue these witnesses across the cover.  Their higher
overlap compatibility, a whole-domain third witness, and the general
original-cycle Green consequence remain \textbf{OPEN}.

The two ingredients proved here are a directed version of SWP with
a finite tail, and a precise eventual-coordinate-independence
extension.  No local $\partial_3$-exactness theorem is assumed.

\subsection{A directed finite sweep with possibly tangent carriers}

Fix coordinates $(x_1,\ldots,x_k,z,\tau)$, where $z$ denotes a
finite list of passive real coordinates, and put
\[
 \Gamma=\{\tau>0,\ x_j> -\tau\ (1\le j\le k)\},
 \qquad w=\sum_{j=1}^k\partial_{x_j},\qquad v=-w.
 \tag{VSW1}
\]
Let $B=B(z,\tau)>0$ be a finite continuous homogeneous PL scalar.
Let $F$ be a finite native actual form, with constant tangential
coefficients on central carriers of pure codimension $c\ge1$,
and with tangential bidegree either $(0,h)$ or $(h,0)$.
Assume the entire identities
\[
 d'F=d''F=0,\qquad
 F=0\quad\hbox{on }\{x_j>B\hbox{ for every }j\}\subset\Gamma.
 \tag{VSW2}
\]
For $x\in\Gamma$ the translated point $x+tw$ remains in
$\Gamma$, and it belongs to the zero region in (VSW2) whenever
\[
                              t>B(z,\tau)+\tau.
 \tag{VSW3}
\]
This is a uniform bound on every compact parameter set and does
not depend on the values of the angular coordinates $x_j$.

Refine the finite source presentation by the walls of $B$ and
the inequalities $x_j=B$.  Delete zero coefficient carriers.
Every remaining carrier is disjoint from the open zero region:
a constant tangential coefficient which is zero on a nonempty
relative open part of a carrier is zero on that carrier.
Thus (VSW3) bounds the time support carrier by carrier as well.

For every carrier $\sigma$ not tangent to $v$, use precisely
the transverse formula SWP4--SWP5, with
$\widehat\sigma=(\sigma+\mathbb R_{\ge0}v)\cap\Gamma$,
the unique $t_\sigma$, the projected coefficient
$\widehat\alpha_\sigma$, and weight $\mu_\sigma\wedge v$.
Define the finite polyhedral expression
\[
 \mathcal S_iF=
    \sum_{v\notin N_\sigma}
       t_\sigma\widehat\alpha_\sigma
                    \wedge[\widehat\sigma,\mu_\sigma\wedge v].
 \tag{VSW4}
\]
No summand is assigned to a carrier tangent to $v$.

\begin{lemma}[Directed actual sweep]
The expression (VSW4) is actual and $D\mathcal S_iF=F$ on
the whole $\Gamma$.  It is compatible with refinement and all
native actual relations, and has polynomial coefficients of degree
one on carriers of codimension $c-1$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Use the same auxiliary distribution contractions as in SWP.
For a carrier tangent to $v$, its normal current is killed by
both contractions.  Since its tangential coefficient has only
one colour, the double contraction $i''_vi'_v$ is zero too.
The transverse carriers give exactly (VSW4), by SWP8.  Hence
the finite expression has the distributional representation
\[
        \mathcal S_iF=
               \int_0^\infty t\,T_t^*(i''_vi'_vF)\,dt,
             \qquad T_t(x)=x-tv=x+tw.
 \tag{VSW5}
\]
The time support is bounded by (VSW3) on every compact test
support.  The full Cartan calculation SWP10--SWP11 therefore
applies and gives
\[
 d'\mathcal S_iF=-\int_0^\infty T_t^*(i''_vF)\,dt,
 \qquad
 d''\mathcal S_iF=\int_0^\infty T_t^*(i'_vF)\,dt,
 \qquad D\mathcal S_iF=F.
 \tag{VSW6}
\]
It remains to check that the single-contraction integrals are
finite polyhedral forms even on the tangent carriers.

The transverse contributions are exactly SWP9.  If $v\in
N_\sigma$, translation stays inside the affine span of $\sigma$.
For a point $x$ in that span, its set of contributing times is
\[
             I_\sigma(x)=\{t\ge0:x-tv\in\sigma\}.
 \tag{VSW7}
\]
It is an interval.  Writing the finitely many linear inequalities
of $\sigma$ bounds its endpoints by maxima and minima of a finite
list of homogeneous linear functions of $x$.  By (VSW3) its upper
endpoint is finite wherever it contributes.  After one finite
polyhedral refinement its length $L_\sigma(x)$ is a homogeneous
linear nonnegative scalar, extended by zero where the interval
is empty.  The integral of the constant tangential coefficient is
therefore exactly
\[
 L_\sigma(x)\,(i''_v\alpha_\sigma)
      \wedge[\sigma+\mathbb R_{\ge0}v,\mu_\sigma]
 \quad\hbox{or}\quad
 L_\sigma(x)\,(i'_v\alpha_\sigma)
      \wedge[\sigma+\mathbb R_{\ge0}v,\mu_\sigma],
 \tag{VSW8}
\]
restricted to $\Gamma$, with the respective sign in (VSW6).
The unused contraction vanishes for a one-colour coefficient.
These are finite polyhedral forms of carrier codimension $c$;
the transverse contributions have codimension $c-1$.
Consequently both full derivatives in (VSW6) are polyhedral.
Mihatsch's exact native criterion used in SWP proves actualness.
The full distributional identity (VSW5) proves presentation and
refinement compatibility.  Its role is to verify the displayed
finite actual output, not to replace that output by a current.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Eventual coordinate independence and genuine zero-entry extension}

Say that a finite native form $\beta$ on $\Gamma$ is
\emph{eventually independent of $x_j$ with bound $B$} if, on
the entire open $x_j>B(z,\tau)$, its carrier expression has no
prime or double-prime $dx_j$ factor and all remaining coefficients
are independent of $x_j$.  Equivalently, locally in the remaining
coordinates it is the pullback of a native actual form under the
linear projection forgetting $x_j$.  For the equivalence, choose
any constant section $x_j=\gamma$ above the bound on a small
neighborhood in the other coordinates; native affine pullback is
actual, and the independence identifies the different choices.
This definition refers to the whole actual form, not to a preferred
formal lift.

If $F$ in (VSW2) is eventually independent of each $x_j$ with
this same bound, so is $\mathcal S_iF$.  Indeed, when $x_j>B$,
the entire ray $x+tw$ remains in that independence region.
The $\partial_{x_j}$ component of each contraction has no effect,
and the remaining translated coefficients are independent of $x_j$.
The common time bound (VSW3) is independent of $x_j$.  The equality
with a projected form follows in the whole distributional native
chart and hence, because both sides are actual, in native $B$.
The operators $d',d'',N,I',I''$ of SWP also preserve eventual
independence: on this open the $x_j\partial_{x_j}$ summand of
the radial contraction is zero, and all other operations commute
with the indicated projection.

Here is the exact analytic extension consequence.  On an original
$K$ analytic open, let $u_1,\ldots,u_k$ be analytic functions,
possibly zero, and let the parameters in $z,\tau$ be valuations
of analytic units, with $\tau>0$.  Suppose
\[
                  v(u_j)>-\tau,
 \tag{VSW9}
\]
where $v(0)=+\infty$.  A native actual form $\beta$ as above,
eventually independent of every $x_j$ with bound $B$, has an actual
extension from the locus where all $u_j$ are units to this entire
open, by the following explicit local rule.

At a point, let $J$ be the indices of its zero functions.
Shrink an ordinary neighborhood so that all $u_j$ for $j\notin J$
are units, the parameters and $B$ are bounded there, and
$v(u_j)>B$ for $j\in J$.  Choose nonzero constants
$\epsilon_j\in K$ with
\[
             v(\epsilon_j)>\sup B\qquad(j\in J).
 \tag{VSW10}
\]
Such constants exist with the original valuation, by powers of
any original uniformizer.  Substitute these constants for the
$u_j$, $j\in J$, in the finite native presentation of $\beta$;
all arguments are now units, so this gives an actual form on the
ordinary neighborhood.  The resulting form is independent of
the constants and of this neighborhood: eventual independence
identifies any two choices as full native forms after projection.
The same argument identifies a constant substitution with an
unmodified nonzero function whenever the latter has valuation
above $B$.  Hence the forms glue on all overlaps, including those
with zero entries.  This is a literal actual sheaf construction,
not an appeal to density of the all-nonzero locus.

The rule commutes with every finite identity among the supplied
eventually-independent forms, and with their full derivatives.
In particular it transfers identities involving SWP's radial
operators after they have been evaluated as actual forms.
It does not define a radial operation on arbitrary Berkovich
forms.  It does not modify the original tuple, ideal, or cycle;
the small constants occur only in the local presentation of the
already specified extending form.

\subsection{The full GR remainder has the required finite tail}

Take the exact three-distinct-entry raw CS cube, of rank $r\ge4$,
with original unit valuations $a,b,c$.  Put $s=r-1$,
$d=r-2$ and $\ell=r-3\ge1$.  For now give all six faces the
same positive auxiliary cap offset $\tau$ from NCP, retaining
all its derivatives.  Use exactly the signed face order
\[
 (ab;f),\ (ac;Bf),\ (bc;f),\ -(bc;Af),\ -(ac;f),\ -(ab;Cf).
 \tag{VSW11}
\]
Let $\mathcal R_f$ be the entire GR20 triple
\[
             u=\mathcal R_f=E_{\rm glob}-C_0^\ell E_{abc}.
 \tag{VSW12}
\]
It is already actual and metric-independent on the punctured
tuple domain.  We do not replace it by selected closed curvature
blocks.  All formal parameter coefficients defining $E_{\rm glob}$
remain precisely those of GR5 and GR13--GR18.

Fix an entry $i$.  On its projective chart normalize $f_i$ to
one and let $x_j=v(f_j/f_i)$ for $j\ne i$.  The native domain
is (VSW1) with $k=r-1$ and passive parameters $z=(a,b,c)$.
The eight normalized potentials are explicitly
\[
 \phi_S=\min\left(w_i(S),\ \min_{j\ne i}
                      \{x_j+w_j(S)\}\right),
 \tag{VSW13}
\]
where $w_1(S),w_2(S),w_3(S)$ are respectively $a,b,c$ when
their index belongs to $S$, and zero otherwise; all other
$w_j(S)$ are zero.  Each face cap is exactly
\[
 \Psi_\nu=\min\bigl(\psi_\nu,
       \phi_{\mathrm{base}(\nu)}+|a_\nu|+|b_\nu|+\tau\bigr),
 \tag{VSW14}
\]
where $\psi_\nu$ is the norm of that face's unchanged passive
entries with its prescribed base tuple.  Thus the entire native
presentation uses finitely many homogeneous minima and absolute
values, including every one of their walls.  Compute (VSW12)
with reference metric zero.  GR17--GR18 make the result independent
of this choice and identify it with the original $u$ on the chart.

Set
\[
       S_0=|a|+|b|+|c|,\qquad B_0=3S_0+2\tau.
 \tag{VSW15}
\]
If a single $x_j>B_0$, that entry is strictly inactive in all
eight full norms and all six capped passive norms.  Indeed, its
value after any of the changes is at least $x_j-S_0>2S_0+2\tau$.
The unchanged normalized entry $i$ bounds every full minimum
above by $S_0$, and every cap's second branch is at most
$2S_0+\tau$.  Therefore deleting entry $j$ does not change
any one of (VSW13)--(VSW14) on this entire ordinary open.
Their full mixed derivatives and every GR parameter coefficient
are unchanged there.  In particular $u$ and both of its curls
are eventually independent of every $x_j$ with bound $B_0$.

We next prove the full tail identity needed for those curls:
\[
       d''u_A=d'u_B=0\quad\hbox{where all }x_j>B_0.
 \tag{VSW16}
\]
On this open the normalized entry $i$ is the unique minimizer
in all eight norms.  Thus all their projective curvatures,
including $C_0$, are zero.  If $i$ is outside the three changed
entries, every face passive norm equals the common full potential,
and all GR normalizer differences are zero; (VSW16) follows.

Suppose, for example, that $i=1$.  The two $ab$ faces have
identical norm, cap and unit arguments, because changing entry 3
cannot affect any of them.  Their full GR residuals cancel with
their opposite signs.  The two $ac$ faces cancel in the same
way.  For each of the remaining $bc$ faces all four potentials
have the same value $\gamma$, where $\gamma=0$ or $a$, and
the cap equals that value because entry 1 is passive.
Relative to the global base potential zero, GR18 reads
\[
 \widetilde p_v=\rho\gamma\quad\hbox{for every vertex},
 \qquad
 \widetilde R=-\widetilde D((1-\rho)\gamma),
 \qquad C_v=0,
 \tag{VSW17}
\]
with $\rho=3t^2-2t^3$.  In CS7 the $\mathfrak a(p,p)$
terms vanish, while the other terms sum to zero by the full
edge-weight sum and its first derivatives.  Thus the entire
unrestricted GR11 residual for this face is
\[
             \tfrac12\mathfrak a(b,c;\widetilde R^{d}).
 \tag{VSW18}
\]
The curvature $\widetilde R$ has zero exterior-free part,
because $D\gamma=0$.  Since $d=r-2\ge2$, its $d$th power
has no single-exterior coefficient.  Hence both coefficients
$F_1^+$ and $F_2^-$ used for the $A,B$ entries of GR5 are zero.
The double-exterior coefficient is not discarded; it can contribute
to the $M$ entry and is irrelevant to this particular conclusion.
This proves that $E_{{\rm glob},A}=E_{{\rm glob},B}=0$ here.
The subtracted $C_0^\ell E_{abc}$ is zero because $\ell\ge1$.
This proves (VSW16).  Exchanging the changed index proves the
same assertion for $i=2,3$.  The cancellation is an entire
actual identity also on all $a,b,c$ sign walls.

The exact finite GR expressions show moreover that $d''u_A$
and $d'u_B$ have constant tangential coefficients of degrees
$(0,3)$ and $(3,0)$ on central carriers of codimension $\ell$.
For the first entry, the single prime parameter coefficient in
GR5 removes one curvature factor from the degree $d$ residual,
leaving $d-1=\ell$ curvature factors and two tangential
double-prime factors with one linear coefficient.  Differentiating
by $d''$ gives constant three-double-prime coefficients; a boundary
term of higher carrier codimension would have negative prime
tangential degree and is zero.  The second entry is the
opposite-colour calculation.  The term $C_0^\ell E_{abc}$
has precisely the same carrier and coefficient types.
These statements may also be read from the full CF4 curl formula;
no curvature factor is cancelled in the actual quotient.

\subsection{Actual local witnesses on the original punctured domain}

Use the directed sweep with $B=B_0$ on the two entire curls:
\[
 F_i=\mathcal S_i(d''u_A),\qquad
 G_i=\mathcal S_i(d'u_B).
 \tag{VSW19}
\]
They are actual, have the required bidegrees and Euler weights
$(1,4)$ and $(4,1)$, and satisfy $DF_i=d''u_A$ and
$DG_i=d'u_B$.  Their time support is uniformly bounded by
$3S_0+3\tau$ on (VSW1).  They and $u$ are eventually
independent of each angular coordinate by (VSW15).

Apply the explicit formula SWP17--SWP20 to $u,F_i,G_i$ in
the verified finite polynomial native carrier space.  Here
$\Gamma$ is a central conical open subdomain: the carrierwise
radial proof SWP12--SWP14 is local and applies there verbatim;
no extension of these forms to the whole half-space is required.
Denote
the resulting actual quadruple by $H_i$.  It satisfies
\[
                              u=\partial_3H_i
                                  \quad\hbox{on }\Gamma.
 \tag{VSW20}
\]
Every operation used to obtain $H_i$ preserves eventual
independence, so all four of its components have the zero-entry
extension described in (VSW9)--(VSW10).

Now choose $\pi\in K$ with $\gamma=v(\pi)>0$ and specialize
the auxiliary unit to $\pi$.  On the original $W=N\setminus
|f=0|$, define the finite ordinary opens
\[
 O_i=\{|f_i|>e^{-\gamma}\|f\|_\infty\},\qquad
                            1\le i\le r.
 \tag{VSW21}
\]
Ratios in this formula are independent of a frame and of the common
metric.  These opens cover $W$; at any point an entry attaining
the maximum belongs to its $O_i$.  On $O_i$, $f_i$ trivializes
$L$, all ratios $f_j/f_i$ are analytic, and their valuations
are greater than $-\gamma$ or are $+\infty$.
The preceding original-$K$ constant-substitution construction
therefore gives actual forms $H_i$ on all of $O_i$, not just
its all-unit locus.  It transfers (VSW20) as a literal actual
identity.  For the already defined triple $u$, the substituted
form is exactly the original GR triple: (VSW15) shows that these
small replacements preserve all eight full norm potentials and
all six capped passive potentials literally on the neighborhood,
so their complete GR parameter coefficients and mixed derivatives
are identical.  No uniqueness from a dense punctured subset is
being used.  There is no rational-point reduction or extension of
arbitrary high-degree forms from another field.

\begin{theorem}[Finite original-field local relative cube witnesses]
For every $r\ge4$, the full raw capped three-distinct-entry cube
has, on the entire ordinary opens (VSW21), actual quadruples
$\mathcal H_i\in L_3^{r-1}(O_i)$ such that
\[
       \mathcal Q_{a,b,c}(f)|_{O_i}
             =\partial_3\mathcal H_i+R^{r-3}E_{abc}|_{O_i}.
 \tag{VSW22}
\]
All original positive cap offsets, all mixed metric coefficients,
all unit-valuation walls, and all proper-entry-zero points are
retained.  The displayed extending triple is defined on the
unpunctured $N$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
First compute the global GR data using common offset $\gamma$.
With $h_0$ exactly as in GR21, GR23 gives
\[
 \mathcal Q_\gamma
       =\partial_3(T_{\rm glob}+K_{h_0}(E_{abc}))
                         +R^{r-3}E_{abc}+u.
 \tag{VSW23}
\]
Insert (VSW20), now actually extended over $O_i$.
For arbitrary original offsets $\varepsilon_\nu>0$, CT on
each of the same six faces supplies a whole-$W$ actual quadruple
$\Delta T_\nu$ with
$q_\nu[\varepsilon_\nu]-q_\nu[\gamma]
=\partial_3\Delta T_\nu$.
Thus the explicit required quadruple is
\[
 \mathcal H_i=
  (T_{\rm glob}+K_{h_0}(E_{abc})
                 +\sum_\nu\sigma_\nu\Delta T_\nu)|_{O_i}+H_i.
 \tag{VSW24}
\]
Every term is a finite actual construction on its stated ordinary
domain.  GR contains the complete metric transport; QHT is applied
only to the independently closed triple $E_{abc}$; CT contains
the full mixed parameter and cap-wall terms.  This proves (VSW22).
\end{proof}

\paragraph{What this does not yet glue.}
The opens $O_i$ cover the punctured $W$, not a supplied unpunctured
neighborhood cover of the cancellation locus in $N$.  On an overlap
$O_i\cap O_j$ the difference $\mathcal H_i-\mathcal H_j$ is
$\partial_3$-closed, but no fourth witness for that difference has
been constructed here.  Taking a partition-weighted sum would
introduce precisely those unproved higher mixed terms.
Consequently (VSW22) is not a whole-$W$ relative cube witness and
does not by itself yield the finite-cover RAG/FG original-$U$
Green construction.  It identifies a smaller, concrete remaining
task: higher compatibility of the displayed vertex witnesses,
with their actual zero-entry extensions already constructed.
The strict proper-fiber obstruction remains intact, and no general
Strong G or OPEN-KERNEL counterexample or solution is inferred.
