\subsection{The fixed whole multiplication relation}

Use an ordinary base $N$ over the original $K$, the original
metrized line bundle, a rank-$r$ tuple $g$, $r\ge3$, and
$W=N\setminus|g=0|$.  The selected block acts in rows $2,3$;
row $1$ is external, and the remaining $r-3$ rows are unchanged.
Let $A\in\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathcal O(N))$, and supply one finite
block word
\[
                      T=T_l\cdots T_1,\qquad A'=TA.
 \tag{GNT1}
\]
Its factors are row units, lower or upper shears, and the fixed
$J_{23}$.  All coefficients are original analytic functions;
the row-unit coefficients are units, and the shears may be zero
or unbounded.  The same symmetric GB partition function and
all original native caps are fixed throughout.

Let $q_T(A;g)$ and $e_T(A)$ be the exact finite multiplication
construction: for row units use GMU, for lower shears use TML,
for upper shears use G3F9, and for a $J_{23}$ factor use
$-q_J(A;g)$ from SGI, with extending error zero.  At each step
replace $A$ by its exact preceding matrix prefix; the common
input tuple remains $g$.  The resulting whole identity is
\[
 \mathcal G(A;g)+p(T;Ag)-\mathcal G(A';g)
                =\partial_2q_T(A;g)+e_T(A)|_W.
 \tag{GNT2}
\]
The extending pair depends on $A,T$ and the original metric, not
on the input $g$.  Each summand of $q_T$ is its complete specified
actual expression, including every nested CT transport and every
ordinary zero-pivot extension.

For an analytic row vector $v=(v_2,v_3)$ use the ordered external
word $V(v)=E_{12}(v_2)E_{13}(v_3)$ from ECR.  Define the following
three already constructed actual crossing triples:
\[
 \begin{split}
 x_A&=q_X(A,v;g),\\
 x_T&=q_w\bigl(vA^{-1};Ag\bigr),\qquad w=T,\\
 x_{A'}&=q_X(A',v;g).
 \end{split}
 \tag{GNT3}
\]
The first and third are the full partitioned EFC witnesses; the
middle is exactly the finite unpartitioned ECR3 algorithm for
the supplied word $T$.  Its coefficients include the entire row
vector $vA^{-1}$, without assuming either entry nonzero.

\begin{lemma}[The exact higher naturality cycle]
The whole actual triple
\[
 \mathcal N_T(A,v;g)
   =q_T(A;V(v)g)-q_T(A;g)-x_A-x_T+x_{A'}
 \tag{GNT4}
\]
satisfies $\partial_2\mathcal N_T=0$ on $W$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Subtract (GNT2) at $g$ from (GNT2) at $V(v)g$.
The actual extending errors cancel because they have precisely
the same input-independent formula.  EFC5 gives
\[
 \mathcal G(A;Vg)-\mathcal G(A;g)
  =\partial_2x_A-\mathcal P_V(v;g)
                         +\mathcal P_V(vA^{-1};Ag).
 \tag{GNT5}
\]
Since $AV(v)=V(vA^{-1})A$, ECR3 for the exact word $T$ gives
\[
 p(T;AVg)-p(T;Ag)
  =\partial_2x_T-\mathcal P_V(vA^{-1};Ag)
                         +\mathcal P_V(v(A')^{-1};A'g).
 \tag{GNT6}
\]
Use the analogous EFC5 formula for $A'$ and subtract it.
The external path pairs cancel literally with their displayed
starting tuples.  The result is
\[
 \partial_2\bigl(q_T(A;Vg)-q_T(A;g)\bigr)
                    =\partial_2(x_A+x_T-x_{A'}),
\]
which proves the lemma with the signs in (GNT4).
\end{proof}

\subsection{Whole multiplication witnesses have the required input stability}

Introduce the independent original-$K$ torus coordinate $t$ and
set $g^t=D_1(t)g$, $v^t=tv$, $\tau=v(t)$.  The exact family
\[
              \mathcal N_T^t=\mathcal N_T(A,tv;D_1(t)g)
 \tag{GNT7}
\]
is actual and closed on the analytic product, including every
prime and double-prime parameter component.  The coefficient
matrices, all their partitions and the metric are base data.
We verify the full positive tail rather than assuming one from
closedness.

The EFC terms in (GNT7) are zero in a locally uniform ordinary
positive tail by XTF4.  The unpartitioned term is also zero there:
every coefficient of its external shear is $t$ times a base
function, since the block matrices commute with $D_1(t)$.
The finite EFT6 whole-call list, applied to the word $T$, kills
its NSC, passive MX, ST, DTC, SAR and same-target terms in one
locally uniform tail.  It remains to prove
\[
 q_T\bigl(A;D_1(t)V(v)g\bigr)
                  =q_T\bigl(A;D_1(t)g\bigr)
                              \quad\hbox{in that tail}.
 \tag{GNT8}
\]

Every input-dependent scalar in the exact GMU, TML, SGI and
G3F9 formulas is obtained from finitely many block norm vertices
and capped subtuple norms.  Locally, after the \emph{whole}
coefficient-zero calls have been removed on their proved ordinary
zero neighborhoods, they have the form
\[
 \|F_jg\|,\qquad
 \max\bigl(\|P_jF_jg\|,\lambda_j\|H_jg\|\bigr),
                  \qquad \lambda_j>0.
 \tag{GNT9}
\]
Here $F_j,H_j$ are finite base analytic invertible matrices acting
only in rows $2,3$, $P_j$ is a subtuple projection, and the
positive floors $\lambda_j$ depend only on the base matrix data.
This list retains separately each old SL/SAR passive cap and
each old two-target cap.  In TML and GMU the additional common
cap has a base-only ceiling offset $(C+1)B$; in SGI it has the
base-only matrix-inverse bound SGI7.  These are also of (GNT9).
No general minimum of smooth functions or deletion of a singular
individual normalizer is used.

The nested CT terms are finite coefficient expressions in those
same cap functions.  If their two endpoint caps agree for two
inputs, the entire interpolating cap, both formal colours and
the full extracted quadruple agree.  Thus equality of the full
finite list (GNT9) implies equality of the whole $q_T$ expression,
including all nested transports and mixed metric coefficients.

Write, in the original common metric,
\[
 w=v_2g_2+v_3g_3,\qquad R_0=\|(g_2,\ldots,g_r)\|,
 \qquad M_1=\max(\|g_1\|,\|g_1+w\|).
 \tag{GNT10}
\]
On the ordinary open $\|w\|<\|g_1\|$, the first-row norms of
the two inputs agree for \emph{all} $t$ by the ultrametric identity;
all other rows are identical.  Therefore the entire list (GNT9)
agrees there, not just its full norm entries.

On $R_0>0$, put locally
\[
 c_0=\min\bigl(1,\|F_j^{-1}\|^{-1},
                         \lambda_j\|H_j^{-1}\|^{-1}:j\bigr)>0.
 \tag{GNT11}
\]
The invertible remaining-row block gives the lower bounds
$\|F_j^{-1}\|^{-1}R_0$ and $\|H_j^{-1}\|^{-1}R_0$ for its
corresponding norms.  On the whole ordinary open
\[
                         |t|M_1<c_0R_0,
 \tag{GNT12}
\]
the changing first row lies strictly below every full norm floor
and every positive capped-subtuple floor in (GNT9).  The full
list is therefore identical for the two inputs there.  The two
base opens just used cover $W$: if $R_0=0$, then $g_1\ne0$ and
$w=0$.  Local boundedness and the positive lower bound in
(GNT11) give one finite constant tail threshold on an ordinary
neighborhood of each base point.  This proves (GNT8), including
all partial zeros of $g$ and the cases $g_2=g_3=0$.

The same finite description supplies the PTH presentation
hypothesis on every bounded $\tau$-interval.  All block
coefficients are base data, every norm vertex has first row
$t h_1$ and the other rows $h_j$, and every external coefficient
is a base function times $t$.  Original-$K$ small constants handle
zero norm entries uniformly on the bounded slab using its positive
upper and lower bounds for $|t|$.  All native units are monomial
in $t$ and base units.  The previously integrated EFC terms have
the required product presentation by XTF, not by an assumption
about arbitrary analytic pushforwards.  The whole family (GNT7)
therefore satisfies precisely the final PTH hypotheses.

\begin{theorem}[Strict full external-row naturality]
The actual quadruple
\[
        H_T(A,v;g)=\Pi K_{\tau_+}(\mathcal N_T^t)
 \tag{GNT13}
\]
satisfies on the whole original $W$
\[
 \begin{split}
 q_T(A;V(v)g)-q_T(A;g)
       =x_A+x_T-x_{A'}+\partial_3 H_T(A,v;g).
 \end{split}
 \tag{GNT14}
\]
All terms retain the original full native presentations and their
ordinary coefficient-zero extensions.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The closedness lemma holds as an actual identity on the product,
and the preceding argument proves the entire locally uniform
actual zero tail.  Apply PTH19 to (GNT7).  Its section at $t=1$
is exactly (GNT4), giving (GNT13)--(GNT14).  PTH verifies actual
zero relations after every base affinoid unit refinement; no
independent movement of the base analytic units has taken place.
\end{proof}
