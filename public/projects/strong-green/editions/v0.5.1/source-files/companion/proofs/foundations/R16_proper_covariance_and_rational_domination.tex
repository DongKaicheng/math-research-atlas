\section{Proper covariance of the obstruction kernel and new rational-dominance cases}
\label{r16cov:section}

The deformation theorem for smooth closed immersions supplies the
covariance that a Chow blowup formula alone could not supply.
This section states the resulting consequences at the level of
Chow classes.  Each exact-support conclusion is obtained afterwards
from the original prescribed curved Green pair.  In particular,
cancelled polar components are not removed by an unproved analytic
extension argument.

\begin{definition}[The obstruction kernel and full homological vanishing]
\label{r16cov:definition}
For a smooth integral projective variety $X/K$, put
\[
 \mathcal K_K^r(X)=\ker\bigl(\mathfrak o_{X,K}^r:
       \operatorname{CH}^r(X)_{\mathbf Q}
              \longrightarrow H_{\mathrm{BC,act}}^{r,r}(X^{\rm an})\bigr).
 \tag{CV16.1}\label{r16cov:kernel}
\]
The degree-zero curvature map is the rank map, so that the notation
also makes sense in degree zero.  Groups in impossible Chow degrees
are zero.  Write $\mathcal V_{r,K}(X)$ for the assertion that every
geometrically homologically trivial rational codimension-$r$ class
lies in $\mathcal K_K^r(X)$, and $\mathcal V_{*,K}(X)$ if this holds
in all degrees.  For a nongeometrically integral $X$, geometric
triviality is required on every geometric component.  These are
statements about the obstruction kernel; they are stronger than
merely asserting strong G for an unspecified representative.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Projective-space push on the curvature image]
\label{r16cov:projective-push}
Let $X/K$ be smooth integral projective and
$\pi:X\times\mathbf P^N\to X$.  For every rational Chow class $a$,
\[
 I_\pi\bigl(\mathfrak o_{X\times\mathbf P^N,K}(a)\bigr)
                  =\mathfrak o_{X,K}(\pi_*a).
 \tag{CV16.2}\label{r16cov:projective-compatibility}
\]
Integration on the left is the induced map on actual Bott--Chern
cohomology, of degree $(-N,-N)$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Use the algebraic projective-bundle decomposition to write uniquely
$a=\sum_{j=0}^N h_{\rm alg}^j\pi^*a_j$.
The Chow pushforward is $a_N$.  The frozen curvature ring map and
pullback compatibility give
\[
 \mathfrak o(a)=\sum_{j=0}^N[h]^j\pi^*\mathfrak o(a_j),
\]
where $h$ is a first Chern representative of $\mathcal O(1)$.
The all-actual integration theorem commutes with $D$, hence acts on
Bott--Chern classes.  Its actual target-factor projection formula,
including negative-output degrees, gives $I_\pi(h^j)=0$ for $j<N$
and $I_\pi(h^N)=1$.  Integrating the displayed decomposition proves
\eqref{r16cov:projective-compatibility}.  This is a compatibility on
the algebraic curvature image established from the two projective
bundle formulas; it is not assumed for arbitrary proper maps.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The obstruction kernel is stable under every proper smooth-projective push]
\label{r16cov:proper-push}
Let $f:V\to X$ be a morphism of smooth integral projective
$K$-varieties, with $\dim V=m$ and $\dim X=n$.  Then
\[
 f_*\mathcal K_K^r(V)\ \subset\
          \mathcal K_K^{r+n-m}(X).
 \tag{CV16.3}\label{r16cov:proper-kernel}
\]
The assertion is understood as zero when the algebraic pushforward
has impossible dimension.  All constant-field and generic cycle
degrees are the original $K$-degrees.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Choose a projective closed embedding $e:V\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^N_K$.
The graph map $i=(f,e):V\hookrightarrow X\times\mathbf P^N$ is a
closed immersion of smooth varieties, of codimension $c=n+N-m$.
Increasing $N$ if necessary makes $c\geq1$.  The decomposition
$f=\pi\circ i$ is algebraic and projective.

For $z\in\mathcal K_K^r(V)$,
Theorem~\ref{r16gi:kernel} gives
$\mathfrak o_{X\times\mathbf P^N,K}^{r+c}(i_*z)=0$.
Apply Lemma~\ref{r16cov:projective-push} and algebraic functoriality
of proper cycle pushforward.  The resulting zero class is
$\mathfrak o_{X,K}^{r+c-N}(f_*z)$, whose degree is
$r+n-m$.  This proves the assertion, including the dimension-zero
output, where the curvature map is the rank map.

No actual form is pushed along $i$ or $f$.  The only actual
integration used is the proved projective-bundle operator.  The
closed-immersion step is the separate deformation proof about the
kernel of a Chow-to-cohomology map.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Exact support after proper push and cancellation]
\label{r16cov:push-G}
Let $f,V,X$ be as above, let $Z$ be a specified rational cycle on
$V$ with $[Z]\in\mathcal K_K^r(V)$, and put $s=r+n-m$ with
$1\leq s\leq n$.  Every specified rational cycle $W$ representing
$f_*[Z]$ has an ordinary actual strong Green form
\[
 \gamma_W\in B^{s-1,s-1}
        (X^{\rm an}\setminus|W|^{\rm an})
\]
on every compact actual complementary test over $K$.
This includes the literal cycle $f_*Z$ after collecting its
nonzero coefficients, even when components collapse or cancel.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{r16cov:proper-push} proves that the Chow class of $W$
has zero obstruction.  Choose the frozen curved Green pair for
the specified final cycle $W$ and subtract its global actual
curvature primitive.  This is the established sufficient
direction to strong G, and its complement is exactly $X\setminus|W|$.
There is no proposed removal of cancelled singularities from a
previously summed Green form.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Stability under algebraic correspondences]
\label{r16cov:correspondences}
Let $V,X$ be smooth integral projective, with $\dim V=m$, and let
$\Gamma\in\operatorname{CH}^{m+d}(V\times X)_{\mathbf Q}$.
The usual action
\[
 \Gamma_*z=(p_X)_*(\Gamma\cdot p_V^*z)
\]
sends $\mathcal K_K^r(V)$ into $\mathcal K_K^{r+d}(X)$.
The kernels therefore form a graded ideal stable under smooth
pullback, proper pushforward and algebraic correspondences.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The frozen pullback and ring properties put
$\Gamma\cdot p_V^*z$ in the obstruction kernel on $V\times X$.
Apply Theorem~\ref{r16cov:proper-push} to $p_X$.
If the product is not integral, decompose it into its disjoint
smooth integral components and sum; the proof and all native
degrees are unchanged.  The other stated stabilities are either
the same theorem or the already frozen pullback and ring identities.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A precise correspondence-generated sector]
\label{r16cov:generated-sector}
For any smooth projective $X/K$, strong G holds for every specified
cycle whose Chow class is a finite sum of correspondence images
of classes in the known obstruction-zero sectors.  These include
all geometrically homologically trivial classes on smooth projective
varieties of dimension at most three, all such one-cycle classes
on smooth projective varieties of arbitrary dimension, and all
degrees on the supplied towers and other full-kernel varieties
proved in this report.

More generally, suppose there are smooth projective $V_i$ with
$\mathcal V_{*,K}(V_i)$ and algebraic correspondences $A_i,B_i$
with the appropriate degree shifts such that
\[
                 [\Delta_X]=\sum_i B_i\circ A_i
                       \quad\text{in }\operatorname{CH}^{n}(X\times X)_{\mathbf Q}.
 \tag{CV16.4}\label{r16cov:diagonal-factorization}
\]
Then $\mathcal V_{*,K}(X)$ holds.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The first assertion follows from Proposition~\ref{r16cov:correspondences},
linearity and the exact-support sufficient direction.  In the second,
if $z$ is geometrically homologically trivial, each $A_{i*}z$ is
geometrically homologically trivial by functoriality of the geometric
cycle class for correspondences.  It belongs to the relevant
$\mathcal K(V_i)$.  Apply the same proposition to $B_i$ and use
$z=\sum_i B_{i*}A_{i*}z$, which follows from the actual Chow identity
\eqref{r16cov:diagonal-factorization}.  A decomposition only in
geometric cohomology would not suffice for this argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Ascent through a smooth blowup in a specified degree]
\label{r16cov:blowup-ascent}
Let $q:Y=\operatorname{Bl}_C V\to V$ be the blowup of a smooth
closed center of codimension $c\geq2$, with $V$ smooth integral
projective of dimension $m$.  Fix a degree $r$.
If $\mathcal V_{r,K}(V)$ and
\[
                    \mathcal V_{r-j,K}(C)
                              \quad(1\leq j\leq c-1)
 \tag{CV16.5}\label{r16cov:center-hypotheses}
\]
hold in the nonzero center degrees, then $\mathcal V_{r,K}(Y)$.
In particular, if both $V$ and $C$ have full homological obstruction
vanishing, then so does $Y$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Write $i:E=\mathbf P_C(N_{C/V})\hookrightarrow Y$ and $\pi:E\to C$.
The algebraic Chow blowup formula gives, uniquely,
\[
 z=q^*a+\sum_{j=1}^{c-1}
                i_*(\xi_{\rm alg}^{j-1}\pi^*b_j),
 \qquad b_j\in\operatorname{CH}^{r-j}(C)_{\mathbf Q}.
 \tag{CV16.6}\label{r16cov:blowup-cycle}
\]
The geometric cohomology blowup formula is the corresponding direct
sum, and the cycle-class map is compatible with these summands.
Thus geometric homological triviality of $z$ implies that of $a$
and of every $b_j$.  The hypotheses put them in their obstruction
kernels.  Pullback and multiplication by a Chern class preserve the
kernel, so each $\xi^{j-1}\pi^*b_j$ lies in $\mathcal K(E)$.
The new closed-immersion kernel theorem, or
Theorem~\ref{r16cov:proper-push}, sends it into $\mathcal K(Y)$.
The first summand is also in that kernel by pullback.  This proves
the claim.  Smooth disconnected centers are treated componentwise.

This final Gysin step is essential.  Before its proof in this round,
the dimension count in \eqref{r16cov:center-hypotheses} did not by
itself justify ascent, even when the center classes had strong G.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Rational domination in dimensions at most five]
\label{r16cov:rational-low-dimension}
Let $X/K$ be smooth projective geometrically integral.  Suppose there
are a finite extension $L/K$, a smooth projective geometrically
integral $L$-variety $V$ of dimension at most five, and a dominant
rational $K$-map $V\dashrightarrow X$.  Assume that $V$, viewed as
a $K$-variety, satisfies $\mathcal V_{*,K}(V)$.
Then $\mathcal V_{*,K}(X)$ holds.  In particular the hypothesis on
$V$ is supplied by a projective-bundle tower over a smooth projective
base of dimension at most three, in the native-field scope of the
frozen tower theorem.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Resolve the rational map over $L$ in characteristic zero by a finite
sequence of blowups of smooth centers on $V$, obtaining
$a:\widetilde V\to X$.  The map is everywhere defined, projective
and dominant.  Each nontrivial center has codimension at least two,
hence dimension at most three.  The endpoint theorems and the
one-cycle theorem give full obstruction vanishing on every such
smooth projective center, including its nongeometrically integral
components in the original $K$-theory.  Apply
Proposition~\ref{r16cov:blowup-ascent} in every degree at every step.
It gives $\mathcal V_{*,K}(\widetilde V)$.

Pull a geometrically homologically trivial class of $X$ to
$\widetilde V$.  Its obstruction is zero.  The all-degree dominant
Bott--Chern detection theorem \texttt{r16pb:dominant} then makes
the original class zero.  The conclusion is on the original
$K$-actual test space; the resolution over $L$ is regarded throughout
as a smooth integral $K$-variety, and no degree is replaced by the
number of geometric sheets.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The two outer codimension ranges under rational domination]
\label{r16cov:rational-outer}
Let $V,X$ be smooth projective geometrically integral $K$-varieties
of the same dimension $m$, and suppose $V\dashrightarrow X$ is
dominant rational.  If $\mathcal V_{r,K}(V)$ holds and
\[
                   r\leq2\qquad\text{or}\qquad r\geq m-2,
 \tag{CV16.7}\label{r16cov:outer-ranges}
\]
then $\mathcal V_{r,K}(X)$ holds.  The same statement holds after a
finite constant extension when the source hypothesis is supplied
in the original-field theory.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
In a resolution by smooth blowups, a center of codimension $c$
has dimension $m-c$.  The exceptional coefficient $b_j$ in
\eqref{r16cov:blowup-cycle} has codimension $r-j$ on it, with
$1\leq j\leq c-1$.
If $r\leq2$, each positive such codimension is at most one;
the divisor theorem applies, while a homologically trivial
codimension-zero class is zero.  This recovers the safe old
codimension-two argument at the kernel level.

If $r\geq m-2$, the dimension of a nonzero center cycle is
\[
           (m-c)-(r-j)=m-r+j-c\leq m-r-1\leq1.
\]
It is therefore a one-cycle or a zero-cycle, governed by the
new all-dimensional one-cycle theorem or the frozen endpoint
theorem.  Impossible dimensions contribute zero.  In both cases
all center hypotheses of Proposition~\ref{r16cov:blowup-ascent}
are met.  Iterate ascent, then descend along the resolved dominant
morphism using \texttt{r16pb:dominant}.  The finite-extension
version uses the same native integral statements componentwise.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Geometrically unirational varieties]
\label{r16cov:unirational}
Every smooth projective geometrically unirational variety of
dimension at most five satisfies strong G for all specified
geometrically homologically trivial rational cycles in every
codimension, on their exact support complements and against all
original actual tests.

In any dimension $m$, such a variety has this conclusion in the
ranges $r\leq2$ and $r\geq m-2$.  For a unirational sixfold the
only positive codimension not covered by this statement is $r=3$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Geometric unirationality supplies, after a finite extension, a
dominant rational map from projective space of the same dimension.
For clarity, the definition allowing a larger projective source
gives this version by intersecting a general linear subspace of
the target dimension with a dominant open of the map; the required
linear subspace and map are defined over a finite extension.
Projective space has no nonzero geometric homological Chow kernel,
so satisfies the native source hypothesis in every degree.
Apply Theorem~\ref{r16cov:rational-low-dimension} or
Theorem~\ref{r16cov:rational-outer}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Consequences for the height domain.}
For a variety over a number field, the above geometric hypotheses
hold after completion at every finite place, allowing finite local
extensions and retaining their native normalizations.  Thus on
the complete complementary homologically trivial Chow groups of
a geometrically unirational variety of dimension at most five,
the height theorem \texttt{r16h:global-height} applies without
any remaining local obstruction hypothesis.  In particular it
constructs the complete middle self-pairing
\[
 \operatorname{CH}^3(X)_{\mathbf Q,\rm hom}\times
 \operatorname{CH}^3(X)_{\mathbf Q,\rm hom}\longrightarrow\mathbf R
\]
for a geometrically unirational fivefold.  This uses the separately
proved residue, reciprocity and almost-all-place vanishing theorem,
not local G alone.

\paragraph{The remaining general problem.}
The new kernel is now stable under algebraic correspondences and
proper maps.  Nevertheless, a general homologically trivial class
has not been shown to lie in it.  In dimension four, arbitrary
codimension-two classes remain the first unrestricted case.
For rational sources in higher dimensions, arbitrary middle-degree
center classes can reappear in a resolution.  The outer-range
calculation above does not cover them.  A diagonal factorization
only in cohomology, an unspecified motivic decomposition, or an
unproved relation between homological and obstruction equivalence
cannot substitute for the Chow identities used in this section.
