\section{An actual angular transgression with one determinant residue}
\label{sec:r9-angular-aeppli-transgression}

The compact torus obstruction for a translated frame remembers
every coordinate first moment.  Those separate moments need not
survive when the angular coordinate hyperplanes are restored.
We construct an actual Aeppli transgression on the full angular
projective space and then pull it back through the displayed
section map.  Only the determinant valuation remains.  The
construction retains all mixed metric coefficients and gives
literal actual primitives, not just an equality in a quotient.

\subsection{Actual angular forms and their finite presentation}

Fix $r\ge2$, put $s=r-1$, and work on $P=\mathbf P^{s}_K$
over the original field and absolute value.  In homogeneous
coordinates $z=(z_1,\ldots,z_r)^t$, write formally
$u_0(z)=-\log\max_i|z_i|$.  This is a metric potential in
a chosen homogeneous frame, not a scalar on $P$; the actual
global form $\omega=-Du_0$ is the standard first Chern form.
For $A\in\mathrm{GL}_r(K)$ define the actual global scalar
and actual closed form
\[
 v_A(z)=-\log\|Az\|_\infty+\log\|z\|_\infty,
       \qquad \omega_A=A^*\omega=\omega-Dv_A.
 \tag{AAT1}
\]
The two homogeneous changes cancel, so $v_A$ is well-defined
on $P$.  Locally one retains the entries of $z$ and $Az$
whose norms can be maximal, takes ratios with one retained
nonzero entry, and obtains finite actual unit presentations.
The function is continuous and piecewise smooth.  Its
differentials and the displayed products are operations in
the actual form sheaf.  Set
\[
 \Xi_A=v_A\sum_{j=0}^{s}\omega_A^j\wedge\omega^{s-j}
                     \in B^{s,s}(P).
 \tag{AAT2}
\]
These objects and their equalities are taken in actual $B$,
with its coefficient-final topology, not in a completion or
an abstract current space.  All restrictions and common unit
refinements are the actual ones.

For the finite calculation, use the elementary diagonalization
EFF5,
\[
 A=U\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_r)V,
 \qquad U,V\in\mathrm{GL}_r(\mathcal O_K),\qquad
 a_i=v(\lambda_i).
 \tag{AAT3}
\]
The matrices $U,V$ preserve the maximum norm over every
completed residue field.  Consequently
$v_A=V^*v_a$, $\omega_A=V^*\omega_a$, and
$\Xi_A=V^*\Xi_a$, where the subscript $a$ denotes the
diagonal matrix.  On the complete angular torus $z_r\ne0$,
$z_i\ne0$ for every $i$, use $x_i=v(z_i/z_r)$ and put
$x_r=0$.  The entire coefficient presentation is
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 u(x)=\min_{1\le i\le r}x_i,\qquad
 u_a(x)=\min_{1\le i\le r}(x_i+a_i),\qquad v_a=u_a-u,\\
 \omega=-Du,\quad\omega_a=-Du_a,\qquad
 \Xi_a=(u_a-u)\sum_{j=0}^{s}(-Du_a)^j(-Du)^{s-j}.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{AAT4}
\]
This is one actual Euclidean delta presentation on
$E=\mathbf R^{s}$.  Its walls have rational directions
and the displayed real intercepts.  The pure corner
products have total carrier codimension $s$, hence are
finite zero-dimensional polyhedral measures; multiplication
by $u_a-u$ is legitimate actual scalar multiplication.
Coincident walls and all their actual relations are retained.

There is also a useful explicit support bound.  If
\[
 \max_i x_i-\min_i x_i>\operatorname{osc}(a)
                         :=\max_i a_i-\min_i a_i,
 \tag{AAT5}
\]
an index attaining $\max_i x_i$ is strictly nonminimal
for both $u$ and $u_a$.  Omit that entry locally for both
potentials.  The two curvature forms then pull back from
one projective space of dimension at most $r-2$, so every
mixed product of total exponent $s=r-1$ is actual zero.
The same argument applies near a vanishing homogeneous
coordinate, which is strictly nonminimal for both minima.
Thus $\Xi_a$ is zero near the coordinate boundary and its
native coefficient is supported in
\[
                         |x_i|\le\operatorname{osc}(a)
                               \qquad(1\le i\le s).
 \tag{AAT6}
\]
In particular it has one compact native presentation on
the complete angular torus, not just support in an open
set with an unproved native-membership assertion.

\subsection{The exact angular mass}

\begin{lemma}[Determinant mass]
\label{lem:r9-angular-determinant-mass}
With the original-field normalization,
\[
                       \int_P\Xi_A=v(\det A).
 \tag{AAT7}
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
It suffices by (AAT3) and actual integration under a
projective automorphism to treat a diagonal matrix.
Arrange the real numbers $a_i$ in nondecreasing order,
writing $a_{(1)}\le\cdots\le a_{(r)}$.

Fix $0\le j\le s$.  At any point in the support of
$\omega_a^j\omega^{s-j}$, let $I_a,I_0$ be the indices
attaining the respective minima in (AAT4).
The actual local dimension factorization of CI2--CI3
gives
\[
                         |I_a|\ge j+1,
                         \qquad |I_0|\ge r-j.
 \tag{AAT8}
\]
Indeed, if fewer entries are active for a given potential,
its relevant power pulls back from a projective space of
smaller dimension and is zero on a neighborhood.  The two
sets in (AAT8) have total size at least $r+1$, so they
intersect.  For an index in their intersection,
$v_a=a_i$.  For every index in $I_a$ one has $a_i\le v_a$;
for every index in $I_0$ one has $a_i\ge v_a$.
Thus at least $j+1$ weights are at most $v_a$ and at least
$r-j$ are at least $v_a$.  This forces
\[
                  v_a=a_{(j+1)}
             \quad\text{on }\operatorname{supp}
                                      (\omega_a^j\omega^{s-j}).
 \tag{AAT9}
\]
The argument includes repeated weights and coincident walls.
The products are genuine zero-dimensional polyhedral
measures in the native torus presentation, so multiplying
them by a scalar constant on their support gives that
constant times the measure; there is no derivative-of-Dirac
term or discarded normal jet here.

Each mixed product has total integral one.  Namely
$\omega_a-\omega=-Dv_a$ globally on the proper $P$,
and factorization of the difference of powers followed
by actual Stokes gives
\[
 \int_P\omega_a^j\omega^{s-j}=\int_P\omega^s=1.
 \tag{AAT10}
\]
The final equality is the standard original-field
projective calibration, also obtained by the full-tie
calculation CMC3 with all exponents one: in angular
coordinates $\omega^s$ is the weight-one point at the
origin.  Therefore (AAT9)--(AAT10) yield
\[
 \int_P\Xi_a=\sum_{j=0}^{s}a_{(j+1)}
            =\sum_{i=1}^r a_i=v(\det A).
\]
The integral units $U,V$ in (AAT3) have determinant of
norm one, and no extension or rescaling of $K$ occurs.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A displayed actual angular primitive}

There is a direct finite primitive which avoids any
compact homotopy.  For the diagonal data, keep the
ordered real numbers $a_{(1)},\ldots,a_{(r)}$ and put
\[
 \begin{split}
 Q_j&=-v_a\sum_{\ell=0}^{j-1}
                     \omega_a^\ell\omega^{s-1-\ell}
                                  \quad(1\le j\le s),\\
 \sigma_a^{\rm alg}&=\sum_{j=1}^{s}a_{(j+1)}d''Q_j.
 \end{split}
 \tag{AAT23}
\]
These are global actual forms on $P$.  The same
scalar-on-carrier identity used in AAT9 gives
$\Xi_a=\sum_{j=0}^s a_{(j+1)}\omega_a^j\omega^{s-j}$
as an actual form, not only after integration.
Since $DQ_j=\omega_a^j\omega^{s-j}-\omega^s$,
the finite formula proves directly
\[
                  d'\sigma_a^{\rm alg}=\Xi_a-c_A\omega^s.
 \tag{AAT24}
\]
Pullback by $V$ gives $\sigma_A^{\rm alg}$ with
the same identity for $\Xi_A$.  This primitive
may meet the angular coordinate boundary, which
is permitted.  All later full-metric transgression
and gluing formulas can use this finite primitive;
they therefore do not require EH/TDC compact
solvers or a topology-comparison theorem.

The diagonal construction also makes sense for
any real vector $a$, directly from the minima
in AAT4 and the corresponding piecewise smooth
metric on projective space.  No element of $K$
of a prescribed real valuation is needed for
the finite actual formulas AAT23--AAT24.  This
observation supplies the original-field interface
for lifted finite constants in CFN.

For reference, a second choice with compact
angular torus support is provided by the older
compact calculus as follows.  It is optional
for the representation theorem below.

Write $c_A=v(\det A)$ and let $\mu=\omega^s$.
In the diagonal coordinates, (AAT6)--(AAT7) show that
the literal compact Euclidean coefficient
$\Xi_a-c_A\mu$ has mass zero.  Choose one nondegenerate
compact rectangle $Q$ containing the box in (AAT6) and
the origin.  The frozen TDC9--TDC10 Aeppli solver gives
the actual compact coefficient
\[
                  \sigma_a^{\rm E}=h'_E(\Xi_a-c_A\mu),
       \qquad d'\sigma_a^{\rm E}=\Xi_a-c_A\mu.
 \tag{AAT11}
\]
Pull this coefficient back by the angular unit tuple
and extend by zero from its compact support in the
angular torus to all of $P$.  This is actual sheaf
gluing, using the compact support in $Q$; the source
coefficient is realized by the pure-torus PAB bridge.
Denote the resulting actual form by $\sigma_a$ and put
\[
                 \sigma_A=V^*\sigma_a\in B^{s-1,s}(P).
 \tag{AAT12}
\]
Since $V$ is a maximum-norm isometry, $V^*\mu=\mu$.
Consequently
\[
                      d'\sigma_A=\Xi_A-c_A\omega^s
                                      \quad\text{on all of }P.
 \tag{AAT13}
\]
This is a constructed actual primitive in one colour.
It does not require zero first moments: those are needed
for a compact $D$-primitive, whereas (AAT11) is the
mass-zero compact Aeppli step.  Its support may meet
coordinate hyperplanes of a different frame after the
projective change $V$.  This is precisely why the full
torus compact-$D$ obstruction of CMC is not contradicted.

For each fixed matrix $A$, the diagonalization, rectangle,
unit tuple, finite wall arrangement, and TDC operators
are fixed before any allowed smooth coefficients vary.
Their mixed seminorm bounds and their compatibility
with actual relations are the frozen TDC and PAB ones.
Different diagonalizations or rectangles need not give
the same $\sigma_A$; every allowed choice gives the
same literal equation (AAT13).  No canonical continuous
section on all matrices or on the whole analytic form
space is needed or asserted.

\subsection{Full metric transgression for a regular tuple}

Let now $X/K$ be smooth projective of dimension $n\ge r$,
let $L$ carry one piecewise smooth metric, and let $s_0$
be a regular $r$-tuple of global sections with zero
scheme $Y_0$.  On $W=X^{\rm an}\setminus|Y_0|$ put
\[
 \Phi=\min_i\{-\log\|s_{0,i}\|\},\qquad
 R=c_1(L),\qquad
 F:W\longrightarrow P,\qquad C_0=F^*\omega=R-D\Phi.
 \tag{AAT14}
\]
For a constant $A\in\mathrm{GL}_r(K)$ write
$v=F^*v_A$, $C_A=F^*\omega_A$, and $\sigma=F^*\sigma_A$.
Thus $C_A=C_0-Dv$ and the scalar potential for $As_0$
is exactly $\Phi+v$.  For $1\le j\le r-1$ define
\[
                  B_j=-v\sum_{\ell=0}^{j-1}
                                   C_A^\ell C_0^{j-1-\ell},
       \qquad DB_j=C_A^j-C_0^j,
 \tag{AAT15}
\]
and set $B_0=0$.  The full mixed primitives are
\[
 \begin{split}
 \alpha_A&=\sum_{j=1}^{r-1}
                     \Phi\,d''B_j\wedge R^{r-1-j}+\sigma
                      \in B^{r-2,r-1}(W),\\
 \beta_A&=\sum_{j=1}^{r-1}
                     d'\Phi\wedge B_j\wedge R^{r-1-j}
                      \in B^{r-1,r-2}(W).
 \end{split}
 \tag{AAT16}
\]
No coordinate or product assumption is imposed on the
tuple $s_0$.  Its projective morphism $F$ and all actual
pullbacks in these formulas are defined on $W$; this
does not assert that $F$ gives a faithful native torus
presentation or a proper actual pushforward.

\begin{theorem}[Actual determinant-residue transgression]
\label{thm:r9-actual-determinant-transgression}
With $G_{As_0},G_{s_0}$ the exact same-metric CI2
expressions, the forms (AAT16) satisfy
\[
       G_{As_0}-G_{s_0}
              =d'\alpha_A+d''\beta_A+c_A C_0^{r-1}
                                             \quad\text{on }W.
 \tag{AAT17}
\]
Every term is an actual form over the original field.
In particular determinant-unit frame changes have a
displayed actual Aeppli primitive on the whole punctured
domain, with all mixed metric terms retained.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The graded Leibniz rule gives
\[
 d'(\Phi d''B_j)+d''(d'\Phi\wedge B_j)
                         =\Phi DB_j-D\Phi\wedge B_j.
 \tag{AAT18}
\]
All $B_j$ have even total degree; the minus sign in
the second differential follows from $d''d'=-D$.
Since $D\Phi=R-C_0$, subtracting the sum of (AAT18)
times $R^{r-1-j}$ from the difference of the CI2
expressions leaves
\[
 \sum_{j=0}^{r-1}
       \bigl(v C_A^j+(R-C_0)B_j\bigr)R^{r-1-j}.
 \tag{AAT19}
\]
For $\Xi_j=v\sum_{\ell=0}^{j}C_A^\ell C_0^{j-\ell}$
and $\Xi_{-1}=0$, the summand in parentheses is
$\Xi_j-R\Xi_{j-1}$.  The full sum therefore telescopes
to $\Xi_{r-1}=F^*\Xi_A$, without suppressing any base,
normal, or metric coefficient.  Equation (AAT13) says
$F^*\Xi_A=d'\sigma+c_A C_0^{r-1}$.  Incorporating
$\sigma$ into $\alpha_A$ proves (AAT17).
\end{proof}

The scalar $c_A$ is explicitly computable from the given
matrix.  The primitive in (AAT16) may be constructed
from the finite actual formula AAT23, actual pullback,
and finite sheaf operations.  The compact Euclidean
homotopy and actual angular zero extension above
provide a second permissible choice.
Thus (AAT17) does not rename an existence assumption,
and it does not demand a continuous linear splitting of
an arbitrary actual quotient.

\subsection{Replacing the angular residue by an extending metric form}

For actual extension, the residue in (AAT17) need not be
made zero: it suffices to replace it by a form already
defined on all of $X$.  Define on $W$
\[
 P_0=\Phi\sum_{\ell=0}^{r-2}C_0^\ell R^{r-2-\ell}
                         \in B^{r-2,r-2}(W).
 \tag{AAT20}
\]
Since $D\Phi=R-C_0$ and both curvatures are jointly closed,
the finite telescoping identity gives
\[
                       DP_0=R^{r-1}-C_0^{r-1}.
 \tag{AAT21}
\]
There is no zero-scheme current in this equality: it is
an equality of actual forms on $W$.
Set $\widehat\alpha_A=\alpha_A-c_A d''P_0$.
Equation (AAT17) becomes the literal identity
\[
       G_{As_0}-G_{s_0}
          =d'\widehat\alpha_A+d''\beta_A+c_A R^{r-1}
                                             \quad\text{on }W.
 \tag{AAT22}
\]
The last term is the restriction of an actual global
form on $X$.  Hence no determinant-zero or equal-
determinant condition is needed merely to extend the
corrected form through a cancelled component.  The
next section localizes the two actual Aeppli primitives
and retains this extending residue separately near
each component.
