\subsection{The actual complex and all-colour insertion}

Let $W$ be an ordinary original-$K$ analytic open on which the
frozen actual calculus and ordinary smooth partitions apply.
Fix $s\ge0$.  In array order define
\[
 L_j^s(V)=\bigoplus_{i=0}^j B^{s-i,s-j+i}(V),\qquad
 (\partial_jv)_i=d''v_i+d'v_{i+1}\quad(0\le i<j).
 \tag{FHD1}
\]
A negative bidegree is zero.  Equality is equality of actual
forms, not equality in a quotient or completion.  This is the
ordering of OES; for $j=2,3$ it is $(B,M,A)$ and $(V,Q,P,U)$,
respectively, and gives the frozen two-colour boundaries.
Full anticommutation proves $\partial_{j-1}\partial_j=0$.

For an ordinary smooth actual scalar $\chi$, put
\[
 (K_j(\chi)v)_i=d''\chi\wedge v_i+d'\chi\wedge v_{i+1}.
 \tag{FHD2}
\]
The complete Leibniz rules give
\[
 \partial_j(\chi v)=\chi\partial_jv+K_j(\chi)v,\qquad
 \partial_{j-1}K_j(\chi)+K_{j-1}(\chi)\partial_j=0.
 \tag{FHD3}
\]
The second equality follows by applying $\partial_{j-1}$ to
the first, including the first equality applied to
$\chi\partial_jv$.  Thus every $D\chi$ and every mixed-colour
term is present before cancellation.  The formula is not a
replacement of the two colours by a single derivative.

Let $W=\bigcup_{i=1}^N W_i$ be a finite ordinary open cover,
and choose an ordinary smooth actual partition $(\chi_i)$ with
\[
 \sum_i\chi_i=1,\qquad \operatorname{supp}_W\chi_i
                      \text{ closed in }W\text{ and contained in }W_i.
 \tag{FHD4}
\]
All cochains below are alternating in their ordered indices;
repeated indices give zero.  Write
\[
 (\delta a)_{i_0\cdots i_{j+1}}
    =\sum_{t=0}^{j+1}(-1)^t
                 a_{i_0\cdots\widehat{i_t}\cdots i_{j+1}}.
 \tag{FHD5}
\]
Restrictions to the full actual intersections are implicit.
All sums below range over all ordered tuples, not just increasing
ones, so no factorial is inserted.

\subsection{The finite formula}

Fix $k\ge1$ and an actual $u\in L_{k-1}^s(W)$.  Supply, for
$0\le j\le N-1$, actual cochains
\[
 a_j\in\check C^j((W_i),L_{k+j}^s)
 \tag{FHD6}
\]
satisfying the displayed entire equations
\[
 \partial_ka_0=u|_{W_i},\qquad
 \partial_{k+j}a_j=\delta a_{j-1}\quad(1\le j\le N-1).
 \tag{FHD7}
\]
For exactly $N$ cover members, $\delta a_{N-1}=0$ automatically
by alternation.  More generally the same construction may be
stopped at any finite level $h$ if $\delta a_h=0$ is supplied.

Define
\[
 \begin{split}
 S_0&=\sum_{i_0}\chi_{i_0}(a_0)_{i_0},\\
 S_j&=\sum_{i_0,\ldots,i_j}\chi_{i_0}
    K_{k+1}(\chi_{i_1})K_{k+2}(\chi_{i_2})\cdots
      K_{k+j}(\chi_{i_j})(a_j)_{i_0\cdots i_j}
                  \quad(j\ge1).
 \end{split}
 \tag{FHD8}
\]
Composition acts from right to left.  Every summand is first an
actual form on its displayed overlap, and is then extended by zero
to $W$.  This extension is legitimate: the support of the scalar
or derivative factor attached to each index is closed in $W$ and
contained in its corresponding $W_i$.  No unweighted cochain is
extended through a missing or singular set.

\begin{theorem}[Displayed finite higher-witness descent]
The actual form
\[
 \boxed{\qquad
 P=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}(-1)^{j(j-1)/2}S_j\in L_k^s(W)
                 \qquad}
 \tag{FHD9}
\]
satisfies $\partial_kP=u$ on the whole original $W$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Introduce the finite actual residuals
\[
 W_j=\sum_{i_0,\ldots,i_j}
       K_k(\chi_{i_0})K_{k+1}(\chi_{i_1})\cdots
       K_{k+j}(\chi_{i_j})(a_j)_{i_0\cdots i_j}.
 \tag{FHD10}
\]
Full differentiation gives
\[
 \partial_kS_0=u+W_0,\qquad
 \partial_kS_j=W_j+(-1)^jW_{j-1}\quad(j\ge1).
 \tag{FHD11}
\]
For the second identity, differentiating the leading scalar in
(FHD8) gives $W_j$.  Move the remaining derivative through all
$j$ insertion factors using (FHD3), giving the sign $(-1)^j$,
and use $\partial a_j=\delta a_{j-1}$.  In the resulting
alternating sum, the term omitting $i_0$ gives $W_{j-1}$ because
$\sum\chi_{i_0}=1$.  Every term omitting $i_t$ for $t\ge1$
is killed by $\sum_{i_t}K(\chi_{i_t})=0$.  These identities
hold on all full intersections before their closed-support
zero extensions are summed.

The terminal residual is zero.  Indeed insert $\sum_l\chi_l=1$
and expand $\delta a_j$ to obtain the exact identity
\[
 W_j=\sum_{l,i_0,\ldots,i_j}\chi_l
     K_k(\chi_{i_0})\cdots K_{k+j}(\chi_{i_j})
                           (\delta a_j)_{l i_0\cdots i_j}.
 \tag{FHD12}
\]
The first alternating term gives $W_j$; each other term omits
one insertion index and is killed by its zero sum.  At $j=N-1$
the cochain on the right has $N+1$ alternating indices and hence
is zero.  Finally the coefficients
$c_j=(-1)^{j(j-1)/2}$ obey
$c_{j-1}+(-1)^jc_j=0$.  All adjacent residuals in (FHD11)
therefore cancel in (FHD9), including the terminal one, and the
remaining term is $u$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The exact four-vertex cube interface}

For the rank-four cube put $s=3$, $k=3$ and use its four ordinary
vertex opens.  The required data are exactly
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 H_i\in L_3^3(W_i),\quad S_{ij}\in L_4^3(W_{ij}),\quad
 T_{ijk}\in L_5^3(W_{ijk}),\quad V_{ijkl}\in L_6^3(W_{ijkl}),\\
 \partial_3H_i=u,\qquad
 \partial_4S_{ij}=H_j-H_i,\qquad
 \partial_5T_{ijk}=S_{jk}-S_{ik}+S_{ij},\qquad
 \partial_6V_{ijkl}=T_{jkl}-T_{ikl}+T_{ijl}-T_{ijk}.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{FHD13}
\]
The full global witness is
\[
 \begin{split}
 H={}&\sum_i\chi_iH_i
       +\sum_{i,j}\chi_iK_4(\chi_j)S_{ij}\\
     &-\sum_{i,j,l}\chi_iK_4(\chi_j)K_5(\chi_l)T_{ijl}\\
     &-\sum_{i,j,l,m}\chi_iK_4(\chi_j)K_5(\chi_l)
                                      K_6(\chi_m)V_{ijlm}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{FHD14}
\]
Then $\partial_3H=u$.  In particular the fourth-overlap term
has a minus sign.  Omitting it would leave the full $W_2$
residual from (FHD10), not a justified zero.  The negative
bidegree convention leaves $L_6^3=B^{0,0}$ in its central array
slot and $L_7^3=0$; no further cover term is needed because
there are only four vertex indices.

If $u$ is the exact GR remainder and all of (FHD13) have actually
been constructed, substituting (FHD14) into GR's full identity
gives the whole-domain relative cube with its displayed extending
cubic error.  This is a certificate implication.  The VSW local
witnesses alone do not supply $S,T,V$, and the vanishing of
projective periods alone does not supply them either.  The further
finite-path-to-original-$U$ Green bridge remains exactly the
separately stated frozen FG/RSP interface; it is not automatic
from the formal definition of the cochains.
