\subsection{The two Galois-cohomology weights}

For $W_t=H^{2t-1}(Y_{\bar L},\mathbf Q_\ell(t))$, the center weight
is $-1$. The cited monodromy theorem identifies the filtration with
weight--monodromy in every relevant degree. In a string of length
$j+1$, its invariant bottom subquotient has weight $-1-j$ and its
coinvariant top subquotient has weight $-1+j$. This yields weights
at most $-1$ on $\ker N=W_t^{I_L}$.

The second term requires the twist that TDR2 retains:
\[
 H^1(I_L,W_t)=\operatorname{coker}(N:W_t\longrightarrow W_t(-1)).
\]
Its subquotient weights are $1+j$, hence strictly positive. Both
Frobenius invariants and coinvariants vanish on the first term; the
Frobenius invariants vanish on the second. The inertia--residue
Hochschild--Serre exact sequence therefore gives $H^1(L,W_t)=0$,
and the first term also gives $W_t^{G_L}=0$. No Frobenius
semisimplicity, weight-zero invariant exactness, or Liu condition N3
is used. The argument needs only the filtration with the indicated
pure subquotients, so it does not silently choose a Frobenius-stable
splitting of every monodromy string.
