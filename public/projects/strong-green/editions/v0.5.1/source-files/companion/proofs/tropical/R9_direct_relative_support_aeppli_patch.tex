\section{Aeppli repairs on the exact complement, including meeting components}
\label{sec:r9-relative-support-aeppli-patch}

A compact global cutoff separating cancelled and remaining
components does not exist in the meeting example MCF.
The actual Aeppli correction needs less.  Its cutoff may
live only on the original complement $U$: differentiated
global tests already have compact support away from the
entire zero union.  This observation gives a genuine
original-support gluing theorem, not merely a Green pair
with an unresolved global curvature.

\subsection{A relative actual cutoff}

Retain the original common-line-bundle CI data and set
\[
 S=|Y_0|\cup|Y_1|,\quad T=|[Y_1]-[Y_0]|,\quad
 U=X^{\rm an}\setminus T,\quad V=X^{\rm an}\setminus S,
                         C_U=S\cap U.
 \tag{RSP1}
\]
The set $C_U$ is closed in $U$; it need not be compact
and its closure in $X^{\rm an}$ may meet $T$.
Let $N\subset U$ be an analytic open containing $C_U$.
There is an ordinary smooth scalar $\chi$ on $U$ with
\[
            0\le\chi\le1,\qquad
            \chi=1\text{ near }C_U,\qquad
            \operatorname{supp}_U\chi\subset N.
 \tag{RSP2}
\]
Here the support is closed in $U$; it is not asserted
compact or bounded away from $T$ in $X$.

For completeness this is the usual locally finite
version of the smooth cutoff construction already
used in the frozen CI proof.  The analytification of
a finite-type variety over the fixed local field has
a second-countable locally compact Hausdorff topology;
its open $U$ is paracompact.  Refine the cover
$\{N,U\setminus C_U\}$ to a locally finite cover
with relatively compact members, choose the local
smooth bumps on a shrinking of that cover, and
normalize their locally finite positive sum.
Sum the normalized functions assigned to $N$.
The union of their supports is closed in $U$ and
contained in $N$ by local finiteness.  The supports
of the other functions form a closed set disjoint
from $C_U$, so the sum equals one on a neighborhood
of $C_U$.  Every compact subset meets only finitely
many terms; these are actual smooth presentations
over the original $K$, not an infinite sum in a
completed form space.

\subsection{The local-to-original-support actual bridge}

Let $g=G_{s_1}-G_{s_0}$ be the raw CI difference on $V$.
Suppose actual forms are supplied on the indicated
domains,
\[
 \alpha\in B^{r-2,r-1}(N\cap V),\quad
 \beta\in B^{r-1,r-2}(N\cap V),\quad
                   E\in B^{r-1,r-1}(N),
 \tag{RSP3}
\]
with the literal actual identity
\[
                   g=d'\alpha+d''\beta+E
                                         \quad\text{on }N\cap V.
 \tag{RSP4}
\]
The theorem below is a gluing interface.  It is useful
only when (RSP3)--(RSP4) are proved by explicit formulas;
RAT supplies such formulas for the verified finite
frame factorizations in the next subsection.

Extend $\chi\alpha,\chi\beta$ by zero from $N\cap V$
to $V$, and denote the resulting actual forms by
$\widetilde\alpha,\widetilde\beta$.  This is legitimate
local sheaf gluing because the closed $U$-support of
$\chi$ is contained in $N$.  Define
\[
                    g'=g-d'\widetilde\alpha-d''\widetilde\beta
                                                    \quad\text{on }V.
 \tag{RSP5}
\]
On a neighborhood of $C_U$ where $\chi=1$, (RSP4)
makes $g'=E$.  Thus $g'$ glues with $E$ to an actual
$g_Z\in B^{r-1,r-1}(U)$.

For every actual $\eta\in B_c^{q,q}(X^{\rm an})$,
$q=n-r$, the frozen support lemma gives
\[
                         K_\eta=\operatorname{supp}D\eta\Subset V.
 \tag{RSP6}
\]
Both $\widetilde\alpha\wedge D\eta$ and
$\widetilde\beta\wedge D\eta$ have compact support
in $V$, even when $\chi$ does not extend across $T$.
Actual Stokes and $d'D\eta=d''D\eta=0$ yield
\[
 \int_Vd'\widetilde\alpha\wedge D\eta=0,
                   \qquad
 \int_Vd''\widetilde\beta\wedge D\eta=0.
 \tag{RSP7}
\]
The full actual CI8 identity for the original tuples,
followed by (RSP5)--(RSP7), therefore proves
\[
                         \int_Ug_Z\wedge D\eta=-\int_Z\eta.
 \tag{RSP8}
\]
No test was replaced by an ordinary smooth one.  The
cutoff was never multiplied into $\eta$ to make an
asserted global test $\chi\eta$; that would in general
be invalid here.  Only the already supported $D\eta$
is multiplied into the local primitives.  This is
the precise distinction from the failed global
cutoff strategy in MCF and from the global-curvature
pair construction ISC.

\subsection{A finite actual frame-factorization criterion}

Suppose on the open $N$ in (RSP1)--(RSP2) there is a
displayed analytic invertible matrix $A$ with
\[
                             s_1=A s_0\quad\text{on }N,
 \tag{RSP9}
\]
and a finite factorization of $A$ into the analytic-
unit diagonal and pointwise maximum-norm isometric
factors in RAT14.  Equality in (RSP9) is an actual
identity of analytic sections of the same $L$,
not just equality of cycle multiplicities.  These
data give equal zero ideals on $N$, without requiring
that the ideals agree at the omitted intersection
points belonging to $T$.

Apply RAT13 to the section tuple on $N$.  It gives
the explicit actual data
\[
 \alpha=\widehat\alpha_A,\qquad
 \beta=\widehat\beta_A,\qquad
                      E=v(\det A)R^{r-1}
 \tag{RSP10}
\]
satisfying (RSP3)--(RSP4).  The residue extends to
all of $N$ because $\det A$ is an analytic unit
there.  Thus (RSP5) and actual gluing construct a
strong Green form (RSP8) on the original exact
complement, even when cancelled components meet
the remaining support in $X$.

This is a sufficient theorem with finite analytic
input, not a restatement of existence of $g_Z$:
the matrix, its factorization and inverse norm
inequalities are supplied analytic data; RAT9--RAT13
are finite explicit formulas for the primitives;
RSP5 is the actual Green form.  In particular a
globally displayed diagonal comparison by analytic
units always satisfies the factorization hypothesis.
There is no assumed compact primitive or actual
descent of a form obtained over another field.

The local presentations of all new forms are the
finite scalar and corner presentations in RAT,
their actual section-map pullbacks, and the
locally finite ordinary smooth presentations of
$\chi$.  Every compact set meets only finitely
many latter charts.  All identities commute with
actual refinements.  The resulting actual $g_Z$
defines a full coefficient-final continuous
functional on tests of degree $(q+1,q+1)$ by
uniform support avoidance for the original $T$;
no new growth condition is imposed near $T$.

\subsection{A repaired meeting-component family in every dimension}

Take the exact MCF data on
$X=\mathbf P^{r+1}\times(\mathbf P^1)^{n-r-1}$,
$n\ge r+1$, with original common $\mathcal O(2)$
tuples
\[
 s_0=(X_0X_1,X_2^2,\ldots,X_r^2),\qquad
 s_1=(X_1(X_0-X_{r+1}),X_2^2,\ldots,X_r^2).
 \tag{RSP11}
\]
Their cycles, regularity and all multiplicities are
exactly MCF4.  The original remaining support is
$T=D_0\cup D_1$, whereas $C_0\setminus T$ is the
cancelled part inside $U$ and accumulates at $T$.
Set
\[
 N=U\cap\{X_0\ne0,\ X_0-X_{r+1}\ne0\},\qquad
             \lambda=\frac{X_0-X_{r+1}}{X_0}.
 \tag{RSP12}
\]
This analytic open contains every point of
$C_0\setminus T$.  The ratio is an analytic unit
on $N$, and there is the exact identity
\[
                      s_1=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,1,\ldots,1)s_0
                                                     \quad\text{on }N.
 \tag{RSP13}
\]
The rank-one relative transgression RAT11--RAT12,
the $U$-only cutoff RSP2, and the formula RSP5
therefore give an actual strong Green form for
the original cycle on its exact complement.
Its local extending value near the cancelled
part is $v(\lambda)R^{r-1}$, with the complete
mixed corrections retained in (RAT11).

This repairs the given quadratic tuples across
the meeting cancelled component.  It does not
contradict MCF6: the cutoff is a scalar on $U$,
not a global scalar on $X$ vanishing near $T$.
It also does not contradict any compact native
torus obstruction: the Aeppli primitives need
neither compact angular support nor extension
through the remaining cycle.  Frozen MCF8 already
gave this particular original cycle another
solution via linear tuples.  The new result is
the relative actual repair mechanism and its
finite-factorization theorem, not a new existence
claim for that previously solved example.

For an arbitrary matrix comparison on a general
analytic neighborhood, a single verified finite
factorization as in RAT14 is still a substantive
input.  Different Gaussian-elimination domains
are not automatically a compatible gluing of
the displayed primitives.  Arbitrary cancelled
cycle multiplicities also do not imply (RSP9).
Accordingly general strong G, full lower-middle
OPEN-KERNEL, general actual descent, and complete
unconditional BB height remain open.
