\subsection{A second genuine product parameter}

Use the original data $(S,v,g)$ of EFC on an ordinary base $N$,
and write $W=N\setminus|g=0|$.  Let $t$ be an independent original-$K$
torus coordinate, $\tau=v(t)$, and define the family by applying the
\emph{same entire EFC construction} over the base $W\times\mathbf G_m$:
\[
       q_X^t=q_X(S,tv;D_1(t)g).
 \tag{XTF1}
\]
Its internal PTH construction uses a second independent torus
coordinate $s$, with $\sigma=v(s)$.  Its full closed triple family
is exactly
\[
 \mathcal T^{s,t}
       =\mathcal T(S,st v;D_1(st)g)
       =m^*\mathcal T^\rho,
 \qquad m(t,s)=st.
 \tag{XTF2}
\]
The final notation on the right denotes the original EFT family with
parameter $\rho$.  This equality is an equality of actual forms on
the two-torus product, including all mixed parameter components.
It follows by applying the explicit native formulas and actual
analytic pullback to each coefficient, norm vertex, cap and metric
term.  Every parameter unit is still a base unit times a monomial
in $s,t$; no unit $1+st a$ is introduced.

The corresponding quadruple appearing in (XTF1) is therefore
\[
          H^t=\Pi_s K_{\sigma_+}(\mathcal T^{s,t}),
                    \qquad \sigma_+=\max(\sigma,0).
 \tag{XTF3}
\]
This is PTH over the actual base $W\times\mathbf G_m_t$, not an
unproved interchange of two analytic pushforwards.

\begin{theorem}[Hereditary product presentation and actual zero tail]
On every bounded $\tau$-interval, the whole family $q_X^t$ has a
finite product presentation in base units and $t$.  For every
$x\in W$ there are an ordinary neighborhood $O_x$ and a constant
$T_x$ such that
\[
              q_X^t=0\quad\hbox{on }O_x\times\{\tau>T_x\}
                          \text{ as a whole actual triple}.
 \tag{XTF4}
\]
The statement retains zeros of all external coefficients and partial
zeros of $g$, not just a strict Gauss chamber.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Choose the ordinary neighborhood and constant from the complete EFT
tail proof.  If $\tau>T_x$ and $\sigma\ge0$, then
$v(st)=\tau+\sigma>T_x$, so (XTF2) is the actual zero form there.
It and all its actual differentials vanish on this open.  If
$\sigma<0$, the full operator $K_{\sigma_+}$ is zero.  Thus the
whole integrand in (XTF3) is zero, including the common wall
$\sigma=0$; near that wall the input is already zero.  PTH's
actual-zero preservation gives $H^t=0$ on that whole tail.
The two chart crossing triples $q_\alpha^t,q_\gamma^t$ are zero
there by EFT6--EFT8.  The EFC4 formula, with its full partition
correction, proves (XTF4).

For the presentation assertion, fix a bounded $\tau$-interval $I$.
The locally uniform EFT bound lets one choose a finite upper
$\sigma$ cutoff larger than $T_x-\inf I$, with a positive buffer.
All positive-$\sigma$ derivatives of this cutoff lie in the actual
zero region of (XTF2).  On the resulting compact rectangle the
monomial two-parameter formula has one finite native polyhedral
presentation, with both real intercept directions and all mixed
jets retained.  Native projection in the $\sigma$ coordinate gives
a finite native form in the base and $\tau$ coordinates.  PTH's
all-base-refinement proof identifies it with the actual form (XTF3),
independently of the local cutoff and presentation.  The other terms
of EFC4 already have the required finite product presentation.
This proves the theorem without an unrestricted parameter-section
or pushforward assertion.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The resulting exact reduction for the three Gaussian faces}

\[
                 M^t=M D_1(t^{-1}),\qquad f^t=D_1(t)f.
 \tag{XTF5}
\]

 \tag{XTF6}

and every external row vector used in G3F13, G3F20 and G3F24 is

\subsection{A safe strong-cap invariance for an individual Steinberg call}

There is a useful literal normalization fact, weaker than changing
the passive block.  For the exact ST ten-edge loop ZDS6, let
$\psi$ be its original unchanged $r-2$-entry passive potential,
let $\Phi_f$ be its original initial potential, and let
$\kappa_{\rm ST}$ be ZDS12.  Supply two finite actual ceilings
$b_0,b_1$ such that
\[
       b_j>\Phi_f+\kappa_{\rm ST},\qquad
                  \Psi_j=\min(\psi,b_j)\quad(j=0,1).
 \tag{XTF9}
\]
The full ZDS21 triples, including their complete MT terms, then
satisfy the literal actual equality
\[
                       q_{\rm ST}[\Psi_0]=q_{\rm ST}[\Psi_1].
 \tag{XTF10}
\]
To prove it, use the two ordinary opens
$\{\psi<\min(b_0,b_1)\}$ and
$\{\psi>\Phi_f+\kappa_{\rm ST}\}$.
They cover by the strict inequalities (XTF9).  On the first,
both caps are literally $\psi$.  On the second, ZDS14 pairs the
entire ten-edge loop into inverse edges before any normalization,
so the complete companion and every MT summand vanish for
\emph{any} common normalization.  Thus both triples are zero
there.  The same inverse-edge argument gives both whole
coefficient-zero extensions in ZDS7.  This proves (XTF10) on
the whole stated ordinary domain, including all mixed jets.

For a finite collection with one and the same original passive
potential, a supplied common ceiling above all of its finitely
many $\Phi_f+\kappa_{\rm ST}$ bounds is therefore allowed.
The minimum in (XTF9) must have its finite actual norm/valuation
presentation, just as in ST; arbitrary minima of smooth functions
are not introduced.  The assertion is \emph{not} a free
normalization theorem for ST, a degree-one residual cancellation,
or permission to replace its passive tuple.  In particular the
three outside ST calls in the Gaussian faces have, at their actual
starting tuples, respective external passive entries
$(Mf)_2,(Mf)_3,(Mf)_3$.  Equality of these different passive
potentials is not automatic.  This is why (XTF10) alone does not
settle the four-item expression above.

\paragraph{Precise unresolved tail statement.}
No positive-tail vanishing or unpunctured extension of the
remaining four-item expression has been proved here.  In particular,
outside pivot and CS witnesses contain diagonal weights such as
$v(d)-\tau$, and their cap floors also vary with that weight.
The block-multiplication witnesses see a shrinking \emph{passive}
row; equality of their full norms alone does not control every
capped subtuple.  These terms cannot be declared zero from
(XTF7), and a three-active ST witness cannot be transported by
the two-target TCT theorem without a new proof.

If an explicit entire product-presented family of extending closed
triples $B^t$ on $N_{adg}\times\mathbf G_m$ were constructed so
that $\mathcal C^t_{adg}-B^t|_W$ had a locally uniform actual
positive zero tail, PTH would give the precise relative third
witness.  This is a concrete remaining target, not an assumption
made by the present result.  The nonmonomial-parameter section
counterexample PTH-NM is not bypassed: every parameter used here
is genuinely monomial, but the necessary remaining tail condition
still has to be established.
