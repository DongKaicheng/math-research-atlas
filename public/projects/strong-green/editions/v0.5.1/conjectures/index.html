The conjectural programme

Four families of questions.

The local construction, global assembly, height structure, and arithmetic finiteness are connected by precise reductions. This page explains that structure; the papers give the formal statements and their context.

Conjecture family

Strong G: actual local Green forms

Construct a zero-curvature Green form for a homologically trivial cycle on its prescribed support complement, over the original non-Archimedean field.

OpenSTRONG-G

Strong zero-curvature Green forms

Ordinary actual forms; every compact complementary actual test; the original absolute value and full finite-extension degree.

  1. Obstruction problem

    The original cycle source

    Under the detector and reconstruction hypotheses, annihilate the image of the original Chow source in the residual period dual.

    Vanishing on the original Chow sourceOpen

  2. Analytic prerequisites

    Actual realization

    Verify finite traces and exact-support Green pairs, and construct the required primitives in the full actual complex.

    Finite traces and exact-support Green pairsOpenTropical realization on the full actual test spaceOpen

What follows, and with which inputs

The recorded analytic package turns source-obstruction vanishing into the exact-support Green identity. Algebraic equivalence and abelian Chow retractions are two conditional routes for particular cycle sources.

The remaining step

The general source-image vanishing theorem and the version-bound certification of its actual analytic interfaces.

Conjecture family

Canonical global height pairings

Assemble the local theory into a canonical Beilinson–Bloch pairing on the complete complementary homologically trivial Chow groups.

OpenBB-GLOBAL

A canonical global Beilinson–Bloch pairing

The global sum, normalization, correspondence adjunction, and independence of representatives belong to the construction.

  1. Intermediate domain

    The admitted global kernel

    H16 assembles the pairing on the intersection of local obstruction kernels. A fixed FD17 witness is an additional finite-detector presentation. Vanishing on localized global classes in both complementary degrees enlarges the domain.

    H16 on its fixed global kernel domainConditionalA curve-normalized height systemConditional

  2. Further research questions

    Metrics and variation

    Compare admissible corrections at bad reduction, then identify the geometric input for height domination and boundary estimates in families.

    Bad-reduction metric compatibilityOpenHeight domination and boundaries in familiesOpen

What follows, and with which inputs

Source-obstruction vanishing supplies strong Green forms. With the principal-push and assembly hypotheses, strong Green forms in complementary degrees give the global pairing. The weaker target of vanishing only on localized global classes is useful in its own right.

The remaining step

A complete source-bound assembly/comparison theorem on the desired domain. Positivity and finiteness are separate arithmetic targets below.

03

Conjecture family

Lefschetz theory and central positivity

Reduce the structure of a pointed product of curves to its odd central Chow components, the algebraic Abel–Jacobi kernel, and the residual height.

OpenOPEN-OFFCENTRAL

Off-central Chow vanishing

All subproducts and finite extensions, with the fixed anchored product polarization. Height statements use H1–H4.

  1. Independent central assertion

    The algebraic radical

    Ask that the rational Abel–Jacobi map be injective on the central algebraically trivial part.

    Central algebraic Abel–Jacobi injectivityOpen

  2. Consequences with central positivity and H1–H4

    Primitive height inequalities

    The central-block identity propagates positivity to primitive height forms and their inequalities. Residual positivity is grouped below under the stronger Northcott conjecture.

    The primitive sum-of-squares identityConditional

What follows, and with which inputs

Off-central vanishing is equivalent to arithmetic hard Lefschetz. With H1–H4, algebraic AJ injectivity and residual positivity supply full central positivity; adding off-central vanishing gives primitive positivity and complementary nondegeneracy.

The remaining step

Chow-level off-central vanishing, the algebraic AJ kernel, and residual real positivity are distinct assertions. The equivalence theorem organizes them without assuming their truth.

04

Conjecture family

Northcott and arithmetic detection

Prove finiteness of classes in the projected integral image when the field degree and the residual quadratic height are bounded.

OpenOPEN-RESIDUAL-NC

Residual integral Northcott

Count classes in the fixed rational quotient, using images of integral cycles and controlled denominators.

  1. A necessary consequence of Northcott

    Real residual positivity

    Finiteness of integral height balls forces positive definiteness after real scalar extension over every fixed number field. This weaker statement is part of the Northcott conjectural hierarchy, not a second independent finiteness claim.

    Real positivity on the residual Griffiths quotientOpenNorthcott forces real positivityInternal proof available

  2. Detection and discreteness

    Over a fixed number field

    Scalar-extended regulator injectivity gives a finite-rank route. A common rational lattice, or a genuine height gap with finite Kummer quotients, supplies integral control.

    Residual rational detectionOpenResidual integral discretenessOpen

  3. Additional field control

    As the field varies

    Bound ramification of the minimal fields of bounded-height classes. A discriminant–height inequality is a stronger quantitative question.

    Ramification of residual class fieldsOpenA quantitative discriminant boundOpen

What follows, and with which inputs

Residual Northcott implies real positivity. Conversely, real positivity plus an integral lattice gives fixed-field Northcott; fixed-field Northcott over all finite fields plus class-field ramification control gives bounded-degree Northcott.

The remaining step

One-prime detection and separate local lattices do not supply a common denominator. Fixed-field arithmetic alone does not control the varying class fields.
