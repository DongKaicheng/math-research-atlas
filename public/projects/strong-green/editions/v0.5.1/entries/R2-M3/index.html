Normalized Determinants and Height Metrics

Statement

Let C\mathcal CC be a smooth proper geometrically connected curve over a complete DVR, with a section, and let its Jacobian be an abelian scheme. Use the absolute value inducing this DVR valuation. For a perfect complex KKK on C\mathcal CC with generic virtual rank zero and determinant degree zero, the origin-normalized determinant model metric agrees with the canonical rigidified Poincaré metric. Any ambient derived pushforward is assumed perfect.

Argument

On the generic fibre the rigidified determinant and Poincaré lines agree. Their model quotient is represented by a vertical divisor. The special fibre of the abelian scheme is integral, so this divisor is a multiple of that fibre. The integral zero-section rigidification forces the multiple to vanish. Normality extends the resulting isomorphism, and equality of model metrics follows.
