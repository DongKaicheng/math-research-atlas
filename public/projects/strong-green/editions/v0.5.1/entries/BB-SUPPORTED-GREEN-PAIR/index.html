Green pairs on the prescribed support complement

A supported model complex supplies the proposed curved Green identity before curvature is removed.

Local Green Forms and Chow Correspondences

Statement

For a specified rational codimension-rrr cycle ZZZ, the draft constructs an ordinary actual form GZG_ZGZ​ on UZU_ZUZ​ and a global separately closed actual form ΩZ\Omega_ZΩZ​ of bidegree (r,r)(r,r)(r,r), with

∫UZGZ∧Dη+∫Zanη=∫XanΩZ∧η \int_{U_Z}G_Z\wedge D\eta+\int_{Z^{\mathrm{an}}}\eta =\int_{X^{\mathrm{an}}}\Omega_Z\wedge\eta ∫UZ​​GZ​∧Dη+∫Zan​η=∫Xan​ΩZ​∧η

for every compact actual test of bidegree (n−r,n−r)(n-r,n-r)(n−r,n−r). The residue is the prescribed cycle with its rational coefficients, and the complement is precisely UZU_ZUZ​.

Draft proof route

Resolve the components of the prescribed cycle by supported model complexes. Their localized leading characters fix the cycle coefficients. Relative line diagonalization and a smooth finite-flat multisection over the entire prescribed complement reduce the construction to PL scalar–curvature expressions. Apply the restricted trace and scalar Poincaré–Lelong, then sum the component constructions with the original coefficients.

The source below contains the expanded argument and its hypotheses.

Proof programme

Check finite flatness over the whole open complement, localized multiplicities, and convergence and adjunction for the full actual test class.
