Removing an exact global curvature

A formal correction preserves the original support complement.

v0.5.1 · Local Green forms

Local Curvature Obstructions and Beilinson–Bloch Height PairingsLocal Green Forms and Chow Correspondences

Statement

Assume a Green pair (GZ,ΩZ)(G_Z,\Omega_Z)(GZ​,ΩZ​) as in BB-SUPPORTED-GREEN-PAIR and a global actual form Ψ\PsiΨ with DΨ=ΩZD\Psi=\Omega_ZDΨ=ΩZ​. Then

γZ=GZ−Ψ∣UZ \gamma_Z=G_Z-\Psi|_{U_Z} γZ​=GZ​−Ψ∣UZ​​

satisfies the zero-curvature identity of STRONG-G on the exact same UZU_ZUZ​. In particular, the obstruction construction and (A2) imply this conclusion whenever oX,Kr([Z])=0\mathfrak o^r_{X,K}([Z])=0oX,Kr​([Z])=0.

Motivation

This is the formal implication from zero obstruction to a strong Green form. The existence of the input Green pair remains its separate analytic dependency.

Argument

Actual Stokes for the global form gives ∫XΨ∧Dη=∫XDΨ∧η=∫XΩZ∧η\int_X\Psi\wedge D\eta=\int_XD\Psi\wedge\eta=\int_X\Omega_Z\wedge\eta∫X​Ψ∧Dη=∫X​DΨ∧η=∫X​ΩZ​∧η. Subtract this equality from the curved Green identity. The right-hand side cancels. Since Ψ\PsiΨ is global, its restriction removes no additional points from the domain of GZG_ZGZ​.
