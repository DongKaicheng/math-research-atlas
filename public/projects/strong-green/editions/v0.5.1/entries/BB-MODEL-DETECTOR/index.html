Numerical model curvature detection

A special-fibre numerical condition is proposed to detect equality of actual model Chern forms.

v0.5.1 · Local Green forms

Read this claim in context

This permanent record is the claim-level reference. The complete mathematical exposition is organized in the linked articles.

Local Green Forms and Chow Correspondences

Statement

Let X\mathcal XX be an integral projective flat model over the valuation ring of KKK, with smooth projective generic fibre XXX of dimension nnn. Let PPP be a homogeneous polynomial of degree rrr in the Chern classes of model bundles. The draft asserts that, if its special-fibre operational class has degree zero against every integral rrr-dimensional closed subvariety of Xs\mathcal X_sXs​, then the corresponding actual model Chern form is strictly zero.

The line-bundle assertion is proved first; the proposed vector-bundle construction descends symmetric root polynomials from simultaneous flag bundles. No smoothness or reducedness of the special fibre is assumed in the source.

Draft proof route

Test the model curvature against products of model Hessians on a common model refinement. The operational projection formula makes each top-degree mass zero. The scalar-density detector successively replaces model-function slots by original-smooth functions using Stokes against one fixed signed measure. A local positive Hessian on a witnessing skeleton then detects a nonzero pure coefficient, giving a contradiction. For vector bundles, compare root polynomials on the double flag bundle, descend along local sections, and retain strict Whitney and Chern-character identities.

The source below contains the expanded argument and its hypotheses.

Proof programme

Audit the scalar-density induction, the witnessing-skeleton test, and the comparison between actual top wedges and native model intersection measures.
