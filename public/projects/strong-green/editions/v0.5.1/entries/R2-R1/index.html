The residual arithmetic regulator

An actual Chow projector yields a regulator on the Griffiths quotient.

Arithmetic Lefschetz and Integral Heights

Statement

For a central residual projector RRR, put Vp=R∗Heˊt2c−1(YF‾,Qp(c))V_p=R_*H^{2c-1}_{\mathrm{\acute et}}(Y_{\overline F},\mathbf Q_p(c))Vp​=R∗​Heˊt2c−1​(YF​,Qp​(c)). There is a canonical arithmetic regulator

independent of the curve-visible lift under the common cohomological identification. No height axioms are needed for this construction.

Proof outline

The actual central projector leaves a single geometric cohomology row. In total degree 2c2c2c, its Hochschild–Serre filtration has F1=HF^1=HF1=H and F2=0F^2=0F2=0, giving the required edge map. Curve-generated algebraically trivial classes map to zero because RRR kills their H1H^1H1-images. Restriction–corestriction descends this vanishing from a finite field of definition. Thus the map factors through GEG_EGE​.
