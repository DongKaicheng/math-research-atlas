Statement

Let VVV be finite-dimensional over Q\mathbf QQ, Λ0\Lambda_0Λ0​ a full lattice, and Λ⊂V\Lambda\subset VΛ⊂V. Suppose for every prime ppp there is an injective map Dp:V⊗Qp→WpD_p:V\otimes\mathbf Q_p\to W_pDp​:V⊗Qp​→Wp​ and a full lattice Mp⊂WpM_p\subset W_pMp​⊂Wp​, with

Motivation

The almost-everywhere equality with one common rational lattice is precisely the extra input still needed for the residual regulators.
