Arithmetic Conjectures for Beilinson–Bloch Heights

Statement

Write Vr(XE)=CH⁡r(XE)hom,QV^r(X_E)=\operatorname{CH}^r(X_E)_{\mathrm{hom},\mathbf Q}Vr(XE​)=CHr(XE​)hom,Q​. For every smooth projective nnn-fold and every finite extension E/FE/FE/F, assume pairings

hX:Vr(XE)×Vn+1−r(XE)⟶R h_X:V^r(X_E)\times V^{n+1-r}(X_E)\longrightarrow\mathbf R hX​:Vr(XE​)×Vn+1−r(XE​)⟶R

satisfying: H1, rational bilinearity and symmetry in complementary degrees; H2, absolute-normalized extension invariance and isomorphism/Galois covariance; H3, full correspondence adjunction hX(Γ∗z,w)=hY(z,tΓ∗w)h_X(\Gamma_*z,w)=h_Y(z,{}^t\Gamma_*w)hX​(Γ∗​z,w)=hY​(z,tΓ∗​w); H4, on a pointed curve, −hC-h_C−hC​ is the positive Néron–Tate form for the canonical principal polarization in the matching Poincaré convention.

Motivation

This interface separates the arithmetic consequences of a pairing from the analytic problem of constructing it.
