Statement

Assume the frozen H16 assembly hypotheses and the fixed FD17 global-witness conditions recorded in the source synthesis. For a number-field variety X/FX/FX/F, with SSS and its detectors fixed before the cycle, set

Hr(X)=ker⁡(CH⁡r(X)Q,geom hom→PXr⨁w∈SVw,r∨). \mathcal H^r(X)=\ker\left( \operatorname{CH}^r(X)_{\mathbf Q,\mathrm{geom\,hom}} \xrightarrow{P_X^r}\bigoplus_{w\in S}V_{w,r}^{\vee}\right). Hr(X)=ker(CHr(X)Q,geomhom​PXr​​w∈S⨁​Vw,r∨​).

The source records a normalized real-valued analytic Beilinson–Bloch pairing on these kernel domains in complementary codimensions. Both factors must lie in their respective domains. Passing to the complete homologically trivial Chow groups requires vanishing of the detector periods of localized global classes in both codimensions.

Motivation

The fixed global-source target may be weaker than strong G for every local Chow class. It gives a distinct intermediate route to the global height problem without asserting positivity or nondegeneracy.

Proof / construction

The complete H16 argument constructs normalized local values, proves reciprocity and independence of the chosen Green form, establishes finite support of the global sum, and descends through rational equivalence on the intersection of the local obstruction kernels at all finite completions. The new global height paper retains this proof under its explicit analytic hypotheses. Identifying this all-completion domain with the finite-detector kernel displayed above requires the additional fixed FD17 witness comparison; good-place vanishing alone does not furnish that comparison. HA16 and MM16 supply adjunction and model comparison under their separate hypotheses.

The source below contains the expanded argument and its hypotheses.
