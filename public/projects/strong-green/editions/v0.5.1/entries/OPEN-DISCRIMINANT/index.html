Arithmetic Conjectures for Beilinson–Bloch Heights

The problem

For each fixed degree bound DDD, do constants AD,BDA_D,B_DAD​,BD​ exist such that every integral residual class of degree at most DDD satisfies

log⁡NF0/QdF0(ξ)/F0≤ADqG(ξ)+BD? \log N_{F_0/\mathbf Q}\mathfrak d_{F_0(\xi)/F_0}\le A_Dq_G(\xi)+B_D? logNF0​/Q​dF0​(ξ)/F0​​≤AD​qG​(ξ)+BD​?

The discriminant ideal is that of the minimal field of the geometric class.

Motivation

This asks for a quantitative inequality stronger than the qualitative ramification conjecture.
