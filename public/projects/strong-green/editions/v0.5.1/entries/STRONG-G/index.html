Motivation

The form-level identity is the local analytic input sought for a higher-codimension intersection theory compatible with global arithmetic heights.

Proof programme

Construct supported Green pairs; identify their closed curvature obstruction; prove vanishing on the image of the original homologically trivial Chow group. Verify finite trace and descent on the full actual test class.
