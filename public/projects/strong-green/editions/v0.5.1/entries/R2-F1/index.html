Arithmetic Lefschetz and Integral Heights

Statement

Let G\mathcal GG be a rational geometric Chow quotient with its discrete Galois action and compatible integral images preserved by restriction and trace. For ξ∈Λ(E)\xi\in\Lambda(E)ξ∈Λ(E), let K=F0(ξ)K=F_0(\xi)K=F0​(ξ) be the fixed field of its stabilizer. Then

K⊆E,[E:K]ξ∈Λ(K). K\subseteq E,\qquad [E:K]\xi\in\Lambda(K). K⊆E,[E:K]ξ∈Λ(K).

If [E:F0]≤D[E:F_0]\le D[E:F0​]≤D, the multiplier is at most DDD.

Argument

The Galois group of F‾/E\overline F/EF/E fixes ξ\xiξ, hence K⊆EK\subseteq EK⊆E. Push an integral representative over EEE down to KKK. Over the algebraic closure its class is the sum over the KKK-embeddings of EEE, and every summand equals ξ\xiξ. Fixed projectors commute with trace and algebraic equivalence is preserved by it.
