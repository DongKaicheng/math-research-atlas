Zero-Curvature Green Forms and Beilinson–Bloch Height PairingsStrong Green Forms and Beilinson–Bloch HeightsGlobal Height Pairings from Admitted Green Classes

Statement

For every number field FFF and smooth projective geometrically integral X/FX/FX/F of dimension nnn, construct a canonical real-valued pairing on

CH⁡r(X)hom,Q×CH⁡n+1−r(X)hom,Q \operatorname{CH}^r(X)_{\mathrm{hom},\mathbf Q}\times \operatorname{CH}^{n+1-r}(X)_{\mathrm{hom},\mathbf Q} CHr(X)hom,Q​×CHn+1−r(X)hom,Q​

with the normalization and functoriality in BB-HEIGHT-DATA, agreeing with the classical Beilinson–Bloch pairing wherever the latter is defined. The construction includes convergence of the local sum and independence of representatives and auxiliary choices.

Motivation

Existence, positivity, and finiteness are separate questions. The first goal is a canonical pairing with the complete expected domain.

Proof programme

Use strong local Green identities together with local comparison, almost-everywhere compatibility, and the global product formula. A weaker intermediate target is vanishing of residual periods only on localizations of global Chow classes. For the H16 route, eliminate the localized global detector periods in both complementary codimensions and verify the assembly/comparison inputs at the relevant source version.
