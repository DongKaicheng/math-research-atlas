The problem

Verify the finite-trace operator on the scalar–curvature module of actual forms: representation independence under all PL relations; compatibility with d′d'd′, d′′d''d′′, and the stated projection identities; the normalization by [L:K][L:K][L:K]; and extension of the integral identities to all actual compact tests. Verify the model-curvature numerical detector and the supported perfect-complex Green construction with their prescribed support and length multiplicities.

Proof programme

Recheck PL orbit sorting and parameter integration independently, then audit the model detector and finite-flat multisection argument. The analytic draft and its proof sources are provided as candidates for that review.
