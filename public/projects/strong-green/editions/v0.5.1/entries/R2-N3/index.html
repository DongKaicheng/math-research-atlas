Five equivalent lattice conditions

Finite-rank Northcott is exactly a discreteness condition.

v0.5.1 · Arithmetic finiteness

Read this claim in context

This permanent record is the claim-level reference. The complete mathematical exposition is organized in the linked articles.

Arithmetic Lefschetz and Integral Heights

Statement

Let Λ\LambdaΛ be torsion-free of finite rank, V=Λ⊗QV=\Lambda\otimes\mathbf QV=Λ⊗Q, and let qqq be positive definite on V⊗RV\otimes\mathbf RV⊗R. The following are equivalent: Northcott on Λ\LambdaΛ; a positive lower bound for qqq on nonzero elements; finite generation of Λ\LambdaΛ; containment in N−1Λ0N^{-1}\Lambda_0N−1Λ0​ for a rational full lattice Λ0\Lambda_0Λ0​; and relative compactness of the diagonal image in V(Af)V(\mathbf A_f)V(Af​).

Argument

Finiteness of the unit ball supplies a positive gap. The gap makes Λ\LambdaΛ uniformly discrete in its finite-dimensional Euclidean space. Reducing modulo the span of a rational basis in Λ\LambdaΛ puts every coset representative in a bounded parallelepiped, so only finitely many occur. Thus that span has finite index and the denominators are bounded. Conversely, bounded subsets of a Euclidean lattice are finite. Finally, finite-adelic compact subsets lie in N−1Λ0⊗Z^N^{-1}\Lambda_0\otimes\widehat{\mathbf Z}N−1Λ0​⊗Z for some NNN; intersection with the rational vector space gives the denominator criterion.
