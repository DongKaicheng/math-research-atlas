Tropical realization on the full actual test space

Pass from chartwise primitives to an ordinary actual form on the exact complement.

v0.5.1 · Local Green forms

Zero-Curvature Green Forms and Beilinson–Bloch Height PairingsStrong Green Forms and Beilinson–Bloch Heights

The problem

Develop a compatible refinement and Aeppli/Bott–Chern theory that realizes chartwise primitives as ordinary actual Mihatsch forms on the prescribed open complement, with independence of presentation, gluing, residue compatibility, and the full compact actual test identity.

Motivation

The related Bedford–Taylor programme concerns intrinsic operations on currents and potentials. Its descent questions are useful foundations, but do not by themselves construct the required actual Green representatives.

Proof programme

Separate topological inverse-limit results from differential, integral, and cohomological comparison. Treat fixed finite certificates as conditional inputs until their existence for every original Chow source is established.
