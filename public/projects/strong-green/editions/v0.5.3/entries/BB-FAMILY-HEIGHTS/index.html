Height domination and boundaries in families

Connect the local theory to adelic positivity and Diophantine finiteness.

Normalized Determinants and Height Metrics

The problem

For families of homologically trivial cycles, identify the correct metrized biextension or height line bundle, establish its local comparison and boundary asymptotics under exact hypotheses, and determine conditions yielding height domination and Northcott on a specified open parameter locus.

Motivation

The Gross–Schoen and Ceresa families provide a geometric bridge to the broader Arakelov and Diophantine programme.

Proof programme

Start with smooth relative biextension comparison. Track the modified-cycle and cohomological hypotheses needed at singular fibres. Separate local semipositivity, geometric nefness, adelic nefness, and arithmetic height domination.
