Arithmetic Lefschetz and Integral Heights

Statement

Assume H1–H4 and use the projectors in BB-CHOW-PROJECTOR. Then UE=eUE⊕RUEU_E=eU_E\oplus RU_EUE​=eUE​⊕RUE​ is height-orthogonal, the signed form on eUEeU_EeUE​ is a positive Néron–Tate form, and

RAE=ker⁡(AJQ∣AE)=AE∩rad⁡(h∣UE)=rad⁡(h∣AE). RA_E=\ker(\mathrm{AJ}_{\mathbf Q}|_{A_E}) =A_E\cap\operatorname{rad}(h|_{U_E}) =\operatorname{rad}(h|_{A_E}). RAE​=ker(AJQ​∣AE​​)=AE​∩rad(h∣UE​​)=rad(h∣AE​​).

The induced form on RUE/RAE≃GERU_E/RA_E\simeq G_ERUE​/RAE​≃GE​ is independent of the chosen maximal curve-visible projector; denote it by hGh_GhG​.

Motivation

The original pairing does not directly descend through the algebraically trivial subgroup: its curve-visible part carries a nonzero Néron–Tate pairing.

Proof outline

A curve correspondence with zero H1H^1H1-action has transpose zero on complementary homologically trivial cycles: Abel–Jacobi functoriality reduces this to the faithful Picard group of the source curve. Adjunction therefore makes its image a height-radical subspace. Maximality of the visible summand applies this argument to RAERA_ERAE​. On eUEeU_EeUE​, Abel–Jacobi has finite kernel and the signed height is nondegenerate. This proves the kernel equalities. Two orthogonalized lifts of a Griffiths class differ by an element of the full radical, proving independence.
