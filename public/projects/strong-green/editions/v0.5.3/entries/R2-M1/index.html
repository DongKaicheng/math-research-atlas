Normalized Determinants and Height Metrics

Statement

Let CCC be a pointed smooth projective connected complex curve, J=Pic⁡0(C)J=\operatorname{Pic}^0(C)J=Pic0(C), and P\mathcal PP its axis-rigidified Poincaré bundle with its standard smooth metric: the axis units have norm one and the curvature is purely mixed. Let XXX be smooth projective connected, M=C×XM=C\times XM=C×X, q:M→Cq:M\to Cq:M→C, and F\mathcal FF a hermitian perfect complex. Choose fixed product Kähler metrics and hermitian vector-bundle representatives. In the cochain determinant convention define

DM(F)=det⁡RpJ∗(PM⊗FJ)⊗(det⁡RΓ(M,F)⊗OJ)−1. \mathscr D_M(\mathcal F)=\det Rp_{J*}(\mathcal P_M\otimes\mathcal F_J)\otimes\bigl(\det R\Gamma(M,\mathcal F)\otimes\mathcal O_J\bigr)^{-1}. DM​(F)=detRpJ∗​(PM​⊗FJ​)⊗(detRΓ(M,F)⊗OJ​)−1.

Define DC(K)\mathscr D_C(K)DC​(K) in the same way on C×JC\times JC×J. Each constant denominator carries the Quillen norm of the same complex and metrics as the numerator at the origin. Then derived projection gives an exact rigidified isometry on all of JJJ:

DM(F)≃DC(Rq∗F). \mathscr D_M(\mathcal F)\simeq\mathscr D_C(Rq_*\mathcal F). DM​(F)≃DC​(Rq∗​F).

Proof outline

Derived projection gives the algebraic rigidified isomorphism. Write Ω=c1(P‾)\Omega=c_1(\overline{\mathcal P})Ω=c1​(P); its bidegree is purely mixed between CCC and JJJ. In the family index formula only Ω2/2\Omega^2/2Ω2/2 contributes to the base degree two, and both normalized determinants have curvature 12rk⁡(Rq∗F)∫CΩ2\tfrac12\operatorname{rk}(Rq_*\mathcal F)\int_C\Omega^221​rk(Rq∗​F)∫C​Ω2. Their norm ratio is a smooth pluriharmonic function on the compact Jacobian, hence constant. The origin rigidification has norm one on both sides, making the constant one.
