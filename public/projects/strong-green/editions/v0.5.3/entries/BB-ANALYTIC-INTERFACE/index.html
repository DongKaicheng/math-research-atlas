Finite actual trace and the height-operation interface

The differentiated finite trace and projection identities needed by the stated height constructions have internal proofs.

v0.5.3 · Local Green forms

Read this claim in context

This permanent record is the claim-level reference. The complete mathematical exposition is organized in the linked articles.

Zero-Curvature Green Forms and Beilinson–Bloch Height PairingsStrong Green Forms and Beilinson–Bloch HeightsGlobal Height Pairings from Admitted Green Classes

Statement

For the normal finite carriers, projective bundles and supported proper-intersection operations specified in Propositions 2.1–2.5 of the global height paper, the required actual trace and fibre integration commute with d′d'd′ and d′′d''d′′, satisfy the stated projection identities and adjunction against all compact actual tests, and retain full native degree normalization. Supported residues use refined intersection multiplicities. These assertions concern the specified operations; they do not assert an arbitrary proper pushforward of actual forms. Green-current products are defined on the algebraic dual of compact actual tests.

Proof / construction

FT16 gives presentation-independent finite actual trace on normal carriers. AD15 and UG15 supply finite ambient presentations and the relative tropical primitive; FC16 gives actual projective-line output with smooth mixed-test adjunction. OUT16 adds the arbitrary actual target-factor identity and upgrades this to all-actual adjunction. The supported K-theoretic construction, immersion-germ extension, projective-bundle cohomology and graph/deformation arguments establish the exact residue and covariance operations used in the height proofs.

The source below contains the expanded argument and its hypotheses.

Proof programme

The original finite-trace and height-operation task is closed in its stated scope. The separate finite-test identification, arbitrary-model projection hypothesis and general Strong Green existence question retain their own statements.
