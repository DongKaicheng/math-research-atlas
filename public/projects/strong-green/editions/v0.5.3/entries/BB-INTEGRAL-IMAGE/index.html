Integral images and bounded-degree Northcott

Count geometric classes with integral representatives, not all rational multiples.

v0.5.3 · Arithmetic finiteness

Arithmetic Conjectures for Beilinson–Bloch Heights

Statement

Fix an algebraic closure F‾\overline FF. Let ΛXr(E)\Lambda_X^r(E)ΛXr​(E) be the image of integral-coefficient cycles over EEE with zero geometric rational cycle class in Vr(XF‾)V^r(X_{\overline F})Vr(XF​). For a fixed rational projector use its projected integral image; for a quotient use the image of that subgroup.

A quadratic form qqq has bounded-degree Northcott if, for every B∈RB\in\mathbf RB∈R and integer D≥1D\ge1D≥1,

{ξ∈⋃[E:F]≤DΛ(E):q(ξ)≤B} \left\{\xi\in\bigcup_{[E:F]\le D}\Lambda(E):q(\xi)\le B\right\} ⎩⎨⎧​ξ∈[E:F]≤D⋃​Λ(E):q(ξ)≤B⎭⎬⎫​

is finite. Fixed-field Northcott fixes EEE instead.

Motivation

A rational vector space admits arbitrarily small rational multiples of a nonzero vector. An arithmetic finiteness statement therefore needs an integral image or a fixed denominator bound.
