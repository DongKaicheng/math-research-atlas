Motivation

This settles the construction input in the obstruction route to Strong Green. The remaining general assertion is vanishing of this obstruction on homologically trivial Chow classes. The Strong G-to-height implication retains its separately stated analytic foundations.

Argument

Construct strict actual model Chern characters using the numerical detector and flag-section descent. Build the scalar–curvature trace by invariant PL descent and one-parameter integration. Apply it to supported perfect complexes, component by component, to retain the prescribed cycle and its exact open complement. Generic acyclic complexes give global actual primitives; rational Adams projectors isolate each Chow degree and prove independence of all model presentations. The integral-scope argument performs every analytic construction over the original valued field.
