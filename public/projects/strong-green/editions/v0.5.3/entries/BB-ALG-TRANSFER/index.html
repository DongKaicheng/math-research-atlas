Algebraic equivalence and compact parameters

The actual obstruction vanishes on geometrically algebraically trivial rational cycles.

v0.5.3 · Local Green forms

Read this claim in context

This permanent record is the claim-level reference. The complete mathematical exposition is organized in the linked articles.

Local Curvature Obstructions and Beilinson–Bloch Height PairingsDomains and Constructions of Beilinson–Bloch Height PairingsLocal Green Forms and Chow Correspondences: Constructions and Geometric Transfer

Statement

For a smooth projective geometrically integral variety X/KX/KX/K over the original finite valued extension K/QpK/\mathbf Q_pK/Qp​, the actual Bott–Chern obstruction vanishes on geometrically algebraically trivial rational cycles. Every prescribed representative of such a class admits a zero-curvature actual Green form on its exact support complement and against all compact actual tests.

Motivation

The Chow obstruction, prescribed pairs and rational-fibre rigidity used here are proved. The conclusion concerns geometric algebraic equivalence; general geometric homological triviality remains a separate problem.

Proof / construction

Generate algebraically trivial classes by curve correspondences and degree-zero divisors. The Poincaré family makes the obstruction additive on a p-adic Jacobian. Rational-fibre rigidity makes it locally constant, so its image on the compact group is finite; additivity and torsion-freeness force it to vanish. The algebraic norm family has a rational fibre representing the full field-degree multiple of the class. Dividing this rational Chow norm identity by the full field degree gives vanishing over the original field. Curvature correction then retains the prescribed support and all actual tests.

The source below contains the expanded argument and its hypotheses.
