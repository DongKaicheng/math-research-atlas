Local integral images of residual regulators

For each coefficient prime, integral residual cycle images lie in a finite module.

v0.5.3 · Arithmetic finiteness

Arithmetic Lefschetz and Integral Heights

Statement

For each fixed EEE and prime ppp, ap,E(ΛG(E))a_{p,E}(\Lambda_G(E))ap,E​(ΛG​(E)) lies in a finitely generated Zp\mathbf Z_pZp​-module. If the scalar-extended map GE⊗QQp→H1(E,Vp)G_E\otimes_{\mathbf Q}\mathbf Q_p\to H^1(E,V_p)GE​⊗Q​Qp​→H1(E,Vp​) is injective, then GEG_EGE​ has finite rational dimension, ΛG(E)⊗Zp\Lambda_G(E)\otimes\mathbf Z_pΛG​(E)⊗Zp​ is finitely generated, and ΛG(E)/pΛG(E)\Lambda_G(E)/p\Lambda_G(E)ΛG​(E)/pΛG​(E) is finite.

Motivation

The scalar-injectivity condition in the second assertion is an open geometric input. Mere rational linear injectivity into a p-adic space is insufficient.

Proof outline

Clear the denominator of the fixed Chow projector. Apply the integral continuous cycle-image bound, intersect its rational image with the residual summand, and only then use the one-row edge map. This gives the finite integral target. Under the explicitly stated scalar-injectivity hypothesis, tensoring the integral-image map with Zp\mathbf Z_pZp​ embeds the tensor lattice into that finite module.
