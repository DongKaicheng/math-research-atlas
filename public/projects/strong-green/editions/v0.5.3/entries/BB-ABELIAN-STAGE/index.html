The semistable abelian numerical stage

Künnemann’s semistable abelian correction supplies a vertical numerical certificate, which descends to the original field.

v0.5.3 · Local Green forms

Domains and Constructions of Beilinson–Bloch Height PairingsLocal Green Forms and Chow Correspondences: Constructions and Geometric Transfer

Statement

For an abelian variety A/KA/KA/K, where KKK is a finite valued extension of Qp\mathbf Q_pQp​, every geometrically homologically trivial rational class of codimension 1≤r≤dim⁡A1\le r\le\dim A1≤r≤dimA has zero actual obstruction. Every prescribed representative has a strong Green form over the original valued field, on its exact support complement and against all compact actual tests.

Motivation

The complete discrete valuation ring with finite residue field is essential to the cited semistable model input. The internal argument proves the specified actual-form realization and does not assume a general standard-conjecture statement for arbitrary models.

Proof / construction

After finite extension choose the regular projective semistable abelian model. Extend the generic class to a Chow class Θ\ThetaΘ. Its component restrictions give a class aaa in the special-fibre component complex. Geometric homological triviality and the cited cycle-class injectivity give a∩[Y]=0a\cap[Y]=0a∩[Y]=0. Exactness supplies a vertical class β\betaβ with i∗i∗β=ai^*i_*\beta=ai∗i∗​β=a. Replacing Θ\ThetaΘ by Θ−i∗β\Theta-i_*\betaΘ−i∗​β makes every component restriction homologically trivial. Its pure virtual model-bundle representative is a numerical certificate. Apply finite numerical descent.
