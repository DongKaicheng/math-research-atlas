Numerical model curvature detection

Special-fibre numerical vanishing detects equality of actual model Chern forms, including singular and nonreduced special fibres.

v0.5.3 · Local Green forms

Read this claim in context

Chow Obstructions and Prescribed Actual Green PairsLocal Green Forms and Chow Correspondences: Constructions and Geometric Transfer

Statement

Fix the original finite valued extension K/QpK/\mathbf Q_pK/Qp​. Let X\mathcal XX be an integral projective flat model over K∘K^\circK∘, with smooth integral projective generic fibre XXX of dimension nnn, and fix 1≤r≤n1\le r\le n1≤r≤n. If a homogeneous degree-rrr polynomial in model Chern classes has special-fibre operational degree zero against every integral rrr-dimensional closed subvariety of Xs\mathcal X_sXs​, its actual model Chern form is strictly zero.

The construction first proves the line-bundle detector, then descends symmetric root polynomials from simultaneous flag bundles. It gives separately closed higher model Chern forms, strict Whitney and Chern-character identities, and genuine model-pullback functoriality. No smoothness or reducedness of the special fibre is required.

Argument

Test the model curvature against products of model Hessians on a common model refinement. The operational projection formula makes each top-degree mass zero. The scalar-density detector successively replaces model-function slots by original-smooth functions using Stokes against one fixed signed measure. A local positive Hessian on a witnessing skeleton then detects a nonzero pure coefficient, giving a contradiction. For vector bundles, compare root polynomials on the double flag bundle, descend along local sections, and retain strict Whitney and Chern-character identities. The actual/Bedford–Taylor comparison, fixed-measure density induction, native multiplicities and flag-section descent are proved in the linked complete source.
