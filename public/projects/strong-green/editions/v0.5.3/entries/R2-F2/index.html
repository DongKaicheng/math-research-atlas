Ramification is the remaining variable-field condition

Fixed-field Northcott plus finite ramification sets is equivalent to bounded-degree Northcott.

Arithmetic Lefschetz and Integral Heights

Statement

Assume a Galois-invariant quadratic form qqq has fixed-field Northcott on Λ(K)\Lambda(K)Λ(K) for every finite K/F0K/F_0K/F0​. Argument

A degree bound and a fixed ramification set leave finitely many fields by Hermite–Minkowski. R2-F1 gives mξ∈Λ(K)m\xi\in\Lambda(K)mξ∈Λ(K), with 1≤m≤D1\le m\le D1≤m≤D and q(mξ)≤D2Bq(m\xi)\le D^2Bq(mξ)≤D2B. Conversely, a finite height ball has finitely many class fields, and their ramified places form a finite set.
