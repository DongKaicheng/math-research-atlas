Finite trace on scalar–curvature forms

The finite trace on the actual scalar–curvature module satisfies adjunction against every compact actual test.

v0.5.3 · Local Green forms

Argument

The pure-curvature trace is constructed by finite orbit sorting. Introduce a parameter ttt, replace the scalar factor by the graph curvature Dmax⁡(u0−t,0)D\max(u_0-t,0)Dmax(u0​−t,0), and integrate χ(t)t\chi(t)tχ(t)t times the resulting pure-curvature trace over the parameter.
