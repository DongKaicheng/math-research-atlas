Local Green Forms and Chow Correspondences: Constructions and Geometric Transfer

Statement

Let K/QpK/\mathbf Q_pK/Qp​ be finite, A/KA/KA/K an abelian variety, and C1,…,CsC_1,\ldots,C_sC1​,…,Cs​ smooth projective geometrically integral curves. Put X=A×K∏iCiX=A\times_K\prod_iC_iX=A×K​∏i​Ci​. The expanded draft proposes strong G for every prescribed geometrically homologically trivial rational cycle of codimension 1≤r≤dim⁡X1\le r\le\dim X1≤r≤dimX. Its proof uses the separate Chow diagonal, simultaneous regular-model construction, and model-sensitive numerical transfer recorded below.

Draft proof route

Split each pointed curve motive as 1⊕h1(Ci)⊕L\mathbf1\oplus h^1(C_i)\oplus\mathbb L1⊕h1(Ci​)⊕L, realize h1(Ci)h^1(C_i)h1(Ci​) through its Jacobian, and tensor the decompositions to obtain actual diagonal factorizations. Numerical model certificates are transported by refined intersection and the projection formula. The proposed analytic trace, detector, and supported Green constructions then connect those certificates to actual forms. All six proof-source files are preserved in the source edition.

The source below contains the expanded argument and its hypotheses.

Proof programme

The immediate review targets are finite-trace representation independence, PL parameter integration, the numerical curvature detector, and exact-support perfect-complex realization.
