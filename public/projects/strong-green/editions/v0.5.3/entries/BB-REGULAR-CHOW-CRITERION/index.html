Statement

Assume the numerical detector, finite numerical descent, and supported numerical correspondence-transfer statements of the standalone draft. Suppose that after a finite extension F/KF/KF/K: (i) XFX_FXF​ has a regular integral projective flat model; (ii) an abelian variety B/FB/FB/F has the regular projective semistable model satisfying thm:kunnemann-local; and (iii) finitely many rational Chow correspondences with opposite degree shifts satisfy

∑jvj∘uj=ΔXF. \sum_j v_j\circ u_j=\Delta_{X_F}. j∑​vj​∘uj​=ΔXF​​.

Then each prescribed geometrically homologically trivial rational cycle on XXX has a strong Green form over the original KKK.

Proof outline

Each (uj)∗zF(u_j)_*z_F(uj​)∗​zF​ is homologically trivial on BBB, so the abelian model supplies a numerical certificate. The supported numerical projection argument transfers each certificate along vjv_jvj​ to the chosen regular model of XFX_FXF​. Summing gives a certificate for zFz_FzF​ by the actual Chow diagonal identity. Finite numerical descent for XF→XX_F\to XXF​→X returns the Green construction with its full factor [F:K][F:K][F:K].

The source below contains the expanded argument and its hypotheses.

Proof programme

Independently audit the supported numerical projection and alteration argument in prop:correspondence-transfer. The regular target model is an explicit hypothesis of this version.
