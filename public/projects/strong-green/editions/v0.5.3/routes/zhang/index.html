Research route

Admissible arithmetic liftings: Zhang’s approach

Shou-Wu Zhang’s construction uses algebraic cycle classes on the special fibres of arithmetic models. Vertical corrections make the closure of a homologically trivial cycle orthogonal to vertical cycles. Arithmetic intersection then gives a number independent of the chosen lifting.

The argument

Each step states whether it is a required input, an open problem or a deduction under the stated hypotheses.

  1. The geometric input

    Algebraic classes on the special fibre

This local construction is made over the completion of a maximal unramified extension, with a geometric special fibre; return to the original place uses cycle descent.

  2. Zhang’s conditional theorems

    Harmonic decomposition and liftings

    The Lefschetz and Hodge standard conjectures on every smooth geometric stratum of a strictly semistable model imply local duality and Lefschetz. The harmonic curvature of a homologically trivial class is then zero. A vertical correction gives a Beilinson–Bloch lifting, and alterations extend the conditional lifting theorem to general reduction.

    Theorem 1.5.1, Proposition 1.6.3 and Theorem 1.6.4 →

  3. Construction of the global pairing

    Arithmetic intersection

    Combine the finite-place corrections with zero-curvature Green currents at infinity. Differences between liftings are vertical classes and zero-curvature archimedean terms. Orthogonality and homological triviality make their intersections with the complementary lifting vanish.

    The lifting and intersection argument →

  4. Published result and remaining conjectures

    Known cases and the general problem

    For a curve times a surface, Zhang verifies the required conditions on a semistable model and then uses alterations. In general, the corresponding standard conjectures, or a direct construction of the vertical correction, remain the substantial open input.

Relation to the Green-form approaches

An arithmetic lifting and an actual non-Archimedean form are different objects. Their heights agree on supplied admissible regular models or specified admissible alterations, with matching local normalizations. Hypothesis 5.1 retains the arithmetic operations required for every model morphism chosen, especially a nonflat one. Zhang’s L-liftings of all Chow classes are a further extension with additional arithmetic standard-conjecture hypotheses, not a premise of the ordinary homologically trivial pairing.
