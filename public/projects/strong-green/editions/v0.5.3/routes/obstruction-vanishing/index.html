Research route

Vanishing of local curvature obstructions

A prescribed cycle is first given a Green form with curvature. One then seeks a global primitive of that curvature. Since the curvature class depends only on the Chow class, algebraic equivalence and Chow correspondences become tools for proving its vanishing.

What is established

The Chow obstruction and prescribed curved pairs, rational-fibre constancy, finite actual trace and Chow-kernel covariance are established. The local height identities construct the pairing on both common local kernels; general vanishing on homologically trivial classes remains open.

Algebraic equivalence and Chow correspondences

Rational-fibre constancy and compact parameters annihilate the obstruction on geometrically algebraically trivial cycles. Proved Chow-kernel covariance and native finite descent transport vanishing through an actual rational Chow decomposition of the diagonal. Each application retains its specified varieties, degree shifts and factorization.

The remaining problems

The central general local question is why homologically trivial Chow classes have zero image in actual Bott–Chern cohomology. A proposed finite system of periods must still be compared with that original map. General existence of admissible arithmetic-model liftings is separate from the established kernel pairing and its comparison on supplied admissible models.
