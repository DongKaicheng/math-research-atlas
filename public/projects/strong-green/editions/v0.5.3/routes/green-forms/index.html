Research route

Direct construction of zero-curvature Green forms

The principal-push theorem gives a direct passage from strong Green forms to Beilinson–Bloch heights. Its proof computes principal-cycle pairings by pushing the scalar Green function −log|u| from a normal carrier, then uses reciprocity to obtain the transformation law for any supplied strong Green form. Theorem 7.5 completes the global construction using the proved analytic operations.

Tropical forms and global primitives

One possible method is to solve for primitives on tropical charts and descend them to the Berkovich space. The comparison must preserve both differentials, integration and compatibility on overlaps. A topological inverse limit alone does not give this statement for forms. A global actual primitive, together with a prescribed curved Green pair, would supply a strong Green form.

The remaining problems

The central general existence problem is Strong G for every prescribed geometrically homologically trivial cycle, with the original field, exact support complement and full actual test space. The principal-push and global assembly arguments are established. Correspondence adjunction holds on the common local kernels, and arithmetic-model comparison holds for supplied admissible liftings or alterations. Extensions beyond those domains, positivity and arithmetic finiteness retain their own hypotheses and research questions.
