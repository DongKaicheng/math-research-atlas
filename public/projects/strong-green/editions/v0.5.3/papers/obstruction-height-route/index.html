Local Curvature Obstructions and Beilinson–Bloch Height Pairings

Curvature correction, Chow correspondences and the global pairing

23 November 2026

Abstract

An obstruction approach to Beilinson–Bloch heights separates the construction of a local Green pair from the removal of its curvature. The Chow-invariant actual Bott–Chern obstruction and curved Green pairs are constructed for every prescribed cycle over the original valued field. We prove curvature correction on the prescribed support complement, vanishing on algebraically trivial classes, and preservation of the obstruction kernels by rational Chow correspondences. The local height identities then give a finite, rational-bilinear global pairing on the common local kernels, invariant under finite extension. Vanishing on localizations of global classes in both complementary degrees would extend this pairing to the full homologically trivial Chow groups. Finite period descriptions still require comparison with the original obstruction, and general existence of admissible arithmetic-model liftings remains separate.

3. Geometric methods for proving vanishing

The obstruction is a linear map on a Chow group. Algebraic equivalence gives an additive parameter space for its values. A Chow decomposition expresses a class through source varieties on which vanishing is known. A numerical model correction provides a third way of producing the needed primitive. These mechanisms can be used independently or combined.

Problem 5.4 (The remaining homologically trivial classes). Under the algebraically trivial vanishing of Theorem 3.3, prove that the induced obstruction on (3.1) is zero, or, for the global goal, prove only (1.3).
