Strong Green Forms and Beilinson–Bloch Heights

A local-to-global research programme

23 November 2026

Abstract

The local problem is to construct an actual non-Archimedean zero-curvature Green form for a homologically trivial cycle, on its prescribed support complement and against all compact actual tests. A Chow-invariant actual Bott–Chern obstruction and curved Green pairs for every prescribed cycle are constructed over the original valued field. Full finite actual trace and rational-fibre rigidity support descent and the vanishing for algebraically trivial classes. The remaining local target is vanishing on the complete homologically trivial Chow source. Globally, the companion construction gives a normalized Beilinson–Bloch pairing, correspondence adjunction and comparison on the intersection of all local obstruction kernels. We distinguish this established domain from a proposed finite-test description and from the full complementary homologically trivial groups. For products of curves, the height system leads to separate questions of arithmetic Lefschetz, positivity, integral discreteness and fields of definition.

1. The local problem and its arithmetic purpose

In higher codimension the same construction asks for a Green object of positive bidegree, compatible with rational equivalence, intersection and change of field. An actual differential-form representative provides more than a numerical local pairing: it places that pairing inside a differential calculus in which its support and functoriality can be studied.

3. Two geometric sources of vanishing
