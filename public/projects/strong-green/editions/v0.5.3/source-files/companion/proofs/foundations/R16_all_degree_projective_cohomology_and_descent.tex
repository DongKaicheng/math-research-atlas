\section{Projective-bundle cohomology and modification descent in every degree}
\label{r16pb:section}

The new input to this section is actual integration on projective bundles
in every bidegree, with the all-actual target projection formula,
$d',d''$ compatibility, and coordinate projective-bundle base change.
These are the precise assertions of
\path{higher_trace/all_actual_projective_bundle_output.tex}.
The diagonal kernel used below is the frozen curved Green pair for a
prescribed cycle, not a zero-curvature Green solution. The other frozen
inputs are the Round 11 Chow-to-actual Bott--Chern curvature class,
the Round 12 graded-ring property of that class, the Round 15 mixed
connecting sequence and localized normal-Chern extension. In particular,
general strong G is not a premise.

\begin{lemma}[A diagonal Green pair with polynomial curvature]
\label{r16pb:diagonal}
Let $S/K$ be smooth integral projective, $N$ a vector bundle of rank
$r+1\geq2$, and $\pi:E=\mathbf P_S(N)\to S$ the space of lines.
Put $T=E\times_S E$ and write $p_z,p_y:T\to E$ and $\pi_T:T\to S$.
Choose separately closed actual forms $C_k$ representing
$\mathfrak o_S^k(c_k(N))$, with $C_0=1$, and a separately closed
actual first Chern form $\xi$ of $\mathcal O_E(1)$. There is an actual
Green form
\[
 \Gamma_\Delta\in B^{r-1,r-1}(T^{\rm an}\setminus\Delta^{\rm an})
\]
whose curvature is the global actual form
\[
 \Omega_\Delta=
       \sum_{i+j+k=r}\xi_z^i\xi_y^j\pi_T^*C_k.
 \tag{PB16.1}\label{r16pb:diagonal-polynomial}
\]
More precisely, for every compact complementary actual test $\Phi$,
\[
 \int_{T\setminus\Delta}\Gamma_\Delta\wedge D\Phi
       =\int_T\Omega_\Delta\wedge\Phi-\int_\Delta\Phi.
 \tag{PB16.2}\label{r16pb:diagonal-green}
\]
Every degree and form in this statement is over the original field.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Let $L_z,L_y$ be the tautological lines, and let
$Q_y=\pi_T^*N/L_y$. The tautological map $L_z\to Q_y$ is a section
of the rank-$r$ bundle $L_z^\vee\otimes Q_y$. Its zero scheme is the
relative diagonal, a regular embedding of codimension $r$. The top
Chern formula and the exact sequence for $Q_y$ give in the Chow ring
\[
 [\Delta]=c_r(L_z^\vee\otimes Q_y)
         =\sum_{i+j+k=r}\xi_{z,\mathrm{alg}}^i
                         \xi_{y,\mathrm{alg}}^j\pi_T^*c_k(N).
 \tag{PB16.3}\label{r16pb:diagonal-chow}
\]
The plus signs agree with the convention of lines: $c(L_y)=1-\xi_y$.

The frozen exact-support curved Green-pair theorem gives
$(\Gamma_0,\Omega_0)$ for this specified diagonal. The Round 11
curvature-class theorem and the Round 12 graded-ring identity turn
\eqref{r16pb:diagonal-chow} into
$[\Omega_0]=[\Omega_\Delta]$ in actual Bott--Chern degree $(r,r)$.
Consequently $\Omega_0-\Omega_\Delta=D\Psi$ for a global actual
$\Psi\in B^{r-1,r-1}(T)$. Define
$\Gamma_\Delta=\Gamma_0-\Psi|_{T\setminus\Delta}$.
Actual Stokes gives \eqref{r16pb:diagonal-green} on every actual
test. This subtracts a global form and therefore keeps the exact
diagonal complement. It uses the vanishing for a Chow identity,
not an unproved vanishing for homologically trivial cycles.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Actual diagonal homotopy]
\label{r16pb:homotopy}
Let $\alpha\in B^{a,b}(E)$ satisfy $D\alpha=0$.
There are ordinary actual forms $G\alpha$, $P\alpha$, $Q\alpha$
in degrees $(a-1,b-1),(a,b-1),(a-1,b)$, respectively, such that
\[
 \alpha=
   \sum_{i+j+k=r}\xi^j\wedge\pi^*
            \bigl(C_k\wedge I_\pi(\xi^i\wedge\alpha)\bigr)
       +DG\alpha+d''P\alpha-d'Q\alpha.
 \tag{PB16.4}\label{r16pb:homotopy-formula}
\]
Negative form degrees mean zero. The transgression currents are the
prescribed integrals of $\Gamma_\Delta p_z^*\alpha$,
$d'\Gamma_\Delta\wedge p_z^*\alpha$ and
$d''\Gamma_\Delta\wedge p_z^*\alpha$ along $p_y$.
They are represented on the ordinary topology and have all actual
compact-test pairings in their stated degrees.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The restrictions of $p_z^*\alpha$ and $p_y^*\alpha$ to $\Delta$
are identical. The frozen actual immersion-germ theorem therefore
makes them equal on an ordinary neighborhood of $\Delta$.
Let $n=\dim E$. A complementary target test for the three proposed
outputs, multiplied by this target pullback of $\alpha$, has degrees
$(n+1,n+1),(n,n+1),(n+1,n)$, respectively. Each product is zero.
This statement holds for arbitrary actual tests by the actual
dimension bound, not merely for smooth test coefficients.

Choose an ordinary smooth cutoff that vanishes near $\Delta$ inside
this zero neighborhood. The three cutoff integrands extend by zero
as actual forms on $T$. Their proper $p_y$ projections exist in all
degrees by the new projective-bundle output theorem. On every paired
compact set the cutoff changes only an identically zero product.
The representatives are therefore intrinsic and have their prescribed
uncut pairings, with ordinary gluing by current injectivity. This
also deals with negative output degrees: the paired integrands then
vanish by dimension and the corresponding currents are zero.

Use \eqref{r16pb:diagonal-green} with
$\Phi=p_z^*\alpha\wedge p_y^*\eta$. The first derivative test
products needed for Stokes vanish on the same ordinary neighborhood
of $\Delta$ by overdegree. Thus all Stokes operations are on global
actual forms extended by zero before integration. The sign calculation
is the following identity away from the diagonal. Since
$\deg\Gamma_\Delta=2r-2$ is even and $D\alpha=0$,
\[
 D(\Gamma_\Delta\alpha_z)
 +d''(d'\Gamma_\Delta\alpha_z)
 -d'(d''\Gamma_\Delta\alpha_z)
                      =-D\Gamma_\Delta\,\alpha_z.
 \tag{PB16.5}\label{r16pb:sign}
\]
Applying the Green identity to the paired expression restores the
diagonal restriction $\alpha$. It follows that
\[
 \alpha=I_{p_y}(\Omega_\Delta\wedge p_z^*\alpha)
                     +DG\alpha+d''P\alpha-d'Q\alpha.
\]
For a summand of \eqref{r16pb:diagonal-polynomial}, the actual target
projection formula removes $\xi_y^j\pi_T^*C_k$ from the integral.
The specific base-change identity for the projective-bundle projection
$\pi:E\to S$ gives
\[
 I_{p_y}p_z^*(\xi^i\alpha)=\pi^*I_\pi(\xi^i\alpha).
\]
These are exactly the tested base changes supplied by the new output
theorem; no unrestricted current base change is invoked. This proves
\eqref{r16pb:homotopy-formula}.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Full actual projective-bundle Aeppli and Bott--Chern formulas]
\label{r16pb:formula}
For every smooth integral projective $S/K$ and vector bundle $N$ of
rank $r+1$, one has, in every bidegree,
\[
 \HA^{a,b}(\mathbf P_S(N))
    =\bigoplus_{j=0}^{r}[\xi]^j\wedge
                       \pi^*\HA^{a-j,b-j}(S).
 \tag{PB16.6}\label{r16pb:HA}
\]
The same formula holds with $H_{\mathrm{BC,act}}$ in place of $\HA$.
In particular the full actual Aeppli and Bott--Chern groups of
$\mathbf P^r_K$ vanish off the diagonal and are $\mathbf R[h^k]$
in degree $(k,k)$ for $0\leq k\leq r$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For an Aeppli class represented by $\alpha$, put
\[
 \beta_j=\sum_{i+k=r-j} C_k\wedge I_\pi(\xi^i\alpha).
\]
It has degree $(a-j,b-j)$ and is $D$-closed because $\xi,C_k$ are
separately closed and integration commutes with both differentials.
Equation \eqref{r16pb:homotopy-formula} proves spanning.
Integration sends every actual Aeppli boundary to an actual boundary:
each of its two primitive forms has a form-valued output, and current
differentiation gives the required identity. Multiplication by any
separately closed $\xi^k$ also preserves these boundaries.

Suppose a sum in \eqref{r16pb:HA} is zero. Apply $I_\pi$.
For $j<r$, $I_\pi(\xi^j)=0$ by negative degree; for $j=r$,
$I_\pi(\xi^r)=1$ by the native all-actual fiber degree formula.
The actual projection formula therefore gives $[\beta_r]=0$.
Subtract that boundary summand, multiply the remaining relation by
$\xi$, and integrate to get $[\beta_{r-1}]=0$. Continue. This proves
directness without a Chern-polynomial relation among the forms.

For a Bott--Chern class, choose $d'\alpha=d''\alpha=0$.
The same excision and actual Stokes computation show
$P\alpha=d'G\alpha$ and $Q\alpha=d''G\alpha$ as actual forms.
Hence the boundary part of \eqref{r16pb:homotopy-formula} is
$-DG\alpha$. The $\beta_j$ are separately closed, so this proves
Bott--Chern spanning. The same descending integration argument proves
directness, now because integration and multiplication by $\xi^k$
preserve $D$-boundaries. On $\operatorname{Spec}K$ the only nonzero
group is degree $(0,0)$, equal to $\mathbf R$, which gives the final
projective-space assertion. The native top period over finite $L/K$
viewed as a $K$-space is $[L:K]$, not one.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[All-degree smooth-center modification descent]
\label{r16pb:blowup}
Let $X/K$ be smooth integral projective and let
$q:Y=\operatorname{Bl}_C X\to X$ be the blowup of a smooth closed
nowhere dense center. Then actual Bott--Chern pullback
\[
 q^*:H_{\mathrm{BC,act}}^{u,v}(X)
             \longrightarrow H_{\mathrm{BC,act}}^{u,v}(Y)
 \tag{PB16.7}\label{r16pb:blowup-inj}
\]
is injective in every bidegree. It remains injective for finite
compositions of such blowups.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Cartier or empty centers give an isomorphism. Work first with an
integral center of codimension $r+1\geq2$, and write
$i:E=\mathbf P_C(N_{C/X})\hookrightarrow Y$, $j:C\hookrightarrow X$,
$\pi:E\to C$. Let $u,v\geq1$ and suppose
$q^*\Omega=D\beta$, with $\Omega$ separately closed. Choose a smooth
first Chern form $\xi$ of $\mathcal O_E(1)$. Define the actual center
primitive
\[
                  \zeta=I_\pi(\xi^r\wedge i^*\beta).
 \tag{PB16.8}\label{r16pb:center-primitive}
\]
The output exists in bidegree $(u-1,v-1)$. Separate closedness,
differential compatibility, and the actual fiber degree formula give
$D\zeta=j^*\Omega$. Thus $\ker q^*=\ker(q^*,j^*)$.

Apply the mixed actual connecting sequence proved in Round 15. Its
kernel is exactly
\[
 \frac{\HA^{u-1,v-1}(E)}
 {i^*\HA^{u-1,v-1}(Y)+\pi^*\HA^{u-1,v-1}(C)}.
 \tag{PB16.9}\label{r16pb:exceptional-quotient}
\]
The projective-bundle formula spans its numerator by
$[\xi]^j\pi^*\HA^{u-1-j,v-1-j}(C)$ for $0\leq j\leq r$.
The $j=0$ summand already belongs to the denominator. For every
$j\geq1$, the Round 15 localized normal-Chern extension theorem
extends every displayed $D$-closed representative to a global
$D$-closed actual form on $Y$. This theorem applies in all degrees;
it extends a degreewise center lift only after multiplying by a
curvature supported where its derivative is zero. It requires no
global closed ambient lift. Thus the quotient is zero.

If $u=0$ or $v=0$, there are no $D$-boundaries. Faithfulness of actual
pullback on the dense isomorphism locus, followed by the frozen dense
faithfulness lemma, gives literal injectivity. Disconnected smooth
centers are disjoint unions of smooth integral components and can be
blown up in sequence. Finally every descended primitive is ordinary
actual, so the argument iterates without a PWS-representative condition.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[All-degree dominant Bott--Chern detection]
\label{r16pb:dominant}
Let $X/K$ be smooth integral projective and let $f:V\to X$ be a
dominant morphism from a normal integral projective variety. In every
bidegree, actual Bott--Chern pullback along $f$ is injective.
For $u,v\geq1$, a supplied identity $f^*\Omega=D\beta$ gives a
global actual primitive $D\Psi=\Omega$ on $X$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Use the frozen flattened finite-multisection diagram
\[
 \begin{array}{ccc}
 T&\xrightarrow{a}&V\\
 g\downarrow&&\downarrow f\\
 X'&\xrightarrow{b}&X,
 \end{array}
\]
where $T$ is normal integral projective, $g$ finite dominant of full
native degree $e$, and $b$ a finite composition of blowups in smooth
centers. The original-field full finite actual trace gives
\[
                \beta'=e^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}_g(a^*\beta),
                     \qquad D\beta'=b^*\Omega.
\]
Apply Theorem~\ref{r16pb:blowup} along the factors of $b$.
The zero-edge degrees follow by finite pullback faithfulness and the
corresponding edge case of that theorem. No proper actual pushforward
along $f$ or $b$ is defined in this argument. In particular it does not
assume that $f$ is finite on a prescribed cycle complement.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{What remains after analytic descent.}
This theorem removes the analytic descent obstruction for a global
curvature primitive. It does not prove that the curvature class of a
geometrically homologically trivial cycle vanishes upstairs. A supplied
regular-model numerical witness now descends through a generically
finite alteration in every degree, but existence of such witnesses in
general remains a separate problem. The sufficient implication
$\mathfrak o_X^r([Z])=0\Rightarrow G(Z)$ is retained; its general
converse is not used.
