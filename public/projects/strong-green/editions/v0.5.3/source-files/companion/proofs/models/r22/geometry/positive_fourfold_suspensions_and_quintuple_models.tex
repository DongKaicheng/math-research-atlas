\section{Positive fourfold suspensions with genuine higher strata}
\label{r22su:section}

\paragraph{The new mechanism.}
The full hyperbolic middle form on a threefold times $\mathbf P^1$
turns a positive good-reduction fourfold into a source of new
fourfold Green models.  Boundary divisors on the good fourfold
may have arbitrary middle cohomology.  Their curve-comparison
gate is the additional input, and for ample divisors it follows
from curve comparison on the ambient by an explicitly proved
Gysin isomorphism.  The construction permits triple, quadruple,
and quintuple strata; none is removed from the numerical gate.

\subsection{An actual weak-Lefschetz curve gate}

Say that a smooth projective variety over a finite field has
$\mathrm{CC}_\ell$ if every actual native rational curve which
is numerically zero has zero geometric $\ell$-adic class.

\begin{lemma}[Ample threefold boundaries of a fourfold]
\label{r22su:ampleCC}
Let $M/k$ be a smooth projective geometrically integral fourfold
with $\mathrm{CC}_\ell$, and let $i:F\hookrightarrow M$ be a
smooth geometrically integral ample divisor.  Then $F$ has
$\mathrm{CC}_\ell$.  The argument reapplies over every finite
constant field for which $M$ has that property, to every new
native curve there.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Let $z$ be a native numerically zero curve on $F$.  For each
native divisor $A$ on $M$, the actual projection formula gives
$\deg_M(i_*z\cdot A)=\deg_F(z\cdot i^*A)=0$.  Hence $i_*z$
is a numerically zero curve on $M$, and its geometric class is
zero by the hypothesis.

Weak Lefschetz for the smooth ample divisor gives the isomorphism
\[
 i^*:H^2(M_{\bar k},\mathbf Q_\ell(1))
                  \xrightarrow{\sim}H^2(F_{\bar k},\mathbf Q_\ell(1)).
 \tag{SU22.1}\label{r22su:weakL}
\]
By Poincare duality and the cohomological projection formula,
the Gysin map
\[
 i_*:H^4(F_{\bar k},\mathbf Q_\ell(2))
                  \xrightarrow{\sim}H^6(M_{\bar k},\mathbf Q_\ell(3))
 \tag{SU22.2}\label{r22su:Gysiniso}
\]
is its dual and is itself an isomorphism.  It follows that
$\operatorname{cl}_\ell(z)=0$.  Thus the cancellation of Gysin
here has a specific proved justification.  It is not an inference
from an arbitrary ambient pushforward equality.  All degrees are
on the smooth projective special-fiber varieties.  The same
argument over each new finite field proves the last assertion.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The general supplied suspension datum}

Let $R=\mathcal O_K$ be the fixed valued trait and
$\mathcal M/R$ a smooth projective geometrically integral
fourfold scheme, with special fiber $M/k$.  Choose sections
$f_1,\ldots,f_b$ of line bundles $\mathcal N_1,\ldots,\mathcal N_b$
whose special zero divisors $F_1,\ldots,F_b$ are smooth and
geometrically integral and whose union is a strict normal-crossings
divisor on $M$.  Require every connected component of every
nonempty $F_I=\bigcap_{i\in I}F_i$ to be geometrically integral
over $k$.  Thus the zero-dimensional intersections are native
points.  This last splitting can be supplied by a finite
unramified preparation; the theorem is stated over the field
where its datum is actually defined.

Choose $d\ge1$ distinct native points of $\mathbf P^1_k$ and
linear forms $l_1,\ldots,l_d$ lifting their equations.  Set
\[
 \mathcal Y=\mathcal M\times_R\mathbf P^1_R,\qquad
 \mathcal L=(\mathop{\otimes}_{i=1}^b\mathcal N_i)
                              \boxtimes\mathcal O(d),\qquad
 P=(\prod_i f_i)(\prod_j l_j).
 \tag{SU22.3}\label{r22su:datum}
\]
Order the special factors with all $F_i\times\mathbf P^1$
first and all $M\times\{p_j\}$ last.  Choose a section $Q$ of
$\mathcal L$ transverse to every connected component of every
nonempty factor intersection.  Require every positive-dimensional
cut to be geometrically integral, every zero-dimensional cut to
be finite etale, and $Q$ to avoid every zero-dimensional stratum.
The last requirement is also what transversality means on a
zero-dimensional stratum.  Assume that
\[
                  X=V(P+\pi Q)\subset\mathcal Y_K
 \tag{SU22.4}\label{r22su:X}
\]
is smooth and geometrically integral.

The two numerical hypotheses are explicit:
\[
 b^-(N^2(M)_{\mathbf R})=\rho(M)-1,\qquad
                    \mathrm{CC}_\ell(F_i)\quad\text{for every }i.
 \tag{SU22.5}\label{r22su:gates}
\]
For a complete all-finite-extension statement require these over
every finite constant field, for its whole new native cycle groups.
It is enough for the second condition to have each $F_i$ ample
and $\mathrm{CC}_\ell(M)$, by the preceding lemma.

\begin{theorem}[Positive suspension, SU22]
\label{r22su:theorem}
For every supplied datum \eqref{r22su:datum}--\eqref{r22su:gates},
the ordered ambient factor blowups give an actual projective
reduced strictly semistable model with $U_2=0$ and with native
curve comparison on every double.  Its generic fourfold has
full strong G in every codimension.  Under the all-constant-field
version of \eqref{r22su:gates}, the same holds for every new
specified geometrically homologically trivial rational cycle
after every finite valued extension of $K$.

The conclusion allows nonempty quintuple intersections.  If
four of the $F_i$ meet, each such point times any chosen point
of $\mathbf P^1$ is a genuine quintuple stratum of the actual
model.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
At a point on $s$ factor divisors the local product is
$x_1\cdots x_s$, where $s\le5$.  At a zero of $\bar Q$
transversality gives a further regular relative parameter $y$
and the actual equation $x_1\cdots x_s=\pi y$.
Necessarily $s\le4$ there, because the ambient has relative
dimension five and $Q$ avoids the zero-dimensional strata.
At a fivefold point $\bar Q$ is a unit and the equation is
already $x_1\cdots x_5=\pi u$, strictly semistable with
five distinct components.

The saturated ordered charts in DS22 apply verbatim to each
nonunit chart.  They prove regularity, reduced special fiber,
and the absence of extra vertical components.  They also give
the complete actual stratum description: if $H_I$ denotes an
old factor intersection, its continuing stratum is its iterated
blowup in
\[
                   H_I\cap H_j\cap V(\bar Q),\qquad j>\max I,
 \tag{SU22.6}\label{r22su:centers}
\]
in decreasing order of $j$.  Each nonempty center has
codimension two in $H_I$.  A preceding center cuts a subsequent
one by the divisor of the remaining factor, since both already
lie in $V(\bar Q)$.  It is Cartier by the given transversality,
so the subsequent center is unchanged by that blowup.  When a
zero-dimensional center could meet a further factor, that would
put $Q=0$ on a zero-dimensional stratum, which is excluded.
Thus the same chart proof handles all dimensions of centers
without silently throwing away the higher intersections.

Every component is consequently an iterated smooth surface
blowup of $F_i\times\mathbf P^1$ or an unchanged copy of $M$.
Each $F_i$ is a threefold, so DS22's full numerical hyperbolic
lemma applies without any positivity hypothesis on its own
middle cohomology.  The condition on $M$ gives the required
signature on the point components.  Every surface blowup adds
the negative of the center's divisor intersection form and
increases the Picard rank by one, componentwise.  The complete
negative middle index remains $\rho-1$.  For the restrictions
of any one actual relatively ample class, every component's
primitive middle form is therefore positive definite.

The unmodified doubles are either $F_i$ or connected components
of $(F_i\cap F_j)\times\mathbf P^1$.  The first have curve
comparison by \eqref{r22su:gates}.  The second are projective
bundles over smooth projective surfaces, so CD17 gives their
entire native curve comparison.  Every actual double is an
iterated smooth curve blowup of one of these original doubles,
by \eqref{r22su:centers}.  CD17 smooth-blowup comparison, or
SP19 birational ascent, retains the property.  This checks every
double, including those adjacent to genuine quadruple and
quintuple strata.

Frozen PU18 now kills the entire $U_2$, because its unique
primitive representative subspace is totally isotropic in the
positive direct sum just established.  The full numerator is
used; the proof does not assert $\ker A=\ker\rho$.
The double comparison closes CL17, MZ20 kills the resulting
cycle-visible obstruction, and VT17 produces the required
ordinary actual Green form on the exact specified support
complement with all compact actual tests.  The already closed
other-codimension fourfold Green theorems supply every degree.

Over each finite unramified extension, reapply these arguments
to the whole new numerical and Chow groups using the explicit
all-constant-field hypotheses.  RI18 then gives every finite
valued extension, with its full ramification and residue
degrees.  The final finite actual trace and VT17 retain the
original norm and the precise original specified cycle support.
There is no arbitrary proper actual pushforward.  The actual
quintuple points described in the statement lie in the unit
charts and survive every factor blowup, proving the last claim.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A new unconditional host for this theorem}

\begin{corollary}[Elliptic fourth-power host]
\label{r22su:host}
Let the good special fourfold be $M=E^4$, where
$E/\mathbf F_5$ is $y^2=x^3-x$, after any finite constant
extension.  For every supplied simple-normal-crossings
arrangement of smooth ample boundary divisors $F_i$ and every
supplied smoothing satisfying the preceding hypotheses, SU22
gives the complete all-finite-extension strong Green property.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Write $M=(E^2)\times(E^2)$.  DS22's explicit all-powers
Frobenius check, using the frozen FP21 theorem, proves the
full middle signature after every finite constant extension,
including the whole $t_2\otimes t_2$ block.  The frozen SP19
comparison applies to $M$ as a product of curves.  The ample
boundary lemma above gives curve comparison on every $F_i$.
All these arguments apply afresh to every new native cycle
over each finite constant field.  The theorem therefore applies.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope boundary and dependencies.}
This is a model-generation theorem under stated finite-field
numerical hypotheses, with an unconditional concrete host.
It is not a universal good-ambient witness, contrary to the
route excluded by ML17.  No assertion embeds every given
generic variety into such a suspension.  The threefold-times-line
hyperbolicity restated in DS22 is already the closed rank-two
projective-bundle case of PC16, with its R7 provenance.  The new
model-generation argument combines that input with the proved
ample-boundary Gysin isomorphism and the actual higher-stratum
resolution.  The remaining frozen interfaces are PC16, FP21,
PU18, CD17/SP19, CL17, MZ20,
RI18, VT17, and the already closed other-codimension results.
Weak Lefschetz and Poincare duality in
\eqref{r22su:weakL}--\eqref{r22su:Gysiniso} are ordinary
geometric etale cohomology statements; no numerical inverse
Lefschetz on an arbitrary threefold is assumed.
