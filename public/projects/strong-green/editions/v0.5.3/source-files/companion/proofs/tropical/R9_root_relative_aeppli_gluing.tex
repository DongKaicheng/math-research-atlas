\section{Relative Aeppli witnesses modulo extending pairs}
\label{sec:r9-relative-aeppli-gluing}

The correct overlap requirement for original-support repair is
weaker than equality of local primitives modulo a strict
two-colour boundary.  A discrepancy which already extends
across the cancelled part is harmless after its differentiated
partition terms have been retained.  This section proves the
precise finite gluing formula.  It is an interface with explicit
cochain inputs, not an assertion that every actual kernel
element admits the required input.

\subsection{The relative objects and their actual witnesses}

Let $N$ be an analytic open in the original space, let $C$ be
a closed subset, and set $W=N\setminus C$.  Fix $s\ge1$.
An actual extending pair means a pair in
\[
 \mathcal L^1_s(N)=B^{s-1,s}(N)\oplus B^{s,s-1}(N),
 \tag{RAG1}
\]
restricted to $W$.  A local actual Aeppli description of
$g\in B^{s,s}(W)$ is a pair $p=(\alpha,\beta)$ on $W$
and a form $E\in B^{s,s}(N)$ with
\[
                 g=\partial_1p+E|_W,
                     \qquad\partial_1p=d'\alpha+d''\beta.
 \tag{RAG2}
\]
These are actual forms and literal identities.  For purposes
of recording equivalence one may form the relative quotient
of $\mathcal L^1_s(W)$ by the sum of restrictions of
$\mathcal L^1_s(N)$ and the image of the actual operator
$\partial_2$ from PPC3.  Give that quotient its quotient
locally convex topology from the specified actual spaces;
no Hausdorffness or closed-image assertion is made.
We never infer a representative from that quotient.
Every use below supplies separately the actual witnesses
\[
             q=(A,B,M),\quad e=(e',e''),
          \qquad p_1-p_0=\partial_2q+e|_W.
 \tag{RAG3}
\]
The degrees of $A,B,M$ are $(s-2,s),(s,s-2),(s-1,s-1)$;
negative degrees mean zero.  The form $e$ is given on the
unpunctured overlap.  The equivalence is thus checked by a
finite differential identity, not by an unproved quotient
lifting or completion statement.

Local presentations are the finite actual presentations of
the supplied cochains and their common refinements.  Addition,
restriction and the two differentials are the operations used.
For each whole fixed presentation the formulas below are
finite polynomial/differential operations, with all mixed
seminorms retained.  No topology of a full analytic relation
image or a simultaneous kernel is identified with this
quotient topology.

\begin{theorem}[Two-open actual gluing modulo extending pairs]
\label{thm:r9-relative-aeppli-two-open-gluing}
Suppose $N=N_0\cup N_1$, put $W_i=N_i\cap W$, and supply
actual data $p_i\in\mathcal L^1_s(W_i)$ and
$E_i\in B^{s,s}(N_i)$ satisfying
\[
                         g|_{W_i}=\partial_1p_i+E_i|_{W_i}.
 \tag{RAG4}
\]
On $W_0\cap W_1$ supply $q_{01}=(A,B,M)$, and on
$N_0\cap N_1$ supply the actual extending pair $e$, with
\[
                     p_1-p_0=\partial_2q_{01}+e|_{W_0\cap W_1}.
 \tag{RAG5}
\]
Then explicit actual $p\in\mathcal L^1_s(W)$ and
$E\in B^{s,s}(N)$ satisfy $g=\partial_1p+E|_W$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Choose an ordinary smooth actual partition of unity
$\chi_0+\chi_1=1$ on $N$, subordinate to its two opens,
and write $\chi=\chi_1$.  On $W$ use the PPC16 formula
\[
 \begin{split}
 \alpha={}&\chi_0\alpha_0+\chi_1\alpha_1
                                  +d'\chi\wedge A+d''\chi\wedge M,\\
 \beta={}&\chi_0\beta_0+\chi_1\beta_1
                                  +d''\chi\wedge B+d'\chi\wedge M.
 \end{split}
 \tag{RAG6}
\]
The derivatives of $\chi$ have closed support contained
in $N_0\cap N_1$.  Therefore all indicated products with
overlap cochains extend by zero as actual forms.  They
need not themselves extend through $C$.

The first two terms in (RAG6) have boundary
\[
       g-\chi_0E_0-\chi_1E_1
          +d'\chi\wedge(\alpha_1-\alpha_0)
          +d''\chi\wedge(\beta_1-\beta_0).
 \tag{RAG7}
\]
Substitute (RAG5).  The four correction terms cancel
exactly the part containing $\partial_2q_{01}$, by
the full two-colour calculation PPC18.  Both
$D\chi\wedge M$ terms are included and cancel with
opposite signs.  Hence
\[
             \partial_1p=g-\chi_0E_0-\chi_1E_1
                              +d'\chi\wedge e'+d''\chi\wedge e''.
 \tag{RAG8}
\]
Set, now on all of $N$,
\[
                 E=\chi_0E_0+\chi_1E_1
                              -d'\chi\wedge e'-d''\chi\wedge e''.
 \tag{RAG9}
\]
Each term is actual on its domain and has closed support
there, so its zero extension is actual.  In contrast with
the original cochains, the pair $e$ was supplied through
$C$, so (RAG9) is an actual extending form.  Equation
(RAG8) proves the asserted identity.
\end{proof}

No extra closedness hypothesis on $e$ is needed: applying
$\partial_1$ to (RAG5) and using (RAG4) already gives
$\partial_1e=(E_0-E_1)|_{W_0\cap W_1}$.  In the frequent
case $E_0=E_1$, the restriction of $e$ to the punctured
overlap is a closed pair.  No assertion of closedness on
the whole unpunctured overlap is needed.  It is still wrong
to discard its contribution in (RAG9).

\subsection{Finite iteration and the original-support bridge}

The two-open theorem can be iterated through a finite
cover if, at each step, actual witnesses (RAG5) are supplied
between the already constructed pair on the preceding
union and the pair on the next open.  The next residue is
then the explicit form (RAG9), not an assumed unchanged
residue.  This is a finite compatibility certificate for
the entire chosen cover.  Pairwise witnesses alone are not
declared to provide the inductive witnesses or every higher
overlap compatibility.

For the original CI difference take
$g=G_{s_1}-G_{s_0}$ on $V$ and an open $N\subset U$
containing the cancelled part $C_U$.  If the local paths
and the finite witnesses just described are supplied,
(RAG6)--(RAG9) construct actual global $\alpha,\beta,E$
on $N\cap V,N\cap V,N$, respectively.  Using this pair
and this actual extending $E$ in RSP5 constructs the
actual Green form on exactly $U$ and proves the strong
formula for every actual compact test over the original
$K$.  The witnesses can be verified in fixed common
finite presentations.  Their existence for arbitrary
analytic matrix pivot covers remains an additional,
presently unproved geometric assertion.

The rank-two commuting-diagonal calculation CDP supplies
an explicit all-wall example of (RAG5) in which $e$ is
nonzero.  On a chamber it also proves that the same
chosen primitive discrepancy has no strict $\partial_2$
witness.  Thus allowing the extending pair is mathematically
necessary for that prescribed family of local choices.
This is a failure of the strict witness route, not a
counterexample to a Green representative, to full
OPEN-KERNEL, or to another choice of primitives.

The relative quotient introduced after (RAG2) is used
only to name this precise compatibility.  It is neither
a renamed existence assumption for general strong G nor
a new exactness theorem for all actual forms.  The actual
realization in each proved application is (RAG6), followed
by (RAG9) and RSP5; when the needed witnesses have not
been constructed, the application remains conditional.
