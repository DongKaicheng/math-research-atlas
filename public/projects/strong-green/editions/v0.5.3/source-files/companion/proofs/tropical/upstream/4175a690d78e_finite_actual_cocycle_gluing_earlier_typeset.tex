\section{Finite actual partition gluing with displayed higher witnesses}
\label{sec:r10-finite-actual-cocycle-gluing}

This section supplies two finite, checkable gluing formulas beyond
the frozen two-open RAG formula.  The first uses a literal cocycle of
actual triples on an arbitrary finite cover.  The second treats a
three-open cover with a displayed quadruple and an extending triple
on its triple intersection.  The existence of those cochains is an
input, not an exactness assertion for an entire actual kernel.

\subsection{Objects and the partition insertion operators}

Let $N$ be an ordinary analytic open over the original $K$, let $C$
be closed in $N$, and put $W=N\setminus C$.  Fix $s\ge1$ and use
the actual spaces
\[
\begin{split}
 L_0^s&=B^{s,s},\\
 L_1^s&=B^{s-1,s}\oplus B^{s,s-1},\\
 L_2^s&=B^{s-2,s}\oplus B^{s,s-2}\oplus B^{s-1,s-1},\\
 L_3^s&=B^{s-3,s}\oplus B^{s,s-3}
                 \oplus B^{s-2,s-1}\oplus B^{s-1,s-2}.
\end{split}                                                    \tag{FG1}
\]
Negative bidegrees mean zero.  We use the exact operators
\[
\begin{split}
 \partial_1(\alpha,\beta)&=d'\alpha+d''\beta,\\
 \partial_2(A,B,M)&=(d'A+d''M,d''B+d'M),\\
 \partial_3(U,V,P,Q)&=(d'U+d''P,d''V+d'Q,d'P+d''Q).
\end{split}                                                    \tag{FG2}
\]
The first two are frozen RAG/CPT inputs; the third is the operator
of FIV1.  Direct full differentiation gives
$\partial_1\partial_2=\partial_2\partial_3=0$.

For an ordinary smooth actual scalar $\chi$, define actual
insertion operators $K_j(\chi):L_j^s\longrightarrow L_{j-1}^s$ by
\[
\begin{split}
 K_1(\chi)(\alpha,\beta)
       & =d'\chi\wedge\alpha+d''\chi\wedge\beta,\\
 K_2(\chi)(A,B,M)
       & =(d'\chi\wedge A+d''\chi\wedge M,
                         d''\chi\wedge B+d'\chi\wedge M),\\
 K_3(\chi)(U,V,P,Q)
       & =(d'\chi\wedge U+d''\chi\wedge P,
          d''\chi\wedge V+d'\chi\wedge Q,
          d'\chi\wedge P+d''\chi\wedge Q).
\end{split}                                                    \tag{FG3}
\]
They retain both differential colours and every mixed coefficient.
The full Leibniz rules give
\[
 \partial_j(\chi u)=\chi\partial_j u+K_j(\chi)u,
 \qquad
 \partial_{j-1}K_j(\chi)+K_{j-1}(\chi)\partial_j=0
                                     \quad(j=2,3).             \tag{FG4}
\]
For the second identity, apply $\partial_{j-1}$ to the first and
use $\partial_{j-1}\partial_j=0$, including when it is applied to
$\chi u$.  Thus the terms involving $D\chi$ are included before
they cancel.  Nothing treats $d'\chi$ and $d''\chi$ as a single
differential.  Also $\sum_i K_j(\chi_i)=0$ whenever $\sum_i\chi_i=1$.

Use a finite ordinary open cover $N=\bigcup_iN_i$ and an ordinary
smooth partition $(\chi_i)$ with $\operatorname{supp}_N\chi_i$
closed and contained in $N_i$.  Write $W_i=W\cap N_i$.
Every product below is extended by zero from its stated overlap.
The factors $\chi_i$, $d'\chi_i$, $d''\chi_i$ ensure that its support
is closed in the ambient $N$ or $W$ and contained in the domain
where its remaining actual coefficient is defined.  In particular,
this does not extend an unweighted singular cochain across $C$.

All cochains below are alternating in their indices, with repeated
indices giving zero.  For example,
\[
 (\delta q)_{ijk}=q_{jk}-q_{ik}+q_{ij},\qquad
 (\delta t)_{lijk}=t_{ijk}-t_{ljk}+t_{lik}-t_{lij}.               \tag{FG5}
\]
These are actual restrictions and finite differences.  The direct
sums in (FG1) have the direct-sum topologies of their own actual
coefficient-final spaces.  No Hausdorff quotient or closed-range
claim is imposed or obtained.

\subsection{A finite cover with a literal triple cocycle}

Supply $g\in L_0^s(W)$, actual $p_i\in L_1^s(W_i)$ and
$E_i\in L_0^s(N_i)$ with
\[
                         g|_{W_i}=\partial_1p_i+E_i|_{W_i}.
                                                                  \tag{FG6}
\]
On every punctured double overlap supply $q_{ij}\in L_2^s(W_{ij})$;
on the unpunctured overlap supply $e_{ij}\in L_1^s(N_{ij})$, with
\[
 p_j-p_i=\partial_2q_{ij}+e_{ij}|_{W_{ij}},\qquad
                         \delta q=0\quad\hbox{on }W_{ijk}.
                                                                  \tag{FG7}
\]
No closedness assumption on the extending pairs $e_{ij}$ is needed.

\begin{theorem}[Finite actual cocycle gluing]
Under the displayed hypotheses, set
\[
\begin{split}
 p&=\sum_i\chi_i p_i+
                   \sum_{i,j}\chi_i K_2(\chi_j)q_{ij},\\
 E&=\sum_i\chi_i E_i-
                   \sum_{i,j}\chi_i K_1(\chi_j)e_{ij}.
\end{split}                                                       \tag{FG8}
\]
The first formula is actual on $W$, the second is actual on $N$,
and they satisfy $g=\partial_1p+E|_W$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Put $\bar p=\sum_i\chi_i p_i$ and
$c_1=\sum_{i,j}\chi_i K_2(\chi_j)q_{ij}$.  Full differentiation
and (FG4) give
\[
\begin{split}
 \partial_1\bar p
       &=g-\sum_i\chi_iE_i+\sum_iK_1(\chi_i)p_i,\\
 \partial_1c_1
       &=W_2-\sum_{i,j}\chi_iK_1(\chi_j)\partial_2q_{ij},\\
 W_2&=\sum_{i,j}K_1(\chi_i)K_2(\chi_j)q_{ij}.
\end{split}                                                       \tag{FG9}
\]
The sum of $\chi_iK_1(\chi_j)(p_j-p_i)$ equals
$\sum_jK_1(\chi_j)p_j$ because $\sum_i\chi_i=1$ and
$\sum_jK_1(\chi_j)=0$.  Using (FG7), it remains to prove $W_2=0$.
Insert $\sum_k\chi_k=1$.  Since
$q_{ij}=(\delta q)_{kij}+q_{kj}-q_{ki}$, summing the last two
terms over $i$ or $j$ kills them.  Hence
\[
       W_2=\sum_{k,i,j}\chi_kK_1(\chi_i)K_2(\chi_j)
                                                    (\delta q)_{kij}=0.
                                                                  \tag{FG10}
\]
All manipulations are on the full actual triple intersections,
with the closed-support zero extensions already specified.
Equations (FG9)--(FG10) yield (FG8) with the stated sign of $E$.
\end{proof}

For two opens, antisymmetry and $\chi_0+\chi_1=1$ reduce the second
sum in $p$ to $K_2(\chi_1)q_{01}$, and the second sum in $E$ to
$K_1(\chi_1)e_{01}$.  Thus (FG8) specializes literally to RAG6--RAG9.

A concrete way to satisfy the cocycle requirement is an actual
normalization on each member of a fixed finite path family.  If
$p_i-p_*=\partial_2a_i+b_i$ are displayed identities against the
same actual reference expression, put $q_{ij}=a_j-a_i$ and
$e_{ij}=b_j-b_i$.  Then $\delta q=0$ literally.  For finite diagonal
paths on one common domain with a common analytic endpoint, SL13
constructs these $a_i,b_i$.  This choice does not prove a third
boundary for the separately chosen raw CS square triples.  Nor does
it assert that arbitrary local pivot domains have a common reference
path or such normalizations.  A globally defined diagonal endpoint
already belongs to the frozen single-path existence classes.

\subsection{Three opens with an actual quadruple witness}

Now let the cover have exactly three members $N_0,N_1,N_2$.
Retain (FG6) and the first identity of (FG7), but replace $\delta q=0$
by displayed actual data
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 t_{012}\in L_3^s(W_{012}),\qquad
 f_{012}\in L_2^s(N_{012}),\\
                         (\delta q)_{012}
                         =\partial_3t_{012}+f_{012}|_{W_{012}}.
 \end{gathered}                                                    \tag{FG11}
\]
Extend these cochains by alternation of their indices.  No existence
of $t$ or $f$ is inferred from the equation
$\partial_2\delta q=-\delta e$.  In particular, closedness of
$\delta q$ alone would not supply (FG11).

\begin{theorem}[Three-open gluing with a displayed next witness]
The explicit actual forms
\[
\begin{split}
 p={}&\sum_i\chi_i p_i+
       \sum_{i,j}\chi_iK_2(\chi_j)q_{ij}
       -\sum_{k,i,j}\chi_kK_2(\chi_i)K_3(\chi_j)t_{kij},\\
 E={}&\sum_i\chi_iE_i-
       \sum_{i,j}\chi_iK_1(\chi_j)e_{ij}
       -\sum_{k,i,j}\chi_kK_1(\chi_i)K_2(\chi_j)f_{kij}
\end{split}                                                       \tag{FG12}
\]
satisfy $g=\partial_1p+E|_W$.  Here $p$ is actual on all of $W$
and $E$ is actual across $C$ on all of $N$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The calculation (FG9) still holds, and the first equality of
(FG10) still expresses $W_2$ in terms of $\delta q$.
Using (FG11) and (FG4) twice gives
\[
\begin{split}
 W_2&=\partial_1 c_2-W_3+
           \sum_{k,i,j}\chi_kK_1(\chi_i)K_2(\chi_j)f_{kij},\\
 c_2&=\sum_{k,i,j}\chi_kK_2(\chi_i)K_3(\chi_j)t_{kij},\\
 W_3&=\sum_{k,i,j}K_1(\chi_k)K_2(\chi_i)K_3(\chi_j)t_{kij}.
\end{split}                                                       \tag{FG13}
\]
In fact
$K_1(\chi_i)K_2(\chi_j)\partial_3
 =\partial_1K_2(\chi_i)K_3(\chi_j)$, with two sign changes;
differentiating the leading scalar $\chi_k$ produces $W_3$.
This verifies the minus sign of $c_2$ in (FG12).

As in (FG10), insert $\sum_l\chi_l=1$ and use the identity
$t_{kij}=(\delta t)_{lkij}+t_{lij}-t_{lkj}+t_{lki}$.
Each of the last three terms is killed by the sum of the $K$ factor
in its absent index.  It follows that
\[
 W_3=\sum_{l,k,i,j}\chi_lK_1(\chi_k)K_2(\chi_i)K_3(\chi_j)
                                                     (\delta t)_{lkij}=0.
                                                                  \tag{FG14}
\]
The last equality is literal: an alternating four-index cochain
on a cover with only three indices is zero.  Substitute (FG13)
in (FG9) and subtract $\partial_1c_2$.  The result is precisely
$g-\partial_1p=E|_W$ in (FG12).  In particular the differentiated
extending triple contributes the last term in $E$ and is retained.
\end{proof}

The same formula works on a finite cover with more than three
members if the actual input also satisfies $\delta t=0$.  Without
that hypothesis the exact unremoved residual is $W_3$, not zero.
Further displayed actual cochains would be needed to remove it.
FIV supplies (FG11) only when the actual overlap triples have first
been identified with its specified five-potential face expression.
It does not identify arbitrary CS, shear, or pivot triples with that
expression.

\subsection{The original-support representative and precise scope}

For the original regular CI difference, retain the frozen notation
$g=G_{s_1}-G_{s_0}$ on $V=X^{\rm an}\setminus(|Y_0|\cup|Y_1|)$,
$U=X^{\rm an}\setminus|[Y_1]-[Y_0]|$, and
$C_U=(|Y_0|\cup|Y_1|)\cap U$.  If a neighborhood $N\subset U$
of $C_U$ carries either of the displayed finite certificates above,
the resulting actual $p=(\alpha,\beta)$ and $E$ are valid RSP inputs.
Choose the frozen ordinary cutoff $\zeta$ on $U$, equal to one near
$C_U$ and with closed $U$-support inside $N$.  On $V$ set
\[
 g_V=g-d'\widetilde{\zeta\alpha}-d''\widetilde{\zeta\beta}.
                                                                  \tag{FG15}
\]
The tildes mean the actual closed-support zero extensions to $V$.
Near $C_U$ this agrees with the actual form $E$, so gluing constructs
an actual $g_Z\in B^{r-1,r-1}(U)$.

For every original actual compact test
$\eta\in B_c^{n-r,n-r}(X^{\rm an})$, the frozen support lemma gives
$\operatorname{supp}D\eta\Subset V$.  Full actual Stokes annihilates
each correction in (FG15), because its product with $D\eta$ has
compact support in $V$.  Frozen CI8 therefore gives
\[
                    \int_U g_Z\wedge D\eta=-\int_Z\eta.
                                                                  \tag{FG16}
\]
No test is multiplied by a cutoff on $X$, and no larger cycle support
is removed.  The fixed field, its absolute value, and all actual
tests are unchanged.

The new content is the explicit finite partition calculation with
its complete higher and extending terms.  It is an actual-certificate
theorem, not an unconditional theorem that arbitrary covers possess
the certificate.  In particular it does not by itself add a new
cycle-existence class to R9 or close general strong G.  Its role is
to specify exactly what a successful higher matrix-relation
construction must provide and exactly how those forms produce the
required representative.

Every formula is a finite local actual presentation, respects full
analytic refinement, and keeps all mixed jets.  On a fixed whole
coefficient presentation its products and derivatives satisfy the
ordinary finite Leibniz bounds into a common refinement.  No
comparison with a probe topology, global strictness assertion,
general higher-form descent, or complete BB-height statement is
made.  The construction is by the root agent; independent reviews
must exclude that constructor and bind the exact final source hash.
