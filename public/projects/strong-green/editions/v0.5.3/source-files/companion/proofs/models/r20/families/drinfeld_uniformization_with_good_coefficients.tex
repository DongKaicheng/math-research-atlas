\section{Drinfeld uniformization with an arbitrary native good coefficient}
\label{r20du:section}

\paragraph{Status and scope.}
This is a current-interface application of established uniformization
geometry, not a priority claim.  The coefficient has good projective
reduction over the field on which the Drinfeld models are supplied.
No claim about an arbitrary potentially-good coefficient is inferred
from weight--monodromy alone.  All Chow groups and motives below have
rational coefficients.

\subsection{The published input and the actual Chow consequence}

We use the following four precise facts from T. Ito,
\emph{Weight--monodromy conjecture for $p$-adically uniformized
varieties}, Invent. Math. 159 (2005), 607--656,
\url{https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0301201}:
Theorem 1.2 proves weight--monodromy in every degree for a projective
Drinfeld quotient; Section 6.1 gives its projective algebraization;
Section 6.2 passes to a finite etale cover with a strictly semistable
model; Sections 4.1--4.3 and 6.2 identify that model's components and
closed strata with products of the varieties $B_d$, obtained by
successive blowups of rational linear subspaces of projective space.
These facts include the existence of the projective model and are
stronger than a statement solely about analytic uniformization.

\begin{lemma}[Native split Tate motives of the model strata]
\label{r20du:tate}
Let $k$ be the native finite residue field.  Each $B_d/k$, and each
closed stratum in the strictly semistable cover just described, has
an actual rational Chow motive which is a finite direct sum of Tate
motives over $k$.  This assertion remains true under arbitrary
constant-field extension.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Use induction on $d$, with $B_0=\operatorname{Spec}k$.
At the stage which blows up the strict transforms of the
$k$-rational $b$-planes, these strict transforms are disjoint and
each is $B_b$.  Indeed their mutual intersections have already been
blown up; restricting the preceding sequence to one $b$-plane
performs exactly the lower-dimensional linear-subspace sequence.
The centers are actual smooth closed subschemes over $k$.

For a smooth center $Z\subset W$ of codimension $c$, the Chow
blowup decomposition is induced by the actual maps
\[
 (\alpha,(\beta_a))\longmapsto
 f^*\alpha+\sum_{a=1}^{c-1}
 j_*\bigl(\xi^{a-1}p^*\beta_a\bigr),
 \qquad \xi=c_1(\mathcal O_{\mathbf P(N_{Z/W})}(1)).
 \tag{DU20.1}\label{r20du:blowup}
\]
The same formula after every smooth projective test product gives
the actual Chow-motive isomorphism
\[
 h(\operatorname{Bl}_Z W)
 \simeq h(W)\oplus\bigoplus_{a=1}^{c-1}h(Z)(a).
 \tag{DU20.2}\label{r20du:motive}
\]
Here the twist notation means the usual Lefschetz summands in the
Chow blowup formula, independently of the variance convention.
Projective space has its standard actual split Tate decomposition.
Induction in \eqref{r20du:motive} therefore proves the statement
for $B_d$.  The stated closed-stratum product description then
proves it for every stratum by the tensor product of the actual
decompositions.  All defining correspondences remain inverse
after constant extension.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[A full cohomological inverse at every invertible prime]
\label{r20du:inverse}
For a regular projective reduced strictly semistable model of
relative dimension $d$ whose components have native split Tate
Chow motives, its incidence correspondence
$A=\sigma\rho$ admits an actual rational matrix
\[
 G\in\bigoplus_{i,j}\operatorname{CH}^{d-1}(D_j\times D_i)
 \quad\text{with}\quad A^H G^H A^H=A^H
 \tag{DU20.3}\label{r20du:inverse-eq}
\]
on full geometric cohomology for every $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}k$.
The same rational $G$ works for all these primes.  Tensoring with
the diagonal of any smooth projective $T_0/k$ preserves this
identity on all mixed cohomology classes.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
PI20 applies numerical-motive semisimplicity to the actual
degree-one incidence correspondence and supplies rational $G$
for which $E=A-AGA$ is numerically zero as a correspondence.
Each component product has a split Tate Chow motive by
Lemma~\ref{r20du:tate}.  For such a motive, the Chow group in any
fixed degree is a finite sum of copies of $\mathbf Q$ with its
perfect complementary intersection pairing.  The actual Tate
decomposition identifies this pairing with the duality pairing
of these summands.  Consequently numerical zero is already zero
in its Chow group.  In particular each entry of $E$ is zero in
Chow, and its class vanishes for every invertible prime.

The product incidence is $\Delta_{T_0}\boxtimes A$ and its
candidate inverse is $\Delta_{T_0}\boxtimes G$; their error is
$\Delta_{T_0}\boxtimes E$.  The Kunneth formula proves its
vanishing on the full cohomology of the component products.
No numerical--homological comparison on $T_0$ is used.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The local result, all new extension cycles, and heights}

\begin{theorem}[Arbitrary native good coefficient and Drinfeld factors]
\label{r20du:local}
Fix a finite valued $K/\mathbf Q_p$ with its original absolute value.
Let $T/K$ be smooth, projective and geometrically integral, with a
smooth projective model over $\mathcal O_K$.  For
$1\leq b\leq m$, let $d_b\geq1$ and let
$\Gamma_b\subset\operatorname{PGL}_{d_b+1}(K)$ be a cocompact
torsion-free discrete subgroup.  Let $X_b/K$ be the smooth
projective algebraization of
$\Gamma_b\backslash\Omega_K^{d_b}$.
Then
\[
 X=T\times_K X_1\times_K\cdots\times_K X_m
 \tag{DU20.4}\label{r20du:generic}
\]
has strong G in every codimension $1\leq r\leq\dim X$.
The assertion holds for every geometrically homologically trivial
rational cycle after every finite extension of $K$, including new
cycles which do not descend to $K$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\emph{Step 1: choose actual strict models.}
For each factor choose the finite-index subgroup in Ito's
Section 6.2.  This gives a finite etale cover $X_b'\to X_b$
whose projective model is reduced strictly semistable and whose
closed strata satisfy Lemma~\ref{r20du:tate}.  The corresponding
product cover has constant positive degree.  We first prove the
result on the cover, and descend only after constructing its
actual Green solution.

\emph{Step 2: retain the actual motive property in the product.}
Apply the staircase construction and the full boundary-stratum
blowup formulas of AT20 to the factor models successively.
Its component towers use actual blowups along smooth closed
boundary strata.  Formula \eqref{r20du:motive} and the projective
bundle formula show, by induction, that every closed stratum of
the resulting projective strictly semistable model is split
Tate.  AT20's fan argument is independent of the dimensions of
the factors.  The product with the smooth model of $T$ is still
regular projective and strictly semistable.  Its component
diagram is the previous diagram tensored with $T_0$.

\emph{Step 3: remove the finite algebraic-image error.}
Use Lemma~\ref{r20du:inverse} on the diagram before tensoring.
The resulting identity on the full coefficient diagram gives
PI20's actual kernel correction in every degree, hence
$\Delta_A=\mathscr R_A=0$ there.  This applies to arbitrary
mixed cycle classes; it makes no assertion that all cycles are
exterior products.

\emph{Step 4: kill the generic inertial term.}
Ito's theorem supplies weight--monodromy for every $X_b'$ in
all degrees.  Smooth proper base change and purity supply it
for $T$.  Under Kunneth, tensor products of the monodromy
filtrations are the centered monodromy filtration for the sum
of the commuting nilpotent operators: this follows by splitting
the associated $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ strings, or equivalently by
the usual tensor-product primitive decomposition.  Tensoring
pure graded pieces adds their weights.  Thus weight--monodromy
holds in every degree of the product.  OW19 gives $\tau=0$
for its geometrically homologically trivial cycles.  AK19 and
CR19 now kill the model obstruction using Step 3; FD17 and
VT17 give the strong actual Green form.  If the total dimension
is at most two, use the already frozen endpoint/surface
theorems instead of invoking AK19 outside its stated range.

\emph{Step 5: treat new cycles after finite extensions.}
First make any finite unramified extension.  The same strict
models base change, their split Tate correspondences remain
actual inverse correspondences, and weight--monodromy is
unchanged.  Reapply Steps 2--4 to every new cycle over that
field.  RI18's all-unramified-to-all-finite norm-detection
theorem then gives G for every new cycle on the product cover
over every finite extension, including ramified extensions.
This invokes its native-model inertial numerical action and
full norm formula, not an assertion that new cycles are
pullbacks or that the base-changed singular model is strict.

\emph{Step 6: descend to the original quotient.}
Over any fixed finite extension $L/K$, pull a specified
homologically trivial cycle on $X_L$ to the finite etale
product cover.  Step 5 applies to that actual pulled cycle.
The frozen all-degree finite actual trace and projection
formula descend its Green form, dividing by the complete
cover degree.  The complement is exactly the inverse image
of the specified original support because this map is finite.
The result has the original support and is tested against
all actual compactly supported forms.  All field descents,
if any, use the complete field degree and the original
absolute value.  No arbitrary proper actual pushforward
or finiteness assumption on an alteration complement enters.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A local criterion for the full height domain]
\label{r20du:height}
Let $Y/F$ be smooth projective and geometrically integral over
a number field.  Suppose at every finite place $v$ there is
a finite extension of $F_v$ over which $Y$ is isomorphic to
a product as in Theorem~\ref{r20du:local}, with its native
good coefficient and the stated Drinfeld factors.  Factors
and the decomposition may depend on $v$.
Then the frozen PH16 pairing is defined on the full
geometrically homologically trivial rational Chow groups in
every complementary degree, over $F$ and over every finite
number-field extension.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The local theorem covers every finite extension and every new
cycle.  For a new completion use a finite compositum with the
supplied local field, then descend by the complete degree.
Thus every finite local obstruction vanishes on the full
cycle domain.  PH16 and GA17 give the asserted global
pairing and regular-model admissible Chow lifts.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Historical bridge and remaining limits.}
The R7 literature audit already records Drinfeld-uniformized
varieties among previously known admissible height cases,
following Zhang's Remark 1.6.5.  We therefore claim no new
standalone height domain for a Drinfeld quotient.  The present
argument records the actual Chow-motive certificate, its
arbitrary native good coefficient, finite products, and
the current ordinary-actual/full-test/full-degree interfaces.
It does not assert that the generic quotient is of abelian
Chow type.  It also does not infer the theorem for an
arbitrary potentially-good $T$ without a common field on
which the needed strict Tate-stratum model is supplied:
after ramification, weight--monodromy alone does not certify
the new component Chow motives.  General G and unrestricted
full Beilinson--Bloch heights remain open.
