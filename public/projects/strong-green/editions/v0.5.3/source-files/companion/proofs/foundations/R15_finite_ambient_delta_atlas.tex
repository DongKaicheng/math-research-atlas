\section{A finite ambient delta presenter after horizontal excision}
\label{r15ad:section}

This construction addresses an ordinary atlas issue.  It does not
extend an intrinsic delta-form from an unspecified tropical image.
The local ambient presenters are kept throughout, and their sum
is formed using genuine Euclidean smooth cutoffs with explicit
domain margins.  No closedness of the ambient presenter is asserted.

\begin{lemma}[Two nested algebraic Laurent neighborhoods]
\label{r15ad:nested}
Let $S$ be a smooth algebraic $K$-variety, let $x\in
S^{\mathrm{an}}$, and let $\alpha$ be an ordinary actual
form on the inverse image of an ordinary neighborhood of
$x$ in $S\times\mathbf P^1$.  After shrinking a compact
algebraic Laurent base neighborhood $A$ of $x$, there
are finitely many pairs of compact algebraic Laurent
domains $A_i\subset B_i$ in one of the boxes
\[
       A\times\{|t_i|\leq R_i\},\qquad
                     t_i=z\ \hbox{or}\ t_i=1/z,
\tag{AD15.1}\label{r15ad:boxes}
\]
with the following properties:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The ordinary interiors of the $A_i$ cover the
complete fiber over $x$, hence all complete fibers over
a smaller ordinary base neighborhood.
\item Each $B_i$ is contained in an ordinary actual
presentation chart for $\alpha$.  Both domains are
defined, relative to the common base domain $A$, by the
same finite polynomial norm functions with positive
constant thresholds, together with the fixed
chart-coordinate bound.  Every such relative inequality
of $B_i$, including its chart bound, holds on $A_i$
with a uniform strict margin.  The common condition
that the base point belongs to $A$ is not one of these
strict relative inequalities.
\item The unit moments in that presentation are analytic
on an ordinary neighborhood of $B_i$, and its Euclidean
delta presenter may be taken globally defined on its
Euclidean coordinate space.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Choose an affine algebraic base chart containing $x$.
At a point of its compact projective-line fiber, choose
one affine fiber coordinate and an ordinary presentation
chart.  The topology on an affine algebraic
analytification is generated by the absolute values of
regular functions.  A finite list of strict positive
scalar bounds therefore defines a neighborhood of that
point inside the presentation chart.  Choose two successively
tighter closed lists of bounds still containing the point
in their strict interiors.  Choose two successive positive
chart-coordinate bounds as well.  The outer closed list
gives $B_i$ and the inner one gives $A_i$.  Its strict
margin is numerical, not an inference from the ordinary
interior of a closed Laurent domain.  After fixing a
compact algebraic Laurent base neighborhood, these are
the asserted compact domains.  Compactness of the fiber
gives finitely many pairs.  Properness of projection
shrinks the base so their interiors cover every complete
fiber there.

The presenting unit map sends the compact set $B_i$
into a compact subset of the Euclidean open on which
its delta presenter is defined.  Multiply that presenter
by a Euclidean smooth compactly supported cutoff equal
to one on a neighborhood of this compact image and
extend by zero.  The cutoff has its support inside the
presenter's original open, so this is a literal
Euclidean delta-form with an ordinary zero collar.  It
does not require extending a delta-form from a
polyhedral image or preserving ambient closedness.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[One compact ambient delta presenter on a local split cover]
\label{r15ad:presenter}
Let $S/K$ be smooth algebraic of pure dimension $d$,
let $U\subset S^{\mathrm{an}}$ be ordinary open, and
let $\alpha\in B^{p,q}(U\times\mathbf P^1)$, with
$p,q\geq1$.  At any $x\in U$, after shrinking to an
ordinary base neighborhood and passing to a finite
etale root-splitting cover, there are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a compact base neighborhood $B$ downstairs and its
entire inverse image $B'$ under the chosen finite etale
cover, whose interiors are the ordinary neighborhoods
under consideration;
\item regular pairwise distinct marks
$a_1=0,a_2,\ldots,a_m$, with all their nonzero
differences units, and the mark at infinity;
\item a finite tuple of base analytic units $b$, and
the genuine unit tuple
\[
             M=(b,w_1,\ldots,w_m),\qquad w_j=z-a_j,
\tag{AD15.2}\label{r15ad:moment}
\]
on the complement $W$ of these marked sections;
\item an ordinary PWS scalar $\rho$ on
$B'\times\mathbf P^1$, zero on ordinary neighborhoods
of all the marked sections and of infinity;
\item a single compactly supported Euclidean delta-form
$\gamma\in B^{p,q}_c(\mathbf R^{N+m})$;
\end{enumerate}
such that, writing $\alpha'$ for the actual pullback,
\[
                    \rho\alpha'|_W=t_M^\star\gamma
\tag{AD15.3}\label{r15ad:actual-presenter}
\]
as an ordinary actual identity.  The current push of
$\alpha'$ along the projective line equals that of
$\rho\alpha'$ against every ordinary smooth compact
complementary base test.  The ambient support of
$\gamma$ is in particular proper over its base
coordinate space.

If $\alpha$ has a fixed intrinsic tridegree, $\gamma$
can be chosen with that same tridegree.  The proposition
is an upstairs presentation statement.  It does not
assert a finite actual trace in bidegrees beyond those
already established.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
\emph{Rational moments, strict domains, and splitting.}
Apply Lemma~\ref{r15ad:nested}.  Approximate each analytic
unit on $B_i$ by a rational function in its algebraic
Laurent affinoid algebra with error strictly below its
positive minimum norm.  The numerator and denominator
are units on $B_i$ and their norm equality holds on an
ordinary neighborhood.  Lemma~\ref{r15tc:norm-tuples}
shows that this exact norm replacement is valid for
arbitrary actual presenters, including positive-delta
ones.

Apply the small original-field coefficient perturbation
of Proposition~\ref{r15b:separable-perturbation} jointly
to all these numerators and denominators and to all the
polynomials defining both layers of domain bounds.
An inner and its paired outer condition on the same
polynomial use one common polynomial occurrence and
one common perturbation.  For that occurrence impose
the error bound below the minimum of every inner and
outer threshold on its entire fixed
box \eqref{r15ad:boxes}.  Threshold stability then
preserves both domains, every strict defining condition,
and all the uniform margins.  For each presenting
numerator and denominator impose the positive minimum
norm bound on $B_i$.  The finite list of requirements
still allows a generic arbitrarily small perturbation
in $K$.  Include the fixed unperturbed chart coordinates
$z$ and $1/z$ in the data rather than perturbing their
bounding inequalities.

The perturbation makes the relevant fiber polynomials
simultaneously squarefree, mutually disjoint, and of
nonzero leading and constant coefficient at $x$.
The local splitting construction of
Lemma~\ref{r15b:local-etale-splitting} therefore supplies a
finite etale cover on which their roots are regular,
distinct, and different from zero and infinity.
Shrink the downstairs neighborhood and choose a compact
base domain $B$ inside it.  Throughout take $B'$ to be
its entire inverse image, not a selected analytic sheet.
The construction below is simultaneous on all of $B'$;
if the algebraic cover has several connected components,
one may perform it on each component and retain all of
them.  Any later finite descent must use the full native
degree of the cover so retained.  Add all nonzero leading coefficients, root
differences, and required nonzero base coefficients
to the base unit tuple $b$.  Every presenting rational
moment has valuation an affine linear function of
\eqref{r15ad:moment}; denote the resulting linear map
for chart $i$ by $L_i$.  The domain norm functions
have the same property.  This includes occurrences
originally written in $1/z$, after clearing the fixed
power of $z$.

For each $i$, let $E_i\subset\mathbf R^{N+m}$ be
the open set of strict relative outer-domain inequalities
for $B_i$, also imposing the strict fixed chart-coordinate
bound.  The common base-domain condition is omitted
from $E_i$, since the source has already been restricted
to $B'$, over a base domain contained in $A$.
On actual points over $B'$ the implication
\[
                          t_M(y)\in E_i\ \Longrightarrow\ y\in B_i
\tag{AD15.4}\label{r15ad:domain-implication}
\]
holds.  The fixed coordinate condition first places
the point in the original box; only there is the
threshold-stability assertion applied.  Thus no estimate
made on one box is used to admit points outside that box.
Moreover all points of $A_i\cap W$ satisfy these
inequalities with their uniform strict margins.

Let $\gamma_i$ be the global Euclidean delta presenter
from Lemma~\ref{r15ad:nested}.  On $B_i\cap W$ the
actual form is therefore
\[
                 \alpha'=t_M^\star(L_i^\star\gamma_i).
\tag{AD15.5}\label{r15ad:local-ambient}
\]
Here $L_i^\star$ is Mihatsch's affine ambient star-pullback
of Proposition 2.38 on the smooth Euclidean source.
It is again a Euclidean delta-form and has the stated
refinement compatibility.  No full-rank hypothesis on
$L_i$ is required.  In particular this is neither a
piecewise affine root-ray pullback nor merely an appeal
to a pullback defined only for surjective affine maps.
The ambient construction and compatibility are precisely
those of Mihatsch Propositions 2.38--2.40.

\emph{Current-neutral horizontal excision.}
For each root section $s_j$, the projection is an
analytic retraction.  Frozen Lemma
\texttt{r13d:retraction-germ} applies to arbitrary
actual forms and gives
$\alpha'=\pi^*s_j^*\alpha'$ on an ordinary neighborhood
of that section.  The same holds at infinity.  Over
compact $B'$ choose uniformly small chordal tubes
inside these finitely many neighborhoods.  This follows
from positivity of the chordal distance on the compact
complement of each neighborhood; its zero locus is
exactly the indicated graph section.

Choose smooth real cutoffs of the chordal-distance
valuation, equal to zero closer to the section and
to one outside its chosen tube, and multiply them.
Their product $\rho$ is ordinary PWS.  Chordal-distance
valuations are finite piecewise affine expressions in
$v(w_j)$, $v(z)$, and the valuations of the nonzero
$a_j$; the term $\min\{v(a_1),0\}$ for $a_1=0$
is the constant zero and introduces no unit.  Thus there is a global
Euclidean PWS scalar $R$ with $\rho|_W=t_M^*R$.
Its nonzero source support avoids uniform tails at all
roots and infinity.  It is a compact subset of $W$
over compact $B'$.

For any complementary ordinary smooth compact base
test $\eta$, a summand of $(1-\rho)\alpha'\wedge\pi^*\eta$
is supported where $\alpha'=\pi^*\beta_j$.
There the paired factor is $\pi^*(\beta_j\wedge\eta)$,
whose bidegree on the $d$-dimensional base is
$(d+1,d+1)$.  It is the zero actual form.  Multiplying
by any of the remaining scalar cutoffs preserves that
zero.  This proves current-neutral excision for every
such test, without a separate closedness hypothesis.

\emph{A Euclidean partition with genuine zero collars.}
Let $S_\rho=\operatorname{Supp}\rho$ on
$B'\times\mathbf P^1$.  This is compact and lies in
$W$.  For every $i$, the set
\[
                      K_i=t_M(S_\rho\cap A_i)
\tag{AD15.6}\label{r15ad:compact-image}
\]
is a compact subset of $E_i$, by the strict numerical
margins.  Choose a nonnegative Euclidean smooth
function $\sigma_i$ of compact support in $E_i$,
equal to one on a neighborhood of $K_i$; if $K_i$
is empty take $\sigma_i=0$.  Since the $A_i$ cover
every retained fiber, $s=\sum_i\sigma_i$ satisfies
$s\geq1$ on $t_M(S_\rho)$.

Choose a smooth real function $H$ that is zero near
zero and equals $1/s$ for $s\geq1/2$, and put
\[
          \chi_i=\sigma_i H\!\left(\sum_j\sigma_j\right).
\tag{AD15.7}\label{r15ad:ambient-partition}
\]
These are genuine global smooth functions with compact
support in $E_i$, and their sum is exactly one on
$t_M(S_\rho)$.  Define
\[
           \gamma=R\sum_i\chi_i\,L_i^\star\gamma_i.
\tag{AD15.8}\label{r15ad:single-ambient}
\]
Products of Euclidean PWS scalars and Euclidean
delta-forms are Euclidean delta-forms.  The union of
the finitely many supports of $\chi_i$ is compact;
thus $\gamma$ is compactly supported in the entire
common ambient space, a stronger condition than
properness in its vertical coordinates.

On a source point where $\rho\ne0$, every nonzero
summand lies in its genuine chart $B_i$ by
\eqref{r15ad:domain-implication}, so
\eqref{r15ad:local-ambient} and the partition identity
give \eqref{r15ad:actual-presenter}.  On the open zero
region of $\rho$ both sides are zero.  At a boundary
point the same identity holds on an ordinary
neighborhood by the globally defined product
\eqref{r15ad:single-ambient} and the chart domains
selected by the supports of $\chi_i$.  Equivalently,
subtract the two sides and use the actual product
identity term by term: each coefficient with nonzero
germ is supported in its valid chart, and the scalar
partition is one on the entire support of $\rho$.

No delta-form has been extended from $t_M(W)$ or
from the union of intrinsic chart images.  All zero
extensions were Euclidean cutoff products with support
strictly inside their original ambient opens.  Scalar
multiplication and affine-linear pullback preserve
the intrinsic delta codimension; hence a homogeneous
input gives a homogeneous presenter as claimed.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope and interfaces not supplied here.}
The proposition gives a single ordinary unit-moment
presenter, after an explicitly harmless current-level
horizontal excision, on a local finite etale cover.
It does not by itself show that its integration against
a universal moving-tree kernel is an ordinary target
actual form.  Nor does it descend an arbitrary
higher-degree actual output through the finite cover;
only the previously established PWS or first-strip
finite traces may be used without an additional
descent proof.  It makes no assertion of a closed
ambient lift for a closed input.
