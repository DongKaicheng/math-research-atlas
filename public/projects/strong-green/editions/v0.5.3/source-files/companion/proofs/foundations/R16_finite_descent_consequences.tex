\begin{theorem}[Exact-support finite descent in every codimension]
\label{r16fc:exact-support}
Let $q:Y\to X$ be a finite surjective morphism of smooth integral
projective $K$-varieties of common dimension $n$, of degree $d$.
Let $Z$ be a specified rational codimension-$r$ cycle on $X$, where
$1\leq r\leq n$. The map $q$ is finite flat. Suppose strong G is
supplied for the exact flat pullback cycle $q^*Z$ on $Y$.
Then strong G holds for $Z$ on its exact original support complement,
with all compact actual tests over $K$. Explicitly,
\[
 \gamma_Z=d^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}_{q|_{Y\setminus|q^*Z|}}\gamma_{q^*Z}.
 \tag{FD16.1}\label{r16fc:Green-descent}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Since source and target are smooth of the same dimension and $q$ is
finite surjective, miracle flatness makes $q$ finite flat. Flat
pullback of the specified cycle carries its generic scheme lengths and
has support exactly $q^{-1}|Z|$. Thus the trace in
\eqref{r16fc:Green-descent} is an ordinary actual form of degree
$(r-1,r-1)$ on precisely $X^{\mathrm{an}}\setminus|Z|^{\mathrm{an}}$.

Take any $\eta\in B_c^{n-r,n-r}(X^{\mathrm{an}})$. The form $D\eta$
has maximum bidegree $n-r+1$, greater than the dimension $n-r$ of
$|Z|$. Mihatsch Corollary 4.9 therefore gives an ordinary neighborhood
of $|Z|$ on which $D\eta=0$. Its restriction to the complement is a
compact actual test. The trace pairing and the supplied strong G give
\[
\begin{split}
 \int_{X\setminus|Z|}\gamma_Z\wedge D\eta
 &=d^{-1}\int_{Y\setminus|q^*Z|}\gamma_{q^*Z}\wedge Dq^*\eta\\
 &=-d^{-1}\int_{(q^*Z)^{\mathrm{an}}}q^*\eta
 =-\int_{Z^{\mathrm{an}}}\eta.
\end{split}
 \tag{FD16.2}\label{r16fc:Green-pairing}
\]
The last equality is the native finite-flat cycle degree formula,
including generic scheme lengths and residue degrees. In particular
it is valid when a component of $Z$ is contained in the branch locus;
replacing the scheme pullback by its reduced inverse image would be
incorrect. The source test $q^*\eta$ is compact because $q$ is finite.
No modification pushforward or assumption about an alteration outside
$|Z|$ is involved.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Finite forms of the small-base tower class]
\label{r16fc:twisted-towers}
Let $X/K$ be smooth projective geometrically integral. Suppose that,
after a finite valued extension $L/K$, there is a smooth integral
projective $L$-variety $Y$, a finite surjective $L$-morphism
$q:Y\to X_L$, and a supplied projective-bundle tower structure on $Y$
over a smooth projective geometrically integral $L$-base of dimension
at most three. Then every specified geometrically homologically trivial
rational cycle on $X$, in every codimension, satisfies strong G over
the original $K$, with its exact support and all original actual tests.
In particular this holds if $X_L$ itself is such a tower.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Regard $Y$ as a $K$-variety and compose $q$ with $X_L\to X$. It is a
finite surjective morphism of smooth integral projective $K$-varieties
of degree $\deg(q)[L:K]$. Its scheme-theoretic flat pullback of the
specified cycle is geometrically homologically trivial, by functoriality
of the geometric cycle class on each geometric component. The Round 15
all-codimension tower theorem supplies its strong G; the finite-constant
version there views the tower itself in the native $K$ theory. Apply
Theorem~\ref{r16fc:exact-support}. No high-degree descent had been
available in Round 15 for an arbitrary variety merely becoming a tower
after extension; that is the new scope supplied here.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Regular dominant images of obstruction-zero varieties]
\label{r16fc:dominant-towers}

\begin{proof}
