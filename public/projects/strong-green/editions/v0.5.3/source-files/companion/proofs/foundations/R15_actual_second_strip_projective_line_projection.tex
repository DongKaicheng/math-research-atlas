\section{Ordinary actual projective-line output through the second input strip}
\label{r15a2:section}

\begin{theorem}[Actual projective-line projection for smaller input degree at most two]
\label{r15a2:projection}
Let $S/K$ be smooth integral algebraic, let $U\subset S^{\mathrm{an}}$
be ordinary open, and let $\pi:U\times\mathbf P^1\to U$.
For every $\alpha\in B^{p,q}(U\times\mathbf P^1)$ with
$p,q\geq1$ and $\min(p,q)\leq2$, there is a unique ordinary
actual form
\[
                   \mathcal I^{\rm act}_U\alpha
                                  \in B^{p-1,q-1}(U)
\tag{A215.1}\label{r15a2:output}
\]
whose current is the proper push of the current of $\alpha$.
It has the prescribed pairing with every ordinary smooth compact
complementary test, including all mixed tests. The operator is
linear and commutes with ordinary restriction. If $D\alpha=0$,
then $D\mathcal I^{\rm act}_U\alpha=0$.
The construction uses the fixed original field and native degrees.
It also defines this operator on projective-line bundles by ordinary
trivialization and current uniqueness.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
At a point of $U$, Proposition~\ref{r15ad:presenter} supplies
a finite etale algebraic splitting cover and an ordinary neighborhood
of its full inverse image on which the horizontally excised input
has one compact ambient delta presentation in the split root units.
The excision has the same current push as the original input for
every smooth compact complementary test.
Theorem~\ref{r15ug:projection} then gives an ordinary actual
upstairs output $\beta'$ of degree $(p-1,q-1)$, with precisely
those prescribed pairings. The construction may be made on a
covering of the full inverse image; all outputs glue by actual-current
injectivity because they represent the same proper current.

Let $a:U'\to U_0$ be the resulting finite map over a smaller
ordinary base neighborhood, of full native degree $e$. Since
$\min(p-1,q-1)\leq1$, the frozen finite actual trace is available.
Put
\[
                 \beta=e^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}^{\rm act}_a\beta'.
\tag{A215.2}\label{r15a2:trace}
\]
For a smooth compact test $\eta$ on $U_0$, its defining pairing
and the upstairs output give
\[
 \int_{U_0}\beta\wedge\eta
  =e^{-1}\int_{U'}\beta'\wedge a^*\eta
  =e^{-1}\int_{U'\times\mathbf P^1}
                     (a\times\mathrm{id})^*(\alpha\wedge\pi^*\eta)
  =\int_{U_0\times\mathbf P^1}\alpha\wedge\pi^*\eta.
\tag{A215.3}\label{r15a2:native-pairing}
\]
The last equality needs the degree formula on an actual top input.
It follows directly from the frozen finite trace of the PWS scalar
$1$: its all-actual-test pairing and
$\operatorname{Tr}^{\rm PS}_{a\times\mathrm{id}}1=e$
give the displayed equality. Thus no trace of that high-degree
actual top input is presumed.

Every local construction therefore represents the same proper
current. Mihatsch Proposition 4.11 gives equality on ordinary
overlaps, independence of covers and cutoffs, and the global
ordinary representative. Linearity and restriction follow the
same way. Proper current differentiation and $D\alpha=0$ give
$D[\beta]=0$, and current injectivity then gives $D\beta=0$.
No operator on the potentially out-of-range form $D\alpha$ is
needed for that conclusion. A projective-line bundle is locally
this product over algebraic trivializations, whose overlap
outputs again represent its same proper current.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The actual three-input hypothesis and second-degree Green outputs]
\label{r15a2:activation}
The hypothesis $(\mathrm A_2)$ of Definition~\ref{r15fs:hypothesis}
holds. For every ordinary actual $(2,2)$ input on
$S\times\mathbf P^1$ the prescribed diagonal Green currents
have ordinary actual representatives
\[
       G_\alpha\in B^{1,1},\qquad
       P_\alpha\in B^{2,1},\qquad
       Q_\alpha\in B^{1,2},
\tag{A215.4}\label{r15a2:green}
\]
with all ordinary smooth compact mixed-test pairings.
No symmetry or closedness is needed for the output assertion.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The three degrees $(2,2),(3,2),(2,3)$ all satisfy the theorem.
For the diagonal Green outputs, the frozen actual immersion-germ
identity makes the source input equal to the pullback of the target
input on an ordinary neighborhood of the diagonal. Its product
with a complementary test is zero there by target overdegree,
exactly as in Proposition~\ref{r15fs:homotopy}. Insert a cutoff,
apply the theorem to the three resulting actual inputs, and remove
the cutoff in every prescribed pairing. Their source degrees are
$(2,2),(3,2),(2,3)$ respectively. Current injectivity proves
ordinary gluing and cutoff independence.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Activated full actual degree-two projective-space calculation]
\label{r15a2:full-HA22}
For every $m\geq2$,
\[
                H_{\mathrm A,\mathrm{act}}^{2,2}(\mathbf P_K^m)
                                      =\mathbf R[h^2].
\tag{A215.5}\label{r15a2:HA22}
\]
In particular this computes the full actual top Aeppli group
of the projective plane. Pullback on actual Bott--Chern degree
$(3,3)$ is injective for a point blowup of any smooth integral
projective variety, and for every finite sequence of such blowups.
All statements also hold in the original $K$ theory for a finite
constant field, with its native top periods.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Use the now proved $(\mathrm A_2)$ in
Corollary~\ref{r15fs:projective-space} and its point-blowup
consequence. That proof uses a fixed-section Green kernel to
avoid a $(3,3)$-input projection with a $(2,2)$ output.
Its finite symmetric-product step uses only the already frozen
actual Aeppli $(2,2)$ injection, not a finite trace on actual
$(2,2)$ forms.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The precise remaining degree boundary.}
The universal kernel and the local ambient atlas exist upstairs
in all bidegrees. The original-field projection theorem above
uses the available actual finite trace on its output; this is
why it stops at $\min(p,q)\leq2$ for general inputs. Neither a
finite trace on arbitrary actual $(2,2)$ forms nor general
projective-line output in that target degree follows. The new
full projective-space calculation also does not give the full
actual $(2,2)$ projective-bundle formula over an arbitrary base,
descent through every positive-dimensional blowup center, or
arbitrary dominant Bott--Chern injectivity in $(3,3)$.
The general strong conjecture G remains open.
