\section{Unconditional target conjugation and passive diagonal--shear relations}
\label{sec:r10-native-mixed-relations}

\paragraph{Status.}
This module is submitted for independent review.  It constructs
two complete native mixed relations for arbitrary analytic
shear coefficients, including zeros and unbounded values.
No common dominating unit is assumed.  The source-entry
conjugation relation is deliberately excluded.

Let $N$ be an ordinary original-$K$ analytic open, let $f$ be an
ordered $r$-tuple of sections of the fixed metrized line bundle,
and let $W=N\setminus|f=0|$.  For $i\ne j$ put
$E(t)=I+t e_{ij}$, with $t\in\mathcal O(N)$ arbitrary.
For an analytic unit $\lambda$ let $D_k(\lambda)$ multiply
entry $k$.  All tuples below have the same zero locus.
Write $\mathcal P_{ij,t}$ for the prescribed native SHT shear
pair, including its actual zero gluing.  Write
$p_{k,\lambda}(f)$ for the SHT one-entry diagonal pair from
$f$ to $D_k(\lambda)f$.  The metric and reference curvature
are fixed throughout.

\subsection{Target-entry conjugation}

The exact analytic identity
\[
 D_i(\lambda)E_{ij}(t)
                 =E_{ij}(\lambda t)D_i(\lambda)                \tag{MX1}
\]
relates two paths with the same final ordered tuple.
Put $g=E(t)f$, $k=D_i(\lambda)f$, and
$h=D_i(\lambda)g=E_{ij}(\lambda t)k$.

\begin{theorem}[Complete native target-conjugation witness]
For every analytic $t$ and analytic unit $\lambda$ on $N$,
there is an explicit actual triple $Q_{\rm tar}$ on all of
$W$ such that
\[
 \mathcal P_{ij,t}(f)+p_{i,\lambda}(g)
       -p_{i,\lambda}(f)-\mathcal P_{ij,\lambda t}(k)
                                      =\partial_2Q_{\rm tar}.
                                                               \tag{MX2}
\]
No boundedness condition on $t$ or $\lambda$ is needed.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
First work on the ordinary open $N_t=\{t\ne0\}$, where
$d=v(t)$ is an actual unit valuation.  Put $a=v(\lambda)$.
The normalized potential
\[
 \Psi=\Phi_{D_i(t)^{-1}f}
       =\Phi_{D_i(t)^{-1}g}
       =\Phi_{D_i(\lambda t)^{-1}k}
       =\Phi_{D_i(\lambda t)^{-1}h}                            \tag{MX3}
\]
is common to all four presentations.  The first equality
uses the constant norm isometry $E_{ij}(1)$; the last two
follow from literal matrix identities.

On $N_t$, apply the strict same-entry loop construction SL9
to the factors
\[
                  \lambda,\quad(\lambda t)^{-1},\quad t,
                     \qquad\hbox{weights }a,-a-d,d.            \tag{MX4}
\]
Starting at $f$, the three norm potentials are
$\Phi_f,\Phi_k,\Psi$; starting at $g$, they are
$\Phi_g,\Phi_h,\Psi$.  Denote their explicit actual SL
triples by $q_f$ and $q_g$, using the same positive cap
constant in both.  Subtracting the two loop identities
and using SHT antisymmetry gives
\[
\begin{split}
 \partial_2(q_g-q_f)
   ={}&p(\Phi_g,\Phi_h,a)-p(\Phi_f,\Phi_k,a)\\
      &+p(\Phi_h,\Psi,-a-d)-p(\Phi_k,\Psi,-a-d)\\
      &+p(\Psi,\Phi_g,d)-p(\Psi,\Phi_f,d)\\
   ={}&\mathcal P_{ij,t}(f)+p_{i,\lambda}(g)
          -p_{i,\lambda}(f)-\mathcal P_{ij,\lambda t}(k).
\end{split}                                                    \tag{MX5}
\]
This fixes all signs for the temporal path convention.

Consider now the ordinary open
\[
                 O=\{|t|<1,\ |\lambda t|<1\}\subset N.          \tag{MX6}
\]
It contains every zero of $t$.  On $O$, both $E_{ij}(t)$
and $E_{ij}(\lambda t)$ are norm isometries, so
$\Phi_f=\Phi_g$ and $\Phi_k=\Phi_h$.  On $O\cap N_t$
the remaining potential $\Psi$ is already common by (MX3).
The unchanged block used in SL9 is literally the same
for $f$ and $g$, because the shear changes only entry $i$.
The weights, cap constant, reference curvature and all
three endpoint potentials are equal in the two constructions.
Every input to the finite formula SL9, including its full
mixed derivatives, is therefore equal:
\[
                             q_g-q_f=0\quad\hbox{on }O\cap N_t.
                                                               \tag{MX7}
\]
Glue this difference on $W\cap N_t$ with the zero triple on
$W\cap O$.  The two ordinary opens cover $W$ and (MX7)
is equality of actual triples, so this defines $Q_{\rm tar}$.
On $O$ both native shear pairs are zero, and the two diagonal
pairs in (MX2) agree because both their endpoint potentials
agree.  Thus (MX2) also holds on the zero-gluing open.
No limit of a singular valuation is taken across $t=0$.
\end{proof}

The coefficient wall $|\lambda t|=1$ lies in $N_t$, as does
$|t|=1$.  Both remain in the full SHT and SL expressions.
The zero gluing does not discard either wall or its mixed jets.

\subsection{A passive diagonal entry}

Assume now that $k\notin\{i,j\}$ and put $B=D_k(\lambda)$.
Then $BE(t)=E(t)B$.  Set $g=E(t)f$, $h=Bg$.

\begin{theorem}[Complete native passive-commutation witness]
There is an explicit actual triple $Q_{\rm pas}$ on all of $W$
such that
\[
 \mathcal P_{ij,t}(f)+p_{k,\lambda}(g)
       -p_{k,\lambda}(f)-\mathcal P_{ij,t}(Bf)
                                      =\partial_2Q_{\rm pas}.
                                                               \tag{MX8}
\]
Again $t$ may have zeros and unbounded values, and no common
dominant pivot is assumed.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On $N_t$ put $U=D_i(t)$, $d=v(t)$ and $a=v(\lambda)$.
Use the full CS square with the ordered diagonal factors
$U^{-1}$ followed by $B$, against their reverse order.
Starting at $f$, this gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 p_{i,t^{-1}}(f)+p_{k,\lambda}(U^{-1}f)
      -p_{k,\lambda}(f)-p_{i,t^{-1}}(Bf)
         =\partial_2q_f+e_{-d,a}R^{r-2}.
 \end{split}                                                    \tag{MX9}
\]
Starting at $g$ gives the same formula with triple $q_g$
and exactly the same extending symbol $e_{-d,a}R^{r-2}$.
These two symbols cancel upon subtraction; neither is
individually declared to extend across $t=0$.

The tuples $U^{-1}f,U^{-1}g$ differ by the constant norm
isometry $E_{ij}(1)$.  Since $k$ is neither $i$ nor $j$,
$B$ commutes with that constant isometry.  Hence the two
potentials at both ends of the middle $B$ edge agree for
the two starting tuples, and those middle primitive pairs
cancel literally.  SHT antisymmetry at the remaining
$U^{-1}$ edges then yields
\[
 \partial_2(q_f-q_g)
        =\mathcal P_{ij,t}(f)+p_{k,\lambda}(g)
          -p_{k,\lambda}(f)-\mathcal P_{ij,t}(Bf).
                                                               \tag{MX10}
\]

On the ordinary open $O=\{|t|<1\}$, $E(t)$ is a norm
isometry, as is its commuting action after $B$.  Therefore
all four square potentials for $f$ and $g$ agree:
\[
 \Phi_f=\Phi_g,\quad
 \Phi_{U^{-1}f}=\Phi_{U^{-1}g},\quad
 \Phi_{Bf}=\Phi_{Bg},\quad
 \Phi_{BU^{-1}f}=\Phi_{BU^{-1}g}.                               \tag{MX11}
\]
The passive block in the CS cap omits the two changed
entries $i,k$.  It is literally unchanged by $E(t)$,
which changes only entry $i$.  The unit weights $-d,a$
and every other input of the full CS construction are
also identical.  With a common cap constant,
\[
                             q_f-q_g=0\quad\hbox{on }N_t\cap O.
                                                               \tag{MX12}
\]
Glue this difference with zero on $W\cap O$ to obtain
the actual triple $Q_{\rm pas}$.  The two ordinary opens
cover $W$, and on $O$ the two shear primitives are zero
while the diagonal primitives have identical endpoints.
Thus (MX8) holds everywhere.

The condition $k\ne i$ makes this a distinct-entry CS
square, while $k\ne j$ makes the normalized constant
shear commute with $B$.  The passive block is unchanged
because it omits entry $i$.  The source-entry exclusion
cannot be dropped from the normalized cancellation.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A finite actual relation certificate and the remaining gap}

The witnesses (MX2) and (MX8) can be inserted into a finite
path comparison on one entire original presentation.
Common prefixes and suffixes cancel as actual expressions;
the remaining displayed triples are added with their
orientation signs.  They can be combined with SL14,
SH11 and DN13 when those separate hypotheses hold.
In a two-open original cancellation problem this gives
the exact RAG witness, followed by the $U$-only cutoff
and actual Green construction CS40--CS42.  For finite
covers, any required higher coherence must still be
supplied as in FG7 or FG11; finitely many pairwise
relations alone do not provide it automatically.

These are new complete relation identities for the
prescribed native primitives.  They are not an assertion
that every analytic frame comparison admits a global
finite path, nor a new independent classification of
original cycles with actual Green representatives.

For the remaining source-entry relation
\[
           D_j(\lambda)E_{ij}(t)
                         =E_{ij}(t/\lambda)D_j(\lambda),
                                                               \tag{MX13}
\]
the normalized constant shear does \emph{not} commute
with the single source diagonal.  Its two native
normalization vertices differ by a simultaneous scaling
of entries $i,j$.  Splitting that simultaneous scaling
into the two prescribed one-entry primitives can pass
through a nonisometric intermediate frame.  The
literal cancellations used above therefore fail.
A separate witness, or the common-frame certificate
DN2--DN3, is needed; (MX13) is not proved here at arbitrary
unbounded coefficients by renaming the target argument.

The constructions keep the original $K$ and absolute
value, all ordinary opens, nonproduct units, arbitrary
passive base directions, and full metric transport.
Every zero extension is justified by literal equality
of actual triples on an ordinary overlap.  No completed
coefficient object, current, probe topology, or arbitrary
higher-form descent is used.  General strong G and
the complete lower-middle OPEN-KERNEL remain open.
