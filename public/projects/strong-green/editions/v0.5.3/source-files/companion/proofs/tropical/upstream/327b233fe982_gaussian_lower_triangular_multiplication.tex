\label{sec:r12-gaussian-lower-triangular-multiplication}

\paragraph{Status and attribution.}

The construction contributors are \texttt{/root} and the R12 matrix
agent \texttt{/root/r11\_challenge}.  The cap transport inputs SAN

\emph{entire original GB pair}, including its original nested caps,

not an automatic coherence theorem for an arbitrary matrix atlas.

\subsection{Data and the statement}

Use the original $K$, absolute value, smooth analytic open $N$, actual
metrized line bundle, tuple $f$ of length $r\ge2$, and
$W=N\setminus|f=0|$ of GB.  All matrices below act in the first two
rows and are the identity in the remaining rows.  Supply
\[
 A=\begin{pmatrix}\alpha&\beta\\\gamma&\delta\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad \Delta=\det A\in\mathcal O(N)^\times,
 \qquad z\in\mathcal O(N),\qquad
 A'=E_{21}(z)A.
 \tag{TML1}
\]
Thus $\alpha'=\alpha$, $\beta'=\beta$, $\gamma'=\gamma+z\alpha$,
$\delta'=\delta+z\beta$ and $\Delta'=\Delta$.  The shear coefficient
$z$ is arbitrary, including zeros and unbounded functions.

Fix a positive real $\varepsilon$ and a smooth function
$\chi:\mathbf R\to[0,1]$ equal to zero on $(-\infty,-\varepsilon]$
and to one on $[\varepsilon,\infty)$.  In the GB construction use
the prescribed ordinary partitions
\[
 \theta=\chi(v(\gamma)-v(\alpha)),\qquad
 \theta'=\chi(v(\gamma')-v(\alpha)),
 \tag{TML2}
\]
with their constant extensions across the zero pivots.  Each is zero
on an ordinary neighborhood of $\alpha=0$, and is one near its own
second-pivot zero.  Fix all positive offsets in the original GB
subrelation caps once.  Write $p_A,p_{A'}$ for exactly these original
GB22 pairs, $R=c_1(\overline L)$, $d=r-2$, and
$\mathcal P_{21,z}(Af)$ for the prescribed native SHT pair.

\begin{theorem}[Entire triangular multiplication relation]
There are explicit finite actual forms
\[
 q\in L_2^{r-1}(W),\qquad e\in L_1^{r-1}(N),
 \qquad
 p_A+\mathcal P_{21,z}(Af)-p_{A'}=\partial_2q+e|_W.
 \tag{TML3}
\]
On $\{\alpha\ne0\}$ the extending pair is
\[
 e=\bigl(-(1-\theta)e_{C,a-C}
                +(1-\theta')e_{C',a-C'}\bigr)R^d,
 \quad a=v(\alpha),\ C=v(\gamma),\ C'=v(\gamma'),
 \tag{TML4}
\]
where $e_{x,y}=(x\,d''y,y\,d'x)$.  A summand is extended by zero
through its second-pivot zero.  The difference in parentheses is
extended by zero through $\alpha=0$ as one whole expression.
The extending pair is not asserted to be independently closed after
the nonconstant weights in (TML4) are inserted.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{A single finite admissible cap}

We give the finite bound used below, rather than assuming a cap valid
on a noncompact open.  Set
\[
 \begin{split}
 M&=\max(1,|z|,\max|A_{ij}|,\max|A'_{ij}|,
                  \max|(A^{-1})_{ij}|,\max|((A')^{-1})_{ij}|),\\
 B&=1+\varepsilon+\log M,\qquad c_0=\max(|\alpha|,|\gamma|),\\
 N_{\rm main}&=\{|\alpha|>e^{-4B}c_0\},\qquad
 N_{\rm small}=\{|\alpha|<e^{-3B}c_0\}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{TML5}
\]
These are ordinary opens covering $N$.  The norm $c_0$ never
vanishes; $e^{-B}\le c_0\le e^B$.  The finite maxima in $M$ have
the constant entry $1$, so their logarithms are actual finite
norm scalars.  At a zero analytic entry one uses a sufficiently
small original-$K$ constant on an ordinary neighborhood; its
contribution remains strictly below the positive floor.  This is
the ordinary native norm presentation, not a calculus on a
nonreduced space and not a general minimum of smooth functions.

The following finite recipe specifies an integer $C_0$ once and
for all, independently of $N,A,z,f$.  Use the elementary matrix
words of GB4--GB5 and all the nine calls of GB10/GB16, their
copies for $A'$, the SAR call with coefficients $\gamma/\alpha,z$,
and the second-pivot substitution list (TML17) below.  Expand the
native unit-edge tables in these finitely many specified sources.
Before assigning a bound, delete each entire contribution which
is literally zero on its frozen small-coefficient open.  The
following rules give an upper bound $e^{bB}$ for each coefficient
or inverse which remains:
\[
\begin{array}{p{0.40\linewidth}p{0.49\linewidth}}
\text{Quantity or complete call}&\text{Bound rule}\\[1mm]
A,A',A^{-1},(A')^{-1},z&1\text{ for each matrix entry or }z;\\
\Delta,\Delta^{-1}&2;\\
\alpha^{-1}\text{ on }N_{\rm main}&5;\\
\gamma^{-1},(\gamma')^{-1}\text{ when their GB weights are nonzero}
 &7\text{ on }N_{\rm main},\text{ using an ordinary }2\varepsilon
 \text{ buffer for derivative supports};\\
\gamma^{-1},(\gamma')^{-1}\text{ on }N_{\rm small}&1;\\
\text{Sums and products}&\max\text{ and addition of exponents};\\
\text{A native shear coefficient }b
 &\text{if }|b|<1\text{ its entire two-edge companion is }0;
   \text{ otherwise }|b|^{-1}\le1;\\
\text{SAR with }b,c,b+c
 &\text{if any has norm }<1\text{ its entire triple is }0;
   \text{ otherwise all three inverses have norm }\le1;\\
\text{MX with unit }\lambda\text{ and coefficient }b
 &\text{its whole triple is }0\text{ if }|b|,|\lambda b|<1;
   \text{ otherwise }|b|^{-1}\le\max(1,|\lambda|);\\
\text{NSC with unit }\lambda\text{ and coefficient }b
 &\text{its whole triple is }0\text{ if }|b|,|b/\lambda|<1;
   \text{ otherwise }|b|^{-1}\le\max(1,|\lambda|^{-1}).
\end{array}
 \tag{TML6}
\]
In the second-pivot list the GPR call is identically zero on
$N_{\rm small}$ and is deleted as a whole.  All other denominators
in the finite list are products of the indicated nonzero pivots,
$\Delta$, the unit $\gamma'/\gamma$ on $N_{\rm small}$, or the
coefficients whose inverses are controlled by (TML6).  Consequently
this recipe terminates.  For each genuine vertex matrix $F$ in the
retained lists, sum its elementary-factor exponent bounds, both for
$F$ and for $F^{-1}$.  Let $C_0$ be one plus the largest resulting
integer.  This is an explicit finite integer algorithm on the
displayed fixed words, not a pointwise choice or a new finite-layer
capture assumption.  For every call which is not deleted,
\[
             \|F\|,\|F^{-1}\|<e^{C_0B},\qquad
                    \Phi_{Ff}<\Phi_f+C_0B.
 \tag{TML7}
\]
The bound for $\Phi_{Ff}$ uses the second matrix norm and the
original common metric.  Prefixes in GB15 are included in $F$;
no bound just for an isolated subword is substituted for (TML7).

For $r\ge3$ let $\psi$ be the potential of the unchanged last
$r-2$ sections, and use the one common actual cap
\[
       \kappa=(C_0+1)B,
       \qquad \Psi=\min(\psi,\Phi_f+\kappa),
       \qquad R_*=R-D\Psi.
 \tag{TML8}
\]
The positive floor $e^{-\kappa}\|f\|$ makes (TML8) an actual
finite norm presentation on $W$, including the common zero set
of all passive entries.  Thus it is an admissible TCT cap for
every retained call on the indicated ordinary regions.  Deleted
calls are zero for every common normalization.  In particular
there is no attempt to bound the singular separate normalizers
inside a native shear contribution which already cancels.
For $r=2$ omit $\Psi$ and all terms involving $h$ below, as in
TCT; every displayed exponent $R_*^d$ then means $1$.

\subsection{The explicit finite triple}

Write $w_\alpha,w_\gamma,w'_{\alpha},w'_{\gamma'}$ for the four
original GB words, retaining their actual starting tuple $f$.
Let $\mathcal J_\Psi$ be the full linear edge companion TCT1,
which includes the entire nonclosed MT polynomial, not only
the CS edge primitives.  On each appropriate pivot overlap put
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 h=-\Psi\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}R_*^jR^{d-1-j},\\
 \sigma_A=(0,0,C(C-a)R_*^d/2)+H_h(e_{C,a-C}),\\
 \sigma_{A'}=(0,0,C'(C'-a)R_*^d/2)+H_h(e_{C',a-C'}).
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{TML9}
\]
Here the base pairs supplied to $H_h$ are independently closed;
the external partition weights below are multiplied only
\emph{after} these complete triples have been formed.

Define the following actual expression on $W\cap\{\alpha\ne0\}$:
\[
\begin{split}
 \widetilde q={}&\mathcal J_\Psi(E_{21}(z);Af)
       +\theta\mathcal J_\Psi(w_\alpha;f)
       +(1-\theta)\mathcal J_\Psi(w_\gamma;f)\\
 &-\theta'\mathcal J_\Psi(w'_{\alpha};f)
       -(1-\theta')\mathcal J_\Psi(w'_{\gamma'};f)
       -(1-\theta)\sigma_A+(1-\theta')\sigma_{A'}.
\end{split}
 \tag{TML10}
\]
Products supported in a pivot chart have their closed-support
zero extensions.  The last two terms are kept as one difference
when approaching $\alpha=0$.

On $N_{\rm small}$ one has $\gamma\ne0$,
\[
       |z\alpha|<|\gamma|,\qquad
       |\gamma'|=|\gamma|,\qquad C'=C,\qquad
                          \theta=\theta'=0.
 \tag{TML11}
\]
These are equalities on an ordinary open, so all actual mixed
derivatives agree as well.  In particular the last difference
of (TML10) is literally zero there.  The remaining formula is
\[
  \widetilde q=\mathcal J_\Psi(E_{21}(z);Af)
                  +\mathcal J_\Psi(w_\gamma;f)
                  -\mathcal J_\Psi(w'_{\gamma'};f),
 \tag{TML12}
\]
which is defined on the entire $W\cap N_{\rm small}$, including
$\alpha=0$.  The whole companions extend through zero shear
coefficients by TCT2.  Equations (TML10)--(TML12) therefore give
one actual finite triple on $W$.  The same ordinary equality
(TML11) proves that (TML4), as one difference, extends by zero
through $\alpha=0$ on $N$.

\subsection{Verification on the first-pivot region}

On $N_{\rm main}$ the first pivot is a unit.  Put
$t=\gamma/\alpha$ and
$g=D_1(\alpha)D_2(\Delta/\alpha)E_{12}(\beta/\alpha)f$.
The first-pivot words differ exactly by the same-root shear
addition $E_{21}(z)E_{21}(t)=E_{21}(t+z)$.  SAN permits its
full SAR witness with the common normalization $\Psi$, and gives
\[
 p(w_\alpha;f)+\mathcal P_{21,z}(Af)-p(w'_{\alpha};f)
   =\partial_2\bigl(\mathcal J_\Psi(w_\alpha;f)
          +\mathcal J_\Psi(E_{21}(z);Af)
          -\mathcal J_\Psi(w'_{\alpha};f)\bigr).
 \tag{TML13}
\]
This includes zeros of $t,z,t+z$.  For the common-cap versions
of the GB overlaps, TCT10 says literally
\[
 Q_A^{\rm com}=\mathcal J_\Psi(w_\alpha;f)
                   -\mathcal J_\Psi(w_\gamma;f)+\sigma_A,
 \quad
 Q_{A'}^{\rm com}=\mathcal J_\Psi(w'_{\alpha};f)
                   -\mathcal J_\Psi(w'_{\gamma'};f)+\sigma_{A'}.
 \tag{TML14}
\]
The bound (TML7) applies wherever the respective factor
$1-\theta$ or $1-\theta'$ is nonzero.  On the ordinary regions
where it is zero the multiplied full formula is zero instead;
this is why no inverse bound at a discarded pivot is needed.

The exact Leibniz identity for GB22, not merely its ordinary
boundary, gives on the first chart
\[
 p_A^{\rm com}=p(w_\alpha;f)
       -\partial_2((1-\theta)Q_A^{\rm com})
       -(1-\theta)e_{C,a-C}R^d,
 \tag{TML15}
\]
and the identical formula for $A'$.  Subtract these formulas,
use (TML13), then substitute (TML14).  Every original edge and
every MT term cancels linearly, leaving exactly (TML10) and
(TML4).  Hence (TML3), with common-cap pairs and triple
$\widetilde q$, holds on the whole first-pivot region.  All
mixed derivatives of $\theta$ in this computation are part of
the product differential; none are omitted.

\subsection{Verification through the vanishing first pivot}

It remains to prove the identity on $N_{\rm small}$ directly;
agreement on a dense unit locus would not suffice.  On this open
put
\[
       \omega=\gamma'/\gamma=1+z\alpha/\gamma,
       \qquad |\omega|=1,\qquad v(\omega)=0.
 \tag{TML16}
\]
Signed-permutation equivariance moves the added shear past $J$:
$E_{21}(z)J=JE_{12}(-z)$.  The following finite substitutions
compare the entire second-pivot words, in matrix-product order:
\[
\begin{split}
 E_{12}(-z)E_{21}(-\alpha/\gamma)
  &\rightsquigarrow
 E_{21}(-\alpha/\gamma')D_2(\omega^{-1})D_1(\omega)
                                      E_{12}(-z/\omega),\\
 E_{12}(-z/\omega)D_1(\gamma)D_2(\Delta/\gamma)
  &\rightsquigarrow
 D_1(\gamma)D_2(\Delta/\gamma)E_{12}(w),
       \qquad w=-z\Delta/(\gamma\gamma'),\\
 D_1(\omega)D_1(\gamma)&\rightsquigarrow D_1(\gamma'),\\
 D_2(\omega^{-1})D_1(\gamma')&\rightsquigarrow
                          D_1(\gamma')D_2(\omega^{-1}),\\
 D_2(\omega^{-1})D_2(\Delta/\gamma)&\rightsquigarrow
                                      D_2(\Delta/\gamma'),\\
 E_{12}(w)E_{12}(\delta/\gamma)&\rightsquigarrow
                                      E_{12}(\delta'/\gamma').
\end{split}
 \tag{TML17}
\]
The first is exactly GPR with parameters $-z,-\alpha/\gamma$.
Its second coefficient and their product have norms strictly
less than one on $N_{\rm small}$.  Its \emph{entire} actual
triple is consequently zero on its frozen zero-neighborhood,
including coefficient zeros.  Its scalar correction and
extending pair are zero because $v(\omega)=0$.
The second line uses the negative target and source witnesses
with precisely the intermediate tuples displayed by this word.
The third and fifth lines are SL; the fourth is CS; the last
is SAR.  The sole CS error is
$e_{v(\gamma'),-v(\omega)}R^d=0$ and its scalar correction
is zero.  The identity
$\delta'/\gamma'=\delta/\gamma-z\Delta/(\gamma\gamma')$
checks the last endpoint without a unit assumption on either
shear coefficient.

All these complete relations may use the common cap by SAN/TCT
and (TML7).  They have zero base corrections on this open.
Their full companion sums therefore telescope literally to
(TML12), not just to a triple with the same boundary.  The two
GB partitions are identically zero here, so the common-cap and
original GB pairs are their second-pivot pairs.  It follows
that (TML3) holds throughout $W\cap N_{\rm small}$, and its
error there is zero.  This proves the common-cap identity on an
ordinary cover of the entire $W$.

\subsection{Restoring the exact original nested caps}

For $A$ the ordinary strip
\[
 V_A=\{\alpha\gamma\ne0,\ |v(\gamma)-v(\alpha)|<2\varepsilon\}
 \tag{TML18}
\]
contains the closed supports of $d'\theta,d''\theta$;
use the corresponding strip $V_{A'}$ for $\theta'$.
The cap (TML8) is admissible for all their nine actual GB
subrelation calls.  TCT9 provides the explicit full
two-parameter-transgression quadruples
\[
 Q_A^{\rm com}-Q_A^{\rm old}=\partial_3\mathcal H_A,
 \qquad Q_{A'}^{\rm com}-Q_{A'}^{\rm old}
                                      =\partial_3\mathcal H_{A'}.
 \tag{TML19}
\]
They include the CT mixed term $-d''J$.  No equality of ordinary
boundaries is substituted for (TML19).  Set
\[
 T_A=K_3(\theta)\mathcal H_A,\qquad
 T_{A'}=K_3(\theta')\mathcal H_{A'},\qquad
                         q=\widetilde q+T_A-T_{A'}.
 \tag{TML20}
\]
Each product has its closed-support zero extension from the
indicated strip.  TCT11 gives
$p_A^{\rm old}-p_A^{\rm com}=\partial_2T_A$ and its primed
counterpart, proving (TML3) for the exact original GB pairs.
This completes the proof of the theorem.

\paragraph{Actual interface and exact scope.}
Every expression is a finite combination of original-$K$ analytic
units on ordinary domains, actual native norm minima, smooth
functions of valuations, the original metric, finite mixed
coefficient transgression and closed-support zero extension.
Actual refinement preserves these identities and all their mixed
jets.  The maps are continuous on each fixed smooth coefficient
presentation by the frozen finite actual operations; no global
continuous linear primitive selector is constructed.  The
extending pair is an actual pair on $N$, not an arbitrary current
or a class modulo $\partial_2$.

This theorem may be inserted as one specified relative transition
in the frozen RAG/RSP finite gluing construction.  Its derivative
and extending error must both be retained in that construction.
An original-cycle application still requires the complete
geometric neighborhood and finite transition certificate, followed
by the ordinary-$U$ cutoff and the actual support/Stokes argument
of RSP.  We do not assert that a three-pivot Gaussian atlas
automatically meets the higher compatibility hypotheses, that its
triple defect is the AGC commuting-diagonal cube, or that arbitrary
$\mathrm{GL}_r$ or homologically trivial cycles have been solved.
