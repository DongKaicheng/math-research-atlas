\section{Recovery revalidation of native same-root additivity}
\label{sec:r11-same-root-additivity-revalidation}

\paragraph{Status and provenance.}
\textbf{PROVED THIS ROUND --- RECOVERY\_REVALIDATION only.}
The construction contributor is \texttt{/root/r11\_challenge}.
R10 historically reported broader fixed-target graph and one-row
affine coherence in CG/RA, whose selected final sources are missing.
The narrowly needed same-root additivity facet is re-proved below
from available exact SHT/CS/MT sources.  This is neither recovery of
the missing bytes nor a new theorem beyond that historical scope.
No other CG/RA claim is thereby promoted to current exact authority.

Let $N$ be an ordinary original-$K$ analytic open, $f$ an $r$-tuple
with the fixed actual line-bundle metric, $r\ge2$, and
$W=N\setminus|f=0|$.  Fix $i\ne j$ and arbitrary analytic $u,v$
on $N$, put $w=u+v$, and write $E(t)=E_{ij}(t)$.  With the literal
native SHT pair, consider
\[
 \mathscr A=\mathcal P_{ij,u}(f)
     +\mathcal P_{ij,v}(E(u)f)-\mathcal P_{ij,w}(f).
 \tag{SAR1}
\]
The coefficients may be zero or unbounded.  We construct a strict
actual second witness for this whole native expression.

\subsection{The fixed six-edge loop}

On $N_{uvw}=\{uvw\ne0\}$ let $a=v(u)$, $b=v(v)$, $c=v(w)$ and
\[
 \begin{array}{lll}
 F=\Phi_f,&G=\Phi_{E(u)f},&H=\Phi_{E(w)f},\\
 S=\Phi_{D_i(u)^{-1}f},&
 T=\Phi_{D_i(v)^{-1}E(u)f},&
 J=\Phi_{D_i(w)^{-1}f}.
 \end{array}
 \tag{SAR2}
\]
The full oriented edge table is
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
 \text{edge}&\text{ordered potentials}&\text{weight}\\\hline
 1&(F,S)&-a\\
 2&(S,G)&a\\
 3&(G,T)&-b\\
 4&(T,H)&b\\
 5&(H,J)&-c\\
 6&(J,F)&c.
\end{array}
\tag{SAR3}
\]
Every unit edge changes only row $i$; the native normalizer uses
the constant shear $E_{ij}(1)$ as its exact norm-isometric step.

Let the passive tuple contain all rows except $i$, and denote its
norm by $P$.  Set $M=\|f\|$, $\psi=-\log P$, with $\psi=+\infty$
at $P=0$, and choose
\[
 \kappa=\log\max(1,|u|,|v|,|w|),\qquad
 \Psi=\min(\psi,\Phi_f+\kappa+\varepsilon),\qquad
 R_*=R-D\Psi,\qquad \varepsilon>0.
 \tag{SAR4}
\]
On the coefficient-unit locus these are finite actual valuation
formulas after a common metric shift.  Near a passive zero the
finite second branch is used on an ordinary neighborhood.

\begin{lemma}[One-target residual vanishing]
On the ordinary open where $\Psi=\psi$, every full CS edge residual
$S_{e,k}$ in (SAR3) is zero for every $k\ge1$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For a nonnegative unit weight $t$, the normalized old and new
potentials satisfy
\[
                           p\le0,\qquad q=\min(p+t,0).
 \tag{SAR5}
\]
There is only one changing entry; all unchanged entries have
normalized maximum norm one.  This proves (SAR5) in the complete
actual norm presentation.  The coefficient identity
\[
                  \mathfrak a(p+t,q;1)=0
 \tag{SAR6}
\]
holds on each of the two closed sign regions and their common
faces: there $q$ equals $p+t$ or zero with the same restricted
first jet.  No arbitrary minimum of smooth actual functions is
being asserted.

Frozen CS15 kills all $C_p$-containing terms in the first residual
symbol.  The rest of the full CS7 expression is
$\mathfrak a(p+t,q;h_{k-1}(R_*,C_q))$, which is zero by (SAR6).
This includes $k=1$.  Reverse the edge for a negative weight.
At weight zero use the full restricted first-jet equality of the
two norm potentials and of the positive/negative weight parts.
Thus every carrier and every intercept wall is retained.
\end{proof}

On the complementary ordinary open $\psi>\Phi_f+\kappa$, the
strict inequalities $|u|P<M$ and $|w|P<M$ give $F=G=H$.
Each native pair in (SAR1) is then two inverse edges, regardless
of its middle potential.  The two opens cover, so the top residual
sum vanishes globally on $N_{uvw}\cap W$.  Let $\mathcal K_s$
be the exact full CS6/CS8--CS10 triple, with $s=r-1$, and let
$Q_{\rm met}$ be the entire MT16 triple for these six edges and
$h=-\Psi$.  Define
\[
          q_{\rm add}=\sum_{e=1}^6\mathcal K_s(e;R_*)-Q_{\rm met}
                 \quad\hbox{on }N_{uvw}\cap W.
 \tag{SAR7}
\]
CS10 and MT16 give $\mathscr A=\partial_2q_{\rm add}$ there.
The metric term includes every $f_k$, $\Gamma_k$ and mixed
component of the full loop; no truncated block is declared closed.

\subsection{Zero gluing of the entire triple}

Put $O_u=\{|u|<1\}$, $O_v=\{|v|<1\}$ and $O_w=\{|w|<1\}$.
These are ordinary neighborhoods of the respective coefficient
zero loci.  We now prove literal inverse-edge cancellation of
(SAR3) on each one, not merely that its boundary is zero.

On $O_u$, use the two ordinary subopens $|v|<1$ and $|v|>|u|$.
They cover $O_u$.  On the first, all three shears are norm
isometries, so $F=G=H$ and each native pair is inverse.  On the
second, $|w|=|v|$ and $|u/v|<1$.  Consequently $b=c$, $F=G$,
and $T=J$: the latter follows by multiplying the changed row
of $D_i(w)^{-1}f$ by the norm-one unit $w/v$ and then applying
the norm isometry $E_{ij}(u/v)$.  The inverse pairs are
$(1,2),(3,6),(4,5)$.

On $O_v$, use $|u|<1$ and $|u|>|v|$.  The first again has all
shears isometric.  On the second $a=c$, $G=H$, and $S=J$
because the diagonal multiplier $w/u$ has norm one.  The inverse
pairs are $(3,4),(1,6),(2,5)$.

On $O_w$, use $|u|<1$ and $|u|>|w|$.  In the first case
$|v|<1$ too.  In the second, $a=b$, $H=F$, and $S=T$.
Indeed $D_i(v)^{-1}E(u)f$ is obtained from $D_i(u)^{-1}f$
by $E_{ij}(1)$ followed by multiplication of row $i$ by $u/v$;
both are norm isometries.  The inverse pairs are
$(1,4),(2,3),(5,6)$.

All these equalities hold on whole ordinary opens.  Equal
valuations such as $b=c$ therefore have equal actual derivatives.
The same cap is used for every edge; inverse-edge antisymmetry
makes $\sum\mathcal K_s$, each full-loop $f_k$, and each
$\Gamma_k$ vanish.  In particular the entire $q_{\rm add}$ in
(SAR7) is zero on its overlap with $O_u\cup O_v\cup O_w$.
The native pair (SAR1) is also literally zero on those opens,
including zero coefficients: the small-coefficient shear is
already zero by SHT, and the other comparisons only divide by
the explicitly dominating nonzero coefficient on their subopen.
No density argument across a zero set is used.

\begin{theorem}[Revalidated same-root native additivity]
The formula (SAR7), glued with zero on $O_u,O_v,O_w$, is an
actual triple on all of $W$ and satisfies
\[
 \mathcal P_{ij,u}(f)+\mathcal P_{ij,v}(E(u)f)
                   -\mathcal P_{ij,u+v}(f)=\partial_2q_{\rm add}.
 \tag{SAR8}
\]
This holds for every $r\ge2$ with arbitrary analytic coefficients
on the entire stated ordinary open, all original norm vertices,
all intercept walls, mixed jets, and the original metric.
\end{theorem}

The ordinary opens $N_{uvw},O_u,O_v,O_w$ cover $N$, proving the
global gluing assertion.  In particular if $u+v$ vanishes identically,
the witness can be taken to be zero: the two native shear pairs
are exact reverses, by the same normalizer isometry.  This is
literal cancellation of a shear with its inverse.
