\section{Finite actual unit-symbol relations, including the entire Steinberg wall}
\label{sec:r12-actual-unit-symbol-relations}

\paragraph{Status and scope.}
\textbf{PROVED THIS ROUND, submitted for independent exact-source review.}
The constructor is \texttt{/root}.  This source supplies actual finite
boundary witnesses for symmetry and Steinberg relations of the higher
unit cochains of HCT.  It includes the nonproduct-unit relation on its
entire ordinary domain, with the common vertex and all mixed directions
retained.  The low-degree first-form Steinberg identity is already
contained in frozen GPR13; (USR15) below is its explicit symmetric
primitive interface, not a new low-degree phenomenon.  The new
constructions are the all-degree anticommutator and permutation
witnesses and the finite propagation of this frozen relation through
the full HCT maps.  It is not a classification of all analytic actual zero
relations, a claim that a higher raw matrix cell equals a unit cochain,
or a general original-support Green theorem.

Use the HCT array complex $L_k^s$ and its full actual maps $J_k$.
All functions and forms live over the original $K$ and absolute value.
For a scalar $a$ with $Da=0$, put $E_1(a)=a$.  In every degree set
\[
 E_k(a_1,\ldots,a_k)
      =J_{k-2}(a_k,E_{k-1}(a_1,\ldots,a_{k-1}))\quad(k\ge2).
 \tag{USR1}
\]
For $k=2$ this uses HCT4 and gives exactly the HCT15 pair.
We also use $D$ as the cycle condition on $L_0$.

\subsection{A full commutator of two unit homotopies}

Let $a,b$ be actual scalars with $Da=Db=0$ and let
$u\in L_j^s$ be a cycle: $\partial_j u=0$ if $j\ge1$, and
$Du=0$ if $j=0$.  Define the actual tuple
$Q_j(a,b;u)\in L_{j+3}^{s+2}$ by its single possibly nonzero
component
\[
      (Q_j(a,b;u))_{j+1}=-ab\,d'u_j,
                  \qquad (Q_j(a,b;u))_i=0\ (i\ne j+1).
 \tag{USR2}
\]

\begin{lemma}[Actual anticommutator homotopy]
There is the literal identity
\[
 J_{j+1}(a,J_j(b,u))+J_{j+1}(b,J_j(a,u))
                         =\partial_{j+3}Q_j(a,b;u).
 \tag{USR3}
\]
This applies also at $j=0$ with the HCT endpoint convention.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The cycle condition implies $Du_j=0$: for $j\ge1$, apply $d''$
to $d''u_{j-1}+d'u_j=0$; for $j=0$ it is the hypothesis.
On a closed input the last two components of $J_j(b,u)$ are
\[
                  b\,d'u_j,\qquad d''b\wedge u_j;
 \tag{USR4}
\]
its earlier components are $-d'b\wedge u_i$ for $i<j$.
Formula (USR4) is also exactly HCT4 when $j=0$.
The output is a cycle by HCT5--HCT6 and $Db=0$.

Expanding both iterated $J$ expressions, their components with
$i<j$ cancel by $d'a\wedge d'b+d'b\wedge d'a=0$.
The final component cancels in the same way in the double-prime
colour.  The only remaining components, at indices $j,j+1$, are
\[
                   -d'(ab)\wedge d'u_j,\qquad
                   -d''(ab)\wedge d'u_j.
 \tag{USR5}
\]
The possible terms with $Da,Db,Du_j$ have been retained before
their stated vanishing.  Differentiating (USR2) gives precisely
(USR5), since $d'^2u_j=0$ and $d''d'u_j=-Du_j=0$.
This proves the full actual equality.  Its component bidegree
$(s-j+1,s)$ is exactly the degree of slot $j+1$ of
$L_{j+3}^{s+2}$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Displayed adjacent-transposition and permutation witnesses}

The $E_k$ are multilinear in the scalar inputs by their finite
formulas.  Each is a cycle in $L_{k-1}^{k-1}$ by HCT.
Let $s_i$ transpose positions $i$ and $i+1$ in an ordered list.
Here is an explicit recursive actual tuple
$C_{k,i}(a)\in L_k^{k-1}$ satisfying
\[
                E_k(a)+E_k(s_i a)=\partial_k C_{k,i}(a).
 \tag{USR6}
\]
Start with
\[
                      C_{2,1}(a_1,a_2)=(0,a_1a_2,0).
 \tag{USR7}
\]
For $k\ge3$ and $i\le k-2$ define
\[
 C_{k,i}(a)=-J_{k-1}(a_k,C_{k-1,i}(a_1,\ldots,a_{k-1})).
 \tag{USR8}
\]
For $i=k-1$, take
\[
 C_{k,k-1}(a)=Q_{k-3}(a_k,a_{k-1};E_{k-2}(a_1,\ldots,a_{k-2})).
 \tag{USR9}
\]
For $k=3$, the last input in (USR9) means $E_1(a_1)=a_1$.
Formula (USR7) is checked by the product rule.  Formula (USR8)
follows from the \emph{nonclosed} HCT identity applied to
$C_{k-1,i}$, retaining its nonzero boundary and using only
$Da_k=0$.  Formula (USR9) is (USR3).  These observations prove
(USR6) in all degrees, including its signs and missing negative
components.

For completeness, this gives a finite witness for any permutation,
without assuming coherence of a collection of choices.  Choose one
specified finite adjacent-transposition word for $\sigma$, for
example a fixed bubble-sort word.  If its successive lists are
$a^{(0)}=a,a^{(1)},\ldots,a^{(L)}=\sigma a$, with the $j$th
transposition at $i_j$, put
\[
 W_\sigma(a)=\sum_{j=1}^L(-1)^{L-j}C_{k,i_j}(a^{(j-1)}).
 \tag{USR10}
\]
Then direct telescoping gives
\[
 E_k(\sigma a)-\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)E_k(a)
                              =\partial_k W_\sigma(a).
 \tag{USR11}
\]
Different adjacent words are not asserted to give the same $W$;
their difference is a next closed ambiguity.  That is a separate
higher-coherence question, not needed for the displayed finite
identity.  In particular, with
\[
 \overline E_k(a)=\frac1{k!}\sum_\sigma
                         \operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)E_k(\sigma a),
 \qquad
 V_k(a)=\frac1{k!}\sum_\sigma
                         \operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)W_\sigma(a),
 \tag{USR12}
\]
one has the actual equality $\overline E_k-E_k=\partial_k V_k$.
If two inputs coincide, (USR6) and an actual permutation witness
give an explicit boundary for $E_k$; no abstract choice of a
primitive is used.

\subsection{The entire actual Steinberg identity}

\paragraph{Frozen low-degree input and the displayed interface.}
Frozen GPR13 proves the entire actual first-form relation
$\mathfrak s(x-y,z-y)=0$ for the three analytic units
$(z_1,z_2,z_1+z_2)$, including the common vertex and every refined
tropical cell.  Apply it to $(\lambda,1-\lambda,1)$ and use the
product rule together with $ab=c^2$ in (USR13)--(USR14).
It gives (USR15).  We include a direct all-refinement verification
to fix the symmetric primitive and its coefficient-zero extension;
this does not reclassify GPR13 as a result of this round.

Let $\lambda$ and $1-\lambda$ both be analytic units on an ordinary
open $N$.  Set
\[
 a=v(\lambda),\qquad b=v(1-\lambda),\qquad
                 c=\min(a,0)=\min(b,0),\qquad F=c^2/2.
 \tag{USR13}
\]
The equality of the two minima follows at every analytic point
from the ultrametric inequality in its completed residue field.
The point $(a,b)$ lies in the whole three-ray set
\[
 \mathcal Y=\{(t,0):t\ge0\}\ \cup\
            \{(0,t):t\ge0\}\ \cup\
            \{(t,t):t\le0\}.
 \tag{USR14}
\]

\begin{theorem}[A finite actual Steinberg boundary]
On all of $N$, in the original pair order $(\alpha,\beta)$,
\[
       e_{a,b}:=(a\,d''b,b\,d'a)=(d''F,d'F)
                        =\partial_2(0,0,F).
 \tag{USR15}
\]
Equivalently, in array order,
$E_2(a,b)=\partial_2(0,F,0)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
All forms in (USR15) have finite common actual unit presentations.
Their first derivatives have carrier codimension zero: their
bidegrees are $(1,0)$ or $(0,1)$, which allow no positive
delta-carrier codimension.  We verify the equality on every refined
tropical presentation, not just on the two strict norm regions.

On a cell mapping to the ray $(t,0)$, the restriction of $b$ and
its tangential differential is zero, and $c=0$.  Both components
of (USR15) vanish.  On a cell mapping to $(0,t)$ the same assertion
holds with $a$ zero.  On a cell mapping to $(t,t)$ with $t\le0$,
one has, as tangential functions and differentials,
$a=b=c=t$; thus both sides are $t\,d''t$ and $t\,d't$.
On any cell mapping to the common vertex, $a=b=c=0$ and the
restricted coefficients and tangential differentials give zero.
The traces of the first forms on the ends of the three rays also
agree; their scalar factors vanish there.  There is no hidden
lower-dimensional delta component in a first form of these degrees.

More explicitly, take any affinoid domain inside $N$ and any finite
unit refinement of $(\lambda,1-\lambda)$, together with any
additional coefficient units used by other forms.  Refine its
tropical cells by the inverse images of the three rays in (USR14).
Every cell projects into one of those rays, since (USR14) holds at
all analytic points.  The preceding tangential calculation shows
that the refined tropical cycle wedged with either component of
the difference in (USR15) is zero, including all boundary cells.
The actual vanishing criterion, Mihatsch Definition 4.2 and
Lemma 4.4, therefore gives the equality in actual $B$.
This uses the criterion for \emph{every} unit refinement, not
injectivity of one many-to-one tropical pushforward.

Finally, the actual chain rule for $F=c^2/2$ gives $d'F=c\,d'c$
and $d''F=c\,d''c$.  The last equality in (USR15) is the literal
definition of the two-colour boundary.  No inference from an
ordinary-smooth test identity is involved.
\end{proof}

There is a useful coefficient-zero extension of precisely this
statement.  Suppose $\lambda$ is analytic on $N$ but may take the
values $0$ or $1$.  On $N\setminus\{\lambda(1-\lambda)=0\}$
use (USR15).  The scalar
\[
                        c=-\log\max(1,|\lambda|)
 \tag{USR16}
\]
is actual on all of $N$ using the nowhere-zero tuple $(1,\lambda)$.
Near $\lambda=0$ the ordinary inequality $|\lambda|<1$ makes
$b=0$ and the entire pair in (USR15) zero; near $\lambda=1$,
$|1-\lambda|<1$ makes $a=0$ and that pair zero.  The same
ordinary neighborhoods have $c=0$.  Hence the pair and its
displayed witness extend by the actual zero form there.  This is
local sheaf gluing, not assignment of a value to $v(0)$ or a
growth assumption.  No extension across a pole of $\lambda$ is
asserted.

\subsection{Higher Steinberg witnesses and the nonproduct triangle}

For a list whose first two entries are as in (USR13), define
\[
 T_2=(0,F,0),\qquad
 T_k=-J_{k-1}(a_k,T_{k-1})\in L_k^{k-1}\quad(k\ge3).
 \tag{USR17}
\]
If the additional $a_j$ are original analytic-unit logarithms,
then, on the entire ordinary open,
\[
                       E_k(a,b,a_3,\ldots,a_k)=\partial_kT_k.
 \tag{USR18}
\]
Indeed HCT5 applied to the \emph{nonclosed} $T_{k-1}$ gives
$\partial_kJ_{k-1}(a_k,T_{k-1})=-J_{k-2}(a_k,\partial_{k-1}T_{k-1})$.
This proves (USR18) by induction.  It is important not to apply
a closed-coefficient-only rule to $T_{k-1}$.
For $k=3$, its array is particularly transparent:
\[
                      T_3=(0,F\,d'a_3,a_3d''F,0).
 \tag{USR19}
\]
If the two Steinberg entries occur at any other positions while
all entries are units, combine (USR10)--(USR11) with (USR17)
to obtain an explicit actual witness.  In the coefficient-zero
extension of (USR16), the ordered construction with those entries
first remains identically zero near their zeros and extends in
every degree.  No assertion is made that an arbitrary reordered
witness, separately containing an unbounded $v(\lambda)$, extends
without this check.

Here is the complete nonproduct-unit relation in the coordinates
required by the stress tests.  On the entire ordinary domain where
$T_1,T_2,T_1+T_2$ are units, put
\[
 x=v(T_1),\qquad y=v(T_2),\qquad z=v(T_1+T_2),\qquad
                            c=\min(x,y)-z.
 \tag{USR20}
\]
Use $\lambda=T_1/(T_1+T_2)$ in (USR15); then
$a=x-z$, $b=y-z$ and $c=\min(a,0)$.
By exact bilinearity and (USR7), in the original pair order,
\[
 e_{x,y}-e_{x,z}-e_{z,y}
       =\partial_2\left(0,0,\frac{c^2-z^2}{2}\right).
 \tag{USR21}
\]
This retains the third, cancellation branch $x=y<z$ as well as
the two strict dominant branches and the full common tie locus.
It remains an actual equality after adjoining arbitrary passive
unit coordinates and multiplying or differentiating any supplied
actual expressions in them.  Equivalently, one may use the moving
puncture presentation $(T,-b,T-b)$ on the original relative curve
$T\notin\{0,b,\infty\}$ and then take any number of passive
directions.  No product-factor assumption is imposed on their
coefficients.

\subsection{A concrete simplification of a supplied Gauss residue}

Suppose a previously verified actual word relation has the literal
form
\[
 p_1-p_0=\partial_2Q+e_{x,d}R^{r-2},\qquad
 x=v(u),\quad y=v(v),\quad d=v(1+uv),
 \tag{USR22}
\]
where $u,v,1+uv$ are analytic units on the whole stated ordinary
open and $R$ is closed in both colours.  The frozen GPR relation
is one instance, with its exact word and cap conventions left
unchanged.  No assertion is made that arbitrary word relations
have the form (USR22).

The Steinberg input $\lambda=-uv$ gives, with
$c=\min(x+y,0)$, the actual identity
\[
                   e_{x,d}+e_{y,d}
                        =\partial_2(0,0,c^2/2).
 \tag{USR23}
\]
In particular, if the independently checkable scalar identity
\[
                             x+y=\kappa x
                 \quad\text{on the whole open},\qquad
                             \kappa\in\mathbb R\setminus\{0\},
 \tag{USR24}
\]
is supplied, then the original, specified relation becomes strict:
\[
 p_1-p_0=\partial_2\left(
       Q+(0,0,c^2R^{r-2}/(2\kappa))\right).
 \tag{USR25}
\]
For example $v=u^j$ gives (USR24) with $\kappa=j+1$ when
$j\ne-1$, on the domain where $1+u^{j+1}$ is a unit.
The equality (USR25) retains every term of the original $Q$;
it does not replace that witness by another unspecified solution.
If the relation (USR22) has already been independently zero-extended
across coefficient zeros with $|uv|<1$, the correction in (USR25)
has the same zero extension because $c=0$ there.  This last assertion
requires that supplied original extension; it does not establish it
for an arbitrary singular word.

This is a strict-witness simplification for a specified loop, not a
new independent regular-CI existence class.  Such a class would
still require full finite overlap data and frozen RAG/RSP on the
original exact $U$, followed by all actual compact-test verification.

\subsection{Objects, topology, and limits of the symbol interpretation}

The objects are the displayed actual $E_k$, and equivalence means
difference by an explicitly constructed actual $\partial_k$ boundary.
For analytic units, multiplicative additivity is literal because
$v(\lambda\mu)=v(\lambda)+v(\mu)$; (USR6) supplies skew symmetry
modulo actual boundaries; (USR18) supplies the Steinberg boundary.
One may consequently map the algebraic unit-symbol quotient with
these specified relations to
$\ker\partial_{k-1}/\operatorname{im}\partial_k$ (using $\ker D$
at degree zero).  This optional quotient is auxiliary.  It does
not classify all actual cochains, is not asserted Hausdorff or
complete, and never supplies a Green form in place of an actual
representative.

The cochain spaces retain their own inherited actual coefficient-final
topologies and the auxiliary cohomology has its quotient topology.
For every fixed finite unit list and finite word, all witnesses above
use one finite common actual presentation and finite full Leibniz
operations; their mixed coefficient seminorms have the corresponding
fixed-stage estimates.  No global continuous choice of permutation
witnesses, no universal Fr\'echet splitting, and no entire
inherited-versus-final kernel comparison is inferred.

Restrictions, refinements and the nonproduct analytic relations are
handled before any quotient is formed.  The proof of (USR15) uses
all refinements in the original field, including irrational-valued
analytic points; there is no evaluation only on rational points.
No field extension or rescaling of its absolute value occurs.
The finite actual boundary formulas can be wedged with any specified
jointly closed diagonal actual factor, retaining all mixed terms.
This neither cancels that factor from another equation nor extends
the scope to a nonclosed coefficient block, as forbidden by LTF.
