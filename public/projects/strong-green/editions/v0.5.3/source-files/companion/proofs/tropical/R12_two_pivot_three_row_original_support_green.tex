\subsection{A whole-neighborhood relative pair}

Fix the original field and absolute value.  Let $N$ be an ordinary
analytic open in the original smooth analytic space, let $f$ be the
given rank-$r$ tuple of sections of the same actually metrized line
bundle, and put
\[
 r\ge3,\qquad s=r-1,\qquad R=c_1(\overline L),\qquad
 W=N\setminus|f=0|.
 \tag{TPG1}
\]
Supply an analytic matrix
\[
 M=\begin{pmatrix}a&b&c\\d&e&f_0\\g&h&i\end{pmatrix}
                       \oplus I_{r-3},\qquad \det M\in\mathcal O(N)^\times,
 \qquad N=N_a\cup N_d,
 \quad N_a=\{a\ne0\},\quad N_d=\{d\ne0\}.
 \tag{TPG2}
\]
In particular $g$ is allowed to vanish or be unbounded.  Neither
$a$ nor $d$ is required to be a global unit on $N$.

Use the exact G3F3 and G3F5 actual pairs $P_a=(\alpha_a,\beta_a)$
and $P_d=(\alpha_d,\beta_d)$ on their punctured charts, with the
same fixed symmetric inner GB partition and all original native
caps.  The Schur matrices and their extending curvatures are
\[
 \begin{split}
 S_a&=\begin{pmatrix}
 e-db/a&f_0-dc/a\\h-gb/a&i-gc/a
 \end{pmatrix},\\
 S_d&=\begin{pmatrix}
 -b+ae/d&-c+af_0/d\\h-ge/d&i-gf_0/d
 \end{pmatrix},\qquad
 E_a=v(a)R^s+\mathcal E(S_a),\quad
 E_d=v(d)R^s+\mathcal E(S_d).
 \end{split}
 \tag{TPG3}
\]
Both Schur determinants are units on their own charts; the other
entries may be zero.  Here $\mathcal E$ is the full actual GB
curvature of G3F6, including the differentiated inner partition.
The frozen G3F identities give, literally on the indicated domains,
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 G_{Mf}-G_f=\partial_1P_a+E_a
               =\partial_1P_d+E_d,\\
 P_a-P_d=\partial_2 Q_{ad}+e_{ad}|_W,\qquad
 Q_{ad}=(A_{ad},B_{ad},M_{ad}),
 \quad e_{ad}=(e'_{ad},e''_{ad})\in L_1^s(N_a\cap N_d).
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{TPG4}
\]
The first line is understood separately on $W\cap N_a$ and
$W\cap N_d$.  The triple in the second line is not merely an
existence datum: it is the complete G3F15 expression
\[
 Q_{ad}=Q^{\rm out}_{ad}(B(S_a)U_af)
       -q_X(S_a,(E,F)/d;U_af)+q^U_{ad}
       +q_{E_{32}(-g/a)D_2(a/d)}(S_a;U_df).
 \tag{TPG5}
\]
Its outside term is the eight exact native substitutions G3F11--12;
the strict external crossing $q_X$ is EFC4, not an unidentified
cube relation.  The same-target term is the fixed SAN companion.
The last term is the finite GMU/TML block-multiplication witness.
Every coefficient-zero extension and every cap and metric term
in those exact sources is retained.  The extending pair is the
whole G3F15 error; its shorthand G3F17 is not interpreted as
separate extensions of its singular summands.

Let $\chi:\mathbb R\to[0,1]$ be smooth, equal to zero on
$(-\infty,-\varepsilon]$ and to one on $[\varepsilon,\infty)$,
for a fixed $\varepsilon>0$.  Define the outer partition by
\[
 \vartheta=\chi(v(d)-v(a))\quad\hbox{on }N_a\cap N_d,
 \qquad \vartheta=0\hbox{ near }a=0,
 \qquad \vartheta=1\hbox{ near }d=0.
 \tag{TPG6}
\]
These prescriptions define an ordinary smooth actual scalar on
all of $N$.  At an $a$-zero, $d$ is nonzero and the first formula
is already identically zero on an ordinary neighborhood; the
other case is analogous.  Thus
$\operatorname{supp}_N\vartheta\subset N_a$ and
$\operatorname{supp}_N(1-\vartheta)\subset N_d$, and the supports
of every differentiated partition factor are closed in $N$ and
contained in $N_a\cap N_d$.  This assertion does not require a
compact overlap or any boundedness at its omitted pivot-zero sets.

With closed-support zero extensions from the indicated charts,
put
\[
 \begin{split}
 \alpha={}&\vartheta\alpha_a+(1-\vartheta)\alpha_d
             +d'\vartheta\wedge A_{ad}+d''\vartheta\wedge M_{ad},\\
 \beta={}&\vartheta\beta_a+(1-\vartheta)\beta_d
             +d''\vartheta\wedge B_{ad}+d'\vartheta\wedge M_{ad},\\
 E={}&\vartheta E_a+(1-\vartheta)E_d
             -d'\vartheta\wedge e'_{ad}
             -d''\vartheta\wedge e''_{ad}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{TPG7}
\]
The pair $(\alpha,\beta)$ is actual on $W$, while $E$ is actual
on the entire unpunctured $N$.

\begin{theorem}[Two specified pivots give an actual relative primitive]
Under (TPG1)--(TPG2), the fully specified forms (TPG7) satisfy
\[
                   G_{Mf}-G_f=d'\alpha+d''\beta+E|_W.
 \tag{TPG8}
\]
This holds in every ambient rank $r\ge3$ and dimension at least
$r$.  It includes the inner GB partition, the outer partition,
all their mixed jets, proper zeros of the original section tuple,
and all analytic zero relations of the actual presentations.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply the exact two-open RAG theorem with $p_0=P_d$, $p_1=P_a$,
$\chi_1=\vartheta$, $q_{01}=Q_{ad}$ and $e=e_{ad}$.
These are supplied by the actual equality (TPG4), not by a
formal equality of matrices.  Differentiating the first two
terms of the pair in (TPG7) gives the extra terms
$d'\vartheta\wedge(\alpha_a-\alpha_d)+
d''\vartheta\wedge(\beta_a-\beta_d)$.
The four remaining terms cancel its $\partial_2Q_{ad}$ part.
In particular the two full $D\vartheta\wedge M_{ad}$ terms
have opposite signs and cancel; none is suppressed before
differentiation.  The residual part is exactly the negative
of the last two terms in $E$, proving (TPG8).
The support statement after (TPG6) justifies each extension
as local sheaf gluing.  No unweighted overlap cochain is
extended across a missing pivot or across $|f=0|$.
If one pivot chart is empty, use the other single chart;
the overlap correction is empty and the same conclusion holds.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The actual Green form for the original cycle}

Let $X/K$ be the original smooth projective geometrically integral
variety of dimension $n$, and let $s_0,s_1$ be original regular
rank-$r$ section tuples of the same line bundle with the same
metric.  Retain the original cycles and exact complements
\[
 Z=[Y_1]-[Y_0],\qquad S=|Y_0|\cup|Y_1|,\qquad
 U=X^{\rm an}\setminus|Z|,\qquad V=X^{\rm an}\setminus S,
 \qquad C_U=S\cap U.
 \tag{TPG9}
\]
Suppose an ordinary open $N_0\subset U$ containing $C_U$ carries
one original-$K$ analytic invertible three-row block $M$ as in
(TPG2), with the exact section identity
\[
              s_1=Ms_0\quad\hbox{on }N_0,
 \qquad C_U\subset\{a\ne0\}\cup\{d\ne0\}.
 \tag{TPG10}
\]
The covering condition is required only on $C_U$.  Replace $N_0$
by its ordinary open subset $N=N_0\cap(N_a\cup N_d)$; this
still contains all of $C_U$.  Invertibility gives equality of
the two zero sets on $N$, so $W=N\cap V$ in (TPG1).
The preceding theorem now supplies actual $(\alpha,\beta,E)$
on precisely the RSP domains.

Choose the ordinary smooth actual cutoff $\zeta$ on $U$ from
RSP2, with support closed in $U$ and contained in $N$, and
$\zeta=1$ near $C_U$.  Extend $\zeta\alpha,\zeta\beta$ by
zero from $N\cap V$ to $V$, denoting them by
$\widetilde\alpha,\widetilde\beta$.  Define on $V$
\[
        g_V=G_{s_1}-G_{s_0}-d'\widetilde\alpha-d''\widetilde\beta.
 \tag{TPG11}
\]
On a neighborhood of $C_U$ where $\zeta=1$, equation (TPG8)
makes $g_V=E$.  Hence these actual forms glue to
\[
                 g_Z\in B^{r-1,r-1}(U).
 \tag{TPG12}
\]
For every original actual compact test
$\eta\in B_c^{n-r,n-r}(X^{\rm an})$, the frozen support lemma
gives $\operatorname{supp}D\eta\Subset V$.  Therefore only the
products $\widetilde\alpha\wedge D\eta$ and
$\widetilde\beta\wedge D\eta$ need compact support; this
property holds even if $C_U$ accumulates at $|Z|$ in $X$.
The full actual Stokes identities give zero for both derivative
corrections in (TPG11).  The original full actual CI identities
for the two tuples, whose curvature terms cancel, now give
\[
                  \boxed{\displaystyle
                  \int_U g_Z\wedge D\eta=-\int_Z\eta.}
 \tag{TPG13}
\]
This is an actual representative on the original exact support
complement, not just a continuous functional or a relative class.
No global cutoff on $X$ and no extra growth condition has been
introduced.  The frozen CI0 hypothesis is not changed; the
present theorem is a separate repair certificate when CI0 fails.

The same argument applies to either other specified pair of
pivots by using the complete G3F21--23 or G3F28 face, with its stated
orientation.  It also applies to a supplied finite product of

\paragraph{Scope and pressure tests.}
Condition (TPG10) is an analytic certificate on the original
cycle, independent of a desired actual primitive.  It is not
automatic for an arbitrary $\mathrm{GL}_3$ matrix: its three
first-column entries can each be essential in the given pivot
cover.  A change of constant frame is not assumed to reduce
every such cover to two charts.  Overlap with previously proved
finite block-word or norm certificates is allowed; no claim
that the present class is disjoint from them is made.

The proof has no restrictions on the base unit relations, in
particular on $(T_1,T_2,T_1+T_2)$, and retains moving punctures
and arbitrarily many passive directions through the exact G3F
and EFC interfaces.  All ordinary opens and cutoffs are over
the original $K$, with its original absolute value.  Irrational
Gauss radii and nonrational points have not been removed, and
no field-degree or higher actual descent claim is used.
The full three-pivot defect, general strong G, the entire
lower-middle inherited-topology problem and unconditional
Beilinson--Bloch height remain open.
