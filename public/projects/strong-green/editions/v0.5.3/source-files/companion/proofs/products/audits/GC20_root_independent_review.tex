\paragraph{Finite data, not finite descent of every cycle.}
An equality in rational Chow has finite cycle representatives and a
finite rational-equivalence certificate. The auxiliary varieties,
correspondences, functions witnessing that equality, and the finite
intersection data descend to one finitely generated field over $k$.
The proof retains the equality itself, which is essential: descending
only its two sides would not suffice. Characteristic zero permits a
smooth nonempty finite-type base with this function field. After
shrinking, all auxiliary families are smooth projective with
geometrically integral fibers of the prescribed dimensions.

I checked the relative composition step. Ambient products are smooth
over the smooth base, hence their total spaces are regular. Relative
composition uses the ordinary diagonal Gysin and proper projection;
its codimensions are the relative ones and its generic fiber is the
original composition. By the finite-cycle description of Chow groups
over the function field, the identity holds on some nonempty open
base. No uniform descent of all classes over the extension field is
being asserted.

\paragraph{Specialization preserves composition.}
A closed point of the smooth base is a regular immersion and has
finite residue field extension over $k$. All relative products are
smooth over that base, so the relevant fiber squares are
Tor-independent. Refined pullback commutes with proper projection,
smooth pullback and the relative diagonal intersection. It therefore
specializes the correspondence identity, not merely numerical or
cohomological realizations. I checked the bivariant and regular
immersion compatibilities in the primary Stacks Project sections
42.33 and 42.59. The specialized auxiliary surfaces may differ from
the originally supplied ones, exactly as the statement allows.

\paragraph{Arithmetic consequences and new cycles.}
After specialization the certificate is an actual Chow diagonal
factorization over a finite extension. Tensoring with the good
coefficient preserves it. DP20 applies to all intermediate surface
products and all their geometric homological cycles; impossible
degrees and homological degree zero are treated as in HM20.
CV16 then kills the original obstruction, and VT17 supplies the form
on the specified support. Full-degree finite actual trace is the only
analytic descent. For every new local extension the compositum
certificate is applied to the newly specified cycle and its new
correspondence images. No old-pullback hypothesis is introduced.

For a number field, one complex certificate is specialized with the
arithmetic variety held constant. Each completion of the resulting
finite extension gives a local certificate, and complete local
degrees are used in the descent. This supplies every finite local
obstruction required by PH16 and GA17, including both full
complementary Chow groups and every new number-field extension.

\paragraph{Published moduli input and exact limit.}
I opened B\"ulles's primary paper, Theorem 0.1, and checked its
explicit complex hypotheses: smooth projective moduli of stable
twisted sheaves or stable objects for a generic stability condition
on a projective K3 or abelian surface. It gives a rational Chow
direct summand of a finite sum of shifted powers of the surface.
Finite-dimensionality is a separate corollary, not a premise of
the motive retraction. The source uses the theorem over the complex
numbers and then the independently proved specialization lemma;
it does not silently promote the published theorem to an arbitrary
$p$-adic field.

Tensor products of the actual certificates preserve the class of
intermediate curve/surface products. The argument does not cover
all hyperkahler varieties, arbitrary deformations, arbitrary
cohomological splittings, or all varieties. The R9 proposal of this
application is acknowledged rather than relabeled as a new discovery.
