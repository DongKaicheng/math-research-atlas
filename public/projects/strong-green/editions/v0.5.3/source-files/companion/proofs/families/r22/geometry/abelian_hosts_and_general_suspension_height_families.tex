\section{Arbitrary supplied abelian-fourfold hosts}
\label{r22as:section}

\paragraph{The exact scope of the extension.}
The preceding suspension geometry requires no special Frobenius
spectrum on the good fourfold host.  Frozen PC16 already proves
the full actual Green conclusion from positive fourfold
components, without a separate curve-comparison condition on
the doubles.  Combining that closed interface with the actual
SU22 resolution extends the local construction to every
supplied good abelian-fourfold host.  A second argument removes
the auxiliary normal-generation restriction from the global
construction: every supplied projective everywhere-good
abelian fourfold has a sufficiently positive integral
polarization which works.  No existence of such a good model
for an arbitrary abelian variety is asserted.

\subsection{Positive hosts without a double-comparison hypothesis}

\begin{theorem}[Positive-host suspension, AS22-local]
\label{r22as:local}
Take exactly the geometric data and transversality hypotheses
of SU22, \eqref{r22su:datum}--\eqref{r22su:X}, and assume only
\[
             b^-\bigl(N^2(M)_{\mathbf R},\deg(xy)\bigr)
                          =\rho(M)-1.
 \tag{AS22.1}\label{r22as:MP}
\]
Do not assume $\mathrm{CC}_\ell(F_i)$.  The actual ordered
resolution is a reduced strictly semistable projective model
whose full $U_2$ is zero.  Its generic fourfold satisfies full
strong G in every codimension.

If \eqref{r22as:MP} holds for the full new native numerical
group after every finite constant extension, the conclusion
holds for every new specified geometrically homologically
trivial rational cycle after every finite valued extension
of the original field.  This assertion makes no additional
claim that the double strata satisfy $\mathrm{CC}_\ell$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The entire chart and stratum calculation in the proof of
SU22 uses only its supplied geometric data.  The components
are unchanged copies of $M$ and iterated smooth surface
blowups of $F_i\times\mathbf P^1$.  The full middle
signature of the latter product is the closed rank-two
projective-bundle calculation in PC16, restated with
proof in DS22.  It uses the perfect numerical pairing
$N^1(F_i)\times N^2(F_i)$, and requires no comparison
between numerical and homological equivalence on $F_i$.
Condition \eqref{r22as:MP} treats the point components.
Each actual surface blowup adds the negative of its
surface divisor form and adds one to the Picard rank,
componentwise with native degrees.  Thus all components
have negative middle index exactly their Picard rank
minus one.  Divisor Hodge index for the restrictions of
one actual relatively ample line bundle proves positivity
of every entire primitive middle form.

PU18 now gives $U_2=0$ on this actual model by its
primitive-isotropic representative theorem.  This is
the whole numerator $\ker\rho_2\cap\operatorname{im}\sigma_2$,
including the genuine higher-stratum terms.

For the Green conclusion use the independent closed route
PC16, Theorem~\textup{r16pos:local}, rather than appending
an unproved double-comparison gate.  Its hypotheses are
exactly a regular projective model with smooth strict
normal-crossings strata and primitive-positive fourfold
components for a relatively ample class, with native
degrees.  They have just been verified.  PC16's
multiplicity-weighted isotropy and pure-operational
residual argument therefore kills the actual obstruction
of every geometrically homologically trivial cycle.
Its actual reconstruction, or equivalently VT17, gives
the required ordinary actual form on the exact original
specified support complement, tested against every
compact actual form.

After a finite constant extension the entire argument
reapplies to the new native cycle groups under the
stated hypothesis.  RI18 then handles every finite
valued extension.  Its full-degree finite actual trace
retains the originally fixed absolute value.  No
new cycle is assumed to descend, and no arbitrary
proper actual pushforward is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[All good abelian-fourfold hosts]
\label{r22as:abelianlocal}
Every supplied SU22 geometric smoothing on a good
abelian-fourfold host has full strong G in every
codimension, after every finite valued extension and
for every new specified cycle there.  Its actual
model has $U_2=0$.

Likewise DS22 applies whenever its two good surface
models are abelian surfaces, with no restriction to
isogenous squares or to a prescribed Frobenius spectrum.
In this two-surface case the independent double
comparison already proved in DS22 remains valid,
and DW22's exact second-monodromy formula is unchanged.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Ancona's Theorem 1.4 gives, for every abelian fourfold
over a field of positive characteristic, the full
numerical middle signature
\[
                 (\rho_2-\rho_1+1,\ \rho_1-1).
 \tag{AS22.2}\label{r22as:Ancona}
\]
This is the published abelian input already activated
in frozen PC16, Lemma~\textup{r16pos:types}; it is not a
new positivity theorem here.  Reapply it to the whole
numerical group over each finite constant extension.
This proves \eqref{r22as:MP} in precisely the scope
needed by Theorem~\ref{r22as:local}.  The product of
two abelian surfaces is itself an abelian fourfold,
so the same argument supplies DS22's only middle
signature hypothesis.  The remaining DS22 and DW22
proofs made no special assumption on the two surfaces.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A complete integral coefficient lemma}

\begin{lemma}[A sufficiently positive, normally generated choice]
\label{r22as:coeff}
Let $R$ be the ring of integers of a number field, and
let $\mathcal A/R$ be a supplied smooth projective
geometrically integral scheme of positive relative
dimension.  There is a relatively very ample line
bundle $\mathcal H$ such that, for every $m\ge1$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $H^0(\mathcal A,\mathcal H^m)$ is a finite
projective $R$-module, its formation commutes with
residue-field extension and finite flat extension of
$R$, and all geometric-fiber higher cohomology
$H^i(\mathcal H^m)$ vanishes for $i>0$;
\item the full section algebra
$\bigoplus_{m\ge0}H^0(\mathcal A,\mathcal H^m)$ is
generated in degree one;
\item a finite free module of degree-$m$ ordinary
ambient homogeneous polynomials surjects onto
$H^0(\mathcal A,\mathcal H^m)$, both integrally and
after every such base change.
\end{enumerate}
For $\mathcal Y=\mathcal A\times_R\mathbf P^1_R$ and
$\mathcal L_0=\mathcal H\boxtimes\mathcal O(1)$ the
same assertions hold for the full section algebra.
In particular the entire degree-five coefficient
space has one finite free integral ambient polynomial
module surjecting onto it and all its residue spaces.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Choose a relative very ample line bundle $\mathcal L$
from a projective embedding into ordinary projective
space over the affine base $\operatorname{Spec}R$.
The graded section algebra
$S=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}H^0(\mathcal A,\mathcal L^n)$
is finitely generated over $R$.  Indeed, for the
closed immersion $i$ into projective space, the
graded sections of the coherent sheaf $i_*\mathcal O_{\mathcal A}$
form a finite module over the homogeneous coordinate
polynomial ring.  This is the projective coherent
finiteness theorem, Stacks Project, Lemma 30.14.1,
tag \texttt{01YR}.  Finite module generators together
with the coordinate sections generate $S$ as an
$R$-algebra.

For all sufficiently divisible $e$, the Veronese
$S^{(e)}$ is generated in degree one, by Stacks
Project, Lemma 10.56.2, tag \texttt{0EGH}.  Choose
such an $e$ also sufficiently large that Serre
vanishing holds in every positive cohomological
degree for $\mathcal L^n$ and all $n\ge e$.
This is the ample-cohomology theorem over the
Noetherian affine base (Stacks, tag \texttt{01XO}).
Set $\mathcal H=\mathcal L^e$.

Here are the integral base-change details.  Each
module $H^0(\mathcal A,\mathcal H^m)$ is finite by
properness and is torsion-free because $\mathcal A$
is flat over the domain $R$ and $\mathcal H^m$ is
invertible.  Hence it is projective over the
Dedekind ring $R$.  Localize at a nonzero prime
$v$, with uniformizer $\varpi$.  The exact sequence
\[
 0\longrightarrow\mathcal H^m
   \xrightarrow{\ \varpi\ }\mathcal H^m
   \longrightarrow\mathcal H^m|_{\mathcal A_{k(v)}}
   \longrightarrow0
 \tag{AS22.3}\label{r22as:basechange}
\]
and the already established vanishing in every
positive degree show that
\[
 H^0(\mathcal A_{k(v)},\mathcal H^m)
       =H^0(\mathcal A,\mathcal H^m)\otimes_R k(v),
 \qquad H^i(\mathcal A_{k(v)},\mathcal H^m)=0
                  \quad(i>0).
 \tag{AS22.4}\label{r22as:fiberbasechange}
\]
Cohomology commutes with localization, as used
here.  Flat cohomological base change then gives
the assertions for finite flat number-ring
extensions, the fraction field, and arbitrary
extensions of each residue field.  In particular
the geometric-fiber assertions follow.  This
argument does not assume that a finite projective
section module is free.

Choose finitely many generators of
$H^0(\mathcal A,\mathcal H)$ and hence a finite free
module surjecting onto it.  They give the relative
very ample embedding.  The degree-one generation
of $S^{(e)}$ says precisely that the polynomial
module in these generators surjects in every
degree.  Tensoring this surjection and using
\eqref{r22as:fiberbasechange} proves all claimed
base changes.

Finally the full section algebra for $\mathcal L_0$
has degree-$m$ term
\[
 H^0(\mathcal A,\mathcal H^m)\otimes_R
                         H^0(\mathbf P^1_R,\mathcal O(m)).
 \tag{AS22.5}\label{r22as:Segre}
\]
These terms have the asserted base changes by the
first part and the projective-line formula.
Every product of $m$ generators from the first
factor and $m$ linear factors from the second
can be regrouped into $m$ paired generators.
Thus the Segre section algebra is generated in
degree one.  Free generators and ordinary ambient
degree-$m$ polynomials give the desired surjection.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A complete global family for each supplied good abelian host}

\begin{theorem}[Abelian-host complete-height family, AS22-global]
\label{r22as:global}
Let $F_0$ be any number field and let
$\mathcal A/\mathcal O_{F_0}$ be any supplied
projective abelian scheme of relative dimension four.
Choose a sufficiently positive $\mathcal H$ as in
Lemma~\ref{r22as:coeff}, and an odd rational prime
$p$ unramified in $F_0$.  Put
\[
 \mathcal Y=\mathcal A\times\mathbf P^1,\qquad
 \mathcal L_0=\mathcal H\boxtimes\mathcal O(1),\qquad
                          c=\deg(\mathcal H^4)>0.
 \tag{AS22.6}\label{r22as:globaldatum}
\]
There is one fixed finite extension $F/F_0$,
unramified at all places over $p$, and a
Zariski-dense family of smooth geometrically
integral fourfolds in $|\mathcal L_0^5|_{Y_F}$
with the following properties:
\begin{enumerate}
\item At every finite place, after every finite
extension $F'/F$, every new specified geometrically
homologically trivial rational cycle on the
corresponding completion has full strong G in
every codimension.
\item The frozen PH16 construction therefore
defines the full complementary BB heights on
\[
 \operatorname{CH}^r(X_{F'})_{\mathbf Q,\mathrm{geom\,hom}}
 \times
 \operatorname{CH}^{5-r}(X_{F'})_{\mathbf Q,\mathrm{geom\,hom}},
                      \qquad1\le r\le4,
 \tag{AS22.7}\label{r22as:heights}
\]
and GA17 supplies admissible regular-model Chow
lifts on these full proved domains.
\item At every distinguished place above $p$,
\[
        \operatorname{rank}(N^4|H^4)=4(5c-4)>0,
                       \qquad N^5=0.
 \tag{AS22.8}\label{r22as:N4}
\]
The minimum strict semistable dominance depth,
taken over all finite valued extensions and all
smooth projective dominant witnesses, is exactly
four; its lower bound holds over each fixed
finite valued extension.
\item Every member has $b_1=8$ and $b_2=29$.
\end{enumerate}
The number field is fixed before varying the
dense family.  The assertion concerns supplied
everywhere-good abelian schemes and their
constructed divisors; it does not assert that
an arbitrary abelian variety acquires everywhere
good reduction or that every divisor in this
linear system satisfies G.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
We give the finite-place construction because a
local family at one completion does not suffice
for \eqref{r22as:heights}.  The coefficient module
is the full finite free degree-five ambient
polynomial module supplied by
Lemma~\ref{r22as:coeff}; all residue data below
can be lifted in that module.

At each place of $F_0$ above $p$, after a finite
residue extension choose five divisors
$F_i\in|\mathcal H|$ with the simultaneous Bertini
properties of SQ22: every intersection of one,
two or three is smooth geometrically integral,
every fourfold intersection is finite etale,
and fivefold intersections are empty.  Split
every fourfold intersection by a further finite
residue extension.  Each consists of exactly
$c$ native points, since the divisor classes
are all $\mathcal H$ and all local intersection
multiplicities are one.  Choose five distinct
native projective-line points and a section
$Q$ of $\mathcal L_0^5$ satisfying every SU22
transversality condition and avoiding the
zero-dimensional quintuple strata.
These conditions define nonempty opens over
the algebraic closure in the relevant very
ample section spaces, so a finite residue
extension supplies them all.  This is a
finite-field preparation, not an assertion
that an arbitrary prescribed residue field
already has the needed rational sections.

Choose a smooth generic reference section $H_0$
and let $S_0$ be the finite set of rational
primes, other than $p$, containing all its
bad reductions and the prime $2$.  At every
place over $S_0$ choose, after a finite residue
extension, a smooth replacement section $G_v$.
One monic irreducible CRT polynomial, of degree
divisible by all the selected residue degrees,
gives a fixed field $F/F_0$ realizing all these
extensions and unramified at every selected
place.  The irreducible reductions impose
separability and the required residue
extensions; irreducibility over $F_0$ follows
from any one of them.  This is precisely
DG22's fixed-field construction, now using
Lemma~\ref{r22as:coeff} for its section lifts.
In particular $p$ remains a uniformizer at
each distinguished place.

Lift each of the five divisor sections and
each of the five point forms by CRT.  Their
actual product
\[
 P=(\prod_{i=1}^{5}f_i)(\prod_{j=1}^{5}l_j)
               \in H^0(\mathcal Y,\mathcal L_0^5)
 \tag{AS22.9}\label{r22as:P}
\]
has bidegree $(5,5)$ and therefore a degree-five
Segre polynomial representative.  The individual
factors are not mistaken for degree-one Segre
sections.  Lift all the chosen $Q_v$ as well.
Set $B=\prod_{q\in S_0}q$ and $M_0=p^2B$.
Impose in the free coefficient module
\[
 H_1\equiv P+pQ\pmod{p^2\mathcal O_F},\qquad
 H_1\equiv G_v\pmod{\mathfrak p_v}
                         \quad(v\text{ over }S_0).
 \tag{AS22.10}\label{r22as:CRT}
\]
This is a nonempty full lattice coset, hence is
Zariski dense over $F$.

Intersect this coset with the nonempty
fixed-reference Lefschetz-pencil open.  The
closed degree-five ambient pencil theorem used
in DG22 applies to every smooth projective
embedded ambient: degree five supplies its
required jets, and $H_0$ is smooth.  Thus it
applies to this embedded $\mathcal A_F\times
\mathbf P^1_F$ as well.  After fixing an allowed
$H_1$, spread that pencil and let $T_0$ be the
finite set of exceptional rational primes.
Choose infinitely many integers $a$ with
\[
 a\equiv1\pmod{p^2B},\qquad
 a\equiv0\pmod q
           \quad(q\in T_0\setminus(S_0\cup\{p\})),
 \qquad X_a=V(M_0H_0+aH_1),
 \tag{AS22.11}\label{r22as:Xa}
\]
omitting the finitely many singular generic
parameters.  At a distinguished place, divide
by the unit $a$.  The resulting equation is
$P+pQ_a$, where
$Q_a=Q+pR+pB a^{-1}H_0$ and
$\overline{Q_a}=\bar Q$.  It is exactly the
supplied actual suspension of
Theorem~\ref{r22as:local}.  Ancona supplies its
host positivity over every finite constant
extension.

At places above $S_0$ the reduction is the
smooth $G_v$.  At all other exceptional places
it is a nonzero scalar multiple of the
smooth reference $H_0$.  At every remaining
place it is a smooth or ordinary-one-node
member of the spread pencil.  All
characteristic-two places belong to $S_0$.
There is therefore no omitted finite place.

The Cartier flatness and geometric integrality
checks are also uniform.  Each special section
is nonzero on the integral smooth ambient.
On every geometric fiber
$H^0(\mathcal L_0^{-5})=0$, and Kunneth gives
\[
\begin{split}
 H^1(\mathcal A_{\bar k}\times\mathbf P^1,
                     \mathcal H^{-5}\boxtimes\mathcal O(-5))
  ={}&
 H^1(\mathcal A_{\bar k},\mathcal H^{-5})
                        \otimes H^0(\mathbf P^1,\mathcal O(-5))\\
 &{}\oplus
 H^0(\mathcal A_{\bar k},\mathcal H^{-5})
                        \otimes H^1(\mathbf P^1,\mathcal O(-5))
       =0.
\end{split}
 \tag{AS22.12}\label{r22as:connected}
\]
Both vanishing factors are simply degree-zero
sections of negative ample line bundles.  No
abelian higher-cohomology vanishing is needed
in this step.  The divisor sequence gives
connectedness.  Smooth divisors and divisors
with at most an isolated ordinary node in
dimension four are normal, hence these
connected geometric fibers are integral.
The distinguished reducible fiber has the
explicit integral regular model supplied by
the actual ordered resolution, with smooth
geometrically integral generic fiber by our
choice of parameter.

At the distinguished places apply
Theorem~\ref{r22as:local} to every new cycle
after every finite valued extension.  At
all other places apply the frozen good-
reduction and ON17 all-finite-extension
theorems.  For any finite $F'/F$ this checks
all completions and their entire new cycle
groups.  PC16, RI18 and VT17 retain the
exact original support, every ordinary
actual compact test, and the full finite
degrees with the extending absolute values.
The full local hypothesis of PH16 is now
proved, and its closed archimedean input
gives \eqref{r22as:heights}.  GA17 gives
the stated Chow lifts.

The field, residue extensions and congruence
coset were fixed before varying $H_1$.
For each allowed direction infinitely many
parameters $a$ satisfy \eqref{r22as:Xa}.
If a polynomial vanishes on the resulting
family, its restriction to each of these
affine lines has infinitely many roots and
vanishes identically.  Evaluating at one
nonzero parameter shows it vanishes on a
translate of a dense set of directions.
It is therefore the zero polynomial.
This proves density in the full free
coefficient space, and its surjection
gives density in the full section space.
Allowing the exceptional set $T_0$ to
depend on $H_1$ does not alter this
argument.

The complete extreme-weight calculation
in SQ22 depends only on the incidence
data, native degree-one quintuple points,
and unchanged quadruple curves.  These
conditions hold here verbatim, with
$c=\deg\mathcal H^4$ in place of $1944$.
Its five-divisor incidence complex has
$5c$ tetrahedra and boundary rank four;
joining with five points gives top
cohomology dimension $4(5c-4)$.
The whole extreme Gysin is the transpose
of the restriction incidence matrix,
and the common-stratum monodromy map is
the rational harmonic kernel-to-quotient
isomorphism.  Thus \eqref{r22as:N4}
and the stated dominance-depth conclusion
follow with exactly SQ22's proof, including
its full finite-extension normalization.
Finally weak Lefschetz identifies $H^1$
and $H^2$ of each smooth ample fourfold
with those of $\mathcal A_F\times\mathbf P^1$.
An abelian fourfold has $b_1=8$ and
$b_2=\binom82=28$; the projective-line
factor adds one to $b_2$.  This proves
the last assertion.
\end{proof}
