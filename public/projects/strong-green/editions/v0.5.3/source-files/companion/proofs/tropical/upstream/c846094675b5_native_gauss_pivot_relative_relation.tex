\section{An actual relative witness for the opposite-root Gauss pivot}
\label{sec:r11-native-gauss-pivot}

\paragraph{Status and provenance.}
\textsc{Proved draft, submitted for exact-hash independent review.}
The R11 root and challenge agents are construction contributors and
must be excluded from its counted nonconstructor reviews.  The new
statement concerns one specified native opposite-root matrix relation.
It is not a general factorization theorem, an arbitrary-cover coherence
theorem, or a solution of Strong G.  The extending coefficient pair is
retained; no strict witness is imposed where the proof gives a relative
one.  No missing R10 CG, RA or RSC source is used.

Let $N$ be an ordinary analytic open over the original fixed $K$, let
$L$ carry its fixed piecewise smooth metric, and let
$f=(f_1,\ldots,f_r)$ be analytic sections of $L$, with $r\ge2$.
Set
\[
 W=N\setminus|f=0|,\qquad s=r-1,\qquad D=d'd'',\qquad
 R=c_1(\overline L).
 \tag{GPR1}
\]
No regular-sequence assumption is needed for the local relation.
Fix distinct indices $i,j$.  Let $a,b\in\mathcal O(N)$ be arbitrary
analytic functions, and assume only that
\[
                       \delta=1+ab\in\mathcal O(N)^\times.
 \tag{GPR2}
\]
In particular $a,b$ may vanish and need not be bounded.  Define
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 P=E_{ij}(a),\qquad Q=E_{ji}(b),\qquad
 P_0=E_{ij}(a/\delta),\qquad Q_0=E_{ji}(b/\delta),\\
 H_i=D_i(\delta),\qquad H_j=D_j(\delta^{-1}),
 \qquad PQ=Q_0H_jH_iP_0.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{GPR3}
\]
Matrices act on column tuples, rightmost first.  Thus the temporal
left path is $Q,P$ and the temporal right path is $P_0,H_i,H_j,Q_0$.
Put
\[
 f_b=Qf,\quad f_*=PQf,\quad
 g_1=P_0f,\quad g_2=H_i g_1,\quad g_3=H_jg_2,
 \qquad Q_0g_3=f_*.
 \tag{GPR4}
\]
The prescribed native SHT pair defect is
\[
\begin{split}
 \mathscr G={}&\mathcal P_{ji,b}(f)+\mathcal P_{ij,a}(f_b)
 -\mathcal P_{ij,a/\delta}(f)\\
 &-p_{i,\delta}(g_1)-p_{j,\delta^{-1}}(g_2)
 -\mathcal P_{ji,b/\delta}(g_3).
\end{split}
\tag{GPR5}
\]
Here $\mathcal P$ is exactly the frozen SHT native shear pair,
including its zero-coefficient extension, and $p$ is the prescribed
full antisymmetric SHT unit-entry pair.  The two diagonal weights
sum to zero, so $\partial_1\mathscr G=0$ by full endpoint
telescoping; this alone would not construct a witness.

\subsection{The extending pair and the complete ten-edge presentation}

Write $N_a=\{a\ne0\}$ and set, there,
\[
 A=v(a),\qquad u=v(\delta),\qquad
 e_{A,u}=(A\,d''u,\ u\,d'A).
 \tag{GPR6}
\]
On the ordinary open $\{|ab|<1\}$ one has $|\delta|=1$, hence
$u=0$ as an actual scalar with all derivatives zero.  The pair in
(GPR6) is therefore zero on its overlap with this open, and glues
with zero to a pair $e_{A,u}\in L_1^1(N)$ on all of $N$.
The two domains cover $N$.  This notation always denotes that
actual extension, not a value assigned to $v(0)$.
Since $DA=Du=0$ on $N_a$, the signs in the two components give
\[
                         \partial_1e_{A,u}=0
                                      \quad\text{on all of }N.
 \tag{GPR7}
\]
The scalar $Au$ similarly extends by zero across $a=0$.

On $N_{ab}=\{ab\ne0\}$ also write $B=v(b)$.  Put
\[
\begin{array}{llll}
 F=\Phi_f,& M_b=\Phi_{D_j(b)^{-1}f},& F_b=\Phi_{f_b},
   & M_a=\Phi_{D_i(a)^{-1}f_b},\\
 F_*=\Phi_{f_*},& M_{b/\delta}=\Phi_{D_j(b/\delta)^{-1}g_3},
 &G_3=\Phi_{g_3},&G_2=\Phi_{g_2},\\
 G_1=\Phi_{g_1},&M_{a/\delta}=\Phi_{D_i(a/\delta)^{-1}f}.&&
\end{array}
\tag{GPR8}
\]
The exact oriented unit-edge loop of (GPR5) is
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{edge}&\text{ordered potentials}&\text{weight}\\\hline
1&(F,M_b)&-B\\
2&(M_b,F_b)&B\\
3&(F_b,M_a)&-A\\
4&(M_a,F_*)&A\\
5&(F_*,M_{b/\delta})&u-B\\
6&(M_{b/\delta},G_3)&B-u\\
7&(G_3,G_2)&u\\
8&(G_2,G_1)&-u\\
9&(G_1,M_{a/\delta})&u-A\\
10&(M_{a/\delta},F)&A-u.
\end{array}
\tag{GPR9}
\]
Each native midpoint has its two genuine representatives related
by the constant norm isometry $E_{ij}(1)$ or $E_{ji}(1)$.
Every representative leaves all rows outside $i,j$ unchanged.
All ten weights sum to zero, including the two diagonal edges.

\subsection{The exact degree-one residual, including cancellations}

For one of the two colours, write
$\mathfrak s(x,y)=x\,dy-y\,dx$.  All calculations below are
performed separately for $d'$ and $d''$, with the opposite sign
in the second component prescribed by
\[
 \mathfrak a(x,y;Q)=
 ((x\,d''y-y\,d''x)Q,-(x\,d'y-y\,d'x)Q).
 \tag{GPR10}
\]
For a unit edge $(p,q,c)$, the degree-one scalar symbol of the
frozen CS residual is
\[
 S_1(p,q,c)=\mathfrak s(p,q)
       +\mathfrak s(c_+,q)+\mathfrak s(c_-,p),
 \qquad c_+=\max(c,0),\quad c_-=\min(c,0).
 \tag{GPR11}
\]
No claim is made that this edge residual is closed.

\begin{lemma}[Two elementary full-wall symbol identities]
For the finite piecewise affine valuation scalars used below, and
also after adding one common actual scalar to both of them, the
minimum has the displayed finite actual presentation and satisfies
\[
 \mathfrak s(\min(x,y),x-y)=-\mathfrak s(x,y).
 \tag{GPR12}
\]
For analytic units $z_1,z_2,z_1+z_2$ on an ordinary open,
writing $x=v(z_1)$, $y=v(z_2)$, $z=v(z_1+z_2)$,
\[
                         \mathfrak s(x-y,z-y)=0.
 \tag{GPR13}
\]
Both identities are actual first-form identities and retain the
full relations on all valuation walls.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For (GPR12), substitute the two possible minima; both yield the
same right side and hence the same restricted first jets on their
common wall.  For (GPR13), divide by $z_2$.  The possible
valuation pairs of $t$ and $1+t$ lie on the three rays
$\{x=0,z\ge0\}$, $\{z=0,x\ge0\}$ and
$\{x=z\le0\}$.  On each ray $x\,dz-z\,dx=0$, including
the restricted first jet at the origin.  This is an identity on
every allowed refinement of the actual unit presentation.  It
does not replace $v(1+t)$ by a generic minimum or discard its
cancellation locus.
\end{proof}

Suppose for the moment that all ten norm vertices are determined
by rows $i,j$, and work on a local unit presentation for the four
entries about to be displayed.  In a local frame put
\[
 x=v(f_i),\quad y=v(f_j),\quad
 X=v(\delta f_i+a f_j),\quad Y=v(bf_i+f_j).
 \tag{GPR14}
\]
A common metric potential may be added to all four values; it
will cancel from the resulting full-loop expression.

For a rank-one unit edge changing the first of two active rows,
with other-row potential $y$, the full-wall one-entry minimum
calculation gives
\[
 S_1(p,q,c)=\mathfrak s(p-q+c,y).
 \tag{GPR15}
\]
Indeed subtract $y$ from both endpoints.  Their normalized
potentials are $\min(t,0)$ and $\min(t+c,0)$.  Substitution
in (GPR11), separately on the two signs of $c$, gives zero with
the full restricted first jets.  The effect of adding $y$ back
is exactly $\mathfrak s(p-q+c,y)$.  The same formula with
$x$ holds when the second row changes.  The two edges of a
native shear have opposite weights and the same unchanged row,
so their sum is $\mathfrak s(p-q,y)$ or
$\mathfrak s(p-q,x)$, respectively.

Let
\[
 F=\min(x,y),\quad C=\min(x,Y),\quad H=\min(X,Y),
 \quad S=\min(X,y).
 \tag{GPR16}
\]
Write $R_1=\Phi_{g_1}$ and $T_1=\Phi_{g_3}$ temporarily.
Applying (GPR15) to the six steps in (GPR5) gives
\[
\begin{split}
 \sum_eS_{e,1}={}&\mathfrak s(F-C,x)+\mathfrak s(C-H,Y)
       -\mathfrak s(F-R_1,y)\\
 &-\mathfrak s(R_1-S+u,y)
       -\mathfrak s(S-T_1-u,X)-\mathfrak s(T_1-H,X)\\
 ={}&\mathfrak s(x-X,Y-y)-\mathfrak s(X-y,u).
\end{split}
\tag{GPR17}
\]
For the last equality first telescope $R_1,T_1$, and then use
(GPR12) on the four corners (GPR16).  Thus no native midpoint,
diagonal weight, or mixed derivative has been omitted.

Now put
\[
 z_0=\frac{f_i}{a f_j},\qquad
 V_0=1+\frac{bf_i}{f_j}=1+abz_0,\qquad
 W_0=1+\frac{\delta f_i}{a f_j}=1+\delta z_0,
 \tag{GPR18}
\]
and let $t=v(z_0)$, $v_0=v(V_0)$, $w_0=v(W_0)$.
The crucial relations are the actual analytic equalities
\[
                         W_0=V_0+z_0,
                 \qquad W_0=1+\delta z_0.
 \tag{GPR19}
\]
By (GPR13) they imply
\[
 \mathfrak s(t-v_0,w_0-v_0)=0,\qquad
 \mathfrak s(u+t,w_0)=0,
 \qquad
 \mathfrak s(t-w_0,v_0)=\mathfrak s(w_0,u).
 \tag{GPR20}
\]
The last identity is their explicit subtraction.  Since
$x-X=t-w_0$, $Y-y=v_0$ and $X-y=A+w_0$, (GPR17) becomes
\[
                  \sum_e S_{e,1}=-\mathfrak a(A,u;1)
 \tag{GPR21}
\]
as an actual pair, for the complete ten-edge presentation.

\paragraph{Zero active entries are not a density argument.}
Here is an ordinary local verification where one of the four entries
in (GPR14) vanishes.  All two-by-two matrices defining the finitely
many norm vertices are invertible on $N_{ab}$, and the active
two-tuple is nonzero.  Choose a local analytic frame.  At the given
analytic point choose a vector $v\in K^2$ avoiding the kernels of
the finitely many relevant nonzero row functionals, and then choose
an arbitrarily small $\epsilon\in K$ avoiding the finitely many
values for which one of the four entries of
$f_{\rm act}+\epsilon v$ vanishes at that point.  This is possible
over the infinite original local field.  Make $\epsilon$ small
enough that its image under each of the finitely many matrices has
strictly smaller norm than the original transformed two-tuple.
The ultrametric inequality and continuity then give one ordinary
neighborhood on which \emph{all} displayed norm potentials are
unchanged by this auxiliary replacement, while the four entries
needed for (GPR18) are units.  Equation (GPR21) proved there is
therefore the same actual identity for the original potentials.
The replacement is only a proof of equality of already fixed
finite norm expressions: it is never used to replace the original
cycle or the original Green data.

The sum (GPR21) is also invariant under any one common actual
scalar shift of all vertex potentials.  From (GPR11) its change
is $\sum_e\mathfrak s(p_e-q_e+c_e,h)$, which is zero by
endpoint and weight telescoping.  Thus it retains the original
metric and the cap change used next.

\subsection{All-rank transport and the actual triple}

For $r\ge3$ let $\psi$ be the norm potential of the unchanged
$r-2$ rows outside $i,j$, with value $+\infty$ at their common
zero set.  On $N_{ab}\cap W$ put
\[
 \kappa=2\bigl(|A|+|B|+|A-u|+|B-u|+|u|\bigr),
 \quad\Psi=\min(\psi,F+\kappa+\varepsilon),\quad
 R_*=R-D\Psi,
 \tag{GPR22}
\]
where $\varepsilon>0$ is one fixed real constant.  This is the
sum of all ten absolute edge weights.  The cap is actual on the
entire punctured unit locus by the ordinary-neighborhood argument
of frozen CS24, including passive-zero points.  The ordinary opens
\[
 W_{\rm pas}=\{\psi<F+\kappa+\varepsilon\},\qquad
 W_{\rm two}=\{\psi>F+\kappa\}
 \tag{GPR23}
\]
cover.  Each vertex differs in potential from $F$ by at most
$\kappa$, because each unit edge changes a norm by at most the
absolute value of its weight.  Thus only rows $i,j$ determine
every norm on $W_{\rm two}$.

Compute all CS residuals using the shifted potentials
$\Phi_\nu-\Psi$ and reference $R_*$.  On $W_{\rm pas}$,
frozen CS21 applies to every edge: its common passive tuple has
$r-2$ entries and is unchanged by all the actual intermediate
representatives.  Thus $S_{e,s}=0$ and $R_*^{s-1}=0$ there.
On $W_{\rm two}$ the full CS17 two-entry recurrence and (GPR21)
give the top residual.  Actual locality proves
\[
                  \sum_eS_{e,s}=-\mathfrak a(A,u;R_*^{s-1})
                   \quad\text{on all of }N_{ab}\cap W.
 \tag{GPR24}
\]
This is not a separate-closedness claim about the lower-degree
pieces and uses no division by a curvature form.

For each of the ten edges use the explicit frozen CS6--CS10
triple
\[
 \mathcal K_s(e;R_*)=(0,0,M_{e,s})
       +\tfrac12\bigl(Q^{\rm tet}_{e,s}-R_*Q^{\rm tet}_{e,s-1}\bigr).
 \tag{GPR25}
\]
Here, for edge $(p,q,c)$, $t=|c|$, $C_p=R_*-Dp$, $C_q=R_*-Dq$,
\[
 M_{e,s}=-\tfrac14\left[
 (q^2-p^2)h_{s-1}(R_*,C_p,C_q)
 +t(q-p)h_{s-1}(C_p,C_q)
 +c\{p h_{s-1}(R_*,C_p)+q h_{s-1}(R_*,C_q)\}\right].
 \tag{GPR26}
\]
The tetrahedron triple is the specified CS9 polynomial in
$(t,p,q)$, whose curvature factor is
$h_{s-2}(R_*,R_*-D|c|,C_p,C_q)$; it is zero in degree at
most one.  In particular (GPR25) denotes that exact finite actual
formula, not a chosen primitive.  All its terms are also displayed
in the R11 Steinberg source ZDS27--ZDS31.

Let $Q_{\rm met}$ be the exact full-loop MT15 triple for the
ten unshifted vertex potentials, their edge weights in (GPR9),
and the common scalar $h=-\Psi$.  Thus
\[
 Q_{\rm met}=
 \sum_{k=1}^{s-1}\left(\sum_{\ell=0}^{s-k-1}R_*^\ell R^{s-k-1-\ell}\right)
     \mathcal H_{-\Psi}(f_k)
 -\left(0,0,-\Psi\sum_{k=1}^s\Gamma_{k-1}R_*^{s-k}\right),
 \tag{GPR27}
\]
where $f_k,\Gamma_k$ are the complete finite edge sums MT5--MT6,
not assumed closed, and
$\mathcal H_h(\alpha,\beta)=(d''h\wedge\alpha,-d'h\wedge\beta,h\,d'\alpha)$.
These polynomials are also displayed in ZDS34--ZDS37.  The sign
convention is $\mathscr G^*-\mathscr G=\partial_2Q_{\rm met}$.

Set $d_0=s-1=r-2$ and, for $r\ge3$, put
\[
 h_*=-\Psi\sum_{\ell=0}^{d_0-1}R_*^\ell R^{d_0-1-\ell},
 \qquad Dh_*=R_*^{d_0}-R^{d_0}.
 \tag{GPR28}
\]
The closedness (GPR7), not a false closedness of a residual block,
allows the frozen CPT transport
\[
 e_{A,u}R_*^{d_0}-e_{A,u}R^{d_0}
                     =\partial_2\mathcal H_{h_*}(e_{A,u}).
 \tag{GPR29}
\]
For even $h_*$ the same component formula defines $\mathcal H$,
with wedge multiplication and the indicated bidegrees.
The required explicit triple on $N_{ab}\cap W$ is
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 q_{\rm G}=
 \sum_e\mathcal K_s(e;R_*)-Q_{\rm met}
 -(0,0,Au R_*^{s-1}/2)+\mathcal H_{h_*}(e_{A,u}).
 \quad}
 \tag{GPR30}
\]
Indeed the full edge formula and (GPR24) give
$\mathscr G^*=\partial_2\sum_e\mathcal K_s
 +\mathfrak a(A,u;R_*^{s-1})/2$.
The exact identity
\[
 \tfrac12\mathfrak a(A,u;R_*^{s-1})
 =e_{A,u}R_*^{s-1}-\partial_2(0,0,AuR_*^{s-1}/2)
 \tag{GPR31}
\]
and (GPR27)--(GPR29) prove
\[
                \mathscr G=\partial_2q_{\rm G}
                                      +e_{A,u}R^{r-2}|_W.
 \tag{GPR32}
\]

For $r=2$, the whole norm calculation is already two-entry and
(GPR21) applies on all of $N_{ab}\cap W$ without a passive cap.
Set $s=1$ and use simply
\[
                   q_{\rm G}=\sum_e(0,0,M_{e,1})-(0,0,Au/2).
 \tag{GPR33}
\]
The MT loop correction is zero in degree one, and (GPR32) holds
with $R^0=1$.  This handles the endpoint without a projective
space of negative dimension or an undefined $h_*$.

\subsection{The triple itself extends through coefficient zeros}

Define
\[
 O_a=\{|a|<1,\ |ab|<1\},\qquad
 O_b=\{|b|<1,\ |ab|<1\}.
 \tag{GPR34}
\]
They contain the zero loci of $a,b$ respectively.  On their union
$u=0$, both diagonal transformations are norm isometries, and
the corresponding unit-edge triples are zero.

On $O_a\cap N_{ab}$, the shears with coefficients $a,a/\delta$
are norm isometries.  Thus $F_b=F_*$ and $F=G_1=G_2=G_3$.
The two remaining native shear pairs have identical three
potentials and identical weights: their middle tuples are
\[
 D_j(b)^{-1}f=(f_i,f_j/b,f_{\rm pas}),\qquad
 D_j(b/\delta)^{-1}g_3=(\delta f_i+a f_j,f_j/b,f_{\rm pas}).
 \tag{GPR35}
\]
On the two displayed rows the latter is obtained from the former
by the matrix
\[
                   \begin{pmatrix}\delta&ab\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{GPR36}
\]
Both it and its inverse have maximum operator norm at most one,
since $|ab|<1$ and $|\delta|=1$.  Hence $M_b=M_{b/\delta}$.
The inverse-edge pairings in (GPR9) are $(1,6),(2,5),(3,4)$,
$(9,10)$, with edges $7,8$ zero.

On $O_b\cap N_{ab}$, the shears with coefficients $b,b/\delta$
are norm isometries.  Thus $F=F_b$ and $F_*=G_1=G_2=G_3$.
The other middle potentials agree because, writing
$X_0=f_i/a$ and $Y_0=f_j$, their two active rows are
\[
               (X_0,abX_0+Y_0),\qquad(\delta X_0,Y_0).
 \tag{GPR37}
\]
Both tuples are norm-isometric to $(X_0,Y_0)$ on this open.
The weights agree because $u=0$.  The inverse-edge pairings are
$(1,2),(5,6),(3,10),(4,9)$, again with edges $7,8$ zero.

For either pairing, a common shift by the same cap leaves every
inverse relation intact.  The exact antisymmetry of the CS edge
triple makes its entire sum zero.  Each MT source sum $f_k$ and
$\Gamma_k$ cancels term by term, so $Q_{\rm met}=0$ as well.
Finally $Au=e_{A,u}=0$ there, and hence both remaining terms of
(GPR30) vanish.  The rank-two formula (GPR33) also vanishes.
Consequently the specified triple, not just its boundary, satisfies
\[
                   q_{\rm G}=0
              \quad\text{on }(O_a\cup O_b)\cap N_{ab}\cap W.
 \tag{GPR38}
\]
Glue it with zero on $(O_a\cup O_b)\cap W$.  These ordinary
opens and $N_{ab}\cap W$ cover $W$, producing a genuine actual
triple on all of $W$.  On the zero domains the native defect is
also literally zero by the same norm identities, while its
extending pair is zero.  Thus (GPR32) holds everywhere.

\begin{theorem}[Original-field relative native Gauss pivot]
For every $r\ge2$, the original data (GPR1)--(GPR5) give an
explicit actual triple $q_{\rm G}\in L_2^{r-1}(W)$ and the actual
closed extending pair $e_{A,u}\in L_1^1(N)$ such that
\[
 \boxed{\qquad
 \mathscr G=\partial_2q_{\rm G}+e_{A,u}R^{r-2}|_W.
 \qquad}
 \tag{GPR39}
\]
The formulas are (GPR22), (GPR25)--(GPR30) for $r\ge3$ and
(GPR33) for $r=2$, with the proved zero gluing (GPR38).
No bound on $a,b$, analytic root, base change, coefficientwise
choice of a source layer, or enlargement of the deleted zero set
is used.  In particular $a=0$, $b=0$, cancellation in $1+ab$,
and zeros of any individual original or intermediate section
are included whenever the stated pivot $1+ab$ remains a unit.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{The original-support actual Green interface}

Retain original global regular CI tuples $s_0,s_1$ of the same
metrized line bundle on smooth projective $X/K$, and set
\[
 Z=[Y_1]-[Y_0],\quad T=|Z|,\quad S=|Y_0|\cup|Y_1|,
 \quad U=X^{\rm an}\setminus T,\quad V=X^{\rm an}\setminus S,
 \quad C_U=S\cap U.
 \tag{GPR40}
\]
Suppose an ordinary $N=N_0\cup N_1\subset U$ contains $C_U$,
and that supplied finite original-$K$ analytic frame paths on
$N_0,N_1$ give the literal full SHT/RSP data
\[
 G_{s_1}-G_{s_0}=\partial_1p_i+E_i\quad\text{on }N_i\cap V,
 \qquad E_i\in B^{s,s}(N_i).
 \tag{GPR41}
\]
Supply a finite sequence of Gauss substitutions (GPR5) on the
whole overlap, with each actual pivot $1+ab$ an analytic unit
there.  If these transform the entire $p_0$ into $p_1$, retaining
literal common prefixes and suffixes, summing (GPR39) gives
\[
 p_1-p_0=\partial_2(A_{01},B_{01},M_{01})+\epsilon_{01}|_{N_0\cap N_1\cap V},
 \quad \epsilon_{01}=R^{r-2}\sum_{\rm substitutions}\pm e_{v(a),v(1+ab)}.
 \tag{GPR42}
\]
Every summand in $\epsilon_{01}$ is actual on the unpunctured
overlap, including coefficient zeros.  This is a finite independent
analytic certificate, not an assumption that overlap primitives
are already exact.  It may also be combined with separately
proved finite relation certificates by adding their full witnesses
and their genuinely extending errors.

For an ordinary smooth partition $\theta_0+\theta_1=1$ subordinate
to $N_0,N_1$, put $\theta=\theta_1$.  Frozen RAG supplies
\[
\begin{split}
 \alpha={}&\theta_0\alpha_0+\theta_1\alpha_1
                     +d'\theta\wedge A_{01}+d''\theta\wedge M_{01},\\
 \beta={}&\theta_0\beta_0+\theta_1\beta_1
                     +d''\theta\wedge B_{01}+d'\theta\wedge M_{01},\\
 E={}&\theta_0E_0+\theta_1E_1
          -d'\theta\wedge\epsilon_{01}'-d''\theta\wedge\epsilon_{01}''.
\end{split}
\tag{GPR43}
\]
All overlap-supported products are actual zero extensions.  The
extending-pair terms are retained, and the two mixed
$D\theta\wedge M_{01}$ terms cancel only in the full
differentiated identity.  Therefore
$G_{s_1}-G_{s_0}=d'\alpha+d''\beta+E$ on $N\cap V$.

Choose the frozen RSP cutoff $\zeta$ only on $U$, equal to one
near $C_U$ with closed $U$-support inside $N$.  Extend
$\zeta\alpha,\zeta\beta$ by zero to $V$ and set
\[
          g_V=G_{s_1}-G_{s_0}-d'\widetilde\alpha-d''\widetilde\beta.
 \tag{GPR44}
\]
It equals $E$ near $C_U$ and hence glues with $E$ to an actual
$g_Z\in B^{r-1,r-1}(U)$ on the original exact complement.
For each actual $\eta\in B_c^{n-r,n-r}(X^{\rm an})$, frozen
support avoidance gives $\operatorname{supp}D\eta\Subset V$.
The two correction products have compact support there, so full
actual Stokes and CI8 prove
\[
                         \int_Ug_Z\wedge D\eta=-\int_Z\eta
                       \quad\text{for every actual compact test}.
 \tag{GPR45}
\]
The original cycle, support, field and absolute value are unchanged.
No growth condition or illicit test $\zeta\eta$ on all of $X$ is
introduced.  This is a two-open criterion with supplied complete
paths and substitutions, not an automatic gluing theorem for
arbitrary elimination covers or arbitrary cycle cancellations.

\paragraph{Actual presentations and topological scope.}
No new form space has been defined.  The pair and triple spaces
are the frozen actual CPT spaces with their own inherited
coefficient-final topologies.  The formulas use finite norm
minima, the actual full differentials, closed curvature powers,
and finite wedge sums on a fixed whole presentation.  All wall
relations, non-product units such as $(T_1,T_2,T_1+T_2)$, and
mixed parameter jets remain in that presentation and its allowed
refinements.  The local unit-substitution argument proves equality
of fixed norm formulas on ordinary opens, not equality merely on
rational points or on a dense probe set.  Moving punctures and
arbitrarily many passive base directions are allowed on the
ordinary domain where the supplied coefficients and pivot are
