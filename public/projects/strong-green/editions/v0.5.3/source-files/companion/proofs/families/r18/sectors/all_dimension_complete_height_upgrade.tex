\section{Complete height domains in every dimension of the product families}
\label{r18gh:section}

The original GF18 and NG18 sources state full complementary
height domains in dimension four and the complete
codimension-two pairing in higher dimensions.  The
new EV18 and OD18 arguments close the remaining local
degrees for the same supplied models.  This section
records that upgrade as a separate result; none of
the earlier source files or their bound reviews is
silently strengthened.

\begin{theorem}[Full local G for every specified product model]
\label{r18gh:local}
Let $n\ge4$ and take exactly the native nonlinear-factor
smoothing hypotheses of NF18, including all factor
smoothness, simple-normal-crossings and $Q$-transversality
conditions, the exact uniformizer-exponent-one equation,
and the smooth geometrically integral generic fiber.
Then every specified geometrically homologically trivial
rational cycle on that generic fiber has strong G in
every codimension, with its exact support, ordinary
actual output and every original compact actual test.
The theorem applies to all new cycles after any
unramified finite valued extension.
The linear hyperplane model is included.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
In dimension four this is the already separately
reviewed full local conclusion of NF18.
For even $n\ge6$, apply EV18's explicit nonlinear-factor
extension, Corollary~\ref{r18ev:nonlinear}.  Its edge
complete intersections and all their specified centers
are odd-dimensional; all their algebraic-cycle degrees
have the proved nonmiddle comparison.  The single
global ample-class argument and the degree induction
therefore close every remaining middle defect and
compatibility gate.
For odd $n\ge5$, apply
Theorem~\ref{r18od:full-G}.  OD18 retains the extra
middle numerical classes on its even-dimensional
edges, proves that their orthogonal complement lies
in the actual specified Gysin kernel, and constructs
geometrically compatible representatives in its
nondegenerate displayed subspace.  Thus it closes
these degrees without a general middle cycle-class
comparison.

These two proved alternatives cover every $n\ge4$.
They both use the frozen obstruction reconstruction,
so the conclusion has precisely the support and
test space stated here.  After an unramified valued
extension the same native exponent-one equations
and all their geometric hypotheses remain valid;
apply the corresponding argument anew to each
specified cycle over the new field.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Full complementary-degree heights on the dense families]
\label{r18gh:global}
Fix $n\ge4$ and either of the following arithmetic
constructions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the hyperplane families of GF18 of degree
$d\ge n+2$;
\item the nonlinear-factor families of NG18 with
a fixed degree profile $d_1,\ldots,d_m$ of positive
top monodromy rank.
\end{enumerate}
Keep their prime $q$, coefficient congruences,
fixed-member Lefschetz opens, spreading procedure,
parameter progressions and finite exclusions exactly
as stated there.  Then every member $X$ has full
local strong G in every codimension at every finite
place.  For every number field $F$ unramified at $q$
and every $1\le r\le n$, PH16 supplies the height
on the complete groups
\[
 \operatorname{CH}^r(X_F)_{\mathbf Q,\mathrm{geom\,hom}}
 \ \times\
 \operatorname{CH}^{n+1-r}(X_F)_{\mathbf Q,\mathrm{geom\,hom}}
                         \longrightarrow\mathbf R.
 \tag{GH18.1}\label{r18gh:all-heights}
\]
In each construction, allowing the direction form
$F_1$ to vary in its fixed lattice and open set
gives the same Zariski dense set of hypersurface
coefficient classes as in GF18 or NG18.  The exact
nonzero top monodromy rank remains fixed as proved
there.  For any preassigned number field $F$, the
construction can choose $q$ unramified in $F$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
At the distinguished prime the defining equation
is exactly the one already verified in GF18 or
NG18: the factor product plus $qQ_a$, with
$\bar Q_a$ equal to the chosen transverse form.
Theorem~\ref{r18gh:local} now supplies all
codimensions on that native model.  At every
other finite place the frozen GP17 good or
ordinary-quadratic theorem already supplies all
codimensions with its permitted arbitrary finite
thickness.  Thus the complete local hypothesis
of PH16 is satisfied in every complementary
Chow degree.

If $F/\mathbf Q$ is unramified at $q$, each
completion above $q$ still has the exponent-one
native model after its residue-field extension.
Apply Theorem~\ref{r18gh:local} there to each
new specified cycle.  At the other places,
reapply the same frozen good or ordinary-quadratic
theorem over the new local field.  All finite
auxiliary descents in that frozen theorem keep
the complete field degree and the original
chosen absolute value.  No assertion that a
new cycle is pulled back from the earlier
field is needed.  PH16 now gives
\eqref{r18gh:all-heights}, and GA17 supplies
admissible model lifts on this established
complete domain.

Nothing in this argument changes a coefficient
condition, a spreading set or a parameter
progression.  In particular the valid density
proofs in GF18 and NG18 apply verbatim to the
same members: for each permissible $F_1$,
infinitely many allowed integer parameters
force a polynomial vanishing on the union
to vanish on that entire pencil, and then
on the dense translate of the direction
lattice.  The top monodromy calculation is
also unchanged.  Finally, the original prime
selection can avoid the discriminant of any
preassigned number field.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Precisely what has and has not been upgraded.}
The new conclusion is the full pair of complementary
homologically trivial Chow groups in every degree,
for every dimension of these specific families.
It is stronger than the earlier higher-dimensional
codimension-two-only assertion because EV18 and
OD18 provide new local proofs in the remaining
degrees.  The coefficient families are dense;
they are not all hypersurfaces.  The extension
range in this theorem remains fields unramified
at the distinguished prime.  No separate candidate
ramified-base-change argument is a dependency.
The general conjecture G and the full
unconditional height domain for arbitrary
smooth projective varieties remain open.

\paragraph{Dependency binding.}
The local upgrade depends on the completed
nonlinear-factor version of EV18 and on OD18,
each with its independent review, as well as
NF18 and the frozen reconstruction.  The
global step uses the already bound GF18/NG18
coefficient and placewise arguments and
PH16/GA17.  This statement is to be frozen
only after the final nonlinear EV18 source
and its review are bound; the original GF18
and NG18 scopes remain accurately recorded
by their own unchanged files.
