About the project

Mathematics with a visible history.

A research programme on strong non-Archimedean Green forms and Beilinson–Bloch height pairings.

Mathematical status

Permanent records link each assertion to its statement, hypotheses, argument and declared dependencies.

LabelMeaning
OpenAn unresolved conjecture or research question.
Draft · audit pendingA proposed construction or argument whose indicated interfaces require review.
ConditionalA deduction under its stated hypotheses. Each dependency retains its own status.
Internal proof availableAn argument is supplied in the linked research manuscript. This label does not mean journal peer review or formal verification.
Review recordedA recorded review matched to exact statement, source and dependency versions. Publication alone never supplies this label.

Research and review

The manuscripts and records were developed with AI-assisted research and editing. Source versions and proof dependencies are preserved. Automated checks test document consistency and links; mathematical validity requires examination of the argument.

The Chow obstruction and prescribed Green-pair theorem has a completed internal proof and source-bound AI cross-review of its assembly and analytic supplements. This closes the precise construction asserted in Hypothesis 2.2. General vanishing on homologically trivial cycles, the broader height-theory interfaces, external peer review and formal verification retain their separate status.

One feedback system

This project uses the same GitHub Issues board as the rest of Conjecture Atlas. General comments, mathematical questions, corrections, references and source uploads are welcome. A record or version is useful for a precise correction but is not required for general feedback.

Issues, comments, GitHub account names and attachments are public. Do not submit confidential material. Posting an Issue does not edit a theorem or publish a proof.

Revisions and historical editions

A proposed correction is reviewed against its exact source and downstream dependencies. Accepted changes are linked to a reviewable repository change and a new edition. Permanent record IDs are retained; earlier public editions remain available at fixed URLs. Privacy removals are identified separately from mathematical corrections.
