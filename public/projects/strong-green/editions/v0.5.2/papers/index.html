Sources and mathematical status

The Chow obstruction and prescribed Green-pair theorem establishes Hypothesis 2.2. The geometric transfer and global height papers retain their additional analytic hypotheses, and the arithmetic papers retain their height-system hypotheses. Internal proof, open conjecture, external peer review and formal verification remain distinct.
