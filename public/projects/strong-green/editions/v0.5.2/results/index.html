Established local construction and remaining hypotheses

The Chow-invariant actual obstruction, its genuine pullbacks and the prescribed curved Green pair are established by the new construction theorem, after internal source-bound AI cross-review. The theorem includes smooth integral varieties that are not geometrically integral, every positive codimension up to the dimension, the exact support complement and all compact actual tests. Geometric deductions and global heights still use their stated rigidity, numerical, broader trace, Chow-correspondence, intersection and reciprocity hypotheses. 1. Pairings on cycle groups

The general construction first gives a pairing on the common kernel of the local obstruction maps. Algebraic equivalence and the divisor, zero-cycle and one-cycle calculations then identify subgroups on which it is defined.

A height pairing when the local obstruction vanishes

Under the Green-form and reciprocity hypotheses, the sum of local Green integrals is independent of representatives and Green forms. It defines a rational-bilinear pairing, symmetric in the complementary factors and invariant under finite extension of the number field.

Proof of the result

At each finite place, subtract a global primitive from a Green form to remove its curvature. The residue identity gives the local transformation law under rational equivalence. Almost all local terms vanish, and the number-field product formula makes their weighted sum descend to Chow classes. The archimedean terms use the classical complex Green construction.

Hypotheses used

  • The supported Green-form construction and the local point-residue identity

  • Stokes, finite trace, good-reduction vanishing and the principal transformation law

  • Correspondence adjunction and arithmetic-model comparison for the additional formulas below

What remains to be proved

For a general variety, the unresolved extension is to prove that every localized global homologically trivial class has zero obstruction in each complementary degree. The finite-period description of this kernel additionally requires the specified comparison theorem.

Proof of the result

Express an algebraically trivial cycle through a family parametrized by a smooth projective curve. The Poincaré bundle makes the obstruction an additive, locally constant map from a compact p-adic abelian group to a real vector space. Its image is finite and torsion-free, hence zero. Alternatively, transfer the vanishing for degree-zero divisors on the parameter curve by its Chow correspondence. Descent along the finite field extension returns the result to the original field.

Hypotheses used

  • The global pairing on the common local kernel

What remains to be proved

To replace the second factor by its entire homologically trivial Chow group, one must establish local obstruction vanishing in that complementary degree.

2. Chow decompositions and geometric applications

If a rational Chow decomposition expresses a cycle as a sum of correspondence images of classes with vanishing obstruction, the obstruction of the original cycle vanishes. Applying this in both complementary degrees gives pairings on the entire homologically trivial groups.

3. Model criteria and arithmetic families

A cycle extension with zero intersection against the relevant vertical cycles has vanishing local obstruction, under the numerical comparison. Applying the criterion at every finite place in both complementary degrees gives a global height pairing.

Comparison identifies the constructed pairing with established height pairings or arithmetic intersection numbers on their common domains. Positivity, nondegeneracy and finiteness are additional questions about a pairing that has already been defined.
