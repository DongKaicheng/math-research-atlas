A research programme in arithmetic geometry

Beilinson–Bloch Heights and the Strong Zero-Curvature Green Conjecture

For degree-zero divisors on a curve, height pairings connect intersection theory with the arithmetic of its Jacobian. The goal here is a canonical Beilinson–Bloch height pairing for homologically trivial cycles of complementary codimension on smooth projective varieties over number fields.

Theorem 7.5 · PH16 · proved conditional implication

Strong Green forms give Beilinson–Bloch heights

Within the stated analytic foundations, the principal-push theorem proves that Strong G at every finite completion, in both complementary codimensions, gives a canonical real Beilinson–Bloch height pairing on the full geometrically homologically trivial rational Chow groups.

The proof establishes independence of Green choices, reciprocity, finiteness of the sum over places, and descent through rational equivalence in both variables. Thus solving the general strong Green conjecture supplies the local existence input for this full height construction.

General Strong G remains open. Classical comparisons, positivity, nondegeneracy and arithmetic finiteness retain their own hypotheses and research questions. The analytic foundations retain their stated mathematical review status.

Chow obstructions and prescribed Green pairs

The Chow obstruction and prescribed Green-pair theorem establishes the local construction over the original finite extension of the p-adic numbers. For every smooth integral projective variety, every positive codimension up to its dimension, and every prescribed rational cycle, it constructs a Chow-invariant actual Bott–Chern class and an actual Green form with that curvature on the exact support complement. The variety need not be geometrically integral, and the cycle need not be homologically trivial.
