\section{Complete height families from supplied positive projective hosts}
\label{r22phs:section}

\begin{theorem}[Supplied positive-host arithmetic construction, PSH22]
\label{r22phs:theorem}
Let $F_0$ be a number field and
$\mathcal M/\mathcal O_{F_0}$ a supplied smooth
projective geometrically integral scheme of
relative dimension four.  Choose an odd
rational prime $p$ unramified in $F_0$.
Assume that, for every $v\mid p$ and every
finite extension $k'/k(v)$, the whole native
numerical middle group of the special fiber
satisfies
\[
 b^-\bigl(N^2(M_{k'})_{\mathbf R},\deg(xy)\bigr)
                            =\rho(M_{k'})-1.
 \tag{PSH22.1}\label{r22phs:positivity}
\]
No such signature hypothesis is imposed at
the other finite primes.

Choose a sufficiently positive $\mathcal H$
by Lemma~\ref{r22as:coeff}, and put
$\mathcal L_0=\mathcal H\boxtimes\mathcal O(1)$
on $\mathcal M\times\mathbf P^1$ and
$c=\deg(\mathcal H^4)>0$.
There is one fixed finite extension $F/F_0$,
unramified above $p$, and a Zariski-dense
family of smooth geometrically integral
fourfolds in $|\mathcal L_0^5|$ over $F$
such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item after every finite $F'/F$, all new
specified geometrically homologically trivial
rational cycles in every codimension on every
finite completion satisfy full strong G;
\item PH16 and GA17 give the complete
complementary geometrically homologically
trivial Chow height pairings and their
admissible regular-model lifts;
\item at every place above $p$,
\[
            \operatorname{rank}N^4=4(5c-4)>0,
                  \qquad N^5=0;
 \tag{PSH22.2}\label{r22phs:rank}
\]
the minimum strict semistable dominance depth
over all finite valued extensions and
smooth projective dominant witnesses is
four, with the lower bound over every fixed
finite extension;
\item $b_1(X)=b_1(M)$ and
$b_2(X)=b_2(M)+1$.
\end{enumerate}
The full strong-G assertion has exact original
specified support, every ordinary actual
compact test, full finite field degrees, and
the extending original absolute values.
This is a theorem for the supplied host
and the displayed dense family.  It is
not an existence theorem for good hosts
of arbitrary given varieties.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
We verify which parts of the full AS22
arithmetic construction need an abelian
hypothesis; none does once
\eqref{r22phs:positivity} is supplied.

First, Lemma~\ref{r22as:coeff} was proved
for an arbitrary supplied smooth projective
geometrically integral scheme over a number
ring.  Its very ample high Veronese has
all residue and finite-flat base changes
and a full finite free degree-five
ambient coefficient module.  Apply it
to this $\mathcal M$.  Every intersection
of one, two or three general members
$F_1,\ldots,F_5\in|\mathcal H|$ is smooth
geometrically integral by very ample
Bertini, every fourfold intersection is
finite etale of degree $c$, and the
fivefold intersections are empty.  At
each distinguished residue field make
one finite extension to obtain these
conditions, split all fourfold points,
choose five distinct projective-line
points, and choose $Q\in|\mathcal L_0^5|$
transverse to every stratum and avoiding
the zero-dimensional quintuple strata.
The finite simultaneous Bertini open
is nonempty over the algebraic closure,
and hence these choices exist over a
finite residue extension.

Choose a smooth generic integral
reference section $H_0$ in the full
coefficient module.  Let $S_0$ contain
all rational primes other than $p$
under its bad reductions, including
zero special sections, and include $2$.
At every place over $S_0$ choose a
smooth replacement $G_v$ after a
finite residue extension.  Take one
integer degree divisible by all the
residue degrees just used, and a monic
CRT polynomial with irreducible
separable reductions of that degree
at every selected prime.  Exactly
as in DG22, its number-field quotient
gives one fixed $F/F_0$ realizing the
data and unramified above every
selected place.  In particular the
rational integer $p$ is a uniformizer
at every distinguished place.

The proved section base changes let
CRT lift the five divisor sections,
the five point forms, and all $Q_v$.
Their actual product
$P=\prod_i f_i\prod_j l_j$ is a
section of $\mathcal L_0^5$ of
bidegree $(5,5)$ and has an ordinary
ambient degree-five representative.
With $B=\prod_{q\in S_0}q$ and
$M_0=p^2B$, impose the literal
coefficient congruences
\[
 H_1\equiv P+pQ\pmod{p^2\mathcal O_F},
 \qquad
 H_1\equiv G_v\pmod{\mathfrak p_v}
                             \quad(v\text{ over }S_0).
 \tag{PSH22.3}\label{r22phs:CRT}
\]
Their solution coset is Zariski
dense.  The degree-five ambient
jets supply the nonempty
fixed-reference Lefschetz-pencil
open of frozen CG18/SC18, as
checked in DG22.  The jet statement
depends on a smooth projective
embedding, not on abelian
cohomology, so it applies to this
fivefold.  Choose $H_1$ in the
intersection, spread its pencil,
and let $T_0$ contain the finitely
many exceptional rational primes.
For infinitely many integers $a$,
take
\[
 a\equiv1\pmod{p^2B},\qquad
 a\equiv0\pmod q
          \quad(q\in T_0\setminus(S_0\cup\{p\})),
 \qquad X_a=V(M_0H_0+aH_1),
 \tag{PSH22.4}\label{r22phs:parameters}
\]
discarding the finitely many
singular generic parameters.

At a distinguished place, division
by the unit $a$ gives $P+pQ_a$ with
$\bar Q_a=\bar Q$.  The actual SU22
ordered resolution, in its
positivity-only AS22-local form,
applies.  It gives the whole
$U_2=0$ and full G by PC16.
Hypothesis \eqref{r22phs:positivity}
is explicitly over every finite
constant field, so RI18 applies
to every new cycle after every
finite valued extension.

The remaining finite places are
exhaustive and require no positivity
of the host: above $S_0$ the
reduction is the chosen smooth
$G_v$; at the other exceptional
places it is a nonzero unit
multiple of the smooth $H_0$;
elsewhere the spread pencil has
smooth or one-node reduction.
Characteristic two belongs to
the smooth regime.  Frozen ON17,
Theorem~\textup{r17on:actual} and
Corollary~\textup{r17on:extensions},
apply to an arbitrary integral
flat projective model with
geometrically integral special
fiber and finitely many ordinary
quadratic points in odd residue
characteristic.  They have no
abelian-ambient or projective-space
hypothesis.  These exact frozen
statements were rechecked for
this extension.

For completeness, the needed
integrality check also uses no
abelian input.  Negative ampleness
on $M$ and $\mathbf P^1$ makes
$H^0(\mathcal L_0^{-5})=0$, and
the two terms of
\[
 H^1(M,\mathcal H^{-5})\otimes
                  H^0(\mathbf P^1,\mathcal O(-5))
 \ \oplus\
 H^0(M,\mathcal H^{-5})\otimes
                  H^1(\mathbf P^1,\mathcal O(-5))
 \tag{PSH22.5}\label{r22phs:negative}
\]
are zero.  The divisor sequence
gives connectedness on every
geometric fiber.  Smooth or
isolated-node Cartier fourfolds
are normal, so the corresponding
connected geometric fibers are
integral.  Nonzero special
sections give relative Cartier
flat models, and the smooth
connected generic fiber is
geometrically integral.  The
distinguished model is the
explicit regular integral
ordered resolution instead.

The frozen good-reduction theorem
and the just checked ON17 theorem
therefore apply anew to the
whole new cycle groups at
every nondistinguished place
after every finite extension.
This, together with the
distinguished-place argument,
is the full local input for
PH16 on every finite $F'/F$.
Its archimedean input and GA17
are already closed.  All support,
test, trace-degree and norm
conditions are exactly those
of PC16, RI18 and VT17.

The fixed field and residue
coset precede the choices of
$H_1$.  Infinitely many
parameters on every allowed
line force any polynomial
vanishing on the constructed
family to vanish on each full
line; dense directions then
force it to vanish identically.
Surjection of the full
coefficient module gives the
claimed full-section density.
The varying exceptional sets
$T_0$ do not change this proof.

Finally the actual quadruple
curves and native quintuple
points are exactly the ones
used by SQ22, with $c$ in
place of $1944$.  Their
incidence complex, the
degree-one Gysin transpose,
the whole extreme terms and
the rational harmonic
kernel-to-quotient proof
therefore give
\eqref{r22phs:rank} and its
stated dominance-depth
consequence.  Weak Lefschetz
for the smooth ample divisor
in $M\times\mathbf P^1$
gives the asserted $b_1,b_2$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Supplied projective-bundle hosts]
\label{r22phs:bundles}
The hypothesis \eqref{r22phs:positivity}
holds automatically at every finite
place and after every constant
extension if the supplied global
host is any of the following:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\mathbf P_{\mathcal B}(\mathcal E)$
for a supplied smooth projective
geometrically integral threefold
scheme $\mathcal B/\mathcal O_{F_0}$
and a rank-two vector bundle;
\item a projective rank-three
bundle over a supplied smooth
projective geometrically integral
surface scheme;
\item a projective rank-four
bundle over a supplied smooth
projective geometrically integral
curve scheme;
\item $\mathbf P^4_{\mathcal O_{F_0}}$.
\end{enumerate}
Consequently PSH22 gives complete
height families for all these
supplied hosts.  In the first
case their generic Betti numbers
are $b_1(\mathcal B_{F_0})$ and
$b_2(\mathcal B_{F_0})+2$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
These are exactly the closed
PC16 projective-bundle signature
calculations, Lemma
\textup{r16pos:types}, with its
R7 provenance.  Their actual
Chow projective formula and
native numerical duality use
the full numerical groups
after every constant extension.
They require no standard-D
statement for an arbitrary
threefold base.  The supplied
global schemes and vector
bundles make the hosts smooth
and projective everywhere, so
every hypothesis of PSH22 is
met.  The Betti formulas
follow from the geometric
projective-bundle formula.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A concrete bidegree $(5,5)$ family]
\label{r22phs:P4}
There is a fixed number field
$F/\mathbf Q$, unramified above
$5$, and a Zariski-dense family
of smooth fourfolds of bidegree
$(5,5)$ in
$\mathbf P^4_F\times\mathbf P^1_F$
with full local G at every
finite place after every
finite extension, and complete
PH16 heights in every
complementary codimension.
At every distinguished place
over $5$ they have
\[
              \operatorname{rank}N^4=4,\qquad
              N^5=0,\qquad b_1=0,\quad b_2=2.
 \tag{PSH22.6}\label{r22phs:P4invariants}
\]
Their minimum semistable
dominance depth is four in
the scope of PSH22.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Take $\mathcal M=\mathbf P^4_{\mathbf Z}$,
$\mathcal H=\mathcal O(1)$,
$p=5$.  This $\mathcal H$ already
has every coefficient and
base-change property of
Lemma~\ref{r22as:coeff}; no
additional power is necessary.
Take the five coordinate
hyperplanes as the distinguished
arrangement.  Their positive
intersections are projective
spaces, every fourfold
intersection is one native
coordinate point, and no
fivefold intersection exists.
Thus $c=1$.  The required
five projective-line points
and a simultaneously transverse
$Q$ exist after finite
residue preparation as in
PSH22.  All its arguments
apply with $c=1$, giving
$4(5c-4)=4$.

The Betti numbers follow
from weak Lefschetz and
the projective product.
They distinguish these
generic fibers from the
abelian-host AS22 families
and from projective-space
complete-intersection
fourfolds, whose second
Betti number is one.
The nonzero fourth monodromy
also precludes an ordinary-
node dominance witness of
depth at most three at
the distinguished places.
No separation from every
previous abstract motivic
criterion is claimed.
\end{proof}

extension of the completely
verified AS22 arithmetic
argument; the additional
contribution is the removal
of the abelian restriction
and the explicit projective-
bundle and bidegree examples.
Its positivity remains the
closed PC16 input, whose
exact source and independent
review bridge are recorded
in AS22.  The ON17 source
is the frozen
\path{research_input/round17/root/ordinary_quadratic_models_and_exact_normal_forms.tex}.
No assertion gives a good
host for every generic
variety; ML17 is unchanged.
General G and arbitrary-
variety complete heights
remain open.
