\section{Reduced conormal words with two-pivot three-row factors}
\label{sec:r12-two-pivot-conormal-word}

\paragraph{Status and attribution.}
\textbf{PROVED THIS ROUND, submitted for independent exact-source review.}
The constructor of this corollary is
\texttt{/root/r11\_challenge}.  Its new input is the exact TPG
three-row relative pair and the coefficient-uniform literal-germ
theorem UTG.  The conormal algebra, the small final norm-isometry,
and the actual locally finite germ-gluing method are frozen CBW
and GIC inputs, not counted as new constructions here.

The conclusion concerns original regular CI differences satisfying
an independently supplied finite coefficient certificate.  No
factorization of a general matrix and no primitive of the full
three-pivot Gaussian defect are asserted.  In particular, this is
not a theorem for arbitrary rank-three same-ideal differences.

\subsection{The coefficient certificate on a reduced scheme}

Keep the original finite extension $K/\mathbf Q_p$ and its original
absolute value.  Let $X/K$ be smooth, projective and geometrically
integral of dimension $n$, and let $n\ge r\ge3$.  Let $s_0,s_1$ be
regular rank-$r$ tuples of global sections of the same actually
metrized line bundle $\overline L$ allowed by frozen CI.  Put
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 Y_i=V(s_i),\qquad Z=[Y_1]-[Y_0],\qquad
 U=X^{\rm an}\setminus|Z|,\\
 W=X^{\rm an}\setminus(|Y_0|\cup|Y_1|),\qquad
 C_U=(|Y_0|\cup|Y_1|)\cap U.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{C3W1}
\]
Suppose a Zariski open $V\subset X$ is supplied with
\[
 C_U\subset V^{\rm an},\qquad
 \mathcal I_{Y_0}|_V=\mathcal I_{Y_1}|_V=:\mathcal I.
 \tag{C3W2}
\]
The open $V$ is not required to be affine.  Write
$Y=V(\mathcal I)$ and $Y_{\rm red}=V(\sqrt{\mathcal I})$ inside
$V$.  The regular-sequence calculation in ICC and CBW gives the
canonical conormal comparison
\[
 \overline M\in\operatorname{GL}_r\Gamma(Y,\mathcal O_Y),
 \qquad
 \overline M_{\rm red}\in\operatorname{GL}_r A,
 \quad A=\Gamma(Y_{\rm red},\mathcal O_{Y_{\rm red}}).
 \tag{C3W3}
\]
Both conormal frames have the same scalar line-bundle twist, which
cancels in their comparison.  Thus (C3W3) does not require a
trivialization of $L$ on $V$.  The reduction is used only for a
coefficient certificate; the schemes $Y_i$ and their original
cycle multiplicities are not replaced by reductions.

Supply one finite matrix word, executed from right to left,
\[
              \overline M_{\rm red}
                    =\overline F_m\cdots\overline F_1
                                      \quad\hbox{over }A.
 \tag{C3W4}
\]
Each factor is one of the following, with its row labels and
all its coefficient data fixed:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a shear $E_{ij}(b)$, where $b\in A$;
\item a one-entry unit multiplication $D_i(\lambda)$, where
      $\lambda\in A^\times$;
\item an invertible two-row block, with determinant in $A^\times$;
\item an invertible three-row block, the identity on all other
      rows, with determinant in $A^\times$ and two specified
      first-column entries $\overline a,\overline d$ satisfying
\[
    C_U\subset
       D_{Y_{\rm red}}(\overline a)^{\rm an}
          \cup D_{Y_{\rm red}}(\overline d)^{\rm an}.
 \tag{C3W5}
\]
\end{enumerate}
In (C3W5), $C_U$ is identified with its subset of
$Y_{\rm red}^{\rm an}$; the two zero schemes have the same support
on $V$.  Requiring the two principal opens to cover all of
$Y_{\rm red}$ is a stronger purely algebraic sufficient condition.
Only the displayed condition on $C_U$ is used below.
For a three-row factor, fix an ordering of its three rows and use
that ordering to relabel the entire TPG construction.  This is a
definition of one prescribed program, not an equivariance claim
about arbitrary independently chosen primitives.

The inverses of every unit and block determinant are part of the
finite coefficient data.  Equations (C3W4)--(C3W5) are independent
of the desired Green form: they ask for a finite identity of
matrices over a specified ring and a specified two-open covering.
No actual-form calculus is imposed on the possibly singular
scheme $Y_{\rm red}$.  Coefficients are evaluated at its analytic
points only to test vanishing and invertibility.

\begin{theorem}[Original-support Green form from a certified conormal word]
Under (C3W1)--(C3W5), there is a constructed actual form
\[
                         g_Z\in B^{r-1,r-1}(U)
 \tag{C3W6}
\]
over the original $K$ satisfying
\[
 \int_U g_Z\wedge d'd''\eta=-\int_Z\eta
       \qquad\hbox{for every actual }
       \eta\in B_c^{n-r,n-r}(X^{\rm an}).
 \tag{C3W7}
\]
The canceled part $C_U$ need not be closed in $X^{\rm an}$ or
separated there from the remaining support.  The local lifts of
the word need not agree as analytic matrices away from the reduced
common zero set.  No global analytic lift or affine common
neighborhood is required.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Executing local lifts without changing the original cycle}

\begin{proof}
Lift all coefficients of the finite word locally through the
surjection of sheaves
$\mathcal O_V\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal O_{Y_{\rm red}}$.
There are simultaneous lifts on an affine cover near $Y$; one
does not use surjectivity on the global sections of the possibly
nonaffine $V$.  Invert the lifted unit and determinant functions
on ordinary analytic neighborhoods of $C_U$.  For every
three-row factor also restrict to the union of its two specified
nonzero pivot opens.  By (C3W5) these restrictions do not remove
any point of $C_U$.  Since the word is finite, all restrictions
can be made simultaneously.  Denote the local factors by
$F_{i,j}$ and their product by
\[
                         T_i=F_{i,m}\cdots F_{i,1}.
 \tag{C3W8}
\]

Independently choose a local exact comparison $s_1=M_i s_0$;
it exists by (C3W2).  Regularity gives uniqueness of its reduction
modulo $\mathcal I$ and invertibility along $Y$, by the precise
ICC regular-sequence relation calculation.  Hence $M_i$ and
$T_i$ have the same reduction over $Y_{\rm red}$, namely
$\overline M_{\rm red}$ in (C3W3).  In particular
\[
       H_i=M_iT_i^{-1}=I_r+E_i^{\rm mat},\qquad
                  E_i^{\rm mat}|_{Y_{\rm red}}=0.
 \tag{C3W9}
\]
Shrink the ordinary neighborhoods $N_i\subset U$ around $C_U$
so that every entry of $E_i^{\rm mat}$ has norm less than one.
This is possible at every analytic point of $C_U$, including
nonrational points.  In each completed residue field both
$H_i$ and its inverse preserve the sup norm.  Consequently
\[
                 G_{s_1}=G_{T_i s_0}
                           \quad\hbox{on }N_i\cap W.
 \tag{C3W10}
\]
Here only the complete fixed CI expression is compared, using
its dependence on the tuple norm and the common metric.  No
norm-isometry invariance of an arbitrary primitive is assumed.

Execute (C3W8) at its actual intermediate tuples.  Use the frozen
SHT primitive for a shear, the frozen unit primitive for a diagonal
unit, the canonical GB pair for a two-row block, and the full
TPG7 pair and residue for a three-row block.  Fix all cap offsets
and all inner and outer smooth partition functions once for each
position in the word, and use these same choices on every local
lift.  In particular, each three-row call retains all four terms
of its G3F face, its strict EFC crossing, and its full differentiated
inner and outer partitions.  The local telescoping sum and
(C3W10) give
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 p_i=(p_i',p_i'')\in
 B^{r-2,r-1}(N_i\cap W)\oplus B^{r-1,r-2}(N_i\cap W),\\
 E_i\in B^{r-1,r-1}(N_i),\qquad
 G_{s_1}-G_{s_0}=\partial_1p_i+E_i|_W.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{C3W11}
\]
The differential form $E_i$ is not the small matrix error in
(C3W9).  All intermediate matrices are invertible, so the
punctured domain stays exactly $N_i\cap W$ at every word step.

\subsection{Literal primitive germs, not just equal boundaries}

For two local lifts and $x\in C_U\cap N_i\cap N_j$, corresponding
coefficient values agree in $\mathscr H(x)$ because their
restrictions to $Y_{\rm red}$ agree.  So do the prefix matrices
preceding each word factor.  UTG14 and its finite-word consequence,
together with GIC for the old factor types, therefore give one
ordinary neighborhood of $x$ on whose punctured part
\[
                              p_i=p_j.
 \tag{C3W12}
\]
This is literal equality of the full actual pairs, including all
mixed coefficients.  UTG proves it for the integrated EFC terms
by a coefficient-only tail bound, independent of the direction
in which $s_0$ approaches zero, followed by a uniform comparison
of the entire product presentation on a bounded parameter slab.
The positive floor of every capped subtuple norm is included.
Thus (C3W12) does not follow merely from agreement of endpoint
norms, equal second boundaries, or pointwise limits on $C_U$.

Let $N=\bigcup_iN_i$.  Choose an ordinary smooth locally finite
partition $(\rho_i)$ on $N$, with each support closed in $N$
and contained in its indicated domain.  With closed-support zero
extensions, set on $N\cap W$
\[
 p=\sum_i\rho_i p_i,\qquad
 H=\sum_i\bigl(d'\rho_i\wedge p_i'
                       +d''\rho_i\wedge p_i''\bigr).
 \tag{C3W13}
\]
Near $x\in C_U$ only finitely many supports meet.  Shrink to avoid
the closed supports not containing $x$, and then intersect the
finitely many neighborhoods from (C3W12) for the remaining
indices.  Their pairs are one common pair on the punctured part
of this neighborhood.  Since $\sum_i d'\rho_i=\sum_i d''\rho_i=0$,
the entire form $H$ is zero there.  Therefore $H$ has a genuine
actual zero extension $\widetilde H$ to $N$.  Define
\[
                    E=\sum_i\rho_iE_i-\widetilde H.
 \tag{C3W14}
\]
The complete Leibniz calculation in the actual sheaf gives
\[
                G_{s_1}-G_{s_0}=d'p'+d''p''+E|_{N\cap W}.
 \tag{C3W15}
\]
All sums are locally finite in ordinary neighborhoods and finite
on a fixed compact set.  No completion or quotient-class
representative is substituted for an actual form.

\subsection{Original-support cutoff and all actual compact tests}

Apply the exact RSP cutoff on $U$: choose an ordinary smooth
actual $\zeta$ with support closed in $U$ and contained in $N$,
equal to one on a neighborhood of $C_U$.  On $W$ define
\[
 g_W=G_{s_1}-G_{s_0}
              -d'\operatorname{ext}_0(\zeta p')
              -d''\operatorname{ext}_0(\zeta p'').
 \tag{C3W16}
\]
Equation (C3W15) makes this equal to $E$ near $C_U$.  Actual
sheaf gluing therefore constructs (C3W6) on precisely the
original complement $U$.

For a test $\eta$ as in (C3W7), the frozen support lemma places
$D\eta$ compactly inside $W$.  It is the products of the two
correction forms with $D\eta$, not necessarily the correction
forms themselves, that have compact support.  Since
$d'D\eta=d''D\eta=0$, full actual Stokes makes both correction
pairings in (C3W16) vanish.  The two original actual CI formulas
have identical curvature; subtracting them proves (C3W7).
Neither the cycle nor its support, multiplicities, metric, field
or absolute value has been changed.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Consequences and exact limits}

For $r=3$, one may take the word to be the canonical conormal
matrix itself if two specified entries in its first column
satisfy (C3W5).  It then gives an original-support actual Green
form even without a global analytic lift and even when the
canceled part accumulates on the remaining support.  This is
an additional checkable two-pivot hypothesis, not an automatic
property of an arbitrary conormal matrix.  Changing a constant
frame is not assumed to make that hypothesis hold.

In any rank, a supplied word can mix old two-row factors with
these new certified three-row factors, acting on different
selected rows.  This remains a theorem about one fixed word.
The construction compares its congruent local lifts, not different
words or different three-pivot programs.  It requires neither a
literal shared tail of the original tuples nor a global section
surjectivity assertion on $V$.

Nonreduced common schemes are allowed: the reduced scheme is
used only for coefficient lifting and norm-small differences.
Its finite constant fields, when present, need not equal $K$.
All lifts are nevertheless original-$K$ analytic coefficients;
no higher actual form is descended from an auxiliary field.
The proof applies at all analytic points, including irrational
Gauss radii, and does not replace the full actual relation space
by product probes.  The nonproduct unit triple
$(T_1,T_2,T_1+T_2)$, moving punctures and additional passive
directions are retained through the exact TPG, UTG and PTH
interfaces.

The relevant object is the supplied finite word with an ordinary
primitive-germ construction.  Its equivalence here is equality
of corresponding coefficient restrictions on $Y_{\rm red}$.
Restrictions, affine unit refinements and subdivisions commute
with the finite native formulas and the restricted product-torus
integration.  On each fixed compact whole presentation the
frozen coefficient-final estimates and support lemma give the
usual continuous integration interface.  No topology on the
set of words, continuous choice of lifts, full-space linear
retraction, or full inherited-kernel comparison is asserted.

The old-word specializations are recoveries of CBW, not counted
as new existence classes.  The full three-pivot native defect
of NFG, general conormal factorization, different-ideal
cancellation, general strong G and the additional unconditional
Beilinson--Bloch height axioms remain \textbf{OPEN}.
