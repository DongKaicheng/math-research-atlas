\subsection{Statement and explicit formulas}

Use the exact GB data and fixed ordinary partitions of TML, with
the same two selected rows and arbitrary $r\ge2$.  Supply an original
analytic unit $\lambda$ on the whole $N$, put $L=v(\lambda)$,
and fix $i\in\{1,2\}$.  Set
\[
 A'=D_i(\lambda)A,\qquad d=r-2,
 \qquad \theta=\chi(v\gamma-v\alpha),\quad
       \theta'=\chi(v\gamma'-v\alpha').
 \tag{GMU1}
\]
The function $\chi$ has the same constant tails as in TML; it need
not be symmetric.  Let $p_A,p_{A'}$ be the entire \emph{original}
GB pairs, including their original cap choices.  Then there are
explicit finite actual $q_i\in L_2^{r-1}(W)$ and
$e_i\in L_1^{r-1}(N)$ such that
\[
         p_A+p_{i,\lambda}(Af)-p_{A'}
                            =\partial_2q_i+e_i|_W.
 \tag{GMU2}
\]
No growth assumption on $\lambda$ or $\lambda^{-1}$ is made.

Here are the formulas, whose ordinary zero extensions are proved
below.  On a first-pivot overlap put
\[
 a=v(\alpha),\quad C=v(\gamma),\quad
 a'=v(\alpha'),\quad C'=v(\gamma'),\quad
 \epsilon_A=e_{C,a-C},\quad
 \epsilon_{A'}=e_{C',a'-C'},
 \tag{GMU3}
\]
where $e_{x,y}=(x\,d''y,y\,d'x)$.  For $i=1$ set
$\epsilon^{\rm first}_1=0$ and $\rho_1=0$.
For $i=2$ set
\[
 \epsilon^{\rm first}_2=-e_{L,a},\qquad
 \rho_2=(0,0,aL R_*^d/2)-H_h(e_{L,a}),\qquad
 h=-\Psi\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}R_*^jR^{d-1-j},\quad R_*=R-D\Psi.
 \tag{GMU4}
\]
All inputs of $H_h$ are independently closed unit-valuation pairs;
external partition coefficients will be inserted afterwards.
The desired extending pair is
\[
 e_i=\bigl(\epsilon^{\rm first}_i
          -(1-\theta)\epsilon_A
          +(1-\theta')\epsilon_{A'}\bigr)R^d.
 \tag{GMU5}
\]

Use one actual common cap $\Psi$ constructed as follows.  In TML5
replace the matrix data by $A,A'$ and include both $|\lambda|$ and
$|\lambda^{-1}|$ in $M$, omitting $|z|$.  Retain
$B=1+\varepsilon+\log M$, $c_0=\max(|\alpha|,|\gamma|)$,
\[
 N_{\rm main}=\{|\alpha|>e^{-4B}c_0\},\qquad
 N_{\rm small}=\{|\alpha|<e^{-3B}c_0\}.
 \tag{GMU6}
\]
On the first region $|\alpha|^{-1}<e^{5B}$ and
$|\alpha'|^{-1}<e^{6B}$.  Wherever the corresponding second-chart
weight or partition derivative is active, its second-pivot inverse
is bounded by $e^{9B}$, using the same $2\varepsilon$ ordinary
buffer.  On the second region $c_0=|\gamma|$,
$\theta=\theta'=0$, and the second pivots and their inverses have
norm at most $e^{2B}$.  The unit $\lambda$ and its inverse have
bound $e^B$.  Apply exactly the finite exponent recipe TML6--TML8
to the nine GB calls for $A,A'$, the first-pivot substitutions
(GMU10)--(GMU11), and the two second-pivot substitution lists
following (GMU12) and (GMU14).  Replace the pivot bound $7$ by $9$ and
include the extra unit bounds just stated.  The remaining
coefficients and inverses are controlled by the same whole-call
zero neighborhoods of MX/NSC/SAR.  This supplies one finite integer
$C_i$ and the actual common cap
\[
       \Psi=\min(\psi,\Phi_f+(C_i+1)B)
 \tag{GMU7}
\]
on $W$, admissible on the respective ordinary regions for every
retained call.  The whole finite recipe, not a coefficient-by-
coefficient choice, is used.  For $r=2$ omit the cap and $H_h$;
every exponent $R_*^d$ is then $1$.

With $\mathcal J=\mathcal J_\Psi$ denoting TCT1, put
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \sigma_A=(0,0,C(C-a)R_*^d/2)+H_h(\epsilon_A),\\
 \sigma_{A'}=(0,0,C'(C'-a')R_*^d/2)+H_h(\epsilon_{A'}),\\
 \begin{split}
 \widetilde q_i={}&\mathcal J(D_i(\lambda);Af)
       +\theta\mathcal J(w_\alpha;f)
       +(1-\theta)\mathcal J(w_\gamma;f)\\
 &-\theta'\mathcal J(w'_{\alpha'};f)
       -(1-\theta')\mathcal J(w'_{\gamma'};f)
       +\rho_i-(1-\theta)\sigma_A+(1-\theta')\sigma_{A'}.
 \end{split}
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{GMU8}
\]
Finally form the explicit TCT quadruples on ordinary neighborhoods
of the closed supports of the partition derivatives and set
\[
       q_i=\widetilde q_i
              +K_3(\theta)\mathcal H_A
              -K_3(\theta')\mathcal H_{A'}.
 \tag{GMU9}
\]
They are the same full CT formulas, including the mixed
$-d''J$ term, as in TML19--TML20.  These derivative-supported
products have their closed-support zero extensions; no singular
unweighted overlap form is extended by zero.

\subsection{First-pivot word comparisons and the second-row gauge}

Write $t=\gamma/\alpha$, $u=\beta/\alpha$ and
$h_0=\Delta/\alpha$, so that
$w_\alpha=E_{21}(t)D_1(\alpha)D_2(h_0)E_{12}(u)$.
For $i=1$ the successive exact substitutions are
\[
 D_1(\lambda)E_{21}(t)
      \rightsquigarrow E_{21}(t/\lambda)D_1(\lambda),
 \qquad
 D_1(\lambda)D_1(\alpha)\rightsquigarrow D_1(\lambda\alpha).
 \tag{GMU10}
\]
They are NSC and SL, both strict.  Their full common-cap triples
sum to the companion of the closed word, by SAN/TCT and literal
edge linearity.  Thus there is no first-chart base correction.

For $i=2$ the successive substitutions are
\[
 \begin{split}
 D_2(\lambda)E_{21}(t)&\rightsquigarrow
                      E_{21}(\lambda t)D_2(\lambda),\\
 D_2(\lambda)D_1(\alpha)&\rightsquigarrow
                      D_1(\alpha)D_2(\lambda),\\
 D_2(\lambda)D_2(h_0)&\rightsquigarrow D_2(\lambda h_0).
 \end{split}
 \tag{GMU11}
\]
These are MX, the positive CS square of temporal weights $a,L$,
and SL.  The standard CS formula gives the companion correction
$-(0,0,aL R_*^d/2)+H_h(e_{a,L})$ and the error $e_{a,L}R^d$.
We use instead precisely (GMU4).  This change is legitimate as a
full actual formula: since
\[
 e_{a,L}+e_{L,a}=\partial_2(0,0,aL),\qquad
 Dh=R_*^d-R^d,\qquad
 \partial_2H_h(e_{x,y})=Dh\,e_{x,y},
\]
the differential of the difference of the two triples equals
$(e_{a,L}+e_{L,a})R^d$.  Hence the new error is
$-e_{L,a}R^d$, exactly as claimed.  No cancellation of a curvature
factor and no separately closed low-degree block of a path is
used here.  All displayed $e$ pairs are independently closed
before this calculation.

Insert these two first-word identities into TML15 for $A,A'$.
The same literal companion cancellation gives (GMU8) and (GMU5)
on all of $N_{\rm main}$.  In particular the entire derivatives
of the two distinct partitions remain in the product formulas.

\subsection{Direct identities on the zero-first-pivot neighborhood}

On $N_{\rm small}$ the two partitions are identically zero.
For $i=1$ one has $a'=a+L$, $C'=C$.  Therefore, as entire
actual expressions on the punctured pivot overlap,
\[
 \begin{split}
 \epsilon_1^{\rm first}-\epsilon_A+\epsilon_{A'}&=e_{C,L},\\
 \rho_1-\sigma_A+\sigma_{A'}
               &=-(0,0,CL R_*^d/2)+H_h(e_{C,L}).
 \end{split}
 \tag{GMU12}
\]
The right sides are defined on the whole $N_{\rm small}$ because
$\gamma$ and $\lambda$ are units there.  For a direct verification
through $\alpha=0$, move $D_1(\lambda)$ past $J$ by
$D_1(\lambda)J=JD_2(\lambda)$.  In the second Gaussian word use
MX to move $D_2(\lambda)$ past $E_{21}(-\alpha/\gamma)$,
then the positive CS square to move it past $D_1(\gamma)$,
and SL to merge it with $D_2(\Delta/\gamma)$.
The CS weights are $C,L$, so its full companion correction and
error are exactly (GMU12).  This proves (GMU2) on this whole
ordinary neighborhood, including zero shear coefficients.

For $i=2$ one has $a'=a$, $C'=C+L$.  Set
\[
                        k=CL+L^2/2,
                         M_k=(0,0,k).
 \tag{GMU13}
\]
Full bilinearity, with the mixed terms retained, gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 \epsilon_2^{\rm first}-\epsilon_A+\epsilon_{A'}
       &=-e_{L,C}-e_{C,L}-e_{L,L}=-\partial_2M_k,\\
 \rho_2-\sigma_A+\sigma_{A'}
       &=M_kR_*^d-H_h(\partial_2M_k).
 \end{split}
 \tag{GMU14}
\]
The logarithm $a$ has disappeared from both formulas.  These
are therefore actual extensions to $N_{\rm small}$, not just
vanishing singular parts in a quotient.

The second-pivot matrix comparison is now strict: move
$D_2(\lambda)J=JD_1(\lambda)$, then use NSC to move
$D_1(\lambda)$ past $E_{21}(-\alpha/\gamma)$, and SL to
merge it with $D_1(\gamma)$.  The other factors already agree
with the second word for $A'$, since
$\Delta'/\gamma'=\Delta/\gamma$ and
$\delta'/\gamma'=\delta/\gamma$.
The whole companion therefore has boundary the second-word pair
discrepancy, with no error.  On the other hand
\[
 \partial_2\bigl(M_kR_*^d-H_h(\partial_2M_k)\bigr)
                    =(\partial_2M_k)R^d.
 \tag{GMU15}
\]
The negative error in (GMU14) cancels this differential exactly.
This directly verifies (GMU2) on $N_{\rm small}$.

All the weighted companions in (GMU8) have closed-support zero
extensions at their discarded pivots.  Their whole native shear
contributions extend through zero coefficients by inverse pairing.
Equations (GMU12) and (GMU14) extend the remaining combined
terms and the errors.  Thus $\widetilde q_i$ is actual on $W$
and $e_i$ is actual on $N$.  The two ordinary regions cover $N$;
there is no passage from a dense unit locus.  Finally (GMU9)
restores the precise old GB caps by TCT11, completing the proof.

\paragraph{Finite compositions and the remaining obstruction.}
The relations (GMU2) and TML3 may be inserted at the exact
intermediate matrix and tuple of any supplied finite word of
lower shears and row units in these same two rows.  Their finite
actual triples add, and their errors add as actual pairs on $N$.
This is a literal word-by-word statement and requires no
continuous linear selector.

These relations address multiplication of a coefficient matrix
inside one block.  They do not supply a comparison under a shear
of an \emph{external} row of the input tuple.  In a three-pivot
Schur calculation that external-row input change is a separate
full native relation, whose passive block changes.  Neither
TCT nor the present theorem identifies its higher defect with
the fixed AGC commuting-diagonal cube.  An original-$U$ Green
application still requires all of the finite transition data
of RAG/RSP; no new class of arbitrary cycles is asserted here.
