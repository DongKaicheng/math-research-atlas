\section{The actual cubic unit residue in every rank}
\label{sec:r11-cubic-unit-residue}

\paragraph{Status.}
This module is constructed by \texttt{/root/r11\_cube} and is submitted
for independent exact-source review.  It identifies the full raw CS
cube on an explicitly specified ordinary domination open, and constructs
its actual quadruple and extending triple there.  It also gives an
all-rank counterexample to a \emph{strict} third witness for that raw
cube.  The general cube modulo an extending triple is not solved, and
neither the domination hypothesis nor a general finite-cover certificate
is asserted near a regular complete-intersection cancellation locus.

\subsection{A full two-colour bilinear homotopy}

Use the exact actual complexes CPT1 and QH1.  For an even diagonal
actual form $h$ and an independently closed pair $p=(p',p'')$, write
\[
 \mathcal H_h(p)=(d''h\wedge p',-d'h\wedge p'',h\wedge d'p').
 \tag{CU1}
\]
Let $w=(\sigma,\tau)\in L_1^k$ and $p\in L_1^a$ satisfy
$\partial_1p=0$.  Define the actual quadruple
\[
 \mathcal J(w,p)=
 (-d''\sigma\wedge p',\ d'\tau\wedge p'',\
                  \sigma\wedge d'p',\ \tau\wedge d'p')
                                      \in L_3^{a+k+1}.
 \tag{CU2}
\]
Then the exact finite identity is
\[
       \partial_3\mathcal J(w,p)=\mathcal H_{\partial_1w}(p).
 \tag{CU3}
\]
In particular no separate closedness of $\sigma$ or $\tau$ is required.

\begin{proof}
Closedness of $p$ gives $d''p''=-d'p'$ and $Dp'=0$.  Since
$\sigma,\tau$ have odd total degree, full differentiation of the
first entry of $\partial_3\mathcal J$ gives
\[
 -D\sigma\wedge p'-d''\sigma\wedge d'p'
          +d''\sigma\wedge d'p'-\sigma\wedge d''d'p'
                  =-D\sigma\wedge p'.
 \tag{CU4}
\]
The second is
\[
 -D\tau\wedge p''+d'\tau\wedge(d''p''+d'p')
                  =-D\tau\wedge p''.
 \tag{CU5}
\]
The third is $(d'\sigma+d''\tau)\wedge d'p'$.
These are exactly the three entries of the right side of (CU3),
because $d''\partial_1w=-D\sigma$ and $d'\partial_1w=D\tau$.
All identities are in actual $B$ before refinement.
\end{proof}

For units $\lambda_a,\lambda_b,\lambda_c$ on an ordinary open $N$,
put $a=v(\lambda_a)$, $b=v(\lambda_b)$, $c=v(\lambda_c)$.
The extending pair $e_{ab}=(a\,d''b,b\,d'a)$ is independently
closed on $N$.  Since $Dc=0$, CPT gives
\[
 E_{abc}=\mathcal H_c(e_{ab})
     =(-a\,d''b\wedge d''c,\ b\,d'a\wedge d'c,
                                      c\,d'a\wedge d''b),
       \qquad \partial_2 E_{abc}=0.
 \tag{CU6}
\]
This is a genuine actual extending triple.  In particular its first
entry satisfies
\[
                 d''(E_{abc})_A=-d''a\wedge d''b\wedge d''c.
 \tag{CU7}
\]
Thus allowing extending triples at the cube level is substantive.

\subsection{An exact all-rank raw-cube identity on a domination open}

Let $f=(f_1,\ldots,f_r)$, $r\ge3$, be a tuple of analytic sections
of the same metrized line bundle on $N$.  Put $W=N\setminus|f=0|$,
$s=r-1$ and $\ell=r-3$.  The commuting matrices $A,B,C$ multiply
the first, second, and third entries by $\lambda_a,\lambda_b,
\lambda_c$, respectively.  Fix the same positive real cap constant
$\varepsilon$ in all six raw CS36 triples.  Let $R$ be the original
metric curvature and put
\[
                P=\|(f_3,\ldots,f_r)\|_\infty.
 \tag{CU8}
\]
All norms use that same metric.  Define the ordinary open $\Omega$
inside $W$ by the following two strict inequalities:
\[
 \begin{split}
 \max(\|f_1\|,\|\lambda_a f_1\|)
                   &<e^{-(|b|+|c|+\varepsilon)}P,\\
 \max(\|f_2\|,\|\lambda_b f_2\|)
                   &<e^{-(|a|+|c|+\varepsilon)}P.
 \end{split}
 \tag{CU9}
\]
In particular $P>0$ on $\Omega$.  The exact raw cube is
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal Q={}&q_{a,b}(f)+q_{a,c}(Bf)+q_{b,c}(f)\\
              &-q_{b,c}(Af)-q_{a,c}(f)-q_{a,b}(Cf),
 \end{split}
 \tag{CU10}
\]
where every $q$ means the frozen full CS36 expression, not merely
an unspecified primitive with the same boundary.

Let $f^{\rm pas}=(f_3,\ldots,f_r)$, of rank $k=r-2$, and let
$Cf^{\rm pas}=(\lambda_cf_3,f_4,\ldots,f_r)$.  On $\Omega$ put
\[
 \phi_i=-\log\|C^i f^{\rm pas}\|,\qquad
 C_i=R-D\phi_i,\qquad
 G_i^{\rm pas}=\phi_i\sum_{j=0}^{\ell} C_i^jR^{\ell-j}
                         \quad(i=0,1).
 \tag{CU11}
\]
The use of $C_i$ as a curvature is local to (CU11); the matrix $C$
has already been specified.  These are literal lower-rank CI scalar
expressions on the same original analytic domain, not currents.

\begin{theorem}[Full cubic residue on the specified domination open]
The whole raw cube (CU10), with all six caps and metric terms retained,
satisfies on all of $\Omega$
\[
       \mathcal Q=\mathcal H_{G_1^{\rm pas}-G_0^{\rm pas}}(e_{ab}).
 \tag{CU12}
\]
There is an explicitly specified actual pair
$w\in L_1^\ell(\Omega)$, given below, such that
\[
       G_1^{\rm pas}-G_0^{\rm pas}
                         =\partial_1w+cR^\ell.
 \tag{CU13}
\]
Consequently the actual quadruple and actual extending triple
\[
       T=\mathcal J(w,e_{ab}),\qquad e_2=R^\ell E_{abc}\in L_2^s(N)
 \tag{CU14}
\]
give the full relative identity
\[
                   \mathcal Q|_\Omega=\partial_3T+e_2|_\Omega.
 \tag{CU15}
\]
For $r=3$, take $w=0$ and the equality is
$\mathcal Q=E_{abc}$ on $\Omega$ itself.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The norm of $C f^{\rm pas}$ is at least $e^{-|c|}P$.  Equations
(CU9) therefore make entries $1,2$ strictly inactive in every one
of the eight full tuple norms, even after either is scaled.  The
eight potentials are consequently $\phi_0$ or $\phi_1$, depending
only on whether $C$ has been applied.

We must compare the caps as well, not just those vertex potentials.
For the $a,c$ face, the common-cap norm is
\[
 \max\{\|f_2\|,\|(f_4,\ldots,f_r)\|,
                           e^{-(|a|+|c|+\varepsilon)}P\}.
 \tag{CU16}
\]
For the face based at $Bf$, replace only $\|f_2\|$ by
$\|\lambda_b f_2\|$.  The second inequality in (CU9) makes both
of those entries strictly smaller than the displayed final term.
Thus the two cap scalars are literally identical on the whole
$\Omega$.  All normalized potentials, original metric terms,
CS edge triples, and MT source sums therefore agree, proving
$q_{a,c}(Bf)=q_{a,c}(f)$ as entire actual triples.  The first
inequality proves $q_{b,c}(Af)=q_{b,c}(f)$ in the same manner.
Empty passive subtuples in (CU16) have norm zero; this includes $r=3$.

Only the two $a,b$ faces remain.  On the face based at $C^i f$,
all four full norms equal the corresponding passive norm, and its
CS cap is exactly $\phi_i$.  All normalized vertex potentials are
zero.  Hence the entire CS edge triple sum is zero.  The MT source
sums $f_j$ and $\Gamma_j$ are zero as well: the endpoint differences
are zero, the vertex curvatures are identical, and the unit weights
sum to zero.  The passive projective map takes values in
$\mathbf P^{k-1}=\mathbf P^\ell$, so $C_i^{s-1}=C_i^{\ell+1}=0$.
The scalar correction in CS36 is therefore also zero.  Its remaining
curvature homotopy is
\[
 q_{a,b}(C^i f)=\mathcal H_{h_i}(e_{ab}),\qquad
 h_i=-\phi_i\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}C_i^jR^{\ell-j}=-G_i^{\rm pas}.
 \tag{CU17}
\]
Subtracting proves (CU12), including its sign.

If $r=3$, the passive rank is one, $\phi_1-\phi_0=c$ and
$G_i^{\rm pas}=\phi_i$, giving (CU13) with $w=0$.
For $r\ge4$, apply the exact frozen RAT11--RAT12 construction to
the \emph{actual passive tuple of rank $r-2$} and its one-entry
unit change.  It gives (CU13) with every mixed term retained.
The complete formula for $w$ is specified next.  Finally apply
the independently proved (CU3) and the linearity of $\mathcal H$.
Since $R$ is closed in both colours,
$\mathcal H_{cR^\ell}(e_{ab})=R^\ell\mathcal H_c(e_{ab})$.
This proves (CU14)--(CU15).  Its extending triple is defined on
all of $N$ from the original $R,a,b,c$, regardless of whether the
passive tuple is nonzero outside $\Omega$.
\end{proof}

For clarity, when $\ell\ge1$, set $v=\phi_1-\phi_0$ and
\[
 B_j=-v\sum_{t=0}^{j-1}C_1^tC_0^{j-1-t}\quad(1\le j\le\ell),
 \qquad P_0=\phi_0\sum_{t=0}^{\ell-1}C_0^tR^{\ell-1-t},
 \qquad c_+=\max(c,0).
 \tag{CU18}
\]
The exact pair in (CU13) is
\[
 \begin{split}
 \sigma&=\sum_{j=1}^{\ell}\phi_0\,d''B_j R^{\ell-j}
                       +c_+d''B_\ell-c\,d''P_0,\\
 \tau&=\sum_{j=1}^{\ell}d'\phi_0\wedge B_j R^{\ell-j}
                       +d'c_+\wedge B_\ell-d'c\wedge P_0.
 \end{split}
 \tag{CU19}
\]
The full wall term at $c=0$ is handled by RAT8, not by dropping
an absolute-value derivative.  This displays every actual form
used in $T$.

\subsection{A strict raw-cube obstruction in every rank}

The extending triple in (CU15) cannot in general be deleted.
Here is a proper-fibre actual test proving this for every $r\ge3$.
Let $\ell=r-3$ and take
\[
 B=\{1<v(T_a)<2,\ 1<v(T_b)<2,\ 1<v(T_c)<2\}
                         \subset(\mathbb G_m^3)^{\rm an},
 \qquad N=B\times\mathbf P^\ell_K.
 \tag{CU20}
\]
The inequalities define ordinary analytic opens; Gauss points
give their nonemptiness over the original $K$ even if those real
intervals contain no valuations of elements of $K^\times$.
Let $L$ be the pullback of $\mathcal O(1)$ with its standard
projective metric, and write $z_0,\ldots,z_\ell$ for its homogeneous
sections.  Choose an integer $M$ sufficiently large that
$|\pi|^M<e^{-(4+\varepsilon)}$ for a fixed original-$K$ uniformizer
$\pi$.  Put
\[
 f=(\pi^M z_0,\pi^M z_0,z_0,z_1,\ldots,z_\ell),
 \qquad (\lambda_a,\lambda_b,\lambda_c)=(T_a,T_b,T_c).
 \tag{CU21}
\]
There are $\ell+3=r$ entries.  The passive tuple generates $L$,
so $f$ has no common zero on $N$ and its norm is one.  The two
strict inequalities (CU9) hold on all of $N$: the units have
norm less than one, $|a|+|c|<4$ and $|b|+|c|<4$.  Thus (CU15)
holds globally with $\Omega=W=N$.

Let $R$ be the pullback of the projective standard form and let
$\pi_B:N\to B$ denote the projection.  The original-field calibration
is $\int_{\mathbf P^\ell}R^\ell=1$, also for $\ell=0$.
Choose a nonnegative smooth function
$\chi\in C_c^\infty((1,2)^3)$ with nonzero integral, and set
\[
 \alpha=\chi(a,b,c)\,d'a\wedge d'b\wedge d'c
                                  \in B_c^{3,0}(B).
 \tag{CU22}
\]
The faithful unit-torus presentation gives
\[
 \int_B d''a\wedge d''b\wedge d''c\wedge\alpha\ne0.
 \tag{CU23}
\]
Only nonvanishing matters; orientation fixes its sign.  Since
$\dim B=3$, the actual form $D\alpha\in B^{4,1}(B)$ is zero.

Suppose for contradiction that the specified raw cube had a strict
actual witness $\mathcal Q=\partial_3(U,V,P,Q)$ on $N$.
Its first entry would satisfy
\[
                       d''\mathcal Q_A=-DU.
 \tag{CU24}
\]
The product with $\pi_B^*\alpha$ has compact support because the
projective factor is proper.  Two full actual Stokes operations and
$D\pi_B^*\alpha=0$ therefore give
\[
                   \int_N d''\mathcal Q_A\wedge\pi_B^*\alpha=0.
 \tag{CU25}
\]
For $r=3$, $U$ has negative primed degree and is zero; the same
conclusion follows directly.

On the other hand, (CU15), the same Stokes calculation for its
explicit $T$, (CU7), and the proper product calibration give
\[
 \begin{split}
 \int_N d''\mathcal Q_A\wedge\pi_B^*\alpha
 &=-\int_N R^\ell\wedge d''a\wedge d''b\wedge d''c
                                            \wedge\pi_B^*\alpha\\
 &=-\int_B d''a\wedge d''b\wedge d''c\wedge\alpha\ne0.
 \end{split}
 \tag{CU26}
\]
This contradicts (CU25).  Every form used is actual; in particular
no assertion that arbitrary proper pushforward preserves actual
forms is used.  The test is an external product only to detect a
particular obstruction, not to replace the full actual test class.

This proves only that the raw CS cube need not be a strict third
boundary.  The very same model has the explicit extending-triple
solution (CU15).  It is not a counterexample to relative cube
solvability, to Green existence, to OPEN-KERNEL, or to the original
regular-CI support problem.  The example concerns the specified
local cube identity, for which no regularity of $f$ was required.
Products with arbitrary additional passive directions preserve the
mechanism after inserting compact actual tests in those directions.
