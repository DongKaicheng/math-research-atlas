\subsection{The full actual primitive}

Use the original-field data and literal native conventions of ECR:
an analytic two-row block $S$ in rows $2,3$ of a rank-$r$ tuple,
$r\ge3$, the external row $1$, the analytic row vector $v=(v_2,v_3)$,
and the ordered native pair of $V(v)=E_{12}(v_2)E_{13}(v_3)$.
The other $r-3$ rows remain unchanged.  Write $N$ for the ordinary
base open, $g$ for the original tuple, and $W=N\setminus|g=0|$.
Let $p_S(g)$ be the entire prescribed GB pair, with its original
ordinary partition $\theta$ and all its original nested caps.

On the ordinary overlap of the two pivots of $S$, the whole actual
triple $\mathcal T(S,v;g)$ is exactly ECR7.  Add the genuine
original-$K$ torus coordinate $t$, put $\tau=v(t)$, and use the
actual family
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal T^t={}&q_\alpha(tv;D_1(t)g)-q_\gamma(tv;D_1(t)g)\\
 &-Q_S(D_1(t)V(v)g)+Q_S(D_1(t)g).
 \end{split}
 \tag{EFC1}
\]
By the final EFT theorem this family is closed in the entire actual
complex on the analytic product, is product-presented on every
bounded real $\tau$-interval, and has a locally uniform ordinary
positive zero tail.  In particular these statements retain both
parameter colours, the metric, all passive cap floors, coefficient
zeros and proper zeros of entries of $g$.  Its parameter units are
precisely monomials $t^a u$ in base analytic units and $t$.

Define the actual quadruple by the restricted PTH integration:
\[
 H(S,v;g)=\Pi K_{\tau_+}(\mathcal T^t),\qquad
 \tau_+=\max(\tau,0),
 \qquad \partial_3H(S,v;g)=\mathcal T(S,v;g).
 \tag{EFC2}
\]
Here $K$ has the full QHT ordering from PTH17.  In particular, for
an input $(A,B,M)$ it is
\[
 K_h(A,B,M)
   =(d''h\wedge A,-d'h\wedge B,-h\wedge d''M,-d'h\wedge M).
 \tag{EFC3}
\]
The integration in (EFC2) is not a general proper analytic
pushforward: it is the exact product-presented, locally
slab-supported construction of PTH.  The local cutoffs in that
construction are equal to one on the relevant interval and have
derivatives in the actual zero tail.  Actual zero relations are
preserved on every base affinoid unit refinement, so the result is
an actual form, rather than a functional or a class of a native
expression.  PTH19 and EFT3 prove the last equality in (EFC2).

\begin{theorem}[Strict full external-row crossing]
There is a specified actual triple on all of $W$,
\[
 q_X(S,v;g)=\theta q_\alpha(v;g)+(1-\theta)q_\gamma(v;g)
                   +\operatorname{ext}_0 K_3(\theta)H(S,v;g),
 \tag{EFC4}
\]
whose complete differential is
\[
 \begin{split}
 \partial_2q_X(S,v;g)={}&\mathcal P_V(v;g)+p_S(V(v)g)\\
 &-p_S(g)-\mathcal P_V(vS^{-1};\operatorname{diag}(1,S)g).
 \end{split}
 \tag{EFC5}
\]
The two weighted chart triples in (EFC4) have their prescribed
closed-support zero extensions.  The last product is formed on
the ordinary pivot overlap before it is extended by zero.  Every
derivative of $\theta$ has closed support in that overlap.
No unweighted singular quadruple is extended by zero.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The exact ECR9 identity reads
\[
 \mathcal X=\partial_2\bigl(\theta q_\alpha+(1-\theta)q_\gamma\bigr)
                      -K_2(\theta)\mathcal T.
\]
Substitute (EFC2) and use the full differentiated-partition identity
$\partial_2K_3(\theta)+K_2(\theta)\partial_3=0$.
This gives (EFC5) with exactly the sign in (EFC4).
All identities take place before closed-support zero extension on
their ordinary opens, so RAG's locality and support rule extend
them to $W$.  The whole expression includes all mixed partition
jets and no curvature factor has been canceled.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Range and exclusions.}
The result holds in arbitrary ambient rank and dimension and for
arbitrary analytic external coefficients, including zeros and
unbounded coefficients.  It allows base unit relations such as
$(T_1,T_2,T_1+T_2)$ and moving punctures; PTH retains their complete
actual zero relations.  It changes neither the ground field nor
its absolute value and makes no claim about high-order form descent.
The strict conclusion concerns this explicitly constructed ECR
family.  It does not contradict the diagonal-symbol strict-witness
obstructions, provide a primitive of every closed triple, or prove
the three-chart compatibility needed for an arbitrary $3\times3$
Gaussian block.  Those further chart defects must still be written
and treated in their original whole presentations.
