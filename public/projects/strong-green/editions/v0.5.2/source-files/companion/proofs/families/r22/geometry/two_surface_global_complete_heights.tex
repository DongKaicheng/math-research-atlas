\section{A fixed-field dense family in an elliptic fourfold times a line}
\label{r22dg:section}

\paragraph{Contribution and limits.}
We now construct a family at every finite place, so that the new local
DS22 theorem supplies a complete height domain rather than only a local
example.  The ambient fivefold is $E_0^4\times\mathbf P^1$.
Its smooth divisor fourfolds have $b_1=8,b_2=29$, distinguishing them
from the named SG18 and MG21 families, whose $b_1=4,b_2=7$.
At the distinguished places their exact second monodromy has rank
$802816$ and their third monodromy vanishes.  This is a distinction
of generic fibers and not just of chosen special-fiber models.
We do not classify overlap with every older abstract motivic or
correspondence criterion.  The full arbitrary-variety Green and
unconditional complete-height problems remain open.

\subsection{Global good data and actual coefficient modules}

Use the frozen and independently reviewed SG18 global good-reduction
datum
\[
 E_0/\mathbf Q:\ y^2=x^3-x,\qquad
 F_0=\mathbf Q(E_0[3]),\qquad
 \mathcal E/\mathcal O_{F_0},\qquad
 \mathcal S=\mathcal E^2,
 \qquad \mathcal A_0=\mathcal O_{\mathcal E}(3[0])
                         \boxtimes\mathcal O_{\mathcal E}(3[0]).
 \tag{DG22.1}\label{r22dg:good}
\]
The field contains all geometric $3$-torsion coordinates.  SG18 proves
that it is unramified at $5$ and that $\mathcal E$ is an everywhere
smooth projective elliptic scheme.  Its proof at $2$ uses potential
good reduction from complex multiplication, finite $3$-adic inertia,
the torsion-freeness of $1+3M_2(\mathbf Z_3)$, and
Neron--Ogg--Shafarevich; it does not assert that the original displayed
Weierstrass equation is globally smooth.  The exact frozen source is
\texttt{research\_input/round18/sectors/}
\texttt{abelian\_surface\_ambient\_complete\_heights.tex},
Lemma \texttt{r18sg:global-good}; its SHA-256 is
\texttt{14fb50ae6121f72918f18850c4b8e8c17ac7289ca8305c9205cb7497751f3905}.

The new ambient and polarization are
\[
 \mathcal Y=\mathcal S\times\mathcal S\times\mathbf P^1_{\mathcal O_{F_0}},
 \qquad \mathcal L_0=\mathcal A_0\boxtimes\mathcal A_0\boxtimes\mathcal O(1).
 \tag{DG22.2}\label{r22dg:ambient}
\]
Both surface copies and this fivefold are good at every finite place.

\begin{lemma}[Full sections from literal ambient coefficients]
\label{r22dg:coefficients}
There is a fixed relative embedding
$\mathcal Y\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^N_{\mathcal O_{F_0}}$ with
$\mathcal O(1)|_{\mathcal Y}=\mathcal L_0$ such that the finite free
module $V_5$ of degree-five ambient polynomials surjects onto
$H^0(\mathcal Y,\mathcal L_0^5)$ and onto every field-fiber section
space.  This remains true after every finite number-field base
extension.  In particular every section
\[
 P=f g\prod_{j=1}^5l_j,\qquad
 f,g\in H^0(\mathcal S,\mathcal A_0^5),\quad
 l_j\in H^0(\mathbf P^1,\mathcal O(1)),
 \tag{DG22.3}\label{r22dg:P}
\]
has a literal degree-five ambient polynomial representative.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The degree-three line bundle on each elliptic fiber embeds it as
a plane cubic.  The plane-cubic exact sequence and
$H^1(\mathbf P^2,\mathcal O(t))=0$ show that its coordinate
polynomials surject onto every positive tensor degree of that
line bundle.  The four elliptic embeddings and the standard
$\mathbf P^1$ embedding combine by Segre.  Fiberwise multiplication
in degree $m$ is consequently surjective onto the full
$H^0(\mathcal L_0^m)$: a pure tensor of products of $m$ sections
from each factor is the product of $m$ external-product sections.

Positive elliptic line bundles and positive projective-line line
bundles have no higher cohomology.  Kunneth and cohomology and base
change therefore make the section modules locally free and identify
their fibers.  Choose a finite free module surjecting onto the
degree-one section module.  Its evaluation gives a relative closed
immersion into an ordinary projective space; redundant generators
do not change the very ample property.  Fiberwise surjectivity in
every degree and Nakayama give the asserted global surjectivity.
No section module over a Dedekind ring was assumed to be free.
The cohomology calculation and surjectivity commute with the finite
flat extension of number rings.

The three groups of factors in \eqref{r22dg:P} have multidegrees
$(5,0,0)$, $(0,5,0)$, and $(0,0,5)$.  Their product has
multidegree $(5,5,5)$, exactly $\mathcal L_0^5$.  It is not
incorrectly counted as a degree-fifteen Segre polynomial.
The proved surjection supplies its degree-five representative.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The new complete-height family}

\begin{theorem}[Two-surface global family, DG22]
\label{r22dg:main}
There is one fixed finite extension $F/F_0$, unramified at every
place over $5$, and a Zariski-dense family of smooth geometrically
integral fourfolds
\[
                              X\in|\mathcal L_0^5|_{Y_F}
 \tag{DG22.4}\label{r22dg:family}
\]
with the following properties.
\begin{enumerate}
\item For every finite extension $F'/F$, every finite place $w$ of
$F'$, every codimension, and every specified geometrically
homologically trivial rational cycle on $X_{F'_w}$, strong G holds
with its exact support, every compact ordinary actual test, the
complete auxiliary field degrees, and the given absolute value.
\item For every such $F'/F$, the frozen PH16 pairings are defined
on the full complementary geometric homological Chow groups:
\[
 \operatorname{CH}^r(X_{F'})_{\mathbf Q,\mathrm{geom\,hom}}
 \times\operatorname{CH}^{5-r}(X_{F'})_{\mathbf Q,\mathrm{geom\,hom}}
                    \longrightarrow\mathbf R,\qquad1\le r\le4.
 \tag{DG22.5}\label{r22dg:heights}
\]
GA17 supplies regular-model admissible Chow lifts on these complete
proved domains.
\item At every place of $F$ over $5$, the actual DS22 model has
triple strata and no quadruple strata, and on its generic $H^4$
\[
                  \operatorname{rank}N^2=802816,\qquad N^3=0.
 \tag{DG22.6}\label{r22dg:N}
\]
Its minimum strict semistable dominance depth, taken over all
finite valued extensions and their witnesses, is exactly two;
the lower bound holds over every fixed finite extension.
\item Every member has $b_1=8,b_2=29$.  It is not geometrically
isomorphic to a member of the named SG18 or MG21 families, or to a
smooth projective-space complete-intersection fourfold.  The claimed
density is over the one fixed field $F$ in the full section space;
it is not a statement about every section of that space.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\emph{1. Fix reference and residue data before fixing the field.}
Choose an integral polynomial $H_0\in V_5$ with smooth generic
divisor.  Smooth Bertini in characteristic zero gives a nonempty
open, and the integral coefficient lattice is Zariski dense.
Let $S_0$ be the finite set of rational primes other than $5$
lying below a bad reduction of this reference divisor, together
with $2$.  A place where the entire defining section reduces to
zero is included.  At every place $v$ of $F_0$ above every prime
of $S_0$, even if the reference is already smooth there, choose
a smooth degree-five special section $G_v$ after a finite residue
extension.

At every $v\mid5$, choose after a finite residue extension two
smooth geometrically integral curves
$C_v=V(f_v)$ and $D_v=V(g_v)$ in $|\mathcal A_{0,v}^5|$,
five distinct native points of $\mathbf P^1$, their linear forms,
and a section $Q_v$ transverse to all factor strata of DS22,
with every positive-dimensional cut geometrically integral.
The very ample restriction of $\mathcal L_0^5$ to each smooth
stratum gives a nonempty Bertini open.  The finite intersection
of these opens is nonempty over the algebraic closure, and thus
has a point over a finite residue extension.  This also supplies
all center-integrality hypotheses needed in DW22.

Choose an integer $N$ divisible by the finitely many residue
extension degrees just used.  At each selected maximal ideal of
$\mathcal O_{F_0}$ choose a monic irreducible polynomial of degree
$N$ over its residue field.  Coefficientwise CRT produces one
monic $g_0\in\mathcal O_{F_0}[T]$ with all these reductions.
It is irreducible over $F_0$: a nontrivial monic factor has
coefficients integral over, hence in, the integrally closed number
ring and would factor one of the irreducible reductions.
Put $F=F_0[T]/(g_0)$.  At every selected place this extension is
unramified and inert of degree $N$, because the chosen residual
polynomial is separable and irreducible.  The corresponding local
finite etale algebra is already the unramified integer ring.
All residue choices are therefore available over this one $F$.
In particular $F/\mathbf Q$ is unramified at $5$.

\emph{2. Literal polynomial congruences and the Lefschetz open.}
Positive elliptic cohomology and base change let CRT lift all
$f_v$ to one global section $f$ and all $g_v$ to one global
section $g$ of $\mathcal A_0^5$.  The point forms lift in the free
module $\mathcal O_F^2$.  Form their actual product $P$ and use
Lemma~\ref{r22dg:coefficients} to choose its representative in
$V_5$.  The same free coefficient module lifts the $Q_v$ to $Q$
and represents all $G_v$.  All subsequent congruences are literal
ambient polynomial congruences.

Put $B=\prod_{p\in S_0}p$ and $M=25B$.  Impose
\[
 H_1\equiv P+5Q\pmod{25\mathcal O_F},\qquad
 H_1\equiv G_v\pmod{\mathfrak p_v}\quad(v\text{ above }S_0).
 \tag{DG22.7}\label{r22dg:H1}
\]
The conditions are compatible and their solutions form a translate
of a full integral coefficient lattice.  Unramifiedness at $5$
gives $25\mathcal O_F=\prod_{v\mid5}\mathfrak p_v^2$.

Require in addition that $H_1$ be smooth and that the pencil
$[uH_0+vH_1]$ have smooth base locus and only transverse one-node
singular geometric members.  This is the nonempty fixed-smooth-member
Lefschetz open of frozen CG18/SC18, used in SG18 and MG21.
The input on the present ambient is checked directly: degree-five
polynomials in its relative projective embedding supply second
jets at one point and independent first jets at two distinct
points.  The marked-singularity incidence has the expected
codimension, its nonsingular-Hessian locus is nonempty, and
projection from the fixed smooth point is generically etale in
characteristic zero.  Avoiding the non-node and branch loci gives
the stated pencil open.  These jet assertions do not depend on
the old ambient being $E_0^2\times\mathbf P^3$; they apply to
the present smooth fivefold.  The congruence lattice meets this
open densely.

\emph{3. Spread each pencil and choose infinitely many parameters.}
After fixing one such $H_1$, spread the smooth base locus, the
finite separated critical-value scheme, the nonzero Hessians, and
the smooth complement of the critical points.  Outside a finite
set of finite places every geometric pencil member is smooth or
has exactly one ordinary quadratic point.  Spread also the
independence of the two sections, so that no pencil parameter
there gives a zero section.  Include every place over
$S_0\cup\{5\}$ and every characteristic-two place in the
exceptional set.  Let $T_0$ be its finite set of underlying
rational primes.  This set may depend on $H_1$; the field $F$
and the previous congruence lattice do not.

There are infinitely many integers $a$ satisfying
\[
 a\equiv1\pmod{25},\qquad a\equiv1\pmod B,\qquad
 a\equiv0\pmod p\quad
                (p\in T_0\setminus(S_0\cup\{5\})).
 \tag{DG22.8}\label{r22dg:a}
\]
For them put
\[
                          X_a=V(MH_0+aH_1)\subset Y_F.
 \tag{DG22.9}\label{r22dg:Xa}
\]
Remove the finitely many singular generic pencil parameters.
All remaining generic members are smooth.

\emph{4. Verify every finite place and actual local model.}
At $v\mid5$ write $H_1=P+5Q+25R_1$.  The integer $a$ is a
unit; dividing by it gives the actual local equation
\[
 P+5Q_a=0,\qquad
 Q_a=Q+5R_1+5B a^{-1}H_0,\qquad \bar Q_a=\bar Q.
 \tag{DG22.10}\label{r22dg:local}
\]
The number $5$ is a native uniformizer.  This is the precise
DS22 datum with $\mathcal A=\mathcal B=\mathcal A_0^5$ and
$d=5$.  Its special surfaces are both the square of the reduction
of $E_0$, so Corollary~\ref{r22ds:elliptic} supplies full middle
positivity over every finite constant field.

At every place above $S_0$, $M$ reduces to zero and $a$ to one;
the special divisor is the chosen smooth $G_v$.  At any
exceptional place outside $S_0\cup\{5\}$, $M$ is a unit and
$a$ reduces to zero; the divisor is the smooth reference $H_0$.
The definition of $S_0$ ensures that reference is smooth at
every such place.  All other special fibers are smooth or have
one ordinary quadratic point.  Thus every finite place is
accounted for, and all characteristic-two places are smooth.

Every displayed defining special section is nonzero.  In the
smooth ambient with integral fibers it is a nonzerodivisor;
the relative Cartier divisor is flat over the trait.  Smooth
and one-node geometric divisors here are geometrically integral,
as follows explicitly from the divisor sequence.  On every
geometric field fiber,
\[
                    H^1(Y,\mathcal L_0^{-5})=0.
 \tag{DG22.11}\label{r22dg:negative-H1}
\]
Indeed $Y$ is a product of four elliptic curves and $\mathbf P^1$,
and in every degree-one Kunneth term at least three of the elliptic
factors occur in $H^0$ of a negative-degree line bundle, which
is zero.  Also $H^0(Y,\mathcal L_0^{-5})=0$.  The divisor
sequence therefore gives $H^0(X,\mathcal O_X)=\bar k$.
A Cartier fourfold is $S_2$; a smooth or isolated-node fourfold
is also $R_1$, so it is normal.  Connectedness and normality
give geometric integrality.  The same applies to the smooth
generic members.  Their flat closures are integral: flatness
makes the structure sheaf torsion-free over the trait, and the
generic integral ring injects its affine local rings after
localization.  These are actual projective flat local models,
not only prescribed equations on unrelated special fibers.

\emph{5. All new extension cycles and the complete heights.}
At places above $5$, DS22 gives full G for every new cycle after
every finite valued extension, by its separate all-unramified
reapplication and RI18 ramified argument.  At all other places
the frozen good-reduction or ON17 ordinary-quadratic theorem
applies with its all-finite-extension scope.  Now take an arbitrary
finite $F'/F$ and every finite completion $F'_w$.  Apply the
corresponding local theorem to its entire new homological Chow
group.  No such group is replaced by classes pulled back from
$F$ or from the original completion.

The frozen PH16 hypotheses consequently hold in every
complementary degree at every finite place.  Together with its
already closed archimedean input, PH16 gives
\eqref{r22dg:heights} on the full original global homological
Chow groups over $F'$.  GA17 then supplies the regular-model
admissible lifts on those full proved domains.  The local
identities have exactly the supports of the specified cycles,
all ordinary actual compact tests, complete auxiliary field
degrees, and the extending original absolute values.

\emph{6. Density over the one fixed field.}
The field $F$, the residue targets, and the congruence lattice
were fixed before varying $H_1$.  The allowed $H_1$ in that
lattice and the Lefschetz open are Zariski dense in $V_{5,F}$.
For every such direction infinitely many $a$ survive
\eqref{r22dg:a} and the finite generic exclusions.  A polynomial
vanishing on all constructed vectors $MH_0+aH_1$ vanishes on
the full affine line in every such direction, hence at
$MH_0+H_1$.  Those points are dense, so the polynomial is zero.
The coefficient surjection of Lemma~\ref{r22dg:coefficients}
makes the images dense in the full section space, and then in
its projectivization.  Dependence of $T_0$ on the chosen pencil
does not change this argument or the fixed field.

\emph{7. Exact generic-fiber invariants.}
On the abelian surface $S=E^2$ the canonical class is zero and
$\mathcal A_0^2=18$.  The chosen curves have classes
$5\mathcal A_0$, hence
\[
                 C^2=D^2=450,\quad g(C)=g(D)=226,\quad
                 p_C=p_D=452-4=448.
 \tag{DG22.12}\label{r22dg:genus}
\]
DW22 with $d=5$ yields
$\operatorname{rank}N^2=448^2\cdot4=802816$ and $N^3=0$,
as well as the exact minimum depth two.  These are actual
generic cohomology invariants and persist under finite valued
extension.  In particular these members have no potential good
reduction at the distinguished places and no depth-one
semistable dominant witness there.

Weak Lefschetz for the smooth ample divisor in the fivefold
gives the full low-degree cohomology of $Y$ in degrees one
and two.  Since $H^1(E^4)$ has dimension eight and
$H^2(E^4)=\bigwedge^2H^1(E^4)$ has dimension $28$,
\[
                         b_1(X)=8,\qquad b_2(X)=28+1=29.
 \tag{DG22.13}\label{r22dg:Betti}
\]
The named SG18 and MG21 families have $b_1=4,b_2=7$ by their
own frozen calculations, whereas a smooth projective-space
complete-intersection fourfold has $b_1=0,b_2=1$ by weak
Lefschetz.  These immutable geometric invariants prove the
claimed nonisomorphism.  The comparison with MG21 is also
consistent with its nonzero third monodromy, in contrast to
\eqref{r22dg:N}.  This proves every assertion.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact contribution to the open problems.}
The new closed domain consists of every specified homologically
trivial cycle in every codimension on the displayed dense family,
including all new cycles after all finite field extensions.
This is a new explicit family of generic fibers relative to the
named previous constructions.  A dense family is not the whole
ambient linear system.  Positivity of arbitrary products of
surfaces, all nonarchimedean actual period comparison, general G,
and complete unconditional heights for arbitrary varieties are
not deduced here.
