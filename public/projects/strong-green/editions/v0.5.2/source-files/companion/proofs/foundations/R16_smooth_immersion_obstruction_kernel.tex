\section{A deformation argument for the obstruction kernel under a smooth closed immersion}
\label{r16gi:section}

This argument uses the new Bott--Chern projective-bundle formula and
smooth-center blowup injectivity, but does not require a form-valued
pushforward for a closed immersion.  It also does not use the unproved
reverse implication from a strong Green solution to obstruction
vanishing.  Every occurrence of a proper cycle push below is an
algebraic Chow operation used to specify a cycle before its obstruction
class is taken.

\begin{lemma}[Two components of the deformation fiber detect its general-fiber class]
\label{r16gi:detection}
Let $X/K$ be smooth integral projective and
$i:C\hookrightarrow X$ a smooth integral closed immersion of
codimension $c\geq1$.  Set
\[
 q:\mathcal W=\operatorname{Bl}_{C\times\{0\}}
                    (X\times\mathbf P^1)\longrightarrow X\times\mathbf P^1.
\]
Its exceptional divisor is
$E=\mathbf P_C(N_{C/X}\oplus\mathcal O_C)$, and the strict transform
of $X\times\{0\}$ is $F=\operatorname{Bl}_C X$.  Choose
$t\in\mathbf P^1(K)\setminus\{0\}$, for example $t=1$, and
identify the fiber $X_t$ of $\mathcal W\to\mathbf P^1$ with $X$.
If an actual Bott--Chern class $\alpha$ on $\mathcal W$ restricts
to zero on both $E$ and $F$, then its restriction to $X_t$ is zero.
All bidegrees are permitted.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Choose a separately closed actual representative $A$ of $\alpha$.
The zero restriction class on $E$ means $i_E^*A=D\sigma$ for an
actual form $\sigma$ on $E$.  Extend $\sigma$ degreewise to an
actual form $\widetilde\sigma$ on $\mathcal W$, using the frozen
ordinary closed-immersion extension theorem, and replace $A$ by
$A-D\widetilde\sigma$.  Its restriction to $E$ is now literally
zero.  If either primitive degree is negative, the boundary space is
zero and this step simply uses $\sigma=0$.

The frozen relative double-complex isomorphism for this blowup is
\[
 q^*:\ker\bigl(B(X\times\mathbf P^1)\to B(C\times\{0\})\bigr)
       \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
       \ker\bigl(B(\mathcal W)\to B(E)\bigr).
 \tag{GI16.1}\label{r16gi:relative}
\]
Its inverse commutes with $d',d''$.  Hence the modified $A$ is
$q^*B$ for a global separately closed ordinary actual form $B$ on
$X\times\mathbf P^1$.  This is an actual descent, not a descent
of a current followed by a representability assertion.

The new full Bott--Chern formula for a projective line gives uniquely
\[
 [B]=\operatorname{pr}_X^*a+
       h_t\wedge\operatorname{pr}_X^*b,
 \tag{GI16.2}\label{r16gi:product-class}
\]
where $h_t$ is the first Chern class of
$\operatorname{pr}_{\mathbf P^1}^*\mathcal O(1)$ and the negative
coefficient degrees mean zero.  Its pullback to any point fiber
is zero.  Restricting \eqref{r16gi:product-class} to $F$ therefore
gives $q_F^*a$, where $q_F:F\to X$ is the blowup along $C$.
The assumed zero restriction to $F$ gives $q_F^*a=0$.
Theorem~\ref{r16pb:blowup} makes this pullback injective, so $a=0$.
When $c=1$, $q_F$ is an isomorphism and the same conclusion holds.
Restriction to $X_t$ of the modified representative has class $a$,
and the earlier modification was a global $D$-boundary.  Thus the
original $\alpha|_{X_t}$ is zero as asserted.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The lifted center and its two special restrictions]
\label{r16gi:geometry}
With the preceding notation, the strict transform of
$C\times\mathbf P^1$ is a closed immersion
\[
 k:C\times\mathbf P^1\hookrightarrow\mathcal W.
\]
It is disjoint from $F$.  Its intersection with $E$ is the section
$s:C\to\mathbf P_C(N_{C/X}\oplus\mathcal O_C)$ corresponding
to the trivial line summand, with intersection multiplicity one.
For any rational Chow class $z\in\operatorname{CH}^u(C)_{\mathbf Q}$,
put $\widetilde z=k_*\operatorname{pr}_C^*z$.  Then
\[
 i_F^*\widetilde z=0,\qquad
 i_E^*\widetilde z=s_*z,
 \qquad i_t^*\widetilde z=i_*z\quad(t\ne0).
 \tag{GI16.3}\label{r16gi:restrictions}
\]
For the convention that projectivization parametrizes lines, if
$\xi=c_1(\mathcal O_E(1))$ and $\pi:E\to C$, then
\[
 s_*z=
   \left(\sum_{j=0}^{c}\xi^j\pi^*c_{c-j}(N_{C/X})\right)
                                 \wedge\pi^*z.
 \tag{GI16.4}\label{r16gi:section-class}
\]
These are algebraic Chow identities.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The pullback of the blowup ideal to $C\times\mathbf P^1$ is the
invertible ideal of $C\times\{0\}$.  The universal property gives
the indicated lift.  Locally choose regular normal coordinates
$x_1,\ldots,x_c$ for $C$ and a parameter $t$ at zero.  In the
$t$-leading blowup chart, write $x_j=t u_j$.  The lifted center is
defined by $u_1=\cdots=u_c=0$; thus it remains isomorphic to
$C\times\mathbf P^1$, and meets the exceptional divisor $t=0$
transversely with multiplicity one.  Its central line is the
$t$-direction, which is exactly the trivial summand of
$N_{C/X}\oplus\mathcal O_C$.

The strict transform $F$ meets the exceptional divisor along
$\mathbf P_C(N_{C/X})$, so it misses this section.  Outside the
central fiber it is already disjoint from the lifted center.
These facts give \eqref{r16gi:restrictions}, by ordinary refined
intersection and the projection formula, also for rational cycle
coefficients and arbitrary representatives of $z$.

On $E$ the map from the tautological line to $\pi^*N_{C/X}$ is a
section of the rank-$c$ bundle
$\mathcal O_E(1)\otimes\pi^*N_{C/X}$.  Its regular zero scheme
is precisely $s(C)$.  Its top Chern class is
$\sum_{j=0}^c\xi^j\pi^*c_{c-j}(N_{C/X})$.
Multiplication by $\pi^*z$ and the algebraic projection formula
give \eqref{r16gi:section-class}.  In particular no analytic
Gysin covariance has entered this calculation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The obstruction kernel is stable under smooth closed push]
\label{r16gi:kernel}
Let $i:C\hookrightarrow X$ be a smooth integral closed immersion
of codimension $c\geq1$ between smooth integral projective
$K$-varieties.  For every rational Chow class
$z\in\operatorname{CH}^u(C)_{\mathbf Q}$ one has
\[
 \mathfrak o_{C,K}^u(z)=0
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \mathfrak o_{X,K}^{u+c}(i_*z)=0.
 \tag{GI16.5}\label{r16gi:kernel-stability}
\]
The assertion is entirely in the original-field actual theory,
with no degree or dimension restriction.  It holds componentwise
for disconnected smooth centers.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply the frozen Chow-to-actual Bott--Chern map to the single
algebraic cycle class $\widetilde z$ of
Lemma~\ref{r16gi:geometry}, and put
\[
 \alpha=\mathfrak o_{\mathcal W,K}^{u+c}(\widetilde z).
\]
Only the contravariant functoriality and graded-ring property of
that map are now used.  Its restriction to $F$ is zero by the
first identity of \eqref{r16gi:restrictions}.  Its restriction
to $E$ is, by \eqref{r16gi:section-class},
\[
 \alpha|_E=
   \sum_{j=0}^{c}[\xi]^j\wedge
      \pi^*\bigl(\mathfrak o_{C,K}^{c-j}(c_{c-j}(N_{C/X}))
                       \wedge\mathfrak o_{C,K}^{u}(z)\bigr)=0.
 \tag{GI16.6}\label{r16gi:exceptional-zero}
\]
Lemma~\ref{r16gi:detection} therefore gives $\alpha|_{X_t}=0$.
The last identity of \eqref{r16gi:restrictions} and pullback
compatibility identify this restriction with
$\mathfrak o_{X,K}^{u+c}(i_*z)$.  This is
\eqref{r16gi:kernel-stability}.

The proof never compares two arbitrary Green curvatures via their
current equations.  It proves a zero class in actual Bott--Chern
cohomology directly, using a deformation whose special restrictions
are computed in the Chow ring.  Native constant-field conventions
are unchanged in the blowups, products, restrictions, and
cohomology decompositions.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Exact-support Green forms for the pushed obstruction-zero sector]
\label{r16gi:strong-G}
Under Theorem~\ref{r16gi:kernel}, let $Z$ be any specified rational
cycle on $C$ with $\mathfrak o_{C,K}([Z])=0$.  Then its specified
push $i_*Z$ has a strong Green form in
$B^{u+c-1,u+c-1}(X^{\mathrm{an}}\setminus|i_*Z|^{\mathrm{an}})$,
with zero curvature against every compact complementary actual
test over $K$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Apply the frozen sufficient direction from obstruction zero to
the exact-support all-actual strong Green identity to the class
vanishing just proved.  No converse from strong G to obstruction
zero is asserted or needed.
\end{proof}
