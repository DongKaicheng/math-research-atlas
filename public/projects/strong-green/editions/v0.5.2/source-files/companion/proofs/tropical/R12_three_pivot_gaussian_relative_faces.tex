\subsection{The three actual local pairs}

Let $N$ be an ordinary open in the original smooth $K$-analytic
space, let $\overline L$ be the given actual metrized line bundle,
let $f$ be the original rank-$r$ tuple, and put
$W=N\setminus|f=0|$, $s=r-1$, $R=c_1(\overline L)$ and $d_0=r-2$.
Supply one analytic invertible block
\[
 M=\begin{pmatrix}a&b&c\\d&e&f_0\\g&h&i\end{pmatrix}
                       \oplus I_{r-3}.
 \tag{G3F1}
\]
The notation $f_0$ in the matrix distinguishes that entry from the
section tuple $f$.  The three ordinary opens
$N_a=\{a\ne0\}$, $N_d=\{d\ne0\}$ and $N_g=\{g\ne0\}$ cover $N$.
Indeed, simultaneous vanishing of the first column at an analytic
point contradicts invertibility in its completed residue field.

Choose the symmetric smooth partition function $\chi$ of SGI2,
with a fixed positive tail offset.  Every GB subblock in this source
uses this same function and its original native cap prescriptions.
The symmetry is a new specified choice, not a claim for arbitrary
previous GB partition choices.  Write $\mathcal G(S;x)$ for the
entire original GB pair of a two-row block $S$ starting at a tuple
$x$.  Write $p(w;x)$ for the chronological native pair of a finite
elementary word; its rightmost factor acts first.  The fixed signed
permutations have their prescribed native pair zero.

On $N_a$ put
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 t=d/a,\qquad u=g/a,\qquad
 S_a=\begin{pmatrix}E&F\\H&I\end{pmatrix}
       =\begin{pmatrix}e-tb&f_0-tc\\h-ub&i-uc\end{pmatrix},\\

 \end{gathered}
 \tag{G3F2}
\]
Then $M=L_aD_1(a)B(S_a)U_a$ and $\det S_a=\det M/a$ is a unit.
Define the actual pair
\[

 \end{split}
 \tag{G3F3}
\]

$\mathcal G(S_a;-)$ is the whole two-chart GB pair.

On $N_d$ use the same rule for $J_{12}^{-1}M$, with first pivot $d$,
and append $J_{12}$ on the left.  On $N_g$ use the rule for

\[

\subsection{The face from $a$ to $d$}

Work on the entire ordinary overlap $N_a\cap N_d$, so $t$ is a unit
but $u$ may be zero.  Set $x=U_af$ and $y=B(S_a)x$, and introduce

\[
 \begin{split}
 O_a&=E_{31}(u)E_{21}(t)D_1(a),\\

                  E_{32}(-u)D_2(t^{-1})E_{12}(d^{-1}).
 \end{split}
 \tag{G3F11}
\]

list specifies a whole native witness $Q^{\rm out}_{ad}(y)$,
with left word minus right word as orientation:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Replace $E_{21}(t)$ by the exact GB9 pivot word, using GB10.
\item Move $E_{12}(t^{-1})$ to the right of $D_1(a)$ by reversed
      target MX; its new coefficient is $d^{-1}$.
\item Merge $D_1(t)D_1(a)$ into $D_1(d)$ by SL.
\item Swap $D_2(t^{-1})D_1(d)$ into $D_1(d)D_2(t^{-1})$ by
      the positive CS square with temporal weights $v(d),-v(t)$.
\item Use the literal signed-permutation rule
      $E_{31}(u)J_{12}=J_{12}E_{32}(-u)$.
\item Use ST in the exact form
      $E_{32}(-u)E_{21}(-t^{-1})
       =E_{21}(-t^{-1})E_{31}(u/t)E_{32}(-u)$.
\item Commute $E_{21}(-t^{-1})$ and $E_{31}(u/t)$ by DTC.
\item Move $E_{32}(-u)$ to the right of $D_1(d)$ by passive MX.
\end{enumerate}

chronological tuple of the subword in this finite list.  This is
the exact finite substitution convention of GB7, so all prefixes

\[

the list imposes no nonvanishing assumption: the ST, DTC and MX
triples retain their full frozen coefficient-zero extensions.

Put $v_{ad}=(E,F)/d$.  Direct matrix multiplication gives
\[
 U_d=V(v_{ad})U_a,\qquad

 \tag{G3F13}

 p(U_a;f)+p(V(v_{ad});U_af)-p(U_d;f)=\partial_2q^U_{ad}.

\subsection{The exact remaining third relation}

On $N_a\cap N_d\cap N_g$ form the specified whole expressions
\[
 T_{adg}=Q_{ad}+Q_{dg}-Q_{ag},\qquad
 E_{adg}=e_{ad}+e_{dg}-e_{ag}.
 \tag{G3F29}

actual on the entire unpunctured ordinary overlap.  Adding the three

\[
             \partial_2T_{adg}=-E_{adg}|_W,
                          \qquad \partial_1E_{adg}=0.
 \tag{G3F30}
\]
The second identity holds on the whole unpunctured overlap, not

\[
 T_{adg}=\partial_3 H_{adg}+F_{adg}|_W,
 \qquad H_{adg}\text{ actual on }W\cap N_a\cap N_d\cap N_g,
 \quad F_{adg}\in L_2^s(N_a\cap N_d\cap N_g)\text{ actual}.
 \tag{G3F31}

construct $F_{adg}$ with $\partial_2F_{adg}=-E_{adg}$ on the
whole unpunctured overlap; no injectivity of restriction to $W$
is inferred here.  Even such a construction would leave the
separate closed-triple primitive requirement for
$T_{adg}-F_{adg}|_W$.  Neither task follows merely from
the closedness in (G3F30).  In particular the three full faces
contain ST, DTC, GB, SGI, GMU and the genuine product-torus

\paragraph{The precise conditional original-cycle bridge.}

 \begin{split}

compact support; no additional growth condition on $p$ is imposed.
