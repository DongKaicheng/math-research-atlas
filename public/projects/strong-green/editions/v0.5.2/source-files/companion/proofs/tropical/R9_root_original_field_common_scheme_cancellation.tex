\section{The original-field common-scheme cancellation theorem}
\label{sec:r9-original-field-common-scheme-cancellation}

The preceding angular transgression and analytic constant-field
lift give an original-field actual existence theorem without the
matrix-certificate restrictions of ICC, SCL, or CFC.  Those earlier
certificates still classify particular constant-normalization
procedures, but are not hypotheses for the present construction.

\begin{theorem}[Actual cancellation of separated identical CI germs]
\label{thm:r9-original-field-common-scheme-cancellation}
Fix the original finite $K/\mathbf Q_p$ and absolute value.  Let
$X/K$ be smooth projective geometrically integral of dimension $n$,
let $2\le r\le n$, and let $s_0,s_1$ be regular $r$-tuples of
global sections of one line bundle $\mathcal L$, whose zero schemes
$Y_0,Y_1$ are pure of codimension $r$.  Put
\[
 Z=[Y_1]-[Y_0],\quad T=|Z|,\quad
 C=(|Y_0|\cup|Y_1|)\setminus T,\quad q=n-r.
 \tag{OFC1}
\]
Assume that $C$ is closed in $X$, disjoint from $T$, and that
the ideal sheaves of $Y_0$ and $Y_1$ agree on a Zariski
neighborhood of $C$.  No reducedness of the common zero scheme,
geometric connectedness of $C$, common invariant lattice,
or determinant compatibility is required.

For any one common piecewise smooth metric on $\mathcal L$,
there is a displayed construction of an actual form
\[
 g_Z\in B^{r-1,r-1}(X^{\mathrm{an}}\setminus T),\qquad
 \int_{X^{\mathrm{an}}\setminus T}g_Z\wedge D\eta=-\int_Z\eta
 \quad\bigl(\eta\in B_c^{q,q}(X^{\mathrm{an}})
                                  \text{ arbitrary actual}\bigr).
 \tag{OFC2}
\]
The cycle, its full multiplicities, its exact support complement,
the common metric, and the original field are unchanged.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Give $C$ its reduced closed subscheme structure and decompose it
into its finitely many connected components $C_j$.  These are
proper reduced connected schemes.  The standard proper-global-
functions result (Stacks Project, Lemma 33.9.3, Tag 0BUG,
parts (1)--(3)) gives a finite field
$F_j=H^0(C_j,\mathcal O_{C_j})$ over $K$; characteristic zero
makes it separable.  This is a known algebraic input, not a new
claim about connectedness implying $F_j=K$.

The regular-sequence argument of ICC gives a canonical conormal
matrix $A_j\in\operatorname{GL}_r(F_j)$.  Apply CFN to embed
$F_j$ as analytic functions on a neighborhood of $C_j$ inside
the original $X^{\mathrm{an}}$.  Shrink to pairwise disjoint
neighborhoods $N_j$ disjoint from $T$, whose only original
zero support is $C_j$.  Exact freezing (CFN6) makes the local
norm of $s_1$ equal to the norm of $A_js_0$ on $N_j$.
The latter notation uses the lifted analytic entries only on
$N_j$, and is not a global tuple with coefficients in a
new ground field.

Put $R=c_1(\mathcal L)$ and $c_j=v(\det A_j)$, using the
unique extension of the original valuation.  Diagonalize
$A_j=U_j\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_{j,i})V_j$ over $F_j$.
In the angular formulas take the real constants
$a_{j,i}=v(\lambda_{j,i})$.  For arbitrary such real constants,
$u_a=\min(x_i+a_i)$ defines an actual weighted projective
metric, even if no element of $K$ has the prescribed value.
The finite coefficient construction of AAT gives
$\sigma_a$ with its literal differential identity.  Its
pullback by the ratios of the components of $V_js_0$ is
the original-$K$ unit presentation (CFN7).
Consequently the entire AAT22 identity, with every mixed
metric term, gives actual forms on $N_j\setminus C_j$:
\[
 \alpha_j\in B^{r-2,r-1},\quad
 \beta_j\in B^{r-1,r-2},\qquad
 G_{s_1}-G_{s_0}=d'\alpha_j+d''\beta_j+c_jR^{r-1}.
 \tag{OFC3}
\]
Every term is defined by finitely many analytic units on the
original neighborhood.  No descent theorem for forms on
$X_{F_j}$ is used.

Choose ordinary smooth cutoffs $\chi_j$ compactly supported
in $N_j$, equal to one on smaller neighborhoods $N'_j$ of
$C_j$.  On
$V=X^{\mathrm{an}}\setminus(|Y_0|\cup|Y_1|)$ define
\[
 g_V=G_{s_1}-G_{s_0}
       -\sum_j\bigl(d'(\chi_j\alpha_j)+d''(\chi_j\beta_j)\bigr).
 \tag{OFC4}
\]
The cutoff products are extended by zero outside $N_j$ on
$V$ before their full derivatives are taken.  Their supports
may approach $C_j$, where no extension of the primitives is
claimed.  On $N'_j\cap V$, (OFC3) makes $g_V=c_jR^{r-1}$.
Glue it with this actual extending form on $N'_j$ to obtain
the actual $g_Z$ on precisely $X^{\mathrm{an}}\setminus T$.

For any actual test in (OFC2), the frozen degree support lemma
makes $D\eta$ compactly supported away from both $Y_i$.
The products $\chi_j\alpha_j\wedge D\eta$ and
$\chi_j\beta_j\wedge D\eta$ are therefore actual compact
forms of degrees $(n-1,n)$ and $(n,n-1)$ on $V$.
Actual Stokes and $d'D\eta=d''D\eta=0$ show that every
correction in (OFC4) has zero integral against $D\eta$.
The original two all-actual CI8 formulas have equal curvature
$R^r$, so their difference gives the remaining term
$-\int_Z\eta$.  This proves (OFC2) entirely on the original
$K$-analytic $X$.  There is no field-degree adjustment.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Actual objects and independently checkable inputs}

The proof supplies the actual representative, not merely its
class modulo $\operatorname{Ann}(\operatorname{im}D)$:
equations (OFC3)--(OFC4) and the local extending values are its
definition.  The annihilator calculation verifies that explicit
representative.  The equality of local ideals is an algebraic
condition on the given schemes; the finite connected support
decomposition and the conormal matrices can be checked without
assuming strong G, OPEN-KERNEL, FT, or bornologicality.
Hensel root lifting supplies the needed constants rather than
assuming an arbitrary high-degree actual descent.

Objects are the displayed actual presentations, identified by
the usual all-refinement relation of $B$.  All transitions are
restrictions, common unit refinements, and actual sheaf gluing.
On each whole fixed target-test presentation, support avoidance
is uniform.  A finite actual presentation of the fixed $g_Z$
on the complementary compact set gives complete mixed
coefficient seminorm estimates for the integral.  Its Green
functional is therefore continuous for the full coefficient-
final topology.  There is no new comparison of the full
lower-middle analytic kernel with a native model.

For $n=r$, the separation condition is automatic, because
the zero supports are finite.  Thus arbitrary closed, not
necessarily $K$-rational, common points with identical local
CI ideals are covered.  In particular differences of reduced
zero-dimensional regular complete intersections of the same
line bundle are covered even when their supports overlap.
For every $n\ge r$, the theorem covers positive-dimensional
proper connected clusters of cancelled components, including
intersections among those cancelled components, provided
the entire cluster is separated from the remaining support
and the local ideal equality holds.

Equality of cycle coefficients alone is not that ideal
condition.  Different nonreduced transverse CI ideals may
have the same generic length; CMC/EMR and HNF treat further
explicit norm classes, not all such ideals.  Components
meeting the remaining support require a separate relative
construction on the original open $U$; their exclusion here
does not assert their nonexistence.  The general homologically
trivial-cycle period problem, general actual representation,
arbitrary actual descent and full unconditional BB height
are not solved by this theorem.
