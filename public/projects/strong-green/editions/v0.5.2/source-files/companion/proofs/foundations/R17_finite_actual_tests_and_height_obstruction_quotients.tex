\section{Finite ordinary actual tests for the cycle obstruction}
\label{r17fd:section}

\paragraph{Scope.}
\textbf{Internally reviewed.} The final claims register identifies the independent audits and frozen dependencies.
This section extracts a finite testing theorem from VT17.
The finite family is chosen once for the variety and the
codimension, before choosing a cycle or its representative.
It detects strong G for all rational Chow classes, not only
the homologically trivial classes. It does not say that the
finite obstruction coordinates vanish on the latter group.
All analytic output is ordinary actual, and all asserted
Green solutions retain the exact specified support and the
full original actual test space.

\subsection{A rational finite-dimensional model quotient}

Let $K/\mathbf Q_p$ and its absolute value be fixed, let
$X/K$ be smooth projective geometrically integral of dimension
$n$, and fix $1\le r\le n$.
Choose once a finite valued extension $L/K$, a smooth
projective geometrically integral $Y/L$, a projective dominant
generically finite morphism $g:Y\to X_L$, and a regular
projective reduced strictly semistable model
$\mathcal Y/L^\circ$. Such data exist by the version of
de Jong's Theorem 6.5 used in VT17.
Let $p:X_L\to X$, let $m=[L:K]$, and put
$c_L=-\log|\varpi_L|>0$ with the extending absolute value.

For the native smooth components $D_i$ of $\mathcal Y_k$,
use the numerical system of VT17:
\[
 C^a_{\mathcal Y}=\bigoplus_iN^a(D_i)_{\mathbf Q},\qquad
 A_{r-1}:C^{r-1}_{\mathcal Y}\longrightarrow C^r_{\mathcal Y},
 \qquad
 V_{\mathcal Y,r}=\ker A_{n-r}.
 \tag{FD17.1}\label{r17fd:spaces}
\]
The group $C^r_{\mathcal Y}/\operatorname{im}A_{r-1}$
is naturally the rational dual of $V_{\mathcal Y,r}$ by
the perfect componentwise numerical degree pairing and
the transpose identity VT17.2.

\begin{definition}[The model obstruction coordinates]
\label{r17fd:coordinates}
For $z\in\operatorname{CH}^r(X)_{\mathbf Q}$, choose any
cycle $W$ in the Chow pullback class $g^*p^*z$ on $Y$, and
any rational Chow extension $\Theta_W$ on $\mathcal Y$.
Define
\[
 \nu_{\mathcal Y,g,p}^r(z)(v)
       =\langle r_r\Theta_W,v\rangle,
       \qquad v\in V_{\mathcal Y,r}.
 \tag{FD17.2}\label{r17fd:nu}
\]
Here all degrees are in the native finite residue field of
$L$, with the residue fields of the actual cycle components
retained.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Well-definedness and exact kernel]
\label{r17fd:numerical-kernel}
The map in \eqref{r17fd:nu} is a well-defined rational linear
map
\[
 \nu_{\mathcal Y,g,p}^r:
 \operatorname{CH}^r(X)_{\mathbf Q}
                  \longrightarrow V_{\mathcal Y,r}^{\vee}.
\]
Its kernel is exactly $\ker\mathfrak o_{X,K}^r$.
Consequently it is independent of the representative $W$
and of every chosen extension, and its zero locus is
independent of the witness $(L,Y,g,\mathcal Y)$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Chow localization says that two extensions of the same
generic Chow class differ by a sum of actual rational
vertical cycles $\sum_i j_{i*}u_i$. Their component
restrictions differ by $A_{r-1}u$, which pairs to zero
with $v\in\ker A_{n-r}$ by VT17.2. This also covers
different generic representatives of the same Chow class.
Linearity follows by adding extensions.

If $\mathfrak o_{X,K}^r(z)=0$, finite-constant comparison
and obstruction pullback give
$\mathfrak o_{Y,L}^r(g^*p^*z)=0$. VT17 on the supplied
model places $r_r\Theta_W$ in the image of $A_{r-1}$,
so every coordinate in \eqref{r17fd:nu} is zero.

Conversely, if every coordinate is zero, finite-dimensional
perfect numerical duality puts $r_r\Theta_W$ in that
image. Choosing actual rational representatives of a
numerical preimage gives a vertically numerically zero
extension of $g^*p^*z$. The frozen model detector gives
zero obstruction on $Y$. The all-degree dominant
Bott--Chern pullback injection descends this through $g$,
and the full finite-constant injection descends it through
$p$, giving $\mathfrak o_{X,K}^r(z)=0$.
The last finite degree is exactly $m=[L:K]$; no
geometric sheet count is substituted. This proof never
requires the chosen cycle to pull back in proper position.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Actual test representatives of every coordinate}

\begin{theorem}[A finite rational-valued actual test criterion]
\label{r17fd:finite-tests}
There exist finitely many global ordinary actual forms
\[
 \eta_1,\ldots,\eta_t\in B^{n-r,n-r}(X^{\mathrm{an}}),
 \qquad D\eta_j=0,
 \qquad t=\dim_{\mathbf Q}V_{\mathcal Y,r},
 \tag{FD17.3}\label{r17fd:actual-tests}
\]
such that the following hold.
\begin{enumerate}
\item For every specified rational codimension-$r$ cycle
      $Z$ on $X$, each period $\int_Z\eta_j$ is rational
      and depends only on $[Z]\in\operatorname{CH}^r(X)_{\mathbf Q}$.
\item If $v_1,\ldots,v_t$ is a chosen rational basis of
      $V_{\mathcal Y,r}$, the forms may be chosen so that
\[
       \int_Z\eta_j
            =\nu_{\mathcal Y,g,p}^r([Z])(v_j)
                \quad\hbox{for every }Z.
 \tag{FD17.4}\label{r17fd:periods}
\]
\item Every such specified $Z$ satisfies
\[
 \boxed{\quad
   G_X(Z)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
            \int_Z\eta_j=0\ \text{for all }1\le j\le t.
       \quad}
 \tag{FD17.5}\label{r17fd:criterion}
\]
The Green conclusion is the exact-support all-actual
statement over the original $K$. There is no homological
triviality assumption in this theorem.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Choose actual rational cycle representatives of the basis
$v_j$ on the native components, and use the framed VT17
construction once to obtain
\[
       \beta_j\in B^{n-r,n-r}(Y^{\mathrm{an}}),\qquad
       D\beta_j=0,
       \qquad
       \int_W\beta_j
         =c_L\langle r_r[\overline W],v_j\rangle
 \tag{FD17.6}\label{r17fd:vertical-values}
\]
for every rational cycle $W$ of codimension $r$ on $Y$.
The scalar is the same $c_L$ for every $j$ and $W$.
It is the uniformizer scalar in the fixed-framed MM16.3
comparison; the complete resolution differential is retained.

Apply AD17 to $g$ over $L$ and choose once a global
$D$-closed actual $b_j\in B^{n-r,n-r}(X_L^{\mathrm{an}})$
whose pairing with every global separately closed actual
$\omega$ of degree $(r,r)$ is
\[
             \int_{X_L}b_j\wedge\omega
                    =\int_Y\beta_j\wedge g^*\omega.
 \tag{FD17.7}\label{r17fd:adjoint}
\]
No canonical choice of $b_j$ is needed.

For a specified $Z$, let $(g_{Z_L},\Omega_{Z_L})$ be its
frozen curved pair over $L$. Because $Db_j=0$ literally,
the all-actual curved identity gives
\[
                \int_{Z_L}b_j
                     =\int_{X_L}\Omega_{Z_L}\wedge b_j.
\]
Choose any cycle $W$ representing the Chow class $g^*[Z_L]$.
Obstruction functoriality gives a global actual $\Psi$ with
$g^*\Omega_{Z_L}-\Omega_W=D\Psi$. Stokes and
$D\beta_j=0$ remove this difference. Equation
\eqref{r17fd:adjoint} and the curved pair for $W$ then give
\[
 \int_{Z_L}b_j
      =\int_Y\beta_j\wedge\Omega_W
      =\int_W\beta_j
      =c_L\nu_{\mathcal Y,g,p}^r([Z])(v_j).
 \tag{FD17.8}\label{r17fd:upstairs-period}
\]
Thus the graph adjoint preserves exactly the framed
period, not only its vanishing. No support condition on
$g^{-1}|Z_L|$ was used.

It remains to obtain original-$K$ actual tests with the
correct normalization. Read $b_j$ on the $K$-variety $X_L$
using the identical genuine analytic-unit presentations,
as in FG16's finite-constant corollary. Apply the frozen
full actual finite trace over $K$, and set
\[
          \eta_j=\frac{1}{m c_L}\operatorname{Tr}_{p}^{K}(b_j).
 \tag{FD17.9}\label{r17fd:normalized-test}
\]
Its output is ordinary actual and is $D$-closed. The
finite-flat cycle-trace identity FG16.1 applies to every
prime of $Z$, including cycles contained in branch loci;
for the constant extension all native flat lengths are
retained. Mihatsch's finite-constant integral comparison,
in the precise FG16 formulation, gives
\[
 \begin{split}
  \int_{Z/K}\eta_j
    &=\frac{1}{m c_L}\int_{Z_L/K}b_j\\
    &=\frac{1}{c_L}\int_{Z_L/L}b_j
     =\nu_{\mathcal Y,g,p}^r([Z])(v_j).
 \end{split}
 \tag{FD17.10}\label{r17fd:native-calibration}
\]
The common multiplier in the second line is the full
$[L:K]$, including ramification and residue degree. The
absolute value is extended without renormalizing it, so
$c_L=c_K/e(L/K)$; formula \eqref{r17fd:normalized-test}
keeps this scalar rather than silently replacing it by
$c_K$. This proves the exact rational period assertion.

All choices of basis cycles, framed complexes, graph
extensions, cutoffs and adjoint representatives were made
before $Z$. Projectivity makes each $\eta_j$ a compact
actual test. Necessity in \eqref{r17fd:criterion} therefore
also follows immediately by inserting these literal
$D$-closed tests into a supplied strong equation.
For sufficiency, their periods vanish if and only if
$\nu_{\mathcal Y,g,p}^r([Z])=0$. The preceding lemma gives
obstruction zero, and the frozen sufficient direction
constructs a Green form for this very specified $Z$ on
its exact support complement, valid for every compact
actual test. No smaller finite testing space replaces the
test space of the resulting Green identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The algebraic cycle obstruction quotient is finite]
\label{r17fd:local-finite}
Write
\[
 \mathcal G_K^r(X)=\{z\in\operatorname{CH}^r(X)_{\mathbf Q}:
      \text{every specified representative of $z$ has strong G}\}.
\]
Then $\mathcal G_K^r(X)=\ker\mathfrak o_{X,K}^r$ is a
rational vector subspace, and the period vector induces
an injection
\[
 \operatorname{CH}^r(X)_{\mathbf Q}/\mathcal G_K^r(X)
       \hookrightarrow\mathbf Q^t,
 \qquad [Z]\longmapsto\left(\int_Z\eta_j\right)_j.
 \tag{FD17.11}\label{r17fd:local-quotient}
\]
In particular the rational span of cycle obstructions in
actual Bott--Chern cohomology is finite-dimensional.
The same finite family separates this cycle-generated
sector under the Bott--Chern/Aeppli integration pairing.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
VT17 identifies the existence kernel with the obstruction
kernel on all rational Chow classes. The preceding theorem
gives the stated injection, whose image is precisely the
image of the rational linear map $\nu$ in the chosen basis.
For a separately closed cycle curvature, replacing it by
a global $D$-boundary does not change any of its pairings
with the $D$-closed tests. Thus their separation of the
cycle-generated obstruction sector is the same assertion.
This says nothing about the dimension or nondegeneracy of
the full actual Bott--Chern and Aeppli groups.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Removing the cohomologically compatible part of the kernel}

Assume in this subsection that $n\ge3$, as in KC17, and
fix one $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}k_L$.

\begin{definition}[The rational compatible-representative subspace]
\label{r17fd:compatible}
Let $K_{\mathrm{comp},\ell}(\mathcal Y,r)$ consist of those
$v\in V_{\mathcal Y,r}$ for which there exist actual rational
Chow representatives
\[
          \zeta_i\in\operatorname{CH}^{n-r}(D_i)_{\mathbf Q},
          \qquad [\zeta_i]_{\rm num}=v_i,
\]
such that the geometric classes $\operatorname{cl}_\ell(\zeta_i)$
have equal restrictions on every double intersection.
This is a condition on the existence of representatives;
no cycle-class map from a numerical group is presumed.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[The finite residual test space for homological classes]
\label{r17fd:homological-refinement}
The set $K_{\mathrm{comp},\ell}(\mathcal Y,r)$ is a rational
vector subspace of $V_{\mathcal Y,r}$. On geometrically
$\ell$-homologically trivial classes, the map $\nu$ factors as
\[
 \operatorname{CH}^r(X)_{\mathbf Q,\mathrm{geom\,hom},\ell}
   \longrightarrow
   \bigl(V_{\mathcal Y,r}/K_{\mathrm{comp},\ell}(\mathcal Y,r)\bigr)^\vee.
 \tag{FD17.12}\label{r17fd:residual-map}
\]
Its kernel is still exactly the strong-G existence kernel
on that homological subgroup. Thus there is a family of at
most
\[
       \dim_{\mathbf Q}V_{\mathcal Y,r}
          -\dim_{\mathbf Q}K_{\mathrm{comp},\ell}(\mathcal Y,r)
 \tag{FD17.13}\label{r17fd:residual-number}
\]
fixed rational-valued global $D$-closed actual tests detecting
strong G within that subgroup.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
If representatives $\zeta_i$ and $\xi_i$ establish the
condition for $v$ and $w$, then $a\zeta_i+b\xi_i$ establish
it for $av+bw$ for every $a,b\in\mathbf Q$. The zero
representatives establish it for zero. This proves the
subspace assertion directly on actual Chow classes.

Take a homological class $z$ as stated, an extension
$\Theta_W$ of $g^*p^*z$, and a compatible tuple $v$ with
the chosen representatives. The pure operational class of
$\Theta_W$ supplies the global model cohomology class $a$
used in KC17; it restricts to zero on the geometric generic
fiber. The compatible-representative lifting lemma KC17
supplies a global model cohomology class $b$ restricting to
$\operatorname{cl}_\ell(\zeta_i)$ on every component.
It need not be algebraic or Frobenius fixed. The model is
reduced, so the native top trace of KC17.5 gives
\[
 \nu(z)(v)
       =\sum_i\deg_{k_L}\bigl((j_i^!\Theta_W)\zeta_i\bigr)
       =\operatorname{Tr}_{Y_{\bar L}}
                      (a|_{Y_{\bar L}}b|_{Y_{\bar L}})=0.
\]
This uses the trace theorem for an arbitrary global top
cohomology class; it does not assert that the cohomological
lift of $b$ is represented by a model Chow class.
Thus $\nu(z)$ annihilates the defined rational subspace,
which proves \eqref{r17fd:residual-map}.

Choose a rational basis of $K_{\mathrm{comp},\ell}$ and
extend it to a rational basis of $V_{\mathcal Y,r}$. In the
finite actual test construction the first block has zero
period on every class under consideration. The remaining
block suffices by Theorem~\ref{r17fd:finite-tests} and has
the size in \eqref{r17fd:residual-number}. This is an upper
bound; the image of $\nu$ may have smaller dimension.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A fixed finite set of local coordinates for the height domain}

Let $F$ be a number field and $X/F$ smooth projective
geometrically integral. Let $\mathcal H^r(X)$ be the R16
height domain, equivalently the homologically trivial classes
having local strong G at every finite place by VT17.
Use a single arithmetic witness $(E,Y,f,\mathcal Y)$ of
GA17, chosen before the classes. Choose once an auxiliary
rational prime $\ell$, and let $S$ be the finite set of
places $w$ of $E$ at which $\mathcal Y$ is not smooth,
together with all places above $\ell$.
For $w\in S$, use the strictly semistable completed model
and put
\[
        V_{w,r}=\ker A_{n-r,w},\qquad
        t_w=\dim_{\mathbf Q}V_{w,r}.
\]
Smooth completed fibers included in $S$ are also reduced
strictly semistable, with one geometric component after
retaining the native decomposition.

\begin{theorem}[Finite rational obstruction coordinates for the height domain]
\label{r17fd:global-finite}
There is a rational linear map
\[
 \mathcal P^r_{X,\mathcal Y}:
 \operatorname{CH}^r(X)_{\mathbf Q,\mathrm{geom\,hom}}
             \longrightarrow\bigoplus_{w\in S}V_{w,r}^{\vee}
 \tag{FD17.14}\label{r17fd:global-map}
\]
whose kernel is exactly $\mathcal H^r(X)$.
Every coordinate is the period of a fixed global ordinary
$D$-closed actual form on $X_{F_v}^{\mathrm{an}}$, where
$v$ is the place below $w$, evaluated on the original
specified $Z_{F_v}$. All these tests and the finite set $S$
are independent of the cycle and its representative.
Consequently
\[
 \dim_{\mathbf Q}
 \left(\operatorname{CH}^r(X)_{\mathbf Q,\mathrm{geom\,hom}}
                         /\mathcal H^r(X)\right)
                  \le\sum_{w\in S}t_w<\infty.
 \tag{FD17.15}\label{r17fd:global-bound}
\]
If $n\ge3$, choose for each $w\in S$ one
$\ell_w\ne\operatorname{char}k_w$, and put
$K_w=K_{\mathrm{comp},\ell_w}(\mathcal Y_w,r)$.
The target in \eqref{r17fd:global-map} can be replaced by
$\bigoplus_{w\in S}(V_{w,r}/K_w)^\vee$, and the bound
improves to
\[
 \dim_{\mathbf Q}
 \left(\operatorname{CH}^r(X)_{\mathbf Q,\mathrm{geom\,hom}}
                         /\mathcal H^r(X)\right)
             \le\sum_{w\in S}(t_w-\dim_{\mathbf Q}K_w).
 \tag{FD17.16}\label{r17fd:global-refined-bound}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
At $w\in S$, apply the preceding construction with
$K=F_v$, $L=E_w$, generic map
$f_w:Y_{E_w}\to X_{E_w}$, and the fixed completed model.
The resulting map is
\[
   z\longmapsto
       \bigl[v\longmapsto
          \langle r_r[\overline{(f^*z_E)_w}],v\rangle\bigr]
          \in V_{w,r}^{\vee}.
\]
Choosing a rational basis and the normalized forms
\eqref{r17fd:normalized-test} realizes every entry by an
original-$F_v$ actual period. The full local field degree
$[E_w:F_v]$ is used for this particular summand, without
an additional factor from the other places of $E$ over $v$.
Take the direct sum of these maps.

For $w\notin S$, the model is smooth and the fixed $\ell$
is invertible. Every geometric homologically trivial
$z\in\operatorname{CH}^r(X)_{\mathbf Q,\mathrm{geom\,hom}}$
has geometrically zero $\ell$-adic class, as does $f^*z_E$.
Smooth proper specialization makes every closure restriction
geometrically cohomologically zero on the smooth special
fiber. The native degree pairing with every algebraic test
is therefore zero. The frozen model detector gives local
strong G for $(f^*z_E)_w$. This argument applies to the
entire indicated homological group using the same fixed
model and $\ell$, and hence the same finite set $S$.

If $z\in\mathcal H^r(X)$, obstruction pullback and native
finite-constant comparison make all displayed coordinates
zero. Conversely their vanishing gives obstruction zero
for $(f^*z_E)_w$ for every $w\in S$, by the local
finite-test theorem or the supplied-model VT17 criterion.
The good-place argument gives the same vanishing for every
remaining $w$. Thus $f^*z_E\in\mathcal H^r(Y)$.
For each finite $v$ of $F$, choose one $w$ above it.
Dominant Bott--Chern injectivity for $f_w$, followed by
finite-constant injectivity for $E_w/F_v$, gives
$\mathfrak o_{X_{F_v},F_v}^r(z_{F_v})=0$.
Hence $z\in\mathcal H^r(X)$. The quotient injection and
dimension bound follow by rational linear algebra.
For the refinement, geometric homological triviality over
the number field gives the zero class for each chosen
$\ell_w$. Apply Proposition~\ref{r17fd:homological-refinement}
at each of the finitely many places. Choose bases adapted
to $K_w$ and omit the universally zero blocks of periods.
The resulting smaller finite family still has exactly
the same kernel.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{What is still open.}
The finite numerical spaces and their bases are existence
objects; the theorem does not assert an effective algorithm
for computing all their numerical cycle groups. The period
map need not surject onto the displayed finite target.
Changing witnesses changes its coordinates, while their
kernel and the quotient that embeds into their image are
intrinsic. General local G is now precisely the assertion
that these coordinates vanish on every geometric homological
class. The unrestricted global BB construction would follow
if the finite map \eqref{r17fd:global-map} vanished on the
complete geometric homological Chow group. Neither vanishing
has been proved by the finite-dimensional description.
