% Round 11. A precise sufficient obstruction, with no converse asserted.
\section{The remaining curvature class and the next research target}
\label{r11o:section}

The supported-complex construction changes the form of the remaining
problem.  For every prescribed cycle it provides an actual Green pair
on the exact complement.  The issue in the general homologically
trivial, bad-reduction case is now the removal of an actual model
curvature.  The following formulation records only implications that
have been proved.

For a smooth projective integral $X/K$ set
\[
 H_{\mathrm{BC,act}}^{r,r}(X)=
 \frac{\{\omega\in\mathcal B^{r,r}(X^{\rm an}):
                  d'\omega=d''\omega=0\}}
      {D\mathcal B^{r-1,r-1}(X^{\rm an})},\qquad D=d'd''.
\tag{OC1}\label{r11o:BC}
\]
This is an algebraic quotient of actual global form spaces.  No
Hausdorff topology, finite-dimensionality, or comparison with a
different cohomology theory is assumed.

\begin{proposition}[A model-curvature class of a generic Chow class]
\label{r11o:class}
The constructions of this round give a well-defined homomorphism
\[
  \mathfrak o_X^r:\operatorname{CH}^r(X)_{\mathbb Q}
                       \longrightarrow H_{\mathrm{BC,act}}^{r,r}(X).
\tag{OC2}\label{r11o:map}
\]
If $\xi$ is a model virtual vector bundle whose generic degree-$r$
Chern character is $a$, then
\[
                  \mathfrak o_X^r(a)=[\operatorname{ch}_r(\bar\xi)].
\]
It is independent of the model, model metrics, and virtual-bundle
presentation.  It is compatible with pullback by morphisms between
smooth projective integral varieties.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The rational Chern character of the smooth projective generic fiber
is an isomorphism from $K_0(X)_{\mathbb Q}$ onto its graded Chow ring.
Choose a generic virtual bundle with degree-$r$ character $a$ and
extend its finitely many bundles to a projective flat model as in
Corollary~\ref{r11p:any-cycle-pair}.  The actual Chern form is
separately closed, so defines a class in \eqref{r11o:BC}.

Two such choices pull back to a common projective model refinement.
Their difference has generic degree-$r$ Chow character zero.
Proposition~\ref{r11p:generic-chow-primitive} supplies a global actual
$D$-primitive for the difference of their degree-$r$ model characters.
Pulling model bundles to a refinement preserves their original model
metrics on $X^{\rm an}$.  This proves independence and additivity.

For a morphism $f:X\to Y$, extend it after choosing a projective
graph model and a common refinement.  Model Chern functoriality gives
$c(f^*\bar E)=f^*c(\bar E)$ strictly.  Mihatsch's actual pullback
commutes with $D$, so the equality passes to the quotient.  The
pullback on Chow groups is the usual operational pullback for smooth
varieties.  This proves the claimed compatibility.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A sufficient route to strong G]
\label{r11o:sufficient}
For any prescribed codimension-$r$ cycle $Z$, if
$\mathfrak o_X^r([Z])=0$, then strong G holds for $Z$ on exactly
$X^{\rm an}\setminus|Z|^{\rm an}$, over the original field and on
all actual tests.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Use the exact-support pair $(G_Z,\Omega_Z)$ from
Corollary~\ref{r11p:any-cycle-pair}.  Vanishing of
\eqref{r11o:map} means precisely that there is a global actual
$\Psi$ with $D\Psi=\Omega_Z$.  Subtract $\Psi$ from $G_Z$ on the
exact complement.  Stokes subtracts its curvature on every actual
test and does not change the cycle term or introduce a new pole.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A numerical model lift gives strong G]
\label{r11o:numerical-model-lift}
Let $Z$ be a prescribed cycle.  Suppose an integral projective flat
model of the original $X/K$ carries a virtual model vector bundle
$\xi\in K_0(\mathcal X)_{\mathbb Q}$ such that
\[
 \operatorname{ch}_r(\xi|_X)=[Z],\qquad
 \deg_V\bigl(\operatorname{ch}_r(\xi|_{\mathcal X_s})\cap[V]\bigr)=0
\tag{OC4}\label{r11o:numerical-lift}
\]
for every integral $r$-dimensional subvariety
$V\subset\mathcal X_s$.  Then $\mathfrak o_X^r([Z])=0$, and
strong G holds on the exact complement of the prescribed $Z$.
No good-reduction hypothesis is required for this implication.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The second condition is precisely operational numerical triviality
in the degree needed by the Round 10 model-Chern detector.
It gives $\operatorname{ch}_r(\bar\xi)=0$ as an actual form.
Proposition~\ref{r11o:class} then gives
$\mathfrak o_X^r([Z])=0$, and
Corollary~\ref{r11o:sufficient} supplies the exact-support form.
\end{proof}

This is the precise interface to a numerical-admissibility approach
to heights.  On a regular model, a pure operational Chern-character
representative of a numerically vertical-orthogonal Chow lift has
the properties in \eqref{r11o:numerical-lift}.  The present
corollary does not prove that such a lift exists for every cycle.
If a lift has only been obtained on a generically finite alteration,
one must still produce the stated model data on the original $X$,
or justify a suitable analytic descent.  A general proper actual-form
pushforward cannot be inserted into that step without proof.

\begin{proposition}[Vanishing through a finite good cover]
\label{r11o:finite-good-vanishing}
Under the finite good-cover hypothesis of
Corollary~\ref{r11p:potential-good-G}, the class
$\mathfrak o_X^r([Z])$ vanishes for every geometrically
homologically trivial $Z$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Write $h:Y\to X$ for the finite flat map, of native degree $a$.
Choose any model character $\Omega_X$ representing $[Z]$.
On a common model of $Y$, compare $h^*\Omega_X$ with the
character of the resolutions of the closures of $h^*Z$ on its
good model.  The latter character is zero as an actual form by
the numerical argument in Corollary~\ref{r11p:potential-good-G}.
The difference has generic degree-$r$ Chow character zero.

The proof of Proposition~\ref{r11p:generic-chow-primitive}
provides more than abstract existence: its primitive is a finite
linear combination
\[
 \beta_Y=\sum_j q_jd_j^{-1}
             \operatorname{Tr}^{\mathscr M}_{b_j}(G_j),
          \qquad D\beta_Y=h^*\Omega_X,
\]
where $b_j:T_j\to Y$ is finite flat on all of $Y$ and $G_j$ is
a global PL-scalar/model-curvature line sum.  Indeed its construction
uses only generically acyclic complexes, whose prescribed generic
open is $Y$ itself.  Each has separately closed actual curvature
$\Omega_j$, with $DG_j=b_j^*\Omega_j$ and
$\sum_jq_j\Omega_j=h^*\Omega_X$.

One does not take the trace of an arbitrary already constructed
primitive.  Instead use the permitted original line sums and define
\[
 \beta_X=a^{-1}\sum_jq_jd_j^{-1}
                    \operatorname{Tr}^{\mathscr M}_{h\circ b_j}(G_j).
\]
Every composite is finite flat on the whole $X$.  The minimal-trace
pairing, Stokes, and the native finite-degree formula give, for
each compact actual test $\eta$ of degree $(n-r,n-r)$,
\[
 \begin{split}
 \int_X\beta_XD\eta
 &=a^{-1}\sum_jq_j\int_Y\Omega_jh^*\eta\\
 &=a^{-1}\int_Yh^*(\Omega_X\eta)
 =\int_X\Omega_X\eta.
 \end{split}
\]
Current injectivity gives $D\beta_X=\Omega_X$ as actual forms.
Thus its class in \eqref{r11o:BC} is zero.  This argument retains
the original field and uses no general trace on source primitives.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{No converse has been established.}
If another zero-curvature Green form exists, subtracting it from
$G_Z$ gives a primitive only on $X\setminus|Z|$.  Such a difference
need not extend as an actual form across the cycle.  Thus the argument
does not identify strong G with the vanishing of
$\mathfrak o_X^r([Z])$.  It gives a concrete sufficient target.
Likewise the normal-Chern obstruction in Section~\ref{r11n:section}
tests one specified distance kernel; it is not an obstruction to all
possible Green representatives.

\paragraph{The next precise question.}
Let $X/K$ be smooth projective with arbitrary reduction, and let
$Z$ have zero geometric $\ell$-adic class.  The new target is to prove
\[
             \mathfrak o_X^r([Z])=0,
\tag{OC3}\label{r11o:target}
\]
or to produce a different exact-support construction that bypasses
this sufficient condition.  The current proofs establish the
vanishing for a fixed smooth good model, a finite good cover, and
generic Chow-zero classes.  In top degree the weighted vertical-component
calculation provides an actual primitive when the stated
$\mathbb Q$-Cartier model exists.  None of these arguments proves
\eqref{r11o:target} in arbitrary middle codimension at bad reduction.

An important test case for the present actual-form route is
codimension two on a threefold with bad reduction.  In that case
the desired global correction has degree $(1,1)$, while the curvature
has degree $(2,2)$.  A scalar component-graph calculation by itself
controls top-degree measures and does not supply this middle-degree
primitive.  Any proposed proof should identify the actual global
form that supplies it, or the precise descent complex used to glue
local primitives.  This is not a withdrawal of the distinct Round 9
numerical-admissibility/real-height candidate for threefolds.  That
construction and an actual Green form on the prescribed analytic
complement have different final requirements.  No implication from
the former to the latter is assumed without the analytic comparison
and descent it would require.

\paragraph{Two secondary interfaces that remain open here.}
For arbitrary nonreduced isolated complete intersections, equality
of lengths has not been proved to determine the local polar Aeppli
class.  The parameter-power theorem resolves a substantial explicit
class, and model truncation resolves the cancellation problem under
a separate common-model hypothesis.  For a general regular immersion,
vanishing of the lower actual normal Chern forms is necessary for
the canonical distance kernel to work; sufficiency has been proved
only for the projective-bundle zero-section family.  Neither missing
interface may be replaced by equality of ordinary currents alone.

\paragraph{What this means for the height project.}
The present results concern the local non-archimedean strong Green
input.  They do not by themselves prove a canonical height pairing,
independence of all choices, global reciprocity, or a full
unconditional Beilinson--Bloch height in arbitrary codimension.
Those assertions require their own constructions and comparisons.
