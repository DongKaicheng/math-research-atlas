\section{A strict actual witness for the symmetric Gaussian pivot involution}
\label{sec:r12-symmetric-gaussian-involution}

\paragraph{Status and provenance.}
\textbf{PROVED THIS ROUND, submitted for independent exact-source review.}
The constructor is \texttt{/root}.  The objects compared here are the
two complete prescribed GB pairs, with their original nested caps and
all differentiated-partition terms.  The result is an explicit actual
second witness, not literal equality of the pairs and not an inference
from equality of their matrix endpoints.  It uses a new, specified
\emph{symmetric} choice of the GIC partition function.  Frozen GIC7
did not impose that symmetry; no assertion for its arbitrary previous
choice is retroactively strengthened.

Let $N$, $L$, $f$, $W=N\setminus|f=0|$, $R=c_1(\overline L)$,
$r\ge2$ and $s=r-1$ be the original-field data of GB.  Only the
first two entries are affected.  Write
\[
 A=\begin{pmatrix}\alpha&\beta\\\gamma&\delta\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad \Delta=\alpha\delta-\beta\gamma\in\mathcal O(N)^\times,
 \qquad J=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{SGI1}
\]
Matrices are extended by the identity on the passive $r-2$ rows.
Choose once and for all a smooth real function $\chi$ such that
\[
 \chi(t)=0\ (t\le-\varepsilon),\qquad
 \chi(t)=1\ (t\ge\varepsilon),\qquad
 \chi(-t)=1-\chi(t),\qquad \varepsilon>0.
 \tag{SGI2}
\]
For instance choose a nonnegative even smooth density supported in
$(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)$ with integral one and take its
cumulative integral.  This is an ordinary real coefficient choice,
not an integration of forms along an analytic parameter space.

Denote by $p_A$ the GB22 pair with this partition, but retain
\emph{all original} GB16 nested cap choices.  Use exactly the same
native conventions for $p_{JA}$.  No new primitive replaces a
native edge or a frozen nested relation.

\subsection{Whole native words and an automatically available common cap}

On $N_{\alpha\gamma}=\{\alpha\gamma\ne0\}$ put
\[
 a=v(\alpha),\qquad c=v(\gamma),\qquad t=c-a,
 \qquad \theta=\chi(t),\qquad d=r-2.
 \tag{SGI3}
\]
The GIC ordinary extensions of $\theta$ are used on $N$: it is zero
near $\alpha=0$ and one near $\gamma=0$.  Its differentiated
supports are closed in $N$ and contained in $N_{\alpha\gamma}$.
Since the first column of $JA$ is $(-\gamma,\alpha)$, its specified
partition is exactly $1-\theta$, including these ordinary extensions.

Write $w_\alpha(A),w_\gamma(A)$ for GB4--GB5, and write
$p_\alpha(A),p_\gamma(A)$ for their literal native pairs.  We first
need the stronger, presentation-level identities
\[
 \begin{split}
 p_\alpha(JA)&=p_\gamma(A),&
 p_\gamma(JA)&=p_\alpha(A),\\
 \mathcal J_\Psi(w_\alpha(JA);f)&=
                  \mathcal J_\Psi(w_\gamma(A);f),&
 \mathcal J_\Psi(w_\gamma(JA);f)&=
                  \mathcal J_\Psi(w_\alpha(A);f)
 \end{split}
 \tag{SGI4}
\]
for the same arbitrary supplied normalization $\Psi$.  The second
line refers to the complete TCT1 companion, not to the boundary of
that companion.

\begin{proof}[Verification of (SGI4)]
The first word for $JA$ is
\[
 E_{21}(-\alpha/\gamma)
 D_1(-\gamma)D_2(-\Delta/\gamma)E_{12}(\delta/\gamma).
 \tag{SGI5}
\]
The word for the right-hand side is
$J E_{21}(-\alpha/\gamma)D_1(\gamma)D_2(\Delta/\gamma)
E_{12}(\delta/\gamma)$.
The initial shear is the same.  Each diagonal weight is the same,
since $|-1|=1$ in every completed residue field.  Before the first
and second diagonal steps, the differing tuples are related by
the indicated diagonal signs, so every endpoint norm agrees.
Before the last shear, the two selected rows are both negated;
this simultaneous sign change commutes with its shear and preserves
the norm of its native middle normalizer.  Thus all three SHT shear
potentials and both weights agree.  The final $J$ has its prescribed
zero pair and does not change a norm vertex.

The second Gaussian word for $JA$ is literally $Jw_\alpha(A)$,
so it has the same chronological nonzero native contributions as
$w_\alpha(A)$.  This proves the first line, including arbitrary zero
shear coefficients through the SHT ordinary zero extension.  The
complete CS edge companion and each MT15 summand depend on these
same norm potentials, weights, reference metric and common $\Psi$.
They therefore agree term by term as well, proving the second line.
This argument does not assert equivariance under a general
norm-isometric matrix.
\end{proof}

We now construct, rather than assume, the common cap needed to use
TCT9 simultaneously for $A$ and $JA$.  When $r=2$ no cap is needed.
Assume $r\ge3$.  In GIC13, the genuine two-target calls are the
listed WNR, NSC and CS calls.  Their pivot parameters
$\alpha,\gamma,\Delta,\gamma/\alpha,\Delta/\alpha,
\Delta/(\alpha\gamma)$ and their nonzero signed products are units
on the entire $N_{\alpha\gamma}$.  Their full unit-edge vertices,
including all intermediate normalizers and their exact prefixes,
are consequently
\[
                    V_1f,\ldots,V_Mf,
       \qquad V_j\in\mathrm{GL}_r(\mathcal O(N_{\alpha\gamma})),
 \tag{SGI6}
\]
for one explicitly finite list obtained by expanding those two GB
tables.  Include both $A$ and $JA$ tables and, if desired, any
additional whole analytic invertible word vertices.  No inverse of
$\beta$ or $\delta$ is required in (SGI6).  The SAR call involving
$\beta/\alpha$ or $\delta/\gamma$ and the SL/MX calls are instead
covered by SAN for \emph{arbitrary} normalization; they require no
admissibility bound.  Their coefficient-zero gluing is the entire
inverse-edge cancellation in TCT, not a discarded summand.

Let $\|V_j^{-1}\|$ be its maximum entry norm and define
\[
 B=\log\max(1,\|V_1^{-1}\|,\ldots,\|V_M^{-1}\|),
 \qquad b=\Phi_f+B+\varepsilon,
 \qquad \Psi=\min(\psi,b),
 \tag{SGI7}
\]
where $\psi$ is the common passive-subtuple potential.  At a
passive zero, the minimum means the finite second branch on its
ordinary neighborhood.  Matrix inversion is analytic on the stated
pivot overlap.  The maxima in $B$ are logarithms of norms of one
finite nowhere-zero analytic tuple (including the constant $1$),
so $B$ has a finite actual valuation presentation.  Both entries
of the minimum share the same original metric term; after that
term is removed the formula is a finite piecewise affine norm
formula.  Hence $\Psi$ is actual.  No closure of arbitrary smooth
functions under minimum is invoked.

For each analytic point and each nonzero section vector, the
ultrametric operator bound gives
\[
 \|f\|\le\|V_j^{-1}\|\,\|V_jf\|,
 \qquad \Phi_{V_jf}\le\Phi_f+\log\|V_j^{-1}\|<b.
 \tag{SGI8}
\]
This includes nonrational points and has no rank factor or global
boundedness hypothesis.  It proves precisely the all-vertex
admissibility required by TCT4.  Thus the complete finite TCT
transport construction supplies quadruples $\mathcal H_A$ and
$\mathcal H_{JA}$ with
\[
 Q_A^{\rm com}-Q_A^{\rm old}=\partial_3\mathcal H_A,
 \qquad Q_{JA}^{\rm com}-Q_{JA}^{\rm old}
                                    =\partial_3\mathcal H_{JA}.
 \tag{SGI9}
\]
They exist on the whole punctured pivot overlap with every original
metric term, wall and mixed coefficient.  For $r=2$ the quadruple
space is zero and both differences in (SGI9) are zero, by the
rank-two clause of TCT.

\subsection{The exact full overlap sum}

For a scalar $F$ write $M_F=(0,0,F)$ in the original triple order
$(A_2,B_2,M_2)$.  Set
\[
 F=-\frac{(a-c)^2}{2},\qquad
 \epsilon_A=e_{c,a-c},\qquad
 \epsilon_{JA}=e_{a,c-a}.
 \tag{SGI10}
\]
Here the $e$ have no curvature factor yet.  Direct full product
differentiation gives
\[
                   \epsilon_A+\epsilon_{JA}=\partial_2M_F.
 \tag{SGI11}
\]
For example the first component is
$c\,d''(a-c)+a\,d''(c-a)
=-(a-c)d''(a-c)=d''F$; the second is $d'F$.
Only $Da=Dc=0$ and actual multiplication are used.

Put $R_*=R-D\Psi$ and, when $d\ge1$, put
\[
        h=-\Psi\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}R_*^jR^{d-1-j},
                 \qquad Dh=R_*^d-R^d.
 \tag{SGI12}
\]
The companion terms in the two TCT10 formulas cancel by (SGI4).
Their scalar coefficients satisfy
$c(c-a)/2+a(a-c)/2=(a-c)^2/2=-F$.
Consequently the \emph{full actual triples}, not merely their
boundaries, satisfy
\[
 Q_A^{\rm com}+Q_{JA}^{\rm com}
      =-M_F R_*^d+H_h(\partial_2M_F)
      =-M_F R^d-\partial_3K_h(M_F).
 \tag{SGI13}
\]
The last equality is exactly the nonclosed-triple QHT identity.
It is essential to retain $H_h(\partial_2M_F)$; $M_F$ is not
declared closed.  Both original coefficient pairs in TCT are
independently closed because they are pairs of unit logarithms,
so the CPT use inside that formula is valid before they are added.

Define
\[
 T_A=-K_h(M_F)-\mathcal H_A-\mathcal H_{JA}
                         \in L_3^s(W\cap N_{\alpha\gamma}).
 \tag{SGI14}
\]
For $d=0$, omit the $h$ and $K_h$ terms and set $T_A=0$.
Equations (SGI9)--(SGI13) give
\[
                  Q_A^{\rm old}+Q_{JA}^{\rm old}
                      =\partial_3T_A-M_F R^d.
 \tag{SGI15}
\]
This comparison has restored every original nested cap, rather
than replacing the GB pair by a new cap-dependent choice.

\subsection{The partition term is an explicit extending boundary}

Recall $K_j(\theta)=\partial_j(\theta\,\cdot)
-\theta\partial_j$, with the full two-colour insertion of FHD2.
In particular
\[
 K_2(\theta)(A_2,B_2,M_2)
 =(d'\theta\wedge A_2+d''\theta\wedge M_2,
   d''\theta\wedge B_2+d'\theta\wedge M_2).
 \tag{SGI16}
\]
Using (SGI4), the weighted first-level pairs in GB22 cancel in
$p_{JA}-p_A$.  Since the new partition is $1-\theta$, the entire
remaining difference is
\[
 \begin{split}
 p_{JA}-p_A
  &=-K_2(\theta)(Q_{JA}^{\rm old}+Q_A^{\rm old})\\
  &=\partial_2\bigl(K_3(\theta)T_A\bigr)
                           +K_2(\theta)(M_F R^d).
 \end{split}
 \tag{SGI17}
\]
Here the sign uses $\partial_2K_3+K_2\partial_3=0$, including
all $D\theta$ and mixed-colour terms before cancellation.

Choose the explicit smooth real function
\[
       H(t)=-\frac12\int_0^t u^2\chi'(u)\,du.
 \tag{SGI18}
\]
It is constant on each of the two tails outside
$[-\varepsilon,\varepsilon]$, with possibly different constants.
Since $\alpha$ and $\gamma$ do not vanish simultaneously, the
scalar $H(c-a)$ on the pivot overlap therefore glues to these
constants near the two pivot-zero loci.  This is an actual ordinary
smooth scalar $\widehat H_A$ on \emph{all} of $N$, including the
common section-zero set.  It needs neither bounded pivot logarithms
nor an asymptotic extension theorem.

The ordinary chain rule, the full closure of $R$, and
$F=-t^2/2$ show
\[
 \begin{split}
 K_2(\theta)(M_F R^d)
   &=(F\chi'(t)d''t,F\chi'(t)d't)R^d\\
   &=\partial_2(M_{\widehat H_A}R^d)
                        \quad\hbox{on }W\cap N_{\alpha\gamma}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{SGI19}
\]
Both sides vanish on the ordinary constant-partition neighborhoods,
so the equality holds on all of $W$ after the stated extensions.

The factors $d'\theta,d''\theta$ in $K_3(\theta)T_A$ have
supports closed in $N$ and contained in $N_{\alpha\gamma}$.
Their product with the overlap quadruple is consequently an actual
triple on $W$ by closed-support zero extension.  The unweighted
$T_A$ is not extended.  Define
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 q_J(A;f)=\operatorname{ext}_0\bigl(K_3(\theta)T_A\bigr)
                         +M_{\widehat H_A}R^{r-2}\big|_W.
 \quad}
 \tag{SGI20}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Strict symmetric-pivot involution witness]
For every $r\ge2$ and every original analytic invertible block
(SGI1), the explicit actual triple (SGI20) satisfies
\[
                         p_{JA}(f)-p_A(f)=\partial_2q_J(A;f)
                         \qquad\hbox{on all of }W.
 \tag{SGI21}
\]
All primitive pairs on the left are the complete original GB
formulas with the newly specified symmetric partition (SGI2).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On the pivot overlap combine (SGI17) and (SGI19).
Near $\alpha=0$ or $\gamma=0$, the partition is constant and the
selected Gaussian native pairs agree by (SGI4).  The first term
of (SGI20) is zero there and the second has constant scalar
coefficient times the jointly closed $R^{r-2}$, so its boundary
is zero.  This proves the equality on an ordinary cover of the
whole original $W$, not by density.  The constructions above
also cover an empty pivot chart, where the differentiated terms
are absent and the same constant-tail argument applies.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope, actual interface and remaining gap.}
The equality is of actual pairs and an actual finite triple, over
the original field and absolute value.  Signed permutations do
not require field extensions, including in residue characteristic
two.  All formulas commute with analytic restriction and
refinement.  Nonproduct units, coefficient-zero loci, arbitrary
real intercept walls, passive directions and the complete metric
transport are retained.  On each fixed whole finite presentation
the displayed operations have the usual finite mixed-coefficient
estimates; no new full kernel or inherited-topology identification
is inferred.

This supplies one concrete second-level coherence for the original
two-chart construction.  It can be substituted into a separately
verified finite word comparison by the literal GB substitution rule.
It does not solve the external-row shear equivariance of a Schur
block whose passive input has changed, and does not supply a full
three-chart Gaussian overlap or arbitrary $\mathrm{GL}_r$ coherence.
No new independent regular-CI existence class is counted from this
identity alone; the actual original-$U$ and all-test bridge remains
the separately checked RAG/RSP construction when its complete data
are supplied.  An asymmetric frozen choice of $\chi$ is outside
the strict theorem (SGI21).
