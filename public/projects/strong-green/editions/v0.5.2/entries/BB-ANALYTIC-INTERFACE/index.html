The remaining broader finite-trace interface

The construction required for Hypothesis 2.2 is proved; the stronger differentiated trace and projection package remains a separate question.

v0.5.2 · Local Green forms

This permanent record is the claim-level reference. The complete mathematical exposition is organized in the linked articles.

The problem

The numerical model detector, scalar–curvature actual trace with all-test adjunction, prescribed exact-support Green pairs and Chow obstruction are established in BB-CHOW-GREEN-PAIRS and its inputs.

The remaining broader task is a trace package on the differentiated forms needed by the stated intersection and global-height arguments, with compatibility with both d′d'd′ and d′′d''d′′, the required projection identities and native degree normalization. The restricted scalar–curvature theorem alone does not prove this stronger package.

Proof programme

Audit the additional trace domains and each differential/projection identity against the precise downstream application. Use the proved construction as an input; retain the separate structured-primitive, base-change, point-slice and global-height requirements where they are needed.
