Algebraic equivalence and compact parameters

A geometric deduction from a Chow-invariant actual obstruction and its support theorem.

v0.5.2 · Local Green forms

Read this claim in context

This permanent record is the claim-level reference. The complete mathematical exposition is organized in the linked articles.

Local Curvature Obstructions and Beilinson–Bloch Height PairingsDomains and Constructions of Beilinson–Bloch Height PairingsLocal Green Forms and Chow Correspondences

Statement

Assume the Chow-invariant actual Bott–Chern obstruction and the exact-support Green-pair construction stated as (A1) and (A2) in the conditional transfer manuscript, together with its rational-fibre-rigidity input. Then, for every smooth projective X/KX/KX/K, the obstruction vanishes on geometrically algebraically trivial rational cycles. Every prescribed representative of such a class therefore admits the zero-curvature Green form of STRONG-G.

Motivation

This isolates the geometric part of the algebraically trivial case from the finite-trace and actual-form foundations. The obstruction and prescribed-pair inputs (A1)–(A2) are established by BB-CHOW-GREEN-PAIRS. The other rigidity, numerical, finite-descent and correspondence premises used by this route retain their stated status.

Proof outline

Generate algebraically trivial classes by curve correspondences and degree-zero divisors. The Poincaré family turns the obstruction into an additive map from a ppp-adic Jacobian. The actual-form parameter argument makes this map locally constant. A locally constant map from a compact ppp-adic group to a rational vector space has finite image; additivity and torsion-freeness force that image to be zero. The algebraic norm family on the Weil restriction has a rational fibre representing [L:K]z[L:K]z[L:K]z. Compact rigidity annihilates its obstruction, and rational linearity allows division by [L:K][L:K][L:K]. The full argument, including its analytic premises, is in the linked source.

The source below contains the expanded argument and its hypotheses.
