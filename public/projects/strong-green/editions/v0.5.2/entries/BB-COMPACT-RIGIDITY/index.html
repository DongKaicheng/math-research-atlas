Compact additive maps into torsion-free groups

An elementary compactness lemma used in the algebraic-equivalence argument.

Local Green Forms and Chow Correspondences

Statement

Let AAA be a compact topological abelian group and WWW a torsion-free abelian group. If an additive map φ:A→W\varphi:A\to Wφ:A→W has kernel containing an identity neighbourhood, then φ=0\varphi=0φ=0.

Motivation

The group lemma is independent of the new actual-form constructions; the analytic work is to produce the required locally constant additive map.

Argument

Let U⊆ker⁡φU\subseteq\ker\varphiU⊆kerφ be an identity neighbourhood. Choose an open identity neighbourhood VVV with V−V⊆UV-V\subseteq UV−V⊆U. Compactness gives a finite cover of AAA by translates of VVV. For any a∈Aa\in Aa∈A, two points among sufficiently many successive multiples 0,a,2a,…0,a,2a,\ldots0,a,2a,… lie in the same translate. Their difference is ma∈Uma\in Uma∈U for some integer m>0m>0m>0. Thus mφ(a)=φ(ma)=0m\varphi(a)=\varphi(ma)=0mφ(a)=φ(ma)=0. Torsion-freeness implies φ(a)=0\varphi(a)=0φ(a)=0. This holds for every aaa.
