Removing an exact global curvature

Zero actual Bott–Chern obstruction gives a zero-curvature Green form on the original support complement.

v0.5.2 · Local Green forms

Statement

For a prescribed rational cycle ZZZ in the scope of BB-CHOW-GREEN-PAIRS, assume oX,Kr([Z])=0\mathfrak o^r_{X,K}([Z])=0oX,Kr​([Z])=0. The proved construction gives (GZ,ΩZ)(G_Z,\Omega_Z)(GZ​,ΩZ​), and the definition of the actual Bott–Chern quotient gives a global actual Ψ\PsiΨ with DΨ=ΩZD\Psi=\Omega_ZDΨ=ΩZ​. Then

γZ=GZ−Ψ∣UZ∈Br−1,r−1(UZ) \gamma_Z=G_Z-\Psi|_{U_Z}\in\mathcal B^{r-1,r-1}(U_Z) γZ​=GZ​−Ψ∣UZ​​∈Br−1,r−1(UZ​)

satisfies the zero-curvature identity of STRONG-G against every compact actual test. The exact same support complement UZU_ZUZ​ is retained.

Motivation

The conditional badge records the obstruction-zero premise. Existence of the curved Green pair and its Chow-invariant curvature class is now proved. General vanishing on homologically trivial Chow classes remains open.

Argument

Actual Stokes for the global form gives ∫XΨ∧Dη=∫XDΨ∧η=∫XΩZ∧η\int_X\Psi\wedge D\eta=\int_XD\Psi\wedge\eta=\int_X\Omega_Z\wedge\eta∫X​Ψ∧Dη=∫X​DΨ∧η=∫X​ΩZ​∧η. Subtract this equality from the curved Green identity. The right-hand side cancels. Since Ψ\PsiΨ is global, its restriction removes no additional points from the domain of GZG_ZGZ​.
