The problem

The source-sensitive strategy seeks an obstruction map

oX,Kr:CH⁡r(X)Q,geom hom⟶RX,r∨ \mathfrak o^r_{X,K}:\operatorname{CH}^r(X)_{\mathbf Q,\mathrm{geom\,hom}} \longrightarrow\mathcal R_{X,r}^{\vee} oX,Kr​:CHr(X)Q,geomhom​⟶RX,r∨​

and a reconstruction theorem on its kernel. The general remaining target is oX,Kr=0\mathfrak o^r_{X,K}=0oX,Kr​=0 on the original homologically trivial Chow source, with all hypotheses of the proposed actual reconstruction verified.

Motivation

An auxiliary residual quotient may be nonzero while every relevant algebraic source pairs trivially with it. The latter is the needed conclusion.

Proof programme

Identify selected periods through model boundaries or arithmetic Abel–Jacobi data; retain the original detectors and exact extension factors. Prove a source-specific annihilation or a strict finite-period estimate that forces the original periods to vanish.
