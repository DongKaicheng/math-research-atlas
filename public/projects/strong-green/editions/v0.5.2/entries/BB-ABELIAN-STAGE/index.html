The semistable abelian numerical stage

Vertical correction on a Künnemann model supplies the certificate needed by finite numerical descent.

v0.5.2 · Local Green forms

Statement

Assume the local semistable model theorem stated as thm:kunnemann-local in the source and the analytic conclusions of BB-FINITE-NUMERICAL-DESCENT. For an abelian variety A/KA/KA/K, every geometrically homologically trivial rational class of codimension 1≤r≤dim⁡A1\le r\le\dim A1≤r≤dimA has zero actual obstruction. Each prescribed representative then has a strong Green form over the original valued field.

Motivation

Künnemann already constructed classical height pairings on abelian varieties. This stage concerns the proposed stronger actual-form realization and its support and test conditions.

Proof outline

After finite extension choose the regular projective semistable abelian model. Extend the generic class to a Chow class Θ\ThetaΘ. Its component restrictions give a class aaa in the special-fibre component complex. Geometric homological triviality and the cited cycle-class injectivity give a∩[Y]=0a\cap[Y]=0a∩[Y]=0.
