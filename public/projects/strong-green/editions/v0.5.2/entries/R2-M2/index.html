Normalized Determinants and Height Metrics

Statement

In the normalized complex setup of R2-M1, a virtual-rank-zero perfect complex KKK on CCC has a unique rigidified isometry

DC(K)≃⟨P,pC∗det⁡K⟩C×J/J. \mathscr D_C(K)\simeq\langle\mathcal P,p_C^*\det K\rangle_{C\times J/J}. DC​(K)≃⟨P,pC∗​detK⟩C×J/J​.

For the cycle comparison, let dim⁡X=n\dim X=ndimX=n, Γ∈CH⁡r(C×X)Q\Gamma\in\operatorname{CH}^r(C\times X)_{\mathbf Q}Γ∈CHr(C×X)Q​, and W∈CH⁡n+1−r(X)hom,QW\in\operatorname{CH}^{n+1-r}(X)_{\mathrm{hom},\mathbf Q}W∈CHn+1−r(X)hom,Q​. Choose rational K0K_0K0​-lifts EΓ,EWE_\Gamma,E_WEΓ​,EW​ whose Chern characters begin with these cycles in their indicated codimensions, and put

F=EΓ⊗LpX∗EW,K=Rq∗F. \mathcal F=E_\Gamma\otimes^{\mathbf L}p_X^*E_W,\qquad K=Rq_*\mathcal F. F=EΓ​⊗LpX∗​EW​,K=Rq∗​F.

Filtered Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch gives

rk⁡K=0,c1(det⁡K)=q∗(Γ⋅pX∗W)=tΓ∗Win CH⁡1(C)Q. \operatorname{rk}K=0,\qquad c_1(\det K)=q_*(\Gamma\cdot p_X^*W)={}^t\Gamma_*W\quad\text{in }\operatorname{CH}^1(C)_{\mathbf Q}. rkK=0,c1​(detK)=q∗​(Γ⋅pX∗​W)=tΓ∗​Win CH1(C)Q​.

The divisor class has degree zero, and the resulting normalized determinant has the flat Poincaré metric attached to that class.

Motivation

This is a scoped determinant/GRR comparison. Identifying it with a Green-form height additionally requires the corresponding metric and biextension comparison.

Proof outline

On a smooth curve, the rank-zero class is [det⁡K]−[O][\det K]-[\mathcal O][detK]−[O]. Determinant identities identify the line with the Deligne pairing. Both normalized metrics are flat and have unit norm at the origin, so the rigidified isomorphism is an isometry. For the cycle statement, the top filtered Chern character has codimension n+1n+1n+1 on C×XC\times XC×X; pushforward gives the transposed divisor class. Common tensor powers clear rational denominators, and taking the positive metric root fixes the rational line.
