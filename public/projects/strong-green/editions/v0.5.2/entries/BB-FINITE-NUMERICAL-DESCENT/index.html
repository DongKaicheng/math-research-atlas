Descent from a vertical numerical certificate

A certificate controls special-fibre intersections before a finite trace returns to the original field.

Local Green Forms and Chow Correspondences

Statement

An rrr-numerical certificate for b∈CH⁡r(V)Qb\in\operatorname{CH}^r(V)_{\mathbf Q}b∈CHr(V)Q​ consists of an integral projective flat model V\mathcal VV and ξ∈K0(V)Q\xi\in K_0(\mathcal V)_{\mathbf Q}ξ∈K0​(V)Q​ such that

ch⁡r(ξ∣V)=b,deg⁡(ch⁡r(ξs)∩[T])=0 \operatorname{ch}_r(\xi|_V)=b,\qquad \deg\bigl(\operatorname{ch}_r(\xi_s)\cap[T]\bigr)=0 chr​(ξ∣V​)=b,deg(chr​(ξs​)∩[T])=0

for every integral vertical rrr-dimensional closed subscheme TTT.

Let h:V→Xh:V\to Xh:V→X be finite dominant between smooth integral projective KKK-varieties. The draft asserts that a certificate for h∗zh^*zh∗z implies oX,Kr(z)=0\mathfrak o^r_{X,K}(z)=0oX,Kr​(z)=0, and hence a strong Green form for each prescribed representative of zzz. The source also allows a smooth integral variety over a finite larger constant field regarded as a KKK-variety, retaining its native degree and special-fibre multiplicities.

Motivation

The composite-trace construction uses the original generators; it does not require a previously traced form to belong again to the trace module.

Draft proof route

Pass to the closure of the generic graph so that the certificate and the pulled-back model character live on a common model. Model refinements preserve the certificate by operational projection. The numerical detector makes the certificate curvature strictly zero, and generic Chow invariance supplies a structured global primitive upstairs. Apply the restricted trace to the original scalar–curvature generators along their composite finite maps, divide by the full native degree, and test against all compact actual forms. This produces the proposed global primitive downstairs. Finally apply BB-GREEN-CORRECTION.
