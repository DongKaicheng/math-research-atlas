The full rational cohomological target is controlled over each fixed field.

Arithmetic Lefschetz and Integral Heights

Statement

For the representation VpV_pVp​ in R2-R1, let SES_ESE​ contain the archimedean places, places above ppp, and the places of a fixed bad-reduction set. Then

H1(GE,SE,Vp)→∼H1(E,Vp) H^1(G_{E,S_E},V_p)\xrightarrow{\sim}H^1(E,V_p) H1(GE,SE​​,Vp​)∼​H1(E,Vp​)

is finite-dimensional. For a stable free Zp\mathbf Z_pZp​-lattice, the corresponding global H1H^1H1 is finitely generated. At good finite places away from ppp, H1(Ev,Vp)=0H^1(E_v,V_p)=0H1(Ev​,Vp​)=0.

Motivation

This controls cohomology over a chosen field. It does not bound the ramification of the fields of definition as those fields vary.

Proof outline

Purity gives weight −1-1−1, so neither Frobenius on VpV_pVp​ nor the inertia-invariant term Vp(−1)V_p(-1)Vp​(−1) has eigenvalue one at a good place away from ppp. Local rational H1H^1H1 vanishes. The cocycle calculation then factors global classes through the quotient unramified outside SES_ESE​. Finiteness for finite coefficient modules, inverse limits, and compact Nakayama give integral finite generation and rational finite dimension.
