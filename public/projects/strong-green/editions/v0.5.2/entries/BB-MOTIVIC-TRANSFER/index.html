Transfer through abelian Chow retracts

An actual diagonal factorization transports the abelian input.

v0.5.2 · Local Green forms

Read this claim in context

This permanent record is the claim-level reference. The complete mathematical exposition is organized in the linked articles.

Local Curvature Obstructions and Beilinson–Bloch Height PairingsDomains and Constructions of Beilinson–Bloch Height PairingsLocal Green Forms and Chow Correspondences

Statement

Assume the analytic packages (A1), (A2), (N), and (C) of the conditional transfer manuscript. Suppose that after a finite extension, the diagonal of XXX admits a rational Chow factorization through abelian varieties with the required Tate shifts. Then every geometrically homologically trivial rational cycle on XXX admits the strong zero-curvature Green form on its prescribed complement.

Motivation

The factorization must be an identity in the rational Chow category; a cohomological or numerical factorization alone does not supply this transfer. The obstruction and prescribed-pair inputs (A1)–(A2) are established by BB-CHOW-GREEN-PAIRS. The other rigidity, numerical, finite-descent and correspondence premises used by this route retain their stated status.

Proof outline

Use the abelian special-fibre numerical input to obtain the required obstruction vanishing on the source factors. Apply correspondence covariance to each term in the Chow identity ∑jvj∘uj=Δ\sum_j v_j\circ u_j=\Delta∑j​vj​∘uj​=Δ. Their sum is the original Chow class. Finite-extension trace, with its full field-degree normalization, descends the vanishing. The support theorem then produces the form for the specified representative.
