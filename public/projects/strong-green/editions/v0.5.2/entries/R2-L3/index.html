Arithmetic Lefschetz and Integral Heights

Statement

Assume H1–H4 for a fixed pointed product and its anchored polarization. The following packages are equivalent:

  1. Arithmetic hard Lefschetz holds in every relevant degree over every finite extension, and every primitive integral image has bounded-degree Northcott for qℓ,rq_{\ell,r}qℓ,r​.
  2. Off-central vanishing holds on all subproducts; the algebraic rational Abel–Jacobi kernel vanishes on every odd central core; every residual integral image satisfies bounded-degree Northcott.

Either package implies real primitive positivity and nondegeneracy of the complementary pairings over each fixed number field.

Argument

From (2), R2-L1 gives hard Lefschetz, R2-N1 gives central integral Northcott, and R2-N2 gives real central positivity. The sum-of-squares identity and its fixed coefficient denominator propagate positivity and Northcott to the primitive groups.

Conversely, hard Lefschetz gives off-central vanishing. Inflate a central class uuu by fundamental classes on omitted factors. Primitive Northcott therefore gives central Northcott, and R2-N1 supplies the other two assertions in (2).
