Off-central Chow vanishing

Only the arithmetic middle degree should survive in the all-h¹ homological kernel.

v0.5.2 · Chow and Lefschetz

Arithmetic Conjectures for Beilinson–Bloch Heights

Statement

For every subproduct YSY_SYS​, every finite extension E/F0E/F_0E/F0​, and every codimension ccc with ∣S∣≠2c−1|S|\ne2c-1∣S∣=2c−1,

USc(E)=0. U_S^c(E)=0. USc​(E)=0.

Here F0F_0F0​ is one fixed field defining the projectors under consideration.

Motivation

The exact Chow-block decomposition turns this vanishing into the arithmetic hard Lefschetz condition for the anchored product polarization.

Proof programme

Study the original all-h¹ Chow source on each subproduct. Isolate the noncentral projector pieces rather than asking only for vanishing of their ordinary cohomology.
