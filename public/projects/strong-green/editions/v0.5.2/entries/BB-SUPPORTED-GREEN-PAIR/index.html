Green pairs on the prescribed support complement

Every prescribed rational cycle has a curved actual Green pair on exactly the complement of its nonzero support.

v0.5.2 · Local Green forms

Argument

Resolve the components of the prescribed cycle by supported model complexes. Their localized leading characters fix the cycle coefficients. Relative line diagonalization and a smooth finite-flat multisection over the entire prescribed complement reduce the construction to PL scalar–curvature expressions. Apply the restricted trace and scalar Poincaré–Lelong, then sum the component constructions with the original coefficients.
