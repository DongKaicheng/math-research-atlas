Bad-reduction metric compatibility

Determine the correction that connects semistable model determinants to actual Green heights.

Normalized Determinants and Height Metrics

The problem

For a fixed semistable source curve over the original valued field, construct the admissible correction to the determinant model metric and compare its rigidified metric with the canonical Poincaré metric. Establish compatibility with the proposed actual-form Green construction, including finite local lengths, trace normalization, and the prescribed representatives.

Proof programme

Use the smooth-model comparison as a normalization test; compute the vertical and graph corrections in semistable reduction; then verify equality with the actual-form construction on its complete test space.
