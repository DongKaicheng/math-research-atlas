The Chow diagonal of a pointed product

A purely algebraic factorization through one abelian variety, with degree shifts.

v0.5.2 · Chow and Lefschetz

Statement

Let FFF have characteristic zero. Let A/FA/FA/F be abelian and C1,…,CsC_1,\ldots,C_sC1​,…,Cs​ smooth projective geometrically integral curves with FFF-rational points. Put

X=A×F∏iCi,B=A×F∏iJac⁡(Ci). X=A\times_F\prod_i C_i,\qquad B=A\times_F\prod_i\operatorname{Jac}(C_i). X=A×F​i∏​Ci​,B=A×F​i∏​Jac(Ci​).

There are finitely many rational Chow correspondences uj:X⇝Bu_j:X\rightsquigarrow Buj​:X⇝B and vj:B⇝Xv_j:B\rightsquigarrow Xvj​:B⇝X of opposite degree shifts with

∑jvj∘uj=ΔXin CH⁡dim⁡X(X×FX)Q. \sum_jv_j\circ u_j=\Delta_X \quad\text{in }\operatorname{CH}^{\dim X}(X\times_FX)_{\mathbf Q}. j∑​vj​∘uj​=ΔX​in CHdimX(X×F​X)Q​.

Motivation

This Chow identity is an algebraic ingredient. Its validity neither supplies regular models nor proves the analytic correspondence-transfer interface.

Proof outline

The pointed curve projectors split h(Ci)=1⊕h1(Ci)⊕Lh(C_i)=\mathbf1\oplus h^1(C_i)\oplus\mathbb Lh(Ci​)=1⊕h1(Ci​)⊕L. The Abel–Jacobi and Poincaré correspondences identify h1(Ci)h^1(C_i)h1(Ci​) with the degree-one summand of its Jacobian motive. Tensor these splittings and the identity on AAA. Each summand factors through AAA times a subproduct of the Jacobians, with a Tate twist. Projection from BBB to that subproduct has a zero-section, giving a Chow retraction. Sum the finitely many inclusions and retractions to obtain the diagonal.
