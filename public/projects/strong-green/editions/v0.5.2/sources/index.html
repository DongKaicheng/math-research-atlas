Primary references

  1. Andreas Mihatsch — δ-Forms on Lubin–Tate Space

    2024 version; actual δ-forms and complete-intersection Green forms

    A primary analytic framework for actual δ-forms on good Berkovich spaces. The general strong zero-curvature target in this project has additional hypotheses and conclusions.

  2. Shou-Wu Zhang — Standard Conjectures and Height Pairings

    Conjecture 2.3.1; 2022 version

    Arithmetic Lefschetz and Hodge-type structures provide the conjectural background for the product-of-curves reductions.

  3. Shou-Wu Zhang — Height pairings for algebraic cycles on the product of a curve and a surface

    Tunisian Journal of Mathematics 6 (2024), 481–501

    An unconditional construction of a Beilinson–Bloch type pairing on a product of a curve and a surface. This established result is distinct from the general actual-form programme.

  4. Ziyang Gao and Shou-Wu Zhang — Heights of Ceresa and Gross–Schoen cycles

    2026 version; abstract and main theorem

    Lower bounds and Northcott on a Zariski-open dense moduli locus for genus at least three. The result concerns variation in a family, not positivity of every residual Chow class.

  5. Sergey Gorchinskiy — Notes on the biextension of Chow groups

    Determinant-of-cohomology and biextension comparisons

    Connects several constructions of the Chow-group biextension, including determinant of cohomology.

  6. Yuta Nakayama — Asymptotics of local height pairing

    Theorem 10.9; Theorem 11.2 and Lemma 11.1

    Relative biextension comparison and boundary asymptotics. The singular-fibre modification used in the cited extension has its own cohomological and Hodge-conjecture conditions.

  7. Wayne Raskind — Higher ℓ-adic Abel–Jacobi mappings and filtrations on Chow groups

    Proposition 2.5 and Corollary 2.6

    Arithmetic cycle classes and Hochschild–Serre filtrations form part of the residual regulator background.

  8. Bruno Kahn — On the injectivity and non-injectivity of the ℓ-adic cycle class maps

    Theorem 2.4; source version recorded in Round 2

    The integral cycle-image finiteness input used in the local lattice argument.

  9. Bismut–Gillet–Soulé — Analytic torsion and holomorphic determinant bundles I

    Theorem 0.1, with the determinant convention fixed in the manuscript

    The family index formula supplies the curvature calculation for normalized Quillen determinants.

  10. J. S. Milne — Arithmetic Duality Theorems

    Second edition, Corollary I.4.15 and Proposition I.0.22(c)

    Finiteness and continuous arithmetic cohomology used in the weight-minus-one target.

  11. Kiran Kedlaya — Weil cohomology in practice

    Chapter 10: Comparing Galois representations: the Faltings–Serre method; Theorem 10.2.2

    The finite-ramification form of Hermite–Minkowski used to control minimal class fields.

  12. Federico Scavia and Fumiaki Suzuki — Non-injectivity of the cycle class map in continuous ℓ-adic cohomology

    Theorems 1.3–1.4

    Integral torsion counterexamples concern different groups from the rational residual quotient; they delimit, rather than disprove, the proposed residual detection problem.

  13. Chow Obstructions and Prescribed Actual Green Pairs

    Theorem 1.1 and its complete proof; v0.5.2

    The internally proved Chow obstruction and prescribed curved actual Green pairs over the original finite extension of Q_p, with exact support and all compact actual tests.
