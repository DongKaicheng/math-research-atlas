Route 1

Vanishing of local curvature obstructions

Start with the pairing on the common kernels of the local curvature maps. Prove that both kernels contain all homologically trivial classes.

Main difficulty. Annihilate the curvature class of every localized global cycle, in both complementary codimensions.

Route 2

Direct construction of zero-curvature Green forms

The principal-push theorem (PH16; Theorem 7.5) constructs the full height pairing from Strong G at every finite completion in both complementary codimensions, within the stated analytic foundations.

Main difficulty. Construct the required actual strong Green forms for every prescribed geometrically homologically trivial cycle, on its exact complement, against all compact actual tests, over the original local field.

Route 3

Admissible arithmetic liftings: Zhang’s approach

Correct cycle closures by vertical cycles on arithmetic models, then use arithmetic intersection. The general construction follows conditionally from the standard conjectures.

Main difficulty. Prove the required duality and Lefschetz statements for algebraic classes on special fibres, or construct the necessary vertical corrections directly.
